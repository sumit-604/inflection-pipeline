ORDER
<#> The Applicant Company herein has filed an application in eform INC-23 under Section 12(5) & 13(4) 7 of Companies
Act, 2013 read with Rule 28 and 30 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014], vide SRN AC2799290 dated 27/03/2026
for rule 28 and 30 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014] for Change in ROC within the same state.
 <#> The Applicant Company has stated in its application that, The Company has been operating from both premises
situated in Mumbai and Pune for several years and owns both the properties. The Company already has substantial
business operations and administrative set-up at Pune, which is conveniently located near to our main manufacturing plant
situated at Walchandnagar, Indapur, District - Pune. In order to achieve administrative convenience, operational efficiency
and cost optimization, and considering the proposed disposal of Mumbai premises, the Company proposes to shift its
Registered Office from Mumbai to Pune. The proposed shifting will not adversely affect any stakeholders including
shareholders, creditors or employees..
<#> The application has been examined and now, therefore,  in exercises of the powers vested with the undersigned vide
notification number S.O.4090 (E) dated 19.12.2016, r/w the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2019, it is hereby ordered that,
after the consideration of reasons as submitted by the Applicant Company, for Shifting of Registered office which will lead to
,Change in ROC within the same state i.e. from ROC(existing) Registrar of Companies, Maharashtra, Mumbai to ROC(New)
Registrar of Companies, Pune is allowed subject to the following conditions:
