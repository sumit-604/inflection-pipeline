From the MD's Desk
4
Tarun Sharma
Promoter, Chairman & MD
As we reflect on H1FY27, our focus remains on building Credent Connect N Care Limited into an integrated 
healthcare logistics and pre-analytics platform. With over 14 years of experience, a pan-India network and 
strong healthcare relationships, we continue to strengthen our capabilities across sample collection, 
temperature-controlled logistics and technology-enabled services. 
We have commenced FY27 on a strong note, with H1 consolidated revenue growing 53% YoY to Rs 1,381 Mn., 
EBITDA grew 53% YoY to Rs 182 Mn, while PAT increased 95% to Rs 147 Mn, reflecting strong business momentum 
and improved profitability.
Our listing marks a significant milestone in our growth journey, reinforcing our commitment to transparency, 
accountability and long-term shareholder value creation.
With growing demand for reliable, technology-enabled healthcare services, our integrated offerings, trained 
workforce and established customer relationships position us well to expand across markets and healthcare 
segments. Our digital initiatives further strengthen operational visibility, efficiency and scalability. Building on 
these strengths, we are targeting ~45% revenue CAGR through FY30.
We remain focused on operational excellence, digital transformation and service expansion, with a continued 
commitment to sustainable, profitable growth and lasting shareholder value.

Credent | A Leading Integrated Healthcare Logistics Platform
6
Credent Connect N Care Limited is a market leader in the healthcare-focused operations and logistics industry, enabling the seamless movement of diagnostic samples, 
medical supplies and patient-facing healthcare services across India. With an integrated network spanning logistics, phlebotomy, cold-chain, supply-chain, technology 
and corporate wellness, the Company serves as an end-to-end operational backbone for diagnostics, hospitals and healthcare enterprises, while catering to corporate 
clients through Occupational Health Centres (OHCs) and Annual Health Check-ups (AHCs).
