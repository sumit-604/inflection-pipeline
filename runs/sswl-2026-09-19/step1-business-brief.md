## The engine

The thesis would rest on a MIX SHIFT: steel wheels (a steel-price pass-through
converter) to alloy wheels and aluminium knuckles, with Bhuj as an
export-facing plant. EBITDA per wheel rising from Rs 262 to Rs 314 is the
observable. The counter-reading is that margin has fallen for four years and
cash conversion is thin, so the mix shift has not yet shown in the annual
numbers.
