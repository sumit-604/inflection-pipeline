- **Revenue recognition**: Sale of goods recognised at the point control transfers, defined as Ex-Factory
  terms — i.e., when goods are made available at the factory gate ready for dispatch, for BOTH domestic
  and EXPORT sales (Annexure IV §2.13, F-12). 🟡 Watch — recognising export revenue on Ex-Factory terms
  (rather than on shipment/bill-of-lading or customer acceptance) is an aggressive-leaning convention for
  precision-engineering exports to aero/defence/rail customers, where risk and reward typically transfer
  later (FOB port, or on inspection/acceptance for aero/defence). This shortens the revenue recognition
  point relative to physical/economic transfer of risk in many export contracts and merits scrutiny
  against actual sales contracts (not disclosed in this document). Services revenue (design/engineering)
  is recognised on rendering per contract terms (Annexure IV §2.13, F-12); a new revenue line
  ("Sale of Services — Domestic") of ₹2,300.00 Lakh (₹23.00 Cr) appeared for the FIRST TIME in FY26 with
  NIL in FY25 and FY24 (Annexure XXIV, F-25) — see Section 11.

- **Other income recognition**: Interest income on time-proportion basis; other income recognised when
  right to receive is established (Annexure IV §2.14, F-12). 🟢 Clean, standard.

- **No first-time Ind AS adoption** (not applicable, company reports under IGAAP throughout the restated
  period). **NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT** as N/A.

- **Tax regime change, FY26**: The company transitioned from the concessional 15% corporate tax rate
  (Section 115BAB, new manufacturing company rate) to the standard corporate regime under Section 115BAA
  because, per the Statement of Tax Shelters footnote, "due to the acquisition of second-hand machinery
  previously used in India in Fiscal 2025, the company no longer meets the 'new plant and machinery'
  requirement mandated under Section 115BAB" (Annexure XLI, F-39). 🟡 Watch — this is a real, disclosed,
  self-inflicted loss of a favourable tax status (buying used machinery broke Section 115BAB eligibility)
  and permanently raises the effective tax rate going forward; MAT rate is shown as 0.00% in all three
  years (Annexure XLI, F-39), consistent with 115BAA opt-in (MAT does not apply under 115BAA).
