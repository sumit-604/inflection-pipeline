### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

For every miss/shortfall, the reason given:
- Government fiscal-grant delay (May-June expected, delayed to October) — **external-blame** (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar)
- Maharashtra/Bihar state elections stalling work — **external-blame** (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar)
- Survey of India indigenization ("Made in India to Owned in India") causing self-inflicted delay to capture a stronger multiplier — **mixed**: framed as a deliberate strategic choice, not pure externality (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar)
- Customer AI-readiness assurance requests (30% private, 70% government) delaying billing — **external-blame**, though also framed as validating AI investment (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar)
- GST rate cut (Aug 15 PM announcement) slowing auto sales through September — **external-blame** (Q2 FY26, Rakesh Verma)
- Rs20cr automotive IoT order delayed by "regulatory things," not "execution capability" — **external-blame** (Q4 FY26, Rakesh Verma)
- State energy-response-system order delayed by the government counterparty itself — **external-blame** (Q4 FY26, Rohan Verma)
- IoT government tender sent to re-tender — **external-blame** (Q4 FY26, Rohan Verma)
- **Honest admissions (rare but present):** "I understand we are a bit conservative now when we project volume" (Rohan Verma, Q4 FY26, PDF p.16); "I know it not only confuses you guys, it confuses us also" on the C&E/A&M reporting split (Rakesh Verma, Q4 FY26, PDF p.10-11)

**Pattern check:** Predominantly external-blame (government timing, elections, re-tenders, customer readiness, hardware price inflation), with two clear honest-admission moments. Management proactively raised the "weak quarter" framing at the *top* of the Q3 FY26 call before any question was asked ("I'd like to start saying very openly... the Q3 has been a weak quarter," Rakesh Verma, PDF p.2) and proactively disclosed the open order book figure for the first time ("normally we don't talk about it every quarter," same page) — genuine proactive transparency. But the granular account-level bridge to the Q4 shortfall (Rs45-50cr) only emerged after three rounds of sustained pressure from one analyst (Gautam Rathi), not proactively.

**Classification: external-blame-heavy**, with credible but limited proactive-disclosure counterweight.

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A Competitor commentary
No named competitor was referenced by management in any of the three scored calls (Q2/Q3/Q4 FY26) or in the Q1 FY26 context call. Only indirect, unnamed references appear: "other folks are focused on [pure survey work]... while we are focused on a genuine platform" (Q2 FY26, Rohan Verma, PDF p.10) and a DSO benchmark against "those who are in the government or even who are in our similar space of GIS listed or unlisted" (Q4 FY26, Rohan Verma, PDF p.14) — again unnamed. Credibility check: this consistent avoidance of naming competitors across four consecutive calls is itself a pattern worth testing against peers — does management ever name specific rivals, or is blanket avoidance company policy regardless of results.

### 3B Industry and market intelligence dropped in the calls
- Auto production grew ~18% YoY in Q3 FY26 (cited unchallenged by analyst Rishabh Rathi) vs MMI's A&M growth of ~15% in the same quarter — management attributes the gap to technology penetration being concentrated in "connected vehicles," not total volume (Sapna Ahuja, Q3 FY26, PDF p.6).
- GST rate cut following the Aug 15, 2025 PM announcement caused an automotive sales slowdown through September 2025, with only partial recovery by quarter end (Q2 FY26, Rakesh Verma, PDF p.9).
- Government business has held steady at ~18-20% of total company revenue across FY25 and FY26 (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar/Anuj Jain, PDF p.4).
- Global DRAM/NAND memory price inflation is raising IoT hardware costs industry-wide (Q4 FY26, raised by analyst Shrinarayan Mishra, confirmed by Rakesh Verma: "prices of the hardware is going up, the cost of purchase or acquisition is going up," PDF p.18).
- "Made in India to Owned in India" government procurement shift cited as the reason the Survey of India project required in-house indigenization, causing a delivery delay (Q3 FY26, Nikhil Kumar, PDF p.3).
- AI-adoption theme (explicitly referencing the "Global AI Summit" and Anthropic) cited as causing customer-side scope-change delays across ~30% private and ~70% government accounts in Q3 FY26 (Nikhil Kumar, PDF p.5).
- Fixed-price contract share in the order book has risen materially over the "last 3, 4 years... specifically last 2 years" per an analyst's own tracking, not disputed by management (Q4 FY26, Gautam Rathi, PDF p.15-16); management response suggests volume-linked contracts have historically "flexed up only" but the company has become "a bit conservative" in volume projections recently (Rohan Verma, PDF p.16).
- Order-book-to-revenue conversion efficiency across the industry/company has declined structurally: 21-22% (FY22) → ~13% (FY25) → ~18% (FY26) (Q4 FY26, Anmol Garg/Rakesh Verma, PDF p.5).

**Grading rationale:** The framework requires grading on promise-delivery evidence, not tone.
