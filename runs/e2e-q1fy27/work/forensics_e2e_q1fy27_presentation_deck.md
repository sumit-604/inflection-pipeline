## 4. CRITICAL RECONCILIATION — EXIT MRR vs QUARTERLY REVENUE (thesis crux)

Ledger rows 16.4 (slide 16 L456-457) and 18.1-18.18 (slide 18 L530-554), cross-checked to filing revenue (L79).

- Exit MRR Jun-26 = ₹718 Mn/month = ₹71.8 Cr/month. Annualised = 718 x 12 = ₹8,616 Mn (₹861.6 Cr) run-rate.
- Q1 FY27 quarterly revenue = ₹1,568 Mn (₹156.8 Cr) = ₹522.7 Mn average month. Exit (718) is 1.37x the quarterly average -> a steep intra-quarter ramp, consistent with the ₹5,334 Mn B200 CWIP "deployed in May 2026" (slide 17 L506) going live mid-quarter.
- Prior Q4 exit MRR ₹374 Mn -> exit MRR +92% QoQ while booked revenue grew +64% QoQ: an exit-loaded quarter, MRR outrunning realised revenue.
- Annualised exit run-rate ₹8,616 Mn = 3.5x FY26 full-year revenue (₹2,456 Mn, slide 20 L593) and +37% above a simple Q1 annualisation (1,568 x 4 = 6,272).
- Verdict — internally CONSISTENT mechanically (a mid-quarter capacity add fully explains exit > average), but durability is UNVERIFIED. The MRR series is step-shaped and lumpy (Dec-25 145 -> 280, plus a Sep-25 dip 165 -> 160), i.e. capacity-driven bursts, not demonstrably contracted recurring revenue. With zero named customers, no contract duration and no utilisation %, whether ₹71.8 Cr/month persists into Q2 is the open question the whole thesis turns on.
- Monitorable #1: ₹71.8 Cr blows past the green band (₹35-40 Cr); prior ₹37.4 Cr sat mid-green. Level is a decisive beat; durability is the caveat. Lean bear until an anchor customer and contract term are disclosed.
