## FLAG-PROMOTER — CONCERN

Verdict: CONCERN.

Top two findings:
- SEBI order 29 Sep 2025 found deliberate misstatement of FY15 to FY21 financials, non consolidation of Merino Shelters, unapproved related party transactions and fund diversion, with a two year trading ban on the Chairman, MD and a former ED/CFO. SAT stayed the order on 10 Oct 2025, conditional on a 50% penalty deposit (B08 adverse_findings; media reported, cross verified 4+ outlets plus sebi.gov.in order listing).
- NFRA banned the FY16 to FY17 audit engagement partner (Rohira Mehta & Associates) for five years in Nov 2023 for professional misconduct on the same non consolidation matter (B08; nfra.gov.in, cross corroborated).

Further findings: Rs97.68 Cr loan from promoter controlled Limitless Contracting Pvt Ltd with no disclosed rate or tenure (B08; AR FY25 Note 36C/D consolidated p.232-233). Escalating pattern of 5 to 6 SEBI actions 2014 to 2025. Live Supreme Court dispute between two Mansukhani promoter branches over share title and a Rs466.75L dividend entitlement (B08; AR Note 34(d)/35(d) p.160). Pledge of 65,00,000 promoter shares to SBI, about 8.7% of shares outstanding, flat in absolute count FY25 to Jun 2026.

Transition evidence: Ashish Kacholia, Carnelian Asset Management and Ovata Capital entered via a Rs255 Cr preferential issue (Jul 2025). Professional external CFO Sandeep Kumar (ex Welspun) hired within ten days of the prior CFO's exit. Merino Shelters consolidated since FY23-24, and CRISIL now consolidates Merino Shelters in full (CRISIL RR, 01-Jun-2026), the remediation of the core SEBI/NFRA disclosure failure.

Linked rating trigger: CRISIL lists as an explicit downward trigger "Any liability due to SEBI audit observations impacting the business or financial risk profile of the company" (CRISIL RR, 01-Jun-2026). The SEBI/SAT appellate outcome therefore feeds both the promoter flag and the rating.

## FLAG-CASH — GROWTH-INDUCED

Determination: GROWTH-INDUCED (event driven). Not STRUCTURAL. Not INDETERMINATE. Made on the full evidence, not inherited.

Rating agency evidence (CRISIL RR, 01-Jun-2026):
- Long term rating UPGRADED to CRISIL A+/Stable from A/Stable; short term A1 reaffirmed.
- Liquidity rated "Strong": net cash accruals above Rs450 Cr against yearly term repayment of Rs230 to 250 Cr; cash Rs657 Cr, of which Rs300 Cr unencumbered.
- CRISIL attributes the FY25 to FY26 working capital stretch to named causes: the FY25 Taiwan order execution delay completed in Q1 FY26, and the FY26 Middle East conflict UAE shipping blockade. It states working capital is "expected to normalize in the current fiscal year" on the Fujairah port approval.
- Verbatim rating language: weakness listed as "Working-capital-intensive operations"; liquidity "Strong"; working capital "expected to normalize in the current fiscal year" (CRISIL RR, 01-Jun-2026).
- Interest coverage 3.1x FY26; gearing 0.25x FY26; OPBDIT margin 12.3% FY26 versus 8 to 10% in the past.

DSCR reconciliation (not a raw contradiction): the audited sub-1.0x DSCR, 0.59x standalone and 0.52x consolidated FY25 (B02/B03; Note 40 standalone p.176-177, Note 41 consolidated p.244), is the SEBI format regulatory ratio whose denominator rolls in working capital debt drawn during the year. CRISIL measures term debt service on an NCA versus term repayment basis, roughly 1.8 to 2.0x. A company drawing more revolver in a build year shows sub-1.0x on the SEBI format while term coverage stays comfortable. The two bases reconcile.

Structural residuals held open, not waved through: standalone CFO/PAT 0.69x and consolidated 0.44x FY25 (B03; verified at AR standalone CF anchor, CFO Rs95.06 Cr / PAT Rs137.12 Cr); the 6 month to 1 year receivables bucket grew 11.5x, Rs796.96 lakh to Rs9,182.28 lakh (B02; Note 7 p.145-147); the Rs97.68 Cr promoter entity loan (B02/B08); Rs95.58 Cr disputed receivables over 3 years static with about 13.6% ECL coverage (B02; Note 7(a) p.145-146). CRISIL itself keeps receivables a "key monitorable" with Rs109 Cr of Rs1,250 Cr debtors under arbitration (CRISIL RR, 01-Jun-2026).

Falsification metric (single quarterly print): standalone debtor days. From about 128 days in FY26, if debtor days do not fall back toward 100 by Q4 FY27, or the 6 month to 1 year receivables bucket grows again in any FY27 quarter, the GROWTH-INDUCED read fails and the cash gap reverts to STRUCTURAL. Watch the quarterly results receivables note and FY27 AR Note 7.
