### 2C. Growth embedded in capex

The standard formula (capex under execution × historical fixed-asset
turnover = implied incremental revenue) does not map meaningfully onto an
asset-light SaaS roll-up where the fixed-asset base is trivial relative to
revenue and the actual capital deployed for growth is M&A spend, not
fixed-asset capex. Forcing the formula would understate the real
capital-allocation story, which is captured instead under Category H1
(Section 3) with its own arithmetic (revenue multiples, payback periods).
**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 0 / NOT APPLICABLE**, stated explicitly
rather than estimated.

2018 Indonesia entry (AR p.7, timeline), which is not an emerging item.
