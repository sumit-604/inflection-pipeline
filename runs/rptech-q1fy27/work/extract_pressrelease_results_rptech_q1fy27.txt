157	About Rashi Peripherals Limited:
158	
159	Incorporated in 1989, Rashi Peripherals Limited is one of India’s leading distributors of Information and
160	Communication Technology (ICT) products and solutions, serving as a vital link between global
161	technology OEMs and a diversified network of channel partners. The Company operates a robust pan-
162	India distribution infrastructure spanning 700+ locations, supported by 57 branches, 50 service
163	centres, and 73 warehouses, enabling efficient logistics and strong last-mile reach.
164	
165	Through its two verticals-Personal Computing and Enterprise Solutions (PES) and Lifestyle & IT
166	Essentials (LIT), the company offers a comprehensive portfolio including laptops, desktops, servers,
167	storage, networking solutions, peripherals, lifestyle electronics, embedded and semiconductor
168	products, and IT accessories. Partnering with 80 global brands and serving over 10,250 channel
169	partners, the Company offers a comprehensive range of services including pre-sale activities, solution
170	design, technical support, marketing services, credit solutions, and warranty management.
