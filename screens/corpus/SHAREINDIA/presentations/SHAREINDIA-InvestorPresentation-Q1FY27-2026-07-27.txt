Company Overview. Share India Securities Ltd was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Noida,
Uttar Pradesh. A 31-year young, technology-driven financial services leader, recognised for strong
capabilities in Algorithmic Trading and advanced Fintech solutions. Successfully evolved from a
traditional financial services firm into a Fintech-focused organisation, delivering
latency-optimised, high-performance trading solutions. Offers equity, derivatives, commodities,
wealth management, portfolio management services, advisory & alternate investment solutions.
Pan-India distribution network with 225 branches and franchises across 19 states and UTs.
Proprietary platforms uTrade Algos and Algowire Trading Technologies deliver AI and ML driven
algorithmic strategies.

An Integrated Algorithmic Trading Ecosystem.
uTrade Solutions Pvt Ltd: operates uTrade Algos, a no-code, institutional-grade algorithmic trading
platform aligned with the "Har Ghar Algo" vision. Enables retail and HNI investors to deploy
pre-built or custom strategies with AI-powered analytics, backtesting, and risk tools.
Algowire Trading Technologies Pvt Ltd: provides latency-optimized, institutional-grade trading
infrastructure. Powers the Group's proprietary trading desk and client-facing algo platforms.
Silverleaf Securities Research Pvt Ltd: the Group's quantitative research and strategy development
arm for systematic trading. Builds rules-based, data-driven strategies designed for automation,
scalability, and repeatability rather than discretionary calls.

===== PAGE 15 =====
A Structural Shift in Business Quality. Share India's Evolution from Traditional Broker to Fintech
Platform.
Traditional Broker: Manual/Dealer Driven; Transaction Dependent Revenue; Limited IP; Human
Execution; Volume Cyclicality; Client = Trader.
Fintech Platform: Algorithmic & API-driven; Platform + subscription + interest-led revenue;
Proprietary strategies, models & tech; Low-latency automated execution; Higher recurring & annuity
income; Client = Platform user.

Future Growth Strategies. Providing Portfolio Management Services and strengthening presence in
Wealth Management. Establish flagship retail branches in high-visibility locations. Scale the MTF
book to deepen client engagement, improve retention and support sustained trading activity.
Leverage the International Presence and GIFT City IFSC to pursue cross-border trading
opportunities. Scale algorithmic trading adoption through uTrade by enabling open API-based algo
trading for retail investors. Offer intuitive algorithmic strategies and automation tools to expand
retail participation beyond index-based trading. Integrate advanced analytics and automation.
Diversification across merchant banking, secured NBFC lending and insurance brokering.
