       Aurum PropTech Q2 FY27 total income up 57% to ₹137 crore;
Housing.com acquisition to power the AI-native real estate operating system
 Adjusted EBITDA margins widen 840 bps and PBT margins 1,270 bps as profitable growth continues
Aurum PropTech Limited’s Q2 FY27 marked progress on three fronts: sustained growth and improving
profitability across its existing platforms; integration of Housing.com, India’s leading consumer real estate
marketplace, into the Aurum ecosystem; and continued development of the AI-native real estate operating
system, with AI now embedded across platforms.

Management Commentary
Commenting on the results, Mr. Onkar Shetye, Executive Director, Aurum PropTech Limited, said:

“Q2 FY27 reflects the growing strength of Aurum PropTech’s operating model, with total income growing
57% to ₹137 crore, Adjusted EBITDA margins expanding to 11.5%, and PBT margins reaching ~5%. Our
fourth consecutive quarter of positive PBT reflects disciplined execution, improving monetisation and a
structurally more efficient operating model.

With Housing.com bringing consumer intent at scale, we are building India’s first AI-native real estate
operating system, connecting Discovery, Intelligence, Transactions, Financing and Living. Our next phase of
growth will be driven by deeper monetisation of consumer demand, AI-led operating efficiencies and the
network effects of an integrated ecosystem.

We see AI as a structural driver of revenue productivity, operating leverage and long-term margin
expansion.”

About Aurum PropTech Limited
Aurum PropTech Limited (BSE: 539289 | NSE: AURUM) is an integrated PropTech platform bringing
transparency, trust, and digital transformation to the Indian real estate sector. The Company owns and
operates Housing.com, NestAway, HelloWorld, Aurum Analytica, Sell.do, and PropTiger, serving consumers
across the real estate lifecycle. For more information, visit www.aurumproptech.in
