## Monitorables

Eight items, deduplicated across the stage record. Each says what to watch, the number, where it is, and which leg of the thesis it tests.

1. Recompute EBITDA yourself from each filed quarterly consolidated P&L, as profit before tax plus finance cost plus depreciation. Do not use the figure stated on the call. It must be positive for two consecutive quarters before the FY27 breakeven claim means anything. FY26 audited consolidated EBITDA was minus Rs 4.56 cr including other income against a claimed plus Rs 1.06 cr. Source: BSE quarterly results filings. Tests the whole operating leverage thesis.

2. Watch the rebate and other financial liabilities balance in the FY27 annual report Note 20 equivalent, alongside the cash flow statement. It nearly doubled from Rs 840.81 lakh to Rs 1,598.00 lakh in FY26 and is the entire reason operating cash flow printed positive at Rs 4.996 cr. If it reverts toward the FY25 ratio to revenue, FY26's cash conversion was a timing effect, not an inflection.

3. Track receivables and their ageing every quarter. Rs 105.11 cr on Rs 181.29 cr is 212 days. The over six month bucket improved to 24.3% and must keep falling. Ask directly for the split between government beverage corporation dues and private party dues, because that split is what makes the 212 days structural or fixable. Source: quarterly balance sheet and the annual report Note 10(a). This is the missing evidence behind the INDETERMINATE cash flag.

4. Watch group cash against the Rs 114.50 cr guarantee. Group cash is Rs 7.78 lakh and the holding company holds Rs 0.88 lakh. Any Regulation 30 filing showing the guarantee invoked, or another year of cash at token level, tests the going concern note's silence directly. Source: standalone Note 6 and Note 42(a), and Regulation 30 filings.

5. Track Shotgun on three numbers together, not one: cases, outlet count and realisation per case. FY27 target is 200,000 cases against roughly Rs 18 cr and 9,000 outlets in FY26, and realisation already fell about 10% from Rs 2,000 to Rs 1,800 per case while the state count rose from 11 to 18. Outlets stalling below 15,000, or realisation falling again, kills the growth engine. Source: quarterly investor presentations.

6. Check FY27 revenue against the Rs 240 cr and 30% guidance every quarter, on a stated like for like basis. Q1FY27 printed plus 22.5%, already below pace. The bridge needs plus Rs 55 cr; doubling RTD supplies about Rs 18 cr, leaving Rs 37 cr from a bottled business that declined 15% to 16% in FY26. Source: BSE quarterly results.

7. Read the wine segment result in the Ind AS 108 segment note each year. It ran plus Rs 18.92 cr in FY24, minus Rs 10.49 cr in FY25 and minus Rs 10.61 cr in FY26. A third straight loss year says the brand does not price. Source: annual report Note 37 equivalent.

8. Follow two governance items. First, whether Bhupinder Kumar Sekhri takes a board seat, or the company states a rationale for the 20.79% gift transfer, within two quarters. Second, the CIT(Appeals) outcome on the Rs 663.03 lakh Section 270 misreporting penalty for AY 2018-19. Source: Regulation 30 filings, the next AGM notice, and the contingent liability note.
