## 1A. THE GPCB RESTRICTION (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

### (1)-(2) Exact wording, by section

**Risk Factors, item 16 (p.42-43):**

> "16. We may not be able to expand our production capacity and products manufactured at Unit – III as GPCB has restricted any such expansion in the area where our Unit – III is situated i.e. Padra region, near Vadodara, Gujarat due to ground water contamination related issues. We are therefore limited to the manufacturing of only certain products at our Unit - III that are viable under such restrictions. Hence, there has been a decrease in the capacity utilization for aforementioned periods."
> — KRONOX Prospectus 6-Jun-2024, p.42, Section II - Risk Factors

> "Due to ground water contamination related issues, GPCB has restricted the expansion of manufacturing units in the Padra region, near Vadodara, Gujarat, where our Unit – III is situated. We have installed primary effluent treatment plant at all of our Manufacturing Facilities. For Unit – III, we have Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) permission where we have to treat the effluents generated during our manufacturing process, while for our other units, we have the permission to discharge the effluents to the CETP, Umraya, Padra. ZLD requires installation of a range of advanced wastewater treatment technologies to recycle, recovery and re-use of the 'treated' wastewater to ensure there is no discharge of wastewater to the environment. The adoption of ZLD requires research and process design as the wastewater generated is different for industries and processes. Installation and operation of ZLD equipment to treat the wastewater generated from the production process is a high cost proposition and we have therefore limited the use of this facility to some products. We therefore have to consider the viability of manufacturing certain products at Unit – III due to the use of effluent treatment equipment under ZLD during the manufacturing process. ... We are therefore limited to the manufacturing of only certain products that are viable in this situation till we have further clarity from GPCB going forward."
> — KRONOX Prospectus 6-Jun-2024, p.42-43, Section II - Risk Factors

> "In view of the GPCB restrictions on expansion in the Padra region where our Unit – III is situated, we have acquired a land at GIDC, Dahej – II admeasuring to 20,471 square meters, where we have planned our expansion. In the event the restrictions from GPCB continue to subsist, as above or any directions to reduce the capacity or closure of Unit III by GPCB, we may not be able to fully operate Unit – III and utilise its production capacity which may lead to loss of production which may affect our business, financial condition and results of operations."
> — KRONOX Prospectus 6-Jun-2024, p.43, Section II - Risk Factors

Comment: this is the sole place in the document that describes the GPCB restriction narratively. It is entirely conditional/forward-looking language ("in the event the restrictions... continue," "any directions to reduce... or closure... by GPCB") — the company is flagging a hypothetical, not reporting a received closure/reduction order.

**Government and Other Approvals (p.310)** — this is the section that carries Unit III's actual, current environmental consent (see (4)-(5) below for the quote). It contains NO restriction language and NO reference to the "expansion restriction" — only a valid, unrestricted Consent Order.

**Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments (p.306-307):** GPCB is NOT mentioned anywhere in this section.

> "As on the date of this Prospectus, there are no outstanding actions initiated by Statutory or Regulatory Authorities against our Company."
> — KRONOX Prospectus 6-Jun-2024, p.307, Section I(C) - Litigations Involving Our Company

Comment: the document affirmatively states there is no outstanding regulatory action against the company. This confirms the GPCB restriction is not framed anywhere as an order, notice, or proceeding against KRONOX — it is described only as an area-wide policy constraint in the Risk Factors narrative.

**Objects of the Offer (p.88-89):** GPCB / Unit III / Dahej is NOT mentioned at all in this section (see (8) below — the Offer is a pure Offer for Sale; the Company receives no proceeds, so there is no "use of proceeds" narrative to attach a GPCB/Dahej framing to).

**Our Business (multiple pages: 172, 179-182, 189-190):** covered under (7), (8), (9) below.

### (3) Date of notice/order and reference/file number of the RESTRICTION itself

NOT DISCLOSED. The document never gives a date, order number, notification number, or file reference for the GPCB "restriction on expansion in the Padra region." It is described only in the generic terms quoted above ("GPCB has restricted... due to ground water contamination related issues"). No GPCB circular/order is cited or annexed.

Contrast: the document DOES give a precise reference number and date for Unit III's own operating consent (a different, and current, instrument) — see (4)-(5).

### (4) Expansion-only vs. existing-operations restriction — operative words

The Risk Factor text is explicit that the restriction is on **expansion**, not on existing operation:

> "GPCB has restricted any such **expansion** in the area where our Unit – III is situated"
> — p.42

> "GPCB, has restricted the **expansion** of manufacturing units in the Padra region"
> — p.42-43

> "In view of the GPCB restrictions on **expansion** in the Padra region..."
> — p.43

No sentence anywhere states that GPCB has capped, reduced, or restricted Unit III's EXISTING/current production. The only language touching existing operations is conditional and hypothetical: "any directions to reduce the capacity or closure of Unit III **by GPCB** [not yet issued, per the document]... **may** not be able to fully operate."

This reading is corroborated by the Government and Other Approvals section, which shows Unit III currently holds an unrestricted, freshly issued Consolidated Consent and Authorization:

> "3. Consolidated Consent and Authorization in respect of Unit III under Water Act, Air Act, Hazardous Waste Rules. [Issuing Authority:] Gujarat Pollution Control Board [Reference No.:] Consent Order & Authorization No. AWH-128299 [Date of issue/renewal:] August 9, 2023 [Expiry Date:] March 31, 2028"
> — KRONOX Prospectus 6-Jun-2024, p.310, Government and Other Approvals, Section III.2 "Environmental registrations"

Comment: this CCA was issued/renewed on 9-Aug-2023 (after the "ground water contamination" restriction narrative would have arisen, since the company already describes decreasing Unit III utilisation across FY21-FY23 and 9M FY24) and runs to 31-Mar-2028 with no quantity cap or condition disclosed in the approvals table. The document nowhere states this CCA was reduced, conditioned, or is under threat of revocation. Taken together with the Outstanding Litigation section's blanket "no outstanding regulatory action" statement, the document's own evidence points to: restriction = EXPANSION ONLY. Existing Unit III operation continues under a current, unrestricted 2023-issued consent valid to 2028.

**ANSWER TO THE HEADLINE QUESTION: Does GPCB restrict EXISTING Unit III operations — the document says NO** (existing operations continue under a valid, unconditioned, unrestricted CCA to Mar-2028; the restriction is textually and repeatedly scoped to "expansion" only). The lower utilisation trend at Unit III (92.58% FY21 → 22.93% 9M FY24) is attributed by the company to a self-limiting product-mix decision under ZLD cost economics ("we have therefore limited the use of this facility to some products"), not to any GPCB-imposed operating cap. NOT DISCLOSED: any explicit statement ruling out a future operating restriction.

No production tonnage cap, no effluent discharge volume/limit, and no CTO "volume" figure is stated anywhere in the document for Unit III.

### (6) Timeline, condition precedent, compliance requirement, appeal, remediation

NOT DISCLOSED beyond:

- The company's own forward-looking hedge: "till we have further clarity from GPCB going forward" (p.43) — no date, milestone, or compliance step is attached to this "further clarity."
No appeal, show-cause response, remediation plan, or compliance timeline tied to the ground-water contamination issue is disclosed anywhere in the document.

### (7) What happens to Unit III once Dahej is built

NOT DISCLOSED. The document never states a plan (continue / relocate / reduce / close / surrender consent) for Unit III post-Dahej commissioning. The only forward statement is the hypothetical risk-factor language already quoted in (1)-(2): "any directions to reduce the capacity or closure of Unit III by GPCB, we may not be able to fully operate Unit – III" — this is a risk being flagged, not a stated company plan. Nothing in "Our Business" (pp.172-195) describes Unit III's fate after Dahej starts.

### (8) Dahej framed as ADDITIONAL or REPLACEMENT — Objects of the Issue vs. Our Business

**Objects of the Offer (p.88-89):** Dahej is NOT MENTIONED at all. The section opens:

Our Company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer."

Comment: because this IPO is a pure Offer for Sale (100% of shares sold are Promoter Selling Shareholders' existing shares; the Company gets zero proceeds), there is no "use of net proceeds" narrative in which Dahej could be framed as additional or replacement capacity. The two sections cannot be compared on this point because one of them is silent — this itself is a finding, not an omission to paper over.

**Our Business (p.179-182):**

> "Additionally, we are planning to establish a new manufacturing facility for which we have acquired a land admeasuring to 20,471 sq. mtrs. at GIDC - Dahej II Industrial Estate..."

Comment: "Our Business" frames Dahej as ADDITIONAL capacity layered on top of the existing 7,242 TPA across Units I-III ("Additionally... establish a new manufacturing unit"), driven by growth/new-customer demand — not as a replacement for, or relocation of, Unit III.
