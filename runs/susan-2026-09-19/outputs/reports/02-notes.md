   rising PAT, and what is the plan to normalise working-capital
   consumption (inventory + receivables + short-term advances) relative to
   revenue growth?
2. The RHP's own restated financials show the ₹8.89 Lakh "prior period
   adjustment" in AR Note 3 relates to FY25, not FY26, and that FY24/FY25
   as-filed audited accounts omitted statutory gratuity, bonus and

   explained in the FY26 Annual Report as filed?

   Note 32 discloses only a "demand notice" rather than the search,
   seizure and confiscation-threat facts stated in the RHP.

   Note 16 (₹733.58 Lakhs, stated as fully reclassified to Note 13), Note
   16's footnote ("₹14.90 crore" total, "₹1.40 crore" lien), and Note 4a's
   security schedule (₹322 Lakhs of FD liens) — which figure is the

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

  P&L bypass (Note 3's ₹8.89 Lakh charge), independently shown by the
  company's own RHP restatement to belong to FY25, not FY26 — the clearest
  accounting-quality red flag in the corpus.
- **Aggressive/incomplete prior-year accounting**: FY24 and FY25 as-filed
  audited accounts omitted statutory gratuity (AS-15), bonus, and
  MSME-creditor interest, corrected only at the SEBI-mandated restatement
  stage — meaning the as-filed FY24/FY25 financial statements understated
  expenses in ways only surfaced by the IPO process, not by the ordinary
  audit cycle.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: the FY26 GST matter was a search-and-
  seizure action with a confiscation threat, disclosed in the AR as a
  routine demand notice — a materially thinner characterisation of a
  regulatory enforcement action than what the RHP's own litigation section
  states occurred.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: the government-to-private revenue mix
  reversal (90.55% to 35.78% government share in two years) is nowhere in
  the audited AR, despite being available and material information the
  company itself later disclosed in the RHP.
- **Internal control / disclosure-quality weakness**: three non-reconciling
  "prior period" figures and three-to-four non-reconciling Fixed Deposit
  totals, both within the AR's own notes, for the same balance sheet date.
- **Disclosure-quality weakness**: Note 34's wrong subsequent-events date
  and the omission of the BSE SME DRHP approval as a subsequent event.
