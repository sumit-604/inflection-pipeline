### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — capex_embedded_growth_pct (formal handoff to Stage 9)

**This was NOT FOUND last run for want of the balance sheet. It is now computable directly from the audited AR.**

The AR discloses a formal, audited **Fixed asset turnover ratio** (Note 6 of the "Details of changes in key ratios"
table, AR p.70): **3.0x for FY26, 2.9x for FY25**, defined explicitly by the company as "revenue from operations
divided by fixed assets at the end of the year," where fixed assets = Property, plant and equipment + Capital
work-in-progress + Other intangible assets + Intangible assets under development (AR p.70, footnote 6).

**Arithmetic:**
1. FY27 capex under guidance (Q4 FY26 call, Sumeet Sood): **INR300cr**.
2. Historical fixed asset turnover ratio (most recent audited year, FY26, AR p.70): **3.0x**.
3. Implied incremental revenue capacity = INR300cr × 3.0 = **INR900cr** (once the new assets reach the same blended
   revenue-productivity as the company's existing fixed-asset base).
4. Current revenue base (FY26 revenue from operations, consolidated, AR p.70/p.299): **INR4,359.02cr** (INR43,590.17mn).
5. **capex_embedded_growth_pct = 900 / 4,359.02 = 20.6%.**

**Caveats attached to this figure (read alongside it, not instead of it):**
- This is a **ceiling/asymptotic estimate**, not a near-term guaranteed outcome. The company's own AR states
  consolidated capacity utilisation is running at only ~47% (AR p.33) — meaning even the *existing* asset base is
  running well below the productivity implied by a naive full-utilisation read of the 3.0x ratio. New capex will
  face the same multi-quarter-to-multi-year ramp lag before contributing at the historical blended turnover rate.
- The FAT ratio is a **company-wide blended** figure across all five segments (CDMO, API, Domestic Branded
  Formulation, International Branded Formulation, Trade Generics) and all plants. New capex concentrated in
  Zambia (a JV equity contribution, not a wholly-owned fixed asset on Akums' own balance sheet in the same sense)
  or in Europe (a requalification of existing assets, not new capacity) may not convert to revenue at the same
  blended rate as the historical mix — the true domestically-productive capex-embedded-growth figure could be
  lower than the mechanical 20.6% headline.
- Year-over-year, the ratio itself improved only modestly (2.9x → 3.0x, AR p.70), consistent with "improved asset
  utilisation with higher revenue growth" as the AR's own stated reason for the change — a supportive but not
  dramatic underlying trend.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 20.6%**, shown with full arithmetic per the formal handoff instruction. This is a
genuine, audited-data-sourced improvement over last run's NOT FOUND.
