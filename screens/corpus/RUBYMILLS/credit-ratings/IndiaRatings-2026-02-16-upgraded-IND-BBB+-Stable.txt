India Ratings Upgrades The Ruby Mills's Bank Loan Facilities to 'IND BBB+'/Stable; Off Rating Watch with Developing Implications; Rates Additional Limits
Feb 16, 2026 | The Ruby Mills Limited | Residential / Commercial Projects

Details of Instruments
Bank loan facilities | INR2,482 million (reduced from INR2,500) | IND BBB+/Stable | Upgraded; Off Rating Watch
Bank loan facilities | INR1,357.70 million | IND BBB+/Stable | Assigned
Bank loan facilities | INR396.10 million | IND BBB/Stable/IND A3+ | Assigned

Analytical Approach: standalone view of TRML.

Detailed Rationale of the Rating Action
The upgrade reflects TRML's improved operating profitability margin in 1HFY26, exceeding the agency's expectations. This improvement is likely to lead to healthy EBITDA growth during 4QFY26 and FY27, resulting in enhanced credit metrics. Ind-Ra has resolved the Rating Watch following the successful execution of the memorandum of association (MOA) with Mindset Estates Pvt Ltd (MEPL) and the consequent clarity regarding the impact on TRML's business and financial profile. Lease rentals that were previously credited to MEPL will now be credited to TRML.

Key Rating Drivers
Strengths: Scale of operation likely to improve in near term due to closure of the agreement; Established track record and experienced management; Reputed tenants, moderate renewal risk; Cash flow waterfall ensures debt servicing.
Weaknesses: Single asset concentration risk; Credit metrics to remain stretched in near term; Working capital cycle likely to remain elongated; Resolution on Enforcement Directorate attachment remains a key monitorable.

Scale of Operation likely to Improve due to Closure of the Agreement: During 1HFY26, TRML earned revenue of INR1,552 million with EBITDA of INR270 million and EBITDA margin of 17.41%. Of total revenue, INR1,342.66 million came from textiles and INR209.50 million from real estate lease rentals. Ind-Ra expects a healthy improvement in revenue in FY26 due to the closure of the MOA, as ownership of the leased assets has been transferred to TRML at end-December 2025. TRML's revenue was INR2,427.08 million in FY25 (FY24: INR2,370.20 million; FY23: INR2,596.50 million), driven by textiles at INR2,087.52 million (INR1,971.70 million; INR2,261.40 million). Revenue from real estate lease rentals declined to INR339.56 million in FY25 (FY24: INR398.50 million), attributed to the vacancy of a key tenant. EBITDA declined to INR454.04 million in FY25 (FY24: INR553.66 million; FY23: INR515.62 million), margin 18.71% (23.40%; 19.90%). Ind-Ra expects an improvement in scale during 4QFY26 and FY27 due to closure of the MOA with MEPL in December 2025.

Established Track Record and Experienced Management: Incorporated in 1917, TRML is a composite textile manufacturer producing cotton and synthetic fabrics, also engaged in development, sale and leasing of commercial real estate. Promoted by the Shah family, managed by the third and fourth generations: Hiren M. Shah (chairman), Bharat M. Shah (managing director), Viraj M. Shah (managing director), Purav H. Shah (executive director, CEO and CFO), Rishabh V. Shah (president).

Reputed Tenants, Moderate Renewal Risk: Long-term contracts with tenants such as Ernst & Young, Cathay Pacific Airways Limited, DSP Asset Managers Pvt Ltd, which together account for 75% of total leased carpet area. At end-January 2026, the building had 67% occupancy, with total carpet area of 0.412 million sq ft. TRML has a sizable renewal coming up in FY27; management expects the lease to be rolled over. The renewal risk is a key monitorable.

Cash Flow Waterfall Ensures Debt Servicing: Rentals flow directly to an escrow account on which the lender has a charge. First use of rentals is debt servicing, then taxes and O&M expenses per the business plan, then DSRA and an interest fluctuation reserve.

Single Asset Concentration Risk: Reliance on a single asset exposes TRML to adverse changes in the asset's performance or in demand-supply dynamics of the Dadar area; the property being a well-known landmark mitigates this to some extent.

Credit Metrics to Remain Stretched in FY26: Net leverage (adjusted net debt/EBITDA) deteriorated significantly to 4.91x in FY25 (FY24: 2.53x), likely to exceed 5.0x in FY26, driven by an increase in long-term borrowings to INR3,140.85 million (INR1,896.07 million). Interest coverage declined to 9.57x in FY25 (FY24: 11.91x). The company availed a lease rental discounting loan of INR2,500 million in December 2024, for making payment towards the development partner and upgradation of parking, lobby renovation, among others.

Working Capital Cycle Elongated: Gross working capital cycle 379 days in FY25 (FY24: 304; FY23: 195), mainly from inventory days of 396 (321; 208), stemming from unbilled revenue and work-in-progress. Creditor days 58 in FY25 (49; 48).

Enforcement Directorate Attachment a Key Monitorable: The ED attached bank balances worth approximately INR26 million of TRML at end-October 2025, linked to a 2012 transaction with Rajput Retail Ltd. While the company returned the principal amount, the ED claims interest earned on related term deposits is proceeds of crime, which TRML is contesting.

Liquidity: Adequate. DSRA equal to three months of repayment obligations, INR85 million at FYE25. Cash balance INR110 million at end-September 2025 (FYE25: INR48.94 million; FYE24: INR184.32 million) and liquid investments in mutual funds and government securities of INR1,168.49 million (INR1,003.66 million; INR548.173 million). Beyond the escrowed property, TRML generates rental income of INR10.9 million per month. DSCR likely to remain above 1.20x during the loan tenor. Scheduled debt obligations INR306.56 million in FY26, INR379.43 million in FY27 and INR442.19 million in FY28. The company holds vacant floors, which provide a liquidity cushion.

Rating Sensitivities
Positive: DSCR maintained above 1.2x and resolution of the outstanding governance issue.
Negative: A decline in the textiles division's margins, DSCR below 1.1x, and any negative impact on liquidity due to the outstanding governance issue.

About the Company: Spinning and weaving unit at Dhamni, fabric processing unit at Kharsundi, Maharashtra. Key tenants of The Ruby Tower include Cathay Pacific Airways, IMCD India, DSP Asset Managers, Signet Excipients, and Ernst & Young.
