## MY RULINGS

Every call below is made. Each carries a confidence tag and the single fact that would prove it wrong.

**3. Workup intent: first workup.** `sure`. No companies/AMAGI.md exists and run_type is full. FTTCP sits after the evidence stages and before valuation in this initial workup. Wrong if a prior run folder for AMAGI existed, which it does not.

**6. Backward margin: FIRING, but off a thin and distorted base.** `fairly sure`. **8. Cash conversion, structural versus growth induced: INDETERMINATE.** `fairly sure`. Receivables jumped 35.7% in the six months to September 2025 against broadly flat revenue, which does not fit a clean growth induced build, yet the same period carries about Rs 135 Cr of one time pre-IPO ESOP and SAR cash settlements and IPO effects, and no credit rating rationale or full year receivables ageing was available to separate the two. Both readings stated: growth induced if the next clean quarter shows reported OCF turning positive with receivables growth falling back to at or below revenue growth; structural if reported OCF stays negative with receivables still outpacing flat to slow revenue. The draft scores it the conservative way. Wrong if the CRISIL or ICRA rationale, once obtained, calls the working capital intensity clearly temporary or clearly structural.

**9. Forward cash conversion: STAGNANT, catalyst strength Weak.** `genuinely uncertain`. Reading one, STAGNANT: the deterioration is real but heavily one time distorted, the balance sheet is net cash Rs 1,664 Cr with zero debt so there is no funding spiral, and net revenue retention of 126% supports collection, but no documented working capital program exists, so it does not improve on evidence either. Reading two, DECLINING: reported OCF is negative in the profit year, receivables are going the wrong way, and management named no fix across two calls, which is the Kernex signature. The draft uses STAGNANT because the fortress net cash position and the size of the one time distortion separate this from Kernex, where CARE called the intensity structural and the company was levering up 4x to fund it. The Kernex cap does not engage because the verdict is not DECLINING and a weak passive catalyst exists, the roll off of the one time settlements. Wrong, and it flips to DECLINING with the Kernex cap live, if the first clean post settlement quarter still prints negative reported OCF with receivables still growing faster than revenue.

Reported ROCE was negative every year FY22 to FY25 and only reached about 5.2% in FY26, held down further by Rs 1,664 Cr of idle IPO cash in the denominator. The business has never earned above 18% in the record available. Wrong if a clean ex surplus cash ROCE, computed at stage 11, shows the operating business already at premium returns.

**11. The Normalized ROCE anchor (Amendment 4.5) does NOT apply.** `sure`. That anchor requires an evidenced pre depression cycle where ROCE sat at a high level. Amagi has no such history; it was a cash burning growth company that never earned high returns, so the pre depression median is NOT FOUND. Pillar 1 uses the standard FTTCP table, not the normalized blend. Wrong only if a pre-IPO high ROCE cycle surfaced in a fuller history.

**12. Forward ROCE: STAGNANT, catalyst strength Weak.** `fairly sure`. The one real lever is margin expansion, which in an asset light model flows to ROCE, but the Rs 1,664 Cr cash pile is undeployed with no framework named, so reported ROCE will not move much over 12 months. No capex commissioning or working capital unwind catalyst exists. Wrong if management announces a specific deployment, buyback or large distribution that shrinks the idle denominator inside the window.

**13. Composite +3, position DEEP WATCH.** `fairly sure`. Revenue plus 2, margin plus 1, cash 0, ROCE 0. Two of four transitions are not firing forward, and both are the balance sheet ones. No Kernex cap, no TRIM. Wrong if cash conversion resolves clearly growth induced next quarter, which would lift cash toward STARTING and the composite toward BUY-candidate.

**14. Confirmation bias check passed.** `sure`. With no prior position, the same read holds: a fast growing, asset light SaaS with a huge runway whose profit turn has not yet shown up in cash, sitting on a very high revenue multiple, is a wait for the cash to prove out. That is DEEP WATCH.

## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue, what could keep it firing.** The cloud shift is early: management cites roughly 10% cloud penetration of a large broadcast to streaming market, and the three product lines all grew. Net revenue retention of 126% means existing customers spend more each year without a single new logo. What confirms it: the segment lines holding above 25% growth and the count of million dollar customers rising. What kills it: the net new logo deceleration, 67 to 29 adds and net negative in Q4, spreading into revenue as churn.

**Margin, what could expand it.** The cost ratios are still falling as the platform scales, and gross margin near 69% leaves room if mix improves. What confirms it: adjusted EBITDA margin stepping above the roughly 10% management calls steady. What kills it: more client renegotiations like the top five deal that cut Q4 gross margin, or currency reversing the 7 point tailwind in reported growth.

**Cash conversion, the missing piece.** There is no documented catalyst here. Management pointed to an adjusted operating cash flow that strips out IPO and ESOP cash, but named no receivables program, no factoring line, no payment term change. The one mechanical positive is passive: the large one time ESOP and SAR settlements will not repeat, so next year's reported cash should look better on that alone. What confirms a real fix: reported operating cash flow turning clearly positive with receivables growth back at or below revenue growth. What kills it: another negative reported OCF quarter with receivables still climbing.

**ROCE, why it stays stuck.** The lever is margin, and the drag is Rs 1,664 Cr of cash earning treasury yield inside the capital base. What confirms improvement: a deployment plan with size discipline, a buyback, or a large distribution, plus the margin climbing. What kills it: the cash sitting idle for another year, or an overpriced acquisition that bloats the base further.

Compact evidence read:

| Transition | Best catalyst | Evidence tier | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Cloud shift plus 126% NRR, documented segment growth | Mixed documented and management | Strong |
| Margin | Operating leverage, cost ratios falling, one guidance figure delivered | Documented plus management | Moderate |
| Cash | NONE FOUND as a specific program; only passive one time roll off | Absence | Weak |
| ROCE | Margin flow through; cash deployment NONE FOUND | Absence | Weak |

The two absences are the finding. Two consecutive calls named no plan for either the working capital build or the idle cash. That silence is the reason cash and ROCE score zero rather than positive.
