Financial and Operational Highlights for Quarter ended September 30, 2026:

       Revenue surged 52.52% YoY to Rs. 2,947.57 Mn, while PAT grew 21.24% to Rs. 250.20 Mn.

       Absolute profitability expanded strongly.

       EBITDA margin of 16.35% reflects global input cost headwinds such as crude oil driven polymer

       price inflation and Red Sea linked freight cost surges impacted the entire industry including

       packaging industry in Q2 FY27; as an exporter to 75 countries, customer pricing revisions to

       pass on input cost increases are currently underway, with adjustments expected to reflect in

       realizations from Q3 FY27 onwards subject market stabilization.

       PAT Margin of 8.22% is slightly impacted by a Rs. 60.60 Mn Mexico JV loss in H1 FY27. The

       Mexico JV is in peak scale-up investment phase, and this loss flows directly into consolidated

       PAT with zero revenue offset today; as JV operations reach breakeven, this drag reverses and

       PAT margin recovers fully.

Packaging Limited, commented "We reported a stable performance for Q2 and H1 FY27. In Q2FY27

across domestic and international markets and strong customer relationships.

During the quarter, the EBITDA increased in absolute terms, while the EBITDA margin slightly declined
on a YoY basis. This was primarily due to higher input costs, including crude oil-driven polymer price
inflation and increased freight costs associated with Red Sea disruptions. To mitigate this, we

materialise over next few quarters.

of our integrated manufacturing model, operational execution and established customer relationships
across domestic and international markets.

Going forward, we will continue to focus on strengthening our manufacturing capabilities, expanding
customer relationships and increasing our presence across domestic and international markets. Our
priorities will include investments in capacity, growth, innovation and sustainability, supported by an
emphasis on operational efficiency and disciplined growth."
