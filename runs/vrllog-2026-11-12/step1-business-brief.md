## Business read
1. Goods transport by road, parcel loads. LTL (less than truckload) was 90.4%
   of H1 FY27 sales, FTL 8.2%, other 1.4%.
   (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/vrl-q2fy27-profit-doubles-ppt-cmvzpx9x1004t2vpe9enb3m7y)
2. The fleet is owned, about 5,941 vehicles, feeding a branch and hub network.
   The model is asset-heavy: margin comes from load factor on owned trucks,
   bulk diesel buying and in-house maintenance, not from a broker spread.
   (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/vrl-fleet-branch-network-expansion-ppt-cmvzq1owf004v2vpee1vft89q)
3. FY26: revenue from operations Rs 3,221 Cr against Rs 3,161 Cr (+2%); PAT
   Rs 236.8 Cr against Rs 182.9 Cr (+29%). Price went up and low-margin
   contracts went out, so volume was weak while profit rose.
   (https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/transportation/VRL-Logistics-FY26-Profit-Jumps-29percent-on-Operational-Efficiency/6a0ac4f4591776c1328a3119)
4. Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026): revenue Rs 961.9 Cr (+21% YoY); EBITDA Rs 225.6 Cr,
   margin 23.3% against 19.7%; PAT Rs 103 Cr against Rs 49.9 Cr (+106%).
   Tonnage 1,079 thousand tonnes (+11%); realisation Rs 8,840 per tonne (+9%).
   (same multibagg article as line 1)
5. H1 FY27: total income Rs 1,851.4 Cr (+19%); EBITDA Rs 418.2 Cr, margin
   22.6%; PAT Rs 183.5 Cr (+84%); tonnage 2,099 thousand tonnes (+10%);
   operating cash flow Rs 362.5 Cr; net debt Rs 337 Cr against Rs 440 Cr at
   March 2026; 67 branches added. (same article)
6. Cost structure, Q2 FY27: fuel 25.6% of revenue (diesel Rs 97.05 a litre
   against Rs 85.44), staff 16.5% (from 18.3%), tolls 7.1%, lorry hire 4.5%.
   The margin gain came from price and volume spreading the staff and toll
   cost, with fuel passed through. (same article)
7. Guidance trail on FY27 volume growth: 6-7% after Q4 FY26, 8% after Q1
   FY27, 9-10% for H2 FY27 after Q2. EBITDA margin guide: 20-21% earlier, now
   "21-22% sustainable". FY27 capex Rs 220-240 Cr, vehicles and property.
   (https://www.sahi.com/news/vrl-logistics-targets-8-volume-growth-and-outlines-220-240-crore-capex-plan-3763-PE1_COR ;
   https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/vrl-logistics-q2-net-profit-jumps-106-103-crore/55163140 ;
   https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/vrl-branches-volume-growth-target-concall-cmw64e25k009f2bnxx17uhlun)
8. Capital return: tender buyback of up to 87.5 lakh shares at Rs 320, cap
   Rs 280 Cr, board 2026-08-04, postal ballot closed 2026-10-03, record date
   2026-10-22. Promoters Vijay Sankeshwar (28.33%) and Anand Sankeshwar
   (31.43%) said they will not tender, so the promoter share rises. A 1:1
   bonus issue was reported with the FY26 results.
   (https://www.kotakneo.com/news/stocks/vrl-logistics-buyback-approved-280-crore-offer/ ;
   https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/vrl-logistics-buyback-vote-results-cmuwemxml000138mi6mmfh7ry)
9. Customers: fragmented small and mid-size shippers and traders, domestic
   only. No export revenue. Customer concentration figure: NOT FOUND on web.
10. The ONE engine: price discipline plus branch-led tonnage growth on a fixed
    owned network. The thesis rests on EBITDA margin holding at 21% or more
    while tonnage grows 8-10%. FY26 proved the price half with flat volume.
    H1 FY27 is the first half year with both.

## Peers (Step C)
- TCI Express (TCIEXP), https://www.screener.in/company/TCIEXP/ : B2B surface
  express, parcel loads through a branch and sorting-centre network. Same
  product (LTL parcels), opposite asset model (hired fleet). It separates the
  price cycle from the owned-fleet effect.
- Delhivery (DELHIVERY), https://www.screener.in/company/DELHIVERY/consolidated/ :
  the largest part-truckload network after its Spoton merger. It is the price
  setter VRL meets on contract PTL freight. Tests the "rational pricing" claim.
- Transport Corporation of India (TCI), https://www.screener.in/company/TCI/consolidated/ :
  freight division runs LTL and FTL with a part-owned fleet. The closest
  old-economy road transporter with listed disclosure on freight margins.
No listed pure-play asset-heavy LTL trucker exists besides VRL. Gati (Allcargo)
was the other candidate; TCI was taken for cleaner freight disclosure.
