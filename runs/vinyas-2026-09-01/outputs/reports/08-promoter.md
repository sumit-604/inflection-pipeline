**Red flag — RPT/Note 33 vs AGM Notice timing (resolves the flagged
"contradiction," but raises a different, real concern):** Nexus and UVT are
correctly ABSENT from the FY26 (year ended 31-Mar-2026) Note 33 related-party
list and from Note 19 ("The Company does not have any associate company as of
31 March 2026," p.50) — both JVs were board-approved around 5-Aug-2026, i.e.
AFTER the FY26 balance sheet date and shortly before the AGM Notice
(28-Aug-2026) that proposes forward RPT ceilings for them. This is a timing
gap, not a falsification. However: (1) the FY26 AR's "Events after the
Reporting Period" notes (Note 37(v), Note 3.5) do NOT disclose these two
material post-balance-sheet JV commitments as subsequent events, which is a
disclosure-quality gap under Ind AS 10 given their scale; (2) the AGM is
being asked, within roughly three weeks of the JVs' formation, to approve a
combined Rs 550 Cr RPT ceiling for two brand-new counterparties — a sum
exceeding the company's entire FY26 consolidated turnover (Rs 514 Cr). This
is procedurally compliant (Reg 23/Reg 30 disclosed, non-interested-party
voting carve-out stated) but is an aggressive authorization velocity worth
the operator's continued monitoring, distinct from any finding of wrongdoing.
