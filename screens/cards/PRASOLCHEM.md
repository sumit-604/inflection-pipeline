## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Prasol buys acetone and phosphorus and converts them into 150+ derivative chemicals for 1,600+ customers in 65+ countries (Q2 deck, p.13). FY26 revenue was Rs 1,232.6 crore: 43% acetone-based, 38% phosphorus-based, 19% other specialty (Q2 deck, p.17). Exports were 27%. Two plants run 98,644 MTPA: Khopoli at 78,784 and Mahad at 19,860 (Q2 deck, p.13 and p.18). Basic first-level derivatives are about 50% of revenue. Secondary and tertiary derivatives are 30% to 35% (Q1 call, p.5).

**What is changing?** Three moves. Mahad is filling: utilisation went from 13% in FY24 to 44% in FY26 and "60%-plus" in Q1 FY27 (Q1 call, p.9 and p.15). The mix is moving up the derivative chain. A Rs 250 to 300 crore Phase 1 capex adds 60,000 MTPA, to 158,844 MTPA by FY29 (Q2 deck, p.24).

**Why now?** FY26 PAT doubled to Rs 83.1 crore (Q2 deck, p.28). H1 FY27 PAT of Rs 99.0 crore already exceeds all of FY26 (Q2 deck, p.9). The IPO cleared Rs 60 crore of debt (Q1 call, p.11).

**What must be true?** Core EBITDA margin must hold at 14% or better once the Q1 price windfall is gone. Q2 FY27, the first clean quarter, printed 14.0% (Q2 deck, p.9). Phase 1 must commission on schedule and sell.

**What breaks it?** Three things. First, the revenue growth is mostly price: Q1 volume grew only "mid-single digit" (Q1 call, p.9). An acetone price fall cuts the top line. Second, the Mahad site. The operator flags a MIDC notice to vacate one Mahad plot and a safety record. No corpus document confirms either. Mahad is the growth site. Third, working capital: H1 operating cash flow was negative.

## 2. Business model and archetype

The company makes derivatives. It also distributes products it does not make. Purchases of stock-in-trade were Rs 173.1 crore in FY26, 14.0% of revenue (Q2 results, p.2). Management says distribution earns 5% to 6% EBITDA. A product moves into own manufacture, at about 15%, once the company holds more than 50% of its Indian market (Q1 call, p.19). That is the engine: distribute, learn the customer, then make.

The margin comes from the spread over acetone and phosphorus, plus purity tuned to each customer. Management claims 9 to 10 product "streams" for one lubricant product, each made to one customer's spec (Q1 call, p.8). The filings report one segment, Specialty Chemicals (Q2 results, p.3).

**Archetype: two. Commodity converter for the basic-derivative slice (about 50%; Amendment 17 binds). Build-to-spec supplier for the secondary and tertiary slice (30% to 35%).** The library has no clean label for a spec'd specialty chemical maker. The fit is imperfect, and that is a finding.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Forming.** Customer approval cycles run 1 to 4 years (Q2 deck, p.20). The deck claims a 60% to 70% share in targeted Indian acetone derivatives, a management estimate (Q2 deck, p.20). Concentration is low. The top 10 customers were Rs 292 crore in FY26, under 25% (Q2 deck, p.17 and p.20). The largest customer is Rs 55 to 60 crore (Q1 call, p.7). Pricing power is partial. The company passed rising raw-material prices through in Q2 (Q2 deck, p.5). It did not hold Q1's 37.9% gross margin. The Q2 gross margin was 30.1% (Q2 deck, p.9). Switching cost is real. Pricing power is not proven.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 to R2, a cost-advantaged converter of basic acetone and phosphorus derivatives. TO R3, a spec'd supplier of secondary and tertiary derivatives. One rung, in line with the base rate.

Management's claim: "in the coming years, the intent is to go for the primary, secondary, and tertiary derivatives because the baseline of 30-year basic chemistry has been achieved" (Q1 call, p.5).

The ROCE line supports it: 12.6%, then 15.0%, then 22.4% (Q2 deck, p.27). FY26 sits inside the R3 band of 20% to 25%. It is one year at that level.

## 8. Proof check

The guide excludes the Q1 windfall (Q1 call, p.17). H1 core EBITDA is about Rs 122.1 crore (Rs 148.0 crore less Rs 25.9 crore). H2 must deliver Rs 118 to 128 crore, which is Rs 59 to 64 crore a quarter. Q2 delivered Rs 57.7 crore. The guide needs a small step up, not a leap.

**Proof point present: Q2 FY27 core EBITDA margin of 14.0% against 10.4% a year earlier, in a clean quarter.** It is one clean quarter. The proof point absent is volume: no Q2 volume figure is held.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED, narrowly.** ROCE rose from 12.6% to 22.4% over three years. Q2 FY27 core margin is 14.0% against 10.4%. This is delivery. The R3 product triggers (antibacterial, mining, Phase 1) are not yet commercial.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, provisional.** The negative cash flow is a raw-material pre-buy in a rising market (Q1 call, p.21). The Mahad losses are shrinking. This reverts to STRUCTURAL if a vacate notice or a safety failure hits the Mahad site.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis is the Bull AI company record, not a filed document: market cap Rs 4,846.8 crore, 2026-11-04. That is about 58x FY26 PAT and about 30x the FY27 core estimate (step 13). The R3 neighbourhood is about 19x. The market already pays above the TO rung.

**Posture: EARNINGS-ONLY.** The climb is real and already priced. Returns ride EPS growth alone.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The growth is real and the multiple has paid for it. The one fact that could break the case, a Mahad vacate notice, appears nowhere in the filings.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. Does a MIDC notice to vacate a Mahad plot exist? Which plot is it: the 29.5-acre operating site or the 10 acres just bought? What is its status? Source it from MIDC, the RHP litigation section or the company.
2. The cause of the FY24 Mahad shutdown and the site's incident history. The RHP risk factors are the first place to look.
3. Promoter holding, pledge, and the names of the OFS sellers (RHP, shareholding pattern).
4. Q3 FY27 core EBITDA of Rs 59 crore or more, with volume growth stated separately from price.

**What would change the view**
- A clean answer on items 1 and 2, plus a Q3 near guide, moves this to PROCEED.
- A price fall that moves the multiple back toward the R3 neighbourhood reopens the re-rating case.
- A confirmed vacate notice on the operating Mahad plot, or a safety stoppage, makes this a PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
