43	       Sammaan Capital Marks First Full Quarter Under IHC with AUM
44	                         Gaining Momentum
45	Mumbai, August 13, 2026 — Sammaan Capital Limited, one of India's leading non-banking financial
46	companies (NBFCs), reported its financial results today for the first quarter of the year ending June 30, 2026.
47	
48	Key Highlights:
49	First quarter as part of the IHC Group, marking a transformational phase for Sammaan Capital
50	
51	   •    AUM grew to ₹ 56,239 Crore from ₹53,160 crore in Q4FY26
52	
53	   •    Disbursed ₹ 3,875 crore in Q1FY27 across five products, driving diversified growth without
54	        concentration in any single segment.
55	
56	   •    97% of the disbursements were secured, while 3% were unsecured
57	
58	   •    Gross recoveries: ₹ 424 Crores; Net recoveries after taking into account provisions and other credit
59	        costs: ₹240 Crore
60	
61	   •    Maintained a growth-focused approach within prudent risk guardrails, while actively pursuing both
62	        organic and inorganic growth opportunities
63	
64	   •    Credit ratings upgraded to AA+ (Stable) by CRISIL, CARE and ICRA; international rating upgraded to
65	        BB- (Stable) by S&P Global within 90 days of IHC's investment
66	
67	   •    Steady reduction in stock borrowing cost — ~85 bps reduction from Q2FY26 to Q1FY27, with
68	        further ~75 bps reduction expected by end of FY27
69	
70	   •    Strengthening leadership team with key senior hires across functions to support new product
71	        launches and accelerate business growth
72	
73	   •    Identified 53 AI use cases for phased implementation across FY27-28
74	
75	   •    AI-ready infrastructure initiated during the quarter — unified loan origination platform, unified HR
76	        life cycle platform with employee self-service, major database and cloud infrastructure, modern
77	        core LMS enabling automated onboarding & straight-through processing and scalable, secure cloud
78	        powering LOS, data lake & AI workloads
79	
80	   •    Strengthening cross functional integration with IHC across Risk, Finance and IT functions

99	
100	Mr. Gagan Banga, Managing Director & CEO of Sammaan Capital Limited, said:
101	"This quarter marks an important milestone for Sammaan Capital as our first quarter as part of the IHC
102	Group. With the capital infusion now in place, a strengthened balance sheet and the backing of a global
103	parent, we believe Sammaan Capital is well positioned to move decisively onto its next phase of growth. Our
104	focus remains on growth-oriented disbursals within clearly defined risk guardrails, ensuring that growth is
105	both calibrated and sustainable.
106	Technology remains at the core of our strategy. Our digital-first approach is creating a platform for all
107	individual and MSME product segments.
108	As we look ahead, our priorities are clear to accelerate growth responsibly, strengthen earnings, progressively
109	reduce our cost of funds, leverage the capabilities of our parent, deepen our technology-led distribution
110	platform and maintain disciplined risk management.”
111	
112	About Sammaan Capital Limited:
113	Sammaan Capital Limited is a large NBFC in India and a group company of Abu Dhabi-based International
114	Holding Company (IHC). With a network of 200+ branches and 8,000+ channel partners, the company offers
115	multiple financial products, combining digital innovation with on-ground reach to deliver accessible,
116	customer-first financial solutions. Focused on accessibility, speed, and customer-first service, Sammaan
117	Capital is committed to empowering individuals and businesses with practical and inclusive financial
118	solutions.
