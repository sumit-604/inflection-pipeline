### 6B. Classification

**CONCERN.**

Rationale: no deal-breaker fires (see 6C), the legal/regulatory record is clean, the auditor
relationship is clean, and the promoter's own share dealing (creeping purchases, zero pledge)
is a genuine positive. But dimension 4 — promoter/family compensation rising sharply
(CMD +29% actual, ceiling raised twice in ~12 months, plus a new Rs 48L payment to the
promoter's spouse as a Non-Executive Director with no disclosed executive function) against
+2.1% revenue growth and negative CFO in both years — is a well-evidenced, quantifiable pattern
of value extraction moving in the opposite direction from operating delivery. Promoter-family
cash compensation (CMD salary + spouse NED remuneration + spouse rent + father's salary) rose
from roughly 11-12% of PAT in FY25 to roughly 19% of PAT in FY26 [INFERENCE: computed from
AR Note 28 RPT figures and B03's reported FY25/FY26 PAT margins on FY25/FY26 revenue; not a
figure stated directly in any single filed document]. Combined with the CAUTION cluster on
disclosure/mechanism clarity and CS turnover, this crosses from a routine promoter-pay increase
into a pattern the framework's own Section 3B language ("salary rising through performance
declines") is written to catch. This is not a CAUTION-only picture; it also is not AVOID, since
there is no fraud, no pledge, no SEBI action, and no evidence of intent to defraud minorities —
the redirection of IPO proceeds and the pay revisions were both put to a shareholder vote and
passed transparently.
