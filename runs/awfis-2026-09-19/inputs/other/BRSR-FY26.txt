  c. A brief on types of customers
   Awfis Space Solutions Limited serves a broad and diversified customer base comprising start-ups, small 
and medium enterprises (SMEs), large Indian corporates, multinational corporations, and individual 
professionals. Early-stage and growth-stage start-ups engage Awfis for cost-efficient and flexible 
workspace solutions that offer scalability, shared infrastructure, and short-term commitments aligned 
with dynamic business needs. SMEs utilize Awfis’ ready-to-occupy and managed office offerings to 
support business expansion, entry into new geographies, and operational continuity without undertaking 
long-term real estate commitments or significant upfront capital expenditure.
   Large Indian corporates and multinational enterprises represent a key customer segment, leveraging 
Awfis’ customized and managed office solutions for project-based teams, satellite offices, and hybrid 
working models. Multinational corporations entering or expanding their operations in India rely on Awfis 
for rapid market entry through flexible seating arrangements, managed offices, and access to a pan-
India network of workspaces, enabling speed-to-market and operational flexibility.
   In addition, individual professionals, freelancers, consultants, and remote workers utilize Awfis’ co-
working desks, meeting rooms, and short-term workspace solutions to meet on-demand and flexible 
working requirements. 
   Overall, Awfis’ diversified customer mix ranging from individual professionals to large domestic and 
global enterprises, supports a resilient business model and enables the Company to address evolving 
workplace requirements through flexible, scalable, and customized workspace solutions across India.
