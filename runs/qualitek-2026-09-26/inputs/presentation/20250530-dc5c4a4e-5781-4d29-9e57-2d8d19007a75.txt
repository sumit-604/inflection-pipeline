  Geographic/Scope Expansion

    Bhubaneswar new lab established for mineral testing and NABL is under process.
     Baddi lab commercial operation started from Jan’25 in PPP mode.

     Inspection business.
      Successfully completed the PICS Audit for testing of Pharmaceutical products export to Ukraine.
     Approved from Spices Board of India and also commercial testing for Tea products in India.
     Accredited for testing of food & Agri-commodities export to Indonesia.
