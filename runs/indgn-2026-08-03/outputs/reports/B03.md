  transfer pricing order dated 18 March 2026... the amount of tax demand will be as per the final order
  which is not received as on date"** (PDF p.204-205 / p.165). Arithmetically, tax on the ₹1,114mn
  proposed TP adjustment at the 25.17% enacted rate is ≈₹280.4mn; a Section 144B final order carrying
  ₹436.88mn "including interest" for the identical AY is broadly consistent with that principal plus
  several years of accrued interest — suggesting these may be **the same underlying dispute at two
  different disclosure stages** (a Sec 144B assessment order crystallising tax demand, versus the TPO's
  draft order still working through MAP). The AR does not cross-reference the two disclosures to each
  other anywhere in the text. Two readings are both plausible: (a) they are the same matter and the
  Board's Report is the more concrete, more current data point (in which case the "unprovided, contingent,
  pending MAP" framing in Note 33 is materially incomplete as of the report signing date, 29 April 2026);
  or (b) they are genuinely separate income-tax matters for the same AY that happen not to be linked in
  the text. **This is flagged as an unresolved cross-reference contradiction for verifier/operator
  attention — it bears directly on how live and quantified the ₹1,114mn TP contingent liability (B02
  rank #2) really is.** 🔴
