Q2 FY27: Overview

 01   Q2 FY27 Procurement volumes increased to 1.73 MLPD (7% higher YoY) and Procurement prices increased to Rs
      47.73/L (11% higher YoY).


      Q2 FY27 Milk Sales volumes stable at 1.24 MLPD up 2.5% YoY, The average selling price increased to ₹60.34/L up
 02
      6% reflecting stable market positioning and pricing power.


      Value-Added Products (VAP) segment, achieving a 32% increase in revenues, reaching Rs. 4,510 million in
 03
      Q2FY27. Contribution of VAP to total revenue improved by 4.1% YoY to 35.4% from 31.3% in Q2FY26.


      When including consumer packs of Ghee and Butter, VAP revenue reached Rs. 5,408 million, up 31% YoY in
 05
      Q2 FY27. This segment is up 4.6% YOY to reach 42.5% to total revenue compared to 37.9% in Q2 FY26.




                                            We are pleased to report another quarter of strong execution, with Heritage Foods delivering 19% YoY revenue growth
                                            and a 98bps sequential recovery in EBITDA margin to 5.6%, despite elevated milk procurement costs. This performance
                                            reflects the resilience of our integrated dairy model, strong consumer franchise and continued traction across our VAP
                                            portfolio.
                                            Our focus remains on strengthening the procurement ecosystem, expanding VAP capacity and accelerating innovation
                                            across premium and nutrition-led categories. Broad-based volume growth, calibrated pricing and an increasing
                                            contribution from higher-value products are strengthening our business mix and positioning us for sustained profitability
                                            improvement.

                                            While near-term cost pressures persist, we remain confident in our ability to drive sustainable margin improvement
                                            through continued premiumisation, tight cost control measures, improved operating leverage and disciplined execution.
