## SECTION 3: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Permanent Magnets Ltd makes magnets, electricity-meter components, automotive
sensing parts, current transformers, gas meters, and specialty alloys (B04).
It builds to spec for OEMs in India and abroad and exports about 47% of what it
sells (B02, B09). FY26 standalone revenue was Rs 225 Cr, up 13%, and standalone
PAT was Rs 20.7 Cr, up 36% (B03, B05).

Who buys and why. The customers are electricity-meter makers, Western
automotive Tier-1s, and industrial buyers of alloys (B04, dossier V1). They buy
because PML is already qualified into their parts. Re-qualifying a new supplier
costs more than the component. That switching cost is the moat (B04, B07 B2).

Why demand exists. Meters need shunts, CTs, and flux concentrators. Cars need
magnetic, current, and torque sensing. Both are secular: India's RDSS
smart-meter rollout has installed 65 million of a 250 million target as of
Apr-2026 (B05, B09), and Western auto electrification pulls sensing content
(B04, B09). Alloys serve aerospace, oil and gas, and industrial buyers, backed
by an AS9100D certificate (B05).

Why demand grows or does not. The base grows through content-per-unit, not just
volume. Alloys rose from about 1% of sales in FY24 to about 10% in FY26 (B04,
B05). The optional legs, latching relay (guided H2 FY27) and NdFeB inside the
subsidiary Quantum Magnetics (Phase 2 guided Q3 FY27, 5,000 tonnes by FY31),
could lift content sharply if they fire (B03, B05, B07). Both are pre-revenue
and both have slipped once (B03, B07). The mature customers themselves circle a
shrinking pond: the segments PML supplies show soft order intake (dossier V1).

Where the competitive advantage sits, per line. Mature lines: real, from
qualification lock-in and a widening component basket (B04, B07 B2). Relay:
a licensed technology wedge (REL Developments, UK) against a Chinese incumbent,
unproven until an order prints (B07 H2, dossier V2). NdFeB: a China+1 policy
bet with committed capex but zero revenue and negative subsidiary net worth
(B02, B07 E2). The advantage is present in the core and only prospective in the
two options.
