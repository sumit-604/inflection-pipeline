Leadership Perspective — Message from the Managing Director, Rishab Mohan Gupta.
"Yet another record-breaking quarter!"
"Rossell Techsys has commenced FY 2026-27 on a strong note, reflecting the strength
of our business model, execution capabilities, customer trust, and employee
commitment. Revenue grew 78% YoY to Rs 154.71 crore from Rs 86.99 crore in Q1 FY26.
Profit Before Tax increased to Rs 9.60 crore, compared to Rs 4.01 crore in the
corresponding quarter last year.
During the quarter, we successfully onboarded a major global semiconductor customer
following an extensive qualification process, creating significant opportunities for
future growth. We are also witnessing strong demand visibility from key customers
across both the Aerospace & Defence (A&D) and Semiconductor sectors, with clear
production ramp-up plans. We have aligned our capacity, resources, and infrastructure
to support this anticipated growth.
Our subsidiary, RTI, achieved several key milestones, including successful
qualification audits by leading global aerospace and defence organizations, receipt
of a Letter of Intent from an international defence company, and approval to
participate in developmental projects associated with a global satellite
communications program.
Business development remained robust, with Rs 350 crore of bids submitted during the
quarter and Rs 240 crore of new purchase orders received. To support growing demand,
we have secured an additional manufacturing facility within Aerospace Park,
Bengaluru, expected to become operational during H2 FY27 - enhancing our capabilities
across Aerospace, Defence, Space, Semiconductor, and Industrial programs."
Focus areas listed: scaling revenues and profitability; expanding participation
across Aerospace, Defence, Semiconductor, Industrial; evaluating entry into
Commercial Aerospace; building capabilities in MRO, system integration, advanced
manufacturing; creating long-term value.

Company Snapshot.

  Commercial Aviation & MRO.

  Ltd in December 2024.
- Future Revenue Visibility: Rs 3,000 Cr strategic opportunities; Rs 800 Cr order book
  as on 30 June 2026.

  contribution.

  (Q1 FY27).

===== PAGE 16 =====
Key Strengths and Growth Catalysts. "This is structural growth, not a one-time
event - secular tailwinds across four high-barrier industries."
- Strategic Industry Exposure: Aerospace + Defence + Space + Semiconductor.
- Long-term Optionality: MRO + System Integration. "Phase 2 expansion creates
  excitement, scalability perception - without distracting from real scaling today."
- Expanding Value Capture: "Moving up the value chain - from harnesses to integrated
  electromechanical assemblies to subsystem integration. Margins improve at every
  step."
- Trusted Global Relationships: "Very hard for any new entrant to replicate these
  8-12 year partnerships."
- Qualification & Execution Barriers: "ITAR, AS9100D, NADCAP (40 processes).
  Multi-year qualification cycles. These are the moat - competitors cannot simply
  enter."
- Structural Growth Tailwinds: "Not cyclical. Long-term, durable demand from defence
  budgets, commercial aerospace rampup, Global semicon mission, and space
  liberalization."
- "Rossell is becoming deeply embedded - switching costs are high, qualification takes
  years." "Transitioning from component manufacturer -> integrated electromechanical
  systems platform."
