**Judging growth-induced vs structural (load-bearing fact 3):** the
evidence supports a **mixed reading, tilted toward growth-induced but not
fully explained by it.** The observation that would separate the two
readings cleanly: **the Q2/Q3 FY27 cash flow statement.** If unbilled
revenue reverts toward the ~10-12% exit-quarter-revenue ratio as the new
work gets billed and collected (i.e., a lag that closes), the build is
growth-induced and temporary. If the ratio holds or rises further while
revenue growth continues, the build is structural — a genuine lengthening

This AR, being a single fiscal year with no contract-asset rollforward, **
cannot settle this question**; only the next 1-2 quarters of cash flow
data can.
