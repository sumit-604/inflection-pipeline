## GARP REGIME NOTE (one paragraph, the dial for destination PEs)

Earnings growth is currently PLENTIFUL, not scarce: Q1 FY27 delivered the highest Nifty profit growth in ten quarters, the beat was broad-based across 19 sectors, upgrades outnumber downgrades 1.5x, and small and midcap indices grew PAT faster than large caps. Per the growth-scarcity principle, when growth is everywhere the market pays less of a premium for it, so the re-rating leg of transition theses is weaker and destination PEs should sit toward the conservative end of their bands. Compounding this, the Nifty Smallcap 250 trades 22% above its own 5-year median multiple while the Nifty 50 sits at its historical average, meaning the smallcap basket as a whole is the expensive side of the market: every within-sector "cheap" claim this quarter needs the sector-dislocation check before it is believed, and relative PE comparisons should note that the smallcap band itself is elevated.
