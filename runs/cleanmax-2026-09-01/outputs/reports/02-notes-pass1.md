## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

**🔴 Useful life extension, solar/wind assets, 25 → 30 years, mid-year, no
restatement of prior periods (Note 2, footnote (vii), AR p.400–401).**
Effective 1 Jan 2026, the Group extended the depreciable life of "new
technology" solar and wind assets from 25 to 30 years. Treated as a change
in accounting estimate under Ind AS 8, applied prospectively (not
retrospectively) per policy at AR p.389 (Note 1.3). Company-disclosed
effect on depreciation expense (net of tax) (AR p.401):
| FY26 | FY27 | FY28 | FY29 | FY30 | FY31 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹11.44 Cr (114.41M) | ₹42.54 Cr | ₹42.50 Cr | ₹43.19 Cr | ₹45.79 Cr | ₹48.66 Cr |
Net of deferred tax the effect is smaller (₹8.56 Cr FY26 rising to ₹36.4 Cr
by FY31). This change lands in the exact year the company reports its
first operating-profit crossover (per company memory, Q1 FY27) and the exact
year of listing. It is disclosed and quantified, so not concealed, but it
mechanically flatters reported earnings and ROCE going forward and should
be backed out when testing whether the "transition" is a real margin story
or partly an estimate change. 🔴 Watch/Red flag — timing coincidence with
the profitability inflection the whole investment thesis rests on.

**🟡 Impairment discount rate widened and lowered, PPE/CWIP (Note 2,
footnote (iii), AR p.400).** Value-in-use discount rate for PPE/CWIP
impairment testing: 8%–11% FY26 vs a single point 10.5% FY25. The band now
extends below the prior year's point estimate. No impairment recognised
FY25; FY26 depreciation line includes impairment of ₹0.17 Cr (17.45M) —
immaterial in quantum, but the discount-rate methodology change (point →
range, and the range dipping below the old point) is a judgment call in the
company's favour that deserves a management question.

**🟡 Goodwill impairment test, WACC 8.53% (Note 4(a), AR p.402).** Goodwill
₹22.01 Cr (220.12M), CGU = Clean Max Alpha LeaseCo FZCO. Value-in-use DCF,
WACC 8.53%. No sensitivity numbers disclosed beyond a bare assertion that
"no reasonably possible changes to key assumptions" would cause impairment.
Immaterial in size; flagged only for the boilerplate sensitivity language
repeated verbatim across three separate notes (PPE, goodwill, deferred tax
recoverability) — a pattern flag, see Pass 3.

**🟢 Revenue recognition** — point-in-time (sale of power, sale of goods)
vs over-time (projects, O&M, common infra services) split disclosed
