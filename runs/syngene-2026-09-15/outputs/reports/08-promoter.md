## SECTION 2: LEGAL & REGULATORY RECORD

### 2A. SEBI actions

No SEBI action found against Syngene International, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw,
or Biocon Limited as a company/promoter entity in the run window or in the
searchable historical record. Two historical Biocon-linked SEBI orders
predate this window and involve **third parties trading in Biocon shares**,
not Biocon's promoters:
- Allegro Capital / Kunal Ashok Kashyap, SEBI order 08-Jul-2021: insider
  trading in Biocon shares around the Jan-2018 Sandoz collaboration
  announcement; Rs 10 lakh fine each plus disgorgement of Rs 24.68 lakh,
  one-year market ban. Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw publicly called the order
  factually flawed ("Agatha Christie fiction"). MEDIA REPORTED (Business
  Standard, 09-Jul-2021 and 10-Jul-2021).
- Six-entity price-manipulation case in Biocon scrip, SEBI order
  May-2021: Rs 10 lakh fine each, two-year market ban, unrelated third
  parties. MEDIA REPORTED (Moneylife).
- A Biocon employee (Vandana Singh) fined Rs 3 lakh for insider trading,
  Oct-2020 SEBI order. VERIFIED (SEBI order page).
None of these three name Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, Syngene, or Biocon itself as
a penalised party; they concern third-party or single-employee trading in
Biocon shares. No consent order, adjudication order, or SAT appeal
involving Syngene itself or its promoter group was found. **Clean record
at the Syngene entity level.**

### 2B. Criminal cases

**2022 Bioinnovat Research bribery case** (MEDIA REPORTED, Business
Standard 06-Jul-2022; Tribune, ThePrint, Scroll): CBI arrested five
persons including a Joint Drugs Controller, a CDSCO official, and the
director of Bioinnovat Research Services, alleging bribes to waive a
Phase-III trial requirement for Biocon's Insulin Aspart injection.
Bioinnovat is reported to have handled regulatory-affairs consulting for
the Biocon group. Syngene International's own response was to terminate
its retainer/advisory contract with Bioinnovat immediately upon the
allegations surfacing (Business Standard headline: "Biocon arm Syngene
ends contract with Bioinnovat Research over graft case"). Biocon Biologics
denied the bribery allegations. No named Syngene director or KMP was
charged; the case centred on Biocon Biologics and Bioinnovat personnel,
and Syngene's contract-termination response is a mitigating fact, not an
adverse finding against Syngene's own conduct. Charge sheet filed by CBI
by Aug-2022 (MEDIA REPORTED). No update located in this run on case
disposition; carried as an unresolved historical item, PENDING LIVE
VERIFICATION for current case status.

No PMLA, ED, SFIO, or economic-offence action against Syngene, Biocon, or

## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. RPT history

Land is leased from Biocon since 1-Oct-2006 (AR26 Note 26(iii)). FY26
standalone RPT flows with the holding company: rent Rs 99mn (Rs 88mn
FY25), power and facility charges Rs 163mn (Rs 183mn FY25), other
expenses reimbursed to the company by Biocon Rs 266mn (Rs 281mn FY25),
sale of services to Biocon Rs 9mn (Rs 17mn FY25) (AR26 Note 26, p.263).
These are small against Rs 34,907mn standalone revenue and moved modestly
in Syngene's favour year on year. VERIFIED, no royalty or management-fee
line disclosed. No loans to group entities disclosed in this note. No
guarantee for a private promoter entity disclosed.

**"Other related parties" sale of services jumped 155%**, Rs 820mn to
Rs 2,091mn (6.1% of standalone revenue), with the matching receivable
building from Rs 5mn to Rs 314mn (AR26 Note 26, p.264; B02 rank-9
finding). This category is populated by entities where "a director of
the Company is a member of board of directors" — Bicara Therapeutics
Inc., Abergy Therapeutics Inc. (w.e.f. 04-Nov-2025), Third Arc Bio (w.e.f.
01-Jul-2025), Therapoma Inc (w.e.f. 01-Jul-2025), plus longer-standing
entries Narayana Hrudayalaya Limited, Immuneel Therapeutics Private
Limited, Puretech Health USA, Cage Therapeutics, Thomas Cook (India)
Limited, SOTC Travel Limited (AR26 Note 26, p.262-263). One entry,
**Jeeves**, is disclosed as "Enterprise in which relative to a director
of the Company is proprietor" (AR26 Note 26, p.262) — a related enterprise
owned by a director's relative, not further named or quantified in the
transaction table by counterparty. Neither the AR nor the two Reg 30
filings in this corpus break the Rs 2,091mn figure down by counterparty,
so it is not possible from filed documents to attribute how much of the
155% jump ties to the newly added director-linked biotech entities versus
Narayana Hrudayalaya/Thomas Cook/Jeeves. This is the single largest
unresolved RPT item in the corpus (B02/B03 concur) and sits squarely in
Section 3A's "non-arm's-length" test ground, without evidence either way
on pricing.

No royalty payments, no promoter warrants, no non-arm's-length asset
sale disclosed.

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour

Dividend flat at Rs 1.25/share for FY26 (same as FY25) despite standalone
PAT falling ~35% (B02 rank-14), implying a materially higher payout ratio
in a down year — a signal worth watching but not itself an abuse; no
promoter-directed buyback or warrant issue found. Executive Chairperson
remuneration (Rs 4 cr from Syngene, Rs 25 cr combined with Biocon) was
newly created in the same year PAT fell 35% and guidance was missed —
timing that a minority shareholder can reasonably read as salary rising
while performance declined, addressed directly in the vote outcome below
(3D). No preferential allotment to promoters found in this run's window;
the equity issuance in FY26 was to the Syngene Employee Welfare Trust for
the PSU/ESOP plans, not promoter-directed.

### 3C. Promoter share transactions

**Stake trend**: Promoters 54.72% (Jun-2024) -> 52.68% (through most of
FY26) -> 52.59% (Jun-2026), per screener SECONDARY table. Biocon's
multi-year Syngene monetisation pattern (MEDIA REPORTED, several Business
Standard articles): 10% pre-listing placement to Silver Leaf Oak
(2014-15), 2% at Rs 2.3bn (May-2018), 5.4% for Rs 1,220cr (Sep-2022),
9.96% via open market (Feb-2023), 2% at ~Rs 825-857/share for Rs
660-686cr (Dec-2024). This is a steady, multi-year, disclosed monetisation
programme by the holding company, not a single suspicious pre-announcement
sale; no sale in this run's evidence window is timed immediately ahead of
a disclosed adverse event. **No pledge or encumbrance**: Biocon filed a
formal SEBI SAST Regulation 31(4) declaration (02-Apr-2026, MEDIA
REPORTED/regulatory filing) confirming zero encumbrance on its Syngene
holding for FY26. The four scanned SAST filings in the corpus (10-Apr-2026,
17-Feb-2026, 15-Apr-2026, 22-Jul-2026) carry no extractable text and could
not be independently cross-checked from the corpus; this is an input gap,
not a finding of pledge.

### 3D. Minority treatment

**The decisive finding in this section**: the postal ballot on Kiran
Mazumdar-Shaw's move to Executive Chairperson and Rs 4cr remuneration
passed 91.4% in favour overall, but **Public-Institutions voted 21.57%
against** (30,204,762 of 140,045,579 institutional votes polled) and
Public-Non-Institutions voted 7.65% against (Postal Ballot Outcome,
19-Jun-2026, Annexure A). Promoter and Promoter Group votes (99.48% of
their holding polled) were 100% in favour, as expected on a resolution in
which the promoter is the interested party. Stripped of the promoter block
(which represented 52.68% of votes cast and voted unanimously for its own
compensation resolution), roughly one in five institutional votes and
nearly one in thirteen non-institutional public votes opposed the
appointment and pay package — a meaningful, quantified minority dissent
signal on a resolution where "promoter/promoter group are interested in
the agenda" is explicitly disclosed (Annexure A). This is not disqualifying
(the resolution needed, and got, only a simple majority of votes cast),
but it is the clearest documented minority-shareholder pushback in the
corpus and belongs in the verdict.

No SCORES complaint count disclosed. No known proxy-advisory (IiAS/SES/
InGovern) recommendation against this resolution was located in this
search pass; web search found only the vote outcome, not proxy-firm
commentary, so this sub-item is PENDING LIVE VERIFICATION. No unfair
delisting attempt found. RPTs (Note 26) are disclosed as arm's length in
the Board's Report without an audit-committee-approval breakdown by
transaction visible in this corpus.

### 3E. Auditor relationship

B S R & Co. LLP completed its second and final permitted term (10 years,

### 5C. Key person risk and succession visibility

Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, at 73, has now taken on an executive (not just
chair) role for a 5-year term to 2031, concentrating operating authority
in the founder at an age when most governance codes flag succession risk.
The stated rationale (Postal Ballot Notice, Explanatory Statement) is that
her external roles are "strategic and governance-focused" rather than
operational, and that she will "devote adequate time and attention" —
an assurance from the interested party's own board, not independently
tested. Succession below her (Siddharth Mittal as MD & CEO) is a known,
credentialed internal candidate from the wider Biocon group, which
reduces near-term key-person risk on day-to-day operations even as it
raises the independence question addressed in 6A/6D.
