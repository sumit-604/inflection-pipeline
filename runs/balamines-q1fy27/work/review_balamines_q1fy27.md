### 2C. Mandatory diagnostic answers

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes — standalone +32.4%, consolidated +27.2%. No Notion base case exists (new coverage), so no guidance comparison is possible; recorded as ND. But see the **volume/realization split** below — the growth is NOT volume-led.

2. **Did op EBITDA margin expand?** Yes, materially. Standalone op EBITDA margin **17.68% (Q1FY26) → 24.47% (Q1FY27) = +679 bps**; consolidated **15.26% → 25.41% = +1,015 bps**. This is genuine YoY expansion, not a sequential artefact.

3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY?** Yes, and it grew FASTER than reported PAT — standalone core PBT +103.3% vs reported PAT +81.3%; consol core PBT +151.7% vs owners PAT +97.2%. **The headline growth is real operating growth, not treasury-driven.** In fact other income DECLINED YoY in both books (standalone −15.1%, consol −38.7%), so the reported growth is if anything understated by the OI drag. On the other-income axis this is high-quality earnings.

4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT growth and reported-PAT growth?** The gap runs the "wrong" way for once: core PBT grew faster than PAT because (a) other income fell (a drag on reported PBT), and (b) the effective tax rate rose (standalone 23.96% → 26.25%; consol 25.48% → 26.45%), taking a larger slice. Each delta is quantified in the Step 4 bridge. There are no exceptional items in any period.

5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** No. Depreciation is essentially flat YoY (standalone +4.4%, consol −1.4%) despite the DME plant commissioning — see the capex-absorption flag below. Finance costs are immaterial (standalone 0.27 Cr; consol 1.41 Cr, <1.5% of EBITDA). There is no absorption-deficit signal in D&A **yet**, but the flat depreciation alongside a newly commissioned 100,000 TPA DME plant is itself a question (Step 8.5 Q4): either DME capex has not yet been capitalised into depreciation (CWIP not fully transferred), or its asset base is small relative to the existing block. This matters because future quarters will carry incremental DME depreciation that this quarter's margin does not.

6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Falling, not rising. OI/PBT compressed standalone 13.9% → 6.3% and consol 18.4% → 5.2%. No treasury-timing distortion to strip out; the trajectory reads cleaner without it.
