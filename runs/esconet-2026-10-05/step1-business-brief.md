# Step 1 business brief: Esconet Technologies Ltd (NSE SME: ESCONET)

Written 2026-10-05 by /step1. COMPANY MEMORY only: weighed by stages, never anchored evidence. Every stage number comes from this run's filings.

Identity: Esconet Technologies Ltd, NSE Emerge (SME), symbol ESCONET, ISIN INE0RQZ01017. Screener: https://www.screener.in/company/ESCONET/consolidated/ (HTTP 200; CMP Rs 247, market cap Rs 326 Cr on 2026-10-05). IPO prospectus dated 20 Feb 2024, fresh issue Rs 28.22 Cr (inputs/prospectus/EsconetTechnologies_PROSP.pdf p1).

1. What it sells. A Delhi IT infrastructure system integrator: servers, storage, HPC and NVIDIA GPU systems, plus integration services, for government research bodies, PSUs and enterprises. Own server brand HexaData (rack, GPU, liquid-cooled) assembled in India. Source: https://www.esc.co.in/server ; Q1 FY27 transcript (inputs/concalls/Concall_Aug_2026_Transcript.pdf).
2. Structure. Four layers per management: HexaData hardware, ZeaCloud sovereign cloud (subsidiary ZSPL), Fluidech cybersecurity, Esconet systems integration. Esconet Singapore Pte contributed about Rs 50-53 Cr of FY26 revenue. Cloud is "very small" of revenue. Sources: CRISIL rationale 2026-07-02 (inputs/rating/); Q1 FY27 transcript.
3. Scale and margin. FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 354 Cr, up about 50-53%; PAT Rs 6.2 Cr, down about 23%. CRISIL operating margin 2.60% FY26 vs 5.09% FY25, blamed on AI-driven hardware price inflation. Sources: screener Data Sheet; https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/esconet-fy26-revenue-surges-53-to-357-84-crore-pat-falls-23/41580622 ; CRISIL rationale.
4. Latest print. Q1 FY27 (first voluntary quarterly print): revenue Rs 116.3 Cr, EBITDA margin 8.55%, PAT about Rs 6.5 Cr, roughly all of FY26's PAT in one quarter. Debtor days quoted 66.8 to 20.2. Sources: Q1 FY27 transcript; https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ESCONET_12082026174006_Outcome_BM_12082026.pdf
5. Cash. FY26 CFO negative (screener Data Sheet: -8.83 Cr); inventory rose from Rs 18.9 Cr to Rs 51.5 Cr; borrowings Rs 0.5 Cr to Rs 13.2 Cr. CRISIL BBB-/Stable, upgrade trigger operating margin above 6%, downgrade below 3-4%.
6. Guidance. FY27 revenue Rs 370-400 Cr (CRISIL rationale, management expectation). Management says the hardware price trend runs through FY27 and possibly FY28 (Q1 FY27 transcript).
7. Recognition events. NVIDIA Preferred Partner elevation (NSE filing 2026-04-29). Order wins filed Sep-Oct 2025 and Jul 2026; Rs 39.78 Cr order cited on the web.
8. Governance items. Income Tax summons under s.131(1A), Feb 2026, arising from a search of the IPO lead manager Corporate Capital Ventures; it asks about the IPO process, pre-IPO allotments and use of IPO proceeds; the company states it is not accused (NSE filing 2026-02-28). NSE flagged PDF vs XBRL discrepancies in FY26 results (EPS), re-filed July 2026. 2,13,600 preferential warrants at Rs 345 lapsed in April 2026; Rs 1.84 Cr forfeited (NSE filing 2026-04-29).

THE ENGINE the thesis would rest on: a mix shift from low-margin traded hardware (2-3% operating margin) to own-brand HexaData AI/HPC systems plus cloud and cybersecurity, visible as a margin step-up toward or above CRISIL's 6% trigger. Q1 FY27 at 8.55% is the first print that claims it.
