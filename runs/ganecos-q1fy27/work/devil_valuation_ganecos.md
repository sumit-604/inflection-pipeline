## 3. REGULATORY MOAT — does EPR lift ROCE, or only volume? (Test 3)

**The distinction that decides it:** EPR / mandated minimum-recycled-content is a large, real **TAM and volume tailwind**. The question the framework forces is whether it is a **returns-on-capital lever** (which would justify a higher Pillar 1 ROCE or a Pillar 3 premium) or a **demand lever** (which does not lift returns).

Evidence points to demand/volume, not returns:
- Recycling is **capital-heavy**: serving the mandate requires capacity — Warangal Rs 192.58 Cr CWIP, the 22,500 TPA brownfield "underway", the 42,000 TPA granules ramp. More mandated demand pulls in more capital. If every recycler expands into the same mandate, margins are competed toward the commodity level and **ROCE stays near the ~9% ceiling** — the classic commodity-with-tailwind trap.
- Q1 FY27 **volume fell 11.2%** despite the EPR backdrop (review §Step 2) — the mandate is not yet producing pricing power or volume growth for GANECOS.
- The margin gain was **price/mix on falling volume**, attributed to polymer prices — no documented food-grade premium capture. Management questions Q11/Q14 (is the margin structural?) are UNANSWERED.
- A regulatory mandate is **not a contracted revenue stream to GANECOS** — it fails the Pillar 3c duration test (no order book, no LoA, no annuity tenor).

**Where the bull has a real point:** food-grade approval is a **scarcity / barrier-to-entry** — GANECOS has approvals and 40 brands in the approval pipeline. IF that scarcity converts to **documented pricing power**, it is a genuine Pillar 3b **moat-formation** case. But 3b is EM-gated and **no EM scan was ever run** — the +0 is an un-run input, not a finding. This is the honest gap in the AVOID: the regulatory moat has never been formally assessed. Quantified: 3b at max +4x → additive raw 16.4x → Bull FV Rs 721, still 37% below CMP.

**Conclusion on the regulatory moat:** EPR is a demand/TAM tailwind that (on today's evidence) does **not** lift returns on capital above the ~9% ceiling and does **not** earn an additional Pillar 3 premium beyond the +2x already credited. Its only path to lifting the valuation is via a future 📄-evidenced pricing-power / EM-scan result — a watchlist condition, not a present credit.
