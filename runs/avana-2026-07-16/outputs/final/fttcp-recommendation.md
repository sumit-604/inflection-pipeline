## THE DEVIL'S LIVE CHALLENGE (surfaced, not buried)

The devil's advocate rates valuation safety DESTROYED and the thesis WEAKENED BUT ALIVE. These counters challenge the operator's two overrides directly and belong in front of the decision:

1. The 25% hurdle clears only on the forward price to earnings method. The 20.9x destination on FY30 forward EPS decomposes to a 27.2x trailing multiple at the FY29 exit, above the 25x absolute sector cap, meaning the base case assumes no multiple compression over a three year hold. On honest trailing math (20.9x on FY29 EPS Rs 11.40 plus cash) fair value is about Rs 250 and the return is 19.6%, which matches the trailing exit Hurdle Ratio of 1.63, a fail. Verifier C's counter point stands alongside: the forward Hurdle Ratio 2.12 is internally algebraically consistent, not a raw arithmetic error of the SFL kind, so the disagreement is about the no compression ECONOMIC assumption, not a math bug.

2. The operating ROCE 40% base (Override 1, 26.1x) rests on stripping about Rs 22 Cr of cash that the RHP earmarks for working capital (about Rs 8.6 Cr) plus KIADB capex, so it may not be surplus. Put the WC tranche back and ROCE is about 35.8% (base PE about 24.8x); deploy the full raise into capital employed and headline ROCE is about 27.8% (base PE about 21.4x). The cash is arguably triple claimed: stripped from the ROCE denominator as non operating, added back at about Rs 12 a share as returnable surplus, and relied on to fund KIADB as growth capital. A modest, defensible haircut sinks the base below 25% CAGR.

3. The shared catalyst is a circular single point of failure. The cash strip that produces 40% ROCE is only valid if KIADB proceeds; a KIADB slip leaves the cash idle AND collapses the ROCE base AND reverts revenue toward the SOM 15% path at once, so Pillar 1 and Pillar 3 share a failure mode, not just a catalyst. The 22 May 2026 deadline has already passed unconfirmed after a roughly six year slip.

The devil's recommended action is to re anchor entry below about Rs 120 to Rs 130 (govern off the Track 1 RRM floor, hard gate KIADB on a primary filing, resolve INDETERMINATE cash to 1.0x before crediting any destination above about 17x). Present this as the devil's view alongside Role 1's Rs 145 to Rs 165, and let the decision stay the operator's.

OPERATOR RESOLUTION (2026-07-17, Override 3): the sector cap binds the FORWARD destination PE, not the implied trailing exit multiple. "forward earnings should be kept by the sector cap." The 20.9x forward destination is within the 25x cap, so counter 1's cap-breach claim is answered and the valuation stands at target Rs 322, entry Rs 145 to 165, unchanged.
