# SECTION E — PLAIN-LANGUAGE BRIEF (mandatory, house style)

## Summary narrative

DEE makes big industrial pipes and steel fabrications for power, oil and gas, and chemical plants. This quarter revenue rose 31.6 percent from a year ago to ₹294 crore on the group numbers. Profit rose 22.4 percent to ₹16 crore. The parent company alone grew faster, up 47 percent, because the subsidiaries that hold the heavy fabrication and biomass businesses slowed down. Operating margin was 16.9 percent. Management guides to above 19 percent for the year, so this quarter is about 2 points short. The company says about ₹25 crore of sales was ready to ship but got held back by Middle East disruption and customer issues, and should land next quarter.

Two things did not improve. The heavy fabrication business grew only 3.7 percent, and its profit fell 7.2 percent. The thesis says if that business grows under 20 percent for two quarters in a row, a warning bell rings. This is the first of those two quarters. The other issue is the audit. The auditor again refused to sign off cleanly on the group accounts because of a small power plant, Malwa Power, worth ₹50.8 crore, whose value they cannot confirm. The filing now uses new words about that plant, calling its future uncertain and mentioning strategic alternatives. That reads like a possible write down or sale later.

That leg added ₹5.56 crore of extra profit, essentially all of the gap between the group and the parent. Source: this quarter's filing (line 497). The model drift to watch is that the second leg is slowing while the first leg carries the quarter.
