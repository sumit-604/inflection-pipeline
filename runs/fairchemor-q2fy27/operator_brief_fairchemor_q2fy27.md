## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Fairchem turns waste from edible-oil refining (acid oil, deodoriser distillate) into fatty acids, dimer acid, isostearic acid and tocopherols. It has one plant and sells almost all of it in India.
2. Q2 sales were Rs 166.92 Cr, 49.7% more than a year ago (res R013 R014). The year-ago quarter was the low point of a three-year margin slide, so the base was weak.
3. Operating margin was 10.51%, against 3.76% a year ago. Net profit was Rs 10.17 Cr, against Rs 0.77 Cr. The gain came from operations, not from one-offs.
4. The margin is back near the FY24 peak of 10.78%, not above it (deck). The climb is a recovery, not yet a new level.
5. Sales fell 5.2% from Q1. The call gave tonnage for the first time: about 12,300 t sold in Q2 against about 13,500 t in Q1 [ASR]. Volume fell about 8.9%; price per kg rose about 4.0%.
6. Q1 sold a lot of old stock and Q2 sold much less (res L234). So Q2 is the honest run-rate, and little stock is left to sell.
7. H1 cash from operations was Rs 24.05 Cr, but Rs 22.31 Cr of it came from cutting inventory. Without that cut, free cash flow was about minus Rs 2.78 Cr. Cash conversion is INDETERMINATE.
8. The FY26 buyback (Rs 34 Cr at Rs 800) was funded with bank debt. In H2 FY26, liabilities rose Rs 33.60 Cr while equity fell Rs 31.00 Cr (res L374-376, L507-510). The dividend fell from Rs 7.5 to Rs 1.0 per share.
9. The Board approved buying two land parcels next to the plant from the CMD and his relatives for Rs 12.01 Cr (res L582-584). No valuer is named. The call says the company already uses this land for operations and a worker colony [ASR] (call L205). The terms of that earlier use are NOT FOUND.
10. The call was cautious. Management would not give a usable capacity or utilisation figure, and the two figures it gave conflict [ASR]. The new product slipped to Q3. US exports are stuck on freight and European cosmetic demand is down.
11. The only margin promise is to "maintain" today's margin, if conditions hold. Management "would love to see" 15-18% but gave no year [ASR]. A possible bear point: Chinese rivals may have cheaper feedstock (call L146-149, speaker unclear).
12. Watch next: Q3 FY27 gross margin with the inventory line, results due by 14-Feb-2027. Bull: gross margin 22.7% or more, sales Rs 166.92 Cr or more, no stock drawdown. Bear: gross margin below 20.3%.
