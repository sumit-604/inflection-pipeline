## FLAG-CASH — determination: INDETERMINATE

Cash conversion is deteriorating and the record cannot yet separate a growth artifact from a structural feature of the model.

Cited evidence:
- Operating cash flow negative in 3 of 4 years: FY23 -15.62, FY24 -82.75, FY25 +19.70, FY26 -51.47 (B01 Block B). FY26 free cash flow -158.58, the most negative of the four year history, against FY26 PAT 138.08 (B01). Cumulative CFO/PAT -0.44 (B01 Block B, B1).
- Standalone CFO/PAT 22.7% in FY25 (CFO Rs 19.85 cr vs PAT Rs 87.48 cr), following negative CFO in FY24 despite positive PAT both years, driven by a Rs 45 cr plus other current assets build on top of receivables growth (B03 FLAG-CASH).
- Capitalised development cost about Rs 107 cr in FY26 (H1 Rs 30 cr, H2 Rs 56 cr per one answer; Rs 107 cr per another, unreconciled in the same call), an earnings quality and free cash flow drain not surfaced as a red flag upstream (B12b MAJOR; B07 FLAG-EMOAT).
- Rs 40 cr to 50 cr short term borrowing taken while holding about Rs 440 cr cash (B12b MINOR; B05).
- Incentive and cashback funding moved to about 68% of program fees, the wrong direction versus the stated 50% in 5 years glide path (B05 red_flags). Propel gross margin fell to 4% in Q4 FY26 versus a 7% to 9% recovery promised in Q2 FY25; recovery target itself lowered to about 5.5% (B05 promise_delivery). This is the redemption model economics moving against the cash thesis, not a one time item.
- Receivables composition is contested inside the record and unresolved: standalone trade receivables grew from Rs 40 cr to Rs 129 cr YoY (B07 top_moat_risks), while B02 reports net receivables up 23.1% against 68% revenue growth with turnover improving 5.59x to 6.69x and about 84% of the receivables book concentrated in customers each above 5% of the balance (B02 rank 6, receivables_trend). No ageing schedule is available to adjudicate collectibility.
- The party that could resolve the direction declined to: the question of when operating cash flow turns sustainably positive was deflected in each of Q2, Q3 and Q4 FY26 (B05 repeated_evasions).

Rating agency input: CARE assigned A- (Stable) obtained 16 July 2025 (B08 transition_evidence, AR p.75, 178), following an ACUITE upgrade from BBB (Stable) to BBB+ (Positive). The CARE A- rating rationale verbatim liquidity commentary is NOT FOUND in the provided sources; it is not fabricated here and is named as a gap below.

Why INDETERMINATE and not GROWTH-INDUCED: the classic growth signatures are present (revenue and PAT CAGR 51% and 82%, net cash balance sheet, capitalised R&D and M&A absorbing cash). Against them sit structural markers (incentive ratio drifting the wrong way, Propel take rate collapsing, short term borrowing despite a war chest) and three pieces of missing evidence that the resolving determination depends on. The record cannot clear it either way.

Missing evidence that would settle the determination:
1. Trade receivables ageing schedule split by bank partner settlement, to test whether the receivables build is collectible growth lag or deteriorating quality (next AR Note 13 or quarterly disclosure).
2. The CARE A- rating rationale verbatim liquidity and working capital commentary (CARE Ratings rationale document).
3. A consolidated operating cash flow bridge that quantifies the Propel redemption model change and separates the capitalised development cost, and that reconciles the Rs 107 cr versus Rs 86 cr figure (quarterly cash flow statements and concall).

Because this is INDETERMINATE, the verdict caps at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. No falsifying quarterly metric is attached in place of a determination; the missing evidence above is named instead.

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated)

1. Watch consolidated operating cash flow toward the FY27 positive target in quarterly and annual cash flow statements; the number that matters is OCF crossing zero. This tests whether the P&L growth ever becomes cash, the core cash flag question.
2. Watch CFO/PAT sustaining above 0.7x in the quarterly cash flow statement; below that, the FY25 22.7% conversion reads as structural rather than a one off working capital build.
3. Watch the incentive and cashback ratio as a share of program fees, disclosed in concall; falling toward 50% supports the monetisation thesis, holding near 68% or rising erodes it.
4. Watch Propel net take rate and gross margin off the 4% Q4 FY26 low toward the lowered 5.5% target; a further fall confirms redemption model deterioration.
5. Watch net revenue growth excluding Propel pass through in quarterly results; healthy is at or above 25%, red flag below 15%, and this is the real scale metric, not gross revenue.
6. Watch standalone trade receivables growth, turnover and, when disclosed, ageing in the annual report notes; receivables outrunning revenue with no ageing detail keeps the cash flag INDETERMINATE.
7. Watch capitalised development cost per year and its reconciliation in the cash flow statement; about Rs 107 cr in FY26, unreconciled against Rs 86 cr in the same call, this masks true free cash flow.
8. Watch execution and credibility catalysts in concall and filings: FY27 EBITDA guidance reissuance after DICE integration, a named OMC fleet contract, and US or MENA operations going live; repeated slippage tests the grade C delivery record.
