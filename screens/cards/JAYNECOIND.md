## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** JNIL runs a 1 MnTPA integrated steel plant at Siltara, Raipur, fed by two captive iron ore mines with about 7 MnTPA of capacity (Annual_Report_2026, p.54). It sells alloy steel bars, wire rods and bright bars, mainly to auto component makers. It also sells merchant pig iron, sponge iron, pellets and iron ore, and runs foundries. In FY26, billets and rolled products earned Rs 4,554 crore of Rs 7,132 crore revenue (Annual_Report_2026, p.130). The money is the spread between steel prices and a cost base that owns its ore.

**What is changing?** The blast furnace went from 0.75 to 1.00 MnTPA after an 84 day rebuild in FY25 (Annual_Report_2026, p.8, p.119). The main mine went from 2.95 to 6.00 MnTPA (p.55). The 17.5% debt was refinanced at 12.5% in December 2025 (p.56). FY26 EBITDA rose 40.8% to Rs 1,341 crore and PAT rose from Rs 113 crore to Rs 463 crore (p.59).

**Why now?** The first two quarters of FY27 printed the full effect. Q2 FY27 PAT was Rs 229.81 crore against Rs 105.13 crore a year earlier. Finance cost fell from Rs 112.32 crore to Rs 61.57 crore (filings/…Results30092026.pdf, p.4). The promoters also bought Rs 200 crore of warrants at Rs 89.13 in October 2026 (filings/…REG30_01102026_ALLOTMENT_OF_SHARES.pdf, p.1).

**What must be true?** Two things. EBITDA per tonne must hold near the Rs 6,000 to 6,500 band through a steel or coking coal turn. The legal cases on the Gare Palma IV/4 coal block must not reach the plant, the promoters' pledged shares or the MD's seat.

**What breaks it?** A steel price fall with coking coal up. The company is a price taker on the product that the "value added" story rests on. Rolled product realisation fell in FY26 while commodity prices rose (step 6).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One economic engine: captive ore and a now full blast furnace, turned into alloy long products at low cost. India Ratings puts EBITDA per tonne at Rs 6,489 in 9M FY26 against Rs 5,242 in FY25 (credit-ratings/01-IndiaRatings-2026-02-05.txt, p.1). Steel segment result was Rs 1,042.62 crore in FY26 on Rs 6,643 crore of segment revenue (filings/…OutcomeandResults24042026.pdf, p.14).

FY26 sales: rolled product 6,87,864 t against 5,15,429 t; pig iron 1,46,004 t against 80,451 t; pellets 8,53,347 t against 9,09,980 t (filings/…Presssrelease30062026.pdf, p.36).

**Archetype: Commodity converter.** Amendment 17 binds. A build-to-spec slice exists: JNIL is an approved Tier 2 supplier to auto component makers and its foundries are Tier 1 to OEMs (Presssrelease30062026, p.6, p.7). CARE said more than 50% of sales went to the auto sector (credit-ratings/05-CARE.pdf, p.1).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Present: cost.** Ore is captive and allocated, so JNIL pays no auction premium (filings/…Credit_Rating_Disclosure_Reg_30_210802025.pdf, p.3). Leases run to 2052 and 2055 (01-IndiaRatings, p.1). Captive power covers about 60% of need (same page). The MD's own claim is cost: "one of the lowest-cost integrated alloy steel manufacturers in the country" (Annual_Report_2026, p.10).

**Forming at best: switching cost.** OEM approvals are slow to win, and the top 10 customers are only 30% to 35% of revenue (01-IndiaRatings, p.1). But cost passes through only with a lag (same page), and CARE calls steel producers "essentially price-takers" (05-CARE.pdf, p.2). **Absent: pricing power.** Castings, the other value-added line, earned a Rs 10.56 crore segment result on Rs 520 crore of FY26 revenue, about 2% (OutcomeandResults24042026, p.14).

## 4. The promoters

Promoter and group hold 55.15%: 4.60% as individuals and 50.55% through 18 bodies corporate (Annual_Report_2026, p.75). **53,48,04,690 shares are pledged** to the NCD holders (p.126) out of 53,55,19,366 held (p.75). That is 99.87%. Arvind, Ramesh and Avneesh Jayaswal also give personal guarantees (p.126). India Ratings records that 50% of the pledge releases after 50% repayment (Credit_Rating_Disclosure_210802025, p.3).

Skin in the game rose. Vibrant Enterprises, a family partnership, took 2,24,39,134 warrants at Rs 89.13 for Rs 200 crore and paid Rs 50 crore up front (ALLOTMENT_OF_SHARES, p.1).

Pay is rising. The Chairman and the MD each drew Rs 3.32 crore in FY26 (Annual_Report_2026, p.136). From January 2026 each gets Rs 31.67 lakh a month, rising 20% a year, plus commission up to 3% of net profits (filings/…PostalBallotNotice28102025.pdf, p.3, p.4).

Related parties: group firm Neco Mining was paid Rs 290.67 crore for overburden removal in FY26, up from Rs 229.17 crore (p.136). That is 21.7% of FY26 EBITDA. NSSL bought Rs 28.45 crore and sold Rs 33.47 crore (p.136). A lender-appointed agency vets RPT contracts (01-IndiaRatings, p.1).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

Management's claim, quoted once: "highest-ever alloy steel sales of 7,23,744 MT" (Annual_Report_2026, p.54). The claim is volume, not price.

**FROM R1, TO R2: delivered.** Before 2022 this was a distressed price taker, referred to the NCLT in 2018 (01-IndiaRatings, p.1). Captive ore, a full furnace and cheaper debt now give a cost-advantaged converter with 18% to 20% EBITDA margin. That is R2.

**R3 claim: not evidenced.** India Ratings says value-added special steel is "around 70% of revenue" (01-IndiaRatings, p.1). The filed mix says rolled and billets were 63.9% of FY26 revenue against 62.9% in FY25, and castings fell from 9.7% to 7.3% (Annual_Report_2026, p.130). Commodity lines rose.

Price says the same. Billets and rolled revenue divided by steel sales gives about Rs 62,900 a tonne in FY26 against about Rs 67,400 in FY25, a 6.6% fall (p.130; Presssrelease30062026, p.36). Pellets rose about 3% a tonne and pig iron about 4% on the same arithmetic. The MD&A confirms it: selling prices rose "of all products except Rolled product" (Annual_Report_2026, p.56). The next project, a Rs 720 crore pellet plant, monetises ore as merchant pellets (Presssrelease30062026, p.46). That points toward commodity, not up the ladder.

Two readings. R3: OEM approvals, more than 50% auto sales and record rolled volume build spec-in share. R2: the furnace is full, so volume grew, and price fell with the market. One observation separates them: rolled realisation against a market benchmark through a steel downturn. The corpus does not disclose it.

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim volume, cost and interest. All three show. Revenue rose 27.7% in Q1 FY27 and 23.2% in Q2 FY27 year on year. EBITDA margin held at 19.3% and 20.4%. PAT more than doubled in both quarters (Presssrelease30062026, p.40; Results30092026, p.4). Q1 FY27 sold 1,81,419 t of rolled product, up 11%, and 57,452 t of pig iron, up 93% (Presssrelease30062026, p.43).

**The gap:** Q2 FY27 volumes are NOT FOUND because the press release is unreadable. No quarter shows rolled realisation. The proof fires for the recovery, not for the climb.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **MD convicted in a non-group company case.** A Special CBI Court convicted Ramesh Jayaswal in the Abhijeet Infrastructure matter; the sentence is suspended and the Delhi High Court admitted his appeal (Annual_Report_2026, p.143; auditor's report p.106). Flag, load-bearing.
- **Coal block trial.** The company and the MD face cheating and conspiracy charges over the Gare Palma IV/4 block, under trial (p.119).
- **Emphasis of matter on Rs 307.58 crore of ED attachments** at Dagori and Siltara. The Supreme Court sent the ED to the Delhi High Court on 29-Jul-2026; next hearing 28-Oct-2026 (Results30092026, p.3, p.5).
- **99.87% of promoter shares pledged** to lenders (step 4).
- **SEBI settlement applications** by the company and promoter-group members over undisclosed promoter-group persons. Pending with SEBI (Annual_Report_2026, p.62).
- **DATASEL S.R.L., Italy**, a subsidiary of group firm Neco Defence Munitions, drew media reports. JNIL says it has no dealings with either (filings/…Datasel_SRL.pdf, p.2). The substance of the reports is NOT FOUND.
- **Rs 290.67 crore paid to promoter-group Neco Mining**, up 27% in a year (step 4).
- Disputed dues not deposited: Rs 208.72 crore, mainly electricity duty and energy cess (Annual_Report_2026, p.108). Contingent liabilities Rs 144.23 crore (p.134).
- No dividend, citing NCD covenants and past losses (p.59); preference dividend arrears Rs 17.01 crore (p.137).

- 21.82% of shares sit with non-institutional bodies corporate (p.75). ACRE held about 26% in December 2023 (05-CARE.pdf, p.1). The current holder is NOT FOUND.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

India Ratings, 5-Feb-2026: **IND BBB+/Stable** affirmed, with Rs 700 crore of bank lines rated (01-IndiaRatings, p.1). Net adjusted leverage fell to 1.62x in 9M FY26 from 3.1x in FY24. The agency expects EBITDA margin of 18% to 20% and EBITDA per tonne of **about Rs 6,000** over the medium term. That is below the 9M FY26 level of Rs 6,489, so the agency builds in some give-back. It names contingent liabilities and the coal block case as weaknesses. Downgrade trigger: leverage above 3.0x.

In August 2025 the agency listed "governance structure" as a weakness and liquidity as stretched (Credit_Rating_Disclosure_210802025, p.2, p.4). The transition it sees is deleveraging, not product.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED for the R3 climb.** Fired for the recovery: the last three quarters ran at 19% to 20% margin. But the value-added share is flat and its price fell.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, with a legal rider.** The pledge, nil dividend, negative retained earnings and 12.5% debt are the cost of leaving restructuring. They shrink as debt falls. The legal cases are not an artifact; they are unresolved and could become STRUCTURAL.
- **Recognition gap: OPEN to R2, R3 unproven.** Trailing PE is 14.0 (market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record). That sits between the R1 (~12x) and R2 (~15 to 17x) neighbourhoods. Market cap went from Rs 2,938 crore in March 2025 to Rs 7,002 crore in March 2026 (Presssrelease30062026, p.29) and Rs 9,770 crore today, so part of the recovery is priced.

**Posture: RESEARCH / WATCH.** The cell is proof NOT FIRED, ARTIFACT, gap OPEN. The recovery itself reads as EARNINGS-ONLY on a converter multiple.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The case for PROCEED meets the same bar. Earnings are cash backed. Debt fell about Rs 100 crore a month in H1 FY27. Promoters put in fresh money at Rs 89.13. The forward multiple is about 11x. The case against: this strategy hunts a rung climb, and the filed numbers show a full furnace and cheaper debt, not pricing power. Add a convicted MD, a live coal block trial and a 99.87% pledge. A `/step1` now would mostly re-prove a cyclical recovery.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. Rolled product realisation per tonne by quarter, against a market long-product benchmark.
2. The 28-Oct-2026 Delhi High Court hearing on the ED revision, and the status of the Abhijeet Infrastructure appeal.
3. Neco Mining contract terms: rate per cubic metre and how it was benchmarked.
4. Q2 FY27 volumes from a readable press release.
5. Who holds the 21.82% bodies-corporate block.

**What would change the view**
- Rolled realisation rising against benchmark, or value-added share above 70% in filed data: moves to PROCEED.
- A clean DHC outcome plus a 50% pledge release: removes the main governance discount.
- EBITDA per tonne falling to the agency's Rs 6,000 while steel prices hold: confirms R2 and caps the case.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
