Key Performance Highlights

  ▪ Q1FY27 revenue of ₹ 281.8 crore, an increase of 23.7% YoY;
    driven by -
      • Steady volume growth with treatments up 13.3% YoY to
         10.31 lakh (Q1FY26: 9.10 lakh) and guests up 13.0% YoY to
         38,262, alongside an 9.2% YoY improvement in revenue per
         treatment to ₹2,733 (Q1FY26: ₹2,503)

  ▪ Q1FY27 Adjusted EBITDA* of ₹ 65.1 crore, an increase of
    30.7% YoY; driven by -

            • Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 120 bps YoY to 23.1%
              (Q1FY26: 21.9%) and improved 220 bps sequentially over
              Q4FY26 (20.9%), reflecting continued operating leverage

            • Adjusted PAT grew 41.7% YoY to ₹36.8 crore (Q1FY26:
              ₹26.0 crore)




*EBITDA adjusted for Saudi expenses and ESOP expenses
