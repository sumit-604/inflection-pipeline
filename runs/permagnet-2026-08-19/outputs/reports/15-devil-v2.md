## MANDATORY ATTACK 2 — DILUTION OVERLAY

**The valuation's netting move.** Slice 3B takes mature project equity Rs 4,725 Cr (350 Cr PAT × 13.5x),
subtracts Rs 650 Cr unraised capital, and attributes the net Rs 4,075 Cr to the CURRENT 0.8598 Cr shares.

"debt and equity, yet to finalize" (dossier V6) against a Rs 758 Cr market cap means real equity dilution.

**The netting double-counts the benefit.** Current holders neither put in the Rs 650 Cr themselves nor,
in the model, get diluted for it. Both cannot be true. If the capital is raised as equity, new holders
receive a claim on the same Rs 4,725 Cr mature equity, and current holders' fraction shrinks.

**Model the raise at CMP Rs 882.** Rs 650 Cr / Rs 882 = **0.737 Cr new shares**. Total = 0.8598 + 0.737
= **1.597 Cr shares** (+85.7%, roughly a doubling). Current holders now own 0.8598 / 1.597 = **53.8%**.

- Correct existing-holder full-plan value = 4,725 (do NOT net capital; it is inside via new shares) ×
  0.538 × 0.12 (prob) × 0.4972 (PV) / 0.8598 = **Rs 176/share**, versus the model's Rs 283.

**Model the raise at a pre-success discount Rs 500** (a more realistic pre-order price). Rs 650 Cr / Rs
500 = **1.30 Cr new shares**. Total = **2.16 Cr shares** (+151%). Current holders own 0.8598 / 2.16 =
**39.8%**.

- Existing-holder full-plan value = 4,725 × 0.398 × 0.12 × 0.4972 / 0.8598 = **Rs 130/share**.

**So the full-plan slice reachable BY EXISTING HOLDERS is Rs 130-176/share, not Rs 283.** The model
overstates the slice that existing holders can actually capture by **Rs 107-153/share**.

**Fair value with dilution-correct full plan:**

|---|---|---|---|

**Is the 12% slice even reachable by existing holders? Partly, and less than half of it.** The full plan,

## 7. PRE-MORTEM

It is August 2028. An investor who bought near the top of the entry zone (Rs 361) in a dip is down ~40%,
at ~Rs 216. Here is what happened.

Meanwhile the core melted quietly. Itron kept consolidating; the auto book kept shrinking; copper and rare

16.5% core ROCE that "cleared CoC on both ends" printed 13% and stopped clearing it. The Rs 421 core floor

honest, ex-lottery, cycle-normal core. Nothing was hidden — every one of these was in the FY26 filings and
the dossier. The mistake was paying inside a zone whose width was built on the lottery.
