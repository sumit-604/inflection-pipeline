Q&A. Yogansh Jeswani (Mittal Analytics) on margins: "Margins have been sequentially
weaker and impact lower than what we have been aiming for and guiding for. So any
specific reasons for the same...?"
Rishab Gupta: "Rossell is investing a lot for the future, whether it's on people,
whether it's on infrastructure, whether it's on training. We've also got a lot of new
customers with a lot of new contracts where a lot of qualification is going on. A lot
of energy and efforts are being spent on these qualifications, which doesn't
necessarily yield in revenue this year, but in the following years to come, all these
qualifications will result in repeat orders making our profitability much higher
moving forward."
On FY27 guidance: "...we saw the scale that we had last year and we're targeting the
same kind of growth this year as well on our revenue moving forward. The breakup in
the split of revenue, I would say is 50% aerospace and defense and 50%
non-aerospace and defense for the year '26-'27."
On margins: "I've always said the margins will be between 17% to 22%. We are definitely
going to show an increased margin this financial year with a lot of efficiencies and
the scale we're looking at."
On semiconductors being high-volume low-margin: "The margins in the current competency
that we do, whether it's for defense or non-defense, will be in the range of 17% to
22%. Yes, definitely for our non-aerospace and defense customers, we're doing a lot of
automation, which also increases our bottom line and our profitability."

Q&A summary as filed.
Inventory management and debt strategy. Yogansh Jeswani expressed concern about
increasing debt on the balance sheet and the need for fundraising; highlighted
anticipated high growth in FY27 and the requirement for significant working capital
due to inventory-heavy business operations; sought clarity on the strategy to manage
debt.
Rishab Gupta: goal to reduce inventory levels from the current 7.6 months to a target
of 4 months; efforts to improve inventory management and fundraise primarily to
strengthen the balance sheet for future growth.
Boeing order execution update. Rahil Dasani asked about the execution status of the
loss-making Boeing order in Q4 and the remaining execution planned for FY27.
Rishab Gupta: approximately INR 75 crores were executed this year, with an additional
INR 70 crores remaining for the next financial year; the majority of the remaining
execution would occur in the first two quarters of FY27.
Space and semiconductor revenue growth. Rishab Gupta forecast: Space ~300% to 400%
growth compared to the previous year; Semiconductor ~300% to 400% growth compared to
the previous year.

Q&A summary as filed.
Back-to-back agreements and escalations. When signing a five-year contract with
customers, Rossell Techsys also signs five-year contracts with suppliers. Suppliers
provide escalation details; Rossell may or may not add escalation to their bid; in
some cases escalation is passed on to the customer as part of the cost.
Passing prices to customers: Rossell can pass on price increases to customers in some
cases; contract adjustments include EPA (Equitable Price Adjustment) clauses, requests
for advance payments and upfront payments for long-lead items or single-source
materials to de-risk programs. Some customers agree, others do not.
Manpower costs: FY2026 15.5% of revenue; FY2027 15% of revenue; improvement expected
in FY2027 as the company scales up.
