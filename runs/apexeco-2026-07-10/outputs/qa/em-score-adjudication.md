## Scoring method (for reference)

B07 scores each category raw 0 to 4 on a likelihood-by-impact matrix, then multiplies by an evidence-quality factor: documented 1.0x, management claim 0.7x, analyst inference 0.5x. No evidence scores 0. Adjusted values sum to the EM score. The discipline that separates 10.1 from 41 is entirely in the evidence multiplier and the no-evidence zeros, not in the matrix.

## The three over-credits that produce a 41

1. Marketing accepted as fact (H2, plus the E2 and geographic framing). The investor presentation sells a Veolia "strategic alliance" and an "established international presence." Management denied the Veolia partnership on a live call, and audited exports fell year on year with the Gulf push abandoned. An external scorer reading the deck rather than testing it against the transcript and Note 22 would credit two to three documented moats that do not exist. Source verification zeroes them.

2. Self-reported firsts treated as documented IP (A2, A3, E1). EDR-first, MVR-first, electrocoagulation, ">98% recovery" are specific but entirely self-reported, with no patent, filing, or third-party confirmation. The scan credits E1 at claim weight (0.7x) and scores A2/A3 zero for lack of any filing or data. A 41 credits these as documented technology moats. No document supports that tier.

3. Concentration and a strong balance sheet double-counted as an ecosystem and a growth engine (C1, F2, G1). One corporate group drives about 70% of execution. The scan credits the two orders as fact but caps the "ecosystem" reading at Moderate claim weight and books the concentration as a risk (C2). A 41 upgrades C1 and F2 to strong documented moats and ignores C2 and the broken-promise ledger.

## Conclusion

The external Emerging Moat score of 41 fails source verification. It can only be reached by crediting management claims and investor-deck marketing as documented moats in categories where the audited annual report, the three concall transcripts, and the presentation either provide no evidence (A2, A3, D1, D2, A4, B3, H3) or document the opposite (B1 backward integration, C2 concentration, E2 exports, G2 working capital), and by upgrading the one contradicted claim (H2 Veolia) that management denied on record. The source-supported score is 10.1 (10.3 corrected), classification NONE. Pillar 3 growth premium is therefore +0x, and the destination PE consequence stands: the valuation earns no growth-visibility premium from an emerging moat, because none is documented.
