## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition (Note 3.2, p.176-178, PDF p.91-92):** Interest income on EIR method for
  amortised-cost and FVOCI assets (Ind AS 109 standard). Interest on Stage 3 loans is calculated
  on **net** amortised cost (after ECL), reverting to gross basis on cure — standard, not
  aggressive. 🟢
- **Income from direct assignment (Note 3.2, p.177):** "Gains arising out of direct assignment
  transactions comprise of the difference between the interest on the loan portfolio and the
  applicable rate... recorded **upfront** in the Statement of Profit and Loss." This is a
  judgment-heavy, upfront-recognition policy (excess interest spread, EIS) common to NBFCs but
  worth watching given DA income growth outpaced loan book growth this year (see Section 11). 🟡
- **ECL / staging policy (Note 3.6(j), p.181-183; Note 44.1(a)-(e), p.224-226):** Three-stage ECL
  (Stage 1: 0-29 DPD, Stage 2: 30-89 DPD or OTR-restructured, Stage 3: 90+ DPD or reasonable/
  supportable forward-looking indicators). PD via Through-The-Cycle → Point-in-Time conversion
  using a Vasicek model with macro overlay; LGD via internal workout model. Management explicitly
  discloses use of **management overlays** to model-derived PDs/LGDs "for certain pool of loans...
  given the uncertainty on forward looking risks" (Note 3.6(j), p.183) — undisclosed in quantum. 🟡
- **Gold loan Stage 3 exception (Note 3.6(j) p.181, Note 44.1(a) p.225, Note 48.30(B) p.250):** For
  gold loans, 90+ DPD is classified Stage 3 only if the loan "does not fulfil the conditions for
  minimum collateral cover." A formal "90-day rebuttal" mechanism kept 6,043 accounts / ₹7,756
  Lakh (₹77.56 Cr) outstanding as at 31-Mar-25 (7,888 accounts / ₹9,056 Lakh / ₹90.56 Cr in
  FY24) **out of** Stage 3 despite being 90+ DPD, based on collateral-sufficiency judgment. This
  is an RBI-permitted NBFC-specific carve-out, not a general accounting irregularity, but it is a
  genuine judgment area an investor should track for consistency of application. 🟡
- **PPE/Intangibles (Note 3.3-3.4, p.176-178):** Computer equipment on SLM, other classes on WDV;
  useful lives match Schedule II exactly (no divergence flagged: Computer 3yr, Server 6yr, Office
  equipment 5yr, Furniture 10yr, Vehicles 8yr). Software intangibles amortised over 3 years
  (company policy, not tied to Schedule II). No revaluation of PPE/intangibles/ROU during current
  or previous year (Note 57(ii), p.258). 🟢
- **Leases (Ind AS 116, Note 3.14 p.179-180, Note 47 p.233-234):** ROU asset and lease liability
  discounted at incremental borrowing rate (specific rate NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — not disclosed as
  a single number). ROU (building) grew from ₹11,242 Lakh (₹112.42 Cr) to ₹15,394 Lakh (₹153.94
  Cr), +37.0% YoY, on ₹7,422 Lakh (₹74.22 Cr) of net additions (branch network expansion, consistent
  with 694 branches disclosed in MD&A). Lease liability rose ₹12,558 Lakh → ₹16,854 Lakh (+34.2%). 🟢
- **First-time adoption / policy changes:** Note 48.14 (p.243, "Net profit or loss for the year,
  prior period items and change in accounting policy"): "There are no prior period items and no
  changes in accounting policy." Note 59 (p.258) states prior year numbers "have been
  regrouped/reclassified, wherever considered necessary" but gives **no specifics** of what was
  regrouped or the amount — boilerplate disclosure without quantification. 🟡
- **Impairment of non-financial assets (Note 3.7, p.183-184):** Standard Ind AS 36 language;
  no impairment testing assumptions (growth/discount rates) disclosed because no impairment was
  recognised or CGU test triggered in the year — consistent with an asset-light NBFC balance sheet
  (net PPE only ₹3,191 Lakh / ₹31.91 Cr). NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (no goodwill on balance sheet;
  not applicable).
