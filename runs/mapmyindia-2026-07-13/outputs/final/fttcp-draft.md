## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

Revenue. The thing that could restart growth is the order book converting. There is more than Rs1,750 Cr of it, about 3.7 years of revenue, and inside it more than Rs200 Cr of government orders plus the signed IOCL five-year deal worth Rs110 Cr and a Survey of India order in hand. What confirms it: government-tagged revenue growing again toward 15 to 20% in the Q1 and Q2 FY27 prints, and the roughly Rs20 Cr FY27 IOCL contribution showing up. What kills it: another quarter or two of the same deferrals, re-tenders, and grant delays that took FY26 growth to 2.3%.

Margin. The thing that could hold or lift margin is the IoT mix shifting toward SaaS and the map licensing operating leverage returning if revenue re-accelerates. The Q4 FY26 exit margin near 44% is the evidence it can. What confirms it: blended EBITDA margin above 38% for two straight quarters. What kills it: the high-margin map core continuing to shrink while low-margin hardware carries the top line.

Cash. This is where the catalyst search comes up nearly empty. There is no named working-capital program, no factoring facility, no payment-term renegotiation, and management deflected the DSO question in two consecutive calls. The only path to improvement is mechanical: government receivables are collectible, so if the deferred orders convert and the state customers pay, receivables come down. That path is real but undocumented and hostage to the same government-conversion timing. What confirms it: Q1 FY27 consolidated receivables falling back toward the FY24 base. What kills it: receivables climbing further above Rs176 Cr with the over-six-month ageing bucket widening.

ROCE. There is no standalone ROCE catalyst. It is the last transition in the sequence and it moves only when the first three do. The idle treasury could be deployed into returns, but management has stated no capital-allocation plan, so that optionality is unpriced. What confirms a turn: revenue and margin turning together with receivables normalising. What kills it: the map core erosion and treasury drag persisting.

Shared catalyst warning: government order conversion sits under revenue, cash, and ROCE at once. It is one point of failure wearing three hats. The whole forward case leans on the Q1 and Q2 FY27 government prints.
