Dear Sir/Ma’am, 
 
Subject: – Clarification on the News Article 
 
This has reference to your email dated October 23, 2025 seeking clarification from the  Company in 
terms of the provisions Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing  Obligations and Disclosures) Regulations, 
2015 (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’) with reference to news article published in The Hindu Businessline , 
“Syngene expands Biologics facility with new ADC bioconjugation capability”. In this regard we hereby 
confirm the facts stated in the news article.  
 
The Company plans to provide fully integrated, end -to-end services for antibody -drug conjugates 
(ADCs), from discovery through GMP manufacturing. The suite will accelerate development timelines 
by enabling both Monoclonal Antibodies (mAb) production and GMP bioconjuga tion at a single site 
and complement Company’s existing commercial capabilities in payload and linker manufacturing. 
 
The said investment in bioconjugation capabilities is in the Ordinary Course of its business, and is not 
deemed to be ‘material’ as it does not meet any of the Materiality criterion currently as specified 
under the Industry Standards Note  (ISN) on Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing  Regulations read with 
Clause 4 of Para B of Part A of Schedule III and regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations. 
 
We trust this provides the necessary clarification. 
 
The above -mentioned information will also be made available on the website of the Company 
www.syngeneintl.com.
