## The picture in one paragraph

The count of stocks above their 20 day line rose to 897, the best since at least late September and past the level where the mid October repair stalled. The count above the 200 day line rose to 756, about one stock in three. The Nifty rose about 1.6 percent and closed back above its thirty week line. The bounce now looks stronger on the trend counts, but buyers still have not come in size, and the lift is coming from the top of the market.

## Where the market stands

The restated sheet counts about 2,310 stocks. Each has three trend lines: 20 days, 50 days and 200 days. A stock above a line is in an uptrend on that clock. On Tuesday 897 stocks stood above the short line, almost four in ten. 643 stood above the middle line, a little over one in four. 756 stood above the long line, about one in three. So two stocks in three still sit in long downtrends.

The year gives these counts their scale. In the restated record the spring low came on 23 March, when only 289 stocks held the long line. On 1 April came the great thrust day, when 1,119 stocks rose four and a half percent or more. The climb ran four months, and the long count peaked at 1,367 on 7 August. The autumn has walked down a staircase since. In late September the long count sat near 1,020. On 8 and 9 October it fell to the high 800s. A mid October repair lifted it to 981 on 16 October. Then the floor broke, and on 23 October only 602 stocks held the long line. Tuesday's 756 is about 150 stocks above that low. It is still well short of the early October floor.

The short count tells the more hopeful part of the story. During the mid October repair, the short count stalled near 840 on two separate days. It never got past that level, and the repair broke the next week. On Tuesday the short count reached 897. That is the best reading since at least late September. The last brief named this level as one of four tests. Tonight it passed.

The other tests did not pass. Tuesday was a narrower day than Monday. 131 stocks rose four and a half percent or more, and 56 fell that hard. On Monday 252 rose that hard. About one stock in four traded four percent above Monday's close at some point. Only about three in ten of those kept the gain into the close. On Monday more than four in ten kept it. More stocks sat quiet, with about three in ten inside a three percent range all day.

Volume improved a little and stayed light. The sheet compares the number of stocks trading on heavy volume with the number on light volume. On Tuesday there was about one heavy stock for every two light ones, a touch better than Monday and the best of the last five sessions. Turnover came to about 450 crore, down from about 494 on Monday. The two heavy selling days, 23 and 30 October, each carried about 610 crore. ## What would change this read

The read is a bounce that has passed the October short stall, on light money, led by the largest companies. It turns worse if a heavy selling day returns and the next session fails to answer it. A day of 300 or more hard fallers would be the first sign. A long count back under about 600, the 23 October low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows back above about 300 would say the same. A short count back under about 750, below Monday's level, on rising turnover would repeat the way the October repair broke. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has taken charge.

The cautious read fails if buying turns heavy and spreads to the smaller stocks. The first test is a wide up day on turnover above about 610 crore. Tuesday reached about 450. The second test is the long count above about 875, the early October floor. Tuesday reached 756. The third test is new yearly highs past about 50 while new lows stay under 100. Tuesday had 33 highs and 49 lows, so half of that test holds. The fourth test is the small company indices keeping pace with the Nifty on an up day. Tuesday they rose less than a third as much. A VIX close under 15 would support the turn. Two of these passing in the same week, with the short count holding above 840, would change the read from bounce to repair.
