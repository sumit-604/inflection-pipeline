No change in useful lives disclosed this year. 🟢 Standard, disclosed.
- **First-time standard adoption**: Ind AS 117 (Insurance) and Ind AS 116 leaseback amendments adopted 1 April 2024 — Group assessed "no significant impact" (Note 1D, p.284). Ind AS 21 amendment (foreign currency exchangeability) notified 9 May 2025, effective 1 April 2025 — impact "currently being assessed," not yet quantified. 🟢 Routine, forward flag only.
- **Borrowing cost capitalisation**: Standalone ₹29.60 cr capitalised in FY25 at 6.33% p.a. capitalisation rate (up from ₹18.65 cr FY24) (Note 2, p.208-209 approx). Consolidated ₹96.40 cr capitalised in FY25 at 6.35% (FY24: ₹89.03 cr at 6.29%) (Note 2, p.285). Rising capitalised-interest base tracks the heavy capex programme; watch for future depreciation drag once assets go live. 🟡 Watch.
- **Impairment testing**: No impairment losses recognised on investments in either standalone or consolidated FS for FY25 or FY24 (Note 7(d), p.220 standalone; Note 7 consol p.290). No PPE/goodwill impairment testing sensitivities disclosed (Group carries no goodwill). 🟢 Clean.
- **ECL matrix**: Provision-matrix approach for trade receivables at both standalone and consolidated levels, based on ageing (Note 12(a), standalone p.221; Note 12(a) consol p.292). Standard, no unusual conservatism or aggression flagged.
- **Ind AS 116 leases**: Standalone lease liability ₹2.21 cr (FY25) vs ₹2.09 cr (FY24) (Note 21, p.~213). Consolidated lease liability ₹9.97 cr (FY25) vs ₹9.47 cr (FY24) (Note 21, p.301); ROU assets ₹71.42 cr (FY25) vs ₹72.62 cr (FY24) (Note 3, p.285-286), dominated by land ROU (₹70.57 cr). Immaterial relative to balance sheet size. 🟢 Clean.
- **Segment policy vs disclosed reality**: Company states it has "only one business segment, viz. iron and steel" at BOTH standalone (Note 47, p.251) and consolidated level (Note 48, p.328), despite the Group's disclosed principal activities spanning steel, ferro alloys, aluminium foil manufacturing (Shree Venkateshwara Electrocast), power generation (Shyam Energy Ltd), and mining (S.S. Natural Resources, Nirjhar Commodities). No product-level segment profitability is disclosed anywhere in the notes. 🟡 Watch — disclosure minimalism; see Pass 3 pattern note below.
