   CyberTech Reports Q2FY27 Revenue  780.3 million; up 16.9% YoY

Mumbai, India - 22nd October, 2026: CyberTech Systems and Software Ltd. (BSE: 532173;
NSE: CYBERTECH), a CMMI Level 3 next-generation Enterprise Cloud Transformations provider,
announced today its financial results for the second quarter and half year FY27 ended on 30th
September 2026.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Vish Tadimety, CEO and Founder, CyberTech, stated: "The second
quarter built steadily on the first. Operating revenue was INR 733.0 million, up 24.7% year-over-year
and 7.1% over the previous quarter, and total revenue was INR 780.3 million, up 16.9% year-over-
year. As client demand increased, we expanded delivery through a mix of our own engineers,
specialist partners, and AI-assisted delivery, and our operating revenue grew at a faster pace than
our delivery costs. Profit after tax for the quarter was INR 86.8 million, reflecting lower other income
compared with the same period last year.
For the first half, operating revenue increased 21.2% to INR 1,417.4 million and profit after tax
increased 7.9% to INR 181.5 million. These results indicate that the areas we have chosen to invest
in, namely specialized engineering, AI-enabled delivery, and cloud, are contributing to the operating
performance of the business, and we will continue to build on them selectively.
Geospatial remains central to our business. Our standing as an Esri Platinum Partner continues to
support client interest in our Managed ArcGIS Cloud Services, and we are applying GeoAI in
engagements where location data informs operational decisions.
Our SAP practice continues to support enterprises moving to S/4HANA and building on the Business
Technology Platform (BTP). Our collaboration with the SAP Migration Factory helps clients plan and
execute their transitions to the SAP cloud in a structured way.
As AI changes the economics of technology services, we believe the advantage lies with firms that
pair specialized expertise with AI in delivery, rather than those that scale primarily through
headcount. That is the model we have been building at CyberTech, and our partnerships with AWS
and Microsoft Azure keep our work aligned with the platforms our clients rely on.
We will continue to manage the business with discipline, both in our operations and in how we
deploy capital. My thanks to our Board of Directors and our shareholders for their support, and to
our clients and our team for their trust and commitment."

About CyberTech

CyberTech Systems is a leading enterprise cloud transformation company specializing in cloud-
based SAP solutions and Esri ArcGIS Enterprise platforms. Founded in 1995, the company helps
global organizations digitalize their SAP and GIS landscapes through managed ArcGIS cloud services,
SAP S/4 transformations, enterprise integration, and utility network transformation. Serving
utilities, government, public safety, manufacturing, and healthcare industries, CyberTech is an Esri
Gold Partner and SAP Gold Partner providing comprehensive cloud transformation services on AWS
and Microsoft Azure from offices in North America and development centers in India For more
