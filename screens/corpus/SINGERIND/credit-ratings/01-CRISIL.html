Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Singer India Limited (SIL).

 

The ratings continue to reflect the company’s well-established brand with a strong presence in the sewing machine industry in India, as well as in the home appliances business; product and geographical diversification across India; and an asset-light business model, resulting in a comfortable return on capital employed (RoCE). The ratings also factor in prudent working capital management and a comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India, leading to weakened operating margins.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SIL.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths

Well-established brand and strong market presence: SIL has a trademark agreement with the owners of the ‘Singer’ and ‘Merritt’ brands, namely, Singer Sourcing Limited LLC, a subsidiary of SVP Worldwide. SIL has been involved in the sewing machine business since 1977 and has a strong distribution and after-sales service network for the sewing machine and appliance businesses. SIL presently derives 75–80% of its revenue from sewing machines and the balance 20–25% from the home appliance segment. Although the scale of operations remained stable at around Rs 431 crores in FY 2025, the company has already recorded sales of Rs 230 crores as of September 2025 and is expected to record sales of more than Rs 530 crores in FY 2026, driven by greater dealer demand in the sewing business and a new large order received under the government welfare program supporting sales.

 

Prudent working capital management and a healthy financial risk profile: Gross current assets (GCAs) range from 170 to 200 days as of March 31, 2025. A decline in inventory days, from 107 to 91 days, led to a rise in GCAs during fiscal 2025. The asset-light model has helped maintain a healthy capital structure, reflected in a total outside liabilities to tangible net worth (TOL/TNW) ratio of 0.52 times as of March 31, 2025. In the absence of any major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) plans for the medium term, the TOL/TNW ratio is expected to remain low, at 0.5–0.6 times. Net worth has also improved to around Rs 158.64 crore as of March 31, 2025, and is expected to improve to more than Rs 170 crore in FY 2026.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses

Exposure to intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India leading to weakened operating margins: The entry of several large players in the consumer durable segment over the past few years has led to significant price competition, which has adversely affected the operating profitability of most players. In FY 2025, the operating margins saw an improvement from 0.4% to 1.47%, primarily due to an increase in realization from the sewing division and the discontinuation of the loss-making segment in the home appliances segment, which helped boost overall margins. In H1 FY 2026, the operating margins remained at around 0.26% , mainly due to a loss in Q1 resulting from a reduced bulk order from the government for black sewing machines and a hit in demand for domestic appliances due to a shorter summer. Profitability will remain a challenge for most players in the industry due to intense competition and consolidation witnessed across large consumer players in the domestic market. It is expected that with the improvement in Revenue from Sewing Business the company will close the year with operating margins of around 2.5%–3% in FY 2026. Sustained improvement in operating margins will remain a key monitorable

Liquidity Adequate

Bank limit utilisation was unutilised over the 12 months ended September 30, 2025. Cash accrual of over Rs 9-10 crore is expected per fiscal against nil term debt obligation over the medium term. Current ratio was healthy at 2.81 times as on March 31, 2025. Low gearing and healthy networth offer financial flexibility to withstand adverse conditions or downturn in the business.

Outlook Stable

SIL will continue to benefit from its strong brand and well-established distribution and after-sales service networks.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Improvement in operating margin to over 2% on a consistent basis, leading to improvement in the overall cash accruals of the company.
  • Sustenance of liquidity and financial risk profile, particularly the capital structure
     

Downward factors

  • Decline in revenue or operating margin (below 0.4%) on a sustained basis, impacting net cash accrual over the medium term
  • Higher-than-expected debt-funded capex or sizeable dividend pay-out, weakening the financial risk profile.

About the Company

Incorporated in 1977, SIL is 31% owned by RHBV as of March 31, 2023. RHBV sold SIL 22.34% of its equity in the open market in August 2022, as a part of the restructuring plans for SIL. The business profile, which includes assembly and trading of sewing machines and home appliances in India, under the Singer and Merritt brands, will remain unchanged. The manufacturing facility is at Jammu.
