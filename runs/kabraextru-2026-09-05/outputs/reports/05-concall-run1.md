### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Only one class of miss (extrusion revenue decline) receives an explicit explanation, and it is **100% external-blame**: Jal Jeevan Mission fund-disbursement delays, state-government infrastructure spending delays, and export-market weakness from "geopolitical challenges and currency volatility" (AR FY26 p.36). No internal or execution-side factor is named for this miss.

Every other miss or contradiction identified in 2A is met with **silence**, not an excuse:
- The battery segment's widening loss despite revenue growth (2A row 4) — never addressed.
- The R&D spend cut against an "accelerated R&D" claim (2A row 5) — never addressed.
- The dropped 40% market-share claim (2A row 2) — never addressed; the number simply stops appearing.
- The two-step CRISIL downgrade during FY26 (see 2D) — disclosed only in the mandatory Corporate Governance table with the generic reason "Basis performance reported for Quarter 3" (AR FY26 p.51); no discussion in the chairman's letter, Director's Report narrative, or MD&A of what drove the Q3 miss or the downgrade.
- The stale HEVPL receivable note (see 2D) — carried forward unchanged.

**Pattern check:** management does not say "we made a mistake" anywhere in either report. The single acknowledged shortfall (extrusion demand) is blamed entirely on externals. The credit-self-improvement instances that do exist (E-3W/swapping entry, RESS/BESS/D2C launch) are stated proactively and with some specificity — this is the one area where management appears reasonably forthcoming. But hard topics (rating downgrade, KMP churn, R&D cuts, dropped share claim) are never raised proactively; they surface only in numbers a reader must independently compare across two documents or mandatory disclosure tables.

**Classification: external-blame-heavy**, with a substantial silence component on company-specific misses.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **The CRISIL two-step downgrade is never discussed narratively.** Long-term rating moved CRISIL A+/Negative → A/Negative → A-/Stable and short-term moved CRISIL A1 → A2+ during FY26 (AR FY26 p.51). This is disclosed only inside the mandatory Corporate Governance "Credit Rating" table with a one-line generic reason ("Basis performance reported for Quarter 3"). Neither the chairman's letter nor the MD&A mentions it. Likely reason for silence: it directly undercuts the "well-positioned," "strong balance sheet" framing used elsewhere in the same report.
- **The R&D spend cut is never mentioned.** Extrusion R&D fell 67.1% (Rs 1,116.62 lakh → Rs 367 lakh) and Geon R&D fell 78.6% (Rs 866.78 lakh → Rs 185.18 lakh) year on year (AR FY25 p.30-31; AR FY26 p.28-29), even as the Geon narrative claims R&D was "accelerated" (AR FY26 p.29). Likely reason: acknowledging a cash-conservation-driven R&D cut would contradict the "dual growth engine" and innovation-led narrative built around Geon.
- **The battery segment's negative operating leverage is never reconciled.** Segment revenue rose 7.2% (Rs 126.98 Cr → Rs 136.11 Cr) while the segment loss widened 69.8% (Rs 25.53 Cr → Rs 43.35 Cr, Note 38). The "moving towards profitability as volumes scale up" claim (p.38) sits in the same document as this evidence without addressing it.
- **Senior-management churn is disclosed only in mandatory KMP/senior-management tables, never in the narrative.** Confirmed directly from AR FY26: COO–Geon Division Mr. Subhabrata Ghosh was appointed 01-Aug-2025 and ceased 16-Feb-2026 — a tenure of under seven months (Corporate Governance Report, senior management table, p.47-48, printed p.44-45). CFO Mr. Daulat Jain resigned effective 27-Apr-2026, just after FY26 close, with an interim CFO (Mr. Uttam Singh, 13-May-2026 to 19-Jun-2026) before Mr. Bhavin Sheth took over 20-Jun-2026 (Corporate Information, p.3; Note 39 KMP list, p.107). Extrusion Division CEO also turned over at the start of FY26 (Mr. Atanu Maity resigned 16-May-2025 per AR FY25 Corporate Information p.3; Mr. Yogesh Deo appointed the same date per AR FY26 senior-management table). None of this is mentioned in the chairman's letter or MD&A. (Note: the AR FY26 senior-management table records Company Secretary Ms. Shilpa Rathi and Head–Strategy Mr. Arnab Saha as "No" change during FY26 — this analysis cannot corroborate a Company Secretary or Head-Strategy exit from the AR corpus; if such exits occurred they are not disclosed here.)
- **The HEVPL (Hero Electric) receivable and NCLT insolvency exposure never appears in the MD&A Risks and Challenges section of either report**, despite being a named customer-credit event. The Rs 30.39 Cr (Rs 3,039 lakh) receivable and the CIRP order of 20-Dec-2024 are disclosed only in the financial-statement notes (AR FY25 p.86-87, standalone and consolidated; AR FY26 p.87-88, standalone and consolidated), and the disclosure text is **identical, word-for-word, in both years' notes**, still describing the position "as at March 31, 2025" inside the FY26 annual report. This is a stale disclosure: the FY26 AR does not appear to have refreshed the receivable balance or NCLT case status for the year it covers.
- **No order-book total is disclosed.** A single ~Rs 150 Cr order is named (AR FY26 p.37) but no opening/closing order-book value, book-to-bill ratio, or aggregate pipeline is given for either segment in either report.
