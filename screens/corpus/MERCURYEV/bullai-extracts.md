   5,030.34, a gap of 90.00. FY25: 6,494.27 plus 142.41 equals 6,636.68, but
   total revenue is printed as 6,066.68, a gap of 570.00. Q4 FY25 column shows
   the same defect: 2,165.91 plus 96.27 equals 2,262.18, printed as 2,113.17.
2. **A March 2023 balance sheet is bundled inside the FY26 results (p3).** The
   page is headed "STATEMENT OF STANDALONE ASSETS AND LIABILITIES AS AT MARCH
   31, 2023", carries FY22 comparatives, and is signed "DATE: 30.05.2023". The
   FY26 filing therefore contains no current balance sheet in the pages read.
   The 2023 figures must not be used as FY26.

   "JIGNESH R. THAKKAR, DIN: 01631093" (p2) against "JAYESH RAICHANDBHAI
   THAKKAR, DIN: 01631093" (p4, p5). Page 3 shows "JAYESH RAICHANDBHAI THAKKAR,
   DIN: 06631693", a different DIN again.

   Block No. 29 Mangalej Ta-Kadi; Block No. 28 Vill-Manghej Ta-Kadi; Block No.
   28 VIII-Maneja Ta-Karjan; Block No. 26 VIII-Manglej Ta-Karjan.
