Sub: Change in Management - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board

Dear Sir/Madam,

Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III, Part A, Para A (7) of the Securities and Exchange
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (`SEBI Listing
Regulations'), we wish to inform you of the following change in management of the Company:

1. Mr. Subhendu Kumar, Head of Chemistry and a member of Executive Committee (Senior
    Management Personnel or SMP) have changed the designation from SMP to Non-SMP category
    w.e.f. close of business hours of October 27, 2026.

2. Mr. Priyaranjan Pattanaik, Head of Biology and a member of Executive Committee (Senior
    Management Personnel or SMP) have changed the designation from SMP to Non-SMP category
    w.e.f. close of business hours of October 27, 2026.

3. Mr. Ajay Tandon, Head Strategy & Corporate Development and a member of Executive
    Committee (Senior Management Personnel or SMP) will be resigning from the Company with
    effect from close of business hours on October 30, 2026.

4. Mr. Deepak Jain, Chief Financial Officer and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of the Company has
    decided to move on from the Company for personal reasons and will pursue opportunities
    outside the Company. He will continue in his current role to ensure a smooth transition, with the
    transition timeline to be mutually agreed upon.

Details with respect to change in management as required under Regulation 30(6) read with Para A
(7) of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI Listing Regulations and SEBI Circular no.
SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-PoD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 issued in this regard, are
given in Annexure A to this letter and a copy of the resignation letter received from Mr. Ajay Tandon
dated October 23, 2026 is enclosed as Annexure B.

The above information will also be made available on the website of the Company at
www.syngeneintl.com.
