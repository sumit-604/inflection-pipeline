As the requisite quorum was present, with the consent of the Chairman, the Company
Secretary called the meeting to order.
Mr Deepak Suryavanshi, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, requested Mr
Thalavaidurai Pandyan, Chairman and Managing Director of the Company, to address the
shareholders.
The Chairperson welcomed the members and briefed them on the proposed acquisition of
Winwin Speciality Insulators Limited, the proposed QIP for funding the Company's expansion
plans and future strategic opportunities, and the promoter financing arrangements. He
thereafter invited the Chief Financial Officer, Mr Rajesh Jeyaraman, to explain the resolutions
and the relevant financial and procedural details to the members.
After the compilation of the speech of Mr Thalavaidurai Pandyan, Chairman and Managing
Director, Mr Deepak Suryavanshi, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer, requested Mr
Rajesh Jayaraman, Chief Financial Officer of the Company, to address the shareholders.
The Chief Financial Officer briefed the shareholders on the proposed acquisition of Winwin
Speciality Insulators Limited, including its strategic rationale, consideration and expected
operational benefits.
He elaborated on the proposed QIP of up to 700 crore, outlining the intended utilisation of
proceeds towards the acquisition, working capital requirements, establishment of the Global
Design Centre at Sangli and general corporate purposes.
He also explained the terms and rationale of the proposed unsecured promoter bridge loan
facility of up to 175 crore, carrying interest at 9% per annum for 11 years, to facilitate the
acquisition pending receipt of QIP proceeds
