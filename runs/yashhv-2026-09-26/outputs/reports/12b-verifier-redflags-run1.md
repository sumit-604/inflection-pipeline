[INFERENCE] The base case for FY27 has no single management number to anchor to; Role 1 must state which one it uses and why. Confirming observation: H1FY27 revenue run-rate against Rs165-200cr (half of each end of the range).
5. Items 3 + 21. Capex scope grew and a Rs100-110cr equity raise follows an IPO that was said to cover capacity to 2030. [INFERENCE] Per-share growth will trail company growth over FY27-28. Confirming observation: final issue size and price in the raise filing.
