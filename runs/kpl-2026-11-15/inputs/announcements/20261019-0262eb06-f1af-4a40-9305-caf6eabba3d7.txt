Subject: Acquisition of Land for Manufacturing Expansion at Amritsar, Punjab 
Kwality Pharmaceuticals Limited is pleased to announce the acquisition of 4 acres of 
land adjacent to its existing manufacturing facility in Amritsar, Punjab, to support 
its next phase of growth. 
The Company plans to develop a fully automated manufacturing facility comprising 
4 injectable lines and 6+ lines for tablets, capsules, ophthalmic and other oral 
solid dosage formulations, designed with due consideration to applicable EU GMP 
requirements, including Annex 1, wherever relevant. 
The expansion aims to meet growing demand for existing and upcoming products, 
create capacity for future launches and bioequivalence-led opportunities, and 
strengthen the Company's Amritsar manufacturing campus. 
The project is targeted for completion by Q4 FY2028, with full operations expected by 
Q2 FY2029, reinforcing the Company's commitment to scalable capacity and 
sustainable growth.
