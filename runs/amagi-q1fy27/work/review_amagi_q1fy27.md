## 1. SUMMARY NARRATIVE
Amagi's first quarter as a listed company (three months to 30 June 2026) is operationally the cleanest print the thesis has seen. Consolidated revenue was Rs 436.88 Cr, up 32.4% from Rs 330.06 Cr a year earlier. Consolidated profit after tax was Rs 33.91 Cr, versus Rs 3.94 Cr a year earlier. The important part is that the profit is real operating profit, not treasury income: core operating profit before tax, stripped of other income, swung to plus Rs 22.82 Cr from minus Rs 7.68 Cr a year earlier, and the year-on-year PAT increase of Rs 29.97 Cr was roughly all driven by operations, with other income adding only about Rs 3.3 Cr. Operating margin expanded 719 basis points to 6.83%. Both the standalone and consolidated auditor reports are clean, with no qualification or going-concern flag. Do not be misled by earnings per share falling from Rs 3.44 to Rs 1.49; that is purely because the share count roughly sextupled after the IPO and the conversion of preference shares, not an earnings decline. Three cautions temper the good print. First, and most important, this Q1 filing carries no cash-flow statement (that is only mandatory half-yearly), so the thesis's central open question, whether this profit converts to cash, is still unanswered and moves to the H1 results around November. Second, the Indian parent company on its own is still loss-making at the operating level once you remove treasury income (minus Rs 1.50 Cr); the real profit engine is the foreign, largely US, subsidiaries, and their profit contribution actually fell 25% quarter-on-quarter. Third, the group pays tax well below the statutory rate because the Indian entity pays none on carried-forward losses; that roughly 900-basis-point tax cushion reverses over time and will drag future earnings. On governance, the board also cleared a change to the memorandum that explicitly enables future equity raising, even though the company already sits on roughly Rs 1,664 Cr of cash. No thesis-breaking tripwire fired this quarter. The verdict is PROCEED WITH CAVEATS because cash conversion is indeterminate. The stock stays WATCHLIST and AVOID on valuation at around Rs 580 against an entry zone of Rs 109 to 155; the decision gate is held to the H1 cash-flow print.
