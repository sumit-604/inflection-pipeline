## Flag blocks

### FLAG-CASH — ACTIVE — determination: INDETERMINATE

Two legs point different ways, so the combined flag cannot be resolved from the corpus.

Leg 1, the current ratio collapse, is GROWTH-INDUCED and one time. Consolidated current ratio fell 26.40 percent (1.97x to 1.45x) and standalone fell 45.82 percent (4.67x to 2.53x), both breaching the company's own 25 percent materiality threshold (B02, Note 51 standalone / Note 50 consolidated). The drivers are documented: the first-ever secured borrowing, an ICICI term loan (4,200 facility, 4,025 drawn) to fund the Unbound Medicine acquisition, converting the company from net cash to net debt (B02, Note 14); and a one time labour code liability jump (gratuity PVO +58.0 percent, standalone compensated-absence liability +578.9 percent), disclosed as an exceptional item (B02, Note 32 / Note 28(b)).

Leg 2, the receivables credit-quality slip, is NOT resolvable to growth. Consolidated ECL allowance grew 35.4 percent (317.36 to 429.85) against gross receivables up 15.0 percent, the "significant increase in credit risk" ageing bucket grew 32.0 percent YoY (256.54 to 338.65), and >180-day ECL coverage rose to 90.8 percent, all at the AJE subsidiary level and invisible in the standalone accounts, coinciding with an AJE customer-advance write-back (B02, Note 11 / Note 34 / Note 28(e)). The allowance outgrowing receivables is a quality signal, not a scale signal.

Because leg 2 cannot be closed from the filed record, the determination is INDETERMINATE.

Missing evidence named (needed to resolve to GROWTH-INDUCED or STRUCTURAL):
- Consolidated receivables ageing schedule at the AJE subsidiary granularity. Source: FY27 AR consolidated notes, or a forced concall answer.
- The absolute AJE revenue base, never disclosed in any concall. Source: AR Directors' Report subsidiary detail (AJE LLC standalone FY26 revenue ~INR 10,097.28 lacs, ~USD 12m, p.38-39) as the nearest corpus anchor.
- A credit rating rationale with working capital commentary. Source: any rating agency rationale. None exists in the corpus; MPS may be genuinely unrated.

Falsification metric for the growth-induced leg (monitor quarterly): consolidated current ratio. If it holds at or above 1.3x through FY27 with the debt still on the books, leg 1 was one time as claimed. If it falls below 1.3x consolidated (or standalone below 2.0x), the liquidity drop is turning structural.

Falsification metric for the credit-quality leg (monitor at each annual close): consolidated ECL allowance growth versus receivables growth. If the allowance keeps outpacing receivables beyond FY26's 35.4 percent versus 15.0 percent, the AJE deterioration is structural.

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated)

1. Consolidated current ratio, each quarter, in the balance-sheet note. Watch below 1.3x consolidated or below 2.0x standalone. Tests whether the FY26 liquidity drop was the one time debt-plus-labour-code event claimed or the start of genuine stress.
2. Consolidated ECL allowance growth versus receivables growth, at each annual close, in the receivables note. If the allowance keeps outpacing receivables (FY26: 35.4 percent versus 15.0 percent), the AJE credit-quality slip is structural.
3. FY27 consolidated EBITDA run-rate, quarterly. About Rs 75 cr per quarter is needed to clear the stated Rs 300 cr floor. Tests the credibility of the "floor" guidance behind the whole growth case.
4. Corporate Learning segment revenue and profit, and any second goodwill impairment, in the segment note. Resolves the ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB versus STRUCTURAL question the transition thesis cannot skip.
5. Unbound Medicine EBITDA margin, in the concall. Confirm at mid-20s or higher by Q2 or Q3 FY27; kill if stuck at 18 to 20 percent through H2 FY27. Tests the acquisition case and integration execution.
6. Standalone versus consolidated revenue growth gap, in the revenue and intercompany notes. If the gap persists or widens beyond FY26's ~19 points, the recharge-driven inflation is systemic, not one year.
7. Amortisation charge on Unbound and AJE intangibles, in the FY27 P&L. A full-year step-up over FY26's partial charge could compress EPS despite real operating growth.
8. ADI BPO amalgamation NCLT outcome, in the scheme note. Any retrospective restatement of FY26 comparatives changes the earnings base and the RPT continuity picture.

## Falsification line

The single next-quarter print that would do the most damage: a Q2 FY27 consolidated EBITDA run-rate materially below about Rs 75 cr, undershooting the Rs 300 cr FY27 floor when H2 FY26 earnings already declined. That one number breaks the highest-conviction guidance the growth case rests on.
