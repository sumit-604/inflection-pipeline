### 4B. Hurdle Ratio at CMP (Tier A threshold 1.953) — applied honestly on the forward EPS

**HR = (1 + EPS CAGR)³ × (Destination PE mid ÷ Current PE)**

- Current PE (forward-consistent) = CMP 152 ÷ forward EPS 11.55 = **13.16x**.
- Destination PE mid = **13.0x** (approved). Ratio 13.0/13.16 = **0.988** → essentially NO re-rating headroom: the stock is already priced at its own forward destination multiple.
- Base EPS CAGR (3-yr, SOM-consistent) = **14%**. Bull EPS CAGR: grade **C**, so bull is capped at Base + 5% = **19%** (Amendment 2 — cannot use face-value guidance on grade C).

| Row | Calculation | HR | vs 1.953 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Base (14%)** | 1.14³ × 0.988 = 1.4815 × 0.988 | **1.46** | FAIL |
| **Bull (19%, capped)** | 1.19³ × 0.988 = 1.6852 × 0.988 | **1.66** | FAIL |
| Memo: even at face-value 25% | 1.25³ × 0.988 = 1.9531 × 0.988 | 1.93 | FAIL |

**HURDLE VERDICT = STOP.** The base fails and the bull fails. Because the destination PE (13x) equals the current forward PE (13.16x), there is no de-rating tailwind; the hurdle can only be cleared by >25% SUSTAINED EPS growth, which grade C and a 13.8% SOM CAGR do not support. Even the guidance-face-value 25% falls just short (1.93). Using the trailing true-share-count PE (152/8.63 = 17.6x) instead makes the ratio a de-rating headwind (13/17.6 = 0.74) and the HR worse — the STOP is robust to the PE-basis choice.
