Destination multiple held at the Section 1B figure (29-30x, using 29.5x); FY28E

| Scenario | FY28 PAT | FY28 EPS | Destination px (x29.5) | 25% CAGR entry | CAGR at Rs446 |

conservative ~0%. So Rs446 is a BET ON DURABILITY, not a floor.

## 5. CAVEATS / NOT FOUND (why this is provisional)

1. Revenue durability — does Rs71.8 Cr/month MRR hold and grow? Zero named
   customers, no contract terms. If it fades, conservative column governs and
   Rs446 is expensive.
2. Margin — held 68-76%; no utilisation % or realised pricing disclosed.
3. Asset life — 6-yr assumed; if GPUs are 3-4 yr assets (B300/Vera Rubin
   roadmap), depreciation higher and PAT lower. Single most dangerous line.
4. NOT FOUND until Q2 FY27 filing: June net debt, operating cash flow, ROCE
   denominator (capital employed) — the last could move the 29-30x multiple in
   a formal Section 1B run.
5. Horizon — used ~2-3 yr FY28 destination (consistent with the "3-year base"
   EV table); the old headline entry Rs1,400-1,700 was a 10-year horizon.
