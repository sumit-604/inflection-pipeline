     JYOTI STRUCTURES LIMITED 
 
 
 
 
 
Item no.1 
 
The Board of Directors at its meeting held on August 14, 2025, approved the allotment of 5,02,333 Equity 
Shares of the Company under the Employee Stock Options granted under JSL ESOS 2021 Scheme. 
Subsequently, upon internal verification, it was observed that an inadvertent error in calculation of 
allotment had occurred, resulting in an incorrect issuance of the said 5,02,333 equity shares. 
 
In view of the above, and in compliance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (Share 
Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021, the said allotment is required to be cancelled 
in entirety. 
 
It is further noted that: 
 
1. The aforesaid equity shares have not been listed on the stock exchanges and therefore no trading has taken 
place in respect of the said shares. 
2. The Distinctive Number Range (DNR) numbers from 119,07,42,805 to 119,12,45,137 generated for the above-
mentioned 5,02,333 shares stand stands cancelled upon cancellation of the allotment. 
3. The Company will take on record the cancellation of the said shares and make necessary filings/disclosures 
with the Stock Exchanges and Depositories as per applicable SEBI regulations.
