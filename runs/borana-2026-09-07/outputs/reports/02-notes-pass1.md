## SPEAR FACT 1: THE 23.6% EBITDA MARGIN AND ITS SOURCE

Cost stack, FY26 vs FY25 (Statement of P&L, Annual_Report_FY2026.pdf p.93; notes 21-28, pp.111-114):
- Revenue from Operations: Rs 38,859.31 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 29,031.04 lakh (FY25), +33.9% (Note 21, p.111)
- Cost of Materials Consumed (100% yarn): Rs 22,777.63 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 17,803.18 lakh (FY25), +27.9%
  (Note 23, p.113)
- Changes in inventories: Rs 7.10 lakh (FY26) vs Rs (29.35) lakh (FY25) (Note 24, p.113)
- Employee Benefit Expenses: Rs 2,814.13 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 1,915.45 lakh (FY25), +46.9% (Note 25, p.113)
- Other Expenses: Rs 4,108.74 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 3,024.08 lakh (FY25), +35.9% (Note 28, p.114)
- Finance Costs: Rs 349.05 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 505.04 lakh (FY25) — DOWN despite the capex ramp (Note 26)
- Depreciation: Rs 1,781.02 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 1,306.03 lakh (FY25) (Note 27, p.114)

Computed operating EBITDA = Revenue - COGS - Employee cost - Other expenses (excludes D&A, finance cost,
other income) = 38,859.31 - (22,777.63 + 7.10 + 2,814.13 + 4,108.74) = Rs 9,151.71 lakh = Rs 91.52 cr.
Margin = 91.52 / 388.59 = 23.55% ≈ 23.6%. 🟢 This independently reconciles the claimed margin from the raw
cost-stack notes; it is not a constructed or adjusted number. (Notes 21, 23, 24, 25, 28, cross-added by this
analyst, source AR pp.93, 111-114)

Source of the spread — established from the notes:
- Raw material mix flipped in FY26: Imported Materials Rs 7,452.74 lakh (32.72% of raw material) vs
  Indigenous Rs 15,324.89 lakh (67.28%). In FY25, imports were NIL (0%), 100% domestic (Note 39(e), p.126).
  Foreign-currency raw material purchases: USD 85.77 lakh (FY26) vs nil (FY25); CIF value of raw material
  imports Rs 7,726.59 lakh (FY26) vs nil (FY25) (Note 39(c)/(d), p.126). 🟡 Watch — this is a first-time,
  material sourcing-mix change in the exact year the margin is being defended; it is evidence-worthy on its
  own, separate from the related-party question (SPEAR fact 2 below).
- Manufacturing/other-expense components (Note 28, p.114): Factory Power Expenses (net) Rs 1,955.77 lakh
  (FY26) vs Rs 1,628.44 lakh (FY25), +20.1% — power cost as % of revenue improved from 5.61% to 5.03%,
  consistent with capacity utilisation gains. Jobwork Expenses Rs 898.81 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 447.73 lakh
  (FY25) [RPT note shows Rs 442.98 lakh, see discrepancy flagged below], +101%/+102% — this entire line is
  paid to related party Borana Industries LLP (see SPEAR fact 2). Millgin Expenses + Millgin (Texturising
  Oil) together Rs 777.34 lakh (FY26) vs Rs 551.71 lakh (FY25), +40.9%, roughly tracking volume.
- Effective tax rate 17.16% both years, consistent with the concessional Section 115BAB new-manufacturing-
  company rate (15% base + surcharge + cess = 17.16%); actual effective rate 17.58% FY26 due to disallowances
  and special-rate income (Note 36, p.120). 🟢 Clean, explains why PAT margin (16.6%) tracks close to PBT
  margin without unusual tax engineering.

CONCLUSION ON FACT 1: The cost-stack notes support the 23.6% margin arithmetically. Two structural changes
sit inside that margin in FY26 specifically: (a) a first-time 32.7% shift to imported yarn, and (b) a
doubling of related-party jobwork spend. Both need to be understood before the margin is read as durable
"weaving margin" rather than a one-year input-mix and outsourcing artefact. 🟡 Watch.
