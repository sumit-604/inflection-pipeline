This brief compares Wednesday with the 29 September brief. It was written on
Thursday, one day late, because the Wednesday run stopped before it saved
anything. The last brief named four ways the cautious view would fail and four
ways it would get worse. None of the eight came true on Wednesday. The
long-line count did not recover. The large-cap list did not reach 60 names. No

## The picture in one paragraph

Wednesday was a quiet repair day with a flat floor. The rush of new yearly lows
eased. Lows fell from 165 to 83, and new highs rose from 40 to 63. Rising and
falling stocks came out close to even. Big risers beat big fallers 88 to 47 for
the second day. The list of stocks with fresh daily momentum nearly doubled.
Almost 200 stocks turned up from a deeply sold reading, and many of them were
large. But the bid inside the day faded. The large-cap list above the day's
average price fell back from 32 names to 17. The count of stocks above the
two-hundred-day line stayed at 971, the lowest since 11 June. The Nifty slipped
again while media, realty, defence and banks rose. Fear stayed just under its
average. The short end is mending. The long floor has stopped falling, but it
has not started to rise.

## Where the market stands

It still sits about six percent above its thirty-week trend line, the furthest

The Nifty closed at about 22,620, down under half a percent. It sits between

long trend. The large end still does not.

their two-hundred-day line. On 15 September came the washout, when 343 stocks
fell four and a half percent in one session. The long count dropped to 1,036.

fifty-day cushion is only about 25 names. The fifty-day line is the hinge
tonight. It has not broken its washout low, and it has not pulled away from it

The loudest change sits in the yearly highs and lows. On Tuesday the market
printed 165 new yearly lows, the most since 30 March. On Wednesday it printed
83. New highs rose to 63, the most since 23 September. Lows still beat highs,

Within thirty percent, the high side still leads. The crowd at the top holds.

Wednesday was an even day under the surface. About 47 stocks in 100 rose and

The day's action showed buyers reaching and then letting go. About one stock in

## What would change this read

The read tonight is cautious, with a short-term repair inside it. The cautious
read fails if the two-hundred-day count climbs back above its washout level of
about 1,035 and stays there. It fails if new yearly highs outnumber new lows for
several sessions. Wednesday came close, at 63 against 83. It fails if the
large-cap list above the day's average price climbs past about 60 and holds. It
also fails if a thrust day arrives, 300 or more stocks jumping four and a half
percent in one session, on volume clearly above its year-ago level. That would
turn the last week into a shakeout inside a base.

The cautious read turns worse, and the constructive case fails, if the
fifty-day count falls under about 705, its washout-day low. That line is now
only about 25 names away. It fails if the twenty-day count falls back under
about 475. It fails if a second washout arrives, 300 or more stocks falling four
and a half percent in one session. It also fails if new yearly lows climb back
above about 165 while the VIX rises above its thirty-week average of about 13.5.
The two-hundred-day count is the first place the damage would show. It held flat
on Wednesday.
