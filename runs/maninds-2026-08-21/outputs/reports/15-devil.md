## SEEDED DIMENSION 1 — SELLER PRICE SIGNAL (NPC mature-revenue and margin)

**The bull/base assumption under attack.** Base case carries NPC at Rs2,800cr FY29 at 16% EBITDA (operator base, deliberation ledger line 64), already haircut from management's Rs3,500-4,000cr peak guide. That base drives FY30E base operating EPS Rs71.9 and the Rs611 RRM base fair value (B11 Section 3).

**The bear counter.** Nippon Steel-Sumitomo plus Saudi shareholders sold a debt-free asset earning ~USD52m EBITDA and ~USD38m PAT for USD102m with USD83m liquid assets included (dossier §2, [FILED press 21-May-2026]). That is ~2x CY25 PAT with more than 80% of the price back in cash on day one. Sophisticated industrial sellers do not exit a growing asset at 2x PAT. Two readings, both bearish: CY25 (25% OPBDIT, ~Rs2,000cr revenue, [AGENCY CRISIL]) was a cycle peak, or the asset carries a problem the sellers wanted gone. Management's own forward guide confirms the peak reading — FY27 Rs1,500-2,000cr at "15%+", flat-to-DOWN on CY25 delivered, and the sustainable band is guided 15-18% (dossier §2, [MGMT Q4FY26]). The deal was sold on 20-25% EBITDA; the honest number is 15-18% (B12b finding, Q4FY26 concall p.16).

**Converter test (v3.7 Amendment 17 — is the current margin the company or the cycle?).** Steel plate is the input. JPC Delhi 10mm plate 5-year range ~Rs45,000-87,320/t; Jun-2026 Rs68,610 sits ~55th percentile; the all-time high was Rs87,320 Apr-2022 (dossier §V4, [SECONDARY JPC/CEIC]). At the 5-year median input (~Rs66,000-68,000, close to spot), the standalone converter does NOT earn the Q1FY27 15.3% it just printed. That 15.3% came from tenders bid Jan-Apr on multi-year-low HRC (Rs47,500-49,500/t early 2026) now executing while HRC has run to Rs55,000-60,450 (dossier §V4, +15-20% in six months). CRISIL puts historical OPBDIT at 8-10% and FY26 at 12.3% (dossier §V4, [AGENCY]). The base case assumes 14-15% EBITDA sustaining through FY30 (B11 Section 2). That is above through-cycle, on a fixed-price tender book with no pass-through on a 2-4 month lead. The current margin is the cycle, not the company.

**What breaks if the seed is right.** If NPC matures at the bear Rs2,000cr @15% (CY25 never improves — the operator's own bear anchor, deliberation line 64) AND the standalone converter reverts to 11-12% through-cycle, the honest scenario is the current BEAR, not the base. Bear FY30E EPS is Rs42.0; bear RRM fair value is Rs340 (B11 Section 3). The base Rs611 is then optimistic by ~44%.

**VERDICT: WEAKENED (toward the bear).** The NPC ramp is real (completed, exchange-filed acquisition, Aramco vendor). The MAGNITUDE the base assumes is not. The seller-price signal and the converter cycle test together move the honest base from Rs611 toward the bear Rs340. Strongest single counter: a sophisticated seller exited at ~2x CY25 PAT and management guides the mature margin 700-1000bps below CY25 delivered — the base case pays for a margin the vendor was selling away from.

## SEEDED DIMENSION 9 — MARKET ALREADY PAYING ~83% (is any upside left?)

**The assumption under attack.** Thesis B says patience earns the entry: wait for Rs219-313, buy Small, ride fair value to Rs611. It assumes the stock will visit the zone and that the AVOID is a temporary state.

**The bear counter (market is right, twice).** At Rs714 the stock trades 17.7x forward FY27E operating EPS (B11 Section 4) against an earned converter destination of 8.1-12.6x (B11 Section 1B). The market-implied success probability (~83%) exceeds the analyst reference (65-70%, deliberation line 44). Invert it as the framework demands (Section 4, Variant Perception): what does the market see? NPC's 25% CY25 margins, the direct five-year Aramco Asia MoU (Nov-2025, dossier §V2 [FILED]), the in-kingdom LSAW-plus-HSAW edge peers' Saudi HSAW plants do not hold (S3), the Rs470cr Ind AS 103 bargain gain, and named institutional entry (Kacholia, Carnelian, Ovata via the Rs255cr preferential, dossier §V5). If the market is right that NPC sustains nearer 20%, the base is too conservative and Rs714 is closer to fair — which kills the AVOID's implied "wait for a cheaper price."

**But the same seed kills Thesis B harder than it kills Thesis A.** Two ways:
1. No margin of safety survives at CMP. Even if the market's 83% is correct, you are paying full price for a converter with three open regulator findings against a sitting Chairman and MD (V5). Upside/downside at CMP is 0.69x (B11 Section 4), below 2x. Paying 83% for the good case and eating the full governance tail is a poor trade regardless of who is right on NPC.
2. The zone may never print. If the market keeps paying ~83%, the stock does not visit Rs313, let alone Rs219. Rs313 is a 56% drawdown from Rs714. The plan "buy Small at Rs219-313" is then a permanent bench: a watchlist item whose trigger never fires. The constructive tail is un-actionable unless a governance shock or a filed India-book collapse drives the de-rating — and the entry conjunction (14-thesis Section 7) correctly withdraws the zone in exactly that case. So the only paths into the zone are the paths that also break the thesis.

**VERDICT: SURVIVES for the AVOID (no upside at CMP is confirmed and reinforced); WEAKENED for the constructive tail.** The AVOID call is correct and the seed strengthens it. But the "Small BUY at Rs219-313" is either never reached or reached only through a thesis-breaking event, so it is close to un-investable in practice. Strongest single counter: at Rs714 there is no margin of safety on either reading of NPC, and the only routes to the entry zone are the routes that void it.

## SEEDED DIMENSION 10 — WORKING CAPITAL SCALING (cash conversion and the ROCE gradient)

**The assumption under attack.** The transition-alpha core is the corrected ROCE walk climbing 13.8% (FY26) to 16.5% (FY29 base), which lifts Pillar 1 from 14.5x to 15.75x and the destination PE gradient with it (B11 Step C, Section 1B). The only capital-employed change the correction made was adding the Rs470cr bargain gain to CE from FY27 (B11 Step C, stated explicitly as "the ONLY change").

**The bear counter — capital-employed inflation defers the ROCE improvement even if EBIT arrives.** NPC's own working capital runs USD100-125m on a USD200m topline, ~50-62% of revenue, ~Rs500-625cr per Rs1,000cr of NPC revenue (transcript p.15, anchor A6 [MGMT]). At the base NPC Rs2,800cr FY29, that is Rs1,400-1,750cr of working capital for the NPC line alone. Stack that on the India standalone book, which CRISIL describes as structurally working-capital-intensive with gross-current-asset days ~200 and debtors over 100 days "in the past" (dossier §V3 [AGENCY]). The corrected base CE walk shows FY29 CE at Rs5,370cr (B11 Step C). If the NPC WC scaling is even partly under-modelled — add a conservative Rs500-700cr of incremental NPC working capital as the line ramps from ~Rs1,500cr (FY27) to Rs2,800cr (FY29) — FY29 ROCE falls from 16.5% toward 885 / (5,370 + 600) = ~14.8%.

**What that does to the gradient.** At ~14.8% FY29, the base Pillar 1 anchor (midpoint of 13.95% and the FY29 figure) drops from 15.2% to ~14.4%; Pillar 1 from 15.1x to ~14.7x; base additive from 12.1x to ~11.8x; base RRM from 8.5x to ~8.2x. The "transition" 13.8 -> 16.5 becomes 13.8 -> ~14.8 — a flat-to-slightly-up line, not a re-rating driver. The COMPOUNDER label (FV CAGR 21.2%) is carried by EPS growth on the NPC ramp, not by ROCE (B11 Am19 decomposition already concedes "no re-rating lever"). But the ENTRY ZONE rests on the base FV Rs611, and Rs611 is anchored to the base destination PE 8.5x on the 15.2% ROCE midpoint. Knock the FY29 ROCE to ~14.8% and the base destination compresses toward the bear card, dragging the base FV toward Rs450-500 and the entry zone below Rs219-313.

**Cash conversion, separately.** Cumulative WC days rose 116 (FY24) to 261 (FY26); consolidated CFO/PAT was 0.44x FY25 (dossier §V3 [FILED]); the 6m-1y receivables bucket rose 11.5x to Rs9,182 lakh; Rs95.58cr of disputed >3-year receivables sit at ~13.6% ECL (§V3 KAM). CRISIL's "normalises FY27" is tied to two named one-off shipments, not a structural change — and CARE-style structural WC assessment takes precedence over single-year improvements (framework Pillar 2 rule). Pillar 2 is already 0.80x with NO growth offset (S1). The devil's point: the normalisation removes a penalty; it does not earn the base case a clean, self-funding ramp. NPC's own WC intensity means the more NPC grows, the more cash the group locks up — the ramp that drives the EPS also starves the ROCE.

**VERDICT: DESTROYED.** This is the sharpest attack because it hits the transition premise directly and quantifiably. The base case adds Rs470cr of bargain gain to the denominator but does not appear to load the NPC WC scaling its own transcript disclosed. Load it, and the ROCE gradient that justifies the Pillar 1 uplift largely disappears. The "13.8 to 16.5 transition" is the thing being bought; on the company's own WC-per-revenue number it flattens toward ~14.8%. Strongest single counter: NPC needs ~Rs1,400-1,750cr of working capital at the base FY29 revenue, and the corrected CE walk changed CE only for the Rs470cr gain — so the ROCE gradient double-counts an EBIT lift the balance sheet does not fund.

## 7. PRE-MORTEM

It is August 2028. A holder who bought a Small position in the zone in late 2026 is down 40%. What went wrong, in the actual numbers.

The Q2FY27 half-year print (14-Nov-2026) confirmed two of the three fears at once. India standalone printed Rs2,150cr, below the Rs2,300cr break line, and the 6m-1y receivables bucket grew again rather than shrinking toward 100 days — CRISIL's "normalises FY27" narrative did not hold on the first real balance-sheet test, because it was tied to two shipments, not a structural fix. Consolidated EBITDA slid to 12.4% as the tenders bid on Rs48k HRC executed on Rs58k plate. The stock, which had drifted into the low Rs300s on the falling book (which is why the buyer entered), kept going.

Through FY28 the NPC ramp underdelivered the base. Utilisation reached ~60%, not 80-85%, so NPC revenue landed near Rs2,100cr, not Rs2,800cr, at 15% not 16% — exactly the seller-price signal the deal price had flagged. Worse, the NPC working capital scaled as its own transcript warned: ~Rs1,200cr locked into the Saudi line, so consolidated ROCE never left the 13-14% band. The transition the buyer paid for did not arrive; the balance sheet funded the EBIT growth away.

In March-2028 SAT upheld the SEBI findings with narrowed directions (the 45% base case), and the Limitless loan still sat undisclosed in the FY27 AR — the named thesis-breaker. The governance cap that kept the position Small was the only thing that limited the damage to a Small position. The buyer had waited for the zone, met the India-book condition on a brief head-fake quarter, and still lost, because the base fair value Rs611 the entry was reverse-engineered from was a cycle-peak number the WC drag was always going to defeat.
