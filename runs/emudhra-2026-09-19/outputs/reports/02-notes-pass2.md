## NEW FINDING 1 — Unbilled revenue is a large, fast-growing, previously unflagged working-capital item (HIGH SIGNIFICANCE)

Pass 1 covered trade receivables (Note 10/42) in detail but did not surface "unbilled revenue," which sits in a different note and is comparable in scale to trade receivables itself.

**Consolidated Note 8, "Other current assets" (p.248-249)**: Unbilled revenue Rs 1,033.49 Mn (FY26, "Others" portion) vs Rs 734.30 Mn (FY25), +40.8%. Total "Other current assets" Rs 1,188.16 Mn (FY26) vs Rs 812.78 Mn (FY25), +46.2% — unbilled revenue is 87.0% of this line (FY26) / 90.3% (FY25), i.e. it is the line.

**Related-party unbilled revenue**: Rs 1.99 Mn (FY26) vs Rs 2.20 Mn (FY25), Smart Craft Private Limited (Note 8(i), cross-ref Note 44.2, p.269).

**Total unbilled revenue (Others + related party)**: Rs 1,035.48 Mn (FY26) vs Rs 736.50 Mn (FY25), **+40.6% YoY, ahead of the 35.1% revenue growth print**. This total is independently cross-confirmed twice more: (a) Note 42's trade-receivables-ageing footnote (p.268) states "the above amount does not include unbilled revenue of Rs. 736.50 [FY25] ... disclosed under note 8"; (b) Note 48(i) Credit risk table (p.286) shows the identical pair, Rs 1,035.48 Mn / Rs 736.50 Mn, and its own internal total — Trade receivables gross Rs 1,935.07 Mn + Unbilled revenue Rs 1,035.48 Mn − ECL Rs 42.94 Mn = Rs 2,927.61 Mn — reconciles to the penny against the FY25 equivalent (Rs 1,481.20 Mn + Rs 736.50 Mn − Rs 26.53 Mn = Rs 2,191.17 Mn). Three independent notes, same figure. High confidence.

**Accounting policy** (Note 23(iii)(j)-(k), p.260): "Unbilled revenue comprising revenue in excess of billing where the right to consideration is unconditional and is due only after passage of time. Unbilled revenue is recognised based on the satisfaction of performance obligations which is measured based on the satisfaction of the internal milestones by the company using the input method (i.e., resources consumed, costs incurred)." This is a judgment-heavy, percentage-of-completion-style estimate — management determines "internal milestones" and measures cost-based progress. The auditor's sole Key Audit Matter (both reports) is revenue recognition, specifically citing "judgements used in determining the satisfaction of those performance obligations over time or at a point in time" (p.199-201/291-293) — unbilled revenue sits squarely inside that KAM's scope, even though the KAM text does not name it separately.

**Standalone (parent-only)**: "Total other current assets" Rs 562.09 Mn (FY26) vs Rs 370.36 Mn (FY25), +51.8% YoY (Note 8, p.336-337) — directionally consistent and growing even faster at the parent level, but the extraction scrambles the component rows (Advance to suppliers / Others / Prepaid / Contract cost / Balance with govt authorities / Unbilled revenue all interleaved) enough that I will not force a precise standalone unbilled-revenue split. ## NEW FINDING 2 — Cryptas 49% put/call option carries a revenue FLOOR, not only an uncapped EBITDA ceiling

Pass 1 flagged (rank 1) that the 49%-stake put/call option (exercisable 2028-2030, priced at 10x trailing-four-quarter EBITDA) has "no cap disclosed." Re-reading Note 4a(c) in full (p.243) surfaces a detail Pass 1's summary did not carry: the option is **"subject to a minimum of one time revenue during the same period"** — i.e., a floor as well as an open-ended ceiling. Quote in full: "a Put/Call option on the remaining 49% exercisable from 2028 to 2030 is also reserved based on enterprise value at 10 times [EBITDA] ... for the immediately preceding four quarters before the date on which such Put/Call option is exercised subject to a minimum of one time revenue during the same period."

🟡 WATCH — sharpens, does not change, Pass 1's red flag. Even if Cryptas's EBITDA margin compresses or turns negative in the trailing four quarters before exercise, the buy-in price for the remaining 49% cannot fall below 1x that period's revenue. This bounds the downside scenario for the eventual cash outflow (useful for Stage 11) but confirms there is no scenario in which the 49% stake is acquired cheaply if Cryptas underperforms on margin — only if it underperforms on revenue too.

## NEW FINDING 4 — MSME payables ageing ambiguity from Pass 1 is now SETTLED

- FY26 row-set summing to exactly Rs 68.06 Mn (Note 40's MSME total): Not due Rs 35.93 Mn, <1yr Rs 8.22 Mn, 1-2yr Nil, 2-3yr Rs 2.30 Mn, **>3yr Rs 21.61 Mn**. This is the MSME row (confirms Pass 1's reading).
- FY26 remaining row-set, Rs 532.86 Mn: Not due Rs 495.53 Mn, <1yr Rs 21.28 Mn, 1-2yr Rs 11.77 Mn, 2-3yr Rs 4.28 Mn, **>3yr Nil**. This is the non-MSME ("Others") row.
- Grand total: Rs 68.06 Mn + Rs 532.86 Mn = **Rs 600.92 Mn**, tying exactly to Note 18's trade payables total and to the Note 47 Financial Instruments table's trade-payables line (p.285) and the Note 48 liquidity-risk maturity table (p.286) — three independent notes, same figure, high confidence. **This directly settles the task's instruction to confirm the Rs 600.92 Mn payables figure: consolidated trade payables total is Rs 600.92 Mn (FY26) / Rs 314.34 Mn (FY25), cross-tied across Note 18, Note 43, Note 47 and Note 48.**
- FY25 row-set summing to Rs 28.79 Mn (Note 40's FY25 MSME total): Not due Rs 3.66 Mn, <1yr Rs 1.22 Mn, 1-2yr Nil, 2-3yr Rs 2.30 Mn, **>3yr Rs 21.61 Mn** — identical to FY26. Non-MSME row (Rs 209.96 + 49.92 + 25.09 + 0.58 + Nil = Rs 285.55 Mn) has **>3yr Nil** both years.
- Standalone (Note 45, p.351-352): the MSME row-set is identical, Rs 68.06 Mn (FY26) / Rs 28.79 Mn (FY25) including the same Rs 21.61 Mn >3yr figure — confirming Pass 1's finding that all group MSME exposure sits at the Indian parent.
