Management Commentary
   Q2 FY27 marked a record quarter for Karnika Industries, with the highest-
   ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA and PAT, reflecting strong business
   momentum.
   India’s branded kidswear market presents a significant long-term opportunity,
   supported by rising brand awareness and the shift towards organised retail.
   Our diversified brand portfolio, integrated manufacturing capabilities and
   expanding pan-India presence position us to capitalise on this opportunity.
   Our integrated B2B and B2C strategy remains central to our growth
   roadmap, combining established sourcing and distribution capabilities with an
   expanding retail and omnichannel presence to strengthen consumer reach and
   create additional growth avenues.
