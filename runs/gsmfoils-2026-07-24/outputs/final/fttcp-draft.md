## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue.** This is where the evidence is real. The Ahmedabad plant is running
and disclosed monthly revenue, ROPP caps started commercial production in June
2026, and Q1 FY27 already prints Rs 97 Cr. What confirms it: quarterly revenue
climbing toward Rs 110 Cr. What kills it: revenue flat near Rs 97 Cr through H1
FY27. Management guides Rs 400 to 450 Cr for FY27, and the exit run rate roughly
supports it, though the guidance has no cross quarter track record yet.

**Margin.** The catalysts for expanding margin past 11.5% are all words, not
documented moves. Ahmedabad is said to carry lower overheads, ROPP caps a higher
margin, and forward integration into specialised printing is claimed to add 8 to
10 points on that line by end FY27. None is a signed or commissioned fact yet,
and management itself guides only to sustain, not expand. What confirms it: a
disclosed higher margin revenue line actually printing. What kills it: aluminium
falls and the inventory gain reverses, taking margin below 10%.

**Cash conversion.** The catalyst search returns nothing documented. There is no
factoring facility, no debtor day target hit in reported numbers, and the one
funding move is a working capital loan that adds debt. Management raised the Q4
receivables spike to Rs 94 Cr as a one time letter of credit and currency delay
and said Rs 30 to 40 Cr was collected by mid April, but that is operator relayed,
not in any filing, and cash questions were deflected on two calls. Absence of a
plan across two concalls is the loudest signal in the protocol. What confirms a
turn: a statutory cash flow statement showing CFO near PAT. What kills it:
receivables holding above Rs 80 Cr into Q2 FY27.

**ROCE.** The counter catalysts dominate. Fresh rights equity and new unit capex
sit in the base before they earn, so the near term pressure is down, not up. What
would help: the new units earning fast on a base that stops growing. What hurts:
another capital raise or a further working capital build.

**Intent and action ledger.** On every weak pillar management has vision and no
documented action. Cash has a normalization promise and no facility. Margin has a
forward integration promise and no commissioned line. ROCE has a scale up promise
and a denominator that is bloating. The ledger confirms the conservative reads
and does not loosen any verdict.
