Executive Chairperson, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, said, "Syngene's strategic priority remains to
strengthen its position as an integrated research, development and manufacturing partner to the
global life sciences industry, with a renewed emphasis on differentiated, high-value services and
CDMO capabilities as key drivers of long-term growth.

It is encouraging to see early signs of progress in this direction, with improved performance in our
CDMO business and growing customer engagement across our development and manufacturing
services. This reinforces our confidence in the long-term potential of our investments in global
manufacturing infrastructure, advanced capabilities and emerging modalities."

Commenting on the performance, Managing Director and CEO, Siddharth Mittal said, "Our second
quarter saw sequential revenue growth of 13%, supported by positive traction in our CDMO
business. Operating EBITDA margin improved by 6 percentage points, while PAT stood at Rs.32
crore, compared with a loss of Rs.9 crore in the preceding quarter.
[page 3]
  On a year-on-year basis, revenue declined 9%, while operating EBITDA margin stood at 18%, in line
  with expectations, primarily reflecting lower Zoetis revenues and forex hedge losses. Adjusted for
  the forex hedge loss, the operating EBITDA margin would be in the mid-20s range for the quarter.

  A key highlight of the quarter was the signing of our first manufacturing agreement for the Bayview
  biologics facility in the U.S., strengthening our global manufacturing capabilities.

  Our focus remains on enhancing our integrated drug discovery offering, sharpening our commercial
  execution, improving asset utilization and cost competitiveness, and translating our scientific
  capabilities into sustainable business growth."

  Key Business Highlights:

 Integrated Drug Discovery (IDD): Syngene strengthened its integrated drug discovery offering
    by bringing its Chemistry, Biology and Biotherapeutics capabilities under a unified leadership.
    Effective October 1, 2026, Dr. Jayashree Aiyar, Chief Scientific Officer, assumed additional
    responsibility for Discovery Services. The move is aimed at delivering a more comprehensive
    drug discovery offering to customers.

    As part of this transition, Subhendu Kumar, Head of Chemistry and Priyaranjan Pattanaik, Head
    of Biology, will be moving on from Syngene at the end of November and December respectively.

 Bayview Biologics Facility: Syngene signed its first manufacturing agreement for the Bayview
    biologics facility with a large pharmaceutical customer, marking an important milestone in the
    Company's large molecule manufacturing journey. The engagement strengthens Syngene's
    integrated India-U.S. delivery model, with process development activities scheduled to begin in
    India this fiscal, and drug substance manufacturing at Bayview expected to begin in the next
    financial year. The agreement reinforces the facility's strategic role in enabling dual-continent
    supply chains for global biologics customers.

 Biologics Manufacturing: The Company continued to secure additional manufacturing contracts,
    including an agreement for clinical supplies from its biologics manufacturing facility in Bengaluru
    (Unit 3). These engagements reflect encouraging customer traction and progress in building a
    broader pipeline for our manufacturing business.

 Emerging Modalities: Syngene successfully completed a complex oligonucleotide manufacturing
    program for a leading global pharmaceutical company. The engagement involved the
    development and manufacture of a siRNA molecule requiring specialized expertise in
    oligonucleotide technology, process optimization and scale-up, underscoring the Company's
    track record in delivering high-value programs for global clients.

 Capability Enhancement: Syngene commissioned a new Clinical Translational Sciences (CTS)
    Laboratory in Bengaluru, bringing together clinical biomarker development and validation,
    biobanking, patient stratification, precision medicine and bioanalytical capabilities within an
    integrated ecosystem. The facility strengthens Syngene's ability to connect discovery research,
    development, bioanalysis and clinical research, supporting clients across the drug development
    continuum.

 Artificial Intelligence and Automation: Continuing its investments in AI, automation and digital
    technologies, Syngene strengthened its digital capabilities across Discovery Services. The
    Company implemented ChemVoyager, an AI-enabled synthesis planning platform, alongside
[page 4]
    automated analytical systems and end-to-end sample tracking solutions, enabling more
    connected, efficient and data-driven research workflows.

 Cost Competitiveness: Syngene continued to implement various cost optimization initiatives
    aimed at improving operational efficiency. Manpower related cost optimization has been
    completed, while initiatives focused on non-manpower costs will continue. The benefits from
    these measures will be realized over the coming quarters.

 Recognition for Digital Innovation: Syngene's Central Compound Management (CCM) team
    received the Platinum Award at the 12th CII National Kaizen Circle Competition 2026. The
    recognition was awarded for a digital innovation initiative that enhanced automation,
    strengthened data integrity and improved operational efficiency across compound management
    workflows.

 Leadership Transition: Deepak Jain, Chief Financial Officer, has decided to move on from
    Syngene for personal reasons, and will pursue an opportunity outside the Company. Deepak will
    continue in his current role, at least until the close of the third quarter's financial results, to
    support a smooth transition of his responsibilities and ensure continuity. The Company thanks
    Deepak for his contributions and wishes him success in his future endeavours.

Note 12. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified the four Labour Codes - the Code on Wages, 2019, the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, the Code on Social Security, 2020, and the
Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 - consolidating 29 existing labour laws. The Ministry of Labour & Employment published draft Central Rules and Frequently Asked
Questions(FAQs) to enable assessment of the financial impact due to changes in regulations. The Group initially assessed and disclosed the incremental impact of these changes on the basis its existing
remuneration structure. Considering the materiality and regulatory-driven, non-recurring nature of this impact, the Group presented such incremental impact under "Exceptional Items" in the standalone and
consolidated financial results for the period ended December 31, 2025. The incremental expense consisting of gratuity of Rs. 658 million and Rs. 706 million in the standalone and consolidated financial
results respectively primarily arose due to change in wage definition.
During the quarter ended 31 March 2026, the management reassessed the impact of the new labour codes based on the revised remuneration structure. Accordingly, the gratuity obligation was
remeasured, resulting in a credit of Rs. 229 million in the standalone financial results and Rs. 244 million in the consolidated financial results for the current quarter.
For the year ended 31 March 2026, the net expense recognised under Exceptional Items amounted to Rs. 429 million and Rs. 462 million in the standalone and consolidated financial results, respectively.
The Group continues to monitor the finalisation of Central / State Rules and clarifications from the Government on other aspects of the Labour Code and would accordingly take necessary steps for
compliance thereof and also provide appropriate accounting effect on the basis of such developments, as needed.
