# FTTCP Gate Recommendation (Phase 1, evidence only)

## VERDICT: PROCEED WITH CAVEATS

⚠️ PROMOTER FLAG: CONCERN. Top findings: (1) Attempted transfer of the B2C consumer business to the founder's son Rohan Verma via a 10% stake plus a Rs35 Cr convertible debenture, reversed within about 24 hours of investor pushback (Nov to Dec 2024); InGovern publicly rebuked the board and the stock fell about 25% (B08). (2) ClarityX Analytics and Zenithra Tech, both promoter family directed, grew related party transaction volume 638% YoY (Rs0.83 Cr to Rs6.13 Cr FY25) against Rs24+ Cr of Board approved but mostly unused ceilings, disclosed only as "entities with common director" (B08, B02, B03). Additional: 74 year old sole MD reappointed to 2030 with no succession plan and the CEO seat vacated with no external replacement; Rs142.6 Cr promoter block deal sale (B08).
Transition evidence: institutional ownership rose +1.89pp to 13.34% (FY24 11.45%); board reversed the Rs35 Cr self dealing commitment within about 24 hours; statutory auditor rotated to MSKA & Associates (BDO affiliate); new independent director Dr Ranjan Kumar Mohapatra joined 9 May 2025; promoter pledge remains 0% (B08).

⚠️ CASH CONVERSION FLAG: consolidated trade receivables +32% (Rs133 Cr to Rs176 Cr) on flat revenue (+2.3%); WC days 85 to 101; CFO fell Rs113 Cr to Rs93 Cr; CFO/PAT 0.77x; other income above 20% of PBT.
Determination: INDETERMINATE.
Evidence and weighing: the build cannot be GROWTH-INDUCED because revenue was flat, so a 32% receivables rise is not explained by scale (B01 Block B trend; B04; B05). It is concentrated almost entirely at the Gtropy hardware and IoT subsidiary, whose overdraft is "fully utilised" and grew +50.1% YoY, while the standalone map and data core ECL charge actually improved -34.8% (B02, B03). It has NO peer analogue: Newgen DSO held 123 to 125 days and Nucleus receivables declined, so this is company specific, not the industry wide government payment cycle (B06 contradicted). Whether the Gtropy build is a transient hardware cycle working capital swing or a permanent structural deterioration cannot be resolved, because there is NO credit rating rationale PDF and NO FY26 receivables ageing schedule in the record (B00 input_gaps; B02, B03 input_gaps). Per the CLAUDE.md NEVER rule and Section 4, INDETERMINATE never silently resolves to PROCEED and caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS with the missing evidence named. The missing evidence is: the credit rating rationale (obtain from the rating agency press release / BSE filing) and the FY26 consolidated receivables ageing schedule with standalone versus subsidiary ECL split (obtain from the FY26 annual report notes 8/23/29).
Resolving metric (to close INDETERMINATE toward structural or benign): the Q1 FY27 consolidated trade receivables print. If receivables rise further above the ~Rs176 Cr FY26 close on flat or declining sequential revenue with the >6 month ageing bucket expanding, treat as STRUCTURAL; if receivables fall back toward the FY24 base as Gtropy hardware sales normalise, treat as a growth or cycle swing.
