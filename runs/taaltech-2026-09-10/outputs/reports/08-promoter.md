## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A Related-party transaction history — the Rs10 crore Vishkul loan
This is the report's central finding.

**What is disclosed, and where:**
- Note 37 (Standalone Financial Statements), "Related party disclosures":
  under "Entities under common control," Vishkul Enterprises Pvt Ltd —
  "Loans given to related parties during the year: Rs1,000.00 lakh"
  (Rs10cr) and "Interest income: Rs98.55 lakh" for FY26 (nil in FY25) (✅
  AR). Derived rate ≈ 9.855% on a full-year basis; exact tenure/dates are
  not disclosed, so the true annualised rate could differ if the loan
  was outstanding for less than the full year.
- Note 39 (Fair value hierarchy): "Loans" of Rs1,000.00 lakh appear as a
  Level 3 financial asset at amortised cost, closing balance equal to
  the full amount advanced (i.e., outstanding at year-end) (✅ AR).
- CARO Annexure A, clause 3(iii)(a): explicitly names Vishkul Enterprises
  Pvt Ltd, discloses the Rs1,000 lakh amount granted and the Rs1,000 lakh
  balance outstanding at balance-sheet date (✅ AR).
- CARO Annexure A, clause 3(iii)(c): states repayment schedule "has been
  stipulated" and "repayments of principal amounts and receipt of
  interest are regular as per stipulation" — implying a formal repayment
  schedule exists (✅ AR), but **no tenure, no contractual rate, and no
  security are stated anywhere in the AR for this specific loan.**
- CARO Annexure A, clause 3(iii)(d), four sub-clauses later in the same
  annexure: "the Company has not given any advances in the nature of
  loans to any party during the year." **This directly contradicts
  clause 3(iii)(a) in the same document.** Read most charitably, this is
  a template/boilerplate drafting error by the statutory auditor (TLB &
  Co.) — the loan is disclosed prominently elsewhere in the same
  annexure and in two notes to the financial statements, so this does
  not look like concealment. It is nonetheless a genuine internal
  inconsistency in a signed audit annexure and is flagged as a
  documentation-quality issue on the auditor's work product.
- Note 37(C), "Terms and conditions of transactions with related
  parties": states generically that related-party balances are
  "unsecured and interest free except for borrowings" — again in tension
  with the fact that interest of Rs98.55 lakh WAS charged and received on
  this specific loan. Likely boilerplate not updated for this
  transaction, same pattern as above.
- **Form No. AOC-2 (Annexure "E" to the Board's Report), signed by Salil
  Taneja, 6 Aug 2026**: both Part 1 (not-at-arm's-length contracts) and
  Part 2 (material arm's-length contracts) are filled in as "NA" across
  every field — name of related party, nature, duration, value, board
  approval date, all "NA." **The Rs10cr Vishkul loan does not appear in
  AOC-2 at all**, despite being disclosed in CARO and in two financial
  statement notes of the same annual report. AOC-2 is the statutory
  Section 134(3)(h) related-party disclosure; its blank treatment of
  this transaction, next to CARO's explicit disclosure of the same
  transaction, is the clearest documentary inconsistency found in this
  review. It may be technically defensible if the company's Board-approved
  RPT materiality threshold is set high enough that Rs10cr falls below
  it and the loan is treated as "ordinary course, arm's length" (which
  would exempt it from AOC-2 Part 2 disclosure under the LODR
  materiality carve-out) — but whether lending Rs10cr to one's own
  unlisted holding company is "ordinary course of business" for an
  engineering/IT-services company is itself a judgment call the AR does
  not make explicit.
- **No Reg 30 disclosure or shareholder vote found.** The AR's General
  Body Meetings / Postal Ballot section lists only one FY26 postal
  ballot — the company name change (notice 10 Sept 2025) — and no special
  resolution covering the Vishkul loan. The Board's Report states in
  general terms that RPT approvals were taken "from the Audit Committee,
  Board and Members respectively" "as applicable," but no member
  (shareholder) approval event for this specific transaction is
  documented anywhere in the AR. Multiple web searches for a standalone
  Reg 30 filing on this loan failed to run or returned no on-topic
  result; direct fetch of candidate BSE filing PDFs returned HTTP 403.
  **STATUS: UNRESOLVED** — cannot confirm or rule out that a Reg 30
  disclosure exists.
- **Interest-rate reasonableness**: ~9.855% is within the broad range of
  typical unsecured short-term inter-corporate/FD rates in India in
  2025-26; it is not obviously a below-market "sweetheart" rate on the
  evidence available. The concern here is disclosure completeness and
  process, not an obviously concessional rate.

**The audit-committee conflict:**
Narayan Karbhase is simultaneously (a) a Non-Executive, Non-Independent
Director and Audit Committee member of TAAL Tech (the lender) from 4 Aug
2025, and (b) a director of Vishkul Enterprises Private Limited (the
borrower and 50.74% promoter shareholder of TAAL Tech), per the AGM
director-profile annexure's own "Directorship held in other Companies"
listing (✅ AR — this is TAAL Tech's own filed disclosure, not an
external inference). The AR nowhere states that Karbhase declared this
interest under Section 184 of the Companies Act, nor that he recused
from any Board or Audit Committee discussion of the loan. Because AOC-2
shows no approval trail at all for this transaction, this report cannot
