### 6B Classification: CAUTION
Decisive evidence: no integrity-level event (no pledge, no SEBI, no criminal, no auditor exit), but a cluster of six structure-level cautions: the long-running Thane land PILs on the flagship asset, the new promoter-to-subsidiary Sulcus deal with goodwill and assumed debt, promoter-family and board fee flows, and CFO turnover inside 13 months.

Why not TRUSTWORTHY: the three land PILs and the Sulcus transaction are unresolved and each has a named observation that would clear it. Why not CONCERN: every adverse item is disclosed by the company itself, the sums are small, and I found nothing that suggests concealment.
