### 2C Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic)

- Capex under execution: CWIP Rs 30.69 Cr (H1 results p.7, Rs lakh 3,069.27). Kutch and Bay-4 are already in PPE, so they are not in this base.
- Historical fixed asset turnover: FY26 revenue Rs 143.27 Cr / closing net block Rs 136.2 Cr (PPE 135.9 + intangible 0.3; H1 Pres. slide 26) = 1.05x. FY25 78.6 / 59.2 = 1.33x. FY24 54.1 / 63.3 = 0.85x. Latest year used: 1.05x.
- Implied incremental revenue: 30.69 x 1.05 = Rs 32.3 Cr, equal to **22.5% above FY26 revenue** (Rs 143.27 Cr), and 6.8% of the FY27 guidance midpoint of Rs 475 Cr.
- Separate headroom arithmetic on capacity already built [INFER]: 612 towers x FY26 revenue per tower of Rs 0.68 Cr = Rs 417 Cr; at the H1 FY27 realisation of Rs 1.44 Cr per tower (153.97 / 107) the same count gives Rs 881 Cr. Both are ceilings at 100% nameplate and not forecasts. They show the growth is a utilisation and mix ramp on built assets, not a capex ramp.
