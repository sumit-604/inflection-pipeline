# Forward view: 2026-10-18 shallow screen, eight names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework (operator ruling 2026-09-21). Next
year's bottom line, the growth rate, and the forward price-to-earnings ratio, so
the run can be ranked on growth against multiple.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result. Two names below screen cheap and mislead, and
each is marked.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the screener.in company page fetched on 18 October
2026, held at `screens/corpus/<TICKER>/market-data-2026-10-18.md`. It is a
service record, not a filed document. This run used screener.in, not Bull AI,
because the corpus was downloaded directly and no Bull AI call was needed. Every
earnings figure traces to a filing, cited in the card.

## The two that mislead

- **MARUSHIKA** screens cheapest. FY26 operating cash flow was Rs 1.90 crore
  against profit of Rs 9.03 crore, receivables sit at 180 days, and the auditor's
  CARO annex flags an undocumented interest-free loan to an MD-owned company.
  The guide is real; the profit is not yet cash.
- **CALSOFT** shows +58% growth on one quarter annualised. The auditor qualified
  the FY26 opinion: it says profit is overstated by the overdue receivables.
  Adjusted, FY26 profit before tax falls from Rs 14.32 crore to about Rs 3.25
  crore. Customers paid about Rs 1.24 crore in FY26 against Rs 19.66 crore of
  revenue.

## Notes per name

- **ONWARDTEC.** The only PROCEED. Guide raised on 16-Oct-2026 to 15-18% revenue
  growth at a 13.8-14.3% EBITDA margin. Net of Rs 97.1 crore cash the multiple is
  14.9x to 16.0x. Q2 FY27 margin holds a Rs 2.93 crore R&D credit; without it H1
  margin is flat on FY26.
- **TIPSMUSIC.** Guided +20% PAT. H1 grew 10.5%, so H2 must grow about 28%.
  The lower bound is the H1 pace.
- **JTLIND.** Operating line from the May-2026 guide (30% volume, 10-15% more
  EBITDA per tonne); items below EBITDA annualised from Q1 FY27. Q1 profit times
  four gives 25.6x.
- **ORIENTHOT.** A merger stub. The 24-Aug-2026 scheme swaps 117 OHL shares for
  25 IHCL shares, so the price tracks IHCL, not OHL's own earnings. Reported
  FY26 holds an Rs 11.40 crore deferred-tax one-off; on the clean base growth is
  +3% to +15%.
- **MAHASTEEL.** EBITDA margin of 2.2% to 3.0% in every recent quarter. The
  multiple prices a 130 MW solar plan that is not yet underway.
- **DELAPLEX.** H2 FY26 annualised. No rupee guidance. H2 came in at 46% of the
  year against a guided 55-60%.
