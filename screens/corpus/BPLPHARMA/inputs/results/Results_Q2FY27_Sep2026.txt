Margins: raw material inflation 
The sequential decline in EBITDA margin came from gross margin. Raw material prices rose sharply because 
of the ongoing geopolitical conflict, increasing raw material cost by 490 bps of revenue QoQ. Gross margin was 
34.9%, against 39.8% in Q1 FY27. Cost discipline partly offset the impact: operating expenses rose 33.2% YoY 
to ₹20.31 Cr, well below revenue growth of 88.4%. 
Much of the outstanding order book was priced before the rise in input costs, so margins on these orders will 
remain sensitive to raw material prices until they ease. The order book stood at ₹117.83 Cr on 30 September 
2026, providing revenue visibility for H2 FY27.
