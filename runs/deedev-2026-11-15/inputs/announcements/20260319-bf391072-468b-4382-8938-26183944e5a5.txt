A.  MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT  
Total Capital Expenditure incurred for the Seamless Pipe 
Manufacturing Plant: approximately ₹ 89.75 Crore. The 
materiality threshold under Regulation 30(4)(i)(c) — being the 
lower of (i) 2% of consolidated net worth, (ii)  2% of consolidated 
turnover, and (iii) 5% of the average absolute value of PAT for the 
preceding three financial years — has been assessed by 
management. Based on the Company’ s latest audited 
consolidated financials, the applicable materiality threshold is 
₹ 1.38 Crore. The capex of ₹  89.74 Crore exceeds the applicable 
materiality threshold.  
 
B.  EXPECTED FINANCIAL IMPACT  
 
The Company has already declared receipt of an Order of Rs. 
58 Crores for seamless plant on 27th February 2026. 
 
This plant is expected to contribute incremental revenue from 
seamless pipes and tubes commencing Q1 FY  2026-27, 
supported by the Company ’s existing customer relationships, 
order backlog, and integrated product portfolio. 
 
Progressive improvement in EBITDA margins and operational 
leverage is considered possible as capacity utilisation ramps up 
during the ensuing four quarters, subject to market demand, 
customer offtake, input cost conditions, and operational 
efficiencies at the new plant. 
 
Any immediate material impact on consolidated profit after tax is 
assessed as remote in the near term, given that the plant is in the 
initial ramp -up phase and full -capacity utilisation will be 
progressive over the four-quarter horizon.
