### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Aequs machines and assembles aircraft parts under long contracts, mainly at Belagavi. It also runs two consumer plants: electronics parts at Hubballi and toys at Koppal.
2. Group sales rose 45% on a year ago, to Rs 409 Cr (R-L375). Aerospace drove it, up 44% to Rs 351 Cr (R-L563).
3. Consumer sales fell 21% in one quarter, from Rs 73.4 Cr to Rs 57.7 Cr (R-L564). The consumer segment lost Rs 40.1 Cr before interest and depreciation (R-L567).
4. Consumer plant use fell to 17% (P-L184, filed deck). Q1 was 22% (Notion and the call; not in the filings).
5. This breaks the test the draft model set. The model said the thesis fails if the consumer loss reaches Rs 36.1 Cr with use at or below 23%. Both legs fired.
6. Management withdrew the Q4 FY27 consumer breakeven date (P-L265). It gave no new date. On the call it blamed a product qualification delay and a toy customer change.
7. The reported 14% rise in group EBITDA came from forex other income. Without other income, EBITDA fell 6%, to Rs 13.9 Cr.
8. The net loss was Rs 52.4 Cr for the quarter and Rs 105.6 Cr for the half (R-L398). The group still pays tax on a loss: Rs 24.8 Cr in H1 (R-L393). It did so in Q2 FY26 too, before the merger plan.
9. Cash went the wrong way. H1 operating cash flow was negative Rs 80.8 Cr (R-L523). Net debt before leases rose from Rs 47 Cr to Rs 361 Cr in six months.
10. Aerospace still looks sound. The order book rose 9% to USD 1,096 Mn (P-L193). The company added 238 new aerospace parts, against 86 in Q1.
11. Funding plans now exceed the stated spend. FY27 capex drops to about Rs 320 Cr from Rs 660 Cr (call). The promoter warrants bring Rs 325 Cr on allotment. The second Rs 325 Cr is optional, and the stock trades below the Rs 231.55 warrant price. The deck also names an undated Rs 2,856 Cr MoU with the Karnataka government (P-L560).
12. Watch Q3 consumer segment revenue in the filed segment table. At or above Rs 73.4 Cr with a named breakeven quarter, the miss was timing. Below Rs 57.7 Cr, or no date again, the consumer plants are a structural drag.
