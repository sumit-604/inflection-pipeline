    38	                          iValue Reports Steady Q1FY27 Results
    39	    Q1FY27 delivered gross sales of ₹641.2 crore, up 5.7% YoY, and PAT increased 51.7% YoY,
    40	            indicating resilient operational performance and ongoing demand strength
    41	Bengaluru, India – 29th July, 2026: iValue Infosolutions Limited (BSE: 544523) (NSE: IVALUE), the
    42	Strategic Technology Advisor, announced today its financial results for the First Quarter of FY27 ended
    43	on 30th June 2026.
    44	Commenting on the results, Sunil Pillai, Chairman and Managing Director, iValue Infosolutions,
    45	said, “We are pleased to report a stable start to FY27, reflecting the resilience of our business model and
    46	the growing relevance of our Technology Advisor proposition. We delivered healthy gross sales of ₹641.2
    47	crore, a growth of 5.7% on YoY basis. Demand for cybersecurity, cloud, data management, AI-led
    48	infrastructure, and digital resilience solutions continues to strengthen as enterprises accelerate their
    49	technology transformation journeys.
    50	During the quarter, we continued to deepen engagement with customers, OEM partners, system integrators,
    51	and service providers while expanding our solution capabilities across high-growth technology areas. We
    52	also strengthened our leadership team and organizational capabilities to support the next phase of growth.
    53	Our differentiated advisory-led approach and well-disciplined execution capabilities position us well to
    54	capture emerging opportunities and continue creating long-term value for all stakeholders.”
    55	Commenting on the results, Swaroop Muvvala, Chief Financial Officer, iValue Infosolutions, said,
    56	“In Q1FY27, we delivered gross profit of ₹52 crore, an increase of 26.5% on YoY basis, with margin of 8.1%.
    57	Operating EBITDA before exceptional item stood at ₹20.2 crore, a growth of 27.7% YoY and PAT for the
    58	quarter was at ₹15.7 crore with 51.7% YoY growth. The improvement in profitability was mainly supported
    59	by growth in gross profit & operational frugality. We remain focused on scaling higher-value solutions and
    60	services and increasing the contribution from annuity-led revenues. As we move forward, our focus remains
    61	on improving profitability, enhancing shareholder value, and delivering consistent financial performance.”
