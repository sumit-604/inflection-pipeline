## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Airfloa is a real business doing real things. It makes railway coach interiors and components for Indian Railways and is pushing into aerospace and defence, revenue jumped 66% to Rs 319.6 Cr, and it earns a genuine 25% return on capital. That is the investable core, and it is why this is a watch name and not a discard.

The problem is two-fold and both parts are about honesty with yourself. First, the price. At Rs 315 the Hurdle Ratio STOPs on both valuation tracks, meaning even on grade-C-capped bull earnings there is no path to 25% a year. The probability-weighted return is under 7%. You would be paying a full price for a business that has to compound about 30-plus% a year just to earn a normal return, and nothing here suggests it can. The entry zone is Rs 175 to Rs 218, which is 44 to 80% below today.

Second, the cash. Profit rose but operating cash flow went the other way, to minus Rs 57.45 Cr, because receivables ballooned. The operator has ruled this growth-induced rather than structural, which is defensible, but it is a provisional ruling with a hard falsifier attached: if the next half-year print shows OCF still negative with ageing still climbing, it reverts to structural, the multiple drops, and fair value falls further. Add the governance items (personal penalties under appeal, Rs 38 Cr of personal guarantees on partly unregistered family land) and a defence JV that has already slipped twice, and the verdict is clear. Avoid at Rs 315. Watch the cash print. Re-engage only in the zone, only if the cash confirms, and only Small.
