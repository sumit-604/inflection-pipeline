## 2B Excuse pattern analysis

Every miss surfaced in the H2/FY26 call is attributed to an external factor: cotton
prices, the Gulf war (freight/insurance costs), "recent political changes" in the US,
or Kidcity-acquisition "transitional changes." No instance of a self-attributed error
("we made a mistake," "we misjudged demand/timeline") was found in either transcript.
None of the six misses above were raised proactively in opening remarks — the H2/FY26
call opening statement calls FY26 "one of the strongest years in the company's
history" without mentioning the store-count miss, the Kidcity revenue miss, the margin
reversal, or the broken quarterly-cadence promise. Every miss was surfaced only
because an analyst asked a pointed question (Deepesh Sancheti on margins, Ledan Shah
on the US order, Ronak on Kidcity's revenue shortfall). The Kidcity 75+ counter miss
was never raised by anyone and remains formally unexplained.

**Pattern: external-blame-heavy, reactive not proactive.**
