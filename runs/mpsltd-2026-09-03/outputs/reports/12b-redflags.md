## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the raw transcripts alone)

Each item anchored (call, speaker, page). Severity is this verifier's read.

**A. EPS guidance masks H2 FY26 earnings decline.** Q3 call, Rahul Arora guided
FY26 EPS to "comfortably surpass INR 100" (p.6). Madhur Rathi pushed back: trailing
12-month EPS was already INR 104 at the Sep-2025 quarter, so a full-year figure
"crossing 100" implies Q4 de-growth (Q3 call, p.12). Rahul did not engage the
arithmetic: "There are a bunch of numbers that are bigger than INR 100, but that's
all we can share" (p.13). Outcome: FY26 EPS landed at INR 102.11 (Q4 call, p.2),
below the Sep-2025 TTM of 104 — H2 FY26 earnings did fall. MEDIUM.

**B. Unbound margin: "high-margin" claim vs 14-19% actual.** Q3 call, Rahul called
Unbound "a high-margin, subscription-led business model" (p.6). The acquisition call
disclosed the true standalone EBITDA margin at 14% (p.8, p.16), and Q4 disclosed the
first 50-day print at 18.5%-19% (p.10) — well below the MPS group ~30%+. The asset
is presented as accretive-quality while running materially below group margin.
MEDIUM.

**C. Dividend promise reversed within three months.** Acquisition call, Rahul: "we'll
be comfortable as a board to continue to distribute surplus capital through dividends
and buybacks" and "capital distribution in the form of dividends or buybacks will
continue for the foreseeable future" (p.13, p.16-17). Q4 call: "the Board has chosen
not to recommend a final dividend" — first break in a 7-year, INR 650 cr payout
record (p.7). A specific reassurance given in Feb was reversed in May. MEDIUM.

**D. Buying a flat-revenue business, projecting a double.** Acquisition call, Madhur
Rathi: Unbound's published revenue "has been flat for this business for the past few
years, since FY'22 to '24" (p.11). Rahul declined the 2025 figure ("unable to share…
accounts not finalized," p.11), gave only 2024 top line USD 8.88m (p.13), then pivoted
to "double the revenue… looks very doable" via ex-North-America expansion (p.11). The
core growth thesis rests on unproven geographic cross-sell against a 3-year-flat base.
HIGH.

**E. Forex / organic-adjusted growth deflected across two quarters.** Q4 call, Gunit
Singh noted FY26 numbers were "largely… flat" in constant currency given forex gains;
Rahul: "The constant currency is not correct. So maybe we can take that off-line"
(p.15). Q1 call, Nachiket Kale asked the forex component in Corporate Learning; Rahul
deflected with "North America is not as big for corporate" and never quantified it
(p.13-14). The forex contribution to reported growth was never sized across two calls.
MEDIUM.

**F. A brand-new COO vanished in one quarter.** Q3 panel listed Mr. Sreenivas T.V. as
Chief Operating Officer, whom Rahul introduced as "a new entrant into our industry…
come in a senior position" (Q3 call, p.1, p.10). By the Q4 panel he is gone; Sukhwant
Singh appears as Chief Delivery Officer (Q4 call, p.1). Rodney Beach (President,
Corporate Learning, Q3 panel) is also gone, replaced by Soma Bhaduri. When Krushi
Parekh asked about management exits (Q1 call, p.8), Rahul answered only in culture terms
("sometimes you make mistakes"; "EEE values"), naming no one (p.8). MEDIUM.

**G. Client count fell quarter-on-quarter, 906 to 841.** Q1 call, Kashish Mehta: client
count "gone down from 906 to 841" QoQ (p.9). Rahul attributed the entire drop to AJE
pruning (p.10). A 65-account decline in a quarter, framed solely as intentional. LOW-MEDIUM.

**H. Vision 2027 segment-mix target shifted, unflagged.** Acquisition call stated a
40:40:20 (Research:Education:Corporate) target and Rahul admitted a prior split
forecast was "completely wrong" (p.16). Q4/Q1 calls give ~55:35:10 instead (Q4 p.16),
never flagged as a revision. MEDIUM.

**I. "We don't give guidance" alongside detailed multi-year guidance.** Repeated
across Q3 ("we don't like giving guidance," p.6) and Q4 ("often do not share guidance,"
p.15) while issuing EPS, FY27 EBITDA (INR 300 cr+), and FY28 (INR 1,500 cr revenue /
INR 450 cr EBITDA) targets. MEDIUM.

**J. AJE never sized in absolute revenue.** A unit large enough to distort every
headline growth comparison, described only via ex-AJE growth rates and "pruning"
language across all four calls. MEDIUM.

**K. FY28 INR 1,500 cr target vs 6% FY26 growth.** FY26 group revenue INR 768 cr,
up 5.7% (Q4 p.2). Reaching INR 1,500 cr by FY28 needs ~40% CAGR over two years, most
of it inorganic on management's own account. Reaffirmed "in absolute line of sight"
(Q4 p.8). The FY27 EBITDA CAGR of "~21%" is also measured from an FY24 base, not
recent run-rate (Q4 p.3). MEDIUM.

**L. Record FY26 EPS is flattered by a positive exceptional.** Q4 call: "FY'26 saw a
positive contribution of INR 7.64 crores" in exceptional items, said to "not impact
the underlying earnings trajectory" (p.3). FY26 PAT was INR 173 cr, EPS INR 102.11
(p.2). The INR 7.64 cr exceptional is ~4.4% of PAT (~INR 4.5/share); underlying
ex-exceptional EPS is ~INR 97-98, below the INR 100 milestone management "comfortably
crossed." This also flips against the negative Labour Code item (-INR 3.5/share) named
in Q3 (p.13). Feeds the Role 1 entry-EPS basis. HIGH.

**M. Inconsistent TAM figures inside one call.** Acquisition call cites a "$15 billion
market" (product demo, p.4), "$188 billion by 2030" (p.3), and a "$3 billion to $102
billion by 2034 at… 42% CAGR" subsector (p.3) — three different market sizes, all
management-sourced, none attributed. LOW-MEDIUM.

**N. Founder to advisor at close.** Acquisition call: founder/CEO Bill Detmer "is now
transitioning to an advisor role… a few hours a week" and "looking to move out of the
business" (p.12, p.14). Key-person risk for an asset whose moat is domain authority and
institutional relationships (e.g., Johns Hopkins). LOW.

Observation (not counted as a discrete red flag): the Unbound deal took 8-9 months
term-sheet-to-close, "very atypical" vs the usual 2-3 months (acquisition call, p.14).
Framed benignly as "meeting of the minds"; worth watching, not red-flag-grade alone.
