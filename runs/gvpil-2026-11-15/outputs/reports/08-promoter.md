## SECTION 3: Business conduct

3A RPT history (the core of this verdict).
- Cash pool lending. GVPIL lent INR M 4,500.0 to LM Wind in FY26 (nil prior year), interest income INR M 199.8; FY25 it borrowed and repaid INR M 1,019.0 from the same header (Note 36 p.181). Balance was at the INR M 4,500 approved cap (postal ballot 29 Mar 2025). Notice Item 8 says the statutory s.186 limit is INR M 4,156.4 so the enabling approval was needed (Notice p.311). Cap to rise to INR M 10,000; borrowing limit to INR M 3,000 (Item 9). The ICD equals 51.0% of LM Wind FY25 turnover when the full INR M 13,000 is counted (Notice p.314). LM Wind net worth INR M 9,366.2, PAT 2,917.1 (FY25). Notice says LM Wind has no available rating, never defaulted, GEV rated BBB (S&P) and BBB+ (Fitch). Lending rate 6.71% (GIND1YR+64 bp) vs 5.25-5.50% on 90-day bank deposits, so the rate is above the bank alternative. Callable on demand. VERIFIED.
- Security is inconsistent. Item 9 table says lending is "Secured" by a GEV guarantee of INR M 10,000, 100% coverage. B02/B03 read Note 16 and CARO as unsecured at 31 Mar 2026; the guarantee was proposed at the AGM. The Notice lists the same ICD as "unsecured" for borrowing. Open until the AGM outcome and the guarantee deed are seen. Reading A: guarantee covers the balance after the vote, so credit risk is GEV's (BBB). Reading B: balance was bare for 5+ months and the guarantee is aspirational. Separating observation: Q3 FY27 notes stating the guarantee in force against the ICD.
- Parent guarantee received for bank lines: INR M 7,977.6 used at year end (peak 20,432); INR M 16,000 sought (Item 10). Note 36 label "Corporate Guarantee issued" is wrong in direction; Directors' Report says no guarantee given (B03).
- Royalty: see fact check. FY26 INR M 435.7 in Note 36, equal to 3.4% of FY26 revenue from operations INR M 12,692.7 (calc). Where the INR M 310.0 is booked is not stated.
- Purchases from group: materials INR M 697.6 FY26 across named suppliers, services INR M 241.0 (Note 36 p.181, sum by calc is partial). Group revenue and other income are material: group-sourced income INR M 851.9, 25.1% of profit before exceptional items (B02).
- Asset sales: gas INR M 438.6 and hydro INR 1.0 to fellow subsidiaries in FY25 under common control. Hydro had net liabilities INR M 2,978.9 passing to the buyer, with a policy gain of 2,369.9 and capital reserve 609.0 (B02). Fair-value basis NOT FOUND. Transfer favours GVPIL on net liabilities, which is not the usual direction of abuse.
- Competing entities: GE group wind and hydro are not competitors of the steam services business.
3B Capital allocation: no dilution; promoter flat at 68.58%. Final dividend Rs 7 per share proposed (AGM filing 2026-08-14), the first noted by this stage; prior dividend history not checked. MD remuneration INR M 48.4 (FY26) vs 30.9 (FY25), including RSU and ESOP exercised, while the company swung to profit; perquisites INR M 26.85. Sitting fees small. VERIFIED (AR26 p.105, Note 36).
3C Share transactions: pledge NOT FOUND (no pledge column; screener table has none). Promoter 68.58% in all 12 quarters Dec 2023-Sep 2026. FII holding 0.73% and DII 0.70% at Sep 2026 (from 3.87% DII Dec 2023); public 29.59%. Minimum public float is met at 31.4% non-promoter. Not a selling pattern.
3D Minority treatment. Item 9 (LM Wind RPT) and Item 10 (GE Vernova Inc. RPT) go to non-related members only. All 10 resolutions "duly approved with requisite majority" (AGM summary 14 Aug 2026); percentages NOT FOUND (voting-results PDF not retrieved: fetch failed). Four ledger heads for Pillar 3 are for Role 2; none computed here.
Tripwires: guarantee deed against the ICD in force (Q3 FY27 notes); ICD above INR M 4,500; reconciliation of the INR M 310.0 royalty gap; permanent CFO still in seat at AR27; vote percentages on Items 8-10.
