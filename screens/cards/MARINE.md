## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Marine Electricals makes the
electrical systems that go inside ships, and the same competence sold ashore.
It reports two segments. Marine supplies switchboards, integrated platform
management systems, navigation and communication gear, and hybrid and battery
propulsion, to naval and commercial vessels. Industry sells integrated powering
solutions into data centres, metro rail, railways and general industry
(Investor Presentation FY2025 Q1, page 30).

The naval record is the asset. The company names supply to Scorpene-class
submarines under Project 75 and the indigenous nuclear submarine, equipment to
Project 17A and P15B ships, supply to CSL and GRSE for 16 anti-submarine
corvettes, work on INS Vikrant, and repair work for Coast Guard ships
(Investor Presentation FY2026 Q3, page 17).

**What is changing?** The Industry segment has overtaken Marine. In the quarter
to 30 June 2026, Industry revenue was Rs 156.36 crore and Marine Rs 102.80
crore. A year earlier Marine was Rs 78.82 crore and Industry Rs 88.16 crore
(Q1 FY27 results, page 10). Industry also earns more: segment profit before
interest and tax of Rs 14.10 crore against Marine's Rs 9.26 crore in the same
quarter.

**Why now?** Growth accelerated. Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 259.16 crore was 55%
above the Rs 166.98 crore of Q1 FY26. Profit before tax rose from Rs 15.47
crore to Rs 23.75 crore.

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Orders must keep arriving. This
is an order-book business and the corpus contains no order book after 31 March
2025. On that date the book was Rs 524.00 crore against revenue of Rs 700.58
crore for the year, and it had fallen from Rs 601.00 crore a year before
(Annual Report FY2025, page 83). Revenue has grown since by consuming backlog
faster than the corpus can show it being replaced.

**What breaks it?** Two things, and both are in the documents. A subsidiary,
Eltech Engineers Madras Private Limited, has fully eroded its net worth and its
auditors have raised a going-concern uncertainty (Q1 FY27 results, page 8). And
the company's own equity issue was under-subscribed (ICRA Monitoring Agency
Report, page 4).

Sources: FY25 results page 19; Q1 FY27 results page 10.

Beyond the two reported segments the company has built three optionalities. It
sells vessel traffic systems under TITAN SENTINEL and TITAN AVIPS, on software
acquired from Xanatos of Canada "with complete Transfer of Technology (ToT) and
know-how", and notes that vessel traffic services are mandated by regulation
for all ports and channels (Annual Report FY2025, page 82). It sells electric
vehicle charging under the brand Bijlify, including a partnership model with
Pune Municipal Corporation. And it targets service contracts for degaussing and
weapon systems (Annual Report FY2025, page 83).

**Archetype: order-book business, defence and capital goods variant.** Order
inflow, book-to-bill, execution pace, working capital and margin on backlog.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Real in Marine, thin in Industry. A supplier already qualified on a Project 75
submarine or a Project 17A frigate is inside a programme that runs for a
decade. Naval qualification is slow, and that is a barrier. The Xanatos
technology transfer for vessel traffic systems is an owned position in a
regulation-mandated product.

Industry is the opposite: integrated powering for data centres and
infrastructure is a competitive, bid-driven market. It is also the segment now
carrying growth. Moat: present in Marine, absent in the part that is growing.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R3, a spec'd supplier qualified into naval programmes. TO R4 on the
company's own plan, by moving from supplying components to supplying complete
packages and then services: "Focusing on complete installation package for
Naval Ships" and "Targeting to receive Service Contract for Degaussing System
and Weapon Systems" (Annual Report FY2025, page 83).

A service contract on a degaussing or weapons system is a genuine rung change.
It converts a one-time supply into an annuity on an installed base the company
already sits inside.

Management's claim, quoted once, on the industry side: "Foray into integrated
powering solutions for Data centres, Commercial, Industry, and Infrastructure"
(Investor Presentation FY2025 Q1, page 30).

Note the tension. The naval service ambition is the rung climb. The growth is
coming from data centre power, which is a lower rung. The two are pulling in
opposite directions.

## 8. Proof check

The last two quarters move the right way. Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 277.48 crore with
profit before tax Rs 22.38 crore. Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 259.16 crore with profit
before tax Rs 23.75 crore, against Rs 15.47 crore a year earlier. Industry
segment margin improved: Rs 14.10 crore of PBIT on Rs 156.36 crore of revenue
in Q1 FY27 against Rs 8.64 crore on Rs 88.16 crore a year before.

**Name the absence, because it is the whole point.** For an order-book
business the proof is the book, and the book is unobserved for eighteen months.
The last reading showed it falling. Revenue growth since then is consistent
with strong new orders and equally consistent with a backlog being run down
fast. This corpus cannot tell the two apart.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Going-concern emphasis on a subsidiary.** "Eltech Engineers Madras Private
  Limited ('Eltech'), a subsidiary company, have accumulated losses and its net
  worth has been fully eroded, its current liabilities exceeded its current
  assets as at 30 June 2026. These conditions indicate the existence of
  material uncertainty about Eltech's ability to continue as a going concern."
  The parent has committed support (Q1 FY27 results, page 8). Flag at the top.
- **The equity issue was under-subscribed** (ICRA Monitoring Agency Report,
  page 4). Flag.
- Order book fell from Rs 601.00 crore to Rs 524.00 crore in FY25, and no later
  figure exists in the corpus. Flag.
- Rs 0.51 crore of issue proceeds paid to KDU Enterprises Private Limited, a
  related party, for electrical parts (ICRA Monitoring Report, page 10). Small,
  and flagged because it is issue money going to a related party.
- Depreciation in the Q1 FY27 filing reads Rs 23.10 crore on Rs 259.16 crore of
  revenue, and Rs 92.78 crore on Rs 876.94 crore for FY26. Near 11% of revenue
  is very high for an electricals maker and is unexplained in the corpus. Flag,
  and note the page it sits on is the damaged one.
- **The Q1 FY27 profit and loss page returned damaged figures** through the
  reader, including a cell reading "2,5737.04", and its expense lines do not
  reconcile to the stated profit. No line from that page is used here. Flag as
  a corpus limitation, to be resolved from the original PDF.
- Promoter holding, pledge and a credit rating NOT FOUND.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**PARTIAL.** ICRA appears in the corpus, but as monitoring agency for the issue
proceeds rather than as a rating agency. Its June 2026 report confirms that
utilisation matches the offer document, that no shareholder approval was needed
for deviations, and that no major deviation was observed against earlier
reports (ICRA Monitoring Agency Report, page 4). That is useful assurance on
where the money went. It carries no opinion on creditworthiness, leverage or
the durability of earnings. A rating rationale was looked for and NOT FOUND.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED on delivery, UNOBSERVABLE on continuation.** Four years
  of compounding and a 55% quarter are delivered. The order book that would say
  whether it continues has not been published since March 2025.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL-FEATURE, provisionally.** A subsidiary with fully
  eroded net worth and a going-concern uncertainty, plus an under-subscribed
  equity issue, are not the debris of a climb. They are balance-sheet facts.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a corpus document: Bull AI
  company record, market capitalisation Rs 5,306.69 crore. FY26 consolidated
  profit before tax was Rs 75.75 crore. The market capitalisation is about 70
  times pre-tax profit, before any tax at all. Defence exposure is in favour and
  the price reflects it.

**Posture: AVOID to DEEP WATCH.** Structural ugliness with the gap closed is the
dominating overlay. The classification is provisional because Eltech is one
small subsidiary inside a growing group, and disproving the structural reading
is exactly what a later look should attempt.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** No `/step1` at this price.

The business is doing well. Revenue up 55% and pre-tax profit up 54% in the
latest quarter, four years of compounding behind it, and a naval qualification
record that a new entrant cannot buy. None of that is in dispute.

Three things stop a full run. The order book, which is the forward variable for
this archetype, has not been published for eighteen months and was falling when
last seen. A subsidiary carries a going-concern uncertainty in the current
auditor's report. And the market already pays about 70 times pre-tax profit,
which leaves the 25% compound target depending entirely on growth that the
corpus cannot yet evidence forward.

**The gate, stated so it can be checked**
A published order book, with book-to-bill above 1.0 times against trailing
revenue, and Eltech either resolved or ring-fenced.

**Load-bearing facts a later run must verify first**
1. Order book at 30 June 2026 or later, split Marine against Industry.
2. Eltech Engineers Madras: size, losses, and what parent support commits the
   group to.
3. The depreciation charge near 11% of revenue. What asset produces it.
4. Promoter holding and pledge.
5. The under-subscribed issue: how much was sought, how much raised, and at
   what price.
6. Whether any degaussing or weapon-system service contract has been won. That
   is the rung change, and today it is only a target.

**What would change the view**
- A published order book above Rs 1,000 crore with named naval programmes.
- A first service contract on an installed naval system.
- A price fall that reopens the gap against pre-tax profit of Rs 75.75 crore.

No valuation was run. No target price. This verdict decides only whether the
name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
