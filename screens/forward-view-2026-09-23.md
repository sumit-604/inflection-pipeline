# Forward view: 2026-09-23 shallow screen, fourteen names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework, per the operator ruling of
2026-09-21. Next year's bottom line, the growth rate, and the forward
price-to-earnings ratio, so the run can be ranked on growth against multiple.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result, not a conclusion. Four names below screen
better than they are. Each is flagged.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the Bull AI company record at 23 September 2026. It is
a service record, not a filed document. Every earnings figure traces to a filing
or a company presentation, cited in its card. Where a card derives a number, the
arithmetic is on the card.

## The flags that stop a misread

1. **SOUTHBANK's FY26 profit leaned on treasury.** Treasury and forex income
   was Rs 463 crore in FY26 against Rs 272 crore in FY25. Operating profit
   excluding treasury fell 4.4%. Q4 FY26 treasury was zero. The low end uses the
   Q1 FY27 run-rate for that reason.
2. **TRUALT's guided number failed by 35% last year.** FY26 volume was about 24
   crore litres against 37 crore litres guided in February. The guided row
   (15.0x) and the delivered row (36.7x) are both shown. The delivered row is the
   better evidenced one.
3. **SCODATUBES has two answers.** Management reaffirmed FY27 guidance after a
   Q1 FY27 PAT fall of 26%. The guided row is 15.6x to 16.7x. The Q1 run-rate is
   36.4x. Q2 FY27 separates them.
4. **SUNFLAG's multiple ignores its biggest asset.** The standalone balance
   sheet held Rs 7,695 crore of investments at 31 March 2026, mostly shares of
   Lloyds Metals & Energy. The market capitalisation is Rs 6,404 crore. A PE on
   steel earnings alone misstates what the market pays for.
5. **MUKANDLTD's reported PE of about 3.4x is land, not steel.** FY26 PAT of Rs
   604.15 crore includes a Rs 554.31 crore surplus on asset sales and a Rs 92.02
   crore deferred tax credit. PBT excluding other income was negative in FY26 and
   in Q1 FY27.
6. **CUBEXTUB's PAT is mostly other income.** Other income was Rs 8.22 crore
   against PBT of Rs 9.64 crore in FY26.
7. **STEELXIND's multiple ignores pending dilution.** Warrants from IMR Resources
   (Rs 300 crore) are outstanding and Q1 FY27 revenue fell 11.2%.
8. **KDDL is read on attributable profit.** The consolidated PAT includes the
   minority share of listed Ethos. The share count is dated FY24.

## What ranks

On growth against multiple, **ADVANCE** is the only name that screens well
without a flag that reverses it: 39% to 59% growth at 12.4x to 14.2x on a
delivered quarter. Its caveat is on the card: the proof gate (Unit-4 technicals)
has not fired, and one seasonal quarter carries the estimate.
