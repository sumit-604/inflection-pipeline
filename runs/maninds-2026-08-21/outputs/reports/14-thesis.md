And SEBI found the promoters misstated years of accounts; the ban is stayed, not cleared. So the verdict is WATCHLIST at Rs714, up from AVOID. The name becomes a Small buy only in the Rs412-589 zone, only with the India book back above Rs2,500cr on a filed print, and only if no tripwire has fired. One live caveat: NPC ties up a lot of working capital. If that ramp is heavy, the honest earnings base is lower, and the targets fall with it. Watch the 14-November half-year print first.
