Defence : Opportunity landscape
23
Record Budget Backing: Rs 7.85 lakh Cr defence budget for FY27 (+15.2% over FY26 Budget 
Estimates), including Rs 2.19 lakh Cr capital outlay, supporting defence modernisation.
Manufacturing Scale-Up: Defence production reached a record Rs 1.78 lakh Cr in FY26 (+15.6% YoY), 
reflecting strengthening indigenous manufacturing capabilities.
Export Acceleration: Defence exports surged 62.7% YoY to a record Rs 38,424 Cr in FY26, with Indian 
defence products exported to over 80 countries.
Robust Procurement Pipeline: Capital procurement contracts worth Rs 2.28 lakh Cr signed in FY26, 
alongside acquisition approvals worth Rs 6.81 lakh Cr.
Import Substitution Drive: Sixth Positive Indigenisation List notified in August 2026, covering 405 
additional items with estimated business potential of Rs 3,070 Cr.
Private Sector Momentum: Private sector accounted for 24% of defence production and 45.2% of 
defence exports in FY26, reflecting increasing industry participation.
Domestic Procurement Focus: Approximately 75% of FY27 capital acquisition budget, amounting to 
Rs 1.39 lakh Cr, earmarked for domestic defence industries.
Long-Term Growth Visibility: Government targets Rs 3 lakh Cr in annual defence production and Rs 
50,000 Cr in defence exports by 2029, supporting long-term industry opportunities
Government enhances focus on “Make In India” in Defence
Source: Ministry of Defence; Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India – Union Budget FY27, Defence Production & Exports FY26, Capital Procurement FY26 and Defence Indigenisation Updates (2026)
