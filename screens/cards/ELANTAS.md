## Business Understanding Narrative

Elantas Beck India makes the materials that stop electricity going where it should not.
Its Electrical Insulations business makes wire enamels, varnishes, impregnating resins and
casting and potting systems that insulate the copper windings inside motors, generators
and transformers (search extracts, AR 2024 p.26). Its second business, Engineering and
Electronic Resins and Materials, makes electronic compounds, hardeners, and engineering
materials for industrial flooring, coatings, structural repairs and adhesives (search
extracts, AR 2023 p.72). It is a subsidiary of ALTANA of Germany and takes technology from
ELANTAS GmbH, benchmarking against overseas affiliates through a group programme called
Altana X (search extracts, AR 2023 p.76).

Money comes from being specified into a motor or transformer design and then supplying it
for years. Pricing holds: through the volatile 2023 input year the company "managed the
volatile costs' situation through effective price management" and reported record revenue
(search extracts, AR 2023 p.71).

What is changing is the mix. The smaller, higher-margin Engineering and Electronic Resins
segment grew from Rs 10,367 lakh in calendar 2023 to Rs 12,308 lakh in 2024 to Rs 16,464
lakh in 2025, up 33.8% in the last year, while the core insulation business grew 8.8%
(search extracts, segment tables). That segment earned a 28.8% return on its revenue in
2025 against 17.0% for insulation. Its share of revenue has gone from about 15% to 19.4%.

Why now: three moves. The Von Roll India resins and tapes assets were integrated and
lifted the insulation business (search extracts, AR 2024 p.19). A further acquisition of a
target's resin business assets was approved in August 2024. And a land parcel at Bharuch,
Gujarat was approved for up to Rs 6,700 lakh (search extracts, Financial Results filing
p.5).

What must be true for a transition thesis: the E and E segment must keep compounding at
30% and pull group growth above where it is.

What breaks it: the growth rate. Revenue went Rs 645 crore, Rs 681 crore, Rs 751 crore,
Rs 848 crore across 2022 to 2025. That is about 9.5% compound. Profit before tax went
Rs 118 crore, Rs 184 crore, Rs 183 crore, Rs 199 crore. That is a fine business and it is
not a 25% compounding machine.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Archetype: build-to-spec component maker, with licence-and-technology characteristics from
the parent. Revenue is tonnes of resin and enamel sold into a customer's qualified design;
margin comes from formulation and from being hard to switch out of a motor build.
Two segments. Electrical Insulations is 80.6% of 2025 revenue and earns a 17.0% segment
margin. Engineering and Electronic Resins is 19.4% and earns 28.8% (derived from the
segment tables). The company describes the second as "specialty products in Electronics,
Auto Electricals, Filters & High Voltage Capacitors application, in addition to emerging
opportunities in E Mobility" (search extracts, AR 2023 p.72).
The one economic engine is technology transfer from the parent, converted into local
formulation. The R&D centre works on "the absorption of technology acquired from overseas
affiliates of ELANTAS division for the manufacture of certain wire enamels, varnishes and
casting and potting systems" (search extracts, AR 2024 p.26). That is a moat the company
did not have to build and cannot lose while ALTANA owns it.

## 3. Competitive advantages
Strong and stable, which is the point and also the problem.
Pricing power is evidenced rather than claimed: through a year of supply chain disruption
the company protected margin by price management and still grew revenue to a record
(search extracts, AR 2023 p.71). Quality is such that "sales returns, if any, due to
quality issues are practically negligible" (search extracts, AR 2023 p.76). Products are
qualified into customers' designs, and 14 new solutions were launched in 2024 alone
across automotive including e-mobility, power, solar and rotating machines (search
extracts, AR 2024 p.26).
The parent relationship gives formulations, benchmarking and a global R&D pipeline for
free. Capacity has been added by debottlenecking rather than capex: the company met a
"surge in market demand without major CAPEX" (search extracts, AR 2024 p.22).
Against that, the same parent relationship caps the story. A subsidiary does not choose
its own strategy, its own export markets or its own acquisitions beyond what the group
allocates. Moat: present, durable, and owned by someone else.

## 5. Financial trajectory
By calendar year, income from operations (Rs lakh): 64,534 (2022), 68,101 (2023), 75,140
(2024), 84,809 (2025). In crore: Rs 645, Rs 681, Rs 751, Rs 848. Three-year compound
growth about 9.5%.
Profit before tax (Rs lakh): 11,841 (2022, before exceptional), 18,362 (2023, before
exceptional), 18,345 (2024), 19,854 (2025). In crore: Rs 118, Rs 184, Rs 183, Rs 199.
Derived margins: profit before tax was 18.3% of revenue in 2022, 27.0% in 2023, 24.4% in
2024 and 23.4% in 2025. The trend since 2023 is down.
Segment detail is where the interest is. Electrical Insulations revenue rose 8.8% in 2025
and its segment margin fell from 18.9% (2023) to 17.4% (2024) to 17.0% (2025). Engineering
and Electronic Resins rose 33.8% in 2025 and its margin was 31.0%, 27.2% and 28.8% over
the same years.
Two structural facts. Finance costs are Rs 51 lakh on Rs 848 crore of revenue: the company
carries essentially no debt. And other unallocable income was Rs 4,785 lakh in 2025,
Rs 5,188 lakh in 2024 and Rs 5,184 lakh in 2023 (search extracts, segment reconciliation).
That is about 24% of 2025 profit before tax arriving from outside the operating segments,
almost certainly treasury income on a large cash balance. Any operating analysis must strip
it out.
Quarterly, 2025 ran Rs 20,619, Rs 21,021, Rs 21,642 and Rs 21,527 lakh of income from
operations, with profit before tax of Rs 4,429, not printed, Rs 4,856 and Rs 5,284 lakh.
Profit is rising through the year while revenue is flat, which is the mix shift showing.
Inflection classification: **margin-mix led, and mild.** Real, documented, and running at
roughly a tenth of the pace the strategy's hurdle requires.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder
Management's claim, quoted once: "Strategic investments in new product development and
additional manufacturing assets allowed your Company to position its products to meet the
demands of emerging applications" (search extracts, AR 2023 p.71).
FROM R4, franchise and share-of-wallet leader. Customer capture through design
qualification, repeat economics, negligible quality returns, pricing power held through an
input shock, no debt, and cash on the balance sheet. That is the R4 definition met on the
documents.
TO R4. There is no rung migration claimed and none visible. The company is not leaving a
tier; it is shifting mix inside one, from 17% margin insulation toward 29% margin
electronic resins.
This matters for the mandate. The re-rating engine that transition alpha hunts is the
destination-PE delta from a rung migration. Elantas has no such delta available. Whatever
return it offers has to come from earnings growth of about 10% plus whatever the
recognition gap is worth today, and multinational subsidiaries in India rarely carry an
open gap.

## 8. Proof check
The numbers move the way the triggers claim. They just do not move fast.
What fired: the mix shift is real and measurable across three years, not narrated. The
Engineering and Electronic Resins segment grew 18.7% in 2024 and 33.8% in 2025 while the
core grew 8.8%, and it carries roughly 12 percentage points more segment margin. Quarterly
profit before tax rose through 2025 on flat sequential revenue, which is exactly what a
mix shift does. Von Roll integration is named in the annual report as having lifted the
insulation business. Debt is nil.
What has not fired: nothing has failed, but nothing has accelerated either. Group revenue
growth was 5.5%, 10.3% and 12.9% over the last three years. Profit before tax margin has
fallen from 27.0% to 23.4% since 2023. And roughly a quarter of profit before tax is
non-operating income.
Named proof point: whether Engineering and Electronic Resins can hold 30% growth for two
more years. At 19.4% of revenue growing 30%, with the core at 9%, group growth reaches
about 13%. To clear a 25% hurdle on earnings alone this segment would have to become the
majority of the company, which on these rates takes most of a decade.

## 11. Posture: Transition Decision Matrix
- Proof gate: FIRED, on the mix shift. Three years of segment data show the higher-margin
  business growing three to four times faster than the core. That is proof, not narrative.
- Ugliness: ABSENT. There is no ugly optic. The matrix's ugliness axis does not engage
  with this company at all.
- Recognition gap: INDETERMINATE from the corpus, and the honest prior is CLOSED.
  Multinational subsidiaries with no debt, cash on the balance sheet, negligible quality
  returns and a German parent are among the most persistently well-rated names on the
  Indian market.
Proof FIRED plus no ugliness plus a probably-closed gap maps to **EARNINGS-ONLY**. Under
the matrix that is "actionable only if growth clears the Hurdle without help from
re-rating." Here it plainly does not. Group earnings are compounding in the low teens at
best, and about a quarter of profit before tax is treasury income that will not compound
at all.

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS for this mandate.** This is not a judgement on the business, which is excellent: a
debt-free, cash-rich, technology-fed franchise with real pricing power, negligible quality
failures, a documented mix shift toward a segment earning 29%, and a parent investing in
Indian capacity. Someone hunting quality compounders should own it.
It does not fit transition alpha. There is no rung to climb, because the company already
sits at R4 and has done for years. There is no re-rating engine, because there is no
migration to price. And earnings are compounding at roughly 10% a year while the mandate
needs 25%. Spending a full `/step1` here would produce a careful document whose conclusion
is already visible from four years of segment tables.
Revisit conditions, which are specific and checkable:
1. The Engineering and Electronic Resins segment passing about 30% of revenue while still
   growing above 25%. At that point group growth changes character and the name becomes a
   genuine mix-led transition. On current rates that is roughly three years away.
2. A price that opens the recognition gap wide enough that low-teens earnings growth clears
   the hurdle. Establish CMP before dismissing this permanently; the prior that the gap is
   closed is a prior, not a measurement.
3. Any ALTANA action on the shareholding, an open offer or a delisting intent, which would
   change the question entirely.
If any of those three fires, promote this straight to `/step1`. Until then it belongs on a
quality watchlist, not in the transition pipeline. No price here.
