Q2 FY27 witnessed healthy theatrical momentum, supported by a diverse content slate across 
languages and genres.  
July opened on a strong note, led by the strong performance of “Spider -Man: Brand New Day”, 
supported by “The Odyssey” and “Dhamaal 4”, which contributed meaningfully to overall 
theatrical activity. “Alpha” delivered a moderate performance, while several s maller releases saw 
limited audience traction. 
August saw a more varied performance across the content slate. “Hanuman Ansh” emerged as a 
notable sleeper hit, significantly exceeding initial expectations and gaining momentum through 
strong audience word -of-mouth. “Awarapan 2” also delivered a strong th eatrical performance, 
while “Batwara 1947” underperformed despite expectations around the film. “Toxic: A Fairy Tale 
for Grown -ups”, despite being a highly anticipated release, also fell short of expectations, 
highlighting the increasingly selective nature of theatrical audiences. 
September continued to see varied audience response, with “Mirzapur: The Movie” delivering a 
strong performance, while “Haiwaan” underperformed and saw limited theatrical traction. Other 
releases contributed to a mixed content environment across Hindi and regional markets. The 
quarter concluded with “The Vvaan: Force of the Forrest”, which opened with a decent response. 
Overall, Q2 FY27 reflected healthy theatrical activity, with strong performances from both major 
releases and unexpected sleeper hits. At the same time, the varied performance of highly 
anticipated titles reinforced the importance of content quality, audie nce connect and word -of-
mouth in driving theatrical success. With a healthy pipeline of releases across languages and 
improving audience sentiment, we remain optimistic about the growth prospects of the business in 
the coming quarters.  
In total, 386 movies were released (including versions/languages) during the quarter, compared to 
462 in Q2FY26 and 399 in Q1FY27.  
On the screen network front, our advertising footprint now stands at 3,946 screens, comprising 
2,606 multiplex screens and 1,340 single screens.  
Turning to the key figures for the quarter and half year ended September 2026 –  
• The consolidated revenue for Q2 FY27 stood at ₹1,299 million, compared to ₹1,113 million in 
Q2 FY26. and ₹1,118 million compared to Q1 FY27. 
• EBITDA stood at ₹213 million in Q2 FY27, compared to ₹218 million in Q2 FY26, and ₹189 
million compared to Q1 FY27. 
• The company reported a net profit of ₹64 million in Q2 FY27, compared to a net profit of ₹75 
million in Q2 FY26 and a net profit of ₹57million in Q1 FY27. 
• Regarding half year performance, consolidated revenues amounted to ₹2,417 million, compared 
to ₹2,203 million in H1 FY26. EBITDA for H1 FY27 was ₹402 million, compared to ₹411 million 
in H1 FY26. On the PAT front, the company reported a net profit of ₹121  million in H1 FY27 
against a net profit of ₹141 million in H1 FY26. 
• The consolidated cash as of 30th September was ₹1,657 million, and the net cash was ₹865 
million after considering outstanding debt.
