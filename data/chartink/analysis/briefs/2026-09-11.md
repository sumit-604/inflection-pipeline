## What would change this read

The constructive view has now failed two of its four tests. It fails further if
the count above the two-hundred-day line closes under about 1,150. That would take
out the 8 July low of 1,145 and turn a crack into a downtrend. It fails if fresh yearly lows hold near one stock in twenty-five for a full week. The weak half would then be trending. It fails on the first session where hard fallers
outnumber hard risers, which has not happened since 30 July. It also fails if
defence or healthcare drops under half its stocks above the four-week line.

The cautious view fails if a real thrust day arrives. That means three hundred or more stocks jumping four and a half percent in one session. It fails if the twenty-day count climbs back above about 900 within a week. That would mark Thursday and Friday as a shakeout. It fails if the two-hundred-day count returns above about
1,250. It also fails if the large-cap list above the volume-weighted price holds
above seventy while bank breadth follows the bank index. That needs more than
half of bank stocks back above the four-week line.
