## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue has the strongest evidence in the file, and it is documented rather than promised.** Bhiwadi is built and running, with production commenced in June 2025 and 24,000 MTPA of copper tube capacity, and Global Copper was merged in under an NCLT order. Silvassa adds Rs 86 crore of capacity approved by the board in March 2026 for delivery within twelve months. None of this is a plan; it is spent money and commissioned plant. What would confirm the transition continuing is copper tube revenue climbing back above Rs 361 Cr a quarter. What would kill it is copper tubes declining for a second and third consecutive quarter, which would say the Bhiwadi ramp has stalled rather than paused, and would leave the company growing only with the copper price.

**Margin has a real mix catalyst and a real competitive threat, and they point opposite ways.** The mix shift is documented in the segment note: copper tubes went from 14% to 22% of revenue and carry a better spread. Against that, both listed peers are expanding capacity at the same time, Vidya Wires nearly doubling and Bhagyanagar adding, and Bhagyanagar's own analyst raised the risk of overcapacity from Adani and Hindalco entering. A capacity race in a conversion business competes away spread. Confirmation would be EBITDA margin holding above 5% for two consecutive quarters with copper tubes still gaining share of revenue. The kill signal is margin back below 4.5% while volumes rise, which would mean the extra capacity is being bought with price.

**Cash conversion has no catalyst, and that absence is the finding.** I searched every category the protocol lists. A working capital programme with a debtor day target: not found. A renegotiation of customer payment terms: not found. An inventory reduction programme with a target: not found. A newly sanctioned factoring facility: the facility exists and grew past Rs 186 Cr, but it grew through the very year cash conversion collapsed, so it is already in the base and it funds working capital rather than converting it. That is the same shape as the Kernex case, where a larger borrowing line against working capital was correctly read as the wrong signal. Management has published nothing explaining the reversal, and because there is no earnings call, no analyst has been able to make them address it. Under this protocol, silence on a transition that is going backwards is the loudest signal available, and it is why the cap engages.

**ROCE has one documented catalyst and two documented counter catalysts.** In favour, Bhiwadi earns for a full twelve months in FY27 for the first time, against roughly nine months in FY26. Against, Silvassa is fresh capital that will not earn inside the window, and working capital is growing faster than revenue and sits directly in the denominator. The confirm signal is EBIT growing faster than capital employed for two consecutive quarters. The kill signal is the reverse, and it is the same event that would confirm the cash problem is structural, which is why these two transitions cannot be treated as independent bets.
