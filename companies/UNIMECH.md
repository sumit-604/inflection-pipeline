## OPERATOR RULING (2026-08-04, on the acquisition/bullish framing)
Operator pushed back on the cautious framing: Hobel is acquired and
permanently consolidated, so the earnings are real and recurring and the
shareholder owns that stream now. Ruling adopted into the record:
- The quarter is CONSTRUCTIVE / LEAN-POSITIVE on the earnings BASE and the
  franchise. The step-up in size, margin (Hobel ~50%+ EBITDA), and
  diversification is permanent, not a one-off. Near term the reported base
  likely rises further because Q1 FY27 carried only 2 months of Hobel; Q2
  gets a full 3 months.
- The analyst "strip out Hobel" exercise is NOT a claim the earnings are
  unreal. It answers a different question (organic growth RATE, not earnings
  LEVEL). The +71% is a one-time step-up, not a repeatable rate; it recurs
  next year only if the organic core also grows.
- The one real shareholder catch is PER-SHARE, not total: Hobel was funded
  with debt + CCDs and a Rs 750 Cr QIP now sits on top, so measure earnings
  per share NET of dilution and against the ~10% ROCE, not just total profit.
- Net operator label: bullish on franchise and earnings base; two "prove it"
  conditions — (1) organic ex-Hobel core still growing, (2) the Rs 750 Cr
  raise deploys accretively rather than diluting faster than it earns. Both
  get tested at the Aug 04 concall (organic split) and QIP pricing.

(China+1 sourcing shift; record Airbus/Boeing backlogs with rising India
content; FY27 defence budget ~$88bn). Nearest listed peers: Azad Engineering
(FY26 rev ~Rs 603 Cr, PAT ~Rs 133 Cr, ~37% EBITDA, order book ~10x revenue,

shortest-order-book (~Rs 280-314 Cr, ~1x revenue) and earliest-stage of the
basket: highest optionality, highest execution/capital risk.
