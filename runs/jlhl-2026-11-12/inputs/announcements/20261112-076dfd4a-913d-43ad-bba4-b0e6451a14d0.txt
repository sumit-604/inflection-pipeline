Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations,

from the Thane Municipal Corporation (“TMC”) for the expansion of its existing Thane Hospital.  
The proposed expansion involves the addition of about 40-50 beds to the existing bed capacity of the 
Thane Hospital.

SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 (‘SEBI Circular’) is

Sr. No Particulars Details 
1. Existing Capacity 
 
The Company along with its subsidiary has an existing 
operational bed capacity of 1,259 beds. 
2. 
 
Existing Capacity Utilization The Company’s occupancy was 60% of its capacity as on 
September 30, 2026. 
3. 
 
Proposed Capacity Addition The Company proposes to add approximately 40-50 beds 
to its existing Thane Hospital, pursuant to the permission 
received from TMC. 
4. 
 
Period within which the 
proposed capacity is to be 
added 
The construction and commissioning of the additional 
bed capacity are expected to be completed within 
approximately one year. Construction will commence 
after receiving requisite Environmental Clearances. 
5. Investment required The estimated project cost for the proposed expansion is 
approximately ₹25 crore. 
6. Mode of financing Internal Accruals & as may be decided by the Board of 
Directors. 
7. Rationale. The proposed expansion is intended to augment the bed 
capacity of the existing Thane Hospital to cater to the 
growing demand for quality healthcare services in the 
region and to enhance the Company's ability to serve a 
larger number of patients.
