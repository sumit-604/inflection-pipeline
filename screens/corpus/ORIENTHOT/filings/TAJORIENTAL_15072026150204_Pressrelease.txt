PRESS RELEASE 
OHL ANNOUNCES Q1 FY 2026-2027 FINANCIAL RESULTS 
 
MUMBAI, JULY 15, 2026: Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) reported its results for the first quarter 
ending June 30, 2026.  
 
Mr. Pramod Ranjan, Managing Director & CEO, Oriental Hotels Ltd., said: "OHL in the first quarter of 
FY 2027 reported a steady performance with a revenue of INR 114.4 crores, EBITDA of INR 26.6 crores 
and a Profit After Tax (PAT) of INR 11.4 crores.”  
 
He added, " with extensive asset enhancement initiatives across the OHL portfolio and continued 
strength in domestic demand, the company is well-positioned to deliver a sustained performance in 
the quarters ahead.”  
 
KEY HIGHLIGHTS  
  
 
Aligned with IHCL’s ESG+ framework of Paathya, OHL hotels continued to advance their 
sustainability agenda, with 71% of total energy consumption sourced from renewable energy 
 
IHCL’s iconic brand Taj is ranked as India’s Strongest Brand 2026 and World’s Strongest Hotel 
Brand 2025 as per Brand Finance. OHL has three Taj branded hotels in its portfolio.  
 
About Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) 
Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) is an associate company of The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL). 
The company has seven hotels – Taj Coromandel, Chennai Taj Fisherman’s Cove Resort & Spa, Chennai 
Taj Malabar Resort & Spa, Cochin, Vivanta Coimbatore, Vivanta Mangalore; Gateway Madurai and 
Gateway Coonoor.
