## Defects found

1. **results/q-2026-09.pdf is mislabelled.** It is byte identical to the AGM voting result
   (StExcAGM2026VRScrRepFinal). It is not the Q2 FY27 result. The intake brief stated Q2 FY27 was
   reported; the corpus does not support that.
2. **Duplicates.** Every results/ PDF except q-2026-09 has a byte identical copy in filings/. The
   14-Nov-2025 board outcome is held twice in filings/.
3. **Image-only financial statements not extracted.** results/q-2026-03.pdf pp.4-7 and 15-18 (P&L,
   balance sheet, cash flow, notes) yield only signature text in the .txt. They were read from the
   page images.
4. **Unmarked OCR.** results/q-2026-06.txt, q-2025-09.txt and q-2025-12.txt carry scanner OCR text
   with no `[OCR]` marker. Digits garble (for example 662.58 for 662.88). Used digits were checked
   against the page images.
5. **OCR digit corruption in FY25 annual report.** p.154 OCR reads "246.37 crores" and "211.59
   crores"; the page image shows Rs 46.37 crore and Rs 11.59 crore. p.155 OCR reads "218.00 crores";
   the image shows Rs 18.00 crore.
6. **Errors inside the filed documents (not extraction errors):**
   - Q1 FY27 filing shows Q4 FY26 total comprehensive income 618.28 and EPS 1.00 against PAT 919.74;
     the audited FY26 filing shows 919.74 and 1.49.
   - FY26 standalone cash flow subtotal "cash generated from operations" (1,384.71) does not add; the
     lines sum to 104.07, and the net figure (260.01) is consistent with 104.07.
   - Cash flow labels rights call money as "Dividend on OCRPS".
   - Consolidated FY26 notes still cite the FY25 board meeting of 30-May-2025; the consolidated
     impact statement is headed FY ended 31-Mar-2025.
   - FY26 AR: directors' report shows consolidated profit "00.00"; note 3 sentence "Includes dues
     from companies where directors" is truncated; editor's note left in text (p.56); foreign exchange
     earnings Rs 19.5 crore (p.58) against 0 in the notes (p.121); highlights claim 100+ employees
     against 45 permanent employees (pp.7, 56); deck and report PAT Rs 10.09 crore against audited
     Rs 10.63 crore.
   - Board outcome of 31-Jan-2026 gives the call due date as 27-Feb-2026; the 2-Mar-2026 outcome
     calls the original date 2-Mar-2026.
7. **Market cap does not reconcile to filed share count.** screener.in Rs 271.25 crore at Rs 33.32
   implies 8.14 crore shares. Filed count is 6,18,28,424 (3,50,35,310 fully paid and 2,67,93,114
   partly paid at 22-Sep-2026). At filed count the value is about Rs 206 crore.
8. **Financials.xlsx** has empty P&L, Quarters, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow sheets. Only the Data
   Sheet is populated.
