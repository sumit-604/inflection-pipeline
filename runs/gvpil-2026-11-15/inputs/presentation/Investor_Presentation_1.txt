Key Dynamics – Durgapur facility
© 2026 GE Power India Limited. All rights reserved. 6
Overview
The Durgapur facility, located in West Bengal, has been instrumental in the manufacturing and supply of
critical power equipment, including power boilers components, pressure vessels, piping, and coal mills for
thermal power plants. The facility comprises of industrial factory unit and a residential township spread
across -661 acres of leasehold land.
For the past years, the factory’s utilization has been limited to service work and non-coal activities. In the
last two years (2023-2025) there has been a significant underutilization of Durgapur’s capacity ~ INR 27
crores average loss per year booked in GEPIL.
GEPIL evaluated multiple options including the demerger of the Durgapur business or retention of the
factory. GEPIL’s key objectives included simplifying GEPIL’s portfolio by exiting the asset, which is today
under-utilized.
The Buyer organization, JSW Energy Limited (“ JSWE”), is an established and Indian public listed energy
company and a well-known name in the industry, and we believe the acquisition of the Durgapur factory by
JSWE will ensure well utilization of the facility in the future in view of the Indian Industry Power demand.

Maximizing Shareholder Value
GEPIL & JSW Energy Strategic Demerger
© 2026 GE Power India Limited. All rights reserved.
7
Overview
GE Power India Limited (GEPIL) is undertaking a Scheme of Arrangement to demerge its Durgapur business unit—specializing in
the manufacture of power boilers, pressure vessels, piping, and coal mills—to JSW Energy Limited.
Operational Continuity : Upon sanction of the Scheme by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), the business will be
transitioned on a “going concern, as-is-where-is” basis, retrospectively effective1st July 2025.
Value Proposition for Shareholders
This strategic initiative is designed to unlock inherent value for GEPIL shareholders through:
• Enhanced Entitlement: Shareholders will receive 10 fully paid-up equity shares of JSW Energy (₹10 each) for every 139 fully
paid-up equity shares of GEPIL (₹10 each).
• Preserved Equity: Existing number of shares owned in GEPIL will remain unchanged, while you gain direct equity participation
in JSW Energy without diluting your current position in GEPIL.
• Strategic Agility: The demerger allows the Durgapur business to pursue a dedicated growth trajectory under JSW Energy,
while enabling GEPIL to streamline operations and accelerate its core services strategy.
Call to Action
The Board of Directors has unanimously approved this resolution. We invite shareholders to exercise their discretion to vote on
the scheme. Why Vote "Yes"?
• Regulatory Efficiency: Supports a smooth and timely transfer process.
• Focused Growth: Provides both entities with the specialized focus and agility required to lead in the evolving energy sector.

Management Assurance on Continuity of Manufacturing and Fabrication Support
• No disruption to manufacturing and fabrication support is expected for the Core Services business. 
• A 5-year manufacturing services agreement with JSW Energy secures reserved capacity at pre-agreed schedules 
and pricing. 
• Concurrent efforts to establish an independent supply chain are progressing well, with full supply chain 
independence targeted in near future. 
• This phased transition is designed to protect order execution and service delivery commitments while building a 
resilient long-term manufacturing ecosystem. 
Shareholder Ownership Continuity and Value Creation Post Demerger
Assume you currently own 139 shares of GEPIL (assuming with a market price of INR 873 per share), you will 
continue to hold the same 139 GEPIL shares after the demerger. In addition, based on the approved share 
entitlement ratio, you will receive 10 shares of JSW Energy (assuming with a market price of INR 550 per share) 
directly.
Result:
• GEPIL holding remains unchanged – 139 shares; equivalent to INR 121,347
• Additional ownership in JSW Energy – 10 shares; equivalent to INR 5,500
• No dilution of your existing stake in GEPIL
