- **The "Marine Valves (33%)" segment label (deck p.12, p.15) does not match the "Marine" row in the
  company's own revenue table (deck p.29), which is 8.34% of FY26 revenue.** It reconciles only by
  adding the separate "Shipbuilding & Repair" row of 24.51%, giving 32.85%. The same addition for
  FY23 gives 0.49%, matching the deck's "0.5% in FY23". The claim is arithmetically defensible and
  the label is misleading. See the card, step 9.
- **Within that block the two rows rotated sharply in FY26**: "Marine" fell from Rs 1,463 lakh to
  Rs 444 lakh while "Shipbuilding & Repair" rose from Rs 538 lakh to Rs 1,305 lakh. Neither category
  is defined anywhere in the deck. A reclassification is at least as likely an explanation as a
  business change.
- **Order book is stated as three different numbers around the same date.** The deck says "Rs 33 cr
  as on May'26" (p.7, p.20). On the call the same day the promoter says "approximately 42 crores"
  (Transcript p.6) and, when challenged, explains that the difference is a Rs 8.55 crore
  letter-of-intent from Shree Refrigerations not yet converted to a purchase order (Transcript p.17).
  The reconciliation is given and it holds; the deck's headline number is the conservative one.
- **Capex is stated as two different sets.** The deck says "4 Automated Test Benches and New VMC
  Machine ordered" (p.20). The call says "2 VMC machines and 5 testing benches" (Transcript p.6). The
  Q1 FY27 business update says "2 Vertical Machining Centres (VMCs); 6 Hydraulic Testing Benches
  Machines". Three counts across three documents in six weeks.
- **The company's own name, website, email and NSE symbol vary across its filings**: "Rappid Valves
  (India) Limited" and "Rapid Valves (India) Limited"; www.rappidvalves.in, www.rapidvalves.in and
  www.rapidvalves.com; and the 9 July letter gives "NSE Symbol: RAPID" where the symbol is RAPPID.
  Cosmetic, and a disclosure-hygiene signal for a company this small.
- The transcript renders the classification-society body as "ISCS"; it is almost certainly IACS, the
  International Association of Classification Societies.
