## PHASE 7: MULTI-STRATEGY SIGNAL EXTRACTION

### GARP — WATCHLIST (fullest reasoning per operator mandate)

### Turnaround — WATCHLIST, early-stage evidence (fullest reasoning per operator mandate)

**Evidence FOR:** (1) The Formulation segment turnaround is real and quantified — segment PBT loss narrowed from −₹237.09L to −₹73.00L (a ~69% improvement, Note 45, p.89), alongside a sharp fall in product returns (Stage 2 Pass 1: −79%). **Evidence AGAINST / caution:** (1) Consolidated PBT/PAT are essentially flat — the segment-level formulation improvement and cost savings have not yet reached the whole-company bottom line, because Unallocable/corporate costs grew 34% YoY (−₹328.03L vs −₹244.18L) and finance costs grew 32.76%, absorbing the API segment's strong profitability (₹912.04L PBT) and the formulation improvement. **Top 3 reasons for WATCHLIST:**
