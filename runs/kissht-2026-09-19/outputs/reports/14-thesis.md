## SECTION 1: ONE-LINE THESIS

Stated as the thesis would be bought. The verdict today is AVOID (Section 7).

"Buying KISSHT at or below ~Rs 350 because AUM compounds at a held 4.57% return on average AUM. That grows
diluted EPS from Rs 29.89 (FY28) to Rs 57.80 (FY31) over three years. The SOTP target is Rs 737.7 at
31-Mar-2030: own book at 0.8x P/B, partner half at 18.62x P/E, about 12.6x FY31 EPS for the whole company.
From Rs 350 that is a 23.8% CAGR. FV CAGR is 21.2%, label COMPOUNDER. The return is fair value compounding
through partner-half PAT. It is not a discount closing. About 2.6 pp of the 23.8% comes from entry below
today-value. Key risk: two partners hold 95% of the parent's DLG pools, and the parent guarantee was 228%
of parent net worth in FY26, both behind a write-off-assisted GNPA. Thesis breaks if: Q2 FY27 Stage 2 >
4.11% or GNPA > 2.92%, or H2 FY27 all-in loss > 11.6% of avg AUM, or a top-two partner cuts its pools by
more than a third."

## NARRATIVE

Kissht lends small sums to young, mass-market Indians through an app. It charges high rates and loses about
11% of the managed book a year. About 5% of AUM is left as profit. Half the profit comes from loans it holds.
The other half comes from loans it finds for two big partners, and it carries their first 5% of losses.

The business is improving. New defaults fell by a third in one year. Funding is cheaper. The founders rebuilt
the model after a 2023 regulatory shock and did not post a loss year. AUM grew 32% in the first half of FY27.

Three facts hold the thesis back. First, the true loss rate is visible only once or twice a year. Second, two
partners carry the half of the business that the valuation values most. Third, one undisclosed date, the
years of an RBI inspection, decides how far the asset-quality numbers can be trusted.

At Rs 369.55 the stock pays a fair price for the base case. It pays nothing for the climb. The framework says
AVOID today because Gate 0 is AVERAGE and the near-term downside is larger than the upside. The operator's
zone near Rs 350 becomes live only after a Gate 0 ruling, a clean Q2 asset-quality test, and a price at or
below Rs 347. The proof that matters arrives on 31-May-2027.
