**Six mandatory diagnostics:**

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes — consolidated revenue from operations **+52.38%** (+54.0% on the deck's forex-inclusive basis). But **a material part is inorganic**: Q1FY27 consolidates a full quarter of the Escorts Kubota railway business (acquired 01-Jun-2025), vs ~1 month in Q1FY26 (Note 2). Single-segment reporting (Note 7) prevents isolating the organic vs inorganic split from the filing (R-A3-F12-01). Deck-implied organic growth is not separable here.
2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand, contract, or stay flat YoY?** **Contracted.** Q1FY27 operating EBITDA margin **23.26% vs 23.72% in Q1FY26 = −46 bps** (/rev from ops); on the deck's forex-inclusive basis **23.09% vs 23.80% = −71 bps** ("~0.7% lower", deck line 690, P-A3-F17-03). Against the FY26 baseline of 24.7–24.88% the compression is **~160 bps** (PR-FD9, tripwire 6). Management cause: "product mix and higher input prices, partially offset by operating leverage" (deck line 692).
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-Other Income) grow YoY?** **Yes, strongly: +65.28%** — *faster* than reported PAT (+44.71% owners). Because Other Income FELL 41.85% and there was no exceptional benefit this year, **reported PAT growth understates operating improvement, not overstates it.** The headline growth is operationally real (the opposite of a treasury-flattered print). **FX-neutral qualifier (A5 graft — now netted into the quality conclusion, not left as a management question only):** the +65.28% **retains the net foreign-exchange line**, which swung favourably from **−Rs 3.01 Cr (Q1FY26) to +Rs 9.17 Cr (Q1FY27)** — a **+Rs 12.18 Cr favourable YoY swing** (consolidated results extract L470: forex 91.69 mn vs −30.09 mn; deck L701 concedes "Revenue includes net gain from foreign exchange", P-A3-F16-04). The remaining cautions stand: the improvement is **margin-dilutive** at the EBITDA line and **part-inorganic** (railway) at the revenue line.
4. **What drove the gap between core-operating-PBT growth (+65% / ~+55% ex-forex) and reported-PAT growth (+45%)?** Walk (Rs Cr): Other Income **−18.49** (44.19→25.70), Finance costs **+5.15** (drag), D&A **+9.89** (drag), Exceptional **+9.17** favourable base (Q1FY26 had a charge, Q1FY27 nil), Tax **+18.90** higher absolute (ETR 26.34%→25.91%, broadly flat). 5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** D&A +14.76% vs revenue +52% — **no absorption gap** (D&A growing far slower than revenue; capex is being absorbed by volume). Finance costs +96.53% but off a tiny base (Rs 5.33→10.48 Cr) and immaterial against Rs 226 Cr operating EBIT; the company is **net cash** (Net Debt/EBITDA −1.06x, deck line 721). No early-warning capex-absorption signal.
