**LBF-1: Freight gross-up in revenue.** 🔴 RED FLAG (disclosure gap, not a
fraud finding).
The AR's Note 23 "Revenue from Operations" (standalone p.135, consolidated
p.180) shows only one line, "Sale of Products (Net of Discount)": Rs
20,619.03 lakh FY26 vs Rs 15,645.08 lakh FY25 (both statements, identical).
NO note anywhere in the AR — not Note 23, not the accounting-policy Note
1.2.2(f) Revenue Recognition (standalone p.120-121, consolidated identical
wording), not Note 30 Other Expenses — discloses that freight was added to
revenue and neutralised in other expenses. A full-text search of this
201-page AR for "freight," "neutralise," and "gross-up" in that context
returns nothing. The disclosure exists ONLY in the FY26 audited results
filing to the exchange (runs/titanbio-2026-09-10/work/extracted/results/
2026-05-30_FY26_audited_results.txt, line 385): "Freight amount has been
added in revenue from operations for the purpose of calculation of sales
including GST in current year. Freight also added in total in other
expenses to neutralise the impact of its addition in revenue in current
year." This is a material accounting-policy-adjacent disclosure that
appears in the Reg 33 results filing footnote but is absent from the
audited AR notes proper — a disclosure inconsistency across filings by the
same company for the same year.
Cross-check: AR_FY2025.txt's own Note 22 (Revenue from Operations, p.99 and
consolidated p.130 of that year's AR) shows FY25 revenue as Rs 15,645.08
lakh — IDENTICAL to the FY25 comparative shown in the FY26 AR. The FY25
figure was NOT restated for any freight gross-up between the two ARs.
Note 30 (Other Expenses, standalone p.136): Cartage & Freight Outward Rs
584.41 lakh FY26 vs Rs 426.95 lakh FY25 (+36.9%, matches MD&A commentary at
p.101 exactly). If the entire incremental freight amount (Rs 584.41 lakh)
was added to FY26 revenue with no equivalent addition in the FY25
comparative, ex-gross-up FY26 revenue would be approximately Rs 20,034.62
lakh, i.e. growth of ~28.0% over FY25's Rs 15,645.08 lakh, versus the
reported 31.8%. This is an estimate built from the two disclosed cartage
numbers, not from an AR-stated reconciliation — none exists in the notes.

**LBF-2: Peptech Biosciences reconciliation.** 🟢 RESOLVED, no residual gap.
Consolidated Statement of Profit & Loss (p.161) shows the actual bridge
between standalone and consolidated PAT explicitly: "Share in profit of
associate: Rs 243.80 lakh" FY26 (Rs 326.05 lakh FY25), added to standalone
PAT Rs 2,744.72 lakh to give consolidated PAT Rs 2,988.52 lakh (Note 32 EPS

Consolidated Note 5 Investments (p.171) shows the build-up: Peptech
"Add: Share in Profit of Associate" Rs 239.91 lakh FY26 (fully-paid
tranche) + Titan Media "Add: Share in Profit of Associate" Rs 3.73 lakh =
Rs 243.64 lakh, reconciling to the P&L line within rounding (the residual
Rs 0.16 lakh sits in the OCI share-of-associate line, "(0.17)", p.161).
This RESOLVES the apparent AOC-1 mismatch flagged pre-run: AOC-1 (Annexure-1
to Directors' Report, p.80) states Peptech "Profit... Considered in
Consolidation: Rs 651.12 Lakhs" and Titan Media "Rs 7.71 Lakhs." These are
NOT TBL's proportionate shares — they are the ASSOCIATES' OWN full-year
profit figures. Proof: Rs 651.12 lakh x 36.87% holding = Rs 240.09 lakh,
matching Note 5's Rs 239.91 lakh addition to within rounding; Rs 7.71 lakh
x 48.44% = Rs 3.73 lakh, an exact match to Note 5. The AOC-1 column header
"Considered in Consolidation" is confirmably the associate's full profit
(as opposed to a pre-acquisition portion that would fall under "Not
Considered"), not TBL's equity-pickup amount — a labelling point that could
mislead a reader who assumes AOC-1 states the parent's share, but the
underlying numbers are internally consistent across the AOC-1, Note 5, and
the consolidated P&L. Peptech's implied own FY26 PAT is therefore
approximately Rs 1,766 lakh (Rs 651.12 lakh / 36.87%) against an implied
100% net worth of roughly Rs 201.6 cr (Rs 7,432.90 lakh / 36.87%) — an
implied RoNW near 8.8%, a genuine external data point about the associate,
NOT drawn from TBL's own audited notes (Peptech's own financials are not in
this AR) and NOT to be anchored beyond this arithmetic implication.
Carrying value tie-out: Peptech consolidated investment = cost Rs 1,230.01
lakh + cumulative retained equity-pickup Rs 807.01 lakh (opening) + Rs
239.91 lakh (FY26 addition) = Rs 2,276.93 lakh; Titan Media = cost Rs
406.88 lakh + cumulative pickup Rs 19.95 lakh + Rs 3.73 lakh = Rs 430.56
lakh. Sum Rs 2,707.49 lakh matches Note 5's consolidated "Total unquoted
investments" line exactly (p.171).

**LBF-4: FY26 investing outflow Rs 34.41 cr split.** 🟡 WATCH (a genuine
inconsistency found, plus a capital-allocation signal).
Consolidated/Standalone Cash Flow Statement (p.117 standalone, p.162
consolidated), both identical: "Investments in debt instruments and equity
instruments (2,515.67)" lakh + "Purchase of property, plant and equipment
including capital work in progress (742.83)" lakh + proceeds Rs 0.43 lakh
(PPE sale) + Rs 3.48 lakh (bank deposits) = **Net cash used - investing
activities Rs (3,254.59) lakh (Rs 32.55 cr)**. This does NOT match the
MD&A's own stated figure of "Investing activities net outflow amounting
to Rs (3,441.49) Lacs" (Rs 34.41 cr, p.104), repeated twice in the MD&A
table. The two numbers, both in the same Annual Report, differ by Rs
186.90 lakh (Rs 1.87 cr) — an internal inconsistency between the MD&A and
the audited Cash Flow Statement. The audited statement (Rs 32.55 cr) is
the authoritative figure; the MD&A number appears to be either a drafting
error or references a different (unreconciled) computation.
The split itself, per Note 5 Investments (standalone p.128, consolidated
p.171): the entire increase in investments (Rs 4,965.67 lakh standalone
balance FY26 vs Rs 2,449.99 lakh FY25, a rise of Rs 2,515.68 lakh) is
"Investments Measured at Fair Value through Profit & Loss" — "Investments
in debt instruments quoted, fully paid up," rising from Rs 813.10 lakh to
Rs 3,328.78 lakh. This is a quoted-debt-instrument portfolio, not equity or
capacity-building. Per Note 2(a) PPE, gross additions were Rs 740.08 lakh
(buildings Rs 241.58 lakh, plant & equipment Rs 417.25 lakh, vehicles Rs
72.30 lakh, office/furniture Rs 8.95 lakh); Note 2(b) CWIP additions were
Rs 573.92 lakh with Rs 569.57 lakh capitalised in-year (closing CWIP Rs
172.73 lakh, almost entirely "less than 1 year" ageing, p.126). So of the
Rs 32.55 cr audited investing outflow: roughly Rs 25.16 cr (77%) went into
a quoted-debt financial-asset portfolio and roughly Rs 7.43 cr (23%) into
PP&E/CWIP additions. No capacity figure, production-line description, or
commissioning date is disclosed anywhere in the notes for the PPE/CWIP
additions — Note 46(ix) only confirms title deeds are in the Company's
name; there is no "estimated amount of contracts remaining to be executed
on capital account" commitment disclosure under Note 36(II) Commitments,
which lists only "Uncalled liability on partly paid-up shares" (Rs 406.86
lakh). Capacity/commissioning detail: **NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT**.
Capital-allocation signal: Note 45 Ratios, "Return on investment (%)" fell
from 7.04% (FY25) to 3.64% (FY26), a -3.40 percentage point / ~48% relative
decline, even as the investment base nearly quadrupled. The growing pool
of cash parked in quoted debt instruments is earning a falling return —
worth watching as a capital-allocation quality question, separate from the
capex-vs-financial-asset split itself.
