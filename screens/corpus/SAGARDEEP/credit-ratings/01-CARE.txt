About the Company 
Sagardeep Alloys Ltd (SAL) was incorporated in 2007 as Sagar Deep Alloys Pvt. Ltd. and was subsequently converted in to 
a public limited company (closely held) in 2012. SAL is engaged in manufacturing of various copper and brass products as 
well as trading of Stainless Steel (SS), Mild Steel (MS), copper and brass products. SAL has its manufacturing facility (ISO 
9001:2008 certified) located near Ahmedabad, Gujarat with an installed manufacturing capacity of 2400 Metric Tonne Per 
Annum (MTPA) as on March 31, 2015. 
During FY15 (refers to the period April 1 to March 31), SAL reported a total operating income of Rs.132.61 crore (FY14: 
Rs.155.47 crore) with a PAT of Rs.0.73 crore (FY14: Rs.0.67 crore).
