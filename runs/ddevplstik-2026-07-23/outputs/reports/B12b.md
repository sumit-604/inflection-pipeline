## PART A — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, before reading B05/B06)

Anchors are (call, speaker, page).

1. **BESS revenue guidance walked down inside two quarters of launch, including a same-call self-correction.** In Q3 FY26 opening remarks the CEO framed BESS as "revenue in the range of Rs 800-Rs 900 crores from just 1 gigawatt... projected to contribute around 20% to our overall revenue" (Q3 FY26, Ddev Suranna, p.3). Minutes later the CFO issued "just a correction in the statement, it is expected to be in the range of within Rs. 300-Rs. 500 odd crores" for FY27 (Q3 FY26, Arihant Bothra, p.5-6). By Q4 FY26 this was cut again to "close to INR200 crores... INR200-plus crores of revenue will be a good breakeven point" and "close to INR250-odd crores" (Q4 FY26, Arihant Bothra, p.7 and p.11). Two downward revisions of a headline number for a brand-new, capital-intensive, unproven vertical. **Severity: MAJOR (borderline CRITICAL — a two-quarter downward-revision pattern on a thesis trigger).**

2. **FY26 volume guidance missed.** Guided "210,000 tons to 220,000 tons" (Q2 FY26, Arihant Bothra, p.12-13); actual "volumes for financial year 2026 stood at 2,01,370 metric tons" (Q4 FY26, Arihant Bothra, p.4). Management reframed rather than reconciled: "Overall volume growth of 6% would have approached 10% under normal conditions" (Q4 FY26, Ddev Surana, p.2-3). **Severity: MAJOR.**

3. **132 kV XLPE trial slipped repeatedly and its outcome was never actually confirmed.** Q1 FY26: product "ready," trial expected "last quarter of FY '26" (Q1, Rajesh Kothari, p.7). Q2 FY26: "this is a topic where we have not seen any progress yet... two key customers... running almost at full capacity," pushed to "January to March 2026" with a newly-agreed third customer (Q2, Rajesh Kothari, p.6-7). Q3 FY26: not raised by any analyst, no update. Q4 FY26: only an implicit "we have reached up to the capacity of 132 kV and 66 and 132" (Q4, Arihant Bothra, p.19) — no confirmed successful trial, customer approval, or commercial supply was ever stated. A promised milestone that slid two quarters and then dissolved into a passing reference. **Severity: MAJOR.**

4. **Volume growth materially lagged cable-maker peers; the market-share question was answered but not resolved.** Analysts pressed twice in Q2: "if you look at the wire and cable players like KEI and Polycab, they have shown about 15% volume growth" against DDev's 8% (Q2, Bobby Jay, p.8), and "only power cable companies... have also reported almost like 20%, 25% volume growth" (Q2, Rishabh Agarwal, p.10-11). Management attributed the gap to product-mix and monsoon and stated "we do not have an access to all the numbers of every cable company" (Q2, Rajesh Kothari, p.7-8). Plausible but unverified; demand/share risk if the gap persists. **Severity: MAJOR.**

5. **BESS customer pipeline is entirely pre-order/pre-MOU despite Rs 800-900 Cr/GWh revenue weighting.** Q3 FY26: Tiwari read a list of ~15 government/semi-government and ~30 private names, all framed as "targeting" (Q3, Rakesh Tiwari, p.7-8). Q4 FY26: "We had already discussed with all the Tier 1 developer... after factory readiness, we'll sit together and we'll sign MOU" (Q4, Rakesh Tiwari, p.9-10). No binding order exists behind a vertical the model leans on. **Severity: MAJOR.**

6. **A fresh external shock is invoked as the primary miss-explanation every single quarter.** Monsoon + US tariffs (Q2, Rajesh Kothari, p.6), US-tariff volatility with a Nov recovery then mid-Dec relapse (Q3, Rajesh Kothari, p.8), and a newly-introduced Israel-Iran conflict "escalated from 28th February" with RM up ">50%" (Q4, Ddev Surana / Arihant Bothra, p.2-3, p.5). Each is independently real, but no "clean" quarter has yet tested the base business. **Severity: MINOR-MAJOR (watch-item).**

7. **BESS return claims sit uneasily against BESS margin claims.** ROCE guided at "high double digits in the range of 25%-30%" with 2-3 year payback (Q3, Arihant Bothra, p.11-12), before a single unit shipped. Yet in Q4 the same business is described with "initial EBITDA margin... 5% to 8% lower than the core compounding business" (i.e. ~3-6% on ~11% core) and merely "targeting breakeven this financial year" (Q4, Varun Singh / Arihant Bothra, p.10-11). A 25-30% ROCE on a thin-margin, import-dependent (China) assembly line is an optimistic pairing. **Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.**

8. **The headline EBITDA-per-ton metric management steers investors toward includes non-operating other income.** "We see the numbers on the basis of EBITDA per ton and that too on the gross basis including the other income" (Q1, Rajesh Kothari, p.11), confirmed again when asked "we are also factoring the other income component into it... Yes" (Q2/Q4 framing; Saket Kapoor / Arihant Bothra, Q2 p.17). Other income is "majorly... interest income and exchange gain" on FD/mutual-fund treasury balances (Q2, Arihant Bothra, p.10). The metric is thus partly treasury-inflated — a quality-of-earnings caveat on the number management repeatedly redirects the margin question to. **Severity: MINOR (amounts small: ~Rs 1.5 Cr Q1, ~Rs 10.8 Cr Q2 against Rs 75-80 Cr EBITDA).**

9. **FY26 "growth" is mostly price, not volume.** Revenue "+13% year-on-year" (Q4, Arihant Bothra, p.4) against volume +6%; the gap is RM-driven selling-price pass-through, not the guided volume route. Disclosed, not hidden. **Severity: MINOR.**

10. **Token interim dividend versus reported earnings.** "Rs. 0.50 paisa when we have posted EPS of Rs. 14 for 9 months" (Q3, Saket Kapoor, p.16); management answer was generic ("just an interim number... followed the same benchmark," Q3, Arihant Bothra, p.18). **Severity: MINOR.**

11. **FY28-capex disclosure promised and not delivered.** "By next quarter, a lot of details are being published or revealed" on FY28 capacity/capex (Q3, Arihant Bothra, p.6); Q4 gave only FY27 capex (Rs 175 Cr) with no distinct FY28 figure. **Severity: MINOR.**

12. **Adani/UltraTech PVC-offtake milestone dropped after its guided window.** Justified the 44k->69k PVC build; guided to start "first quarter of the calendar year 2026, or the worst case, first quarter of financial year '27" (Q2, Rajesh Kothari, p.11-12); by Q4 no status update on actual offtake. **Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.**

13. **BESS FY30 contribution math does not obviously tie.** 5 GWh installed "with each gigawatt projected to generate approximately INR800 crores to INR900 crores" implies ~Rs 4,000-4,500 Cr, yet the guided FY30 incremental top line is "INR2,000 crores to INR2,500 crores" (Q4, Ddev Surana, p.3). Reconcilable only if sold volume by FY30 is ~2.5-3 GWh (installed != sold), which was never spelled out. **Severity: MINOR.**

14. **Peer cross-read: DDev's "RM normalized by April 2026" is contradicted by an independent same-shock witness.** DDev: prices "comparatively stabilized... stabilize from April" (Q4, Arihant Bothra, p.5-6). APCOTEXIND, describing the identical event on its 7-May-2026 call: "March 1st is when the whole war situation started," "crude prices have gone up by about 70%," "even though there is a ceasefire today, the Strait of Hormuz is still closed," "we are covered till the end of June" (APCOTEXIND Jun-2026 transcript, Abhiraj Choksey / Vivek Thakur, p.5-6, p.20-21). The shock's existence/scale/timing corroborate DDev; the "normalized by April" resolution does not. **Severity: MAJOR (peer contradiction on a sub-claim).**

## PART D — FINDINGS (consolidated)

- **F1 (MAJOR)** — B05 promise-delivery table (Section 2A / `promise_delivery`) classifies the 132 kV XLPE trial as **"delivered (implied)"** and counts it toward the 5-delivered tally, though no call in the set confirms a successful trial, customer approval, or commercial supply — only a passing "we have reached up to the capacity of 132 kV" (Q4, Arihant Bothra, p.19). Correct classification is unconfirmed/partial; the tally is more honestly **4 delivered / 4 partial / 4 missed**. Decision and B-grade survive, and B05 was transparent about the uncertainty in its `input_gaps` and `timeline_slippages`, hence MAJOR not CRITICAL.
- **F2 (MINOR)** — Management's preferred EBITDA-per-ton metric includes non-operating other income (treasury interest/FX); B05 presents EBITDA/ton disclosure as an unqualified transparency positive (Section 3B, tone table) without the quality caveat. Source partly in the 3-cap-excluded Q1 call (p.11).
- **F3 (MINOR)** — BESS customer pipeline is entirely pre-order/pre-MOU (Q3 p.7-8; Q4 p.9-10); B05 notes "pre-revenue" but does not elevate the total absence of a binding order to a stated red flag given BESS's revenue weighting.
- **F4 (MINOR)** — BESS ROCE 25-30% (Q3 p.11-12) versus initial EBITDA margins management itself pegs 5-8% below the ~11% core (Q4 p.10-11); B05 captures the over-promotion generically but not this specific internal tension.

No CRITICAL findings. B05 and B06 are strong, well-anchored analyses; the criticisms above are refinements, not corrections that move the decision.

**credibility_grade concur:** Concur with B (Good), at the lower end. Core capacity/margin/export guidance was genuinely delivered on schedule, which justifies a passing grade; but the 132 kV "delivered" classification is generous, BESS guidance moved twice in its first two disclosed quarters, and the base business has not yet been tested by a shock-free quarter. B is defensible; B- would also be defensible. Not higher.
