## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the transcripts alone, with anchors)

### R1. Gross-margin 67-68% guidance was reiterated across FOUR calls, not three, and missed
Q1 FY26 (Aug-2025): "Gross margin for the quarter was 67.7% ... We expect the gross margin to
remain at around 68% on an annual basis" (Aug-2025, Rahul, p.4). Then 67-68% band in Q2 (Nov, p.4),
Q3 (Feb, p.9), Q4 (May, p.6). FY26 exit gross margin 65.5% (May, Rahul, p.6). So the band was
carried and missed over four consecutive calls, one more than B05 credits.

### R2. Restaurant-count slippage (~270-275 → 265 → 262)
Referenced as prior guidance by Viraj Mehta in Q3 (Feb, p.11: "end of last year and even in the
first quarter you had mentioned we should end the year at around 270, 275"); revised to ~265
(Feb, p.11-12); actual FY26 close 262 (May, p.5). Note: the 270-275 baseline is NOT in the Aug-2025
transcript I read — Aug-2025 gave "300-325 by FY27" (Kayum, p.3), so the 270-275 comes from a call
outside the four-call set; B05's "referenced retrospectively" caveat is correct.

### R3. Internal inconsistency in FY27 gross-margin recovery guidance, same call
Q4 FY26: "approximately 100 basis points to 200 basis points of gross margin recovery in FY'27
over and above the Q4 numbers" (May, p.6, financial-detail prepared remarks) vs "approximately 100
to 150 basis points of gross margin recovery" (May, p.8, priorities prepared remarks) vs "improve
... by 100 basis points to 150 basis points" (May, p.10, Q&A to Devanshu Bansal). The 100-200
figure sits in prepared remarks and the 100-150 in both prepared remarks and Q&A — B05's "opening
100-150 vs Q&A 100-200" attribution is directionally reversed, but the underlying inconsistency is
real.

### R4. Unreconciled "25% revenue growth every quarter FY27" analyst attribution
Jatin (Q4, May, p.19-20) attributes to the Q3 call: "we were saying that we are expecting around
25% revenue growth in the coming next quarters." Not found verbatim in the Q3 transcript — Q3 gave
"25% transaction growth" and an "annual run rate of around INR1,500 crores" (Feb, p.8), not 25%
revenue growth per quarter. Management did not correct the attribution, only noted "we have
delivered 23%" (May, p.20).

### R5. Middle East input-cost inflation, unquantified, "difficult to predict"
2 of 8 international restaurants (Bahrain, Dubai) impacted by West Asia crisis inflation (May, p.6,
p.12). Management: "how this shapes up, it's very difficult to predict, frankly, today" (May, p.12).
No bps/currency-adjusted segment margin number given despite Devanshu Bansal probing local-currency
SSSG (May, p.12).

### R6. Renewed expansion pace (40/yr toward 400-425 by FY30) revives the FY23 over-expansion pattern
Rushabh Sharedalal (Q4, May, p.14): "the last time that we did that, it really hasn't worked out
well and we had to shut many stores." Management concedes "in FY23, some of the restaurants didn't
work out. We closed some of these in Tier 2, Tier 3 markets" and the reassurance ("learnings ...
always been incorporated") is self-assessed (May, p.14). Foreshadowed in Aug-2025: Viraj Mehta and
Harshid Vohra both pressed on whether to stop expanding until the core stabilises (Aug, p.6, p.15).

### R7. FY26 actual capex never reconciled against the ~INR125cr guidance
Q2 guided ~INR125cr FY26 capex (Nov, p.7). Q4 gives the FY27 INR140cr plan in detail (May, p.9) but
states no FY26 actual capex figure. Promise-delivery loop on capex not closed.

### R8. Intra-call data inconsistencies (already minor)
Q3: consolidated pre-Ind AS ROM stated 15.7% (Feb, p.4) then "quarter 3 was around 16%" (Feb, p.15).
Q4: Premium CDR dine-in volume growth 28% (Kayum, May, p.3) vs "approximately 27%" (Rahul, May, p.5).
Low severity.

### R9. Repeated "is the model broken / why do you lag peers" deflection across Q1 AND Q2
This is the central bear question, and it was put to management in two consecutive quarters, dodged
both times via a "you can't compare us to QSRs / can't discuss listed peers" reframe:
- Q1 FY26 (Aug), Viraj Mehta: "they basically suffered negative SSSGs far later than us, and they
  are reviving far before us ... we are not recovering even after they are recovering. So, is this
  a business model problem?" Rahul: "both businesses are different ... very difficult to make a
  like-to-like comparison between QSRs and us" (Aug, p.6-7).
- Q2 FY26 (Nov), Mathur Rathi: "the rest of the industry, at least the listed players, they turned
  SSSG positive 2 quarters back, whereas we are still clocking negative SSSG" and "there is already
  a fire and you go and start a new fire." Rahul: "I can't discuss on the performance of other
  listed players" (Nov, p.11-12).
B05 saw the Q2 instance (and its own 2B labels it "deflection/minimisation") but, lacking the Q1
call, recorded `repeated_evasions: []` and "NO REPEATED UNANSWERED QUESTIONS FOUND." It is a
repeated 2-quarter dodge. Mitigant: the underlying concern was substantially resolved by the actual
Q3/Q4 SSSG inflection, so decision impact is limited — but the pipeline affirmatively claimed zero
repeated evasions, which is wrong.

### R10. Management's OWN Q1 admission of competitor price drops contradicts the four-call
"unchanged competitive intensity" claim
Management asserts unchanged competitive intensity in Q1, Q2 and Q3 (Aug, p.10 "the competitive
intensity has not increased as such ... at least stable"; Nov, p.17 "we have not seen any increase
in competitive intensity"; Feb, p.15 "we have not seen the competitive intensity change"). Yet in
the SAME Q1 call management volunteers the opposite at the market level where it matters most:
"There has been a response, we are seeing some drop in prices by competition as a number which does
not sustain at a margin level" (Aug, p.8) and, on Bangalore/South, "There is also a pressure on
pricing in these markets, which is taking away some business ... the number of trade area, newer
a-la-carte options that we are seeing is slightly higher" (Aug, p.10). This is an internal
contradiction inside UFBL's own transcripts, independent of B06's peer-based contradiction, and it
directly undercuts the "no defensive discounting needed" premise beneath the margin-recovery story.
B05 missed it (excluded Q1) and even called the unchanged-intensity claim a "positive credibility
signal — no shifting story" (B05 §3A).

### R11. Back-end/corporate-cost "normal" quietly reset upward
Q1 FY26 (Aug, p.14): corporate cost "over a longer term ... should not go beyond 5% to 6%.
Historically, pre-COVID, these numbers have always been around 5% to 6%." By Q4 FY26 back-end cost
had stepped to 7.1% and the guided path is 6.5% then 6% "over the longer term" (May, p.7). So the
FY27 9-10% EBITDA bridge leans on back-end compressing to 6.5% — which is merely a partial return
toward the old 5-6% "historical normal," now reframed as operating-leverage upside. B05 caught the
6.1%→7.1% step-up (as honest-admission) but not that the structural baseline had been reset above
management's own stated historical range. (B06 separately shows peer overhead ratios falling, not
rising.)

### R12. Network ceiling drift, 300-325 → 300+ (MINOR)
Aug-2025 target "300-325 restaurants by FY27" (Kayum, p.3); later calls say "300-plus by FY27" and
"400-425 by FY30" (Feb, p.11; May, p.8). The 325 upper bound quietly disappears. Cosmetic.

### R13. Marketing-spend baseline ambiguity (MINOR)
Aug-2025: "just for our core dine-in business, it is around 3% right now" (Aug, p.14). Q3/Q4: "3%
of sales ... new steady state" and "1.9% of revenue to 3% of revenue" step-up (Feb, p.8; May, p.7).
The 1.9%→3% (+110bps) framing uses a different denominator (consolidated revenue) than the Q1
"~3% of core dine-in," so the step-up magnitude is fuzzier than B05's clean +110bps reads. Cosmetic.

## PART 4 — CREDIBILITY GRADE

B05 assigned grade **B**. I concur with B, and note a defensible lean toward **B-**: two thesis-
central items the pipeline missed both cut against management candour — (a) the repeated 2-quarter
deflection of the "why do you lag peers / is the model broken" question (R9), which B05 wrongly
recorded as zero repeated evasions, and (b) management's own Q1 admission of competitor price drops
and pricing pressure in South/Bangalore (R10), which contradicts the four-call "unchanged
competitive intensity" claim that B05 characterised as a credibility positive. Offsetting these:
the results genuinely inflected (8.2%→14.4% SSSG), disclosure quality in Q3/Q4 is strong (volunteered
ROM bridge, monthly gross-margin trend), and promise-delivery is 5-of-9-plus. Net: B holds; do not
grade higher.
