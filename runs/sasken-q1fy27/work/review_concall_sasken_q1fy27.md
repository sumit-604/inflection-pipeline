## 2. MANAGEMENT-GRADE DETERMINATION (the single most important output)

**Prior: Grade B, STRUCTURALLY CAPPED.** Role 4 capped the grade at B on the rationale "Sasken hosts no live earnings calls, so no Q&A, no promise-vs-delivery scoring, no credibility ratio, no archetype; the standard Role 5 machinery cannot run and the grade cannot rise to A regardless of results."

The load-bearing assumption behind the cap is broken. This was the first live call, with the promoter (CMD and CEO Rajiv Modi) on the line answering eight analyst Q&A blocks directly, and management committed to recurring quarterly calls.

The credibility-testing mechanism the cap rested on is restored. Remove the "no-concall / no-Q&A-possible" rationale from the thesis. The B ceiling is no longer structural.

### 2B. But no upgrade is earned this call

The cap lifting only removes an artificial ceiling; it does not by itself move the grade up. On substance this call did not earn an upgrade, for four reasons:
1. **Zero of 12 outstanding results-review questions were answered specifically** (7 partial, 2 evaded, 3 not addressed; Section 4). For a call whose entire purpose was to restore the pressure test, zero specific answers on the twelve standing questions is a weak substance result.
2. **Guidance was declined at least four times** (margins t35 l.71 and t27 l.55; revenue targets in "black and white" t23 l.47; vertical and offering split t15 l.31 and t17 l.35). Specificity ratio is low (Section 3).
3. **Management guided the single most important gate signal the wrong way.** The CFO volunteered that FY27 will also carry "a large consumption of cash" (t37 l.75). Management is guiding against pass-criterion 3 (consolidated CFO positive).
4. **The headline 60x4x3 timeline slipped** for the first time, from three years to "one or two more years" (t9 l.19; t23 l.47).

### 2C. What offsets, and what would earn an upgrade

Offsetting candour is real and should be credited qualitatively (it is why the grade holds at B and does not fall): the promoter was present and direct; the CFO gave a frank cash-consumption admission rather than spinning it, and explicitly denied a collection problem (t37 l.75, *"not that we've had any challenges in our collections"*); and the software-services margin-recovery history was corroborated on the record (t27 l.55).

**What would earn an upgrade to A next quarter or beyond:** (a) two to three quarters of delivery against the commitments logged here (Section 7), producing a trailing-4 credibility ratio above 80%; (b) a published consolidated (or H1) CFO figure; (c) disclosure of at least one of the currently withheld items (Top-3 concentration, SSTPL revenue, or a vertical split). Until then the grade stays B on merit, not on structure.
