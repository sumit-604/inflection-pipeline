Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited continues to build on its growth momentum, delivering a strong
performance during the second quarter and half year ended 30th September, 2026. During the quarter, Revenue
from Operations stood at Rs. 403.5 Cr, registering a robust growth of 58.2% YoY, supported by sustained demand
and improved business performance across the Company's Chemical and Fertiliser segments. From a profitability
perspective, the Company delivered EBITDA of Rs. 50 Cr with an EBITDA margin of approximately 12.0%,
reflecting a growth of 88 YoY. Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at Rs. 35.7 Cr, registering a significant growth of 96.2%
YoY. For the first half of FY27, Revenue from Operations reached Rs. 683.6 Cr, while PAT stood at Rs. 58.6 Cr
registering a significant growth of 50% demonstrating continued operational strength and profitable growth.
In line with its strategic growth objectives, the Board of Directors has approved a new capital expenditure plan of
Rs. 70 Cr at Ratnagiri Unit 5 for the establishment of a new Chemical Plant with an annual capacity of 3,000 MTPA.
The project is expected to be commissioned by March 2028 and is aimed at catering to the growing demand from
customers. The investment will be funded through a combination of internal accruals and preferential issuance of
Fully Convertible Warrants to the Promoter, subject to requisite approvals. This expansion further strengthens the
Company's capacity enhancement roadmap and its commitment to scaling up operations.
To support the proposed expansion and strengthen its capital base, the Board has also approved the issuance of
up to 8,54,060 Fully Convertible Warrants to the Promoter, Mr. Punit Gopikishan Makharia, Chairman and
Managing Director, at an issue price of Rs. 526.90 per warrant, aggregating up to Rs. 45 Cr, subject to
shareholders' and other applicable approvals. The proposed fund raising reflects the Promoter's continued
commitment towards the Company's long-term growth strategy and expansion plans.
With a strong focus on integrated manufacturing, operational efficiencies, circular manufacturing and sustainable
energy adoption, Shree Pushkar continues to pursue a disciplined approach towards growth and capital allocation.
The Company's ongoing and planned capacity expansions, supported by a robust operational platform and
strategic investments, are expected to strengthen its market position, enhance profitability and support long-term
value creation for shareholders.
