Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
With reference to your query regarding “Segment details not submitted on consolidated 
basis” in respect of the Audited Financial Results for the quarter and half year ended 
September 30, 2025, we submit as under: 
 
 
Valiant Laboratories Limited (the Holding Company) is engaged in the manufacture of 
Paracetamol. Valiant Advanced Sciences Private Limited, its wholly owned subsidiary, 
is engaged in the manufacture of Acetic Anhydride, which is a key raw material used in 
the production of Paracetamol. Accordingly, the operations of the Holding Company 
and its subsidiary form part of an integrated manufacturing process within the same 
value chain. 
 
The Group’s operations are managed as a single business and are reviewed by the Chief 
Operating Decision Maker (CODM) on a consolidated basis. The financial performance 
and allocation of resources are assessed as one integrated operation and not as separate 
business segments. 
 
Accordingly, in terms of Ind AS 108 – Operating Segments, the Group has only one 
operating segment. Since there are no separately identifiable reportable segments, 
separate segment information is not required to be disclosed in the consolidated 
financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025. 
 
Hence, the consolidated financial results submitted do not contain segment-wise 
disclosure. 
 
However, from the subsequent quarter onwards, the same will be appropriately 
disclosed in the notes to the financial statements submitted to the Exchange. 
 
We trust the above clarifies the matter.
