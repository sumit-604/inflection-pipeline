## 3. Flags from the documents

- A Company Secretary resigned with immediate effect after a short tenure and
  was replaced the next day. On its own this means little. On a name with no
  other disclosure it is worth recording. Flag, not investigated.
- The lease is signed for the company by a director, with the lessor named as
  an individual. There is nothing in the corpus that says whether the lessor is
  connected to the promoters. Flag as unverified, not as a finding.
- The absence of any financial filing in the index is itself the largest flag.
  It may be a Bull AI coverage gap for a small BSE SME name rather than a
  company failure to file. **This card cannot tell those two apart, and says
  so.**
