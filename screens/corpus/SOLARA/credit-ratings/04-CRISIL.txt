 Detailed Rationale 

 CRISIL Ratings has   reaffirmed its ratings on the bank loan facilities of   Solara Active Pharma Sciences Ltd (Solara) at ‘CRISIL BBB/ Negative/ CRISIL A3+’ . 

 The rating factor s in the weaker than expected operating performance of the company resulting in weaker accruals in fiscal 2024 . Despite moderate recovery in the Q4 of FY24, t he company has reported operating income of Rs.1288 .92 cr ore for FY24 with net loss of Rs.566 cr ore largely led by inventory and receivable write off . Weaker operating performance and one-time expenses have resulted in negative cash accruals against sizeable repayment obligations in fiscal 2024 . Moreover, the bank lines were utilized at an average 9 5 % in   the 12 months ended December 202 3 . The financial risk profile of the company is further weakened with net worth of Rs.545.33 crore for FY 24 against Rs.1083.01 crore in FY23 owing to weaker operating performance. 

 H owever , the company is in the process of raising equity through rights issue which is expected to close in by Q1 of FY25 which should aid in improving the fin ancial and liquidity profile of the company. Improvement in business performance in current fiscal and quantum and ti meliness of fund raising would remain key rating monitorable s over the medium term . 

 The ratings continue to reflect Solara's established market position in its key active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), such as Ibuprofen and Praziquantel, and strong relationships with customers and suppliers. The ratings also factor in the company's moderate financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to regulatory changes, large working capital requirement, and volatility in operating profitability.

 Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description 

 Strengths : 

 Established market position in key APIs, along with strong customer and supplier relationships: Solara has a strong portfolio of APIs in key therapeutic segments, with expertise in anthelmintic, anti-malaria, anti-infective and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory. Furthermore, it has been increasing its focus on the   non-steroidal anti-inflammatory segment by adding capacity and working on other therapeutic segments. Solara has a diversified customer base, with more exposure to regulated markets. Its longstandin g presence in the industry has helped Solara build healthy relationships with customers and suppliers. 

 Moderate financial risk profile: Solara’s financial risk profile is moderate marked by comfortable capital structure, albeit constrained by expected weakening of debt protection metrics. Gearing remained healthy at less than 1 time as on March 31, 2023, while networth was robust at Rs 1083.01 crore. However, networth and gearing are expected to deteriorate to Rs 835.98 crore and 1.09 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2024 led by net loss owing to the fire incident. Debt protection metrics, likely to be n egative in fiscal 2024, are expected to improve in fiscals 2025 and 2026. Improvement in financial risk profile would remain a key monitorable. 

 Weaknesses: 

 Exposure to risks relating to strict regulations: Most of the products manufactured   by Solara face increased inspections and regulatory actions by authorities, such as the US Food and Drug Administration (US FDA). Additionally, production of a few products involves waste discharge, which needs to be treated in effluent treatment plants (ETPs). Thus, Solara needs to invest continuously to upgrade ETPs and bring efficiency in the process to reduce waste discharge. 

 Large working capital requirements: Working capital requirements are sizeable as reflected in significant receivables and   inventory of around 142 days and   156 days, respectively, as on March 31, 2023 and is estimated to be over 120 days each for fiscal 2024. CRISIL Ratings expects working capital requirements to gradually improve over the medium term with an increase in revenue contribution from the new plant. Correction in working capital requirements that shall aid liquidity shall be a key monitorable. 

 Volatility in operating profitability: Operating profitability fluctuated between 23.78% and 9.3% in the last 3-5 fiscal and in the current fiscal the company is making further losses due to the fire accident. Going forward, the ability of the company to demonstrate sustained improvement in operating margins will be a key sensitivity factor. 

 Liquidity : Adequate 

 Bank limit utilization was high at 95.68% over the 12 months through December 2023. Cash accruals are negative in the current fiscal against term debt obligation of Rs 117.25 crore. Improvement in cash accruals in the ensuing fiscals with improvement in operating performance would be a key monitorable. Also, the company is raising equity through rights issue which will remain key monitorable. 

 The current ratio was moderate at 1.08 times on March 31, 2023. 

 Outlook : Negative 

 CRISIL Ratings believes Solara will continue to benefit over the medium term from the management’s extensive experience. However, the business risk of the company will continue to remain a key monitorable. 

 Rating Sensitivity factors 

 Upward Factors : 

 Sustenance of revenue growth and operating margins improving to more than 1 0 % 

 Efficient working capital management and improvement in liquidity profile either by way of larger accruals or through lowering of debt through equity infusion . 

 Downward Factors : 

 Further revenue degrowth or o perating profitability leading to n et cash accrual to repayment obligations consistently remaining below 1 time 

 Deterioration in working capital cycle or any large debt funded capital expenditure further impacting financial risk profile 

 D elay in raising equity resulting in further deteriorating of the financial and liquidity risk profile 

 About the Company 

 Established in October 2017, Solara was formed through the demerger of the API business of Strides Shasun Ltd (currently named Strides Pharma Sciences Ltd). Solara acquired the human API business from Sequent Scientific Ltd during the same time, and hence, is a pure play API company. The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange .
