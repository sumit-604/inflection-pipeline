### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Neochem makes specialty chemicals at one plant at Mora, with 22,000 tonnes of yearly capacity (call l.5). Textiles take 75% of sales (call l.5).
2. H1 FY27 revenue was Rs 68.92 Cr, up 50.4% on a year ago (res l.184). Volume rose 20% and price added 7 to 8 points (call l.5, l.47).
3. PAT rose 43.1% to Rs 7.85 Cr (res l.212). EPS rose only 1.3%, because the FY26 share issue added many shares (res l.214).
4. Gross margin fell from 35.8% to 30.7% (res l.188-189). Management blames war-linked crude costs and expects about 33% for the full year (call l.11).
5. For FY27, management guides to more than 40% growth and a 10 to 12% PAT margin (call l.7). It restates a 45:55 split between H1 and H2 (call l.31).
6. Plant use rose from 53% in FY26 to 62% in H1 (call l.5). Management targets 65% by March 2027 and 75% in FY28 (call l.19). New capacity planning starts at 70 to 72% (call l.16).
7. Cash is the weak point. Over 18 months, PAT was Rs 19.87 Cr and operating cash was minus Rs 17.99 Cr (review Step 5). Management promises positive operating cash for FY27 (call l.37).
8. Receivable days fell from 161 to 127 (call l.6). Receivables older than six months fell from Rs 22 Cr to under Rs 9 Cr (call l.63).
9. The July cyber fraud took Rs 1.47 Cr. About Rs 0.8 Cr is back and Rs 0.67 Cr is still pending (call l.6). The filing gives no amount (res l.238-239).
10. The CFO change is planned succession, management says. The old CFO is 64 to 65 and stays as senior VP finance (call l.108).
11. Three related-party items came out on the call. About Rs 7.5 Cr bought offices held in the promoters' names (call l.29, l.101). Rs 6.25 Cr sits with two private companies on short-term call terms (call l.88). Sales to a promoter-group entity have stopped (call l.61).
12. Net reading: growth is real in the P&L and guidance is strong. Cash and the related-party items are not settled. The verdict stays PROCEED WITH FLAGS.

### 4. GROWTH STORY AND WHETHER IT IS FALLING INTO PLACE

Growth story [MGMT unless marked]:
- Spare plant capacity: use went from 35% (FY24, 27-May call note) to 53% (FY26) to 62% (H1 FY27, call l.5). No large capex is needed until use reaches 70 to 72% (call l.16).
- Volume, not only price: volume rose 20% and price added 7 to 8 points (call l.5, l.47).
- Better customers: the company moved from white label to its own brand about three years ago (call l.83). It is moving from small textile mills to corporate buyers (call l.75). That shift also explains high receivables.
- Mix away from textiles: home care, coatings and PFAS-free lines should lift gross margin to 33 to 35% (call l.7).
- New leaders: CFO, a commercial head with 30 years' experience and a textile business head (call l.4).
