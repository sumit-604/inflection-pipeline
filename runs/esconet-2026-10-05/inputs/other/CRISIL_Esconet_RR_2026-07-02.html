Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable’ corporate credit rating to Esconet Technologies Ltd (ETL).

 

The rating continues to reflect the established market position of the company, an increase in the scale of operations supported by extensive experience of the management and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by decreasing operating margin due to volatility in the raw material prices.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of ETL, its wholly owned subsidiary, ZeaCloud Services Pvt Ltd (ZSPL), Fluidech IT Services Pvt Ltd and Esconet Singapore Pte Limited, given their significant operational and financial linkages.

 

Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.

Key rating drivers - Strengths

  • Increasing scale of operations with diversified revenue stream: Operating Income grew at healthy rate of 50%, to reach Rs 354 crore in fiscal 2026 (Rs 236 crore in fiscal 2025). ETL expects continuous increase in scale of operations with favorable industry demand, increasing government spending for IT development & cyber security, moderate order book and continuous bidding for new orders. Esconet Singapore Pte Limited has seen an increase in revenue in fiscal 2026 and has contributed around Rs 50 crore in the overall revenue. Going forward, the company is expecting an increase in revenue to Rs 370-400 crore, the same will be driven by increase in revenue in their subsidiaries.

 

  • Healthy debt protection metrics: The company’s debt protection metrics are expected to be at a healthy level in fiscal 2026. The net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio remains healthy at 0.18 in FY2026. Further total outside liability to tangible net worth is 0.67 times (0.63 times in fiscal 2025) and expected to remain healthy in the range of 0.5-0.6 over the medium term given the company does not avail long term debt facilities

Key rating drivers - Weaknesses

  • Modest operating margin amid intense competition: Operating margin has remained on the lower side at 2.60% in fiscal 2026 as compared to 5.09% in fiscal 2025. The margin has reduced with substantial increase in raw material prices of data storage computers and devices due to increase in demand for these products after emergence of Artificial Intelligence. However, the company has taken certain steps to mitigate the margins and seen improvement in H2FY26 with operating margins improved from 2% to 3%. Intense competition has led to severe pricing pressure for ETL leading to constraints of low realization on their products. ETL tries to offset this risk by providing additional consultancy services/solution to its customers and introducing their cloud services subsidiary which will help in increasing margins. Improvement of operating margin and its sustenance will be a key monitorable.

Liquidity Adequate

Expected annual cash accrual of over Rs 7 -10 crore over the medium term which are expected to remain sufficient against negligible debt obligation over the medium term. The company has working capital facility of Rs 7.50c crore, however, the utilization remains nil as the company relies on its own accrual for working capital expenses.

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believes ETL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters in the IT consulting domain and their healthy relationship with the customers.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Sustained growth in revenue and operating margin above 6% leading to higher net cash accrual.
  • Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile.

 

Downward factors:

  • Decline in revenue or operating margin falling below 3-4%, leading to lower-than-expected cash accrual
  • Large debt-funded capex, impacting liquidity or financial risk profile.

About the Company

ETPL, founded in 2012, offers a wide range of IT solutions such as high-performance supercomputing, data centre facilities, encompassing storage servers, network security, virtualisation, and data protection.
