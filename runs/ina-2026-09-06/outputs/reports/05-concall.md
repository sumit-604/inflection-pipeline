### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every miss traced to a specific reason falls into **external-blame** or
**deflection**; no instance of an unprompted "we made a mistake" or similar
admission was found across all three transcripts.

- Revenue miss (Q3 FY26 call): external-blame — "delay in production capacity...
  due to monsoon and other factors which are beyond our control" (Ravi Dusad,
  p.13).
- Production target cut (Q4 FY26 call): deflection — reframed via a DCR/non-DCR
  split rather than acknowledging a lower number for the same non-DCR base
  (Manish Gupta, p.15).
- Debtor days / collection plan (Q4 FY26 call): deflection — answered *why*
  receivables rose (new 45-day LC terms on larger orders) but never gave the
  *number* asked for, current or target (Ravi Dusad, p.10).
- Cash accrual vs actual negative operating cash flow: **silence** — the claim
  was never revisited or reconciled once results showed the opposite.
- Capacity utilization by unit (Q3 FY26 call, asked twice by different
  analysts): deflection — "not wise to give you the exact... capacity
  utilization" until the whole INA-3 unit is commercialized (Ravi Dusad, p.13).

**Pattern check.** Management credits itself for wins in execution language
("supported by consistent execution," "operational excellence," "strong
execution ecosystem") while attributing misses to external, uncontrollable
factors (monsoon, raw-material price swings, market conditions). No hard
topic (cash flow, receivables, corporate guarantees, the SPV structure, the
new large customer) was raised proactively by management on any of the three
calls; every one of those topics either went entirely unmentioned or was
raised only when an analyst asked a narrower, adjacent question.

### 2C. Tone ratings (1-5, with evidence)

- **Transparency: 2/5.** No mention across three calls of corporate
  guarantees, the ~76 step-down SPV structure under Insolation Green Energy
  Pvt Ltd, or the new customer Solarworld Energy Solutions Ltd (12.4% of FY26
  revenue). Specific debtor-days number withheld when directly asked.
- **Specificity: 3/5.** Detailed numbers volunteered for realization/watt,
  IREDA rates, capex breakdowns, incentive terms; but utilization percentages
  and debtor days consistently withheld.
- **Consistency: 2/5.** PAT margin guidance given two different ways in the
  same FY25 call ("9.5% to 11%" vs "11.1%"); ALCM effective date stated as
  "1 June 2026" (FY25 call) then "January '26" (Q3 FY26 call); cell COD stated
  as both Q3 and Q4 FY27 within the same Q4 FY26 call.
- **Accountability: 2/5.** Every miss traced to an external cause; no
  self-attributed error found in any transcript.
- **Defensiveness: 3/5.** A long, data-heavy rebuttal to an "overcapacity"
  question (Q3 FY26 call, Manish Gupta, p.6-7) reads as prepared and
  somewhat over-argued rather than a brief factual answer.
- **Over-promotion: 4/5 (high).** Every call opens with an extended macro
  scene-setter (global renewable TAM, government targets, India rankings)
  before any company-specific number is given; repeated "vision of $1
  billion," Forbes recognition mentions, "landmark," "world-class" language.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **Corporate guarantees** (Rs99.68cr to Rs1,654.01cr per prior-stage
  finding): never mentioned in any call. Given the scale of the increase,
  this is a material silence.
- **The ~76 step-down SPV structure** under subsidiary Insolation Green
  Energy Pvt Ltd: never explained. The one adjacent mention (Ravi Dusad,
  Q3 FY26 call, p.10, naming "Insolation green energy, the main company where
  we raised money") stops short of any structural detail.
- **Solarworld Energy Solutions Ltd**, 12.4% of FY26 revenue and nil in FY25:
  never named by management, never asked about by any analyst, in any of the
  three calls.
- **The negative operating cash flow itself** (Rs73.13cr negative in FY26
  against Rs34.68cr positive in FY25): no analyst used the term "operating
  cash flow" or cited the number to management. Management's own claim of
  ~Rs220cr FY26 "cash accruals" (Q3 FY26 call) implicitly promised the
  opposite of what the results show, and was never revisited.
- **Zero expected-credit-loss provisioning** against a receivables book that
  is 39.5% aged beyond six months (per prior-stage finding): not discussed.
- **Receivables aging and collections mechanics**: asked for directly
  (Q4 FY26 call) and not answered with a number.

Likely reason for silence in each case: these are the topics most damaging
to the "strong execution, scale, disciplined growth" narrative management
otherwise tells consistently and at length; volunteering them would
undercut that story more than any guidance miss would.

## SECTION 4: KEY TAKEAWAYS & TRIGGERS SUMMARY

### 4A. Investment-ready trigger list (ranked by earnings impact)

1. **Narmadapuram 4.5GW TOPCon cell backward integration.** Type: margin.
   Timeframe: medium (COD Q3/Q4 FY27, internally inconsistent). Conviction:
   Medium. Confirms: cell line reaches commercial production and EBITDA
   margin visibly moves toward the "20% plus" guided range in FY28 numbers.
   Kills: further COD slippage past FY27, or margin stuck near 14-15%
   through FY28.
2. **FY27 revenue growth ("at least" FY26's rate).** Type: revenue.
   Timeframe: near. Conviction: Low-Medium, given the FY26 guidance record
   (35% miss on the original number) and the un-covered Q1 FY27 profit
   halving. Confirms: quarterly revenue tracking at or above the 60% YoY
   pace management describes. Kills: a repeat of the FY26 pattern — a soft
   quarter followed by a quiet downward revision.
3. **IPP KUSUM 400MW portfolio.** Type: revenue+margin. Timeframe: near-
   medium. Conviction: Low-Medium, given only ~40MW commissioned as of the
   Q4 FY26 call against a ~400MW target originally paced to complete around
   September 2026. Confirms: commissioning pace visibly accelerates in
   H1 FY27 filings. Kills: further slippage with no revised date given.
4. **Aluminium frame facility (Q1 FY27 COD).** Type: cost/margin.
   Timeframe: near. Conviction: Medium. Confirms: commissioning on schedule,
   feeding the cell line. Kills: slippage similar to the cell line pattern.
5. **Wafer/Ingot expansion.** Type: margin/capacity. Timeframe: long.
   Conviction: Low. Confirms: a firm capex figure, technology partner and
   DPR finalization announced. Kills: continued "under evaluation" status
   past FY27.
6. **ALMM/ALCM/ALMM Part 2 & 3 regulatory tailwind.** Type: regulatory-
   policy/sectoral. Timeframe: medium. Conviction: Medium (the underlying
   policy direction looks real; management's own dates for it have been
   inconsistent). Confirms: ALCM/ALMM Part 2 enforcement visibly restricts
   non-compliant supply, as management predicts. Kills: enforcement delay
   or the ~30GW non-ALMM overhang persisting.
7. **Main Board migration (delivered 9-Mar-2026).** Type: sectoral
   (liquidity/index eligibility). Timeframe: realized. Conviction: High
   (already delivered, ~3 months later than originally implied).
8. **BESS entry.** Type: revenue. Timeframe: long. Conviction: Low —
   unchanged "evaluating" status across all three calls; treat as stalled,
   not as a near-term trigger.
9. **US export market entry.** Type: revenue. Timeframe: long. Conviction:
   Low — only "quality certification" step named, no order or timeline.

### 4B. Questions for peer verification (handoff to stage 6)

1. **Cell/module capacity and overcapacity claim.** INA argues there is no
   real overcapacity in Indian solar manufacturing through the 2030s, citing
   ~142GW ALMM-registered cell capacity, ~70-75GW "effective" TOPCon/G12R
   capacity, against ~65-70GW DC demand implied by government tenders
   (Q3 FY26 call). Why it matters: this is the load-bearing justification for
   INA's continued capacity expansion and margin guidance. Check peers:
