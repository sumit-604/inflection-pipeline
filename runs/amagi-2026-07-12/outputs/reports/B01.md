## DECISION LINE

AMAGI scores 44/100 core and 12/60 moat (Grand Total 56/160), classified
**AVERAGE**. The balance sheet is fortress-clean post-IPO (Block D 20/20,
net cash, current ratio 3.98x) and growth has been uninterrupted (Block C
15/20, revenue CAGR 47.02%, zero decline years), but returns on capital
have been negative in 4 of the last 5 years and cash generation has been
worse than accounting losses would suggest (Block A 0/20, Block B 1/20).
The company turned its first full-year profit only in FY2026, its first
year as a listed entity, so there is exactly one clean data point behind
the turnaround thesis. This is a classic quantitative-screen picture for
a recent IPO transitioning out of a growth-at-all-costs, ESOP-heavy
private-company cost structure — the numbers do not yet prove the
transition is durable, they only show FY2026 as a first plausible
inflection point. Flags propagate; this AVERAGE classification and its
deal-breaker drivers should inform, not halt, downstream stages.
