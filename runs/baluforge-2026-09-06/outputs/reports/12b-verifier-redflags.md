## 0. METHOD AND THE STRUCTURAL ASYMMETRY

I read the four company decks, the two annual reports and the twelve peer transcripts
first, built the flag list below from those sources alone, and only then opened B05 and
B06. The comparison in Section 3 is therefore a genuine coverage measurement.

**EVASION CANNOT BE ASSESSED FOR THIS COMPANY.** Balu Forge holds no earnings calls. No
analyst has ever questioned this management on the record. Dodged questions, non-answers,
answers that change between analysts, tone under pressure, and repeated-question tracking
are all unmeasurable here. **The company is NOT scored clean on those tests; the tests do
not exist for it.** This is a permanent evidence gap, not a favourable finding, and any
downstream stage that reads "no evasion found" as a positive has misread the mode.

The nearest proxy is the FAQ block in the May 2026 deck ([[PAGE 22]]–[[PAGE 27]]). It is
not testimony. The company selects the questions, writes the answers, and faces no
follow-up. Its own header reads: "We will be summarizing some of the recurring questions
received from the investor community each quarter." That wording makes the FAQ *reactive*
(investors asked repeatedly), not proactive. And the entire FAQ block — including the
Income Tax search and the Enforcement Directorate disclosures — is **absent from the June
2026 deck that was actually presented to the investor/analyst community at the Non-Deal
Roadshow**.

The peer set is the control. All three peers were cross-examined live, four times each,
across the identical quarters. Where a peer volunteered a bad number, quantified a loss, or
was pushed on a prior-call claim, that is the behaviour Balu Forge's disclosure regime never
has to produce. I use the peers for that purpose throughout.

### 1B. Earnings quality

**F12 — Depreciation suppression, asserted as policy, never flagged as a risk, contradicted
by peer practice. CRITICAL.**
Nov 2025 [[PAGE 13]], stated by the company in its own words: "Balu Forge imports most
machinery under the Government of India's MOOWR scheme... **MOOWR assets are not subject to
depreciation, even after being commissioned and in use.**" The arithmetic consequence:
FY26 D&A of Rs 100 Mn against PPE of Rs 4,879 Mn plus CWIP of Rs 2,773 Mn (Jun 2026
[[PAGE 31]], [[PAGE 32]]) — 1.3% of gross fixed assets. Across FY22-FY26 D&A ran 11, 13, 21,
34, 100 Rs Mn while revenue went 2,861 to 11,074 (Jun 2026 [[PAGE 34]]). This single policy
inflates EBIT, EBIT margin, ROCE, PAT and PAT margin simultaneously — every headline metric
in the equity story.
Peer contradiction: RKFORGE, Jul 2026 transcript l.527-528, on commissioning new lines:
"that's why the **depreciation has gone up** and it will continue at this level in the
upcoming period." Peers depreciate commissioned assets; Balu Forge states it does not.
Peer outcome contrast: HAPPYFORGE FY26 converted a 30.4% EBITDA margin into a 19.5% PAT
margin (May 2026 transcript l.112-114) — a 10.9-point drop for D&A, interest and tax. Balu
Forge converted 27.0% into 22.7% (Jun 2026 [[PAGE 31]]) — a 4.3-point drop. Balu Forge
reports a *lower* EBITDA margin than Happy Forge and a *higher* PAT margin. That inversion
is the finding.
