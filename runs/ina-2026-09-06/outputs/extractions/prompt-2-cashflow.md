## PRELIMINARY: THREE STRUCTURAL FACTS ABOUT THIS STATEMENT

**Fact 1. The bridge does not start at PAT. It starts at PBT.**
Quote, annual-report__Annual_Report_2026.txt, extraction PAGE 117,
CONSOLIDATED STATEMENT OF CASH FLOWS FOR THE YEAR ENDED ON 31ST MARCH 2026,
first line under heading A:

> "Net Proﬁt before Tax as Per Statement of Proﬁt & Loss   24,528.53   15,305.84"

Comment. The task asks for a PAT-to-operating-cash bridge. The filed statement
does not present one. It runs from Profit Before Tax. PAT is NOT a line in
either cash flow statement. PAT is disclosed only in the Statement of Profit
and Loss (same page 117, line IX): Consolidated PAT FY2026 20,063.15,
FY2025 12,558.81. The tables below reproduce the statement as filed, and add
a PAT memo line marked [P&L, not CFS] so the analyst can see the gap.

**Fact 2. Neither cash flow statement carries a note reference column.**
The consolidated statement (extraction PAGE 117 and 118) and the standalone
statement (extraction PAGE 160 and 161) have four numeric columns only
("Details" and "Amount" for each year) and no "Note No." column. Every
"Note ref" cell below therefore reads NONE. This is a presentation fact of the
filed document, not a gap in extraction. Two notes sit BELOW the consolidated
statement on extraction PAGE 118, unnumbered, headed "Notes to the cash ﬂow
statement: 1." and "2."; they are reproduced in the Part B closing section.

**Fact 3. The AR labels BOTH year columns "Previous Year".**
Quote, annual-report__Annual_Report_2026.txt, extraction PAGE 117, column
header row of the consolidated cash flow statement:

> "Previous Year 2025-26    Previous Year 2024-25"
> "Details    Amount    Details    Amount"

Comment. The left numeric pair is the year ended 31-Mar-2026 and the right
pair is the year ended 31-Mar-2025, verified by tying the header line
24,528.53 and 15,305.84 to the P&L line VII "Proﬁt before tax (V-VI)
24,528.53 / 15,305.84" on the same page. The word "Previous" on the FY2026
column is the AR's own error and is quoted here as printed. The standalone
statement, extraction PAGE 160, repeats the identical header error.
