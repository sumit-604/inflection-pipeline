Brief profile (in case of appointment); 
M/s. Vivek J. Vakharia & Associates is a Peer Reviewed Firm of 
Practicing Company Secretaries with extensive experience in 
Corporate, Securities and Regulatory laws. The Firm 
specializes in listing of securities on recognised stock 
exchanges, including direct listing of companies migrated from 
regional stock exchanges to BSE and Metropolitan Stock 
Exchange, and provides periodic and event-based compliances 
under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, SEBI (Prohibition of 
Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (SAST) 
Regulations, 2011. 
 
The Firm undertakes compliance and audit assignments for 
stock brokers in accordance with SEBI and exchange 
guidelines, including NSE, BSE, MCX and NCDEX, and conducts 
Internal Audit and System Audit as prescribed. 
 
Its practice areas also include RBI compliances for NBFCs, 
filings under FDI policy, comprehensive compliance under the 
Companies Act, 2013 and LLP Act including incorporation, 
XBRL filings, due diligence and search reports, merger and 
amalgamation matters, and representation before NCLT in 
matters such as restructuring, IBC proceedings and revival of 
Companies.
