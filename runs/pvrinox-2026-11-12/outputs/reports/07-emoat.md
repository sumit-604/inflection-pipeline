### 2C Growth embedded in capex

- Standalone PPE Rs 28,538 Mn (AR p.171 standalone balance sheet).
- Standalone revenue proxy: Rs 432 total income per admission x 150.1 Mn admissions = Rs 64,843 Mn (derived from AR MD&A unit economics, B04).
- Fixed asset turnover = 64,843 / 28,538 = 2.27x (derived).
- Capex under execution, FY27 midpoint Rs 310 Cr = 3,100 Rs Mn.
- Implied incremental revenue = 3,100 x 2.27 = 7,037 Rs Mn (derived).
- As % above current revenue = 7,037 / 64,843 = 10.9%.

Cross-checks: low case 3,000 Rs Mn gives 10.5%, high 3,200 gives 11.2%. Growth-only capex (strip renovation Rs 80-100 Cr, an earlier split not restated) is Rs 200-240 Cr, giving 7.0-8.4%. Limits: the 2.27x ignores ROU assets (leased screens) and developer-funded screens, so it overstates revenue per rupee of PVR capex on asset-light screens and understates it on leased ones. It also assumes the new screens hit the fleet's occupancy, while FY26 admissions (150 Mn) equal FY24 (151 Mn) on more screens (B03). Treat 10.9% as a ceiling for FY27 capex, not a forecast. Reported field: 10.9.
