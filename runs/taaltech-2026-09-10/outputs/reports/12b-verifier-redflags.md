# PART 1 — MY INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST

## 1A. EARNINGS QUALITY AND CASH

**F1. A fifth of consolidated profit is an unrealised mark-to-market gain,
and the increase in that gain is 84% of the entire profit increase.**
Mark to market gain on changes in fair value of investments: Rs 1,204.05
lakh FY26 versus Rs 554.53 lakh FY25 (annual-report, standalone Note 25
"Other income"; Q4FY26 results, p.17, consolidated cash flow adjustment).
That is 21.2% of consolidated PAT of Rs 5,671.50 lakh. Consolidated PBT
rose Rs 771.44 lakh year on year (7,436.21 versus 6,664.77, Q4FY26 p.14).
The MTM gain rose Rs 649.52 lakh over the same period. So 84% of the
reported profit growth is a change in the fair value of a securities
portfolio, not operating performance. Not mentioned in the MD&A.

**F2. The MD&A reports operating margin rising while the true operating
margin fell.** MD&A Key Financial Ratios (annual-report, p.37) gives
"Operating Profit Margin (%) 37.66% (FY26) / 36.00% (FY25), Change 4.62".
Both figures reconcile exactly to PBT divided by revenue: 7,436.21 /
19,742.92 = 37.66%; 6,664.77 / 18,514.02 = 36.00%. That ratio includes
Rs 1,902.72 lakh of other income and the exceptional gain. The actual
operating margin, revenue minus total expenses over revenue, is 27.84%
FY26 against 28.34% FY25 (Q4FY26 p.14). The MD&A's headline margin rises
166 basis points; the real one falls 50. The reader is not told which
basis is used.

**F3. Consolidated operating cash flow fell 54% while profit rose 16%.**
Net cash from operating activities Rs 1,874.29 lakh FY26 against
Rs 4,102.40 lakh FY25 (Q4FY26 p.17). CFO over PAT: 33.0% against 84.1%.
No narrative section mentions cash flow at all.

**F4. Receivables plus unbilled revenue rose 51.9% on 7.1% revenue
growth.** Standalone trade receivables Rs 5,133.31 lakh against
Rs 3,812.05 lakh (Q4FY26 p.7). Unbilled revenue Rs 1,359.75 lakh against
Rs 462.22 lakh, up 194.2% (annual-report, standalone Note 14 "Current
financial assets - Others"). Together 124.7 days of revenue against 87.9,
an increase of 37 days.

**F5. Standalone cash flow is held up by stretching the payable to the
company's own US subsidiary.** Balance payable to TAAL Technologies Inc,
USA: Rs 2,000.51 lakh at 31 March 2026 against Rs 603.70 lakh a year
earlier (annual-report, Note 37(B), p.98). Against annual purchases from
that subsidiary of Rs 4,734.48 lakh, that is 154 days of intra-group
payables, up from 50. The Rs 1,396.81 lakh increase is most of the
standalone cash flow's "increase in trade payables 1,745.97" (Q4FY26 p.8).
It eliminates on consolidation, which is exactly why standalone CFO is
Rs 2,885.27 lakh and consolidated CFO is Rs 1,874.29 lakh.

**F6. The Q4 effective tax rate dropped ten points in the quarter that is
a balancing figure.** Q4 FY26 consolidated tax Rs 335.01 lakh on PBT
Rs 2,050.85 lakh = 16.3%. Q4 FY25: Rs 432.47 lakh on Rs 1,646.06 lakh =
26.3% (Q4FY26 p.14). Note 3 on p.15 states the March quarter figures "are
the balancing figures". No note explains the tax movement.

**F7. A Rs 371.05 lakh customer-claim provision was reversed into profit
and netted against a labour-code charge in the same quarter.** Q3FY26 p.4,
notes 2 and 3: labour codes charge Rs 332.76 lakh, customer-claim reversal
Rs 371.05 lakh, presented as a single net exceptional item of Rs 38.03
lakh. The existence of a customer claim of that size appears nowhere else
in any filed document, in a year when receivables rose 34.7%.

**F8. The labour-code charge changed between two filings with no
correction.** Rs 332.76 lakh in the Q3 FY26 results (p.4 and p.8);
Rs 333.02 lakh in the annual report (Note 48). Rs 371.05 minus Rs 333.02
equals the Rs 38.03 lakh net item, so the annual report figure is the
correct one and the quarterly filing carried an arithmetic error that was
never corrected.

**F9. The largest asset on the balance sheet is a securities portfolio
that the MD&A never mentions.** Standalone investments total Rs 14,432.21
lakh, of which debentures Rs 10,733.83 lakh, mutual funds Rs 2,092.62
lakh, shares Rs 1,562.60 lakh (annual-report, p.80). That is 56.6% of
standalone total assets of Rs 25,499.45 lakh, and the debenture book alone
is larger than half of annual revenue. Neither the composition nor the
counterparties are described in any narrative section.

**F10. The year's growth sits in the unaudited balancing quarter.**
Consolidated Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 5,704.34 lakh, up 24.6% sequentially and
27.6% year on year, and 28.9% of full-year revenue (Q4FY26 p.14). That
quarter is a balancing figure, not separately audited (note 3, p.15). This
is a watch item, not proof of anything: Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 6,481.12
lakh, up 41.6% year on year (Q1FY27 p.6), is consistent with a genuine
ramp rather than a year-end pull-forward. Both readings must stand until
Q2 FY27 separates them.

## 1B. RELATED PARTY AND GOVERNANCE

**G1. The listed company lent Rs 10 crore upstream to its unlisted holding
company.** Note 37(A) (annual-report, p.97) names Vishkul Enterprises
Private Limited as the **holding company**. Note 37(B) (p.98) records
"Loans given to related parties during the year 1,000.00" to Vishkul and
"Interest income 98.55". It is booked as an inter-corporate deposit
("Interest income - ICD 98.55", Note 25). No rate, tenor, security, board
approval date or repayment schedule is disclosed anywhere. Rs 98.55 lakh
on Rs 1,000 lakh implies 9.86% if outstanding for the full year and more
if placed part-way; the cash flow shows it was placed during FY26
(Q4FY26 p.17, "Loans to Related Party (1,000.00)"), so the rate cannot be
derived. Two readings sit side by side: a treasury yield pickup over the
bank-deposit rate (bank interest income fell from Rs 193.13 lakh to
Rs 64.94 lakh in the same year), or promoter funding routed through the
listed entity. The one observation that separates them is the loan
agreement's date and terms, which are not disclosed.
