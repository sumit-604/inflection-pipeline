Management Commentary

Commenting on the performance Mr. Sanjay Sharma MD and Co-Founder said :

                                                                    “Q2 FY27 highlighted the resilience of India’s micro-enterprises, as businesses continued to demonstrate adaptability amid deficient monsoon rainfall
                                                                    and localised drought conditions. Business activity remained steady, reflecting underlying economic strength, while credit demand sustained its
                                                                    momentum. The lending environment remained encouraging, with healthy loan demand and resilient asset quality through the seasonally weaker
                                                                    September quarter. Early signs of festive demand also provided a positive outlook for the coming months. Against this backdrop, the significant
                                                                    financing needs of India’s MSME sector continue to offer opportunities for lenders to support small businesses and drive sustainable growth.

                                                                    At Aye Finance, we remained focused on pursuing disciplined and sustainable growth. Disbursements increased 15% quarter-on-quarter to Rs. 1,403
                                                                    crore, taking H1 FY27 disbursements to Rs. 2,622 crore. This growth was achieved while maintaining a calibrated lending approach, with
                                                                    disbursements moderated in drought-affected regions of Maharashtra, Karnataka and Madhya Pradesh. Our Assets Under Management stood at Rs.
                                                                    7,597 crore, registering growth of 26% YoY and 4% QoQ.

                                                                    We continued to expand our customer franchise, adding approximately 49,000 new borrowers in Q2 FY27 and taking our active borrower base to 6.9
                                                                    lakh customers. Improving productivity and asset quality remain key priorities for FY27. AUM per employee increased to Rs. 0.67 crore from Rs. 0.57
                                                                    crore in Q2 FY26, while the Opex ratio declined to 8.61%, its lowest level in the last eight quarters. These improvements reflect our continued focus
                                                                    on enhancing operational efficiency as we scale the business.

                                                                    We also continued to make progress on asset quality, with GNPA declining to 4.20%, an improvement of 29 bps sequentially. PAR X improved by 7
                                                                    bps QoQ to 6.94%, representing an improvement of 109 bps from 8.03% in Q2 FY26. Credit cost for the quarter stood at 3.82%, bringing it within our
                                                                    guided range of 3.5–4.0%. Collections remained healthy, with Non-OD collection efficiency at 99.2% and Bucket 1 collection efficiency at 52.8%.

                                                                    Our financial performance also strengthened during the quarter. Gross Total Income grew 24% YoY and 8% QoQ to Rs. 527 crore, while Net Interest
                                                                    Margin stood at 15.6%. Profit After Tax increased 151% YoY and 16% QoQ to Rs. 87 crore, marking our highest ever quarterly profit.

                                                                    For H1 FY27, Gross Total Income grew 23% YoY to Rs. 1,017 crore, with Net Interest Margin at 15.9%. Pre-Provision Operating Profit increased 47%
                                                                    YoY to Rs. 377 crore, while Profit After Tax rose to Rs. 161 crore from Rs. 65 crore in H1 FY26, representing growth of 148%. Return on Assets and
                                                                    Return on Equity for H1 FY27 stood at 3.90% and 12.34%, respectively, reflecting the improvement in our overall profitability.

                                                                    As we enter the seasonally stronger second half of the year, we remain focused on building on this progress, sustaining improvements in productivity
                                                                    and asset quality, and delivering on our FY27 guidance.”
