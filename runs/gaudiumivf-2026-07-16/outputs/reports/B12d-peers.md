B06's citation discipline is strong: every quote spot-checked across all 12 transcripts (roughly 30 checks, prioritising every figure feeding a verdict) was verbatim or near-verbatim accurate and correctly attributed to speaker, with two minor exceptions (one wrong-quarter dating on an otherwise-genuine quote, one unanchored-but-accurate background figure). No peer transcript was left unused when it should have been used. No SUBSTANTIVE tag rests on a fabricated or unfindable citation. No verdict was upgraded from peer silence. No peer_question was skipped.
