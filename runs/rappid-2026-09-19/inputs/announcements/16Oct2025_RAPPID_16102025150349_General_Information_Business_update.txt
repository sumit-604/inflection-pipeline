2. New Certifications:  
 
a) Class NK (Japan) Type approval - Rappid Valves India Limited Achieves Prestigious Type Approval 
from ClassNK (Japan) for Marine Shipbuilding Valves. 
 
Rappid Valves proudly announces the successful attainment of Type Approval Certification from 
ClassNK (Japan) for its Marine Shipbuilding Valves — a major milestone affirming our commitment 
to world-class engineering and quality. 

[page 2]
 
 
 
Following months of rigorous testing as per DME 463 standards, the certification covers the 
following NAB valve types: 
• 
Top Entry Ball Valves 
• 
Gate Valves 
• 
Globe Valves ROV 
 
The comprehensive qualification process included hydrostatic, leakage, operational, material, and 
endurance testing, all under the supervision of TPI inspectors. 
 
This achievement places Rappid Valves among a select group of Indian manufacturers with ClassNK 
certification — essential for Hull Valves used in global shipbuilding. It enhances our global 
credentials and paves the way for partnerships with leading shipyards and marine OEMs. 
 
We remain dedicated to advancing India’s footprint in marine and industrial valve manufacturing 
through quality, innovation, and engineering excellence. 
 
b) UL Approval for Specialized Ball Valves (Export to the USA) 
 
Rappid Valves is proud to announce the successful indigenous design and manufacture of 
specialized Ball Valves for our prestigious customer in the United States. 
 
These valves underwent extensive testing at the customer’s laboratory in the USA and have 
received UL (Underwriters Laboratories) Approval for Process Media – Water Quality, granted 
exclusively on behalf of our USA customer.  
 
This achievement marks a major milestone for Rappid Valves, as these UL-approved valves will be 
exclusively manufactured and exported from India to the USA, showcasing our ability to deliver 
globally certified products through in-house engineering and manufacturing expertise. 
 
The certification reinforces Rappid Valves’ commitment to innovation, quality, and world-class 
engineering standards. 
 
3. New Design Development: 
 
Rappid Valves is proud to announce a significant milestone in our journey of engineering 
excellence — the successful indigenous development of large-size Globe Valves in Nickel 
Aluminium Bronze (NAB) for the prestigious Fleet Support Ship (FSS) project. 
 
The newly developed valves include: 
• 
450 mm (18") Globe Valve – 840 kg 
• 
550 mm (22") Globe Valve – 1035 kg 
 

[page 3]
 
 
 
 
Manufacturing these high-capacity valves required advanced design simulation, precision 
machining, and specialized foundry coordination to meet the stringent demands of naval 
applications, including leak-tightness, metallurgical integrity, and durability. 
 
These valves play a critical role in the Hull and Seawater 
Systems of FSS, designed to withstand harsh marine 
environments with high corrosion resistance and reliability. 
This achievement highlights Rappid Valves’ in-house 
capability to deliver complex, high-performance NAB 
valves, reinforcing our position as one of the few Indian 
manufacturers able to meet the specialized needs of 
defence shipbuilding and marine engineering. 
 
We remain committed to delivering innovative, world-class 
solutions to the Indian Navy, shipyards, and global marine 
industries.
