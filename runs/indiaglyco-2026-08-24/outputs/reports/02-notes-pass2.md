## 1. Hedge cover collapsed 92% while the underlying unhedged FX exposure roughly doubled 🔴 Red Flag

Note 51 standalone / Note 46 consolidated, [[page 143]]/[[page 187]]: outstanding
forward contracts/options fell from USD 52.86 million (₹45,182.09 Lakh) at
FY25 year-end to USD 3.73 million (₹3,534.49 Lakh) at FY26 year-end, a -92.2%
collapse in hedge notional. Over the same period the open (unhedged) FX
exposure went the other way: export debtors net of packing credit rose from
USD 11.14 million (₹9,518.16 Lakh) to USD 16.27 million (₹15,432.38 Lakh)
(+62.1%), and payables rose from USD 7.34 million (₹6,277.81 Lakh) to USD
14.91 million (₹14,136.33 Lakh) (+103.7%). No narrative explains the change
in hedging practice. This directly explains the near-doubling of the FX
sensitivity in Note 45(i)(a)/Note 42(i)(a) — a 1% USD move now swings PBT by
₹295.69 Lakh (FY26) vs ₹155.46 Lakh (FY25) (Note 45(i)(a), [[page 139]]; Note
42(i)(a) consol, [[page 183]]) — but Pass 1 recorded only the sensitivity
number without connecting it to the hedge-book drawdown behind it. Combined,
this is a genuine change in risk posture (materially less hedged, materially
larger open position) with zero management commentary. Worth a direct
question to management: why was the hedge book run down 92% in a year when
the underlying exposure doubled.

## 2. Ind AS 112 JV summary table mixes 100%-basis and 49%-share figures in the same disclosure — CISCPL's true FY26 profit is ~₹9,473 Lakh, not ₹4,641.68 Lakh as the note (and Pass 1) imply 🟡 Watch

Note 58(a) standalone / Note 51(a) consolidated, [[page 153]]: the Ind AS 112
JV summary states "Profit/(Loss) for the year: 4,641.68" (FY26) alongside
Assets, Liabilities and Revenue (₹64,005.25 Lakh) that are plainly 100%-JV
figures. Cross-checked against Form AOC-1 Part B, [[page 146]]: "Profit/Loss
for the year — i. Considered in Consolidation: 4,641.68; ii. Not Considered
in Consolidation: 4,831.13." Dividing 4,641.68 by 49% gives an implied 100%
JV profit of ₹9,472.81 Lakh; 51% of that (Clariant's share) is ₹4,831.14
Lakh, matching the AOC-1 line almost exactly. This confirms the "4,641.68"
figure in the Ind AS 112 table is IGL's 49% equity-accounted share, not the
JV's 100% profit — inconsistent with the 100%-basis Assets/Revenue lines
sitting in the same table. Pass 1 (§6, Investments) read 4,641.68 as the
JV's "100% basis" profit; on this cross-check that reading understates
CISCPL's actual profitability by roughly half. Net margin on the naive
reading is 7.25% (4,641.68/64,005.25); on the corrected reading it is 14.80%
(9,472.81/64,005.25) — a meaningful difference for anyone assessing the JV as
a standalone earnings engine or valuing IGL's 49% stake. This is a disclosure
clarity issue (mixed consolidation bases in one table), not a restatement of
IGL's own consolidated numbers, which are unaffected.

## 3. Promoter GROUP holding remains 59.62% despite Kashipur Holdings alone falling below 50% — tempers the severity of Pass 1's Finding #2 🟢 Clean (context)

threshold — allottee identity is still NOT FOUND IN THE NOTES and remains a

## 7. Cash tax rate is far below both the statutory and reported effective P&L tax rate — 5.7% cash tax vs 23.01% effective, driven by DTL build 🟡 Watch

Note 59(A)/(D) standalone, [[page 154]]; Note 53 consol, [[page 195]]: of the
total standalone tax expense of ₹8,437.26 Lakh (FY26), only ₹2,107.02 Lakh is
Current Tax; the remaining ₹6,330.24 Lakh is Deferred Tax charge (i.e., 75.0%
of the reported tax expense is non-cash). Current Tax / PBT = 2,107.02 /
36,669.99 = 5.7% cash tax rate, versus the 23.01% reported effective rate and
25.17% statutory rate already discussed by Pass 1. This is the same DTL
build already flagged under Deferred Tax (heavy PP&E timing differences from
capex, DTL grew ₹44,237.61 Lakh to ₹50,510.79 Lakh), but Pass 1 did not
translate it into a cash-tax-rate figure. For an investor modelling free cash
flow, PAT materially overstates the near-term cash tax drag relative to the
P&L tax line; this reverses over time as the capex-driven timing differences
unwind, but it is a genuine quality-of-earnings note worth a direct line in
any DCF/FCF build.
