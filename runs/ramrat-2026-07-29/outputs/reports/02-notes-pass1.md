Total income-tax contingent liability up 409.8% YoY to ₹10,361.69 Lakhs, ~17.6% of net worth on the tax demand alone. 🔴

2. **Inventory more than doubled (+111.7%) against 40.1% revenue growth**, with Work-in-Progress up 186.9% and Finished-Goods-in-Transit up 87.2%; Inventory Turnover fell 16.19x→13.31x; zero inventory write-downs recognised despite the build-up (Note 8, p.204; Note 25, p.223; Note 37, p.234). 🔴

3. **Debt-Equity ratio jumped 0.57x→1.04x (+82.5%)** as total borrowings nearly doubled (+120.2%) to fund the Bhiwadi capex and working capital; DSCR still improved (1.65x→1.77x) as a mitigant, but leverage direction and pace warrant monitoring (Note 13A/13B, Note 37, p.213-216, 233). 🟡

4. **Title deeds for ~₹23 Cr of land/factory-building assets remain in the name of Global Copper Pvt Ltd**, the erstwhile subsidiary merged into the Company effective 1 April 2024, with regulatory transfer still pending as at report date (Note 2.4, p.200). Legal-registration risk directly linked to the same merger generating the tax search exposure in #1. 🟡

5. **Receivables/payables factoring and channel-financing off-balance-sheet volumes more than doubled**: ₹18,681.22 Lakhs of trade receivables derecognised via channel-financing/factoring (FY26) vs ₹8,366.68 Lakhs (FY25); ₹64,742.73 Lakhs of trade payables/working capital already routed through Supplier Finance Arrangements (Note 9.4/9.5, p.206; Note 19.3, p.221). Reported DSO/DPO ratios materially understate/overstate true trade credit given this scale of off-balance-sheet intermediation. 🟡

6. **₹333.01 Lakhs Labour Code past-service cost booked as a "non-recurring exceptional item,"** swinging the standalone gratuity plan from net-asset to net-liability funded status; final Labour Code rules notified only in May 2026 (post year-end), with further impact assessment still pending — recurrence risk not fully closed out (Note 38(a)(7), p.236-237). 🟡

7. **Managerial remuneration to Executive Directors more than doubled (+102.0%) to ₹903.87 Lakhs**, coinciding with three new promoter-family executive appointments in FY26 (Joint MD, Executive Director, Additional Executive Director) — run-rate for FY27 likely higher still (Note 35, p.227-230). 🟡

8. **Extensive promoter personal guarantees remain outstanding** on both term loans (~₹52,000 Lakhs combined for Tribhuvanprasad and Mahendrakumar Kabra) and working capital facilities (~₹152,150 Lakhs combined across secured/unsecured), stated to be released under a May 2026 consortium agreement pending formal lender confirmation (Note 13.7, p.216; Note 35, p.232). 🟡

9. **Letters of credit and bank guarantees issued by banks rose 78.6% to ₹56,437.05 Lakhs** (Note 30(b)(iii), p.225), and Copper Tubes/PLI-scheme revenue (now ~22.6% of total, +115.4% YoY) carries incentive-realisation risk pending "necessary regulatory reviews, assessments and approvals" (Note 51, p.247) — growth engine partially contingent on scheme continuity. 🟡

10. **Goodwill of ₹2,484.83 Lakhs (consolidated) carried unchanged with no disclosed impairment-test assumptions or sensitivity**, and Product Development intangible up 75.9% to ₹4,890.60 Lakhs — capitalisation judgment and impairment-testing rigor both warrant a management question (Note 2C consolidated, p.258-260). 🟡

**Overall read**: no going-concern language, no covenant breaches, no MSME interest escalation of consequence, and improved hedging discipline are genuine positives (all 🟢). But the year is characterised by a working-capital-intensive expansion (inventory, receivables-financing, and leverage all growing faster than revenue) layered on top of an unresolved, auditor-flagged tax search exposure and a residual title-deed registration gap, both inherited from the FY25 Global Copper merger. These threads should be treated as connected, not independent, when weighing FLAG-CASH and overall accounting-quality scoring in Pass 3.
