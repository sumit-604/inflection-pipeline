### 2C. Growth embedded in capex

Arithmetic (per prompt: capex under execution × historical fixed asset turnover = implied
incremental revenue, as % of current revenue):

- Capex under execution = capital commitment not yet spent (Rs 31.77 Cr, AR26 p.110, Note 41b)
  + CWIP already spent but not yet commissioned at FY26 year-end (Rs 9.12 Cr, AR26 p.83, Note 2B)
  = **Rs 40.89 Cr**.
- Historical fixed asset turnover (Sales ÷ Net Block, FY26) = Rs 451.05 Cr ÷ Rs 246.46 Cr = **1.83x**
  (per B01-gate0 Block F, M3, computed from screener Data_Sheet + AR26 Note 2A/2B).
- Implied incremental revenue = Rs 40.89 Cr × 1.83 = **Rs 74.83 Cr**.
- As % of current revenue (Rs 451.05 Cr, FY26): 74.83 / 451.05 = **16.6%**.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 16.6%.**

This is a mechanical, formula-driven number, not a company guidance figure, and it should be read
against two important caveats: (1) it assumes the *new* capex earns revenue at the *same* blended
turnover as the existing, mostly-Extrusion-weighted asset base — a shaky assumption if the new
spend is Geon-specific (Geon's own capacity claims imply a far higher notional turnover, since
management's own "Rs 1,500+ Cr on the existing facility" claim, AR26 p.37, is ~11x the current
Battery segment revenue of Rs 136.11 Cr on assets already in place); and (2) the one hard,
audited, forward-committed capex number in this corpus (Rs 31.77 Cr, Note 41b) is small relative
to the scale of ambition asserted elsewhere in the same report. The gap between a ~16.6%
capex-implied revenue uplift and a qualitative "10x+ headroom" claim with no capex plan behind it
in this corpus is itself a finding: the aspirational GEON scale claim is **not** underwritten by
any capex commitment visible in these filings.

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

### FAMILY A — Product & Technology

**A1. Rare manufacturing capability — NO EVIDENCE FOUND.** Plastic extrusion machinery
manufacturing is a mature, globally competed field (Battenfeld-Cincinnati, KraussMaffei,
Reifenhäuser and others are larger and more advanced; AR26 p.24 itself frames Battenfeld-Cincinnati
as KET's *technology partner since 1983*, i.e. KET is a licensee/collaborator, not the technology
leader). Li-ion battery pack assembly (cell-agnostic system integration) is likewise a widely
practised capability in India (Exide, Amara Raja, Log9, Lohum and others compete in the same
space). No global-manufacturer-count, qualification-timeline, or long-lead-equipment evidence is
in the corpus.

**A2. Patent and IP pipeline — NO EVIDENCE FOUND.** Full-text search of both the FY2024-25 and
FY2025-26 Annual Report text twins for "patent," "trademark," "intellectual property," "IP
portfolio" returned **zero matches in either year**. This is a materially negative finding for a
company that markets itself on "R&D-focused" positioning (AR25 p.37) — there is no filed-patent
evidence to examine at all.

**A3. Process innovation — NO EVIDENCE FOUND (scored) / weak narrative only.** Annexure-4
(technology absorption, AR26 p.28-29) describes ongoing R&D — "in-house multi-physics simulation
and deep cell characterization," "robotic manufacturing setup" (AR26 p.37) — but none of it comes
with a yield figure, a unit-cost trend, or a waste-to-value metric. Solar energy usage of 73,796
kWh (AR26 p.28) is disclosed with no percentage of total plant energy, making it immaterial as
process-efficiency evidence. Narrative-only, not scored.

**A4. Product platform / modular architecture — MODERATE.** GEON's strategic choice not to
manufacture cells, combined with a shared system-design/BMS architecture, has let it expand from
2W-only battery packs (its origin) into 3W, 4W, high-voltage, e-bus/commercial, and now a D2C
inverter/RESS product (📄 AR26 p.5, p.28-29, p.36-37) without a proportional new-cell-technology
investment — a genuine platform-style SKU expansion, not proportional R&D scaling. This is the
single strongest documented item in this scan and is discussed further under Section 5 scoring.
