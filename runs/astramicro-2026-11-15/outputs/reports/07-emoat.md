## READING KEY

- Evidence tags. [DOC] = documented (signed order, filed statement, asset on a balance sheet, Reg 30 filing). [CLAIM] = management claim (call or deck text, no signed contract or committed capital). [INFER] = analyst inference. These map to the three evidence classes of the instruction file.
- Anchors. "AR p.N" = PDF page of Annual_Report_2026.txt. "Inv. Pres. p.N" = PDF page of the Q2 FY27 deck (12 Nov 2026). Call anchors use the PDF page marker of the transcript file (printed page = PDF page minus 1). Calls: Q3 FY26 = 13 Feb 2026, Q4 FY26 = 27 May 2026, Q1 FY27 = 11 Aug 2026. "Q2 results p.N" = Q2 FY27 results filing, Rs Lakhs. B01, B03, B04, B05 = prior stage blocks (derived, not primary).
- Units. Rs Cr unless stated. AR statements and results filings are in Rs Lakhs and are labelled. No silent conversion. "calc" = my arithmetic on the cited inputs.
- Strength bands (my definition, because the instruction file sets none). Strong = adjusted score 3.5 or more. Moderate = 2.0 to 3.4. Weak = 0.1 to 1.9. None = 0.
