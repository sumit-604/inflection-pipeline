## Business Understanding Narrative

The name says food. The accounts say software. In Q1 FY27 the Software and IT
services segment earned Rs 34.67 crore of the group's Rs 40.14 crore of
revenue, or 86.4%. Food and Restaurant Services earned Rs 5.47 crore, 13.6%,
and lost Rs 0.80 crore doing it (Result Q1FY27 p6). Software made Rs 6.62 crore
of segment profit. A reader who bought this company for its restaurants would
own an IT services business with a loss-making food arm attached.

The structure matters more than the segments. The listed parent is close to
empty. Standalone Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs 2.37 crore and standalone profit was
Rs 0.05 crore (Result Q1FY27 p2). Consolidated profit was Rs 4.81 crore, and
the auditor discloses that the three wholly owned subsidiaries produced
Rs 4.75 crore of it (Result Q1FY27 p8). Ninety-nine percent of the group's
profit sits in subsidiaries. The listed entity is a holding shell.

Those subsidiaries are Mirchi Wild Wings Private Limited, its own subsidiary
MWW Gachibowli Private Limited, Chicken Wild Wings Private Limited, and Teksoft
Systems Inc of the USA (Result Q1FY27 p8). Three chicken-wing restaurant
companies and one American software company, under a former agencies business
renamed after a restaurant brand.

What is changing: everything, and all at once in 2025. The company changed its
name on 8 August 2025, changed its registered office on 30 July 2025, changed
its directorate on 19 September 2025, held four EGMs to 30 June 2025, and filed
two SAST Regulation 29(2) disclosures on 24 June 2025 (Bull AI availability
map). A rename, a relocation, a board change, shareholder meetings and
substantial-holding disclosures inside four months is the signature of a
control change or a reverse merger.

What breaks it: the disclosure does not support the size. Bull AI reports a
market capitalisation of Rs 2,083.70 crore against Rs 113.2 crore of
consolidated equity and a company that has never held an earnings call,
published a presentation, or carried a credit rating.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Two unrelated businesses under one listed shell.

| Segment, Rs lakhs | Rev Q1 FY27 | Result Q1 FY27 | Rev Q1 FY26 | Result Q1 FY26 |

| Software and IT services | 3,467.07 | 661.55 | 2,561.33 | 141.84 |

Source: Result Q1FY27 p6. Software revenue grew 35.4% year on year and its
segment result grew 4.7 times. Food revenue fell 18.1% and stayed loss-making.
Employee benefit expense was Rs 14.35 crore in Q1 FY27 against Rs 40.14 crore
of revenue, or 35.8%. That is an IT services cost structure, not a restaurant
one. The economic engine is Teksoft Systems Inc, a US software subsidiary about
which the corpus says nothing beyond its name and country.
Archetype: **outsourcing partner**, on the library, read for the software
majority. Client concentration, wallet share, capacity fill and contract
stickiness are the right levers, and not one of them is disclosed. The food
segment fits brand consumer and is immaterial and loss-making.
The mismatch between the company's name and its economics is itself the finding.

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS.**
This is not a shallow-read judgement about a business. It is a judgement about
whether the filings can be relied on at all. A listed company that prints three
different CINs and four spellings of its Chairperson's name in one document,
that moves Rs 159 crore of assets between two unrelated segments without a
note, that has holes in its quarterly record, and that corresponds from a Gmail
address, has not met the minimum standard for a full `/step1`.
The underlying software business is growing and profitable. That is the honest
counterweight, and it is not enough. The listed entity holds it at one remove,
discloses nothing about it, and was a differently named company eighteen months
ago.

Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides:
1. What caused the Rs 159 crore segment-asset reallocation in Q1 FY27.
2. Who controls the company after the 2025 rename, relocation, board change and
   SAST disclosures.
3. Where the FY26 Q2 and Q4 results are, and the FY26 audited full-year
   figures.
4. What Teksoft Systems Inc actually does, and for whom.
5. Consolidated cash flow, absent for every period.
6. Why other equity fell Rs 9.78 crore in a profitable quarter.
7. The live market capitalisation, since Rs 2,083.70 crore against Rs 113.21
   crore of equity needs confirming before it is believed.

What would change the view: a clean audited FY26 with a balance sheet and cash
flow, a disclosed controlling shareholder, and an explanation of the segment
reallocation. Until then this name should not consume pipeline time.
No price, no position.
