### 3B. Industry and market intelligence dropped in the AR

- Global NdFeB magnet demand: ~119,000 tonnes (2020) projected to rise to ~387,000 tonnes by 2030
  (source: DAE/AMD, US DoE, Neo Performance Materials — AR-FY26 p.18).
- China's share of global NdFeB magnet production capacity: ~92% (source: US Department of Energy —
  AR-FY26 p.18).
- RDSS smart-meter programme: 224 million meters sanctioned, 151 million awarded, 65 million
  installed as of 30-Apr-2026, against a 250-million target now expected complete by March 2028
  (source: Ministry of Power — AR-FY26 p.17).
- India smart-meter penetration ~26% of the RDSS target vs. USA ~94%, Canada ~96%, EU ~63%,
  Australia ~57% (AR-FY26 p.16).
- Global smart electricity meters market: USD 29.51 bn (2026) → USD 48.04 bn (2034), ~6% CAGR;
  broader smart meters market USD 39.58 bn (2026) → USD 105.43 bn (2034), >13% CAGR (source: Straits
  Research, Fortune Business Insights et al. — AR-FY26 p.16).
- Asia Pacific accounted for ~58% of global smart electricity meter volumes in 2025 (AR-FY26 p.16).
- Global EV sales reached ~21 million units in 2025, +20% YoY (source: IEA — AR-FY26 p.16).
- US tariff-related disruptions specifically affected PML's exports during H1FY26, recovering in
  H2FY26 (AR-FY26 p.4, p.20).
