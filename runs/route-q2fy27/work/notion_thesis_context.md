## Section 3B — Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1 — Margin Expansion via Client Mix (PRIMARY, HIGH CONVICTION)

- <b>What it is:</b> Deliberate exit from low-margin ILD SMS aggregator traffic, focusing on direct enterprise contracts, OTT channels (WhatsApp/RCS), and high-margin firewall solutions
- <b>Timeline:</b> 6–12 months for full P&L flow-through (already 3 quarters into pivot)
- <b>Confidence level:</b> HIGH — gross margin moved 21.1% → 24.5% over Q3 FY25 to Q3 FY26 (📄 documented across 4 quarterly results)
- <b>What confirms it:</b> EBITDA margin crossing 13% sustained, gross margin above 25% for 2 consecutive quarters
- <b>What kills it:</b> Reversal in gross margin below 22% for 2 consecutive quarters; aggressive price cuts to retain large enterprise accounts; further WhatsApp pricing dilution from Meta

### Trigger 2 — Proximus Cross-Sell Synergies (SECONDARY, MEDIUM CONVICTION)

- <b>What it is:</b> Cross-selling Route's CPaaS to Telesign's US/EU enterprise clients; cross-selling Route's firewall (365guard) via BICS's 400+ MNO relationships globally
- <b>Timeline:</b> 12–24 months for material revenue contribution (currently mostly COGS synergies, low-margin \~10–11% EBIT)
- <b>Confidence level:</b> MEDIUM — Claro LATAM firewall deal in pilot/final testing; APAC green shoots; concrete revenue billing pending
- <b>What confirms it:</b> ₹100–200+ Cr of FY28 revenue clearly attributable to Proximus cross-sell; multiple firewall deal wins via BICS
- <b>What kills it:</b> Synergies remain COGS-only through FY27; Proximus strategic review; failure to convert BICS pipeline into billed revenue

### Trigger 3 — New Products WhatsApp/RCS/Voice/Email (SECONDARY, MEDIUM CONVICTION)

- <b>What it is:</b> Pivot from legacy SMS to higher-margin omnichannel (38% YoY growth in FY25 to ₹317 Cr; 14.5% YoY growth in 9M FY26)
- <b>Timeline:</b> Ongoing, multi-year
- <b>Confidence level:</b> MEDIUM — industry-wide tailwind (Twilio +5x QoQ RCS, Sinch +260% YoY), but Route faced 12–13% pricing dilution from Meta in Q3 FY26
- <b>What confirms it:</b> Sustained >20% YoY growth in new product revenue; RCS adoption scales beyond pilot; sticky enterprise wins (L&T Metro, PT MRT Jakarta ticketing)
- <b>What kills it:</b> Meta/Google extract more value via further pricing changes; RCS adoption stalls at iOS uptake; client mix over-indexed on marketing messages

### Trigger 4 — Telecom Network APIs / Konera Initiative (TERTIARY, LOW CONVICTION)

- <b>What it is:</b> Monetizing telco network APIs for fraud prevention, geolocation, authentication; Telesign ecosystem integration
- <b>Timeline:</b> 18–24+ months — genuinely nascent
- <b>Confidence level:</b> LOW — pilots in India only; revenue contribution speculative
- <b>What confirms it:</b> First material revenue contribution (₹50+ Cr) from Network APIs; pilot conversion to paid contracts
- <b>What kills it:</b> Enterprises find APIs too complex/expensive; competitors (Twilio Verify) capture market
