## 2. Strategic Partnerships & New Product Areas

- Products: Double-leaf plug doors for freight EMU; automatic single-leaf
  plug doors for freight EMU; automatic single-leaf plug doors for Vande

## 4. Capacity Expansion & Operations Model

### Manufacturing Footprint & New Facility (June 2026 presentations & concall)
- Existing footprint: 5 main units around Chennai with 90% utilization.
- Plan: new 14-acre integrated facility with 50,000–100,000 sq.ft shed
  initially, expandable to 300,000–400,000 sq.ft over time.
- Concall: land already acquired; intend to spend Rs 30–35 crore in FY27 on
  building this infrastructure, funded via term loans; consolidate some rented
  facilities to save ~Rs 20–25 lakh/month in rent and realize economies of
  scale; longer term envisage two main rent-free plants.

### Shift Pattern & Execution Model
- Since late FY26, moved to two-shift operations (particularly last 4 months
  of FY26). At current capacity and two shifts, management believes they can
  reach ~Rs 400–450 crore revenue without significant new machinery, and use
  channel partners to go beyond that to the guided Rs 500 crore.

### Channel Partner Strategy (Refurbishment & Zonal Railways)
- Creating channel partners (regional suppliers) who bid locally and execute
  on site; Airfloa manufactures key components in Chennai and supplies them.
- Benefits: saves on EMD, BGs, PBG, security deposits and heavy local
  infrastructure; allows scaling to Rs 1,000–2,000 crore revenue potential
  without building plants near every workshop; margins shared with channel
  partners but overall profitability believed to improve vs in-house.
- Refurbishment: initial government allocation ~Rs 26,000 crore and ~25,000
  coaches over 3–4 years; target ~Rs 100 crore of refurbishment orders in FY27.

  (21 Jan 2026) aggregated penalties at Rs 90 lakh for FY20–FY23 CSR timing

  stated the matter has "no impact on the company's operations or
  profitability" and penalties are not material.
