## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. The notes carry no segment result. On a note-level cost inference,
   products gross margin compressed ~2.7pp FY25→FY26 while services'
   direct-cost ratio improved. Which line, products or services, actually
   drove the FY26 consolidated EBITDA margin decline (16.3%→15.0%), and will
   a segment result be disclosed going forward?
2. Trade receivables >6 months aged rose from 14.5% to 30.6% of the "due"
   book, standalone turnover fell to 2.70x, and DSCR fell to 0.79x, yet zero
   ECL provisioning is recognised. What is the collection plan for the aged
   book, and why has no provisioning policy been disclosed or applied?
3. What is the interest rate on the ₹1,644.09 lakh inter-corporate deposit
   owed to Saarathi Healthcare, and how was that rate set given Saarathi
   carries a 24% minority shareholding?
4. CARO confirms the company holds leased buildings under executed
   agreements, yet no Ind AS 116 right-of-use asset or lease liability
   appears anywhere. What is the basis for non-recognition (short-term or
   low-value exemption, or otherwise), and why is no Significant Accounting
   Policies note present in the filed annual report at all?
5. Roughly 38.3% of the consolidated group's MSME-due payables balance has
   remained unpaid for more than a year. What is driving this, and what is
   the remediation timeline?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- Zero ECL/doubtful provisioning maintained against a receivables book whose
  >6-month aged share doubled year on year (aggressive-accounting-adjacent:
  provisioning not keeping pace with disclosed deterioration).
- Complete absence of the Significant Accounting Policies note in the filed
  AR (undisclosed-risk-indicator: no stated basis for revenue recognition,
  depreciation, impairment, or ECL anywhere in the document).
- Complete absence of Ind AS 116 lease recognition despite CARO-confirmed
  leased premises (undisclosed-risk-indicator / potential standard
  non-application).
- Standalone Note 37 trade payables ageing table blank for the current year
  against a real, non-zero payables balance (disclosure control lapse).
- No segment result disclosure, blocking independent verification of which
  business line drives margin (disclosure gap that happens to obscure the
  single most-asked investor question in this file, LBF1).
- Two unquantified prior-period adjustments (gratuity DBO restatement,
  unquantified "regrouping" language) with no explanatory detail.

No evidence of outright earnings management (no exceptional-item smoothing,
no EPS dilution masking, no off-note revenue recognition anomaly, unqualified
audit opinion both years, clean contingent-liability and CSR picture) — the
red flags cluster on DISCLOSURE COMPLETENESS and PROVISIONING ADEQUACY, not
on fabrication or manipulation.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate to concerning accounting practices, driven more by
what is missing than by what is misstated. Key concern: no accounting
policies note, no segment result, and zero ECL provisioning against a
receivables book that deteriorated sharply in FY26. Key strength: clean
contingent liabilities, no EPS dilution, no exceptional-item smoothing, and
an unqualified audit opinion both years, with the RPT scale itself immaterial
to revenue. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
