## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring)

1. Awfis's occupancy plateau is company specific, not market wide. All three peers run higher: Smartworks 92% mature committed (Q1 FY27 call p.6), Indiqube 86% (Q1 FY27 call p.14), DevX 91.93% (Q1 FY27 call p.3). Verifier B: on Smartworks's operational basis the figure is 81%, a 5 point gap, and the stage mixed committed and operational bases (Smartworks Jul-2026 p.6).
2. The 2021 lease reset as a margin drag. The stage filed this as unverifiable. Verifier B reads it as a directional contradiction. Smartworks reprices or churns 2020 to 2021 vintage clients by choice (Nov-2025 p.17, Jan-2026 p.12, Jul-2026 p.11). Indiqube calls the landlord and client escalation spread a tailwind of about 1 point (Nov-2025 p.6, p.11). Awfis's own average client tenure of 37 to 38 months means most 2021 clients should already have repriced.
3. Size of the cash margin gap. The stage verified a peer band of 16 to 21% against 10.1%. Verifier B calls "roughly half" a basis error and puts the like for like gap at 2 to 9 points (Awfis May-2026 p.11; Indiqube Nov-2025 p.6, p.12; Smartworks Jul-2026 p.3; DevX Aug-2026 p.3).
4. Churn as the industry's natural state. Awfis says 1.5 to 2% of inventory churns every month (Aug-2026 p.8). Indiqube reports retention above 95% (Feb-2026 p.12, Aug-2026 p.15), and DevX reports 99.7% (May-2026 p.6).
5. Undisclosed revenue per seat. Awfis gave no figure on three calls. Smartworks gives ₹181 per sq ft against ₹170 (Jul-2026 p.13, p.16), Indiqube ₹192 (May-2026 p.15), and DevX ₹7,500 to 8,000 per seat (Nov-2025 p.14).
