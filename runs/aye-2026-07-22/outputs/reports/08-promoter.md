# Stage 8: Governance Background Check — Aye Finance Limited (AYE)

## REFRAME NOTICE

The Prospectus states explicitly and repeatedly: **"OUR COMPANY DOES NOT HAVE AN
IDENTIFIABLE PROMOTER"** (Prospectus, multiple locations incl. p.32 risk factor
#26, "Our Management" p.283, "Our Principal Shareholders" p.302; also
Prospectus text at internal lines ~1765, ~3957-3967, ~19754-19756 of the
collected extract). The Board formally noted the company as "professionally
managed" by resolution dated October 30, 2024 (Prospectus, RF #26). There is
no promoter group, no promoter shareholding, no promoter pledge, and no
minimum-promoter-contribution lock-in requirement (Regulation 14(1) SEBI
ICDR).

Per task instruction, this stage is therefore reframed from a promoter check
to a **management + major-shareholder + board governance check**, applying
the same verdict scale (EXEMPLARY / TRUSTWORTHY / CAUTION / CONCERN / AVOID)
to: (a) founder-MD integrity and track record, (b) board quality and
independence, (c) major institutional shareholder reputation, (d) related-party
conduct, (e) regulatory/litigation record. Section 1 (family mapping) and
Section 3C/6C-pledge (promoter share transactions) are answered "NOT
APPLICABLE — no identifiable promoter" rather than populated with estimates.
