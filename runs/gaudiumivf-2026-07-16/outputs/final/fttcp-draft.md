## MY RULINGS

Every call below is made. Each carries a confidence tag and the single fact that would flip it.

1. **Forward window: 3 months primary, 6 months secondary, 12 months for ROCE.** The company files quarterly, so a 3-month print exists to confirm or refute. `sure`. Wrong only if reporting turns semi-annual.

2. **Business type: standard operating business, four standard transitions.** The revenue block calls it hybrid, but that describes a services-plus-trading revenue mix, not a lender. `sure`. Wrong only if it were a bank, NBFC, MFI or HFC, which it is not.

3. **Workup intent: first workup.** No prior company memory file exists and the manifest run type is full, so the destination PE, prior thesis and prior devil's advocate fields are not applicable because FTTCP runs before valuation. `sure`.

4. **Sector cap row: corrected to Hospitals / dialysis / healthcare services, 35x.** The manifest says Pharma / CDMO, which is wrong for a single-specialty fertility and women's-health hospital chain. The business block describes asset-light healthcare services with EV/EBITDA as the primary method, which is the hospitals-and-healthcare-services row in the Section 1B cap table, at a 35x ceiling. Phase 3 valuation must inherit this corrected row. `sure`. Wrong only if the operator reads the pharmacy-trading line, now 31% of revenue, as reclassifying the whole company toward a distribution multiple, which the core IVF economics do not support.

5. **Backward revenue: FIRING.** Three-year revenue CAGR is about 33% and the last year grew 48%. `sure`. Wrong only if the FY26 consolidated revenue of Rs 104.36 Cr were misstated, and it is not, it ties to the audited consolidated result.

6. **Backward margin: COMPRESSING.** Consolidated EBITDA margin fell from about 45% to 36% over three years as low-margin pharmacy trading grew from under 1% to 31% of revenue. `fairly sure`. Wrong if the compression is read as pure mix with an intact core, which is true at the standalone IVF level near 51%, but the consolidated number is what the transition scores.

7. **Backward cash conversion: DETERIORATING.** CFO over PAT was 0.46x in FY25 and 0.30x in FY26, and receivable days rose from single digits to about 187. `sure`. Wrong only if the FY26 receivables of Rs 53.38 Cr were misstated, and they tie to the audited balance sheet.

8. **Backward ROCE: TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED, not DECLINING.** Reported ROCE fell from about 54% in FY25 to about 20% in FY26, and the cause is identified and mechanical: roughly Rs 81 Cr of unspent IPO money sits in the capital base earning almost nothing. The core business, stripped of that idle cash, still earns about 39% on standalone capital. `fairly sure`. Wrong if the receivables build, not the idle cash, turns out to be the real drag on returns, in which case the depression is structural and the verdict is DECLINING.

9. **Forward cash conversion: DECLINING, and the determination is INDETERMINATE.** This is the pivot of the whole call. Receivable days were still rising into FY26, the only fix on offer is a price change management described on the call but has not yet shown in any print, and peers collect far faster, so the long cycle is not a sector norm. The structural-versus-growth test does not resolve cleanly because the affordability model, revenue booked at embryo transfer while patients pay across the cycle, is partly how they have chosen to sell, not only a growth artefact. Indeterminate cash never resolves to a clean pass, so it caps the disposition at DEEP WATCH / proceed with caveats. `fairly sure` on DECLINING, `genuinely uncertain` on structural versus growth-induced. Both readings: growth-induced would say a slowdown releases the receivables and the price change fixes it, structural would say the model itself carries the cash lag; the draft used INDETERMINATE, which is the conservative resolution and the one the missing rating rationale forces. Q1 FY27 receivable days settle it.

10. **Forward ROCE: RECOVERING, at moderate probability, credited in Pillar 1.** The idle-cash depression is mechanical and the core return is high, so on a normalized basis the recovery is real. But the cash is being spent on new hubs that will not earn for 12 to 24 months, which delays the recovery showing in the reported consolidated number, and the receivables drag persists. `genuinely uncertain`. Both readings: RECOVERING credits the normalized core-return base and scores +1; STAGNANT says the fresh hub capex and the receivables drag keep reported ROCE flat near 20% for a year and scores 0. The draft used RECOVERING at the low end of moderate. It flips to STAGNANT if Q1 FY27 shows no receivables improvement and hub openings slip.

11. **Forward revenue: FIRING; forward margin: STAGNANT.** Revenue momentum is strong before any new hub opens, on existing-centre volume, AI-assisted pricing and patient financing. Margin has no documented expansion catalyst and a live pharmacy-dilution trend, but management deprioritises pharmacy and the core margin is intact, so it rounds to STAGNANT rather than DECLINING. Revenue `fairly sure`, margin `fairly sure`.

12. **Composite +2, DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID. The Kernex cap does not formally engage.** Revenue +2, margin 0, cash −1, ROCE +1. Cash is DECLINING but a specific pricing action was named, so catalyst strength is Weak not None, and the automatic DEEP WATCH cap that needs a None catalyst does not strictly fire. The verdict lands in the same place on the score alone. `fairly sure`. Wrong upward only if the cash catalyst were documented and operational, wrong downward if you judge the pricing change to be no catalyst at all, which would engage the cap and hold the verdict at DEEP WATCH regardless.

13. **Tier A, 25% hurdle.** Institutions hold about 5% combined, above the 3% absence test, but the quality gates for the lower Tier B hurdle fail: the quality screen is AVERAGE not GOOD, and the promoter verdict is CONCERN not trustworthy. So the standard 25% tier applies. `sure`.

14. **Undiscovered-alpha multiplier: not applied.** It needs a listing at least 12 months old and institutions under 3%. The company listed about 5 months ago and institutions are near 5%, so it fails on both counts. `sure`.

15. **Single credit and shared catalyst.** The ROCE recovery is credited once, in Pillar 1, and the strategic re-rating premium for ROCE stays barred. The same event, spending the IPO money on hubs, drives both the revenue growth premium and the ROCE recovery, so it is flagged SHARED CATALYST for the devil's advocate to stress as one point of failure. `sure`.

## The catalyst story in plain words

What could make the missing transitions fire, and what would confirm or kill each.

**Cash conversion, the binding one.** The catalyst is the pricing change already described: patients now pay Rs 80,000 upfront instead of Rs 40,000, and owe another Rs 40,000 if they stall the transfer beyond a year. If it works, receivable days fall and cash conversion climbs back toward half of profit. What confirms it: Q1 FY27 receivable days below the roughly 150 to 187 day FY26 level, with the bad-debt provision flat or lower. What kills it: days holding or rising with the provision growing. The honest read today is that this is a claim, not yet an action you can see, and peers collect in about 105 to 115 days or take cash upfront, so Gaudium is starting from a worse place than the sector.

| Catalyst | Evidence | Confirms | Kills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upfront payment doubled, late-transfer fee added | Management claim, Q4 FY26 call, stated effective now | Q1 FY27 debtor days below ~150, provision flat | Days flat or rising, provision up |
| Patient financing tie-ups (HDFC, NBFCs) | Management claim, Q4 FY26 call | Tier 2/3 volume up, receivables mix improves | No visible volume shift |
| Nineteen new hubs, Rs 50 Cr IPO money earmarked | Documented earmark, but Rs 0 spent at FY26 close | Openings track the 10, 8, 1 schedule in quarterly prints | Openings slip, capex stays unspent |
| AI embryology (SiD, ERICA) lifting success rates | Management claim, live since April 2026 | A full-quarter success-rate print holds above the 58% base | Claim not repeated or not sustained |
| International spokes (Nigeria, Sydney, Paris) | Management claim, "this quarter" | Foreign-exchange earnings finally appear, currently nil | Spokes do not open, forex stays nil |

**Revenue** does not need a catalyst to keep firing near term, existing centres are still ramping. The nineteen-hub plan is the medium-term engine, but none of it is spent yet, so it is a plan.

**Margin** has no documented expansion catalyst. The one thing to watch is the pharmacy share: if it keeps climbing, the consolidated margin keeps sliding even while the clinic stays strong.

**ROCE** recovers only through the same event that drives revenue, spending the IPO cash on hubs. That is why it is flagged as a shared catalyst: if the hub rollout disappoints, both the growth and the return recovery miss together.

An important absence: there is no exchange-announcements feed in this run and no credit rating, so the documented-action ledger runs on the call and the annual report alone. On the binding transition, cash, management has vision and a named price change but nothing yet documented as operational, which under the intent-and-action rule confirms the conservative read rather than lifting it.

## THE VERDICT

Composite score +2 out of 8: revenue firing (+2), margin stagnant (0), cash declining (−1), ROCE recovering (+1). That places the forward call at DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID, which asks for an extreme margin of safety and a strict entry zone before any starter. The Kernex cap, the automatic hold-at-DEEP-WATCH for a transition going backwards with no catalyst at all, does not strictly engage because management did name a specific price change on cash, so the score carries the verdict on its own. The whole call turns on one print: Q1 FY27 trade receivable days. If they fall from the roughly 150 to 187 day base with the bad-debt provision flat, cash starts to earn a pass and the picture improves. If they hold or rise, the affordability model is funding growth structurally and the case weakens further.
