  "unrecoverable balances in receivables due to cumulative changes in foreign exchange
  rates"**, in the quarter ended 30-Sep-2025. This item sits in "Other Expenses" (Note

  is flagged as a disclosure-quality item for Pass 2/3 scrutiny. Same item and amount
  appears at Note 44 in the consolidated set (p.374).
