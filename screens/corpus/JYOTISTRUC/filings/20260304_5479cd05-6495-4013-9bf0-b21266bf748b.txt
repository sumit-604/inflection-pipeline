Sub: Intimation on Successful Completion and Commissioning of 400 kV Gadag–II Transmission 
Line Project in Karnataka. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL”) is pleased to inform about the successful completion and 
commissioning of the 400 kV Gadag–II Transmission Line Project for its client, Renew Power, in the 
state of Karnataka. 
 
The project comprises approximately 100 CKM of twin High-Temperature Low-Sag (HTLS) 
transmission line between Gadag Pooling Station and Koppal Pooling Station, traversing through 
challenging Right of Way (ROW) districts, namely Koppal and Gadag, and difficult interior areas 
characterized by black cotton soil conditions. 
 
The project forms part of the Inter-State Transmission System (ISTS) for evacuation of power from 
renewable energy sources in the Koppal Solar Energy Zone (1500 MW). 
 
The successful completion and commissioning of this nationally significant project will support 
renewable power evacuation and contribute towards India’s renewable energy targets.
