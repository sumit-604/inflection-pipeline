**Mandatory diagnostics:**
1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, +4.44% (₹119.94→125.27 Cr). The media release/deck round this to **"+5%"** — a ~0.5pp overstatement; the precise figure is +4.4% (barely above discretionary-apparel inflation). No management revenue guidance exists in Notion to compare against; this is the baseline reading.
2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand/contract/stay flat YoY?** **Contracted hard: Q1FY27 21.21% vs Q1FY26 25.89% = −468 bps.** (Media release states "−460 bps" — see reconciliation flag below; the accurate figure on operating EBITDA is −468 bps, and on the deck's rounded margins 21.2%−25.9% = −470 bps. Media's "−460" understates by ~8-10 bps — a minor internal inconsistency, flagged not fatal.)
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY?** **No — it fell −84.3% (₹6.62→1.04 Cr).** This is worse than the reported PAT fall (−63.7%) because rising Other Income (+₹0.48 Cr) and a lower tax charge partly cushioned reported PAT. **The headline decline understates the operating deterioration: on a clean core basis the quarter almost broke even.** Other Income / PBT leapt to **66.9%** (from 19.7%) — two-thirds of the little PBT that remains is non-operating.
4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT and reported-PAT change?** Other Income +₹0.48 Cr; Depreciation +₹1.01 Cr (drag); Finance cost +₹0.09 Cr (drag); **tax fell −₹1.07 Cr (₹1.93→0.86 Cr), adding to PAT despite a HIGHER ETR (27.3% vs 23.5%)** because the PBT base collapsed. Each delta is quantified in the Step 4 bridge.
5. **Are D&A / finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** Depreciation +5.5% vs revenue +4.4% — modestly faster (asset-light; RoU/lease depreciation dominant). Finance cost flat. Not an absorption-deficit story (no plant commissioning); the margin damage is in **Other Expenses (marketing)**, not capex.
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Q1FY27 OI ₹2.10 Cr is 66.9% of PBT (vs 19.7% a year ago) — not because OI spiked (it is small) but because **core PBT collapsed**. Stripping OI, the quarter's core PBT is ₹1.04 Cr. Reading it clean makes the quarter look materially worse than the −63% headline.

**Root cause (from deck P&L, line 231-252 & MD comments line 109-113):** Other Expenses +₹6.81 Cr YoY (34.37→41.18), of which **marketing/brand-building = +₹5.3 Cr (₹10.7 Cr = 8.5% of revenue in Q1FY27 vs ₹5.4 Cr in Q1FY26)**. Marketing alone explains ~78% of the other-expenses rise and essentially the entire ₹4.48 Cr operating-EBITDA decline. Management frames this as a deliberate MUFTI 2.0 premiumisation investment, guided at **8-10% of revenue through FY27** (i.e., the drag is guided to persist all year — A3 FF-01/F6-A).
