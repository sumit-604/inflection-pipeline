===== PAGE 7 =====
AWFIS AT A GLANCE. Rs 425 Cr reported revenue Q1 FY27. 38.2% reported operating EBITDA.
55% ROCE. 18 cities including 9 Tier 2. Signed supply 267 centres / 185K seats. Total
supply 251 centres / 170K seats. Managed Aggregation portfolio 57%. Mature centre
occupancy 83% / blended 76%. Weighted average total tenure ~38 months, lock-in ~26 months.
Footnote: "Managed Aggregation: In this model operators & space owners share capex as well
as revenues". ROCE defined as (IndAS EBITDA - actual lease payments + impact of finance
lease) / average capital employed.
