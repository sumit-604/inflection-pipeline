# Stage 12 Verifier B: Independent Concall Red-Flag Audit — FABTECH

Run date: 2026-08-04 | Model: claude-opus-4-8 | Emits: B12b

Scope: I read all four Fabtech concall transcripts myself, fresh, before opening any
upstream analysis: Nov-2025 (Q2 FY26), Feb-2026 (Q3 FY26), Apr-2026 (Q4/FY26),
Aug-2026 (Q1 FY27). I then compared my independent red-flag list against B05 (concall)
and B06 (peers).

STRUCTURAL NOTE THAT SHAPES THE SCORE: B05 explicitly analysed only the **three most
recent** quarters (Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27) and **excluded the Nov-2025 / Q2 FY26
transcript** "per the 3-most-recent-quarters contract" (B05 report lines 12-14). I read
Nov-2025 in full. Several red flags live only in that call, or are only visible as a
Nov→Feb baseline shift. Those are genuinely uncaught, but the miss is a stated
methodology choice, not maker negligence; I severity-adjust them down accordingly and
flag them so the orchestrator can decide whether the 3-quarter contract should have
admitted Nov given the post-listing tone break sits exactly there.

Numbers-existence questions are Verifier A's non-overridable domain; I anchor to what

## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the raw transcripts alone)

Each item: (call, speaker, page).

1. **FY27 growth guidance walked down twice, pre-delivery.** "30 is the target"
   (Feb, Ashwani, p13) → "25 to 30%" (Apr, Karan, p8) → "20 to 25%" (Aug, Ashwani/Aman,
   p3-4,8). Downward drift before FY27 posted a single full quarter.

2. **FY27 number given at conferences does not reconcile to on-call guidance.** Analyst
   Rahil: "Vivro meet... Arihant... you had given an FY27 revenue targets of like 530 to
   600 crores, but right now you are seeing this 25% on the top line growth, so that is
   not matching" (Apr, p18). Management pivoted to a standalone-vs-consolidated
   "communication difference" (Apr, p19) and never gave a reconciled number.

3. **FY26 PAT landed below the guided floor.** Guided Rs 39-41 Cr PAT (Feb, Aasif, p7);
   delivered reported net profit Rs 38.36 Cr, with FY25 having carried a Rs 17.85 Cr
   exceptional (Apr, Ashwani p3, Karan p6-7). Net profit fell YoY (46.45 → 38.36) and the
   like-for-like operational figure sits under the floor.

4. **Revenue-recognition method unresolved across every quarter, timeline lengthening.**
   Shipment/BL-triggered recognition (Feb p12, Apr p15, Aug p10). Resolution horizon
   moved from "six months or a year" (Feb, Aasif p17) to "next few years" (Aug, Karan
   p19). Three-plus quarters of analyst pressure, no date.

5. **Management openly describes discretion to time revenue by holding or releasing
   shipments.** "Sometimes we hold the material for relationship's sake... We have the
   liberty to ship. We can ship it... But when we see the LC is getting expired, no,
   we'll ship it. We have the flexibility, so we operate with a lot of flexibility"
   (Feb, Aasif, p18); "Otherwise the LC says, ship it, we can ship it, dump it there,
   we'll recognise our revenue" (Feb, Aasif, p11). This is an earnings-timing / smoothing
   lever, not just a predictability issue. Recurring ~Rs 20 Cr "at the port" both Q3 FY26
   (Feb, Karan p5: 20.3 Cr) and Q1 FY27 (Aug, Karan p15: 20-22 Cr).

6. **A majority of FY26 net profit is non-operating other income.** Other income "more
   than doubled... to 21 crores," of which ~Rs 12 Cr is forex and ~Rs 7 Cr is FD interest
   on parked IPO money (Apr, Karan, p17). Against reported PAT of Rs 38.36 Cr, that is
   ~55% of net profit from volatile forex + soon-declining FD interest. Quality-of-earnings
   flag. Surfaced only under analyst questions (Girish, Dhruv, Falak).

7. **Receivables ballooning; cash conversion marginal.** Receivables Rs 204-215 Cr on
   ~Rs 431 Cr FY26 revenue (~48%) (Apr p3; Aug p10). Operating cash flow only "negative
   36 crores to a Positive 50 lakhs" for FY26 (Apr, Karan, p7); in Feb management said it
   "will always remain cash flow negative" during growth (Feb, Aasif/Karan, p20).

8. **Single-customer Saudi concentration.** Saudi receivable of Rs 72.57 Cr is essentially
   one Rs 120 Cr contract (Aug, Karan, p10).

9. **Hot-lead / pipeline figure is volatile and definitionally unstable.** "$455 million"
   hot-lead bank (Feb, Aasif, p6) → "close to 200 million odd USD... with offers in the
   market" (Apr, Aman, p10) → "hot leads worth over Rupees 3,800 crores" inside a new
   "active inquiries exceed Rupees 9,300 crores" funnel (Aug, Ashwani, p3). $455M ≈ Rs
   3,800 Cr, so the Feb and Aug headline numbers roughly agree while Apr's $200M is a
   ~55% air-pocket, with the metric re-labelled each call. Meanwhile the actual order book
   is flat at ~Rs 900-926 Cr across all four calls.

10. **Europe / "JBN" acquisition slips repeatedly.** "First quarter" / "could surprise
    you if it happens before March 31st" (Feb, Aman/Aasif, p12-13) → "yet to go through"
    (Apr, Aman, p14) → "next two to three quarters" (Aug, Aman, p18). Never closed.

11. **IPO-fund deployment delay plus a monitoring-agency restriction.** "The monitoring
    agency did not allow the 50 crores of working capital to be reimbursed... a technical
    reason" (Apr, Karan, p14); acquisition due diligence still open (Apr p14; Aug p18).

12. **Related-party procurement and a dense group web.** Procures ~11% cleanroom panels
    from sister-listed FTCL and ~15% air-handling units via Advantek (~26% combined)
    (Apr, Aman, p16; Aug "not more than 11%" p18). Group: FABL LLP (49%), Fabtech LLC UAE,
    FTS Cleanrooms, Mark Maker (33%), TSA Process Equipments ("exiting," never updated),
    Fabtech Saudi (100%), SACE (51%), FT Institutions (Feb p4-5; Apr p4-5; Aug p3).

13. **Governance churn / founder re-entry after the crash.** Non-exec director Chirag
    Doshi (Nov) → absent Feb, where Founder-Promoter Aasif Khan appears and dominates →
    "transition to executive chairman" announced (Apr, Ashwani, p3) → Aasif absent again
    (Aug). IR framing shifts from Chirag Doshi to Karan Doshi. A founder returning to an
    executive role after a stock crash, with no clean continuity explanation.

14. **Volunteered, then recurring, shareholder-trust problem.** "the stock has crashed,
    leading to the loss of shareholder trust. Honestly speaking, we were not very
    communicative" (Feb, Aasif, p13). Vikas Gupta raises eroding shareholder value / weak
    institutional participation in Feb, Apr and Aug; each time deflected to "the stock is
    something which we really can't control" and "introduce us to the right investors"
    (Apr p8; Aug p9-10, p20).

15. **Order-book geography/segment split refused across all quarters.** "no breakup as
    such" (Feb, Aasif, p11); "I'll just pull up the data" then nothing (Apr, Karan, p13);
    "Vignesh, we do not" (Aug, Karan, p14).

16. **Win-rate / conversion figures never reconcile.** "10 to 12 percent thus far...
    increased to 15%... 20 to 25%" (Feb, p8) → "11%... reaching 16 to 17 percent" (Apr,
    Ashwani, p14). The Feb 20-25% target never appears again.

17. **"Asset-light / not capital-intensive" narrative contradicts the capital reality.**
    Repeatedly "we are not capital-intensive," "asset-light," same team can double revenue,
    only constraint is working capital (Nov, Ashwani/Aman, p12; Apr "asset-light model"
    p5). Against this: Rs 24 Cr+ approved into subsidiaries (Aug p3), IPO-funded
    acquisition hunt, and admitted persistent negative operating cash flow.

18. **Nov-2025 baseline (excluded upstream): the sharpest guidance evasion sits here.**
    Blanket refusal to guide margin or growth — "we would not want to be in a situation
    where we overcommit and underachieve" (Nov, Aman, p4); 3-5-year vision deflected to
    "these initiatives will be published in all leading newspapers" (Nov, Ashwani, p14-15).
    Also in-call factual sloppiness: "20 projects worth INR137 crores" answered as "65% of
    these 12 orders" (20 vs 12) and the questioner Pramod addressed as "Alok" (Nov, p15);
    the FTCL dividend question deflected as "not related to Fabtech Technologies Limited"
    (Nov, Aman, p6-7).

19. **Country count and project-cycle/ticket-size figures wobble.** "operates across 22
    countries" (Nov p3) vs "over 62 countries" (Nov p11, and 62+ thereafter) — both inside
    the Nov call. Project cycle: 18-24 mo (Nov) → 9-18 mo "speeding" (Feb p6) → 23-27 /
    18-24 mo (Apr) → 12-24/12-36 mo (Aug). Ticket size inflates $1.5-3M → $7-10M (Feb) →
    $30-40M → $70M (Apr) with definitions ("ticket" vs "order") used loosely.

Peer cross-read (from B06, which I take as read): no peer contradicts Fabtech by name;
the nearest contradiction is that Ion Exchange and Praj describe percentage-of-completion
/ milestone billing as the settled EPC-industry norm, and both openly quantify war-driven
deferred/lost orders (Rs 60 Cr, Rs 300 Cr) — disclosure Fabtech does not match while
claiming the identical macro driver. This reinforces items 4, 5 and 14 above.

## PART 4: CREDIBILITY-GRADE CONCURRENCE

B05 assigns **C**. I concur. The three genuine gaps I add — the ~55%-of-PAT non-operating
other income (item 6), the pipeline-figure volatility (item 9), and the sharper reading of
revenue-timing discretion (item 5) — all push in the same direction as the C rationale
(earnings quality, guidance/pipeline reliability, cash-conversion). They reinforce C rather
than move it. I would not grade higher; a case exists for C-minus, but C fairly captures the
mix of real delivery (FY26 revenue beat, Q4 PAT met, Saudi localization executed) against
walked-down guidance, unresolved revenue recognition, and thin disclosure.

## PART 5: CONSOLIDATED FINDINGS (standard severity)

No CRITICAL. The one candidate for a CRITICAL (a repeated 2+-quarter evasion missed
entirely) does not apply: every repeated evasion — order-book split, revenue-recognition,
stock/institutional participation — was caught by B05. The revenue-timing-discretion angle
was partially caught. B05's red-flag coverage is, on the whole, strong and honest.

- **MAJOR** — Non-operating other income (~Rs 21 Cr: ~Rs 12 Cr forex + ~Rs 7 Cr IPO-FD
  interest) is ~55% of FY26 reported net profit (Rs 38.36 Cr); earnings-quality dependence
  on volatile/one-off income never flagged by B05 or B06. (Apr, Karan, p17; Ashwani p3)
- **MAJOR** — Hot-lead/pipeline headline is unstable and re-labelled: ~$455M (Feb) →
  ~$200M (Apr) → ~Rs 3,800 Cr (Aug) within a new Rs 9,300 Cr "active inquiries" funnel,
  while the order book stays flat ~Rs 900-926 Cr. Bears directly on trigger #1 (pipeline
  conversion); uncaught. (Feb p6; Apr p10; Aug p3)
- **MAJOR** — Revenue-timing discretion under-weighted: management states it holds/releases
  shipments at its choosing ("we have the flexibility... if the LC is getting expired,
  we'll ship it"), an earnings-smoothing lever, not merely a quarter-lumpiness issue as
  B05 frames it. (Feb, Aasif, p11, p18)
- **MINOR** — "Asset-light / not capital-intensive" narrative contradicts Rs 24 Cr+
  subsidiary deployment, IPO-funded M&A and admitted persistent negative operating cash
  flow. Underlying facts are present in B05/B06; the contradiction is never drawn. Feeds
  Pillar 1 (ROCE/capital efficiency), so worth surfacing to valuation/synthesis.
- **MINOR** — Nov-2025/Q2 FY26 call structurally excluded by B05, so the strongest
  guidance-refusal baseline, the in-call 22-vs-62-countries contradiction, and factual
  sloppiness (wrong investor name; "20 projects" answered as "12 orders") are uncaught.
  Corroborates the existing HIGH guidance-discipline flag; does not change the decision.
- **MINOR** — Project-cycle and ticket-size figures wobble across calls; B05 treats
  ticket-size growth as a validated positive trigger without flagging the inconsistency.
