## LOAD-BEARING FACTS CHECK-IN (first verification priority)

**LBF1 — what produced ~12% EBITDA margin.** CONFIRMED, with a labelling
defect. The AR's own highlights page (p.5) prints "EBITDA" FY26 = ₹2,462.27
Lakhs, margin 9.14%. That number is actually **PBT before exceptional items**
(P&L line V, "Profit/(Loss) exceptional and extraordinary items (III-IV)",
p.57/Note ref P&L). True EBITDA = PBT + Depreciation (Note 24, ₹152.00 Lakhs)
+ Finance Cost (Note 23, ₹654.76 Lakhs) = ₹3,269.03 Lakhs, margin 12.13% on
revenue from operations (₹26,935.66 Lakhs) — this is the ~12% the load-bearing
fact cites. 🟡 Watch (Note 23/24, p.77; highlights p.5).
Composition: gross margin (Revenue − Cost of Material Consumed + inventory
build, Notes 18/20/21) ≈ 17.6%; the FY26 profit also benefits from a
₹1,253.63 Lakh increase in finished-goods inventory (Note 21, p.76-77)
booked as a P&L credit ("Changes in Inventories of Finished Goods"), which
is a bigger single swing than the entire other-income line (Note 19,
₹60.81 Lakhs). No separate "other operating income" or segment EBITDA
(LT ~6.5%/HT ~7%, per company memory) is disclosed in the notes to
triangulate against; NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT for a segment-level P&L.
