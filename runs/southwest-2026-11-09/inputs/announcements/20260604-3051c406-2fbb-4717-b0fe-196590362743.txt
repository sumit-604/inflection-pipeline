Subject: Announcement for receipt of Letters of Award/Work Orders 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We are pleased to inform that South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (SWPE) has been recently awarded two   
Letters of Award/Work Order for imparting exploration, drilling and related support services total valuing over ₹ 
15.17 Crores (including GST) and one work order through its wholly owned subsidiary namely M/s South West 
Resources Private Limited valuing ₹ 6.36 Crores (including GST) as summarised below: 
A) Letter of Award (LOA) received from Central Mine Planning & Design Institute Limited (CMPDI) 
 
• Detailed Exploration (G-1) in a Coal  Block in the state of Chhattisgarh.  
• The aggregate value of work order is approx. ₹14.04 Crores (including GST).  
• Central Mine Planning & Design Institute Limited (CMPDI), is a wholly owned Subsidiary of Coal India Limited. 
 
B) Work Orders from Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd (GSIPL) 
I) Reverse Circulation Drilling 
• Work order is to carry out Reverse Circulation drilling in a Gold mining project in the state of Andhra Pradesh.  
• Reverse circulation (RC) drilling is a new domain of exploration and only a select few companies are operating  
in this domain of drilling.    
• The aggregate value of work order is approx. ₹ 1.13 Crores (including GST) 
• Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd. ( GSIPL) is a established player in mining sector and has made several 
discoveries of Gold and Precious metals in the past .  
• For us GSIPL is a new client, added to our order book. 
II) Daimond Core Drilling 
• Work order is for Daimond core mineral drilling in a Gold mining Project in the state of Andhra Pradesh.  
• The aggregate value of work order is approx. ₹ 6.36 Crores (including GST)
