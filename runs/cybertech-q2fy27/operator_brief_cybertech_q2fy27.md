## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Cybertech sells IT services. It works mainly on maps and location data (GIS, geographic information systems) for US public-sector and utility clients. The GIS detail is background, not checked this run.
2. Revenue grew 24.7% from a year ago, to Rs 73.30 Cr in the quarter.
3. Profit after tax did not grow. It stayed at Rs 8.68 Cr, up only 0.3%.
4. The reason is other income. Interest and investment gains fell by Rs 3.21 Cr and cancelled the operating gain.
5. The core business did much better. Profit before tax without other income nearly doubled, from Rs 3.46 Cr to Rs 6.81 Cr.
6. The gain sits in the US subsidiaries. Their margin jumped from about 2.5% to 11.2% in one quarter. The Indian parent was flat.
7. Management said nothing. The company held no call and filed no presentation or press release.
8. Good: the auditor's review is clean. The company has no debt and holds about Rs 168 Cr of net cash.
9. Worrying: the parent sent Rs 41.13 Cr to a subsidiary it does not name. No note says why.
10. Worrying: equity shows a Rs 12.15 Cr debit with no note. It equals Rs 4.00 a share, so an unpaid dividend is the likely reason. The filing does not confirm it.
11. Worrying: the margin jump is only one quarter old, and a weaker rupee may have helped it. The filing also has four drafting errors.
12. Watch next quarter: core profit before tax. Rs 6.81 Cr or more means the step holds. Rs 4.43 Cr or less means it was a one-off. Results are due by 14-Feb-2027.
