86	Commenting on the Q1FY27 results, Mr. Vikram Vuppala – Chairman & MD said,
87	“Dialysis services across the World are mostly delivered by Pure play dialysis networks. In
88	India, today, only 21% of the dialysis services market is served by Pure play dialysis networks.
89	16 years ago, this number was zero. As the dialysis industry is shifting from Unorganized to
90	Organized, NephroPlus, as the market leader in India stands to gain disproportionately due to
91	its focus on clinical quality, service excellence and patient centricity.
92	As we all know, the dialysis market is growing fast due to the ever-increasing burden of
93	Diabetes and Hypertension and this additional lever of shift from Unorganized to Organized
94	will help NephroPlus expand across India including the Tier 2/Tier 3 cities and beyond.
95	As we continue to scale up in existing countries, we are also exploring entry into new countries
96	via strategic acquisitions or long-term partnerships with payors. We will continue to invest in
97	identifying and understanding new geographies where we believe we can make massive
98	impact on dialysis patients’ lives while creating shareholder value in the long term.”
99	
100	Mr Rohit Singh, Group Chief Executive Officer said,
101	“Q1 FY27 was a quarter of strong financial performance and continued strategic expansion
102	for NephroPlus. Revenue grew 23.7% year-on-year to ₹282 crore, and EBITDA grew 30.7%
103	YoY to ₹65 crore. Guest volumes grew 13% YoY to 38,262, as we crossed 10,30,000
104	treatments in the first quarter of this financial year. Our network reached 550 operating clinics
105	across 5 countries, with a significant 50-clinic milestone in the Philippines. NephroPlus
106	continues to demonstrate that it is a dialysis platform made in India for the world.
107	This past quarter, we launched the NephroPlus International Dialysis Academy (NIDA), an in-
108	house training program based in India and the Philippines, to build a robust pipeline of renal
109	nurses qualified to work anywhere in the world. This will enable us to efficiently staff our
110	international dialysis clinics where hiring renal nurses is a huge challenge. Also, we have
111	undertaken initiatives to increase fistula creation and reduce cross-infections, and have
112	launched the NephroPlus Dialysis Index, a composite health score designed to monitor clinical
113	outcomes at the patient level, which can be aggregated at the Clinic, Cluster, Zone, and
114	Country levels over the next few quarters. Overall, we remain confident in our ability to grow
115	profitably and efficiently while delivering superior clinical quality across every market we
116	serve.”
117	
118	Mr. Prashant Goenka, Chief Financial Officer added,
119	"Q1 FY27 reflects the strength of our India platform — operating profitably at some of the
120	lowest price points globally, a solid reflection of the underlying efficiency of our model. This
121	efficiency, combined with high barriers to entry in dialysis care, gives us the confidence to
122	scale into the higher price-point international markets, while preserving margin discipline
123	rather than chasing growth at any cost.
124	Revenue grew 23.7% year-on-year, while Adjusted EBITDA grew faster at 30.7% and Adjusted
125	PAT faster still at 41.7% — each line outpacing the one above it, a clear signature of operating
126	leverage as platform strength converts scale into higher profitability and returns. Capital
127	continues to be deployed with discipline, keeping us on track for durable, long-term value
128	creation"
129	About Nephrocare Health Services Limited:
130	Nephrocare Health Services Limited (NSE: NEPHROPLUS; BSE: 544647) is Asia’s largest
131	dialysis network, owning and operating 550 dialysis centres under the NephroPlus brand. The
132	Company has a strong and growing footprint across India, Nepal, the Philippines, Uzbekistan,
133	and Saudi Arabia, delivering standardized, high-quality dialysis through a scalable and asset-
134	light model.
