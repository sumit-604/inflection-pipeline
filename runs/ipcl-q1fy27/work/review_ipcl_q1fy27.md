6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Falling, not rising (Rs 0.22 -> 0.15 Cr; OI/PBT 7.39% -> 2.15%). No treasury-timing distortion to strip out. The quarter is clean of Other-Income masking (the quality question is inventory, not treasury — diagnostic 3).

Net: thesis INTACT, operating planks STRENGTHENED but with a cash-quality caveat, governance and balance-sheet planks unchanged-to-worse, one binding trigger un-testable by the company's own disclosure choice.

### 2. SECTOR INTELLIGENCE

*(Provenance: sector framing from prior Notion/Gate-0 work; this-quarter datapoints from the filing as tagged.)*
IPCL is an Indian precision investment-casting foundry serving industrial, automotive, and — per the prior thesis — a growing aerospace-and-defence (A&D) end-market (prior Notion; EM 36/80 STRENGTHENING). The certifications on the filing letterhead (NADCAP for NDT, AS9100/EN9100, IATF 16949, ISO 13485 medical, ISO 9001/14001/45001; l.19-27) are the sector's real entry barriers — an aerospace and medical casting supplier cannot ship without them, and they take years to earn. The demand cycle read this quarter is favourable on the numbers actually printed: +21.2% revenue YoY and +619 bps margin (this filing, l.147, l.162) point to firm order flow and either better pricing or better mix — though part of the profit is a non-cash inventory build (l.154) whose demand-backing is unconfirmed at Q1. The structural tailwind the thesis leans on — India's defence indigenisation and aerospace-component localisation — is NOT quantified anywhere in this bare Reg 33 filing (NOT DISCLOSED), and Note 2's segment collapse (l.208) now hides the A&D revenue line that would have shown whether the tailwind is converting. Input-cost read: power-and-fuel intensity fell to 8.17% of revenue (l.158/l.147) from 9.62% a year ago — a genuine positive for an energy-intensive melting business, whether from the company's solar programme (prior Notion: solar P&F intensity FY25 12.0% -> FY26 10.5%) or favourable tariffs. There is no payer-mix or regulatory-reimbursement dimension (this is a manufacturer, not a healthcare or lending business). The sector headwind visible in the filing is the rising reliance on outsourced/job-work processing (33.17% of expenses, l.159/l.161) — either capacity is constrained and work is being farmed out, or the value chain is shifting outside the company's own plant, which caps the margin ceiling.

### 3. BUSINESS-MODEL INTELLIGENCE

*(Provenance: model structure from prior Notion/Gate-0; all ratios this-quarter from the filing as tagged.)*
IPCL makes money by converting metal and engineering into finished precision castings ("You Design, We Cast", l.21). The economics visible this quarter: for every Rs 100 of revenue, cost of materials is ~32% (l.152/l.147), external processing ~29% (l.159/l.147), power-and-fuel ~8% (l.158/l.147), and employees ~6% (l.155/l.147), leaving an operating EBITDA margin of 20.73% (this filing). A key model signal this quarter is the inventory build: the Rs 2.60 Cr Q1 build (l.154) flatters reported margin by crediting expenses, and in a working-capital-heavy foundry the truest test — whether that stock turns to cash — is NOT disclosed at Q1 (INDETERMINATE until the Q2 half-yearly). The second drift signal is external processing: it rose to 33.17% of total expenses from 30.35% a year ago (l.159/l.161) — the business is increasingly a design-and-finish orchestrator that outsources a rising share of physical processing, which lifts asset-light flexibility but hands margin and quality control to third parties (F13, monitoring item 3, RED). The third is the balance sheet: gross bank debt Rs 77.60 Cr and rising (l.102), so this is a capital-employing, interest-paying model (finance cost Rs 1.72 Cr, l.156), not a self-funding one. Unit economics improved on operating leverage — depreciation (+18.3%) and finance cost (+8.0%) both grew slower than revenue (+21.2%) — but roughly a third of the PBT gain is the unverified inventory credit, so the cash-earnings improvement is smaller than the reported one. The wholly-owned subsidiary I&PCL Vacuum Cast Limited is presently a non-operating shell (Nil revenue, Rs 0.23 L loss, l.332-335); its name hints at a future vacuum-casting/A&D line, but the filing shows no revenue, no capex and no timeline (F3). Governance is a live model risk, not a footnote: MD compensation is structurally tied to sanctioned bank credit (1% guarantee commission) and to net profit (3% uncapped profit commission) per prior Notion — meaning rising debt and rising profit both mechanically increase promoter pay, and this quarter delivered more of both.

### 4. COMPETITION INTELLIGENCE

*(Provenance: peer/competitive framing from prior Notion/Gate-0; this filing discloses NO peer or market-share data — that gap is named.)*
This filing discloses **no** competitor names, no market share, and no order-book comparison (NOT DISCLOSED — a bare Reg 33 results filing carries none of it); the competitive read is therefore carried from prior Notion/Gate-0 work and cannot be refreshed against peers this quarter. Where IPCL wins structurally: the stacked certification wall (NADCAP, AS9100, ISO 13485 medical; l.19-27) makes it a qualified supplier in aerospace, defence and medical castings — segments where switching a foundry requires re-qualification measured in years, which is a genuine moat at the customer-relationship level even though Gate-0 scores the overall Moat only 8/60 (prior Notion). Where it is structurally weaker: it is a small-cap foundry (quarterly revenue ~Rs 53 Cr) competing for A&D and industrial work against far larger, better-capitalised precision-casting and forging houses; its rising external-processing dependence (33.17% of expenses, l.159/l.161) signals it lacks the in-house capacity to keep the full value chain, which larger integrated peers retain. The competitive risk to watch: because Note 2 removed the A&D segment disclosure (l.208), an investor can no longer see whether IPCL is gaining or losing share in its highest-value niche — the one place the thesis says it competes best is now the one place the filing has gone dark. That opacity, more than any named rival, is the competitive signal of the quarter. Peer-concall cross-check is not performable this quarter (no concall filed; Role 5 N.A.).
