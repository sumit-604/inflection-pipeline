### 3. BUSINESS-MODEL INTELLIGENCE

**Provenance: this quarter's filing + the recovered thesis (business-model frame).** The company makes money three ways, visible in the standalone-vs-consolidated spread: (a) the core garment-export/manufacturing engine (parent plus Young Brand Apparel/YBAL), which does the bulk of profit — standalone PAT Rs 26.5 Cr on Rs 275 Cr revenue, 9.6% PAT margin (filing), and YBAL is profitable inside the Garment Division at 17.6% EBITDA (filing L227–228); (b) a branded-retail arm (S.P. The unit economics that matter: the parent is now lightly levered (finance cost just Rs 2.4 Cr) and margin-rich, while the group is levering the subsidiary layer (consolidated finance cost up 26%) to fund overseas expansion. The model drift this quarter is exactly what the thesis feared — profit is reinvested (only ~8% dividend payout) into overseas subsidiaries that are, for now, diluting group returns and pushing the group tax rate to ~30% because their losses earn no shield.
