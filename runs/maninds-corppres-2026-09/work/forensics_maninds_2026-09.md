## ROCE-BASIS RESOLUTION (load-bearing)

Deck claim (slide 4, L124): "18.4% FY26 ROCE" and "9.2% FY26 ROE", both with NO definition or formula anywhere across the 37 slides (checked; no ROCE/ROE basis note exists).

What reconciles:
- ROE 9.2% = PAT 1,705 / AVERAGE net worth ((16,073+20,865)/2 = 18,469) = 9.23%. So the deck uses AVERAGE denominators.
- Plain EBIT / (net worth + debt) year-end: EBIT (PBT 2,370 + finance 1,520 = 3,890 Mn) / (NW 20,865 + borrowings 4,997) = 15.0%; incl leases = 14.3%. Near the operator's 13.8-13.95%.
- Ex-other-income EBIT: (EBITDA 4,679 - other income 286 - D&A 789 = 3,604) / 25,862 = 13.9% -> this is the operator's 13.8% construction.
- To reach 18.4% the denominator must drop to ~21,141 Mn. That is EBIT (incl other income) 3,890 / average capital employed with the Rs326 Cr Jammu CWIP EXCLUDED (avg CE ~20,952 -> 18.6%). Nothing else gets there.

Resolution: 18.4% is flattered on three levers, none disclosed:
1. Numerator includes other income (the deck's own non-standard EBITDA definition, L1118) inflating EBIT.
2. Denominator strips the growing Rs326 Cr Capital-WIP (Jammu, non-earning) out of capital employed.
3. Average (not year-end) capital employed, which is lower than the FY26 year-end base.
The tell: a 18.4% ROCE against a 9.2% ROE is a 920bps gap, far wider than a lightly levered pipe-maker should show; the ROCE numerator is inflated and/or the CE understated. Do NOT adopt 18.4%. The operator's consolidated 13.8% (EBIT ex-other-income over full year-end capital employed including CWIP) is the conservative, defensible basis and survives this deck. Cite both deck numbers at L124; no reconciling basis note exists in the source. -> A4 management question.
