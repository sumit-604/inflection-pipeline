## 1. SUMMARY NARRATIVE

1. Karnika makes kidswear in Howrah and sells it through large buying houses. It also owns 75% of Kidcity, a smaller retail and trading arm.
2. Q2 standalone revenue rose 17.4% to Rs 81.83 Cr (FILED). That includes Karnika's sales to Kidcity, which joined the group after last year's quarter. If 60% of Kidcity's purchases came from Karnika, sales to outside customers grew about 6% (INFERENCE). Management quoted 52.3%, but that compares the group with last year's standalone company (FILED, MGMT).
3. Core profit before other income rose only 7.4% (FILED). About half of the profit gain came from other income (FILED, derived).
4. Operating margin fell to 18.53% from 20.72% (FILED). Q2 gross margin held flat on a weak base. Over the half it fell 3.84 points, so the raw material pass-through is not proven (FILED, derived). Processing and staff costs rose.
5. The business used cash in the half. Standalone operating cash flow was minus Rs 4.88 Cr on profit of Rs 18.17 Cr (FILED).
6. Customers owe more and pay slowly. Receivable days rose to 141.7 from 109.7 (FILED, derived).

10. Karnika paid about Rs 3.67 Cr for 75% of Kidcity (FILED, derived; the filing gives no price). That is less than one year of Kidcity's profit. Kidcity's margin doubled after the deal, on goods Karnika makes, so the transfer price decides where group profit lands (INFERENCE). Kidcity turned only Rs 0.47 Cr of its Rs 5.30 Cr half-year profit into cash (FILED, derived).
