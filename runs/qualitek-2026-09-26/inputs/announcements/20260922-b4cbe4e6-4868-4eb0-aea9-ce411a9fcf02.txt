    Dear Sir/Madam,

     In reference to the captioned subject and in terms of the provisions of Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
    Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as amended from time to time (“SEBI Listing Regulations, 2015”), we wish to inform that the Board
     of Directors of Qualitek Labs Limited (“the Company”), at their meeting held today i.e. Tuesday, September 22, 2026, has inter alia,
     considered and approved the following business:

1.   Preferential issue of Equity Shares

    The issuance of Equity Shares on a preferential basis under Chapter V of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital
    and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended (‘SEBI ICDR Regulations”), subject to the approval of the shareholders
     of the Company, as applicable:

   Up to 3,86,000 Equity Shares of face value of ₹ 10/- each, at an issue price of ₹518.55/- each including the premium of ₹508.55/-,
     aggregating to an amount up-to ₹ 20,01,60,300/- to the proposed allottees as indicated in Annexure A1, belonging to the Promoter and
    non-promoter category.

    The detailed disclosure as required pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated
    January 30, 2026, is enclosed as “Annexure-A”

2.   Preferential issue of Fully Convertible Warrants

    The issuance of Warrants on a preferential basis under Chapter V of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and
     Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, as amended (‘SEBI ICDR Regulations”), subject to the approval of the shareholders of
     the Company, as applicable:

   Up to 5,20,000 Fully Convertible Warrants (“Warrants”), each carrying a right to subscribe to 1 Equity Share of face value of ₹10/-
    each of the Company, at an issue price of ₹ 518.55/- each, aggregating to an amount up to ₹ 26,96,46,000/-, to the proposed allottee as
     indicated in Annexure B1 belonging to the promoter and promoter group.

    The detailed disclosure as required pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated
    January 30, 2026 is enclosed as “Annexure-B”

3.   Decided to call Extra-Ordinary General Meeting of the Company on Friday, October 16, 2026, and approved the draft notice of Extra
    Ordinary General Meeting of the Company.

    The meeting of the Board of Directors commenced at 04:30 PM and concluded at 08:30 PM.

   You are requested to kindly take the same on your records.
