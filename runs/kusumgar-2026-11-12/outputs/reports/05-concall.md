## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE

### 3A Competitors
- Gliders India Limited called a "peer", a mandated supplier of some products to the Indian military; Kusumgar claims raw material integration gives faster turnaround and cost (Concall p.10). Kohli Enterprise question not answered directly. Credibility: partly checkable (Gliders mandate unverified here).
- "one of only two suppliers globally" for zero-porosity parachute fabric and "only Indian manufacturer of Kevlar filament fabrics" (Concall p.5). Later: "there are lots of people ... all around the world" for parachute systems (Concall p.14). Prospectus: "one of the major players in military parachute fabrics outside the United States and China" (Prospectus p.198). Two framings of the moat; treat the "two suppliers" claim as MANAGEMENT VIEW, unverified.
- Q2 Q&A 9: "few, if any, qualified players" in specialised systems; wider field in less specialised products (Q2 deck p.23).

### 3B Industry intelligence
US is 10-15% of revenue; blocked shipments released when tariffs lifted in Q4 FY26 (Concall p.7); Middle East finished goods not meaningful (p.9); military textiles are consumables with steady plus surge demand (p.9-10); India incumbent, global challenger (p.10). Q2 deck: India defence budget +15% to 7.85 lakh crore, global spend 2.9 trillion dollars in 2025, defence exports 38,424 crore in FY26, +63% (Q2 deck p.19). Raw materials: polyester yarn, nylon yarn, coating chemicals, most price rises passed with a lag; imported materials 38.91% of FY26 consumption vs 27.92% (Q2 Q&A 12-13, p.23-24).

### 3C Toughest questions
1. Receivables and cash flow (P. Shrinavas Reddy): answered with Q4 anomaly; not satisfactory, no number; real risk (H1 CFO 16.8% of EBITDA).
2. Order book: refused; real risk (no visibility).
3. Margin volatility 19% to 37% to 31% (Aadesh Gosalia): "very similar numbers in 27"; partly satisfactory. The CFO said Q4 FY26 margin 41% while the analyst said 37%; not reconciled on the call (Concall p.6, p.8).
4. Capacity and capex (Nishita Jain): answered with ranges; the "next quarter" fill claim is untested.
5. Segment split: refused.

### 3D Customer and order signals
Customers refused by name; bulletproof jacket fabric sold to domestic manufacturers; PSUs (Gliders, Troop Comforts) buy directly; foreign sales through registered agents (Concall p.10, p.13-14). New parachute programme is under licence from a technology partner, not own IP (Concall p.13). Government receivables carry an ECL (Q2 Q&A 6). Outdoor and Lifestyle brand projections shift materially (Concall p.8). No wins, losses or pricing renegotiation disclosed.
