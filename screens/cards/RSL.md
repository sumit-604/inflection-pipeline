## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Rajputana Stainless melts and
rolls stainless steel long products. Its revenue splits into rolled black bar,
billets, rolled bright bar, forging ingots, flat and patti, wire rods, and
small volumes of round-cornered square and mill scale (RHP, page 408). Rolled
black bar alone was 59.05% of Fiscal 2025 revenue. The rolling mill has an
installed capacity of 36,000 MT a year (RHP, page 245). Wire rods are made
entirely by a third party on job work, and third-party units also supplement
in-house output.

The money is the conversion spread between scrap and alloy inputs and the bar
price, multiplied by tonnes. It sells mostly in India; exports are a smaller
share (RHP, page 247).

**What is changing?** Margin, not volume. Profit before tax went from Rs 28.58
crore in Fiscal 2023 to Rs 42.32 crore in Fiscal 2024 to Rs 54.64 crore in
Fiscal 2025, while revenue was flat (RHP, page 103). Cost of materials fell
from 78.99% of revenue in Fiscal 2023 to 73.47% in Fiscal 2025, computed from
the same statement.

**Why now?** The company listed six months ago and has raised money to
integrate forward. It intends to make stainless-steel seamless pipes from its
own rolled bars: "By utilizing these in-house produced rolled bars, the
proposed forward integration initiatives would enable us to produce
stainless-steel seamless pipes" (RHP, page 245).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The spread must hold and the
pipe plant must get built and filled. Nothing else in this business creates
value. It is a converter.

**What breaks it?** The spread, and the cash. Net cash from operating
activities collapsed from Rs 31.49 crore in Fiscal 2024 to Rs 7.08 crore in
Fiscal 2025, in the year profit before tax rose 29% (RHP, page 417). Cash and
cash equivalents at the start of the six months to September 2025 were Rs 2.93
lakh. This company runs on bank lines, not on cash.

Wire rods fell from Rs 91.07 crore to Rs 7.93 crore in one year. That is a

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds any
later valuation. Spread, utilisation, cycle position and cost-curve rank are
the variables. Never feed spot-year ROCE or rupee working-capital trends into
Section 1B for this name.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Thin, and the prospectus does not claim otherwise. It names "over two decades
of operating experience", "cordial relationships with a wide base of
customers" and "a customer-centric approach, offering stainless-steel products
tailored to specific customer requirements" (RHP, page 247). Tailoring bar
grades is normal practice, not a moat.

The one real advantage is in-house rolled bar as feedstock for the proposed
pipe plant, which the company says gives "operational efficiency, reducing
production costs, ensure consistent raw material supply" (RHP, page 245). That
advantage does not exist until the plant does. Moat: absent.

**Classification: margin-led, on flat volume, inside a commodity cycle.**
Revenue was Rs 947.67 crore, Rs 909.81 crore and Rs 932.16 crore across three
years. It did not grow. Profit before tax nearly doubled because the input
spread widened. That is cycle position, not a business improving.

The cash line is the warning. Fiscal 2025 converted Rs 54.64 crore of pre-tax
profit into Rs 7.08 crore of operating cash. The company also began buying
traded goods for the first time in Fiscal 2025, Rs 59.49 crore, rising to
Rs 47.42 crore in a single half-year. Trading revenue is not conversion
revenue, and it changes what the margin means.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 commodity price-taker. TO R2 cost-advantaged converter, on the
strength of backward-integrated feedstock into seamless pipes.

The mechanism is stated and it is sound in principle. A bar maker that makes
its own pipe feedstock takes out a supplier's margin and a freight leg. The
prospectus also cites China plus one, Make in India, the specialty steel PLI
scheme and the Smart Cities Mission as demand drivers (RHP, page 245).

One rung, inside the base rate. But the rung has not been climbed: the pipe
plant is an object of the offer, not an asset.

## 8. Proof check

There are no quarters. The last period in the corpus is the six months to
30 September 2025, and the company listed six months after that.

That half-year did deliver: profit before tax of Rs 32.32 crore against
Rs 54.64 crore for the whole of Fiscal 2025, so the run rate improved again.
Operating cash also recovered to Rs 23.52 crore in the half.

**Name the absence:** no reported period exists as a listed company, and no
document held says when the seamless pipe plant will run. The entire
transition is unevidenced.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- Revenue is flat across three years while profit doubles. The gain is spread,
  not growth. Flag, and it sets the archetype.
- Operating cash of Rs 7.08 crore against pre-tax profit of Rs 54.64 crore in
  Fiscal 2025. Flag.
- Cash and cash equivalents of Rs 2.93 lakh at the start of H1 FY26. The
  business holds effectively no cash. Flag.
- Purchase of traded goods appears for the first time in Fiscal 2025 at
  Rs 59.49 crore, then Rs 47.42 crore in one half-year. Trading is being mixed
  into a manufacturing margin. Flag.
- Wire rod revenue fell from Rs 91.07 crore to Rs 7.93 crore in a year, with no
  explanation in the pages held. Flag.
- Wire rods are made "exclusively on a job work basis by a third-party
  manufacturer" (RHP, page 247). Job-work dependence. Flag.
- Product and revenue tables are certified by the statutory auditors
  M/s Ruparel & Bavadiya. Noted, not a flag.
- Promoter holding, pledge and credit rating NOT FOUND.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** The seamless pipe plant is a proposal. The profit
  improvement to date is a spread move in a flat-revenue converter.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL-FEATURE.** Near-zero cash, weak conversion and bank
  dependence are the normal shape of a small stainless converter. They are not
  the debris of a climb.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a corpus document: Bull AI
  company record, market capitalisation Rs 1,506.72 crore. Fiscal 2025 profit
  before tax was Rs 54.64 crore. The market capitalisation is roughly 28 times
  pre-tax profit, before any tax, for a business whose revenue has not grown in
  three years.

**Posture: AVOID.** Structural ugliness with the gap closed is the dominating
overlay.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.** Do not spend a `/step1` on this name now.

A commodity converter earns its multiple at the bottom of a cycle, not after
the spread has already doubled its profit. Revenue has been flat for three
years. Cash conversion in the best profit year was Rs 7.08 crore on Rs 54.64
crore of pre-tax profit. The one genuine transition, seamless pipes, is a line
in the objects of the offer with no capacity, no date and no rupee number
attached. The market already pays about 28 times pre-tax profit for all of it.

**Load-bearing facts that would have to be verified before this is reopened**
1. Seamless pipe plant: capacity, cost, commissioning date, and how much of
   the offer proceeds fund it.
2. Promoter identity and post-offer holding.
3. Why wire rod revenue collapsed from Rs 91.07 crore to Rs 7.93 crore.
4. How much of the margin gain is manufacturing spread and how much is the new
   trading book.
5. The two listed quarters that now exist and are not in this corpus.
6. Working capital days and the bank facility structure behind Rs 2.93 lakh of
   cash.

**What would change the view**
- A commissioned pipe plant with volume, which would be a real rung change.
- A price fall that restores a converter multiple to a converter.
- Evidence that revenue, not just spread, is growing.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the
name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
