PAT five year CAGR is 52% (FY21 to FY26, B03) and revenue three year CAGR is 25% CN (B10). Six of twelve Gate 0 moats confirmed (moat block 23/60, B01).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**
1. Bhiwadi copper tube ramp (HIGH conviction; commissioned Jun-2025). Confirms if copper tubes segment revenue and utilisation rise sequentially; kills if segment revenue stalls or falls for two or more consecutive quarters. **SHARED CATALYST flag: this same commissioning carries Pillar 1 forward ROCE and any growth premium and sits behind the FY26 inventory build.**

4. Copper tube import substitution tailwind (MEDIUM, long term; management claims ~70% import dependence, unverified, B09).

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Ram Ratna Wires is a decent copper conversion business trading at a price that assumes it is a great one. ROCE of 23.55% SA leads the peer set and margins are the best of the three listed names, but this is a 5% EBITDA commodity converter whose earnings the four pillar framework says are worth about 15.4x, not the ~36x forward the market is paying. Even if everything goes right and FY29 EPS reaches roughly Rs 25 (bull), 15.4x gives Rs 389, still below today's Rs 454.40. There is no version of the arithmetic where buying here delivers 25% a year; the Hurdle Ratio fails even on the bull case.

Underneath the valuation problem sits a live question mark. In its record profit year, operating cash flow went sharply negative (Rs 95.85 Cr negative SA) because inventory more than doubled and receivables jumped 64%, with no write-downs and no management explanation, and there was no concall to ask. FTTCP could not decide whether that is the price of growth or a leak in the model, so it ruled INDETERMINATE and withheld the cash credit. Promoter governance is CONCERN, genuinely improving (professional CFO, guarantees released, zero pledge, half the board independent) but still carrying a roughly Rs 103.62 Cr contingent tax exposure from a 2023 search. The Bhiwadi copper tube ramp that the whole growth story leans on is the same event behind the inventory build and, on the Q4 numbers, is decelerating rather than ramping.

The disciplined call is to wait. Re-engagement earns a look only near Rs 134 to 153 and only once the cash reversal is explained and reversed and the copper tube ramp is visible in segment revenue. Until then this is a watch, not a buy, and the entry conjunction protects against buying the dip that arrives for the wrong reason.
