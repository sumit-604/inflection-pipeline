## 2C. Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic, shown and then corrected

Mechanical calculation as instructed: total capex under execution x historical fixed asset
turnover = implied incremental revenue.

- Total capex under execution (disclosed, current figures): Rs 750 cr (7-ACA) + Rs 150 cr
  (Cefiderocol) + Rs 90 cr (midpoint of the Rs 80-100 cr oral API expansion) = **Rs 990 cr**.
- Historical fixed asset turnover, FY25 (both figures AR p.23, audited): Revenue Rs 921.93 cr
  / Net Fixed Assets Rs 612.9 cr = **1.50x**.
- Naive implied incremental revenue: Rs 990 cr x 1.50x = **Rs 1,485 cr**, or **~161% of FY25
  revenue** (Rs 921.93 cr).

This naive figure OVERSTATES the true picture for two evidenced reasons, and should not be
read at face value:

1. Management's own guidance is that 80% of the 1,000 MT 7-ACA capacity is for IN-HOUSE USE
   (Concall_Aug_2026 line 232). That portion is a cost input, not a new sales line; applying a
   sales-based turnover ratio to it double-counts value that shows up in gross margin, not
   revenue.
2. The 1.50x ratio is drawn from the EXISTING API/FDF export asset base. A captive
   intermediate-chemical plant is not the same kind of asset and has no comparable revenue
   ratio disclosed anywhere in this corpus.

A more defensible reading applies the 1.50x turnover only to the revenue-facing slices: the
~20% third-party-sale portion of 7-ACA (notionally ~Rs 150 cr of the Rs 750 cr, using the
guided 20% split), plus the full Cefiderocol (Rs 150 cr) and oral API (Rs 90 cr) capex, i.e.
Rs 390 cr x 1.50x = **Rs 585 cr, ~63% of FY25 revenue**, spread over a multi-year ramp with no
company-stated target to check it against.

Both figures are ANALYST INFERENCE (🔍), not company-stated targets. capex_embedded_growth_pct
below carries the naive/literal figure per the instructed formula, flagged here as an
overstatement; the adjusted 63% figure is the more defensible one and should be preferred by
any downstream stage that reads this number.
