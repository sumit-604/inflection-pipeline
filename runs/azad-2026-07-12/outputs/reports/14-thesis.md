## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** EBITDA margin 36.88% FY26 (B10), well above the peer median 20.69% (B10), and above the company's own guided 33-35% band (B05), which is the clearest single mark of pricing power in the file. PAT Rs 132.16 Cr on revenue Rs 590.38 Cr is a 22.36% PAT margin (B10). Revenue CAGR FY20-26 30.04% and PAT CAGR 35.78% (B10). The moat profile is FORTRESS with 6 of 12 backward moats confirmed (B01). The clear negative: cumulative CFO/PAT FY20-26 is 0.04 and FY26 CFO is negative 123.26 Cr against positive PAT (B01, B10). Statutory ROCE is only 8.84% FY26; the operator-ruled operational ROCE, stripping idle QIP cash, CWIP and capex advances, is about 12% (FTTCP override 1). Quality is real on the income statement and unproven on the cash statement.

Note the SOM 5-year (Rs 2,232 Cr) exceeds currently-committed-capex capacity by about Rs 781 Cr, so the top of the runway needs capex phases beyond current commitments (B09).

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

AZAD is a genuinely good business trading at a price that gives you nothing. The margin is best in class at 37%, the order book is eleven times revenue, and the moat is a real qualification lock-in that takes rivals three to four years to clear. The forward transition case earns a fair exit multiple of about 21 times, which is a multiple I would happily pay for this quality. The problem is arithmetic, not judgment. You are being asked to pay 121 times today for a business whose earned exit multiple is 21 times. No plausible three-year earnings growth outruns that compression, so the base case is a 34% annual loss from here. The valuation says AVOID, and it is right.

Underneath the price, one real question is still open. FY26 operating cash flow was negative 123 crore against positive 132 crore of profit. Management, CARE, and the FTTCP call all read that as growth-induced, cash tied up in inventory and receivables while eight new factories fill up, not a business that structurally leaks cash. That read is probably correct, but it is a forecast, not a fact, and the fact arrives in August 2026 with the Q1 FY27 consolidated cash flow. If that print is negative with working-capital days still at or above 344, the growth-induced story fails, the cash multiplier falls, and the thesis is broken. If it is positive, the transition is confirmed and the case strengthens.

So the action is patience with a specific number attached. Do nothing at 2,480. Watch the August cash print. The name becomes a small starter, two to three percent, only if price falls into 295 to 368 and the cash print has not broken the thesis. If price falls into that zone because the cash print was bad, that is not the opportunity, it is the exit being confirmed.
