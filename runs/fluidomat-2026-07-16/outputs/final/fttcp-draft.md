## MY RULINGS

Every call below is made. Each carries a confidence tag and the single fact that would prove it wrong.

1. **Forward window: 3 months primary, 6 months secondary, 12 months for ROCE.** The company files quarterly results, so a 3 month print exists. `sure`. Wrong if the next filing turns out to be semi annual only.

2. **Business type: standard operating business (asset-light-leaning industrial manufacturer), standard four transitions.** B04 reads business_type manufacturing, single segment, product sales 99%. Not a lender. `sure`. Wrong if a segment reclassification shows a finance or leasing book, which nothing suggests.

3. **Workup intent: first workup.** No companies/FLUIDOMAT.md exists and run_type is full. I am testing whether a forward transition case exists at all, from a standing start. `sure`. Wrong if a prior run folder for this ticker surfaces.

4. **Sector cap row: "Cables / Industrial products" at 25x, corrected from the manifest.** The manifest says "Pharma / CDMO", which is wrong for a maker of industrial fluid couplings. B04 sets EV/EBITDA as the primary method for a debt-free cyclical capital-goods manufacturer, secondary a normalised P/E, tertiary P/B. The closest Section 1B rows are "Cables / Industrial products" 25x and "Recycling / Manufacturing" 25x. Both land at 25x, so the ceiling is robust at 25x whichever you pick. Phase 3 stage 11 must inherit 25x, not the pharma cap. `fairly sure`. Wrong if you judge a niche 40% margin engineered product deserves a higher engineered-products ceiling, but 25x is the framework's designated industrial-products row and I will not invent one above the table.

5. **Revenue forward verdict: STAGNANT (score 0).** Three year revenue growth was real (roughly 17% a year FY23 to FY26) but FY26 was flat at plus 0.4% and the quarters are lumpy. The Rs 35 Cr capacity doubling is documented but completes around FY28-29 and adds nothing in the 3 to 6 month window. `fairly sure`. Wrong if Q1 FY27 revenue prints another quarter near the Rs 29 Cr Q4 FY26 level, which would say the topline re-accelerated rather than stalled.

6. **Margin forward verdict: STAGNANT (score 0), backward SUSTAINED at a premium.** EBITDA margin ran 31% in FY24, 41% in FY25, about 35% in FY26. The level is roughly double the peer median, but it compressed about 560 bps in FY26 and management did not quantify why. There is no documented catalyst to re-expand it and it already sits above the peer ceiling. `fairly sure`. Wrong if two consecutive FY27 quarters hold EBITDA margin at or above 38 to 40%.

7. **Cash conversion forward verdict: DECLINING (score minus 1), catalyst NONE FOUND.** This is the binding call. CFO to PAT fell to 0.64x in FY25 and about 0.54x in FY26, debtor days stretched from about 90 to about 149 over two years, receivables rose 32% while revenue was flat, and the company books zero provision against Rs 143 lakh of receivables aged beyond two years. No factoring facility, no debtor-day target, no payment-term change, and no management commentary on any of it. `sure` on the deterioration and the absence of a catalyst. Wrong if a documented working-capital programme (a sanctioned factoring line, a stated and hit debtor-day target) surfaces in the next filing.

8. **Structural versus growth-induced cash: INDETERMINATE, leaning structural.** The test is: if growth stopped tomorrow, would working capital release. Growth already stopped in FY26 (flat revenue) and working capital did not release, it expanded, which argues structural. The competing benign reading is a Q4 timing bulge, because Q4 FY26 revenue was Rs 29 Cr (about 40% of the year) and those receivables may collect in Q1 FY27. I cannot separate the two without the FY26 receivables ageing schedule and a credit-rating rationale, neither of which is on file. Per the rule, INDETERMINATE does not resolve to a clean pass: it caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS and names the missing evidence. `fairly sure` it is indeterminate; `genuinely uncertain` which way it resolves. Both readings are live; the draft scores cash as DECLINING regardless, because even the benign reading has no documented catalyst. The Q1 FY27 receivables print resolves it.

9. **ROCE backward verdict: SUSTAINED at a premium (score forward STAGNANT, 0).** Reported ROCE ran about 29% FY24, 37% FY25, 28% FY26. The two year endpoints (FY24 to FY26) sit within a couple of points at a premium level, with FY25 a margin-driven peak. It is not TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED (28% is high, not depressed), so the normalized-ROCE anchor (Amendment 4.5) does not apply. Forward it is STAGNANT: a fresh Rs 35 Cr capex cycle and a growing idle-cash pile both pull the denominator up faster than profit over the next 12 months. `fairly sure`. Wrong if reported ROCE climbs back above 33% within a year without the capital base falling.

10. **A large part of capital employed is idle. Reported ROCE understates operating ROCE.** Of the Rs 96 Cr capital employed, roughly Rs 50 Cr is fixed deposits, investments and cash earning treasury yield, not operating. Operating ROCE stripped of surplus cash is closer to 45 to 50%. `fairly sure`. This is the AMAGI and MapMyIndia pattern and it is a stage 11 decision, not mine to finalise, but I am flagging it so Pillar 1 does not price a 28% blended number as the operating return. Wrong if the FDs turn out to be pledged or operationally required working cash rather than surplus.

11. **Composite score minus 1 out of 8. Disposition: forward-transition AVOID at the current price, functionally DEEP WATCH because re-engagement is one quarter away.** The Kernex cap is independently active (cash DECLINING with catalyst NONE caps at DEEP WATCH), and the raw score already sits in the AVOID band, so nothing rescues it upward. `fairly sure`. I am not overriding the mechanical score; there is no override-worthy catalyst (no binding acquisition, no regulatory award). Wrong if the Q1 FY27 cash print turns clean, which would lift the cash transition and the whole composite in a single step.

12. **Tier A, 25% hurdle.** Institutions hold about 0.8% (FII 0.58%, DII 0.22%), below the 3% undiscovered-alpha threshold, and the cash flag would bar Tier B regardless. `sure`. Wrong if a shareholding filing shows combined FII plus DII at or above 3%.

## The catalyst story in plain words

Only one transition has a real documented catalyst, and it is years out. The Rs 35 Cr modernisation and capacity doubling is board-approved and shows up in rising work-in-progress, so it is documented, not talk. But only about 9% of it was spent by FY26 close, the plan's own completion date conflicts by more than a year inside a single presentation (end 2027 on one slide, FY26 to FY29 on another), and it does not add revenue or lift ROCE inside the 3 to 12 month window this protocol scores. It is a FY28-29 story.

On margins there is no catalyst to re-expand a number that already sits at twice the peer level and just compressed. On revenue the near-term catalyst is absent: the order-book figure went dark, exports are declining, and the government thermal and steel demand story is a sector tailwind that ELECON's own calls show converting to revenue slower than guided, not a company-specific share gain. On cash conversion the catalyst search returns NONE FOUND across every category.

| Transition | Best catalyst | Evidence | Confirms it | Kills it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Rs 35 Cr capacity doubling | 📄 board approval + CWIP Rs 4.2 Cr | order book reappears and grows; capacity commissions on time | KPI stays dark; completion date keeps slipping |
| Margin | none near-term | 🎙️ mix and premiumisation talk only | two FY27 quarters hold 38-40% EBITDA | margin stays in the 30-35% band |
| Cash conversion | NONE FOUND | no facility, no target, no commentary | a factoring line or a hit debtor-day target is filed | receivables and ageing grow again with zero ECL |
| ROCE | capacity earning (FY28-29) | 📄 capex, but earns with a lag | ROCE climbs above 33% as capacity ramps | idle cash and capex keep the base bloated |

Step 2E intent-and-action check: management has vision on capacity and modernisation, and real documented action on the capex, but that action is early and distant. On the binding transition, cash, management has neither vision nor action. So the cross-check confirms the conservative read and the Kernex cap stands; it does not loosen anything.

## THE VERDICT

The composite forward score is minus 1 out of 8. Not one of the four wealth-creating transitions is firing forward, and the most important one, cash conversion, is going backward with no catalyst, which trips the Kernex cap on its own. This is a high-quality, debt-free, high-return niche business whose forward transitions have all stalled at once, so on the forward evidence it is an AVOID at today's price, functionally a DEEP WATCH because the single thing the whole call turns on is one quarter away. That one thing is the Q1 FY27 receivables and cash-flow print: if receivables collect back toward Rs 22 Cr and cash conversion clears 0.70x, the cash transition flips and the score lifts in a single step; if receivables hold above Rs 30 Cr with the aged buckets growing and still zero provision, the cash flag hardens from indeterminate to structural and the AVOID deepens.
