## Contradicted claims from the peer stage

Formal contradictions: none. The peer stage verified one claim, partly verified five and could not verify two. These peer readings cut against company claims and are priority monitoring items:

1. Price and stock gain in margin. Netweb denies any memory price gain in its margin (Netweb Q1 FY27 call p.8, p.13). Orient's rebound from 3.19% to 7.57% EBITDA came from fixed price rate contracts ending 31 Mar 2026 (Orient Q3 FY26 p.4 to 5; Q1 FY27 p.4, p.6). Rashi allows a stock gain "to some extent" and refused to size it (Rashi Q3 FY26 p.12, p.16).
2. AI mix and margin. Netweb says strategic AI orders carry 150 to 200 basis points lower PBT margin than its base (Netweb Q2 FY26 p.7; Q3 FY26 p.9).
3. GPU allocation. Netweb holds the regional NVIDIA OEM tier (Netweb Nov 2025 call p.8). Esconet's own filing describes Elite as the top tier of the Solution Provider category, with no allocation claim and no immediate material financial impact (NSE Reg 30, 29 Apr 2026). The MD says Esconet buys "from the NVIDIA channel" (AUG26 p.16). Verifier B found the peer stage's verified item Q7 overstated support for Esconet.
4. Order book and cash flow disclosure. Netweb and Orient disclose order books (Orient Q1 FY27 p.5: ₹375.43 Cr). Netweb and Rashi disclose receivable days, inventory days and operating cash flow. Esconet says it has no order book model (AUG26 p.15).
5. Cloud build. Netweb refuses cloud services (Netweb Q2 FY26 p.16). Orient's SOC build shows capex first and moving revenue dates (Orient Q1 FY26 p.6 to 7; Q2 FY26 p.5, p.9). Esconet is the lone peer expanding into asset heavy cloud.
6. Unverifiable: market share gain against Cisco, HP, Lenovo and Dell; MeitY empanelment timing and sovereign cloud ramp.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Read the Q2 FY27 standalone gross margin in the H1 FY27 results on NSE, due by mid November 2026. Q1 printed 29.2% and FY26 13.7%. Holding 20% or more after stock is replaced at current cost supports the mix reading. This tests the margin engine.
2. Check whether the same results or the call give HexaData revenue and gross margin by line. Without it, the mix reading stays untestable for another quarter.
3. Track consolidated EBITDA excluding other income for two straight quarters at 5% or more (Q1 7.03%, FY26 2.49%). CRISIL's upgrade trigger is an operating margin above 6%, and its downgrade trigger is below 3% to 4% (CRISIL rationale 2 Jul 2026, lines 36 and 39). This tests whether the step lasts.
4. Read the first H1 cash flow statement. CFO excluding FDR movements must be positive, with CFO/PAT above 0.7 and no half year inventory build above ₹5 Cr. This resolves the cash flag.
5. Read receivables older than six months in the H1 balance sheet or the FY27 AR Note 2.13.1. FY26 was 19.9% with no provision. Below 10% clears the collection worry; a first provision or a share above 20% confirms it.
6. Find where the ₹1.84 Cr warrant forfeiture was booked, in the H1 FY27 notes. If it went through other income, it is about 20% of Q1 standalone pre tax profit and inflates the Q1 margin.
7. Watch NSE Reg 30 filings for any demand, SEBI referral or named allottee tie from the income tax summons, and for any promoter sale disclosure. Read the September 2026 shareholding pattern against the 59.15% derived holding. Any of these can move the promoter verdict to CONCERN.
8. Split Q2 revenue into standalone, Singapore and the rest. Standalone above ₹74 Cr a quarter confirms the HexaData and government order trigger; staying near ₹61 Cr does not. Singapore was 45.8% of Q1 at a 0.56% net margin, so consolidated growth alone says little.
