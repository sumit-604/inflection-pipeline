# Rail components growth outruns its cash conversion

Airfloa Rail Technology makes rail coach interiors and components, AC ducting, lighting, seating, doors and blinds, plus aeronautical and defence structures, and sells to Indian Railways, ICF and other government and PSU buyers on a tender basis. FY26 revenue was Rs 320 Cr, PAT Rs 39 Cr at a 12.2% margin, and the shares trade at Rs 315 against the Rs 140 IPO price from the September 2025 BSE SME listing. ICF alone was 54% of FY25 revenue and the top five customers were 82%.

Revenue grew 66% in FY26 and has compounded about 50% a year across FY23 to FY26. The company is moving up the value chain toward turnkey interiors and a defence and aerospace push, carrying a Rs 469 Cr order book and a Rs 25 Cr defence electronics JV (BBBS) in the pipeline. ROCE has held near 25% on the company reported restated basis, so growth and returns are real.

Cash tells the other half. Standalone operating cash flow was minus Rs 57 Cr in FY26, worse than minus Rs 4 Cr in FY25, even as PAT grew 52%, because trade receivables absorbed Rs 86 Cr in a single year, more than the entire Rs 59 Cr the IPO earmarked for working capital. The operator ruled the negative cash growth induced rather than structural, applied a 0.80x cash multiplier, and attached a falsifier: over six month receivables sit at 23.4% with zero provisioning, and the call reverts to structural if that ageing keeps rising or H1 FY27 cash stays negative.

Gate 0 scored the company AVERAGE at 76 of 160, with two years of history, the cash sub block at 0 of 20, and cumulative operating cash flow to PAT at minus 0.93 times. Management delivered FY26 revenue above its Rs 300 Cr promise but missed the harder items. The Rs 1,000 Cr order book target for March 2026 came in at Rs 469 Cr, FY27 guidance was cut from Rs 550 Cr to Rs 500 Cr before the year began, the 14 acre facility promised live by December 2026 has not started construction, and defence is about 6% of the order book against a stated 50:50 ambition. Credibility grades C.

Peers do not back the margin excuse. FRONTSP expanded EBITDA margin 533bps to 26.80% in the same steel price window while Airfloa compressed about 490bps to 20.1%, which points at company specific procurement rather than a sector shock. TEXRAIL's own six year gap between Rs 1,625 Cr of EBITDA and Rs 50 Cr of operating cash flow shows this cash pattern can persist for years once it sets in.

The market runway is real but the near term ceiling is not. Addressable market is about Rs 13,150 Cr with a STRONG runway class, yet the three year sales opportunity of Rs 568 Cr already exceeds the roughly Rs 450 Cr capacity ceiling by Rs 118 Cr, and management's Rs 16.7 lakh crore headline is whole of sector spend inflated about 152 times over the addressable figure.

Governance sits at CAUTION. Both Managing Directors carry RoC penalty orders under appeal, Rs 38 Cr of company debt is personally guaranteed against family land with one Rs 8.73 Cr parcel unregistered, and the office and factory are rented from the promoters. Against that, pledge is zero, no promoter has sold, and the independent directors are credentialed, including an ex Integral Coach Factory General Manager who led Vande Bharat.

Valuation closes the case. The four pillar destination PE is 16.0x on the additive track and 13.1x on the governing RRM track, the Hurdle STOPs on both, and the probability weighted three year return is 2.6%. Fair entry opens only at Rs 155 to Rs 193, so at Rs 315 the decision is AVOID on valuation. The devil's advocate stress test survives on every count that matters, and the AVOID holds even when the growth premium is zeroed to raw 16x.
