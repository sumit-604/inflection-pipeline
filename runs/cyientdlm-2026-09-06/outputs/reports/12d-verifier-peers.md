## PART 2: ANCHOR-INTEGRITY FINDINGS

Every quote and figure I checked (>45 discrete citations across the 12 transcripts) exists,
verbatim or near-verbatim, in the peer's transcript, attributed to the correct call and
correct quarter. I found **zero fabricated or invented figures/quotes** and **zero
misattributions to the wrong peer or the wrong call**. What I found instead is a recurring,
narrow **page-citation imprecision**, concentrated in the KAYNES and SYRMA transcripts (which
carry a filing cover page AVALON's do not), typically off by one page and in one case off by
three:

1. KAYNES Q4/FY26: the "122 days for FY26" consolidated NWC figure is cited as p.2 in the Q7
   answer; it actually sits on p.4, in the same paragraph as the (correctly cited) 83→53 day
   core-EMS figure. MINOR.
2. KAYNES Q1 FY27: the "6 to 8 months even for order booking" phrase is folded into a citation
   range (p.14-15) alongside the "30%-35%" price-rise and "gone back to COVID times" quotes;
   the order-booking phrase in fact appears earlier, on p.5, in a different part of the call
   (Ramesh Kunhikannan's PCB remarks vs. the later general-components discussion). The content
   is genuine and belongs to the same call, but the merged citation misdirects a page-level
   check. MINOR.
3. KAYNES Q1 FY27: receivables "to INR1,925 crores" cited p.14, actual p.13. MINOR.
4. SYRMA Q2 FY26: the "5%, 6% of my total revenue" tariff quote and "the last ball is yet to
   be born" quote are both cited p.6; both are actually on printed p.7. MINOR (two instances,
   same page-off, same paragraph).
5. SYRMA Q3 FY26: "gestation period is pretty long and the orders could also be lumpy" cited
   p.12, actual p.11. MINOR.
6. SYRMA Q4/FY26: FY26 revenue +27% cited p.5 (actual p.4); OCF INR290cr and 63/58-day NWC
   cited p.4 (actual p.5) — these two slips run in opposite directions within the same
   transcript, indicating approximate rather than systematic mis-paging. MINOR.
7. SYRMA Q4/FY26: the "$1 billion company... Jabil... $20 billion, $25 billion" and "global
   addressable market... north of $600 million" quotes cited p.14, actual p.13. MINOR.
8. SYRMA Q4/FY26: the L&T INR50bn EMS capex competitive-threat flag (Part 2D) cited p.15,
   actual p.12 — the largest single gap found (3 pages). MINOR but the largest of the set.
9. SYRMA Q1 FY27: NWC-to-71-days and net-cash-INR122cr both cited p.5, actual p.6. MINOR.

None of these nine instances is a MISMATCH in the Verifier-A sense (the number itself is
