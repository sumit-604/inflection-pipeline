### Items B05 also surfaced (context — see Part 2 for scoring)

  Rs 5,559 lakhs = profit on sale of land, booked in the Dec-2025 quarter
  [Q4 FY26 note (c), line 404; standalone P&L Dec-25 column, line 184]. Standalone
  pre-exceptional FY26 result is a **loss of Rs 1,603 lakhs** [line 183];
  consolidated pre-exceptional PBT is only **Rs 150 lakhs** [line 623].
- **I2** Q4 FY26 consolidated pre-exceptional PBT deteriorated YoY: **Rs -583
  lakhs vs Rs +240 lakhs** in Q4 FY25 [line 623], as Q4 finance cost rose to
  Rs 1,662 lakhs (vs 1,162) and depreciation to Rs 1,038 lakhs (vs 771) [line 622].
- **I3** QIP up to Rs 250 cr (approved Jan-2025) not executed; "management still
  evaluating market conditions" [AR lines 203-212], superseded by a Rs 93.03 cr
  rights issue [Q4 note (e), line 408]. No reconciliation of the instrument change.
- **I4** MPS: public float raised 10.00% -> 10.13% [AR line 199], mandatory 25%
  [AR line 196]; rights issue stated as directed at 25% MPS [Deck p.7]; post-issue
  25% achievement not confirmed in any collected filing.
- **I5** CFO K. Rajavel resigned 9-Jul-2026 to subsidiary SMART, no parent
  succession plan disclosed [Digest s.4].
- **I6** Bengaluru MMLP "upcoming," no commissioning date/capex at any point
  [Deck p.14].
- **I7** Mining order book wholly PSU/coal (SECL, Coal India OCPs); no
  counterparty diversification [Deck p.11-12].
- **I8** Related-party cross-collateralisation: parent guarantees + land mortgages
  securing subsidiary/fellow-subsidiary bank lines [Digest s.3] (digest-only anchor).
- **I9** Promotional headline framing (74% revenue / 264% EBITDA / "turnaround")
  crowds out the operating picture [Deck p.6-8, p.29-31].

### Items B05 did NOT surface (new independent finds)
- **I10 [MAJOR]** The headline deleveraging (D/E 4.1x -> 1.6x) is computed on
  **total** consolidated equity of Rs 26,957 lakhs, of which **non-controlling
  interest is Rs 13,828 lakhs — larger than the Rs 13,129 lakhs attributable to
  the parent's own owners** [Q4 FY26 consolidated B/S, lines 673-675]. On
  owners' equity alone, total borrowings of Rs 43,344 lakhs [lines 679, 687] imply
  ~3.3x, not 1.6x. The flattering denominator is not disclosed against the D/E claim.
- **I11 [MAJOR]** The audited results report **a single business segment**
  ("integrated logistics services… single business segment per Ind AS 108") with
  no segment breakdown [Q4 FY26 note (b), lines 401-403 / 756-758]. The entire
  43% / 36% / 21% Mining / Terminals / Warehousing revenue mix that the thesis
  rests on exists **only in the unaudited investor deck** [Deck p.22], with no
  audited segment disclosure to check it against.
- **I12 [MINOR]** Consolidated operating cash flow of Rs 5,229 lakhs is struck
  **before interest** — finance cost paid of Rs 4,705 lakhs sits in financing
  activities [Q4 FY26 consol cash flow, lines 724, 732]. The deck's "Net Cash from
  Operating Activities Rs 523 mn" headline [Deck p.33] is therefore near-zero after
  servicing interest; this is not flagged.
- **I13 [MAJOR]** Promoter **forwent its entire rights entitlement** and did not
  subscribe or renounce [Q3 FY26 outcome letter, lines 40-42]. The full Rs 93 cr
  was funded by public shareholders; the promoter injected no fresh equity into a
  still-levered turnaround. (Mechanically consistent with raising public float, but
  a governance/skin-in-the-game signal not raised anywhere.)
- **I14 [MAJOR]** The company **emerged from insolvency only ~3 years ago**
  (went into insolvency March 2021, asset write-downs FY21-23, acquired by Pristine
  Jan-2023) [Deck p.7]. The "return to profit" reads very differently against an
  unproven ~3-year-old turnaround; B05 does not surface this history at all.
- **I15 [MINOR]** Consolidated **other income of Rs 3,689 lakhs exceeds the entire
  pre-exceptional consolidated PBT of Rs 150 lakhs** [lines 619, 623]; the barely
  positive pre-exceptional result is itself dependent on non-operating income, a
  sharper point than the exceptional-gain framing alone.

Minor/low, noted not scored: consolidated trade receivables +33% YoY to Rs 9,595
lakhs [line 660] against PSU counterparties (collection risk undiscussed); statutory
auditor is a small firm (SRSV & Associates) while EY only prepared the deck [Deck p.38].
