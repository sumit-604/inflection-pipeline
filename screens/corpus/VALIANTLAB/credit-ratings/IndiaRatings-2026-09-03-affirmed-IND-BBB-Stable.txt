India Ratings Affirms Valiant Laboratories's Bank Loan Facilities at 'IND BBB'/Stable/'IND A2'
Sep 03, 2026 | Valiant Laboratories Limited | Pharmaceuticals

Details of Instruments
Bank loan facilities | RBI | Size INR 560 million | IND BBB/Stable/IND A2 | Affirmed

Analytical Approach
Ind-Ra has taken a fully consolidated view of VLL and its wholly owned subsidiary, Valiant Advanced Sciences Private Limited (VASPL), to arrive at the ratings, on account of the similar line of business and a common management. Furthermore, the ratings benefit from the availability of operational and financial flexibility from the company's promoters and group companies, namely Aarti Industries Limited (AIL; debt rated; 'IND AA'/Negative), and Valiant Organics Limited (VOL), which are part of the broader Aarti Group.

Detailed Rationale of the Rating Action
The affirmation reflects VLL's improving operating performance and credit metrics in FY26 and 1QFY27, driven by higher capacity utilisation, improved sales volumes, and stable product realisations, along with backward integration initiatives through the production of acetic anhydride, which began in November 2025, at VASPL. Ind-Ra notes that while 1QFY27 benefitted from higher historical inventory levels and improving spot prices, the same are unlikely to sustain in the near-to-medium term.

VLL is among the largest manufacturers of paracetamol in India, and an associate of Valiant Organics Limited (VOL). It also has a common shareholding in AIL, and is a part of Aarti Group. The company benefits from Aarti Group's established presence in the chemical industry and has significant share of raw material purchases and sales of acetic anhydride to group companies.

Key Rating Drivers
Strengths: Presence across key paracetamol value chain with backward integration within group entities; Improved financial performance in FY26, although strong 1QFY27 performance unlikely to sustain in near term; Strong group support; Improved credit metrics in FY26, likely to sustain over near-to-medium term.
Weaknesses: Small scale of operations; product concentration; Volatility in paracetamol API and raw material prices.

Presence Across Key Paracetamol Value Chain with Backward Integration within Group Entities: VLL manufactures paracetamol active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) from para-aminophenol (PAP) and acetic anhydride. According to the management, it is among the top five manufacturers of paracetamol in India, based on quantity produced, accounting for approximately 15% share of India's manufacturing capacity. The company majorly procures PAP from its group company, VOL, and has successfully completed the backward integration of its plant for manufacturing acetic anhydride under VASPL. VLL's key raw material, PAP, which accounted for more than 80% of the raw material costs in FY26, is primarily supplied by VOL, which is an indirect shareholder (33%) in VLL. In addition to meeting its internal requirements, the company supplies acetic anhydride to group entities under annual supply agreements, providing an additional source of revenue and improving the capacity utilisation of the newly commissioned plant.

Improved Financial Performance in FY26; Although Strong 1QFY27 Performance Unlikely to Sustain in Near Term: On a consolidated basis, the revenue increased 78% yoy to INR2,375 million in FY26, driven by higher capacity utilisation, improved sales volumes, and stable product realisations. The capacity utilisation of the paracetamol manufacturing plant improved significantly to 85% in FY26 (FY25: 55%). To support future growth, the company expanded its installed production capacity by 20% in FY26. In November 2025, the company commissioned its backward-integrated acetic anhydride plant. The plant's average utilisation was 35% from November 2025 to June 2026.

The positive momentum continued in 1QFY27, with revenue increasing 146% yoy to INR1,153 million. This strong performance was primarily driven by a significant improvement in product price realisations and the availability of higher volumes of low-cost inventory, which enhanced margins amid favourable market conditions. However, Ind-Ra notes that the exceptional performance is unlikely to be sustained in the near term, given the depletion of low-cost inventory and the historically volatile nature of finished product prices. As inventory costs normalise and market conditions fluctuate, profitability and revenue growth may moderate from the elevated levels witnessed in 1QFY27.

Strong Group Support: VLL has common shareholding in AIL. It is also an associate company of VOL with 80% of its key raw material, PAP, requirements emanating from its group entity. Of the promoter shareholding of 74.9%, the Vora family holds 18.7%, Mr. Paresh Shah holds 9.4%, while the remaining is held by Dhanvallabh Ventures LLP (whose partners are one of the shareholders in AIL). In the past, promoter entities have infused funds towards capex and other initiatives.

Improved Credit Metrics in FY26: In FY26, VLL's gross interest coverage improved to 2.0x (FY25: negative 26.7x), while net leverage was 2.0x, supported by improved EBITDA and a decline in total debt. During FY26, the company raised around INR815 million through a rights issue of equity shares. These proceeds were utilised partly to repay the entire outstanding unsecured loans of INR594 million extended by the promoters. Ind-Ra expects net leverage to remain below 2.0x over the near-to-medium term.

Small Scale of Operations; Product Concentration: Consolidated revenue of INR2,375 million in FY26 (FY25: INR1,334 million) and EBITDA of INR64 million (negative INR47 million). Significant historical fluctuations in revenue and EBITDA, primarily due to COVID-19-induced stocking by distributors and pharmaceutical companies, which increased revenue during FY21-FY22 and caused fluctuations in paracetamol prices. VLL derives all its revenue from the sale of a single product (paracetamol). Ind-Ra expects revenue to improve in FY27, driven by improved capacity utilisation and revenue from commercialisation of the acetic anhydride facility.

Volatility in Paracetamol API and Raw Materials Prices: While China has large manufacturing facilities for PAP, India is the largest supplier of paracetamol API globally. Volatility in prices over the last two-to-three years, due to supply disruptions from China for PAP supply and COVID-19-related uncertainties. Pricing has reverted to the long-term median range of INR200-300/kilogram in 4QFY26.

Liquidity: Adequate. Cash balances and liquid investments INR332 million at FYE26 (FYE25: INR760 million). Fund-based limits unutilised during the 12 months ended June 2026. Scheduled debt repayments of INR168.5 million in FY27 and INR168.5 million in FY28. Net working capital cycle improved to 69 days in FY26 (FY25: 97 days), receivable period 144 days (197 days). Ind-Ra estimates capex of INR250 million in FY27, largely toward improvement initiatives at the paracetamol facility and the acetic anhydride plant. Cash flow from operations negative at INR104 million in FY26 (FY25: negative INR13.1 million), primarily due to the low EBITDA of INR64 million.

Rating Sensitivities
Positive: A substantial increase in the scale of operations, a sustained improvement in EBITDA margins, and an improvement in liquidity and credit metrics, along with net leverage falling below 3.0x, all on a sustained basis.
Negative: deterioration in the scale of operations and/or lower-than-expected EBITDA margins; any further elongation of the working capital cycle with net leverage increasing above 4.0x; a weakening of the operational and financial flexibility available from the promoter and group companies; a delay in the ramping-up of operations at VASPL.
