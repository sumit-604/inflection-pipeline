   Agastya Dave: "the initiative that you have announced that you'd be

   calibration finding in Part D: the "broken" conclusion rests on absence in
   an input set the pipeline itself labels a "known collector defect," and
   "quarterly updates" is not necessarily "quarterly concalls."

2. **Aerospace AS9100D slippage, unacknowledged.** [M25 p.9, Saksham: "...you
   would see a lot of these expressions of interest convert into orders when
   we have an update for you next quarter"] vs [M26 p.7: "our efforts on
   aerospace are right now at the last leg... within what I feel two months
   our certification will come"]. ~12 months later, still uncertified, with no
   reference to the earlier near-term timeline. Weight: MEDIUM.

3. **Free cash flow negative, no committed timeline.** [M26 p.18, Samarth
   Gupta asks when FCF turns positive; Sanjeev Verma: "tough to say, when our
   free cash flow will be positive, but the cash flow which whichever will be
   generated is of course going back into the business"]. Continuous capex
   funded by a mix of equity (Rs.43.3cr preferential issue) and debt. Weight:
   MEDIUM.

4. **Near-term margin guide reset lower, externally explained.** [M25 p.10,
   Sanjeev: "our target of at least expanding our EBITDA margin by 4%, 5% over
   the next three, four years"] vs [M26 p.4, Saksham: "what I hope is that we
   can still grow our margins by 1% this year"], blamed on tariffs, the Iran
   war and shipping costs. Weight: MEDIUM — but note ~1%/yr is arithmetically
   consistent with 4-5pp over 3-4 years, so this reads closer to
   reaffirmation-at-the-low-end than an outright "cut" (see Part D).

5. **Non-auto order book declined nominally while diversification target
   slips.** Non-auto order book Rs.250cr [M25 p.2] to Rs.230cr [M26 p.2],
   even as total grew Rs.700cr+ to Rs.1,200cr+ (automotive Rs.460cr to
   Rs.980cr). Meanwhile M26 opening reports non-auto revenue +150% YoY, yet
   the FY25 target of auto down to ~65% / non-auto ~35% within 2-3 years
   [M25 p.10] is behind pace after year one (management guides ~80-85% auto
   for FY26, [M25 p.17 Sanjeev, restated tone in M26]). Weight: MEDIUM.

6. **Rising single-customer concentration, framed only positively.** [M26
   p.17, Saksham: "Tata AutoComp was our 10th largest customer one year back
   and this year... this month it was our second largest"], tied to indirect
   Tesla demand ("hand-in-glove with Tesla"). No risk framing from management
   or the questioning analyst. Weight: LOW-MEDIUM.

7. **Vague answer on competitive differentiation.** [M26 p.14, Vardhman
   Lunia asks what differentiates OBSC's capability; Saksham: "there is no...
   magic pill... everything comes down to first principles and I don't know
   how our peers are different from us"]. Weight: LOW.

8. **"Surpassed on both" claim not cleanly reconcilable.** [M26 p.2: "we had
   a guidance of 40% revenue growth and 2% gross margin improvement. We have
   surpassed on both"]. Revenue (54%) clearly beat; but the reported figure
   is a 19.5% EBITDA margin, and no gross-margin bridge is shown against the
   FY25 base ([M25 p.10, Deepak Poddar: "from 17.8% without other income"]).
   Weight: LOW.

9. **Aggressive near-term revenue claim on an unproven program (over-
   promotion).** [M26 p.10, Soumil Jain: "Humanoids is expected to become a
   very large part of your revenues this year itself?" Saksham: "I hope so,
   yes. This year itself, yes"], on a program still described as trial-phase
   (>4,000 parts supplied, prototyping/first-batch validation only). Projecting
   an unvalidated trial program to be "a very large part of revenue this year"
   is a promotional over-reach worth flagging in its own right. Weight:
   MEDIUM (thesis-relevant near-term over-promise).

10. **Quick return to the equity market.** A Rs.43.3cr preferential issue in
    FY26 [M26 p.3] came within ~18 months of the Oct-2024 IPO and while
    Rs.20cr of IPO proceeds still sat unutilized as of [M25 p.3]. The IPO
    Rs.20cr deployment plan is never revisited in M26. A mild capital-
    allocation / dilution signal. Weight: LOW.

11. **Peer contradiction — margin guidance discipline (cross-read).** OBSC
    lowers its FY27 margin-improvement guide to ~1% citing tariffs/Iran-war/
    shipping, yet peers naming the identical cost driver in the same quarter
    reaffirm unchanged guidance: Talbros ("We will be able to maintain the
    margin," and a 17-18% EBITDA target, TALBROAUTO May-2026 lines 251, 501-
    502); Precision Camshafts frames the Iran-war RM inflation as "short-term"
    (PRECAM Jun-2026 lines 288-289). Weight: MEDIUM. (Independently spot-
    verified in the peer .txt; corroborates B06's Q5 verdict.)

## PART D — CALIBRATION FINDINGS (consolidated, standard severity)

- **MAJOR — B05's top-ranked red flag (quarterly-disclosure "broken promise,"
  HIGH) overstates its evidentiary basis.** It states as fact that "only one
  further call [was] held in 12 months" and drives the Accountability sub-
  grade (2/5) and the 4C "communication cadence: Weak" verdict, yet the
  evidence is the absence of Q1-Q3 FY26 transcripts in an input set B05's own
  YAML labels a "known collector defect," compounded by conflating "quarterly
  updates/disclosure" with "quarterly concalls." The underlying promise is
  real and well-anchored; the "broke it, unacknowledged" conclusion is
  over-confident for a HIGH flag. B05 does hedge this in 2E and its input-gap
  notice — which is why this is MAJOR-calibration, not a fabrication — but the
  flag as written in the red_flags list carries no such hedge.

- **MINOR — humanoid near-term revenue over-promise under-weighted.** The
  "very large part of revenue this year itself" claim on a trial-phase program
  [M26 p.10] belongs on the red-flag list as an over-promotion item; B05
  captured the over-promotion tendency (4/5) and the aspirational trigger
  status but did not elevate this specific claim.

- **MINOR — margin "quietly cut" characterization slightly overstated.** ~1%/yr
  FY27 is within the 4-5pp/3-4yr target envelope; management neither restated
  nor abandoned the medium-term ambition. B06's Q5 "guidance cut" premise
  inherits the same imprecision (OBSC still guides margin up, just modestly).

- **MINOR — quick return to equity not flagged.** Rs.43.3cr preferential issue
  within ~18 months of IPO while Rs.20cr IPO proceeds sat unutilized is an
  un-surfaced capital-allocation/dilution signal.

No CRITICAL items. No repeated 2+-quarter evasion exists to find (only two
calls, ~12 months apart), so the CRITICAL-on-repeated-evasion trigger cannot
arise from this data set.

### Credibility-grade concurrence

order-book growth), while the real drag is high over-promotion (Tesla/humanoid
