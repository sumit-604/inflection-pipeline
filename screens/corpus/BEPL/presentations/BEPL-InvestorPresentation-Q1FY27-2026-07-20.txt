About Us.
"Bhansali Engineering Polymers Limited is India's leading manufacturer of Acrylonitrile
Butadiene Styrene (ABS) and Styrenic Resins, with a strong legacy since 1984.
Established as a pioneer in international-quality ABS, with a consistent focus on
operational efficiency and cost leadership, among the lowest-cost ABS producers in India,
with quality standards at par with global benchmarks.
Operates two manufacturing facilities at Abu Road (Rajasthan) and Satnoor (Madhya
Pradesh), supported by an in-house R&D centre at Abu Road.
Strategic joint venture with Nippon A&L Inc., Japan, strengthening technology, expanding
product portfolio, and reinforcing BEPL's position as a key indigenous player.
Diversified portfolio of high-performance engineering polymers catering to automobiles,
consumer durables, electronics, healthcare, and kitchenware."
Key statistics: 2 manufacturing facilities; 75,000 MTPA installed capacity; 80+ variants
of ABS and specialty polymers; 1 R&D centre; 404+ employees; ~Rs 645+ cr cumulative
dividend payout (cumulative dividends distributed since FY 2021-22).
Mission includes: "To be the lowest-cost ABS producer in India with quality standards at
par with global benchmarks."

===== PAGE 10 =====
Product Portfolio. ABS (terpolymer of acrylonitrile, butadiene, styrene): high impact
resistance, good tensile strength, toughness at low temperature, moderate heat resistance
-80C to 100C. Applications: refrigerator liners, automotive interior and exterior parts,
electronic device housings, consumer product casings, pipe and fittings.
ASA (acrylonitrile styrene acrylate): weather-resistant alternative to ABS, UV resistance
from the acrylate rubber. Applications: outdoor furniture, automotive exterior trim,
building and construction, garden equipment, recreational vehicle components.

===== PAGE 11 =====
Product Portfolio continued. PC-ABS (polycarbonate/ABS alloy): high impact strength, high
heat resistance, dimensional stability, flame retardancy. Applications: electrical
enclosures, automotive interiors, household appliances, medical devices.
Specialties: "tailor-made polymer compounds and blends that are specifically designed to
meet unique application and performance requirements... custom-engineered solutions
rather than standard resins". Base polymers ABS, ASA and PC-ABS.

===== PAGE 12 =====
Backward Integration of ABS. Key raw material manufactured in-house:
SAN (styrene acrylonitrile) copolymer: styrene gives transparency, rigidity,
processability; acrylonitrile gives chemical resistance, strength, heat resistance.
HRG (high rubber graft): styrenic or acrylonitrile monomers grafted onto a polybutadiene
rubber backbone; gives impact resistance, stiffness and toughness, thermal and
dimensional stability.
ABS applications: automotive, electronics, home appliances, healthcare, packaging,
electrical.

===== PAGE 13 =====
Production Process Flow of ABS. Inputs SAN pellet, HRG powder, pigments -> high speed
mixer -> powder storage -> extruder -> die head -> cooling bath -> air knife ->
pelletizer -> ABS pellet bin -> ABS pellet storage. Water drain quantity 0.5 m3 per day.

===== PAGE 14 =====
Applications. Major appliances (refrigerators, ACs, washing machines) under the Abstron
brand, grades such as SE-325 for extrusion. Consumer electronics: laptops and cameras,
anti-scratch properties and colour options. Kitchen appliances: mixers, juicers,
grinders, grades such as TIM200. Electrical and electronics: flame retardant grades with
UL certification for switches, CCTV cameras, security systems. Miscellaneous: luggage,
stationery, toys, some grades FDA compliant.
Product examples: end cap (IM127, IM17A, IM-146L, IM-170, IM-174T); door handle (AP-8A,
AP-78EP); refrigerator liner (IM127, IM17A, IM174HF, ABS/PMMA extrusion grade); front and
outer casing (IM127, IM17A, ABS/PMMA antistatic IPM3000).
