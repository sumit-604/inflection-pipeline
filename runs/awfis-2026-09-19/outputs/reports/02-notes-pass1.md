## 10. DEFERRED TAX (Note 39, PDF p.121–122 standalone) — explains the "unusually low effective tax rate"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
**Effective tax rate is 0% in both FY26 and FY25** — current tax Nil, deferred tax Nil, for both
continuing and discontinued operations, both years (Note 39(A)(i)). Reconciliation from the 25.17%
statutory rate (Section 115BAA concessional regime; FY25 was 29.12%, i.e. the Company itself changed
its effective statutory-rate assumption/regime election between the two years — worth a management
question, see Section 12) to 0% actual tax:

| Line | FY26 (₹mn) | FY25 (₹mn) |
|---|---|---|
| Profit before tax | 667.87 | 655.61 |
| At statutory rate (25.17% / 29.12%) | 168.09 | 190.91 |
| Tax impact of temporary differences on which DT not considered | (168.93) | (89.19) |
| Tax impact of income at special rates | 15.60 | 111.63 |
| Adjustment of tax losses | (14.76) | (213.35) |
| **Net tax expense** | **0.00** | **0.00** |

The mechanism is visible in the deferred tax balance-sheet note (Note 39(B)): the Company carries large
offsetting DTL and DTA — DTL of Rs2,616.93mn on PPE/intangibles/ROU carrying-value differences and
Rs901.90mn on finance lease receivables (new this year, was nil at FY25) totalling DTL Rs3,798.65mn;
against DTA of Rs3,650.04mn on lease liabilities plus Rs249.35mn unabsorbed depreciation/business-loss
carryforward plus Rs269.39mn other, totalling DTA Rs3,798.65mn (**exactly netted to zero**, footnote:
"the company has recognised deferred tax asset to the extent of deferred tax liability since it is not
probable that sufficient profits will be available in the future years against which deferred tax
assets can be realised"). 🟡 This is a **structural feature of the Ind AS 116 lease book** (large DTA on
lease liabilities exactly offsetting large DTL on ROU/finance-lease assets), not an aggressive tax
position — if anything it is conservative (management is choosing NOT to recognise the incremental DTA
that unabsorbed losses (Rs594.72mn expiring 2027-2030, tax impact Rs149.68mn, Note 39(C)) and unlimited
unabsorbed depreciation (Rs396.01mn, tax impact Rs99.67mn) would in principle allow). Net effect for a
reader: PAT is NOT tax-shielded by an aggressive DTA recognition policy; it is tax-free because of a
genuine zero net-tax position that itself depends on continued non-recognition of a large gross DTA
pool. If the Company becomes consistently profitable, a future DTA-recognition event (crystallising the
~Rs150-250mn of currently-unrecognised carryforward benefit) is a plausible one-time non-cash tax
credit to watch for, not a current earnings quality concern.
