## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- No new standards first-adopted in FY25; accounting policies stated consistent with prior year
  (Standalone Note 2.1, p.116). 🟢
- EBITDA presented as a separate voluntary line on the face of the P&L per SEBI circular dated
  July 11, 2023 allowing additional line items (Standalone Note 2, p.128). Company defines EBITDA
  as profit from continuing operations before D&A, finance costs and tax; explicitly states this
  "may not be comparable with similarly titled measures" used by other companies (Standalone
  Note 2, p.127-128). 🟡 Standard restaurant-sector disclosure but a non-GAAP metric management
  chose to headline; worth cross-checking against P&L face for any EBITDA definitional drift YoY.
- Depreciation useful lives (Standalone Note 2.2(i), p.119-120): Furniture & fixtures 10 yrs,
  Plant & machinery 15 yrs, Service equipment 2-10 yrs, Computers 3 yrs, Vehicles 3 yrs.
  Leasehold improvements amortised over lower of useful life or lease term. These differ from
  Schedule II default lives (explicitly disclosed as based on "technical assessment made by
  technical expert and Management estimate" — Note 2.2(i), p.119). No change in useful lives
  disclosed this year. 🟢 disclosed rationale, not a red flag on its own.
- Intangibles: Liquor licences amortised over lease term of respective restaurant; Software &
  licences 6 years; Brands — indefinite useful life, tested annually for impairment (Standalone
  Note 2.2(j), p.120). 🟢
- Impairment test assumptions (goodwill/CGU, Standalone Note 7, p.132-133 and Note 10A,
  p.136-137): Value-in-use over 5-year budget + perpetuity, terminal value = higher of (a) 3%
  growth in perpetuity and (b) 12x EBITDA exit multiple. Discount rate for the BNHL/Red Apple CGU
  fell to 14.61% (FY25) from 15.00% (FY24); MENA discount rate fell sharply to 7.91% from 10.00%;
  Blue Planet discount rate fell to 17.81% from 18.00%. 🟡 A falling discount rate raises
  recoverable amount holding cash flows constant — flagged below in Section 12 as this coincides
  with widening losses, and the 12x EBITDA multiple floor is a new input this year (not used
  FY24, where "-" is shown), a methodology change that also raises recoverable amount.
- ECL matrix: Company uses Ind AS 109 practical expedient / provision matrix for trade receivables
  (Standalone Note 2.2(s), p.124); ECL allowance moved from ₹0.00mn to ₹10.26mn in FY25 on
  standalone receivables (Standalone Note 14, p.139-140) — see Section 4 below.
- Ind AS 116 leases (Standalone Note 2.2(m), p.121; detail in Note 8 and Note 38): incremental
  borrowing rate used (rate implicit in lease not readily determinable). Effective interest rate
  on lease liabilities: 9.66% (FY25) vs 9.90% (FY24), maturities 2025-2042 (Standalone Note 8(b),
  p.134). ROU net block ₹4,661.75mn (₹466.18cr) standalone / ₹5,685.36mn (₹568.54cr) consolidated
  (Consolidated Note 8(a), p.221). Lease liability ₹5,856.71mn (₹585.67cr) standalone /
  ₹6,880.04mn (₹688.00cr) consolidated (Consolidated Note 8(b), p.221). This is the single largest
  balance sheet item after PP&E/ROU combined and drives ₹533.23mn (standalone)/₹610.69mn
  (consolidated) of finance cost and ₹720.35mn/₹928.09mn of depreciation (Standalone Notes 30-31,
  p.150-151; Consolidated Notes 30-31, p.224). 🟡 material by nature of the industry, flagged for
  scale not for aggressiveness — no evidence of off-balance-sheet lease treatment.
- Going concern: management explicitly assessed no material uncertainty exists and prepared
  accounts on a going concern basis, despite FY25 being the third consecutive year (at least) of
  standalone net losses (Standalone Note 3(e), p.129; Consolidated equivalent p.199-200 area).
  🟡 flagged for scrutiny given widening losses (Section 12/Section D below), not because the
  going-concern language itself is unusual boilerplate.

🔴 **APPARENT DISCLOSURE INCONSISTENCY — Barbeque Nation MENA Holding:** Note 9(c) (p.135) states

Barbeque MENA was fully impaired." However the investments table on the same page shows Cost of

₹249.65mn increase in cost arose from conversion of a related-party loan into equity during the
year (109,457 new shares allotted, Note 9(c)) but the impairment allowance was not correspondingly

interest receivable combined) held flat at ₹777.05mn in both FY24 and FY25 despite the mix shifting

impaired" refers only to the originally-impaired ₹313.09mn tranche, not the newly converted
₹249.65mn, or (b) a genuine gap between the qualitative statement and the quantitative disclosure.

and has committed open-ended financial support to (Note 36) has fluctuating impairment treatment
that does not fully reconcile in the notes as drafted.
