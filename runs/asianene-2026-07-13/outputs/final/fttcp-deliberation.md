## Operator input and how it moved the analysis

The operator did not question a single figure. The operator pasted a bull writeup and asked for it to be integrated and the four pillars reviewed. Treated as a review input.

- **Draft determination before the input:** margin forward STAGNANT (0); ROCE forward STAGNANT (0) with a genuine DECLINING tail; composite +3, DEEP WATCH.
- **Operator input:** FY27 40% standalone growth is order backed; Kuiper was bought cheap at $9.25m (~3x EBITDA) and is worth more; the standalone plus Kuiper alone roughly cover the current market value, so the Oilmax merger and the exploration book come nearly free; Oilmax is a high margin cash cow (stated 56% EBITDA, 128 Cr revenue, merging at Rs2,582 Cr); Duarmara can put the thesis in high gear, with the delay reframed as encountering oil rather than execution failure; onshore fields reduce the offshore risk the operator has been burned by before.
- **How it was integrated, with discipline:** the order backing, the light FY27 capex, the cheap Kuiper price, and Oilmax being a high margin producer are corroborated by this run's own sources (B03, B05, B07) and were adopted; they moved margin to STARTING and removed the ROCE DECLINING tail, taking the composite to +4. The Oilmax standalone financials (56% margin, 128 Cr, 290 Cr networth) and the "encountered oil, positive surprise" Duarmara framing are NOT in this run's inputs (merger scheme and Antelopus/Selan presentations, not collected); this run's own concall record says only "tested tighter than expected," graded honest technical disclosure. These were weighed as directional support, not adopted as anchored evidence, and the merger accounting basis (pooling at book vs fair value) is logged as the pivotal unverified fact. The ~50% upside claim is a Role 1 question and was not adopted in FTTCP.

**Operator's stated reasoning, in the operator's words:**
> "This seems to me to be a very undervalued business... At least I feel I am not overpaying at current levels as the valuation barely even covers standalone business + Kuiper as of today... If my calculations are right, there is likely a 50% upside here based on valuation alone in near-term. Medium/long-term I think can do very well if Duarmara production starts."

## Final FTTCP verdict, in the operator's words

The operator signed off with "ok, done" and moved the workup to valuation. The operator's own framing of the disposition: an undervalued turnaround where the buyer is not overpaying at current levels because the standalone business plus Kuiper roughly cover today's price, the Oilmax merger and the exploration book are the largely unpriced upside, and Duarmara production is the medium term high gear. This maps to the FTTCP composite +4, DEEP WATCH leaning BUY-ON-DIPS, carried into Phase 3.
