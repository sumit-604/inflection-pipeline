 Detailed Rationale 

 Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of Singer India Limited (SIL) to ‘ Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+ ’ from ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’. 

 The upgrade reflects the sustained improvement in SIL's operating performance and profitability profile. Revenue is expected to grow at a steady pace in fiscal 2027 after growing to Rs 55 7 crore in fisc al 2026 from Rs 432 crore in fiscal 2025 , supported by improved demand in the sewing machine segment, execution of outstanding government orders worth Rs 67 crore by Q3- FY 27 , better demand and improving acceptance of newer products in the home appliance segment. Operating margin improved to 2.8% in FY2026 from 1.5% in FY2025, supported by better realizations in the sewing machine division and the discontinuation of loss-making products in the home appliances segment. Company reported operating margin of 2 . 5 % in Q1- FY 20 27 and it is expected to improve to 3- 4% over the medium term backed by higher realizations from the sewing machine segment, discontinuation of loss-making product categories in the appliances business, better product mix, ability to pass on cost increases, and benefits derived from operational initiatives , including the fan segment and domestic manufacturing of zig-zag machines. 

 The ratings reflect the company’s well-established brand with strong presence in sewing machine and home appli ances business es ; product and geographical diversification across India; and asset-light business model, resulting in a comfortable return on capital employed (RoCE). The ratings also factor in a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India, leading to low operating margins and moderately high working capital cycle . 

 Analytical Approach 

 Crisil Ratings has considered the standalone business and financial risk profile s of SIL. 

 Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

 Well-established brand and strong market presence: SIL has a trademark agreement with the owners of the 'Singer' and 'Merritt' brands and benefits from a strong distribution and after-sales service network built over decades. The company derives around 75-80% of its revenue from sewing machines and the balance from home appliances. Revenue is expected to grow at a steady pace in FY2027 from Rs 557 crore in FY2026, supported by healthy dealer demand in the sew ing machine segment , execution of government welfare programme order s and addition of new products under home appliance segment. 

 Product and geographical diversification with an asset-light business model: SIL entered the home appliances segment a few years ago, to diversify its product profile further. The segment forms around 20-25 % of revenue currently. The company is present across India, with most of the sewing machine sales in the southern region. It follows an asset-light model through outsourced c ontract manufacturing with back-to-back warranty with vendors for major defects. The company's asset-light business model continue to s upport comfortable financial risk as reflected in ROCE of around 11% in fiscal 2026 which is expected to remain in the similar rrange over the medium term. . 

 Healthy financial risk profile : The financial risk profile is expected to strengthen further in fiscal 2027, supported by improved profitability and healthy accrual generation. Net worth is expected to increase to Rs 1 80 -190 crores as on March 31, 2027 from Rs 166.1 crore as on March 31, 2026, driven by continued profit retention, while leverage indicators are expected to remain comfortable with total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOL/ANW likely to remain below 1 time over the medium term (0.7 time as on March 31, 2026). The company's asset-light business model and absence of any sizeable debt-funded capital expenditure plans are expected to support a comfortable capital structure over the medium term. Debt protection metrics are comfortable, with projected interest cover of around 40 times for fiscal 2027 (24 times in fiscal 2026). 

 Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 

 Exposure to intense competition in the consumer durables sector in India leading to low operating margins: The operating profitability of the company remains constrained by intense competition in the consumer durables industry. However, operating margins improved to 2.8% in FY2026 from 1.5% in FY2025, supported by better realizations in the sewing m achine division and the discontinuation of loss-making products in the home appliances segment. Operating Margin moderated in Q 1 - FY2027 to 2. 49 % compared to 4.16% in Q4- FY 20 26 , impacted by lower government bulk orders for black sewing machines and weak demand for domestic appliances due to an abbreviated summer season. Nevertheless, with the expected improvement in revenue from the core sewing machine business and a better product mix, the company is expected to report operating margins of around 3-4% in FY2027. Sustained improvement in profitability while maintaining revenue growth will remain a key monitorable over the medium term. 

 Moderately high working capital cycle : The working capital cycle is expected to remain moderately high, with gross current assets (GCAs) likely to remain in the range of 150- 170 days over the medium term. As on March 31, 2026, the company reported GCAs of around 156 days, driven by inventory of 71 days and debtors of 25 days. These are adequately supported by internal accrua ls, and creditor support (creditors of 48 days as on March 31, 2026). 

 Liquidity Adequate 

 Bank limit utilisation was unutilised over the 12 months ended August 2026 . Net c ash accrual of around Rs 20- 2 2 crore annually are expected to be generated over the medium te r m which will remain comfortable a gainst nil term debt obligation s . 

 Company has free cash and bank balance of around Rs 90 crore as on March 31, 2026. 

 Current ratio is expected to remain comfortable at 2. 5 times as on March 31, 202 7 (2.3 times a s on March 31, 2026) . 

 Outlook Stable 

 Crisil Ratings believes that SIL will continue to benefit from its strong brand and well-established distribution and after-sales service networks .

 Incorporated in 1977, SIL is 31% owned by RHBV as of March 31, 2023. RHBV sold SIL 22.34% of its equity in the open market in August 2022, as a part of the restructuring plans for SIL. The business profile, which includes assembly and trading of sewing machines and home appliances in India, under the Singer and Merritt brands, will remain unchange d. The manufacturing facility is at Jammu.
