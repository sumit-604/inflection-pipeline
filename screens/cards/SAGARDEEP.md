Shallow Analysis Framework v1.0. Screened 2026-10-18 by Claude Code.
NSE SAGARDEEP | ISIN INE976T01013 | CIN L29253GJ2007PLC050007 | Santej, Gandhinagar, Gujarat (Ahmedabad district).
Corpus: `screens/corpus/SAGARDEEP/` (ledger in `MANIFEST.md`). Every number cites a file and a PDF page.
Market data is a screener.in service record dated 2026-10-18, not a filed document.

**Verdict: PASS.** A 2% margin copper converter and metal trader, the same size as in FY2014, where interest, asset sales and other operating income made 51% of FY26 pre-tax profit and the books contradict themselves on who borrowed Rs 6.16 crore.

---

## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Sagardeep makes copper and copper-alloy products at one plant in Santej: pipes, tubes, flats, coils, rods, busbars, anodes and nuggets. It also trades stainless steel and non-ferrous scrap "on getting trading opportunity" (screener/Annual_Report_2025.pdf, p.3). CARE recorded an installed capacity of 2,400 tonnes a year in 2015 (credit-ratings/02-CARE.pdf, p.1). The company keeps the gap between scrap and finished metal. In FY26 that gap left a standalone EBITDA margin of 2.16% on revenue of Rs 165.34 crore (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.91). The company has 16 permanent employees (p.46). It also earns interest on loans, rent, and gains on property sales. Two filings now name a second line: "construction of property, leasing of property & other real estate related business" (filings/SAGARDEEP_06022026194444_Final_signed.pdf, p.6).

**What is changing?** Volume. Standalone revenue was Rs 26.42 crore in Q1 FY26, Rs 70.39 crore in Q4 FY26 and Rs 57.07 crore in Q1 FY27 (filings/SAGARDEEP_11082026175428_Outcome_of_BM_signed.pdf, p.8; filings/SAGARDEEP_09072026124830_Disclosure_s.pdf, p.12). Management names no cause.

**Why now?** No catalyst is filed. The chairman's letter promises "capacity enhancement, product diversification, technological improvement" with no number, date or rupee (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.8). The MD&A spends four pages on the stainless steel industry (p.49 to p.51).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 volume must be repeat business at a margin above 2.5%, and it must convert to cash. FY26 operating cash flow was negative Rs 2.04 crore consolidated against profit of Rs 2.69 crore (p.128, p.127).

**What breaks it?** The record. CARE reported FY14 revenue of Rs 155.47 crore and profit of Rs 0.67 crore (credit-ratings/01-CARE.pdf, p.1). Twelve years later revenue is Rs 178.57 crore consolidated and profit Rs 2.69 crore (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.127). The business has not climbed. It has cycled.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One plant, one bank line, one subsidiary. Plant and machinery has a gross block of Rs 7.61 crore (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.100). Power cost was Rs 1.30 crore, 0.8% of revenue (p.107). The subsidiary, Sagardeep Engineers Pvt Ltd, earned Rs 16.91 crore of income and Rs 0.05 crore of profit in FY26 (p.40). Consolidated accounts book Rs 16.95 crore of "purchase of stock in trade" (p.127).

The company reports one segment. The notes say a second business exists below the reporting threshold (p.113). The Q1 FY27 filing names it as "real estate activities" (filings/SAGARDEEP_11082026175428_Outcome_of_BM_signed.pdf, p.10).

**Archetype: commodity converter, with a trading book and a small non-operating income stream.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds. Spread, utilisation, cycle position and cost-curve rank are the variables. The corpus discloses none of the four.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent. Copper scrap is bought at a market price and sold as a commodity product. The MD&A states the problem plainly: "there has been no surge in margin and profitability of the company" (Annual_Report_2025.pdf, p.49). The Directors' Report records nil R&D, nil technology absorption and nil energy conservation spend (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.36 to p.37). Debtor turnover of 19.33 times means about 19 days of credit (p.51). That signals near-cash buyers, not a franchise.

## 4. The promoters

The Mehta family. Satishkumar Asamal Mehta is Chairman and Managing Director, paid Rs 24 lakh a year (p.11, p.62). His wife Sangita Mehta joined the board as a non-executive director on 16 May 2025 (p.31). Promoter and group held 1,23,98,536 shares, 72.69%, at the September 2026 AGM (filings/SAGARDEEP_01102026130715_Outcome_and_Report.pdf, p.6). The MD declared no encumbrance for FY26 (filings/team_sandeshc_05052026131920_31.pdf, p.1).

Holdings shift inside the family. Satish Mehta's own stake fell 13.84 points in FY25 to 0.04%. Krish Mehta's rose from 4.61% to 8.63% in FY26 (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.103). The consolidated note still shows Satish Mehta at 13.88% (p.150). The screener record shows the group at 74.24% in September 2025 and 72.69% by March 2026 (market-data, service record). No SAST filing for that 1.55-point fall is held.

Related-party items, standalone FY26 (p.111):
- Loan to the subsidiary: Rs 13.82 crore lent and Rs 15.27 crore repaid in the year (p.85).
- Corporate guarantee for the subsidiary's loan (p.30).
- "Reimbursement of expenses" to Satish Mehta Rs 74.02 lakh, the full amount still payable at year end. That is 28% of standalone profit after tax.

The Directors' Report says the company "has not entered into any transactions with Related Parties" (p.37). Its own note 38 lists the items above.

**Ledger line (Rule G).** Built: a plant in 2009 to 2010 (Annual_Report_2025.pdf, p.3). Raised: authorised capital lifted from Rs 20 crore to Rs 46.25 crore on 23 November 2024, nothing issued yet (p.21). Deployed: CWIP of Rs 2.31 crore "temporarily suspended" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.101). Delivered: revenue back to the FY14 level. Pillar 3 line: no building record to lift any cap.

**Classification: one-off, pending proof.** Q4 FY26 carried 43% of FY26 revenue and 62% of standalone pre-tax profit. Q1 FY27 kept revenue up 116% year on year and profit up 127%. Finance cost tripled in Q1 FY27 against Q1 FY26.

Two readings. Reading one: the plant found real customers and volume now runs at Rs 55 to 70 crore a quarter at a 2.5% to 3.8% margin. Reading two: Q4 FY26 was a traded lump funded by the bank line, which rose from Rs 2.37 crore to Rs 8.97 crore in the year (p.105), and inventory rose from Rs 1.67 crore to Rs 5.76 crore (p.102). **The observation that separates them is H1 FY27 revenue with positive operating cash flow, due with the Q2 FY27 results.**

## 8. Proof check

Revenue moves. Margin and cash do not yet. Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin was 2.48% against 2.03% a year earlier. FY26 operating cash flow was negative. The proof point a converter needs, a higher spread per tonne, cannot be read: the company reports no tonnes. **Proof point absent.**

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **The Rs 6.16 crore loan.** Standalone note 12 calls it "Loan to Others" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.103, read visually). Consolidated note 10 calls the same Rs 615.80 lakh "Loans and Advances to Related Party" (p.149, read visually). It equals 19% of standalone net worth. The counterparty is not named.
- Directors' Report says no related-party transactions (p.37). Note 38 shows a Rs 15.27 crore subsidiary loan flow, a guarantee and Rs 74.02 lakh to the MD (p.111).
- Directors' Report gives FY26 standalone revenue as Rs 10,533.97 lakh (p.28). The audited P&L says Rs 16,533.97 lakh (p.91). The auditor reported "nothing to report" on other information (p.77).
- The consolidated share reconciliation shows 1,64,23,900 shares; the standalone shows 1,70,57,400 in both years (p.151, p.103). Consolidated FY26 statements are signed for a WTD and CFO who left in 2024 (p.126; p.32).
- Consolidated receivables include Rs 106.13 lakh "considered doubtful", disputed and over three years old, with no allowance (p.148).
- "Certain Balance of Debtors, Creditors, Loans & Advances for Capital expenditures are non-moving / sticky"; confirmations awaited (p.113). Capital advances of Rs 3.89 crore barely moved in the year, from Rs 3.84 crore (p.101).
- CARO says stock statements to the bank "are not in agreement with the books" and then shows zero difference in every quarter (p.85).
- Secretarial audit: promoter PANs not frozen in three trading windows, late SDD entries, late Reg 30 filings, a Reg 17 board fine (p.42 to p.43; p.68).
- NSE queried both the Q2 FY26 and FY26 results filings (SAGARDEEP_15012026222435...pdf, p.1; Disclosure_s.pdf, p.1).
- Turnover in governance: three CFOs and three company secretaries in 18 months; the secretarial auditor resigned on results day (p.32, p.11; Disclosure_s.pdf, p.4). The outgoing statutory auditor became internal auditor (p.36).
- Positive: unmodified audit opinion, no KAM, no pledge, no preferential issue, no warrants, no change of control found.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

CARE rated the Rs 15 crore cash credit CARE BB in February 2015, February 2016 and March 2017. It withdrew the rating on 13 June 2017 at the company's request (01-CARE.pdf, p.1 to p.2; 02-CARE.pdf, p.1). **The rating view is nine years stale.** It records a BB-grade borrower with 0.5% net margins. No agency has rated the company since. Today's filings win, and they show the same margin band.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Two strong revenue quarters, no margin step, negative cash, no tonnes.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** A 2% margin and sub-cost-of-capital returns in every year held, back to FY14. The second reading calls it ARTIFACT, a plant ramping into new volume. Q2 FY27 cash flow separates them.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market capitalisation Rs 48.9 crore, trailing PE 17.1x, 1.4x book (market-data, service record). That sits above the R1 neighbourhood of about 12x. No climb exists for the market to miss.

**Posture: AVOID.** Gap CLOSED plus STRUCTURAL dominates. On the ARTIFACT reading the cell is PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP). Both reach the same answer. Failure pattern: micro-cap converter whose profit leans on treasury and property income. Success catalogue pending.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

No operating business that earns above its cost of capital is shown. Half the profit is not from metal. The accounts disagree with themselves on a related-party loan worth a fifth of net worth.

**Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides to /step1**
1. The counterparty of the Rs 6.16 crore loan, and whether it is related.
2. What the Rs 74.02 lakh reimbursement to the MD paid for.
3. Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 revenue by product, customer and manufactured versus traded.
4. Tonnes produced and capacity today.
5. The split of other operating income between rent and metal.

**What would change the view**
- H1 FY27 revenue above Rs 110 crore with positive operating cash flow and EBITDA margin above 2.5%.
- A clean reconciliation of the related-party loan and the share count.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
