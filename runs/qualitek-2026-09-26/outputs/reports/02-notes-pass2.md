## PASS 2 NEW FINDINGS

1. **Note 33(d)(i) gives the direct revenue-contribution figures for ITCPL that Pass 1 did not extract, and they are the single most load-bearing number for LBF1 (organic vs acquired growth).** The consolidated financial statements text states: "The consolidated revenue includes ₹5,651.73 Lakh (previous year: ₹2,564.75 Lakh) and consolidated net profit includes ₹613.80 Lakh (previous year: ₹276.49 Lakh) for the post-acquisition period" of ITCPL (Note 33(d)(i), p.134/[page 140]). Cross-checked against standalone revenue (₹6,762 Lakh, Note 19 standalone per Pass 1) and QTIPL's own contribution (₹37.63 Lakh, Note 33(d)(iii) below) plus LabOps (₹0.00 Lakh), the sum ₹6,762 + ₹5,651.73 + ₹37.63 + ₹0.00 = ₹12,451.36 Lakh reconciles almost exactly to consolidated revenue of ₹12,451.81 Lakh (Testing services ₹12,414.17 Lakh + IT services ₹37.64 Lakh, Note 19 consol). **This means ITCPL alone supplied ₹5,651.73 Lakh, 45.4% of FY26 consolidated revenue** — the acquired subsidiary is now nearly as large a revenue contributor as the standalone parent. 🟡 Watch — feeds LBF1 directly: FY26 consolidated revenue growth (+77%) is close to half a consolidation-scope effect (ITCPL going from a partial-year FY25 stub to a full FY26 year), not all organic.
   - [INFERENCE, labelled]: ITCPL's FY25 contribution of ₹2,564.75 Lakh covers a partial period (post-11-Sep-2024 acquisition of control, so roughly 6.5 months). Annualising that stub to a full year gives approximately ₹4,735 Lakh. ITCPL's FY26 full-year contribution of ₹5,651.73 Lakh against this annualised base implies ITCPL's own like-for-like growth was roughly +19%, materially slower than the 77% headline consolidated growth rate and slower than the 47% standalone growth rate. This is an estimate built from the note's own disclosed figures, not an AR-stated number; a downstream stage should treat it as a directional cross-check, not an anchored fact.

2. **Note 33(d)(iii) gives QTIPL's own revenue contribution: ₹37.63 Lakh FY26 (FY25: ₹2.26 Lakh)** (p.134-135/[page 140]-[page 141]) — ties out exactly to Note 19's "IT Services" revenue line, confirming QTIPL is the sole source of the IT-services revenue line and that this business remains immaterial in scale (0.3% of consolidated revenue) two years after acquisition, despite a ₹500 Lakh purchase price. 🟢 Clean cross-reference, but reinforces Pass 1's Section 6 finding that QTIPL is a loss-making, non-scaling acquisition.

3. **Note 33(d)(ii) confirms LabOps Global contributed ZERO revenue in its first (partial) year, only a ₹23.07 Lakh loss** (p.134/[page 140]) — an acquisition that has not yet started operating at the AS21 disclosure level. 🟡 Watch — a third consecutive acquisition (after QTIPL) that is a pure earnings drag with no revenue to show for it yet; worth a management question on when LabOps is expected to contribute.

4. **The Mumbai food-testing-lab slump sale (Note 33(d)(iv)) gets NO revenue/profit contribution disclosure, unlike the two subsidiary acquisitions.** The note only states consideration ₹882.00 Lakh, net assets acquired ₹662.14 Lakh, goodwill ₹219.86 Lakh, effective 01-Sep-2025 (p.134/[page 140]) — no revenue or profit contribution figure is given for this acquisition anywhere in the notes. 🟡 Watch — this is explainable (AS 21's revenue/profit contribution disclosure is a subsidiary-consolidation requirement; a slump-sale asset purchase folds directly into standalone operations with no separate contribution note required), but it means a downstream stage cannot isolate this acquisition's organic performance from the standalone Panchkula/parent business the way it can for ITCPL and QTIPL. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT for the food-testing lab's own revenue contribution.

5. **The actual FY26 goodwill amortisation charge is ₹25.65 Lakh, not the ~₹44 Lakh/yr Pass 1 estimated from a straight 5-year divide.** Note 11b (Intangible Assets, gross block/accumulated depreciation roll-forward, p.126/[page 132]) shows: gross addition ₹219.86 Lakh (the food-testing-lab goodwill), accumulated depreciation "provided during the year" ₹25.65 Lakh, closing net block ₹194.21 Lakh. 🟢 Clean, refines rather than contradicts Pass 1: ₹25.65 Lakh is a PARTIAL-YEAR charge (acquisition effective 01-Sep-2025, so ~7 of 12 months), consistent with ₹219.86 Lakh ÷ 5 years × 7/12 = ₹25.65 Lakh. The full-year run-rate from FY27 onward will be approximately ₹43.97 Lakh/yr as Pass 1 estimated, but FY26 itself carried roughly 58% of that. A downstream margin-bridge stage should use ₹25.65 Lakh as the FY26 actual, not the annualised figure.

6. **A separate, pre-existing, fully-amortised goodwill balance of ₹113.96 Lakh (gross, with ₹113.96 Lakh accumulated depreciation, i.e., zero net book value) was disposed/derecognised from the consolidated books in FY26** — visible only as a "(113.96)" deduction against both the gross block and accumulated depreciation columns in the same Note 11b roll-forward (p.126/[page 132]), with no narrative explanation anywhere in the notes of what this legacy goodwill was or why it was removed this year. 🟡 Watch — zero P&L impact (already fully written off before removal) but an unexplained housekeeping item Pass 1 did not surface; a downstream stage should note the standalone equivalent note (Note 11B standalone, ~line 9116) does NOT show this same disposal (standalone closing net goodwill is ₹239.65 Lakh vs consolidated ₹194.21 Lakh), meaning the ₹113.96 Lakh legacy goodwill sat at a subsidiary level and was eliminated only on consolidation. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT for the narrative reason.

7. **The consolidated auditor's report also states "no Key Audit Matters," closing the verification item Pass 1 flagged as open.** "We have determined that there are no key audit matters to communicate in our report" appears in the Independent Auditor's Report on the Consolidated Financial Statements (Annual_Report_2026.txt, page marker [page 94], printed page 88), in addition to the standalone report Pass 1 already quoted (p.140-141). 🟡 Watch (confirmed, not new in substance) — both the standalone AND consolidated statutory audits determined no KAM in a year with four acquisitions, a 2.4x goodwill increase, and a material related-party share swap; this strengthens rather than weakens Pass 1's finding #10 (auditor communication choice, not substantive clearance).

8. **A new income-tax demand appears for the first time this year: CIT(Appeals), Assessment Year 2023-24, ₹7.99 Lakh** (Note 28(A) consolidated, p.132-133/[page 138]-[page 139], adjacent to the AY 2018-19 demand of ₹5.48 Lakh Pass 1 already captured). Total consolidated tax contingent liability is therefore ₹104.68 Lakh (₹5.48 + ₹7.99 + ₹91.21), not the ₹96.69 Lakh Pass 1 reported — 0.69% of consolidated net worth, still immaterial in amount, but notable because AY 2023-24 is the company's most recent post-IPO assessment year to draw a fresh demand, unlike the older AY 2018-19/FY2017-22 GST items. 🟡 Watch — first fresh tax dispute touching a post-listing year; worth a management question on nature/status.

9. **The disputed trade receivables (₹13.87 Lakh gross, first-time appearance Pass 1 already flagged) are concentrated entirely in the aged 2-3 year (₹10.78 Lakh) and >3 year (₹3.09 Lakh) buckets** (Note 15 consolidated ageing table, p.121/[page 127]) — i.e., these are OLD disputes surfacing in this year's disclosure for the first time, not new-year disputes. 🟡 Watch, refines Pass 1's flag: the concern is a disclosure-timing lag (old disputes only now being labelled "disputed"), not a fresh deterioration in current-year collections.

10. **Consolidated Capital Work-in-Progress (CWIP) nearly tripled YoY, ₹1,566.34 Lakh to ₹4,528.25 Lakh (+189%), and Pass 1 did not report this movement at all** (Note 11c, p.124-125/[page 130]-[page 131]). Sub-note: "Capital work-in-progress includes land and building under development amounting to ₹1,813.01 Lakh acquired during the year which are mortgaged as security against term loan of ₹1,300.30 Lakh from Bank" — real, quantified, bank-funded capex already underway (Note 11c(i)). 🟡 Watch — this is a genuine growth-quality data point that pairs directly with Pass 1's #1 finding (standalone ROE/ROCE decline): capital is being deployed into CWIP at nearly 3x the prior year's pace, ahead of the earnings it will eventually generate, which is consistent with (and helps explain the mechanism behind) the standalone return-ratio deterioration Pass 1 already flagged as the top finding.

11. **The CWIP ageing schedule shows an unchanged ₹151.81 Lakh balance ageing from the "2-3 years" bucket at FY25 into the "more than 3 years" bucket at FY26** (Note 11c(iii)(a), p.130/[page 136]) — i.e., the same project has sat in capital work-in-progress, uncompleted, for over three years — while the very next line states: "There are no projects under capital work-in-progress whose completion is overdue or has exceeded its cost compared to the original plan" (Note 11c(iii)(b)). 🟡 Watch — a genuine internal tension worth naming per Amendment 25/Pass-3-pattern logic: a static balance crossing the 3-year ageing threshold reads, on its face, as overdue by any ordinary meaning of the word, yet management's own overdue-assessment note asserts none are overdue. Cannot be resolved from the notes alone; a management question candidate.

12. **The Capital Commitments note's blanket "no material non-cancellable contractual commitments" statement (Note 28(ii) consolidated, already flagged by Pass 1) sits two pages after the CWIP note's ₹1,813.01 Lakh land-and-building-under-development disclosure with a named ₹1,300.30 Lakh secured term loan** (Note 11c(i) vs Note 28(ii), p.124/[page 130] vs p.132/[page 138]). 🟡 Watch — clarifies rather than deepens Pass 1's flag: the FY27 capex the operator's load-bearing facts reference is not absent from the notes, it is simply not classified as a "commitment" in the note's narrow contractual sense; it is already partly executed and appears instead as CWIP additions and a specific secured borrowing. A downstream stage should read the CWIP note, not the Capital Commitments note, for the FY27 capex evidence trail.
