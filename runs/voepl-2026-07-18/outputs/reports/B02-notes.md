## PASS 3: PATTERN-PASS FINDINGS

**PATTERN A — Unmodified/clean audit opinion coexists with three explicit scope-limitation admissions inside the notes themselves.**
The independent auditor's report (standalone, p.~65-70 of the AR pagination; "Opinion" and "Basis for Opinion" headings) contains no "Qualified Opinion," "Adverse Opinion," "Disclaimer of Opinion," or "Emphasis of Matter" heading anywhere in the document — a search across the full extract for these terms returns zero hits. The opinion is unmodified: "In our opinion... the aforesaid standalone financial statements give a true and fair view." The same auditor, in the same report package, separately discloses via Note 38(b) ("test check" sample vouching only), Note 38(d) (no physical verification of cash or closing stock), and Note 37 (trade receivables, trade payables, loans & advances, and other current/other-liability balances all "subject to confirmation... management does not expect any significant variance") — a set of scope limitations that in combination touch the majority of the balance sheet's current-asset and current-liability captions plus the single largest asset class (inventory, 35% of total assets). The Board separately certifies "internal financial controls... adequate and operating effectively" (Directors' Responsibility Statement) and the auditor separately issues an unmodified "Report on Internal Financial Controls" opinion that IFC "were operating effectively as at 31st March 2025." Taken together, an auditor who has not physically verified the largest asset on the balance sheet, has not obtained independent confirmation of receivables/payables/loans, and has relied on sample (not full) vouching nonetheless signs an unmodified opinion with adequate/effective IFC — with none of this framed anywhere as an Emphasis of Matter or Key Audit Matter for a non-listed-on-main-board SME platform company. This is a genuine internal tension in the assurance package, not merely a disclosure gap: the substance of what was and was not tested does not obviously support the strength of language used in the opinion and the IFC certification. 🔴 Red Flag — new pattern-level finding, not previously named as a single item in Pass 1 or 2 (which flagged the three scope-limitation notes individually/in combination but did not connect them explicitly to the unmodified opinion and IFC certification).

**PATTERN B — Standalone/consolidated inconsistency is not confined to one note; it recurs across at least six independent notes covering solvency-relevant facts (Pass 2 findings, restated as a single pattern).**
Corroborating Pass 2's individual findings, re-checking confirms the pattern is systemic rather than a single clerical slip: guarantor count (2 vs 3 entities, Note 6(a)(ix)), new-plant count and location (Janori/Plant B2 vs Mohadi & Vilholi), a lender line present in one note set and silently absent in the other (Siemens Finance, ₹1,378.70L, ~5.5% of LT book), an actuarial assumption for the identical prior year (gratuity discount rate 7.25% vs 7.50%), and MSME comparative-year mislabeling plus a ₹3.18L numeric mismatch. Every one of these six items describes the SAME underlying fact for the SAME fiscal year, disclosed two different ways in the two note sets prepared from what should be a single consolidation working-paper base. No item is individually material in rupees, but the reconciliation control failure they collectively imply is itself the finding: a reader relying on only one of the two note sets (as most retail investors would, reading the standalone or consolidated set but not both) receives a materially different picture of counterparty guarantee exposure and capex footprint depending on which set they read. 🔴 Red Flag (consolidation of Pass 2 Findings 2, 4, 5, 6, 12 into one pattern-level red flag).

**PATTERN C — No going-concern qualification or doubt language anywhere in the document; boilerplate-clean.**
Full-document search for "going concern" returns only standard boilerplate: the Directors' Report states "no significant or material orders passed by the Regulators/Courts which would impact the future operations/going concern status of the Company" (p.25 of AR pagination), the Directors' Responsibility Statement affirms statements were "prepared on a going concern basis," and both standalone and consolidated auditor's reports contain only the standard SA-570-style procedural paragraph on evaluating going-concern appropriateness as part of normal audit methodology — no conclusion of material uncertainty, no qualifying language, no auditor doubt expressed. 🟢 Clean — genuinely no going-concern issue found; this is a true negative, not an unexamined gap (relevant given the auditor's separately disclosed scope limitations in Pattern A above, which raise assurance-quality questions but do not extend to solvency doubt).

**PATTERN D — "Previous period figures regrouped/reclassified" is boilerplate, not a substantive restatement; no PY figures were found materially reclassified as evidenced by comparability of every YoY table extracted in Passes 1-2.**
Standard closing language ("(h) Previous period figures have been regrouped, re-arranged and re-classified wherever necessary to conform to current period's classification") appears identically in both standalone (p.~53 of the extract's internal numbering) and consolidated notes. No note anywhere flags a specific reclassification with a quantified prior-year restated amount alongside an as-originally-reported amount (the pattern one would expect for a genuine restatement disclosure). Every prior-year (FY24) figure used as a comparative throughout Passes 1 and 2 (depreciation ₹1,826.91L, receivables ₹2,121.56L, MSME payables ₹245.00L, etc.) reconciles internally across the notes in which it appears (net of the specific consolidated/standalone contradictions already catalogued in Pattern B). 🟢 No material restatement found — the regrouping clause is standard Companies Act boilerplate with no substantive instance behind it this year.

**PATTERN E — Events after balance sheet date: Virtuoso Compressors Pvt Ltd incorporation confirmed as the only subsequent event, and it is disclosed asymmetrically (Directors' Report only, not as a financial-statement note).**
No new subsequent event surfaced on the pattern re-read beyond what Pass 1 already identified (Virtuoso Compressors Pvt Ltd, incorporated 29-Apr-2025, within the window before the 30-May-2025 board approval date, "yet to commence operations"). The financial-statement notes themselves (Note II(q), Note 38(j)) assert no material subsequent events, while the Directors' Report AOC-1 footnote is the only place this new subsidiary is disclosed. This is a disclosure-placement gap (confirmed, not new) rather than a contradiction of fact. 🟡 Watch (restates Pass 1, no change in severity).

**No further material new findings emerged from the pattern-based re-read beyond Patterns A-E above.** The dominant NEW insight from this final pass is Pattern A (the tension between an unmodified audit opinion/clean IFC certification and the auditor's own disclosed scope limitations) and the explicit confirmation that Pattern B's six standalone/consolidated inconsistencies (surfaced individually in Pass 2) constitute one systemic control-quality pattern rather than isolated clerical errors.

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Are Filaments & Filaments, Luma Lamp/Lumalamp Limited, and Reprolite Papers (India) Pvt Ltd promoter- or relative-controlled entities? If so, why do they not appear (or appear only inconsistently) in the Note 32 related-party universe, particularly given Filaments & Filaments sold the Company ₹2,418 lakhs of its own plant land this year while simultaneously guaranteeing/mortgaging property for the Company's bank debt?
2. What is the business rationale and quantified go-forward impact of changing the depreciation method from WDV to SLM in FY25, and why was this change made in the same year the Company completed a large capital raise and is under active analyst/investor scrutiny of profit growth?
3. Given the auditor's own disclosures of test-check (sample) vouching, no physical verification of cash or closing stock, and no independent balance confirmation for trade receivables, trade payables, loans & advances, and other current assets/liabilities, what specific additional procedures support the unmodified opinion and the "adequate and effective" internal financial controls certification?
4. What accounts for the standalone-vs-consolidated inconsistencies in guarantor count (2 vs 3 entities), new-plant location and count (Janori/Plant B2 vs Mohadi & Vilholi), and the FY24 gratuity discount rate (7.25% vs 7.50%) — were the two note sets not cross-checked before filing, and will FY26 reconcile them?
5. What is the nature of the new "Service Income" revenue line (₹13.13L to ₹627.82L, a ~48x increase), the first-time ₹39.99 lakh GST Assessment Payment, and the status of the ongoing GST input tax credit reconciliation flagged as unresolved in Note 38(a)?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management signal**: depreciation method change (WDV→SLM) removed ₹806.83 lakhs of expense in the same year as a large capital raise, explaining ~75% of PBT growth; combined with 100% R&D capitalisation and a liability-reducing gratuity assumption shift, multiple profit/expense levers moved in the same profit-inflating direction in a single year.
- **Aggressive accounting**: full capitalisation of R&D spend with none expensed through P&L.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: related-party-like guarantor/mortgagor/land-seller entities (Filaments & Filaments, Reprolite Papers, likely Luma Lamp) sit outside or only partially inside the disclosed related-party universe.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: ₹14.38 Cr GST contingent liability omitted from the Company's own contingent-liability note, visible only via the CARO annexure.
- **Assurance-quality red flag**: unmodified audit opinion and clean IFC certification issued alongside self-disclosed test-check vouching, no physical verification of the largest asset class, and no balance confirmation across the bulk of working capital.
- **Disclosure-control red flag**: at least six standalone-vs-consolidated contradictions on solvency/capex-relevant facts, plus two additional internal (single-document) inconsistencies (Authorised Capital table, ESOP cancellation sentence).

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal concerning accounting practices. Key concern: a depreciation-method change and full R&D capitalisation together manufactured most of this year's profit growth, layered on an assurance package whose unmodified opinion sits in tension with its own disclosed scope limitations and an incompletely disclosed related-party universe. Key strength: genuine receivables-collection improvement and largely clean, arm's-length operating-side related-party transactions. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
