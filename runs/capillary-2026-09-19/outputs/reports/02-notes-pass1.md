## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition (Ind AS 115, 5-step model)**: Standalone Note 2.3(c), p.127-129;
  Consolidated equivalent in the auditor's Key Audit Matter, p.179-180. Retainership revenue
  recognised over the subscription period; campaign (SMS) services on gross/net basis depending
  on principal-vs-agent judgement; installation revenue over time using an input (milestone)
  method. "Service income from Group Companies" is a fourth, standalone-specific revenue stream
  where subsidiaries act as Limited Risk Distributors and the Company guarantees them an agreed
  profitability (Note 2.3(c)(iv), p.128). 🟡 Watch — the LRD/guaranteed-profitability arrangement is
  a related-party revenue construct (see Section 2 below), not third-party economics, at the
  standalone level.
- **No policy changes with quantified P&L impact** were identified. MCA's FY26 amendments to
  Ind AS 21, Ind AS 1, Ind AS 7 and Ind AS 107 were all assessed as having **no significant impact**
  (Note 2.5, p.142-143, both standalone and consolidated). 🟢
- **Depreciation useful lives** (Note 2.3(e), p.130-131, Schedule II basis, WDV method): Office
  equipment 5 years, Computers 3 years — both shorter than Schedule II's typical SLM defaults, not
  unusual for a software company. No changes in useful life during the year. 🟢
- **Intangible amortisation**: Computer software 5 years; **internally generated intangible assets
  amortised over 3 years only** (Note 2.3(f), p.131-132). A 3-year life for the company's core
  platform IP is aggressive/fast (keeps net book value low but drives a very high annual
  amortisation charge — ₹377.63mn standalone / ₹664.68mn consolidated in FY26, see Section 12).
  🟡 Watch.
- **Capitalisation of internally generated software** is the single largest accounting judgement in
  the filing. It is a named **Key Audit Matter** in the consolidated auditor's report (p.178-179):
  ₹366.15mn capitalised in FY26 (both standalone and consolidated — the capitalised spend is
  100% standalone/India-originated). This equals ~34% of consolidated EBITDA (₹1,065.85mn) and
  ~140% of consolidated profit before exceptional items and tax (₹261.50mn). Capitalisation
  criteria per Ind AS 38 are recited in full (Note 2.3(f) and 2.4(g), p.131-132, 138-139) but the
  underlying technical-feasibility/probable-future-benefit judgement is manual and unaudited by
  a bright-line test. 🟡 Watch — high capitalisation-to-earnings ratio, judgement-heavy, flagged by
  the auditors themselves.
- **Impairment of non-financial assets / goodwill**: standard Ind AS 36 language (standalone Note
  2.3(i), p.132-134; consolidated Note 2.3(h) "Impairment of Goodwill", p.207-208). Goodwill tested
  annually using DCF/value-in-use; **WACC (post-tax discount rate) = 27.70%** for both FY26 and
  FY25, annual growth 4-10% over 5 years, terminal growth 4% (consolidated Note 4, p.220). This is
  a Key Audit Matter (auditor's report, p.180-181). No impairment recognised either year. 🟡 Watch
  — a WACC this high (27.7%) leaves limited headroom on a ₹3,095.76mn goodwill balance if
  cash-flow assumptions soften even modestly; the company itself notes it ran a sensitivity
  analysis but does not disclose the sensitivity outcome/headroom quantum.
- **Ind AS 116 leases**: Incremental borrowing rate 8.54%-9.5% (consolidated, up from a flat 9.5%
  standalone-only rate last year — reflects new geography-specific leases post-Kognitiv). Lease
  term generally 1-3 years group-wide, ~2 years standalone (Note 30/33, p.164/261-262). 🟢
  Standard, no aggressive discount-rate assumptions.
- **ECL matrix**: simplified approach (lifetime ECL from initial recognition) for trade receivables,
  standard provision-matrix methodology, no unusual conservatism or leniency flagged
  (Note 2.3(l), p.135-137; Note 8.1). 🟢
- **First-time adoption / new standards**: none material this year (Note 2.5). The Group notes
  supplier-finance-arrangement disclosure amendments (Ind AS 7/107) with no impact — the
  company does not appear to use supplier finance/reverse factoring. 🟢
- **IPO transaction cost accounting policy** (standalone Note 2.3(r), p.139-140): incremental costs
  of the fresh issue taken to equity (net of tax); listing-related costs expensed through P&L. Of
  total IPO expenses, ₹137.85mn was adjusted against securities premium and ₹32.36mn was
  expensed to the standalone P&L (Note 12(a)1, p.148-149). 🟢 Correctly bifurcated per Ind AS
  guidance.
