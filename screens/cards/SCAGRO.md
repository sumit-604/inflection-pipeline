## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** SC Agrotech trades
agricultural raw materials and products. It buys stock and sells it on credit.
FY26 revenue was Rs 87.92 crore of agri trading and Rs 0.14 crore of farmer
producer organisation fees (AR26, page 91). It has 6 employees (AR26, page 33).
Its fixed assets are land of Rs 1.13 crore and a computer (AR26, page 90).

**What is changing?** A shell became a trader in one year. In FY25 the company
had nil revenue from operations. It earned Rs 2.47 crore of other income, of
which Rs 1.99 crore was profit on a land sale (AR26, page 91). It described
itself as a franchisee and vertical-garden business (AR25, page 92). Then it
issued 7 crore warrants at Rs 16 to 19 allottees and raised Rs 112 crore
(AR26, page 30). Shares went from 59.95 lakh to 7.60 crore (AR26, page 83).

**Why now?** Q1 and Q2 FY27 printed Rs 9.56 crore and Rs 10.21 crore of profit
after tax. All of FY26 earned Rs 3.34 crore (Q1R, page 3; Q2R, page 3).

**What must be true?** The Rs 122.60 crore of trade receivables at September
2026 must be real and must turn into cash (Q2R, page 5). The 30% margins must
come from trading, not from how stock and receivables are booked.

**What breaks it?** The cash. H1 FY27 operating cash flow was minus Rs 0.09
crore against profit after tax of Rs 19.77 crore (Q2R, page 7). The auditor
qualified FY26 because documents were not made available (FY26R, page 9).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment. The filing says "the company is dealing only in Agri
Trading Raw Material and Products" (Q2R, page 4). AR26 MD&A claims seeds,
fertilisers, pesticides, contract farming and agro-processing (AR26, page 32).
The notes show none of that. The chairman's letter cites "energy-efficient
manufacturing" (AR26, page 4) for a company with no plant.

The engine is a spread: buy agri stock, sell on credit. The margin is the gap
between sale price and stock cost. Gross margin was 5.7% in FY26 and 1.7% in
Q4 FY26. It jumped to 33.8% in Q1 FY27 and 30.6% in Q2 FY27 (my arithmetic,
FY26R page 2, Q1R page 2, Q2R page 2).

**Archetype: commodity converter (Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds).** A
trader is a spread business with no conversion step. A deck adds a
platform/network claim, AgriBridge, that has no revenue (Deck, page 15).

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent. A six-person agri trader has no pricing power, no switching cost and
no cost position the corpus can name. A 30% gross margin on traded farm
commodities is not a moat signal. It is an anomaly to explain. AgriBridge is a
concept with "DEMO DATA - NOT LIVE" on every screen (Deck, pages 19, 22, 23).

## 4. The promoters

There are none. The promoter category is nil at 31 March 2026 (AR26, page 51).
The Chandak family held 22.08% (S N Chandak) and 10.83% (Sheela Vigneswar) at
March 2024 and nothing at March 2025 (AR25, page 78). Jivab Trading Pvt Ltd
then held 20.33% and Sproutcraft Enterprises 5.09%.

The warrant notice stated the promoters would not subscribe (AR25, page 28).
After conversion the 19 allottees own 92.1% of the company (7.00 crore of 7.60
crore shares). Seven individuals hold 34.0 lakh to 37.3 lakh shares each, and
Saize Enterprise Pvt Ltd 37.05 lakh (AR26, pages 83 to 84).

The board is new. The MD, Pratikkumar Bharatbhai Patel, joined on 29-Jul-2025.
Every other director joined in 2025 or 2026 (AR26, page 20). Director holdings:
NOT FOUND (looked in AR26 board report and share notes). Pledge: NOT FOUND, and
with no promoter it does not apply. Skin in the game: a Rs 0.10 crore
unsecured loan from a director (AR26, page 85).

funded credit. The margin step that followed is not confirmed by cash.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R0 non-operating: FY25 earnings came from a land sale and franchise fees.
TO R1 commodity price-taker as an agri trader. The deck also implies a jump to
a platform rung through AgriBridge. Management's claim: "Building the next
chapter across agricultural trade and a proposed digital farm-to-buyer
platform" (Deck, page 2).

R0 rules say a transition must first prove an operating engine exists. Profit
with no cash does not prove it. The platform leg is a multi-rung leap with no
revenue, which the ladder treats as a red flag in itself.

## 8. Proof check

Revenue and profit moved the way the deck claims. Q2 FY27 revenue rose 19.6%
on Q1 (Deck, page 7). The proof a trader must show is cash, and it is absent.
H1 operating cash flow was nil against Rs 19.77 crore of profit. Cash fell to
Rs 0.13 crore. The deck itself flags it: "Collections and customer ageing
require active monitoring" (Deck, page 13). The proof point is absent.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Audit qualification, FY26.** The company "did not make available ...
  complete supporting documents, reconciliations and certain statutory
  records", including related-party transactions. The auditor could not verify
  the audit trail (FY26R, page 9). The impact statement claims nil effect
  (FY26R, page 19).
- **Emphasis of matter, Q2 FY27.** Receivables, payables and all loans and
  advances are "pending comprehensive verification" (Q2R, page 9).
- **Unrecorded fire note.** AR26 states a fire caused an inventory loss of
  Rs 42.40 crore (AR26, page 88). FY26 shows no exceptional item (FY26R, page
  2). The stated loss exceeds the Rs 29.05 crore closing stock.
- **Deck contradicts the filing.** Deck page 4 shows Q2 PAT Rs 10.74 crore and
  EPS Rs 1.41. The filing shows Rs 10.21 crore and Rs 1.34 (Q2R, page 3). The
  filed deck carries "MANAGEMENT REVIEW DRAFT - DO NOT DISTRIBUTE" (Deck, page
  2).
- **Dilution.** 92.1% of the equity went to 19 allottees at Rs 16 (AR26, page
  30). The price now is Rs 42.26 (Financials.txt, Data Sheet, secondary).
- **Borrowing beside cash.** A Rs 10 crore demand loan from Sumadhurainfracon
  Pvt Ltd sits beside Rs 112 crore of fresh equity (AR26, page 85).
- **Document errors.** Board approval dated 29-Oct in a 28-Oct filing (Q2R,
  page 4). An FY26 audit opinion dated "31 March 2024" (FY26R, page 9). The
  related-party note names former directors and omits the current MD (AR26,
  page 94). The demat table totals 3.52 crore shares against 7.60 crore issued
  (AR26, page 52). The board says the nature of business did not change
  (AR26, page 18).
- **Contact trail.** A gmail address and three websites across filings (Q2R,
  page 1; FY26R, page 1; Deck, page 1).
- Customer concentration: NOT FOUND (looked in AR26 notes 14 and 17).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Profit printed. Cash did not follow. The auditor
  cannot confirm the receivables that carry the profit.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** A qualified audit, unconfirmed balances and zero
  cash conversion are not costs of a climb. They are features of how the
  numbers arise.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market capitalisation is Rs 321.2 crore (Bull
  AI company record, 2026-11-04, a service record). That is 2.3 times
  September equity of Rs 137.80 crore, of which Rs 122.60 crore is unconfirmed
  receivables. Trailing PE is 14.0 times on Rs 22.88 crore of last-four-quarter
  profit. For an R0 relic the floor is net assets, and the price sits well
  above it.

**Posture: AVOID.** STRUCTURAL with gap CLOSED, the dominating overlay. Value-
trap risk at best.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The screen hit is real arithmetic on unverified numbers. A shell raised Rs 112
crore from 19 allottees, booked 30% margins on traded farm goods, and collected
almost none of it in cash. The auditor qualified the accounts and flagged every
receivable. The deck misstated the quarter it presented.

**Load-bearing facts, if anyone reopens it**
1. Independent confirmation and post-September collection of the Rs 122.60
   crore of trade receivables.
2. Who the customers and suppliers are, and whether any link to the 19
   allottees.
3. The Rs 42.40 crore fire-loss note: real event or copied text.
4. Where the Rs 112 crore went, from the Brickwork monitoring report.

**What would change the view**
- Two quarters of operating cash flow near reported profit, with receivables
  falling.
- An unqualified FY27 audit with balance confirmations obtained.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides
only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
