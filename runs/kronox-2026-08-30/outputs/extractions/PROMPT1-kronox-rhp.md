## 1A. THE GPCB RESTRICTION (highest priority)

The GPCB restriction is disclosed in **Risk Factors (Risk Factor 16, pages
42-43)** and in **Our Business — Capacity and Capacity Utilisation (page 190)**.
It is NOT in Outstanding Litigation (that section is NIL, pages 306-308) and is
NOT recorded as an order or notice in Government and Other Approvals (pages
309-311). Objects of the Offer (pages 88-91) does not mention it (pure Offer
for Sale).

### Mention 1 — Risk Factor 16 heading (page 42)
> "We may not be able to expand our production capacity and products manufactured at Unit – III as GPCB has restricted any such expansion in the area where our Unit – III is situated i.e. Padra region, near Vadodara, Gujarat due to ground water contamination related issues. We are therefore limited to the manufacturing of only certain products at our Unit - III that are viable under such restrictions. Hence, there has been a decrease in the capacity utilization for aforementioned periods."

Reading: the restriction named is on EXPANSION. The product limitation is
tied to viability under the restriction and (below) to ZLD cost.

### Mention 2 — Risk Factor 16 body, ZLD (page 42)
> "Due to ground water contamination related issues, GPCB has restricted the expansion of manufacturing units in the Padra region, near Vadodara, Gujarat, where our Unit – III is situated. We have installed primary effluent treatment plant at all of our Manufacturing Facilities. For Unit – III, we have Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) permission where we have to treat the effluents generated during our manufacturing process, while for our other units, we have the permission to discharge the effluents to the CETP, Umraya, Padra. ... Installation and operation of ZLD equipment to treat the wastewater generated from the production process is a high cost proposition and we have therefore limited the use of this facility to some products. We therefore have to consider the viability of manufacturing certain products at Unit – III due to the use of effluent treatment equipment under ZLD during the manufacturing process ... We are therefore limited to the manufacturing of only certain products that are viable in this situation till we have further clarity from GPCB going forward."

Reading: the current product limitation is a company decision driven by ZLD
cost, not a GPCB output cap. Unit III holds a ZLD permission (zero liquid
discharge), while Units I/II discharge to CETP Umraya, Padra.

### Mention 3 — Risk Factor 16 body, Dahej and contingent risk (page 43)
> "In view of the GPCB restrictions on expansion in the Padra region where our Unit – III is situated, we have acquired a land at GIDC, Dahej – II admeasuring to 20,471 square meters, where we have planned our expansion. In the event the restrictions from GPCB continue to subsist, as above or any directions to reduce the capacity or closure of Unit – III by GPCB, we may not be able to fully operate Unit – III and utilise its production capacity which may lead to loss of production which may affect our business, financial condition and results of operations."

Reading: reduction or closure of Unit III is stated as a CONTINGENT future
risk ("In the event ... any directions ... by GPCB"), not a current order.

### Mention 4 — Our Business capacity cross-reference (page 190)
> "For details in relation to risk associated with Unit III please see, 'Risk Factors - We may not be able to expand our production capacity and products manufactured at Unit – III as GPCB has restricted any such expansion in the area where our Unit – III is situated i.e. Padra region, near Vadodara, Gujarat. We may therefore have to limit the use of this manufacturing facility.' on page 36."

### Answers, 1A items 1-8

**1. Exact wording of the restriction** — see Mentions 1-3 above (quoted in
full).

**2. Which section** — Risk Factors (Risk Factor 16, pages 42-43) and Our
Business (Capacity and Capacity Utilisation, page 190). NOT in Outstanding
Litigation. NOT as an order/notice in Government and Other Approvals.

**3. Date of notice/order, reference/file number** — NOT DISCLOSED. The RHP
describes the restriction narratively. No notice date, order number, or file
reference is given for the restriction itself. The only GPCB reference numbers
in the document are the Consolidated Consent & Authorization (CC&A) numbers
for each unit (see item 5), which are consents to operate, not restriction
orders.

**4. Expansion-only or also existing operations?** — Operative words restrict
EXPANSION: "GPCB has restricted any such expansion" and "GPCB has restricted
the expansion of manufacturing units in the Padra region." A reduction/closure
of existing Unit III operations is stated only as a contingent future risk:
"In the event the restrictions from GPCB continue to subsist ... or any
directions to reduce the capacity or closure of Unit – III by GPCB." The
current product limitation is attributed to ZLD cost/viability, a company
decision, not a GPCB cap on existing output. No current GPCB cap on existing
Unit III operations is disclosed.

**5. Any quantitative cap (consented quantity, effluent limit, CTO volume)?**
— NOT DISCLOSED. The only Unit III quantity is installed capacity 1,098 TPA,
certified by the Chartered Engineer (page 190), not a GPCB-consented cap. No
effluent limit or consented production volume is stated. Government and Other
Approvals (page 310) lists Unit III's Consolidated Consent & Authorization
(CC&A) from GPCB, "Consent Order & Authorization No. AWH-128299," issued
August 9, 2023, expiry March 31, 2028 (under Water Act, Air Act, Hazardous
Waste Rules) — with NO quantitative figures given. (Unit I: AWH-107799, April
16, 2020, expiry Dec 31, 2024. Unit II: AWH-116715, Jan 21, 2022, expiry Sept
30, 2026.)

**6. Timeline, condition precedent, compliance, appeal, remediation** —
> "We are therefore limited to the manufacturing of only certain products that are viable in this situation till we have further clarity from GPCB going forward." (page 42)

Primary effluent treatment plant and ZLD are already installed. No appeal, no
remediation timeline, no compliance deadline is disclosed beyond "further
clarity from GPCB going forward."

**7. What happens to Unit III once Dahej is built (continue/relocate/reduce/
close/surrender)?** — NOT DISCLOSED. The RHP does not state that Unit III will
continue, relocate, reduce, close, or surrender its consent once Dahej is
built. Dahej is framed as expansion that "will add more capacity" (page 32).
The only reduction/closure language is the contingent GPCB risk in Mention 3.

**8. Dahej as ADDITIONAL or REPLACEMENT capacity — Objects vs Our Business:**
- **Objects of the Offer (pages 88-91):** SILENT. This is a pure Offer for
  Sale. "The Objects of the Offer are to: (i) carry out the Offer for Sale of
  95,70,000 Equity Shares* aggregating to ₹ 1,301.52 million* by the Promoter
  Selling Shareholders; and (ii) achieve the benefits of listing ... Our
  Company will not receive any proceeds from the Offer." (page 88). Dahej is
  not an object of the issue; no capacity is described here.
- **Our Business (pages 32, 181):** ADDITIONAL. "We plan to establish our new
  unit at this location which will add more capacity and will be used to
  manufacture High Purity Speciality Fine Chemicals" (page 32). Section
  heading page 181: "Expand our capacity for existing products and diversifying
  into new products by setting up a new manufacturing facility." "We plan to
  establish new unit at this location to manufacture High Purity Speciality
  Fine Chemicals from our existing product portfolio as well as new products."
- **FLAG:** the two do not conflict on framing; they differ in that Objects is
  entirely silent because the company raises no primary capital (pure OFS).
  There is no Objects-of-the-Issue capacity framing to compare against Our
  Business. Dahej is funded by "equity and/or debt and/or the internal
  accruals," not IPO proceeds (page 32, page 181).
