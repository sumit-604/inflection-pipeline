## The engine the thesis would rest on

A lab roll-up plus a capex build in Indian TIC, where demand is pulled by
regulation (BIS/QCO certification, automotive and EV homologation, FSSAI food
testing, pharma export testing). The bull reading: a young, accredited
multi-vertical network compounds at 35-40% with 85%+ gross margin, and
operating leverage on new labs lifts EBITDA toward 26% and ROCE well above
12%. The bear reading: growth is bought (acquisitions and capex funded by
debt, a share swap and a fresh preferential issue), receivables run 120-150
days, and FCF stays negative. The observation that separates them: organic
growth of the standalone and like-for-like labs, with CFO/EBITDA and debtor
days, in the H1 FY27 print.
