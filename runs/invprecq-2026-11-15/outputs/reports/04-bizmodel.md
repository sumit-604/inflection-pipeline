## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

### 1A One line
I&PCL makes metal parts to a customer's drawing by pouring molten metal into a ceramic mould built around a wax copy, then machines and inspects most of them, mostly for car, tractor and engine makers (IP p.4; AR p.172-173).

### 1E Chai-stall version
Think of a pressure-cooker maker who forms each cooker part by pouring hot metal into a clay mould that cracks open afterwards. I&PCL does that for engine and turbine parts. Cars and tractors are the daily customers, and they pay a fair price but squeeze hard. Aeroplane and defence customers audit the shop for months before they buy. After that they keep ordering the same part for years. The owner now has spare machines (about 60% busy, IP p.17), so each extra order adds profit fast. Risks: a lot of metal sits half-made in the shop, and a loan funds that stock.
