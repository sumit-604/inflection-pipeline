Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of ASM Technologies Ltd (ASMTL) to ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ from ‘Crisil BBB-/Stable/Crisil A3’.

 

The upgrade reflects the improvement in the company’s overall credit profile, which is expected to sustain over the medium term.  The operating income grew to Rs 289 crore in fiscal 2025 from Rs 203 crore in fiscal 2024, driven by increase in the share of income from existing customers, addition of new clients, and rise in design-led manufacturing business. The revenue is expected to increase steadily over the medium term, aided by expectation of an increase in business from all the segments. The operating margin also improved to 17% in fiscal 2025 from 4.16% in fiscal 2024, resulting in improvement in net cash accrual to Rs 32 crore. This was due to better absorption of fixed costs, resulting from ramp up in operations and share of design-led manufacturing business. The margin is expected to sustain at similar levels over the medium term.

 

The financial risk profile remained comfortable, backed by comfortable networth and capital structure. The interest coverage ratio also improved to 4.6 times in fiscal 2025 from 1.01 times in fiscal 2024 with higher operating profit. The financial risk profile is expected to stay comfortable with heathy accretion to reserves, despite debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) plans over the medium term. 

 

The ratings reflect the extensive experience of the promoters, diversified service offerings supporting scale and providing sustainability, and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital cycle, sizeable fixed costs, vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuations in foreign exchange (forex) rates, and customer concentration in revenue.

Analytical approach

Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of ASMTL and its subsidiaries, which are strategically important to, and have a significant degree of operational integration with, ASMTL.

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description

Strengths:

Extensive experience of the promoters: The promoters have more than two decades of experience in the information technology (IT) industry. The key promoter and managing director, Mr Rabindra Srikantan, has an MS degree in computer engineering and computer science from the University of Louisiana, the USA.

 

Diversified service offerings support scale and provide sustainability: The company is an established player in the internet software and services industry. Clientele is strong and spread across the semiconductor equipment, network devices, hi-tech, medical equipment, automotive and aerospace segments, which protects against drop in demand from any one industry. Furthermore, the share of manufacturing business increased in fiscal 2025, led by increase in business of manufacturing  machinery used in the manufacture of electronic parts. This is expected to grow steadily over the medium term. The group offers consulting services in engineering and product research and development. It is present across India, the United States, Singapore, the United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico and Japan.

 

Comfortable financial risk profile: The networth improved to Rs 137 crore as on March 31, 2025 from Rs 119 crore as on 31st March 2024 driven by steady accretion to reserve and despite losses reported in overseas subsidiaries due to expansion and build-up capacity and initial phase of operations. Gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio remained comfortable at 0.56 time and 0.80 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics also improved due to higher profitability, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 4.6 times and 0.43 time, respectively, for fiscal  2025, against 1 time and nil, respectively, previous fiscal. The financial risk profile is expected to remain at comfortable over the medium term with gradual repayment of term loans and healthy accretion to reserve amidst debt-funded capex plans.

 

Weaknesses:

Large working capital cycle: Gross current assets remained high at about 205 days as on March 31, 2025, due to higher receivables and large inventory of 115 days and 87 days, respectively. The receivables level was high at balance sheet date due to higher revenue booking in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Payables were also high at 139 days. With increasing ramp up in operations, incremental working capital requirement and its management will remain monitorable.

 

Sizeable fixed cost, susceptibility to employee attrition and vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuation in forex rates: Most of the expenses are fixed cost in nature (employee costs and rentals), making it susceptible to the quantum of work received and subsequently, the level of billing. Variation in operating margin depends on the nature of contracts awarded. Operations are also susceptible to employee attrition, which is typical of the  IT/IT-enabled services industry. Furthermore, as 25-30% of revenue comes from the international market, any sharp fluctuation in forex rates can adversely affect realisations and cash accrual.

 

Customer concentration in revenue profile: The top 5 customers account for 60-70% of the total revenue. This exposes the group’s revenue growth and profitability to its key customers’ growth plans. However, the group added new clients in fiscal 2025 which, along with expected organic acquisitions over the medium term, is likely to mitigate customer concentration in revenue profile.

Liquidity: Adequate

Bank limit utilisation averaged around 85% for the 12 months through April 2025. Expected annual cash accrual  of Rs 35-45 crore will be sufficient to meet yearly term debt obligation of Rs 10-14 crore, over the medium term. The cash & and bank balance and current ratio remained at around Rs 13 crore and 1.89 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025.

Outlook: Stable

The company will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and established relationships with clients.

Rating Sensitivity Factors

Upward factors

  • Steady improvement in revenue and sustenance of operating margin (over 15%), leading to cash accrual of over Rs 40 crore on a sustained basis
  • Improvement in working capital management, financial risk profile and liquidity

 

Downward factors

  • Decline in profitability or operating income resulting in cash accrual below Rs 20 crore
  • Large, debt-funded capex or stretch in working capital cycle weakening liquidity and financial risk profile

About the Company

Incorporated in 1992 by Mr Rabindra Srikantan, ASM Technologies Limited is a publicly listed company in India. With over three decades of experience, ASM has been supporting customers in the areas of Engineering Design led Manufacturing. ASM has multiple delivery locations in India with a global presence in USA, Singapore, UK, Canada, Japan, Thailand and Mexico.

 

The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
