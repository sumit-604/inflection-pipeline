**This is the single most decision-relevant reframe this stage adds to B02.** B02 correctly flags OCF turning sharply negative as a RED cash-quality signal — the fact is accurate. But the mechanism matters: **Operating Profit before Working Capital Changes rose 56.2% YoY** (Rs33.92 Cr → Rs52.97 Cr), broadly tracking the 48% PAT growth and 55% EBITDA growth the MD's letter claims. The entire negative-OCF outcome is attributable to the working-capital movement layer — inventory +Rs43.31 Cr, receivables +Rs21.31 Cr, loans/advances +Rs12.01 Cr, other current assets +Rs5.40 Cr, partially offset by trade payables +Rs8.52 Cr and other current liabilities +Rs3.48 Cr (Cash Flow Statement, p.52). Per Amendment 17's spirit (never read a converter's rupee WC swing as a standalone quality signal without spread/utilisation context): this WC build is **growth-induced** — 100% raw-material inventory (see 2E) ahead of the Unit-3 ramp, and a receivables build that is 90% non-related-party and 99.5% current by ageing. It is **not** evidence of margin decay or channel-stuffing on the scale the headline number might suggest.

That said, three qualifications keep this a genuine WATCH rather than a clean pass:
1. FY24's own OCF/PAT of 213% was itself inflated by a large inventory **unwind** (FY23's WIP of Rs35.52 Cr collapsing to Rs7.32 Cr by FY24-end) — i.e., cash generation has been **lumpy and WC-driven in both directions** across all three years, not a steady compounding pattern.
2. The FY25 WC build was partly funded by IPO proceeds sitting temporarily in bank/FD (Rs45.02 Cr unutilized, Note 45) rather than by operating cash discipline — the "net debt-free" optics partly reflect this cash cushion, not organic WC efficiency.
3. CFO quality checks: **no** interest-classification games (interest paid Rs149.93 lakh sits cleanly in financing activities, consistent both years); **no** payable-stretching (payables actually grew, a source of cash, appropriately disclosed); **no** unsustainable inventory rundown (the opposite — a build). The one-time inflator present is the Rs798.99 lakh IPO-deposit interest sitting in Other Income within "Operating Profit before WC Changes" — this modestly overstates the "core" operating cash-generation improvement narrative above; excluding it, core operating profit before WC still grew a healthy ~40-45%.
