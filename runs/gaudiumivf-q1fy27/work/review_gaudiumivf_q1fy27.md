### Six diagnostic answers

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes. Standalone +11.4% (12.27→13.68), consolidated +9.1% (17.76→19.38). Modest single-digit-to-low-double-digit growth. No Notion base case exists to compare against (pre-position name).
2. **Margin expand, contract, or flat?** Contract, severely. Standalone operating EBITDA margin **15.89% Q1FY27 vs 33.86% Q1FY26 = −1,797 bps**. Consolidated **12.51% vs 29.02% = −1,651 bps**. This is the headline of the quarter.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow?** No — it **fell 61.8% standalone (3.08→1.18)** and **67.2% consolidated (4.05→1.33)** while revenue rose ~10%. Reported PAT fell only 29% (SA) / 42% (consol) because Rs 1.12 Cr of new other income (FD interest on IPO cash) plugged the gap. **The headline PAT decline understates the core-operations decline. The reported number is cushioned by transient treasury income.** (A3-F1b.)
4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT and reported-PAT?** Standalone PAT bridge Q1FY26→Q1FY27, ΔPAT = −0.69 Cr: operating EBITDA −1.98, plus other income +1.12, minus depreciation −0.10, plus lower finance cost +0.17, plus lower tax +0.09. The +1.12 other income (FD interest, `ledger_results` Note 1e L297) and +0.17 finance-cost relief are what convert a −1.98 operating hit into only a −0.69 PAT hit.
5. **Are D&A / finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** Depreciation +16.4% standalone (+18.7% consol) vs revenue +11.4% — a mild absorption gap consistent with pre-operational new hubs (Delhi South Extension, Gurgaon/Nagpur under construction) carrying depreciation before generating revenue. Finance costs FELL 35% (debt repaid from IPO proceeds), a genuine positive.
6. **Is other-income concentration changing?** Dramatically. Other income / PBT jumped from 0% (Q1FY26) to **48.9%** (Q1FY27). This is IPO-cash FD interest (Rs 102.24 L, `ledger_results` L297; confirmed by monitoring FD schedule, `ledger_monitoring` L568-597). It **decays as capex deploys** — a self-liquidating income source. Strip it and the operating decline is fully visible.
