**Six mandatory diagnostics:**

Billable transactions rose 39.3 -> 45.8 bn (+16.5%, deck L392), so **revenue grew slower than volume** — realization per transaction fell from ~Rs 0.267 to ~Rs 0.251 per txn (−6%), a pricing/mix-compression tell that matches the gross-margin decline.

2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand, contract, or stay flat YoY?** On the **reported** basis it expanded +22 bps (8.94% -> 9.16%) — but this is an artifact. On the **forex-neutral adjusted** basis it CONTRACTED **−150 bps (11.0% -> 9.5%)**. Gross margin contracted −54 bps (21.42% -> 20.88%). The honest read is **margin contraction**; the reported expansion exists only because Q1 FY26 opex carried a Rs 24.73 Cr forex loss (note C-6, L462) that did not recur.

BUT reversing the forex reclassification kills most of it: Q1 FY26 opex included Rs 24.73 Cr forex loss; ex-forex Q1 FY26 opex was Rs 37.85 Cr vs Q1 FY27 opex Rs 57.34 Cr (with the 0.61 gain sitting in OI, not opex) — i.e. **ex-forex other expenses ROSE ~Rs 19.5 Cr (+51%)** YoY, absorbing the finance-cost and volume benefit. Headline core-PBT growth is not clean operational improvement.

4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT growth and reported PAT growth?** PBT change +Rs 14.90 Cr decomposes to: gross profit +15.37; employee cost −9.03; other expenses +5.24 (of which ~Rs 25 Cr is the forex swing offsetting a ~Rs 19.5 Cr real cost rise); D&A −1.23; finance costs +4.46; other income +0.09. Tax rose Rs 5.13 Cr (ETR 23.23% -> 25.06%, i.e. the abnormally low base normalised). Net PAT +9.77. **The recurring, clean drivers are the finance-cost drop (+4.46) and volume (+21.57 gross-up); everything else is forex base effect and cost inflation.**

5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** No absorption gap: D&A +5.47% (below revenue +9.58%); finance costs −76.63%. No capex-absorption warning. This is an asset-light platform, not a capex-commissioning story.

6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Consol OI flat YoY (10.97 -> 11.06), 12.09% of PBT — no treasury-timing distortion at consol level. At **standalone** level OI is 93.68% of PBT (Rs 18.97 Cr of Rs 20.25 Cr PBT), so the parent is almost entirely dependent on intra-group dividend (Rs 4.22 Cr, note S-7) + treasury for its profit. See Step 4/S-vs-C.

**Step 2 verdict: revenue grew genuinely; profitability deteriorated once forex noise is reversed. The reported YoY PAT growth of +16.6% is low-quality — driven by a forex base effect and a finance-cost drop, not by operating margin.**
