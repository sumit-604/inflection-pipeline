estimation risk given no independent market check.
FVTPL fixed maturity plan book (Note 6b, p.117-119) totals ₹197.05cr, a long

## NOTE 20: TAX EXPENSE AND DEFERRED TAX LIABILITY (p.127-128)
🟡 Effective tax rate: 98.85/501.33 = 19.72% vs statutory rate 25.17% (Note
20.1.3, p.127) — the FY19 comparative effective rate was 187.77/531.86 =

36). Investors extrapolating PAT growth without adjusting for this
regime-switch effect would overstate the earnings trend. Deferred tax
liability (net) fell from ₹58.34cr to ₹36.06cr accordingly (Note 20.2,
p.128). No MAT credit entries are disclosed — NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: any MAT
