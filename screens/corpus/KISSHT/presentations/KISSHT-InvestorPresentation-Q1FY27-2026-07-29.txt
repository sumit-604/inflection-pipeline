===== PAGE 11: products =====
Personal Loans (PL): "Digital-first unsecured lending platform focused on speed, convenience and
repeat customer engagement." Focus segments salaried and self-employed. Operating model: zero
paperwork, instant decisioning, minimal documentation. Ticket size up to Rs 5 Lakhs, tenure up to 5
years. AUM Rs 7,384 Cr, 92.3% of total AUM, 100% digital, 17,000+ pin codes.
Loan Against Property (LAP): "Secured lending platform expanding portfolio granularity with
longer-tenure and lower-risk assets." Focus segments residential and commercial property owners,
MSMEs, self-employed borrowers. Operating model: digital lead generation, model-based underwriting,
branch-led fulfilment. Ticket size up to Rs 15 Lakhs, tenure up to 10 years. AUM Rs 617 Cr, 7.7% of
total AUM, 48% LTV.
"101 LAP branches across 8 states & UTs": Maharashtra 26, Telangana 24, Tamil Nadu & Puducherry 18,
Uttar Pradesh & Uttarakhand 18, Gujarat 10, Andhra Pradesh 5.

"On-book AUM: Aggregate of current principal outstanding and overdue principal outstanding for all
loans originated and held by Company's subsidiary NBFC."
"Off-book AUM: Aggregate of current principal outstanding and overdue principal outstanding for all
loans originated by subsidiary NBFC and assigned/co-lent to partner banks/NBFCs (100%, co-lending or
DA)."
"PCR = Provision Coverage Ratio = ECL Stage 3 Provisions / Stage 3 Gross Exposure."
"RoAAUM = PAT / Avg AUM." "RoAE = PAT / Avg Net worth."
"FLDG = First Loss Default Guarantee = Guaranteed Amount / Total Loan Portfolio x 100."
