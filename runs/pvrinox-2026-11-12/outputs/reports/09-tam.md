## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

1A. Boundaries.
- Product: the four streams a multiplex chain earns from a seat-filling visit. Tickets (53.1% of FY26 revenue), F&B (31.4%), advertising (7.0%), convenience fees (3.6%). Total 95.1% of Rs 66,462 Mn = Rs 63,208 Mn = Rs 6,321 Cr (B04 mix; revenue Rs 66,462 Mn per B03/AR consolidated P&L, as carried).
- Excluded: film production and distribution (3.3%), virtual print fees (0.9%), other (0.6%). These are 4.9% of revenue, not exhibition demand.
- Geography: India (10,003 screens). Sri Lanka (Colombo, 9 screens of 1,798) is inside company revenue and not separated; immaterial, flagged.
- Customer: cinema-going public plus advertisers.
- Channel: physical cinema; online booking (72.0% of tickets in Q2 FY27, Deck p.15 note 2) sits inside the pool.
- Price segment: all. Premium formats are about 16% of PVR screens (B04).
- Basis: ticket revenue is stated net of tax and is what the exhibitor books. Film hire (about 44.5-45.3% of tickets) stays inside the pool because PVR books ticket sales gross of it.

1B. Management's TAM claims.
- Claim 1 (AR [page 82], printed p.91): India has about 10,003 screens, 7 per million; China 64, UK 66, France 95, US 109. "Half of China's density needs approximately 45,000 screens, four times the base." Credibility: BROAD. It is a screen count, not a rupee market. Check: 1,450 Mn people (AR) x 32 per million = 46,400, so the 45,000 ties. But it is "4.5x", not "four times".
- Claim 2 (Deck p.25-26; Nov 2026 call): SMART Cinemas, about 300 cities and about 1,000 screens. Credibility: SPECIFIC but UNPROVEN. One cinema (4 screens) is open (B07 FLAG-SMART-UNPROVEN). Unit economics NOT FOUND.
- Claim 3 (AR [page 82]): 16,350 pin codes have no screen. Corroborated by web: 3,150 of 19,500 pin codes have a cinema, so 16,350 non-screened (secondary blog repost of FICCI-EY, treat as medium).
- Claim 4 (AR printed p.91): "Box office compounded 7-8% over the last decade." Tested in Section 2: the AR's own chart does not give 7-8% over ten years.
- Management gives no rupee TAM. For the Section 2 ratio only, the implied rupee value of Claim 1 is derived: 45,000 screens x Rs 1.3515 Cr per screen (Rs 13,519 Cr FY26 box office / 10,003 screens) = Rs 60,817 Cr. Label: DERIVED, not management's figure.

5B. Runway.
- Headroom = SAM / current revenue = 16,456 / 6,321 = 2.60x. Most of that is competitors' revenue.
- TAM growth: 4.9% central from FY28 (8.0% reading); FY27 floor +10.2%.
- Company vs market: H1 FY27 PVR four-stream +16.4% vs SAM +22%. PVR is riding the market and slightly behind it, not gaining share.
- Years to saturate SAM: at H1 pace of 16.4% vs 22% there is no catch-up. At the FY27 floor market growth of 10.2% and a 16.4% company rate, 100% share would take ln(2.60) / ln(1.164/1.102) = 0.9555 / 0.0547 = 17.5 years. Not meaningful: share is already 38% and a share cap near 50% is the practical limit.

5C. Class: MODERATE. Rule applied (the file gives no thresholds; stated so it can be rejected): headroom 2-4x and market growth above 8% with proven share gain would grade GOOD. Here headroom is 2.6x, central market growth is 4.9% from FY28, and share gain is not evidenced. A premium-format and Tier 3-4 story is a lever, not yet a result.

5D. SAM expansion levers PVR is pursuing.
- SMART Cinemas, about 1,000 screens in about 300 cities. Indicative ticket-only value if each screen fills like the industry average (83.2k admissions) at ATP about Rs 190 (Nov 2026 call via B07): 1,000 x 83.2k x Rs 190 = Rs 1,581 Cr. That is 81% of the whole derived single-screen pool (Rs 1,956 Cr) held by 5,200 screens, so SMART would have to earn 4.2x a single screen's ticket revenue (1.58 vs 0.376 Cr per screen). Confidence L. SMART unit economics NOT FOUND. Revised SAM headroom if fully realised: (16,456 + 1,581) / 6,321 = 2.85x.
- Alternate content, loyalty and digital ads (B07 optionality register): no disclosed rupee value. NOT FOUND, not added.
