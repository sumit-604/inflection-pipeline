Accord Transformer & Switchgear Receives Vendor Approval from  
Aditya Birla Renewables  
Strengthening Presence in India's Renewable Energy Sector 
   
Gurugram, July 06, 2026 – Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited, one of India's leading manufacturers 
of transformers and switchgear solutions, has received Vendor Approval from Aditya Birla Renewables  
Limited for the supply of transformers for its renewable energy projects. This approval marks anot her 
significant milestone in the Company's growth journey and further reinforces its position as a trusted partner 
in India's fast-growing renewable energy ecosystem. 
 
Aditya Birla Renewables, a part of the Aditya Birla Group, is focused on developing and operating large-scale 
renewable energy projects across India. The vendor approval enables Accord Transformer & Switchgear 
Limited to participate in opportunities for supplying transformers across the company's renewable energy 
portfolio, subject to project requirements and applicable commercial terms. 
 
The approval follows a comprehensive evaluation of the Company's manufacturing capabilities, quality 
management systems, technical expertise, and operational standards. It reflects the confidence placed by 
Aditya Birla Renewables in Accord's ability to deliver high-quality, reliable, and efficient transformer solutions 
for clean energy infrastructure. 
 
This development further expands the Company's approved customer base in the renewable energy segment 
and creates new opportunities to support India's energy transition through participation in utility -scale solar, 
wind, and hybrid power projects. 
 
Mr. Pradeep Kumar Verma, Chairman & Managing Director, Accord Transformer & Switchgear 
Limited, commented, "The vendor approval from Aditya Birla Renewables is an important milestone for 
our Company and reflects the strength of our manufacturing capabilities, quality standards, and 
commitment to delivering reliable transformer solutions. As India accelerates its transition towards clean 
energy, we are pleased to partner with one of the country's leading renewable energy developers. We 
believe this approval will further strengthen our presence in the renewable energy sector and support our 
long-term growth strategy.” 
 
The approval aligns with the Company's strategic focus on expanding its presence across renewable energy 
developers, power utilities, industrial infrastructure, and transmission & distribution projects while continuing 
to deliver reliable, efficient, and future-ready electrical solutions. 
 
 
About Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited: 
Accord Transformer &  Switchgear Limited is a leading provider of comprehensive energy solutions, 
specializing in the design, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning of transformers, substations, and 
switchgear systems. With advanced manufacturing facilities in Bhiwadi and a strong presence across power 
transmission, renewable energy, and industrial sectors, the Company is committed to delivering reliable, 
efficient, and future-ready infrastructure solutions.
