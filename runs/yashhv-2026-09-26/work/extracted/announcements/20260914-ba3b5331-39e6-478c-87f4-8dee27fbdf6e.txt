Yash Highvoltage’s subsidiary YASH HV USA INC. Expands US Market Footprint 
through Strategic Agreement with ENSALES ELECTRICAL ASSOCIATES, INC. 
 
 The 36-month agreement covers sales representation and utility homologation and 
approvals across 19 US states 
 The partnership comes as Yash Highvoltage® continues to expand its global footprint 
and build capabilities across advanced bushing technologies 
 
Vadodara, 14th September 2026 – Yash Highvoltage Limited (“Yash®” or the “Company”), a 
leading manufacturer of condenser graded transformer bushings for the power generation and  
transmission sector, announced that its subsidiary, YASH HV USA INC., has entered into a 
Representation Agreement with ENSALES ELECTRICAL ASSOCIATES, INC., headquartered in 
North Carolina, USA, with sales oƯices in several US states.  
 
The agreement is eƯective from 11 September 2026 and will remain valid for 36 months (three 
years) from the date of execution. The partnership is aimed at strengthening Yash Highvoltage’s 
engagement with utilities and customers in the US by creating a stronger platform for the 
company’s global growth. 
 
The agreement covers a portfolio of Yash Highvoltage’s transformer bushings, including Low 
Voltage (LV) High Current Bushings up to 25,000 Amps and Oil-Impregnated Paper (OIP) 
Condenser Bushings up to 245 kV, including Oil-Air, Oil-Oil and Air-Air conﬁguration types. It 
also covers Resin Impregnated Paper (RIP) and Resin Impregnated Synthetic (RIS) Condenser 
Bushings up to 230 kV, available from 2027 onwards. The company’s portfolio of RIP , RIS and OIP 
technologies positions it to address evolving requirements across power generation and 
transmission infrastructure. Additional products can be incorporated into the agreement through 
mutual written consent. 
 
Speaking on this development, Mr. Keyur Shah, Chairman & Managing Director, Yash 
Highvoltage Limited, said: “The US represents an important strategic market for Yash 
Highvoltage®, and this partnership marks a signiﬁcant step in strengthening our engagement with 
utilities and customers across the country. Ensales brings valuable market representation 
capabilities and an established presence across multiple US states. Beyond sales development, 
the focus on utility homologation and approvals will help us build a stronger foundation for 
sustained participation in the US power infrastructure ecosystem. As we expand our portfolio of 
advanced RIP , RIS and OIP technologies, we see signiﬁcant potential to serve the evolving needs 
of the global grid. ” 
 
Under the agreement, Ensales Electrical Associates, Inc. has been appointed as a Sales 
Representative of the Company. Its mandate includes promoting, marketing and representing 
the sales of the Company’s products, along with driving utility homologation & approvals for the 
Company’s products within the 19 territories.
