## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the transcripts alone)

Each item anchored (call, speaker, page). Classification vs pipeline follows in Part 2.

**IF-1. Organic growth deceleration masked by inorganic headline.**
Organic constant-currency YoY growth fell from 18.3% (Q3, Manish, p.2: "excluding BioPharm... 18.3%
year-on-year and 5.9% quarter-on-quarter") to 12% (Q4, Yash Mehta Q&A, Suhas, p.14: "12% organic and
a little north of 3% QoQ"), while the reported Q4 headline accelerated to 32.8% YoY (Q4, Suhas, p.8)
on BioPharm/Cake/Warn consolidation. The 12% figure was volunteered only under direct analyst
questioning, never in prepared remarks. Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-2. FY26 headline growth is partly a bookings story recognized in FY27.**
The marquee wins land in FY27: $10mn+ ACV top-10 pharma omnichannel "revenues will start accruing
from Q2 FY27" (Q3, Manish, p.2); $20mn TCV biotech "$5 million of revenues in the first 12 months"
(Q3, p.3); Germany Tectonic "latter half of FY27 will see this deal converting to revenue" (Q4,
Manish, p.3); Q3-won $10mn ACV deal "entirely recognized in FY27" because outcome-priced (Q4, Suhas,
p.14). Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-3. Standalone vs consolidated PAT divergence never discussed.**
Consolidated FY26 PAT declined 1.4% to Rs4,011mn on +23.6% revenue (Q4, Suhas, p.8). Management never
frames results standalone-vs-consolidated, never isolates core-India profitability from acquired-
subsidiary drag. Genuine disclosure gap across all four calls (NOT FOUND). Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-4. Margin-recovery specificity softened across quarters.**
Q2: "around 20% and a little north of that" over 6-8 quarters (Q2, Suhas, p.11). Q3: "return
approximately to 20% over the next 6 to 8 quarters," sequential improvement from Q1 FY27 (Q3, Suhas,
p.5). Q4: the "20%" number is dropped: "second half of FY27 will see us revert to the earlier levels
of higher margins" (Q4, Suhas, p.10). Reinforced by Manish capping the ceiling: "we're not saying
that our margins will expand beyond what they used to be... anything above that range... we are going
to reinvest" (Q4, Prolin Nandu Q&A, p.13). Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-5. $10mn+ revenue client cohort flat at 10 accounts YoY.**
Surfaced only when asked: "more than $10 million revenues, there are 10 clients which are same as
compared to last year" (Q4, Prakash Kapadia Q&A, p.11), answered candidly ("one of our clients was
just tad below that $10 million"). Not in any prepared remarks. Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

**IF-6. BioPharm consolidated at GROSS revenue inflates headline growth and dilutes blended margin.**
BioPharm CY24 gross revenue $38.1mn vs net $29.2mn; the ~$9mn gap is "largely media buying expenses...
pass-through" (Oct meet, Suhas, p.6). Management confirmed it will "report gross revenues" per
accounting standards (Q2, Sameer Dosani Q&A, Suhas, p.12). BioPharm did $10.3mn standalone in Q3 (Q3,
Suhas, p.7) and grew ~15% sequentially in Q4 (Q4, Suhas, p.10). Consequence: a slice of the
accelerating INR headline is zero-margin pass-through media grossed up, which simultaneously flatters
YoY growth and drags reported blended margin, feeding the very "150bps compression" narrative.
Never flagged as a revenue-quality item. Severity: MAJOR (modest magnitude, ~3% of revenue, but
directly on the operator's inorganic-masking probe). **This is the one clean MISS.**

**IF-7. BioPharm was a 3-year-stagnant asset, yet the earn-out underwrites 15-20% growth.**
Management volunteered that BioPharm revenue had been "stagnating around $40 million gross revenue"
for three years, "run primarily for margins" by Omnicom, "not a great job was done before" (Oct meet,
Sankaranarayanan Q&A, Manish, p.8). The earn-out requires "15% to 20% growth year-on-year... for the
full payout" (Oct meet, Ahmed Madha Q&A, Suhas, p.8), and margin normalization to 27-28% assumes a
turnaround. Underwriting a step-change on a demonstrably flat asset is an integration/execution risk.
Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-8. Reported-to-adjusted PAT bridge leans on a retroactively-applied hedge reclassification.**
Reported FY26 PAT -1.4% (Rs4,011mn) is presented alongside an adjusted PAT of Rs4,583mn, +12.7% (Q4,
Suhas, p.8). The bridge uses (a) the Rs203mn TCPA add-back and (b) an Ind AS 109 cash-flow-hedge
reclassification effective Jan 2026 that was applied RETROACTIVELY "as if the contracts... between
April 2025 and December 2025 were also considered as cash flow hedges," removing a Rs241mn FX charge
from adjusted EBITDA (Q4, Suhas, p.8). A retroactively-applied accounting change plus add-backs
converts a headline PAT decline into double-digit "adjusted" growth. Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-9. Cake Kommunikations disclosed with no deal value, multiple, or integration timeline.**
Single mention: "now you can understand why we did Cake" (Q4, Manish, p.6), unlike BioPharm (9.6-9.8x
EBITDA, Oct meet, p.6) and Warn (GBP2mn upfront, Q2, p.9). Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

**IF-10. Numeric deflection under margin-math pressure (Q2).**
Sameer Dosani pressed for a clean base to model the 1.5% hit; the CFO never confirmed a number and
left the analyst to derive 17.7% himself ("From the current quarter, these would be likely impacting
us on a go-forward basis rather than maybe the cumulative") (Q2, p.11-12). Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

**IF-11. Margin recovery is a repeated question answered with shifting/softening specifics (2+ qtrs).**
Asked every quarter (Q2 Ahmed/Sameer/Divyansh; Q3 Kawaljeet/Siddharth/Mohammed; Q4 Prolin), the
target/date eroded from an explicit "~20% / 6-8 quarters" to a numberless "earlier levels / H2 FY27."
A 2+ quarter softening pattern. Severity: MAJOR.

**IF-12. Tectonic disclosure cadence lapsed in Q3.**
Precisely tracked in Q2 ("$2 million in H1... from 4 customers," p.5; Suhas p.14), then NO customer-
count or revenue figure in Q3 prepared remarks (only platform description, p.4), returning in Q4 (5
customers, 2 scaled, p.6). Severity: MINOR.

**IF-13. TCPA class-action tail.**
Rs203mn provision; mediation "close to completion... court approval is still awaited"; self-assessed
"we do not believe that there are any further such liability exposures" (Q4, Suhas, p.8-9). Severity:
MINOR-MAJOR.

**IF-14. Q4 tone shift markedly promotional, with aspirational leaps from a flat base.**
Q4 opening is qualitatively different from Q2/Q3: "category-defining," "industry's new default,"
"3 months to 3 days," plus "hoping this customer is going to cross over and become our first $50
million-plus customer" (p.3) and "$100 million clients in some years" (p.15) — asserted even though
the largest customer was "broadly stable... a bit of a hold" in FY26 (p.3) and the $10mn+ cohort is
stuck at 10. Severity: MINOR.

**IF-15. Renewal price concessions = early AI-pricing-pressure inside Indegene's own book.**
"We proactively approach them with our Gen AI-enabled solutions and have been successful in offering
certain benefits on price in lieu of volume or scope expansion" (Q3, Suhas, p.5, reiterated p.9).
Framed as productivity-sharing upside; it is also the first sign of AI-driven price give-back in the
company's own renewals. Severity: MINOR.

**IF-16. Q3 "the 150bps is already behind us / won't enhance" assurance vs continued non-recovery.**
"At the cost of repetition, these have already impacted us in Q3. These are not costs that will
enhance in the future. This is already in the past" (Q3, Mohammed Patel Q&A, Suhas, p.15) — while the
same call disclosed NEW "elevated costs related to upfront investments and go-live costs in large
deals" (p.4) and recovery was still pushed to H2 FY27 by Q4. Severity: MINOR.

**IF-17. Cash-conversion headline mechanically inflated by rising non-cash amortization.**
OCF/PAT of 154% (Q3, Kawaljeet Q&A, p.8) and 162% for FY26 (Q4, p.8) are, on management's own words,
"higher due to the higher non-cash expenses and amortization charges" (D&A rose Rs234mn Q2 to Rs396mn
Q3, Q3 p.6). The underlying DSO improvement (72 to 63 days, Q4 p.8) is genuine, but the headline ratio
overstates pure working-capital quality. Severity: MINOR.

Independent flags found: **17**.
