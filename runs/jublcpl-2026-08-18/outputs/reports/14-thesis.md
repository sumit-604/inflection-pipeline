## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy — the polymer claim)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** The retained business earns 67.5% ROCE at segment level and 36% at group level (deliberation; AR p.7). Gate 0 core is 71/100 with moat STRONG and no deal-breakers (B01). The balance sheet is near debt-free with a war-chest emerging moat rated Strong/documented (B07 G1). This is a genuinely high-return, low-leverage business — the quality is real. The one counterweight the numbers carry: FY26 standalone CFO/PAT fell to 0.59x from 1.49x in FY25 on a 40.4% rise in trade receivables (B01 block_b_trend) — but the deliberation locates that drag in the demerging agri half, not the retained polymer entity, which is why Pillar 2 stayed at 1.15x. That location is a HYPOTHESIS until the first standalone accounts confirm it (see Section 6).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, B05):**
1. Adhesives double-digit growth + margin expansion — conviction **H**. Q1 FY27 adhesives +19% revenue / +40% EBIT (valuation-sotp). 5-year adhesive CAGR ~35% FY20-25 vs Pidilite 13.5% (MNCL broker lead, non-anchored, market context only). Confirms: continued double-digit growth Q2 FY27. Kills: deceleration below high-single-digit.
2. Agri Division demerger / JASL listing — conviction **M-H**. Every regulatory gate cleared on time through Aug-2026; meetings 5-Sep-2026 then NCLT final order. Confirms: NCLT sanction + JASL listing. Kills: scheme withdrawal / adverse creditor vote / prolonged delay.
3. Samlaya full-capacity ramp — conviction **M**. Partial commercial start 3-Jun-2026 (Reg 30), full ramp ~6-8 months out. **SHARED CATALYST** — see Section 6.
4. Phase-2 SBR latex / construction-chemicals entry — conviction **M**, target end Q3 FY27. Kills: delay beyond Q3 FY27.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Backward moat STRONG (B01); forward scan MODEST 22.5, dominated by balance-sheet strength and incremental execution, not category-defining expansion (B07). Strategic scarcity is genuine but narrow: #1 India / #2 global-ex-China in VP latex, sole food-grade PVAc maker in India (valuation-sotp) — this earns the +2x strategic premium in Section 1B, nothing wider. The moat is stable-to-slowly-expanding, not a re-rating engine. Adhesives is a share-gain catch-up story (80,000 MTPA vs Pidilite 600,000-plus), not a mature-market defence — but catching a generational Fevicol brand is a low-base-rate bet (valuation-sotp).

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter verdict CAUTION (B08): four caution findings cluster on governance concentration — a Finance Committee with Rs 1,250 Cr borrowing authority and zero independent directors; KMP pay +63.6% YoY vs 9.8% average, 99.91x CEO:median; 100% of CSR routed through the promoter family foundation; a dated 2018 SEBI PIT fine (SAT-adjusted 2019) on the two founding promoters at a *different* group entity. Offset by real 2024-26 transition evidence: external professional CEO (Mohandeep Singh, ex-Samsung), independent-director demerger fairness review, clean Big-4-audited relationship, no ID exits, 0% pledge. Delivery grade **C — Mixed** (B05): Samlaya delivered exactly as guided with Reg 30 corroboration, agri inventory liquidation missed on monsoon (external-blame), demerger in-progress with no slippage. Excuse pattern is external-blame-heavy. Trustworthy enough not to force AVOID, not trustworthy enough to lift position size above Small.

**3E. Market Opportunity.** Retained PP&C TAM Rs 50,143-61,259 Cr, SAM Rs 47,132 Cr, current SAM share 2.47%, revenue headroom 40.5x, runway STRONG (B09). But the SOM-implied 24.3-24.4% CAGR sits well above the audited 7.48-8.75% FY25-26 PP&C growth; the Q1 FY27 27.5% print is one quarter, not a confirmed year (B09). Treat SOM CAGR as an upper bound, and 3C capacity (not TAM) as the binding near-term constraint.

**3F. Peer Positioning.** Adhesives competes with Pidilite (Fevicol), Astral, Jyoti Resins; VP latex with Apcotex, NOCIL/BSF India; agri with Coromandel, Chambal (valuation-sotp, B05). JACPL is a fast-growing share-gainer in adhesives (fastest 5-yr CAGR in India per the MNCL lead) but a minnow against Fevicol's scale and brand. No anchored peer financial set was supplied this run (B01 M2/M5/M6/M7/M9 scored 0 PEER DATA NEEDED) — peer comparison is directional, not scored.

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

The market has already done the easy work here. A year ago JACPL looked like a cheap fertilizer company with a hidden polymer jewel; a 58% re-rating priced that gross mispricing out. What is left is cleaner and harder. Strip the company into its two demerging halves and almost all the value, and all the quality, sits in the retained polymer arm: a business earning 67.5% on segment capital, nearly debt-free, with a genuinely scarce position in tyre-cord latex and food-grade gum base, and a branded-adhesives line growing high-teens as it chases a much larger Fevicol. The agri half is a subsidy-linked commodity cyclical that lost money at segment level in FY25 and is the source of the cash drag; it leaves as JASL, and it is an AVOID stub.

The problem is price and proof. At Rs 2,342 you are paying about 27x forward for the polymer claim, which is fair for the quality but not the 25% entry the strategy needs — the Hurdle Ratio is 1.87, a conditional pass that clears 25% only on bull earnings or a 12-15% dip. And every attractive number is a segment figure that has never been reported as a standalone company. The cash conversion that fell to 0.59x in FY26 is assumed to belong to the agri half; if the first standalone polymer accounts show otherwise, the 35x quality base is wrong. Samlaya carries revenue, margin and ROCE at once, so a single commissioning slip breaks three parts of the thesis together.

So the discipline is patience. This is a good business you want to own, at a price you should not pay today. Buy on a 6-15% pullback into the zone, or when the first standalone accounts confirm the 67.5% return and clean cash — and never buy a dip that exists because Samlaya slipped. Small size, capped by a conditional hurdle, a mixed delivery record, and a promoter you should watch.
