### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **Order book / diversification**: STRENGTHENING every quarter (Rs13,295cr -> Rs18,554cr -> Rs18,568cr); renewables share of order book rose from 22% (Nov-25, fourth transcript context) to 19% of the much larger FY26 book (May-26) — a share dip on a bigger base, not flagged by management as such.
- **HAM monetization**: STRENGTHENING in narrative terms (one deal closed, two more "pipelined") but WEAKENING in evidentiary terms — sale price never disclosed in any of three calls, and the "before September" 2026 target for the next two deals (Feb-26 call) had already been pushed out to Q2/Q3 FY27 by the May-26 call, with no update at all in the Aug-26 call.
- **International expansion**: UNCHANGED in stated caution ("baby steps," "very conservative") across all three calls, but the underlying pipeline claim ballooned (Romania bid quoted at ~Rs13,000cr for a single 17km project in the May-26 call, a scale roughly 65-70% of the entire order book per the analyst's own math) then went completely unmentioned in the Aug-26 call, alongside a new, unexplained excuse ("this war situation," Aug-26 call, p.11) for staying conservative. DROPPED without update: the Sobha/Dubai and Romania/EU bid outcomes.
- **NHAI awarding pace**: management declined to quantify twice (Feb-26, May-26), each time citing the NHAI website as the source rather than giving even an approximate company figure — UNCHANGED (evasive both times).
- **Margins**: STRENGTHENING and increasingly well explained — the May-26 call finally clarified that FY24/FY25 EBITDA included one-off bonus/royalty income that the FY26/FY27 guided band strips out, after two rounds of analyst pressure in the same call.
- **NEW in Aug-26, unexplained why now**: commercial paper issuance (Rs100cr) against working-capital limits — a new short-term financing instrument introduced with no forward reference in the two earlier calls, alongside the ROE (14%) vs ROCE (~19-20%) divergence question, both first raised in Q1 FY27.
- **DROPPED**: Jaipur Rail Corporation project (Rs918cr, L1 status announced Feb-26 call, p.11) never mentioned again in the May-26 or Aug-26 calls; LOA status unresolved and unaddressed.
- **NEVER APPEARING in any of the three calls, despite being current-year, audited, material events**: the Rs89.65m procurement fraud, the qualified IFC opinion (both levels), the DGGI GST search action, the NHAI termination of the step-down SPV (Ceigall Ludhiana Rupnagar Greenfield Highway), the associated subsidiary auditor resignation, the contingent-liability rise to 83.7% of standalone net worth, and any leadership-change explanation (see Section 2 below).

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

- Northern Ayodhya bypass execution slowdown (Aug-26 call): attributed to monsoon rain — EXTERNAL-BLAME, plausible and seasonal, not disputed.
- Southern Ludhiana bypass slower ramp (Aug-26 call): attributed to land availability at 62% — EXTERNAL-BLAME (department-side), consistent with land-status commentary in earlier calls (HONEST, not new).
- ROE decline to 14% vs stable ROCE ~19-20% (Aug-26 call): attributed entirely to post-IPO/QIP equity-base expansion — plausible, standard attribution, not independently verifiable in this corpus (no shareholding/ratio corpus).
- Margin "decline" in Slide 41 optics (May-26 call): initially answered as "EBITDA margin is stable, that's not decreasing," then, on a second and third round of the same analyst's questioning, corrected to acknowledge FY24/FY25 EBITDA included one-off bonus/royalty income — DEFLECTION on first pass, HONEST-ADMISSION only after repeated pressure.
- PPT slide-38 equity-invested error (Feb-26 call): management directly said "It's a mistake, but otherwise entire equity has been put by the company only" — a rare, clean HONEST-ADMISSION.
- Cash/FDR figure correction (May-26 call): handled as a silent written erratum appended to the filed transcript rather than a live correction or any subsequent-call acknowledgment — closer to SILENCE than honest-admission, given the scale (Rs171cr, ~71% of the stated unencumbered liquidity).
- Fraud, IFC qualification, NHAI termination, auditor resignation, leadership churn, contingent-liability growth: SILENCE across all three calls — never raised proactively, never asked, never addressed.
- Pattern check: management readily proactively raises OPERATIONAL wins (order wins, appointed dates, margin beats) but never proactively raises the governance/compliance/cash-flow items that the audited accounts carry as top findings. When pressed on a genuinely uncomfortable number (margin optics), the first response deflects before a later, more honest answer emerges under repeated questioning. No instance across three calls of management naming a compliance or governance issue unprompted.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence dropped in the calls

- MoRTH FY27 budgetary allocation raised ~8% to ~Rs3.1 lakh crore (Feb-26 call, p.3).
