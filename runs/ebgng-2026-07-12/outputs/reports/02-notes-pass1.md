### Note 2 — Summary of Material Accounting Policies (pp.248–260)
- **2.1 Basis of preparation**: Restated Consolidated Financial Information prepared
  per Ind AS 101 with transition date 1-Apr-2021; three different audit regimes stitch
  together the track record — FY22 special-purpose Ind AS financials (Indian GAAP
  transition), FY23/FY24 special-purpose Ind AS financials, H1FY25 Ind AS 34 interim
  financials (Note 2.1, p.248–249). 🟡 Watch — restated info is explicitly stated
  "not suitable for any other purpose."
- **Auditor's Examination Report itself (pp.239–241, before Note 1)**: The main
  auditor (Shankarlal Jain & Associates LLP) states it **did not audit** the
  consolidated financials of subsidiary Electronics Bazaar FZC (UAE) for H1FY25/FY24
  (audited by NBN Auditing of Accounts), FY23 (Kothari Auditors & Accountants) or FY22
  (Youssry & Co. Auditing & Consultancy) — opinion "based solely on reports of other
  auditors." FZC's H1FY25 total assets ₹3,424.90M / total revenue ₹3,802.15M
  (p.240) — i.e. this un-primary-audited entity is **~46% of group total assets and
  ~63% of group total revenue** at H1FY25 (group total assets 7,412.42M, group revenue
  6,079.62M — FZC revenue exceeds group total because of intra-group elimination
  differences; directionally FZC dominates). Additionally, step-down subsidiary Bright
  World Technology Inc (USA) is **wholly unaudited** ("not subject to audit as per
  local law of the country of incorporation") — H1FY25 total assets ₹111.41M, revenue
  ₹191.55M (p.240). 🔴 Red Flag — majority of group revenue and profit runs through
  an entity not audited by the principal Indian auditor, plus one subsidiary with no
  audit at all. (Auditor's Examination Report, p.239–240)
- **2.4 Revenue recognition**: standard Ind AS 115 control-transfer language; export
  incentive recognised "on certainty of receipt" (p.251) — reasonably conservative
  phrasing, not pre-recognised on shipment. 🟢

  no TAX on Income no Provision for Tax is made on Profit" (p.253). This single line
  explains why consolidated effective tax rate runs far below the Indian statutory
  rate across all periods (see Note 33/35 below). 🟡 Watch — flags a structural
  earnings-quality dependency on a zero-tax UAE free-zone subsidiary.
- **2.8 Depreciation**: WDV method per Schedule II for Indian entity — Computers 3
  yrs, Office Equipment 5 yrs, Furniture & Fixture 10 yrs, Leasehold Building 30 yrs,

  **different, rate-based (not useful-life-based) WDV schedule**: Office Equipment
  10%, Furniture & Fixture 10%, Leasehold Building 5%, Vehicles 10% (p.254) —
  disclosed transparently but a genuine policy divergence between parent and foreign
  sub. 🟡 Watch.
- **2.12 Provisions / warranty**: "the warranty-related expenses do not have a
  material impact, they are accounted for as and when incurred" (p.255) — i.e. **no
  warranty provision is carried on the balance sheet at all**, expensed on a cash/
  incurred basis, for a refurbished-electronics business whose entire value
  proposition per the MD&A (p.304) is "industry leading warranty terms." 🟡 Watch —
  no warranty movement schedule exists anywhere in the notes (see also Note 18/23
  Provisions below, which show only gratuity, no warranty line).
- **2.19 Contingencies and Commitments**: states "there has been no other known
  contingent liability or commitment... as of financial position Date" (p.260) — this
  directly contradicts Note 44 (Contingent Liabilities), which discloses income-tax
  and GST contingent liabilities at three of the four balance sheet dates (see below).
  🟡 Watch — policy boilerplate not reconciled with actual disclosure; flag for
  Pass 3 pattern check.
- Remaining sub-policies (2.3, 2.5, 2.7, 2.9–2.11, 2.13–2.18, 2.20) are standard
  Ind AS boilerplate (fair value hierarchy, financial instruments classification,
