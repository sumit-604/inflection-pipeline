Cheap net cash compounder, one print governs

DDev Plastiks makes polymer compounds for cable and wire makers. PVC, XLPE and Sioplas, HFFR, engineering grades and Antifab, sold on cost-plus B2B terms into India and 55 export markets. FY26 revenue was about Rs 2,948 Cr, PAT about Rs 202 Cr, and the EBITDA margin held near 11%. It sells one tonne at a time, roughly Rs 1.45 lakh of revenue and Rs 15,800 of EBITDA per tonne. The company was carved out of Kkalpana Industries through an NCLT-approved demerger in 2022 and has run net cash since. Gate 0 reads GOOD at 74 out of 100 on the core score, capped there by one deal-breaker: Block B cash conversion scored 7 against a required 8.

Two things are changing at once. Capacity stepped up, with the Bhiwadi XLPE line at 48,000 MTPA commissioned in the last week of April 2026 and 5,000 MT of HFFR plus 10,000 MT of PVC in by December 2025. Alongside it sits a battery energy storage assembly vertical with zero operating history, revenue recognition starting in the second half of FY27. These are two different businesses. One is a proven cost-plus compounder, the other a pre-revenue assembly bet, and they earn different margins on different capital, so they are valued separately.

Cash conversion is the number the whole call turns on. Working capital days rose from 63 to 82 across FY24 to FY26, almost entirely on an FY26 inventory build. Cumulative CFO to PAT over the three years was 0.59, and FY26 alone turned only 42% of PAT into operating cash. Finished goods inventory rose 60% while revenue rose 7%. Against that, the balance sheet is net cash, carries no term borrowings, and funds its capex from accruals rather than debt. Whether that inventory is stock awaiting dispatch from the new lines or an early sign of a sell-through slowdown is not provable from the record yet. The determination is INDETERMINATE, leaning growth-induced, and it caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. It does not resolve silently to a clean proceed.

Management credibility grades B. The company delivered its capacity additions, roughly 30% export growth, and the 10 to 12% margin band it guided. It missed FY26 volume, printing 201,370 MT against 210,000 to 220,000 MT guided. BESS FY27 revenue guidance was cut twice within two quarters of launch, from Rs 300 to 500 Cr down to Rs 200 to 250 Cr, and Phase-1 capex was revised up from Rs 150 Cr to about Rs 200 Cr. A guided Adani and UltraTech PVC offtake was dropped silently after its window passed. An external shock was cited as the primary excuse in each of the three quarters reviewed. The promoter read is TRUSTWORTHY: royalty paid to Kkalpana fell 72.5% while purchases from the same entity rose 88.9% with no explanation, Chairman and MD pay fell from Rs 93 lakh to Rs 87 lakh while PAT grew 9%, and the promoter group filed zero share encumbrance in April 2026. Two of the three independent directors also sit on Kkalpana's board, a governance-proximity item to watch given the related-party flows.

Peer corroboration is thin. Only Apcotex Industries is usable, and it has zero product overlap with DDev beyond shared crude-linked feedstock. Apcotex confirms the raw material shock existed at roughly the magnitude DDev described, but its own 7-May-2026 call contradicts DDev's claim that prices normalized by April 2026, with the Strait of Hormuz still closed and coverage managed only to end-June. No direct cable-compound peer P/E, margin or ROCE median exists in this run, so the valuation rests on the absolute framework, not a relative discount claim.

Runway reads STRONG. Core serviceable market near Rs 9,600 Cr leaves 3.25 times revenue headroom on a market growing about 9% a year, with a served share around 31%. SOM-implied revenue CAGR of 22% over three years sits well above the 11% base case used in the valuation, so the growth path runs far under the market ceiling. Management's own "2.5 lakh tonne market, one-third share" claim is stale and internally impossible against its own 2.01 lakh tonne FY26 output, and carries zero weight in the sizing. BESS is sized as a separate runway.

The valuation lands both destination PE tracks at 25x. The four-pillar additive build runs a 22.0x ROCE base times a neutral 1.00x cash multiplier plus a 2x growth premium to a raw 24x, lifted to 30x by the Undiscovered Alpha multiplier, then capped at the absolute 25x Cables and Industrial products ceiling. The RRM track raws at 23.3x and caps to the same 25x. Applied to FY29 trailing core EPS of Rs 20.33 bear, Rs 26.66 base and Rs 31.62 bull, that gives fair values of Rs 508, Rs 666 and Rs 790. The Hurdle Ratio is 2.45 and passes. At CMP Rs 272 the stock trades 13.96x trailing, so the base case compounds about 34% over three years and even the bear barely loses money because the cheapness itself is the cushion. BESS is a separate slice worth Rs 0, Rs 13 or Rs 39 per share, never blended into the core multiple. The decision is BUY at the margin-of-safety floor, carried as PROCEED WITH CAVEATS, size Small at 2 to 3%.

The devil's case is WEAKENED BUT ALIVE, and its live points are real. The shared catalyst is one correlated bet: a stalled utilization ramp makes cash look structural and removes the 2x growth premium at the same time, so the headline 14.1x upside-to-downside asymmetry, which treats them as two independent risks, is overstated. Every trailing quality metric deteriorated two years running, ROCE 39 to 29, gross margin 19.5 to 17.3, CFO to PAT 0.74 to 0.42, working capital days 63 to 82, so this could be a down-inflection value trap rather than a confirmed up-inflection. Strip the UA multiplier under a 0.80x cash outcome and fair value falls about 17% to Rs 552; structural cash plus a stalled ramp pulls the destination toward 18x and a roughly 10% dead-money CAGR. At CMP 272 against MoS 273 there is zero margin of safety, and the governing print lands in about six weeks, so the devil argues watchlist-until-print over buy-at-floor. The reconciliation is honest: the gate is PROCEED WITH CAVEATS, the Hurdle passes and price is at the floor, but the entry conjunction is not clean while the cash print is open. The single condition that resolves it is the first FY27 quarter's net working capital days read against volume.
