## Q3 FY26 (Dec-2025 qtr) — from Q3FY26 deck (6-Mar-26) + Q3FY26 concall (this is the collected transcript)
- AUM Rs6,356cr (+23.5% YoY, +5.5% QoQ); Q3 disbursements Rs1,310cr (+35% YoY); 41,015 new borrowers.
- Medium-term AUM aspiration ~30% CAGR.
- Asset quality: PAR X (1+DPD) 7.64%; GNPA 4.94%; NNPA 1.98%. Credit cost 4.67% of AUM (4th consecutive qtr of decline).
- Collection efficiency (non-OD) 99.3-99.4%; Bucket-1 collection 36.9% (Q3FY25) -> 55.2% (Q3FY26).
- Mix: mortgage ~21%, secured hypo ~40%, unsecured hypo ~37.5%, Saral ~1.6%. Target ~30% mortgage/70% hypo over 3y.
- Tech: 100% paperless origination, 100% cashless disbursement; underwriting 32% AI/ML, 68% cluster; 96.8% NACH.
- 571 branches, 18 states + 3 UTs; growth from deepening branches (60% AUM/branch, 39% repeat, 1% new branches Dec24-Dec25).
- CoB ~10.96% Q3 (incremental 10.31%); CRAR 31.45% Dec-2025 (pre-IPO).
- 3-yr vision: AUM CAGR 28-33%, credit cost 3.25-3.75%, opex/ATA 7-7.5%, RoA 4-4.5%, RoE 17-20%.

## Q4 FY26 / FY26 — from Q4FY26 deck (27-Apr-26) + Q4FY26 concall (28-Apr-26; NOT collected)
- FY26 AUM Rs7,044cr (+26-27% YoY); FY26 disbursements Rs5,169cr (+~20%). Q4 disbursements Rs1,655cr (+25% YoY).
- Q4FY26 PAT Rs86cr (+110% YoY, +100% QoQ); FY26 PAT Rs194cr (+13% YoY). Q4 RoA 5.1%, RoE 16.0%; FY26 RoA 3.1%, RoE 9.3%.
- Asset quality (5th consecutive qtr improving): PAR X 6.88%, PAR30 6.12%, GNPA 4.77%, NNPA 1.80%; credit cost 4.30% ATA Q4 (vs 6.16% Q4FY25). Stage-2 1.12%; ECL coverage ~4.1%; PCR 63.7%.

- FY27 guidance: AUM growth 25-30%, RoA 4-4.5%, credit cost 3.5-4.0%, opex/ATA 8.25-8.75%, NIM 14.25-14.75%.
