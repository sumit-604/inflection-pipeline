## CROSS-CUTTING NOTE (read first — bears on the binding thesis gate)
The Notion thesis makes Q1 FY27 the **binding CASH-CONVERSION gate** (CFO/PAT, working-capital days, debtor days) with hard kill-switches on Going Concern, material weakness, promoter pledge, and Net D/E. Forensic reality of THIS document:
- **No cash flow statement, no balance sheet, no debtor/WC-day disclosure** in a quarterly LRR filing. Therefore CFO/PAT, WC days, debtor days, ROCE, Net D/E, promoter pledge and promoter holding **cannot be evaluated from this filing** — they live in the shareholding pattern / cash-flow filings, not here. A4 must not read silence here as a pass on the gate.
- **What the P&L does tell us about cash:** a very large Q1 inventory build (standalone change in inventories (6,514.50)m = **₹651 Cr build**, consol (7,589.71)m = **₹759 Cr build**, ~5x the Q1 FY26 build), with purchases of stock-in-trade (52,382.92m) **exceeding** revenue (48,322.18m). This is the classic working-capital-absorption quarter and is the strongest in-document signal that Q1 CFO is likely weak/negative — the exact Pillar-2 concern. See F2-2.
- **Kill-switches clearable from this doc:** Going Concern (#7) = CLEAR; material weakness (#6) = CLEAR (both LRRs unmodified, no EoM). See F5.
- **EBITDA margin ~3.0%** (standalone ₹146 Cr / consol ₹155 Cr) clears the ≥2.7% green threshold (checklist metric 6). Revenue +58% (S) / +62% (C) YoY clears metric 5.
