Protocol 1.1. Peers: RPTECH (Rashi Peripherals), CPPLUS (Aditya Infotech), REDINGTON. 12 transcripts, all read in the relevant sections. Anchors use (PEER, quarter call, speaker). Peer names map to calls as: RPTECH Nov-25 = Q2 FY26, Feb-26 = Q3 FY26, May-26 = Q4 FY26, Aug-26 = Q1 FY27. CPPLUS the same pattern (Nov-25, Feb-26, May-28-2026 call = Q4 FY26, Aug-26 = Q1 FY27). REDINGTON Feb-26 = Q3 FY26, May-26 = Q4 FY26, Jul-30-2026 = Q1 FY27, Nov-4-2026 = Q2 FY27. Units are Rs Cr unless stated. "Derived" marks arithmetic done here on a stated figure.

- No peer names Creative Newtech. See 2D.
- RPTECH and CPPLUS have no Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026) call in the set; only REDINGTON reports that quarter. This limits Q9.
- CPPLUS is a share gainer talking its own book (45.4% share claim); RPTECH calls the cycle a "super cycle" repeatedly. Optimism bias is noted where it matters.

## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

**2A Demand.** Consensus on a price-driven boom with weakening units. Value up: REDINGTON Q2 FY27 revenue +48%, RPTECH Q1 FY27 +62% (30-35 pts price, 20-25 pts units; Goenka), CPPLUS Q1 FY27 +89.5%. Units: consumer down (REDINGTON activations -20-30%, RPTECH IDC -5 to -10%), commercial flat to up (REDINGTON 0-7%). Pull-forward and forward buying are named by all three (RPTECH Q3 FY26 and Q4 FY26 Pansari; REDINGTON Q2 FY27 Hariharan; CPPLUS Q4 FY26 trade pre-buy). RPTECH Q1 FY27 (Goenka) also says tier-2 channel inventory is built up because partners hold for extra margin. CNL's "inventory build" rationale fits the industry, but the same peers flag it as a late-cycle behaviour. CNL's 20% FY27 growth guidance is far below peers' 40-60%, which is a different read from "industry-wide boom".

**2B Pricing and inputs.** All three: component costs rising through 2027 (CPPLUS Q4 FY26 "until 2027"; RPTECH Q1 FY27 "persist through 2028"), absolute margin per unit up, percentage margin flat. REDINGTON Q4 FY26 and Q3 FY26 explicitly: no extra gross margin. This matches CNL's pass-through story but not an expansion story (see Q3).

**2C Capex cycle.** Mixed, not a capacity race for the distributors. Distributors (RPTECH, REDINGTON) add branches, VDA/Restar JV, AI COEs, minor fixed assets (REDINGTON net assets 591 to 653 Cr, Q3 FY26) and are asset-light; CPPLUS is a lone heavy expander in surveillance (capex raised to 200-300 Cr, capacity to double by 2028; Khemka Q4 FY26), partly pre-empting STQC-certified rivals. So surveillance is a leader-led capacity build, while distribution is capital-light with funding strain: REDINGTON (Krishnan, Q2 FY27) flags "capital raise will become requirement" if growth continues; RPTECH ROCE rose with borrowings for early-payment discounts (Shah, Q3 FY26).

**2D Competitive mentions.** No peer mentions Creative Newtech by name or description. Closest: CPPLUS Q2 FY26 (Khemka, answering Aniruddha Joshi on global brands): "some brands like, I believe Honeywell has some certification already there but we don't see any products out" and global brands "most of the DNA has been enterprise and government" and "will take a lot of time" to cover mass distribution. This names Honeywell, CNL's licensor brand (per B05), but the transcript does not say it refers to CNL's licensed product category. Treat as an indirect, unconfirmed mention.

**2E Risks peers discuss that CNL does not.**
- Component allocation and shortage forcing prepayments, shrinking creditor days (CPPLUS Q3-Q4 FY26, Nair).
- Consumer affordability and unit elasticity (REDINGTON Q2 FY27 Hariharan; RPTECH Q4 FY26 Goenka).
- War-risk insurance withdrawal and receivable delays in conflict regions (REDINGTON Q4 FY26 Hariharan/Krishnan).
- AR provisioning 213 Cr in one quarter (REDINGTON Q2 FY27 Krishnan).
- Large-deal and neo-cloud concentration, lumpy orders (REDINGTON Q2 FY27 Hariharan; RPTECH Q2 FY26 Yotta).
- Funding capacity of growth: debt/equity and possible raise (REDINGTON Q2 FY27 Krishnan).
- Channel inventory build (RPTECH Q1 FY27 Goenka).
- Competitor STQC catch-up from 2028 (CPPLUS Q4 FY26 Gajiwala Q&A).
- Forex: CPPLUS hedges 85-100% of receivables and creditors weekly (Nair, Q1 FY27); RPTECH cites rupee depreciation as a turnover tailwind (Q2 FY26). CNL discloses no equivalent hedge policy in B05.
- Unit demand drop and 12-18 month component recovery window (REDINGTON Q3 FY26 Hariharan).

## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY

- Claims verified: 2 sub-claims of 10 questions (Q2 cost and pass-through; Q10 AI/DC revenue). Partially verified: 6 (Q1, Q3, Q4, Q7, Q8, Q9). Unverifiable: 2 (Q5, Q6), plus the Q10 Super Micro/cabling sub-claim.
- Claims contradicted: 1 sub-claim (Q2 "without demand loss"). Priority for synthesis.
- Most consequential contradiction: Q2. REDINGTON (Q2 FY27) reports consumer units declining with PC activations "negative 20%, 30%" and RPTECH (Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27) cites IDC unit -5 to -10% and consumer H2 softness. Pass-through is working on price, but not without volume loss, which bears on CNL's inventory-build rationale and market-entry growth.
- Strongest independent confirmation: component price rises. Three peers independently report 20-50% increases on memory/PC/smartphone categories and pass-through (REDINGTON 30-50%, RPTECH RAM 2-3x, CPPLUS cumulative 15-20% hikes), matching or exceeding CNL's 30-40%.
