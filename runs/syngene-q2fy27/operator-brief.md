### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Syngene does research and makes drugs for other pharma companies. Research sells scientist time. Manufacturing sells plant capacity by the batch.
2. Q2 revenue was Rs 832.7 Cr. It fell 8.6% on a year ago. It rose 13.1% on Q1.
3. Most of the rise on Q1 is seasonal. The same Q1 to Q2 step happened in FY24 (+12.6%) and FY25 (+12.8%).
4. Revenue beat the Notion base path of about Rs 790 Cr. The filings give no split of research against manufacturing. So the research tests R2 and T1 cannot run.
5. Operating margin was 17.7%, down 4.2 points. A forex hedge loss of Rs 61.4 Cr caused most of the fall. Without it, the margin was 25.1%.
6. That 25.1% is flattered. The company capitalised Rs 66.1 Cr of Bayview start-up cost in H1. Expensed, the H1 margin without the hedge loss falls from 22.3% to about 18.1%.
7. Profit quality is low. In H1, other income was larger than profit before tax and exceptional items. Core profit before tax, without other income, fell 63% in Q2.
8. Cash went the wrong way. H1 operating cash flow was minus Rs 143.2 Cr, so tripwire T3 fired. H1 FY26 was also negative, so part of this is seasonal.
9. Bayview is the US biologics plant in Baltimore. It signed its first manufacturing customer. The filing does not name the customer or the contract size.
10. Drug substance output at Bayview starts only in FY28. On the strict reading, tripwire T4 fired. The other reading says plant readiness by March 2027 still holds.
11. Four senior people are leaving: the CFO, the head of strategy, and the heads of chemistry and biology. The press release and the Reg 30 filing give different CFO exit terms.
12. Watch Q3 research revenue in January 2027. Rs 614 Cr or more completes R2. Below Rs 580 Cr fires T1.
