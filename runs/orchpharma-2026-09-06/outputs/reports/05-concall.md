### The single most important finding of this stage: the FY26 revenue "growth" in the screener
### aggregate is a consolidation-scope artifact, not organic growth

The orchestrator brief's screener-aggregate pointer states FY26 revenue rose ~34% to ~Rs 1,233
cr. The transcripts show why, and it is not organic growth:
- The Q4 FY26 call (Concall_Jun_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.3, Manish Dhanuka) reports FY26 full-year
  STANDALONE revenue of Rs 811 crores, DOWN from Rs 922 crores in FY25 (Orchid solo, both years).
  That is a 12% decline, not growth.
- The Q1 FY27 call (Concall_Aug_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.3, Manish Dhanuka) discloses that the
  Dhanuka Laboratories merger became legally effective on 10-Jul-2026 with an APPOINTED DATE of
  1-Apr-2024, so FY25 and FY26 have been RESTATED to a combined (Orchid + Dhanuka) basis. On that
  restated basis, FY26 revenue is Rs 1,233 crores against a restated FY25 of Rs 1,398 crores — a
  12% DECLINE on a like-for-like combined basis too.
- Rs 922 cr (old FY25 standalone, Orchid only) to Rs 1,233 cr (new FY26 combined, Orchid +
  Dhanuka) is +33.7% — almost exactly the screener's "~34%" figure. That comparison mixes a
  standalone prior year against a combined current year. It is a scope change, not performance.
- On every basis actually stated by management (standalone vs standalone, or combined vs
  combined), FY26 revenue FELL, roughly 12%, against FY25. The business shrank in FY26; it did
  not grow 34%.
This is not a management deception — the restatement mechanics are disclosed plainly on the Q1
FY27 call — but it is a trap for any later stage that pulls the screener's "1,233 cr, +34%"
figure without reading past the headline. Flagged as a MAJOR analytical hazard for Stage 11.

### PBT is never once discussed by management across all four calls

Management discusses revenue, gross margin and EBITDA on every call. PBT/PAT is never mentioned
by name or by number in any of the four transcripts read for this stage. The screener-aggregate
pointer states PBT fell from ~Rs 96 cr (FY25) to ~Rs 10 cr (FY26) and that three of four FY26
quarters printed a loss before tax with Q4 printing a large positive. The EBITDA trajectory
management DOES disclose is directionally consistent with that pattern — Q2 FY26 EBITDA Rs 6 cr
(Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.3), Q3 FY26 EBITDA margin 6% (Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.pdf,
p.2), Q4 FY26 EBITDA Rs 42.3 cr (Concall_Jun_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.3) — a weak-weak-weak-strong
pattern that would plausibly translate into three loss quarters and one strong quarter once
depreciation and interest are applied. But management never gives the PBT number itself, on any
call, even when EBITDA is given unprompted every time. That consistent, four-call-long silence on
the line that most exposes the capex/interest burden of the 7-ACA and Cefiderocol build is itself
a finding (Section 2D).

### CWIP and borrowings: partially corroborated, not fully reconcilable from transcripts alone

CWIP rising from ~Rs 81 cr to ~Rs 341 cr (screener) is directionally consistent with disclosed
capex: 7-ACA planned debt of Rs 450 cr with Rs 170 cr drawn by Q3 FY26
(Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.6, Manish Dhanuka), and a first-ever disclosure of TOTAL
7-ACA project capex of Rs 750 cr plus Cefiderocol capex of USD 20-25 million in the Q1 FY27 call
(Concall_Aug_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.9, Mridul Dhanuka). No transcript states the CWIP figure
itself. Borrowings roughly doubling to ~Rs 363 cr (screener, FY26-end) cannot be cleanly
triangulated from what management said: Q2 FY26 call gives total debt as "only INR47 crores"
(Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.9, Sunil Gupta); Q3 FY26 call gives Rs 170 cr drawn against
the 7-ACA-specific Rs 450 cr facility plus Rs 75 cr cash (Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.6-7).
Neither the Q4 FY26 call nor the Q1 FY27 call restates a total-debt or total-cash figure at all —
this disclosure item DROPPED after Q3 FY26, exactly as leverage was reportedly building
(Section 2D, dropped_triggers).

### 1C. Trigger evolution across the four calls

**7-ACA (Jammu, backward integration).** Q2 FY26: fermenters erected, rain delays, target
"as announced earlier" (undated in this transcript). Q3 FY26: explicit target given for the
first time — mechanical completion by September 2026, pilot yields "fully absorbed."
Q4 FY26: target revised to Q1 CY2027 (~March 2027) — a 4-6 month slip, disclosed unprompted in
opening remarks, not only when pressed. Q1 FY27: March 2027 reaffirmed, plus first disclosure of
total project cost (Rs 750 cr) and a detailed in-house/third-party utilization plan. TREND:
STRENGTHENING in specificity and disclosure quality even as the DATE itself slipped once.

**Cefiderocol (Shionogi/GARDP).** Q2 FY26: "on track for production readiness by Q4 of '26"
(Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.4, Manish Dhanuka — read literally this is Q4 FY26, i.e.
Jan-Mar 2026). Q3 FY26 (three months later): production start now stated as "sometime in
December [2026]," confirmed twice on the call (Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.7, Manish
Dhanuka: "December 2026, right"). Taken at face value this is a ~9-month slip disclosed between
two consecutive calls without management ever using the words "delay" or "slip." FLAG: this may
instead reflect the Q2 FY26 call speaker loosely saying "Q4 of '26" to mean calendar Q4 2026
(Oct-Dec), in which case there is little or no slip and the two statements are consistent; the
transcript itself does not disambiguate, so BOTH readings are carried here per the instruction to
flag internal chronology conflicts rather than resolve them by assumption. Independent of that
ambiguity, the India COMMERCIAL LAUNCH date has moved outward at every subsequent call: "5-6
months of registration" implied post-readiness (Q3 FY26) -> "Q2/Q3 CY2027" (Q4 FY26) -> "Q3 of
[FY28]," i.e. Oct-Dec 2027 (Q1 FY27) — a genuine, repeated outward slip on the launch date, not
just the facility-readiness date. TREND: WEAKENING (commissioning date ambiguous-to-flat;
commercial launch date has slipped at every one of the last three calls).

**Enmetazobactam (Exblifep) ex-India licensing.** Q2 FY26: US deal "hopeful... in next 12
months," acquisition of global rights just completed. Q3 FY26: "hopefully we should be signing US
deal within this year," discussions with 3 parties, binding term sheet in one EM market signed.
Q4 FY26: the "every quarter, one or two announcements" cadence promised the prior quarter is
directly acknowledged as MISSED ("unfortunately, we don't have the definitive agreement signed
yet" — Sagar's question, Concall_Jun_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.6); US timeline downgraded to "this
quarter, coming quarter, at least" for a CLOSE on discussions, not a signed deal; peak-sales
framing materially revised upward and lengthened (from an earlier, analyst-cited "$250 million"
peak reached in "3 years" to management's own "$1-2 billion lifetime, peak in year 4-5"). Q1 FY27:
one deal DELIVERED (Russia, ~$178m 10-year estimated value, signed ~Jul 2026) but the US timeline
is now the vaguest it has been across all four calls — "in discussion with prospective candidates
in US and China," no date offered, down from "advanced discussions with 3 parties." TREND:
MIXED — one geography delivered (Russia) after a full quarter's miss, but the flagship US market
promise has run four consecutive quarters with steadily DECREASING specificity. Classified as
WEAKENING on the specific claim analysts keep pressing (US deal), STRENGTHENING on the general
platform (more geographies engaged, Europe volumes compounding quarter on quarter).

**AMS platform.** Q2 FY26: drag down to Rs 1.8 cr/quarter from Rs 6 cr; "next year [FY27] should
be breakeven." Q3 FY26: sales stabilizing, hiring is the binding constraint, hospital-segment FY27
revenue guided down to "I'll be happy if we do INR20 crores" against an analyst's Rs 50-60 cr

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **PBT/PAT**, on any of the four calls, ever — despite EBITDA being given unprompted every
  quarter and despite the screener aggregate showing PBT collapsing from ~Rs 96 cr to ~Rs 10 cr
  over FY26. The gap between EBITDA (given) and PBT (never given) is exactly where the interest
  and depreciation load of the 7-ACA/Cefiderocol capex program would show up. Likely reason: this
  is the least flattering line given the capex build currently sitting on the balance sheet
  pre-revenue; EBITDA is a materially more favorable number to lead with.
- **Total debt / total cash**, after Q3 FY26. Given cleanly on Q2 and Q3 FY26 calls, absent from
  the Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls even in the closing "financial performance" sections, and not
  pressed by any analyst in the transcripts read. This is the period the screener aggregate shows
