**P3-1. CONTRADICTION — MD&A/Chairman narrative vs the Notes' own ratio disclosure 🟡**
The Chairman's Message and MD&A (non-Notes sections, p.1, p.34-39) frame FY25 as a year of improving performance
("highest ever profitability," 16.4% ROE cited approvingly). Note 51's own 11-ratio table (p.77) shows ROCE FELL
to 17.66% from 22.03% (-19.81% relative decline) and Return on Investment fell to 9.48% from 11.47% (-17.31%) in
the SAME year the narrative sections celebrate. The Notes do not themselves flag this as a concern; the positive
framing sits entirely in the non-audited narrative sections while the audited Note carries the less flattering
number. This is a genuine narrative-vs-note contradiction pattern, not a new number (see Pass 1 Finding 2), but
elevated here specifically because Pass 3 is looking for contradictions across sections describing the same
period. (Note 51, p.77, vs Chairman's Message p.1 and MD&A p.34-39.)

**P3-2. DELIBERATELY UNEVEN DISCLOSURE DEPTH — Atlas RPT vs SMT Packaging RPT 🟡**
Comparing disclosure density across the SAME Note (Note 41) for two related parties transacting in the same
year: Atlas Transformers gets a full AOC-2 annexure entry (p.30-31), a dedicated Notice explanatory statement
with % of turnover, sale/purchase sub-limits, and Audit Committee review narrative (Notice p.13-15) — several
pages of disclosure for one counterparty. SMT Packaging Pvt Ltd's ₹4.20 Cr "Advance for Land & Building" gets a
single line in Note 41b (p.74) and a cross-reference in Note 22 (p.66) with no valuation basis, no terms, no
AOC-2 entry, and no Audit Committee commentary at all. The company had the disclosure apparatus (AOC-2, Notice
explanatory statements) and chose not to apply it to this transaction. This asymmetry, not just the transaction
itself (already flagged A1a in Pass 1), is the pattern-level point: the AR shows it CAN disclose RPT terms in
depth when it chooses to. (Note 41 p.73-74; Note 22 p.66; AOC-2 p.30-31; Notice p.13-15 — contrast confirmed.)

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Cash-conversion failure**: Operating cash flow -₹35.46 Cr FY25 against PAT +48% growth, driven by a
  raw-material inventory build (+283.7%) and receivables growth (+54.8%) both far outpacing the 14.0% revenue
  increase (Cash Flow Statement p.52; Notes 18, 19). This is the clearest earnings-quality red flag in the notes:
  reported profit growth is not backed by operating cash generation.
- **Unreconciled ₹5.47 Cr IPO-expense gap** between the Fund Utilization Statement and the P&L (Note 45 p.75 vs
  Note 29(b) p.69, Note 16 p.63) — a quantifiable, undisclosed-bridge discrepancy in a document that otherwise
  presents itself as internally cross-checked.
- **Undisclosed-terms related-party asset transfer**: ₹4.20 Cr land/building advance to SMT Packaging Pvt Ltd
  with no valuation basis, no AOC-2 entry, and no Audit Committee commentary disclosed, in an AR that otherwise
  gives extensive disclosure depth to its largest related party (Note 22 p.66; Note 41 p.74 vs Note 41/AOC-2
  treatment of Atlas).
- **Aggressive-adjacent (not disqualifying) accounting choice**: depreciation on the revalued land/building
  portion is charged directly against the Revaluation Reserve, bypassing the P&L, inflating both reported PAT
  and the net-worth denominator used in the company's own ROE/ROCE disclosure (Note 4b p.57; Note 13 footnote
  p.62; Note 51 p.77).
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: single-customer concentration reportedly cited by ICRA at 40-55% of revenue is
  NOT FOUND anywhere in the audited Notes; the entity discloses itself as a single business segment with no
  customer-level revenue breakdown (Note 40 p.73).
- **Recurring drafting-integrity pattern**: at least 4 distinct arithmetic tie-failures across 5 notes in the
  same filing (Note 31; Note 45 internal and vs Board's Report; Note 29b vs Note 30), plus a KMP remuneration
  percentage mismatch — none individually material in rupee terms (except the ₹5.47 Cr item above, counted
  separately), but collectively a signal that this filing's numbers should not be taken as internally
  self-verifying without independent cross-checks.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices, with concentrated concern in related-party fairness and
cash-conversion quality. Key concern: operating cash flow turned negative despite reported profit growth,
alongside an undisclosed-terms related-party land advance sitting outside the AGM RPT ceiling. Key strength:
conservative, non-amortized treatment of one-time IPO expenses and a genuinely clean set of CARO/statutory
representations with no going-concern indication. Overall accounting quality: 6/10.
