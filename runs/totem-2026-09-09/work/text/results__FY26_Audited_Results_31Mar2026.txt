requirements that are relevant to our audit of the Standalone Financial Results under the provisions 
of the Act and the Rules thereunder, and we have fulfilled our other ethical responsibilities in 
accordance with these requirements and the ICAl's Code of Ethics. 
We believe that the audit evidence we have obtained is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis 
for our opinion.

Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone Financial Results 
Our objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the Statement as a whole is free

an audit conducted in accordance with SAs will always detect a material misstatement when it exists.

aggregate, they could reasonably be expected to influence the economic decisions of users taken 
on the basis of the Statement.
