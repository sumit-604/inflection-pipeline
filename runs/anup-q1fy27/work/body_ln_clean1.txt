194	HIGHLIGHTS FOR Q1 FY27
195	• Anup reached Revenue & EBITDA of ₹125.2 Cr and ₹9.5 Cr respectively.
196	
197	• Performance during the quarter reflects a planned lower execution due to low order booking during last year, while ongoing global
198	  uncertainties, supply chain challenges, elevated freight and energy costs, added additional pressure on availability and prices of raw material.
199	• Despite challenges on growth front, we chose to wait out, protecting profitability over short term growth.
200	
201	• EBITDA margins were impacted entirely by lower revenue leading to under-absorption of fixed costs, while our gross margin remain intact.
202	
203	• Highest ever order booking during the quarter of ~₹315 Cr leading to best pending orderbook visibility (including LOI) of ₹985 Cr.
204	• Booked order of more than ₹150 Cr for Thermal Power plants, entering in to elite group of manufacturers of Critical Heat-Exchangers for the
205	  sector, which is expected to see significant growth in near future.
206	
207	• Bagged order of two proprietary license products in line with strategic intent to move in to niche segment.
208	
209	• Started execution of two large Air-Cool Hear Exchanger for a marquee customer in Germany.
210	
211	• Good traction in the Technical Services business, supported by strong execution and high customer satisfaction
