Commenting on the Results:

Mr. Om Prakash Lohia — Chairman & Managing Director said, “During Q2 FY27, we delivered a strong
performance, with revenue from operations improving despite a challenging operating environment.
Geopolitical developments in the Middle East led to an increase in crude oil prices, resulting in higher
raw material costs and continued volatility across the polyester value chain.

During the quarter, we reported consolidated total income of 1344.0 crore, EBITDA of X117.7 crore with
an EBITDA margin of 8.8%, and PAT of X74.1 crore. For H1 FY27, consolidated total income stood at
X2284.2 crore, EBITDA at X225.7 crore with a margin of 9.9%, while PAT stood at ¥137.8 crore.


Looking ahead, we remain focused on further strengthening our operating and financial performance. As
part of our growth strategy, we plan to invest approximately X200 crore over the next 12—18 months to
diversify in specialty fibre and filament products. This investment is expected to broaden our product
portfolio, increase our participation in value-added segments and support sustainable growth over the
medium term.”

About Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Ltd

Established in 1986, Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Ltd. is an established Indian polyester manufacturer with an
integrated manufacturing platform at Butibori, Nagpur. The company has built a diversified presence across the
polyester value chain, with products spanning Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF), Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY/PQY), DTY,
polyester chips and bottle-grade PET resin. With 6.72 lakh TPA of polyester production capacity, including 2.52 lakh
TPA of PET resin capacity and 1.75 lakh TPA of DTY capacity, Indo Rama combines manufacturing scale with
downstream capabilities. Its products reach customers across 19 states/UTs in India and 28 countries globally.

The company's manufacturing facility incorporates advanced technologies sourced through collaborations with
leading technology providers from Japan, Germany, the United States and other countries. It is also supported by
stand by 71.08 MW of captive power generation capacity, comprising coal-based and furnace-oil-based systems,
supporting operational reliability.
