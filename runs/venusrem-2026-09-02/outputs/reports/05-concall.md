### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

The corpus offers only one genuinely disclosed shortfall pattern to test
(Venus Pharma GmbH) and one partial delivery (Nepal). Both are handled the
same way: management names the specific structural cause (ongoing
European-operations restructuring; regulatory registration still in
process) rather than blaming external market conditions in general terms,
and the auditors — not management — are the ones surfacing the going-
concern language each quarter it applies. This is closer to
HONEST-ADMISSION / TRANSPARENT-BY-AUDITOR-DISCLOSURE than to
external-blame or deflection. Countervailing point: outside of these two
auditor-forced disclosures, the AR narrative voice is uniformly
self-congratulatory ("What Compounds, Endures," "the strongest year in
our history") and the one internally-authored scorecard (the 8-item R&D
table) contains zero self-rated misses — a pattern more consistent with
selective self-reporting than with proactively surfacing hard topics.
No instance of management saying "we made a mistake" was found anywhere
in the corpus.
