### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **MTF book**: STRENGTHENING/STABLE. Rs 443 Cr (Q2, context) -> Rs 457 Cr (Q3 FY26)
  -> Rs 424 Cr (Q4 FY26, seasonal Q4 dip, flagged and explained by Abhinav Gupta as
  volatility-driven, not structural) -> Rs 470 Cr (Q1 FY27, recovering). The
  Rs 1,000 Cr / two-year target is repeated in Q2, Q3 and Q1 FY27 calls with a
  consistent framing; the interim Rs 650 Cr FY27 milestone (Q4 FY26) is on the
  same glide path. No slippage.
- **PMS**: STRENGTHENING. License obtained (Q2 context) -> incorporated as a
  subsidiary vehicle, AIF filed (Q3 FY26) -> launched, Rs 100 Cr+ AUM, FY27
  target Rs 200 Cr (Q4 FY26) -> Rs 150 Cr AUM at Q1 end, target RAISED to
  ~Rs 250 Cr (Q1 FY27). Genuine, quantified, ahead-of-plan delivery.
- **AIF**: WEAKENING (timeline slippage). "May take time" (Q2 context) ->
  "license applied... new company formed" (Q3 FY26) -> "approval expected end
  of Q2 [FY27], operations to start this financial year" (Q4 FY26, 20-May-2026)
  -> "still working with regulatory things... launch in Q3 [FY27]" (Q1 FY27,
  27-Jul-2026). The launch quarter moved from Q2 FY27 to Q3 FY27 inside one
  quarter of guidance. FLAG: timeline slippage.
- **Silverleaf merger**: WEAKENING, then QUIETLY DE-EMPHASISED. "SEBI approval
  received, in NCLT, advanced stage, NCLT approval should come by end of this
  quarter [Q4 FY26]" (Q3 FY26) -> "still in NCLT... we hope it is in the last
  leg... maximum quarter's time" (Q4 FY26, i.e. push to ~Q1/Q2 FY27) -> in
  Q1 FY27, Silverleaf is mentioned only in reply to an analyst's question about
  "the tech company acquired from Bombay," with historical background (12-year
  track record, IIT Bombay founders, geographic-expansion rationale) but **no
  NCLT status update at all**, despite management having volunteered NCLT
  timing unprompted in the two prior calls. FLAG: dropped trigger, quiet
  disappearance of a proactive update.
- **uTrade multi-broker expansion**: WEAKENING, effectively DROPPED. "Next
  financial year, core management focus... offer to multi-brokers... 2x-3x
  revenue growth in 1-2 years" (Q3 FY26) -> analyst-prompted reveal that a new
  SEBI circular makes multi-broker infrastructure "extremely costly," so the
  plan is "facing some challenges" (Q4 FY26, only surfaced because Murtaza
  asked) -> no mention of multi-broker plans at all in Q1 FY27; only
  subscription-count metrics (+20% QoQ) are given. FLAG: dropped trigger.
  Management never revisited this setback proactively after Q4 FY26.
- **Prop-to-client mix**: the numerator and denominator change every quarter
  (see 2D); nominally STRENGTHENING in management's telling (volume share of
  client business: >53% "for the first time" in Q4 FY26, 60% in Q1 FY27) but
  the metric being reported shifts between turnover/volume, revenue and
  profitability across the three calls, making the trend impossible to verify
  from the calls alone. Cross-checked against the FY26 Annual Report's own
  BRSR Annexure (73.47% of turnover from "Trading in Securities" vs 15.19%
  from "Stock Broking Services," per B03), the "60% client" claim as stated in
  concalls is a volume metric that does not reconcile with the revenue split
  the company itself files elsewhere. FLAG: potentially misleading framing,
  carried across two consecutive calls.
- **RBI prop-desk funding curb**: NEW in Q4 FY26 (25-30% intraday-limit impact,
  managed via bank-guarantee conversion), followed up and reaffirmed as
  contained in Q1 FY27 (best-ever quarter delivered through the transition
  window). Genuine proactive disclosure and consistent explanation across two
  quarters; no slippage.
- **Branch network**: STEADY. Two branches (Q2 context) -> five-pilot-branch
  plan named but not yet opened (Q3 FY26) -> seven branches operational, six
  more targeted by end of FY27 (Q4 FY26) -> same seven-branch list repeated in
  Q1 FY27 with no net addition reported in the quarter, plus the first
  quantified unit-economics claim (8-month payback). Consistent, on the stated
  pace, but Q1 FY27 shows zero incremental branches versus Q4 FY26's count,
  worth watching next quarter.

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every miss or slippage identified above is explained by an EXTERNAL cause: a
new SEBI circular (uTrade multi-broker), NCLT court pace (Silverleaf),
"regulatory things" (AIF), or market volatility (MTF Q4 dip, margin
compression). No instance across three calls of management saying "we made a
mistake" or naming an internal execution shortfall. Hard topics (RBI funding
curb, the SEBI prop-desk-cap rumour, the stock's poor price performance) are
raised more often in response to analyst pressure than volunteered
unprompted, though the RBI topic was volunteered in Q4 FY26's opening remarks
(a genuine proactive disclosure) before being defended in Q&A.
Classification: **external-blame-heavy**, with one instance of proactive
disclosure (RBI curb, Q4 FY26 opening remarks) offsetting the pattern
slightly.

### 2C. Tone ratings (1-5, with evidence)

- **Transparency: 2/5.** No subsidiary (uTrade, Algowire) ever gets a revenue
  number across three calls, only client/subscription counts. The prop-vs-
  client metric is stated in three different units (turnover/volume, revenue,
  profitability) across the three calls without ever reconciling them.
  Material balance-sheet items (pledge, contingent liabilities, negative
  operating cash flow, the May-2026 NCD early-redemption resolution) are never
  raised.
- **Specificity: 3/5.** Concrete, repeated numbers for MTF, PMS AUM, ADTO,
  institutional client counts, branch counts, and (new in Q1 FY27) branch unit
  economics. Vague on subsidiary revenue and monetization models (uTrade,
  Algowire, Silverleaf never get a hard number beyond "2x-3x in 1-2 years").
- **Consistency: 3/5.** Operational KPIs (MTF, PMS, institutional clients)
  track consistently quarter to quarter. The prop/client split is the
  exception: Abhinav Gupta gives "52% profitability standalone / 49%
  consolidated" from prop in one breath in Q1 FY27, then "58-60% of revenue"
  from prop moments later in the same call, a shift an analyst (Chirag Sehgal)
  had to ask to clarify.
- **Accountability: 2/5.** Zero self-attributed misses found in three
  transcripts; every slippage is externally sourced (regulator, court,
  market).
- **Defensiveness: 3/5.** Generally even-keeled, but one notably defensive
  exchange: when Chirag Sehgal (Q1 FY27) pointed out prop dependency "has not
  come down" as much as claimed, Abhinav Gupta opened with "I'm sorry to cut
  you off on this. I'm not sure where you're getting your data from" before
  restating the historical comparison.
- **Over-promotion: 4/5.** Consistently promotional framing across all three
  calls ("strongest quarterly performance to date," "Zerodha movement for
  algo trading... Share India can take the lead" [Q4 FY26], repeated
  "cautiously optimistic"/"hopeful" language even through a quarter management
  itself calls marked by "rapidly evolving regulatory landscape").

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **The prop-vs-client revenue/profit split, precisely.** Every call repeats a
  version of "client share is rising" but never in the same unit twice
  (volume/turnover in Q3 and Q1 FY27 remarks by Kamlesh Shah, profitability in
  Q4 FY26 remarks by Sachin/Abhinav Gupta, revenue only when an analyst forces
  the distinction). The AR's own BRSR Annexure discloses a materially
  different picture (73.47% of turnover from Trading in Securities vs 15.19%
  from broking, per B03) that management's "60% client" framing across two
  calls does not reconcile with. Likely reason for the framing choice: the
  volume/turnover metric is the most favourable of the three available cuts.
- **The May-2026 Board-approved NCD early redemption** (per AR Note 17e/18e,
  the original NCD end-use change was refused by holders/Trustee, prompting
  the Board's 19-May-2026 pivot to early redemption). This event predates the
  Q1 FY27 call (27-Jul-2026) by over two months and is directly relevant to
  the Company's own disclosed DSCR caveat, yet it is not mentioned in that
  call at all.
- **Negative operating cash flow** (CFO/PAT -0.55x standalone, -0.56x
  consolidated in FY26, per B03) is never raised in any of the three calls,
  despite balance-sheet growth (MTF book +69.8% YoY) being funded by new
  debt/NCDs rather than retained cash.
- **Contingent liabilities growth** (+48.3% YoY to Rs 3,233 Cr, ~123% of net
  worth, per B01/B03) is not raised; bank-guarantee discussion in Q4 FY26 is
  framed entirely around the RBI-curb workaround, not the underlying growth in
  guarantee exposure itself.
- **The promoter pledge level** (BSE SHP: rising to 57.80% of promoter holding
  by Jun-2026) is not mentioned in any of the three calls; no analyst asked
  about it either.
Likely reason for silence on the last three: none is flattering, and no
analyst on these three calls asked directly (the closest is Sanjeev Pandya's
SEBI prop-cap question in Q4 FY26, which management answered, and the RBI
funding question, also answered — but nobody asked about cash flow,
contingent liabilities or the pledge).

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. Competitor commentary and credibility check

- **Zerodha, Groww, Kotak Bank** named (Q3 FY26, analyst Nitin's question) as
  aggressive MTF advertisers; management's response ("we are more of a
  physical presence, brick-and-mortar player... not targeting mass-mass")
  is a credible positioning statement but self-serving and unverified against
  any hard market-share data.
- **Motilal Oswal, Dhan** named (Q4 FY26) as prospective uTrade multi-broker
  partners; the plan stalled on the SEBI vendor-infrastructure circular before
  any deal closed — credible because the obstacle (a named circular) is
  specific and checkable, even though the outcome (no partnership) is a miss.
- **"We have performed far, far better [than peers]"** (Kamlesh Shah, Q1
  FY27) — an unqualified, unquantified competitive claim. Credibility: LOW as
  stated (no metric, no named peer); flagged to stage 6 for peer verification.
- Smaller/discount MTF competitors referenced generically in the earlier Q2
  FY26 context call (yield-cutting peer) — consistent with the funding-cost
  and NCD/CP diversification strategy repeated in the three primary calls.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence dropped in the calls

- GDP growth estimate 7.4-8.2% for 2026, RBI repo rate at 5.25% after a 25 bps
  Dec-2025 cut (Q3 FY26, Kamlesh Shah opening remarks).
- Commodity market (gold, silver, crude) volatility cited as a structural
  ADTO driver in all three calls; Q3 FY26 ADTO growth quantified at 29%
  quarter-on-quarter (Rs 7,500 Cr to Rs 9,700 Cr), attributed partly to
  commodities.
- RBI's prop-desk funding curb (no bank intraday limits for proprietary
  trading, effective ~1-Jul-2026) and management's estimate that "Rs 40,000
  to Rs 50,000 crores worth of capital will be taken out from the market" as
  a result (Sachin Gupta, Q4 FY26) — a strong, quantified, checkable
  industry-wide claim.
- SEBI reportedly having no formal prop-book-size cap despite a Jane
  Street-scandal-driven media narrative (Q4 FY26, in response to Sanjeev
  Pandya); management frames this as "more of a media hype than regulatory
  action."
- Product-suitability-framework discussion (raised in the Q2 FY26 context
  call) as an industry-association ask to SEBI, alongside a push to reinstate
  Bank Nifty weekly options — background only, not repeated in the three
  primary calls.
- Q1 FY27: management describes the quarter as delivered "despite" SEBI
  derivative-segment measures and RBI prop-desk funding norms, positioning
  the company's diversified funding (CP rated Crisil A1+, NCD programme) as
  the industry differentiator versus "smaller players" who "would find it

## SECTION 4: KEY TAKEAWAYS & TRIGGERS SUMMARY

### 4A. Investment-ready trigger list (ranked by earnings impact)

1. **MTF book scale-up to Rs 1,000 Cr.** Type: Volume. Timeframe: Medium (2
   yrs). Conviction: M. Confirms: quarterly MTF book prints hold the Rs 424-
   470 Cr trajectory and Rs 650 Cr FY27 interim milestone. Kills: a sustained
   MTF book contraction below ~Rs 400 Cr for two consecutive quarters, or a
   funding-cost blow-out that erases the direct-branch margin advantage.
2. **Tier-3 branch rollout economics (8-month payback, 30 branches).** Type:
   Volume+Margin. Timeframe: Medium. Conviction: M. Confirms: net new branch
   openings each quarter and reported per-branch MTF ramp toward the Rs 15 Cr
   in 8 months bar. Kills: branch count stalling (already flat Q4 FY26 to
   Q1 FY27) or management invoking the 12-month hard-stop on multiple
   branches.
3. **Prop-to-client mix shift, verified on a consistent metric.** Type:
   Price-mix. Timeframe: Long. Conviction: L (credibility concern, see 2D).
   Confirms: a single, consistently-defined revenue AND profit split
   disclosed the same way two quarters running, reconciling with the AR's
   BRSR segment split. Kills: continued shifting of the metric definition, or
   a BRSR/segment-note split that keeps showing >70% prop-linked revenue.
4. **PMS/AIF/wealth fee ramp.** Type: Price-mix. Timeframe: Near-medium.
   Conviction: M. Confirms: PMS AUM continuing to outrun its own stated
   targets (as it did Q4 FY26 to Q1 FY27); AIF actually launching in Q3 FY27
   as now guided. Kills: a further AIF timeline slip past Q3 FY27, or PMS AUM
   growth stalling below Rs 200 Cr.
5. **Silverleaf merger completion.** Type: Inorganic. Timeframe: Medium.
   Conviction: L (timeline slipping, now undisclosed status). Confirms: NCLT
   approval news and Silverleaf's Rs 50-60 Cr revenue actually appearing in
   FY27 consolidated numbers. Kills: another quarter passing with no
   management update on NCLT status.
6. **RBI/SEBI regulatory transition absorbed without margin damage.** Type:
   Regulatory-policy. Timeframe: Near. Conviction: M. Confirms: continued
   strong quarters through the FY27 transition window, as Q1 FY27 showed.
   Kills: a quarter where bank-guarantee/limit constraints visibly compress
   prop-desk profitability.
7. **Institutional broking growth.** Type: Volume. Timeframe: Near.
   Conviction: H (cleanest delivering metric). Confirms: continued client-
   count growth quarter on quarter. Kills: a quarter of flat or declining
   institutional empanelment.
