## 2. THE 25% ROCE ANCHOR — THE ONE NUMBER THAT MOVES EVERYTHING (stress test)

The operator override to a **25% pre-Ind AS mature ROCE** (deliberation Override 1) is the single largest sensitivity in the file (11-valuation Section 1B independent-divergence note). It is NOT from printed history — best lease-inclusive year ~8.97%, ten-year median 3.25% (B01). It sits above the operator's *own opening read*: the operator first said **"the future ROC by FY30 is around 15%, that is the base,"** then overrode to **"Use 25% pre-Ind AS ROCE"** (deliberation Override 1, operator's words).

**Why the mature ROCE plausibly lands BELOW 25%:**
- Pricing power is "weak" (B04 pricing_power). SSSG is explicitly volume-led, no price hikes (B05 trigger 1). A format that cannot price cannot defend margin against input inflation.
- Gross margin COMPRESSED (68.5%→65.5%) while peers expanded (B06). The margin recovery is the softest, thrice-missed leg (B05).
- Permanent employee attrition is 112% FY25, rising from 88.2% FY23 (B03 monitorables; B04 flag) — a structural cost drag on a people-intensive live-grill format scaling to 400–425 stores.
- Rising peer competition and value-price wars (B06 contradicted) cap pricing further.

**What a lower mature ROCE does to fair value and the entry zone** (my recompute on the framework, same FY30 base EPS Rs 25.41 and cash 1.15x / growth +2.0x stack):

| Mature ROCE assumption | Pillar-1 base PE (0.5×ROCE+7.5) | Destination PE (×1.15 +2) | FY30 base target | Entry @25% (÷1.953) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **25% (operator override, governing)** | 20.0x | **25.0x** | **Rs 635** | **Rs 325** |
| 22% (low end of operator's cited 22–25 range) | 18.5x | 23.3x | Rs 592 | Rs 303 |
| 18% (still double the best printed year) | 16.5x | 21.0x | Rs 534 | Rs 273 |
| **15% (operator's OWN opening read)** | 15.0x | 19.25x | Rs 489 | Rs 250 |

**The finding.** At the operator's own first-instinct 15% ROCE, the additive destination collapses to ~19.25x — essentially the RRM track's 17.5x (11-valuation Section 1B). Fair value drops to ~Rs 489 and the honest entry zone drops to ~Rs 250, not Rs 325. **The override to 25% did not make this a buy — it made the AVOID's re-entry zone Rs 40–75 too generous.** The AVOID at CMP is not weakened by attacking the anchor; it is *reinforced*, and the re-entry price the operator will act on is optimistic. This is the more important devil finding than the CMP verdict, which no reading disturbs.

**Is the AVOID TOO HARSH via the anchor?** No. To make Rs 772 clear the 25% hurdle you would need a destination PE of ~30x on FY30 base EPS (25.41 × 30 = Rs 762 ≈ CMP → ~0% CAGR). Even at the **absolute Hotels sector cap of 30x** — the maximum the entire framework permits — the base case delivers ~0% CAGR and the bull (43.35 × 30 = Rs 1,300) delivers only +19.0% 3yr CAGR (HR 1.68 < 1.953, still STOP). **There is no admissible exit multiple in the framework at which Rs 772 clears the hurdle.** The AVOID is robust to the multiple in both directions.

## 3. THE SHARED CATALYST — SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE (mandatory stress test)

Section 1B flags SHARED CATALYST: store rollout + SSSG recovery drives **both** Pillar 1 ROCE (the 25% mature anchor rests on the FY26 cohort maturing and back-end cost falling 7.1%→6.5%) **and** Pillar 3a growth premium (+2.0x) (11-valuation Pillar 3; deliberation Section 5). A single execution miss collapses two pillars at once.

**The trip mechanism is dated and specific.** The deliberation itself states: "If SSSG falls to low single digits as the H2 FY26 base is lapped, revenue and margin drop to 0 each and the composite falls to +2" (deliberation Section 1). The H2 FY26 base gets lapped from **Q3 FY27** (B05 trigger 1 kill_signal). The task-cited Q1 FY27 SSSG surge (+28.7%) and the Q4 FY26 14.4% (B05, the anchored figure) are both measured against a *contracting* base — FY25 SSSG −3.8%, Q1 FY26 −3.4% (B01 flag, ICRA). A large positive print off a negative base is arithmetic, not proof of durable double-digit throughput.

**What the joint failure does:** if SSSG lapses to low single digit, (a) Pillar 3a +2.0x → 0, (b) the FY30 cohort-maturation story that underwrites the 25% ROCE stalls, dropping the defensible ROCE toward the mechanical RECOVERING blend of ~5.4% (11-valuation independent-divergence note), and (c) FY30 EPS falls as margin fails to leverage. The destination PE and the earnings collapse *together*. Fair value would fall toward the Bear Rs 229 (11-valuation 4A). **For the AVOID at CMP this is confirmatory.** For any *future* buyer at Rs 289–325 it is the dominant risk: the shared catalyst is precisely why the entry conjunction (price in-zone AND no trigger fired) is mandatory — a price that falls into the zone *because* SSSG collapsed is a withdrawn zone, not an opportunity (14-thesis Section 7).

## 5. VALUE TRAP TEST

**Is the AVOID hiding a compounder (false negative)?** The bull tells would be: FTTCP +4 turnaround, first positive PAT in Q1 FY27 (+Rs 3.09 cr owners' share; B01), strong cash conversion (CFO/EBITDA 115.6% FY26; deliberation Section 5), negative working capital. These are real. But a compounder needs *returns on reinvested capital*, and a decade of statutory ROCE (median 3.25%, best 8.97%; B01) plus weak pricing power (B04) plus 112% attrition (B03) says the reinvestment does not compound at 25%. The 25% is conviction, not observation (deliberation Override 1). This is the classic value-trap inversion: strong operating cash flow that funds store rollout at sub-cost-of-capital returns looks like a compounder and behaves like a treadmill.

**Melting ice cube check:** CDR is the slowest-growing organised sub-segment (B04 mgmt_questions); QSR and cloud kitchens grow faster. UFBL's "first_deterioration_signal" for the structural risk is the CDR format growing slower than QSR (B04). Not melting, but structurally slow, in a format with weak pricing power. The AVOID is the correct read; the value-trap risk cuts *against* a future buyer, not against the AVOID.

## 6. VARIANT PERCEPTION CHECK

**The thesis's variant perception** is subtle: it agrees with the market that this is not a buy at Rs 772, but disagrees on *why it is worth Rs 772 to anyone* — the thesis implicitly holds that the fair destination is ~25x and the market is overpaying. **Argue the market is right and the thesis is too generous:** the market at Rs 772 may not be "overpaying for a good business" — it may be a momentum/first-positive-PAT/QSR-basket bid that will unwind, AND the framework's own governing 25x is above what the RRM track (17.5x) and the mechanical read (10.2x) say (11-valuation Section 1B). On both counts the operator's approved base is the *upper* bound, not a conservative one. The genuine variant-perception risk in this file is that the AVOID's re-entry zone (Rs 289–325) is itself built on the most generous defensible multiple stack; the market-clearing value is more likely the RRM Rs 445 / entry Rs 228 (11-valuation 4E). The market is not obviously wrong to price the equity below where the additive track lands over time.

**Where the market could be right against the AVOID's mildness:** the thesis grants "investable business." A harder read of B01 (AVOID, 5 deal-breakers), B12c (combined assessment AVOID, not TURNAROUND), B04 (weak pricing power, cyclical) and B08 (governance CAUTION with interlock) is that this is a *low-quality cyclical*, and "investable at the right price" flatters it. That would not change the CMP verdict but would argue for a still-lower re-entry and smaller size.

## 7. PRE-MORTEM (it is 2028, a Rs 325 re-entry is down 40% to ~Rs 195)

*Written for the future-buyer scenario, since no position is held at CMP.*

The store rollout continued to 300+ but the FY26 SSSG cohort never matured into the promised 17–18% ROM. Through H2 FY27 the H2 FY26 base was lapped and consolidated SSSG fell from double digit to 3–4% (the dated kill signal; B05), then flat, as the GST demand pull faded and the three peers who had ridden the same tailwind (B06) normalised. Gross margin, missed for a fourth and fifth call, held around 64–65% — weak pricing power (B04) meant Middle East input inflation and the domestic LPG/gas cost shock the peers flagged (B06 risks_peers_raise, not in UFBL's own risk list) were absorbed, not passed on. 112% attrition (B03) kept training and labour costs elevated as the network scaled.

With SSSG and margin both soft, the 25% mature-ROCE conviction anchor was quietly abandoned; the market re-based the multiple toward the RRM/mechanical read (17.5x / 10.2x; 11-valuation Section 1B). Pillar 3a's +2.0x evaporated with the growth. Net debt, guided to hold at ~Rs 100–102 cr, breached the ceiling as internally-funded expansion outran a stalled operating recovery (B05 trigger 4 kill_signal; FLAG-CASH falsifier). FY30 pre-Ind AS EPS came in nearer the Bear Rs 9 than the Base Rs 25 (11-valuation Section 2), and the stock settled toward the Bear Rs 229 (11-valuation 4A) — below even the Rs 325 re-entry. The shared catalyst did exactly what the flag warned: two pillars fell on one miss.

A governance overhang ran in parallel: a further RPT expansion with the Sayaji group (29.69% holder and rent/purchase counterparty; B03) and an unresolved Rule 11(g) audit-trail limitation (B03) kept a discount on the multiple the recovery case never overcame.
