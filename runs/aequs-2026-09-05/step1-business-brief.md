## WHAT IT MAKES AND SELLS
- Precision contract manufacturer. Two engines under one integrated
  ecosystem model (Belagavi, Karnataka SEZ cluster).
- Aerospace: precision machined components, forgings, and aerostructures for
  global OEMs (commercial aero supply chain). ~83% of FY26 revenue.
- Consumer: consumer-electronics and durables contract manufacturing (toys,
  and a new consumer-electronics build-out). ~17% of FY26 revenue.
- Model is build-to-spec / outsourcing partner: certification barriers, long
  design-win cycles, capex-heavy capacity, customer-driven ramps.

## WHERE THE MARGIN COMES FROM
- Aerospace carries the group margin: mature utilisation (70-78%), spec-in
  content, long contracts. Consumer is margin-dilutive today: heavy
  depreciation on new consumer-electronics lines plus low utilisation (22%)
  drive the net loss. Management frames the loss as ramp cost, not structural.
  [investing.com] This ARTIFACT-vs-STRUCTURAL call is the run's core question.

## CUSTOMER AND EXPORT PROFILE
- Aerospace is export-led, tied to global commercial-aero OEM build rates and
  the USD 1 bn+ order book. Customer names and concentration are a
  filing-verify item (RHP / annual report).

## THE ONE INFLECTION THE THESIS RESTS ON
- Consumer segment ramp turning from loss to contribution as utilisation
  climbs from 22%, while the aerospace order book (USD 1 bn+) converts to
  revenue. The thesis is: today's widening loss is the cost of two capacity
  build-outs that have not yet filled. If utilisation climbs on schedule, the
  loss inverts to operating leverage. If it stalls, the loss is structural.

## PEERS SELECTED (Step C — economic-engine match, not sector basket)
Aequs has a dual engine: aerospace precision manufacturing + consumer EMS.
The trio spans both. Screener URLs need a local 200-check (screener is

1. Azad Engineering (AZAD, BSE 544061) — precision machined/forged aero and
   energy components, export, design-win and capex-ramp dynamics. Closest
   listed comp for the aerospace precision engine.
2. Dynamatic Technologies (DYNAMATECH, BSE 505242) — aerostructures Tier-1
   for Airbus/Boeing/Bell; order-book-driven build-to-print + assembly. The
   purest aerospace order-book comp.
3. Dixon Technologies (DIXON, BSE 540699) — India EMS / consumer-electronics
   contract-manufacturing leader; mirrors the consumer engine's capex-heavy,
   low-initial-margin ramp. Structural comp for the consumer engine.
No single listed name mirrors both engines at Aequs's scale; the trio is
chosen to bracket the two engines.
