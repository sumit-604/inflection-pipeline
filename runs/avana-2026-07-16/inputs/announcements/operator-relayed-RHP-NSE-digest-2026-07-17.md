## 1. Capacity expansion & manufacturing footprint (RHP)
- Relocating two existing units into a single integrated manufacturing unit on
  KIADB land at Avverahalli Industrial Area, near Bengaluru. Stated purpose:
  consolidate the two operational units into one integrated unit, streamline
  operations, optimize resource utilization.
- Capacity change post-relocation:
  - Protection relays: 70,000 -> 175,000 units (+150%)
  - Control & relay panels: 600 -> 1,500 units (+150%)
  - Built-up area: ~12,500 -> ~58,286 sq ft
- Timeline (RHP): civil + MEP targeted complete by Mar to mid-Apr 2026; trial
  run Apr to early May 2026; commercial production targeted mid-May 2026.
- No workforce change (existing workforce reused); no significant supplier or
  customer impact (continuity of supply chain); downtime risk low (movable
  tools/equipment, not heavy fixed machinery).
- KIADB regulatory deadlines: extension to start commercial production up to
  22-May-2026, and project implementation up to Oct-2026. RHP warns failure to
  start production by these dates could lead to consequences including potential
  loss of the land.
- NUANCE: As of the last six months of public disclosures, NO exchange
  announcement confirms the new plant actually STARTED commercial production in
  May 2026. Only the planned schedule plus IPO-proceeds monitoring/clarification.

## 2. Product, R&D, operational model (RHP)
- Product lines: control & relay panels (11 kV-220 kV); numerical protection
  relays; electromechanical relays and accessories; substation automation
  panels, relay test blocks, semaphore indicators, busbar protection panels.
- Asset-light assembly model: major fabrication OUTSOURCED (mechanical, non-core);
  company does assembling, wiring, relay fixing, termination, testing, dispatch.
- R&D: in-house engineers + firmware/software developers; R&D salary spend rising
  FY23-FY25 (incremental development, not a one-off shift).
- Capacity ramp is SPACE-DRIVEN, not machinery-driven: "does not require
  additional machinery… only requires additional physical space… components…
  purchased from vendors."

## 3. Customers, order book, markets (RHP, late-2025 data)
- Order book as of 30-Nov-2025: total pending orders Rs 5,223.65 lakh (= Rs 52.24 Cr).
- Customers served: FY23 275 -> FY24 307 -> FY25 367. Repeat customers: 128 -> 150 -> 163.
- Geography: ~48% of H1 FY26 revenue from Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka.
- Channel (H1 FY26): ~76% private players, ~23% tenders/government, minor dealers.
- Exports: commenced to a Kuwait customer in FY26; H1 FY26 export revenue ~Rs 14.2 lakh.
  Intent to expand exports via overseas dealers / representative offices.
- No new exchange filing Mar-Jun 2026 announces large fresh orders or a materially
  changed order book; those remain anchored to the RHP disclosures.

## 4. Supply chain & working capital (RHP)
- Raw materials ~98% domestic (H1 FY26), small imports mainly from Germany.
- No long-term supplier contracts; top-10 suppliers a bit under 60% of purchase value.
- Logistics fully outsourced.
- H1 FY26 net working-capital cycle ~245 days, with high inventory days.
- IPO proceeds include Rs 860 lakh (Rs 8.6 Cr) earmarked for working capital, to be
  largely deployed in FY26, to support larger scale and longer cycle.
