### (c) Toray sensitivity blocks: PARTLY RESOLVED (labels absent; mapping rests on inference)
- The six sensitivity statements print with no year label (Prospectus p.323, Note 46). In print order: (1) growth +/-0.50%: +2.82 / -2.60; (2) discount rate +/-1%: -13.68 / +16.15; (3) growth +/-1%: +3.80 / -10.94; (4) discount rate +/-1%: -9.06 / +10.60; (5) discount rate +/-1%: -31.41 / +40.33; (6) growth +/-1%: +28.47 / -22.78. NOT RESOLVED at the page: which block belongs to which year-end.
- Mapping by size test [INFERENCE]. Only Mar 2024 has growth 0.50%, which fits block (1) to Mar 2024. A discount-rate step moves value by roughly 1/(rate minus growth). Mar 2024 (13.30% less 0.50% = 12.8) and Mar 2025 (17.60% less 5.00% = 12.6) should show similar, modest swings. Mar 2026 (14.00% less 5.00% = 9.0) should show the largest. That gives Mar 2024 = blocks (1) and (4); Mar 2025 = (2) and (3); Mar 2026 = (5) and (6).
- If that mapping is right, then at Mar 2026 a 1-point discount rate move changes the 154.19 carrying value by -20.4% / +26.2% (derived), and a 1-point growth move by +18.5% / -14.8% (derived). Block (3) is odd under any mapping: a growth increase adds less (3.80) than a decrease removes (10.94), the reverse of normal convexity. 🟡 (Level 3, investee is a related party).
- Fair-value movement against inputs: discount rate fell 17.60% to 14.00% (360 bp) while carrying value rose only 3.0% (149.66 to 154.19) (Prospectus p.323, Note 46; Prospectus p.301, Note 10). [INFERENCE] The forecast cash flows were cut by about as much as the rate fell. The note gives no revenue or margin inputs, only discount rate, terminal growth, beta, market premium.
- Implied risk-free rate if the discount rate is beta times market premium plus a risk-free rate only: 4.75% (Mar 2026), 7.34% (Mar 2025), 5.50% (Mar 2024) (derived from the printed inputs). [INFERENCE] The note gives no build-up, so a size premium or other term may exist. The swing is large and unexplained.
- Per-share value (derived): 154.19 / 72,000 shares = INR 2,141.5, against purchase cost 108.00 (cash flow, Prospectus p.283) / 72,000 = INR 1,500 and face INR 1,000 (Prospectus p.301, Note 10). Ownership % NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT.
