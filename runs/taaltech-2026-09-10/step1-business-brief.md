## STRUCTURE AND CORPORATE HISTORY
- Taneja Aerospace and Aviation Ltd demerged its air-charter business and its
  investment in TAAL Tech India Pvt Ltd into TAAL Enterprises Ltd, effective
  21 August 2015 under a Madras High Court scheme.
  Source: https://taal.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Court_Convened_Meeting_2015.pdf
- Charter operations stopped after an aircraft accident. TAAL Tech India Pvt Ltd
  was then merged into the listed parent. Standalone total assets moved from
  Rs19cr (Mar 2024) to Rs207cr (Mar 2025) on that merger, so the standalone
  series breaks at FY25 and only the consolidated series is continuous.
  Source: https://www.screener.in/company/TAALTECH/
- 1:5 stock split approved at the 2 September 2026 AGM, record date
  22 September 2026. Face value moves Rs10 to Rs2.

## PEERS SELECTED (economic engine: billable-engineer ER&D outsourcing)
1. Tata Elxsi Ltd (TATAELXSI). https://www.screener.in/company/TATAELXSI/consolidated/
   Why it fits: the listed ceiling for design-led ER&D margin. ROCE 60.0%,
   market cap Rs21,203cr, TTM sales growth 10%. It marks the quality rung
   TAAL Tech's 30% OPM implicitly claims to sit near.
2. Cyient Ltd (CYIENT). https://www.screener.in/company/CYIENT/consolidated/
   Why it fits: the closest service-line map. Plant engineering, aerospace,
   transportation and networks, sold the same way. FY26 sales Rs7,268cr, OPM 12%,
   ROCE 12.3%. It sets what the same service lines earn at scale.
3. Onward Technologies Ltd (ONWARDTEC). https://www.screener.in/company/ONWARDTEC/consolidated/
   Why it fits: the size and micro-cap dynamics comp. ER&D pure-play, FY26 sales
   Rs544cr, OPM 13%, ROCE 22.9%, 2,800+ engineers, same client-concentration and
   utilisation mechanics at a comparable scale.
No listed Indian ER&D firm of TAAL Tech's revenue size earns a 30% operating
margin, so a like-for-like margin peer does not exist. The three above bracket
the margin ladder instead: 60% ROCE at the top, 12% OPM at scale, 13% OPM at
similar size. AXISCADES and ASM Technologies were considered and dropped:
AXISCADES carries a defence-order-book engine, ASM carries a products-plus-
services mix, and neither is a cleaner engine match than the three chosen.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority for every stage)
1. **The 30% operating margin.** FY25 OPM rose from 25% to 32% while revenue
   fell from Rs187cr to Rs185cr. No listed Indian ER&D peer of any size earns
   this. Verify in the FY26 and FY25 annual report P&L: is the margin operating,
   or does other income, forex gain, or treasury income sit inside it? Name the
   exact P&L lines and the note references.
2. **The Q1 FY27 revenue jump.** Rs64.81cr against Rs45.77cr, up 41.6%, after
   three years at 7% CAGR. Verify from the Q1 FY27 results filing and the FY26
   annual report whether the step is organic, one large client, a scope or
   consolidation change, or an acquisition. Name the evidence either way.
3. **Cash conversion.** Consolidated CFO fell to Rs18.74cr in FY26 against PAT
   Rs56.72cr, a 0.33x conversion, down from 0.84x in FY25, while working capital
   days moved 77 to 120 and receivables rose Rs38.61cr to Rs52.04cr. Verify
   receivables and unbilled-revenue ageing in the FY26 annual report notes, the
   composition of the Rs143.89cr held in investments, and whether the fall is
   growth-induced or structural. This is the FLAG-CASH determination.
4. **Related-party exposure to the Taneja group.** Taneja Aerospace and Aviation
   Ltd is a separately listed entity of the same promoter lineage. Verify the
   FY26 related-party note in full: balances, transactions, guarantees, and any
   receivable from group entities.

## STANDING CAUTION
Screener's sector label ("Airline Industry") and the FY16-FY21 P&L rows both
carry the aircraft-charter past. The FY16 loss and the FY21 profit spike are
not engineering-services results. Stages must not read the pre-merger years as
an ER&D operating history without checking the segment note first.
