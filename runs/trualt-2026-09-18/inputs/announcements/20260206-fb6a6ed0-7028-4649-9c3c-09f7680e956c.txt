Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 – Outcome of Writ 
Petition before the Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that the Hon’ble High Court of Karnataka, 
vide its order dated February 4, 2026, has allowed the writ petition filed by the Company in relation to 
the shortfall quantity of 1,56,292 KL of ethanol, having an estimated value of approximately ₹1,075 
crore, allocated for Q3 and Q4 of ESY 2024–25 to the Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), and has 
issued a writ of mandamus directing the concerned OMCs to consider the Company’s representation 
dated October 23, 2025. 
 
Pursuant to the said order, the Company may be enabled to supply the contracted quantities within an 
extended timeline, which could have a positive impact on the Company’s financial performance. 
 
Further, in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations and Clause 1 of Part A of 
Schedule III thereof, read with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 
11, 2024, we are enclosing the requisite disclosure details as Annexure-A to this letter. 
 
The aforementioned information is also available on the Company’s website: www.trualtbioenergy.com.
