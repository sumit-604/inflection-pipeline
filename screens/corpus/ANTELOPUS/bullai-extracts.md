## Independent check: India Ratings press release, 09-Jun-2026
- Rating: proposed bank loan facilities INR 3,000 mn, IND A/Stable/IND A1, Assigned.
  Standalone view. Sector tagged "Oil Exploration & Production". Net leverage "NM".
  Interest coverage 217.3x FY26 (181.8x FY25). (p.1, p.2, p.4, p.5)
- **Reserves.** "At FYE26, the company held proven and probable (2P) reserves of oil and
  gas at 10.68 million barrels of oil (MMbbl) and 296 billion cubic feet (bcf)". "The
  in-place volumes are significantly higher than the 2P reserves because 2P reserves
  reporting is restricted to the duration of the current RSC/PSC validity period. The
  total in-place volumes of oil and gas are 195.13MMbbl and 1,371.65bcf." "the current
  production relative to reserves is quite low, making the ramp-up in production crucial."
  (p.2, p.3)
- **Contract validity.** "The PSC for Bakrol, Karjisan and Lohar is valid until 2030,
  while that for Cambay extends until 2029." Dangeru is under a revenue sharing contract.
  Nine contract areas, five producing, four in pre-development or development (D-31, D-11,
  Duaramara, Elao). (p.3)
- **Concentration.** "In FY26, 91% of the overall production came from these two blocks
  [Bakrol and Karjisan] (FY25: 93%, FY24: 89%). The company plans to drill additional wells
  in these two areas, further increasing reliance on these two assets." (p.3)
- **Cost structure and production.** EBITDA INR 1,586 mn FY26 (FY25 1,375 mn, FY24 721.4
  mn); margin 57% FY26 (53%, 44%). Average production 1,402 boepd FY26 (1,240, 778).
  Fixed operating costs "constituting 80%-85% of total costs". (p.2, p.3)
- **Price dependence.** "ASEL derives 80%-85% of its gross revenue from the sale of crude
  oil (FY26: INR2,385.7 million; FY25: INR2,206.6 million) and natural gas (INR456.6
  million; INR433.8 million)". Realised price average USD 69.14/bbl FY26 (FY25 75.36,
  FY24 80). "Due to the oversupply of crude oil and gas in the global market, the
  realisations for crude oil and gas have been under pressure in the last two fiscals."
  Ind-Ra expects USD 90-100/bbl in the short term. (p.3, p.4)
- **Liquidity.** Free cash and equivalents INR 228.8 mn at FYE26, against INR 1,163.4 mn
  at FYE25 and INR 667.8 mn at FYE24. No scheduled debt repayments, no outstanding term
  debt. Working capital "rose to INR 9,971 million in FY26 (FY25: INR 5,236 million;
  FY24: INR 3,476 million), mainly due to higher inventory and receivables" (magnitude
  inconsistent with revenue; direction reliable, decimal place to verify). FY27 capex INR
  2,000-2,100 mn, "entirely funded through internal accruals", growth and discretionary.
  (p.4)
- **Sensitivities.** Positive: sustained volume ramp-up, increased acreage, extension of
  PSCs, reduced concentration. Negative: slower-than-expected ramp-up, higher-than-expected
  capex raising net leverage. (p.4)
- Key rating weaknesses as listed: concentration risk as production skewed towards two
  assets; profitability dependent on crude and natural gas prices; geological risk. (p.2)
