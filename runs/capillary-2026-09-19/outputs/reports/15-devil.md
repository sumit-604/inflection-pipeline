## CONTRADICTIONS THE PIPELINE MISSED (highlighted, per the RULES section)

1. **"Organic" absorbs migrated acquired customers, and the proof gate is measured on it.** The company defines organic business as "Business from all customers on the Capillary platform" (Q1 FY27 deck p.29 glossary; also p.22: "Organic = All revenue from customers on Capillary platform"). The CFO: "organic for us is essentially revenues that are on the Capillary platform" (call p.16). The like-for-like adjustment is stated for NRR only: a migrated Kognitiv customer enters "NRR calculations next year, not in this year" (call p.16). No equivalent adjustment is stated for organic revenue growth. Stage 5 noted the NRR quirk (05-concall 3C item 3). No later stage carried it into the proof gate, ledger row L3, or the 42% "organic CAGR" credited in 09b B5 and 14-thesis 3G.
2. **Management's own long-term slide builds FY30 revenue below the approved bear.** May-2026 deck p.64 and p.51: FY27 organic Rs 673 Cr growing at a 15% CAGR adds "Rs 350 cr more topline in 3 years"; FY27 inorganic Rs 391 Cr settles at "Rs 290-310 Cr" at 75% retention. That is about Rs 1,323 Cr [INFERENCE: 673 + 350 + 300]. Approved bear FY30: Rs 1,358 Cr. Approved base: Rs 1,620 Cr (dossier 6.5).
3. **The acquired-revenue retention record is 68%, the ledger asks for 80%.** May-2026 deck p.48: "Revenue retention: 68% post upgrades (average 3-4 years post M&A)", Rs 265 Cr of first-year revenue down to Rs 181 Cr retained in FY27. ### 1A. Attacking the chains first (Role 2 Section 3.5)

The strongest counters attack the second-order chains. Each block says where the chain breaks, at what tier, and what observation would prove the bear wrong.

**Chain 1 (SI channel): the larger fact is what new ACV is made of.**
- Total new ACV is flat for three years: Rs 114.59 Cr (FY24), Rs 122.36 Cr (FY25) (UDRHP-I p.55), Rs 121.4 Cr (FY26) (AR p.81; May-2026 deck p.41: "Total new ACV 1,224 / 1,214 (-) 1%") [FILED].
- One large healthcare customer made Rs 60.7 Cr of FY25 new ACV and Rs 38.0 Cr of FY26 new ACV (May-2026 deck p.41) [FILED]. Ex that account, new ACV rose from Rs 61.7 Cr to Rs 83.4 Cr (+35%) on 34 to 38 deals.
- [INFERENCE] The new-logo engine ex the account is about Rs 83 Cr a year, 11.4% of FY26 revenue. The FY27 guide is "at least 30%-40% more ... new ACV than last year" (call p.23). On the total base that is Rs 158-170 Cr. The account did not grow in Q1 FY27 (call p.18). If it adds nothing, ex-account new ACV must rise 89-104%. Role 2's confirm line (Rs 157 Cr) tests a near-doubling of the ex-account engine, not a 30% step.
- Bull side at the same bar: TTM new ACV ex the account rose 75% to Rs 92.6 Cr (Q1 FY27 deck p.16); average deal size ex the account rose 22% (May-2026 deck p.41).
- Strength: Moderate. Bear wrong if: TTM ex-account new ACV is Rs 100 Cr or more on the Q2 FY27 deck (Nov-2026) and FY27 new ACV ex the account is Rs 110 Cr or more (May-2027).

**Chain 2 (SessionM bridge): five numbers, and the gap is not debt.**
- (1) USD 20.00 m consideration (AR Note 44 p.259), called "enterprise value ... $20 million" (call p.22). (2) USD 9.00 m paid from IPO money: USD 6,325,655 + 1,332,539 + 1,341,806 (MA report p.9) [FILED]. (3) "net ~$0.6M cash paid" (May-2026 deck p.60). (4) Rs 169.1 mn net of debt-like adjustments (Q1 FY27 deck p.14). (5) "about INR17-odd crores ... $1.5 million ... or probably a couple of million" (call p.7) [MGMT].
- The CFO: the EV "would be adjusted for any net debt items at the time of closing". The CEO: "there is no debt on the SessionM entities ... These are debt-free entities" (call p.22) [MGMT].
- [INFERENCE] The USD 11.0 m deducted between EV and price paid is therefore not debt. In a business that bills ahead, the most likely debt-like item is customer prepayments: service Capillary must now deliver on a platform whose server cost runs at "50% of revenue, actually more" (call p.23). If so, the economic price is closer to USD 13 m (about USD 2 m net cash plus the cost of serving about USD 11 m of prepaid contracts at break-even) than to USD 1.5-2 m, and FY27 operating cash carries the unwind. Results Q1 FY27 p.10 confirms the working-capital determination with Mastercard is still open.
- Bull side: even at USD 20 m EV a USD 32 m ARR book costs about 0.6x ARR [arithmetic]; Adyen paid about 12.5x ARR for Talon.One (dossier V4) [SECONDARY].
- Strength: Strong on disclosure trust; Moderate on value. Bear wrong if: the H1 FY27 notes (Nov-2026) show assumed contract liabilities below Rs 30 Cr and a reconciliation of the five figures.

**Chain 3 (retention before margin): the base rate and the sizing break it.**
- Base rate: the three earlier books earned Rs 265 Cr in their first year and Rs 181 Cr from retained customers in FY27, "68% revenue retention" (May-2026 deck p.48) [MGMT, company deck]. The plan for Kognitiv and SessionM is 75% (May-2026 deck p.51, p.62). Ledger L5 needs 80%. The ledger asks Kognitiv to beat both the record and the plan.
- Kognitiv has already lost customers. The seller paid CAD 3.88 m because "customer contract terminations during the post-acquisition period exceeded the agreed threshold" (Results Q1 FY27 p.11) [FILED]. The AR says Kognitiv brought "over 30 enterprise brands" (AR p.78; "more than 30", AR p.10) [FILED]; the CEO now counts "about 16-17 customers" (call p.11) [MGMT]. Brands and customers can differ; no filing reconciles them.
- Sizing: the bridge credits Rs 27.9 Cr of Kognitiv EBITDA in FY30. The CEO sizes the remaining upside at Rs 10-20 Cr and says some gains are already realised (call p.11); Kognitiv was at "5%, 10% margins" when bought and is "already at like a decent" level (call p.15). [INFERENCE] The bridge credits Rs 8-18 Cr more Kognitiv EBITDA than management claims, and part of what it credits may already sit in the +260 bps printed step. That is a single-credit concern. At 30x the excess is Rs 21.6-48.9 per share [arithmetic: Rs 7.9-17.9 Cr x 0.75 / 8.24 x 30].
- Mechanism: on the May deck, the inorganic book already runs at 57% gross margin in FY27 (p.51). The "30% to 65%" lift the bridge names (Q1 FY27 deck p.9) is mostly cost removal below gross margin. Management also says the customer-facing teams do not change: "in none of our acquisitions do we change the customer-facing teams" (call p.15).
- Bull side: Persuade, Brierley and Rewards+ reached 41.2%, 40.4% and 45% contribution margin (AR p.80) [FILED, company-reported]. The bridge's SessionM lever (Rs 44.6 Cr, about 16% margin by FY30) sits below management's path of 15% "in a year" and 35-40% "over another year" (call p.23).
- Strength: Strong. Bear wrong if: Kognitiv retains 75% or more of its revenue at full migration (Sep-2027, reported Nov-2027) and inorganic NRR prints 90% or more in the Q4 FY27 deck.
