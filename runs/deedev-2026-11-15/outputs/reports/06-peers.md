Freight and war costs are absorbed (Ratnamani Q2 FY27 p.10; ISGEC Q1 FY27 p.3). Matches DEEDEV's margin story only if its own contracts carry the same fixed-price cover, which B05 does not show.

DEEDEV is one of several expanders, not a lone one, but only ISGEC mentions piping shops. This is a selective multi-peer capex phase, not a capacity race in process piping.

2E Risks peers raise that DEEDEV's calls did not. ## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY
- Claims verified: 0 of 7. Partially verified: 3. Unverifiable: 4.
- Claims contradicted: 0.
- Most consequential gap: the working-capital level. Peers support long cycles but run about 120-165 days; none supports 190-200 and DEEDEV's 263 days in Q1 FY27 sits well outside. This goes to synthesis as the priority item (not a contradiction by any peer statement).
- Strongest independent confirmation: thermal power demand direction. Ratnamani (next two-three years, government and private) and ANUP (14 GW, thermal orders in Q4) agree.
- Overall: the peer set supports the direction of the thermal and nuclear demand story and complicates the cash story. It gives no support on BHEL timing, mill credit terms, HRSG scarcity, P91/P92 tightness or material-supplied margin. Peer margins are lower than DEEDEV's 19% plus guide, and peers describe PSU-weighted terms as cash-heavy.

## PART 5: CROSS-PEER HYPOTHESIS
Across the three peers, cash cycle length follows the advance share of new orders and the customer mix, not execution speed. ANUP's working capital rose to 2.2-3x turns only after order intake fell and advances dried up. ISGEC cut working capital by dropping long-duration PSU milestone work. Ratnamani's cycle lengthened when water orders and deferred shipments piled up. No peer says this. Test on DEEDEV: its order book rose from Rs 1,303 cr to Rs 2,531 cr (B05) but H1 FY27 operating cash flow was Rs -6.79 cr. Customer advances (contract liabilities) should have built. If they did not, DEEDEV's new orders carry thin advances (a BHEL-style milestone profile). If they did and cash is still negative, the leak is inventory or debtors.
