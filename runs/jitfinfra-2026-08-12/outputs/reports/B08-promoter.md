The group's waste-to-energy business carries a sustained, multi-year, multi-source environmental-compliance

- 2021: DPCC fined Delhi's three waste-to-energy plants (Okhla, Narela-Bawana, Ghazipur) Rs 5 lakh each for
  failing prescribed environmental norms (📰).
- 2024: A New York Times investigation reported cadmium, lead and arsenic emissions near the Okhla plant
  (📰, referenced via multiple secondary sources).
- Community protest around the Okhla plant has been continuous since 2003 (Sukhdev Vihar RWA and residents),
  including ongoing litigation up to the Supreme Court (case status as of the referenced 2024 hearing date

This is a structural, ongoing reputational and regulatory exposure for the group's core waste-to-energy
segment, not a one-off event — recorded here as 📰 MEDIA REPORTED / partially regulator-sourced (CPCB/DPCC/NGT
are official bodies; their findings are relayed via credible secondary reporting rather than a primary order
