Cheap turnaround, avoid on governance and leverage

HCP Plastene makes and exports FIBC jumbo bags, small PP woven sacks and AD Star bags from the Kandla and Mundra port cluster in Gujarat, for bulk packaging customers in India and abroad. It is a post insolvency recovery, built on the distressed Gopala Polyplast facility the promoters took over, with the subsidiary K.P. Woven now running at roughly three times the parent's scale. Rs 207 cr market cap, Rs 194 a share, and the group did Rs 588 cr of revenue and Rs 23 cr of profit in FY26. The shares trade at 8.9 times FY26 earnings of Rs 21.6.

What is changing is the model. The company is shifting from a job work packer to a direct exporter, and export was 53.7% of sales in FY25. Revenue rose 26.8% at the group level and 145% at the standalone parent between FY25 and FY26, EBITDA margin climbed into the guided 7 to 9% band, and ROCE moved from 8% in FY24 to 24% in FY25 to 29% in FY26. Infomerics lifted the outlook from Negative to Positive at BBB minus on 2 March 2026.

The cash tells a harder story. FY26 was the first year in six with positive operating cash flow, at Rs 16 cr at the group level, but standalone cash flow got worse not better, and cumulative operating cash across FY21 to FY26 is still negative against Rs 90 cr of cumulative profit. Net debt is Rs 229 cr, Net Debt to EBITDA is 4.1 times and interest cover is 2.4 times, which trips the leverage deal breaker outright. The rating agency calls the operations working capital intensive with a 96 day cycle and Rs 99 cr of bank working capital borrowing. The FTTCP read on cash conversion is INDETERMINATE, not a clean pass.

The related party picture is the reason this is not investable. Related parties are 47% of standalone revenue and up to 70% of consolidated cost of goods, the dominant counterparty Plastene India Ltd is also a 4.68% promoter group shareholder and this year lent the subsidiary Rs 33 cr at zero interest, and cross guarantees across the group exceed twice net worth. Accounting quality scores 4 of 10. A minority holder cannot independently verify the reported margins or ROCE inside a structure this entangled.

Management says the right things and does not always follow through. The credibility grade is C. A Malaysia joint venture was announced as a growth opportunity and then dissolved during FY26 with no explanation, a Saudi joint venture MOU from July 2024 has gone silent the same way, and a consolidated cash flow statement was left out of the FY26 results within nine months of a written assurance to improve disclosure. Three company secretaries came and went in about 14 months, the January 2026 resignation cited unresolved matters requiring clarification, and the replacement is also the company secretary of Plastene India Ltd. Against that, the promoter's personal record is clean, pledge is zero across five quarters, and the FII plus DII holding rose to 9.3%.

The peers contradict two comfort points. Emmbi and Kanpur Plastipack run their FIBC lines at 75 to 90% utilisation, including during expansions, against HCP at 31% in FY25 on its enlarged 24,300 MTPA base, and the FY26 utilisation figure is disclosed nowhere. Kanpur announced a Rs 105 cr capex and acquisition program in the same window HCP frames as capex free, so the no capex stance is a company choice, not an industry pause. The market itself is large, SAM near Rs 57,000 cr against 0.81% current share, but the runway is capacity gated to about 3.6 times revenue over five years and the TAM numbers rest on an unnamed source.

On valuation the numbers are genuinely cheap. The four pillar destination multiple is 12.2 times on the governing RRM track and 17.4 times on the additive track, the Hurdle Ratio passes on the depth of the discount, the entry zone is Rs 177 to 222 with a margin of safety price of Rs 177, and CMP Rs 194 sits inside that zone. The decision is still AVOID, and the reason is governance and leverage, not price. A binding leverage deal breaker, a Promoter CONCERN verdict, and INDETERMINATE cash each override a passing hurdle and an in zone price, and the probability weighted return of 17.4% falls below the 25% target once the leverage driven downside is weighted.

The devil's advocate destroyed the case rather than dented it. The bear point runs one way: add the related party payables back to capital employed and ROCE falls from 29% toward 18%, which collapses the destination multiple toward 10 to 11 times and erases the cheapness the bull case rests on. The single utilisation ramp carries revenue, margin and ROCE together, so one stall breaks all three at once, and the FY26 utilisation number that would test it is hidden. Re-engage only if gearing falls below 2 times, related party concentration drops, and operating cash flow to profit holds above 0.70 for two consecutive years.
