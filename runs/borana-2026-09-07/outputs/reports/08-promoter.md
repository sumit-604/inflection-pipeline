### 3E. Auditor relationship — second-sharpest finding of this check

**Secretarial auditor churn, three filings in six days, July 2026.**

  appointed as Secretarial Auditor for the full FY2025-26 to FY2029-30 term under the

  "pre-occupation with professional assignments and unavoidable professional

  its face, explain why a five-year mandate accepted only months earlier (April 2025)

  Company's post-IPO life, and mid-way through preparing the very FY26 secretarial
  audit report he was engaged for.

pattern that a careful reader should flag, even though director non-disqualification
certification and secretarial audit are procedurally distinct engagements under
Companies Act practice.
