About Dynamic Cables Ltd.:

Dynamic Cables Ltd. is manufacturer of power infra cables that includes LV, HV, MV, Power control
& instrumentation cables, and signaling cables. It supplies cables to Government Discoms, Private
Distribution companies, Private EPC contractors, industrial and Export clients. Company has 4
manufacturing plants are situated at Jaipur and Reengus. Business operations are managed through
corporate office in Jaipur and 5 regional sales offices across India.
