the platform to meet changing customer requirements and expectations. The 
expenditure on procurement of computer systems help them in enhancing the overall 
efficiency and productivity of the workforce by equipping teams with modern, high- 
performance computing devices, replacing technologically obsolete hardware assets, 
supporting business continuity, data management and secure access in both on 
premises and hybrid work environment.
