Rationale  
This plant is a first of its kind in India. In the Indian 
heavy commercial vehicle industry, the axle beam is 
traditionally manufactured as a four-piece design 
that is welded together to make the axle beam. This 
plant produces the axle beam as a single piece 
construction by extrusion process with no welding or 

[page 2]
 
 
joints. This new technology increases strength, 
stiffness, reliability, improves tire life and reduces 
weight.
