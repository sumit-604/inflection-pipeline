## PART 2 — COMPARISON / COVERAGE

**Caught well (10).** The asset-quality-optics cluster (#1,2), gold concentration
(#4,5), guidance silence (#6), CASA discontinuity (#7), ECL and West Asia peer
risks (#11,12), weak core profitability (#13), and dividend/capital posture (#15)
are all surfaced, correctly weighted, and anchored in B05/B06. The single largest
flag (write-off-driven GNPA optics with the 7.45% incl-write-off and 53.63%
ex-write-off ICRA figures) is B05's headline "High" red flag — correctly graded.

**Partially caught (1).**
- #3 NNPA-rose-while-GNPA-fell. B05 folds asset quality into one "optics" flag but
  never states the specific banking tell that ICRA calls out — NNPA% *rising*
  (0.86%→1.11%) on *lower* provisioning even as GNPA fell — nor the Q4 magnitude
  (Net NPA Rs154.13cr Dec-25 → Rs75.40cr Mar-26, ~halved in one quarter to reach
  the 0.51% year-end headline). This is under-weighted, not absent. MINOR.

**Missed (4).**
- #8 Top-20 depositor (24.50%) and top-20 exposure (147% of Tier I capital)
  concentration. This is a core banking concentration red flag explicitly on the
  methodology list, disclosed prominently by ICRA, and appears nowhere in B05 or
  B06. The 147%-of-Tier-I asset concentration is the more material half. MAJOR.
- #9 RBI monetary penalty in FY25. The Bank's own AR admits corrective action on
  "monetary penalties levied by Reserve Bank of India during the financial year."
  A governance/RBI-penalty item — squarely a red-flag category — uncaught. The
  amount is not disclosed in the AR text, which is itself a disclosure-quality
  point. MAJOR.
- #10 Two RBI-appointed directors on the board. An enhanced-oversight governance
  signal ICRA flags directly; neither B05 (which discusses governance themes) nor
  B06 mentions it. MAJOR.
- #14 Q4 back-ended earnings / balancing-figure quality. A quality-of-earnings
  nuance a careful reader raises; not thesis-flipping. MINOR.

**Pipeline flag NOT fully supported (1).**
- B05 repeatedly carries a "restructured/vulnerable book up ~47%" flag
  (Medium-High) as "carried stage 2/3 context, not independently re-derivable."
  Against the in-scope sources this is OVERSTATED: ICRA p.3 puts the vulnerable
  book (SMA-1/2 + standard restructured) at just Rs124cr = **10% of Tier I capital**
  (Dec-25), and the Covid resolution-framework standard book is only Rs15.32cr
  (Results FY26 p.12). Nothing in scope corroborates a "+47%" alarming trend, and
  the absolute level is small. B05 does hedge it as unverifiable, so this is a
  weakly-supported carried flag rather than a fabricated one. MINOR.
