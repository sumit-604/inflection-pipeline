===== PAGE 3 =====
Moderator: welcome to Q1 FY27 Earnings Call of Capillary Technologies India Limited, on
behalf of EY IR Practice. Hands over to Aneesh Reddy.

Aneesh Reddy: Capillary operates in the loyalty and customer engagement space globally.
Regarded as a market leader by any analyst globally in this space. US is the largest market,
about 60% of revenue; 15% Europe; about 25% Asia. Works with about 465 brands globally, 25
Fortune 500 customers, about 2.7 billion consumer profiles on the platform, customers live in
about 53 countries, 19 offices globally, about 750 employees. More than 90% of revenue comes
from long-term subscription contracts, typically 3, 5, 7 year contracts with large global
enterprises. Cloud-native product, implementations across four colos globally.

===== PAGE 4 =====
Aneesh Reddy (continued): Forrester Wave, most recent about 9 months ago [Q4 2025], 5 on 5
score on 22 of 27 criteria, 3 on 5 on the remaining five. Most AI-first platform in the space.
Product intent: deliver loyalty and retention as an outcome, broader than a pure loyalty
stack (unlike agencies such as Epsilon, Bounteous). Over 90% of revenue globally comes from
the loyalty stack. Engage stack is roughly 5% of revenue. Insights is not monetised, a layer
on top. Rewards stack (more for banks and telcos) is about 5-7% of revenue.

Q1 highlights: NRR roughly 111% expansion from existing customer cohort, from three levers:
platform usage/inflation increases, product upgrades, new brands and geographies. Excluding
the single largest customer, NRR was 116%.

===== PAGE 5 =====
Aneesh Reddy (continued): Second growth axis is new customer wins; TTM new ACV grew about 75%
YoY, excluding the one large healthcare customer won the year before. Good momentum across
Asia, UK, Europe, US.

M&A: SessionM acquired from Mastercard, closed May 1 [2026]; two full months of integration.
Strengthens footprint in North America and LATAM, adds to APAC portfolio. SessionM's product
stack is known for QSR and airline customers, categories where Capillary had less penetration.
Kognitiv acquisition closed May 1, 2025; upgrade process for Kognitiv customers has started,
using AI this time, expected to be faster with less customer effort than earlier migrations.

Profitability levers: NRR-related expansion comes at higher gross margin because servicing
cost is already covered. Blended subscription gross margin (organic + inorganic) is 66%.
Organic gross margins have been consistently upwards of 75% for the last few quarters. About
60% of costs are not linked to customer revenue / not COGS, and scale at a much lower pace
than revenue; non-COGS cost growth YoY is at least 12-15% lower than revenue growth. Third
lever: M&A customer upgrades, typically from ~30% gross margin to 70-75% post-migration. Q1
FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin about 17-18%, versus roughly 10% in the same quarter last year;
organic side EBITDA margin over 20%.

===== PAGE 6 =====
Aneesh Reddy (continued): Margin gains driven by successful migration of Brierley, Persuade
and Rewards+ acquisitions (all done before June 2023), now fully migrated. In the last 6-9
months, built an AI-led upgrade platform simplifying migration from acquired platforms to
Capillary.

On AI: Capillary sits in the system of record (like a "banking ledger" for points and
rewards); priced per-transaction or per-member. Expanded into a system of intelligence (aiRA
stack), replacing manual analytics work, priced on outcomes (queries asked). Of about 150
customers, about 26 are live on this product, fewer than 10 are paying for it currently, on an
outcome-based pricing approach after initial use. aiRA is becoming a differentiator in new
deals; win rates continue upwards of 35%.

===== PAGE 7 =====
Aneesh Reddy (continued): Action Agents piece (Engage+, about 5% of revenue) still being built
out. About 10,000 queries read via aiRA, used as a learning loop for the product.

SessionM update: two months in, on plan. $32 million ARR of acquired customer contracts, no
surprises, all agreed to move to Capillary. SessionM turned fully profitable; first two months
generated small free cash, roughly Rs 5-6 crore. Net of adjustments, paid about Rs 17-odd
crore for SessionM (i.e., roughly $2 million-plus for a $32 million ARR business). Expect
break-even/payback within this year.

CustomerGlu tuck-in (not material financially): enables front-end experiences (games,
interactive content) without IT-team dependency; rebranded Experiences+; usage-based pricing.

===== PAGE 8 =====
Anant Choubey: Q1 revenue Rs 256 crore, 43% growth over Q1 last year. EBITDA Rs 44 crore, 132%
improvement YoY. PAT -Rs 9.5 crore for the quarter. Adjusting for one-time exceptional loss
from the cyber-fraud incident and a one-time deferred tax liability, normalised PAT is Rs 25
crore. Rule of 40 (growth rate + EBITDA %): Capillary at 60 (43% growth + 17% EBITDA). Organic
business alone: grew 17% YoY at about 23% EBITDA (also near Rule of 40).

ARR grown from Rs 608 crore (FY25) to Rs 765 crore (FY26) to Rs 1,026 crore (Q1FY27), 34% YoY
growth with contribution from SessionM, NRR and new ACV. TTM new ACV, excluding one large
healthcare customer, grew about 75% YoY to about Rs 92 crore.

QoQ: adjusted EBITDA improved from Rs 35 crore (Q4FY26) to Rs 44 crore (Q1FY27), despite Q1
carrying annual salary hikes. Normalised PAT improved from Rs 19 crore to Rs 25 crore Q4 to
Q1, including increased amortisation from SessionM.

EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA are nearly equal historically, adjusted for finance income and ESOP
expenses. Depreciation and amortisation Rs 20 crore for Q1 this year, up from Rs 17 crore same
time last year, reflecting SessionM (this year) and Kognitiv (last year) acquisitions.

===== PAGE 9 =====
Q&A begins.
Vinay Menon: asked what percentage of SessionM clients have migrated and the FY27 run rate.
Aneesh Reddy: SessionM acquisition just closed; typically takes 2-3 years for all customers to
upgrade. SessionM migrations will not start till end of this year/early next year, except 3-4
of the ~45 SessionM customers who want to move immediately. Operational efficiencies (server
costs etc.) already being realised without waiting for platform upgrades; expects SessionM
business can reach 5-10% margins even without upgrades, given it was run inefficiently inside
Mastercard.

Vinay Menon (follow-up): whether subscription revenue rises as clients migrate.
Aneesh Reddy: upgrading a customer alone does not increase the contract price (existing
contract); increase comes when customers buy add-ons (aiRA, Engage, Experiences+). SessionM's
$32 million book has potential to add another 10-15% additional revenue per year through the
NRR motion.

===== PAGE 10 =====
Vinay Menon: asked about aiRA monthly run rate.
Aneesh Reddy: currently about $2-2.5 million in revenue run rate on aiRA; hopes for 5-10% of
revenue from aiRA this year. Adoption strong but subject to large-enterprise budgeting cycles.

Vinay Menon: asked about the CustomerGlu acquisition's coding capability.
Aneesh Reddy: today customers need their own IT team to build front-end experiences (e.g. a
promotions page), which can take 2-3 months in an IT development queue. CustomerGlu/
Experiences+ is an SDK plugged into the website/app once, after which marketers can configure
front-end experiences (games, scratch cards) without IT dependency, similar to how they
already configure back-end promotions.

===== PAGE 11 =====
Shaurya Yadav: asked on Kognitiv migration progress, challenges, and incremental margin from
post-upgrade.
Aneesh Reddy: migration process has started; first customer fully migrates September 1
[2026]. Migration uses an AI platform to look up code and implementations, auto-determine what
must migrate, configure the Capillary platform and build any needed middleware, without human
migration teams. Kognitiv is a 1980s-founded company; its oldest customer is 30 years old on
that platform. About 16-17 Kognitiv customers in total. Prior migrations (Brierley, Rewards,
Persuade) took 3-5 years each; the AI-led approach targets an 18-24 month upgrade cycle, aiming
to finish Kognitiv upgrades by September 2027 (bought May 1, 2025). Acquisitions typically run
at 30% gross margin, reaching 65-70%+ post-migration. Kognitiv revenue was roughly Rs 75 crore;
Aneesh estimates another Rs 10-20 crore of EBITDA as migrations proceed. Referencing the prior
year's guide of Rs 1,065 crore total revenue: organic core platform revenue (75% gross margin)
was about Rs 673 crore; acquired revenue was near break-even, with about Rs 20 crore of margin
projected from acquired revenue at the time.

===== PAGE 12 =====
Aneesh Reddy (continued): the projected Rs 20 crore of acquired-revenue margin (on about Rs
390 crore of acquired revenue) has the potential to reach Rs 120-130 crore incrementally as
migrations happen; even on Kognitiv alone (~Rs 80 crore revenue) expects at least Rs 20-odd
crore of additional margin next year.

Shaurya Yadav: asked to bifurcate organic NRR of 111% between upsell, cross-sell and price

Anant Choubey: roughly 4-5% from inflationary price increases and overages; roughly 10%
