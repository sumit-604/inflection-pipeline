### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour
- Dilution pattern: promoter holding fell from 73.35% (Mar-2024) to 54.19% (Mar-2025) to
  56.46% (Mar-2026), driven by two acquisition-funded equity issuances and a QIP-style
  preferential structure, not open-market promoter selling (screener aggregation, weighed not
  anchored; filed figure at Mar-2026 in AR Note F, [page 5395], confirms 56.46%).
- New (22-Sep-2026, board; EGM 16-Oct-2026): a Rs 20.02 Cr preferential equity issue (3,86,000
  shares at Rs 518.55) to 26 allottees, of which only 1,200 shares (0.03% of the tranche) go to
  a promoter (Kamal Grover); the rest is broad-based to non-promoter investors including
  veteran public investor Ashish Rameshchandra Kacholia (1,73,200 shares, the single largest
  allottee) (Reg 30 filing 22-Sep-2026 [Annexure A1]; corroborated by web search: WhalesBook,
  Trade Brains, EquityBulls all confirm the Kacholia allotment). Separately, a Rs 26.96 Cr
  fully-convertible-warrant issue of 5,20,000 warrants goes ENTIRELY to TIC Services (the
  promoter entity) at the same Rs 518.55 price, with the SEBI-mandated 25% paid up front and
  75% payable on exercise within an 18-month tenor, unexercised warrants lapsing with
  forfeiture of the 25% paid (Reg 30 filing 22-Sep-2026 [Annexure B]). This is standard ICDR
  structure, not itself a red flag, but it is a promoter-only instrument layered on top of an
  already-diluted base, worth watching for exercise-vs-lapse behaviour (3C).
- Separately, in Dec-2025 the board approved a Rs 65 Cr secured, unlisted NCD private
  placement (Vistra ITCL as trustee), whose contemplated security package explicitly includes
  "pledge of shares," corporate guarantees by group entities, AND personal guarantees by
  promoters/directors (Reg 30 filing 20-Dec-2025). Rs 65 Cr is more than half of FY26
  consolidated revenue (Rs 124.5 Cr) raised as secured debt in the same year CFO/EBITDA was
  0.46 and FCF was negative Rs ~32 Cr (B03-ardeep FLAG-CASH). Whether any promoter shares have
  actually been pledged under this facility is NOT FOUND in the corpus (no filed shareholding
  pattern holds a pledge column); this is the single most important promoter-risk metric to
  chase at Halt 1/live verification.
- An AGM item to raise the OVERALL managerial remuneration ceiling from 28% to 35% of net
  profits was placed on the 25-Sep-2026 AGM notice (29-Aug-2026) and then WITHDRAWN by circular
  resolution on 07-Sep-2026, before the vote, with the company stating only that the existing
  28% limit "shall continue to remain in force" (Reg 30 filing 07-Sep-2026). No reason for the
  withdrawal is disclosed. This reads two ways: a governance over-reach attempt corrected
  before it reached a vote (mildly positive, self-correcting), or a proposal pulled after
  informal investor pushback the company chose not to disclose (mildly negative, opaque). ❓
  UNVERIFIED which; PENDING LIVE VERIFICATION (proxy-advisory commentary, if any, from IiAS/SES
  on this item was searched and not found).
- Dividend conduct: NOT FOUND in the AR extracts read (no dividend declaration referenced); the
  company is reinvesting/raising capital rather than distributing, consistent with an early-stage
  growth-by-acquisition small-cap. Salary-rising-through-decline: MD Antaryami Nayak's pay rose
  116% and WTD Mangesh Bhosale's rose 71% in the same year standalone ROE fell to -23% and ROCE
