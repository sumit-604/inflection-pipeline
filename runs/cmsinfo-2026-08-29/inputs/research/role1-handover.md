## 6. PROJECTION GUIDANCE

Build FY27 through FY30. The FY28 ROCE outcome is the fork; everything else follows from it.

**Three cases, with probability weights:**

**Case A — one-off build (40%).** Capex Rs 125 Cr then Rs 250 Cr then Rs 280 Cr, settling at maintenance level. Capital employed roughly flat. EBITDA margin 26.0% / 26.8% / 27.1%. FY28 ROCE ~23.8%, FY29 ~26.7%. Proof gate FIRES.

**Case B — treadmill (40%).** Every new contract needs new machines. Capex Rs 250 Cr then Rs 350 Cr then Rs 400 Cr. Capital employed keeps rising. FY28 ROCE ~20.8%, FY29 ~22.0%. Proof gate MISSES in FY28.

**Case C — margin fails (20%).** EBITDA margin reverts to 24.5%, capex overshoots. ROCE stuck near 15.8%, roughly cost of capital. **This is the transition falsifier firing.**

**Probability-weighted FY30E EPS: Rs 31.5** (Case A Rs 35.0, Case B Rs 32.5, Case C Rs 22.5).

**Binding constraints on the projection:**

1. **FY27 is the depreciation peak, not FY26.** Q1 FY27 D&A was Rs 72.8 Cr, annualising near Rs 291 Cr against FY26's Rs 208 Cr, up 40%. Do NOT model FY27 as the recovery year.
2. **The FTTCP draft projects FY27 ROCE at ~16%. I disagree — it counts the depreciation rise but not the EBITDA rise.** With EBITDA at ~Rs 730 Cr and capital flat, FY27 ROCE should be ~19-20%. Test both and state which you use.
3. **Capital employed falls back if capex stays below depreciation.** Depreciation runs ~Rs 290 Cr; capex is guided Rs 100-125 Cr. The asset base shrinks each year in Case A. Do not treat the FY26 capital base as permanent.
4. **Revenue must be projected on SERVICES revenue with total shown alongside.** Hardware resale fell 25.9% by deliberate choice and drags total revenue. Q1 FY27 services grew 9.5% YoY (Rs 571 Cr to Rs 625 Cr), or ~13.8% adjusting back the Rs 25 Cr currency-shortage loss. Total revenue grew only 1.2% and is the wrong line.
5. **The FY27 guide requires a step above the all-time record.** Services guide low end Rs 2,650 Cr against Q1 actual Rs 625 Cr needs Rs 675 Cr per quarter for three quarters, +8% on the highest quarter CMS has ever printed. High end needs Rs 708 Cr, +13%. Low end plausible; high end a stretch.
6. **Discount revenue guidance by at least one band. Take margin guidance near face value.** Three years of revenue misses, but the margin call is backed by a printed 27.2% quarter and was raised while revenue was cut.
7. **Do not model a return to FY25 segment returns.** Combined Cash Management + Managed Services capital employed rose Rs 12,123m to Rs 16,141m. On consolidated ROCE the constraint is milder (capital +24%), but restoring FY25 profit on FY26 capital gives 20.3%, not 25.2%.

## 7. METHOD SELECTION GUIDANCE

**Primary: P/E.** Business is profitable, earnings are clean, Section 1B is settled.

**Secondary: EV/EBITDA — and it WILL disagree with P/E.** CMS at 5.8x against Brink's 8.2x and Radiant 8.1x implies meaningful upside. The disagreement is a leverage artefact and is informative, not noise. Also run **EV/EBIT** (CMS 8.9x vs Brink's 12.3x), which strips out both leverage and depreciation differences and is the fairest single comparison for an asset-owning business.

**Tertiary: DCF, with a hard constraint.** FY26 free cash flow was NEGATIVE Rs 19 Cr. Pillar 2 is set at 1.15x, one notch below the top band, precisely because of that. **FCF as a percentage of revenue in the DCF must be consistent with a 1.15x cash multiplier. Do not assume cash conversion magically improves.** Note also that capex guided at Rs 100-125 Cr against depreciation of ~Rs 290 Cr is not sustainable indefinitely; model capex reverting toward depreciation by FY30 or state why not.

**Consider but likely reject: P/B.** Book value does not capture the contracted rental book. CMS at 1.8x against Radiant 1.4-1.5x is a weak signal.

**Do NOT use:** EV/Sales (mature business), DDM (payout 42%, not a dividend story), EV/capacity (machine counts are not disclosed cleanly and brown label sits under sponsor banks).
