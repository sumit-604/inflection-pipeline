### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Sky Gold makes gold jewellery for retail chains and distributors. It does not sell to shoppers. Its revenue carries the value of the gold, except where the customer supplies the metal.
2. Group sales grew about 61% on a year ago, to Rs 2,391 Cr. Profit grew about 93%, to Rs 129 Cr. The half year shows the same pattern.
3. Part of the growth is bought. Shri Rishab, acquired in December 2025, is not in last year's base. The company does not give organic growth.
4. The company does not give kilograms processed. So nobody can split the growth between a higher gold price and more volume. Four of the eight watch items are silent: volume, the price and volume split, customer shares and the gold metal loan balance.
5. The margin gain sits in the subsidiaries. The parent's operating margin fell slightly. The subsidiaries earned an implied 11.65% margin, against 6.01% a year ago. Other auditors review two of them, and these two carry 21% of group profit.
6. Cash is the main worry. Operating cash flow for the half year was Rs 137 Cr. But trade payables rose Rs 345 Cr in the same half. Without that supplier credit, operating cash flow was minus Rs 209 Cr.
7. Net debt rose to Rs 874 Cr, up about 43%. That holds unless Rs 382 Cr of other financial assets is free cash. The filing does not say whether those deposits are pledged.
8. The board approved a raise of up to Rs 1,800 Cr. That equals 1.27 times group equity. At Rs 858, it means about 13.6% more shares. The notice also allows convertibles and warrants, and it puts no amount against any object. The same day's press release does not mention the raise.
9. The EGM is on 18-Nov-2026. It has one item, the raise. Promoters will not subscribe to a QIP. The cover letter gives two different cut-off dates, 11-Nov and 18-Nov.
10. The ESOP grant is small. It covers 3,480 options at Rs 10 each, about 0.002% of the shares. It does not change the dilution picture.
11. Disclosure quality is weak this quarter. A new auditor reviewed it, with no reason given for the change. The quarter carries a Rs 7.35 Cr fraud loss and a Rs 17.32 Cr equity-to-debt move with no explanation. The press release misstates its own adjusted profit.
12. Watch the subsidiary margin in Q3 FY27, without Purvi Gems. At 10.6% or above, the step-up holds. At 6.0% or below, it was a one-quarter effect. On the 31-Oct call, ask first what the Rs 345 Cr of payables are.
