### 2C Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic shown)
Historical fixed asset turnover (DERIVED): revenue from operations Rs 3,221.11 Cr (AR lakh 322,111) / net PPE Rs 1,706.79 Cr (AR lakh 170,679, p.170) = 1.887x. ROU assets sit outside this PPE figure.
- Headline method: H2 capex Rs 150-160 Cr x 1.887 = Rs 283.1 Cr to Rs 301.9 Cr incremental revenue = 8.8% to 9.4% of FY26 revenue. Midpoint Rs 155 Cr x 1.887 = Rs 292.5 Cr = 9.1%.
- Why the headline overstates: land and buildings were 53% of FY26 additions and vehicles 37% (AR p.34, p.189 via B03 3A). Land does not add revenue by itself. Vehicle-only method: Rs 155 Cr x 37% = Rs 57.4 Cr; vehicle net block Rs 916.0 Cr (AR lakh 91,599, via B03 4B) gives turnover 3.516x (3,221.11 / 916.0); Rs 57.4 Cr x 3.516 = Rs 201.7 Cr = 6.3% of FY26 revenue. The 37% mix is an assumption borrowed from FY26, since H2 mix is NOT FOUND.
- Cross-check: management guides revenue growth 10-12% with no realisation change (Q2 FY27 call, B05 guidance), tonnage 9-10%. The capex-embedded 6.3% to 9.1% is the same order as that guide, but H1 added only 9 net vehicles, so the volume growth is coming from utilisation and not from capacity.
capex_embedded_growth_pct reported: 9.1 (headline), with 6.3 as the vehicle-only floor.
