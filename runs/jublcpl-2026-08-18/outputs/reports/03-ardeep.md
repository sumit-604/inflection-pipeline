   148.2% to 59.0% and FCF fell 62.9% YoY (₹96.10 Cr → ₹35.68 Cr), driven by a working-
   capital drag concentrated in the fertilizer subsidy receivable and near-doubled cash
   taxes paid (Statement of Cash Flows pp.101-102; feeds FLAG-CASH).
3. **Governance/disclosure gaps**: the audit-fee split between audit and non-audit
   services is not disclosed (only a blended ~₹3.19 Mn total across the group, p.62); the
   Finance Committee (₹1,250 Cr borrowing authority) has zero independent-director
   representation (p.67); and a direct MSME disclosure contradiction exists between Note
   48(vi) and Note 19.1 (per B02).
