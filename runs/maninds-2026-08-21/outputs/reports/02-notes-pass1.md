business (no insurance contracts, no sale-and-leaseback found).

1.4 Revenue recognition (Note 3.09, p.134/p.205): point-in-time recognition on transfer of control (title,
risk, present right to payment), generally at shipment/delivery. Freight billed to customer treated as a

manufacturing/trading business; bill-and-hold policy is a standard industry practice for large steel-pipe
orders (staged delivery) but is a judgment area worth watching for pull-forward risk — no bill-and-hold
balance is separately quantified in the notes (NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT).

1.5 ECL policy (Note 3.08(c), p.130): simplified approach under Ind AS 109 for trade receivables — lifetime
expected losses recognised from initial recognition. Reiterated at Note 46 (standalone, p.178) / Note 51
(consolidated, p.249). See Section 4 below for adequacy assessment — 🔴 the >3yr disputed non-current
receivables ECL coverage looks thin against the ageing profile.

1.6 Ind AS 116 (leases): incremental borrowing rate used when implicit rate not determinable (Note 3.03,
p.127); no explicit discount rate % is disclosed anywhere in the lease note (Note 5(c)). NOT FOUND IN
DOCUMENT — the discount rate used for ROU/lease liability measurement is not quantified.
ROU assets: standalone ₹1,703.84 lakh (₹17.04cr) at 31-Mar-25 vs ₹1,503.62 lakh (₹15.04cr) prior year
(Note 5(c), p.138-139). Lease liabilities total ₹1,863.02 lakh (₹18.63cr) standalone, ₹2,027.52 lakh
(₹20.28cr) consolidated (Note 5(c), p.139/p.208). New machinery lease of ₹705.47 lakh added FY25. 🟢
Immaterial relative to balance sheet size (~0.5% of total assets).

1.7 Impairment testing (PPE/intangibles): qualitative policy only (Note 3.05); no goodwill impairment test
disclosed for the ₹6,882.05 lakh (₹68.82cr) Goodwill on Consolidation (up from ₹6,392.81 lakh) shown on the
consolidated balance sheet (p.188). NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — no impairment testing note, no CGU
allocation, no growth/discount rate assumptions for goodwill anywhere in the consolidated notes. 🔴 This is
a disclosure gap for a ~₹68.8cr goodwill balance (~4.3% of consolidated equity) that grew ₹489.24 lakh

inference, not stated).
