ESCONET TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED CLARIFIES RECEIPT OF INCOME TAX SUMMONS; 
CONFIRMS NO IMPACT ON BUSINESS OPERATIONS 
 
New Delhi | 28 February 2026: 
 
Esconet Technologies Limited (NSE: ESCONET), a leading provider of enterprise IT infrastructure and 
technology solutions, today issued a clariﬁcation regarding the receipt of a summons from the OƯice of 
the Deputy Director of Income Tax (Investigation), Noida, under Section 131(1A) of the Income-tax Act, 
1961. 
 
The summons has been issued in connection with proceedings arising from a search and seizure action 
conducted by the Income Tax Department in the case of Corporate Capital Ventures Private Limited, 
which had acted as the Book Running Lead Manager to the Company’s Initial Public OƯer (IPO). 
 
The Company believes it is important to provide clear and comprehensive information to its 
shareholders and stakeholders to avoid any speculation or misunderstanding. 
 
NATURE OF THE COMMUNICATION 
 
The summons is part of an ongoing investigation in the case of the aforesaid intermediary and seeks 
certain information and documents relating to: 
 
 The Company’s IPO process and related approvals 
 Engagement of intermediaries during the IPO 
 Pre-IPO / private placement allotments, if any 
 Utilisation of IPO proceeds 
 Supporting documentation and corporate records 
 
The communication is investigative and procedural in nature and is intended to assist the Income Tax 
Department in its proceedings involving the said intermediary. 
 
IMPORTANT CLARIFICATIONS FOR INVESTORS 
 
 Esconet Technologies Limited is not subject to any search, seizure, raid, or enforcement 
action. 
 The Company is not accused of any wrongdoing. 
 No demand, penalty , tax claim, or adverse ﬁnding has been issued against the Company. 
 The summons does not restrict the Company’s operations, ﬁnancial transactions, banking 
activities, or business engagements. 
 The Company is cooperating fully and professionally with the authorities. 
 
NO IMPACT ON BUSINESS OR FINANCIAL POSITION 
 
After internal review and consultation, the management conﬁrms that: 
 
 There is no material ﬁnancial exposure arising from this development. 
 There is no operational disruption to ongoing projects or customer engagements. 
 There is no impact on order execution, receivables, cash ﬂows, or banking relationships. 
 There is no impact on management continuity or strategic plans. 
 
The Company continues to execute its growth strategy across enterprise and government segments and 
remains focused on delivering strong operational performance.
