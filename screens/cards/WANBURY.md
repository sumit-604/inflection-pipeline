## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Wanbury makes active pharmaceutical ingredients and sells them to generic drug makers abroad. API revenue was Rs 574 crore in FY26, and about 80% of it came from exports to the EU, Brazil and the US (AR26, page 44). The company claims about 10% of global Metformin and about 30% of global Sertraline (AR26, page 45). Those two molecules make up 85% to 90% of API revenue (INF, page 6). A small domestic branded formulations business sold Rs 76.93 crore (AR26, page 45). Production runs from two USFDA-approved sites, Tanuku and Patalganga (Q1R, page 11).

**What is changing?** A distressed company has finished its repair and now claims growth. Net worth was negative at end FY23 (INF, page 4). Borrowing cost fell from about 21% to 12.5% from 1 March 2025 (AR26, page 46). FY26 gross margin rose to 57.6% from 51.5%, and PAT more than doubled to Rs 66.13 crore (IP, page 28). The next claimed step is four new molecule launches a year from FY27 (IP, page 19).

**Why now?** Three balance-sheet events landed in 2026. Infomerics upgraded the rating from IVR C+ Issuer Not Cooperating to IVR BBB-/Stable on 5 March 2026 (INF, page 1). The company borrowed Rs 205 crore from NBFCs to repay high-cost debt. Promoters' pledged shares, 85,20,330 of them, were released on 7 July 2026 (Q1R, page 11).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The FY26 margin must be structural, not a one-year raw-material gift. New molecules must add revenue on top of a flat Metformin and Sertraline base. Infomerics carries Rs 60 to 70 crore of incremental annual revenue from launches (INF, page 3).

**What breaks it?** Q1 FY27 already tested it and failed. EBITDA margin fell to 10.0% from 18.3% in Q4 FY26. PAT fell to Rs 3.24 crore (Q1R, page 10). Metformin is "highly commoditised, with intense competition and limited pricing power" (INF, page 6). If Q1 is the new base, the turnaround was a cycle.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "Pharmaceuticals" (Q1R, page 11). Two engines sit inside it. API was 88% of revenue (INF, page 4). Formulations was 12% in FY26, with fully outsourced manufacturing and 100% acute therapy (IP, page 22).

Metformin is a volume commodity. It made 50% to 56% of revenue in FY24 and FY25 (INF, page 6). Sertraline is a narrower molecule with better entry barriers. Margin comes from yield, solvent recovery and debottlenecking, not price. Reactor capacity is about 500 KL, with about 100 KL more brownfield identified (AR26, page 44).

**Archetype: Commodity converter for the Metformin slice (Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds). Build-to-spec supplier for the Sertraline and new-molecule slice. Brand/franchise consumer for the 12% formulations slice.** The engine is the spread between API price and solvent and raw-material cost, at high utilisation.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming, and narrow. The real asset is regulatory qualification: DMFs and CEPs filed with customers, and zero-observation inspections at USFDA in June 2024 and MFDS Korea in April 2026, plus TGA Australia in June 2026 (AR26, page 44). A customer whose dossier references Wanbury's API cannot switch fast. That is a switching cost. Sertraline scale, about 30% of global supply on 200 TPA, gives some price influence (INF, page 6). Metformin scale gives cost position but no pricing power. The company stays out of the price-controlled Indian API market (INF, page 4). No customer concentration figure appears in the corpus. Moat present in Sertraline, absent in Metformin.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R0 toward R1: a balance-sheet survivor with negative net worth in FY23 (INF, page 4), now an R1 commodity price-taker in Metformin. It CLAIMS R2 then R3: cost-advantaged converter through yields and debottlenecking, then spec'd supplier through regulated-market molecules aiming for "~50% gross margins" (IP, page 19).

Management's claim: "Operational & financial turnaround over last 3 years, now transforming into growth momentum" (IP, page 5).

The repair is delivered. The R2 cost position had one good year and one bad quarter. R3 is a pipeline. A two-rung climb in under three years breaks the base rate and needs extraordinary proof. The corpus has none yet.

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim new molecules add revenue and cheaper debt lifts profit. The last two quarters do not show it. Q1 FY27 revenue was up 1.5% year on year and 0.6% quarter on quarter. Finance cost was Rs 7.97 crore against Rs 8.06 crore, so no refinance benefit shows yet. Q4 FY26 revenue fell 4% year on year, blamed on West Asia dispatch delays in March (IP, page 29). The proof point is absent. The Q1 margin fall is a counter-signal with no explanation in the corpus.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

Infomerics upgraded Wanbury to IVR BBB-/Stable on 5 March 2026, on Rs 40 crore of cash credit (INF, page 1). It cited a "structural turnaround in business operations, capacity enhancement, and yield improvement" (INF, pages 1-2). It expected FY26 interest cover of 3.6x (INF, page 5). FY26 delivered that: EBITDA of Rs 107.69 crore over finance cost of Rs 29.98 crore. The agency flagged Metformin commoditisation, 85% to 90% product concentration, and NCD repayments of Rs 40 crore a year to February 2030 (INF, page 6). A further upgrade needs gearing below 1.0x, sustained (INF, page 2). The view rests on FY25 and 9M FY26. Q1 FY27 postdates it, and today's filing wins.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** The repair fired. The claimed climb did not. Launches show no revenue in Q1 FY27, and the margin went backwards.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, provisional.** Higher employee cost fits the stated R&D step-up and formulations field-force build (AR26, page 46; IP, page 24). The 8-point gross-margin fall is unexplained. If it is Metformin pricing, the reading flips to STRUCTURAL.
- **Recognition gap: OPEN on FY26, CLOSED on Q1.** Market capitalisation is Rs 664.9 crore (Bull AI company record, 2026-11-04, a service record, not a filed document). That is 14.0x tax-normalised FY26 PAT, below the R3 neighbourhood. On the Q1 run-rate it is above 40x.

**Posture: RESEARCH / WATCH.** Narrative until a quarter proves the FY26 margin and the launches.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The repair is real and the balance sheet is cleaner. The climb is unproven, and the latest quarter cut against it. A `/step1` now would spend its effort on one quarter that the Q2 FY27 filing, already intimated, will explain within weeks.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. The Q2 FY27 gross and EBITDA margin. Does gross margin return toward 57% to 60%, or stay near 52%?
2. The cause of the Q1 FY27 fall: Metformin price, raw material, mix, or one-off cost.
3. The rate on the Rs 205 crore NBFC loan, and any new pledge behind it.
4. Revenue from the new molecules, starting with the anaesthetic.
5. Customer concentration within Metformin and Sertraline.

**What would change the view**
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin back above 15%, with tax-normal PAT near Rs 11 crore a quarter, moves this to PROCEED.
- A second quarter near 10% makes FY26 a cycle peak and moves this toward PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
