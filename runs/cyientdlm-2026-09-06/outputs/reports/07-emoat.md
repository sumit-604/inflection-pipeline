### FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability.** Evidence: full certification stack (AS9100-D, NADCAP AC7120/AC7121, ISO13485, ITAR, Industrial License to manufacture defence equipment, IATF16949) held simultaneously across aerospace, defence, medical and now automotive (AR FY2025-26, p.13-14). CFO on-record: "aerospace is a double-edged sword. It's very, very difficult to get into. But once you're in you're in... a moat that's impossible to reach for a new entrant" (Q1 FY27 call, 21-Jul-2026, p.17-18). No global-manufacturer-count figure is disclosed anywhere, so the "rare" claim is not independently sized. Evidence type: 🎙️ (the rarity claim) supported by 📄 (the certification list). Strength: **Weak** (real certifications, but the differentiation claim itself is unquantified narrative).

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline.** No patent filed, no filing trend, no licensing revenue disclosed anywhere in the AR or four calls. "Product organisation capabilities" and "own IP-led products" are named only as a FY30+ transform-phase ambition (Q1 FY27 call, p.4-5). **NO EVIDENCE FOUND** (concept stage only).

**A3 — Process innovation.** MES investment mentioned (Q3 FY26 call, p.4-5), Kinaxis Rapid Response planning tool (AR p.57, risk mitigation), Hand Soldering Championship win (Q4 FY26 call, p.4-5, quality/skill recognition, not a cost/yield metric). No yield disclosure, no unit-cost trend given. 🎙️ only. Strength: **Weak**.

**A4 — Product platform / modular architecture.** B2S "platform play" language used repeatedly (Q3 FY26 call, p.5) but no SKU count, no launch-frequency data, no shared-architecture evidence. 🎙️, concept stage. Strength: **Weak**.

### FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN

**B1 — Backward integration and RM security.** "The Company procured critical components in advance and secured long-term vendor commitments" to manage FY26 supply shocks (AR FY2025-26, p.27, KPI narrative — 📄, a filed statement tied to the NWC line item). This is defensive stocking and multi-year vendor agreements, not captive raw-material ownership or captive power. Strength: **Weak-Moderate**.

**B2 — Qualification lock-in.** The strongest category in the scan. AS9100-D, NADCAP AC7120/AC7121, ISO13485, ITAR, Industrial License, and a newly confirmed IATF16949 (2026, AR p.9 timeline; Q3/Q4 FY26 calls) held concurrently, plus explicit management characterisation of aerospace and medical as switching-cost businesses: "the high switching cost once qualified and the long qualification cycle serve as a barrier to entry, not just a constraint" for Med Tech (AR FY2025-26, p.51-52). Multi-year, multi-decade program relationships named: Honeywell, Thales, Collins Aerospace, BEL, BAE Systems, Rafael (AR p.24-25 timeline; Q1 FY27 call, p.17-18). Contract lengths of up to 20 years cited for some B2S programs (Q2 FY26 call, p.15). Evidence: 📄 (certifications, filed AR narrative) + 🎙️ (management framing of the barrier). Strength: **Strong**.

**B3 — Supply chain network effect.** No platform, no hub-and-spoke, no aggregation model. **NO EVIDENCE FOUND**.

### FAMILY C — CUSTOMER

**C1 — Customer ecosystem / embedded relationships.** Early-stage design engagement across accounts: "we are deeply embedded in their design and manufacturing cycle" (Q3 FY26 call, p.2-3); customers "showing interest in partnering... both for redesign and new product launches" positioning the company "not only as a manufacturing partner but as a strategic collaborator through the product life cycle" (same page). Contract assets (unbilled revenue tied to B2S) exist as a distinct balance sheet line (Q4 FY26 call, p.16 — "Contract assets is unbilled revenue... more from the B2S thing," 📄-adjacent since it is a real balance sheet item, though the amount was not quantified on the call). No wallet-share %, no cross-sell count disclosed. Evidence: mostly 🎙️, one 📄-adjacent balance sheet fact. Strength: **Moderate**.

**C2 — Customer concentration improving.** Consolidated top-customer revenue concentration fell from 69.21% (FY25) to 55.71% (FY26) — AR FY2025-26, Note on major customers, p.171 (📄, audited). The consolidated improvement is substantially an ARTIFACT of Altek (US subsidiary) consolidation changing the denominator and customer mix, not organic India-core diversification. New-logo additions are real and roughly consistent across calls (2 per quarter in three of four quarters), but the headline concentration number should not be read as "moat" without the standalone caveat. Strength: **Moderate**, with the artifact explicitly flagged.

The "net cash" position is a balance-sheet snapshot funded substantially by unspent IPO proceeds and reserves, not by organic cash generation. No independent credit-rating upgrade for the COMPANY itself is disclosed (ICRA appears only as the IPO-proceeds monitoring agency, AR p.46, not as an issuer rating). Strength: **Weak**.

**G2 — Working capital improvement trajectory.** This category runs in REVERSE. Score: 0, flagged as a negative indicator, not merely absent evidence.**
