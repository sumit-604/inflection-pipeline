### 1E. Chai-stall-uncle version
Think of a tailor who makes special cloth for the army's parachutes. The army tests his cloth for years before it trusts him. Once it does, he is hard to replace. Now he has also started stitching the whole parachute himself, which pays more per order. But the army places big orders only when it wants, so some years he is flooded and some years he waits. He also sells outdoor and car cloth, which orders more steadily. He built a much bigger workshop last year, and it is half full. Meanwhile customers owe him a lot, and he must wait for them to pay.

### 2D. Lifecycle
