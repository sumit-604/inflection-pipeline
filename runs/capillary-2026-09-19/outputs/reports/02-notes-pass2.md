## A. PASS 1 OPEN ITEMS — SETTLED

### A1. Contingent liabilities: CONFIRMED genuine gap, not an extraction artifact
Read directly at the line level with page breaks intact (standalone Note 31, p.166; consolidated
Note 34, p.244). Both notes read, verbatim: "...the Company/Group records a liability for any
claims where a potential loss is probable... For potential losses that are considered possible, but
not probable, the Company/Group provides disclosure... **The following is a description of claims
and assertions where a potential loss is possible, but not probable.** The Company/Group believes
that none of the contingencies described below would have a material adverse effect..." — and then,
in both notes, the very next line is the heading of the NEXT note (32/35, Related party
disclosures). No table, no bullet, no single item follows the sentence that promises one. The text
flow across the page boundary is continuous (footer page number to `[page N]` header, no gap),
so this is not a PDF-to-text drop. 🟡 Watch — this reads as genuinely NIL contingent items, but the
note's own drafting is internally inconsistent: it promises "a description of claims... below" and
then supplies none. Either the boilerplate should have read "there are no such claims" or a
schedule was omitted from the final filed AR. The auditor's Rule 11(h)(i) statement (Note 34
cross-reference, consolidated auditor's report — same phrase both years) treats Note 34 as the
complete pending-litigation disclosure, which supports a genuine-NIL reading over a data-loss
reading. (Standalone Note 31, p.166; Consolidated Note 34, p.244; Auditor's Report Rule 11(h)(i)
cross-reference to Note 34.)

### A2. Note 36(iii) major-customer >10% disclosure: CONFIRMED genuine drafting defect in the filed AR
Read at the line level (consolidated Note 36(iii), p.246-247): "Revenue from one customer for
March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 that individually accounted for more than 10% of the total
revenue." — the sentence has a subject and a qualifying clause but no predicate: no name, no
amount, no "Nil." It is immediately followed by "(b) Geographical information" (a new
sub-heading), with no intervening table row. This sits mid-page, not across a page break, so
extraction loss is not the explanation. 🟡 Watch — the intended disclosure was very likely "Nil / no
single customer exceeds 10%" (consistent with USA 55.6% / UK 15.4% / Others 29.0% geographic
mix and no single-customer language anywhere else in the AR), but as filed the sentence is
grammatically incomplete and the Ind AS 108 disclosure requirement is not actually discharged.
Worth a direct management query rather than treating "Nil" as established fact. (Note 36(iii),
p.246-247.)

### A3. Churn-indemnity acquisition identity: RESOLVED — it is Kognitiv, and the CAD currency is now explained, not anomalous
The Notes to Financial Statements (consolidated Note 28, p.235) do not name the acquisition, as
Pass 1 found. But the Management Discussion & Analysis section of the same Annual Report (p.82)
states directly: "An exceptional item of ₹249.6 million — compensation received under **the
Kognitiv acquisition agreement's** Churn Indemnity Clause, following the seller's non-fulfilment of
certain commitments — is included within profit before tax." Cross-referencing Note 39 (Business
Combinations, p.254) and the Group structure note (p.256): **Kognitiv Solutions Inc. is a Canada
(Waterloo)-incorporated step-down subsidiary**, acquired 1-May-2025 for **CAD$23.44 million
(₹1,447.43mn)**. This fully resolves Pass 1's flagged "minor inconsistency" — the churn-indemnity
receipt being CAD-denominated (CAD 3.88mn / ₹249.60mn) is exactly consistent with a
Canadian-jurisdiction target; it is not a currency anomaly at all. 🟢 Resolved. (MD&A, p.82; Note 28,
p.235; Note 39, p.254; Group structure note, p.256.)

## B. NEW FINDINGS (not in Pass 1)

### B1. Standalone Ratio Analysis note (Note 36, p.174-176): standalone profitability DETERIORATED sharply, and one footnote reason contradicts the reported direction
Pass 1 touched Note 36 only for its "Inventory turnover ratio is not applicable" line. The rest of
the note was not extracted. It shows, standalone (India entity) only:
| Ratio | FY26 | FY25 | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Return on equity ratio | 0.44% | 0.74% | -40.24% |
| Net profit ratio | 1.36% | 2.01% | -32.27% |
| Return on capital employed | 0.79% | 2.13% | -62.81% |
| Return on investment (treasury) | 4.50% | 12.06% | -62.67% |
| Trade receivable turnover ratio | 3.49 | 3.97 | -11.91% |
| Trade payables turnover ratio | 0.93 | 1.31 | -28.73% |

🟡 Watch — every standalone return metric fell YoY even as standalone revenue grew 21.7% and the
consolidated growth story (EBITDA +35.65%, PBT +378%) was being reported one note-set over.
The mechanism: the equity base roughly doubled post-IPO (₹4,777mn → ₹8,232mn) while
standalone net profit stayed near-breakeven, so ROE and RoCE compress mechanically even though
nothing operationally worsened. This is a legitimate reminder that the standalone/India entity is
a cost-plus service arm, not the profit engine (consistent with Pass 1 Section 1/2/10 findings on
the LRD structure) — but it is also a genuinely new, quantified data point Pass 1 did not surface.
Separately, the note's own explanatory footnote (b), used for both the ROE and net-profit-ratio
rows, reads **"Due to decrease in losses during the year/period"** — language that describes an
IMPROVEMENT, yet the ratios in the same row moved in the opposite (worsening) direction. This is
an internal inconsistency in the note's own drafting, not merely an ambiguous explanation. (Note
36, p.174-176.)

### B2. Capital Management note: "Total Fund" excludes ₹3,232.40mn of fixed deposits disclosed elsewhere, at both standalone and consolidated level
Standalone Note 35 (p.173-174) and Consolidated Note 38 (p.253) both define "Total Fund" as
**Cash and cash equivalents + Current Investments only**:
- Standalone: Cash & cash equivalents ₹104.14mn (FY26) = Total Fund ₹104.14mn.
- Consolidated: Cash & cash equivalents ₹358.11mn + Current Investments ₹1,246.23mn = Total
  Fund ₹1,604.34mn.
Neither figure includes the **₹3,232.40mn "Other bank balances"** (fixed deposits, substantially the
unutilised IPO proceeds, per Note 10/Note 9 footnote already flagged in Pass 1 Section 12) that sits
on the same balance sheet. Consolidated Net Debt is shown as **₹(1,068.27)mn** (net cash) using
only the ₹1,604.34mn Total Fund figure — an investor who reads Note 38 in isolation would
understate the Group's true liquid position by roughly ₹3.2bn (true liquid assets ≈ ₹4.84bn against
total debts of ₹536.07mn). This is standard Ind AS 7 classification (fixed deposits with a specific
earmark/longer tenor are not "cash equivalents"), so it is not an accounting error, but the
Capital Management note's own "Total Fund" line is a materially incomplete liquidity picture unless
cross-referenced to Note 10. 🟡 Watch — disclosure-clarity issue, understates rather than overstates
strength, so not a risk flag in itself, but worth naming for anyone building a leverage/liquidity view
off Note 35/38 alone. (Standalone Note 35, p.173-174; Consolidated Note 38, p.253; Note 10 "Other
bank balances," ₹3,232.40mn both note sets.)

### B3. Fair value hierarchy confirms: no forward-contract balance anywhere, despite a stated hedging policy
Pass 1 flagged this as a policy-statement-vs-disclosure gap; this pass confirms it at the most
granular level available. The standalone fair value hierarchy table (Note 34, p.172) lists exactly
one line item — "Current investments" — at Level 1/2/3, all "-", for both years. There is no
forward-contract or derivative line in the table at all, not even a "-" row. Standalone Note 34(2)
(p.172-173) states in the same note-set: "The Company holds forward contracts such as foreign
exchange contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates on foreign currency
exposures," immediately followed by a currency-exposure table showing only gross USD/other
receivable and payable balances, no forward notional or MTM value anywhere. 🟡 Watch — confirmed
gap, not a Pass 1 read error: either the stated hedging policy is not currently being executed
(dormant policy language carried from a template) or a required disclosure (forward notional, fair
value, hedge effectiveness) is missing from the filed note. A direct management query is
warranted; do not assume active FX hedging is in place based on the policy sentence alone. (Note
34(2), p.172-173.)

### B4. Standalone-level Inter-Corporate Deposit (ICD) activity in FY25 — nuances Pass 1's "no ICDs found" statement
Pass 1 Section 6 stated "No ICDs or loans given to related parties or third parties were found in
either note set." The standalone cash flow statement (p.145) and consolidated equivalent both
show, for FY25 only: "Corporate deposits placed (400.00)" and "Proceeds from redemption of
corporate deposits 799.99," alongside "Interest income on corporate deposits 25.18" (FY25) in the
Other Income note (standalone Note 21; consolidated Note 23, p.233-234) — versus ₹Nil for all
three lines in FY26. 🟢 Informational, not a red flag: this confirms the Company/Group did use
short-term corporate deposits as a treasury instrument in FY25 (unwound before FY26, consistent
with the FY25 investments being redeemed ahead of the Kognitiv acquisition funding need), and no
counterparty name is given so there is no evidence these were related-party placements. Pass 1's
statement is accurate for the FY26 closing balance but should not be read as "the Company has
never used ICDs" — it had, one year prior. (Note 21 standalone / Note 23 consolidated, "Other
income," p.233-234; cash flow statement financing/investing sections.)

### B5. "MS Biotech Private Limited" — a related party with zero disclosed transactions, missing from Pass 1's RPT table
Both the standalone (Note 32(a), category D "Other related parties," p.166) and consolidated

### B6. "Restated Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss" — a labelling artifact in Note 2.1, likely carried over from IPO drafting

necessary" boilerplate, Note 40 standalone, p.177). Flagged for completeness under
`restatements_found`, not as a substantive restatement. (Note 2.1, p.195.)
