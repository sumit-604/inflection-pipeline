## Definitions

- **Operating PAT** = (profit before tax − other income − one-time items) ×
  (1 − tax rate). One-time items removed: exceptional items, forex gains and
  losses, inventory gains from price spikes, liquidated damages, write-backs,
  provision reversals, one-off tax credits, non-recurring orders. Banks keep
  interest and fee income; only treasury gains and one-offs are removed.
- **Growth**: FY27E operating PAT over FY26, and FY28E over FY27E.
- **Probability**: chance that actual operating PAT lands within 15% of the
  estimate, as a percentage.
- **Operating PE**: today's market cap (screener.in, 30-Oct-2026, service
  record) over operating PAT.
- Sources: the card, then the committed corpus (filed), then screener.in's
  public P&L tables (service record, rounded to Rs 1 crore, labelled in each
  row). Quarters filed after a card were used where screener.in shows them.
  Row-level detail with citations: `work/opgrowth/all.json` in the session.
