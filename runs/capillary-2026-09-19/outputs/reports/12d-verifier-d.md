## PART 2: FINDING D-1 (MAJOR-leaning paraphrase distortion)

**Location**: B06 report, Q6 verdict table, peer-evidence cell (NEWGEN
Srinivasu K exchange).

**B06's characterisation**: "concedes agentic workflows will disrupt
'more ad hoc or departmental or user-centric workflows... low-code
companies,' while arguing 'serious enterprise workflows'... stay
defensible — a segmented, not blanket, defense."

**Source text** (NEWGEN Jan 2026 call, Virender Jeet, lines 603-611):
"there is a segment of market, which is typically what we call more ad hoc
or departmental or user-centric workflows... That is an area which is
getting slightly more transformed. But again, in that transformation, the
traditional BPM or low-code companies are the ones who are going to WIN
that area."

**The distortion**: B06's prose groups "low-code companies" together with
the disrupted segment, implying low-code companies are what gets
disrupted. The transcript says the opposite of that grouping: low-code
companies are positioned by Virender Jeet as the WINNERS of that
ad hoc/departmental segment (i.e., NEWGEN's own product line is what
stays out of that segment, not what wins it) — NEWGEN is conceding a
segment to a *different category of competitor* (low-code vendors), not
naming low-code companies as agentic-AI casualties. The individually
quoted fragments are verbatim and correctly located, so this is not a
fabricated citation; it is a comparison-direction error in the connecting
prose that could mislead a reader of B06 into thinking NEWGEN sees
low-code vendors as at risk, when NEWGEN sees them as beneficiaries of
a segment NEWGEN itself is ceding.

**Severity**: MAJOR-leaning-MINOR. It does not change Q6's overall
verdict (PARTIALLY VERIFIED still holds on the weight of the other two
peers' clearer statements, and the segmented-vs-blanket framing point
survives independent of this one clause), so I grade it MAJOR rather than
CRITICAL (nothing verdict-card level rests on this specific clause) but
flag it for correction since it materially mischaracterises what one
peer said about competitive dynamics — exactly the kind of claim
Role 2/Role 3 could lean on if read without the source.
