## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

**3A — RPT history.** No royalty, management fee, inter-company loans, or guarantees for private entities disclosed.
Only RPT items in FY24-25: three directors' spouses each paid an **identical** Rs 6.19 lakh/year salary (✅ AOC-2) —
a mild pattern-flag (round, identical figures across unrelated individuals suggest formulaic rather than
role-differentiated pay), though quantum is immaterial (~0.7% of employee benefit expense). Zero transactions with
the three promoter-controlled, co-located enterprises despite disclosed "significant influence" status (see 1B).

**3B — Capital allocation behaviour.** 9:1 bonus issue (capitalizing reserves) ahead of IPO; fresh issue of
34,99,200 shares at Rs 73 (Rs 63 premium) in the IPO itself; no promoter warrants issued or lapsed. IPO proceeds are
transparently and fully applied against stated purposes — working capital Rs 1,700 lakh (100% utilized), general
corporate purposes Rs 616.03 lakh (Rs 17.63 lakh unutilized as of 31 Mar 2025), issue expenses Rs 238.39 lakh (✅
CARO clause (x)(a) — no evidence of proceeds diversion). No dividend declared; Company states this is to conserve
resources for growth, a defensible stance for a growth-stage SME with full IPO-proceeds deployment.
⚠️ **Flag:** Director remuneration rose 37.02% (Dosajh, Aiyer) and 53.35% (Raina) in the IPO year versus a median
**employee** remuneration increase of only 11.98% and non-managerial average increase of 7.19% — average managerial
increase (38.79%) is roughly 5.4x the average employee increase (7.19%) (✅ AR Annexure-III, fully disclosed).
Absolute quantum remains modest (Rs 47–87 lakh per executive) and ratio-to-median-employee (13–17x) is not extreme
in isolation, but the *direction and magnitude of the gap in a listing year* is worth monitoring.
❓ The MDA's own boilerplate risk factor states: "Apex Ecotech has allotted Equity Shares during the preceding year
at a price potentially lower than the IPO price, which could raise concerns for investors." No specific transaction,
price, or allottee is named anywhere in the FY25 AR itself; this reads as standard SME-prospectus risk-factor
language referencing historical pre-IPO private placements rather than a specific identified promoter-favorable
allotment. The full RHP "Outstanding Litigations & Material Developments" chapter (p.240 per its own index) that
would substantiate or dismiss this could not be retrieved during this run (403 Forbidden on the NSE archive) —
carried as an open item, not resolved either way.

**3C — Promoter share transactions.** Pledge: **0.00%** (📰 Trendlyne, current shareholding pattern, corroborates
AR-derived promoter figures). No creeping acquisition, no evidenced promoter selling (total promoter share count
unchanged YoY per Note 2(d) — the % decline is dilution, not sales, see 1A), no warrant exercise/lapse, no

**3D — Minority treatment.** SME-Emerge listing exempts the Company from the full Corporate Governance Report

**3E — Auditor relationship: CLEAN.** Statutory auditor Raman Chawla & Associates continuing on a multi-year term
