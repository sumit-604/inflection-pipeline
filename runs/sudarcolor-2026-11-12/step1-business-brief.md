## The one engine
The thesis rests on one thing: the new parent loading the Roha plant with group volume. The evidence is the 47% Q2 revenue jump and the shift from traded goods to own manufacture. Two readings exist. Reading one: a lasting step-up as Sudarshan moves Heubach products to its low-cost India site. Reading two: a one-time inventory and channel fill routed through group entities. The observation that separates them is the related-party share of sales and receivables in H1 FY27 against FY26.

## Peers (selected by economic engine: pigment manufacture, same end markets)
- SUDARSCHEM, Sudarshan Chemical Industries: https://www.screener.in/company/SUDARSCHEM/consolidated/ . The parent and India's largest pigment maker. Same products, same customers, and its calls discuss this company's plant and the Heubach integration.
- MOL, Meghmani Organics: https://www.screener.in/company/MOL/consolidated/ . Large phthalocyanine pigment segment with export-led sales; reads pigment demand and pricing from a non-group maker.
