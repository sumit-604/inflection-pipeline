Management Views, Mr. Mohit Vohra, Managing Director:
"Q1 FY27 reflects the continued execution of our strategy across both business segments.
In Specialty Cables, strong year-on-year growth of ~41% was driven by sustained demand
from railways and other safety-critical applications, reinforcing our confidence in the
long-term opportunities available across our target markets.
In the Train Control and Signalling segment, we continue to make steady progress toward
KAVACH 4.0 certification. Following approval from the Research Designs and Standards
Organisation (RDSO) to commence passenger trials, our onboard system is currently
undergoing live field validation on a dedicated train and route allocated by Indian
Railways. This represents the final regulatory milestone before full-scale commercial
deployment.
Our total order book of Rs. 8,457 Mn, remains robust, supported by mandates from
Chittaranjan Locomotive Works (CLW), Integral Coach Factory (ICF), Banaras Locomotive
Works (BLW), and Patiala Locomotive Works (PLW), alongside our trackside deployment
project being executed in partnership with RailTel.
These engagements provide strong revenue visibility for FY27 as certification milestones
are achieved..."

Our Journey.
2015-16 Incorporation. 2017 Commissioned the 2.5 MeV Electron Beam Accelerator and cable
manufacturing facility; AERB licence for the irradiation centre; RDSO approval for supply
of specialty railway cables. 2018 Approved by the naval defence industry for cables for
ships and submarines. 2019 Conversion from private to public limited. 2020 Software
development and embedded centre at Bengaluru; initiated development of TCAS (KAVACH);
installed automated rail signalling hardware systems facility. 2021 First KAVACH order at
South Central Railways. 2022 RDSO approval on TCAS infrastructure and capabilities; first
in-house Kavach system for prototype testing by RDSO; solar and EV cable development.
2023 Listing on NSE and BSE. 2024 Interim Independent Safety approval for KAVACH 4.0; BIS
approval for solar cables. 2025 Received approval to commence passenger trials for Kavach
4.0; RDSO approval to commence passenger trials for Kavach 4.0.

Specialty Cables overview. Electron beam irradiated cables using a 2.5 MeV electron beam
industrial accelerator. "A leading supplier to Railways and Defence PSUs, diversifying
revenue streams by expanding into Solar Power and EV cable markets." "In-house compounding
& backward integration creates margin resilience against commodity price swings; an active
order book of Rs 407 Mn."
Revenue (Rs Mn): FY22 1,042 | FY23 1,510 | FY24 1,506 | FY25 1,462 | FY26 1,530
Revenue split by customer (as of Q1FY27): Public sector 72%, private sector 28%.
READER NOTE: cable revenue has been flat between Rs 1,462 Mn and Rs 1,530 Mn for four
years since FY23.

===== PAGE 15 =====
Electron Beam Cables. E-beam processing gives enhanced cross-linking and polymer
modification for superior mechanical and thermal properties; no cure time, so energy
efficient and higher throughput; equal dose distribution with online data logging; no
additives and no hazardous chemical by-products; improved resistance to tensile shear,
abrasion, thermal, flame propagation, deformation and cut-through, with weight reduction
and enhanced fire safety. Applications named: EVs, fuel cell vehicles, renewable energy,
aerospace, high-rise infrastructure.
