## PASS 1 SUMMARY — TOP 10 FINDINGS (ranked by investor importance)

1. **Related-party revenue concentration is 28–32% of standalone revenue**, all routed through
   non-consolidated "same management" foreign entities (Aimtron Corporation USA, Aimtron
   Systems USA, American Pinball USA, Aimtron Technologies LLC). Sales alone = ₹4,381.87 lakh /
   27.68% of revenue FY25 (down from 44.41% FY24 but still very high); sales + services =
   ₹4,991.12 lakh / 31.53%. (Note 35, Note 19, pp.105, 108–109) 🔴 Red Flag.

2. **Trade receivables ballooned 417% YoY (₹1,667.78 lakh → ₹8,621.71 lakh) vs 70.3% revenue
   growth; DSO nearly tripled (turnover 9.28→3.08). 46.3% of total receivables (₹3,992.14 lakh)
   is owed by related parties**, including a ₹2,152.54 lakh receivable from Aimtron Corporation
   USA that flipped from a ₹1,061.76 lakh payable the prior year even as sales to that entity
   *declined*. (Note 15, Note 35, Note 38, pp.103, 108–111) 🔴 Red Flag — **feeds FLAG-CASH.**

3. **Contingent liabilities surged to ₹2,090.94 lakh (₹20.91 Cr) = 13.48% of net worth**, from
   ₹4.04 lakh a year earlier — GST "Excess ITC Claim" dispute ₹1,300.00 lakh (audit initiated
   by department) + Income Tax appeal ₹790.81 lakh. Flagged by the auditor as a Key Audit
   Matter. No provision made, resting entirely on management's own assertion that demands are
   "excessive and erroneous." (Note 17, Note 30, pp.96, 108) 🔴 Red Flag.

4. **A brand-new, unexplained related-party expense of ₹404.99 lakh** ("Services Availed" from
   Aimtron Technologies Private Limited) appears for the first time in FY25 with nil FY24
   comparative and no description of the nature of the services. (Note 35(ii), p.109) 🔴 Red
   Flag.

5. **EBITDA margin compressed ~390bps (25.4%→21.5%, MD&A p.77) despite 70.3% revenue growth**,
   driven by rising raw-material intensity (61.6%→69.2% of revenue, Note 21) from a mix shift
   toward higher-material-content box-build work. PAT margin nonetheless *improved* (14.63%→
   16.26%, Note 38), but that improvement is explained by non-operating tailwinds — interest
   income up 39.5x (Note 20) and interest expense down 84.5% (Note 24) following full debt
   repayment — not by core operating leverage. (Notes 20, 21, 24, 38) 🟡 Watch — quality-of-
   earnings flag.

6. **Finished goods inventory collapsed 93.9% (₹688.30 lakh → ₹41.65 lakh)** while total
   inventory stayed flat and revenue grew 70.3% — plausibly improved fulfilment efficiency, but
   pattern is also consistent with year-end revenue-timing pressure, especially set against
   finding #2's receivables spike. (Note 14, p.102) 🟡 Watch.

7. **Employee benefit expense (salaries) fell 15.8% YoY (₹532.50 lakh→₹448.28 lakh)** despite
   70.3% revenue growth and an MD&A narrative of active hiring/upskilling (~175+ employees) —
   unexplained divergence between the P&L line and the qualitative HR narrative. (Note 23,
   p.106; MD&A p.78) 🟡 Watch.

8. **Export revenue mix collapsed from 73.9% to 44.7% of total revenue** — exports grew only
   3.0% (₹6,872.64 lakh→₹7,078.07 lakh) while total revenue grew 70.3%, implying the growth
   engine has shifted almost entirely to domestic industrial/IoT contracts. Strategically
   significant, not flagged or explained anywhere in the notes. (Note 31 vs Note 19, p.108) 🟡
   Watch.
