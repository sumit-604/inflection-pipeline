## PASS 3 — PATTERN FINDINGS

This pass re-approached the document non-sequentially, specifically targeting: (a) contradictions
between notes, (b) note-vs-main-statement mismatches, (c) deliberately vague/minimal disclosure next
to detailed disclosure elsewhere, (d) restatements, (e) subsequent events, and (f) going-concern
language. This required a targeted re-read of the Independent Auditors' Report, CARO Annexure-A and
Annexure-B (p.67-74) — sections that Pass 1 and Pass 2 already treated as legitimate cross-reference
sources for note-anchored findings (e.g. the Note 6 inventory red flag, the Note 18/Item 18 surety
finding) but had not yet read for their own internal-consistency content against the notes.

### 1. 🔴 Key Audit Matters section is structurally present but contains ZERO actual matters
The Independent Auditors' Report (p.67) carries a full "Key Audit Matters" heading with standard
boilerplate: "Key audit matters are those matters that, in our professional judgment, were of most
significance in our audit... We have determined the matters described below to be the key audit
matters to be communicated in our report." The very next heading is "Management's Responsibility for
the Financial Statements" — **no matter is actually listed or described.** For a year featuring a
₹72.91 Cr static, unexplained Deferred Tax Asset (Note 2(m)/Balance Sheet), a 145.9% finished-goods
inventory swing verified only "on an estimation basis" (Annexure-A item 2, p.70), two consecutive
years of large, differently-sourced exceptional items propping up PBT (Note 27), a fresh ₹37.74 Cr
re-leveraging event (Notes 13/16), and a solvency-ratio note that mischaracterizes deteriorating
metrics as improvements (Notes on Accounts item 17) — the complete absence of any communicated KAM is
itself a disclosure-quality concern: either a templating carry-over error from a standard-form small-
company audit report, or a genuine failure by the auditor to flag any area of elevated audit risk in a
year with this much financial-statement complexity. (Independent Auditors' Report, p.67-68.)

### 2. 🟡 Auditor's CARO attestation on RPT compliance does not sit easily against the notes' own RPT disclosure gaps
CARO Annexure-A item 13 (p.72) states: "transactions with the related parties are in compliance with
section 177 and 188 of the Act, where applicable and the details have been disclosed in the financial
statements as required under applicable Accounting Standard." This is a direct, unqualified sign-off
on RPT disclosure adequacy. Yet the RPT note itself (Notes on Accounts Item 12, p.92) omits
prior-year comparatives (required under Ind AS 24), omits a relationship description for its sole
listed related individual, and — per Pass 2 Finding #1 — omits PCR Holdings, Char Investment and
Trading Ltd, Lenzing Poly Packs Ltd and DBG Leasing & Housing Ltd entirely, despite Note 11's own
"Promoters/Promoter Group shareholders" table (p.79-80) confirming their promoter-group status one
page earlier in the same financial statements. The auditor's clean CARO attestation and the actual
notes content do not align. (Auditor's Annexure-A item 13, p.72; cross-ref Notes on Accounts Item 12,
p.92; Note 11, p.79-80.)

### 3. 🟡 "No cash losses" (CARO) sits in tension, though not technical contradiction, with the negative operating cash flow already flagged
CARO Annexure-A item 17 (p.72): "The company has not incurred cash losses in the financial year and
the immediately preceding financial year." In Indian CARO usage "cash loss" is a defined term (broadly
net loss adjusted for non-cash charges), distinct from "negative cash flow from operating activities" —
so the statement is technically accurate given PAT was positive both years. However, read next to Pass
1 Finding #1 (operating cash flow of -₹11.06 Cr in FY25 despite positive PAT of ₹13.95 Cr) and CARO
item 19's separate clean assurance that "nothing has come to our attention" regarding one-year
liability-discharge capability, the cumulative tone of the audit report's liquidity-adjacent
representations reads more reassuring than the company's own Solvency Ratio note supports (current
ratio 0.80x, DSCR 0.23x, both below 1.0x/0.5x in both years). Not a contradiction in the technical
sense — a juxtaposition worth flagging. (Auditor's Annexure-A items 17 & 19, p.72; cross-ref Cash Flow
Statement p.85; Notes on Accounts item 17, p.93.)

### 4. 🟢 No going-concern qualification, no Emphasis of Matter paragraph anywhere in the report
The Auditor's Responsibility section's standard going-concern paragraph (p.68) and CARO item 19 (p.72)
both give clean, unqualified language; no "material uncertainty related to going concern" disclosure
exists anywhere in the document, and there is no separate Emphasis of Matter paragraph. This is
recorded factually rather than as a red flag — see `going_concern_language: NONE` in the YAML below —
but is held alongside Pattern Finding #3 as a place where the audit opinion's tone and the ratio note's
own numbers (sub-1x current ratio, sub-0.5x DSCR, both years) diverge.

### 5. No new restatement quantification found
CARO/Annexure-A does not identify or quantify any specific restatement beyond what Pass 1 already
flagged as Notes on Accounts item 22's generic "figures regrouped/recast wherever necessary"
boilerplate (p.94), which remains undetailed and unquantified. No note anywhere names a specific
line item restated, no prior-year restatement note, no SEBI/regulatory-directed restatement. This
confirms rather than adds to Pass 1's existing observation.

### 6. No new subsequent (events-after-balance-sheet-date) items found
No dedicated "Events after the Reporting Period" note exists in either the balance-sheet-linked note
sequence (Note 1-27) or the Notes on Accounts (Item 1-22) — NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT as a formal note.
The only post-year-end dated items in the AR are procedural (AGM notice dated 03/09/2025, Directors'
non-disqualification certificate dated 03/09/2025, Corporate Governance certificate dated 27/08/2025,
Auditor's Report itself dated 30/05/2025) — none disclose a subsequent event with a quantified P&L or
balance-sheet impact.

- **Aggressive/inconsistent accounting:** FVOCI-classified quoted investments carried at cost (not fair value) in the primary "Value" column despite Ind AS 109 classification and an OCI fair-value movement that ties exactly to the P&L's Total OCI line; static Deferred Tax Asset carried with no reassessment against a stated non-calculation policy.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators:** MSME creditor status unknown company-wide; surety for director-interested "other Companies" left unquantified and contradicting a separate Corporate Governance representation; GAIL gas dispute wholly unquantified; empty Key Audit Matters section in the audit report itself.
