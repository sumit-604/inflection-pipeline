Piyush Tamboli
Chairman & Managing Director
“Our focus is capability before volume and capacity we have 
already built.”
Over five decades, I&PCL has grown from a traditional foundry into a precision-com ponent
manufacturer serving demanding global industries.
Our priority remains capability rather than tonnage: bringing critical processe s in-house and
earning the approvals that let us quote for higher-value work.
In the first half of FY 2026 –27, revenue rose 26% to ₹111.4 crore and net profit more than
doubled to ₹10.8 crore - already 92% of last year’s full-year profit. Aerospace and defence grew
to 11% of revenue, and EBITDA margin rose to 21.6%. In the second half, our focus i s moving
more nominated parts into monthly supply and continuing to build new capabilities. Our focus as
always shall be on new technologies, automation and R&D.
Further, I’m happy to share that our Board of Directors has approved a capital investment of up
to ₹100 crore towards the development and execution of various projects in the Aero space,
Defence & Energy sector, aimed at strengthening the Company’s capabilities, expanding its
infrastructure, and capitalising on emerging business opportunities in these strategic sectors.

R I S K S  &  O U T L O O K
Key risks and how they are managed
Customer and segment concentration The top five customers account for about half of revenue. Automotive remains the largest segment, though a falling share as industrial and aerospace & defence work grows.
Schedule variability Call-offs follow customers’ production, so volume changes reach us within weeks; ten end markets spread the exposure.
Technology shift in automotive Parts serve engines, transmissions and emission systems; diversification reduces dependence on any one powertrain.
Long qualification cycles New parts take months to years to approve; a wide base of qualified, repeat parts cushions the lag.
Working capital intensity Most inventory is work-in-progress in a long process, not idle stock. Our focus remains on prudent working capital management.
Input cost and currency Managed through pass-through contract terms and own renewable generation.
