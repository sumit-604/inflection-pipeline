### NEW FINDING A — Note 36's own printed ratios do not reconcile to
its own disclosed base-value table (FY26, fully self-checkable)

Note 36 (p.69, lines 9627-9842) discloses both the ratio values AND the
base-value inputs (Section B of the note) with an explicit formula
reference per row. Recomputing three rows using the note's own stated
formula and its own stated FY26/FY25 base figures:

| Ratio | Note 36's formula | Note 36's base values (FY26/FY25) | Note 36's printed ratio | Independent recomputation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade Receivables Turnover | L ÷ average of E | L=5,323.3; E=2,483.2/1,923.2 | **2.14x** | 5,323.3 ÷ 2,203.2 = **2.42x** |

By contrast, Current Ratio (2.55x), Debt-Equity (0.35x), Return on Equity
(13%) and Net Profit Ratio (12.17%) all recompute to the printed value
exactly using the same base table — the errors are confined to the three

base]" structure. This is consistent with (not proof of) the same
off-by-one-period drafting defect Stage 2 already found at Note 3.5, Note
32 (item 6 above) and elsewhere in this filing: a stale or misaligned
averaging base carried into the current-year column. **A separate,

recomputation.** The direction of travel (deterioration) is confirmed by
all three versions; the magnitude is not consistent across any two of
them. Rating: RED FLAG (extends B02's disclosure-transparency finding;

### NEW FINDING B — The IPO-proceeds redirection is described three
different ways across three sections of the same Annual Report

| Section | Mechanism stated | Date stated |
|---|---|---|
| MD&A, Capital Allocation & IPO Proceeds (p.27, lines 1955-1958) | "Extraordinary General Meeting" | 17 April 2026 |
| Note 32, Utilisation Summary (p.68, lines 9576-9580) | "EGM" | held on April 17, 2026 |
| Board's Report, Material Changes section (p.36, lines 3407-3410) | "Special Resolution passed through Postal Ballot" | 29 April 2026 |
| Board's Report, General Meetings table (p.39-40, lines 4059-4077) | Postal Ballot: notice 24-Mar-2026, voting 31-Mar to 29-Apr-2026 | Result declared 30 April 2026 |
| Secretarial Audit Report, Annexure A supplementary note (p.44, lines 4870-4903) | "Special Resolution passed through Postal Ballot" | 29 April 2026 |

Three independent sections — the Board's Report itself (twice, internally
consistent with each other), and the external Secretarial Auditor's
report — describe a Postal Ballot process with a notice date of 24-Mar-2026
and results declared 30-Apr-2026, with no mention of an EGM at all. Two
sections — the MD&A and Note 32, both part of the financial-reporting
package rather than the compliance package — describe an "Extraordinary
General Meeting held on 17 April 2026" instead. Both cannot be literally
correct: an EGM and a postal ballot are different Companies Act
mechanisms (Sections 96/100 vs Section 110), and 17-Apr-2026 falls inside
the Postal-Ballot voting window (31-Mar to 29-Apr-2026) described
elsewhere, not at its start or end. This reads as the same rolled-forward/
stale-draft defect pattern already identified at Note 3.5 and Note 32's
own subscription-year error (B02 finding 6): the MD&A and Note 32 sections
likely carried forward language from an earlier draft (when an EGM may
have been the contemplated mechanism) that was never reconciled against
the Postal Ballot process the company actually ran. The underlying rupee
amount (₹764.51 Lakh) and the substance of what was approved are
consistent across all five citations — this is a mechanism/date
inconsistency, not a numbers error. Rating: RED FLAG (a filed,
Secretarial-Auditor-countersigned compliance document disagrees with the
company's own financial-reporting narrative on how a 25%-of-proceeds
capital reallocation was actually approved).
Anchor: as tabulated above.

### LOAD-BEARING FACT 3 VERIFICATION — "IPO proceeds and investments"

Note 14 (Non-Current Investments, p.65, lines 8852-8870) resolves the
"investments rose ₹0.57 Cr to ₹4.76 Cr" question cleanly: the FY26
increase is entirely **"Deposits — Maturity More than 12 months"**
(₹475.6 Lakh, up from ₹39.8 Lakh), i.e. long-term bank fixed deposits, not
an equity or related-party investment. The FY25 unquoted-equity-shares
line (₹17.0 Lakh) fell to **nil** in FY26. There is no evidence of a
related-party investment or an undisclosed equity stake; the increase
reads as margin money / FD placement against the enlarged short-term
borrowing facility, consistent with a working-capital-stressed balance
sheet parking surplus cash in interest-bearing deposits rather than
deploying it. No RPT link found in Note 28's related-party list for this
investment. RESOLVED, not a red flag on its own terms — but it means the
₹475.6 Lakh is locked up (>12-month maturity) at the same time operating
cash (₹250.7 Lakh) has fallen 73.6% and short-term borrowings have more
than doubled, which is a liquidity-mix observation worth carrying into
Phase 3.
Anchor: Note 14 (p.65, lines 8852-8870).
