## OPERATOR OVERRIDES

**Override 1 — ROCE forward verdict.**
- Draft determination: ROCE forward STAGNANT (0), read off the reported ~5% ROCE. Composite +3, DEEP WATCH.
- Operator ruling: ROCE forward RECOVERING (+1). Composite +4, DEEP WATCH leaning BUY-ON-DIPS.
- Operator's stated reasoning, in the operator's words: "the ROC needs to be calculated after taking out the cash, so we need to focus on operational ROC." On operating capital of about Rs 340 Cr the business already earns roughly 20% to 40%, and it compounds up over three to five years as margin expands and the ESOP charge normalizes, so the forward ROCE is recovering, not stagnant.

**Override 2 — Pillar 1 ROCE input for Phase 3.**
- Draft determination: Pillar 1 uses current reported ROCE (~5%), base PE ~10x.
- Operator ruling: Pillar 1 uses operating ROCE ex surplus cash on a normalized ESOP basis (~20% to 30%), base PE ~18x to 22x.
- Operator's stated reasoning, in the operator's words: "focus on operational ROC." Do not let the idle cash pile and the temporary high ESOP charge price down the quality base. Operating capital = total capital employed less surplus cash and treasury investments (about Rs 340 Cr at FY26); normalize ESOP toward a 4% to 5% SaaS norm.

**Correction accepted on review (not an override, a factual fix).** The draft implied customer count was flat to shrinking. Verified against the Q4 FY26 call (Vijay NP, p.15-16): total customer count grew 396 (FY24), 463 (FY25), 492 (FY26); the over-1-million-dollar cohort grew 28 to 35. What slowed is the pace of net adds (67 to 29). Revenue growth is expansion-driven, carried by net revenue retention around 125% (constant currency north of 120%). The retention engine, not the logo count, is the revenue transition to watch.

## FINAL FTTCP VERDICT (operator's position)

Composite +4 out of 8. DEEP WATCH leaning BUY-ON-DIPS.

In the operator's frame: value the operating business, not the idle balance sheet. Once the cash pile and the shrinking ESOP charge are taken out, the operating return is high and rising, and three of the four transitions are working: revenue firing, margin starting, ROCE recovering on the operating base. The one unresolved transition is cash conversion, which is INDETERMINATE and holds the disposition no better than PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. The whole call turns on the first clean quarter free of IPO and ESOP settlement cash, because that single receivables-and-cash print decides both the cash flag and whether operating ROCE keeps compounding or stalls. Business investable; a small starter is defensible only with a strict entry zone, and only after the Phase 3 price work sets that zone.

Operator sign-off: given 2026-07-12. Overrides above adopted. Proceed to Phase 3 (/finalize).
