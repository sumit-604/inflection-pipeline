Run date: 2026-09-07. Five transcripts analysed, oldest first: Q4 FY25 (Concall_Q4FY25_Jun2025_Transcript.pdf, 30-May-2025), Q1 FY26 (Concall_Q1FY26_Aug2025_Transcript.pdf, 13-Aug-2025), Q2 FY26 (Concall_Q2FY26_Nov2025_Transcript.pdf, 18-Nov-2025), Q3 FY26 (Concall_Q3FY26_Feb2026_Transcript.pdf, 16-Feb-2026), Q4 FY26 (Concall_Q4FY26_Jun2026_Transcript.pdf, 2-Jun-2026). **HEADLINE FINDING, stated up front**: across all five calls management consistently used the wine-subsidiary revenue figure as "the company's revenue," while the filed consolidated results for the same periods (FY24, FY25, and Q1 FY25) also carried a large legacy agro-commodity/steel-abrasives trading book. Management never once named this legacy business, its wind-down, or its effect on comparability, on any of the five calls (confirmed by exhaustive keyword search: zero hits for "agro," "steel," "trading," "discontinued" across all five transcripts). This silently flatters every FY24-vs-FY25 and Q1 FY25-vs-Q1 FY26 growth comparison management gave verbally. Separately, the recomputed EBITDA (PBT + finance cost + depreciation, the same method that produced the standing correction of -2.5% FY26 margin against management's claimed +0.57%) shows the SIGN of management's own claimed EBITDA is wrong in three of the last four quarters reported on these calls (Q3 FY26 9-month, Q4 FY26 quarter, and FY26 full year all recompute negative against positive claims made live on the calls). Both findings are set out in full below.

### 1C. Trigger evolution across the five quarters

- **EU/UK FTA narrative — materially evolved, never reconciled to itself.** Q4 FY25 and Q1/Q2 FY26 calls describe only the concluded Australia/UK FTAs: wines under $5 FOB stay protected at unchanged 150% duty (Q4 FY25 call, p.9; Q1 FY26 call, p.11; Q2 FY26 call, p.7-8). From Q3 FY26 call onward, a substantially different EU FTA structure is introduced with no acknowledgment that it supersedes the earlier framing: duties phasing down to 20% (premium) / 30% (mid-range), applying above a EUR2.5 CIF floor (Q3 FY26 call, p.4). The "luxury segment exposed to FTA" salience is also given as two different numbers in adjacent quarters: "13%" (Luxury+Super-Premium combined, Q2 FY26 call, p.13, after Hemant Arora's own clarification mid-answer) versus "7%" (Luxury alone, above Rs2,000 MRP, Q3 FY26 call, p.10 and Q4 FY26 call, p.7). These are not necessarily contradictory (7% is a plausible subset of 13%) but management never states the reconciliation itself.
- **Karnataka greenfield winery — DROPPED.** Named once, as a firm capex-relevant plan ("possibly setting up a new Greenfield winery in Karnataka," Q1 FY26 call, p.4), and never mentioned again in Q2, Q3, or Q4 FY26 calls. No explanation for the disappearance.
- **Hospitality/wine-tourism — timeline and budget both slipped, and the budget figure was internally inconsistent within a single call.** See 1B table. Net effect: construction start pushed by at least a year, opening (2028, stated once) never reaffirmed, and budget oscillated Rs100cr → Rs70-75cr → Rs100cr → Rs70-80cr across four calls.
- **Fundraise plan — introduced then quietly dropped.** Q2 FY26 call: "we are thinking along the lines of doing a fundraise… via equity and not by debt" for the Rs100cr capex programme (p.6-7). Not mentioned again on the Q3 or Q4 FY26 calls; by Q4 FY26 the capex programme itself had shrunk to Rs6-10cr/year plus a deferred hospitality budget, and Preeti Shah's direct question on financing plans (Q4 FY26 call, p.15) got only "we don't see any major capex need… if we require [capital] we have many tools available," with no reference back to the earlier equity-fundraise plan.
- **Cost-optimisation quantification — stated once, never re-quantified.** "200-250 bps" EBITDA benefit from solar/operational efficiency given once (Q1 FY26 call, p.9) and never revisited with an actual delivered number on any later call, even as EBITDA became the central topic of every subsequent call.
- **EBITDA/breakeven narrative — strengthening in stated confidence, but weakening in reconciliation to the filed numbers** (see Section 2A and Required Check 7 below): the gap between what is claimed on the call and what the same day's filed P&L recomputes to widens, not narrows, across Q2→Q3→Q4 FY26.
