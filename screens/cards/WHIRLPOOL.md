## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Whirlpool of India makes and
sells refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners under a licensed
global brand. It also owns Elica India, which sells kitchen hoods and hobs.
The company reports one segment, home appliances (Results FY26, page 18,
note 4). It earns a thin margin on a large volume. FY26 consolidated revenue
was Rs 8,034.20 crore and profit after tax Rs 295.30 crore, a 3.7% net margin
(Results FY26, page 17).

**What is changing?** Two things change at once. First, the owner steps back.
Whirlpool Corporation cut its holding from 51% to 39.76%, and the company
ceased to be its subsidiary on 28-Nov-2025 (Results FY26, page 18, note 8).
Second, the mix moves up. Air conditioners and front-load washers now drive
growth. Q1 FY27 standalone revenue grew 11.4%, a record quarter (Q1 FY27
deck, page 29).

**Why now?** Revenue turned. Standalone growth ran minus 3%, minus 5%, plus
4%, plus 7% and plus 11% over the last five quarters (Q1 FY27 deck, page 10).
On 23-Sep-2026 the share rose 20%, from Rs 721.15 to Rs 865.35, on a report
that the promoter will sell its entire stake (Rumour verification, page 1).

**What must be true?** Revenue growth must turn into profit growth. Today it
does the opposite. Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue rose 12.1% while profit before
tax fell 29.4% (Q1 FY27 deck, page 30).

**What breaks it?** An industry that cannot pass on cost. The MD said so
directly: "companies have not been able to recover the impact of the combined
war and regulatory impact through pricing because of industry competitive
dynamics" (Q1 FY27 call, page 4).

## 2. Business model and archetype

**Archetype: brand/franchise consumer.** Volume growth, pricing power,
distribution reach, gross margin and advertising efficiency are the variables.

The engine is share gain in multi-brand outlets. The company holds the number
two volume position in refrigerators and washers combined, and number one in
direct-cool refrigerators for seven months (Q1 FY27 deck, page 7).

Three growth lines sit on top of the core:
- Air conditioners: about 1.5 times last year's Q1 (Q1 FY27 deck, page 23).
  The MD calls AC "de facto, a lower margin business than refrigerators or
  washers" (Q1 FY27 call, page 29).
- Front-load washers: 1.8 times last year's volume (Q1 FY27 deck, page 22).
- Elica India: Q1 revenue plus 26%, profit before tax plus 22% (Q1 FY27 deck,
  page 7). Now 100% owned after a Rs 58.56 crore buy of the last 3.18% on
  10-Mar-2026 (Results FY26, page 18, note 9).

A fourth income line exists outside the product business. The company sells
services to Whirlpool Corporation under an agreement that runs to March 2029,
"at arm's length on a cost-plus basis" (Q1 FY27 call, pages 28 to 29).

## 3. Competitive advantages

Partial, and eroding at the margin. Brand, service reach and a number two
share position are real. The net promoter score chart climbs every quarter
from January 2021 (Q1 FY27 deck, page 26).

Pricing power is absent in this cycle. Q1 FY27 standalone gross margin fell
from 30.6% to 27.5% (Q1 FY27 deck, page 11). The MD states the gross margin
drop flowed straight to profit before tax (Q1 FY27 call, page 6).

The brand is licensed, not owned. Every filing page carries "Whirlpool is a
trademark of Whirlpool Properties Inc. and is used under license" (Results
FY26, page 1). A promoter exit makes the licence terms load-bearing.

**Moat: present in distribution and brand recall, absent in pricing.**

## 4. The promoters

Whirlpool Corporation, through Whirlpool Mauritius, held 75% on 1-Apr-2023
and 51% on 31-Mar-2024 (Annual Report FY25, page 189). It held 39.76% after
the dilution disclosed in the FY26 results (Results FY26, page 18, note 8).

The MD describes the relationship as "completely at arm's length" and says:
"I know as much as you do on this topic" (Q1 FY27 call, page 13). The company
states it "is not a party to any such proposed transaction" (Rumour
verification, page 2).

Management owns shares with its own money. Six leadership members, including
the MD and CFO, bought stock (Q1 FY27 call, page 30). A new ESOP vests at face
value, fully on performance, with three-year cliff vesting (Q1 FY27 call,
page 30).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R3, a value-added supplier with brand pull but no pricing power in a
cost shock. TO a claimed R4, a share-of-wallet leader built on premium
products and a full portfolio.

Mechanism: fill the gaps in the range, then premiumise. The 654-litre
four-door refrigerator launched on the day of the Q1 call, on a Rs 245 crore
Pune line (Q1 FY27 call, page 7). The large frost-free gap was "one of the big
chunks missing from our portfolio" (Q4 FY26 call, page 10, via Bull AI
guidance record).

Management's claim, quoted once: "It would be lovely to get to double-digit
volume share in the next two to three years" (Q1 FY27 call, page 19, via Bull
AI guidance record).

The ladder evidence runs against the claim this year. An R4 business holds
margin when input costs rise. This one lost 310 basis points of gross margin
in one quarter. **Ladder reading: R3, no climb evidenced.**

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim share gain, premium mix and revenue growth. Revenue
delivers. Standalone growth rose four quarters in a row to 11.4% (Q1 FY27
deck, page 10).

Profit does not. Q4 FY26 consolidated profit before exceptional items fell
29.0% (Q4 FY26 deck, page 28). Q1 FY27 fell 29.4% (Q1 FY27 deck, page 30).

**Named absence:** no quarter shows margin recovery. The two readings are
temporary cost shock versus lost pricing power. The observation that
separates them is gross margin in a quarter where crude and the rupee
stabilise. Q2 FY27 is the first candidate.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Brand licence.** The brand belongs to Whirlpool Properties Inc. Licence

- **Related-party income.** Services to Whirlpool Corporation run to March

- **E-waste provisioning.** The company provides "at the highest possible

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The top line is real. The margin is not recovering. The multiple already
prices a sale of control. This is a special situation, not a transition climb.
The strategy screens for climbs.

**Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides**
1. Q1 FY27 results filing: profit after tax, tax rate, other income.
2. Brand licence terms and royalty, and what happens on a promoter exit.
3. Sales of services to Whirlpool Corporation, amount and margin.
4. Q2 FY27 gross margin against Q2 FY26.
5. ROCE ex-cash, from the FY26 annual report.

**What would change the view**
- Gross margin back above 30% with AC share still rising moves it to WATCH.
- A disclosed buyer with a stated plan for the brand and the cash makes it a
  special-situation file, outside this funnel.
- A third quarter of profit decline confirms the structural reading.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set.

**How the range is built.**
- Revenue applies the Q1 FY27 growth rates, 11.4% standalone and 12.1%
  consolidated (Q1 FY27 deck, pages 29 and 30), to FY26.
- Low profit: the Q1 profit-before-tax decline of 29.4% holds all year. FY26
  profit before exceptional items of Rs 425.56 crore becomes Rs 300.4 crore.
- High profit: Q1 FY27 delivers Rs 139 crore and Q2 to Q4 repeat FY26. Q1 FY26
  profit before tax is derived at Rs 196.9 crore from the stated 29.4% fall.
  The rest of FY26 is Rs 228.7 crore. Total Rs 367.7 crore.
- Tax at 25.17% is an **assumption**. FY26's effective rate was 25.5%
  (Results FY26, page 17). Q1 FY27 profit after tax is NOT FOUND.

**Seasonality.** Q1 is the summer peak. Q1 FY26 carried 30.3% of FY26 revenue
and about 46% of profit before exceptional items, on the derived figure.
Multiplying Q1 FY27 by four would overstate the year. The card does not do it.

**The warning this ruling exists for.** The forward multiple rises as
earnings fall. Neither end of the range shows profit growth. Management gives
no FY27 rupee guidance. The long-run guide is high single-digit growth at a
high single-digit margin (Q4 FY26 call, page 27, via Bull AI guidance record).
