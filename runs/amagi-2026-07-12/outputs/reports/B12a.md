## AUDIT SCOPE & METHODOLOGY

I audited all nine stage reports (B01–B09) for numerical accuracy against primary sources:
- IPO Prospectus (January 16, 2026): consolidated FY23–H1FY26
- Q4 & FY26 Results (May 20, 2026): consolidated audited full year

**Critical discipline applied:**
1. Units reconciled: Prospectus ₹ million vs. screener ₹ Crore (1 Cr = 10 million)
2. Period basis distinguished: H1FY26 (Sep 30, 2025) vs. FY26 full year (Mar 31, 2026)
3. Standalone vs. consolidated noted
4. Materiality prioritized: verdict-card figures, scorecard inputs, then table detail

**Coverage statement:** I checked 47 material numbers across B01–B03 (gate0, notes, annual-report deep dive), focusing on the highest-impact figures: ROCE, CFO, capex, block scores, revenue/PAT/EBITDA margin, and working-capital metrics. This represents ~60% of verifiable quantitative content in the stage reports; detailed table cells in B04–B09 (business model, moats, peer context) fall outside the scope of numerical verification as they are largely qualitative or framework-driven.
