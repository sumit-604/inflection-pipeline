## Risk to the verdict

The devil's advocate review left the AVOID weakened but alive. The strongest counters to the AVOID:
- The 35% base holding company discount may be too harsh. Only about Rs 65.59 Cr, roughly 20% to 24%, of the book is lien-marked, the securing working-capital term loan was fully repaid, and CARE rates the group net-debt-negative. At a 20% to 25% discount the base SOTP flips above CMP, so "the market already fully prices it" is partly an artifact of the chosen discount.
- Eimco may be conservative at 8 times: a 31% PBIT-margin engineering franchise with a Rs 257 Cr order against a 25x engineering sector cap; 12 times adds about Rs 6.5 per share, and order execution could re-rate the slice above Rs 250 Cr.
- The structural sugar-loss label may be overstated: PBIT was positive Rs 6.51 Cr in FY24 and collapsed on a 39% to 42% cane-crush drop; a cane and E20 recovery swings PBIT by roughly Rs 20 to 25 Cr, the gap between base and bull.

Holding-company-discount sensitivity, stated explicitly: the AVOID is conditional on the 35% discount. At 20% to 25% the base flips above the current price and the decision would move toward WATCHLIST-active rather than AVOID. Against that sensitivity sit the value-trap counters: the discount may be permanent (no demerger, buyback or dividend catalyst, FII about 3.31% and fragmented), family incentives favor keeping the Rs 332 Cr portfolio undistributed, and roughly Rs 30 Cr a year of cash burn erodes the asset while the unfiled order remains a single point of failure.
