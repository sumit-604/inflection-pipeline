## NARRATIVE

MACPOWER is a good business at the wrong price. The numbers that matter for quality are real: 23.75% ROCE, a net-cash balance sheet, a TRUSTWORTHY promoter with zero pledge across three years, an order book worth 1.37x revenue, and a genuinely massive addressable market. Only one of the four transitions is firing, though, and it is revenue. Margin, cash, and capital efficiency are holding, not improving. The cash question is the live one: CFO/PAT fell to 0.41x while receivables grew 57.5% against 8.6% revenue and over-six-month ageing rose to 15.4%. The pipeline calls that INDETERMINATE, and INDETERMINATE caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS.

The price is the disqualifier. At Rs 1,481 the stock trades at 34.4x forward earnings against a destination PE that is cap-bound at 25.0x even after crediting undiscovered alpha. Base fair value is Rs 1,075, bull is Rs 1,100, and both sit below the current price. The 25% hurdle STOPS on base and bull earnings alike, and the probability-weighted three-year return is negative 10.4%. There is no version of the model at today's price that clears the bar.

The verdict is AVOID on valuation, with the business kept on watch. The two things that would change the call are concrete: EBITDA margin clearing 17.5% while operating cash conversion clears 0.5x. If both print, the transition score moves to +4 and the name earns a re-run. Until then it stays a fast grower that does not yet convert, and the price still has to fall by roughly half to reach the Rs 440 to Rs 550 entry zone. Buy only when price and a clean quarter arrive together.
