### 2C Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Using pending capex under execution (₹10,617cr, the amount not yet spent) and the FY26 average net fixed-asset turnover:
- Net PP&E: Mar-25 ₹6,196.7cr, Mar-26 ₹7,968.7cr (Inv. Pres. p.58, Consolidated Balance Sheet); average = ₹7,082.7cr
- FY26 Revenue from operations: ₹18,552cr (Inv. Pres. p.57/p.39)
- Implied historical fixed-asset turnover = 18,552 / 7,082.7 ≈ **2.62x**
- Implied incremental revenue from pending capex = 10,617 × 2.62 ≈ **₹27,817cr**
- As % of current (FY26) revenue: 27,817 / 18,552 ≈ **150%**

🔍 ANALYST INFERENCE — this mechanical calculation is a multi-year (management guides 3–5 years for most of the pending capex to commission) figure, not a next-12-month number, and it should be read with skepticism: it assumes the *new*, more capital-intensive stainless/HR-mill/SBQ capacity converts revenue at the *same* turnover as the existing, more commodity-heavy asset base, which is unlikely (new lines are generally lower-turnover, higher-margin). As a sanity check, management's own FY31E revenue guidance (₹42,647cr vs FY26 ₹18,552cr) implies +130% cumulative growth over 5 years (Inv. Pres. p.46) — directionally consistent with, but somewhat below, the mechanical 150% estimate, supporting the flag that the mechanical figure likely overstates near-term convertibility.
