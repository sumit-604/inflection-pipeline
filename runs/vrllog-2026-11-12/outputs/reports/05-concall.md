### LB1. MARGIN PAIR: Q2 FY27 23.3%, H1 22.6% against "21-22% sustainable" and the earlier 20-21%

- FACT: Q2 FY27 EBITDA Rs 226 Cr vs Rs 158 Cr, margin 23.3% vs 19.7%, +367 bp (Q2 FY27 call, p.3-4, CFO opening). Q1 FY27: EBITDA Rs 193 Cr, 21.8% (Q1 FY27 call, p.4). 226/967 = 23.37% (DERIVED), so management's margin is on total income, other income included. B03 analyst_note puts Q2 FY27 ex other income at 22.97% and H1 at 22.14% (screener, DERIVED). Management never separates other income on any call. The H1 22.6% figure itself is not stated on any call.
- What management says drove the gap (Q2 FY27 call, p.3, opening): freight realisation +9% (Rs 8,079 to Rs 8,840 per tonne); employee cost 18% to 16.48% of income; administrative expense 2.28% to 1.43% ("lower legal and professional expenses incurred in the last year", a base effect); other operating expense 10.48% to 10.06%. Mix of the three cost lines = 1.52 + 0.85 + 0.42 = 2.79 points of the 3.67-point gain (DERIVED from the stated ratios). The remaining ~0.9 point is not itemised on the call. Fuel as % of income is NOT stated on the Q1 or Q2 FY27 calls (it was stated on Q3 FY26 at 24.8% and on Q4 FY26 at 24%).
- Fuel cost per litre rose Rs 85 to Rs 97 (Q2 FY27 call, p.3). Bulk purchase from refineries stopped from Q1 FY27 because the bulk price runs Rs 15 above the pump price (Q1 FY27 call, p.14; Q2 FY27 call, p.11, bulk Rs 120-125 against Rs 98-100). The fuel hit was recovered by price: realisation +9% against fuel +Rs 11-12 per litre.
- What management guided, call by call:
  - Q3 FY26 (6 Feb): FY27 EBITDA "around 20.5%"; "around 20% definitely"; new normal "20%, 21%" (p.7).
  - Q4 FY26 (19 May): "21% plus" for Q1; "20% plus going forward" (p.14); a few bp pressure from lost bulk purchase (p.6).
  - Q1 FY27 (5 Aug): "20% to 21% is maintainable, even for next 3 to 4 years" (p.10); "maintenance of existing profitability margins" (p.16).
  - Q2 FY27 (5 Nov): "21%-22% is a sustainable EBITDA margin even going forward" (p.6, p.8, p.11, p.13). In the same call, answering Alok Deora: "the 23% plus margin is sustainable", with a "small correction" if toll and overhead rise (p.12). Closing: "at least around 21% plus" (p.14). Cost-impact estimate: "not more than 1%-1.5%" off 23% (p.11).
- Reading: three different margin statements sit in one call (21-22%, 23% plus, 21% plus). The 21-22% band is the one repeated four times. The gap between 23.3% and 21-22% is 1.3 to 2.3 points, which management itself attributes to expected cost leakage (toll, handling, wages) and to a fuel-price reversal pass-through. It does not name a specific item that takes the margin down. FACT: no wage increment is guided in Q2 FY27 (see 2A row 8).
- Two readings and the one observation that separates them (A26.3 rule): (1) 23% is the new level because the price reset plus operating leverage is structural; (2) 23% is a peak with fuel-driven price embedded, and it reverts to 21-22% as fuel reverses and wage increments land. Separating observation: Q3 FY27 margin after the autumn wage increment decision and after any fuel-led freight cut. Flagged, decision stays human.
