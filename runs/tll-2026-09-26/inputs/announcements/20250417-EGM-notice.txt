TO INCREASE THE AUTHORIZED SHARE CAPITAL OF THE COMPANY AND MAKE CONSEQUENT ALTERATION

In order to broad base capital structure of the Company and to enable the Company to issue further shares, it is proposed to increase the
authorized share capital of the Company from existing Rs. 12,00,00,000/- (Rupees Twelve Crore Only) divided into 1,20,00,000 (One

company, aggregating to Rs. 3,00,00,000 (Rupees Three Crore only).

requires the approval of members of the Company in general meeting under Sections 13, 61, 64 and other applicable provisions of the
Companies Act, 2013, as well as any other applicable statutory and regulatory approvals.

Members’ Inspection.

is, in anyway, concerned or interested in the above Resolution except to the extent of their shareholding in the Company.
