         Glossary (3/6)
         Term                 Description
          Revenue             Revenue from operations plus income on finance lease



          EBITDA              EBITDA is calculated as profit / loss after tax plus tax expense, finance cost, depreciation and amortization
                              expense for the period



          EBITDA margin (%)   EBITDA margin is calculated as EBITDA divided by revenue



          EPS                 Annualized PAT divided by number of equity shares



          Recurring Revenue   Portion of a company’s income that is stable, predictable, and expected to continue in the future,
                              typically generated from ongoing customer contracts, subscriptions, or repeat services.



          One Time Revenue    Portion of a company’s income earned from non-recurring transactions, such as a single sale, project, or
                              service, that may not repeat in the future.
