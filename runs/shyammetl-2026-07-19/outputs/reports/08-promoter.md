No IT search-and-seizure, transfer-pricing dispute, benami finding, or promoter arrest found.

### 2D. Other regulators

### 2E. Civil litigation
Long-running trademark/passing-off suit: **Shyam Steel Industries Limited (unrelated competitor) v. Shyam Sel and Power Limited** (SMEL's material subsidiary) and another. Calcutta High Court Division Bench ruled for Shyam Steel Industries on 24-Dec-2019, granting an injunction restraining the SMEL-group defendants from using the "SHYAM" mark pending final disposal; matter proceeded to the Supreme Court on procedural grounds, interim injunction continued, remanded for expedited hearing [📰 MEDIA/legal-database — TaxGuru, IndianKanoon case citation, JusIP, Khurana & Khurana; ✅ VERIFIED via IndianKanoon]. This is an IP/brand dispute between competitors, not a fraud or promoter-conduct matter — CAUTION-tier, not a red flag.
