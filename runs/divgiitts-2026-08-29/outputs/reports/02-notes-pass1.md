# PASS 1 SUMMARY

Top 10 most significant findings, ranked by investor importance:

1. **🔴 RED FLAG — Spear cash-decline fact not corroborated by the Notes.** Cash & cash equivalents (Note 10(a), p.226) rose from Rs 154.36 Mn to Rs 315.20 Mn, and combined cash-like assets (cash + bank deposits, Notes 10(a)+10(b), p.226) rose from ≈Rs 284.8 Cr to ≈Rs 294.5 Cr in FY26 — an increase, not the "~Rs 275 Cr net cash decline" carried as the Spear's load-bearing fact. This must be reconciled (scope, period, or metric mismatch) before it drives any downstream valuation or verification work. *Why it matters: if the Spear's fact is simply wrong or mis-scoped, downstream stages built on it inherit the error; if it is right and the Notes are incomplete, that is itself a disclosure gap of the first order.*
