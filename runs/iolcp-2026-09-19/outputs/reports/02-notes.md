## PASS 3: PATTERN RE-READ

Re-read the notes a third time, this time hunting for contradictions, note-to-note or

**NEW: Note 35(B)(ii) table says Nil, but its own footnote discloses ₹11.31 Cr executed (p.161-162).**
The commitments table states "Export obligations under Advance Authorisation/Duty Free Import
Authorisation" = ₹0.00 Cr in both FY26 and FY25. The footnote marked "#" against that exact line
reads: "During the year, the company has executed bonds for an aggregate amount of ₹11.31 Crore
(Previous Year ₹26.16 Crore) in favour of The President of India... for fulfilment of the
obligation under the [Advance Authorization Scheme]" (p.161-162). A bond executed under an export
obligation scheme, worth ₹11.31 Cr, sits directly below a table row that records the corresponding
obligation as zero. The company applies the same "bond value ≠ outstanding obligation" logic
elsewhere in the same note (IGCR: ₹27.65 Cr of surety executed, but only ₹2.83 Cr of "obligations
against concessional-duty imports" carried as outstanding, Note 35(iii) — a distinction the AR
states explicitly there). For Advance Authorisation, the AR does NOT state that the ₹11.31 Cr bond
corresponds to a fully-discharged (Nil outstanding) obligation — it simply shows Nil with no
reconciling sentence. 🟡 Watch — plausibly benign (bond executed and obligation met within the
same year, consistent with the IGCR pattern), but this is the one note pair in the whole set
where the headline figure and its own footnote do not visibly reconcile, and the AR gives the
reader no bridge. Carried to Question 5 below.
