## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

**2A. Demand environment.** The peer set's collective framing across Nov-2025 to Aug-2026 is broadly *strong-with-short-lived-dips*, not "industry-wide volume decline" as DPABHUSHAN frames it. SENCO under-guides and beats every quarter (FY26 actual 33% YoY revenue growth against 18-20% guidance). PNGJL beats and raises. RBZ is smaller and choppier (a genuine Q2 FY26 slowdown tied to GST-2.0 timing and gold-price confusion, per SENCO; job-work volume degrowth at RBZ) but ends FY26 "broadly in line with guidance." The short dips that do appear (SENCO's Sep-2025 wait-and-watch, the May-2026 PM-appeal footfall dip, Adhik Maas) are consistently described as **days-to-weeks**, not quarter-defining, and are consistently followed by strong recoveries the peers quantify (SENCO's October 2025: highest-ever single month; April 2026 Akshaya Tritiya: +66-67%). This complicates rather than supports DPABHUSHAN's framing of a sustained, industry-wide volume/demand problem.

**2B. Pricing and input costs.** Two consistent sector threads not fully echoed in DPABHUSHAN's own narrative (per B05): (1) the customs-duty hike (6%→15%) is discussed by every peer primarily as an **inventory-valuation tailwind** realised over Q1-Q2 FY27 (SENCO estimates ~9% of gold inventory value, ~INR400-450cr gross, tempered to "at least 6-7%" after competitive discounting), not primarily as a demand shock; SENCO explicitly flags the **symmetric downside** — a future duty cut would reverse this gain into a loss, as happened in FY25 (an INR57cr negative hit when duty fell from 14-15% to 6%) — a forward risk DPABHUSHAN's calls do not appear to address (per B05, no symmetric-downside discussion is recorded). (2) Old-gold exchange has become the dominant sourcing channel across the sector (SENCO ~44-50% of revenue by Q4 FY26, up from 25-30% a few years back; RBZ discusses corporate clients shifting toward GML/job-work for the same reason), consistent with DPABHUSHAN's own DP Swarn Plus emphasis (per B05) but at a scale (44-50% of revenue at SENCO) that suggests DPABHUSHAN's exchange-share trend deserves a peer-scale comparison DPABHUSHAN's own disclosures (per B05) don't provide a directly comparable percentage for.

**2C. Capex cycle.** This is an **industry-wide capacity race**, not a lone expander. SENCO: 18-20 new stores/year skewed toward franchise. PNGJL: 25 stores/year, targeting 150 stores by FY28 (from 66 at end-2025), explicitly funding this off internally generated cash plus an unexercised QIP option. RBZ: smaller in absolute terms but adding two large-format (INR125-150cr inventory each) flagship stores in Surat and Rajkot, funded by debt plus inventory transfer, deliberately not asset-light. DPABHUSHAN's own 51-by-FY30 ambition (per B05) sits inside this same broader organized-sector land grab — which raises the stakes on DPABHUSHAN's already-flagged store-pace math not reconciling (per B05 FLAG-GUIDANCE-WHIPSAW): if peers with materially larger balance sheets and better guidance discipline are opening 18-25 stores/year, DPABHUSHAN's 3-6/year pace is not keeping up with the land grab even on its own numbers.

**2D. Competitive mentions.** No peer transcript names DPABHUSHAN, "D.P. Abhushan," "D.P. Jewellers," or gives any unmistakable description of it (targeted search across all 12 files, zero hits). This is a genuinely silent corpus on this point — DPABHUSHAN does not register as a competitor of note to SENCO, PNGJL or RBZ management in any of the 12 calls read.
