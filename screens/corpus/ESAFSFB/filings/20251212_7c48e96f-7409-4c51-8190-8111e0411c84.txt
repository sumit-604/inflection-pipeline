Sub: Disclosure under Regulations 30 and 51 of Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 - Outcome of 
Board Meeting held on Friday, December 12, 2025 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing 
Regulations”), we hereby inform that, the Board of Directors of the Bank, in its meeting held 
on Friday, December 12, 2025, have, inter alia, considered and approved the following:  
 
1. 
Proposal for sale of NPA and written-off loans to Asset Reconstruction Company (ARC) 
as listed below: 
  
➢  Pool size up to ₹1700 crores (Including Technical Write off Pool)  
➢  Bank carries an overall provision of 94 % on the above pool. 
  
The Board of Directors have authorized the Asset Sale Committee of Executives to 
negotiate and finalise the valuation under Swiss challenge method. 
    . 
2. 
Took note of the retirement of Shri. Ajayan Mangalath Gopalakrishnan Nair (DIN: 
09782416), Non-Executive Nominee Director of the Bank with effect from December 12, 
2025.
