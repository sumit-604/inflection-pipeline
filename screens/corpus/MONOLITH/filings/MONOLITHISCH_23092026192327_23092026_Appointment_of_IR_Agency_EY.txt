Subject: Appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as Investor Relations (IR) Partner. 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that Monolithisch India Limited has appointed Ernst & 
Young LLP (EY) as its Investor Relations (IR) Partner, effective October 1, 2026, for a period of one 
year. “EY”, recognised amongst one of the globally Big Four professional services networks, 
brings extensive global reach, strong capital markets expertise and deep experience in investor 
communications and advisory. 
 
Through this strategic engagement, EY will work closely with Monolithisch to strengthen and 
institutionalise its Investor Relations function, with a focus on building structured, transparent 
and consistent engagement with the investor and analyst community. EY's scope will encompass 
investor engagement and communication planning, investor targeting and mapping, sentiment 
tracking, preparation and refinement of investor communication materials, quarterly earnings 
call support, analyst and investor interactions, investor conferences and Non-Deal Roadshows 
(NDRs), along with other strategic Investor Relations initiatives. 
 
This appointment reinforces Monolithisch India Limited's commitment to robust investor 
communication, enhanced stakeholder engagement and sound corporate governance, while 
supporting the Company's ongoing Investor Relations initiatives. 
 
The requisite disclosure of details as prescribed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) 
Regulations, 2015 and applicable SEBI circulars is enclosed as Annexure A.
