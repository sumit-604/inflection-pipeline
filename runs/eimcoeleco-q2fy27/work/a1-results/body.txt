 Executive Director’s Remarks

 “Our performance in the second quarter of FY27 reflects the sustained momentum in our
 business and the effective execution of our strategic priorities. Revenue for the quarter
 increased to ₹57 crore from ₹33 crore in the corresponding period last year, representing strong
 year-on-year growth of 70.9%. This performance was driven by robust execution of our order
 book, supported by healthy order inflows and sustained demand across key business drivers.

 This encouraging performance in the first half of the year reinforces our confidence in the
 underlying strength of our business and the long-term growth opportunities across the mining
 and infrastructure sectors.

 Our EBITDA for the quarter increased by 108.8% year-on-year to ₹11 crore, with the EBITDA
 margin improving to 18.8%, primarily driven by an improvement in operational leverage on the
 back of higher revenues and better capacity utilisation, alongside disciplined cost management.
 PAT stood at ₹8 crore, registering a 46.5% year-on-year increase. Margins improved year-on-
 year, supported by better capacity utilization and efficient execution, which helped optimise
 other operating costs. Margins are in line with our targeted range of 18-20%. We remain
 focused on driving operational excellence while continuing to invest in capabilities that
 strengthen our competitive position and create sustainable value for our stakeholders.

 Looking ahead, we remain optimistic about the demand environment, supported by ongoing
 infrastructure development and increased mining activity. With a healthy open order pipeline of
 ₹133 crore, strong customer relationships and a continued focus on innovation, quality and
 execution, we are well positioned to sustain our growth momentum and capitalize on the
 opportunities ahead.”


 - Kamlesh N. Shah

 Executive Director, Eimco Elecon (India) Limited

About the Company

Eimco Elecon (India) Limited is a leading manufacturer of underground mining and construction
equipment, delivering engineered solutions that enhance safety, productivity and operational
efficiency.

Backed by a legacy of engineering excellence, the Company offers advanced mining systems,
innovative battery-operated vehicles and high-performance construction equipment designed to
address the evolving requirements of the mining and infrastructure sectors.

The Company’s capabilities extend beyond equipment manufacturing, supported by a strong
service and spares network that enables reliable after-sales support and helps customers
maintain equipment performance throughout its operating lifecycle.

With a focus on product development, reliability and customer-centric solutions, Eimco Elecon
continues to strengthen its portfolio and broaden its capabilities across mining and construction
applications. Through continuous innovation and technology-driven development, the Company
remains committed to enabling safer, more efficient and sustainable mining and infrastructure
projects while creating long-term value for its customers and stakeholders.
