## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Achyut buys and resells active pharmaceutical ingredients and finished pharma products. It earns a thin trading spread. FY26 revenue was 1,159.47 against purchases of traded goods of 1,018.87 (AR26, p.101). Exports were 444.76 of that revenue (AR26, p.108). Profit after tax was 31.57 (AR26, p.20). The company started as Achyut Securities Pvt Ltd in 1996 and entered pharma trading in 2015 (Prosp, pp.2, 19).

**What is changing?** Two things. First, the company is building a formulation plant at Bhat, Ahmedabad, for tablets, capsules, soft gels, sachets and a Sevoflurane inhalation liquid, aimed at WHO and EU GMP standards (Scheme, p.4). Capital work in progress stood at 3,161.91 on 30-Sep-2026 (Q2 res, p.3). Second, on 14-Jul-2026 the board approved a merger of Achyut into Zenith Healthcare Ltd, a group company under the same promoters (Scheme, pp.1, 3). Achyut holders get 119 Zenith shares for every 50 Achyut shares (AR26, p.58). Achyut will be dissolved.

**Why now?** It is not obvious. Revenue rose 3.8 times in FY26, but H1 FY27 revenue fell 15% against H1 FY26 (Q2 res, p.2). The listed price moved because of the main-board migration and a preferential issue at Rs 6. The shareholder count rose from 514 in Dec-2025 to 3,925 in Sep-2026 (MKT).

**What must be true?** The plant must start, get its approvals, and earn a manufacturing margin that trading never gave. The merger ratio must be fair to Achyut's public holders. And the combined Zenith entity must do better than either part.

**What breaks it?** A third missed plant date. A merger ratio that transfers value to the promoter group. Or the trading line shrinking further, as it did in Q2 FY27.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reportable segment, "Pharmaceuticals Business" (Q2 res, p.2). Today it is all trading: the P&L carries "Purchase of Traded Goods" and no material cost (Q2 res, p.2). Gross spread in FY26 was about 14% of revenue: revenue 1,159.47 less purchases 1,018.87, plus an inventory build of 24.67 (AR26, p.101, derived).

Where the money goes below gross margin matters more. FY26 other expenses were 126.09 against 18.85 a year earlier. Commission was 63.94, freight 20.81 and bad debts written off 15.00 (AR26, p.101). Commission alone was 1.7 times profit before tax of 37.58 (AR26, p.20).

The MD&A describes the company as operating in "Pharmaceutical formulations in the forms of Tablets, Capsules, Oral liquid and Injectable" (AR26, p.68). That describes the plant, not the current business.

**Archetype: today, none in the library.** A pure trader with no pricing power. The plant would make it an outsourcing or export-generic formulator, the nearest library entry being "Outsourcing partner (CDMO/EMS)". The archetype changes only when the plant runs.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Absent.** Trading margin is thin and volatile. Operating margin was 1.47% in FY26 and negative 4.92% in FY25 (MKT, service record). Profit before tax was 3.2% of revenue in FY26 (AR26, p.20, derived). The company itself says "Very high competition from large and small and new comers in the Pharma field it is very difficult to stand in stable position" (AR26, p.68). It also flags price-control risk under the DPCO (AR26, p.68).

The planned EU GMP plant would be a moat-forming asset if it gets accreditation. The merger rationale says the facility is "intended to be in conformity with the EU Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certification" (AR26, p.57). Intended, not held. **Moat: absent today; a plant-dependent possibility.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R1, commodity price-taker.** A trader with no pricing power and ROCE near zero.

**TO: R3, a value-added, spec'd supplier.** The mechanism is an EU GMP formulation plant whose products reach regulated markets. The scheme filing states the plant will let the merged company "access larger production capacity and specialized products range which will unlock new market opportunities in the regulatory markets with rigorous compliance standards" (Scheme, p.5).

Ladder check. This is a two-rung claim with no plant output yet. The base rate is one rung per two to three years. A multi-rung leap needs extraordinary proof. None exists in the corpus.

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point would be plant output: capitalised assets, manufacturing revenue, a material-cost line. It is absent.** Nothing has been capitalised from CWIP in three years. The FY26 note shows additions only, and 349.93 of the balance is more than three years old (AR26, p.94). CWIP rose again to 3,161.91 in Sep-2026 (Q2 res, p.3). The P&L still shows only traded goods (Q2 res, p.2).

The trading line also did not hold. H1 FY27 revenue was 478.82 against 563.87 (Q2 res, p.2).

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Merger into a smaller related company.** Zenith had net worth of 742.42 and turnover of 1,093.65; Achyut had net worth of 3,506.12 and turnover of 1,197.15 (Scheme, p.3). Achyut is about 4.7 times larger by net worth (derived) but is the one dissolved. After the merger the promoter group holds 45.80% of Zenith (AR26, p.58). Achyut's public holders vote, and the scheme proceeds only if more public votes favour it than oppose it (Scheme, p.4). The valuation report behind 119:50 is not held. This is the single most important document for any holder.
- **A plant date reprinted after it passed.** The AR signed 05-Sep-2026 still says production is expected "in the month of January, 2026" (AR26, p.21). The FY24 AR had said January 2025 (AR24, p.15).
- **Dilution.** Four rounds of equity: IPO, preferential issues in 2023, 2024 and 2026, plus bonus issues and a ten-for-one split (AR26, p.68). Shares went from 93.45 lakh in Mar-2023 to 24.14 crore in Mar-2026 after the split (AR26, p.68). The Jan-2024 round went wholly to the non-promoter public group (AR24, p.15).
- **Preferential price.** 58 lakh shares at Rs 6 against a SEBI floor of Rs 5.67 (EGM, p.26). The stock is Rs 7.30 (MKT). 38 lakh of those went to three non-promoters: two Ruparelia brothers and Strikar Lifescience LLP (EGM, p.6).
- **Commission expense.** 63.94 in FY26 against 1.44 in FY25, with no payee named (AR26, p.101).
- **Sloppy filings.** The FY25 board's report shows PAT as 15.58 and 54.77 in the same table (AR25, p.18). The FY26 report calls total income of 1,197.15 "revenue from operations" (AR26, p.20). It calls the chairman an executive director (AR26, p.47) while the board table lists him as non-executive (AR26, p.25).
- **Exchange penalty.** Rs 5,900 for a late related-party disclosure for H1 FY26 under Reg 23(9) (AR26, p.47).
- **Company secretary turnover.** The CS resigned 17-Apr-2025; a successor joined 05-Jun-2025 (AR26, p.24).
- **Auditor.** Unmodified opinion. Emphasis of matter only on first-time Ind AS adoption (AR26, p.69). Doshi Doshi & Co, peer-reviewed (AR26, p.31).
- **Valuation.** PE 705 and 5.02 times book (MKT, service record).
- **Exchange price queries.** None in the 313 BSE announcements read.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** No plant output; two missed dates (step 8).
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, low confidence.** Near-zero ROCE comes partly from 31.62 crore of idle CWIP. If the plant runs, that optic is temporary. If it does not, it is structural. The corpus cannot tell which.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market cap of Rs 176 crore against net worth of Rs 35.25 crore and PAT of Rs 0.32 crore (MKT; Q2 res, p.3; AR26, p.20). The price already assumes the plant works.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** Clean-sounding story, nothing proven, and the TO state is priced. The merger adds a second reason to wait: the asset being priced will become a minority-diluted part of another company.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

There is no operating transition to study yet. The listed entity is a merger stub. A `/step1` on Achyut would study a company that will cease to exist. If the operator wants this exposure, the name to screen is Zenith Healthcare (BSE 530665) after the scheme is sanctioned.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first (if re-screened as Zenith)**
1. The share-exchange valuation report and fairness opinion of 14-Jul-2026, and how they value Achyut's CWIP (Scheme, pp.3-4).
2. Bhat plant status: commissioning date, capitalisation, GMP and EU inspection dates (AR26, pp.21, 57, 94).
3. FY26 commission of 63.94: payee and whether it repeats (AR26, p.101).
4. BSE observation letter and NCLT timetable for the scheme (not yet filed).

**What would change the view**
- Up to WATCH: CWIP capitalised and a first manufacturing quarter filed, plus a published valuation report.
- Stays PASS: a third missed plant date, or a scheme sanctioned without the valuation basis disclosed.
