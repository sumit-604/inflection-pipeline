### 2C. Tax and revenue
The **company** (not promoters personally) has an indirect-tax litigation
history with the CGST department, at the company level, disclosed in
third-party case-law aggregators (📰 MEDIA REPORTED — casemine.com,
taxscan.in, web search 05-Sep-2026; primary court-document sites
indiankanoon.org and taxreply.com were blocked by this session's egress
proxy and could not be read directly):
- A tax-department Senior Intelligence Officer coercively recovered Rs 3
  crore from Yasho Industries without issuing a show-cause notice or
  completing adjudication. Yasho challenged this in Gujarat High Court,
  arguing (successfully, per secondary reporting) that the officer lacked
  proper "proper officer" authorization and that the recovery violated
  natural justice.
- Separately, the Supreme Court dismissed a Special Leave Petition filed by
  the **Union of India** (i.e., the tax department was the appellant, not
  Yasho), upholding a Gujarat High Court ruling that allowed Yasho to use
  its Electronic Credit Ledger for the mandatory 10% GST-appeal pre-deposit.
Both instances resolve in the company's favor against tax-department
overreach on procedural grounds; neither is a finding of undisclosed income,
transfer pricing abuse, benami holding, or DRI/SFIO action, and neither
names a promoter individually. No search-and-seizure action on the company
or promoters was found. Treated as **informational, not a red flag** — a
mid-cap manufacturer with ordinary GST litigation against an aggressive tax
department, where the company prevailed.

## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. Related-party transaction history

- **Director/KMP loans to the company**: The company borrows from its own
  directors as an unsecured funding source. Balance payable "Loans From
  Directors" (non-current) was Rs 4,650.00 lakh at 31-Mar-2026 (up from Rs
  3,850.00 lakh a year earlier), plus Rs 50.02 lakh current, totaling ~Rs
  4,700 lakh (~Rs 47 crore) — matching the RPT note's "Loans taken from
  KMP" balance of Rs 4,700.01 lakh (VERIFIED: AR Notes 15 and 39, lines
  5751, 5825, 6255). Interest paid to KMP on this was Rs 452.88 lakh in
  FY26 (~9.6% effective rate), against secured bank term loans priced at
  7.5-8.5% p.a. (VERIFIED: AR lines 5761-5795, 6230). The unsecured-loan
  rate premium over secured bank debt is directionally consistent with
  market pricing, not obviously favorable to the promoters; the company
  states all RPTs are at arm's length (line 6247-6248). This is flagged as
  a **funding dependency**, not a governance abuse: the promoters are also
  giving Personal Guarantees on the secured Greenfield-project term loans
  (multiple "PG of Directors" notations, AR lines 5762, 5770, 5779, 5787),
  meaning promoter personal balance sheets are pledged behind company
  credit, which cuts toward alignment rather than extraction.
- **The MNC 15-year supply contract** (₹150 cr projected annual revenue,
  ₹98.12 cr customer advance received by Q1FY27) was explicitly disclosed
  by the company as having **no promoter or promoter-group interest** in
  the counterparty and as **not** falling under the RPT category (📰
  MEDIA REPORTED: multiple trade-press reports, Business Standard,
  ChemAnalyst, Indian Chemical News, web search 05-Sep-2026). This directly
  addresses the company-memory-flagged first verification priority: the
  advance-funded capex is company-to-third-party, not promoter-adjacent.
- Rent paid to related parties: Rs 27.13 lakh FY26 (Rs 29.48 lakh FY25),
  a modest, declining figure (VERIFIED: AR line 6246).
  Dividend paid to KMP/related parties (Rs 27.33 lakh + Rs 13.66 lakh) is
  proportional to shareholding, not preferential.
- Trade receivables from wholly-owned subsidiaries rose from Rs 1,846.00
  lakh to Rs 3,297.05 lakh YoY (VERIFIED: AR line 6262) — a near-doubling,
  worth monitoring as working-capital funding of the export subsidiaries,
  but these are operating entities, not RPT shell vehicles.
- Directors' Report and AR state no materially significant RPT posed a
  potential conflict with company interest during FY26 (VERIFIED: line
  2119-2122).

- **No buyback** found in the AR or search.
- **Remuneration**: Parag Jhaveri (MD & CEO) drew Rs 388.65 lakh in FY26

  EPS rose from Rs 5.25 (FY25) to Rs 17.81 (FY26) (VERIFIED: AR line 6113)

  not divorced from it. A forward revision for Yayesh Jhaveri (cap of Rs
  3.57 crore over 3 years from Apr-2026) is disclosed for shareholder
