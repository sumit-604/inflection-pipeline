## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **No policy changes.** Per the AGM Deposit Circular (Section 4(e), p.24): "There is no change in Accounting Policies during the last three years." Directors' Report Item 14 (p.24) and subsidiary Directors' Reports (p.122, p.172) also affirm no deviation from applicable Accounting Standards. 🟢
- **Mixed depreciation methods within the same asset class.** Note 2(g) (p.86): depreciation is on a straight-line basis for PP&E generally, **except assets situated at the Registered Office, which are depreciated on a written-down-value (WDV) basis.** No rationale is given for splitting method by location rather than by asset nature/usage pattern. 🟡 Watch — unusual, non-standard practice; low quantum (Registered Office assets are a small pool) but a disclosure-quality flag.
- **Impairment testing policy is generic — no company-specific assumptions disclosed.** Note 2(c) (p.83–84) describes the impairment framework (value-in-use, discount rate, growth rates) in boilerplate terms only. No discount rate, growth rate, or sensitivity figures are given anywhere in the standalone or consolidated notes. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT.
- **Capitalisation threshold:** NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — no Rupee threshold disclosed for capitalising subsequent expenditure (Note 2(g), p.86, only qualitative "probable future economic benefits" language).
- **ECL matrix / expected credit loss method:** Note 2(u) (p.92) states the company applies Ind AS 109 "simplified approach" — lifetime ECL on trade receivables. However, the disclosed impairment allowance is **static at ₹27.54 lakh (₹2,753,857) in both FY21 and FY20** (Note 10, p.98) even though receivables >180 days grew from ₹695.25 lakh to ₹751.25 lakh over the same period (Note 48 ageing table, p.110). The allowance does not appear to scale with the growing overdue bucket. 🟡 Watch.
- **Ind AS 116 (Leases):** Note 2(k) "Assets taken on lease" (p.88) is written in pre-Ind AS 116 language (classifying leases as "operating" vs "finance" for the lessee, rather than describing a right-of-use asset/lease liability model). No ROU asset or lease liability appears anywhere on the standalone balance sheet. Rental charges are immaterial (₹318,000, Note 34, p.103), so this is likely within the short-term/low-value lease exemption in substance, but the accounting-policy wording itself has not been updated to reflect Ind AS 116. 🟡 Watch (disclosure quality, immaterial in quantum).
- **First-time adoption of any new standard this year:** NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — no new standard adoption disclosed for FY21.

## 12. OTHER CRITICAL NOTES

### 12a. Core operating result vs reported profit — the central finding
From the Statement of Profit and Loss (p.81) directly:
- Revenue from Operations (Note 27): **₹2,993,915,695**
- Total Expenses (Note IV, sum of Notes 29–34): **₹3,325,900,295**
- **⇒ Core operating result (Revenue − Total Expenses, excluding Other Income) = a LOSS of ₹(331,984,600)** — a loss of ₹33.20 crore from the business itself.
- Other Income (Note 28): **₹447,624,028**, of which **₹315,814,900 is "Change in fair value of financial asset"** — an unrealized, non-cash mark-to-market gain on the FVTPL equity portfolio (§6 above).
- **⇒ Profit Before Tax = ₹115,639,428**, entirely attributable to Other Income, not operations.
- Add: Deferred tax credit ₹75,997,051 (§10) ⇒ **Profit After Tax = ₹191,636,479.**
🔴 **Red Flag — the single most important finding in the notes.** Reported PAT of ₹191.6mn is a product of (i) a ₹315.8mn unrealized equity fair-value gain and (ii) a ₹76.0mn deferred-tax-asset recognition, together far exceeding the ₹(332.0)mn core operating loss. Prior year's mirror-image: in FY20 the same fair-value swing operated in the opposite direction as an *expense* of ₹119,459,890 (Note 34, PY column), contributing to the FY20 reported loss. **The Company's reported profitability over the last two years has been dominated by equity-market-linked swings, not by the sugar/chemicals/power business.**

### 12b. Structural capacity reduction — Lakshmipuram unit closure (Note 43, p.106)
- Sugarcane crushing operations at Lakshmipuram have been **suspended since the 2019-20 season** "on account of inadequate supply of sugarcane."
- Management states an "extensive study showed that there are no scope for improving the availability of sugarcane to revive the crushing operations" — this is a **permanent closure**, not a temporary cyclical pause.
- Cane cultivation area redirected to the Vuyyuru unit; Lakshmipuram employee dues settled (tying to the large gratuity payout in §9); **movable fixed assets of the closed unit are being sold** — reclassified to "Assets held for sale" of ₹78,579,550 (Note 15, p.99: Sugar plant ₹54,299,400 + Cogen plant ₹24,280,150), a new balance-sheet line item this year (PY: Nil).
- **No impairment testing disclosed** for these held-for-sale assets (Ind AS 105 requires measurement at lower of carrying amount and fair value less cost to sell) — NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT. 🟡 Watch — potential disclosure gap.
- Group-wide production data confirms the magnitude: aggregate cane crushed fell from 705,446 MT (FY19-20 season) to **411,324 MT (FY20-21 season), a 41.7% decline** (Financial & Operational Highlights table, p.6); at the Company (Vuyyuru-only, post-Lakshmipuram) level, cane crushed for the financial year fell from 784,964 MT to 411,324 MT (**-47.6%**), crushing days fell from 133 to 91, and recovery % improved slightly (9.22% → 9.27%) (Note 42, p.106; MD&A, p.31–32).

### 12c. New unrelated-business diversification — Engineering Division (Note 55, p.115)
- The Company purchased **5.00 acres of land with an engineering industrial shed, stores and administrative building at SIDCO Industrial Estate, Thuvakudi, Trichy**, via e-auction conducted by an Official Liquidator appointed by the NCLT (i.e., an insolvency-process asset sale), at a cost of **₹10,32,67,500 (₹10.33 crore)**, plus registration and stamp duty of **₹1,36,57,600 (₹1.37 crore)** — a **combined outlay exceeding ₹11.7 crore** (≈4.3% of standalone net worth).
- Stated intent: to "setup an Engineering Division... for manufacturing of engineering goods such as equipment, general fabrication of mild and stainless steel structures and machining activities" (also referenced in MD&A Item 3(iv), p.32).
- The Company has "also purchased some equipment from the official liquidator and sourcing of further equipment's for the unit" — no capex budget, timeline, or expected-return figures are disclosed anywhere in the AR.
- 🔴 Red Flag / management question — this is a material capital-allocation decision to diversify into an entirely unrelated business (industrial engineering/fabrication), acquired via a distressed/insolvency auction, occurring in the same year that: (i) an existing sugar unit was permanently closed, (ii) the core sugar segment turned loss-making, and (iii) the Company is carrying an outsized proprietary equity-investment book (§6). No strategic rationale, synergy case, or return expectation is disclosed for the Engineering Division investment.
- Note: this new division is distinct from the existing, unrelated, wholly-owned subsidiary **The Eimco-K.C.P. Ltd**, which already manufactures liquid-solid separation equipment and is profitable (see §12e).
