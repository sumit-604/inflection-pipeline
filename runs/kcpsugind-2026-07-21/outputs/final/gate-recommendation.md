## FLAG-CASH (active)

Determination: STRUCTURAL, with no growth catalyst. The FY26 cash reversal traces to core operating deterioration, not to investment in growth.

Why not GROWTH-INDUCED: capex fell 36.1% year over year and now runs at roughly 0.67x depreciation, and CWIP is about 0.1% of revenue (B02 finding 10, Note 76 p.128; B07 capex_embedded_growth_pct 0). Cash is not leaving to build capacity. The Urad Dal growth line received zero capex (B02 finding 10, Note 35 p.112).

Why STRUCTURAL: the core sugar segment result has widened its loss three years running, positive Rs 6.51 Cr FY24 to negative Rs 8.21 Cr FY25 to negative Rs 17.31 Cr FY26 (B03 red_flags_top3; FY26 Audited Results segment table p.4). Consolidated operating cash flow swung from positive Rs 47.79 Cr FY25 to negative Rs 30.89 Cr FY26 (B01 block_b_trend, screener-data). The standalone entity recorded its first actual cash loss of Rs 11.36 Cr in FY26, confirmed under CARO Annexure A clause (xvii) (B03 flags; FY26 Audited Results pp.7, 20), breaking the "no cash loss" comfort the FY25 AR relied on. Standalone operating cash flow itself was about negative Rs 23.10 Cr in FY26 versus positive Rs 38.24 Cr prior (B12b MINOR, FY26R p.7). Debt Service Coverage Ratio collapsed to 0.25x from 1.52x, down 84% (B02 finding 4, Note 73 p.126). Receivable collection velocity deteriorated 29%, turnover 25.66x to 18.14x, DSO lengthening from about 14.2 to 20.1 days, even as the absolute book fell 12.8% (B02 receivables_trend, Note 10 p.106-107, Note 73 p.126).

The cushion, cited for balance: the company is net debt negative, and the drain is being absorbed by a large financial portfolio, about 46% of total assets in FVTPL quoted equity and mutual funds (B02 finding 8, Note 5/9 p.104-106). CARE reaffirmed CARE A minus; Stable on 07 October 2025. Rating agency verbatim, on liquidity: "Liquidity remains strong, marked by free cash and investments of Rs 204.08 crore as on March 31, 2025. Average working capital utilisation stood at 37.65% for 12 months ended June 2025, providing headroom for additional working capital requirements" (CARE Rating 2025-10-07, p.3). And on leverage: "The company is net debt negative as on March 31, 2025" (CARE p.2).

Read: the liquidity buffer is genuine and buys time, but it is masking a core that is losing money and consuming cash for structural reasons, not funding growth. There is no identified operating catalyst that reverses the sugar-segment trend on the evidence to hand.

No SEBI action, no litigation, no whistleblower record surfaced.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Sugar segment result. Watch for it to stop widening beyond FY26's negative Rs 17.31 Cr and turn back toward zero; a fourth year more negative confirms the core loss is structural, not cyclical. Find it in the quarterly and annual segment note (Reg 33). This is the central turnaround test.

2. Standalone operating cash flow and the CARO cash-loss clause. Watch for CFO to return positive and for no repeat of the FY26 Rs 11.36 Cr cash loss; a second, larger cash loss signals liquidity stress distinct from the earnings-quality story. Find it in the cash flow statement and CARO Annexure A clause (xvii).

3. Eimco order book converting to revenue. The consolidated Engineering segment fell to Rs 78.64 Cr in FY26 from Rs 96.87 Cr in FY25 despite the order book rising 30.4%; watch for segment revenue resuming year over year growth. Find it in the quarterly consolidated segment note. This tests whether the group's profit engine is still growing.

4. The Rs 257 Cr Hyundai order confirmation. Watch for a Reg 30 exchange filing confirming the Eimco order, then its appearance in Engineering segment revenue recognition. Absent a filing, treat it as unconfirmed. Find it in BSE/NSE Reg 30 filings and the FY27 segment note. It is the single largest named catalyst and is currently unanchored.

5. Distillery utilisation under E20. Watch alcohol production recover toward the FY23-24 level of about 65 lakh litres; continued sub-15 lakh litre output means the ethanol tailwind is passing the company by. Find it in the next AR product-wise performance note.

6. Standalone short-term borrowings. Up 47% year over year in FY26 while the core lost money; watch for continued double-digit growth funding working capital against operating losses. Find it in the standalone balance sheet borrowings note.

7. Related-party deposit share and Schedule V remuneration. Watch whether related-party deposits climb above the current 26.9% of the public deposit book and whether a third family executive enters the minimum-remuneration mechanism, plus shareholder dissent on ratification. Find it in the AR deposit note, Auditor's Report clause (g), and AGM resolutions. This tests governance drift alongside the earnings decline.

8. Investment-portfolio fair value and deferred tax liability. About 46% of assets sit in FVTPL equity and mutual funds; watch for a large adverse mark or a DTL-crystallising disposal, either of which swings reported earnings and the "net debt negative" framing. Find it in the investment and deferred-tax notes.

## Falsification line

The single next-quarter print that would do the most damage: a sugar segment result more negative than FY26's Rs 17.31 Cr loss, confirming a fourth consecutive year of widening core losses and removing any read that FY26 was a cyclical trough.
