Flagged only because an investor reading Note 3 in isolation, as Pass 1 did, could overstate FY25's actual cash capex intensity; the cash flow statement is the more decisive number for FCF-based valuation work in later stages.

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Given consolidated cost of materials consumed actually fell 2.8% YoY, why does the standalone ratio-variance note attribute the net-profit-ratio decline to "increase in raw material costs" rather than the ₹30.38 Cr negative swing in inventory destocking that the notes show is the actual driver?
2. What is the transfer-pricing policy and methodology governing sales/margins between the Indian manufacturing parent and Tatva Chintan USA Inc./Tatva Chintan Europe B.V., given the parent's consolidated profit share fell from 97% to 6% in one year while the subsidiaries (holding under 0.01% of Group non-current assets) captured 81%?
3. What was the business rationale for the 27.9% remuneration increase awarded to all three executive/whole-time directors in a year when consolidated PAT fell 81.2% and standalone ROE fell to 0.05%, and will FY26 compensation be linked more directly to performance?
4. Given zero ECL allowance is recognised on trade receivables in both FY24 and FY25 despite the top-3 customers representing 61% of the FY25 receivables book, what customer-level credit assessment supports "no impairment allowance required," and has this been tested against a formal ECL matrix?
5. What drove the 6.4x increase in the deferred tax asset on unabsorbed losses (₹2.15 Cr to ₹13.88 Cr) — which Group entity generated these tax losses, and what is management's basis for judging recovery as probable given FY25's profit collapse?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management signals:** none of outright fabrication identified; every note figure ties out exactly to the primary statements (validated in Pass 3). The concern is narrative integrity, not numerical integrity — management's stated explanation for the profitability decline (rising raw material costs) contradicts the notes' own cost-of-materials-consumed line, which fell.
- **Aggressive accounting:** none identified. Revenue recognition is conservative and fully reconciled; no revaluation of PPE/ROU/intangibles in either year; no impairment reversals; cash-flow-to-notes reconciliation is clean across all statements.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators:** zero ECL provisioning against a concentrated receivables book (a conservatism gap, not a fabrication); first-year capitalisation of internally generated development costs (small scale, a judgement area to monitor rather than a current concern); profit migration to foreign trading subsidiaries supported only by an unverifiable "arm's length" assertion.
- **Governance:** KMP pay-performance disconnect (27.9% raises against 81% PAT decline); children of two of three executive directors employed in company operating roles.
