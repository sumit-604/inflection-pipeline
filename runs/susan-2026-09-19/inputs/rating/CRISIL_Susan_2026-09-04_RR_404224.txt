 Detailed Rationale 
 Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ ratings to the bank facilities of Susan Electricals India Limited (SEIL). 
 The ratings reflect the extensive industry experience of SEIL’s promoters and healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the competitive nature of electrical wires, cables, and conductors manufacturing industry & exposure to risks re lating to raw material price fluctuation and to tender based nature of business, and high working capital cycle. 
 Analytical Approach 
 Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SEIL. 
 Unsecured loans of Rs. 4.10 crore as on March 31,2026 have been treated as debt owing to the limited track record of their sustenance and their need-based nature. 
 Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 
 Extensive industry experience of the promoters: The promoters have experience of around two decades in electrical equipment and power infrastructure industry. This has given them an understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. The company reported strong revenue growth in fiscal 2026, with operating income increasing by 98% to Rs. 269.5 crore from Rs. 135.9 crore in fiscal 2025. The operating margin improved to 11.96% in fiscal 2026 from 8.92% in fiscal 2025 supported by better fixed cost absorption and expansion in product profile to High-Tension (HT) cables. Revenue visibility remains adequate supported by an order book of approximately Rs. 142 crore and a pipeline of around Rs. 150 crore as of June 2026. Further, the company reported revenue of around Rs. 95 crore in Q1-FY2027 compared with Rs. 38 crore in Q1-FY2026; revenue is expected to grow to over Rs 400 crore in fiscal 2027 supported by a diversified product profile. 
 Healthy financial risk profile: SEIL’s capital structure was moderate with gearing of 2.3 times and total out side liabilities to adj tangible networth (TOL/ANW) of 2.9 times for year ending on 31st March 2026. However, there has been equity infusion of Rs 60.2 crore and Rs 8.2 crore, respectively, with the Initial Public Offering (IPO) and pre-IPO in Q1-FY2027. Accordingly, gearing and TOL/ANW ratio are expected to moderate to sub-1 time as on March 31, 2027. Networth is projected to improve to around Rs 130 crore for the fiscal ending March 31, 2027 from Rs 38.5 crore as on March 31, 2026. SEIL’s debt protection metrics have also been at comfortable level despite leverage due to moderately healthy profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt (NCAAD) ratio were 4.9 times and 0.2 time for fiscal 2026 and expected to improve to around 6 times and around 0.5 time in fiscal 2027. 
 Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 
 Competitive nature of electrical wires, cables, and conductors manufacturing industry and exposure to risks relating to raw material price fluctuation and to tender based nature of business: The cables & wires industry is intensely competitive with presence of large players.   The intense competition in the market has led to rapidly evolving products and innovations and thus high technology obsolescence risks. Also, the revenue and profitability entirely depend on the ability to win tenders. Thus, the players remain exposed to intense competition in the wire and cable industry, necessitating aggressive bidding for orders and thereby constraining operating margins. This affects bargain ing power of the players in the industry and operating margins fluctuate based on the tenders and orders received by the players in the industry. Also, the prices of the key raw materials such as preform, aluminum rods, wires, cables, copper etc. are volatile in nature and comprises of 80-85% of its revenue and thus affect the margins. 
 High working capital cycle : The working capital requirement remains high as reflected in the gross current assets (GCA) of 147 days as on March 31, 2026, with receivables a nd inventory of 68 days and 78 days, respectively. It is required to extend long credit period in line with the industry standards as the customers are small and medium-sized players who require credit. Furthermore, to meet its business requirement, it holds large work in process & inventory. As a result, inventory days and receivable days remained in the range of 70-85 and 60-70 respectively over the two fiscals ended March 2026. These are supported by enhancement in working capital limits and creditors su pport (outstanding at 31 days as on March 31, 2026) and internal accruals. 
 Liquidity Adequate 
 Bank limit utilisation is high at around 90.3 percent for the past twelve months ended Jun-26. Net cash accruals are expected to be Rs. 25-32 crore annually which will remain more than sufficient against yearly term debt repayment obligations of Rs 1.0-1.5 crore over the medium term. In addition, it will act as cushion to the liquidity of the company. Current ratio is moderate at 1.2 times on March 31, 2026. The p romoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans to meet its working capital requirements and repayment obligations. Cash and bank balance was around Rs. 6.56 crore as on March 31, 2026. 
 Outlook Stable 
 Crisil Ratings believes that SEIL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter and established relationships with clients.

 Improvement in working capital cycle 
 Downward factor 
 Decline in revenue and/or operating margin falling below 7%, leading to weakening of net cash accruals 
 Large debt-funded capital expenditure or a stretch in the working capital cycle thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile. 
 About the Company 
 SEIL (previously known as Suvish Insulation Private Limited) was incorporated as private limited company in December 2007 and renamed to Susan Electricals India Private Limited in 2010 . Subsequently, it was reconstituted as public limited company in December 2025. SEIL is engaged in manufacturing of aluminium and copper-based electrical winding wires, conductors and cables (High-Tension and Low-Tension) in various specifications, sizes and configurations. 
 Its listed on Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Limited – SME platform. SEIL is promoted & managed by Mr. Vishal Jain and Ms. Mahak Jain.
