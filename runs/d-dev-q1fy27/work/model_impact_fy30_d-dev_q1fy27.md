## The two model levers this quarter moved
1. **Margin timing walk-back.** The >19% EBITDA margin carried as an FY27 event is now guided
   "19-20% by FY30" (deck p29), with no FY27 margin figure shown; Q1 actual 16.89%. Removes ~3 years
   of margin-led earnings upgrade from the FY27-FY29 window (the hurdle years).
2. **Revenue base off-path.** Q1 annualises to Rs.1,178 Cr vs the deck's own FY27E Rs.1,500 Cr
   (~21% light); the compounding base for FY28-30 likely starts lower than plan.
Third, smaller lever: dilution. 7.52 Cr shares base; contingent upside dilution from the Sec 62(3)
convertible and the authorised-capital hike.

## NET READ
This quarter shifts the earnings curve RIGHT by roughly a year, with the margin walk-back as the mechanism.
Management's FY30 destination (Rs.2,500 Cr / Rs.225-250 Cr PAT) is unchanged (reaffirmed), but its
credibility and timing weakened: margin must climb 16.9% -> 19-20% while revenue nearly doubles, all
back-end. Model adjustment to feed the next refresh:
- Top line FY30: hold ~Rs.2,300-2,500 Cr, bias to the lower half until Q2 confirms the ramp.
- Bottom line FY30: ~Rs.200-240 Cr PAT / EPS ~Rs.27-32 undiluted, haircut toward the low end for the
  off-path start, delayed margin, and dilution.
- FY27-FY29 (hurdle years): LOWER than a >19%-from-FY27 model; this is where the walk-back bites and
  the base case gets trimmed.
- Fair value / destination PE: UNCHANGED this quarter (Rs.473 / Rs.512); the re-rate belongs in the next
  Role 1 refresh, which should (a) lower the FY27-29 margin path, (b) start revenue off the lower
  run-rate, (c) confidence-weight the FY30 target down.

## CONFIRM / DENY

- Confirms the haircut: a second sub-20% Heavy-Fab quarter (fires trigger 4) and margin still ~17%.
