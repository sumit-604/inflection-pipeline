## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

### 1A. One-line description
Indegene is an asset-light global services company that runs the "commercialization and
medical operations" back office and front office for large pharma, biotech and med-device
companies — content, marketing, regulatory writing, medical affairs, safety and pharmacovigilance
— increasingly delivered through its own GenAI platforms rather than pure headcount (AR
Management Discussion & Analysis, p.62-63; "Indegene is not a life sciences IT company, a
horizontal outsourcer, a specialty agency, or a contract research organization" — AR p.62).

### 1E. The chai-stall-uncle version
Think of a pharma company like a shopkeeper who has invented a brilliant new sweet (a drug)
but has no idea how to get the word out to every doctor in the country, fill out all the
government paperwork correctly, or track complaints if someone reacts badly to the sweet.
Indegene is the professional agency the shopkeeper hires to do all of that — design the
posters and pitch (commercial), write the government forms (regulatory/medical), and log
every complaint properly (safety/pharmacovigilance) — for hundreds of such shopkeepers at
once, using its own AI tools so the same team can serve more shopkeepers each year without
proportionately more staff. Indegene doesn't make or test the sweet itself; it doesn't run the
factory (no manufacturing, no CDMO). It earns its money by being paid for the work delivered
(campaigns run, documents filed, cases processed), and increasingly by results, not just hours
billed (Inv. Pres. slide 9, "~60% revenue outcome-aligned").

### 2D. Industry lifecycle stage
Secular growth / mid-cycle expansion. AR frames a "$2 trillion" global pharma revenue
opportunity by 2030 and $500bn+ of annual industry R&D/commercialization spend (AR MD&A

marketing & sales, regulatory & medical affairs, pharmacovigilance, drug discovery/clinical
trials and manufacturing/supply chain, with outsourcing operations spend growing at an

year (AR MD&A p.62), with active clients up from 70 to 105 YoY as of Q1 FY27 (Inv. Pres. fact
sheet).
