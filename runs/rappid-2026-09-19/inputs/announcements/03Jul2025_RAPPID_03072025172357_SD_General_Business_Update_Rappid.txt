A. Key Financial & Operational Highlights 
 
Revenue Growth: 
 
Rappid Valves India Limited reported Q1 FY25-26 sales of ₹11.63 Crores, reflecting operational 
resilience and sustained momentum, with strong performance across both marine (defence) and 
non-marine segments and anticipates further growth acceleration in the forthcoming quarters. 
 
Order Book & Contracts:

1. Current Order Book:  
The Company has received a new order valued at ₹4.45 Crores from Deepak Fertilisers 
and Petrochemicals Corporation Limited. This addition further strengthens our order 
pipeline and underlines our growing credibility across industrial sectors. 
 
As of June 30, 2025, the total outstanding order book stands at ₹24.64 Crores, bifurcated 
as follows: 
 
• Marine (Defence): ₹11.12 Crores 
• Non-Marine: ₹13.52 Crores

industrial sectors. 
 
3. Pipeline: Awaiting results of commercial bids for large orders; technical qualification 
achieved for multiple projects. 
 
Operational Expansion: 
 
The Company has commissioned a new manufacturing unit, significantly enhancing overall 
production capabilities. This plant is tailored to reduce turnaround time and serve niche, export-
driven demands efficiently. 
 
Strategic Initiatives: Launch of ‘Rappid Valves 2.0’ 
 
1. Backward Integration: Commissioning of ‘Rappid Valves 2.0’, an advanced 
manufacturing facility with world-class infrastructure and cutting-edge machinery, aimed 
at strengthening in-house capabilities and reducing reliance on external processes. 
 
2. Market Diversification: Successful development and qualification of a prototype 
industrial valve for a prestigious U.S. based clients, with potential high-volume export

B. Future Outlook 
 
1. Development of Next-Generation Industrial Valve Applications: 
 
The Company aims to advance its product portfolio by focusing on the design and
