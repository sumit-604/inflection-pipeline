                    Q1 FY27: Strong Execution, Building Momentum for the Next Phase of Growth


                    “This quarter marked a strong start to the year, reflecting improving business momentum, increasing scale and operating leverage. As we move forward, we
                    remain focused on converting the opportunities built over the past few years into sustainable and profitable growth business”


                    Q1FY27 Performance Highlights
                    • The company delivered its highest-ever quarterly performance, delivering total income of ~Rs. 142 Crs, surpassing all our historical milestones

                    • EBITDA stood at ~Rs. 42 Crs, with EBITDA margins at ~30%, supported by favourable product mix, higher margin transfer case products, export contribution and

                    Segmental Highlights

                    1. Transfer Case : Strong Growth Led by Indonesia Program
                    o Transfer Case remained the primary growth driver, supported by the ramp-up of the Indonesia 4x4 program
                    o Strong product mix and execution further supported healthy profitability

                    o Exports remained strong at ~Rs. 23 crore in Q1FY27
                    o Components continued to witness healthy demand and increasing scale, supported by precision-machined products and exports
                    o Growing customer engagement is creating opportunities across Transfer Cases, Components and drivetrain systems, with increasing potential for recurring global

                    o EV revenues remained subdued due to timing delays in platform adoption, parts co-location and customer testing
                    o Customer programmes continue to progress, supported by Divgi-TTS’ in-house EV transmission design, development and manufacturing capabilities

Highlights from Management
FY27 & Beyond

1. Transfer Case – Sustaining Growth & Expanding Global Footprint

o   Continued momentum expected from the Indonesia programme, alongside efforts to enter into newer export geographies
o   Incremental Indonesia volumes are expected to continue for the upcoming quarters of FY27
o   H2 FY27 expected to witness a ramp-up in the domestic end market volumes with launch of new and facelift programs by key OEM customers
o   Focus remains on next-generation products, technology enhancement and global expansion

2. Exports – Building a Recurring Global Business

o Exports expected to remain a key growth engine, with FY27 component exports targeted at ~Rs. 80 crore
o Focus on converting export opportunities into recurring global programmes and moving towards higher-value drivetrain solutions
o North America expansion expected to strengthen engagement with global customers

3. EV Transmission – Moving Towards Commercialisation

o Sigma production expected to commence in Q2FY27, following customer approval
o Ongoing localisation and validation programmes are expected to support the transition towards commercial-scale EV transmission business
o Continued focus on EV, hybrid and emerging transmission technologies continue

4. Components & New Transmission Opportunities

o Components expected to scale further through export programmes and new customer opportunities
o Automatic Transmission progressing in the Proof-of-Concept stage, while Manual Transmission opportunities are being evaluated
o Partnership with Toyota Susho and new AWD and PTU programmes provide additional avenues for future growth and product diversification
