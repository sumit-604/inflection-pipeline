## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts)

### RF1 — FY28 revenue target creep with a softening hedge
- Q3 FY26 (Parag, opening, p4): "scale of our revenue in FY '28 to approximately INR1,500 crores
  at around 40% utilization of available space." Hedged hard under Manish Gupta (p6): "We are not
  saying that we will achieve, but there is a potential."
- Q4 FY26 (Parag, Harshit, p5-6): "peak potential of both the plants combined is around INR1,450
  crores... as per the existing capacity." Dheeraj (p3-4): reaffirmed 1,500 driveable.
- Q1 FY27 (Parag, opening, p3-4): "revised our FY28 revenue target to more than Rs. 1,600 crores,"
  stated flatly, minimal hedge.
- The headline number rises 1,500 → (1,450 reframed as ceiling) → >1,600 while the hedge thins. On
  a ~INR830 cr FY26 base this is the single largest number in the thesis.

### RF2 — Margin step-up 17.4% → 24.2% durability, one quarter, peer-divergent
- Q1 FY27 (Parag, opening, p2): "increase our EBITDA margin from 17% to 24%," "confident to
  sustain." CFO (p5): 24.2%, "endeavor to maintain." FY26 full year was 17.4% (Q4, p3); the
  immediately prior quarter Q4 FY26 was 18.1% (p3). The scripted comparator "17%" is the full-year,
  not the sequential 18.1%, which makes the jump read larger.
- Rests on ONE quarter of print. B06 shows every commodity/converter peer (NOCIL 6.4-11.2%,
  CAMLINFINE 2-14%) COMPRESSING margins on the same war-driven petrochemical inflation in the
  identical window.

### RF3 — Plant-level utilisation (Pakhajan vs Vapi) evaded across 2+ quarters
- Q4 FY26 (Laveena Jagwani, p17): "What will be the individual capacity utilization for each
  plant?" → "I'd like to stick to the Yasho level capacity."
- Q1 FY27 (Amar Maurya, p10-11, twice): pushed on Pakhajan → "I am not talking about the single
  unit capacity utilization"; then "You can make assumptions. I will not make statements."
- This is the single load-bearing operating-leverage variable behind RF2. The refusal masks whether
  Pakhajan (the growth asset, below 50% since Q2 FY26 per Q3 p8) is actually filling, or whether
  the blended 60-65% is propped by a near-full small plant.

### RF4 — "We have shut Vapi" contradicts "Vapi is 95-98%" and is never reconciled  [B05 MISSED]
- Q1 FY27 (Parag, Shivam Vashi, p15): "We have decided to scale up our facility at Pakhajan today.
  We have shut Vapi also. And we have diverted all the efforts to the Pakhajan facility."
- Same call (Amar Maurya, p11): "Because our Vapi is more or less 95%-98%." → Parag: "You can make
  assumptions. I will not make statements" (neither confirms nor corrects).
- Q4 FY26 (Parag, Anupam, p12): FY26 capex included "INR7 crores, INR8 crores into the Vapi... for
  some adjustment into the new product" — Vapi was still an operating spend one quarter earlier.
- So within one call Vapi is described as both "shut" and (uncorrected) "95-98%." Whether "shut"
  means the plant or only Vapi R&D is genuinely ambiguous. Either reading matters: if Vapi is
  shut/tiny, the entire utilisation-and-margin ramp is a Pakhajan-only story, and the "Yasho-level"
  utilisation figures blend two very different assets. B05 never surfaces the "shut Vapi" statement
  at all.

### RF5 — MNC LTSA timeline slip, Q4 FY27 → Q1 FY28
- Q2 FY26 (Parag, opening, p4): "plant becoming operational by Q4 FY '27," ~INR150 cr annual
  revenue from FY27-end, capex INR50-75 cr.
- Q3 FY26 (Parag, opening, p4): cost firmed INR85-90 cr, advance INR19.9 cr, "commercialization
  targeted for Q1 FY '28." Never labelled as a change.
- Q4 FY26 advance INR51.4 cr (p3), capex ~INR90 cr (Rupesh, p7), MNC share of the INR125 cr FY27
  plan = "Zero" (Anupam, p12). Q1 FY27: no new advance figure, "on track" (p4).

### RF6 — Stated asset turn cut 4:1 → 2.5x while the target it feeds rose  [under-weighted]
- Q3 FY26 (Parag, opening, p4): "we expect a 4:1 revenue to capex ratio for incremental
  investments."
- Q1 FY27 (Pujan Shah, p8-9; Subhanu, p17): "2.5x" on the INR250 cr capex.
- The single load-bearing capex-to-revenue assumption behind the FY28 build fell ~37% even as the
  FY28 target itself rose from 1,500 to >1,600. B06 computes ~1.0-1.1x from the one comparable peer
  (NOCIL Dahej), CONTRADICTING even the 2.5x.

### RF7 — Celebrated WC beat (190 → 143 days) partly involuntary  [B05 MISSED]
- Q1 FY27 (Parag, Aman Thadani, p17): "the inventory base came down, probably we did not have
  sufficient raw material coming in. We are facing a genuine supply issue on our raw material
  side... our team is struggling a lot."
- WC cycle 190 → 143 days (CFO, p5) is presented by B05 as "delivered early and exceeded" and a
  clean quality signal. Management's own words say the inventory drop was partly a forced RM
  shortage, not pure discipline. That inverts a headline positive into a mixed one.

### RF8 — India-EU FTA timing described inconsistently
- Q3 FY26 (Parag, opening, p3): "the evolving India-EU trade environment has supported improved
  engagement" — implies already helping.
- Q4 FY26 (Parag, Yash Mehta, p14): FTA "will come in place only by early January, hopefully."
- B06 corroborates the later framing: NOCIL (Feb 2026) puts the FTA in "calendar year '27 roughly."

### RF9 — Debt-intent reversal / +INR100 cr borrowing amid a deleveraging narrative
- Q2 FY26 (Parag, Hardik, p12): "no intention, no plan to reduce the absolute debt."
- Q3 FY26 (Parag, Pujan, p8): "I'm not in a favor of reducing the overall debt... it won't increase
  from here."
- Q1 FY27 (CFO, p5): "expects to raise approximately Rs. 100 crores through borrowings during
  FY27" to fund the doubled capex. Absolute debt rising, not held flat; not framed as a change.

### RF10 — FY27 volume-growth guidance wobbled inside one call (Q4 FY26)
- 40-50% (Pujan, p11) → "30% to 40%" then "40%, 45%" (Anupam, p12) → settled "35% to 45%" twice
  (Karan Gupta, p14). Delivered +42% in Q1 FY27 (p3), inside the settled band.

### RF11 — New-molecule revenue threshold inconsistent across calls
- INR50-100 cr (Q2 FY26 p9; Q1 FY27 Pujan p7) vs INR25 cr "at least" (Q3 FY26 Manish, p5) vs INR50
  cr (Q4 FY26 Dheeraj, p10-11). The R&D screen keeps moving with no delivered molecule.

### RF12 — Capex plan doubled 125 → 250 within one quarter
- Q4 FY26 (Parag, opening, p2; Anupam p12): FY27 capex INR125 cr, "internal accruals."
- Q1 FY27 (Parag, opening, p4): "enhance our planned capital expenditure for FY27 from Rs. 125
  crores to Rs. 250 crores," now needing ~INR100 cr new debt. Payoff pushed out: "Real growth
  coming from FY29" (Amar, p17); the two buildings are excluded from the >1,600 FY28 target.

### RF13 — Margin-sustainability confidence softens from script to Q&A
- Scripted (p2, p5): "confident to sustain," "endeavor to maintain."
- Q&A (Rohit Sinha, p13-14): "one can't promise. Tomorrow how those things will play out... I don't
  want to say that... I would like to maintain this margin rather than to grow this margin." The
  volunteered candour is real, but the opening script over-states the certainty.

Lesser items noted, not scored as red-flag-grade: (a) Q3 FY26 Parag "No, we have not seen that so
far" on aggressive Chinese capacity/dumping (Pujan, p7), contradicted by his own later "strong,
very strong... they come hard" (Q4 p13) and by B06 peer evidence of active Chinese builds through
FY27 — substance is covered by B06; (b) the once-stated INR150 cr MNC annual-revenue figure
(Q2 FY26 p4) never reconfirmed, later only "revenue to start FY28."
