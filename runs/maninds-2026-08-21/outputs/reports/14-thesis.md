## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy) — inside the entry zone only

**3A. Quality Evidence.** Consolidated ROCE FY26 is 13.8%, through-cycle 13.95% (Am17.1). The corrected base ROCE walk (bargain-gain CE correction applied) climbs to 16.5% by FY29. Balance sheet is net cash on a screener basis, CRISIL A+/Stable (upgrade 01-Jun-2026), liquidity Strong (NCA over Rs450cr vs term repayment Rs230-250cr, cash Rs657cr). But cash conversion is weak: cumulative working-capital days rose 116 (FY24) to 261 (FY26). Pillar 2 is 0.80x, growth-phase drag with no offset on a structural WC-heavy baseline. Quality is AVERAGE, not high; the thesis is a transition, not a compounder-at-any-price.

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction).**
1. NPC Saudi ramp (conviction H). NPC enters FY27 at Rs1,500cr (ceiling, not base), reaching Rs2,800cr by FY29 (operator base). Confirm: NPC quarterly run-rate Rs300-500cr from Q2FY27, margin near 15-18%. Kill: utilisation stalls or the FY27 NPC guide (already stated two ways, Rs1,500cr vs Rs1,200cr, within one call) is cut further.
2. EBITDA margin sustain 14-16% (conviction H). Four straight quarters of margin over-delivery. Kill: margin below 12%; watch the unexplained Q1FY27 gross-margin QoQ drop as the early tell.
3. FY27 consolidated revenue ~Rs5,000cr (conviction M). Kill: further cut, or India standalone below Rs2,000cr.
4. Jammu SS plant and Dammam coating, March 2027 (conviction M). Kill: any further slip past March 2027 (Jammu already ~11 months late).
5. India standalone order-book recovery (conviction M-L). Currently Rs2,200-2,300cr, falling four quarters. This is the named BUY condition and the primary tripwire.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** EM STRENGTHENING (32), built on a completed, exchange-filed acquisition (NPC, Aramco vendor since 2005), not narrative. Active moats: qualification lock-in (Strong), industry-consolidation beneficiary (Strong), rare manufacturing capability (Moderate). Falls short of EXPANSION because F2 execution scores Weak (FY26 guidance missed ~half; Jammu slipped over 12 months) and G2 working capital is actively deteriorating. The Saudi first-mover position is NOT exclusive: Welspun and Jindal SAW are building comparable in-Kingdom capacity on the same FY27-29 timeline. NPC scarcity was struck in deliberation (S3); in-kingdom LSAW is not scarce. Moat is heading up but from a low base, with a real oversupply risk on top.

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter CONCERN. SEBI order 29-Sep-2025 found deliberate misstatement of FY15-21 financials, non-consolidation of Merino Shelters, unapproved RPTs and fund diversion; a 2-year trading ban on Chairman, MD and a former ED/CFO, stayed by SAT 10-Oct-2025 on a 50% penalty deposit. NFRA banned the FY16-17 audit engagement partner for 5 years on the same matter. A Rs97.68cr loan from promoter-controlled Limitless Contracting Pvt Ltd carries no disclosed rate or tenor (AR FY25 Note 36). MCA Section 206(5) notice pending. A live Supreme Court dispute runs between two Mansukhani branches over share title and dividend. Offset: named institutional entry (Kacholia, Carnelian, Ovata) via the Rs255cr preferential raise, and a professional external CFO (ex-Welspun) hired within 10 days of the predecessor's exit. Credibility grade C: margin delivery is a genuine four-quarter strength, but revenue guidance and Merino quantum change between and within calls, and the SEBI stay went unmentioned for four consecutive calls.

**3E. Market Opportunity.** TAM runway MASSIVE (revenue headroom 56.5x SAM share). SOM 5-year Rs13,740cr implies revenue CAGR ~31%. But SOM exceeds committed capacity (NPC + Dammam + Jammu, ~Rs10,200-11,500cr at 80-90% utilisation) by ~Rs2,900cr. Capacity and execution, not market size, bind the 3-5 year thesis. Management's own "Regional Opportunity" slide mixes stock and flow metrics; the literal sum reads inflated (2.63x conservative TAM).

**3F. Peer Positioning.** MANINDS is the smallest by market cap and thinnest by margin of the four listed peers (vs Jindal SAW, Ratnamani, Welspun Corp). The strongest peer finding is adverse: all three peers report stable-to-record order books through FY26 (Welspun Rs25,750cr, Jindal 1.78mn tons stable, Ratnamani Rs2,160cr up) while MANINDS's book fell 37% over three quarters. That makes the decline look company-specific, not sector-wide. Welspun disclosed it scouted and passed on a Saudi asset (near-certainly NPC) citing quality and reputation, not valuation, which complicates the "1.5x EV/EBITDA cheap vs 7-10x peer" framing central to the NPC bull case.

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Man Industries makes big steel pipes for oil, gas and water lines. It just bought a Saudi maker, NPC. That deal is the engine now. On the numbers, fair value grows about 21% a year to a Year-3 base of Rs611. That is real business compounding, not a one-off.

The problem is the price. At Rs714 the stock trades 17.7 times forward earnings. A pipe converter of this quality earns 8 to 12 times. So the price already pays for a re-rating the business has not earned. From Rs714 the return is negative. The fair value compounds; the price does not.

Two hard facts cap the call. The India order book has fallen four quarters straight to Rs2,200-2,300cr, below the Rs2,500cr line, while peers post record books. And SEBI found the promoters misstated years of accounts; the ban is stayed, not cleared. So the verdict is AVOID at Rs714. The name becomes a Small buy only in the Rs219-313 zone, only with the India book back above Rs2,500cr on a filed print, and only if no tripwire has fired. Watch the 14-November half-year print first.
