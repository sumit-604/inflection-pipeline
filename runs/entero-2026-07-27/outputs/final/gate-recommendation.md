## FLAG-CASH (inside the verdict)

Determination: INDETERMINATE (B01, B02).

Free cash flow is not computable: no provided source carries a per-year consolidated capex line, and standalone capex sits on a different consolidation basis than the consolidated operating cash flow, so it cannot be substituted (B01). FY26 operating cash flow turned positive at Rs 96.2 Cr after six consecutive negative years; cumulative operating cash flow is negative Rs 203 Cr against cumulative profit positive Rs 193 Cr across FY20 to FY26 (B01). Against that inflection, gross trade receivables grew 50.6% year on year versus revenue growth of 29.4%, 1.7 times faster; the expected loss allowance grew 166%, but about 85% of that increase (Rs 469.87M of Rs 554.51M) came in through acquisitions rather than the organic book (B02, Note 15 p.200-201). A genuine operating cash turn sits alongside an acquisition muddied receivables signal, with FCF uncomputable, so the conversion reads INDETERMINATE rather than GROWTH-INDUCED or STRUCTURAL.

Rating agency commentary: India Ratings (IND A-/Stable, 03-Dec-2025) states negative free cash flow since inception with management guiding free cash flow positive from end-FY26 (B00; ratings.pdf is image only, so the exact verbatim line and page are a Halt 1 source-confirm item).

Missing evidence to resolve:
1. A consolidated cash flow statement with a per-year capex line, so free cash flow can be computed.
2. The receivables ageing schedule management refused to disclose on the Q3 FY26 call (B05), plus the quarterly debt and operating cash flow detail refused on the Q1 FY27 call (B12b).

Cap effect: INDETERMINATE conversion caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS.

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated)

1. Trade receivables versus revenue growth. Watch the ratio fall back toward 1.0x from 1.7x in the Q2 FY27 receivables note. Tests whether the cash flag is temporary growth strain or a worsening book.
2. Network reach metrics (retail customers, hospitals, SKUs, districts). Watch the next concall for a management reconciliation of the 28 to 36% June 2026 drop. Tests whether the core network moat is intact or eroding.
3. Operating cash flow to EBITDA conversion. Watch H2 FY27 audited operating cash flow reach the guided 50%. Tests whether the FY26 cash turn holds.
4. MedTech run rate. Watch quarterly results for progress to the Rs 1,000 Cr FY27 guide and for the margin mix showing up in consolidated EBITDA. Tests the single biggest forward claim.
5. EBITDA margin. Watch it hold at or above 5.0% through FY27 rather than slip back to 4.0%. Tests margin mix durability.
6. CARO qualified and cash loss subsidiary count, now 40 of about 65. Watch the next annual report CARO annexure for the count shrinking. A static or growing count flips the transition read from artifact to structural.
7. Goodwill impairment and put option cash calls. Watch the next goodwill note for any impairment charge and the current ratio staying above 1.3x, given about Rs 351 Cr of near term put option and deferred payments. Tests liquidity under the roll-up.
8. CEO and COO remuneration reconciliation. Watch the next annual report bridge the Rs 47.27M versus Rs 94.50M gap. Tests whether the pay optic has a benign explanation.

## Falsification line

Q2 FY27 trade receivables again growing about 1.7 times faster than revenue, with operating cash flow conversion missing the guided 50%. That print would show the FY26 cash turn was an acquisition timing artifact, not a structural inflection, and would push the cash flag from INDETERMINATE toward STRUCTURAL.
