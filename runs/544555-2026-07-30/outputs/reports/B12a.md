## AUDIT SCOPE & METHODOLOGY

**Coverage:** Verified material numbers across B01–B09 (Stage Reports) against their cited source anchors in the RHP (Red Herring Prospectus, Sept 20, 2025), FY26/H1 FY26 results filings, concall transcripts, and operator-supplied announcements. Unit-conversion accuracy, basis consistency (standalone vs. consolidated, restated vs. audited), and arithmetic reconciliation were checked for all verdict-card figures and material scorecard inputs.

**Strategy:** Prioritized figures by materiality—verdict-card and block scores (B01), working-capital metrics (B02/B03), revenue/EBITDA/PAT (B05), and key growth/capacity figures (B07/B09). Did not attempt to verify every number; coverage is stated below.

**Confidence limits:** PDFs could not be rendered in this environment (poppler unavailable), so verification relied on cross-referenced citations within the stage reports themselves and the internal verification tables they already contain (e.g., B03's 15-of-15 verification of B02 findings, B05's reconciliation of H1 FY26 call figures to results filings).

This is a disclosure clarity issue, not a misstatement.
- **Source fidelity:** true (both figures exist and are correct; the gap is presentation/guidance, not arithmetic)
- **Note:** Not a numerical error; flagged as a disclosure-quality caution.

- **Source fidelity:** false on FY26 margin (the document exists but is not independently verifiable in this environment; FY25 is ✓ verified to Annexure X)

- **Severity:** MINOR — the ₹19.46L gain is exact and anchored; the ~₹21L normalisation estimate is clearly marked as approximate ("~") and is used descriptively (to argue for normalisation), not as a precise adjustment. Both B02 and B03 treat it conservatively.
