### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic

- Consolidated net Property, Plant & Equipment: Rs 7,145.72 Lakh FY26 vs Rs 5,899.81 Lakh FY25 (AR consolidated Note 11a, line 4527).
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 124.5 Cr FY26 (per B02 memory/screener), Rs 70.2 Cr FY25.
- Implied fixed-asset turnover (revenue / average or year-end net PPE), FY26: 124.5 Cr / Rs 71.46 Cr net PPE = approx 1.74x [I, arithmetic from filed figures].
- FY27 capex plan Rs 63 Cr x 1.74x historical turnover = approx Rs 110 Cr of theoretical incremental revenue capacity, or approx 88% over the FY26 consolidated revenue base.
- **This is a mechanical ceiling, not a forecast, and management's own FY27 guide (35-40% revenue growth, per B05) sits far below it.** The gap is the tell: new labs take one to three years to ramp (H1 FY26 MD&A discloses the 9 labs commissioned in the prior 18 months lost Rs 3.3 Cr at EBITDA in H1 FY26 alone, per B03/B04 cross-reference), so a naive turnover multiplier overstates near-term embedded growth. Treat the 88% figure as the theoretical asset base being built, not a growth rate to expect within FY27.
