# Aimtron Electronics (AIMTRON) — Forward P/E Valuation

Supplementary cross-check, prepared 2026-07-12 at operator request, after the phase-3 finalize. This is a market-multiple lens beside the pipeline's four-pillar valuation (B11). It does NOT override the framework decision: the sole earned exit multiple remains the Section 1B destination PE (8.9x RRM / 12.7x additive), and the pipeline decision remains AVOID on valuation. This document exists to show what the market is paying on forward earnings and what you would have to believe for it to work.

Company: Aimtron Electronics Ltd. Ticker: AIMTRON. CMP: Rs1,390. Market cap: Rs2,864 Cr. Earnings basis: consolidated.

---

## 1. Method

- Hold period 3 years. Buy now (FY27 underway), exit end of FY29.
- At exit the market prices roughly one year forward, so the exit is valued on FY30 earnings.
- Exit price = FY30 EPS x terminal P/E.
- Entry price for the target return = exit price / (1 + hurdle)^3.
- Hurdle 25% (Tier A, strategy core return bar) -> divisor 1.953.
- Margin-of-safety price = entry x 0.80 (20% below).

## 3. Forward EPS build (illustrative, three scenarios)

Revenue compounds off Rs301 Cr; PAT margin as stated per scenario. FY27-FY30 columns are ILLUSTRATIVE, built from management guidance (40-50% revenue CAGR; FY27 revenue Rs450-600 Cr "depending on inorganic contribution") and the fact that a large part of near-term growth is the AIC/ICS acquisition (which runs ~11% EBITDA and dilutes the group).

| Scenario (assumptions) | FY27 | FY28 | FY29 | FY30 EPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull — 38% rev CAGR, 15% PAT margin (guidance met, margin holds) | Rs30 | Rs42 | Rs58 | Rs79 |
| Base — 27% rev CAGR, 13% PAT margin (guidance partly met, some give) | Rs24 | Rs31 | Rs39 | Rs49 |
| Bear — 15% rev CAGR, 10% PAT margin (organic stalls, margin normalizes to peer) | Rs17 | Rs19 | Rs22 | Rs26 |

Probability weights, skewed to base/bear because of the cash, governance, and margin flags: Bull 25% / Base 45% / Bear 30%.

Probability-weighted FY30 EPS = 0.25 x 79 + 0.45 x 49 + 0.30 x 26 = Rs50.

## 5. Terminal multiple selection (the judgment call, documented)

Reference points:
- Framework earned destination: 8.9-12.7x (what the four pillars pay for this company).
- Framework sector cap: 25x (the absolute ceiling for a CLEAN manufacturer; Recycling / Manufacturing row, per the FTTCP deliberation override of the manifest's mis-set Pharma/CDMO 38x).
- Listed EMS peers, market: 35-55x forward (theme-driven momentum).

For this specific company the earnings themselves are the problem: cash conversion is INDETERMINATE, governance is CONCERN, and the 24% EBITDA margin failed analyst scrutiny on the H2 FY26 call (Box Build mix rose but margin did not; the IP-ownership claim contradicted itself). A high multiple applied to earnings you cannot trust is how you lose money. Terminal is therefore anchored at 12-15x: a small premium to the 12.7x additive destination to credit the genuinely long, capacity-backed runway and the premium ROCE, and a deep discount to peers to reflect the quality flags. 20x and 25x are shown only to expose how much a buyer at today's price is reaching.

## 6. Valuation output

Probability-weighted FY30 EPS Rs50, exit end-FY29, discounted 3 years at 25%:

| Terminal P/E | Exit price (FY29) | Entry (25%-return price) | MoS price (-20%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12x (fundamentals) | Rs600 | Rs307 | Rs246 |
| 13-15x (recommended anchor) | Rs650-750 | Rs333-384 | Rs266-307 |
| 20x (reaching) | Rs1,000 | Rs512 | Rs410 |
| 25x (sector cap, generous) | Rs1,250 | Rs640 | Rs512 |

Sensitivity — the margin dominates. If the clean, non-related-party margin is really 10-11%, probability-weighted FY30 EPS falls toward Rs40, and the 13-15x entry drops to Rs265-305 with MoS Rs210-245. If the margin is genuinely 15% and durable, the bull EPS of Rs79 at 15x would justify an entry near Rs600. The entire spread is a margin bet.

## 7. Recommended entry and margin of safety

- Fair value today (central case): ~Rs330 (13-15x forward on probability-weighted FY30 earnings).
- Suitable entry (25%-return price): Rs305-385.
- Margin-of-safety buy price: ~Rs250 (band Rs246-307, centred low because the earnings quality is unproven).

On the forward-P/E method: start-to-accumulate around Rs300, and only commit with conviction below ~Rs250.

The forward-P/E number is higher because it credits four years of consolidated growth at a 13-15x market-ish multiple, whereas the four-pillar model applies a punitive RRM multiple (8.9x) to nearer earnings and routes the cash and governance penalties through the required return. The truth sits between them.

All-in read:
- Watch / first-tranche ceiling: ~Rs250 (the forward-P/E MoS, and only if you believe the growth story).
- High-conviction accumulation: Rs130-160 (the framework answer, protected even if the margin turns out to be a related-party mirage).
- Against CMP Rs1,390, fair value is roughly Rs330, so the stock trades at about 4x fair value; downside to fair value is about 76%.

## 9. Non-negotiable conditions before any entry is actionable

None of the entry numbers above are buyable until:
1. The H1 FY27 consolidated operating cash flow (~Nov 2026) turns solidly positive DRIVEN BY third-party collections, not related-party netting. This is the gate on whether the EPS base is even real.
2. The related-party revenue share falls and the Rs120 Cr ceiling gets an external fairness opinion.
3. The margin holds above ~20% as the acquisition consolidates and related-party sales shrink, which is the test of whether the 24% is genuine or manufactured.

Until those clear, treat even Rs250 as conditional, because every EPS in this model rests on a margin that broke under gentle questioning on the last earnings call.

## 10. One-line answer

On forward P/E, fair value is about Rs330, a suitable entry is Rs305-385, and the margin-of-safety buy price is around Rs250, with high-conviction buying only near Rs130-160 and nothing actionable until the November 2026 cash print proves the earnings are real.
