2B. UTILISATION TRAJECTORY (CD ratio as the banking analogue)

Net Credit-to-Deposit ratio: 70.69% (31-Mar-2024) → 74.68% (31-Mar-2025) → 77.99%
(31-Dec-2025) — ICRA rating note, p.2, 24-Mar-2026. Rising CD ratio = the bank is

2C. GROWTH EMBEDDED IN CAPACITY — CAPITAL-HEADROOM ARITHMETIC (banking analogue to
capex × fixed-asset-turnover)

Anchors: CRAR 17.19% and Tier I 14.47% at 31-Dec-2025, with capital funds of ₹1,453cr and
Tier I capital of ₹1,223cr (ICRA rating note, p.1-2, 24-Mar-2026).

- Implied RWA = Capital funds ÷ CRAR% = ₹1,453cr ÷ 17.19% ≈ ₹8,453cr (🔍 analyst
  derivation from the two anchored figures above).
- RBI Basel III minimum total-capital requirement including the capital conservation
  buffer is 11.5% — general regulatory norm, not a Dhanlaxmi-specific document figure, used
  here only as the denominator for the headroom calculation, flagged as such.
- Excess capital over the 11.5% floor = (17.19% − 11.5%) × ₹8,453cr ≈ ₹481cr.
- Converted to incremental RWA headroom at the 11.5% floor: ₹481cr ÷ 11.5% ≈ ₹4,183cr.
- Net advances of ₹13,912cr (31-Dec-2025, ICRA p.1) against implied RWA of ₹8,453cr imply a
  blended book-wide risk-weight density of ~60.8% (reflecting the low-RWA gold-loan tilt).
  Applying that density to the RWA headroom: ₹4,183cr ÷ 0.608 ≈ ₹6,880cr of additional
  advances the bank's CURRENT capital base could in principle support before breaching the
  regulatory minimum.
- Against the ₹13,912cr book, that is **≈49% headroom-implied growth capacity**
  (capex_embedded_growth_pct in the YAML uses this figure, clearly labelled as a
  capital-headroom analogue, not a fixed-asset-turnover figure).

Caveat, stated plainly: ICRA itself qualifies this — "its ability to meaningfully improve
its market position will depend on the raising of fresh capital in the future" (ICRA p.1)
because internal accruals remain modest. The 49% figure is a point-in-time capital-adequacy
snapshot, not a funded, disclosed growth plan; profit accretion, dividend policy (none paid,
AR p.1) and further capital raises would all move it. It is evidence quality 🔍 layered on
📄 anchored inputs, not a management-disclosed target.

institution with hard, multi-period evidence of financial-metric recovery — is a TURNAROUND
pattern, not a steady AVERAGE one.

The emerging-moat scan, however, is the honest check on whether this turnaround is being
accompanied by a strengthening COMPETITIVE position (which would upgrade the case toward

item (D2, digital platform at 88% transaction share) is real but unmonetised, and the
niche product wins (Pay Smart 2, gold-loan mix shift) are each single, unscaled, and
undisclosed in rupee terms.

**combined_assessment: TURNAROUND.** The financial trajectory is real and improving on hard
numbers; the competitive-moat evidence supporting that trajectory's durability is thin
(MODEST, at the low end of its band). This is a turnaround in balance-sheet and asset-
quality metrics, not yet a confirmed transition into a moat-widening growth company — the
distinction matters for position-sizing and for what would falsify the thesis (see 6B).
