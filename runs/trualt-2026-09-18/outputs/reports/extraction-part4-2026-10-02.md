### 1.1 The formula text, in full, as printed

Risk Factor 8 (the only place the words "predefined margin" appear in the file):

"These supply agreements have specific formulae for supply of each product, including sugar syrup/juice, B-heavy molasses and C-heavy molasses. Such formulae typically account for the basic fair and remunerative price set by the Government of India or Government of India derived ethanol rate, the quantity of supply as well as a predefined margin. If we cannot reasonably offset increases in the prices of raw materials due to changes in factors affecting pricing with increase in the prices of our products, we will experience lower margins." (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.48)

Comment: The margin is described, not quantified. No rupee amount, no percentage. The word "typically" means the three inputs may not be the full formula.

NSL MSA (Nirani Sugars Limited, dated April 25, 2024, SOW and Addendum April 25, 2024, Amendment May 22, 2024, Novation July 26, 2024):

"Our Company procures: (i) bagasse at a price linked to the basic fair and remunerative price fixed by the Government of India for cane; (ii) molasses at a price linked to the government-derived ethanol rate; and (iii) syrup at a price linked to the basic fair and remunerative price fixed by the Government of India for cane." (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.339)

Comment: Same wording for each of the three MSAs. "Linked to" is the only operator stated. No base, no multiplier, no margin figure.

MRN Bhima MSA (MRN Bhima Sugar and Power Private Limited, dated April 25, 2024): identical three-limb sentence, "Our Company procures: (i) bagasse at a price linked to the basic fair and remunerative price fixed by the Government of India for cane; (ii) molasses at a price linked to the government -derived ethanol rate; and (iii) syrup at a price linked to the basic fair and remunerative price fixed by the Government of India for cane." (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.337)

MRN Canepower MSA (MRN Canepower and Biorefineries Private Limited, dated April 25, 2024): identical three-limb sentence (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.338).

Comment: MRN Bhima and MRN Canepower are each "a wholly owned subsidiary of Nirani Sugars Limited" (p.338, p.339). So all three formulas sit inside one promoter group.

MD&A restatement:

"We have a pricing formula embedded into our supply agreement, that links the price at which we source raw material to the price of sugarcane in the market. Hence, in spite of assured raw material supply at a stipulated pricing formula, volatility in price of sugarcane could impact the price at which we acquire sugar syrup and molasses from our Promoter Group entity." (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.529)

Comment: Same page says the agreements are "at prices linked to market rates". Two readings: FRP-linked (p.339) and market-linked (p.529). The agreement itself would separate them.

Arm's length support: BSR & Co. LLP issued "'Benchmarking Analysis for Inter-company transactions'... on May 6, 2024, which mentioned that upon certain conditions being met, it may be considered that the group of inter-related transactions undertaken by our Company have been conducted at arm's length." (TRUALT-Prospectus-01OCT2025.pdf, p.339-340)

Comment: The benchmark covers Fiscal 2024 only. The "certain conditions" are not listed.
