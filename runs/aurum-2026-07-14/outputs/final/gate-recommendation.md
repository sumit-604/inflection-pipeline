## FLAG-PROMOTER: verdict CONCERN

Clean on the classic fraud, SEBI, pledge and minority treatment axes and net promoter accumulating, but three independent red items on disclosure and control quality place this at CONCERN, materially offset by a credible strategic investor entry and a real operating inflection running in parallel.

**Top findings:**
1. Auditor MSKA & Associates, reappointed 29-Apr-2024 for a second five year term, withdrew consent to act as statutory auditors by email dated 25-Sep-2024, no reason disclosed anywhere in the annual report or any filing found (AR FY24-25 Directors' Report Item 22, p.53). [B08]
2. A ₹50 Cr omnibus authorisation for the company to lend to promoter entity AREDL, ₹10 Cr already utilised and equal to 18.95% of FY25 consolidated turnover, a direction reversed against the historical pattern of AREDL lending to the company (12th AGM Notice bundled with AR FY24-25, p.16-19). [B08] Alongside a confirmed statutory default (CARO ix(a)) at subsidiary Integrow Asset Management inside a seven entity CARO exception year, unquantified, under a first year auditor covering only ~56% of consolidated assets directly (AR FY24-25 Auditors' Report Annexure B, consolidated p.204 / standalone p.90). [B08]

**Transition evidence (offsetting):**
- REA India / REA Group received a 5.5% equity stake (42,42,537 shares) via share swap for the PropTiger acquisition, Jul-Aug 2025. [B08/B07 H2]
- Promoter group honoured the full rights issue subscription commitment, including standing ready for unsubscribed shares, final call by the Mar-2025 record date (AR FY24-25 Note 9). [B08]
- Co promoter open market purchase of 25,000 shares, 10-Mar-2026, a net buying signal. [B08]
- Promoter holding decline Jun-2025 (49.81%) to Sep-2025 (47.04%) reconciles arithmetically to new share issuance to REA for PropTiger, not to promoter selling. [B08]
- Pledge 0%, stable, moderate confidence on secondary sources; primary SHP Table II not directly reviewed. [B08]
- Non core building sale (Q5/Q6) at ~15% above the independent valuer estimate, proceeds to debt paydown, debt free declared 21-May-2026. [B08]
- New statutory auditor (Kirtane & Pandit LLP) appointed via a transparent Board then EGM process; two new independent directors added 23-Apr-2026 (credentials not independently verified). [B08]

## FLAG-CASH: determination INDETERMINATE

The cash conversion signal does not resolve cleanly to STRUCTURAL or GROWTH-INDUCED on the evidence held, and the single piece that would adjudicate it (the credit rating rationale) was not collected. INDETERMINATE never silently resolves to PROCEED; it caps this verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS with the missing evidence named.

**Evidence weighed:**
- For growth induced: headline consolidated receivables grew +24.8%, tracking revenue growth +23.3% (B02, Note 4.c p.139); CFO grew to ₹62.93 Cr FY26 from ₹27.68 Cr FY25 and free cash flow swung to +₹47.08 Cr FY26 from -₹84.24 Cr FY24 (B01). [B01/B02]
- Against that (structural pressure): the receivables ageing tail deteriorated sharply behind the headline, one to two year bucket +259.8% YoY, two to three year bucket +1,233% YoY, >1yr total +327% YoY, while the ECL significant increase in credit risk bucket shrank from ₹159 Lakhs to ₹109 Lakhs (B02/B03, Note 4.c consolidated p.139). [B02/B03]
- FY25 combined lease cash service of ₹70.50 Cr exceeded CFO of ₹27.68 Cr by ~2.5x, funded through financing (equity raise) not operations (B03; B02 rank 10, Cash Flow Statement p.128). [B03/B02]
- Profit quality: the maiden Q3 FY26 PAT of ₹2.71 Cr rode ₹9.72 Cr of non operating other income from a HelloWorld IndAS lease liability reversal and is an operating loss without it; the separate Q4 FY26 ₹17.72 Cr building sale gain was kept outside reported continuing profit (Verifier B; B05). [B12b/B05]
- Mooting factor: the company declared itself debt free on 21-May-2026 after selling non core buildings for ₹112 Cr and repaying the LRD facility, which partly retires the lease and debt service concern going forward (OPERATOR_CONTEXT items 3, 7). [B00/operator]

**Named missing evidence:** no credit rating rationale was collected this run (rating/ folder absent, B00). Without it the structural versus growth call cannot be closed. [B00]

**Single falsification metric (Section 4):** the FY26 annual report trade receivables >1yr ageing bucket. After +327% YoY in FY25, a further YoY increase in the >1yr buckets falsifies the "growth induced, will normalise" read and confirms structural collection deterioration; a decline supports growth induced. Watch it in the FY26 AR trade receivables ageing note.
