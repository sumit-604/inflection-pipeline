### 2C. Mandatory diagnostics (answered)

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, strongly. Standalone +87.4% (61.52→115.26); consolidated +38.5% (125.75→174.19). **The gap is itself a signal**: standalone grew more than 2x faster than consolidated, meaning intra-group / eliminated revenue expanded. Standalone (115.26) + KP Woven (86.36, l.628) − consolidated (174.19) = **Rs 27.43 cr eliminated intercompany (15.7% of consolidated revenue)** — a large related-party trade footprint (feeds FLAG-RPT / monitoring item 3). Comparison to base case: no Bear/Base/Bull revenue projection was passed inline this run (marked ND in Step 6A); the run-rate is comfortably above the FY26 quarterly average.
2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand YoY?** Yes, sharply. Consolidated Q1 FY27 margin **18.86% vs Q1 FY26 7.28% = +1,158 bps**. Standalone **13.02% vs 3.52% = +950 bps**. The comparison base (Q1 FY26) was a genuinely weak quarter (3.5% standalone margin), so part of the YoY jump is a low-base effect. Sequential note in Step 3.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY?** Yes — and this is the cleanest positive. Consolidated core PBT 3.20→25.55; standalone 0.18→11.90. **The headline PAT growth is NOT an Other-Income artefact** — in fact consolidated Other Income FELL 88.7% (6.49→0.73) and PBT still rose 171%. This is the opposite of the treasury-timing trap the protocol warns against.
4. **What drove the gap between core PBT growth and reported PAT growth?** Consolidated: core op PBT +698% but reported PAT (total) +157.6%. The compression comes from (a) Other Income −5.76 cr, (b) JV share −0.03 cr (gone to nil; the raw 2.53 is in Lakhs = Rs 0.03 cr, not Rs 2.53 cr), (c) tax +5.37 cr (ETR rose to 30.2% from 26.5%), (d) finance costs +1.29 cr. Each is quantified in the Step 4 bridge.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** Depreciation is FLAT (consol +1.6%, std +7.3%) despite revenue +38.5%/+87.4% — **no capex-absorption drag visible, but also no evidence of major new plant commissioning in D&A**. Finance costs scaled +31% consol / +76% std — **debt is growing**, consistent with the segment-liability build in Step 5 (A3-08).
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Yes, favourably at the headline: consolidated OI/PBT fell from 67% (Q1 FY26 was OI-dependent) to 2.8% (Q1 FY27 is cleanly operational). Stripping OI does not weaken the quarter.

**The one caveat that colours all six answers:** the entire YoY step-up in consolidated earnings is concentrated in the unreviewed KP Woven subsidiary (Step 5C / A3-02, A3-03). The operational quality is real *on the face of the filing*, but 50.9% of it is management-furnished and not independently reviewed.
