1. Background and Regulatory Reference 
This Framework has been formulated pursuant to the Circular No. NF-25013/3/2025- 
NFRA dated 7 January 2026 issued by the National Financial Reporting Authority 
(NFRA), titled “Effective Communication Between Statutory Auditors and Those Charged 
with Governance, Including Audit Committees.” 
The Framework is intended to operationalise the applicable requirements of the 
Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, and the Standards 
on Auditing prescribed thereunder, particularly SA 260 (Revised), Communication with 
Those Charged with Governance, and SA 265, Communicating Deficiencies in Internal 
Control to Those Charged with Governance and Management. 
This Policy was framed and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 28% 
October 2026. 
2. Objective and Purpose 
The objectives of this Framework are to: 
o 
Establish an effective, transparent, timely and documented two-way communication 
process between the Statutory Auditors and Those Charged with Governance (TCWG), 
including the Audit Committee, wherever applicable. 
o 
Facilitate effective oversight of financial reporting, the audit process, internal financial 
controls, risk management and regulatory compliance. 
« 
Ensure timely communication and resolution or appropriate escalation of significant audit 
matters, accounting judgements, control deficiencies, suspected fraud and other matters 
relevant to the audit. 
o 
Provide clarity regarding responsibilities, communication channels, nodal persons, 
meeting frequency and documentation requirements. 
o 
Ensure that significant matters communicated by the Statutory Auditors receive 
appropriate consideration, deliberation, response and follow-up by TCWG. 
3. Scope 
This Policy applies to: 
o The Board of Directors. 
o The Audit Committee. 
o The Statutory Auditors and their engagement team. 
o The Chief Financial Officer, Finance and Accounts function, Internal Audit, Risk 
Management, Compliance, Legal, and Company Secretary.

« Such other officers, committees, or functions as may be involved in financial reporting, 
internal controls, audit coordination, or governance oversight. 
This Policy covers communication relating to statutory audit of the Company’s financial 
statements, internal financial controls, fraud, related-party transactions, regulatory 
matters, and other matters required under applicable law or auditing standards.

Governing Principles of Communication: 
Communication between TCWG and the Statutory Auditor shall be governed by the following 
principles: 
a) Timeliness: Matters shall be communicated sufficiently early to enable TCWG to take 
appropriate action. 
b) Clarity and Precision: Communications shall be clear, specific, and free from ambiguity. 
c) Completeness: All matters required to be communicated under applicable auditing standards 
shall be covered. 
d) Two-way Engagement: Communication shall be interactive and not limited to one-way 
reporting. 
e) Proportionality: The form, detail, and frequency of communication shall be commensurate 
with the significance of the matter.

issues, or matters requiring prompt attention. 
c) Completion Stage: Communication of significant audit findings, qualitative aspects of 
accounting practices, and matters affecting the auditor’s report. 
d) Ongoing / As Required: Communication of auditor independence matters, going 
concern issues, fraud-related matters, or other significant issues as they arise.
