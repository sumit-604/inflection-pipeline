### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

- Capex under execution = CWIP (Rs 255.34 lakh, Note 5A p.64) + contracted-not-spent commitments (Rs
  29.67 lakh, Note 40a p.83) = **Rs 285.01 lakh**
- Historical fixed asset turnover (FAT) = Revenue from Operations FY24-25 (Rs 12,600.79 lakh) ÷ average
  net fixed assets (PPE net block + intangibles net block, excluding CWIP):
  - FY23-24 net block: Rs 3,981.83 lakh (PPE) + Rs 17.49 lakh (intangibles) = Rs 3,999.32 lakh
  - FY24-25 net block: Rs 4,480.84 lakh (PPE) + Rs 15.66 lakh (intangibles) = Rs 4,496.50 lakh
  - Average = Rs 4,247.91 lakh
  - FAT = 12,600.79 ÷ 4,247.91 = **2.97x**
- Implied incremental revenue = 285.01 × 2.97 = **Rs 846.5 lakh**
- As % of current revenue (Rs 12,600.79 lakh) = 846.5 ÷ 12,600.79 = **6.72%** above current revenue

(A CWIP-only calc, excluding contracted commitments, gives 255.34 × 2.97 = Rs 758.4 lakh = 6.02% — the
range is roughly 6-7% either way. This is modest embedded growth, consistent with modernization capex
rather than a capacity-expansion story.)
