### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic
Because no committed capex programme exists, the standard "capex under execution × historical fixed asset turnover" calculation is **not meaningful** for this company. For completeness: FY26 average net fixed assets ≈ (₹117.96L + ₹293.93L)/2 = ₹205.95L; FY26 revenue = ₹14,865.07L; implied fixed-asset turnover ≈ 72x. Applying this to the ~₹1.76cr incremental fixed-asset base would imply an enormous "capacity-driven" revenue figure that is an artefact of the asset-light model, not evidence of embedded growth — that turnover ratio reflects working-capital and bank-guarantee capacity (financed by IPO equity), not physical plant capacity. **capex_embedded_growth_pct = 0** (arithmetic not applicable; growth driver is balance-sheet/bank-guarantee headroom, tracked instead under G1 below).

### 2D. New geography/market entries
Egypt and Nigeria export sales booked in FY25 (📄, AR Note 22); Vietnam export/advance from FY24 not repeated in FY25; Gulf states explicitly abandoned (🎙️, May 2026 call, quoted above). Net: contraction, not expansion, versus the FY25 AR's "established presence... Bangladesh and Vietnam" framing (Inv. Pres. slide 18), which is not supported by the FY25/FY26 export figures.

Early warning sign: any slowdown or renegotiation on Reliance-linked orders would simultaneously hit "ecosystem" and "concentration" metrics.
- **E1 First-mover claims (Moderate, weak evidence)**: risk is these claims are entirely unverified and self-reported; any independent disclosure (a competitor claiming the same "first," a customer RFP showing multiple qualified EDR/MVR vendors) would collapse this to a "no evidence" item. Early warning sign: watch for whether Stage 11 valuation work or subsequent filings surface any patent or exclusivity documentation — none exists as of this scan.
