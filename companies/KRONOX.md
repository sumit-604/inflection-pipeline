## MENTAL MODEL — DRAFT, UNSIGNED (pending Halt 1)

**Archetype:** Excellent-returns niche franchise arrested by owner behaviour,
not by economics. The same setup Zenrock bought at Indo Borax, seven months later.

**FROM state:** High-purity inorganic salts maker earning 36-44% on capital with
26% net margins held for four years, growing revenue at 6% over three years.
Cash parked in fixed deposits. An approved plant at Dahej left unbuilt for two
years. Promoters took 30-40% of PAT as remuneration pre-IPO, then Rs 130 cr out
through an OFS that gave the company nothing, then sold control at Rs 103.22
against a mandatory open offer price of Rs 157.27.

**TO state:** The export channel and the coastal footprint of a larger inorganic
chemicals platform. Dahej built. Boron products added to a catalogue already
qualified into pharma, laboratory and nutraceutical customers.

**ENGINE:** Reinvestment. The business has never had an owner who wanted to
reinvest at 36%. It now has one.

**DOMINANT VARIABLES (3):**
  1. Dahej Unit IV. Two years of approvals, zero construction. This is the
     single binary in the entity
  2. Whether the export book (32.39% of FY26 revenue, up from 26.57%) can carry
     Indo Borax product
  3. Customer concentration, which is a genuine unknown post-IPO. Top 10 were
     45-56% of revenue in the RHP years. NOT DISCLOSED for FY24, FY25, FY26

**WHAT THE MODEL REJECTS AS NOISE:**
  - The Rs 103.22 block price as a statement about business quality. Sellers who
    already took Rs 130 cr out in 2024 and are keeping 9.95% for transition
    support are not price-maximising sellers
  - The FY23 161:1 bonus and buyback. Pre-IPO housekeeping
  - Q1 FY27 PAT +16.24% as evidence the growth problem is solved. One quarter

**PROOF GATE:**
  (a) Dahej Unit IV construction start by 31-Mar-2027
  (b) FY27 revenue growth above 10% (against 1.03% in FY26 and 6% over three years)
  (c) Disclosed customer concentration in the FY27 AR under new ownership

**FALSIFIER:** No Dahej start by Mar-2027 AND FY27 revenue growth below 5%.
Two years of approvals and a third year of nothing means the constraint was
never the owner.
