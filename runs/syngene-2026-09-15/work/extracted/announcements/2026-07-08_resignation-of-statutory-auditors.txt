As you are aware, we were appointed as the Statutory Auditors of Syngene Scientific Solutions 
Limited ('the Company'), pursuant to the shareholder's resolution dated 21 July 2023 to hold 
office until the conclusion of the 6th Annual General Meeting of the Company to be held after the 
end of financial year ending 31 March 2028. 
We have completed our statutory audit till 31 March 2026 and we issued our audit report on 28 
April 2026. 
Please refer to our ongoing discussions in connection with our continuation as statutory auditors 
of the Company. As per discussions, management intends to align auditors with the auditors of 
Syngene International Limited, the holding company, due to mandatory rotation of our firm as 
auditors of the holding company under the Companies Act, 2013. In view of the above, we would 
not be in a position to continue as statutory auditors of the Company for the year ended 31 March 
2027. We have also discussed this matter with the Management of the Company. 
Please find attached completed Annexure A, with regard to the information to be obtained by the 
Company from the auditors for the resignation as required by Section V-D of the SEBI Master 
circular no. SEBI/HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-PoD2/I/3762/2026 dated 30 January 2026, 
pertaining to 'Resignation of statutory auditors from listed entities and their material subsidiaries'. 
As per the requirement of the Companies Act, 2013, we shall be forwarding the copy of the ADT-
3, as filed with ROC, in due course. 
Please accept our resignation with immediate effect. 
Yours faithfully,
