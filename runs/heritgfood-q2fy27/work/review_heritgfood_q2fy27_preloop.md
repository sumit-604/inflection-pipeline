### 3. Business-model intelligence

- How it earns: it buys milk from 300,000+ farmers in 9 states at about Rs 47.73 per litre landed and sells liquid milk at about Rs 60.34 per litre (PR-L76-84; P-l588-597) [MGMT]. - Margin engine: value-added products (curd, paneer, lassi, ice cream, yogurt) carry higher gross margin. VAP share of consolidated revenue rose from 30.72% to 34.18% YoY on a consistent base [FILED/MGMT, derived]. The company prints 35.4% because it divides by standalone revenue [FILED, derived].
- Gross margin moved against the mix shift: 24.54% to 21.84% YoY [FILED, derived]. Milk cost overwhelmed the mix gain this quarter.
- Model drift: three moves. (1) Asset-heavy: PPE rose 54% in FY26 and CWIP rose again in H1 FY27, funded by term debt [FILED/MGMT].
