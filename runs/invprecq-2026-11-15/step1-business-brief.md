## Business

1. Lost-wax investment castings, 8 g to 330 kg, 500+ alloy specs incl. vacuum-melted nickel and cobalt superalloys, sold as-cast or machined, built to customer print (P).
2. Capacity 3,060 tonnes a year at Bhavnagar, incl. vacuum casting up to 100 tonnes a year (C). Utilisation about 60% in H1 FY27 against 51% in FY26 (P).
3. One reportable segment, "Investment Casting Activities" (R, note 2). End-market mix, H1 FY27: automotive 62%, industrial/energy/general engineering 27%, aerospace and defence 11%. FY26: 64% / 30% / 6% (P).
4. Domestic sales about 88% of FY26 sales, so exports about 12% (C). Top five customers about 50% of FY26 income. Named auto customers: Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, Royal Enfield (C).
5. Subsidiary I&PCL Vacuum Cast Ltd is dormant: revenue Nil, loss Rs 0.21 lakh in Q2 FY27 (R). Standalone and consolidated are near identical.
6. Margin drivers: raw-material pass-through clauses with major customers; job work about 33% of FY26 cost of sales (C); external processing Rs 16.53 Cr in Q2 FY27 on Rs 58.00 Cr revenue (P, P&L slide); own 6.5 MW solar plus wind covers about 40% of energy (P).
7. Latest quarter, Q2 FY27 (quarter ended 30-Sep-2026, filed 14-Nov-2026): revenue Rs 58.00 Cr (+31.6% YoY), EBITDA incl. other income Rs 12.91 Cr, margin 22.17% (17.45% a year ago), PAT Rs 5.80 Cr (+91% YoY), EPS Rs 5.80 (P). H1 FY27 revenue Rs 111.4 Cr (+26.3%), PAT Rs 10.8 Cr (+107%) (P).
8. Trend: FY26 revenue Rs 188 Cr, OPM 16%, PAT Rs 12 Cr; FY22 revenue Rs 132 Cr, OPM 10% (S). Six quarters of rising OPM, 14.5% in Q1 FY26 to 21.9% in Q2 FY27 (S).
9. Engine a thesis would rest on: mix shift into aerospace, defence and energy on existing approvals (EN 9100, Nadcap NDT and heat treatment, CEMILAC, DGAQA, HAL approved). New programmes in H1: superalloy aero castings for HAL, aluminium defence castings for MKU (P). Board approved capex up to Rs 100 Cr for aerospace, defence and energy projects, no timeline or funding split stated (R, note 6).
10. No guidance on revenue or margin in the filing or presentation (P, R). The CMD letter says H2 focus is moving nominated parts into monthly supply (P).
11. Balance sheet: borrowings Rs 80.19 Cr at 30-Sep-2026 against Rs 70.10 Cr at 31-Mar-2026; gearing 0.71x; interest cover 5.12x in H1 (R, P). Inventory Rs 63.16 Cr against Rs 51.70 Cr in six months (R).
12. Concalls: NO. Screener lists four "concall" entries (Nov 2024 to May 2026), but each has only a PPT link; the Transcript chip is not a link (S, page HTML checked). Investor contact is one-on-one meetings, e.g. Motilal Oswal on 16-Nov-2026 (https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=1813754a-a11d-4ded-b0a2-313bc1ae4925.pdf). The pipeline should run in no-concall mode.
13. Credit rating: CARE BBB; Stable / CARE A3+, upgraded from BBB- / A3 on 09-Jul-2026 (C). CRISIL suspended its rating in Sep 2014 for non-cooperation (C).
14. Market data, 13-Nov-2026 close: price Rs 1,322, market cap Rs 1,322 Cr, PE 74.7, promoter 51.97%, FII 0.00% (S). Shares about 1 crore (equity Rs 10 Cr at FV Rs 10, S).

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS

1. Cash conversion of the margin jump. H1 FY27 operating cash flow Rs 3.49 Cr against PAT Rs 10.8 Cr (R, cash flow statement). Inventory rose Rs 11.46 Cr in six months (R). Change-in-inventories credit was Rs 6.04 Cr in Q2 FY27 against Rs 0.26 Cr in Q2 FY26 (P, P&L slide). Verify how much of the 22.17% margin is WIP absorption, and reconcile the working-capital gap: screener cash conversion cycle 245 days FY26 (S) against CARE operating cycle 133 days FY26 (C).
2. Aerospace and defence share and its delivery. Claimed rise from 6% (FY26) to 11% (H1 FY27) of revenue (P). Verify against filed annual report or notes, name the HAL and MKU programmes, and test the "monthly supply" ramp claim.
3. Rs 100 Cr capex approval (R, note 6) against TNW Rs 103 Cr at 31-Mar-2026 and CARE's stated assumption of no major debt-funded capex (C). Verify funding mix, timeline, and whether it changes the debt and rating path.
4. Revenue basis mismatch. FY26 revenue from operations Rs 185.79 Cr on the presentation P&L slide, Rs 189.7 Cr on its five-year slide, Rs 188 Cr on screener, Rs 189.20 Cr TOI at CARE (P, S, C). Also Q2 standalone revenue Rs 57.95 Cr in R against Rs 58.00 Cr in P. Fix the base year figure from the audited FY26 annual report before any projection.
