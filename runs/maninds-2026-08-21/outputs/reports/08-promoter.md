### 1D History before the current company: succession, disputes
The Man Group was founded by **J.L. Mansukhani** in the 1970s; two sons, **R.C. Mansukhani (RCM)** and **J.C. Mansukhani (JCM)**, ran the group jointly. Bombay High Court ruled in the company's favour; the losing group has appealed to the Supreme Court, admission pending as of the FY25 AR date (12 May 2025) (VERIFIED, AR p.160). Current status of the Supreme Court admission: NOT FOUND (no update located beyond the AR).

## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A Related-party transaction history
- **Limitless Contracting Private Limited (the priority lead)**: FY25 loan taken from this promoter-group entity Rs111.20cr, repaid Rs12.51cr, interest expense Rs2.65cr (of which Rs2.28cr capitalised), **closing loan payable Rs97.68 crore as at 31 March 2025** (VERIFIED, AR consolidated Note 36C/D, p.232-233; standalone equivalent Note 35). **No interest rate or tenor is disclosed in the note** — the AR discloses the transaction quantum but not the pricing/terms, consistent with the lead's characterisation of "undisclosed terms." This is the group's largest single related-party financing relationship and is with an entity majority-owned by the MD's wife and sister.
- Note: a separate, numerically identical figure — **Rs97.68 crore** — appears elsewhere in the AR as a CARO qualification on subsidiary Man Stainless Steel Tubes Limited (short-term borrowed funds used for long-term purposes; AR p.65/subsidiary CARO annexure). **These are two unrelated facts that happen to share a rupee figure**; they should not be conflated.
- **Man Finance Private Limited**: rent paid Rs4.74cr/yr FY24 and FY25 (flat); received warrant application money; converted 25,00,000 warrants into equity in May 2025 (Section 3B).
- Extensive intercompany lending to fellow subsidiaries (Man Stainless Steel Tubes, Man Offshore and Drilling, MSPL) is disclosed via Ind AS 24 notes; this is the same category of loans-to-subsidiaries that SEBI's Sep 2025 order found to have been inadequately disclosed for the FY15-21 period.
- Historic core finding (SEBI 2A.6): unapproved related-party transactions and fund diversion to subsidiaries FY15-21 — this is the single most serious RPT finding against the promoters.

### 3B Capital allocation behaviour
- **No dividend paid in FY25** (VERIFIED, auditor's Rule 11 confirmation, AR p.184) — company is in a capex-heavy growth phase (Jammu SS plant, Saudi NPC acquisition per operator-ferried summary), so cash retention is explainable, not obviously value-destructive.
- **Promoter warrants**: 25,00,000 warrants allotted to Man Finance Pvt Ltd (promoter group) on 1 Dec 2023 at Rs183.50, 25% paid up-front. As of the FY25 AR (year end 31 Mar 2025) these were **not yet exercised**. They were subsequently **converted to equity on 28 May 2025** (MEDIA REPORTED, multiple corporate-announcement aggregators), raising Man Finance's stake from 9.99% to 13.34% and total promoter-group holding from 46.21% to 48.21%. This is a promoter **conversion**, not a lapse — a mild positive (promoter capital commitment), contrary to the lead's implicit framing risk of a "warrant that lapsed."
- **Preferential issue to non-promoters, 28 Jul 2025**: Rs255cr raised via 77,74,383 equity shares at Rs328 to 25 institutional/strategic investors, including Ashish Kacholia, Carnelian Asset Management, Ovata Capital (Hong Kong), Ashika Global Finance, Capri Global Holdings, RBA & Finance (MEDIA REPORTED, corroborated across NBM&CW, Dealroom.co, ScanX aggregator coverage of the BSE/NSE filing). A further ~Rs1cr convertible-warrant tranche was raised 2 Aug 2025. **This preferential dilution to non-promoter investors is the best-evidenced explanation for the ~5pp single-quarter drop in promoter % into Sep 2025** — it is capital raised for Jammu/Saudi capex, not a promoter share sale or pledge invocation. No evidence of a promoter sale was found despite a specific search for one.
- **Executive compensation**: R.C. Mansukhani salary Rs7.74cr FY25 (Rs7.76cr FY24, flat); Nikhil Mansukhani Rs3.85cr FY25 (Rs3.14cr FY24, +22.7%) (VERIFIED, AR Note 36C/35C). This rise occurred in a year the company was under active SEBI/NFRA scrutiny; whether it is disproportionate to performance could not be fully assessed from the documents reviewed (FY25 standalone PAT trend not in the extracted governance sections) — flagged for downstream financial-stage cross-check, not asserted as a violation here.

### 3C Promoter share transactions, pledge
- **Pledge (priority lead)**: FY25 AR discloses **65,00,000 promoter shares pledged** to State Bank of India as security for the SBI foreign-currency term loan, plus 18,789 Merino Shelters equity shares, plus personal guarantees of both R.C. Mansukhani and Nikhil Mansukhani (VERIFIED, AR p.150-151/Note on secured borrowings, disclosed only in the borrowings note as the lead states). At the FY25 AR date this was ~21.7% of promoter holding / ~10% of shares outstanding (per task brief, consistent with the smaller FY25 share base).
- **Current pledge level (Jun 2026)**: 20.05% of promoter holding pledged (MEDIA REPORTED, aggregator shareholding-pattern search). Cross-check: 43.21% promoter holding x 20.05% x 75,009,570 total shares outstanding = 64,99,832 shares — **matches the original 65,00,000-share pledge almost exactly**. **Conclusion: the pledge has NOT been reduced in absolute share-count terms; its percentage terms have fallen only because the promoter holding and total share base both grew (warrant conversion + non-promoter preferential issue), not because shares were released.** As % of total shares outstanding, pledge is now ~8.7% (65,00,000 / 75,009,570), down from ~10% at FY25 AR date purely on denominator growth.
- A Regulation 31(4) LODR declaration filed for FY26 states "no new encumbrance" was created on promoter shares (MEDIA REPORTED, ScanX aggregator headline; direct page fetch blocked). This is a routine no-new-pledge attestation, not a pledge-release confirmation.
- **Pledge trend: stable (flat absolute share count, ~65 lakh shares, since at least FY25 AR through Jun 2026); falling only as a % ratio due to base dilution, not due to release.**
- No creeping-acquisition pattern, no sales timed ahead of bad news, and no suspicious pre-announcement trading were found in the searches performed.

### 3D Minority treatment
- The core SEBI finding — non-consolidation of MSPL for six years, suppressing group-level losses/liabilities while showing inflated standalone profits (SEBI 2A.6) — is itself the paradigm minority-shareholder harm: investors were denied an accurate financial picture for a materially large subsidiary (MSPL's assets/liabilities were ~19-29% of MIIL's per the NFRA order, Section 3E) for years.
- No proxy-advisory (IiAS/SES/InGovern) report specific to MIIL was located in searches performed; this is most likely a coverage gap (small/micro-cap, limited institutional float historically) rather than a clean bill — **NOT FOUND, not confirmed clean**.
- No delisting attempt, no SCORES-complaint data (not typically public), found.

### 3E Auditor relationship
- **Current statutory auditor**: A Sachdev & Co, Chartered Accountants (FRN 001307C), appointed at the 34th AGM (29 Sep 2022) for a five-year term to the 39th AGM (VERIFIED, AR p.32/Board's Report). No resignation; audit signed by partner Manish Agarwal.
- FY25 **consolidated audit opinion carries three Emphasis of Matter paragraphs** (the SEBI forensic-audit sub-judice matter, the MCA 206(5) notice to the company/directors, the Merino Shelters 206(5) compounding) but the **opinion itself is unqualified/"not modified"** (VERIFIED, AR p.183-184).
- FY25 standalone Board's Report separately discloses **qualifications in the CARO/audit report of subsidiary Man Stainless Steel Tubes Limited**: short-term funds (Rs97.68cr) used for long-term purposes due to delayed term-loan disbursement; unapproved-by-audit-committee related-party transactions (because that subsidiary's Audit Committee was only formed 13 Oct 2024); and cash losses of Rs205.64 lakh FY25 / Rs83.02 lakh FY24 (VERIFIED, AR p.32-33). This is a subsidiary-level qualification, not a qualification on MIIL standalone.
- **NFRA order, 24 Nov 2023**: NFRA imposed a **Rs5,00,000 fine and a 5-year ban on CA Nilesh Chheda**, engagement partner at **Rohira Mehta & Associates (now RMA & Co.)**, statutory auditor of MIIL for FY2016-17, for professional misconduct — incorrectly issuing a qualified (rather than adverse) opinion on the non-consolidation of MSPL, whose assets/liabilities were ~19.2%/28.96% of MIIL's totals, and failures under SA 200 and SA 240 (VERIFIED, corroborated across NFRA.gov.in document listing, Business Standard, Moneylife, TaxGuru — meets the multi-source bar). This is a serious, independently adjudicated finding against the audit function covering the same period as the SEBI misstatement finding, though the sanctioned individual/firm is not the current auditor.
