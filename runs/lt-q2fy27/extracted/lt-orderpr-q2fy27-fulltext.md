L&T Wins Orders (Major*) for Dhaka Metro

Mumbai, October 27, 2026: The Transportation Infrastructure business vertical of L&T has secured
major Design & Build orders for two key systems packages of the Dhaka Mass Rapid Transit
Development Project (Line 1), that is Dhaka Metro, in Bangladesh.

The orders comprise Contract Package CP09 - Track Works & Electrical Systems, and Contract
Package CP10 - Signalling, Telecommunications & Platform Screen Doors (PSD). These pertain to
the first underground metro project in Bangladesh.

Prior to this L&T had won and delivered the integrated railway systems package for Dhaka Metro
Line 6, Bangladesh's first metro rail corridor, which is currently in commercial operation.

Commenting on this, Ramkumar S, Senior Executive Vice President & Head - Transportation
Infrastructure, L&T, said: "The repeat orders from Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL)
are a testament to L&T's capabilities in delivering large scale, integrated urban transit projects
globally. With a strong track record of delivering E&M systems for the Dhaka Metro Line 6 with the
highest standards of quality and timely execution, and more recently, for the Jakarta Metro, which
is under execution, we are well positioned to leverage our experience in seamlessly integrating
multiple systems in close coordination with OEMs and stakeholders".

The Dhaka Metro Line 1 project, funded by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), will
provide a modern mass transit system spanning approximately 31 km. It includes the Airport Route
from Kamalapur to Airport and the Purbachal Route from Natun Bazar to Purbachal Terminal, with
19 stations and a depot at Pitalganj.

Together, the two packages form the core operational systems of Dhaka Metro Line 1, and will
deliver a safe, reliable and world-class urban transit solution for the people of Dhaka.

Under CP09, L&T, along with its Japanese partner M/s Sumitomo Corporation, will undertake the
design, procurement, installation, testing and commissioning of the complete track works, power
supply & distribution system, traction substations, overhead catenary system and associated
electrical facilities across the corridor.

Under CP10, L&T will execute the signalling, telecommunication and platform screen door for the
entire network.

The Transportation Infrastructure vertical of L&T is a leading EPC player, which has delivered some
of the most complex rail infrastructure projects in India and international markets, consistently
maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality and project execution.
