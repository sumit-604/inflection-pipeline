6. What it makes: build-to-spec AC generators for steam and gas turbines up to 250 MVA, hydro up to 45 MVA, wind, diesel and gas engines up to 25 MVA, plus induction, synchronous and traction motors. Technology base: Siemens licence for 2-pole generators to 250 MVA (2011), long-term gas-engine generator supply agreement with INNIO, Alstom traction stator and rotor supply (2016). [FILING] presentation, Journey and Products slides.
7. Segments: one reported segment (AC generators and electric motors), so no line-by-line margin. FY26 revenue: AC generators Rs 1,449.2 cr, generator spares and components Rs 197.2 cr. No split by prime mover. [FILING] FY26 AR, Note 27 and Note 43 (p.237, 248, read through a machine extraction, verify page). Management says it gives no data-centre split of inflow. Q1 call p.8 [FILING].
8. The engine: Q2 inflow names large serial-production gas-engine generator orders from global OEMs for data-centre and distributed power (13.8 kV, Americas), and volume gas-turbine generator orders from a major US-based gas-turbine OEM plus a second global OEM. Management cites a "tremendous shortage" of gas turbines, gas engines and generators (Q1 call p.3). [FILING] presentation, Highlights slides; Q1 call.
9. Export shift: consolidated outside-India revenue rose from Rs 431.1 cr in FY25 (34%) to Rs 1,121.8 cr in FY26 (60%). Domestic incl. deemed exports fell from Rs 847.7 cr to Rs 734.4 cr. [FILING] FY26 AR, Note 43, machine extraction, verify.
10. Concentration: FY26 consolidated Customer A 23.17%, Customer B 10.74% of revenue (FY25: 14.10%, 12.32%). Standalone, three customers each exceed 10% (25.06%, 24.08%, 11.62%). Names not disclosed. [FILING] FY26 AR, Note 43, machine extraction, verify.
11. Margin source: gross margin is flat, 33.5% Q2 FY27 vs 33.4% Q2 FY26, and 33.6% FY26 vs 35.6% FY25. EBITDA gain comes from operating leverage: employee plus other costs fell from 14.6% to 12.8% of income YoY. Price-variation clauses exist in contracts (Q1 call p.6). Management guides 18-19% EBITDA (Q1 call p.10). [FILING] presentation P&L; Q1 call.
12. Cash: CFO Rs 40 cr FY25 (23% of PAT), Rs 129 cr FY26 (54% of PAT); debtor days 112, 125, 146 for FY24-FY26. [SCREENER]. At Sep 2026 receivables are Rs 952.9 cr (Mar 2026: Rs 742.1 cr), cash and bank Rs 418.5 cr (Rs 198.6 cr), other current liabilities Rs 430.3 cr (Rs 190.3 cr), borrowings nil. FY26 customer advances Rs 187.2 cr. [FILING] presentation balance sheet; FY26 AR Note 24.
13. Ownership: promoters 26.51% (down 0.36 pt in the quarter), MF and AIF 29.2%, FII 22.7%. A SEBI SAST Reg 29(1) disclosure was filed 14 Nov 2026, content not read. [SCREENER]; [FILING] presentation shareholding slide.
14. Bull reading: inflow is at a record, the book is 74% export, a US gas-turbine OEM and global gas-engine OEMs place serial volume, and capacity rises to Rs 3,200 cr. Bear reading: the book is under one year of sales, one customer is 23% of revenue, gross margin is not rising, CFO trails PAT, and receivables grew 28% in six months. The separating observation: H2 FY27 inflow and the Q3-Q4 CFO-to-PAT ratio.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS

1. Guidance vs delivery: FY27 revenue guidance rose from Rs 2,400 cr+ (Q4 FY26 call) to Rs 2,600 cr (Q1 call, 12 Aug 2026, p.3) to Rs 2,900-3,000 cr (Q2 presentation, 12 Nov 2026). H1 delivered Rs 1,481.5 cr. Verify H2 delivery and that FY28 Rs 3,200 cr is a capacity figure, not guidance.
2. Customer concentration: FY26 consolidated Customer A 23.17% and Customer B 10.74% of revenue; standalone three customers above 10%. Verify against FY26 AR Note 43 (p.248) and test whether Customer A is the US gas-turbine OEM named in Q2 inflow.
3. Cash conversion: CFO/PAT 23% FY25 and 54% FY26, debtor days 146 FY26; receivables Rs 742.1 cr to Rs 952.9 cr in H1 FY27, other current liabilities Rs 190.3 cr to Rs 430.3 cr. Verify the H1 FY27 cash flow statement and the nature of the liability jump (customer advances vs other).
4. Governance and related party: promoter holding 26.51%, down 0.36 pt in Q2, with a SAST Reg 29(1) filing on 14 Nov 2026; FY26 subsidiary transactions Rs 256.3 cr (Europe GmbH) and Rs 167.1 cr (USA Inc). Verify who filed the SAST disclosure and the transfer-pricing basis of subsidiary sales.
