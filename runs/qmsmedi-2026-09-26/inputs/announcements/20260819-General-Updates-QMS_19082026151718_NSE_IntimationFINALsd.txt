Sub: Achievement of Gold Certification in Cybersecurity Assessment by QMS Medical Allied

We wish to inform the Exchange that QMS Medical Allied Services Ltd ("the Company") has successfully 
completed a comprehensive cybersecurity assessment conducted by CyberVadis, a globally recognised 
third-party cybersecurity risk-rating and assessment platform, and has been awarded the "Gold" 
certification for the year 2026. 
The Company scored 918 out of 1000 in the assessment, placing it in the "Mature" cybersecurity 
maturity category. The certification is valid until 17 August 2027, following which a re-assessment will 
be undertaken in the ordinary course. 
About the CyberVadis Assessment 
CyberVadis is an independent cybersecurity risk assessment platform widely used across global supply 
chains, particularly in healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and life sciences, to evaluate an organisation's 
information security posture against recognised industry frameworks such as ISO 27001, NIST, and 
GDPR-aligned controls. The assessment evaluates areas including governance, data protection, access 
control, asset management, third-party risk management, business continuity, and incident response. 
Assessments are scored on a 1,000-point scale and mapped to maturity bands (ranging from Basic to 
Mature to Advanced), with recognition levels of Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum awarded based on 
the score achieved. A CyberVadis certification is increasingly sought by multinational customers, payers, 
hospital networks, and pharmaceutical partners as a pre-qualification requirement before onboarding 
vendors and service providers, particularly where sensitive patient and health data is handled. 
Significance for the Company and Stakeholders 
● 
Regulatory and client trust: In the healthcare and medical services sector, demonstrable 
cybersecurity maturity is a material factor in client due diligence, hospital and insurer 
empanelment, and cross-border data-handling arrangements. 
● 
Risk mitigation: An independent, externally validated assessment reduces the Company's 
exposure to data-breach, ransomware, and third-party supply-chain risk — risks that carry direct 
financial, reputational, and regulatory consequences for listed entities. 
● 
Competitive differentiation: Gold-level certification distinguishes the Company in vendor 
empanelment processes where cybersecurity scorecards are increasingly a mandatory evaluation 
criterion.
