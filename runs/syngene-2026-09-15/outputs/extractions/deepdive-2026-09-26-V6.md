## V6 Q21 — Feb-2025 USFDA pre-approval inspection at FEI 3003600340 (Biocon Park)

**The FEI number, the exact Feb-2025 date, the product name, and the manufacturing
scale are NOT DISCLOSED anywhere in the corpus.** No document names FEI
3003600340. No document names "February 2025" next to any inspection. The
corpus gives two inspection-outcome tables (one per AR) that are the closest
tie-able disclosures; neither names a product, a scale, or a final NAI/VAI/OAI
classification for the FY25 event, and the classification given in FY26 (VAI)
is dated to FY26 (April 2025–March 2026), not to February 2025.

### Every regulatory-inspection statement quoted, with date and site, and its
### tie (or non-tie) to the Feb-2025 PAI

**1. Annual_Report_2025.txt, PDF page 66 (printed folio 63), FY25 audit table
(covers FY25 = year ended 31 March 2025, so it is the period containing
February 2025):**

> "In FY25, Syngene underwent an unprecedented 111 audits, including client
> and regulatory inspections."

> "Small Molecule & Biologics Audits / USFDA / Positive Pre-authorization
> inspection for biologic drug substance; GMP inspection for commercialized
> small molecule drug substance, drug product, and stability studies"

Comment: this is the single best candidate in the corpus for the Feb-2025
PAI named in the question. It is a USFDA pre-authorization (pre-approval)
inspection of a biologic drug substance, disclosed in the FY25 Annual Report.
It does NOT name the product, the site (Biocon Park is not named in this
row), the FEI number, or a classification finer than "Positive." It cannot be
confirmed as the same event as the Feb-2025 / FEI 3003600340 inspection named
in the question; it can only be flagged as the same-year, same-type
candidate. Two other agencies got the same treatment in the same FY25 table:
EMA/FAMHP/VMD "Positive Pre-authorization inspection for biologic drug
substance," and Health Canada "Positive GMP inspection for biologic drug
substance, small molecule drug substance, small molecule drug product, and
stability studies" — none of these are dated to February or tied to an FEI
number either.

to a USFDA outcome, and the only place a VAI classification appears. But it
is dated to FY26 (April 2025–March 2026), a full fiscal year after the

as a later, possibly follow-up, USFDA action at Biocon Park (Form 483
response accepted and closed; EIR with VAI) — NOT DISCLOSED whether this is
the CAPA/re-inspection outcome of the Feb-2025 PAI or an unrelated,
independent inspection. The GCP/BA-BE NAI row is explicitly a bioequivalence
study audit, not a manufacturing PAI, so it does not tie either.

**5. Annual_Report_2025.txt, page glossary/appendix, general USFDA

**CAPA response / re-inspection:** NOT DISCLOSED as a named CAPA plan tied to

## V6 Q23 — Emergent capacity-rights/supply/reserved-capacity disclosure;
## Bayview acquisition description

**No capacity-rights, supply agreement, transition-services agreement, or
reserved-capacity arrangement with Emergent BioSolutions is disclosed
anywhere in the corpus.** Every mention of "Emergent" in the corpus is the
acquisition note itself (accounting/CWIP treatment) or a repetition of it in
later filings; none describes an ongoing commercial relationship, committed
volumes, or revenue expected from Emergent. Confirmed by exhaustive search
of Annual_Report_2025.txt, Annual_Report_2026.txt, all four results filings,
all three investor decks, and all announcements — "Emergent" appears only in
the following places, all of which are the acquisition note or its
restatement:

**How the corpus describes the Bayview acquisition itself:**

**Seller, date, structure — Annual_Report_2026.txt, page 293 (standalone
Note 42) and page 378 (consolidated Note 43):**

> "During the quarter ended 31 March 2025, Syngene USA Inc. (wholly-owned
> subsidiary of the Company) has acquired biologics site in the USA fitted
> with multiple monoclonal antibody (mAbs) manufacturing lines from Emergent
> Manufacturing Operations Baltimore, LLC (a subsidiary of Emergent
> BioSolutions Inc.). This acquisition will increase the company's total
> single-use bioreactor capacity to 50,000L for large molecule discovery,
> development, and manufacturing services... The transaction was accounted
> for as an 'asset acquisition' under Ind AS 103 during the year ended 31
> March 2025."

Comment: seller = Emergent Manufacturing Operations Baltimore, LLC (subsidiary
of Emergent BioSolutions Inc.); date = quarter ended 31 March 2025 (i.e.
Q4FY25, calendar Jan–Mar 2025); accounting treatment = ASSET ACQUISITION
under Ind AS 103, explicitly NOT a business combination.

**Price — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 4 (printed folio, Group entities
section):**

> "Syngene USA Inc. acquired a state-of-the-art biologics facility from
> Emergent Manufacturing Operations Baltimore, LLC (a subsidiary of Emergent
> BioSolutions Inc.) for USD 36 million enabling Syngene to further expand
> its global biologics footprint."

Comment: acquisition price = USD 36 million (this is the only place in the
corpus stating a headline purchase price; the CWIP capitalization figures
below are cost-to-date, not the purchase price).

**Cost-to-date / capitalization detail — Annual_Report_2026.txt, page 293
(standalone Note 42):**

> "The cost incurred till 31 March 25 eligible for capitalization was

> additional pre-operating cost of Rs. 787 million (USD 8.4 million) eligible

**Funding — Annual_Report_2026.txt, page 396 (printed folio 391):**

> "The Company invested around USD 50 Mn in Syngene USA Inc. (SUSA), a
> wholly owned subsidiary of the Company to finance the cost of acquisition
> of biologics facility from Emergent Manufacturing Operations Baltimore,
> LLC., and for meeting the general corporate and day-to-day operating
> requirements."

**Bioreactor scale added — Annual_Report_2026.txt, business review, page 51

## V6 Q24 — Mangalore: gross block/capex, capacity, utilisation, site history

**Gross block / capex specific to Mangalore: NOT DISCLOSED as a standalone
figure anywhere in the corpus.** The corpus discloses company-wide gross
block plus CWIP only:

> "Investment (Gross Block + CWIP) of (~USD 700 Mn) in infrastructure and
> facilities" — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 19 area (printed folio, "Our
> value proposition" summary slide-style page). This is a whole-company
> figure across Bengaluru, Mangaluru and Hyderabad; it is not broken out by
> site.

**Capacity in KL or any other manufacturing-scale unit: NOT DISCLOSED for
Mangalore.** Mangalore is described only qualitatively as a small-molecule
API site ("commercial-scale production of active pharmaceutical ingredients
(APIs)" — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 1 printed folio, and "complex chemical
synthesis and scale-up" — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 45). Every KL/
kilolitre figure found in the corpus under a Mangalore heading is a WATER
withdrawal/consumption/discharge figure (BRSR environmental disclosure), not
a manufacturing capacity figure — e.g. "Total volume of water withdrawal (in
kilolitres) 1,32,438 1,87,490" (Annual_Report_2026.txt, BRSR section) is not
site-specific to Mangalore in the extracted table and in any case measures
water, not production capacity. No reactor volume, MT/annum, or batch-size
figure for Mangalore is stated anywhere in the corpus.

**Utilisation figure: no percentage or numeric utilisation figure for
Mangalore is ever disclosed.** All utilisation references are qualitative
trend language:

> "We did mention of the fact that we're seeing the utilization levels, and
> we don't call out the actual level, but we're seeing the trend lines on
> the utilization levels improve." — Deepak Jain, Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt,
> PDF page 8 (Page 8 of 16) — an explicit refusal to disclose the number.

> "MSEZ operations recorded steady progress across commercial execution...
> Following the successful delivery of commercial intermediates and APIs,
> the site secured repeat business that has contributed to improved
> manufacturing capacity utilization." — Annual_Report_2026.txt, page 43 area
> (Business Review, MSEZ Operations (Mangaluru) section)

> "Mangalore had a very low utilization in the past years... this fiscal
> year itself, you will see a significant ramp-up in utilization." —
> Siddharth Mittal, Concall_Jul_2026_Transcript.txt, PDF page 6 (Page 6 of 14)

Comment: management confirms Mangalore utilisation was "very low" historically
and gives a forward qualitative ramp claim (FY27), but never a number,
consistent across FY25 AR narrative, FY26 AR narrative, and all four calls.

**Site history (acquisition/commissioning dates):** NOT DISCLOSED in this
corpus. No document states when Syngene built, acquired, or first
commissioned the Mangalore (Mangaluru / MSEZ) facility, or under what
transaction structure. The only site-history disclosures of this kind in
the corpus are for OTHER sites, by contrast:
- Unit 3 (Bengaluru biologics): "On 04 July 2023, the Company's Board of
  Directors entered into a binding term sheet for acquiring Unit 3 biologics
  manufacturing facility in Bangalore, India, from Stelis Biopharma Limited
  (SBL). The unit has been acquired effective 01 December 2023 on a slump
  sale basis at a total cash consideration of Rs. 5,632 [million]... will add
  20,000 litres of installed biologics drug substance manufacturing capacity
  for Syngene. The site has the potential for future expansion of up to a
  further 20,000 litres." — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 65 (printed folio
  257, Note 43).
- Bayview: see Q23 above (acquired Q4FY25 from Emergent for USD 36 million).
- Syngene USA Inc.: "Established in FY 2018" — Annual_Report_2025.txt, page
  9 (printed folio, Group entities section, line 5281).

For Mangalore, the only datable event in the corpus is a facility addition
WITHIN the existing site, not the site's founding: "a state-of-the-art
facility at our Mangaluru site was commissioned to handle OEB-4

(printed folio, Capability Expansion section), undated beyond "during FY25."
