## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Supreme Power makes electrical transformers in Tamil Nadu. It builds each unit to the customer's order.
2. It sells to power utilities, EPC contractors and wind and solar developers. An EPC contractor builds whole projects for others.
3. In this half, group sales rose 80% to Rs 135 Cr. Profit rose 45% to Rs 13.65 Cr.
4. Profit grew slower than sales. The new Kannur plant added depreciation and interest, and the margin fell for the fourth period in a row.
5. About one fifth of the sales growth came through Danya Electric. Danya is a partnership firm the company owns 90% of. The MD and a director own the other 10%.
6. Danya's figures are not audited. A note in the filing still shows two unfilled choices for who checked them.
7. Cash moved the wrong way. Operations used Rs 5.7 Cr. Cash fell to Rs 0.25 Cr, and net debt rose 42% to Rs 57 Cr.
8. On the call, the CMD raised the FY27 sales target to Rs 300-350 Cr. The filed deck and press release give no number. The last two targets were missed (company memory).
9. The good news: orders are strong. The company won Rs 209 Cr of new orders in the half. The order book is Rs 566 Cr.
10. The worries: falling margin, negative cash flow, the Danya structure, and test certificates that larger tenders need and the company does not yet hold.
11. The new plant runs at only 20-30% of capacity. Management expects 40-50% by March 2027 (ASR).
12. Watch next: cash flow in the second half, the Rs 15.8 Cr warrant payment due by 27-Feb-2027, and whether FY27 sales reach Rs 300 Cr.
