## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Rajapalayam Mills spins cotton yarn and weaves and processes fabric at four plants around Rajapalayam, Tamil Nadu. It runs 1,40,160 spindles, 2,960 rotors and 328 looms (CARE-Jul26, p.4). In FY26 yarn sold Rs 587.67 crore and fabric Rs 340.68 crore (AR26, p.49 to p.50). Its 35.15 MW of windmills supply 56% of its power (AR26, p.50). It is also the flagship holding company of the Ramco group. It holds equity-accounted stakes in The Ramco Cements, Ramco Industries and Ramco Systems (Q2, p.11). In FY26 those stakes contributed Rs 137.10 crore to a consolidated profit of Rs 114.36 crore, while the textile company lost Rs 15.12 crore (Q2, p.2 and p.8).

**What is changing?** The mix is moving from yarn to fabric. Fabric revenue rose 41%, from Rs 241.37 crore to Rs 340.68 crore. Fabric exports rose from Rs 57.44 crore to Rs 149.67 crore (AR26, p.50). The textile segment went from a loss of Rs 9.17 crore to a profit of Rs 29.70 crore (AR26, p.188).

**Why now?** H1 FY27 standalone profit after tax was Rs 20.83 crore. H1 FY26 was a loss of Rs 3.76 crore (Q2, p.2). Revenue grew 30% in the half. This is the first profitable half after two loss years.

**What must be true?** Operating margin must stay above 15% without help. Debt must start to fall. CARE names exactly these two as upgrade triggers (CARE-Jul26, p.1).

**What breaks it?** Leverage. Gross debt is Rs 1,170 crore against Rs 437 crore of equity (Q2, p.3). Interest cover was 1.34x in FY26 (CARE-Jul26, p.2). Repayments of Rs 126 crore fall due in FY27 and need refinancing (CARE-Jul26, p.3). A cotton spread reversal stops the climb before the balance sheet heals.

## 2. Business model and archetype

FY26 revenue was Rs 942.04 crore (Q2, p.2). Yarn was about 62% and fabric 36% (AR26, p.49 to p.50). CARE puts exports at 30% to 40% and the top 10 customers at about 50% of sales (CARE-Jul26, p.2). Cotton is 50% to 60% of production cost (CARE-Jul26, p.3).

Margin comes from the yarn spread, fabric value-add and captive wind. In FY26 the windmills earned Rs 29.61 crore of segment profit, the same as the whole textile segment at Rs 29.70 crore (AR26, p.188). From Q1 FY27 power is folded into textiles and no segment is disclosed (Q2, p.5).

**Archetype: commodity converter (Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds), with a holding-company layer on top.** The listed equity value sits in the associates, not the mill. Any later valuation must split the two.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Moat absent. Cost advantage is forming. Captive wind covering 56% of power is a real cost edge in Tamil Nadu (AR26, p.50). Group buying, marketing and quality control give scale (CARE-Jul26, p.1). Pricing power is absent. Value-added yarn volume fell to 2,800 tonnes from 3,255 tonnes (AR26, p.49). Customer qualification with brands for processed fabric is the one switching cost that could form. The corpus names no customer.

## 4. The promoters

Promoter and promoter group hold 56.27% through 14 holders (AR26, p.101). The Ramco group is chaired by P.R. Venketrama Raja, who also chairs RML (Q2, p.5; CARE-Jul26, p.2). CARE records group-articulated support and zero default across nine decades (CARE-Jul26, p.2). Promoter pledge on RML shares: NOT FOUND, searched in the AR26 shareholding pages.

Related-party dependence runs both ways (AR26, p.187):
- RML guarantees Rs 200 crore of loans for Sandhya Spinning Mill, up from Rs 160 crore. Sandhya is rated CARE BB+ (CARE-Jul26, p.1).
- RML borrows Rs 1.65 crore from the Chairman and Rs 7.40 crore from Thanjavur Spinning Mill.
- RML has lent Rs 9.50 crore to Ramaraju Surgical Cotton Mills.

RML pledges its associate shares to fund itself. A Rs 75 crore loan carries pledged shares worth Rs 188.10 crore (AR26, p.123). Between 40% and 45% of total debt is secured on these investments (CARE-Jul26, p.3). Dividend is Rs 0.50 a share (AR26, p.199).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 commodity price-taker, in yarn. TO R2 cost-advantaged converter, through captive power, integration and fabric. One rung, which fits the base rate.

Management's claim: "The Company's modernization of spinning mills and forward integration through the establishment of fabric manufacturing and processing facilities are expected to drive top-line growth and protect margins during the current year" (AR26, p.52).

ROCE of 5% sits far below R2's mid-teens (AR26, p.51). The rung is claimed, not reached. The consolidated bottom line is a separate R0-type layer: associate earnings, not RML's own operations.

## 8. Proof check

Revenue proof is present. Q2 FY27 revenue rose 23.9% year on year (Q2, p.2). Margin proof is thin. The H1 margin rose one point, and Q2 was flat on Q2 FY26. Balance-sheet proof is absent. Borrowings rose to Rs 1,170.44 crore from Rs 1,141.47 crore in March 2026 (Q2, p.3). The proof point the thesis needs is two quarters above 15% with debt falling. It has not printed.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- Consolidated profit depends on associates. Q3 FY26 associate share derives to Rs 67.45 crore, 49% of the FY26 total. The corpus does not explain it (Q1, p.6; Q2, p.8).
- Associate share is falling: Rs 16.23 crore in Q2 FY26, Rs 12.53 crore in Q2 FY27 (Q2, p.8).
- A non-cash "fair value movement and financial guarantee obligation" item of Rs 18.97 crore is added back in H1 FY27. FY26 had a Rs 16.33 crore credit (Q2, p.4). Its P&L line is not shown, and it can move the margin by about 3 points.
- The Q1 FY27 segment merger changed the power-cost presentation. Power and fuel went from Rs 4.15 crore to Rs 14.44 crore year on year (Q2, p.2 and p.5).
- Rs 200 crore guarantee to a BB+ group mill (AR26, p.187).
- No current tax. Book tax is all deferred. Deferred tax is a key audit matter. So are receivables and investment valuation (AR26, p.112 to p.114). The opinion is unmodified.
- CRISIL cut to A/Negative in April 2025. The company then had that rating withdrawn in August 2025, after CARE assigned A/Stable in May 2025 (05-CRISIL, 03-CRISIL, 04-CARE).

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

CARE A/Stable, reaffirmed on 8 July 2026. CARE confirms the FY26 margin improvement "as envisaged". It expects further improvement from exports, mix and cotton spread (CARE-Jul26, p.1). It also calls the capital structure leveraged and debt cover weak, with a "tightly matched" debt-service ratio. Working-capital use averaged 94%. The operating cycle ran 204 days and the current ratio was below one (CARE-Jul26, p.3). Liquidity rests on Rs 1,524 crore of unencumbered quoted investments, out of Rs 3,299 crore total (CARE-Jul26, p.3). Upgrade needs a margin above 15% sustained. Downgrade triggers are a margin below 12%, or encumbrance on group shares above 75%. CRISIL last held it at A/Negative before the withdrawal.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** The margin is just above CARE's 15% line for one half. Debt is rising.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, contested.** The fabric capex debt fits management's account (AR26, p.200). The group guarantee and the habit of refinancing lean structural.
- **Recognition gap: OPEN, of the holding-company kind.** The Bull AI company record, dated 2026-11-04, gives a market capitalisation of Rs 650 crore. It is a service record, not a filed document. Quoted investments are worth Rs 3,299 crore against Rs 1,170 crore of gross debt. That discount is long-standing. It does not show that a climb has gone unpriced.

**Posture: RESEARCH / WATCH.**

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The mill is improving: fabric is up, wind cuts costs, and the half is profitable. But the operating proof is one seasonal half, and the balance sheet has not moved. The cheap headline multiple comes from Ramco Cements, not the mill.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. Where the Rs 18.97 crore H1 fair-value and guarantee item sits in the P&L. It decides whether the H1 margin is 16% or about 19%.
2. How much of Q2 standalone PBT is dividend income.
3. Why the Q3 FY26 associate share reached Rs 67.45 crore.
4. Whether the FY27 refinancing of Rs 126 crore is done, and how much group stock is now encumbered.

**What would change the view**
- Q3 FY27 margin above 15% with borrowings falling: PROCEED.
- Margin back toward 12%, or new guarantees to weak group mills: PASS.

No Role 1 valuation and no price. This verdict only decides `/step1` eligibility.
