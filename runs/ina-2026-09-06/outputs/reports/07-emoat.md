### 2C Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic

Per the CFO's own figures on the Jun-2026 call, total capex under execution for FY27 = Rs1,500cr (cell line, including contingency/working capital) + Rs1,000cr (KUSUM IPP) = **Rs2,500cr**.

Historical fixed asset turnover, FY2026 (consolidated): Revenue Rs2,163.52cr / Closing net PPE Rs473.77cr (both AR p.116 / Inv. Pres. slide 30-31) = **4.57x**.

Implied incremental revenue = Rs2,500cr x 4.57 = **Rs11,420cr**, i.e. **+428% above FY2026 revenue** of Rs2,163.52cr.

This number is not credible and the report flags it as such rather than presenting it as a finding: a 4.57x turnover measured on a barely-utilised, mostly-under-construction asset base mechanically overstates what new capacity can produce once actually built, because the denominator itself is immature. The company's own (already-cut) FY28 revenue target of "more than Rs5,000cr" (Q4 FY26 call, down from the original Rs8,500cr+ FY28 vision given on the Jun-2025 call) implies only **+131%** above FY2026 revenue over the same horizon — itself unproven, but at least internally consistent with management's own segment build-up (module Rs5,000cr + cell Rs3,000-3,500cr + aluminium Rs700-800cr, stated on the Feb-2026 call, which arithmetically sums to well above Rs5,000cr and was never reconciled with the cut FY28 headline number). Both figures are CLAIM-grade; neither should be read as a forecast.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct: 428** (mechanical FAT result, flagged above as an overstatement; the more conservative management figure of +131% is the one this analyst would weight if forced to choose).

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

**FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY**

- **A1 rare manufacturing capability**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Management's own words undercut any rarity claim: "lots of other companies are also coming with the cell manufacturing" (Jun-2025 call); ~100GW of TOPCon/G12R capacity is already registered nationally, with government tenders absorbing most of it by management's own estimate (Feb-2026 call). This is a capacity race with many entrants, not a scarce capability.
- **A2 patent and IP pipeline**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND, and the negative is DOCUMENTED. BRSR discloses R&D expense at **0.00% of total R&D+capex investment for FY2026** (0.00083% in FY2025) (AR p.96/97). The "Intellectual Property" bullet in the MD&A PESTLE analysis (AR p.36) is generic industry commentary, not a company-specific IP claim. BRSR's traditional-knowledge IP disclosure is "Not applicable" (AR p.109).
- **A3 process innovation**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No yield or unit-cost data disclosed anywhere. The cell-line "80%+ plant efficiency" target (Jun-2026 call) is standard TOPCon-class efficiency, not a proprietary process claim, and is unbuilt.
- **A4 product platform / modular architecture**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Mono PERC and TOPCon M10R/G12R lines are industry-standard SKUs; no evidence of accelerating launch cadence or shared architecture beyond what any module assembler runs.

**FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN**

- **B1 backward integration and RM security**: MODERATE evidence, DOCUMENTED in physical/financial terms. Cell facility under construction (PEB erection 100%, civil work 100%, machinery FAT 60%, capex financed 70%, Inv. Pres. slide 13); IREDA has sanctioned Rs1,134cr with Rs340cr drawn (Jun-2026 call, AR-adjacent loan facility). This is the single most real emerging-moat candidate in the entire scan. It is discounted for: (i) COD stated on three different bases across the corpus (Q3 FY27 / Q4 FY27 / "Dec 2026"); (ii) the backward integration itself depends on a Chinese equipment and technology partner with two years of on-site Chinese-Indian joint support before "handover" (Jun-2026 call) — i.e., import dependence is being relocated from finished cells to cell-making equipment and know-how, not eliminated; (iii) it is funded on rising leverage against negative operating cash flow (established context: FY26 CFO -Rs73.13cr).
- **B2 qualification lock-in**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND as a *moat*. ALMM/BIS/IEC/TUV/UL/CE, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 certifications are all DOCUMENTED (AR p.2-3, p.49, p.93) but are baseline regulatory requirements every ALMM-listed peer manufacturer also holds — they gate market access, they do not lock in a specific customer via sole-source language, customer-funded tooling, or a multi-year post-qualification agreement. Scored 0 to avoid crediting table-stakes compliance as differentiation (completionist guard).
- **B3 supply chain network effect**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND.

**FAMILY C — CUSTOMER**

- **C1 customer ecosystem / embedded relationships**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND, and the dominant customer relationship is a structural risk, not a moat: 79.74% of the parent's own revenue is modules sold to its own subsidiary (established context), an intra-group flow, not a demonstrated third-party embedded relationship. No AMC, cross-sell, or co-development evidence exists for any named external customer.
- **C2 customer concentration improving**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND; the DOCUMENTED trend runs the other way. Dealer/distributor count fell from 93 to 82 and dealer-channel sales share fell from 12.44% to 11.61% of total sales (AR p.96, BRSR, FY26 vs FY25). Separately, a new concentration risk emerged and was never discussed on any call: Solarworld Energy Solutions Ltd at 12.4% of FY26 revenue (established context, B05 red flag).

**FAMILY D — DATA & DIGITAL**

- **D1 proprietary data asset**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND.
- **D2 digital platform**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND.

**FAMILY E — GEOGRAPHIC & ACCESS**

- **E1 geographic first-mover**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Narmadapuram, MP land was secured (Jun-2025 call, CLAIM: 45 acres at Rs1/sq.m/year lease, subsidised power at Rs4.36/unit for 5 years, subsidised water, and a claimed 17-35% capex subsidy) but none of this incentive package appears anywhere in the AR's financial statements — no government grant, subsidy income, or capital-subsidy receivable line exists in the notes searched. It remains CLAIM-only. It is also not a "first" — peers are running comparable MP/Gujarat/Rajasthan expansions on similar terms per management's own peer comparisons (Feb-2026 call).

**FAMILY F — TALENT & ORGANISATIONAL**

- **F1 talent density**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No R&D headcount disclosed; R&D spend is 0.00% (AR p.96). KMP fixed remuneration is modest (Chairman Rs15.00 lakh, MD Rs14.27 lakh fixed component, AR p.85) — not indicative of a premium-technical-talent strategy.
- **F2 execution moat**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND; DOCUMENTED negative per the injected B05 promise-delivery record (Grade D: 1 delivered / 5 partial / 5 missed of 11 tracked promises, excuse pattern entirely external-blame, zero self-attributed error). Every FY26 guidance line missed (revenue -35%, PAT margin -180bps, PAT -33%). Cell-line COD stated inconsistently within a single call. The FY26 "cash accruals ~Rs220cr" claim was directly contradicted by an actual CFO of -Rs73.13cr and was never reconciled on any call. The aluminium frame capacity was quietly cut from 54,000 MT (Jun-2025 call) to 18,000 MTA (every subsequent disclosure, including the AR itself) with no acknowledgement of the revision anywhere in the corpus. This is the clearest possible failure of the execution-moat test.

**FAMILY G — FINANCIAL & STRUCTURAL**

- **G1 war chest**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND; DOCUMENTED negative. Borrowings rose from Rs108cr to Rs835cr in one year (Jun-2026 call); total debt including the IREDA facility is guided to a peak of ~Rs1,500cr in FY27 (Jun-2026 call); FY2026 operating cash flow was -Rs73.13cr against PAT of +Rs200.6cr (established context, B01). This is leverage-funded growth against negative cash generation, the opposite of a war chest.
- **G2 WC improvement trajectory**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND; DOCUMENTED negative. Working capital days rose from 31.48 (FY25) to 62.58 (FY26) (B01), driven by inventory (Rs76.98cr to Rs379.05cr) and receivables (Rs110.09cr to Rs281.59cr) both outrunning payables. Management attributes the receivables jump to a shift from advance payment to 45-day LC terms on larger orders (Jun-2026 call) — a plausible mechanical explanation, but it is a WORSENING trend regardless of cause, and receivables aged beyond 6 months are 39.5% of the book with zero ECL provision (established context).

**FAMILY H — ECOSYSTEM & EXTERNAL**

- **H1 industry consolidation beneficiary**: WEAK. ALMM/ALCM/ALMM Part 2 compliance-cost regulation is real and DOCUMENTED as government policy (referenced identically across all three calls and the presentation), and management's own figures (142GW registered ALMM capacity vs ~70-75GW "effective" TOPCon/G12R capacity, Feb-2026 call) imply a consolidation dynamic toward compliant manufacturers. But the benefit is explicitly shared with every ALMM/ALCM-compliant peer (Waaree, Premier, Websol are named by management as beneficiaries of the same dynamic on the same calls), management itself concedes a ~30GW non-ALMM-compliant overhang is *still* available in the market as of the Jun-2026 call (the B05 kill-signal for this trigger has not fired favourably), and the presentation's own slide 25 discloses an "ALMM-II exemption for eligible projects extended till Dec '26" — i.e., the regulatory net is being loosened for some projects even as management cites its tightening as a tailwind. ALCM's own effective date has been stated inconsistently across calls (1-Jun-2026 vs January 2026, B05 red flag).
- **H2 strategic partnerships**: WEAK-MODERATE, CLAIM-dominant. Named large customers (NTPC, SECI, SJVN) appear in the presentation (slides 16-18) and named recent orders (NTPC Rs558cr, MEIL/L&T Rs362cr, RREC Rs340cr, IPP+KUSUM AP Rs516cr — Inv. Pres. slide 25, dated Jul-2026, i.e. outside the FY2026 reporting period covered by the three concalls) are quantified and counterparty-named, which is stronger than a bare narrative claim, but this analyst has only the company's own presentation as the source, not the underlying exchange filing, and the company's promise-delivery record (Grade D) counsels treating even quantified company disclosures with real discount. The MP government incentive package (land/power/water/capex subsidy) remains CLAIM-only — no subsidy income or grant line exists in the AR. The China cell-technology partnership includes a documented two-year on-site support commitment (Jun-2026 call) but is itself evidence of continued import dependence, not of a partnership moat.
- **H3 ESG moat**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND beyond baseline certification already counted under B2. BRSR shows self-declared "100% sustainable sourcing" with no independent verification disclosed, a 450kW rooftop solar installation for office use (immaterial), and standard end-of-life recyclability claims common to every crystalline-silicon module maker. No SBTi commitment, no third-party ESG rating improvement documented.

**FAMILY I — STRUCTURAL ASYMMETRIES**

- **I1 talent asymmetry**: 0. Part (a) fails: no named inventors on any patent filing (none exist — R&D spend is 0.00%), no identified ex-DRDO/ex-HAL/global-major technical concentration among KMP (the independent directors' PSU/government backgrounds — ex-Discom, ex-GAIL, ex-Intelligence Bureau, ex-IAS — are governance appointments, not technical R&D hires), and KMP remuneration is below, not above, sector norms (Chairman/MD fixed pay ~Rs15 lakh each, AR p.85). Part (b) is therefore moot.
- **I2 cannibalization barrier**: 0. Applying the required test to every moat claimed in this scan (channel partners, MP incentives, ALMM compliance, cell backward integration): a well-resourced competitor does not need to destroy any internal pricing regime, channel, cost structure, or existing product line to copy any of it. Waaree, Premier Energies and Websol are cited by INA's own management as doing the *same* backward-integration and ALMM-compliance moves on the same timeline (Feb-2026 call). The honest answer is "nothing must be destroyed" — this is a capacity and timing race (execution lead), not a configuration-protected moat, and execution leads close, particularly against a Grade D execution record.
