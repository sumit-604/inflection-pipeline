## Business read

1. Product. Gelatine (about 75% of FY25 net sales) and its by-product di-calcium phosphate, DCP (the rest), plus ossein, from crushed animal bones. One plant near Jabalpur, MP. Capacity 2,400 MT a year gelatine and 10,600 MT a year DCP. CARE press release 06-Mar-2026, p.2-3. https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202603130313_Narmada_Gelatines_Limited.pdf
2. Scale. FY26 standalone sales Rs 215 Cr (+14%), operating profit Rs 41 Cr (OPM 19% vs 13% in FY25), PAT Rs 28 Cr vs Rs 17 Cr. TTM sales Rs 226 Cr at 20% OPM. Screener standalone P&L. https://www.screener.in/company/SHAWGELTIN/
3. Quarterly turn. Consolidated OPM went 13.7% (Dec-24), 15.0%, 16.4%, 18.3%, 18.8%, 22.3% (Mar-26), 20.8% (Jun-26). Q1 FY27 sales Rs 56.37 Cr, PAT Rs 9.33 Cr. Screener quarters table. Q2 FY27 results are due 12-Nov-2026 (BSE board meeting intimation 02-Nov-2026) and are NOT in this corpus.
4. History. Ten years of OPM between 3.3% and 16%, ROCE between 4% and 21%, 10-year sales CAGR 5%. FY26 ROCE 28%, the best in the series. Screener standalone ratios.
5. Margin source (claimed). CARE credits process improvement, a 2.15 MW solar plant, lower coal prices and higher DCP prices; PBILDT margin 12.96% FY25, 18.26% 9MFY26; CARE expects 18-20% near term. One aggregator quotes the FY26 MD&A: gelatine average price -5.20%, DCP +17.17%. CARE p.1.
6. Customers. Capsule and pharma makers: Geltec, ACG Associated Capsules, Fortcaps Healthcare. Top 10 customers about 42% of FY25 sales (39% FY24). CARE p.2.
7. Ownership change. Pioneer Jellice Group (PJG) and Ashok Matches bought 75% from Alfamont (Mauritius) in June 2023, with an open offer at Rs 303. Promoter 75.00%, no pledge; DII 0.01%, FII nil (BSE shareholding Sep-2026). CARE p.1, p.3; SEBI open offer filings.
8. Group deal. Dec-2024: NGL and the group bought about 53% of listed rival India Gelatine & Chemicals (IGCL); NGL holds about 14% (about 10 lakh shares at Rs 408, about Rs 40 Cr). The group's open offer for IGCL closed by Apr-2025. IGCL is an associate; share of profit Rs 3.55 Cr in FY26. CARE p.1; Q4 FY26 audited results; Business Standard 06-Dec-2024. https://business-standard.com/companies/news/pioneer-jellice-and-ashok-matches-launch-open-offer-for-igcl-stake-124120601185_1.html
9. Capex. Rs 33 Cr over two years, Rs 25 Cr term loan plus accruals. Adds 600 MT gelatine (+25%) and 1,500 MT DCP. Commercial production from Jan-2027. CARE rates a Rs 31.35 Cr term loan to Mar-2030. CARE p.2-3.
10. Cash. FY26 consolidated CFO Rs 25 Cr against PAT Rs 31 Cr (CFO/EBITDA 79%). Inventory 156 days, working capital days 97, debt Rs 8 Cr. FY23 dividend payout 396%: reserves fell from Rs 139 Cr to Rs 88 Cr in the year before the sale. Screener cash flow, ratios, P&L.
11. Rating. CARE BBB; Stable / CARE A3+ reaffirmed 06-Mar-2026. Cash credit Rs 20 Cr, about 14% average use.
12. No earnings calls and no investor presentations on record. concalls_available: false.

THE ONE ENGINE: a margin reset from a 10-13% band to about 20% under Pioneer Jellice ownership (group know-how, export access, imported bones, solar power), with a 25% gelatine capacity add from Jan-2027 on top. The open question is whether the reset is structural (group integration, cost position) or cyclical (DCP price, coal, bone prices).
