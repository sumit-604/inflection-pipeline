# DEEDEV Step 1 business brief (2026-11-15)

DEE Development Engineers Ltd (DDEL). NSE: DEEDEV. BSE: 544198. Listed on NSE and BSE on 26 June 2024 at an issue price band of Rs 193-203 (https://upstox.com/ipo/dee-development-engineers-ipo/; scrip code and symbol: BSE filing 11 Nov 2026, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=74e7b622-d2b3-4b57-8643-601fc8600fea.pdf).

## BUSINESS

1. DDEL makes process piping systems to customer specification: high-pressure piping, prefabricated pipe spools, fittings, induction bends, pressure vessels and modular skids. It sells to power (including nuclear), oil and gas, refining, petrochemical and fertiliser projects. Heavy fabrication (wind towers, stacks) runs in subsidiary DFIPL at Anjar. Two Punjab biomass plants (Abohar 8 MW, Muktsar 6 MW) and a 72,000 MTPA pellet unit form the non-core power arm (Q1 FY27 investor presentation pp. 15, 21-23, 27, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=58826f6f-b253-45d8-892f-135ce2f753ff.pdf).
2. Segment mix FY26 (gross segment revenue, Rs 1,152.85 cr): piping Rs 1,009.40 cr (87.6%), heavy fabrication Rs 80.49 cr, power Rs 58.84 cr, unallocated Rs 4.13 cr. Piping earned all segment profit: EBIT Rs 152.83 cr against a power loss of Rs 9.26 cr (Q2 FY27 results, consolidated segment page, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=0f6763ea-da21-4c04-b688-3620cdc11cb2.pdf).
3. Margin source. Steel pipe is ~50% of FY26 operating income. Contracts are fixed price with no escalation clause, so DDEL buys ~50% of order value in material at order receipt and imports 60-70% under LC. Margin comes from engineering and fabrication value add on difficult metallurgy, and from utilisation (CARE rationale 9 Nov 2026 pp. 2-3, https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202611131156_DEE_Development_Engineers_Limited.pdf). FY26 PBILDT margin 17.77% (FY25 16.85%) (CARE p. 2).
4. Customers. GE, BHEL, Doosan, L&T and Thermax. Top-10 customers were ~80% of FY26 operating income, up from 59% in FY25 (CARE p. 2). Management says exports have historically run above 50% of revenue and plans ~50/50 for FY27 (Q1 FY27 transcript, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=d14e2ad4-3cf2-494f-91af-0701ba55cc00.pdf, p. 11; management claim, not a filed split). Filed export share: NOT FOUND in this pass.
5. Order book Rs 2,531.04 cr at 30 Sep 2026, against Rs 1,228.17 cr at 31 Mar 2025; 2.20x FY26 operating income; 12-15 month execution; power ~65%, oil and gas plus others ~35% (CARE pp. 1-3). It reached Rs 2,586.43 cr at 31 Oct 2026; FY27 inflow to 31 Oct was Rs 1,348.52 cr (BSE monthly order update 13 Nov 2026, https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=2bc19af2-8c1c-4e90-92ea-089d6872cd3d.pdf).
6. Capacity. Piping 93,500 MTPA and heavy fabrication 32,400 MTPA across 7 plants: Palwal 36,000 MT (3 units), Anjar pipe fabrication 30,000 MT (scaled from 6,000 by Sep 2025), Anjar seamless pipe 7,000 MT (commercial production March 2026), Numaligarh 6,000 MT, Bangpakong (Thailand) 14,500 MTPA (Q1 FY27 presentation pp. 6, 23-24). Barmer facility: NOT FOUND in any source fetched. FY27 capex plan ~Rs 150 cr, of which ~Rs 40 cr term loan (CARE p. 3). Management calls the expansion capex "majority behind us" (presentation p. 13).
7. Q2 FY27 print (quarter to 30 Sep 2026, board 14 Nov 2026, limited review by S.R. Batliboi). Revenue Rs 292.99 cr, +8.5% YoY (Rs 270.00 cr), -0.5% QoQ (Rs 294.46 cr). PAT Rs 23.45 cr, +31.8% YoY (Rs 17.80 cr). Piping EBIT Rs 44.11 cr on Rs 273.55 cr. Power segment revenue fell to Rs 4.64 cr from Rs 18.37 cr in Q1. H1 FY27 revenue Rs 587.45 cr; H1 operating cash flow -Rs 6.79 cr against PBT Rs 49.92 cr, as receivables rose Rs 63.42 cr and inventories Rs 57.01 cr (results pp. 4, 6, 7, URL in item 2). Derived from the same page: Q2 operating EBITDA Rs 58.90 cr (revenue less expenses before finance cost and depreciation), 20.1% margin.
8. Guidance. FY27 revenue Rs 1,500 cr plus and EBITDA margin above 19%; Rs 1,150-1,250 cr of it from piping (Q1 FY27 transcript pp. 8-9). Vision 2030: Rs 2,500 cr revenue, 19-20% EBITDA margin, PAT 9-10% of revenue by FY30 (presentation p. 29). Working capital cycle 261 days in FY26; management targets 190-200 days from FY27 (CARE p. 3).
9. Balance sheet event. Preferential issue July 2026: 59,76,096 shares at Rs 502, Rs 300 cr gross, Rs 225 cr used to repay short-term borrowings (results note 4, p. 8). CARE upgraded long-term rating to CARE A; Stable from CARE A-, short-term to CARE A1 from A2+, on 9 Nov 2026 (CARE p. 1).
10. Governance and contingent items. Auditor Emphasis of Matter on the Abohar tariff: PSERC cut the tariff to Rs 5.88/unit retrospectively from 1 Jan 2024, implying Rs 13.85 cr refundable; DDEL has made no adjustment as the High Court stayed the order (results note 5 and review report para 7, pp. 2, 8-9). Management cannot assess impairment on Rs 50.63 cr of MPPL assets (note 6, p. 9). Loans to subsidiaries Rs 41.82 cr and guarantees Rs 9.76 cr at 31 Mar 2026 (CARE p. 3). Promoter holding fell to 65.13% (Sep 2026) from 70.18% (Mar 2026), consistent with the preferential issue (screener, https://www.screener.in/company/DEEDEV/consolidated/).
11. THE ENGINE. Utilisation of the new Anjar pipe fabrication (30,000 MT) and seamless pipe (7,000 MT) capacity on a power-heavy order book (BHEL and L&T thermal orders, ~65% of book). Power orders convert faster than oil and gas orders. The thesis rests on that mix lifting margin from 17.8% toward 19-20% and cutting the cash cycle from 261 days toward 190-200 days. The Q2 operating margin of 20.1% is the first print in that zone. H1 operating cash flow of -Rs 6.79 cr is the observation that has not yet moved.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS

1. Guidance vs delivery. FY27 guidance is Rs 1,500 cr plus revenue at above 19% EBITDA margin. H1 delivered Rs 587.45 cr, so H2 must deliver about Rs 913 cr, which is +55% over H1. Management said ~Rs 25 cr of Q1 revenue was deferred and would land in Q2, yet Q2 revenue was Rs 292.99 cr, below Q1's Rs 294.46 cr. Verify against the 17 Nov 2026 transcript and the H2 print.
2. Cash conversion. H1 FY27 operating cash flow -Rs 6.79 cr on PBT Rs 49.92 cr. FY26 operating cycle 261 days (inventory 227, debtors 100, creditors 66). Management and CARE expect 190-200 days from FY27. Verify the FY26 AR cash-flow statement and the 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet.
3. Abohar tariff contingency and MPPL carrying value. Rs 13.85 cr of potential refund to PSPCL is unprovided under a High Court stay, flagged by the auditor as Emphasis of Matter. Rs 50.63 cr of MPPL assets carry an unassessed impairment. Verify the FY26 AR contingent-liability and related-party notes, plus the Rs 41.82 cr subsidiary loans and Rs 9.76 cr guarantees.
4. Concentration. Top-10 customers ~80% of FY26 operating income (59% in FY25) with BHEL and L&T driving power inflow. Verify the AR customer note and the order book split by customer.

## PEERS

No listed pure-play process piping fabricator exists in India in the sources fetched. These are the closest structural comps. All three hold quarterly calls (screener Concalls list shows Nov 2026 entries for each). URLs verified OK 200 with tools/check_url.py on 2026-11-15.

1. ANUP, The Anup Engineering Ltd, https://www.screener.in/company/ANUP/consolidated/. Why it fits: order-book fabricator of heat exchangers, pressure vessels, reactors and columns for oil and gas, petrochemical, fertiliser and power projects; same engineered-to-spec, fixed-price, metallurgy-led engine.
2. ISGEC, ISGEC Heavy Engineering Ltd, https://www.screener.in/company/ISGEC/consolidated/. Why it fits: makes process plant equipment and boiler pressure parts and executes boiler and power plant projects; shares DDEL's power-OEM order cycle. Caveat: it also carries EPC and a sugar subsidiary.
3. RATNAMANI, Ratnamani Metals & Tubes Ltd, https://www.screener.in/company/RATNAMANI/consolidated/. Why it fits: stainless and carbon steel pipe and tube maker selling into oil and gas, power and process industries on a disclosed order book; mirrors DDEL's input cost and its new seamless-pipe backward integration. Caveat: a pipe maker, not a spool fabricator.
