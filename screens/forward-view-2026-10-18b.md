# Forward view: 2026-10-18 second shallow screen, thirteen names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework (operator ruling 2026-09-21). Next
year's bottom line, the growth rate, and the forward price-to-earnings ratio, so
the run can be ranked on growth against multiple.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result. Four names below screen cheap and mislead, and
each is marked.

Mahamaya Steel was on the operator's list for this batch. It was screened
earlier the same day, from the same corpus, and is in
`screens/forward-view-2026-10-18.md` (PASS, 172x to 238x). It was not repeated.

## The four that mislead

- **VALIANTLAB.** The 8.7x end annualises one strong quarter. India Ratings
  ties Q1 FY27 to cheap inventory and spot prices and calls it "unlikely to
  sustain". FY26 was a loss.
- **SOLARA.** The profit is untaxed. At a 25.17% tax rate the multiple is 40.8x
  to 52.5x. A quarter of FY26 revenue went to Strides, a promoter-group company.
- **SAGARDEEP.** Interest, asset-sale gains and rent made 51% of FY26 profit
  before tax and 92% of FY25. The operating business earns near its cost of debt.
- **JAYNECOIND** is cheap for named reasons, not hidden ones: the MD's
  conviction is under appeal, 99.87% of promoter shares are pledged to the NCD
  holders, and about 31% of the Q2 profit gain is lower interest. Read it as
  WATCH, not as a bargain.

## Notes per name

- **SILKFLEX.** Tier A guide. The thesis rests on the manufacturing margin,
  which the CFO could not reconcile on the April 2026 call. H1 FY27 decides it.
  Diluted for the proposed preferential issue: 18.0x to 22.8x.
- **DHANBANK.** A lender: price to book 0.93x on RoE of 6.97%. Gold loans rose
  from 31% to 46% of net loans.
- **NATFITTING.** The post-scheme multiple cannot be computed: the merging
  promoter company's profit is NOT FOUND, and the scheme issues 78% new equity
  to the promoters.
- **SINGERIND.** The high end of earnings leans on a one-off government order
  and on other income, which was 40% of FY26 profit before tax.
- **MONOLITH.** Tier A guide, but the company missed its last two revenue guides.
  A Rs 157 crore take-or-pay mine commitment starts in early 2027.
- **SHARVAYA.** H2 FY26 annualised, after the EBITDA margin fell to 2.9%.
- **MCLEODRUSS.** Loss-maker. The gate to profitability is EBITDA of about
  Rs 146 crore against a best recent year of Rs 24.36 crore.
