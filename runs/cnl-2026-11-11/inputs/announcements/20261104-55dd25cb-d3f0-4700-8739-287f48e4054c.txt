Subject: Outcome of the Meeting of Board of Directors pursuant to Regulation 30 of

Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) and the applicable 
SEBI Master Circular, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of  Creative 
Newtech Limited (“Company”), at its meeting held today, i.e. Wednesday, 04th November 
2026, has, inter alia, considered and approved the following: 
 
1. Acquisition of Zicom SaaS Private Limited: 
The Board has approved acquisition of the entire 100% paid-up equity share capital of 
Zicom SaaS Private Limited (“Zicom SaaS”), for an aggregate proposed consideration of 
approximately Rs. 50.50 crore (Enterprise Value), subject to definitive documentation, 
statutory and regulatory approvals and completion conditions. 
 
The proposed consideration comprises: 
(i) consideration of approximately Rs. 25.50 crore towards acquisition by way of share 
swap against proposed allotment of 2,25,000 equity shares of the Company of face value 
Rs. 10 each, on a preferential basis, for consideration other than cash, to Onyx SaaS Private 
Limited, at such price as may be determined in compliance with Chapter V of the SEBI (Issue 
of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 (“ICDR Regulations”) , subject 
to approval of the members of the Company. 
 
(ii) Balance through cash consideration of Rs. 25.00 crore. 
 
Upon completion of the acquisition of the entire shareholding, Zicom SaaS will become a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
