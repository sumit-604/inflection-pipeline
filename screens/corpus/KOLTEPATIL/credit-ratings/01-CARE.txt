Rationale and key rating drivers 
Reaffirmation of long-term instrument of Kolte-Patil Developers Limited (KPDL) derives strength from its operating track record 
of over 35 years and established market position. The company has developed over 68 residential, commercial, and township 
projects aggregating over 330 lsf of saleable area across Pune, Mumbai and Bengaluru. The rating also factors in strategic 
association with Blackstone, which holds a 40% stake in KPDL and exercises joint control. Blackstone, the world’s largest 
alternative asset manager, had global assets under management (AUM) exceeding US$1.3 trillion as of June 30, 2026.  
The rating reflects KPDL’s healthy operating performance regarding pre-sales and collection which witnessed substantial year-on-
year growth in Q1FY27. The Company also achieved significant growth in average realisation supported by price revisions and a 
higher contribution from Mumbai projects. The rating further derives strength from the company’s comfortable financial risk profile 
and strong liquidity. As of June 30, 2026, gross debt declined to ₹1,014 crore, while cash, cash equivalents and current 
investments stood at ₹960 crore. The company also had a sizeable project portfolio of ~380 lsf, with an estimated gross 
development value (GDV) of ₹34,400 crore, providing visibility for launches in the medium term.  
However, these rating strengths are constrained by execution and approval risks associated with the sizeable development 
pipeline, continued geographical concentration in Pune, relatively nascent stage of the Mumbai redevelopment portfolio and 
inherent cyclicality of the real estate sector. The addition of six redevelopment projects in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, aggregating 
~20 lsf, further increases importance of timely approvals and prudent execution. Timely execution of ongoing and planned 
projects, achievement of targeted sales and collections, and maintenance of comfortable liquidity and leverage amid expansion 
plans will remain key rating monitorable. 
Rating sensitivities: Factors likely to lead to rating actions 
Positive factors 
• 
Significant growth in overall booking and collections, with meaningful diversification across geographies and improvement 
in receivable coverage ratio to over 80% on a sustained basis. 
 
Negative factors  
• 
Significant decline in bookings and collections below ₹1800 crore due to lower-than-envisaged sales momentum.  
• 
Considerable decline in the percentage of committed receivables to cover balance project cost and outstanding debt.  
• 
Significant debt addition in ongoing/new projects leading to increase in gross debt/collection beyond 0.80x on a sustained 
basis. 
 
Analytical approach: Consolidated, while factoring linkages with Blackstone Group. 
CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has adopted consolidated approach for analysing KPDL. Subsidiaries/associates/joint 
ventures (JV) and the parent company, KPDL, have been consolidated being under a common management and having 
managerial, operational, and financial linkages. Entities whose financials have been consolidated are listed under Annexure-5. 
 
Outlook: Stable 
The stable outlook reflects CareEdge Ratings’ expectation that KPDL’s overall business risk profile will improve significantly on 
timely launch of projects and achievement of healthy bookings in the early stage of execution aligned with its past track record. 
This shall ramp-up the bookings and ultimately the company’s overall collections to scale up operations, without significant impact 
on the existing leverage profile.

Strong track record and established brand presence 
The company has an operating track record of over 35 years and an established position in the Pune residential market, with a 
growing presence in Mumbai and Bengaluru. As on June 30, 2026, the group developed over 68 residential, commercial, and 
township projects aggregating over 330 lsf of saleable area. As on June 30, 2026, the group’s ongoing development portfolio 
comprises projects with a total saleable area over 158 lsf. It caters to the mid-premium and premium segments through the Kolte-
Patil brand, the luxury segment through 24K, and large-scale township housing through Life Republic. The company’s diversified 
product offerings, strong sales traction and limited inventory in completed projects support its established market position.  
Strategic shareholding by the Blackstone Group 
In FY26, BREP Asia III India Holding Co VII Private Limited, a Blackstone affiliate, acquired a 40% stake in KPDL through a 
preferential allotment and secondary acquisition and now exercises joint control over the company. Blackstone is the world’s 
largest alternative asset manager, with global AUM exceeding US$1.3 trillion as of June 30, 2026, across real estate, private 
equity, credit, infrastructure and other alternative assets. The association strengthened KPDL’s board oversight, institutional 
processes, capital-allocation discipline and access to growth capital.  
Healthy sales traction and collections 
The company maintained healthy operating performance despite a relatively measured residential market. Collections in FY26 
grew steadily in FY26 to ₹2,689 crore achieving year-on-year growth of 11%. In Q1FY27, KPDL recorded pre-sales of ₹617 crore, 
while collections increased by 30% year-on-year to ₹715 crore. Average realisation improved by 29% year-on-year to ₹9,442 per 
sq. ft., supported by price revisions and a higher contribution from Mumbai projects. the launch of Project ‘Vyana at The Reserve’ 
at Vadgaon Khurd in September 2026, recorded sales exceeding ₹600 crore within ~60 hours, reflecting strong customer 
acceptance and brand positioning.  
Comfortable financial risk profile and strong liquidity 
The company’s financial risk profile remains comfortable, supported by strong liquidity and improving leverage indicators. As on 
June 30, 2026, gross debt declined to ₹1,014 crore from ₹1,142 crore as of March 31, 2026, while cash, cash equivalents including 
RERA balances and current investments stood at ₹960 crore. Consequently, annualised gross debt-to-collections remains 
comfortable at 0.35x. Proceeds from equity infusion of ₹417 crore by Blackstone is yet to be utilised fully and is held in the form 
of liquid investments. The company generated operating cash flow of ₹212 crore in Q1FY27 and fully redeemed ₹140 crore of 
secured unlisted non-convertible debentures (NCDs) issued to India Realty Excellence IV ahead of maturity in June 2026, and 
outstanding amount of ₹37.93 crore (Issue size: ₹110.90 crore) of secured listed NCDs issued to Marubeni Corporation.  
The company has moderate reliance on construction finance but has diversified its funding by raising NCDs from Marubeni 
Corporation, which account for over 40% of total debt outstanding as on June 30, 2026. These long-tenure instruments offer 
repayment flexibility, with collections from earmarked projects utilised for redemption and timely project execution and 
achievement of the envisaged sales realisations is important for payment of NCDs. NCDs shall be redeemed at premium, which 
is linked to collections made from sale of the earmarked units.   
For ongoing projects, balance funding requirement is expected to be comfortably met through a mix of customer advances and 
unutilised external borrowings. However, the company has significant expansion plans in pipeline and its ability to maintain a 
comfortable leverage profile with such expansion will remain monitorable.  
Sizeable project pipeline providing revenue visibility 
As on June 30, 2026, KPDL had 55.6 lsf of ongoing and unsold projects, along with 67.1 lsf under approval and a land bank of 
257.5 lsf. The sizeable pipeline provides strong launch and revenue visibility in the medium-to-long term, subject to receipt of 
timely approvals and calibrated execution.   
 
Key weaknesses 
 
Execution and approval risks associated with sizeable development plans 
As on June 30, 2026, KPDL had substantial area under development and in pipeline. The sizeable pipeline exposes the company 
to risks related to timely approvals, launches, construction progress, cost escalation and funding. Given the significant project 
launches in recent years, overall execution remains at an intermediate stage, with a sizeable portion of the project cost yet to be 
incurred. The balance cost is expected to be largely funded through customer advances. Committed receivables cover ~40% of 
the balance project cost and consolidated outstanding debt, indicating dependence on future sales and collections to meet the 
remaining funding requirements. Six redevelopment projects added across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, aggregating ~20 lsf and 
estimated GDV of ₹6,000 crore, entail rehabilitation, approval and execution risks. KPDL’s ability to sustain healthy bookings and 
collections while executing the planned expansion remains a key monitorable.

Geographical concentration in Pune 
As on June 30, 2026, Pune accounted for ~75% of GDV of KPDL’s project portfolio, and thus, the company is still exposed to 
geographical concentration risks related to slowdown, regulatory challenges or increased competition in the micro markets. While 
Pune remains KPDL’s core market, the company is strengthening its presence in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. In August 
2026, it added six society-redevelopment projects across Santacruz, Andheri-Lokhandwala, Oshiwara, Versova, Ghatkopar and 
Vashi. Projects are in established residential catchments and are expected to improve geographical diversification and increase 
the contribution from the higher-realisation Mumbai market. 
 
Inherent cyclicality and competition in the real estate sector 
KPDL remains exposed to cyclical nature of the residential real estate sector, which is sensitive to economic conditions, home-
loan interest rates, affordability and customer sentiment. Intense competition in Pune and Mumbai could exert pressure on pricing, 
sales velocity and inventory liquidation. These risks are partly mitigated by the company’s established brand and steady collection 
momentum as demonstrated by healthy Q1FY27 collections of ₹715 crore, up 30% year-on-year. 
Liquidity: Strong 
As of June 30, 2026, KPDL held cash, cash equivalents and current investments of ₹960 crore (including unutilised qualified 
institutional placements [QIP] proceeds to be utilised per defined objects of the issue, majorly for execution of new projects), 
against gross debt of ₹1,014 crore. The sizeable liquid balance and healthy collections provide adequate coverage for near-term 
project expenditure and debt-servicing obligations. The company has receivables from sold inventory over ₹2,400 crore, which 
provides cash flow visibility in the near term and partially covers balance project cost and outstanding debt. 
 
The debt profile is supported by a predominance of collection-linked term loans, while the zero-coupon NCDs are structured to 
be redeemed from collections generated through the sale of earmarked units, aligning debt repayments with cash flow generation 
from the underlying project. 
 
Assumptions/Covenants: Not applicable 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Environment, social, and governance (ESG) risks    
CareEdge Ratings believes that KPDL’s ESG profile supports its credit risk profile, despite environmental and social risks inherent 
in real estate construction. The company adopts sustainable materials, energy-efficient lighting, rainwater harvesting, wastewater 
treatment and renewable-energy solutions across its projects. Over 90% of treated wastewater is reused, while projects typically 
allocate 20-30% of the plot area to green cover and pursue IGBC, GRIHA and EDGE certifications. At Life Republic, 170 kW of 
the planned 300 kW solar capacity had been commissioned as of FY26. In FY26, the company achieved 13.6 million safe person-
hours, with zero fatalities and zero workdays lost due to safety incidents. It also conducted ~20,000 safety inspections and 1,696 
job-specific training programmes covering 43,894 participants. Governance is supported by a six-member board, including two 
independent directors, and board-level Audit and Risk Management Committees.
