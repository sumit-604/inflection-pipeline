# CMS Info Systems (CMSINFO) — Notion Save Payload

## BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

CMS Info Systems runs three businesses that move, guard, and automate physical cash for India's banks. The largest, ATM Management Solutions at 58% of services revenue, owns the machines, replenishes the cash, monitors the sites, and bills a fixed monthly fee on contracts of five to ten years. A bank cannot easily pull this in house, because CMS carries the equipment risk and runs a cash in transit network with 97% geographic coverage that took sixteen years to build. Retail Solutions and Currency Logistics, 26% of services revenue, collects cash from organised retailers and moves currency and bullion on mostly fixed fee terms, and it is the legacy labour network the company is now pruning. Technology and Payment Solutions, 16% of services revenue and up 42% in FY26, sells HAWKAI vision AI site monitoring, the ALGO cash forecasting software that runs on roughly 65,000 machines, and payment cards. The customers are a short list of large banks, State Bank of India, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and India Post Payments Bank, plus organised retail chains on the retail side. Banks buy through multi year tenders, and CMS won its flagship SBI mandate as the lowest qualified bidder, so switching costs and contract tenure hold the revenue more than pricing power does. Present demand rides on cash still in circulation and on the physical ATM estate, which the RBI currency in circulation series and the total India ATM count both track, and on the AGS Transact CIRP resolution, which is freeing share as a weaker rival exits. National cash fulfilment fell after the AGS collapse and CMS revenue dipped with it, so the national cash fulfilment rate signal tells whether that drag is a passing shock or a permanent step down. Demand should grow because CMS gains share as banks tighten credit on weaker operators, a claim the SBI, ICICI, HDFC, and IPPB contract ramp signals can confirm quarter by quarter. The externally verifiable evidence is real: ATM Management grew 5.3% in FY26 while national ATM withdrawal value fell about 7% on the RBI and NPCI series, a twelve point gap that only share gain explains. The counter signal is UPI transaction volume, tracked at NPCI, which measures how fast digital pulls demand off the cash rails over time. On competitive advantage, the moat sits mainly in ATM Management, where the scan credits a proprietary sixteen year transaction dataset across 150,000 touchpoints, route optimisation that cut costs over 10%, and embedded multi year bank contracts. Technology and Payment Solutions carries the platform and full stack hardware strengths, but the scan flags that ALGO's installed base is essentially one customer today and that no patent evidence backs the proprietary language, so its moat is promising rather than proven. Retail and Currency Logistics shows no durable moat in the scan, a labour network competing on price that is being shrunk by design. The industry consolidation benefit from the AGS exit is scored two sided and unresolved, a share gain that management itself concedes cuts both ways.

## Monitoring checklist

1. Consolidated post tax ROCE: above 22% in FY28 on flat capital fires the proof gate; below 20% with capex at guide is the falsifier.
2. One to two year overdue receivables bucket: moderating toward the FY25 level (about Rs 17.8 Cr consolidated) is green; widening while Cash Management revenue falls is red.
3. Standalone loss allowance cover vs aged receivables: cover rises with the bucket is green; diverges below 0.29x is red.
4. National cash fulfilment vs CMS services revenue: normalises AND revenue responds means the shock is temporary; recovers AND revenue does not means structural.
5. Services EBITDA margin: at or above 26.5% for two straight quarters confirms; sliding toward 24-25% is red.
6. Services revenue vs FY27 guide Rs 2,650 to 2,750 Cr: low teens YoY confirms; sub 5% YoY is stagnant.
7. Capex vs Rs 100-125 Cr guide: at or below is a one off build; above is a treadmill with capital rising.
8. ICICI ALGO go live on 10,000+ machines in FY27: live outside SBI validates the wedge; single customer past FY27 is red.
