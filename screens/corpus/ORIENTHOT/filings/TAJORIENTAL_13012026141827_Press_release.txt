PRESS RELEASE 
 
ORIENTAL HOTELS LIMITED (OHL) ANNOUNCES Q3 & NINE MONTHS FY 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS 
 
CHENNAI, JANUARY 13, 2026: Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL), an associate company of The Indian 
Hotels Company (IHCL), reported its results for the third quarter ending December 31, 2025.  
 STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2025 
 
 STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE NINE MONTHS ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2025 
 
Mr. Pramod Ranjan, Managing Director & CEO, Oriental Hotels Ltd. said, “In Q3 FY2026, OHL 
reported a revenue of INR 140.56 crores, 15% growth over the previous year. EBITDA for the quarter 
stood at INR 43.35 crores and a PAT of INR 20.23 crores, a 45% growth over last year.” 
 
He added, “The strong demand trend witnessed in our key markets of Chennai and Cochin will 
continue into the fourth quarter delivering a double-digit revenue growth for this fiscal.” 
 
KEY HIGHLIGHTS  
• 
IHCL’s iconic brand Taj is ranked as India’s Strongest Brand 2025 and World’s Strongest Hotel 
Brand 2025 as per Brand Finance-UK. OHL has 3 Taj branded hotels in its portfolio. 
• 
With a focus on promoting Environmental Stewardship under IHCL’s ESG+ framework of 
Paathya, OHL hotels renewable energy consumption stood at 61%. 
• 
Southern Spice and Golden Dragon at Taj Coromandel have been honored at the Hospitality 
Horizon Epicurean Restaurant Ratings 2025. 
About Oriental Hotels Limited  
 
Oriental Hotels Limited is an associate company of The Indian Hotels Company Limited. The company 
has seven hotels – Taj Coromandel, Chennai Taj Fisherman’s Cove Resort & Spa, Chennai Taj Malabar 
Resort & Spa, Cochin, Vivanta Coimbatore, Vivanta Mangalore; Gateway Madurai and Gateway 
Coonoor.
