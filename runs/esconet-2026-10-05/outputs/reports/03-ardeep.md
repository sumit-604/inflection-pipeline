## 0. READ FIRST

**Units.** Both ARs, the results filings and the prospectus print "Lakhs" on their face. Every figure is in INR Lakhs unless the line says Cr. 100 Lakhs = 1 Cr. The anchor tag names the unit: "L" means INR Lakhs, "Cr" means INR Crore, "%" and "x" are ratios.

**Anchor keys.** AR26 = FY2025-26 AR (page = PDF [page N] marker; the printed folio is N minus 2). AR25 = FY2024-25 AR (same marker rule). PROSP = prospectus dated 20 Feb 2024 (marker pages). R30-0228 = Reg 30 filing of 28 Feb 2026 (summons). R30-0429 = Reg 30 filing of 29 Apr 2026 (warrant lapse). RES-0528 = results outcome of 28 May 2026 (FY26 and half-years). Q1BM = results outcome of 12 Aug 2026 (Q1 FY27). Q1PR = Q1 FY27 press release, 12 Aug 2026. CALL26 = Aug 2026 concall transcript. SHP = NSE shareholding XBRL extracts (Mar-2026, Jun-2026). AGM-N = AGM notice of 24 Aug 2026. B02 = stage 2 output. "Derived" = my arithmetic on anchored inputs. [INFERENCE] = a reading the filing does not state.

**Page note.** B02 sometimes cites the printed folio. Where I checked, the PDF marker differs: Note 14 (related parties) is on AR26 p.117-118 (B02 says p.116), the fixed asset table is on p.116, the consolidated inventory note is on p.141 (B02 says p.139). I use markers throughout.

**Verdict labels.** CLEAN, WATCH, RED FLAG. They stand for the three tiers the instruction file marks green, yellow and red. Plain words keep the file free of symbols.

**Framework note.** Accounts follow Indian GAAP (AS under s.133), not Ind AS (AR26 p.105). No ECL matrix, no fair-value hierarchy. Silence on those is framework, not concealment.

## 0.2 FIRST VERIFICATION PRIORITIES (checked before the phases)

### Priority 1. Q1 FY27 margin jump: mix shift or one-time gain

The company reports EBITDA with other income inside it (Q1PR p.2; AR26 p.76). The only like-for-like pair is therefore 8.55% against 3.42% on that basis. I rebuilt every period on both bases.

(EBITDA = PBT + finance cost + depreciation, derived from each filing. Consolidated H1 depreciation is 116.36 L as printed. H2 is derived as 245.30 less 116.36 = 128.94 L, and it ties to the H2 total expenses of 20,460.75 L. The H1 total income prints 14,620.97 L in RES-0528 p.13. Revenue plus other income is 14,629.97 L and ties to the printed PBT of 168.43 L, so I treat the print as a typo. The two halves sum to the FY26 ex-other-income EBITDA within 0.07 L. Q1 consolidated EBITDA of 1,011.16 L ties to PBT 899.07 L plus 112.09 L of finance cost and depreciation, Q1BM p.7.)

What the numbers show:

1. **One-time other income does not explain the jump.** Strip all other income and the Q1 consolidated margin is 7.03% against 2.49% for FY26. Strip only the 184.23 L forfeiture (if it sits in Q1 other income) and the margin is 7.10% (derived: 826.93 / 11,641.67).
2. **Other income is odd either way.** Q1 other income was 193.21 L. The FY26 quarterly average was 85.91 L (343.62 / 4, derived). The forfeiture of 184.23 L (R30-0429 p.1; AR26 p.122) exceeds the 107.30 L excess. Reading A: the forfeiture sits in Q1 other income and the underlying run-rate fell to about 9 L. Reading B: the forfeiture went to reserves and other income more than doubled. The Q1 note only says other income "includes interest income, gain on sale/disposal of property, plant and equipment, and miscellaneous income" (Q1BM p.8). It does not name the forfeiture. Separating observation: the Q1 other income note or equity statement. NOT FOUND in the filings read.
3. **The lift is a standalone gross margin event.** Standalone gross margin (revenue less purchases and change in inventories) was 10.2% in H1 FY26, 16.9% in H2 FY26 and 29.2% in Q1 FY27 (derived from RES-0528 p.5 and Q1BM p.4; Q1 = 6,107.71 - (4,845.14 - 518.50) = 1,781.07 L). FY26 was 13.7% (AR26 p.103). The subsidiaries, by difference, earned about 0.71% ex-other-income EBITDA on 5,524.98 L of Q1 revenue, so they diluted the group margin. Standalone Q1 EBITDA of 968.93 L equals 86.9% of FY26 standalone company-basis EBITDA of 1,115.10 L, earned on 20.5% of FY26 revenue (derived).
4. **Management names two causes outside mix.** The MD said "the hardware price volatility has played some role in building up these margins" (CALL26 p.10) and "those inventory levels have contributed to some extent to our margins as well" (CALL26 p.12). He also called HexaData "a key enabler of margin expansions" (CALL26 p.18) but gave no HexaData revenue or margin.
5. **Inventory build travelled with the margin.** Standalone closing stock rose 826.72 L in FY26 and a further 518.50 L in Q1 FY27 (AR26 p.103; Q1BM p.4). Under FIFO at cost (AR26 p.106), stock bought before a price rise and sold after it widens gross margin. That gain is one-time in kind, not recurring.

**Two readings.** (A) Structural: own-brand HexaData servers and services carry roughly twice the trading gross margin and the mix is shifting. (B) Cyclical: hardware price inflation on stock bought earlier lifts gross margin until replacement stock costs more. **One observation separates them:** HexaData revenue and gross margin by product line, plus Q2 FY27 gross margin once the Q1 stock is replaced. Neither is in the AR. Q1 is also the first voluntary quarterly print and has no comparative quarter (Q1BM p.5).

Verdict on Priority 1: the 8.55% is not an other-income artefact. Whether it is mix or price remains unresolved, and it depends on the Q2 print. The 2.60% FY26 margin in company memory has no basis in the AR (B02 closing state). Use 3.42% (company basis) or 2.49% (ex other income).
