## 1. CASH FLOW STATEMENT — NOT EXTRACTED IN PASS 1 (the single biggest miss)
(Annexure III, standalone SFS7 / consolidated CFS7)

Pass 1 never pulled the Restated Statement of Cash Flows, even though it directly tests every working-capital
concern (receivables ageing, inventory build, DSCR<1x) Pass 1 flagged qualitatively. The standalone 3-year
trend:

| ₹ Cr | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBT | 2.34 | 20.60 | 34.98 |
| Operating Profit Before Working Capital Changes | 15.48 | 32.05 | 47.47 |
| Trade Receivables (increase)/decrease | +15.38 | **(52.94)** | (26.05) |
| Inventories (increase)/decrease | (9.86) | +4.52 | (16.64) |
| Trade Payables increase/(decrease) | (3.67) | +10.83 | +4.12 |
| **Net Cash from Operating Activities** | **+10.08** | **+3.46** | **(4.43)** |
| Net Cash from Investing Activities | +2.58 | +3.19 | (6.17) |
| Net Cash from Financing Activities | (20.64) | (7.48) | +12.70 |

🔴🔴 **Major new red flag.** Standalone net cash from operating activities went **negative in FY25
(₹(4.43) Cr)** even as reported PBT nearly tripled to ₹34.98 Cr. FY24's OCF of only +₹3.46 Cr already
masked a ₹52.94 Cr swing into receivables that year, offset only by an equally large trade-payables
stretch (+₹10.83 Cr). In FY25 the same pattern recurs (₹26.05 Cr into receivables, ₹16.64 Cr into
inventory) but this time payables could not offset it and operating cash flow flipped negative. Growth
in FY25 was funded almost entirely by the fresh equity raise (+₹26.90 Cr gross proceeds) and continued
short-term borrowing, not by the business's own cash generation. This is the hardest evidence yet for
FLAG-CASH and materially outweighs the company's own "improving" DSCR/current-ratio narrative — it should
be read together with, not in place of, Pass 1's DSCR<1x finding.

Consolidated FY25 cash flow (Annexure III, CFS7) confirms the same picture at group level: OCF
(₹4.45) Cr, Investing (₹6.16) Cr, Financing +₹12.71 Cr (fresh shares ₹26.91 Cr less short-term
borrowing repayment ₹11.58 Cr and interest paid ₹10.37 Cr).

## 2. UNREGISTERED LAND TITLE — PROMOTER PERSONAL NAME, PLEDGED AS BANK COLLATERAL, ON COMPANY BOOKS
(Additional Regulatory Information Annexure XLV item (i), standalone SFS39, cross-referenced to PP&E
Annexure XIV/XV and Borrowings Annexure XXXII)

🔴🔴 **Major new red flag, not in Pass 1.** The standalone regulatory-information note discloses one
piece of immovable property whose title deed is **not held in the company's name**:

> "Description of Property: Free Hold Land, Gross Carrying Value ₹872.97 Lakhs, Held in the name of:
> Mr. Dakshinamoorthy Venkatesan & Mr. Dakshinamoorthy Manikandan (Director), Period held: 2 years,
> Reason for not being held in the name of the company: Sale agreement entered between the company and
> parties during FY 2021-22, the same is in the process of registration."

This is the "Land-Nehru Nagar" line sitting on the company's own PP&E schedule at **₹8.73 Cr gross
carrying value** (Annexure XIV/XV) — roughly 3.4% of total consolidated assets. The transfer/sale
agreement dates to FY2021-22 and is **still unregistered four fiscal years later** (as of the FY25
restated balance sheet). Cross-referencing the borrowings terms note (Annexure XXXII), this same land
is listed as **collateral security pledged to Axis Bank** for the company's working-capital facility
("Land admeasuring 2.99 acres and factory building at mettupalayam road... owned by the company" / other
Axis Bank collateral schedules also list residential property "in the name of Mr. Venkatesan and Mr.
Manikandan" and a vacant plot "in the name of Mrs. V Revathy"). A material balance-sheet asset therefore
carries unclear legal title, sits with the promoter-MDs personally, and is simultaneously used to secure
company debt — a governance and asset-recoverability question that belongs alongside Pass 1's Section 185
and personal-guarantee findings, not a separate footnote.

## 3. FY23 RESTATED PROFIT WAS ~3x THE ORIGINALLY AUDITED FIGURE — DRIVEN BY A SINGLE ₹2.26 CR INTEREST REVERSAL
(Annexure IV Note 3, standalone reconciliation, SFS13)

Pass 1 flagged the restatement qualitatively but did not quantify the year-by-year swings. The standalone
net-profit reconciliation table shows:

| ₹ Lakhs | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAT as per originally audited accounts | 45.80 | 1,351.15 | 2,578.25 |
| Interest expense reversal | **+225.68** | – | – |
| Other adjustments (net) | (122.24) | +72.13 | (22.45) |
| **PAT as restated** | **149.36** | **1,423.28** | **2,555.80** |

🔴 **New finding.** FY23's restated profit (₹1.49 Cr) is **226% higher** than what was originally audited
(₹0.46 Cr), almost entirely because a ₹2.26 Cr interest expense that the prior auditor (G. Sekar
Associates) had booked was found to be wrong and reversed on restatement. FY23 is the base/anchor year
for every growth-rate calculation in this analysis (revenue, PBT, PAT CAGR), and its own profit figure
required a near-tripling correction. This materially raises the bar on trusting any pre-restatement
number from this company and specifically flags FY23 PAT/PBT as the least reliable data point in the
three-year window, even after restatement.

**Net worth reconciliation moves the other way**: standalone net worth as restated at FY23 (₹41.75 Cr) is
actually **₹0.81 Cr lower** than originally audited (₹42.56 Cr) (Annexure IV Note 4, SFS14) — implying
the cumulative negative adjustments to opening (pre-FY23) reserves were larger than the FY23-specific
profit correction. This is evidence the control weaknesses behind the restatement (gratuity, CSR, MSME
interest, statutory-dues interest) extend to periods **before** the three-year restated window even
starts, not just within FY23–FY25.

## 4. LIQUIDITY STRESS PREDATES THE RESTATED WINDOW — FY22 CURRENT RATIO ALREADY BELOW 1.0x
(Significant Accounting Ratios, standalone, SFS44 — company-disclosed, includes FY22 comparative)

| Ratio | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current Ratio | 0.95 | 0.99 | 1.12 | 1.44 |
| Net Capital Turnover Ratio | 14.80 | **(22.70)** | 15.24 | 4.74 |
| Trade Receivables Turnover | 2.03 | 1.69 | 1.59 | 1.68 |
| Inventory Turnover | 2.91 | 1.38 | 1.54 | 2.30 |

🟡 **New finding.** FY22 and FY23 both carry current ratios below 1.0x (current liabilities exceed current
assets), and FY23's Net Capital Turnover Ratio is **negative** (working capital itself was negative that
year). Pass 1's DSCR<1x and receivables-ageing findings are corroborated and extended one year further
back than the restated window's start: the trade receivables turnover ratio has been on a **four-year
declining trend** (2.03 → 1.69 → 1.59 → 1.68), not just the softening within FY23–FY25 that the ageing
buckets alone suggested. The improvement to a 1.44x current ratio by FY25 is real but is substantially
an artifact of the FY25 equity raise inflating current assets (cash), not of a structural working-capital
turnaround — consistent with Finding 1 above.

## 5. ADDITIONAL NEW ITEMS (moderate significance)

- **"Interest on Supplier Overdue/Loan from others"** is a distinct, sizeable Finance Cost line item not
  previously called out: ₹523.41 Lakhs (FY23), ₹350.61 Lakhs (FY24), ₹149.33 Lakhs (FY25) (Annexure
  XXVIII). The company has paid material interest to suppliers on overdue balances in every one of the
  three years, on top of the MSME-interest and statutory-dues-interest items Pass 1 already flagged — a
  third, independent strand of the same "chronic late payment" pattern, now declining but still ₹1.49 Cr
  in FY25. 🟡

- **V Revathi**, a relative of the promoter-directors, is a related party **not captured in Pass 1's RPT
  table**: receives rent (₹8.40 Lakh/year) and has an unsecured loan to the company outstanding ₹28.40
  Lakh (FY23) / ₹28.15 Lakh repaid (FY24) / nil (FY25) (Annexure XXXV). She also personally holds a
  vacant plot pledged as Axis Bank collateral (Annexure XXXII) — the same promoter-family
  asset-entanglement pattern as Finding 2. 🟡

- **Advertisement and Business Promotion Expenses** rose from ₹38.86 Lakh (FY23) to ₹107.21 Lakh (FY24)
  to ₹206.71 Lakh (FY25) — more than 5x in two years, for a B2B rail-component fabricator, coinciding
  with the pre-IPO period (Annexure XXX). Nature of spend is not disclosed. Worth a management question. 🟡

- **Raw-material imports (₹7.36 Cr CIF, Annexure XL/XLI) do not reconcile with the "Expenditure in
  Foreign Currency" note (Annexure XLII)**, which shows **NIL** for "Purchase of Components and spare
  parts" and every other category, in all three years, both standalone and consolidated. A direct
  inconsistency between two adjacent notes in the same annexure — possibly explained by imports routed
  through a domestic intermediary paid in INR, but this is not disclosed anywhere. 🟡

- **Statement of Tax Shelters (Annexure XXXVIII)** quantifies the growing "permanent difference" drag from
  disallowed compliance costs: ₹71.47 Lakh (FY23) → ₹123.36 Lakh (FY24) → ₹245.12 Lakh (FY25). A new
  line within this, **"Disallowance under section 36"** (₹29.62 / ₹35.80 / ₹32.73 Lakh across FY23–25),
  is typically driven by late-deposited employee PF/ESI contributions — a fourth strand of chronic
  late-statutory-payment behaviour, beyond the TDS and MSME interest Pass 1 already flagged. 🟡

- **EBITDA margin swung sharply and non-linearly**: 15.4% (FY23) → 29.0% (FY24) → 24.6% (FY25)
  (EBITDA ₹14.68 Cr / ₹34.58 Cr / ₹47.42 Cr per Annexure XXXVII). The swing is substantially explained by
  large, volatile "Changes in Inventories of WIP & Finished Goods" (₹(8.82) Cr FY23, +₹2.88 Cr FY24,
  ₹(20.48) Cr FY25 per Annexure XXVI) rather than steady operating leverage. Company's own explanatory
  note (SFS44) attributes the FY24 spike to "orders with high margin ratio" — an unverified,
  order-level claim with no supporting segment or customer disclosure. 🟡

- **CSR shortfall predates the restated window.** The opening CSR provision carried into FY23 was already
  ₹70.40 Lakh (i.e., unpaid CSR obligations existed before FY22/23), growing to ₹90.04 Lakh by FY24
  year-end before the ₹107.89 Lakh catch-up payment in FY25 (Annexure XLVI Note 2, SFS45). This extends
  Pass 1's CSR non-compliance finding further back than the "FY23–FY24" framing implied. 🟡

- **Consolidated summary ratios not extracted in Pass 1**: Return on Net Worth 23.06% (FY25 consol.),
  EBITDA ₹47.41 Cr (24.6% margin), Current Ratio 1.47 (Annexure XXXVII, CFS33) — useful anchors for later
  valuation stages, informational only. 🟢

- **Balance confirmations not available** for Earnest Money Deposit, Security Deposit and certain Fixed
  Deposits (Annexure XVII/XX, both standalone and consolidated) — the auditor relied on FD receipts as
  alternate evidence in lieu of third-party confirmation. Minor audit-evidence-quality note, not a
  standalone red flag but worth being aware of. 🟡
