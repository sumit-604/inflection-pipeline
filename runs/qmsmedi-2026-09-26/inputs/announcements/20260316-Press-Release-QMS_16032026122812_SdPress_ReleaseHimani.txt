QMS Medical Allied Services Appoints Himani Pruthi as Strategy & 
Marketing Business Unit Head 
Mumbai, March 16, 2026: QMS Medical Allied Services Ltd (QMS), a leading healthcare 
management solutions company, has announced the appointment of Himani Pruthi as 
Strategy & Marketing Business Unit Head. With over 15 years of experience in healthcare 
and pharmaceutical marketing, she brings extensive expertise in strategic brand planning, 
integrated marketing, patient support programs, and digital health transformation. 
In her new role, Himani will spearhead the Strategy & Marketing Business Unit at QMS, 
leading initiatives to strengthen the organisation’s strategic capabilities and advance integrated 
growth initiatives across QMS healthcare programs and platforms. She will also focus on 
building high-impact brand and marketing frameworks that support scalable patient-centric 
platforms and long-term value creation. 
Himani has held senior leadership roles at Publicis and MIMS, where she led multidisciplinary 
teams and drove large-scale healthcare marketing and strategic communication initiatives 
across diverse healthcare portfolios. Earlier in her career, she worked with Novartis, where she 
gained experience in pharmaceutical brand management and commercial strategy. 
She was recently recognised as part of BW Marketing World’s 40 Under 40 – Future Masters 
2025, acknowledging her contributions to strategic healthcare marketing and scalable patient-
centric platform development. 
Himani holds a Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy and an MBA in Marketing, combining strong 
scientific understanding with business strategy and brand leadership expertise. 
At QMS, she will oversee end-to-end marketing strategy across QMS patient service programs 
and healthcare initiatives, from insight generation and annual planning to the execution of 
integrated campaigns and patient support programs designed to drive measurable healthcare 
impact. 
About QMS Medical Allied Services Ltd 
QMS Medical Allied Services Ltd (QMS MAS) is a leading healthcare company specialising in 
innovative patient screening services and the supply of advanced diagnostic medical devices. Through 
its pioneering Patient Service Programs (PSP), QMS MAS provides patients nationwide with access to 
critical diagnostic tests for conditions such as Osteoporosis, Diabetes Mellitus, Chronic Obstructive 
Pulmonary Disease, and Hypertension. Committed to promoting early intervention and preventive 
healthcare, QMS MAS plays a vital role in improving patient outcomes and enhancing the overall 
healthcare experience. With a dedication to excellence and a forward-thinking approach, QMS MAS 
continues to expand its impact in the healthcare industry, delivering value-added services that benefit 
patients and healthcare providers alike.
