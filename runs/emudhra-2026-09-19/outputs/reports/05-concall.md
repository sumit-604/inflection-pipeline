### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **Enterprise Solutions mix shift**: strengthening every quarter. Q3 FY26: product offsetting US services softness. Q4 FY26: formal FY26 segment split (59% of revenue, +55% YoY, 23% organic/32% acquisitive). Q1 FY27: Enterprise Solutions ~50% YoY growth, 65% of total income, EBITDA margin at 26.2% (highest of the three quarters shown).
- **Cryptas cross-sell**: strengthening. Q3/Q4: "conversations ongoing," "8-9 conversations in progress." Q1 FY27: two named, closed wins (large German data centre via CertiNext; Austrian city municipality via emSigner/PrimeSign) — the trigger converted from narrative to evidence in one quarter.
- **US InCommon / product**: NEW in Q4 FY26 call (not named in Q3 FY26, though the underlying US data centre was already live in Q3). Strengthens further in Q1 FY27 with volume detail (1,000-1,500 certs/day).
- **UAE QTSP / data centre**: WEAKENING on timeline. Q3 FY26: "2-3 months" audit, implying commissioning around April-June 2026. Q4 FY26: no update given at all (silent quarter). Q1 FY27: still pending, "final step," now targeted ~Sep-Oct 2026. Flagged as a timeline slippage below.
- **Bolt-on AI-cybersecurity acquisition**: fading. Q3 FY26: live consideration, "6-9 months." Q4 FY26: "nothing in the pipeline... maybe 3rd or 4th quarter." Q1 FY27: not raised by management or any analyst — DROPPED from the conversation, no resolution stated either way.
- **Trust Services / India DSC-eSign**: reversed. Q3 FY26: optimistic, 22-25% FY26 growth guided. Q4 FY26: delivered 32% FY26 growth (beat). Q1 FY27: temporary DECLINE (FIPS 140-3 token recertification transition, channel destocking) — a tailwind that flipped to a headwind inside two quarters, though management frames it as transition-related and temporary.
- **NIS2/DORA (Europe)**: present in Q3 and Q4 with specifics (named regulations, named customer segments). By Q1 FY27, reduced to a generic line in the tailwinds list, no fresh specifics — flag as fading specificity, not necessarily fading substance.
- **Agentic AI**: NEW as framing in Q4 FY26 (AI as "capability layer"). Expands materially in Q1 FY27 into a full problem-statement answer, but management is candid that adoption and revenue are "still pretty early stage... hard to quantify what could be the opportunity right now" (Kaushik Srinivasan, Q1 FY27 call, p.19).

**Pattern check:**
- Blame externals while crediting themselves? Reasonably balanced — genuine,
  named external events (H-1B policy, FIPS 140-3 mandate, Middle East war)
  rather than vague "market conditions," and one setback (India Q4 dip) is
  explained with an internal, deal-timing reason rather than blamed outward.
- Ever say "we made a mistake"? Not found in any of the three transcripts.
  One live self-correction of a verbal slip exists (Q3 FY26, Venkatraman
  Srinivasan correcting "Rs 200 crore" to "Rs 400 crore" pipeline,
  Siddharth Mishra's question, p.13) — a minor positive honesty signal, not
  an accountability admission.
- Raise hard topics proactively or only under pressure? Mixed. The 3i
  Infotech matter was raised proactively via a dedicated special call within
  two business days of the allegation. The Q1 FY27 Trust Services decline
  was flagged in prepared remarks before any question. But the UAE DC delay
  and the stock-in-trade drag were both raised only when an analyst asked
  (Q3 FY26), and neither was volunteered again afterward.

  concalls are silent on both sides.

  discussed in any of the three calls; no analyst asks despite fixed assets

- **3i Infotech's threatened SEBI complaint.** 3i Infotech said (per the
  04-Feb-2026 clarification and the special call) it intended to approach
