Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
This is with reference to our earlier intimation dated April 13, 2025, wherein we had intimated that the 
Management has withdrawn the suspension of operations order and has instead declared lockout at the 
Company’s Plant situated at Satara, Maharashtra w.e.f. April 12, 2025. 
 
Further, we wish to inform you that after multiple discussions with the Recognized Union and Workers 
Representatives, the lockout has been called off  w.e.f. November 24, 2025 and maintenance work of 
the plant has started. We further wish to inform you that operations of the Company at the Plant is 
expected to start from December 20, 2025 in phases.
