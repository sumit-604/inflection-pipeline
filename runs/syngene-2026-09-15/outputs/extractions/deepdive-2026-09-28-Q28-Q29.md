That fits option contracts moving less than one-for-one with the rate. An INR-million reading is off by a factor of about 70. The table figures are almost certainly USD million, mislabelled by the page banner. The filings do not state the unit correction; only the company or auditor can confirm it.

Read as USD million, the consolidated Mar-2026 book is about USD 574mn (computed), of which USD 289mn falls due within one year (computed: 204 + 85).
