## Indo Borax & Chemicals Ltd (INDOBORAX) — run date 2026-08-30

## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A. Precise boundaries

**Product scope (in):** boron-based industrial and agri chemicals actually sold by
Indo Borax today — Boric Acid Technical grade, Boric Acid I.P./pharma grade, and
Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate (DOT). Boron Oxide is a project under
construction, not yet commercial (B07: zero matching CWIP in FY26 AR, flagged
UNPROVEN); it is carried as an upside lever (Section 5D), not in the TAM/SAM
base.

**Product scope (out):** Borax Pentahydrate (mostly traded, pass-through margin,
1.43% of FY26 revenue per B04), Gypsum (a by-product, not a sold product line
with independent demand), and the entire Kronox Lab Sciences specialty
fine-chemicals catalogue (a separate listed company, separate product
chemistry — treated in Section 5D as an M&A adjacency, not folded into this
TAM).

**Geographic scope:** India only. Export revenue was NIL in FY24, FY25 and FY26
(AR FY24 p.32, AR FY25 p.33; B04 flag). Export ambition is stated (MD letter,
concall) but unproven — zero addressable revenue today, an upside lever only.

**Customer scope:** B2B industrial and agri buyers — steel and refractories
(55-60% of boric acid demand), ceramics/tiles, plywood, pharma, personal
care/FMCG, agriculture (DOT), insecticides/pesticides (Investor Presentation
p.14; Concall pp.2, 25-26).

**Channel scope:** direct sale and decades-long distributor relationships
(concall p.16: "the top 10 to 15 customers have been same for many years").

**Price segment:** dual-tier — commodity-adjacent Technical grade (bulk of
volume) and a premium I.P./pharma grade (10-15% higher realisation, concall
p.20).

**Why this boundary matters:** the company itself frames its opportunity as
"boric acid + DOT + forward integration," and 98.57% of FY26 revenue is
exactly this basket (B04). Widening the scope to include Kronox or exports
before either exists in the P&L would inflate the TAM into a claim, not a
measurement — the framework explicitly warns against management (or an
analyst) treating TAM as SAM as growth runway.

## SECTION 2: TAM ESTIMATION — MULTIPLE METHODS

All figures ₹ Crore unless noted. Conservative-bias rule applied: where two
sourced numbers diverge, the lower is carried into the headline.

### Method 1 — Top-down (India boric acid + DOT, volume × realised price)

Indo Borax's own FY26 blended boric acid realisation was ₹127-128/kg, up
from ₹114-115/kg in FY25 (Concall p.17, Shashikant Bharuka, CFO). Management
also states it "always command[s] a premium... pricing is slightly higher
always than the market" (Concall p.23). A market-average price is therefore
haircut below Indo Borax's own realisation.

- Conservative: 38,000 t (low end of FY26 range, Investor Presentation
  slide 14 chart) × ₹115/kg (near FY25 realisation, used as a market-average
  proxy) = ₹437 Cr
- Realistic: 40,000 t (mgmt's stated FY26 point figure, Concall p.4) × ₹120/kg
  (partial haircut off Indo Borax's own ₹127-128/kg) = ₹480 Cr

DOT: 8,500 t (Concall p.19) × ₹150/kg (Indo Borax's own current DOT
realisation, stated directly, Concall p.19 — no separate market-vs-company
premium given since Indo Borax calls itself "currently a small player" in
DOT) = ₹127.5 Cr (single point estimate, used for both conservative and
realistic legs).

**Method 1 total: Conservative ₹564.5 Cr (≈₹565 Cr); Realistic ₹607.5 Cr
(≈₹608 Cr).**

### Method 2 — Bottom-up (addressable unit = tonne of boric acid/DOT sold)

Addressable unit: 1 tonne of finished boron chemical (boric acid or DOT) sold
to an Indian industrial/agri buyer. Total units: ~46,500-48,500 t combined
(38-40k boric acid + 8,500 DOT), FY26. This is a mature, 100%-penetrated
industrial input market, not an emerging product needing a separate
penetration ramp — so bottom-up necessarily converges on the same volume base
as Method 1 once revenue-per-unit is applied. Revenue per unit: ₹115-120/kg
(boric acid, market-average proxy), ₹150/kg (DOT, company-stated). This
reproduces **₹560-585 Cr**, confirming Method 1's range rather than adding
independent information — flagged as a convergence, not a triangulation,
because both methods share the same underlying tonnage source.

### Method 3 — Peer revenue aggregation (implied from Indo Borax's own share claim)

Indo Borax FY26 manufactured boron-product revenue: ₹212.4 Cr (98.57% of
₹215.45 Cr, B04). Management states ~50% share of the boric acid market
(concall p.1). Segment mix disclosed directly by management: boric acid
~85-90% of revenue, DOT ~8-10%, other (Boron Oxide + traded + Borax
Pentahydrate + Gypsum) ~2-5% (Concall p.25-26). Using midpoints: boric acid
revenue ≈ ₹188.5 Cr, DOT revenue ≈ ₹19.4 Cr.

- Implied boric acid market = ₹188.5 Cr ÷ 50% = ₹377 Cr
- Implied DOT market = ₹19.4 Cr ÷ (980 t ÷ 8,500 t = 11.5% share) = ₹168.7 Cr

**Method 3 total: ≈₹546 Cr.** This is materially lower than Method 1 on
boric acid (₹377 Cr vs ₹437-480 Cr) because a 50% share claim, applied to
Indo Borax's own revenue, backs into a *smaller* implied market than
management's own tonnage claim does when priced at a market-average rate —
a sign the ~50% share figure and the 38-40kt figure are not fully consistent
with each other. Named search for independent competitor capacity (Method
below) could not resolve which is closer to truth. Confidence: Medium.

### Method 4 — Import substitution

India imported ~3.7 million kg (≈3,700 t) of "Oxides of boron; boric acids"
(HS 281000) in 2023, split Turkey (2.39M kg), USA (0.77M kg), China (0.56M
kg), Japan and Germany (small) (UN Comtrade via World Bank WITS, calendar
2023 — **STALE, >2 years old at run date, informs direction only**). Volza
trade-shipment data shows Indian boric acid import shipment counts fell ~77%
in the twelve months to Feb-2024 vs the prior twelve months (**STALE**,
shipment-count based, not volume-based — low confidence).

3,700 t of finished-product imports against a ~38-40kt domestic market is
only **~9-10% of the market**. This is a SMALL, not a large, import
substitution opportunity — it contradicts a narrative in which "tapping
import substitution" is a major incremental TAM lever. At ₹115/kg this
import volume is worth roughly ₹42 Cr — the realistic ceiling on pure
import-substitution upside, not a market-doubling opportunity.

### Method 5 — Global benchmark

Global boric acid market: ~1,046,820 tonnes in 2025 (IMARC Group, boric
acid market report, accessed 2026 — within staleness window). India's
volume share = 40,000 ÷ 1,046,820 ≈ **3.8%** of global tonnage.

Global DOT consumption: 230,000-270,000 metric tonnes estimated for 2026
(IndexBox, DOT market forecast, accessed 2026). India's 8,500 t = **~3.2-3.7%**
of global DOT volume.

Both product lines show India holding a broadly similar (~3-4%) share of
global volume, consistent with India's weight in global industrial activity
rather than evidence of severe under-penetration awaiting a catch-up re-rating.
This is a useful discipline check: the growth story here is capex-cycle and
mix-linked (steel capex, premium-grade shift), not a "India is 20 years behind
and about to catch up" penetration story.

**Global boron oxide figures are excluded from this TAM.** Search returned
wildly inconsistent global boron oxide sizing — USD 2.98 Bn (2023) to USD
4.12 Bn (2030) from one source set, versus USD 1.12 Bn (2024) to USD 1.85 Bn
(2033) from another (both accessed 2026) — a ~3x spread suggesting the
reports conflate "boron oxide" with the broader "boron compounds" category.
No India-specific boron oxide figure was found. **NOT FOUND** is carried
forward rather than averaging two incompatible numbers; Boron Oxide is
handled instead via Indo Borax's own disclosed capacity and pricing ratio
(Section 5D), not a market-size claim.

**Conservative estimate carried forward: ₹565 Cr.**
**Realistic estimate: ₹608 Cr.**

**Management's implied TAM** (management gives no ₹ Cr figure directly; this
is a transparent derivation, not a management-stated number): 40,000 t ×
₹127.5/kg (Indo Borax's own FY26 realisation, the only price anchor
management gave) + 8,500 t × ₹150/kg = ₹510 Cr + ₹127.5 Cr = **₹638 Cr**.

**mgmt_claim_ratio = 638 ÷ 565 = 1.13x → READ: reasonable** (within the 1.5x
band). Note this ratio is *flattered* by using Indo Borax's own premium
realisation as the market-wide price; on a true market-average price the
ratio would be closer to 1.0x. Management is not inflating the market here —
if anything the self-reported figures pull in two different directions
(Method 1 tonnage vs Method 3 share-implied revenue), a genuine internal
inconsistency rather than promotional inflation.

## SECTION 3: SAM & SOM

### 3A. SAM

Applying the five filters to the conservative TAM of ₹565 Cr:

- **Product fit:** Boric Acid + DOT are exactly Indo Borax's core, filed
  product lines. No subtraction.
- **Geography:** TAM was already defined as India-domestic; export markets
  are 0% realised today (AR FY24-26), so they add nothing to SAM yet and
  subtract nothing (they were never counted in). No subtraction.
- **Channel:** Existing direct/distributor relationships already reach the
  dominant steel/refractory segment (55-60% of demand) where Indo Borax has
  decades of stickiness (Concall p.16). No material subtraction.
- **Customer:** New-age applications (semiconductor, defence, display glass —
  Concall p.8) are explicitly "exploratory stage," not yet a served customer
  segment, but they sit inside the "Others" bucket already counted in TAM at
  a small percentage; no separate subtraction to avoid double-counting.
- **Capability:** Boric Acid production is described as "fully utilised... at
  peak capacity" (Concall p.9) against a 20,000 MTPA nameplate, with realised
  output historically 15,000-16,000 t due to planned/unplanned shutdown
  inefficiency management calls "LEAF" (Concall p.26). This is a SOM-stage
  constraint (how much share is capturable), not a SAM-stage constraint (what
  is addressable in principle) — held for 3C.

**SAM = ₹565 Cr (≈100% of conservative TAM).** This is the central tension
this stage was asked to size: Indo Borax's own product/geography/channel
scope already matches its entire defined market. There is close to no SAM
expansion available by simply "serving more of the market it already
serves" — the filters do not shrink the number because the company already
occupies the full addressable space by definition. Growth beyond this
requires stepping OUTSIDE the current SAM (exports, Boron Oxide, Kronox) —
see 5D.

### 3B. SOM at 3 and 5 years

**Current SAM share:** Indo Borax's manufactured boron revenue (₹212.4 Cr,
B04) ÷ SAM (₹565 Cr) = **38%** (blended across boric acid and DOT). This is
lower than the headline "~50% market share" claim because that claim refers
to boric acid alone; DOT share is materially lower (980 t ÷ 8,500 t = 11.5%,
Concall p.19), and blending the two segments by revenue pulls the combined
figure down. This is a real finding, not a rounding artefact: the growth
headroom inside the current SAM is concentrated in DOT, not in boric acid,
where the company is already near-saturated.

**Boric acid volume path (capacity-capped, not share-gain):**
Management states Boric Acid is at peak utilisation; the only volume
increase on the table is 1,000-1,500 t of debottlenecking (Concall p.6,
p.25). No committed capacity expansion beyond this exists in filed sources
(the ~₹90 Cr FY27-28 capex figure referenced in the operator brief is NOT
FOUND in any AR, presentation, or concall — treated as unanchored and
excluded). Using the midpoint of the debottleneck range:
- FY26 base: 15,900 t (15,000 Technical + 900 I.P., Concall p.4-5)
- 3-year (FY29): 17,100 t (+1,200 t)
- 5-year (FY31): 18,000 t (a further modest gain, still below the 20,000 t
  nameplate — no new capex assumed)

Revenue at flat ₹127.5/kg (FY26 realisation held flat — **no price inflation
assumed**, per conservative bias, to isolate the volume/share-driven
component and avoid conflating it with the ore-cost pass-through cycle):
- 3yr: 17,100 t × ₹127.5/kg = ₹218.0 Cr
- 5yr: 18,000 t × ₹127.5/kg = ₹229.5 Cr

**DOT volume path (genuine share-gain, off a small base):**
Management guides 980 t (FY26) → 1,500 t (FY27, an explicit management
number, Concall p.4-5, p.19) and separately states a target of "increasing
50% of the business in a year's time" continuing into "high double-digit"
growth thereafter (Concall p.20). Extrapolating that stated cadence
(moderating growth rates, not compounding 50% indefinitely):
- FY27: 1,500 t (mgmt-guided)
- FY28: ~2,000 t (+33%, moderating)
- FY29 (3yr): ~2,600 t (+30%, moderating) → share of the 8,500 t DOT market
  rises from 11.5% to ~31% (DOT market itself held flat at 8,500 t — its
  growth rate is NOT FOUND, so no market-size inflation is assumed on this
  side either)
- FY30: ~3,400 t; FY31 (5yr): ~4,200 t (+24%, further moderating) → ~49%
  share of a still-flat 8,500 t DOT market — this is an AGGRESSIVE share
  path (>5pp/year), justified only because Indo Borax starts this segment
  from a genuinely small base with a stated raw-material synergy advantage
  (boric acid as its own DOT feedstock, Concall p.19) and explicit
  management intent; it should be read as the upper edge of what "faster
  share gain off a low base" can mean, not a normal-case assumption.

Revenue at flat ₹150/kg:
- 3yr: 2,600 t × ₹150/kg = ₹39.0 Cr
- 5yr: 4,200 t × ₹150/kg = ₹63.0 Cr

**Other (traded goods, Borax Pentahydrate, Gypsum — held flat, immaterial):**
₹7.5 Cr (residual of FY26 total, B04 mix). Boron Oxide is deliberately
EXCLUDED here (see 3C).

**SOM_3yr = 218.0 + 39.0 + 7.5 = ₹264.5 Cr (≈₹265 Cr)**
**SOM_5yr = 229.5 + 63.0 + 7.5 = ₹300 Cr**

**Implied revenue CAGR (against the FY26 base of ₹215.45 Cr, per formal
handoff instruction):**
- 3-year: (265/215.45)^(1/3) − 1 ≈ **7.2%**
- 5-year: (300/215.45)^(1/5) − 1 ≈ **6.9%**

**This is the single most important number in this report.** Management's
own FY27 revenue growth guidance is 20-35% ("we are moving between 20% to
35% of revenue growth... ₹40 crores to ₹50 crores to ₹60 crores or
something like that," Concall p.24, Suresh Kalra). The anchored, capacity-
and share-based SOM computed here implies only ~7% CAGR. The gap is not
necessarily dishonesty — management itself attributes its 20-35% guide to
"increased realisation, slightly higher volumes... and some new products"
(Concall p.24), i.e., PRICE/mix and unproven new-product optionality, not
market-share capture. But it does mean: roughly three-quarters of
management's growth guide sits OUTSIDE the anchored, volume-based addressable
market computed here — it depends on the ore-cost/realisation cycle (which
B04 already flags as the dominant swing factor, not a demand signal), on the
unproven Boron Oxide capex, or on the unclosed Kronox acquisition. None of
these are in the SOM figures above.

### 3C. Capacity cross-check

The B07 capex-embedded-growth figure for this run is explicitly UNPROVEN
(Boron Oxide, ₹20 Cr / 4,000 MTPA, zero matching CWIP in the FY26 AR) and the
₹90 Cr FY27-28 total capex figure referenced in the operator brief is NOT
FOUND in any filed source (AR, presentation, or concall) — treated as
unanchored per pipeline rule.

Checking the anchored SOM against EXISTING nameplate capacity only (no new
capex assumed):
- Boric Acid: SOM_3yr requires 17,100 t and SOM_5yr requires 18,000 t,
  both below the 20,000 MTPA nameplate. **Sufficient**, achievable via the
  disclosed debottlenecking alone.
- DOT: SOM_3yr requires 2,600 t and SOM_5yr requires 4,200 t, both well
  below the 6,000 MTPA nameplate. **Sufficient**, with headroom to spare.

**capacity_check: sufficient.** The anchored SOM does not require the
unproven Boron Oxide capex or the unanchored ₹90 Cr plan to be realised — if
either proceeds, it is upside beyond SOM_5yr (see 5D quantification), not a
gap that needs filling to hit the numbers above. Neither side is the
"optimistic" one here because the anchored case does not depend on the
capex at all.

### 4C. Market structure

- **Competitor count:** Indo Borax plus several small, unlisted regional
  manufacturers identified in search (Gujarat Boron Derivatives Pvt. Ltd.,
  Prime Industries, Samir Tech-Chem Pvt. Ltd., Veeransh Chemicals), plus
  finished-product importers from Turkey/USA/China. No listed peer of
  comparable scale was found.
- **Top-3/concentration:** Indo Borax alone claims ~50% of the boric acid
  segment (self-reported, unverified independently); the remainder is
  fragmented among the small manufacturers above and imports (~9-10% of the
  market per Method 4).
- **Organised vs unorganised:** the standard Indian-market assumption of a
  large (30-60%) unorganised tail does **NOT** appear to apply here. This is
  a licensed (BIS/FDA), B2B industrial-input category; the "other half" of
  the market looks like small FORMAL manufacturers and imports, not an
  informal/unbranded tail. Flagged as a deviation from the framework's
  default assumption.
- **Consolidating or fragmenting:** at the OWNERSHIP/platform level, active
  consolidation is underway — Zenrock/EAAA took control of Indo Borax
  (Jan-2026) and Indo Borax itself signed an SPA (20-Aug-2026) to acquire
  64.26% of Kronox Lab Sciences, with an open offer for a further 25.79%
  (search: MarketScreener, AlchemPro, ScanX, 20-21 Aug 2026 reporting). The
  underlying competitive market (small manufacturers) has not consolidated.
- **Price vs differentiation:** Indo Borax states its product carries "the
  best technical specifications" per customer feedback (Concall p.16) and
  commands a market premium — a differentiation-based position with its top
  customer tier, not pure price competition.
- **Entries/exits:** Zenrock Chemicals / EAAA India Alternatives as new
  promoter (Jan-2026); no material domestic manufacturing-competitor
  entries/exits found.
- **Import share trend:** Volza shipment-count data shows a ~77% YoY decline
  in Indian boric acid import shipments (Mar 2023-Feb 2024 vs prior twelve
  months) — directionally consistent with import substitution, but
  **STALE** (2023-24) and shipment-count rather than volume-based; low
  confidence.

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY & RUNWAY

### 5A. Funnel

```
TAM (conservative)        ₹565 Cr   (India boric acid + DOT, full market)
  → SAM                   ₹565 Cr   (≈100% of TAM — product/geo/channel/
                                      customer already fully overlap)
    → SOM (3yr, FY29)     ₹265 Cr   (47% of SAM; capacity-fit, no new capex)
    → SOM (5yr, FY31)     ₹300 Cr   (53% of SAM; capacity-fit, no new capex)
```

### 5B. Runway assessment

- **Revenue headroom** = SAM ÷ current revenue = 565 ÷ 215.45 = **2.62x**
- **TAM growth rate** = 7-8% CAGR FY26-FY30 for boric acid (mgmt/"reports"
  claim, single-sourced); DOT market growth rate is **NOT FOUND** (held
  flat in this model, a conservative assumption)
- **Company CAGR vs TAM growth:** Indo Borax's 5-year historical revenue
  CAGR is 8.4% (company memory mental model, cross-checked against AR
  revenue trend), closely tracking the claimed 7-8% TAM growth. This
  confirms the company has broadly been **riding the market, not gaining
  share**, over the medium term — consistent with a business that already
  holds roughly half of a mature, capacity-constrained segment.
- **Years to saturate SAM:** the "saturation" framing does not translate
  cleanly here, because the company already holds an estimated ~38% blended
  share of a SAM that equals its full defined TAM. There is no large
  unclaimed pool waiting to be captured within the current product/geography
  scope — the real ceiling is capacity (3C), not un-served demand.

### 5C. Runway classification: **MODERATE**

Revenue headroom of 2.6x and TAM/SOM growth in the mid-to-high single digits,
with the company already riding rather than gaining share, sit squarely in
the MODERATE band (not LIMITED, because a genuine ~38% blended share leaves
room to grow inside the existing SAM, particularly in DOT; not GOOD or
higher, because the dominant boric acid segment — 87.5% of revenue — is
capacity-capped near its current output and the TAM growth rate itself is
single-digit). This classification applies to the CURRENT product/geography
scope only; it would move toward LIMITED if the dominant segment stayed flat
and DOT's aggressive share-gain path did not materialise, or toward STRONG
if the adjacency levers in 5D are realised and folded into scope in a future
run.
