Sky Gold’s Growth Blueprint: Strengthening Foundations for Long-Term Value

  Evolution of Sky Gold & Diamonds Ltd.

  Founded in 2005, Sky Gold & Diamonds Ltd. vision was to serve large retailers and build a leadership position through innovative design. Post IPO, we leveraged investor trust to
  scale rapidly through customer penetration and geographic expansion, leading to improved brand recognition and profitability. Following the preferential allotment from marquee
  investors in FY24, we guided for ₹3,000 Cr revenue, ~3% PAT margin, and cash-flow neutrality by FY27–28 (vs FY24 revenue of ₹1,745 Cr).
  Since then, execution has exceeded expectations, and we have progressively upgraded our guidance. In H1 FY27, we have achieved ₹ 4,403 Crs of revenue with PAT margin of 5.3%,
  while generating operating cash flows of ₹ 136 Crs+, without compromising growth.

 The "Advance Gold" Advantage

  The advance gold model operates on a client-supplied basis, wherein the customer provides gold upfront to the company. The company bills only for the making charges. Since the
  underlying metal is not purchased or financed by the company, the model eliminates inventory risk, reduces capital intensity, and enables high-volume scalability with minimal
  balance sheet impact.
  However, its impact on ROCE will be transformative as this category requires zero working capital investment for inventory, minimum investment on receivable, allowing us to scale
  volume without requiring additional working capital to service this category.

 Impact of Quality and Mix Improvement on Unit Economics

  Company has expanded its margins over the years and currently generates a PAT margin of ~4.5%, demonstrating the impact of the initiatives outlined above.
  Working capital days has improved, because of the scale benefits, higher export mix, increased spot payments and greater share of advance gold volumes.

Track Record of Capital Allocation and Business Evolution

  Sky Gold had previously indicated that expansion would be supported through internal cash generation. The Indian jewellery industry continues to witness an
  increasing presence of organised manufacturers within the overall market, creating a favourable backdrop for companies with established capabilities, scale, and
  customer relationships.

  Sky Gold has developed capabilities across design, manufacturing, merchandising and retailer engagement over the years. Its operations span a diverse customer
  base, product portfolio and distribution network across domestic and international markets. The evolving industry landscape presents opportunities across multiple
  product categories, customer segments and geographies.

  Sky Gold's business has evolved through a combination of organic expansion and strategic investments. Historically, these initiatives have contributed to the
  expansion of manufacturing capabilities, customer relationships, product offerings, and operational scale. The company has also established relationships across
  retailers, metal lenders, banking partners, and other stakeholders within the jewellery ecosystem.

  Management has historically evaluated investments through the lens of return on capital and long-term value creation. The company has maintained a track record
  of generating returns above its cost of capital, supported by disciplined capital allocation and prudent deployment of resources. Its asset-light advance gold model
  has helped minimize working capital intensity while supporting efficient use of capital.

  The improvement in operating cash flow generation during H1 FY27, alongside continued business expansion, reflects developments in operational execution. The
  company's working capital cycle has improved over time, while revenues have expanded. Management recognizes that working capital has historically been an area
  of investor focus; recent operating cash flow performance provides additional context regarding the company's operating model and cash generation profile.

  Similar capital-raising initiatives undertaken in the past have coincided with periods of business expansion and increased operating scale. Over the years, the
  company has strengthened its manufacturing capabilities, broadened its product portfolio, expanded customer relationships, and maintained a focus on return-
  oriented capital deployment. This track record has been supported by the company's asset-light advance gold model, which has contributed to positive operating
  cash flow generation and healthy returns on invested capital.

                              Mr. Mangesh Chauhan

                                             jewellery. Starting in Mumbai’s Zaveri Bazar, he pioneered India’s first organised B2B jewellery manufacturing model and steered the company from inception to
                                             NSE main-board listing with a market cap exceeding ₹5,000 crore. His blend of strategic vision, financial discipline, and operational innovation has built long-term

                                             quality, integrity, and innovation.
