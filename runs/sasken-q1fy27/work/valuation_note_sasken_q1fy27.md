## 1. OPERATIONAL ROCE (Section 1B Route A, denominator fix)

Statutory ROCE ~6% is distorted by idle treasury. Route A strips non-operating cash and investments from capital employed AND the income they generate from EBIT (consistency rule).
- Numerator: operating EBIT ex-other-income, ex-exceptional. FY26 Rs49 Cr; Q1 FY27 run-rate ~Rs85 Cr annualised.
- Denominator: capital employed ~Rs875 Cr (equity + NCI, debt-free) minus ~Rs374 Cr net cash+investments = ~Rs500 Cr operating capital (still includes Rs313 Cr Borqs goodwill/intangibles).

| ROCE basis | Calc | ROCE |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory | EBIT over full Rs875 Cr incl treasury | ~6% |
| Operational, FY26 actual | Rs49 Cr / ~Rs500 Cr | ~10% |
| Operational, sustained Q1 FY27 margins | ~Rs85 Cr / ~Rs525 Cr | ~15-16% |

The operating business is a ~15-16% ROCE business, not 6%. Base PE (0.5 x ROCE + 7.5): statutory ~11x -> operational ~15x. BUT the cash-conversion multiplier (Pillar 2) stays frozen at its 0.65x structural floor (no cash flow statement this quarter; framework bars re-banding until Q2 H1 CFO). Route declared: A-Operational; blend skipped (operational ROCE feeds formula alone).
