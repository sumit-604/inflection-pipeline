## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Policy changes with quantified P&L impact:** None identified this year. Note 3.20 states MCA made no Ind AS amendments effective 1 April 2025 applicable to the company (Note 3.20, p.62). Statement of Changes in Equity shows "Changes in Equity Share Capital due to prior period errors" = Nil for both years (p.50/pre-notes) — no restatement. 🟢
- **Revenue recognition aggressiveness:** Point-in-time recognition on dispatch/delivery; credit terms 60–90 days (Note 3.5, p.53); reconciliation note confirms "amounts receivable... become due after expiry of credit period which on average is less than 90 days" (Note 26, p.77). Standard for API/bulk-drug manufacturer, not aggressive. 🟢
- **Depreciation useful lives vs norm:** WDV method used (not SLM), per Schedule II Part C, EXCEPT Plant & Machinery, Other Factory Equipment and R&D Equipment set at 23 years, "estimated by the management supported by technical advice" (Note 3.7, p.55). No named technical evaluator or basis disclosed — a judgment area with limited supporting disclosure. 🟡. Residual value capped at ≤5% of original cost (consistent, standard).
- **Capitalisation threshold:** NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (no monetary threshold disclosed for capitalising vs expensing).
- **Impairment test assumptions (growth/discount rate):** NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — Note 3.9 (p.56) is boilerplate; no company-specific discount rate, cash-flow growth rate, or CGU-level sensitivity disclosed because no material impairment was recognised this year (only ₹0.02 Lakh vs ₹30.45 Lakh in FY24, Note 32, p.79).
- **ECL matrix conservatism — INCONSISTENCY FLAGGED 🔴:** Policy states a provision rate of "2.5% to 7.5%…typically applied" for receivables presumed to have significantly increased credit risk (>365 days overdue) (Note 3.3/3.19, p.52/60). Actual ageing-linked provisioning in Note 10 (p.67) shows: 1–2 yr bucket ₹7.22L provisioned ₹0.22L (3.0%, within range); 2–3 yr bucket ₹9.26L provisioned ₹0.44L (4.75%, within range); but the >3-year bucket — ₹219.67L, the single largest and highest-risk aged balance, which includes the ₹216.15L under active litigation (Note 10/46(p)) — is provisioned at only ₹0.24L, i.e. ~0.11%, far below the stated 2.5–7.5% policy band. This is a material internal inconsistency: the most overdue, litigated receivables carry the thinnest ECL coverage.
- **Ind AS 116 (leases):** ROU asset ₹13.51L (FY24: ₹18.96L); lease liability ₹14.26L (FY24: ₹19.19L); current ₹10.74L / non-current ₹3.52L (Note 5(B), p.65). Discount rate used is described only generically as "incremental borrowing rate" — the actual % rate is NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (only derivable approximately from interest of ₹2.07L on the liability). Short-term lease exemption applied; short-term lease expense ₹14.05L (Note 5(B), p.65).
- **First-time standard adoption:** None this year.
- **Borrowing cost capitalisation:** ₹13.15 Lakh of interest capitalised to PPE/CWIP during the year (Note 47(e), p.94); also ₹3.16L of gratuity expense and ₹2.04L of leave-encashment expense capitalised to PPE (Note 41, p.84/87) — capital-project-linked payroll cost capitalisation, disclosed but a point worth watching for expense-capitalisation honesty.
