Executive Summary : Q2 FY27

    Business
         ⮚ AUM grew 36.6% YoY to ₹22,035 Cr
         ⮚ Disbursals increased by 28.3% YoY to ₹6,676 Cr
         ⮚ Gold Loan AUM grew 77.0% YoY to ₹11,917 Cr
         ⮚ Net growth in Gold AUM of ₹726 Cr in this quarter
         ⮚ Mortgage AUM grew 13.5% YoY to ₹9,982 Cr

    Branches
         ⮚ Commissioned 74 new branches this quarter, bringing our total footprint to 831 branches (across 18 states & UTs)

    Profitability
         ⮚ Net Interest Income for this quarter has grown by 37.4% YoY to ₹403.7 Cr
         ⮚ Operating Profit for this quarter has grown by 47.5% YoY to ₹205.1 Cr
         ⮚ PAT for this quarter has grown by 63.1% YoY to ₹130.8 Cr reflecting a RoA of 2.8% and RoE of 17.0%

    Asset Quality and Provisioning
         ⮚ Gross Stage III stood at 1.8%, Net Stage III stood at 1.2%
         ⮚ Credit Cost for this quarter is 0.6%
