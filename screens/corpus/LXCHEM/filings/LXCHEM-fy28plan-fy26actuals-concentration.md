## 2. PROGRESS AGAINST THAT PLAN, TWO YEARS IN
Anchored figures, then the arithmetic.
- FY25 consolidated revenue **Rs 29,854 Mn**, up 5% (EgJBZk p.26). FY25 EBITDA **Rs 2,796
  Mn**, up 9.4% (EgJBZk p.23). FY25 volumes up 11%.
- FY26 consolidated revenue **Rs 28,467 Mn** (U2eSzQ p.21). Management: "our revenues have
  **degrown by 6%**" (KvbP1q p.6).
- FY26 consolidated PBT Rs 879.71 Mn, PAT Rs 793.62 Mn, basic EPS Rs 2.87; finance cost
  Rs 220.88 Mn, depreciation Rs 766.35 Mn, other income Rs 153.05 Mn (w8BufV p.9, year-ended
  column).
DERIVED, arithmetic shown: FY26 EBITDA = PBT 879.71 + finance 220.88 + depreciation 766.35
minus other income 153.05 = **Rs 1,713.89 Mn**. Against FY25 of Rs 2,796 Mn that is a fall
of **38.7%**.
DERIVED against the plan: the plan was set off an FY24 base. FY24 revenue implies about
Rs 28,432 Mn (FY25 of Rs 29,854 Mn at plus 5%). FY26 revenue of Rs 28,467 Mn is therefore
**flat on FY24 after two of the plan's four years**, against a target to double. EBITDA is
**below** the FY24 base, against a target to triple.
This is the load-bearing finding of the sweep for this name. It does not mean the plan
fails. It means two of four years produced no progress, and everything now rests on Dahej.
