| Full Rs 100 Cr approval (not committed), net-block turnover | 10,000 | 1.910x net | 19,100.0 | 101.4% |

Three limits on these numbers.
1. The approval has no funding split, no phasing and no PO. Only the 1.9% (3.9%) rows are under execution. The 49.1% to 101.4% range is a ceiling, not an embedded growth figure.
2. The FY26 turnover is not a causal rate. FY26 capex of 2,403.28 lakh was mostly solar (B03), yet revenue rose 2,337.25 lakh (+14.2%) on auto volume. Turnover applied to capex therefore overstates what a power asset yields and may understate what a lost-foam line yields.
3. Existing plant has room. 3,060 t against 1,557 t dispatch is 1.97x tonnage (derived). The new money buys capability (lost-foam, vacuum, machining), not tonnes, so revenue growth in the next two years does not depend on it ([INF]).

Funding arithmetic (derived, an illustration only): Rs 100 Cr is 3.3x to 4.3x the NCA of Rs 23 to 30 Cr a year that CARE expects for FY27 to FY29 (CARE p.3). If all of it were debt, gearing would move from 0.71x (8,018.66 / 11,261.47 lakh, results p.5) to 1.60x ((8,018.66 + 10,000) / 11,261.47). B05 names 0.80x as its kill signal. The actual mix is NOT FOUND.
