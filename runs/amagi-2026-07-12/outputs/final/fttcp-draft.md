## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue, what could keep it firing.** The cloud shift is early: management cites roughly 10% cloud penetration of a large broadcast to streaming market, and the three product lines all grew. Net revenue retention of 126% means existing customers spend more each year without a single new logo. What confirms it: the segment lines holding above 25% growth and the count of million dollar customers rising. What kills it: the net new logo deceleration, 67 to 29 adds and net negative in Q4, spreading into revenue as churn.

**Margin, what could expand it.** The cost ratios are still falling as the platform scales, and gross margin near 69% leaves room if mix improves. What confirms it: adjusted EBITDA margin stepping above the roughly 10% management calls steady. What kills it: more client renegotiations like the top five deal that cut Q4 gross margin, or currency reversing the 7 point tailwind in reported growth.

**Cash conversion, the missing piece.** There is no documented catalyst here. Management pointed to an adjusted operating cash flow that strips out IPO and ESOP cash, but named no receivables program, no factoring line, no payment term change. The one mechanical positive is passive: the large one time ESOP and SAR settlements will not repeat, so next year's reported cash should look better on that alone. What confirms a real fix: reported operating cash flow turning clearly positive with receivables growth back at or below revenue growth. What kills it: another negative reported OCF quarter with receivables still climbing.

**ROCE, why it stays stuck.** The lever is margin, and the drag is Rs 1,664 Cr of cash earning treasury yield inside the capital base. What confirms improvement: a deployment plan with size discipline, a buyback, or a large distribution, plus the margin climbing. What kills it: the cash sitting idle for another year, or an overpriced acquisition that bloats the base further.

Compact evidence read:

| Transition | Best catalyst | Evidence tier | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Cloud shift plus 126% NRR, documented segment growth | Mixed documented and management | Strong |
| Margin | Operating leverage, cost ratios falling, one guidance figure delivered | Documented plus management | Moderate |
| Cash | NONE FOUND as a specific program; only passive one time roll off | Absence | Weak |
| ROCE | Margin flow through; cash deployment NONE FOUND | Absence | Weak |

The two absences are the finding. Two consecutive calls named no plan for either the working capital build or the idle cash. That silence is the reason cash and ROCE score zero rather than positive.
