### 2C Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

- Capex under execution (not yet commissioned, i.e., CWIP at FY26
  year-end): Rs 6.82 cr (AR2026 Note 5, p.201).
- Historical fixed-asset turnover = FY26 revenue ÷ FY26 net fixed assets
  = Rs 1,068.48 cr ÷ Rs 163.03 cr (Land Rs 7.13 cr + Building Rs 56.19 cr +
  Plant & Equipment Rs 92.40 cr + Furniture Rs 2.62 cr + Office Equipment
  Rs 3.91 cr + Vehicles Rs 0.78 cr, AR2026 Note 4, p.201) = 6.55x.
- Implied incremental revenue on commissioning of the outstanding CWIP =
  Rs 6.82 cr × 6.55 = Rs 44.68 cr ≈ **4.2% above current revenue.**

Caveat, stated plainly: this 4.2% figure badly understates the true
embedded-growth signal because the actual capacity actions taken in FY26
(new Rewari facility, third-phase FWM line, SMT upgrade) were already fully
commissioned WITHIN FY26 — i.e., already inside both the Rs 163.03 cr net
block and the Rs 1,068.48 cr revenue used in this formula, not sitting ahead
of it in CWIP. Read correctly, INEL is NOT running a large unmonetised
capacity cushion; capacity additions and revenue have been moving together,
year by year, with no evidence of a big incremental-revenue unlock still to
come from capital already spent.
