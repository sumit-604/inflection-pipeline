**2C. Growth embedded in capex — computed (rework item 17)**

Run-1's stated blocker was that the AR's "Purchase of Fixed Assets" cash-flow

"Purchase of Fixed Assets" (Rs 3,647.24 lakh FY26) on its own line, separate

balance-sheet CWIP figure, not the cash-flow purchase line.

Arithmetic, run:

- Consolidated Capital Work-In-Progress, FY26: Rs 1,749.91 lakh = **Rs 17.50
Cr** (Annual_Report_2026.txt line 10021, confirmed on read). The AR states
TLL Parenterals is substantially the asset behind this figure.
- Historical fixed asset turnover, FY26: Revenue Rs 129.02 Cr / net fixed
assets Rs 60.73 Cr = **2.12x** (same basis as B01 M3).
- Implied incremental revenue = Rs 17.50 Cr × 2.12x = **Rs 37.2 Cr**.
- As a % of FY26 revenue (Rs 129.02 Cr): **28.8%**.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 28.8**

Caveat, carried beside the number, not used to suppress it: the FAT
denominator (net fixed assets Rs 60.73 Cr) already includes gross block for
TLL Parenterals and other newly capitalised, still pre-revenue or
near-zero-revenue subsidiaries. A historical FAT ratio computed on an asset
base that includes capacity not yet generating revenue can overstate how
quickly the CWIP itself will convert to revenue once commissioned — the
28.8% figure is the mechanical output of the formula the framework specifies,
not an independent forecast of when that revenue arrives. No disclosed
conversion timeline exists beyond the deck's own undated peak-revenue claims
(TLL Parenterals Rs 200 Cr, per B05 guidance table).
