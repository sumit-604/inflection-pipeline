### Note 1: Corporate Information & Material Accounting Policies (p.101-104)
- Standard Ind AS basis, historical cost, 12-month operating cycle. 🟢
- New Ind AS amendments effective FY26 (Ind AS 1 current/non-current liability classification,
  Ind AS 7/107 supplier finance disclosures, Ind AS 12 Pillar Two, Ind AS 21 lack of

  boilerplate.
- Ind AS 1 covenant-breach amendment (current/non-current classification) becomes effective
  1-Apr-2026 (next year), not yet applicable (p.102). NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: any indication the
  company anticipates a covenant issue; moot in any case as the company carries no bank borrowings
  (see Note 39, Note 42).
- Depreciation: useful-life method per Schedule II, no residual value taken on PPE (p.103). 🟢
  Standard, slightly conservative (no residual value assumed).
- Intangible assets: finite-life, straight-line amortised over "estimated useful economic lives";
  Note 2C shows 20-year useful life applied to both Patent IPR Technologies and Product & Process
  Development intangibles (p.103, p.105). 20 years is a long amortisation period for pharma
  patents/IP relative to typical patent life (many jurisdictions cap patent protection near 20
  years from filing, and commercial life of a given molecule/dossier is often shorter); this
  policy alone is a 🟡 Watch item on capitalisation/amortisation conservatism, revisited under
  Note 2C below.
- Revenue recognition: point-in-time on delivery/dispatch/acceptance depending on customer terms;
  credit terms up to 120 days (p.104). Bill-and-hold revenue recognised per Ind AS 115 Appendix B
  only when substantive commercial purpose, goods identified/ready, and company does not retain
  ability to redirect goods (p.104). 🟢 Policy language is textbook-correct; no evidence in the
  notes of bill-and-hold being used to pull forward revenue (no bill-and-hold quantum disclosed
  separately -- NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT, so cannot independently size this exposure).
- Employee benefits, forex, borrowing cost, government grants, provisions/contingencies, income
  tax, EPS, leases (Ind AS 116): all standard-form policy language, p.104. 🟢
