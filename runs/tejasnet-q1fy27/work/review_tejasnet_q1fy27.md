### 2C. Six mandatory diagnostics
1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes — standalone +99.05% (₹201.93 → ₹401.95 Cr), consolidated +99.11%. Nearly doubled off the FY26 trough base, consistent with the thesis recovery narrative. But the base (Q1 FY26) was the trough quarter, so the % is base-inflated; absolute ₹402 Cr annualises to ~₹1,608 Cr — below the ₹2,000 Cr FY27 thesis-break floor (see Step 6C).

3. **Core operating PBT (ex-OI) YoY?** Improved +₹27.15 Cr standalone (−306.93 → −279.78). Cleanest operational read is positive: the improvement is NOT an Other Income artifact (OI actually fell −0.58). Core health improved.
4. **Gap between core-PBT growth and reported PAT growth?** Reported PBT improved +₹26.57 Cr, yet PAT worsened −₹8.33 Cr. The entire reversal sits in the **deferred-tax line**: the tax benefit shrank from ₹103.47 Cr (Q1 FY26) to ₹68.57 Cr (Q1 FY27), a −₹34.90 Cr swing that more than erased the +₹26.57 Cr PBT gain. **Headline "PAT loss widened" is misleading on operations — it is a smaller tax-shield, not deteriorating operations** (A3-F8). Effective tax rate normalised from 34.79% toward the statutory ~25.32%.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** D&A FLAT (−2.19% YoY) while revenue +99% — favourable absorption (fixed asset base being better utilised). Finance costs +13.86% — the one adverse scaler; interest is climbing with the rising net-debt load (₹3,531 → ₹4,277 Cr, Step 5), and will keep flowing through future quarters.
6. **Other Income concentration?** OI ₹8.97 Cr = 2.2% of revenue, down YoY; FY26 OI ₹33.46 Cr. No treasury-timing distortion of the quarterly trajectory. Nothing to strip.
