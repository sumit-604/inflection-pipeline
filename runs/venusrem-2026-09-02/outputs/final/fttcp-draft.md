## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**What could make revenue keep growing.** Venus holds 1,100-plus granted export permits and uses about 511 of them. Turning dormant permits into live sales needs no new plant, and the company did win 90 first-market grants across 33 country-product combinations in FY26. Saudi Arabia approved Plerixafor, the first global approval for that molecule. The ESI hospital channel went from nothing to 74 hospitals. **What would confirm it:** the active-permit count rising faster than the granted count, disclosed in the FY27 annual report. **What would kill it:** the active count flat while grants keep rising, which would say approvals are not converting into sales. **The ceiling:** the plant is near full. Beyond roughly FY28 this catalyst needs the new building, and the new building has no production date.

**What could keep the margin up.** The export mix keeps shifting toward oncology and anti-infective specialty work, where gross contribution ran 77% in FY26 against 72% in FY25. **What would confirm it:** gross contribution holding at or above 75% across two more disclosed FY27 periods. **What would kill it:** reversion toward 72%, or the platinum-linked API cost inflation that Beta Drugs quantified on its own call showing up in Venus's numbers. **The known drag:** the PLI incentive, Rs 15.70 Cr in FY26 and zero in FY25, ends in FY28. That is roughly 15% of FY26 profit that simply stops.

**What could keep cash converting.** Nothing needs to happen. This one already works and has for five years. **What would kill it:** payable days holding above 79 into FY27, or reverting to about 61 while CFO/PAT drops under 1.0x, which together would say FY26's cash was borrowed from suppliers rather than earned.

**What could lift ROCE.** Margin holding while the capital base stays flat. **What would kill it:** the Rs 51.36 Cr of construction staying unproductive, the Rs 20.84 Cr aged tranche still unresolved in the FY27 accounts, or a fresh capex cycle at the Venus Industrial Park starting before the current building earns anything.

**What is NOT a catalyst, and this matters.** The research pipeline is carried at zero and the signed model refuses to discuss it in catalyst terms. VRP-034 is a Phase 1 trial in healthy volunteers that has not started recruiting. The lyophilisation demand claim of roughly two times capacity appears nowhere in any filed document; two peers do run their freeze-dry lines at 85% to 90% and are adding capacity, which supports the direction but not the number. The "H2 FY27" milestone that the original thesis leaned on turns out, in the filed text, to be the commissioning of the new building's energy monitoring and effluent treatment systems, not a production line.

**Step 2E, management intent versus action.** On revenue, vision and real documented action both exist, but the action is capacity-bounded. On margin, the action is done and one-time. On cash, no action is needed. On ROCE, the documented action is capex that is not yet earning, which is dilutive in the near term. The ledger CONFIRMS every Step 2 verdict. Nothing was loosened, and the promise-vs-delivery discount applies against a missed revenue target and an unverified permit-activation promise.

### THE EARNINGS-BASIS QUESTION — operator decides, I do not

The destination multiple must be applied to one basis, and the same basis must be used at entry and at exit (Amendment 18.1 bars mixing).

- **One-year-forward P/E** — the multiple applies to next year's EPS. Fits a business whose earnings are stepping and where the buyer at exit is pricing the year ahead.
- **Trailing P/E** — the multiple applies to the year just reported. Fits a business whose earnings are lumpy or whose forward year carries a known discontinuity.

**The note that matters here:** Venus has a known discontinuity. The PLI incentive stops in FY28, so the FY29 destination year is roughly Rs 15.7 Cr of profit lighter than a naive roll-forward, and Q4 FY26 alone was about 34% of full-year revenue at about 22% PBT margin, which makes any single-year basis sensitive to quarter mix. Whichever basis you choose, the FY29 earnings must be struck post-PLI. **This is your call at the gate; I have not picked it.**
