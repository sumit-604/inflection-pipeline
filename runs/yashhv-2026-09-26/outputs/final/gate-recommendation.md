## Active flags

### FLAG-CASH (determination: INDETERMINATE)

CASH CONVERSION FLAG: CFO/PAT fell to 0.24x in FY26, free cash flow negative two years running, working capital days rising.

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

The capex half of the gap is growth induced. Capex of Rs 59.20 Cr in FY26 and Rs 38.45 Cr in FY25 went into a plant that is not yet commercial (B01 deal_breakers; B03 flags, standalone cash flow p.98, CWIP Rs 239.24 lakh to Rs 4,303.46 lakh). The operating half, CFO of Rs 8.83 Cr on PAT of Rs 37.34 Cr, cannot be classed from the filed record. Evidence on both sides:

- Growth reading. Order book Rs 150 Cr to Rs 400 Cr plus (B03 guidance_table, AR FY26 Board's Report). Customer advances up 335.6%, Rs 244.69 lakh to Rs 1,066.04 lakh (B02 rank 14, Note 26 p.126-127). Payables up only 2.6% against inventory up 124.7%, so suppliers were not stretched; gearing 16.90%; no covenant breach (B02 rank 13, Note 47 p.147, Note 21 p.120-122). Imported cores carry 12 to 16 week lead times, which forces stock ahead of orders (B04, Inv. Pres. p.37).
- Structural reading. Bank quarterly stock statements differed from the books for 12 consecutive quarters, nearly always books above bank, widest at each year end (Mar-24 minus 12.18%, Mar-25 minus 25.35%, Mar-26 minus 17.76%); only 2 of 12 quarters carry a reason (B02 rank 1, Note 45 p.143-144). The statutory auditor confirms it under CARO clause ii(b) (B03 flags, Annexure A to Auditor's Report p.91). The not yet due receivables bucket fell from 68% (Rs 1,894.14 lakh) to 3% (Rs 91.59 lakh) of the total, and the under six months overdue bucket rose from 30.3% to 93.2%, while the summary turnover ratio improved from 7.44x to 8.07x (B02 receivables_trend, Note 10 p.114-115, Note 53 p.147). Inventory turnover fell from 5.83x to 4.88x (B02 rank 4, Note 9 p.113). FY25 CFO changed from Rs 918.68 lakh to Rs 953.13 lakh between the FY25 and FY26 annual reports with no cash flow reconciliation (B02 rank 3; B03 flags; AR FY25 p.39, AR FY26 p.101-102, Annexure A p.153-157).
- Rating agency verbatim quote: NOT FOUND. No rating rationale exists in the corpus or on the agency sites searched; the company may be unrated (B00 input_gaps).
- Capex commissioning timeline: trial production end of Q2 FY27, commercial three to four months later, slipped from March 2026 (B05 timeline_slippages; Concall May-2026).
- Management silence: cash conversion and Note 45 were never raised on any of the four calls (B05 FLAG-SILENCE).

Missing evidence that would settle the determination:

Observation: a bank stock statement gap is an early cash conversion tell that most readers skip. CARO clause ii(b) makes the auditor say whether the quarterly stock statements a company files with its bank match its books. At Yash the books sat above the bank figure in nearly every quarter for three years, widest at each year end, and only 2 of 12 quarters carry a reason. Worth publishing as a concept teardown because the check sits in every Indian annual report and takes two minutes.
