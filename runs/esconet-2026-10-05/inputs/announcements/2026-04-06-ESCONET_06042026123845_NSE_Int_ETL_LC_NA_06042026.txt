Dear Sir/ Ma’am, 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of the above-referred SEBI Circulars read with applicable circulars issued by the 
National Stock Exchange of India Limited from time to time, in respect of the framework for fund raising by 
issuance of debt securities by entities identified as “Large Corporates” (LC), we hereby submit as under: 
 
As per the criteria prescribed under Chapter XII of the SEBI Operational Circular dated August 10, 2021, an entity 
shall be classified as a “Large Corporate” if, as on the last day of the financial year, it satisfies all of the following 
conditions: 
 
1. The entity has its specified securities or debt securities or non-convertible redeemable preference shares 
listed on a recognized stock exchange in terms of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015;  
2. The entity has outstanding long-term borrowings of ₹1,000 Crore or above (excluding exemptions as 
specified under the aforesaid circular); and  
3. The entity has a credit rating of “AA” or above in respect of its unsupported bank borrowings or plain vanilla 
bonds. 
 
In this regard, we hereby confirm that Esconet Technologies Limited does not meet any of the aforesaid criteria, 
Accordingly, the Company does not fall under the category of a “Large Corporate” as on March 31, 2026. 
 
In view of the above, the requirements pertaining to submission of Initial Disclosure and Annual Disclosure as 
stipulated under the aforesaid SEBI Circulars, are not applicable to the Company for the Financial Year ended 
March 31, 2026. 
 
We request you to kindly take the same on record.
