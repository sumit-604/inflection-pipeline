## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

### 1A. One-line description
JITF Infralogistics is a two-layer company: a nearly empty holding-company shell (standalone) that earns management-consultancy fees from its subsidiaries (AR p.15, MGT-9), sitting on top of an operating group that builds and operates water/wastewater infrastructure under government contracts (JWIL Infra) and owns/runs waste-to-energy power plants under 20-30 year municipal concessions (JUIL and its SPVs), with a small residual steel-trading business.

### 1E. The chai-stall-uncle version
Think of this company like a family that owns two very different shops. One shop builds water pipelines for the government — you get paid as you lay each stretch of pipe, and sometimes you also get paid to maintain it for years afterward. The other shop burns the city's garbage to make electricity, and sells that electricity to the state power board at a fixed rate locked in for 20-30 years — like a long lease, steady but capped. The company itself, JITF Infralogistics, doesn't run either shop directly; it just charges a small "manager's fee" to the family businesses that do the real work, so almost all of what you'd care about as a shareholder is buried in the subsidiaries, not the parent's own books. And right now the family owes a lot more than it owns — the group's combined equity is actually negative, because a big chunk of the money funding these shops is unsecured loans from other family-owned companies, not banks.

### 2D. Industry lifecycle stage
Both sub-industries are in an **early-growth, policy-driven build-out phase**, not mature: India's urban sewage treatment gap is stated at 72,368 MLD generated vs only 26,776 MLD (37%) installed capacity and 20,263 MLD (28%) actually treated (MD&A p.79); WtE installed capacity is only 522 MW against an estimated 5,690 MW potential (MD&A p.81). The company's position is that of an early-mover incumbent scaling capacity (WtE portfolio target ~220 MW vs ~110 MW operational, MD&A p.83) inside a structurally underpenetrated, government-capex-funded market.

### 3A. Ignore-these, track-these

| Commonly tracked ratio | Why misleading/irrelevant here |

### 3D. Unit economics — the physics of the business

Because the group runs two structurally different economic engines, unit economics are defined separately.
