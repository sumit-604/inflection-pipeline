## 1. THE FINDING: BLOCK II IS ABOUT SIX QUARTERS LATE
Trace the same commitment across four filings.
- **Q2FY25 deck** (lnz3Mv p.7): "Civil Construction is ongoing on Block II of ~2 Lac Sq. Ft.
  and Completion **expected by Mar'25**."
- **Q3FY25 deck** (72Cf8g p.7): the same sentence, unchanged. "Completion expected by
  Mar'25."
- **Q4FY25 deck** (gYtwLk p.9): "Block II - **Civil construction nearing completion**." No
  date.
- **Q4FY26 deck** (Sutxnj p.6): "Block II of ~2 Lac Sq. Ft **expected to be commercialized
  by end of Q1FY27**."
- **Q1FY27 deck** (IcSouS p.9): "Block II of ~2 Lac Sq. Ft **partially commercialized in
  Q2FY27**."
From "complete by March 2025" to "partially commercialised in Q2FY27" is a slip of about
six quarters, and the latest wording adds the qualifier "partially". Block III is still
described as "construction progressing as planned", the identical phrase used in the Q3FY25
deck twenty months earlier.
This matters because the card's step 5 already found that plant, property and equipment did
not move at all across FY26 (Rs 1,482 Mn in both years) while capital work in progress rose
Rs 519 Mn. The capex is not being capitalised because the blocks are not opening.
