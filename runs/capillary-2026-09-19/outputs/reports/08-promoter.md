### 3D. Minority treatment

🚩 **Finding**: at the 06-Feb-2026 to 12-Mar-2026 postal ballot on ratifying the ESOP 2021
extension to subsidiary-company employees, **Public-Institutional shareholders voted 55.9%
AGAINST** the resolution (7,609,333 against vs 6,002,291 in favour, 78.24% of the
institutional block's shares polled) — this despite the company issuing a 24-Feb-2026
clarification (in response to unnamed proxy advisor feedback) capping the ESOP exercise-
price discount at 20% of 90-day VWAP. The resolution nonetheless **passed** (86.48% in
favour overall) purely because the Promoter & Promoter Group block voted 100% in favour
(41,195,648 of 41,467,159 shares polled) and Public-Non-Institutional (largely retail)
voted 99.995% in favour (Voting Results, Reg 44(3), filed 13-Mar-2026, Scrutinizer BMP &
Co. LLP). ✅ VERIFIED from the filed voting-results table. **Assessment**: this is a
genuine, numbers-based institutional-investor pushback signal against a promoter-board-
recommended resolution in the company's very first postal ballot as a listed entity — not
disqualifying, since the underlying pricing mechanism (max 20% discount to 90-day VWAP,
NRC-administered, standard ESOP economics) is unremarkable and the ratification was a
technical SEBI SBEB-Regulations compliance step (extending an existing pre-IPO scheme to
subsidiary employees) rather than a fresh dilution grab. But it shows institutional holders
were not automatically deferential to the promoter block on a dilution-adjacent vote, and
that the promoter block's near-52% stake was decisive in carrying a resolution the
institutional class rejected by a clear majority.

No unfair delisting attempt, no RPT pushed through without minority approval, no SCORES
complaint, and no proxy-advisory adverse recommendation beyond the ESOP feedback above was
found. AGM (28-Aug-2026) proceedings show ordinary business (accounts adoption, Aneesh
Reddy Boddu's rotational re-appointment) passing without recorded controversy; 35 members
attended by VC, standard for a company this size, three-week-old post-quarter close.
