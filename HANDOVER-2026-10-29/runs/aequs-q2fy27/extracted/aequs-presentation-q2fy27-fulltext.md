Revenue
The first half of FY27 marked continued progress in expanding our business and
strengthening our long-term growth opportunities. Revenue grew 50% YoY to ₹8,046 Mn,
driven by 42% growth in Aerospace and more than doubling of Consumer revenue on a
YoY basis. Our Aerospace order book stood at USD 1,096 Mn, providing long-term revenue
visibility. The Safran A320 aircraft wheels agreement marks our first serial-production
contract with a tenure exceeding 15 years. Together, these milestones give substance to
the long-term business we are building.
Aerospace continued to deliver strong growth and profitability, supported by healthy
demand and program execution. In Consumer, the Q2 volume ramp progressed slower
than anticipated due to extended qualification timelines and supply chain constraints. We
view this as a timing-related shift rather than a change in the underlying opportunity, with
customer programs continuing to progress. Given these factors, we are recalibrating the
path to Consumer EBITDA breakeven, with our near-term focus on improving utilisation
and translating installed capacity into profitable growth.
To support our next phase of growth, we continue to invest in our manufacturing
capabilities. H1 FY27 capital expenditure stood at ₹1,597 Mn, and the ₹6,500 Mn promoter
warrant is intended to support the next phase of expansion, including Hosur. Following
quarter-end, new Safran agreements expanded our relationships to six entities. As these
capabilities and partnerships grow, our focus remains on disciplined execution and
stronger returns.
