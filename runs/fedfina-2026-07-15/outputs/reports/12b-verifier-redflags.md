## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts alone)

Numbered IF# items. Severity is my own; "caught status" is assigned in Part 2.

**IF1 — Headline gold AUM growth is overwhelmingly gold-price-driven; tonnage flat; the
"seasonal tonnage pickup in Q3/Q4" narrative did not materialise in absolute tonnage.**
Q1 (Parvez opening, p4): "Tonnage growth for the year came in at 10% Y-o-Y to 11.2 tons,"
gold AUM ₹6,332cr. Q2 (CVG, p6): "our tonnage growth has been flat." Q3 (Parvez opening,
p4): "tonnage growth for the year came in at 5% Y-o-Y... touching 11.2 tons in totality,"
gold AUM ₹7,905cr. So tonnage is 11.2 tons in Q1 and still 11.2 tons in Q3 — flat across
the fiscal — while gold AUM rose ~25% over the two quarters and 52% Y-o-Y. Roughly all of
the headline gold growth is gold-price appreciation, not volume. Across Q1 and Q2
management repeatedly deflected flat-tonnage concern with "it picks up in Q3 and Q4"
(Q2, Parvez, p11); in Q3, the actual pickup quarter, absolute tonnage did not visibly
grow. Revenue recognition is additionally back-ended (Q3, Jagadeesh/CVG, p17), so even the
price-driven AUM lags into the P&L. This is a growth-quality flag on the single engine
funding the entire turnaround. Severity: MAJOR.

**IF2 — Asset-quality disclosure opacity: Stage 2 refused (Q1) and old/new book split
withheld (Q3).** Q1 (CVG, p11): "So Stage 2, we don't disclose. We normally don't give
that information." Q3 (CVG, p16): within the ~40% small-ticket portion of the mortgage
book, "there is a further split between old book and new book. Now that is not public
information," and the intra-old-book geographic split is likewise not given. The company
whose central thesis risk is ST LAP asset quality declines to disclose the two datasets
(Stage 2 flow indicator; old-vintage book size) that would let an outsider size that
exact risk. Severity: MAJOR.

**IF3 — Weak cure rate on a secured book, analyst-flagged, deflected.** Q2 (Shubhranshu
Mishra, p9): "1-plus is hovering anywhere between 7.5% to 8% and 30-plus is around 4.5% to
5%. So we are able to cure somewhere around 30%, which seems a lower number, given the
fact that these are secured exposures." Management answered with rebuild context, no cure
data. Q3 (CVG, p5) shows only marginal improvement (1+ 7.5→7.1%, 30+ 4.6→4.5%). A ~30%
cure rate on collateralised paper is a genuine collections-effectiveness signal.
Severity: MAJOR.

**IF4 — Gold-mix guidance walked from a disciplined 45-49% range to "unconstrained."**
Q1 (Parvez to Digant, p9): gold mix "will remain between that 45% to 49%... we will be in
that range between 45% to 48%." Q3 (CVG to Chintan, p11-12): "there is no board limit or
internal limit... the lakshmanrekha of 50%. So we don't have any lakshmanrekha... our gold
loan growth will be unconstrained." The self-imposed mix ceiling was quietly removed,
concentrating the book further into a gold-price-cyclical product — in tension with the
Q1 claim of wanting to "minimize cyclicality on the earnings" (Q1, CVG, p14).
Severity: MAJOR.

**IF5 — Implied >15% ROE extrapolation (Q1) vs 12.7% actual (Q3).** Q1 (Renish, p14):
frames ">15% ROE"; CVG replies "that will lead you to a number like the one you seem to be
indicating within a certain timeline." Q3 (CVG opening, p5): ROE 12.7%. Hedged
("within a certain timeline"), so soft, but it is an optimistic steer on the key return
metric that the actual trajectory has not yet met. Severity: MINOR.

**IF6 — PCR fell to ~32% via ARC sale + ECL refresh while GNPA rose and Stage 2 is
undisclosed.** Q2 (CVG, p6-7): PCR ~32% after selling deeper-bucket NPAs and the ECL
refresh, down from ~38-40%. Q3 (CVG, p5): GNPA up to 2.1% on higher stage2→stage3 forward
flows, PCR ~32.3%. A falling PCR into a rising-GNPA quarter, with Stage 2 hidden, is worth
flagging as a provisioning-adequacy watch item. Management gave a coherent product-mix
explanation (small vs medium-ticket LGD). Severity: MINOR.

**IF7 — Recurring "look at core yield, exclude DA optics" analyst-steering.** Q1 (CVG, p5)
normative-yield adjustment; Q3 (CVG, p8): "our yields... are vitiated by the DA income
number... I would request everybody to look at the yield, excluding the impact of DA."
Legitimate as far as it goes, but it is a repeated pattern of directing analysts away from
the reported line whenever optics are unfavourable. Severity: MINOR.

**IF8 — ECL refresh timing driven by an RBI supervisory observation.** Q2 (CVG to Pawan,
p16): "when we had our regulatory supervision, there were some observations around
strengthening the documentation around the ECL exercise. So we took the opportunity to
mitigate the observation." A volunteered but real supervisory finding on provisioning
documentation. Severity: MAJOR (but see Part 2 — caught).

**IF9 — CRAR/Tier 2 disclosure dropped in Q3, the quarter leverage rose most.** Q2 (CVG,
p6): CRAR 21.64%, "we will take steps to supplement our Tier 2 during H2 FY '26." Q3: no
CRAR figure and no Tier 2 update anywhere in the call, while D/E rose 3.78→3.99 (Q3 CVG,
p5) and gold disbursal hit an all-time ₹7,853cr. Severity: MAJOR (caught).

**IF10 — Collections in-housing completion slipped two quarters.** Q1 (Parvez opening,
p2): "full completion expected by early Q2." Q3 (Parvez to Nischint, p8): "which would
happen by Q4." Severity: MAJOR (caught).

**IF11 — ST LAP disbursals flat despite doubled collection headcount.** Q2 ₹206cr / Q3
₹208cr (+1% QoQ), against Q2 (Parvez, p10) headcount "200... increased it to about 400."
Severity: MAJOR (caught).

**IF12 — ST LAP credit-scorecard/risk-model rollout (dated Q3 FY26) never confirmed
live.** Q1 (Parvez, p3): "credit scorecards, risk models targeted for rollout by Q3 FY
'26." Q2/Q3 reference only the BRE transition as complete; the scorecard/risk-model layer
is not confirmed. Severity: MEDIUM (caught; see overstatement note in Part 3).

**IF13 — MT LAP BRE/scorecard pilot dropped.** Q1 (Parvez, p3): "a pilot planned in the
coming quarters"; no follow-up Q2/Q3. Severity: LOW (caught).

**IF14 — Internal branch-count inconsistency in the Q3 call.** Q3 opening (Parvez, p4):
"54 new gold branches this quarter... 113 for the year"; later (Jagadeesh, p10): "we have
opened 58 branches in the last quarter." (57+54 also = 111, not 113.) Severity: LOW
(caught).

**IF15 — GNPA uptick in Q3 alongside a "complete control" framing.** Gross Stage 3
1.9→2.1% (Q3, CVG/Parvez opening, p5); Parvez (p8): "We are in complete control of those
flows." Severity: LOW (caught).

**IF16 — Q1 geographic branch-mix discrepancy never resolved.** Q1 (Mayank/Renish, p12,
14): "let me check that and get back"; no correction in Q2/Q3. Severity: LOW (caught).

**IF17 — Credit-cost guidance not raised despite three quarters of over-delivery; repeated
deflection.** Core credit cost ~0.7-0.9%; Q2 (Digant, p8-9) and Q3 (Sandeep Joshi, p17)
asked to lower the 1% band / give an FY27 number; both declined/deferred. Severity: MAJOR
(caught).

**IF18 — ST-LAP-normalisation timeline repeatedly slipping.** Q1 "by Q3" (p18) → Q2 "6
months away" (p10) → Q3 "by Q4 / FY27" (p8, p18). Severity: MAJOR (caught, repeated).

**IF19 — Tamil Nadu ST LAP stress persistent and pushed out.** Q3 (Pavan, p9): "still wait
for Q4... moved 20-30%... still cautious there in Tamil Nadu." Severity: LOW-MEDIUM
(caught).

## PART 4: CREDIBILITY-GRADE ASSESSMENT

B05 assigned credibility_grade **B**. I **concur**, at the low end of B.

Headline delivery is genuinely strong and honest: credit cost inside the 1% ± 10bps band
every quarter, full secured-mix transition executed and reconciled, DA reduction delivered,
consistent unprompted "it was our issue, not the environment" framing, and candid
disclosure of the ARC sale, write-off correction (₹9-10cr vs an analyst's ₹50-60cr guess),
labour-code charge, and even the RBI ECL-documentation observation. That supports B and
argues against anything lower.

Pulling toward the low end: the asset-quality disclosure opacity (Stage 2 refused; old/new
book split "not public information"), the weak ~30% cure rate left unexplained, the
gold-mix walkback to "unconstrained" against an earlier "minimize cyclicality" claim, and
the fact that the entire turnaround is funded by a gold engine whose headline growth is
~90% gold price, not volume (tonnage flat at 11.2 tons across the fiscal). These are the
kinds of items a management earns a grade on the strength of its disclosures for — and here
the disclosures thin out exactly on the most thesis-relevant risk. B, not B+, and I would
not defend anything above B.
