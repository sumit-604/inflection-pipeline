# VERIFIER B (B12b) — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG AUDIT
## Millworks Technologies Limited | Run 2026-08-22 | NO-CONCALL MODE

**Scope.** Millworks holds no earnings calls (just-listed BSE SME, 21-Jul-2026).
Per the degradation rule, I audited stages 5 (B05-concall) and 6 (B06-peers)
against the RHP management narrative and the one post-listing Reg 30 filing,
NOT against transcripts. I read both Millworks sources myself, fresh, scanned
independently for red flags across revenue quality, related-party structure,
cash conversion, customer/supplier concentration, governance and compliance,
then compared what I found against what the upstream stages caught. Peer
transcripts were used only as context (sector-wide vs idiosyncratic); the peer
coverage audit itself is Verifier D's job.

**Anchoring.** All RHP citations use the embedded "PAGE N of 365" extracted
markers. In the Risk Factors chapter the printed page equals the extracted
page (extracted PAGE 24 = printed "24 | Page"); in the Business chapter the
printed number runs ~7 behind the extracted marker, so B05's "p.130"-style
citations there point to printed pages, not extracted markers. That anchor-base
drift is a Verifier A matter; I flag it once here and anchor my own findings to
the unambiguous extracted markers.

## PART 5: CREDIBILITY GRADE

B05 assigned grade C (NO-CONCALL default floor; B unreachable cleanly, A
impossible in this mode). I CONCUR with C. The additional structure I surfaced
(V3 Technologies related-party dealings, the BBB / Bill-to-Ship-to execution

## PART 6: ASSESSMENT

B05 caught the material financial red flags cleanly and anchored them well:
receivables quality, customer concentration, cash conversion, capex timing,
growth durability and compliance are all present and correctly severity-rated,
and B06's peer work strengthens the receivables flag by showing it is
idiosyncratic, not sector-wide. Nothing was invented.

The gap is a cluster on the RIGHT-HAND side of the business: the RELATED-PARTY
and SUPPLY structure. B05 treated related-party risk as a Quick-Pay-only story
and missed V3 Technologies entirely, missed the BBB / Bill-to-Ship-to execution
dependency, and missed supplier concentration. Each was within the stage's
explicit NO-CONCALL scan remit. None is fatal on its own, and all are disclosed
in the RHP, so no CRITICAL. But three MAJOR misses in one structural theme is a
real completeness gap and puts acceptance at 62%, just above the REWORK line.
Recommend synthesis pick up H, I and J before the valuation stage weighs
governance and revenue quality.
