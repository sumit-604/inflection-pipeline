Mandatory diagnostic answers:
1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, in value. GST-net revenue rose 39.63% (Q2 FY27 101.43 Cr vs Q2 FY26 72.64 Cr). The filing gives no volume or realisation, so the split between units sold and price is ND (see the revenue-quality readings below). Company guidance: ND (filing has no forward statement, A1 FORWARD = 0). Base-case projection: ND (no thesis).
2. **Operating EBITDA margin?** Contracted. Q2 FY27 margin of 13.22% vs Q2 FY26 margin of 15.60% = -238 bps change. Sequentially Q1 FY27 to Q2 FY27 it fell from 14.81% to 13.22% (-159 bps). The driver is gross margin: 37.29% to 31.16% YoY (-613 bps). A lower opex ratio offset part of it: employee plus other expenses excl. GST fell from 21.70% of net revenue (15.76 / 72.64) to 17.94% (18.20 / 101.43), +375 bps (-613 + 375 = -238). Whether that +375 bps is fixed-cost leverage or a larger price-led denominator is open (Reading 3 below).
3. **Did core operating PBT grow YoY?** Yes. Core PBT rose 19.42% (11.99 Cr vs 10.04 Cr). Reported PBT rose 19.18%. The headline growth is real in rupees at the operating level. It is far below revenue growth of 39.63%.
4. **Gap between core PBT growth (+19.42%) and PAT growth (+22.47%):** Other Income +0.16 Cr pre-tax (adds about 0.12 Cr post-tax); finance cost +0.11 Cr (already inside core PBT); ETR fell from 27.18% to 25.17%, adding 0.26 Cr to PAT (13.11 x 2.01 pp; A3 F-04); exceptional items nil. The ETR effect explains most of the 3.05 pp gap.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** D&A no: +1.72% vs revenue +39.63%. Finance cost yes in percentage (+84.62%) but tiny in rupees (0.24 Cr, 1.8% of Op EBITDA). No absorption deficit yet. CWIP of 3.97 Cr (L321) has not capitalised, so a D&A step-up is ahead (F-08).
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** No material change. OI/PBT 8.54% in Q2 FY27 vs 8.73% in Q2 FY26; FY26 5.51%. Q1 FY27 was 4.86%, so Q2 OI rose 0.53 Cr QoQ. Stripping OI does not change the read: core PBT +19.42% YoY.

Revenue quality: three readings (A5-M3 graft). The filing discloses revenue in value only. Volume, realisation per unit and capacity are ND.
- Reading 1, resin lag. Resin cost rose, the company passes it on with a lag, volume grew strongly, and the conversion-cost ratio fell through fixed-cost leverage. Margin recovers as prices catch up.
- Reading 2, mix or price cuts. The company shifted toward lower-margin products or exports, or cut price to win volume. Margin stays down.
- Reading 3, price-led revenue on modest volume. Gross profit rose 16.69% and conversion costs 15.48%, against net revenue +39.63% and material cost +53.28% (FILED, derived). If conversion costs move with volume, volume grew near 15%, and resin pass-through in price carried the rest of the 39.63% [INFERENCE]. On this reading the +375 bps opex-ratio gain is a larger denominator, not fixed-cost leverage. It reverses when resin falls and revenue follows it down, and the gross-margin loss is the true unit-margin change. The quarterly cost pattern points both ways: other expenses excl. GST rose 14.78% QoQ (812 to 932, L88) against net revenue +16.15%, near-proportional, but only 1.37% from Q2 FY26 to Q1 FY27 (801 to 812) against +20.22%.
