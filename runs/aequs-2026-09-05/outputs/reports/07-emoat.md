### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Historical fixed asset turnover (company-wide, consolidated), as stated by the
CFO on the Q4 FY26 call (Dinesh Iyer, 26-May-2026): **FY25 = 1.84x, FY26 =
1.18x** ("Fixed asset turnover moderated to 1.18x in FY26 from 1.84x in FY25").
I anchor to this concall statement rather than the AR's own p.14 "Key Financial
Highlights" infographic, because Stage 1 (B01) already found that infographic's
column order unreliable on text extraction (a Net Debt/Equity cross-check failed
~4x) and poppler-utils is not available in this environment to re-render it
visually; the concall figures carry explicit, unambiguous year labels from the
CFO and are treated as the reliable source here.

FY27 planned capex = Rs 660 Cr (🎙️, Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls).

- **Using FY26's actual (depressed) turnover, 1.18x:** Rs 660 Cr × 1.18 = **Rs
  778.8 Cr** implied incremental revenue potential = 778.8 / 1,230.44 (FY26
  revenue) = **63.3%** of FY26 revenue.
- **Using FY25's turnover, 1.84x** (before the underutilised Consumer Electronics
  assets depressed the ratio): Rs 660 Cr × 1.84 = **Rs 1,214.4 Cr** = **98.7%**
  of FY26 revenue.
- **Using management's own segment-specific target** for the Consumer capex slice
  only (🎙️, Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls: "our consumer asset turns overall would be
  about 1.5x and that remains stable"): Rs 500 Cr (of the Rs 660 Cr, Consumer's
  original share, now conditional) × 1.5 = **Rs 750 Cr** incremental Consumer
  revenue potential against a Consumer base of only Rs 184 Cr in FY26 — implying
  Consumer could be roughly 4-5x its current size once this single capex layer is
  fully utilised (a multi-year outcome, not a same-year one).

**capex_embedded_growth_pct reported = 63%** (the FY26-turnover-based figure,
the most recent and most conservative of the three), with the range 63%-99%
flagged explicitly as the honest uncertainty band. The spread exists because the
ratio's denominator (net fixed assets) already carries the newly added,
still-underutilised Consumer Electronics assets, which mechanically depresses
FY26's multiplier versus FY25's; neither year is obviously "the" right base, and
no aerospace-only or consumer-only fixed asset turnover series exists in this
corpus to settle it cleanly (NOT FOUND).

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

### FAMILY A — Product & Technology

**A1. Rare manufacturing capability.** Aequs already operates India's largest
declared aerospace machining footprint and several genuine "firsts": India's
first notified Engineering & Manufacturing SEZ (Belagavi, 2009), India's largest
hydraulic press (10,000 tons), the first Indian surface-treatment facility
approved by both Airbus and Boeing, and now the first fully assembled,
100%-India-made Airbus A320 wheel (all 📄, AR p.18; Q1 FY27 call). The
FORMING piece — not yet in the financials — is the Hosur aero-engine/landing-
gear ecosystem: a genuinely new, higher-complexity capability category, with an
MoU signed and a Phase-1 timeline given, but revenue only from FY29 and a
"roadmap [that] is obviously internal" (Aravind Melligeri, Q1 FY27 call), i.e.
no public execution detail beyond the headline numbers. Global competitor count
for engine/landing-gear at this scale in India NOT FOUND. **Strength: Moderate.
Evidence: 📄 (MoU, capex committed). Time to materialise: 24-36 months to first
revenue (FY29), longer to full vertical integration (~2030, 🎙️).**

**A2. Patent and IP pipeline.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Management explicitly
disclaims a patent strategy: "we don't really patent them, that's more of an
internal process what we consider" (Q4 FY26 call, Priyansh Miri Q&A). The AR's
own standalone Technology Absorption annexure states, for every disclosed
innovation item: "Expenditure incurred on Research and Development — NIL" (AR
Board's Report, Form B). **Score 0.**

**A3. Process innovation.** The Board's Report technology-absorption annexure
discloses five specific internal tools/process changes in FY26 (📄, statutory
disclosure, not marketing copy): a Capex-request digitisation tool (ROI
Management System), a Material Coverage Application, a Quality Control System
application, Tilted Work Plane 3D/On-Machine-Inspection closed-loop machining,
and Horizontal Honing Technology (imported from Deepmaya Honing, 2025, "100%
absorbed"). All are incremental efficiency/quality tools, not yield or unit-cost
disclosures, and each again shows "R&D expenditure — NIL." **Strength: Weak.
Evidence: 📄. Time to materialise: already ongoing/incremental (0-12m).**

**A4. Product platform / modular architecture.** SKU count has grown from 5,221
(Dec-2025, Q3 FY26 call) → 5,654 (Mar-2026, AR p.11) → 5,740 (Jun-2026, Q1 FY27
Investor Pres slide 5), i.e. accelerating quarter to quarter, without disclosed
proportional capex or R&D growth (R&D itself is NIL, per A2/A3 above), and
management explicitly frames this as shared-infrastructure leverage: "our
integrated ecosystem gives a strong operating leverage, shared infrastructure
across ecosystems" (Q3 FY26 call, Rajeev Kaul). **Strength: Moderate. Evidence:
📄 (SKU counts disclosed each quarter). Time to materialise: ongoing (0-12m).**
*Caution: the AR p.19 SKU-by-category table (Landing Systems/Engine
Systems/Assemblies/Interiors & Cargo/Structures/Turnings/Actuation Systems
against 187/283/73/519/244/121/4,227) has a text-extraction order that cannot be
reliably mapped category-to-number from this corpus; I use only the
prose-stated totals above, not the category breakdown, for the same reason B01
excluded the AR p.14 chart.*
