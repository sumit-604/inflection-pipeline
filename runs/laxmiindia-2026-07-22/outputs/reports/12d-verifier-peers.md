B06 is a well-constructed, materially useful peer-triangulation report. Two issues keep this from a clean pass:
1. **MAJOR** — the Q2 DA-dependency claim is labeled VERIFIED on single-peer (UGRO-only) evidence, violating the stage's own Rule 4 (should be PARTIALLY VERIFIED), and inconsistently so given the report correctly downgrades other single/dual-peer claims (Q4, Q6) elsewhere.

Neither finding would flip the net_narrative_effect ("complicates") or change the decision-relevant substance of B06's output; the underlying peer evidence for both the flood-framing contradiction and the DA-dependency corroboration is real, findable, and accurately transcribed. The issue is verdict-label rigor on one claim, not fabrication or misreading of the transcripts.
