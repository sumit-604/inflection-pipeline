## BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

TOTEM makes round metal cutting tools. The catalogue is taps, dies, drills,
thread mills, end mills, reamers and burrs in high speed steel and in carbide,
plus spring lock washers. Manufactured finished goods are 98.96% of revenue.
Traded goods, service income, export incentives and scrap sales together make
up the remaining 1%. The tool cuts metal and wears out, so the customer buys
the same tool again, which makes this a consumable stream and not a machine
sale.

Customers are named only as classes. The annual report names automotive and
auto components, defence, railways, aerospace, oil and gas and general
engineering. No customer is named and no concentration is published. The
company reports one segment and gives no product line, industry or geography
split of revenue, so the run could not size any end market from filings. Sales
run through a distributor channel of 200 plus partners, 12 divisional sales
offices and 60 plus application engineers, all figures from the 2024
Information Memorandum and never restated since. The switching cost is
application qualification at aerospace, defence and auto accounts, backed by
ISO 9001, IATF and AS 9100D certification. Exports were 12.7% of FY26 revenue.

Demand today is Indian metal cutting activity. The run named six external
signals that carry it. IMTMA machine tool production and consumption data
leads consumable demand by 12 to 15 months on Kennametal's own testimony. SIAM
automotive production volumes track the largest named end market. Kennametal
India quarterly results act as the listed sector proxy. US and Mexico tariff
policy is named in the FY26 MD&A as the cause of the export fall. HSS and
tungsten carbide import prices from Europe and China drive 31% of material
cost. Defence and Aerospace procurement budgets sit behind the third named
growth claim.

Demand should grow for three reasons the run could check outside the company.
The conservative India round tools market is Rs 6,650 cr and grows 5.8% a year,
against a current served share of 3.8%. Migration from single point high speed
steel toward CNC and carbide is confirmed in Kennametal commentary, though
Kennametal excludes high speed steel and taps from its own remarks, so it
speaks to the smaller carbide slice only. Two capacity items went in during
FY26, an austempering furnace adding 90 MT and CNC lines adding about 350,000
high speed steel drill units a year.

Competitive advantage cannot be read per business line, because the company
publishes no line level revenue. That is a finding, not a gap papered over. The
emerging moat scan carried evidence on 5 of 23 rows and cleared 3. Process
innovation and qualification lock in, meaning a customer must requalify any new
supplier, are both documented Strong across two or three consecutive annual
reports. The execution moat is Moderate on the capex completion leg alone; the
promise leg does not match it. Categories 21 and 22 both scored zero. The scan
total is 9.7 and the classification is NONE. Both documented moats close a gap
to global majors rather than open one.

## Flat profit three years, capacity now installed

TOTEM sells branded consumable cutting tools from one plant in Chhatrapati
Sambhajinagar. It was demerged from Forbes and Company, the Scheme effective
01-Mar-2024, and listed on BSE on 11-Jun-2024, so it has three years of its own
audited history. The promoter is Shapoorji Pallonji and Company. The company
holds no earnings call and publishes no investor presentation; the FY26 annual
report says at page 52 that it does not present to institutional investors and
analysts.

Revenue was Rs 228.5 cr in FY24, Rs 232.7 cr in FY25 and Rs 251.0 cr in FY26.
Profit was Rs 29.7 cr, Rs 28.8 cr and Rs 28.8 cr. Three years of revenue growth
produced no profit growth. Then Q1 FY27 printed revenue Rs 67.6 cr, up 28.9%,
operating margin 22.9% against 16.1%, and profit Rs 9.0 cr.

The claimed transition is operating leverage on new capacity plus a mix shift
from commodity high speed steel toward carbide and high performance tools. One
leg is delivered and filed: the furnace and the CNC drill lines are in. The mix
leg has no filed evidence at all, because no segment or product split is
published in any year.

Gate 0 scores core 58, moat block 20 of 60, grand total 78 of 160, with four
moats confirmed and moat class STRONG. The classification is AVOID and it is
mechanical. Core 58 sits in the AVERAGE band, the pledge deal breaker caps at
AVERAGE, and a three year LIMITED history downgrade takes it one tier lower.
The history is short because of the March 2024 demerger, not because of
anything the business did.

Cash went the wrong way in FY26. Operating cash flow fell from Rs 51.3 cr to
Rs 27.6 cr, a 46% fall, on an inventory build from Rs 31.9 cr to Rs 56.4 cr.
The quick ratio fell below 1.0 for the first time. The annual report gives no
inventory ageing, no write down and no movement in the obsolescence provision,
so stock built for the drill ramp and stock that did not sell look identical in
the filings.

Management credibility grades C, which is the floor of the scale in no earnings
call mode. Ten annual report claims were tested against later results: one
delivered, two partial, seven missed. The export claim is the worst of them.
The FY25 report called the export trend improving and the FY26 report called it
stable, while FOB exports fell 15.9% to Rs 3,189 lakh. The direction was stated
backwards two years running, and stated correctly only in the annexure the Act
forces.

Peers complicate the read rather than confirming it. Wendt India reported
exports down 12% in the same fiscal window TOTEM called improving, which is the
single strongest contradiction in the corpus. Kennametal's own record on price
pass through is mixed across three calls, which makes TOTEM's softer language
look company specific and not a sector ceiling. Birla Precision has no
transcript in the corpus at all. The newest Kennametal call is from March 2024.

The market is not the constraint. Conservative India round tools TAM is
Rs 6,650 cr growing 5.8% a year, TOTEM serves 3.8% of it, and the runway class
is STRONG. But the reachable share over three to five years implies a revenue
CAGR of 20.7% to 22.1%, which sits below the strategy's 25% hurdle, and it
leaves a gap of about Rs 180 cr by year three against the 3.3% a year organic
signal embedded in disclosed capex.

The bull reading is straightforward. Real capacity went in during FY26, Q1 FY27
printed 28.9% growth with margin up, the balance sheet is near net cash with
ROCE about 22%, and there is 26 times revenue headroom in the served market.

The bear reading is equally straightforward. Profit has not grown in three
years. Q1 FY26 was FY26's trough quarter, and Q1 FY27's PBT margin sits four
points below Q4 FY26, so the gain over the FY26 full year is 1.8 points and not
6.8. Kennametal grew 47.7% and Wendt 36.6% in the same June 2026 quarter while
Birla Precision was flat, which reads as a cycle concentrated among recent
capex holders. Kennametal's own structural commentary describes migration away
from single point high speed steel, which runs against the one delivered
trigger.

Neither reading is settled, and nothing in this corpus settles it. What
separates them is one number and one disclosure: whether operating margin holds
above 20% for a second consecutive quarter in Q2 FY27, and whether the FY27
annual report finally shows inventory ageing.

One fact sits above both readings. 94.4% of the 73.85% promoter holding is
pledged to Catalyst Trusteeship, which is 100% of Shapoorji Pallonji and
Company's own 69.71% direct stake, unchanged across the June 2025, March 2026
and June 2026 filings, against a live group refinancing crisis rated CareEdge
B+ in May 2026. The operating company itself sends the group no loans, no
deposits and no guarantees, and its auditor and secretarial records are clean.
The pledge appears in none of the three annual reports.
