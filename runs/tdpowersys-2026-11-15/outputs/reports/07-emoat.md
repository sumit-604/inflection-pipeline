### 2C. Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic)
- Historical fixed-asset turnover, FY26 consolidated: revenue Rs Mn 18,562.34 (Pres. slide 34) over PPE plus CWIP at Mar-26 of Rs Mn 2,642.99 + 173.25 = 2,816.24 (Pres. slide 31). Turnover = 6.59x.
- Capex under execution that has an amount: Rs 50 cr (FY27 debottlenecking). Implied revenue = 50 x 6.59 = Rs 329.6 cr. That is 17.8% of FY26 revenue of Rs 1,856.2 cr, and 11.4% of the Rs 2,900 cr low-end FY27 guide.
- Excluded because NOT FOUND: the large-generator capex and the FY29-FY30 plan. Counting nothing for them is not a view that they are small.
- Cross-check on the Rs 3,200 cr claim: it sits Rs 1,343.8 cr above FY26 revenue. Unit 3 plus FY25 capex of Rs 164.5 cr (Rs 55.97 cr + Rs 108.52 cr, B01 data notes, AR p.198) x 6.59 = Rs 1,084 cr. Adding Rs 329.6 cr gives Rs 1,414 cr. The capacity claim and the capex arithmetic land within about Rs 70 cr of each other. This is a consistency check, not proof of capacity.
- Using CRISIL's Rs 60-80 cr a year instead gives 21-28% (my arithmetic). The 17.8% figure carries forward.

### FAMILY I: STRUCTURAL ASYMMETRIES

**I1 Talent asymmetry.** Score 0. Part (a) is a hiring story at best: an ex L&T-MHI and GE CEO. No named inventors, no patent trail, no remuneration annexure analysis of technical hires (NOT FOUND). Part (b), that competitors cannot pay for this class without breaking their economics, has no evidence. Top-band rule needs one 📄 source for leg (b). None exists.

**I2 Cannibalization barrier.** Score 0. For each moat claimed: what must the best-resourced competitor destroy to copy it?
- OEM qualification (B2): a rival must win OEM qualification. It destroys nothing.
- Siemens framework (H2): Siemens Energy outsources because its own capacity is full. The cost to the partner is a make-or-buy choice, not a P&L sacrifice. Execution lead.
- Large generators (A1): Siemens, Baker Hughes and the Japanese makers sell their own generators. Nothing in a competitor filing in this corpus shows a pricing regime, channel or product line they would give up to match TDPS. No competitor filings were read.
- Installed base (C1): no 10-year service lock-in (Q1C p.11).
The honest answer is "nothing must be destroyed". Execution lead, not configuration.
