## 8. TRADE RECEIVABLES (Note 15 standalone p.128-129, Note 14 consol p.177-178) - PRIORITY FINDING

- Gross trade receivables (considered good): standalone Rs 20,673.82 Lk (FY24) -> Rs 27,764.58 Lk (FY25),
  +34.3%; consolidated Rs 23,465.34 Lk (FY24) -> Rs 33,765.86 Lk (FY25), +43.9%.
- ECL allowance: standalone/consol Rs 1,615.60 Lk (FY24) -> Rs 1,039.13 Lk (FY25), a *decrease* of 35.7% in
  absolute provision even as gross receivables grew.
- Ageing (standalone, Note 15 table p.129): >6 months overdue (sum of 6mo-1yr + 1-2yr + 2-3yr buckets) rose
  from Rs 6,783.79 Lk (FY24: 5,855.72 + 928.07) to Rs 16,338.50 Lk (FY25: 11,869.31 + 4,469.19) - a 141%
  increase, while total receivables grew only 34.3%. As a share of gross receivables, the >6-month bucket
  rose from 32.8% (FY24) to 58.8% (FY25). The consolidated ageing table shows the same absolute >6-month
  figures (Rs 6,783.79 Lk -> Rs 16,338.50 Lk) against a larger base, i.e. 28.9% -> 48.4% of consolidated
  gross receivables.
- Yet the company's own ratio disclosure (Note 48(l) standalone, p.151) reports "Trade Receivables Turnover
  Ratio (in days)" *improving* from 179 days (FY24) to 138 days (FY25), a -23% "improvement." This ratio
  uses average receivables against revenue and is dominated by the large FY25 revenue growth denominator; it
  masks the underlying ageing deterioration visible in the Note 15 ageing schedule.
- 🔴 Red flag: at the same time ageing worsened (58.8% of standalone receivables now >6 months overdue vs
  32.8% a year ago), the ECL allowance was *reduced* by Rs 576.47 Lk net (standalone Note 36 Other Expenses,
  "Provision for doubtful debts (576.48)" - a P&L *credit*, versus a Rs 724.54 Lk *charge* in FY24). The
  consolidated P&L shows a similar net credit of Rs (479.88) Lk in FY25 vs a Rs 840.73 Lk charge in FY24.
  This ECL reversal directly inflated reported profit in a year when the underlying receivable quality (by
  the company's own ageing disclosure) deteriorated materially. This is the single most significant
  accounting-quality finding in the notes and should be tested against actual subsequent collections.
  [Note 15 ageing table + Note 36 Other Expenses, standalone, p.129 and p.138; Note 14 + Note 35, consol,
  p.177-178 and p.196]
- Disappeared disclosure: FY2024 AR's Trade Receivables note (Note 15, p.143 FY2024 AR) carried point "iv.
  Balance confirmation from customers was called for by the Company. The company has received few
  confirmations, balance are awaited." This caveat is entirely absent from the FY2025 AR's equivalent note
  (only points i-iii remain, standalone p.129 and consol p.178). No statement that confirmations were
  since obtained. Silent removal of an auditor-relevant caveat, worth a management question. 🟡
- Single customer concentration: "revenue arising from one customer in India... more than 10%" (new in
  FY25; "Nil" in FY24) and "one customer outside India... more than 10%" both years (Note 41/segment,
  standalone p.149). No customer name or exact % disclosed. 🟡 Watch (concentration risk, unquantified).
- Related-party receivables: Safa Otomotiv FZ-LLC (Dubai subsidiary) Trade Receivable-Dr of Rs 1,301.27 Lk
  shown as UNCHANGED between FY25 and FY24 in the FY2025 AR's RPT table (Note 47, p.149). See Pass-3 pattern
