- **Impairment loss recognised on Financial Asset: (0.24)** in FY24 (a

rising to 101.67 from 42.46. So the FY24 margin jump is NOT explained by
one-off credits. If anything, one-offs were a headwind in FY24.

## 10. IPO-related expenses: treatment

> "The Company made Initial Public Offer of upto 95,70,000 Equity Shares at a
> price of Rs. 136 per Equity Share including a premium of Rs. 126 per share by
> way of Offer for Sale by its promoter selling shareholders viz. Mr.
> Jogindersingh Gianchand Jaswal, Mr. Ketan Ramani and Mr. Pritesh
> Vinodchandra Ramani" (KRONOX AR FY24, p.33, Directors Report)

The IPO was **100% Offer for Sale (OFS)** by the three promoter selling
shareholders. There was **no fresh issue**. Evidence:
- Issued/subscribed capital is UNCHANGED: 3,71,04,000 shares of Rs 10 =
  3,710.40 lakh in both FY24 and FY23 (KRONOX AR FY24, p.111, Share Capital
  note). No new shares were issued in the IPO.
- Post-listing: promoters 74.21% (2,75,34,000 shares), public 25.79%
  (95,70,000 shares) — the 95,70,000 public shares came OUT of promoter
  holdings, confirming OFS (p.33).

Consequence for IPO-expense treatment:
- **P&L:** NOT DISCLOSED as any IPO/offer-expense line in the P&L. No such line
  in Note 26 or elsewhere in the FY24 statements.
- **Securities premium:** NO securities-premium movement — because there was no
  fresh issue, the company received no share proceeds and booked no premium.
- **Borne by selling shareholders:** in a pure OFS the company receives no
  proceeds, so offer expenses fall on the selling shareholders, not the
  company. The financial statements carry no company-borne IPO cost. The FY24
  profit therefore is not depressed by IPO expense; the margin is clean of any
  IPO-cost distortion.

(FY23 did carry an "Increase of Authorised Capital Expense 29.25" inside Note
26, tied to the FY23 bonus issue / capital restructuring, nil in FY24 — this is
pre-IPO housekeeping, not IPO offer cost.)

## 12. Note 1 Significant Accounting Policies (Note 1, p.88-89)

**Revenue recognition (policy 9, p.89):**
> "Revenue from sale of goods is recognised when control of the goods or
> services are transferred to the customer at an amount that reflects the
> consideration entitled ... Generally, control is transferred upon shipment of
> goods to the customer or when the goods is made available to the customer,
> provided transfer of title to the customer occurs and the Company has not
> retained any significant risks of ownership ..." Interest income on bank fixed
> deposits booked at the bank's fixed rate; other interest on EIR method.

**Inventory valuation (policy 11, p.89):**
> "Raw materials, Work in Progress, Finished Goods and Packing Material are
> stated at lower of cost and net realizable value. For calculating inventories,
> the cost method for evaluation, it has been considered at FIFO Method. Cost
> comprises expenditure incurred in the normal course of business in bringing
> such inventories to its present location and condition and includes, where
> applicable, appropriate overheads based on normal level [of] activity."

**Depreciation method + useful lives (policy 2, p.88):**
> "The Company depreciates property, plant and equipment over their estimated
> useful lives using the Written down value method." ... "Depreciation on
> Property, Plant and Equipment is provided on the reducing balance method over
> the estimated useful life of the assets as prescribed under Schedule II to the
> Companies Act, 2013."
> Asset-wise useful-life table: the AR states "The management has estimated the
> useful life of the Tangible Assets as mentioned below:" but the numeric
> asset-by-asset table did NOT extract in the text file (image/table not OCR'd).
> Useful-life VALUES per asset class = NOT CAPTURED in text; method (WDV /
> reducing balance, Schedule II) is disclosed. [Confirm against PDF p.88 if the
> per-asset years matter.]

**Property, plant and equipment basis (policy 2, p.88):**
> "Property, plant and equipment are stated at cost, less accumulated
> depreciation and impairment, if any. Costs directly attributable to
> acquisition are capitalized until the property, plant and equipment are ready
> for use ... Other Indirect Expenses incurred relating to project ... prior to
> its intended use, are considered as pre-operative expenses and disclosed under
> Capital Work-in-Progress." Historical-cost basis (policy B, p.88).

**Remaining policies by heading (Note 1, p.88-89):**

9. Revenue Recognition and Other Income (above).
10. Income Taxes (current + deferred; balance-sheet method for deferred tax).
11. Inventories (above).
12. Foreign Currency Transactions (transaction-date rate; monetary items at
