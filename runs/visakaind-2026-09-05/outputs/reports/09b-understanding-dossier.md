## SECTION 3: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Visaka Industries makes and sells four products (B04). Cement roofing sheets, 53% of revenue, are
pressed from cement, fly ash and imported chrysotile fibre and sold to rural households through
4,974 dealers and stockists because they cost less than a steel roof and stay cooler underneath
(B04, AR FY26 p.106 dealer count via B01). Fibre cement boards and panels, sold as V-Next, are 30%
of revenue and replace plywood and gypsum in walls, ceilings and facades for builders and
architects, backed by GreenPro, TUV and ISI certification (B04). Synthetic yarn, 15% of revenue, is
spun on twin air-jet machines for named fabric mills, Donear, Siyaram Silks, Raymonds, Grasim
Bhiwani Textiles, D'Decor, BSL Suiting, Arvind Mills, Slok Industries, Bidhata (AR FY26 p.60). ATUM
Solar Roof is the fourth line, a roof panel that generates power, with no disclosed revenue since
its 2018 launch (B01/B03/B05/B07).

No single customer takes more than 10% of Visaka's revenue in either the standalone or the
consolidated segment note (AR FY26 p.202, Note 37); the yarn customers above are named as
"prominent downstream clients," not disclosed by transaction size (AR FY26 p.60). Roofing demand
runs on rural income and house-building; the B09 downstream candidates tie this directly to
PMAY-Gramin/PMAY-Urban physical progress, IMD monsoon bulletins, and Ministry of Agriculture MSP
data (B09). Boards demand is tied to organised construction and green-building specification, with
the peer BirlaNu putting boards-industry growth at 10-14% a year against 2-3% long-run roofing
growth (B06), and both PM Surya Ghar enrolment and the domestic steel-roofing price index named as
external series that would move the roofing-versus-substitute and solar-adjacent pictures (B09).

Demand should grow fastest in boards, because the segment is running at 86% of a 3,55,750-tonne
capacity with incremental volume flowing mostly to margin (B04 unit_economics), and the AR's own
disclosed non-asbestos revenue share rose from 43% to 47% of Building Products revenue FY25 to
FY26 (B03/B04 must_track_metrics), consistent with the boards/roofing mix continuing to shift.
Roofing itself has no comparable growth driver in the corpus; management's own risk table rates it
"High-moderate" on oversupply and input-cost exposure (B03 Phase 4A), and dealer count and
dealer-channel revenue share both declined FY26 (B01 data_notes).

Competitive advantage sits in boards, and nowhere else with confidence. Boards carry certification
and near-full-utilisation-driven partial pricing power (B04 moats_present, B07 A3 "process
innovation" scored Moderate). Roofing has forty years of distribution reach but no pricing power of
its own (B04 pricing_power at company level; B03 risk-grid finding above). Yarn has no moat
identified in either B04's or B07's scans. ATUM's patents in India, the US and South Africa are
claimed but unverifiable in this corpus (no registration number, B07) and carry no revenue after
eight years; the emerging-moat scan scored the whole company MODEST at 14.5 of roughly a 92-point
ceiling, with the talent-asymmetry and cannibalisation-barrier categories both scoring zero (B07).

## SECTION 5: PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY

1. Visaka makes four things: cement roofing sheets, fibre cement boards sold as V-Next, synthetic
   yarn, and ATUM solar roofing panels (B04).
2. Roofing is 53% of sales, boards are 30%, yarn is 15%. ATUM has never reported a rupee of
   revenue since it launched in 2018 (B04, B03).
3. The company is two established businesses (roofing, yarn) funding a growing third (boards) and
   an unproven fourth (ATUM) (B04 analyst_note).
4. Rural households buy roofing sheets through 4,974 dealers because they cost less than steel and
   stay cooler (B04, AR FY26 p.106).
5. Builders and architects buy boards to replace plywood and gypsum, drawn by GreenPro, TUV and
   ISI certification (B04).
6. Named fabric mills, Donear, Siyaram, Raymonds, Grasim Bhiwani, D'Decor and others, buy the
   yarn on repeat orders, not long contracts; no single customer anywhere in the company takes
   more than 10% of revenue (AR FY26 p.60/202).
7. Roofing demand tracks rural income and house-building; boards demand tracks organised
   construction and industry growth the peer BirlaNu puts at 10-14% a year, well above the 2-3%
   long-run roofing growth it separately cites (B06, B09).
8. Boards demand should grow because the plant runs at 86% of installed capacity and incremental
   output mostly turns into margin, not extra cost (B04).
9. Roofing has no comparable growth driver; its own dealer count and dealer-channel sales share
   both fell in FY26 with no explanation given (B01).
10. The moat sits in boards, where certification and near-full utilisation give partial pricing
    power. Roofing has forty years of reach but no pricing power. Yarn has no identified moat.
    ATUM's patents are claimed but unverified in this corpus and carry no revenue after eight
    years (B04, B07).
11. The mental model in one line: two mature, price-taking lines fund a genuine, partly-proven
    climb in boards, alongside an unresolved eight-year bet on ATUM (Section 2).
12. The fragility read: boards is a single point of failure for the whole climb, and ATUM is the
    one variable with no outside way to check it, the combination reads FRAGILE, not robust
    (Section 4c).
13. The corpus could not establish a boards-only profit margin (it is always blended with
    roofing), or any current ATUM revenue, patent registration number, or promoter share-pledge
    figure (Section 6).
14. The corpus also could not establish why the "Promoters - Indian" shareholding category jumped
    4.82 percentage points in one year while the two named individual promoters' own holdings did
    not move at all, or the current status of a November 2023 investigation at the company's own
    office (B01, B08).
15. The biggest open questions carried to Halt 1: does the boards segment ever get its own
    disclosed margin, does the Rs450cr capex plan turn into named committed capacity, does ATUM
