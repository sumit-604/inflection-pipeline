## WHAT IT SELLS

Syngene is a Biocon subsidiary (Biocon 52.41%, AR FY26 shareholding table) selling
contract research, development and manufacturing to global pharma and biotech. Two
reported service lines: Research Services (discovery chemistry and biology, dedicated
centres for BMS, Amgen, Baxter) and CDMO (small-molecule manufacturing at Mangalore,
biologics at Unit 3 Bengaluru and the Bayview site in Maryland, USA).

Mix: "two-third towards the CRO business, one-third towards the CDMO business" in FY26
(Q4FY26 call, 30-Apr-2026, p.8); Q4FY26 was 59/41; Q1FY27 was 78/22 (Q1FY27 call,
30-Jul-2026, p.5).

## WHERE THE MARGIN WENT

Three things took margin from 29% to 12% in five quarters:
1. Zoetis Librela offtake stopped. The 10-year biologics deal was signed Jul-2022, "up to
   $500 million" over its life (Syngene press release, 15-Jul-2022,
   https://cdn.syngeneintl.com/2022/07/15014333/Syngene-signs-10-year-biologics-manufacturing-agreement-with-leading-animal-health-company-Zoetis.pdf).
   The chairperson put the impact of the one client at "nearly $50 million" (Q1FY27 call p.9).
2. New-site costs before revenue. Unit 3 Bengaluru took "almost 18-odd months" to
   capitalise (Q4FY26 call p.8). Bayview was bought from Emergent for $36.5 million in
   Mar-2025 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1367644/000136764425000083/pressrelease-3192025bayvie.htm)
   and is still not capitalised.
3. Research-services attrition. Management says the book "drifted towards the larger
   share of commoditized research services" and lost clients on price (Q1FY27 call p.2, p.7).

## THE ONE ENGINE THE THESIS WOULD REST ON

CDMO refill. New management (Siddharth Mittal, MD & CEO from 1-Jul-2026, ex-Biocon) calls
CDMO "the primary growth engine" (Q1FY27 call p.3). The case: Bayview and Unit 3 biologics
capacity fills with non-Zoetis molecules, small-molecule commercial molecules ramp at
Mangalore, and research services move back to differentiated work. Target: "double-digit
sustainable growth ... starting next fiscal" (Q1FY27 call p.9). FY27 itself is guided down.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (the run's first verification priority)

1. **Guidance drift and the H2 arithmetic.** FY27 guidance moved from "broadly flat" (30-Apr-2026)
   to "single-digit decline" (30-Jul-2026) inside one quarter, with margins held at mid-20s after a
   12% Q1. Stage 5 builds the full guidance-versus-delivery table from the FY25 cut through FY27,
   and states the H2 revenue and margin the guidance implies. Stage 1 checks Q1FY27 against the
   filed results (inputs/results/2026-07-29_Q1FY27_unaudited_results.pdf).

2. **Zoetis exposure: peak share, residual, and any contract protection.** The deal was "up to $500
   million" over 10 years; the one-client impact was "nearly $50 million"; Zoetis holds "inventory
   for the next couple of years" yet Syngene has "certain delivery obligations" in H2FY27 (Q1FY27
   call p.10). Stages 2, 4 and 5 must find the peak revenue share, any minimum-offtake or
   take-or-pay term, any compensation booked, and any impairment test on the biologics assets.

3. **Capital not yet earning.** CWIP Rs 1,046 cr at Mar-2026 against net block Rs 3,000 cr; Bayview
   still uncapitalised; a news report says capitalisation was deferred to end-FY27 to keep
   depreciation off the current P&L (sahi.com, 31-Jul-2026, SECONDARY). Stages 2 and 3 split CWIP
   by project, test the capitalisation and borrowing-cost policy, and size the FY28 depreciation
   step-up. ROCE of 9.99% is the number this item moves.

4. **Research-services attrition versus the anchored contracts.** Research services were 78% of
   Q1FY27 sales, and management admits client losses on price. Against that sit the BMS extension to
   2035 and the Amgen and Baxter centres named by CRISIL. Stages 4, 5 and 6 separate dedicated-centre
   revenue from discovery churn (client count, top-10 share where disclosed) and check the Anthem and
   Sai Life discovery lines for a sector read.
