## NARRATIVE (plain English)

GNG is a fast-growing, asset-light device refurbisher whose earnings have compounded hard (revenue 40.6% CAGR, PAT 107.8%) but whose cash has not followed — operating cash flow has been negative every year since FY20, working-capital days have climbed from 136 to 178, and the whole growth story leans on one thing: a memory and component price supercycle that is lifting refurbished-device demand, ASPs and margins together. That same supercycle also underwrites a Rs 743 Cr inventory bet, so it is a single point of failure, not a diversified set of drivers.

The business is genuinely decent — operational ROCE near 28%, a MASSIVE addressable market, institutional money entering, a CARE upgrade — but it is not a transition-alpha buy at Rs 634. At roughly 55 times trailing earnings, exiting three years out at a 20 times forward multiple (the operator's own, generous, growth-duration-based number) still produces a lower price than today in the base case. The de-rating swamps the earnings growth. The Hurdle Ratio stops the trade even on bull-case earnings, and the probability-weighted return is negative 5.6%.

So this is an AVOID on valuation and a DEEP WATCH on the business. The number to wait for is Rs 261-293, and even there two conditions must both hold before buying: the price is inside the zone, and no thesis-broken trigger has fired on the way down. The cash question is the swing factor — the first proper post-IPO statutory cash-flow statement will either confirm the drag is growth-induced and temporary or structural and disqualifying. Until that resolves and the price is right, do nothing but watch.
