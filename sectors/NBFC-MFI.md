## STANDING SECTOR MODEL (compounding view; revise as evidence accrues)
- Cycle position: FY26 was a stress trough (Q1 FY26 the worst quarter, sector
  credit costs ~4.5%). FY27 is early recovery: collection efficiency back near
  99.9% X-bucket, sector PAR falling. Much of the FY27 YoY profit growth across
  MFIs is a low-base recovery, not fresh strength; always separate base effect
  from underlying.
- Unit economics: earn the spread between lending yield (~21-23%) and cost of
  funds (~8%); NIM ~13-15%. Through-cycle ROA of 2.5-3.5% is healthy; ~4.7% is
  a peak, not a run-rate. High leverage is normal (D/E 3-4x); CRAR ~25%+ is
  comfortable.
- Seasonality: Q1 (Apr-Jun) is a seasonally SOFT disbursement quarter; Q4 is
  the strongest. A QoQ disbursement dip in Q1 is normal, not weakness on its
  own. Q4 return ratios flatter (lowest credit cost, lumpy assignment income).
- Structural risks / what breaks companies here:
  - Over-indebtedness is THE risk. Blow-ups are preceded by AUM growing via
    larger tickets on a near-static borrower base rather than new borrowers.
    Watch active-client growth vs AUM growth, new-client run-rate, average
    ticket, and active-loans/exposure per borrower — these lead GNPA.
  - Asset-quality optics: GNPA (quoted on-book) can be "cleaned" via write-offs
    and ARC sales. The honest read requires the ECL / stage-movement walk
    (opening, additions, gross write-offs, ARC/DA-of-NPA, recoveries, closing)
    and the Stage-2 book, not the headline number.
  - Direct Assignment / securitisation: MFIs sell loan pools to banks for
    gain-on-sale and to move ~20-25% of the book off-balance-sheet. Inflates
    reported ROA in lumpy quarters; headline GNPA understates true exposure.
    Always ask for assignment VOLUME, not just DA income.
  - Geographic concentration: state-level events and loan-waiver politics are a
    recurring shock; concentration in UP/Bihar/Assam/West Bengal is common.
  - Foreign borrowing brings INR-USD hedge risk that can swing reported profit
    (large FX finance-cost / fair-value lines with no hedge note = watch item).
  - "Management overlay" buffer provisions are often excluded from headline
    ROA/ROE (non-GAAP), flattering returns; a growing overlay is a fresh charge
    that depresses reported PAT while the headline ratio adds it back.
- Regulation: RBI microfinance guardrails (caps on number of lenders per
  borrower and on indebtedness) govern how fast the sector can grow;
  priority-sector status is what lets banks fund MFIs cheaply via DA.
- Competitive frame: peers include CreditAccess Grameen, Spandana, Muthoot
  Microfin, Fusion, Satin. On their own decks MFIs rank themselves favourably;
  treat peer comparisons as self-selected until cross-checked against peer
  filings.
- Reading tricks: separate base effect from underlying on any big YoY; demand
  the ECL walk and assignment volume; check borrower count vs AUM; read return
  ratios on the reported (overlay-inclusive, GAAP) basis, not the adjusted one.

  but that is largely a Q1 FY26 trough base; PAT fell 24% QoQ and return ratios

  borrower base = over-indebtedness early warning ahead of a clean 2.18% GNPA.
