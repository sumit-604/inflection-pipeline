## Where the market stands

The sheet counts about 2,230 stocks. Each has three trend lines: 20 days, 50 days and 200 days. A stock above a line is in an uptrend on that clock. On Monday 712 stocks stood above the short line, a little under one in three. 528 stood above the middle line, about one in four. 678 stood above the long line, about three in ten. The other seven in ten are still in long downtrends.

The year explains why three in ten is low. The spring low came on 23 March, when only 288 stocks held the long line. On 1 April the market had its great thrust day, when 1,114 stocks rose four and a half percent or more. The climb from there ran four months. The long count peaked at 1,357 on 7 August. That was about six stocks in ten. Since then the autumn has walked down a staircase. In late September the long count sat near 1,000. It slipped to about 840 in early October. A mid October repair lifted it back to 938 on 16 October. Then the floor broke hard, and on 23 October only 579 stocks held the long line. Monday's 678 is about 100 stocks above that low. It is about halfway back to the early October floor.

## What would change this read

The read is a wide, light bounce on top of a falling staircase. It passed one hard test on Friday, but buying still runs on light money and selling on heavy money. It turns worse if a heavy selling day repeats and the next session fails to answer it. A day of 300 or more hard fallers would be the first sign. A long count back under about 580, the 23 October low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows back above about 300 would say the same. A short count back under about 450 on rising turnover would repeat the way the October repair broke. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has returned.

The cautious read fails if buying turns heavy and keeps going. The first test is a wide up day on turnover above about 610 crore, the level of both recent selling days. Monday reached about 494. The second test is the short count above about 800, where the October repair stalled. Monday reached 712. The third test is the long count above about 840, the early October floor. Monday reached 678. The fourth test is new yearly highs past about 50 while new lows stay under 100. Monday had 39 highs and 91 lows. A VIX close under 15 would support the turn. Two or more of these passing in the same week would change the read from bounce to repair.
