Sugar core loses money, investments carry group

K.C.P. Sugar and Industries has run for roughly eight decades as an integrated sugar, distillery, cogeneration and chemicals producer in the Krishna belt of Andhra Pradesh, with an engineering unit at Trichy and a wholly owned engineering subsidiary, The Eimco-K.C.P. Limited. FY25 standalone revenue was Rs 227 Cr. Sugar is about 57% of the parent's sales, black gram (urad dal) processing about 20%, and the rest is molasses, alcohol, bio-fertiliser, calcium lactate, cogen power and standalone engineering. The Velagapudi and Sethi family runs it, with Vinod Sethi, a former Morgan Stanley India CIO, as Executive Chairman and Irmgard Velagapudi as Managing Director.

The company is really two businesses stacked in one listing. The standalone sugar core is loss making, and the group stays in the black only because the Eimco engineering subsidiary earned Rs 16.6 Cr PAT in FY25 with its order book up 30% year over year, and because a large financial portfolio sits on the balance sheet. Quoted equity plus mutual funds are about 46% of total assets. Consolidated PAT was positive both years, Rs 14.4 Cr in FY25 and Rs 11.1 Cr in FY26, while the standalone entity swung to loss.

What is changing is thin and unconverted. Urad dal scaled roughly fourfold, from 3.9% to 20.2% of parent revenue, but turned loss making as it grew. The national E20 ethanol mandate is a genuine sector tailwind, yet the company's own alcohol production fell 82% year over year in FY25, so it is not capturing what it calls its structural opportunity. A Rs 257 Cr order for Eimco from Hyundai Engineering, dated 20 July 2026 and around 3.3 times the engineering segment's FY26 revenue, is operator-relayed and press-reported but absent from every exchange filing and text cache searched, so it counts as a pending catalyst, not evidence.

The screen result is AVOID. Gate 0 scored 27 out of 160 with zero confirmed moats, median 10-year ROCE of 6.93%, and revenue lower year over year in 6 of the last 9 years. The forward-looking Emerging Moat scan also came back NONE, with 17 of 20 categories showing no evidence and the single active category being the industry-wide ethanol policy that the company is not yet capturing. The core sugar segment result has moved the wrong way three years running, from positive Rs 6.5 Cr in FY24 to negative Rs 8.2 Cr in FY25 to negative Rs 17.3 Cr in FY26.

Cash turned this year for the first time. Consolidated operating cash flow swung from positive Rs 47.8 Cr in FY25 to negative Rs 30.9 Cr in FY26, and the standalone entity booked its first actual cash loss, Rs 11.4 Cr, confirmed under CARO. Debt service coverage fell to 0.25 times from 1.52 times, receivable collection velocity slowed even as the absolute book shrank, and capex fell 36% to below depreciation, so the cash drain is not being caused by growth spending. The counterweight is real. The company is net debt negative, CARE reaffirmed CARE A minus with a Stable outlook in October 2025, and the rating notes free cash and investments of Rs 204 Cr, which cushions the operating shortfall for now.

Management credibility grades C, held down by no-concall mode and by outlook claims the same year's numbers contradict. The annual report framed ethanol as the opportunity while alcohol output collapsed, and said sugar recovery should stay firm while the sugar segment loss widened 71% into FY26. Promoter conduct reads better than the operations. Pledge is 0%, the promoter side was a net buyer through the loss year, stake rose from about 40.6% to 42%, and three independent directors joined in April 2024. Against that sit two caution items: related-party fixed deposits have grown to 26.9% of the public deposit book, and both family executives drew full Schedule V minimum remuneration for a fifth straight year of inadequate profits, landing in the first loss year.

Peer triangulation is not available this run. No peer concall transcripts were supplied, so the peer verification stage was skipped, and the open sector question stays open: whether the 39% collapse in cane crushed is a Krishna-belt sector problem or specific to this company's execution remains unverified against peers such as Bannari Amman, Ponni or Rajshree.

The market behind the business is large on paper but slow in practice. The blended addressable market runs to roughly Rs 1.8 lakh Cr across sugar, ethanol, urad dal and process equipment, but that headroom is a commodity-market artifact, so the runway is classed MODERATE at about 3.7% underlying growth. The three to five year serviceable opportunity is around Rs 343 Cr to Rs 373 Cr, and nearly all of the modelled growth comes from one capex-free lever, urad dal running up its already installed 22,000 MTPA capacity. Management put no quantified market-size claim on record.
