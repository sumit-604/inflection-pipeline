Jyoti Structures Limited Wins GETCO Order for 765 kV Transmission Line Project in 
Gujarat  
Mumbai, 23 October 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL), a listed global Engineering, Procurement 
and Construction (EPC) company specializing in power transmission, has received a Letter of Award 
from Gujarat Energy Transmission Corporation Limited (GETCO). The order, valued at ₹689.93 crore, 
excluding GST, is for the 765 kV DC Pachchham–Saykha Transmission Line project in Gujarat. 
The scope includes the design, testing, supply, erection, and commissioning of the transmission line 
on a total turnkey basis. The project is scheduled for completion within 42 months from the date of 
the Letter of Award. 
The order adds to JSL’s portfolio of transmission projects in Gujarat, where the company is executing 
multiple projects for private developers and utilities. The state continues to see investment in 
transmission infrastructure alongside renewable energy development, creating demand for high-
voltage transmission lines and power evacuation networks. 
To support its expanding portfolio in the state, JSL recently inaugurated a dedicated project office in 
Ahmedabad. The facility houses project management teams and is designed to strengthen 
coordination between engineering, site execution, and customer teams. With closer oversight of 
project sites and improved access to stakeholders across Gujarat, the office supports the 
management of ongoing projects and the company’s engagement with future transmission 
opportunities. 
Commenting on the order, Amit Dutta, COO, Jyoti Structures Limited, said, “Gujarat is an important 
market for us, with several transmission projects already under execution. Orders of this nature 
reflect the continued demand for specialised EPC capabilities in the power transmission sector. Our 
experience across transmission projects enable us to manage the engineering and execution 
requirements involved in delivering such infrastructure. We remain focused on building our project 
portfolio through the consistent delivery of projects to the required standards of quality and safety.” 
The GETCO order follows JSL’s recent contract in Sri Lanka, where the company partnered with 
Hayleys Fentons Limited to develop a 220 kV double-circuit transmission line between Mannar and 
Mullikulam. The project will support the evacuation of wind power to Sri Lanka’s national grid.

With over five decades of experience in power transmission EPC, JSL has constructed more than 
37,600 circuit kilometers of transmission lines and delivered over 1,800 high-voltage substation bays. 
Its integrated capabilities span design and engineering, manufacturing, prototype tower testing, 
construction and project management. JSL also undertakes turnkey projects across substations, 
underground cabling and optical ground wire (OPGW) installations in India and international markets. 
About Jyoti Structures Limited 
Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL) is a listed engineering, procurement and construction company with 
over five decades of experience in the power transmission sector. Incorporated in 1974, the company 
provides turnkey solutions across design and engineering, consultancy, prototype tower testing, 
manufacturing, construction and project management. Its capabilities include high-voltage 
transmission lines, substations, underground cabling and optical ground wire (OPGW) projects in 
India and international markets.
