## Final rulings, after review

1. Forward window. 6 months primary, 12 months for the return on capital transition. The company reports roughly half yearly and broke its quarterly disclosure promise, so no reliable 3 month print exists.
2. Business type. Standard operating business, asset heavy precision components manufacturer. Four standard transitions, not the lender set.
3. Workup intent. First workup. Role 1 derived fields N/A because FTTCP precedes valuation.
4. Sector cap row. CORRECTED to Manufacturing 25x. The manifest's "EPC / Civil construction" 20x is wrong; OBSCP is a precision components manufacturer, not a civil contractor. Phase 3 stage 11 uses 25x.
5. Cash conversion nature. GROWTH-INDUCED (changed from INDETERMINATE on review, see Override 1). Confidence fairly sure. Falsifier: the over 12 month receivables bucket and the ECL charge in FY25, FY26 and FY27.
6. Cash conversion forward verdict. STAGNANT (changed from DECLINING, see Override 1). Catalyst strength Weak, not None. The Kernex cap lifts.
7. Return on capital, backward. TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED. The Oct 2024 listing raised about Rs 57 Cr and bloated the capital base faster than profit, dropping return on capital from 33.4% FY24 to 19.0% FY25.
8. Return on capital, forward. RECOVERING at about 45% probability. Genuinely uncertain; the operator may flip it to DECLINING, which would drop the composite to +2. Falsifier: FY26 return on capital, once anchored, prints below 18% with FY27 showing no lift.
9. Margin forward. STAGNANT. Management cut the medium term expansion ambition to about 1 point for FY27.
10. Revenue forward. FIRING. 54% growth FY26, order book above Rs 1,200 Cr, capacity commissioning.
11. Composite and position. +3 out of 8, DEEP WATCH (up from +2 DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID after the cash ruling). Kernex cap NOT engaged.
12. Disposition. FLAG-CASH now GROWTH-INDUCED, so the INDETERMINATE cap lifts and the gate disposition moves to PROCEED WITH FLAGS carrying the falsifier. On price the valuation lands at WATCHLIST deep.

## Operator overrides

### Override 1 — cash conversion is GROWTH-INDUCED, not INDETERMINATE

- Draft determination: INDETERMINATE, which capped the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS, because the receivables ageing schedule that proves collection quality sits in the truncated annual report pages.
- Evidence the operator brought (screener.ai pull plus the CRISIL report dated 2 July 2026): the over 6 month receivables bucket is small and stable through FY24 at 2.0%, 0.6% and 2.7% of receivables with negligible doubtful debts; those receivables totals reconcile exactly to this run's anchored figures (Rs 15.58 Cr, Rs 19.97 Cr, Rs 21.53 Cr); CRISIL, with full account access covering FY26, frames the elevated gross current assets of 223 days as "working capital intensive operations" and "long credit periods to export customers," with no mention of rising bad debts; related party receivables are modest and the large Omega Bright Steel balance is a loan to OBSC, funding, not receivables parked with a promoter entity.
- Operator's stated position, in the operator's framing: GROWTH-INDUCED working capital stress, not yet a structural collection or asset quality problem.
- Ruling applied: GROWTH-INDUCED. Cash multiplier moves to 0.80 base plus 0.20 growth offset equals 1.00x. Cash forward verdict moves to STAGNANT, catalyst Weak, Kernex cap lifts, composite moves to +3 DEEP WATCH.
- Residual gap and falsifier: the explicit FY25 and FY26 over 12 month ageing buckets and ECL were not visible to screener.ai either. If that bucket blows past roughly 15% of receivables or provisions jump, it reverts to STRUCTURAL and the cap returns.

### Override 2 — the order book is genuine, credit the duration premium

- Draft determination: Pillar 3c duration premium = +0x, held because the order book was concall stated, not filed LoAs with stated values, and 3c is documented tier only.
- Operator ruling, verbatim: "the stated order book is genuine only, so we need to give them duration premium as well."
- Ruling applied: 3c = +2x. Order book Rs 1,200 Cr is 5.5x revenue, past the 4 year tenor line. Pillar 3 becomes 3a +3 plus 3b +0 plus 3c +2 equals +5x, inside the +6x cap.
- Effect: raw destination PE moves from about 19.5 to 20.5x up to about 21.5 to 22.5x; destination PE range about 20x to 24x, midpoint 22x.
- Note recorded for the devil's advocate: the order book now earns 3a and 3c and underpins revenue and the return on capital recovery. SHARED CATALYST is hardened; Role 3 must stress test the order book firmness and the Rs 100 to 200 Cr a year conversion pace.

## Valuation conclusion (Phase 3 preview, computed at operator request)

- Four pillar destination PE 20x to 24x, midpoint 22x. Pillar 1 base 16.5 to 17.5x pending the FY26 ROCE anchor, Pillar 2 cash 1.00x, Pillar 3 +5x, Pillar 4 +0x, UA not applied, Manufacturing cap 25x not binding.
- Current trailing PE about 63.7x (market cap Rs 1,720 Cr on FY26 PAT Rs 27.01 Cr).
- Hurdle Ratio, Tier A 25%: base 0.85, bull 1.03, both far below the 1.953 pass line. Hurdle verdict STOP. 25% CAGR is infeasible from this price even on bull earnings, because the de-rating from about 64x to about 22x swamps the earnings growth.
- Three year targets: bear about Rs 409, base about Rs 575, bull about Rs 688. Even the bull target roughly equals today's price.
- Entry zone about Rs 290 to Rs 350, MoS about Rs 235 to Rs 280, both roughly half of CMP. MARKET-UNLIKELY ZONE; price history was not in the run inputs.
- The conclusion is robust to the open FY26 ROCE anchor and to the RRM versus additive track choice: at the high end of the ROCE band the destination only reaches about 24x, and the conservative RRM track is lower still, so the hurdle STOPs either way.

## Final FTTCP verdict

FTTCP composite +3 out of 8, DEEP WATCH, Kernex cap lifted. The business is genuinely growing and the order book is real, so this is not an AVOID on the company. On price it is WATCHLIST deep: not a buy at Rs 666, fair entry near Rs 290 to Rs 350, roughly a halving from here. The whole call turns on two prints, the FY27 operating cash flow and the over 12 month receivables bucket; a positive cash turn and a clean ageing confirm the growth induced read, a second negative cash year or a rising overdue bucket flip it back toward STRUCTURAL and AVOID.
