## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring)

1. TruAlt claim: national ethanol demand and supply are roughly balanced at current blending, with no material new capacity. Contradicted by TRIVENI ("the massive overcapacity that exists in the nation", TRIVENI Feb-2026 call p.8, corrected from p.16 by Verifier D round 1; national capacity against offtake, TRIVENI Aug-2026 call p.10-11) and BALRAMCHIN ("At the industry level, definitely there is an overcapacity", Pramod Patwari, BALRAMCHIN Q4 FY26 call, file Jun_2026, p.11 per Verifiers D and B round 2; the stage cites p.10). TruAlt itself called the same absolute figures "already an oversupply" in Nov-2025 (Concall Nov-2025 p.22) and "almost at par" in Aug-2026 (Concall Aug-2026 p.24).
2. Peer raised risk TruAlt never names, logged by Verifier B as missed: restriction on B-heavy and cane juice diversion to ethanol in ESY 2026-27 (BALRAMCHIN Aug-2026 call p.4, Vivek Saraogi: "it is reasonable to assume that there will be no diversion allowed towards B and juice"; TRIVENI Aug-2026 call p.5-7). TruAlt guides 44 crore litres for FY27 and plans to run all five plants on syrup for 100 days (Concall Aug-2026 p.8; May-2026 p.16).
3. Peer raised risk TruAlt never applies to itself: solvency stress for standalone distilleries as PSU bank interest moratoriums expire (TRIVENI Aug-2026 call p.10-11, Tarun Sawhney).

Partly verified, not contradicted: the FY26 allocation shortfall (all three peers), the grain margin squeeze as a point in time sensitivity (GULPOLY only), and scheme dependency as a sector feature (all three peers). Unverifiable from peer calls: private OMC reneging and pricing, and the related party sourcing reconciliation.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. **Half year cash print.** Check consolidated inventory and H1 CFO in the Q2 FY27 results (quarter to 30-Sep-2026, due on BSE by mid-Nov-2026). Inventory below ₹528 Cr with positive H1 CFO supports the timing reading. Inventory at or above ₹528 Cr with negative CFO supports the structural reading. This tests the cash flag and the year round thesis.
2. **Quarterly ethanol volume.** Track sales volume in crore litres each quarter against the 44 crore litre FY27 base, about 11 crore litres a quarter, and utilisation against 60.57% (Concall Aug-2026 p.8; Investor Presentation Q1 FY27 p.6). Source: quarterly results and the investor presentation. This tests the proof gate. Management missed 8 of 11 tracked promises, so read any guided figure as the top of a range.
3. **ESY 2026-27 sugary feedstock policy.** Watch for a Food Ministry or MoPNG order on B-heavy molasses and cane juice diversion before the ethanol year starts in November. About 53% of Q1 FY27 volume was sugar based and two of five units run only on sugar (Concall Aug-2026 p.8). This tests the volume base more directly than any company guidance.
4. **Maize price.** Track maize in ₹ a kg on Agmarknet each month. Margin was ₹6 to 7 a litre at ₹25.50 (Concall Aug-2026 p.8-9). Recovery below ₹20 to 22 restores the grain leg. This tests whether dual feed is a hedge or a cost.
5. **The 15 crore litre allocation.** Follow the Supreme Court BPCL matter and any Reg 30 filing on supply against the Karnataka High Court order of 04-Feb-2026 (about ₹1,062 to 1,065 Cr of value, Concall Feb-2026). It has been open for three quarters with no timeline. Actual invoicing against the order is the only confirm signal.
6. **Scheme cash.** Check whether the PLI and interest subvention receivable (₹129.5 Cr at March 2026, FY26 AR Notes 15/16; CFO cites ₹107 Cr from NABARD, Concall Nov-2025 p.9) turns into cash, in the receivables and other income lines of the Q2 FY27 results. 73.2% of standalone PBT depends on these schemes, and one peer books them only on receipt.
7. **Promoter and disclosure layer.** Read the Sep-2026 shareholding pattern for the 36.85% pledge, the next RPT disclosure for the related party purchase share and a corrected guarantee note, and exchange filings for the woman independent director appointment. Claude web should confirm the current status of the Factories Act prosecution (last data point: trial court appearance 06-Oct-2025, prospectus p.567-568). This tests whether the CONCERN verdict can move.
8. **First biogas plant COD.** Watch Reg 30 board outcomes for commercial operation of the three TGPL plants, guided for Q3 FY27 after a slip from June 2026 (Concall Aug-2026 p.3-4). A COD with a clear statement of how JV revenue is consolidated confirms. Another quarter of "near commissioning" kills the near term biogas leg.
