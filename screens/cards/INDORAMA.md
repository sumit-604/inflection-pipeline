## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Indo Rama makes polyester. One
plant at Butibori near Nagpur runs 672,000 tonnes a year of capacity. The
product list is polyester staple fibre 231,000 TPA, polyester filament yarn
189,000 TPA, draw texturised yarn 175,000 TPA and polyester chips 252,000 TPA
(Annual Report FY2024, page 6). The company buys PTA and MEG, converts them to
fibre and yarn, and sells to spinners and weavers in India and 34 other
countries. It reports one segment, "manufacturing and trading of polyester
goods" (Annual Report FY2024, page 199). The money is the spread between the
polyester price and the PTA-MEG price, multiplied by tonnes.

**What is changing?** The loss stopped. FY2024 delivered a loss before tax of
Rs 141.66 crore. FY2025 delivered a profit of Rs 20.08 crore. FY2026 delivered
Rs 110.03 crore (Annual Report 2024-25, page 74; Board Meeting outcome
29-Jul-2026, page 4). Production of filament yarn rose 25% in FY2025 and chips
rose 32%. India Ratings moved the outlook on the bank facilities from negative
to stable in January 2026.

**Why now?** Two things moved together. The polyester spread recovered, and the
company stopped burning cash. Interest coverage went from negative 0.50 times
in FY2024 to 1.47 times in FY2025. Debt to equity fell from 2.31 to 1.90
(Annual Report 2024-25, page 74).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The spread must hold. Indo Rama
is a price taker with one plant and no captive raw material. It discontinued
its captive power in June 2020 and now buys from the state DISCOM (Annual
Report 2024-25, page 75). Nothing in the corpus gives it pricing power.

**What breaks it?** Cheap Chinese imports. The company's own MD&A says India
saw a 50% rise in man-made fibre textile imports from China over three years,
and calls for protective trade measures (Annual Report 2024-25, page 73). A
spread squeeze puts a business with 1.47 times interest cover back into loss
quickly.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One product family, one plant, one segment. Revenue is tonnes multiplied by
spread. The company names its parentage as an advantage: it is a subsidiary of
Indorama Ventures Public Company Limited, which produces PTA and MEG (Annual
Report 2024-25, page 74). Raw material still comes from local suppliers where
possible, to cut price volatility and transit time.

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds any
later valuation of this name. Spread, utilisation, cycle position and
cost-curve rank are the variables, not ROCE or rupee working capital.

## 3. Competitive advantages

None found. The MD&A names long client relationships and parentage. Neither is
a moat. The company competes against imports priced Rs 4 to Rs 5 a kilogram
below its own, and it says so. Pricing policy is "synchronised with public raw
material price indices" (Annual Report 2024-25, page 74). That is the sentence
of a price taker. Moat: absent.

## 4. The promoters

Om Prakash Lohia is Chairman and Managing Director, appointed 1986. Vishal
Lohia is an executive promoter director, appointed 2002. They are the only two
promoter directors on an eight-member board, which is half independent (Annual
Report 2024-25, page 77). The related-party list names Urmila Lohia, Aloke
Lohia, Ritika Kumar and Rimple Lohia as relatives of the two (Annual Report
2024-25, page 207). Aloke Lohia founded Indorama Ventures. The family therefore
sits on both sides of the parent relationship. Promoter holding percentage and
pledge: NOT FOUND in this corpus. Looked for in the corporate governance
section and in SAST disclosures.

**Classification: margin-led, and inside a commodity cycle.** Q1 FY27 revenue
fell 31% against Q1 FY26. Operating profit still rose. Operating margin went
from 6.0% to 8.8%. Cost of materials fell from 74.2% of revenue to 71.9%. Other
expenses fell 37%, from Rs 136.41 crore to Rs 85.37 crore, with no explanation
in the filing.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 commodity price-taker. TO R1 commodity price-taker, now profitable.
That is a cycle position change, not a rung climb. Nothing in the corpus shows
new pricing power, new switching cost or a new cost-curve rank. Management
quote on a transition: none exists. The company files no presentation and holds
no earnings call.

## 8. Proof check

The last two quarters move the way a spread recovery moves, not the way a
transition moves. Q4 FY26 profit before tax was Rs 43.77 crore on revenue of
Rs 1,096.92 crore. Q1 FY27 was Rs 33.75 crore on revenue of Rs 817.91 crore.
Profit held while revenue fell 25% sequentially. The proof point for a spread
story is present. The proof point for a quality climb is absent, because no
climb is claimed.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

India Ratings and Research affirmed IND A-/Stable and IND A2+ on Rs 1,620
crore of bank loan facilities on 27 January 2026, and revised the outlook to
stable from negative (Credit Rating intimation, page 1). The agency holds
management's numbers and moved the outlook up. That corroborates the cash
recovery. The rationale itself was not reachable, so the agency's reasoning on
sustainability and on the transition is NOT FOUND.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** No transition is claimed, so no gate exists to
  fire. The profit recovery is a cycle observation.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL-FEATURE.** Leverage near 1.9 times, interest cover
  near 1.5 times and a current ratio below 1.0 are the normal shape of a
  single-plant polyester converter. They are not the debris of a climb.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a corpus document: Bull AI
  company record, market capitalisation Rs 2,378 crore. Paid-up capital of
  Rs 261.11 crore at Rs 10 face value gives 26.11 crore shares (Board Meeting
  outcome 29-Jul-2026, page 4). FY2026 earnings per share were Rs 4.21, and
  those earnings carried no tax. The market already pays a full multiple for
  untaxed cycle earnings from a price taker.

**Posture: AVOID.** Gap closed combined with structural ugliness is the
dominating overlay in the matrix.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.** Do not spend a `/step1` on this name now.

The reasoning is short. There is no transition to test. The business climbed no
rung. It moved from loss to profit inside one commodity cycle, on a spread it
does not control, with no captive raw material, no pricing power and no captive
power. The market already pays roughly a full multiple for earnings that carry
no tax. A shallow read cannot find the claim that a deep read would verify,
because management publishes neither a presentation nor a transcript.

**Load-bearing facts a later run must verify first**
1. Whether the carried-forward tax losses are near exhaustion. Full tax would
   cut reported earnings by roughly a quarter.
2. The reason other expenses fell 37% year on year in Q1 FY27.
3. Promoter holding, pledge, and the terms of every related-party flow to
   Indorama Ventures entities.
4. The India Ratings rationale of January 2026, for the agency's own view on
   whether the FY2026 spread holds.

**What would change the view**
- A company capex or product-mix commitment with a date and a rupee number,
  stated on the record by management.
- Evidence that the parent is moving PTA or MEG supply on captive terms, which
  would be a real cost-curve move.
- A price that reopens the gap against fully-taxed earnings.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the
name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
