Management on the call: Mr. Kamlesh Shah, Managing Director; Mr. Rajesh Gupta, Director;
Mr. Sachin Gupta, Chief Executive Officer and Whole-Time Director. IR: Valorem Advisors.
Kamlesh Shah, opening: "The 1st Quarter was characterized by a rapidly evolving regulatory
landscape, heightened geopolitical uncertainties and continued volatility in the global financial
market. Domestically, while India's macroeconomic fundamentals remain strong, the capital market
industry witnessed significant changes, including regulatory measures introduced by SEBI in
derivative segment and tighter funding norms prescribed by Reserve Bank of India for top-desk
[prop-desk] trading."
