# CNL Step 1 business brief (2026-11-11)

Company: Creative Newtech Ltd (formerly Creative Peripherals & Distribution). NSE: CNL (was CREATIVE). BSE: 544631.
Screener: https://www.screener.in/company/CNL/consolidated/ (check_url OK 200). CMP Rs 1,472, mcap Rs 2,210 Cr, P/E 27.4 on 11-Nov-2026 (screener).

## Business read

1. Two engines. "Market Entry Specialist" (MES) is pass-through distribution of third-party IT, imaging and consumer-tech brands, 31+ branches, 10,000+ channel partners. "Brand Business" is owned and licensed brands: the Honeywell licence (accessories, structured cabling, fibre), WOZOYO (own brand, India and US), CyberPowerPC. Q2 FY27 investor presentation (IP), slides 3, 21. https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=1bfd4ff2-6210-415d-890f-c35ebff19f25.pdf
2. Scale. FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 2,717.5 Cr (+51% YoY), EBITDA Rs 103.95 Cr, PAT Rs 70.29 Cr. H1 FY27 total income Rs 1,231.65 Cr (+16.6%), EBITDA Rs 65.01 Cr (5.28% margin vs 3.95%), PAT Rs 39.47 Cr (+35.5%). IP slides 19-20, 31.
3. Mix. Brand Business was 19.03% of Q2 FY27 revenue (Rs 143.12 Cr) vs 12.83% in Q2 FY26; 17.37% of H1 FY27. IP slides 21-22.
4. Margin source. Management states brand gross margin is 40-42%, converting to about 10-11% PAT. The MES engine runs near a 2-3% operating margin. Q2 FY27 consolidated gross margin rose to 11.34% from 7.90%. Q2 FY27 transcript p.10, p.17-18; IP slide 8. https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=ab45d6d5-8e65-49fd-9dd0-f748fb74250e.pdf
5. Where the profit sits. Q2 FY27 PAT: standalone Rs 12.65 Cr, consolidated Rs 25.94 Cr. Tax is Rs 4.19 Cr in BOTH. So about Rs 13.3 Cr of quarterly PAT comes from subsidiaries at nil tax. The brand engine sits in Hong Kong: Secure Connection Ltd (HK, majority owned, Honeywell licence) and Creative Peripherals and Distributions Ltd (HK). IP slide 32; CRISIL rationale 06-Aug-2026. https://www.crisil.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/CreativeNewtechLimited_August%2006_%202026_RR_400704.html
6. Cash. FY26 CFO was -Rs 271.9 Cr. Debtor days rose to 76 from 49. Gearing rose to 0.84x from 0.23x. H1 FY27 CFO was +Rs 351.24 Cr. Net working capital days fell to 45 from 75. Management guides 52-55 days long run. IP slide 9; CRISIL; screener cash flow table.
7. Concentration. CRISIL says the top customer fell to about 25% of revenue, against a historical 50-60%. CRISIL 06-Aug-2026.
8. Rating. CRISIL BBB+/Stable and A2, bank facilities Rs 209 Cr, utilisation about 73%.
9. Guidance. FY27 revenue growth about 20%. FY29 revenue about Rs 6,000 Cr (30% CAGR from FY26), with 40% (Rs 2,400 Cr) from brands at 10-11% PAT margin (management also says 8-10%). IP slide 10; transcript p.10, p.18-19.
10. Government project. Advance work order from BSNL for the BharatNet Middle Mile Network, Odisha circle, about Rs 3,194.83 Cr total (capex about Rs 1,700 Cr over about 3.5 years, opex about Rs 1,200 Cr). Management expects Rs 100-200 Cr revenue in FY27 and Rs 500-600 Cr in FY28, at 6-7% net margin. Transcript p.3, p.13-14.
11. Acquisitions (surveillance push). Infinova (India) Pvt Ltd, budget up to USD 4 Mn, for an STQC-certified Make in India camera platform (IP slide 11). Zicom SaaS Pvt Ltd, 100% for Rs 50.50 Cr EV: Rs 25.00 Cr cash plus about Rs 25.50 Cr by share swap; EGM on 28-Nov-2026 for the preferential issue. BSE filing 04-Nov-2026. https://www.bseindia.com/stockinfo/AnnPdfOpen.aspx?Pname=5d162444-bd9b-4f14-a4c2-9f57aef09e03.pdf
12. Ownership. Promoters 56.62%, flat since Mar-2025. FII 0.48% plus DII 2.23% = 2.71% (Sep-2026), under the 3% UA qualifier. Screener shareholding.

THE ONE ENGINE: mix shift from 2-3% margin pass-through distribution to owned and licensed brands at 40%+ gross margin (19% of revenue now, 40% claimed by FY29). The BSNL BharatNet order and surveillance acquisitions are a second, project-type engine that adds working-capital and execution risk.

## Peers (auto-selected, no operator pause)

- Aditya Infotech (CPPLUS), https://www.screener.in/company/CPPLUS/consolidated/ (OK 200). Why it fits: owned surveillance brand (CP Plus) built on a distribution base. It is the destination CNL claims for its brand and surveillance engine, with STQC and Make in India in common.
- Redington (REDINGTON), https://www.screener.in/company/REDINGTON/consolidated/ (OK 200). Why it fits: scale benchmark for IT value-added distribution. It sets the margin, working-capital and ROCE norms the MES engine trades against.
- No listed pure play licences a global brand into Indian accessories. The brand engine therefore uses CPPLUS as the closest structural comp.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)

1. Subsidiary profit and tax. Verify from the FY26 AR consolidated accounts and the Q2 FY27 results: standalone vs consolidated PAT, the Hong Kong subsidiaries' revenue, PAT and tax, the minority share in Secure Connection Ltd, and related-party flows between CNL and the HK entities. Q2 FY27 shows identical tax (Rs 4.19 Cr) on standalone PAT Rs 12.65 Cr and consolidated PAT Rs 25.94 Cr.
2. Cash conversion. Reconcile FY26 CFO of -Rs 271.9 Cr with H1 FY27 CFO of +Rs 351.24 Cr. Test whether the turn came from receivables collection, payables stretch, TReDS or bill discounting, or customer advances (for example BSNL mobilisation). Debtor days 76 at Mar-2026.
3. Brand margin claim. Verify the 40-42% brand gross margin and the 19% revenue share against the AR segment note. Check whether the brand segment is reported as a segment with its own result, or only in the presentation.
4. BSNL BharatNet order. Verify the Rs 3,194.83 Cr order from the exchange filing: the contracting entity, consortium share, bank guarantee and funding needs against Rs 209 Cr bank limits, and the revenue recognition basis.
