### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every quarter in this three-call sample volunteers a *different* external shock as the primary explanation for a shortfall: heavy/extended monsoon + US tariffs (Q2 FY26 call), continued US-tariff volatility with a brief Nov recovery then a mid-December relapse (Q3 FY26 call), and the Israel-Iran conflict from 28-Feb-2026 disrupting exports/RM and demand (Q4 FY26 call). Each cited shock is independently real and externally verifiable (monsoon severity, US tariff timeline, Israel-Iran conflict dates are all matters of public record), so this is **not** fabrication — but the pattern of "a fresh exogenous excuse every single quarter" is itself worth naming as a mild credibility watch-item, since it has not yet been tested by a "clean" quarter with no external distraction.

Classification: **external-blame-heavy, but each instance independently verifiable.** Balancing points toward honesty: management self-admitted a company-caused (not external) HFFR output loss from a planned capacity-installation shutdown in Q2 FY26 ("we have taken a bit of shutdown also to get ready for that new machine installation... so some output has been lost because of that as well" — Q2 FY26 call, Kothari), and the CFO volunteered that March 2026 was "the worst performing month" for RM pass-through before April improved (Q4 FY26 call) — both are candid, quantified, non-deflecting admissions rather than pure blame-shifting. Management does raise hard topics without always waiting for analyst pressure (e.g., volunteering the FY26 capex shortfall context, the BESS mid-call self-correction), but it has never said "we made a mistake" in as many words across the three calls; corrections are framed as clarifications, not errors.

- **Low-voltage/PVC**: KLJ Polymers named as an Indian competitor in the low-voltage fire-retardant segment (Q4 FY26 call).
- **HFFR**: Shakun Polymer, "now part of Orbia Group from Mexico," named as the direct HFFR competitor; DDev claims to be "the only listed player in India" in HFFR (Q3 and Q4 FY26 calls) — a specific, checkable claim.

- **Cable makers (customers, not competitors)**: KEI, Polycab, RR Kabel referenced by an analyst (not management) as a volume-growth benchmark in Q2 FY26 call; management's response was that these players have a different (heavier building-wire, indoor-installation) mix than DDev's power-cable-weighted exposure, which is a plausible but unverified explanation for DDev's weaker Q2 volume performance relative to these peers.

Credibility check overall: competitor claims are specific and named (a positive), but every comparative claim (market share, "only listed HFFR player," dominance beyond 11kV) is self-sourced within the calls with no third-party corroboration available in this document set.
