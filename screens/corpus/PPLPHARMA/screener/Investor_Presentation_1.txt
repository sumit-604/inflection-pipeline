Mumbai, India | October 27, 2026: Piramal Pharma Limited (NSE: PPLPHARMA | BSE: 543635), a leading 
global pharmaceutical, health and wellness company, today announced its standalone and consolidated 
results for the Second Quarter (Q2) and Half Year (H1) ended 30th September 2026.

Key Highlights 
• 
Broad-based Revenue Growth: Revenue grew 28% YoY in Q2FY27 and 23% YoY in H1FY27, driven by 
strong performance across all three businesses. 
• 
Significant Profitability Improvement: EBITDA increased 72% YoY in Q2FY27 and H1FY27, with over 
350bps EBITDA margin expansion in both periods. This was driven by higher capacity utilization, 
operating leverage, disciplined execution, and sustained cost optimization initiatives. 
• 
Robust RFP Activity and Order Inflows: Added 22 new customers and successfully hosted 110 
customer audits in H1FY27. 
• 
Best-in-Class Quality & Compliance: Successfully completed 12 regulatory inspections and 110 
customer audits during H1FY27, while maintaining the Company’s track record of no Official Action 
Indicated classifications across US FDA inspections. 
• 
Upgrade FY27 Outlook: Based on the strong first-half performance and continued business 
momentum, we upgrade our FY27 outlook and now expect revenue growth of 19% to 20%, 
accompanied by meaningfully higher profitability. 
Nandini Piramal, Chairperson, Piramal Pharma Limited said, “We delivered a strong H1FY27, with 
revenue growth of 23% and EBITDA margin expansion of over 350 basis points, driven by healthy demand 
across our businesses, disciplined execution, and improved operating leverage. The performance was led 
by our CDMO business, which grew 28% on the back of strong order inflows and robust execution. Our 
CHG business complemented this momentum with 24% growth, supported by inhalation anesthesia sales 
in ex-US markets and the integration of the Kenalog, while Consumer Healthcare continued to deliver 
steady 14% growth through the strong performance of Power Brands and e-commerce. 
With H2 typically stronger than H1, we are well positioned to deliver a strong full-year performance, 
supported by a healthy order book, encouraging customer engagement, and continued operating 
discipline.”

Key Business Highlights 
Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO): 
- 
Robust Growth: Broad-based growth across India and overseas sites, supported by healthy 
commercial demand, improved execution and disciplined cost management. India and North 
America led performance across the network. 
- 
Enhanced Profitability: Better capacity utilization and operating leverage drove a meaningful 
improvement in profitability. 
- 
Strong Commercial Momentum: Strong RFP activity and order inflows resulted in addition of 22 new 
customers during H1FY27. Phase III pipeline increased to 28 molecules from 25 as of March 2026. 
- 
Innovation and On-Patent Revenue Growth: Innovation-related revenues grew 24%, while on-
patent commercial manufacturing revenues grew 39% in H1FY27. 
- 
Differentiated Capabilities: Strengthened biologics capabilities through acquisition of a controlling 
stake in Yapan Bio. The Lexington expansion remains on track, will add sterile fill-finish capabilities, 
completing the integrated ADC offering. Commissioned a new microbiology laboratory at the site. 
- 
ADC Services continued to gain momentum, with revenues up 51% YoY in H1FY27. 
- 
Deepening Customer Engagement: Successfully hosted 110 customer audits during H1FY27, 
reflecting continued customer engagement and confidence in the Company’s quality systems. 
Complex Hospital Generics (CHG): 
- 
Inhalation Anesthesia Leadership: Maintained leadership in the US Sevoflurane market with a 48% 
value share, while building traction in ex-US markets. (Source: IQVIA) 
- 
Kenalog® Integration: Kenalog® contributed to CHG revenue during Q2FY27 and is expected to be 
an important growth driver for the business in FY27. 
- 
Intrathecal Therapy: Maintained the #1 position in the US Intrathecal Baclofen market. (Source: IQVIA) 
- 
Injectable Pain Management: Continued collaboration with suppliers to ensure product availability. 
- 
Building a Differentiated and Specialty Portfolio: Continued investments in 505(b)(2) products, 
complex and differentiated generics, branded products, in-licensing and co-development to 
strengthen the long-term portfolio. 
Piramal Consumer Healthcare (PCH): 
- 
Power Brands Continue to Outperform: Power Brands grew 21% YoY in H1FY27, contributing 53% 
of PCH sales, reflecting the strength of the core portfolio. 
- 
Strong E-commerce Momentum: Grew 32% YoY in H1FY27 and contributed 28% of PCH sales. 
- 
Innovation and Premiumization: Continued focus on fewer, larger, and higher-potential launches to 
enhance portfolio quality and margins. Launched the 'Vizible' range under Lacto Calamine to 
strengthen presence in premium skincare. 
- 
Sustained Brand Investments: Invested approximately 11% of PCH sales in media and trade 
promotion across digital and traditional channels to support long-term brand growth. 
- 
Bayer Distribution Agreement: The distribution agreement will naturally expire in December 2026. 
The transition is being jointly managed to ensure business continuity, with no material impact 
expected on FY27 guidance.

About Piramal Pharma Limited: 
Piramal Pharma Limited (PPL, NSE: PPLPHARMA I BSE: 543635), offers a portfolio of differentiated products and services 
through its 17 global development and manufacturing facilities and a global distribution network in over 100 countries. PPL 
includes Piramal Pharma Solutions (PPS), an integrated contract development and manufacturing organization; Piramal Critical 
Care (PCC), a complex hospital generics business; and the Piramal Consumer Healthcare (PCH) business, selling over-the-
counter consumer and wellness products. In addition, one of PPL's associate companies, Abbvie Therapeutics India Private
