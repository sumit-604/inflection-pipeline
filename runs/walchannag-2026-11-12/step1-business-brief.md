## The one engine
Order-book business (archetype: EPC/defence/capital goods order book). The thesis rests on one thing: quarterly execution holding near or above Rs 90 Cr on a strategic backlog (aerospace, nuclear, defence), so fixed cost is absorbed and the company moves from loss to profit. Two readings. (a) The three quarters at about Rs 90 Cr are a new run rate fed by fresh inflow. (b) They are a backlog burn: order book fell from about Rs 580-670 Cr to about Rs 375 Cr and inflow of about Rs 100 Cr a quarter only just replaces revenue. The separating observation: book-to-bill over the trailing four quarters and the closing order book at 30 Sep 2026.
