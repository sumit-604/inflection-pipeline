Identity: MMP Industries Ltd, NSE MMP (no BSE listing found), ISIN INE511Y01018. Screener: https://www.screener.in/company/MMP/consolidated/ (HTTP 200, consolidated view present). CMP Rs 450, market cap Rs 1,142 Cr, P/E 25.6 (screener, 2026-10-04).

1. Scale: FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 824.0 Cr (+19.1%), EBITDA Rs 66.3 Cr (8.0%, down 133 bps), PAT Rs 31.0 Cr (-20%) after a Rs 6.96 Cr post-tax net exceptional loss (Q1 FY27 investor PPT p.4-5; FY26 press release, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MMP_23052026181319_MMPPressRelease31032026.pdf).
2. Latest print, Q1 FY27: revenue Rs 232.8 Cr (+27% YoY), EBITDA Rs 21.0 Cr (9.0%, +187 bps), PAT Rs 13.7 Cr against a Rs -5.4 Cr loss in Q1 FY26 (https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MMP_10082026134252_MMPILPressRelease30062026.pdf).
3. What it makes: aluminium converter, Nagpur/Umred. Ingot to atomised, pyro/flake and leafing powder (capacity 12,000 / 16,800 / 300 MTPA; 8,000-9,000 MTPA atomised used captively), rolled and printed foil (8,400 MTPA rolling), conductors and ABC cables, and new polymer insulators (Q1 FY27 PPT).
4. Mix Q1 FY27: Powders Rs 153.8 Cr (62%, +45% YoY), Foils Rs 60.5 Cr (26%, +29%), Conductors Rs 17.1 Cr (-42%), Polymer insulators Rs 0.5 Cr (press release Q1 FY27 p.1).
5. Where the margin comes from: conversion spread per MT over the aluminium price. Powders go to explosives, AAC blocks, pesticides, paints and pyro; foil goes to pharma blister packaging. Gross margin 21.0% in Q1 FY27 vs 20.5% FY26 (PPT).
6. Exports: management guides +40-50% export growth in FY27 (West/Central Asia, Europe, US) at higher margins than domestic (press release Q1 FY27 p.2).
7. FY27 guidance: revenue +15-18%, blended EBITDA margin to improve "subject to stable metal prices" (press release Q1 FY27 p.2).
8. New engines, all in ramp-up and loss-making: MMP Electricals (polymer insulators, vendor approvals in progress), MMP Cables (18,000 MTPA wire rod, commercial production targeted Q4 FY27), LT power cables (1,200 MTPA, BIS approval pending, sales Q3 FY27), lidding foil (launch Q3 FY27), 7 MW captive solar (Rs 30 Cr, Q3 FY27). New subsidiaries lost Rs 4.08 Cr EBITDA in FY26 and Rs 1.37 Cr in Q1 FY27 (FY26 and Q1 FY27 press releases).
9. Associates: Star Circlips & Engineering (26.06% held; Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 54.4 Cr, PAT Rs 8.4 Cr) and a Toyo Aluminium K.K. JV (26% held). Associate profit shows as a separate line between EBIT and reported PBT (Q1 FY27 PPT; press release p.5).
10. April 2025 Umred incident: estimated Rs 45-50 Cr revenue loss and Rs 7-8 Cr EBITDA hit in Q1 FY26; insurance claim receivable and salvage credited in Q4 FY26 (FY26 press release).
11. Credit: CRISIL BBB+/Stable / A2, bank lines enhanced to Rs 135 Cr from Rs 80 Cr; migrated back from ISSUER NOT COOPERATING on 2026-04-27 (CRISIL rationale, https://www.crisil.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/MMPIndustriesLimited_April%2027_%202026_RR_389209.html).
12. Ownership: promoter 74.49% (Jun 2026), flat at 74.48% for eight quarters, no pledge; FII 0.02% (NSE SHP XBRL).
13. Disclosure cadence: one earnings call a year (May 2026; prior on screener July 2022). NSE sought clarifications on the financial results on 2026-03-05 and 2026-06-23 (NSE announcements API).

The ONE engine the thesis would rest on: a mix shift inside powders (export and specialty grades at higher EBITDA per MT) plus backward integration (wire rod, FRP rods, solar) that lifts the blended margin from 8% toward the double digits while new subsidiaries stop losing money.
