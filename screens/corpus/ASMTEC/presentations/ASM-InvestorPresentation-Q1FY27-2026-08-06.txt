"Our Q1FY27 performance reflects the continued efforts of our teams and the confidence our customers have 
placed in us. During the quarter, we reported revenue from operations of ₹199 Crs., EBITDA of ~₹46 Crs. with an 
EBITDA margin of 23.2%, and Profit After Tax of ~₹27 Crs. Our focus continues to be on disciplined execution, 
operational efficiency, and delivering consistent value to our stakeholders.
As industries continue to adopt automation, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and intelligent manufacturing, we remain 
committed to strengthening our capabilities across the Semiconductor, Electronics, Solar, Engineering, 
Aerospace, and other advanced industries that we serve. We will continue to integrate AI across our engineering 
and manufacturing processes to enhance productivity, improve quality, and support our customers' evolving 
requirements.
Looking ahead, we remain committed to building on our capabilities with a long-term perspective, maintaining 
strong governance and execution standards, and creating sustainable value for our customers, shareholders, 
employees, and all our stakeholders.”
