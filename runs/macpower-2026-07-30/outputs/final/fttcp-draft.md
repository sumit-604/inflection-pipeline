# FTTCP DRAFT, Macpower CNC Machines Ltd (MACPOWER)

CMP Rs 1,481. Run date 2026-07-30. First workup, not a refresh. Concalls available, so this is NOT a no-concall run. Business type: standard operating business (capital goods manufacturer), so the four standard transitions apply, not the lender set.

This is a plain-language forward read on four things: is revenue growing, are margins widening, is the cash real, and is the capital earning. Every judgment call below is made, with a confidence tag and the single fact that would prove it wrong. The valuation itself is not here; this hands a P/E base to Phase 3.

**Transition calls**

5. Revenue forward verdict: FIRING (+2). Order book Rs 456 cr at June 2026 is 1.37 times FY26 revenue, Q1 FY27 revenue grew 56.1 percent, and installed capacity has reached 2,500 machines. `fairly sure`. Wrong if two consecutive quarters show sequential order-book decline.
   - Note on the backward label: the trailing revenue CAGR is about 17.5 percent, just under the 20 percent line that a strict FIRING backward label needs, dragged by a soft FY25 of 8.6 percent. 6. Margin forward verdict: STAGNANT (0). `genuinely uncertain`. The two readings: STAGNANT because the printed margin has sat at 16 percent for three straight prints (FY26 16.2, Q1 FY27 16.2) and the 25 percent aspiration is vision gated on undated land; STARTING (+1) because EBITDA margin did rise 148 basis points over two years (14.7 to 16.2) and the NEXA premium mix at 40 percent of the order book is a real documented lever. The draft used STAGNANT, rounding down, because the reported margin has not actually moved and B02 found the FY25 margin gain was substantially a mechanical inventory-build effect, not demonstrated efficiency. The single fact that flips it to STARTING: EBITDA margin above about 17.5 percent for two consecutive quarters.

7. Cash conversion forward verdict: STAGNANT (0), determination INDETERMINATE. `fairly sure` on the verdict, `genuinely uncertain` on structural-versus-growth. Operating cash to profit ran 0.70, 0.27, then 0.41 across FY24 to FY26, so it is low and improved off the FY25 trough rather than declining. The determination is INDETERMINATE, not a clean growth-induced pass, because three things block a clean call: there is no credit rating in the inputs to confirm the working capital is a recognised growth phenomenon, receivables ageing deteriorated (over-6-month bucket up to 15.4 percent of gross from 12.2), and management said on the Q4 FY26 call that cash conversion will not improve rapidly. INDETERMINATE caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. Wrong if the next credit rating rationale or receivables ageing schedule shows the working capital self-reversing.

8. Structural versus growth-induced test: the pattern reads growth-linked (about 80 finished machines worth roughly Rs 100 cr are built and dispatched but not yet booked, waiting on customer subsidy and loan paperwork, and raw-material inventory is stocked ahead of demand), so if growth stopped tomorrow that working capital would largely release. But it cannot be called cleanly growth-induced because the evidence that would confirm it (rating rationale, ageing schedule) is absent and management itself will not promise a quick turn. Held at INDETERMINATE. `genuinely uncertain`. Wrong if a rating rationale confirms the working-capital intensity is temporary and growth-linked.

9. ROCE forward verdict: STAGNANT (0). `fairly sure`. Return on capital is still premium at 23.75 percent (FY25, Note 38), down from 26.70 percent (FY24), so it is high and softening, not depressed and not collapsing. It is not TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED because the fall is about 295 basis points, well short of the 500 basis point collapse that label needs, and it is not DECLINING because the cause is growth capex and working-capital build with the capital still earning a premium return. A fresh capex cycle (the 13-acre plant, the possible 60-acre plant) is the classic counter-catalyst that holds ROCE flat as the capital base grows. The draft scores it STAGNANT because a premium ROCE being held, not expanded, scores zero on the forward change, even though the level is high. Wrong if ROCE expands 200 basis points a year for two years (would become FIRING) or falls through 18 percent with no capex-commissioning offset (would become DECLINING).

10. Normalization route for Phase 3: NONE. Neither Route A nor Route B of the v3.5.1 reconciliation applies. Route A fails because idle capital (CWIP about Rs 4.86 cr, near-zero net cash, no idle raised capital) is well under 20 percent of capital employed. Route B is barred because the ROCE verdict is STAGNANT, not TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED or RECOVERING. Statutory ROCE feeds Pillar 1 directly. ROCE recovery credited via: not credited, there is no recovery to credit. `fairly sure`. Wrong if stage 10 anchors an FY26 ROCE that reframes the verdict.

11. Composite and position: +2 out of 8, DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID. `fairly sure`. Only revenue is firing forward; margin, cash, and capital efficiency are all holding, not improving. This is the incomplete-transition case: a fast-growing top line that the other three transitions have not yet joined. Wrong if margin prints above 17.5 percent and cash conversion clears 0.5 times together, which would move the score to +4 and the read to a genuine four-transition inflection.

12. Kernex cap: does not engage. The cap needs a transition that is both DECLINING and has catalyst strength NONE. Cash is the candidate, but it improved off its trough (not declining) and has a weak documented catalyst (the finished-goods release), so the cap does not fire. It is a genuine close call and harmless here, since the composite already sits at DEEP WATCH. `fairly sure`.

## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

What could make each transition fire, what the evidence is, what confirms it, what kills it.

**Revenue (firing).** The order book is Rs 456 cr and growing, the bid pipeline is over Rs 1,000 cr, capacity has reached 2,500 machines, and the premium NEXA range is 40 percent of orders. This is documented (order book and capacity in the presentation and results), not just talk. Confirms it: order book keeps rising and utilisation moves toward 90 percent. Kills it: two quarters of sequential order-book decline, or a capital-goods demand cycle turning down.

**Margin (contested).** The levers are real but slow. Premium mix (NEXA at Rs 29 to 32 lakh against Rs 15 to 16 lakh base machines) and higher utilisation should lift margin; backward integration is capped because the controllers stay imported. The 25 percent aspiration is gated on a plant the company has not signed. Confirms it: margin above 17.5 percent for two quarters. Kills it: margin stuck at 16 percent while raw-material cost or competition bites.

**Cash (weak, no documented program).** There is no factoring facility, no stated debtor-day target that has actually been hit, and management is deferring rather than committing. The one real near-term mechanic is the roughly Rs 100 cr of finished machines that convert to cash once customer paperwork clears. Confirms it: operating cash to profit back above 0.5 to 0.6 times with receivable and inventory days reversing. Kills it: another year below 0.5 times with days still rising, which would make the drag structural.

**ROCE (held flat).** The capital is earning 24 percent; the question is whether the new plant lifts or dilutes it. Confirms an upside surprise: ROCE expands 200 basis points a year as capex commissions and utilisation rises. Kills it: ROCE falls through 18 percent with the new capex block sitting idle.

Management intent-and-action ledger, in one line each: revenue has real documented action (capacity reached, order book, 13-acre lease); margin has vision plus a real mix lever but no printed step-up; cash has vision and no documented action, which confirms the conservative read; ROCE has capex action that cuts both ways. No transition is loosened by documented action beyond where the evidence already put it.

## 4. THE VERDICT

The forward score is +2 out of 8: revenue firing (+2), margin stagnant (0), cash stagnant (0), capital efficiency stagnant (0). That is DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID. Only one of the four transitions is firing forward; the top line is growing fast but margin, cash, and capital efficiency have not joined it, which is the incomplete-transition case. The Kernex cap did not engage and the trim rule did not apply. The whole call turns on one pairing over the next two or three quarters: does EBITDA margin clear 17.5 percent while operating cash conversion clears 0.5 times. If both print, this becomes a +4 four-transition inflection worth far more work; if neither does, it stays a fast grower that does not convert, capped by the cash question.

**THE EARNINGS-BASIS QUESTION (operator decides, I do not pick).** One-year-forward P/E applies the exit multiple to next year's EPS; trailing P/E applies it to the last reported EPS.
