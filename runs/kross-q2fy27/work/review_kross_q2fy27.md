### 3. Business-model intelligence
Kross earns money in two ways [MGMT].

It makes most inputs in-house: forging, casting, heat treatment and machining [MGMT].
Its H1 plant loading was 65% in forging, 72% in machining and 75% in casting [MGMT].
Each Rs 100 of sales left Rs 43.90 after materials and Rs 13.02 after all operating costs in Q2 [FILED].
Gross margin moved between 40.7% and 46.1% over FY22-FY26. FY26's 45.7% was the high point since FY22 [MGMT].
The business ties up cash. Debtors, stock and other current assets take about 124 days of cash cycle [FILED, derived].
Model drift this quarter has three parts [INFERENCE].
Growth moved to the trailer half, which the Notion work says has weaker pricing power (Notion) [SECONDARY].
Growth moved to fewer customers [MGMT].
Capital moved upstream into seamless tubes and cold drawn tubes. These are new products with commodity traits [INFERENCE].

### 3. Business-model intelligence
Kross earns money in two ways [MGMT-DECK].
First, it builds trailer axles and suspension sets. They were about 49% of Q2 sales [MGMT-CALL, MGMT-DECK].
Second, it forges, casts and machines parts for truck and tractor makers. They were about 51% of Q2 sales.
It makes about 4,000-4,500 trailer axles a month. About 80% now use the new single-piece extruded beam [MGMT-CALL].
Each Rs 100 of sales left Rs 43.90 after materials and Rs 13.02 after all operating costs in Q2 [FILED].
Gross margin moved between 40.7% and 46.1% over FY22-FY26 [MGMT-DECK].
The largest customer is about 15-16% of revenue. The top 5 are 64.21% [MGMT-CALL, MGMT-DECK].
The business ties up cash. The cash cycle is about 124 days [FILED, derived]. Management targets about 80 receivable days [MGMT-CALL].
Model drift has three parts [INFERENCE].
Growth moved to the trailer half and to fewer customers.
Value per trailer axle rose about 39-47% in one quarter, on an analyst's Q1 volume base. Suspension mix and steel price passed through drove it, and pass-through earns little margin.
Capital is moving upstream into seamless and cold drawn tubes. Seamless phase 1 uses 30% of capacity for own needs [MGMT-CALL]. The deck plans to sell surplus capacity outside [MGMT-DECK]. That part behaves like a commodity [INFERENCE].
Not disclosed: segment margins, unit volumes in public filings, export revenue in rupees, and tipping-jack revenue.

### 4. Competition intelligence
On the call, management named York as the trailer-axle market leader. It also named Tata Motors, Hendrickson and one more firm as competitors [MGMT-CALL].
Tata Motors is also a customer (Notion) [SECONDARY]. A customer that makes the same product can take the work in-house [INFERENCE].
Kross's edge: India's first axle-beam extrusion plant. The beam uses less material and needs no welding [MGMT-DECK]. Management says the benefit comes "over a longer time period" [MGMT-CALL].
Kross's second edge: deep in-house integration. On many component products it is the single or majority source [MGMT-CALL].
Kross is weaker on price. Gross margin fell even after price revisions [FILED, MGMT-DECK]. Management claims retrospective compensation. The Notion work says the trailer half has no such mechanism (Notion) [SECONDARY].
The risk to watch: the market leader's response to the extruded beam, truck-maker approvals of the beam, and Tata Motors' choice to buy or make.
