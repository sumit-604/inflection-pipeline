### Finding 1 (MINOR): Speaker misattribution, Claim 3
B06 attributes the CENTURYPLY Feb-2026 (Q3 FY26) organised-share progression
quote ("we were at 4%, 4.5%... we are around 8%, 8.5%") to **Keshav Bhajanka**.
The transcript shows the actual speaker is **Nikita Bansal** (plywood segment
head), responding to an analyst question about plywood industry growth
(lines 367-379 of CENTURYPLY-Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.txt). The quote
itself, its content, and its quarter are all correct; only the named speaker
is wrong. Low materiality — does not change the verdict or the evidentiary
weight of Claim 3, which is corroborated by two other peers independently.

### Finding 2 (MAJOR): Wrong-call citation, Claim 3 / peer coverage map
B06's Claim 3 narrative and its Part 3 peer coverage map both cite the
CENTURYPLY **Nov-2025 (Q2 FY26)** call for the quote "we need to stop looking
at cycles... we need to start concentrating more towards capturing the
unorganized to organized... the growth is going to be there irrespective of
the real estate cycle" (Nikita Bansal). This exact quote does **not appear**
in the Nov-2025 transcript. It is found instead in the **Aug-2025 (Q1 FY26)**
transcript (lines 450-459), correctly attributed to Nikita Bansal there. The
Nov-2025 call does contain a related but distinct exchange on the same
topic (Sanjay Agarwal, on plywood's <10% organised share and QCO-driven
import restriction, lines 304-321), so the Nov-2025 SUBSTANTIVE tag survives
on the Punjab-flood citation and this adjacent exchange, but the specific
quote as sourced is not findable at the cited location. This is a genuine
anchor error (wrong call/quarter, not merely a paraphrase) and would mislead
anyone spot-checking the Nov-2025 transcript specifically for this evidence.
Rated MAJOR per the rubric (a SUBSTANTIVE tag's specific citation not
findable at the stated source) rather than CRITICAL, because the quote is a
real, correctly-worded statement by the correct speaker at the correct
company, only misdated by one call; it does not fabricate a signal, and
Claim 3's overall VERIFIED status is independently supported by Wakefit and
Greenlam citations that check out cleanly.

No other citation checked (19 of the ~23 sampled) showed a mismatch, wrong
attribution, or unfindable anchor. Coverage of this audit: roughly 20-23
