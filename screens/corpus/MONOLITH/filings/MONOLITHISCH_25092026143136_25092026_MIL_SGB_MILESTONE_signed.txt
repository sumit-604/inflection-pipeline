Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform you that SGB Limited, a refractory lining solution widely 
used in integrated steel plants, has demonstrated improved refractory lining material life and 
performance during trials conducted at Shree Ram Multimetals, Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab. 
 
The successful trial represents a significant development in the performance and application of 
SGB Limited’s refractory lining solution in the western region of India. 
 
A brief overview of the development is set out below: 
 
Technical & Operational Highlights: 
 
Lining Life: SGB Limited’s lining material ran for 32 heats benchmark,surpassing the 
prevailing peer standard of 22 to 25 heats. 
 
 
Challenging Charge Mix Parameters: The 32 heats were executed under an intensive 
operating charge mix of 70% scrap and 30% sponge and pellets, demonstrating 
outstanding resilience against severe mechanical wear, thermal shock, and aggressive 
slag chemistry. 
 
 
Consistent Steel Grade Metallurgy: The trial ran continuously on mild carbon steel 
across the 0.16% to 0.20% carbon range. 
 
 
Immediate Commercial Traction: Driven by the remarkable performance at Shree 
Ram Multimetals, multiple additional manufacturing units are undergoing commercial 
trials for SGB Limited’s lining material. 
 
 
Strategic Multi-Region Expansion:Having already established itself as a formidable 
market force across Eastern India, setting this high-water mark in North-West region 
operating corridors signals a major geographical boost for the company.
