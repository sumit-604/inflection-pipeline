### Mandated specific assessments

**(1) The ~Rs 6 Cr overlay inside the 4.01% credit cost (F6.1).** Reported credit cost
4.01% (L8) INCLUDES a ~Rs 6 Cr management-overlay build this quarter (L39). On a ~Rs 7,324
Cr book, ~Rs 6 Cr ≈ ~33 bps annualised, so **ex-overlay credit cost ≈ 3.68%** — comfortably
below the top of the 3.5-4% band. **Is the improvement management-managed? YES, partly.**
Management states it deliberately "absorb[s] some of the profits into these overlays...
because this is a good year" and targets overlay at ~5% of book (L39, L50, L51); total
overlay on balance sheet is Rs 11 Cr (L39). This is conservative, cross-cycle provisioning
(a quality positive), but it also means the reported 4.01% "top-of-band" print is a
management-chosen figure, not purely incurred loss. Tripwire #5 therefore reads GREEN on
the reported number AND on the ~3.68% underlying — but the mechanism is discretionary, so
future "improvement" could be overlay release rather than incurred-loss decline. Watch the
overlay direction (build vs release) each quarter.

**(2) Other-income step-down: DA nil + forex→OCI (F9.1, F1.1).** Two simultaneous moves cut
recurring other income: (a) **DA nil this quarter** (L20), vs ~Rs 20 Cr DA gain in Q4 —
management "did not feel the need to borrow" post-IPO; DA is a discretionary lever targeted
at 5-7% of AUM (L55) and can be re-pulled any quarter, re-introducing PAT-on-DA reliance;
(b) **forex volatility reclassified P&L→OCI from this quarter** (L22), permanently removing
the ~Rs 12 Cr prior-quarter forex P&L benefit and routing future forex below the line.
Combined ~Rs 32-35 Cr of prior-period other income (L22) does not recur in P&L. Remaining
disclosed other income is thin: cross-sell ~Rs 9 Cr of Rs 490 Cr revenue (L107, L108). Net
read: PAT quality IMPROVED (headline no longer leans on DA/forex), but the forward
other-income run-rate is lower and DA re-pull is the swing factor → QfM Q6, Q11.

**(3) The twice-deferred slippage disclosure (F1.2, F7.1, F17.4).** Two different analysts
(Sonal Minas L95; "sust"/Radhi L117) asked for the gross slippage number; management
"haven't reported slippage number... don't have that number readily available... can share
eventually" (L95) and "we haven't mentioned the slippages number" (L117). **Compounding
tension:** management simultaneously cites *reducing slippages* as a NIM tailwind (L117) —
i.e., it uses an undisclosed metric as a positive driver while declining to quantify it.
Slippage is the direct feed to credit-cost and PAR trajectory; its absence blocks
independent verification of the "structural and sustainable" improvement claim (L8). This
is the single most important disclosure gap of the call → QfM Q2, Q3; first tracked quarter
of silence (F17.4), re-check mandatory at Q2.

**(4) NIM 15.9% vs 14.25-14.75% guidance (F7.2).** Q1 NIM 15.9% (L8) printed ~115 bps ABOVE
the top of the 14.25-14.75% guidance band (L36, L116), yet guidance was **not raised**, with
an explicit unpriced upside conceded ("there's an upside there," L117). Analyst flagged the
likely cause: "this is also from the IPO proceeds" (L36) — equity-funded book depresses the
borrowing-cost drag, inflating NIM until leverage normalises (3.15x today → 4-4.5x target,
L88). Management also flags mortgage-mix shift as a future NIM headwind (lower-yield
mortgage rising, L29). So the held guidance is **defensible sandbagging**: NIM will
mechanically compress as (a) IPO cash is deployed into leverage and (b) mix shifts to
mortgage. Either the guidance is deliberately conservative (credibility-positive) or a NIM
step-down is expected later in FY27 — both point to NOT extrapolating 15.9% forward → QfM
Q4. Tripwire read: NIM strongly above guidance is a GREEN this quarter but not a forward
run-rate.

**(5) Approval rate 55%→45% vs 44,000 adds — over-lending angle (F17.3, F7.3).** Approvals
tightened from ~55% to ~45% of applications (L27), yet Aye still added 44,000 borrowers
(+38% YoY, L8, L27) and declined ~55,000/quarter, of whom 20%+ take gold loans elsewhere
(L40). Management's explanation — huge underpenetrated market (2-3% penetrated, L40) — is
plausible AND the direction is reassuring (tightening while GNPA falls, not rises). BUT the
over-lending tripwire (#8) cannot be tested: **AUM per borrower and repeat-loan share are
not disclosed**, and management admits it does not systematically track borrower external
leverage ("we don't have that data... my guess would be," L75, L80). So over-lending is
UNVERIFIABLE, not cleared → QfM Q12.
