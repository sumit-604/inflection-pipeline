Explanation for the Deviation 
/ Variation 
The estimated Issue Expenses were ₹433.59 Lakhs. However, the 
actual Issue Expenses incurred by the Company amounted to 
₹400.36 Lakhs, resulting in a saving of ₹33.23 Lakhs 
Such Saved ₹33.23 Lakhs will be utilised towards incremental cost 
that will be incurred in  Setting up of 7.20 MW (AC) / 9.99 MW 
(DC) Ground Mounted Solar Power Plant.
