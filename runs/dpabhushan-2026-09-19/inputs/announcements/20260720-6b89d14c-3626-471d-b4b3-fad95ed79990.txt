Subject: Opening of Company owned Showroom in Dahod, Gujarat

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015,
read with the Company's Policy for Determination of Materiality of Events and Information, we wish to
inform that the Board of Directors of the Company has approved the proposal for opening a Company-owned
jewellery showroom at Dahod, Gujarat, as part of the Company's retail expansion strategy. The said showroom
will be opened at Ambika Society, Dahod (Guj.).

The proposed showroom, having an approximate retail area of 3,200 square feet, will be the Company's first
showroom in the State of Gujarat and will mark the Company's strategic entry into the Gujarat retail market.
The showroom will be owned and operated by the Company and is expected to establish the Company's retail
presence in the region, enhance customer accessibility, and strengthen its brand recognition among customers
in Gujarat. The showroom will offer the Company's comprehensive range of jewellery products in accordance
with its established quality standards and customer service practices.

The expected date of commencement of operations are presently under finalisation and shall be implemented
in due course.

The Board meeting commenced at 11:30 AM (IST) and concluded at 12:40 PM (IST).

Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
