Rationale for 
Alteration of Main 
Objects 
The Company is exploring to foray into the business of designing, developing, 
manufacturing, engineering etc. of products, systems and solutions etc. for semi- 
conductors, aerospace applications, drones defense, and other related technologies 
etc. by itself or through its subsidiaries. 
To diversify into the new business activities, the Company shall be required to 
insert new clause under its objects to the Memorandum of Association (“MOA"). 
Accordingly, it was considered necessary to alter the object clause of the MOA to 
enable the Company to enter into the abovesaid new business activities. The said 
alteration in object clause of the MOA is subject to the approval of the shareholders 
of the Company.
