   dragging reported PBT growth below core-op growth; (b) **ETR fell to 23.72% from 28.88%**

   ETR drop (holding 28.88%), Q1FY27 CN PAT would be ~32.8 Cr not 35.16 (~7% of PAT is from

   otherwise real growth print.
5. **D&A / finance scaling faster than revenue?** Depreciation +54.8% and finance +96.3% vs
   revenue +88.6%. Finance costs are scaling roughly with revenue (WC-debt funded, not a
   pure absorption gap); depreciation is scaling slower than revenue (Bhiwadi already
   contributing). No acute absorption deficit, but **finance cost at +96% YoY confirms the
   growth is debt-and-WC-heavy** — ties to the cash flag.
6. **Other Income concentration changing?** OI fell YoY (3.67 → 2.34 Cr) and is only 5.1% of
   PBT — not masking anything. Clean on this axis.
