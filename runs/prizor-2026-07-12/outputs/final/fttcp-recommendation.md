## FLAG-CASH (ACTIVE)

Determination: INDETERMINATE, leaning structural.

Cash conversion has been broken across three consecutive years. Operating
cash flow was minus Rs 1.82 Cr in FY24, minus Rs 14.10 Cr in FY25 against PAT
of Rs 10.15 Cr (conversion minus 139%), and plus Rs 0.29 Cr in FY26 against
PAT of Rs 20.76 Cr (conversion about 1.4%) (B02, Cash Flow Statement p.73;
B12b, Inv.Pres. slide32/p33).

Cited evidence. Finished goods inventory grew 281.7% in FY25 against revenue
up 99.4% (B02, Note 16/32); inventory turnover fell 4.19x to 3.32x (B01).
Receivables rose from Rs 15.74 Cr to Rs 38.63 Cr into FY26 with H2 FY26
carrying 72% of full year revenue (B12b, Inv.Pres. slide26/p27). Short term
borrowings rose from Rs 2.76 Cr to Rs 31.82 Cr funding that build (B04,
Inv.Pres. slide26). That is the signature of a working capital heavy scale
up. Against it, FY26 conversion stayed near 1.4% even as PAT tripled, which a
purely growth induced build would not predict, and direct peer Sahasra treats
negative operating cash flow as a structural multi year feature of scaling
with no return to positive guided before FY2028 (B06). The record cannot
confirm growth induced (FY26 did not recover) and cannot confirm structural
(audited FY26 cash flow and receivables ageing absent). INDETERMINATE.

Rating agency working capital rationale: NOT FOUND. No rating PDF exists in
the input set (B00).

Missing evidence to resolve it: the credit rating working capital rationale
(obtain the CRISIL or equivalent rationale), and the full FY26 audited cash
flow statement plus receivables ageing schedule (obtain from the FY26 annual
report via BSE and NSE filings when published).

Falsification metric. Next audited or interim cumulative operating cash flow
must turn clearly positive and reach at least about 40% of PAT. A third
straight year near or below zero while PAT prints above Rs 20 Cr hardens the
structural read. Threshold: CFO under about 20% of PAT confirms the bear.

Cap effect: INDETERMINATE caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS before
the confidence band downgrade, and the cash multiplier holds at the
conservative 0.65x in Pillar 2 (B11).
