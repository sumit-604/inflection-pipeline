## MY RULINGS

Every call below is made. Each carries a confidence tag and the single fact that would flip it.

1. **Forward window: 6 months primary, 12 months for capital efficiency.** Aimtron reports half yearly, not quarterly, so no 3 month print exists. The next confirming print is the H1 FY27 half year result. Confidence: sure. Would flip if: the company moves to quarterly reporting, which it promised twice and has not done.

2. **Business type: standard operating business, run the four standard transitions.** B04 reads business_type manufacturing, an EMS and ODM contract manufacturer of assembled boards and finished boxes. Not a lender. Confidence: sure. Would flip if: B04 were a bank, NBFC, MFI or HFC, which it is not.

3. **Workup intent: first workup.** No companies/AIMTRON.md exists and the manifest run_type is full. Destination PE, prior thesis and prior devil's advocate are N/A because Role 1 has not run. Confidence: sure. Would flip if: a prior company memory file or a refresh run_type were present.

4. **Sector cap row: Recycling / Manufacturing, 25x. The manifest row Pharma / CDMO, 38x, is wrong and is overridden.** Aimtron is an EMS contract manufacturer, not a pharma or CDMO business, and its four peers (Avalon, Centum, Cyient DLM, Vinyas) are all EMS names. The Section 1B cap table has no dedicated EMS or electronics manufacturing row, so the closest honest fit is the generic manufacturing row at 25x. Phase 3 stage 11 must use 25x, not 38x. Confidence: fairly sure. Closest alternative is Telecom equipment at 30x, rejected because Aimtron is a diversified contract manufacturer serving many verticals, not a telecom equipment maker. Would flip if: a dedicated EMS row were added to the Section 1B table at a different cap, which is an operator decision, not mine to invent.

5. **Cash conversion determination: INDETERMINATE.** The cash drain could be growth funding a real scale up, or it could be value leaving through related parties. The evidence that would separate the two is absent. This caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS and it caps nothing lower only because the growth is genuine. Confidence: fairly sure the label is INDETERMINATE, genuinely uncertain which way it resolves. The growth reading: receivables and inventory grew during a funded post listing ramp, and if growth paused the working capital would release. The value transfer reading: 46.3% of FY25 receivables are owed by promoter controlled related parties, one Aimtron Corporation USA balance flipped from a Rs1,061.76 lakh payable to a Rs2,152.54 lakh receivable while sales to it fell, a new Rs404.99 lakh related party expense appeared with no description, and unexplained loans and advances to unnamed other parties surfaced on the H2 FY26 call at Rs70, Rs58.3 and Rs16.8 Cr with the CFO unable to answer. The draft uses the INDETERMINATE reading. Would resolve to growth induced if: H1 FY27 consolidated operating cash flow turns solidly positive and debtor days fall below 120. Would resolve to structural if: operating cash flow is negative a third straight period, or the related party receivables keep rising.

6. **ROCE state: SUSTAINED, not temporarily depressed and not declining.** ROCE is 24.0% in FY26 and rising from 20.79% in FY25. The apparent fall from 31.48% in FY24 is a pre-IPO artifact, because FY24 was a capital light base before the June 2024 listing added equity. Post listing the number is premium and rising. Confidence: fairly sure. Because ROCE is not depressed, the Amendment 4.5 normalized-ROCE anchor does NOT apply. Would flip to declining if: the greenfield capex block lands and ROCE falls below 18% for a full year with no revenue ramp behind it.

7. **Forward verdicts: Revenue FIRING, Margin STAGNANT, Cash DECLINING, ROCE STAGNANT.** Composite +1. Confidence: fairly sure on Revenue and Cash, genuinely uncertain on Margin. The margin uncertainty: the bull reading is that the acquired US unit ramps from 11% to 18 to 20% EBITDA and lifts the group, the bear reading is that the box build mix shift and the acquisition dilution hold the group margin flat to down. The draft used the flat reading, STAGNANT, because the ramp is an unproven claim and the mix is working the other way. Would move Margin to FIRING if: the acquired unit prints 18%+ EBITDA within a year.

8. **Position: DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID, Kernex cap engaged.** The +1 composite already sits in the DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID band, and the Kernex cap holds it there independently because cash conversion is DECLINING with no observable catalyst. Confidence: sure on the cap, fairly sure on the leaning. Would upgrade one band if: three or more monitoring triggers fire favourably and cash conversion moves from below 30% to above 50% probability, chiefly a positive H1 FY27 operating cash flow print.

## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

Revenue. The strongest and most documented catalyst is the acquired US operation, AIC and ICS, now consolidating for a full year in FY27. That is real and it is signed. The weaker catalysts are the greenfield plant, which lands at the very end of the window around Q4 FY27, and a large but static order pipeline described the same way across three calls. What confirms revenue firing: FY27 consolidated revenue above Rs430 Cr. What kills the quality of it: an organic, ex acquisition number that stays flat, which management already struggled to defend.

Margin. The one catalyst that would move the margin band is the acquired unit climbing from 11% to 18 to 20% EBITDA. It is a management claim with no print behind it. Against it sit the acquisition dilution and a mix moving toward lower value box build. What confirms it: the acquired unit prints 18%+ EBITDA within a year. What kills it: consolidated EBITDA margin drifts below 20%.

Cash. This is where the search comes up empty. No factoring line, no debtor day target, no collection program, no timeline offered when asked. Management reassured on working capital in the H2 FY25 call and the reassurance did not hold, with FY26 consolidated cash flow at negative Rs40 Cr. What would confirm a turn: a positive H1 FY27 operating cash flow with debtor days below 120. What confirms the bad case: a third straight negative operating cash flow, or related party receivables climbing again.

ROCE. The catalyst is operating leverage on a high asset turn base. The counter catalyst is the greenfield capex treadmill. What confirms sustained: ROCE stays above 20% through the build. What kills it: ROCE below 18% for a full year with the plant still not earning.
