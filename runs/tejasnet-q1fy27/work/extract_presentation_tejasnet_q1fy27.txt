   47	                                      Press Release
   48	                              Tejas announces Q1FY27 results
   49	
   50	
   51	Bengaluru, July 27, 2026: Tejas Networks [BSE: 540595, NSE: TEJASNET] today reported its
   52	financial results for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026. Tejas Networks designs, develops,
   53	manufactures and sells high-performance telecom and networking products, which are used
   54	to build high-speed communication networks.
   55	
   56	                                                       Amount in Rs. Cr
   57	                           Ql-FV27            Ql-FV26             FV26
   58	 Net Revenue                    402                202           1,103
   59	 PBT                          (271)              (297)         (1,354)
   60	 PAT                          (202)              (194)           (909)
   61	
   62	
   63	Mr. Arnob Roy, MD and CEO of Tejas Networks said, "Ql revenues were driven by
   64	international shipments of 5G radios and domestic shipments of 100G/400G Optical and FTTx
   65	products. We registered our first commercial win for an end-to-end 5G network deployment
   66	in South America, marking a significant milestone for the company. We witnessed growing
   67	demand for our fiber broadband, packet and optical transmission products from leading
   68	carriers and utilities, in India and in international markets, driven by growth in datacenter
   69	deployments."
   70	
   71	
   72	Some of the other key highlights of Ql FY27 were:
   73	   •   Supplied GPON OlTs to Tier-1 Indian telcos for their countrywide rollouts
   74	   •   Selected as a vendor for the communication network modernization of a large power
   75	       utility
   76	   •   Won expansion order for l00G+ Coherent DWDM equipment from a leading
   77	       bandwidth wholesaler in Africa
   78	   •   Filed 46 patents in Ql FY27 taking our cumulative global patent filing count to 722
   79	
   80	Mr. AVS Prasad, CFO said, "In Ql FY27 we had a revenue of Rs. 402 Cr, a QoQ growth of
   81	21%. We ended the quarter with an order book of Rs. 1,529 Cr. Our net debt was Rs. 4,277
   82	Cr; gross debt of Rs. 4,866 Cr and cash of Rs. 589 Cr."
