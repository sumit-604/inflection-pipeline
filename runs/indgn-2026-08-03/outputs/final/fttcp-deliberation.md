forward P/E).**

  forward credits a recovery the margin transition says is not yet visible while

- Operator ruling: "Let's take the forward PE method."
- Operator reasoning (as recorded; operator did not elaborate a verbatim reason):

- Phase 3 consequence: Role 1 applies the destination multiple to forward EPS and must
  build the EPS ladder to the year AFTER the exit year (forward-PE-at-exit convention).
  The Hurdle Ratio must hold one consistent EPS basis on both numerator and denominator
  (the SFL discipline), and the forward-basis choice must be applied consistently.

conservative default that would set the entry zone, additive 31.5x). Operator ruled
31.5x.**
