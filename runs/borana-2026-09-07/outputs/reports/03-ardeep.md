### 4C. MD&A deep dive
**Industry claims**: global textile market USD 1,210bn (2026) to USD 1,610bn (2033), 4.2% CAGR (AR p.68); India
textile industry to USD 350bn by 2030 at 10% CAGR (AR p.71) — both externally sourced, unattributed to a named

**Margin explanation — THE central test (Spear Fact 1)**: the FY26 AR's MD&A NEVER explains the 23.6% EBITDA
margin, never uses the words "anti-dumping," "BIS," or "import," and offers no raw-material-cost narrative anywhere
in its "Financial Performance" section (AR pp.73-74) — a striking omission given that TWO MONTHS EARLIER, on the
Q3FY26 (27-Jan-2026) maiden earnings call, CFO Rajkumar Borana had already told investors, in detail, what was
driving the margin: *"there is not actually an anti-dumping duty. They have quoted as a BIS that those companies
who are having BIS certificate in China, they can only export to India. But that was now removed in December. So,
many of the Chinese companies are now offering our raw material. And that is cheaper from the Indian raw
materials"* (Concall_Q3FY26_Jan2026_Transcript, pp.4-5). This is a genuine, evidenced, cost-side (not price-side)
mechanism: a BIS quality-control restriction on Chinese POY/PTY imports was LIFTED in December 2025, letting Borana
buy cheaper Chinese yarn, which is why raw-material imports jumped from 0% to 32.7% of the FY26 raw-material base
(B02, Note 39(c)/(e)) and why FY26 gross margin improved sequentially through the year. **The single-cause
"anti-dumping duty" story named in the run brief does NOT fit the record, confirmed**: this BIS-removal mechanism
is a DECEMBER-2025 event; it cannot explain the run brief's own earlier data point — 27.2% EBITDA margin already in
Q4FY24 (before any of this), falling to 16.5% in Q2FY25 (also before this), before recovering. **No document in
this corpus offers ANY explanation for the Q4FY24-to-Q2FY25 swing** — not the AR, not the RHP, not the FY25 AR's
MD&A (checked, no anti-dumping/duty/raw-material-cost narrative found, Annual_Report_FY2025.pdf, searched in
full). The only raw-material-cost mechanism management has EVER put on the public record (the Jan-2026 concall) is
a FAVOURABLE one that postdates the swing it might be assumed to explain. This is itself a finding: **management
explains margin publicly only when it is improving; the FY24-25 swing has no disclosed explanation anywhere in the
corpus.** NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: any management commentary on the Q4FY24 (27.2%) to Q2FY25 (16.5%) EBITDA margin
compression.
There is also a tone inconsistency within the SAME concall: opening remarks frame "the government recently
