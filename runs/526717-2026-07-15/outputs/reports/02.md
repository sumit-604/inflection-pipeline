## PASS 3: PATTERN-PASS NEW FINDINGS

### 1. Consolidated Cash Flow Statement's own footnote on cash & cash equivalents does not reconcile to Note 12 or to the current year at all — a fourth instance of stale, uncorrected template data
The Consolidated Cash Flow Statement (Annual_Report.pdf, statement page following the primary financials) carries a
footnote: "Cash and Cash equivalents consists of cash on hand INR 1.01 L (1.27 L), Bank Balance INR 23.31 L (121.21 L)
and deposit account INR 47.63 L (14.05 L). Refer Note 12." None of these six figures is the FY25 closing cash and
cash equivalents breakdown per Note 12 (cash in hand ₹1.06 lakh, bank balances ₹5.15 lakh, FDR ₹71.68 lakh, total
₹77.89 lakh, FY25). Instead, the un-bracketed figures (1.01 + 23.31 + 47.63 = ₹71.95 lakh) exactly match the FY24
closing cash balance, and the bracketed figures (1.27 + 121.21 + 14.05 = ₹136.53 lakh) exactly match the FY23 closing
cash balance shown as the FY24 column's opening balance in the same cash flow statement. This footnote is therefore
the prior year's (FY24 annual report's) footnote, carried over unedited into the FY25 filing. 🟡 Watch — combined with
Pass 1's going-concern paragraph citing the wrong year's turnover, the PPE schedule headers captioned "for the year
ended September 30th, 2024" (Note 3 standalone, Note 4(A)/(C)/(D) consolidated), and standalone Note 42 using "the
Group" language throughout, this is now the **fourth independent location** where stale/uncorrected prior-period text
survives into the current filing — a systemic disclosure-hygiene pattern, not an isolated slip, though none of the
four affects the audited primary-statement numbers themselves.

### 2. Negative PPE net book value is confirmed to originate entirely at the subsidiary level, not the parent
Direct comparison of the standalone and consolidated PPE schedules (Annual_Report.pdf, Note 3 standalone vs Note 4(A)
consolidated) shows: standalone "Plant & Machinery" (undivided) net block at 31 March 2025 = ₹1,868.53 lakh
(positive, healthy); the standalone schedule's own Normal/Continuous-Process split shows "Plant & Machinery —
Continuous Process" net block = ₹150.04 lakh (positive) at parent level. The consolidated schedule's identical line
item, "Plant & Machinery — Continuous Process," shows a **net block of ₹(66.42) lakh** (negative) at the same date —
i.e. accumulated depreciation (₹4,980.64 lakh) exceeds gross block (₹4,914.21 lakh) at the consolidated level only.
Since parent-level continuous-process P&M is solidly positive (₹150.04 lakh), the implied subsidiary-only net block
for this category is approximately ₹(216.46) lakh negative — the entire anomaly sits inside K.P. Woven Private
Limited's books, the subsidiary whose financials the principal auditor explicitly relies on another auditor's report
for (Pass 1 §0, consolidated "Other Matter" paragraph, p.120). The FY24 comparative rollforward shows the mechanical
source: a "Transfer from Vehicle to P&M" of ₹3,808.40 lakh (gross) / ₹1,067.23 lakh (accumulated depreciation) moving
value from Continuous Process into Normal Process, an entry that is absent from the standalone (parent-only)
rollforward and therefore also subsidiary-only. 🔴 Red Flag — sharpens Pass 2's finding #8 with the parent/subsidiary
split: this is a subsidiary-level fixed-asset schedule error that a reader working only from the standalone accounts
would never see, and it directly concerns the entity that is 3.2x the parent's revenue but audited by a different
firm.

### 3. Statement of Changes in Equity confirms no restatements or prior-period errors, closing one open question
The Consolidated and Standalone Statements of Changes in Equity both carry a "Change in Equity Share Capital due to
prior period error" column and a "Restated Balance Sheet at the beginning of the current period" row for both FY24
and FY25 — both are nil/blank in every period shown (Annual_Report.pdf, Statement of Changes in Equity, consolidated
and standalone). This is a clean, mechanical confirmation that no restatement has occurred in either year covered by
this filing, closing out the "restatements found" checklist item for Pass 3 with a positive (no-restatement) result
rather than a gap. 🟢 Clean, but recorded here because the pattern-pass mandate specifically asks the question.

### 4. No new post-balance-sheet / subsequent-events disclosure found on re-read
Consistent with Pass 1's "NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT" conclusion, no dedicated subsequent-events note was located in the
pattern re-read of the primary statements or the pages surrounding the auditor's report sign-off (26 May 2025). The
Board's sign-off block (both standalone and consolidated) lists Prakash Parekh (Managing Director), Anil Goyal
(Chairman), Shweta Jhawar (Company Secretary), and Dhrumil Shah (Chief Financial Officer) as at 26 May 2025 with no
accompanying events-after-reporting-date paragraph. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT stands as the final answer on this item.

No further pattern-level contradictions, deliberately-vague disclosures, or going-concern language beyond what Pass 1
already quoted (consolidated Note 3(a) stale turnover figure) were found on this targeted re-read. PASS 3 IS NOT
EMPTY — findings #1 and #2 above are material new items.

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Please explain the ~2,689x increase in standalone Work-in-Progress inventory (₹0.33 lakh to ₹887.50 lakh, FY24 to
   FY25) — is this a genuine order-backlog build ahead of the FY25 revenue surge, a cut-off issue between WIP and
   Finished Goods, or a large year-end order not yet shipped? Why is there no qualitative note anywhere in the filing?
2. What is the basis for the assertion that all related-party transactions are "on terms equivalent to arm's length"
   given the new ₹3,320.29 lakh, 0%-interest loan from Plastene India Limited (a promoter-group shareholder and the
   company's dominant customer) to the subsidiary? Can margin/pricing comparisons against unrelated-party transactions
   be disclosed for the FIBC/woven-sack products sold to and purchased from related parties?
3. Please provide the exact ownership percentage held in K.P. Woven Private Limited, and reconcile the negative net
   book value of ₹(66.42) lakh shown for "Plant & Machinery — Continuous Process" (consolidated Note 4(A)), including
   the mechanics and rationale of the ₹3,808.40 lakh "Transfer from Vehicle to P&M" entry recorded in the FY24
   comparative rollforward.
4. Please provide the components of the deferred tax asset (carried-forward losses vs depreciation timing differences
   vs other items), the effective-vs-statutory tax rate reconciliation, and management's basis for concluding the
   ₹2,573.27 lakh (standalone) / ₹2,418.18 lakh (consolidated) DTA balance is more likely than not to be realised,
   given ₹4,316.58 lakh of accumulated losses remain on the books post-CIRP.
5. Given negative operating cash flow of ₹(581.08) lakh standalone / ₹(1,852.78) lakh consolidated in FY25 despite the
   profit turnaround, and growth funded by ₹3,240.40 lakh of fresh borrowings, what is management's plan and timeline
   to convert reported profit into operating cash — and how does the widening bank drawing-power reconciliation gap
   (up to ₹1,874.61 lakh at the subsidiary in March-25, attributed to exclusion of related-party receivables) factor
   into that plan?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management / profit-favorable accounting choice**: 56% actuarial employee-turnover assumption at the
  parent (unchanged FY24-FY25), materially higher than and structurally inconsistent with the subsidiary's tiered
  15-25% assumption for the identical employee-benefit obligation — mechanically reduces the reported gratuity
  liability and is favorable to reported profit (Note 30.1 p.107, Note 32.1 consolidated p.164).
- **Aggressive/unverifiable related-party accounting**: 0%-interest ₹3,320.29 lakh loan from the dominant related
  customer to the subsidiary, directly contradicting the filing's own arm's-length RPT assertion (Note 33(C)(2)
  consolidated p.170; Note 35.2A/37.2A arm's-length language).
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: bank drawing-power reconciliation gaps (quarterly, worsening, reaching ₹1,874.61
  lakh at the subsidiary in March-25) indicate a material portion of reported trade receivables is not accepted as
  bankable collateral, a fact disclosed only in CARO/Note 19-33 boilerplate rather than surfaced as a standalone
  receivables-quality risk (CARO Annexure A p.68-69; Note 19/33 consolidated p.156-157).
- **Aggressive/unverifiable fixed-asset accounting**: negative net book value in a PPE category (consolidated Note
  4(A), subsidiary-level) traced to a mislabeled large intercategory transfer, unexplained in the notes.
- **Deliberately thin disclosure**: Ind AS 102 ESOP note gives no grant date, exercise price, option count, vesting
  schedule, or valuation-model assumptions despite an active P&L charge and reserve balance (Note 41 standalone,
  Note 43 consolidated); DTA note gives no reconciliation despite being ~23% of standalone total assets; subsidiary
  ownership percentage never stated numerically despite the subsidiary being 3.2x parent revenue.
- **Disclosure regression**: RPT transaction table granularity moved from individually-named counterparties (FY24)
  to aggregated categories (FY25), reducing a reader's ability to track which specific related entity transacted how
  much (Note 35.3 standalone p.108, Note 37.3 consolidated p.171).
