### N3. Forward contract book is far larger than the on-balance-sheet exposure it covers; the sensitivity table leaves it out 🟡

Anchors: Note 29, p.89; Note 30(C), pp.91-92; Note 38, p.95; standalone Notes 28, 29, 36, pp.126-129, 131.

- Forwards are held by the parent. Parent on-balance-sheet foreign exposure (standalone Note 29(C)(i), p.128): USD assets 278.37 less liabilities 218.46 = net 59.91; EUR net 65.31; GBP net 766.47. Forward SELL book: USD 2,097.99 (35.0 times net USD), EUR 575.15 (8.8 times net EUR), GBP nil (FY25 87.98). Total forward notional 2,673.14 against parent net exposure 891.69 across the three currencies (derived).
- So the forwards hedge forecast sales (the note says "firm commitments (sales orders) and highly probable forecast transactions", Note 38), not recognised balances. They are "not designated as hedges" (Note 29, Level 2 table) so the mark to market (liability 129.78, 4.9% of notional, derived; FY25 asset 3.54) runs through P&L while the forecast revenue is booked later at spot. Note 38's wording "has hedged its cash flows" and Note 29's "not designated as hedges" describe the same contracts two ways. No hedge accounting is applied.
- The GBP net exposure 766.47 is unhedged (FY25 forward 87.98 lapsed). Parent GBP is 86% of the parent's net exposure (derived).
- Sensitivity gap: both sensitivity tables are 5% of the gross net balance (standalone USD 3.00 = 5% x 59.91; GBP 38.32 = 5% x 766.47; consolidated EUR 17.99 = 5% x 359.85), before tax and before derivatives, though the heading says "profit after tax". A 5% INR move on the forward book would move the mark to market by about 5% x 2,673.14 = 133.7 (derived), roughly three times the parent's balance sheet sensitivity (5% x 891.69 = 44.6, derived). The disclosed table omits the larger swing. 🟡
- Consolidated sensitivity 179.05 for USD treats US subsidiary balances as P&L exposure. Onward Technologies Inc. carries USD assets of 4,074.12 trade receivables (consolidated) against the parent's 257.74. Translation of those balances goes to OCI (FCTR +470.88, OTI OCI 377.32, Note 36, p.94). The P&L-relevant USD exposure is the parent's. Functional currency of OTI: not stated in the notes; policy 2(d)(ii) says foreign operations translate through OCI.
- FY26 swing: other income FX line went from +79.58 to (20.02), a move of 99.60 (derived); the mark to market alone swung 133.32 (3.54 asset to 129.78 liability, derived). Without the derivative swing the underlying FX line would have been a gain of about 33 (derived).
