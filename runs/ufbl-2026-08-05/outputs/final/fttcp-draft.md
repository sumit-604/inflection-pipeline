## 6. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**What could make revenue keep firing.** The company opened 35 restaurants in FY26, more net additions than the previous two years combined, and plans 40 more in FY27 with 15 already under construction. Separately, the existing restaurants are selling more: same store sales went from negative in FY25 to 28.7% in the June 2026 quarter, and the growth is volume, not price, with dine in transactions up 63.5%. Confirmation would be same store sales holding double digit through Q2 and Q3 FY27 without new discounting. What kills it is the lap: H2 FY27 runs against the strong Q3 and Q4 FY26 base, and management said so itself.

**What could make margins keep expanding.** Management laid out arithmetic rather than ambition. Mature restaurant margin of about 16% moves to 17% to 18%, gross margin recovers 100 to 150 basis points, new store drag settles at 1.5% to 1.8%, and head office cost falls from 7.1% to 6.5% of revenue, together taking margin before lease accounting to 9% to 10% for FY27. The Q1 print delivered 8.1% against 4.6%. Confirmation is gross margin above 66% for two consecutive quarters. What kills it is competition: three peers describe rising intensity and value price wars over exactly this window while UFBL told its investors intensity was unchanged, and peer gross margins expanded while UFBL's compressed.

**What could keep cash healthy.** The model itself is the catalyst. Customers pay immediately, suppliers do not, so growth funds itself as long as new restaurants earn. Management guided net debt flat at about Rs 102 cr through the FY27 expansion, funded from internal accruals. Confirmation is that number holding while restaurant count rises. What kills it is a debt funded rollout, which is the single falsification metric carried from the phase 1 gate.

**What could lift returns on capital.** The FY26 cohort of 35 restaurants was a drag on FY26 and becomes a contributor in FY27 as it matures. Head office cost is being spread over a bigger base. Neither needs new capital. What kills it is the treadmill: 40 more restaurants a year each add a fresh lease liability to capital employed, so the denominator keeps growing, and this is the same pattern that preceded the FY23 store closures.

**Where the evidence is weakest.** Exchange filings for the last year were not collected, so recent documented actions cannot be graded. The most important quarter in this analysis, Q1 FY27, has a results filing and a presentation but no management call, so nobody has cross examined it. The credit rating is nine months old, carries a negative outlook and describes a company that no longer resembles the one printing these numbers.

### THE EARNINGS BASIS QUESTION, YOUR CALL

The multiple has to be applied to an earnings figure and there are two choices. I am not picking this one.

**Trailing.** Apply the multiple to trailing twelve month earnings per share. UFBL's trailing earnings are NEGATIVE, minus Rs 15.17 per share for FY26, so a trailing multiple is arithmetically meaningless today. It becomes usable only once four positive quarters are on the board, which on the current path is somewhere in FY28.
