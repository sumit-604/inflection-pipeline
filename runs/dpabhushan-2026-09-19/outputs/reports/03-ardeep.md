### 2A. Accounting policy aggressiveness — extension

**New finding this stage**: the MD&A's own Key Financial Ratios table (PDF p.77, printed
p.148) labels a row "Return on Net Worth," reporting 9.28 (FY26) / 4.97 (FY25), unit
"Times," +86.60% change. Independently recomputed: **9.28 = PAT ÷ Equity Share Capital**
(₹21,184.04 Lakh ÷ ₹2,282.79 Lakh = 9.28, exact) and **4.97 = same ratio for FY25**
(₹11,269.55 Lakh ÷ ₹2,266.04 Lakh = 4.97, exact) — this is **PAT divided by paid-up share
capital**, not net worth. The correctly-computed Return on (Average) Net Worth — PAT ÷
average of opening and closing Total Equity — is **40.87%** for FY26 (₹21,184.04 Lakh ÷
average of ₹63,261.24 Lakh and ₹40,413.50 Lakh), which ties exactly to the **separate** "ROE
%" figure shown on the Key Performance Indicators infographic (PDF p.11, printed p.17: "ROE
40.87 / 35.06 / 29.47" for FY26/25/24). So the document carries two different figures under
two different labels for what should be the same metric, and the MD&A's Schedule III-styled
"Return on Net Worth" row is mislabeled — it reports a "PAT/paid-up capital" multiple, not
return on net worth. 🟡 This is a genuine filed-document labeling defect (both years tie to
two decimal places on the alternate formula, so it is not a text-extraction artifact), not
an extraction error in this pipeline's reading. It sits alongside finding 11's nuance as a
second example of internal inconsistency between the MD&A and other document sections.

Revenue recognition: per Note 2 (accounting policy, cited by B02) gold received under Gold
Metal Loan and gold-lease arrangements is recognised as inventory with a corresponding
