## WHAT IT IS

IEX runs India's power exchange. It matches buyers and sellers of physical
electricity and takes a transaction fee per unit traded. It owns almost no
operating assets: net block Rs96.68cr against total assets Rs2,435.74cr
(Data_Sheet, FY26). Revenue is a take rate on volume, not a spread and not a
product margin. Group companies: Indian Gas Exchange (IGX, gas), Indian Carbon
Exchange, and Indian Coal Exchange, incorporated 01-Jun-2026
(inputs/announcements/Indian_Coal_Exchange_Incorporation_2026-06-01.pdf).

## THE ONE THING THE THESIS RESTS ON

Market coupling. CERC ordered day-ahead market coupling in July 2025 and named
Grid India the coupling operator. Coupling moves price discovery off IEX and
turns IEX into a bid-collection venue. APTEL declined to set the order aside on
13-Feb-2026. The Supreme Court admitted IEX's appeal on 11-May-2026 and refused
an interim stay on 27-Jul-2026, so CERC may finalise the regulations.
(https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/sc-lets-cerc-proceed-with-market-coupling-rules-rejects-iex-plea-now-126080301173_1.html;
https://solarquarter.com/2026/02/16/aptel-disposes-iex-appeal-allows-cerc-to-proceed-on-market-coupling-framework/)

The offset management argues is mix. Day-ahead market fell from 95% of volume
in FY16 to 39% in FY26 as the real-time market scaled. RTM volume rose 41% to
54.9BU in FY26 and 23% to 16.0BU in Q1FY27. Total Q1FY27 volume was 37.5BU, up
15.9%. Coupling as ordered hits DAM first; management sizes the DAM volume risk
at 20% to 40%.
(https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/bankingfinance/Indian-Energy-Exchange-Posts-Strong-FY26-Revenue-of-indian-rupee747-Crore-Plans-IGX-IPO/6a6c9c3210bce1fd20375262;
https://www.sahi.com/blogs/iex-q1-fy27-results)

Second engine: IGX. IEX filed IGX's DRHP with SEBI on 14-Jul-2026, a pure offer
for sale of 1.671cr shares that cuts IEX's holding from 47.3% to the PNGRB
ceiling of 25%. Listing is targeted for December 2026. IGX earned PAT Rs42cr in
FY26. (inputs/prospectus/IGX_Draft_Abridged_Prospectus_2026-07.pdf;
https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/ipo-corner/story/indian-gas-exchange-files-drhp-for-ipo-iex-to-sell-1-67-crore-shares-via-ofs-543007-2026-07-15)

## PEERS SELECTED (no operator pause; step1 rule C)

Screener classifies IEX under Financial Services, industry "Exchange and Data
Platform". The economic engine is a regulated market-infrastructure platform:
transaction fee per unit of volume, near-zero variable cost, regulator-set fee
caps, network effects, and a structural threat from a regulator changing the
market design. The three peers mirror that engine, not the power sector.

- MCX (https://www.screener.in/company/MCX/consolidated/, 200). Commodity
  derivatives exchange. Same take-rate-on-volume model, same SEBI fee-cap
  exposure, and it survived a regulator-forced technology and product reset.
- BSE (https://www.screener.in/company/BSE/consolidated/, 200). Stock exchange.
  Shows what happens to an exchange's economics when the regulator changes the
  product design, which is exactly the coupling question.
- CDSL (https://www.screener.in/company/CDSL/consolidated/, 200). Depository.
  Regulated market infrastructure with a price-capped per-transaction fee and a
  duopoly structure, the closest analogue to a licensed monopoly platform.

Rejected: PTC India and power generators. They trade or generate electricity and
carry the commodity on their balance sheet. IEX never owns an electron.
