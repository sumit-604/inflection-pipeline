Loan Details 

Loan Details

Consumer Loan (Term Loan): Unsecured term loans (no collateral required), for financing various needs, for salaried as well as self-employed customers. With rate of interest starting from 12.5% up to 30% per annum, term loans available for loan tenure up to 60 months. Customers can avail loans starting from ₹100,000 up to ₹10 Lakhs.

Business Loan (Merchant Cash Advance): Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) is an unsecured loan facility, catering to small businesses owners who require financing to meet their business needs. Smaller than a typical Business term loan, the MCA facility provides financing with loan ticket size starting from ₹1,000. Merchant Cash Advance loan is sanctioned to borrowers, basis details of their transactions or sales volumes.

Note: The Loan products listed here are exclusively offered through the Digital Partners of SMFG India Credit Co. Ltd.

#Foreclosure charges (The below charges are indicative. Detailed break-up of charges will be a part of the Loan agreement):

In case of foreclosure of entire Loan with interest amounts outstanding, following will be the foreclosure charges:

  • 1 to 6 EMI’s fully paid: 6% of principal outstanding on day of pre-payment. 
  • 7 to 17 EMI’s fully paid: 5% of principal outstanding on day of pre-payment. 
  • 18 to 23 EMI’s fully paid: 4% of principal outstanding on day of pre-payment.
  • 24 to 35 EMI’s fully paid: 3% of principal outstanding on day of pre-payment. 
  • 36 or more EMI’s fully paid: no charges will be applicable.
