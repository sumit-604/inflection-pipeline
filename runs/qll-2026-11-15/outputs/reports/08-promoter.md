### 6B Classification: CAUTION
Decisive evidence: no integrity or legal finding anywhere, and the promoters carry personal and corporate guarantees with no pledge; against that, the compliance function churned three times in 16 months, a 342-day late AR, related-party terms set by insiders without independent benchmarks, and repeated narrative-versus-note conflicts in the AR. This is a STRUCTURE concern (CLAUDE.md OR-14 wording), not an INTEGRITY concern. I did not rate it CONCERN because no finding shows deceit, a regulatory penalty on a promoter, or promoter selling. I did not rate it TRUSTWORTHY because the four problems above are recent (FY25 to FY27) and unresolved.

The one observation that separates the two readings (a young company with weak back-office versus insiders using the listed vehicle): independent valuation of the ITCPL swap and the SKM rent, plus collection of the AAL balances and a filed, qualified replacement company secretary.
