## PASS 3 — PATTERN RE-READ

Read one final time for contradictions between notes, numbers not matching the main statements, deliberately thin disclosure next to detailed disclosure elsewhere, restatements, subsequent events, and going-concern language. Two genuine cross-note patterns surface; both are syntheses of facts Pass 1/Pass 2 already anchored individually, not new rupee figures, so they are reported as PATTERN findings rather than added to the numbered new-finding sequence.

**PATTERN A — Working capital is deteriorating on the liability/unbilled side, not the receivables side, and the two components point in different directions if read separately.** Trade receivables (net) grew 30.0% YoY (Rs 1,454.67 Mn to Rs 1,892.13 Mn, Note 10/42, consol p.249/268) — slower than the 35.1% revenue growth, and the >6-month ageing bucket improved (13.5% to 12.3% of gross). Read alone, receivables look fine. But unbilled revenue grew 40.6% (Rs 736.50 Mn to Rs 1,035.48 Mn, Note 8/42/48, consol p.248-249/268/286) and trade payables grew 91% (Rs 314.34 Mn to Rs 600.92 Mn, Note 18/43, consol p.255-256/268-269) — both well ahead of revenue. On a combined receivables-plus-unbilled basis the picture is close to revenue growth (33.6% vs 35.1%, Note 48 credit-risk table), so the deterioration is not visible in any single note; it only appears when Note 8, Note 10, and Note 18 are read together. [INFERENCE, cross-note synthesis] This is the notes-level counterpart to load-bearing fact 2 (FY26 FCF Rs -52 Cr, screener, company memory): a growing share of FY26 revenue was recognised on internal milestone judgment (unbilled) rather than billed and collected, while the company also stretched its own vendors further (payables +91%) to fund that growth. Neither line alone would trigger a flag; together they explain the negative free cash flow despite profitable, growing revenue.

**PATTERN B — The two largest judgment-heavy fair-value items on the balance sheet (combined ~Rs 3,821.91 Mn: goodwill Rs 2,940.46 Mn + contingent-consideration liability Rs 881.45 Mn, Note 4a/17a) both lack the disclosure an investor would expect for items of this size, while smaller items elsewhere in the same note set (employee benefit actuarial sensitivity, Note 45.1; foreign-currency 5% sensitivity, Note 38) are quantified in detail.** Goodwill: no discount rate, growth rate, or headroom stated for the CGU impairment test (Note 2.5a/34). Contingent consideration: no EBITDA-multiple sensitivity, discount rate, or scenario-probability weighting for the Level 3 valuation (Note 47/48). [INFERENCE, cross-note synthesis] The company clearly has the disclosure capability — the gratuity actuarial note runs five separate entities with named discount-rate and salary-growth assumptions and a stated DBO sensitivity band — so the absence on the two acquisition-related fair-value items reads as a gap in this year's specific disclosure choices around the Cryptas/AI Cyberforge deal, not a general disclosure-quality shortfall across the AR. Auditor's sole KAM (revenue recognition) does not cover either item (Note: consol p.199-201, standalone p.291-293).

No contradicting numbers were found between notes and the face financial statements (every cross-tie attempted in Pass 1/Pass 2 reconciled: payables Rs 600.92 Mn ties across Note 18/43/47/48; receivables-plus-unbilled ties across Note 42/48; net debt/equity ties to Note 48(v); EPS ties to Note 32). No prior-year restatement or reclassification language was found anywhere in Note 1-65 or the standalone equivalents (the useful-life change, Note 5a(iii)(a), is a prospective accounting-estimate change under Ind AS 8, correctly not treated as a restatement). No going-concern language, qualification, or emphasis-of-matter paragraph appears in either audit report (consol p.199-201, standalone p.291-293); CARO clause (xix), standalone p.305-306, states nothing has come to the auditor's attention indicating inability to meet liabilities as they fall due over the next twelve months. No events after the balance sheet date beyond the proposed final dividend (Rs 1.25/share, ~Rs 102.55 Mn, Note 65) and the 3i Infotech legal-notice chronology already captured inside Note 36(f) (Pass 1).

- **Possible earnings-management signal, not confirmed**: the useful-life extension on intangibles (cutting FY26 amortisation ~Rs 39.96 Mn) is timed with the acquisition-driven asset build; properly disclosed as an Ind AS 8 estimate change with quantified effect, so this is a WATCH-level timing observation, not an established red flag.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators**: no quantified goodwill impairment-test assumptions; no Level 3 sensitivity for the earn-out liability; no MAT credit disclosure; no lease discount rate %; no customer-concentration disclosure; FX exposure appears run unhedged with no derivative/hedge-accounting note despite meaningful USD/EUR sensitivity (~Rs 31.79 Mn per 5% move).
