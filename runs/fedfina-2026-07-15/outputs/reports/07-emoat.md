### 2C. Growth embedded in "capacity" build — arithmetic

Using AUM-per-branch as the lender analogue to fixed-asset turnover (a traditional capex table was NOT FOUND for this NBFC; branch leasehold buildout is not disclosed in ₹Cr):

- Gold branches under execution, FY26 guidance: 150 new branches (🎙️ CLAIM), of which 113 confirmed opened through Q3 FY26 (📄 DOCUMENTED, Q3 FY26 call, p.3)
- Mature/steady-state productivity proxy: ₹17.7 Cr AUM per gold branch (Q1 FY27 actual, 📄 DOCUMENTED, Inv. Pres. p.20)
- Implied incremental AUM at full maturity = 150 × ₹17.7 Cr = **₹2,655 Cr**
- Current total AUM (Q1 FY27) = ₹21,136 Cr (Inv. Pres. p.6)
- Implied incremental AUM as % of current AUM = 2,655 / 21,136 = **≈12.6%**

🔍 ANALYST INFERENCE caveat: this assumes all 150 branches eventually reach the *current* mature-branch average productivity (₹17.7 Cr), which itself has been rising quarter over quarter, so the 12.6% figure is likely conservative if productivity keeps improving, but it also assumes zero cannibalisation between co-located branches and no macro/gold-price reversal. Ramp lag of 9-12 months per branch (Q2 FY26 call, p.5) means full realisation is a FY27-FY28 event, not immediate. This figure feeds `capex_embedded_growth_pct` below.

### 6D. Combined classification

This is the textbook profile the operator is looking for: a company whose backward-looking quality score is depressed by a specific, named, actively-being-fixed problem, with genuine documented (not merely claimed) evidence of the fix working on schedule.

However, this is graded TURNAROUND rather than the stronger HIGH POTENTIAL/EXCELLENT+ band for three reasons that must be weighed against the operator, not smoothed over:
1. The emerging-moat score (25.3) is at the very bottom of the STRENGTHENING band, not EXPANSION (≥40) — this is a moderate, borderline signal, not a strong one.
2. Half of the six Strong/Moderate categories (R1, H2 in part, H1) are industry-wide or shared-partner tailwinds rather than proprietary FEDFINA advantages — they lower execution risk but do not confer differentiation versus Muthoot/Manappuram/other gold-loan or LAP peers.
3. The single strongest data point (F2) is self-derived from concall cross-referencing in the absence of stage 5's formal documented-action record (B05_PROMISE_DELIVERY was not available for this run) — it should be reconciled against stage 5's Reg-30/exchange-filing record once available, and the classification here should be treated as provisional pending that reconciliation.

The FY27 test is explicit and dated: management has itself deferred the two hardest promises (cost-to-income structural improvement, FY27 credit-cost guidance) to the Q4 FY26 call. If those are delivered on schedule, this moves toward a cleaner TURNAROUND-confirmed or HIGH POTENTIAL read at the next Gate 0 refresh; if deferred again, the AVOID classification should be treated as unchanged and the execution-moat evidence discounted accordingly.
