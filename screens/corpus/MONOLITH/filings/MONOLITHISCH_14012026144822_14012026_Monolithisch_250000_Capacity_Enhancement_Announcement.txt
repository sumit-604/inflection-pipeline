Press Release 
 
 
Capacity Enhancement at Monolithisch India Limited 
 
Monolithisch India Limited extends its warm greetings and best wishes to all its stakeholders 
on the auspicious occasions of Makar Sankranti, Bihu, Pongal and Lohri. May these festivals 
bring prosperity, growth, and happiness to everyone. 
 
Further to the above, the Company is pleased to inform that, as part of its ongoing strategic 
capacity expansion program, trial runs will be carried out with effect from 14th January, 
2026. 
 
Pursuant to the successful completion of the trial runs, the Company expects that the new 
installed manufacturing capacity of 250,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum) shall become 
operational with effect from 21st January, 2026. 
 
This development is in continuation of the Company’s previously disclosed phased capacity 
expansion initiatives and reflects sustained operational improvements, efficient execution, and 
adoption of advanced manufacturing technologies. The enhanced capacity will enable the 
Company to meet growing demand for its existing products range as well as its premium offering, 
SGB-Limited, while ensuring timely deliveries and consistent quality across domestic and 
international markets. 
 
This expansion will support the Company’s objective of achieving a net installed group capacity 
of 574,000 TPA by April/ May 2026, thereby strengthening its leadership position in the 
unshaped refractory materials segment and making the Company largest manufacturer in terms 
of installed capacity. 
 
The Company remains committed to sustainability, operational excellence, and customer-centric 
growth, supported by continuous investments in infrastructure, technology, and skilled 
manpower.
