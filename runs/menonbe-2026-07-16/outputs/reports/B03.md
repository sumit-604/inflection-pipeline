### One discrepancy found (not in B02's original list, surfaced by this pass's direct P&L re-derivation)

B02 Finding #2 states standalone "PAT grew 64.4%." Direct re-derivation from the Standalone Statement of
Profit & Loss (AR p.98) gives: PAT (before OCI) ₹3,262.93L (FY26) vs ₹2,015.73L (FY25) = **+61.9%**, not
64.4%. The 64.4% figure appears to arise from dividing FY26 PAT (₹3,262.93L) by FY25's **Total
Comprehensive Income** (₹1,985.45L, which nets out an OCI actuarial loss) rather than FY25's PAT proper
(₹2,015.73L): 3,262.93/1,985.45 = 1.644 = +64.4% exactly. This is a minor, immaterial-to-thesis
denominator mismatch in the prior stage's calculation, not a source-document error — the underlying
standalone financial statements are internally consistent; only the growth-rate arithmetic in B02 needs
correction. Recorded here as the one required discrepancy for the YAML block.

**STRUCTURAL FINDING (new to this stage, material for interpreting every backward comparison in this
report):** The FY2021-22 AR states explicitly, "The Company does not have any subsidiary, associate or joint
venture company" and "During the financial year 2021-22 the Company had no subsidiary"
(`_txt/annual-report__Annual_Report_2023.txt`, line 4939, p.90ish), while the same-era loan security note
refers to "Bearing and Alkop division" as a single undivided charge base (line 7129-7130) — confirming
Alkop (aluminium die-casting) was a **division of the standalone company**, not a subsidiary, in FY22. Menon
Alkop Limited was only **incorporated on 23 January 2024** (Board's Report §15.13, AR p.83) as a new legal
entity into which the aluminium division was hived off, becoming a SEBI-designated "material subsidiary"
once it crossed the 10% consolidated-turnover threshold. This means the FY22→FY26 standalone "backward
span" is NOT a like-for-like comparison of the same business: post hive-off, the standalone entity is a
bearings-only company (the higher-margin ~72% of the group), while the FY22 standalone entity contained
both bearings AND aluminium die-casting. The Board's Report explicitly attributes the FY26 Return on Net
Worth jump (16.43%→22.25%, +35.37%) to "separation of Aluminium Division (Alkop) from Menon Bearings
Limited" (Board's Report Item 14, AR p.60) — a rare instance of management directly naming a structural,
non-operational driver of a headline ratio improvement, which this stage credits as candid but which also
means the standalone-entity margin trend cannot be read as pure organic improvement. This also
explains why the ₹29cr corporate guarantee exists (funding the newly separated, capital-intensive Alkop
entity's growth) and is the most plausible single explanation for the divergence between "standalone
receivables/cash stress" and "consolidated ageing stable" in Findings #1/#7 above: the entities' working
capital cycles are now genuinely distinct.
