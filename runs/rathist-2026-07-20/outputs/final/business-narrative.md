Rathi Steel restarts its mill without margin

Rathi Steel & Power is a Ghaziabad based secondary steel maker in the NCR that melts scrap into carbon TMT rebar and stainless steel billets, wire rods, bright bars and flats. It sells through a North India dealer network of about 300 dealers and direct B2B customers. The company reports a single segment, so no split between TMT and stainless is disclosed anywhere. The stock trades at Rs 23.8 for a market cap of about Rs 206 Cr.

Net worth went from negative Rs 100 Cr in FY23 to positive Rs 137 Cr in FY25. That repair came from a lender one time settlement with Bank of Baroda and ACRE in February 2024, term loan waivers, and a Rs 114.71 Cr preferential equity raise placed with allottees outside the promoter group. The idle TMT bar mill restarted in Q1 FY26 and rolled tonnage roughly doubled to about 102,000 MT. Cost of debt fell from 18% to 16%. The promoter family left the board and professional managers now run the company, with Mahesh Pareek as MD.

Gate 0 lands at AVOID. Core score is 24 out of 100, moat score is 4 out of 60 after verifier correction, grand total 28 out of 160, one moat confirmed, THIN. The score carries the history: net worth was negative from FY17 through FY23, FY20 revenue collapsed to Rs 37.53 Cr in a plant shutdown year, and free cash flow was negative in both years that could be computed, negative Rs 33.66 Cr in FY25 and negative Rs 24.97 Cr in FY26. The forward emerging moat scan lands at 9.9 out of about 80, which is NONE.

Cash conversion is the live problem. Operating cash flow swung from positive Rs 24.15 Cr in FY24 to negative Rs 11.06 Cr in FY25 while PAT stayed positive at Rs 13.95 Cr. The swing was driven by finished goods inventory rising 145.9% and receivables rising 52.3%, both against flat revenue growth of 2%, not against growth. The cash pile fell 80%. FY26 operating cash flow improved to negative Rs 1.32 Cr but stayed below zero. The inventory build was verified only on an estimation basis and no rating rationale exists to test the working capital story. Two audited filings disagree on FY25 borrowings, Rs 37.74 Cr in the annual report against Rs 0.00 Cr in the Q4 comparative, with no reconciling note.

Credibility grades C. Management delivered the debt cost cut and the GreenPro certification on their stated dates. It missed on margin, on capex timing, and on disclosure. EBITDA margin held flat near 4% across three straight quarters against three straight promises of utilization led improvement, and slipped to 4.03% in FY26 from 4.81% in FY25 even as revenue grew 41.7%. The TMT direct charging capex slipped past its own stated window. Utilization figures do not reconcile, 60 to 65% quoted in Q3 FY26 against a 51 to 53% FY26 average given in Q4. Management deflected every question on a sustainable margin band, on the product split, and on any green premium. In Q2 FY26 the production numbers dropped out of the disclosure pack and management declined to give them on the call. Green power was more than 25% of consumption in FY26, not the roughly 30% sometimes cited, which was the TMT share of revenue.

Peers complicate the story rather than confirm it. The two peer documents are VRAJ Iron & Steel, read through a CARE rating rationale, and Scan Steels, read through an investor presentation, neither a concall. Scan Steels directly contradicts Rathi's claim to be India's first and only hot charging operator, listing hot charging as an already completed cost lever. VRAJ shows utilization climbing monotonically from 48% to 67% with no reversal, against Rathi's inconsistent figures, and VRAJ runs margins two to three times Rathi's flat 4%. Both peers are running larger expansion programs funded through a mix of equity, bank term debt and internal accruals, while Rathi's capex is smaller, internal accrual funded, and already delayed.

Runway class is GOOD on the market alone. TAM sits near Rs 150,000 Cr on the conservative read, SAM near Rs 67,000 Cr, and Rathi holds about 1.07% of its SAM, leaving 93.6x revenue headroom. Management's 20 to 25% CAGR and double the turnover in five years implies FY31 revenue near Rs 1,975 Cr. That exceeds the plant's ceiling at 100% nameplate rolling utilization, about Rs 1,391 Cr, by 42%, against NIL contractually committed capex in the annual report. The claim reads inflated at a ratio of 2.4.

The recorded deal breaker sits on the promoters. Pradeep Rathi and Udit Rathi carry a 2016 CBI Special Court conviction for cheating and criminal conspiracy in the Kesla North coal block allocation, with three year sentences, an appeal to the Delhi High Court unresolved, and the sentence suspended on bail. Weighed against that: the family is off the board, the balance sheet turnaround came through a negotiated lender settlement rather than an imposed resolution, the ED PMLA complaint on the same matter was declined cognizance in March 2026, there is no pledge, and the personal guarantees stay in place. On the other side sit related party disclosure gaps around promoter controlled entities and five leadership departures inside nine months.
