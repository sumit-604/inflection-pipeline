### 1D History before the current company

Fabtech Technologies Limited did not originate as an independent company. It began as a shell
("Fabtech Technologies Private Limited," incorporated 2018) whose original, unrelated incorporators —
Sanjay Tulshiram Dhumal and Manisha Sanjay Dhumal — held all 10,000 shares and transferred them for a
nominal ₹10 each to Fabtech Technologies International Private Limited (FTIPL) and Aasif Ahsan Khan
(as FTIPL's nominee) on March 24, 2020. [✅ VERIFIED, p.289] Control then passed from FTIPL to FTIPL's
own shareholders — the four current promoters — via an NCLT-approved Scheme of Arrangement (order
dated November 19, 2020) that demerged FTIPL's export division into this company. [✅ VERIFIED, p.289]

This means: (i) the company is an engineered demerger vehicle, not an organically founded business;
(ii) Aasif Ahsan Khan's formal promoter/shareholder association with THIS company dates only to July
9, 2021, despite prospectus language describing "over three decades of experience" — that broader
biography relates to his career, not tenure at this specific entity; (iii) Aarif Ahsan Khan has been
associated with the wider Fabtech group (FTIPL) since August 2001 in a hands-on manufacturing role.
No succession dispute, no founder-vs-inheritor conflict, and no unexplained career gaps were found in
the disclosed record. [✅ VERIFIED, p.287-289]

### 2B Criminal cases
