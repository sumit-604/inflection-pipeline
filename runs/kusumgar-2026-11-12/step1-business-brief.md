# STEP 1 BUSINESS BRIEF: Kusumgar Ltd (KUSUMGAR)

Run date 2026-11-12. Screener URL: https://www.screener.in/company/KUSUMGAR/consolidated/ (verified OK by tools/check_url.py). NSE KUSUMGAR, BSE 544821. This brief is COMPANY MEMORY from live web. It is never anchored evidence. Every stage number comes from the run's filings.

## The business (web read, 2026-11-12)

1. Kusumgar makes woven, coated and laminated synthetic fabrics (nylon 6 and 66, polyester, polyurethane chemistry). It was incorporated in 1990. It listed on 15 July 2026 through a Rs 650 Cr IPO that was 100% offer for sale, so the company received no IPO cash. (https://www.screener.in/company/KUSUMGAR/consolidated/ ; https://www.financialexpress.com/market/stock-insights/beyond-uniforms-2-textile-stocks-tapping-indias-mission-critical-defence-fabrics/4344954/ ; https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/kusumgar-ipo-review)
2. Four segments. Aerospace and Defence Fabrics plus Aerospace and Defence Solutions are about 55% of FY26 revenue. Industrial and Automotive Fabrics are about 24%. Outdoor and Lifestyle Fabrics are about 19%. Other sales are about 2%. (https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_kusumgar_iponote_jul26.pdf)
3. Revenue: Rs 302 Cr FY23, Rs 468 Cr FY24, Rs 779 Cr FY25, Rs 692 Cr FY26 (down 11%). PAT: Rs 37 Cr, Rs 84 Cr, Rs 112 Cr, Rs 98 Cr. EBITDA margin: 22%, 28%, 24%, 27%. (screener URL above)
4. The margin comes from specification. Parachute fabric and parachute systems (cargo, tactical, drogue, recovery, the Gaganyaan re-entry module) carry long qualification cycles. Management says margins move with product mix, operating leverage and raw material prices. (ICICI Direct note above; https://www.arthneeti.com/insights/kusumgar-q2-fy27-earnings)
5. Customer profile: top 6 customers were 49.35% of FY26 revenue (about Rs 333 Cr), top 10 about 60%. One Indian government customer anchors the defence fabric line. Exports were 40.0% of FY26 revenue, up from 23.2% in FY25. (ICICI Direct note above)
6. Capacity: utilisation at the processing and coating factories fell from 94.3% in FY24 to 42.3% in FY25 and 49.5% in FY26, after new capacity came on line. Fixed assets rose from Rs 98 Cr (Mar 2023) to Rs 307 Cr (Mar 2026). (ICICI Direct note; screener URL)
7. Latest print, Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026): revenue Rs 250.9 Cr, up 81% on year. EBITDA Rs 71.3 Cr at 28.4%. PAT Rs 40.6 Cr, up 272%. H1 FY27 revenue Rs 498.2 Cr, up 91%. Growth came from execution of aerospace and defence solutions contracts. (https://www.arthneeti.com/insights/kusumgar-q2-fy27-earnings ; https://blog.perivis.com/2026/11/05/kusumgar-q2-fy27-earnings-and-board-highlights/)
8. Guidance: no margin guidance and no order book number (customer confidentiality). Management asks investors to read annual trends because military tender timing moves quarters. At the IPO it said revenue should exceed Rs 2,000 Cr by FY30 with no major capex for 4 to 5 years. H1 FY27 capex was Rs 44.1 Cr. (arthneeti URL above; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lBdNQTYPp3E)
9. Cash: screener shows debtor days at 26 (FY25) and 123 (FY26). Cash from operations was Rs 201 Cr in FY24, minus Rs 155 Cr in FY25 and Rs 28 Cr in FY26. Other liabilities fell from Rs 326 Cr (Mar 2024) to Rs 72 Cr (Mar 2025). Borrowings were Rs 323 Cr at Sep 2026. (screener URL above)
10. New item: on 5 Nov 2026 the board approved a non-binding term sheet for a 50.1:49.9 India joint venture with Certoplast for tape manufacturing. (https://blog.perivis.com/2026/11/05/kusumgar-q2-fy27-earnings-and-board-highlights/)
11. Price context on 11 Nov 2026: Rs 560, market cap Rs 5,883 Cr, P/E 46. Promoters hold 75.01%, FIIs 1.24%, DIIs 13.18% (Sep 2026). (screener URL above)

## The one engine

Fill of the new coating and processing capacity (about 50% utilised) by aerospace and defence solutions contracts, that is, a mix shift from fabric to parachute and camouflage systems. H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 498 Cr against Rs 692 Cr for all of FY26 is the first evidence. The open question is whether this is a durable step or one lumpy tender cycle.

## Peers (selected by economic engine, not the textile basket)

- GARFIBRES, Garware Technical Fibres: https://www.screener.in/company/GARFIBRES/consolidated/ (OK). Specified technical textiles with qualification cycles, export led, 20% plus margins. The closest listed comp and the first peer named in the offer document.
- ARVIND, Arvind Ltd: https://www.screener.in/company/ARVIND/consolidated/ (OK). Its Advanced Materials division sells protective, defence and industrial engineered fabrics. Only that division mirrors the engine; the rest is apparel textiles. Named as a peer in the offer document.
- MAYURUNIQ, Mayur Uniquoters: https://www.screener.in/company/MAYURUNIQ/consolidated/ (OK). A coated fabric maker with automotive OEM qualification cycles and exports. It mirrors the Industrial and Automotive coated fabric line.
- No listed Indian parachute or aerial delivery systems pure play exists. The defence solutions line has no direct listed comp. SRF (the third offer document peer) was rejected: technical textiles are a small part of a fluorochemicals group.

1. Cash conversion. Debtor days 26 to 123 and cash from operations of minus Rs 155 Cr (FY25) and Rs 28 Cr (FY26) against PAT of Rs 112 Cr and Rs 98 Cr. Verify against the prospectus restated cash flows and the H1 FY27 balance sheet. Find what the Rs 326 Cr of other liabilities at Mar 2024 was (customer advances or not) and why it unwound.
2. Segment mix and its lumpiness. Verify the 55 / 24 / 19 / 2 split, the FY26 revenue fall of 11%, and how much of H1 FY27 revenue came from aerospace and defence solutions contracts. Verify the single Indian government customer share.
3. Capacity fill. Verify utilisation of 94.3%, 42.3% and 49.5% and the installed capacity behind it. Pair it with the IPO statement of Rs 2,000 Cr revenue by FY30 with no major capex (guidance against delivery).
4. Concentration and governance. Top 6 customers at 49.35%, exports at 40%. The IPO was 100% offer for sale: verify who sold, any related-party items, and borrowings of about Rs 300 Cr that the IPO did not repay.
