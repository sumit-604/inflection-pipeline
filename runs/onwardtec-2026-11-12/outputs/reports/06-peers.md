It does not match a claim of rate-led margin gain.

2C Capex cycle. Not a capacity race. Peers invest in AI platforms and centres, not seat capacity: LTTS EI centre in Munich and an NVIDIA lab (Q2 p.4; Q1 p.4), Tata Tech a new Pune office with higher D&A (Q2 p.11), Cyient acquisitions (TAO, USD 218 mn deal value, Jun call p.8) and semiconductors. Onward's Rs 33 Cr ODC and about Rs 25 Cr labs (B05) are small, mid-cycle expansion in a cautious-to-mixed peer set; it is closer to a lone organic expander than to an industry race, though Tata Tech also adds headcount.

2D Competitive mentions. No peer mentions Onward Technologies by name or by unmistakable description (keyword search, nothing found). Tata Tech says it seldom meets Indian ESPs outside India (Harris, Q2 p.27-28), so Onward is not a rival there.

## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY

- Claims verified: 2 of 11 (Q7 DSO, Q10 wage and attrition). Partially verified: 5 (Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6). Unverifiable: 3 (Q3, Q8, Q9).
- Claims contradicted: 1 (Q11 "fastest organic growth"; weak, current-period only). It goes to synthesis as an item to drop or re-source, not as a hard contradiction.
- Most consequential contradiction: Tata Technologies grew +22.1% YoY cc ex ES-Tec in Q2 FY27 (Gujrati, p.9), above Onward's H1 +17.5%. The "fastest growth" line cannot stand unqualified. The harder tension is indirect: no peer links margin to U.S. policy or offshore share, and Cyient's trouble with 15% EBIT shows margin climbs slip when growth lags.
- Strongest independent confirmation: wage cost. Tata Tech (100 bps, Q2 p.10) and LTTS (50-80 bps, Q2 p.18) both price increments at about 0.5-1.0 point of margin, and attrition runs 14.7-15.6%. Data-centre and power engineering pull is also confirmed at one peer (LTTS), with a named business line.
- Overall: the peer set complicates the Onward narrative. It supports the demand theme in industrial/energy engineering and the 14% EBITDA level as plausible. It does not support the offshore-policy story, gives no check on the R&D credit or concentration, and shows peers either holding margin with larger scale (LTTS, Tata Tech) or missing margin goals when growth stalls (Cyient). The bear reading: Onward's H2 margin guide must absorb Q3 increments that cost peers 50-100+ bps. The bull reading: the best peers held margin through the same increment cycle while growing, and Onward's DSO is within the peer range.

## PART 5: CROSS-PEER HYPOTHESIS

cross_peer_hypothesis: "All three peers are moving contract structure away from pure T&M toward fixed-price, outcome-based or turnkey work (LTTS fixed price 35.6%, Q2 FY27 p.9, large deals on fixed-price or outcome models, Q1 p.13; Tata Tech turnkey full-vehicle ownership, Q2 p.4-5, p.26; Cyient outcome-based models, TAO call p.5), and none states that this protects margin from AI-driven productivity gains. LTTS alone admits it passes on productivity to win bids (Q1 p.15). Inference: the vendors that hold margin through wage and AI pressure are those that own outcomes and keep the productivity saving, while T&M vendors hand it to the client at renewal. If true, an 84-85% T&M vendor like Onward should show rate or hours concessions at the next large renewals, and its ex-credit margin should lag the fixed-price peers. Test: Onward's T&M share and any rate concession disclosed in the Q3 and Q4 FY27 calls, against LTTS fixed-price mix rising above 36%."
