Proptech rebuild reaches its first profit

Aurum Proptech is a proptech platform running three segments: Rental, which manages NestAway lettings and HelloWorld co living beds; Distribution, which sells Aurum Analytica developer leads, the Sell.do CRM and PropTiger primary brokerage; and Capital, a nascent SM-REIT and asset management arm. FY26 total income was ₹424 Cr, up 49% YoY, revenue from operations was ₹381 Cr, and the company posted its first full year profit of ₹1.9 Cr. ARR crossed ₹500 Cr and management guides to ₹1,000 Cr in three years at an 8 to 10% PAT margin. Market cap sits at ₹1,726 Cr.

The whole business is a rebuild. Selling the Majesco US software arm collapsed sales from ₹988 Cr in FY19 to ₹10 Cr in FY20, and everything since is a proptech company grown from that shell to ₹381 Cr by FY26. FY26 is the inflection: two consecutive PAT positive quarters in Q3 and Q4, adjusted EBITDA margin swinging from minus 3.4% to plus 5.9%, and Distribution reaching roughly 60% of income by the March quarter as the profit engine.

Two events anchor the transition. REA India, the News Corp backed group, took a 5.5% strategic stake via a share swap for the PropTiger acquisition in mid 2025. And the company sold its non core Q5/Q6 buildings for ₹112 Cr, about 15% above the valuer estimate, repaid the LRD facility and declared itself debt free in May 2026.

Gate 0 scores this AVOID: 58 out of 160 on the grand card, core 36 out of 100. The score is backward looking and FY22 through FY25 were all loss years, so median ROCE is negative and PAT was negative in FY24 and FY25. The moat block is separately STRONG, 5 of 12 confirmed on pricing power, switching costs, negative working capital and growth, and the forward emerging moat scan reads STRENGTHENING.

Cash is the unresolved question. CFO grew to ₹62.9 Cr in FY26 and free cash flow swung positive at ₹47 Cr, but the FY25 receivables ageing tail worsened badly, with the one to two year bucket up 260% and the two to three year bucket up 1,233% YoY while headline receivables tracked revenue and hid it. FY25 lease cash service of ₹70.5 Cr ran about 2.5 times CFO of ₹27.7 Cr, funded from financing not operations. The maiden Q3 profit rode ₹9.72 Cr of other income from a HelloWorld lease liability reversal and was an operating loss without it, and the Q4 ₹17.72 Cr building gain sat outside reported profit. No credit rating rationale was collected this run.

Management credibility grades B. It delivered adjusted EBITDA positive, PBT and PAT positive, and ₹500 Cr ARR each on or ahead of the stated timeline. Against that, the SM-REIT launch has slipped in all four calls since the July 2025 license, the ecosystem cross sell revenue it calls its own moat was admitted "still single digit" after a year untracked, and a Q3 claim that NestAway rationalisation was "completed" was contradicted by houses still down 23% YoY in Q4.

Peers do not fully corroborate the story. No pure Indian proptech peer exists, so the four analogues (CARTRADE, RATEGAIN, ZAGGLE, NAZARA) leave five of nine claims unverifiable. Two peers actively contradict the AI as a moat claim: RateGain and Zaggle back their AI positioning with quantified product metrics while Aurum rests on a single early pilot, and Zaggle discusses cash conversion openly every quarter where Aurum never raises it.

The runway is genuinely large. Independent sizing puts TAM near ₹54,000 Cr and SAM at ₹32,900 Cr against a current 1.29% SAM share, a STRONG runway class with 77.6 times revenue headroom. The ₹1,000 Cr management target is conservative against an independent three year SOM build, a claim ratio of 0.60, which is an unusual case of management under claiming rather than over claiming.

The governance file is heavy. Auditor MSKA & Associates withdrew consent five months after a second term reappointment with no reason disclosed, shareholders were asked to approve a ₹50 Cr omnibus authorisation for the company to lend to promoter entity AREDL, a confirmed statutory default sits at subsidiary Integrow inside a seven entity CARO exception year, and goodwill is 61% of consolidated net worth concentrated 80.5% in two negative net worth subsidiaries. A first year auditor covered only 56% of consolidated assets directly, and a rights issue proceeds parking deviation was flagged by CARE and rectified.

Set against that, the promoter signals are clean. The 5.5% dilution reconciles to new shares issued to REA for PropTiger, not to selling; a co promoter bought 25,000 shares on the open market in March 2026; the promoter honoured the full rights issue including standing for unsubscribed shares; and pledge reads 0% on secondary sources. A July 16 2026 board meeting will consider an equity fund raise from the now debt free base, likely growth capital, with dilution and use of proceeds still to be seen.
