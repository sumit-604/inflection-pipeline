Fabtech Technologies Limited Secures ₹31.23 Crore Veterinary  
Vaccine Facility Project in Botswana 
Mumbai, June 15, 202 6 – Fabtech Technologies Limited (FTL), a global leader in pharmaceutical and life 
sciences infrastructure, has secured a ₹31.23 crore contract for the critical internal infrastructure scope of a 
new Veterinary Vaccine Manufacturing Facility in Botswana.  The project further strengthens Fabtech’s 
growing presence across Africa’s healthcare, biotechnology, and vaccine manufacturing sectors. 
Advancing Africa's Journey Towards Vaccine Self-Sufficiency 
The facility will play a key role in enhancing Southern Africa’s veterinary vaccine manufacturing capacity, 
supporting regional efforts in animal health, food security, and disease prevention. As African nations 
increasingly invest in local vaccine production, the project represents a significant step toward reducing 
import dependence and strengthening regional healthcare resilience. 
Under the project scope, Fabtech will deliver advanced GMP-compliant internal infrastructure, precision HVAC 
systems, clean utilities, and critical MEP services engineered to international manufacturing standards.  
Fabtech will provide an integrated infrastructure platform designed for safe, efficient, and  scalable vaccine 
production. 
Beyond veterinary healthcare, the project strengthens Fabtech’s position within Africa’s emerging vaccine and 
biologics manufacturing ecosystem. The infrastructure being deployed today lays the foundation for broader 
capabilities in critical vaccines, biological products, and sterile injectable manufacturing across the continent, 
supporting Africa’s long-term ambition of healthcare and vaccine self-sufficiency. 
This milestone further reinforces Fabtech’s vision of becoming a leading Life Sciences Infrastructure Platform, 
enabling world-class pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and vaccine manufacturing across emerging markets.
