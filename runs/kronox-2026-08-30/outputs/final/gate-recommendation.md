The inventory build could be forward stocking or a genuine drag; the evidence cannot decide.

Missing evidence to close the flag: management explanation of the inventory and receivables build, the FY27 working capital trajectory, and the Note 1 accounting policies from the filed exchange copy.

Rating agency quote: none available. The company states it has not obtained any credit ratings (AR FY26).
