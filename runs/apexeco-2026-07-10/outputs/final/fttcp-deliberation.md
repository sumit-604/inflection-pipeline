# FTTCP Deliberation — Apex Ecotech Ltd (APEXECO)

Run date 2026-07-10. Operator: Keerti Kaushik. Operator rulings applied and final.

## The story in plain words

Apex Ecotech builds water treatment plants to order. It takes a contract, buys the equipment,
assembles the plant, and hands it over. About 96 paise of every rupee comes from these projects.
Only 4 paise comes from running and maintaining plants afterward.

The company is small. Sales were about 149 crore in FY26. The market value is about 319 crore.
It listed on the SME board in December 2024. It carries almost no debt and holds about 35 crore of cash.

The last year was a big one. Sales more than doubled. Profit almost doubled. One order from Reliance
did most of the heavy lifting, roughly 70 percent of the second half.

The good part of the story is growth and high returns on capital. The weak part is cash and margins.
Profit looks strong on paper, but the cash behind it has been slow to arrive, because a growing
project business ties money up in unfinished work and money held back by customers until a plant is
signed off. Margins have drifted down three years running as raw material got dearer.

So this is a fast growing, high return, asset light business that has not yet proven it can turn its
profit into cash as it scales. FTTCP exists to ask, for each of the four engines, is it firing forward,
and is there real evidence for it. Here is the answer, engine by engine.

## Step 0. Setup (operator confirmed)

- New company, first look. No prior file.
- Forward window: 6 months to the next print, which is H1 FY27 around November 2026. 12 months for ROCE.
  The company reports twice a year, so there is no 3 month check.
- Business type: standard operating business, asset light water treatment EPC. High return bar is 20 percent.
- Sources: latest concall, results, presentation and four peers are all present. One required source is

## Step 1 and Step 2. The four engines

Numbers to FY26 are actual, from the screener sheet and the annual report. Numbers FY27 to FY31 are
expected and illustrative, built from management guidance and the catalyst evidence, not facts.

### Engine 1. Revenue growth

| Year | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | FY27e | FY28e | FY29e | FY30e | FY31e |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales (INR Cr) | 19.5 | 34.6 | 53.1 | 71.0 | 148.7 | ~200 | ~250 | ~310 | ~375 | ~450 |
| Growth % | +67 | +77 | +53 | +34 | +110 | ~34 | ~25 | ~24 | ~21 | ~20 |

Plain line: growth is real and fast, and the order book plus the Reliance tail cover the next year, but
growth slows from here and depends on winning fresh orders, since new orders have been coming in slower
than old ones ship. Decision: FIRING, confidence Moderate.

### Engine 2. Margins

| Year | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | FY27e | FY28e | FY29e | FY30e | FY31e |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA margin % | neg | 12.4 | 16.8 | 15.7 | 14.6 | ~14.5 | ~14.5 | ~15 | ~15 | ~15 |

Plain line: margins have slipped three years in a row as material costs rose to about 76 percent of
sales, the company has shown no firm pricing action, and peers saw only a small cost bump, so the best
honest call is flat in the mid teens with no expansion assumed. Decision: STAGNANT, confidence Low to moderate.

### Engine 3. Cash conversion

| Year | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | FY27e | FY28e | FY29e | FY30e | FY31e |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash from operations / profit | n/m | 0.74 | 1.01 | neg | 0.40 | ~0.5 | ~0.6 | ~0.65 | ~0.7 | ~0.7 |
| Receivable days | 65 | 74 | 60 | 114 | 41 | ~45 | ~45 | ~45 | ~45 | ~45 |

Plain line: cash conversion fell apart in FY25 and only part recovered in FY26, but collection days
already dropped back to 41, and the money still tied up is mostly retentions and unfinished work that
release when projects finish, so the strain reads as caused by fast growth, not a broken business.
Operator ruling: GROWTH-INDUCED. It is allowed to proceed. The test that would prove us wrong is below.
Decision on the engine: STARTING, confidence Moderate.

### Engine 4. Return on capital (ROCE)

| Year | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | FY27e | FY28e | FY29e | FY30e | FY31e |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ROCE % (reported) | neg | ~56 | ~60 | ~25 | ~35 | ~32 | ~31 | ~31 | ~30 | ~30 |

Plain line: returns look like they crashed, but the FY23 and FY24 highs were on a tiny pre listing
capital base, the FY25 dip was just the IPO cash sitting in the bank, and FY26 at about 35 percent is a
genuine premium return earned with almost no fixed assets. Context only: strip out the roughly 35 crore
of idle cash and the underlying operating return is far higher, near 77 percent. Operator ruling: treat
this as staying high, not climbing further. Decision: FIRING as in staying at a premium, confidence
Moderate to high, with no extra credit taken for future re-rating.

Verdict: **BUY-candidate.** Standard conviction, small to medium size if the later gates clear.

Honest caveat: two of the four scores are low confidence, margin and cash. If both disappoint at the
H1 FY27 print, the score falls to +2, which is DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID. If both hold, the +5 stands.
The whole call turns on one print in about November 2026.

The hard AVOID cap does not apply. No engine is declining with no fix available. The cash engine, the
usual trap for this kind of company, has a clear reason to improve and already shows collection days
falling.

## Step 5. What to watch (next 6 to 12 months)

| Watch this | The line that matters | When |
|---|---|---|
| Cash vs profit | rises toward 0.7, does not fall below it | H1 FY27 |
| Working capital days | fall as growth cools | H1 FY27 |
| Any bad debt write off | none taken | FY27 |
| EBITDA margin | holds at or above 14.6 percent | H1 FY27 |
| Order book and new wins | book to bill back above 1.0, book above 150 crore | H1 FY27 |
| Customer spread | top customer share disclosed and falling | FY27 |
| ROCE | stays above 25 percent | FY27 |
| Cash credit facility | terms disclosed and matched to the borrowing note | FY26 report |

The falsification trigger for the growth-induced cash call: if H1 FY27 cash from operations to profit
prints below 0.7 while working capital days climb faster than sales, the growth story is wrong and the
cash strain is structural. That single result flips the cash engine and drops the position to DEEP WATCH.

## Handoff to valuation (phase 3)

- ROCE engine verdict: FIRING, sustain. Pillar 1 uses the reported ROCE of 33.39 percent, which the
  formula lifts to the 24x cap.
- ROCE recovery is credited in Pillar 1 only. No separate re-rating premium is taken. Per operator, no
  further multiple expansion is underwritten from ROCE.
- Context only, not used in Pillar 1: the ex cash operating ROCE is near 77 percent, so the reported
  figure understates the true operating return; recorded here for phase 3 awareness.
- Shared point of failure: a working capital unwind would help both the cash engine and ROCE, so the
  devil's advocate must stress test that single lever.
