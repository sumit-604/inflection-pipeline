## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Zelio assembles low-speed
electric scooters in Haryana and sells them through 400+ dealers. Low-speed
means no licence, no registration, no mandatory insurance, priced Rs 50,000 to
Rs 75,000 (Concall May 2026, page 4). E-2Ws were 96.53% of FY25 revenue
(Investor Presentation, page 17). FY26 volume was 70,000+ units on Rs 310.71
crore revenue, about Rs 44,000 a unit (Concall May 2026, pages 4 and 7). The
engine is dealer volume on a gross margin near 23%.

**What is changing?** The company calls itself a "CKD assembler today" on
71.51% imported content. It claims 80% localisation by FY28 (Investor
Presentation, page 10). It is also adding 3Ws, high-speed models and two
regions.

**Why now?** Capacity went from 72,000 to 240,000 units across four plants in a
year (Concall May 2026, page 4). Management guides 125,000+ units and 75% to 80%
revenue growth for FY27 (page 5). The October 2025 IPO raised Rs 62.83 crore (H2
FY26 results, page 8).

**What must be true?** Volume grows 75%+ with no fall in the 11% EBITDA margin.
Cash starts to follow profit. Localisation lifts gross margin above 23%.

**What breaks it?** Cash. Five years of profit total Rs 54.7 crore. Five years of
operating cash flow total minus Rs 17.2 crore (Financials.txt, Data Sheet,
secondary). And the price: 76x trailing profit for an assembler of imported
kits.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "Manufacturing/Assembling of Electric Vehicles" (H2 FY26
results, page 8). No 2W/3W split is filed. 3W volume was 800 units (Concall May
2026, page 14).

Gross margin, revenue less materials and inventory change, was 23.1% in FY25
standalone and 23.3% in FY26 consolidated (H2 FY26 results, pages 7 and 15),
computed. It is flat. A trading subsidiary, Zelio Auto Components, began 13 May
2025 (page 16).

**Archetype: Brand/franchise consumer as pitched, with commodity-converter
economics underneath.** Volume and reach have moved. Gross margin has not.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent, possibly forming. Share is about 5% of low-speed E-2Ws (Investor
Presentation, page 7). Capacity is cheap to add: Hisar goes from 72,000 to
120,000 units "within the same premises", and Odisha cost under Rs 3 crore
(Concall Nov 2025, page 5). That helps returns. It also means low entry
barriers. Dealers are 70% exclusive, target 100% (Concall May 2026, page 9). The
flat gross margin shows no pricing power.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 commodity price-taker: a CKD assembler on 71.51% imports, flat 23% gross
margin. TO R3 value-added supplier with a brand, via 80% localisation.

Management's claim: "From CKD assembler today → India-first manufacturer
tomorrow" (Investor Presentation, page 10).

That is a two-rung leap. The ladder rule demands extraordinary proof for it.
Localisation was 30% in Nov 2025 (Concall Nov 2025, page 5). No later figure
exists in the corpus.

## 8. Proof check

Volume moved as claimed. Both FY26 targets were beaten. Both halves grew about
80%.

The transition has no proof point. Gross margin held at 23%. EBITDA margin
slipped from 12.2% to 11.3%. Operating cash flow went more negative. The proof
a climb needs is a gross-margin step, and it is absent.

H1 FY27 is not in the corpus. H1 was 43.4% of FY26 revenue. On the guided band,
H1 FY27 should print Rs 236 to 243 crore.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Cash conversion.** Operating cash flow negative in FY25 and FY26 while PAT
  grew (H2 FY26 results, pages 10 and 18).
- **Related-party purchases.** Rs 19.17 crore in FY26 from four firms tied to
  director relatives: Torque Innovation EV Auto Rs 8.19 crore (from Rs 0.89
  crore), Jai Bharat Auto Components Rs 5.21 crore, AVR Auto Industries Rs 3.68
  crore, Jai Bharat Engineering Tools Rs 2.10 crore (Annual Report FY26, page
  80). That is 8% of standalone materials.
- **Related-party customer.** Rajdhani Machinery Store, where a director and
  relatives are partners, bought Rs 8.45 crore (Annual Report FY26, page 57).
- **Guidance escalation.** FY27 revenue was Rs 350 to 400 crore, then "400 to
  450" (Concall Nov 2025, pages 10 and 19). Six months later it became about
  Rs 544 to 559 crore.
- **Inconsistent dates.** The 29 May call says the July 2026 plants were
  commissioned (page 4), then says Coimbatore opens "next month" (page 9).
- **IPL cost lands in FY27** as a prepaid expense (Concall May 2026, page 11).
- **IPO capex slow.** Rs 7.85 crore of Rs 19.45 crore used by March 2026 (H2
  FY26 results, page 22).
- **Trading surveillance.** The stock "keeps on getting thrown into periodic
  call auction" (Concall May 2026, page 6).
- Small Hisar auditor, Murari Garg & Co, unmodified opinion (H2 FY26 results,

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Volume is delivered. The rung climb is not.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, provisional.** Negative cash sits in inventory,
  supplier advances and GST refunds tied to growth. It turns structural if
  refunds stall and inventory days keep rising.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a filed document: Bull AI
  company record, market capitalisation Rs 2,149.4 crore, 2026-11-04. That is
  75.7x FY26 PAT and 42x to 43x guided FY27 PAT. The TO rung, R3, sits near
  19x. Screener shows Rs 315.10 at March 2026 against Rs 1,016.25 now
  (Financials.txt, Data Sheet).

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** Clean growth optics, a narrated climb, and
a price that has already paid for it.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** Hard watch, no `/step1` yet.

The growth is real and audited. The climb is unproven, and the price assumes
it. A `/step1` cannot find a recognition gap at 42x guided earnings.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. H1 FY27 revenue near Rs 236 crore with positive operating cash flow.
2. H1 FY27 gross margin. Above 23% is the first sign of localisation.
3. Size and price of the promoter offer-for-sale, from the prospectus.
4. Terms of the Rs 19.17 crore related-party purchases.
5. Patan and Coimbatore commissioning dates.

**What would change the view**
- Gross margin up two points with positive cash flow fires the proof gate.
- A price fall that reopens the gap, after proof, moves this to PROCEED.
- Missed H1 FY27 growth or rising inventory days moves it to PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides
only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
