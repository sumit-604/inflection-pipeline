Dear Sir/Madam, 
We refer to the Memorandum of Family Settlement (“MOFS”) dated 11 January 2021, entered 
into amongst the members of the Chowgule family, providing for the settlement, allocation and 
realignment of their respective shareholdings in various companies forming part of the family 
business structure. 
As part of the implementation of the MOFS, the members of Group A and Group B (defined in 
Schedules 1 and 2 of the said MoFS) have agreed to undertake the transfer and realignment of 
their respective shareholdings in accordance with the understanding, allocation and 
arrangements contemplated under Schedule D of the said MOFS. 
In this regard, we hereby express our intent to transfer total 4,09,940 shares of Rs. 10 each fully 
paid up held by the Company in Keltech Energies Ltd (KEL), bearing ISIN INE881 E01017 in 
favour of the persons (named in the attached Annexure hereto) who are the Members of Group B 
and also the Promoters of CSL, in accordance with the terms of the MOFS. The number of shares 
proposed to be transferred to each such person is mentioned in the said Annexure. 
The proposed transfer shall be undertaken in good faith for the purpose of implementing the 
family settlement and achieving the agreed allocation and realignment of shareholdings amongst 
the concerned family members.
