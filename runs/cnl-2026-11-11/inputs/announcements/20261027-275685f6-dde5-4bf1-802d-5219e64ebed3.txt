Media Release  
 
Creative Newtech’s Subsidiary Secure Connection Limited Wins  
‘New Rising Star’ Award  
for WOZOYO at Amazon Ads Global Peak Season Ads Summit 2026. 
 
 
 
Mumbai, 27th October 2026: Creative Newtech Limited (NSE: CNL; BSE: 544631) , an 
integrated technology growth partner, has marked a significant milestone with its 
subsidiary Secure Connection Limited’s consumer technology brand, WOZOYO, winning 
the ‘New Rising Star’ award at the Amazon Ads Global Peak Season Ads Summit 2026.  
The recognition marks an important step in WOZOYO’s growth as it continues to strengthen 
its presence across digital marketplaces globally and expand its reach among consumers. 
It also highlights the growing role of digital engagement in helping br ands improve 
visibility, connect with relevant audiences and build stronger consumer connections. 
As e -commerce transforms how consumers discover, evaluate and purchase products, 
digital platforms are creating new opportunities for businesses to expand the ir market 
reach. This aligns with Creative Newtech’s vision of growing its brand portfolio alongside 
its established distribution operations and technology solutions capabilities.

Company Overview: 
 
Established in 1992, Creative Newtech Limited is a well-established company in India’s Information 
and Communication Technology distribution market. Through a robust network of partners and 
distribution channels, the Company has made a strong foothold in the IT distribution market in the 
country. The Company has an omni-channel network across all three channels of online, offline and 
retail trade. 
 
Creative Newtech specializes in market entry and penetration for global brands. The Company 
offers demographic intelligence, and enables the formulation and execution of marketing strategies 
for it s clients. In addition to such services, Creative continues to focus on timely delivery, 
diversification of its product portfolio, sustained long-term relationships with its channel partners, 
and extending value-added services over and above distribution.

Creative has a unique value-added business model. They provide end-to-end solutions, from market
