## PASS 3 — PATTERN PASS (new findings only; Pass 1 and Pass 2 findings not repeated here, see full detail in B02-pass1.md and B02-pass2.md)

Approach: re-read looking for contradictions between notes, notes-vs-main-statement mismatches, deliberately thin disclosure, restatements, post-balance-sheet events, and going-concern language, rather than sequential note-by-note reading.

1. **🔴 CONFIRMED, with direct quote: narrative-vs-financials contradiction on operating cash flow.** MD&A / CEO's Message (p.15) states verbatim: *"Also in the second half of the year, we turned operating cash flow positive."* The Consolidated Cash Flow Statement (p.167) shows net cash **used** in operating activities of ₹(768.66)m for the FULL YEAR FY25, versus ₹(366.12)m FY24 — i.e., the full-year figure got *more negative* year-on-year, not less. Whatever occurred within H2 alone (unquantified, not disclosed separately anywhere in the Notes or MD&A), the full-year consolidated cash generation picture is a genuine, verifiable contradiction of the headline claim in the Chairman's/CEO's message. Pass 2 had already isolated the two negative OCF numbers; this pass locates and confirms the exact MD&A sentence creating the tension, with no offsetting H1/H2 split disclosed anywhere to allow an investor to verify the claim. 🔴 Highest-priority pattern finding.

2. **🟡 New: CFO transition landing exactly at the FY25 signing/audit-close boundary.** Board's Report / RMC composition note (p.55): "Mr. CV Ram resigned as Chief Financial Officer w.e.f. close of business hours on April 10, 2025, stepped down as a member of RMC with effect from 21 March 2025 and Mr. Balakrishnan Natesan Kaushik, President Finance (Group CFO w.e.f. April 11, 2025) was inducted." The outgoing CFO stepped down from the Risk Management Committee three weeks before FY25 year-end (21 March 2025, before the 31 March close) and left the CFO role itself the day before the new Group CFO's appointment (10/11 April 2025), i.e., during the FY25 financial-statement finalisation and audit window. Also noted: Company Secretary/Compliance Officer turnover mid-year (Jayant Prakash out 12 Dec 2024, Sanu Kapoor in 16 Jan 2025, per Secretarial Audit Report p.36-37, already flagged in Pass 2). Individually explainable (routine succession), but layered onto the audit-trail exception (finding below) and the OCF narrative gap, this is a governance-quality pattern worth naming rather than dismissing as noise.

3. **🟡 New: an actual monetary regulatory penalty corroborates the "technical glitch" framing of the RPT filing delay.** Corporate Governance Report, "Details of Capital Market Non-Compliance" (p.59): *"fine of Rs. 10,000/- each by BSE and NSE for delayed submission of Related Party Transactions for half year ended March 31, 2024."* Pass 2's Secretarial Audit finding described this only as a compliance-process note ("filed... two days after the due date... because of technical glitches"); this pass confirms the lapse actually carried a real (if small) monetary consequence from both exchanges, which is a harder governance signal than the Secretarial Audit's own softer framing suggests.

4. **🟢 Confirmed (not a new red flag): "material uncertainty" language is present but is standard audit boilerplate, not an actual going-concern qualification.** The phrase "material uncertainty" occurs 4 times in the full document; on inspection each occurrence sits within the standard SA 570 "Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Financial Statements" section of both the standalone and consolidated Independent Auditor's Reports (the boilerplate paragraph on concluding on management's use of the going-concern basis of accounting). ### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Full-year consolidated operating cash flow was negative ₹(768.66)m and worsened year-on-year despite the CEO's message claiming the group "turned operating cash flow positive" in H2 FY25 — please disclose the H1 vs H2 OCF split so this claim can be independently verified.
2. What is the specific valuation/fair-value basis for the ₹12.22-per-share price paid to NCI in the Getwell Medicare rights-issue/put-option settlement, and is the same valuation methodology applied consistently across the other ~10 FY25 acquisitions where NCI stakes were bought out or diluted?
3. Given ~40 of ~43 subsidiaries carry a CARO Clause xvii "cash losses" qualification and ₹245.49m of DTA remains unrecognised on subsidiary losses, what is management's specific remediation or turnaround timeline for these entities, and at what individual-CGU threshold would goodwill impairment testing be triggered given the discount-rate ceiling has already risen from 14.72% to 18.44%?
4. What is the commercial rationale for the new ₹181.52m purchase relationship with Dedeepya Pharma Pvt Ltd (up from a ₹4.92m base) and the reverse commission expense paid by the Holdco to subsidiary Novacare Healthcare Solutions (₹8.26m FY25), and were either priced against an arm's-length benchmark beyond the standard boilerplate assertion?
5. What is the remediation timeline for the audit-trail (edit-log) deficiencies that produced a formal exception in the standalone auditor's report on "proper books of account," across the 7 group accounting-software environments that lack the feature entirely?

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings-management-adjacent structuring:** the Curever Pharma debt/interest forgiveness (₹460.51m) is booked as a standalone "exceptional item" while its mirror-image gain flows through the AOC-1 pre-elimination profit-contribution table as ordinary subsidiary profit — a real cash/economic loss at the listed-entity level that substantially nets out on consolidation, meaning readers of subsidiary-level tables alone would misread the source of Curever's reported "profit."
- **Aggressive but compliant PPA practice:** 100% of acquisition premium across all reviewed FY24/FY25 deals is routed to goodwill with an explicit, recurring assertion that customer/vendor relationships "do not meet the criteria for recognition... under Ind AS 38." This avoids any amortisation drag on reported earnings and concentrates risk in goodwill subject only to thin, qualitative annual impairment testing.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators:** the OCF narrative-vs-financials gap is unresolved with no H1/H2 split provided anywhere; the Getwell NCI pricing basis is not given; the reagent-rental/customer-site equipment treatment for the newly acquired MedTech (Peerless) business is not addressed anywhere in the lease note; the consolidated CSR obligation for subsidiaries required to spend under s.135 is not quantified in any Note despite being disclosed narratively in the front-matter.
- **Direct-to-equity credits bypassing P&L:** ₹19.75m common-control business-combination gain and ₹155.69m put-option fair value are both booked directly to equity/reserves rather than through profit — correct under Ind AS but should be stripped out of any read of "organic" net-worth growth.

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate to concerning accounting practices. Key concern: full-year cash generation lags and worsens against reported profit growth at every level of the group, while roughly 40 of 43 acquired subsidiaries carry CARO cash-loss qualifications. Key strength: an extensive, largely Ind-AS-compliant and self-critical disclosure architecture, including company-identified control gaps. Overall accounting quality: 5/10.
