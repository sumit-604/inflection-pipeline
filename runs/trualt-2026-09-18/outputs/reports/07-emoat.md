### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Using the 📄-committed basis (Note 56 capital commitments, the only capex figure with a contractual anchor):

- Capex under execution (consolidated, contracted, net of advances): **Rs163.73cr** (AR Note 56, p.293-294)
- Consolidated net fixed assets (PPE net block + CWIP, 31-Mar-2026): Rs2,052.10cr + Rs67.71cr = **Rs2,119.81cr**
  (AR Note 5, p.263)
- Consolidated revenue from operations FY26: **Rs1,727.51cr** (AR Note 27/P&L, p.245)
- Historical fixed asset turnover = 1,727.51 / 2,119.81 = **0.815x**
- Implied incremental revenue = Rs163.73cr × 0.815 = **Rs133.4cr**
- As % of current consolidated revenue = 133.4 / 1,727.51 = **7.7%**

This 7.7% figure is deliberately conservative: it uses only capex with a signed contractual commitment (Note 56).
The much larger CBG (~Rs760cr gross, 🎙️) and SAF (Rs2,000-2,250cr, 📄 MoU value but 🎙️ on final scope/timing)
programmes are guided, not committed, and are excluded from this calculation by design — they sit in the
optionality register below, not in the scored capex-embedded-growth figure. If both fully convert at guided
scale, the aspirational addition (~Rs2,760-3,010cr combined) would be roughly 160-174% of current consolidated
revenue, but none of that capital carries a Note 56 anchor today, and B05's credibility record (grade C, 6 of 10
promises missed) argues for treating any such figure as upside, not base case.

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

**FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY**

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No global-manufacturer-count claim, no 12-36 month
qualification-timeline disclosure, no long-lead-equipment or multi-year regulatory-approval evidence for any
TruAlt process. Multi-feed conversion is a demonstrated capability (see A3), not shown to be rare.

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND — and explicitly negated. AR discloses R&D expenditure as
"Nil" (📄 AR p.191) and zero patent filings anywhere in the corpus. Management was asked directly: "do we own any
process patents...whether we wish to get into the patents" and answered: "we don't foresee to have any patents
or we don't foresee to have any technology advantage over others" for CBG or SAF (🎙️ Concall Nov-2025 p.13-14).
This is a rare case of a category actively closed by the company's own words, not merely undocumented.

**A3 — Process innovation.** Evidence found, strength MODERATE. Multi-feed conversion of three distilleries
(mono-feed to dual-feed, enabling maize/rice alongside molasses) was funded (Rs150.68cr allocated, Rs140.14cr
drawn, 📄 AR p.163-164), completed on schedule (dual-feed integration 3Q FY26, 📄 Ind-Ra rating rationale p.2),
and standalone capital commitments collapsed 96.4% as the programme wound down (B03 cross-check). This is a real,
delivered capability improvement, though its economic payoff (the grain-margin leg) has already compressed
sharply (see analyst note). Time to materialise: already delivered (12m).

**A4 — Product platform / modular architecture.** Evidence found, strength WEAK. The CBG build-out is explicitly
standardised: 4 identical 20 TPD Sumitomo units and 6 identical 12 TPD GAIL units (📄 AR p.164, spec disclosed),
and management describes "repeating the same model across our CBG infrastructure" for yield learning (🎙️ Concall
Aug-2026 p.16). This is real modular replication logic, but the revenue base today (2.4% of FY26 revenue) is
small and the replication is unproven past the first unit. Time to materialise: 12-24m.

**FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN**

into molasses/cane (Ind-Ra rating rationale p.2, 📄); its feedstock security instead runs through related-party

risk, not a documented captive-supply moat). Scored 0: the framework asks for RM% "declining vs peers" or
long-term captive agreements; neither is evidenced, and the dependency reads as risk, not advantage.

**B2 — Qualification lock-in.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No IATF/AS9100/cGMP-style certification, sole-source language,

### 4B. Government policy tailwinds

- **Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme**: sector-wide remunerative pricing; national blending reached 20.0%
  (Nov-2025 to Jun-2026 average) against a 2025/26 target (📄 AR p.72-73). Shared by every ethanol producer.
- **PM JI-VAN Yojana**: USD230mn (~Rs1,969.50cr) national envelope for advanced-biofuel capex, of which
  ~USD100mn (~Rs908.25cr) had been deployed across ten plants nationally as of the AR's writing (📄 AR p.72);
  TruAlt's Rs150cr SAF grant sits inside this envelope, approved and company-specific in amount though the scheme
  itself is open to qualifying competitors.
- **CBG Blending Obligation (CBGO)** and **CBG-CGD synchronisation framework**: mandates CGD entities to blend
  CBG into CNG/PNG from 1% in FY26, progressively rising; Union Budget 2026-27 removed central excise duty on the
  CBG component of blended CNG, correcting a structural double-taxation issue (📄 Inv. Pres. Q1FY27 p.16). Applies
  to all CBG producers equally.
- **State subsidies**: Maharashtra (40-100% capital subsidy for new ethanol projects), Madhya Pradesh (40% up to
  Rs200cr for cellulosic-ethanol), Karnataka/Bihar duty exemptions (📄 AR p.73). Sector-wide, not TruAlt-specific.
- **Interest subvention + PLI**: already active and material — 73.2% of standalone FY26 PBT (📄 B02/B03) — but
  this is an earnings-quality dependency already recognised in FY26, not an emerging tailwind, and it is flagged
  elsewhere (B02/B03 FLAG-EARNINGS-QUALITY) as a risk to the reported profitability base, not scored again here.

### 4C. Regulatory moat assessment

Active, not emerging, and mostly sector-wide: every scheme above (EBP, PM JI-VAN, CBGO, state subsidies) is
equally available to GULPOLY, BALRAMCHIN, TRIVENI and any other qualifying ethanol/CBG producer. The one
genuinely differentiating element is timing and sequencing — TruAlt was first to OMC status and has signed,
named JV partners (Sumitomo, GAIL) and an approved SAF grant while its scale is still being built — but "first"
is a lead, not a barrier (see I2 above). Sustainability: policy-contingent and time-bound throughout; the AR
itself discloses no discontinuation-sensitivity analysis for any of these schemes (B02 rank 1). Time to kick in:
SAF not until FY28; CBG ramping now but slipping against every guided date (B05).
