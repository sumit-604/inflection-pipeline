### Six mandatory diagnostics

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, decisively — consolidated +106.3% (303.89→626.88), standalone +109.5% (293.93→615.92). Driver is the 43% installed-capacity increase (1,400→2,000 KLPD, presentation L335) plus dual-feed integration of ~1,300 KLPD moving the ethanol platform from seasonal-sugar to near-year-round multi-feed. No Notion base case exists (fresh coverage), so no guidance-variance benchmark — **ND** for base-case comparison.

2. **Operating EBITDA margin YoY — and what actually drives it (attribution DOWNGRADED, surviving bear counter #1).** Margin expanded materially: consolidated **13.67%→21.18% (+751 bps)**; standalone **11.87%→20.56% (+869 bps)**. Management attributes this to the ~6% higher profitability of grain-based vs sugar-based operations (presentation L338). **This is only a PARTIAL explanation, not a fully "confirmed" structural edge.** Arithmetic: grain/dual-feed is ~1,300 KLPD of 2,000 KLPD installed = **~65% of capacity** (pres L335/L338). A **6-point** profitability edge applied to 65% of the mix contributes **at most ~0.06 × 0.65 ≈ 3.9pp** of blended-margin uplift (and that is the generous ceiling, assuming grain fully displaces sugar at the margin). The observed jump is **+7.51pp** (consolidated). Therefore **the majority residual (~3.6pp) is NOT feedstock mix — it is volume-driven fixed-cost absorption**: revenue more than doubled while D&A (+19.9%) and finance (+16.5%) barely moved, spreading a fixed cost base over ~2× volume. **That residual is utilisation-dependent (current utilisation 60.57%, pres L341) and reversible** if utilisation slips or ESY allocations tighten. Net: margin expansion is real and OI-independent (OI actually FELL), but its durability is conditional on holding utilisation, not a locked-in ~6% grain structural moat. **Attribution: PARTIAL / utilisation-dependent** (was: CONFIRMED).

3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY — and was the base loss-making?
