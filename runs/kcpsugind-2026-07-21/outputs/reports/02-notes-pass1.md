## PASS 1 SUMMARY — TOP 10 MOST SIGNIFICANT FINDINGS

1. **Reported profit collapsed from ₹5,626.48 lakhs (FY24) to a net loss of ₹(172.24)
   lakhs (FY25), a 103% swing; net profit margin went from ~+19.2% to ~-0.8/-1%** (P&L
   p.112; Note 73 Ratios p.126; Note 74 EPS p.127). 🔴 Red Flag.

2. **Quality of earnings — the FY24 profit and the FY25 loss are both dominated by
   non-operating items, not core operations.** FY24's ₹5,626.48 lakhs profit was driven
   substantially by a ₹4,819.63 lakhs unrealised fair-value gain on the equity investment
   portfolio (Note 30, p.111) plus an ₹804.42 lakhs asset-sale exceptional gain; in FY25
   the equivalent fair-value gain shrank to ₹437.57 lakhs (-90.9%) and the exceptional
   gain to ₹480.54 lakhs, while the core Sugar segment itself swung to a segment
   operating loss of ₹(821.03) lakhs from +₹651.41 lakhs a year earlier (Note 76, p.128).
   Additionally, a ₹663.89 lakhs deferred tax charge (driven mostly by the DTL on
   unrealised investment gains, Note 22, p.110) converted a modest pre-tax profit
   (₹530.03 lakhs) into the reported net loss. 🔴 Red Flag.

3. **Managerial remuneration of ₹60.53 lakhs paid to the MD and Executive Chairman for
   the fifth consecutive year of inadequate profits, in the same year the company
   recorded a net loss, pending shareholder special-resolution ratification** (Note 54,
   p.125; Auditor's Report (g), p.79). 🔴 Red Flag — governance.

4. **Deferred tax liability on fair-value gains on investments more than doubled**
   (₹583.87 lakhs → ₹1,299.04 lakhs, +122.5%, Note 22, p.110), reflecting continued
   embedded unrealised appreciation in the ₹18,727.62 lakhs quoted equity portfolio (Note
   5, p.104-105) even as the pace of P&L-recognised gains slowed sharply — a real,
   market-sensitive future cash tax exposure. 🟡 Watch.

5. **Trade receivable collection efficiency deteriorated 29% YoY** (Turnover ratio 25.66x
   → 18.14x, implied days ~14.2 → ~20.1, Note 73 p.126) even as absolute receivables
   fell, with 43% of the FY25 book >6 months overdue and a static, unchanging ₹372.67
   lakhs disputed-receivable balance carried for two consecutive years without
   resolution or reprovisioning (Note 10, p.106-107). 🟡 Watch — feeds FLAG-CASH.
