### Gate 0 — one MINOR finding

**MINOR — FY26 ROCE computed on average capital employed, not the framework's
per-year year-end denominator.** The fixed formula is
`ROCE = EBIT ÷ (Total Assets − Current Liabilities), per year`
(`prompts/01`, FORMULA DEFINITIONS; "do not substitute alternatives"). The
maker used *average* capital employed (30,723.68 = avg of FY26 32,103.16 and
FY25 29,344.20), giving 9.72%. The year-end figure is
2,986.11 ÷ 32,103.16 = **9.30%**. Recompute impact: A1 median stays 11.54%
(→1); A2 min 9.30% stays in 8-11.9 (→1); A4 decline widens to 8.13pp, still
>5pp (→0); M3 still <12 (→0). **No score, no classification change.** Likely
carried over from the ROE rule, which does specify averaging. Flagged for
formula-fidelity only.

**Gate 0 verdict: fully compliant on every score, band, deal-breaker and the
history_downgrade semantics. 1 MINOR formula-basis deviation, zero decision
