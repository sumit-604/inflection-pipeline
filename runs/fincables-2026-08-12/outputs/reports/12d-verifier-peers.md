## PART 6: OVERALL ASSESSMENT

B06 did substantively engage the peer set it was given: the great majority of
its SUBSTANTIVE citations (10 of 11) check out verbatim or near-verbatim
against the source transcripts, the four UNVERIFIABLE verdicts are honestly
and correctly graded against genuine peer silence (confirmed independently
across all 12 transcripts — no mention of Aditya Birla, germanium/preform, or
OFC market share/Sterlite/Birla Cables/HFCL benchmarking anywhere in the
supplied set), and the peer-selection gap flagged for the OFC-related claims
(1, 2, 6, 7) is real and correctly diagnosed, not a cover for missed work.

The one MAJOR issue is a genuine citation-fidelity problem: a quote used as
decisive evidence for Claim 2 (and cited in support of Claim 6) is attributed
to the wrong call, and this same error caused a peer-quarter that actually
contains unique substantive content (Paracables Feb-2025) to be filed as
CITED-ONLY. This does not change any of B06's seven verdicts — Claim 2 stays
UNVERIFIABLE either way, since the corrected attribution (Feb-2025, not
Nov-2024) still shows Paracables self-describing as an uncompetitive OFC
player rather than corroborating Finolex's pricing claim — but it is a real
anchor error that a downstream reader relying on B06's stated call date would
not be able to verify.

The remaining findings are MINOR: two RR Kabel figures misattributed to
adjacent calls, one overstated claim of cross-call recurrence, one paraphrase
presented as verbatim, one instance of unused corroborating evidence within an
already-read transcript, and one verdict-calibration note. None change a
verdict, none breach the ≥2-anchor VERIFIED rule, and no verdict was ever
upgraded from silence.
