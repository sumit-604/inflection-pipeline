## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

**2A Demand.** Split, not consensus. Energy and aerospace demand is strong at Azad (order book above Rs 6,500 Cr, 11-12x revenue: Azad Q4 FY26, Malpani, [page 6]) with customer supply limited by capacity. This matches I&PCL's pass-through narrative; it does not test nickel or cobalt.

**2C Capex cycle.** Capacity race, not a lone expander. Azad is building 8 dedicated plants (Azad Q1 FY27, Malpani, [page 13]-[page 14]). I&PCL is one of four expanders in a six-company set, but the only one whose programme is 88.8% of equity (B05) at 60% utilisation.

## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY

- Claims verified: 1 of 9 (Q5, pass-through principle; nickel and cobalt trend not covered).
- Claims partially verified: 6 of 9 (Q2, Q3, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q9). Unverifiable: 2 of 9 (Q1, Q7).
- Claims contradicted: 0. No peer states the opposite of an I&PCL claim. Priority items for synthesis are the peer-pace and WIP-margin tensions, carried as flags in the block.
- Most consequential tension: pace. Azad needed 2.5 years from engagement to a Rolls-Royce qualification batch (Azad Q4 FY26, Chopdar, [page 10]-[page 11]). Steelcast has had approved defence prototypes since June 2025 with no serial order by July 2026 (Steelcast Q1 FY27, [page 8]). B05 trigger 1 expects monthly supply in H2 FY27. No peer shows that pace for a new defence programme. Weight: I&PCL's orders are nominations, not new-engine qualifications.
- Strongest independent confirmation: pass-through. Three peers in three metals (steel, aluminium, superalloy) describe formula-based pass-through with a lag of one month to one quarter. This matches the I&PCL claim. Weight: Azad's 5% band shows the contract can leave a band at the supplier.
- Overall: the peer set complicates the narrative. It supports pass-through and the view that ICE and hybrid castings stay in demand. It gives base rates, not proof, for the A&D ramp: slow conversion, margin lifted by WIP content or one-offs, and capex funded from cash or fresh equity. Where I&PCL looks different from peers, it is on funding and on the pace it implies.
