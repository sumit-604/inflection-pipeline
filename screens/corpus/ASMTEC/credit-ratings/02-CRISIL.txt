 Detailed R ationale 

 C risil Ratings has upgraded its   ratings on the bank facilities of ASM Technologies Ltd (ASMTL) to ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’ from ‘C risil BBB-/Stable/C risil A3’ . 

 The upgrade reflects the improvement in   the   company’s   overall credit profile , which is expected to sustain over the medium term.     The operating income grew to Rs 289 crore   in fiscal 202 5   from   Rs 203 crore   in fiscal 202 4 ,   driven by increase in the share of income from existing customers, addition of new clients, and rise in design- led manufacturing business. The revenue is   expected to increase   steadily   over the medium term , aided by   expectation of an increase in business from all the segments . The operating margin also   improved to 1 7 % in fiscal 202 5 from 4.16 % in fiscal 202 4 , resulting in improvement in net cash accrual to Rs 32 crore . This was due to better absorption of fixed costs , resulting from ramp up in operations and share of design-led manufacturing business . The margin is expected to sustain at similar levels over the medium term. 

 The financial risk   profile   remained comfortable , backed by   comfortable networth   and capital structure. The interest coverage ratio also improved to 4. 6 times in fiscal 202 5 from 1 .01 times in fiscal 202 4 with higher operating profit.   The financial   risk   profile is expected to stay comfortable with heathy accretion to reserves , despite debt - funded capital expenditure ( capex ) plans over the medium term.   

 The ratings   reflect the extensive experience of the promoters, diversified service offerings supporting   scale and   providing   sustainability , and   comfortable   financial   risk   profile . These strengths are partially offset by large working capital cycle, sizeable fixed   costs , vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuations in foreign exchange (forex) rates , and customer concentration in revenu e.

 Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description 

 Strengths : 

 Extensive experience of the promoters:   The promoters have more than two decades   of experience   in   the   information technology (IT)   industry.   The key promoter and managing director ,   Mr Rabindra Srikantan,   has an   MS   degree   in   computer engineering and computer science   from   the   University of Louisia na, the USA . 

 Diversified service offerings support scale and   provide   sustainability:   The   company   is an established player in the   internet   software   and   services industry.   Clientele is strong and spread across the semiconductor equipment, network devices, hi-tech, medical equipment, automotive and aerospace   segments, which protects against drop in demand from any one industry . Further more, the share of manufacturing business increased in fiscal 2025 , led by increase in business of manufacturing   machinery used in the manufacture of electronic parts . This is expected to grow steadily over the medium term. The group offers consulting services in engineering and product   research and development.   It is present   across India, the U nited S tates , Singapore, the U nited K ingdom , Canada, Mexico and Japan. 

 Comfortable   financial   risk   profile :   The networth improved to Rs 137 crore as on March 31, 2025 from Rs 119 crore as on 31 st March 2024 driven by steady accretion to reserve and despite losses reported in overseas subsidiaries due to expansion and build- up capacity and initial phase of operations. Gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio remain ed comfortable at 0. 56 time and 0.80 time , respectively, as on March 31, 202 5 .   Debt protection metrics   also improved due to higher profitability ,   with interest coverage   and net cash accrual to adjusted debt   ratios   of 4. 6   times   and   0. 43   time ,   respectively,   for fiscal   202 5 ,   against 1   time and nil , respectively, previous fiscal . The financial risk profile is expected to remain at comfortable over the medium term with gradual repayment of term loans and healthy accretion to reserve amidst debt - funded capex plans . 

 Weakness es : 

 Large w orking c apital c ycle : Gross current assets remained   high at about 205   days as on March 31, 202 5 ,   due to   higher   receivables and large inventory of 115 days and 87 days , respectively .   The receivables level was high at balance sheet date due to higher revenue booking in the fourth qua rter of fiscal 2025. Payables were also high at 139 days . With increasing ramp up in operations , incremental working capital requirement and its management will remain monitorable . 

 Sizeable fixed   cost , susceptibility to employee attrition and vulnerability of operating margin to fluctuation in forex rates :   Most of the expenses are   fixed cost   in nature (employee costs and rentals), making it susceptible to the quantum of work received and subsequently, the level of billing. Variation in operating margin depends on the nature of contracts awarded. Operations are   also   susceptible to employee attrition, which is typical of the   IT/IT-enabled services   industry. Further more, a s   25-30 % of revenue comes from the international market,   any   sharp fluctuation in forex rates   can adversely   affect realisations   and   cash   accrual. 

 Customer concentration in revenue profile:   The top 5 customers account for 60 -7 0 % of the total revenue . This exposes the group’s revenue growth and profitability to its key customers’ growth plans. However, the group added new clients in fiscal 2025 which , along with expected organic acquisitions over the medium term , is likely to mitigate customer concentration in revenue profile. 

 Liquidity : Adequate 

 Bank limit utilisation   averaged   around 8 5 %   for the   12   months   through   April 202 5 .   Expected annual cash accrual   of Rs   35-45   crore   will be   sufficient   to meet yearly   term debt obligation of Rs   10 - 14   crore,   over the medium term.   The cash & and bank balance and current ratio remained at around Rs 13 crore and 1.89 times , respectively , as on March 31, 202 5. 

 Outlook : Stable 

 The company   will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters   and established relationships with clients. 

 Rating S ensitivity Factors 

 Upward factors 

 Steady improvement in revenue and sustenance of operating margin   (over 1 5 %), leading to cash accrual of over   Rs 40 crore on a sustained basis 

 Improvement in working capital management , financial risk profile and liquidity 

 Downward factors 

 Decline in profitability or operating income resulting   in   cash accrual below Rs 2 0 crore 

 Large,   debt-funded capex or stretch in working capital cycle weaken ing liquidity and financial risk profile
