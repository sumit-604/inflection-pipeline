shared by every licensed distillery in India, not a KCP-specific advantage, and KCP's own distillery output fell
82% YoY in the very period the E20 mandate went national (Section 2B). **Assessment: active industry tailwind,
zero current company-specific capture evidenced.** Time to kick in for KCP specifically: contingent on the
company reversing its own utilisation collapse — NOT FOUND as a management-guided timeline. Sustainability:
policy-dependent; the CARE note's own language ("India lifted restrictions...for the 2025-26 ESY") implies these
diversion rules have moved before and could move again.
