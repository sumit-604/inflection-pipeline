### 2A. SEBI Actions

Sourced from the Secretarial Audit Report (Form MR-3, Annexure I) annexed to the FY25 AR, dated 13 August 2025, and the separate Corporate Governance Compliance Certificate Annexure I (AR pp.51, 87):

1. **SEBI LODR Regulation 23(9) — RPT disclosure non-compliance.** Failure to disclose related party transactions on a consolidated basis for the half-year ended 31 March 2024. Company confirms fine "duly paid." ✅ VERIFIED, source: AR Annexure I (Secretarial Audit) and Annexure I (CG Certificate), pp.51 & 87.

2. **SEBI SAST Regulation 31(4) — late promoter declaration.** BSE raised a query for clarification over delayed submission of the promoter's yearly encumbrance declaration — the company's explanation: this was the first year post-listing (IPO Dec 2023) and the confirmation was awaited from the promoter/statutory auditors before capturing it in the FY24 financials; resolved 22 April 2024, no monetary penalty recorded. ✅ VERIFIED, source: AR Annexure I, p.51.

This is under review, and appropriate action will be initiated as per our policy on prohibition of insider trading." As of the secretarial audit's own date (13 August 2025 — five months into the current fiscal year and after FY25 year-end), **this matter was still unresolved and no penalty had been imposed.** The identity of the designated person, the nature of the trade, and quantum are NOT disclosed in the AR, and no subsequent resolution was located via web search. ✅ VERIFIED as to the fact of an unresolved compliance gap (company's own regulatory filing); ❓ UNVERIFIED as to underlying facts (who, what, outcome). **This is the most material adverse finding in this report** — an actual insider-trading code violation occurred, the company delayed informing the exchange, and as of the audit date had not completed disciplinary/penalty action.

## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. RPT History

### 3B. Capital Allocation Behaviour

- **No dividend** declared for FY24 or FY25 (post-IPO); Board states this conserves resources for capex/growth (AR p.37). Company grew revenue 32.9% and PAT 51.1% (standalone) in FY25, is capacity-constrained and expanding (₹700 Cr QIP, ₹94,899 sq.m Phase-1 expansion) — a defensible growth-stage rationale, not evidence of cash hoarding for its own sake.
- **Promoter remuneration:** Rakesh Chopdar's pay rose from Rs 43.49 Mn (FY24) to Rs 48.00 Mn (FY25), +10.37%, a ratio of 162.12x median employee remuneration (AR p.53, Annexure D). In absolute terms this is under 5.5% of FY25 standalone PAT (Rs 885.25 Mn) — not disproportionate on a percent-of-profit basis, but the multiple itself is high and was flagged by proxy advisor SES (see 3D) regarding the newly appointed MD's package, not the Chairman's.
- **Dilution:** ₹700 Cr QIP (Feb 2025) at Rs 1,280/share, ~8.5% dilution, executed via QIP rather than a rights issue (which would have preserved pro-rata access for all existing shareholders). SES's proxy report on the associated Jan 2025 EGM criticized the company for not providing adequate justification for bypassing a rights issue (📰/✅ published proxy-advisory finding, corroborated via secondary web summary as direct PDF fetch was blocked — see Search Log). No promoter warrants were issued or lapsed in FY25.
- **Salary vs performance:** Both PAT and remuneration rose together in FY25 (no divergence pattern of rising pay through declining performance).

⚠️ CAUTION — growth-stage dividend policy is defensible; QIP-over-rights-issue governance critique and high pay-multiple (though not high pay-to-profit) are the drivers.

### 3C. Promoter Share Transactions

- **Pledge:** Zero. Multiple independent sources (Screener, promoter's own BSE/NSE Regulation 31(4) disclosures) confirm no promoter share pledge since listing (Dec 2023) through the most recent April 2026 filing, which explicitly declares no new encumbrance created in FY26 and no PAC encumbrance. ✅ CLEAN.

- No warrant lapse events found (company has not issued convertible warrants).

✅ CLEAN on pledge and sale-timing; dilution pattern is capital-raise driven and disclosed.
