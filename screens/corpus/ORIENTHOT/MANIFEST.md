## OCR notes

- Garbled text layers without an `[OCR]` marker: results/q-2026-06.txt and results/q-2025-12.txt. Digits such as "LHi:.?1" and "111%5" appear in the tables. No number on the card comes from these garbled cells; each was cross-checked against a clean comparative column in a later filing.

The annual report says 28.54% direct and 37.09% with subsidiaries (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.23 and p.49). The card uses the annual report.
