## ANALYST NOTE

India Glycols scores AVERAGE (42/100 core, 4/60 moat) on a consolidated
group that is mid-dissolution. Two things drive the grade more than
business quality: a 2-year-only balance-sheet window (source gap, not
company gap) that caps A1/A2/A4/D-block precision, and total absence of
promoter/pledge data that zeroes three of four Block E tests by default,
not by a confirmed misalignment. Strip those data gaps and the underlying
signal is a low-teens ROCE (11.85-12.08%) improving marginally, strong
cash conversion (CFO/PAT 2.06x over 10 years) but two years of heavy,
capex-driven negative FCF tied to a Bio-MEG/EO capacity build (Gross Fixed
Assets +19% in FY26 alone), and a sub-1x current ratio that needs watching
independent of the demerger. Revenue growth is unremarkable (5.97% CAGR,
3 of 9 years down) while PAT growth (26.6% CAGR) is flattered by a
depressed FY17 base and one FY22 other-income outlier (Rs260cr). Zero
moats scored ≥3, mostly for lack of peer data (M2/M5/M9/M7), not
demonstrated absence — downstream verticals should target segment peer
comparables (MEG/EO chemicals, IMFL spirits, bio-pharma CDMO) before
treating NONE as final. The controlling fact for every later stage: this
consolidated history ends 1 Sep 2026, and three separately listed entities
(residual chemicals, IGL Spirits, Ennature Bio Pharma) inherit pieces of
it on different earnings bases.
