### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **Enterprise Solutions mix shift**: strengthening every quarter. Q3 FY26: product offsetting US services softness. Q4 FY26: formal FY26 segment split (59% of revenue, +55% YoY, 23% organic/32% acquisitive). Q1 FY27: Enterprise Solutions ~50% YoY growth, 65% of total income, EBITDA margin at 26.2% (highest of the three quarters shown — but see the row 3 caveat in Section 2A on the quarterly reported-margin dip that occurred in between, Q4 FY26 at 22.4%).
- **Cryptas cross-sell**: strengthening on deal evidence (two named wins in Q1 FY27), but the profitability narrative behind it drifted and was never reconciled across the three calls — see the correction note and Section 2B.
- **US InCommon / product**: NEW in Q4 FY26 call (not named in Q3 FY26, though the underlying US data centre was already live in Q3). Strengthens further in Q1 FY27 with volume detail (1,000-1,500 certs/day).
- **UAE QTSP / data centre**: the physical infrastructure is operating (stated in Q4 FY26 call prepared remarks, alongside the US, Europe and India data centres). It is specifically the **QTSP licence** that has slipped: Q3 FY26 implied a ~2-3 month audit (commissioning ~Apr-Jun 2026); the Q4 FY26 call gave no update on the licence specifically (even while confirming the DC itself was live); Q1 FY27 confirms the licence is still pending, "final step," now targeted ~Sep-Oct 2026.
- **Bolt-on AI-cybersecurity acquisition**: narrowing, not fully dropped. Q3 FY26: live consideration, "6-9 months." Q4 FY26: "nothing in the pipeline... maybe 3rd or 4th quarter." Q1 FY27: reframed narrowly as a possible AI-security bolt-on ("if at all, some bolt-on capability... nothing immediately," Kaushik Srinivasan, p.19) rather than the broader consideration of earlier calls.
- **Trust Services / India DSC-eSign**: reversed, and the reversal is at least partly mechanical. Q3 FY26: optimistic, 22-25% FY26 growth guided. Q4 FY26: delivered 32% FY26 growth (beat), driven in part by token-portal sales and combo certificates (p.11). Q1 FY27: temporary DECLINE explicitly described as partner destocking reversing the prior-period stocking pattern ahead of the September FIPS 140-3 deadline — meaning some of the FY26 "beat" likely reflected pulled-forward channel demand, not purely organic strength.
- **NIS2/DORA (Europe)**: present in Q3 and Q4 with specifics. By Q1 FY27, reduced to a generic line in the tailwinds list.
- **Agentic AI**: NEW as framing in Q4 FY26. Expands materially in Q1 FY27, but management is candid that adoption and revenue are "still pretty early stage... hard to quantify" (Kaushik Srinivasan, Q1 FY27 call, p.19).
- **eSign daily transaction volume**: an internal disclosure inconsistency, not a trigger reversal — Q3 FY26 call cites "more than 4 lakh per day" (p.10); Q4 FY26 call cites "well over 3 lakh daily transactions" (p.4), a lower figure quoted one quarter later with no explanation for the apparent decline or change in measurement basis.
