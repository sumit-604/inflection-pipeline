- Analysed 2026-09-06 per `screens/SHALLOW_ANALYSIS_FRAMEWORK.md`, from the corpus in `screens/corpus/RATNAVEER/`, read via Bull AI. Every number cites a file and page. No forensics, no valuation.
- Price +88% year to date, +95% over one year, near its 52-week high of ₹314.90; market value about ₹2,564 crore (Trendlyne, 2026-09-04). Passes the under-150% filter.

## Business understanding narrative

Ratnaveer makes stainless-steel things in Vadodara: washers, nuts and bolts, tubes and pipes, finished decorative sheet, and sheet-metal parts. It is India's largest exporter of stainless-steel washers, sells into 31 countries, and runs a closed loop where its own scrap becomes ingot, then sheet, then product. It earns a thin, steady spread on volume; it has no real pricing power, and half its revenue is low-value sheet finishing.

What is changing is a jump, not a step. The company is building India's first integrated Copper Clad Laminate plant. CCL is the fibreglass-and-copper board that every printed circuit board is built on, and India imports over 90% of it. Ratnaveer wants to make it here, and it frames this as turning "from an Indian exporter into a Global Industrial Conglomerate."

Why now: an import-substitution wave, PLI and Make-in-India support, and a first-mover claim in a market with no domestic supplier.

For the thesis to work, a stainless-steel washer maker must successfully run an electronics-grade chemical process, win PCB customers it does not yet have, and earn the 20% margins it projects, four times its current 6%.

What breaks it: the plant does not start until November 2026, so none of it is proven; the stock has already doubled on the announcement; the core business consumes cash as it grows; and the managing director is also the chief financial officer.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Five stainless-steel verticals, one integrated chain. Revenue mix FY26: finishing line 52.4%, washers 14.7%, sheet-metal components 11.0%, tubes and pipes 6.5%, nuts and bolts 5.5%, scrap sales 9.0%. (`presentations/Ratnaveer-InvestorPresentation-Q4FY26-a.txt`, page 21.) So the famous washer business is only about 15% of revenue, and more than half the company is the finishing line, the low-value surface-treatment of steel sheet, which actually shrank year on year, from ₹597 crore to ₹565 crore. (Same file, page 8.)

The economics are a volume story with no pricing power. EBITDA per tonne was ₹24,709 in FY23 and ₹26,011 in FY26, flat over three years; PAT margin sits near 6%. (Page 21; page 19.) Growth comes from making more tonnes, not from charging more.

Archetype: **commodity converter and build-to-spec component maker**. Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 applies: read spread, utilisation and cost position, not spot ROCE.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Cost and integration, not pricing. The moat is a 25-year export franchise, the number-one Indian position in stainless washers, a fully backward-integrated closed loop that turns scrap into finished product, 31 export markets and Fortune 500 OEM relationships, and an uninterrupted EEPC star-exporter record since 2003. (`presentations/Ratnaveer-InvestorPresentation-Q4FY26-a.txt`, pages 3 and 14.) That is a real cost and reliability advantage. It is not a pricing moat: flat EBITDA per tonne and a 6% net margin say the company is a price-taker in a "highly competitive and fragmented" industry, which is exactly how its own rating agency describes it. (Infomerics, 2026-04-28.) On the ladder this is a solid R2, a cost-advantaged converter, no higher. The potential new moat is CCL first-mover scarcity, which does not exist yet.

## 4. The promoters

Founder-run, and concentrated in one family. Vijay Ramanlal Sanghvi is managing director and chief financial officer, a founding promoter with 25 years in metals. His wife, Seema Vijay Sanghvi, previously the chief sales coordinator, is now an executive director. (`presentations/Ratnaveer-InvestorPresentation-Q4FY26-a.txt`, page 5.)

Two cautions sit in plain sight. One person holding both the managing-director and chief-financial-officer roles is a governance weakness, because the person who runs the business also signs off its accounts. And promoter holding has fallen about ten percentage points over two years since the 2023 IPO, though it rose about 2.75 points last quarter. (Trendlyne parameters, 2026-09-04.) The investor materials are heavily promotional: a full media-coverage slide, "Global Industrial Conglomerate" framing, and a CEO letter written in the language of destiny. That tone is itself a flag to weigh.

## 5. Financial trajectory

Growing fast, earning thin, and consuming cash (`presentations/Ratnaveer-InvestorPresentation-Q4FY26-a.txt`, pages 19, 22 and 23):

- Revenue FY24 ₹602 crore, FY25 ₹892 crore, FY26 ₹1,069 crore.
- PAT FY24 ₹31 crore, FY25 ₹47 crore, FY26 ₹64 crore; PAT margin about 5.2% to 6.0%.
- EBITDA margin rose from 9.5% to 11.4%; production volume rose 29% to 46,668 MT.
- ROCE 13.3%, down from 17.5% a year earlier; ROE 9.6%. (Page 24; Trendlyne.)
- June 2026 quarter revenue ₹314.6 crore, up about 19% year on year. (`results/Ratnaveer-result-20260724.txt`, page 1.)

The balance sheet shows the cost of the growth. Trade receivables jumped from ₹66 crore to ₹175 crore in one year; borrowings rose from ₹195 crore to ₹335 crore; finance cost rose from ₹12.7 crore to ₹20.5 crore and depreciation from ₹17.1 crore to ₹25.4 crore. (Page 23; page 22.) Operating cash flow turned sharply negative year on year. (Trendlyne parameters.)

Inflection type: **volume-led scale-up, thin flat margins, funded by working capital, debt and equity**. The profit grows; the cash does not follow.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

The claim, from the managing director's letter: "India currently imports over 90% of its Copper Clad Laminate demand. We are commissioning the country's first integrated CCL facility, with Line 1 targeted for November 2026. This is not an adjacency. It is a new growth engine." (`presentations/Ratnaveer-InvestorPresentation-Q4FY26-a.txt`, page 18.)

CCL is FR-4 laminate, the base material of every printed circuit board, made by pressing copper foil onto resin-impregnated glass fabric. The market case: India's PCB demand growing at 16% a year, over 90% of CCL imported, and Ratnaveer as the first domestic integrated maker. (Page 11.) Five lines planned, ₹330 crore fundraise announced, first line's machinery inspected in China, commissioning November 2026. (Page 13.) Alongside it, a European acquisition to bring the technology home, part-funded by ₹185.5 crore already raised. (Page 26.)

Ladder: this is a claimed leap from R2, a stainless-steel converter, to R3 or R4, an import-substitution electronics-materials maker with scarcity value. The framework's own rule is that a rung jump runs one rung per two to three years, and a multi-rung leap "is itself a red flag." This is a multi-rung leap into a process the company has never run.

## 8. Proof check

Split cleanly. The core scale-up is delivered: volume up 29%, revenue up 20%, margin up. The transition is not: the CCL plant does not start until November 2026, has produced nothing, and when an analyst asked directly whether any PCB company had placed a pre-order, management moved on without answering. (`concalls/Ratnaveer-Concall-Q4FY26-2026-05-14.txt`, page 12.) The projected CCL economics, 20% EBITDA and 29% return, are more than three times the core business's own margins, and no delivered number supports them. And the core's proof is undercut by its cash: receivables and borrowings both jumped, and operating cash flow went negative. The proof gate for the transition has not fired.

Infomerics upgraded to IVR A-/Stable on 28 April 2026, with the outlook revised to Stable from Positive, and enhanced rated bank facilities to ₹388.47 crore from ₹295.78 crore. It notes FY26 revenue up about 23% to roughly ₹1,100 crore and the new CCL segment at 5,280 sheets a day in Gujarat. ## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- Proof gate: NOT FIRED. The CCL transition is a plan with no plant, no output and no confirmed customer.
- Ugliness: leans STRUCTURAL. Thin margins, negative operating cash flow and a falling ROCE are features of a fragmented commodity converter, not a temporary optic.
- Recognition gap: CLOSED. The stock has roughly doubled since May 2026, when the same deck showed it at ₹179, and it now sits near its high. The CCL story is already in the price.

Posture: **PRICED NARRATIVE, the trap cell**. An unproven transition, already priced, on a structurally thin-margin base. The matrix's warning applies word for word: the downside is double, because the transition can fail and the multiple can compress at the same time.

## 12. Verdict

Candidate for /step1: **WATCH, hard watch, no work until the gate moves.**

This is a genuine transition idea, and that is the danger. CCL import substitution is exactly the theme the strategy hunts, and a first domestic maker of a 90%-imported electronics input is a real prize if it is delivered. But nothing is delivered: the plant starts in November 2026, there is no PCB customer, the projected margins are triple the core, the core itself consumes cash, the promoter is his own CFO, dilution is coming, and the price has already run 88% this year on the story alone. That combination, unproven plus priced plus a thin structural base, is the priced-narrative cell the framework says to avoid or watch with no position.

Load-bearing facts a full /step1 must verify before it is worth running:
1. CCL commissioning actually happening in November 2026, and the first quarter of real CCL revenue and margin against the 20% claim.
2. A signed PCB customer, since none is confirmed.
3. Operating cash flow turning positive, and the receivables build reversing.

What fires the gate to PROCEED: a commissioned line with real CCL sales at a margin near the claim, plus a customer. What confirms PASS: a slipped commissioning date, CCL margins near the commodity core, or the receivables and cash position worsening through the raise.
