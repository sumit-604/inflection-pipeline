### 1D. History before DPIL: founder vs inheritor

DPIL itself is not a founder-led startup: it is a **December-2020-incorporated vehicle** that
received, via NCLT-approved demerger (order dated 04.03.2022), the entire compounding business of
Kkalpana Industries (India) Limited, a business the Surana family has run since KIIL's 1985
incorporation, with family involvement in polymer compounding "for over five decades" per CRISIL
(rating pdf p.2, 17-Jul-2026) — i.e., tracing to roughly 1976, consistent with the company's own
"Ddev Group…pioneering force in the polymer compounding industry since 1977" positioning (web,
ddevgroup.in, 2026). Narrindra Suranna is therefore best characterised as a **multi-generation
family-business operator** (likely second-generation within the five-decade span, not a
first-generation founder of the underlying trade, though founder of KIIL's 1985 corporate form and
architect of the 2022 demerger) rather than either a pure founder or a passive inheritor. Ddev
Surana represents the family's third generation, brought up through KIIL operating roles (2019
onward) before taking the CEO chair at DPIL. No succession dispute, no prior venture failure, and
no unexplained career gap surfaced in any source reviewed. TRANSITION EVIDENCE NOTE: the
demerger itself — separating the higher-growth, higher-margin compounding business into a
cleanly-capitalised standalone listed entity — is a structural improvement worth carrying into
Section 6D.
