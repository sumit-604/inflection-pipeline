═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  HOW TO FIND A COMPANY'S SCREENER.IN URL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Go to screener.in in your browser
  2. Search for the company name
  3. Click on it
  4. Look at the browser address bar — it shows:
     https://www.screener.in/company/COMPANYNAME/
  5. Copy that URL and use it in the command above
