KEY HIGHLIGHTS
   Revenue surged 52.52% YoY to 2,947.57 Mn and PAT grew 21.24% to 250.20 Mn -- absolute profitability is expanding strongly, with EPS holding firm at 2.07, very strong
   net-worth fully absorbing post-IPO equity dilution in the very first quarter post-listing.
   EBITDA margin of 16.35% reflects global input cost headwinds -- crude oil-driven polymer price inflation and Red Sea-linked freight cost surges impacted the entire industry
   including packaging industry in Q2 FY27; as an exporter to 75 countries, customer pricing revisions to pass on input cost increases are currently underway, with
   adjustments expected to reflect in realizations from Q3 FY27 onwards subject market stabilization.
   PAT Margin of 8.22% is slightly impacted by a Rs. 60.60 Mn Mexico JV loss in H1 FY27. The Mexico JV is in peak scale-up investment phase, and this loss flows directly into
   consolidated PAT with zero revenue offset today; as JV operations reach breakeven, this drag reverses and PAT margin recovers fully.
