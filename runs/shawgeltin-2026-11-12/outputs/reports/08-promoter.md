[INFERENCE] PJ also makes gelatine and ossein, so it is both a supplier and a sector peer. The 7.04% share is below the 10%-of-turnover material threshold, so no minority vote was needed (threshold from Reg 23; not checked against the AR's own text).
   - Two readings: A, PJ supplies a input NGL cannot get cheaper, at a fair price. B, PJ sells to NGL at a margin that moves profit to the promoter body. One separating observation: PJ's price per kg to NGL vs the third-party price in AOC-2 or the Reg 23 half-year RPT filing. Not in the corpus.
2. IGCL stake: the money question.
   - Facts (VERIFIED): on 5-Dec-2024 the promoter bodies signed an SPA for 27,95,932 IGCL shares (39.42%) at Rs 408.90 from Olive Speciality Services LLP, Viren Mirani and Shefali Mirani, for Rs 114.3 Cr (DPS II.2). Same day, NGL bought 10,03,000 IGCL shares (14.14%) in a bulk deal at Rs 408.8637 from Seller 1 (Olive), about Rs 41.0 Cr (DPS I.A.ix). NGL's AR carries it at 4,115.31 lakh (B03; Note 6). The open offer (26%, Rs 408.90) drew 5,36,597 shares (7.56%), all to AMTI (POAd 7.2-7.9). SPA closed 31-Jan-2025.
   - Computed result: promoter bodies 33,32,529 shares (46.98%) plus NGL 10,03,000 (14.14%) = 61.12% of IGCL (POAd item 7.9 plus DPS). The brief's "about 53%" equals the SPA 39.42% plus NGL 14.14% = 53.56%, before the open offer.
   - Price fairness: NGL paid the same price as the controllers (408.86 vs 408.90). No price transfer from NGL to the promoter bodies is visible.
   - Who benefits: [INFERENCE] The promoter bodies gain control and a 46.98% stake. NGL adds a second block that helps the group cross 60% and helps the open-offer sellers exit. NGL's own return is a dividend of 50.15 lakh on 4,115 lakh cost (1.2%, B03) plus an unproven strategic link. NGL minorities (25%) carry 25% of the cost. The audit-committee chair of NGL joined IGCL's board on 31-Jan-2025, the day the SPA closed (Tofler).
   - Governance of the decision: a 4-Dec-2024 NGL board approval "to invest surplus funds" appears in one secondary summary (❓; source file not read). Section 186 shareholder approval: NOT FOUND, and probably not required if within limits (limits not checked).
   - IGCL is not listed as a related party in Note 37, which names only PJ and AMTI, though Note 6, 39 and AOC-1 treat it as an associate (B03; Note 37 header read at AR PDF p.72). A disclosure gap in the RPT table. Tier VERIFIED for the omission; its cause NOT FOUND.
   - Two readings: A, strategic. Gelatine consolidation gives sourcing, capacity or a later merger, and NGL's surplus cash was otherwise idle. B, NGL minority cash funded promoter control for a 1.2% yield. Separating observation: any NGL-IGCL trade, loan, scheme of arrangement or guarantee in H1 FY27, and whether NGL ever marks the stake against IGCL's price. No merger or scheme found in searches (search 10).
3. Loans and guarantees: "the Company did not advance any loan to any of its directors or companies and firms in which directors are interested" in FY26 (AR PDF p.30). CARO clean (B03).
4. Competing promoter entities: PJ and IGCL both make gelatine. [INFERENCE] The group now controls three gelatine-capacity names (PJ, NGL, IGCL). Allocation of customers, bone supply and capex between them is not disclosed. NOT FOUND.
