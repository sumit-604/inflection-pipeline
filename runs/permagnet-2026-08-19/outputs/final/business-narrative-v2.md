# BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Permanent Magnets Ltd builds the magnetic parts that sit inside other companies' finished products. Three revenue lines each clear a tenth of sales. Electricity meter components run about 40% of sales: shunts, current transformers, and flux concentrators that measure the current inside a smart meter. A meter maker cannot ship a certified meter without a sensing component that has already passed qualification, so the part matters more than its small unit price. Automotive sensing runs about 23%: magnetic, current, and torque sensing parts for Tier-1 auto suppliers. Alloys run about 10%: specialty nickel, cobalt, and ZAMAK alloys for aerospace, oil and gas, and industrial buyers, a line that grew from roughly 1% of sales in FY24 as the furnace commercialised in Q4 FY26. The customers are named and few. Meter buyers include Itron, where the company holds preferred supplier status, plus Landis and Gyr and Jabil. Auto buyers include Delta, Valeo, Siemens, Pegatron, Mahle, and Mahindra. The top five customers are about 44% of sales and the top ten about 53%, and that concentration is rising, with most work done on purchase orders rather than long term contracts. Present demand traces to three tracked drivers by name: the India RDSS smart-meter rollout progress, where 185 million of a 250 million target are still to install; global light-vehicle and EV production volumes across the Western Tier-1 base the company supplies; and the top-3 global electricity-meter manufacturers that form its largest customer class. Demand should grow if these drivers hold: the RDSS rollout runs to about 2030-31 on the official pace, the China NdFeB rare-earth export-licence regime under MOFCOM gates whether a rare-earth magnet line can ship, REL Developments Ltd (UK) supplies the relay licence behind the guided H2 FY27 ramp, and Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd is the listed AMISP proxy for India meter pull-through. The competitive advantage is uneven across the lines. Meters and current transformers hold a real moat, qualification lock-in, because re-approving a new supplier costs the customer more than the part; the tailor keeps the school because switching tailors is dear. Alloys hold a moderate advantage from backward integration into in-housed cores, not a lock-in. Auto sensing rides the same qualification-following logic, weaker because the moat circles a shrinking pond: Itron device solutions revenue fell 9% and Landis and Gyr sold its entire EMEA metering business. The latching relay line has a named licence partner but no moat yet, because it is pre commercial with zero orders in four years. The NdFeB magnet line has no moat at all: China holds about 92% of capacity, the product is a commodity converter, and the run did not establish any cost-curve edge for the company.

# Magnet maker funds two unproven growth bets

Permanent Magnets Ltd makes the magnetic parts inside electricity meters, cars, current transformers, and gas meters, and it converts specialty alloys for aerospace and industry. It builds to spec for OEMs in India and abroad and exports about 47% of what it sells. FY26 standalone revenue was Rs 225 Cr, up 13%, and standalone PAT was Rs 20.7 Cr, up 36%. The shares trade at Rs 882 for a market cap of Rs 758 Cr, about 38 times forward core earnings. The 52 week range is Rs 619 to Rs 1,230.

Three bets are meant to change the story. Alloys grew from about 1% of sales in FY24 to about 10% in FY26 after the furnace commercialised in Q4 FY26, and FY27 is guided at three to four times FY26. A latching relay is guided for H2 FY27, government approved on 30 March 2026, still pre commercial with zero orders in four years of licence. A neodymium magnet line sits inside the subsidiary Quantum Magnetics, government approved on 18 August 2026, pre revenue, with Phase 2 targeted for Q3 FY27 and 5,000 tonnes by FY31.

The backward numbers are average, not strong. Gate 0 scored the business 57 of 160, an AVERAGE classification. Over the five year window PAT compounded 2.5% while revenue compounded 14.9%, so profit decoupled from sales. Consolidated cash flow from operations fell 55% to Rs 17.6 Cr while consolidated PAT fell 6%, and free cash flow swung to minus Rs 25.8 Cr. Standalone receivables rose 39.4% against 13% revenue growth. The read on core quality lifted this run: strip the loss making subsidiary and core operating ROCE is 16.5%, above a cost of capital near 13.5%.

The subsidiary is where the cash and leverage went. Quantum Magnetics revenue collapsed 85.5% to Rs 0.8 Cr in the same year it drew a Rs 47.8 Cr unsecured foreign currency loan, posted a Rs 5.6 Cr loss, and ran negative net worth. Consolidated debt to equity rose from 0.17 times to 0.56 times. The standalone parent stayed free cash flow positive both years. The cash flag reads growth induced, not structural, because the drain traces to the subsidiary capex build, not a failing core.

Management says more than it has proven, so credibility grades C. It hits annual profit and loss guidance four years running and misses new-vertical timelines three checkpoints running. The alloys ramp was delivered and peers corroborate the demand. The relay timeline slipped and was self disclosed. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 19% but profit fell 13% to 38% with no explanation on file, since there is no concall. Governance grades CAUTION: a same sector family entity, Taparia Magnetics, is not disclosed as a related party, half the board is promoter family, and MD pay is disclosed two ways in one report, up 68.6% in one note and 28.01% in another. This is offset by a credible independent Chairman from the JSW group and zero pledged shares.

Peers confirm the direction and contradict nothing. MIDHANI corroborates strengthening aerospace and industrial alloys demand. SALZERELEC corroborates the metering runway and frames qualification friction as an industry wide reality. TDPOWERSYS is a weak match for the rare earth claims because it carries no neodymium in its raw material basket. All three peers run debt and working capital funded capex against separate demand stories, so the receivables build is not unique to this company.

The market runway is real but narrower than the headline. TAM is Rs 31,000 Cr to Rs 49,000 Cr, the serviceable slice is Rs 5,574 Cr, and the three year obtainable market is Rs 328 Cr, about 29 times current revenue. Runway grades STRONG rather than MASSIVE because most of the automotive current sensing TAM sits at the semiconductor layer the company does not occupy, and because the relay and neodymium lines still carry execution risk.

Valuation says AVOID at Rs 882. The sum of the parts base fair value is Rs 704 on the governing RRM track and Rs 791 on the additive track, both under the price. The core exit multiple is 16.1 times on the RRM track and 19.6 times on the additive track, with the growth premium held at plus zero: the Amendment 16 gate now opens at 16.5% core ROCE, but the operator holds it shut on single-credit reasoning because the transformation is already priced in the option slices. Forward core PE at the current price is 38.5 times against those 16 to 20 times destinations. The hurdle test stops, the hurdle ratio is 0.59 on the RRM track and 0.72 on the additive base, the probability weighted three year CAGR is minus 5.8% additive and minus 10.2% RRM, and the entry zone is Rs 252 to Rs 361 with a margin of safety price of Rs 252.

The devil's advocate survives and hardens the AVOID. Its sharpest points: the full-plan neodymium slice is about 40% of RRM fair value, Rs 283 a share, on a 12% probability, pre revenue, 13 to 18 times revenue-plan bet whose modal value is zero. Netting Rs 650 Cr of capital while holding the share count fixed double counts, since an equity raise near the market cap would roughly double shares and cut that slice to Rs 130 to Rs 176. A one year slip is the base case given three timeline misses, and slippage should cut the odds, not only the clock. The entry zone itself embeds the full-plan slice, about Rs 283 a share at 12% probability; if price ever enters the zone because a neodymium kill trigger fired, the zone must be recomputed to the ex-full-plan entry near Rs 216 with a margin of safety near Rs 151 before acting.
