Press Release 
 
 
Monolithisch Takes Center Stage as Principal Sponsor at BigMint India 
Ferrous Week 2025 
 
Monolithisch India Limited (Monolithisch), a global leader in advanced refractory solutions and 
steel-making innovation, is proud to serve as the Principal Sponsor of BigMint India Ferrous 
Week 2025, to be held from 19th to 21st August, 2025 at JW Marriott, Kolkata. 
 
BigMint India Ferrous Week stands as one of India’s foremost industry gatherings, bridging the 
entire ferrous value chain—from iron ore and pellets to scrap, coal, and steel. The three-day 
conference will unite producers, traders, policymakers, miners, technology providers, and 
logistics stakeholders for strategic dialogue on evolving market dynamics shaping India’s steel 
trajectory. 
 
Reaching its 2025 edition, the event integrates flagship conferences including the 8th India Iron 
Ore & Pellet Summit, 7th Indian DRI, Scrap & Steel Forum, and 5th India Coal Outlook Conference. 
It will deliver thought leadership sessions on demand‑supply trends, policy shifts, raw material 
outlook, and the role of scrap in decarbonisation and long-term sustainability  
 
As Principal Sponsor, Monolithisch underscores its commitment to supporting India’s industrial 
transformation—bringing cutting-edge technologies and industry expertise to the fore. Their 
leadership role at the event reflects the company’s dedication to fostering collaborative platforms 
that drive strategic progress in the ferrous ecosystem. 
 
About Monolithisch: 
Monolithisch is committed to excellence in the manufacturing and supply of high-performance 
industrial refractory materials. With an unwavering focus on quality, innovation, and customer-
centricity, we specialize in the production of premium-grade ramming mass—a critical heat 
insulation and lining consumable extensively used in induction furnaces across the iron, steel, 
and foundry sectors. its advanced refractory products and engineering solutions serving the steel, 
metallurgical, and high-temperature processing industries.
