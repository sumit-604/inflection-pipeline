## SECTION 6: TIMELINE, RISKS & COMBINED ASSESSMENT

### 6A Moat evolution timeline

### 6B Risks to top-scoring emerging moats
- **R1 (regulatory)**: SM-REIT product could remain in "wait and watch" indefinitely — the company has now said this in three consecutive quarters (Q2, Q3, Q4 FY26) without a launch. Early warning: another quarter passes with the same "evaluating pipeline" language and no scheme filed.
- **H2 (REA stake)**: risk that the 5.5% stake stays purely financial with no board seat, technology licensing, or joint go-to-market ever announced. Early warning: no REA-specific update in the next two concalls.
