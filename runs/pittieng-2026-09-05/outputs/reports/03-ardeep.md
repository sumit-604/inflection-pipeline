## PHASE 7: MULTI-STRATEGY SIGNAL EXTRACTION

### GARP (full reasoning per operator mandate)
Revenue/EBITDA growth is real (revenue +12.2%, adjusted EBITDA +19.9%, margin expansion to 17.03%) — the "growth" half of GARP is present and AR-evidenced. The "reasonable price" half is harder to clear: trailing consolidated PAT (Rs 117.81cr) against Rs 4,085cr market cap implies a trailing P/E of ~34.7x (or ~31.9x on management's Rs 128.07cr adjusted PAT) — not a multiple that screams "reasonable" for a year in which reported PAT **fell** 3.66% and net debt **rose** 26.3%. The bull case rests entirely on FY27+ delivery: the DTL/tax-rate reversal normalising, the capex ramp converting to volume/margin, and the promised working-capital release actually landing. None of these are yet confirmed by this AR. **Verdict: WATCHLIST.** The growth engine is real and AR-evidenced; the price is not obviously reasonable against a currently-declining bottom line, and the entry thesis depends on unconfirmed FY27 guidance.

### Turnaround (full reasoning per operator mandate)
Pitti Engineering does not fit the classic Turnaround archetype: PAT has been **positive every year FY20-26** (Gate 0 confirms no loss-to-profit swing), there is no default, no negative equity, no distressed-asset-sale history, and ROCE — while declining three straight years from an FY24 peak of 18.39% to FY26's 13.75% — has never approached value-destructive territory. This is better read as a **quality-ladder transition** story (Section 1B/quality-ladder framework territory: R2 cost-advantaged converter climbing toward R3 value-added supplier via the machined-components/casting mix shift), not a distress-to-recovery turnaround. **Verdict: FAIL** for the classic Turnaround strategy — there is no distress phase to turn around from; redirect this name's "climb" narrative to the Quality Ladder / Transition Decision Matrix machinery instead, where it plausibly fits better.

### Value+Quality
Trailing P/E ~34.7x is not "value" by conventional screens; quality is moderate-not-high (accounting quality 6/10, ROCE mid-teens and declining, leverage rising, disclosure gaps on goodwill/customer identity). **FAIL.**

### Capex-Led Growth
Strongest fit in this run: two active capex programmes (Rs 150cr brownfield, ~Rs 100cr already spent; Rs 290cr new project), justified by high and rising utilisation (76%/81%/71% across the three businesses) and named global-OEM demand visibility (Caterpillar, Voith, Progress Rail, Siemens Mobility, Medha Servo). CFO/PAT 1.74x is healthy on its face, but the interest-classification nuance (Phase 3A) means reported CFO is flattered relative to a company that classifies interest paid as operating; the FY26 working-capital reversal is a real, disclosed headwind. **Verdict: WATCHLIST** — the compounding case needs the WC release to actually land in FY27.

### Contrarian
Possible if the market is over-punishing the FY26 headline PAT miss without pricing in the tax-timing explanation; but genuine leverage and disclosure-selectivity concerns (6A/6E) mean this is not simply noise. **Verdict: WATCHLIST.**
