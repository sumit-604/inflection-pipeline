## SECTION 2: CAPACITY & CAPEX PIPELINE

**2A Capex programme table.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No capex programme, project
list, funding source, or commissioning schedule appears anywhere in the FY26
AR, the four concall transcripts, or the four investor decks. Grep of the
full AR text for "capex", "capital expenditure", "commissioning", "new
plant", "new facility" and "land bank" returns zero matches (AR MD&A p.51-55
confirmed boilerplate, verbatim-repeated from FY25 per B03). The AR's own
Corporate Social Responsibility disclosure states "Capital Investment on
energy conservation equipment — No Capital Investment yet" (AR p.[CSR
annexure], confirming zero disclosed capex intent even at the smallest
scale). Actual (not programmatic) capex spent in FY26, from the cash flow
statement's "Payment made to creditors towards purchase of assets" line, was
Rs 578.66 lakh (~Rs 5.79 Cr) consolidated / Rs 554.29 lakh (~Rs 5.54 Cr)
standalone (AR p.94/p.124, 📄). This is a maintenance-level number for an
asset-light distribution-plus-services model, not a growth capex programme.

**2B Utilisation trajectory per facility.** NOT APPLICABLE / NOT FOUND. QMS
runs no manufacturing plant of its own; it is a trading/distribution and
people-intensive services business. No facility utilisation metric is
disclosed.

**2C Growth embedded in capex.** Arithmetic: FY26 actual capex (Rs 5.79 Cr
consolidated, per above) × a historical fixed-asset-turnover ratio would, in
a capital-intensive model, imply incremental revenue capacity. Fixed-asset
turnover cannot be computed with confidence here: the AR discloses no
separate net fixed-asset base isolated from goodwill and investment lines in
the extract read, and the business model itself (distribution + services)
does not scale primarily through fixed-asset capacity. **capex_embedded_growth_pct
= NOT FOUND** (no capex programme exists to project against; the FY26 actual
spend of ~Rs 5.79 Cr consolidated is a maintenance figure, not a growth
programme, and applying a fixed-asset-turnover multiplier to a
distribution/services model would manufacture a number the business's own
economics do not support). This 0/NOT-FOUND figure is the one Stage 9
should carry forward.

**2D New geography or market entries.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND of geographic
expansion (new state, new country, new export market). The AR's Future
Outlook language ("expand its business operations on a national scale") is
identical, word-for-word in substance, to the FY25 AR per B03's finding —
NOT CREDIBLE AS STATED, no operational evidence in either year. The HCAH
scheme references "35+ cities" for the combined post-scheme entity but does
not name which are new versus already-served by QMS or Saarathi (🎙️/📄
mixed, city-level breakdown NOT FOUND).

## SECTION 4: REGULATORY & POLICY TAILWINDS (Category R1)

**4A Regulatory approvals in pipeline.** NOT FOUND. QMS itself holds
import/distribution licences (implied by its CDSCO-regulated import
business per B04) but no NEW regulatory application, approval status, or
timeline is disclosed for QMS in this filing cycle.

**4B Government policy tailwinds.** NOT FOUND. No PLI scheme, tax
incentive, import-substitution measure, or procurement-preference scheme is
named anywhere in the AR or concalls as applicable to QMS. The AR's own
Risk and Concerns section instead flags import-duty increases as a THREAT,
not a tailwind (AR p.55, 📄).

**4C Regulatory moat assessment.** The one genuine "regulatory" tailwind in
this scan is EXTERNAL and INDIRECT: management repeatedly cites the GLP-1
(semaglutide-class) generic launch wave in India as a demand driver for new
PSP-tied programmes, because some GLP-1 molecules have come OFF patent
(enabling Indian generic manufacturers to launch, each needing a PSP
partner) while others remain ON patent in adjacent categories (🎙️, Concall
Aug 2026 lines 287, 563-568, 728-751). This is a pharma-industry patent-
cliff dynamic that QMS benefits from as a service provider, not a
regulatory approval or policy scheme QMS itself holds. Classification:
EMERGING, not yet ACTIVE in a measurable revenue line (B05's priority-1
trigger names this as the confirm/kill signal for FY27 PSP ramp, still
unresolved as of Q1 FY27). Sustainability: tied to the ongoing generic-
launch cadence in India, likely a multi-year but not permanent tailwind as
the initial generic wave normalises.
