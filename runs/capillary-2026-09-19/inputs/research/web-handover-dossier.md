### 6.1 ROCE basis: operating ROCE (RULED)

Operator ruling: ROCE is measured on operations only. This is a standing rule, not specific to Capillary.

- Operating EBIT = Adjusted EBITDA - ESOP expense - depreciation and amortisation. It excludes finance income, asset disposal gains, fair value gains, FX gains and all exceptional items.
- Capital = Invested Capital as defined by the company (Capital Employed minus cash and cash equivalents, bank and demand deposits and short-term investments; Q1 FY27 deck glossary p.29). This removes unused IPO cash.
- Operating ROCE = Operating EBIT / Invested Capital.

| Period | Operating EBIT (Rs Cr) | Invested Capital (Rs Cr) | Operating ROCE |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY25 | 6.6 | 431.9 | 1.5% |
| FY26 | 21.2 | 549.4 | 3.9% |
| TTM Q1 FY27 | 41.0 | 598.6 | 6.8% |
| Q1 FY27 annualised (run rate) | 81.2 | 598.6 | 13.6% |
