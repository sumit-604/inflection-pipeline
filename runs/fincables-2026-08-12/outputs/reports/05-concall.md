### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

For every miss above, the reason given classifies as:

| Miss | Classification | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Preform trial slippage | Deflection → eventual honest admission | No excuse offered for the second and third slips beyond restating "under production trials"; only in Q4, once delivered, does management retrospectively narrate the full timeline plainly |
| Margin recovery miss | External-blame (mix, competition) with partial honesty | Consistently cites product/channel mix and, from Q4, an identifiable external shock (Middle East, FX, RM) — plausible, not obviously self-serving, but never couples the explanation with a concrete corrective plan or number |
| BharatNet miss | External-blame | Squarely blamed on government program execution delays — historically consistent (management notes NOFN was announced in 2010 and Phase 3 was still not fully underway as of Aug 2025, Q1 p.7), so this pattern predates the review period and is not a self-serving new excuse |
| Fiber draw capacity miss | External-blame (new: germanium/China-Europe export restriction) | A newly introduced, dated, externally verifiable constraint; not obviously convenient, since it also directly explains the Q4 inventory build narrative |
| Promoter dispute engagement | Silence (Q3, Q4) after partial concession (Q1) then flat refusal (Q2) | This is the one item in the whole record with a genuine "narrowing candor" pattern — not silence from day one, but declining engagement over time |

**Pattern check**: Management does NOT visibly blame externals while crediting itself for
wins — the delivered items (solar, e-beam, exports, Sumitomo JV) are attributed candidly to
execution and utilization improvements, not luck. Management does raise some hard topics
proactively (the Rs6cr gratuity provision was volunteered unprompted in opening remarks, Q3
FY26 p.3; the inventory-build explanation was given unprompted in opening remarks, Q4 FY26
p.4). It does NOT ever use the words "we made a mistake" or equivalent, even where a
timeline has slipped three consecutive quarters (preform) — the closest is an implicit
correction via restated dates. On the promoter dispute specifically, management's engagement
narrows over time rather than staying constant, which is a mild negative signal distinct from
the rest of the record.

**Classification: excuse_pattern = balanced.** The record is not "external-blame-heavy" in
the pejorative sense — several excuses (BharatNet government delays, germanium export
restrictions, monsoon effects on agri/fans) are externally verifiable and consistent across
quarters rather than shifting. But it falls short of "honest" because of the repeated,
unexplained internal timeline slips (preform, fiber draw) and the narrowing candor on the
promoter dispute.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **Receivables build (~Rs128cr per Gate 0 flag).** Never addressed in any of the four calls,

  Middle East shock and pre-buying dynamic could plausibly extend to elongated customer

  never reconciled within the call itself.
- **Quantified explanation of why the FMEG Rs500cr-by-2028 target, reaffirmed in Q2, is not
  mentioned again** — the topic returns in Q4 only as "under par" performance, with no
  reference back to the earlier target or whether it still stands.
- **A specific number or timeline for the CEO/CFO transition's operational implications**

Likely reason for silence in each case: receivables and the promoter dispute both touch

concall; the fiber-draw date inconsistency and the FMEG target drop read more as loose
