## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the raw transcripts alone)

Anchors are (call, speaker, PDF page).

**R1 — Showroom/EBO target cut three times and still missed.**
1,000 (Q1, Rahul Gautam, p.3) → ~800 (Q2, Rahul Gautam, p.3) → ~700 (Q3,
Rahul Gautam opening, p.3) → ~600 actual net adds for FY26 (Q4, Rahul Gautam,
p.3). Progressive downward revision every quarter, missed on the lowest
revision. Management reframes 800 as an "aberration" (Q2, Amit Gupta, p.16) and
never names it as a miss. Thesis-relevant (distribution is the core volume
lever). MAJOR-grade.

**R2 — Kurlon synergy tail (Rs 40-60cr) machine slippage across four quarters.**
In Q1 (p.6) the Rs 60cr balance was tied to "growth of top lines," NOT a
machine. From Q2 it becomes a malleable-fibre machine: install end-Q3, results
Q4 (Q2, Amit Gupta, p.7) → "almost reaching facility... mid of this quarter"
(Q3, Amit Gupta, p.13) → "delayed by 1.5 quarters... mid or end of this quarter"
i.e. Q1 FY27 (Q4, Amit Gupta, p.12). Same excuse each quarter; a two-plus-
quarter repeated slippage. MAJOR-grade (and the repeated-evasion pattern would
be CRITICAL if it had been missed).

**R3 — EBO/showroom count never reconciled.**
6,000 (Q1 presentation, p.3) → 5,300 (Q2 presentation, raised by Ritesh Shah,
p.7) despite +420 stores → in Q3 a new bucket appears: 3,800 "showroom format"
(2,900 Sleepwell + 900 Kurlon) plus 2,500 "category exclusive dealers"
(Rahul Gautam, p.20), never bridged to 5,300. Asked twice (Ritesh Shah Q2 p.7;
Pranav Doshi Q3 p.20), answered "I will just look at this... get back to you"
and then re-defined rather than reconciled. MAJOR-grade for metric integrity.

**R4 — Depreciation useful-life change flatters FY27 reported PAT.**
Written-down-value switched to a 40-50 year useful-life basis (Q4, Amit Gupta,
p.12-13). FY27 consolidated D&A guided to Rs 140-145cr (Q4, p.7) versus the
~Rs 180cr run-rate an analyst cited in Q3 (Ravi Purohit, p.9). Ritesh Shah asks
pointedly "what is the motivation of changing the depreciation policy right
now?" (Q4, p.12). Framed purely as an accounting-accuracy correction; the
mechanical PAT uplift during the turnaround narrative is not acknowledged.
MAJOR-grade earnings-quality item.

**R5 — Middle East war RM shock; unresolved Q1-Q2 FY27 margin risk.**
TDI to Rs 275 (main supplier GNFC) and polyol to Rs 180, both above 5-yr
averages of ~Rs 240 / ~Rs 120; polyol spiked to Rs 220 end-Feb (Q4, Rakesh
Chahar/Rahul Gautam, p.7,9). Blended TDI/polyol +25-35% (Q4, Amit Gupta, p.7).
Rahul Gautam calls pricing "crystal ball gazing" (Q4, p.6). Online channel not
yet repriced (Q3, Rakesh Chahar, p.17). Live, time-bound MAJOR-grade risk.

**R6 — Furlenco Q4 PAT step-up is unusually large.**
9M PAT Rs 18cr (Q3, Rahul Gautam, p.4) → FY26 PAT ~Rs 60cr (Q4, Rahul Gautam,
p.4), i.e. ~Rs 42cr in Q4 alone versus a ~Rs 2cr/month (~Rs 6cr/quarter) run
rate stated in Q2 (Amit Gupta, p.15). A 7x single-quarter jump in an associate
that SFL says it does not run. Warrants independent verification. MAJOR-grade
(bears on the H2 group PAT surge; see R10).

**R7 — Kurlon-vs-Sleepwell brand-level profitability never disclosed.**
Declined every time (Q2, Amit Gupta, p.12; Deepali Kumari Q2 p.11-12). Removes
the ability to verify whether Kurlon's mid-single-digit-to-~10% margin catch-up
is real or cross-subsidised. MINOR-to-MAJOR.

**R8 — U2O / Tarang-Aaram volume-mix % repeatedly withheld.**
Asked Q1 (Ritesh Shah, cross-ref p.21), Q3 (Bhavin Rupani, p.12) and Q4 (Ritesh
Shah, p.11); each time growth/value given, volume-mix declined as "circular
discussion." Repeated deflection. MINOR-to-MAJOR.

**R9 — Management's own admission that competitive intensity has NOT moderated.**
Rakesh Chahar, Q3 (p.19): "there is no change as such that they have reduced the
competitive activity... hoping... more level playing field going forward." SFL's
ASP-premiumisation thesis implicitly assumes intensity eases as peers list; SFL
itself does not yet see that. MAJOR-grade (margin durability).

**R10 — H2 PAT surge concentration / run-rate framing.**
Of FY26 consolidated PAT Rs 161cr, Rs 144cr came in the last two quarters (H1
only ~Rs 17cr, hit by MTM); management guides investors to annualise the
">Rs 70cr per quarter" H2 run rate (Q4, Amit Gupta, p.5-6). That H2 run rate is
simultaneously lifted by the depreciation-policy change (R4), the Furlenco Q4
step-up (R6), favourable RM-inventory timing (Q4, p.9) and the absence of the
H1 MTM drag. The individual drivers are each disclosed, but the aggregate
"annualise the flattered H2 print" framing is a MINOR-to-MODERATE over-promotion
risk that only surfaces on cross-quarter reading.

Lower-severity observations (not elevated to red-flag rows): other-income
guidance was walked up within one quarter (Q2 Rs 7-8cr/qtr, Amit Gupta p.18 →
Q3 actual Rs 16cr, revised to Rs 10-12cr, p.17 — favourable direction, minor);
Q4 highlights "volume growth ~15%" (p.2) adjacent to the 15% target that was
actually a value/top-line goal (FY26 value +11%), a mild conflation.

## PART 4: CREDIBILITY GRADE

I concur with B05's grade **B (Good)**. Strong delivery on headline financials
(record top line, +78% PAT, first dividend, Furlenco and international
turnarounds, directional debt reduction) sits against chronic under-delivery on
discrete operational commitments (showroom, synergy machine) and an
earnings-quality overhang (depreciation-policy timing plus an H2-loaded PAT
that management invites investors to annualise). The one genuinely positive
credibility signal — Amit Gupta's unprompted "we struggled with it for a year
or two, and I think you all are witness to that" (Q4, p.15) — is real and
correctly weighted by B05. B is defensible; if anything the H2 run-rate framing
(R10) argues for holding the grade at B rather than nudging higher.
