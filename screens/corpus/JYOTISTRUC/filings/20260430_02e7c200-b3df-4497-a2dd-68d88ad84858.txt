Jyoti Structures Limited Announces Robust Financial Performance for Q4 & Year Ended 
FY2025-26 
Strong growth momentum continues across key financial metrics 
 
Mumbai, April 30, 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL” or “the Company”), a leading infrastructure 
and power transmission engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company, today announced 
its financial results for the quarter and year ended on March 31, 2026. 
 
The Company delivered a strong operational and financial performance during the period, driven by 
disciplined execution, effective cost management, and steady progress across its order book. 
Performance during the quarter reflects continued momentum across core businesses.
