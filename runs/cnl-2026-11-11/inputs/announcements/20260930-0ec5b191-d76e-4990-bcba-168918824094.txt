Annexure A 
 
Proceedings of 22nd Annual General Meeting of Creative Newtech Limited  
 
The 22nd Annual General Meeting ('AGM') of the Company, Creative Newtech Limited, was held on 
Wednesday, 30 th September, 2026 at 11.00 a.m. through Video Conferencing (‘VC’) and Other 
Audio-Visual Means (‘OAVM’). 
 
Time of commencement: 11:00 A.M. 
Time of conclusion: 12:02 P.M. 
 
In attendance 
1. Mr. Ketan Chhaganlal Patel   Chairman and Managing Director 
2. Mr. Vijay Kimatrai Advani   Whole-time Director 
3. Mr. Mihir Mahendra Shah   Independent Director 
4. Mr. Kurian Pallathuseril Chandy  Independent Director 
5. Mr. Suresh Bhagavatula  Independent Director

11. Mr. Vipul Somaiya    Internal Auditor 
12. Mr. Satyajit Mishra    Secretarial Auditor and AGM Scrutinizer 
 
Total 67 Shareholders joined the Annual General Meeting through Video conferencing. 
 
1. Mr. Ketan Patel, Chairman and Managing Director of the Company, took the Chair, 
welcomed the Members attending the Meeting through VC/OAVM and, the requisite 
quorum being present, called the Meeting to order. 
 
2. The Chairman introduced the Directors and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company 
who had joined the AGM through video conferencing, including the Whole -time Director, 
Chief Strategy Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Compliance Officer & Company 
Secretary and Independent Directors.

3. The Chairman thereafter introduced the Statutory Auditor, Internal Auditor and 
Secretarial Auditor present at the Meeting and informed the Members that M/s Satyajit 
Mishra & Co., Practising Company Secretaries, had also be en appointed as the Scrutinizer 
for the AGM. 
 
4. Thereafter, Mr. Tejas Doshi, Chief Compliance Officer and Company Secretary, informed 
the Members about the regulatory framework applicable to the Meeting and stated that 
the AGM was being conducted through  VC/OAVM in accordance with the applicable 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, SEBI Listing Regulations and circulars issued by 
MCA and SEBI. 
 
5. Mr. Tejas Doshi then gave general instructions to the Members regarding participation in 
the Meeting, mute /unmute facility, Q&A session, non -availability of proxy facility and 
electronic inspection of statutory registers and other documents. 
 
6. With the permission of the Members, the Notice convening the 22nd AGM and the Directors’ 
Report were taken as read.  The Members were also informed that the Statutory Auditors 
had issued an unmodified opinion and, accordingly, the Statutory Auditor’s Report was 
also taken as read. 
 
7. Mr. Tejas Doshi informed the Members about the remote e-voting facility provided by the 
Company, the period during which remote e -voting was available, appointment of the 
Scrutinizer and the manner in which the voting results and Scrutinizer’s Report would be 
declared and disseminated to the stock exchanges, i.e. NSE & BSE along with on website of 
the company i.e. www.creativenewtech.com . 
 
8. Mr. Tejas Doshi thereafter explained the flow of the AGM and invited Mr. Ketan Patel, 
Chairman and Managing Director, to address the shareholders. 
 
9. The Chairman addressed the shareholders and highlighted the Company’s performance 
during FY 2025 -26, developments across its business verticals, expansion into new 
technology areas, business and operational growth, BSE Main Board listing and the 
Company’s future direction and growth plans. Thereafter, he requested Mr. Tejas Doshi to 
take the Members through the agenda items of the AGM.
