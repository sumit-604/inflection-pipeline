# Step 1 Business Brief — Yasho Industries Ltd (YASHO)

Run date: 2026-09-05. CMP Rs 4,134. Market cap Rs 4,985 cr (manifest).
Author: Claude Code Step-1 intake. This brief is COMPANY MEMORY, weighed,
never anchored evidence. Every stage number still comes from this run's filings.

## What it makes and sells
- Specialty chemicals maker, incorporated 1985 (Jhaveri family), plants at
  Pakhajan (42 acres) and Vapi, Gujarat. 140+ products, 2 divisions, 50+ R&D
  staff, exports to 50+ countries. Source: Q1FY27 investor presentation, pp.4-9
  (inputs/presentation).
- Two divisions. INDUSTRIAL (rubber chemicals: accelerators and antioxidants for
  tyres, conveyor belts, latex gloves, condoms; lubricant additives for
  hydraulic, engine and gear oils, greases, metalworking fluids; stabilisers for
  acrylics, inks, resins). CONSUMER (aroma chemicals for flavours and
  fragrances, personal care, oral care, agro and pharma intermediates, food and
  animal-feed additives). Source: presentation p.7.

## Revenue mix and margin source
- Q1FY27 (quarter ended Jun-2026): revenue Rs 307.74 cr, EBITDA Rs 74.42 cr
  (24.2% margin), PAT Rs 36.05 cr, EPS Rs 29.9. FY26 full year: revenue
  Rs 830.03 cr, EBITDA Rs 144.46 cr (17.4%), PAT Rs 25.26 cr. Source:
  presentation p.15-16; https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/yasho-industries-consolidated-net-profit-rises-890-38-in-the-june-2026-quarter-126073100848_1.html
- Mix Q1FY27: Industrial 89%, Consumer 11%. Geography: international 69%,
  domestic 31%. Margin lift attributed to improved product mix, cost
  efficiencies and operating leverage on 42% YoY volume growth. Source:
  presentation p.12, p.16.

## Balance sheet and cash
- Debt/EBITDA improved to 1.86x in Q1FY27 from 3.75x in Q4FY26. Working-capital
  cycle cut from 190 days to 143 days. Bank rating upgraded BBB+ to A- by CRISIL
  and ICRA. Source: presentation p.12, p.17.
- Capacity utilisation >65% in Q1FY27. FY27 capex outlay raised Rs 125 cr to
  Rs 250 cr (2 new Pakhajan buildings); ~Rs 100 cr to be borrowed, keeping
  Debt/EBITDA below 2.5x. Source: presentation p.12-13.

## The one inflection the thesis rests on
- A dedicated-capacity ramp for a signed MNC contract plus a mix shift toward
  higher-value industrial chemistries. For the MNC contract signed last year the
  company has received Rs 98.12 cr to date, paid Rs 70.89 cr vendor advances,
  equipment delivery in Q3FY27, trial Q4FY27, commercialisation Q1FY28. On
  long-term customer commitments management revised the FY28 revenue target to
  more than Rs 1,600 cr and guides 30-40% annual growth for 2-3 years. Source:
  presentation p.13.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority for every stage)
1. GUIDANCE VS DELIVERY. FY28 revenue target >Rs 1,600 cr against FY26
   Rs 830.03 cr is ~39% two-year CAGR, on a 30-40% guide. Verify the target and
   the volume/capex path against the four concalls (Nov-2025 to Aug-2026) and the
   Pakhajan commissioning timeline in the AR. A guide of this size on a WC-heavy
   base is the thesis; test how firm the customer commitments are.
2. MARGIN STEP-UP DURABILITY. EBITDA margin jumped to 24.2% in Q1FY27 from 17.4%
   FY26 and 18.2% in Q4FY26. Verify whether the lift is structural (mix,
   operating leverage) or a single strong quarter, against concall commentary and
   the AR segment margins. A margin that reverts halves the earnings story.
3. CASH CONVERSION. Working capital ran at 190 days and the WC cycle drop to 143
   days is one quarter old. Verify CFO/PAT and receivables/inventory trend across
   FY24-FY26 in the AR cash-flow and balance-sheet, and whether the capex ramp
   plus the growth guide widen WC again. This is the FLAG-CASH determination:
   structural vs growth-induced.
4. MNC-CONTRACT CONCENTRATION AND EXECUTION. Commercialisation of the MNC line is
   Q1FY28, funded partly by customer advances (Rs 98.12 cr received). Verify
   single-customer concentration, the contract's take-or-pay or volume terms, and
   execution slippage risk in the concalls and AR related-party / advances notes.
