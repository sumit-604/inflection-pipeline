### NEW PATTERN FINDING: Note 46 ("no subsequent events") omits the proposed final dividend
🟡 Watch, new in Pass 3. Note 46 (p.114) states in full: "There are no subsequent events that
occur after the reporting period." The Board's Report (p.~9, cross-referenced in Pass 1 under
Note 15) recommends a final dividend of Rs 10/share (100% of face value), pending shareholder
approval at the AGM, for FY26 — a decision necessarily taken after the 31-Mar-2026 balance sheet
date. Under Ind AS 10, a dividend declared after the reporting period but before the financial
statements are approved for issue is a classic non-adjusting event and is conventionally named
(even if not booked as a liability) in the events-after-reporting-period note. Note 46's blanket
"no subsequent events" statement is not literally accurate against the company's own Board's
Report in the same filing. This is a disclosure-drafting gap, not a numbers issue — the dividend
recommendation itself is properly excluded from the FY26 liability figures (correct treatment) —
but the note's categorical wording is inconsistent with a fact disclosed elsewhere in the same AR.
Source: Note 46 (p.114, line ~18170); Board's Report (p.~9, line ~9587); Note 1 policy on Ind AS
10 (p.104).
