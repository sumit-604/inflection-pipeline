## 2A Accounting policy aggressiveness — extended assessment

- **Revenue recognition**: simple point-in-time recognition on service completion (Ind AS 115, five-step model); no bill-and-hold, no percentage-of-completion judgment calls found. Not aggressive on the policy itself; the concern (per B02 and confirmed here) is the internally inconsistent customer-concentration disclosure, not the recognition policy.
- **Depreciation lives**: standard SLM lives (buildings 30/60 yrs, vehicles 8 yrs, P&M 5/14 yrs) unchanged YoY at both scopes — no useful-life stretching detected.
- **Ind AS 116 (leases)**: incremental borrowing rate disclosed at 10% (standalone) / 10-11% (consol) for the new equipment ROU lease — a specific, checkable number, not merely narrative. The 5-year lease with Pristine Malwa was subsequently (per the AGM Notice, Sep-2025) shortened to 3 years "to manage liquidity," itself a signal that the original 5-year commitment was already a stretch relative to standalone cash generation.
- **Capitalisation incl. borrowing costs**: no evidence of aggressive capitalisation of interest or opex; Capital Work-in-Progress ageing schedule (Note 2f, standalone) shows no long-overdue projects.
- **Impairment assumptions**: standalone PPE impairment allowance Rs 8.00 Cr carried forward from FY22 (Plant & Machinery), and the Sical Iron Ore Terminal (Mangalore) investment fully impaired (Rs 2.50 Cr) — **no discount rate or cash-flow growth assumption is disclosed anywhere** for any impairment test, standalone or consolidated. This is a genuine disclosure gap under Ind AS 36 given the materiality of the impaired balances.
- **ECL matrix**: simplified-approach lifetime ECL, standard Ind AS 109 language; the matrix itself (ageing-bucket loss rates) is disclosed in full detail (a disclosure strength) but the Unbilled-bucket freeze (finding 8) shows the matrix is not being genuinely recalibrated year to year despite the standard boilerplate claiming an annually updated provision matrix (Note 1.12, standalone, p.90: "the historical observed default rates are updated and changes in forward-looking estimates are analysed" — contradicted in practice by the frozen Rs 28.58 Cr figure).
- **No quantified policy change** found in either note set this pass (confirms B02).
