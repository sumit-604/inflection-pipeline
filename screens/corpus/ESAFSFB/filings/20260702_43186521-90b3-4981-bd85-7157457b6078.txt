Management Commentary: 
 
1. 
Deposits: 
• 
Total deposits increased by 18.62% YoY to ₹26,925 Crore as on June 30, 2026. 
• 
CASA deposits witnessed a 11.90% YoY growth, reaching ₹6298 Crore as on June 
30, 2026.  
• 
The CASA ratio stood at 23.39% as on June 30, 2026 as against 24.79% as on June 
30, 2025. 
 
2. 
Advances: 
• 
Gross advances increased by 27.39% YoY to ₹23,216 Crore as on June 30, 2026. 
• 
Advances including IBPC increased by 32.87% YoY to 24,216 Crore as on June 30, 
2026. 
• 
MARG (MSME, Agri Retail & Gold Loan) - MARG advances grew by 35.32% and 
stood at ₹14,483 Crore as on June 30, 2026.  
• 
Secured advances constitute 62.38% of the gross advances as on June 30, 2026 as 
against 58.73% as on June 30, 2025.   
• 
Disbursements increased from ₹7,700 Crore in Q1 FY26 to ₹8,381 Crore in Q1 FY27, 
representing a YoY growth of approximately 9% 
• 
CD Ratio computed as a percentage of gross advances to deposits stood at 86.22% 
as on June 30, 2026 as against 80.29% as on June 30, 2025 
 
3. 
Total Business 
• Total business of the Bank (including IBPC) stood at ₹51,141 Crore as on June 30, 
2026 as against ₹40,923 Crore as on June 30, 2025. 
 
4. 
Customer Base: 
• 
The Bank’s customer base stood at 1.04 Crore as on June 30, 2026, with 1.86 lakh 
new customers added during this Quarter. 
 
5. 
Distribution Network: 
• 
As on June 30, 2026, the Bank’s distribution network comprised 821 branches, 721 
ATMs and 1065 Customer Service Centres spanning across 24 states and 2 union 
territories.
