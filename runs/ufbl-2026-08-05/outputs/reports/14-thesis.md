## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why the business is investable)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** The Gate 0 AVOID (27/160) is substantially an Ind AS 116 lease-accounting optic, not a cash failure: screener "Borrowings" bundles ~Rs 750.84 cr of lease liabilities into FY26's Rs 885 cr total, and the binding deal-breaker #6 (ND/EBITDA 4.13x, IC 0.21x) is itself lease-driven (B01). The real cash record is strong for the format: cumulative CFO FY17–26 is +Rs 1,477 cr against cumulative PAT of −Rs 267 cr (B01); working capital is negative (customers pay at the table, receivables under one day; deliberation Section 1); CFO/EBITDA ran 91.5% FY25 and 115.6% FY26, FCF positive four years (deliberation Section 5). ICRA rates liquidity "adequate" with external borrowings of only Rs 69.5 cr of the Rs 757.5 cr total debt (B01). Q1 FY27 turned consolidated PAT positive at +Rs 3.09 cr owners' share — the first positive quarter in the screener series after the FY26 loss of −Rs 59.13 cr (B01).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction; B05).**
1. Volume-led SSSG momentum into FY27 — conviction H. Q4 FY26 SSSG accelerated to 14.4% from 8.2% in Q3; independently corroborated by Speciality's Q4 FY26 call naming UFBL's "14% SSG" (B06). Confirm: Q2/Q3 FY27 SSSG holding at/above double digit without incremental discounting. Kills: SSSG reverting to flat/negative as the H2 FY26 base is lapped from Q3 FY27.
2. Gross margin recovery to 67–68% — conviction M. Confirm: sequential prints above 66% through FY27. Kills: Middle East input-cost inflation or daypart-mix dilution keeps margin below 66%. **Missed at FY26 close (65.5% actual) and reiterated across all three calls (B05) — the softest leg.**
3. Operating leverage to 9–10% pre-Ind AS EBITDA margin — conviction M. Q1 FY27 actual 8.1%; back-end cost target 7.1%→6.5% (B05).
4. Store network 300+ by FY27, 400–425 by FY30 funded internally — conviction M. Confirm: net debt holds ~Rs 100–102 cr through FY27 while stores grow (B05).

**3C. **3D. **3E. **3F. Peer Positioning.** Peer evidence complicates, not confirms, the bull narrative (net_narrative_effect: "complicates", B06). UFBL's gross margin COMPRESSED through FY26 (65.5% vs 68.5%) while peer margins broadly expanded (RBA ~70%, Westlife near records, Speciality up); peers report FALLING corporate overhead while UFBL's rose 6.1%→7.1%; peers describe RISING competitive intensity and value-price wars while UFBL asserts intensity is unchanged across three calls (B06). ## NARRATIVE

United Foodbrands is the rare file where the business is getting better and the stock is still a pass. Four transitions are turning at once — revenue, margin, cash and capital efficiency all print a starting-to-recovering +1, giving an FTTCP composite of +4 and a DEEP WATCH leaning BUY ON DIPS. The cash engine underneath the scary-looking balance sheet is real: negative working capital, receivables under a day, and Rs 1,477 cr of cumulative operating cash against Rs 267 cr of cumulative accounting losses over a decade. Q1 FY27 was the first positive quarter in the series. On the operator's own generous base — a conviction-led 25% pre-Ind AS mature ROCE and a 25.0x forward destination multiple — the story is coherent.

The problem is arithmetic, not narrative. That 25.0x on forward pre-Ind AS earnings compounds FY27's roughly Rs 6.5 of EPS to about Rs 25 by FY30 and lands a base target of Rs 635. The stock is Rs 772. The base target sits below today's price, the bull target clears only 12% a year, and the Hurdle Ratio fails even on bull earnings. Every quality flag — the Gate 0 AVOID, the promoter CAUTION, the emerging-moat NONE, the accounting-quality marks — points the same way, but none of them is the reason. The reason is that the market has already paid, at 2.4x the top of the operator's entry zone, for a recovery that has barely started and whose softest leg (gross margin) has missed three calls running while peers expanded.

So the discipline is simple. Watch the two prints that carry the whole thesis: double-digit SSSG and a 66% gross margin, held together, quarter after quarter, without the net-debt ceiling breaking. The name earns a real second look near Rs 289–325 — and only if the catalyst is still firing when it gets there. Not before.
