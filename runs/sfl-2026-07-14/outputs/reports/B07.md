### 1B. Diversification direction

- **Product**: pillows (standalone), sofa (concept, Furlenco-linked), sub-Rs10,000 SKUs.
- **Customer**: U2O (unorganized-to-organized) — a genuinely new customer tier (8,400+ dealers,
  5,000+ towns, 24 states per Q4 FY26 call/Digest), distinct distributor network from mainline.
- **Geographic**: GCC/UAE (exclusive stores across all seven Emirates, local manufacturing
  partnership, collaborations with large-format retail chains signed but not yet executed —
  "over the next three to six months", Q3 FY26 call).
- **Channel**: e-commerce (brand.com direct vs. marketplace), Furlenco integrated stores.
- **Vertical integration**: none confirmed; the GCC manufacturing move is outsourced/partnered,
  not owned capacity — NOT a vertical-integration moat in the strict B1 sense.

### 2B. Utilisation trajectory per facility

No plant-level utilisation % is disclosed in any of the four calls or the AR (consistent with
B01's input-gap note that the CSV/company does not disclose distribution-network or utilisation
metrics). Management's qualitative claim, repeated across three consecutive calls (Q1, Q3, Q4
FY26), is that **current installed capacity supports 2-2.5x present volume via debottlenecking
and efficiency capex only** — no new production capacity needed (Q1 FY26 call: "before 2029,
2030"; Q3 FY26 call, Amit Gupta: "sufficient to take us to 2X to 2.5X of our current capacity");
this is a 🎙️ MANAGEMENT CLAIM, repeated but never independently verified against a disclosed
utilisation %.

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

The standard formula (total capex under execution × historical fixed-asset turnover = implied
incremental revenue, as % above current revenue) **cannot be computed from the documents injected
into this stage**: none of the AR, the four concalls, or the investor presentation discloses a
Net Block / Gross Block figure from which a fixed-asset-turnover ratio can be derived, and B01's
own capex proxy (delta Net Block+CWIP + Depreciation) was built from a screener CSV not provided
to this stage. **NOT FOUND** — flagged as an input gap below, not estimated.

As a qualitative substitute (anchored, not treated as equivalent to the arithmetic test):
management's repeated 2-2.5x capacity-headroom claim (Q1/Q3 FY26 calls) implies capex-light
growth capacity of roughly 100-150% above current volume before major new capex is required —
this is a 🎙️ claim, not a computed number, and is not used as capex_embedded_growth_pct in the
YAML block (set to 0 / NOT FOUND per the no-estimate rule).

## SECTION 6: TIMELINE, RISKS & COMBINED ASSESSMENT

### 6A. Moat evolution timeline

- **Next 12m**: Kurlon synergy machine (malleable fibre/comfort layer, Rs40-60cr tail) installs
  and shows results — the single most-delayed, most-watched catalyst (B05 trigger 2); U2O and
  e-commerce continue scaling from small bases; GCC retail-chain collaborations execute (Q3 FY26
  call window: 3-6 months); dividend continuation decision for FY27.
- **12-24m**: Furlenco integrated stores scale toward 100 (FY27 target); India debt approaches
  zero (~1-1.5 years per Q4 FY26 call); pillow category build-out visible as a discrete line;
  Furlenco IPO/monetisation decision (flagged as already slipped once in B05).
- **24-36m**: GCC region either shows a material, disclosed revenue contribution or remains
  sub-scale; H1's industry-consolidation tailwind either proves durable (competitors permanently
  exit) or reverses as RM supply normalises — the single largest swing factor in the whole scan.
- **3-5yr**: Management's standing 15% revenue growth / 15% EBITDA margin target for the India
  business (reiterated Q2-Q4 FY26 calls) is the aggregate test of whether these emerging
