## 3B. Balance sheet — the ROCE reconciliation (COMPANY MEMORY open item)
Three DIFFERENT return-on-capital figures appear across this Annual Report and the operator's own
prior tracking, none reconciled to each other in the document:

1. **Standalone, audited, formula-disclosed (Note 57, p.168):** ROCE = -3.73% (FY26), using
   (PBT + Finance Costs + D&A − Other Income) ÷ (Total Equity + Total Borrowings). Applying this
   exact formula to the STANDALONE primary statements read this pass (PBT ₹301.86M + Finance costs
   ₹46.08M + D&A ₹117.22M − Other Income ₹1,137.23M ≈ −₹672M; Capital Employed ≈ ₹17,907M) reproduces
   -3.75%, matching the disclosed -3.73% almost exactly (small rounding gap) — CONFIRMED formula.
   The negative figure exists because the standalone parent's own trading revenue barely covers its
   operating costs; nearly all standalone profit comes from Other Income (predominantly intercompany
   interest), which this formula deliberately strips out.
2. **Management-reported, consolidated, formula NOT disclosed (Corporate Overview p.3 and MD&A
   p.31-32):** ROCE "improving to 14.60% in FY26 (from 10.7% in FY25)." This figure appears twice in
   the narrative (and in the Performance Highlights bar chart, p.26, showing a five-year climb from
   1.49% in FY22) but NO formula or footnote is given for it anywhere in the AR — the MD&A's "Key
   Ratios (Consolidated basis)" table (p.32) that DOES carry a "Reason for Variance" column and a
   RoE formula footnote does not include a ROCE row at all.
3. **Analyst-calculated, applying the audited Note 57 formula to the CONSOLIDATED primary
   statements (this stage, not company-disclosed):** using the same formula as (1) above —
   (PBT ₹1,793.36M + Finance Costs ₹543.85M + D&A ₹432.93M − Other Income ₹192.37M) ÷ (Total Equity
   ₹17,525.46M + Total Borrowings ₹5,666.83M) ≈ ₹2,577.77M ÷ ₹23,192.29M ≈ **11.1%** for FY26. This
   is well below management's own reported 14.60% and close to the operator's own back-of-envelope
   "~10.5% balance-sheet-derived" figure from COMPANY MEMORY.

**Conclusion for the COMPANY MEMORY item:** the gap between the company's press/MD&A-reported ROCE
(14.60% FY26; a still-higher 21.1% was reported for Q1 FY27 per COMPANY MEMORY, not verifiable from
this annual document) and a balance-sheet-derived figure (~10.5%-11.1%) is NOT evidence of a
calculation error found in this AR — it is evidence that the company discloses NO formula for its
own headline consolidated ROCE number, while the ONE ROCE formula it DOES disclose in full (Note 57,
standalone) is deliberately conservative and produces a NEGATIVE figure for the same company in the
same year. The two are simply not the same metric, and the AR does not bridge them. This should be
a standing question for management: what precisely is capital employed and what precisely is EBIT
in the 14.60%/21.1% figures.

## 4A. Disclosed risks — real vs boilerplate
Seven risk categories disclosed (MD&A "Risk and Concerns and Risk Management," p.33): M&A
Integration Risk, Supply Chain Disruptions, Liquidity Risk, Product Integrity, Operational Risk,
Technology Risk, People Risk. Each carries a one-line risk statement and a one-line mitigation.
M&A Integration Risk and Liquidity Risk are the two most relevant to this Company's specific
profile, but both are written in generic, hypothetical, forward-looking language ("could be
adversely affected," "may cause difficulties") even though — per Phase 1D and B02 Finding 2 — the
integration and cash-generation problems in a meaningful slice of the subsidiary base (40 of ~65
CARO-qualified, predominantly cash-loss) are ALREADY REALISED, not hypothetical. Product Integrity,
Technology Risk and People Risk read as largely boilerplate for a distribution business of this
scale (standard language, no company-specific metrics or historical incident disclosure).
