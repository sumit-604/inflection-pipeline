### 3A Cash flow — THE HEADLINE FINDING OF THIS STAGE

Standalone Cash Flow Statement (p.94 [page 97]) and Consolidated Cash
Flow Statement (p.124 [page 127]) both classify the year-on-year MOVEMENT
IN SHORT-TERM BORROWINGS inside "Changes in Working Capital," i.e. inside
CASH FLOW FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES, not inside financing activities:

- Standalone: "Short Term Borrowings" line inside the operating
  working-capital block: **+₹1,666.31 lakh FY26** (+₹1,429.37 lakh FY25).
- Consolidated: same line, same placement: **+₹1,666.92 lakh FY26**
  (+₹1,429.37 lakh FY25).

No policy note anywhere in the filing explains this classification (this
stage searched; none found, consistent with triple-pass Finding 9's
"no financial risk/policy note" gap). Under Ind AS 7, movements in
short-term borrowings are ordinarily FINANCING activity; a working-capital
revolving facility can occasionally be treated as operating only under a
narrow, disclosed exception (facility integral to day-to-day cash
management), which this filing does not claim or disclose.

This reframes the FY26 cash story completely. As reported, CFO/PAT looks
