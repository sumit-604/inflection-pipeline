## 1. INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built from transcripts only)

I graded every item before the comparison. CRITICAL = repeated evasion (2+ quarters) or a
contradiction that could change a decision. MAJOR = thesis-relevant, single-occurrence or
walkback. MINOR = tone, imprecision, low weight.

### CRITICAL

**C1. Defence financials do not reconcile. Management deflects in 2 quarters, and the CEO and CFO contradict each other on the call.**
- JUN26 [page 6]-[page 7]: CEO gives FY26 defence revenue Rs 46 Cr and EBITDA Rs 29 Cr. That is 63.0% (calc). He says "depreciation and the interest, it has increased the EBITDA." The analyst (Deepak Poddar) objects 3 times that depreciation and interest sit below EBITDA. The CFO then adds a second reason, "material lying at the port."
- JUN26 [page 19]: Shashank Kanodia notes consolidated minus standalone gives about Rs 33 Cr, not Rs 46 Cr. Cross-check (calc): consolidated total income Rs 4,100.25 Cr minus standalone sales Rs 4,067.71 Cr = Rs 32.54 Cr. Consolidated EBITDA Rs 418.49 Cr minus standalone Rs 395.80 Cr = Rs 22.69 Cr (JUN26 [page 6]). Neither figure matches Rs 46 Cr / Rs 29 Cr. Basis caveat: consolidated is on total income and standalone is on sales, so this is a rough cross-check only.
- JUN26 [page 19]-[page 20]: asked if material moves from standalone to the defence subsidiary, the CEO says "No." The CFO says "Yes, there is sir." The CEO then says "We will let you know." He closes with "these numbers, I hope, on this platform we should not discuss."
- AUG26 [page 11]-[page 12]: asked why defence is not reported as a segment, the CEO says "we have given." The CFO says "This is only 1 segment, iron and steel, even Defence sector, it falls under iron and steel segment only."
- Why it matters: defence carries the whole margin thesis (30-35% against a 3-5% legacy CR/pipe business, AUG26 [page 11]). The only audited check on it (consolidated minus standalone) did not match management's FY26 number.

**C2. The order book is never quantified, and order claims contradict each other across all 4 calls.**
- NOV25 [page 15]: "their order book is not there... here demand is not important, order is not important." NOV25 [page 17]: refuses the domestic/export split ("I will not be able to reveal").
- FEB26 [page 4] (Chairman): "eight months order in hand and two years LOI." FEB26 [page 5] (CEO), same call: "we are booked for next one year and LOIs for next two years."
- JUN26 [page 16]: "We have the orders that we can supply to for up to 2 years." JUN26 [page 18]: asked for the amount, "Our order book is healthy; I can say that." No number given.
- AUG26 [page 7]: "whatever advances we get on that basis only, we declare the orders." AUG26 [page 20]-[page 21]: "as a policy, we do not announce till we have technically and commercially clear order. This is the reason we have announced only 50000 piece order."
- Disclosed orders total Rs 307 Cr (Rs 255 Cr plus Rs 52 Cr, AUG26 [page 3]). The FY27 defence guide is Rs 300-350 Cr (AUG26 [page 5]). The claimed "2 years" of orders is not visible in any disclosed number.
- The question was asked in 4 quarters and dodged in 4 quarters. The order horizon changed from "no order book" to "8 months" to "1 year" to "2 years".

**C3. Shell pricing and unit economics are refused in every quarter.**
- NOV25 [page 12]: raw-material price, "We would not like to share."
- FEB26 [page 14]: asked for current shell pricing, "price will be decided by only the pricing needs... So don't worry about the prices." Chairman: "putting a figure of 30% is imaginary. It can be more."
- JUN26 [page 10]: "we want to avoid giving these prices." Asked about the press-reported Rs 26,100 per shell: "what the prices of this order, I'm not connected."
- AUG26 [page 19]: margin split by shell variant refused, "I still hold my guidance." AUG26 [page 17]: shell count for the quarter, "I don't remember."
- Why it matters: the Rs 255 Cr / ~50,000 shell order implies about Rs 51,000 per shell (calc, AUG26 [page 7]). The Rs 52 Cr / 20,000 shell order implies about Rs 26,000 per shell. Realisation differs by 2x across variants. The 30-35% margin guide cannot be tested without unit prices.

**C4. GDAL dilution and the IPO-versus-demerger question are deflected in 3 quarters.**
