## PART A — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built from raw transcripts)

### A1. Guidance vs delivery

1. **FY26 20% revenue-growth guidance missed; never acknowledged as a miss.**
   Given Q1 ("entire revenue would be approximately 20% higher", p3; "15 to
   20%", p17) and reiterated Q2 ("comfortably deliver our 20% revenue growth
   guidance", p3). Delivered standalone +11% to INR3,508 cr (Q4 p5),
   consolidated INR3,592.5 cr. Management reframed via steel prices "25% down"
   and a "like-to-like 36%" core-pipe number (Q4 p5, p7) rather than stating
   the top-line target was missed.

2. **FY27 revenue guidance walked down three times.** Q2: "around 7,000
   crores" (p5; India 4,500 + Saudi 2,000 + Jammu 500, p9). Q3: analyst quotes
   an earlier 6,500 cr back at management; MD gives 5,500-6,000 cr (p10). Q4:
   formal guidance 5,000-5,500 cr (p4). A ~21-29% cut across two quarters,
   presented each time as a fresh number, not a reduction.

3. **Within-call FY27 inconsistency (Q3):** "25%-30% growth" guideline vs
   "internal goal 50-55%" vs an explicit 5,500-6,000 cr, in the same call
   (p10, p13-14).

4. **EBITDA margin guidance beaten (the one clean delivery).** 11-12% (Q2 p3)
   upgraded to 13-14% (Q3 p2), delivered 13% consol / 14% standalone (Q4 p5).
   Not a red flag; recorded for balance.

### A2. Order book and bid pipeline

5. **Order book falls every quarter while each call is "best ever."**
   3,200 cr (Q1 p2) -> 4,750 cr (Q2 p2) -> 4,000 cr (Q3 p2) -> 3,000 cr (Q4
   p13). Peaks Q2, then -37%. Management did not raise the decline in any
   opening remark; only addressed when an analyst (Sandeep, Q4 p13) asked
   directly, and deflected to the bid book.

6. **Bid pipeline volatile and used as deflection.** 15,000 (Q1) -> 15,000
   (Q2) -> 11,500 (Q3 p4, analyst flags the drop p15) -> 15,000-16,000 (Q4
   p13). Each order-book question is answered by pivoting to the bid book.

### A3. Project timeline slippage

7. **Saudi Dammam greenfield: reaffirmed on-track one quarter, then scrapped.**
   Q1: commence Q3/Q4 FY26, "completed by FY26" (p2). Q2: Q4 CY2026 (p3). Q3:
   "advancing as planned... completed by Q1 FY27", trials in Q1, 50-60%
   utilisation, 1,500-2,000 cr revenue (p2, p7). Q4: "scrapped because of the
   NPC acquisition" (Yash Mehta, p13). A near-complete plant with live trials
   in Feb became abandoned by May; the FY27 Saudi revenue number simply
   transferred to NPC.

8. **Jammu SS plant slipped ~2 years.** Q1: "up and running by FY26", Q4 hot
   trials (p2-3, p9). Q2: "start production from 1st April 2026. That is our
   target date" (p3). Q3: "slightly delayed", war + flooding blamed (p13, p15);
   FY27 Jammu contribution quietly fell 500 cr (Q2 p9) -> ~300 cr (Q3 p13). Q4:
   first-year utilisation 35-40% in FY28 (p6) — FY27 contribution now ~zero.

9. **Merino Shelters launch slipped and quantum keeps moving.** Q1: launch
   Sep/Oct 2025, ~70-80 cr this year, 700-800 cr total over 5 yrs (p4, p15).
   Q2: not launched, "700 crores... next five years" (p15-16). Q3: "March 1st
   week" launch, 600-700 cr = 30% of collection over 6-7 yrs (p14). Q4:
   commencement certificate received, revenue starts Q2 FY27, 800-900 cr over
   7 yrs (p7). The FY26 70-80 cr contribution implied in Q1 never came.
   Aggressive "no cost, everything to the bottom line" framing throughout
   (Q3 p5; Q4 p18).

### A4. SEBI matter

10. **SEBI stay order disclosed once, terse, never volunteered.** Only mention
    across four calls: Q2 p9, after an analyst had to redirect from an
    order-book question — "SEBI order, we got the stay order... now the matter
    is sub judice." No substance on what the order concerned; never revisited
    in Q3 or Q4.

### A5. Evasion patterns

11. **Volume/tonnage refused in all four quarters (repeated evasion).** Q1:
    "We don't disclose that number" (p5), "We share those details
    independently" (p7). Q2: Chairman "your question is not clear", answers only
    in % (p6-7), "Tonnage is very difficult" (p7). Q3: pricing-vs-volume split
    "We can share the data with you" — never given (p11-12). Q4: NPC CY25
    volume — "send an e-mail across to Sandeep Garg" (p15); another volume
    question cut off on audio (p11). Four straight quarters of deferral limits
    any check on whether margin gains are price, mix, or volume.

12. **Merino quantum evasion.** See item 9; the total, the horizon, and the
    per-year figure differ in every call.

### A6. NPC acquisition

13. **"Too cheap" claim with unusual analyst insistence.** 1.5x EV/EBITDA vs
    peers 7-10x; $102 m including $83 m cash (Q4 p2-3). Three separate analysts
    pushed on why so cheap / distressed / fire sale (Viraj p8, Divyansh p9,
    Viraj again p18). Relationship narrative (Nippon/Sumitomo exiting steel)
    offered; not verifiable from the call.

14. **NPC key management "mostly changed" post-deal — integration/key-person
    risk on the very Aramco relationship the thesis rests on.** Disclosed only
    on a direct question (Q4 p19), absent from the long promotional opening.

15. **NPC margin step-down not flagged.** Acquisition press release cited
    20-25% EBITDA (analyst Sahil, Q4 p16); management now guides NPC/KSA
    sustainable 15-18% (p3) / 15%+ FY27 (p7), attributing the higher historic
    figure to a one-off Aramco order book. A real downshift in the number the
    deal was sold on.

### A7. Consolidated-profit optics

16. **Unhedged forex MTM (~INR25 cr) dragging consolidated EBITDA; management
    coached by an analyst to add an "extraordinary note."** Q4 p5, p14-15.
    Consol PAT (171 cr) came in BELOW standalone PAT (196 cr); subsidiaries a
    net drag. Management confirmed subsidiary-level hedging "is not allowed" —
    a structural gap that recurs while capex continues.

17. **Other-income add-back inflates the "highest ever margin" story (Q1).**
    An analyst noted operating margin ex-other-income fell 10% -> 7% QoQ (Q1
    p11); management insisted other income (incentives, forex) is operational
    and must be added into EBITDA. The record-margin narrative leans partly on
    this add-back.

### A8. Peer contradictions

18. **Order-book decline is company-specific.** MANINDS -37% over three
    quarters while Jindal SAW (19.25 lakh tons, "highest in so many years"),
    Welspun (record 23,500 -> 25,750 cr) and Ratnamani (flat ~2,000-2,160 cr)
    all held stable-to-record books through the same window, while confirming
    the same demand headwinds. (B06 Q6.)

19. **Saudi LSAW/HSAW oversupply risk understated.** MANINDS dismissed
    overcapacity for "the next 10 to 12 years" (Q1 p14). Jindal SAW is adding
    300k+300k MTPA in a Saudi JV (FY29) and Welspun is bringing its own Saudi
    LSAW plant onstream FY2027 — the same window. (B06 Q2.)

20. **Welspun scouted and passed on what is very likely the NPC asset, on
    quality grounds, not price** (WELCORP Jul 2026 p12-13; B06 Q5) —
    complicates the "cheap because the market missed it" framing.

21. **Hormuz disruption under-disclosed vs peers.** MANINDS: only 20-25% of
    shipments toward Hormuz affected, "we are not seeing a decline", "Q1 is
    quite strong" (Q4 p16, p18). Jindal SAW: "all outward shipments... suspended
    since March 2026" (Jul 2026 p4). If both carry MENA export exposure, the
    "no decline / Q1 strong" claim sits awkwardly against a peer reporting a
    full MENA shipment halt.

### A9. Numeric framing note

22. **Consolidated revenue was essentially FLAT YoY, starker than a "+13%"
    read.** FY25 consol included ~INR369 cr of one-off Merino real-estate
    revenue (Q4 p5). Adding that to the FY25 pipe base (~3,178 cr, Q2 p14) puts
    FY25 consol near ~3,547 cr, so FY26 consol 3,592.5 cr is roughly flat, not
    +13% off a pipe-only base. The FY26 revenue miss vs the 20% guidance is
    therefore even larger than a pipe-only comparison implies.
