The transition evidence concentrates in ownership and process, not in the RPT structure itself. Flag is unmissable; the decision stays with the operator.

## FLAG-CASH: INDETERMINATE

⚠️ CASH CONVERSION FLAG: operating cash flow negative in 5 of 6 years to FY25 (cumulative CFO minus Rs 79.95 cr), negative at both entities in FY25 despite the profit turnaround, FY26 consolidated positive while standalone worsened.

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

Evidence:
- Cumulative CFO minus Rs 79.95 cr against cumulative PAT Rs 90.00 cr, FY21-FY26 (deal-breaker #4, B01); five of six years CFO-negative despite four of six years nominally profitable (B01 block_b_trend).
- FY25 CFO negative at both levels despite profit: standalone minus Rs 5.81 cr (PBT plus Rs 7.62 cr), consolidated minus Rs 18.53 cr (PBT plus Rs 18.01 cr); consolidated working-capital consumption of Rs 60.38 cr and receivables absorption of Rs 33.65 cr drove it, funded by Rs 32.40 cr of fresh borrowings (Cash Flow Statements AR p.79 / p.122; B02, B03).
- FY26 consolidated CFO turned positive at plus Rs 16.25 cr, but standalone CFO worsened to minus Rs 7.59 cr from minus Rs 5.81 cr, and no source explains the divergence (B05 promise_delivery; RATING p.2).
- Capex near zero (capex/depreciation 0.06-0.07x FY25), so the cash drain is working capital, not investment; this is not a capex-embedded growth story (B03; B04).
- A material slice of receivables is related-party and excluded from bank drawing power: subsidiary drawing-power reconciliation gap up to Rs 18.75 cr at March-25, related-party receivables up to 49.2% of consolidated (CARO Annexure A p.68-69; Note 19/33 consolidated p.156-157; B02).

Why INDETERMINATE, not GROWTH-INDUCED and not STRUCTURAL: the growth reading is undercut because capex is near zero, so the drain is a working-capital build, not growth investment, and that build is dominated by related-party receivables that are excluded from bank collateral, so they cannot be assumed to unwind like arm's-length trade receivables. The structural reading is not yet provable either, because FY26 consolidated CFO did turn positive for the first time. One year of positive consolidated cash flow, set against a standalone figure that worsened in the same year with no explanation, is not enough to call the question either way.

Missing evidence that would resolve it (name and source): (1) the standalone-versus-consolidated FY26 CFO divergence explanation, absent from every source read and obtainable from the FY26 Annual Report MD&A / Directors' Report when filed; (2) the FY26 receivables ageing schedule and the subsidiary bank drawing-power reconciliation, from the next AR Notes 10/11 and CARO clause ii(b); (3) an updated Infomerics rating rationale assessing the FY26 audited cash flows, none post-March-2026 being in the run folder. INDETERMINATE does not resolve to PROCEED; it caps at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS, and the flag stays open until the standalone CFO turns positive alongside the consolidated for two consecutive periods.

Resolving quarterly metric to watch: standalone operating cash flow. A second consecutive negative standalone CFO in FY27, or a consolidated CFO reverting negative, would move the determination toward STRUCTURAL; standalone turning positive alongside consolidated for two straight periods would move it toward GROWTH-INDUCED resolving.

## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring, B06)

1. "31% FY25 capacity utilisation on the newly expanded 24,300 MTPA base is an industry-typical ramp curve" — CONTRADICTED by Emmbi and Kanpur Plastipack, both running FIBC capacity at 75-90% including during active expansion (Emmbi Q1 FY22 call, 12 Aug 2021, p.3: "capacity utilization was up to 87%"; Kanpur Q1 FY26 call, 19 Aug 2025, p.15: "capacity utilization for the last financial year was about 75%"; Kanpur Q4/FY26 call, 4 May 2026, p.5/17: "~83-85%"). HPBL's low utilisation reads as company-specific execution lag, not sector ramp.
2. "'No major capex FY27-28' reflects an industry-wide capex pause" — CONTRADICTED by Kanpur Plastipack (Q2 FY26 call, 13 Nov 2025, p.4: "We have also planned a capex of INR105 crores for capacity expansion ... over the next 12-18 months"). HPBL is the lone capital-conservative operator in its comparison set, not part of a sector pause.

## Falsification line

The single next-quarter print that would do the most damage: standalone operating cash flow staying negative for a second consecutive year in FY27 while consolidated CFO also reverts negative. That would confirm the FY26 consolidated positive was a one-off working-capital timing swing rather than durable conversion, and move the cash determination from INDETERMINATE to STRUCTURAL.
