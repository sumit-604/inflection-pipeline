8. Plant level utilisation disclosure and the Vapi shutdown. Watch for a Pakhajan vs Vapi split and a reconciliation of "we have shut Vapi" against "Vapi 95 to 98%". Concall. Tests whether the ramp is a Pakhajan only story.
