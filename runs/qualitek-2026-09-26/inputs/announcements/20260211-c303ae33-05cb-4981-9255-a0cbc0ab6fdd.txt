observation raised by the Exchange regarding submission of additional details under SEBI Circular No.

In this regard, the Company hereby submits the following clarification:


Mr. Mayank Garg (ACS: 76038) had tendered his resignation from the post of Company Secretary &
Compliance Officer (Key Managerial Personnel) vide letter dated 21 November 2025. The resignation
was duly considered and accepted by Mr. Antaryami Nayak, Managing Director of the Company on 07
February 2026, and upon mutual understanding, early relieving was granted on the same date, as

Annexure B respectively.


Accordingly, Mr. Mayank Garg ceased to be the Company Secretary & Compliance Officer with effect
from the close of business hours on 07 February 2026, which constitutes the date of occurrence of the
material event for the purposes of Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations.


The effective date of cessation, i.e., 07 February 2026, fell on a Saturday (non-working day). Therefore,
the disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI LODR Regulations was made to the Exchange on the next
working day, i.e., 09 February 2026, after completion of internal procedural formalities.


There are no material reasons for the resignation other than those stated above. Mr. Mayank Garg does
not hold any shares in the Company and is not related to any Director of the Company.


The Company submits the above clarification and additional details in compliance with Regulation 30 of
the SEBI LODR Regulations read with the aforesaid SEBI Circular and respectfully requests the Exchange
to kindly take the same on record.
