Dear Sir/ Madam, 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (hereinafter referred to 
as the “SEBI LODR Regulations”) and in continuation to our intimation no. 
20/SE/CS/NOV/2025-26 dated November 24, 2025 relating to the proposed Scheme of 
Arrangement between the Promoter Company and its Identified Shareholders, we hereby 
inform that, ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited (“the Bank”) has received a further 
communication today from the Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”).  
 
Through the said communication RBI has informed that the revised Scheme of Arrangement 
submitted by the Promoter Company has been taken on record, subject to compliance with 
applicable statutes, regulations and guidelines. 
 
The Bank reiterates that it is not a party to the Scheme of Arrangement, which is proposed 
between the Promoter Company and its identified shareholders. As the Scheme and the 
related regulatory communications have a bearing on the promoter shareholding and dilution 
milestones of the Bank, the above communication from the RBI is being disclosed in 
continuation of the earlier disclosures made by the Bank, in accordance with the requirements 
of the SEBI LODR Regulations.
