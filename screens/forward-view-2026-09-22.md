# Forward view — 2026-09-22 shallow screen, fourteen names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework, per the operator ruling of
2026-09-21. Next year's bottom line, the growth rate, and the forward
price-to-earnings ratio, so the run can be ranked on growth against multiple.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result, not a conclusion. Two names below screen well
and are flagged, and one screens so badly that the flag runs the other way.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the Bull AI company record at 22 September 2026. It is
a service record, not a filed document. Every earnings figure traces to a filing
or a company presentation, cited in its card.

**One market capitalisation needs live verification before it is used.**
INDOMIM's service record of Rs 57,537.80 crore implies about Rs 1,192 per share
against an IPO price of Rs 485 on 30 July 2026, a 2.46-fold rise in under two
months. Its card's PASS verdict rests on that number. See the note below.

## Full-page verification pass, 2026-09-22

The four names carrying live verdicts, QLL, AVIENCE, OPTIEMUS and METALIC, were
re-read at full-page level after this table was built. **No forward PE in the
table changes.** Three things behind them do.

- **QLL.** The FY27 guidance is stronger evidence than the table implied. It is
  not a step-change from a 12% full-year PAT margin; it is the delivered H2 FY26
  exit run-rate of 14.1% carried forward. Against that, consolidated net debt to
  EBITDA is **about 3.0x**, newly found, which fires a tripwire the card itself
  set at 2.5x. FY26 PAT is precisely Rs 14.66 crore, so growth reads +75% to
  +99%. EBITDA margin guidance is 25-27%, not 25-26%.
- **OPTIEMUS.** The growth is measured off a declining base. **FY26 revenue fell
  6.4%**, from Rs 1,890.00 crore to Rs 1,768.62 crore, and FY26 PAT grew only
  4.2%. Separately, Rs 14.44 crore of FY26's Rs 90.67 crore pre-tax profit was
  share of associate profit, not operations, and two company decks disagree on
  FY26 EBITDA by that same Rs 14.44 crore. The forward multiple of 50x to 60x is
  unchanged and now rests on a weaker base than the table suggested.
- **AVIENCE.** The 60% growth guidance is confirmed as a direct, attributed
  quote from the Managing Director, which firms the tier A rating. But the
  288-product target is 88 approvals plus **200 applications yet to be filed**,
  not 288 approvals.

## Ranked on growth against multiple

**The two that screen best, and why each needs its card read.**

**AVIENCE, 12.8x on 60% growth**, is the best ratio in the run and the least
supported. The revenue leg is tier A, management's own dated 60% target with a
named Rs 47.46 crore order behind it. The margin leg is not the company's: it
holds the FY26 PAT margin of 16.7% flat, which is my assumption and labelled as
one. The company listed on 25 June 2026 and **has filed no post-listing result
of any kind**, so the proof gate cannot even be tested. Hold revenue growth at
the delivered FY26 rate of 16.1% instead and the forward PE is 17.6x on 16%
growth, which is an ordinary screen result. Its card is a WATCH, not a PROCEED,
for exactly this reason.

**QLL, 22.4x to 25.4x on 76% to 99% growth**, is the run's strongest
combination of cheapness, growth and evidence. Both legs are tier A: management
guides 35-40% revenue growth and a 15-17% PAT margin, with a date. The catch is
that the margin guidance does all the work. PAT margin today is 12%, and the
guidance asks for a 300 to 500 basis point step in one year. **Hold the FY26
margin flat and the forward PE is 32x to 33x on 35% to 40% growth**, which is
fair rather than cheap. The balance sheet is the other reason to read the card:
borrowings rose Rs 36.77 crore in FY26 and another Rs 63 crore of capex is
planned against Rs 14.6 crore of profit.

**The one that screens well and is correctly cheap.** ISFT at 10.2x is the
lowest absolute multiple in the run. It is cheap because it is an Amazon
reseller with a 3.5% operating margin, no brand, one platform and one country,
whose revenue is lower than it was six years ago. Its card classifies the
ugliness STRUCTURAL and the posture VALUE-TRAP RISK. This is the cheap multiple
on a permanent problem that step 11 exists to catch.

**The one whose flag runs backwards.** TIRUPATIFL at 189.7x on **negative 23%
growth** is the worst combination in the run and needs no interpretation. FY26
revenue rose 41% and net profit fell 20%. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 18% and net
profit fell 14%. Two consecutive periods of rising revenue and falling profit,
with 40% of FY26 pre-tax profit coming from foreign-exchange gains, and
37,00,000 further warrants approved to promoters in July 2026. There is no
reading of the cards that rescues it.

**The quality name that price disqualifies.** INDOMIM is the best business
screened in this run: 28% EBITDA margin, 23.5% ROCE, NADCAP and AS 9100 and ISO
13485 certifications, falling customer concentration, a domestic market opening.
At 85.6x to 89.9x forward it is the EARNINGS-ONLY cell of the decision matrix,
where the climb is real and entirely priced, and 26% earnings growth does not
carry that multiple against a 25% compound target. **Verify the market
capitalisation on live web first.** At the IPO price of Rs 485 the trailing
multiple would be 43.9x, which is expensive but arguable, and the verdict would
need revisiting.

## Four blanks, and why each is blank rather than filled

The framework's tier D rule is that where no forward basis exists, the forward
PE is left blank and not filled. Four names qualify, for three different reasons.

- **TANAA.** Zero guidance records, no presentation, no earnings call, latest
  period six months stale, and **FY26 profit after tax itself is NOT FOUND**;
  only pre-tax profit of Rs 22.51 crore was located. Revenue fell 1.1% in FY26
  and Q4 revenue fell 6.8%, so tier C has no positive trend to extrapolate.
- **DICIND.** Zero guidance records, latest period nine months stale, and the
  delivered trend points down: revenue compounded at about 4.6% a year over four
  years and profit fell 11.1% in CY2025. A brand-new parent-nominated Managing
  Director took office on 1 September 2026 and the first period under him is
  unreported.
- **STEAMHOUSE.** The corpus is empty. Bull AI returned a complete, untruncated
  document list containing nothing, five days after listing. There is no
  delivered year, no quarter and no trend, so tiers A, B and C all fail for the
  same reason.
- **CRESTO and PCS.** Neither company resolved in the Bull AI index at all, by
  name or by BSE code. No card was written for either.

**STLNETWORK is NOT APPLICABLE rather than blank**, per the framework's third
prohibition: no forward PE is computed for a loss-making company. Its card names
the gate to profitability instead and sizes the three routes to it. FY26
consolidated EBITDA was Rs 47.85 crore against finance costs of Rs 116.73 crore,
so interest is 2.4 times operating profit and the pre-tax loss was Rs 106.15
crore. No single route closes that gap.

## Guidance coverage, recorded as a finding

Bull AI guidance calls were run on eight names. **Five returned zero records:
TIRUPATIFL, INDOMIM, STLNETWORK, DICIND and TANAA.** Two returned rich, dated,
quantified guidance: OPTIEMUS and QLL. One, ISFT, returned only a catalogue
expansion target with no rupee figure and no date.

That distribution is the run's clearest signal about disclosure quality, and it
tracks the verdicts almost exactly. The two names with real guidance are the two
with the most testable theses. The five with none are four PASS verdicts and one
WATCH.

AVIENCE's tier A guidance came from its investor presentation rather than a
guidance call, so it is nine names with a forward statement of some kind out of
twelve screened.

## The three prohibitions, held

No target price appears anywhere in this run. No buy or sell instruction appears
anywhere. No forward PE was computed for the loss-making name. The forward
multiples above are screening metrics and are not valuations: Section 1B governs
exit multiples and none was derived here.
