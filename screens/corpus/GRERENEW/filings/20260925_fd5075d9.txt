ANNEXURE 
Summary of Proceedings of The 18th Annual General Meeting of GRE Renew Enertech Limited 
The 18th Annual General Meeting of the Shareholders of the company (‘Members’) was held on Friday, 25th 
September, 2026 at 4:30 P.M. at Hotel Shivalik, Nugar, Iskcon Circle, Opp. Essar Petrol Pump, Bypass 
Highway, Mehsana – 384002, Gujarat. 
Mr. Kamleshkumar Dahyalal Patel, Managing Director of the Company, Chaired the meeting. 
Following Directors of the Company were present in the Annual General Meeting: 
• Mr. Kamleshkumar D. Patel – Promoter and Managing Director 
• Mr. Mukeshkumar P. Trivedi – Promoter and Whole Time Director 
• Mr. Lokesh Dave – Independent Director and Chairperson of Audit Committee 
• Mr. Mehul Rajput – Independent Director  
 
The following officials/invitee also attended the Annual General Meeting: 
• Mr. Alpesh Agarwal, former Chief Financial Officer of the Company 
• Mr. Rohan Dhruve, Company Secretary of the Company 
• Mr. Varun Pandya - representing M/s. Dhiren H. Pandya & Associates LLP, former Statutory Auditor of the 
Company, and 
• Mr. Vivek Vakharia -representing M/s. Vivek J. Vakharia & Associates, Secretarial Auditor of the Company. 
 
The attendance records reflected the presence of the requisite quorum throughout the Annual General 
Meeting. A total of 8 Members attended the Annual General Meeting, including 2 Promoter Members. 
 
All the requisite Statutory Registers and other necessary documents, as required under the applicable 
provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 were available for inspection by the members during the Annual 
General Meeting. 
Pursuant to the provisions of section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 20 of the Companies 
(Management and Administration) Rules, 2014 and Regulation 44 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and 
Disclosures Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the Company had provided remote e-voting facility to the 
members entitled to vote as on cut-off date to cast their votes electronically on all resolutions set out in the 
notice of the Annual General Meeting. Remote e-voting commenced on Tuesday, 22nd September 2026 at 
09:00 A.M. (IST) and concluded on Thursday, 24th September 2026 at 05:00 P.M. (IST). 
M/s. Vivek J. Vakharia & Associates, Practising Company Secretaries, was appointed as the Scrutinizer to 
scrutinize the remote e-Voting and the voting conducted by Poll at the Annual General Meeting and to submit 
the report thereon in accordance with the applicable provision. 
Upon confirmation by the Company Secretary that the requisite quorum was present, the Chairperson called 
the Meeting to order and welcomed the Members. The Chairperson thereafter delivered his address to the 
Members. 
The Notice convening the 18th Annual General Meeting, together with the audited Standalone and 
Consolidated Financial Statements for the financial year ended 31st March 2026, the Board’s Report, 
Statutory Auditors’ Reports and other accompanying documents, having been circulated to the Members 
within the prescribed time, were taken as read with the consent of the Members.

The Members who had not already voted through remote e-Voting and were entitled to vote were requested 
to record their assent or dissent on the Polling Paper and deposit the same in the Poll Collection Box provided 
for the purpose. 
After allowing reasonable time for the Members to complete and deposit their Polling Papers, the 
Chairperson declared the Poll closed and requested the Scrutinizer to collect and scrutinize the Polling 
Papers and consolidate the votes cast through Poll with the votes received through remote e-Voting. 
There being no other business to transact, the Chairperson thanked the Members, Directors, Auditors, 
Scrutinizer, Key Managerial Personnel and all other persons present for their participation and support and 
declared the 18th Annual General Meeting concluded at 5:30 P.M. (IST). 
The combined voting results of the remote e-Voting and voting by Poll at the Annual General Meeting, 
together with the Consolidated Scrutinizer’s Report, shall be submitted to BSE Limited within two working 
days of the conclusion of the Annual General Meeting in accordance with Regulation 44(3) of the SEBI LODR 
and shall be placed on the website of the Company. 
The Summary of the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting shall also be made available on the website 
of the Company.
