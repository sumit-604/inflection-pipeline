# J-CURVE TRACKER — Venus Pipes (Transition Inflection Monitor)

Purpose: Venus is mid-capex. Profit and ROCE are depressed while depreciation and
interest run ahead of volume; the payoff is meant to arrive FY28 as capacity fills
and higher-margin fittings/spooling kick in. Every capex story gives you the dip;
only some complete the J - the rest flatten into an L. This tracker separates the
two BEFORE FY28.

Reframe: the J is in the BOTTOM LINE and RETURNS, not the top line. Revenue slopes
up smoothly (+16% now, ~20% guided) either way. Watch PAT growth, margin, ROCE.

Scoring: 4-5 GREEN = J confirmed, move toward buying in the entry zone.
2-3 GREEN = mixed, hold watchlist, re-gate to Q3. 0-1 GREEN = L-curve risk rising.

## Five proof-points (thresholds for Q2 FY27)

1. Capacity fills. Now: seamless 85-90%, welded ~60%, new lines commissioned end-May.
   GREEN: welded rising toward 65%+ AND new seamless lines adding revenue.
   RED: welded stuck <=60%; new capacity idle; seamless flat.

2. Value-added products carry margin (THE swing factor). Now: no fittings revenue
   yet; spooling not live till Q3; blended op-EBITDA margin 16.08%.
   GREEN: real fittings revenue (>=3-5% of Q2 topline) AND blended margin >=16.5%.
   RED: fittings "approvals underway"/negligible AND margin <=16.1%.

3. Debt doesn't eat the payoff (biggest L-risk). Now: net debt ~250-280cr, gross
   ~325cr; finance cost ~1.5-2x depreciation; cash flow INDETERMINATE.
   GREEN: net debt flat/down AND H1 CFO/PAT >=1.0x AND interest/depreciation not widening.
   RED: net debt >300cr AND H1 CFO/PAT <1.0x.

4. Growth is real volume, not steel price. Now: volume >7% vs revenue +16% (~half price).
   GREEN: volume >=12% YoY AND EBITDA/kg holding/rising even as nickel/steel softens.
   RED: volume <=7% again AND growth still steel-price-led.

5. ROCE inflection (master signal). Now: CWIP-inclusive ROCE ~22.9% (reported 27%
   excludes CWIP); CWIP 123.7cr, nearly doubled.
   GREEN: CWIP-inclusive ROCE rising toward 24-25% as CWIP capitalises.
   RED: CWIP-inclusive ROCE <=23% or falling; CWIP keeps growing without capitalising.
