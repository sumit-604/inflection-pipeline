## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Minda Corp is the flagship of the Spark Minda group. It makes locksets and vehicle access systems, wiring harnesses, instrument clusters and die castings for Indian vehicle makers. FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 6,185 crore from 42 plants (Q2 deck, page 3). India gave 89% of Q2 FY27 revenue (Q2 deck, page 9). It also owns 49% of Flash Electronics, an EV powertrain electronics maker. Flash earned Rs 137 crore PAT on Rs 1,803 crore revenue in FY26 (Q2 deck, page 10). The money comes from content per vehicle times Indian vehicle volume.

**What is changing?** The company wants to move from single parts to systems. It has added a sunroof line, Minda Vast consolidation, a Turntide motor-controller JV and a Toyodenso switches JV. EV products were 10% of revenue in Q2 FY27, up 113% year on year (Q2 deck, page 5).

**Why now?** Q2 FY27 revenue was Rs 2,189 crore, up 43%. Normalised PAT was Rs 143 crore, up 69% (Q2 deck, page 17). Industry growth was 18% (Q2 deck, page 5).

**What must be true?** The EBITDA margin must climb from 11.6% to the 12.5% Vision 2030 target. Growth must stay organic once Minda Vast laps its first year.

**What breaks it?** OEM bargaining power. CRISIL expects margins "stable around 11.5%" over the medium term (`credit-ratings/03-CRISIL.txt`). If CRISIL is right, the stock carries a systems multiple on a component-supplier margin.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Two management verticals: Mechatronics and Aftermarket, and Information and Connected Systems. Both grew 38% to 46% in Q2 FY27 (Q2 deck, page 8). The statutory filings report one segment, auto components (Q2 results, page 19, note 3). Product mix in Q2 FY27, on my reading of the chart: wiring harness 31%, vehicle access 24%, die casting 15%, clusters 15%, others 15% (Q2 deck, page 9). Gross margin has held at 35% to 38% for five years (Q2 deck, page 18). The engine is content per vehicle on OEM platforms won by design.

**Archetype: Build-to-spec component maker.** Design-win pipeline, content per unit and input pass-through are the variables. Flash is an associate and needs separate treatment in any valuation.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Present, narrow, and not pricing power. CRISIL calls the group one of the largest Indian makers of two-wheeler security systems and a leader in two-, three-wheeler and CV harnesses (`03-CRISIL.txt`). Spec-in and OEM validation create switching costs. The lifetime order book is Rs 10,000 crore over 48 to 60 months (May call, page 18). Pricing power is absent. Commodity pass-through runs with a one to two quarter lag (Aug call, page 14). The top three customers gave about 35% of 9MFY26 revenue (`03-CRISIL.txt`).

## 4. The promoters

The Minda family. Ashok Minda is Chairman and Group CEO. Aakash Minda is Executive Director (Aug call, page 2). At 31 March 2026 the holders were Ashok Minda 34.2%, Minda Capital 16.1%, Sarika Minda 14.0% and Whiteline Barter 0.55%, so 64.85% in total (AR26, page 266). Holdings did not change in FY26.

Skin in the game is rising. Minda Capital, a promoter company, holds 76,50,000 warrants at Rs 550. It has paid 25%, Rs 105.2 crore (AR26, page 106). The 18-month exercise window from 2 June 2025 closes about 2 December 2026. The balance is about Rs 315.6 crore.

Pledge: NOT FOUND. I looked in AR26 pages 265 to 266, Q2 deck page 20 and `Financials.txt`.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R3 value-added, spec'd supplier: ROCE in the low 20s, partial pricing power. TO R4 share-of-wallet systems leader: kit value doubled, ROCE above 25%.

Management's claim: "From component supply to systems-led, higher-content mobility platforms" (AR26, page 61). The Vision 2030 targets are revenue of Rs 17,500 crore or more, EBITDA margin above 12.5%, net debt to equity of 0.3x and ROCE above 25% (AR26, page 84).

The kit-value mechanism is stated. A Minda-only e-2W kit is Rs 12,000 to 15,000. With Flash it is Rs 35,000 to 40,000 (AR26, page 64). Two of the four targets show no movement yet.

## 8. Proof check

Growth triggers are proved. Q1 and Q2 FY27 revenue grew 33% and 43% against industry growth of 22% and 18% (Aug call, page 4; Q2 deck, page 5). Harness grew more than 30% and clusters more than 35% in Q1 (Aug call, page 9). That shows share-of-business gains.

The climb is not proved. EBITDA margin was 11.5% and 11.8% in the two quarters. Q4 FY26 was 11.9% (May call, page 5). ROCE held at 23.1% from March to September 2026 (Q2 deck, page 11). The proof point for R4 is a margin above 12% held for two quarters. It is absent.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Vision 2030 base conflict.** AR26 page 84 sets Rs 17,500 crore against the FY25 consolidated base of Rs 5,056 crore: "3.5x growth; ~28% revenue CAGR". On the May call the target is group level, includes Flash and Vast, and needs M&A (May call, page 19). The two documents describe different targets.
- **ROCE conflict.** The company reports 23.1% excluding investments (Q2 deck, page 11). CRISIL puts FY26 ROCE at about 13% to 14% (`03-CRISIL.txt`). The Flash purchase sits in the gap.
- **Associate share of PAT.** Share of associates and JVs was Rs 81 crore of Rs 358 crore FY26 PAT, 23% (Q2 deck, page 18). That profit is booked, not received in cash.
- **Debt rose in H1.** Gross debt went from Rs 1,212 to 1,432 crore. Short-term debt went from Rs 669 to 962 crore (Q2 deck, page 11). A September 2026 NCD raise appears on BSE and is not in the corpus.
- **Non-cash gain.** Q1 FY27 carried a Rs 124 crore Ind AS 110 remeasurement gain on Minda Vast (Q2 results, page 19).
- **Fire.** A Noida plant burned on 30 May 2026. Rs 105.36 crore of loss is booked against an insurance receivable. Rs 60 crore has come in so far (Q2 results, page 19).
- **Related party.** A building worth Rs 33.7 crore came from Minda Capital. Title was registered only after the year end (AR26, page 225).
- **Auditor.** The only key audit matter is revenue recognition (AR26, page 305). No emphasis of matter was found.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

CRISIL upgraded the long-term rating to AA/Stable from AA-/Positive on 24 April 2026. The reasons are scale, kit value and the Flash synergies (`03-CRISIL.txt`). The agency underwrites "stable growth rate of 10-12%" and a margin "stable around 11.5%". It expects gearing of 0.52x by FY27. The downgrade trigger is net debt to EBITDA above 2x. The 9 June 2026 bulletin kept the rating after the fire. The plant gives 4% to 5% of group production and is insured (`02-CRISIL.txt`). India Ratings is IND AA/Stable (Q2 deck, page 11). Its rationale is not held.

The agency agrees on sustainability and leverage. It does not underwrite the transition: its margin view is flat.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Growth is delivered. The R3 to R4 climb is not visible in margin or ROCE.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** The fire is insured. The Vast gain is an accounting event. Debt is within CRISIL's triggers.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Forward PE is 29.7x to 35.2x (step 13). That is above the R4 neighbourhood of about 21x.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** This label means the systems climb is already priced before margins prove it. It does not mean the business is weak.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** The quality is real: AA rating, 1.9x cash conversion, share gains. The multiple has already paid for Vision 2030. A `/step1` now would mostly confirm a good company at a full price.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. Minda Vast revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27. These split the 43% into organic growth and consolidation.
2. Whether the Vision 2030 Rs 17,500 crore is consolidated or group, and how much of it is M&A.
3. The September 2026 NCD and October 2026 acquisition filings.
4. Exercise of the warrant balance by about 2 December 2026.

**What would change the view**
- Two quarters at or above 12.3% EBITDA margin, with organic growth above 25%.
- A price fall that brings the forward PE near the low 20s.
- A margin back at 11% or below. That confirms CRISIL's flat view.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. The verdict decides only whether the name earns a `/step1`. It is not a position.
