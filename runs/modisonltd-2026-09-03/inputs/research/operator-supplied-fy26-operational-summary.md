## FY26 (year ended 31 Mar 2026) — operator digest
- Revenue from operations ~Rs 710.3 cr (FY25 Rs 490.2 cr), ~+45% YoY.
- Single operating segment: "Manufacturing of Electrical Contacts"; no product-wise split.
- India revenue Rs 630.8 cr vs Rs 415.5 cr (~+52%); Outside India Rs 85.2 cr vs Rs 78.0 cr (~+9%).
- Export turnover (FOB incl rupee exports) Rs 82.16 cr vs Rs 77.67 cr (+5.8%).
- EBITDA margin 9.6% -> 16.1%; PAT nearly tripled.

### Working capital / balance sheet (load-bearing: Spear fact "CFO negative 3yrs, debt 174 Cr")
- Inventories Rs 219.8 cr vs Rs 127.5 cr (large WIP build-up).
- Trade receivables Rs 160.0 cr vs Rs 86.1 cr.
- NEGATIVE operating cash flow FY26 despite high PAT.
- Total borrowings Rs 174.5 cr vs Rs 72.8 cr, incl. new Working Capital Demand Loan Rs 99 cr.
- Management frames this as capacity/scale-up, not distress. VERIFY 3-yr CFO trend in CF statement/CSVs.

### Capex / expansion
- FY26 capex (PPE + intangibles + CWIP) ~Rs 15.1 cr (similar to FY25).
- PPE additions Rs 13.3 cr (plant & machinery incl SF6-related plant, factory building).
- CWIP Rs 3.5 cr at year end.
- Board sought to raise borrowing limit Rs 300 cr -> Rs 500 cr ("accelerating plans to expand
  and scale up capacity"). Clearest formal near-term expansion signal.

### R&D / technology
- R&D spend FY26 Rs 1.59 cr. Developments: contact brazing/soldering for bimetal rivets on
  copper carrier; tungsten recovery from CuW scrap; Ag+Cu+Ag cladded contacts; AgSnO2 contacts;
  ISRO import-substitute supplier for brazing alloy foils (Russian standard).

- FY26 hedging-contract profit ~Rs 2.61 cr, in exceptional items.
- Foreign exchange earned Rs 90.15 cr; FX outgo Rs 54.51 cr; net exporter, natural hedge + forwards.
