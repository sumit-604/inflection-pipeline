## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from transcripts alone)

**F1 — The maiden PAT-positive quarter (Q3 FY26) is propped by non-operating other income, not operations.**
Q3 reported PBT +INR 2.04 Cr and PAT +INR 2.71 Cr (Jan call, Kunal Karan, p.5), but other income
jumped to INR 9.72 Cr from INR 5.16 Cr. Rahul Jain (Dolat) forced the admission that "a major part
of it has mostly come from HelloWorld" via reversal of IndAS lease liabilities on properties let go
(Jan call, Kunal Karan, p.7-8: "much of the liability that was sitting in the balance sheet as on 30
September, could be reversed"). Kunal also confirmed this gain "does not get reflected in the segment
results" (p.8). Net: strip the ~INR 9.72 Cr non-operating other income and the "defining inflection
point... transitioned to PAT profitability" (Onkar, p.4) quarter is a ~INR 7 Cr operating loss. This
is thesis-central: the entire inflection story rests on an accounting reversal in its first quarter.

**F2 — "Structurally profitable, not cyclical" is overstated; FY26 continuing-ops is still a loss.**
Deora: "we are now a profitable company. And we believe this is not cyclical; we are structurally
profitable" (Apr call, p.2-3) after exactly two profitable quarters. Yet full-year loss before tax
from continuing operations was INR 14.96 Cr (Apr call, Shrikant Jagtap, p.5). The INR 17.72 Cr
building-sale gain was explicitly carved out to discontinued operations and excluded from the headline
("This total income for the year excludes other income of INR 17.72 crores," p.5-6).

**F3 — Organic-vs-inorganic revenue bridge dodged.** FY26 revenue +50.8% continuing (p.5), distribution
segment +117.7% (p.6) is overwhelmingly PropTiger (consolidated only from 26-Sep-2025; ~INR 30 Cr/qtr
in Q3 per Rihen, Jan p.6; INR 42.8 Cr gross commission Q4). Amit Chandra asked directly: "Is it all
organic or is there any inorganic component?" (Apr, p.9-10). Onkar never isolated an organic-only
growth rate, pivoting to "distribution segment got augmented by PropTiger and that also answers part
of your question one, which is organic growth."

**F4 — The ecosystem/cross-sell "moat" is admitted single-digit.** After promoting "network effect
strengthen and compound" every quarter (Q2 p.2), Deora conceded cross-sell "numbers are still in
single digit" and "we have not been able to do justice to the ecosystem revenue" (Apr, p.14-15).
Multi-product penetration was put at "around 22% of our entire distribution network" (Rihen, p.14) but
monetized cross-sell revenue remains single-digit and only newly instrumented.

**F5 — SM-REIT launch slipped four quarters; the INR 2,000 Cr AUM target silently vanished.**
Q1: license received, launch "either Q4 or Q1 FY27" and "India's largest SM REIT platform within 3
years," INR 2,000 Cr AUM (Jul, Onkar p.10, Ashish Kumar Q&A p.16). Q2: "wait and watch... slow uptake."
Q3: "very patient... no date." Q4: "we'll definitely come out with a SM REIT in this financial year
[FY27]" (Rihen p.15). The INR 2,000 Cr AUM figure was never repeated after Q1.

**F6 — Guidance instability: CAGR self-corrected mid-answer; ARR framing flatters vs actual revenue.**
Q1 Onkar gave "25% CAGR," then "Sorry, just to correct myself... 38% CAGR" within one answer (Jul p.6).
The company reports a "crossed INR 500 crores ARR" milestone (Apr p.4) that is a Q4-annualized run-rate
(~123.85 x 4), while actual FY26 continuing revenue was INR 381.09 Cr (p.5) — the ARR framing runs
~30% ahead of realized revenue and annualizes the seasonally strong quarter.

**F7 — PropTiger turnaround promise leans on removing REA related-party charges, not operations; and
standalone profitability is never confirmed.** Q1 Onkar located the turnaround in eliminating three
intercompany payments to the REA parent — "business support services," "royalties" for IP, and
"marketing fee" (Jul p.9). By Q4 management substituted "highest-ever quarterly gross commission
INR 42.8 crores" (Apr p.5) for any statement that PropTiger is now profitable. The promised
"2-3 quarters" turnaround (Q2, Ashish p.12) is never confirmed or denied.

**F8 — Integrow deconsolidated (subsidiary to associate) flatters the P&L, and the Capital/SM-REIT
segment is actually shrinking while sold as key optionality.** Effective 1-Jul-2025 the company put its
Integrow "board control rights in abeyance till March 31, 2026" and reclassified it as an associate
(Q2, Kunal p.5). Rahul Jain probed the mechanics hard (Q2 p.8-11): line-by-line consolidation of a
loss-making, "very people dependent... high quality resources... significant cost" business is removed,
"a positive impact in terms of profitability." Capital segment revenue collapsed: FY25 INR 15.94 Cr to
FY26 INR 7.84 Cr (Apr p.6); 9M FY26 INR 3.55 Cr vs INR 11.77 Cr prior year (Jan p.5). The segment
carrying the SM-REIT optionality is contracting, partly by design, even as it is promoted as a growth pillar.

**F9 — Rental segment stagnation: flat revenue, falling occupancy, declining NestAway units.**
Rental revenue essentially flat: Q1 47.84, Q2 54.11, Q3 54.55, Q4 continuing 52.70 Cr. Blended occupancy
fell 77% (Q1 p.4) to 73% (Q2 p.4). NestAway houses/signed units declining YoY (Aditya Yadav, Apr p.12-13:
"it's been declining year-on-year"); management reframes as "Win a Pin Code" rationalization. Onkar
concedes rental growth "was not as anticipated" and student-living/Kota "did not come through" (Jan p.16).

**F10 — Repeated evasion (2 quarters) on quantifying PropTiger synergies / AI benefit.** Jimit (Emkay)
asked to quantify synergy savings/margin uplift in Q3 ("Is there any way we can quantify this?", Jan
p.18) and again in Q4 on measuring AI benefit vs operating leverage (Apr p.15-16); Dipesh Mehta asked
for quantified ecosystem/AI savings in Q3 (Jan p.21) and Q4 (Apr p.18). Each was deferred ("early days,"
"still early to quantify," "revenue per team member... couple of quarters").

**F11 — Q4 "AI-first / Unified Brain" narrative pivot is aspirational and timed to the cash windfall.**
A full new two-layer AI framework (Business Value Optimization + "Unified Brain" agentic layer)
appears in Q4 (Apr p.3, p.7) after only passing AI mentions before; surplus building-sale cash is
earmarked "to accelerate our AI initiatives" (p.3). "Unified Brain" has no revenue; Reuben and Jimit
both pressed whether the AI moat survives stripping proprietary data (p.13-15) and got qualitative answers.

**F12 — Unexplained leadership change.** Ashish Deora's title moved from "Founder & CEO, Aurum Ventures &
Director" (Q1-Q3 mastheads) to "Non-Executive Chairman" (Q4 masthead, p.2), during the first profitable
year, unremarked on the call and unasked by any analyst.

**F13 — Continuing related-party lead-purchase dependency on REA's Housing.com.** PropTiger was
previously "obliged to buy leads from Housing.com" with an "embargo" on other aggregators; post-deal
"that limitation goes away" but "we have a long-term contract... with Housing to purchase leads"
(Q2, Onkar p.17). REA (which owns Housing.com) is also a 5.5% Aurum shareholder — an ongoing
related-party procurement contract that is presented purely as a positive.

**F14 — Nestr Dubai promoted as a Q2 growth lever, then faded.** Launched Q2, with an unnamed comp
"does approximately Rs. 800 crores of annual revenue... up to 60% from the Dubai market" cited as
potential (Q2, Onkar p.18), framed as "a POC for another quarter." No Dubai revenue disclosed in Q3 or Q4.

**F15 — "Debt-free" is presented as achieved but is contingent on the building sale closing.** Deora:
"we are a debt-free company. The sale of buildings Q5 and Q6 will ensure that we retire all lease rental
discounting debt in full by June 30, 2026" (Apr p.3) — a conditional, forward event stated in the present tense.

**F16 — Cash conversion / receivables vs the profit turn are never discussed.** Across all four calls
there is no analyst question or management commentary on trade receivables, DSO, or CFO vs PAT — notable
given growth runs well ahead of profitability and the profit turn is other-income assisted.

Independent flags found: **16**.
