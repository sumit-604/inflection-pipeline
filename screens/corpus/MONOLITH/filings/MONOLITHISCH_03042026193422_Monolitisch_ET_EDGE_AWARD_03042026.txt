Press Release 
 
Monolithisch India Limited Recognized as ‘Bharat’s Best to a Billion 
Brand – 2026’ by ET Edge 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is proud to announce that it has been recognized as one of 
‘Bharat’s Best to a Billion Brands – 2026’ by ET Edge, a prestigious platform under The 
Times Group.  
 
This recognition underscores the Company’s commitment to excellence, innovation, and its 
significant impact in serving customers at scale across India. 
 
This recognition celebrates high-performing Indian companies that have demonstrated 
exceptional growth, scalability, and the potential to contribute significantly towards building a 
billion-dollar economy. Monolithisch India Limited’s inclusion in this distinguished list 
underscores its commitment to excellence, innovation, and sustainable growth.  
 
The recognition reflects the Company’s consistent performance, strong market position, and its 
role in supporting critical industries through high-quality refractory solutions. 
 
Speaking on the recognition, the leadership team of Monolithisch India Limited said: 
 
“We are honoured to be recognized among ‘Bharat’s Best to a Billion Brands – 2026’. This 
achievement is a testament to our team’s dedication, our focus on quality and innovation, 
and our vision to contribute meaningfully to India’s industrial growth story.” 
 
This milestone further strengthens the Company’s positioning as a key player in the refractory 
materials segment and reinforces its long-term growth strategy. 
 
About Monolithisch India Limited: 
 
Monolithisch is committed to excellence in the manufacturing and supply of high-performance 
industrial refractory materials. With an unwavering focus on quality, innovation, and customer-
centricity, we specialize in the production of premium-grade ramming mass—a critical heat 
insulation and lining consumable extensively used in induction furnaces across the iron, steel, 
and foundry sectors. its advanced refractory products and engineering solutions serving the steel, 
metallurgical, and high-temperature processing industries.
