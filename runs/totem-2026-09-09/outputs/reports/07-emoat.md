### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Historical fixed-asset turnover (Sales / Net Block, screener Data_Sheet.csv,

- FY24: Rs 228.50 cr / Rs 89.32 cr = 2.56x
- FY25: Rs 232.66 cr / Rs 110.01 cr = 2.12x
- FY26: Rs 251.01 cr / Rs 109.84 cr = 2.29x
- 3-year average = (2.56 + 2.12 + 2.29) / 3 = 2.32x

Capex still under execution (i.e. NOT yet commissioned) as of the FY26
year-end: Capital work-in-progress Rs 3.56 cr (Rs 2.79 cr tangible + Rs
0.78 cr intangible, all in the "less than one year" ageing bucket, none

Implied incremental revenue = Rs 3.56 cr x 2.32x = Rs 8.26 cr
As % of FY26 revenue (Rs 251.01 cr) = 3.3%

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 3.3%.** This number is intentionally small.
The large, headline capex (the austempering furnace, the CNC drill lines,
the robotic flute grinding, the Rs 14.99 cr FY26 gross-block addition) is
already commissioned and already inside the FY26/Q1 FY27 actuals; it is not
"embedded forward growth" under the Section 2C definition, it is realised
growth. The only genuinely forward capex pipeline still open at FY26
year-end is the residual Rs 3.56 cr CWIP, and it implies a modest 3.3%
further revenue headroom on top of what has already printed. [ANALYST
INFERENCE 🔍: the capex cycle that produced the Q1 FY27 print is largely
spent; the next leg of growth, if any, has to come from utilisation of
what is already installed, not from a fresh capex wave, unless a new
project is announced in the FY27 AR.]

FY26 R&D expenditure is stated as Nil (FY26 AR Annexure IV(B)(v), p.40).

**A3. Process innovation.** STRONG, 📄 DOCUMENTED. Three consecutive annual
reports show cumulative process-technology investment: FY24, CNC
measurement technology plus advanced edge-preparation for aerospace
micro-geometry, Rs 1.50 cr, "fully absorbed" (FY24 AR Annexure III, p.25-26);
FY25, Tap XCells and 7-axis CNC machines (FY25 AR Annexure IV, p.27); FY26,
an austempering furnace with SCADA control (bainitic microstructure,
reduced internal stress, higher fatigue life for washers), robotic flute
grinding for High Performance Tools, camera-based auto-sorting and shot
blasting "to ensure the supply of zero-defect products," and servo/PLC
coiling-cutting machines (FY26 AR Annexure IV(B)(i), p.40). All installed
and operating, not promised. [INFERENCE 🔍: impact is capped at MEDIUM, not
HIGH, because the already-verified Q1 FY27 margin bridge (B01-gate0 LBF-1,
using the same filed P&L this stage draws on) shows the OPM move from 16.1%
to 22.9% running through employee-cost and other-expense compression on
higher volume, with the net material-cost line actually a modest drag, not
a quality/yield gain. The process-technology story and the margin-proof
point are not cleanly the same story; the furnace and CNC lines are real
and installed, but the number usually cited as their result (the Q1 FY27
OPM jump) is better explained by operating leverage on a low base than by
yield or quality improvement.]

**A4. Product platform / modular architecture.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND at the
level the category asks for. Thread mill software and Simufact simulation

## SECTION 6: TIMELINE, RISKS & COMBINED ASSESSMENT

### 6A. Moat evolution timeline

- **Next 12 months (to Q2/Q3 FY27 results):** watch whether OPM holds above
  ~20% for a second consecutive quarter, separating a durable mix/capacity
  gain from the Q1 FY26 base-effect confound (Stage 5 finding: Q1 FY26 was
  the low point of the ten-quarter margin series).
- **12-24 months (FY27 AR, due mid-2027):** watch for a repeat capex/
  certification disclosure in Annexure IV (continuing the FY24-FY26 pattern)
  or, conversely, silence on further investment, which would confirm the
  2C finding that the capex cycle is largely spent.
- **24-36 months:** watch whether the aerospace-carbide development named in
  the FY26 AR (B2/1A) produces a disclosed revenue line or customer name,
  converting B2 from a certification event into a revenue-linked moat.
- **3-5 years:** watch whether the company ever discloses a product-line or
  customer-concentration split (Section 1C, C2); absent that, this scan
  cannot be refreshed with any more precision than it has today.

### 6B. Risks to the top-scoring emerging moats

- **A3 (process innovation):** the Q1 FY27 margin proof point most often
  cited for this capex is, on the already-verified P&L bridge (B01-gate0
  LBF-1), an employee-cost/other-expense compression story on volume, not a
  material-cost or yield story. Early warning: if Q2/Q3 FY27 OPM reverts
  toward the 18-20% range seen in prior non-Q1-FY26 quarters, the "process
  innovation" read was mostly base effect.
- **B2 (qualification lock-in):** the certifications close a gap to
  Kennametal and other global majors; they are a necessary, not sufficient,
  condition for aerospace revenue. Early warning: no aerospace-specific
  revenue or customer name appears in the FY27 AR despite the certification
  and R&D language being in place for two years running.
- **F2 (execution moat, capex leg):** the capex-completion record is real,
  but the qualitative promise-delivery record (B05, Grade C) is weak on
  exactly the claims (exports, inventory discipline, Taps growth) that
  would confirm the capex is producing durable, not one-off, results. Early
  warning: continued unquantified "substantial growth" language into a
  third consecutive AR with still no number attached.
