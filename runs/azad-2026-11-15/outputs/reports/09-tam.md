## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A Boundaries
- Product scope. Precision airfoils (fixed, moving, last stage; compressor; hot gas parts), rotating and stationary hardware, special machined parts, combustion assemblies, and assembled engine units (Q2P [page 18]; AR26 [page 30]). Includes steam and nuclear turbine airfoils (Q2P [page 17]).
- Included end markets. Gas, steam and nuclear turbines (energy); aero engine and airframe parts, defence engine assembly (A&D); oil and gas drilling and compressor parts.
- Geography. Global. Azad sells to OEM procurement offices in 11+ countries (AR26 [page 30]); exports 93% of FY26 revenue (AR26 [page 30]), 88% in Q2 FY27 (Q2C [page 6]).
- Customer scope. Qualified OEMs and Tier 1 only. Azad does not sell to the aftermarket or to utilities directly.
- Channel. Direct OEM contracts of 3 to 10 years; build-to-print (AR26 [page 28]).
- Price segment. Mission and life critical, zero ppm defects, 30 to 48 month qualification per component (AR26 [page 28]).
- Excluded. Castings and forgings value (Azad does not cast; investment castings were deflected on the Aug call, B05 dropped_triggers). Raw material. Aftermarket MRO labour. Defence platforms. Domestic Indian demand sits inside the global figure.
- Oil and gas. The drilling market figures are total OFSE-company markets that "do not take into account the outsourcing share" (Pro [page 194]). Revenue is under Rs 10 Cr (B04 FLAG-SEGMENT-GAP). Excluded from headline TAM, not sizeable on current evidence.

### 1B Management's TAM claim

- Oil and gas leg. Rs 930 bn; no definition on the slide; the prospectus equivalent (drill bits plus downhole tools) is Rs 60k Cr in 2022 (Pro [page 193-194]).
