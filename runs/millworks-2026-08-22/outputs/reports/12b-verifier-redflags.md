## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the sources, before reading B05/B06 conclusions)

Each item anchored to the source.

**IF1. Related-party top-customer revenue-quality cluster.** Quick Pay
Private Limited is the top customer at 47.02% of FY26 revenue (Rs 6,992.76
lakh) and is simultaneously an equity investee of Millworks with common
shareholders (RHP p.25, Risk Factor #3). Defence revenue jumped from Rs
150.08 lakh (FY25) to Rs 10,325.82 lakh (FY26), a ~68x single-year surge,
69.43% of FY26 revenue (RHP p.29). FY26 operating cash flow was negative
Rs 1,076.29 lakh against PAT of Rs 3,706.39 lakh (RHP p.30, p.36). A top
customer that is also an investee, driving an explosive one-year revenue
jump that is largely uncollected, is a revenue-quality / round-tripping
pattern, not only a concentration risk.

**IF2. Receivables closing ratio understated.** RHP Risk Factor #2 states
FY26 closing trade receivables of Rs 13,868.68 lakh equal **93.22% of FY26
revenue** (RHP p.24). Risk Factor #9 separately gives receivable **days of
178** (average basis) (RHP p.30). The 93.22% closing ratio (~340 days) is
the more damning figure and it is the company's own headline table number.

**IF3. Reg 30 Annexure-A related-party denial vs same-release investee
promotion.** Annexure-A answers Q7 (promoter/group interest in the
order-awarding entity) "No" and Q8 (whether orders fall within related-party
transactions) "NA" (Reg30 20.08.2026, Annexure-A). The same release
headlines Quick Pay, "an investee company in which Millwork technologies
holds an equity stake," and states "a significant portion of these orders
has come from a customer that is part of a leading global manufacturing
group" (Reg30 20.08.2026 press release). The "significant portion from one
customer" wording contradicts the letter's "multiple domestic and
international customers" framing and re-introduces single-customer
concentration in the new Rs 53.74 cr intake.

**IF4. Section 185 violation.** Company advanced loans to Rashmi Sridhar
Acharya (Rs 10.00 lakh), and to Sowmya Madhu (Rs 1.14 lakh FY24, Rs 2.00
lakh FY25); compounding application filed 07-Feb-2026 (RHP p.27, item 5).

**IF5. Restatement cluster is two risk factors, not one.** Risk Factor #12
(DRHP-to-RHP working-capital revision plus FY25/FY24 holding-day
"inadvertent calculation error" using unaudited revenue, RHP p.31) AND Risk
Factor #16 ("Certain changes made in financial numbers pertaining to Fiscal
2025 and Fiscal 2024 compared to DRHP to RHP," attributed to rounding and
regrouping, RHP p.36). Two separate restatement disclosures.

**IF6. Statutory non-compliance persists to the eve of listing, against a
"strengthened compliance" claim.** EPF/ESIC filing delays run through March
2026 and spike late: EPF Dec-2025 delayed 73 days, Feb-2026 52 days, ESIC
Jan-2026 42 days (RHP p.33-35). Each row claims the company "strengthened
its accounts compliance by dedicating a team," yet delays worsened into the
listing window. The corrective-action claim is not credible against its own
data. Also: Section 10A default, Rule 14(6) PAS-3, Schedule III FY22 (RHP
p.26-27).

**IF7. FCGPR filings pending with RBI.** FCGPR for foreign-shareholder
allotments filed belatedly (20-05-2025), re-filed 10-11-2025, still "Under
Process with the RBI"; two later allotments' FCGPR unsubmitted and pending
(RHP p.26-27, item 3).

**IF8. V3 Technologies related-party lease + similar-line-of-business
conflict.** Unit-1 and the Registered Office are leased from V3 Technologies,
a partnership where the promoters are partners (RHP p.31-32). V3 also
operates a business identical to Millworks (railway/aero/defence/drone/semi
components); non-compete in place but conflict flagged by the company (RHP
p.39-40, Risk Factor #23).

**IF9. No formal FX hedging policy** for imported raw materials (RHP p.39,
Risk Factor #21).

**IF10. Order-book concentration + low execution.** Order book Rs 6,714.06
lakh, 44 customers, ~9.7% executed as of 05-Jun-2026 (RHP order-book table,
per B05). Two customers ~62% of book.

**IF11. No long-term or exclusive customer contracts; purchase-order basis
only** (RHP p.28-29, Risk Factor #6).

**IF12. Rs 61.03 cr IPO-funded machinery not yet ordered** as of RHP filing
(RHP p.36, Risk Factor #15).

**IF13. MD over-promotion on a five-week-old listing.** MD Sridhar Acharya:
new orders are "an important validation of our execution track record"
(Reg30 20.08.2026 press release), for a company listed 21-Jul-2026.

**IF14. Export country-count inconsistency.** Press release claims exports
to customers "across twelve countries" (Reg30 20.08.2026); B05 cites the RHP
as "9 countries" (RHP p.131). Nine vs twelve is an unreconciled disclosure
inconsistency. (RHP p.131 not independently re-opened by me; flagged on the
face of the two documents.)

**IF15. Receivables "projection" is flat, not a normalization.** The RHP
trade-receivables projection of Rs 14,015.00 lakh by March 2027 (RHP p.90,
per B05) is roughly equal to the FY26 close of Rs 13,868.68 lakh. In
absolute terms receivables do not fall.
