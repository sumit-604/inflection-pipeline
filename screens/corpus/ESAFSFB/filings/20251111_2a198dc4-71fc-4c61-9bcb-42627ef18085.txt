Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI (LODR)"), we hereby inform that, the 
Board of Directors of the Bank, in its meeting held on Tuesday, November 11, 2025 have

1. 
Re-appointment of Smt. Kolasseril Chandramohanan Ranjani (DIN:01735529) as Non-
Executive Independent Director of the Bank for a period of Three (3) consecutive years

Director of the Bank for a period of Three (3) consecutive years with effect from December

members of the Bank through Postal Ballot.

Retirement of Dr. Joseph Vadakkekara Antony (00181554), Non-Executive Director of the 
Bank with effect from November 17, 2025.
