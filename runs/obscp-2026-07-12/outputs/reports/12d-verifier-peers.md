"Feb 2026" label refers to the call date, not the filing/transcript date reflected in the
filename — this is a labeling convention difference, not a wrong-transcript error; content
matches the Q3 FY26 quarter correctly. Read in full. Contains: capex breakdown (heat
treatment plant replacement, Noida expansion), the road-tax/RoDTEP-benefit-halved
disclosure (used substantively in B06's 2E, see Part 1 row 14 above), EU-FTA/duty-advantage
commentary that echoes but does not add new information to the China+1 narrative already
sourced from the Jun 2026 call, and routine guidance reaffirmation (565cr +/-5%, consistent
