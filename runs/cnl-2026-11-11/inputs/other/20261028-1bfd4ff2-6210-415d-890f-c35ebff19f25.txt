Commenting on the Company performance, Mr. Ketan Patel, Chairman & Managing Director of Creative Newtech said:
Management Comment
“Q2 FY27 reflects Creative Newtech’s continued execution across its technology-focused distribution platform, with sustained profitable growth and a sharper
focus on working capital discipline and cash generation. Despite a dynamic operating environment, we delivered healthy growth in earnings while converting that
performance into strong operating cash flow.
For Q2 FY27, our consolidated total income stood at ₹754.80 crore, registering a growth of 14.43% YoY compared to ₹659.59 crore in the corresponding quarter last
year. EBITDA increased by 50.70% YoY to ₹40.11 crore, while EBITDA margin improved to 5.31% from 4.04% in Q2 FY26, reflecting stronger operating leverage and
improved operational efficiencies. Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹25.94 crore, registering a growth of 36.88% YoY despite higher finance costs during the quarter.
For H1 FY27, consolidated total income was ₹1,231.65 crore, up 16.55% YoY, with EBITDA of ₹65.01 crore (YOY up 55.78%) at a margin of 5.28%, and PAT of ₹39.47
crore (YOY up 35.49%).
A key priority for this period was strengthening working capital management and operating cash flow. Cash flow from operating activities for H1 FY27 stood at
₹351.24 crore, a significant turnaround from an outflow of ₹91.65 crore in H1 FY26 and ₹271.88 crore in FY26. Net working capital days stood at 45 as of 30
September 2026. Maintaining tight control over receivables, inventory and payables remains central to funding our growth and moderating finance costs.
The quarter was marked by several strategic milestones that further strengthened our long-term growth platform. Proposed acquisition of Infinova (India) Private
Limited to build a Make in India surveillance technology platform, strengthening the Company’s capabilities in video surveillance and security solutions.
Strengthened our new tech portfolio with new partnership with Drona Aviation to broaden access to drone-based STEM learning, expanding opportunities in drone
education and technology and Jupiter Electric Mobility to strengthen the Company’s drone technology portfolio and expand its presence in the emerging drone
ecosystem.
The rapid adoption of artificial intelligence, increasing investments in digital infrastructure, and growing demand for intelligent surveillance, cybersecurity,
enterprise networking, and connected technologies continue to create significant opportunities across our focus areas. We believe our diversified portfolio, deep
technology partnerships, and value-added distribution model position us well to capitalize on these long-term growth trends.
Looking ahead, our focus remains on sustaining profitable growth while improving working capital efficiency and cash conversion, expanding our technology
portfolio, strengthening strategic partnerships, and integrating our recent acquisitions. Backed by strong execution capabilities and an expanding ecosystem of
global technology partners, we remain confident in our ability to deliver sustainable long-term growth and create lasting value for all our stakeholders.”
