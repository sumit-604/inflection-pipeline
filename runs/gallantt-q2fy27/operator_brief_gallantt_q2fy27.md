## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Gallantt makes steel bars for building work (TMT bars) in Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat. It runs the whole chain itself: iron pellets, sponge iron, billets, then bars, with its own power.
2. This quarter, sales held flat at about Rs 1,146 Cr. Profit after tax fell by about a third, to Rs 60.51 Cr.
3. The company sold less of its own steel. TMT bar sales fell to 164 thousand tonnes, from 192 thousand in Q1.
4. Revenue held up only because the company bought and resold about Rs 99 Cr of goods (trading). It also sold more sponge iron and pellets.
5. Management blamed two plant shutdowns at Gorakhpur and dearer coal. It did not mention the traded goods.
6. The Gujarat (Kutch) plant also fell 18-26% at every stage, with no shutdown named. The call said its mill improved; the deck shows it got worse.
7. Cash is weak. Operating cash covered only half of the half-year profit. Cash on hand fell to about Rs 1 Cr, and debt rose.
8. Management says the company is "net cash". That is true only if Rs 266 Cr of loans to others counts as cash. The company has not named who owes that money.
9. In FY26 the company also sold Rs 110 Cr of fixed assets. It has not said what it sold or to whom.
10. Good news: a 23% capacity increase at Gorakhpur starts about 10 November. The promoter holding rose about 0.9 points, but no document shows a purchase.
11. Worrying: about Rs 548 Cr of spending and dividend falls due in the second half. Half-year operating cash was only Rs 91 Cr. The standalone accounts were filed two days late.
12. Watch next quarter: gross margin (sales minus raw material and traded-goods cost, over sales) against 27.35% (good) or 21.00% (bad). Also watch whether the company names the loan borrowers.
