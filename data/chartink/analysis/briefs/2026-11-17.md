## What would change this read

The read is an early uptrend that took a hard hit on Monday and rested on Tuesday. Banks lead, and a narrow group of small leaders is still climbing the screens. It turns worse if a hard down day comes back soon. A day of 300 or more hard fallers would match the October pattern. A long count under about 1,050 would confirm that last week's progress is gone. Tuesday closed at 1,065. A short count under half the market, about 1,170, would hand the last clock back to the sellers. Tuesday closed at 1,253, and that count is drifting down. A long count under about 605, the 23 October low, would erase the whole bounce. New yearly lows above about 100 would say sellers have returned in force. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has taken charge again. A sharp fall in banks on heavy volume would remove the group now carrying the index.

The cautious read fails if the repair picks up again from here. The first sign is the 50 day count back above half the market, about 1,170, within a few sessions. Tuesday closed at 1,103. The second is the long count turning toward half from 1,065. The third is a wide up day, with 200 or more hard risers, on turnover above about 610 crore. The fourth is a VIX close under 15, against 15.5 on Tuesday. The fifth is gains held into the close. About half of the stocks that trade four percent up should keep it, and Tuesday held about a third. The first of these, with any one of the others in the same week, would put the repair back on its track.
