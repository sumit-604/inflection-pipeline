**READ THIS FIRST — CORPUS EXTRACTION DEFECT (governs confidence on everything below).**
This PDF holds two printed pages per PDF page (a print spread). pdftotext -layout, used to
produce the page-marked .txt this stage reads (pdftoppm/image rendering unavailable this
session), tries to preserve left-right column position, and for wide multi-column tables that
straddle both halves of the spread it frequently interleaves cells from adjacent, unrelated rows
and even from the facing note. The effect is severe on the primary statements (Balance Sheet, P&L
face, Cash Flow Statement, pages 83-84 / printed 160-163) and on several notes with wide tables
(Note 7 Inventories, Note 12 Borrowings instrument table, Note 8/16/17/18 current asset/liability
breakdowns, Note 33.25/33.26). It is mild-to-absent on notes that are narrow, single-column, or
prose (accounting policies, security/covenant text, pending litigation, capital management,
segment reporting, MSME interest fields, gratuity actuarial tables, quantitative gold/diamond/
silver stock table). Every figure below is marked HIGH / MODERATE / LOW confidence; LOW-confidence
figures are named as candidates, not facts, per the "never estimate" rule. Where two or more
independent appearances of the same figure across different notes/statements agree, or where an
addition/subtraction cross-check on the note's own numbers closes, confidence is raised to HIGH
even inside an otherwise garbled block.
