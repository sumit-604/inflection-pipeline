Large, underpenetrated market opportunity:  Indegene's addressable

AI that expands margins, not erodes them: Unlike typical IT services, 
where AI productivity gains get passed back to clients as pricing cuts, 
Indegene increasingly bills per outcome (per document, per campaign) 
rather than per hour. Its proprietary Cortex and NEXT platforms trained on 
pharma-specific regulatory data and let capture efficiency gains as margin 
rather than surrendering them to procurement. 
Irreplaceable client relationships: Indegene serves all of the world's 
top-20 biopharma companies, with deep ly embedded, multi -year 
workflows spanning regulatory writing, safety reporting and marketing. 
Switching costs run into millions of dollars and take years to replicate. 
This creates a durable moat that is extremely difficult for competitors 
(whether IT majors or boutique agencies) to break into or dislodge. 
Fast-growing "Beyond -20" client base: Beyond its top -20 anchor 
clients, Indegene's smaller emerging -biotech and mid -sized pharma 
accounts nearly doubled from 27 to 53 (USD 1mn+ clients) since IPO. This 
diversifies revenue away from client concentration risk while tapping into 
a structurally growing pool of innovation -led biopharma companies with 
limited in-house commercialization capability. 
Clean balance sheet, strong cash generation: Indegene is debt -free 
with healthy net cash, and operating cash flow has exceeded reported 
profit every year since listing which is a rare quality in small/mid -cap 
growth stories. This gives the company flexibility to self -fund acquisitions, 
pay rising dividends, and weather  short-term volatility without diluting 
shareholders or straining its balance sheet. 
Attractive valuation based on growth compared to peers : Despite 
offering 18 -20% projected revenue & PAT growth rate and is 
comparatively cheaper than the several Mid -cap I T services companies 
with slower growth rates, Indegene is trading  at roughly 18-20x forward 
earnings FY27E/FY28E. As the only pure -play, India -listed life sciences 
commercialization specialist, it lacks a direct comparable which allows 
Indegene a unique p osition to demand a premium which market hasn't 
fully priced in yet. 
We initiate coverage on Indegene with a BUY rating and a target 
price of INR 620, valuing the stock at 22.5x FY28E EPS, implying 
~22% upside. We forecast FY26 -28E revenue/PAT CAGR of ~18% , 
supported by expanding margins, strong cash conversion, and a 
durable pharma-outsourcing moat.

Executive Summary 
Indegene is a Bengaluru-headquartered, India-listed company that helps 
large global pharma companies market, launch and manage their drugs — 
everything from medical writing and regulatory paperwork to AI -powered 
marketing campaigns aimed at doctors and patients. Think o f it as a 
specialized outsourcing partner sitting between big pharma and their day-
to-day commercial and medical operations, rather than a traditional IT 
services company. 
The business case is straightforward. Indegene works with all of the 
world's top -20 biopharma companies, and these relationships are sticky 
— once embedded in a client's workflows (running safety reporting, 
managing thousands of marketing assets, writing regulatory documents), 
switching costs run into the millions and take years to unwind . In FY26, 
top-20 clients contributed ~72% of revenue, but the more exciting growth 
is coming from smaller "Beyond-20" clients — emerging biotech and mid-
sized pharma companies — where we see significant uptick in smaller 
accounts (>USD 0.25mn - <USD 1mn). This diversifies the business away 
from a handful of large customers. AI is a genuine tailwind here, not a 
threat. Indegene has built proprietary AI platforms (Cortex, NEXT) trained 
specifically on pharma's regu latory and scientific language,  something 
generic AI tools like ChatGPT can't easily replicate given the compliance 
and domain complexity involved. Because Indegene increasingly charges 
per outcome (per document reviewed, per campaign run) rather than per 
hour worked, AI-driven efficiency gains flow  to Indegene's margins rather 
than getting passed  back to the client as savings which is  a rare setup 
where AI helps the vendor, not just the customer. 
The business is in good shape: no debt, strong cash generation (operating 
cash flow has exceeded reporte d profit every year since listing), and a 
large, high-quality client base that keeps expanding wallet share. Revenue 
grew ~24% in FY26, aided partly by acquisitions, with organic growth also 
healthy. A large new deal with its biggest client (expected to cr oss USD 
50mn in FY27) shows Indegene can win and execute large, complex, multi-
year contracts. The stock isn't expensive relative to its growth. It trades at 
roughly 18-20x earnings of FY27E/FY28E, cheaper than several Indian IT -
services peers with much slower growth, and Indegene is really the only 
pure-play, India-listed company doing this specific kind of work at scale 
— most global comparable (EVERSANA, Syneos) are private.

Risks worth watching  are (1) a large share of revenue still comes from a 
small number of clients (2) recent acquisitions need to integrate smoothly 
and (3) US drug-pricing reforms create some sentiment overhang, though 
history suggests pricing pressure tends to push pharma co mpanies 
toward more outsourcing, not less. 
Valuation Outlook 
Indegene's numbers tell a simple story: this is a fast -compounding 
business with a genuinely clean balance sheet. FY26 revenue came in at 
INR 35,105mn, up ~24% YoY, and Q4FY26 alone crossed INR 10,000mn 
for the first time, growing 32.8% YoY. The company is debt -free, holds 
more cash than debt, and delivers ROE of ~17% and ROCE of ~18%, 
delivered strong returns for a business that ties up very little capital in 
plant or inventory. Growth looks broad -based rather than a one -off; large 
clients paying ov er USD 1mn/year h ave grown to 53 , North America 
(~74% of revenue) remains the fastest -growing region, and Enterprise 
Commercial Solutions (~72% of sales) continues to be the main engine. 
Because Indegene runs an asset -light, people-driven model, its cost base 
is mostly technology platforms, senior talent and delivery infrastructure — 
meaning each additional rupee of revenue gets cheaper to serve once the 
upfront investment is made. Revenue per employee has climbed from 
~USD 56K to ~USD 75K in three years, aided by AI -led productivity gains. 
FY27E EBITDA margin dips slightly to ~18.1% on recent acquisition costs, 
but should recover to ~18.6% by FY28E as integration completes and AI 
investments start paying off — with ROCE climbing steadily from 17.8% to 
18.9% over FY26 -28E. T he Q4FY26 margin softness (down 180bps YoY) 
reflects investment, not weakening demand. 
At current levels, the stock trades at ~30x trailing earnings but only ~21.4x 
FY27E and ~18.4 x FY28E EPS — well below its own historical peak 
multiples of 34 -43x. Applying a fair through -cycle multiple of ~22.5x to 
FY28E earnings gives us a target price of Rs 620. We believe this premium 
is justified: Indegene is the only listed, pure -play life sc iences 
commercialization company at scale, with global rivals like EVERSANA and 
Syneos (both are private), and its 18%+ earnings growth comfortably  
supporting a valuation toward the lower -middle end of Indian IT -services 
peer range.

Company Background 
Founded in 1998, Indegene Limited is a digital -first life sciences 
commercialization company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. The 
Company enables biopharmaceutical, emerging biotech, and medical 
device companies to develop, launch, and scale the impact of their 
products by combining deep healthcare domain expertise, fit -for-purpose 
proprietary technology, and an agile global operatin g model. Indegene 
operates at the intersection of healthcare and technology, describing its 
purpose as enabling healthcare organizations to be "future -ready," and its 
vision as being the trusted partner to healthcare organizations for 
improved health outcomes. 
The Carlyle Group and Brighton Park Capital invested USD 200mn in 
Indegene in 2021, and the Company listed on the NSE and BSE in 2024. 
Over more than two decades, Indegene has expanded from Indian 
medical-education services into a full commercializati on value -chain 
partner with a global delivery footprint spanning North America, E urope, 
and Asia, supported by multiple  value-accretive acquisitions  (e.g., 
CultHealth, MME, Trilogy, MJL Life Sciences).  
Indegene operates through 9 delivery hubs and 19 offi ces worldwide, 
spanning North America (Princeton, Durham, Oxford, Los Angeles), Europe 
(London, Cambridge, Cork, Frankfurt, Binningen, Madrid), and Asia -Pacific 
(Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Singapore, Shanghai, Tokyo), enabling 
round-the-clock, multi -language client engagement. Majority of the 
revenue come from developed markets; North America contributed 71.6% 
and Europe 25.5% of FY26  revenue, with India and the rest of the world 
together contributing just below 3%.

on branded drugs without generic competition. This could extend beyond 
Medicare into Medicaid and even private insurance markets. It i ncludes

middlemen), pressuring other countries to pay more for drugs, and linking 
pricing to international reference benchmarks. Overall, the industry trade

Specialized skills limits the GCC threat 
GCCs start with easy work, not hard work. When a pharma company sets 
up a GCC, it does not begin by hiring regulatory writers or 
pharmacovigilance specialists. It starts with the easy stuff such as IT 
support, finance and accounting, HR operations, procure ment, general

learning curve is long, and getting something wrong can mean a 
regulatory rejection or a safety issue. No captive center builds this 
capability first. It's usually one of the last things they attempt, if they 
attempt it at all. 
GCCs cannot hold on to specialist talent, and that helps Indegene. A 2025 
study by CIEL HR found that talent retention is the single biggest 
headache for GCCs today. Around 51% of the captive centers (surveyed)

attrition stays high. Indegene, by contrast, has been doing this exact work

Risk Factors 
 Client Concentration 
Top-5 clients contribute approximately 45% of revenue per IPO -era 
disclosures; the largest client alone is expected to generate >USD 50mn in 
FY27 post the Tectonic Germany engagement ramp. Loss or  scope 
reduction at any one of these accounts would have a material revenue 
impact. 
 Currency translation 
Approximately 66% of revenue is dollar denominated and ~25% Euro -
denominated; Rupee  appreciation against the USD would be a direct 
margin headwind, though partially offset by cost base  (India). Rupee  
depreciation (likely scenario in current Trump tariff environment) is 
supportive. 
 Acquisition integration risk  
Three near-simultaneous acquisitions (BioPharm, WARN, Cake) in H2 FY26 
create execution risk on cultural integration, ERP/systems consolidation, 
and customer cross -sell. The Q4 FY26 reported PAT  decline of 32.2% YoY 
reflects one-time integration & TCPA costs; Trajectory of H1 FY27 margin 
recovery is a key monitorables. 
 Talent inflation  
Indian life-sciences-specialized GenAI talent is scarce and salary 
expectations are rising. Wage  inflation beyond ~6 to 8% would compress 
margins, which is likely to offset GenAI productivity leverage. 
 Regulatory and policy risk  
A sustained, severe contraction in global pharma R&D  budgets in 
response to MFN/IRA viewed as low -probability considering the current 
funding trajectories that  would eventually feed through to  services 
demand. Tariffs on Indian services export s would directly affect 
economics. 
 Technology disruption  
A scenario in which general -purpose LLMs (GPT, Claude and Gemini ) with 
its GenAI capabilities poses strong challenge to Indegene.  These LLMs do 
not have the access to life-sciences-specific data, regulatory rule sets, and 
integration with pharma RIM/safety systems which Indegene has. 
 Promoter / private equity exit overhang 
Carlyle exited a substantial stake during FY26 (June 2025 block trade); 
residual private equity ex its could create supply overhang. Block trade 
absorption has been orderly to date.
