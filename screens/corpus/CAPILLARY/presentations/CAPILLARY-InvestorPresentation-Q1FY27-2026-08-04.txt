Q: What constituted the exceptional expense incurred this quarter?
Cyber fraud incident in SessionM Czech Republic s.r.o., a step-down subsidiary.
Background: Between June 24 and July 2, 2026, the Czechia subsidiary was subjected to a
sophisticated social engineering fraud where perpetrators impersonated the Group CEO and a
purported M&A counsel of a global law firm, inducing a senior executive of the subsidiary to
effect unauthorised remittances. Rs 333.9 Mn (EUR 3.05 Mn) of remittances were made between
the aforementioned dates to third-party bank accounts in Hong Kong and Singapore. An
exceptional expense of this amount has been provided for pending potential insurance
settlement. In relation to remittances to Singapore accounts, beneficiary accounts containing
approximately Rs 47.0 Mn (EUR 428K) have been frozen by banking authorities as part of ongoing
recovery.
Status: Fund Recovery & Legal Proceedings. Criminal complaints filed in Czechia (July 3,
2026), Singapore and Hong Kong (July 4, 2026); filings also made in India; beneficiary
accounts frozen, fund recall requests initiated.
Hong Kong (EUR 2.07 Mn): engaged a leading Hong Kong law firm with fraud litigation practice.
Counsel estimates recovery through Hong Kong courts may take approximately 6-8 months.
Singapore (EUR 975K): beneficiary account frozen July 4, 2026 pursuant to a Singapore Police
order; Singapore Police traced onward movement of funds to Hong Kong accounts; Hong Kong
court proceedings extended to cover these amounts.
Status: Forensic Audit & Insurance Claim. Forensic Audit: KPMG (Forensic Services), Prague
team, conducting independent investigation; laptop imaging & IT-artefact collection in
progress; no evidence of malware or device compromise so far; final report expected later in
August [2026]. Insurance Claim: notified under Crime Insurance Policy; coverage indicated
under Social Engineering (CEO Fraud) provisions; surveyor appointed, process to conclude in
approximately 5-6 months.

===== PAGE 22 =====
Q: What is the NRR for Q1 FY27 and what does the Organic vs Inorganic breakdown show?
TTM ending June 30, 2026: Total NRR 113%, Organic NRR 111%, Inorganic NRR 120%. Majority of
business is organic revenue. Organic NRR (111%) reflects healthy expansion within existing
customer base. Kognitiv/SessionM cohorts expected to reach organic-comparable retention only
once platform upgrades conclude. Inorganic NRR (120%) usually reflects migration dynamics from
acquired customers (which normally push NRR below 100%); in TTM June 2026, the Kognitiv
acquisition base was only two months old in the year-ago comparison period, inflating the
inorganic NRR reading.
