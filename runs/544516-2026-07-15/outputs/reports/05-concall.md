### 3D. Customer and order book signals

- **Internal inconsistency flagged:** In the H1 FY26 call, management states "in just a single week, we secured fresh orders worth over INR1,013 crores from key customers, including Integral Coach Factories and Modern Coach Factories," yet in the same call states the total active order book is "more than INR455 crores." These two figures are irreconcilable within the same transcript (a single week's fresh orders cannot exceed the total order book stated moments later). This is flagged as a transcript-internal numeric inconsistency — most likely a transcription/dictation artifact (e.g., intended "Rs10-13cr") — and is reported here exactly as extracted, per the "trust and flag conflicts" rule, rather than silently corrected.
- A second, smaller intra-call inconsistency in the same H1 FY26 call: order book stated as "more than INR455 crores" in the opening remarks and in response to Mukesh Panjwani, but later reconciled as Rs405cr (railways) + Rs65cr (defense) = Rs470cr in response to Ashit Kothi — a ~Rs15cr gap, plausibly explained by orders won mid-call ("another INR30 crores of order on hand" mentioned in the opening remarks) but not explicitly reconciled by management.
- Order book bifurcation (FY26 call): government ~Rs415-420cr, non-government ~Rs50-55cr — non-government share still modest (~11%) versus the H1 FY26 call's stated expectation that "this non-government sector will increase."
- Large named private order: Acme India Industries Ltd, ~Rs62.36cr (per announcements summary, 15-Mar-2026) — the largest single non-government order in the window, supporting (but not yet proving out) the stated diversification narrative.
- Customer concentration: Indian Railways (directly or via manufacturing units ICF/MCF/RCF) remains the dominant end-customer; metro (Chennai, Kolkata) and defense (HAL) are early-stage diversification, together well under 15% of the order book as of the FY26 call.
- New channel-partner distribution strategy for zonal railway and refurbishment work (FY26 call) — no partner names, count, or margin-share disclosed; a genuine new operating-model risk (execution/quality control, margin dilution) that analysts did not stress-test in depth.
