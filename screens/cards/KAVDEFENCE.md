## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** The facts held describe a
maker of radio frequency, antenna and defence wireless products. The business
description page was not read, so products, customers and pricing are NOT
FOUND. FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 8.42 crore (results, doc dfb3a7b0,
page 29; rupee figures on this card are derived from Rs lakh).

**What is changing?** Revenue is falling fast. Consolidated revenue halved from
Rs 17.12 crore in FY25 (same page). Standalone Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs 0.44
crore against Rs 2.86 crore a year earlier (results, doc d9861a77, page 3). That
is a fall of 85% (derived).

**Why now?** Nothing in the corpus marks a turn. Market capitalisation is Rs
470.95 crore (Bull AI company record, 23 September 2026, a service record).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Orders must return at a scale
that covers fixed cost. Q1 FY27 standalone total income was Rs 0.67 crore
against listed expenses of Rs 2.19 crore (results, doc d9861a77, page 3).
Revenue has to rise about four times from Q1 to reach the Rs 1.8 crore
fixed cost run rate named in step 13.

**What breaks it?** No return of orders. The FY26 profit came from other income
and a write back of old payables, not from sales. Without those, the company
runs at a loss.
