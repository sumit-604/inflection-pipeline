## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT AND ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A RPT history. This is LOAD-BEARING FACT 2 (Danya Electric structure)

Structure as filed (VERIFIED, AR p.29-33 and Notes):
1. Danya is a partnership firm. SPEL holds 90% of profit share. The other 10% sits with the MD (7.5%) and the WTD (2.5%) (AR p.30-31, p.33). Both are promoters and directors of SPEL.
2. Rating BB-/A4+, sub-investment grade (AR p.32). The Notice states in the same section "sound financial standing" (AR p.32). The two statements sit together.
3. SPEL guarantee to IndusInd Bank: Rs 14.70 Cr (Note 21, 1,470 lakh, Board resolution 28-Mar-2025), covered by personal guarantees of the MD and WTD (AR p.33). Authority sought for FY27: guarantee up to Rs 25 Cr (Item 6) and up to Rs 75 Cr under s.185 (Item 9, "for having given and for continuing to give").
4. FY27 RPT authority with Danya: Rs 125 Cr (Item 6, AR p.29). Split: guarantee up to 25, sales, purchases, advances and loans up to 100. FY26 gross trade 50.18 (purchases 25.58 + sales 24.60, AOC-2 p.47; DERIVED sum). So the FY27 authority is 2.5x last year's gross trade. A web-found FY26 notice shows the FY26 limit at Rs 75 Cr (MEDIA, search snippet of the 20th AGM notice; the document itself timed out on fetch, so this is not confirmed).
5. Terms: loans to Danya repayable on demand, unsecured, at "8.00%-9.00%", sourced from internal accruals, "no financial indebtedness incurred" (AR p.31-32). Price basis "market factors in an independent manner". No bidding. No external valuation or benchmark: "Not Applicable" (AR p.30, p.31). Management says there are "no unrelated comparable business entities" (AR p.30).
6. Promoter money into Danya: consolidated Note 7 shows an unsecured "From Related Party" loan of Rs 3.11 Cr; Note 21 names it as "Loan Received from Partners, Vee Rajmohan 311.30 lakh" (consol AR p.85, p.94). Rate, tenor and security NOT FOUND. Other partners' capital rose 3.69 to 4.07 Cr, including additions of 0.17 (consol Note 6 AR p.85).
7. Intra-group elimination: gross SPEL-Danya trade 50.18 against net balances. Payable to Danya 6.63 (Note 21 p.74). No unrealised-profit elimination visible (B02 finding 1, B03 analyst note).

Observations, each separated into fact and inference:

(a) Section 185 timing. Fact: Item 9 asks members to approve the Board "having given" the guarantee (AR p.22). Fact: the guarantee dates from the 28-Mar-2025 Board resolution (AR p.74). Fact: SPEL held an EGM on 14-Jul-2025 and an AGM on 19-Sep-2025 (AR p.44). Fact: CARO says ss.185 and 186 are complied with (B03 analyst note). [INFERENCE] If neither the EGM nor the 19-Sep-2025 AGM carried a s.185 special resolution, the guarantee ran for about 18 months before the retrospective ask, and the CARO statement sits against it. The 20th AGM notice was not readable in this run, so the inference is open. Separating observation: the s.185 special resolution date, if any, in the 14-Jul-2025 EGM or 19-Sep-2025 AGM notice.

(b) Rate inversion. Fact: SPEL's own ICICI term debt costs 9.50% (AR Note 6 p.65-66). Fact: Item 6 sets the Danya loan rate at 8.00%-9.00% (AR p.31). Fact: Danya is rated BB-. [INFERENCE] SPEL would lend below its own borrowing cost to a weaker credit. Pricing to a related party that falls below the lender's cost transfers value to the firm in which two directors hold 10%. The stated range is a ceiling-and-floor statement, not an executed rate. Actual interest charged is NOT FOUND.

(c) Source-of-funds claim. Fact: Item 6 says "internal accruals" and "no financial indebtedness incurred" (AR p.31-32). Fact: FY26 CFO is below capex and no undrawn lines exist (B02/B03 FLAG-CASH). [INFERENCE] Any new loan to Danya in FY27 is funded by SPEL's own borrowing or supplier float, so the "internal accruals" line does not hold for a capex-heavy year.

(d) Size of the promoters' economic slice. Fact: Danya PAT 2.35 (AR p.43). 10% = Rs 0.235 Cr (DERIVED), against SPEL PAT 20.44 (AR p.35). That is 1.2% of SPEL profit. Fact: MD and WTD pay Rs 1.14 Cr (60 + 54 lakh), 5.6% of SPEL PAT (DERIVED). [INFERENCE] The profit-share slice alone is too small to make leakage the main motive. The larger exposure is the SPEL guarantee of Rs 14.70 Cr, which equals 87.8% of Danya net worth 16.74 (DERIVED), and the loop of volume with a BB- firm. The cost of the structure falls on SPEL minorities if Danya fails. The benefit to the promoters is the 10% slice plus the loan interest to the MD, amount NOT FOUND.

(e) Capital versus profit share. Fact: other partners' capital 4.07 (consol Note 6) against Danya net worth 16.74 (AR p.29). [INFERENCE, DERIVED] The other partners' capital is about 24.3% of Danya net worth against 10% of profit share. The partnership deed (withdrawal rights, capital ratio, interest on capital, exit) is NOT FOUND. This is the document that decides whether SPEL's 90% is a real economic claim.

(f) Jai Bharath. Fact: purchase 1.54 from a firm where the MD and WTD are partners, sales 0.01, receivable 0.57 (standalone) up from 0.09 (AR Note 21). Fact: sales fell 99%. [INFERENCE] The Rs 0.57 Cr receivable against Rs 0.01 Cr of sales is not a trade balance. It may be an advance or a long-dated claim. Nature NOT FOUND (B02 finding 11).

(g) Disclosure gaps in the RPT table (B02 finding 11, VERIFIED against Notes): personal guarantees by Savita Pradeep and V Rajagopalan on the IndusInd loan are not in the AS 18 table (AR p.66, p.73-74). A Rs 15.00 lakh payment to the NED (Krishna Iyer) is labelled "Remuneration, Professional Fees & Sitting Fees" in Note 21. The Board report says non-executive directors receive "only sitting fees" (AR p.41). The two statements conflict by an unexplained amount.

Competing readings and what separates them:
- Reading A (structural convenience): Danya is a second plant and customer channel for state tenders, held 90% by SPEL, run on disclosed terms; the 10% promoter slice is a legacy of partnership days. 
- Reading B (value-shifting vehicle): the loop of volume, guarantee and cheap funding moves profit between SPEL and a firm where promoters hold a slice, and the BB- firm borrows on SPEL's name.
- The one observation that separates them: the Danya partnership deed plus a benchmark of SPEL-Danya transfer prices against third-party prices for the same transformer classes. Neither exists in the AR.
