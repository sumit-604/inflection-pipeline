### 2C Growth embedded in capex

Method per instruction: capex under execution x historical fixed-asset turnover, as a percentage above current revenue.

Historical fixed-asset turnover = total income / closing net block (screener Data_Sheet, Rs Cr): FY22 733.12/683.92 = 1.07; FY23 892.54/719.70 = 1.24; FY24 1,073.44/717.63 = 1.50; FY25 1,302.40/978.00 = 1.33; FY26 1,499.79/1,383.88 = 1.08. Five-year average 1.245x. FY26 is depressed because Dombivli sits in the block with a few weeks of revenue.

Capex counted (Rs Cr): Pune South 750 + Mira Road 400 + BKC 954 + Thane 25 = 2,129. Dombivli phase 2 and the IV plant are excluded (cost NOT FOUND for the first; not a bed project for the second).

Arithmetic: 2,129 x 1.245 = 2,650 Rs Cr of implied incremental annual revenue. Base: FY26 total income Rs Cr 1,499.8 (Inv. Pres. Q2 p.8). 2,650 / 1,499.8 = 176.7% above FY26.

Sensitivity: at FY26 turnover 1.084, 2,307 Cr, 153.8%; at FY24 peak 1.50, 3,185 Cr, 212%.

Cross-check by bed-days (🔍 inference): 1,200 new beds (Pune 500 + Mira 300 + BKC 400) x census share 84.4% (1,063 census / 1,259 operational, Inv. Pres. Q2 p.7) = 1,013 census beds. x 365 x 64.8% occupancy (ex-Dombivli H1 FY27) x Rs 71,800 ARPOB = Rs Cr 1,720 = 114.7% above FY26. This holds today's ARPOB and mature occupancy from day one. It is generous on ramp time and silent on price.

Read. The capex method (177%) overstates, because turnover on a depreciated block runs above turnover on fresh cost. The bed method (115%) assumes no ramp. The reported figure uses the instruction's method: 177%, with 115% as the floor cross-check. Delivery spans CY28 to CY31, so this is a five-year, not a one-year, embedded growth, and it excludes the Dombivli ramp from 200 to 500 beds. Only Pune South (about 35% of the counted capex) is under construction today. The growth rests on bed supply, not on a pricing edge: mgmt says mature ARPOB grows with inflation only (Q2 FY27 call p.9).
