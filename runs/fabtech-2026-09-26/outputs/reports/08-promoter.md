### 2B. Criminal cases

**Against FABTECH itself: none** (RHP p.410, reaffirmed by no adverse web
finding). **Against Promoters personally**: one pending matter. In a
dispute where FTIPL (group company) successfully prosecuted a NI Act
cheque-dishonour complaint against Centech Engineering Ltd and won a

FTIPL/Aasif Khan/Arbani's revision application against that summons is
pending, and the Sessions Court has **stayed** the counter-complaint
(28-Mar-2025) [✅ VERIFIED, RHP disclosure]. Read in context — a
retaliatory complaint filed after the complainant lost the underlying NI
Act case FTIPL won — but it remains an **outstanding criminal proceeding
naming a Promoter personally** and must be named as such, not
minimised.

Two further criminal items are FILED BY (not against) group entities:
FABTECH's own NI Act recovery complaint against a former employee who
admitted taking illegal vendor kickbacks (Rs 3.75 lakh recovery sought,
partial recovery already made) — the company caught and pursued internal
fraud, a mild positive signal for internal controls, not a promoter
issue. FTIPL's FIR (2012, still open, unprosecuted further) against a
former GM for a Rs 34 lakh fraudulent scrap sale — again the company as
victim.

Group company Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Ltd. has an open FIR
(0242/2023, Bengaluru) for an accidental worker death; settled via
insurance and Lok Adalat, case not further prosecuted, FIR technically
still open — group-level, low materiality, insurance-resolved.
