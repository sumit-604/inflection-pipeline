## 5. TRIPWIRE TEST (Notion pre-committed triggers vs this deck)

**ROCE < 18% = thesis-broken trigger.** NOT fired. Deck slide 10: FY26 ROCE 20.4%, ~2.4pp above the 18% line. But the series is 43.1 (FY22) -> 37.0 -> 31.3 -> 23.6 (FY25) -> 20.4 (FY26*): six years of monotonic compression, ROE likewise 38.0 -> 14.1. Cross-referencing the companion filing, the compression is corroborated by real cost pressure: consol depreciation +32% YoY (7.84 vs 5.93 Cr) and finance cost +96% YoY (0.35 vs 0.18 Cr), both rising faster than the return base. Additionally, the 03-Feb-2026 Rs ~55 Cr preferential raise sits largely unspent (Rs 11.14 Cr idle, General Corporate Purpose only Rs 1.64 Cr of Rs 12.02 Cr used — companion Reg 32), inflating the FY27 capital-employed denominator before the capex earns. **Net: ROCE is above the trigger but the trajectory plus an inflated denominator make a sub-18% print a live near-term risk. The deck's "Planned Capacity Expansion" asterisk (A3-F06) reframes the decline as intentional and does not discuss it candidly. FLAG, not fired.** A3-F08 must be resolved first: the deck's ROCE definition (FY25 23.6%) differs from the Notion MD&A ROCE (prior 28.43%), so the very number the tripwire watches is defined two ways in the company's own documents.

**Skid economics vs the Rs 325 Cr peak cap and the 20-22% guide.** Q1 skid revenue 32.4 Cr = 22.3% of consol revenue (145.38) — at/just above the top of the guided 20-22% band. Annualised run-rate ~130 Cr, far under the Rs 325 Cr peak-skid cap, so no cap breach. But the Notion ~Rs 37.5 Cr estimate was missed by ~14% (32.4 vs 37.5), and the miss is entirely an ASP effect: volume 1,040 beat expectation while ASP fell to Rs 3,11,459 (A3-F07). **FORWARD-SIGNAL: reaching the Rs 325 Cr cap now needs even more volume as ASP declines.** Mix / discounting-to-win-the-anchor / scale pass-through must be disaggregated -> A4.

**CFO/PAT capex-absorption concern.** Deck presents no cash flow at all (silence, A3-F11). Indirect corroboration of the absorption gap: fresh Rs 55 Cr equity raised and only partly deployed, plus the rising D&A/finance-cost profile. Cannot test CFO/PAT from the deck; flag for concall.

**Single-customer / India-only exclusivity.** A3-F10. Deck leans harder into the concentration (anchor customer front-and-centre slide 13; domestic +162.98%, mix 28->42%) without naming the customer or quantifying dependence. Notion's previously-untested India-only-exclusivity pillar is now the growth engine on display — raises, not lowers, the concentration question.

**Italica RPT governance.** Deck is silent (Italica is a non-consolidating fellow subsidiary). No RPT figure to test here; the cleanest metric (FY27 RPT cap in the AGM notice) is not a deck artifact.

No thesis-broken condition has fired. Consistent with Notion's standing rule: flag, do not decide.
