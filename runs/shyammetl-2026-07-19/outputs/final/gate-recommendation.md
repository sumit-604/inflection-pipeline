- Consolidated working capital deterioration: inventory up 37.7% and finished goods up 53.7% against revenue up 14.7%, with the NRV writedown expense up 9.4 times (B02, Note 11 consol p.291-292). Standalone trade payables were paid down 31.8% (payment days 92 to 67) while consolidated payables rose 23.2% (B02, Note 26 standalone and consol p.305).
- Standalone cash conversion collapse: standalone CFO/PAT fell to 0.57x from 1.44x the prior year, and standalone free cash flow was negative Rs 223.49 cr in a year standalone PAT grew 39.3% (B03, CF Statement p.198). The collapse is mechanically explained by a roughly Rs 924 cr negative trade payables swing, an inventory build, and intra group funding into subsidiary SSPL (Rs 800 cr OCD plus loan movements) (B03; B02 rank 12).

Why GROWTH-INDUCED, not structural: consolidated net working capital days actually improved to 9 from 22 (B04); the balance sheet is net cash and carries CRISIL AA plus (Stable) validation of the self funded Rs 13,902 cr capex programme (B07, ratings.pdf); the standalone cash drain is deployment into an active build out, not an inability to generate cash. If the standalone drain persists after capex tapers and SSPL profit keeps falling, the read flips to structural.

Single falsifying quarterly metric (GROWTH-INDUCED): consolidated finished goods inventory growth exceeding 2 times revenue growth for a second consecutive quarter into Q1 FY27, with the NRV writedown expense rising again. That flips the read from growth timing to structural demand softness and reclassifies this flag. Falsification threshold: FG inventory growth greater than 2x revenue growth, next quarter (Inventory note, quarterly results).
