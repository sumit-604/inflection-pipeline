Management Commentary 
5
Mahesh Makhija
Founder, Chairman and Managing Director  
Q1FY27 marks a strong start to the year for QMS Medical Allied Services, reflecting the strength of our integrated 
healthcare business model and the investments made over the past year. We delivered revenue of Rs. 56.9 crore, 
growing 22% YoY, while EBITDA increased 27% YoY to Rs. 8.3 crore, with EBITDA margins improving to 14.6%. PAT 
stood at Rs. 4 crore, representing a 25% YoY growth.
Our Products business continued to demonstrate healthy traction, supported by our expanding distribution 
network, Q-Devices portfolio and presence across QMSMEDS, e-Grameen and other digital platforms. At the 
same time, our Services business continued to emerge as a key growth engine, driven by deeper pharma 
partnerships, increasing adoption of Patient Support Programs and the scaling of our healthcare camp operations.
We currently hold a 76% stake in Saarathi Healthcare and expect to acquire the balance stake by the end of 
Q2FY27. This will further strengthen our capabilities in disease management and Patient Support Programs, 
enabling us to address the entire patient journey more comprehensively. During the quarter, we also conducted 
11,497 B2B health camps, demonstrating our growing execution capabilities and reach.
As we move forward, our focus remains on scaling Patient Support Programs, strengthening strategic 
partnerships, expanding Q-Devices and Point-of-Care offerings, and leveraging our technology platform to deliver 
more integrated and scalable healthcare solutions. 
We remain confident that our growing capabilities, strong customer relationships and expanding presence across 
the healthcare ecosystem position QMS well for sustainable growth and long-term value creation.
