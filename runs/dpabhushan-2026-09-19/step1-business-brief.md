## The engine the thesis would rest on

A store-count and mix transition, tested against a gold-price tailwind. The
bull reading: a tier-2/3 regional leader quadruples stores to 51 and doubles
the studded share, so margin rises on mix and making charges. The bear
reading: FY26-Q1 FY27 margin is an inventory holding gain on rising gold, and
growth is price inflation on flat grams, funded by rising debt and negative
CFO. The observation that separates them: gross margin in a flat or falling
gold-price quarter, alongside gold grams sold.
