"Best Ever Performance" — Timex Group India Limited reports best ever financial performance.
- Breaking all records, the Company delivered its highest ever annual revenue growing 48%
  over the previous year.
- The Company also recorded its highest ever EBITDA (growing 134% over previous year) and
  PBT (growing 138% over previous year).
- Timex led growth across the brand portfolio, driven by growing resonance with consumers
  across the country. Momentum came from rising acceptance of premium franchises and strong
  engagement with fresh, culture-led collaborations. Guess followed close with a strong
  growth momentum of its own.
- Robust growth across E-Commerce and Trade channels significantly accelerated overall
  business performance.
- In Q4'FY26 revenue growth momentum accelerated with a 73% YOY growth in revenue and a
  172% growth in EBITDA.

===== PAGE 19 =====
"Financial Performance for Q4 & FY 2025-26" — Profit before exceptional items & tax tripled
for Q4 and grew to 2.5x for the financial year.
- Total Income for the quarter Rs 23,583 lacs, growth of 73% over Rs 13,611 lacs in the
  corresponding quarter last year. Annual growth 48% year-on-year.
- EBITDA before exceptional items for the quarter Rs 4,100 lacs (grew to 2.7x), vs Rs 1,508
  lacs corresponding quarter last year. Annual EBITDA before exceptional items Rs 11,614
  lacs (grew to 2.3x), vs Rs 4,968 lacs last year.
- Profit before exceptional items and tax for the quarter Rs 3,868 lacs (grew to 3x), vs
  Rs 1,304 lacs corresponding quarter last year. Annual PBT before exceptional items Rs
  10,725 lacs (grew to 2.5x), vs Rs 4,277 lacs last year.
- The impact of the new Labour Codes resulted in a one-time increase of Rs 531 lakhs towards
  gratuity and leave encashment liabilities, classified as an Exceptional Item. Excluding
  this impact, core operating performance remained unaffected for the year ended 31 March
  2026.
- Profit before tax for the quarter Rs 3,658 lacs (grew to 2.8x), vs Rs 1,304 lacs
  corresponding quarter last year. Annual PBT Rs 10,194 lacs (grew to 2.4x), vs Rs 4,277
  lacs last year.
- Other expenses included advertising and sales promotion expenditure of Rs 2,270 lacs for
  the quarter and Rs 6,603 lacs for the financial year, vs Rs 1,026 lacs and Rs 4,390 lacs
  respectively in the corresponding periods last year. Royalty expenses stood at Rs 463
  lacs for the quarter and Rs 2,772 lacs for the financial year, vs Rs 632 lacs and Rs 2,154
  lacs respectively in the corresponding periods last year.

===== PAGE 47 =====
"About TGIL" — Timex Group India Limited (TGIL) is a group company of Tanager Group B.V.
(formerly known as Timex Group B.V.). Entered India in 1988. Locations: Noida
(Headquarters), New Delhi (Registered Office), Baddi, Himachal Pradesh (Factory); Regional
Sales Offices in Noida, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Chennai.
Timeline: 1988-1990 joint venture between Timex Group and Jayna Times Industries Ltd.;
1990-2000 joint venture with Titan Company, ended in 2000; listed on BSE since 1994;
subsidiary of Timex Group Luxury Watches B.V. since 2000.

===== PAGE 51 =====
"Baddi Plant: Superior Watchmaking & Supply Chain" — SA8000:2014 and ISO 45001:2018
certified watch assembly unit in Baddi, Himachal Pradesh. Assembles watches from piece
parts to complete watch; handles over 150 types of movements and a wide array of
cases/dials/attachment types. Includes assembly of Analog Quartz, Mechanical, Digital,
Ana-Digi and Connected watches. "The unit produced about 4 Mn units last year with capacity
increased to 6 Mn units, in a single-shift operation, this year" (i.e. ~4 million units
produced in FY25, capacity raised to 6 million units for FY26, single shift). Online and
offline assembly capability for high and low volume movements. Timex is Government
e-Marketplace (GeM) registered.
