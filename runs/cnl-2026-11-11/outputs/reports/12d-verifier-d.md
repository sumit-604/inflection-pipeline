## 4. Findings

1. MINOR. B06 Q10 "Peers silent" and flags state "Super Micro (name not in any transcript)". This is false. RPTECH Q1 FY27 (Pansari, l.605): "we have continued to participate with NVIDIA, with Supermicro and with other OEMs" in neo-cloud / data-centre opportunities; RPTECH Q2 FY26 (l.772) lists Supermicro among OEMs that build GPU servers and sell via distributors. This is channel-presence evidence, not revenue, so the UNVERIFIABLE verdict on CNL's Super Micro revenue stands, but the sentence should be corrected and the two quotes added as partial corroboration that Supermicro products flow through Indian distributors.
2. MINOR. Q10a VERIFIED leans on RPTECH for "converting to revenue", but B06's own text says RPTECH did no project business in FY26 and its AI-DC revenue was the FY25 Yotta project. Current conversion rests on REDINGTON alone (TSG +91%, INR3,800 Cr large deals). Two peers exist for the demand theme, but one for current revenue; PARTIALLY VERIFIED wording would be safer. Not escalated to MAJOR because RPTECH's FY25 project and Aug-26 neo-cloud strategy are real revenue evidence for the theme.
3. MINOR (industry-context miss). CPPLUS Q2 FY26 (Khemka, l.638-650) on Honeywell is used only in 2D as an indirect mention. The fuller statement is claim-relevant to Q1: global brands like Honeywell have enterprise/government DNA, "will take a lot of time" to cover mass distribution, and brands that "only source from others" are not a major share play. That bears on a Honeywell licensee outsourcing manufacture; B06 should have carried it into Q1/Q7 as an adverse-to-share-gain signal. Verdicts unchanged.

No CRITICAL or MAJOR findings.
