## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION
### 1A Boundaries
- Product: steel tubular onshore wind turbine towers, up to 140 m, plus internals (ladders, platforms, cable racks). Source: H1 deck p.14 to p.16.
- Geography: India onshore. Anawil's plants are Koppal (Karnataka) and Kutch (Gujarat). Towers travel by road, and the customer arranges transport (Prospectus p.115). A 38-tower order from Koppal serves an Andhra Pradesh project (Press release 2026-09-10; B07). No state subtraction is made because site geography is NOT FOUND (B04).
- Customer: turbine makers (WTG OEMs) and renewable developers. Order book names Envision and Sany towers (H1 deck p.21; orders 2026-09-10, 2026-10-29).
- Channel: third-party fabrication sold to the OEM or developer. In-house OEM towers (Inox Wind) are inside the TAM and outside the SAM.
- Price basis: three bases, because contract models differ about sixfold.
  - Full tower value (steel included, Model 3): about Rs 1.07 to 1.11 Cr per MW. 38 towers x 3.3 MW = 125.4 MW for Rs 133.98 Cr ex GST = Rs 1.068 Cr/MW (Order 2026-09-10). 10 towers x 5 MW = 50 MW for Rs 58.32 Cr incl. GST; ex 5% GST Rs 55.54 Cr = Rs 1.111 Cr/MW (Intimation 2026-10-29). GST 5% is derived from Rs 140.68 / 133.98 Cr in the 2026-09-10 order.
  - Billing blend (headline basis): Rs 395.58 Cr / 320 towers = Rs 1.236 Cr per tower (H1 deck p.21). Note the book mixes ex-GST and GST-inclusive values (B04).
  - Conversion only (Model 1): Rs 58.7 lakh per tower (Sany: Rs 55.8 Cr / 95 towers, H1 deck p.21).
- Includes: new-build onshore. Excludes: offshore (no project in operation, Prospectus p.100), lattice and concrete towers, transmission and telecom towers. Repowering (potential 25,406 MW, minimum 3 MW and hub above 120 m; Prospectus p.100) is a lever, not in TAM.

FY26 realisation Rs 68.2 lakh per tower is B04 derived (143.27 Cr / 210). The mix gain is the Model 3 step (51.3% of book, 64.3% of H1 revenue) and carries 14 to 16% margin.
Floor case (6.05 GW flat, 4.0 MW, share 42.1% + 3 pp yr3 / +5 pp yr5): FY29 225 towers, Rs 278 Cr, CAGR 24.8%; FY31 235 towers, Rs 291 Cr, CAGR 15.2%.
Against guidance: FY27 midpoint Rs 475 Cr / Rs 960 Cr = 49.5% of SAM; at the H1 realisation of Rs 143.9 lakh that is 330 towers, 42.5% of SAM (+6.7 pp in one year). From the Rs 475 Cr base the SOM grows 0.2% a year to FY29 and 8.0% a year to FY31. Revenue beyond FY27 therefore rides market growth (SAM about 13% a year, FY27 to FY31), not share gain, unless SAM is wider than 33%.

- Committed: CWIP Rs 30.69 Cr x 1.05 = Rs 32.2 Cr, 22.5% of FY26 revenue (B07). Capex plan: NOT FOUND.
- SOM FY29 386 towers (Rs 477 Cr): 63% of nameplate, 88% of the heavy-tower limit. Sufficient.
- SOM FY31 522 towers (Rs 646 Cr): 85% of nameplate; best half so far is 148 towers = 48% of a 306-tower half (H1 deck p.18; B05). Above the heavy-tower limit by Rs 106 Cr. Committed capex (Rs 32 Cr) does not close it.
- Verdict: sufficient on towers at nameplate; Rs 106 Cr gap by FY31 on the 474 t reading. The SOM is the optimistic side on weight mix and on utilisation; the capex plan is undisclosed, so it cannot be called optimistic or not.

### 5B Runway
- Headroom: SAM Rs 960 Cr / Rs 143.27 Cr = 6.7x (FY26). Against guidance midpoint 2.0x.
- TAM growth: 16.9% a year FY26 to FY31 (6.05 to 13.2 GW). Floor 0%.
- Company vs market: FY26 to FY27 guidance implies share gain from 35.8% to 49.5% of SAM. Beyond FY27 the SOM rides the market (13% SAM growth).
- Years to saturate: at FY27 guidance the company already holds 49.5% of SAM. No saturation date is computed (SAM definition too soft).
### 5C Class: GOOD
Reason: units grow about 17% a year on a roadmap that itself misses 100 GW; SAM is 6.7x FY26 revenue but only 2.0x guidance; the SAM is LOW confidence; supply entrants and in-house shifts cap the share. The repo defines no numeric matrix, so this is judgement.
### 5D SAM levers
Kutch for western OEMs (Gujarat plus Karnataka 43.95% of base); internals plant (Rs 1.0 to 1.1 Cr Model 2 per tower); repowering (25.4 GW potential, no timing: revised headroom NOT FOUND); more OEM approvals beyond Envision and Sany. None is quantified from filed evidence.
### 5E Output card
At 49.3% revenue CAGR implied by SOM (FY26 to FY29), with PAT margin of 12.1% (FY27 guidance midpoint Rs 57.5 Cr / Rs 475 Cr; claim tier), the earnings growth embedded here is 17.0% CAGR (PAT Rs 36.0 Cr to Rs 57.7 Cr); at the H1 FY27 PAT margin of 9.3% it is 7.2%. Five-year: 16.8% at 12.1%, 10.8% at 9.3%. P/E 20.0x at CMP Rs 391 (step1 brief, screener.in, unanchored, re-anchor at stage 11). The growth is below the 25% CAGR target on both margins, so the TAM cross-check does not support the current valuation unless margin holds above 12% or SAM is wider than 33%. Flag only; stage 11 decides.
