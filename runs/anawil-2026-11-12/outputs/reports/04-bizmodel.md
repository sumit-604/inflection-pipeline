### 1E Chai-stall version
A tailor stitches suits for a big shop. In Model 1 the shop brings the cloth and the tailor charges a stitching fee. The fee is high against his cost, so his profit looks big as a percentage. In Model 3 the tailor buys the cloth too and sells the finished suit. The bill is three to five times bigger, and his profit in rupees can be higher. His profit as a percentage looks smaller. But he now holds cloth stock, pays suppliers early, and carries the risk that cloth prices move before the shop pays. The shop also asks for most suits in the second half of the year.
