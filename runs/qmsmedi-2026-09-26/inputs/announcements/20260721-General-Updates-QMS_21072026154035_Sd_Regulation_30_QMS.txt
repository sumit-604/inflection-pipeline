We are pleased to confirm that SAARATHI HEALTHCARE PRIVATE LIMITED has successfully 
completed an independent assessment of its controls relevant to the SOC 2 Type II Framework based on 
the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. 
The audit confirmed that the organization has designed, implemented and operated effective controls over 
the reporting period to safeguard customer information, maintain system availability, protect confidential 
information, ensure processing integrity and preserve privacy throughout its healthcare support operations. 
The assessment covered patient support operations, healthcare CRM platforms, digital workflow systems, 
cloud applications, data management processes, call centre operations, vendor management and information 
security controls.
