## WHY Rs196 Cr A QUARTER DOES NOT MEAN Rs785 Cr A YEAR (seasonality)

The business is heavily back-loaded to Q4. FY26 consolidated quarterly shape:

| Quarter | Revenue | Share of FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 FY26 | Rs142 Cr | 14% |
| Q2 + Q3 FY26 | Rs437 Cr | 43% |
| Q4 FY26 | Rs446 Cr | 43% |
| Full FY26 | Rs1,026 Cr | 100% |

Q1 is the seasonal trough (~14% of the year); Q4 alone is more than double Q1. Holding the
same shape, Rs196 Cr / 0.14 = ~Rs1,400 Cr — before any growth, just from seasonality.

## MIPL TIMING NUANCE (pulls the full-year figure DOWN)

MIPL consolidated only from 01-Oct-2025 (start of Q3 FY26). So MIPL inflates Q1 and Q2 FY27
YoY (Q1/Q2 FY26 had no MIPL) but gives little YoY lift to Q3/Q4 FY27 (Q3/Q4 FY26 already
contained MIPL). Built quarter-by-quarter: Q1 196 + Q2 ~255 + Q3 ~250 + Q4 ~490 = ~Rs1,200-1,300 Cr.
That is why the honest base is ~Rs1,300 Cr, not the guidance Rs1,500-1,600 Cr.

## CORE (STANDALONE) ORGANIC GROWTH — the deceleration is real

Stripping MIPL entirely (standalone SRM only):

| Metric | Q1 FY26 | Q1 FY27 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standalone revenue | Rs143.1 Cr | Rs150.3 Cr | +5.0% |

This is a sharp slowdown: FY26 standalone grew +61% (FY25 Rs527 Cr to FY26 Rs851 Cr). Going
from +61% to +5% is a genuine deceleration, not noise.

## AWARDS vs EXECUTION — the government-capex-cycle worry

Revenue measures work BILLED (execution). Whether business is "moving" is the ORDER BOOK
(awards). They diverge this quarter:
- Order book ~Rs2,250-2,300 Cr at Mar-2026 (~2.2x sales); +Rs501 Cr fresh wins 30-Jun-2026
  (MSIDC Rs211, NF Railway tunnel Rs230, MoRT&H Rs60) to a claimed ~Rs3,500 Cr — roughly
  4 years of standalone work in hand. PROVENANCE: prior Notion work, NOT this filing.
- So on the evidence held, awards are NOT drying up; the issue is slow CONVERSION of the
  book into billed revenue in a seasonally weak quarter.

Likely reasons Q1 billing was slow (cannot confirm from a P&L-only filing): seasonality and
Himalayan terrain execution windows; mobilisation lag on newly-won June orders; and possible
working-capital drag if government payments are slowing — the exact number this filing hid.

CAVEAT: this filing has no order book / commentary / segment / concall, so award health is
from prior work, not the filing; and one soft Q1 proves nothing either way.

THE REAL TEST TO WATCH:
1. Does revenue ACCELERATE in Q2-Q4? With ~Rs3,500 Cr in hand, a business genuinely moving
   should show it by the year-end quarters.
2. Do working-capital days blow out at H1? Slow billing PLUS ballooning receivables = the
   government-payment-slowdown signal, hitting growth and cash at once.
A large order book that keeps NOT converting over 2-3 quarters is the red flag — not one Q1.

## OWNER EARNINGS — what a retail investor actually gets

- True per-share earnings is the post-NCI owner EPS (~Rs52 base), not the ~Rs57 headline:
  ~1 rupee in 11 of "group profit" belongs to MIPL's 49% partners, not shareholders.
- Even that is partly paper until cash converts: CFO is payables-financed (creditor days
  40 to 90), so reported profit runs ahead of cash generated; true free cash to owners is lower.
- Near-term cash yield ~nil: no dividend declared; reinvesting growth company. Return is
  entirely capital appreciation (re-rating toward the 18-20x destination PE + earnings growth).
- On Rs542 cost, base owner EPS ~Rs52 = ~10x — optically cheap, but that is the whole bet
  (re-rating conditional on execution, governance, and the cash question). If organic stays
  at +5% and the flattering effects fade, bear owner EPS ~Rs44 and the cheapness is a trap.
