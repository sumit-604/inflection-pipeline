Jyoti Structures Limited Announces Robust Financial Performance for Q2 & H1 FY2025-26 
Strong growth momentum continues across key financial metrics. 
 
Mumbai, November 13, 2025: Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL” or “the Company”), a leading 
infrastructure and power transmission engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company, 
announced its results yesterday for the quarter and half year ended on September 30, 2025. 
 
The Company has reported strong operational and financial performance, reflecting sustained growth 
momentum across its businesses and efficient execution of its order book.
