## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition (Note 2.4, p.321/614)**: Interest income recognised under EIR method per Ind AS 109 on gross carrying amount of non-credit-impaired assets; credit-impaired assets recognise interest as and when realised. Standard for an NBFC lender; not aggressive. 🟢
- **Ind AS 116 leases (Note 2.5, p.322/614; Note 50, p.378–379/614)**: Company is lessee only (office space). No short-term/low-value lease exemption elected. Discount rate = incremental borrowing rate (lease-specific) or SBI deposit rate basis for security deposits. ROU asset ₹383.70mn and lease liability ₹402.40mn as at Sep-25, up from ₹214.31mn / ₹236.31mn at FY24 (Note 10C / Note 50.1.1–50.1.2, p.331–332, 378/614) — growth consistent with branch expansion. 🟢
- **ECL policy (Note 2.14, p.325–327/614; detailed mechanics Note 49.1, p.366–370/614)**: Three-stage ECL model. Stage 1 = 0–30 DPD (12-month ECL); Stage 2 = 31–90 DPD (lifetime ECL, "significant increase in credit risk" trigger is >30 DPD); Stage 3 = >90 DPD (lifetime ECL, credit-impaired). PD/LGD computed on collective/portfolio basis from historical cash flows; forward-looking macro overlays applied qualitatively (GDP, unemployment, RBI rates, inflation) but no quantified sensitivity table disclosed. 🟢 policy design, 🟡 disclosure — no quantitative macro-overlay sensitivity given despite mention of overlays being used.
- **Management overlay (Note 5, p.328/614)**: A discretionary "management overlay" is added on top of the model in Stage 3 of the Hypothecated portfolio: ₹150.00mn (Sep-25), ₹145.00mn (Sep-24), ₹255.00mn (FY25), ₹200.10mn (FY24), ₹Nil (FY23). The overlay appeared in FY24 and has grown since. 🟡 — a rising discretionary overlay alongside rising Stage 3 balances (see §12/ECL below) both signals prudence and raises the question of whether the base PD/LGD model is keeping pace with actual deterioration.
- **Employee benefits / gratuity actuarial assumptions (Note 35.4, p.351/614)**: Discount rate declining with market yields: 7.39% (FY23) → 7.25% (FY24) → 7.04% (FY25) → 6.38% (Sep-25) — routine, tracks G-Sec yields. **Attrition assumption jumped sharply**: up-to-30-yrs band 33.10% (FY23–FY25 unchanged) → 40.00% (Sep-25); 31–44-yrs band 28.40% → 35.00%; above-44 band 6.20% → 15.00% (Sep-25). This is a discrete, large change in a single period, not a gradual drift, and it mechanically lowers the defined benefit obligation (higher assumed attrition = shorter expected tenure = lower liability). 🟡 flag — no narrative explanation given for why attrition assumptions roughly doubled/tripled in one half-year.
- **Fair value / ESOP valuation (Note 2.13, p.323–324/614; Note 39, p.356–357/614)**: Black-Scholes model; because shares are unlisted, "expected median volatility for listed peer group" is used as a proxy input — a judgment area, standard practice for pre-IPO NBFCs but a source of estimation uncertainty. 🟢 (disclosed, standard).
- **No first-time standard adoptions or restated accounting policy changes are disclosed** (Note 44, p.359/614: "There are neither new standards nor amendments to existing standards which has effect on the current financial statements and are effective for the annual period beginning from April 1, 2025"). 🟢
- **Auditor's Examination Report — Audit Trail (IT General Controls) finding (extract p.311–312/614, paras 6)**: For FY24, "audit logs at database level for the accounting software were not enabled and certain parameters of audit trail were not captured for loan management software" for part of the year; the deficiency was remediated with audit trail features "enabled and made effective from September 19, 2024 onwards" for FY25. No instance of tampering was found. 🟡 flag — an ITGC / audit-trail compliance gap disclosed by the auditor for a substantial part of FY24, remediated only from Sep-24; this is a control-environment observation an investor should note even though the auditor found no evidence of misuse.
