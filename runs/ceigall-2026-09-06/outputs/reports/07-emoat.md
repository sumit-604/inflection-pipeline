### 6B. Risks to each top-scoring (WEAK) emerging moat, with early warning signs
- **F2 (execution)**: risk is that the specialised-structure delivery record does not generalise to the much larger and more diverse FY26-27 order book (renewables, T&D, international are all new terrain). Early warning: further timeline slippages beyond the three already logged (B05), or a first missed early-completion outcome on a marquee project (Danapur, Jaipur Metro).
- **C2 (geographic diversification)**: risk is that diversification is geographic only, not counterparty — a state-government funding shock (e.g., Punjab or Bihar fiscal stress) still hits a concentrated government-client base. Early warning: a state-specific payment delay showing up in receivable/contract-asset ageing.
- **E1 (Arunachal first-mover)**: risk is pure execution risk in a security-sensitive, logistically difficult border region with zero track record yet. Early warning: any of the five packages showing land/security-driven delay in the next 2-3 quarters.
- **R1 (regulatory tailwind)**: risk is that the benefit is symmetric across the top-10 peer set and Ceigall's share of the resulting opportunity proves no better than peers'. Early warning: NHAI award data showing peers capturing a larger share of the newly-restricted bidder pool than Ceigall.

Ceigall does not fit either cell: the backward score is not merely average, it is AVOID-level on Gate 0's own deal-breaker rules (a real, evidenced cash-conversion failure — CFO negative 5 of 6 years against rising PAT — not a data artefact), and the forward score found here is NO MEANINGFUL EMERGING MOAT, not even MODEST.
