# FTTCP Gate Recommendation (Phase 1, evidence only)

## VERDICT: PROCEED WITH CAVEATS

This gate decision judges the evidence, not the stock, and no investment decision forms here. Growth is real and the balance sheet is net cash, but two active flags and an INDETERMINATE cash determination hold the ceiling below a clean PROCEED. Rule 3 fired because a flag is active; the INDETERMINATE cash conversion determination caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS rather than PROCEED WITH FLAGS, and names the missing evidence. Overall phase 1 confidence of 63 sits in the 60 to 74 band, which independently downgrades any clean PROCEED one level, so both paths land in the same place. No valuation ran in phase 1; the BUY, WATCHLIST or AVOID call, the entry zone, and the destination multiples are deferred to phase 3.

### PROMOTER FLAG: CONCERN

Top findings:
1. Chairman Mukesh Vasani is externally disclosed as CEO of Aimtron Corporation USA, the single largest related party counterparty at roughly 19 to 22% of FY25 standalone revenue on its own (B08).
2. The FY26 AGM sought a combined Rs120 Cr related party ceiling, about 76% of FY25 turnover, with no external valuation report (B08).

Transition evidence: American Pinball, a related party vehicle, sold to unrelated third party JB Vincent LP in January 2026 (B08); 0% promoter pledge, confirmed free of encumbrance for FY2025-26 (B08); promoter Mukesh Vasani a net buyer of 4,000 shares in the open market (B08); non family professional Sneh Shah elevated to Whole-Time Director (B08); SEBI LODR Reg 23 material related party shareholder vote regime applies from FY2025-26 with related directors abstaining (B08); related party loan balances shrinking toward nil (B08).

### CASH CONVERSION FLAG: deteriorating, cumulative standalone CFO/PAT -0.13x FY24 to FY26

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

Evidence: standalone operating cash flow was negative Rs17.69 Cr in FY25 against positive PAT of Rs25.74 Cr, a CFO/PAT of -0.687 in the year profit grew 89% (B01, B03); FY26 consolidated operating cash flow was negative Rs40 Cr, making it a two year pattern (B05); trade receivables rose 417% in FY25 against 70% revenue growth, with 46.3% owed by related parties and one Aimtron Corporation USA balance that flipped from a Rs1,061.76 lakh payable to a Rs2,152.54 lakh receivable while sales to it fell (B02); unexplained loans and advances to 'other parties' surfaced on the H2 FY26 call with figures cited inconsistently at Rs70 Cr, Rs58.3 Cr and Rs16.8 Cr, and the CFO could not answer (B12b).

Why INDETERMINATE and not GROWTH-INDUCED or STRUCTURAL: the growth is genuine, the balance sheet is net cash, and a post listing scale up plausibly funds receivables and inventory, which argues growth induced. Against a clean growth reading sit the related party concentration in the receivables, the payable to receivable flip on declining sales, the still negative FY26 cash flow, and the unexplained outflows to 'other parties'. The single evidence that would separate growth funding from related party value transfer is absent: the rating rationale is ABSENT because no rating PDF exists and the operator confirmed none is available (B00), and receivables ageing detail is limited (B02).

Missing evidence to resolve: a credit rating rationale with working capital commentary, obtainable only if a rating is initiated or from a future rating filing on BSE; and the receivables ageing schedule, obtainable from the next annual report Note 15 and Note 38. Because the determination is INDETERMINATE, the verdict caps at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS and does not resolve to PROCEED.

Single most damaging next print: H1 FY27 consolidated operating cash flow printing negative for a third consecutive reporting period, or debtor days staying above 120, which would move the cash drain from growth induced toward structural.

## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring)

1. Revenue per SMT line of roughly Rs100 Cr, contradicted five to six times over by peer disclosure of Rs500 to 600 Cr per line (Vinyas H2/FY26 call, May 2026, Sumukh Narendra). This inflates the capacity to revenue math behind the Rs1,000 Cr capacity ambition.
2. Defence framed as low margin, contradicted by disaggregated peer disclosure of defence and BTS at 20 to 25% EBITDA versus 9 to 11% in general EMS (Centum Q3 FY26 call, February 2026, Sundararajan Parthasarathy). Defence reads higher margin, not lower.
3. The RFQ pipeline described as static at roughly Rs700 to 900 Cr across three calls while all four peers grew order books over the same period, a comparative underperformance signal.
4. The headline TAM claim is inflated about 21 times because it quotes India's entire national electronics output target of roughly Rs43 lakh Cr rather than the addressable EMS market (B09).
5. Related party revenue stated as under 20% on the H2 FY25 call, unreconciled against 27.68% to 31.53% documented in the annual report (B05).

## Monitorables (act on these)

1. Operating cash flow and CFO/PAT: watch for positive operating cash flow and CFO/PAT above 0.7 in the H1 FY27 half yearly cash flow statement; this is the direct test of earnings quality behind the cash flag.
2. Debtor days and receivables turnover: watch for a return toward under 60 days from about 118 in the FY27 results, Note 15 and Note 38; tests whether the FY25 receivables spike normalises or entrenches.
3. Related party revenue share and Rs120 Cr ceiling use: watch for related party revenue above 35% or ceiling utilisation outpacing proportionate growth in the FY26 annual report Note 35 and any interim related party disclosure; tests whether growth diversifies from same management counterparties.
4. Organic versus inorganic FY27 growth split: watch for the ex AIC/ICS organic growth number and management's reconciliation in the FY27 results and concall; tests whether the 40 to 50% guidance is real demand or acquisition arithmetic.
5. Loans and advances to 'other parties': watch for reconciliation and repayment of the Rs70/58.3/16.8 Cr outflow in the FY26 annual report cash flow and notes; tests related party fund leakage during negative cash flow.
6. AIC/ICS EBITDA margin ramp: watch for the acquired unit moving from about 11% toward the guided 18 to 20% within a year in the consolidated FY27 result; tests acquisition accretion.
7. Greenfield Vadodara SMT line commissioning: watch for the first lines operational and shipping by around Q4 FY27 per the phased schedule in company disclosure; tests the primary capacity catalyst.
8. EBITDA margin: watch for recovery toward or above FY24's 25.4% in the FY27 MD&A financial table; tests whether the operational efficiency narrative is real.
