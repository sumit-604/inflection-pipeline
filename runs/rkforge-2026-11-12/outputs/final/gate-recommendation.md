## MONITORABLES AND TRIGGERS

1. Net debt at 31 December 2026. Read it in the Q3 FY27 results and call, and ask for the basis. At or below Rs 1,700 Cr keeps the Rs 1,500 Cr March 2027 target alive. Another half year at the first half pace (Rs 126 Cr) breaks the deleveraging thesis and the credibility grade.
2. Receivable quality. In the FY27 annual report receivable note, check the share of gross receivables older than six months (21.7% now) and foreign currency receivables (Rs 556 Cr now). Below 15% and below Rs 450 Cr says the Rs 42 Cr provision closed the export book problem. Higher says the cash problem is structural.
3. Working capital at March 2027. In the Q4 FY27 consolidated balance sheet, compare receivables (Rs 1,025 Cr at September) and inventory (Rs 1,469 Cr) with H2 revenue. Growth faster than revenue again falsifies the growth explanation for negative free cash flow.
4. Financing outside borrowings. In the trade payables note and the receivables sold note, acceptances plus receivables sold should stay flat or fall from Rs 1,050 Cr. A rise means leverage is growing outside the borrowings line.
5. Bank stock statements. In the CARO report and the borrowings note, the gap between bank returns and books should fall within 2% each quarter, with the unit named. A persistent gap points to a control failure, not integration lag.
6. Capacity fill and EBITDA per tonne. On the investor deck capacity slide, total utilisation should move from 72% toward 80%, with EBITDA per tonne at or above Rs 0.38 lakh. Below Rs 0.30 lakh per tonne means the new fixed cost is not absorbed. This tests whether the Rs 2,491 Cr build earns.
7. Exports and the North America cycle. Export revenue should hold above Rs 400 Cr a quarter (Rs 425 Cr in Q2 FY27). Two quarters of decline fails the export recovery. Read it beside ACT Research and FTR Class 8 orders and any US tariff notice on steel and aluminium auto parts.
8. Rail wheel joint venture. Look for a written Railways acceptance of the first 300 wheels and December 2026 production of 2,000 to 3,000 wheels, in a Reg 30 filing or the Q3 FY27 call. Another slip, or an RDSO requirement surfacing, fails the only emerging advantage the moat scan found.

Falsification line: net debt at 31 December 2026 above Rs 1,700 Cr on the company's own stated basis, reported with the Q3 FY27 results.
