# Forward view — 2026-09-21 shallow screen, sixteen names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework, added by operator ruling
2026-09-21. Next year's bottom line, the growth rate, and the forward
price-to-earnings ratio, so the run can be ranked on growth against multiple.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result. Three names below screen well and mislead, and
each is marked.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the Bull AI company record at 21 September 2026. It is
a service record, not a filed document. Every earnings figure traces to a filing
or a company presentation, cited in the card.

## Ranked on growth against multiple

**Cheap and fast.** PRECOT, TLL, AVTNPL, QMSMEDI. Forward multiples of 9x to
21x against bottom-line growth of 34% to 135%.

**Fast, and you pay for it.** SHADOWFAX at 52.8x on +150%, BCCPP at 31.7x on
+164%. The growth is real and largely guided. The multiple already assumes it
continues.

**The FY28 story.** FILATEX screens poorly on FY27 at 20.4x on +12%, because
the year absorbs depreciation and interest before the capex earns. On FY28 it
is 14.4x on +41%.

**Expensive for the growth.** FABCLEAN, RSL, MARINE, SYMBIOTEC. SYMBIOTEC is
the extreme: 58x for about 10% growth.

**Not rankable.** ZOTA is loss-making. HSIL, YAASHVI and LIBAS have no forward
basis.

## Three that screen well and mislead

**INDORAMA, 17.6x on +23%.** The earnings carry **no tax at all** because of
carried-forward losses, in FY24, FY25, FY26 and Q1 FY27. On a fully taxed basis
FY27E profit is about Rs 101 crore and the multiple is 23.5x. Revenue also fell
31% year on year in Q1 FY27; the profit rose only because the spread widened.
The card's verdict is PASS and the screen does not change it.

**BCCPP, 31.7x on +164%.** The growth rests on **one quarter**. Q1 FY27 pre-tax
profit of Rs 5.40 crore was 66% of the whole of FY26. The largest single driver
was other expenses falling 21% sequentially with no note. Separately, you hold
the **partly paid line**, whose call money and schedule are NOT FOUND.

**PRECOT, 9.4x to 11.3x on +95% to +135%.** The cheapest number on the page and
built on one quarter, with no guidance anywhere because the company holds no
earnings calls. The estimate applies FY26's own seasonal shape: Q1 FY26 was
31.5% of that year's profit, so Q1 FY27's Rs 26.61 crore scales to about Rs 84
crore. If cotton turns, it does not hold.

## Basis, name by name

**PRECOT — Tier B.** No guidance exists; Bull AI holds none and the company has
never held an earnings call. FY27E applies the FY26 seasonal factor of 3.17
times to Q1 FY27 profit after tax of Rs 26.61 crore, giving Rs 84.4 crore. The
Rs 70 crore low end assumes partial mean reversion. Context that supports the
climb rather than the cycle: Q1 FY23 earned Rs 13.95 crore on Rs 253 crore of
revenue, a 5.5% margin; Q1 FY27 earned Rs 26.61 crore on Rs 236 crore, an 11.3%
margin. The margin doubled on lower revenue. A stale FY23 target to lift
technical textiles to 35% of revenue by FY25 was missed; FY24 was 25.5%.

**TLL — Tier C.** The only FY27 statement is "The outlook for FY27 remains
robust". FY27E applies +40% against a four-year profit compound of about 46%
and H2 FY26 growth of 46%. Two facts from the FY26 deck worth carrying: the
company holds **3,625 registration applications of which 1,091 are registered**,
and **domestic revenue reached 49% of FY26**, so the export framing now
understates it. Reports half-yearly; latest period ends 31 March 2026.

**AVTNPL — Tier C.** No guidance records exist. The company's own filing warns
that a quarter "is not indicative of full year's expected performance" because
of seasonality, so Q1 FY27 is **not** annualised. Annualising it would give
Rs 95.5 crore and a 13.7x multiple; that breaches the company's warning and is
not used. FY27E instead applies FY26's 31% revenue growth with a modest margin
gain.

**QMSMEDI — Tier A.** Management guided FY27 blended EBITDA margin to 18% to
19% against 15.0% delivered in FY26, and publishes segment targets of **12% to
15% for products and 30% to 35% for patient support programmes**. FY27E revenue
of about Rs 225 crore at an 18% margin, less finance and depreciation, gives
about Rs 20 crore. The Rs 16 crore low end is Q1 FY27 annualised with no margin
gain. It also holds 76% of Saarathi Healthcare and expects the balance by the
end of Q2 FY27.

**INDORAMA — Tier B.** Q1 FY27 annualised. No presentation and no earnings call
exists for this company, so no guidance is possible.

**FILATEX — Tier A, and the card understated it.** The current deck sets out a
Rs 690 crore programme with rupee EBITDA against each leg: ECOSIS recycling
Rs 300 crore capex for **Rs 80-85 crore EBITDA a year**, 26,750 tonnes,
commissioning **expected October 2026**; PFY brownfield Rs 235 crore for
**Rs 60 crore**, adding 55,000 tonnes; a **steam distribution platform Rs 85
crore for Rs 60-65 crore**; renewable energy Rs 30 crore for Rs 18-20 crore of
savings, lifting renewable share from 26% to 55% of captive power; automation
Rs 40 crore for Rs 4 crore. **Total steady-state EBITDA of Rs 222-234 crore a
year against FY26 EBITDA of Rs 346.50 crore.** ECOSIS is guided at 30-35%
EBITDA margins. FY27E carries only a partial year net of new depreciation and
interest. FY28E assumes the full programme less an assumed Rs 48 crore of
depreciation and Rs 32 crore of interest; **those two charges are my
assumption, not disclosed.**

**FABCLEAN — Tier A.** Management guided FY27 growth as "20 to 30 to 40
percent". FY27E applies the midpoint at FY26's 7.1% profit margin. Temper it
with FY26's own record: **revenue rose 46.93% and profit after tax rose only
18.95%**, from Rs 13.30 crore to Rs 15.82 crore. Margin compressed while the
order book grew.

**BCCPP — Tier B.** Q1 FY27 pre-tax profit annualised, taxed at an assumed 25%.
FY26 profit after tax is likewise derived from disclosed pre-tax profit at 25%.
Both tax rates are assumptions. Market capitalisation is the fully paid line.

**RSL — Tier C.** The corpus ends at the half-year to September 2025. The
estimate annualises that half. Revenue has been flat for three years at
Rs 910-948 crore; the profit growth is spread, not volume. FY25 converted
Rs 54.64 crore of pre-tax profit into Rs 7.08 crore of operating cash.

**SHADOWFAX — Tier A, and the guidance was raised.** Management states: "We are
now revising our FY27 revenue growth guidance from 27% to 30% that we had given
earlier to **38% to 40%**." It also guides **100 to 120 basis points of
profitability improvement a year to FY28**, and 200 to 250 basis points a year
after that. FY27E applies 39% revenue growth to FY26's Rs 4,202 crore, at the
4.8% profit margin already delivered in Q1 FY27. Capex guided at Rs 180-190
crore, intensity falling to 2.5-3.5%. 100 dark stores targeted in FY27.

**SYMBIOTEC — Tier C.** Listed 1 September 2026; no result exists as a listed
company and the only document is the prospectus. FY27E applies +10% against a
three-year profit path of Rs 100.06 crore, Rs 96.79 crore, Rs 109.90 crore. The
biologics facility is "yet to be commissioned".

**MARINE — Tier B.** **Bull AI holds zero guidance records for this company.**
FY27E annualises Q1 FY27 pre-tax profit, taxed at an assumed 25%. No order book
has been published since 31 March 2025, when it had fallen to Rs 524 crore.

**ZOTA — NOT APPLICABLE.** FY26 pre-tax loss of Rs 73.84 crore before
exceptional items; Q1 FY27 pre-tax loss of Rs 43.65 crore and widening. The
gate is group profit before tax narrowing for two consecutive quarters on
growing revenue. Management guides EBITDA positive in two to three quarters,
which is not the same thing: under Ind AS 116 the store rent sits below EBITDA.

**HSIL — Tier D, blank.** Reports half-yearly, latest period 31 March 2026, no
guidance, and the FY26 revenue doubling is unexplained on the record. A trailing
multiple of about 34x is computable and not meaningful next to operating cash
flow of minus Rs 32.02 crore.

**YAASHVI and LIBAS — Tier D, blank.** No financial statement exists in the
corpus for either.

## Corrections this exercise forced on the cards

Three cards carried numbers that the guidance search corrected. All three are
fixed in the same commit as this file.

1. **FILATEX.** The card said the capex adds about Rs 150 crore of EBITDA. The
   current deck says **Rs 222-234 crore**, and includes a steam distribution
   platform worth Rs 60-65 crore that the card omitted entirely. The PFY leg
   was revised down from Rs 70 crore to Rs 60 crore, ECOSIS up to Rs 80-85
   crore, and ECOSIS commissioning moved from August-September to **October
   2026**.
2. **FABCLEAN.** The card lacked FY25 profit after tax. It was **Rs 13.30
   crore**, so FY26 profit grew only 18.95% while revenue grew 46.93%. The card
   also carried the prospectus figure of 33.33% for the Kelvin holding; the
   stake is now **60%**, going to 70-72%.
3. **TLL.** The card framed the company as an exporter. **Domestic revenue
   reached 49% of FY26.**
