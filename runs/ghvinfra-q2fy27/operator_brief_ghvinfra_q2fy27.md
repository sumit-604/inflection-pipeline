## 1. Narrative

1. GHV Infra is a building contractor. It wins civil, rail and electrical jobs and hands most of the work to sub-contractors.
2. Its main customer is its own promoter's private company, GHV (India) Pvt Ltd. That customer gave 100% of FY25 receivables (Notion record).
3. This quarter, sales rose 63% from last year, to Rs 300 crore. Half-year sales are already 86% of all of FY26.
4. Profit grew less than sales. Profit before tax, without other income, rose 38%. Interest cost doubled and ate most of the gain.
5. A one-time tax credit lifted net profit to Rs 18 crore. No note explains the credit.
6. The cash went the wrong way. In six months the business used Rs 244 crore of cash, against Rs 30 crore of profit.
7. Debt more than doubled, to Rs 412 crore. Almost all of it is short-term borrowing.
8. The UAE subsidiary's big contract (Erisha, Ras Al Khaimah) ended early in September. The parties are now in legal proceedings. The company booked no loss for it.
9. No auditor checked the two foreign subsidiaries. The parent has up to Rs 25 crore at risk in them.
10. Management held no earnings call. The filing says only that it will "monitor and reassess" the UAE matter.
11. Good: a full audited-review filing, real promoter cash of Rs 50 crore, and a new finance head with fund-raising experience. Worrying: cash burn, short-term debt, the UAE dispute, and the promoter buying shares at Rs 80 while the market pays Rs 245.
12. Watch next quarter: any UAE write-off, interest cost, and what the Rs 452 crore of "other financial assets" really is. The decisive test is FY27 operating cash flow, due by 30-May-2027.
