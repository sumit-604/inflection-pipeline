## PASS 3: PATTERN RE-READ

Method: contradictions between notes, notes against primary statements, thin disclosures, restatements, post balance sheet events, going concern language. I re-tested the Pass 2 leads against the AR text.

P3-1. Receivable summary conflict resolves (narrows a Pass 1 red).
- Standalone Note 2.14 (p.113) shows FY26 "More than six months: nil", "Less than six months 4,485.25". The ageing in Note 2.13.1 (p.112) gives 3,606.15 + 377.54 + 472.79 + 28.77 = 4,485.25 undisputed. So the summary puts the entire undisputed balance into "less than six months". The nil is a mislabel. The ageing governs: over six months is 893.06 (19.9%) including 13.96 disputed. The FY25 summary split correctly (660.77 over six months, 4,669.25 under), so the FY26 summary changed method with no note. Derived.
- Consolidated (Note 2.13, p.142) shows over six months 103.04 (plus 13.96 disputed) and FY25 over six months nil. The parent alone has 879.10 undisputed over six months in FY26 and 660.77 in FY25 (p.112-113). A consolidated figure below the parent's cannot arise from adding subsidiaries unless intra-group balances were eliminated or the buckets are mislabelled. Zeacloud owes the parent for 568.79 of FY26 sales, so elimination is plausible [INFERENCE]. The notes state neither. Separating observation: the consolidated ageing table cited as Note 2.13.1 (NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT) and Zeacloud's payable to the parent. Rating: WATCH on top of the Pass 2 red.

The notes are detailed on mechanical items (CSR, gratuity table, car loans) and silent exactly where judgement sits: goodwill, the 221.96 other income, OD terms, forfeiture accounting, seller valuation, the summons, intra-group profit elimination, related-party balances. Note 14 promises balances outstanding and gives none.
