  years (₹40.59cr consolidation restatement, ₹4.41cr/₹1.91cr ownership-interest changes, ₹0.44 lakh
  reclassification) with zero explanatory notes; combined FY25 items alone equal 29.4% of consolidated
  reported profit.

  balance static for two years, independently flagged by the statutory auditor as a Key Audit Matter.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators surfacing only via a subsidiary's own audit report**: MSSTL's two years
  of cash losses (worse than its accounting loss) and its Audit Committee RPT-approval gap appear only
  in the CARO Annexure to the consolidated auditor's report, not narrated anywhere in the parent's own
  notes.
- **Related-party financing at undisclosed terms**: no interest rate is disclosed for any related-party
  loan in either direction (loans given to MSSTL/Merino Shelters/Man Offshore/MISIC, or the Limitless
  Contracting loan taken), a consistent gap across every intercompany financing line in the notes.
- **Internal document inconsistency on a core disclosure**: the segment-count contradiction between
  Note 44 and Note 49, left uncorrected in the same audited filing, alongside the stray "plastic
  products" reference.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal concerning accounting practices. Key concern: an unexplained ₹40.59 crore direct
credit to equity bypassing profit and loss, sitting alongside a promoter-entity bridge loan of the same
₹97.68 crore magnitude as a subsidiary's own disclosed short-term-for-long-term funding gap, and sub-1.0
times debt service coverage at both entity levels. Key strength: granular ageing, related-party, and
borrowing disclosures that make the working-capital deterioration and financing pattern fully traceable
despite the absence of narrative explanation. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
