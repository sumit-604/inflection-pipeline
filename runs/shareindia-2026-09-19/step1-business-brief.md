## The engine the thesis would rest on

A MIX SHIFT from a proprietary-trading earnings stream (volatile, regulation-
exposed, capital- and guarantee-heavy) to client and fee annuities (MTF
spread, algo software subscriptions, PMS/AIF fees, institutional broking).
If the shift is real, the multiple should move from a trading-house multiple
toward a broker/wealth multiple. If it is narrative, earnings stay a function
of F&O volumes and prop P&L, and the 0% 3-year profit CAGR is the base rate.
