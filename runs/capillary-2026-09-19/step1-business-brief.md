## What it sells and where the margin comes from
1. Cloud SaaS for loyalty management, customer data and engagement, sold to
   large consumer brands (retail, CPG, BFSI, hospitality, airlines). 415+
   brands, 20 Fortune 500 customers, 49 countries, ~700 staff at Mar-26
   (screener About, company-sourced).
2. Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 256.6 Cr: subscription Rs 242.4 Cr (94.5%),
   implementation and campaign services Rs 12.2 Cr. Employee cost Rs 119.8 Cr
   (47% of revenue), software and server charges Rs 45 Cr (17.5%)
   (https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/capillary-technologies-revenue-grows-43-to-rs-257-cr-in-q1-fy27-12226775).
   Margin is operating leverage on a people-plus-hosting cost base.
3. Five-year arc: revenue Rs 171 Cr (FY21) to Rs 735 Cr (FY26); operating
   profit Rs -64 Cr (FY23) to Rs 93 Cr (FY26), OPM 13%. FY26 PAT Rs 52 Cr
   includes other income Rs 39 Cr (Q4 FY26 alone Rs 31.7 Cr). CFO Rs -46 Cr
   (FY25) to Rs 150 Cr (FY26); debtor days 90 (screener consolidated page).
4. Concentration: top 10 customers 58.71% of FY25 revenue; four of them in the
   US (https://geplcapital.com/ipo/ipo_14_Nov_25_09_37_09_Capillary-Technologies-India-Limited.pdf).
   NRR 114% organic in FY26, above 120% average over the prior 3 years
   (https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/capillary-technologies-ipo-review).

## The engine (the one thing the thesis rests on)
5. Acquisition-led ARR scale-up plus operating leverage. SessionM bought from
   Mastercard for about USD 20 m, closed 01-May-2026, USD 32 m ARR, profitable
   within two months per management
   (https://thewisemarketer.com/capillary-technologies-to-acquire-sessionm-from-mastercard/;
   https://quartr.com/companies/capillary-technologies-india-limited_22533).
   It follows Brierley (US) and Kognitiv. The roll-up buys ARR at under 1x
   and claims to lift it to group margin.
6. Q1 FY27: revenue +43% YoY, +34% QoQ; adjusted EBITDA Rs 44 Cr (+132%);
   organic growth 17% at 23% organic EBITDA margin (management). ARR Rs 1,026.6
   Cr, +43% YoY. Exceptional loss Rs 33.4 Cr from a cyber-enabled banking
   fraud at the Czech subsidiary; reported net loss Rs 9.55 Cr
   (https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/technology/Capillary-Technologies-Q1-FY27-Revenue-Surges-43percent-Normalized-Profit-Jumps-132percent/6a71a2e510bce1fd203bd5e6).
7. Guidance: FY27 revenue Rs 1,000-1,050 Cr (Q1 FY27 call, 04-Aug-2026); aiRA
   AI products targeted at 5-10% of revenue this year
   (https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/capillary-technologies-q1-results-earnings-call-audio-released/47405788).
8. Holding Jun-26: promoters 51.45% (down 0.78 pt QoQ), DII 19.38%, FII 3.34%
   (screener). Seven senior management appointments on 01-Sep-2026 (BSE Reg 30).
