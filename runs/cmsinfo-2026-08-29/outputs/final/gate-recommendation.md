## FLAG-CASH (active)

Metric and direction:
- Trade receivables 1 to 2 year overdue bucket up 8.5x consolidated and 16.2x standalone year on year (B02 Note 12, CON p.131/147, SA p.99/147).
- Standalone loss allowance released 14.1%, from Rs 533.94m to Rs 458.80m, in the same year the standalone 1 to 2 year overdue bucket grew 16.2x (B02 Note 12/37, SA p.99/147).
- Post tax ROCE fell from about 25% to 16.6% consolidated (B03 MD&A Key Ratios, AR p.52; SA Schedule III ratio table).
- Core Cash Management services revenue down 4.5% standalone and 6.6% consolidated; segment result down 25% (B02 Note 19/38, CON p.124/133/147).

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

Weighing the two readings:
- Growth induced case. Capex nearly tripled from about Rs 154 Cr to about Rs 409 Cr to build for the SBI, HDFC, ICICI, IPPB, and HAWKAI ramp (B01 block_b_trend). The order book was about Rs 1,400 Cr as of December 2025 (B05; ICRA rationale p.1). The rating agency attributes the receivable rise to delayed payments from certain managed service providers and reports a securing arrangement. ICRA rating rationale, 31-Mar-2026, p.1: "ICRA also notes the increase in receivables during H1 FY2026 due to delayed payments from certain Managed Service Providers (MSPs). However, CMS has entered an arrangement with MSPs to secure these dues, and receivable levels are expected to moderate to FY2025 levels by the end of the fiscal."
- Structural or quality case. The aged bucket grew while the not due bucket fell 29% to 33%, so this is receivables ageing, not fresh billing on new contracts (B02 Note 12). Both direct peers improved DSO in the same window, so there is no sector wide cause (B06: RADIANTCMS 70 to 65 days, SIS 63 to 67 days). The standalone provision was released against a 16.2x ageing surge, a provisioning adequacy question (B02/B03 Note 37). The deterioration concentrates in the contracting core segment, not the growing technology segment (B02 Note 19/38). One overdue customer receivable was converted into a three year secured term loan of Rs 580.12m, a distressed workout (B02/B03 Note 7/48).

Why INDETERMINATE, not GROWTH-INDUCED. The one external opinion that framed the rise as transitory rests on 9 month data to December 2025 and forecast moderation to FY2025 levels by fiscal year end. The audited full year FY26 ageing shows the opposite: the 1 to 2 year overdue bucket did not moderate, it grew 8.5x consolidated. The ICRA forecast was not borne out in the year end audited figures. The evidence does not cleanly support growth induced, and it does not conclusively prove structural.

Missing evidence named (required to resolve INDETERMINATE):
1. Management's explanation of the standalone loss allowance release against the 16.2x ageing surge. Never given. The segment profitability and DSO questions were deflected every quarter (B02/B03 questions_for_mgmt; B05 repeated_evasions).
2. A post March 2026 receivables ageing disclosure showing whether the 1 to 2 year bucket moderates toward FY25 levels as ICRA expected, or whether the MSP securing arrangement collects.
3. Cash Management segment level profitability and whether its contraction is the currency supply shock or a structural loss. Segment reporting was foreclosed the same quarter the segment weakened (B05).

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Standalone and consolidated 1 to 2 year and 2 to 3 year overdue receivables buckets. Any further increase is unfavourable; moderation toward FY25 levels is favourable. Find it in the next annual report Note 12/37 or any quarterly ageing. Tests whether the receivables ageing is resolving or continuing.
2. Loss allowance coverage ratio, standalone and consolidated separately. Coverage should track the ageing profile, not diverge from it. Find it in the next Note 12/37. Tests whether the standalone release was justified or the book is under reserved.
3. Post tax ROCE, consolidated. Watch for recovery toward the 20% to 25% range; sustained sub 18% beyond FY27 is the red line. Find it in the MD&A Key Ratios table. This is the single most consequential number the deep dive surfaced; it tests the transition thesis directly.
4. DSO and CFO to PAT. Healthy is DSO stabilising near 116 to 120 days with CFO to PAT above 1.2x; DSO past 126 days rising, or CFO to PAT below 1.0x, is the fail. Find it in quarterly and annual filings.
5. Cash Management segment revenue and result. Recovery toward FY25 levels signals a one year air pocket; a further decline signals structural loss. Segment reporting was foreclosed, so watch the MD&A ATM Management cut. Tests the growth induced versus structural question.
6. Currency supply fulfilment normalisation, from 70% at Q1 to 80 to 85% now, target normal by end Q2 FY27, and whether CMS revenue responds in lockstep. Kill signal: fulfilment recovers but CMS revenue does not, which falsifies the external shock framing.
7. Services revenue against FY27 guidance of Rs 2,650 to 2,750 Cr, and Technology and Payment Solutions crossing 20% of services revenue by Q4 FY27. Find it in quarterly results and the investor deck.
8. FSS acquisition close and funding of the Rs 550m balance, and buyback terms against the Rs 340 approved price now underwater at CMP Rs 243. Find it in Reg 30 filings and the next annual report.
