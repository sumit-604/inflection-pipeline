AC contract maker bets on compressors

Virtuoso Optoelectronics makes air conditioners, EMS boards, deep freezers and now compressors on contract for other brands from Nashik, Maharashtra, and the Bharati family has run it since incorporation. FY26 revenue was Rs824 Cr, EBITDA margin 10.4%, PAT Rs15 Cr, and the shares trade at Rs509 for a market cap of Rs1,621 Cr, which is 108 times trailing earnings. Air conditioners are about 60% of sales, EMS about 15%, commercial refrigeration about 10%, and compressors and components the rest. A single customer and a single product, room ACs, sat above 75% of revenue in FY25, down from over 80% a year earlier.

The company is trying to become four things at once. It is moving from an SME listing to the BSE mainboard, effective 1 July 2026, which forces full LODR governance. It is moving from building to other firms' designs toward its own ODM designs. It is integrating backward into reciprocating compressors for refrigerators, which went commercial in January 2026 under a China+1 import substitution window. Foreign funds went from 0% to 11.29% of the register, with Malabar India Fund and India Insight Value Fund putting in Rs60 Cr of an Rs85 Cr FY26 tranche and Malabar committing Rs140 Cr in total. Promoters converted Rs60 Cr of warrants rather than let them lapse, and the company hired a professional CFO and an executive director from outside the family.

Gate 0 scores the backward record AVOID at 55 of 160, driven almost entirely by FY26 leverage. Net debt to EBITDA ran 4.0 times and interest coverage 1.73 times in FY26. Revenue compounded 24.5% and profit 21.5% across FY24 to FY26. Cash conversion is the sharp break. Operating cash flow ran 1.35 times profit in FY24 and 2.17 times in FY25, then dropped to 0.22 times in FY26. That drop lands in the largest capex year in the company's history, roughly Rs143 Cr, as the compressor line, AC, freezer and EMS capacity all ramped and inventory and receivables built. The cash call reads growth induced, not structural, but it rests on the working capital build reversing over the cycle, and Note 37 leaves most of those balances unconfirmed.

Accounting quality reads 4 of 10 on the FY25 audited book. A depreciation switch from written down value to straight line removed about Rs8 Cr of expense and explains roughly 75% of that year's pretax profit growth, with deferred tax nearly doubling behind it. Three entities that guarantee or mortgage company debt, including one, Filaments and Filaments, that sold the company its own head office plot for Rs24 Cr, sit outside the related party disclosures, and one of them shares directors named as relatives of key management in the company's own notes. Note 37 says most working capital balances are subject to confirmation, a Rs21 Cr government grant receivable has sat uncollected for over two years, and a Rs14.38 Cr GST dispute appears only in the CARO annexure, not the contingent liability table. The promoter pledge percentage could not be sourced at all, so the pledge deal breaker check stays unresolved rather than cleared.

Management earns a B on credibility, delivering some of what it promised and reframing the rest. It beat its own FY26 EBITDA margin guide, 10.4% against 8.5% to 9%, and brought the compressor plant in on schedule. Against that, the mainboard listing has slipped across four consecutive calls, the informal FY27 revenue target was quietly cut from around Rs2,000 Cr to Rs1,100 to Rs1,155 Cr without being called a cut, capex overshot its guide by 30% to 40%, and the FY26 PAT margin missed at 1.8% against 2.5% to 3%. Execution shows 7 missed against 4 delivered promises over the tracked quarters.

Four peers, AMBER, ELIN, EPACK and PGEL, confirm the parts of the story that matter most and complicate three claims. They confirm that OEM brands bringing AC capacity in house are not shrinking third party order flow, and that raw material cost pass through of 10% to 15% is real and industry wide. Every peer reporting FY26 shows margin compression despite that pass through, so the claim of margin resilience is not supported. Peers place the BEE demand air pocket in January and February 2026, not the October to November 2025 window the company frames. And the capex overshoot the company treats as a sector trait is specific to AMBER. An earlier apparent contradiction, a 40% versus 30% compressor import allowance, resolved cleanly, since 40% applies to the reciprocating compressors the company makes and 30% to the rotary compressors its peers make.

The market gives real room. India contract manufacturing for room ACs, reciprocating compressors and small commercial refrigeration is a Rs12,600 Cr opportunity on realistic assumptions, with a serviceable slice of Rs7,560 Cr and a five year reachable revenue of Rs2,533 Cr. That runway is rated GOOD, about 9 times current sales, and it implies a 25% revenue CAGR. Management's own Rs2,500 Cr peak revenue claim lands almost exactly on the independent five year figure, a 0.99 times match.

Valuation says AVOID. The four pillar destination PE is 14 times on the governing RRM track, a band of 13.5 to 15.5, and 20 times on the additive track, a band of 19 to 22, against 108 times trailing earnings. FY26 EPS of about Rs4.7 can compound to roughly Rs14.6 by FY30 on 22% revenue growth and a compressor led margin recovery, yet even 45% bull EPS growth de-rating into that earned multiple gives a negative return. The probability weighted four year CAGR to FY30 is negative 20.8% and the Hurdle Ratio is STOP against the Tier A 25% bar. Fair value on the governing track runs Rs114 bear, Rs210 base, Rs299 bull. The stock is buyable only below about Rs86, with a margin of safety price of Rs69, which sits roughly 59% under the lowest price the market has ever tested since listing, so it is a deep watch name, not an actionable one. The thesis comes out AVOID and it survives its own devil's advocate: valuation safety and management trust both read destroyed, growth triggers and moat durability weakened, and the single compressor commissioning around March 2027 carries both the returns recovery and the growth premium, so one slip zeroes both. The whole investable-business read rests on two one sentence operator overrides, that returns are recovering and cash is improving, and the first FY27 cash and ROCE prints are what will confirm or kill it.
