Formula note: the screener Data_Sheet's individual P&L expense lines do not sum to the reported PBT (e.g. FY20:
Sales 22.56 + Other Income 0.85 − sum of listed expenses (incl. D&I) 19.43 = 3.98, vs reported PBT of 1.56 —
a ~2.4cr gap in every year, indicating unlisted/rounded sub-lines in this scrape). EBIT and EBITDA are therefore
**not** built bottom-up from the expense lines; they are computed top-down as:
- EBIT = PBT + Interest (screener Data_Sheet)
- EBITDA = EBIT + Depreciation (screener Data_Sheet)

Two further structural gaps affect several lines below and are treated consistently throughout (never estimated
without a stated basis):
- The Data_Sheet does not split Current vs Non-Current Assets/Liabilities. Proxy used: **Capital Employed =
  Total Assets − Other Liabilities** (= Net Worth + Borrowings); **Current Liabilities proxy = Other Liabilities**;
  **Current Assets proxy = Receivables + Inventory + Cash & Bank**. Current-portion-of-borrowings is not
  separable but FY26 Borrowings (0.77cr) are immaterial to this proxy.
- Trade Payables are not reported separately from Other Liabilities (which also holds provisions/other current
  liabilities). **Payable Days proxy = Other Liabilities ÷ Revenue × 365.**
- Capex (purchase of PP&E/intangibles) is **not** broken out from the aggregate "Cash from Investing Activity"
  line. Per the framework's formula definition (capex = PP&E + intangibles purchase only, excluding
  acquisitions), this cannot be reconstructed reliably from this Data_Sheet. **FCF is therefore marked N/A (not
  in provided data) and scored 0** rather than estimated (B2, B3).

FY26 note: the AVANA P&L has blank cells for Power & Fuel, Other Mfr. Exp., and Selling & Admin — these do not
affect the EBIT/EBITDA figures above (computed via PBT+Interest) but do affect M11's selling-expense-% test
(handled below).

FY26 capital-structure flag: Equity Share Capital jumped from 0.79cr (FY25) to 22.65cr (FY26) — a ~28.7x
increase — alongside Reserves (21.01→36.43cr) and Cash from Financing Activity of +19.78cr (screener Data_Sheet,
FY26). This is consistent with a capital raise and/or listing-related restructuring coincident with the
Kaycee Industries → Avana Electrosystems rebrand, not organic retained-earnings dilution. Flagged for stage 11
(share count/EPS base) and stage 13 (synthesis) — see YAML flags.
