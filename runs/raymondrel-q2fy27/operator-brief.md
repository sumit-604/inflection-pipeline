### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Raymond Realty builds homes in Mumbai and Thane. Thane runs on its own land. Mumbai runs on joint development agreements (JDAs), where a landowner gives land for a share of the project.
2. Bookings rose 98% in Q2, to Rs 902 Cr. Collections rose 67%, to Rs 682 Cr. These are company-compiled figures. At the company's own share of each project, bookings rose about 81%.
3. Revenue rose only 7.4%, to Rs 749 Cr. Builders book revenue as construction progresses, so revenue lags bookings by years.
4. Operating EBITDA rose 15%, to Rs 106 Cr, at a 14.2% margin. Part of the gain is a label. Interest paid to the approval authorities (BMC, TMC, MHADA) sits below EBITDA. Counted as a project cost, EBITDA rose only 8.3%.
5. Profit fell. Finance cost rose 70%, to Rs 44 Cr. Net profit fell 12%, to Rs 53 Cr. H1 profit fell 13%.
6. Cash is still going out, but less. H1 operating cash flow was minus Rs 147 Cr, against minus Rs 319 Cr a year ago. Net debt rose to about Rs 916 Cr, from Rs 658 Cr in March. That is about Rs 129 Cr a quarter.
7. Two of the thesis's first warning signals are now lit. Net debt is above Rs 900 Cr. Gross debt is 0.75 times equity. The Rs 1,000 Cr red line has not fired. At the H1 pace it fires in Q3.
8. The deck disagrees with itself on JDA revenue. One slide says Rs 1,930 Cr to date. The project slides add up to Rs 2,549 Cr. At two projects, revenue exceeds total bookings. Nobody has explained the Rs 619 Cr gap.
9. ICICI Pru's real-estate fund will put up to Rs 210 Cr into the subsidiary that holds a Mumbai project. The filing names no instrument, coupon or put. On the call, management said the fund takes 60% of project capital and profit. If the money is equity, net debt falls. If it is debt-like and spent, net debt reaches about Rs 1,126 Cr.
10. Guidance weakened. Management dropped its 20% revenue guide. It kept 17-19% EBITDA margin, but H1 was 14.3%. The kept 20% bookings guide implies H2 bookings about 10% below last year. Debt will not fall for one to two years. A further Rs 400-600 Cr of equity may come later.
11. The margin stop needs your ruling. The press-release basis gives Q1 13.1% and Q2 15.2%, so a 15% stop does not fire. The operating basis gives 11.6% and 14.2%, so a 15% stop fires. If you rule a stop fired, the review proposes AVOID.
12. Watch next: the ICICI securities filing and first tranche by late November, the warrant money, the Mahim-2 launch by December, and Q3 margin on both bases by 14-Feb-2027. Decision Status stays WATCHLIST / AVOID until you rule.
