## NOTE 3.1-3.3: PROPERTY, PLANT AND EQUIPMENT, CWIP, INTANGIBLES (p.147-149) — NOT OPENED IN PASS 1

Pass 1's capex-funding discussion (Section 6/7, LBF3) worked entirely from the capital-commitments
figure in Note 35. It never opened Note 3, which carries the actual asset-movement numbers and
tells a materially different story about FY26 capex pacing.

- **Gross PP&E additions collapsed year on year: ₹88.30 Cr FY26 vs ₹308.55 Cr FY25 (Note 3.1,
  p.147)** — a -71.4% swing. Plant & machinery additions alone: ₹65.69 Cr FY26 vs ₹218.35 Cr
  FY25.
- **CWIP additions also fell: ₹147.09 Cr FY26 vs ₹202.86 Cr FY25 (Note 3.2, p.147) (-27.5%)**,
  and the amount actually capitalised (moved from CWIP into finished PP&E) fell even more sharply:
  ₹74.29 Cr FY26 vs ₹281.88 Cr FY25 (-73.6%). Closing CWIP consequently swelled from ₹20.81 Cr to
  **₹93.61 Cr (+349.9%)**.
- **CWIP ageing (Note 3.3a, p.148-149): ₹91.68 Cr of the ₹93.61 Cr closing balance is <1 year
  old** — the new capex programme is only just starting construction and sitting in work-in-
  progress, not yet productive. Nothing is aged >2 years (no stalled-project signal).
- Net effect: total PP&E net block grew only ₹1,158.00 Cr → ₹1,165.07 Cr (+0.6%) in FY26, after
  growing 25.7% in FY25 (₹921.12 Cr → ₹1,158.00 Cr).

🟡 Watch, directly load-bearing for LBF3. Two readings, one observation to separate them: either
(a) FY26 was a genuine pause/pre-ramp year ahead of the guided ₹500 Cr + ₹1,200-1,400 Cr
programme, with construction activity only beginning in the second half (consistent with 98% of
CWIP being <1 year old), or (b) the guided programme is running behind the pace the operator's
company-memory notes imply. The capital-commitments jump Pass 1 already flagged (₹25.42 Cr →
₹69.35 Cr) is the forward-looking counterpart to this backward-looking slowdown — commitments
rising while actual spend/capitalisation fell is the observation that argues for reading (a), a
pause before the real ramp, but this needs a management question, not an assumption.
