### The paragraph (postal ballot notice)

File: inputs/announcements/2026-05-20_postal-ballot-notice.pdf. Cover letter dated May 20, 2026 enclosing the "Postal Ballot Notice ... dated April 29, 2026". Quote: "Not Applicable. The Company has been making sizeable profits year on year. - Search result: no other transfer, slump sale or business-transfer filing to SSSL appears in the FY26 AR or in any Reg 30 filing in the corpus (2025-09-27 to 2026-08-24).

### Answer

- The corpus shows one transfer to SSSL, the Hyderabad operations, effective 01-Apr-2023. The notice does not say whether its "Discovery business" is that transfer or a later one.
- [INFERENCE] The notice explains FY26 standalone profitability. A transfer three years earlier would affect FY24 onward, not FY26 alone, so the wording leaves room for a later or further transfer. Hyderabad is described as research ("To meet the growing demands of our research operations, we have expanded to Hyderabad", Annual_Report_2025.pdf p.20), but no filing equates "the operations of the Company in Hyderabad" with "the Discovery business".
- [INFERENCE] The OCRPS of 3,150 is close to the 3,171 net assets transferred. The filings do not say the OCRPS were the consideration.
- Where it would be found: management question; SSSL's own statutory accounts and board minutes; any Reg 30 filing on a business transfer outside the corpus window.
