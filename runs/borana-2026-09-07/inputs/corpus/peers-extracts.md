# Peer corpus extracts (Bull AI page-cited): Borana peer set

## SWARAJ SUITING (SWARAJ) — closest weaver benchmark (air-jet, backward-integrated)
- Bhilwara: 123 air-jet looms, 2.10 cr metres/annum. Neemuch Unit-1: +72 air-jet looms adding 1.23 cr metres, plus an indigo dyeing range (72 lakh metres); first spinning project at Neemuch Unit-2 (22,656 spindles, 7,344 tonnes yarn/annum, Mar 2025). (Reg. 34(1) Annual Report, FY2025, Page 19)
- Greige-fabric capacity by plant ~85% utilisation; expansion +9.00 mn metres at Rs 500 Mn capex (Investor Presentation, FY2025, Q2, Page 21; FY2024, Q4, Page 25).
- Moved from job-work to grey-fabric manufacturing: "profit percentage in percent decreased ... raw material consumption increased ... profit in absolute terms increased." (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2024, Q4, Page 18) — shows mix shifts change margin optics.
- Raw material is cotton; MD&A flags competition from China/Bangladesh and cotton-price/monsoon risk (Reg. 34(1) Annual Report, FY2025, Pages 17, 20).
- Read-through: Bull AI cites ~19.3% EBITDA for Swaraj. Even this backward-integrated air-jet weaver sits below Borana's 24%.

## GHCL TEXTILES (GHCLTEXTIL) — "normal" integrated-textile margin
- Revenue Rs 611 (FY21) to Rs 1,335 cr (FY26); yarn ~88% of revenue, fabric ~12%; capacity utilisation 98-99% (Investor Presentation, FY2027, Q1, Page 6; FY2026, Q4, Pages 6, 17).
- Strategy: vertical integration yarn to knitted/woven/dyed fabric, specialised yarn, green energy to 75% of needs (Investor Presentation, FY2027, Q1, Page 11).
- Margin: "if you see our last 10 years, we have delivered 15% ... normal EBITDA for the current set of products itself is about 15%"; guidance 15-18% (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2026, Q4, Page 13).
- Read-through: a well-run, fully utilised integrated textile earns ~15% EBITDA through the cycle. Borana's 24% is ~9 points above.

## BSL LTD (BSL) — sector-stress / commodity floor
- FY26 revenue ~Rs 657 cr; segments 9MFY26 Furnishing ~48%, Fabric ~35%, Yarn ~12%, Cotton Fabric ~2% (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q3, Pages 9, 11).
- Growth levers are generic: "cost optimization ... product mix optimization toward higher-margin ... value-added" (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q4, Page 25; FY2027, Q1, Page 23).
- Bull AI cites EBITDA ~7.6% and ROCE falling 10.9% (FY22) to 5.9% (FY26).
- Read-through: commodity fabric/furnishing in a weak patch earns single-digit EBITDA with falling returns. The downside case for a weaver.

## Peer margin ladder (the core test)
BSL ~7.6% < GHCL ~15% < Swaraj ~19.3% < Borana 24%. No cotton/yarn peer reaches
Borana's level. The deep dive must show Borana's premium is a durable synthetic
value-added mix, not a PFY/POY spread plus the anti-dumping-duty change management
cited for the FY26 gross-margin gain (Borana Concall FY26 Q3 p.6, in shallow corpus).
