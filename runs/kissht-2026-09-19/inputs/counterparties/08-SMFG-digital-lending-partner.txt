Loan Details
Loan Details
Consumer Loan (Term Loan): Unsecured term loans (no collateral required), for financing various needs, for salaried as well as self-employed customers. With rate of interest starting from 12.5% up to 30% per annum, term loans available for loan tenure up to 60 months. Customers can avail loans starting from ₹100,000 up to ₹10 Lakhs.
Non-revolving line of credit: An unsecured financing option that can be availed to meet personal requirements. An approved credit limit is provided to the borrower for a loan tenure up to 60 months. The borrower can withdraw the amount multiple times, up to a total outstanding equal to the sanctioned credit limit. Unlike term loans, borrower has the option to utilise the entire sanction amount in a single go or in multiple transactions. The interest is only charged on the loan amount outstanding with the borrower, with rate of interest between 13% to 33% per annum. Financing options start from ₹1,000 up to ₹30 Lakhs.
--> Consumer Loan (BNPL): Short term financing option for consumers to make instant purchases, without having to pay immediately. Customers can pay back the lender as lump sum or in periodic instalments. With credit limit valid for up to 36 months, customers can avail the financing option for amounts starting ₹250 up to ₹30 Lakhs. Rate of interest for BNPL products start from 0% (subvention programs) up to 33% per annum.
--> Business Loan (Term Loan): Financing options for Medium, Small, and Micro Enterprises (MSME) with unsecured term loans up to ₹75 lakhs (starting from ₹50,000). Customers can start, grow and expand their businesses, with loans from 12 to 60 months in loan tenure. Rate of interest starting from 13% to 33% per annum.
--> Business Loan (Line of Credit): Business owners can avail credit lines to get quick and easy financing. With a Line of credit facility, borrowers can get a Sanction limit starting ₹1 Lakh up to ₹75 Lakhs, having a validity of 36 months. Borrowers can withdraw funds, up to the sanctioned limit, through one or more transactions. Moreover, interest would only be charged on the amount outstanding with the borrower.
-->
Business Loan (Merchant Cash Advance): Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) is an unsecured loan facility, catering to small businesses owners who require financing to meet their business needs. Smaller than a typical Business term loan, the MCA facility provides financing with loan ticket size starting from ₹1,000. Merchant Cash Advance loan is sanctioned to borrowers, basis details of their transactions or sales volumes.
Business Loan (Supply Chain Finance): Vendors and Suppliers can utilize Supply Chain Finance (SCF) loans to manage their cash flows better. With loans from ₹1 Lakh up to ₹10 Crore, borrowers can reduce the waiting time on receivables, and utilize their funds towards growing their business. The sanctioned limit for Supply chain finance loans can be used within 36 months of loan sanction.
