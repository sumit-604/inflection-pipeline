New Milestones Achieved 
Highest-ever quarterly sales: Rs. 1,019 crore in Q2 FY27, up 52% YoY

Pune, October 13, 2026: Kolte-Patil Developers Limited (BSE: 532924, NSE: KOLTEPATIL; KPDL), one of 
India’s leading real estate developers with a strong foothold across Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru, today 
announced its operational performance for the quarter ended 30th September 2026.

Key Operational Highlights: 
• 
Record Quarterly and Half-Yearly Sales 
o KPDL recorded its highest-ever quarterly sales of Rs. 1,019 crore in Q2 FY27, up 52% year-on-year. 
For H1 FY27, sales reached a record Rs. 1,636 crore, up 27% year-on-year. 
• 
Strongest-Ever Launch performance 
o During Q2 FY27, the Company launched approximately 1 million sq. ft. at ‘Vyana at The Reserve’ 
in Vadgaon, Pune. The project generated sales of Rs. 616 crore, marking the strongest launch 
performance in the Company’s history and reflecting strong customer response to KPDL’s product 
proposition and established presence in Pune. 
• 
Robust Half-Yearly Collections 
o Collections grew 33% year-on-year to Rs. 795 crore in Q2 FY27. 
o For H1 FY27, collections reached Rs. 1,509 crore, up 32% year-on-year, reflecting sustained 
collections momentum across the portfolio.

Improved Average Realization 
o Average realization for H1 FY27 increased 10% year-on-year to approximately Rs. 8,368 per sq. 
ft., supported by strategic price revisions across projects and a higher contribution from Mumbai 
projects compared with the corresponding period last year. 
• 
Business Development: Landmark Mumbai expansion  
o The Company strengthened its Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) portfolio with the addition 
of six redevelopment projects across Mumbai’s Western and Central suburbs and Navi Mumbai, 
with an estimated combined gross development value (GDV) of Rs. 6,000 crore. This represents 
KPDL’s largest annual business development addition in Mumbai to date and reinforces the 
region’s importance to its growth strategy. The additions expand the Company’s presence across 
key urban markets and reflect its focus on development opportunities in high-demand micro-
markets. 
Commenting on the performance, Mr. Hrishikesh Parandekar, Chief Executive Officer, Kolte-Patil 
Developers Limited said,  
“Our robust performance in Q2 and H1 FY27, with record quarterly and half-yearly sales and strong collections, 
reflects the resilience of our business. The landmark launch of ‘Vyana at The Reserve’ and sustained sales 
across our ongoing portfolio demonstrate our brand’s appeal and the relevance of our offerings. 
While demand has softened in the near term across the broader market, particularly in Pune, we have 
continued to deliver strong business momentum. The long-term fundamentals of India’s residential real estate 
market remain favourable, driven by urbanisation, rising aspirations and an increasing preference for quality 
housing. The sector is also witnessing consolidation, with established developers well positioned to benefit 
from evolving homebuyer preferences. With an established presence across Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru, 
we see significant opportunities to deepen our market presence and expand our development portfolio. The 
addition of six redevelopment projects in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region further underscores our 
commitment to MMR as a key growth market. 
Over the past year, we have strengthened our management team and organisational capabilities while 
sharpening our growth priorities. Backed by our three-decade legacy and a diversified portfolio, these efforts 
position us well to pursue the next phase of sustainable growth."

About Kolte-Patil Developers Limited:  
Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd. (BSE: 532924, NSE: KOLTEPATIL; KPDL), incorporated in 1991, is one of India’s 
leading real estate developers, with a strong foothold across Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru. In FY26, the 
Company entered into a strategic partnership with global investment firm Blackstone with the latter acquiring 
a 40% stake in the Company following a two-phase transaction involving the preferential allotment of equity 
shares and a secondary equity share acquisition from existing promoters.  
 
Kolte-Patil is a trusted name with a reputation for high quality standards, design-uniqueness, corporate 
governance, transparency, and timely delivery of projects. The Company has developed and constructed over 
82 projects, including residential complexes, integrated townships, commercial complexes, and IT Parks 
covering a saleable area of >34 million square feet across Pune, Mumbai and Bengaluru. The Indian Green 
Building Council (IGBC) has certified several of the Company’s projects. KPDL markets its projects under two 
brands: ’Kolte-Patil’ (addressing the mid-premium/premium segment) and ‘24K’ (addressing the premium 
luxury segment).   
 
Consolidating its leadership position in Pune, the Company forayed into the Mumbai Metropolitan Region 
market in 2013, focusing on society redevelopment projects that have lower capital intensity. The Company 
has signed seventeen projects (seven completed, three on-going, seven future projects) till date at prime 
locations across the city.  
 
KPDL has seamlessly navigated varied economic cycles enabled by one of the lowest debt levels in the sector. 
The Company’s long-term bank debt has been rated ‘AA-/Stable’, short-term bank loan facilities as ‘A1+’ by 
CRISIL and non-convertible debentures as ‘AA-/Stable’ by CRISIL & CARE.  
 
The Company’s growth trajectory, internal processes and corporate governance practices have benefitted from 
partnerships with marquee financial institutions like KKR, JP Morgan Asset Management, Portman Holdings, 
ASK Capital, Motilal Oswal, ICICI Ventures, IL&FS, Planet Smart City and Marubeni Corporation. 
 
Over the years, KPDL has received multiple awards and recognitions including Times Realty ICONS Awards 
2026 - Developer of the Year 2026; Times Realty ICONS Awards 2026 - Marketing Campaign of the Year 2026 
for 'Vyana at The Reserve'; Times Realty ICONS Awards 2026 - Residential Project of the Year for 'Vyana at The 
Reserve'; The ET Real Estate Awards 2026 - Residential Project - Township (West: Ongoing) for Life Republic; 
Asia Pacific Property Awards – ‘25-’26 - Residential High Rise Architecture India for 24K Manor; ET Real Estate 
Awards ’25 - Integrated Marketing Campaign (360 Degree) for Canvas at Life Republic; Golden Bricks Awards 
Dubai 2025 - Marketing Campaign of the Year for Qrious at Life Republic.
