Subject: Announcement for receipt of Letters of Award (LOA) from CMPDI 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We are pleased to announce that South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (SWPE) has got two Letters 
of Award from M/s Central Mine Planning and Design Institute Ltd. (CMPDI), a Mini Ratna Public sector 
company and subsidiary of Coal India Ltd. for Detailed Exploration of Coal in the state of Madhya 
Pradesh and Maharashtra. The aggregate value of both the LOA’s including GST is ~ Rs. 30.45 Crores. 
The work awarded in the state of Madhya Pradesh is a long term project and in Maharashtra is a short 
term project. Both the LOAs will help in further strengthening the order book and will contribute in 
increasing in top and bottom line.
