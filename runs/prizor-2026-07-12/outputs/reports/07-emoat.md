### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

**Method 1 (capacity-based, primary).** FY26 disclosed assembly capacity = 16 lakh cameras/annum,
scalable to 40 lakh/annum (Inv. Pres. slide 13) — a 2.5x (150%) headroom on the built footprint. If FY26
CCTV segment revenue of Rs 114.25 Cr is treated as roughly proportional to current unit throughput (an
assumption, since utilisation % is NOT FOUND), then:
Implied ceiling revenue on existing footprint = Rs 114.25 Cr × 2.5 = **Rs 285.6 Cr**
Implied incremental revenue = Rs 285.6 Cr − Rs 114.25 Cr = **Rs 171.4 Cr**
As % of FY26 total company revenue (Rs 147.94 Cr): Rs 171.4 Cr / Rs 147.94 Cr = **~116%**
This rests on two unverified assumptions (current utilisation ≈ 100% of the 16-lakh base, and constant
revenue per unit at scale) and on management's unquantified "low incremental capex" claim for the
40-lakh step-up. 🔍 ANALYST INFERENCE built on a 🎙️ capacity claim — flagged, not treated as audited.

**Method 2 (fixed-asset turnover, sanity check — flagged unreliable).** FY25 net fixed-asset turnover =
Revenue FY25 (Rs 71.09 Cr) / Net PP&E FY25 (Rs 8.27 Cr) = 8.6x. Applying this to FY26 investing outflow
(Rs 40.86 Cr) implies ~Rs 351 Cr of incremental revenue, or +237% of FY26 revenue. This method is
explicitly flagged as unreliable for Prizor: FY25's high turnover reflects an asset-light **trading** model
(finished-goods resale), not a manufacturing capacity relationship, so it overstates what the new
backward-integrated asset base can actually support. Not used as the headline number.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 116** (Method 1).

### 4B. Government policy tailwinds

non-Indian") surveillance products (Inv. Pres. slide 22, slide 34). No specific circular/notification number,
amount, or expiry date is disclosed in either document (NOT FOUND for the exact regulatory instrument).

peers certify (12-36 months is the standard qualification-timeline window referenced in the A1 rubric,
applied here by analogy — 🔍 ANALYST INFERENCE, not company-disclosed).

(152-176% YoY across lines) is already realised and explicitly attributed to it by management. Time to
kick in: already active. Sustainability: **Moderate**, not structural-forever as management frames it —
the barrier favours the whole compliant industry, not Prizor exclusively, and the durability of Prizor's
relative edge depends on how quickly competitors certify. This caveat is carried into the R1 score as a
🎙️ discount on the "permanent" framing while the underlying facts (certification + revenue delivery) are
credited at 📄 weight.
