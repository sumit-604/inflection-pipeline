## PART 5: CROSS-PEER HYPOTHESIS

Across the combined peer set, two patterns that no single peer states together are visible when the calls are read side by side: (1) all three peers were SIMULTANEOUSLY adding alloy/aluminium-related capacity through FY26 into FY27 on a multi-year secular thesis (premiumisation, EV, alloy-penetration growth), and (2) UNOMINDA explicitly reports a TEMPORARY alloy-penetration dip in Q4 FY26 specifically because GST-2.0-driven affordability gains pulled in a larger share of ENTRY-LEVEL vehicle buyers (who skew steel, not alloy), before penetration began recovering in Q1 FY27 as the mix normalised.

**Hypothesis:** the alloy/aluminium supply side of this sector is structurally running ahead of realised demand-mix penetration in any GST-affordability-driven volume surge, because a broad-based, price-sensitive demand boom mechanically dilutes the PREMIUM-mix share (alloy, higher-value castings) even as absolute volumes rise — creating a systematic lag where alloy/aluminium-heavy suppliers' margins and mix-share look weakest in exactly the quarters where headline industry volume growth looks strongest, and only catch up one to two quarters later as the demand base normalises back toward its higher-value mix.
