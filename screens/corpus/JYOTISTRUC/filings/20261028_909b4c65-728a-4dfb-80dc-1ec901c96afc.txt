Jyoti Structures Secures ₹648.91 Crore Power Grid Order for ±800 kV HVDC 
Transmission Line Package in Gujarat 
 
Mumbai, 28 October 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL), a listed engineering, procurement and 
construction (EPC) company specialising in power transmission infrastructure, has received a 
Notification of Award (NOA) from Power Grid Corporation of India Limited for Package TL05 of 
the ±800 kV HVDC Bipole Barmer-II and South Kalamb Transmission Lines project in Gujarat. The 
order is valued at ₹648.91 crore, excluding GST. 
 
The scope includes the supply, erection, testing and commissioning of ±800 kV high-voltage 
direct current (HVDC) transmission lines on a total turnkey basis. The project carries a completion 
period of 38 months from the date of the NOA. 
 
The latest award follows two orders worth a combined ₹949.35 crore received by JSL from Power 
Grid Corporation of India Limited on 26 October 2026 for Packages TL01 and TL02 under the 
same transmission project in Rajasthan. With the addition of Package TL05, the combined value 
of the three awards stands at ₹1,598.26 crore, excluding GST. 
 
JSL’s recent order wins also include a ₹689.93 crore contract from Gujarat Energy Transmission 
Corporation Limited (GETCO) for the 765 kV DC Pachchham–Saykha Transmission Line project in 
Gujarat. The company is also executing a 220 kV double-circuit transmission line project in Sri 
Lanka in partnership with Hayleys Fentons Limited to support the evacuation of wind power to 
the country’s national grid. 
 
Commenting on the development, Amit Dutta, COO, Jyoti Structures Limited, said, “The 
engineering and execution of ±800 kV HVDC transmission lines involve complex technical 
requirements and close coordination across multiple stages of the project. Our integrated EPC 
capabilities bring together design and engineering, manufacturing, prototype tower testing and 
site execution, enabling us to address these requirements through a coordinated approach. 
Attention to engineering detail, site conditions and quality at every stage remains central to how 
we execute transmission projects.” 
 
Jyoti Structures brings over five decades of experience in power transmission EPC, with 
capabilities spanning design and engineering, manufacturing, prototype tower testing, 
construction and project management. The company has constructed more than 37,600 circuit 
kilometers of transmission lines and delivered over 1,800 high-voltage substation bays across 
India and international markets. Its prototype tower testing facility at Ghoti, near Nashik, 
supports the testing of tower designs under prescribed load conditions, with testing capability 
up to 1,200 kV.

About Jyoti Structures Limited 
 
Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL) is a listed engineering, procurement, and construction company 
with over five decades of experience in the power transmission sector. Incorporated in 1974, the 
company provides turnkey solutions across design and engineering, consultancy, prototype 
tower testing, manufacturing, construction, and project management. Its capabilities include 
high-voltage transmission lines, substations, underground cabling and optical ground wire 
(OPGW) projects in India and international markets.
