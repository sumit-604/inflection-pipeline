## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why the business is investable)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** Revenue CAGR 24.54% and PAT CAGR 21.57% FY24-FY26 (B01 line 13). FY26 EBITDA margin 10.4%, a record, beating the 8.5-9% guide (B05 line 23). Normalized (pre-depression) ROCE 25.66% in FY24 (B04 line 7; B11 line 59) shows the asset base can earn well once capacity turns commercial. FTTCP judged ROCE forward RECOVERING and cash forward STARTING (operator overrides, deliberation lines 8, 32-44). Caveat carried honestly: the FY26 cash-conversion collapse to CFO/PAT 0.22x and negative FCF (B01 line 13; B11 line 76) and ND/EBITDA 4.0x with interest coverage 1.73x (B01 line 23) are the quality holes the transition must close.

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**
1. Compressor ramp 2.8mn to 6mn units, margin 6-7% to 11-12% (conviction M, FY27). Confirms: utilisation crossing 60%-80% with QCO terms holding. Kills: QCO liberalised beyond the 40% import cap or new entrants undercut before VOEPL scales (B05 line 13). This is the SHARED CATALYST.
2. AC capacity 1mn to 1.8mn units incl. Chennai plant (conviction M, FY27). Confirms: Chennai reaching >5-6% revenue in FY27 (B05 line 14).
3. AC OEM-to-ODM transition and customer diversification (conviction L-M). Confirms: Voltas share falling below 50% of AC revenue with margin uplift; note this promise reversed to flat-or-growing in Q3 FY26 (B05 lines 15, 50).
4. FY27 revenue delivery vs the diluted 35-40% CAGR guide (~Rs1,100-1,155cr) (conviction M) (B05 line 16).
5. EMS capacity doubling 4-8-12 lakh CPH (B05 line 17).

**3C. Peer cross-read COMPLICATES the thesis (B06 line 56): peers independently cite a 30% QCO reciprocating-compressor import allowance vs VOEPL's 40% claim (B06 line 22); peers place the BEE demand air-pocket in Jan-Feb 2026, not Oct/Nov 2025 as VOEPL frames it (B06 line 10); no peer confirms VOEPL's "margin resilience" claim — all report real FY26 margin compression (B06 line 11). ## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Virtuoso is a real, growing contract manufacturer that may be quietly turning from a low-quality, Voltas-dependent AC assembler into a diversified, backward-integrated compressor and appliance maker. The FTTCP call, after two operator overrides, is that returns are recovering and cash is starting to improve, judged over the capital cycle to FY30 — a +4 composite, DEEP WATCH leaning BUY-ON-DIPS. That is a call on the business, and it is defensible.

It is not a call on the stock at Rs509. The market is paying 108 times a mechanically depressed FY26 earnings base, roughly three-quarters of whose growth came from a depreciation-method change, not operations. The four-pillar framework earns this business a destination multiple of 14 times on the governing RRM track and 20 times additive. No realistic path — not even 45% bull-case earnings growth over four years — closes that gap; every one of the eighteen scenario cells shows a negative CAGR, and the Hurdle Ratio is STOP. Layer on a CONCERN promoter flag whose related-party universe is understated around the company's own headquarters land, an auditor who confirms almost none of the working capital, and 4x leverage in the middle of the largest capex program in the company's history, and the verdict is unambiguous: AVOID at this price.

The discipline is to wait. The falsifier is clean and near — the H1 FY27 cash print and the first FY27 ROCE will tell you within two quarters whether the transition is real. If it is, and if the price ever falls into the Rs74-86 zone for reasons other than the trigger firing, this becomes a Small position and nothing larger while the promoter flag stands. Until then it is a watch item, not a buy.
