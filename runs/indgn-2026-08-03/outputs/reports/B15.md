## 3. VALUE TRAP TEST

What makes INDGN LOOK like a good investment: net cash Rs 1,337 cr, nil debt, asset-light, Gate 0
GOOD+ (90/100), TAM MASSIVE (243x headroom), CFO/PAT 1.62x, no promoter red flags. On the surface a
quality compounder at a "modest" forward premium.

What is hiding behind the good numbers:

1. FY26 PAT FELL 1.4% on +23.6% revenue (B14 line 179). A business whose top line grew 23.6% while
   the bottom line SHRANK is either buying growth at negative incremental margin or seeing genuine
   operating deleverage. The mechanical FY19-26 CAGR scores that feed Gate 0 do not capture this most
   recent inflection (B14 line 182). The good Gate 0 score is backward-looking; the most recent data
   point is deterioration.

2. The growth is partly gross-consolidation optics. Organic constant-currency has HALVED, 18.3% to
   12% (B14 line 135). BioPharm is consolidated at GROSS revenue, inflating both the growth rate and
   the apparent scale while diluting margin (B14 line 133). This is exactly the profile of a services
   roll-up that looks like a compounder because acquisitions mask organic deceleration.

3. AI disruption to effort-based billing. B14 line 258 itself flags "AI-exposed effort-based-billing
   services" as the theme. Indegene sells medical/regulatory/pharmacovigilance operations where
   headcount is the cost base and 15.7% TTM attrition is already a binding constraint (B09
   capacity_check). If generative AI compresses the effort content of these workflows, revenue per
   client can fall even as the client relationship persists. That is the melting-ice-cube signature:
   sticky relationships, shrinking economics.

Verdict on the trap test: this is NOT a clean value trap (net cash and no leverage bound the
downside, and the TAM is real), but it carries three of the classic disguise markers. The
compounder label is doing work the most recent year's numbers do not support.

## 4. VARIANT PERCEPTION CHECK

My variant perception (the thesis's implicit bet): the market is mispricing Indegene because it is
anchored on the one-off-depressed FY26 trailing earnings, and once the contractual amortization
step-down and large outcome-based deals lift FY27 earnings, the stock re-rates from ~27x forward
toward a 31.5x destination.

Now argue the MARKET IS RIGHT and I am wrong:

The market is not anchored on stale trailing earnings. The market has SEEN Q1 FY27 (revenue 1,063.1,
PAT 116.2, EBITDA margin 16.9%, B11 line 130) and is pricing exactly what it observed: an organic
line decelerating to 12%, a margin that did NOT recover in the first forward quarter, and a
management that runs a no-guidance policy so it cannot even commit to the recovery on which the bull
case rests. The market prices INDGN at 32.8x trailing / 27x on-a-recovery-forward precisely because
it is unwilling to pay up for a recovery it has not seen. The 12% divergence between the RRM 28.1x
and the additive 31.5x (B11 line 96), and the operator's election of the HIGHER track, is the
analyst reaching for a multiple the market is deliberately withholding. The market's refusal to
re-rate is not inefficiency, it is discipline: it is waiting for the margin print that FTTCP says has
no documented action behind it. If the market saw a genuine, evidenced recovery, INDGN would already
trade at 31.5x. It does not, because there is nothing yet to see.

The uncomfortable conclusion: the variant perception requires the market to be wrong about a
company it has full, current-quarter visibility into. That is a much weaker edge than a
genuinely-undiscovered micro-cap. INDGN has FII+DII 18.66% (B01) and a full institutional float. UA
does not apply (B11 line 22). There is no informational moat here for a retail workup to exploit.

## 5. CATALYST DEPENDENCY (SHARED CATALYST stress test — the core of this attack)

The implicit catalyst: revenue growth on a fixed capital base. Per the FTTCP SHARED CATALYST flag
(FTTCP delib line 130; B11 Sec 0), this SINGLE driver powers BOTH:
- Pillar 1 forward ROCE recovery (statutory 15.4% recovers toward operational 25.8% as revenue grows
  on the fixed asset-light base and goodwill amortizes), AND
- Pillar 3 +5x growth premium (3a +2x growth visibility rests on revenue reaccelerating above 12%).

This is a single point of failure. If revenue-on-fixed-capital stalls, BOTH pillars fall together:

- Pillar 1: revenue does not grow into the fixed base, statutory ROCE stays near 15.4%, Pillar 1 base
  = 0.5x15.4+7.5 = 15.2x. Quality base 15.2 x 1.30 = 19.8x.
- Pillar 3: revenue moves FIRING->STARTING, composite +5->+4, the 3a +2x growth-visibility credit is
  pressured toward +0x (SENSITIVITY-B, B11 4E-b; FTTCP delib line 140). Pillar 3 falls +5x->+2x or +3x.

Run BOTH simultaneously (the honest reading of a SINGLE shared driver failing): destination =
19.8 (quality base under statutory ROCE) minus the lost Pillar-3 points, landing 17-19x, NOT the
19.8x the report models by dropping only Pillar 1 while keeping Pillar 3 at +5x. B11 4E-a moves ONE
lever (treasury) and holds the other. B11 4E-b moves the OTHER lever (organic) and holds treasury.
Neither models the shared-catalyst reality that the SAME stall trips both at once. The true combined
destination is BELOW both single-sensitivity outputs.

At destination 17-19x on bear FY30 EPS 26.98: FV = Rs 459-513, i.e. 6-16% BELOW CMP over 3 years.
The report's "true floor" of Rs 534 (-2%, B11 4F) understates the combined-catalyst-failure downside
because it does not compound the two correlated levers.

What if the catalyst is merely DELAYED 2-3 years rather than absent? The amortization step-down
(Rs 50mn/qtr from Q3 FY27, B11 line 125) lifts reported PAT mechanically regardless, which is the
one genuine cushion. But mechanical amortization relief is financial-engineering EPS, not operating
EPS, and the market discounts it accordingly. While you wait 2-3 years for the operating recovery,
the stock is dead money at best (base drifts to bear ~Rs 850 at exit = 16% CAGR, B11 4E-b) and a
15-25% drawdown at worst as the forward-PE crutch is repriced. The opportunity cost against the 25%
target is real.

on the PE exit and the guidance vacuum, not on ownership thinness.

## 7. PRE-MORTEM

It is August 2028. INDGN is down ~40% from Rs 545, trading near Rs 330. Here is what happened.

The margin recovery never arrived. Through FY27 the EBITDA margin oscillated between 16.5% and 17.5%,
never printing the sequential expansion toward 20% that the entire thesis required (B14 thesis-broken
condition, line 27). Management, running its no-guidance policy, kept describing a "6-8 quarter
journey" (B05) but the journey never started, exactly as the FTTCP STAGNANT verdict warned when it
noted NO documented action behind the recovery (FTTCP delib line 89). The base FY27 EPS of 20.22 that
anchored the whole valuation turned out to be the bear 17.51, because H2 FY27 never delivered the
margin lift baked into the 18.0% assumption. The forward PE the market was willing to pay collapsed
back toward trailing, and 27x on rising forward EPS became 24x on flat actual EPS.

The organic line confirmed the bears. Constant-currency ex-M&A growth, already halved to 12% by Q4
FY26 (B14 line 135), printed 10-11% for three of the next four quarters, tripping the falsification
line (organic at/below 12% for two consecutive quarters). Revenue moved FIRING->STARTING, the
composite slipped +5->+4, and the +5x Pillar 3 premium the market had been granting evaporated. The
"large outcome-based deals" were recognized more slowly than the go-live schedule implied, and one
was rescoped, so the acquisition-inflated 23.6% headline of FY26 gave way to a mid-teens headline and
a low-double-digit organic reality.

And the treasury sat there. Through the whole period no buyback, no material M&A, no special
dividend deployed the Rs 1,100-1,337 cr non-operating cash (SENSITIVITY-A). The market, watching idle
cash and stalled operations, refused to grant the operational-ROCE stripping the analyst had assumed,
and priced INDGN on its 15.4% STATUTORY ROCE. Pillar 1 was 15.2x, Pillar 3 was gone, and the
destination the stock actually earned was ~17-18x, not 31.5x. On the FY30 EPS that materialized
(nearer the bear 27 than the base 37), that multiple put fair value in the Rs 460-500 range, and
sentiment overshot it to Rs 330. The one cushion, the amortization step-down, lifted reported PAT a
little but the market saw through the financial-engineering relief. Everything that broke was named
in the risk matrix. The error was paying at the floor and calling the absence of a cushion a margin
of safety.
