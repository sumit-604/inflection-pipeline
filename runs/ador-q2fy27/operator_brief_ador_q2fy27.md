# Operator brief: Ador Welding (ADOR), Q2 FY27

Written 2026-10-24 by the orchestrator from the committed review files (commit 6652b470). No agent was re-run. Sources: runs/ador-q2fy27/work/review_ador_q2fy27.md (A5 COMPLETE after two loops) and the A1 extracts. Concall evidence is a machine (ASR) transcript; every call quote is ASR-tier.

## 1. Narrative

1. Ador makes welding consumables (electrodes and wires), welding machines, repair alloys and automation systems. It sells through dealers across India and exports, mostly to the Middle East.
2. Q2 sales rose 23% on last year, to Rs 345 Cr. This came after a flat FY26, when sales grew under 2%.
3. Part of this growth is price. The managing director (MD) put the underlying growth at "plus 10%".
4. Profit grew much less than sales. Q2 profit after tax rose about 8%, to Rs 27 Cr.
5. The reason is cost. Steel, copper and freight cost more, and Ador raised its prices late.
6. Gross margin (sales minus material cost, as a share of sales) fell to 34.2%. That is below even FY25's 35.4%.
7. Half-year profit looks up 159%. Most of that is a base effect: last year's half carried a Rs 28 Cr charge on one late project.
8. Cash is the main worry. Operations used Rs 8 Cr of cash in the half, while profit was Rs 55 Cr. Ador paid suppliers much faster than in March.
9. Ador borrowed Rs 27 Cr and paid a Rs 40 Cr dividend in the same half. It still held Rs 120 Cr of cash and liquid investments. The MD called the borrowing "LCs and BGs" (bank guarantees), but the filing shows a cash loan.
10. Management targets a 12-13% operating margin and a 36-38% gross margin, with no date. Other costs already run near target, so gross margin is the one lever left.
11. Management would not give the order book or the steel share of its costs. It also gave two different capex numbers for FY27.
12. Watch next quarter: Q3 gross margin, due by 14-Feb-2027. At 36% or above, the price lag is closing. Below 34.2%, the squeeze is getting worse. The FY27 audited cash flow (May 2027) is the cash test.
