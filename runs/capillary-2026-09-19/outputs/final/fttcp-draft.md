## 3. THE CATALYST STORY

Revenue. SessionM is the big FY27 lift. It closed on 01-May-2026 and brings about USD 32 m of ARR (Step-1 brief; B05). That is filed and mechanical. The harder engine is organic growth. It ran 15.5% in FY26 and 17% in Q1 FY27. A Fortune 50 contract worth USD 20 m over five years (Reg 30, 04-Mar-2026) should go live during FY27. New ACV grew 75% on a TTM basis excluding one healthcare customer (deck p.8). What confirms it: the Q2 FY27 deck shows constant currency organic growth at 15% or more. What kills it: organic growth below 15% in constant currency for H1 FY27, or core NRR ex Optum below 105% for two quarters.

Margin. Three levers. First, cost leverage: non-COGS cost grew 32% against revenue at 43% in Q1 FY27 (deck p.9); peers confirm the same mechanism (B06, verified). Second, SessionM: management says it was "profitable" within two months; the falsifier is SessionM below USD 27 m ARR by Q4 FY27 or still at break-even. Third, Kognitiv migration: first customer live 01-Sep-2026, full migration by Sep-2027 (Q1 FY27 call). Migrated books move from about 30% to about 65% gross margin (deck p.9). What confirms it: adjusted EBITDA margin at 17% or more in Q2 FY27, and a dated Kognitiv go-live on the Q2 call. What kills it: organic EBITDA margin falling in constant currency (falsifier 4), or the Optum warrant charge stepping up to about Rs 31 Cr a year in FY28 and FY29 without offset.

Cash. Cash depends on billing customers in advance. FY26 showed the good side; FY25 showed the bad side. What confirms it: FY27 OCF/EBITDA at 60% or more and receivable days at or below 90 in the H1 FY27 balance sheet. What kills it: a large assumed deferred revenue in the SessionM purchase price allocation, or receivable days above 98.

ROCE. No catalyst moves ROCE across 13.5% inside 12 months. EBITDA growth helps. Three things hold ROCE down: Rs 322.9 Cr of unused IPO money, goodwill of Rs 309.58 Cr, and more acquisitions funded from cash (Rs 85.01 Cr IPO object "funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and general corporate purposes", Results Q1 FY27 p.5). Each new acquisition adds goodwill before it adds margin. What confirms a climb: clean ROCE above 10% for two consecutive periods. What kills it: a goodwill impairment, or a new acquisition that resets ROCE lower.
