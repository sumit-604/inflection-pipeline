## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**What could make revenue keep going.** The capacity is already in and paid for.
The FY26 annual report documents CNC lines worth about 350,000 HSS drill units a
year and a 90 MT austempering furnace. Q1 FY27 then grew 28.9%. What confirms it:
FY27 revenue at or above Rs 300 cr. What kills it: Q2 FY27 growth below 10%. The
honest caution is that Kennametal grew 47.7% and Wendt 36.6% in the same June
quarter, so the cycle is doing a lot of the work; Birla Precision grew 1.4% in
that quarter, which says the cycle is not lifting everyone equally.

**What could make margin hold.** Two things already visible in one print:
operating leverage on a fixed cost base, and an other-expense line that fell in
absolute rupees on 29% more revenue. What confirms it: other expenses at or below
23% of revenue in Q2 and Q3 FY27. What kills it: the same line reverting toward
30%, which is where FY26 ran. Against it, the input side offers no help before
2027 on the dossier's own reading, and Kennametal's CFO has said publicly that
its own margin came from a 5-6 month raw material inventory lag and reverses when
tungsten falls. TOTEM discloses no such lag.

**What could make cash convert.** The inventory that went in has started to come
back. What confirms it: H1 FY27 total inventory at or below Rs 50 cr. What kills
it: inventory still above Rs 55 cr at the half year, or raw material stock staying
near Rs 20 cr, which would mean the build was an input hedge and is permanent.

**What could make capital efficiency recover.** Nothing new needs to be bought.
EBIT rising on a flat asset base does it arithmetically. What confirms it: FY27
ROCE above 22.5%, back through the FY25 level. What kills it: a new capex cycle
announced before the current one has earned, or the inventory staying where it is.

**What is genuinely absent.** There is no order book, because this business does
not have one. There is no segment disclosure, so the mix shift from HSS toward
carbide that the growth story depends on cannot be checked from outside at all.
And there is no management commentary of any kind on a call, because there are no
calls. Under this protocol, absence of commentary across two consecutive calls is
the loudest negative signal available. Here there have never been any calls, which
is a different and more structural fact: it is not that management went quiet on a
problem, it is that no channel has ever existed.
