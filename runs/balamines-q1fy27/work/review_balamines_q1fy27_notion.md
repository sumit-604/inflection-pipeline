### 6C. Realization/volume reconciliation vs the Q4 FY26 concall (resolves the central open question)

The Q4 FY26 concall Q&A disclosed that raw-material prices "doubled, even three times" on geopolitics + competitor plant shutdowns, which drove Q4 pricing, and that "production was briefly impacted March 2026 due to external geopolitical situation." **This means the Q1 FY27 realization surge (~+60% YoY per MT) is almost certainly the SAME geopolitics-driven pricing, not a durable structural mix step-up.** The press-release "West Asia crisis" line (L156-157) is the same theme.

Two consequences:
1. **The durability flag SHARPENS.** Growth is price-led AND the price is geopolitics-contingent (crisis-driven RM inflation + competitor outages). It reverses when the crisis / competitor-outage window closes. The cash-multiplier reversal candidate (6A) should be read against this — a geopolitics-priced margin beat is weak evidence for a structural 1.00x -> 1.15x reversal.
2. **Repeat candor issue.** The Q1 press-release claim "supported by stable commodity prices" (L89-91) directly CONTRADICTS the company's own Q4 concall admission that RM doubled/tripled. Combined with "volumes maintained at similar levels" (L89-91) vs the disclosed −21.7% volume fall (L82), and "diversified portfolio" (L154) vs single-segment profit concentration (2E), that is three internally contradicted narrative claims in one release — a recurring management-candor pattern (the Q4 opening remarks similarly claimed "stable raw material conditions" that the Q4 Q&A contradicted).
