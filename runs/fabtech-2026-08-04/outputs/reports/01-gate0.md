## DATA SOURCE NOTE (read before the numbers)

FABTECH listed on NSE/BSE on 2025-10-07 (IPO priced Rs 191/share, issue closed 2025-10-01).
Its RHP/Prospectus (dated 2025-10-03) restates **consolidated** financials for FY23, FY24
and FY25 only (prospectus p.5 definition of "Restated Consolidated Financial Statements";
p.102-106 Summary of Financial Statements). FY26 (year ended 2026-03-31) comes from the
audited consolidated results filed 2026-04-27. Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 2026-06-30) comes
from the unaudited consolidated results filed 2026-07-24.

`screener-Data_Sheet.csv` also carries FY20-FY22 rows. These are **excluded from all
scoring** below: the company was incorporated as a shell ("Globeroute Ventures Private
Limited"), renamed and re-purposed only after an NCLT-approved demerger effective
2020-11-19 transferred the turnkey pharma-engineering export business into it (RHP p.107,
General Information). Equity capital in those years (Rs 0.01-2.79 Cr) confirms this was
not yet the operating entity being scored. Using FY20-22 would fabricate continuity the
restated financials do not support, so per "never estimate a missing number," they are
marked NOT FOUND for scoring purposes and only FY23-FY26 (4 years) are used. This also
means FY23-FY26 figures are cross-checked: Data_Sheet.csv values for FY23-FY26 (Sales,
PAT, CFO) reconcile to the RHP-restated and results-filed consolidated figures within
rounding, confirming Data_Sheet.csv is itself built on the consolidated basis.

Company is a turnkey engineering / cleanroom / pharma-machinery / process-equipment
supplier to pharma and allied industries (RHP p.242, "supply of pharmaceutical machinery
and equipment") — judged here as a project-engineering capital-goods business, not a
CDMO, per operating instructions.

**Mechanical classification: AVOID**

This is driven by two identifiable, name-able factors rather than a broad operating
weakness: (a) the RHP's 3-year restatement window plus one further audited year gives only
4 years of usable history — a structural feature of any company that IPO'd 10 months ago,
not a quality signal; and (b) cumulative FY23-FY26 cash conversion of 8% is real but
concentrated in two specific years (FY23, FY25) of large receivables build typical of
milestone-billed turnkey EPC contracts, against one very strong year (FY24, 222%
conversion) and one weak-but-positive year (FY26). Per pipeline operating rules, this
classification is not a halt; the flags below carry these depressors forward for
downstream position-sizing judgment, consistent with "downstream position sizing may
override AVERAGE for documented post-IPO rebase / legacy cleanup cases."

**Strongest block:** D (Balance Sheet Strength, 19/20) — though itself substantially a
function of Rs 207.5 Cr of undeployed IPO cash rather than multi-year deleveraging.

**Weakest block (by data availability):** E (Shareholder Alignment, 1/20) — driven by
absent shareholding-pattern data (input gap), not evidenced misalignment.

**Weakest block (by evaluated substance):** B (Cash Generation Quality, 5/20) — every
metric was computable from provided data and all four scored 0 or low; this is the
substantive concern the classification is built on, alongside the short history.

## DECISION LINE

Gate 0 mechanical output for FABTECH: **AVOID**, driven by LIMITED history (4 years,
post-IPO restatement window) and weak cumulative cash conversion (CFO/PAT 8.1%,
concentrated in FY23 and FY25). Growth (Block C, 16/20) and balance-sheet strength
(Block D, 19/20, IPO-cash-assisted) are genuinely strong; return on capital (Block A,
10/20) and cash generation (Block B, 5/20) are the real watch items, with the FY26 ROCE/ROE
dip and net-cash balance sheet both traceable to the same October 2025 IPO proceeds not yet
deployed. Flags propagate; no STOP verdict is issued at this stage.
