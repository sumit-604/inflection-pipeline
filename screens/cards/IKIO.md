## Business Understanding Narrative

IKIO designs and makes LED lights and electronics for other companies' brands. It is an
ODM, an original design manufacturer: it does the design work itself, then makes the
product, and the customer sells it under their name (IP Q1FY27 p.15). It has run this
way for about 25 years, starting with fan regulators in 1999 and moving through DVD
players, LED lighting in 2008, refrigeration and instore lighting in 2014, RV lighting
in 2018, hearables and wearables in FY24, and automotive lighting and electronics
manufacturing services in FY26 (IP Q1FY27 p.11). It runs five factories across about 5
lakh sq ft and holds over 3,000 SKUs (IP Q1FY27 p.15). Money comes from designing a
part, winning a slot in a large brand's range, and making it at a gross margin near
40%.

What is changing is the customer mix, not mainly the products. For years one customer,
Signify, took about half of revenue and once about 70% (Concall Q4FY26 p.9). That
dependence is being diluted. Home Lighting ODM revenue fell from Rs 2,077 Mn in FY25 to
Rs 1,698 Mn in FY26, while everything else grew from Rs 2,782 Mn to Rs 4,255 Mn, up 53%
(Concall Q4FY26 p.4; IP Q1FY27 p.10 as corrected). Other Business is now 71% of revenue,
up from 57% (Concall Q4FY26 p.4).

Why now: capacity. A greenfield project funded by IPO proceeds adds about 5 lakh sq ft.
Block I opened in May 2024, Block II was partly commercialised in Q2FY27, Block III is
under construction (IP Q1FY27 p.13).

What must be true: the new capacity must fill, and the new verticals must earn better
than the old one. Management screens each new category on EBITDA margin before entering
(Concall Q4FY26 p.7).

What breaks it: returns. The company earns about 8% on capital while it keeps adding
buildings. If Block II and III fill slowly, the equity base grows faster than profit,
and a diversification story becomes an idle-asset story.

## 2. Business model and archetype
IKIO sells designed-and-made products to brand owners. Revenue is unit volume times
price across lighting (home, instore, commercial refrigeration, RV, automotive) and
non-lighting (hearables and wearables, solar panels, ABS pipes, IPS controllers, EMS,
fire panels, amplifiers). About 80% to 85% of what it makes is ODM rather than pure
contract assembly (Concall Q4FY26 p.7). Archetype: outsourcing partner. Client
concentration, wallet share, capacity fill and contract stickiness are the variables
that matter, exactly as the library says.
The one economic engine is backward integration paying for design. The company runs its
own tool room, injection moulding, powder coating, CNC machining, SMT and testing (IP
Q1FY27 p.20, p.21). That is why gross margin sits at 40.2% for FY26, high for a
manufacturer of other people's products (IP Q1FY27 p.7). The engine's weakness is that
there is no order book at all. Revenue rests on customer production plans, not
contracts (Concall Q4FY26 p.6).

## 3. Competitive advantages
Moderate and asymmetric. IKIO holds real share in narrow places: about 23% to 24% of the
functional decorative side of Indian home lighting per a 2023 Frost & Sullivan survey,
and it claims to be the largest in commercial and refrigeration lighting (Concall Q4FY26
p.8). It is the largest supplier to Signify in its category and is single source with
some customers (Concall Q4FY26 p.8, p.9). Design plus backward integration is a genuine
switching cost: a brand that has qualified an IKIO-designed SKU does not move it
casually.
Against that, there is no contractual lock, no order book, and the newer verticals carry
no share position at all. In automotive the company sells aftermarket retrofit LED light
sources, not original equipment, and is careful to say it does not compete with Lumax
(Concall Q4FY26 p.10). In hearables and wearables 60% of output is low-margin OEM work
(Concall Q4FY26 p.11). Moat: present in legacy lighting, absent in the growth verticals.

## 4. The promoters
Hardeep Singh is Chairman and Managing Director, with over two decades in television
kits, electronic components and LED lighting. Surmeet Kaur is a Whole Time Director and
promoter, with the company since inception, responsible for human resources. Sanjeet
Singh is Whole Time Director and holds both the CEO and the CFO title, with the company
since February 2019 (IP Q1FY27 p.22).
Two governance points. One person holding CEO and CFO at once removes an internal check
in a company running five factories and an acquisition programme. And an 88% stake was
acquired in Gravus Tech during FY26 to strengthen go-to-market, with no consideration,
counterparty or relationship disclosed in the held corpus (Concall Q4FY26 p.4).
The independent bench is credible: a former Chairman and MD of Steel Authority of India,
and a former MD and CEO of Vijaya Bank (IP Q1FY27 p.22).

## 5. Financial trajectory
Revenue Rs 4,859 Mn (FY25) to Rs 5,953 Mn (FY26), up 23%. EBITDA Rs 600 Mn to Rs 775 Mn,
margin 12.3% to 13.0%. PAT Rs 324 Mn to Rs 416 Mn, up 28% (IP Q1FY27 p.7). Three-year
revenue CAGR 18% from FY23 (IP Q1FY27 p.10).
Q1FY27: revenue Rs 1,693 Mn, up 41% year on year; EBITDA Rs 220 Mn, up 94%; PAT Rs 110
Mn, up 365% (IP Q1FY27 p.6).
The year-on-year headline hides the sequential move. Against Q4FY26, Q1FY27 revenue rose
2% while EBITDA fell 15% and PAT fell 37%. Gross margin fell from 44.6% to 41.2%, and
EBITDA margin from 15.7% to 13.0% (IP Q1FY27 p.7). Q1FY26 was a weak base.
The balance sheet is the more important read. Equity Rs 6,094 Mn against total
borrowings of Rs 412 Mn: the company is close to debt free (IP Q1FY27 p.24). Cash fell
from Rs 1,097 Mn to Rs 576 Mn over FY26, while inventories rose Rs 623 Mn and capital
work in progress rose Rs 519 Mn. Plant, property and equipment did not move at all,
Rs 1,482 Mn in both years, so the new blocks are not yet capitalised.
Derived from those disclosures, not stated by the company: FY26 EBIT is Rs 466 Mn
(EBITDA 775 less depreciation 309). Capital employed is Rs 5,930 Mn (equity 6,094 plus
borrowings 412 less cash 576). Return on capital employed is about 7.9% pre-tax. Return
on equity is about 7.1% on average equity of Rs 5,857 Mn. Excluding the Rs 956 Mn of
capital work in progress that earns nothing yet, ROCE is about 9.4%.
**The sweep explains why the assets did not move: the capacity is late.** Block II of about
2 lakh square feet was guided "Completion expected by Mar'25" in the Q2FY25 deck and again,
word for word, in the Q3FY25 deck. The Q4FY25 deck says "nearing completion" with no date.
The Q4FY26 deck says "expected to be commercialized by end of Q1FY27". The Q1FY27 deck says
"**partially commercialized in Q2FY27**" (sweep file s.1). That is a slip of about six
quarters, and the latest wording adds "partially". Block III is still "construction
progressing as planned", the identical phrase used twenty months earlier.
**FY27 guidance, the first this card has.** Revenue "somewhere close to around a 20% to 22%
jump from this year to the next one", so Rs 714 to 726 crore off FY26's Rs 595 crore.
Margins "expect them to stay in line to what we have achieved right now", about 16%. Longer
term the target is 18% to 20%. Non-lighting mix from 25% to 30-32%. Rs 35 to 36 crore of IPO
capex left to spend this year. And the size of the prize: "somewhere around INR1,500 crores
top line we would be able to achieve... post the commissioning of this CapEx at full
utilization" (sweep file s.2).
Inflection classification: earnings-led on mix, with margin help. It is not an asset event
and not a one-off. But the returns are below any reasonable cost of capital, and the capacity
that is meant to fix that is six quarters late. Those two facts together are the defining
statement about this business today.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder
Management's claim, quoted once: "IKIO transitioning from lighting to integrated
technology solutions... we have evolved from our core Home Lighting segment to more
diversified offering across Home and Commercial Lighting, Hearable and Wearables, Energy
Solutions, Electronic Components and Automotive Lighting" (Concall Q4FY26 p.3).
FROM: the business does not sit cleanly on a rung, and that is the finding. On
capability it looks R3, a value-added spec'd supplier with design, backward integration
and single-source status. On returns it sits below R2: R2 requires durable mid-teens
ROCE and IKIO earns about 8%. A 40% gross margin that arrives at 7% net is the shape of
a business with capability and no operating leverage yet.
TO: R3 proper, a value-added supplier earning R3 returns, once the new blocks fill.
The honest reading is that this is not a rung climb at all. It is a business trying to
earn the returns its existing rung already implies. Note also what "diversification"
means here. Other Business is 71% of revenue, but non-lighting is only about 25%
(Concall Q4FY26 p.4, p.11). Most of the diversification is lighting sold into different
end-markets, not a move out of lighting.

## 8. Proof check
Mixed, and the direction of the last quarter is the wrong way.
What fired: Other Business grew 53% in FY26 to Rs 4,255 Mn, and 53% again in Q1FY27
(Concall Q4FY26 p.4; IP Q1FY27 p.6). Exports grew 53% to Rs 1,101 Mn (IP Q1FY27 p.9).
EBITDA margin rose from 12.3% to 13.0% across FY26. The mix shift is documented, not
narrated.
What has not fired: returns and sequential momentum. Q1FY27 gross margin fell 340 basis
points against Q4FY26 and PAT fell 37%, on revenue up 2% (IP Q1FY27 p.7). Export share
in Q1FY27 was 12.2% of revenue against 18% for FY26 (IP Q1FY27 p.12). Cash fell Rs 521
Mn over FY26. And the headline utilisation question was declined: management said it is
"very hard to comment on the utilization level on a console basis", offering only that
mature units run "vaguely around 70% plus" (Concall Q4FY26 p.5).
Named proof point: EBITDA margin and ROCE through FY27 as Block II fills. If Block II
and III come on stream and ROCE does not move off 8%, the capacity is ahead of the
demand.

## 9. Flags from the documents
- **Three counterparties recorded as CUSTOMERS carry the group's own name.** Bull AI's
  counterparty record holds 12 entries, marked complete. It names Royalux (customer, LED
  lighting), Znergy ("referred to as 'major customer'... alongside Royalux") and IKIO World
  ("explicitly confirmed as customer by Hardeep Singh"). It separately records **Royalux
  Lighting Private Limited** and **Royalux Export Private Limited** as IKIO subsidiaries,
  alongside IKIO Solutions and Ritech Holdings (sweep file s.5). A deep run must establish
  whether revenue described as customer revenue is booked to entities inside the group. This
  corpus cannot settle it. It is a question, not an accusation, and it is now the first item
  on this name's verification list.
- **An assured USD 8 million US order with no follow-up.** The Q3FY25 deck announced that
  Metco Engineering "assures business of 8 Mn USD in the next 6 months". That is about
  Rs 70 crore, roughly 12% of FY26 revenue. No delivery statement against it appears
  anywhere in four subsequent filings (sweep file s.4). The silence is the finding.
- **Block II is about six quarters late** against a date the company published twice
  (sweep file s.1).
- Return on capital employed of about 7.9% (derived from IP Q1FY27 p.7 and p.24). The
  business earns less on capital than it plausibly costs.
- Plant, property and equipment unchanged at Rs 1,482 Mn while capital work in progress
  more than doubled to Rs 956 Mn (IP Q1FY27 p.24). Capital is parked, not producing.
- Cash down Rs 521 Mn and inventories up Rs 623 Mn in FY26 (IP Q1FY27 p.24), against a
  stated working capital cycle of "around 60 to 75 days" (Concall Q4FY26 p.12).
- Two errors in the filed deck. The FY26 segment row is transposed against the concall,
  and the Mar-26 liabilities total does not foot (IP Q1FY27 p.10, p.24). Filing quality
  is a governance signal.
- The core legacy business is shrinking. Home Lighting ODM fell 18% in FY26, and
  Signify, the anchor customer, is going through a joint venture and a new entity
  (Concall Q4FY26 p.9).
- No order book of any kind (Concall Q4FY26 p.6). Revenue visibility rests on customer
  plans.
- Utilisation not disclosed on a consolidated basis (Concall Q4FY26 p.5).
- No segment profitability disclosed, by policy (Concall Q4FY26 p.11).
- One person is CEO and CFO (IP Q1FY27 p.22).
- The Gravus Tech acquisition (88%) has no disclosed price or counterparty in the held
  corpus (Concall Q4FY26 p.4).
- "Front-loaded certain strategic expenses, which remain elevated" (Concall Q4FY26 p.4).
  An open-ended explanation for a thin margin.
- The US business had a lost year on tariffs, described by the CFO as "something that we
  also would like to forget" (Concall Q4FY26 p.12).
Not seen: promoter holding and pledge, related-party detail, contingent liabilities,
auditor opinion, customer concentration percentages, IPO proceeds utilisation statement.

## 11. Posture: Transition Decision Matrix
- Proof gate: NOT FIRED. The mix shift is proven; the returns transition is not. ROCE at
  about 8% with capital work in progress rising is the opposite of the proof required.
- Ugliness: the thin return is currently AN ARTIFACT OF THE CLIMB on management's
  account, front-loaded expenses and new verticals still ramping (Concall Q4FY26 p.4,
  p.5). The reading is fragile. Block I opened in May 2024, more than two years ago, and
  returns have not moved. If FY27 passes with Block II live and ROCE still near 8%, the
  classification flips to STRUCTURAL-FEATURE.
- Recognition gap: INDETERMINATE. No price, no market cap in the corpus.
Proof NOT FIRED plus ARTIFACT ugliness plus an unresolved gap maps to RESEARCH / WATCH.
Watch the gate, not the story. The gate is ROCE, and its date is FY27.

## 12. Verdict card
**WATCH.** IKIO is a competent, nearly debt-free ODM with real design capability, real
backward integration and a documented mix shift away from a single dominant customer.
It is not yet a transition worth a full `/step1`. The reason is arithmetic: about 7.9%
return on capital employed, a plant that has not been capitalised while capital work in
progress doubled, and a quarter in which revenue rose 2% while profit after tax fell
37%. The company is adding a third building before the second is full.
Load-bearing facts a deep run must verify first:
1. The FY26 segment split. The filed deck contradicts the filed transcript. Establish
   which is right from the annual report before any model is built.
2. Return on capital employed and asset turns, and the IPO proceeds utilisation
   statement. The whole question is whether the capital works.
3. Signify: current share of revenue, contract status, and what the joint venture and
   the new entity mean for the ODM slot.
4. Segment profitability. Management does not disclose it, so the annual report
   segment note is the only route.
5. The Gravus Tech acquisition: consideration, counterparty, and whether it is a
   related party.
6. Cash flow from operations for FY26, against the Rs 521 Mn fall in cash.
What would change the view: two quarters with EBITDA margin above 15% and ROCE moving
through the low teens as Block II fills would make this a genuine R2-to-R3 climb and a
clear PROCEED. A year in which the buildings finish and returns stay near 8% makes the
ugliness structural, and the name a PASS. No price here.
