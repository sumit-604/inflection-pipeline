Proposed Rs. 50.50 Crore Acquisition of  
Zicom SaaS Private Limited  
 
to Strengthen Its Surveillance and Managed Security Services 
Business 
 
Mumbai, 04th November 2026: Surveillance is entering a new phase, where intelligent 
monitoring, remote intervention and continuous service management are becoming 
increasingly important to security operations. Creative Newtech Limited (NSE: CNL; BSE: 
544631) is positioning itself to  capitalise on this evolution through the proposed 
acquisition of Zicom SaaS Private Limited, combining its established strengths in 
technology distribution with specialised capabilities in managed security services. 
 
Over the years, Creative Newtech has built an extensive distribution network, cultivated 
strong channel partnerships and established its presence across technology categories, 
including surveillance and networking. These strengths have helped the Company bring 
technology products to customers at scale. The proposed acquisition offers an 
opportunity to extend this role beyond equipment supply into the continuous 
monitoring, intelligence and management of security infrastructure. 
 
With Zicom SaaS’s experience in centralised monitoring, intelligen t surveillance and 
subscription-based security services, Creative Newtech aims to strengthen its 
surveillance business, deepen customer relationships, participate in larger institutional 
opportunities and pursue recurring revenue streams through ongoing se rvice 
arrangements. 
 
Transaction Overview 
• Target: Acquisition of 100% of the equity share capital of Zicom SaaS Private 
Limited from its holding company, Onyx SaaS Private Limited. 
• Total consideration: Approximately Rs. 50.50 crore (Enterprise value). 
• Cash component: Rs. 25.00 crore. 
• Share swap component:  Approximately Rs. 25.50 crore through the proposed 
preferential issue of 2,25,000 equity shares of Creative Newtech Limited at an 
issue price of Rs. 1,133.33 per share, in accordance with the applicable provisions 
of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, 2018.

Zicom SaaS: An Established Business in Continuous Security Management  
With roots in India’s electronic security industry dating back to the 1990s, Zicom SaaS 
Private Limited was established in 2011 as part of the Zicom Group. Rather than being 
limited to the sa le of CCTV cameras or security equipment, Zicom SaaS has developed a 
Security as a Service (SaaS) model, under which customers can access surveillance 
equipment, remote monitoring, maintenance and security management services against 
recurring subscription charges. This approach can reduce the need for customers to build 
and independently manage complex security operations. 
 
Through its centralised monitoring infrastructure, Zicom can remotely supervise 
geographically dispersed facilities, including bank br anches, ATMs, retail outlets and 
other business premises, monitor the functioning of security systems and coordinate 
responses to alerts. 
 
Its principal offerings include: 
• 24×7 Command and Control Centre:  Centralised monitoring of large networks 
of sites, real-time alerts and coordinated incident response. 
 
• ATM Active Deterrence:  AI-enabled detection of suspicious activity or 
equipment tampering, supported by operator verification, two -way audio 
warnings and response coordination where installed systems permit. 
 
• Virtual Security Guard:  Centrally monitored cameras, sensors and remote 
intervention capabilities that can supplement physical guarding at selected 
locations. 
 
• Surveillance System Health Monitoring:  Automated alerts for camera 
malfunction, hard -disk failure, recording interruption and connectivity loss, 
helping customers maintain operational readiness of installed systems. 
 
• AIoT Solutions:  Applications combining artificial intelligence and connected 
devices for energy management, attendance monitoring, footfall counting, and 
video-based housekeeping and hygiene monitoring. 
 
• Residential Security (MYCS – Make Your City Safe): Surveillance, access control 
and equipment maintenance for residential communities.  According to 
information cited in Zicom’s business materials, MYCS serves more than 4,500 
housing societies through a service network spanning over 800 cities and towns.

A Strategic Addition to Creative Newtech’s Surveillance Business 
Creative Newtech has an established technology distribution business, an extensive 
network of channel partners and relationships across multiple customer segments. The 
Company is exploring opportunities to expand its surveillance business across cameras, 
recording equipment and related security solutions. 
 
Zicom SaaS complements these strengths with remote monitoring infrastructure, 
Command and Control Centre operations, intelligent video analytic s, ongoing 
maintenance capabilities and relationships across the security industry. Together, these 
capabilities create an opportunity for Creative Newtech to move beyond the supply of 
surveillance hardware and become a long -term provider of integrated tec hnology and 
managed security solutions. 
 
The proposed combination could create three avenues for growth: 
• Greater Business from Existing Markets:  Zicom’s banking, enterprise and 
residential customers could generate demand for surveillance equipment, 
replacements and upgrades, while Creative Newtech’s distribution and channel 
network could help Zicom’s services reach a broader customer base. 
 
• Comprehensive Security Contracts: The ability to offer equipment, installation, 
integration, monitoring, analytics and maintenance through a coordinated 
solution could address more of each customer’s security requirements. 
 
• Participation in Institutional Projects:  Combined hardware and managed -
service capabilities could improve the Company’s ability to pursue larger banking, 
enterprise, industrial and government surveillance opportunities requiring 
centralised monitoring and sustained support.

Expanding from Product Sales to Long-Term Service Relationships 
For a surveillance business, the initial sale of equipment represents one revenue 
opportunity. The years of monitoring, maintenance and security management that follow 
represent another. Zicom’s service -led model prese nts the possibility of extending 
customer relationships beyond the point of product installation. 
 
Creative Newtech sees scope to develop bundled offerings under which surveillance 
cameras, recording equipment, installation, remote monitoring and maintena nce 
services may be provided through monthly or annual arrangements, subject to 
commercial feasibility, technical compatibility and customer requirements. For 
customers operating numerous locations, a single coordinated solution may simplify 
procurement, service management and accountability. 
 
There may also be opportunities to use the same surveillance infrastructure for multiple 
applications. For example, a multi -location retail customer could potentially combine 
security monitoring with footfall analysis , attendance tracking, operational compliance 
and energy management, creating additional service opportunities without entirely 
separate equipment deployments. 
 
Creative Newtech’s market reach and experience in building technology businesses could 
support the expansion of Zicom SaaS’s managed -security services, while Zicom’s 
customer relationships could contribute to demand for surveillance products and 
upgrades across the equipment lifecycle. 
 
Strategic Significance for Creative Newtech 
Creative Newtech believes the proposed acquisition offers the following strategic 
opportunities: 
• Service Capability:  Addition of centralised monitoring, analytics, remote 
intervention and ongoing maintenance alongside surveillance products. 
• Recurring Revenue Opportunity:  Scope for longer -term service arrangements 
and improved visibility of customer relationships and revenue over time. 
• Integrated Offerings: Potential to combine hardware, software, monitoring and 
service support under one solution. 
• Cross-Selling and Reach:  Opportunities to introduce Creative Newtech’s 
surveillance products to Zicom’s existing customers and extend Zicom’s services 
through Creative Newtech’s channel network. 
• Larger Opportunities: A broader offering for enterprise, banking, industrial and 
institutional surveillance projects that require monitoring capability and long -
term service support. 
 
The benefits of the proposed acquisition will depend on successful completion and 
integration, customer adoption, contract economics, capital requirements and  effective 
execution of the combined business strategy.

Management Comment: 
Commenting on the proposed acquisition, Mr. Ketan Patel, Chairman & Managing 
Director of Creative Newtech Limited, said: 
“Creative Newtech began its journey in technology distribution and, over the past three 
decades, has steadily expanded its capabilities in brand development, market expansion and 
technology solutions. Our approach has been to build on our existing strengths and identify 
opportunities that allow us to participate more extensively in the businesses we serve. 
 
The proposed acquisition of Zicom SaaS is an important step in this direction. We have an 
established network for bringing surveillance technology to market, and Zicom brings the 
experience and infrastructure required to monitor, manage and maintain security systems. 
We see an opportunity to combine these capabilities and offer customers a wider range of 
solutions, supported by ongoing services. 
 
The commercial opportunity is particularly interesting bec ause security requirements 
continue throughout the life of the equipment. We believe this could help us develop 
recurring revenue streams, deepen customer relationships and expand our presence across 
enterprise and institutional markets. Subject to success ful completion and integration, our 
objective is to build surveillance and managed security services into a significant long-term 
business vertical for Creative Newtech.” 
 
Company Overview:
