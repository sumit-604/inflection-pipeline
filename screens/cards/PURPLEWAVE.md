## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Purple Wave is a Delhi professional audio-visual integrator, incorporated 2007 (AR p. 4). It designs, supplies, installs and maintains boardroom AV, LED walls, digital signage, smart classrooms and command centres (AR p. 7). It also distributes AV hardware, sells AMC contracts and runs a small SaaS content platform, Streampurple (AR p. 8). FY26 revenue was Rs 146.10 crore and PAT Rs 13.43 crore (AR p. 61). Revenue is one line: "Sales, SITC receipts & AMC Receipts" (AR p. 96). The company has 83 employees (AR p. 75).

**What is changing?** Two things. It listed on BSE SME on 5 December 2025 and raised Rs 31.45 crore gross at Rs 126 a share (AR p. 67). It built a manufacturing facility at Manesar, which "commenced operations only in May 2026" (AR p. 37). The claim is a move from reselling other brands' hardware to making its own displays, kiosks and interactive panels under brands like Saipur and TheIVY (AR p. 37, 73).

**Why now?** FY26 margins stepped up. EBITDA margin rose to 13.5% from 11.0%, and PAT grew 48.2% on revenue growth of 14.4% (AR p. 61). The plant started one month into FY27.

**What must be true?** Manesar output must lift margin above an integrator's, and working capital must come back. Inventory days doubled to 72 from 37, and the cash conversion cycle went to 122 days from 74 (AR p. 66).

**What breaks it?** Three things. Cash: operating cash flow was minus Rs 10.61 crore in FY26 (AR p. 65). Tax: a DGGI GST demand of Rs 8.39 crore sits unprovided (AR p. 68). Competition: the company's own SWOT names "intensifying price-based competition" (AR p. 70).

## 2. Business model and archetype

Revenue mixes project SITC work, hardware distribution and AMC. The AR gives no split. Segment note: NOT FOUND (searched AR notes and MD&A). The margin comes from design and integration on hardware bought from global OEMs. Foreign exchange outgo was only Rs 1.25 crore in FY26 (AR p. 37). By inference, most hardware comes through domestic distributors.

The engine is project execution against an order book. The MD&A attributes the inventory build to "a larger order book" (AR p. 67). Order-book value: NOT FOUND.

**Archetype: Order-book business (system integration, SITC).** An own-product manufacturing slice is forming at Manesar. It carried no FY26 revenue.

## 4. The promoters

Manoj Kumar Singh is Chairman and Managing Director. Sandhya Singh is Whole Time Director. Their daughter Ananya Singh is an Executive Director (AR p. 21-22, 90). Promoter holding at 31 March 2026 was 71.92%: Manoj Kumar Singh 68.81%, Sandhya Singh 3.11% (AR p. 94). A year earlier the pair held 100% (AR p. 94).

Sandhya Singh's holding fell by 1,00,000 shares, to 2,88,008 from 3,88,008 (AR p. 93). The IPO was a fresh issue only: 67,50,000 plus 24,96,000 makes 92,46,000 (AR p. 94). The route of that transfer is not stated. Pledge: NOT FOUND, no shareholding pattern held.

Related parties carry weight:
- Purplewave India Pvt Ltd is owned by the same promoters. In FY26 it bought Rs 1.57 crore from the company and received Rs 0.50 lakh royalty (AR p. 91). A royalty paid out suggests some name or IP sits outside the listed entity.
- The AGM seeks a limit of Rs 15 crore for FY27 purchases and sales with Purplewave India, about 10% of FY26 revenue (AR p. 21).
- A second Rs 15 crore limit covers Nano Theatre Pvt Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary incorporated 30 March 2026 with no operations (AR p. 22, 60).
- Rs 15.00 lakh of CSR went to Durga Saptashati Foundation, a director's entity (AR p. 91).

Director remuneration was Rs 1.16 crore, 8.6% of PAT (AR p. 90). Skin in the game is high by holding. No dividend was paid (AR p. 24).

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **GST demand.** DGGI raised Rs 8.39 crore for FY21 to FY25, order dated 25 March 2026. No provision. Auditor Emphasis of Matter (AR p. 68). That equals 62% of FY26 PAT and 14.6% of net worth. It covers the years when margins jumped.
- **Cash conversion.** Operating cash flow was negative in three of five years. The FY26 cycle was 122 days (AR p. 66).
- **Pre-listing margin ramp.** EBITDA margin went from 3.3% in FY23 to 13.5% in FY26 (Screener; AR p. 61).
- **Related parties.** A promoter company shares the company's name, receives royalty and carries a Rs 15 crore FY27 limit. CSR went to a director's foundation (AR p. 21, 91).
- **Promoter transfer.** 1,00,000 shares left Sandhya Singh by an unstated route (AR p. 93).
- **CEO exit.** Shaurya Somani resigned effective 24 March 2026, under four months after listing (AR p. 25).
- **Dilution.** The ESOP of 4,00,000 options is 4.3% of 92,46,000 shares (AR p. 25).
- **Filing quality.** The Directors' Report gives PAT growth as 43.75% (AR p. 24). The MD&A gives 48.2% (AR p. 61), which is correct. Sixteen board meetings are stated and seventeen dates listed (AR p. 25).
- **IPO cost.** Issue expenses were Rs 4.40 crore, 14% of gross proceeds (AR p. 67). Objects and utilisation: NOT FOUND, beyond the auditor's line that funds "were applied for the purpose" (AR p. 80).

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** Hard watch, no `/step1` yet.

The margin climb is real on paper. The plant meant to carry the transition has no reported output. Cash has not followed profit, a tax demand is unprovided, and the price already pays for an R3 outcome. A `/step1` now would mostly audit a narrative.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. H1 FY27 results, due November 2026: margin, operating cash flow and inventory after Manesar started.
2. The DGGI demand of Rs 8.39 crore: its grounds, and whether it touches FY24 to FY25 revenue.
3. The prospectus restated financials and objects of issue, to test the FY23 to FY24 margin step.
4. What Purplewave India Pvt Ltd owns, and what the royalty covers.
5. The shareholding pattern, any pledge, and the route of Sandhya Singh's 1,00,000 shares.

**What would change the view**
- H1 FY27 with positive operating cash flow, inventory days back near 40 and margin above 13.5% opens a `/step1`.
- A GST outcome against the company, or negative H1 operating cash flow, moves this to PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
