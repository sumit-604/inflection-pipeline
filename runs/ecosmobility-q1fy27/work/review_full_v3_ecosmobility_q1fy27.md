- Organised corporate ground transport, split between employee transportation (ETS, daily commute, high volume, low yield) and chauffeur-driven car rental (CCR, premium) (business-model context = Notion/prior-work; the 59%/41% split = this quarter's press release and concall L12).
- Demand runway is large and confirmed by the deck's third-party data (this quarter's deck slide 16, ₹ Bn): organised CCR ₹98 Bn to ₹227 Bn by 2030 (12.7% CAGR), organised ETS ₹78 Bn to ₹198 Bn (14.2% CAGR). Management put the organised share at 15 to 20% of the market, the other 80% the future opportunity (this quarter's concall L53). Ecos at ~₹808 Cr FY26 revenue is a small share of a growing organised market.
- The structural sector risk is pricing power: cars and drivers are vendor-sourced, so input-cost inflation passes through to thin margins. Management confirmed this quarter that competition and pricing pressure are "more intense than anticipated, especially in ETS," and that it absorbs lower client prices out of vendor margins (this quarter's concall L10/L57). That risk is now showing in flat operating EBITDA on 16.7% growth and an 8% fall in revenue per trip (this quarter's filings).
- Regulation and payer mix: demand tied to corporate travel and outsourced-transport budgets; no regulatory catalyst disclosed. Single reportable segment; a board-approved event-management line could create a second segment later (this quarter's results filing, Note 4; concall L37).

### 8.3 Business-model intelligence
- How it makes money: asset-light corporate car rental; cars and drivers sourced from third-party vendors, Ecos holds the customer relationship and takes a spread. ### 8.4 Competition intelligence
- Where it wins: scale and breadth in organised corporate mobility (151 cities, 100+ countries, 70+ Fortune 500 and 75+ BSE 500 clients, ~1,400 active clients up 18%, this quarter's press release and concall), a 30-year vendor supply base management frames as its "right to win" (this quarter's concall L53), an asset-light near-zero-debt balance sheet with ₹137.6 Cr net cash (this quarter's deck), and a promoter judged TRUSTWORTHY with Management Grade B, present and candid on the call (Notion/prior-work plus this quarter's concall).

ETS competitive intensity is the near-term risk, and management refused to say whether pricing pressure is moderating (L83), which itself signals the pressure is still live into Q2.
