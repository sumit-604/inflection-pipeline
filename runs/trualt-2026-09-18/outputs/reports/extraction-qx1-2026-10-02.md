(d) READING: DO THE PASSAGES CONFLICT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

For the current NSL agreement, the passages do not genuinely conflict. They describe one formula in two vocabularies, and the purely market-price text covers a different period and a different kind of transfer. Every passage that describes the April 25, 2024 agreements names a cane-price formula: "a price linked to the basic fair and remunerative price fixed by the Government of India for cane" for syrup (p.339), with "a predefined margin" (p.48). The only prospectus text that says "market" about these agreements is the MD&A (p.529). In the same paragraph, it defines the mechanism as "a pricing formula embedded into our supply agreement, that links the price at which we source raw material to the price of sugarcane", at "a stipulated pricing formula". The cane price in that formula is set by "fair and remunerative price, premium and recovery levels fixed by the Government of India". So "market rates" on p.529 reads as loose wording for a cane-indexed formula, not a separate spot-price basis. [INFERENCE] The phrase "recorded at market prices" (pp.466, 487, 508) covers inter-unit transfers inside the Seller Companies in FY2021 to H1 FY2023, before the October 1, 2022 BTAs. It is a carve-out accounting convention, not an MSA price. "Arm's length" (pp.340, 434; AR pp.169, 181, 349) is a compliance conclusion about the terms. It is not a pricing basis, and it fits a formula price. Two real residual gaps remain. First, molasses: the prospectus links it to "the government-derived ethanol rate" (p.339), but the FY27 Reg 23 notice links "Syrup, B Molasses, C Molasses and clear juice" to "the FRP / sugarcane price ... and prevailing market dynamics" (notice p.20). That is either a changed formula for FY27 or a looser summary. The text does not say which. Second, the FY27 notice adds "prevailing market dynamics" as a formula input, and the AOC-2 shows a contract duration of "April 01, 2025 to March 31, 2026" (AR p.181) against a seven-year term to 2031 (prospectus p.339). The observation that separates the readings is the NSL SOW and Amendment text itself, or the FY27 audit committee RPT pack. Neither is in the corpus.
