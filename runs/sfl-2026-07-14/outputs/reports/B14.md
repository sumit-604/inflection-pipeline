## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy — the business, not the price)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** The trough is mechanical, not structural. PAT bottomed at Rs 89.35 Cr FY25 and recovered to Rs 159.61 Cr FY26, +78.6% YoY (B01). Borrowings were paid down from the FY24 peak of Rs 1,493.11 Cr to Rs 907.98 Cr FY26, down 39.2% (B01). Cash generation is genuinely elite: FY26 CFO/PAT 2.56x, ten-year cumulative CFO/PAT 1.37x, FCF +Rs 212.49 Cr with FCF swinging from minus Rs 21.6 Cr FY23 (B01). The reported ROCE of 6.7% FY26 is a Kurlon-goodwill artefact; operating (Cash) ROCE on the tangible-capital base runs 13% to 14% FY26, roughly twice reported (B04, B11 Pillar 1). Core EBITDA margin recovered from 7.3% FY25 to 10.8% FY26, plus about 300bps (fttcp-deliberation; B05 records 10.8% full-year FY26 versus 8.5% FY25 on its basis).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**
1. E-commerce / brand.com scale (conviction H): brand.com +136% YoY FY26; confirms if it holds above 50-60% YoY FY27; kills if growth falls below 50% as the base scales.
2. U2O (unorganized-to-organized) scaling profitably (conviction M-H): FY26 exit run-rate about Rs 120-125 Cr; confirms if value growth stays ahead of volume without margin dilution; kills if the segment is disclosed as EBITDA-dilutive.
3. RM pass-through holding through the TDI/polyol spike (conviction M, the single make-or-break print): 25-35% pass-through taken against a 25-35% input spike; confirms if gross margin is flat-to-up YoY in Q1/Q2 FY27; kills if compression exceeds 150bps in either quarter.
4. Kurlon synergy tail Rs 40-60 Cr (conviction L-M, SHARED CATALYST): machine install now pushed to Q1 FY27 after slipping all four quarters analysed; confirms on install plus a visible EBITDA step-up; kills on a fifth slip with no hard date.
5. Deleveraging to net-cash by FY28 (conviction M): about Rs 300 Cr remaining; FY26 reduction Rs 156 Cr.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Existing moats: distribution (13,000+ dealers, 1,800+ EBOs, durability moderate-high) and the Sleepwell + Kurlon dual brand (durability moderate-high, but dual-brand channel overlap is an unquantified execution risk) (B04). The largest-brand franchise position (~30% organized share) is what the operator credited with the +3.5x Strategic Premium at phase-3 review; the framework itself scored the brand gate at +0x because the documented-pricing-power sub-condition was not independently met (FY25 margin collapse on the RM shock; competition not limited). Emerging moats: EM 27 STRENGTHENING with six genuinely documented categories (VPF process line, U2O, e-commerce/Staqo, GCC first-mover, war-chest/first dividend, strategic partnerships — B07). Direction is strengthening but execution-unproven, which is exactly why the combined rating caps at TURNAROUND rather than HIGH POTENTIAL (F2 execution moat came back mixed against B05).

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter verdict CAUTION with a clean legal, regulatory and criminal record across all five sub-areas (the decisive positive), against a decade of zero dividend (2016-2025) alongside family profit-linked pay up to 3.75% of PBT, CFO turnover, external-CEO departure after about two years amid a weak quarter, and four independent directors exiting on term-expiry within a 2026 window (B08). Credibility grade B: strong delivery on headline financials (margin expansion, 78% PAT growth, first-ever dividend, Furlenco outperformance) offset by chronic under-delivery on discrete operational commitments (showroom target cut three times and still missed at about 600 versus 1,000; synergy tail slipping all four quarters) plus an earnings-flattering depreciation-policy change not proactively flagged (B05).

**3E. Market Opportunity.** TAM runway STRONG. SAM Rs 7,000 Cr organized mattress/foam, SFL at about 30% share, revenue headroom 3.33x, SOM-implied revenue CAGR 15.6% three-year, TAM growth 8.6% (B09). The Rs 1,00,000 Cr organized-market claim is read as inflated (4.8x the conservative TAM); the conservative Rs 7,000 Cr SAM is carried. Capacity is sufficient (SOM year-5 needs about 2.06x current volume, inside the stated 2.0-2.5x debottlenecking headroom).

**3F. Peer Positioning.** SFL's operating ROCE of 13-16% sits at or above a peer cohort itself in an earnings trough (CenturyPly ROCE 10.8%, Greenlam 7.9%, Nilkamal 10.0% — B11 tertiary). But peer read complicates the thesis, not supports it (B06 net_narrative_effect: complicates): Wakefit states competitive intensity and discounting have increased, not moderated, over the last six to nine months, directly contradicting SFL's implicit ASP-premiumisation assumption; and SFL's 25-35% claimed pass-through exceeds Wakefit's ~15% and CenturyPly's 7-15% over the same window, a gap not explained by segment mix alone.

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Sheela Foam is a good business at the wrong price. The turnaround is real and measurable: profit has roughly doubled off the FY25 trough, debt is down 39% from its peak, cash conversion is genuinely elite, the company paid its first dividend in a decade, and the four transitions score +5 of 8 on FTTCP, an investable business. None of that is the problem. The problem is that the market never punished the stock for the trough; the multi-year price consolidation held the price flat while earnings collapsed and are now recovering, so the multiple has stayed rich the entire time. At Rs 807 the stock trades at 55x reported and about 18x FY30 forward earnings, which means the recovery is already paid for. The operator granted a brand-franchise Strategic Premium and floored the destination PE at 20x; even so, the price at Rs 807 still fails the hurdle, and the valuation math stays unambiguous: not one of the nine scenario cells clears a 25% return, only the low-probability bull-high cell clears 15% at +15.5%, the base case returns just +3.1% over three years, the probability-weighted expectation is +2.4%, and the Hurdle Ratio stops at 0.95 against a 1.953 pass bar. The operator's instinct that consolidation has discounted the weakness is the right question and Role 1 tested it directly, twice; the answer is that the weakness sat in the earnings, not in the price.

So the discipline is simple and it is the whole point of the file: this is a WATCHLIST-for-entry name, not a buy. The business earns a place on the watchlist because the transition is genuine and the runway is strong; it does not earn capital until the price falls into Rs 362 to Rs 453, which is about 44% below today. Two things must also stay true while we wait: operating ROCE has to print above 14% by FY28, and the make-or-break near-term test, core EBITDA margin holding through the TDI and polyol spike in Q1 and Q2 FY27, has to land. If the price reaches the zone because one of those broke, the zone is withdrawn. If the business keeps delivering and the price simply drifts down to the zone, that is the buy, at Small size, capped by the promoter CAUTION. That the operator's more generous 20x floor still cannot make Rs 807 clear the hurdle only sharpens the point.

**Publication candidate: NO.** Verdict is AVOID-on-valuation with a 44% gap to entry; not a current-action idea.
