58	  Pace Digitek Limited Delivers a Strong Start to FY2027 with Robust Revenue
59	                    Growth and Continued BESS Expansion
60	       Q1 FY27 Revenue from Operations at ₹555.4 crore; Order Book at ₹ 10,803.3 crore
61	                           PAT stood at ₹62.5 crore, up 14.3% YoY
62	
63	Bengaluru, Wednesday, Aug 05, 2026: Pace Digitek Limited (NSE: PACEDIGITK | BSE: 544550), an
64	integrated telecom and energy infrastructure solutions company, announced its financial results for the
65	quarter ended June 30, 2026.
66	
67	During Q1 FY2027, the Company continued expanding its execution capabilities across Energy and
68	Telecom & ICT businesses, supported by growth in Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), telecom
69	infrastructure deployment, manufacturing integration and lifecycle service operations.
70

81	Operational Highlights – Energy
82	During the quarter, the Company has successfully delivered 90 Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)
83	containers. As of Aug 05, 2026, the executable Energy order book stood at ₹ 8,453 crore, while overall
84	BESS order visibility over 5 GWh, providing strong growth visibility for the business. Subsequent to the
85	quarter, the Company has also operationalized its additional BESS manufacturing platform with an
86	installed capacity of 2.5 GWh, bringing its total BESS manufacturing capacity to 5 GWh.
87	
88	The Company continued to strengthen its manufacturing capabilities through localization initiatives,
89	including the development of in-house container fabrication and manufacturing automation, aimed at
90	enhancing operational efficiency, supply chain integration, and execution capabilities. Pace Digitek also
91	progressed the development of Commercial & Industrial (C&I) energy storage prototype solutions, which
92	are currently under evaluation for commercial deployment.
93	
94	The Company continued to expand its presence across the Manufacturing, EPC, and selective Build-
95	Own-Operate (BOO) segments, supporting long-term revenue visibility and creating opportunities for
96	recurring income streams.
97	
98	Operational Highlights – Telecom & ICT
99	The executable Telecom & ICT order book stood at ₹ 2,350.3 crore as on Aug 05, 2026.
100	
101	During Q1FY2027, the Company has received an Advance Work Order from Bharat Sanchar Nigam
102	Limited (BSNL) for Design, Supply, Construction, Installation, Upgradation, Operation and Maintenance
103	
104	[page 3]
105	(O&M) of the middle-mile & last-mile network under the BharatNet programme in the Sikkim Telecom
106	Circle, with a contract value of ₹264 crore.
107	
108	The Company continued leveraging its telecom infrastructure execution capabilities and pan-India
109	deployment network to support expansion across adjacent infrastructure opportunities.
110	
111	Execution & Manufacturing Capabilities
112	During Q1 FY27, Pace Digitek continued to strengthen its EPC execution, commissioning, and deployment
113	capabilities across its telecom and energy infrastructure businesses. Subsequent to the quarter, the
114	Company commissioned an additional 2.5 GWh BESS manufacturing line, increasing its total installed
115	BESS manufacturing capacity to 5 GWh, enhancing its ability to meet growing customer demand.
116	
117	The Company also continued investing in the phased expansion of its BESS manufacturing capacity from
118	5 GWh to 10 GWh and remains on track to commission its in-house container fabrication facility, further
119	strengthening localization, operational efficiency, and supply chain integration.
120	
121	Other Strategic Developments
122	During the quarter, the Company continued to strengthen its BESS business through strategic
123	partnerships and manufacturing expansion. Pace Digitek entered into an Original Equipment
124	Manufacturer (OEM) partnership with NEC XON Systems Proprietary Limited, South Africa, to market,
125	distribute and deploy its grid-scale BESS and related energy solutions across South Africa, Botswana,
126	Mozambique, Namibia and Mauritius. The partnership provides the Company with an established
127	regional go-to-market platform to support its expansion into select African markets.
128	
129	Following the quarter-end, Pace Digitek further strengthened its long-term growth platform through key
130	strategic initiatives. The Company entered into a strategic cooperation agreement with MEGMEET
131	Electrical India to support opportunities in AI data center power infrastructure. It also established the
132	Pace-Lineage Research Center in Pune, a dedicated R&D facility focused on advancing research,
133	engineering and product development in Advanced Chemistry Cells (ACC) and energy storage
134	technologies. These initiatives reinforce the Company's technology capabilities, integrated energy
135	infrastructure platform and long-term innovation strategy.

164	About Pace Digitek:
165	
166	Pace Digitek Limited (BSE: 544550, NSE: PACEDIGITK) is an integrated infrastructure and energy
167	solutions company operating across the Energy and Telecom & ICT business segments. The Company
168	operates across the value chain, spanning Manufacturing, Engineering, Procurement & Construction
169	(EPC) and Operations & Maintenance (O&M) activities, supporting both project execution and product
170	supply.
171	In the energy segment, the Company undertakes Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) design,
172	manufacturing, system integration and deployment. Through its subsidiary, Lineage Power Private
173	Limited, the Company manufactures energy storage systems and related power infrastructure products.
174	In the Telecom & ICT segment, the Company manufactures telecom power systems and delivers EPC and
175	O&M services for telecom infrastructure, fibre networks and digital infrastructure projects across India.
