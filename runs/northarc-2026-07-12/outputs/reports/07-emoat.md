### 2C. Growth embedded in "capex" — arithmetic

Fixed-asset-turnover × capex-under-execution is **NOT APPLICABLE** for an NBFC: the balance sheet is a
liability-funded loan book, not a plant. Applying a classic fixed-asset-turnover multiplier to
NORTHARC's ₹41cr FY25 capex (largely office/IT infrastructure) would produce a meaningless
"implied incremental revenue" figure disconnected from how the business actually grows (AUM growth is
funded by borrowings against capital adequacy headroom, not by PP&E). capex_embedded_growth_pct is
therefore recorded as NOT APPLICABLE (0 in the YAML field per the B01 NBFC-inapplicable-metric
convention). The NBFC-analog "capacity headroom" metric is capital adequacy: 22.6% at Mar-26 vs the
regulatory minimum, explicitly cited by management as giving "ample headroom to grow the balance
sheet over the next two to three years" (Concall_May_2026, line 299-300). 🎙️/📄 (ratio is disclosed;
the growth-headroom characterisation is management framing).

presentation, or three concalls.
