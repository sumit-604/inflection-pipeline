# FRATELLI VINEYARDS — Step 1 business brief

Run: runs/fratelli-2026-09-07 | BSE 541741, BSE-only line | CMP Rs 135,
market cap Rs 589 cr (screener, 07-Sep-2026).

## What the business is

Fratelli makes and sells premium Indian wine. It owns vineyards at Akluj,
Maharashtra. It runs vineyard tourism. It has added a ready-to-drink line,
Shotgun. The listed shell was Tinna Trade Ltd, an agri-commodity trader. Tinna
issued 3,07,79,184 shares to buy Fratelli Wines outright, renamed itself
Fratelli Vineyards, and phased out the trading book.
(https://www.just-drinks.com/news/agri-group-tinna-trade-to-buy-indias-fratelli-wines/)

## The numbers, and the trap in them

Consolidated sales run Rs 421 cr (FY24), Rs 276 cr (FY25), Rs 181 cr (FY26).
That looks like collapse. It is not one business. FY24 still carried the agri
trading book. The wine business alone did Rs 215.6 cr net revenue in FY24 with
Rs 28.6 cr EBITDA, 70% of it premium.
(https://www.indianwineacademy.com/articles/item_2_956/)
FY26 at Rs 181 cr is wine-only. So the real question is not why sales halved.
It is why the wine line went from Rs 216 cr to Rs 181 cr while it was being
called a growth story.

Losses are real and widening. Net profit: Rs -0.15 cr (FY24), Rs -13.98 cr
(FY25), Rs -24.91 cr (FY26). Operating profit FY26 is Rs -7.8 cr on Rs 181 cr.
(inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv)

Q1FY27 is the first turn. Standalone revenue Rs 45.4 cr, up 22% on Rs 37.1 cr.
EBITDA back to Rs +1.1 cr from Rs -2.3 cr. PAT still Rs -3.8 cr, narrowed from
Rs -5.8 cr. RTD sales doubled. Bottle sales grew 8%.
(https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/fratelli-vineyards-q1-results-ebitda-turns-positive-as-revenue-rises-22/48075673)
Management guides 30% growth and PAT breakeven in FY27.
(https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/fratelli-vineyards-to-hold-q4-fy26-earnings-call-on-june-2/41251885)

## Where the money is stuck

Receivables Rs 105 cr against sales Rs 181 cr. That is 212 days. Inventory
Rs 95 cr. Cash Rs 1.65 cr. Borrowings Rs 137.6 cr against reserves Rs 92 cr.
Cash from operations was Rs -7.0 cr in FY25 and Rs +5.0 cr in FY26.
(inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv)

## The engine the thesis rests on

One engine: operating leverage. Gross margin is high, brand spend is fixed, and
volume is meant to cover it. Vision 2030 targets Rs 500 cr revenue at 20%
margin. Nothing in the filed record yet proves the leverage works. FY26 says the
opposite. Q1FY27 is one quarter of evidence.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (verify against the filings first)

1. **The revenue basis.** Reconcile FY24 consolidated Rs 421.35 cr to the wine
   business's Rs 215.6 cr. Read the FY25 and FY26 AR segment notes. Establish
   which years are wine-only and which carry the trading book. Every growth or
   decline claim in this run depends on this reconciliation. Get it wrong and
   the whole trend line is fiction.

2. **The 77-80% gross margin claim.** It does not reconcile to the consolidated
   P&L. FY26 raw material cost Rs 54.98 cr on Rs 181.29 cr sales is 70% gross
   margin before the Rs 15.26 cr inventory change; FY25 is 54%. Find the basis
   management uses. Then find the actual advertising and brand-spend line: FY26
   "Other Expenses" is Rs 114.14 cr against Rs 7.53 cr in FY25, while "Selling
   and admin" (Rs 77.12 cr in FY25) is blank in FY26. That is a
   reclassification, not a real jump. Until the A&P number is named, "brand
   spend eats the margin" is unverified.

3. **Guidance versus delivery.** Management guides 30% growth and PAT breakeven
   in FY27. Read the five concalls in inputs/concalls/, Q4FY25 to Q4FY26, chronologically and score
   what was promised against what printed. This company has guided through two
   loss-making years.

4. **Receivables at 212 days.** Wine sells through state excise corporations,
   which pay slowly. Check whether 212 days is the category norm (SULA's
   Data_Sheet gives the comparison) or something else. Check the ageing and any
   related-party balances in the FY26 AR.

5. **Promoter stake consolidation.** A promoter took 20.79% by gift in 2026.
   Read the disclosure and the related-party note.
   (https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/fratelli-vineyards-promoter-consolidates-20-79-stake-via-gift/48859606)

## PEERS AND WHY

- **SULA (Sula Vineyards)** — the only listed Indian wine pure-play. Same
  product, same excise route-to-market, same wine-tourism line. It is also the
  cautionary case: sales flat at Rs 556-579 cr for three years and net profit
  down from Rs 93 cr to Rs 26 cr. It tells you what the category's ceiling and
  its working capital look like.
- **TI (Tilaknagar Industries)** — the transition-proof comp. Alco-bev, went
  from deep losses to profit on premiumisation and brand spend. Sales Rs 1,434
  cr to Rs 2,346 cr in the last year. This is the climb Fratelli claims.
- **SDBL (Som Distilleries & Breweries)** — beer and RTD. The Shotgun comp.
  Brand-spend heavy, capacity-ramp driven. Sales Rs 1,229 cr, net profit
  collapsed from Rs 104.5 cr to Rs 9.4 cr, so it also shows how fast brand-led
  alco-bev margin can break.
