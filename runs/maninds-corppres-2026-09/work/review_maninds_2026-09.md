### 6A ROCE — do NOT adopt the deck's 18.4% (FN3, load-bearing)

The deck prints "18.4% FY26 ROCE" and "9.2% FY26 ROE" (L124) with NO basis
note anywhere across the 37 slides (A3 checked; none exists). Reconstruction:
- ROE 9.2% ties to PAT 1,705 / average net worth ((16,073+20,865)/2 = 18,469)
  = 9.23%. So the deck uses AVERAGE denominators.
- Plain EBIT (PBT 2,370 + finance 1,520 = 3,890) / year-end capital employed
  (NW 20,865 + borrowings 4,997 = 25,862) = 15.0%; including leases ~14.3%.
- Ex-other-income EBIT (EBITDA 4,679 - OI 286 - D&A 789 = 3,604) / 25,862 =
  **13.9%**. This is the operator's 13.8% construction and it survives the deck.
- To reach 18.4% the denominator must fall to ~21,141: it requires EBIT with
  Other Income IN the numerator AND the Rs325.8 Cr Jammu CWIP stripped OUT of
  capital employed AND an average (not year-end) base. Three undisclosed levers.
- The tell: an 18.4% ROCE against a 9.2% ROE is a 920bps gap, far wider than a
  lightly levered pipe converter should show. The numerator is inflated and/or
  the capital base understated.
- **Resolution: reject 18.4%. The operator's consolidated 13.8% stands** (EBIT
  ex-other-income over full year-end capital employed including CWIP). Cite both
  deck numbers at L124; state that no reconciling basis note exists. This drives
  the explicit ROCE-basis management question.
