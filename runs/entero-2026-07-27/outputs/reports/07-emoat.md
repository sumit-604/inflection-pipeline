## FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN

**B1 backward integration and RM security (re-purposed here as manufacturer-relationship security,
the distributor analogue).** Manufacturer relationships grew from 1,700 (FY22) to 3,300 (FY26), a
~94% increase over four years (AR p.27; Inv. Pres. FY26 deck slide 22/34). MD&A explicitly claims
"exclusive pan-India distribution partnerships with leading manufacturers" in MedTech (AR p.30) and
names distributed brands (Medtronic, Terumo, MED-EL, BrainsWay, ASAHI, AR p.9-10) consistent with
genuine manufacturer relationships. WEAKNESS: no manufacturer is named as contractually exclusive, no
contract duration or termination terms are disclosed, and the relationship COUNT fell from 3,300+
(FY26 year-end) to 3,000+ at Q1 FY27 (Inv. Pres. Q1FY27 deck slide 11) — a ~9% sequential decline,
even as it still grew YoY (2,600+ Q1FY26 → 3,000+ Q1FY27, +15%). Evidence type: 🎙️/📄 mixed (count is
📄, "exclusive" claim is 🎙️ unbacked by named contracts). Strength: WEAK. Likelihood/Impact: MM(2).

**B2 qualification lock-in.** MedTech distribution requires manufacturer authorisation, and the AR
frames several FY26 deals as bringing "exclusive" distribution rights (AR p.30), but no design-in,
customer-funded tooling, or multi-year post-qualification agreement is documented with specifics.
Countervailing evidence: a subsidiary (Atreja Healthcare Solutions) had its OWN drug licence
temporarily suspended for 3 days in the year (Karnal Zone, 30-Jan to 1-Feb-2026, per 03-ardeep.md
Phase 5E) — a reminder that licence-based qualification cuts both ways and is a compliance risk, not
only a moat. Evidence type: 🎙️ claim. Strength: WEAK. Likelihood/Impact: MM(2).

**B3 supply chain network effect.** This is the strongest candidate in the scan and the company's own
stated core pitch. Entero explicitly describes a "hub and spoke" model connecting warehouses and
supply points (AR p.17; Inv. Pres. FY26 deck slide 37), aggregating supply from 3,300+ manufacturers
to 1,05,300+ retail customers and 3,600+ hospitals across 523 districts via 136 warehouses (AR p.2,
p.6, p.27). Management directly invokes a "2-way network effect" (100,000+ customers, 3,000+ supplier
relationships) when challenged on durable moat (Q4 FY26 call, Bharat Shah exchange, per 05-concall.md
Section 3C). COUNTER-EVIDENCE, weighed honestly: (a) the retail-customer and hospital-customer counts
that anchor this network fell sharply — 1,05,300+ → 72,000+ retail (-32%) and 3,600+ → 2,300+ hospital
(-36%) Q4 FY26 → Q1 FY27 (AR p.27 vs Inv. Pres. Q1FY27 deck slide 11), unreconciled beyond a cited
"~2.5% revenue drag from low-margin exits" that does not obviously scale to a 28-36% count drop (per
B05 red_flags); (b) WAREHOUSE count kept RISING over the same quarter (136 → 138, +1.5%, Inv. Pres.
Q1FY27 deck slide 11) even as customer counts fell sharply — an internal inconsistency worth naming
on its own: a pure "pruning low-margin sub-distribution accounts" story would not obviously require
MORE warehouse capacity in the same quarter customer counts fall by a third; (c) India's top-3
distributor share is itself only 8-10% of the market (Inv. Pres. FY26 deck slide 33, prospectus-
sourced), meaning no single player, including Entero, has yet built a market-dominant network position
— the network effect claim is directional, not yet proven at scale versus rivals. Evidence type: 📄
for the scale/model description, but the CURRENT-QUARTER TREND evidence cuts against "strengthening."
Strength: MODERATE (existence and historical scale are real and documented; near-term trajectory is
genuinely contested, not a clean expansion story). Likelihood/Impact: HM(3).

**I2 cannibalization barrier.** Applied to the strongest moat claimed in this scan (B3 network

"the set of distributors available for acquisition is huge, while the buyers are only 2 or 3" (Q3

sitting at only 8-10% (H1 above) is itself direct evidence that no player has yet built a structural
moat a rival cannot copy with capital and time. Per the framework's explicit rule, this is an
execution lead, not a configuration barrier, and execution leads close. Score: 0.
