## SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

### CRITICAL FINDINGS (Deal-Breaker Level)

1. **FY26 Other Income Mismatch**: B01 claims Rs 51.7 cr (520 mn); Annual Report shows Rs 720 mn (72.0 cr). Difference: 200 mn (20.0 cr, 28% variance). This affects the P&L margin narrative in B01.
   - **Impact**: FY26 Other Income materially overstated the drag on PAT. Correct figure (720 mn) is higher, reducing the "other income headwind" narrative.
   - **Severity**: CRITICAL
   - **source_fidelity: true**

### MAJOR FINDINGS (Material Accuracy Errors)

2. **Standalone PAT YoY Growth (B02)**: Claimed +16.9% not independently verified. Standalone P&L exists in annual report but not extracted. Consolidated PAT declined -1.4%, not standalone.
   - **Severity**: MAJOR
   - **source_fidelity: true**

3. **Hedge Loss Balance Sheet Position (B02/B03)**: Claimed Rs 552 mn; Annual Report shows derivative financial liabilities Rs 586 mn. Mismatch: 34 mn (6.1%).
   - **Severity**: MAJOR
