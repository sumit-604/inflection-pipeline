1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- No policy changes with quantified P&L impact disclosed this year. First-time adoption note (Note
  2, p.91-92 standalone / p.163-164 consolidated) covers Ind AS 21 lack-of-exchangeability
  amendment (effective FY26, not yet adopted) and Ind AS 116/117 amendments — "had no impact." 🟢
- Revenue recognition (Note 1(b), p.80-81 standalone): mix of input-method (cost-to-cost %
  completion) for engineering/construction/O&M and output-method (units/km/shot-points) for seismic
  survey. Judgmental (KAM in both audit reports, p.62 and p.135) — inherent estimation risk in
  contract-cost budgets and warranty/LD provisioning. 🟡
- Warranty obligation deducted from contracted transaction price grew from ₹72.84 Lakhs to
  ₹323.90 Lakhs YoY, a 4.4x increase (Note 50(a), p.128 standalone / Note 49(a), p.200
  consolidated) — outpaces the 52% revenue growth, consistent with larger/more complex project
  mix carrying higher LD/warranty exposure. 🟡
- Depreciation: Holding Co. and one subsidiary use internally-assessed useful lives (10-15 yrs for
  survey/communication and ground electronics equipment; 5-15 yrs for portable drilling rigs)
  instead of Schedule II defaults (Note 1(f)/(e), p.82-83 standalone, p.154-155 consolidated).
  Whether these lives are more or less conservative than Schedule II defaults is NOT FOUND IN
  DOCUMENT (no side-by-side comparison given).
- Oil & gas assets: "successful efforts" method — exploration costs pre-legal-rights expensed
  immediately; unit-of-production depletion (Note 1(g), p.83). Standard, reasonably conservative.
- ECL matrix: ageing-based rates 0.10%-5.00% (<180 days) rising to 100% (>545 days) (Note 41,
  p.115-116 standalone / p.187 consolidated) — reasonable gradation but note the largest single
  receivable concentration (related-party JVs, see §2) sits mostly in the "not due"/<180-day
  bucket where the ECL rate is minimal, so the matrix does not really price the concentration risk
  of a handful of JV counterparties. 🟡
- Ind AS 116 leases: right-of-use asset and lease liability recognized; low-value/short-term lease
  exemption used extensively — machinery/vehicle hire charges of ₹4,001.52 Lakhs (standalone,
  Note 39.2(iii), p.114) and ₹4,005.38 Lakhs (consolidated, Note 39.2(iii), p.185) are expensed as
  short-term leases rather than capitalized, materially reducing balance-sheet lease liabilities
  versus a company that capitalized all such arrangements. This is a judgment call but a large one:
  ROU assets carry at only ₹43.52 Lakhs (Note 6, p.95) while ₹4,000+ Lakhs of machinery/vehicle
  hire runs through the P&L as opex. 🟡
