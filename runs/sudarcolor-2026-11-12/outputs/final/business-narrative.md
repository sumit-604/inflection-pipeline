# Sudarshan Colorants India Ltd (SUDARCOLOR): business narrative, phase 1

Run 2026-11-12. Phase 1 lite: evidence stages only. No valuation ran, so this file carries no fair value, no entry zone, no destination PE and no decision. All figures in ₹ Cr unless the source says otherwise; the source unit is on the face of the document, not in the filename.

## BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Sudarshan Colorants makes colour pigments at three Indian plants, Roha, Cuddalore and Nagda. The range runs to more than 500 products: organic pigments such as quinacridone, DPP and phthalocyanine, inorganic pigments such as bismuth vanadate, rutile and cobalt blue, plus pigment preparations and dyes. Paint, plastics and ink makers mix these powders and pastes into their own products to give them colour. Sales of goods to outside customers are about 72% of revenue, sales of goods to the company's own promoter group are about 25%, and services of an undisclosed kind are about 3%. The group line is finished pigment shipped to affiliates abroad, mainly a Singapore affiliate that took ₹137 Cr, for resale through the parent's network.

The outside customers are more than 100 domestic buyers in coatings, plastics and printing inks, plus special uses such as seed colour and anodising dyes. The company discloses no major customer, so concentration outside the group is not known. Inside the group, one Singapore affiliate takes 17.6% of revenue, and the parent decides plant loading, product transfers and sourcing. The annual report claims that its grades are approved into customer specifications, for example in automotive coatings. The run did not establish qualification cycle lengths or switching costs, because the filings carry no retention data.

Present demand comes from paint, the largest named application, and the Indian paint makers' volume growth signal tracks it. Plastics, packaging and printing ink producer demand covers the other two core applications. Group offtake is the third source, and the Sudarshan Chemical Industries consolidated results and half-yearly related-party filing is the signal for it. In FY26 domestic revenue grew 3.6% while sales to the US affiliate fell from ₹77 Cr to ₹21 Cr.

Forward demand rests on five drivers. First, the end of customer destocking, which the parent and two independent peers describe in part. Second, the parent's move of Azo products from Frankfurt to Roha, named in the chairman's letter, with the parent saying it has gained some intercompany business. Third, the shift in electric vehicle shades, tracked by the automotive and EV production volumes signal. Fourth, recycling compatible and food contact grades for Indian packaging rules, for which the company names no rule and no date. Fifth, duty protection against China, tracked by the anti-dumping or safeguard orders on Chinese pigments signal, and against Chinese pigment export prices and India pigment import volumes. Independent peers put industry growth at 3% to 4% a year, and the run's market sizing puts India demand growth at 4.1%.

No business line has a confirmed moat. In the third party line, specification approvals and one product registered with the US EPA are candidates only, and gross margin fell from 42% to 39% in FY26 while peers passed input costs through year on year. The group line has no moat of its own, because the parent allocates its volume. The parent platform is the only emerging moat category rated moderate, and its benefit sits with the controlling owner, not with the minority holder. The run did not establish what the services line is or whether it has an edge. The emerging moat score is 9.9 against a threshold of 12, class NONE, and talent asymmetry and the cannibalisation barrier, categories 21 and 22, both score zero. The Asahi Songwon pigment utilisation and margin commentary is the independent read on whether any Indian pigment maker holds pricing power.

## The parent now decides this company's growth

Sudarshan Colorants is an Indian pigment maker incorporated in 1956. It was Clariant Chemicals (India), then Heubach Colorants India, and took its present name in December 2025. FY26 revenue was ₹781 Cr, down 5.4% from ₹825 Cr, and PAT was ₹45 Cr. Revenue has shrunk 2.5% a year over nine years. The company has no borrowings beyond leases and held ₹235 Cr of cash and bank balances on a net worth of ₹570 Cr at March 2026. It has paid no dividend since FY21.

Ownership changed in March 2025, when Sudarshan Chemical Industries bought the global Heubach pigment business. An open offer took the promoter group to 70.26%. The parent says it moved Frankfurt products to the Indian site at Roha and gained some intercompany business. Q2 FY27 revenue was ₹270 Cr against ₹183 Cr, up 47%, and H1 FY27 revenue was ₹492 Cr, up 25%. H1 FY27 PBT was ₹59.67 Cr against ₹44 Cr. Materials consumed rose while traded goods purchases fell, which reads as own manufacture replacing trading. Two readings exist. One is a lasting step up as the parent loads Roha with group volume. The other is a one time fill routed through group entities. The H1 FY27 related party filing, which splits group and outside sales, separates them, and it is not yet in the record.

The backward score is weak and the cash record is worse. Gate 0 scores 60 of 160 with zero moats, class AVERAGE, because median ROCE is 8% on operating profit. On the screener convention, which keeps other income in, median ROCE is 10.2% and the class becomes GOOD; the operator has not yet ruled which convention applies. The depressors are surplus cash in capital employed and four early years, FY17 to FY20, at 1% to 4% ROCE. Operating cash flow fell from ₹111 Cr in FY25 to ₹34 Cr in FY26 and to minus ₹24 Cr in H1 FY27. About ₹21 Cr of the FY26 figure came from released balances with government authorities, so the underlying FY26 figure is closer to ₹14 Cr. In H1 FY27 inventories rose to ₹255 Cr from ₹156 Cr and receivables to ₹245 Cr from ₹179 Cr. At March 2026 overdue trade payables were ₹62 Cr, 37.5% of payables, against ₹1 Cr a year earlier, while the company held its cash pile.

Earnings quality cuts both ways. Other income was 43% of FY26 profit before exceptional items, and core profit fell 38.5%. Gross margin fell to 39.3% from 42.2%. On the other side, H1 FY27 operating EBITDA margin rose to 12.2% from 10.7% on 25% more revenue, which shows a fixed cost base at work. Part of that gain may be overhead held in the H1 FY27 stock build of ₹45 Cr in finished goods, work in progress and traded stock, against ₹26 Cr a year earlier. The amount of overhead absorbed is not disclosed.

Management gives direction but no numbers. The company holds no calls and has no rating. The credibility grade is C. The one live promise, a return to growth in FY27, is met so far at H1. The letters also print figures the accounts do not support. Markets outside the US "grew 7%" while outside India revenue fell 24%. The FY25 chairman's letter gave revenue of ₹779 Cr against filed revenue of ₹825 Cr, and a renewables claim conflicts with the FY25 sustainability report. The FY25 audit opinion was qualified over ₹25.81 Cr of Airoli assets moved to Clariant India without board approval. The new owner recovered them in October 2025, and the FY26 opinion is clean but gives no accounting effect. The interim CFO is the parent's CFO, and the audit trail is not enabled at database level. The promoter read is CAUTION, not CONCERN: no deal breaker, a clean record in the searches run, and group purchases rising to 32% of the total, priced on assertion. That search stage was partial. The chairman, who also runs the parent, cut the acquired group's FY26 EBITDA guidance twice, then beat it in Q4 FY26.

Peers complicate the story but contradict nothing. The parent explains three parts of the jump: a low base at the destocking trough, volume recovery from Q4 FY26, and transfers from Frankfurt to Roha. Meghmani's pigment revenue rose about 40% year on year in Q2 FY27 and 23% quarter on quarter, close to this company's 21% sequential step. So a low base and the pass through of input costs into prices explain part of the year on year size. Independent peers show plants at 39% to 51% utilisation and industry growth of 3% to 4%, which makes 47% hard to read as open market demand alone. Corrected peer figures put the company's FY26 EBITDA margin of 7.4% against a peer median of 8.2%, and its gross margin of 39.3% level with a peer median of 39.0%. Peers held gross margin year on year under input inflation while this company's fell.

The market is larger than the company but grows slowly. The run sizes India coloured pigment demand at ₹8,037 Cr to ₹14,074 Cr, growing 4.1% a year. Outside sales of ₹589 Cr are 7.2% of the serviceable market, with headroom of 13.9 times, runway class MODERATE. Holding share flat gives outside sales of about ₹665 Cr in three years. Gaining 1.5 points of share gives ₹803 Cr, a 10.9% annual rate. Outside sales were flat in FY26 at ₹589 Cr against ₹590 Cr, and the H1 FY27 split is not filed. Plant utilisation is not disclosed, and two market report pages could not be fetched, so the sizing stage is partial.
