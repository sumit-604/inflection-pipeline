# B12b Verifier B: Concall Red Flags. Creative Newtech Ltd (CNL), run 2026-11-11, phase 1

Model: claude-opus-5-5. Inputs read: 3 CNL transcripts (Q2 FY26 12 Nov 2025; Q4 FY26 15 May 2026; Q2 FY27 29 Oct 2026), 12 peer transcripts (RPTECH, CPPLUS, REDINGTON, four each), B05 report and block, B06 report, CNL results filings (Q4 FY26, Q2 FY27) for delivery checks.

Units: ₹ Cr unless stated. The results filings say "Rs. in Lakhs" / "Rs. in Lacs" on their face; I converted at /100. Anchors: "Q2FY26 p7" = CNL Q2 FY26 transcript, page 7. Peer anchors give peer, call quarter and speaker.

Method: I read all three CNL transcripts in full first, then the peer transcripts (CPPLUS x4 and RPTECH Q4 FY26 / Q1 FY27 / REDINGTON Q2 FY27 in full; the other RPTECH and REDINGTON calls by targeted search on the Middle East, freight, receivables, credit and growth). Only after building my list did I open B05 and B06.

Scope limits: the presentations and announcements folders are outside my inputs. B05 items that rest on them (Infinova's status in the Q1 FY27 presentation, the Zicom filing, the BSNL AWO missing from announcements) I checked only for consistency with the transcripts. Zicom does not appear in any CNL transcript (searched).

## 4. Credibility grade

B05 grade: C (Mixed). My view: **lower (C toward D).** The 6 delivered / 5 partial / 8 missed tally supports C on its own. Three things the grade does not price:

- Two explanations B05 files as external-blame fail the peer test (F12 Middle East, F13 Diwali).
- The CFO's debt bridge to two analysts does not reconcile to the audited balance sheet (F18).
- The H1 FY27 cash-flow headline rests on payables and a 197 Cr unexplained liability rise (F2).

Strong PAT and margin delivery are real, which is why I would not go further than D.
