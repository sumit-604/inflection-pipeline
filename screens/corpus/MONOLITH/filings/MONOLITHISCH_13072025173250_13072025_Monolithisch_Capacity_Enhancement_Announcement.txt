Press Release 
 
 
Capacity Enhancement at Monolithisch India Limited 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is proud to announce a significant expansion in its installed 
manufacturing capacity as part of its ongoing strategic growth initiative. Effective 15th July, the 
company's production capacity will increase from 132,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum) to 
156,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum). 
 
This enhancement marks a major milestone in our long-term vision to reinforce Monolithisch 
India's leadership in the unshaped refractory materials segment. The capacity upgrade is a 
direct result of sustained operational improvements, the adoption of advanced technologies, 
and our unwavering commitment to delivering superior value to our customers. 
 
As part of the phased expansion currently underway, Monolithisch India is targeting to increase 
its capacity to 215,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum) around 15th September and reach to 
250,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum) around 31st December. This increase in capacity in 
phases is designed to meet the rising demand for our existing product range as well as our 
newly launched premium offering, SGB-Limited. The added capacity will ensure timely 
deliveries, improved performance, and consistent quality across domestic and international 
markets. 
 
This initiative is a strategic step towards our ambitious goal of reaching a net installed capacity 
of 574,000 TPA (Tonnes Per Annum) [including Subsidiary- Metalurgica India Private 
Limited] over a period of time. With this, we aim to serve the evolving needs of the secondary 
steel sector with enhanced scale and efficiency. 
 
All current and future upgrades are aligned with our focus on sustainability, operational 
excellence, and customer-centricity. By investing in next-generation technology, infrastructure, 
and talent, Monolithisch India continues to position itself at the forefront of the ramming mass 
industry.
