## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** NOCIL makes rubber chemicals, antioxidants and accelerators, for tyre makers (the Q3 FY26 call discusses its tyre customers: doc 5eb8ac01, page 6, search snippet). It buys chemical inputs, converts them, and sells on a spread. FY26 revenue was Rs 1,303 crore, operating EBITDA Rs 101 crore at 7.7%, PAT Rs 56 crore (Q4 FY26 investor presentation, doc 03a8fa05, pages 7 and 28).

**What is changing?** Margin is recovering from a four year slide. Q1 FY27 operating EBITDA was Rs 45 crore at 11.2%, up 210 basis points year on year (Q1 FY27 investor presentation, doc 565d872a, page 7). A Rs 250 crore Dahej plant is in trial production (Q4 FY26 investor presentation, doc cf585e0f, page 14).

**Why now?** Volume turned. H2 FY26 volume grew 12% after a 5% fall in H1 (doc cf585e0f, page 6). Q1 FY27 revenue rose 20% to Rs 403 crore and PAT rose 61% to Rs 28 crore (doc 565d872a, page 7).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The spread must widen and stay wide. Management guides EBITDA "from the base of financial year '26 by another 150 basis points" (Q4 FY26 call, doc cb0c08df, page 15). Specialty share must rise from 15% to 20% (page 17).

**What breaks it?** Chinese supply. The company names domestic dumping and price pressure (investor presentation, doc 03a8fa05, page 6, search snippet). If that pressure is structural, the FY23 margin of 15.6% does not return.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One engine: tonnes sold times spread. Spread is realisation minus input cost. Volume index reached 150 in Q4 FY26 on a Q1 FY20 base of 100 (doc cb0c08df, page 4). FY26 volume grew 3% (doc cf585e0f, page 6).

Specialty products are about 15% of the mix (doc cb0c08df, page 17). The rest is standard accelerators and antioxidants sold into tyre plants. Tyre industry growth is guided at 4% to 6% a year (Q4 FY25 call, doc bd47e02e, page 3).

**Archetype: Commodity converter.** v3.7 Amendment 17 binds. The variables are spread, utilisation, cycle position and cost curve rank. This card uses no spot year ROCE. It also does not feed the rupee working capital release into any valuation input.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Present but narrow. Management claims it "grew over 5 times the global market growth" from 2012 to 2023 (FY24 investor presentation, doc 98be532a, page 23). It aims "to double its market share" across Asia, Europe and the US (doc cf585e0f, page 15).

Pricing power is absent in this cycle. Margin fell from 15.6% in FY23 to 7.7% in FY26 while volume grew. A price maker does not lose half its margin on rising volume. Verdict: **cost advantage present, pricing power absent**.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R2 COST-ADVANTAGED CONVERTER. TO R3 VALUE-ADDED / SPEC'D SUPPLIER, claimed.

The mechanism: more specialty grades, sold on specification, raise realisation per tonne. A second Rs 130 crore capex for specialty chemicals was announced on 16 March 2026, due by H1 FY28 (doc cb0c08df, page 4).

Management's claim, quoted once: "We would be at about 15%. And I think with this, we should be able to go to 20% is what our expectation is" (doc cb0c08df, page 17).

A 5 point mix shift leaves 80% of the mix commodity grade. The card holds NOCIL at R2 until the shift shows in margin.
