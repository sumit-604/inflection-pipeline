### 2B. Diagnostic answers (six mandatory + one grafted margin answer)

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** No. Standalone revenue fell **−21.5%** (Rs132.24 Cr → Rs103.85 Cr [96]). Press attributes it to "subdued billing… customers deferring dispatches amid macroeconomic and supply chain uncertainty" (l.822). There is no Notion base case to compare against (new name); this is the initiation baseline.
2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand or contract YoY?** Contracted hard: **Q1 FY27 operating margin −0.36% vs Q1 FY26 5.23% = −559 bps.** The business ran an operating loss at the EBITDA line this quarter.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-Other Income) grow YoY?** No — it **deteriorated from (0.51) to (6.63) Cr, a Rs6.12 Cr decline.** Core operations were already marginally loss-making a year ago; the loss deepened roughly 13x. The headline loss is a REAL operating deterioration, not a treasury or one-off artifact.
4. **What drove the gap between core operating PBT and reported PAT? [GAP 1 GRAFT — finance-cost driver disputed]** There is essentially no favourable gap this quarter: reported PBT (−5.95) is close to core PBT ex-OI (−6.63), the Rs0.68 Cr difference being small Other Income. No exceptional item, no tax benefit (tax was a Rs0.04 Cr charge on a loss), no treasury rescue. The one line that partially cushioned the loss was finance costs, down Rs1.10 Cr (−17.3%) YoY — **but this must NOT be read as clean net-debt deleveraging.** Three items in this very filing point the other way: (a) the press release attributes the fall explicitly to "improved working capital utilisation" (l.828), i.e. lower drawdown, not principal repayment; (b) the segment "Unallocated" net-borrowing proxy WORSENED by ~Rs46 Cr QoQ, from Rs(148.94) Cr at Mar-26 to Rs(195.05) Cr at Jun-26 [985]; and (c) the going-concern KAM flags "certain overdue payments to creditors" (l.1046). The likely driver is therefore payable-stretch / a lower working-capital drawdown, NOT a reduction in net debt — which makes the Rs1.10 Cr cushion low-quality and potentially self-reversing (stretched payables must eventually be paid, adding back finance/working-capital cost). Treated conservatively as a non-durable tailwind (feeds Question 8).
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue? [GAP 1 GRAFT]** No — both FELL YoY (D&A −5.6%, finance −17.3%) while revenue fell −21.5%. There is no capex-absorption gap; D&A is flat/declining and there is no new-plant ramp. The finance-cost fall, however, is NOT confirmed steady-state deleveraging: Note 5 states restructured JLF term loans were repaid (l.284), yet the segment unallocated-borrowing proxy worsened ~Rs46 Cr QoQ [985] and the press ties the saving to working-capital utilisation (l.828). So the reduction more plausibly reflects lower short-term/WC borrowing drawdown (or payable-stretch) than a durable fall in the debt stack. Conservative read: do not bank the Rs1.10 Cr as a recurring structural saving until the H1 FY27 balance sheet confirms net debt actually fell.
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Quarterly Other Income is immaterial (Rs0.68 Cr, 0.7% of revenue) and falling. The concentration issue is at the FY level: FY26 Other Income of Rs11.06 Cr is property-sale-inflated (Note 10, l.381), so FY26's reported PBT of Rs8.75 Cr is not a recurring operating run-rate. Stripped, FY26 core PBT ex-OI ex-exceptional is only Rs5.78 Cr.
7. **Did gross margin improve? [GAP 2 GRAFT — grafted margin answer]** Only on the flattered basis. The press's "material cost improved to 69.1% from 71.9%" (l.826) is a NET-materials ratio that nets a Rs9.12 Cr inventory build (change in inventories (912) vs (159) lakhs YoY, l.101) against consumed materials. On the honest, like-for-like **materials-CONSUMED basis, gross margin DETERIORATED ~470 bps, to 22.2% from 26.9%** (Step 1C note v). The apparent improvement is an inventory-timing artifact; on the cost that actually flowed through COGS, unit economics worsened. And building Rs9.12 Cr of inventory while revenue fell −21.5% is a working-capital warning sign, not evidence of demand — it either pre-positions for the promised H2 order-book conversion (bull) or is unsold stock accumulating against a weak market (bear).
