### 2C Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

Capex under execution: ~Rs 100 Cr (IPO capex objects, per Aug 2026 call) = ~Rs 70 Cr Nimji + ~Rs 30 Cr B33
(both largely committed/executed as of Q1 FY27).

Historical fixed-asset turnover (standalone, FY26): Total Income from Operations Rs 354.20 Cr (Rs
3,542.03 Mn, Investor Pres. p.31) against gross block (Land+Buildings+Plant&Equipment+Furniture+Vehicles+
Computers+Office equipment, "Total" column, AR2026 fixed-asset note, standalone) = Rs 1,098.24 Mn (as at
Mar-26, per note extract, AR2026 p.13069) = Rs 109.82 Cr.

Fixed-asset turnover = 354.20 / 109.82 = ~3.23x (🔍 analyst calculation from 📄 disclosed figures).

Implied incremental revenue from Rs 100 Cr of new capex = 100 x 3.23 = ~Rs 323 Cr.
As % of FY26 standalone revenue (Rs 354.20 Cr): ~91% uplift potential, on a like-for-like historical
turnover multiple, IF the new capacity runs at the same asset efficiency as the existing base once fully
utilised — a mechanical extrapolation, not a management guidance figure. Cross-check against management's
own long-run marker: "long-term inspiration remains to build a INR650 crores plus revenue platform"
(consolidated, May 2026 call, line ~139) against FY26 consolidated revenue of Rs 406.63 Cr (Rs 4,066.28
Mn, Investor Pres. p.35) implies ~60% growth over an unstated multi-year horizon ("next 24 to 36 months"
for capacity stabilisation, same call). The mechanical fixed-asset-turnover extrapolation (91%) and
management's own stated aspiration (60% over 2-3 years) are in the same directional ballpark but not
identical; both are shown, arithmetic disclosed, no single number is presented as fact.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct: 91** (mechanical fixed-asset-turnover extrapolation, 🔍 analyst inference;
management's own "Rs 650 Cr+" aspiration implies ~60% consolidated growth over 24-36 months as a
cross-check, 🎙️).
