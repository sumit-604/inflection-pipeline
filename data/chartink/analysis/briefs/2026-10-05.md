## The picture in one paragraph

Monday was a quiet bounce inside a damaged market. The count of stocks above
the two-hundred-day line rose from 899 to 922. The fifty-day count rose from 619
to 641, still well under its washout level of 702. New yearly lows fell from
214 to 90, but they still beat new highs for the seventh session running.
Rising and falling stocks came out about even. Volume ran level with a year

carried little force. The index rose more than the average stock. 31 of 35
indices closed up, led by FMCG, which had been the weakest index. Healthcare

small end still sits above its long trend. The large end still sits well under
its own. The down tail kept growing under the surface.

## What would change this read

The read tonight stays cautious, with a small repair. The cautious read fails
if the fifty-day count climbs back above about 702 and holds. It fails if the
two-hundred-day count climbs back above about 970, the shelf that broke last
week. It fails if new yearly highs outnumber new lows for several sessions,
against tonight's 43 and 90. It also fails if a thrust day arrives, 300 or more
stocks jumping four and a half percent in one session, on volume above its
year-ago level. In May and June the long count recovered from this zone within
two weeks. Monday's light bounce does not yet put this dip in that pattern.

The cautious read turns worse if the twenty-day count closes under about 475,
its washout low. Monday moved it about twenty names further away. It turns
worse if the two-hundred-day count falls under about 880, the May low. It turns
worse if a second washout arrives, 300 or more stocks falling four and a half
percent in one session. It also turns worse if the share of stocks down ten
percent in five days keeps rising past about one in 15 while the VIX climbs
above about 15. The down tail is the place the next step would show first.
