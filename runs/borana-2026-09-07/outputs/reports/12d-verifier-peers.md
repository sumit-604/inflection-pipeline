## PART 2: FINDINGS

### F1 — MAJOR — Misattributed CRISIL-analyst quote (FILATEX Q1FY27 / Aug-2026)
**Location:** B06 report Part 1, Q3 section, "Peer evidence" bullet 3; B06 Part 3 coverage map,
FILATEX Q1 FY27 row; B06 YAML `peer_coverage_map` FILATEX/"Q1 FY27" entry.
**Claimed:** "A CRISIL analyst on FILATEX's Aug-2026 call frames it as settled fact: 'since the
revocation of QCO in November have we seen any impact on our business for Q4?'
(FILATEX-Concall_Aug_2026, p.9-10)."
**Actual:** This exact quote, from "Saloni Munshi from CRISIL," appears verbatim in
FILATEX-Concall_May_2026 (the Q4 & FY26 results call, 4-May-2026), extraction page 10
(printed "Page 9 of 13") — not in FILATEX-Concall_Aug_2026 at all. The Aug-2026 (Q1FY27,
31-Jul-2026) transcript has no CRISIL speaker and no such question.
**Effect:** The substance is not fabricated — a CRISIL analyst really does treat the November
QCO-revocation date as settled fact, so B06's underlying point (three sources agree on
November) is directionally sound. But the citation is wrong on both the source file and the
call quarter, which matters because this exact quote is offered as the third, independent
corroboration behind the Q3 CONTRADICTED verdict. Strip out the misattributed anchor and the
Q3 verdict still stands on two correctly-anchored peers (FILATEX Feb-2026, SANATHAN Feb-2026),
so the verdict itself survives — but the coverage map's claimed contribution for the FILATEX
Q1FY27 transcript is not actually in that transcript.

### F2 — MAJOR — Unused, directly relevant segment-margin disclosure (SANGAMIND Q1FY25 / Aug-2024)
**Location:** B06 Part 1, Q1 section (the central UNVERIFIABLE verdict); B06 `input_gaps`
("No peer discloses a fabric-only or per-loom EBITDA margin (not even SANGAM...)").
**What the transcript actually contains** (SANGAMIND-Concall_Aug_2024_Transcript, extraction
p.16-17, printed "Page 15-16 of 18"):
> Aditya: "roughly synthetic yarn in have a margin 8% to 10% or below that... what is the margin
> difference between fabric synthetic yarn and garment?"
> Anurag Soni: "if we take out the average of the last decade, then yarn margins have always
> been around... above 10%... yarn margins can be as high as anywhere between 15%, 20% and as
> low as anywhere between 3%, 5%... long term average we can say that 10% margin always
> remains."
> Aditya: "Fabric and garments?"
> Anurag Soni: "Garmenting margin if the capacity utilization is complete then it can go up till
> 20%, maybe 15% to 20% range is also there in the garmenting. Fabric side if I don't count my
> yarn margin so I can add anywhere between so yarn margin can be added 4% to 5% for the value
> addition on the fabric front."
**Effect:** This is a genuine, quantified, management-sourced statement of the fabric
value-addition margin uplift (roughly +4-5 points over the yarn base) and the garmenting margin
ceiling (15-20% at full utilisation) — directly on the topic B06 says no peer, including SANGAM,
ever quantifies. It does not settle Q1 (SANGAM's fabric is denim/PV woven, much of it dyed and
processed downstream of Borana's unprocessed greige output, and "4-5 points added" is a delta,
not an absolute fabric EBITDA%), so UNVERIFIABLE likely remains the right general verdict — but
the report should have surfaced this figure and explained why it still falls short of settling
Q1, rather than stating flatly, in four separate places (Part 1 Q1, `input_gaps`, the
`unverifiable` YAML block, and the `analyst_note`), that no such disclosure exists anywhere in
the corpus. This is the single most consequential miss the task asked me to check for.

### F3 — MAJOR — False claim that SANGAM is silent on BIS/QCO (SANGAMIND Q4FY26 / Apr-2026)
**Location:** B06 Part 1, Q4 section, "Peers silent" line: "SANGAMIND does not discuss
anti-dumping duty, QCO or BIS in any of its four transcripts (see Part 3)"; B06 Part 3 coverage
map and YAML `peer_coverage_map`, SANGAMIND Q4 FY26 row: "silent on Borana/Surat and on BIS/QCO
by name despite falling inside the event window."
**Actual:** SANGAMIND-Concall_Apr_2026_Transcript, extraction p.12 (printed "Page 11 of 18"):
> Madhur Rathi: "I wanted to understand how has the spreads for polyester fiber changed post the
> quality control order?"
> ... [MD answers a different part of the question] ...
> Madhur Rathi: "I was asking more on the front of the quality control order removal on the –
> our raw materials for manufacturing this yarn. How has the spreads improved post that post the
> quality control order removal? Have we been able to get better pricing for our raw materials
> versus what we were sourcing from Indian IOCL or Reliance?"
> Anurag Soni: "See, frankly, we hardly source anything from Reliance. So probably 5% of our
> polyester fiber actually comes from Reliance. So, it has kind of not made any kind of effect
> on our raw material costs."
**Effect:** SANGAM is not silent on QCO in this transcript — an analyst names "the quality
control order" twice and management answers, on the record, that the removal made no material
difference to SANGAM's raw-material costs. That answer is itself a usable data point (a
downstream buyer reporting the QCO removal as a non-event for its own cost base, which sits in
tension with the "cheap Chinese POY" mechanism narrative) that B06 never surfaces. The Borana/
Surat-silence half of the same sentence is accurate (confirmed by direct read and by grep across
the full peer corpus); only the BIS/QCO-silence claim is false.

### F4 — MINOR — Presentation coverage-claim overstatement
**Location:** B06 report, opening paragraph: "Twelve peer transcripts... and six peer investor
presentations... read in full."
**Actual:** Only 3 of the 6 presentations are ever cited anywhere in the report
(FILATEX-Investor_Presentation_Q1FY27, SANGAMIND-Investor_Presentation_Q1FY27,
SANGAMIND-Investor_Presentation_Q4FY26). The two SANATHAN presentations
(Q1FY27, Q4FY26) and the FILATEX Q4FY26 presentation are never drawn on anywhere in Parts 1-5.
Having read both SANATHAN presentations directly: they contain standard result-deck content
(revenue/EBITDA/PAT trend tables, capacity tables, business overview) that duplicates
information already sourced from the SANATHAN call transcripts, and SANATHAN has no fabric or
weaving segment at all, so nothing analytically relevant was lost by not citing them. This is a
coverage-honesty gap (claimed "read in full" for six, evidenced for three), not a missed
finding.

### F5 — MINOR — Quote attributed to the wrong call quarter (SANGAMIND "close to 10%")

8 of 14") — not during the Q4FY26 (Apr-2026) call as the citation implies. The page number (p.9)
is correct; the source-call attribution is wrong. Content and page survive; only the "which call
said this" label is off.

### F6 — MINOR — Malformed presentation page anchor

**Actual:** "p.slide on trade" is not a page number. The cited claim (India-EU FTA driving

### F7 — MINOR — Verdict discipline: Q5 CONTRADICTED rests on one peer with an acknowledged
machine-class mismatch
**Location:** B06 Part 1, Q5 section; YAML `contradicted` block.
SANATHAN's Aug-2026 asset-turn figure (0.8-0.85x, for cotton-yarn spindles) is the only
quantified peer capex-efficiency benchmark in the corpus, and it is a single-peer,
cross-machine-class comparison against Borana's claimed 2x-3x for water-jet looms/texturisers.
B06 states the caveat prominently and transparently every time this figure is invoked
(including in the `analyst_note`, which calls it "suggestive rather than dispositive"), so this
is not a hidden weakness — but the bare YAML verdict tag is an unqualified "CONTRADICTED" rather
than a hedged label (e.g., PARTIALLY VERIFIED / CONTRADICTED-WITH-CAVEAT), which a reader
scanning only the structured block, not the prose, could take as more dispositive than the
underlying single-peer, cross-machine-class evidence supports.

## PART 4: OVERALL ASSESSMENT

The stage genuinely read all twelve transcripts; every SUBSTANTIVE claim in the coverage map
traces to real content in the named transcript, and the report's central triangulation
(verified/contradicted/unverifiable split) is well-reasoned and mostly well-anchored. The
report's own analyst_note and caveats show real interpretive care (e.g., flagging the
machine-class mismatch on Q5 unprompted). Against that: two of the twelve coverage-map entries
carry a materially wrong claim inside their stated contribution (F1, F3), and the single most
important verdict in the report — Q1's UNVERIFIABLE call on the 23-25% margin claim — rests on
an absence claim ("no peer discloses...") that a careful re-read of the very call the stage
already marked SUBSTANTIVE for other reasons shows to be not quite true (F2). None of these
findings flips a verdict outright, but F2 in particular means the report's plausibility argument
against Borana's margin claim is less airtight than presented: SANGAM's peer ceiling isn't just
"blended margin never exceeds ~13%" (true and cited), it also includes a management-quantified
fabric/garment margin delta the report never engages with.
