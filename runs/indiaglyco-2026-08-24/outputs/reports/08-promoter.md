### 2C. Tax and revenue
No SFIO, DRI, benami, or search-and-seizure action against the promoters
personally was found. At the **company** level, the FY26 Business
Responsibility Report discloses several long-running excise/customs
disputes (AR FY26 L6890-6972):
- Central Excise classification dispute on RAB, FY2005-06 to 2008-09:
  original demand Rs 41.84 crore, confirmed at Rs 10.39 crore + penalty
  by Commissioner (2019), set aside in full by CESTAT (20 Aug 2024); now
  under department appeal at Uttarakhand High Court (AR FY26 L6917-6928).
- Transitional ITC dispute FY2017-18: demand of Rs 1.92 crore + penalty,
  fully quashed on appeal to Commissioner (Appeals), Noida (AR FY26
  L6929-6941).
- Customs classification dispute (Ennature Bio-Pharma imports,
  2020-2024): duty demand Rs 32.95 lakh confirmed, company had already
  deposited it under protest; appeal filed Feb 2026 (AR FY26 L6942-6955).
- **Largest matter**: differential customs duty on denatured ethyl
  alcohol imports (Jul 2017-Feb 2021). Original order (22 Mar 2024)
  confirmed Rs 33.43 crore duty + Rs 82 crore penalty + Rs 191.76 crore
  redemption fine (aggregate Rs 307.19 crore). On appeal (27 Feb 2026),
  the penalty was reduced to Rs 41 crore and the Rs 191.76 crore
  redemption fine was **set aside**; duty demand of Rs 33.43 crore
  upheld; company is appealing the balance to CESTAT Allahabad (AR FY26
  L6956-6972). This is a large, real, ongoing company-level tax/customs
  exposure — not a promoter-personal issue, but material enough that
  synthesis should weigh it in the risk register.
- Numerous smaller, older disputed statutory dues across customs, excise,
  and GST forums are itemised at AR FY26 L8524-8582; standard for a
  chemicals/ethanol manufacturer of this vintage and scale, no single
  item individually material.

CARO 2020 clause (viii): "the Company has not surrendered or disclosed
