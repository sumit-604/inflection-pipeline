## Contradicted claims

The peer stage contradicted no claim. Of six peer questions, four were partially verified by one peer and two were unverifiable. The record holds these contradictions from other stages and the verifiers. Each is a priority monitoring item.

1. Raw material imports. CARE 06-Mar-2026 p.2 says the company "has started importing some part of its raw material requirement", and the MD&A at AR p.11 says the import opportunity "is being utilized". The FY26 foreign exchange outgo at AR p.19 shows no raw material line. AR Note 43 at p.77 says major raw material is sourced domestically, with no foreign currency exposure. The FY26 cost relief therefore cannot rest on imported bone.
2. Capex timing. CARE's April 2025 release, at p.2, put ₹33.30 Cr over FY25 to FY26 including solar, with an ₹18 Cr loan. CARE 06-Mar-2026 p.2 puts ₹33.00 Cr excluding solar, a ₹25 Cr loan, and production from January 2027. AR Note 4 at p.61 says there is no cost overrun or time delay. The company has made no statement on the change.
3. Direction of the materials line. The MD&A at AR PDF p.12 says material consumed "increased by 1.29%". Note 28 at PDF p.68 shows a 1.29% fall.
4. Customer concentration. AR Note 43 at p.76 calls it low. CARE p.2 puts the top ten customers at about 42% of FY25 sales.
5. Gelatine revenue. Volume +16.17% and price -5.20% imply revenue +10.1%. Note 26 shows +8.44%, a gap of about 240 ₹ lakh with no explanation (AR p.12, p.68).
6. Reported figures. EBITDA prints as 40.88 ₹ Cr against 42.08 ₹ Cr computed. MD pay prints as +10.41% in Annexure IV against +49.7% in Note 37. Note 48 prints inventory at 72 days, which ties on no basis tested (AR PDF pp.5, 12, 20, 73, 79).

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Read the September 2026 quarter results, filed 12-Nov-2026 on BSE. Operating margin before other income should hold at 18% or more (June 2026 quarter: 20.79%). Below 16.4% means the reset is reversing. This tests whether the reset persists.
2. In the same results, compute materials plus inventory change as a share of revenue. It should stay at or under 52% (June 2026 quarter: 50.6%; FY25: 58.1%). Compare IGCL's quarterly ratio. If both rise together, the gain was the bone cycle. This tests structure against cycle.
3. Track power and fuel as a share of revenue each quarter. It should stay at or under 11% (FY26: 10.8%; June 2026 quarter: 11.9%). Above 12.7% with coal flat means the solar and plant saving is fading. This tests the one company specific cost lever.
4. Read the half year cash flow and balance sheet filed with the September 2026 quarter. CFO to PAT at or above 90% confirms the cash read; below 80% falsifies it. Receivables over six months should stay below ₹30 lakh. This tests the cash flag.
5. Watch the capex in the same half year balance sheet and in BSE Reg 30 filings. CWIP (528.97 ₹ lakh at March 2026) and the term loan (728.65 ₹ lakh) should rise toward the ₹25 Cr plan. A commissioning notice is due by January 2027. No notice by March 2027, or a cost above ₹33 Cr that takes gearing above 0.5x, kills the trigger. This tests the volume leg.
6. Read the half year related party filing under Reg 23 and the FY27 AOC-2. Pioneer Jellice purchases should stay at or under 13% of purchases. Flag any new NGL and IGCL transaction, loan or scheme. This tests the promoter tripwires.
7. Check the FY27 annual report foreign exchange outgo (Annexure III) for a raw material import line. A line confirms the import claim; none extends the contradiction. This tests the claimed source of cheaper bone.
8. Track selling prices. DCP revenue share should hold near 29% with price steady, and gelatine price and volume should move as in Natural Capsules calls and India HS 3503 import data. If DCP price falls back while gelatine is already down 5.20%, net price turns negative. This tests the price side of the margin.
