## PART 3: FINDINGS

### F1 — MAJOR — SYRMA PCB-plant capex overstated as "total across all phases" (Claim 5, coverage map)
Location: 06-peers.md, Claim 5 peer evidence bullet 2, and PART 3 coverage-map row "SYRMA... new PCB
plant capex Rs400cr (Claim 5)."
Claimed: "a complete new greenfield multi-layer PCB manufacturing PLANT (buildings, equipment, full
facility) costs ~Rs400cr total across all phases (Rs130cr funded so far)."
Source truth: SYRMA CFO Bijay Agrawal, Aug 2026 call: "the first initial phase, we are incurring
around Rs. 400 crores, of which is about Rs. 130 crores already funded so far... when we grow
further, like the next year, when we try to fund another Rs. 400 crore, a majority of that can also
come through the government incentives." Phase 1 alone is ~Rs400cr; a second phase of comparable
size is explicitly flagged as coming, and a third phase exists beyond that (unquantified). Total
project cost across all phases is therefore materially higher than Rs400cr, likely Rs800cr+.
Why it matters: Claim 5 is described in B06's own Part 4 as "the single clearest contradiction in the
peer set" and "the most consequential contradiction" in the whole report — its persuasive force rests
on peer capex intensity being an order of magnitude below VINYAS's implied per-line figure. The
SYRMA data point, as actually stated in the transcript, is roughly 2x higher than what B06 reports,
which narrows (without eliminating) the gap B06 is arguing for that one peer. The AVALON and KAYNES
legs of Claim 5 are independently confirmed and unaffected, so the CONTRADICTED verdict most likely
still stands, but the SYRMA magnitude cited is wrong and should be corrected before this goes forward.

### F2 — MAJOR — CYIENTDLM "$0.5bn order pipeline confirmed" (Claim 4, coverage map) is not supported
Location: 06-peers.md PART 3 coverage-map rows "CYIENTDLM (Jul 2026)... $0.5bn order pipeline (Claim
4)" and "CYIENTDLM (Apr 2026)... $0.5bn order pipeline confirmed (Claim 4)."
Claimed: CYIENTDLM's two transcripts are marked SUBSTANTIVE contributors to Claim 4, with a "$0.5bn
order pipeline" figure "confirmed."
Source truth: (a) Claim 4's actual Part 1 write-up in 06-peers.md never mentions CYIENTDLM at all —
its peer-evidence bullets cite only ASTRAMICRO, KAYNES, DATAPATTNS and CENTUM. The coverage-map
citation therefore has no findable home in Parts 1-2, which is the rubric's own test for a
SUBSTANTIVE-without-real-citation finding. (b) Reading the Jul 2026 transcript directly: the "$0.5
billion" figure was proposed BY AN ANALYST (Balasubramanian), estimating the order pipeline at
"INR4,000 crores to INR5,000 crores." Management's actual response was: "No, I think we have more
than that order pipeline, okay?... we have a substantially higher pipeline at this point of time...
Very, very high sales pipeline." Management explicitly disputed the $0.5bn figure as too LOW; it was
never confirmed by management, in this call or (unverified by this audit) the Apr 2026 call.
Why it matters: this is a citation that does not exist where claimed, on a fact the coverage map
represents as management-confirmed when the transcript shows the opposite (management said the real
number is higher, unquantified). Per rubric rule 2, SUBSTANTIVE without a real, findable citation is
MAJOR. It does not change Claim 4's verdict (already PARTIALLY VERIFIED/UNVERIFIABLE, and CYIENTDLM
was never actually used for it), but it is a genuine defect in the coverage map's honesty about what
was and was not used, and should be corrected.

### F3 — MAJOR — ASTRAMICRO's own order-book guidance mischaracterized as an independent "sector
pipeline" (Claim 4)
Location: 06-peers.md Claim 4 peer-evidence bullet 1, and the PART 4 "Overall" paragraph line:
"Astra Microwave's independently cited Rs8,000-10,000cr sector pipeline gives some plausibility to
VINYAS's number."
Claimed: ASTRAMICRO "discusses a sector-level pipeline of 'INR8,000-10,000 crores over next 5-6
years' tied to specific HAL/DRDO radar and EW programs."
Source truth: the quoted figures ("INR8,000 crores to INR10,000 crores worth of orders coming in
over the next 5 years"; "at least INR8,000 crores to INR9,000 crores to be executed over the next 5
to 6 years") are Astra's own internal order-intake guidance for Astra's own named, specific programs
(QRSAM, Su-30/Virupaksha/Angad, the Astra Rafael Comsys JV) — explicitly built up program-by-program
in the call ("INR700-800cr QRSAM... INR500-750cr Astra Rafael Comsys... INR3,000cr Su-30/Virupaksha/
Angad... add it up, is INR8,000 crores to INR9,000 crores"). This is a single company's own
order-book buildup projection, structurally identical to the KAYNES figure that B06 itself correctly
discounts one paragraph later in the same claim: "Kaynes: overall order book >Rs8,900-9,000cr...
this is Kaynes's own diversified order book... not a shared sector-pipeline estimate; not a clean
comparator." B06 applies the discount to KAYNES but not to the numerically similar ASTRAMICRO figure,
then uses the ASTRAMICRO number as its strongest evidence that VINYAS's claimed pipeline "sits in a
real order-of-magnitude range."
Why it matters: this is not a fabricated quote (the numbers are real and correctly transcribed) but a
mischaracterization of what kind of evidence it is. An independent sector-wide market-sizing estimate
and one company's own order-intake target are different evidentiary classes; treating the latter as
the former overstates Claim 4's plausibility finding and understates how genuinely uncorroborated
VINYAS's Rs9,000-10,000cr figure is — no peer in the sampled set actually offers an independent,
non-self-referential sector sizing.

### F4 — MINOR — DATAPATTNS single-vendor GeM order value conflates two separate exchanges (Claim 7)
Location: 06-peers.md Claim 7 peer-evidence bullet 1: "cites a specific single-vendor GeM-portal
negotiated order (~Rs1,700cr)."
Source truth: the transcript contains two adjacent but distinct exchanges — Hardik Rawat's question
about "order book orders negotiated but not yet part for order book... INR17-odd billion" (=
Rs1,700cr, a total negotiated-but-unbooked figure across multiple contracts), and a separate later
comment from S. Rangarajan referencing "GeM portal release that we are L1 in one large contract...
which is a single vendor contract," with no rupee value stated for that specific GeM contract. The
transcript never states these are the same order. B06 presents the Rs1,700cr figure as if it
specifically sizes the single-vendor GeM order; that link is a plausible but unstated inference.

### F5 — MINOR — Claim 6's VERIFIED verdict quietly redefines the question it verifies
Location: 06-peers.md Claim 6 heading and verdict line.
The injected question (B05 4B item 6) asks whether peer defence-EMS players disclose customer
concentration. B06 marks this "VERIFIED (that such disclosure occurs in this peer group), with a
coverage caveat" — a defensible read given only 2 of the 5 named peers (SYRMA, AVALON) actually
supplied a number in the transcripts sampled, with the other 3 simply not asked. The verdict itself
is honestly caveated in the prose, and rests on 2 independent, confirmed anchors (satisfies the
rubric's >=2-anchor rule for VERIFIED), so this is not a verdict-discipline failure — but the
practice of upgrading "2 of 5 peers answered" to a clean VERIFIED headline, rather than PARTIALLY
VERIFIED, is worth naming as an imprecision in how confidently the headline reads relative to the
actual hit rate.

## PART 5: SUMMARY

B06 is, on the sampled evidence, a genuinely substantive and largely accurate peer-verification
exercise: the great majority of spot-checked quotes (AVALON capex/margins, SYRMA customer
concentration/export/margin, CYIENTDLM margin/DSO/working-capital, CENTUM export/margin, DATAPATTNS
export/margin) are verbatim, correctly attributed, and fairly interpreted, including the report's
strongest single piece of analysis (the CYIENTDLM DSO-vs-NWC decomposition in Claim 3, which is
precisely anchored and directly supports the report's own cross-peer hypothesis in Part 5). No
finding here rises to CRITICAL: no fabricated quote, no verdict upgraded from silence, no VERIFIED
claim resting on one peer.

But three MAJOR-grade issues sit inside the two most load-bearing claims in the report — Claim 4
(pipeline size) and Claim 5 (capex, "the single clearest contradiction"). Claim 5's SYRMA capex
figure is materially understated (F1). Claim 4's coverage map claims a CYIENTDLM citation that does
not exist in the actual analysis and, read directly, shows management disputing rather than
confirming the number attributed to it (F2). Claim 4's strongest supporting evidence, ASTRAMICRO's
figure, is a company's own order-intake target mischaracterized as independent sector sizing, while
the same report correctly declines to give KAYNES's numerically similar figure that same credit (F3)
— an inconsistent evidentiary standard applied within a single claim. These should be corrected
before Claim 4 and Claim 5 are relied on at face value downstream; the underlying verdict directions
(Claim 4 PARTIALLY VERIFIED/UNVERIFIABLE, Claim 5 CONTRADICTED) most likely survive the corrections,
but the stated magnitude and independence of the supporting evidence do not currently hold up on a
direct read of the source transcripts.
