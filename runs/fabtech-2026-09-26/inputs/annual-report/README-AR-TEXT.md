# FY26 Annual Report text layer (read this before citing the AR)

- Annual_Report_2026.pdf is the BSE Reg 34 filing (31-Jul-2026), 224 pages,
  image scan with no text layer.
- Annual_Report_2026.txt is OCR text (Windows.Media.Ocr, 200 dpi, rows
  rebuilt by word position) of the company-site copy of the same report:
  https://fabtechnologies.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Annual-Report-2025-26_Final_New_6_Comp.pdf
  (223 pages, 523 MB, not committed).
- PAGE ALIGNMENT: txt [page N] = site copy page N = BSE filing page N+1.
  Cite AR pages as "AR p.N (txt)". A reader opening the BSE PDF adds 1.
- OCR limits: digits can be misread and commas dropped (for example
  "20A33.51"). Any load-bearing figure is confirmed against a second source
  (the FY26 audited results filing in results/, a note total, or the page
  image) and the check is stated.
- Section index (txt pages, approximate): MD&A 43-58; Corporate governance
  49-78; AOC-2 30; Secretarial audit 40-42; Standalone auditor report 86-88
  (CARO 88); Standalone statements 98-100, notes from ~101; Consolidated
  auditor report 140-148; Consolidated balance sheet 150, P&L 151, cash
  flow 152-153, notes from ~154; related party notes 134-135 (standalone),
  192-194 (consolidated); segment notes 124-126, 179-184.
- KNOWN ITEM FOR STAGES 2 AND 3: the consolidated balance sheet face (txt
  p.150, line "Trade receivables 13 24,151.90 15,073.61") and the Note 13
  total (txt "Total 20,433.51 15,073.61") and the MD&A (txt p.44, Rs 20,433.51
  lakh) disagree for FY26. The FY26 audited results filing shows 20,433.51.
  Two independent OCR engines read 24,151.90 on the balance sheet face.
  Settle from the page image and explain; do not treat it as an OCR error
  without checking.
