2C CAPEX CYCLE. This is a capacity race in cables and conductors, with an under-used build-ahead in extrusion. Two facts bear on that: APARINDS targets high-spec, not building wire, and MMP's LT cable pilot is 100 MT a month (B05).

## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY

- Claims verified: 0 of 8. Partially verified: 5 (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q8). Unverifiable: 2 (Q2, Q4). Contradicted: 1 (Q6, scoped to West Asia, freight and price resistance).
- Claims contradicted (priority for synthesis): the export growth and demand claim. MMP guides exports +40-50% while MAANALU (Q4 FY26 p.7; Q1 FY27 p.6) and APARINDS (Q4 FY26 p.13) report halted Gulf shipments and freight up 5-10x.
- Most consequential contradiction, in 3 lines: exports are the largest single lever in MMP's FY27 powder story (+40-50%, B05 trigger 1). Two peers say the Gulf route is stalled and discretionary export demand was lost in 2026 (MAANALU Q3 FY26 p.7, p.11). Peers sell extrusions and conductors, not powder, so the contradiction is on route and price conditions, not on powder demand itself.
- Strongest independent confirmation, in 3 lines: APARINDS (Q1 FY27 p.3, p.14) shows a metal price spike deferred conventional conductor deliveries in the exact bucket MMP sells (AL-59 and conventional), and that deferred orders resume once metal eases. This backs MMP's metal-price explanation in direction, and it points to a recovery test in Q2 FY27.
- Overall: the peer set complicates the MMP narrative. It supports the direction of the metal-price explanation and of slow approvals. It does not support the size of the conductor fall (peer domestic conductor revenue grew 19.3%), the payment-delay cause (no peer cites it), or exports +40-50% (peers report stalled routes). The benchmarks it adds are unfavourable on working capital (MMP 102.5 days against 45-60 days) and funding (peers use equity). On the favourable side, peers show the same new-vertical delays (MAANALU Tata, Dewas), so MMP's slippage is a sector pattern, not only a company one. Powder and foil, where MMP's transition thesis sits, have no peer evidence at all.
