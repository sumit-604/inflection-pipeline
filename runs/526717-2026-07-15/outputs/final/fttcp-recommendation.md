## ACTIVE FLAG BLOCKS

### FLAG-PROMOTER (in the verdict line)

⚠️ PROMOTER FLAG: CONCERN. Top findings: (1) related party COGS 62.6% standalone / 70.4% consolidated involving Plastene India Ltd, an unlisted dominant counterparty and 4.68% promoter group shareholder, with a new Rs 3,320.29 lakh 0%-interest loan to the subsidiary and cross guarantees above 2x consolidated net worth (B08.top_findings); (2) Secretarial-Auditor-confirmed Regulation 23(9)/30 non-compliance FY25, three Company Secretary changes in ~14 months with the Jan-2026 resignation citing unresolved "matters requiring internal clarification", and recurring special resolutions to pay an Independent Director above LODR limits (B08.top_findings, verdict_basis).
Transition evidence: FII 0.07% to 4.50% and DII 4.80% (Jun to Sep 2025); 0% pledge across 5-plus consecutive quarters through Mar-2026; Jan-2026 CS vacancy filled in ~1 month by a credentialed outside hire; Secretarial Auditor upgraded to a full 5-year term; MD forwent his own remuneration; the 2022 raise was a pro-rata rights issue, not a preferential promoter allotment (B08.transition_evidence). Transition evidence sits in ownership and process, not in the related party structure itself.

### FLAG-CASH

⚠️ CASH CONVERSION FLAG: operating cash flow negative at both entities in the FY25 turnaround year (standalone -Rs 5.81 cr, consolidated -Rs 18.53 cr); cumulative six-year CFO/PAT -0.888x; FY26 consolidated CFO turned positive (+Rs 16.25 cr) but standalone worsened (-Rs 7.59 cr).
Determination: **INDETERMINATE** (fttcp-deliberation.md p.2, authoritative). Capex is near zero (0.55x depreciation), which argues the drain is working capital not growth investment; the receivables build is dominated by related party balances excluded from bank drawing power; FY26 consolidated turned positive while standalone did not, so neither a clean structural leak nor a confirmed growth-induced drag is established.
Evidence: Infomerics rating rationale verbatim (RATING.txt p.5, 2 Mar 2026): "The company's operations are working capital intensive, as reflected in an elongated operating cycle of 96 days in FY25 (88 days in FY24), driven mainly by higher receivable days of 61 days and inventory holding of 39 days. Consequently, the company continues to rely on bank working capital borrowings, which increased to ₹98.68 crore as on March 31, 2025, thereby increasing sensitivity to timely collections and inventory management." Capex commissioning: +6,000 MTPA live from 1 Apr 2025 (total 24,300 MTPA), no major capex guided FY27-28 (RATING.txt p.5). Receivables: single-name concentration above 80% of standalone receivables across three counterparties, two related; subsidiary bank drawing-power reconciliation gap Rs 1,874.61 lakh at Mar-2025 (B02, CARO Annexure A p.68-69).
Consequence: INDETERMINATE never silently resolves to PROCEED. It caps the disposition below a clean pass and holds the cash multiplier at 0.80x with the growth offset barred. Because it is INDETERMINATE, no single falsifying quarterly metric is attached as a PROCEED condition; the resolution test is the FY27 standalone operating cash print (see falsification line).

## CONTRADICTED CLAIMS (peer stage, priority monitoring)

- HCP's implied "31% FY25 utilisation is an industry-typical ramp" does not hold. Emmbi ran up to 87% (Q1 FY22 call, 12 Aug 2021, p.3) and Kanpur ran 75% to 83-85% across FY26 (Kanpur Q1 FY26 p.15, Q4/FY26 p.5/17). Watch for the FY26 utilisation disclosure; a figure still near 31% confirms company-specific execution lag, not a sector ramp curve (B06.contradicted).
- HCP's "no major capex FY27-28 reflects an industry-wide pause" does not hold. Kanpur announced Rs 105 cr of capex plus M&A over 12-18 months (Q2 FY26 call, 13 Nov 2025, p.4). HCP's capex free stance is a capital constraint choice, not a sector signal; watch whether it reflects prudence or an inability to fund growth without more debt (B06.contradicted).
