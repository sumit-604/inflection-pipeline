## PASS 3: PATTERN RE-READ

Re-reading the notes one final time for contradictions, mismatches, deliberate vagueness,
restatements, subsequent events, and going-concern language (rather than sequentially by note
number) surfaces one material pattern not separately itemized in Pass 1 or Pass 2, plus
confirmation of several items already flagged as internal contradictions:

**Meta-pattern — a first-listed-year cluster of profit- and optics-favourable choices.** Taken
together rather than individually, six items from Passes 1–2 all land in the same single year
(FY2020-21, the Company's first full year listed, 14 July 2020) and all point the same direction
— toward flattering reported profit or capital-markets optics in the year profit jumped >10x off a
low base: (i) first-ever borrowing-cost capitalisation (Note 22/1(c)); (ii) a fire-loss write-off
netted against Gross Block rather than run through P&L, with an unreconciled insurance receivable
booked larger than the loss (Note 1(b)/Note 7); (iii) executive pay re-linked to net profit via a
commission structure introduced for the first time, effective the day after year-end (Notice Item
5); (iv) the Company's first-ever dividend declared in the same year working capital deteriorated
sharply (Note 32.2); (v) a new CFO in the seat from the first quarter of the listed year signing
off on all of the above (Directors' Report p.14); and (vi) a special resolution seeking ~48%
additional borrowing headroom tabled at the same AGM. No single item here is disqualifying and each
was individually rated Watch in Passes 1-2, but the clustering in one year is itself a pattern an
analyst should weigh — it argues for treating this year's reported profit growth with more
scepticism than the raw ₹22.92 Cr PBT / ₹21.64 Cr PAT figures suggest at face value. 🟡 Watch (pattern-level, new in Pass 3).

**Contradiction confirmed**: Directors' Report (p.16) states "no material changes and commitments
affecting the financial position of the company... between the end of the Financial Year... and
the date of the report," yet the same Annual Report's Notice discloses both the post-year-end
executive remuneration revision (Item 5) and the ₹225 Cr borrowing-ceiling special resolution
(Item 7) — neither treated as a subsequent event in the financial statements' own disclosures.
Already surfaced individually in Passes 1–2; Pass 3 confirms this is a genuine, reproducible
internal inconsistency rather than an isolated omission.

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management indicators**: first-year borrowing-cost capitalisation in a high-capex,
  high-profit-growth year (Note 22); fire-loss write-off netted against Gross Block rather than
  expensed through P&L (Note 1(b)); static ECL provisioning despite a tripled receivables base
  (Note 5); executive pay newly linked to net profit effective the day after year-end (Notice Item
  5) — none individually disqualifying, but clustering in a single (listing) year raises the bar
  for scepticism on reported profit growth.
- **Aggressive accounting**: 0% Redeemable Preference Shares classified as debt (Borrowings, Note
  11.1(b)) despite company-optional redemption and no cash coupon — inflates leverage optics
  (Net Gearing 1.09x) relative to economic substance.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators**: undisclosed ICD counterparty (₹820 Lakhs, Note 11); undisclosed
  relationship of Nikhil Holdings Private Limited, a new >5% shareholder (Note 9.4); no
  quantified FX exposure or hedging despite a meaningful USD export book (Note 33.B); no covenant
  terms/breaches disclosed for secured term loans (Note 11).
- **Cash conversion / FLAG-CASH**: trade receivables +203.7% YoY vs revenue +43.0%; trade payables
  +305.9% YoY vs COGS +43.4% (Note 5; Balance Sheet/Note 14 area). This is the single largest and
  most concrete red flag in the notes and should cap this run's verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS at
  most per pipeline rules on cash-conversion findings, pending management clarification.
