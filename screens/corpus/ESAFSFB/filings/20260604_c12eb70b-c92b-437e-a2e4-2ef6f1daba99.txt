Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing

1. 
Appointment of Shri. G Narasimha Murthy as Chief Risk Officer, Senior Management

Senior Management Personnel, on July 31, 2026.

Management Personnel of the Bank, for a period of three years with effect from July 01, 
2026, upon completion of the employment contract of Shri. Sivakumar P as Head – 
Internal Audit and Senior Management Personnel, on June 30, 2026. 
 
The disclosures as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Circular no.  HO/ 49/ 14/ 14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/ I/ 3762/ 2026 dated January
