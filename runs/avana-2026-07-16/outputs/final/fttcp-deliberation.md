## OPERATOR OVERRIDES

**Override 1 Pillar 1 anchors on operating ROCE, not a normalized-down blend, and surplus cash is valued separately.**
- Draft determination: Pillar 1 ROCE set by the Amendment 4.5 blend at about 30.8% (Normalized anchor 30%, conservative), base PE about 22.9x, with the idle IPO cash left in the ROCE denominator.
- Operator ruling: anchor Pillar 1 on the operating ROCE the FTTCP analysis itself found (about 40%), giving a higher base multiple (about 26x via the ROCE above 33% formula), and value the surplus IPO cash separately at 1x rather than letting it drag the denominator.
- Operator's stated reasoning (operator's words): "It is a high-growth company, and you are giving it a price-to-earnings of less than 20. Have you gone mad?" and "In FTTCP analysis, you are telling that future ROCE can be near 40%, so you should take that. If it is a 40% ROCE company, your starting base multiple should be higher."

**Override 2 growth is priced in forward earnings, not stripped. Use the forward price to earnings method.**
- Draft determination: Pillar 3a growth visibility +0x (documented gates unmet), destination PE about 18x framed against a trailing 28x as a de-rating that sat below CMP.
- Operator ruling: giving zero growth premium AND valuing on trailing earnings strips the growth twice; apply the destination PE to the exit-year forward EPS (forward price to earnings). At sign-off: "use the forward price-to-earnings method for valuing."
- Operator's stated reasoning (operator's words): "You are not giving any score for the growth visibility. Either you take it to the forward price-to-earnings method. That may work if you are not giving a score in growth visibility. If you are not giving a score in growth visibility, then you need to take it to the forward price-to-earnings."

**Effect of the two overrides, carried to Phase 3 as authoritative:**
- Pillar 1: operating ROCE about 40%, base PE about 26x. Surplus cash about Rs 22 to 27 Cr valued separately at 1x. Recovery credited via Pillar 1; Strategic Premium ROCE route barred (single credit).
- Pillar 2: cash multiplier 0.80x, INDETERMINATE (the main swing; 1.0x only if the receivables ageing clears it, and INDETERMINATE never resolves to a clean pass on its own).
- Pillar 3: +0x, growth carried in the forward earnings ladder. Pillar 4 Strategic: +0x.
- Destination PE band about 21x (cash 0.80x) to about 25x (cash 1.0x, at the sector cap), mid about 23x, applied to exit-year forward EPS.
- Valuation on forward price to earnings: exit-year forward EPS times destination PE, plus surplus cash.
