The SEBI (LODR) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2024, effective from April 1, 2025, prescribe enhanced norms for the appointment of 
Secretarial Auditors, including:
•	
The Secretarial Auditor must be a peer-reviewed Practicing Company Secretary.
•	
He/she must be independent of the company, its promoters, and management.
•	
The appointment must be for a fixed term of five consecutive financial years, subject to cooling-off norms.
•	
The Board, based on the recommendation of the Audit Committee, shall approve such appointment and disclose it appropriately.
