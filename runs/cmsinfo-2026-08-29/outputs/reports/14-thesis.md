## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** The balance sheet is pristine. Net cash about Rs 442 Cr, zero borrowings, ICRA AA+ (Stable) / A1+ (B10). Gate 0 Block D scored 20/20 (B01). CFO ran above PAT for five straight years: 1.14 / 1.37 / 1.27 / 1.30 / 1.28 (B10). Post-tax ROCE was 25.4% and 25.2% in FY24 and FY25 before the FY26 step-down to 16.6% (B10). The business is profitable through the cycle. PAT margin 12.2%, EBITDA margin held in a narrow 24.0% to 28.1% band across FY22-FY26 (fttcp-draft Sec 2.2).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction).**
1. Margin recovery. Strongest evidence in the file. Q1 FY27 printed 27.2% EBITDA on services and guidance was raised to about 27% while revenue was cut. Backed by a printed quarter (web-dossier Sec 4). Confirmed by a second print at or above 26.5%; killed by a slide back toward 24-25%.
2. Signed bank contracts. SBI Rs 1,000 Cr / 10yr (live Jan-2026, 4,000-5,000 ATMs), HDFC Rs 400 Cr / 5yr (6,000 ATMs from FY27), FSS asset buy Rs 115 Cr (~8,000 units, closed Q1 FY27, ~4% of FY27 services revenue), ICICI ALGO on 10,000+ machines in FY27 (B10). Filed and signed, not guidance. Confirmed by services revenue printing low-teens growth; killed if contracts ramp but revenue stays flat.
3. ROCE recovery. Capex guided down from about Rs 350 Cr (FY26) to Rs 100-125 Cr (FY27), and Rs 8,126m of contracted lease rentals is arriving (+117.6%) (fttcp-draft Sec 3). Confirmed by FY28 ROCE crossing 22% on flat capital; this is the proof gate.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Contracted multi-year bank relationships with switching costs, national cash-in-transit network density, and a proprietary 16-year transaction dataset across 150,000+ touchpoints (B10). Post the AGS Transact collapse, banks tightened credit across every managed-service provider; a strong balance sheet became the qualification to compete, and CMS has one (web-dossier Sec 1). ATM Management grew 5.3% in FY26 while the pond's withdrawal value fell 7.2%, a twelve-point share-gain gap (web-dossier Sec 2 V1). Emerging moat understated: four of twelve moat tests scored zero on a collector bug, and fixing it could push EM above 25 and add +1x to Pillar 3 (B07 caveat).

**3D. Management Quality.** TRUSTWORTHY, no promoter, professional management and board, zero SEBI/criminal/tax/NCLT adverse findings (web-dossier Sec 4). CEO Rajiv Kaul raised his personal stake from about 2.6% to 6.43% through the PE exit and the price fall, with no disclosed selling (web-dossier Sec 4). Reliable on near-term margin calls it controls; the FY27 margin guide was raised and held.

**3E. Market Opportunity.** Revenue headroom 9.1x; runway STRONG (web-dossier Sec 1). The national ATM estate is growing 1.3% a year, not shrinking, so fixed monthly fees sit on a base that is stable to rising even as transaction-value falls (web-dossier Sec 2 V1, S1). Digital is separating from cash, not converging: the UPI-to-ATM ticket ratio fell to 0.233 from 0.247 (web-dossier Sec 2 V2).

**3F. Peer Positioning.** CMS trades cheap on enterprise value. EV/EBITDA 5.8x against Brink's 8.2x TTM and Radiant 8.1x; EV/EBIT 8.9x against Brink's 12.3x (B10). On quality CMS leads: post-tax ROCE 16.6% against Brink's ROIC 6.5% and Radiant 12.7%, EBITDA margin 24-27% against 19%, net cash against Brink's 2.7x net debt (B10). The Brink's / NCR Atleos deal (7.0x 2026E EBITDA with control, Feb-2026) values precisely the owned-and-operated ATM model CMS is building organically (web-dossier Sec 2 V5).

## SECTION 3-BEAR: THE BEAR CASE (Why Not, symmetric)

**3A-B. Quality is deteriorating under a clean headline.** FY26 free cash flow was NEGATIVE Rs 19 Cr as capex nearly tripled (B10). Post-tax ROCE collapsed 8.6 points in one year (25.2% to 16.6%) (B10). Underneath the strong CFO/PAT, the consolidated 1-2 year overdue receivables bucket grew 752% (Rs 177.94m to Rs 1,516.32m) in a book that grew 5.1%, and the standalone loss allowance was released, not built (B10 / web-dossier Sec 3 S3). One aged block is going bad at once. ROE is only about 14.1% (B10).

**3B-bear. Triggers are unconfirmed and coupled.** The margin print is one quarter. The contracts ramp against a withdrawal-value pond falling about 7% a year, so all the growth is share gain, not a rising tide (fttcp-draft Sec 2.1). The FY27 services guide low end needs +8% on the highest quarter CMS has ever printed (role1-handover Sec 6 constraint 5). SHARED CATALYST: the FY28 ROCE recovery and any future growth premium both ride the same capex-fill event, a single point of failure (fttcp-deliberation Sec 4).

**3C-bear. The moat is thin and partly single-customer.** No licence, no scarcity. CMS won the flagship SBI mandate as L1, the lowest bidder, after being the only qualified participant in a scrapped first round, and is waiting on an IBA committee for pricing relief. A company with pricing power does not need a committee (B10). ALGO's installed base is essentially one customer (the entire SBI network); the hardware-displacement wedge is unproven outside its home account (web-dossier Sec 2 V4). The transition was NECESSITY EXECUTED FROM STRENGTH: forced by a dying transaction model, so destination returns need not match origin returns (web-dossier Sec 1). The recovery is PARTIAL by construction; combined Cash Management plus Managed Services capital rose Rs 12,123m to Rs 16,141m, and restoring FY25 profit gives only 20.3%, not 25.2% (11-valuation Sec 2.3).

**3D-bear. Management misses revenue and withdraws disclosures.** Grade B-, downgraded from B. Three consecutive years of guiding, walking down within the year, and missing (web-dossier Sec 4). Three dropped commitments in twelve months: the 74-75k ATM target, DSO normalisation, and the Nov-2025 promise of an 80% independently-reportable network, reversed May-2026 as "not possible and feasible" (web-dossier Sec 4). Fragility HIGH on disclosure withdrawal.

**3E-bear. The pond leaks value.** ATM withdrawal value is falling three years running at an accelerating rate (FY24 -0.7%, FY25 -4.5%, FY26 -7.2%) (web-dossier Sec 2 V1). Fixed fees break the link on the new book, but the thesis rests on winning share fast enough to outrun a 7%-a-year value decline.

**3F-bear. The cheap EV read is partly a leverage artefact and partly earned.** Brink's low equity PE reflects its 2.7x net debt, which inflates its EV multiple and depresses its PE (11-valuation Sec 3.3). On the PE method CMS still screens at a 32% premium to the peer median even after the trim to 17.0x, which is why the destination was trimmed at all (fttcp-deliberation Sec 2, Override 2). The spread between the 16.6% ROCE and the roughly 13% cost of equity is about three points. Positive but thin (web-dossier Sec 1).

## NARRATIVE

CMS owns cash machines and gets paid a fixed monthly fee to run them. The old model paid per transaction, and that model is dying: withdrawal value is falling about 7% a year. So CMS changed how it charges. It now owns the machine and leases it on long bank contracts. The clue that the move was forced, not chosen: CMS won its flagship SBI mandate as the lowest bidder, and it is waiting on a committee for pricing relief.

The numbers show the cost of the change. Returns on capital fell hard in FY26, from 25% to 17%, because CMS bought a lot of machines that are deployed but not yet fully earning. Free cash flow turned negative. A block of receivables went bad at once. The bull case is that the machines fill, capex drops, and returns recover toward the low twenties as the contracted rentals arrive. The bear case is that CMS bought a book that earns barely more than its cost of capital, and preserved revenue while destroying returns.

One number settles it: the FY28 return on capital. Above 22% on flat capital, the transition is proven. Below 20% with capex already cut, it has failed. Until that prints, this is research and watch, not a trade. The price is attractive today, 14% below the entry that clears a 25% return, and the balance sheet is safe. But price does not clear the proof. Watch the FY28 gate and the next receivables print. Buy only when the gate fires and the price is still in the zone.
