## WHAT THE PIPELINE ALREADY ASKED OF THESE DOCUMENTS

Open items the calls and decks did not settle, carried from the phase 1 gate:
1. FLDG (first-loss default guarantee) utilisation in bps of off-book AUM, and whether it books
   in credit cost or in opex. Management could not give the number on the Q1 FY27 call and gave
   answers that do not agree.
2. The numeric write-off trigger. The Q4 FY26 call states the point moved from 120 to 150 days
   overdue. No filed note in the annual report or the prospectus states any numeric trigger.
3. Why the Rs 832 Cr preferential issue at Rs 314.11 came four months after the IPO with capital
   adequacy at 40.2%. It postdates both calls, so neither addresses it.
4. Guidance drift inside one quarter: credit cost "10-15%" became "15%", RoE "19-21%" became
   "20% plus", and the AUM horizon moved from FY27 to "next 12 months".
5. The parent guarantee to Si Creva's lenders, 228% of the parent's own net worth. Neither call
   mentions it, and no analyst asks.
