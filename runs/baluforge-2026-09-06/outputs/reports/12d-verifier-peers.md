## PART 2: THE CENTRAL CLAIM — DOES THE EVIDENCE SUPPORT "CONTRADICTED"?

I read all twelve transcripts before reading B06's Part 1 Claim-1 write-up, as instructed,
so the following is my own read compared against B06's, not a rubber stamp of B06's framing.

**Balu Forge's claim (per Stage 5):** the Q4 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2026) revenue decline (-2.3% YoY)
is attributed to a Middle East geopolitical disruption to India-UAE logistics, raised only
after 9M FY26 growth (29.0%) had already fallen below the 40-45% guidance floor.

**What the peer transcripts actually show, independently confirmed by me:**
- RKFORGE (Q4 FY26 call, p.3): names a real, dated Middle East conflict, felt as "energy
  price volatility... and renewed supply chain disruptions." Same call, p.4: Rs.1,216.78cr
  revenue, **+28% YoY**, EBITDA +111% YoY — RKFORGE's best quarter of the year, in the
  identical window Balu Forge says it declined. p.17 (not p.19 as B06 states, but the quote
  is genuine): asked directly about West Asia exposure, management answers "No, no revenue
  and no exposure" — RKFORGE felt the event only through cost, with zero direct trade-lane
  exposure, and still grew 28%.
- HAPPYFORGE (Q4 FY26 call, p.4 and p.6, both correctly anchored): names the "U.S. Iran
  issue" strictly as a raw-material/manufacturing-cost item, in the same quarter it posted
  Rs.424cr revenue, **+20.4% YoY**, its best-ever quarterly performance across every metric.
- MMFL (investor conference, 5 Mar 2026, p.3, correctly anchored): names Hormuz Strait/Iran
  fuel-supply risk unprompted, in the same breath as "the fourth quarter seems to be
  strong... Class 8 truck orders have shot through the roof."
- The one peer data point that DOES tie a geopolitical/logistics event to an actual revenue
  effect is HAPPYFORGE's Q1 FY27 call (Aug 2026, p.4, correctly anchored, and the Mihir Vora
  exchange, genuinely on p.8): CV export de-growth of ~12% because DDP contracts meant "a
  month of deliveries were pending" due to transit delays. This is real, but it lands in
  Apr-Jun 2026 — a full quarter after Balu Forge's claimed Jan-Mar 2026 window — and is
  confined to one segment (HappyForge's total Q1 FY27 revenue still grew 27% YoY).

**My independent conclusion: CONTRADICTED is the correct classification and is not
overstated.** The event Balu Forge invokes is real (two, arguably three, peers name it
unprompted in the identical quarter) — B06 says this plainly and does not attempt to deny
the underlying event. But every peer that names it in the Jan-Mar 2026 window describes a
COST-side effect only (energy, fuel, shipping), and all three peers posted flat-to-record
revenue growth in that same window. Against Stage 5's own stated test — "if peers report
normal or strong demand, it is company-specific" — the peer set clears that bar cleanly.
The one genuine revenue-tied logistics effect in the whole twelve-transcript corpus
(HappyForge CV exports) sits in the wrong quarter and the wrong segment to rescue Balu
Forge's specific mechanism claim. B06's own prose (Part 5, "Cross-Peer Hypothesis") already
carries the appropriate hedge — it does not claim to have disproven an idiosyncratic,
concentrated India-UAE trade-lane exposure that none of these three peers happens to share,
only that the mechanism is unsupported by every available comparator. That hedge is
intellectually honest and belongs in the verdict language too, not just the prose; as
written it is a defensible, not an inflated, CONTRADICTED.

Verdict: **the quotes support the classification.** They neither overstate nor understate
it, once the anchor-page errors above are corrected (none of those errors change which
transcript, speaker, or substantive content is being relied on — they are page-number
errors, not evidentiary errors, with the single exception of the MMFL/HappyForge
misattribution in Claim 5, which does not touch Claim 1).
