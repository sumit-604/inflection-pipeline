Included as adjacent levers, not in core SAM: cybersecurity services (Fluidech, 1.52% of revenue) and government cloud (ZeaCloud, no MeitY empanelment). ## SECTION 3: SAM AND SOM

3A SAM (hardware core):
1. Product fit: servers only in the central figure (storage NOT FOUND, so no subtraction possible). Rs 40,928 Cr.
2. Geography: India already (Singapore excluded by definition).
3. Channel: tender, GeM, SI: no sourced split, NOT FOUND.
4. Customer: remove hyperscale 48.35% (Mordor 2025) = 40,928 x (1 - 0.4835) = Rs 21,139 Cr. The percentage comes from Mordor and is applied to the IMARC base (mixed sources, flagged). Using Mordor alone: 63,077 x 0.5165 = Rs 32,579 Cr.
5. Capability: no sourced filter for Tier-1 BFSI and telecom buying branded fleets (Dell, HPE, Lenovo): NOT FOUND, so SAM is a ceiling.
SAM = Rs 21,139 Cr = 25.4% of realistic TAM (21,139 / 83,321) and 51.65% of hardware TAM. Adjacent levers outside SAM: cyber Rs 27,121 Cr, government cloud Rs 15,272 Cr (see 5D).

3B SOM. Current share: 299.96 / 21,139 = 1.42% (Mordor-based SAM gives 0.92%). Share evidence: revenue FY23 94.59, FY24 140.55, FY25 230.30, FY26 354.40 Cr (screener Data Sheet); FY23-FY26 CAGR 55.3% ((354.40/94.59)^(1/3) - 1) against India DC systems +29.2% in 2025 (AR26 p.75). The history shows share gain, but history is the cross-check, not the base.

SAM growth carried: 9.81% (IMARC, 2026-2034 CAGR); Mordor says 7.61% (2026-2032). Upside reading: +20.5% in 2026 (AR26 p.75) and Netweb's 38% AI CAGR (B06). Separating observation: whether 2027 value growth stays above 15% after memory and GPU price rises ease (RPTECH says price-rise speed is halving, B06). Value growth includes price, so unit growth is lower.

SAM FY29 (3 years) = 21,139 x 1.0981^3 = 21,139 x 1.3241 = Rs 27,992 Cr. SAM FY31 (5 years) = 21,139 x 1.0981^5 = 21,139 x 1.5967 = Rs 33,751 Cr. (CY2025 base used as the FY26 base; the year offset is not adjusted.)

Two readings. A: market absorbs the rule-band gain (FY26 growth 54% vs 29.2% market; Netweb +172.1% YoY, RPTECH +62%, B06). B: guidance and balance sheet bind. FY27 guidance Rs 370-400 Cr (CRISIL record, step1 brief) is +4.4% to +12.9% on 354.40; the MD said FY27 revenue is "not significantly bigger"; standalone Q1 FY27 is 18% below the FY26 quarterly average (B03); working capital is the stated constraint (B06) and the four quantified forecasts were missed (B07). Separating observation: Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue ex-Singapore and HexaData revenue by line. Stage 11: use it as a check that market size does not cap the base case; the guidance-anchored path (4% to 13% FY27) is the nearer forward evidence.

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY AND RUNWAY

5A Funnel (Rs Cr): TAM realistic 83,321 (conservative 55,332; high 105,470) -> SAM 21,139 (25.4%) -> SOM 3 yr 677, 5 yr 986 -> current India revenue 299.96 (1.42% of SAM).

5B Headroom = 21,139 / 299.96 = 70.5x. TAM growth: IMARC rates weighted by realistic TAM = (40,928 x 9.81 + 27,121 x 13.60 + 15,272 x 12.58) / 83,321 = 11.6%. Company CAGR FY23-26 55.3% versus market 29.2% (2025): gaining share. At guidance (+4.4% to +12.9%) the company is at or below market growth in FY27. Years to saturate SAM: at the FY23-26 company CAGR against SAM growth 9.81%, ln(70.5) / ln(1.553/1.0981) = 4.256 / 0.347 = 12.3 years (not a forecast; the growth gap will not hold).

5C Class: STRONG. The stage file gives no threshold matrix; the call rests on headroom (70.5x, which alone reads MASSIVE), tempered by market growth near 11.6% and a 1.42% share held with a weak moat. Runway is not the binding constraint.

5D SAM expansion levers: (i) cyber services Rs 27,121 Cr TAM; Fluidech revenue Rs 5.39 Cr (538.96 Lakhs), loss-making; potential addition NOT FOUND (capability share unknown). (ii) Government cloud Rs 15,272 Cr; ZeaCloud turnover Rs 5.69 Cr (568.79 Lakhs, 106.4% of it intra-group per B04); needs MeitY empanelment, which has slipped since Jun 2025. (iii) Singapore, outside the India SAM. Revised headroom with levers: (21,139 + 27,121 + 15,272) / 299.96 = 211.8x on paper, but levers carry no revenue evidence, so it is not carried.

5E Output card. Realistic TAM Rs 83,321 Cr; SAM Rs 21,139 Cr; SOM Rs 677 Cr (3 yr) and Rs 986 Cr (5 yr); runway STRONG. Valuation implication: At 31.2% revenue CAGR implied by SOM, with margin trajectory held at the FY26 ex-other-income EBITDA margin of 2.49% (B04), the earnings growth embedded here is about 31.2% CAGR, which does not support the current valuation of 52.9x trailing P/E (market cap Rs 325.68 Cr / FY26 PAT Rs 6.16 Cr, screener) on the market-size evidence alone. If the Q1 FY27 7.03% margin (B04) were structural, earnings growth would be higher, but that margin is unresolved between mix and stock gain (B03/B07). Section 1B governs the exit multiple; this line is a cross-check only.
