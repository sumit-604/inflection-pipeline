Rationale 
The rating  upgrade of Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited (RPEL)  favourably factors in  
increase in overall revenue and profitability with improved capital structure and debt coverage 
indicators. The ratings continue to factor  in healthy order book position, diversified customer 
profile and backward integrated nature of operations  which supports process efficiency . 
Infomerics, notes the benefits of the on -going capex in terms of wider product portfolio and 
capacity enhancement together with the investment in the solar capacity which will result in 
higher cash flows.  
The rating is however constrained on account of working capital-intensive nature of operations, 
highly competitive and fragmentated nature of industry , vulnerability of  profit margins to 
fluctuations in steel price movements and foreign exchange risk. 
Outlook: Stable:  
The Stable outlook reflects Infomerics’ expectation of continued  growth in revenue and 
profitability margins with the successful commercialisation of ongoing capex and savings from 
captive power.

Key Rating Drivers with Detailed Description 
Strengths 
• Increase in overall revenue and improvement in profitability. 
Overall revenue improved year -on-year by 49.80% in FY25, reaching ₹891.88 crore compared 
to ₹595.38 crore in FY24. This substantial increase was supported by higher volumes and 
improved realizations. Total operating income (TOI) further improved to ₹819.70  crore in 
9MFY26, with more than ₹30 crore contributed by value -added products. With the increase in 
topline and supported by increase in product range, EBITDA and PAT margins improved to 
9.69% and 5.23% in FY25, compared to 8.40% and 5.15% in FY24. In 9mF Y26 the company 
reported EBITDA margin of 10.52% driven by ability to offer complete fastening solutions and 
power cost savings from solar power capacity of 2MW. 
Revenue growth in FY26 is expected to be around 23% over FY25, reaching approximately 
₹1,100 crore. This is further projected to improve by over 25%, rising to  over ₹1,400 crore by 
FY27. The overall increase in revenue will be driven by higher volumes, improved realizations, 
the addition of value-added products, and expansion through the upcoming Phase II capex. 
With the increase in topline and the broader product range, EBITDA and PAT margins are 
expected to improve to around 10%- 11% and around 5%-6% respectively in FY26. The EBITDA 
margin is projected to be maintained at similar level in FY27. 
 
• Improved capital structure and debt service indicators 
The capital structure, reflected in the TOL/TNW ratio, improved to 1.01x at the end of FY25 
compared to 1.21x at the end of FY24. It is expected to strengthen further to below 0.50x at end 
FY26. Going forward in FY 27, the TOL/TNW is likely to remain steady at similar level as FY 26 , 
with the increased debt on account of capex related term loans being offset by increase in net 
worth accretions of profits. Total Debt/EBITDA improved to 1.67x at the end of FY25 from 1.87x 
at the end of FY24. This ratio is further expected to improve to below 1.40x at end FY26 and below 
1.20x at end FY27, driven by growth in overall EBITDA. 
The DSCR improved to 5.25x at the end of FY25. Infomerics expects DSCR to moderate to  around 
3.50x-4.00x over FY26 and FY27 as repayments of project term loans commence.

• Backward integration supports process efficiency 
RPEL processes scrap generated during the manufacturing of its products and converts it back 
into raw material for reuse in production. As a result, a portion of the raw material requirement 
is met in -house, while waste produced in the manufacturing proces s is fully utilized, ensuring 
minimal wastage.  This backward integration, implemented through the steel melting shop 
(comprising an induction furnace), enhances efficiency in the production process and provides a 
competitive advantage. It contributes to lo wer product costs, greater control over raw material 
supply, and reduced dependency on third parties for operations. 
 
• Long track record of operation and extensive experience of promoters in steel 
industry. 
With a track record of over two decades in the stainless -steel industry, the company has 
established strong relationships with customers and achieved a successful presence in both 
domestic and global markets. In addition to extensive experience of promoter s, RPEL is 
supported by a well-experienced and qualified management team. 
 
• Diversified customer profile. 
RPEL sells its products in both domestic and international markets. The company has been 
exporting to destinations including Germany, the UK, Spain, the Netherlands, and other countries. 
This diversified customer base mitigates risks related to customer concentration, sector-specific 
challenges, global market fluctuations, and changes in international relations. 
 
Weaknesses 
• Working capital intensive nature of operations. 
RPEL’s operations remain working capital-intensive, as the business requires significant funding 
due to the considerable time gap between the purchase of raw materials and the collection of 
receivables after sales. The overall working capital cycle is driven by large WIP inventory days as 
the company supplies various types of washers along with nuts, bolts, circlips etc as part of a 
typical order. In addition, RPEL extends credit periods to customers to foster long -term 
relationships. The operating cycle of RPEL remains long, standing at 107 days in FY25 (as against 
157 days in FY24). Also, the same is expected to remain long at 100 days in FY 26 and further at 
94 days in FY 27. Efficient working capital management will continue to be a key rating sensitivity 
from a credit perspective.

• Operates in highly competitive and fragmentated industry. 
RPEL operates in a highly competitive and fragmented industry, facing competition from both 
organized and unorganized players. However, Infomerics notes that competitive intensity in the 
stainless-steel washer and fasteners segment is relatively lower comp ared to the mild 
steel/carbon steel products segment. 
• Profit margins exposed to fluctuations in steel price movements and foreign exchange 
risk. 
RPEL remains vulnerable to volatility in raw material and finished goods prices. The prices of key 
raw materials, such as stainless-steel scrap, SS sheets, and SS coils, have exhibited a volatile trend 
over the years, being largely determined by market for ces. Considering the volatility in price of 
stainless steel, the company places back-to-back orders for around 75% of the raw material with 
suppliers like Jindal Stainless Ltd, Sambhav Metals and Alloys Ltd as well as scrap suppliers after 
receiving confir med orders from clients to hedge against price volatility. In addition, RPEL’s 
profitability is exposed to adverse fluctuations in foreign currency, as the company does not 
hedge 100% of its net FX exposure. The company hedges the net foreign currency payables 
position (after netting of FX receivables from exports against payables from raw material 
imports) to the extent of 80% through forward contracts.  
 
Liquidity – Adequate 
RPEL’s cash flow from operations are estimated to be adequate to meet its, repayment obligations 
of ₹7.60 crore in FY26, ₹9.67 crore in FY27, and ₹16.36 crore in FY28. Bank limits have remained 
utilized at around 59.94% over the past 12 months ended February 2026.  Furthermore, RPEL 
had free cash and bank balances of ₹70 crore as of September 2025.

About the Company: 
RPEL, manufactures stainless steel products in Vadodara (Gujarat). RPEL is promoted by Mr. 
Ramanlal Sanghvi and currently managed by Mr. Vijay Sanghvi and family. The Company 
manufactures stainless steel washers, solar mounting hooks, finishing sheets and SS tubes & pipes 
products, which find application in elevators, doors, and home appliances. The company produce 
more than 2500  types of washers in different sizes and international standards. The company 
incorporates an in -house backward integrated manufac turing facility, which makes them 
independent to manufacture any product size at any time. The manufacturing facilities located at 
Vadodara and Ahmedabad, Gujarat with an  installed capacity of  41000 metric tons per annum, 
which was utilized around 80% during last three years ended as on March 31, 2025. The company 
has completed capex Phase 1 as of February 2025. The total capacity increased to 67194 metric 
TPA from which 23194 metric TPA is from a result of the capex in phase 1 and rest is due to the 
enhancement in the  existing capacity of finishing line which has been shown to be higher at 
32,000 TPA in FY25 (compared to 21,000 TPA in FY24) with an additional increase of 3,000 MTPA  
in FY26. This increase in capacity is  attributable to changes in the thickness of stainless-steel 
sheets processed . The company has also initiated Phase II capex which enhance its product 
portfolio capacities and installation of 2MW solar power plant for captive use  and has planned 
Phase III capex for Entry into Copper Clad Laminates (CCL) segment (electronics/PCB industry) 
with capacity of 5,280 sheets/day at Gujarat. The total capacity will increase by 21736 metric TPA 
as a result of the capex in phase II.
