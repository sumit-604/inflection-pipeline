PRESS RELEASE 
OHL ANNOUNCES Q2 AND H1 FY 2026-2027 FINANCIAL RESULTS 
 
MUMBAI, OCTOBER 15, 2026: Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) reported its results for the second 
quarter and half year ending September 30, 2026.  
Mr. Pramod Ranjan, Managing Director & CEO, Oriental Hotels Ltd. said, “In Q2 FY27, OHL reported 
an operating revenue of INR 117 crores driven by an 9% growth in RevPAR. The Company achieved 
an operating EBITDA of INR 27 crores and a PAT of INR 12 crores. For the first half of the year, 
operating revenue stood at INR 228 crores, operating EBITDA of INR 50 crores and PAT of INR 23 
crores, a 10% growth over last year. Enabled by key asset upgradation in the OHL portfolio, we are 
well positioned to deliver a strong performance in the coming quarters.”

About Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) 
Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) is an associate company of The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL). 
The company has seven hotels – Taj Coromandel, Chennai Taj Fisherman’s Cove Resort & Spa, Chennai 
Taj Malabar Resort & Spa, Cochin, Vivanta Coimbatore, Vivanta Mangalore; Gateway Madurai and 
Gateway Coonoor.
