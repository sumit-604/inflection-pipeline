## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. RPT history

This is the section that most warrants a caution flag, not because anything
is undisclosed or non-arm's-length, but because of concentration. AOC-2 (AR
FY2025-26, p.90-91) states plainly: **"The Company has not entered into any
contract or arrangement or transaction with its related parties which is not
at arm's length during FY 2026,"** and no RPT required Section 188(1)
materiality disclosure. ✅ VERIFIED. The transaction table (Notes to
Consolidated Financial Statements, AR FY2025-26, p.167-168) itemises,
₹ million:

| Nature | Party | FY26 | FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations | Cyient GmbH | 63.47 | – |
| Revenue from operations | Cyient Inc. | 39.27 | – |
| Revenue from operations | Cyient Limited | 15.03 | 4.57 |
| Services availed | Cyient Limited | – | 62.40 |
| **Sub-contracting charges** | **Cyient Limited** | **537.56** | **–** |
| Reimbursement of expenses (net) | various group entities | ~51 | ~83 |
| **Financial guarantee closure** | **Cyient Limited** | **4,470.00** | **–** |
| Term loan repaid | Cyient Limited | 746.72 | 248.91 |
| Interest on loans | Cyient Limited | 14.43 | 74.93 |
| CSR expenditure | Cyient Foundation | 16.14 | 11.66 |

Two items stand out and are flagged as **⚠️ CAUTION, not adverse**: (1)
sub-contracting charges to the parent of ₹537.56mn appeared new in FY26
(nil in FY25) — roughly 5.7% of standalone FY26 revenue — and (2) a ₹4,470mn
financial guarantee from the parent was **closed** during the year. Read
together with the term-loan repayment trend (₹746.72mn repaid to parent in
FY26 vs ₹248.91mn in FY25) and falling interest paid to the parent
(₹14.43mn vs ₹74.93mn), the direction of travel is toward **less** financial
dependency on the parent, not more — the guarantee closure and accelerating
loan repayment both reduce, rather than deepen, the promoter-linkage. All
items were Audit-Committee-approved per the charter (AR FY2025-26, p.94-95)
and no minority-shareholder-approval requirement was triggered because
nothing was found materially non-arm's-length. ❓ The absolute quantum and
purpose of the sub-contracting charge is not further explained in the
extract available; this is a gap, not a finding of misconduct.

No loans/guarantees to unrelated private promoter entities were found;
"Loans and advances in the nature of loans to firms/companies in which
Directors are interested: Nil" (AR FY2025-26 p.101).
