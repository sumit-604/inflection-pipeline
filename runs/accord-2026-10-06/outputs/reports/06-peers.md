## LBF-1 cross-check: guidance plausibility against peer seasonality and cover

These are arithmetic cross-checks on sourced figures. They are not forecasts.

1. H1 share of full-year revenue (calc DS and calls). Danish FY25 38.2% (163 of 426.71) and FY26 40.5% (211 of 521.45). Shilchar FY25 38.1% and FY26 50.6% (Q1+Q2 over full year, DS quarterly). Voltamp FY25 42.7%, FY26 42.1%. Accord FY26 39.6% (27.72 of 70.07, B04). Peers also say H2 is the heavy half: Danish "structurally H2 heavy" (D-M26, p5 L186-188); Shilchar "first quarter... always low sales... Q4 is the maximum" (S-A25, Alay Shah, p12 L93).
2. Applied to Accord H1 FY27 revenue 52.68 (B05): at 38.1-50.6% the full year is Rs 104-138 Cr; at the five peer-and-own shares of 38.1-42.7% it is Rs 123-138 Cr; at Accord's own 39.6% it is Rs 133 Cr. Rs 120 Cr needs an H1 share of 43.9%, inside the peer range. Rs 180 Cr needs 29.3%, below every observation (minimum 38.1%). On this read the low half of the band is supported and the upper half is not.
3. Order cover at a comparable point (calc). Danish had Rs 500 Cr against a Rs 700 Cr target at the start of FY27 (71%). Shilchar had about Rs 500 Cr against the remaining Rs 665 Cr of its Rs 800 Cr (75%) at 14-Aug-26. Accord has Rs 173 Cr against an H2 need of Rs 67.3-127.3 Cr (B05), cover of 1.36x to 2.57x. Accord's cover is higher than either peer's. Cover is not the weak point. Conversion and unfiled orders are (B05 flags). Accord's book at Jan-26 was 2.3x FY26 revenue while revenue fell (B04), against peer books of about 0.5x to 1.0x revenue; a book that large relative to output has failed to convert before.
4. Peer guidance track record. Shilchar targeted "Rs 750 crores" for FY26 (S-A25, p12 L93; S-O25, p8 L318) and delivered 651.94, 13.1% short (calc).
