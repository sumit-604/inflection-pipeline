Mr. Shetty served as Senior Director –

East, overseeing business operations 
and driving sustained market share and

drive efficiency and reduce  costs. 
Passionate about attracting  and 
nurturing top talent, he  has led 
impactful initiatives that have built 
high-performing and engaged teams. 
He is a Graduate and Master’s in 
Personnel Management. He is always 
open to new challenges and is 
committed to  fostering a positive, 
growth-oriented workplace through 
strategic human capital practices.
