Subject: Announcement for receipt of Letter of Award 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We are pleased to announce that South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (SWPE) has been awarded 
a Letter of Award (LOA) for appointment as consultant for detailed exploration of bauxite mines, in 
the state of Orisha from  National Aluminium Company Limited (NALCO, A Government of India 
Enterprise). The aggregate value of work order is approx. ₹11.78 Crores (including GST).  
As per LOA project is to be completed within 24 months, however, we are hopeful of completing it in 
shorter duration than envisaged.  
It is to be noted here that NALCO is new addition to our client list from whom we have received the 
very first order.

is attached as Annexure-I.
