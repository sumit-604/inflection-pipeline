## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

2A DEMAND. The picture splits. Demand is strong and specific at MTAR (hot-box orders tied to a US power-for-data-centre customer, about 70% of FY27 revenue from clean energy per MTAR, Q4 FY26, Srinivas Reddy; nuclear book about 820 Cr). It is strong at ISGEC across 15 to 16 sectors (record 8,757 Cr standalone book). It is mixed at ELECON: overseas intake +35% (ELECON, Q2 FY27, Prayasvin Patel) while domestic is "relatively muted" (ELECON, Q1 FY27, C. Shah). Defence is slow everywhere: ELECON's Navy orders slid again (IAC RFP Q1 FY27, order Q4 FY27 per ELECON, Q4 FY26, Dalwadi; P-17 Bravo order Q1 FY29 per ELECON, Q2 FY27, Dalwadi) and MTAR's domestic defence is at prototype (4 Cr AMCA order). Silence is informative: no peer speaks of a space-launch or missile boom, which weakens any DNA demand framing that implies an industry-wide surge. Consensus on nuclear is MTAR only.

2B PRICING AND INPUT COSTS. See Q4. Peers hold margin through contingency and repricing; ELECON gear raised prices and kept 22.1% EBIT in Q2 FY27; MHE lost margin where it could not lock prices. Peer margins did not rise on pricing power. They held. Does not match a margin-expansion narrative for WIL.

2C CAPEX CYCLE. This is a broad capacity build across all three listed peers, not a lone expander. Differences are funding and utilisation. ISGEC (internal accruals) and ELECON (net cash, 60% utilisation) expand from strength. MTAR expands on customer forecasts, 75% debt, with 100% utilisation on hot boxes. WIL expands under leverage and negative FY26 operating cash flow. Sector read: a capacity race funded by balance sheet strength, in which WIL is the weakest-funded entrant.

2D COMPETITIVE MENTIONS. None names WIL. Closest relevant: MTAR is qualified for End Shield and Calandria, the scope in WIL's tender. ELECON lists MHE competitors L&T, TKIL, TAKRAF, AUMUND and says TRF closed its MHE business (ELECON, Q2 FY27, K. Patel) and McNally went bankrupt (ELECON, Q3 FY26, K. Patel). No WIL mention.

2E RISKS PEERS DISCUSS THAT WIL DOES NOT
- Single-customer concentration and AI-capex slowdown fear (MTAR, Q2 FY27, Ashray Sheth question; Q1 FY27, Jenish Karia question).
- Customer-driven dispatch deferment after goods are ready: ELECON 70 to 77 Cr (ELECON, Q4 FY26, C. Shah); ISGEC 130 Cr order on hold with 95% paid (ISGEC, Q1 FY27, Chatnani).
- Revenue cut-off: ELECON had about 70 Cr of finished goods dispatched but not recognised under Ind AS (ELECON, Q1 FY27, C. Shah).
- Order cancellations (ISGEC about 550 Cr, Q4 FY26, Puri).
- Reporting corrections: MTAR segment misclassification of 60 Cr and presentation re-upload (MTAR, Q2 FY27).
- Subsidiary losses and forex gains inside other income, with disclosure that separates them: ISGEC slide 19 "profit from business operations" (ISGEC, Q2 FY27, Puri); forex other income of 103 Cr consolidated, half offset in other expenses (ISGEC, Q4 FY26, Chatnani). Relevant to WIL's other-income dependence.
- US tariff of 50% on India-made goods and a Mexico step-down subsidiary response (ELECON, Q4 FY26, Management reply to Raj Shah).
- Freight, container and ship availability under the West Asia conflict (ISGEC, Q2 FY27, Puri).
- PSU budget dependence (ELECON, Q1 FY27, C. Shah) and defence RFP slippage (ELECON, multiple).
- Goodwill impairment of 102 Cr (ELECON, Q4 FY26, C. Shah).
- Management turnover: ELECON changed CFO (ELECON, Q4 FY26, Aayush Shah). WIL's CFO exit after 15 months is in B05.
