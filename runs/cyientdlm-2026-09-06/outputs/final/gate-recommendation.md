## MONITORABLES AND TRIGGERS

Eight items, deduplicated across the evidence stages.

1. Watch consolidated net working capital days every quarter in the investor presentation and the key ratios table. The number that matters is 161 days at Q1 FY27. A fall to 120 or below with positive operating cash flow in the same quarter breaks the STRUCTURAL cash determination. Any print above 161 confirms it. This is the single load bearing item in the file.
2. Watch standalone India parent revenue and PAT, reported separately from consolidated, in the quarterly results. Standalone revenue fell 29.9 percent and standalone PAT fell 26.6 percent in FY26 while consolidated PAT rose 7.65 percent. A continued standalone decline says the transition thesis is being priced on an asset that is still shrinking.
3. Watch the Build to Spec share of revenue in the quarterly deck and the annual report revenue disaggregation note. The working number is 6 percent for FY26 against a double digit FY27 target. Ask management to reconcile 6 percent against the 25 percent BRSR figure in writing. This is the central margin transition claim.
4. Watch the Altek earn out remeasurement direction in Notes 21 and 33.1.4 each reporting period. Any further downward remeasurement books another fair value gain and is further evidence the acquisition is missing its case. Goodwill of Rs 718.77mn sits on the same cash generating unit with no separate local audit.
5. Recompute debt service coverage from the primary cash flow statement each period, not from the ratio note. FY26 stands at 0.62x. Persistence below 1.0x is the signal; the ratio note alone is not trustworthy here because the two annual reports disclose the same FY25 year two different ways.
6. Watch the related party sub contracting flow to Cyient Limited in Note 30. It appeared at Rs 537.56mn from nil in one year, with the payable up 257 percent and no arm's length benchmarking disclosed. Any further growth, or a second year without benchmarking, is the trigger.
7. Reconcile the order book bridge each quarter: opening order book plus intake minus revenue against the closing figure. Verifier B found the bridge fails in every FY26 quarter, by about Rs 30 Cr in Q2, Rs 26 Cr in Q3 and about Rs 120 Cr for the full year against the claimed 1.5 times book to bill, with no cancellation, de scope or FX disclosure anywhere. This tests whether the order book itself is a clean number.
8. Watch the gross margin explanation on the next call. Peers disclosed a component and PCB cost shock in the same January to June 2026 window and the company did not, and its stated cause for the decline, operating expense investment, cannot produce a gross margin move. This is the CRITICAL item stage 5 missed.
