## 1. STAGE 1 CASH RECONCILIATION GAP — RESOLVED, NO GENUINE GAP IN THE AR

Stage 1 flagged an apparent ~Rs 232 cr gap between an implied CFO+CFI+CFF closing cash of
~Rs 753.03 cr and a Balance Sheet Cash-and-Bank line of Rs 520.94 cr. Working the Consolidated
Statement of Cash Flows and Notes 14/14A directly closes this with anchored figures, and the
AR's own numbers show NO unexplained gap:

- Consolidated CFO FY26 = Rs (73.13) cr [Rs (7,312.82) lakh] — matches Stage 1's own CFO figure
  exactly, confirming these are the right base numbers (Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows,
  p.117).
- Consolidated CFI FY26 = Rs (185.57) cr [Rs (18,556.75) lakh] (p.118).
- Consolidated CFF FY26 = Rs 697.80 cr [Rs 69,779.58 lakh] (p.118).
- Net increase in cash FY26 = Rs 439.10 cr [Rs 43,910.01 lakh] = CFO+CFI+CFF, exactly (p.118).
- Opening cash (= FY25 closing) = Rs 2.27 cr [Rs 226.95 lakh]; **Closing cash per the Cash Flow
  Statement itself = Rs 441.37 cr [Rs 44,136.96 lakh]** (p.118).
- The CFS's own Ind AS 7 reconciliation note ("Cash & Cash Equivalents included in the cash
  flow statement comprise the following balance sheet amounts", p.118) itemises this Rs 441.37
  cr as: Cash on hand Rs 0.48 cr + Balance with banks in current a/c Rs 402.29 cr + Balance with
  banks in fixed deposit a/c Rs 38.60 cr = Rs 441.37 cr.
- The Balance Sheet's own Current Assets lines (p.116) show, separately: "Cash and Cash
  Equivalents" (Note 14) = Rs 87.99 cr [Rs 8,799.10 lakh] and "Bank balances other than (iii)
  above" (Note 14A) = Rs 432.95 cr [Rs 43,294.74 lakh]. **Sum = Rs 520.94 cr exactly** — this is
  the figure Stage 1 (and screener) picked up as "Cash and Bank."
- Note 14A (p.123) splits its Rs 432.95 cr into: Fixed Deposits with maturity 3-12 months Rs
  79.57 cr [Rs 7,956.88 lakh] + "In Current Accounts (Earmarked)" Rs 353.38 cr [Rs 35,337.86
  lakh]. Note 14's own current-account balance (Rs 48.91 cr) plus this earmarked Rs 353.38 cr
  = Rs 402.29 cr, which is exactly the "Balance with Banks in Current A/c" figure the CFS
  reconciliation note uses (above).

**Conclusion**: Rs 520.94 cr (Balance Sheet) minus Rs 441.37 cr (Cash Flow Statement closing
cash) = Rs 79.57 cr, and that Rs 79.57 cr is precisely the Note 14A fixed-deposit tranche with
3-12 month maturity, which Ind AS 7 correctly excludes from "cash equivalents" because it is not
short-term/liquid enough. The AR reconciles perfectly on its own terms. The "~232 cr gap /
~753.03 cr implied closing" figure in Stage 1's brief does not match any combination of the
audited Cash Flow Statement's own CFO/CFI/CFF/opening-balance figures reproduced above;
whatever screener-sourced inputs produced that number diverge from the audited AR, not from a
real inconsistency inside it. Downstream stages should use the anchored figures above, not the
~753.03 cr figure. 🟢 Clean (once traced) — flagging as a corrected input, not a red flag.

Also note (new): Rs 353.38 cr of the Rs 520.94 cr "Cash and Bank" balance sits in EARMARKED
current accounts (typically project escrow/DSRA accounts tied to specific lender facilities,
consistent with the Rs 1,134 cr IREDA project financing and other project term loans) and is
NOT free cash available to the group at will, even though the CFS treats it as a cash
equivalent. A liquidity read of the balance sheet that treats the full Rs 520.94 cr as
discretionary cash would overstate the group's real financial flexibility. 🟡 Watch.

## 2. PARENT (STANDALONE) REVENUE ACTUALLY FELL WHILE THE GROUP GREW 61% — NEW, MATERIAL

The Standalone Statement of Profit and Loss (p.160) shows:
- Standalone Revenue from Operations: **FY26 = Rs 98.03 cr [Rs 9,802.52 lakh], FY25 = Rs 113.02
  cr [Rs 11,302.35 lakh] — a 13.3% YoY DECLINE** at the parent level, even as consolidated
  revenue rose 60.9% (Rs 1,333.77 cr to Rs 2,146.02 cr, per Pass 1).
- Standalone PBT nonetheless rose (Rs 6.68 cr FY25 to Rs 9.35 cr FY26) and PAT rose 51.1% (Rs
  4.75 cr to Rs 7.18 cr), driven by lower cost of materials consumed (Rs 91.32 cr to Rs 75.78
  cr) and lower other expenses (Rs 8.92 cr to Rs 5.08 cr), not by revenue growth.
- Standalone Basic/Diluted EPS: Rs 0.22 (FY25) to Rs 0.33 (FY26), consistent with the above.

This means **100% of the group's FY26 revenue growth, and then some, is attributable to the
subsidiary Insolation Green Energy Private Limited** (EPC/generation/trading), while the
parent's own module-manufacturing revenue line actually contracted. This sharpens Pass 1's
receivables/borrowings localisation finding (Section 4/7): it is not only the working-capital
deterioration that sits entirely at the subsidiary — the entire growth narrative does too. A
valuation or quality-of-growth assessment that treats "Insolation Energy" as one growing
manufacturing business should be aware the manufacturing parent is flat-to-shrinking on its own
top line. 🟡 Watch — belongs in the top findings.

5.94 = Rs 5.94 (FY25) (Statement of Profit and Loss, p.117). Pass 1 (Section 12/Note 44)

not state this explicitly in the EPS note itself, so it is a minor open question, not asserted
here as a discrepancy. 🟡 Watch (minor), worth a management question rather than a top finding.
