## 1. MENTAL MODEL DECLARATION (SIGNED — operator, 07-Sep-2026; supersedes stage 09b draft)

**Archetype (split, per business line):**
- **Line A — wear franchise (~75% of revenue: special welding consumables, wear plates and parts, on-site reconditioning):** BUILD-TO-SPEC COMPONENT MAKER with a COMMODITY CONVERTER slice (plain stick electrodes). Parts made to customer-approved alloy recipes for a maintenance-driven (opex) demand stream. Edge is cost position from own strip/wire (Unit 5) plus approval switching costs. Realisation Rs 1,000-1,200/kg on special electrodes [MGMT-2H]. NOT a brand franchise (no name premium), NOT an outsourcing partner (customers buy parts, not a process). Supersedes 09b's "brand franchise + outsourcing partner hybrid".
- **Line B — heavy engineering (~25% of revenue, ~76% of order book):** OUTSOURCING PARTNER / CONTRACT MANUFACTURER TO OEMs, with order-book characteristics. Build-to-drawing fabrication for European and Japanese OEMs and large private plants: "we become the factories and the manufacturing arms of some of these engineering companies" [FILED — Aug-2026 call p.19]. No proprietary machine designs, no patents [FILED — corpus silent]. Wins on quality and delivery, not price [MGMT]. Lower margin than Line A because an OEM layer sits between Diffusion and the end customer [MGMT-2H]. Realisation Rs 120-125/kg ex-wear plates, Rs 160-170/kg incl. wear plates; RM Rs 40-45/kg [MGMT-2H]. NOT an EPC order-book business of the ISGEC type. Supersedes 09b's "order-book business" label.

**Quality ladder.** Line A: R2 today (mid-teens ROCE, margin from cost position), claim R3 via railway/nuclear/defence qualification. One rung, plausible, undated. Line B: R2 as a qualified contract manufacturer; no credible rung claim inside the hold. **Blended: R2 today, R2 at FY29.**

**Transition classification: VOLUME-LED CAPACITY TRANSITION, NOT QUALITY-LED.** Unit 4 (Nimji) doubles heavy engineering from 9,000 to 18,000 TPA on ~Rs 50-60 Cr capex at 2-2.5x asset turns, ramp from Aug-2026, optimum (75-80%) in 2-2.5 years [MGMT-2H; consistent with FILED capex]. Adds ~Rs 85-110 Cr revenue at Line B margins by FY29 [INFERENCE]. The customer is moving from buying consumables to buying finished parts and machines [FILED — three calls]; Diffusion follows. Blended margin is pulled down by mix and pushed up by leverage; working capital settles near 100 days. The market is told "value chain move up"; the evidence shows "sticky franchise bolts on a contract fabrication shed".

**Dominant variables (dials):**
1. Unit 4 utilisation — commissioning date that holds, tonnage dispatched, CWIP falling as fixed assets rise.
2. Blended EBITDA margin against revenue mix (consumables share vs heavy engineering share).
3. Cash cycle — annual debtor days (guided 80-90 [FILED]), CFO/PAT, the Q4 bulge.
4. Qualification wins in Line A — railway (RITES/Vande Bharat), nuclear, defence — measured by a first commercial order with a rupee value, not a workshop approval.
Dropped: order-book mix (consumables are cash-and-carry and never enter the book; use revenue mix).

**Analogy.** A family tailor who has stitched the same school uniforms for forty years buys a large shed to make bulk workwear for two or three brands under their labels. Uniforms still earn the best margin per garment; workwear brings volume, pays slower, and the brands could move on price or delivery. The shop doubles in size; margin per garment falls a little; the shop is worth more rupees of sales, not more per rupee.

**What the model rejects as noise.** Tungsten/cobalt/nickel commentary (six peer transcripts across two years never mention it); order announcements under Rs 10 Cr; Tejorup/VSHORADS (real option, immaterial inside three years; all defence 1.5-2% of revenue [FILED]); UAE and Turkey; retail X sentiment and plant-visit hype; the Rs 800-900 Cr "on current assets" claim (needs the second Unit 4 build-out).

**Model falsifier.** Wrong if by FY28 heavy engineering exceeds 40% of revenue AND blended EBITDA margin ≥15% AND CFO/PAT >0.7 for two consecutive years — that would prove Line B carries consumables-grade economics and the transition is quality-led. Confirmed if margin stays 12-14% and CFO/PAT stays <0.7 as revenue grows.

**Gate positions.** Proof gate: NOT FIRED. Ugliness: STRUCTURAL floor (90-100 days for five years) with GROWTH OVERLAY to 115 [FILED company chart FY22-26; AGENCY]. Recognition gap: closing on narrative. Cell: RESEARCH-WATCH drifting toward PRICED NARRATIVE. Decision: PROCEED.
