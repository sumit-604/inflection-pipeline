Press Release 
 
Monolithisch India Limited Champions Skilled Workforce Development in 
Ramming Mass Applications 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is proud to announce the establishment of a dedicated training 
course for lining experts which will be known as “Monoramix Experts” upon course completion. 
 
This initiative is aimed at developing a skilled workforce of professionals specialized in the 
application and maintenance of high-performance ramming mass manufactured by 
Monolithisch india. Trainees will undergo intensive, multi-level training covering modern lining 
techniques, safety protocols, and application-specific best practices, all under the guidance of 
seasoned industry veterans and will be put on site training upon finishing the first two stages . 
 
The structured program includes: 
 
Level 1 – Beginner: Introduction to refractory basics, Monoramix handling, tools, furnace types, 
and safety fundamentals. 
 
Level 2 – Intermediate: Application techniques across varied conditions, quality control, 
surface preparation, and issue identification. 
 
Level 3 – Advanced: Mastery of complex lining tasks, optimization of furnace performance, and 
leadership in site operations. 
 
Additionally, select high-performing individuals may be considered for engagement under the 
official payroll of Monolithisch India Limited and could be deployed to high-consumption steel 
and foundry plants across the country. The objective is to support our valued customers with 
consistent, reliable, and technically skilled manpower, ensuring: 
 
• Superior lining quality 
• Optimized furnace performance 
• Reduced downtime and operational costs 
• Longer service life of refractory linings 
 
This strategic initiative reflects our commitment to strengthening end-to-end refractory 
application standards and delivering value that goes far beyond the product.
