## NARRATIVE

India Glycols is not a buy today. The price says one thing and the parts say another.

On 1 September 2026 the company splits into three listed lines. Add the earned value of the parts and you get Rs 742 to Rs 838 per original share. The market pays Rs 1,191. That is 30 to 38 percent above what the pieces are worth on their own multiples.

The reason is simple. The market applies one blended multiple of about 25.5x forward to the whole. Each part earns less: the chemicals line 14.5x, the spirits line about 16.6x. When the parts trade separately, the blended premium has nowhere to go but down. Fair value itself grows only 11.5 percent a year, and almost all of that is ordinary earnings compounding, not a re-rating tailwind. There is no re-rating tailwind. The multiple can only fall.

So every part fails the 25 percent hurdle at today's price. Role 1 marks it STOP on base and on bull earnings. The honest return you can expect from Rs 1,191 over three years is negative.

The operator did not reject the company. Both operating businesses are DEEP WATCH: the spirits franchise is a good business to own at the right price, and the chemicals line deserves another look on the next bio-glycol realisation print. The pharma line is INSUFFICIENT CONVICTION until a quarter arrives without the guar-gum transfer distorting it. That is a wait, not a no.

The wait is a long one. The entry zone is Rs 380 to Rs 429, roughly a third of today's price. Reaching it needs a deep correction or a sharp re-pricing after the split. And even at that entry, the return is mostly the discount closing, not the business compounding. Buy the gap between Rs 380 and Rs 742, because the compounding alone is too slow for the hurdle.

Two things would turn watch into avoid. If the filed standalone balance sheets show worse leverage than the estimate, especially more than about Rs 1,050 Cr on the spirits line, the equity return compresses and fair value falls. If bio-glycol realisation per tonne drops again while the Asia MEG index holds or rises, the chemicals catalyst dies and the cap engages. Watch both. Buy neither until the numbers are filed, not estimated.
