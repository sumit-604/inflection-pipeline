## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Advance makes crop protection
formulations. It buys technical grade pesticide, formulates it, and sells the
finished product. Installed capacity is nearly 90,000 MTPA (Investor
Presentation FY2026 Q4, doc 409302c5, p3). The margin is the gap between the
technical it buys and the formulation it sells.

**What is changing?** The company plans to make its own technicals. Unit-4 at
Gidani, Rajasthan, is the first step, at a first phase capex of about Rs 250 mn
(doc 409302c5, p24). Construction of Unit-5 at Dahej II, also for
technicals, has started (Investor Presentation FY2027 Q1, doc 23387b30, p23). 2,4-D capacity goes 4x to 10,000 MT by Q4 FY28 (doc 409302c5,
p17).

**Why now?** Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs 3,304.0 mn, up 96% on Rs 1,686.1 mn. PAT
was Rs 225.4 mn, up 152% on Rs 89.4 mn (doc 23387b30, p25). IPO money cut debt
to equity from 0.8 to 0.3 in FY26 (doc 23387b30, p28, full page).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Unit-4 must start and must cut
cost on the products it serves. The FY29 target is revenue of Rs 1,100 crore
at an EBITDA margin of 12%+ (doc 23387b30, p20). That needs about 2 points of
margin over FY26, and the cost cut is the only named mechanism.

**What breaks it?** Unit-4 slips again: it moved from Q2 FY27
(doc 409302c5, p24) to Q3 FY27 (doc 23387b30, p23). The Q1 surge is kharif
channel filling that reverses. Working capital days went from 40 in FY24 to 75
in FY25 (doc 23387b30, p28, full page), and FY26 days are NOT FOUND.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1 COMMODITY PRICE-TAKER. TO R2 COST-ADVANTAGED CONVERTER.

The mechanism is backward integration. A formulator buying technicals takes
the supplier's price (R1). A maker of its own technicals earns margin from cost
position (R2). One rung, inside the base rate.

Management's claim, quoted once: "By moving from a net buyer to a manufacturer
of technicals, the Company targets a 25-30% reduction in COGS for specific
products" (doc 409302c5, p15).

The claim covers "specific products". Their revenue share is NOT FOUND, so
the margin lift cannot be sized.
