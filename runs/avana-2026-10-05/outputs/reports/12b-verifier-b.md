# AVANA Stage 12, Verifier B: independent red-flag audit (B12b), NO-CONCALL MODE

Run 2026-10-05. Model claude-opus-5-5. Company: Avana Electrosystems Ltd (NSE Emerge: AVANA).
Units: INR lakh as printed in the AR, RHP and results, unless stated. "Derived" means arithmetic on printed figures.

## 0. Scope and method

- Mode: the company holds no earnings calls. Per the orchestrator, the communication record is the filed text: AR FY26 (Board report, MD&A, notes), RHP 31-Dec-2025, FY26 results and the 23-Jun-2026 legible re-filing, and the exchange announcements. Peer evidence: DANISH transcripts (Nov-2025, May-2026), SPCL deck (Dec-2024), MARINE deck (Oct-2025).
- Order of work: I read the sources first and built the list in Section 1. Then I read B05 and B06 and compared. I did not read B03 or any other stage output (structural rule). B05 often cites B03; where B05 carries a B03 figure, I checked the figure against the source, not against B03.
- Red-flag types used (rule 2, mapped to filings): dodges become silence on a material item; guidance walkbacks become RHP schedule versus later filings; analyst insistence becomes regulator queries; contradictions become statements in one filed document that another filed document, or the same one, contradicts.
- Anchors: "AR p<N>" and "RHP p<N>" are pdf pages of the page-marked text. "RES p<N>" is the 23-Jun-2026 legible re-filing (same text as the Reply-to-NSE file). "BO19Aug" is the Board outcome of 19-Aug-2026. DANISH pages are transcript page numbers.
- [INFERENCE] marks a reasoned link that no document states.

## 5. Credibility grade

B05 grade: C (no-concall default). My view: lower, by one notch. Three reasons. (1) The RHP blames COVID for a delay whose first deadline passed about 15 months before COVID (R2). (2) The AR, the only channel, contains statements its own numbers contradict: margin "no significant change" (R3), interest cover backwards (R13), pay "increased marginally" over a +45% to +65% table (R6), cost records and CSR versus the auditor (R14, R15). (3) The regulator had to chase four defects in the first annual results (R16). If the scale has no grade below C, hold C at its floor and gate on a Reg 30 commissioning filing by 26-Oct-2026.
