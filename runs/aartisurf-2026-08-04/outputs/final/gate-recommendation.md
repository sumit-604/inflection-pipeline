## FLAG-CASH

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

The evidence does not settle whether the cash weakness is structural margin erosion or a cyclical raw material trough that reverses.

Against a structural reading: receivables ageing improved, with amounts overdue beyond six months falling from 7.94% to 6.03% (B02), so the strain is not in collections. The margin compression is peer corroborated as an industry wide raw material event rather than a broken model, with Galaxy describing 30 year wide fatty alcohol spreads (B06). Q1FY27 operating margin recovered to 7.49% from 5.22% the prior quarter (B05), a first recovery data point.

Against a growth induced reading: the FY26 operating cash recovery to about Rs 77 Cr came substantially from a one time working capital release, inventory down Rs 15.69 Cr and payables up Rs 32.88 Cr (B01), not from margin and not from a receivables funded growth ramp. FY26 revenue growth was raw material passthrough, cost of materials up 33% against revenue up 30% (B12b), not evidenced volume led. No clean falsifier attaches, because the cash improvement is not funding a growth ramp in character.

Underlying cash collapse: FY25 CFO fell 78.3%, Rs 52.00 Cr to Rs 11.30 Cr, CFO to EBITDA from 82.4% to 24.8%, and free cash flow turned negative, Rs minus 8.63 Cr against Rs plus 35.91 Cr (B03).

Rating agency verbatim: "Reported PBILDT margin has continued to remain below the negative sensitivity threshold of 7% on a sustained basis in FY25 and H1FY26" (CARE rating p.1). CARE downgraded 19-Dec-2025, A minus / BBB plus to BBB plus / BBB, "citing continued subdued profitability margin in H1FY26" (CARE rating p.1).

Missing evidence: whether operating margin sustains above the recovery level and whether reported CFO and PAT hold without leaning again on stretched payables. Two quarters of prints are needed to close this.

Resolving metric: Q2 and Q3 FY27 operating margin both sustaining at or above 7.5%, with reported CFO and PAT not reliant on a further payables stretch. If margin reverts below about 6% toward the Q4FY26 5.22% print, or CFO again leans on stretched payables, the weakness reads structural.
