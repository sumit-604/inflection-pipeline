## WHAT IT SELLS
1. Consumable round cutting tools under the TOTEM brand: HSS and carbide taps,
   dies, drills, thread mills, end mills, reamers, burrs, plus spring lock
   washers. The tool wears out and the customer rebuys. Revenue is a
   consumable stream, not a capex sale.
   (https://forbesprecision.co.in/company/about-us/)
2. One plant, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad). ISO 9001, IATF, AS 9100D
   certified. Customers are auto, auto-component, defence, railways, aerospace,
   oil and gas, general engineering.
3. Distribution is the stated moat: 200+ distributors, 12 divisional sales
   offices, 60+ application engineers, exports to about 40 countries.
   (https://dhruva.substack.com/p/forbes-precision-tools-at-141-has;
   https://www.screener.in/company/544186/)
4. NO segment split is published. No product-level revenue for carbide versus
   HSS. No regular earnings call and no investor presentation with utilisation
   data. Disclosure is thin by design.

## THE INFLECTION THE THESIS WOULD REST ON
Capacity went in during FY26 and volume arrived in Q1 FY27. Fixed assets rose
Rs 89 cr to Rs 110 cr across FY24 to FY26. FY26 capex added CNC lines worth
about 3.5 lakh HSS drill units a year, robotic flute grinding for
high-performance tools, and a 90 MT spring-washer furnace. Q1 FY27 then printed
revenue Rs 67.55 cr, up 28.9% year on year, OPM 22.9% against 16.1%, and PAT
Rs 9.03 cr, up 137%.
(https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/forbes-precision-tools-machine-parts-standalone-net-profit-rises-137-01-in-the-june-2026-quarter-126081200803_1.html)

The engine claim is operating leverage on installed capacity plus a mix shift
from commodity HSS toward carbide and high-performance tools. The counter-claim
is an industry cycle: Kennametal India grew sales 29% in FY26 and Birla
Precision 15%, so a 29% quarter proves the cycle, not share gain. Nothing
published separates the two. That separation is the whole job.

## PEERS SELECTED (Step C, no operator pause)
- KENNAMETAL INDIA (KENNAMET, https://www.screener.in/company/KENNAMET/).
  The closest economic twin. Carbide round tools sold as consumables to the
  same auto, defence, railway and general engineering base. Sales Rs 1,511 cr
  FY26, OPM 20%, ROCE 33.2%. It is what TOTEM claims to be climbing toward, so
  it sets the destination margin and the destination multiple.
- WENDT INDIA (WENDT, https://www.screener.in/company/WENDT/consolidated/).
  Super-abrasives and precision grinding tools. Same consumable-tooling
  economics, same customer industries, similar scale at Rs 236 cr FY26 sales.
  Its FY26 collapse in OPM from 23% to 14% and ROCE to 7.8% is the live
  cautionary case for a small tooling name that loses operating leverage.
- BIRLA PRECISION TECHNOLOGIES (BIRLAPREC,
  https://www.screener.in/company/BIRLAPREC/consolidated/). Machine-tool
  accessories, tool holders and precision components, Rs 247 cr FY26 sales,
  OPM 7%, ROCE 9.4%. Nearest size-matched Indian comp and the low end of the
  quality ladder. It brackets TOTEM from below the way Kennametal brackets it
  from above.
All three URLs returned HTTP 200 on 2026-09-09. No listed Indian pure-play in
branded HSS round tooling exists other than these; Sandvik, Guhring and
Ceratizit do not list here.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority for every stage)
1. THE Q1 FY27 MARGIN BRIDGE. OPM moved 16.1% to 22.9% while raw-material cost
   reportedly worsened by 4.3 percentage points, with employee cost falling
   21.6% to 18.4% of sales and other expenses 30.6% to 22.5%. Verify each leg
   against the filed Q1 FY27 result. A margin gain built on other-expense
   compression is not the same asset as one built on mix.
2. THE INVENTORY BUILD. Inventory rose from Rs 31.9 cr to Rs 56.4 cr in FY26.
   Inventory days went 146 to 255, cash conversion cycle 91 to 152 days, and
   CFO fell from Rs 51 cr to Rs 28 cr. Verify from the FY26 annual report
   inventory note and ageing. Stock pre-built for the drill ramp and stock that
   did not sell look identical in the ratio and opposite in the thesis.
3. THE PROMOTER PLEDGE. 94.4% of the 73.85% promoter holding is pledged as at
   June 2026, promoter is Shapoorji Pallonji & Co. Verify the pledge
   disclosure, the pledgee, the obligation secured, and whether the operating
   company gives any guarantee or has any related-party exposure to the group.
   This is the governance item that can cap position size.
4. THE FY26 REVENUE AND EXCEPTIONAL ITEM. Screener shows FY26 revenue Rs 251 cr;
   a third-party read shows Rs 241 cr, up 7.9%, with a Rs 5.9 cr pre-tax Labour
   Codes charge inside FY26 profit. Verify the audited FY26 revenue basis and
   the exceptional item. Every growth rate and every margin in the run depends
   on which number is right.
