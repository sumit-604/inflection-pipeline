### 4C. The three most important exchanges

**Exchange 1 — Margin recovery timeline (Suhas Prabhu, Tr p.24).**
- *Q (Samrat/Pramod, paraphrased):* Why didn't profit follow the 30–40% ECS revenue growth; will consolidated operating margin settle at ~16% vs the historical 18–20%; what concrete steps restore the old margin?
- *A (verbatim core):* two drags — (1) ~150 bps GTM/front-end-leadership investment "expected to normalise by the end of this fiscal year… impact our EBITDA margins positively starting Q4 of '27"; (2) a ">USD 10 million ACV" outcome-based deal signed Dec'25, revenue from Q3, "1% to 1.5% favourable impact," so "Q4 should see our margins get back into that 19% to 20%… EBITDA margin." At PAT level, amortisation "lowered starting Q3 by about INR 50 crores every quarter."
- *Said:* a specific, bridged, quarter-dated EBITDA recovery to 19–20% by Q4 FY27.
- *Did NOT say:* the FY27 full-year exit margin; whether the >USD 10 mn deal's revenue is committed/take-or-pay; the EMS margin; any number attaching to the 16%-settle scenario Samrat floated (implicitly rejected, not addressed head-on).
- *Thesis implication:* This is the linchpin. If the two named bridge items land, FY28 is a margin-and-EPS-recovery year off a depressed FY26 base — the classic transition setup. If Q3/Q4 FY27 prints below ~19%, the "investment-driven dip" narrative weakens toward structural.
- *Follow-up we would have asked:* "What is the FY27 **full-year** EBITDA margin you are underwriting, and is the >USD 10 mn ACV recognised ratably or milestone-based?"

**Exchange 2 — Acquisition return discipline (Manish Gupta, Tr pp.22–23).**
- *Q (Santosh, paraphrased):* When approving acquisitions, does the board set a minimum return / ROCE / payback / FCF-conversion target over three years, including the return on the FY26 deals — a **quantitative** answer, please.
- *A (verbatim core):* "The board continues to look at EBITDA, ROCE, ROE… we factor that in our calls. However… we are willing to take short-term hits if we are convinced that finally we'll turn this around… strong governance process… investment subcommittee of the board."
- *Said:* a credible **process** answer (subcommittee, business case, board sign-off).
- *Did NOT say:* any **number** — no hurdle rate, no payback period, no post-integration ROCE for BioPharm. This was a direct quantitative question answered qualitatively. Graded **D on the specific ask.**
- *Thesis implication:* INR ~1,700 cr of cash is earmarked for more M&A across the lifecycle (Tr p.23), yet the board will not externally commit to a return hurdle. For an acquisition-led growth story, the **absence of a stated return threshold is the main capital-allocation watch item.** Not disqualifying (process discipline is real and BioPharm closed a month early), but it means acquisition value-creation must be verified ex-post from ROCE trend, not trusted ex-ante.
- *Follow-up:* "What post-integration ROIC did BioPharm underwrite to, and by when?"

**Exchange 3 — BioPharm economics and organic core (Manish + Suhas, Tr pp.18–19, 26).**
- *Q (Jasmeet):* Did BioPharm contribute ~90% of last quarter's topline (implying the pre-acquisition core stalled)? What did BioPharm cost?

Cost: Manish "INR 887 crores"; Suhas "US$104 million… USD 65 million paid at closing… pending earn-out payment of USD 19 million due in 2027." Ex-BioPharm organic growth "strong mid-teen to 18%" and "higher than FY25" (Tr p.26).
- *Said:* the core is growing double-digits organically; BioPharm is ~5.4% of FY26 revenue, not the growth engine the shareholder feared.
- *Did NOT say:* the exact organic-vs-inorganic split by quarter (management redirected to the Q3/Q4 FY26 earnings materials); BioPharm's standalone margin (only that its third-party cost ratio is 20–22% vs Indegene's 6–7%, Tr p.26).

- *Follow-up:* "What is BioPharm's own EBITDA margin, and is it accretive or dilutive at the consolidated level today?"
