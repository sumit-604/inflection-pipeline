## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Premier Polyfilm makes flexible PVC flooring, film and sheets. It reports one segment and has no subsidiaries (FY26 results, doc 6af25d23, pages 2 to 4). It converts PVC into finished rolls and sheets. The margin is the spread between input cost and selling price.

**What is changing?** Nothing that management has said. The corpus holds no earnings call, no investor presentation and zero guidance records. The only change is in the numbers. Q1 FY27 revenue, reported including GST, rose 34% to Rs 100.06 crore from Rs 74.44 crore. PAT rose 51% to Rs 9.08 crore from Rs 6.00 crore (results, doc 6af25d23, pages 2 to 4; Q1 FY27 board outcome, doc 3111b6c9, page 4).

**Why now?** Only the quarter makes it a candidate. FY26 PAT was Rs 31.88 crore. Q4 FY26 PAT was Rs 8.58 crore and Q1 FY27 Rs 9.08 crore (same pages). The run rate is rising.

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The Q1 growth must repeat, and it must come from volume or mix rather than a passing input price gap. The corpus does not say which.

**What breaks it?** The spread. A converter with no stated pricing power earns what the input price allows. A rise in polymer cost that the company cannot pass on would reverse the quarter.
