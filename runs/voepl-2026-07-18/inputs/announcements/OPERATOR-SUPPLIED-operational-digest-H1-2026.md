# VOEPL Operational Digest ~mid-Jan to mid-Jul 2026 — operator-supplied

PROVENANCE: relayed by operator mid-run 2026-07-18 as a plain-language
summary compiled from VOEPL's Q3 & Q4 FY26 investor presentations, concalls,
and exchange announcements. Status: DIRECTIONAL LEAD + CROSS-CHECK layer,
NON-ANCHORED. The Q3/Q4 FY26 concalls and the investor presentation ARE in
inputs/ (anchored) and each stage must read those PDFs itself; this digest
corroborates and points, it does not replace them. The specific Reg 30
announcement dates below (OCD 28-May-2026; mainboard migration 30-Jun-2026)
are events whose underlying exchange-filing PDFs were NOT collected; treat
those as directional-to-corroborate.

## 1. Financials (FY26 consolidated)
- Revenue Rs825.99 Cr (Rs8,259.9 Mn), +17.4% YoY.
- EBITDA Rs85.89 Cr (Rs858.9 Mn), margin 10.4% vs 8.6% FY25.
- PAT Rs15.03 Cr (Rs150.3 Mn), +22.4% YoY.
- Guidance: ~35-40% revenue CAGR over 3-5 yrs; EBITDA margin 9-10%; PAT
  margin +50-100 bps over ~2 yrs as Ind-AS transition normalises and scale.

## 2. Product mix / diversification
- AC was ~75% of revenue a year ago; FY26 ACs ~60%, non-AC ~40%.
- FY26 mix commentary: AC (IDU+ODU) ~60%; EMS (LED/PCBA/controllers/remotes)
  ~15%; commercial refrigeration (deep freezers) ~10%; components/washing
  machines/others ~7%; compressors the balance (first year).
- Stated long-term comfort: AC 50-60% of revenue.

## 3. Air conditioners (RAC)
- Installed AC capacity FY26 ~1.0 M units (IDU+ODU), incl. Nashik + acquired
  Chennai (Star Eltech) unit; plan to 1.8 M units by end FY27 (1.3 M by the
  coming AC season, then 1.8 M within FY27).
- Chennai: IDU+ODU, backward integrated except moulding; FY27 first year,
  ~5-6% of AC volumes.
- Utilisation: Q4 FY26 peak 90-95%; target 60-65% annualised on expanded base.
- ODM transition: fully moved OEM to ODM in AC; ~40-50% of FY26 AC revenue
  from ODM; AC customers currently 4, adding 1-2; Voltas remains anchor.
- AC EBITDA 6-8% (lower margin); copper/metal inflation largely passed
  through via conversion / quarterly pass-through pricing; ~10-15% price
  increases passed on across the chain.

## 4. Compressors (reciprocating refrigeration)
- Commercial production started January 2026.
- Q3 (Feb 2026): 50%+ utilisation, "three months ahead of schedule"; 2.8 M
  units capacity; ~60% of CY2026 capacity booked, targeting 70-80%.
- Q4 (Jun 2026): ~60% utilisation on 2.8 M; target 6.0 M units by end FY27;
  expansion funded by ~Rs150 Cr OCD in subsidiary Virtuoso Compressors (OCD
  Debenture Trust Deed signed 28-May-2026).
- Economics: asset turns ~3.5-4x; 2.8 M capacity ~Rs400 Cr revenue at full,
  ~Rs240 Cr at 60%. EBITDA ~5% now, guided 6-7% FY27, trending to low double
  digits (11-12%) over 4-5 yrs as backward integration rises 5-10% to ~60%.
- QCO/policy: imports of reciprocating compressors capped at 40% of FY24-25
  base till Mar 2027 (60% must be local). Management: VOEPL "as of now the
  only one doing reciprocating compressors in OEM/ODM space"; 2-3 competitors
  expected to localise around late FY27/FY28.

## 5. Commercial refrigeration (deep freezers)
- Facility commenced Q4 FY25, ramped in FY26. Capacity FY26 150,000 units/yr,
  scaling to 400,000 (phase 1 250k, phase 2 400k). Added glass-top range;
  assembling premium frost-free/multi-door refs for select customers. Q3:
  existing capacity fully booked; deferred expansion now pursued in FY27.

## 6. EMS & lighting
- LED lighting, controller boards, remotes, EV-charger electronics pilots.
- SMT capacity 4,00,000 CPH now; to 8,00,000 CPH ~Aug 2026, 12,00,000 CPH by
  end FY27. 4L CPH supports ~Rs150-180 Cr top line. Utilisation 70-80% FY26.

## 7. Components / polymers / backward integration
- Chennai (VPPL polymers/components) EBITDA positive from Q3 FY26; Sanand
  polymers EBITDA positive late FY26. New Nashik in-house tool room
  operational. In-house content in AC/components ~60%, targeting 75-80%.

## 8. Washing machines & other new products
- Semi-automatic washing machines: pilots done, field trials in Q3 FY26;
  ramp Apr-May 2026 (Q1 FY27); capacity supports ~Rs100 Cr at peak; FY27
  utilisation guide 50-70%. Water dispensers scaling. EV-charger controller
  pilot production started.

## 9. Aggregate capacity / revenue potential post-capex
- FY27 planned capacities: AC 1.8 M, compressors 6.0 M, deep freezers 400k,
  EMS 12,00,000 CPH. Management: suite can support >Rs2,500 Cr annual revenue
  at peak over time (more than two years out; FY28 may not fully reach it).

## 10. Capital structure / listing (operationally relevant)
- Compressor capex: ~Rs150 Cr OCD in Virtuoso Compressors Pvt Ltd (DTD
  28-May-2026).
- Other capex: EMS ~Rs25 Cr; deep freezers Rs25-50 Cr; AC Rs40-50 Cr.
- Net debt: management expects +Rs50-60 Cr at holdco FY27, plus Rs150 Cr OCD
  at compressor subsidiary.
- Enabling approval to raise up to Rs250 Cr equity (NOT a committed raise).
- Migration to BSE Mainboard effective 1-Jul-2026 (announcement 30-Jun-2026);
  NSE in-principle expected; earlier guided mainboard listing H1 FY27.

## Takeaway (operator's words)
Stabilised/scaled after a weak H1 FY26 to +17-18% revenue and 10.4% EBITDA;
commercialised compressors (~60% util, expanding to 6M via OCDs); non-AC now
~40% of revenue; capacity added across AC/EMS/deep freezers/compressors
targeting a >Rs2,500 Cr platform; held AC growth/margins through RM inflation
via ODM shift and backward integration.
