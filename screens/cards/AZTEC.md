## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Aztec sells industrial coding and marking printers. These print batch numbers, expiry dates, MRP and QR codes onto pipes, packs and bottles on the production line. The printer is the razor. The inks, make-up fluid and wash solution are the blades. In FY26 consolidated revenue of Rs 96.53 crore split into printers Rs 24.66 crore, make-up and wash Rs 37.95 crore, ink Rs 20.39 crore, services Rs 4.30 crore and spares Rs 9.17 crore (AR26 p.194). Consumables were 60% of revenue. Management puts the annual consumables spend per printer at Rs 80,000 to Rs 1.2 lakh, about the price of the printer itself (T-Jun26 p.12).

The company began in 2013 by importing Leadtech printers from China (AR26 p.18). It still calls itself exclusive distributor for Lead Tech (Zhuhai) Electronic Co. (D-Jun26 p.16). Printers are assembled at Kanera from partly imported parts. In 2024 it bought Jet Inks Pvt Ltd, a Chennai ink maker and dealer, for Rs 17 crore (AR26 p.200).

**What is changing?** Management claims a move from distributor to technology owner: in-house print heads, laser components and AZTRACE, a traceability platform launched at the AGM on 10-Sep-2026 (filings/2026-09-14_press-release-Sep26.pdf p.2).

**Why now?** Operating EBITDA before other income rose from Rs 9.86 crore to Rs 13.09 crore in FY26 (derived from AR26 p.171). Consumables grew about 20% while printer revenue fell 12.5% (AR26 p.194).

**What must be true?** The consumables tail must keep compounding faster than printer placements slow. The technology claims must start to show as disclosed revenue.

**What breaks it?** Price competition on printers, import dependence, and governance. Growth guidance has missed by a wide margin twice. The subsidiary was classified NPA by its bank for seven months, and the annual report does not say so.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "Manufacturing of Printers, trading of inks used therein and consumables thereof" (AR26 p.203). Verticals in FY26: extrusion 31.4%, food, pharma and FMCG 26.2%, industrial automation 12.5%, agriculture 8.7%, packaging 4.6%, others 16.6% (AR26 p.13). Exports were Rs 7.50 crore standalone (AR26 p.153).

Gross margin is flat: material cost was 54.1% of revenue in FY26 and 53.7% in FY25 (derived from AR26 p.171). Imports on a CIF basis were Rs 38.99 crore, 47% of standalone revenue (AR26 p.153). The economic engine is placements times the consumables pull per placed printer.

**Archetype: no library archetype fits cleanly.** Nearest is the build-to-spec component maker: printer placements act as design wins, consumables per printer act as content per unit, and imported parts make input-cost pass-through live. The true engine is an installed-base annuity, which the library lacks. Flag for operator ruling.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Forming in consumables, absent in hardware.** Each cartridge carries a chip that pairs it to the printer, so a rival's fluid will not run (T-May25 p.8). Management claims 95% to 98% customer retention, held by a 12-hour service promise within 100 km (T-May25 p.6 to 7). A BIS licence takes a new entrant 6 to 12 months (T-Nov24 p.12).

The hardware side has no pricing power. The MD said Aztec prices "slightly less margin" than Control Print to win customers (T-Nov24 p.28). The CFO said larger rivals give printers away free and recover on consumables (T-May25 p.7). Industry size is about Rs 900 to 1,200 crore with Aztec near 10% (T-Nov24 p.9).

## 4. The promoters

Pulin Vaidhya (CMD) holds 55.63% and his wife Amisha Vaidhya (WTD) 18.92%; his father Kumudchandra Vaidhya holds 100 shares and sits as non-executive director (AR26 p.183). Promoters held 74.76% at Mar-2026 (market-data-2026-10-18.md, screener.in service record). Pledge: NOT FOUND (no SAST filing held; AR26 silent). Three of five directors are one family, and the MD sits on the audit committee (AR26 p.87, p.89).

**Related-party dependence, from audited notes (AR26 p.151 to 152):**
- Fluidtech Corporation, "Entity in which KMP are interested": purchases and job work Rs 8.12 crore in FY26, Rs 8.11 crore in FY25. It also rents space from Aztec.
- Axis Druckfarben LLP, "Entity in which Relative of KMP are interested": sales Rs 6.49 crore (6.7% of consolidated revenue), purchases Rs 1.14 crore, receivable Rs 1.47 crore.
- Director pay: Rs 120 lakh to Pulin and Rs 90 lakh to Amisha, 28% of FY26 consolidated PAT. The AGM sought a cap of "Not Exceeding Rs. 350 Lacs p.a." each for three years (AR26 p.69, p.74).
- Rent to both promoters Rs 6.30 lakh each, with a new cap of Rs 15 lakh each a year for a guest house (AR26 p.82 to 83).

Skin in the game is high. The flows around it are large for a Rs 7.41 crore profit.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM R2, a cost-positioned assembler and distributor.** It prices printers below the market leader, imports nearly half its revenue in parts, earns mid-teens ROCE, and keeps a consumables tail.

**Claimed TO: R3 to R4, an IP-owning technology partner.** Management's claim, once: the deck calls the strategy "our pivot from a 'Distributor' to a Technology Partner" (D-Jan26 p.3).

What the filings show:
- Consumables and services grew; printers shrank (AR26 p.194). That is a real move up the annuity curve, inside R2.
- Track and trace earned Rs 1.75 to 1.95 crore in FY25 with a target of Rs 4.5 to 5 crore (T-May25 p.14). FY26 delivery: NOT DISCLOSED.
- The print-head partner is unnamed, "a Global Technology Major" (D-Jan26 p.12). AZTRACE carries "No specific revenue, customer-adoption or timeline commitments" (Sep-26 PR p.4).
- Inks are booked as "Traded Goods (Ink)", Rs 20.39 crore, while Jet Inks sold only Rs 0.60 crore to the parent (AR26 p.146, p.152). The MD claims ink is "100% backward integration" (T-Jun26 p.4). [INFERENCE] The make-up and wash line may rest on job work from promoter-linked Fluidtech.

Reading: **R2, with the claimed climb unproven.** The two readings separate on one disclosure: track and trace plus AZTRACE revenue in H1 FY27.

## 8. Proof check

**SME reporting is half-yearly, so the two-quarter test becomes a two-half test.** H1 FY26 against H1 FY25: revenue up 6.2%, PAT down 7.8%. H2 FY26 against H2 FY25: revenue up 12.4%, PAT up 6.6%. H2 ran below H1 in both years, which management attributes to seasonality and customer destocking (T-May25 p.16 to 17).

The proof point that exists is the consumables tail. The proof point that the transition needs, technology revenue, is absent. H1 FY27 is due by mid-November 2026 and is not in the corpus.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Subsidiary NPA, missing from the annual report.** Jet Inks' bank account was classified NPA from 1-Aug-2025 to 9-Mar-2026 over a dispute on "security arrangements between the erstwhile and incoming promoter groups" (filings/2026-03-11_presentation-Mar26.pdf p.2). AR26 says only that Jet Inks "returned to full operational capacity" (AR26 p.109).
- **Draft notes published to the exchange.** That filing ends: "When jet inks purchased, aztec needs to service bank liabilities, 1 personal g on ey promoters 2 land guarantee Company sale" (same file, p.4, read visually). D-Jun26 p.10 carries "put words more growth oriented kind of".
- **Guarantee contradiction.** The board approved a Rs 7.65 crore corporate guarantee to ICICI for Jet Inks (filings/2026-02-04_board-outcome-Feb26.pdf p.2). The directors' report says the company "has not given any guarantee or security" (AR26 p.86).
- **Capital tied in a weak subsidiary.** Rs 17 crore equity plus a Rs 10.78 crore loan with "no formal schedule or agreement" for repayment (AR26 p.117, p.143), for Rs 0.26 crore of PAT. Jet Inks also carries a static Rs 4.94 crore "Corporate loan Given" to related parties since acquisition (AR26 p.191, p.201).
- **Receivables.** Rs 7.67 crore of Rs 27.33 crore is over one year old, including Rs 3.83 crore disputed but "considered good", unprovided (AR26 p.192).
- **Bank statements disagree with books every quarter**, by up to Rs 1.09 crore on receivables at Mar-2026 (AR26 p.116).
- **Pay disclosure conflicts.** Annexure F shows the MD at Rs 78.57 lakh and the WTD at Rs 60.57 lakh (AR26 p.105); the audited note shows Rs 120 lakh and Rs 90 lakh (AR26 p.151).
- **Two CFOs.** Harsh Rawal signs as CFO; Devraj Pandya runs the calls as "Group CFO" at three times the pay (AR26 p.124, p.151).
- **Installed base arithmetic.** 5,000+ in FY25 becomes 8,000+ in FY26 with about 1,450 printers sold (D-Jan26 p.5; D-Jun26 p.5; queried at T-Jun26 p.3).
- **Dilution.** 1.42 lakh options at Rs 15 against a Rs 109 price (filings/2026-05-29_ESOP-grant.pdf p.2). Equity for capex is "a long shot" (T-Jun26 p.4).
- **IPO proceeds: no deviation.** Rs 24.12 crore used: Rs 14 crore Jet Inks, Rs 3.72 crore debt, Rs 6.40 crore general purposes (filings/2025-11-14_results-H1FY26.pdf p.16; FY26 results, recovered image).
- Positive: unmodified audit opinions and no key audit matters in FY26 (AR26 p.111, p.161).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Consolidated revenue grew 9.2% and PAT fell 2.1%. Technology revenue is undisclosed.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** Flat PAT comes from depreciation on new assets, goodwill amortisation and lower treasury income. The operating line improved. The governance items are flags, not the optic.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market cap Rs 148 crore (screener.in service record, 18-Oct-2026) is 20.0x FY26 PAT. The R2 neighbourhood is 15 to 17x; 20x already sits at R3.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** The market pays the R3 multiple for an R2 business whose climb is narrated, not filed.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The bull case carries real evidence. Consumables rose about 20%, services 48%, operating EBITDA 33%, and cash flow beat profit in FY26. The bear case carries the same bar and wins. FY26 revenue came in 20% to 26% under the Nov-2024 guide, and the 20 to 25% growth claim of late 2025 delivered 9.2%. The subsidiary absorbed Rs 27.78 crore and earns almost nothing. A seven-month NPA went unmentioned in the annual report. Promoter-linked entities sit on both the supply and sales side. At 20x trailing for single-digit growth, the price leaves no room for those flags.

**Load-bearing facts, if the name is reopened**
1. H1 FY27 revenue growth and the consumables share against H1 FY26 (Rs 48.92 crore).
2. Track and trace and AZTRACE revenue, disclosed in rupees.
3. What Fluidtech supplies, at what price, and whether Axis Druckfarben is an arm's-length customer.
4. Whether the Rs 7.65 crore guarantee was executed, and the recovery of Jet Inks' Rs 4.94 crore related-party loan.
5. Provision status on the Rs 3.83 crore disputed receivables.

**What would change the view**
- Two halves of 15%+ revenue growth with technology revenue disclosed, plus a clean RPT note, moves the name to WATCH.
- A rights issue, a fresh bank classification event at Jet Inks, or a receivable write-off confirms PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a `/step1`. It is not a position.
