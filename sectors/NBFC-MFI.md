# SECTOR MEMORY — NBFC-MFI (Indian microfinance)

Durable per-sector knowledge, appended (never overwritten) by every concall,
quarterly, and annual-report analysis of an NBFC-MFI, via the SECTOR KNOWLEDGE
block (see CLAUDE.md "NARRATIVE + SECTOR"). Read as SECTOR MEMORY at the start
of any run in this sector. Memory to weigh, never anchored evidence.

## STANDING SECTOR MODEL (compounding view; revise as evidence accrues)
- Cycle position: FY26 was a stress trough (Q1 FY26 the worst quarter, sector
  credit costs ~4.5%). FY27 is early recovery: collection efficiency back near
  99.9% X-bucket, sector PAR falling. Much of the FY27 YoY profit growth across
  MFIs is a low-base recovery, not fresh strength; always separate base effect
  from underlying.
- Unit economics: earn the spread between lending yield (~21-23%) and cost of
  funds (~8%); NIM ~13-15%. Through-cycle ROA of 2.5-3.5% is healthy; ~4.7% is
  a peak, not a run-rate. High leverage is normal (D/E 3-4x); CRAR ~25%+ is
  comfortable.
- Seasonality: Q1 (Apr-Jun) is a seasonally SOFT disbursement quarter; Q4 is
  the strongest. A QoQ disbursement dip in Q1 is normal, not weakness on its
  own. Q4 return ratios flatter (lowest credit cost, lumpy assignment income).
- Structural risks / what breaks companies here:
  - Over-indebtedness is THE risk. Blow-ups are preceded by AUM growing via
    larger tickets on a near-static borrower base rather than new borrowers.
    Watch active-client growth vs AUM growth, new-client run-rate, average
    ticket, and active-loans/exposure per borrower — these lead GNPA.
  - Asset-quality optics: GNPA (quoted on-book) can be "cleaned" via write-offs
    and ARC sales. The honest read requires the ECL / stage-movement walk
    (opening, additions, gross write-offs, ARC/DA-of-NPA, recoveries, closing)
    and the Stage-2 book, not the headline number.
  - Direct Assignment / securitisation: MFIs sell loan pools to banks for
    gain-on-sale and to move ~20-25% of the book off-balance-sheet. Inflates
    reported ROA in lumpy quarters; headline GNPA understates true exposure.
    Always ask for assignment VOLUME, not just DA income.
  - Geographic concentration: state-level events and loan-waiver politics are a
    recurring shock; concentration in UP/Bihar/Assam/West Bengal is common.
  - Foreign borrowing brings INR-USD hedge risk that can swing reported profit
    (large FX finance-cost / fair-value lines with no hedge note = watch item).
  - "Management overlay" buffer provisions are often excluded from headline
    ROA/ROE (non-GAAP), flattering returns; a growing overlay is a fresh charge
    that depresses reported PAT while the headline ratio adds it back.
- Regulation: RBI microfinance guardrails (caps on number of lenders per
  borrower and on indebtedness) govern how fast the sector can grow;
  priority-sector status is what lets banks fund MFIs cheaply via DA.
- Competitive frame: peers include CreditAccess Grameen, Spandana, Muthoot
  Microfin, Fusion, Satin. On their own decks MFIs rank themselves favourably;
  treat peer comparisons as self-selected until cross-checked against peer
  filings.
- Reading tricks: separate base effect from underlying on any big YoY; demand
  the ECL walk and assignment volume; check borrower count vs AUM; read return
  ratios on the reported (overlay-inclusive, GAAP) basis, not the adjusted one.

## RUN LOG (dated, newest first; one block per analysis)

### 2026-07-31 SATIN Q1 FY27 earnings concall (run: runs/satin-q1fy27; Role 5, credibility Grade B)
- Buffer/overlay cycle-proofing is now an explicit MFI management philosophy AND a
  discretionary earnings lever: Satin runs on-book provision Rs 250 Cr vs RBI-required
  Rs 152 Cr, plus an overlay built "through good quarters" with NO committed ceiling.
  Reported credit cost/ROA are therefore partly discretionary (1.97% ex-overlay vs 3.06%
  reported). Always demand reported AND ex-overlay, plus a buffer policy tied to Stage-2.
- Guardrails 2.0 sector share shift: bank share of micro-loans fell to 26.4% from 32.6%
  YoY while NBFC-MFI rose to 43.7% from 38.9%; industry GLP stabilised ~Rs 3.31 lakh Cr
  after 8 quarters of decline; 95% of exposure now with borrowers having <=3 lenders.
  Specialist lenders now set sector quality; cycle position repair -> early expansion.
- NatCat (natural-catastrophe) insurance is a live MFI risk-mitigation tool: Satin's
  Assam flood exposure (~44,000 borrowers, Rs 149 Cr) was ~Rs 97 Cr NatCat-covered, net
  residual ~1%. NatCat + overlay can contain a state-level weather shock to a low
  single-digit net hit; check peers for equivalent coverage before pricing weather risk.
- DA (direct assignment) run-rate norm ~20-22% of AUM; DA income is quarterly-lumpy (Q4
  seasonally heavy) but the assigned book is steady. Use the band to judge whether
  gain-on-sale income is structural or timing-pulled-forward.
- Management-named stable-state bands (weigh as guidance, not fact): steady-state NIM
  ~14.35-14.50%; stable-state credit-cost aspiration 2.5-3.0% for unsecured MFI; reported
  credit-cost guide 3.0-3.5% incl. buffer; standalone ROA 3.5-4% reported; new-branch
  breakeven ~1,000 customers (~9 months). Write-offs can pull GNPA down even as reported
  credit cost rises when the buffer is being built (Satin: write-off Rs 127 Cr > slippage
  Rs 49 Cr drove the 90 bps GNPA fall while credit cost rose on overlay).
- Over-indebtedness watch is the MFI risk most likely to go UNASKED on a congratulatory
  boutique-analyst call: whether AUM growth is new borrowers vs larger tickets on a
  near-static borrower base. Build it into the question list every quarter regardless of
  whether the Street raises it (all 7 analysts on this call missed it).

### 2026-07-30 SATIN quarterly results Q1 FY27 (run: runs/satin-q1fy27)
- 20th consecutive profitable quarter. Consolidated PAT Rs 123 Cr (+172% YoY)
  but that is largely a Q1 FY26 trough base; PAT fell 24% QoQ and return ratios
  cooled from the Q4 peak (consol ROA 4.71% -> 3.30%, ROE 23.31% -> 16.75%).
  Confirmed the sector rule: 4.7% ROA was a peak, sustainable is low-to-mid 3s.
- AUM +27.5% YoY but active clients only +3% (34 vs 33 lakh); new-client adds
  fell every quarter since Q2 FY26 (2.5 -> 1.5 lakh); ~Rs 61,000 JLG ticket;
  AUM/loan-officer Rs 1.08 -> 1.39 Cr. Textbook ticket-led growth on a static
  borrower base = over-indebtedness early warning ahead of a clean 2.18% GNPA.
- GNPA improvement (3.74% -> 2.18%) unverifiable from the filing: no gross
  write-offs, no ECL/stage walk, no ARC/DA-of-NPA volume, Stage-2 undisclosed;
  ~25% of AUM off-book. Reinforces "demand the ECL walk" rule.
- Rs 36 Cr management overlay (grew Rs 15 Cr QoQ) excluded from headline
  ROA/ROE (~+300 bps flattery). Rs 62.43 Cr FX finance-cost credit offset a
  Rs 57.11 Cr FV loss with no hedge-policy note (INR-USD risk live).
- Q1 is a soft disbursement quarter (disb -21% QoQ) — seasonal, disclosed only
  in tables, not narrated. Sector PAR improving, collection efficiency 99.9%.
- Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAVEATS (asset-quality mechanism INDETERMINATE cap).
