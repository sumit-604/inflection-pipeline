### 2B. Utilisation trajectory
~80-85% (Dec-2025, Q3 FY26 call) → ~80% average FY26 (Q4 FY26 call) → target ~90% FY27 (Q4 FY26 call). All 🎙️ management-stated utilisation figures; no independently audited capacity-utilisation metric found in the AR.

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic
Historical fixed asset turnover (FY25, 📄 AR-derived): Revenue from Operations ₹261.82cr (AR Note 22, p.159) ÷ Net block Total PP&E ₹51.90cr (AR Note 3, p.157) = **5.05x**.

Applying this to the FY27-guided capex programme (₹40-52cr, midpoint ₹46cr, 🎙️ management guidance, Q4 FY26 call):
₹46cr × 5.05x = **₹232.3cr implied incremental revenue capacity**, or **~70% above FY26 revenue** (₹333.18cr, per Q1 FY27 results outcome comparative column, 📄).

This mechanical figure should be treated with material skepticism: (a) the FY25 asset base generating the 5.05x ratio is machinery-heavy; the new capex is weighted toward building/leasehold/assembly-shop space, which historically turns over slower than plant & machinery; (b) management's own unit-capacity guidance (2,500 machines → 3,500-4,000 machines across Phase 1+2) implies a more grounded **+40% to +60%** capacity uplift, before any NEXA-mix ASP effect. The credible range is therefore 40-60%, not the ~70% mechanical output; I report the mechanical arithmetic per instruction but flag it as an overstatement.

### 2D. New geography/market entries
Export revenue +202% YoY off a <1% base (📄, AR Segment Note); EMO Hannover exhibition attendance (Sept-2025, Chairman's message, 🎙️); domestic branch expansion into Baroda, Gurgaon (opened), Nashik, Kolkata (upcoming) (Q4 FY26 call, 🎙️). This is domestic network densification more than genuine new-geography entry; true export scale-up remains aspirational.

## SECTION 3: THE 20-CATEGORY SCAN

**FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY**

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability.** Macpower supplies CNC machines to 35 defence factories (DRDO/ordnance, 200+ machines cumulative) and 6 aviation/aerospace customers including HAL and ISRO (100+ machines cumulative), named end-uses include BrahMos, Chandrayaan-3, T-90 modernisation, BSF (Q3 FY26 call, 🎙️). No qualification-timeline language, global-manufacturer-count claim, or long-lead-equipment evidence found. No PO or contract copy corroborates the installed-base figures; a recent HAL order was quoted at only ~₹3cr with a ~6-month lead time (Q4 FY26 call, 🎙️), suggesting current defence revenue is genuinely small ("single digit % of total revenue," Q4 FY26 call). **Weak.** Evidence: 🎙️.

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline.** FY25 AR Annexure IV (Technology Absorption, p.68-71) discloses R&D expenditure as **NIL** (both capital and revenue) and states no technology was imported. Intangible assets register (AR Note 3, p.157) totals only ~₹43.77 lakh net (technical know-how ₹26.10L + design ₹0.40L + software ₹8.20L + CWIP technical know-how ₹9.37L) — a negligible IP base. This directly contradicts the Q3 FY26 concall claim that "R&D team doubled YoY" and the Q4 FY26 call's reference to "₹1.8cr" of design/pattern intangibles (which does not reconcile to the AR's ~₹0.44cr intangible base). **NO EVIDENCE FOUND** for genuine IP moat formation; flagging the claim/disclosure contradiction explicitly. Evidence: 📄 (the Nil disclosure itself) contradicting 🎙️ (the "doubled" and "₹1.8cr" claims).

**A3 — Process innovation.** No yield disclosures, unit-cost-vs-quality data, or automation/waste-to-value metrics found. **NO EVIDENCE FOUND.**

**A4 — Product platform / modular architecture.** Consistent quarterly cadence of new model announcements across all three concalls plus the Jul-2026 presentation (DCM, LX, TOM, V-series, GX-series families), suggesting a shared platform architecture being extended across weight classes and process types (turning, milling, double-column, multi-tasking). No SKU count or launch-frequency metric is disclosed, and no independent evidence (patents, tooling investment, R&D capitalisation) corroborates the platform claim beyond the launch cadence itself. **Moderate** on the strength of the repeated, consistent pattern across three independent quarters; evidence remains 🎙️ throughout.

**B2 — Qualification lock-in.** No IATF/AS9100/NADCAP/cGMP certification, design-in language, customer-funded tooling, or post-qualification multi-year agreement disclosed in any document reviewed. **NO EVIDENCE FOUND.**

**B3 — Supply chain network effect.** No platform/hub-and-spoke evidence. **NO EVIDENCE FOUND.**

**G2 — WC improvement trajectory.** This category shows **deterioration, not improvement**: the injected B01 Gate 0 block already flags cumulative CFO/PAT of 0.4538 (<0.50 deal-breaker threshold), driven by FY25 capex spike and an FY26 inventory build; the Q4 FY26 call describes a first-time finished-goods inventory of ~80-82 machines (~₹100cr) held because revenue cannot yet be recognised pending customer bank-loan/subsidy paperwork (🎙️, and consistent with the audited FY25 inventory-swing already visible in the Cash Flow Statement, where inventory absorbed ₹18.34cr of cash, 📄). **Score 0 / negative signal**, not counted as an active category.

## SECTION 4: REGULATORY & POLICY TAILWINDS (R1)

**4A. Regulatory approvals in pipeline.** Defence/aerospace vendor status with DRDO, ordnance factories, HAL, ISRO is referenced repeatedly across concalls (🎙️) but no specific registration/empanelment document, approval number, or competitor-comparison was found. Land-use approvals for the 60-acre plot are "~90% obtained" per the Chairman's message (AR p.18-19, 🎙️/qualitative), with signing itself still pending. **4B. Government policy tailwinds.** A new Gujarat state industrial/defence policy is under formulation; management is deliberately delaying the 60-acre land signing to benefit from a lower effective land cost under this policy (Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26 calls, 🎙️). The specific subsidy percentages (up to 25% capital subsidy on plant & machinery/building, 7% interest subsidy) referenced in operator context are **NOT FOUND** in any anchored document reviewed for this run (they derive from the 21-Jul-2026 NSE announcement and a Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 reference outside inputs/) — flagged as an input gap. Duration, enrolment status, and whether competitors share the benefit: **NOT FOUND** in anchored sources.

**4C. Regulatory moat assessment.** Emerging, not active. Time to kick in: multi-year, and the timeline has already slipped three times on the land-policy dependency alone. Sustainability: low differentiation — any state-level industrial policy or defence-indigenization tailwind would likely be available to other domestic CNC machine-tool manufacturers competing for the same tenders, not exclusive to Macpower.
