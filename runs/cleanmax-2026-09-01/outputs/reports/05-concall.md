### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

The one clear miss (Bikaner curtailment) draws external-blame (PGCIL/grid, "outside our control," "sub-judice" for DSM) — but this is a legitimate external attribution (grid congestion is not within a generator's control), and management volunteers the bad number unprompted each quarter (it appears in Kuldeep Jain's opening remarks in Q1 FY27, not only in response to analyst pressure) and quantifies the Rs-crore / % EBITDA impact every time, including the worsening trend. That is honest-admission behaviour layered on top of an external-blame framing, not deflection.

Separately, management explicitly volunteers a self-critical admission with no analyst prompting for it specifically: asked in Q1 FY27 whether organisational capacity has any gaps, Kuldeep Jain says "it would be wrong to say that we have no gap…someone is lying if they said that," and discloses that the board's NRC subcommittee reviewed "five-six areas of gaps" the prior Friday. This is a genuine "we have work to do" admission, not deflection.

Weaker spots: the Osaka Gas JV and land-acquisition metric are simply not brought back up by management (silence, not denial); and in the same Q1 FY27 call, EBITDA is described with three different labels and two different Rs-crore figures in close proximity without reconciliation (see Section 4D).

Pattern classification: **balanced** (external-blame where the cause genuinely is external, offered proactively and quantified; real self-admission of organisational gaps; but a few load-bearing metrics simply go quiet rather than being explicitly closed out).

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. Competitor commentary and credibility

- Implicit competitor framing (Q3 FY26): CleanMax's superior capex-to-EBITDA ratio (5.8x vs an industry ~7x cited by a retail-investor question) is attributed to a "retail vs wholesale" business-model distinction — direct C&I contracting at a tariff premium vs reverse-auction SECI/discom bidding by utility-scale peers. Consistent, credible, and repeated with the same logic in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 (EBITDA margin 83-84% vs utility peers' 89-91%, offset by higher tariff and superior cash ROE).
- Explicit named-competitor commentary (Q1 FY27 only): Adani, Reliance and NTPC's stated interest in entering C&I is acknowledged directly (Nirmal, Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC). Management's rebuttal cites the 15-year focus, 600+ customer base with 80% repeat-business rate, wind+solar co-location capability across the four biggest industrial states, and market fragmentation (leader's own share is only ~14%, "not a winner-takes-all market"). Reasonably argued but self-serving; no quantification of how large-player entry might compress future tariffs or win rates is offered.
- Market-share figure sourcing softens over time: Q3 FY26 cites "12% all-India market share" with an explicit source ("industry report" in the DRHP). Q1 FY27 cites "14%…as per some recently released statistics" with no named source — a specificity downgrade worth flagging.

### 3B. Industry/market intelligence

- ALMM/ALCM module-and-cell policy transitions: tariff impact of 7-10% cited for the pre-2026 transition (Q3 FY26); ALMM2 deferred to 31-Dec-2026, ~Rs60 lakh/MW cost benefit for projects able to commission before the cutoff, with management "pulling forward" commissioning to capture it (Q1 FY27).
- DSM (Deviation Settlement Mechanism): a new, industry-wide regulatory mechanism, described as onerous, currently sub-judice, with MNRE/Ministry of Power reportedly in active dialogue to resolve it (Q4 FY26); still unresolved and unquantified as of Q1 FY27.
- Data-center demand-to-RE-capacity conversion ratio: management states 1 GW of hyperscaler IT load requires ~1.5 GW of round-the-clock power, translating to ~6 GW of new RE generation capacity and an estimated Rs40,000 Cr capital investment (Q1 FY27) — a striking macro figure worth independent verification.
- Conventional C&I TAM sizing: only 7-8% of India's industrial power is currently sourced via bilateral green PPAs, against an estimated Rs3 lakh Cr EBITDA-pool-equivalent market at ~Rs3.6/unit generation-bus-bar pricing (Q1 FY27).
- Wind PLF improved (32%→35%) in FY26 attributed to a strong wind season; management flags El Nino-linked lower-wind/higher-solar-radiation dynamics as a forward consideration (Q4 FY26).
- Cross-subsidy surcharge / Draft Electricity Amendment Bill and banking-norm changes: raised once (Q3 FY26), quantified at a combined max 1.5% of EBITDA impact if fully enacted; not revisited since.
