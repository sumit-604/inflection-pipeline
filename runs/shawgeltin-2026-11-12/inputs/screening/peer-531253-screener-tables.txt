About
Incorporated in 1973, India Gelatine & Chemicals Ltd is engaged in manufacturing and export of Ossein, Di-Calcium Phosphate (DCP)& Gelatine.
Key Points
Product Mix and Market Exposure: [1]
The company manufactures gelatine,
Di-Calcium Phosphate (DCP), ossein
and fish collagen. In FY25, gelatine
contributed 66% of product sales,
DCP 23% , ossein 10.8% , while fish
collagen remained only 0.2% —indicating
that the earlier Everpure marine collagen
launch has not yet become a meaningful
revenue stream.
About 60% of revenue came from outside India , with the Far East being the largest export region; customers contributing at least 10% each have collectively accounted for 34.2% of product sales , creating exposure to export pricing, currency and customer concentration. [2]
