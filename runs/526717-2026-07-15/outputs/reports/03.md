**New finding — EBITDA figure inflated by other income in the company's own Directors' Report table.** The Directors'
Report's summary table (AR p.6) labels a line "Profit/(Loss) before Interest, Depreciation & Taxation (EBDITA)" as
₹3,991.26 lakh (consolidated FY25). Reconstructing operating EBITDA directly from the P&L (Revenue − Cost of
materials − Δ inventory − Employee cost − Other expense, excluding Other Income) gives **₹3,523.64 lakh** — a
difference of ₹467.62 lakh, which is almost exactly the consolidated Other Income line (₹467.78 lakh, Note 24).
**The company's own headline "EBDITA" figure in the Directors' Report includes Other Income**, overstating apparent
operating profitability by roughly 13%. The correctly-scoped operating EBITDA figure (₹3,523.64 lakh / ₹35.24 crore)
matches the independent rating agency's EBITDA figure exactly (Infomerics rating PDF p.3, ₹35.24 crore FY25),
confirming the rating agency used the clean operating definition while the company's own investor-facing table did
not. 🟡 Watch — a mild but real presentation-quality issue, additive to Stage 2's Rank-14 stale-template finding as
a further instance of imprecise self-reporting.

### 2B. RPT map — verification and extension
Stage 2's RPT concentration figures (46.6%/37.5% of standalone/consolidated revenue, 62.6%/70.4% of COGS, the
₹3,320.29 lakh 0%-interest loan, and the >2x-net-worth cross-guarantees) were not independently re-derived from the
scanned RPT note pages in this pass (time-boxed to primary statements and CARO); no contradiction was found in
anything cross-checked. **New finding — K.P. Woven ownership percentage, previously "NOT FOUND" in Stage 2, is now
resolved.** Form AOC-1 (Annexure A to Directors' Report) states: "Extent of Shareholding (in percentage): 98.45% of
total voting rights, 51.33% of total capital" (AR p.15). This differs slightly from the rating agency's stated
consolidation percentage of 51.23% (Infomerics rating PDF p.7) — a small (0.1pp) but real discrepancy between the
two disclosed ownership percentages, and **the voting-rights/capital-percentage split itself (98.45% vs 51.33%) is
unusual and unexplained anywhere in the filing** — consistent with either a differential-voting-rights structure or
a residual minority holding of a separate share class connected to the CIRP resolution-plan mechanics, but the
mechanism is NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT. This answers Stage 2's Question-for-Management #3 in part (the percentage now
exists) while opening a new, narrower question (why voting % and capital % diverge by 47 points).

**New finding — the MD&A's "new subsidiary in Malaysia" is not actually a subsidiary.** The MD&A's Opportunities
section states "Our new subsidiary in Malaysia promises to strengthen our international exposure" (AR p.57). Form
AOC-1 Part B (Associates and Joint Ventures) instead lists "HCP PLASTENE BULKPACK PLT," a Malaysian entity, as a
**60%-held Associate/Joint Venture** (equity-method consolidated, not a subsidiary), acquired October 2023, with
investment of ₹5.50 lakh, net worth attributable ₹4.73 lakh, and a loss for the year of ₹(4.31) lakh — whose 60%
share, ₹(2.59) lakh, reconciles exactly to the Consolidated P&L's "Share of Profit/(Loss) of Joint Venture using
Equity Method" line of ₹(2.59) lakh (AR p.16, AR p.125). The cash figures reconcile cleanly; the terminology does
not. 🟡 Watch — another small instance of imprecise disclosure language, consistent with Stage 2's Rank-14 pattern.

**New finding — Secretarial Auditor flagged two RPT/disclosure-timing non-compliances during FY25.** The Directors'
Report reproduces the Secretarial Auditor's observations verbatim: "Non-compliance of Regulation 23(9) of SEBI LODR
Regulation as there was delay in submission of related party transaction details for the half year ended on
September 30, 2024," and separately, "The Company has not complied with the Regulation 30… as the outcome of the
Board meeting… were not submitted within the prescribed timeline" (AR p.13). Management's response to both was a
generic assurance of future timeliness, with no root-cause explanation. 🔴 Red Flag addition — this is a *procedural*
compliance failure specifically on RPT disclosure timing, at a company where RPT concentration is already the single
largest quality-of-earnings risk; it sharpens rather than merely echoes Stage 2's Rank-15 "disclosure regression"
finding.
