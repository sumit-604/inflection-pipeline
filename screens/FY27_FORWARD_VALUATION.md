# FY27 forward valuation worksheet — nine screened names

Built 2026-09-08. Companion to the nine cards in `screens/cards/`. Sits OUTSIDE the
shallow analysis framework, which forbids valuation inside a card. Nothing here is a
Section 1B exit multiple, a fair value, or a recommendation. It answers one operator
question: what does each name cost against the earnings the current financial year is
running at.

## READ THIS FIRST: there is no live market price

Claude Code has no live web access. The container's proxy allows only
`api.anthropic.com`, `mcp-proxy.anthropic.com` and package registries. Every quote host
returns 403. Bull AI, the only market data source reachable here, carries no price feed.

So every price below is a **dated transaction price**: the average price of a bulk, block
or insider deal reported to NSE or BSE on the day named. On the day of a large deal that
price is a real market price. It is not today's price.

Staleness runs from 3 weeks (Manorama, 14 August) to 16 months (Forbes, April 2025).
Elantas has no usable price at all: its last recorded deal is 7 December 2022.

**Therefore the "price for 20x" column is the operational output of this sheet.** Look up
the live quote yourself. If it sits below that number, the name clears your 20x bar on the
earnings basis stated. Every forward PE in the table is an anchor point, not a verdict.

## Method

Two earnings bases, both anchored, neither estimated:

- **Base A, Q1FY27 annualised.** The reported Q1FY27 earnings per share multiplied by
  four. Purely mechanical. It assumes the June quarter repeats three more times. It says
  nothing about seasonality, and for a cyclical it annualises whatever point of the cycle
  the quarter caught.
- **Base B, last full year actual.** The audited or reported FY26 (or CY2025) earnings per
  share. This is trailing, not forward. It is here as the sanity check on Base A.

Where Base A and Base B diverge widely, the divergence IS the finding. Read both.

No management bottom-line guidance exists for eight of the nine. Goodluck alone gave a
revenue growth band. It gets a third base.

Every figure carries its source. NOT FOUND appears where the corpus is silent. Nothing is
estimated.

## Against the 20x bar

Three names sit at or below 20x on Base A:

- **Bhansali Engineering Polymers, 11.5x.** Cheapest of the nine on both bases.
- **Laxmi Organic, 17.4x.** Cheap on Base A only. On the FY26 actual it is 59x.
- **Goodluck India, 18.6x.** Cheap on Base A. 25.9x trailing.

Two of those three are converters. Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds both. Their Base A
earnings are annualised from a single high-spread quarter. Read the per-name notes before
treating either number as an entry multiple.

None of the other six is near 20x on any basis in the corpus.

## Per-name derivation

### Bhansali Engineering Polymers (BEPL) — 11.5x on A, 16.8x on B

- Q1FY27 PAT Rs 65.6 crore, revenue Rs 472.2 crore, up 53.3% (Investor Presentation
  Q1FY27, p.7, p.8).
- Shares 24,88,58,460 of face value Re 1 (Annual Report FY2025, p.196). No change since
  the July 2023 bonus.
- Q1FY27 EPS = 65.6 / 24.8858 = **Rs 2.636**. Annualised = **Rs 10.54**.
- Price Rs 121.45 buy and Rs 121.47 sell, Junomoneta Finsol, NSE bulk, 22 July 2026.
- Forward PE = 121.46 / 10.54 = **11.5x**.
- Base B: FY25 basic and diluted EPS Rs 7.23 on PAT Rs 179.82 crore (Annual Report FY2025,
  p.155). FY24 EPS Rs 7.22. PE on FY25 = 16.8x.

**Amendment 17 warning.** BEPL is a CONVERTER without qualification. Revenue is an ABS
price over an imported styrene and acrylonitrile cost. Installed capacity has been 75,000
MTPA since 2015. Q1FY27 revenue rose 53.3% on that unchanged capacity, so the growth was
price, not tonnes, and EBITDA margin fell 86 basis points while it happened. Annualising
that quarter annualises a spread. The honest band is 11.5x at the Q1 spread to 16.8x at
the FY25 spread. Both clear 20x. That is the finding worth having.

Note also a Rs 132.46 to Rs 133.01 print on 21 July 2026, one day before the Rs 121.46
used here. A 9% move in one day. Use the live quote.

### Laxmi Organic (LXCHEM) — 17.4x on A, 59.1x on B

- Q1FY27 consolidated PAT Rs 677.16 million, revenue Rs 9,683.38 million, basic and
  diluted EPS **Rs 2.44** (Outcome of Board Meeting FY2027 Q1, p.9).
- Shares 27,72,11,567 of face value Rs 2, after 63,694 ESOP shares allotted 3 April 2026
  (same document, p.10, note 7).
- Annualised EPS = 2.44 x 4 = **Rs 9.76**.
- Price Rs 169.18 to Rs 169.80, NK Securities and Blitzquant, NSE bulk, 3 June 2026.
- Forward PE = 169.50 / 9.76 = **17.4x**.
- Base B: FY26 consolidated PAT Rs 793.62 million, basic EPS **Rs 2.87** (same document,
  p.9, year ended column). PE on FY26 = 59.1x.

**This is the widest divergence in the table, and it is the Amendment 17 trap in its
purest form.** Q1FY27 EBITDA rose 272% year on year. Volume growth was about 10%; the
rest was price. Acetic acid rose about 200% from a March base and ethyl acetate spreads
ran above their 12-year average (Concall Q1FY27, p.4, p.7, p.8, p.9). Q3FY26 was the
mirror image, a quarter management described as one where "margins were under quite a lot
of pressure".

So 17.4x is the multiple on a peak-spread quarter held for four quarters. 59.1x is the
multiple on the last full year, which contained both the peak and the trough. The
mid-cycle number is somewhere between and this corpus cannot locate it. Treat 17.4x as
the bull case, not the base case.

One further caution: the Q1FY27 tax charge is not comparable. The holding company moved to
the 25.17% regime under Section 200 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 during the quarter, after
reverting to the old 34.94% regime in Q4FY26 (same document, p.10, note 5). Part of the
PAT jump is a tax-regime change, not operations.

### Goodluck India (GOODLUCK) — 18.6x on A, 21.5x on guidance, 25.9x on B

**Every per-share figure Goodluck filed before 21 August 2026 is pre-bonus.** A 2:1 bonus
went ex on 21 August 2026: two new shares for each one held, so the count trebles and
per-share figures divide by three (corporate actions, NSE; recorded in
`screens/corpus/GOODLUCK/filings/GOODLUCK-ownership-guidance-counterparties.md` s.1).

- Q1FY27 consolidated: total income Rs 1,287.44 crore up 31%, PAT Rs 67.22 crore up 67%,
  reported EPS **Rs 19.13** pre-bonus (Concall Q1FY27, p.6).
- Shares pre-bonus 3,32,38,509 of face value Rs 2. Post-bonus 9,97,15,527.
- Post-bonus Q1FY27 EPS = 19.13 / 3 = **Rs 6.38**. Annualised = **Rs 25.51**.
- Price Rs 1,420.00 average, promoter-family insider sales on NSE, 1 July 2026, pre-bonus.
  Post-bonus equivalent = 1,420 / 3 = **Rs 473.33**.
- Forward PE = 473.33 / 25.51 = **18.6x**.
- Base B: FY26 consolidated PAT Rs 182.58 crore (Q4FY26 call, via guidance file). FY26
  post-bonus EPS = 182.58 / 9.9716 = **Rs 18.31**. PE on FY26 = 25.9x.
- Base C, guidance: management guided 15% to 20% revenue growth for FY27 (Concall Q1FY27,
  p.18). At the top of that band and flat margin, FY27 EPS = 18.31 x 1.20 = Rs 21.97 and
  the PE is **21.5x**.

The gap between Base A (18.6x) and Base C (21.5x) is the question. Base A implies 39% PAT
growth. Guidance is 15% to 20% on revenue. Goodluck can beat the revenue band on profit
because the mix is shifting: the defence subsidiary made 6% of consolidated revenue and
22% of consolidated EBITDA in Q1FY27. But defence revenue guidance for FY27 was itself
revised DOWN, from Rs 350-400 crore to Rs 300-350 crore, because the expansion slipped
(Concall Q1FY27, p.5, p.8). Take the range 18.6x to 21.5x, and note that the top of it
does not clear 20x.

The Rs 19.13 EPS is computed on profit attributable to owners, roughly Rs 63.6 crore, not
on the Rs 67.22 crore consolidated PAT. The difference is minority interest in the defence
subsidiary. The owners' basis is the correct one and is used above.

### Manorama Industries (MANORAMA) — 33.8x on A, 47.3x on B

- Q1FY27 consolidated PAT Rs 7,865.82 lakh, revenue Rs 40,400.60 lakh, basic EPS **Rs
  13.17** (Outcome of Board Meeting FY2027 Q1, p.4).
- Shares 5,97,08,530 of face value Rs 2 at 30 June 2026, unchanged from 31 March 2026.
- Annualised EPS = 13.17 x 4 = **Rs 52.68**.
- Price Rs 1,779.63 buy and Rs 1,780.85 sell, Microcurves Trading, NSE bulk, 14 August
  2026. The most recent price of the nine.
- Forward PE = 1,780.85 / 52.68 = **33.8x**.
- Base B: FY26 consolidated PAT Rs 22,491.80 lakh, basic EPS **Rs 37.67** (same document).
  PE on FY26 = 47.3x.

Two adjustments push the real multiple higher, not lower.

First, other income was Rs 16.18 crore of the Rs 106.33 crore profit before tax, 15% of
it, from fixed deposit income and forex gains (Concall Q1FY27, p.12). Strip it and the Q1
EPS falls to about Rs 11.17, the annualised figure to Rs 44.65, and the PE rises to about
**39.9x**.

Second, the QIP. Management confirmed the money arrived in Q1FY27 ("that amount we have
received in this quarter", Concall p.12), but paid-up capital at 30 June 2026 is unchanged
at Rs 1,194.17 lakh. So the QIP shares are not yet in the count. When they are, EPS falls
and the multiple rises again. The dilution is NOT FOUND in this corpus and must be
verified before any entry work.

Manorama has the cleanest operating trajectory of the nine: 85% of the 39.5% revenue
growth was volume, ROE 39.4%, ROCE 46.2%. It is also the third most expensive.

- FY2026: revenue Rs 25,101 lakh up 7.9%, PAT Rs 2,877 lakh up 0.1%, EPS **Rs 5.58**
  (Financial Results FY2026 Q4, p.5).
- Shares 5,15,90,000 of face value Rs 10 (paid-up capital Rs 5,159 lakh).
- Price Rs 203.20, Shapoorji Pallonji sale to Forbes Campbell Finance, BSE bulk,

Profit was flat in FY26 while revenue grew 7.9%: employee costs and overheads absorbed the

### IKIO Technologies (IKIO) — 40.6x on A, 46.4x on B

- Q1FY27 PAT Rs 110 million, revenue Rs 1,693 million, diluted EPS **Rs 1.39** (Investor
  Presentation Q1FY27, p.6, p.7).
- Annualised EPS = 1.39 x 4 = **Rs 5.56**.
- Price Rs 225.25 to Rs 226.71 across four NSE bulk deals, 17 July 2026. Rs 226.00 used.
- Forward PE = 226.00 / 5.56 = **40.6x**.
- Base B: FY26 diluted EPS **Rs 4.87** on PAT Rs 416 million (same page). PE = 46.4x.

**Filing defect flagged.** Share capital is Rs 773 million at face value Rs 10 in both
Mar-26 and Mar-25, so 7.73 crore shares. FY26 PAT of Rs 416 million on that count gives
EPS Rs 5.38, not the Rs 4.87 printed. FY25 reconciles exactly (324 / 77.3 = 4.19). The
FY26 EPS line and the balance sheet do not agree. The Q1FY27 figure (110 / 1.39 = 79.1
million shares) is internally consistent and is the one used here. This is the second
arithmetic defect found in this deck; the FY26 segment row was also transposed.

Whichever EPS is right, IKIO is 40x forward on a business earning about 7.9% pre-tax
return on capital employed. Nothing in this sheet changes the card's reading.

### Rossell Techsys (ROSSTECH) — 121.6x on A, 163.6x on B

- Q1FY27 PAT Rs 698.18 lakh, revenue Rs 154.7 crore, basic and diluted EPS **Rs 1.85**
  (Investor Presentation Q1FY27, p.10, p.11).
- Annualised EPS = 1.85 x 4 = **Rs 7.40**.
- Price Rs 900.00, the promoter-family sale to Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund, 18,47,117 shares
  on BSE and NSE, 31 July 2026. A genuine institutional print at scale.
- Forward PE = 900 / 7.40 = **121.6x**.
- Base B: FY26 EPS **Rs 5.50** on PAT Rs 2,074 lakh, face value Rs 2. PE = 163.6x.

The multiple is not the main objection. The cash statement is. Operating cash flow was
negative Rs 482 million in FY25 and negative Rs 108 million in FY24. Free cash flow was
negative Rs 699 million in FY25. Net working capital ran 607 days. Total debt was Rs 409
crore at 31 March 2026, 84% of one year's revenue (India Ratings rationale, 20 November
2025, p.3, p.4; ownership file s.3). The profit and loss is inflecting. The cash flow has
not started to. At 121x forward, the price allows for no delay.

### Elantas Beck (ELANTAS) — EPS derivable, PE not

**This company runs a January to December year.** "FY27" does not apply to it. Its current
financial year is CY2026 and no CY2026 quarterly result exists in Bull AI.

- CY2025 audited: revenue Rs 84,780.93 lakh, PAT Rs 14,777.98 lakh, basic and diluted EPS
  **Rs 186.41** (Financial Results, year ended 31 December 2025, p.6). CY2024 EPS Rs
  176.05, up 5.9%.
- Shares 79,27,700 of face value Rs 10 (paid-up capital Rs 792.77 lakh, unchanged since
  at least 2023).
- **Price NOT FOUND.** The last recorded deal is Rs 4,100 on 7 December 2022, three years
  and nine months old. Bull AI holds six market transactions for this company in total.
  There is no usable price.
- **Price for 20x on the CY2025 EPS: Rs 3,728.**

Growth is 5.9% on earnings and 9.5% compound on revenue over three years. Profit before
tax margin has fallen every year since 2023: 27.0%, 24.4%, 23.4%. About 24% of CY2025
profit before tax is unallocable other income, almost certainly treasury on a large cash
pile, and must be stripped from any operating read. Against a 25% CAGR target this is not
a candidate at any plausible multiple. The Rs 3,728 figure is arithmetic, not a target.

## What this sheet does not do

- It sets no exit multiple. Section 1B is the sole exit multiple authority and is not
  consulted here. Nothing above may be carried into a Section 1B or FTTCP run as a
  destination PE.
- It applies no Hurdle. The 25% CAGR test needs an exit multiple and a holding period,
  neither of which this sheet has.
- It does not adjust for net cash or net debt. These are equity PEs, not enterprise
  multiples. BEPL, IKIO, Forbes and Elantas hold net cash; Rossell and Manorama carry
  material debt. On an enterprise basis the ranking would shift.
- It does not resolve the converter problem. For BEPL and Laxmi Organic the mid-cycle
  earnings power is the number that matters, and this corpus does not contain enough cycle
  history to compute it.
