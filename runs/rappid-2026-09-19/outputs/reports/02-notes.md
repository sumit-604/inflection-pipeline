NEW FINDING - Directors' Report "no material changes" boilerplate
contradicts Note 32's own disclosure of a material post-year-end event

Directors' Report (AR FY26, p. corresponding to line ~3389, section
"MATERIAL CHANGES AND COMMITMENTS... AFFECTING THE FINANCIAL POSITION"):
"There have been no material changes and commitments, affecting the
financial position of the Company which occurred during the period
between the close of the financial year 2025-26 and the date of this
report." The Directors' Report carries its own signing block dated
29-Aug-2026, Palghar (AR FY26, p. near lines 3034-3161).

Note 32 (Utilisation Summary, p.68-69), in the SAME filing, discloses that
an EGM held 17-Apr-2026 approved redirecting Rs.764.51 Lakh of unutilised
IPO proceeds - the ENTIRE Rs.400 Lakh "inorganic growth/acquisitions"
budget (never spent) plus the unspent Rs.364.51 Lakh capex balance - into
working capital. 17-Apr-2026 falls squarely inside the window the
Directors' Report just described as having "no material changes ...
affecting the financial position" (31-Mar-2026 to 29-Aug-2026).

A shareholder-approved, formal variation of objects covering 25.1% of
gross IPO proceeds, redirecting capital away from the two growth pillars
named in the prospectus (new capacity and acquisitions) into working
capital, is a change affecting the financial position of the company by
any ordinary reading. Two readings, one observation separating them: (a)
the Directors' Report boilerplate is generic, carried forward without a
line-by-line check against events the notes elsewhere already disclose,
consistent with the rolled-forward-text pattern found at Note 3.5, Note
32's own date error, and Note 35 (Pass 1/2); (b) the company judged the
EGM redirection immaterial to "financial position" because it does not
change reported FY26 numbers, only forward deployment. The observation
that separates them: the same EGM item is treated as significant enough
to warrant a full paragraph and a shareholder resolution inside Note 32,
but as non-existent inside the Directors' Report's own subsequent-events
section three pages earlier in the same document - an internal
inconsistency in materiality judgment, not merely a date typo.
Rating: RED FLAG (disclosure-quality / materiality-judgment
inconsistency, not a quantification error - the underlying EGM facts and
rupee amounts tie out correctly in Note 32 itself).
Anchor: Directors' Report, "Material Changes and Commitments" section (AR
FY26, p. near line 3389, signed 29-Aug-2026); Note 32 (p.68-69, EGM
17-Apr-2026).

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. What is the quantified Rupee impact of the FY26 useful-life and
   residual-value revision (Note 2.6) on the depreciation charge, and on
   what specific asset classes?
2. Receivable days rose from ~82 to ~170 over three years and inventory
   (finished goods especially, +93.6%) nearly doubled on flat revenue -
   why has zero doubtful-debt provision and no NRV write-down been
   recognised in either year, and is a provisioning policy review under
   way?
3. The Directors' Report states no material change occurred between
   31-Mar-2026 and the report date, yet Note 32 discloses a 17-Apr-2026
   EGM redirecting Rs.764.51 Lakh of IPO proceeds, including the entire
   acquisitions budget, to working capital - does the Directors' Report
   need correction, and what was the specific trigger for the redirection?
4. What explains the Rs.93.7 Lakh MSME payable balance sitting entirely in
   the ">3 years" ageing bucket in both FY25 and FY26 with zero interest
   accrued under the MSMED Act - is this a disputed, legacy, or
   mis-tagged balance?
5. Several notes carry unrefreshed FY25 narrative text in the FY26 filing
   (Note 3.5's CCPS date, Note 32's IPO subscription/allotment dates, Note
   35's CSR year) alongside unrelated cross-note number mismatches (Note
   29 deferred tax, Note 21 unbilled revenue, the P&L face EPS figure) -
   what changes are planned to the financial-statement drafting and
   review process before the FY27 filing?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- No evidence found of revenue-recognition gaming, hidden recurring items
  dressed as one-off (aside from the "Rates & taxes" swing, Watch-rated,
  not confirmed as deliberate), or non-arm's-length RPT pricing.
- A genuine, repeated pattern of disclosure-control weakness: at least
  three instances of unrefreshed, rolled-forward FY25 narrative text
  (Note 3.5, Note 32, Note 35), at least three cross-note number
  mismatches (Note 21 unbilled-revenue roll-forward, Note 29 FY25
  deferred-tax comparative, Note 36 "Principal repayments" row), and two
  direct self-contradictions within the same filing (Note 37 (iii) vs
  (xxv) on contingent liabilities; Directors' Report vs Note 32 on
  "material changes"). None individually moves reported PAT, but the
  density of errors across a first full post-IPO annual filing is itself
  a signal on process maturity.
- An undisclosed-risk-indicator pattern: zero receivables/inventory
  provisioning maintained through two years of worsening turnover ratios
  that the company's own Note 36 discloses without comment.
- No fraud, no whistleblower complaints, no qualified audit opinion, no
  covenant breach, no willful-defaulter finding (CARO, p.51-54).

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate to concerning accounting practices. Key
concern: a three-year, monotonic cash-conversion deterioration
(receivables, inventory, GST credit) carrying zero provisioning, compounded
by a dense cluster of internal contradictions and unrefreshed rolled-forward
text across the first full post-IPO filing. Key strength: clean, unchanged
revenue-recognition and depreciation policy, an unmodified audit opinion, no
debt covenant issues, and full (if error-prone) disclosure of the IPO
proceeds redirection. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
