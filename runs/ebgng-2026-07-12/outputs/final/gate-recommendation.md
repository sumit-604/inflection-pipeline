The evidence points both ways and cannot be separated on the current record.

Growth induced signals: CARE ties the elongated operating cycle explicitly to supporting high business growth (B01); revenue compounded 40.6% and PAT 107.8% FY20 to FY26 (B01); the elevated Rs 743 Cr inventory is a deliberate management bet on rising memory prices, quantified on the call (B05); post listing gearing fell from 2.0x to 0.53x on IPO proceeds (B01).

Structural signals: CARE records negative cash flow from operations since FY20, more than six years, on a consolidated basis (B01); debt service coverage never exceeded 1.0x across four periods, 0.43 to 0.40 to 0.25 to 0.12 (B02, B03); working capital loan draws are classified as an operating adjustment rather than financing, so reported CFO depends on continued short tenor debt rollover, and in FY24 the WCL inflow of Rs 2,071 M exceeded cash generated from operations of Rs 1,025 M itself (B02, B03); cumulative CFO to PAT is minus 0.93 and cumulative FCF to PAT minus 1.80 (B01); operating cash flow was negative in both FY25 and FY26 (B04); working capital days rose 136 to 178 (B01).
