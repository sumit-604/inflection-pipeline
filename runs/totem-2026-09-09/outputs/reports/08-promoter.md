### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour

- **Dividend conduct**: ~90% payout ratio (company memory/screener), Rs 19.05 cr flowing
  to the promoter group in FY26. TOTEM carries near-net-cash balance sheet (Rs 17 cr
  borrowings vs Rs 117 cr reserves per company memory), so this reads as ordinary

- **MD remuneration — the standout unresolved finding.** Mr. Tahilyani's total
  remuneration rose from **Rs 265.52 lakh (FY25) to Rs 464.00 lakh (FY26), +74.7%**

  Report states "the percentage increase in remuneration of Key Managerial Personnel are

  combined cost moved roughly Rs 95.82 lakh to Rs 102.11 lakh (~+6.6%), i.e. **the
  Annexure II "average 7.5%" figure appears to describe only the CFO and CS, silently
  excluding the MD** — even though Note 30 itself classifies the MD as a "Key Managerial
  Person" for related-party purposes (line 7140). **The AR nowhere explains or flags the
  MD's own 74.7% increase.** PAT was roughly flat FY25 to FY26 (~Rs 29 cr both years per
  company memory), so this is a material pay rise unaccompanied by a matching profit
  move, and unaccompanied by any disclosed rationale (no special resolution on MD
  remuneration was passed in FY26 — the only FY26 special resolution was on non-executive
  director commission, line 3278-3282). The Nomination & Remuneration Committee (which
  the MD does not sit on — see Section 5) met once in FY26 (11-Aug-2025), which may be
  where a remuneration decision was taken, but no minute detail or component breakdown
  (fixed vs. profit-linked commission) is disclosed. **This finding is presented as an
  unreconciled disclosure gap, not proven misconduct**; a benign explanation (e.g., a
  profit-linked commission formula with a threshold effect, or an under-disclosed base
  revision approved at listing) is plausible but not evidenced in the corpus.

**SEBI LODR disclosure requirement — checked fairly, per the task's instruction.**
Promoter share pledge/encumbrance disclosure is governed by the SEBI (Substantial
Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and SEBI LODR Regulation 31: it
must be disclosed to the stock exchanges within a short window of creation, and it appears
in the quarterly shareholding pattern (Table II) — which is exactly where this run found
it, consistently, in all three filings. **No specific requirement was found mandating
reproduction of the pledge figure inside the Annual Report / Corporate Governance Report
itself** (Schedule V format); that format's mandatory content runs to board composition,
committee charters, RPT policy, and similar governance items, not a restatement of the
quarterly pledge disclosure. **On this reading, the pledge's absence from three
consecutive TOTEM annual reports is not a clear technical SEBI/LODR violation** — but it
remains a real completeness and reader-orientation gap, especially since the company's
own Information Memorandum flagged this exact risk to investors by name at listing

document (the AR) that most retail/analyst readers would consult after listing. The
Board's Report and Corporate Governance Report discuss "Assets pledged as security" only
in the context of the company's own property, plant and equipment pledged against its
DCB Bank ECLGS 2.0 term loan (✅ VERIFIED, Annual_Report_2026.txt lines 6048, 8186-8203) —
an entirely different, immaterial, and properly disclosed item, confirming Stage 3's
finding that the promoter *share* pledge specifically is nowhere named in the AR.
