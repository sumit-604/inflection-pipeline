## FINDINGS

**Finding 1 (MAJOR) — IKS Health coverage-map row, Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025 call)**
Part 3's coverage map credits this transcript with "headcount/revenue decoupling data (24%
rev vs 1.5% headcount growth) for Q3/2B." That specific data point ("24% year-on-year...
1.5% growth in headcount") is not in the Nov 2025 transcript. It is in the **Feb 2026**
transcript (IKS-Concall_Feb_2026_Transcript.txt, l.116-172, l.638). The Nov 2025 call's
actual analogous data point is a different pair of numbers: 17% revenue growth vs ~3%
headcount growth (l.8334-8425). Part 1's own Q3 body text correctly cites this as "IKS Feb
2026 call" — so the error is confined to the Part 3 table, but it is a real, checkable
misattribution of a quarter within the same peer.

**Finding 2 (MAJOR) — IKS Health coverage-map row, Q3 FY26 (Feb 2026 call)**
The same table credits this transcript with "RPE/EBITDA-per-employee non-disclosure for
Q3" (the "I don't find it very easy to model the business out" answer to Nilabja Dey). That
exchange is not in the Feb 2026 transcript. It is in the **Nov 2025** transcript
(IKS-Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, l.6276-6833). Part 1's Q3 body text correctly cites
this as "IKS Nov 2025 call" — again the error is confined to Part 3. Findings 1 and 2 are
a matched pair: the two IKS data points appear to have been swapped between the two
coverage-map rows. Rated MAJOR per the rubric (SUBSTANTIVE claim in the audited coverage
table without a citation findable at the stated location), though materially softer than a
fabrication since both underlying quotes are real, correctly cited elsewhere in the same
report, and belong to the same peer.

**Finding 3 (MAJOR) — Sagility coverage-map row, Q1 FY27 (Jul 2026 call)**
Part 3 credits this transcript with "long-sales-cycle confirmation for Q7." A targeted
search of SAGILITY-Concall_Jul_2026_Transcript.txt for "cycle," "sales cycle," "long,"
"deal timeline," "complex deal," and related terms found no content addressing large-account
deal-cycle length; the transcript's only "cycle" reference is to the U.S. open-enrolment
seasonal cycle (l.247-251), an unrelated topic. The Q7 sales-cycle-lengthening evidence
Sagility actually contributes is correctly and exclusively anchored to the **Feb 2026** call
in Part 1's own text ("large end-to-end managed constructs... typically have longer sales
cycles," SAGILITY Feb 2026 call). The Jul 2026 row's other claimed contribution
("payer-cost-pressure demand framing for 2A") is genuinely supported by that transcript
(l.84-91). So this row is half-correct: real usage on 2A, an unsupported specific citation
on Q7.

**Finding 4 (MINOR) — speaker-attribution ambiguity**
Part 2A cites "ECLERX Jul 2025 call, Aashray Vasa Q&A: 'the only thing we have experienced
is a delayed decision-making.'" The quote is actually spoken by Kapil Jain in response to
Aashray Vasa's question (l.887-906), not by Vasa himself. The report's own convention
elsewhere (e.g., "Prakash Kapadia Q&A" labels in B05) uses the analyst's name to mark which
Q&A round a management answer came from, so this is consistent internal style rather than a
location error, but on its own it is momentarily ambiguous about who is speaking and is
worth a name-check tightening.

No CRITICAL findings. No instance found of a peer statement misattributed to the wrong peer
company (no ZAGGLE-class error) — the errors located are all within-peer, cross-quarter
mislabels confined to the Part 3 summary table, not fabrications and not corroboration-count
inflation (the underlying peer count behind each verdict is unaffected by these three
row-level errors).
