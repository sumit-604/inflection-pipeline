## SULA VINEYARDS (SULA) — direct wine peer

### Performance track record (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q3, Page 19; FY2027, Q1, Page 18)
- Revenue: FY21 417.9, FY22 453.9, FY23 553.2, FY24 608.7, FY25 619.4, FY26 596. (CAGR ~6-10%; FY26 dipped.)
- Operating EBITDA: FY21 60.9, FY22 113.3, FY23 157.5, FY24 175.9, FY25 149.1, FY26 103.
- Operating EBITDA margin: 14.6%, 25.0%, 28.5%, 28.9%, 24.1%, 17.3% (FY26).
- PAT: FY21 3.2, FY22 52.1, FY23 84.1, FY24 93.3, FY25 70.2, FY26 26. PAT margin peaked 15.2%, FY26 4.3%.
- Own Brands revenue CAGR 12-17%; Elite & Premium revenue CAGR 21%; Wine Tourism CAGR 32-35%.

### Margin ladder (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2026, Q4, Page 16)
- Rajeev Samant: "the elite wine has by far the highest margin, followed by the premium, followed by the economy and then the popular." (Confirms premium mix drives margin.)

### Read-through
- A scaled wine leader earned 24-29% EBITDA at peak but fell to 17.3% in FY26 as
  the category softened. Wine is cyclical. Fratelli's 20% FY30 target is at the
  peer's good-year level, not a floor.

## RADICO KHAITAN (RADICO) — premiumisation exemplar

### P&A mix shift (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q4, Pages 7-9; FY2027, Q1, Page 9)
- Prestige & Above volume share: FY19 28.3%, FY20 29.0%, FY22 30.8%, FY23 37.5%, FY24 45.6%, FY25 46.1%, FY26 45.6%.
- P&A brands CAGR ~21% since FY21. P&A volume up >212% since FY19.
- Realisation Rs/case: P&A 1,307 (FY19) to 1,834 (FY26); total 773 to 1,181.

### Why margins moved (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q4, Page 10; Page 5)
- "Ongoing premiumization coupled with a stable raw material scenario has led to
  significant margin expansion, and stronger return ratios."
- Chairman: "Our Prestige & Above portfolio delivered over 28% growth during the
  year ... improved our margin profile."

### Read-through
- Premiumisation works, but it took ~6 years to move P&A mix from 29% to 46%, and
  margins also needed a raw-material tailwind. A base rate for how slow and
  input-dependent a margin climb is.

## TILAKNAGAR INDUSTRIES (TI) — live margin-transition comp

### Volumes and realisation (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q3, Page 40)
- Total volumes: FY20 6.44 to FY25 11.91 mn cases. Brandy ~91-94% of volume.
- NSR per case Rs: 1,005 (FY20) to 1,282 (FY24), 1,214 (FY25).
- South India ~86% of volumes (concentration).

### Premiumisation moves (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2026, Q1 Page 3; Q2 Page 3)
- Luxury foray Monarch Legacy Edition Brandy launched across states; super-premium
  via Spaceman Spirits Lab (TI owns 21.36%, intends to raise stake).
- Third-largest P&A IMFL player in Karnataka; market-share gains in key states.

### Read-through
- A mid-cap climbing the same ladder: pricing per case rising, luxury launches,
  inorganic super-premium. Shows the playbook Fratelli must run, and that
  realisation gains are gradual.
