KEY TAKEAWAYS

01 The bulk of our largest capex cycle is now live. We added 11.7k desks in Q2, our largest quarterly
           addition, taking operational capacity to 145.3k desks across 85 centres in 8 cities, up 26.9% YoY.
           First-half additions of 18.4k, with ~4.5k more desks opened in the first week of October, take us
           past the 22k we set out in Q1. A further ~47k desks are secured through signed leases and LOIs,
           32% beyond today’s capacity.

02 Demand kept pace with the largest openings in our history, across WeWork branded spaces and
           managed offices. Members grew 32.5% YoY to 121.7k, ahead of 26.9% growth in capacity, and
           portfolio occupancy rose 357 bps YoY to 83.7%. Mature centres reached 87.7%, up 355 bps,
           and centres open less than a year are already at 64.8%, well above our 55.7% breakeven. The
           114 bps QoQ dip is new capacity still filling.

03 Revenue grew 31.6% YoY and 10.1% QoQ to ₹768.7 Cr, and the platform is compounding on top of
           the core. VAS + Digital grew 38% YoY to ₹108.8 Cr, ahead of 31.7% growth in core operations, with
           Digital up 45.6%. Our order book runs well ahead of our commitments: ₹3,843 Cr of core revenue
           is already locked in, up 66% YoY and 2.7 times the ₹1,421 Cr of rent committed against it.
