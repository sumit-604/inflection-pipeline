## The one engine the thesis would rest on
Operating leverage on a fixed-cost screen network, with the balance sheet now
clear of debt. Two readings exist. Reading A: the recovery is structural
(occupancy rising, closures of loss-making screens, asset-light growth lifts
return on capital). Reading B: FY27 is a content-led peak year, and price, not
footfall, did the work (admissions +4%, ticket price +14%). The one observation
that separates them: admissions per screen and occupancy across a weak-content
quarter.

## Peers (Step C)

after the PVR and Inox merger. Closest structural comps:

  https://www.screener.in/company/UFO/consolidated/ (OK). Four transcripts.

  https://www.screener.in/company/WONDERLA/consolidated/ (OK). Four transcripts.
