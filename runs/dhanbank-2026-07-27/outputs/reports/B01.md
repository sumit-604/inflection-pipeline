## METHODOLOGY NOTE — SCHEDULED COMMERCIAL BANK ADAPTATION (read first)

Dhanlaxmi Bank Ltd is an old-private-sector scheduled commercial bank
(established 1927, South India/Kerala-concentrated, gold-loan tilted,
~0.2% loan-book market share) (rating p.3). Manufacturing-oriented metrics
in this scorecard — ROCE-on-capital-employed, EBITDA margin, cash
conversion (CFO/PAT is still computed as a mechanical ratio but "capex"
has no fixed-asset-heavy meaning), working-capital days (receivables/
inventory/payables), current ratio, fixed-asset turnover — have **no
banking analogue** and are marked **N/A (bank — metric not applicable)**
per the task brief. Per that brief, the LENDER metric set is substituted
wherever the scorecard calls for a profitability/efficiency/leverage line:
RoA substitutes for ROCE (A1/A2/A4); ROE is used as-is (A3, it already
applies to banks); CRAR substitutes for D1; PCR substitutes for D2; D3
uses the framework's fixed "Financials: default 3" rule. No number is
estimated — every N/A is stated with the reason, never silently
scored as if data were simply missing.

**UNIT RECONCILIATION (mandatory per task brief):** the two results
filings (Q4 FY26 audited, Q3 FY26 unaudited) are in **Rs Lakh**; the
screener Data_Sheet and the FY2024-25 Annual Report Schedule 12/Balance
Sheet extract are in **Rs Crore** (AR schedule text renders as Rs '000,
which divides by 10,000 to crore). Cross-checks performed and confirmed
exact:
- Capital: AR Schedule "39,46,989" (₹'000) ÷ 10,000 = ₹394.70 cr = screener FY25 Equity Share Capital 394.70 (screener-Data_Sheet). ✓
- Deposits+Borrowings: results Q4 FY26 Note 1 (Rs Lakh), 31.03.2026: Deposits 18,64,288 lakh (₹18,642.88 cr) + Borrowings 73,663 lakh (₹736.63 cr) = ₹19,379.51 cr = screener FY26 "Borrowings" (combined) row 19379.51 exactly (results Q4 FY26 p.10; screener-Data_Sheet). ✓
- Net profit: results Q4 FY26 row 14, year ended 31.03.2026, "10,275" lakh = ₹102.75 cr = screener FY26 Net profit 102.75 exactly (results Q4 FY26 p.8; screener-Data_Sheet). ✓
