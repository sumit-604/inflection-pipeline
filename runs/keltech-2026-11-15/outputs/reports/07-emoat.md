- Reading A: defence or merchant energetics bet. It would create A1 and R1 and open a recognition question.

- Separator: a licence or offtake naming a buyer, or first booked revenue outside the Explosives and Perlite segments.
I do not shade toward either. Conservatism belongs in position size.
