## 4. VARIANT PERCEPTION CHECK — argue the MARKET IS RIGHT

**My/thesis variant perception:** the market pays 1.35x book for a lender whose sustainable ROE (13.5%)
sits below its CoE (14.5%), so theoretical fair P/B is <1.0x and the stock is 40% over-valued; the

**Why the MARKET may be right and the thesis wrong:**
1. **The full-year ROE 11.1% is a lagging, depressed average; the Q4 exit ROE was 14.0%** (fttcp
   Transition 4). If 14% is the true run-rate — depressed in FY25 by IPO equity bloat plus a one-off
   credit spike, both explicitly temporary — then the "ROE < CoE" framing is a rear-view artifact.
   On a 14% run-rate ROE, fair P/B ≈ 0.97x rising to bull 1.17x, and 1.35x is a modest forward premium
   for a lender re-rating toward mid-teens with AUM FIRING, not a 40% mistake.
2. **The market prices the exit, not the trough.** The additive-PE ceiling cross-check shows Base
   +15.2% / Bull +24.0% (B11) — the market may simply be discounting the +5 composite that two clean
   quarters would produce, at which point 1.5-1.6x book is defensible for a discovered AA- growth NBFC.
3. **Book compounds regardless.** With DPS 0 (full retention) book compounds at retained ROE ~13%
   (B11), so even a flat multiple delivers low-teens returns over 3 years — the market is paying for a
   compounding book, not a static one.
4. **The entry zone is unreachable in the market's base case.** Requiring Rs 153-173 (−47 to −53%) for
   a growing, discovered, rated lender is asking for a dislocation the market does not expect and, absent
   the shared-catalyst failure, is unlikely to deliver. The market's implicit view: this never trades
   there without a crisis, so 1.35x is the real clearing price.

**Rebuttal (devil stays brutal on the market too):** even granting the Q4-exit 14% run-rate, the FY28
17% needed to justify 1.35x is STRETCHED and was diluted across three consecutive calls (B05); the
additive +24% bull STILL misses the 25% hurdle and embeds an unjustified 1.88x exit P/B (B11); and the
"book compounds anyway" argument ignores that book compounding at sub-CoE ROE creates no shareholder
value. The market is paying for a mid-teens ROE the company keeps postponing. **On balance: the market
is more right than the thesis about the FRANCHISE (not a 40% error), but the thesis is right about the
HURDLE (25% CAGR is infeasible from 325). Both can hold: good business, wrong price for THIS investor's
return bar.**

## 7. PRE-MORTEM (it is 2028; the stock is down 40% from a Rs 300 entry)

It is mid-2028. Anyone who ignored the AVOID and bought near Rs 300-325 in 2026 is looking at ~Rs 185.
Here is the most likely story.

The FY27 first-half prints did what the notes warned. Provision coverage, already stripped to 44.5%,
had nothing left to release, so when small-ticket LAP and the freshly-re-accelerated rural/MSME book
(branches surged 278→342 in one quarter, into the segment that caused the prior stress) began rolling
over, the credit cost printed 3.4% in Q2 FY27 with GNPA at 1.7%. There was no buffer to smooth it, so
it hit the P&L raw. The RBI, having already forced the FLDG/ECL correction once, took a second look at
the Stage-2 coverage drop from 24% to 12% and the earlier ECL aggressiveness resurfaced as a fresh
provision top-up. RoE, which the market had extrapolated from the 14% Q4-FY26 exit, fell back toward
10-11%, and the FY28 "17%" guide — already diluted across three calls in FY26 — was quietly retired.

The re-rating was violent because the entry multiple assumed a recovery that turned out to be half
accounting. A lender earning ~11% on equity against a 14.5% cost of equity does not trade at 1.35x book;
it trades at 0.77x. The multiple compressed from 1.35x toward 0.8x even as book value kept nominally
compounding, and the two forces netted to a ~40% price decline. The Pragati subsidiary loss deepened and
the un-retested goodwill was written down, adding a headline-negative quarter. Complaints and attrition,
flagged at +930-1000% and 38.1% back in the FY25 notes and never addressed by management, turned out to
have been the leading indicator of a collections franchise under strain.

Nothing here required a black swan. Every element — thin coverage, regulator-corrected ECL, a peer
(FEDFINA) already saying the stress was company-specific not industry-wide, a thrice-diluted ROE guide,
lone-expander rural risk — was visible in the 2026 analysis and flagged in the notes the thesis
under-weighted. The mistake was paying 1.35x book for a recovery that had not been earned, in front of
the two quarters that would decide it.
