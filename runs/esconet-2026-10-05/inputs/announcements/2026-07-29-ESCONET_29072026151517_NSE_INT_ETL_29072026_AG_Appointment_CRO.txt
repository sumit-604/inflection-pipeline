experience in business strategy, revenue management, enterprise sales, market expansion, channel

EMC, Hewlett Packard India, and Bharti Teletech. His proven track record in driving business transformation, 
accelerating revenue growth, strengthening strategic partnerships, and leading high-performing teams is 
expected to significantly contribute to the Company's continued growth and long-term strategic objectives.
