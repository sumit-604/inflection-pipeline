## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** GRE Renew builds solar plants for factories, offices and government buyers on a turnkey basis (EPC: engineering, procurement, construction). It also owns some plants and sells their power under 25-year contracts (RESCO). Solar EPC was Rs 111.79 crore of Rs 122.92 crore FY26 revenue; power sale was Rs 2.32 crore (FY26 res, p.18). "Others" of Rs 8.82 crore holds LED lighting and DK USA Inc (FY26 res, p.18). DK USA runs department stores, a convenience store and a gas station in Tennessee (DRHP, p.186). It earned Rs 1.91 crore of profit in FY26 (AR26, p.27). The company has 52 permanent employees (AR26, p.24).

**What is changing?** Size of order and asset ownership. On 30-Jun-2026 it signed a Rs 175 crore order for a 50 MW AC plant (Solarium, p.2). FY26 revenue was Rs 122.92 crore. It commissioned its own 7.20 MW AC plant on 27-May-2026, funded by the IPO (Comm, p.1). Owned capacity is projected at 15,525 kWp in FY27 from 3,833 kWp in FY26 (Deck, p.16).

**Why now?** The order book rose from about Rs 52 crore at 31-Mar-2026 (AR26, p.13) to Rs 248.01 crore including GST at 15-Jul-2026 (AR26, p.3). FY26 PAT rose 93% to Rs 13.58 crore (FY26 res, p.15). CRISIL assigned BBB-/Stable on 31-Jul-2026 (CRISIL).

**What must be true?** The Rs 175 crore order must execute at something near the FY26 EPC margin. A 52-person company must deliver a project larger than its whole last year. Working capital must not absorb the profit, as it did in FY26. The new auditor must sign H1 FY27 and FY27 clean.

**What breaks it?** The large order slips or loses money. The loans to the promoter's relative do not come back. The new auditor finds what the old one did not. Or the Rs 2,000 crore of subsidiary EPC limits move profit into entities the market cannot see.

## 2. Business model and archetype

FY26 standalone revenue: solar goods Rs 75.82 crore, solar services Rs 35.97 crore, power Rs 2.32 crore, LED Rs 1.22 crore (AR26, p.40). EPC segment profit before tax was Rs 20.39 crore, an 18.2% segment margin before Rs 5.41 crore of unallocated cost (FY26 res, p.18; derived). EBITDA margin was 13.10% against 11.35% (AR26, p.22).

The engine is project margin on bought-in modules and inverters. Raw material was Rs 84.30 crore of solar purchases against Rs 115.35 crore standalone revenue (AR26, p.40). RESCO adds tariff income at Rs 4.00 to Rs 5.27 per kWh (Deck, pp.17-18).

**Archetype: Order-book business (EPC).** Order inflow, book-to-bill, execution pace and working capital decide the result. A small RESCO annuity sits beside it.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Execution record: present, small.** Over 100 MWp installed by June 2026 (AR26, p.4); 61 MW of EPC at 31-Mar-2026 (AR26, p.13). **Pricing power: absent.** CRISIL calls the industry "highly fragmented" with intense competition constraining pricing power (CRISIL). **Customer stickiness: weak.** FY24 top client Uma Kraft was 25.14% of revenue and top ten 61.46% (DRHP, pp.53, 167); FY26 concentration NOT FOUND (looked AR26 pp.13-24, 39-42). **Cost position: no evidence.**

**Verdict: no moat. A regional EPC contractor with a track record, in a fragmented market.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**TO (claimed): R2.** Larger utility orders for scale economics, plus RESCO annuity for "stronger ROCE and long-term value creation" (AR26, p.4).

Management's claim, once: the Rs 175 crore order "marked an important transition towards larger-value renewable energy projects" (AR26, p.9).

Ladder check. One rung, so the base rate holds. RESCO at Rs 4.00 per kWh for 25 years (Deck, p.18) is a utility return, not pricing power. The second reading is R1 to R1 at larger size. The observation that separates them is the gross margin on the Solarium order, which no filing will show directly.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**CRISIL BBB-/Stable and A3, assigned 31-Jul-2026, on Rs 35 crore of HDFC Bank facilities** (CRISIL; filings/20260801_4d03d570, p.1). Strengths: promoter experience, an order book of about Rs 248 crore for 6 to 18 months, gearing of 0.02 times, interest cover of 82 times. Weakness: "moderate scale" and intense competition. CRISIL expects cash accrual "above Rs 18 crore per fiscal" and bank-limit use was 9%. Upgrade needs revenue above Rs 200 crore with EBITDA margin above 10%. Downgrade follows a 30% revenue fall, EBITDA margin below 9%, or a stretched working capital cycle. The rating predates both the auditor and the CFO exits. It is silent on DK USA loans.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Both halves grew, but no filed half holds the utility order or the RESCO plant (step 8).
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** The loans to the promoter's relative were there in FY25 and grew in FY26. The auditor and CFO exits, the subsidiary limits and the withheld customer names are governance choices. They do not leave when the order book executes.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Trailing PE 23.3x on Rs 13.58 crore (derived; MD market cap). Forward PE 18.3x on the step 13 run-rate. Both sit at or above the R2 neighbourhood of about 15x to 17x.

**Posture: AVOID.** Gap CLOSED plus STRUCTURAL dominates. Proof NOT FIRED plus STRUCTURAL alone reads VALUE-TRAP RISK.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The growth is real in the base business and the order book is large. The price already pays for an R2 outcome that no filed half has shown. The governance record of the first listed half is the wrong way round for a /step1 spend.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first (if revisited)**
1. H1 FY27 revenue, PAT and operating cash flow, signed by Manubhai & Shah.
2. The Rs 6.96 crore Hasmukh Patel and Laxmi Vishnu loans: terms, security, repayment (AR26, p.55).
3. Solarium order: execution period, margin, advance received (Solarium, p.2).
4. CFO appointment and the AGM vote on the new auditor.

**What would change the view**
- Up to WATCH: H1 FY27 PAT above Rs 6 crore with positive operating cash flow, a new CFO, the related-party loans repaid.
- Up to PROCEED: H2 FY27 shows the Solarium order converting at an EPC margin above 15%, cash included.
- Confirms PASS: a modified or delayed H1 FY27 review, a second auditor change, or fund flows to Solarsource or Solarcatch.
