### Reading

[INFERENCE] Syngene USA's turnover (1,087.22 FY26; 960.7 FY25) is almost equal to the business-support fee the parent books from its subsidiaries (1,033; 960). So USA's revenue looks mostly intra-group, and it is eliminated on consolidation. That leaves SSSL as the only subsidiary with material third-party revenue, from CRAMS and clinical research at Hyderabad. If so, one or both of the two customers above 10% buys research services from the Hyderabad campus, which points to the research side, not biologics manufacturing (Unit 3 and the Biocon Park biologics sit in the standalone company). The filings do not state any of this. It is the question to put to management.
