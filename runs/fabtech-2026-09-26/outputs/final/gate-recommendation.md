CASH CONVERSION FLAG: operating cash flow to PAT fell from positive in FY24 to minus 0.78 times in FY25 and 0.01 times in FY26; receivables ageing is worsening.

**Determination:** INDETERMINATE. The filed evidence leans STRUCTURAL. Items that would settle it are missing from the run record.

**Evidence:**

- FY26 operating cash flow is itself lifted by a company disclosed inventory swing from the FABL deconsolidation, so the underlying drag is larger than the headline (B03 FLAG-CASH).

- Receivables older than six months 35.3% FY25 to 43.6% FY26 of the gross book. ECL rates in the one to three year buckets 3.4 to 3.8% FY25 to 10.1% FY26. Auditor key audit matter on Rs 5,887.55 lakh overdue beyond 365 days (B02 rank 1; AR Note 13 consolidated; Auditor's Report p.139-140 txt).

- Saudi receivable Rs 72.57 Cr against Q1 FY27 Saudi revenue of Rs 17.14 Cr, tied to one contract of about Rs 120 Cr (B12b MINOR; Concall_Aug_2026 p.3-4, p.11).
- Retention terms contradicted across calls: "no retention over 6 months" (Concall_Feb_2026 p.13) against "retention period of one to two years, 10 to 15%" (Concall_Aug_2026 p.11) (B12b MAJOR).

## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring)

1. **"We compete only with unnamed European turnkey players, never Indian or Chinese firms."** Contradicted in shape by SETL (Nov-2025 call) and HLE Glascoat (Aug-2025 Omeras call), which describe mixed Indian, European, North American and Chinese fields. Weak contradiction: neither peer builds whole pharma plants (Concall_Aug_2026 p.18; SETL-Concall_Nov_2025 L263-273).
2. **SACE status.** The growth officer says "acquired"; the CEO calls SACE an incorporation with the Saudi acquisition still proposed (Concall_Aug_2026 p.4-5 vs p.6, p.19). Acquisition filings exist dated 02-Jun-2026 and 25-Jun-2026. Treat the acquisition as NOT CONFIRMED CLOSED.
3. **Retention terms.** "No retention over 6 months" (Concall_Feb_2026 p.13) against "one to two years, 10 to 15%" (Concall_Aug_2026 p.11).
4. **Revenue recognition.** Described as project progress, bill of lading, and milestones with customer approvals by different speakers (Concall_Apr_2026 p.16; Concall_Aug_2026 p.3, p.8, p.9). The AR policy says actual shipments (AR p.158 txt). The founder also admitted pulling Q3 revenue into Q2 and holding shipments that the LC permits (Concall_Feb_2026 p.12).
5. **Hot lead pipeline.** USD 455m (Concall_Feb_2026 p.7), then close to USD 200m (Concall_Apr_2026 p.11), then Rs 3,800 Cr hot leads plus Rs 9,300 Cr enquiries (Concall_Aug_2026 p.4, p.7).
6. **International share.** About 55% on the Q1 FY27 investor presentation (slide 12) against about 89% in the FY26 AR MD&A (AR p.44 txt).
7. **FY27 revenue figure.** Rs 530 to 600 Cr reportedly given at investor conferences, unreconciled with the official 20 to 25% growth guide (Concall_Apr_2026, analyst question).
8. **Broken promises.** "Margins better than last year, for sure" (Concall_Nov_2025 p.15), then EBITDA margin fell from 19.9% to 14.1%. "Q1 and Q2 the receivable will be reduced immediately" (Concall_Apr_2026 p.18), then Rs 211 to 215 Cr at Q1 (Concall_Aug_2026 p.3-4).

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated, 8)

1. **H1 FY27 cash flow.** Read the half year statement of cash flows filed with the Q2 FY27 results (half year to 30-Sep-2026). Operating cash flow above half of H1 PAT is the first real easing. A negative number confirms the cash drag. This settles FLAG-CASH.
2. **Aged receivables.** Watch total receivables against the Rs 211 to 215 Cr Q1 level, and the over six month share against 43.6% in the FY27 AR Note 13. Watch the Rs 58.9 Cr beyond one year and the Rs 72.57 Cr Saudi receivable for collection. This tests whether the ageing is retention or impairment.
3. **Order book burn.** From each quarter's disclosure, compute inflow as closing book minus opening book plus revenue. The book must move above the Rs 900 to 926 Cr band, with a numeric breakdown by geography. This tests the whole growth claim.
4. **Q2 FY27 revenue growth.** Q1 grew 10.3%. Growth near 10% again in Q2 makes a fourth guidance cut likely and breaks the second half weighting claim. Source: Q2 FY27 results and call.
5. **SACE and Saudi.** Watch for an exchange filing that confirms the Saudi acquisition closed, and for a second exchange filed Saudi civil or MEP order within two quarters. No filing kills the localisation trigger.
6. **Earnings quality.** Watch other income as a share of PBT (42.6% in FY26; below 15% is healthy) and the FX hedge ratio against about Rs 221 Cr of foreign currency assets. Source: quarterly results notes and the FY27 AR Note 32 and Note 48.
7. **Related parties and governance.** Watch for the counterparty behind the Rs 23.5 Cr related party loan, the G7 Universal commission, related party sales against the Rs 60 Cr FY27 ceiling sought at the AGM, and the profit of FTS Cleanrooms Systems LLC (about a third of consolidated PAT; Rs 11.6 Cr to Rs 7.0 Cr in FY26 on higher revenue). Also watch the new Company Secretary appointment and the named Executive Chairman. Source: FY27 AR Note 49 and exchange filings.
8. **IPO proceeds and the Italy deal.** Watch monitoring agency reports and deviation statements for the first rupee against the Rs 30 Cr acquisition bucket, and for the Italy closing before March 2027. A third slip retires the Italy trigger.
