### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

Every miss across the three mandatory quarters is explained by an external factor: Q3 FY26
softness (shipment timing at the port), FY26 margin pressure (RMC cost +33%, execution/
remobilisation cost +43%, both tied to the Middle East war and freight costs, Apr-2026
call), Q1 FY27 UAE weakness (regional war, not company execution). This is
external-blame-heavy. One clear counter-example of honest admission exists: "Honestly
speaking, we were not very communicative" on pre-IPO investor engagement (Aasif Khan,
Feb-2026 call). Management does raise some hard topics proactively (UAE weakness, RMC
inflation, receivable build-up) rather than waiting to be asked, which is a mitigating
signal. But no miss is ever framed as "we made a mistake in planning or delivery" — every
shortfall is a market condition, a timing effect, or a deliberate strategic trade-off.

Classification: **external-blame-heavy**, with one notable admission on communication style.

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A Competitor commentary and credibility check

Management repeatedly declines to name competitors ("we usually refrain from talking
about competition... we prefer not talking about European companies," Feb-2026 call) but
asserts the field is European, not Indian or Chinese ("Fabtech Technologies Limited...
that's why we don't compete with Asians. We compete with Europeans," Aug-2026 call). No
competitor is ever named, and no market-share number is given for Fabtech versus these
unnamed European players. This is an unverifiable claim — it should be tested against the
peer set (SETL, HLEGLAS, PRAJIND all compete on domestic/export pharma-process equipment,
not the same turnkey design-build niche Fabtech claims, so the peer check is partial by
design; see peer questions below).

### 3B Industry and market intelligence

- Market size claims: "north of $30 billion" for pharmaceutical-emerging-market (MENA,
  Africa, Southeast Asia) infrastructure over the next 10 years; "more than $70 billion"
  for the West over the same period (Aug-2026 call, Aman Anavkar) — NON-ANCHORED, no
  source cited, third-party verification needed.
- 78% of Fabtech's revenue comes from MENA/GCC/ECO/Persian Gulf as of Q1 FY27 (Aug-2026
  call, Aman Anavkar).
- Vision 2030/2040 Saudi localisation driving demand for in-kingdom manufacturing partners
  (repeated across all three calls); local content described as moving from "preference"
  to "hard qualification criteria" for Saudi tenders (Aug-2026 call).
- Post-COVID push in Africa and the Middle East towards animal health/veterinary
  manufacturing described as "opening now," earlier than management expected (Feb-2026
  call, Aasif Khan).
- Input-cost inflation: RMC (raw material cost) up ~33% YoY, execution/remobilisation cost
  up ~43% YoY in FY26, both attributed to global supply-chain and geopolitical disruption
  (Apr-2026 call, Aman Anavkar).
- Donor/government funding cycles (World Bank, DFI-backed programmes) cited as a growth
  channel for African vaccine/health infrastructure projects, with some large-ticket
  opportunities "slipped... due to geopolitical conditions and donor/government funding
  cycles" but "not cancelled" (Aug-2026 call).
