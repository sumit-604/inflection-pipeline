### 1C-1D. Background, succession history

Prakash and Deepak Chhabria are first cousins, not father-son; the "succession
dispute" here is a **sibling/cousin-generation control fight** following the 2016
death of founder Prahlad P. Chhabria, not a founder-vs-professional-management
story. At its root (per web sources) is a 2016 gift deed on a ₹500 stamp paper
purporting to transfer 100,300 Orbit Electricals shares (70.4% of Orbit) from
Prahlad to Prakash — a transfer Deepak and his mother Krishna Chhabria challenged
in Pune Civil Court, alleging an oral agreement that both family branches would
hold Orbit equally (Business Standard, "Dispute over inheritance worth Rs
1,500-cr flares up family feud at Finolex," 2019-04-20; M&A Critique, 2024).
📰 MEDIA REPORTED, corroborated across multiple outlets, not officially litigated
to a final finding on the underlying gift-deed validity as of this run date.
