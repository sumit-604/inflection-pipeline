## MENTAL MODEL — DRAFT, UNSIGNED (pending Halt 1)

**Archetype:** Sponsor-led roll-up of under-invested high-return niche franchises.
Listed vehicle used as the acquisition platform.

**FROM state:** Domestic-only borate converter. 55% of revenue is dollar-denominated
imported ore from a supplier (Eti Maden) that also competes downstream. Zero exports.
Landlocked plant at Pithampur. Half the balance sheet in mutual funds. Family-owned
for forty years, promoter taking ~15% of PAT. Revenue CAGR 8.4% over five years,
roughly equal to pond growth.

**TO state:** Export-capable inorganic chemicals platform with a coastal footprint,
a regulated-markets channel, and a specialty mix that dampens the ore-price swing.

**ENGINE:** EAAA/Zenrock deploying a Rs 700 cr Section 186 envelope through control
acquisitions of the same setup they bought here, executed by a leadership team hired
from WR Grace, Brenntag and Lupin ahead of the first deal.

**DOMINANT VARIABLES (4):**
  1. Borate ore cost and INR (55% of revenue, entirely uncontrolled)
  2. Whether boric acid IP grade enters Kronox's export catalogue
  3. Whether Dahej becomes the site for boron oxide (value density finally
     supports freight at 2-3x boric acid price)
  4. Capital allocation quality on acquisitions 2, 3 and 4 inside the remaining
     ~Rs 300-450 cr envelope

**WHAT THE MODEL REJECTS AS NOISE:**
  - Q1 FY27's 28.1% EBITDA margin as evidence of structural improvement.
    It is above every full year in the record. Ore spread until proven otherwise.
  - The Rs 40 FY26 dividend as a governance signal in either direction.
    Affiliate-funded structure removes the extraction read; treasury repair is
    the simpler explanation.
  - Reported FY26 PAT of Rs 49.74 cr. Includes Rs 10.15 cr exceptional from
    selling property and vehicles to the exiting promoters. Core PAT ~Rs 42 cr,
    flat against FY25.

**PROOF GATE (any two must fire):**
  (a) Dahej Unit IV construction start announced by 31-Mar-2027
  (b) First disclosed boric acid IP / export revenue line by the FY28 AR
  (c) Second acquisition inside the envelope at <= 12x attributable earnings

**FALSIFIER:** Two consecutive quarters of clean operating EBITDA below 22%,
AND no Dahej construction start by Mar-2027, AND no export revenue line by FY28.
All three together = the transition is narrative only.
