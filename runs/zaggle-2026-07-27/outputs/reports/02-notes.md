**NEW FINDING (Pass 3) — Pass 1 mislabeled a Related Party Transaction figure: the true KMP-specific ESOP charge fell 82.2%, not 38.2%**

Direct re-check of standalone Note 32(b), "Transactions with related parties" (p.230), against Note 46(v), "Break-up of employee stock compensation expense" (p.304, consolidated equivalent; standalone Note 46 mirrors), surfaces a genuine cross-note numerical mismatch in Pass 1's own extraction:

- Note 32(b)(ii), "Employee stock option plan (Refer Note 46)" — the RPT-note line item that specifically discloses the KMP-attributable ESOP charge — is **₹0.373 Cr (3.73 Mn) FY25 vs ₹2.098 Cr (20.98 Mn) FY24, a fall of 82.2% YoY.**
- Note 46(v) separately splits the ESOP 2022 Plan expense into "KMP: 3.73 Mn / 20.98 Mn" and "Employees other than KMP: 88.87 Mn / 128.79 Mn," summing to the **company-wide total of ₹9.26 Cr (92.60 Mn) FY25 vs ₹14.98 Cr (149.77 Mn) FY24 (-38.2%)** — this total-company figure is what Pass 1 placed into its RPT table under "Employees incl. KMP | ESOP charge," incorrectly presenting a whole-company expense as though it were the related-party (KMP) disclosure quantum.

This is a genuine, source-verified correction, not a matter of interpretation: the KMP-specific ESOP-RPT charge collapsed 82.2% YoY (₹2.10 Cr to ₹0.37 Cr), a materially sharper decline than the 38.2% company-wide figure Pass 1 reported as if it were the RPT number. The direction (stock-comp cost declining) does not change, but the magnitude attributed to KMP specifically is nearly 4x smaller in FY25 than the mislabeled figure implied, and the prior-year KMP-specific base was likewise smaller than stated. 🟢 Clean/minor — a correction to Pass 1's RPT table, surfaced only by the pattern-pass practice of cross-checking note tables against each other rather than reading each note in isolation. Flagged as a reminder that Pass 1/Pass 2 RPT and ESOP figures should be read using the corrected KMP-specific line above, not the total company-wide ESOP charge.

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

The one substantive red flag is valuation/M&A-quality, not manipulation: ₹36.35 Cr of goodwill (57% of Enterprise Value) booked for a target that is loss-making with negative net assets in its post-acquisition stub period, tested for impairment with only a qualitative sensitivity assertion at a discount rate (22.24%) that itself signals elevated risk being priced in.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators:** ₹36.14 Cr Effiasoft acquisition advance with zero qualitative narrative; unnamed related "body corporate" providing a corporate guarantee on the Company's secured borrowings; a dormant foreign subsidiary's disappearance from the group structure with no explanation.
- **Non-recurring items blended into recurring lines:** the ₹2.275 Cr one-time Ind AS 103 step-acquisition remeasurement gain sits inside ordinary "Other income" with no exceptional-item caption, inflating the apparent quality of reported PBT growth if not manually adjusted out by the reader.
- **Note-quality/precision issue (self-referential):** Pass 1's own extraction mislabeled the KMP-specific ESOP-RPT charge, understating the true magnitude of decline (82.2% vs the 38.2% company-wide figure it used); corrected in this Pass 3 consolidation. This is a caution on note density, not a company-side disclosure defect.

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices, genuinely clean on working capital and deleveraging but concerning on acquisition-accounting judgment. Key strength: receivables ageing, ECL provisioning, and secured-debt reduction all show genuine, well-documented improvement with no restatements or going-concern language.
