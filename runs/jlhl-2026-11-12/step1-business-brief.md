# STEP 1 BUSINESS BRIEF: Jupiter Life Line Hospitals Ltd (JLHL)

Run date 2026-11-12. Status: COMPANY MEMORY. Web-sourced, not anchored evidence. Every stage number comes from the filings.

Identity: Jupiter Life Line Hospitals Ltd, NSE JLHL, BSE 543980. Screener: https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/ (check_url OK 200). Listed September 2023 (IPO; prospectus is a mandatory attempt).

1. Size and price. Market cap Rs 9,391 Cr, CMP Rs 287, P/E 49.4, ROCE 14.8%, ROE 13.1%, book value Rs 49.7 (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/).
2. What it sells. Tertiary and quaternary hospital care under the "Jupiter" brand in western India. Four hospitals: Thane, Pune (Baner), Indore, Dombivli. 1,248 operational beds, 140 of them non-census (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/). One reporting segment (healthcare services); the mix that matters is per hospital and per payor.
3. Growth record. Sales Rs 463 Cr (FY20) to Rs 1,500 Cr (FY26), TTM Rs 1,621 Cr. Operating margin 23% in each of FY23 to FY26 (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/).
4. Where the margin comes from. Mature hospitals (Thane, Pune) with high ARPOB and owned land and buildings ("all hub, no spoke" model). ARPOB Rs 71,800 per day, up about 9% YoY; in-patient ARPOB Rs 58,700; ALOS 3.79 days (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/jupiter-life-line-q2-results-cmvtmb5kt002d2gqiunynyav0 ; https://plindia.com/ResReport/JLHL-27-10-26-PL.pdf).
5. Latest quarter, Q2 FY27. Total income Rs 456.5 Cr (+16% YoY), EBITDA Rs 106.9 Cr, PAT Rs 56.4 Cr against Rs 57.4 Cr a year ago. H1 FY27: income Rs 867.5 Cr, EBITDA Rs 186.2 Cr, PAT Rs 93.9 Cr (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/jupiter-life-line-q2-results-cmvtmb5kt002d2gqiunynyav0).
6. The profit squeeze. Revenue grows 15-16% but PAT is flat: FY25 Rs 194 Cr, FY26 Rs 194 Cr, TTM Rs 187 Cr. Depreciation rose from Rs 57 Cr (FY25) to Rs 100 Cr (TTM). Interest rose from Rs 11 Cr to Rs 43 Cr (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/).
7. Occupancy. H1 FY27 occupancy 59.5% across all hospitals, 64.8% excluding Dombivli (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/jupiter-life-line-q2-results-cmvtmb5kt002d2gqiunynyav0).
8. The engine: bed doubling. Dombivli (about 500 beds planned) opened in 2026. Its EBITDA loss fell from Rs 9.5 Cr (Q1 FY27) to Rs 0.2 Cr (Q2 FY27); occupancy mid-30%; cashless insurance empanelment still pending (https://plindia.com/ResReport/JLHL-27-10-26-PL.pdf). Pipeline: Pune South (about 500 beds, construction started, Rs 150 Cr land), Mira Road (regulatory stage), BKC Mumbai (planning; land deal about Rs 354 Cr). Target about 2,900 beds in four to five years (https://in.tradingview.com/news/moneycontrol:4d3e29c7c094b:0-jupiter-life-line-in-rs-1-400-crore-expansion-push-in-maharashtra-to-double-capacity-by-2029 ; https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/jupiter-hospital-bkc-land-deal-cmn984bzz0009pa0jiw4a2b63).
9. Balance sheet. Borrowings moved from Rs 0 (Mar 2024, after IPO repayment) to Rs 669 Cr (Sep 2026). CWIP Rs 194 Cr. FY26 CFO Rs 268 Cr, free cash flow minus Rs 234 Cr (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/). Web sources disagree on funding: one says internal accruals only, the balance sheet shows debt.
10. Ownership. Promoters 40.91%, flat since listing. FII 9.13%, DII 19.13% at Sep 2026 (https://www.screener.in/company/JLHL/consolidated/). FII plus DII above 3%, so the UA qualifier does not hold on this reading.
11. Customers. Retail patients, insurers and corporates in MMR, Pune and Indore. No export profile. Payor mix percentages NOT FOUND on the web; the corpus must supply them.
12. The one inflection. Operating leverage from new beds: Dombivli ramp now, Pune South, Mira Road and BKC later. The thesis rests on new hospitals reaching the Thane and Pune margin while ARPOB keeps rising. The counter-reading: a 23% margin plateau, falling ROCE during a multi-year capex cycle, and a P/E of 49 that already prices the climb.

Sector cap row (Step D): "Hospitals / dialysis / healthcare services", 35x (section-1b chunk 05).

## PEERS (selected by economic engine: regional tertiary cluster, owned assets, greenfield bed additions)
- Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences (KIMS): https://www.screener.in/company/KIMS/consolidated/ . Regional cluster leader adding greenfield beds, including new Maharashtra units (Thane, Nashik). It meets Jupiter in its home market and shows the same depreciation and interest drag from new units.
- Yatharth Hospital and Trauma Care (YATHARTH): https://www.screener.in/company/YATHARTH/consolidated/ . Single-region (NCR) cluster of similar size, listed 2023, growing by bed additions and ARPOB improvement. The closest size and stage match.
- Global Health (MEDANTA): https://www.screener.in/company/MEDANTA/consolidated/ . Owned, large-format tertiary and quaternary hospitals; new-unit ramp (Lucknow, Patna, Noida) shows the maturity curve Dombivli must follow. It also plans a Mumbai hospital.
No listed western-India pure-play hospital of comparable scale exists; these are the closest structural comps. Apollo, Max and Fortis were left out: national scale and acquisition-led growth do not mirror a three-city greenfield model.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)
1. Dombivli ramp: EBITDA loss Rs 9.5 Cr (Q1 FY27) to Rs 0.2 Cr (Q2 FY27), occupancy mid-30%, opened ahead of the Q1 FY27 guidance. Verify against the Q1 and Q2 FY27 results, presentations and transcripts (guidance-vs-delivery pair).
2. Profit squeeze: PAT flat near Rs 194 Cr in FY25 and FY26 while revenue grew 15%; depreciation Rs 57 Cr to Rs 88 Cr and interest Rs 11 Cr to Rs 33 Cr (FY25 to FY26). Verify against the FY26 AR and results; confirm the mature-hospital margin held.
3. Capex funding and debt: borrowings Rs 0 (Mar 2024) to Rs 669 Cr (Sep 2026); pipeline of Pune South, Mira Road and BKC (land about Rs 354 Cr). Verify total committed capex, its phasing, and the "internal accruals" claim against the cash flow statement and the borrowing note.
4. Occupancy and ARPOB: H1 FY27 occupancy 59.5% (64.8% excluding Dombivli); ARPOB Rs 71,800. Verify the definitions (census beds, blended against in-patient ARPOB) and the hospital-wise split against the presentation.
