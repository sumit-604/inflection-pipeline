## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition** (Note 2(i), Consol. p.235–236): point-in-time on dispatch/delivery; no over-time/percentage-of-completion recognition despite long manufacturing lead times. Practical expedient taken to not disclose remaining performance obligations for contracts ≤1 year. 🟢 Clean, no aggressive recognition found.
- **Depreciation useful lives** (Note 2(r), Consol. p.237): Plant & machinery 1.5–10 years, Computers 3–6, Furniture 1.5–5, Vehicles 10, Leasehold improvements 10-or-lease-term. Explicitly stated as **higher than Schedule II** "to reflect actual usage" per management's technical evaluation — a longer life than the statutory default understates annual depreciation vs. the Schedule II floor, which is favourable to reported profit in a year the Group posted a wider loss. No quantified P&L impact of the policy vs. Schedule II is given. 🟡 Watch.
- **Capitalisation threshold**: NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (no monetary threshold disclosed for capex vs. expense).
- **Impairment test (goodwill)** (Note 6, Consol. p.248–249): discount rate 14.13%–17.40% across years/CGUs, terminal growth 5.00%, five-year cash-flow projections; sensitivity to ±0.5% sales/terminal growth quantified. Reasonably transparent. 🟢 Clean.
- **ECL matrix**: simplified Ind AS 109 approach for trade receivables (Note 2(q), p.235; Note 28(A)(ii), Consol. p.278–279), but **no ageing-bucket loss-rate table is disclosed** — only a rollforward of the aggregate allowance. Less granular than best-practice ECL disclosure. 🟡 Watch — see also Finding 5 below (coverage falling while receivables grow).
- **Ind AS 116 leases** (Note 2(l) p.238–239, Note 5 p.246–247): lease term 6–10 years; incremental borrowing rate **6%–13.5% p.a.** — a wide range spanning India/France/US leases; the top end (13.5%) is high and not attributed to a specific lease. Total lease liability ₹297.35 Cr (2,973.53 Mn) FY26 vs ₹347.99 Cr (3,479.85 Mn) FY25.
- **First-time adoptions**: none in the current year. FY26 is the first year reporting as a listed entity (IPO 10-Dec-2025), so the Schedule III "Financial ratios" note (Note 33, Standalone only) is a new mandatory disclosure this year.
- **Direct debits/credits to reserves bypassing P&L**: IPO/pre-IPO costs of ₹47.62 Cr (476.22 Mn) total; ₹3.90 Cr (39.02 Mn) expensed as exceptional, **₹43.72 Cr (437.20 Mn) charged directly to Securities Premium**, bypassing P&L (Note 26 Consol. p.277 / Note 24 Standalone p.199–200). Standard IPO treatment, not itself a red flag, but the checklist requires it be named.
