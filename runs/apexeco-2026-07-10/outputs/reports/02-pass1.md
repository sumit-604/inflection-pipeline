## NOTE 1 — MATERIAL ACCOUNTING POLICIES (p.49–50)

- **Basis of preparation**: IGAAP, historical cost, accrual basis, policies "consistent with those of
  previous year" (Note 1a, p.49). No first-time standard adoption this year. 🟢

- **Revenue recognition**: Sale of goods recognized on transfer of risk/rewards; sale of services
  recognized "as services are rendered based on agreements/arrangements with customers" (Note 1e,
  p.49). No percentage-of-completion / stage-of-completion methodology is disclosed despite the company
  being an EPC/turnkey water-treatment contractor with large project retentions (Note 10 shows ₹1,056.03
  lakh of retentions within trade receivables). The policy language is generic and does not describe how
  revenue is recognized on long-duration EPC contracts specifically. 🟡 Watch — vague revenue recognition
  policy for an EPC contractor (Note 1e, p.49; cross-ref Note 10, p.55).

- **Depreciation**: WDV method. Useful lives: Plant & Machinery 20 yrs, Vehicles 8 yrs, Furniture &
  Fixtures 5 yrs, Office Equipment 5 yrs, Computers 3 yrs, Software 3 yrs. Company explicitly states these
  "might be different from those prescribed in Schedule II of the Act" (Note 1g, p.50) — an admitted
  deviation from the statutory schedule with no independent technical/actuarial justification disclosed. 🟡
  Watch.

- **Impairment**: No impairment testing disclosure — not applicable; no goodwill, minimal intangibles
  (software only, net block ₹0.61 lakh). 🟢

- **Employee benefits**: Gratuity per AS-15 (Revised) actuarial valuation (Note 1l, p.50) — but no
  actuarial assumptions (discount rate, salary escalation, mortality table) are disclosed anywhere in the
  notes (cross-ref Note 5). 🟡 Watch.

- **Trade receivables / doubtful debts**: No ECL matrix (IGAAP, not applicable) and no formal
  provisioning policy for doubtful debts is stated at all — only ad hoc "bad debts written off" appears in
  Note 19. Cross-ref Note 10 for the resulting red flag on zero provisioning against a receivables base that
  grew 155% YoY. 🔴 Red flag (detailed under Note 10).

- **Deferred tax**: Standard recognition policy, virtual/reasonable certainty test disclosed (Note 1i, p.50).
  🟢
