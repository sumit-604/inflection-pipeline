RESULTS AND UPDATES FOR THE QUARTER ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026
Q2 FY27 revenue grows 77% year-on-year; EBITDA rises to Rs 1,342 Lakhs 
 WIL reports sustained profitability with fourth consecutive quarter of positive PBT 
 
Mumbai, November 2, 2026:  Walchandnagar Industries Limited (“WIL” / “Company”), a renowned 
engineering company known for its de sign, en gineering & h igh co mplex precision manufacturing 
capabilities with operational legacy of more than 117 years, announced its results for the quarter 
ended 30th September, 2026. 
 
Q2 FY27 Results and Outlook: 
 
 WIL reported total revenue of Rs 9,815 Lakhs in Q2 FY27, an increase of 77% over Rs. 5,542 
Lakhs in Q2 FY26. Revenue from operations grew by 81% year-on-year to Rs. 9,353 Lakhs 
 EBITDA increased significantly to Rs . 1,342 Lakhs, compared with Rs . 61 Lakhs in the 
corresponding quarter of the previous year, reflecting the impro vement in operating 
performance 
 The Company recorded a Profit before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 47 Lakhs in Q2 FY27, as against a loss 
of Rs. 1,190 Lakhs in Q2 FY26. 
 Importantly, WIL has now delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of positive PBT. This 
sustained performance demonstrates that the improvement in profitability is consistent and 
is translating through to the bottom line 
 Revenue from operations remained broadly stable sequentially at Rs. 9,353 Lakhs compared 
with Rs. 9,083 Lakhs in Q1 FY27, while the Company continued to report positive EBITDA and 
PBT 
 The continued improvement reflects the benefits of disciplined execution, operating 
leverage, cost focus and a sharper emphasis on profitable business

Order Inflows and Aerospace Business 
During Q2 FY27, WIL secured orders aggregating Rs. 99.46 Crores, including orders worth Rs. 38.13 Crores 
from its Aerospace business. The contribution from Aerospace reinforces the Company's positioning in 
high-precision engineering and manufacturing for strategic sectors and supports its focus on building a 
stronger order pipeline in its chosen growth businesses. 
 
Nuclear Business – Order Flow Initiated 
The Nuclear business has reached an important milestone with the release of the tender for End Shield. 
This marks the initiation of the anticipated order flow in the Nuclear segment and is an encouraging 
development as WIL continues to build its presence and capabilities in the sector.

The passage of the Shanti Bill by Parliament in December 2025 is expected to create a wider framework 
for private sector participation in India's nuclear power program. Notifications and detailed rules under 
the legislation are expected to be announced in the near future. With the enabling framework taking 
shape, a number of private sector players are expected to firm up their plans and place orders over the 
next 12 months. This could create a meaningful opportunity for qualified engineering and manufacturing 
companies like Walchandnagar Industries.
