## DECISION LINE

Mechanical Gate 0 classification is AVOID, driven by a decade dominated by
NCLT-insolvency-era losses and near-zero/negative equity, with cumulative CFO
still short of cumulative PAT and no confirmed moat evidence in the data
provided. Per pipeline operating rules, this classification does not halt the
run — company-quality signals never stop the pipeline, and there is no STOP
verdict in this framework. The classification and every deal-breaker are
flagged (FLAG-GATE0) for downstream stages to weigh against the
post-resolution turnaround narrative: FY26 marked the first reported profit
(Rs45.40cr) since the NCLT takeover, cash flow from operations has grown
roughly 7.9x over three years (Rs6.61cr FY23 to Rs52.29cr FY26), and a Rs93cr
rights issue (promoter foregoing entitlement) is underway. However, the FY26
profit and the FY26 ROCE improvement are **substantially driven by a
Rs55.59cr one-time land-sale gain** (pre-exceptional consolidated PBT FY26
was only Rs1.50cr) — this materially qualifies the turnaround narrative on an
operating basis and should be weighted heavily by any downstream stage that
treats FY26 as evidence of sustained operating recovery. Shareholder-alignment
metrics (promoter %, pledge) rest on a 16-month-stale AR and are flagged as
needing SHP-filing confirmation before being relied upon.
