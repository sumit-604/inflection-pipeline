Existing manufacturing capacity is stated at 100Mn injectable units p.a. (AR p.36), consistent with
the "~10 Crore units" near-term operating range cited as the base VIP is meant to multiply (AR

month on the same infrastructure and energy footprint, without new plant (AR p.42). This is
cross-checked against financials: gross Plant & Machinery block grew by only Rs3.63 Cr in FY26
(Note 2(A), AR p.104) while revenue grew 20% and EBITDA nearly doubled — consistent with utilisation
gains rather than new-capacity-driven growth in FY26 itself. 📄.

2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Capex under execution used: Rs51.36 Cr (FY26 CWIP balance, Note 2(B), AR p.99/105). VIP is
excluded because it carries no disclosed rupee figure — it is a target-capacity statement, not a
capex commitment, and folding it in would violate the "never estimate" rule.

Two fixed-asset-turnover bases were tested, and they diverge sharply:
- Net-block basis: FY26 net PP&E (WDV) = Rs110.23 Cr, FY25 = Rs110.93 Cr, average Rs110.58 Cr
  (Note 2(A), AR p.104). FY26 revenue Rs769.60 Cr (AR p.36). FAT(net) = 769.60 / 110.58 = 6.96x.
  51.36 x 6.96 = Rs357.5 Cr implied incremental revenue = 46.4% of current revenue.
- Gross-block basis: FY26 gross PP&E = Rs322.52 Cr (Note 2(A), AR p.104). FAT(gross) =
  769.60 / 322.52 = 2.39x. 51.36 x 2.39 = Rs122.6 Cr implied incremental revenue = 15.9% of
  current revenue.

The net-block figure is flagged as unreliable and not used: WDV is only 34% of gross carrying
value (Rs110.23 Cr of Rs322.52 Cr), meaning the asset base is heavily depreciated. Applying a
turnover ratio computed off a mostly-depreciated denominator to fresh capex overstates the implied
revenue add — it is an artefact of accounting age, not of asset productivity. The gross-block
figure (~16% of current revenue) is used as the conservative, defensible read and is carried to
capex_embedded_growth_pct. Both are shown per the instruction to "show the arithmetic"; only the
conservative one is scored.
