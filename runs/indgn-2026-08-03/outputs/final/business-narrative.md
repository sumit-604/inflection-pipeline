The margin print is the whole thesis

Indegene sells outsourced commercialization, medical, regulatory and digital operations services to the world's pharmaceutical companies, not drugs and not manufacturing capacity. FY26 revenue was Rs 3,510 Cr, PAT Rs 401 Cr, and the balance sheet holds net cash near Rs 1,337 Cr with nil debt. Two segments do the work: Enterprise Commercial Solutions at 70% of revenue and Enterprise Medical Solutions at 27%, with a small Consulting and Clinical bucket at 3% that loses money. The shares trade near 33 times trailing earnings at Rs 545 and a market value of Rs 13,142 Cr. Management states in the annual report that it is not a CRO, CDMO or generic IT vendor.

Two of four transitions are firing. Revenue and cash conversion are both accelerating, capital efficiency is recovering on a mostly mechanical basis, and the margin transition is stagnant, the one leg with no documented action behind it. Headline revenue grew 23.6% in FY26, but organic constant currency growth decelerated from 18.3% in Q3 to 12% in Q4 as the BioPharm acquisition was consolidated at gross revenue. Management pushed the EBITDA margin recovery toward roughly 20% out to H2 FY27 and quietly dropped the earlier explicit target.

FY26 PAT fell 1.4% even as revenue rose 23.6%, and that gap is the single most important fact in the numbers. The drag is a Rs 20.3 Cr TCPA litigation provision, a roughly Rs 42 Cr fall in other income, and rising acquisition amortization from the BioPharm, Warn and Cake deals. Standalone India PAT grew 16.9% over the same year, so the drag sits entirely in the international and acquired subsidiaries, not in the core. Gate 0 scored GOOD+ at 90 core and 101 grand total, with no deal breakers. Cash quality improved for real: operating cash flow to PAT rose to 1.62 times from 1.09 times, though receivables grew 30.7% and unbilled revenue 77.5%, faster than sales, which is why the cash flag is live and classified growth induced.

Management earns a B on credibility. It completed BioPharm support function integration ahead of schedule, honored a stated no guidance policy through every quarter despite repeated analyst pressure, and improved debtor days from 72 to about 63. Against that, it softened the margin recovery specificity between quarters, never once walked analysts through the standalone versus consolidated PAT bridge behind the reported decline, and did not proactively flag the organic deceleration. Executive pay was flat to down in the company's largest acquisition year.

Adjacent peers complicate the story rather than confirm it. eClerx, IKS Health, Sagility and Tata Elxsi are the closest listed comparators, none in pharma commercialization, so the demand and revenue per employee claims stay unverifiable. On the claim that matters most, the "we have recovered margins in 6 to 8 quarters before" precedent is contradicted: IKS beat its own recovery timeline by 3 to 4 quarters and Sagility absorbed its margin drag inside one fiscal year, which frames Indegene as slow rather than proven. Three of four peers showed the same Jan to Mar 2026 organic softness, but Sagility accelerated, and eClerx openly conceded possible AI driven price deflation on FY27 bookings.

The runway is not the constraint. Independent triangulation puts the addressable market above USD 150 bn against Indegene's own USD 135 bn claim, so management's number reads conservative at 0.98 times, and current revenue sits 243 times below the five year serviceable opportunity, a runway class of MASSIVE. The binding limit is domain trained talent and attrition near 15.7%, not capital and not demand.

Valuation lands at BUY on dips. The destination multiple is 31.5 times forward earnings on the operator elected additive track, or 28.1 times on the more conservative return recovery track, applied to a forward EPS that climbs from about Rs 20 in FY27 to Rs 37 in FY30 on the margin recovery and the contractual amortization step down. That produces a base fair value near Rs 1,154 and a probability weighted three year CAGR of 27.4%. The Hurdle Ratio is 2.12 against the Tier B threshold of 1.728, a PASS. The entry zone is Rs 534 to Rs 668 with the ideal margin of safety floor at Rs 534, and CMP Rs 545 sits inside the zone about 2% above that floor. The stock closed FY26 at Rs 434.6, below the floor, so the zone is reachable now, not a market fantasy. Position size is Medium 4 to 6%, with a staged Small starter scaling as the margin catalyst confirms.

The bear case is not weak, and the honest read carries it in full. The devil's advocate grades the thesis WEAKENED BUT ALIVE with valuation safety destroyed. At Rs 545 there is no 20% to 30% margin of safety, because the price already equals the combined sensitivity floor of Rs 534. Rebuild the Hurdle on the model's own no margin recovery bear FY27 EPS of Rs 17.51 and the forward PE is 31.14 times with a Hurdle Ratio of 1.56, below the 1.728 threshold and a FAIL. The PASS therefore exists only because the assumed margin recovery is credited into both the forward EPS denominator and the destination multiple at once. That recovery has no documented action behind it, so the whole call rests on whether operating margin expands sequentially for two consecutive quarters from 16.4% starting Q2 FY27.
