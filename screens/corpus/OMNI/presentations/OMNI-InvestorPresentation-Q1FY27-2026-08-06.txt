===== PAGE 8: Financial Performance ratios =====
ROCE (%):                FY24 23.8 | FY25 21.6 | FY26 11.7 | Q1 FY27 16.8
ROE (%):                 FY24 14.8 | FY25 16.1 | FY26 13.7 | Q1 FY27 17.8
Net Debt to Equity (x):  FY24 2.87 | FY25 1.60 | FY26 0.34 | Q1 FY27 0.41
Net Working Capital Days:FY24  197 | FY25  283 | FY26  294 | Q1 FY27  233
"Q1 FY27 figures are Annualized."
Definitions as printed: ROCE is EBIT divided by Capital Employed, where Capital Employed is Total
Equity (including non-controlling interest) plus Non-current Borrowings plus Current Borrowings, and
EBIT is Profit for the Year plus Finance Costs and Total Tax Expenses. Net Debt to Equity is Net Debt
(Non-current plus Current Borrowings less Cash and Cash Equivalents) divided by Total Equity. Net
Working Capital Days is Net Working Capital divided by Revenue from Operations times 365, where Net
Working Capital is Receivables plus Inventory plus Other Financial and Current Assets (excluding cash
and current investments) minus Payables and Other Financial and Current Liabilities (excluding
current borrowings).
