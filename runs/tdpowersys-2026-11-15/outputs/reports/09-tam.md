## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

1A. Boundaries.
- Product: AC generators for gas engines and gas turbines (largest line by words only, share NOT FOUND, B04), steam turbines (captive, biomass, waste heat), hydro (new and refurbishment), wind; large motors; spares. - Geography: global. About 70% of FY26 group sales overseas (CRISIL p.1); standalone exports 56.4%, domestic 42.8% (AR p.170 per B04). 111 countries (CRISIL p.2). Own sales offices in Europe and USA.

- Channel: build-to-print and serial OEM supply; no minimum volume in the Siemens Energy framework (B04 flag).

Method 1, top-down.
- Start: gas generator, global, 2026: USD 5.9 bn x 9,525 = Rs 56,198 Cr (AR p.57).
- Subtract nothing for geography: TDPS sells in 111 countries (CRISIL p.2). Subtract nothing for price segment: no sourced split between small and large gas gensets exists. This is why the figure is called conservative on scope but unfiltered on size. Excluded by definition: steam, hydro, wind generators and large motors, which have no sourced generator-level size.
- Result: Rs 56,198 Cr. Confidence M (source unnamed, internally inconsistent charts, FX at one date). FY31: USD 8.92 bn x 9,525 = Rs 84,963 Cr at constant FX. Growth check: (8.92/5.9)^(1/5) - 1 = 8.6%, matches the stated 8.64%.

Method 2, bottom-up by demand pull (cross-check, confidence L).
- Addressable unit: one gas turbine or gas engine of OEM output. Global turbine build capacity 60-70 GW a year against about 110 GW of orders (Wood Mackenzie, via Power Magazine, search result Aug 2026). Q2 2026 orders 38 GW. Backlogs: GE Vernova 116 GW including 63 GW reservations, Siemens Energy 69 GW, MHI 35 GW (Power Magazine and Oilprice, Jul-Aug 2026; counting rules differ). INNIO engine capacity 3.5 GW in 2025, target about 10 GW by 2030 (INNIO releases). Data-centre demand: Bloom 80 GW load by 2030 (vendor report), 35 GW behind-the-meter by 2030 (Turbomachinery, Jul 2026), Enverus 29.6 of 31.6 GW US behind-the-meter capacity gas by 2030.
- Revenue per unit: alternator value per kW NOT FOUND in any source. The search found no alternator price. TDPS's own blended revenue per unit is Rs 2.08 Cr (B04), but MVA per unit is NOT FOUND.
- Internal consistency only: USD 5.9 bn divided by (63.5 to 73.5 GW, turbine build plus INNIO engines) is USD 80-93 per kW. This is arithmetic on sourced numbers, not a benchmark price. It says the AR gas generator figure is the right order of magnitude if alternator content is near 1-2% of plant cost; no source confirms that.
