PRESS RELEASE



                   Cyient DLM Announces Q2 FY27 Results
              Revenue grows 26.5% YoY; Normalized PAT up by 25.8%
                        with Order Book at All Time High

Hyderabad, October 15, 2026: Cyient DLM, an integrated electronics manufacturing solutions partner for
Design-Led Manufacturing, announced its financial results for the quarter ending September 30,
2026, delivering continued revenue growth, healthy profitability and an all-time-high order book.

Financial Highlights

    •   Revenue for Q2 FY27 INR 393.1 crores, with 26.5% YoY growth
    •   Q2 FY27 EBITDA grew 9.5% YoY to INR 34.2 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 8.7%
    •   Normalized PAT increased 25.8% YoY to INR 15.82 crore, with a margin of 4.0%.
    •   Reported PAT for Q2 FY27 at INR 34.0 crore, including a one-off gain INR 18.2 crore earnout
        reversal
    •   Order Book reached an all-time high of INR 2,813.7 crores, increasing by INR 214.8 crores QoQ
    •   Free cash flow for Q2 FY27 stood at INR 17.2 crore, reflecting an improvement in cash
        generation during the quarter


Commenting on the performance, Rajendra Velagapudi, Managing Director and CEO of Cyient DLM, said,
“We maintained strong growth momentum in Q2 FY27, supported by solid execution across our key
business segments and a record-high order book, providing strong revenue visibility and confidence in our
growth outlook.


Looking ahead, we remain focused on strengthening our core business while investing in high-growth
areas such as AI and data centre infrastructure, robotics, and semiconductor capital equipment to drive
sustainable long-term growth.”


Business Highlights

The quarter saw continued momentum across Cyient DLM’s key business segments, led by Aerospace and
Industrial, with Defence and Medical also contributing to growth. Order momentum remained healthy
during the quarter, with a book-to-bill ratio of 1.6. Order intake increased 11% QoQ and 15.4% YoY, and
the company added three new customer logos across its core businesses and new growth areas.

Cyient DLM continued to invest in strengthening its sales, strategy and Build to Specifications (B2S)
capabilities to support growth and expand its participation across the product lifecycle. The company is
[page 3]
also seeing opportunities in newer areas including AI infrastructure, data centre technologies, robotics
and semiconductors as part of its longer-term growth agenda.

During the quarter, the company completed the integration of Altek Electronics, acquisition in
Connecticut, U.S. which has been renamed Cyient DLM Electronics Inc. The integration further
strengthens Cyient DLM’s global operating footprint and its ability to serve customers across geographies.

Cyient DLM remains positive on the outlook for the year, supported by its record order book and
continued demand across its key end markets.

The company will remain focused on executing its existing order book and maintaining operating
discipline, while continuing to invest selectively in capabilities and new growth areas. Over the longer
term, Cyient DLM will seek to deepen relationships with existing customers, add new customers and
broaden its participation across the product lifecycle and targeted high-growth markets.

About Cyient DLM

Cyient DLM (Estd: 1993, NSE: CYIENTDLM) is a leading Integrated Electronics Manufacturing Solutions
provider that offers Design Led Manufacturing (DLM) solutions to customers. We take ownership of
design, manufacturing, testing, precision machining and certification support to ensure that customers'
products meet robust reliability, safety and performance standards. We have expertise in safety-critical
electronics in highly regulated industries. With a global presence and a strong commitment to delivering
value-driven solutions, the company has been instrumental in transforming businesses through
innovation and technology.
