Marushika Technology Limited Announces Commencement of 
Business Partnership with Sanare Medical 
Mumbai, 23rd September, 2026 – Marushika Technology Limited (MTL) (NSE - SME: 
MARUSHIKA), Marushika Technology Limited is a leading IT & telecom solutions provider 
specializing in Data centre Management, IT infrastructure, SaaS platform, telecom systems, power 
management  and Cyber security & surveillance solutions, has announced the commencement of 
its Master Business Partner Agreement with Sanare Medical Pvt. Ltd., with September 23, 2026 
confirmed as the commencement date of the agreement. 
Under the agreement, Sanare Medical has appointed Marushika as an exclusive distributor, 
marketer and reseller for the ExoAtlet II product variant to End Users within the Government 
Territory. The agreement covers the ExoAtlet II, along with associated software, accessories, spare 
parts and related services 
Key Terms of the Agreement                                                                   
• Parties: Marushika Technology Limited and Sanare Medical Pvt. Ltd. 
• Commencement Date: September 23, 2026 
• Nature: Master Business Partner Agreement 
• Role of Marushika: Exclusive distributor, marketer and reseller for ExoAtlet II in the 
Government Territory 
• Products & Services: ExoAtlet II, along with associated software, accessories, spare parts 
and related services 
 
Scope of Partnership                                                                     
• Government Market Focus: The agreement covers Central and State Government 
departments, agencies, PSUs, government hospitals and government medical colleges in 
India, along with other entities mutually agreed by the parties. 
 
• Government Procurement: Marushika will undertake marketing, promotion, sale and 
distribution of the Products and Services and will act as the Seller of Record on government 
procurement portals, including GeM. 
 
• Tender Support: Sanare Medical will provide technical specifications, certifications, test 
reports, OEM authorisation letters and other documentation required for government 
tenders, RFPs and GeM bids 
 
• Product & Service Support: Sanare Medical will be responsible for procurement, delivery, 
installation and commissioning of the ExoAtlet II, along with AMC support including 
preventive and breakdown maintenance and spare parts.
