New Delhi, 26" October 2026: Paramount Communications Limited, a leading manufacturer of wires and
cables, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Commenting on the results, Mr. Sanjay Aggarwal, Chairman & CEO, Paramount Communications
Limited, said:
“Paramount's performance reflects the resilience of our business model, the strength of our customer relationships
and our ability to respond decisively to changing market conditions. We remain focused on sustainable growth,
financial discipline and operational resilience.
The structural demand outlook for the wires and cables industry remains encouraging, driven by investments in
power transmission and distribution, renewable energy integration and critical infrastructure. As power generation
capacity expands, strengthening the infrastructure required to transmit and distribute electricity efficiently will be
equally important, creating meaningful long-term opportunities for established players with proven capabilities.
Our priorities remain clear: strengthening our core business, restoring operating profitability, expanding our
international presence prudently and enhancing our product mix. Investments in specialized manufacturing
capabilities will enable us to address more technically demanding applications and participate in higher-value
segments. We remain committed to disciplined capital allocation, prudent risk management and translating the
industry's long-term growth potential into sustainable performance and enduring stakeholder value.
