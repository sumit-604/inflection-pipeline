### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Knack makes printed, laminated woven polypropylene bags and pinch-bottom bags. Buyers pack rice, pet food, fertiliser and cement in them. It sells to about 75 countries.
2. Group sales in Q2 rose 52.5% on a year ago, to Rs 294.76 Cr (filed). Tonnes rose only about 13% (call). Price drove most of the growth.
3. Polymer is the main raw material. Its price jumped in the quarter. Management says it moved from about Rs 92/kg to Rs 160/kg (call).
4. Knack passed the cost on late. Gross margin fell to 34.64%, from 42.07% in Q1. Operating margin without other income fell to 13.59%, from 21.66% in Q1.
5. The press release shows a 16.35% margin. That figure adds other income and divides by total income. It hides most of the fall.
6. Profit after tax rose 21% to Rs 25.02 Cr. About 71% of that rise came from other income and a one-time tax credit. Core profit grew about 4% on a year ago and fell 41% on Q1.
7. Other income was Rs 9.70 Cr in Q2. Filed interest for the whole half year was only Rs 6.13 Cr. The source of the rest is not disclosed.
8. The IPO raised Rs 380 Cr of fresh money in July. About 94% of the net proceeds still sit in cash and bank. Only 0.22% of the Rs 320 Cr new plant budget is spent. The plant is due in October 2027.
9. Cash from operations in the half was Rs 22.06 Cr, 0.40 times profit. Last year it was 0.94 times. Receivables rose Rs 55.73 Cr. About Rs 15 Cr of that is other current assets, cause not stated.
10. The Mexico joint venture lost Rs 2.62 Cr in Q2. Its book value is now nil. The group lends to it in ways the filing does not name.
11. Plants run at 90-91% (call). Volume can grow only through a rented plant from November-December 2026 until the new plant starts. One contract is about 18% of FY26 sales and ends in December 2028.
12. Watch the Q3 gross margin, due by 14-Feb-2027. At 40% or above, Q2 was a timing lag. At 36% or below, Knack has lost some pricing power at high polymer prices.
