### 2.3 Exactly where the three disagree

1. Note 4 (consolidated p119) says "Title Deeds of all Immovable properties are held in the name of
   Company." Note 18A (consolidated p126) says the factory Land and Building at Khasra No. 766/2,
   Village Bagwara is "in the name of Sh. Manish Gupta and Sh. Vikas Jain Director of the Company".
   Same document, same financial statements, same property.
2. Note 4 (standalone p163) says "Title Deeds of all Immovable properties (PPE) are held in the name
   of Company." Note 17A (standalone p169) says the same factory Land and Building is "in the name of
   Sh. Manish Gupta and Sh. Vikas Jain Director of the Company".
3. The standalone CARO clause (i)(c) (p156) certifies "the title deeds of all the immovable
   properties ... are held in the name of the company", with a lessee-lease carve-out only. The
   Khasra No. 766/2 factory is not a lease. Note 17A places it in two directors' names (p169).
4. The consolidated CARO annexure (p114) carries no clause (i)(c) at all. It reports only paragraph
   3(xxi). So at consolidated level there is no auditor title-deed statement to compare against
   Note 18A.
5. The same Khasra No. 766/2 land is quoted in two units and two spellings: "admeasuring 5645.89 sq.
   mtrs" (p126, p169) and "measuring 60,771.85 sq. ft." (p126, p169); "Village Bagwara" (p126, p169)
   and "village Bhagwara" (p126).
