 Detailed Rationale 

 CRISIL has reaffirmed its 'CRISIL BBB+/Stable/CRISIL A2' ratings on the bank facilities of Menon Bearings Limited (MBL).

The ratings continue to reflect MBL's established market position and clientele and healthy financial risk profile because of healthy net worth, low gearing and strong debt protection metrics. These strengths are partially offset by moderate scale of operations, moderate working capital requirement, and high dependence on the cyclical automobile sector. 

 Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description 

 Strengths: 
 * Established market position and clientele: Long-standing presence of over two decades in the bearings industry has helped the company to establish its position across diverse markets and industries. It has healthy relationship with key clients, including John Deere, Tata Motors Ltd ('CRISIL AA-/Negative/CRISIL A1+') and International Tractors Ltd, and Brakes India Private Limited ('CRISIL AA+/Stable/CRISIL A1+'). Diversified clientele also reflects the company's sound product and service quality.

 * Healthy financial risk profile: Financial risk profile is healthy marked by net worth of Rs 97 core and comfortable capital structure marked by moderate total outside liability to tangible net worth ratio (TOLTNW) of 0.58 times expected as on March 31, 2020. Debt protection metrics are also strong, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratio of 0.38 times and 6.64 times, respectively, expected for fiscal 2020.

 Weaknesses: 
 * Moderate scale of operations: MBL is expected to report revenues of Rs 140 ' 145 crore in fiscal 2020 indicating moderate scale of operation. Further scale is likely to remain subdued in the near term on account of sluggish demand from the auto industry.

 * Moderate working capital-intensive operations: Gross current assets (net of cash and bank balance) are expected at 137 days as on March 31, 2020, driven by inventory and receivables of 50 days and 91 days, respectively.

 * Revenue concentration in the automobile industry: Of the total revenue, 65-70% was contributed by the automobile sector mainly commercial vehicles, and the remaining was derived from the oil and gas, electrical, and engineering industries. High dependence on a single, cyclical sector exposes revenue and profitability to fluctuations, as seen in the 9MFY20. 

 Liquidity Adequate 

 Liquidity will remain adequate. Annual accrual is expected at Rs 13-17 crore over the medium term, after factoring dividend payout of around Rs 8.4 crore in-line with fiscal 2020; against yearly term debt obligation of ~Rs 6.5 crore. Bank limit utilisation averaged 46% over the trailing 12 months ended December 2019. Capex for fiscals 2020 and 2021 of Rs 5-6 crore is to be funded through internal accruals. Liquid investments of Rs 17.5 crore as on September 30, 2019, also underpin liquidity. 

 Outlook: Stable 

 CRISIL believes MBL will continue to benefit from its strong market position in the bearing industry and established clientele. 

 Rating Sensitivity factors 

 Upward factors: 
* Improvement in net cash accruals to over Rs 20 crore
* Sustained improvement in working capital cycle

 Downward factors: 
* Decline in net cash accruals to below Rs 13 crore for fiscal 2021
* Increase in working capital requirement, larger-than-expected, debt-funded capex or acquisition, or more-than-expected dividend pay-out, weakening the financial risk profile, particularly liquidity 

 About the Company 

 Incorporated in 1991 and promoted by Mr Ram Menon and family, MBL is a part of the Menon group. The company manufactures automotive components such as bearings, bushes, thrust washers, and bi-metal strips at its plant in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. MBL's wholly-owned subsidiary, Menon Alkop Pvt Ltd, which manufactured aluminum die-cast products, was merged with MBL in fiscal 2009.
