## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition — gross basis for interchange/program fee and gift-card platform revenue.** Company/Group acts as Program Manager and Business Correspondent; interchange revenue recognised gross (Program Fee), incentives/cashback recorded as a distinct expense line ("Consumption of cards", Note 26 standalone p.227 / Note 27 consolidated). This is standard for the prepaid-card/BC model but inflates both revenue and opex versus a net presentation. (Note 2.2.A, standalone p.206-207; consolidated p.262) 🟡 Watch — industry-standard but worth normalising for margin analysis.
- **No first-time standard adoption / no new pronouncements.** "For the year ended March 31, 2025, MCA has not notified any new standards or amendments applicable to the Company/Group." (Note 2.2.N standalone p.214; Note M consolidated p.271) 🟢 Clean.
- **PPE useful lives conform to Schedule II, no deviation:** Furniture & fixtures 10 yrs, Office equipment 5 yrs, Computers 3 yrs, Vehicles 8 yrs, Building 60 yrs. (Note 2.2.C, p.209) 🟢 Clean.
- **Intangible assets amortised over 3-5 years**, and "internally developed intangibles are capitalised to the extent of direct cost related to development... which mainly includes manpower cost." (Note 2.2.D, p.209) This is the key policy judgment area: large in-house platforms (Zoyer, Zatix and Fleet Solutions, Tax Filing Software) are built substantially from capitalised employee cost rather than expensed. Intangible assets under development (standalone) = ₹22.02 Cr (₹220.17 Mn) at Mar-25 vs ₹49.86 Cr (₹498.63 Mn) at Mar-24 (Note 5b, p.216-217); consolidated = ₹48.06 Cr (₹480.56 Mn) vs ₹49.86 Cr (₹498.63 Mn) (Note 6b, p.275). Capitalisation during the year (standalone) ₹51.16 Cr (₹511.60 Mn — moved from WIP to finished intangibles). 🟡 Watch — capitalisation of manpower cost is a legitimate accounting policy for SaaS platforms but merits scrutiny of whether development-stage costs meet Ind AS 38 recognition criteria versus being period opex.
- **Ind AS 116 leases:** single portfolio discount rate applied (no rate disclosed in the note itself; incremental borrowing rate used where implicit rate not determinable). ROU assets (standalone) net ₹10.09 Cr (₹100.93 Mn) at Mar-25 vs ₹11.91 Cr (₹119.10 Mn) at Mar-24; lease liability closing ₹11.34 Cr (₹113.40 Mn) (Note 4, p.215-216). 🟢 Clean, routine.
- **ECL matrix / trade receivables — sharp swing.** Allowance for credit-impaired receivables fell from ₹5.76 Cr (₹57.56 Mn) to ₹0.79 Cr (₹7.88 Mn); ₹5.83 Cr (₹58.28 Mn) was written off during the year while only ₹0.86 Cr (₹8.60 Mn) new provision was created (Note 13(f), standalone p.220-221). 🟡 Watch — a write-off of this scale relative to opening provision (>100%) needs explanation: is this genuine cash recovery/reclassification or clean-up of aged receivables that were fully provided in the prior year? Document does not explain the nature of the ₹5.83 Cr write-off beyond the roll-forward table — **NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT** (no qualitative note on write-off cause).
- **Deferred tax — no MAT credit disclosed, no unrecognised DTA discussion.** DTA (net) standalone fell from ₹2.76 Cr (₹27.60 Mn) to ₹0.56 Cr (₹5.60 Mn), driven by the fall in "Provision for doubtful receivables" DTA component (₹1.42 Cr to ₹0.20 Cr) consistent with the write-off above (Note 10, p.219; Note 41, p.239). 🟢 Clean — reconciles logically with receivables note.
- **Employee benefits / gratuity actuarial assumptions:** discount rate reduced from 7.10% to 6.75%, salary escalation 5.00% (unchanged), attrition 10% (unchanged), mortality IALM (2012-14) Ult. (Note 38, standalone p.233-234). Gratuity plan is unfunded (no plan assets). 🟢 Clean, standard assumptions.
