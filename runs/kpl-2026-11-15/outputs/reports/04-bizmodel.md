## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

### 1A One-line description
KPL, an Amritsar firm founded 1983 (Q2 Pres slide 19), makes tablets and injectables in five operating plants and sells them under registrations (government permits to sell a drug) in 70+ countries, about half its sales in India and half abroad (AR p.222; Q2 Pres slide 18).

Registration is held either by KPL or by a partner (split NOT FOUND) | Finished tablets, capsules, vials, prefilled syringes, eye drops | 100+ clients: local distributors, licensees and domestic traders (Q2 Pres slide 27). ### 1E The chai-stall-uncle version
KPL is like a tailor who stitches clothes for shops in 70 countries. Some shops hold the licence to sell in their town. In other towns the tailor holds the licence himself. The tailor earns more when he holds the licence, because the shop owner takes no cut. That is the story management tells. The filed papers do not say how many towns are which. The tailor also lets shops pay late, about six months late. Sales are real. The cash comes slowly.
