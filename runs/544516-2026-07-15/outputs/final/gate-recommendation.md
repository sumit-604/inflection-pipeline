## FLAG-CASH — determination STRUCTURAL

The credit rating rationale that normally anchors this call is ABSENT (no rating supplied or found, B00 input_gaps, B01). The determination is made from the four other evidence streams, which converge.

- Two consecutive years of negative and worsening operating cash flow: standalone OCF negative Rs 4.43 Cr in FY25 and negative Rs 57.45 Cr in FY26, against PBT growth to Rs 52.7 Cr; CFO/PAT negative in both years, cumulative negative 0.93x (B03, B01, B02).
- Receivables composition and ageing deteriorating on a three year trend that predates the FY26 growth surge: trade receivables older than six months rose 15.95% to 17.85% to 23.38% across FY23 to FY25 with zero doubtful-debt provisioning in any year; FY26 receivables absorbed Rs 86.42 Cr, larger than the entire Rs 59.27 Cr IPO working-capital allocation, which was fully spent without resolving conversion (B02, B03).
- The capex commissioning timeline rules out a benign growth-capex explanation for the drain: only about 21% of the Rs 13.68 Cr IPO machinery allocation was deployed by FY26 end and the 14 acre facility construction had not started, delayed by a China export-control rule (B05, B07). The cash is being consumed by receivables, not by commissioning new capacity.
- DSCR below 1.0x in every disclosed year (0.21x / 0.42x / 0.68x, FY23 to FY25), a liquidity marker that predates the growth spike (B02).
- Peer cross-read corroborates a structural sector pattern without making it universal: TEXRAIL disclosed a six year cumulative gap of Rs 1,625 Cr EBITDA against Rs 50 Cr OCF, and warned such gaps persist for years without a clear turnaround catalyst (B06); FRONTSP by contrast self-funds capex, so the pattern is real but not sector-wide inevitability.

B03's Phase 3 kill-switch reached the same conclusion in words: the post-listing data shows the cash-conversion problem is structural and worsening, not a pre-listing anomaly that fresh capital and scale would resolve.

Metric that would revise this call toward growth-induced/transitional: a single clean quarter is not enough, but standalone operating cash flow printing positive in H1 FY27 results with the older-than-six-months receivables share falling back below 23.4% and DSO below roughly 240 days would be the first evidence that the drain is growth timing rather than structure. Until that print, the flag stands as STRUCTURAL.
