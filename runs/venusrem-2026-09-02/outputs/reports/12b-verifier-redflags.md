## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built from sources, before reading B05/B06 verdicts)

### A. Venus Pharma GmbH: going concern, unallotted Rs 28.6 Cr, stabilization reversal
- Q3 FY26: subsidiary net worth eroded by INR 94.15 lakh; auditor emphasis of
  matter, going-concern doubt (Q3 file p.3, p.5 Note 4).
- Rs 2,859.72 lakh (Rs 28.60 Cr) share application money pending allotment,
  repeated every quarter (Q3 p.3 Note 5; Q4 p.10 Note 5; Q1 p.4 Note 4).
- Q4/FY26 audited: GmbH swung to PAT Rs 3.48 Cr on revenue Rs 26.84 Cr; going-
  concern language dropped (Q4 p.13, para 13).
- Q1 FY27: GmbH back to net loss Rs 2.55 Cr on revenue Rs 2.08 Cr; "currently
  facing financial challenges" (Q1 p.8, Other Matter). Stabilization not durable.
- **Grade: MEDIUM. Thesis-relevant.**

### B. Lyophilisation "~2x demand" SPEAR fact untraced in company documents
- The load-bearing "lyophilisation demand ~2x capacity" figure appears nowhere
  in the AR MD&A, chairman/JMD letters, results, or AGM proceedings. Its origin
  is untraced; it must not be read as company guidance.
- **Grade: MEDIUM. Load-bearing per SPEAR.**

### C. Zero-miss self-scored R&D scorecard (selective self-reporting)
- The FY25-commitments table self-grades all 8 R&D priorities Delivered /
  Ongoing-on-track / Achieved / Exceeded / Progress made (AR p. "Delivering on
  FY25 commitments", lines 5063-5113). No item rated missed or delayed.
- **Grade: LOW-MEDIUM. Tone flag.**

### D. Vague forward financial guidance; no total capex figure
- International growth guidance qualitative only ("sustainable annual growth"),
  no % or rupee target; no total project cost for TVC or Venus Industrial Park.
- **Grade: LOW.**

### E. Related-party sourcing of the IP/technology pipeline  [NOT IN B05/B06]
- Standalone RPT note (AR p.112, lines 17906-17928), transactions with
  "Entities over which KMP or relative of KMP having Significant Influence":
  - "Purchase of Patent IPR & Technology" Rs 3,000.00 lakh (Rs 30.00 Cr) [FY25,
    shown in brackets per the note's prior-year convention].
  - "Advance for in Licensing for Technology" Rs 2,154.77 lakh (Rs 21.55 Cr) [FY25].
  - Recurring FY26 payments to the same related bucket: "IT Services" Rs 436.08
    lakh (prior Rs 376.49 lakh); "Brand Promotion & Platform fee paid" Rs 125.96
    lakh (prior Rs 188.29 lakh).
- The entire growth thesis rests on the VRP-034 / VRP-048 pipeline and its
  patents. A material slice of that IP base (Rs 30 Cr purchase + Rs 21.55 Cr
  in-licensing advance) was sourced through promoter-influenced entities, and
  the pipeline is narrated in the MD&A as Venus's own achievement without
  surfacing the related-party sourcing. Ongoing IT and brand fees keep the
  company operationally dependent on related entities.
- **Grade: MAJOR. Directly thesis-relevant; a credibility item a communication
  audit in NO-CONCALL mode should catch, because the AR IS the substitute
  transcript.**

### F. Rising GST disputed contingent liability  [NOT IN B05/B06]
- AR Note 44 (p.114, lines 18504-18517): "GST disputed demand pending in appeal"
  rose to Rs 1,926.39 lakh (Rs 19.26 Cr) from Rs 828.32 lakh (Rs 8.28 Cr) —
  more than doubled. "Tax demand pending in appeal" fell to Rs 60.00 lakh from
  Rs 2,991.79 lakh (this one resolved favourably). Capital commitment Rs 2,675.60
  lakh (Rs 26.76 Cr) up from Rs 971.64 lakh.
- A rising, unprovided GST demand ~2.5% of revenue is a genuine off-balance-sheet
  overhang. Neither B05's "what they are not saying" (2D) nor its red-flag list
  (4D) mentions any contingent liability.
- **Grade: MAJOR (single-period, disclosed in AR, but un-surfaced upstream).**

### G. Managing Director is also CFO; family-dominated executive board  [NOT IN B05/B06]
- Pawan Chaudhary signs the audited accounts as "Managing Director & CFO"
  (AR p.114, line 18537). Combined MD+CFO role concentrates operational and
  financial control in the promoter.
- Executive board is family-dominated: Pawan Chaudhary (CMD & CFO), Manu
  Chaudhary (JMD, heads R&D/operations), Saransh Chaudhary (Executive Director,
  appointed May 2026), Akshansh Chaudhary (Executive Director) — all related
  (AR remuneration section lines 2926-2987; Q4 p.19 director-appointment annex).
- **Grade: MAJOR for a 3-5 year hold; governance/financial-control concentration.**

### H. Very broad MOA/AOA object-clause expansion; ~8% public AGM dissent  [NOT IN B05/B06]
- Q1 FY27 filing carries a 36-object new Memorandum: objects now extend to
  lending/advancing money, treasury/derivatives investing, e-commerce/telehealth,
  cell and gene therapy, cosmetics/personal care (Q1 p.12-15).
- AGM voting: the AOA-adoption special resolution passed with only 91.63% in
  favour (~8.37% against); the second-term independent-director resolution drew
  similar dissent (AGM37-VotingResults, lines 1673-1678, 1363-1382). Promoters
  hold 52.40% (line 122-141), so public dissent was meaningful.
- B05 lists the MOA/AOA item only procedurally (1C, AGM row); it never flags the
  scope-creep or the dissent.
- **Grade: MINOR-MEDIUM (routine-appearing and disclosed, but a scope-creep signal).**

### I. Q4 FY26 earnings/margin concentration in the audited balancing quarter
- Standalone Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 259.01 Cr = 34% of full-year Rs 768.73 Cr, at a
  much higher margin (Q4 PBT Rs 57.57 Cr, ~22% vs full-year ~17%) (Q4 p.7).
  Q4 is the balancing figure, not a separately limited-reviewed quarter
  (Q4 p.6 para 12). The FY26 margin step-up is heavily back-ended.
- B05 flagged "sustainability of the FY26 margin step-up" as a self-posed open
  question (3C), so the margin-durability angle is caught; the Q4-concentration /
  balancing-figure angle is not.
- **Grade: MINOR. Partially caught.**

Additional calibration point (not a standalone red flag): B05 3D presents
CONSOLIDATED trade receivables falling Rs 112 Cr -> Rs 103 Cr as an earnings-
quality positive. STANDALONE receivables actually ROSE, Rs 99.26 Cr -> Rs 116.39
Cr (AR Note 38, line 17994; Q4 p.8). The consolidated fall is partly an intra-
group elimination artifact (subsidiary receivable Rs 13.74 Cr eliminated on
consolidation). The positive is one-sided.
