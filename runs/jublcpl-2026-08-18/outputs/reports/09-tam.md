## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A Precise boundaries (retained entity)

- **Product scope**: branded wood-working/consumer adhesives and wood
  finishes (Jivanjor, Charmwood, Ultra Italia); packaging adhesives
  (currently market trials, AR p.21); synthetic latex — Vinyl Pyridine
  (VP) Latex (core, tyre-cord/conveyor-belt dipping), new SBR Latex
  (construction chemicals/waterproofing, Phase-2 commissioning end-Q3
  FY27), NBR Latex; Food Polymers — solid PVAc/SPVA used as chewing-gum
  base.
- **Geographic scope**: India domestic primary (85% of FY26 revenue,
  Inv. Pres. slide 3) for Adhesives/Wood Finishes; genuinely global for
  VP Latex (#1 India / #2 global ex-China, Inv. Pres. slide 2) and PVAc
  (#1 India / top-3 global, AR p.17, "secured new customers in Asia and
  Latin America" in FY25).
- **Customer scope**: B2B2C via distributors/retailers for Consumer
  Products; direct B2B industrial contracts for Latex (tyre/rubber
  OEMs) and PVAc (global confectionery majors, AR p.17: "long-standing
  contracts with leading confectionery companies").
- **Channel scope**: ~1,450 distributors / ~31,000 retailers (Inv.
  Pres. slide 3) for Consumer Products; direct-supply industrial
  contracts for Latex/PVAc.
- **Price segment**: mass-premium branded (Jivanjor/Charmwood) plus a
  premium PU finishes tier (Ultra Italia); Latex/PVAc priced off
  internationally-linked input costs (VAM, butadiene) with a
  pass-through model (AR p.16, p.19).
- **Explicit exclusions**: SSP, bulk NPK, Agri Nutrients (demerging,
  noted separately below); footwear/automotive-OEM/industrial-tape
  adhesives (not a disclosed JUBLCPL category); finished waterproofing
  chemicals/membranes as a product (JUBLCPL supplies the SBR-latex
  raw-material input, not the finished formulated product — see Flags,
  this sub-segment could not be sized at the correct value-chain level
  and is carried as unquantified optionality, not in the headline TAM).

### 3B SOM at 3 and 5 years

Current SAM share = PP&C FY26 revenue ÷ SAM = Rs 1,238.6 Cr ÷ Rs 47,132
Cr = **2.63%**.

Share-gain rules applied: the adhesives sub-market is **highly
organised already** (top-5 >90% share per Method 3 — no >40%
unorganised-formalising tailwind available, ruling out the "faster"
exception). B07's Emerging Moat forward scan is MODEST (22.5,
combined GOOD+, not HIGH POTENTIAL) and F2 execution moat is capped
"Moderate" on a credibility-C promise-delivery sample. This points to
the **normal 1–2pp/3yr band**, not the aggressive 3–5pp band, with a
modest step-up by year 5 justified narrowly by the committed Samlaya
brownfield capacity (+37.5% MTPA, Phase-1 already commissioned Q1
FY27).

- **3yr**: +1.5pp → 4.13% share. SAM grows at a blended weighted CAGR
  of 6.11% (Adhesives 6.9% × Rs31,324 wt. + VP Latex 3.5% [AR p.16 own
  cited 3–4%, midpoint] × Rs3,011 wt. + Gum Base 4.8% × Rs12,797 wt.) →
  SAM(yr3) ≈ Rs 56,300 Cr. **SOM 3yr = 4.13% × 56,300 = Rs 2,326 Cr.**
- **5yr**: +3.0pp (low end of the "aggressive, capacity+execution"
  band, capped there by Moderate execution-moat grade) → 5.63% share.
  SAM(yr5) ≈ Rs 63,380 Cr. **SOM 5yr = 5.63% × 63,380 = Rs 3,569 Cr.**

**Implied revenue CAGR** (from FY26 PP&C base of Rs 1,238.6 Cr):
- 3yr: (2,326/1,238.6)^(1/3) − 1 = **23.4%**
- 5yr: (3,569/1,238.6)^(1/5) − 1 = **23.6%**

Cross-check against actual observed growth: AR segment table shows
27.6% PP&C YoY growth FY25→FY26 (Rs 970.4 Cr → Rs 1,238.6 Cr, AR p.19);
Q1 FY27 PP&C net sales grew 27.5% YoY (Rs 299.3 Cr → Rs 381.5 Cr, Inv.
Pres. slide 8). Both independently land close to each other (~27–28%)
and **above** this stage's 23–24% SOM-implied CAGR — i.e., the
share-gain-derived SOM sits modestly **below** the two most recent
actual growth prints, which is the conservative direction intended.
Separately, the Investor Presentation's own FY24–26 bar chart (Rs
957.7 → 1,104.2 → 1,198.8 Cr, slide 7) and the AR MD&A narrative text
("10% growth") are the low outliers — both already flagged in B04/B07
as an unreconciled three-way internal discrepancy. This stage's SOM
math uses the audited AR segment table per the task's explicit
instruction, and the Q1 FY27 print corroborates that basis over the
Inv. Pres. chart / narrative text.

### 3C Capacity cross-check

Installed + committed capacity, Polymers & Chemicals (Adhesives+Latex+
PVAc+Acid, blended, no product split disclosed): 80,000 MTPA existing +
30,000 MTPA Samlaya brownfield (Phase-1 Adhesives commissioned Q1 FY27;
Phase-2 SBR Latex due end-Q3 FY27) = **110,000 MTPA**, a 37.5% physical
capacity increase (Inv. Pres. slides 4–5). No utilisation % is
disclosed (B04/B07 both flag this NOT FOUND), so a precise
revenue-per-tonne ceiling cannot be computed without estimating —
barred. As an illustrative, explicitly-flagged linear scaling only
(assumes constant utilisation and product mix, which may understate or
overstate the true ceiling):

Capacity-scaled revenue ceiling ≈ Rs 1,238.6 Cr × (110,000/80,000) =
**≈ Rs 1,703 Cr** (flat, since no further capacity beyond the announced
30,000 MTPA is disclosed for either the 3yr or 5yr horizon).

- **Gap at 3yr**: SOM (Rs 2,326 Cr) − capacity ceiling (Rs 1,703 Cr) =
  **≈ Rs 623 Cr shortfall**.
- **Gap at 5yr**: SOM (Rs 3,569 Cr) − capacity ceiling (Rs 1,703 Cr,
  unchanged, no further capex disclosed) = **≈ Rs 1,866 Cr shortfall**.

This directly corroborates B07's own flag on `capex_embedded_growth_pct
= 25.2%` being "likely overstated" (blended group fixed-asset
turnover). **The SOM is the more optimistic side of this pair.** To
close the gap, JUBLCPL needs either (a) realisation/mix upside beyond
linear MTPA scaling — plausible given premiumisation already underway
(Ultra Italia, AR p.21) and Adhesives EBIT margin expansion (8.9%→
12.7% YoY-Q1, Inv. Pres. slides 10-11) — or (b) further brownfield
capacity beyond the announced Phase-1/2, which the company itself
frames as operationally easy ("Manufacturing leverage: Expansion is
brownfield at Vadodara, adding capacity without greenfield land or
approval lead-time," Inv. Pres. slide 5) but which is not currently
committed. **capacity_check: gap of ≈₹623 Cr (yr3) widening to ≈₹1,866
Cr (yr5); SOM is the optimistic side.**

### 4C Market structure

- **Adhesives**: highly concentrated — Pidilite/Fevicol ~70% share,
  top-5 >90% (Method 3). JUBLCPL is a minor branded/regional player far
  behind the leader; further "consolidating," not fragmenting.
  Competition is branding/differentiation-led at the consumer end.
- **VP Latex**: global oligopoly by application; JUBLCPL itself is #1
  India / #2 global ex-China (Inv. Pres. slide 2). Apcotex Industries is
  a named domestic peer (anchored independently via web search, not
  from the non-anchored MNCL note alone: "Apcotex is among the pioneers
  of Vinyl Pyridine latex... manufacturing in India").
- **PVAc/Gum Base**: global oligopoly; JUBLCPL "top three players in
  the world" (AR p.17). Competition is on quality consistency/timely
  delivery, not price (AR p.17).
- **Organised vs unorganised**: Adhesives is atypically organised for
  an India-GARP thesis (see 4A formalisation note); Latex/PVAc are
  inherently industrial/organised B2B categories with no meaningful
  unorganised segment.
- **Entries/exits**: JUBLCPL itself is the notable new entrant —
  SBR-Latex/construction-chemicals and packaging adhesives are its own
  expansion moves; no broader sourced data on sector-wide entries/exits
  was found.
- **Import share trend (finished goods)**: NOT FOUND for
  adhesives/latex/PVAc as end-products; import dependence found is
  input-side only (VAM, butadiene) and, for the demerging Agri side,
  rock phosphate (>90% import-dependent, Method 4).

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY & RUNWAY

### 5A Funnel

```
TAM (conservative)  Rs 50,143 Cr  (realistic: Rs 61,259 Cr)
   -> SAM            Rs 47,132 Cr  (94.0% of TAM)
        -> SOM 3yr    Rs  2,326 Cr  (4.13% of SAM-yr3, 4.9% of current SAM)
        -> SOM 5yr    Rs  3,569 Cr  (5.63% of SAM-yr5, 7.6% of current SAM)
Current PP&C revenue (FY26, AR p.19): Rs 1,238.6 Cr = 2.63% of current SAM
```

### 5B Runway assessment

- Revenue headroom = SAM ÷ current revenue = 47,132 / 1,238.6 = **38.1x**
- TAM growth rate (blended, weighted) ≈ **6.0%/yr**
- Company CAGR vs TAM: recent actual PP&C growth (27.6% AR FY26 YoY;
  27.5% Q1 FY27 YoY) vastly outpaces the ~6% underlying TAM growth —
  JUBLCPL is **gaining share**, not merely riding the market, though
  from a very small (2.6%) base.
- Years to saturate SAM at current company growth rate (~23.5% SOM-
  implied CAGR) vs SAM's own ~6% growth: solving 1,238.6×(1.235)^n =
  47,132×(1.06)^n → **≈ 24 years**. A long runway on the numbers, but
  this assumes both growth rates hold constant for two decades-plus,
  which is not a forecast, only an arithmetic illustration.

### 5C Runway classification: **STRONG**
