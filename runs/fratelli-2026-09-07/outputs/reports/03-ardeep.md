## THE BACKWARD-HISTORY PROBLEM, STATED PLAINLY

Before the eight phases: what backward history actually exists for the WINE
business, and what a reader can and cannot conclude from it.

Fratelli Vineyards Limited is the renamed shell of Tinna Trade Limited, an
agri-commodity trader listed since inception (CIN L11020DL2009PLC186397,
name changed 26-Jul-2024, Directors' Report Item 44, p.130). The wine
operating subsidiary, Fratelli Wines Private Limited, was brought in via an
Ind AS 103 Appendix C common-control pooling restated from 1-Apr-2023
(B02/B01, consistent with Note 1 corporate information and the share-swap
mechanics at Note 33/43).

What a reader actually has, year by year:
- **FY2017-FY2021**: a different business (agri-commodity trading). Not
  usable for any wine-business trend. Excluded by this pipeline's own basis-
  break rule (B01 data_notes).
- **FY2022-FY2023**: absent from every source provided (screener, both ARs).
  A genuine gap, not filled, not estimated.
- **FY2024**: a pooling-restated BLEND. Consolidated revenue Rs 421.35 cr is
  ~47% wine / ~53% legacy agro-trading (B01, B02 Rank 2). The wine SEGMENT
  line within it, Rs 212.88 cr (Note 37, FY26 AR p.187), is the only
  FY24 number usable for a wine-only comparison, and even that segment
  figure sits inside a consolidated set that still carried the exited
  trading book on the same face financials.
- **FY2025**: a transition year, ~59% wine / ~41% legacy (B01). Standalone
  (parent, ex-Tinna Trade shell) revenue was still Rs 12,471.59 lakh
  (Directors' Report Key Financial Highlights, p.39/42) — i.e. FY25's
  legacy trading book was still live for most of the year.
- **FY2026**: the FIRST genuinely wine-only year. Parent standalone revenue
  collapsed to Rs 67.83 lakh (Directors' Report, p.39/42); AOC-1 subsidiary
  turnover Rs 181.20 cr against consolidated revenue Rs 181.29 cr (B01) —
  the Group is now, for practical purposes, the wine subsidiary.

**What this means for every number in this report**: any consolidated
revenue, PAT, or margin CAGR spanning FY24-FY26 is NOT a like-for-like wine
trend — it is a trend across a basis break. The only strictly comparable
wine-only series is the Note 37 segment line, and even that has only THREE
data points (FY24 Rs 212.88 cr, FY25 Rs 178.44 cr standalone-subsidiary
[not the AR's own mislabelled "consolidated" comparator — see Phase 2
Finding A], FY26 Rs 181.20-181.29 cr). Three points is not a trend; it is a
transition still being measured. No claim in this report treats FY24-FY26
consolidated movement as organic wine growth or decline. Where the AR
itself does exactly that (Directors' Report, Chairman's letter, MD&A ratio
table — see Phase 2 Finding A and Phase 6A), it is flagged as a
disclosure-integrity failure, not accepted as evidence.
