# THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

Revenue has the clearest support. The plant is built and paid for, so volume can rise without new spending, and certification rules keep some foreign brands out. The catch is that this tailwind is mostly used up and the market itself grows about 16 percent, so the days of doubling are ending. This one is real and documented.

Margin has no upward catalyst that stands up. Backward integration into in house boards is the story management tells, but the plant only appeared in FY26, the benefit is unproven, and peers confirm that chip and memory costs are rising, which pushes the other way. The bigger issue is that the reported margin is already far above the only scaled peer, so the surprise is more likely to be down than up.

Cash conversion is where the analysis turns, and the honest finding is NONE FOUND. There is no factoring line, no stated working capital target, no payment term change, and not one word of management commentary on why profit is not becoming cash. In this protocol an absence this complete is not a blank, it is a negative signal. A peer in the same cohort treats negative operating cash flow as a multi year structural feature, which raises the bar for reading Prizor's burn as temporary. What would confirm a fix is a single clean print of positive operating cash flow near 40 percent of profit. What would kill the benign case is a third straight year near zero while profit stays above Rs 20 Cr.

ROCE has a counter catalyst, not a catalyst. The fresh debt funded capacity earns almost nothing today at about 5.5 percent utilisation, and until sales fill it, the growing capital base drags the return down. The recovery route exists only if utilisation climbs fast, which is not yet visible.

One governance item sits underneath all of this. A Rs 3 Cr loan was turned into equity just before the IPO and the counterparty could not be traced across three independent checks. It does not score a transition, but it is the single largest open question and it colours how much trust the reported cash and margin numbers deserve.
