CFO quality checks (per pipeline instruction):
1. **One-time inflator:** the new "Other Non-Current Liabilities" line (Rs2,744.97L, the
   customer advance discussed in Phase 1/2) is booked as a **positive working-capital
   adjustment inside CFO**. It is 18% of the entire Rs151.29cr CFO improvement — a customer
   prepayment against future supply, not organic operating-margin cash generation, and one
   that must eventually be worked off against future revenue recognition.
2. **Interest classification choice:** Yasho classifies Finance Cost paid as a **Financing**
   activity outflow (Rs(5,564.86)L CON, p.166-167), not an Operating outflow. This is
   Ind AS-permitted but is the more CFO-flattering of the two allowed choices — it mechanically
   inflates CFO, CFO/PAT and CFO/EBITDA relative to a company that nets interest paid
   within operating activities. Worth normalising before comparing this company's CFO
   quality to peers that make the opposite classification choice.
3. **Payable stretching:** trade payables +Rs2,452.71L (CON) contributed materially to the
   CFO swing, alongside the MSME overdue-principal build (Note 22.1, Phase 2) — some of the
   FY26 cash-conversion "recovery" is working-capital-funded, not pure earnings-driven.
4. **Inventory:** a modest release (+Rs799.59L CON) contributed positively but was not the
   dominant swing factor this year (FY25's inventory *build* of Rs(12,898.63)L CON was the
   dominant negative swing factor that year — see 2E).

**Net read:** the CFO recovery is directionally real (revenue and volume growth are genuine,
per 3C below) but a meaningful share of the swing (new customer-advance liability, payables
stretch, and the interest-classification choice) is working-capital/presentation-driven rather
than pure margin-driven cash conversion. This is consistent with, and adds mechanism detail
to, Stage 2's FLAG-CASH.
