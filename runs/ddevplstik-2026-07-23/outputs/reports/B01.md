## CLASSIFICATION

Core score 74 falls in the 60-79 band. Moat class = MODERATE (not STRONG/FORTRESS),
so the base matrix result is **Core 60-79 + else = GOOD**. Deal-breaker #2 caps
classification at "max GOOD" — the base result is already at that cap, so it does
not move the outcome further.

**CLASSIFICATION: GOOD**

Strongest block: **D (Balance Sheet Strength, 20/20)** — net-debt-free across all
three measured years, negligible leverage, ample liquidity.

Weakest block: **B (Cash Generation Quality, 7/20)** — the working-capital drag
concentrated in FY26 is the single number to watch into FY27; it sits squarely
inside the company's own guided capex/expansion window (PVC/HFFR/XLPE/BESS), so
whether it self-corrects as new capacity utilization ramps (management guides
FY26 utilization at 75% total, PVC only 49%, HFFR ~49-50% — both well below
optimal) is the operationally relevant question for later stages.

Decision line: GOOD, driven by an exceptional balance sheet and strong shareholder
alignment against a moat profile that is genuinely MODERATE (3 of 12 tests) and a
cash-conversion block that just tripped the Block-B deal-breaker on a working-capital
build concentrated entirely in the most recent (capex-heavy) year. No mechanical
halt condition is present; this classification and its drivers pass through to
downstream stages as evidence, not as a verdict.
