## 3. VALUE TRAP TEST — contesting the UGLINESS verdict

The signed model carries **ugliness = ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB** (B09b line 22): the depressed blended ROCE 7.68% is a temporary optic caused by the post-IPO cash pile inflating capital employed, not structural decay. I carry that verdict into the test AND I contest it in the open, per the pipeline rule.

**The contest: this is a STRUCTURAL-FEATURE in waiting, not a temporary artifact.**

The ARTIFACT thesis is true only for the *current* balance sheet, where Rs 293 Cr of idle cash sits on a Rs 355 Cr operating base. The moment that cash is deployed - which is the entire premise of the ROCE recovery - the distortion does not vanish. It converts. Watch the mechanism (all figures deliberation 6.1 / 11-valuation 2C):

- Operating CE rises Rs 355 Cr -> ~Rs 575 Cr as cash funds the Shirwal automated line, Mayflower, and working capital.
- The 20% ROCE requires op EBIT Rs 117 Cr, which requires a **24% margin held to FY29, above the 20-22% guide**.

So on the guided case the "ugly" low ROCE does not climb to the R3 (~19x) neighbourhood the transition claims; it settles in the R2 (~15-17x) neighbourhood permanently, on a now capital-heavy plant. If, on top of that, the Indonesia batch expires unreplaced (Section 2 scenario 2), the newly built automated capacity runs below the utilization the 24% margin assumed, and ROCE can print **below 16%**. That is the textbook definition of a STRUCTURAL-FEATURE: the ugly number is not the leftover of yesterday's IPO, it is the steady-state economics of the deployed business.

**Verdict on the contest:** ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB survives ONLY if the 24% fair-case margin holds. It does NOT hold on management's own 20-22% guide. On the guided case the correct label is **STRUCTURAL-FEATURE (mid-teens ROCE plant)**, and the model's re-rating engine is priced on the fair case, not the delivered case. This is the melting-ice-cube risk disguised as a compounder: clean optics (net cash, "growth"), a narrated climb, and a TO-state already priced at 45x. So one event - Shirwal commissioning at the assumed margin and utilization - carries Pillar 1 and the revenue line simultaneously. **This IS a shared catalyst; the `false` flag is a labelling artifact of crediting the recovery through the balance-sheet route.**

**What breaks if the shared catalyst disappoints (delivers late, or fills at 20-22% not 24%):**
- The cash converts to fixed assets (denominator rises) but the numerator misses (margin 20-22%, or utilization low because Indonesia expired). ROCE falls to 16% or below. The ARTIFACT becomes STRUCTURAL (Section 3).
- Pillar 1 base PE drops 19x -> 16x; the growth premium's evidence thins further; the re-rating engine, already spent, reverses to a de-rating.

A 2-year slip means: no re-rating, EPS compounds at best ~12.6%, the multiple de-rates toward 20x, and the holder sits through a -30% to -50% drawdown (Section 2 scenario 1) while "waiting for the catalyst."
