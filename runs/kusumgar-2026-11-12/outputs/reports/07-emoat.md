### 2C Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic)

- Capex under execution = CWIP 101.04 INR mn at Sep 2026 (Q2 results p.11).
- Historical fixed asset turnover = 3.40x on average PPE (Prospectus p.191; B01 data_notes). Closing-PPE basis 2.94x (6,920.03 / 2,356.68, B01).
- Implied incremental revenue = 101.04 x 3.40 = 343.5 INR mn (derived).
- Current revenue = 6,920.03 INR mn (FY26, Prospectus p.191).
- **Embedded growth = 343.5 / 6,920.03 = 4.96%, stated as 5.0%.**
- Sensitivities: on 2.94x, 297.1 = 4.29%. Adding the 58.33 capital commitment (dates differ; may overlap CWIP): (101.04 + 58.33) x 3.40 = 541.9 = 7.83%.

Reading. New capex adds about 5% to revenue capacity. The real growth vehicle is idle capacity: final output ran at 49.50% in FY26 on 127.80 mm (Prospectus p.204). Filling it to the 55-60% level management cites needs +11% to +21% more volume, and to 100% about 2.0x (1 / 0.495, derived, product-mix dependent; not a revenue forecast). That growth is unfunded by capex but not free: it needs orders (no order book is disclosed, Q2 Q&A 10) and working capital (B01 Block B = 0).
