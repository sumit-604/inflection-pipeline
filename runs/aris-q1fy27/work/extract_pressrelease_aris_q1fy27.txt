    47	       ARIS Delivers Strong Start to FY27 with 37% Revenue Growth and Nearly 4x
    48	                                      Growth in Profit
    49	Mumbai, August 5, 2026 – Arisinfra Solutions Limited (NSE: ARIS | BSE: 544419) today announced
    50	its unaudited consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
    51	The Company delivered a strong start to FY27, with broad-based growth across revenue and a sharp
    52	improvement in profitability, reflecting the strength of Arisinfra's integrated, asset-light business
    53	model, disciplined execution and an improving revenue mix.
    54	Growth during the quarter was supported by increasing contribution from Contract Manufacturing
    55	and Developer-as-a-Service (DaaS), deeper customer engagement, and continued investments in
    56	technology-led procurement and execution.
    57	
    58	Key Highlights – Q1 FY27
    59	   •     Revenue from Operations: ₹2,908 Mn, up 37.1% YoY
    60	   •     EBITDA: ₹305 Mn, up 67.6% YoY
    61	   •     EBITDA Margin: 10.49%, compared with 8.58% in Q1 FY26
    62	   •     Profit Before Tax: ₹267 Mn, up 323% YoY
    63	   •     Profit After Tax: ₹200 Mn, compared with ₹51 Mn in Q1 FY26
    64	   •     Diluted EPS: ₹2.05, up 279.6% YoY, compared with ₹0.54 in Q1 FY26
    65	   •     Business Mix: Contract Manufacturing and Services contributed 63% of revenue, compared with
    66	         49% a year ago.
    67	Business Highlights
    68	The quarter marked continued progress in Arisinfra's strategy of expanding higher-margin,
    69	capital-efficient business segments.
    70	   •     The Company secured a project of ₹650 Cr GDV under Developer-as-a-Service mandate for the
    71	         Wadhwa Wise City integrated township in Panvel — one of our largest DaaS engagements to date.
    72	   •     Secured a ₹79crore work order from the J. Kumar–NCC (GMLR) Joint Venture for the Goregaon–
    73	         Mulund Link Road Twin Tunnel Project in Mumbai, strengthening its infrastructure execution
    74	         portfolio.
    75	   •     Contract Manufacturing contributed 53% of revenue during the quarter, growing 83% YoY to
    76	         ₹1,540 million from ₹839 million in Q1 FY26, reflecting the success of Arisinfra's capital-efficient,
    77	         asset-light manufacturing partnership model.
    78	   •     The Developer-as-a-Service (DaaS) business grew 48% YoY to ₹277 million from ₹187 million in Q1
    79	         FY26, with the portfolio's Gross Development Value (GDV) under execution expanding to ₹18,391
    80	         million across 10 active projects, providing a robust pipeline for future growth while deepening
    81	         Arisinfra's integrated engagement with developers.
    82	   •     The Asphalt business continued to scale, with revenue increasing to ₹529 million from ₹299
    83	         million in Q4 FY26, while the number of customers increased from 28 to 38.
    84	   •     Customer retention remained strong, with the repeat order rate improving to 82%, compared with
    85	         78% in the previous quarter.
    86	Operational Scale
    87	Arisinfra continues to strengthen the scale of its integrated procurement and execution platform.
    88	During the quarter, the Company served 3,412 customers through a network of 2,229 sourcing
    89	vendors across 1,192 PIN codes spanning 23 States and Union Territories. The platform today
    90	operates 10+ Contract Manufacturing plants with over 9 million MTPA reserved manufacturing
    91	capacity, while cumulative material deliveries have exceeded 24 million metric tonnes, supported
    92	by 800+ daily deliveries across project sites.
    93	The Company also continued to enhance its proprietary technology platform through CARA AI and
    94	ArisGPT, improving credit assessment, operational visibility and execution efficiency across its
    95	business.
    96	
    97	Management Commentary
    98	Commenting on the results, Mr. Ronak K. Morbia, Chairman and Managing Director, said:
    99	“We are pleased to begin FY27 with a strong performance that reflects the strength of our
   100	integrated business model and disciplined execution. The quarter demonstrates the operating
   101	leverage inherent in our business, with higher-margin Contract Manufacturing and Developer-as-
   102	a-Service continuing to increase their contribution while driving improved profitability.
   103	Our recent wins, including the DaaS mandate in Mumbai and the materials win for Goregaon–
   104	Mulund Link Road Twin Tunnel Project, reflect the strength of our network and the trust our partners
   105	place in us. We remain confident of sustaining this momentum through the rest of the year.”
   106	
   107	Outlook
   108	India's construction and infrastructure sectors continue to present significant long-term growth
   109	opportunities, supported by sustained public infrastructure investment, urbanisation and
   110	increasing private sector capital expenditure.
   111	Arisinfra remains focused on expanding its integrated Supply–Services–Technology platform by
   112	increasing Contract Manufacturing capacity, strengthening its Developer-as-a-Service portfolio,
   113	deepening its presence across higher-margin product categories, and leveraging AI and technology
   114	to enhance operational efficiency. Backed by a strengthened balance sheet and an asset-light
   115	operating model, the Company is well positioned to deliver sustainable, profitable growth.
   116	
   117	About Arisinfra Solutions Limited:
   118	Arisinfra Solutions Limited (NSE: ARIS, BSE: ARIS) is a tech-enabled B2B company focused on
   119	simplifying procurement of construction materials across India. Operating an asset-light, aggregator-
   120	led model with no inventory risk and minimal fixed assets, the Company integrates sourcing, quality
   121	control, logistics, and documentation into a unified digital supply-chain network.
   122	Founded in 2021 with the vision of building a 'digital orchestration layer' for India's construction
   123	sector with a diversified portfolio spanning aggregates, RMC, steel, cement, construction chemicals,
   124	asphalt, walling solutions, and value-added services.
   125	ARIS’ three-stream network — B2B Supply, Contract Manufacturing, and Developer-as-a-Service —
   126	is powered by its proprietary AI suite (CARA AI and ArisGPT), creating compounding network effects
   127	with every transaction. The Company was listed on NSE and BSE on June 25, 2025.
