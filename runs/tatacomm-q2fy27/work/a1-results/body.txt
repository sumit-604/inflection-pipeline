Pursuant to Regulations 30, 33, 51 and 52 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure

amongst other things, are as follows:

(i)    Unaudited Financial Results:
       The Unaudited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of the Company for the
       quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026, approved and taken on record at the
       meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company held today are enclosed as
       (Attachment A). We would like to state that M/s. S.R. Batliboi & Associates, LLP, Statutory
       Auditors of the Company, have issued a Limited Review Report with an unmodified opinion
       on the above-mentioned financial results (Attachment B). A press release in this regard is
       also enclosed (Attachment C).

(ii)   Other Restructuring:
       The Board of Directors has approved the transfer of the entire shareholding held by the
       Company in Kaleyra, Inc., to Tata Communications (Netherlands) B.V. (‘TCNL’), a
       wholly- owned subsidiary of the Company. As consideration for the aforesaid transfer, the
       Company shall subscribe to and acquire ordinary shares of TCNL at fair market value, as
       determined pursuant to an independent valuation report. The proposed transaction is
       intended to simplify the Company’s existing group structure by consolidating ownership of
       Kaleyra, Inc. under TCNL, without any change in the ultimate ownership and beneficial
       economic interest of the Company in Kaleyra, Inc. and its subsidiaries.

       Details as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read with

  iii. Notes to Segments:
  The Group’s (the Company and its subsidiaries together referred to as “the Group”) operating segments comprise of Voice
  Solutions, Data Services, Transformation services, Real Estate & Campaign Registry. The composition of the operating
  segments is as follows:
  Voice Solutions includes International and National Long Distance Voice services.
  Data Services includes Core connectivity services and Digital platforms & connected services. (Refer note 5).
  Transformation Services includes the business of providing telecommunication network management and support services.
  These services are carried out by the Company’s wholly owned subsidiary Tata Communications Transformation Services
  Limited and its subsidiaries.

  Campaign Registry includes the business of collecting robotically driven campaign information and processing and sharing
  that information with mobile operators and the messaging ecosystem to reduce spam. These services are carried out by the
  wholly owned indirect subsidiary of the Company.

  Real Estate segment includes lease rentals for premises given on lease.
