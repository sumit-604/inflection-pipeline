## 2D What they are not saying

1. CFO exit. The CFO resignation was filed on 18 May 2026, effective 31 May (announcements/20260518-Resignation_CFO_Sivasis_Sen.txt, lines 19-20), the same day as the Q4 call where he sat on the panel (May p.2). The call never mentions it. A director also resigned on 14 May (announcements/20260515-Resignation_Director_Bhaskar_Shah.txt, line 27). From Aug the finance seat is a "Head of Finance", not a CFO. The CEO title also changed from "Joint Managing Director and CEO" (Feb, May) to "Managing Director and CEO" (Aug, Oct) with no comment. Likely reason: personal reasons filed, and no analyst asked.
2. Below-EBITDA squeeze. H1 FY27 PAT is down 7.4% while income grew (Q2 presentation p.8, Rs Cr: 93.9 vs 101.4). Calls on Aug and Oct discuss EBITDA and occupancy only. Depreciation and finance cost never get a number. Management warned in Feb of "a much higher depreciation load" but gave no figure. Likely reason: it is the least favourable part of the story and no analyst has pressed.
3. Receivables and cash conversion. Trade receivables rose from Rs 75.5 Cr (Mar-26) to Rs 118.3 Cr (Sep-26) (presentation p.9), +56.7% (derived) in six months, while H1 CFO was Rs 136.1 Cr against EBITDA Rs 186.2 Cr = 73% (derived, presentation p.10 and Oct p.3). The one cash conversion remark is the "around 70%" line (Oct p.9), with no definition. B03 flags receivables +72.3% and days of 12.1 to 18.4. Not asked in any call.
4. Dombivli Q4 FY26 drag of Rs 9.4 Cr in about six weeks of operation versus Rs 9.5 Cr in a full Q1: the split between pre-opening and operating cost was not asked or given.
5. Capex totals. The "1,700 beds at about Rs 1.5 Cr per bed including land" implies about Rs 2,550 Cr (derived: 1,700 x 1.5), the total outlay is never stated. A3 B03 reports ICRA's plan at Rs Cr 890-900. The reconciliation was not discussed. BKC capex "can't be finalised".
6. BKC date. No call states one. The AR (2030) and presentation (CY31) differ; neither call explains it.
7. Pledge. In Aug a non-promoter pledgor to Catalyst Trusteeship was cited and not named. B00 holds one quarter of shareholding only.
8. IV fluid goodwill. Rs 5.1 Cr goodwill (presentation p.9) against a Rs 3.8 Cr equity price; the answer ("debt of 7-8 crores or 10 crores, I don't know", Oct p.5) does not reconcile.
9. Ex-Dombivli ARPOB and Dombivli revenue not available (Oct p.3, p.7).
10. Regulatory exposure: pharmacy and onco drug margin. Not sized.
11. Dombivli fixed cost unit: Aug says "for a month" (Rs 6-7 Cr); in Oct an analyst says "per quarter" and the CEO does not correct, then says "last time Rs 6 to 8-odd crores". The unit is ambiguous and the Q1 loss does not reconcile with either reading without revenue.
