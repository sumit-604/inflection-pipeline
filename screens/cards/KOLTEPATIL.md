## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Kolte-Patil builds and sells homes, mostly in Pune, with a growing book in Mumbai and a small one in Bengaluru. It sells flats before completion (pre-sales), collects cash as construction progresses, and books revenue only when the occupancy certificate arrives (AR26, p.58). FY26 pre-sales were Rs 2,605 crore and collections a record Rs 2,689 crore (AR26, p.4). The Life Republic township near Hinjewadi is the engine: 1.78 million sq. ft. of FY26 sales (AR26, p.22) and about 34% of Q1 FY27 sales (Q1 deck, p.12).

**What is changing?** Control. Blackstone's BREP Asia III India Holding Co VII bought 25.7% from the founders on 11-Aug-2025 and took 1,26,75,685 new shares at Rs 329, Rs 417.03 crore in all (AR26, p.59). It holds 40.0%; the founders hold 33.8% (Q1 deck, p.23). It "exercises joint control" (CARE, p.1). Three founder directors left the board, a new independent chairman came in (AR26, p.61), and a new CEO joined on 24-Aug-2026 (filings/KOLTEPATIL_19082026164527, p.2).

**Why now?** Q2 FY27 pre-sales were a record Rs 1,019 crore, up 52%. One launch, Vyana at The Reserve, sold Rs 616 crore of it (Q2 ops, p.2). In August the company added six Mumbai redevelopment projects worth about Rs 6,000 crore of GDV (Q2 ops, p.3).

**What must be true?** Sales must grow beyond one-off launches. Mumbai redevelopment must get approvals and deliver its higher price per sq. ft. Blackstone's governance must show up as better execution, not only as board seats.

**What breaks it?** Pune demand softens further; the CEO already says demand "has softened in the near term ... particularly in Pune" (Q2 ops, p.3). The GST demands of about Rs 226 crore go against the company. Or the new owner's plan stays invisible, as it has been for four quarters with no call.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One segment: real estate (Q1 res, p.4, note 2). Three brands: Kolte-Patil for mid-premium and premium, 24K for luxury, Life Republic for township housing (CARE, p.2). Models: outright land, JV and JDA, and Mumbai society redevelopment, which the company calls lower in capital intensity (Q2 ops, p.4).

Q1 FY27 pre-sales by region (Q1 deck, p.13):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Life Republic, Pune | 0.29 | 212 | 7,280 | 294 |
| Pune excl. LR | 0.26 | 220 | 8,324 | 317 |
| Mumbai | 0.10 | 183 | 19,123 | 91 |
| Bengaluru | 0.00 | 2 | 10,153 | 13 |

Portfolio at 30-Jun-2026: GDV Rs 34,400 crore over 38.02 mn sq ft, of which 5.56 ongoing and unsold, 6.71 under approval and 25.75 land bank (Q1 deck, p.21). Pune holds about 75% of GDV (CARE, p.3).

Revenue is booked on completion. FY26 revenue fell 57% to Rs 734.96 crore because occupancy certificates did not arrive (AR26, p.58). So reported profit lags sales by years and swings by quarter. The company itself says a quarter "may not be representative" of the year (Q1 res, p.4, note 3).

**Archetype: fits no library entry cleanly.** The nearest is the order-book business: pre-sales are order inflow, completions are execution pace, collections are working capital, and margin sits on the backlog. The library has no developer entry; that is a finding, not a gap to paper over.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Brand and local scale: present, in Pune.** 35 years, 68 projects, over 33 mn sq ft delivered (Q1 deck, p.6). Realisation rose every year from Rs 6,407 per sq ft in FY22 to Rs 8,314 in FY26 (Q1 deck, p.14). Part of that is mix: Mumbai sells at Rs 19,123 against Rs 7,280 at Life Republic (Q1 deck, p.13).

**Land: present.** Life Republic has 16 mn sq ft of balance development potential on a 400-plus acre parcel (Q1 deck, p.8). Land held at old cost is the usual developer edge.

**Cost of capital: forming.** Blackstone's AA- rated backing and the Marubeni NCDs lower funding cost. CARE credits the "strategic association with Blackstone" (CARE, p.1).

**Pricing power: partial.** The Q2 FY27 average realisation of Rs 7,829 was flat year on year (Q2 ops, p.2). The Q1 jump of 29% came largely from Mumbai mix (Q1 deck, p.12).

**Verdict: a local moat is present in Pune; elsewhere it is forming at best.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R2, a regional developer.** Pricing power in one city, returns near the cost of capital. FY25 PAT of Rs 106.6 crore on net worth of Rs 830 crore is about 13% (FY26 deck, p.15, derived).

**TO: R3, an institutional, multi-city premium developer.** The mechanism is cheaper capital, governance, and a mix shift to Mumbai redevelopment at more than twice Pune's price per sq ft.

Management's claim, once: "The destination is a Kolte-Patil that is meaningfully more capable, more consistent, and more scalable than the one that existed last year" (AR26, p.23). The same letter adds: "We are not claiming completion. We are claiming direction" (AR26, p.23).

Ladder check. One rung, so the base rate holds. But the claim names a process, not a number. No target for pre-sales, margin or return is filed.

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point would be sustained pre-sales growth. It is absent.** Pre-sales fell from Rs 2,822 crore in FY24 to Rs 2,605 crore in FY26 (Q1 deck, p.14). Q1 FY27 was flat at Rs 617 crore (Q1 deck, p.12).

**Q2 FY27 is one launch.** Of Rs 1,019 crore, Rs 616 crore came from Vyana (Q2 ops, p.2). The rest of the portfolio sold about Rs 403 crore (derived). That is below every FY26 quarter. The CEO confirms softer Pune demand (Q2 ops, p.3).

**What did move:** collections, up 32% in H1 FY27 to Rs 1,509 crore (Q2 ops, p.2), and cash. Collections lag sales, so they reflect the FY24 and FY25 bookings.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**CARE AA-/Stable**, reaffirmed on 07-Oct-2026 for Rs 250 crore of NCDs (CARE, p.1). **CRISIL AA-/Stable/A1+**, reaffirmed on 25-Mar-2026 for Rs 800 crore of bank facilities and the NCDs (CRISIL).

CARE leans on Blackstone's "joint control", 35 years of track record and strong liquidity (CARE, pp.1-2). Its weaknesses: execution and approval risk on a large pipeline, Pune concentration, a "relatively nascent" Mumbai redevelopment book, and sector cyclicality (CARE, p.1). It would downgrade if bookings and collections fell below Rs 1,800 crore (CARE, p.1).

CRISIL notes "stagnation in sales and collections in fiscal 2026, primarily due to launches getting deferred" (CRISIL). It expects FY27 sales and collections to grow 25% to 30% (CRISIL).

The agencies see a safe balance sheet with a stronger owner. Neither claims the growth has started. Today's filings agree.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Ownership and governance changed. Sales did not, outside one launch (step 8).
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** The FY26 loss is completed-contract timing, not decay (AR26, p.58). Cash and collections held.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** The price of Rs 451 is 37% above Blackstone's Rs 329 entry and 3.3 times book value of Rs 136 (market-data-2026-10-26.md). The trailing PE is 32.2 on a profit that rests on Q1's completion spike. The R3 neighbourhood is about 19x.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** The Blackstone story is priced; the climb is not proven. Hard WATCH, no position, until the proof gate fires and the price reopens the gap.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The change of owner is real and large. But the transition decision matrix puts it in its most seductive cell. A `/step1` now would mostly study a governance reset that the company itself will not discuss on a call.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. FY27 pre-sales excluding the Vyana launch, quarter by quarter, against FY26's Rs 2,605 crore (Q1 deck, p.14; Q2 ops, p.2).
2. Approval status and timeline for the six Mumbai redevelopment projects (Q1 deck, p.16).
3. The status and overlap of the three GST matters, about Rs 226 crore (step 9).
4. The terms of the "strategic arrangements" behind the MD's Re 1 pay, and any founder exit rights under the 13-Mar-2025 shareholders' agreement (AR26, p.59).

**What would change the view**
- Up to PROCEED: two quarters of sustenance sales above Rs 700 crore without a marquee launch, plus the first Mumbai redevelopment approval, plus the return of earnings calls.
- Down to PASS: bookings and collections tracking toward CARE's Rs 1,800 crore downgrade line, or an adverse GST order on the Rs 103.82 crore notice.
