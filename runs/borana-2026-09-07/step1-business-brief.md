# BORANA WEAVES — Step 1 business brief

Run: runs/borana-2026-09-07 | NSE BORANA / BSE 544404 | listed 27-May-2025 |
CMP Rs 325.25, market cap Rs 870 cr (screener, 07-Sep-2026). Standalone only.

## What the business is

Borana buys polyester yarn, texturises part of it in-house, and weaves
unbleached synthetic greige fabric on water-jet looms in Surat. It runs 1,212
high-speed water-jet looms, 24 texturising machines, 20 folding machines and
nine warping machines across four units. FY26 revenue was 92% greige fabric and
8% polyester textured yarn sold outside.
(https://www.boranagroup.in/about, and the four decks in inputs/presentation/)
The customer is the Surat dyeing and printing trade. The product is a
commodity. There is no brand.

## The numbers

Sales: Rs 42 cr (FY22), Rs 135 cr, Rs 199 cr, Rs 290 cr, Rs 389 cr (FY26).
Net profit: Rs 1.8 cr, Rs 16.3 cr, Rs 23.6 cr, Rs 40.2 cr, Rs 64.6 cr (FY26).
FY26 EBITDA Rs 91.8 cr, 23.6% of sales. FY26 EPS Rs 24.3 on 2.66 cr shares.
At Rs 325 that is 13.4x trailing.
(inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv)

Quarterly operating margin, ten quarters to Jun-2026:
27.2, 26.1, 16.5, 22.8, 21.7, 21.2, 22.7, 24.3, 25.4, 25.6 percent.
Two things follow. The margin did not appear in FY26. It was 27% in Q4FY24,
before the recent duty change. And it swung to 16.5% in one quarter without
warning. Whatever drives it, it is not stable and it is not new.

Revenue has stalled. Rs 111.4 cr in Q3FY26, then Rs 100.7 cr and Rs 100.8 cr.
Two flat quarters while capacity is being doubled.

## Working capital and cash

Receivables Rs 23.6 cr on Rs 389 cr of sales, 22 days. Inventory Rs 47.3 cr,
44 days. Cash from operations Rs 44.4 cr against Rs 64.6 cr of profit, a 69%
conversion. For a Surat textile name these are unusually tight numbers and
should be checked, not admired.

## Where the money went

Net block rose Rs 67.7 cr to Rs 147.6 cr. Capital work in progress rose from
Rs 0.04 cr to Rs 80.9 cr. Investing outflow FY26 was Rs 178.7 cr, financed by
Rs 135.9 cr in, mostly the May-2025 IPO. Equity went from Rs 87.6 cr to
Rs 281.6 cr. Borrowings Rs 70.8 cr. Interest fell to Rs 3.58 cr because IPO
money repaid debt mid-year.

## The engine the thesis rests on

Spread times utilisation. Fabric price minus yarn cost, across as many looms
as run. Management ties recent gains to a duty change on Chinese yarn. New
capacity is landing now. So the question is whether a doubled loom count meets
the same spread, or a normalised one.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (verify against the filings first)

1. **The 23.6% EBITDA margin, and where it comes from.** Reconcile the FY26
   cost stack in the annual report against screener: raw material Rs 227.8 cr
   (58.6% of sales), power and fuel Rs 23.5 cr, employee Rs 28.1 cr, other
   manufacturing Rs 15.6 cr. Establish whether the gross spread is a fabric
   price the company commands or a yarn price it bought well. Then test the
   duty claim against the Filatex and Sanathan concalls, which cover the same
   yarn chain from the seller's side. The margin was already 27% in Q4FY24,
   before the duty change, so the duty story does not fit the whole record.

2. **Related-party yarn purchases.** Read the related-party notes in the FY26
   AR and in the RHP (inputs/prospectus/). Establish the rupee value, the
   counterparty, and the price basis against arm's-length yarn. If yarn is
   bought from a related party, the margin is a transfer-pricing outcome
   until proved otherwise. This is the single fact that could invalidate the
   entire P&L.

3. **SEBI search on the promoter group, April 2026.** On 22-23 April 2026 a
   search under Section 11C(9) of the SEBI Act, ordered by the Special Judge
   (SEBI), was executed at the premises of R&B Denims Ltd, a promoter-group
   entity named in Borana's own RHP, and at the premises of certain promoters
   and the CFO of R&B Denims. Not at Borana's registered office. Read
   inputs/announcements/2026-04-25_Reg30_SEBI-11C9-search-RandB-Denims.pdf,
   then find every R&B Denims link in the RHP related-party and group-company
   sections. Note the company's own IR site files this under the filename
   "it-search"; the document says SEBI.

4. **Secretarial auditor churn.** Resignation 24-Jul-2026, no reason stated.
   A second resignation notice 29-Jul-2026. A new appointment the same day.
   Read all three in inputs/announcements/ and the FY26 secretarial audit
   report.

5. **The capex landing into the spread.** Rs 80.9 cr of capital work in
   progress at FY26 close, against Rs 147.6 cr of net block. Establish the
   loom count added, the commissioning date, and the revenue the company
   expects from it. Then set that against two flat quarters of revenue. The
   presentations carry the guidance; there is no transcript Q&A to test it.

## PEERS AND WHY

Borana's DRHP says the company has no comparable listed peer. The street's
basket (Vardhman, Arvind, R&B Denims, Filatex) is a generic textile list.
Vardhman and Arvind are cotton-integrated. R&B Denims is denim, and is the
promoter-group entity SEBI searched. Peers were chosen on the economic engine.

- **FILATEX (Filatex India)** — polyester yarn: POY, FDY, PTY. This is
  Borana's input. Its concalls carry the chain spread and the Chinese
  import-duty question from the seller's side. The direct test of the duty
  story. Four transcripts to Aug-2026.
- **SANGAMIND (Sangam India)** — integrated synthetic yarn plus fabric
  weaving. The nearest thing to a listed weaving-margin benchmark.
- **SANATHAN (Sanathan Textiles)** — polyester yarn including the POY-to-PTY
  texturising step Borana runs in-house. Listed Dec-2024, so the disclosure
  vintage matches. Four transcripts to Aug-2026.

AYM Syntex was considered and dropped. It has published no concall since 2021,
so it cannot serve peer verification.
