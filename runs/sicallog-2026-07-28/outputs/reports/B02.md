Both prior passes already surfaced most of the load-bearing contradictions (the standalone-vs-consolidated equity divergence, the customer-concentration inconsistency, the P&L-vs-cash-loss tension). This pass adds pattern-level confirmation and two items not previously isolated as such.

1. 🔴 **The "narrowing loss" vs "widening cash loss" contradiction is confirmed as a genuine, document-internal mismatch, not a mis-anchor.** The standalone P&L shows total comprehensive loss narrowing modestly (Rs 60.30 Cr → Rs 45.92 Cr FY24 restated-view per Pass 1's Note 24 read; reported FY25 loss before exceptional items Rs 60.27 Cr improving to Rs 50.60 Cr, then further reduced by the Rs 9.67 Cr exceptional reversal). Set against this, CARO Annexure A clause (xvii) — a mandatory disclosure the auditor is required to reconcile to the accounts — shows actual cash losses of Rs 30.37 Cr (FY25) vs Rs 20.87 Cr (FY24), +45.6% (verified above, line 3648-49). Two disclosures in the same audited document point in opposite directions on the core question "is this company's loss getting better or worse." The mechanical bridge: the P&L improvement is substantially non-cash (provision reversals of Rs 9.67 Cr FY25 / Rs 14.38 Cr FY24, tax-refund credit Rs 6.56 Cr, and depreciation/interest accruals that do not consume cash in the period), while the cash-loss figure captures the operating reality net of these bridges. This is the single most important reconciliation an investor needs and it does not appear as a reconciling note anywhere in the document; the two figures simply sit in different sections (Notes vs Annexure) without cross-reference.

2. 🔴 **Standalone-vs-consolidated divergence pattern, three separate instances, same underlying cause.** (a) Standalone total equity is positive Rs 28.14 Cr while consolidated owners'-attributable equity is negative Rs (4.77) Cr — the difference is entirely subsidiary-level profit (chiefly SMART, step-down, contributing Rs 16.25 Cr) accruing disproportionately to non-controlling interests rather than to the parent's residual claim (Consol Note 38(a), p.191, per Pass 1). (b) The Rs 115.01 Cr indirect-tax contingent liability exists only at consolidated level with zero standalone equivalent — it originates in a subsidiary not individually named anywhere in either note set. (c) Customer concentration swings in opposite magnitude at each level (standalone 76%→33%, consolidated 66.05%→5.93%) — a swing that is larger, not smaller, when subsidiaries are added in, which is arithmetically unusual if the standalone parent is the entity carrying the legacy concentrated customer relationship; this suggests either a subsidiary that itself had extreme customer concentration in FY24 also diversified sharply in FY25, or a disaggregation-methodology change at Group level that is not explained. All three instances share a root cause: the parent (loss-making, legacy-debt-carrying) and the operating subsidiaries (majority profitable, per the entity-wise Schedule III table) are diverging structurally, and the Notes do not narrate this divergence anywhere as a unified story — a reader has to build it by cross-referencing five separate notes across both financial-statement sets.

3. 🟡 **No going-concern qualification found on this third read either.** Both auditor's reports carry unmodified opinions with Emphasis of Matter only (resolution-plan effects, unavailable financial-creditor confirmations). The closest liability-servicing language is CARO Annexure A clause (xix)'s standard boilerplate ("nothing has come to our attention... we neither give any guarantee nor any assurance that all liabilities falling due within a period of one year... will get discharged," p.80) — this is required of every auditor under the CARO Order and is not a bespoke going-concern paragraph. Confirmed absent: any note-level management assessment of going concern, any material uncertainty paragraph, any explicit liquidity-risk narrative beyond the standard financial-risk-management boilerplate.

4. 🟡 **Events after the balance sheet date: technically none disclosed (Note 42(b) consol, p.196: "no events... subsequent to 31 March 2025"), but the same bound document carries an AGM Notice dated 02-Sep-2025 proposing (i) a renewed/modified related-party equipment lease with Pristine Malwa up to Rs 24.95 Cr/year "including the value of similar lease transaction entered... in FY 2024-25," and (ii) a fresh unsecured related-party loan facility up to Rs 30 Cr.** These are correctly outside the FY25 subsequent-events window (the AGM Notice postdates the FY25 balance sheet by five months and is a prospective FY26 authorisation, not a condition existing at year-end), so the "no events" statement is not technically false. But the continuity is direct: the same Pristine-group financing relationship that produced this year's Rs 67.48 Cr lease and Rs 130 Cr KLPL loan is already being extended and enlarged for next year, at the same document sitting.

5. 🟢 **No new restatement or prior-period-error amount found on the pattern pass.** Note 37(a)/40(a) standalone and Note 43 consolidated "regrouped, where necessary" language is generic boilerplate with a Rs 0 prior-period-error line in the Schedule III equity-changes table (standalone, verified: "6,525 - 6,525 6,525," i.e. no change) — confirms Pass 2's read that no quantified restatement sits behind the regrouping sentence.

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Aggressive/uneven provisioning**: Unbilled receivables ECL allowance frozen at an identical Rs 28.58 Cr for two years at both reporting scopes despite 45-49% gross growth in the underlying balance, mechanically easing the bucket's headline loss rate without a corresponding credit-quality improvement.

- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: a new Rs 115.01 Cr consolidated-only contingent liability with a nil prior-year comparative and zero explanatory narrative, and no company named as the subsidiary of origin.
- **Disclosure-scope inconsistency**: standalone-vs-consolidated divergence on net worth sign (positive Rs 28.14 Cr standalone vs negative Rs (4.77) Cr consolidated owners' equity) and on customer-concentration direction/magnitude, both unexplained in the notes as a unified narrative.
