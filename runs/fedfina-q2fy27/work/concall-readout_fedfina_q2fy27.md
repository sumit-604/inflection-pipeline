## 1. What management said (prepared remarks)
- Priorities unchanged for six quarters: secured book, gold + LAP twin engines, "consciously reduce our reliance on direct assignment", lower cost structure, credit cost below 1% (L29-34).
- Gold: disbursals Rs 5,822 Cr (+31% YoY), gold AUM Rs 11,917 Cr (+77%), tonnage +16% YoY, LTV 68%, AUM per branch Rs 16.9 Cr, 74 branches opened, entry into eastern India (L39-47).
- Gold GNPA 0.5% "reflects this transition, not a structural credit concern" (revised gold LTV framework) (L48-51).
- Mortgage: disbursals Rs 855 Cr (+12%), AUM Rs 9,982 Cr (+13.5%); MT LAP Rs 645 Cr (+16%); ST LAP and HL Rs 210 Cr (L52-61).
- Credit cost Rs 27 Cr, about 0.6% of average total assets, annualised (L64-66).
- GNPA 1.8% from 1.6%: "primarily attributable to the ongoing transition in gold lending practices and the flattish AUF as ... direct assignment and co-lending business normalized" (L68-71). PCR 35.4%. CRAR 21%. BVPS 84.4 (L71-73).
- CFO: credit cost fell 0.78% to 0.6% of total assets on better mortgage flows (L104-105, L111-113). Core NII Rs 404.8 Cr (+5.2% QoQ) (L109).
- CFO: core loan yield flat at 16.40%; the reported +29 bps is optical, from a larger DA unwind in Q1 (L114-117). Weighted average cost of borrowing flat at 7.80% for four quarters (L118-119).
- CFO: "We did direct assignments of approximately 700 crores of the gold loan book to de-leverage", plus "higher co-lending in the gold portfolio"; D/E 4.9 to 4.6; CRAR 20.7% to 21% (L124-127).
- CFO: Stage 2 rose 2.7% to 3.6% and Stage 3 1.6% to 1.8%, "entirely on account of" the gold shift from bullet to periodic interest; "technical delinquencies" (L130-139). Mortgage net slippages a record low (L140-142).
- CFO: PCR fell 38.4% to 35.5% "due to a higher proportion of gold loans in stage three", which carry lower ECL cover; mortgage PCR "at similar levels" (L143-147).
- Opex/average assets 4.9% (Q1 4.8%, FY26 5.5%); C/I 52.5% (L148-160). "At least another 150 branches in the second half" (L163-165).

## 3. New information not in the filings
- Rate cycle: analysts reference rate hikes. Funding mix: fixed rate 33%, MCLR-linked 24%, rest EBLR-linked; about 58% of the loan book is short-tenor gold that reprices at maturity (L120-123, L603-612). Management will not promise flat spreads: "It will be wrong of me to give you a guidance that we will play with a flattish yield" (L282-284); a NIM squeeze would be offset through opex and credit cost (L634-635, L654-659).
- Gold competition "heightened" in Q2 among NBFCs, not banks; Fedfina yield fell only about 10 bps (L211-233, L248-256).
- Income-generating gold loan (IGL) product live since April: about 20% of gold disbursals, cash-flow underwritten, LTV cap 85% with tiers 75/78/80/85 (L244-247, L291-300, L458-461).
- ST LAP: minimum ticket raised to Rs 8 lakh; approval rates up from about 30% to the low 40s; credit cost on ST LAP "bucket negatives" (net recoveries); "press the pedal" in Q3-Q4, best quarter in 8-9 promised (L417-420, L429-440, L561-564, L771-772).
- MT LAP: partner-driven, yield about 11.5-12.5%, 15-16 geographies (L787-798).
- Insurance distribution income about 0.1% of assets in the ROA tree; exposed to "insurance distribution related changes" (L174-175, L872-878).
- Tonnage: H1 tonnage "slightly ... positive"; Q3-Q4 are the tonnage quarters (L638-644).
- Gold AUM per branch rose from Rs 8 Cr to Rs 16 Cr over time; management expects C/I on gold to keep falling (L865-869).

## 4. Points that conflict with the filings or with earlier records
- C-1 DA route and product. The CFO says about Rs 700 Cr of DA was from the gold book (L124). The filing shows Rs 876.89 Cr assigned with a weighted residual maturity of 23 months and an 8-month holding period (Res R472-R479), which fits property loans better than 3-12 month gold loans [INFERENCE, A3-12]. The deck shows Rs 1,085 Cr of sell-down. Three different figures; the call does not reconcile them. Question stays open (Q4, Q31).
- C-2 DA reliance. The stated priority is to "consciously reduce our reliance on direct assignment" (L33), and DA is described as a tool "to de-leverage" (L124). In the same quarter the sell-down doubled to Rs 1,085 Cr and the off-book share rose from 23.2% to 26.2% (deck). The words and the numbers point opposite ways.
- C-3 PCR explanation. Management puts the whole PCR fall on gold mix in Stage 3 (L143-147). The review shows Stage 2 cover also fell (16.53% to 12.61%) and stressed cover fell 24.5% to 20.2% (review Step 5L, M3). The call explains the Stage 3 part, not the Stage 2 cover drop.
- C-4 ROA guidance history. On this call the FY27 ROA guide is "+30 to 40 basis points" on 2.4 (L700-701). Notion records the Q1 promise as "+20-30 bps". Either the guide was raised quietly or one record is wrong; check the Q1 transcript.
- C-5 AUM guidance. This call: entity AUM +20-25% at a flat gold price (L705, L859). Notion Q1 record: entity AUM +20-22% and gold AUM +25-30%. The top end moved up from 22% to 25%.
- C-6 Credibility, branches. Q1: 0 of 200 opened. Now 74 opened in Q2 and "at least 150" more promised in H2, about 224 in total, above the 200 plan.
