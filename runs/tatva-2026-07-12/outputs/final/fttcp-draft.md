## MY RULINGS

Every call I made, as a statement. Confidence tag, then the single fact that would prove it wrong.

1. **Forward window: 3 months primary, 6 months secondary, 12 months for ROCE.** `sure`. The company prints quarterly, so a 3 month window is real. Wrong only if it stopped quarterly reporting, which it has not.

2. **Business type: standard operating business, run the four standard transitions.** `sure`. Tatva is an asset heavy specialty chemicals manufacturer, not a lender. Net block plus CWIP is Rs 634 Cr against Rs 506 Cr revenue (screener Data_Sheet, FY26). Wrong only if it were a bank or NBFC, which it is not.

3. **Workup intent: first workup, so all Role 1 derived fields are N/A.** `sure`. FTTCP runs before valuation, and no companies/TATVA.md exists. Wrong only if a prior Notion page turned up, which it did not.

4. **Sector cap row: Specialty chemicals, 35x, NOT the manifest's Pharma / CDMO at 38x.** `sure`. Tatva makes phase transfer catalysts, structure directing agents, electrolyte salts, and pharma and agro intermediates, and sells them as process chemicals. It is not a contract drug manufacturer (B04). Phase 3 stage 11 must use the 35x specialty chemicals row from the Section 1B cap table, not 38x. Wrong only if a future disclosure reclassified the revenue base as regulated CDMO drug manufacturing, which nothing supports.

5. **Backward status: 0 of 4 transitions positive, BACKWARD FAIL.** `fairly sure`. Revenue STAGNANT, margin VOLATILE, cash DETERIORATING, ROCE TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED. None of those is a positive verdict. Wrong if you read the FY26 rebound as already firing on a two year basis, which the three year numbers do not support.

6. **Cash conversion is INDETERMINATE, leaning structural, not clean growth-induced.** `fairly sure`. Debtor days rose from 48 to 86 across FY22 to FY26 (computed from screener Data_Sheet receivables and sales) even through FY23 to FY25 when revenue was flat to falling, which is the part growth cannot explain. The rating rationale that would settle it is not in the inputs. INDETERMINATE cannot resolve to a clean pass, so it caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. Wrong if the missing CRISIL rationale names a purely growth linked working capital build and debtor days fall back below 160 as the ramp matures.

7. **Cash conversion forward verdict: DECLINING, catalyst strength NONE.** `fairly sure`. No debtor day target, no factoring facility, no payment term change was named across three concalls, and ROCE and working capital questions were deflected twice. Absence across two or more calls is the loud signal. Wrong if management names a specific working capital program with a number and a date in the Q1 FY27 call.

8. **The Kernex cap engages: cash DECLINING with catalyst NONE caps the verdict at DEEP WATCH.** `sure`, given ruling 7. The single most diagnostic transition is going the wrong way with nothing observable to turn it, and strength elsewhere cannot average that away. Wrong only if ruling 7 is wrong.

9. **ROCE is TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED backward, RECOVERING forward, not FIRING.** `genuinely uncertain`. Two readings. Bull reading: the collapse from 18% (FY22) to 1.2% (FY25) was post IPO cash bloat plus the Dahej capex deployed but not yet earning plus the FY25 margin trough, all temporary, and FY26 already recovered to 6.6%. Bear reading: ROCE has sat below 15% for four straight years (FY23 to FY26), which is the STRUCTURALLY LOW definition, and a fresh Rs 250 to 275 Cr Jolva capex cycle is about to bloat the denominator again. The draft uses the bull reading for the backward label and the disciplined RECOVERING (not FIRING) for the forward, because the recovery to premium levels is not yet in the reported numbers. Wrong if FY27 ROCE stalls in mid single digits while Jolva capital lands.

10. **Revenue forward verdict: FIRING.** `fairly sure`. FY26 revenue rose 32% to Rs 506 Cr, every quarter of FY26 rose sequentially, and the new Dahej block that drives it is commissioned, not promised. A stricter round-down reading is STARTING, because the three year backward label is only STAGNANT. I used FIRING because the acceleration is already in the reported quarters and the capacity behind it is documented. Wrong if Q1 FY27 revenue growth drops below about 15% year on year or turns down sequentially.

11. **Margin forward verdict: STARTING, not FIRING.** `fairly sure`. EBITDA margin recovered from 9.0% (FY25) to 18.4% (FY26) and the exit quarter hit 21.0%, but that is one year off a trough, the Q4 raw material cost spike already knocked Q4 PAT below Q3, and 18.4% still sits below the peer ceiling. Round down. Wrong if two more quarters hold 20% or better through an input cost cycle.

12. **Composite forward score: +3 of a possible +8, position DEEP WATCH.** `fairly sure`. Revenue +2, margin +1, cash −1, ROCE +1. The Kernex cap holds it at DEEP WATCH even if revenue were scored +1 rather than +2. Wrong if the cash transition gets a catalyst, which would lift the cap and let the score speak.

13. **ROCE recovery is credited via Pillar 1, not the Strategic Premium.** `sure`. Default route under the single credit rule, and the recovery is a forward ROCE story, not a separate re-rating option. Flag: the Dahej commissioning drives both the Pillar 1 forward ROCE and the Pillar 3 growth premium, so this is a SHARED CATALYST for Role 3 to stress test. Wrong only if stage 11 chooses the strategic premium route instead, which it should not.

## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

What could make each transition fire, what the evidence is, what confirms it, what kills it.

**Revenue has the strongest case.** The new Dahej block is commissioned and running, not promised, so the volume is real. Electrolyte salts went from almost nothing to Rs 13 Cr in a single quarter, and management guides them to 8 to 10% of revenue by FY27. A new pharma intermediate stream is guided to Rs 70 to 75 Cr in FY27. What confirms it: FY27 revenue growth holding near the guided 25% and electrolyte revenue actually reaching the guided share. What kills it: the demand recovery that management describes is contradicted by two of its four listed peers. Clean Science and Neogen both described soft or reversing demand across the exact FY26 window Tatva frames as a restocking recovery. So part of the revenue story rests on a sector read the peer set does not support, and the company specific capacity is what carries it.

**Margin rests on operating leverage.** Reactors ran at 64% and assembly lines at 31% in FY25, so there is a long runway of fixed cost dilution as volume fills. Management named Rs 850 to 900 Cr as the full potential of the existing plant. What confirms it: quarterly margin holding 20 to 22% through an input cost cycle. What kills it: another raw material shock like the Q4 FY26 one that the company could not fully pass through, or Chinese producers capping realisations, which Clean Science flagged directly.

**Cash conversion has no catalyst, and that is the finding.** There is no factoring facility, no debtor day target, no payment term renegotiation anywhere in three concalls. Management deflected the working capital and ROCE questions twice. The one thing that would help, the capex cycle ending and freeing cash, is not coming, because Jolva is a fresh Rs 250 to 275 Cr cycle just starting. What would change this: a named working capital program with a number and a date, or the CRISIL rationale showing the receivables build is purely growth linked and about to reverse. Until then this transition is going the wrong way with nothing to turn it.

**ROCE depends on the same capacity fill that drives revenue.** As utilisation rises and margin normalises on a capital base that is done expanding for the existing plant, ROCE mechanically recovers. That is already visible, 1.2% to 6.6%. What confirms it: FY27 ROCE reaching low double digits. What kills it: Jolva capital landing on the balance sheet while its revenue is still two years out, which pushes ROCE back down. Note that this catalyst is the same commissioning that drives the revenue and growth premium, so it is a single point of failure.

## THE VERDICT

The composite forward score is +3 of a possible +8: revenue firing, margin starting, ROCE recovering, cash declining. That lands in the DEEP WATCH band on its own. The Kernex cap then engages, because the cash transition is going the wrong way with no observable catalyst, and that holds the verdict at DEEP WATCH regardless of the score. The whole call turns on one print: whether operating cash flow turns clearly positive and debtor days start falling while revenue keeps growing. If that happens, the cash cap lifts and this becomes a genuine four transition inflection. If the next operating cash flow is negative again on rising revenue with debtor days above 185 on a consolidated basis, the cash flag hardens to structural and the recovery thesis loses its floor.
