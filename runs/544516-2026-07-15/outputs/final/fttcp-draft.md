## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

Revenue has real, documented fuel. The order book and named order wins (Integral Coach Factory, Acme India, BEML Chennai Metro), the Janatics door systems MOU worth up to Rs 95 Cr over FY27 and FY28, and a Q1 FY27 already up 200% are concrete. What confirms it is FY27 revenue tracking toward Rs 500 Cr. What kills it is another guidance cut or the order book shrinking faster than the company executes, which has started (Rs 469 Cr to Rs 433.8 Cr in one quarter).

Margin has no documented catalyst, only a claim. The company says input costs will ease and price variation clauses will help. A peer expanding margin in the same steel window undercuts the sector shock story. What would confirm a real turn is two consecutive halves with EBITDA margin back toward 22 to 25%. What keeps it down is the same input exposure plus the planned step up in R&D spend to 8 to 9% of sales.

Cash conversion has NONE FOUND, and that absence is the whole point of this analysis. There is no sanctioned factoring facility, no debtor day target that has actually been hit, and no operating cash flow turn. Management has talked about working capital for two straight calls without a documented plan, which is a loud negative signal. What would confirm a catalyst is starting is a positive H1 FY27 operating cash flow print with receivables ageing improving. What confirms the structural read is another negative print. This is the transition the whole call turns on.

ROCE needs no catalyst to stay where it is, but has a counter catalyst. The new facility, the JV and the fresh debt are capital that dilutes the return until it earns. What would confirm ROCE holds is the return staying above 20% as that capex lands. What pulls it down is the working capital drain competing for the same capital.
