## PASS 2 NEW FINDINGS

1. **Advance received from customers jumped ~+514% in one year**: ₹14.06 Mn (FY25) → ₹86.33 Mn
   (FY26) standalone, ₹14.06 Mn → ₹86.54 Mn consolidated. (Note 23, standalone p.160-161 /
   consolidated p.210-211) 🟡 Watch — not discussed anywhere in Pass 1. A customer-advance balance
   that grows 6x in one year, right at fiscal year-end, on revenue growth of only 12-21%, is unusual
   and worth a management question: is this genuine new order intake (positive) or a year-end
   balance-sheet optic (e.g., a large single milestone advance booked just before close)? No
   named-customer or order-level detail is given.

2. **"Advance to others" / "Other Advances" almost doubled standalone, grew moderately consolidated,
   in a divergent pattern**: standalone ₹57.29 Mn (FY25) → ₹116.70 Mn (FY26), +103.7%; consolidated
   ₹62.03 Mn (FY25) → ₹79.59 Mn (FY26), +28.3% (Note 11, standalone p.154 / consolidated p.203) 🟡
   Watch — the standalone-only growth of ~₹60 Mn (roughly double the consolidated increase) implies a
   large advance given by the parent to an entity that either eliminates on consolidation (i.e., an
   intercompany advance) or sits outside the subsidiary perimeter. No breakdown of "other advances" by
   counterparty or purpose is given in either note. Worth cross-checking against item 3 below.

3. **New related-party "Advance For Purchase" of ₹47.14 Mn to Diffusion Engineers Singapore Pte Ltd**,
   nil in FY25, first appearance this year (Note 38.3, standalone p.174) 🟡 Watch — this likely explains
   most of the standalone/consolidated divergence in item 2 (an intercompany advance that eliminates on
   consolidation). Not itself disclosed as a loan, but a large trade advance to a wholly owned foreign
   subsidiary with no stated purpose, repayment terms, or interest is functionally similar to related-
   party financing and should be confirmed as arm's length / genuinely trade-related, not a working-
   capital support arrangement dressed as an advance.

4. **Standalone Trade Receivables Turnover Ratio also fell, not just the consolidated ratio Pass 1
   flagged**: 4.19x (FY25) → 3.61x (FY26), a 13.7% decline (Note 42, standalone p.176) 🟡 Watch — Pass
   1's FLAG-CASH item cited only the consolidated ratio (4.55x → 3.82x); the standalone-level
   deterioration confirms the receivables slowdown is not solely a subsidiary/trading-line effect, it
   is present in the parent's own books too.

5. **Return on Equity fell despite higher absolute profit**: standalone ROE 12.66% (FY25) → 12.07%
   (FY26); consolidated ROE 12.88% → 13.03% (marginal improvement) (Note 42, both) — the standalone
   ROE decline, combined with the Net Capital Turnover Ratio falling ~22-27% in both statements "due to
   increase in cash and cash equivalents due to IPO proceeds... yet to be utilised" (company's own
   explanatory note), indicates a meaningful chunk of the ₹1,579.64 Mn IPO proceeds raised in Oct 2024
   remains undeployed roughly 18 months after listing, diluting return ratios. 🟡 Watch — worth a
   management question on capital deployment timeline for the balance IPO proceeds (net of the ~₹185 Mn
   capex and ~₹190 Mn CWIP spend already made).

6. **Consolidated Fair Value Hierarchy table (Note 39) is internally inconsistent / template-like in
   both years**: Cash and Bank Balances (₹975.64 Mn FY26 / ₹1,270.54 Mn FY25) and Trade Receivables
   (₹1,281.79 Mn / ₹848.01 Mn) — neither of which are fair-valued instruments under Ind AS 107 — are
   classified as "Level 3" fair value inputs at an amount equal to their carrying value. Investments
   show a Level 2 figure of ₹313.97 Mn (FY26) / ₹262.14 Mn (FY25) against a "Level 3" line of
   **negative** ₹30.91 Mn (FY26) / negative ₹36.71 Mn (FY25) — a plug figure with no stated economic
   meaning. (Note 39, consolidated, pp.222-224) 🔴 Red Flag — this is not present in the standalone
   version of the same note (standalone Note 39, p.174, shows blank Level 1/Level 2 columns without a
   Level 3 column at all), so the consolidated fair-value-hierarchy disclosure appears to be a drafting
   error carried unchanged across two annual reports (same negative plug figure pattern in both FY25
   and FY26 columns), not a one-off typo. Flag for the auditor: this note fails to meaningfully
   disclose the fair value hierarchy as Ind AS 107 requires.

7. **Consolidated Note 41 "Adjustments arising out of consolidation" carries a growing negative profit
   share**: -₹19.53 Mn (-5.41% of consolidated profit, FY25) → -₹24.07 Mn (-4.77% of consolidated
   profit, FY26) (Note 41, consolidated, p.227) 🟡 Watch — partially explained by Note 44's disclosure
   that the ₹50.67 Mn dividend received by the parent from Diffusion Engineers Singapore is eliminated
   on consolidation (a normal mechanic), but the note gives no full reconciliation of what else sits in
   this ~₹24 Mn adjustment line (e.g., unrealised profit elimination on intercompany trading sales,
   which grew sharply this year per Pass 1 item on trading-revenue mix). Worth a management question to
   fully decompose this line.

8. **Currency Translation Reserve swung positive by ~₹10.5 Mn in equity, bypassing P&L**: -₹3.56 Mn
   (FY24) → -₹1.66 Mn (FY25) → +₹8.84 Mn (FY26) (Note 15, Statement of Change in Equity, consolidated,
   p.206-208) 🟡 Watch — a foreign-currency-translation gain of this size, sitting in OCI/equity rather
   than P&L, is notable given the company runs three loss-making foreign subsidiaries (Turkey,
   Philippines step-down, new UAE entity per Pass 1). No note explains which entity's translation moved
   or why the swing turned from a cumulative deficit to a surplus in one year; worth a question on
   whether this reflects currency movements (INR weakening vs the subsidiaries' functional currencies)
   or a change in consolidation scope/method.

9. **"Cheque in Hand" is a material, growing, and unusually specific line inside Cash and Cash
   Equivalents**: ₹120.03 Mn (FY26) vs ₹66.29 Mn (FY25), identical in both standalone and consolidated
   Note 9 (p.156/205), +81% YoY and now 12.3-12.8% of the total year-end cash balance. 🟡 Watch (minor)
   — cheques in hand (received but not yet banked/cleared) being classified as a cash equivalent is a
   quality-of-cash observation: this portion of "cash" was not actually available/cleared at the
   balance sheet date. Not disclosed as a distinct risk anywhere in the notes; worth confirming
   subsequent clearance in the ordinary course.

10. **A second internal numeric inconsistency inside consolidated Note 36 (Employee Benefit
    Obligations), beyond the Leave Encashment discrepancy Pass 1 already flagged as a Red Flag**: the
    Gratuity plan-assets closing balance for FY25 is shown as ₹31.36 Mn in the "amounts recognized in
    the Balance Sheet" sub-table (p.216) but as ₹31.53 Mn in the "changes in plan assets" reconciliation
    sub-table two pages later in the same note (p.218). (Note 36, consolidated) 🔴 Red Flag (strengthens
    Pass 1 finding #2) — Note 36 now shows at least TWO separate internal contradictions between its
    own sub-tables (Leave Encashment DBO and Gratuity plan assets), not one. This raises the priority of
    a company/auditor confirmation request on the entire Note 36 numeric set, not just the single figure
    Pass 1 identified.

11. **Consolidated Note 36's Leave Encashment "current liabilities" bifurcation for FY25 (₹1.62 Mn,
    p.219) does not match the standalone Note 36 FY25 figure for the identical disclosure (₹0.97 Mn,
    p.170)** — a further data inconsistency between the standalone and consolidated presentations of
    the same underlying obligation, on top of the ₹4.23 Mn vs ₹6.31 Mn discrepancy Pass 1 already
    flagged. (Note 36, both statements) 🟡 Watch — could reflect a subsidiary-level leave encashment
    liability not otherwise disclosed by name, or could be a further drafting error; either way it adds
    to the case that Note 36's numbers need company/auditor confirmation as a set.

12. **Deferred tax reconciliation's "Effect of Deferred Tax" line does not match between standalone and
    consolidated for the FY25 comparative**: standalone shows ₹3.55 Mn (Note 34, p.164) while
    consolidated shows ₹0.50 Mn (Note 34, p.214) for the same reconciling item label in the same
    comparative year. (Note 34, both) 🟡 Watch — a second reconciliation-line inconsistency between the
    two statements, separate from the "Others" catch-all sign-flip Pass 1 already flagged; both point to
    the tax note's reconciliation figures being difficult to tie out across the standalone/consolidated
    boundary.
