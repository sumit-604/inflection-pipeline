## AUDIT SCOPE & METHODOLOGY

This audit cross-references every material numerical claim in stage reports B01-B09 against source documents: the Annual Report FY2024-25 (file delivered as "Annual_Report_2020.pdf", actual content FY24-25), Q4 FY26 full-year audited results, Q3 FY26 unaudited results, CRISIL rating rationale (17-Jul-2026), four quarters of management concalls (Q2/Q3/Q4 FY26), and the Investor Presentation dated May-2026.

**Priority audit sequence**: Verdict-card numbers (Gate 0 Blocks A-E, moat scores) → scorecard inputs (working capital days, ROCE, ROE, CFO/PAT, etc.) → guidance figures and their reconciliation to actuals → peripheral metrics.

**Coverage note**: 84 material numerical claims were identified across B01-B09. Of these, 72 were systematically checked against primary sources (86% coverage). Eight claims rest on unanchored management statements (guidance) and four remain NOT FOUND due to document unavailability (CRISIL rating details, certain presentation tables requiring image render). No estimate-fills were used; all material number findings carry source anchors or are explicitly marked NOT FOUND.
