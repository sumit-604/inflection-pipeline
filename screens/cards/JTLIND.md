## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** JTL makes welded steel tubes and pipes. It buys hot rolled coil from primary mills and billets and strip from the secondary steel market. It forms them into black pipe, hollow sections and galvanised pipe. FY26 sales were 3,95,900 tonnes and revenue Rs 2,136.36 crore (AR26, p.55-56). The money is the spread between coil and pipe. EBITDA was Rs 3,900 a tonne in FY26 (FY26 deck `filings/..11052026144219..`, p.19). No customer is above 10% of revenue (AR26, p.297).

**What is changing?** Three things. A Direct Forming Technology (DFT) line at Mangaon makes large square and rectangular sections without roll changes. A 7 lakh tonne cold rolling and colour coating complex at Mangaon is due by the end of H1 FY27 (AR26, p.19, p.55). And JTL bought RCI Industries out of insolvency for Rs 46.5 crore, now JTL Defence, a copper and brass alloy plant (press release 21-Nov-2025, p.2).

**Why now?** Q1 FY27 was a record quarter. Consolidated revenue was Rs 721.61 crore and profit after tax Rs 35.37 crore, against Rs 543.86 crore and Rs 16.55 crore a year earlier (Q1 FY27 results `filings/..05082026123540..`, p.7). EBITDA per tonne rose from Rs 2,323 to Rs 4,954 (Aug-2026 deck, p.4).

**What must be true?** The margin gain must come from product mix, not from the steel cycle. Cash must start to come back from receivables, supplier advances and loans.

**What breaks it?** Two things. The spread between primary and secondary steel narrows from its present Rs 8 to 12 a kg (Aug-2026 call, p.10). Or the related party loans do not come back.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Two platforms. The coil platform makes pipe and sections from hot rolled coil. The secondary platform makes pipe from billet and strip, and JTL Engineering (66.96% owned) makes narrow HR coil for it (AR26, p.86; Aug-2026 deck, p.7). About 35% of sales were secondary in Q3 FY26 (Jan-2026 call, p.13). Value added products were 27% of FY26 volume (AR26, p.55).

Revenue per tonne did not move with the mix. It was Rs 55,147 in FY25 and Rs 53,963 in FY26 (FY26 deck, p.19). Management says DFT does not sell at a different price from black pipe (May-2026 call, p.7). Export margins are no better than domestic (May-2026 call, p.10).

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B Amendment 17 binds. The variables are the coil to pipe spread, utilisation and cycle position.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Mostly absent. JTL has scale near 1 million tonnes, plants near raw material, and a secondary steel cost option that saves Re 0.75 to Re 1 a kg (Jan-2026 call, p.13). That is a cost position, not pricing power. The certifications are real but narrow: ACRS for Australia, valid to 31-Dec-2026 (Aug-2026 deck, p.17), and CPWD approved maker status (press release 13-Oct-2026, p.2, image page). Management names only "two or three" DFT players in India (Jan-2026 call, p.12). Moat: forming at best, in DFT only.

## 4. The promoters

The Singla and Garg families hold 49.26% (AR26, p.273). Madan Mohan Singla, MD, holds 13.28% and Nikita Singla 13.35%; Rakesh Garg and his HUF hold 14.47% (AR26, p.272). Founder Mithan Lal Singla died on 8-Jun-2025 (AR26, p.66). The promoter group declared no encumbrance on 07-Apr-2026 (`filings/team_sandeshc..`, p.1, image page). The same declaration lists Jagan Industries Private Limited as promoter group (p.2).

**Why holding fell from 56% to 49%.** Promoters did not sell. Their share count was constant from March 2024 to March 2025, 96,118,918 shares before the split and 192,237,836 after (AR26, p.273). The fall came from issues to others. Non promoter warrant holders converted 4,163,323 shares in FY24 (AR26, p.272). A QIP placed 14,218,009 shares at Rs 211, Rs 300 crore, on 23-Jul-2024 (AR25, p.59). More warrant conversions followed to September 2024 (AR25, p.59). The rise to 49.26% is a net 1.4 million share addition, source NOT FOUND. Mithan Lal Singla's 8,793,428 shares moved to Pranav Singla inside the group (Jan-2026 call, p.16; AR26, p.273).

**Skin in the game.** In February 2024 JTL allotted 2.5 crore warrants at Rs 270 to promoter and public allottees. Nobody paid the balance by 01-Aug-2025. Rs 168.75 crore of application money was forfeited to capital reserve (AR26, p.63, p.271). The promoter share of those warrants is NOT FOUND. A Nov-2025 deck still says "Rs 6,750 Mn via preferential allotment by promoters" (`filings/..11112025120003..`, p.30).

**Related party dependence:** the core flag, set out in step 9.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM R1, commodity price taker. CLAIMED TO R3, value added spec supplier.** Management's words: "systematically pivot its production mix towards high-realisation, certified value-added offerings" (AR26, p.11).

The mechanism is DFT, colour coated product and export certification. The evidence for R1 is stronger today. ROCE is 8.6%. Revenue per tonne fell in FY26. Value added share was 24% in FY25 and 27% in FY26 (AR25, p.52; AR26, p.55), against a stated target above 40% (Q3 FY26 deck `filings/..24012026142814..`, p.29). The Aug-2026 call put it at "close to 35%" (p.4). That figure conflicts with the 27% in the annual report. The claimed jump is two rungs. The base rate is one rung in 2 to 3 years.

## 8. Proof check

Earnings: proof present. PAT rose in every quarter of FY26, and Q1 FY27 PAT more than doubled year on year.

Quality: proof absent. Two observations separate the readings. First, Dhruv Singla links the secondary demand to a primary to secondary spread of Rs 8 to 12 a kg, against a normal Rs 4 to 5 (Aug-2026 call, p.10). That is a cycle, not a rung. Second, cash did not follow profit. Receivables rose from Rs 279.92 crore to Rs 414.29 crore, and Rs 98.95 crore of them is more than a year past due (AR26, p.268). The Q2 FY27 numbers, due in mid November, are the next test.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Related party loans.** Loans outstanding at 31-Mar-2026: Jagan Industries Pvt Ltd Rs 84.35 crore, Powersol Metalcraft Rs 98.20 crore, Mirage Infra Rs 12.14 crore (AR26, p.300). Jagan is promoter group (Reg 31(4) declaration, p.2). CARO reports 100% of standalone loans, Rs 233.03 crore, as repayable on demand or without terms; Rs 232.34 crore is to related parties (AR26, p.177). The directors' report says no material related party transactions (AR26, p.71).
- **Powersol.** JTL paid Rs 8.10 crore for 47.97% in February 2026. The board filing says no promoter interest (BM outcome 23-Feb-2026, p.2). JTL's share of its net worth is Rs 1.87 crore, against a Rs 98.20 crore loan (AR26, p.87).
- **Regulators.** An Income Tax provisional attachment under the Benami Act on Mangaon land, received 02-Mar-2026 (Reg 30 filing, p.2). The land, Rs 3.09 crore, sits in the name of Vijay Singla, a director's relative (AR26, p.265, p.303). The Enforcement Directorate seized Rs 40 lakh of cash (AR26, p.241, p.269). CARE in 2022 noted a CBI money laundering case against Vijay Singla, ex director and promoter (`credit-ratings/05-CARE.pdf`, p.1). He also guarantees the bank lines (AR26, p.278).
- **Debt claim.** The annual report says "zero net-debt" (AR26, p.4, p.21, p.23). The company's own deck shows net debt of Rs 1,995 Mn (FY26 deck, p.23). Borrowings are Rs 244.06 crore against cash of Rs 34.75 crore (AR26, p.249).
- **Auditor.** Emphasis of matter on unconfirmed balances, on JTL Defence CIRP aspects whose impact is "not ascertainable", and on the ED seizure (AR26, p.240-241). CARO: a deposit rule breach of Rs 35.29 lakh (AR26, p.177). JTL Engineering's auditor resigned (AR26, p.244). Powersol results are unaudited.
- **People.** CFO Atul Garg left on 11-Sep-2025 after 20 months (AR26, p.66, p.298).

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**Only 2021 to 2023 rationales are held.** The newest, CARE on 16-Oct-2023, reaffirmed A-/Stable and A2+ and withdrew at the company's request (`credit-ratings/01-CARE.pdf`, p.1). It named three constraints: negative operating cash flow, Rs 500-550 crore capex execution risk, and debt if accruals fell short. All three have since happened. Infomerics moved JTL to "Issuer Not Cooperating" in September 2022 (`04-Infomerics.pdf`, p.1), and India Ratings did the same in July 2023 (`01-CARE.pdf`, p.3). AR26 cites an Infomerics IVR A/Positive from 23-Oct-2024, reaffirmed in FY26 (p.121). That rationale is NOT FOUND. Today's filings outweigh the 2023 view.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Profit recovered; mix, price per tonne, ROCE and cash have not moved.
- **Ugliness: split.** Capex in progress is an artifact of the climb. Rs 195 crore of on demand loans to related party firms is a structural feature. The matrix reads the worse one.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Screener service record, 18-Oct-2026: market cap Rs 3,336 crore, trailing PE 29.1x (`market-data-2026-10-18.md`). That is above the R3 neighbourhood of about 19x for an R1 business.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP), with the AVOID overlay (gap CLOSED + STRUCTURAL) live.**

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

Bull case, same bar: a record quarter, DFT volume doubled, Rs 100 crore of capex left (Aug-2026 call, p.6) and a 2 MTPA base. If FY27 meets guidance and the loans come home, ROCE recovers fast. That case is real. It is also priced, and the governance facts sit under it.

**Load bearing facts if the name returns**
1. Repayment status and terms of the Jagan, Powersol and Mirage loans.
2. FY27 operating cash flow sign and receivable ageing.
3. EBITDA per tonne after the primary to secondary spread normalises.
4. Outcome of the Benami attachment and the ED matter.
5. A current credit rationale.

**What would change the view:** the related party loans repaid in cash, positive operating cash flow for two halves, and colour coated volume at a higher price per tonne. No price is set and this is not a position.
