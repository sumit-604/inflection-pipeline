Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from time to time, we are pleased to inform you that 
Marushika Technology Limited as a member of a consortium comprising iRAM Technologies 
Private Limited (Lead Member), Marushika Technology Limited and SRK Buildtech LLP, has 
been elected as a part of the consortium by Nashik Municipal Corporation (“NMC”) for the 
Development of Multi Level Car Parking (MLCP) at Yashwant Mandai, Nashik for 
Simhastha Kumbh Mela on Public Private Partnership (PPP) basis. 
 
The Company has received/been informed of the Letter of Award dated 12th August, 2026 issued 
by Nashik Municipal Corporation in favour of consortium. 
 
Details as required under the SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular 
HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 as amended from time to 
time, are provided in Annexure A to this letter. 
 
The above information is also being available on the website of the Company www.marushika.in  
You are requested to take the above information on your records.
