## DECISION LINE

Entero screens as **AVERAGE** on Gate 0: best-in-class top-line growth (20/20) and a STRONG
moat count (5/12, anchored in distribution reach, receivable-day discipline through scale, and
margin-expanding pricing power) sit against genuinely weak historical capital efficiency (7-yr
median ROCE 3.85%, still 9.41% in FY26 — sub-10%) and a cash-conversion history dominated by six
years of negative CFO before a first-time positive FY26. The improving trajectory across nearly
every metric (ROCE, EBITDA margin, gross margin, CFO) is the central transition-alpha signal for
this name; whether that trajectory is durable rather than a one-year inflection is squarely a
question for downstream stages (moat durability, quality-of-earnings, and valuation), not
resolved by this scorecard.
