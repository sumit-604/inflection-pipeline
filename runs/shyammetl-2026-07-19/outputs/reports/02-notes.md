## PASS 3 PATTERN FINDINGS (new items only)

Not empty — one materially significant new pattern identified; the remainder of the pattern screen (restatements, subsequent events, going-concern language) confirms and sharpens items Pass 1/Pass 2 already carried rather than adding further new items.

**P3-1. "Single segment" disclosure is directly contradicted by the Group's own Note 47 entity-level P&L breakdown.** Pass 1 flagged the "one business segment: iron and steel" claim (standalone Note 47, p.251; consolidated Note 48, p.328) as disclosure minimalism (🟡 Watch). Pass 2 then extracted Note 47's "Statement of net assets and profit or loss attributable to owners and non-controlling interest" (p.326-327), which reports P&L and net-asset contribution entity by entity: SSPL (steel) 45.88% of consol P&L, Shyam Energy (power) near-nil P&L but 58.3% of consol OCI, Shree Venkateshwara Electrocast (aluminium foil) a loss, Ramsarup (steel, 60%-owned) newly profitable. Reading these two notes together shows the Company already prepares and discloses entity-level (functionally business-line-level, since each entity maps to one activity — steel, power, aluminium foil, mining) profitability data inside the financial statements, while simultaneously asserting to the market that only one operating segment exists and declining to disaggregate segment profitability under Ind AS 108. The information the "single segment" claim withholds from a standard segment note is, in substance, already sitting in Note 47 in a different format. This is not a disclosure gap so much as a disclosure choice: the same underlying data is presented in a form (net-asset/NCI statement) that draws far less analyst attention than a segment note would. 🔴 Red Flag (upgraded from Pass 1's Watch rating on re-reading the two notes together). Anchor: Note 47 consol (p.326-327) cross-read against Note 48 consol (p.328) and standalone Note 47 (p.251).

**P3-2. Going-concern language.** The only going-concern-adjacent language anywhere in the two note sets and the two Independent Auditor's Reports is CARO clause 3(xix) applied to Shree Venkateshwara Electrocast/Electroplast Pvt Ltd (90%-owned aluminium foil subsidiary): "material uncertainty exists about the ability of the company to meet its liabilities as they fall due within a period of one year from the balance sheet date" (Consolidated Independent Auditor's Report, CARO Annexure, qualification table, p.260, cross-referenced at p.~258-267). This applies to ONE subsidiary only. Neither the standalone nor the consolidated auditor's opinion itself carries any going-concern qualification or Emphasis of Matter for the Group as a whole, and management's own going-concern assessment (accounting policy note, both standalone and consolidated) asserts the entity will continue as a going concern with no material uncertainty identified at the Group level. NONE at the Group/Holding Company level; ISOLATED to one 90%-owned unit at the subsidiary level.

**P3-3. Restatements/regrouping — confirmed thin.** Consolidated Note 53 (p.330), the sole restatement disclosure, is one boilerplate sentence with no quantification (Pass 2 finding, reconfirmed on this pass). The only substantive prior-period adjustment identifiable anywhere in the notes is the NCLT-approved common-control merger of Shyam Metalics Flat Product Pvt Ltd into SSPL, effective retrospectively from 1 April 2023 under pooling-of-interests accounting (Note 45 consol, p.323) — this restates FY24 comparatives at the SSPL/consolidated level via a mechanism outside the Note 53 boilerplate, and its quantum is not separately disclosed either.

**P3-4. No further cross-note numeric contradictions found beyond the two already identified in Pass 2** (subsidy claim ₹41.83cr KAM vs ₹57.16cr Note 16; electricity duty ₹59.17cr CARO vs ₹54.17cr Note 41(c)). A targeted re-check of the other large contingent-liability, RPT, and provision figures against their cross-references (Note 41 vs CARO; Note 42 vs Note 18 shareholding; Note 47 entity P&L vs Note 46 subsidiary summarised financials) found the figures to tie out consistently.

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management / comparability distortion**: FY24 consolidated PAT was inflated by a one-off ₹(338.57)cr tax-recognition reconciling item (unabsorbed losses/depreciation) that did not recur in FY25 — the true FY24→FY25 operating decline is understated by the reported 12.2% PAT fall once this is stripped out (Note 37(c), p.309-310).
- **Quality-of-earnings dilution**: standalone and consolidated PBT growth are both meaningfully driven by treasury/investment income (fair value gains on FVTPL investments + interest income), ~23% of the standalone PBT increase and a similar proportion at consol level, rather than core steel-cycle operating performance (Note 45(e) standalone; P&L/cash-flow reconciliation both levels).
- **Undisclosed/underdisclosed risk indicator — circular related-party cross-holding**: ₹352.31cr (consol) equity investment in entities that are themselves ~35% promoter shareholders of SMEL, with no explanatory commentary anywhere in the AR on the structural rationale (Note 7(a) p.220/289; Note 18(e)/(f) p.296-297).
- **Aggressive/minimal disclosure choice**: "single business segment" claim (standalone Note 47 p.251; consol Note 48 p.328) is contradicted by the Group's own Note 47 entity-level P&L/net-asset data (p.326-327), which shows materially divergent profitability by business line that a standard segment note would surface.
- **Confirmed regulatory/control failure**: server backup of books of account confirmed NOT kept on India-located servers for some subsidiaries during FY25 (Consol Auditor's Report, p.258) — a Companies (Accounts) Rules compliance failure, not merely an open question.
- **Internal-control weakness in the disclosure/reporting process itself**: two unreconciled cross-note numeric discrepancies (subsidy claim receivable ₹41.83cr KAM vs ₹57.16cr Note 16; electricity duty ₹59.17cr CARO vs ₹54.17cr Note 41(c)) within the same annual report, same balance sheet date.
- **Systemic subsidiary-level distress**: 11 of 13 Group entities (84.6%) carry a CARO clause 3(xvii) "cash losses" qualification, far broader than any single-entity issue, directly corroborating the ₹955.21cr gross unrecognised DTA.

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate-to-concerning accounting practices. Key concern: consolidated profitability is deteriorating at the core operating subsidiary (SSPL) and across 84.6% of Group entities even as the parent injects capital and reports standalone PAT growth of 39.3%, a divergence compounded by a circular related-party cross-holding with promoter entities, confirmed IT/backup control failures, and a "single segment" disclosure choice that the Company's own Note 47 data contradicts. Key strength: standalone (parent-only) revenue recognition, receivables (ex-related-party), contingent liabilities, and actuarial/provisioning disclosures are clean and declining in risk, and no Group-level going-concern qualification exists — the going-concern-adjacent language is isolated to one 90%-owned aluminium-foil subsidiary. Overall accounting quality: 5/10.
