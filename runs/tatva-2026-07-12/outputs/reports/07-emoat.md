### 2C Growth embedded in capex — formal handoff to TAM stage (show the arithmetic)

**Fixed asset turnover (historical, 📄 AR consolidated financial statements):**
- Net PP&E, 31 March 2024: ₹3,965.36mn (AR Note 3)
- Net PP&E, 31 March 2025: ₹4,959.96mn (AR Note 3)
- Average net PP&E FY25: (3,965.36 + 4,959.96) / 2 = ₹4,462.66mn
- Revenue from operations, consolidated FY25: ₹3,827.14mn (AR Note 24 / P&L)
- **Historical fixed asset turnover = 3,827.14 / 4,462.66 = 0.858x**
- (Cross-check using closing-PP&E-only basis: 3,827.14 / 4,959.96 = 0.772x — range 0.77x-0.86x)

**Capex genuinely "under execution" today (run date July 2026):** the new Dahej block (~₹101cr midpoint) is already spent and reflected in FY26 actuals — it is delivered, not embedded future growth. The only capex still ahead of us with a disclosed number is **Jolva** (₹250-275cr, midpoint ₹262.5cr, 🎙️ Q3 FY26 call), though its groundbreaking has slipped three consecutive quarters (B05 HIGH flag) and is not yet capitalised on the balance sheet (AR Note 46 capital commitments as at 31 March 2025 were only ₹118.66mn + ₹0.64mn ≈ ₹11.93cr — i.e. Jolva was not yet a formal capital commitment as of the FY24-25 AR date, since the project was only unveiled in later FY26 concalls).

- Implied incremental revenue = ₹262.5cr × 0.858x = **₹225.15cr** (avg-PP&E method); range ₹202.55cr-₹225.15cr using the 0.77x-0.86x turnover band
- Current revenue base (FY26 actual, 📄 IP p.5/8): ₹505.9cr
- **capex_embedded_growth_pct = 225.15 / 505.9 = 44.5%** (range ~40.0%-44.5% depending on turnover method; ~58.8% if FY25 revenue of ₹382.7cr is used as the base instead of FY26 actuals)

**Flag for the TAM stage**: management's own stated Jolva Phase 1 revenue potential (₹400-500cr, midpoint ₹450cr) implies **~89%** uplift over current revenue — roughly double the ₹225cr the historical-fixed-asset-turnover formula implies. Either Jolva is expected to run at an unusually high asset turnover / higher-value output mix than Tatva's historical blended average, or the ₹400-500cr figure is optimistic. Given the credibility record (Jolva groundbreaking slipped 3x, B05 credibility_grade B), the TAM stage should treat the formula-derived ₹225cr/44.5% as the more defensible base case and the management figure as an unconfirmed upside case requiring the groundbreaking to actually occur.

## SECTION 3: THE 20-CATEGORY SCAN

### FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability.** Evidence: Tatva describes itself as "the largest and only commercial manufacturer of SDA for Zeolites in India" and "2nd largest globally" (🎙️ self-reported, IP p.14, AR p.10-11, no independent market-research citation for share); "one of the few companies globally that uses Electrolysis process in organic synthesis" with disclosed 1-6 year approval/qualification timelines (IP p.15, mixed 🎙️/📄 — the *existence* of the electrolysis process since 2015 and the *general* approval-timeline claim are documented business facts, but "largest/2nd largest" market-share framing is unverified). Semiconductor ultra-high-purity chemicals: genuinely long qualification cycle disclosed for the first time in Q4 FY26 (8-9 years, two-tier MNC-integrator-then-fab process), with one small paid trial order actually received (📄, "a few thousand dollars", Q3 FY26 call). **Strength: Moderate.** Time to materialise: SDA — active now; semiconductor — 1-3 years for material revenue.

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No patent filings are disclosed anywhere in the AR (Annexure F "Technology Absorption" describes process know-how and "prototype" technology transcription from lab to commercial scale but never references a patent application or grant), nor in the concalls or investor presentation. The only IP-adjacent balance-sheet item is "technical knowhow" intangible asset at a carrying value of ₹0.07mn (AR Note 6) — immaterial. Tatva's moat here, to the extent it exists, is trade-secret/process-know-how based, not patent-based.

**A3 — Process innovation.** Evidence: continuous flow chemistry (adopted since 2018, IP p.18); electrolysis-based SDA manufacture since 2007 R&D / 2015 commercial (IP p.15); photo-chlorination "transcribed from lab scale to pilot scale and further to the commercial scale" and bromine-recovery process development (AR Annexure F, p.67, 📄); R&D capex+revenue spend of ₹128.39mn in FY24-25 (📄, AR p.7/68, IP p.22). Waste-intensity and cost-efficiency claims are directly tied to these processes ("lowest possible process mass intensity...leads to higher margins", IP p.18). **Strength: Moderate** (kept distinct from the ESG metrics credited under H3 to avoid crediting the same improvement twice). Time to materialise: already active.

**A4 — Product platform / modular architecture.** Evidence: 12 new products added in FY24-25 against 226 products total as of 31 March 2025 (📄, AR p.7/3) — ~5.6% SKU growth in one year; facilities explicitly "designed to allow a level of flexibility...to modify and customize product portfolio" (📄, IP p.21, AR p.122). However, R&D spend has NOT grown proportionally — it fell from ₹254.67mn (FY23, one-off R&D-centre-build peak) to ₹101.95mn (FY24) to ₹128.39mn (FY25) to ₹78.32mn (FY26) — and the revenue contribution of the 12 new FY24-25 products is not separately disclosed. **Strength: Moderate.** Time to materialise: ongoing; revenue contribution visibility 1-2 years out.

### FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN

**B1 — Backward integration and RM security.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND — and the evidence available points the other way: Q4 FY26 flagged a 30-40% amines/ammonia RM cost spike tied to the Iran conflict, being passed through "with a lag" (🎙️, 05-concall.md), which is inconsistent with meaningful backward integration or captive RM sourcing. No captive power, no 5+ year supply agreements disclosed.

**B2 — Qualification lock-in.** Evidence: marquee, long-tenured customer list spanning multiple segments (Merck, Laurus Labs, Divi's, Atul, SRF, Bayer, MOL, Navin Fluorine, Firmenich, Tosoh, Otsuka, Asian Paints, Hawks Chemical — AR p.8, IP p.25, 📄 logo list but no contract terms disclosed); SDA "entry barriers as product development and approvals take anywhere between 1-6 years" (IP p.15, 🎙️); semiconductor's disclosed 8-9 year, two-tier qualification chain (🎙️, Q4 FY26 call) is itself evidence that once qualified, switching costs would be very high — but Tatva is not yet through that qualification. No signed multi-year agreement terms, design-in language, or customer-funded tooling was found anywhere in the documents. **Strength: Weak.** Time to materialise: 2-5 years (semiconductor in particular).

**B3 — Supply chain network effect.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Tatva is a manufacturer, not a platform/aggregator.

### FAMILY C — CUSTOMER

**C1 — Customer ecosystem / embedded relationships.** Evidence: narrative claims of "long standing relationships" and being a "trusted and preferred global supplier" across the multi-segment customer base (🎙️, AR p.8) — but no cross-sell metric, wallet-share figure, ERP/workflow integration evidence, AMC/installed-base revenue, or co-development disclosure was found. Management itself notes Tatva is explicitly NOT sole-source in pharma intermediates ("2-3 companies globally offering the same product", Q2 FY26 call) — a direct counterweight to any strong-lock-in claim in that segment. **Strength: Weak.**

**C2 — Customer concentration improving.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. The injected concall record explicitly flags this as an undisclosed gap: "no concentration metric (e.g., top-5 customer % of revenue) is ever disclosed across the three calls" despite analysts repeatedly probing customer-specific growth drivers (05-concall.md, section 2D). Debtors turnover ratio actually *declined* (5.59→4.60, MD&A p.124), the opposite direction of what an improving-diversification, faster-collecting customer base would show.

### FAMILY D — DATA & DIGITAL

**D1 — Proprietary data asset.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND.

**D2 — Digital platform.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Generic ERP/IT-infrastructure language exists (AR p.125) but describes internal operations tooling, not a data or platform moat.

### FAMILY E — GEOGRAPHIC & ACCESS

**E1 — Geographic first-mover.** Evidence: both manufacturing facilities sited "in-close proximity to Hazira port" (📄, IP p.21) — a genuine logistics fact; Dahej-III GIDC land bank acquired in 2021 (📄, AR milestones p.4-5), later earmarked for the Jolva greenfield; "largest and only" SDA claims (🎙️, see A1, not re-counted here to avoid double-crediting). **Strength: Weak** (mostly self-reported framing, with the port-proximity and land-bank facts being the only clean 📄 anchors).

**E2 — China+1 beneficiary.** Evidence: "China plus one" positioning cited repeatedly in the MD&A as an industry-wide driver (📄 backdrop, AR p.121, IP p.29 — India only 4% of global chemical industry vs China's 39%, so this is opportunity framing rather than company-specific proof); two new SDA customers onboarding off Euro7 qualification, one invoicing ~Aug 2026 (🎙️, Q3 FY26 call) — the clearest company-specific signal; SDA FY27 export-linked growth guided ≥20% (🎙️). **Strength: Moderate.** Time to materialise: near-term (Aug 2026 is the first hard confirm/kill signal).

### FAMILY F — TALENT & ORGANISATIONAL

**F1 — Talent density.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND for a positive moat — the evidence found is negative. R&D headcount is flat at 56 employees between 31 March 2025 (AR p.7) and 31 March 2026 (IP p.22), and the count of "highly qualified senior scientists" within that team actually *fell* from 29 (AR p.7, FY25) to 26 (IP p.22, FY26) — a discrepancy worth flagging even allowing for wording differences. R&D spend is declining post its FY23 R&D-centre-build peak (₹254.67mn FY23 → ₹128.39mn FY25 → ₹78.32mn FY26, 📄). No ESOP scheme exists for any employees, including technical staff ("Your Company has not provided any Stock Option Scheme to the employees," Board's Report item 7(d), AR p.51, 📄) — no retention-mechanics evidence of the kind this category looks for.

**F2 — Execution moat.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND for a positive moat — cross-referencing the injected B05 promise-delivery record produces the opposite finding. Of 9 tracked promises: 3 delivered, 3 partial, 3 missed; credibility_grade "B". Near-term financial guidance (revenue growth, 20-22% EBITDA margin band) was delivered essentially on schedule (margins tracked 18.0%→19.4%→20.9% across the three quarters, 📄 actuals), but **long-cycle capex execution is the weak point this category specifically tests**: Jolva greenfield groundbreaking slipped in *every one* of the three calls reviewed (Jan/Feb 2026→"this quarter"/Mar 2026→mid-July 2026), each time reframed by management as an engineering optimisation rather than an acknowledged delay (B05 flag, severity HIGH); semiconductor first commercial dispatch missed its original Q4 FY26 target without being explicitly flagged as a miss (severity MEDIUM); the new Dahej block itself was delivered but ~2 months late. This is a genuine, well-evidenced execution weakness, not a moat — carried into Section 6 as a top risk rather than scored here.

### FAMILY G — FINANCIAL & STRUCTURAL

**G1 — War chest.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND for a positive moat — again, the available evidence is negative. Per the injected B01-gate0 block: FCF negative in all 8 computable years (FY19-FY26, cumulative FCF/PAT -1.32), ROCE depressed to single digits since the FY22-26 capex cycle began (min 1.17% FY25). Additionally (new finding this stage): the long-term credit rating was downgraded from CRISIL A-/Stable to A-/Negative during FY24-25 (Board's Report item 28, AR p.57, 📄) — and appears to have been downgraded *again*, to BBB+/Stable, per the Q4 FY26 Investor Presentation dated 16 May 2026 (IP p.11, 📄) — a full-notch deterioration (A- to BBB+) inside roughly nine months, moving in the *opposite* direction from a war-chest thesis. Debt/equity remains structurally low (0.02x-0.33x across FY22-26, IP p.7) but that reflects a small balance sheet more than a growing net-cash cushion.

**G2 — WC improvement trajectory.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND for a positive moat. Per B01: the one WC-days window measurable (FY2025→FY2026, limited by missing pre-FY2025 payables data) shows WC Days *rising* from 175.3 to 185.3 (+9.98 days) — deteriorating, not improving. Debtors turnover ratio declined (5.59→4.60, MD&A p.124, 📄); inventory turnover improved modestly (1.30→1.54, 📄) but is the only bright spot in an otherwise negative trend.

### FAMILY H — ECOSYSTEM & EXTERNAL

**H1 — Industry consolidation beneficiary.** Evidence is genuinely mixed and nets out weak: Euro 7 emission-norm tightening is a real regulatory driver for SDA demand (📄 regulation, 🎙️ company benefit claim) — but in the same set of disclosures, management volunteers that the PTC segment (23% of FY26 revenue) has seen competitor count grow from 2 to 9-10 companies precisely *because* the chemistry is "very easy to replicate" (Q2 FY26 call) — i.e., PTC is *fragmenting*, not consolidating, in Tatva's favour. **Strength: Weak** (net of the offsetting PTC dynamic).

**H2 — Strategic partnerships.** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No JV, exclusivity arrangement, inbound technology licensing, co-development agreement, or strategic equity investor was found anywhere in the documents. (EcoVadis and "Together for Sustainability" are sustainability-audit memberships, not strategic business partnerships — reclassified under H3 below rather than double-counted here.)

**H3 — ESG moat.** Evidence: Zero Liquid Discharge status at Ankleshwar since January 2020, independently maintained (📄, AR p.19); EcoVadis Bronze medal 2023 and REACH-compliant certification (📄, AR p.9); "Together for Sustainability" (TfS) audit completed (📄, AR p.9/milestones p.5); ISO 14001:2015 and 45001:2018 certifications (📄); "Three Star Export House" status (📄, DGFT recognition); hard operational metrics — water recycled 30.31%, hazardous waste recycled/reused 64.67%, waste intensity reduction 4.92%/26.67%, energy consumed from waste-heat source 18% (all 📄, AR p.19). This is the single best-documented category in the scan — third-party-verified (EcoVadis, TfS) and quantified. **Strength: Strong.** Time to materialise: already active; whether it converts into a durable *competitive* advantage (vs. table-stakes cost of doing business with EU/pharma customers) rather than pure ESG compliance is the open question, carried into Section 6B risk.
