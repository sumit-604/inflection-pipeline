### 2C Growth embedded in capex

Formula: total capex under execution × historical fixed asset turnover = implied incremental revenue, as % of current revenue.

- Capex under execution (AR, FY25 balance sheet date): CWIP Rs262.68 Lakhs + Intangible Assets Under Development Rs23.75 Lakhs = **Rs286.43 Lakhs** (~Rs2.86cr).
- Fixed Asset Turnover (consol, FY25): FY25 revenue Rs46,503.81 Lakhs ÷ average net PPE [(FY25 Rs11,492.28 Lakhs + FY24 Rs11,015.18 Lakhs) / 2 = Rs11,253.73 Lakhs] = **4.132x**.
- Implied incremental revenue = Rs286.43 Lakhs × 4.132 = **Rs1,183.5 Lakhs** (~Rs11.84cr).
- As % of FY25 revenue (Rs465.04cr): **~2.5%**.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct ≈ 2.5%.** This is a genuinely low figure and it is an important finding in its own right: AESL's growth engine is not organic fixed-asset capex (confirmed asset-light per management, 2A above) but M&A (Kuiper acquisition, Oilmax merger) and order-book conversion (Vedanta, MCL Lakhanpur contracts). The 2C formula, designed for capex-led manufacturing/capacity stories, understates AESL's real forward growth potential because the primary growth mechanisms sit outside the fixed-asset base entirely. This distinction should be carried into Stage 9 rather than read as "weak emerging growth."

## SECTION 6: TIMELINE, RISKS & COMBINED ASSESSMENT

### 6A Moat evolution timeline

- **Next 12 months (to Jul-2027)**: Oilmax merger NCLT completion (guided Sep/Oct-2026) — the single largest structural catalyst; Kuiper FY27 revenue trajectory toward $60-65M first becomes visible in quarterly results; Tiphuk/Amguri gas-grid connections ("expected over next few months" per May-26 call); L1 NE India O&M contract award/signing.
- **12-24 months**: Duarmara production start (no committable date as of May-26 call, watch for further slippage); Mewad/Indrora ramp toward 1,000 BOPD (targeted by FY27); Kuiper margin ramp toward the 11-12% FY29 target begins to show in quarterly disclosures; coal handling pipeline beyond MCL either converts into new orders or stays aspirational.
- **24-36 months**: Mewad/Indrora ramp toward 1,500 BOPD; Oilmax integration into AESL's combined financials fully reflected across a full fiscal year; coal gasification/critical minerals evaluation either advances to a technology-partner agreement or is shelved.
- **3-5 years**: Oilmax standalone revenue target of Rs800-900cr (by FY29/30) tested against actual delivery; Kuiper $100M revenue target (by FY29) tested; consolidated PAT target of Rs450-500cr (by FY29) is the single number that will validate or invalidate the entire emerging-moat thesis.

### 6B Risks to each top-scoring emerging moat, with early warning signs

- **B1 (Oilmax merger)**: risk of further NCLT/completion slippage — the timeline has already shifted once (from an NCLT order referenced as 22-Apr-2026 in B01 to a shareholder meeting now scheduled Jun-2026, completion guided Sep/Oct-2026). Early warning: any further shift in the shareholder-meeting or NCLT date in subsequent filings.
- **B2 (Vedanta/MCL contracts)**: execution risk on a 7-year (MCL) and 57-month (Vedanta) horizon; the FY26 Minerals segment profit already declined YoY despite the MCL win, partly due to unseasonal monsoon disruption. Early warning: further weather- or client-related delays reported in subsequent quarters.
- **C1/C2 (customer base)**: concentration has genuinely improved, but the new anchor customers (Vedanta, Coal India) are themselves concentrated and large — a single execution stumble on either contract would be highly visible. Early warning: any renegotiation or scope reduction disclosed on these two contracts.
- **E1 (Kuiper)**: integration risk — the acquisition already caused a one-time exceptional cost that pushed Q2 FY26 PAT negative; the margin-improvement target (7% to 11-12%) is unproven. Early warning: Kuiper segment margin disclosure flatlining or missing guided quarterly milestones.
- **G1 (war chest)**: contingent on the Oilmax merger completing on a cash-and-carry, WC-light basis as described; if the merger slips materially, the balance-sheet benefit does not show up when expected. Early warning: any change in the rating agency's outlook trajectory.
- **H2 (AGCL BOOT)**: single-client relationship at modest scale (~Rs60cr); no evidence yet of a second BOOT-model client. Early warning: absence of a second BOOT contract announcement within 12-18 months.
