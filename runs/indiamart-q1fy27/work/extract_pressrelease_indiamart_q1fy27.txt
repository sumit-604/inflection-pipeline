46             First Quarter Ending June 30, 2026 - Results Press Release
47  
48   Noida, India, July 21, 2026: IndiaMART InterMESH Limited (referred to as “IndiaMART” or the
49   “Company”), today announced its financial results for the first quarter ending June 30, 2026.
50  
51  
52  
53                                       Q1 FY2027 vs. Q1 FY2026
54  
55    66
56     ▪   Consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs. 414 Crore, YoY growth of 11%
57     ▪   Standalone EBITDA of Rs. 149 Crore representing EBITDA margin of 40%
58     ▪   Consolidated Cash generated from Operations of Rs. 163 Crore
59  
60  
61  
62    Consolidated Financial Highlights (Q1 FY2027):
63  
64   IndiaMART reported consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs. 414 Crore as compared to Rs. 372
65   Crore in the corresponding quarter of last year, representing a growth of 11%. This includes IndiaMART
66   Standalone Revenue of Rs. 376 Crore, representing YoY growth of 9% and Busy Infotech Revenue of
67   Rs 36 Crore.
68  
69   Collections from Customer grew to Rs. 463 Crore for the quarter, representing YoY growth of 8%,
70   primarily comprising IndiaMART Standalone Collections of Rs. 402 Crore representing YoY growth
71   of 8% and Busy Infotech Collections of Rs 59 Crore.
72  
73   Deferred Revenue as on June 30, 2026 increased to Rs. 2,014 Crore representing a YoY growth of 16%.
74   This primarily includes IndiaMART Standalone Deferred Revenue of Rs. 1,858 Crore and Busy
75   Infotech Deferred Revenue of Rs. 146 Crore.
76  
77   Net Profit for the quarter was Rs. 172 Crore. Cash Flow from Operations for the quarter was Rs. 163
78   Crore. Cash and Investments balance stood at Rs. 3,553 Crore as on June 30, 2026.

85   Standalone Financial Highlights (Q1 FY2027):
86  
87   Standalone Revenue from Operations increased to Rs. 376 Crore as compared to Rs. 346 Crore last
88   year representing a growth of 9%. The growth was primarily driven by improvement in realization
89   from paying suppliers.
90  
91  
92   Collections from Customer grew to Rs. 402 Crore for the quarter representing a YoY growth of 8%
93   and Deferred Revenue as on June 30, 2026 increased to Rs. 1,858 Crore representing a YoY growth
94   of 14%.
95  
96  
97   EBITDA for the quarter was Rs. 149 Crore representing margin of 40%. Net Profit for the quarter was
98   Rs. 176 Crore.

100  
101   Operational Highlights (Q1 FY2027):
102  
103  
104   IndiaMART registered Unique business enquiries of 26 million in Q1FY27. Supplier Storefronts grew
105   to 8.8 million, an increase of 5% YoY and paying suppliers at the end of the quarter were 218K.
