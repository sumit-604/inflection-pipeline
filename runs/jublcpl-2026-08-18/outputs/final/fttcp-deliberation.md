## 2. OPERATOR OVERRIDES AND DIRECTIONS

**Override 1 — analyse as a demerger special situation, both businesses
separately.**
- Draft as first written: ran FTTCP on the blended entity and previewed a single
  blended P/E base card, with the retained-versus-demerging split surfaced but
  not run as two protocols.
- Operator ruling (18 August 2026), in the operator's words: "it is a de-merger
  situation which is going on here. It is a special situation, and we need to
  analyse both businesses separately to understand the investment opportunity
  here," and then "you need to give me FTTCP protocol for both the businesses."
- Action taken: added the sum of the parts (draft section 6), then ran the full
  FTTCP v1.2 protocol separately on Business A polymers (section 6A) and Business
  B agri (section 6B), each with Steps 1 to 5, scorecard, verdict, Pillar 1
  handoff, and its own destination multiple.

**Override 2 — exit multiple base approved as drafted.**
- Draft base: Business A polymers 35x (Specialty chemicals cap binds), Business B
  agri 14x to 17.5x on normalized earnings (Agri processing 20x cap), blended
  29.5x for the pre demerger entity, no quality uplift.
- Operator ruling: approved as drafted. No lever moved.

**Override 3 — earnings basis one-year forward (blended).**
- The draft left this open as the operator's call, offering per business trailing
  (SOTP), trailing blended, or one-year forward blended.
- Operator ruling: one-year forward, blended. No additional reason stated beyond
  the selection.
- Analyst note carried forward, not a re-argument: for a company mid split with no
  numeric guidance in the record (B05 confirms none), one-year forward EPS rests
  on annualizing a seasonally strong Q1 that still contains the agri division, so
  it is the least anchored of the three bases. Phase 3 MUST state the earnings
  perimeter explicitly next to the number, and should build a forward polymer
  only earnings figure as the primary value driver, since the retained business
  is where the value sits.

## 3. FINAL FTTCP VERDICT

The operator's expressed position, in the operator's words: this is "a special
situation" to be judged by "analys[ing] both businesses separately," with the
FTTCP protocol run on each.

The protocol verdicts the two runs produced, which the operator did not override:
- **Business A, polymers (retained, to become Jubilant Industries Ltd): composite
  plus 3, DEEP WATCH**, held at plus 3 only by a two year segment window and the
  absence of a standalone cash flow statement, and set to upgrade to a BUY
  candidate at 6 or 7 on the first standalone accounts. This is the business to
  want.
- **Business B, agri (leaves as Jubilant Agri Solutions Ltd): composite minus 2,
  AVOID**, with the Kernex cash cap also engaged. A subsidy linked commodity
  cyclical that lost money at segment level in FY25. Size small or exit into
  strength.
- **Blended entity as it trades today: composite plus 3, DEEP WATCH**, which is
  the arithmetic average of the two and the reason the SOTP view matters.

No STOP verdict exists in this framework and none is implied. The gate decision
carried from phase 1 was PROCEED WITH FLAGS on the evidence, and nothing in
review changed that; the FLAG-CASH determination is refined here to STRUCTURAL
and located in the demerging half.

## 5. VERIFIER DISAGREEMENT LOGGED IN REVIEW

Phase 1 Verifier A raised a CRITICAL that the polymer segment revenue of Rs
12,386 Mn did not appear in the audited segment table, and phase 1 corrected the
figure. On re-check during this session the number does exist: it is the
consolidated segment revenue of Rs 1,23,858 lakhs in the FY26 column of the Q1
FY27 results filing. It was a standalone versus consolidated basis mismatch, not
a fabrication. Disposition: the phase 1 correction stands, because the growth
rate it replaced (27.6%) was wrong on every audited basis (all read 7.48% to
8.75%), and this session uses the standalone Note 39 basis throughout for
consistency. Recorded so the disagreement is not buried.
