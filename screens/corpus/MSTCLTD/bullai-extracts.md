# Bull AI page-cited extracts: MSTCLTD

Rs crore unless noted.

## FY26 delivery (management guidance, Q4FY26 call/deck)
- Revenue from operations Rs 369.66 cr, highest in last four years. EBITDA Rs 307.49 cr vs Rs 260.04 cr FY25, up 18.25%. PAT (ex exceptional) Rs 221.69 cr vs Rs 180.13 cr, up 23%. Consolidated PAT Rs 218.43 cr vs Rs 407.07 cr (FY25 had FSNL sale exceptional gain). EPS 31.03.
- Value of goods transacted (GTV) Rs 797.37 bn. Sold 10 coal mines; auctioned 200+ major mineral blocks.

## FY27 guidance (management guidance)
- E-commerce organic growth: "the attempt shall be to maintain a double-digit growth if possible" ... "incremental growth", not very high.
- Exit legacy trading vertical during FY26-27, move to pure e-commerce.
- New platforms: EPR certificate trading (ETP live, pending formal approval); travel portal (B2B then B2C); coal/mineral exchanges (feasibility); TReDS (RBI clearance sought). Dividend policy: min 4% of net worth or 30% of PAT, whichever higher (DIPAM).

## Business model (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2025, Q1, Page 9, Equirus)
- "our main foray is the e-commerce segment ... we are a platform ... provide the E-auction services to various Governments, PSUs ... certain private sector players ... pioneers in e-procurement ... facilitate the e-auction of ... scrap ... Mineral resources. Timber forest produces ... spectrum".

## Revenue model (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2023, Q3, Page 15; FY2024, Q3, Page 13)
- "it is a mixed bag of revenue model. One is the value based, where we do the auction, we get a percentage of value, and another is an event based ... we get event-based charges, irrespective of the volume ... whether the auction is successful or not, we get our service charges." (FY23 Q3 p.15)
- Scrap and vehicle scrap and e-auctions and marketing; e-auction profitability is "a mix of both" percentage and flat fee (FY24 Q3 p.13).

## Competition GeM (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2023, Q3, Page 14)
- "very few people are using GeM Portal and our[s] are far superior ... we are doing the complete end to end ... some people who had gone to GeM, they are coming back to us."

## Q1FY25 standalone detail (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2025, Q1, Page 4)
- Total revenue Rs 86.01 cr vs Rs 100.63 cr, down 14%; e-commerce down 15% (Rs 94 cr to Rs 79.46 cr); EBITDA Rs 56.87 cr vs Rs 72.27 cr; PBT Rs 54.48 cr; PAT Rs 40.46 cr; EPS 5.75. E-commerce Rs 79.46 cr = E-auction/E-sale Rs 58.75 cr + E-procurement Rs 2.80 cr + other income Rs 20.04 cr.

## E-commerce softness cause (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2024, Q3, Page 6)
- "scrap rates that have gone down ... year-on-year ... by almost about 10% ... to compensate ... we are always on the lookout for expanding the volume ... iron ore, we are now trading bigger volumes, but there is some stress on reduction of the charges."

## FY24 consolidated (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2024, Q4, Page 4-5)
- GTV Rs 1,41,586 cr; consolidated revenue Rs 961.37 cr vs Rs 879.17 cr; consolidated PBT Rs 340.18 cr vs Rs 329.18 cr. 20 coal mine blocks allotted. Standalone: revenue from operations Rs 316.25 cr vs Rs 324.72 cr; other income Rs 203 cr vs Rs 173 cr. No provision for bad/doubtful debts this year. Standalone PAT Rs 171.91 cr vs Rs 239.24 cr. EPS 29.03 (consol) vs 34.37.

## FSNL disinvestment (Reg. 34(1) Annual Report, FY2024, Page 53)
- CCEA gave in-principle approval to disinvest MSTC's entire equity in Ferro Scrap Nigam Ltd (FSNL) via strategic disinvestment and transfer of management control. DIPAM appointed advisors; global EOIs invited. Shareholders approved.

## Mahindra MSTC Recycling JV (Reg. 34(1) Annual Report, FY2024, Page 53; Concall FY25 Q1 p.8)
- 50:50 JV with Mahindra Intertrade (Mahindra MSTC Recycling Pvt Ltd, MMRPL). India's first authorised collection and dismantling centre, plus RVSF units. Vehicle scrapping volumes below expectation; state-level policy implementation slow. Impairment loss on JV reduced to Rs 1.44 cr (FY26) from Rs 10.06 cr.
- Going-concern audit note references the Group and its Joint Venture (AR FY24 p.192).

## FY26 Q3 (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2026, Q3, Page 6, 12-Feb-2026)
- EBITDA grew from Rs 182.41 cr to Rs 199.95 cr (9.61%). PBT before exceptional Rs 192.29 cr vs Rs 175.29 cr (9.70%). PAT Rs 145.89 cr (prior year had FSNL exceptional). E-commerce is the prime business, grew 9.26%.

## Travel portal revenue model (Earnings Call Transcript, FY2026, Q3, Page 11)
- Travel booking for government and private/individuals; revenue via ticketing cost, platform fee, advertisement, revenue sharing; government route needs entitlement via Department of Expenditure (~4 months+).
