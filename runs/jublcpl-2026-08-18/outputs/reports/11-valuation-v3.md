## What Part B changed

Part B confirmed the earnings base and moved two things.

**B8 re-rating potential reads NONE.** JACPL rose 58% in five months. The
polymer destination of 35x equals 1.71x the market PE. The stock trades at
1.36x now. Reaching 35x needs a re-rating that B8 says will not come. So
the defensible central destination is the low end, 32.5x. The 35x
specialty-chemicals cap survives as the absolute ceiling and the bull-case
exit only. This cut Business A fair value from about Rs 4,375 Cr to about
Rs 4,090 Cr.

**B7 shows the deleveraging is spent.** Interest coverage went from 8.93x
to 23.91x. D/E fell from 0.17 to 0.06. Business A carries the cash and sits
near net cash by Year 3. The lender-to-shareholder migration already
happened. It is banked, not a forward return. Role 1 adds a small net-cash
back-add of about Rs 30 Cr and nothing more.

The other six modules did not move the base. B1 passed both businesses. B2
kept the growth premium at +0x: the ROCE gate clears at YES from FY26, but
the Emerging Moat score of 22.5 sits below the 25 gate and bars Pillar 3
independently. B3, B5, and B6 did not apply. The cyclical margin rule
formalized the Business B downward normalization: FY26 is a subsidy-driven
peak, not a base, so normalized PAT holds at Rs 16 to 18 Cr.

## The value versus price statement

Fair value sits about 22% above the market cap, down from about 29% at v2.
Almost all of it is the retained polymer business at a hardened 32.5x. The
Market-Implied read is FAIRLY PRICED, leaning priced-we-are-late. Part B

the stock trades about 16% above the Rs 2,016 entry, wider than v2's 10%
gap. The 25% hurdle is not met at today's price.
