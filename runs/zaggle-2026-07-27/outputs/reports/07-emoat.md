### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown, flagged as not meaningful for this business model

Standard formula: total capex under execution × historical fixed-asset turnover = implied incremental revenue.

- FY26 capex (management-stated, capitalised tech spend): ~₹107cr
- FY25 standalone closing PP&E (fixed asset base): ₹39.68cr (₹396.8mn, AR FY25 p.215, Note 3)
- FY25 standalone revenue: ₹1,302.65cr (Inv. Pres. slide 33)
- Implied fixed-asset turnover: 1,302.65 / 39.68 ≈ 32.8x
- Mechanical output: ₹107cr × 32.8x ≈ ₹3,510cr implied incremental revenue, or ~190% of FY26 standalone revenue (₹1,852.8cr)

**This output is not meaningful and is not used.** Zaggle's PP&E base (₹37–40cr) is a rounding error relative to revenue because the business is asset-light; nearly all "capex" is capitalised software/product development (an opex-like R&D spend, not a capacity-expansion asset), and management's own framing of the ₹107cr spend is efficiency/dev-velocity investment, not volume-capacity addition. Applying a PP&E-turnover multiplier to intangible capex produces an absurd result. Per the no-estimate rule, **capex_embedded_growth_pct is set to 0 / NOT MEANINGFUL** in the handoff block rather than reporting the mechanical (and misleading) 190%+ figure. Downstream stages should not treat Zaggle's capex as a capacity-expansion signal in the manufacturing sense.
