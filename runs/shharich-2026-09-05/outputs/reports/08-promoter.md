## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

**3A RPT history**: Extensive and already substantially quantified by B02.
FY26 related-party remuneration + relatives' salaries + rent totalled
Rs 596.92 lakh, 112.7% of standalone PBT (AR FY26, Note 35.7, pp.124-129),
up from 84.9% in FY25. Purchases from SPL, simultaneously a CCD subscriber,
were Rs 813.73 lakh (FY26), classified arm's-length with no independent
benchmark disclosed (AOC-2, AR FY26 p.69). A related-party loan of
Rs 1,88,28,133 to wholly-owned subsidiary Shakambhari Dyechem Pvt Ltd was
disclosed only in the Corporate Governance Report (p.91) as "since repaid";
the standalone Note 35.23(c) merely admits the loan's existence without a
figure (p.137) — an intra-group WOS loan (lower concern than an external
RPT loan) but poorly cross-referenced within the AR itself. AGM Item 11
seeks a combined Rs 100 crore omnibus RPT ceiling (Rs 50 cr each) for SPL
and Shubhlaxmi Dyetex against a Rs 156 crore market cap; the latter has had
zero transactions to date (AGM Notice, AR FY26 pp.35-42). No guarantees for
private promoter entities and no directly competing promoter business were
found in the material read.

**3B Capital allocation behaviour**: Two consecutive dilutive instruments
— CCDs at Rs 79/share (FY25) and warrants at Rs 176.10/share (25-Aug-2026,
a ~29% discount to CMP Rs 248) — both issued exclusively to promoter-family
individuals, at SEBI ICDR floor-price-compliant levels but with no public
or rights component. Effect: promoter holding path 54.21% (Mar-2025) →
59.25% (Mar-2026) → 64.25% (pre-warrant, Aug-2026) → a prospective 73.75%
(AR FY26 Note 35.1 p.126; AGM Notice Item 16 p.47). Zero dividend in either
FY25 or FY26 despite profits, stated as "to conserve resources" (AR FY26
p.59). No buyback, no stock options to directors (explicitly stated — a
mild positive, no equity-incentive enrichment beyond the direct CCD/warrant
allotments). ❓ Forward-looking flag: AGM Item 15 proposes broadening the
MOA to add heavy-machinery, EPC/infrastructure, and investment-holding
objects (AR FY26 pp.17-19) that mirror several private family entities
(1B); no capital has been redirected yet, but this is worth tracking as a
capital-allocation-direction risk given the warrant proceeds are nominally
earmarked for "dye & dye intermediates... expansion" (AGM Notice p.45).

**3C Promoter share transactions**: ✅ VERIFIED zero pledge — confirmed
both in the AR (Corporate Governance Report, no pledge disclosure) and
independently via Trendlyne's SAST/insider-trading tracker. Per CLAUDE.md,
this is scored as clean, not treated as neutral-to-negative. The rising
promoter stake is entirely capital-raising-driven (CCD/warrant allotment),
not open-market creeping acquisition or timed selling; no evidence of
promoter sales ahead of adverse news was found. Warrant terms (18-month
conversion window, 25% upfront, forfeiture on non-exercise) are standard
ICDR terms.

**3D Minority treatment**: Interested-party vote exclusion is correctly
disclosed for Item 11 (AR FY26 p.42). No proxy-advisory (IiAS/SES/InGovern)
coverage found — expected at this market cap, not itself a finding. AGM
investor grievances: 2 received, 2 resolved, 0 pending (AR FY26, p.84) —
clean but low-volume, limited signal. No SCORES complaints surfaced in web
search.

**3E Auditor relationship**: Kailash Chand Jain & Co, appointed for a
5-year term (35th to 40th AGM, i.e. through 2027), unqualified opinion, no
resignation, no Section 143(12) fraud reporting, stable fee (Rs 6.20 lakh,

financial-control quality (Section 6), not as an "auditor relationship"
red flag proper, since there is no resignation, qualification or fee issue.
