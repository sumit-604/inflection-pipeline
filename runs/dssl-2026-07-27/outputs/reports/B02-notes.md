### D. QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Who is the counterparty/financier for the ~Rs95.50cr lease liability recognised this year, what discount rate was used to measure it, and what is the credit-quality/maturity profile of the offsetting ~Rs82.85cr Lease Receivable book?
2. Please clarify Cybercons Infosec Pvt Ltd's correct consolidation status: an associate under Ind AS 28 (per the Board's Report and AOC-1) or a subsidiary under Ind AS 110 (per CARO Annexure B clause (xxi) and the Consolidated Auditor's Report's "Other Matters" paragraph), and confirm which treatment the FY26 financials will use.
3. What is the amortisation period and business rationale for the new Rs38.20cr deferred contract cost, and which contract(s) does it relate to?
4. Given the 1-2 year trade receivables bucket grew 238.6% YoY while the ECL allowance has been unchanged at Rs0.14cr for two consecutive years, what is the basis for concluding no incremental credit-loss provision is required?
5. Please reconcile the Rs46.55 lakh shortfall (Singapore PTE Ltd) and Rs46.70 lakh surplus (Cybercons) in the Schedule III net-asset roll-forward (Note C-31.10) between FY24 and FY25 closing balances.
6. Please correct or clarify the interest-rate-risk and derivatives narrative in Note 32.9/31.8, which states the Company has no floating-rate borrowings and actively hedges via derivatives, contradicting the company's own tables showing 95.7% floating-rate debt and zero outstanding forward contracts.

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Disclosure integrity failure on a related entity's legal status**: the statutory auditor's own CARO Annexure and Consolidated Auditor's Report call Cybercons Infosec a "subsidiary" in one place and reference it as "an associate" two pages later, while the Board's Report and AOC-1 consistently call it an associate — an unresolved classification question spanning at least six separate disclosures across two audited statement sets.
- **Primary-statement self-reconciliation failure**: Schedule III Note C-31.10's own year-over-year net-asset roll-forward does not add up for either group entity, with two near-identical, opposite-sign, unexplained gaps (~Rs46.5-46.7 lakh) consistent with a value swapped or mis-attributed between entities.
- **Statutory-filing contradiction (AOC-1 vs Schedule III)**: opposite-sign profit/loss figures for both group entities between a statutory annexure and the audited consolidation working papers, with independent evidence (the auditor's "2 subsidiaries" combined profit figure) confirming Schedule III as the basis actually used and AOC-1 as the unreconciled outlier.
- **Frozen credit-loss provisioning against a visibly deteriorating ageing book** — the clearest earnings-management-adjacent signal in the Notes (understated allowance rather than overstated revenue, but with the same balance-sheet-quality effect).
- **Undisclosed material new financing structure** (lease liability/receivable, ~Rs95.5cr) with none of the counterparty, rate, or credit disclosures an investor would need to assess a new de facto lending business embedded in the balance sheet.
- **Self-contradicting boilerplate risk narratives** (floating-rate debt, derivatives/hedging) that, while not changing any reported number, indicate templated disclosure drafting not reconciled to the company's own data — a disclosure-quality/earnings-quality-adjacent concern about the reliability of qualitative notes generally.

No red flags were found in: inventory (clean, efficiency improved), trade payables (funding discipline, MSME growth still small), deferred tax (clean reconciliation), CSR (fully spent, transparently disclosed related-party CSR), or ESOP/share capital (de minimis, clean).

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal concerning accounting practices. Key concern: an unresolved subsidiary-vs-associate classification for Cybercons Infosec that the company's own auditor cannot even state consistently within a single audit report, compounded by a self-reconciliation failure in the statutory Schedule III table and a frozen ECL against a tripling 1-2 year receivables bucket. Key strength: clean, conservative depreciation and tax reconciliation, disciplined trade payables and inventory management, and immaterial RPT-to-revenue ratio. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
