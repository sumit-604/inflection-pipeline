## OPERATOR OVERRIDES

### Override 1 — Cash conversion: STRUCTURAL to GROWTH-INDUCED

- **Draft determination:** STRUCTURAL. Cash multiplier 0.65x with no growth offset, forward cash verdict DECLINING with catalyst NONE, Kernex cap engaged, composite +1.
- **Operator ruling:** GROWTH-INDUCED. Cash multiplier 0.80x, forward cash verdict STAGNANT, Kernex cap lifted, composite +2.
- **Operator's stated reasoning (operator's words):** "the cash problem is, I think, growth-induced, so we need to see that, growth or structural."
- **What the review surfaced that supported the change:** the source shows debtor days stable to improving (about 217 to 230 across FY23 to FY25, then 195 in FY26), not deteriorating, which is the growth-induced signature of volume rising while the collection period holds. FY23 and FY24 both had positive operating cash flow, so conversion worked before the growth surge and the negative years line up with the acceleration. The buyers are government and PSU (Indian Railways, ICF), the company's own framing is that receivable days are elevated because the business is government and PSU oriented, and the over six month bucket carries zero doubtful debt provision because it is treated as collectible. The "if growth stopped tomorrow" test passes: on a roughly 200 day collectible cycle, moderating growth to 20 to 25% turns operating cash flow positive.
- **The caveat held on the record:** the over six month receivables share rose from 15.95% to 17.85% to 23.38% (FY23 to FY25, Rs 2,983.05 lakh of Rs 12,760.04 lakh at FY25, verified in Prospectus Annexure XIX). Growth volume alone does not age receivables past six months, so the underlying cycle is structurally long even though the negative operating cash flow is growth-induced.
- **Falsification metric attached (required for a growth-induced ruling):** if H1 FY27 standalone operating cash flow is still negative AND the over six month receivables share is still rising above 23.4% AND debtor days tick back up, the determination reverts to STRUCTURAL, the cash multiplier returns to 0.65x, and the Kernex cap comes back at the next refresh. If operating cash flow turns positive, or debtor days hold near 195 to 200 while the ageing stabilises, growth-induced is confirmed.
- **Status:** operator signed off by directing the record to be written. No verbatim rewording of the reasoning was given, so the operator's words above are quoted as stated.

## FINAL FTTCP VERDICT (in the operator's own words)

The operator did not articulate a separate one line position verdict. Read plainly, the operator's call is that this is a growth-induced cash gap to be confirmed by watching the next print, not a structural failure to condemn now. The mechanical position that follows from that ruling, recorded from the scorecard as adjusted by the override, is DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID, composite +2 of 8, Kernex cap lifted, decision to wait for the H1 FY27 operating cash flow print before moving further.
