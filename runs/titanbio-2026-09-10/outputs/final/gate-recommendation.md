### FLAG-CASH: STRUCTURAL

Determination: STRUCTURAL.

The observation that separates the two readings: working capital days peaked at
150.05 in FY25, the year revenue fell 5.7 percent, and sat at 127.67 in FY26, the year
revenue grew 31.8 percent. Heavy working capital is present when growth is absent, so
the level is a feature of the business, not a consequence of growth. The corrected
four year series is 130.85 (FY23), 135.39 (FY24), 150.05 (FY25), 127.67 (FY26), a net
decrease of 3.18 days over four years (B01, corrected this run). The cash conversion
cycle ran 220 days in FY26 and inventory 212 days.

This is a statement about the working capital LEVEL, not about cash conversion
quality. Cash conversion at the profit to cash level is sound: operating cash flow
exceeded profit in both FY26 and FY25, operating cash flow to operating profit has run
85 to 103 percent for four years, and free cash flow grew from Rs 18.11 cr in FY23 to
Rs 22.99 cr in FY26.

Cited evidence behind the flag:

- Finished goods inventory up 53.2 percent against revenue up 31.8 percent (AR FY26
  Note 8, p.129).
- First ever expected credit loss provision of Rs 36.26 lakh, nil in FY25 and FY24 (AR
  FY26 Note 9, p.130).
- Receivables more than six months past due rose from 7.6 percent to 8.4 percent of
  gross receivables (Rs 142.81 lakh of Rs 1,881.63 lakh FY25, Rs 194.44 lakh of Rs
  2,319.12 lakh FY26).
- Total receivables grew 21.3 percent against revenue growth of 31.8 percent, which is
  the positive inside the same note.
- MSME trade payables up 291 percent, Rs 55.03 lakh to Rs 215.11 lakh, all technically
  not due (AR FY26 Note 20, p.135).
- Customer advances up 149 percent, Rs 246.62 lakh to Rs 615.12 lakh (AR FY26 Note 22,
  p.135).

## Contradicted claims from the peer stage

1. **"Fermentation ingredient demand is re-accelerating industry wide in FY26 and
   FY27" is CONTRADICTED.** Advanced Enzyme Q1 FY27 call, 12-Aug-2026, Mukund Kabra:
   "a softer, more muted start to the fiscal year". Vidhi Q4 FY26 call, 14-May-2026,
   Bipin Manek: "a noticeable slowdown in demand across several international
   markets". Advanced Enzyme year on year growth ran 26, 2, 22 and 2 percent across

   demand narrative of its own, so its FY26 rebound has no industry corroboration.

2. **"Titan's non disclosure of capacity utilisation and capex logic is industry

   the commissioning answer and the rupee figures came from two different speakers in

   three years.

## Monitorables

Eight items, deduplicated across stages 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 9.

1. **Related party share of the raw material bill.** Look at Note 41 of the FY27
   annual report. The audited FY26 figure is 39.1 percent of cost of materials
   consumed, Phoenix Bio Sciences alone 26.0 percent. If it rises again with no arm's
   length benchmark printed, the dependency is still widening and the promoter caution
   hardens.

2. **Cost of materials consumed as a share of revenue, every quarter.** It was 48.1
   percent in FY26 against 48.3 percent in FY25. The peer set shows this ratio falling
   when margin expands on better mix. A flat ratio with a rising margin points to
   volume or accounting instead, which is the core of the transition question.

3. **Export revenue in the FY27 annual report note, against Rs 80.33 cr in FY26.**
   Cross check it monthly against DGCIS HS code export data for peptones, culture
   media and collagen. Export growth is the only live emerging moat category and the
   company explains none of it.

4. **The health supplement sentence in the FY27 Directors' Report Future Plans.** A
   fourth identical repeat with no product named ends the claim. A named product with
   a date starts a different conversation.

5. **Net plant additions against net investment additions in the FY27 cash flow.** FY26
   put Rs 25.16 cr of a Rs 32.55 cr audited outflow into a quoted debt portfolio whose
   return fell from 7.04 percent to 3.64 percent. This tests capital allocation and the
   capacity building claim in one line.

6. **Customer advances converting to billed revenue in the H1 FY27 results.** Rs 615.12
   lakh sat as advances at 31-Mar-2026, up 149 percent. If it does not convert, the
   forward order read was revenue optics.

7. **Finished goods inventory growth against revenue growth, plus the expected credit
   loss line, in the FY27 filings.** FY26 ran 53.2 percent against 31.8 percent and
   booked the first ever provision of Rs 36.26 lakh. A repeat makes it a pattern rather
   than a ramp.

8. **Advanced Enzyme and Vidhi quarterly prints.** They are the only current
   management voice on this industry in the corpus and they currently contradict a
   demand re-acceleration. If they turn, Titan's rebound gains an industry
   explanation it does not have today.

## Falsification line

The single next quarter print that would do the most damage: Q2 FY27 revenue growth
below 8 percent year on year in the November 2026 filing, with cost of materials
consumed holding at or above 48 percent of revenue. That combination says the FY26
and Q1 FY27 acceleration was a restock lapping a weak base, not a mix climb, and it
takes the one live emerging moat category with it.
