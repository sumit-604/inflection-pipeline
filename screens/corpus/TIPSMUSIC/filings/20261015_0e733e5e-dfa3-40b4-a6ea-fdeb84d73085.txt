Commenting on the Results:
Mr. Kumar Taurani – Chairman & Managing Director said, “In Q2 FY27, revenue increased 35%
YoY to ₹120.1 crore, while PAT grew 23% YoY to ₹65.5 crore. Our investment in content increased by 97%
compared to Q2 FY26. During the quarter, we continued to see strong engagement across our new release as
well as our catalogue content, supported by both our digital and non-digital segments. We have reported a strong
H1 FY27 results and are quite confident to surpass our annual guidance of 20% growth. During the quarter,
the Company successfully completed a share buyback of ₹44.44 crore and has also announced interim dividend
of ₹6.50 per share reflecting our continued focus on delivering value to shareholders.”
Highlights for Q2 FY7: “During Q2 FY27, we strengthened our content portfolio with the release of 73
songs, comprising 40 film songs and 33 non-film songs. Our new releases performed well across platforms, with
"Ishqnama" crossing 100 million views on YouTube and "Aankhon Se Tune" surpassing 66 million views.
We also witnessed sustained traction in earlier releases, with "Chunari Chunari Let's Go" crossing 100 million
views, while the female version of "Tere Paas Mein" from "Main Vaapas Aunga" continued to perform
strongly on Spotify. Our catalogue remained a key driver of engagement, with "Tera Naam Doon" crossing 1
billion views on Instagram. Reflecting the growing appeal of our content, our cumulative YouTube subscriber
base increased to 164.5 million and total views exceeded 54.3 billion.”

About TIPS Music Ltd:
Founded in 1988 by Taurani Brothers, TIPS Music Ltd. stands as one of India's leading publicly-listed music
company. The company built its reputation through legendary film soundtracks of the 1990s including Khalnayak,
Soldier, Coolie No.1, Rangeela, Pardes, and Taal, while continuing its success story with contemporary hits like
Raaz, the Race franchise, Ramaiya Vastavaiya, Ajab Prem Ki Ghazab Kahani, regional blockbusters Ponniyin
Selvan 1 & 2, and recent chart-toppers including Crew, HanuMan, and the popular Saunkan Saunkne series that
showcases TIPS' expansion into regional cinema.
The label has been home to India's most celebrated artists across generations, from legendary voices like Alka
Yagnik, Kumar Sanu, Udit Narayan, and Sonu Nigam to today's superstars including A.R. Rahman, Diljit
Dosanjh, Badshah, Arijit Singh, B Praak and Aditya Rikhari. With over 38,000 "Must-Have Hits" in its catalogue
spanning multiple languages and genres, serving as an essential partner for digital platforms, streaming services, and
broadcasters while maintaining its commitment and focus to deliver best music content.
