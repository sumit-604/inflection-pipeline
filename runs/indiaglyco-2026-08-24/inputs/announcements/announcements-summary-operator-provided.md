## 1. Group-level financial / operating performance
Q4 FY26 & FY26 (Investor Presentation 18 May 2026; Q4 concall 22 May 2026):
- FY26 net revenue Rs 4,211 Cr (+11.8% YoY); EBITDA Rs 654 Cr (+24.5% YoY);
  PAT Rs 293 Cr (+26.8% YoY); EBITDA margin 15.5% (+162 bps).
- Q4 FY26 net revenue Rs 976 Cr (+13.1% YoY); EBITDA Rs 167 Cr (+13.3% YoY);
  PAT Rs 87 Cr (+35.7% YoY).
- Multi-year margin upgrade: EBITDA margin 9.6% (FY22) to 15.5% (FY26).
- FY26 cash from operations Rs 769 Cr (+112% YoY).
- Prepaid ~Rs 804 Cr debt in Q4 FY26, funded by Rs 467 Cr preferential equity
  (Nov 2025) plus internal accruals.
- Q4 FY26 finance cost Rs 26 Cr (vs Rs 45 Cr Q4 FY25).
- Major capex cycle largely complete: grain distillery expansions at Kashipur
  and Gorakhpur; New Specialty Unit (NSU) Phase I. CARE Mar 2026 and mgmt: no
  major new capex medium-term; focus on sweating assets and deleveraging.

## 2. Segment operational updates
### 2.1 Portable Spirits (country liquor + IMFL)
- FY26 PS revenue Rs 1,331 Cr (+14.4% YoY); EBIT Rs 285 Cr (+11.1%); margin 21.4%.
- Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 306 Cr (+7.9%); EBIT Rs 68 Cr; margin 22.1%.
- Leadership in country liquor (IMIL) in UP and Uttarakhand; "Bunty Bubbly"
  top-selling brand in segment. Entry into CSD/Defence and paramilitary
  channels. Premiumisation: single malt (Amrut partnership), flavored vodkas,
  new whiskies. IMFL ~25% of PS volume and rising.
- Q1 FY27 PS net revenue Rs 361 Cr (+5% YoY); EBIT Rs 75 Cr (+4%); margin 20.8%.
  IGL Spirits pro-forma: IMFL 1.4m cases (+55% YoY vol, +26% rev), ~0.5m cases
  Prestige-and-above (roughly doubled YoY); Non-IMFL 7.5m cases (+6%), revenue
  flat (~Rs 279 Cr). New ENA plant (grain+molasses) commissioning for captive
  needs. Aspirational guidance: FY27 IGL Spirits EBITDA "in excess of Rs 500 Cr";
  medium term Rs 1,000 Cr EBITDA in 4-5 years; top-5 alco-bev; debt-free from FY28.

### 2.2 Bio-Fuel (ethanol)
- FY26 revenue Rs 1,470 Cr (+40.9%); EBIT Rs 115 Cr (+103.3%); margin 7.8%.
- Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 305 Cr (+11.6%); EBIT Rs 30 Cr (+90%); margin 9.9%.
- India hit 20% blending ahead of 2030 timeline; strong OMC offtake; grain
  prices softened, DDGS realisations strong. Kashipur 600 KLPD grain, Gorakhpur
  500 KLPD, near optimum utilisation.
- Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 323 Cr (-7% YoY, pricing-driven); EBIT Rs 27 Cr (+19%);
  margin 8.4%. Excess national ethanol capacity; govt evaluating 21-22% blending
  and limited flex-fuel; utilisation only ~65-75%, no major new capex.

### 2.3 Bio-based Specialities & Performance Chemicals (BSPC)
- FY26 revenue Rs 1,203 Cr (-10.3%); EBIT Rs 141 Cr (+12.4%); margin 11.7%.
- Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 301 Cr (+18.8%); EBIT Rs 37 Cr (+37.4%); margin 12.2%.
- Exited low-margin volumes (glycol ethers, gases); focus on bio-glycols,
  green solvents, performance chemicals (up ~40% in FY26). Improved EO/MEG
  economics vs petro-MEG; Clariant JV benefited.
- Q1 FY27 Chemicals net revenue Rs 363 Cr (+21%); EBIT Rs 31 Cr (-4%);
  margin 8.6% (vs 10.9%). Clariant JV profit share to IGL Rs 21 Cr (vs Rs 19 Cr).
  Aspiration: Chemicals EBITDA >Rs 200 Cr FY27 (incl JV dividend); ~Rs 400 Cr
  EBITDA and ~Rs 2,500 Cr revenue in 4-5 years.

### 2.4 Ennature Bio Pharma (plant APIs, nutraceuticals, nicotine)
- FY26 revenue Rs 208 Cr (vs Rs 219 Cr); EBIT Rs 7 Cr (vs Rs 21 Cr); margin
  3.5% (vs 9.5%) on low nicotine sales and high Gloriosa seed cost.
- Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 63 Cr (+23.2%); EBIT Rs 3 Cr; margin 4.7%.
- Q1 FY27 best-ever: revenue Rs 83 Cr (+65%); EBIT Rs 7 Cr (vs Rs 1 Cr);
  margin 8.7% (vs 2.4%). Thiocolchicoside strong; Kashipur nicotine crude
  processing started Apr 2026 (~6 MT/month). Aspiration: FY27 EBITDA >Rs 50 Cr;
  Rs 130-150 Cr in 4-5 years.

## 3. Structural change — THE DEMERGER (load-bearing for this run)
- 24 Mar 2026: equity shareholders and unsecured creditors approved the Scheme
  of Arrangement (99.999%+ in favour).
- 10 Apr 2026: NCLT Allahabad admitted second-motion petition.
- 17 Jul 2026: NCLT Allahabad sanctioned the Scheme (full order shared 20 Jul).
- 20 Aug 2026: certified true copy of NCLT order received.
- 21 Aug 2026: Board fixed **Effective Date 1 Sep 2026** and **Record Date
  2 Sep 2026**. **Appointed Date 1 Apr 2026.**
- Split into three entities:
  - Residual **India Glycols Ltd** keeps BSPC (glycols/specialty chemicals) + gases.
  - **IGL Spirits Ltd (ISL)**: spirits + bio-fuel undertaking. Swap **1 ISL
    (Rs 5) for every 1 IGL share.**
  - **Ennature Bio Pharma Ltd (EBL)**: bio-pharma undertaking. Swap **1 EBL
    (Rs 5) for every 3 IGL shares.**
  - ISL and EBL to list on BSE and NSE subject to approvals.
- New boards installed 21 Aug 2026 (EBL: Pragya Bhartia Barwale MD; U.S. Bhartia
  and others; 4 independents. ISL: U.S. Bhartia Chairman & MD; independents
  incl. Vimal Bhandari.)
