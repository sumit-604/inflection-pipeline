# FTTCP Recommendation (FINAL): Entero Healthcare Solutions Ltd (ENTERO)

This is the final recommendation. It supersedes gate-recommendation.md, which stands as the phase 1 evidence gate record. Verdict on the evidence is PROCEED WITH CAVEATS: three flags are active (FLAG-PROMOTER, FLAG-CASH, FLAG-GATE0) and the INDETERMINATE cash determination caps the gate at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS rather than the PROCEED WITH FLAGS the two active flags would otherwise carry. The verdict judges the evidence, not the price. The separate investment decision from the valuation is AVOID.

## Investment decision

- DECISION: AVOID (on valuation) at CMP Rs1,273. Market cap Rs5,537 Cr, 4.35 Cr shares.
- Business read: composite plus 5 of 8 on the FTTCP scan, a BUY candidate transition (revenue FIRING, margin STARTING, cash STARTING but INDETERMINATE, ROCE RECOVERING). The price is not there.
- Tier A, 25% hurdle. Tier B was barred by the promoter CONCERN, so no lower hurdle applies.
- Destination (exit) PE: 15.0x flat on BOTH tracks (operator override, reason quoted "high-growth company"), applied to one year forward owners' EPS. The pillar-derived tracks were 13.4x additive and 10.2x RRM; both are reported and not used. 15.0x sits below the 25x sector cap, so no breach.
- Earnings basis: forward owners' PAT (post-minority). FY26 owners' EPS Rs26.45, trailing 48.1x at CMP. Minority share 21% in FY26, guided to persist at 25 to 27% into FY27.
- Fair value (exit, both tracks identical): bear Rs857, base Rs1,112, bull Rs1,422. Base sits below CMP.
- Hurdle Ratio: base 0.74, bull 0.90, threshold 1.953. Verdict STOP. Even bull earnings do not clear a 25% price CAGR, because the mandated de-rate from 48.1x trailing to 15.0x forward (ratio 0.31) needs roughly an 84% EPS CAGR to overcome, which no case delivers.
- Probability-weighted 3 year CAGR at CMP: minus 4.3% (grade B weights 25/50/25).
- Upside/downside: 0.36x (bull plus 11.7% against bear minus 32.7%), target 2x, fails.
- Entry range Rs506 to Rs569. Margin of safety price Rs455. CMP is 2.2x the top of the zone.
- Position size: None. Re-open to WATCHLIST only if CMP enters the zone AND cash and margin durability print (see monitorables). While the promoter CONCERN cap binds, the ceiling stays WATCHLIST even in the zone, never BUY.

MARKET-UNLIKELY ZONE. Reaches entry only via a thesis-relevant shock or a broad de-rating; classify DEEP WATCH, not actionable WATCHLIST, or route to the trading book. ### FLAG-CASH (INDETERMINATE)

Determination INDETERMINATE, leaning growth-induced. The FY26 cash inflection is real but the granular evidence that would confirm it as GROWTH-INDUCED was refused or not readable. Per rule this caps the evidence gate at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS; no clean growth offset is taken in the valuation.

Growth-induced lean: FY26 consolidated OCF turned positive at plus Rs96.2 Cr, first positive year in seven, against negative Rs76.9 Cr in FY25 (B01 block_b_trend; B12a verified 962 Mn, FY26 consolidated cash flow p.167); NWC days improved to 59 in Q4 FY26 from 70 at FY25 close (B05 promise_delivery; B07 catalysts_12m); the FY25 receivables build (+34.9% YoY) and inventory build (+56.7% YoY) were substantially acquisition led (B02 rank 8, 9).

Guard holding it INDETERMINATE: debtor ageing beyond six months refused on a direct analyst request on the Q3 FY26 call (B05 red_flags); the FY25 MD&A claim that OCF turned positive in H2 FY25 is contradicted by the audited full-year negative and YoY-worsening consolidated figure, no H1/H2 split disclosed (B02 rank 1, p.167/p.15; B03 phase 6); receivables days rose 59 to 67 while payables stretched to ~95 days, so the FY26 turn leans on a payable stretch (B10 working_capital table); doubtful receivables +59.9% YoY, related-party receivables +105% (B02 receivables_trend).

Rating agency working capital commentary: NOT FOUND. The credit rating rationale PDF is image-only with no text layer and was not OCR-read this run, so no verbatim rating quote is available to cite (B10 unresolved; B00 rating input).

Missing evidence to close the determination, and where to obtain it: the receivables ageing schedule beyond six months and the doubtful-receivables trend (FY26 annual report trade-receivables ageing note on BSE, or a direct concall answer); the credit rating rationale working-capital commentary (India Ratings / rating agency machine-readable rationale, to be OCR-read); the H1 versus H2 operating cash flow split for FY25 and FY26 (half-year and full-year cash flow statements on BSE). Because the determination is INDETERMINATE, no single quarterly cash falsifier attaches here; the cash concern is carried into the monitorables and the falsification line.

### SHARED CATALYST

Active. The M&A pause drives BOTH Pillar 1 ROCE recovery AND part of the Pillar 3 growth/margin premium; it is a single point of failure. The pause reversed once inside one quarter (Q2 FY26 "spend upwards of Rs400 Cr" to Q3 FY26 "slow down, integrate"), deals still closed during it, and the SOM growth path (33.8% implied) cannot be collected without resuming M&A, which reloads goodwill and borrowings (B11 flags; B15 section 5). Role 3 devil's advocate stress-tested this and the AVOID survived.

## Contradicted and unsupported claims (priority monitoring)

The peer stage logged zero formally contradicted claims. The load-bearing items are the management self-contradiction and the verifier nuances, each with its source anchor:
- MD&A H2 FY25 OCF-positive claim, contradicted by the audited full-year negative consolidated OCF with no H1/H2 split (FY26 consolidated cash flow p.167; CEO Message p.15). The one genuine management-versus-financials contradiction on the record.
- Acquisition 5-7x EV/EBITDA discipline rests on adjusted / add-back EBITDA, not reported; Bioaide reported a ~1.5% FY24 EBITDA margin normalised via salary add-backs (Q2 FY26 call p.9-10). Treat the discipline claim as unverified on a reported basis.
- Q4 FY26 guidance-delivery quarter (4.5% EBITDA margin, 42.6% growth) flattered by a preponed government-project sale in a partially owned subsidiary; the challenge on whether 4.5% is the right number was deflected (Q4 FY26 call p.4-5, 11-12).
- Redington Jun 2026 SSG investor day discloses services gross margin above 20% versus a 5.5-6% core and a 60% margin uplift, directly relevant to Entero's agency margin premium; the peer stage marked it cited-only and left the agency-margin claim unverifiable using RPTech alone (Redington Jun 2026 transcript p.4-5, 14). This datapoint supports, not contradicts, the agency logic and was under-used.
- IPM acceleration to ~12% has zero peer corroboration; only the 7-8% baseline is loosely confirmed by MedPlus. The MedTech 10% plus EBITDA margin claim is unverifiable and the available comparators run below Entero's own 4%.
- Correction to a pipeline overstatement: the peer stage said Entero never mentions the new Labour Code cost risk; Entero recognised a ~Rs6.1 Cr labour-code exceptional charge on the Q3 FY26 call (Feb 2026 call p.2-3, 6). That disclosure gap is not supported.

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated, act on these)

1. Net operating working capital days and receivables days, Q1 and Q2 FY27. Green NWC at or below 60, red above 65; red also if receivables days rise above 70. In the quarterly investor deck and concall. Tests whether the FY26 cash inflection holds or FLAG-CASH is structural.
2. Operating cash flow to EBITDA against the 50% FY27 guide. Red below 50%, hard red below 40%. In the quarterly and annual cash flow statement. Tests whether the first positive cash year sustains or reverses as the payable stretch normalises.
3. Receivables ageing beyond six months and the doubtful-receivables trend, the exact item management refused in December 2025. In the annual report trade-receivables ageing note on BSE, or a direct concall answer. Tests receivables quality behind the cash flag.
4. Core EBITDA margin against the 5.0% FY27 guide, and owners' EPS against the base ladder (FY27 at least Rs41.05). Red margin below 4.6% for a full quarter, red NCI above 27%. In the quarterly P&L and the minority line. Tests the margin engine and the minority leakage that both feed owners' EPS.
5. Organic revenue growth as a multiple of the pharma market, currently ~1.4x, historically 1.7 to 1.9x, red approaching 1.0x. In the concall organic-growth disclosure. Tests the core growth engine management deflected on twice.
6. Whether the 5-7x EV/EBITDA acquisition multiple is quoted on reported or add-back EBITDA in future deals, and whether a deal above Rs100 Cr revenue is announced during the stated pause. In deal announcements and the concall. Tests the M&A discipline claim and the SHARED CATALYST.
7. Goodwill share of equity (43% FY26) and any impairment across the 43 acquired units; CARO clause xi remediation at the three flagged subsidiaries. Red on any impairment charge, a fourth CARO xi subsidiary, or ADT-4 quantum surfacing. In the balance sheet and the auditor annexure / KAM. Tests roll-up integration quality and the promoter concern.
8. Price re-engagement. Green only when CMP enters Rs506 to Rs569 with the base ladder intact and the pause holding. Market price. Tests the sole condition under which the AVOID re-opens to WATCHLIST.

## Falsification line

The single most damaging next-quarter print: net operating working capital days back above 65 in Q1 or Q2 FY27 with operating cash flow to EBITDA below 50%. That combination recasts the FY26 cash turn as a one-year working-capital swing rather than a durable inflection and pushes FLAG-CASH from INDETERMINATE toward STRUCTURAL.
