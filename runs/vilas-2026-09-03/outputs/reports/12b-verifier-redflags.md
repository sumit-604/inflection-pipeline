### Guidance walkbacks (rupee track)

   (Nov-2025, Nilesh Patel, p.19) -> FY26 revenue ~30% growth on Rs 362 cr = ~Rs 470 cr
   (May-2026, opening, p.4). MISS.
2. FY27 Rs 1,000 cr at CRGO Rs 270-280 (May-2025, p.9,22) -> Rs 750-800 at CRGO Rs 210
   (May-2026, Akash Jhaveri exchange, p.14-15). Rs 1,000 cr now conditional on price rise or
   full CTC ramp. REVISED DOWN ~22-25%.
3. FY26 volume 24,000 MT (May-2025, Dhavan Shah exchange, p.15) -> 19,500 MT actual
   (May-2026, Kushal Shah exchange, p.9). ~19% MISS. Note the spin: May-2026 opening (p.3-4)
   calls the 64% volume growth "precisely in line with our target" while the 24,000 MT
   number was itself missed.
4. Nanocrystalline Rs 50 cr FY26 (May-2025, p.5,16) -> Rs 18-20 cr FY27 at 20-25% PAT
   (May-2026, Natasha/Nilesh, p.20-21). ~60-64% CUT. Additionally the stated FULL-CAPACITY
   potential collapsed from "capable of Rs 150 crores turnover" (May-2025, p.16) to
   "full capacity Rs 25-27 crores" on 240 MTPA (May-2026, p.20) — a ~5-6x reduction.
5. Radiator commercial by July-2025 (May-2025, p.4) -> power-connection delay (Nov-2025, p.4)
   -> commercial sales began April-2026 (May-2026, Krupa Desai exchange, p.8). ~9-month slip;
   revenue target Rs 35-36 cr FY26 (May-2025, p.17) -> Rs 25 cr FY27 (May-2026, p.8).
6. Copper commercial "by May 2026" (Nov-2025, Hardik Gandhi exchange, p.10) -> trial end-Sept-2026,
   commercial Oct, revenue H2 FY27 (May-2026, opening + Sidhaant exchange, p.3,16). SLIP.

### Margin / earnings quality
9. Blended EBITDA 13-14% guide (May-2025, p.22) -> FY26 EBITDA 11.17% vs restated 12.68% FY25
   (May-2026, opening, p.5). MISS.
10. Q4 FY26 margin deterioration — took repeated pressure from Kushal Shah and Prolin Nandu
    before management conceded a Q4-specific drop; management would only say "1% or 2% here
    and there" and never cleanly confirmed the Q4 exit margin (May-2026, p.9-11).
11. MARGIN NORMALIZATION ADMISSION: management volunteered that the FY25-type 14% EBITDA
    "comes once in 4 years or once in 3 years" and that "normal margin 10-11%... net margin
    7% to 8% PAT" (May-2026, Prolin Nandu exchange, Nilesh Patel, p.10-11). Foreshadowed in
    May-2025 when Prolin Nandu challenged the 14% as above the stated 11-13% range and Nilesh
    conceded "2%, 3% more margin which we have done in this financial year" (p.9-10). This
    recalibrates the entire earnings base and is thesis-critical for any exit multiple.
12. OTHER-INCOME QUALITY OF EARNINGS: other income runs ~15-20% of total profitability
    (May-2026, Kushal Shah exchange, p.23), and in May-2025 was Rs 9 cr expected to fall to
    Rs 4-5 cr (Rajesh Jain exchange, p.20-21). PAT margin (8.6%) is flattered by treasury/FD
    income; IPO funds are ~95% utilized. Undisclosed as a risk.
13. Circular / shifting explanation of how CRGO price flows to margin — different every call
    (Nov-2025 p.18-19; May-2026 Yash Rathi exchange, p.17-18: "CRGO business typically there
    are no such kind of calculation").
14. Capex/CWIP reconciliation gap: management said Rs 50 cr spent to 31-Mar; Purva Shah
    pointed to filed PFI of Rs 40.81 cr fixed-asset purchase and ~Rs 40 cr CWIP — a Rs 10 cr
    gap, explained on-call as IPO/corporate expense (May-2025, p.14). Loose capex disclosure.
15. Margin-basis drift: FY25 EBITDA reported 14.8% incl other income (May-2025, p.4), H1 FY26
    quoted 13.6% excl other income (Nov-2025, p.6), FY25 restated to 12.68% under Ind AS
    (May-2026, p.5). Comparability across calls is muddied by basis changes.

### Demand / customer health
16. Customer-weakness deflection pattern: Nov-2025 Garvit Goyal asked about a Shilchar
    export/US-tariff slowdown; management deflected ("see its balance sheet... no drop", p.7-8).
    May-2026 Hardik Gandhi noted Voltamp and Shilchar (VTL's #1 and #3 customers, p.7 Nov-2025)
    posted weak Q2 with margins under pressure; management again deflected to firm-price-order
    mechanics, insisting "it's not about overcapacity" (p.21). Two-quarter pattern of
    downplaying a demand-side signal from named top customers.
17. Amorphous core demand skepticism: a sector-expert investor (Miten Shah, 20 yrs EPC) said
    channel checks show "no requirement of Amorphous Core from any customers" in India or
    overseas; management claimed a full order book for 2-3 months (May-2025, p.18-19). Minor
    product line, but an unverified demand claim challenged by a knowledgeable participant.

### Governance / capital allocation
18. HV BUSHING 25% RELATED-PARTY STRUCTURE: a NEW entity for 12-400 kV bushings in which Vilas
    holds only 25% while the promoter holds 75% (May-2026, opening, p.3). It leverages Vilas's
    marquee customer base, a rented group-company building, and Mr. Ramachandran (Yash
    Highvoltage founder) as CEO (p.14). Nilesh: "I didn't wanted to risk any money of Vilas
    Transcore, so I we put up our own fund," with a possible future merger into Vilas "at
    certain level" (p.14-15). Downside sits with the promoter but upside and eventual merger
    terms are unspecified — a promoter-favorable structure that leans on Vilas's ecosystem.
19. New-venture proliferation vs execution: bushing JV announced while radiator (~9-mo slip),
    nanocrystalline (target cut, negligible sales) and copper (pre-commercial) are all still
    ramping or behind first-stated dates (May-2026, p.3).
20. Atlas Transformers group related-party revenue disclosed once at 7-8% (May-2025, Vishal
    Dudhwala exchange, p.13), never revisited.

### Peer contradictions (verified against B06 corpus)

    — contradicted by Jay Bee Laminations naming NLMK among mills that "faced pressure".
22. Government anti-dumping/safeguard duty on CRGO "it seems it will come" (May-2026, p.5-6)
    — contradicted by Jay Bee's explicit "there is no safeguard duty on CRGO".
