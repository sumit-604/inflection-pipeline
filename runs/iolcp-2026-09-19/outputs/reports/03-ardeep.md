### 2.0.1 Extension on rank 3 (capex) — a correction to the framing, not the numbers
The three B02 numbers (gross additions -71.4%, CWIP capitalised -73.6%,
commitments +172.8%) are all exactly right, but read in isolation they
imply capex spending itself collapsed. The cash flow statement says
otherwise: **cash "Purchase of property, plant and equipment including
intangible assets and CWIP" was Rs 172.99 Cr in FY26 vs Rs 213.55 Cr in
FY25 — down only 19.0%**, not 71%. And **CWIP additions (new money going
into assets under construction) were Rs 147.09 Cr in FY26 vs Rs 202.86 Cr
in FY25 — down 27.5%**, still a substantial ongoing spend (cash flow
p.136; Note 3.2 p.147). What actually fell sharply is the **pace of moving
assets from "under construction" to "completed and in the operating
block"** (the capitalisation line, -73.6%) and the **gross additions to
the already-operating block** (-71.4%) — both consistent with a capex
programme that is early-stage (site prep, long-lead equipment, not yet
commissioned) rather than one that has stalled. This reading is also
consistent with company memory's "Rs 150 Cr spent" figure against the
Rs 500 Cr programme, and with the FY26 closing CWIP balance of Rs 93.61 Cr
(Note 3.2 p.147). **This does not clear FLAG-CAPEX** — the genuine,
unresolved question is funding, not spend: **zero term debt, gearing ratio
"N.A." (net cash, Note 42 p.174-175), and only Rs 69.35 Cr of booked
capital commitments** against a guided Rs 500 Cr + Rs 1,200-1,400 Cr
programme is a real gap between what management has committed to on paper
inside this AR and what it has told the market on calls. It does mean the
flag should be framed as **"funding source unproven," not "capex has
stopped."**
