### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Historical fixed-asset turnover (standalone Revenue / Net PPE, restated, pre-ramp years to avoid the FY26 distortion of a mid-construction balance sheet):

- FY21: 1,205.1 / 1,140.6 = 1.06x
- FY22: 1,944.7 / 1,374.3 = 1.42x
- FY23: 2,516.8 / 2,096.8 = 1.20x
- FY24: 3,407.7 / 2,545.4 = 1.34x
- FY25: 4,529.3 / 4,010.2 = 1.13x
(Source for all: Inv. Pres. slide 31, Restated Standalone Balance Sheet / slide 29-30 revenue)

Average FY21-FY25 fixed-asset turnover = (1.06+1.42+1.20+1.34+1.13) / 5 = **1.23x**

Total capex under execution = ₹700cr QIP programme (AR p.11; Q3 FY26 call p.9, "INR700 crores... asset turn... 1.7x, 1.8x").

Implied incremental revenue = ₹700cr × 1.23x ≈ **₹861cr**

As % of FY26 standalone revenue (₹590.4cr, Inv. Pres. slide 6): 861 / 590.4 ≈ **145.8%, rounded to 146%**

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 146**

Caution flag: this is materially more conservative than management's own framing. Management guides an incremental asset turn of 1.7-1.8x (Nov 2025 call p.16-17) to 2x (Feb 2026 call p.8), which would imply ₹1,190-1,400cr of incremental revenue (~200-235% of FY26 revenue) — using management's own multiplier rather than the historical actual would roughly double this figure. Per pipeline rule, the historical actual (1.23x) is used, not the management-asserted forward multiplier (🎙️), and the gap between the two is itself worth monitoring: if realized asset turn tracks closer to the historical 1.2x than management's 1.7-2.0x guide, the revenue ramp implied by the ₹700cr QIP spend is roughly half of what management's own commentary suggests.
