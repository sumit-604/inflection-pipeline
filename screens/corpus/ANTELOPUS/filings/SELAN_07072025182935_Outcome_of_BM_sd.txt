Dear Sir, 
This is in continuation to our previous intimations dated July 03, 2025 and other communications regarding the 
Composite Scheme of Arrangement between Antelopus Energy Private Limited (“Transferor Company”) and Selan 
Exploration Technology Limited (“Transferee Company” or “Company”), along with their respective shareholders 
and creditors, under Sections 230 to 232, read with Sections 66 and 52, and other applicable provisions of the 
Companies Act, 2013 (“Scheme”) sanctioned by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench 
(“NCLT”) vide order dated June 10, 2025 rectified as per order dated July 01, 2025 and received by the Company on 
July 03,2025 (“NCLT Order”).  
In furtherance to the same and in accordance with Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India 
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (as amended), we would like to inform you 
that the Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting held today i.e. on Monday, July 07, 2025, i.e. the meeting 
commenced at 3:00 P.M. and concluded at 06:00 P.M. inter-alia, considered/ noted/ took on record the following:  
A.
Certified True Copy of the NCLT Order
The Company has received the certified true copy of the NCLT Order dated June 10, 2025, rectified as per 
order dated July 01, 2025 and received by the Company on July 03,2025. In accordance with the terms of the 
Scheme as approved by the NCLT, the Appointed date of the Scheme is April 01, 2023. 
B.
Effective Date
Pursuant to the Clause 27 of the Scheme read with provisions of Section 232(5) of the Companies Act, 2013, 
the Transferor Company and the Transferee Company have filed e- form INC- 28 with the Registrar of 
Companies, Delhi and Haryana, (“RoC”) on July 05, 2025, and accordingly, the Scheme has become 
effective from July 05, 2025. 
C.
Intimation of Record Date
Pursuant to the Clause 1.1(xviii) of the Scheme, this is to inform you that the Board of Directors of the 
Transferor Company have fixed the “Record Date” to be the Effective Date of the Scheme i.e., Saturday, July 
05, 2025 as the, for the purpose of determining the list of equity shareholders of the Company to whom equity 
shares of the Transferee Company would be allotted in pursuance of the Scheme. The Transferee Company 
has considered the Record Date as decided by the Transferor Company, for the purpose of issuance of shares

Consolidation and increase in Authorized Share Capital 
 
In terms of Clause 21 of the Scheme, the authorized share capital of the Company automatically stands 
increased to the extent of the aggregate authorized share capital of the Transferor Company, upon 
effectiveness of the Scheme. Accordingly, Clause V of the Memorandum of Association and Articles of 
Association of the Company pertaining to the authorized share capital stands replaced/ substituted by the 
following Clause V: 
 
“The Authorized Share Capital of the Company is INR 82,50,00,000 (Indian Rupees Eighty-Two Crores Fifty 
Lakhs Only) divided into 8,25,00,000 (Eight Crores Twenty-Five Lakhs Only) Equity Shares of INR 10 (Indian 
Rupees Ten only) each.” 
 
E. 
Change in name of the Company 
 
In terms of Clause 21 of the Scheme and the NCLT Order, upon the Scheme becoming effective the name of 
the Company shall stand changed from “Selan Exploration Technology Limited” to “Antelopus Selan 
Energy Limited”. 
 
However pursuant to the aforesaid NCLT Order, the change of name of the Company will be consequent to 
the requisite filings to be made with the RoC by the Company and necessary approvals in accordance with the 
provisions of Section 13, 14 and other applicable provisions of the Act and subject to the provisions of the 
Memorandum and Articles of Association of the Company.  
 
We shall keep you updated upon the completion of the due process.
