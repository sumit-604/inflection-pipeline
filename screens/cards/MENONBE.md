## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Menon Bearings makes engine bearings, bushes and thrust washers from bi-metal strip in Kolhapur. Two wholly owned subsidiaries add aluminium die casting and machining (Menon Alkop) and asbestos-free brake linings (Menon Brakes) (AR FY26, page 5). In Q2 FY27 bi-metal was 72.0% of revenue, Alkop 24.5% and brakes 3.5%. By channel, OEM was 52.3%, exports 33.9% and replacement 7.6% (Investor Presentation Q2 FY27, page 10). Customers include John Deere, Tata Motors, International Tractors and Brakes India. The company is sole or major supplier to most of them (01-CRISIL, 23-Apr-2026).

**What is changing?** Revenue broke out of a flat band. It was Rs 218 crore in FY23 (03-CRISIL, 26-Oct-2023) and Rs 210.75 crore in FY24 (AR FY25, page 33). It rose to Rs 293.81 crore in FY26, up 22.8% (AR FY26, page 5). Q2 FY27 revenue was Rs 98.33 crore, up 57.3% year on year (Q2 FY27 results, page 7). Capacity rose behind it: bi-metal from 486 to 600 lakh units, brakes from 18 to 46 lakh pieces (Investor Presentation Q2 FY27, page 15).

**Why now?** Two consecutive record quarters. Q1 FY27 profit after tax was Rs 14.11 crore and Q2 FY27 Rs 14.74 crore, against Rs 6.79 crore in Q2 FY26 (Q2 FY27 results, page 7). On 30 October 2026 management raised its FY27 growth call to "more than 30%" (Nov 2026 concall, page 3), from 25% in May (May 2026 concall, page 4).

**What must be true?** Volume must carry the growth, not metal prices. Tin rose 72% and copper 50% (Nov 2026 concall, page 6). Management says about 20 of the 30 points are volume (Nov 2026 concall, page 22). The pass-through formula must also hold EBITDA margin at 20% to 22% (Nov 2026 concall, page 15).

**What breaks it?** Three things. A US slowdown or tariff hit, since 80% of exports go to the USA (Nov 2026 concall, page 10). A tractor downturn, since tractors are still 34% of engine bearings (Nov 2026 concall, page 18). Or earnings quality: receivables, related-party sales and a half-year that does not equal its two quarters (step 9).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "Auto Components and engineering parts" (Q2 FY27 results, page 3). The deck splits three divisions. Management gives EBITDA margins of 30% to 35% in brakes, 18% to 19% in Alkop and 23% in bearings (Nov 2026 concall, page 16). Raw material is 45% to 50% of cost, and contracts carry escalation clauses with a lag (01-CRISIL). Auto is 60% to 65% of revenue on management's count (Nov 2026 concall, page 19). CRISIL puts it at 80% to 90%.

The engine: spec-approved parts, sole-sourced, priced on a metal-plus-conversion formula. Margin comes from conversion efficiency and mix, not from the metal.

**Archetype: build-to-spec component maker.** Design-win pipeline, content per unit, input-cost pass-through. The brakes aftermarket is a small brand-like slice, 3.5% of revenue.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming. Switching cost is the real asset. A bearing approved into an engine is costly to re-qualify, and order-to-production lead time is about nine months (Investor Presentation Q2 FY27, page 33). Sole-supplier status (01-CRISIL) supports that. Pricing power is partial: CRISIL says modest scale "restricts pricing power with customers and suppliers" (01-CRISIL). The cost case is productivity, up 25% (Nov 2026 concall, page 6), with headcount up about 5% on 30% growth (page 8). No scarcity asset exists.

## 4. The promoters

The Menon family of Kolhapur, founded by Ram Menon (01-CRISIL). Nitin Menon is Executive Chairman. Arun Aradhye became Managing Director on 4 March 2026 (AR FY26, page 30). Aditya Menon, his son, speaks for the promoter group on calls.

Promoter and group holding at 31 March 2026 was 68.44%: Nitin Menon 27.56%, Menon United Pvt Ltd 24.30%, Sucheta Menon 5.00%, Aditya and Anshul Menon 5.79% each (AR FY26, page 126). Pledge: NOT FOUND, looked for in AR FY26 and AR FY25; the shareholding pattern is not held. Nitin Menon personally guarantees the term loans (AR FY26, pages 119 to 120).

Related-party dependence is material. The board approved sales to Mani Auto Components, a firm in which Nitin Menon is a partner. Limits are Rs 40 crore for FY25, Rs 50 crore for FY26 and Rs 55 crore for FY27 (AR FY26, page 52). The FY26 actual is not legible in the extracted note (AR FY26, page 110). Rs 50 crore would be 23% of FY26 standalone turnover of Rs 213 crore (AR FY26, page 28). Dividend: Rs 2 a share, Rs 11.21 crore (AR FY26, page 29).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R2, cost-advantaged converter: FY23 to FY25 revenue flat, ROCE near 19%. TO R3, spec'd supplier: FY26 ROCE 24%, wider sole-source positions, an export list that now includes John Deere, Honeywell, Eaton and Cummins. One rung, inside the base rate.

The mechanism is spec-in of new parts plus export reach. Management's claim, once: "we hope that why we should not grow at a rate of 30% year-on-year for next four years" (Nov 2026 concall, pages 14 to 15).

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim volume growth from new capacity and new parts. Two quarters move that way. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 47% and Q2 57% year on year. In Q2, OEM revenue rose about 50% and exports more than 75% (Nov 2026 concall, page 12). The proof point is present in revenue and in absolute profit.

The margin proof is thinner. Q2 margin dipped from Q1 as metal costs ran ahead of price revisions. Management expects the revisions "for this quarter" (Nov 2026 concall, page 6). Q3 FY27 is the test.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **The half-year does not equal its quarters.** Filed H1 FY27 PAT is Rs 26.54 crore and EPS Rs 4.74. Q1 plus Q2 is Rs 28.85 crore and Rs 5.15 (Q2 FY27 results, pages 7 to 8). The deck shows H1 at Rs 28.85 crore (page 12). Management cites intercompany eliminations (Nov 2026 concall, page 4). The gap is Rs 2.31 crore, 8%.
- Related-party sales to Mani Auto Components, approved up to Rs 55 crore for FY27 (AR FY26, page 52).
- Receivables up 50% in FY26 on revenue up 23% (deck, page 39). FY26 operating cash flow was 61% of PAT.
- AR FY26 page 5 states a 25.31% EBITDA margin. Its own numbers, Rs 65.20 crore on Rs 293.81 crore, give 22.2%.
- Investors asked for one consistent format across quarterly and annual statements (May 2026 concall, page 5).
- Single-segment reporting hides three divisional margins.
- Auditor ARNA & Associates, Kolhapur: no key audit matters (AR FY26, page 90); no database-level audit trail in the ERP (page 93).
- About 28% of revenue goes to the USA (Nov 2026 concall, page 10).

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

CRISIL reaffirmed BBB+/Stable and A2 on 23 April 2026 (01-CRISIL). The view: established market position and a comfortable balance sheet, against auto concentration, input and forex risk, and modest scale. FY25 gearing was 0.27 times and interest cover 12.34 times. The upgrade trigger is net cash accrual above Rs 40 crore. FY26 accrual was about Rs 37 crore, PAT plus depreciation less dividend, so FY27 should clear it. CRISIL cut the outlook from Positive to Stable in October 2023 on weak tractor demand (03-CRISIL). Today's filings are ahead of that view.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED.** Two record quarters, capacity commissioned, guidance raised.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, provisionally.** The receivable and inventory build matches the US warehouse stocking management describes (Nov 2026 concall, page 23). The H1 gap and related-party sales are unresolved. If either proves structural, this flips.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a corpus document: Bull AI company record, 2026-11-04, market capitalisation Rs 1,749.6 crore. That is 45.7 times FY26 PAT and about 31 times FY27E (step 13). The R3 neighbourhood is about 19 times; R4 is about 21 times. The market already prices the climb.

**Posture: EARNINGS-ONLY.** The climb is real and priced. Returns ride EPS growth alone.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** The documents prove the climb. The strategy hunts an unpriced climb, and this one is priced. A 30% grower at about 31 times forward gets no help from re-rating.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. The H1 FY27 reconciliation. Which base is true: Rs 26.54 crore filed, or Rs 28.85 crore summed?
2. FY26 and FY27 sales to Mani Auto Components, and what that firm does with them.
3. Receivable ageing, and whether any of it is related-party.
4. The volume share of growth against metal pass-through.
5. Pledge status, from the shareholding pattern.

**What would change the view**
- The recognition gap re-opening while Q3 FY27 confirms growth and a 20%+ margin moves this to PROCEED.
- A clean H1 reconciliation and arm's-length related-party sales remove the main quality doubt.
- A Q3 margin below 18%, or a US export drop, moves it toward PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
