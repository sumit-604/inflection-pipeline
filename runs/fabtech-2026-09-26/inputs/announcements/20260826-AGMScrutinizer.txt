on 24th July, 2026 . The AGM notice  contained the detailed procedure to be followed by the 
members who were desirous of casting their votes electronically as well as provided under Rule 
20 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014  read with the General 
Circular No. 18/2020, 19/2020 , 20/2020 ,20/2022, 09/2023 and 09/2024 issued by MCA in this

July 2026.

of Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014.
