1. Identity. OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd, brand Kissht, NSE KISSHT, BSE
   544754. Listed 08-May-2026 at a Rs 162-171 band; fresh issue Rs 850 Cr.
   CMP Rs 356, market cap Rs 6,312 Cr, trailing PE 19.9x.
   (https://www.screener.in/company/KISSHT/consolidated/ ; RHP dated 25-Apr-2026)
2. Business. A digital lender to mass-market and mass-affluent borrowers.
   It lends through its NBFC-ML subsidiary Si Creva Capital Services. AUM
   Rs 8,001 Cr at Jun-26, +61% YoY. Unsecured personal loans are 92.3%;
   loan against property (LAP) is 7.7%, sold through 101 branches.
   (Q1 FY27 press release, BSE 29-Jul-2026)
3. Book model. Off-book AUM is Rs 4,284 Cr (53.6%) across 8 partners, with
   45+ lending and co-lending partners. The company posts a 5% FLDG
   (first-loss default guarantee) upfront on partner books.
   (Q1 FY27 press release; Q1 FY27 concall, Aug 2026)
4. Print. Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 670 Cr (+45% YoY), PAT Rs 95 Cr (+59%).
   FY26 revenue Rs 2,209 Cr, PAT Rs 281 Cr. FY25 revenue fell to Rs 1,352 Cr
   from Rs 1,700 Cr in FY24 while it pivoted to longer-tenure loans.
   (screener consolidated; https://inc42.com/features/kisshts-inr-1000-cr-ipo-fundamentals-remain-a-sticking-point/)
5. Asset quality. GNPA 2.25%, NNPA 0.36%, Stage 3 PCR 84.1%. Credit cost
   6.80% of average AUM, down from 8.85%. GNPA only holds 90-150 DPD loans
   because write-off happens at 150 DPD. (Q1 FY27 press release; Aug-2026
   concall lines ~184, ~366, ~821)
6. Returns and capital. RoAAUM 5.05%, RoAE 21.2% annualised. Net worth
   Rs 2,245 Cr, CRAR 40.2%, D/E 0.91x. CRISIL upgraded Si Creva to A-/Stable
   on 13-Feb-2026. (press release; CRISIL rationale 13-Feb-2026)
7. Guidance. AUM growth >40% in FY27, a 15% cut in credit cost, GNPA
   <2.25%, medium-term RoA 4.5-5% and RoE 19-21%. Cost of borrowing to fall
   in H2 FY27. (https://entrackr.com/news/kissht-parent-crosses-rs-8000-cr-aum-in-q1-fy27-loan-disbursements-rise-37-12133151 ; Aug-2026 concall)
8. Capital raise. On 17-Sep-2026 the board approved a Rs 832 Cr preferential
   issue at Rs 314.11 to 34 non-promoter investors, four months after the
   IPO and below CMP. (BSE filing 17-Sep-2026; https://retailintel.in/signal/kissht-parent-onemi-approves-832-crore-preferential-fundrais-c0c7cbdd)
9. Governance leads. Promoter group holds 24.80%, no pledge. An ED notice
   (2023) and a past FIU "high risk" classification are named in the IPO
   coverage. (BSE shareholding Jun-2026; inc42 as above)
10. The engine. Credit cost falls while AUM compounds 40%+ and funding cost
   drops after the A- upgrade. The mix moves toward secured LAP. RoA holds
   near 5% while leverage rises from 0.9x D/E. That would be a lender
   climbing from a high-loss, high-yield rung to a franchise rung.
