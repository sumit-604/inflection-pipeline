## Load-bearing facts: what the run found

**LBF1, guidance against delivery.** The FY27 guide is explicit and repeated across the Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls. It reads "Rs 6,000 Cr visible; Rs 6,500 Cr if US tariff resolves" (Q3 FY26 call), with exports of ~Rs 600 Cr (Q4 FY26 call). Q1 FY27 delivered revenue of Rs 1,509 Cr, up 27%, and exports of Rs 127 Cr (Q1 FY27 call). The MD called the run rate ~Rs 6,000 Cr. The prior year record splits. FY26 revenue of Rs 5,183 Cr beat the ~Rs 4,800 Cr guide, and FY26 EBITDA beat the Rs 470-480 Cr guide (Q2 FY26 and Q4 FY26 calls). FY26 exports of Rs 454 Cr missed the Rs 600-650 Cr guide (Q2 FY26 p.11; Q4 FY26 p.15) and fell from Rs 561 Cr in FY25. Verifier B corrects the EBITDA guide: ~Rs 650 Cr for FY27 and Rs 700-750 Cr for the year after, not a Rs 650-750 Cr FY27 range (Q4 FY26 p.8, p.17; Q1 FY27 p.11). Finding: the revenue guide has a delivery record; the export guide does not.

**LBF2, mix shift margin.** Rs 314 against Rs 262 EBITDA per wheel is confirmed (Q1 FY27 call). It is a blended figure, EBITDA over all units. No document in the corpus splits EBITDA by product, and the AR reports one segment (Note 44). Alloy share rose from ~28% (FY24) to 32% (FY25) and 36% (1HFY26) (rating rationale p.4), while EBITDA margin fell from 12.7% (FY22) to 9.9% (FY26) (deck Historical P&L). Alloy share has stalled at 35-37% across four calls. Management said domestic alloy margins are "crashing" under competition (Q3 FY26 p.19; Q1 FY27 p.12). FY26 EBITDA per wheel was stated as both Rs 262 and Rs 272 in the same call (Q4 FY26 p.4, p.8). Wheels India calls alloy its lowest return segment (Wheels India Q2 FY26 call). Finding: the per wheel number is real; the claim that mix drives it is unproven.

**LBF3, cash conversion.** Confirmed, and wider than company memory stated. Standalone CFO/EBITDA is 63.3% in FY26 against 103.5% in FY25, AR basis. The 65.0% in company memory and in the Gate 0 block rests on a screener EBITDA of Rs 510.23 Cr that Verifier A flagged (see source fidelity below). The Rs 400-500 Cr factoring limit is debtor side factoring (Q3 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls, Jain p.9). It is a separate line from the Rs 676.50 Cr payables side supplier finance in Note 25 (AR p.166-167). The AR discloses the second and not the first. Standalone year end cash was Rs 6.5 Cr (AR p.141). Finding: two financing supports sit under a 63% conversion ratio. Determination INDETERMINATE.

**LBF4, AMW exceptional gain.** FY24 consolidated other income of Rs 486.76 Cr, against Rs 3-13 Cr in adjacent years, is confirmed (screener Data_Sheet). The ~Rs 138 Cr consideration and the gain break-up are NOT FOUND, because the FY24 AR is not in the corpus. The run found a Rs 100 Cr HDFC Bank term loan raised for "Acquisition of AMW Autocomponent Ltd" (CARO, standalone AR p.136). AMW is a wholly owned subsidiary. It has lost money every year since acquisition (Rs 16.82 Cr in FY26) and its AOC-1 shows turnover of "0" (AR p.239). The parent's loan to AMW is Rs 133.15 Cr principal plus Rs 8.27 Cr accrued interest (Note 41, AR p.171-172). AMW carries a Rs 59.31 Cr DTA (Note 51 consol) and an adverse CARO title remark (AR p.188). Three AR figures for AMW's size do not reconcile: Rs 75.98 Cr, Rs 70.61 Cr and Rs 20.20 Cr. Unexplained reserve transfers of Rs 674.68 Cr (FY25) and Rs 364.52 Cr (FY26) took consolidated retained earnings to minus Rs 826.70 Cr (Note 19, consol AR p.219-220). EBIT excludes other income, so ROCE is not directly inflated. ROE and the equity base are. Finding: the gain is real in size; its accounting trail is the largest open disclosure gap in the run.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. EBITDA per wheel against alloy share. In the Q2 FY27 deck (expected Nov-2026), read EBITDA per wheel and the alloy share of revenue together. Above Rs 310 with alloy share rising supports the mix thesis. Above Rs 310 with alloy flat at 35-37% means price, not mix. Below Rs 280 kills the pricing trigger. Tests LBF2.
2. H1 FY27 cash conversion. In the half year cash flow statement filed with the Q2 FY27 results, CFO above 80% of AR basis EBITDA supports growth induced; below 65% supports structural. Tests LBF3 and the cash flag.
3. Factoring balances. Ask management for the debtor factoring and supplier finance balances at Mar-2025, Mar-2026 and Sep-2026. Growth of the supplier finance line beyond Rs 676.50 Cr means more of reported CFO is financed. Tests LBF3.
4. Export run rate. H2 FY27 quarterly exports in the results deck must imply a full year of Rs 550 to 600 Cr. Exports stuck below Rs 400 Cr annualised, or fresh US tariff notices in the Federal Register, kill the export trigger. Tests LBF1.
5. Bhuj trial production. Watch for a Reg 30 filing or a call statement that confirms trial output by Q4 FY27 with OEM approvals. A slip past FY27 year end, or no approvals, kills the Bhuj trigger. Knuckle revenue of Rs 110 to 130 Cr for FY27 and a third knuckle customer check the knuckle leg.
6. Rating agency leverage. India Ratings puts adjusted net leverage at 3.2x at 9MFY26 and expects it above 2.5x through FY27. Leverage above 2.5x on a sustained basis is a named negative trigger; below 1.5x is the positive trigger (rationale p.3-4). Read the next Ind-Ra review.
7. AMW. In the consolidated results and the next AR AOC-1, watch AMW's loss, any DTA write down, land title registration, and the Rs 133.15 Cr loan. Any write down tests LBF4 and the equity base.
8. Disclosure record. Watch whether the FY25 and FY26 reserve transfers and the customs notice get an explanation in a Reg 30 filing, an AGM answer or the FY27 AR. Silence through the FY27 AR moves the promoter read from CAUTION toward CONCERN.
