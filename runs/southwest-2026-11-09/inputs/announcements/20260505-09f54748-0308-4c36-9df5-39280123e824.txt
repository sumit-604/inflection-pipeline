Commenting on the results, Mr. Vikas Jain, Chairman & Managing Director said, 
 
“I am proud to share that we have delivered strong and encouraging financial and operational performance 
this year by registering an all time high revenue and profits. The company clocked a revenue of INR 2430 Mn 
and PAT of INR 330 Mn on consolidated basis, registering a growth of 35% and 101% respectively YOY. The 
standalone performance is also on similar lines. As regards the Q-4 results, they are also equally impressive 
with significant growth “, Mr. Jain said.  
 
Mr. Jain went on to explain that this performance is despite dynamic and often challenging business 
environment. During this challenging time, the company expanded its footprint, strengthened client 
relationships, and enhanced capabilities across multiple domains of mineral exploration and drilling” Mr. Jain 
added. 
 
Mr. Vikas Jain further said that “Recognizing our services, the clients are equally supportive and made our order 
book quite robust crossing over INR 581 Crs mark giving clear visibility in short to medium term. We expect to 
get few more orders, strengthening it further in times ahead.” 
 
Explaining about the promising outlook of the company he said that “with increasing government focus on 
mineral exploration, policy reforms, and the push for self-reliance in critical minerals, the sector is poised for 
strong growth and the company is well-positioned to play a leading role in this transformation.” 
 
Mr. Jain went on to explain that Mineral, Oil & Gas sector has become GOTO sector for India to become self-
reliant (Atma Nirbhar). India has huge untapped natural resources and government focus on exploration is the 
only way to unlock and extract beneath the ground which will not only enable growth engine but also reduce 
dependence on import thereby saving precious foreign exchange. South West Pinnacle is well poised to be part 
of this vision. 
 
Mr Vikas Jain further emphasized that as the things continue to move forward, we will continue to build on our 
strengths, embrace innovation, and contribute meaningfully to India’s journey towards resource security and 
economic growth and help in fulfilling the dream of Atmanirbhar Bharat.” 
 
About South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited 
 
SWPEL is an Integrated Service Provider, providing end-to-end drilling & exploration of natural resources viz. 
Coal, Ferrous, Non-Ferrous & Atomic Minerals and Conventional & non- conventional Oil & Gas and ground 
water investigation. SWPEL’s expertise also extends to carry out exploration using technological tools like 
surface geographical investigation, downhole geophysics, 2D/3D Seismic and Passive Seismic Tomography etc. 
Company has won a coal block in the state of Jharkhand, spread across 266 Hectares, having Geological 
Reserves of over 84 million tons. It has signed a Coal Mine Development and Production Agreement with the

Ministry of Coal, Government of India. The Company has been recently notified as an accredited prospecting 
agency by Ministry of Coal, Government of India for carrying out prospecting operations for the exploration of 
coal and Lignite. Accordingly, exploration activities at the Coal Block in Jharkhand are  going on at full swing. 
After completion of exploration, GR Preparation and other activities will be undertaken on fast track mode. 
 
Further, Company is having two JVs in Oman. The First one is imparting mining services pursuant to a long 
term mining contract for Copper and Gold in Oman besides carrying out exploration and drilling services 
through a joint venture, namely, Alara Resources LLC., Oman. 
 
The second JV, formed recently has been allocated a mining block by Sultanate of Oman wherein the 
exploration activities are in progress. 
 
The Company has successfully completed more than 165 projects during 19 years of journey since inception for 
most of the leading Government & Private Organizations and presently operating 19 projects on Pan India basis, 
in addition to JV Projects in Oman. 
 
SWPEL has completed approximately 3.2 million meters of drilling, 6.5 Lac meter of Geo physical logging 515 
sq.km. of 3 D Seismic surveys, 411 LKM of 2 D seismic Survey for exploration of Coal, Mineral, Oil and Gas using 47 
state of art drilling rigs having varied capacities to drill upto 2500 Meters depth and other equipment.
