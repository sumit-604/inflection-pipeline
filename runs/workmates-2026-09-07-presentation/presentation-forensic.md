## 3. Red flags and inconsistencies

**Flag 1 (load-bearing): revenue per employee ~Rs 84 lakh.** 143 Cr / 170 staff. Indian IT
services runs Rs 15-30 lakh per head. A 3-5x gap points to a large AWS-resale / billing
pass-through slice booked as gross revenue. If true, the 143 Cr is not all services revenue.
This changes margin, ROCE, and the quality-ladder rung. It is the single fact that governs
the whole thesis. VERIFY gross vs net revenue split first.

**Flag 2: no profit, no balance sheet, no cash.** The deck gives revenue and MRR only. No EBITDA,
no PAT figure (only "double digit", undefined as % or Rs Cr), no margin, no ROCE, no debt, no
working capital, no cash conversion, no promoter holding. Every variable that defines business
quality is absent. Cash conversion is therefore INDETERMINATE. It cannot resolve to PROCEED.

**Flag 3: client count contradicts itself.** "300+" on slides 3 and 17; "350+ enterprise clients"
on slide 8. Same deck, two numbers.

**Flag 4: retention vs churn.** 95% retention (5% churn) on slide 10 vs "<=10% churn" on slide 6.
Reconcilable only if one is by logo and one by revenue. Neither is defined.

**Flag 5: one-step MRR jump.** Exit MRR 9.83 to entry MRR 13.3 is a +35% run-rate step across the
FY26/FY27 boundary. Large for one period. Ask what drove it: live new logos, price rises, or
bookings not yet billing.

**Flag 6: AI TAM label inflated.** Slide 7: base $750-900B (2026) to $3.5-4T by 2033 is a ~24%
CAGR, not the "30-36% CAGR" printed beside it. The deck also uses two different AI market bases
($390B in 2025 on slide 15, $750-900B in 2026 on slide 7). Macro puff, not company-specific, but
it shows the deck rounds up.

**Flag 7: AI transition is narrated, not proven.** The AI products (IntelliOps: SmartOps,
AutoFinOps, ComplianceOps; VoxoRA, VerID, VoyaGE) are tagged "LIVE PRODUCTION" but carry no
revenue, no client count, no margin. The "Cloud to AI" climb is a claim. The proof gate has
NOT fired on this deck.
