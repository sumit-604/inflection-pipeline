## WHERE THE MARGIN COMES FROM

14. Margin comes from mix, not price. The shift runs from commodity magnetos
    toward ECUs, sensors, ISG and EV controllers. Management calls the
    destination "mobility electronics".
    (Investor_Presentation_2026-03-17_InstitutionalMeet.pdf; Q1FY27 deck p7-p8)
15. Q1FY27 margin fell 225 bps QoQ to 10.54%. The company names commodity
    inflation, NdFeB magnet supply constraints and Middle East supply-chain
    disruption. (Q1FY27 deck p14)

## THE ONE INFLECTION

16. The engine is content per vehicle, not 2W volume. INEL is converting a
    single-product magneto franchise into a multi-product electronics supplier.
    Q1FY27 added new business in ISG controllers, DC-DC converters, sensors,
    actuators and controllers. Start of production began for the super-premium
    2W segment. (Q1FY27 deck p14)
17. The 15 percentage-point gap between INEL growth (35%) and 2W industry growth
    (20%) in Q1FY27 is the observable form of that engine. Whether it is content
    gain, share gain or input-cost pass-through is the thing to verify.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority for every stage)

**LB1 — Cash conversion broke while profit rose.**
FY26 cash generated from operations was Rs 70.76 cr against FY25 Rs 71.83 cr,
flat, while operating profit before working capital changes rose from Rs 94.62 cr
to Rs 121.53 cr. Net CFO fell to Rs 40.47 cr from Rs 49.40 cr. Capex was
Rs 42.15 cr, so free cash flow was about negative Rs 1.7 cr. Working capital
absorbed Rs 54.2 cr in FY26 (receivables Rs 35.47 cr, inventories Rs 18.70 cr)
against Rs 32.4 cr in FY25. Screener puts CFO/OP at 60% in FY26 against 128% in
FY23, and working capital days at 46 against 29 in FY24.
*Verify:* is the working-capital build a ramp artifact or a permanent change in
terms? This triggers the Kernex/Tipco cash-conversion guard. INDETERMINATE cash
conversion caps at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS.
(AR FY26 cash flow statement, extracted L12180-12215; screener ratios)

**LB2 — Two customers are 71% of revenue, yet reported related-party sales are 5.38%.**
The AR states 2 customers each exceeded 10% of revenue and together account for
Rs 754.91 cr of FY26 revenue, which is 70.7% of Rs 1,068.48 cr. FY25 was
Rs 623.65 cr on Rs 844.83 cr, or 73.8%. The BRSR separately reports sales to
related parties at only 5.38% of total sales.
*Verify:* name the two customers. Reconcile the TVS Motor relationship, since
Lucas-TVS is the promoter and TVS Motor is described as a relative of the
ultimate parent. Establish whether pricing is arm's length.
(AR Note on major customers, extracted L14603-14610; BRSR, extracted L9298-9306)

**LB3 — Half the treasury book sits in related-party instruments.**
The BRSR reports investments in related parties at 49.36% of total investments
in FY26, up from 47.15% in FY25. The investment book was Rs 531.6 cr, or 65% of
net worth. Other income of Rs 30.6 cr is roughly a quarter of EBITDA.
*Verify:* what the related-party investments are, who issued them, and on what
terms. Determine whether the treasury is a separable non-operating asset for the
equity bridge or a governance flag. Operating ROCE must be computed net of it.
(BRSR, extracted L9305-9306; deck p17; AR Note 29)

**LB4 — The growth split is unproven.**
FY26 revenue rose 26.5% and Q1FY27 rose 35.5%, against 2W industry growth of
20%. Exports rose 162% to Rs 87.3 cr. Management attributes growth to new
product wins in ISG, DC-DC converters, sensors and actuators.
*Verify:* split the FY26 and Q1FY27 revenue increase into 2W industry volume,
market-share gain, new-product content per vehicle, exports, and input-cost
pass-through. The entire mobility-electronics transition thesis rests on the
content share of that split. Also verify NdFeB magnet exposure, since magnetos
need permanent magnets and China export controls are named as a live constraint.
(Q1FY27 deck p14, p16; AR geography note, extracted L14578-14580)
