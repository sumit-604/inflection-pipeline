THE ENGINE: a mix shift from commodity industrial valves toward type-approved
marine and naval valves sold to Indian shipyards (33% of revenue from 0.5%
in FY23), with the FSS shipset orders as the first multi-ship programme.
The thesis rests on whether that shift lifts margin and converts to cash.
So far it has not converted: four years of negative CFO.

## Sector cap row (step D)

Cables / Industrial products, 25x. Rappid is a build-to-spec valve maker
serving industrial and marine buyers. The Defence / strategic row (38x) would
need naval revenue to dominate and be evidenced in the filings; marine is 33%
and includes commercial shipbuilding. Flag for phase-3 confirmation.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)

1. GUIDANCE vs DELIVERY: FY27 growth guided "closing to 50% or more" (Jun-2026 call) against Q1 FY27 sales of Rs 14.87 Cr (+28%) and a Rs 40 Cr executable book. FY26 grew 2% with H2 down 25%. Verify what "Sales" means in the revised Q1 update versus revenue from operations, and test the FY26 guidance given at the Nov-2025 results against FY26 delivery.
2. CASH CONVERSION: CFO negative in each of FY23-FY26 (sum about Rs -26.6 Cr) against PAT of about Rs 17 Cr over FY23-FY26; debtor days 170, inventory days 258. Verify the FY26 AR receivables ageing, the customer mix of receivables (PSU shipyard vs private), inventory composition, and the "March dispatch" explanation against the H1 FY27 print.

IPO PROCEEDS AND INVESTMENTS: Rs 7.65 Cr of IPO money (capex and acquisition objects) moved to working capital on 21-Mar-2026, and balance-sheet investments rose from Rs 0.57 Cr to Rs 4.76 Cr in FY26. Verify the auditor's report and CARO remarks in the FY26 AR, and verify the 33% marine share (and the naval part of it) in the AR, since the deck is the only source.
