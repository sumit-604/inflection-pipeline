Precision parts maker growing faster than cash

OBSC Perfection makes precision engineered metal components to customer drawings for automotive Tier 1s and OEMs. Founded in 2017 and run out of Pune, it listed on NSE Emerge in October 2024 and trades near Rs 666 for a market value around Rs 1,720 Cr. Automotive is 86% of sales, marine 6%, defense 6%, and a small renewables and others bucket 2%. Named customers include ZF, Tenneco, JTEKT and Tata AutoComp, most of them far larger than OBSC itself. Its main raw material, bright bar steel, is sourced structurally from a promoter controlled entity, Omega Bright Steel.

The company is trying to widen from a single domestic auto vendor into a diversified precision engineering supplier across defense, marine, aerospace, humanoid robotics and exports. The order book grew from about Rs 290 Cr in FY24 to Rs 700 to 750 Cr in FY25 to more than Rs 1,200 Cr in FY26. New capacity is coming up at Sanand, a dedicated Tenneco line, plus a Supa mega factory and added stamping and forging lines. Management wants exports at 30% to 35% of the business and non automotive at about a third within two to three years.

FY26 revenue was about Rs 224 Cr, up 54%, with PAT around Rs 27 Cr and an EBITDA margin of 19.5%. Gate 0 places the company at AVERAGE on a core score of 52 out of 100, with full marks on growth and a strong balance sheet score, but held down by weak cumulative cash conversion and by a data gap where the promoter holding and pledge pages of the annual report could not be read. Cash is the sore point. FY26 operating cash flow was negative Rs 1.95 Cr against positive PAT of Rs 27 Cr, and FY25 cash conversion was only about 53%.

The cash gap traces to a working capital build that ran well ahead of sales. In FY25 receivables rose 62%, inventory 79% and payables 118% against revenue growth of 24%. FY26 capex of about Rs 53 Cr was funded by fresh debt and a preferential equity issue rather than internal cash. Whether this is a temporary growth build or a structural drain cannot be settled from the filings, because the receivables ageing schedule sits in annual report pages that were truncated in the source document.

Management earns a credibility grade of B. It delivered 54% growth against a guide of more than 40%, brought the Sanand facility on and grew the order book as claimed across two calls. Against that, it broke its own promise of quarterly disclosure and held only one further call in twelve months, let an AS9100D aerospace certification slip past its implied timeline without acknowledging it, and quietly trimmed a medium term margin ambition from 4 to 5 points down to about 1% for FY27. The promoter record itself reads CAUTION rather than clean: no adverse regulatory or pledge history surfaced, but there is a structural related party dependency on Omega Bright Steel, no non compete, and a recent Rs 12.45 Cr related party asset purchase whose pricing fairness is undisclosed.

Four listed auto parts peers, Divgi TorqTransfer, Precision Camshafts, RACL Geartech and Talbros Auto, largely corroborate the demand backdrop: a China plus one export tailwind, strong commercial vehicle volumes, and an industry wide capex race that makes OBSC's plant buildout look normal for the sector. One claim they contradict matters. OBSC framed its margin guidance trim as forced by tariffs, the West Asia conflict and shipping costs; three peers facing the same cost pressure in the same window reaffirmed their margin guidance rather than cut it. No peer corroborates the commercial vehicle softening that management dismissed.

The addressable market is large and the company is small inside it. TAM sits at Rs 61,000 to 70,000 Cr, the served market at about Rs 18,600 Cr, and OBSC holds roughly 1.2% of that served market today, a runway classed as MASSIVE with headroom near 85 times current served share. The three year served opportunity implies a 39% revenue CAGR and the five year figure 34%, though that served number sits on the optimistic side of the disclosed capex plan and the Supa capex quantum was never disclosed. Management put no explicit market size claim on the record.
