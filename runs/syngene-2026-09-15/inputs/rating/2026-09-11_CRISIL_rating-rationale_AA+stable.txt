 Detailed Rationale 

 Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Syngene International Limited (Syngene). 

 The ratings continue to reflect the established market position of Syngene in contract research, diverse clientele, and healthy financial risk profile. The ratings also factor in benefits derived from being a subsidiary of Biocon Ltd (Biocon; rated ‘Crisil AA+/S table/Crisil A1+’), the leading biopharmaceutical company of India. These strengths are partially offset by the risk related to regulatory changes and competition, ability to timely scale-up the operations and exposure to intense   global   competition and regulatory risks . 

 The business risk profile of the company remains supported by the long-term contracts with   Bristol-Myers   Squibb Co (BMS; rated ‘A/Stable/A1’ by S&P Global Ratings [S&P]), Baxter International Inc (Baxter; rated ‘BBB/Negative/A2’ by S&P) and Amgen Inc (Amgen rated ‘BBB+/Stable/A2’ by S&P) for its research business and a 10-year contract with Zoetis Inc   (Zoetis; rated ‘BBB+/Stable’ by S&P). Revenue grew modestly by ~3% to Rs 3,739 crore in fiscal 2026, due to inventory balancing by its largest biologics customer and muted demand across certain service lines. During the first quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue degrew by 16% to Rs 736 crore compared with the previous corresponding fiscal, owing to the absence of offtake from its largest biologics ma nufacturing customer and attrition in certain research services accounts. Crisil Ratings expects the revenue to degrow by mid-single digits in fiscal 2027, due to a sharp reduction in biologics manufacturing revenue from this customer and the lag in replacing these volumes through new contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) contracts. Over the medium term, growth prospects remain supported by customer diversification efforts, increasing contribution from differentiated research and clinic al services, capacity ramp-up across biologics and CDMO operations, and outsourcing opportunities arising from global supply-chain diversification initiatives. 

 The operating margin moderated to about 25% in fiscal 2026 from 29-30% seen in the last five years owing to lower operating leverage. Crisil Ratings expects the margin to remain in the range of 24-25% in fiscal 2027, constrained by lower operating leverage and underutilisation of recently created capacities, though partly offset by ongoing cost opt imisation initiatives, including employee cost rationalisation, operating expense control and technology-led productivity improvements. Over the medium term, margin is expected to be supported by increasing utilisation of the new facilities, higher contribution from differentiated services and recovery in revenue growth. 

 Financial risk profile continues to be strong, supported by steady accretion to reserve and   a net debt free position as on March 31, 2026. The company shall undertake routine capital expen diture (capex) to add to its existing capacities to support the order book, which shall remain funded through its liquid surplus.   With healthy profitability, prudent working capital management and internally funded organic capex, debt protection metrics will be strong, with gearing expected at less than 0.1   time and interest coverage ratio of more than 20 times over the medium term. 

 Analytical Approach 

 Crisil Ratings has applied its parent notch-up framework to factor in strong operational, financi al and managerial support from Biocon, and has combined the business and financial risk profiles of Syngene and its subsidiaries.

 Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

 Established market position in contract research, diverse clientele, and strong operating capabilities 

 Syngene is a leading contract research and manufacturing services (CRAMS) organisat ion in India. The company began operations with the research vertical and slowly emerged to the development business, with a revenue mix of nearly 61% in fiscal 2026 from the research business comprising of discovery services, dedicated centres and translation and clinical research and balance from the CDMO business. It offers integrated services across drug discovery and development value chain and provides research services in medicinal chemistry and biology to innovator pharmaceutical companies. The esta blished market position of the company is reflected in its large clientele of ~400 companies, including 16 of the top 20 global pharmaceutical majors. Syngene has dedicated research and development (R&D) centres for BMS, Amgen and Baxter, and has demonstrated continued focus on expanding its services in the R&D value chain through its integrated capacities and timely strategic investments and capacity expansion. Strong operating capabilities resulted in a healthy operating margin of 25–28% for the past five fiscals. 

 Strong parentage 

 Syngene is a subsidiary of Biocon and is likely to receive need-based financial support from the parent. Business operations of both the companies differ — Biocon manufactures and markets biopharmaceutical formulations while Syngene undertakes contract research for pharmaceutical, biotechnology, nutrition, agrochemical, animal health and consumer goods entities. While earlier, Syngene accounted for about one-third of the consolidated revenue and operating profit of Biocon, over t he medium term, with higher contribution from the biosimilars segment, the contribution of Syngene to total revenue and profitability is expected to come down. Yet, Syngene will remain critical to Biocon, and hence need-based operational, managerial and financial support will persist. 

 Healthy financial risk profile 

 Adjusted gearing was low at 0.10 time as on March 31, 2026, and should remain at 0.10-0.20 time over the medium term, aided by healthy cash generation, prudent working capital management and int ernally funded capex. Debt protection metrics were comfortable, reflected in interest coverage ratio of 20.77 times in fiscal 2026. The company shall undertake routine capex to add to its existing capacities and investments in automation and artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled technology platforms to support the order book, which shall remain funded through its liquid surplus. 

 Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 

 Susceptibility to regulatory changes and increasing competition 

 The contract research indu stry is highly competitive on account of   increasing global competition from contract research organisations (CRO) in China and Europe, which may have a broader portfolio of services. Hence, more CROs may enter the fray, thereby increase competition and constrain the pricing flexibility of strong players such as Syngene.   Furthermore, competition persists from captive R&D centres and new, smaller entities focusing on a particular therapeutic area. Nevertheless, the company benefits from its wide range of serv ice offerings and strong clientele, apart from the early-mover advantage and long-tenure contracts with customers, partially offsetting the competition. 

 Liquidity Strong 

 Syngene has strong liquidity, driven by a sizeable net cash position of Rs 1,541 crore as on June 30, 2026, and estimated cash accrual of Rs 700–800 crore for the current fiscal, which will be sufficient to cover for the working capital requirement and annual organic capex plans over the medium term. The bank limit of Rs 1,050 crore is sparingly utilised. 

 ESG P rofile 

 Crisil Ratings believes the ESG profile of Syngene supports its already strong credit ri sk profile. The pharmaceutical sector   can have a significant impact on the environment owing to greenhouse gas emissions, water use and waste generation. Social impact is characterised by the impact on the health and wellbeing of consumers on account of its products and on employees and the local community due to its operations . 

 Syngene International Limited has committed to the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), targeting a 50% reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by fiscal 203 3, with its near-term targets validated in fiscal 2025. The company has also committed to engaging suppliers responsible for ~82% of Scope 3 emissions to adopt SBTi-aligned targets within five years 

 The company reported lower-than-peer-average Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions intensity and water withdrawal intensity. Its environmental performance was further supported by a high renewable energy share (~77%), low water discharge (~3% of water withdrawn), and a ~97% waste recycling rate, in fiscal 2026 

 From a soci al perspective, the company reported a higher-than-peer share of female employees (~26%) and workers (~17%), zero workforce fatalities, nil lost-time injury frequency rate, strong employee training coverage, and 100% redressal of stakeholder complaints 

 The governance framework is supported by a 10-member Board, with ~60% independent directors and ~50% women directors, alongside a Lead Independent Director, a Stakeholders Relationship & ESG Committee, and ~95% average attendance of independent directors at B oard and committee meetings. 

 There is growing importance of ESG among investors and lenders. Commitment of Syngene to ESG will play a key role in enhancing stakeholder confidence, given shareholding by foreign portfolio investors and access to both domestic and foreign capital markets. 

 Outlook Stable 

 Syngene will continue to benefit from the expansion of its clientele and increase in time and scope of existing contracts. 

 Rating s ensitivity factors 

 Upward Factors 

 Improvement in the long-term rating of Biocon 

 Sustained annual revenue growth of over 20%, led by improvement in the discovery and development services, and sustained operating profitability above 35% 

 Prudent working capital management and healthy capital structure 

 Downward Factors 

 Moderation   in   the long-term rating of   Biocon or change in stance of financial support from the parent 

 Lower-than-expected revenue growth, with sharp operating profitability reducing below 25% on a sustained basis, impacting cash generation 

 Weakening   of   t he debt protection metrics   due to stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capex   or acquisition . 

 About the Company 

 Syngene is one of the leading CRAMS organisations in India. The company offers R&D services in medicinal chemistry and biology in the early stages of drug discovery, through process development and contract manufacturing of biotherapeutics for human trials. It offers integrated discovery and development services across multiple technology platforms, including small and large molecules, antibody-drug conjugates and oligonucleotides. The company has over 400 clients in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, nutrition, animal health, consumer goods and specialty chemicals industries, including 14 out of top 20 global pharmaceutical companies. It has a team of over 5,600 scientists. 

 About the Group 

 Biocon, set up in 1978, is India’s leading biopharma company. It is fully integrated and delivers biopharma solutions ranging from discovery to development and commercialisation. It has diversified revenue streams covering biosimilars, contract research, small molecules and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).
