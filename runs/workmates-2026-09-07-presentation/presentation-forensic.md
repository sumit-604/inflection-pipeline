- New multi-year base case: FY27E 200.2, FY28E 280.3, FY29E 400.0, FY30E 550.0 (Rs Cr).
  Forward CAGR ~40%. The thesis carried only FY27. This is a public Rs 550 Cr FY30 target.

  below the 25% threshold). Implied H2 FY27 = 200.2 - 91.56 = 108.6 Cr, roughly +57% YoY over

  as "cash outward while India CFO is negative", and noted the Singapore sub lost Rs 0.46 Cr.

## 3. What the deck OMITS (the three things the thesis broke on)

1. CASH. The thesis broke on cash, not revenue. FY26 CFO -Rs 0.07 Cr (trigger FIRED). DPO
   collapsed 119.6 to 62.2 days. CCC swung -36 to +11 days (permanent structural shift). The deck
   shows revenue and MRR only. Zero on CFO, working capital, receivables, payables.
2. GROSS vs NET. The deck shows Rs 143 Cr and a Rs 550 Cr FY30 target. 67.2% (Rs 96.66 Cr of
   143.82) is AWS pass-through "cost of cloud tech". The true business is gross profit ~Rs 37 Cr
   at ~32.8% gross margin. A Rs 550 Cr gross target masks a much smaller GP engine. The deck's own
   revenue-per-employee of ~84 lakh (143 Cr / 170) is the pass-through showing through.
3. CONCENTRATION. Thesis: BFSI 37% + Media 34% = 71% of revenue; top 20 accounts = 73%. The deck
   shows a softer "Industry Focus" split (IT&ITES 37.41%, Financial Services 30.40%, Mfg 8.74%,
   Others 23.19%) and no top-5 or top-1 disclosure.

No PAT figure either. "Double Digit PAT" (slide 3) is undefined. Thesis: PAT Rs 15.92 Cr, margin
11.07%, down from 13.00%; H2 FY26 EBITDA margin 13.05% (worst on record).

## 4. Does the deck move the decision?

No. The recorded decision gates on the H1 FY27 (Q2 FY27) result, on two prints:
- Re-engage ONLY if H1 FY27 revenue > Rs 85 Cr AND CFO > Rs 5 Cr.
The Q2 FY27 result (half ending Sep 2026) is not yet out. This deck is pre-result promotional
material. It resolves neither print. It does not touch the cash break.

Two thesis tripwires are already 1-of-2 fired (Op EBITDA <14%: H2 FY26 13.05%; revenue growth
<25%: H2 FY26 15.65%). On management's own H1 FY27 guide (+22.6%), the revenue tripwire fires a
second time by Dec 2026. The deck does nothing to reopen the gap at Rs 392.

VERDICT: AVOID unchanged. Watch the Q2 FY27 cash print (H1 FY27 CFO and revenue). The deck raises
the over-promise (Rs 550 Cr FY30, 40% CAGR) while staying silent on the cash, gross-vs-net, and
concentration facts that carry the bear case. That silence is itself the signal.
