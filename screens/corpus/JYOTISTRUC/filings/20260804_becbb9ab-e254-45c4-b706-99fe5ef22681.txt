Jyoti Structures Limited Announces Strong Financial Performance for Q1 Ended FY 2026-27 
Disciplined execution and operational efficiencies drive strong quarterly performance. 
 
Mumbai, August 04, 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL” or “the Company”), a leading infrastructure 
and power transmission engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company, today announced 
its financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. 
 
The Company delivered a strong operational and financial performance during the period, driven by 
disciplined execution, effective cost management, and steady progress across its order book. The results 
reflect the Company's continued focus on execution excellence, operational efficiency and timely project 
delivery.
