### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **AC ODM/customer diversification**: strengthening in narrative (customers added: 3 by Q2 FY26 call, "4 total apart from Voltas" onboarding by Q3, "catering to 4 customers" total by Q4), but the underlying metric (Voltas revenue share) did NOT weaken as promised — Q2 FY26 call guided Voltas share down to "40-60% next year"; Q3 FY26 call reversed course: "Voltas share has either remained the same, volume of Voltas remain the same or is expected to grow" (Piyush Jain Q&A). **Flag: this trigger's headline metric weakened even as the qualitative narrative stayed positive.**
- **Compressor ramp**: strengthening. Commercial production delivered close to original FY25-call timeline (Q2 FY26 call confirms start), QCO policy outcome resolved favourably (40% import cap) in Q4 FY26 call, though the capacity target was trimmed from an earlier floated 7.5mn (Q3 FY26 call) to 6mn (Q4 FY26 call) — management frames this as "optimising line productivity," not solely the government outcome.
- **Deep freezer/commercial refrigeration**: weakening/slipping — the 150k→400k capacity expansion promised "before end of next financial year" (Q2 FY26 call) was pushed back six months (Q3 FY26 call), openly attributed to cash conservation during the H1 slowdown (an honest admission, see 2B).
- **Washing machine**: DROPPED / quietly faded. Actively discussed with capacity/revenue numbers in Q2 and Q3 FY26 calls; in the Q4 FY26 call presentation and Q&A there is no status update at all — no mention of pilot results, field trial outcome, or mass-production start. **Flag: timeline kept slipping (mass production "1-2 months" in Q2 call → "April/May" in Q3 call → silence in Q4 call) and then the trigger disappeared from the narrative entirely.**
- **FY27 revenue target**: material dilution. An informal ₹2,000cr FY27 target (cited from an investor conference) was left unconfirmed for two calls ("we hope so, confirm Q4" — Q2 FY26 call; then further deferred to "April call" — Q3 FY26 call), and finally resolved in the Q4 FY26 call to a 35-40% CAGR guide (~₹1,100-1,155cr) — roughly half the earlier informal number. Management's framing ("we are not revising the guidance... the guidance remains same") avoids directly acknowledging the scale of the cut. **This is the single most significant trigger dilution in the three-quarter window.**
- **Mainboard listing**: NEW timeline promised and missed in every one of the four calls read (FY25 baseline call through Q4 FY26 call) — over a year of slippage on what was framed each time as imminent. See 2E.
- **AC capacity 1mn→1.8mn**: on track and consistent across Q2, Q3 and Q4 FY26 calls (Chennai piece slipped a quarter within it — see 2A).
- **EMS capacity doubling**: consistent/on-track across all three quarters.
- **QCO/compressor policy**: strengthened from "uncertain, may extend or lapse" (Q2, Q3 FY26 calls) to a resolved, calibrated 40% import allowance (Q4 FY26 call) — a genuinely NEW, positive, verifiable data point.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **ROE**: explicitly promised "double-digit next FY" (i.e. FY26) in the FY25 baseline call. No analyst asked about it and management gave no update in any of the three FY26 calls — a clean silent topic given this is a capex-heavy, low-ROE-optics business under active investor scrutiny.
- **Absolute debt levels / interest coverage**: D/E ≤1 is repeated as a policy but hard current debt figures, cost-of-debt trend (only one data point: 8-8.25%, Q4 FY26 call) and interest coverage are never laid out systematically, despite the company entering a heavy capex phase across four segments simultaneously.
- **Compressor technology-partner economics**: FY25 call disclosed "no revenue share" with Chinese partners (Huayi/Jiaxipera), only equipment purchase cost. This arrangement is never revisited as the compressor business scales — worth checking given rising volumes.
- **Working capital / receivables detail**: an analyst flagged a "significant increase" in trade receivables in the Q4 FY26 call; management's answer was a one-line dismissal ("nothing extraordinary... at par with what one would expect") with no days-sales-outstanding figure given.
- **Voltas contract terms**: the single largest customer relationship (still ~50%+ of AC revenue, and per Q3 FY26 call "expected to grow") has no disclosed contract duration, pricing-renewal cadence, or minimum-volume commitment across any of the three calls.
- **Washing machine status**: after two quarters of active discussion (capacity, ₹60-100cr revenue potential, field-trial timeline), the Q4 FY26 call presentation and Q&A contain zero mention — the likely reason is the product underperformed the field-trial stage and management does not want to draw attention to a missed timeline on a discretionary line, but this is inference, not stated.

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. Competitor commentary and credibility check

- **Voltas (anchor customer) backward integration**: raised as a risk twice — Akash Jain (Q2 FY26 call) and Anik Mitra (Q3 FY26 call) both ask whether Voltas's own capacity build (Chennai, Pantnagar) threatens VOEPL's order book. Management's response is consistently that Tier-2/Tier-3 brands are growing outsourcing even as top brands insource, and that Voltas "wants a decent portion of their purchase coming from other manufacturers" (Q3 FY26 call, SB). This is self-serving and unverifiable from the transcript alone — **flagged for peer verification** (see 4B).
- **Compressor competitive landscape**: Q4 FY26 call newly discloses "two competitors" planning local reciprocating-compressor manufacturing, expected online by "end of Q3/Q4 [FY27]" and "mid next year" respectively, with total industry capacity (all players) projected at 15-18 million units against a 14-15 million unit import gap. This is new, specific, and checkable — VOEPL's own credibility is bolstered by naming a competitive timeline rather than denying competition exists, but the claim should be corroborated against public compressor-industry sources.
- **Import substitution economics (China)**: management repeatedly frames VOEPL as advantaged versus Chinese imports via "local support, better payment terms, no 3-month wait" (Q2 FY26 call) — plausible but self-reported, no third-party corroboration in the transcripts.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence

- RAC industry size: ~1.5-1.6 crore (15-16 million) units CY25/CY26, roughly flat to +5% YoY (Q2 FY26 call, SB, Achal Q&A); insourcing ~0.8-0.9 crore units, remainder outsourced.
- Refrigeration compressor market: ~22 million units p.a. in India, 13-15 million imported, remainder made locally by "large Korean companies" and "one Indian multinational" — consistent figure repeated in Q2, Q3 and Q4 FY26 calls.
- Copper price trend: $8,000-8,500/tonne → ~$11,000/tonne (Q2 FY26 call, SB) — BOM impact of ~₹500 per $1,000/tonne move on a typical AC unit, net blended impact estimated ₹300-700/unit across the year.
- RM pass-through: management states 10-15% of RM cost inflation was passed through industry-wide from manufacturers to brands to consumers (Q4 FY26 call, SB).
- BEE/star-rating table change: cited as the primary demand disruptor for H1 FY26 (inventory clearance, spec delays) across Q2 and Q3 FY26 calls; channel inventory reported to have "dried up by October/November [2025]" then rebuilt from December.
- QCO/compressor import policy: resolved in the Q4 FY26 call — government allowed 40% of a manufacturer's base-year (FY24-25) import volume, calibrated rather than an outright ban; described by management as "a very positive signal" though objectively a partial, not full, restriction.

### 3D. Customer and order book signals
