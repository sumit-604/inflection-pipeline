How I adapted the red-flag classes to a no-call company:
- Dodged question: becomes a silence in every filing on a topic the numbers demand.
- Guidance walkback: becomes a change in timing or scope wording between documents.
- Volunteered negative: becomes a hard fact disclosed in the governance report or the notes, not the narrative.
- Contradiction between audiences: becomes the same fact told differently to shareholders (AR), the exchange (Reg 30) and the rating agency (CRISIL), or in two consecutive ARs.
- Unusual insistence: becomes the BSE surveillance price-movement query (2026-04-24).
