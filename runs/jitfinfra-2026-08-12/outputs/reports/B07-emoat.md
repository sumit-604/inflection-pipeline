### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Capex under execution (consolidated, 📄 all figures from Notes 1, 3, 12, p.171-172, 222):
- Capital work-in-progress: Rs1,210.03 lakh
- Intangible assets under development (SCA intangibles): Rs19,406.80 lakh
- Capital commitments not yet spent (Estimated contracts remaining to be executed, net of advances): Rs25,586.62 lakh
- **Total capex pipeline = Rs1,210.03 + Rs19,406.80 + Rs25,586.62 = Rs46,203.45 lakh (~Rs462.03 cr)**

Historical fixed-asset turnover (consolidated, FY26):
- Revenue from operations: Rs2,80,802.29 lakh
- Net PP&E: Rs26,075.72 lakh + Net intangible assets (incl. SCA): Rs1,57,776.15 lakh = Rs1,83,851.87 lakh
- Fixed-asset turnover = Rs2,80,802.29 / Rs1,83,851.87 = **1.527x**

Implied incremental revenue = Rs46,203.45 lakh × 1.527 = **Rs70,570 lakh (~Rs705.7 cr)**

As % of current revenue (Rs2,80,802.29 lakh): 70,570 / 280,802.29 = **25.1% above current revenue**

This is a meaningful embedded-growth signal on the arithmetic, but it assumes (a) the
capital-commitment figure converts to productive assets at the historical turnover ratio,
which is optimistic given the group's asset-heavy, long-gestation concession model, and
(b) execution keeps pace — which the FY26 track record (3 completions vs an FY27 target of
11) does not yet support (see F2 below). Treat 25.1% as a ceiling, not a base case.

### FAMILY F — Talent & Organisational

**F1 — Talent density**: MODERATE. JWIL Infra Limited established an Employee Stock Option

a real, board-approved, materially-sized retention mechanic at the subsidiary operating the

**F2 — Execution moat (capex-completion evidence in place of concall promise-delivery
record, per NO-CONCALL MODE)**: MODERATE, cautious. Documented completions in FY26: JWIL
completed Nagapattinam, Guwahati C1 and Chittakhupat projects (📄 self-reported, Board's
Report p.4, per B05 cross-reference — "delivered" grade). Jodhpur civil work reached 70%
completion with major packages (TG Set, Boiler, Air-cooled Condenser, Switchyard) received
and under installation, COD targeted on/before October 2026 (📄 Board's Report p.6 — "partial"
per B05). Kakinada and Nellore concession agreements were signed and groundwork commenced,
but neither is commissioned (📄 Note 15). An independent, non-self-reported signal — credit
rating upgrade to 'A' Stable by both CRISIL and CARE, with weighted average cost of borrowing
falling from 10.19% to 9.16% (📄 Board's Report p.4, MD&A p.84) — supports execution
credibility. Set against this: JWIL's own stated FY27 target is 11 project completions versus
only 3 delivered in FY26 (📄 MD&A p.84), a near-4x step-up in delivery pace with no evidenced
track record at that scale. B05's credibility_grade C is consistent with this — the AR
supports "on track for what's been started," not "proven at the pace now being promised."

### FAMILY G — Financial & Structural

**G1 — War chest**: WEAK. The rating upgrade and lower cost of borrowing are real and
documented (📄 as above), and the SIPCOT HAM's NABFID debt was closed with no corporate
guarantee (📄 MD&A p.84) — a genuinely cleaner financing structure. But the category
definition ("net cash growing while investing") is not met: JWIL's own disclosed Net Debt
rose from Rs118.80cr to Rs214.84cr YoY and its Debt/Equity rose from 0.21 to 0.30 (📄 Board's
Report p.4); at consolidated level the Gearing Ratio rose from 117.83% to 115.63%
(effectively flat/high, 📄 Note 5, p.212) against a backdrop where Gate 0 already flags
ND/EBITDA of 6.71x and interest cover of 1.14x as a deal-breaker. Rating-agency comfort with
capital structure does not equal a war chest; leverage is rising, not falling, even as
investing activity accelerates.

**G2 — WC improvement trajectory**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND — the disclosed trend runs the other
way. Trade receivables rose from Rs83,748.37 lakh to Rs1,27,107.48 lakh, +51.8% YoY (📄 Note
12/24, p.175, 239); contract liabilities (advances from customers) fell from Rs19,566.35 lakh
to Rs15,927.16 lakh, -18.6% YoY (📄 Note 29, p.243); unbilled revenue (contract assets) rose
from Rs42,877.06 lakh to Rs51,375.89 lakh, +19.8% YoY (📄 Note 29). All three data points move
in the wrong direction for this category. Management does state "strengthening operating cash
generation, tightening working-capital and receivables management" as an FY27 priority (🎙️
MD&A p.85) — that is an intention, not evidence, and is carried in the Optionality Register.

### FAMILY H — Ecosystem & External

**H1 — Industry consolidation beneficiary**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND at the company-specific

**H2 — Strategic partnerships**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. The 14 disclosed EPC joint ventures

partnerships with a global leader. No JV meets the category's "global leader / exclusivity /
inbound tech licensing" bar.

**H3 — ESG moat**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND as a distinct emerging category. WtE is inherently a
renewable-energy/waste-diversion business, but no third-party ESG rating, SBTi commitment, or
customer-driven ESG procurement requirement is disclosed — this describes the core business
model, not an emerging edge over the base case already reflected in Gate 0.

  FY26) tracks or slips — this is the direct read on F2 execution credibility.
- **12-24m**: Kakinada and Nellore WtE COD dates (currently undisclosed); first signed
  contracts converting the "exploring" industrial-water and new-geography claims (NTPC,

  Jodhpur all commission on schedule; O&M annuity book scaling materially as the EPC backlog

- **3-5yr**: Full WtE build-out driving a larger annuity-like cashflow base; whether the SBM-
  Urban 2.0 policy window (stated through FY26) is renewed or replaced determines whether the
  grant-funding tailwind persists at the same intensity; sector consolidation under the
  tightened Oct-2025 solid-waste rules is a plausible but currently unevidenced beneficiary
  effect for scaled, compliant operators.

### 6B. Risks to each top-scoring emerging moat, with early warning signs

- **R1 (regulatory tailwinds, score 4.0)**: Company's own risk disclosure flags "concession,
  tariff and policy risk" and "receivables and counterparty risk" from municipal/discom payment
  cycles (📄 MD&A p.85). Early warning: continued receivables growth (already +51.8% YoY) or
  delayed grant disbursement against the committed Jodhpur/Bawana/Kakinada/Nellore amounts.
- **C1 (O&M annuity, score 3.0)**: Depends entirely on EPC projects actually completing and
  transitioning to O&M. If FY27's 11-completion target slips materially, the O&M base does not
  scale on the stated timeline. Early warning: FY27 Board's Report showing a completion count
  well below 11.
- **F1 (ESOP/talent, score 3.0)**: The ESOP grant is unusually large and deep in-the-money
  (Rs10 strike vs Rs400 fair value, Rs4,592 lakh single-year P&L charge at the JWIL level) —
  this is a genuine retention tool but also a minority-dilution risk if repeated at similar
  scale. Early warning: recurring large ESOP tranches without a matching improvement in
  consolidated profitability or NCI equity.
- **F2 (execution, score 2.0)**: The core risk is the pace mismatch itself — 3 delivered in
  FY26 against 11 targeted for FY27. Early warning: any FY27 update showing completions
  tracking below roughly half the target by mid-year.
- **E1 (scale/first-mover, score 2.1)**: The ~50% market-share claim is self-reported with no
  independent citation. Scale in this business does not protect against the dominant risk,
  which is municipal/discom counterparty payment risk, not competitive intensity. Early
  warning: any independent market-share data contradicting the claim, or continued receivables
  deterioration despite the claimed scale advantage.

  both the JWIL and consolidated level, and Gate 0 already flags ND/EBITDA of 6.71x and

  breach of the TOL/EBITDA, DSCR, or secured-coverage covenants referenced in Note 5
  (financial risk management, p.212).

verdict stays AVOID; the emerging-moat evidence should be logged for monitoring (see
Optionality Register and 6A timeline) rather than treated as a reason to revisit the Gate 0
conclusion today.
