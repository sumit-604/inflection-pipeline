## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- Zero doubtful-debt provisioning against a receivables book that grew 73x in two years, alongside FY26
  operating cash flow of negative ₹(1,076.29)L against PAT of ₹3,706.39L (Annexure XX/III, F-21/F-8).
- Premature revenue recognition in both FY24 and FY25, self-disclosed only in the IPO restatement process
  (Annexure IV §3 note g, F-15) — an earnings-management-adjacent pattern, not a single isolated error.
- Auditor-named Section 185 breach: loans to related-party directors in FY24 and FY25 (Examination Report
  §8(iv), F-3).
- Multi-year (FY24-FY26), multi-quarter discrepancies between stock statements filed with lenders and the
  books of account, up to ₹558.60L in a single quarter (Annexure XLIX(vii), F-42).
- MSME payment discipline gap: payables grew 10x in FY26 with growing unpaid statutory interest, and
  management admits no system exists to classify vendors as MSME reliably (Annexure XLVII/XI, F-41/F-20).
- Sixth compliance-timing gap: interest on late payment of income tax grew 10x in FY26 (Annexure XXX/XLI,
  F-27/F-39).
- Unreconciled contradiction on subsidiary existence between Annexure XLIX(xi) and the Non-Current
  Investments note (Annexure XV, F-20).

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal concerning accounting practices. Key concern: receivables grew 20x against 6.7% -- 6.7x
revenue growth while operating cash flow turned negative and doubtful-debt provisioning stayed nil,
compounded by management's own admission that revenue was recognised early in both FY24 and FY25. Key
strength: audit opinions were unqualified and unmodified in all three years, and the restatement cluster
and stock-statement discrepancies were self-disclosed and corrected ahead of the IPO rather than
concealed. Overall accounting quality: 3/10.
