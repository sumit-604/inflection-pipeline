## 1. Independent red-flag list (24 items)

### Material items (16, all MAJOR, no CRITICAL)

**M-01 (MAJOR). The FY26 revenue fall is group offtake, but the MD blames the market.** The MD letter says the fall "reflected global market softness, customer inventory destocking" (AR FY26 p.10). Note 40 tells a different story. Group sales were 191.40 vs 234.91 (derived sum, AR FY26 Note 40 p.93). Third-party revenue was 589.23 vs 590.15 (derived). Group sales explain 43.51 of the 44.43 fall, or 97.9% (derived). USA SLO alone fell from 77.42 to 20.99 (Note 40 p.93).

**M-02 (MAJOR). "Outside the US, markets grew 7%" does not reconcile.** Source: AR FY26 p.10. Outside-India revenue was 200.49 vs 263.80 (Note 41 p.94). Excluding USA SLO it was 179.50 vs 186.38, or -3.7% (derived). Total revenue excluding USA SLO was 759.64 vs 747.64, or +1.6% (derived). No split in the accounts gives 7%.

**M-03 (MAJOR). Three more letter numbers do not reconcile with the filed accounts. The pattern spans two years.**
- The FY25 Chairman letter says "Revenues of ₹779 Crore during FY2024–25" (AR FY25 p.9). The filed figures are revenue from operations 825.06 (AR FY26 p.8 KPI), revenue from contracts 818.04 and sale of goods 791.48 (AR FY26 Note 41 p.94, FY25 column). None equals 779.
- The FY25 MD letter says "domestic sales grew 5% and exports 6%" (AR FY25 p.10). India revenue was 55,423.96 vs 53,344.71 lakh, or +3.9%. Outside India was 26,379.83 vs 24,725.87, or +6.7% (AR FY25 p.82; derived).
- The FY26 MD letter says Roha solar "now meets approximately 50%" and Cuddalore rose "from approximately 62% to 75%. These were targets we set and delivered" (AR FY26 p.10). The FY25 BRSR printed Roha at 57% and Cuddalore at 40% (AR FY25 p.43). The FY25 target was "over 65% renewable energy usage in the near-term" (AR FY25 p.10). The FY26 Chairman prints 58% of total electricity (AR FY26 p.9). Roha fell, and the Cuddalore baseline differs from the prior filing.
- Two readings. (1) This is loose drafting with no intent. (2) The letters round in management's favour. The separating observation is whether the FY27 letters reconcile to the notes. With M-02, four of four checked letter numbers lean favourable.

**M-04 (MAJOR). The company's own AR names the production move into SUDARCOLOR.** The Chairman letter says: "Select AZO products are being transitioned from Frankfurt to Roha, while Cuddalore is building a combination of commodity and specialty products for the broader industrial market" (AR FY26 p.9). The parent says Frankfurt products went "to the Clariant legacy plant" (SUDARSCHEM Feb 2026, Rathi, p.12 of 15). It also says commodity products moved "to the Indian Heubach site in Roha" (SUDARSCHEM May 2026, Rathi, p.10 of 15). SUDARCOLOR is the Clariant legacy entity: its PF trust is the "Clariant Chemicals India Limited Employees Provident Fund" (Results Q2 FY27 note 4 p.6). The plant match is therefore filed evidence, not inference.

**M-05 (MAJOR). Group purchases doubled while the Chairman claims procurement leverage.** The Chairman says ONE Sudarshan "gives us significant procurement leverage" (AR FY26 p.9). The details:
- Group purchases were 159.15 vs 80.72 (derived from Note 40 pp.92-93). Of this, HLD1 was 99.62 vs 45.38, Sudarshan Gujarat 40.95 vs 22.70 and Langelsheim 12.34 vs 0.54.
- That is about 32% of materials consumed plus traded-goods purchases (159.15 of 490.76, derived and approximate).
- New group service charges: 9.49 from Sudarshan Germany Horizons, and a 1.61 royalty to HLD1 (Note 40 p.93).
- Gross margin fell from 42.2% to 39.3% (derived, Results Q4 FY26 p.3).
- The net group trade surplus fell from 154.19 to 32.25 (derived).
- Two readings. (1) Group inputs carry market prices and the margin fall is mix. (2) Transfer prices on purchases move margin to the parent. The separating observation is the H1 FY27 Reg 23 purchase split and its unit prices (NOT FOUND).

**M-06 (MAJOR). Finance and board seats are shared with the counterparty group.**
- Interim CFO Nilkanth Natu has served since 11-May-2026. He has been "associated with the Sudarshan Group for approximately 6 years" (Results Q4 FY26 p.1, Annexure 3 p.13). He is also the SUDARSCHEM CFO (management lists, SUDARSCHEM Nov 2025 p.1 and Aug 2026 p.1).
- The previous CFO left on 9-Mar-2026 (Results Q4 FY26 note 12 p.7). The FY26 year-end close ran with no CFO. The results were approved the same day the interim CFO was appointed.
- Chairman Rathi is the SUDARSCHEM CMD (SUDARSCHEM Feb 2026 p.1). Director Mukhopadhyay is a SUDARSCHEM director (Aug 2026 p.1) and a SUDARCOLOR director (AR FY26 Note 40 p.92).
- Ex-director Velankar signs the parent's filings as "General Counsel and Company Secretary" (SUDARSCHEM cover letters, Nov 2025 to Aug 2026). He resigned from SUDARCOLOR "due to personal reasons" (announcement 1-Sep-2026, Annexure B).
- The counterparty group takes about 25% of sales and supplies about 32% of inputs (M-01, M-05).
- The MD says "I am confident in the integrity of the figures" (AR FY26 p.10). The parent CMD says it is "very difficult to get any financial information" across four SAPs (SUDARSCHEM Aug 2026, p.3 of 14).

**M-07 (MAJOR). Part of the H1 FY27 margin recovery comes from inventory build.**
- FG/WIP/SIT build was 44.66 in H1 FY27 vs 25.67 in H1 FY26 (Results Q2 FY27 p.3). Inventory was 255.22 vs 156.15 (p.4).
- The parent's own "business EBITDA" strips out inventorised overhead. For the acquired group, which holds SUDARCOLOR, reported Q1 FY27 EBITDA of Rs 146 Cr included Rs 18 Cr of it (SUDARSCHEM Aug 2026, Natu, p.4 of 14).
- SUDARCOLOR's absorbed overhead is NOT FOUND.
- Operating EBITDA was 12.2% vs 10.7% (derived, Results Q2 FY27 p.3).

**M-08 (MAJOR). Group allocation moves volume in both directions.** Into India: "some products from Germany ... move ... into India". Out: "given the tariff situation, we are moving some of the products actually to Europe and to Mexico" (SUDARSCHEM Nov 2025, Rathi, pp.10-11 of 11). The outward move fits USA SLO purchases falling from 77.42 to 20.99 (Note 40 p.93). The company exports "through its associated entities" (AR FY26 p.17). [INFERENCE] The origin of the moved products is not stated. SUDARCOLOR's group revenue follows group allocation, in both directions.

**M-09 (MAJOR). The shared Chairman's guidance record is mixed.**
- Nov 2025: Rathi cut acquired-group FY26 EBITDA from about EUR 35m to EUR 25-30m. He said Q3 would be "in the close range probably of Q2" (SUDARSCHEM Nov 2025, pp.3-5 and 9 of 11).
- Feb 2026: an analyst said performance had "materially deteriorated". Rathi replied that they "did not expect this to be at that level" (Feb 2026 p.11 of 15). An analyst's "started with EUR 38 million ... staying at $16 million" went uncorrected (p.7 of 15).
- After that, Q4 delivered EUR 11m against EUR 9-10m projected (May 2026 p.3 of 15). Q1 FY27 was strong, and FY27 guidance was held, not raised (Aug 2026 p.5 of 14).
- Result: two walkbacks, then two beats.

**M-10 (MAJOR). The parent gave no volume-vs-price/restock split for the acquired group. That group contains SUDARCOLOR.**
- May 2026: the restock-vs-demand breakup and the April-May run rate were not given (Gagan Dixit, p.8 of 15). Q4 was called "all volume recovery" (p.10 of 15).
- Aug 2026: an analyst's volume-decline inference was rebutted without a number (Sanjesh Jain, p.6 of 14). The volume/value split went unanswered (Ankur Periwal, pp.7-8 of 14).
- The May "all volume" answer means this is not a clean two-quarter evasion.

**M-11 (MAJOR). The 1-Oct-2026 transfer is tranche one of a stated plan to hold all 70% directly.**
- "Eventually we would like to hold these shares directly from Sudarshan Chemical ... There is nothing further to that" (SUDARSCHEM Aug 2026, Mukhopadhyay, p.9 of 14).
- The analyst asked "Is it like any cash transfer ... to the European entities for any deleveraging purpose". That part went unanswered.

- HLD1 still holds 84,39,385 shares (36.56%) and HLD2 41,09,426 (17.80%) offshore (SAST 29(2) 1-Oct-2026 p.3).
- At Rs 411, the residual 1,25,48,811 shares would be about Rs 515.76 Cr (derived). [INFERENCE] No notice, price or date for that tranche is filed.
- An earlier question on "merge or hive off" got a footprint-only answer (SUDARSCHEM Feb 2026, Rathi, p.8 of 15).

**M-12 (MAJOR). An independent peer has a parallel to the +47.3% quarter.**
- MOL pigment production was 3,233 MT in Q1 FY27 (39% utilisation) and 4,252 MT in Q2 FY27 (51%), up 31.5% (derived). Pigment revenue was 131 and then 161 (MOL Aug 2026 p.2 of 16; MOL Nov 2026 p.3 of 11).
- An analyst said MOL pigment "revenue grew 40% year-on-year", with "production grew 13% year-on-year", and margins "down to 10.1% from 12.1% in Q1". Management did not dispute it. It said the gap was "partly because of the dollar-rupee rate, partly because of the different product mix" (MOL Nov 2026 pp.7-8 of 11).
- Tier: these are analyst figures, unrefuted. The pigment segment is read from the 10.1%/12.1% margin pair, which matches MOL's printed pigment margins. MOL's Q2 FY26 pigment revenue is NOT FOUND.
- Two readings. (1) SUDARCOLOR's jump is base effect plus pass-through, like MOL's. (2) It is group re-allocation. The separating observation is the H1 FY27 Reg 23 split. SUDARCOLOR's falling gross margin (39.1% vs 41.0% Q2, derived) fits pass-through less well than MOL's, so the gap does not close on revenue alone.

**M-13 (MAJOR). Q2 FY27 revenue rose 47.3% with no explanation in the results.** Revenue was 269.76 vs 183.14 (Results Q2 FY27 p.3). The results filings carry no commentary.

**M-14 (MAJOR). H1 FY27 working capital built up and CFO turned negative.** CFO was -24.11 vs -5.65. Inventory moved -99.07, receivables -69.77 and payables +111.71 (Results Q2 FY27 p.5).
