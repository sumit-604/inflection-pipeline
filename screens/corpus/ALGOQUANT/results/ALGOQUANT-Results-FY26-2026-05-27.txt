Note 3: "During the year, the Authorised Share Capital of the Company has been increased from
Rs.350.00 Lakh to Rs.3,000.00 Lakh, pursuant to the approval of the shareholders through Postal
Ballot dated 03-July-2025. The Authorised Share Capital of the Company, which earlier comprised
1,65,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 2 each and 20,000 Redeemable Cumulative Preference Shares of
Rs. 100 each, has been increased to 14,90,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 2 each and 20,000 Redeemable
Cumulative Preference Shares of Rs. 100 each."
Note 4, Share split and issue of Bonus Shares: "The Board of Directors of the Company at its meeting
held on 03-July-2025, a) approved a sub-division of each equity share of face value of Rs.2 each,
fully paid-up into (Two) Equity Shares of face value of Rs.1 each, fully paid-up held as on record
date i.e., 18-August-2025... b) approved issuance of bonus shares, in the proportion of 8:1, i.e.,
8 (Eight) bonus equity share of Rs.1 each, for every 1 (One) existing fully paid-up equity share of
Rs. 1 each held as on the record date (i.e., 18-August-2025), by capitalization of free reserves...
The Company allotted 24,98,63,136 fully paid-up bonus equity shares of Rs.1 each on 19-August-2025
by capitalizing the Company's free reserves."
Note 5: "The Company in line with the requirements of Ind AS 12 has reviewed the un-recognized
deferred tax assets on brought MAT credits and upon considering the benefits available under the
Scheme and considering the principles of prudence, has recognized the deferred tax assets (MAT
credits), which were previously not recognized, during the quarter and year ended 31-March-2026."
