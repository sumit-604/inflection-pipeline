## 1. What was capitalised, and why only part

- Quote (inputs/results/2026-04-29_FY26_audited_results_board-outcome.pdf p.10, note 9): "During the quarter ended 30 June 2025, the Company has been granted license for one of the drug substance production line and bonded warehouse license at biologics manufacturing facility, Bangalore, India which was acquired from Stelis Biopharma Limited and was recorded as capital work in progress in earlier years. Consequently, Rs. 3,438 million has been capitalised as property, plant and equipment which has resulted in a higher depreciation of Rs. 70 million and Rs. 247 million during the quarter and period ended 31 March 2026 respectively."
- Comment: the capitalisation followed the licence of ONE drug-substance production line plus the bonded warehouse. The note does not say how many lines Unit 3 has, or what remains in CWIP.
- Contrast, management (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf p.11): "May, it became operational with all the regulatory licenses in May." AR26 p.192: "the operationalisation and successful licensing of our Unit-3 facility (acquired from Stelis Biopharma) in Q1FY26 ... The facility is now fully functional, catering to multi-product production campaigns". The filed note says one line; management's words read as the whole facility.
- Q4 FY26 call (Concall_Apr_2026_Transcript.pdf p.7), CFO: "on the Unit 3, we've capitalized it as we said in the beginning of the first quarter of FY26. And the impact therefore is -- of the people, et cetera, and all the costs are coming into the P&L."

## 2. What Unit 3 cost, and what went into CWIP

- Quote (Annual_Report_2025.pdf p.257, Note 43): "the Company's Board of Directors entered into a binding term sheet for acquiring Unit 3 biologics manufacturing facility in Bangalore, India, from Stelis Biopharma Limited (SBL). The unit has been acquired effective 01 December 2023 on a slump sale basis at a total cash consideraion of Rs. 5,632." Assets taken over: "Property, plant and equipment 6,207", other assets 104, capital creditors (638), other liabilities (2); value of business taken over 5,671; capital reserve 39.
- FY25 capex, CFO (Annual_Report_2025.pdf p.17): "Around 25% was invested in biologics towards upgradation of Unit 3 and expansion of our PRD labs. Unit 3 is ready for operations, and we expect capitalization to be done in Q1 FY26."
- Comment: acquired PPE of 6,207 was recorded in CWIP (note 9 above says "recorded as capital work in progress in earlier years"), plus later upgrade capex. 6,207 less 3,438 capitalised leaves 2,769 of acquired value (computed), before upgrades. Whether all of it is still in CWIP: NOT DISCLOSED.

### Is the 3,734 in the 2-3 year bucket mainly Unit 3?

- [INFERENCE] At Mar-2026 the 2-3 year bucket holds spend made in FY24 (Apr-2023 to Mar-2024). Unit 3 was acquired 01-Dec-2023, inside FY24, and booked to CWIP. The same vintage sat in the 1-2 year bucket at Mar-2025 (6,486). The vintage fell by 2,752 (computed: 6,486 less 3,734) in FY26, against 3,438 capitalised for Unit 3. The gap of 686 (computed) means the vintages do not reconcile exactly, which could come from part of the 3,438 being later upgrade spend. The pattern fits the 3,734 being mostly the rest of Unit 3. Bayview sits in Syngene USA, not in the standalone book, so it is not in these buckets. The filings do not state what the 3,734 is.

## 5. Licensed versus installed litres

- Installed: "The acquisition will add 20,000 litres of installed biologics drug substance manufacturing capacity for Syngene. The site has the potential for future expansion of up to a further 20,000 litres of biologics drug substance manufacturing capacity. It also includes a commercial scale, high speed, fill-finish unit" (Annual_Report_2025.pdf p.257; also p.26, p.91).
- Licensed: one "drug substance production line" (FY26 results note 9). Its litres: NOT DISCLOSED. Number of drug-substance lines at Unit 3: NOT DISCLOSED.
- Fill-finish: AR25 p.45 says the "commercial-scale fill-finish unit, will be operational in FY26". The Q3 FY26 call (Concall_Jan_2026_Transcript.pdf p.10) says "We have installed as part of our CAPEX programs a sterile fill finish line there", in an answer the transcript labels "Mangalore large molecules" (Mangalore is the small-molecule site elsewhere in the corpus; the site reference is unresolved). Licence and capitalisation status of the fill-finish unit: NOT DISCLOSED.
