### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Netweb designs and builds servers and AI computing clusters in India. It sells them as complete systems to government, cloud firms and banks.
2. Q2 sales were Rs 1,323 Cr, 4.4 times a year ago. Profit after tax was Rs 137 Cr. The half year already beats all of FY26 profit.
3. The operating margin stayed near 15%. Overheads grew far slower than sales and offset a 7 point fall in gross margin.
4. The profit after interest tells a weaker story. Interest on stock funding took the margin after funding cost from 14.7% to 13.1%.
5. Cash went the other way. Operations used Rs 639 Cr of cash in the half year. Cash from operations was minus 2.9 times profit.
6. Inventory is the cause. Stock rose from Rs 805 Cr to Rs 1,931 Cr in six months.
7. The company raised Rs 1,200 Cr from investors in August. It used most of it on working capital by September. Net cash on the company's own measure fell to Rs 14 Cr.
8. Interest cost in Q2 was Rs 23.8 Cr, more than all of FY26. That cost stays until the stock ships.
9. On the call, management said there is "no pressure" on cash. It gave no cash flow figure for the second call running.
10. Management targets Rs 4,000 Cr of sales for FY27. That implies a second half about 30% below the Q2 pace. Management also said the first-half margin is "not to be quoted for the entire year".
11. Your thesis-broken rule #1 says FY27 cash from operations must reach half of profit. The half-year reading fails it by about Rs 750 Cr. Adding is off on either reading.
12. Watch the Q3 change in finished goods and work in progress. A release of Rs 305 Cr or more means stock is turning into sales. Another build means cash stays locked.
