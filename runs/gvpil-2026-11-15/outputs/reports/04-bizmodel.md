## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

### 1A One line
GE Power India fixes, upgrades and supplies spare parts to India's coal power plants (mostly GE-design or BHEL-built units), is winding down its big turnkey projects (FGD pollution-control, new build), and will soon hand its Durgapur factory to JSW Energy.

### 1E Chai-stall-uncle version
Think of a company that built engines for lorry fleets. Now it mostly sells spare parts and repair work for the old engines already on the road. A repair job is small, quick, and pays better than building a whole new lorry. The company is closing its slow, loss-making big building jobs and giving its old factory to a neighbour, who will still make the parts it needs for five years. It also keeps a big pile of cash with its owner's treasury. Most of its reported profit last year came from one-time gains, not from repairs.
