# Cyient DLM: Q2 FY27 in plain words

Source: the committed review, the A5 audit and work/notion-thesis.md (run cyientdlm-q2fy27, commit 1e6f9f35). No agent was re-run.

1. **What the company does.** Cyient DLM builds electronic parts for aircraft, medical machines, defence and industrial customers. The customer gives the drawing, and Cyient DLM builds it to that drawing. Almost all of its work is this kind of build-to-print work. It owns a US unit, the old Altek, which it bought to reach customers abroad.

2. **How this compares with our earlier view.** There is earlier work, but no signed thesis. Our first pass in September found a slow, cash-hungry business that the market had just priced as a fast one. The mental model is unsigned, Halt 1 is pending, and there is no entry price. Before this quarter we named six things to test. This review checks each of them, so treat it as a first read against that list.

3. **What happened this quarter.** Sales grew about 26% on a year ago. The order book hit a record of about Rs 2,800 Cr. Aerospace was the engine, and aerospace plus industrial gave more than all of the growth. The rest of the business shrank.

4. **What improved.** The order book keeps rising, so that test is green. The India business grew its sales by about a quarter. Stock days came down a little.

5. **What got worse.** The company earns less on each rupee of sales. The gross margin fell almost four points, and the operating margin fell to under 9%. Year on year, the cause is the cost of materials. Quarter on quarter, overheads caused most of the fall. Management calls the material jump a one-off. Yet the same deck sells the shift to full assemblies, which use more bought-in material, as the strategy.

6. **The profit is not what it looks like.** More than half of reported profit is a one-off gain. The company cut what it owes the sellers of the US unit, because that unit missed its targets. This is the second year in a row this has happened in the same quarter. Without it, profit still grew, but almost all of that growth was a currency swing. Take out both, and pre-tax profit was nearly flat. The US unit lost money before that gain.

7. **Cash and balance sheet.** Cash is the weak spot. Over the half year, the business turned only a small part of its profit into cash. The India unit took new short-term debt to pay for more stock. Group cash fell. US debt is now mostly due within a year.

8. **What management said.** Management says the outlook for the year is positive, but gives no number. The deck targets a margin of 11% to 13% over FY27 to FY29. The company is moving away from that band, not toward it. It also names new areas, such as chip equipment, robotics and AI data centres. None of them shows any revenue yet.

9. **The red flags in plain words.** A shrinking payout to the sellers of the US unit usually means it missed its plan. So the goodwill on that unit may need a write-down at the year-end test. The stated growth in new orders does not match the order book figures. The filing also has a printing error in last year's staff cost, and one earnings-per-share figure that cannot be right.

10. **Open items.** The audit ran two rounds of fixes, and one small item stays open. In one question, our imagined good answer says the new borrowing was only US refinancing. The filing shows the opposite: the new debt is in India. It changes no number or verdict, and it is flagged in the payload. You also need to rule on how to count working capital days. The filing basis gives 189 days, the deck says 153, and our earlier 169 matches neither.

11. **What this means for the view.** Three of the six tests read red: gross margin, the margin part of the proof gate, and the US unit. The order book is green. Cash waits for your basis ruling. Related-party flows to the parent cannot be seen yet. Nothing here supports the claim that the company is climbing to better work. The decision stays with you at Halt 1.

12. **What to watch next quarter.** The Q3 result is due around mid-January 2027. Watch the gross margin. A move back to about 40% says the drop was a one-off. A margin at or below this quarter's level, with full assemblies still growing, says the strategy itself squeezes margin. Also watch the overhead ratio, the US unit's profit, and whether the India debt is repaid. Run the concall review on the 15 October call first. It may answer the margin question.
