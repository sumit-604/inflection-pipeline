Detailed rationale

CRISIL Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facility of Menon Bearings Limited (MBL) to ‘Positive’ from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at 'CRISIL BBB+'. Also, CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL A2’ rating on the short-term bank facility.

 

The outlook revision reflects the expected improvement in the business risk profile, aided by steady revenue growth, stable operating margin and efficient working capital management.

 

Revenue grew by around 33% to Rs 198 crore in fiscal 2022, driven by increase in sales volume owing to strong demand and higher realisations. The revenue growth has continued in current fiscal as reflected in revenue of Rs.62.9 crore in the first quarter of the current fiscal, as against Rs 47 crore in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal. Operating margin was healthy above 21.5% despite marginal decline.

 

The working capital cycle is managed efficiently, as indicated by gross current assets of 149-168 days for past three fiscals ended 2022. Also reliance on debt continues to be limited. With no large capital expenditure (capex) plans, the financial risk profile should be healthy over the medium term.

 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position and clientele of the company and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by modest scale of operations and revenue concentration in the automobile (auto) sector.

Key rating drivers and detailed description

Strengths:

  • Established market position and clientele: Presence of around three decades in the bearings industry has enabled the company to establish its position across diverse markets and industries. It has healthy relationships with key clients, including John Deere, Tata Motors Ltd (‘CRISIL AA-/Stable/CRISIL A1+’), International Tractors Ltd and Brakes India Ltd (‘CRISIL AA+/Stable/CRISIL A1+’), because of its sound product and service quality.

 

  • Healthy financial risk profile: Capital structure was comfortable, as reflected in moderate networth of around Rs 111.5 crore, and low gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of 0.19 time and 0.48 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2022. Debt protection metrics were strong, as indicated by interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of around 16.03 times and 0.98 time, respectively, in fiscal 2022.

 

Weaknesses:

  • Modest, but improving, scale of operations: Despite improvement in the recent past, revenue remained subdued at Rs 198 crore in fiscal 2022. Scale up of operations will be a key monitorable.

 

  • Revenue concentration in the auto industry: Of the total revenue, 80-90% comes from the auto sector and the remaining from oil and gas, electrical and engineering industries. In the auto segment, the company derives bulk of its revenue from commercial vehicle and tractor segments, which are inherently cyclical. High dependence on a single sector renders revenue and profitability vulnerable to cyclical fluctuations.

Liquidity: Adequate

Cash accrual, expected at Rs 23-30 crore per annum over the medium term, after factoring dividend payout; will comfortably cover yearly term debt obligation of Rs 3-7 crore. Bank limit utilisation averaged 55% over the 12 months through June 2022. Cash and equivalent stood at Rs 8.6 crore as on March 31, 2022.

Outlook: Positive

CRISIL Ratings believes MBL’s scale will continue to improve due to healthy demand from end user industry. MBL will also benefit from strong market position and its established clientele. The financial risk profile is expected to remain healthy

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Increase in revenue by more than 25% and stable operating margin leading to higher net cash accrual
  • Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle

 

Downward factors:

  • Decline in revenue or profitability resulting in net cash accrual below Rs 13 crore
  • Increase in working capital requirement; large, debt-funded capex or acquisition; or sizeable dividend payout; weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity

About the company

Incorporated in 1991 as a part of Kolhapur based Menon group and promoted by Mr Ram Menon and his family members, MBL manufactures auto components such as bearings, bushes, thrust washers and bi-metal strips at its plant in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. It also manufactures aluminum die-cast products.
