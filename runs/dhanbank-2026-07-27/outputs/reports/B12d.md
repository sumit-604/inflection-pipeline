## PART 2: PAGE-ANCHOR RELIABILITY NOTE (systemic, not peer-specific)

The peer transcript .txt extracts carry two overlapping page-numbering schemes: a raw
`===== PDF PAGE N =====` marker (counting the cover-letter/NSE-filing page as PDF page 1) and
the document's own printed footer (`Page X of Y`), which is offset by one from the PDF page
count once the transcript body begins. Example: KTKBANK Q4 FY26, the printed "Page 9 of 17"
footer appears within `PDF PAGE 10`'s text block (the ECL-impact quote, cited by B06 as "p.9",
sits in a range that a raw-PDF-page count would label page 10). This is a page-extraction
artifact common to all 16 files (poppler unavailable per task instructions, so this cannot be
resolved against the original PDF layout), not something specific to B06's sourcing discipline,
and it affects page-citation precision by at most one page in either direction. Every quote
checked was genuinely present in the named file; none of these page-numbering ambiguities rose
to the level of "anchor not found." Flagged as MINOR (systemic, affects reader trust in
pinpoint page cites, does not affect claim veracity).
