- Conclusion: the holder is most probably **Mohit Anand**, SCL's CEO/Director and operator of the Honeywell licence business (AR p.16-17 bio, ✅; Q2 FY27 presentation p.5). Tier: 💬 + derived; NOT confirmed by a primary filing. Sapri Trading has fully exited SCL (derived) and now is a 3.86% CNL holder.

Not a leakage to promoters; it is a conflict and key-person point (licence relationship sits with him). Also, the Q2 FY27 call calls SCL "wholly owned" (transcript l.97-98) versus 77.50% in the AR: a company-document contradiction. ✅
