### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic
Total capex under execution (FY27 guided tranche): ₹25cr (📄/🎙️ — concall-guided, near-term committed programme).
Historical fixed-asset turnover (consolidated, FY26): Revenue ₹10,435.70 lakh ÷ Property, Plant & Equipment ₹746.44 lakh (Investor Pres. Q4&FY26, "Financial Statement Consolidated," slide 30) = **~14.0x**.
Implied incremental revenue = ₹25cr × 14.0x = **₹350cr**, i.e. **~335% above FY26 consolidated revenue (₹104.36cr)**.

**This number is not decision-useful and should not be used downstream.** Gaudium's PP&E base is deliberately tiny (₹7.46cr consolidated) because the hub-and-spoke model is asset-light and lease-based — real estate sits in Right-of-Use Assets, not PP&E, and centres are 100% Rental (Investor Pres. "Center/Hub Wise Details," slide 23: "Owned/Rental: Rental" across all 7 hubs). Applying a fixed-asset-turnover multiple calibrated on a near-zero owned-asset base to new capex produces an economically meaningless multiple-of-revenue output. A more defensible capacity proxy: hub count growing from 7 (FY26) to 26 by end-FY29 = **+271% hub count**, cross-checked against management's own return target of ROCE 35-40% "in the next 3 to 5 years... once all the hubs are completely operational" (Q4 FY26 call p.10) — itself a 🎙️ claim with no interim milestones disclosed.

## SECTION 3: THE 20-CATEGORY SCAN

**FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY**

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability:** NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Gaudium is a clinical-services provider, not a manufacturer; lab equipment (INTEGRA Ti™ for ICSI, Samsung ultrasound machines) is commercially procured, off-the-shelf technology available to any qualified competitor (Hem Securities IPO Report p.1).

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline:** WEAK. GAAT genome-sequencing module capitalized as Intangible Asset Under Development, ₹738.50 lakh as of March-2025 (📄 balance-sheet fact, Deven Choksey IPO Note). But: (i) AR FY24-25 Directors' Report explicitly states "Expenditure on R&D: NIL" and "Efforts made towards technology absorption: NIL" for the same fiscal year (AR FY24-25, Board Report 2024-25, item 13B) — a direct internal inconsistency with the capitalized-intangible disclosure; (ii) ₹250 lakh of GAAT's development cost was paid to Promoter Dr. Manika Khanna, initially booked as "Advance to Supplier" then reclassified as development cost (Deven Choksey note) — a related-party governance flag, not organic IP generation. Separately, 61 trademarks were acquired FROM the promoter via Deed of Assignment dated 17-Feb-2023 for ₹10.25cr, with 3 trademarks still pending registration (same source) — this is a related-party trademark purchase, not new IP creation. Net: real capitalized spend exists (📄) but its credibility as a genuine emerging-IP moat is compromised. Not scored as active.

**A3 — Process innovation:** WEAK-MODERATE. SOP-driven, "cut-copy-paste" clinical-operations model claimed to deliver results "within plus minus 1% of each other" across all centres (🎙️, Q4 FY26 call p.13-15); exit exams for chief embryologist/clinician/nursing staff before centre posting; "freeze-all" protocol reducing repeat ovarian stimulation (🎙️, Q4 FY26 call p.13). No independently audited yield/consistency data; all self-reported.

**A4 — Product platform/modular architecture:** NO EVIDENCE FOUND as an *emerging* item — the hub-and-spoke SOP-replication architecture is the pre-existing operating model (already reflected in B01's confirmed-moat count), not a new capability forming.

This is scored as a moat risk, not an emerging strength (see Section 6B).

**FAMILY H — ECOSYSTEM & EXTERNAL**

**H1 — Industry consolidation beneficiary:** WEAK-MODERATE, 🔍 inference. The ART (Regulation) Act, 2021 and Surrogacy (Regulation) Act, 2021 mandate clinic registration, standardized clinical practice, and gamete-screening/anti-sex-selection safeguards (Deven Choksey IPO Note, "Growth Drivers — Government Support"); management frames this as pushing the ~70%-unorganized market toward the ~30%-organized segment in Gaudium's favour ("it will lead to more and more consolidation in the industry," Q4 FY26 call p.4). Sector-level private-equity consolidation is real and accelerating — KKR (via IVI-RMA), EQT and TPG have all done sizeable IVF-sector deals in India in the past 24 months (Deven Choksey IPO Note, "Major Recent Deals in the IVF/Fertility Sector" table) — but Gaudium is currently a bystander to this M&A wave rather than an acquirer, and explicitly told the May-2026 call it is "not looking at PEs" and does not want dilution (Q4 FY26 call p.14-15). The regulatory benefit is real but shared by every organized competitor (Indira IVF, Nova IVF, Oasis, Cloudnine), which caps its Gaudium-specific moat value.

**H2 — Strategic partnerships:** MODERATE-STRONG — the strongest-evidenced item in the scan. (i) IVF 2.0 (UK) partnership: dated launch 01-Apr-2026, formally inaugurated by Union Minister Shri Nitin Gadkari, founded by named embryologists Dr. Jacques Cohen and Dr. Alejandro Chavez (📄 — concrete, dated, third-party-attended event, Q4 FY26 call p.5); the clinical-outcome benefit (+8-9% first-attempt success) is 🎙️ only, self-reported from an explicitly tiny/early sample ("even if the data is small," same call). (iii) Pan-India patient-financing tie-up with HDFC Bank plus "two or three NBFCs" (🎙️, Q4 FY26 call p.7-8) — no terms, AUM, or patient volumes disclosed.

**H3 — ESG moat:** NOT CREDITED — negative confirmation.
