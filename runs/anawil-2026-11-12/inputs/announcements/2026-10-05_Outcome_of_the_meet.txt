Arihant Capital Investor Conference | Key Outcomes 
1. Strong Order Book and Execution Visibility 
• Order book stood at ~Rs 359.82 Cr as of March 31, 2026 , covering 379 towers  across six 
customers. 
• Subsequently, the company secured its largest order in value terms of Rs 133.98 Cr excluding 
GST, comprising 38 wind turbine towers, with execution expected by Q4 FY27. 
• On a tower count basis, the current order book comprises ~45-50% Pure Conversion, ~30-
35% Conversion + Internals / Paint and ~20% Full Scope / Integrated Supply . The mix is 
different in rupee terms, as Full Scope / Integrated Supply orders have significantly higher 
revenue realisation per tower. 
• Management highlighted continued engagement with customers for additional requirements, 
providing visibility for future order inflows. 
2. Business Model Evolution and Margin Profile 
A key discussion during the conference was the company’s transition from the traditional Pure 
Conversion model towards higher value -added Conversion + Internals and Integrated Supply 
models.

• While EBITDA margins are higher under the Pure Conversion model at ~44%–50%, they are 
lower under Conversion + Internals at ~33%–37% and Integrated Supply at ~14%–16%. As the 
revenue contribution from the latter two business models increase, the overall EBITDA 
margin is expected to moderate; however, EBITDA per tower is expected to increase . 
Therefore, EBITDA per tower provides a more meaningful perspective on the economics and 
profitability of the business than EBITDA margin alone.

• Integrated Supply carries a lower percentage EBITDA margin as the Company also undertakes 
raw material procurement, which significantly increases the revenue base and overall value 
of each tower. 
• The Company is gradually increasing its participation in Conversion + Internals and 
Integrated Supply models, while continuing across all three business models, with EBITDA per 
tower increasing from ~ Rs 22 lakh in FY25 to Rs 29 lakh in FY26 as production increased from 
135 to 210 towers , highlighting the importance of EBITDA per tower as a key measure of 
profitability 
3. Kutch Ramp Up, Location Advantage and Capacity Utilisation 
• The Kutch facility became operational in March 2026, with client approval and certification 
processes currently ongoing. 
• With the addition of Kutch, the company’s total annual manufacturing capacity stands at ~ 
612 towers, providing significant headroom for future volume growth. 
• The location of the company’s facilities provides a strategic advantage, given the high 
transportation cost and logistical complexity associated with oversized wind turbine 
towers. Proximity to key wind project regions can support more efficient and competitive 
execution. 
• FY26 production stood at 210 towers, with utilisation at ~48.2%. The focus remains on ramping 
up Kutch and improving overall capacity utilisation as volumes scale. 
4. Seasonality of the Business 
• Wind tower execution remains seasonally skewed towards H2, with monsoon conditions 
affecting transportation, site access and installation activities. 
• Approximately 70% of FY26 revenue was generated during October to March, highlighting the 
strong H2 contribution to the business. 
5. Increasing In House Value Addition 
• The company is expanding in house manufacturing of tower internals through its Koppal 
Internals Plant, alongside increasing participation in Integrated Supply opportunities. 
• This is expected to improve execution control, reduce outsourcing dependence and increase 
value capture within the business.

About Anawil: 
Anawil Wire & Engineering Limited is a precision-led manufacturer of tubular wind turbine towers 
for the onshore wind energy sector, serving leading WTG OEMs and renewable energy 
companies. With manufacturing facilities at Koppal, Karnataka and Kutch, Gujarat, the Company 
has a combined annual capacity of 612 towers and capabilities to manufacture customised 
towers of up to 140 metres. Its integrated manufacturing and engineering capabilities, expanding 
capacity and focus on  in-house tower internals position Anawil to capture the growing 
opportunities in India’s renewable energy infrastructure market.
