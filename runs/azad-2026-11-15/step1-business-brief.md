# Step 1 business brief: Azad Engineering Ltd (NSE AZAD, BSE 544061)

Run date 2026-11-15. Latest print: Q2 FY27 (quarter ended 30 Sep 2026), results 6 Nov 2026, call 7 Nov 2026, transcript filed 14 Nov 2026 (https://www.screener.in/company/AZAD/consolidated/).

## Business
1. Azad machines mission-critical rotating parts (airfoils, blades, 3D rotating parts) for energy turbine, aerospace and defence, and oil and gas OEMs. Named dedicated-facility customers: Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, GE Vernova Steam Power, Siemens Energy, Baker Hughes (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/azad-q1fy27-97509).
2. FY26 standalone revenue Rs 590.38 Cr (+30.3%). Consolidated Rs 602.98 Cr (+31.8%). EBITDA margin 36.9% standalone, 37.4% consolidated (https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/azad-fy26-94086).
3. Segment mix FY26: Energy and Oil and Gas Rs 481.13 Cr (81.5%). Aerospace and Defence Rs 101.26 Cr (17.2%). Exports 93.0%. The company does not split energy from oil and gas (same source).
4. Margin source: gross margin before depreciation 45.2% in Q2 FY27, EBITDA 37.5% (https://compoundingai.in/market-news/azad-q2-fy27-earnings-call). Founder says the same product lines ran at 18-19% EBITDA years ago; he credits shop-floor process skill (Q1 FY27 transcript, p.~11, https://azad.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Azad-Transcript-Q1FY27.pdf).
5. Customer concentration: NOT FOUND in a filed source today. One unverified site claims top-5 about 82%, top-1 35% (https://dcf-analysis.com/products/azadns-swot-analysis). Management says Azad holds "under 2%" wallet share per customer (call summary above).

## Guidance and engine
10. Guidance: revenue growth over 25% (reiterated Q1 and Q2 FY27); EBITDA margin 33-35%+; Rs 1,300 Cr+ revenue run-rate by end FY28; range update promised in Q3 FY27 (https://compoundingai.in/market-news/azad-q2-fy27-earnings-call).
11. Working capital guidance (Q1 FY27 call, 8 Aug 2026): "targeting H1 around 200 days" and "160 to 180" days by H2. Management states this is "largely back on the bill discounting facility", taking debtor days from 170-180 to 90 (Q1 FY27 transcript p.10-11, URL above). Q2 call: ICICI Bank and DP World discounting lines "in reserve", usable from Q4 FY27; cash generation from Q4 FY27 (Q2 call summary above).
12. THE ENGINE: six customer-dedicated facilities, 44,800 sq m, all commissioned (two new GE Vernova units inaugurated Sep 2026). Throughput starts Q3 FY27, revenue ramp from Q4 FY27, full ramp Q2 FY28. H1 FY27 capex Rs 139.3 Cr. QIP Rs 700 Cr: Rs 621.8 Cr deployed, Rs 78.2 Cr in FDs. Order book about Rs 6,500 Cr (rolling, 5-7 years) (https://compoundingai.in/market-news/azad-q2-fy27-earnings-call).
13. Options: Rolls-Royce aero aerofoil qualification in "last leg"; Mitsubishi hot-section work under 8-year contract; BHEL rate contract about Rs 52.68 Cr; GTRE turbojet in testing (same source).
14. Market: CMP Rs 2,456 (13 Nov), market cap Rs 15,861 Cr, PE 116 (https://www.screener.in/company/AZAD/consolidated/).
