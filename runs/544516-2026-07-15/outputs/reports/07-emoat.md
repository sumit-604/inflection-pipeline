### 2B. Utilisation trajectory

Capacity utilisation (installed-units basis, AR p.8062-8064 and Inv. Pres. slide 3): FY23 77% → FY24 79% → FY25 85% → FY26 90%. Two-shift operations began after September 2025 and were "effectively used for last four months" of FY26 (Jun-2026 call); management states the same two-shift regime, if sustained for the full FY27, closes "an easy gap of achieving Rs400-450cr" without further capex, with the balance to Rs500cr expected to come from the channel-partner model rather than new machinery. This is 🎙️ management assertion, not an independently verifiable capacity model — installed capacity is reported in "Nos." of a customised product mix that the company itself states cannot be broken down by product (AR p.8058-8059).

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Two methods, both shown transparently because they diverge sharply:

**Method 1 — management's own stated multiplier.** New-facility capex Rs30-35cr (midpoint Rs32.5cr) × management's claimed "~4x" revenue-to-investment turnover (Nov-2025 call, Maitri Shah Q&A) = ~Rs130cr of implied incremental revenue. As a % of FY26 actual revenue (Rs319.6cr): **~40.7%**. This is a 🎙️ MANAGEMENT CLAIM, made in passing with no supporting calculation shown, and should be treated as directional only.

**Method 2 — historical fixed-asset turnover.** FY26 revenue Rs319.6cr ÷ FY26 tangible fixed assets Rs42.0cr (Inv. Pres. balance sheet, slide 33) = ~7.6x. Applying this to the total capex under execution (IPO machinery Rs13.68cr + new facility midpoint Rs32.5cr = Rs46.18cr, excluding the BBBS JV investment which is not a fixed-asset capex item) implies ~Rs351cr of incremental revenue, or **~110% of current revenue**. This figure is almost certainly not usable at face value — Airfloa's asset base is unusually small and young (post-IPO, recently listed) relative to revenue, so the historical turnover ratio likely overstates true marginal capacity-driven revenue.

**Conclusion for Stage 9 consumption**: neither figure should be taken as a hard number. The company's own directional claim (~40% embedded growth from the new facility alone) is the more conservative and traceable of the two and is used as `capex_embedded_growth_pct` in the YAML block below, flagged explicitly as a 🎙️ management claim, not an independently derived figure.
