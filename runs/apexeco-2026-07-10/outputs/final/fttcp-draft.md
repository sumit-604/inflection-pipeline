### 1C. Cash Conversion (THE CRITICAL ONE)

| Period | PAT (INR Cr) | CFO (INR Cr) | CFO/PAT | Trade receivables (INR Cr) | Receivable days | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY24 | 6.63 | 6.69 | 1.01 | — | turnover 6.77x (~54d) | B01; B02 Note 29 |
| FY25 | 8.56 | -5.24 restated (-14.08 filed) | neg | 22.13 | ~114 | B01/B03; reader, AR Note 10/29 |
| FY26 | 17.02 | 6.77 | 0.40 | ~16.76 | ~41 | reader (screener/BS); B01 block_b_trend |

Cumulative FY20 to FY26 CFO/PAT 0.41 (reader verified: cumulative CFO 14.84 Cr on cumulative PAT
36.09 Cr). **Ruling: DETERIORATING. Confidence MODERATE.**

The two year path is 1.01 to negative to 0.40, dominated by the FY25 IPO year disruption, and the
cumulative sits at 0.41. That does not meet the IMPROVING bar of a trajectory heading toward 0.50x.
Contrary and important: within FY26 the trade receivable line actually compressed, from 22.13 Cr and
about 114 days to about 16.76 Cr and about 41 days, and B01's working capital day computation shows a
72 day improvement FY25 to FY26. That within year compression is the seed of the forward catalyst and
is why the backward DETERIORATING call is MODERATE not HIGH.

**Framework test, stated explicitly. If Apex stopped growing tomorrow, would working capital days
still be elevated?** Mostly no, with a residual tail.
- The retention component is structural to milestone billed EPC. FY25 trade receivables carried
  1,056.03 lakh of retention against 2,212.83 lakh total, about 48% (B02, AR Note 10 p.55). Retention
  releases on project completion and, at zero growth, is not replaced by new project retention, so it
  unwinds over 12 to 24 months.
- Trade receivable days themselves are not elevated at the FY26 print, about 41 days, so the pure
  collection cycle is not the problem.
- The elevation sits in total working capital, which management quoted at 61.72 Cr, about 151 days of
  revenue (B05, B12b). The gap between 41 day trade receivables and 151 day total working capital is
  inventory, contract assets or unbilled revenue, retention money and advances. Most of that unwinds
  at zero growth, so the honest answer is mostly no, the days would fall materially, with a residual
  EPC tail that never fully disappears.

**Cash determination (the gate flag). Recommended: GROWTH-INDUCED. Confidence LOW.**
For it: retention and unbilled mechanics are documented and release on completion; trade receivable
days already compressed in FY26; the build was funded by IPO equity not stressed borrowing, the
balance sheet is debt free with 35.06 Cr cash (B03, B05).
Against it: FY26 CFO/PAT is still only 0.40x in a year of 109% growth; management named no working
capital program, no factoring facility and no payment term change across three calls (B05); zero
doubtful debt provision was taken despite the FY25 receivables surge (B02 Note 30); the single best
adjudicator, a credit rating rationale with working capital commentary, does not exist; and there is a
live data tension, the screener and balance sheet FY26 trade receivables of about 16.76 Cr do not sit
easily beside management's 61.72 Cr working capital figure, so the FY26 signed balance sheet
receivables, unbilled and retention lines should be reconciled directly before this is closed.
Because the adjudicator is absent and INDETERMINATE cannot resolve to a clean pass (CLAUDE.md), the
gate level cap stays at INDETERMINATE until the H1 FY27 print, even though my read of the mechanics
leans growth induced. Falsification metric: H1 FY27 CFO/PAT below 0.7x while total working capital
days climb faster than revenue.

### 1D. ROCE / Capital Efficiency, with capex overlay

| Period | ROCE | Capex (INR Cr) | Cause of the move | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY24 | 59.61% | negligible | pre IPO, very small equity base | B02 Note 29 |
| FY25 | 24.70% | ~0.10 | IPO fresh issue (listed Dec 2024) bloated capital employed; denominator event | B02 Note 29; reader |
| FY26 | 33.39% disclosed (35.12% computed) | ~1.96 | EBIT nearly doubled; IPO cash absorbed into working capital | B04/B01; B12a; reader |

The overlay is the decisive fact. Capex is negligible across the window, about 1.96 Cr in FY26 and
about 0.10 Cr in FY25. There is no capital deployed not earning, no plant under commissioning, no BOO
treadmill. So the FY25 ROCE collapse was purely a denominator event from the IPO equity raise, and the
FY26 recovery is numerator driven, EBIT growth on an asset light base, plus the raised cash being
absorbed into working capital.

**Ruling: SUSTAINED at premium. Confidence MODERATE.**
The FY24 59.6% is an artefact of a tiny pre listing equity base and is not a level a listed company
could hold, so it should not anchor the trajectory. The meaningful comparison is FY25 to FY26, 24.70%
to about 33 to 35%, a recovery to a premium level well above the 20% asset light bar, and rising.
DECLINING is rejected because FY26 is up, not down. STRUCTURALLY LOW is rejected because 33% is far
above 20%. TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED is a weak fit because there is no depressed current level and no
capex to commission.
Contrary: if the pre IPO 59.6% is treated as the anchor, the two year picture is a large decline, and
the premium ROCE is partly hostage to the working capital that dominates capital employed in this
asset light model, so it shares a failure mode with the cash transition. Flag SHARED CATALYST: a
working capital unwind would lift both the cash transition and ROCE.
