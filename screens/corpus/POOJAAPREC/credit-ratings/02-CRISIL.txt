 Detailed r ationale 

 Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable /Crisil A2 ’   rating s to the   bank facilities of   Poojaa Precision Engg . Limited (PPEL). 

 The rating s reflect   the   extensive experience of the promoters in the auto components and ancillary industry along with diversified product profile , comfortable financial risk profile and moderate working capital cycle of the company . These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicali ty in the end - user industries , susceptibility to customer concentration risk and exposure to project risk . 

 Analytical a pproach 

 Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of PPEL . 

 Key rating drivers -  Strengths 

 Extensive experience of the promoters : 

 The promoters   experience of over three decades in the engineering and auto components and ancillary industry has helped them establish strong relationships with customers leading to repeat orders.   The company   has   an   establ ished customer base   and   has   added clients   across various industries in domestic markets.   This has led to revenue growth   to   around Rs   293   crore   in f iscal 2026   from Rs.130   crore in fiscal 2022.   Revenue is further expected to increase over   the medium term driven by healthy demand, continuous addition of new stock keeping units (SKUs) , customers   and increase in manufacturing capacity. 

 Diversified product profile and end-user industry : 

 The company offers a diverse range of 600+ SKUs — such as cylinder head cover , elbow assembly, clutch housing, housing bearing sleeve assembly, conductor — that cater to automotive, defen c e, energy, healthcare and engineering goods industries; this should strengthen the business risk profile over the medium term. Over the years the company has diversif ied its SKUs and end user industries to protect   revenues   from slowdown in demand in a single industry. 

 Comfortable financial risk profile : 

 With continuous accretion to reserves the financial risk profile is   comfortable   with networth of around Rs.   133   crores   as on March 31, 202 6 . The capital structure has been healthy, with gearing of 0. 31 time and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio of 0.7 4 time as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics were comfortable due to low leverage and healthy profitability. The interest coverage ratio was at 13.56 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio at 0. 91 time for fiscal 2026. The metrics are expected to moderate but remain comfortable over the medium term . The f inancial risk profile may continue to be comfortable , despite a major debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) plan for capacity addition. 

 Moderate working capital cycle : 

 Gross current assets have been 85 – 139 days over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2026 , driven by   receivables   of   42–64   days and inventory of 42 – 84 days . The working capital is supported by payables of 35 – 63 days.   Efficient working capital management,   amid increasing scale of operations,   remains a key monitorable. 

 Key rating drivers -  We a knesses 

 Expo sure to cyclicality in end-user industries : 

 The   demand for PPEL   products depends on key end-user industries — automotive , defence, energy, healthcare and engineering goods. The performance of   these industries   are driven by factors   such as   economic policies, demand and supply situation,   prices of raw materials.   Any downturn in any of the application industries or macro-economic conditions impacting the application industries   will   impact the company ’s topline and its profitability.   However, over the years the   company is continuously diversifying its products and improving its application industries. 

 . 

 Susceptibility to customer concentration risk : 

 Despite diversifying its clientele PPEL generates 75 – 78 % of its revenue from its top five customers, with the largest customer contributing 30 – 35 %. While the company is a key supplier to these customers, any adverse change in their demand patterns or vendor selection policies could impact its business risk profile and will remain a key monitorable over the medium ter m. However, longstanding relationships with top customers and efforts to diversify the clientele should support the business risk profile. 

 Exposure to project risk : 

 PPEL is expected to incur a siz e able capacity addition capex , which is large compared to its networth. Project c ompletion and stabili s ation risk s are likely to persist . Ability of the company to finish th is project with no time or cost overruns will remain monitorable. 

 Liquidity Adequate 

 The fund -based bank limit utilisation   was moderate at around 85 % for the past 1 2 months through June   2026 , with peak utilisation reaching 9 8 % in January 2026 .   C ash   accrual   is expected at   more than   Rs   45   crore   per annum , against yearly debt   obligation   of   Rs   7 –1 6 crore over the medium term.   Current ratio   stood   healthy at   1. 56 times and cash and cash equivalents at Rs 0. 36 crore as on   March 31, 2026.   Liquidity should remain supported by the surplus in cash accrual and bank lines and cash and cash equivalents. 

 Outlook Stable 

 PPEL w ill continue to benefit from th e extensive experience of its promoters   and   their   established relationship with clients , established market position and comfortable financial risk profile .
