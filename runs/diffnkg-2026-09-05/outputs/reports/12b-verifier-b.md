### Main company

1. **Unit 4 heavy-engineering commissioning slipped at every call, never called a delay.**
   Nov-2025: "plant should be up by the end of Q4 [FY26]" (Nov-2025, Garg, PAGE 7).
   Feb-2026: "commissioning ... by the end of FY26" (Feb-2026, Garg, PAGE 4).
   May-2026: "by end of Q1 2027" (May-2026, Garg, PAGE 3-4), framed "progressing well and as planned."
   Aug-2026: "phase-wise manner ... not in a 100% manner right now" (Aug-2026, Garg, PAGE 9).
   Load-bearing capex behind the growth thesis. WEIGHT: HIGH.

2. **EBITDA margin improvement promised repeatedly, delivered never; margin has fallen from an
   18% historical peak to ~13%.** Promised +100-200bps by FY27 (Feb-2026, PAGE 8), narrowed to
   +80-100bps (May-2026, PAGE 16), re-pushed to "next year, year and a half" (Aug-2026, PAGE 16).
   Q1 FY27 consol margin fell to 12.85% from 13.12% (Aug-2026, Mehta, PAGE 6). Analyst Ram Singh:
   "we had peak margins of 18% and from 18% now we are around 13%" (Aug-2026, PAGE 15). WEIGHT: HIGH.

3. **Railway / Vande Bharat conversion timeline stretched every call.** 3-5 months (Feb-2026,
   PAGE 6) -> 6-9 months (May-2026, PAGE 9) -> 9-12 months (Aug-2026, PAGE 16). Three LOIs received
   Feb-2026 (PAGE 6); only "one or two small orders" booked by Aug-2026 (PAGE 16). WEIGHT: MEDIUM.

4. **Earnings quality: reported PAT is materially flattered by non-operating items, and this is
   recurring.** Three separate non-operating props:
   (a) ~INR67cr of unutilized IPO proceeds parked in fixed deposits (Aug-2026, Mehta, PAGE 17),
   generating other income;
   (b) a one-time ~INR5cr dividend from the 100% Singapore subsidiary booked in standalone
   Q1 FY26 (Aug-2026, Mehta, PAGE 7 and PAGE 14) — its absence caused standalone Q1 FY27 PAT to
   FALL to INR99.79mn from INR142.04mn even as revenue rose 30.72% (Aug-2026, PAGE 7);
   (c) share of associate profit (LSN Diffusion) spiking to ~INR4.4cr in Q1 FY27 against a normal
   INR1-2cr, management conceding "plus two ... extra which has come in for this particular quarter"
   (Aug-2026, PAGE 10-11).
   The footprint shows up as PAT growth running far ahead of EBITDA growth: 9M FY26 consol PAT
   +49.55% vs EBITDA +12.60% (Feb-2026, PAGE 5); FY26 consol PAT +39.87% vs EBITDA +21.18%
   (May-2026, PAGE 4). Reported bottom-line momentum overstates operating momentum. WEIGHT: HIGH.

5. **Tejorup Sunmay defence stake — capital allocation into a pre-revenue missile developer on a
   soft valuation basis, no propulsion/missile credentials.** 10% stake, company valued INR40-45cr
   (Feb-2026, Mehta, PAGE 6). Pre-revenue, valued on "estimated value of the PSO ... and the
   estimated orders if the prototype is done" (Feb-2026, Mehta, PAGE 6). Management concedes it has
   no propulsion technology (Feb-2026, PAGE 9). Two analysts (Varun Jain, Bijal Shah) pressed on
   credentials and valuation. WEIGHT: MEDIUM.

6. **Working capital heavier than peer.** Analyst Gunit Singh: DEL debtor days ~113, inventory
   ~123, vs Ador Fontech ~50-53 / ~70 (May-2026, PAGE 15). Management counters with 98 debtor days
   and 66 inventory days for FY26, targeting 80-85 (May-2026, PAGE 15-16). Even on management's own
   numbers the cycle sits well above the named peer. WEIGHT: MEDIUM.

7. **Top-10 customer concentration not producible on request.** Management had no current figure,
   fell back on the RHP's ~70% (Aug-2026, Garg/Mehta, PAGE 19-20). WEIGHT: MEDIUM.

8. **The fastest-growing segment is more cyclical than the "sticky repeat customer" narrative
   implies.** Heavy engineering is INR159cr of the INR209cr order book (~76%) (Aug-2026, PAGE 3-4),
   yet management states heavy engineering is "70% ... new project build, 30% ... spare parts"
   (Aug-2026, Garg, PAGE 18). The "over 80% of revenues from repeat customers" claim (every call)
   and the maintenance-stickiness framing sit uneasily against a mix now driven by capex-linked
   new-build orders. WEIGHT: MEDIUM (touches the quality-ladder / re-rating durability question).

9. **Order-book figure inconsistency across adjacent dates.** ~INR200cr as of 30-Apr-2026
   (May-2026, PAGE 3) vs INR174cr as of Mar-2026 (Aug-2026, PAGE 3). Not reconcilable from the
   transcripts. WEIGHT: LOW.

10. **Leasing-of-equipment model (a prospectus revenue line) quietly dropped, revealed stalled
    only on direct question.** The pilot cement customer chose upfront purchase plus AMC over
    leasing (Aug-2026, Garg, PAGE 18). WEIGHT: LOW-MEDIUM.

11. **Standalone vs consolidated divergence.** Standalone FY26 revenue +12.06% and Q4 PAT -3.05%
    (May-2026, PAGE 5-6) against consolidated FY26 revenue +21.28% (May-2026, PAGE 4). Analyst Neil
    Bahal flagged "standalone ... flattish year" (May-2026, PAGE 7). Consolidated is carried by
    subsidiaries/associate (Philippines, Singapore, Turkey — Aug-2026, PAGE 11). WEIGHT: MEDIUM.

12. **Two of three units run near-idle.** "Unit 1 and Unit 2, Unit 3 we are not operating at
    optimal ... utilization is around 2% or 3%" (Aug-2026, Mehta, PAGE 20). Sits against the
    headline "85% utilization" story used to justify the expansion. WEIGHT: LOW.

13. **Guidance is admitted sandbagging.** "We always believe in overperforming and undercommitting"
    (May-2026, PAGE 9; near-verbatim Aug-2026, PAGE 14). Credibility-positive, but headline growth
    guidance carries little forecasting information. WEIGHT: LOW-MEDIUM.

    lag. WEIGHT: MEDIUM (informative silence, not a contradiction).
