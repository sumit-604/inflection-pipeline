### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic
Per the framework: capex under execution (documented/committed) × historical fixed asset turnover
= implied incremental revenue.

- Capex under execution (📄 committed per AR Note 1, p.90) = **Rs 0** (NIL contractual commitments
  as at 31-03-2025).
- Historical fixed asset turnover (FY25) = Revenue from Operations Rs503.15cr (AR Note 20, p.83) ÷
  Net PP&E Rs83.38cr (AR Balance Sheet, p.75) = **6.03x**.
- Implied incremental revenue from documented committed capex = Rs0 × 6.03 = **Rs0 → 0% embedded
  growth** on the strict, documented-commitment basis.
- For transparency, if the FY25 *trailing actual* capex (Rs28.87cr, already spent and already
  reflected in the FY25/FY26 asset base and the FY26 revenue run-rate) were mechanically run
  through the same multiple: Rs28.87cr × 6.03 = ~Rs174.1cr, or ~34.6% of FY25 revenue. This is
  **not** a forward/incremental signal — it is capex already embedded in the FY26 41.7% revenue
  growth print that B05 flags as substantially a low-base effect from the TMT mill restart, not a
  fresh growth driver. **capex_embedded_growth_pct is set to 0%** on the documented-commitment
  basis; the trailing-capex arithmetic is shown only to demonstrate it is not additive to a
  forward growth thesis.

### 4B. Government policy tailwinds
- **12% provisional safeguard duty** on steel imports from China, South Korea, Japan, effective
  April 2025 (AR MD&A, p.39) — industry-wide protection, shared by every domestic steel producer,
  no Rathi-specific quantification of benefit.
- **PLI scheme for specialty steel**: industry commitments of ~Rs29,530cr against Rs27,106cr
  identified investment, ~18,000 direct jobs expected sector-wide (AR MD&A, p.40-41) — **no
  evidence Rathi is an enrolled participant or beneficiary**; this is background industry context
  only.
- **Steel Scrap Recycling Policy (2019)** and **National Green Hydrogen Mission**: general
  sector-wide policies referenced in the AR (p.42), not company-specific enrolment or benefit
  disclosed.
- **Domestically Manufactured Iron & Steel Products (DMI&SP) Policy**: government-procurement
  preference for domestic steel — general, not shown to be accessed by Rathi (no government/PSU
  contract disclosed).

### 4C. Regulatory moat assessment
Active but **not exclusive** — the safeguard duty and BIS/GreenPro certification framework
genuinely support pricing and market access, but every one of these is available to, or already
held by, competing domestic steel producers. There is no evidence of a Rathi-specific regulatory
advantage (e.g., an exclusive licence, a first-mover regulatory position, or a confirmed PLI
award). Time to kick in: safeguard duty is already active; sustainability of the duty itself is a
policy decision outside company control and subject to renewal/removal risk.
