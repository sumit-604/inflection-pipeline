Accord Transformer & Switchgear Receives Vendor Approval from 
UGVCL, Strengthening Presence in India's Power Distribution Sector 
   
Gurugram, June 24, 2026  – Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited (ATS), one of India's leading 
manufacturers of transformers and switchgear solutions, has received Vendor Registration Approval from 
Uttar Gujarat Vij Company Limited (UGVCL) for the manufacturing and supply of 500 KVA, 11/0.415 KV Level-
1 CRGO Copper Wound Distribution Transformers. The approval marks another significant milestone in the 
Company's growth journey and further strengthens its position in India's power transmission and distribution 
ecosystem. 
 
UGVCL, a subsidiary of Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (GUVNL), is one of the key electricity di stribution 
companies serving the state of Gujarat. The vendor approval enables Accord Transformer & Switchgear 
Limited to participate in tenders floated by UGVCL and other associated power distribution companies under 
the erstwhile Gujarat Electricity Board framework, subject to applicable tender conditions. 
 
The approval follows a detailed evaluation process, including factory inspection and assessment of the 
Company's manufacturing capabilities, quality systems, and operational standards. The registration has been 
granted for a period of five years, reflecting confidence in the Company's product quality and manufacturing 
infrastructure. 
 
This development further expands the Company's approved vendor base across utilities and power sector 
entities, creating opportunities to participate in power distribution projects and strengthening its presence in 
one of India's most industrialized and power-intensive states. 
 
Mr. Pradeep Kumar Verma, Chairman & Managing Director, Accord Transformer & Switchgear 
Limited, commented, "The approval from UGVCL is a significant achievement for our Company and 
reinforces our commitment to delivering high-quality and reliable power distribution solutions. Gujarat 
continues to be one of India's most important industrial and infrastructure growth hubs, and this approval 
enhances our ability to participate in utility-driven projects across the region. We believe this recognition 
will further strengthen our market presence and contribute meaningfully to our long-term growth strategy." 
 
The approval aligns with the Company's strategy of expanding its footprint across state utilities, power 
distribution companies, renewable energy projects, and industrial infrastructure segments while continuing 
to deliver reliable and efficient electrical solutions.” 
 
 
About Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited: 
Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited is a leading provider of comprehensive energy solutions, 
specializing in the design, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning of transformers, substations, and 
switchgear systems. With advanced manufacturing facilities in Bhiwadi and a strong presence across power 
transmission, renewable energy, and industrial sectors, the Company is committed to delivering reliable, 
efficient, and future-ready infrastructure solutions.
