Dear Sir/Madam, 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR Regulations"), 
we would like to inform you that a minor fire incident occurred at approximately 9:50 p.m. on 9th June 2026, 
in Unit-9B, the Company's Minoxidil manufacturing unit 
 
No injuries were reported to any employee or other personnel, and no significant damage to plant/property 
was caused as a result of the incident. The incident was promptly attended to and brought under control 
by the Company's site emergency response team in accordance with the established Onsite Emergency 
Management Plan. The incident was confined to Unit-9B and did not affect the operations of the 
Company's other manufacturing units/plants, all of which continue to operate normally.  
 
As a precautionary measure and to facilitate the necessary inspection, assessment, and restoration 
activities, operations at Unit-9B are expected to remain temporarily suspended for approximately 8–10 
days. The Company is taking all necessary steps to restore operations at Unit-9B at the earliest. 
 
A detailed investigation is currently in progress to determine the root cause of the incident and to identify 
appropriate corrective and preventive actions as may be considered necessary.  
 
Additional details as per SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated 13th July 
2023, and SEBI Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/2023/120 dated 11th November 2024, is 
mentioned in Annexure-A. 
 
This is for your information and records.
