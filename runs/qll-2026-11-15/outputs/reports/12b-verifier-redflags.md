### MAJOR items

**1. PAT margin guide cut within 15 weeks with no reason given, and "guiding" became "aspiration".**
- MD&A 20 May 2026 [page 3]: "we are guiding to consolidated revenue growth of 35–40% ... EBITDA margin in a range of 25 –27% and PAT margin in a range of 15 –17%".
- AR 2026 chairman letter [page 23] (AR p.18): "PAT margin of 14-15%".
- AR 2026 MD&A [page 73] (AR p.68): "the Company has indicated an aspiration ... PAT margin in the range of 14%-15%. These are management expectations".
- Revenue and EBITDA ranges are unchanged.
- Readings: (a) the cut reflects a truer finance-cost and tax view; (b) management saw H1 trading and moved the number without telling shareholders why. Separator: a stated reason at the 24 Nov 2026 call.

**2. Medium-term targets walked back in two steps.**
- May 2025 deck [page 13]: "Way Forward: FY25-26 & FY26-27 ... 2X Revenue in every 2 years & profitability improvement - 30% EBITDA margin".
- MD&A 20 May 2026 [page 3]: "lifting consolidated operating margin above 30% over the next three years, generating at least 30% of revenue from overseas customers by FY31, and operating the business on a cash-surplus, debt-free basis by FY32".
- AR 2026 [page 24] (AR p.19): "₹500+ Crore of consolidated revenue by FY31, while improving operating margins, increasing international business and progressively strengthening our cash-generation capabilities".
- FY26 delivered a 23.5% consolidated EBITDA margin (MD&A [page 2]).
- Readings: (a) prudence after a heavy build year; (b) the targets were never anchored to a plan. Separator: whether any number returns at the 24 Nov 2026 call.

**3. The FY27 margin guide rests on the seasonally strong half.**
- MD&A [page 3]: "the FY27 ranges essentially hold that exit run-rate across the full year", where the H2 FY26 exit was 25.7% EBITDA and 14.1% PAT.
- QLL revenue is weighted to H2 every year. Consolidated: H1 FY26 Rs 54.24 Cr, H2 FY26 Rs 70.27 Cr (MD&A [page 2]). Standalone: H1 FY25 Rs 20.9 Cr, H2 FY25 Rs 24.9 Cr (May 2025 deck [page 5]).
- Margin follows the same pattern. Consolidated EBITDA margin was 20.7% in H1 FY26 and 25.7% in H2 FY26 (MD&A [page 2]). Standalone was 21% in H1 FY25 and 29% in H2 FY25 (May 2025 deck [page 5]).
- Peer evidence: "H2 tends to always do better typically" (VIMTALABS, Q2 FY27 call 30 Oct 2026, Harita Vasireddi, [page 6]). "Q4 tends to be high seasonally, whether it is food or consumption of the budgets by the Indian companies" (VIMTALABS, Q1 FY27 call 20 Jul 2026, Harita Vasireddi, [page 16]).
- H1 FY27 consolidated EBITDA margin was 21.6% (derived: 855.11 + 387.02 + 397.11 - 80.94 = 1,558.30 on revenue 7,226.65; H1 FY27 results [page 11]). PAT margin was 9.1% (660.01 / 7,226.65).
- Same-half comparison: EBITDA margin 20.7% to 21.6% (+0.9 pt); PAT margin 8.8% to 9.1% (+0.3 pt).
- Readings: (a) seasonal. H1 is always the weak half, and the same-half margin rose. To reach the 25% floor for the full year, H2 FY27 EBITDA margin must be 27.4% to 27.6%. Derivation: 25% of FY27 revenue at +35% to +40% (16,810 to 17,432 Lakh), less H1 EBITDA of 1,558.30, divided by H2 revenue of 9,583 to 10,206 Lakh. That is +1.7 to +1.9 pts on H2 FY26's 25.7%. (b) The guide was set off a seasonal peak and will be missed. Separator: H2 FY27 consolidated EBITDA margin against 27.4% to 27.6%.

**4. The ITCPL consolidation date is misstated, so the headline growth includes a base effect.**
- MD&A [page 2]: "Interstellar Te sting Centre was consolidated from H1 onwards".
- AR MD&A [page 70] (AR p.65): "The consolidation of Interstellar Testing Centre Private Limited ("ITCPL") from FY26 onwards".
- May 2026 deck [page 8]: "Consolidation applicable from H1-26 onwards. Hence comparative figures for H2-25 are not available". The same deck at [page 7] prints H2-25 consolidated revenue of Rs 45.2 Cr.
- Filed fact: ITCPL has been a subsidiary since 11 Sep 2024, and FY25 consolidated revenue includes Rs 2,564.75 Lakh from it (AR 2026 Note 34 [page 139]; May 2025 deck [page 6]).
- Full-year FY25 revenue of Qualitek plus ITC, excluding inter-company, was Rs 85.0 Cr (May 2025 deck [page 9]). FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 124.52 Cr. Like-for-like growth is therefore +46.5% (derived), against the 77% headline (MD&A [page 2]).
- Readings: (a) a wording slip, where "consolidated" meant "100% owned" (ITCPL became wholly owned on 10 Sep 2025); (b) framing that lets a part-year base carry the 77% and "4x" headlines. Separator: the filed note settles the fact. Only intent stays open.

**5. The claim that standalone growth is organic is false.**

- Readings: (a) the lab is small (net assets Rs 662.14 Lakh), so organic growth stays near 47%; (b) the lab adds a material share, so organic growth is well below 47%. Separator: the Mumbai lab's FY26 revenue (NOT FOUND).
