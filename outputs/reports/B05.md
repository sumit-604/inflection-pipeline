### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every miss traced in this exercise (margin compression, debt/PBILDT above threshold) is
explained in the filed record via externally-attributed causes: raw material price volatility
(lauryl alcohol, fatty acids, alpha olefin, linked to palm prices — CARE p.2), US tariffs, and
GST-related FMCG demand disruption. No company document reviewed (Chairman's Letter, CEO
Conversation, MD&A, Board's Report) contains language acknowledging an internal execution gap,
a mistiming of pricing pass-through, or a self-critical assessment of the margin miss. The
Chairman's Letter in particular reports the year as one of "resilience and financial recovery"
(p.8) without disclosing that its own headline EBITDA figure embeds a one-time gain — see 2D
and 4D. **Classification: external-blame-heavy.** This is a document-based read (no analyst
pushback exists to test it against, unlike a concall), so it should be weighted as a partial
signal, not a full replacement for a live credibility test.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **The Chairman's Letter never discloses that its cited FY24-25 EBITDA (₹4,981.53 lakhs)
  includes a one-time insurance gain.** Board's Report shows standalone EBITDA (before
  depreciation, finance costs, exceptional items, tax) of ₹4,561.29 lakhs; adding the ₹420.25
  lakh exceptional insurance receipt (Board's Report p.46; results notes 3) yields
  ₹4,981.54 lakhs — matching the Chairman's figure to the rupee. That is a **9.2%
  overstatement** [(4,981.53−4,561.29)/4,561.29 = 9.2%] of the headline profitability number
  relative to the company's own Board's Report calculation, with no footnote or caveat in the
  Chairman's Letter itself.
- **No FY26 quantified guidance at all** — no margin band, no revenue growth target, no capex
  quantum, no debt-reduction target, no ROCE target. Given that CARE's own rating action four
  months later hinges precisely on margin trajectory and leverage, the absence of any company
  numeric target on these exact two variables is notable.
- **The capacity expansion at Pithampur/Silvassa** (~₹85 crore, ~₹60 crore debt-funded,
  targeted December 2026 completion) is disclosed only by CARE, not by the company in its own
  AR — the MD&A's "upcoming capacity expansion and debottlenecking projects" line (p.39) gives
  no quantum, no funding structure, no date.
- **Customer concentration** — CARE states ~92% of FY25 revenue came from the top five
  customers. Neither the Chairman's Letter, CEO Conversation nor MD&A discloses this
  concentration figure anywhere in the AR narrative sections.
- **The August 2026 preference-share redemption** (₹18.50 crore instrument, ₹23.23 crore
  including accrued premium per the Q1 FY27 filing, due 19-Aug-2026 per CARE Annexure-1) is not
  flagged as a near-term liquidity event in the Chairman's Letter or MD&A; it surfaces only in
  CARE's Liquidity section and in the Q1 FY27 results' regulatory disclosure table
  (results__81847a21..., p.8, "Free reserve as on the end of half year," "NCRPS are due for
  redemption during the Financial Year 2026-2027").
