Dear Sir/ Ma’am, 
This is in continuation to our earlier announcement dated August 25, 2025 and August 29, 2025, 
wherein the Company has proposed a Rights Issue of 14,87,500 Equity Shares at an Issue Price of 
Rs. 81 per share, aggregating to Rs. 12,04,87,500/- (Rupees Twelve Crores Four Lakhs Eighty Seven 
Thousand and Five Hundred only). 
The Rights Issue shall be offered in the ratio of 1 (One) Equity Share for every 12 (Twelve) Equity 
Shares held by eligible shareholders as on the Record Date i.e. September 04, 2025. 
The Rights Issue will remain open for subscription from September 12, 2025 to September 22, 2025. 
Further, stakeholders are requested to note the following: 
 
Market Lot & Odd Lots: 
The market lot for trading of Equity Shares is 1000 shares and in multiples of 1000 
shares. Hence, the shares to be tendered will be in lots of 1000 shares; however, 
the allotment of equity shares under the Rights Issue will be as per entitlement. 
As a result, shareholders may be left with odd lots of shares, and the same may 
not be tradable in the market. Investors should take an informed decision before 
participating in the Rights Issue. 
 
 
 Rights Entitlement Lot Size: 
As per the approved ratio of 1:12, the Rights entitlement works out to 83 Equity 
Shares (based on the holding of 1,000 Equity Shares). The detailed terms and 
conditions are set out in the Letter of Offer dated August 29, 2025.
