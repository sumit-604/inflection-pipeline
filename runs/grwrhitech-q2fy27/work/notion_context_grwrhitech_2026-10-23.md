## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS MODEL SUMMARY

<b>What they do:</b> Manufacture specialty high-performance polyester films from crude oil derivatives all the way to final nano-coated product. Three segments: Sun Control Films (SCF, \~50%), Paint Protection Films (PPF, \~25%), Industrial Films/IPD (\~25%).

<b>Moat:</b> Deep backward integration (chips→film→coating→brand). India's only pro-grade PPF and safety glazing manufacturer. Proprietary nano-dispersion technology blocking 99% of IR heat. Transitioning from B2B to B2C/D2C via 250+ Garware Application Studios + Garware Home Solutions.

<b>Financial Snapshot FY26:</b> Revenue ₹2,120 Cr \| EBITDA ₹500 Cr (23.6%) \| PAT ₹338 Cr (16%) \| EPS ₹145 \| ROCE 18% \| Zero debt \| ₹774 Cr net cash

<b>Competitive Position:</b> Global top-3-4 in premium specialty films. Priced 10-15% below US premium peers (3M, Eastman, XPEL) but miles above Chinese/Korean commodity imports. Passed 50% US tariff shock while Cosmo could only pass on 25% — validates pricing power.

<b>Industry Structure:</b> Oligopoly at premium end (\~4 global players). Low threat of new entrants (30+ years R&D, custom machinery, backward integration required). Threat of substitutes low (no direct PPF equivalent; glazing far more expensive to retrofit).
