## Where the market stands

On Monday 1,407 stocks stood above the short line, more than six in ten. 1,036 stood above the middle line, a little under half. 1,003 stood above the long line, about 45 in every hundred. The short trend belongs to the buyers. The middle trend is close to even. The long trend still leans to the sellers, but less each week.

The year gives these counts their scale. On 23 March the long count hit a low of 286. On 1 April came a great thrust day, when 1,121 stocks rose hard. ### For the trader

**1. Monday was a rest, and it proved nothing.** The hard risers and hard fallers came close to even, 68 against 47. Almost four stocks in ten stayed inside a narrow range. A quiet day after a sharp run is ordinary. The next wide day will carry the information.

**2. Volume fell to its thinnest reading since April.** About one stock traded on heavy volume for every four on light volume. Turnover came to about 390 crore, the lowest since mid September. A pause on light volume is mild. A rally on light volume would be weak, so the difference matters.

**3. Friday's pullback passed its first test.** 149 stocks fell hard on Friday, less than half the worst autumn day. The short count held above 1,200 and new lows stayed low. By Monday the count stood higher again. A pullback that holds this well favours the buyers on the short clock.

The spread between them was the largest bank move of the session. Private banks still own the better three months. A second day of this spread would mark a real rotation.

## What would change this read

The read is an early uptrend, resting on thin volume, led by one industry and the largest names. It turns worse if a day of 300 or more hard fallers returns and goes unanswered. A short count back under about 1,200, Friday's level, would say the bounce is losing its grip. A long count back under about 940 would undo the last week's progress. A long count under about 580, the 23 October low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows above about 100 would say sellers have returned. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has taken charge. A sharp fall in healthcare on heavy volume would remove the one group carrying the conviction.

The cautious read fails if the money arrives and the fear gauge falls. The first test is a wide up day, with 200 or more hard risers, on turnover above about 610 crore. Monday reached about 390. The second test is the long count above about 1,125, half the market. Monday reached 1,003. The third test is a VIX close under 15. Monday closed near 17.4. The fourth test is gains held into the close. About half of the stocks that trade four percent up should keep it. Monday held fewer than four in ten. Two of these in the same week, with the short count above 1,200, would confirm the uptrend.
