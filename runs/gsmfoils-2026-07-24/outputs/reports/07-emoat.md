### 1B. Diversification direction

- **Product**: ROPP caps (📄, launched) is the only product diversification with hard evidence — it moves GSM out of pure blister/strip pharma foil into a closures category serving both pharma and alcohol/beverage. Forward-integration into specialised printing (🎙️) remains concept-stage.
- **Vertical integration**: direction is explicitly **forward only** as of Q4 FY26 ("we are not looking at backward integration currently"); this reverses the earlier Lamitubes/Alu-Alu backward-integration ambition (B05 promise-delivery miss).

- **Channel/customer**: management explicitly states it is **avoiding** the largest pharma accounts (Sun Pharma, Cipla tier) because of 90-150 day payment terms (Q3 FY26 call, Priyanshu Jain exchange) — this caps, rather than expands, the addressable blue-chip customer base by design.
- **Export**: indirect only, via merchant exporters into Vietnam, Yemen, Europe, US (🎙️, Q3 FY26 call: "directly, we're not exporting currently anything"). No direct export revenue line disclosed in any of the three periods.

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown, with a methodology caveat

GSM's own capex under execution (documented, excludes Unit 3/4 which are MoU/leased structures not on GSM's own capex line): Ahmedabad ~₹5.5 Cr (largely spent) + Vasai speed-up ~₹0.15 Cr ≈ **₹5.65 Cr**.

Historical fixed-asset turnover (FY26): Revenue ₹258.15 Cr (Inv. Pres. slide 28) ÷ closing net PP&E ₹5.089 Cr (Mar-26, Inv. Pres. slide 29) = **~50.7x**.

Mechanical implied incremental revenue = ₹5.65 Cr × 50.7x ≈ **₹286 Cr, or ~111% above the FY26 revenue base**.

**Flag — this multiplier is not meaningful for GSM and is not used as the reported figure.** Management states repeatedly and consistently across all three calls that this is a working-capital-heavy, asset-light, leased-premises business (Nov-2025 call: "the highest CAPEX that you need is between 4 crores to 5 crores... you require more working capital in it rather than CAPEX"). Applying a 50x historical asset-turnover ratio to forward capex mechanically overstates embedded growth by an order of magnitude relative to what management itself has ever guided. As a decision-useful cross-check instead: management's own FY27 topline guidance is ₹400-450 Cr (+55% to +74% over FY26's ₹258 Cr, Q4 FY26 call) — itself a 🎙️ CLAIM from a management team carrying a C-grade promise-delivery record (B05) that has already missed its own Ahmedabad utilisation target twice, so this figure should be discounted rather than accepted at face value. **capex_embedded_growth_pct is reported as the mechanical 2C output (~111%) per the required formula, flagged in-line as methodologically distorted for this asset-light model; the more decision-relevant, still-unverified figure is management's own +55% to +74% FY27 guide.**
