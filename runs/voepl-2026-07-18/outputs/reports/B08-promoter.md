## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. Related-party transaction history
See Section 1B for the core finding: guarantor/mortgagor/land-seller entities (Filaments & Filaments, Reprolite Papers — Luma Lamp arguably disclosed under an inconsistent name) sit outside or only inconsistently inside the Note 32 related-party universe despite functioning as related parties in substance, most strikingly where the company's own registered-office land was purchased from one of them for Rs 2,418 lakh with zero RPT-note disclosure. 🔴

The MD's unconditional personal guarantee secures VOEPL's term loans, and short-term facilities are separately secured by "Personal/Corporate Guarantee of Directors and Associate Concerns" (Note 6(viii), Note 9(v)) — routine for Indian SME/promoter-led bank lending, but a further data point of promoter-company financial entanglement layered on the guarantor/mortgage pattern above. 🟡

Disclosed operating RPTs (goods/services sale-purchase with KMP- and relative-controlled entities) are small (Rs 15–94 lakh range) and stated to be at arm's length (Note 32 footnote) — consistent with B02's assessment that the operating RPTs specifically are clean. 🟢

KMP and their relatives subscribed to Rs 1,147.95 lakh and Rs 478.31 lakh respectively of share capital/warrants in FY25 (Note 32B) — promoters were putting capital *in* during the same period the company's non-promoter base was growing, not solely cashing out.

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour
No dividend has been paid; the Board did not recommend one for FY25 despite Rs 1,413 lakh PAT — consistent with an active, capital-intensive growth/capex phase (CWIP grew 7x per B02) rather than a red flag on its own, but a zero-payout history worth noting.

Warrant conduct: the FY24 tranche of 31,50,000 warrants (Rs 255.10/warrant, 25% upfront) was fully called and converted to equity in FY25 (Rs 6,026.74 lakh, Note 5), i.e. **no lapsed warrants found** — the classic red-flag pattern of promoters subscribing warrants and later abandoning them (forfeiting the 25% deposit rather than paying up) did **not** occur here. 🟢

FY26 capital programme: a Rs 250 crore enabling resolution was approved 7-May-2026 (📰, ScanX/IndiaIPO); the first Rs 85 crore tranche (EGM 12-Jul-2026) allots Rs 60 crore of equity (16,85,392 shares at Rs 356/share) to **Malabar India Fund Limited and India Insight Value Fund** and Rs 25 crore of warrants (7,02,246 units) to the **promoter group** (📰, ScanX). ICRA's rating rationale (📰, search-snippet sourced — full PDF not directly retrievable, see search log) states Malabar India Fund is committing Rs 140 crore in total, Rs 85 crore by March-2026 and the balance by FY2027, to fund Rs 140-150 crore of FY26 capex and a further Rs 150 crore in FY27. Funds are earmarked: Rs 15.50cr capex (building/machinery for commercial refrigeration), Rs 48.35cr long-term working capital, Rs 21.15cr general corporate purposes. This is a clean, disclosed, purposeful capital-raise pattern, with promoters again participating (Rs 25cr of the Rs85cr tranche) rather than solely diluting out.

MD remuneration rose from Rs 36 lakh (FY24) to Rs 54 lakh (FY25) — a 17.15% KMP salary increase versus a stated 61.36% rise in median employee remuneration and a 10% "last financial year" comparator cited in the AR's own remuneration disclosure (AR p.78-79) — modest in absolute terms (Rs 54 lakh is ~3.8% of FY25 PAT) and disclosed transparently; no performance-decline-with-rising-pay pattern found.

### 3C. Promoter share transactions
Promoter holding fell from 73.51% (Sep-2022) to 49.74% (Mar-2026, held flat through Jul-2026), a -23.8pp decline crossing below 50% by Mar-2026 (operator-supplied SHP series). **Adjudication (per orchestrator instruction), based on AR + web evidence gathered in this stage:** this reads predominantly as **DILUTION VIA FRESH ISSUANCE funding capex, not a promoter sell-down**:
- The 73.51% starting point itself already reflects the Sep-2022 SME IPO dilution from a pre-IPO 100% base.
- The FY24-25 warrant tranche enlarged the share base with KMP and relatives themselves subscribing a combined ~Rs 1,626 lakh of it (Note 32B) — promoters added capital rather than extracting it.
- The FY26 Rs 85cr tranche is 71% institutional (Malabar India Fund + India Insight Value Fund, Rs 60cr) and 29% promoter-group warrants (Rs 25cr) — again, promoters are participating in the raise, not exiting via it.
- No OFS, block deal, or bulk-sale disclosure specific to VOEPL promoters was found in this search pass. One aggregator (📰/❓, likely auto-generated commentary) stated "Promoters seem not to be bullish... and have been selling shares in the open market" — this reads as templated boilerplate keyed off the raw percentage decline rather than a specific, sourced transaction, and is flagged here as **UNVERIFIED, not corroborated** by any concrete trade, filing, or disclosure found. The operator should weigh this caveat: the read of "benign capex dilution" is well-supported by the fresh-issuance mechanics traced above, but a direct BSE SAST/PIT disclosure confirming zero individual promoter *sale* transactions could not be retrieved in this pass (BSE's PIT-corp-information and pledge pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool).
- **Pledge percentage: NOT FOUND.** The operator-supplied SHP screenshot explicitly carries no pledge row; BSE's SAST pledge disclosure page and screener.in's company page were both inaccessible (403) in this search pass. Per pipeline rule, this is recorded as NOT FOUND, not assumed zero, and it directly limits the deal-breaker check on pledge >40% (see 6C) and the overall verdict (see 6B).
