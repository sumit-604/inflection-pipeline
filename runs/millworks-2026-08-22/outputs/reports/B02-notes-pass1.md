## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- Basis of accounting: IGAAP, historical cost, Schedule III Companies Act 2013, accrual basis. No
  first-time Ind AS adoption (company never moved to Ind AS). (Annexure IV, p.201/F9–F10) 🟢
- Depreciation: SLM per Schedule II useful lives; used/second-hand assets depreciated over remaining
  useful life per management estimate "advice from a competent technician" — a judgment-based override
  clause. No specific useful-life table disclosed in the extracted text (component-level lives not
  itemised). (Annexure IV, 2.06, p.202/F11) 🟡 Watch — useful life determination for used machinery is
  management-judgment driven with no quantified schedule; company acquired significant used P&M in
  FY2025 (per tax-shelter note re: Section 115BAB ineligibility, see below).
- Goodwill: amortised straight-line over 5 years per AS-14 (pre-Ind AS treatment; note Ind AS/AS 26
  normally does NOT amortise goodwill on business combinations the same way — this SLM-over-5-years
  policy is IGAAP AS-14 specific and is a real economic amortisation drag, ₹96.76 Lakh in FY2026 alone).
  (Annexure IV, 2.06, p.202/F11; Annexure XIV, p.214/F23) 🟡 Watch
- Impairment: qualitative policy only (indicators-based); no annual mandatory testing, no assumptions
  (growth/discount rate) disclosed anywhere in the notes — consistent with IGAAP (no Ind AS 36 CGU
  testing requirement), but means the ₹613.14 Lakh goodwill has no disclosed impairment test. (Annexure
  IV, 2.08, p.203/F12) 🟡 Watch — NOT FOUND: impairment assumptions (none required/disclosed under
  IGAAP; flagged as an evidence gap for downstream stages, not a company failure).
- ECL / bad-debt provisioning: NO Ind AS 109 ECL matrix (not applicable, IGAAP). Company's stated policy
  is "provision for doubtful trade receivables" via management estimate (Annexure IV, 2.02, p.201).
  ACTUAL PROVISION FOR BAD & DOUBTFUL DEBTS = ₹0 in all three years (FY24, FY25, FY26), despite trade
  receivables >6 months growing from ₹1.03 Lakh (FY24) to ₹42.62 Lakh (FY25) to ₹2,829.41 Lakh (FY26).
  (Annexure XX, p.212/F21) 🔴 Red Flag — zero doubtful-debt provisioning against a receivables book that
  grew 20x in a single year, with the >6-month bucket alone crossing ₹28 crore, is a material
  provisioning-adequacy concern.
- Revenue recognition: goods — recognised at "Ex-Factory" dispatch point (risk/reward transfer at
  factory gate for both domestic and export sales); services (design/engineering) — recognised on
  rendering per contract terms. (Annexure IV, 2.13, p.204/F13) 🟢 policy itself is conventional, BUT see
  restatement note below — actual application had cut-off errors in FY24/FY25.
  - IMPORTANT: FY2026 P&L for the first time shows "Revenue from Sale of Services – Domestic Sales
    ₹2,300.00 Lakh" (Annexure XXIV, p.216/F25) — a new revenue line item not present in FY24/FY25 (both
    show ₹0 for services). This is a first-time appearance of a revenue stream, ~15.5% of FY2026 revenue,
    coincident with the slump-sale acquisition and Quick Pay customer ramp. 🟡 Watch
- Capitalisation threshold: NOT FOUND (no monetary threshold disclosed for PPE capitalisation).
- No Ind AS 116 lease accounting (IGAAP AS-19 used instead) — see Provisions/Leases section below;
  operating leases not capitalised, so "Present Value of Minimum Lease Payments" columns in the AS-19
  note are all shown as ₹0 even though minimum lease payments (undiscounted) total ₹928.33 Lakh (FY26).
  (Annexure XLVIII, p.232/F41) 🟢 (policy-consistent, but investors coming from an Ind AS mental model
  should note the balance sheet does not carry a lease liability / ROU asset).
- First-time standard adoption: none (no Ind AS transition; CSR provisions of Sec 135 became applicable
  to the company only from FY2026, per note, since thresholds not met in FY24/FY25). (Capitalisation
  Statement page, p.235/F44, "OTHER DISCLOSURES") 🟢
