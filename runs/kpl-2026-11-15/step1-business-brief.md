## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)

1. Guidance vs delivery. FY27 guidance Rs 720 Cr+ revenue, EBITDA Rs 189-196 Cr, PAT Rs 122 Cr+. H1 FY27 delivered sales Rs 343 Cr, PAT Rs 55 Cr on screener. Verify the Q2 PAT in the filed results (Rs 29 Cr vs Rs 50 Cr in one call summary) and the Q2 tax rate an analyst queried.
2. Governance. On the Q2 call, an analyst said the company secretary and the statutory auditor resigned on results day; management said KPMG is appointed. No exchange filing found. Verify the resignation letters, reasons, and the KPMG appointment on BSE/NSE.
3. Cash conversion. FY26 CFO Rs 17 Cr against PAT Rs 67 Cr. Debtor days 203 (screener) vs 208 (management). Receivables rose Rs 55 Cr in H1 FY27 while the cash cycle "fell" to 130 days. Rs 60-70 Cr of Middle East debtors were stuck at Q4 FY26 and reportedly collected in Q1-Q2. Verify in the AR receivables ageing and the H1 cash flow.
4. Mix-shift claim. Margin rests on KPL-held registrations (28-32% EBITDA) displacing partner-held ones (24-26%). Verify the share of revenue on own registrations and any partner or customer concentration; neither is disclosed in the web sources.
