## SECTION 0: THE CENTRAL FACT — TWO DIFFERENT COMPANIES, TWO DIFFERENT PROMOTERS

This is not one continuous promoter history. It is two.

**Company A, pre-31-Mar-2020**: Orchid Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd, founder-promoter
Kailasam Raghavendra Rao (Padma Shri, 2011; founded the company 1-Jul-1992).
📰 Rao pledged 7 million personal shares with Religare/Indiabulls around 2007-08 to
fund further open-market purchases (BusinessToday, "A bitter pill", 16-Apr-2008,
web-derived). The company was referred to CIRP in Aug 2017 after RBI listed it among
28 large corporate defaulters (web-derived, multiple sources). ✅ VERIFIED (NCLT/CoC
record, casemine.com judgment index, web-derived): all Powers of Attorney given to
Rao and senior management were revoked 31-Mar-2020 on implementation of the
resolution plan. 📰 The promoter holding vehicle, Orchid Healthcare Pvt Ltd (OHPL),
which held ~22.64% of the pre-resolution company with 99.99% of that stake pledged
to the lender consortium, was itself dissolved by NCLT in Dec 2019 as an asset-less
shell — the NCLT order stated equity holders would recover nothing (Business
Standard, 31-Dec-2019, web-derived).
No SEBI order, SFIO reference, ED/PMLA action, or criminal conviction against
K. Raghavendra Rao personally was found despite multiple targeted searches. The
company's insolvency reads, on available evidence, as a **debt-driven collapse**

result, not a certificate. ❓ classification stands: UNVERIFIED-CLEAN.

## SECTION 2: LEGAL & REGULATORY RECORD

### 2A. SEBI actions
Targeted searches for SEBI adjudication/settlement orders against Dhanuka
Laboratories, Manish Dhanuka, Mridul Dhanuka, Arjun Dhanuka and Ram Gopal Agarwal
returned **no matching order**. This is a clean-record finding, stated as such and
not manufactured into a concern — but it is a search-quota-limited clean record
(❓ tier: absence of a hit in general web search, not a SEBI-database confirmation).

The **one confirmed, materially disclosed regulatory matter** is the Minimum Public
Shareholding (MPS) breach, verified at source (✅ FY2025 AR p.96-97, Corporate
Governance Report):
- Post-resolution, the company was required to reach 25% MPS within 3 years.
- 2021: promoter OFS of 32,80,115 shares (8.04%) took public holding to 10%
  (promoter down to 90%).
- A planned QIP to close the remaining gap failed by 31-Mar-2023 on market
  conditions; the company sought a 1-year extension from SEBI/exchanges under
  Reg 102.
- **20-Apr-2023**: BSE and NSE froze the promoter shareholding under SEBI Circular
  CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2017/115 and barred promoters/promoter group/directors from taking
  any new directorship on any other listed entity until compliance.
- Penalties: ₹5,000 + GST (period to 31-Mar-2023) and ₹5,19,200 (01-Apr-2023 to
  26-Jun-2023) — both paid.
- **27-Jun-2023**: Board allotted 99,02,705 equity shares via QIP (₹391.80 cr raised
  net of ₹8.06 cr issue expenses), lifting paid-up capital from ₹40.82 cr to ₹50.72
  cr. Promoter holding fell from 89.96% to 72.40%; public holding rose to 27.60%.
  Company states compliance achieved w.e.f. 27-Jun-2023.
- A subsequent on-market sale of 13,00,000 shares (2.56%) on 28-Nov-2023 at an
  average ₹571.28/share brought promoter holding down to the current 69.84%
  (web-derived, corroborates and reconciles exactly with the AR's 72.40%→69.84%
  figures: 72.40% − 2.56% = 69.84%).

**Judgment**: this is a **minimum-public-shareholding compliance failure following
an insolvency acquisition**, not misappropriation, insider dealing, or a
disclosure cover-up. The company self-reported, sought an extension in good faith,
was frozen and fined when the extension was not granted, paid the fines, and cured
the breach within about 2 months via a genuine institutional capital raise. 📰/✅
classified as a **CAUTION-tier compliance lapse, cured**, not a CONCERN-tier
integrity finding.

Two further items from the FY2025 Secretarial Audit Report (✅ FY2025 AR p.43-44,
p.312 Annexure X):
- Delayed (by 22 days) shareholder approval under Reg 17(1A) for Ram Gopal Agarwal's
  continuation past age 75; BSE and NSE each fined ₹44,000, paid. Board's explanation
  (AGM timing interdependency) is documented and plausible, not evasive.
- Delayed RPT filing under Reg 23(9); BSE and NSE each fined ₹5,000, paid.
- A warning/cautionary letter from BSE and NSE for delayed intimation of (a) the
  resignation of an erstwhile Company Secretary and (b) an investor-meet schedule
  (✅ FY2025 AR p.170-171, Secretarial Audit Report "we further report" section).

None of these rise to a market ban, fraud finding, or SFIO/ED matter. All are
disclosed, fined, paid, and explained.

## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. Related-party transaction history
**The dominant RPT is Otsuka Chemical (India) Pvt Ltd**, the sole approved source
of GCLE, a key Cephalosporin API raw material, in which MD Manish Dhanuka is a
Director & Member and WTD Mridul Dhanuka is a Member (✅ FY2025 AR AOC-2, p.147-148;
AGM Notice Item 9, p.293-295):
- FY2024-25 actual transaction value: **₹230.72 crore** (against FY24 revenue of
  ₹819 cr, i.e. ~28% of the prior year's turnover — the AR's own percentage
  calculations use the preceding year's base).
- FY2025-26 shareholder approval sought for up to **₹400 crore**, stated by the
  company as ~43.38% of FY25 consolidated turnover — the AR attributes the jump
  to the pending merger, after which Dhanuka Laboratories' own GCLE purchases
  from Otsuka (previously off Orchid's books) consolidate into Orchid.
- Company's own justification: Otsuka was the sole approved GCLE source "even
  before the takeover by Dhanuka group"; pricing is stated to be at prevailing
  market rates, arm's length, Audit-Committee-reviewed, and Dhanuka Laboratories
  (as the interested promoter) is barred from voting on the resolution (✅ AR
  p.294).
- No independent valuation or benchmarking report is disclosed alongside the RPT
  approval (✅ AR p.296: "the transactions do not contemplate any valuation
  report").

**Judgment**: this is a real and growing related-party dependency — a promoter-
family-linked private company supplying a rising share (28% → an approved cap of
43%) of a critical, non-substitutable raw material, with pricing verified only by
the company's own Audit Committee and no external valuation. It is mitigated by:
(a) Otsuka's sole-source status predates Dhanuka's 2020 takeover, (b) all
non-interested directors and shareholders must approve it, (c) the interested
promoter cannot vote, and (d) it is transparently disclosed with percentages, not
buried. This is a genuine CAUTION-tier finding for concentration risk, not
evidence of price-gouging (no benchmarking data exists in the corpus to prove or
disprove fair pricing either way — NOT FOUND).

No other loans-to-group-entities, above-market rent, management-fee, or
guarantee-for-private-entity RPTs were found. The only guarantee/loan disclosed
(₹100.35 cr loan + ₹447.22 cr guarantee) is to/for the company's own **subsidiary**
(Orchid Bio Pharma Ltd, presumed), not a promoter-family private entity (✅ FY2025
AR, Auditor's CARO report).

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour
- **No dividend** declared for FY24-25; Board resolved to retain profits for
  growth capex (Jammu 7-ACA facility, Cefiderocol project) (✅ FY2025 AR p.28-29).
  Reasonable for a company 4-5 years post-resolution still building out capacity;
  not itself a red flag, but also means shareholders have zero cash-return track
  record from this promoter yet to judge.
- **QIP dilution was compliance-driven** (MPS cure), not an opportunistic
  preferential allotment to insiders — QIBs, not the promoter, received the new
  shares (✅ FY2025 AR p.96-98).
- Director remuneration: Manish Dhanuka and Mridul Dhanuka each received an 8.65%
  salary / 8.45% commission increase in FY25 vs FY24, **below** the 12.85% median
  employee salary increase for the year (✅ FY2025 AR p.169-170). This is a
  favourable data point — management remuneration did not outrun rank-and-file pay.
- **The 14,300 Zero Coupon Optionally Convertible Debentures (₹143 crore),
  held by Dhanuka Laboratories Ltd, are a material dilution overhang.** Per the
  approved resolution plan, DLL may convert any or all of these OCDs at any time
  over a 10-year tenor into equity at **par value (₹10/share)** — each ₹1 lakh OCD
  converts into 10,000 equity shares — with no linkage to market price (✅ FY2025
  AR p.94-95). At full conversion this is up to 14.3 crore new shares against
  ~5.07 crore shares outstanding pre-merger — a potential near-tripling of the
  share count, entirely to the promoter, at a fixed price far below any plausible
  market price for a stock that has traded from single digits to the thousands
  since re-listing. This instrument originates from the court-approved resolution
  plan (a legally sanctioned CIRP mechanism, not a later self-dealing addition),
  which is a mitigating fact — but the overhang itself is real, undisclosed as to
  timing intent, and worth carrying forward as a standing dilution risk for any
  valuation or entry-price work downstream.
