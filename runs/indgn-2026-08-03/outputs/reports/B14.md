nil borrowings, PPE only 1.3% of total assets (B04, B11). Cumulative CFO/PAT 1.32x over FY19-26

capital 41.8% of capital employed (statutory 15.4%) (FTTCP delib p.114-115).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, B05 triggers).**
1. Large outcome-based deal revenue realization (top-10 pharma $10mn+ ACV, biotech $20mn TCV,
   Q3-won $10mn ACV omnichannel deal). Conviction H. Timeline FY27 from Q2 FY27. Confirms:
   FY27 calls show revenue recognized on the stated go-live schedule. Kills: further deferral,
   rescoping, or non-mention.

   adjusted margin improvement with a restated explicit target. Kills: Q1/Q2 FY27 flat or down,
   or target language dropped. This is the load-bearing trigger.

   Confirms: additional Tectonic markets/customers named with revenue. Kills: Germany stalls.

   Confirms: organic above the Q3 FY26 18.3% pace. Kills: organic near/below the Q4 FY26 12%

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

its own bear-sensitivity floor. The four-pillar forward destination of 31.5x rests on a ROCE that is
recovering (not yet fired) and a moat that is strengthening (not yet expanded), and it clears the

the treasury is judged undeployable the destination collapses to roughly 19.8x and the stock is only
fairly valued (verdict caps at WATCHLIST / BUY-on-dips), and if organic growth stays at/below 12% the
revenue verdict weakens to STARTING and the base drifts to the bear at roughly Rs 850. The verdict is
BUY-on-dips, size Medium at the Tier B ceiling but built from a Small starter, adds ideal below
Rs 534, and every buy is gated by the entry conjunction: a price that enters the zone because a
trigger fired is a withdrawn zone, not an opportunity.
