## What the business is (numbers first)

1. FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 52.51 Cr (FY25 Rs 45.24 Cr), EBITDA margin
   28.45% (25.35%), PAT Rs 8.75 Cr (+23.3%), per the CFO on the H2 FY26 call
   (https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/AVIENBIO_28072026233742_Transcript_H2_FY26_Concall__Avience.pdf).
   Screener shows FY25 Rs 44.92 Cr and the RHP restated FY25 Rs 45.97 Cr: three
   bases for one year.
2. Noida maker and distributor of IVD (in vitro diagnostics) reagents,
   consumables, rapid test cards and analysers. Revenue is 72% TRADED:
   traded reagents/consumables 59.83%, traded instruments 12.55%; manufactured
   reagents/consumables 14.62%, rapid cards 9.15%, manufactured instruments
   3.21%; balance service income (transcript p7).
3. Margin engine: reagent rental. 80-85% of instruments are placed free on
   roughly five-year contracts; the customer buys reagents at agreed prices;
   80-90% of reagent revenue is closed-system. Management states rental
   margins 40-60% and manufacturing margins 30-70% (transcript p6-7).
4. The traded engine rides one principal: Avience is a regional Mindray
   channel partner (Delhi NCR plus eastern UP). RHP: top supplier 63.0% of
   purchases (10M FY26), 66.3% FY25, 72.7% FY24; Mindray named as a trading
   and raw-material supplier (RHP pdf p38 supplier table; p124, p127 Mindray).
5. Customers: top 10 >50% of revenue, unnamed (RHP pdf p34, risk factor 2). Named
   accounts on the call: Max Healthcare, Sarvodaya Hospital, Dr. Lal PathLabs.
   Standalone mix (10M FY26): B2B 45.3%, B2C (hospitals/labs) 45.2%, B2G
   7.8%, export 1.8%. Delhi 42% and UP 34% of standalone revenue. FY25 export
   Rs 5.24 Cr (18%) was a one-year spike (RHP pdf p159-161; printed pp 155-156).
6. Capacity: rented Noida unit; rapid tests 84%, reagents 85%, analysers 81%
   utilised at 10M FY26 (RHP pdf p161). The IPO funds a new plant at YEIDA Medical
   Device Park, Plot 70, Sector 28: Rs 14.05 Cr of proceeds; consolidated CWIP
   Rs 12.85 Cr at Mar-2026 (screener). Management: completion September,
   production from October 2026, peak revenue capacity ~Rs 250 Cr, FY27
   utilisation 15-20% (transcript p3, p6-7).
7. Cash: consolidated CFO Rs 5.91 Cr FY26 vs operating profit Rs 15.10 Cr
   (CFO/OP 58%); FCF negative three years running (-2.81, -5.87, -7.44 Cr);
   debtor days 146, inventory days 218; borrowings Rs 28.93 Cr (screener
   consolidated). Screener flags "might be capitalising interest cost".

## The one engine the thesis rests on

A MIX SHIFT from traded Mindray reagents to in-house manufactured reagents
on closed-loop placed instruments, enabled by the YEIDA plant. Management
guides FY27 revenue growth of at least 60% (above Rs 100 Cr), backed by a
~Rs 47 Cr order "in hand" from Uttarakhand, a manufacturing:trading mix near
50:50 from ~27:72, FY28 aspiration Rs 160-165 Cr, and exports Rs 5-7 Cr in
FY27 (transcript p3-7). It also discusses a closed-loop Mindray OEM/private
label deal for North India (not signed). None of this is yet in a print: the
next half-year result (H1 FY27) is due by November 2026.
