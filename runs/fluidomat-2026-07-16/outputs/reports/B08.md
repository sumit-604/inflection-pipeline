the operator noting, even where a benign explanation is available.

women in the promoter group. This is a **structural point for the operator to weigh
over a 3-5 year hold** (transition alpha thesis), not a misconduct flag: the transition
appears deliberate and already underway rather than an unaddressed cliff-edge risk.
