## Business Understanding Narrative

Mercury EV-Tech says it makes electric vehicles and solar equipment. Its own
segment note is the fullest description available: "The Company is operating in
renewable segment i.e. EV and Solar, hence reporting under Ind AS-108 is not
applicable" (Result Q4FY26 p5 note 7). That single sentence is the whole
business disclosure. No split between EV and solar is given, no product line is
named, no customer is named, and no capacity is stated. It operates from
Vadodara, Gujarat.

What is changing is the direction, and it is down. FY26 revenue from operations
fell 30.3%, from Rs 64.9 crore to Rs 45.3 crore. Net profit fell 55.7%, from
Rs 6.39 crore to Rs 2.83 crore (Result Q4FY26 p2). Cost of material consumed
fell 64%, while purchase of stock in trade rose 119%. The company bought
finished goods instead of making them.

Why now: the corpus offers no reason, because no management has ever commented.
What it does show is a capital cycle. The company raised Rs 193.26 crore of
share capital in FY25 (Result Q4FY26 p4), then spent Rs 41.13 crore on fixed
assets in FY26 while revenue shrank.

What must be true for any thesis: that the FY26 revenue fall is a transition
year and the Rs 41 crore of new assets start earning. Nothing in the corpus
supports or denies it.

What breaks it: the cash. Operating cash flow was negative Rs 23.40 crore in
FY26 and negative Rs 31.62 crore in FY25 (Result Q4FY26 p4). Two consecutive
years of cash burn on a business reporting a profit. The reported profit and
the cash have moved in opposite directions for two years.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Undisclosed. One sentence describes the business, and it names two unrelated
industries without splitting either (Result Q4FY26 p5 note 7). Electric
vehicles and solar equipment share no customer, no channel and no cost
structure. A company that reports them as one segment is telling you it does
not manage them as businesses.
The cost lines say more than the description. In FY26, cost of materials
consumed was Rs 15.99 crore and purchase of stock in trade was Rs 16.87 crore
(Result Q4FY26 p2). Half of what it sold, it bought ready-made. In FY25 the
ratio was Rs 44.80 crore made against Rs 7.71 crore bought. The business shifted
from manufacturing to trading inside one year, and nobody explained it.
Archetype: **cannot be assigned**. The evidence does not identify what the
company does. That is a finding, not a gap to paper over.

## 9. Flags from the documents
This is the longest flag list of the six names, and every item comes from the
company's own filing.
- **The filing's arithmetic does not add.** FY26 revenue 4,527.21 plus other
  income 593.13 is 5,120.34; the filing prints total revenue as 5,030.34, a
  gap of 90.00. FY25: 6,494.27 plus 142.41 is 6,636.68; printed as 6,066.68, a
  gap of 570.00. Q4 FY25 shows the same defect (Result Q4FY26 p2).
- **A March 2023 balance sheet is bundled inside the FY26 results.** Page 3 is
  headed "STATEMENT OF STANDALONE ASSETS AND LIABILITIES AS AT MARCH 31, 2023",
  carries FY22 comparatives, and is signed "DATE: 30.05.2023" (Result Q4FY26
  p3). The FY26 filing therefore contains no current balance sheet.
- **Depreciation is stated twice, differently.** The P&L shows Rs 384.83 lakhs
  for FY26; the cash-flow statement shows Rs 284.82 lakhs for the same line and
  year (Result Q4FY26 p2, p4). A gap of Rs 100.01 lakhs.
- **Depreciation rose 7.0 times** in FY26, from Rs 55.13 lakhs to Rs 384.83
  lakhs, while revenue fell 30% (Result Q4FY26 p2).
- **The Managing Director's name and DIN vary across pages**, as set out in
  step 4.
- **The registered office is printed four different ways** across pages 1 to 4,
  varying the block number, the village and the taluka (Result Q4FY26 p1 to p4).
- **Two different CINs appear.** L27109GJ1986PLC008770 and
  U27109GJ1986PLC008276 (Result Q4FY26 p2, p3). The "U" prefix denotes an

- **The covering letter is addressed to "RSE Limited"** at BSE's address, and

- **An amalgamation was backdated by two and a half years.** EV Nest Private

- **Rs 67.17 crore of short-term loans and advances went out in FY25**, the

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS.**
The reporting quality alone settles it. A set of audited annual results whose
revenue lines do not sum, which prints two different depreciation figures for
the same year, which bundles a three-year-old balance sheet in place of the
current one, and which spells its Managing Director's name two ways under two
DINs, cannot be underwritten. Add negative operating cash flow in both FY25 and
FY26, a 30% revenue decline, other income exceeding operating profit, and
Rs 67 crore of undisclosed lending out of a Rs 193 crore raise.
No transition thesis is available, because no management has ever spoken on
record.

Recommend adding to the named failure catalogue in `LESSONS.md` as a
**reporting-integrity pattern**: audited results that fail an internal
arithmetic check are a hard stop, not a caveat. That is an operator decision,
not one this card makes.

Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides:
1. The FY26 balance sheet, which is not in the filing.
2. The counterparties for the Rs 67.17 crore of FY25 short-term loans.
3. The composition of Rs 5.93 crore of FY26 other income.
4. The Q1 FY27 result, absent since August 2026.
5. Why the revenue lines do not sum, and whether a corrected filing exists.
6. Promoter holding and pledge, absent entirely.

What would change the view: a corrected and internally consistent set of
accounts, positive operating cash flow for a full year, and a disclosed
EV-versus-solar split. Nothing short of all three.
No price, no position.
