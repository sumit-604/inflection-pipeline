# MAHASTEEL: Mahamaya Steel Industries Ltd

Shallow Analysis Framework v1.0. Screened 2026-10-18 by Claude Code.
NSE MAHASTEEL | BSE 513554 | ISIN INE451L01014 | Raipur, Chhattisgarh.
Corpus: `screens/corpus/MAHASTEEL/` (ledger in `MANIFEST.md`). Every number cites a file and a page.
Market data is a screener.in service record dated 2026-10-18, not a filed document.

**Verdict: PASS.** A 2.7% margin re-roller priced at 203x trailing earnings on a Rs 600 crore solar plan that is not yet built, with two agencies reporting the issuer as non-cooperating.

---

## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Mahamaya melts steel into billets and blooms, then rolls them into structural sections: joists, channels, angles, beams, rounds and railway sleeper bars. Capacity is 200,000 MT of billets and blooms and 255,000 MT of structurals, plus a 900,000 cubic metre gas plant (credit-ratings/01-ICRA.pdf, p.2). Revenue was Rs 882.85 crore in FY26 (filings/..._OBM_26052026, p.3). The company keeps the gap between the steel price and the scrap, sponge iron and power it consumes. In FY26 that gap left an EBITDA margin of 2.7% (01-ICRA.pdf, p.2).

**What is changing?** Management says power cost. The board plans a 130 MW solar plant for captive use, at about Rs 600 crore, "within the next 6 to 8 months" (screener/Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.7). It bought about 350 acres at Janjgir-Champa for about Rs 70 crore on 13 August 2026 (filings/..._DISC30SOLAR13082026, p.1). A logistics park on about 100 acres of owned land is deferred (Annual_Report_2026, p.7).

**Why now?** The share price says the market already believes it. The stock trades at Rs 1,231 against a 52-week low of Rs 550, at 203x trailing earnings and 12.7x book (market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record). The financial year-end price was Rs 265.02 in March 2025 and Rs 949.75 in March 2026 (screener/Financials.txt, Data Sheet, service record).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The solar plant must be built on time and on budget, and the power saving must exceed the cost of the debt that pays for it. Rs 600 crore is 4.1 times standalone net worth of Rs 148.01 crore (filings/..._OBM_26052026, p.4).

**What breaks it?** The record. The 2016 investor deck already said the company "may look at foray into renewable energy, preferably Solar" and promised a "rerating opportunity from steel to power sector valuation multiple" (screener/Investor_Presentation_1.pdf, p.32). Ten years later the margin is the same and the plant is still a plan.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "Steel Segment" (Q1 FY27 results, p.4). Surplus gas sales were Rs 2.29 crore (Annual_Report_2026, p.110). The economic engine is conversion: buy melt inputs, sell rolled sections. The group supplies part of the input. Purchases from Devi Iron & Power, a promoter-controlled sponge iron and power company, were Rs 78.09 crore in FY26 (Annual_Report_2026, p.105).

The 2016 deck states that the top 10 clients took 47.6% of revenue and public sector clients 3.8% (Investor_Presentation_1, p.19). No current concentration figure exists.

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds. Read spread, utilisation and cycle position. Utilisation is NOT FOUND. The rupee spread (revenue less materials, purchases and inventory change) was 18.8% of revenue in FY25 and 20.8% in FY26 (FY26 results, p.3). The same spread fell to 17.4% in Q1 FY27 from 20.6% in Q1 FY26 (Q1 FY27 results, p.4). The rise from 18.8% to 20.8% did not reach EBITDA, because power, fuel and other costs absorbed it.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent. RDSO, BIS and railway approvals are a qualification, not pricing power (letterhead, all filings). Every quarter from Q1 FY26 to Q1 FY27 prints EBITDA between 2.2% and 3.0%. The solar plant is the claimed cost advantage. It does not exist yet.

## 4. The promoters

The Agrawal family. Ramanand Agrawal founded the company in 1988 (01-ICRA.pdf, p.2). Rajesh Agrawal is Managing Director, aged 54, and holds 20.50% (Annual_Report_2026, p.42). Rekha Agrawal, his spouse, is a director.

Promoter group holds 1,19,64,987 shares, 72.80% (Annual_Report_2026, p.71). Escort Finvest holds 24.94%, Rajesh Agrawal 20.50%, Abhishek Steel Industries 8.20%, Rekha Agrawal 7.10%, Rajesh Agrawal HUF 6.33%, Abishek Agrawal 4.92% (p.72). The promoter declares no encumbrance for FY26 (filings/team_bbodade_15042026, p.3).

Three points weigh against the clean pledge line.
- **Cross-holding.** Abhishek Steel holds 8.20% of Mahamaya. Mahamaya holds 31.75% of Abhishek Steel and books its profit as an associate (Annual_Report_2026, p.39).
- **Pay.** Rajesh Agrawal earned Rs 2.52 crore in FY26, up 33.33%, 87.5 times the median employee (p.41). That is 29% of standalone profit after tax of Rs 8.74 crore.
- **Skin in the game, the other way.** The promoter group offers up to Rs 125 crore of unsecured loans for the solar plant, at up to 8.25% or interest-free (p.12, p.25). That is real commitment if drawn. Nothing in the corpus shows a drawdown.

Real free float is thin. Public holds 27.20%, of which the Rathee family holds 8.97% and IEPF 1.55% (p.72).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1, commodity price-taker: ROCE at 8%, margin 2.2% to 3.0%, cash flow below profit. TO R2, cost-advantaged converter, through captive solar power.

Management's claim: the plant "will lead to substantial savings in our power cost and also provide us insulation from future tariff volatility, thereby improving our overall operating margins" (Annual_Report_2026, p.7).

The mechanism is real in principle. Power and fuel cost Rs 118.87 crore in FY26 against Rs 101.87 crore in FY25 (Annual_Report_2026, p.103). That line is five times FY26 EBITDA. A cut of 10% in that line equals half of FY26 EBITDA. That is the bull arithmetic, and it is honest.

The bear arithmetic is just as plain. Rs 600 crore against Rs 7.67 crore of annual operating cash means almost all of it is borrowed. The board raised the borrowing limit to Rs 900 crore (Annual_Report_2026, p.10). The corpus gives no debt terms, tariff, generation or saving in rupees. The net effect is NOT FOUND.

The project has also drifted. FY25: 100 MW, then 150 MW, Rs 500 to 650 crore, land "being searched" (Annual_Report_2025, p.7). FY26: 130 MW, Rs 600 crore, 6 to 8 months (Annual_Report_2026, p.7). The land filing of 13-Aug-2026 says acquisition completes by July 2027 (DISC30SOLAR, p.2). The 6 to 8 month target and the land timeline cannot both hold. Record the ladder claim as **stated, not underway**.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Auditor says the balance sheet overstates assets.** An electricity duty receivable of Rs 11.06 crore dates from 2011-12 to 2018-19 (Annual_Report_2026, p.110). The auditor writes that current assets "should have been reduced to that extent", yet does not qualify (p.80; FY26 results p.7). The receivable exceeds one year of profit. The company still filed a declaration of an "unmodified opinion" (FY26 results, p.15). The directors call the report "devoid of any reservation" (Annual_Report_2026, p.36).
- **Repeated key audit matters** on inventory verification, party confirmations, old dues and related-party transactions, and no slow-moving inventory provision (p.79-80; same in Annual_Report_2025, p.120). CARO calls inventory verification "appropriate" (p.84). The two statements conflict.
- **Statutory audit fee is Rs 4 lakh** on Rs 883 crore of revenue (p.108). KPRK finishes its second term; Chopra A J & Associates, founded 2016, takes over (OBM 31-Aug-2026, p.3).
- **Related-party scale.** FY26 omnibus approval was "not exceeding 410 crores" (Annual_Report_2026, p.40). New approvals: Devi Iron & Power Rs 150 crore, Abhishek Steel Rs 50 crore, Rajesh Agrawal HUF Rs 10 crore for transport (p.11). A separate s.185 approval allows up to Rs 300 crore of loans to group entities (p.11). Money can now flow out to the group as well as in.
- **Rs 17.47 crore sits in unlisted group shares,** mostly Devi Iron & Power preference shares (p.95).
- **Disputed taxes Rs 11.19 crore,** Rs 8.14 crore of it for AY 2014-15 (p.85).
- **NSE fine Rs 1.30 lakh plus GST** for late filing of the H1 FY26 results PDF (Annual_Report_2026, p.36, p.47). That fits the 12-Dec-2025 file: a re-submission of the 13-Nov-2025 results, not a reply to a query. Its cover letter still dates the meeting "13th November, 2024" (re-filed results, p.1).
- **The Directors' Report says no material changes** occurred "between the end of Financial Year 2024-25 and the date of this report", after a Rs 70 crore land purchase (p.29).
- **Credit-rating disclosure is selective.** See step 10.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

Four agencies, two stories.

| CARE | BB+ (Stable) / A4+ | 27-Nov-2025 | Issuer Not Cooperating | 01-ICRA.pdf, p.2 |
| Infomerics | IVR BBB (Stable) | 07-Feb-2026 | Reaffirmed | filings/..._INTOFCREDITRATING07022026, p.1 |
| Brickwork | BBB+ (Stable) / A2 | 22-Jun-2026 | Assigned, mandate 23-Mar-2026 | filings/..._CREDT_RATING_INT_23062026, p.2 |

**What "Non-Cooperating" means.** A rated company signs an agreement to supply information and pay a surveillance fee. ICRA says Mahamaya did neither: "Despite multiple requests by ICRA, the entity's management has remained non-cooperative" (01-ICRA.pdf, p.2). ICRA warns that its rating "may not adequately reflect the credit risk profile". It still rates Rs 83 crore of facilities, including a term loan that matured in March 2023 (p.4).

**Why it is a governance signal.** The company stopped cooperating with the two agencies that rated it lowest. It then took a Brickwork rating six notches above ICRA. The annual report names only Brickwork (Annual_Report_2026, p.73). This is the shape of rating shopping. [INFERENCE] A management that walks away from an unwelcome opinion and does not disclose it may treat other bad news the same way.

ICRA's own numbers are benign: total debt to OPBDITA 2.5x and interest cover 4.7x in FY26 (01-ICRA.pdf, p.2). No agency in the corpus has rated the Rs 600 crore project.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** No solar capacity exists. Land completes by July 2027. Margin is flat to down.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** A 2.2% to 3.0% margin, 8% ROCE and cash below profit sit in every period held, back to the 1.3% to 5.4% margins of FY12 to FY16 (Investor_Presentation_1, p.9). The second reading calls this ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB: high power cost the solar plant will remove. **The observation that separates them is the first full quarter of captive solar generation in the power and fuel line.** It cannot arrive before FY28.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market capitalisation Rs 2,024 crore, PE 203x, 12.7x book (market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record). The price already pays for the TO rung and more.

**Posture: AVOID.** Gap CLOSED plus STRUCTURAL is the dominating overlay. On the alternative reading, ARTIFACT, the cell is PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP). Both readings reach the same conclusion. Failure pattern: a narrative re-rating repeated from 2016. Success catalogue pending.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

A thin conversion business, one unbuilt growth idea first floated in 2016, and a price that already pays for success. The governance record cuts the other way.

**Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides to /step1**
1. Solar financing terms: debt quantum, rate, lender, and promoter-loan drawdowns.
2. Power consumption in units and tariff, to size the saving against the debt cost.
3. Production volume and utilisation for FY25, FY26 and Q1 FY27.
4. Disposal of the Rs 11.06 crore electricity duty receivable.
5. The reason for ICRA and CARE non-cooperation, and the Brickwork rationale of 22-Jun-2026.

**What would change the view**
- Commissioned solar capacity with a power and fuel line that falls as a share of revenue.
- Return to cooperation with ICRA and CARE, or a withdrawal on full information.
- A price that reopens the gap. At 203x trailing earnings, that is a long way off.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
