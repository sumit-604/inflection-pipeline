## 4. MATERIAL NEW FINDINGS — adjudication of the A3 deck findings

### 4.1 The MARGIN-GUIDANCE question (DF-11) — determination: WALK-BACK / deferral

**Deck verbatim (p29):**
- L862: "Unlocking Rs.2,500 Cr Revenue with 20% Margins by FY30"
- L873 / L888: "Op.EBITDA Guidance: 19-20% margin by FY30"

**Notion verbatim (L45):** "Mgmt FY27 EBITDA guidance >19% consol."

**Determination.** This is a **WALK-BACK (deferral) of the >19% margin bar from FY27 out to FY30, not a separate long-range target that coexists with a nearer FY27 path.** The basis is the deck's own text: the 19-20% margin band is anchored **solely and explicitly to FY30**, and the deck presents **no FY27 margin milestone anywhere** — the p29 panel gives FY27E, FY28E and FY30E revenue waypoints (1,500 / 1,800 / 2,500 Cr) but a margin figure only "by FY30." There is no FY27 (or FY28) margin number in the deck that would let a nearer >19% target coexist. Q1 FY27 actual is 16.9% (deck L960). On the evidence in front of the reader, the near-term >19% bar recorded in Notion has been quietly pushed ~3 years out.

**Thesis impact: this is a thesis-relevant downgrade of the forecast and is FLAGGED PROMINENTLY.** It bears directly on **watch item 1** (FY27 EBITDA margin toward >19%) and on the **base-case margin assumption** in the destination-PE work. It does not, by itself, fire any thesis-broken trigger (none is keyed to margin guidance), and it does not touch the audited numbers.

**Conservatism caveat (why this still needs a question, not a conclusion).** Management has not, in words, said "we withdraw the FY27 >19% guidance." The deck is simply silent on any FY27 margin figure while relocating the band to FY30. So the honest read is: **the FY27 >19% guidance is neither shown nor reaffirmed in this deck, and the 19-20% band is now presented as an FY30 target.** Whether that is a formal withdrawal of FY27 >19% or merely an unstated near-term path must be resolved directly — hence the **mandatory clarification question Q26** below.

## 7. UPDATED PLAIN-LANGUAGE BRIEF DELTA

DEE put out a 36-slide investor deck one day after results. Every number in it matches the audited accounts once you allow for rounding: revenue Rs.294.5 crore, operating profit Rs.49.7 crore, net profit Rs.16.1 crore, earnings per share Rs.2.32. So the deck is honest on the figures it shows.

What the deck adds is useful. New orders won in the quarter were Rs.780.87 crore against Rs.294 crore of work executed, so intake ran about 2.65 times execution, which is a genuine bull point. It also lays out a plan to FY30: revenue Rs.2,500 crore, growing about 22 percent a year, with a fuller order book, capacity by plant, and a new idea to park extra biomass pellet capacity in an InVIT.

What the deck conspicuously leaves out is the bad news. There is no balance sheet, no debt figure, and no working capital anywhere, so the biggest open question, whether the company turns profit into cash, is still unanswered. The one debt chart stops at FY26 and skips this quarter, the quarter that took in Rs.300 crore of fresh equity and still saw interest cost rise 50 percent. The audit qualification on the Malwa power plant is not mentioned once in 36 slides; instead that plant is presented as a tariff win.

The single most important shift is on margins. Management had guided to above 19 percent operating margin for this year. The deck now shows 19 to 20 percent only by FY30, with no margin target for FY27 at all, while this quarter ran at 16.9 percent. Read plainly, the above-19 goal has been pushed out about three years. Heavy fabrication grew just 3.6 percent, the first of two weak quarters that would ring a warning bell.

Does it change the picture. No, not the verdict. The deck cannot fix an audited flag, and it adds several disclosure flags of its own. Decision Status stays HELD. The current price is Rs.658, still far above the Rs.210 to 262 buy zone, so watchlist or avoid stands. The next real test is the September quarter, for cash, the deferred sales, and whether margins move toward the goal that just got pushed to FY30.
