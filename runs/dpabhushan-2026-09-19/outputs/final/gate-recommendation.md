## 7. Monitorables and triggers

1. Half year cash flow. With the Q2 FY27 results, read H1 FY27 operating cash flow and compare inventory growth with revenue growth. If CFO is negative again while the half added two stores or fewer, the cash drain is price driven and structural. This tests the cash determination.
2. Gold grams. FY26 grams sold fell 13.3%, and management guides about 10% volume growth for FY27 and FY28. Ask for quarterly grams sold and closing stock on every call. Flat or rising grams confirm real demand. Another double digit fall says growth is price. This tests the margin source and the growth claim.
3. Margin in a flat or falling gold quarter. Q4 FY26 printed 5.45% and Q1 FY27 11.0%. EBITDA margin at 8% or above for two quarters, one of them with flat or falling gold, confirms a structural step. A slide back toward 5 to 6% says holding gains did the work. Source: quarterly results and deck. This tests the transition claim.
4. Hedging line. Ask for a hedge ratio every quarter, as peers give. Track the MCX hedging line (Rs 18.6 Cr gain in FY26, inside revenue) for its sign in a falling gold quarter. Track the unfixed gold kg in the FY27 annual report note on commodity price risk. This tests whether profit includes commodity bets.
5. Store openings and the franchise. FY27 net additions of 5 or more support the 51 store path; fewer than 3 break it. Source: Reg 30 opening filings (Dahod, Jabalpur, Jodhpur). For Jabalpur, obtain the franchisee name and terms, and check the related party disclosures. This tests the volume engine and governance.
6. Borrowings and funding. FY26 borrowings were about Rs 290 Cr at 0.45x gearing. CARE downgrades on sustained gearing above 1x or an operating cycle beyond 75 days. Watch Reg 30 for QIP launch or formal abandonment. This tests whether expansion can be funded without a debt spiral.
7. Diamond share. It is 6 to 7% now against a 12 to 15% target for March 2028. Q1 FY27 diamond revenue was Rs 29 Cr against Rs 31 Cr a year earlier. Two straight quarters of diamond outgrowing gold confirm the target. A flat share through FY27 kills it. Source: deck segment split. This tests the mix leg of the margin claim.
8. Board and family. Watch for a fourth independent director in board composition filings. Read the Kataria Dhulchand Pannalal Jewellers DRHP (SEBI site) for a family link and the former company secretary. This tests whether the promoter verdict stays CAUTION or moves to CONCERN.
