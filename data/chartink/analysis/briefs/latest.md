## What would change this read

The read is a four day bounce on top of a falling staircase. It is wide, quiet, and close in tone to the 16 October repair that failed. It turns worse if selling returns before volume confirms the buying. A day of 300 or more hard fallers would repeat the pattern of 21 and 23 October. A long count back under about 580, the 23 October low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows back above about 300 would say the same. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has returned. The first sign would be the short count back under about 450 while turnover rises. That is how the October repair broke.

The cautious read fails if the buying turns heavy and keeps going. The first test is a wide day on turnover above about 611 crore, the 23 October figure. Thursday reached about 440. The second test is the long count back above about 840. Thursday reached 699. The third test is new highs past about 50 while new lows stay under 100. Thursday had 35 highs and 66 lows. A VIX close under 15 would support the turn. The October repair stalled near 800 on the short count. A count above that would show this bounce has gone further. Monday's sheet will carry two sessions of change, and it can pass or fail several of these tests at once.
