# Emerging Moat Score Adjudication — Apex Ecotech Ltd (APEXECO)

Run 2026-07-10. One-to-one rebuttal of an external Emerging Moat score of 41.3 against the pipeline's source-verified scan (B07 = 10.1).

## Provenance

The external scan (an outside LLM tool, "Screener AI") was provided by the operator and scores the same 20-category-plus-R1 taxonomy at 41.3, classifying "MOAT EXPANSION UNDERWAY" and a combined "HIGH POTENTIAL." The pipeline's stage-7 scan (outputs/reports/07-emoat.md, block B07-emoat.yaml), verified by Verifier C (B12c), scores 10.1 (NONE). Both use the same method: raw 0 to 4 on likelihood by impact, times an evidence multiplier (documented 1.0x, management claim 0.7x, inference 0.5x). Because the method is identical, the entire 31.2-point gap is traceable to specific scoring decisions, category by category, below.

## Correcting the multiplier and zeroing the unsupported categories returns 10.1

Apply the framework as written: 0.7x on management claims, 0x on no-evidence, negative, contradicted, or broken categories, and the score falls from 41.3 to 10.1. Verifier C (B12c) independently audited the pipeline scan and found it rule-compliant, with only a 0.2-point arithmetic slip (10.1 vs a 10.3 re-sum) and no category under-credited.

## Why it matters, and where it does not

The external 41 would map to a Pillar 3 growth-visibility premium of +6x (EM >=40, catalyst 0 to 12 months). The correct EM of 10.1 gives +0x. On the destination PE the difference is muted, because the EPC sector cap of 20x binds regardless: raw would move from 24.1x to about 30x, still capped at 20x. So the exit multiple is unchanged.

The real damage is upstream of valuation. The external score flips the combined classification from AVERAGE (no meaningful emerging moat) to HIGH POTENTIAL (moat expansion underway), which is precisely the transition-alpha setup the strategy hunts and would greenlight for deep pursuit. The pipeline's 10.1 says the opposite: an AVERAGE core business with one documented balance-sheet advantage and no evidenced forward moat, which is why the final decision is WATCHLIST, not a buy candidate. The EM adjudication is therefore load-bearing for whether the name is pursued at all, even though it does not move the capped destination PE.

## Note on external factual errors

Two figure errors in the external sheet, immaterial to the EM score but relevant to its reliability: net worth stated as ₹632.6 Cr (actual ~₹63.26 Cr, a 10x overstatement) and "ESOP 2026 docs" cited as documented evidence for a scheme that was dropped without mention. Both point to source material accepted without verification against the filings and the latest concall.
