## Active flag blocks

### FLAG-CASH

⚠️ CASH CONVERSION FLAG: cumulative CFO FY21 to FY26 minus Rs 1,648.00 Cr against cumulative PAT Rs 671.20 Cr, ratio minus 2.46x; outflow narrowing, minus Rs 661.43 Cr FY25 to minus Rs 460.82 Cr FY26 (B01 B1, screener-data).
Determination: INDETERMINATE.

Lens used: this is a lender, so the stages read cash conversion through the loan book, not through trade working capital. Under Ind AS 7 loan disbursal runs through operating cash flow. The question is not whether CFO is negative but whether the book converts to collected cash net of losses across the whole managed book, on and off balance sheet.

Evidence:
- The CFO outflow tracks loan growth: AUM grew 73% in FY26 (B04 must_track_metrics) and 61% year on year to Q1 FY27 (B05 promise_delivery). Standalone CFO/PAT is above 1.2x (B03 strengths_top3). Three stages read the consolidated outflow as a growth artefact (B01 block_b_trend, B03, B04 irrelevant_ratios).
- Loss content of the on book loans: write offs run about 15.7% of the average book (Rs 4,725.95 mn in FY26, up 10.7%), while new Stage 3 inflow grew 28.9% (B02 rank 2; Consolidated Note 41 pp.127-129, Note 26 p.119). The ECL built, released and rebuilt over FY23 to FY26 with no explanation (B02 rank 6; Note 26 p.119, RHP p.318).
- Loss content of the off book loans (53.6% of AUM): first loss guarantee cost more than tripled to about Rs 2.5 bn in FY26 (B02 rank 3; Standalone Note 25 p.84, Consolidated Note 29 pp.119-120). FLDG outstanding at the parent is Rs 737.80 mn (B03; CARO Annexure A, AR p.118). Whether FLDG cost sits in the 6.80% credit cost or in opex went unanswered on both calls, with three conflicting answers on Q1 (B12b I1; Concall_Aug_2026 [page 19-20], Concall_Jun_2026 [page 13]).
- Q1 FY27 collection signals weakened: Stage 2 2.4% to 3.15%, NNPA 0.29% to 0.36%, collection efficiency 97.15% to 96.82% (B12b I3; Concall_Aug_2026 [page 5-6], [page 8-10]).
- Rating agency, verbatim (CRISIL on Si Creva, 13-Feb-2026, inputs/rating/CRISIL-SiCreva-RR-2026-02-13.txt line 59): "Gross non-performing assets (GNPA) stood at 2.95% as on September 30, 2025 and was at similar levels as on March 31, 2025 (2.92%). Nevertheless, write-offs and FLDG cost for trailing 12-months (as % of AUM) improved to 8.5% as on September 30, 2025 from 12.2% as on March 31, 2025. ... While these measures have shown early signs of improvement in the collection efficiency, sustained improvement in asset quality will need to be seen."
- Rating agency, verbatim (same file, lines 63-64): "Liquidity Strong. As on December 31, 2025, the group had unencumbered cash, cash equivalent and liquid investment of Rs 290.4 crore. This is sufficient to cover one month debt repayments. Additionally, the group's average monthly collections stood at over Rs 500 crore over the last six months."
- Note: these two CRISIL passages were retrieved verbatim at synthesis for this flag block. No stage extracted them. Their figures are carried as quotes only and are not reconciled against the stage blocks (for example CRISIL's Mar-25 GNPA of 2.92% against 2.89% in B03).
- Capex commissioning timeline: not applicable to a lender. The analogue is capital deployment: IPO proceeds of Rs 636.8 Cr of Rs 637.5 Cr reached Si Creva with no deviation (B05; monitoring agency report 20260729-4997d936 p.5, p.8).
- Receivables composition: trade receivables are fee receivables, not the loan book. They are clean, with Rs 14.83 mn over six months (B02 receivables_trend; Standalone Note 7 p.76).

Why INDETERMINATE and not GROWTH-INDUCED: the CFO mechanic is growth led, and the rating agency calls liquidity strong. But the loss content of the book cannot be certified. The off book half of AUM carries losses whose size and booking location management would not state, and the on book ratio depends on a write off trigger that no filed note discloses.

Missing evidence to resolve:

- The numeric write off policy in days past due, plus static pool or vintage loss curves (Si Creva annual report, results annexures, or a management query).

## Monitorables and triggers

1. First loss guarantee disclosure. On the Q2 FY27 call or deck, look for FLDG utilisation in basis points of off book AUM, and for whether it sits in credit cost or opex. A third call with no number tests LBF-2 directly and decides whether the 6.80% credit cost covers the whole book.
2. Stage 2 and gross NPA. In the Q2 FY27 results, Stage 2 should fall back near 2.4% and gross NPA below 2.25%. Another rise under a "seasonal" or "range bound" label breaks the asset quality story and moves credibility toward D.
3. Credit cost. Watch annualised credit cost against 6.80% of average AUM in the Q2 FY27 deck. A sequential rise with no stated one off cause kills the guided 10 to 15% reduction.
4. Disbursement against AUM. Compare Q2 FY27 disbursement with Q1's Rs 3,812 Cr. AUM growth that keeps outrunning disbursement means tenure is lifting the book while originations slow. This tests the quality of the growth guidance.
5. The Rs 832 Cr preferential issue. Watch the 14-Oct-2026 vote and the next call for a numeric capital need. Continued silence at CRAR 40.2% keeps LBF-4 open and weakens the capacity case behind the market runway.
6. Parent guarantee ratio. In the FY27 annual report (Standalone Note 32 equivalent), check the guarantee against parent net worth, now 228%, and look for any stated ceiling. A further rise with no ceiling raises the single point funding risk.
7. Cost to income. In the FY27 KPI table, check the ratio against 56.64%. Improvement toward 50% confirms operating leverage; a further rise contradicts it.
8. Revenue margin. In the quarterly deck, compare total income as a share of average AUM with Q1's level, which fell 249 bps in one quarter. It tests whether the planned 8 to 9 point three year margin cut is being offset by lower opex, funding cost and credit cost as management says.
