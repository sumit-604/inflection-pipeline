### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Neochem makes specialty chemicals in one segment. It lists on the NSE SME platform and reports every half year.
2. H1 FY27 revenue was Rs 68.92 Cr. It rose 50.4% on a year ago (res l.184).
3. Against H2 FY26, revenue rose only 6.3%. Growth has slowed to a plateau at a higher level.
4. PAT rose 43.1% to Rs 7.85 Cr (res l.212). Operating EBIT rose 42.6%, so the operating growth is real.

9. H1 had nil tax paid against a Rs 2.63 Cr provision (res l.266). Payables also stretched by 21 days. Both flatter the H1 cash figure.
10. A cyber fraud hit the company in July 2026 (res l.238-239). The note gives no amount, no recovered figure and no balance.
11. Short-term loans and advances rose from Rs 2.17 Cr to Rs 10.19 Cr with no note (res l.171). That is 61% of the H1 working-capital outflow.
