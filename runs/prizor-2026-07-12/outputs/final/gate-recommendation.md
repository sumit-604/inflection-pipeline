## FLAG-CASH (ACTIVE)

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

Cash conversion has been broken across three consecutive years. Operating
cash flow was minus Rs 1.82 Cr in FY24, minus Rs 14.10 Cr in FY25 against PAT
of Rs 10.15 Cr (conversion minus 139%), and plus Rs 0.29 Cr in FY26 against
PAT of Rs 20.76 Cr (conversion about 1.4%) (B02, Cash Flow Statement p.73;
B12b, Inv.Pres. slide32/p33).

Reasoning for INDETERMINATE. The drivers look growth induced: finished goods
inventory grew 281.7% in FY25 against revenue up 99.4% (B02, Note 16/32),
receivables rose from Rs 15.74 Cr to Rs 38.63 Cr into FY26 with the second
half of FY26 carrying 72% of full year revenue (B12b, Inv.Pres. slide26/p27),
and short term borrowings rose from Rs 2.76 Cr to Rs 31.82 Cr funding that
build (B04, Inv.Pres. slide26). That is the signature of a working capital
heavy scale up. Against that, FY26 conversion stayed near 1.4% even as PAT
tripled, which a purely growth induced build would not predict, since the
FY25 inventory spike should have started to unwind. Direct peer Sahasra shows
negative operating cash flow as a structural multi year feature of scaling in
this cohort, with no return to positive guided before FY2028 (B06). The
record cannot confirm growth induced (FY26 conversion did not recover) and
cannot confirm structural (audited FY26 cash flow and receivables ageing are
not available). INDETERMINATE.

Rating agency working capital rationale: NOT FOUND. No rating PDF exists in
the input set (B00).

Missing evidence that would resolve the determination: the credit rating
working capital rationale (obtain the CRISIL or equivalent rationale), and
the full FY26 audited cash flow statement plus receivables ageing schedule
(obtain from the FY26 annual report via BSE and NSE filings when published).

Monitoring metric that would falsify a benign read: next audited or interim
cumulative operating cash flow. Threshold: OCF must turn clearly positive and
reach at least about 40% of PAT. A third straight year near or below zero
while PAT prints above Rs 20 Cr hardens the structural read.

Cap effect: INDETERMINATE caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS before the
confidence band downgrade.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Cumulative operating cash flow, next audited or interim print. Must turn
   clearly positive or narrow sharply from the FY25 minus Rs 14.10 Cr pace and
   the FY26 near zero print. Found in the FY26 cash flow statement. Tests
   whether the burn is a one time build or structural (the central question).

2. Finished goods inventory growth versus revenue growth. Should decelerate
   toward or below revenue growth, from the FY25 gap of 281.7% versus 99.4%.
   Inventory note, FY26 AR. Tests sell in versus sell through.

3. Receivables and the half year revenue split. Receivables ran Rs 15.74 Cr
   to Rs 38.63 Cr and H2 FY26 was 72% of revenue. Watch DSO and the H1 to H2
   split in the FY26 AR. Tests channel loading.

4. Identity of the Rs 3 Cr loan to equity counterparty. Any specific named
   entity in the RPT note or a direct management answer. Resolves the single
   largest open governance question.

5. Actual FY26 and FY27 promoter family remuneration paid, against the
   approved ceiling of up to Rs 2.5 lakh a month each. Employee benefit note
   and board report. Tests the scale of the approved 150% hike.

6. Short term borrowings to trailing EBITDA. Should stay below about 2 times,
   from the FY26 Rs 31.82 Cr level. Balance sheet, FY26 AR. Tests debt funded
   working capital.

7. EBITDA margin against the peer benchmark. Whether Prizor's audited FY26
   margin holds above the CP Plus 13.7% full year and 14 to 15% new normal
   band. FY26 AR. Tests the contradicted margin claim.

8. The Rs 10 Cr new long term loans and advances on the FY26 balance sheet.
   Purpose is undisclosed while short term borrowing sits at Rs 31.82 Cr. FY26
   AR notes. Tests capital allocation and a possible related party.
