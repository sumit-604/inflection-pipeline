premix silica ramming mass, a critical consumable for the induction furnace steel industry.

capabilities. Our installed capacity has grown from approximately 36,000 tons per annum in 
2018 to 5,76,000 tons per annum today, representing a significant scale-up over the last seven 
years.

commissioned greenfield project is undergoing trial runs, strengthening our manufacturing 
platform and supporting the next phase of capacity expansion.

raw material security through acquisition of stone mining assets in Nawada, Bihar. We believe 
this development can strengthen control over raw material availability, quality consistency and 
long-term integration to our operations. 
At the same time, Monolithisch has announced plans to establish two new manufacturing 
facilities in Western and Southern India, representing an important step towards expanding our 
geographical footprint beyond Eastern India and positioning the company closer to the major 
industrial consumer clusters. 
Together, these developments, capacity expansions, backward integration at Nawada and 
geographical expansion into Western and Southern India, represent important building blocks in 
Monolithisch evolution towards a larger, more integrated and geographically diversified 
manufacturing platform.

expected commercial production by November 2027. The land will be around INR6 crores and 
rest will go on plant and machinery.  
Second, Southern India Facility (Near Hospet, Karnataka) : planned with an initial capacity 
of 1,25,000 metric ton per annum and estimate capital expenditure of INR20 crores, and 
expected commercial production by July 2027. This plant will use equipments that have been 
uninstalled from the Monolithisch plant in the last year, resulting in faster commencement and 
lower capex cost. The land will be around INR7 crores and rest will go on plant and machinery.  
Together, these two facilities represent a combined additional capacity of 3 lakh metric ton per 
annum with approx total capital expenditure of just INR45 crores. Upon the successful 
commissioning of these projects, the company's total production capacity is expected to reach 
approximately 8,75,000 metric ton per annum.  
This expansion establishes our manufacturing presence across Eastern, Western and Southern 
India, positioning our facilities closer to key domestic consumption centers where freight from 
Eastern facilities were not feasible.

logistical advantages for our export expeditions in the Middle East and African regions. This 
development will help us establish pan-India presence, whereas fundamental of keeping freight 
cost low for customers intact.
