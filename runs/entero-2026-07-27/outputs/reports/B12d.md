## 2. Peer coverage map audit

11 of 12 transcripts marked SUBSTANTIVE, 1 (Redington Jun 2026) marked CITED-ONLY, none UNUSED — as stated in B06. Row-by-row content of the substantive claims was confirmed plausible for all 11.

**The CITED-ONLY call is wrong, and this is the most consequential finding in this audit.** The Redington Jun 2026 file is not a Q1 FY27 quarterly earnings call as B06's coverage map implies — its own filing cover letter identifies it as the transcript of a "Virtual Investors Event held on June 17, 2026" (an SSG-segment investor/capital-markets day, titled "From Software Distribution to Intelligent Orchestration"). Mislabeling its type is itself a minor accuracy issue, but the larger problem is substantive: this file contains a directly on-point, quantified data point for Claim 8 (Entero's "agency/commercial-role distribution margin premium... at least double normal margin") that B06 never used. Management states explicitly that Redington's professional-services/orchestrator business "has gross margin, which is in strong double digits, more than 20%" against the core SSG gross-margin range of 5.5-6%, and separately states "when you attach services to any offering, there is a 60% margin uplift to any solution." That is a real, disclosed distribution-adjacent margin premium of roughly 3.5-4x — a closer, more decisive analog to Entero's "at least double" claim than the RPTech project/data-center comparison B06 actually used for Claim 8. B06's Claim 8 verdict (UNVERIFIABLE, checked only against RPTech) was reached without this evidence. Whether it would move the verdict all the way to VERIFIED is a judgment call, but it should at minimum have moved it to PARTIALLY VERIFIED with this cited — this is a genuine coverage miss, not a case of a peer being structurally unable to speak to the claim.
