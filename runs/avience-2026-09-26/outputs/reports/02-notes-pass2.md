## NEW FINDING 1 (HIGH PRIORITY): NOTE 40 PROFIT SPLIT MAY BE MISLABELLED -
DIRECTLY CONTRADICTS PASS 1 RED FLAG #1

Cross-checking Note 40 consolidated (Additional Information, Schedule III
format, p.143-144) against two OTHER numbers in the same Annual Report:

- Note 40 FY26 states: "A: Holding company" - 24% of consolidated profit,
  Rs.205.91 lakh; "B: Subsidiary - DR Meditech" - 76% of consolidated profit,
  Rs.669.38 lakh (p.144).
- The Holding Company's OWN standalone Statement of Profit and Loss (Note 4
  standalone, p.89) shows "Profit as per Statement of Profit and Loss" for
  FY26 = Rs.673.33 lakh - within Rs.3.95 lakh of the amount Note 40 LABELS
  "Subsidiary" (Rs.669.38 lakh), not the amount it labels "Holding company."
- AOC-1 (Annexure-1 to Directors' Report, Form AOC-1, PDF p.56) discloses the
  subsidiary DR Meditech's OWN "Profit/Loss for the year": Rs.20,399.66
  THOUSAND = Rs.203.9966 lakh (unit confirmed: AOC-1's investment figure of
  Rs.5,72,98.23 thousand = Rs.572.98 lakh matches Note 14 standalone exactly)
  - within Rs.1.9 lakh of the amount Note 40 LABELS "Holding company"
  (Rs.205.91 lakh), not the amount it labels "Subsidiary."

In other words: the number Note 40 calls the PARENT's profit share (Rs.205.91
lakh) is almost identical to the SUBSIDIARY's own audited standalone profit
(Rs.204.00 lakh, AOC-1), and the number Note 40 calls the SUBSIDIARY's profit
share (Rs.669.38 lakh) is almost identical to the PARENT's own standalone
profit (Rs.673.33 lakh, Note 4). This pattern is consistent with the two
labels in Note 40 having been SWAPPED at the drafting stage.

🔴 Red flag candidate, NOT a confirmed conclusion: the Net Assets side of the
same Note 40 table does not swap as cleanly (Note 40 shows subsidiary net
assets Rs.648.88 lakh / 21%, while AOC-1 shows subsidiary "Net worth
attributable to shareholding" of Rs.1,004.33 lakh - neither the labelled nor
the swapped reading matches this cleanly, which is expected since AOC-1's net
worth figure is pre-consolidation-adjustment (before elimination of the
investment cost against subsidiary equity) while Note 40's net assets figure
is post-adjustment; the two are not directly comparable the way the profit
figures are). FY25 does not resolve as cleanly either: Note 40 labels
"Holding company" profit at Rs.67.14 lakh (9%) and "Subsidiary" at Rs.642.76
lakh (91%), while standalone Note 4's own FY25 profit is Rs.500.21 lakh -
matching neither the labelled nor a clean swapped reading. No FY25 AOC-1 is
in this corpus (it would sit in the FY25 Annual Report, not held) to complete
the FY25 cross-check.

WHY THIS MATTERS: Pass 1's Red Flag #1 ("the profit engine is not where the
equity story is framed... subsidiary generated 76% of consolidated profit")
rests entirely on Note 40's FY26 labels. If those FY26 labels are swapped -
which the near-exact matches to Note 4 and AOC-1 suggest for FY26 specifically
- then the read INVERTS: the listed HOLDING company (whose YEIDA plant ramp
is the actual growth story, LBF1) would have generated 76% of FY26 group
profit, and the subsidiary only 24%. This is a materially different
narrative for the same underlying fact pattern and it is not resolvable from
the documents in this corpus alone (FY25's numbers do not cleanly confirm
either the printed or the swapped reading, and no restated/audited subsidiary
standalone P&L for FY25 sits in this corpus). FLAG for Role 5.5 / claude.ai:
verify directly with the company or against the subsidiary's own FY26
standalone financial statements (not sighted in this corpus - only the
AOC-1 summary line is available) which entity actually produced the Rs.669-
673 lakh profit figure.
(Note 40 consolidated, p.143-144; Note 4 standalone, p.89; AOC-1, p.56)

## NEW FINDING 2: FINANCE COST NOTES CARRY NO INTEREST LINE FOR THE SIDBI
YEIDA LOAN - INDIRECT CONFIRMATION THAT ITS INTEREST IS FULLY CAPITALISED

Pass 1 flagged that the rupee amount of interest capitalised into YEIDA CWIP
is NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT. This pass finds circumstantial but strong
corroborating evidence for full capitalisation: the Finance Cost note lists
every OTHER interest-bearing facility by name (cash credit from banks, term
loan from banks, working capital loan, MSME dues, delayed statutory dues,
loan processing charges) but has NO line item for the "term loan from
financial institution" (i.e., the SIDBI YEIDA loan, Note 5(a)(iii)) in EITHER
the standalone Finance Cost note (Note 28, p.100) or the consolidated Finance
Cost note (Note 30, p.138), despite that loan's balance growing Rs.352.50
lakh in the year (Rs.602.00 lakh to Rs.954.50 lakh, average balance roughly
Rs.778 lakh). 🟡 Watch, ANALYST INFERENCE not a disclosed figure: this
absence is consistent with 100% of the SIDBI loan's FY26 interest cost being
capitalised into CWIP rather than expensed. Illustrative arithmetic only (NOT
a company-disclosed number, do not anchor Role 1 to it): at a rate in the
8.3-9% range (comparable to the company's other institutional facility, the
SIDBI cash credit at 8.30% per Note 8(iii)), an average balance of ~Rs.778
lakh implies roughly Rs.60-70 lakh of capitalised interest for FY26 - a
reasonable illustrative range, not a filed fact. The actual rupee amount
remains NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT and should still be requested directly.
(Note 28 standalone p.100; Note 30 consolidated p.138; Note 5(a)(iii)
standalone p.90-91)

## NEW FINDING 3: MAJORITY OF NET PPE SITS WITH THIRD PARTIES AND IS
EXCLUDED FROM THE COMPANY'S OWN PHYSICAL VERIFICATION

Note 12(b) standalone (p.95) discloses Plant and Equipment with a Gross block
of Rs.996.43 lakh / Net block of Rs.828.14 lakh "held with third parties" as
at FY26 (vs Rs.693.85/600.79 lakh FY25, net block +37.9%). Note 13(b)
consolidated (p.134, cross-checked) shows the group figure: Gross Rs.1,145.50
lakh / Net Rs.920.27 lakh FY26 vs Rs.839.32/693.77 lakh FY25. At standalone
level this is 72% of total net block (Rs.828.14 / Rs.1,149.02 lakh); at
consolidated level a similarly dominant share. 🔴 Red flag candidate: the
Independent Auditor's Report / CARO annexure (cross-referenced, outside the
Notes proper but directly bearing on Note 12(b)'s figures) states plainly
that "all Property, Plant and Equipment... have been physically verified by
the management at reasonable intervals EXCEPT FOR THE ASSETS HELD WITH THIRD
PARTIES" (CARO clause (i)(b)). The majority of the company's fixed assets -
consistent with a placed-instrument / reagent-rental commercial model (the
same model behind B00's flagged Distribution/trading SOTP question) - are
therefore both growing fast and outside the company's own asset-verification
regime. This is a genuine asset-control and existence-risk data point that
Pass 1 did not surface as a standalone finding (it appeared only as a
one-line note in Pass 1's Investments/Other Critical Notes review, without
the CARO cross-reference or the % of total PPE this represents).
(Note 12(b) standalone, p.95; Note 13(b) consolidated, p.134; CARO clause
(i)(b), cross-referenced)
