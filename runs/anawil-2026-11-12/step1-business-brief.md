## The business, numbers first

1. Product. One product line: tubular steel towers for onshore wind turbines, built to the specification of turbine makers (WTG OEMs) and renewable energy companies, plus tower internals (ladders, platforms, cable racks). Despite the name, it sells no wire. Source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ANAWIL_08112026131143_INVESTOR_PRESENTATION.pdf (slides 12, 15).
2. Scale. Revenue Rs 54.1 Cr (FY24), Rs 78.6 Cr (FY25), Rs 143.3 Cr (FY26). PAT Rs 4.4 Cr, Rs 12.3 Cr, Rs 36.0 Cr. Towers made: 114, 135, 210. Same source (slides 17, 25).
3. Latest print, H1 FY27 (half year to 30 Sep 2026). Revenue Rs 153.97 Cr, up 272.2% YoY. EBITDA Rs 36.08 Cr, margin 23.4%. PAT Rs 14.39 Cr. Towers 107 (the presentation also prints 108). Source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ANAWIL_06112026184523_ANAWIL_PRESS_RELEASE_FINAL.pdf
4. Guidance. FY27 revenue Rs 450 to 500 Cr and PAT Rs 55 to 60 Cr. H2 must deliver Rs 296 to 346 Cr revenue and Rs 40.6 to 45.6 Cr PAT (arithmetic on the press release figures). Management says H2 carries 70 to 80% of annual revenue. Same press release; presentation slide 21.
5. Order book. Rs 395.58 Cr confirmed at 31 Oct 2026, 320 towers, 8 customers, plus letters of intent of Rs 44.05 Cr for 44 towers. Execution targeted by end of Q1 FY28. Customer A holds Rs 144.6 Cr (36.6%). Presentation slide 20.
6. Where the margin comes from. Three contract models. Model 1 pure conversion (customer supplies steel): revenue Rs 50 to 60 lakh per tower, EBITDA Rs 25 to 30 lakh, margin 44 to 50%. Model 2 conversion plus internals: Rs 1.0 to 1.1 Cr, EBITDA Rs 35 to 40 lakh, 33 to 37%. Model 3 integrated supply (Anawil buys the steel): Rs 3.0 to 3.5 Cr, EBITDA Rs 40 to 50 lakh, 14 to 16%. Presentation slide 14.
7. Mix shift. Model 3 was 64.3% of H1 FY27 revenue and 51.3% of the order book. EBITDA margin fell from 42.7% (FY26) to 23.4% (H1 FY27) while EBITDA per tower rose to Rs 32.82 lakh, up 104.7% YoY. Press release.
8. Capacity. Koppal, Karnataka: 420 towers a year, running since December 2022. Kutch, Gujarat: 192 towers a year, started March 2026, approvals and ramp-up under way. Total 612. Utilisation 38%, 45%, 48% in FY24 to FY26. Presentation slide 17.
9. Customers and geography. Top five customers were 89% of FY25 revenue and 79% of FY26 revenue; the largest was 29% in FY26. Karnataka was 93.9% of FY26 revenue. Order descriptions name SANY and Envision turbine towers. No exports disclosed. Presentation slides 20, 22.
10. Balance sheet and IPO. NSE SME IPO, bids 3 to 5 Aug 2026, listed 10 Aug 2026, price Rs 270, issue Rs 177.81 Cr (fresh issue Rs 142.69 Cr, offer for sale Rs 35.12 Cr), lead manager Hem Securities. Sources: https://www.kotakneo.com/ipo/anawil-wire-and-engineering-ltd-ipo/ and https://zerodha.com/ipo/454133/anawil-wire-and-engineering/. Borrowings fell from about Rs 128 Cr (Mar 2026) to about Rs 34 Cr (Sep 2026); net worth rose from Rs 89.5 Cr to Rs 226.3 Cr. Presentation slide 26.
11. Market data. CMP Rs 391, market cap Rs 977 Cr, P/E 20.0, promoters 65.26%, FII 1.73%, DII 10.23% (Sep 2026). Source: https://www.screener.in/company/ANAWIL/
12. Sector backdrop the company cites. India wind capacity 58.52 GW at 31 Aug 2026; record 6.05 GW added in FY26; 28 GW under implementation; 100 GW target by 2030. Presentation slide 9. Stage 9 must test these against independent sources.
13. The one engine. Volume ramp on capacity already built (612 towers against 210 made in FY26), with a wider scope of supply per tower. The thesis rests on towers per year and EBITDA per tower, not on percentage margin.

## Archetype read (for the Halt 1 draft, not a ruling)

Build-to-spec component maker with an order-book overlay. Model 1 is job work on customer steel. Model 3 carries steel on Anawil's own books, so working capital and steel price exposure rise with it. Stage 4 decides whether the Commodity converter archetype applies to the Model 3 slice.

## Peers (auto-selected, no listed pure-play tower maker with earnings calls was found)

- KP Green Engineering Ltd (BSE SME 544150), https://www.screener.in/company/544150/consolidated/ : closest listed product comp. It fabricates steel structures for wind and solar, including windmill towers, for the same customer type.
- Inox Wind Ltd (INOXWIND), https://www.screener.in/company/INOXWIND/consolidated/ : turbine maker that builds towers in house. Its calls give execution pace, tower sourcing and the make-or-buy decision that sets Anawil's demand.
- Suzlon Energy Ltd (SUZLON), https://www.screener.in/company/SUZLON/consolidated/ : the largest Indian turbine maker. Its order book and commissioning pace are the industry volume read, and it also makes towers in house.

Windar Renewables, GRI Towers India and other dedicated tower makers are unlisted. Peer two and peer three are customers-of-the-category, not competitors; the brief says so openly.

The CARE monitoring report says Rs 142.56 Cr of IPO proceeds was used by 30 Sep 2026, seven weeks after listing. Verify the use against the prospectus objects, and check related-party items in the prospectus.
