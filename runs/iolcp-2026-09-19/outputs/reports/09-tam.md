## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A Precise boundaries

**Product scope.** IOLCP is not a diversified pharma company selling into "the API market."
It makes a named, finite basket of generic small-molecule APIs plus a chain of acetyl
chemicals, most of it commodity-priced:

- **Pharma APIs (11 named molecules):** Ibuprofen and salts (12,000 MTPA, expanding to
  18,000 MTPA by Dec 2027), Paracetamol (10,800 MTPA, ~55% utilised), Metformin,
  Clopidogrel, Pantoprazole, Fenofibrate, Levetiracetam, Lamotrigine, Losartan, Minoxidil,
  Sitagliptin (Investor Presentation Q4FY26/Q1FY27, product tables).
- **Specialty/acetyl chemicals:** Ethyl Acetate (1,20,000 MTPA), Acetic Anhydride
  (32,000 MTPA), Iso Butyl Benzene (12,000 MTPA, captive input to Ibuprofen), Mono Chloro
  Acetic Acid (7,200 MTPA, captive), Acetyl Chloride (5,200 MTPA, captive), Triacetin
  (6,000 MTPA, new FY26). Part captive (into Ibuprofen/Paracetamol), part external merchant
  sale. Total company installed capacity 2,13,126 MTPA (API 30,726 MTPA + Specialty
  Chemicals 1,82,400 MTPA) (Investor Presentation Q1FY27 p.14).
- **Emerging, pre-commercial:** CDMO formulations (1,500 mn tablets/yr or DCG-equivalent,
  EU-GMP certified, commercial Q3 FY27) and a dedicated specialty-chemicals tolling line
  under a long-term arrangement with an unnamed "leading global chemical company"
  (Concall_Sep_2026_Transcript.txt p.3-4).
- **Explicitly excluded:** biologics, biosimilars, peptides, oncology/HPAPIs, injectables,
  branded/patented formulations, any B2C or OTC-branded channel. IOLCP sells bulk API and
  (from Q3 FY27) bulk tablets to formulators, never a consumer brand of its own.

**Geographic scope.** Global manufacture from a single integrated site (Barnala, Punjab)
serving 80+ countries; FY26 export mix 24% (Q4FY26 IP) rising to 29% in Q1 FY27 (both IP,
"Geographical Break-up" slide). Regulatory access: 14 USDMFs, 21 CEPs (EDQM), USFDA,
EU-GMP, ANVISA (Brazil), NMPA (China, active from Apr-2025), MFDS (Korea), Russian GMP.

**Customer scope.** B2B only: generic-drug formulators and API/intermediate buyers
("all A-grade customers," per Q4FY26 concall), plus from Q3 FY27 a small number of
European CDMO "anchor customers" and one chemicals tolling counterparty, both currently
undisclosed by name (Reg 30 filing 09-Sep-2026, confidentiality clause; carried forward
from B04/B07 input_gaps).

**Channel scope.** Direct long-term supply contracts (3-5 year tenor, per Sep-2026 concall)
for API; emerging tolling/CDMO fee-for-service for the two new lines. No distribution,
retail, or e-commerce channel.

**Price segment.** Commodity/generic-priced across the core (Ibuprofen, Ethyl Acetate =
~72% of FY25 revenue per CARE rationale Jul-2025, still the two largest single products in
FY26); partial differentiation on newer non-Ibuprofen APIs via DMF/CEP-based switching
costs, not brand or patent protection.

## SECTION 2: TAM ESTIMATION, MULTIPLE METHODS

### Method 1 — Top-down (boundary check, not the TAM)

Global API market USD 256.4bn (2025). Generic APIs (the only segment IOLCP participates
in; it makes no biologics/HPAPIs/peptides) are "58.4%" of this and "expected to grow at
~8.2% CAGR to 2034" (IP Q1FY27 p.24) = **USD 149.7bn (2025)**, generic-API segment.

No source in the corpus or web search breaks this further by therapeutic category (pain/
anti-inflammatory, analgesic/antipyretic, anti-diabetic, anti-platelet, PPI,
anti-cholesterol, anti-convulsant, anti-hypertensive — the ~8 categories IOLCP's 11
molecules actually sit in) against the ~30-40 major categories inside "generic APIs."
**NOT FOUND: category-level split of the $149.7bn.** Method 1 therefore only establishes
an outer boundary; it is not usable as a standalone TAM and is not fed into the
triangulation table below as a numeric input. It does confirm the Section 1B read: the
company-cited headline sits at least an order of magnitude above any credible
product-scoped number.

### Method 2 — Bottom-up (product-level, anchored)

**Ibuprofen** (IOLCP's largest single product, 37.9% of FY26 revenue per B04):
- Global production volume: web search returns a wide range (35,000 MT to 120,000 MT/yr
  across market-research firms); the credible range is **35,000-40,000 MT**, cross-checked
  against IOLCP's own capacity (12,000 MTPA) and its claimed 30-35% global share (screener
  "About," corroborated by three independent secondary sources — tradebrains.in,
  darkhorsestocks.com, chemxpert.com — all citing 30-35%): 12,000 / 35,000 = 34.3%,
  12,000 / 40,000 = 30.0%. The claim holds up against this cross-check.
- IOLCP's own realisation: Ibuprofen revenue (FY26, 37.9% of Rs2,319.06 Cr, B04) =
  Rs 878.9 Cr. Volume at 90-95% utilisation of 12,000 MTPA (Sep-2026 concall, "capacity is
  exhausted up to the level of 90% to 95%") = ~11,100 MT. Revenue/MT = Rs 791,800/MT.
  At INR/USD 95.76 (Federal Reserve H.10, 18-Sep-2026 spot; STALE-risk: single-day spot,
  not an average — used because no FY26-average IOLCP-specific rate is in the corpus) =
  **$8,268/MT**.
- Global Ibuprofen API TAM = 35,000-40,000 MT x $8,268/MT = **$289-331mn ≈ Rs 2,770-3,170 Cr**.

**Paracetamol** (IOLCP capacity 10,800 MTPA, ~55% utilised, task brief):
- Global market value: multiple sources converge near **$1,390-1,430mn (2025)**
  (marketreportsworld.com, globalgrowthinsights.com, reports.valuates.com — three
  independent estimates within a 3% band, unusually tight agreement for this exercise).
  Global volume 210,000-275,000 MT (2024, two sources).
- IOLCP paracetamol-specific revenue is **NOT FOUND** as a standalone line (bundled with
  Metformin/Clopidogrel/Pantoprazole/etc. in B04's "Non-Ibuprofen API basket," 22.3% of
  revenue = Rs 517.2 Cr for the whole basket). Volume cross-check only: capacity 10,800
  MTPA x 55% = ~5,940 MT vs global volume 210,000-275,000 MT implies **IOLCP paracetamol
  volume share ~2-3% of global production** — a minor, non-leadership share, structurally
  different from the Ibuprofen position.
- Global Paracetamol API TAM ≈ **$1,390-1,430mn ≈ Rs 13,310-13,690 Cr**.

**Sum, two anchored molecules only:** $1,679-1,761mn ≈ **Rs 16,080-16,860 Cr**. The other
9 named molecules (Metformin, Clopidogrel, Pantoprazole, Fenofibrate, Levetiracetam,
Lamotrigine, Losartan, Minoxidil, Sitagliptin) each carry their own global generic-API
market, individually plausible in the low hundreds of millions to low billions of USD, but
**none was searched given the effort budget for this stage; each is NOT FOUND**. Method 2
therefore understates the true molecule-basket TAM. It is used below as the conservative
floor precisely because it is the most tightly anchored number, not because it is complete.

### Method 3 — Peer revenue aggregation

B04 names the identifiable listed peer set for IOLCP's combined API + acetyl-chemicals
business: Aarti Drugs (diversified APIs, no confirmed Ibuprofen per B06), Granules India
(Paracetamol/PFI/formulations + peptide CDMO), Laxmi Organic (ketene chemistry/acetyls).
FY26 standalone sales (screener CSVs, FY ending 31-Mar-2026):

| Company | FY26 Sales (Rs Cr) |
|---|---|
| Aarti Drugs | 2,565.31 |
| Granules India | 5,365.64 |
| Laxmi Organic | 2,846.67 |
| IOLCP | 2,319.06 |
| **Organized 4-company pool** | **13,096.68** |

Product overlap with IOLCP is partial, not exact (flagged, not smoothed away): Granules is
paracetamol/formulations-led with a peptide CDMO (not IOLCP's tablet-CDMO shape); Laxmi is
acetyls/ketene-led; Aarti is diversified but does not name Ibuprofen in four transcripts
(B06 input_gap). Adding an unorganised-sector estimate: this niche (GMP/DMF/USFDA-gated
bulk API and acetyl-chemical manufacture) carries a materially lower informal-sector share
than typical Indian small-cap sectors (30-60% range cited in the framework) because
regulatory compliance is a real entry barrier; a **conservative +30% unorganised loading**
is used (stated assumption, not sourced) = Rs 13,096.68 / 0.70 ≈ **Rs 18,710 Cr**.

### Method 4 — Import substitution (directional only)

AR FY26 (p.45-46): PLI scheme for bulk drugs — investment mobilised Rs 4,763.34 Cr against
a target of Rs 4,329.95 Cr (as at 31-Mar-2026), covering "26 critical APIs, KSMs and drug
intermediates" (p.46) / elsewhere "48 critical fermentation-based and chemically
synthesised APIs" (p.47) previously import-dependent (the AR itself is internally
inconsistent on the count, 26 vs 48 — both citations kept, neither resolved in-corpus).
**Molecule-level import/domestic-production/consumption split for IOLCP's specific 11
products is NOT FOUND** in the AR or any injected document. This method contributes
directional tailwind evidence (a live, over-target government substitution programme) but
no numeric TAM input.

### Method 5 — Global benchmark: not used

Per-capita/regional benchmarking (vs China, SE Asia) suits a domestic-consumption
business. IOLCP is a global B2B exporter of bulk intermediates to formulators, not a
consumer-facing business with a domestic per-capita demand curve. Applying this method
would import noise, not signal; skipped with this stated rationale. Minimum method count
(2-3) is met by Methods 1-4 (Method 1 as boundary check only).

Per the standard read (>2x likely inflated), this is **inflated** — not a borderline call.
The company's slide is a legitimate scene-setter for the pharma/chemicals macro story; it
is not, and should not be read as, IOLCP's addressable market.

## SECTION 3: SAM & SOM

### 3A SAM — five filters applied to the realistic TAM (Rs 18,700 Cr)

| Filter | Retained | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | 90% | Peer set (Method 3 base) already product-comparable; residual haircut for adjacent categories peers touch that IOLCP does not (e.g. Granules' broader formulations book) |
| Geography | 95% | 14 DMFs (USFDA), 21 CEPs (EDQM), ANVISA, NMPA (China, active Apr-2025), MFDS Korea, Russian GMP — most target geographies already covered; small haircut for filings still "under review" (USDMF for Paracetamol, Pantoprazole, Metformin per IP regulatory-filings table) |
| Channel | 100% | TAM base (peer revenue) is already B2B bulk/contract-supply, IOLCP's exact channel |
| Customer | 95% | Small haircut for near-term customer concentration on the two new pre-commercial lines (single/anchor-customer dependence, B04/B07 flag) |
| Capability | not applied here (see 3C) | Capacity is a SOM constraint, not a market-addressability filter |

SAM = 18,700 x 0.90 x 0.95 x 1.00 x 0.95 ≈ **Rs 15,190 Cr**, rounded **Rs 15,200 Cr**.
**SAM as % of TAM ≈ 81.3%.** (High because the "realistic TAM" is itself a narrow,
company-comparable base, not the broad industry headline from Section 1B — a SAM this
close to TAM is a direct consequence of scoping TAM tightly at the outset, not a stage
error.)

### 3B SOM at 3 and 5 years

**Current SAM share:** IOLCP FY26 revenue Rs 2,319.06 Cr / SAM Rs 15,200 Cr = **15.3%**.
This is unusually high for a small/micro-cap TAM exercise. The structural reason: IOLCP is
one of only four organized, listed, comparable-scale players the Method-3 base can find in
this specific niche; when "the market" is largely defined by a handful of listed peers, one
of those peers mechanically holds a large share of it. This is a named methodological
feature of Method 3, not a bottom-up finding that IOLCP has captured 15% of a genuinely
open global market — flagged explicitly rather than smoothed over.

**Share-gain assumption:** 3pp over 3 years, cumulative 5pp over 5 years — the
"aggressive" band (3-5pp, per the standard share-gain rules), justified by real dated
capex (Rs 495 Cr tranche, EU-GMP-certified CDMO facility already built) and by B07's
finding that the dated tranche alone implies 39.3% capex-embedded growth (close to
management's own 25-30% incremental guide) — but not the top of the aggressive band, given
the named single/anchor-customer concentration risk on both new lines (B04/B07 flags) and
the industry-wide capacity race among all three listed peers simultaneously (B06).

**SAM growth (tam_growth_pct):** blended 5%/yr — Ibuprofen "3% to 4% every year" (Rakesh
Mahajan, Sep-2026 concall, a direct management quote, not an estimate), Paracetamol global
~4.5% CAGR (web search), generic APIs 8.2% CAGR and Indian API 7.78-8.10% CAGR (company
sources), Indian specialty chemicals 3.65-4.8% CAGR (company sources, both variants). A
molecule basket this weighted toward two mature, single-digit-growth anchor products
(Ibuprofen + Paracetamol = 60%+ of FY26 revenue) does not support using the faster
segment-level headline rates; 5% is the stated blend.

| | SAM (grown) | Share | SOM | FY26 base | Implied revenue CAGR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 3 | Rs 17,596 Cr (15,200 x 1.05^3) | 18.3% | **Rs 3,220 Cr** | Rs 2,319.06 Cr | **11.6%** |
| Year 5 | Rs 19,400 Cr (15,200 x 1.05^5) | 20.3% | **Rs 3,940 Cr** | Rs 2,319.06 Cr | **11.2%** |

This SOM-implied CAGR (~11-12%) sits **below** both management's near-term guidance
(15-20% FY27 revenue growth) and its stated capex payoff (25-30% incremental once the
three Sep-2026 initiatives fully ramp, ~FY29). The gap is the point of this stage: SOM is a
bottom-up, share-gain-disciplined number; management's 25-30% figure is a capacity/asset-
turn claim (peak revenue at full utilisation of specific new lines), not a market-share
claim, and the two should not be read as the same thing.

### 3C Capacity cross-check

Management's own ceiling: "if we achieve 100% for all, this will be around Rs 3,200 crores
to Rs 3,300 crores" — full utilisation of **pre-Sep-2026** installed capacity
(Concall_May_2026_Transcript.txt p.13, Abhay Raj Singh). Add the two dated, Sep-2026
announced lines: Ibuprofen expansion peak revenue ~Rs 600-620 Cr (1.75x-2x asset turn on
~Rs 350 Cr capex, per Sep-2026 concall; optimal utilisation only reached in FY28-29, so
partially phased into the 3-5yr window) and CDMO peak revenue ~Rs 100-150 Cr (1x-1.25x
asset turn on ~Rs 110 Cr capex at 90% utilisation; "optimal utilization will be achieved in
18-24 months," i.e. within the 3yr window). The specialty-chemicals tolling line's revenue
is customer-led and **undisclosed — NOT FOUND**. The Rs 1,200-1,400 Cr greenfield carries
no commissioning date across 4 consecutive call touchpoints (B07 FLAG-CAPEX) and is
excluded from this ceiling entirely, consistent with B07's instruction to weight the dated
tranche, not the undated one.

**Near-term dated-capacity ceiling ≈ Rs 3,900-4,070 Cr** (Rs 3,200-3,300 Cr base + Rs
600-620 Cr Ibuprofen + Rs 100-150 Cr CDMO, tolling excluded as unquantified upside).

- **Year 3 (SOM Rs 3,220 Cr) vs ceiling (Rs 3,900-4,070 Cr): sufficient**, ~Rs 680-850 Cr
  of headroom. Neither side is the optimistic one at this horizon.
- **Year 5 (SOM Rs 3,940 Cr) vs ceiling (Rs 3,900-4,070 Cr): sufficient but thin**, roughly
  Rs 0-130 Cr of headroom depending where in the range actual utilisation lands. Any
  slippage in Ibuprofen-expansion or CDMO ramp timing (both plausible, given the greenfield
  precedent of repeated undated slippage) would flip this to a gap. The undisclosed tolling
  revenue and the undated greenfield are the two named sources of unpriced upside that
  would relieve this, neither counted in the base case.

**capacity_check: "sufficient, thin margin at Yr5 (~Rs0-130 Cr headroom); greenfield and
tolling revenue excluded as unquantified/undated upside, not required to hit SOM."**

### 4C Market structure

- **Competitor count / concentration (Ibuprofen):** IOLCP, Solara Active Pharma, Shandong
  Xinhua Pharmaceutical (China) and BASF (US) collectively hold "approximately 40%" of
  global production capacity (chemxpert.com, web search); most other producers carry
  capacity under 5,000 MT/yr. IOLCP alone is the largest single producer.
- **Organised vs unorganised:** the four-company listed-peer pool (Method 3) is the visible
  organised core of this specific product niche; smaller/regional API and acetyl-chemical
  producers exist but GMP/DMF/USFDA compliance is a real barrier to informal-sector entry,
  unlike more fragmented Indian small-cap sectors.
- **Consolidating or fragmenting:** capacity is expanding industry-wide (all four
  comparable companies simultaneously building), which raises the risk of oversupply even
  as the organised core stays narrow — a "fragmenting on volume, concentrated on players"
  pattern.
- **Price vs differentiation:** dominant mode is price/commodity competition (Ibuprofen,
  Ethyl Acetate — B04 rates company-wide pricing_power "weak"); non-Ibuprofen APIs carry
  partial differentiation via DMF/CEP-based switching costs, not brand or patent protection.
- **Import share trend:** upstream, IOLCP and its peers remain import-dependent on Chinese
  feedstock (DCDA, and majority of chemicals raw material per CARE rationale); downstream,
  India's PLI programme is actively substituting imports at the KSM/intermediate level
  (Rs 4,763 Cr mobilised, AR p.46), a structural tailwind still working through the system.

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY & RUNWAY

### 5A Funnel

```
TAM (conservative, Method 2 floor)........... Rs 16,000 Cr
TAM (realistic, Method 3)..................... Rs 18,700 Cr
  -> SAM (5-filter, 81.3% of realistic TAM)... Rs 15,200 Cr
      -> SOM Year 3 (18.3% of grown SAM)...... Rs  3,220 Cr
      -> SOM Year 5 (20.3% of grown SAM)...... Rs  3,940 Cr
FY26 actual revenue............................ Rs  2,319 Cr (current SAM share 15.3%)
```

### 5B Runway assessment

- **Revenue headroom** = SAM / current revenue = 15,200 / 2,319.06 = **6.55x**.
- **TAM growth rate** ≈ 5%/yr (blended, Section 3B reasoning).
- **Company CAGR vs TAM:** FY26 revenue grew 11.5% YoY (IP Q1FY27 p.5) — well above the 5%
  blended market growth rate. **IOLCP is gaining share, not riding the market**, consistent
  with the non-Ibuprofen mix-shift thesis (37% of Pharma revenue FY26 vs 18% FY21, IP
  p.11).
- **Years to saturate SAM** at management's own guided 15-20% growth (midpoint 17.5%):
  2,319.06 x (1.175)^n = 15,200 → n ≈ **11.6 years**. Comfortably beyond the 3-5yr thesis
  horizon; SAM is not a near-term ceiling on the transition thesis at company-guided growth
  rates, though it becomes binding well within a normal multi-cycle hold.

### 5C Runway classification: **GOOD**

(Working convention used: MASSIVE >20x, STRONG 10-20x, GOOD 5-10x, MODERATE 2-5x,
LIMITED <2x — this banding was not supplied in the injected instructions and is stated
here as the analyst's applied convention.) 6.55x headroom sits mid-GOOD: real, multi-year
runway inside a narrowly (and conservatively) scoped SAM, not a "massive" open market and
not a name running out of room either.
