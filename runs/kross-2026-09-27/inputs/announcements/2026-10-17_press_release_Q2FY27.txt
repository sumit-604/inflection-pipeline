Q2FY27 Performance Summary: 
• 
Revenue from Operations increased 79.6% YoY to INR 2,350.9 million in Q2FY27 from INR 
1,309.2 million in Q2FY26. 
• 
EBITDA grew 107.4% YoY to INR 306.0 million from INR 147.5 million. 
• 
Profit After Tax (PAT) increased 143.5% YoY to INR 196.9 million from INR 80.8 million. 
• 
EBITDA Margin expanded by 175 bps to 13.0% from 11.3%. 
• 
PAT Margin improved by 220 bps to 8.4% from 6.2%. 
• 
Broad-based segmental growth was supported by strong demand in the M&HCV and trailer 
segments, a continued recovery in the tractor and agricultural segments, and capacity 
expansion initiatives. 
• 
Operational efficiency and backward integration helped support performance amid input cost 
pressures. 
 
Strategic Developments: 
• 
Precision hydraulic Tipping Jacks: Supplies progressing in line with plan; further volume scale-
up expected in H2 FY27. 
• 
Axle beam extrusion plant: Commercial production has commenced; current utilisation 
approximately 60-65%. 
• 
Seamless tube facility: Mills installed, production trials targeted for February-March 2027.  
• 
Forging capacity Expansion initiatives on track to support higher volumes and improved cost 
competitiveness. 
• 
High-pressure moulding line (Foundry) Progressing as planned to double existing casting 
capacity. 
• 
Axle Shaft production by Robotic Forging facility: Strategic increase in shaft manufacturing 
capacity & enhanced investment in Robotic Automation for forging operations and specialized 
machining facilities

• 
Cold Drawn Tubes: Forward Integration into manufacturing of Cold Drawn Tubes 
These investments underscore Kross Limited’s commitment to adopting innovative technologies and

Commenting on the performance, Mr. Sudhir Rai – Chairman & Managing Director said 
 
“Kross Limited has continued its strong momentum in Q2 FY27, with year-on-year Revenue, EBITDA & 
PAT growth of 79.6%, 107.4% & 143.5% respectively. Capacity expansion investments are beginning to 
support growth, with utilisation expected to improve in the coming quarters. Supported by healthy 
order books, sustained demand in the trailer and tractor segments, and multiple capacity expansion 
initiatives nearing completion, Kross is well positioned to deliver healthy growth in FY27. Our focus 
remains on profitable growth, operational efficiency, customer relationships, and disciplined execution. 
Through continued emphasis on innovation, operational excellence, and strategic capacity investments, 
we are capitalising on the recovery in key segments while strengthening our competitive position. We 
remain confident that these initiatives, combined with strong execution visibility and contributions from 
new products, will drive sustainable revenue growth, improved profitability, and long-term value 
creation for our shareholders.” 
 
About the company 
Kross Limited is an integrated automotive component manufacturer with over 3 decades of operating 
history, it specialises in trailer axles, suspension assemblies, and safety-critical forged, cast and machined 
components for M&HCVs, tractors, and off-highway vehicles. The company is backward integrated 
across design, forging, casting, heat treatment, machining, and surface coating, and is among the 
prominent trailer axle & suspension manufacturers in India. Company is a key supplier to various OEMs, 
including leading players in the commercial vehicle and tractor segments.
