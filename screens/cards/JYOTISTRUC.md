## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Jyoti Structures builds power transmission lines and substations on turnkey EPC contracts, up to 800 kV HVDC (AR26, p.55). It designs and tests towers at Ghoti, makes them at Nashik, and erects the line at site (PG-PR, p.2). FY26 revenue was Rs 750.87 crore and profit Rs 56.04 crore (AR26, p.90). Customers are Adani, Vedanta, ReNew, Apraava, Torrent and PGCIL (AR26, p.55); the top five were 72.40% of H1 FY25 revenue (LOF, p.34).

**What is changing?** The company left insolvency on 9-Nov-2021 under an NCLT plan that gave control to a board with no promoter (LOF, pp.71-73). It has since rebuilt an operating engine: total income Rs 231 crore in FY23 to Rs 772 crore in FY26 (AR26, p.3), a second plant in Jan-2026 for 72,000 MT a year in all (AR26, p.16), and PGCIL re-entered with an 800 kV HVDC package in Jan-2025 (PG-Khavda, p.1). The claimed next step is scale: Rs 2,274 crore of orders at 1-Apr-2026 (AR26, p.16) plus Rs 2,356 crore won since (SL, GETCO, PG-TL01, PG-TL05, p.1 each).

**Why now?** Three Power Grid 800 kV HVDC packages and one GETCO 765 kV line landed between 23-Oct and 28-Oct-2026, Rs 2,288 crore in six days (GETCO, p.1; PG-TL01, p.1; PG-TL05, p.1). Q1 FY27 revenue rose 61% and profit 74% (Q1 res, p.2). The share has fallen from Rs 22.19 in Apr-2025 to Rs 10.3 (AR26, p.47; MD).

**What must be true?** The new orders must execute above the 6.4% FY26 operating EBITDA margin (step 5). Banks must release the non-fund limits the plan promised, undelivered for five years (NCLAT, p.1). The Rs 311 crore due to secured creditors in plan year six must be paid or settled at net present value without heavy dilution (LOF, p.72). The tax shield must last.

**What breaks it?** Year-six and year-seven instalments of Rs 311 crore and Rs 356 crore meet Rs 97 crore of cash and a Rs 269 crore operating cash outflow (LOF, p.72; AR26, p.89; FY26 res, p.3). The NCD liability accretes Rs 159 crore a year outside the P&L (AR26, p.129). Three of the four top officers changed in five months (CEO-res, p.1; Chair, p.1; CFO, p.1).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Qualification: present.** 37,600 circuit km built, 1,800 bays, 488 tower types tested to 1,200 kV, and three 800 kV HVDC packages from Power Grid (PG-PR, pp.2-3; LOF, p.167). **Integration: forming.** Two Nashik plants and the Ghoti station put design, test, make and erect under one roof (AR26, p.9); the LOF says this lets it bid lump-sum turnkey work with fewer rivals (LOF, p.167). **Pricing power: absent.** The work is tender-based; the 6.4% FY26 operating EBITDA margin is the evidence (step 5). Top ten customers were 93.84% of H1 FY25 revenue (LOF, p.34). **Funding: a handicap.** Banks have not released non-fund limits since 2021, so guarantees run on margin money from equity (LOF, pp.72, 84; NCLAT, p.1).

**Verdict: an execution and qualification moat is present; no cost or price moat; the funding side is weaker than any peer's.**

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED on orders and revenue, NOT on cash; margin half-fired.** Rs 2,356 crore of FY27 inflow and three rising revenue quarters; a rising margin line with one noisy quarter and one clean quarter above 7%; operating cash flow negative in both years and in H1 FY26 (step 8).
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** The 2% ROCE and negative cash flow would be artifacts of a working-capital climb. The Rs 3,674 crore schedule and the Rs 159 crore a year of off-P&L accretion are contract terms, not growing pains. They change only through prepayment at NPV, which needs equity the company does not have (AR26, pp.89, 129).
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Trailing PE is 19.1 on untaxed, pre-accretion profit (MD). Market cap of Rs 1,231 crore plus Rs 2,083 crore of debt less Rs 246 crore of cash is about Rs 3,068 crore against Rs 70 crore of EBITDA, 44x (derived). The R1 neighbourhood is about 12x earnings.

**Posture: AVOID overlay (gap CLOSED + STRUCTURAL), with a CONTRADICTION flag** because the proof gate fired on the operating side. The flag to resolve is whether the secured debt can settle below NPV; the Jun-2025 series settled at 36% of face says it can, once (NCD-OTS, p.2).

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The order book is real and large. The company is a working EPC contractor again. Equity holders, though, sit behind Rs 3,674 crore of scheduled payments, own earnings that are untaxed and exclude Rs 159 crore a year of debt accretion, and pay 19 times those earnings. A full run would spend its effort on the capital structure, not the transition.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. The current secured-creditor instalment schedule after NCLT exclusions, and what fell due in Nov-2025 and Nov-2026 (LOF, p.72).
2. The Q1 FY27 operating margin of 8.5% with no write-back behind it, against Q2 FY27 when filed (Q1 res, p.2).
3. Whether any non-fund limits were released after the 26-May-2026 NCLAT order, which undid the 16-Feb-2026 NCLT direction the lenders appealed on 27-Feb-2026 (NCLT-3, p.1; Appeal, p.1; NCLAT, p.1).
4. The terms of the Jun-2025 NCD settlement at Rs 4.12 crore on Rs 11.49 crore face, and whether secured creditors will take the same (NCD-OTS, p.2).

**What would change the view**
- Up to WATCH: a filed settlement of the secured-creditor NCDs below NPV with under 30% dilution, plus Q2 FY27 operating EBITDA above 8% and positive operating cash flow for the half.
- Up to PROCEED: the above, plus non-fund limits released and a year of operating cash flow above profit.
- Confirmed PASS: an equity raise near the market price to meet the year-six instalment, or a missed instalment.
