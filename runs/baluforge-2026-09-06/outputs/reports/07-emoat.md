### 2C Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic, and why the standard method fails here

The instructed method is: capex under execution × historical fixed asset
turnover = implied incremental revenue. Attempting this literally:

- Capex under execution (consolidated CWIP, FY25) = Rs 417.11 Cr.
- Consolidated net PPE, FY25 = Rs 146.80 Cr; FY24 = Rs 24.17 Cr.
- Consolidated revenue from operations, FY25 = Rs 923.62 Cr; FY24 = Rs 559.86 Cr.
- "Historical fixed asset turnover" (Revenue ÷ average net PPE) = 923.62 ÷
  ((146.80+24.17)/2) = 923.62 ÷ 85.49 ≈ **10.8x**.
- Applying that turnover to Rs 417.11 Cr of CWIP implies ≈ Rs 4,505 Cr of
  incremental revenue — roughly **5x current consolidated revenue**. This
  number is not usable and is stated here only to show why it is discarded:
  the Company's own FAQ explains the tiny PPE base is an artefact of 2020
  reverse-merger deemed-cost accounting (assets carried at written-down value
  of the predecessor proprietorship) plus a strategy of buying used machinery
  (Concall PPT May 2026 p.24-25; confirmed independently by the accounting
  policy note, AR2025 p.[[PAGE ~168]], "elected to continue with the carrying
  value... as its deemed cost"). Fixed-asset turnover computed on this base is
  structurally inflated and cannot be used to size incremental revenue from
  new capex. **Method flagged as unusable; substituted below.**

**Substitute (capacity-ratio) method, clearly an approximation, 🔍/📄 mixed**:
using the one figure that is consistent across the FY2025 AR and every deck
(forging capacity 100,000+ → 150,000+ MTPA), the embedded capacity growth is
**+50%**. This assumes utilisation and price/mix per tonne hold roughly
constant, an assumption not tested by any disclosed utilisation figure (2B).
The machining-capacity growth figure is not used as the primary number because
its own base (45,000+ vs 58,000+ MTPA in different decks) is internally
inconsistent, per Stage 5's cross-deck finding.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 50** (forging-capacity-ratio proxy; NOT a
fixed-asset-turnover-derived figure, and not independently verified against a
disclosed utilisation rate).
