# HFCL Q1 FY27 — Plain-language summary (appended to Notion 2026-07-22)

HFCL Limited posted its Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026) results, and on the face of it the quarter was strong. Consolidated revenue rose 119.85% to Rs1,914.98 crore, operating EBITDA margin expanded to 21.63%, and PAT swung to Rs245.64 crore from a loss a year ago, with about 95% of that swing coming from core operations rather than treasury income. Roughly Rs136 crore of the margin gain, though, is deferred cost sitting in unsold inventory, so part of the beat is timing that only counts once the goods ship and the cash comes in. Profit is also migrating into subsidiaries: the gap between consolidated and standalone PAT jumped from 3.9% to 37.1% of standalone, and a chunk of the consolidated numbers was reviewed by only one auditor. The deferred-tax shield is now exhausted, so future EPS faces a full tax rate, and 7.5 crore in-the-money promoter warrants are not yet showing in diluted EPS. Management raised its FY27 growth "aspiration" to 40%, but that number actually sits about 10% below the current run-rate, which points to either sandbagging or a softer second half. The filings stay silent on receivables, on how much of the export ramp is the flagship hyperscaler contract, and on the loss-making BharatNet order book, while roughly 87% of the defence backlog depends on an acquisition that has not closed. No concall transcript was available yet, so the live question-and-answer review is pending. None of the six exit triggers fired, so the stance stays WATCHLIST and do-not-initiate at about Rs197, with the entry zone held at Rs60 to Rs75. The five-agent pipeline cleared its final adversarial audit, and 15 questions are queued for the August call.
