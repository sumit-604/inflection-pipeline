The frozen-mix margin-expansion pillar (40%->50%+) is not visible; the lower-margin distribution/subsidiary layer and under-absorbed Surat capacity offset scale.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY?** Reported yes (+24.0% consolidated, 22.39 vs 18.06), **but this is ENTIRELY a Note-6 one-off.** A Rs 7.29 Cr US-tariff-refund credit was booked to cost of materials consumed (extract L481), so it inflates gross profit, operating EBITDA and core PBT ex-OI alike. **Strip it and the growth REVERSES:** consolidated operating EBITDA ex-credit is 29.65 − 7.29 = 22.36 Cr vs 23.53 Cr prior year — a **~5.0% DECLINE** (the Rs 7.29 Cr credit exceeds the entire +6.12 Cr reported YoY EBITDA increase). The reported +26.0% EBITDA / +13.4% PAT growth is a tariff-credit artefact, NOT genuine operating growth; ex-credit, reported PAT is flat-to-down YoY (a straight post-tax strip of the Rs 7.29 Cr credit, ~Rs 5.4 Cr, puts PAT near Rs 11.9 Cr vs 15.24 Cr prior). 4. **What drove the gap between reported core-PBT growth (+24.0%) and reported-PAT growth (+13.4%)?** First note both figures are computed on credit-inflated reported numbers (diagnostic 3); the mechanics below explain the wedge between the two reported lines only.
