BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE PROCEEDINGS OF THE 8TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
QUALITEK LABS LIMITED (“THE COMPANY”) HELD ON FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026.

The 8th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Members of Qualitek Labs Limited (“the Company”) was
held on Friday, September 25, 2026 at 11:30 A.M. at Avanta Business Centre, 4th Floor, Statesman
House, 402, Barakhamba Road, Connaught Place, New Delhi.

The AGM was chaired and conducted by Mr. Antaryami Nayak, Managing Director & CEO of the
Company.

The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the Meeting to order.

The Chairman welcomed all the Members, Directors, Scrutinizer and other stakeholders present at the
Meeting and thereafter introduced the Directors and other officials present at the AGM.

The Notice convening the 8th AGM, having already been circulated to the Members, was taken as read
with the consent of the Members present.

The Chairman further informed the Members that the Company had provided the facility of remote e-
voting to the eligible Members in respect of the resolutions proposed in the Notice of the AGM. The
remote e-voting commenced on Tuesday, September 22, 2026 at 09:00 A.M. and concluded on
Thursday, September 24, 2026 at 05:00 P.M. The cut-off date for determining the  eligibility of
Members to vote through remote e-voting was Friday, September 18, 2026.

The Chairman informed the Members that CS Ronak Jhuthawat of M/s Ronak Jhuthawat & Co. had
been appointed as the Scrutinizer to scrutinize the remote e-voting process and the voting conducted
at the AGM in a fair and transparent manner.

Thereafter, the following businesses as set out in the Notice of the 8th AGM were taken up for
consideration:

ORDINARY BUSINESS

Item No. 1: Adoption of the audited standalone and consolidated financial statements of the Company
for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, together with the Reports of the Board of Directors and
the Auditors thereon - Passed as Ordinary Resolution.

Item No. 2: Re-appointment of Mr. Antaryami Nayak (DIN: 07232463), Managing Director & CEO,
who retired by rotation and, being eligible, offered himself for re-appointment - Passed as Ordinary
Resolution.

SPECIAL BUSINESS

Item No. 3: Approval of material related party transactions proposed to be entered into by the
Company during the financial year 2026-27 - Passed as Ordinary Resolution.

The Chairman invited the Members to raise their queries and observations in relation to the businesses
set out in the Notice. The queries raised by the Members were suitably addressed by the Chairman.

The Members who were entitled to vote and had not exercised their voting rights through remote e-
voting were provided an opportunity to cast their votes at the AGM through the voting facility made
available for the purpose.

The Chairman informed the Members that the Scrutinizer would submit his consolidated report on the
remote e-voting and voting conducted at the AGM.
[page 3]
 The Chairman further informed the Members that the voting results along with the Scrutinizer’s Report
 would be submitted to BSE Limited and would also be placed on the website of the Company within the
 prescribed period.

 There being no  other  business  to  transact,  the Chairman thanked  all the Members  for  their
  participation and support and declared the Meeting concluded.

 The AGM commenced at 11:30 A.M. and concluded at 01:30 P.M.
