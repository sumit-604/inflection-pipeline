### 2B. Utilisation trajectory

Single facility (Palghar). Company states 85% capacity utilisation, installed
capacity of 33,500 industrial and marine valves/year [D] (AR p.15, p.20).
This claim does **not** reconcile against realised FY26 revenue of Rs 53.23 Cr:
the CMD separately states the facility is "well equipped up to 120 crores" of
revenue [M] (Jun-2026 call, p.5, p.11) — a claimed headroom of ~125% above FY26
actual revenue that an analyst on the same call could not reconcile with an
85% utilisation figure (Nishita Shanklesha exchange, p.11-12). Management's own
explanation is that capacity is priced in valve size/exotic-metal content, not
unit count, so a utilisation percentage and a revenue ceiling are not the same
axis [M]. This gap is carried as an unreconciled management claim, not as
documented capacity headroom (confirms company-memory red flag; also B05
red_flags list, item 5).

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

- Net block, PPE (tangible + intangible), FY26 = Rs 10.28 Cr (Rs 1,021 Lakh +
  Rs 7 Lakh) [D] (Inv. Pres. p.33, Balance Sheet).
- FY26 Revenue from Operations = Rs 53.23 Cr [D] (AR p.20).
- Implied historical fixed-asset turnover (FAT) = 53.23 / 10.28 = **5.18x**.
- Capex under execution, FY27 (documented rupee figure) = Rs 1.25 Cr [M]
  (Jun-2026 call, p.14).
- Implied incremental revenue = 1.25 × 5.18 ≈ **Rs 6.47 Cr**.
- As % of FY26 revenue (Rs 53.23 Cr): 6.47 / 53.23 ≈ **12.2%**.

**This is the capex-mechanical figure, not management's capacity claim.** The
CMD's Rs 120 Cr/85%-utilisation framing implies far more headroom (~125%
above FY26 revenue) with **no incremental capex**, which is internally
inconsistent as shown in 2B and is excluded from the mechanical FAT
calculation on that basis. `capex_embedded_growth_pct` below carries the
12% FAT-derived figure, flagged, not the unreconciled management claim.
