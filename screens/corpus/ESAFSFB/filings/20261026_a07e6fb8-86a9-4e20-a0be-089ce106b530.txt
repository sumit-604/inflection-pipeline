Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of 
India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended from 
time to time, and in continuation of our earlier intimation referred to above, we wish to inform 
you that ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited (“the Bank”), on October 26, 2026, has 
successfully completed the migration from its existing Core Banking System to the new Core 
Banking System, Oracle FLEXCUBE, along with the upgrade and implementation of various 
business applications, Data centre and other allied applications. 
 
The migration marks a significant milestone in the Bank’s ongoing digital transformation 
programme and is aimed at strengthening its technology architecture, enhancing operational 
efficiency, system resilience and scalability, improving service capabilities, and further 
strengthening its risk management and regulatory compliance framework. 
 
Following the successful completion of the migration, the Bank’s banking services have been 
restored and are available to customers. 
 
The Bank continues to closely monitor the performance and stability of the new system with a 
view to ensuring seamless delivery of banking services to its customers.
