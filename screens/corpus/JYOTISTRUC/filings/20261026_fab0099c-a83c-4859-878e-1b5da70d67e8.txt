 JYOTI STRUCTURES LIMITED 
 
 
 
 
 
Jyoti Structures Secures ₹949.35 Crore (excluding GST) Orders from Power Grid Corporation 
of India for 800 kV HVDC Transmission Lines 
 
Mumbai, October 26, 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL), a listed engineering, procurement and 
construction (EPC) company specialising in power transmission infrastructure, has received 
Notifications of Award (NOAs) from Power Grid Corporation of India Limited for two packages 
under the ±800 kV HVDC Bipole Barmer-II and South Kalamb Transmission Lines project in 
Rajasthan. The combined order value is ₹949.35 crore, excluding GST. 
 
The awards cover Lot 1 under package TL01, valued at ₹474.85 crore, and Lot 2 under package 
TL02, valued at ₹474.50 crore. The scope includes the supply, erection, testing and 
commissioning of ±800 kV high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission lines on a total 
turnkey basis. Both packages carry a completion period of 38 months from the date of the 
respective Notifications of Award. 
 
Commenting on the development, Amit Dutta, COO, Jyoti Structures Limited, said, “We are 
grateful for the opportunity to contribute to India’s transmission infrastructure through projects 
of this scale. Our experience in the sector guides our approach to infrastructure project delivery. 
We look forward to working closely with Power Grid Corporation of India Limited to deliver these 
projects as planned.” 
 
The Power Grid awards follow JSL’s recent ₹689.93 crore order from Gujarat Energy Transmission 
Corporation Limited (GETCO) for the 765 kV DC Pachchham–Saykha Transmission Line project in 
Gujarat. JSL is also undertaking a 220 kV double-circuit transmission line project in Sri Lanka in 
partnership with Hayleys Fentons Limited to support the evacuation of wind power to the 
country’s national grid. 
 
High-voltage direct current technology enables the transmission of large volumes of electricity 
over long distances and plays an important role in strengthening power transmission networks. 
Executing such projects requires specialised engineering, detailed planning and coordination 
across multiple stages of delivery.

JYOTI STRUCTURES LIMITED  
 
 
 
 
 
  
With over five decades of experience in transmission EPC, JSL brings integrated capabilities 
across design and engineering, manufacturing, prototype tower testing and project execution. 
The company has constructed more than 37,600 circuit kilometres of transmission lines and 
delivered over 1,800 high-voltage substation bays across India and international markets. 
 
 
About Jyoti Structures Limited 
 
Jyoti Structures Limited (JSL) is a listed engineering, procurement, and construction company 
with over five decades of experience in the power transmission sector. Incorporated in 1974, the 
company provides turnkey solutions across design and engineering, consultancy, prototype 
tower testing, manufacturing, construction, and project management. Its capabilities include 
high-voltage transmission lines, substations, underground cabling and optical ground wire 
(OPGW) projects in India and international markets.
