# Paisalo Q1 FY27 — Model Variance & Scenario Reconciliation

Reconciles the Q1 FY27 print against the Notion five-pillar model and the
pre-committed bull/mixed/bear branches. Produced 5 Aug 2026 as a follow-up to
the A4 merged review. Decision Status UNCHANGED: WATCHLIST, 0% held.

## The pre-committed Q1 expectation was a GATE, not a headline

Bull/mixed/bear for this print were defined on two gate metrics (cleanest:
Cost-to-Income), not on AUM or PAT.

| Outcome | Trigger condition | Pre-committed action |
|---|---|---|
| Bullish | C/I <=35% AND fees >=Rs12 Cr/qtr | Restore entry Rs29-37, consider Tranche 1 (fees >5% of revenue -> destination P/B to 2.80x) |
| Mixed | Exactly one of the two fails | Stay WATCHLIST, compress entry to Rs25-31 |
| Bearish | Both fail, or C/I >35% specifically | DEPRECATE to AVOID (2nd consecutive >35% fires trigger (d)) |

The quarter disclosed NEITHER gate metric (C/I and fee income absent from a
Reg-30 press release). On the axis that defines bull/mixed/bear, this print is
UNSCORABLE. Gate deferred to the 6-Aug-2026 concall.

Model-flagged caveats:
- The Rs244 Cr Base was flagged "possibly too conservative given the Q4
  run-rate of Rs289 Cr annualised." Q1 annualised (Rs245 Cr) landed on the
  conservative Base, NOT the Rs289 Cr Q4-run-rate path, because PAT fell 15%
  QoQ. Relative to the bullish internal reference, a mild disappointment.
- QoQ softness (PAT -15%, NIM flat despite CoB -64bps, disbursement +128% vs
  AUM +28%) is the early signature of the #1 bear scenario, "The Co-Lending
  Trap" (35% prob: SBI co-lending spreads 1-1.5% not 3-4%). Circumstantial,
  unconfirmed, directionally aligned with the Devil's Advocate fear.

## Bottom line

numbers the model actually gates the decision on, a non-answer: the company
withheld exactly the two metrics that separate bull, mixed, and bear, on the
