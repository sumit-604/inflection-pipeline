## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Mukand makes alloy and specialty steel long products. It is a Bajaj group company. It also owns land and has been selling it. FY26 revenue was Rs 4,889.83 crore, flat on Rs 4,889.99 crore (Outcome of Board Meeting FY2027 Q1, doc 7158c4fd, page 8, full page; FY26 results, doc a978dec9, page 20).

**What is changing?** The land is turning into cash, and the cash is carrying the steel business. FY26 PAT was Rs 604.15 crore (doc 7158c4fd, page 8, full page). Inside it sit a surplus on sale of assets of Rs 554.31 crore (FY26 board outcome, cash flow, doc ad15d838, pages 10 to 11, full page) and a deferred tax credit of Rs 92.02 crore (doc 7158c4fd, page 8, full page).

**Why now?** More land is moving. Kalwe, 3.07 acres, completed on 12 May 2026 (doc ad15d838, page 12). A term sheet with AGP DC Infra for 8.78 plus 0.42 acres at Kalwa was signed on 15 July 2026 (Agreements, doc 9d3c00da, pages 1 to 3). A second tranche of Rs 59.29 crore arrived on 19 August 2026 (doc dd4c7c0c, page 1). Q1 FY27 revenue rose 20.7% to Rs 1,362.21 crore (doc 7158c4fd, page 8, full page).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The steel business must earn a profit before other income. Today it does not.

**What breaks it?** The land runs out before the steel business turns. FY26 operating cash flow was minus Rs 482.04 crore. Working capital absorbed Rs 634.19 crore (doc ad15d838, pages 10 to 11, full page). Land sale proceeds of Rs 616.60 crore (same pages) roughly paid for that build.

The specialty steel segment result was Rs 675.39 crore in FY26 against Rs 213.70 crore. Q4 alone was Rs 509.82 crore against Rs 58.20 crore in Q3 (doc a978dec9, page 20). The Q4 jump points to a one time item inside the segment result; its nature is not stated on that page. Finance cost rose to Rs 150.20 crore from Rs 129.63 crore (doc a978dec9, page 20). Discontinued operations lost Rs 7.46 crore against a profit of Rs 34.35 crore in FY25 (doc 21b49ed6, page 13).

**Archetype: commodity converter, plus land monetisation.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds on the steel business. No single year ROCE is used on this card. The reported earnings sit at R0: accounting driven, from land.
