voting process in accordance with the provisions of the Companies act 2013 & rules made there

remote e-voting "in favor" or "against" the resolutions stated in the notice sent to the members 
for the Extraordinary General Meeting;

connection with the proposed pref erential issue but not limited to engage RTA, Depository for

Company;

the Companies Act, 2013;
