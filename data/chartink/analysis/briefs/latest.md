## Where the market stands

A stock above a line is in an uptrend on that clock. On Thursday 699 stocks sat above the long line, about three in ten. 502 sat above the middle line, a little over one in five. 614 sat above the short line, a little over one in four. On Friday only 178 stocks held the short line. So the short count has more than tripled in four sessions. The long count has risen by about 120 in the same four sessions, and by 35 tonight.

The year gives these counts their scale. The spring bottom came on 23 March, when the long count fell to 287. The climb out began on 1 April, when more than 1,100 stocks rose hard in one session. The long count then climbed for four months, to 1,363 on 7 August. Autumn has been a staircase down from that peak. Shocks came in mid September and again in early October. A repair lifted the count to 935 on 16 October. Then last week broke the floor, and the count fell to 578 on Friday. Tonight's 699 is more than double the March low and about half the August peak. It sits close to halfway back to the old autumn floor near 840.

The day itself was the second widest of the four. 144 stocks rose four and a half percent or more. 45 fell that hard. That is about three risers for every faller. ## What would change this read

The read is a four day bounce, wide on Tuesday and Thursday, quiet on Monday and Wednesday, on top of a falling staircase. It turns worse if the selling returns before volume confirms the buying. Another day of 300 or more hard fallers within the next week would repeat the March pattern, when runs of wide days failed. A long count back under about 580, Friday's low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows back above about 300 would say the same. A VIX close back above about 18 would say fear has returned.

The cautious read fails if the buying turns heavy and keeps going. The first test is a wide day on turnover above Friday's 611 crore. Thursday failed it, at about 440. The second test is the long count back above about 840, the old autumn floor. Thursday reached 699, close to halfway. The third test is new highs past about 50 while new lows stay under 100. Thursday passed the low half for a third day, at 66, and reached 35 on the high half. A VIX close under 15 would support the turn. The weekly momentum crosses grew for a third night, to 26, and the heavy weights joined. Those are the fresh signs in the turn's favour. One wide day on heavier money than Friday would turn this from a bounce into a test of the staircase.
