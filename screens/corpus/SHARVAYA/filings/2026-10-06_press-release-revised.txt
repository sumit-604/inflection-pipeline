SHARVAYA METALS LTD. SUCCESSFULLY COMMISSIONS 
10-INCH ALUMINIUM EXTRUSION PRESS, COMPLETING ITS 
ALLOY-TO-COMPONENT VALUE CHAIN 
New facility moves SML up the value curve into Defence, Aerospace, Railways, EV and 
Engineering applications 
Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra | 6 October 2026: Sharvaya Metals Limited (“the Company”) 
(BSE - SME: 544506) is building an integrated aluminium manufacturing platform focused on 
value-added products, advanced alloys and emerging applications across EV, aerospace, 
defence and industrial sectors, The company is pleased to announce the successful 
installation and commissioning of its new state-of-the-art Aluminium Extrusion Facility at its 
manufacturing plant at Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, INDIA. The facility was formally inaugurated 
on 5 October 2026, marking an important milestone in the Company's growth journey and its 
strategy of expanding into high-value, technology-driven aluminium products. 
The newly commissioned extrusion line is equipped with a modern 10-inch billet aluminium 
extrusion press along with an integrated manufacturing setup comprising advanced billet 
heating, extrusion handling, online quenching, puller and stretcher systems, ageing facilities 
and supporting process-control equipment. 
The new facility substantially enhances Sharvaya Metals Limited's manufacturing capabilities 
and enables the Company to cater to increasingly sophisticated requirements across sectors 
including Defence, Aerospace, Railways, Engineering, Construction, Automotive, Electric 
Vehicles and other high-performance industrial applications. A key focus of the new extrusion 
facility will be the development and manufacture of high-strength and precision aluminium 
extrusions of all alloys, subject to customer-specific technical and quality requirements. The 
facility has been designed with emphasis on stringent process control, dimensional 
consistency, metallurgical performance and repeatability. 
BUILT FOR PRECISION 
The State-of-the-art Aluminium extrusion line integrates advanced billet heating, extrusion 
handling, online quenching, puller and stretcher systems, ageing facilities and process-control 
equipment. 
It 
is engineered for stringent process control, dimensional consistency, 
metallurgical performance and repeatability, for manufacturing high-strength and precision 
extrusions. 
The new line complements SML’s existing strengths: advanced CNC/VMC machining centres, 
precision measuring and inspection equipment, an in-house die manufacturing capability and 
an established track record in complex aluminium components. 
MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY 
Commenting on the milestone, Shreyans Katariya, Chairman & Managing Director, 
Sharvaya Metals Limited, said: 
“This is a defining moment for Sharvaya Metals. With extrusion now under our own roof, we 
control every step from the alloy to the finished component, giving us the speed, quality and 
economics to compete for the most demanding programmes in Defence, Aerospace, 
Railways, EVs & Engineering. Our focus now is on a disciplined ramp-up, rapid customer 
qualifications and converting this capability into sustained, profitable growth for our 
shareholders.”

ABOUT SHARVAYA METALS LIMITED 
Established in 2014, Sharvaya Metals Limited, is engaged in manufacturing and supplying 
aluminium products to domestic and international customers. The Company operates a 
strategically located manufacturing unit in Ahmednagar, Maharashtra, equipped with 
advanced infrastructure, quality control, and R&D facilities. 
Its extensive product portfolio includes aluminium alloyed ingots, billets, slabs, sheets, circles, 
and EV battery housings, catering to industries such as engineering, construction, electric 
vehicles, LED, aviation, defence, automotive, cookware and consumer appliances. 
With over a decade of experience and strong OEM and Tier-1 vendor relationships, Sharvaya 
Metals Ltd. is focused on innovation, customer-centric solutions, and expanding its product 
portfolio to meet evolving industry needs.
