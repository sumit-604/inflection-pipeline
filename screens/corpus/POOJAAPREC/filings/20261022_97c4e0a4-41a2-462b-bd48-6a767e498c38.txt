From MD’s Desk – 
Mr. Sanket Anil Kulkarni
5
H1FY27 marks an important milestone in Poojaa Precision Engineering’s journey as 
a publicly listed company. Building on over 34 years of engineering expertise, we 
aim to emerge as a leading global manufacturer of GDC aluminium 
components.
Our growth strategy is centered on expanding manufacturing capacity, advancing 
engineering capabilities and diversifying across industries. Unit III at Khed, Pune, is 
expected to increase our melting capacity to 28,800 MTPA and casting and 
machining capacity to 12,600 MTPA by September 2027.
We aim to translate this capacity expansion into sustainable growth, targeting ~50% 
revenue growth in FY27, and achieving ~Rs 1,000 Cr in annual turnover by FY30. 
In parallel, we are moving up the engineering value chain through advanced 
components such as cylinder heads, motor housings and large precision-
engineered parts that are currently imported. Expansion into Defence, alongside 
opportunities in Power and Energy Distribution, will further diversify our business, 
with a long-term objective of achieving a balanced 50:50 mix between automotive 
and power and engineering.
Automation, renewable energy and technology-led operational improvements 
will remain central to enhancing productivity, optimizing costs and building a 
future-ready manufacturing platform. With expanding capacity, deeper engineering 
capabilities and access to diverse growth markets, we are focused on disciplined 
execution and building a stronger business that delivers sustainable long-term 
shareholder value.

===== PAGE 16 =====
From CFO's Desk – 
Mr. Bhavya Dakshendra Agrawal
6
We are pleased to report a strong operational and financial performance in H1FY27, 
characterized by robust top-line momentum, disciplined working capital 
management, and structural margin expansion. Our Revenue from Operations 
reached Rs 2,101 Mn, representing a YoY growth of 68% over H1FY26. 
Operating profitability recorded significant expansion, with EBITDA standing at Rs. 
396 Mn (19% margin). Operational efficiencies, improved shop-floor capacity and 
an optimized product mix toward complex, value-added precision castings 
contributed to this margin improvement. Profit After Tax (PAT) came in at Rs. 249
