## Findings

**Finding 1 (MINOR) — Newgen "nothing material" quote misattributed to the wrong call in
the Peer Coverage Map.** B06's Part 3 table lists Newgen's Q2 FY26 call (Oct 28, 2025,
filed as "Nov_2025_Transcript") as contributing a "competitive 'nothing material' framing
feeding style-split finding (Claim 3)." I grepped all 12 transcripts for this phrase: "See,
nothing material" appears only in the Q1 FY26 call (Jul 17, 2025), in response to an
analyst's competition question from Grishma Shah. It does not appear anywhere in the Oct 28,
2025 (Q2 FY26) transcript. This is a genuine quarter-misattribution in the coverage-map
table. It does not affect Claim 3's actual analysis in Part 1 — that section correctly cites
a different, verified quote from the Jan 2026 (Q3 FY26) Newgen call ("broadly, the answer
is, at least, no...") — so the error is confined to the descriptive "contribution" label in
the Part 3 summary table and does not change any verdict.
Location: B06 Part 3, Peer Coverage Map, row "NEWGEN | Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025 call)."

**Finding 2 (MAJOR) — Claim 5's RateGain peer evidence overstates RateGain's role as the
proactive discloser of two quantified figures that were actually first stated by analysts.**
B06 writes that RateGain "discloses a comparable-in-kind event with more upfront
quantification: a lost MarTech client 'which nearly contributed 4% of MarTech's revenue' is
disclosed with the percentage attached in the same breath" and that RateGain "proactively
quantifies a related metric shift — NRR declining from '110 to 100' — and attributes it
candidly to mix effects." In the actual Nov 11, 2025 transcript, both figures are stated
first by analysts in their questions — the "4%" figure by Shubham ("we had lost a key client
for MarTech earlier which nearly contributed 4% of MarTech's revenue rate"), and the "110 to
100" figure by Naeem Patel ("I see our numbers over the years our net revenue retention has
been coming from 110 to 100"). Bhanu Chopra's responses address the underlying causes (a
prior-disclosed client loss; MarTech mix effects) but do not independently volunteer either
number in the transcript excerpts available. This does not overturn the substance of Claim
5's comparison — RateGain plainly does discuss a comparable customer-loss event more openly
than AMAGI's "purely strategic" framing eventually walked back — but the characterization of
RateGain as the party that "proactively" and "contemporaneously" quantifies these figures
"in the same breath" is not supported by the transcript as written; the figures instead
appear to originate from information analysts already had (likely from RateGain's own past
quarterly disclosures, which is plausible but not evidenced in the transcript excerpt
itself) and are being read back to management. This matters because Claim 5's PARTIALLY
VERIFIED verdict — and the standalone flag ("RateGain's contemporaneous, quantified
disclosure...contrasts with AMAGI's initial understatement") — rests specifically on
RateGain's disclosure *behavior* as the point of comparison, and the evidence as cited
overstates that behavior's proactiveness. This same overstatement is repeated verbatim in
the top-level `flags:` block of the YAML, doubling its potential downstream influence in
synthesis if not corrected.
Location: B06 Part 1 Claim 5 peer evidence row; B06 top-level `flags:` list (RateGain
disclosure item).
