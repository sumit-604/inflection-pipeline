## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Gaudium is a genuinely premium fertility brand sitting inside a large, fast-growing, under-penetrated market, and its operating economics — a ~37% ROCE once the idle IPO cash is stripped out and a ~51% standalone core margin — are the kind of numbers that justify a real re-rating IF the cash actually flows and the promoter file stays clean. On one-year-forward multiples the four-pillar destination is 22x, giving a Rs 181 base target; at CMP Rs 137 that is only ~10% CAGR, the Hurdle is STOP, and a 25% return is not available even on bull earnings. The business is real; the price is the problem. The right action is to wait for Rs 90-93 and buy Small, only if receivables have turned and the hubs are opening — not before.
