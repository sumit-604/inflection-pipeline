### 2A Accounting policy aggressiveness — extension
- **Revenue recognition:** point-in-time / bill-and-hold per Ind AS 115 Appendix B (Note 1),

  assessment has not yet been pulled. 🟡

  states unambiguously: "the Balance Sheet of Wholly Owned Subsidiary has been consolidated

- **ECL / provisioning:** zero ECL provision two years running against a clean receivables book

### The Risk Management Committee contradiction (headline finding of this phase)
**MD&A, Risk section (p.69, line 8506-8508): "The Company has established a robust Risk
Management Committee that systematically identifies, assesses, monitors and mitigates key
enterprise risks across its operations."**
**Corporate Governance Report, Section 7 (p.91, line 12481-12484): "Risk Management Committee is
not applicable on the company pursuant to SEBI(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015."**
These two statements, ~2,000 lines apart in the same Annual Report, are **directly and
unambiguously contradictory**. The Corporate Governance Report's statement is the statutorily
governed, precise one (Risk Management Committees are mandatory only for the top 1,000 listed
companies by market cap under Reg 21 of SEBI LODR — a company of Venus's size is correctly exempt,
and it is entirely normal and defensible for the company to not have one). **What is not
defensible is the MD&A's narrative claim of an actively "established... robust" committee that,
per the company's own governance disclosure four pages later, does not exist.** This is either a
templated MD&A risk section carried over without adaptation to the company's actual governance
structure, or a genuine overstatement — either way it is a material, quotable, same-filing
self-contradiction on a governance-substance point. 🔴 Red Flag.

**PHASE 4 SUMMARY / contradictions vs Phases 1-3:** the Risk Management Committee finding is the
standout new item of this phase. Combined with the missing-risks list (4B) — every omission maps
to a Phase 1-3 finding the MD&A had the material to name and did not — the picture is of a risk
section that is specific and well-written where management chose to be specific, and silent
exactly where the filing's own audited numbers would have required naming an uncomfortable risk.
**Verdict: 🔴 Red Flag.** **Informational kill switch:** a human reviewer would have reason to
stop and ask management directly to reconcile the Risk Management Committee statements before
relying on any other risk-section claim at face value; the pipeline does not stop. Continuing to

  **implicit retraction**: the Chairman's letter frames FY26 as the year Venus became able to
  operate with mature, institutional discipline "rather than on individuals" (p.41, discussing
  the new Board of Management), while the audited governance structure shows the same individual
  holding CMD, CFO, and Audit Committee seats simultaneously — a structure that is, by
  definition, still built around one individual at its financial-oversight core. **Materiality:
  this changes the thesis's governance-quality read, not the operating-financial read** — it
  bears directly on how much independent verification the RPT and exceptional-item questions
  received, which are themselves financially material lines.

**Conclusion:** one genuine, moderate-materiality quiet-abandonment-adjacent finding (the
institutional-maturity framing vs the CMD/CFO/Audit-Committee concentration), captured here as an
implicit retraction; the clearer, higher-severity Risk Management Committee self-contradiction is
deliberately kept in Phase 4 to avoid duplicate flagging of the identical underlying fact.

**PHASE 6 SUMMARY:** the Chairman's letter is unusually candid by the standards of this AR
genre — it names past losses, past debt levels, a 15-year-delayed headquarters, and partial
mission delivery without prompting. That candour is a genuine positive that should not be lost
in the flags above. The gap between the front-matter narrative and the governance reality
(Phase 5) is real but narrow and specific, not a pervasive pattern across the whole front matter.
**Verdict: 🟡 Watch.**
