Fast CNC grower, unproven cash, overpriced

Macpower CNC Machines builds computer controlled metal cutting machines, the VMC, HMC, turning, turn-mill and VTL families, from Metoda in Gujarat, and is pushing a premium NEXA range plus defence and aerospace qualification. FY26 revenue was Rs 333 Cr, EBITDA Rs 54 Cr at a 16.2% margin, and PAT Rs 34 Cr. The shares trade at Rs 1,481, which is 38 times trailing profit and 34 times forward. The balance sheet is effectively debt free at net cash, the Mehta family holds 73.2% with zero pledge across twelve straight quarters, and the audit opinion is clean with no going concern doubt.

Four transitions were tested and only one is firing. Revenue is the live leg: the unexecuted order book stands at Rs 456 Cr, about 1.37 times FY26 sales, and Q1 FY27 revenue rose 56% year on year. Margin is flat, not rising, held at 16.2% in both FY26 and Q1 FY27. Cash conversion is weak and unproven. Return on capital is holding near 24%, not improving. The composite transition score is plus 2 out of 8, the incomplete transition case, only revenue firing forward.

Gate 0 scored 81 out of 160, an AVOID, and cash is the reason. Operating cash to profit ran 0.70 times in FY24, then 0.27, then 0.41 in FY26, a cumulative 0.45 against a 0.50 pass line, and the three year record triggered a one tier downgrade. Trade receivables grew 57.5% while revenue grew 8.6%, raw material inventory rose 50.9%, and the over six month ageing bucket worsened to 15.4% of gross from 12.2%. The determination is INDETERMINATE, not resolved either way, because no credit rating rationale was in the inputs and management said on the Q4 call that conversion will not improve rapidly. That caps the disposition at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS.

Management delivers on the near numbers and slips on the far ones. Credibility grades B: FY26 revenue, EBITDA of Rs 54 Cr against a Rs 50 Cr guide, and an order book of Rs 406 Cr against a Rs 300 to 330 Cr guide were all met or beaten, and Q1 FY27 ran ahead of guidance. Against that, the 60-acre government land has slipped three times and more since March 2024, the JV that depends on it has been frozen three quarters, the 25% margin goalpost was narrowed to that undated land only under analyst pressure, machine unit volumes were withheld every quarter, and the stated market share jumped between 4.5% and 1 to 2% without reconciliation.

Peers confirm the demand and contradict the defence story. JYOTICNC and KLBRENG both show capacity constrained demand above 20% CAGR, so the industry tailwind is real. But JYOTICNC executed more than Rs 800 Cr of aerospace and defence in FY26, including Rs 180 Cr of ordnance orders in a single quarter, while Macpower sits in single digits of revenue at roughly 10 to 13% bid conversion. None of the three peers shows genuine pricing power. The controller, sourced 99% from FANUC, Siemens and Mitsubishi, still caps the backward integration margin story.

The market runway is large but the company oversells it. Serviceable market is about Rs 14,900 Cr, Macpower holds roughly 2.2%, and the revenue headroom is 44.7 times on a MASSIVE runway class. Management's own market size claim of Rs 35,000 Cr is about 2.13 times the most credible independent estimate, read as inflated, and the sizing this run is partial.

Valuation settles the call. Forward FY27 EPS of about Rs 43 earns a destination multiple that is cap bound at 25 times on the additive track, which the operator approved and which governs, or 16.5 times on the more conservative return required track that only deepens the AVOID. That gives a base fair value of Rs 1,075 on the additive track and Rs 710 on the return required track, both below the Rs 1,481 price. The 25% hurdle STOPs on the base case at 1.19 and on the bull case at 1.38, and no earnings beat rescues a cap bound multiple. Probability weighted three year CAGR is minus 10.4%. The decision is AVOID on valuation, with an entry zone of Rs 440 to Rs 550 and a margin of safety price of Rs 440, position size Small if it ever gets there.

The adversarial review survives and the AVOID is over determined. The sharpest counter is that the cash drag may be structural rather than growth induced, tied to PSU and subsidy financed customer payment lags. At the framework's 0.65 times cash band, honest entry drops to roughly Rs 320 to Rs 400 on the additive track and about Rs 236 on the return required track, which would make even the Rs 440 to Rs 550 zone too generous. The single print that would confirm the structural read is FY27 operating cash to profit below 0.5 times with receivable and inventory days still rising.
