September 2026 on 05.00 P.M. (IST). The facility for voting through the e-voting system was also 
made available during the meeting for members who had not cast their vote prior to the meeting.  
 
Accordingly, the Consolidated Report (Remote e-Voting and e-Voting at the AGM) pursuant to

along with the Consolidated Scrutinizer Report submitted by Mr. Kashyap Shah & Co., Practising

out in the Notice convening the AGM have been duly approved by the shareholders with the 
requisite majority.
