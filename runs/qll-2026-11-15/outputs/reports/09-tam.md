# Stage 9 TAM / SAM / SOM: Qualitek Labs Ltd (QLL), run 2026-11-15

Units: Rs Cr unless stated. USD converted at USD 1 = Rs 95 (RBI reference rate, MSEI archive, 2-6 Nov 2026 range 94.40 to 95.25; one archive row looks copied, so rate is approximate). Market reports are USD; every Rs Cr figure below is USD bn x 95 x 100, or USD m x 95 / 10.
QLL base: FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 124.52 Cr (12,451.81 Lakh, AR26 P&L p.103 via B03); H1 FY27 Rs 72.27 Cr (7,226.65 Lakh).

## Section 1: Market definition
1A. Product: outsourced, accredited laboratory testing in QLL's three core verticals (transport/defence/mechanical 40-45%, food and feed 25-30%, pharma/cosmetics/life sciences 15-20% of revenue; management ranges, Deck May 2026 p.23 via B04). Geography: India (overseas is 2.7% of FY26 revenue, B03). Customers: manufacturers, exporters, government. Channel: direct per-test and project work, plus PPP operating contracts. Included: testing. Excluded from TAM: minerals, metals, E&E, IAC, LIMS (no sourced size), in-house labs, and all overseas demand. Revenue outside the three verticals (5 to 20%) is in the numerator of share but not in the TAM, so share is slightly overstated.
1B. Management TAM claim: NONE found. AR26 MD&A (pp.70-73) says the sector is "positive" and gives no market size. The Jan 2024 prospectus (pp.135-136) quotes Straits Research: "USD 26 million in 2021 to USD 47 million by 2030, CAGR 6.9%". The unit is wrong (a USD 26 million India TIC market cannot hold a Rs 125 Cr company); it is also 2021 base, so STALE beyond 4 years, direction only. Management does claim a revenue ambition: "Rs 500+ Cr consolidated revenue" (AR26 p.24 and filing p.3, via B03), about 32% CAGR from Rs 124.52 Cr. Credibility of TAM talk: broad and unquantified.

## Section 3: SAM and SOM
3A. Five filters on conservative TAM Rs 24,354 Cr:
- Product fit: already limited to three verticals (done in TAM).
- Channel: outsourced share 65.21% (Mordor 2025). Rs 24,354 x 0.6521 = Rs 15,882 Cr. Assumes the vertical reports include in-house; if they are already outsourced-only, SAM equals TAM and this filter overstates the cut.
- Geography: labs at Pune, Noida, Bhubaneswar, Paradip, Panchkula, Chennai, Baddi, Bengaluru, Mumbai (AR26 p.73) span four regions; regional split NOT FOUND, not applied.
- Customer: NOT FOUND (top-10 share 32.34% at H1 FY24 only, B04).
- Capability (NABL scope): NOT FOUND, not applied.
SAM = Rs 15,882 Cr = 65.2% of TAM. It is an upper bound; two filters are unapplied.
3B. SOM. Current share: 124.52 / 15,882 = 0.78%.
SAM growth 8.9%: vertical-weighted CAGR = (13,288 x 8.3 + 8,605 x 8.22 + 2,461 x 14.3) / 24,354 = 216,215 / 24,354 = 8.88%. (Mordor top-down says 6.51%; both shown, 8.9% carried because the bottom-up matches QLL's verticals. Real-world check: Lal +19%, Vimta +9.9%, B06.)
SAM yr3 (FY29) = 15,882 x 1.089^3 = 15,882 x 1.2915 = Rs 20,512 Cr. SAM yr5 (FY31) = 15,882 x 1.089^5 = 15,882 x 1.5316 = Rs 24,326 Cr.
Share path: the 1-2pp-in-3-years rule suits mid-share players. At 0.78%, +1pp means share 2.28x. Two readings:
- Rule-normal: +1pp by yr3 = 1.78% x 20,512 = Rs 365 Cr, CAGR (365/124.52)^(1/3) = 43%.
- Evidence-capped (carried): +0.5pp by yr3 (half the rule floor), then the same pace. Evidence: capex completion 3 of 9 delivered (B07), H1 FY27 EBITDA 21.6% vs 25-27% guide, H1 FY27 CFO/PBT 42% (B04). SOM yr3 = 1.28% x 20,512 = Rs 263 Cr; CAGR = (263/124.52)^(1/3) = 2.112^(0.333) = 28.3%. SOM yr5 = (0.78 + 5 x 0.1667 = 1.61%) x 24,326 = Rs 392 Cr; CAGR = (392/124.52)^(1/5) = 3.148^(0.2) = 25.8%.
Separating observation: H2 FY27 revenue of at least Rs 95.83 Cr (9,583 Lakh, B04) from existing labs. It would favour the rule-normal path.
Management Rs 500 Cr by FY31 needs 500 / 24,326 = 2.06% share (+1.28pp), inside the 1-2pp normal range for 5 years in share terms, but it exceeds SOM yr5 by Rs 108 Cr (28%) and rests on acquisitions (B03).
3C. Capacity check. B07: capex-embedded growth 72% (range 49-98%). Committed capacity revenue = 124.52 x 1.72 = Rs 214 Cr (range 185 to 247). SOM yr3 Rs 263 Cr: gap Rs 49 Cr. SOM yr5 Rs 392 Cr: gap Rs 178 Cr. Mgmt Rs 500 Cr: gap Rs 286 Cr. The figure rests on a March 2026 land split and FY26 asset turnover 1.743x (B07). Which side is optimistic: at the 98% top of range capacity is Rs 247 Cr, still short of yr3 SOM. SOM is the optimistic side until further capex or acquisitions are funded; the capex plan is not the optimistic side, because CWIP is already debt-funded (B07 FLAG-FUNDING) and borrowings rose 9,297.55 to 11,466.88 Lakh in six months.

## Section 4: Drivers, risks, structure
4A Drivers (evidence tier in brackets):
- Regulation and formalisation: BIS, FSSAI, BEE mandates raise outsourced demand (prospectus pp.135-136, industry report; Mordor: FSSAI NABL mandate pushed about 200 unaccredited food labs out, single-source). Impact: high. [Tier: third-party report]
- Outsourcing: outsourced share 65.21%, growing 7.11% vs market 6.51% (Mordor). Impact: medium.
- EV, defence indigenisation, pharma and life sciences: named by management (AR26 p.70-71) [CLAIM]; life sciences is the fastest-growing vertical at 6.89% (Mordor); healthcare analytical 14.3% (MnM).
- Exports, new applications, technology enablement: stated by management; not quantified.
- Per-capita, premiumisation, demographics: no sourced evidence; not credited.
4B Risks and signals: pharma R&D lull (Vimta Q2 FY27 growth 9.9%, B06) signal: pharma testing demand; price competition from global majors; accreditation lapse (NABL scope notices); government payer delay (Krsnaa DSO 139-155 days, B06) signal: QLL receivables over 6 months share 22.4%; skilled-staff attrition; food export-import disruption.
4C Structure: Mordor lists SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek, TUV SUD, TUV Rheinland, UL, DNV, DEKRA with about 40-45% combined share, concentration "Medium". Over 1,200 labs in India, about one-third NABL accredited (Mordor, single source). Unorganised share NOT FOUND. About two-thirds of labs lack NABL, so formalisation is a driver, but the unaccredited labs may not be QLL's addressable customers. Fragmenting at the mid-tier, with global majors at the top. Price vs differentiation: accredited scope is the differentiator; price power moderate (B04).

## Section 5: Summary and runway
5A Funnel (Rs Cr): TIC all India 2025: 121,600 (Mordor) -> outsourced testing 45,442 (x0.3737) -> QLL three verticals TAM 24,354 -> SAM 15,882 -> SOM yr3 263 -> SOM yr5 392 -> FY26 revenue 124.52. (Mordor 2025: 121,600 x 0.3737 = 45,442.)
5B Headroom: 15,882 / 124.52 = 127.6x. TAM growth 8.9%. QLL FY26 growth 77% (47% standalone organic) vs market 8.9%: gaining share, not riding the market, but about half of FY26 growth was acquired (B04). Years to saturate SAM at 28%: not meaningful; share would be 100% only after 20+ years.
5C Class: STRONG. On headroom arithmetic alone the class would read MASSIVE; it is cut one step because SAM is an upper bound, the market is contestable by global majors, and the binding limit is capacity and funding, not market size.
5D Levers: overseas (30% target by FY31 withdrawn in AR26, B03); EMI/EMC labs (B07 optionality); IAC services; more PPP labs; acquisitions in pharma, food, electricals. Potential addition NOT FOUND (no sizes). Headroom unchanged at 127.6x.
5E Output card: market definition as in YAML; TAM Rs 24,354 to 27,871 Cr; SAM Rs 15,882 Cr (65.2%); SOM yr3 Rs 263 Cr, yr5 Rs 392 Cr; capacity gap Rs 49 Cr and Rs 178 Cr.
Valuation line: At 25.8% revenue CAGR implied by SOM, with PAT margin held at the FY26 level of 11.7% (14.60 / 124.52), the earnings growth embedded here is 25.8% CAGR. At the guided 14.5% PAT margin it is (392 x 0.145 = 56.8; 56.8 / 14.60 = 3.89; ^0.2) 31.2%; at the H1 FY27 PAT margin of 9.1% it is (35.7 / 14.60 = 2.445; ^0.2) 19.6%. Current P/E is 643.0 / 14.60 = 44.0x on FY26 consolidated PAT (market cap Rs 643 Cr, CMP Rs 552, B00; consolidated PAT includes minority share, owners' PAT is lower, so true P/E is higher). At 44x this does not support the current valuation on SOM growth alone: PEG = 44.0 / 25.8 = 1.7. It supports it only if margin reaches guide (PEG 1.4), which H1 FY27 does not evidence. Stage 11 owns the final multiple.
