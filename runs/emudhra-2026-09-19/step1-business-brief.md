THE ENGINE: a mix shift from Indian certificate issuance toward owned
enterprise security software sold abroad (US, Europe, Middle East), where
operating leverage should lift margin. Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin 26.2% vs 23.2%
for FY26 is the first print of that lift.

## Sector cap row (step D)

Platform / SaaS / IT services, 45x. eMudhra owns its software IP. The
"Cybersecurity / VAD" row (25x) is by Amendment 27.4 definition distribution
with vendor certifications, which eMudhra is not; the 21% pure services line
is the part that argues the other way. Flag for phase-3 confirmation.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)

1. ORGANIC GROWTH vs GUIDANCE: FY26 organic about 19% and Q1 FY27 organic about 15% (28% less about 13 points Cryptas) against the 15-18% FY27 organic guide. Verify the Cryptas revenue and consolidation date in the FY26 AR and Q1 FY27 results, and split organic from acquired growth.
2. CASH CONVERSION: FY26 FCF Rs -52 Cr with screener days payable at 420 and other liabilities up Rs 151 Cr. Verify what sits in payables and other liabilities (pass-through vendor payables, deferred revenue, deferred acquisition consideration) and the receivables ageing from the AR notes.
3. 3i INFOTECH CLAIM: the Rs 128 Cr+ claim over the 2008 disinvestment and preference share redemption. Verify the AR contingent-liability note and every Reg 30 disclosure; confirm whether a civil suit or SEBI complaint exists.
4. ASSET BUILD: fixed assets Rs 419 Cr to Rs 727 Cr in FY26. Verify the split between acquisition goodwill and intangibles, capitalised product development, and data-centre or trust-infrastructure capex, and the amortisation policy on capitalised R&D.
