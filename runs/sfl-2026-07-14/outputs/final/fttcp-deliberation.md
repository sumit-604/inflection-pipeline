## Operator overrides

**Override 1 — Margin forward verdict.**

- **Draft determination:** Margin forward STAGNANT (0). The draft measured
  expansion against the restored normal (about 10 to 11 percent) and judged the
  near-term TDI and polyol spike of 25 to 35 percent enough to hold the
  forward-expansion probability below 40 percent. Composite +4, DEEP WATCH
  leaning BUY-ON-DIPS.
- **Operator ruling:** Margin forward STARTING (+1). The transition is the
  recovery off the multi-year trough, and it is already partly printed (core
  EBITDA 7.3 percent FY25 to 10.8 percent FY26, plus 300 bps) with documented
  drivers.
- **Operator reasoning, in the operator's words:** "if margins recover to
  normal, it will be margins firing. The reason is that the price has been under
  consolidation for multiple years, and it has discounted the weakness in the
  business. I am looking at the idea that the business might have bottomed out
  and things might be expanding from here. The expansion is in reference to the
  bottommost point."
- **Resolution:** Margin recorded at STARTING (+1). Composite moves to +5,
  position band moves to BUY-candidate. FIRING (+2) was NOT forced by the
  operator; the analyst read is +1 today and +2 once the recovery prints above
  the raw material noise. It upgrades to FIRING on monitoring trigger #1 (core
  EBITDA above 12 percent in both Q1 and Q2 FY27). Note: at +2 the composite
  would be +6, still inside the same BUY-candidate band (5 to 6), so the band
  decision is settled either way and the print only refines the score inside it.

## Cross-cutting note carried to phase 3

The operator's price argument (multi-year consolidation has already discounted
the business weakness; the business may have bottomed and be expanding from the
trough) is a valuation input, not an FTTCP transition input, and is routed to
phase 3. Stage 11 must not price the FY25 trough as permanent (the Amendment
4.5 normalized-anchor discipline, the TATVA lesson): Pillar 1 anchors on the
recovering normalized ROCE, not the 6.7 percent reported trough, and the Hurdle
Ratio and entry zone decide whether the market has already paid for the
recovery or offers it with margin of safety. Reported ROCE is Kurlon-goodwill
depressed (B04 puts Cash ROCE at roughly twice reported); stage 11 decides
reported versus operating or tangible-capital base, and must not anchor on the
pre-acquisition 17 percent, which sat on a smaller goodwill-free base that no
longer exists.
