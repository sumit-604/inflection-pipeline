### 2C Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic shown)
Historical fixed asset turnover (consolidated revenue over average tangible net block, Inv. Pres. slides 14-15, Rs Cr):
- FY24: 3,705 / ((1,748 + 2,334)/2 = 2,041) = 1.82x
- FY25: 4,034 / ((2,334 + 3,157)/2 = 2,745.5) = 1.47x
- FY26: 4,238 / ((3,157 + 3,776)/2 = 3,466.5) = 1.22x (latest, used)
Capex under execution: FY27 plan Rs 550 Cr (Oct call, NJ p.9).
- Implied incremental revenue = 550 x 1.22 = Rs 672 Cr = 15.9% above FY26 revenue of Rs 4,238 Cr (550/3,466.5, computed). At the FY24 turnover of 1.82x it would be Rs 998 Cr, 23.6%. The block carries 15.9% (latest evidenced turnover).
- Remaining H2 FY27 spend only (Rs 160 Cr): 160 x 1.22 = Rs 196 Cr = 4.6%.
Capacity already built but not yet earning (🔍 cross-check on reported numbers, constant Q2 FY27 realisation and mix, which is generous because casting realises less per tonne):
- Q2 FY27 revenue annualised: 1,405 x 4 = Rs 5,620 Cr = +32.6% over FY26 (Inv. Pres. slide 14; Oct call, LK p.3). Utilisation 72%.
- Same run-rate at 80% utilisation: 5,620 x 80/71.7 = Rs 6,271 Cr = +48.0%.
- Same run-rate at 100%: Rs 7,838 Cr = +85.0%.
- Management claim: Rs 9,000 Cr at 100% utilisation on net block about Rs 3,700 Cr (asset turn 2.5x) = +112% (Jul call, LK/NJ p.19). Actual turnover is 1.22x, half the claim.
Reading: the growth embedded in new FY27 capex is modest (15.9%). The larger lever is filling the FY26 capacity (+29.3% tonnage). Two readings: (a) 80% utilisation by FY27 end delivers +48% on Q2 run-rate; (b) mix drift to lower-realisation casting and idle press (61%) keeps revenue per tonne below the Q2 level. The observation that separates them: Q3 and Q4 FY27 revenue above Rs 1,405 Cr with press utilisation above 61%.
