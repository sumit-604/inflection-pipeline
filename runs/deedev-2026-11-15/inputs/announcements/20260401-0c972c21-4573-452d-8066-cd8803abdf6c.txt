Sub: Intimation of Final Order by Punjab State Electricity Regulatory Commission (PSERC) for Tariff 
Determination of 6 MW Biomass Power Project of M/s Malwa Power Pvt. Ltd. (Wholly Owned Subsidiary) 
 
Dear Sir/ Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations, we would like to inform that the Punjab State Electricity 
Regulatory Commission (PSERC), vide its Order in Petition No. 59 of 2024, has determined the tariff for the 6 
MW biomass -based power plant of t he Company’s wholly owned subsidiary, M/s Malwa Power Pvt. Ltd. 
(MPPL), located at Village Gulabewala, Tehsil Mukatsar, District Mukatsar, Punjab.  
The petition was filed for determination of tariff for the extended period of 10 years upon expiry of the initial 
useful life period of 20 years under the Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) dated February 19, 2004 (amended 
vide Amendment II dated August 26, 20 13), executed with Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL). 
The plant was commissioned on April 27, 2005, and the initial PPA term expired on April 26, 2025. The 
Commission has now determined the tariff for the extended term of the project.

The Commission has determined the tariff at ₹5.224 per kWh for FY 
2025-26 (comprising Fixed Tariff of ₹0.97/kWh and Variable Tariff of 
₹4.254/kWh with 5% annual escalation). 
The plant was receiving an interim tariff of ₹3.50 per kWh as directed by 
Hon’ble APTEL. The revised tariff represents an enhancement of 
approximately ₹1.724 per kWh over the interim rate. 
Based on the revised tariff, the Company estimates an additional revenue 
recovery of approximately ₹5. 80 Crores till date on account of tariff 
differential.  
Whether the order is appealable 
or proposed action 
The above Order has been passed by PSERC pursuant to the directions of 
the Hon’ble Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL) in Appeal No. 
336 of 2025 filed by PSPCL, wherein the Hon’ble APTEL confirmed that 
PSERC has the jurisdiction to determine the tar iff and directed both 
parties to approach PSERC afresh for tariff determination. 
Accordingly, this Tariff Order is subject to acceptance by both parties 
before the Hon’ble APTEL. The Company is evaluating the Order and
