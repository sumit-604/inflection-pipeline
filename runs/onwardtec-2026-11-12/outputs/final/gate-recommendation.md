## 6. Monitorables

1. **Margin without the credit.** Read the Q3 FY27 EBITDA margin net of any R&D credit, with the offshore share, in the results note and deck slide 11. 14% or more with offshore at 72% or more supports the new base reading; near 13% with offshore under 72% supports the peak reading. This tests whether the turnaround is a base or a peak.
2. **December quarter revenue.** Compare Q3 FY27 revenue from operations with Rs 170.2 Cr in the results filing. Rs 170 Cr or more says the 15 to 18% guide is prudent; under Rs 160 Cr says the second half slows. This tests the growth step.
3. **Cash and collections.** Read DSO on deck slide 9 and, at year end, CFO in the cash flow statement. DSO under 75 days by Q3 FY27 and CFO/EBITDA above 70% clear the cash flag; DSO above 80 days for two quarters, or unbilled growing faster than revenue, point to a structural problem. This tests FLAG-CASH.
4. **Guidance basis.** On the Q3 FY27 call, get answers to two questions: does the 13.8 to 14.3% margin guide count the credit, and is the revenue guide in constant currency? A reported H2 margin under 13.5% fails the guide on either basis. This tests management credibility and the size of the currency tailwind.
5. **Customer concentration.** Read the top client share in AR27 Note 34 and the top 5 and USD 1 mn client counts on the deck client slide. Top client above 20% or top 5 above 50% is red; USD 1 mn clients at 19 to 20 confirm account mining, under 16 is red. This tests the binding limit on the runway.
6. **Billing system controls.** Read the FY27 auditor's report. A third year of the audit trail paragraph on the general ledger and revenue billing system is a red flag. This tests whether reported revenue and unbilled can be relied on.
7. **Promoter holding and pledge.** Read each quarterly shareholding pattern. Holding under 39.5% or any pledge is a red flag. This tests insider alignment while the 2023 to 2024 sale stays unexplained.
8. **Cost mix and pricing.** Read sub-contracting as a share of revenue in the other expenses note and fixed price share on deck slide 11. Sub-contracting above 7.0% (FY26 6.27%) or fixed price above 15% says margin is shifting to riskier delivery. This tests the quality of the margin step against peers moving to outcome pricing.
