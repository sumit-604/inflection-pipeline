## PASS 3: PATTERN PASS

Method. I tested the pattern list (contradictions, notes versus statements, thin disclosure, restatements, post balance sheet events, going concern). I skimmed Consolidated Notes 40 to 43 (PDF pp.330-336, text lines 21006-21330), which Pass 2 did not re-read, and compared them with the standalone versions. I read the standalone auditor's CARO annexure (PDF pp.204-206) and tied it to the notes. I re-checked the ageing tables (Note 44, standalone, text lines 17184-17229) and key ratios.

Result: PASS 3 FOUND SIX NEW ITEMS. They sharpen earlier findings. They do not overturn any.

### P3-1. Consolidated undrawn-facility table corroborates the ADT lender limit of 1,500 (YELLOW)
- Cons Note 40 financing arrangements (p.332): undrawn facilities expiring within one year 58,014.50 (FY25 6,081.40). Standalone Note 39 (p.257): 56,514.50 (FY25 5,075.17).
- Difference FY26 = 1,500.00 (calc). FY25 = 1,006.23 (calc) = 1,500.00 less ADT cash credit drawn 493.77 (Cons Note 15, p.311). Both differences tie exactly.
- So the subsidiary ADT has one lender limit of 1,500, undrawn in full at 31 March 2026. AMPL's guarantee for ADT is 3,500.00 (Note 31(ii), p.246) or 4,300.00 (Note 34, p.251). That is 2.3x to 2.9x of the only ADT facility visible in the report (calc). ADT term loans: none shown in Cons Note 15.
- INFERENCE: either the guarantee covers non-fund facilities (bank guarantees, letters of credit) that the notes do not list, or the guarantee amount is stale. Not stated (NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT). This supports Pass 2 P2-9 and RPT-3.

### P3-2. The 16,000.00 year-end deposit: net debt on two readings (YELLOW)
- Cash and cash equivalents 16,456.80 include a term deposit of 16,000.00 "original maturity less than three months", nil a year earlier (Note 10(a), p.233). The same day, working capital demand loan 15,109.45 and cash credit 4,883.82 (new) were drawn at floating 7.15% to 9.80% (Note 14, p.236). Cash is also listed as pledged (Note 36, p.252).
- Company net debt 12,309.93 = 28,705.35 + lease 61.38 - 16,456.80 (calc, ties to Note 40, p.259). Net debt to equity 10%.
- Net debt without the deposit: 12,309.93 + 16,000.00 = 28,309.93 = 21.9% of standalone equity 129,080.69 (calc).
- Two readings. (a) The deposit is genuine surplus liquidity from the 37,595.92 CFO and the 4,349.99 warrant money, so gearing is 10%. (b) The deposit is a year-end holding funded by short-term bank lines, so gearing is closer to 22%. The observation that separates them: the deposit balance and the working capital lines at 30 June 2026 and 30 September 2026. The notes cannot separate them. This is not a finding of window dressing; it is a test to run on the next filing.

### P3-3. ECL cover on the old bucket fell as the bucket grew (RED, extends Pass 2 P2-5)
- More than 2 years: gross 4,591.91 (FY25) to 8,158.33 (FY26), +77.7% (calc). ECL on the bucket 900.84 to 1,219.10 (Note 39, pp.255-256).
- Cover 19.6% to 14.9% (calc: 900.84 / 4,591.91; 1,219.10 / 8,158.33). The consolidated table shows the same: cover 19.6% (900.84 / 4,596.41) to 15.0% (1,225.22 / 8,164.46) (Cons Note 40, p.330).
- The company's own matrix says 100% above 2 years for non-government customers. Cover fell while the exposure rose. The government presumption (nil or negligible risk, case by case; Note 39 p.255; Cons Note 40 p.330) carries the gap. Cons Note 40 p.327 also states that collection "is generally skewed" (company's word, Cons Note 40 credit risk, text line 21023).
- Effect of the FY26 matrix rate cuts (6m-1y 3% to 2%; 1-2y 11% to 10%): on the implied non-government base from Pass 2 (3,338.50 and 403.30) the saving is 33.39 + 4.03 = 37.42 (calc), 0.15% of PBT 24,504.61. Small. On all balances in those buckets the upper bound is 126.68 (calc: 6,037.31 x 1% + 6,630.60 x 1%). Not material to earnings.

### P3-4. Auditor CARO annexure corroborates five note findings (GREEN to YELLOW)
- CARO clause ii(b), PDF p.204: quarterly returns to banks agree with unaudited books, except the company has not filed returns for the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Confirms BOR-3 (Note 43(iii), p.260). The auditor, not only the company, records the omission.
- CARO vii(a), PDF p.205: "generally regular" in income tax and provident fund, "a slight delay in a few cases". Ties to interest on delayed tax payments 104.50 (Note 26, p.241). No arrears over six months.
- CARO vii(b), PDF p.205: disputed dues = Income tax penalty 91.66, F.Y. 2017-18, NFAC. Same item as Note 34 (AY 2018-19). Consistent.
- CARO x(b), PDF p.205: preferential warrant allotment in compliance with Sections 42 and 62; funds used for the purpose raised. Ties to Note 13.
- CARO ix(a), (d); xi(c); xix, PDF pp.205-206: no loan default; no short-term funds used long term; no whistle-blower complaints; no material uncertainty on meeting liabilities within one year. CARO iii(a): investments in "two companies" (matches Navictronics increase and new Fermionic stake, Note 4).
- Not covered by CARO: ARCPL receivable terms, ageing basis, the 912.00 cash flow line.

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- Revenue lift with weak cash: ARCPL sales +115.8% with a receivable that rose 12,129.31 (Note 31). Alternative reading: legitimate trade credit to a JV in a build-up phase. Observation that separates: ARCPL's collections and ARCPL end-customer billing.

Aggressive or thin accounting:
- Cover on old receivables rests on the government presumption; matrix covers at most 31.5% of the under-6-month bucket (Pass 2 P2-5).
- 3-month cancellable leases kept off balance sheet (Note 42): 291.01 expense.
- Guarantee commission booked as income against a "deemed investment" (85.50).
- Level 3 label on every instrument with no inputs (Note 38).

Undisclosed risk indicators:
- March 2026 bank returns not filed (Note 43(iii); CARO ii(b)).
- ARCPL's own lender returns did not agree to its books (Cons Note 44).
- Three CFOs in FY26 and two independent director exits (Note 31(i), p.245). Reasons NOT FOUND.
- Auditor sees no fraud (CARO xi(a)), no whistle-blower complaints (xi(c)); no adverse opinion.

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices.
