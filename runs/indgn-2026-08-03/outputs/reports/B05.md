### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **Tectonic**: Q2 FY26 — 4 paying customers, $2mn H1 revenue, "pipeline shaping up well," conversions hoped by end-December (Q2 FY26 call, Suhas, p.14). Q3 FY26 — **no updated customer count or revenue figure given in prepared remarks**; only generic platform description (Q3 FY26 call, Manish, p.4). This is a disclosure gap worth flagging — the metric that was precisely tracked in Q1/Q2 went quiet for a quarter. Q4/Annual FY26 — returns with detail: 5 customers by year-end, 2 transitioned pilot-to-long-term, headline win: Tectonic engagement with the **largest customer**, starting in Germany (Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.7-8). Net customer-count growth (4→5) across H2 FY26 was slower than the Q2-alone pace (2→4), even as deal *quality* (largest-customer win) improved. **Trend: strengthening in strategic weight, weakening in disclosed cadence/count momentum.**
- **Margin recovery**: strengthening specificity in Q2→Q3 (bps-quantified, sequential-improvement date given), then **specificity softens in Q4** — the "back to ~20%" framing is replaced by "revert to the earlier levels of higher margins" without a restated number, and the horizon moves from "sequential improvement from Q1 FY27" to full reversion only in "second half of FY27." Flag: timeline not clearly broken, but precision is fading — worth probing next quarter.
- **Largest customer**: Q2 FY26 — "pain... largely behind us," bullish near-to-midterm (Q2 FY26 call, Suhas/Manish, p.9-10). Q3 FY26 — still pipeline-stage: "pipeline...is robust. It's just the conversion has taken more time than what we expected" (Q3 FY26 call, Manish, p.8). Q4/Annual FY26 — breakthrough finally lands (Tectonic Germany), but revenue conversion is explicitly pushed into FY27 (Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.3). **Trigger present in all three quarters, timeline slipped ~2-3 quarters from the Q2 "near-to-midterm" framing.**
- **Organic vs. inorganic growth bridge** (probe item per task): Q2 FY26 — fully organic (BioPharm not yet consolidated), +17.1% YoY INR / +12.4% YoY USD (Q2 FY26 call, Suhas, p.8). Q3 FY26 — total +30.8% YoY INR, **ex-BioPharm organic +18.3% YoY / +5.9% QoQ** (Q3 FY26 call, Manish, p.2). Q4 FY26 — total +32.8% YoY, but **organic constant-currency growth explicitly given as only 12% YoY and "a little north of 3%" QoQ** (Q4/Annual call, Suhas, p.16, Q&A Yash Mehta). **This is a material deceleration in organic growth (18.3%→12% YoY) masked by an accelerating headline number driven by BioPharm/Cake/Warn consolidation — flagged as a red flag below (AIMTRON-lesson item).**
- **NEW trigger, Q4/Annual FY26 only**: Cake Kommunikations (DACH) — appears for the first time in the Q4/Annual call ("we just spoke about our Tectonic engagement with our largest client in Germany. And now you can understand why we did Cake" — Manish, p.7-8), consistent with its stated close date being at the very end of Q3 FY26/into Q4. Not a "why now" red flag on timing, but disclosure is thin — no deal value, multiple, or integration timeline given anywhere in the anchored transcripts (unlike BioPharm and Warn & Co, both of which had explicit consideration figures).
- **Dropped/faded**: Warn & Co, introduced with specifics in Q2 FY26 call (GBP2mn upfront + earn-out, "not likely to have any material impact," Suhas, p.9), is never separately quantified again in Q3 or Q4 FY26 calls — folded into a generic "3 acquisitions" reference by Q4/Annual call (Manish, p.7). Consistent with management's own "immaterial" framing at the outset, so this is not evidence of concealment, but it is a trigger that quietly disappeared from standalone tracking.

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. What management says about competitors (with credibility check)

Management repeatedly frames Indegene's "true competition" as **agencies and CROs**, not IT-services peers, and claims to be actively taking share from them, accelerated by GenAI ("Our true competition is agencies and CRO, and we are taking share from them accelerated by GenAI. And the pie itself is growing," Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.8). They also explicitly reject the "IT services company that happens to serve life sciences" categorization (Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.7). **Credibility check**: this is a narrative/positioning claim with no quantified market-share evidence given in the transcripts (e.g., no cited agency-spend erosion data, no named competitor losing business); it should be tested against peer concalls and third-party industry data at the peer-verification stage (see 4B).

### 3B. Industry/market intelligence dropped in the calls

- Life-sciences industry growth: **5%-8% CAGR through 2029** cited in Q3 FY26 call (Manish, p.4); refined to **5%-8% CAGR 2026-2028** in Q4/Annual call (Manish, p.11), alongside **CY2025 industry growth of 9% vs 6.4% in CY2024** (Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.11).
- MFN drug pricing / Pfizer-Trump deal (Sept 30, 2025) and Section 232 pharma tariffs (100% on branded/patented imports from 1 Oct 2025, with manufacturing-in-US exemptions) discussed at length in Q2 FY26 call (Manish, p.2-4); framed as limited-impact for Indegene, "top 30 life science companies regained approximately USD440 billion in value" post-deal (Q2 FY26 call, Manish, p.3).
- H-1B visa fee changes: Indegene states minimal exposure — ~550 US employees, ~45 (8%) on H1B, 5-8 new H1B deputations/year (Q2 FY26 call, Manish, p.4).
- DTC marketing tightening: framed as a potential tailwind (spend shift to digital/HCP channels) rather than a headwind (Q2 FY26 call, Manish, p.4).
- Biotech funding environment: described as recovering ("It's coming up now and been on the upswing," Q4/Annual call, Manish, p.15).
- New India labour code: no material impact cited, since Indegene's comp structure already exceeds the 50% base-pay threshold that triggers higher gratuity/leave-encashment provisions elsewhere (Q3 FY26 call, Suhas, p.14).
