Press Release 
 
Dear Shareholders, 
The Rights Issue of equity shares of Jyoti Structures Limited which was opened on February 
17, 2025 was closed on March 10th, 2025. Against the issue size of Rs 499.09 crores, a 
subscription of Rs. 459.69 Crores, amounting to 92.11% of the issue size was received. The 
Company is sincerely grateful to the shareholders for standing by the company. 
 
Based on the orders already in hand, the carry forward order book of the company will be 
approximately over Rs. 1,800 crores, the majority of which is to be executed in the next 12 
to 18 months. 
 
By your continued support, the company will strive to reach greater heights.
