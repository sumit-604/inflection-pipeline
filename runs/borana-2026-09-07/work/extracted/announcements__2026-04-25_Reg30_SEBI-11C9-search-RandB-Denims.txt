Dear Sir/Madam,  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform you 
that a search proceeding was initiated pursuant to an order passed by the Special 
Judge, Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”), under Section 11C  (9) of the 
Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992. 
 
The said search was conducted at the premises of R & B Denims Limited ( the 
promoter group entit y as mentioned in RHP of the Borana Weaves Limited ) of the 
Company, as well as at the premises of certain promoters and the Chief Financial 
Officer of R & B Denims Limited , on Wednesday, April 22, 2026, and concluded on 
Thursday, April 23, 2026 at 06:00 PM. 
 
The Company clarifies that the aforesaid search was not conducted at the registered 
office of Borana Weaves Limited. Further, based on information currently available, 
there is no material impact on the financials, business operations or any other 
activities of the Company due to the aforesaid search proceedings. 
 
In the event of any material developments requiring disclosure under the SEBI Listing 
Regulations, the Company shall promptly inform the Stock Exchanges in accordance 
with applicable regulatory provisions. 
 
The details as required under Clause 19 of Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI 
Listing Regulations read with relevant SEBI Circulars are enclosed as Annexure. 
 
You are requested to take the above on record and disseminate to all concerned.
