# RACLGEAR — RACL Geartech Ltd

Shallow read, 2026-09-16. Framework: screens/SHALLOW_ANALYSIS_FRAMEWORK.md v1.0.
Corpus: screens/corpus/RACLGEAR/. BSE 520073, NSE RACLGEAR, ISIN INE704B01017.

## Business Understanding Narrative

**What it does, and how it makes money.** RACL cuts precision gears and transmission parts
for vehicle makers, at two plants in Noida and Gajraula. One operating segment, split
geographically into India and outside India (Results Q1FY27, p.4). The customers are the
reason to look. FY26 revenue by country was Germany Rs 156.49 cr, Austria Rs 142.43 cr,
India Rs 110.20 cr, Italy Rs 19.12 cr and Japan Rs 18.98 cr. Exports were 75 percent of
sales and Europe was 69 percent. The money is made by winning a nomination on a specific
part for a specific programme, then supplying it for that programme's life. Margin comes
from the precision and the premium segment, and it is high for an auto component maker: 25.21
percent consolidated EBITDA in FY26.

**What is changing.** Two things at once. First, the customer base is widening from two
European anchors into eight vehicle segments. FY26 mix was 2-wheeler 30 percent, commercial
vehicle 20, recreational vehicle 18, passenger car 13, tractor and agriculture 10,
industrial 5, 3-wheeler 3 and e-mobility 1. Second, the balance sheet was repaired. Total
debt fell from Rs 297.59 cr to Rs 221.82 cr in FY26, debt to equity from 1.30 to 0.63, and
debt to EBITDA from 3.17 to 1.75.

**Why now.** FY25 was a wreck and FY26 was a recovery. KTM, the Austrian customer, went
through insolvency. Management is blunt about the cost: growth "would have at least 15% if
KTM would have not gone bankrupt" instead of the 1 percent it got (Q2FY26 call, p.19). FY25
profit fell to Rs 25.60 cr from Rs 39.81 cr in FY24. FY26 then delivered Rs 512.42 cr of
consolidated revenue, up 20.57 percent, with PAT margin up from 5.96 to 9.30 percent. A
stack of new programmes starts producing between October 2026 and FY28.

**What must be true.** The nominated programmes must reach mass production on their stated
dates. BMW's Project Titan must start in October or November 2026. The BRP shift drum must
run at 150,000 parts a year. Domestic growth must keep offsetting the European cycle. And
the new plant must fill without pushing leverage back up.

**What breaks it.** A second European demand shock breaks it, because Austria and Germany
were still 56 percent of Q1 FY27 customer revenue. A programme slipping a year breaks the
growth arithmetic, because the revenue is nominated years ahead. And capex funded by fresh
bank debt undoes the balance-sheet repair that the rating agency just rewarded.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Revenue is won years before it is earned. The corpus is full of nominations with start of
production dates: Kawasaki has one component in production, five resourced components start
January 2027, a premium 2-wheeler transmission programme starts July 2027 at Gajraula, one
programme starts October 2027, another in FY28. Margin follows content per unit and the
premium positioning, which CARE names directly: "healthy profitability margins considering
better operating margins in the premium segment".

The business is capital-intensive and working-capital heavy. Fixed asset turnover was 1.45
at H1 FY26, improved from 1.33. Debtor days were 82 and inventory days 77 in FY23. CARE
lists "working capital-intensive operations" as a constraint. That combination caps returns
however good the margin looks.

**Archetype: build-to-spec component maker.** A clean fit. The four drivers are the customer
capex cycle, the design-win pipeline, content per unit and input-cost pass-through, and all
four are visible. It also carries outsourcing-partner traits, notably client concentration
and wallet share.

Sector cap row: no auto-components row exists. The nearest is Cables and industrial products
at 25x. **Operator ruling needed before Stage 11.**

## 3. Competitive advantages

Switching costs are real and documented. A gear nominated into a BMW or Kawasaki programme
is qualified for that programme, and requalifying costs the customer time it does not have.
The proof is the depth within single accounts. Management describes supplying BMW across
300cc, 390cc, 800cc, 1.2 litre and 1.8 litre platforms rather than one model (Q2FY26 call,
p.19). Five "resourced" components means work moved to RACL from another supplier, which is
a competitive win, not a volume order.

Two advantages are absent. There is no pricing power over raw material: the FY23 annual
report lists vulnerability to steel, energy, industrial oils and forgings costs. There is no
scale advantage; at Rs 512 cr of revenue RACL is small against the global gear makers.

**Moat: present but narrow.** It is programme-level spec-in, held one nomination at a time,
inside a cyclical end market.

## 4. The promoters

Gursharan Singh is Chairman and Managing Director and holds 33.46 percent after the
preferential allotment, down from 36.59 percent. Prabh Mehar Singh is Chief Operating
Officer and holds nil. Total promoter and promoter group holding is 42.68 percent, from
46.66 percent before the issue. **Encumbrance is nil.** CARE credits "experienced promoters
along with long track record of operations".

Two items belong on the record. First, a promoter is a state agency: The Pradeshiya
Industrial and Investment Corporation of U.P. holds 8.51 percent and is classified as
Promoter. Second, that agency sold 5 lakh shares in the open market, disclosed by the CFO on
the Q4 FY25 call. That is promoter-category selling, and it is a state body managing its own
portfolio rather than a family exit. Surfaced, not investigated.

Malabar India Fund holds 5.87 percent after the allotment, up from 4.27 percent. An
institution took up the issue.

Skin in the game is genuine but diluted: the family paid for the deleveraging with equity,
not with cash from operations. More on that in step 5.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

Management's claim, quoted once: "vision wise, we are very much focused to achieve in next
three to five years, this 1000 crore milestone" (Q4FY26 call, p.19).

- **FROM: R3 value-added and spec'd supplier.** Spec-in and switching costs give partial
  pricing power. That is RACL today: nominated parts, premium segment margins, a 25 percent
  EBITDA margin.
- **TO: R4 franchise and share-of-wallet leader.** Customer capture and repeat economics,
  with returns durable in the 25 to 30 percent range.

The mechanism for the climb is the resourcing wins and the multi-programme depth. Five
components moved to RACL from other suppliers. BMW buys across five platforms. Kawasaki has
started. That is wallet share increasing inside existing accounts, which is exactly the R4
definition.

Two things hold the claim back. Returns are unproven, because ROCE is NOT FOUND and the
asset base is heavy. And the revenue target implies a rate the guidance does not support:
Rs 1,000 cr in three to five years from Rs 512 cr needs roughly 15 to 25 percent compound
growth, while FY27 is guided at Rs 565 cr, which is about 10 percent. The rung-jump base
rate of one rung per two to three years is respected, so this is not a multi-rung red flag.

## 8. Proof check

The last two quarters move the way the triggers claim.

FY26 full year: consolidated revenue Rs 512.42 cr, up 20.57 percent; EBITDA margin 25.21
percent from 22.34; consolidated PBT Rs 65.73 cr from Rs 32.8 cr, up more than 100 percent.
Q1 FY27: consolidated revenue Rs 132.60 cr, up 21.99 percent; EBITDA Rs 32.19 cr at 24.28
percent; PBT Rs 16.82 cr, up 49.32 percent. Operating revenue grew 31.50 percent.

**Named proof point: the growth in Q1 FY27 came from India, not from Europe.** Revenue within
India was Rs 45.40 cr in the quarter against Rs 110.20 cr for all of FY26, which is a
quarterly run rate up about 65 percent. Austria was Rs 34.07 cr against an FY26 quarterly
average of about Rs 35.6 cr, so flat. Germany was Rs 38.69 cr against about Rs 39.1 cr, also
flat. The export share fell to 63 percent of standalone revenue from 75 percent in FY26. So
the diversification management claims is visibly doing the work while the European anchors
sit still.

**Proof gate: FIRED.** Margin, profit and the mix all moved, and the mix moved in the
direction the thesis needs.

One tension sits against it. FY27 is guided at Rs 565 cr plus or minus 5 percent, about 10
percent above FY26, while Q1 delivered 22 percent. Either the guidance is deliberately soft
or the next three quarters decelerate sharply. **The absence of the Q1 FY27 transcript means
the corpus cannot tell which.** That is the first thing a deep run should close.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Geographic concentration 56 percent.** Austria plus Germany were Rs 72.76 cr of about
  Rs 129.2 cr of Q1 FY27 customer revenue, and 67 percent of FY26. No filed top-customer
  percentage exists, so this is the proxy.
- **A customer already went insolvent once.** KTM's bankruptcy cost RACL about 14 points of
  FY25 growth on management's own arithmetic, and KTM volumes were guided at 40 to 50 percent
  of prior levels through FY26. By Q3 FY26 the tone had reversed to KTM doing "extremely
  robust". Both quotes are in the corpus. The recovery is management's read, not a filed
  number.
- **The deleveraging was equity-funded.** Roughly Rs 80 cr of preferential issue proceeds
  went to debt prepayment. Treat the 1.30 to 0.63 fall accordingly.
- **New capex reverses the direction.** "Budgeted capex plan for FY 2026-27 is Rs. 77.45 Cr
  and corresponding bank debt will be availed accordingly" (Q4FY26 deck, p.13). The stated
  policy is debt to equity not crossing 1.
- **Promoter-category selling.** PICUP sold 5 lakh shares in the open market.
- **A subsidiary margin that needs explaining.** RACL Gearsys GmbH reported Q1 FY27 revenue
  of Rs 8.19 cr and PAT of Rs 3.11 cr, a 38 percent net margin, on figures "reviewed by the
  Management" and expressly "not material to the Group" (Results Q1FY27, p.10). A wholly
  owned distribution arm earning 38 percent net is a transfer-pricing question, not a
  finding. Surfaced.
- **A transcript arithmetic error.** The Q4 FY26 call states FY26 EBITDA as "229.16 crores,
  which is 25.21%". Revenue of Rs 512.42 cr at 25.21 percent is Rs 129.2 cr, and the deck
  prints Rs 129.16 cr. This card uses Rs 129.16 cr.
- **Forex exposure is structural.** CARE lists foreign exchange fluctuation risk. Management
  says export terms are at port, so tariffs sit outside its costing.
- No related-party item, auditor emphasis or litigation surfaced.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

CARE Ratings reaffirmed CARE A-; Positive on Rs 101.88 cr of long-term term loan, CARE A-;
Positive and CARE A2+ on Rs 162.50 cr of working capital limits, and CARE A2+ on Rs 9.00 cr
of non-fund-based limits, on 10-Oct-2025. The outlook was revised from Stable, against CARE
A-; Stable on 04-Oct-2024.

This is the most useful independent line on the card set, because the agency both upgrades
the outlook and refuses to upgrade the rating. It credits the same things this card does:
experienced promoters, long track record, reputed clientele, healthy premium-segment margins,
diversified geographic mix, and the roughly Rs 80 cr equity infusion used to prepay debt. It
then holds the grade at A- for "moderate debt coverage indicators, raw material price
volatility, foreign exchange fluctuation risk, working capital-intensive operations, and the
cyclical nature" of the business.

Read that as the agency saying the recovery is real and the capital structure is still
ordinary. It is two notches below IOLCP's A+. The company's own summary attributes the
outlook revision to debt falling from Rs 336.93 cr to Rs 273.38 cr, which again is the
equity-funded repair.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED.** FY26 margin and profit expansion, Q1 FY27 continuation, and the
  mix shifting toward domestic while Europe stayed flat.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT OF CLIMB.** The ugly optic is FY25: PAT down to Rs 25.60 cr from
  Rs 39.81 cr and a 5.96 percent margin. The cause was exogenous, a customer's insolvency,
  and the company diversified through it rather than shrinking. The confirming observation is
  already on the record: domestic revenue grew about 65 percent per quarter in Q1 FY27 while
  Austria was flat. A structural reading would require the margin to have stayed down, and it
  did not.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Confirmed 2026-09-17 on a dated price, and it survives the
  forward arithmetic. At Rs 1,756.7 on 13-Sep-2026 the market cap is Rs 2,071 cr, which is
  within half a percent of the undated Bull AI figure this card first used. FY26 standalone
  PAT of Rs 46.56 cr is 44.5 times. Consolidated FY26 PAT is NOT FOUND; derived from
  consolidated PBT of Rs 65.73 cr it is about Rs 49.2 cr, or 42.1 times. The addendum below
  puts the forward multiple at 34.6 to 40.6 times on management's own FY27 guidance, and
  28.2 times even on the fastest defensible path. The nearest sector cap row is 25x, so the
  gap stays closed on every path. No valuation is performed here and no fair value is
  stated.

**Posture: EARNINGS-ONLY.** Proof fired, ugliness an artifact, gap closed. The matrix reads
this as a real climb that is already priced: the re-rating engine is spent and returns must
come from earnings growth alone. That makes the guidance tension decisive. Earnings-only
works if growth clears the Hurdle without help from a re-rating, and FY27 is guided at about
10 percent. The company needs the Rs 1,000 cr ambition, not the Rs 565 cr guidance, for this
posture to pay.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** A good business at a price that leaves nothing for error. Do not spend a full
/step1 yet.

This is not a quality objection. RACL is the highest-quality operator of the three names read
today: real switching costs, a dense dated pipeline, margin that survived a customer
bankruptcy, and an agency crediting the recovery. The objection is arithmetic. Roughly 44
times delivered earnings, a nearest sector ceiling of 25x, and FY27 guided at about 10
percent growth. Buying a proven climb after it is priced is the EARNINGS-ONLY cell, and the
guided growth does not clear a 25 percent compound target on its own.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first, whenever this name is promoted.**

1. **The FY27 guidance gap.** Q1 delivered 22 percent against a full-year guide of about 10
   percent. Get the Q1 FY27 transcript of 25-Aug-2026, which the corpus lacks, and establish
   whether the guide is conservatism or an expected H2 deceleration. This single fact decides
   whether the name is cheap or expensive against forward earnings.
2. **ROCE, on any basis.** NOT FOUND for every year. The company does not report it. Build it
   from the FY26 annual report. A 25 percent EBITDA margin on 1.45 fixed asset turnover with
   82 debtor days and 77 inventory days may not produce R4-grade returns, and the whole
   FROM-R3-TO-R4 claim rests on returns.
3. **Customer concentration, filed.** Austria plus Germany were 56 percent of Q1 FY27
   customer revenue and 67 percent of FY26. Get the actual top-customer percentage from the
   FY26 annual report, and KTM's share specifically, given it has been insolvent once inside
   the last two years.
4. **Leverage direction under the new capex.** FY27 capex of Rs 77.45 cr is to be funded with
   "corresponding bank debt". Test that against the stated policy of debt to equity below 1
   and against the CARE constraint of moderate debt coverage. The deleveraging was
   equity-funded once and cannot be again without dilution.
5. **The German subsidiary's 38 percent net margin.** Rs 3.11 cr of PAT on Rs 8.19 cr of
   revenue, management-reviewed only. Establish the transfer-pricing basis before
   consolidated earnings are used in any valuation.

**What would change the view.** Two beats against the Rs 565 cr guide, with Project Titan and
the BRP shift drum in mass production and ROCE proven above 20 percent, would move this to
PROCEED. A price that re-opens the gap would do it faster. On the other side, a European
order deferral or a programme date slipping past FY28 would move it to DEEP WATCH.

**Nothing here is a price, a target or a position.** The shallow verdict decides only whether
the name earns a full run.

# Addendum: FY27 top line, bottom line and forward PE

Added 2026-09-17 at the operator's request. **This section sits outside the shallow
framework**, which stops before any forward estimate. It states no fair value, no exit PE
and no target price. Section 1B is not run and the Mental Model Declaration is unsigned.

## The top line is guided. The bottom line is not.

**Guided, verbatim.** "we have guidance of 565 plus minus 5% for FY27 in Q3... At the
moment, we are not [revising] because this plus minus 5 is a flexibility which we will
keep. But yeah, we are very confident we should be able to meet whatever we are giving as a
guidance" (Q4FY26 call, p.25). So the top line is **Rs 537 to 593 cr**, midpoint Rs 565 cr,
against FY26 consolidated revenue of Rs 512.42 cr. That is 4.8 to 15.8 percent growth,
midpoint 10.3 percent.

No bottom-line guidance exists. Every bottom line below is derived by me.

**Derivation.** I work from the profit-before-tax margin, because it is the margin the
company reports consistently on both bases. FY26 consolidated PBT margin was 12.83 percent
and Q1 FY27 was 12.68 percent. I take a band of 12.7 to 13.5 percent: operating leverage on
higher volume pushes up, and the new plant's depreciation from January 2027 trial production
plus interest on the Rs 77.45 cr capex, which management says will be met with
"corresponding bank debt", push down. Tax at 25.2 percent, the rate implied by FY26
standalone PAT of Rs 46.56 cr.

**One number on this card is derived, not disclosed, and it matters.** Consolidated FY26 PAT
is NOT FOUND in the corpus. Rs 49.2 cr is my figure, from consolidated PBT of Rs 65.73 cr at
25.2 percent. The German subsidiary pays German tax, so the true blended rate differs. The
FY2026 annual report would settle it and is not held.

**The tension this addendum sharpens.** Step 8 of this card flagged that Q1 grew 22 percent
against a full-year guide of about 10 percent, and said the corpus could not tell whether

year, it is on 28 times. **Both sit above the 25x nearest sector cap row**, which is why the
recognition gap reads closed either way.

## What this does to the verdict

**WATCH stands, and the arithmetic strengthens it.** The EARNINGS-ONLY posture says returns
must come from earnings growth alone, because the re-rating is spent. Test that against the
Hurdle. Guided FY27 growth on the bottom line is roughly 4 to 22 percent over the derived
FY26 consolidated PAT of Rs 49.2 cr, midpoint about 13 percent. The strategy targets 25
percent compound. Management's own Rs 1,000 cr ambition in three to five years would clear
it; the Rs 565 cr guidance for the year in front of us does not.

So the name needs either a guidance beat or a lower price. That is exactly what step 12
already asks for, and the forward numbers give it a threshold: **two prints above the Rs 565
cr path, with Project Titan and the BRP shift drum in mass production, would move this to
PROCEED.** Nothing here is a price, a target or a position.
