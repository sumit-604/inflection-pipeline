11. Capacity: 430+ net hires in six months to Sep 2026; fourth Pune design centre opened Oct 2026; Chennai AI lab due Q4 FY27 or early 2027 (yahoo URL).
12. THE ENGINE the thesis would rest on: a margin and growth step from account mining in North American industrial clients (data-centre power and cooling equipment demand named by management) delivered offshore. Reading one: the 15.6% margin is a new base from offshore leverage. Reading two: Q2 is a peak helped by a one-off credit and a pre-policy offshore shift, and FY27 guidance of 13.8-14.3% says management expects it to fall back. The observation that separates them: Q3 and Q4 FY27 EBITDA margin excluding the R&D credit, with offshore mix and DSO.
