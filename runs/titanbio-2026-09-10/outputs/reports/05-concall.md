### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

The only miss with an explanation attached is the FY25 revenue decline (row 5), and the
explanation is external-blame: "Economic Slowdown in the Global market, stiff
competition, rising cost" (AR FY25 MD&A, PDF p.98) with no discussion of what the
company itself could have done differently, and no revisiting of that explanation once
FY26 revenue rebounded (the rebound sentence names no cause at all, so the FY25 external
blame is never tested against what actually changed in FY26). The health-supplement
non-delivery (row 4) and the investing-outflow contradiction (row 3) get silence, not
even an acknowledgment that the plan or the description needs updating. No annual report
in the set contains a sentence of the form "we did not deliver X because Y" that
attributes a miss to the company's own execution. Pattern: external-blame-heavy for the
one miss that is explained; silence for the two misses that are not.

Titan market share, capacity, or positioning against these totals) — low information
