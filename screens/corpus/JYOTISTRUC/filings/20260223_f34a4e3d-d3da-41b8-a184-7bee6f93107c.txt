Sub: Intimation on Successful Completion and Charging of 400 kV Multi-Circuit Line-in-Line-Out 
(LILO) of Pirana (Torrent Power) – Pirana (Power Grid) at POWERGRID Substation, 
Ahmedabad. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL”) is pleased to share the successful completion and charging of the 
400 kV Multi-Circuit Line in Line Out (LILO) of Pirana (Torrent Power)- Pirana (PowerGrid) at 
POWERGRID substation, Ahmedabad. 
 
This strategically important line is a key component of the 4.5 GW Renewable Energy evacuation system 
for Khavda Phase-II. 
 
The project involved execution of 240 circuit kilo meters (CKM) of High Temperature Low Sag (HTLS) 
line, including three crossings of the Sabarmati River and several critical road crossings in the urban 
vicinity of Ahmedabad. The team worked through significant challenges such as Right of Way (ROW) 
constraints, limited shutdown availability, and water-logged locations, adopting innovative 
construction approaches including porta mats, high-capacity dewatering pumps, and heavy-lift cranes. 
 
We are grateful to Torrent Power Grid Ltd for their trust, guidance, and continued cooperation 
throughout the project. 
 
We request you to take the same on record.
