Sub: Outcome of the Meeting of the Board of Directors held on March 02, 2026 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we hereby inform you that the Board of Directors of California Software Company Limited 
(“the Company”) at its meeting held today, i.e., Monday, March 02, 2026, inter alia, considered and 
approved the following: 
 
Extension of Time for Payment of First and Final Call on Partly Paid-up Equity 
Shares 
The Board of Directors has approved the extension of the last date for payment of the First and Final Call 
of ₹7.50 (Rupees Seven and Paise Fifty only) per partly paid-up equity share on the outstanding partly 
paid-up equity shares issued pursuant to the Rights Issue of the Company, in accordance with the terms of 
the Letter of Offer dated January 07, 2025, the Articles of Association of the Company and applicable 
provisions of law. 
The original last date for payment of the First and Final Call was March 02, 2026. 
Considering representations received from certain shareholders seeking additional time for payment and 
taking into account the prevailing market environment and liquidity conditions, and in the interest of 
ensuring equitable opportunity to all shareholders and facilitating smooth completion of the Rights Issue, 
the Board has approved extension of the payment period.
