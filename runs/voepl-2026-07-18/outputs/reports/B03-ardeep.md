**Nuance on the ii(a) vs Note 38(d) "tension" flagged by B02 Pattern A:** these are not strictly the same statement. CARO ii(a) evaluates whether *management's own* physical verification of inventory was reasonable in frequency/coverage (it was, per the auditor). Note 38(d) is the auditor's *own* procedural disclosure that *the auditor* did not personally physically verify cash/closing stock and instead relied on management's certification ("Due to the high volume and nature of business, it is not possible to verify quantitative details of the goods manufactured and traded by the Company," AR p.71/92 standalone, p.90/92 consolidated). This is standard practice for many statutory audits of manufacturers (auditors routinely rely on management counts with test-check observation rather than performing a full independent recount) and is not per se inconsistent with an unmodified opinion. That said, B02's underlying point stands and is corroborated here: the *combination* of (i) auditor non-verification of the largest asset class (₹21,245.49L inventory, ~35% of standalone total assets), (ii) test-check-only vouching of purchase/sales/expense transactions (Note 38(b)), and (iii) no independent balance confirmation across nearly all of net working capital (Note 37) — issued by a smaller regional firm (Jain Chhajed & Associates, sole-city Nashik practice) alongside an unmodified opinion and a clean "adequate and effective" IFC certification — is a legitimate assurance-quality flag for a company of this balance-sheet size (₹610 Cr standalone total assets). 🔴 Red Flag, confirmed but reframed with the CARO ii(a) nuance above.
