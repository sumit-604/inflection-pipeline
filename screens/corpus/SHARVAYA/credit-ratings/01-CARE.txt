Rationale and key rating drivers 
CARE has reaffirmed and withdrawn the outstanding ratings of ‘CARE B-; Stable; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING* (Single B Minus; 
Outlook: Stable; ISSUER NOT COOPERATING*)’ assigned to the fund-based cash credit limit of Sharvaya Metals Limited (SML) 
with immediate effect. The rating withdrawal is at the request of SML and ‘No Objection Confirmation’ received from the lenders 
who have extended the facilities rated by CARE. 
Further, CARE has also withdrawn rating assigned to term loan facility with immediate effect as SML has surrendered said 
facility rated by us and there is no amount outstanding under said facilities as on date.  
The ratings assigned to the bank facilities of SML remained constrained by its modest scale of operations and moderate 
profitability margins, moderately leveraged capital structure and moderate debt coverage indicators. The ratings are further 
constrained on account of susceptibility of profit margins to volatile material prices and company’s presence in a competitive 
and fragmented industry. Ratings however, derive strength experienced promoters in the industry. 
 
Analytical approach: Standalone 
 
Outlook: Stable 
 
Detailed description of the key rating drivers 
At the time of last rating dated July 31, 2024, the following were the rating weaknesses and strengths (updated based on 
information available from SML). 
 
Key Weakness 
Modest scale of operations and moderate profitability margins 
The scale of operations remained modest and marked by a total operating income (TOI) of Rs. 71.45 crores for FY24 as 
compared to Rs. 70.53 crore for FY23.  
Moreover, profitability marked by PBILDT margin slightly declined and remained moderate at 5.09% during FY24 (P.Y.: 5.44%) 
on account of increased cost of materials consumed. Consequently, PAT Margin also declined and was low to 1.40% during 
FY24 (P.Y.: 2.81%). However, in absolute terms profitability remained low marked by a PBILDT and PAT of Rs. 3.64 crore and 
Rs. 1.00 crore for FY24 (P.Y.: Rs. 3.84 crore and Rs. 1.98 crore respectively). 
 
Moderately leveraged capital structure and moderate debt coverage indicators  
Capital structure of the entity improved due to increased networth base on account of profit accretion but remained moderately 
leveraged on account of high debt level vis-à-vis its net worth base. Thus, overall gearing of the company stood at to 1.70x as 
on March 31, 2024 compared to 2.16x as on March 31, 2023. 
Further, the debt coverage indicators also remained moderate with interest coverage ratio of 2.74x during FY24 (P.Y. 2.64) 
although, total debt to gross cash accruals (TD/GCA) weakened to 10.41 years as on March 31, 2024 compared to 6.61 years 
as on March 31, 2023 account of decline in gross Cash Accruals (GCA) during FY24.

Susceptibility of profit margins to volatile material prices 
The material is the major cost driver (constituting about 93% of total cost of sales in FY24) and the prices of the same are 
volatile in nature therefore cost base remains exposed to any adverse price fluctuations in the prices of the aluminum scrap 
being major cost components amongst all materials are volatile in nature. Accordingly, the profitability margins of the entity 
are susceptible to fluctuation in material prices. However, SML pass on the increase in prices of raw material to the clients. 
 
Presence in competitive and fragmented industry 
SML operates in a highly competitive and fragmented aluminum product industry. The entity witnesses intense competition 
from both the other organized and unorganized players domestically and globally. This fragmented and highly competitive 
industry results into price competition thereby posing a threat to the profit margins of the companies operating in the industry. 
 
Key Strengths 
Experienced promoters in the industry and association with reputed utensil brands 
SML is managed by Mr. Shreyans Katariya, Managing Director and Mr. Balasaheb Kale, Executive Director who have more than 
a decade of experience in the business. Directors are further assisted by second line of management and experienced force of 
labourer who undertake day to day activities. Company has established relationship with various suppliers and customers in the 
short span of time which is enabling continuous and repeated orders from client. SML’s products are sold to authorized raw 
material suppliers of well-known utensil brands such as Hawkins, Prestige and Nirlep. Further, company’s products are also sold 
to automobile industry and multiple industries.

About the company 
Sharvaya Metals Limited (SML) (formerly known as Sharvaya Metals Private Limited) is engaged in manufacturing of various 
aluminium products. SML was incorporated as a private limited company in the year 2014. SML is managed by Mr. Shreyans 
Katariya, Managing Director and Mr. Balasaheb Kale, Executive Director who have more than a decade of experience in 
business. The company was converted into a public limited company from February 2024. Company is engaged in 
manufacturing of Aluminum Alloyed Ingots, Aluminium circles, Aluminium Billets and Aluminium Sheets of different 
measurements and sizes as per the requirement of clients. The company is also manufacturing high precision Aluminium 
components required in Electric Vehicles.
