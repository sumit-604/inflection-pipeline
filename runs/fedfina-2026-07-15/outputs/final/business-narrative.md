Bank parented gold lender mid credit repair

Fedbank Financial Services (FEDFINA) is a secured NBFC parented by Federal Bank, which owns 60.7%. It lends against two collateral pools. Gold is about 53% of AUM at yields near 17.8%, and property backed LAP plus small ticket home loans make up most of the rest. Federal Bank parentage supplies funding access and a CRISIL AA+ rating. The book ran through 757 branches at last count and earns roughly 85% of revenue as net interest spread.

The thesis is a turnaround, not a clean compounder. Revenue compounded 26% and profit 41% across FY21 to FY26, then FY25 broke the line: PAT fell 8% to about Rs 225 Cr as the impairment charge jumped 229% to Rs 216 Cr. The damage came from small ticket LAP and the wind down of an unsecured business loan book. Management exited the unsecured book entirely, derecognised a Rs 770 Cr portfolio in Q1 FY26, and is rebuilding around the two secured engines. The best fit read is GARP and Turnaround, tied.

Gate 0 scores 48 out of 160 and prints AVOID. Two of the five blocks score zero, and both are mechanical rather than a judgment on the business. Return on capital is not computable because an NBFC balance sheet carries no current liabilities line, and operating cash flow reads negative every year because a lender books loan growth as an operating outflow under Ind AS 7. Strip those two zeros and the growth block scores full marks. The genuine, non mechanical depressors are real: provision coverage fell from 40% to 32%, credit cost spiked to 1.8% of average assets in FY25, and profit dipped before recovering.

Cash flow looks alarming and mostly is not. Cumulative operating cash flow to profit ran at negative 5x across six years, and every year was free cash flow negative. For a growing lender that is structural. Disbursements are an operating outflow, financing inflows fund them, and the cash pile grew rather than shrank. The one genuine earnings quality wrinkle was direct assignment gain on sale income, which rose to about 50% of pre tax profit in FY25 from 28% a year earlier. Management is de-emphasising it: nine month FY26 DA income was Rs 1 Cr against Rs 62 Cr a year before.

Credibility grades B. Headline turnaround promises landed cleanly. Credit cost held inside the 1% band every quarter of FY26, the secured mix transition completed, DA income came down as promised, and 113 of a planned 100 to 150 gold branches opened by Q3. Against that, two dated technical commitments got dropped without mention, collections in-housing slipped two full quarters, and CRAR and Tier 2 disclosure vanished in Q3 exactly as leverage rose most. A verifier surfaced four asset quality misses the concall read had softened: Stage 2 disclosure refused, a roughly 30% cure rate on secured paper, the gold mix ceiling walked from 45 to 49% to unconstrained, and the fact that 52% gold growth was about 90% price, not tonnage. A FY25 fraud of Rs 5 Cr across 11 accounts sat absent from the risk narrative. Senior leadership turned over almost completely in 18 months, replaced by credible external hires including a 29 year BFSI MD, and the promoter pledge is nil.

Peers confirm some claims and puncture others. The sub Rs 5 lakh ticket LAP stress does trace to MFI sector spillover, corroborated across three lenders. But Fedfina's cited 10 to 12% gold tonnage growth collides with Manappuram's actual 2.8% year on year, which reframes the 52% gold AUM print as a gold price effect. Fedfina's cost to income near 56 to 57% sits 15 to 25 points above SBFC at 35%, Five Star at 31 to 41%, and MAS Financial at 37%. No peer reported the Maharashtra or Tamil Nadu stress Fedfina named, which supports its company specific framing but leaves the geography story uncorroborated.

Runway is MASSIVE on any conservative cut. The organized secured lending market it plays in is around Rs 23 lakh crore, its current share sits under 2%, and revenue headroom runs roughly 59x. Management's own TAM claim of Rs 92 lakh crore is inflated: a FY23 vintage all India MSME credit gap, about 4x the conservative number and far broader than the gold plus secured LAP niche the company actually serves. The five year growth implied by its own opportunity needs a 27% AUM CAGR against branch capex that supports only about 13%, so the gap has to come from per branch productivity and Federal Bank funded co-lending, not new branches.
