## Monitorables and triggers

1. Q1 FY27 consolidated operating cash flow, in the August 2026 results, needs to print positive with working capital days below 344; negative with days at or above 344 is the falsifier and turns the cash call structural.
2. Working capital days should trend toward the 160 to 170 day target in the quarterly results; flat or above 344 is red and tests whether the ramp is converting to cash.
3. Revenue growth should hold at or above 25% year on year; below 25% for two consecutive quarters breaks the growth pillar.
4. EBITDA margin should hold at or above 35% in the segment disclosure; compression of more than 200bps year on year without a one off signals the peak has passed.
5. The order book should rise above the static Rs 6,500 Cr with new wins reconciled; flat for two or more quarters with undisclosed reconciliation is a credibility red.
6. Top five customer concentration should stay at or below 51% and top one below about 20% (MD&A, investor presentation); a top one crossing 20% or top five crossing 65% concentrates the risk further.
7. The remaining four of eight dedicated factories should commission near the November 2026 guide with utilisation disclosed, and net debt to equity should stay below about 0.5x; slippage plus rising leverage means the ramp is debt funded.
8. The open SEBI PIT code of conduct matter should resolve cleanly with RPT quantum stable (secretarial audit, exchange filings); a new adverse order or an auditor or independent director exit is red. Price is actionable only at Rs 295 to Rs 368 with the checklist clean, and the zone is withdrawn if it is reached because the OCF falsifier fired.
