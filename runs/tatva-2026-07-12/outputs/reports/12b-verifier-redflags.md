## PART A — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, anchored)

Anchors are (call, speaker, transcript location).

1. **Jolva groundbreaking slipped three consecutive calls, each reframed as engineering upside.**
   Q2 FY26 (Chintan Shah, p.11): "we want to break ground by January, February of 2026." Q3 FY26
   (Chintan Shah, p.4-5/13): "break ground in this current quarter" / "somewhere around mid-February."
   Q4 FY26 (Chintan Shah, p.10): "we break ground in mid-July," with the delay attributed to detailed
   engineering that "reduces our need for a number of reactors" — a slip presented as optimization.

2. **Jolva commissioning date pushed out pre-construction.** Q3 FY26 (Chintan Shah, p.11):
   "September or October of 2027." Q4 FY26 (Ajesh Pillai/Chintan Shah, p.6/10): "January to March
   production ... early calendar year '28." Moved ~4-6 months before ground was even broken.

3. **Semiconductor first commercial dispatch missed its original Q4 FY26 target without acknowledgment.**
   Q2 FY26 (Chintan Shah, p.5): "Our target is to deliver this commercial batches in quarter 4 of
   current financial year." Q3 FY26 (p.7): "early February we'll start our first plant scale batch."
   Q4 FY26 (Ajesh Pillai/Chintan Shah, p.4/6): "dispatch would happen in the current quarter" (Q1 FY27),
   framed as a "significant milestone ... completed successfully" with no reference to the missed original
   target.

4. **Semiconductor full-commercialization date crept ~1 year later across the three calls.**
   Q2 FY26 (p.9): "from 2028. Maybe end of '27, early 2028." Q3 FY26 (p.8): "around 2028." Q4 FY26 (p.6/14):
   "end of 2028, early 2029."

5. **ROCE forward target deflected in two consecutive quarters (repeated evasion).** Q2 FY26
   (Krishan Parwani, p.14): "I don't have the ROCE calculation with me ... Ajesh can tell you offline."
   Q3 FY26 (Jay Vaghasia, p.17): "It's not going to be this 7% or 10% ... it will have an impact" — no
   forward number given either time.

6. **Electrolyte Salts guidance metric reframed three times.** Q2 FY26 (Chintan Shah, p.9): "~INR15
   crores ... FY26; 10% of revenue by FY27." Q3 FY26 (p.7): "calendar year '26 will show at least 7% to
   8% ... revenue," blamed on internal shipment delays. Q4 FY26 (Ajesh Pillai, p.5): "Around 8% to 10%"
   of FY27. Metric shifted (absolute-INR to %-of-revenue) and reference period shifted (FY to CY and back).

7. **Goods-in-transit / working-capital disclosure raised once, never revisited.** Q2 FY26
   (Chintan Shah, p.10): goods in transit "about INR26 crores" (up from "INR16 crores-INR17 crores").
   Not updated in Q3 or Q4 despite repeated revenue-recognition-timing questions.

8. **Dividend declared against rising short-term borrowings; the finance-cost point was not engaged.**
   Q4 FY26 (Jay Vaghasia, p.13): "our loans have increased ... Short-term borrowings have increased. And
   at the same time, we are paying dividends. So it's not justifiable, right?" Management answered only
   payout philosophy ("retain 80% ... distribute nearly about 10%").

9. **PTC commoditization admission, volunteered but never re-addressed with a mitigation.** Q2 FY26
   (Chintan Shah, p.13): PTC "is very easy ... anyone can replicate ... we were two companies in India
   ... now I believe there are 9 or 10." PTC is still ~24% of quarterly revenue (Q4 INR311mn of INR1,341mn)
   yet no mitigation for the segment is revisited in Q3/Q4.

10. **Customer concentration never quantified.** Growth is repeatedly tied to "2 or 3 new customers"
    (Q2 p.9; Q3 p.15) but no top-customer concentration metric is disclosed in any of the three calls.

11. **Destocking-to-restocking "industry recovery" narrative pushed in all three calls, not corroborated
    by peers.** Q2 FY26 (Ajesh Pillai, p.3): "destocking phase seems to be clearly over ... onset of
    restocking." Q3/Q4 continue the theme. Peer spot-reads contradict: NEOGEN May 2026 (opening remarks):
    "the global chemical industry continues to operate in a challenging environment marked by persistent
    overcapacity, pricing volatility, and subdued demand"; CLEAN Nov 2025: volume recovery "Not Q3 for
    sure" (Siddharth Sikchi).

12. **"Sole supplier" framing for hybrid-battery electrolytes overlaps a live India competitor.** Q2 FY26
    (Chintan Shah, p.16): "from India we are proud that we are the sole supplier." NEOGEN (May 2026) is
    itself an India-based lithium electrolyte/electrolyte-salt manufacturer ("Neogen Ionics"), building
    "India's first non-FEOC compliant electrolyte salt manufacturing platform." The narrower "hybrid"
    sub-segment is not directly addressed by any peer.

13. **SDA revenue fell sequentially in Q3 and again in Q4 while the narrative is "consistently growing,"
    and a direct sequential-weakness question was reframed away.** SDA: INR593mn (Q2) -> INR534mn (Q3,
    -10% QoQ) -> INR525mn (Q4, -2% QoQ). Q3 FY26 (Raman KV, p.8), asked about the sequential decline,
    Chintan Shah: "Don't track SDA on a sequential basis, I would request please track SDAs on an annual
    basis." Legitimate campaign-lumpiness explanation, but a metric-reframe on the #1 trigger.

14. **PTC described in Q4 as "steady organic growth in line with historical trends" the same quarter it
    printed -20% YoY.** Q4 FY26 (Ajesh Pillai, p.2/3): PTC revenue INR311mn "lower by 20% on a year-on-year
    basis," yet outlook language is "steady organic growth in line with historical trends." Disclosed
    (not hidden), but a narrative-vs-number tension. (Note Q4 was +11% QoQ; the -20% is off a high base.)
