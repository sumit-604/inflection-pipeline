Subject: Opening of Jewellery Showroom at Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh under Franchisee Owned Company

Operated (FOCO) Model

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read

with the Company's Policy for Determination of Materiality of Events and Information, we wish to inform that the

Board of Directors of the Company has approved the proposal for opening a jewellery showroom at Jabalpur, Madhya

Pradesh, under the Franchisee Owned Company Operated Model.

The proposed showroom, having an approximate retail area of 3,750    square feet, will mark the Company's first

expansion through the Franchisee Owned Company Operated              model (FOCO), representing a significant

milestone in its retail growth and business expansion strategy. The  Company believes that the adoption of the

franchisee model will facilitate expansion into new markets through  an asset-light approach, strengthen its brand

presence, and enhance customer outreach.

The proposed showroom is expected to offer the Company's range of jewellery products in accordance with the

Company's prescribed standards, branding guidelines, and quality specifications, thereby ensuring a consistent

customer experience across its retail network.

The expected date of commencement of operations are presently under finalisation and shall be implemented in due

course.
