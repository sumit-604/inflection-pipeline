## Rung migration
FROM **R1 commodity price-taker**, bordering R2 cost-advantaged converter -> TO
**R3 value-added supplier**, CLAIMED on one year of proof. Two cautions:
(1) converter -> spec'd-supplier is a change of SHAPE, not only a rung climb,
harder than a single-rung move; the library base-rate flags multi-rung claims as
a red flag. (2) R3 neighbourhood ~= 19x, and Venus already trades ~21x FY26 EPS

## The analogy (the difficult kitchen)
Venus owns a sterile factory clean enough for intravenous injectables and
equipped for cytotoxics, inspected and approved by several countries' regulators.
The kitchen has a fixed number of cooking hours a year; profit is hours x the
margin on what is cooked in them. Taped to the wall are 1,100 export permits;
~500 are in use. Until ~2 years ago Venus also ran a German warehouse that took
the kitchen's output and passed it to European buyers at a loss; that warehouse
is wound down and Venus now ships to Europe direct from India, leaving a small
German office to hold the permits. FY26: more valuable dishes, kitchen run harder
than ever, no German warehouse — profit roughly doubled. The kitchen is now near
full. A new building is going up next door; management says it is for the most
valuable dishes but has not said how big, what it costs, or when the ovens go in.
