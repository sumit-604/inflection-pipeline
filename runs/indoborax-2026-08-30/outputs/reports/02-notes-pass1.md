## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Mixed depreciation method within one PP&E class of assets** (AR p.88,
  policy (c)): "The company has followed written-down value method of
  providing depreciation with respect to assets located at Head Office"
  while SLM is used for Factory premises assets. Useful lives per Schedule
  II are standard (Buildings 60/30 yrs, Plant & equipment 10-20 yrs, Office
  equipment 5 yrs, Computers 3 yrs, Electrical installation 10 yrs,
  Furniture 10 yrs, Vehicles 8 yrs). No change in useful lives or method
  disclosed this year. 🟡 Watch - two depreciation methods for the same
  asset class (location-based split) is unusual and not separately
  quantified; no P&L impact of a method change since none occurred, but
  the policy itself is a disclosure-clarity flag.
- **No impairment indicators / no impairment testing disclosed** (AR
  p.88-89, policy (d)). No CGU, no growth/discount rate assumptions
  anywhere in the notes - consistent with a business with no goodwill and
  no intangibles on the balance sheet. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (not
  applicable given no goodwill/intangibles recognised).
- **Ind AS 116 (leases) - new leases entered in FY26** (AR p.94, Note 3
  standalone / p.132 consol): "Right to use leased asset" addition of Rs
  128.43 lakh during the year (prior year balance was land-only, Rs
  10.15 lakh). Lease Commitment during the year Rs 154.12 lakh,
  undiscounted future payments Rs 155.41 lakh, "Effect of discounting" Rs
  18.70 lakh, "Interest on leasehold rights" Rs 6.29 lakh. The company
  does NOT disclose the discount rate used as a percentage anywhere in
  this note (unlike the gratuity note, which states 7.69%). 🟡 Watch -
  discount rate for lease liability computation: NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT as
  an explicit %.
- **ECL / provisioning**: policy (AR p.89-90) is a standard 12-month/
  lifetime ECL matrix approach; in practice, FY26 saw an abrupt Rs 126.84
  lakh "Provision for doubtful debt" (Note 33, AR p.106/144) and a new
  deferred tax asset of Rs 58.61 lakh on "Provision for Doubtful Debts"
  (Note 19, AR p.102/140) that did not exist in FY25. See Trade
  Receivables section below - this looks like a legacy disputed
  receivable (Rs 118.22 lakh, disclosed as "Disputed... considered
  doubtful" in the FY25 comparative ageing column) being reclassified and
  fully provided in FY26. 🟡 Watch, not aggressive - looks like overdue
  conservatism, not earnings management, but the one-year jump from zero
  provisioning to full provisioning of a multi-year-aged item raises the
  question of why it wasn't provided earlier.
- **Revenue recognition**: Ind AS 115, 5-step model, adopted since
  1-Apr-2018 (AR p.92/129-130); sale of goods, control transfers on
  delivery. No contract assets/liabilities note, no unsatisfied
  performance obligations disclosure, no disclosure that these balances
  are nil. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (a completeness gap against Ind AS 115
  para 116-118 even if the true balance is zero, positive disclosure of
  "nil" is expected and is missing). 🟡 Watch.
- **No first-time standard adoptions this year.** No policy changes with
  quantified P&L impact disclosed anywhere in Notes 1-51/52. 🟢 Clean.
