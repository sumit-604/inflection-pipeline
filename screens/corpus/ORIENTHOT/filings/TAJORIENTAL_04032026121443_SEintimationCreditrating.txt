The rating(s) are specific to the terms and conditions of the instruments as indicated to us by you, and any change in the

assigned. Notwithstanding the foregoing, any change in the over-all limit of the instrument from that specified in this 
letter, would constitute an enhancement that would not be covered by or under the said Rating Agreement.
The rating(s) assigned must be understood solely as an opinion and should not be treated, or cause to be treated, as
