Dear Sirs, 
 
We wish to inform you, pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, that Esconet Technologies Limited  (“the Company”) has received a 
Letter of Registration (LOR) from Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) dated 3 rd March 2026, 
registering the Company as a Registered Manufacturer. 
 
The registration covers HCI / vSAN / VDI Ready Nodes, GPU Servers / HPCC Compute Nodes, Other Servers, 
Procurement of Servers, and Procurement of Exploration-related Hardware & Software.  
 
The registration is valid up to 31st March 2027 (Financial Validity), with technical enlistment validity based 
on experience documents up to 31st December 2030 , as per the terms specified in the LOR. This 
empanelment with ONGC, a premier Government of India enterprise in the energy sector, significantly 
strengthens the Company’s credentials and enhances its eligibility to participate in high-value tenders in the 
oil & gas and public sector domains. The registration reinforces the Company’s technical capabilities, 
financial strength, and compliance standards, and aligns with its strategic focus on expanding its presence 
in the energy and government infrastructure segments. 
 
Further details regarding this disclosure are provided in Annexure A , in compliance with SEBI Circular 
CIR/CFD/CMD/4/2015 dated September 9, 2015. 
 
You are requested to take the above information on record.
