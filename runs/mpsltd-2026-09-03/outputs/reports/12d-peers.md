## PART 4: OVERALL ASSESSMENT

B06's citation discipline is strong at the level of "is this fact real
and does it say what B06 says it says" — nearly every direct quote I
checked against source (roughly 20 spot-checks across quotes,
percentages, and dollar figures) is verbatim or near-verbatim accurate,
and the cross-peer hypothesis in Part 5 is a genuine synthesis, not
invention. The verdict discipline (no VERIFIED-on-one-peer, no
silence-to-verdict upgrades, all peer_questions addressed) is clean.

The two structural weaknesses are:
1. A recurring quarter/date transposition pattern: real quotes correctly
   captured but pinned to the wrong quarter's transcript (ECLERX
   consolidation + hedging content mis-dated to Oct-2025 instead of
   Jan-2026; INDGN's "agency/RO/CRO consolidation" and "GenAI equalizes
   access" quotes mis-dated to Jul-2026 instead of Apr-2026). In each
   case the fact survives (it is correctly dated somewhere else in the
   same report), so this does not change any verdict, but it means an
   operator or downstream reader who tries to verify the Q2 FY26 ECLERX
   or Q1 FY27 INDGN cell specifically, as labeled, will not find the
   claimed content there.
2. NIITMTS, the one peer treated as CITED-ONLY throughout, contains
   specific, quantified, sector-named evidence in its two most recent
   quarters (Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27) — a named client-budget pullback in
   Tech/Telecom and Management Consulting — that directly bears on
   Claim 3's Corporate Learning cross-read and was mischaracterized as
   "stabilization" rather than being surfaced as a live, continuing
   headwind.
3. One basis-confusion in the peer-usage narrative: Part 2A cites
   "eClerx... H1 FY26 USD revenue +20% YoY," but the Oct-2025 transcript
   states USD growth was +17% YoY; the +20% figure is the INR-terms
   growth rate for the same period. This is folded into the ECLERX Q2
   FY26 finding below since it draws on the same transcript and the same
   general area of the report.

None of these findings change the report's verdicts (all peer
triangulation outcomes stand as PARTIALLY VERIFIED / UNVERIFIABLE with
no claim of full VERIFIED status resting on the misattributed material),
so no CRITICAL is warranted. All are MAJOR (wrong or missed but the
conclusions survive) except the two labeling/precision points noted as
MINOR.
