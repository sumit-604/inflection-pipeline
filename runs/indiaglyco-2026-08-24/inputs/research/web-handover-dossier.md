## 1. MENTAL MODEL DECLARATION (SIGNED — operator, 25-Aug-2026)

India Glycols is not one archetype. It is three businesses inside one pre-demerger shell that stops existing as a single security on 1-Sep-2026 (Effective Date), Record Date 2-Sep-2026 [FILED — Reg-30 disclosure 20-Aug-2026 via exchange reporting]. Ratios: 1 IGL Spirits share per 1 IGL share; 1 Ennature Bio Pharma share per 3 IGL shares. Each entity is declared separately.

**Entity A — India Glycols residual** (bio-glycols, glycol ethers, NSU/performance chemicals, industrial gases, 49% of Clariant IGL Specialty Chemicals).
- Archetype: COMMODITY CONVERTER with a specialties option. Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds the glycols line by name; the converter multiple (0.5×ROCE + 7.5) is the ceiling until NSU proves itself as a separately reportable slice.
- Dominant variables: (1) bio-glycol realisation per unit against the petro-MEG spread; (2) NSU/performance chemicals revenue against the Rs 150 Cr FY27 guide; (3) Clariant JV dividend (~Rs 40 Cr p.a. [AGENCY — CARE 6-Mar-2026]) and any stake monetisation.
- Rejects as noise: consolidated group history; gases (~Rs 13 Cr/quarter); management's 4-5 year aspirations.
- Falsifier: BSPC realisation falls in a quarter where the APAC MEG index is flat or rising. That proves customer loss, not cycle.
- Mandate fit: the transition-alpha candidate of the three. Worst backward metrics, first inflecting quarter on record (Q1 FY27 chemicals net revenue +25% YoY, 11.4% EBITDA margin [MGMT]), no standalone price yet.

**Entity B — IGL Spirits** (IMFL, country liquor, ENA, fuel-grade ethanol under EBP).
- Archetype: two lines, two archetypes. Spirits is LICENCE-GATED DISTRIBUTION with captive-ENA cost integration; the brand-franchise claim is not yet earned. Bio-fuel is a REGULATED QUOTA-TAKER (the OMCs hold the scarcity, not the supplier).
- Dominant variables, in order: (1) UP/Uttarakhand excise policy and country-liquor price notifications — the FY25 margin gain was government-led [AGENCY — CARE]; (2) IMFL net realisation per case and owned-vs-distributed mix; (3) ESY 2026-27 OMC allocation from the October 2026 tender.
- Rejects as noise: consolidated PAT growth (finance-cost driven); the Rs 1,000 Cr 4-5 year EBITDA aspiration.
- Falsifier: FY27 IMFL realisation per case falls a second consecutive quarter while volume rises.
- Mandate fit: quality-at-margin-expansion, which the framework deprioritises; in scope only because it is unpriced until listing.

**Entity C — Ennature Bio Pharma** (plant APIs led by Thiocolchicoside, nutraceuticals, nicotine derivatives, guar gum post-scheme).
- Archetype: OUTSOURCING PARTNER with single-product concentration.
- Dominant variables: (1) Thiocolchicoside share of revenue and its price against new small competitors [SECONDARY — HDFC Sec Aug-2024]; (2) nicotine end-market split between NRT pharma and vaping inputs (export-only; India bans e-cigarettes) [INFERENCE from company product pages]; (3) Gloriosa superba seed cost, the agricultural raw material for Thiocolchicoside and Colchicine.
- Rejects as noise: branded nutraceutical platforms until any one reaches Rs 25 Cr revenue.
- Falsifier: a single quarter where Thiocolchicoside revenue falls >20% YoY on price.
- Mandate fit: micro-cap by construction (~2.2 Cr shares at 1:3 on ~6.7 Cr IGL shares [INFERENCE from Rs 33.51 Cr paid-up at Rs 5 face]); Q1 FY27 annualises to ~Rs 360 Cr revenue / ~Rs 40 Cr EBITDA. Forced selling by holders who cannot hold the fragment is the likely first-month pattern.

**The analogy (one shop, three registers).** One shop today with four counters under one roof. From 1-Sep-2026 it becomes three shops with three registers. One ownership paper becomes three (1:1 spirits-and-fuel, 1:3 pharma; the chemicals shop is what is left of the original paper). Each shop lives or dies on its own counter's regulars and suppliers from that date.

**Fragility, restated.** The demerger single-point failure is resolved (dates filed, boards seated). The new single point of failure is common to all three: the allocation of ~Rs 1,793 Cr of debt (31-Jan-2026 [AGENCY — CARE]) across three balance sheets, which nobody outside the company has seen. Verifiability ratio falls to 2 of 4 until the opening balance sheets are filed.
