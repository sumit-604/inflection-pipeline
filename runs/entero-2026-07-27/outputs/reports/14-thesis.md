## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Entero grows fast and the operator was right that the business is worth understanding. Revenue compounds near 30%. Margins are climbing from 4% to 5%. MedTech adds a higher-margin leg. The problem is not the business. The problem is the price.

The stock trades at about 55 times next year's earnings. A distributor that keeps 4-5% at the EBITDA line cannot hold that multiple. The framework says the right exit multiple is 19 times. To make 25% a year from here, the stock would have to keep its 55x multiple and grow into it. It will not. The multiple must fall to 19x. A buyer today pays for that fall. That is why the expected return from Rs 1,806 is minus 20% a year.

Fair value today is about Rs 617. The buy zone is Rs 451 to Rs 501, with a Rs 432 margin-of-safety floor. That is roughly a quarter of today's price. Reaching it needs a market fall, not a drift. Two other gates stay shut even at that price. The cash flow question is open until about November 2026. Six of the last seven years had negative operating cash flow, and the rating agency expects more of the same. The promoter file carries an unreconciled doubling of CEO pay inside one audited report and a subsidiary write-off with no stated reason. Both cap the verdict.

So the answer is Avoid. Not because the business is bad. Because the price already assumes the business becomes something the evidence does not yet prove, and it assumes a multiple the sector does not support. Watch the margin, the cash, and the M&A tripwire. Re-open the file only if the price comes to the zone and no red flag has fired first.
