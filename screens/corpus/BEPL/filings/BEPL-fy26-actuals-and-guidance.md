## 2. THE FINDING THAT CHANGES THE CYCLE READ
**FY26 revenue FELL 8.7%. Q1FY27 revenue ROSE 53.3%.**
Quarterly path: Q1FY26 Rs 307.9 crore, Q4FY26 Rs 341.6 crore, Q1FY27 Rs 472.2 crore. The
June 2026 quarter is 38% above the March 2026 quarter sequentially, on installed capacity
unchanged since 2015.
That is a spread, not a business. Annualising Q1FY27 annualises the spread. Any forward
multiple built on it is a peak-cycle multiple, and Amendment 17 binds.
Note also that FY26 EBITDA margin of 20.0% is ABOVE the Q1FY27 margin of 19.2%, so the
quarter is a revenue event, not a margin event.
