What makes this LOOK cheap: a 67.5% ROCE, a "30% below fair value" SOTP, a scarce VP-latex/PVAc franchise, and a clean-looking 58% re-rating that already "priced out" the gross fertilizer-stub mispricing (B14 narrative). The structural deterioration hiding behind good numbers:

If the high-ROCE core is a low-growth, share-losing commodity-adjacent business and only the small adhesives sliver compounds, the blended franchise is far less of a compounder than 67.5%-ROCE headline implies. This is the value-trap shape: a slow, high-return core dressed up by a fast, small, low-base adhesives story chasing Fevicol from 80,000 MTPA vs 600,000+ (B14 §3C).

Verdict: **partial value-trap risk is real.** Not a classic melting ice cube — the scarcity is genuine — but the "cheap + high-quality" surface rests on segment optics and one quarter.

## 5. VALUATION SAFETY — THE HURDLE-RATIO AND ROCE REBUILD (the core attack)

### 5A. The forward-PE denominator manufactures the CONDITIONAL pass

The reported Hurdle Ratio (B11; 11-valuation §2.5):
- HR(Base) = (1.13)³ × (35 ÷ 27) = 1.443 × 1.296 = **1.87** → below 1.953.
- HR(Bull) = (1.18)³ × (35 ÷ 27) = 1.643 × 1.296 = **2.13** → passes. **This single cell is the entire CONDITIONAL verdict.**

The "27x current PE" uses FORWARD FY27 PAT Rs 125 Cr as the denominator (imputed price ~Rs 2,224 ÷ FY27 EPS ~83, 11-valuation §2.5). But FY27 is the strong, Samlaya-driven, un-guided year (EPS jumps 67→83, +24%). Folding that jump into the "current" base removes it from the hurdle test.

**Re-anchor to ACTUAL FY26 polymer earnings** (~Rs 102 Cr → EPS ~67, imputed ~Rs 2,224 ÷ 67 = **~33.2x**):
- HR(Base, 13%) = (1.13)³ × (35 ÷ 33.2) = 1.443 × 1.054 = **1.52** → **STOP**.
- HR(Bull, 18%) = (1.18)³ × (35 ÷ 33.2) = 1.643 × 1.054 = **1.73** → **STOP**.

On honest current earnings, the destination multiple (35x) barely exceeds the multiple you pay TODAY (~33x). Multiple expansion = 35/33.2 = 1.05x, essentially nil. The ENTIRE return is EPS delivery — and EPS delivery depends on the shared catalyst. The CONDITIONAL pass exists only because "current PE" was set to a forward number.

### 5B. No-acceleration / no-margin-recovery bear EPS rebuild

Bear case (rev +9%, margin flat ~14%, FY27 EPS ~74, CAGR 9%, 11-valuation §2.3), even on the FAVOURABLE forward 27x denominator:
- HR(bear) = (1.09)³ × (35 ÷ 27) = 1.295 × 1.296 = **1.68** → **STOP**.
- On the actual-earnings 33x anchor: (1.09)³ × (35 ÷ 33.2) = 1.295 × 1.054 = **1.37** → **deep STOP**.

**The CONDITIONAL pass survives in exactly ONE of six computed cells** — forward denominator AND bull (18%) EPS. Change either input to something more honest (actual current earnings, or no-acceleration EPS) and it is STOP. Per framework Section 1B, HR(Bull) < 1.953 → STOP → per Role 2 decision rules that maps to AVOID, not WATCHLIST. The thesis is one favourable assumption away from AVOID.

## 7. PRE-MORTEM (2 years out, stock down 40%)

It is August 2028. The stock is ~Rs 1,405, down ~40% from a Rs 2,342 entry. What went wrong: Samlaya's full ramp slipped through FY27 into early FY28 — the partial start of June 2026 never converted to full run-rate on the guided ~Dec-2026 window (B14 break #1). FY27 PP&C revenue grew ~9%, not the 13-14% base, landing near the audited historical 7.48-8.75% rate (thesis §4); segment EBIT margin drifted back below 14% for two consecutive quarters as sulphur/VAM/butadiene costs bit (B14 risk #3; B06 confirms the sector-wide input spike, sulphur ~Rs27,500→~Rs1,00,000/tonne). FY27 polymer PAT came in ~Rs 112 Cr (bear), not Rs 125 Cr.

Then the first standalone Jubilant Industries accounts landed and did two kinds of damage. The whole-company ROCE printed in the low-30s, not 67.5% — the segment number was always an artefact — and, worse, retained CFO/PAT came in at 0.62x, below the 0.70x break line, because a chunk of working-capital drag the deck had assigned to agri actually sat with the sulphuric-acid and industrial receivables that stayed in polymer (B14 break #2; the cash-location hypothesis falsified). The Section 1B destination re-rated from 35x toward the high-20s on the lower ROCE and the 0.80x cash multiplier (Section 5C), and the market, which had already paid ~33x, compressed the multiple toward 20x on the earnings miss (11-valuation §5 downside path). Multiple compression, not the earnings miss alone, did most of the damage.

The governance overlay made it worse: in the split, debt and the cleaner receivables were allocated in a way that favoured the listed-remainco optics but left minority holders unable to challenge it, because the Finance Committee that approved the allocation had no independent directors (B08). The 40% loss was ~half earnings miss and ~half de-rating, and every piece of it traced to the same root the thesis had flagged and I had chosen to wait through rather than avoid.
