## March 2026

**1. Securens becomes 100% subsidiary (Vision AI).** 9-Mar-2026 announcement.
CMS acquired an additional 25,639 CCPS at Rs158.41 each in Securens Systems; holding
to 100% fully-diluted; Securens a wholly owned subsidiary. Securens = AIoT remote-
monitoring in BFSI/retail, audited FY25 turnover Rs76.6 Cr. Consolidates HAWKAI Vision
AI; combined BFSI Vision AI share ~36%, "2X of closest competitor".

**2. FSS ATM Managed Services acquisition (~8,000 ATMs).** 28-Mar board approval,
29-Mar media release. Buys all operating assets of FSS's ATM Management business +
novation of customer contracts. Consideration up to Rs115 Cr cash. Managed-services
base ~31,000 -> ~39,000 units (separate from ~68,000 ATMs served by currency logistics).
Brings new private-bank relationships for cross-sell.

## May 2026 (FY26 results + Q4 FY26 inflection) — 14-May media release, 15-May concall

- Q4 FY26 services revenue Rs609 Cr, +6% QoQ, first quarter above Rs600 Cr after 8
  quarters in Rs535-575 Cr band. EBITDA margin 25.6%, +280 bps QoQ (from 22.8% Q3).
  Management: Q3 was the bottom, Q4 the "decisive inflection".
- FY26 headwinds: tepid H1 consumption; delay of the Rs1,000 Cr SBI outsourcing contract
  after capacity was pre-invested; large competitor exit (AGS) caused short-term ATM
  shutdowns.
- Actions: AI/ML route optimisation (~10% route reduction Sep25-Mar26); shift from
  transaction-linked to fixed-fee pricing (walked away from a Rs700 Cr PSU ATM contract at
  record Rs18.95 transaction fee); rationalised low-yield retail; Rs40 Cr tech invest in
  FY26 (~1.6% of revenue); ~Rs15 Cr gig model (2,500 gig partners, ~20% of retail touchpoints).
- Platform: Cash Logistics share +~200 bps (~1,400 CIT, 4,000+ optimised routes).
  Managed Services position #5 -> #3 post-AGS + FSS. ATM Mgmt services rev ~Rs1,300 Cr FY25,
  guided ~11% CAGR to FY30.
- Tech & Payments (T&PS): ~Rs370 Cr FY26, 25%+ 5-yr CAGR, doubled in 3 yrs. HAWKAI ~Rs200 Cr,
  50,000+ sites, BFSI share 36%+, 15 non-BFSI sectors. ALGO MVS powering ~68,000 SBI ATMs
  (~40% share); ICICI 10,000+ ATMs live FY27.
- Customer mix de-risking: FY25 24% SBI / 26% Private+Retail / 34% MSPs / 16% other PSBs;
  FY26 20% / 30% / 28% / 22%. "Materially more diversified than the one we listed in 2021".
- Order book: FY26 cumulative wins Rs2,000 Cr+; managed-services order book ~Rs1,400 Cr at
  31-Dec-2025. SBI 10-yr Rs1,000 Cr (~5,000 ATMs, executed Q4, ~Rs500 Cr incremental).
  ICICI Rs400+ Cr TCV, 1,500 CRMs over 7 yrs + ALGO MVS 10,000+ ATMs. ~85% of FY27 services
  revenue already contracted.
- **May-2026 guidance:** FY27 total revenue Rs2,800-2,900 Cr (13-17%); services Rs2,700-2,800 Cr
  (17-21%); EBITDA margin 25%+. FY30 aspiration: services Rs3,750-3,950 Cr (13-14% CAGR FY26-30);
  mix 20-22% T&PS / 26-28% Retail & Currency Logistics / 50-52% ATM Management.

**HDFC Bank 5-yr mandate.** 10-May media release. 5-yr integrated ATM managed-services,
Rs400 Cr, 6,000 ATMs incl. currency forecasting/logistics + HAWKAI. Private-bank mix 22%
(FY25) -> 25%, aiming 30% by FY27. "Landmark wins at three of India's largest banks" (SBI,
ICICI, HDFC).

**Buyback.** 14-May board + 25-May Letter of Offer. ~49.39 lakh shares (~3% equity) at Rs340,
~Rs168 Cr. No debt funding; net-cash. Since IPO: ~Rs2,275 Cr operating cash, Rs1,000 Cr capex,
Rs190 Cr acquisitions (HAWKAI, Securens, FSS), Rs438 Cr dividends.

## Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30-Jun-2026) — 10-Aug media release, 11-Aug concall

- Total revenue Rs635 Cr (flat QoQ, +1.2% YoY). Services Rs625 Cr (highest ever, +9.3% YoY,
  +2.6% QoQ). EBITDA Rs173 Cr (+8.9% YoY, +6.8% QoQ). EBITDA margin 27.2% (+190 bps YoY,
  +170 bps QoQ). PAT Rs84 Cr (+5.8% QoQ, -10.6% YoY on higher depreciation + prior-year base).
- **Currency-supply shock:** "sharpest currency-supply disruption in a decade". Banks supplied
  only ~70% of currency indented. Hit transaction-linked BLA ATM revenue (~12% of revenue base).
  Quantified: ~Rs18 Cr loss in own BLA estate + ~Rs7 Cr on cash logistics for other MSPs
  (~Rs25 Cr total). Under-supplied ATMs -27% transactions; well-supplied flat YoY. Early Q2
  supply ~80-85%; normalisation expected end-Q2; will prune structurally weak BLA ATMs.
- Cost inflation: min wage +6% to +60% in key states, fuel +8% over 6 months. Repricing:
  private/retail increases under negotiation (target close by Q2); PSU via IBA committee.
- Segments: Cash Logistics rev Rs403 Cr (-3% YoY, +1% QoQ), EBIT Rs81 Cr (-18% YoY), margin
  20.3% (vs 23.9% Q1FY26). Managed Services & Tech (incl Card) rev Rs305 Cr (+18% YoY, +4% QoQ),
  EBIT Rs32 Cr (-13% YoY, -24% QoQ), margin 10.3% (flow-through of Rs18 Cr BLA hit + higher
  depreciation from FY26 capex + FSS).
- Platform mix (services rev): FY25 59/29/12; FY26 58/26/16; Q1FY27 57/25/18 (ATM Mgmt /
  Retail & Currency Logistics / Tech & Payments). T&PS expected to cross 20% by Q4.
- Q1 new wins ~Rs500 Cr: HDFC 6,000-ATM mandate live; two PSU recycler mandates ~1,000 ATMs.
  T&PS: HAWKAI Enterprise RMS at a leading PSU bank; ALGO MVS win at another large bank;
  ALGO MVS live across ICICI 10,000+ ATMs FY27.
- Capex: FY26 Rs352 Cr (predominantly HAWKAI + fixed-price managed services). FY27 guided
  DOWN to Rs100-125 Cr (vs ~Rs200 Cr long-run avg) -> lower incremental depreciation from FY28;
  keeps ~Rs400 Cr cash, returns genuine surplus via dividend/buyback.
- **Aug-2026 revised guidance:** services Rs2,650-2,750 Cr (was Rs2,700-2,800); total
  Rs2,750-2,850 Cr (was Rs2,800-2,900); EBITDA margin RAISED to ~27% (was 25-26%); capex
  Rs100-125 Cr. Platform growth: ATM Mgmt + Retail/Currency ~11-14% each; T&PS ~35-40%; net
  services growth ~15-19% FY27.

**Board.** Will Poole (Managing Partner/Co-founder Capria Ventures, ex-Microsoft CVP) added
to Board to strengthen AI-platform / Vision AI oversight.
