 On a standalone basis, in Q2 FY27, revenue stood at nearly INR658 crores, up by 18% year-
on-year basis. Net profit for the quarter increased by 6% to INR45 crores on a year-on-year 
basis, while our EBITDA remained flat at INR70.9 crores. Our EBITDA margin for the 
quarter was impacted by elevated input and logistics costs.  
 If we talk about the revenue mix in Q2 FY27, the Crop Protection segment constitutes about 
75% of the total revenue, while the balance 25% was from the Pigment segment. 
 Now let us look at the segment-wise performance. In the Crop Protection segment, production 
stood at nearly 10,000 metric tons, and the capacity utilization for the segment stood at 72%. 
Revenue and EBITDA stood at nearly INR496 crores and INR54 crores, respectively. 
EBITDA margin for the segment was at about 11%.  
 For the Pigment segment production stood at 4,252 metric tons, and capacity utilization for 
the segment stood at 51%. The segment reported revenue and EBITDA of INR161 crores and 
INR16 crores, respectively. EBITDA margin for the segment was at about 10%.  
 For the half year ended 30th September 2026, revenue from operations stood at INR1,181 
crores, which is up by 3% year-on-year basis. Net profit increased by 23% to approximately 
INR103 crores, and EBITDA grew by 9% to INR164 crores. 
 Moving to our Crop Nutrition segment.  We have commenced the production of our newly 
introduced Nano fertilizer products like Nano DAP, Nano NPK, and Nano Zinc at our existing 
manufacturing facility in Sanand. These products complement our existing product portfolio 
and position us well to participate in the growing adoption of next -generation crop nutrition 
solutions. We remain optimistic about the long-term growth prospects of this business. 
 In titanium dioxide, operations continue to remain suspended due to commercial unviability 
arising from the elevated raw material costs and weaker price realization following the 
withdrawal of antidumping duty.  
 Recently, on 24th October 2026, the Ministry of Finance re -imposed antidumping duty on 
titanium dioxide imports from China for a period of 5 years.  While this is a positive 
development and should support a gradual improvement in the price realization, the 
economics of restarting the plant remain challenging. Sulfuric acid, a key raw material, is 
currently at a significantly elevated level, with prices having increased by nearly 7 to 8x from 
the normal levels. 
 Considering these factors, restarting operations is not commercially viable.
