# Magnet maker funds two unproven growth bets

Permanent Magnets Ltd makes magnets, electricity meter components, automotive sensing parts, current transformers, gas meters and specialty alloys. It builds to spec for OEMs in India and abroad, and exports about 47% of what it sells. FY26 standalone revenue was Rs 225 Cr, up 13%, and standalone PAT was Rs 20.7 Cr, up 36%. The shares trade at Rs 882 for a market cap of Rs 758 Cr, about 37 times standalone FY26 earnings and about 51 times consolidated earnings. The 52 week range is Rs 619 to Rs 1,230.

Three bets are meant to change the story. The alloys division commercialised its furnace in Q4 FY26, and alloys grew from about 1% of sales in FY24 to about 10% in FY26 for aerospace, oil and gas, and industrial buyers. A latching relay is guided for H2 FY27, government approved on 30 March 2026, still pre commercial. A neodymium magnet line sits inside the subsidiary Quantum Magnetics, government approved on 18 August 2026, pre revenue, with Phase 2 targeted for Q3 FY27 and 5,000 tonnes by FY31.

The backward numbers are average, not strong. Gate 0 scored the business 57 of 160, an AVERAGE classification. Consolidated ROCE is 12.54%, below a cost of capital near 13.5%. Over the five year window PAT compounded 2.5% while revenue compounded 14.9%, so profit decoupled from sales. Consolidated cash flow from operations fell 55% to Rs 17.6 Cr while consolidated PAT fell only 6%, and free cash flow swung to minus Rs 25.8 Cr. Standalone receivables rose 39.4% against 13% revenue growth.

The subsidiary is where the cash and leverage went. Quantum Magnetics revenue collapsed 85.5% to Rs 0.8 Cr in the same year it drew a Rs 47.8 Cr unsecured foreign currency loan, posted a Rs 5.6 Cr loss, and ran negative net worth. Consolidated debt to equity rose from 0.17 times to 0.56 times, up 238%. The standalone parent stayed free cash flow positive both years. The cash flag reads GROWTH INDUCED, not structural, because the drain traces to the subsidiary capex build rather than a deteriorating core.

Management says more than it has proven, so credibility grades C. The alloys ramp was delivered and peers corroborate the demand. The relay timeline slipped once and was self disclosed. The subsidiary assemblies revenue was missed and blamed on China rare earth export controls, which is at least corroborated in the same report. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 19% but profit fell 13% to 38% with no explanation on file, since there is no concall and the results carried no commentary. Governance grades CAUTION: a same sector private family entity is not disclosed as a related party, half the board is promoter family, and MD pay rose 28% against a 13.7% employee average, offset by a credible new independent Chairman from the JSW group and zero pledged shares.

Peers confirm the direction and contradict nothing. MIDHANI corroborates strengthening aerospace and industrial alloys demand. SALZERELEC corroborates the metering runway, 65 million of 250 million RDSS meters installed, and frames qualification friction as an industry wide reality. TDPOWERSYS is a weak match for the rare earth claims because it carries no neodymium in its raw material basket. All three peers are running debt and working capital funded capex against separate demand stories, so PML's receivables build is not unique to PML.

The market runway is real but narrower than the headline. TAM is Rs 31,000 Cr to Rs 49,000 Cr, the serviceable slice is Rs 5,574 Cr, and the three year obtainable market is Rs 328 Cr, about 29 times current revenue. Runway grades STRONG rather than MASSIVE because most of the automotive current sensing TAM sits at the semiconductor layer PML does not occupy, and because the relay and neodymium lines still carry execution risk.

Valuation says AVOID at Rs 882. The sum of the parts base fair value is Rs 330 on the governing conservative track and Rs 404 on the additive track, with option slices for relay and neodymium included. The core exit multiple is 14.1 times on the conservative track and 17.3 times on the additive track, with the growth premium gated to zero because return on capital sits below the cost of capital. Forward core PE at the current price is 38.5 times against those 14 to 17 times destinations. The hurdle test stops, the probability weighted three year return is minus 14.5%, and the entry zone is Rs 118 to Rs 169 with a margin of safety price of Rs 118.

One tension in that base is worth carrying to the next refresh. The core multiple used consolidated ROCE of 12.54%, but the sum of the parts already quarantines the loss making subsidiary into its own slice with its debt in the bridge, so the subsidiary is penalised twice. Strip it out and core operating ROCE is about 16%, above the cost of capital, which would lift the point estimate about 35% to roughly Rs 450 to Rs 614 and move the 25% return entry to about Rs 230 to Rs 314. The decision holds either way: at the corrected Rs 614 ceiling the current price is still 30% to 44% above fair value, the hurdle still stops even at the sector cap, and clearing a 25% return needs about 44% EPS growth a year against 12% to 17% the evidence supports.
