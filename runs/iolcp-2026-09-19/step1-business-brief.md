1. Two segments, both backward-integrated converters in Barnala, Punjab. Pharma
   (APIs: ibuprofen and its salts, paracetamol, metformin, clopidogrel,
   pantoprazole, fenofibrate, levetiracetam) and Chemicals (acetyls: ethyl acetate,
   acetic anhydride, triacetin). Q4 FY26 segment revenue: pharma Rs 322.7 Cr vs
   Rs 261.4 Cr; chemicals Rs 287.9 Cr vs Rs 250.7 Cr.
   https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/iol-chemicals-pharmaceuticals-reports-17-revenue-growth-to-6195-crore
2. Ibuprofen: 12,000 MTPA capacity at 90-95% utilisation; claimed ~35% global share
   (screener "About"). China ibuprofen averaged USD 9.9/kg in Q2 2026 (chemanalyst),
   so IOL competes on a cost curve, not on price.
   https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/ibuprofen-1331
3. Five-year stall, then a break: revenue Rs 2,184 Cr (FY22) to Rs 2,079 Cr (FY25), OPM
   10-12%, PAT Rs 168 Cr to Rs 101 Cr. FY26 revenue Rs 2,319 Cr (+11.5%), EBITDA
   Rs 290 Cr (+29%), PAT Rs 138 Cr (+36%). (screener P&L;
   https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/iolcp-revenue-rises-115-yoy-to-rs-23191-crore-in-fy26-20287)
4. Latest print Q1 FY27 (call 13-Aug-2026): revenue Rs 756 Cr (+37%), EBITDA Rs 111 Cr
   (14.6% margin vs 12.4%), PAT Rs 64.5 Cr (+90%). Exports 28.5% of revenue.
   https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9039784/
5. THE ENGINE: mix shift away from ibuprofen. Non-ibuprofen share of pharma revenue
   18% (FY21), 37% (FY26), 43% (Q1 FY27); non-ibuprofen revenue +67% YoY; target 50:50.
   Paracetamol (10,800 MTPA) runs at 55% utilisation, the swing asset.
   https://insights.dsij.in/dsijarticledetail/iol-chemicals-plans-rs-500-crore-expansion-to-deepen-ibuprofen-lead-and-enter-cdmo-formulations-59587
6. Guidance FY27: revenue +15-20%, EBITDA margin 14-15%, exports 25-30%; FY28 EBITDA
   margin 15-17%. Management did not raise guidance after Q1. (gurufocus, above)
7. Capex: Rs 500 Cr programme (Rs 150 Cr spent): ibuprofen +6,000 MTPA to 18,000 MTPA
   by Dec 2027; EU-GMP formulations CDMO, 1,500 mn tablets/yr, commercial Q3 FY27;
   specialty chemical unit under long-term tolling with an unnamed global chemical
   company. Separate Rs 1,200-1,400 Cr greenfield (101 acres), Rs 200-250 Cr/yr over
   4-5 years. All stated as internally funded. Management says peak revenue at 100%
   utilisation of current assets is Rs 3,200-3,300 Cr. (dsij above;
   https://tradebrains.in/pharma-stock-surges-170-in-4-months-as-fiis-increase-stake-company-unveils-1400-cr-capex/)
8. Cash: CFO Rs 214 Cr, FCF Rs 43 Cr (FY26); FCF negative in 3 of 5 years FY22-FY26;
   debtor days 79 to 95 over FY22-FY26 (screener cash flow and ratios).
9. Ownership: promoter 48.19% (Sep 2024) to 62.28% (Jun 2026), share capital flat. The

   https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/bankingfinance/IOL-Chemicals-Promoter-Group-Restructures-Shareholding-Stake-Rises-to-2188percent/6a4219497465030c38bbd017

## PEERS (economic-engine match, screener URLs verified HTTP 200)
- AARTIDRUGS (Aarti Drugs, /consolidated/): volume generic API maker (metformin,
  ibuprofen, NSAIDs) with a specialty-chemicals arm; closest mirror of both segments
  and of the ibuprofen cost-curve fight.
- GRANULES (Granules India, /consolidated/): large-volume paracetamol, metformin and
  ibuprofen API maker that moved forward into formulations; the template for IOL's
  API-to-CDMO-formulations step and its paracetamol swing asset. Larger (Rs 23,882 Cr).
- LXCHEM (Laxmi Organic Industries, /consolidated/): India's large ethyl acetate and
  acetyl-intermediates maker; mirrors the chemicals segment's spread economics.
No listed pure-play ibuprofen maker other than IOL; Solara Active Pharma makes
ibuprofen but is loss-making and restructuring, so it was not taken.
