### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

No miss is ever explicitly acknowledged anywhere in this corpus — there is no sentence
in any AR or deck that says "we missed X" or "we fell short of Y." Headwinds that are
named (rare-earth/NdFeB magnet supply, US tariffs, Middle East-driven commodity inflation,
China export licensing) are consistently attributed to external, verifiable geopolitical
and macro events, and the company consistently credits itself for the mitigations (VAVE,
alternate sourcing, capacity expansion, product-mix shift). This is not unusual for
prepared promotional material and the named headwinds are real, industry-wide events, not
fabricated excuses for a specific miss. The pattern that stands out is different: the two
most decision-relevant claims this stage tested (customer-concentration reduction,
working-capital-days improvement) are not explained away with an excuse at all — they are
simply asserted, unchanged, against contradicting evidence sitting in the company's own
other documents. Classification: **external-blame-heavy** for genuine headwinds, but the
more serious pattern is silent, unacknowledged contradiction rather than deflection under
pressure — because there is no pressure in this corpus to deflect. Nowhere does management
proactively raise the CFO/PAT decline, the receivables-ageing trend, or the WC-days
discrepancy; every one of these was surfaced by upstream stages (B01/B02/B03) reading the
Notes and financial statements, not by management's own narrative sections.

### 2C Tone ratings (1-5), evidence-based, CAVEAT: assessed on prepared text only, no
cross-examination possible

- **Transparency: 2/5.** CFO/PAT, receivables ageing, customer-concentration percentages,
  Lucas TVS ownership %, capacity utilisation %, and product-category revenue mix never
  appear in any deck or the AR narrative (all flagged NOT FOUND at B01-B03/B07). The one
  cash-adjacent metric management does volunteer (WC days) is internally contradicted.
- **Specificity: 2/5.** "Market share gains," "new business secured," and "SoP commenced"
  are asserted every quarter with no accompanying percentage, Rs value, unit count, or
  customer name in the overwhelming majority of instances (the ~2 million FWM units figure
  and the Generac USA award are the rare exceptions).
- **Consistency: 2/5.** The BorgWarner EFI-ECU slide is repeated verbatim for a full year
  while the AR reports a pivot away from it; the WC-days narrative in the AR letter
  contradicts the company's own deck chart.
- **Accountability: 3/5.** Real, verifiable external headwinds are named rather than hidden,
  and AR2026's own Note 51 proactively flags that the exceptional land-compensation item
  inflates ROE/ROCE/Net Profit (a genuine self-disclosed caveat, per B02 finding 7). This one
  item pulls the accountability rating above the others.
- **Defensiveness / over-promotion: not independently ratable.** No analyst pressure exists
  in this corpus to test defensiveness against. The material reads as standard, professionally
  produced (Valorem Advisors) promotional IR content: consistently upbeat framing, headwinds
  named but softened ("effectively managed," "alternate sourcing initiatives under active
  development").
