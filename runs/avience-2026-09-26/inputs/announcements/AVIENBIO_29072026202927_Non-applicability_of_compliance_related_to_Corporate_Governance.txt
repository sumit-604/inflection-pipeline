Sub.: Non-applicability of the Corporate Governance provisions

As per Regulation 15 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the compliance with the Corporate
Governance provisions specified under Regulations 17, 17A, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 24A,
25, 26, 26A, 27 and Clauses (b) to (i) and (t) of sub-regulation (2) of Regulation 46 and
Paragraphs C, D and E of Schedule V shall not apply to the following class of companies:


(a) Listed companies having paid-up equity share capital not exceeding ₹10 Crores and net
worth not exceeding ₹25 Crores as on the last day of the previous financial year;

(b) Companies whose equity share capital is listed exclusively on the SME Exchange, except
Regulation 23, which shall be applicable to such companies meeting the criteria specified
under the proviso to Regulation 15(2)(b).

We hereby certify that the compliance with the Corporate Governance provisions specified in
the aforesaid Regulations, except Regulation 23, is not applicable to the Company for the
quarter ended June 30, 2026, as the Company falls within the  criteria specified under
Regulation 15(2)(b) read with the proviso thereto.
