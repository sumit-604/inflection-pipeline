The following explanatory statement sets out material facts 
relating to the special business set out in the accompanying 
Notice of Extra Ordinary General Meeting:
Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi was appointed as an Independent 
Non-Executive Director of the Company by the Members at the 
Extra Ordinary General Meeting held on February 29, 2024, for a 
period of three (3) consecutive years, commencing from March 30, 
2024 up to March 29, 2027. At the time of his appointment, he had 
not attained the age of seventy-five (75) years.
Pursuant to Regulation 17(1A) of the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, the appointment or continuation of directorship 
of a person as a Non-Executive Director who has attained the age of 
seventy-five (75) years requires the approval of Members by way of a 
Special Resolution.
Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi will attain the age of seventy-five (75) 
years on February 3, 2026. Accordingly, approval of the Members is 
required for the continuation of his directorship with effect from 
February 3, 2026, i.e., the date on which he attains the age of 75 years, 
till the expiry of his current term on March 29, 2027 which was earlier 
approved by the Members at the EGM held on February 29, 2024.
Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi joined the Indian Police Service 
(IPS) in the year 1976, being allotted to the Maharashtra cadre, 
where he served in various capacities across the State. He retired as 
the Director General of Police, Maharashtra State, in the year 2011. 
He is presently serving on the Boards of various companies.
Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi possesses extensive experience and 
expertise in public policy and general administration, business and 
corporate governance, business development, and security including 
IT domain expertise. The Board is of the opinion that his continued 
association would be of significant benefit to the Company.
Further, Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi continues to meet the 
criteria of independence as prescribed under Section 149(6) of the 
Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 16(1)(b) of the SEBI LODR 
Regulations, 2015.
Accordingly, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and 
Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors recommends the 
passing of the Special Resolution as set out in the Notice for approval 
of the Members.
Except Mr. Sivanandhan Dhanushkodi and his relatives, none of 
the other Directors, Key Managerial Personnel of the Company, or 
their respective relatives are concerned or interested, financially or 
otherwise, in the Resolution.
