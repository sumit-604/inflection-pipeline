Sub: Announcement on vetting of Geological Report by Accredited Agency and submission to Nominated 
Authority, MOC, Government of India - A Significant Milestone in the Development of Jog eshwar 
Jogeshwar Khas Coal Mine in Jharkhand  
Dear Sir, 
This is to inform you that in connection with development of Coal Mine, the Company after completion of 
exploration related activities , has prepared and submitted the Geological report  (GR) with an NABET 
accredited agency for its peer review in line with the SOP issued by Nominated Authority the Ministry of Coal, 
Government of India (NA-MOC) dated 21st January 2026.  
The Accredited agency has, accordingly, reviewed the GR and confirmed it vide their letter dated 06/09/2026. 
The same has now been submitted with NA -MOC on their portal. A hard copy of the GR shall also be 
submitted to CMPDIL for record maintenance and updating of coal resources as per the aforesaid SOP of NA-
MOC. 
The Vetting and submission of the Geological Report marks a significant milestone in the Company’s journey 
towards development of the coal mine. It follows extensive exploration, geological investigation and 
technical evaluation undertaken by the Company and represents an important culmination of the exploration 
phase. 
The Geological Report vetting and submission to NA-MOC provides the technical and geological basis for the 
further evaluation and progression of the coal mine. The company is accordingly, preparing the mining plan 
and expect to submit the same with NA-MOC in due course for their approval.  
This achievement is an important step towards taking the coal Mine to the next stage of development and 
eventual production, subject to the requisite statutory approvals, clearances and other applicable processes. 
The Company believes that the successful vetting and submission of the GR strengthen the long -term 
potential of the coal asset and commitment to developing mineral assets in a responsible and sustainable 
manner.
