Commercial Highlights

                                                   Expanded AI-led omnichannel capabilities through the acquisition of Heltar Technologies' AI-driven customer engagement
                                                   business, completed in July 2026. The acquisition strengthens Route Mobile's non-SMS portfolio across WhatsApp, RCS, voice and
                                                   communication analytics, while adding AI-native, no-code customer journey orchestration capabilities across India and
                                                   international markets.
                                                   Expanded Route Mobile's global CPaaS footprint and leadership across Latin America with the appointment of Marko Erkic as
                                                   CEO of Masiv, Route Mobile's Colombian CPaaS subsidiary, supporting the continued growth of the business across LATAM and
                                                   the broader Proximus Global ecosystem.
                                                   Strengthened BFSI engagement and strategic ecosystem partnerships at Global FinTech Fest (GFF) 2026, where Route Mobile
                                                   participated as Cloud Communications Partner and Exhibitor. The company showcased its omnichannel messaging, intelligent
                                                   routing and digital engagement capabilities, while engaging with financial-services leaders and technology partners, on
                                                   WhatsApp Business, conversational engagement, RCS and the evolution of digital customer journeys across onboarding,
                                                   servicing, payments and support.
                                                   Expanded enterprise omnichannel capabilities through the launch of a Salesforce-native messaging app. Launched a new
                                                   messaging application on Salesforce AgentExchange in August 2026, enabling enterprises to send SMS and WhatsApp messages
                                                   directly from Salesforce. The solution supports zero-code deployment, workflow automation and engagement analytics, with
                                                   customer data remaining within the organization's Salesforce environment.
                                                   Proximus Global won the Carrier Community Global Award 2026 for Best Application Service Provider, recognizing its
                                                   capabilities across the communications ecosystem.
