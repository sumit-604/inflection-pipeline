               PRESS RELEASE | Q2 and H1 FY27 Financial and Operational Performance

                Tinna Rubber Posts Record H1FY27 Profitability; EBITDA Grows 50% YoY

October 22, 2026, New Delhi: Tinna Rubber and Infrastructure Limited (TRIL), among the largest end-of-life tyre recyclers
globally, announced results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026.

H1 FY27 (YoY)                            Q2 FY27(YoY)

Revenue  313 Cr                     25%  Revenue  157 Cr   31%
                                    50%                    37%
EBITDA   63 Cr                      60%  EBITDA   29 Cr    45%

PAT      38 Cr                           PAT      17 Cr

Numbers are on consolidated basis

Financial Highlights

 Achieved record H1 profitability on both standalone and consolidated bases, with standalone EBITDA exceeding
     Rs. 61 crore and PAT surpassing Rs. 36 crore, with strong EBITDA and PAT margins of above 20% and 12%,
     respectively.

 Consolidated Q2FY27 revenue, EBITDA and PAT grew 31%, 37% and 45% YoY, with margins above 18% and 10%,
     respectively.

 H1FY27 segment performance remained strong, led by 38% YoY growth in Infrastructure, 19% in Industrial, 11% in
     Consumer and 8% in Steel, driven by infrastructure demand.

 Balance sheet strength improved in H1FY27, with debt declining 9% to Rs. 111 Cr. from Rs. 121 Cr. in FY26. The
     interest coverage ratio increased to 9.91x (H1FY27) from 7.49x (FY26), and net debt-to-equity ratio improved to
     0.32x IN H1FY27.

Operational & Strategic Highlights

 CARE Ratings upgraded the Tinna's credit ratings to CARE BBB; Stable for long-term facilities and CARE A3+ for short-
     term facilities.

 The ongoing 3,500 MTPA MRP capacity expansion is progressing as planned and will be commissioned by Q3 FY27.
     This will increase MRP capacity to 20,000 MTPA, positioning the Company to become the world's largest producer
     of MRP.

 Polymer Composites & Masterbatch (PCMB) Update: PCMB revenue tripled to Rs. 27 Cr in H1FY27 from Rs. 8 Cr in
     H1FY26. The total capacity has expanded to 18,000 TPA.

 Capex: Completed Rs. 45 Cr. of capex in H1 FY27 against the planned Rs. 100 Cr. investment over FY27 - FY28
 Integrated Facility at Andhra: Plans to invest up to Rs. 250 crore over the next three years to develop a 40-acre

     integrated recycling facility in Andhra Pradesh, with capacities across rubber and plastic recycling, pyrolysis and
     recovered Carbon Black (rCB). This facility is envisaged to be one of the largest integrated rubber and plastic recycling
     plants globally.

 Renewable energy accounted for 40% of total power consumption in H1FY27, supported by an increase in
     renewable capacity from 1.23 MW to 4.48 MW. Solar power initiatives generated savings of Rs. 2.37 Cr.

 The Oman facility operated at 90% capacity utilization in H1FY27, with EBITDA margins improving to 11.5% from
     6.43% in FY26.

 Update on Chile: Established a wholly owned subsidiary, Tinna Rubber Chile SpA, to strengthen the Company's
     global ELT sourcing network and expand its international recycling footprint.

Management's Perspective

Tinna delivered its most profitable half-year ever in H1 FY27, powered by strong year-on-year growth in Q2, a stronger
balance sheet and a growing share of high-value products.

The road ahead is equally promising. Policy momentum is building in Tinna's favour, with proposed mandates for modified
bitumen in road construction and tighter EPR rules for waste tyres. Rising natural rubber prices and the industry's push
for sustainable materials are further accelerating demand for recycled rubber.

Tinna is building on this momentum with clear ambitions under Vision 2029: to reach 1,000 Cr in revenue, growing at a
CAGR of over 25%, while delivering profitability growth of over 33% CAGR, sustaining EBITDA margins above 18% and
earning a return on capital employed above 30%, as it expands its operations from 8 to 10 locations.

About Tinna Rubber and Infrastructure Ltd.

Tinna Rubber and Infrastructure Limited (`TRIL'), headquartered in New Delhi, is among the world's largest end-of-life
tyre recyclers. Its current 8 plants in India and Oman have 2,00,000 MT of tyre-crushing capacity, and it also operates in
South Africa. Future expansions are envisaged in Saudi Arabia and Chile. TRIL recovers about 99% of each tyre, making
crumb rubber, CRMB, micronized rubber powder, reclaimed rubber, steel abrasives and polymer compounds for
customers worldwide. These products serve infrastructure, tyre, rubber goods, sports-surface and steel customers
worldwide.
