### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Historical fixed-asset turnover (standalone, FY26):
- Revenue from operations (Note 37 total) = ₹1,67,558.66 lakh = **₹1,675.59cr**
- Net PP&E ₹67,597.62 lakh + CWIP ₹485.20 lakh = ₹68,082.82 lakh = **₹680.83cr** (AR FY26 p.152/155)
- Fixed-asset turnover = 1,675.59 / 680.83 = **2.462x**

**Scenario 1 — 📄-anchored (Note 39 contracted capex only):**
- ₹16.87cr × 2.462 = **₹41.5cr** implied incremental revenue
- ₹41.5cr / ₹1,675.59cr = **2.5%** growth embedded above current revenue

**Scenario 2 — 🎙️ narrative capex (AR MD&A "~₹450cr by 2030"):**
- ₹450cr × 2.462 = **₹1,108cr** implied incremental revenue
- ₹1,108cr / ₹1,675.59cr = **66.1%** growth embedded above current revenue, *if and when the full
  ₹450cr is actually committed and executes at the historical turnover ratio*

We report **2.5%** as the anchored `capex_embedded_growth_pct` because only Scenario 1 clears the 📄
bar. Scenario 2 is a genuine, repeatedly-stated management figure (3 separate AR pages) and is the
more economically interesting number, but crediting it as embedded growth would mean scoring a
promise, not a commitment — precisely what the promise-delivery record (B05, Grade D, Midnapore cost
overrun, FY24 Vnext growth miss) warns against. It is carried in the optionality register instead.

  confers no differential advantage; early warning sign would be a peer (e.g. BirlaNu) citing
