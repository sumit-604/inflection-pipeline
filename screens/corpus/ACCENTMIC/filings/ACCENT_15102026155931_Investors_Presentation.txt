CORE BUSINESS
Accent Microcell Ltd. makes the ingredients that turn a medicine
into a tablet.
A tablet is mostly not medicine. The active drug is usually only part of
its weight; the rest comprises inactive ingredients called excipients.
Excipients give tablets their size and shape, hold them together, help them flow
through tablet machines, and enable them to break apart in the body at the right time.
The company’s main product, Microcrystalline Cellulose (MCC), is
one of the most widely used excipients.
MCC is a fine white powder made from purified wood pulp.
MCC is also used in food supplements, food products, cosmetics and
certain industrial goods.
