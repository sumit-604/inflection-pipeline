## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue.**
- What could fire it: volumes returning after a year when customers ran down their stock, plus new customers in 11 countries (FY26 AR MD&A PDF p.80). Later, cefiderocol from Q3 FY28 and small Exblifep income from Europe and Russia.
- Evidence: one quarter of 15.6% growth (Aug-2026 call transcript p.3-4).
- Confirmation: Q2 FY27 revenue above Rs 290 cr (tracker row 2).
- What kills it: the oral price war. Cefuroxime axetil fell 9.3% in 90 days (dossier V1, [SECONDARY]). The largest rival, Covalent, is carrying debt of about 3.5 times OPBDITA and needs volume ([AGENCY], dossier V1).

**Margin.**
- What could fire it: gross margin repair now that the costly stock is gone (Jun-2026 call transcript: "we decided we need to sell at the current market prices rather than hold inventory"), cheaper raw material, and merger savings "in the next financial year" (Aug-2026 call transcript p.4).
- The one lever that changes the business: Jammu. Own 7-ACA would replace about USD 60/kg imports (Aug-2026 call p.6), but only after COD in Mar-2027, and only if the plant makes it cheaper than China. Management has given no cost per kg (extraction X2).
- What kills it: gross margin back below 31%.

**Cash.**
- No catalyst found, and management has said nothing about receivables across four calls. That silence is the loudest signal in the file.
- The inventory release already happened.
- Otsuka, a related party, gives about 100 days of supplier credit (dossier V3). Any change in those terms moves about Rs 60 to 67 cr of working capital the wrong way.

**ROCE.**
- The recovery path runs entirely through Jammu, and it lands outside the 12-month window.
- Inside the window, capital employed rises by the remaining Jammu and cefiderocol spend. CON commitments are Rs 379.02 cr (CON Note 43, PDF p.328). CON CWIP is Rs 340.52 cr (CON Note 5, PDF p.313).
- What would confirm recovery: the first commercial batch, then material cost falling below 63.5% of sales for a sustained period.

**Proof gate** (09b Section 2 Part B3, verbatim): "First commercial 7-ACA batch at disclosed yield/quality, guided ~March 2027; thereafter material cost % of revenue falling sustainably below the FY25/FY24 63.5% baseline".
- Ruling: NOT FIRED. No batch has run. Until it fires, the transition is narrative and the name is research, not a trade.
- Line 3 proof gate ("At least one further definitive licensing agreement, beyond Russia, signed and disclosed with economics, within FY27"): NOT FIRED as of 28-Sep-2026.

**Consistency with the signed model.** Every catalyst above sits on one of the four signed dominant variables: spread, Jammu, cash wall, Exblifep US partnering. None falls outside them.
