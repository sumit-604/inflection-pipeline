## OPEN ITEM 1 — CFO discrepancy: FY26 AR shows FY25 comparative CFO of -₹398.50L (-3.99 Cr); FY25 AR shows FY25 CFO of -₹1,024.17L (-10.24 Cr). RESOLVED — mechanism found.
Both years' "Cash from Operations Total-A" (before working-capital adjustment) match exactly across the two ARs: ₹2,047.05L FY25 in both the FY25 AR (Consolidated Cash Flow Statement, p.84) and the FY26 AR's FY25 comparative column (p.96). The entire divergence lives inside the working-capital adjustment block ("Total-B").
- FY25 AR (p.84): Total-B for FY25 = -₹2,482.07L, built from 7 line items (Inventory, Trade Receivable, Other Current Assets, Short Term Loans & Advances, Trade Payable, Other Current Liabilities, Short Term Provision). No "Working Capital Facilities" line exists.
- FY26 AR (p.96), FY25 comparative column: Total-B = -₹1,856.40L, built from 8 line items — the same 7, PLUS a new line "Changes in Working Capital Facilities" = +₹625.67L (FY25 comparative) / +₹945.65L (FY26).
- The arithmetic closes exactly: -2,482.07 + 625.67 = -1,856.40. The entire ₹625.67L gap in the FY25 comparative CFO between the two ARs is this one reclassified line, and no other line moved.
- No restatement, regrouping, or reclassification note exists anywhere in the FY26 AR explaining the introduction of this line (searched "restat", "regroup", "reclassif" — the only "Previous Year's Figures" hits are unrelated fixed-asset-schedule headers, not a restatement note). (Annual_Report_2026.txt, p.96; Annual_Report_2025.txt, p.84) 🔴 NEW RED FLAG.
- What "Changes in Working Capital Facilities" is: not defined anywhere, but the label and its effect (a positive add-back to operating cash) is consistent with reclassifying the year's net drawdown on cash-credit/overdraft facilities from a financing-activity movement into the operating working-capital block — the opposite of the conventional AS-3 treatment, which treats CC/OD movement as financing. This is a judgment call made only at the FY26 audited-annual stage; it is NOT present in the H1 FY26 results (revised, 14-Nov-2025), whose cash flow statement (OCR text, page 13 of that filing) uses the conventional 7-line WC block with short-term borrowings movement shown separately in the financing section, not folded into operating WC. So the reclassification decision was made specifically between the H1 FY26 filing and the FY26 annual audit, and applied retroactively to the FY25 comparative, without disclosure. 🔴 The auditor signed off on both years without a qualification on this point (Independent Auditor's Report, standard unmodified opinion on cash flow statement, both years).
- Net effect: this reclassification flatters reported operating cash generation in both years shown in the FY26 AR (FY26 CFO ₹469.07L would have been meaningfully lower, and FY25 comparative -₹398.50L is materially better than the -₹1,024.17L the FY25 AR itself reported for the same year, absent the line).
