===== PAGE 14 =====
"D.P. Swarn Plus: Structured Gold Accumulation Scheme." Context: government raised gold
and silver import duty from ~6% to 15%, to curb imports and support the rupee. Scheme
design: 10-month instalment plan (Rs 5,000+ monthly), gold locked in month by month,
mandatory redemption into jewellery (no cash payout). Customer incentive: redemption
within 301-330 days gets up to 40% discount on gold making charges, 50% on diamond
making charges.
