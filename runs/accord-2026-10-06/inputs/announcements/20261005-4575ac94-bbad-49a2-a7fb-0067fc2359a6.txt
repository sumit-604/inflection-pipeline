Accord Transformer & Switchgear Ltd
H1 FY27 Business Update
Powering Growth Across Energy and Infrastructure
Accord Transformer & Switchgear Limited manufactures transformers, switchgear panels, compact substations, 
busduct systems and EV charging infrastructure solutions. The Company serves customers across power 
transmission and distribution, renewable energy, industrial infrastructure and electric mobility through its 
manufacturing facilities in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan.
During H1 FY27, Accord continued to build its presence in renewable energy and power infrastructure. New 
transformer orders, customer approvals and work on its manufacturing expansion supported the Company’s 
plans for a broader product and customer base.

Recent Business Developments
➢ Vendor approvals: Accord received vendor approvals from Aditya Birla Renewables, UGVCL, Sterling and 
Wilson Renewable Energy Limited, Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Limited, BLUPINE and SMCC 
Construction India Pvt. Ltd.
➢ CPRI testing: The Company completed the 17.6 type test at CPRI.
➢ Orders for Aditya Birla projects: The Company received orders worth ₹37 crore for Aditya Birla projects, 
excluding GST, to supply 119 transformers with execution within four to five months from the purchase order 
date.
➢ Received orders worth approximately ₹77 crore in H1 FY27 across solar projects, wind projects, and other 
transformer segments.
[page 3]
➢ Sterling and Wilson order: Accord secured an order worth approximately ₹0.51 crore from Sterling and 
Wilson Renewable Energy Limited for auxiliary dry type and power transformers.
➢ UGVCL registration: The Company received five-year approval to manufacture and supply 500 kVA 
distribution transformers, enabling participation in eligible UGVCL tenders.
➢ Land for future expansion: Accord acquired 20,300 sq. metres of land at Khairthal-Tijara, Rajasthan, for 
potential manufacturing, warehousing and testing facilities. The land consideration was ₹8.85 crore, with 
additional expenses of ₹1.82 crore.
