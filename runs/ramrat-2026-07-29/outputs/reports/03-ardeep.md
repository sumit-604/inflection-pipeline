**DuPont decomposition (standalone, independently derived) — is ROE operational or leverage-driven?**
ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Leverage (Avg Assets/Avg Equity).
- FY26: 2.20% × 3.18x × 2.96x = 20.72% (≈20.71% disclosed — ties out)
- FY25: 1.98% × 3.29x × 2.38x = 15.53% (≈15.52% disclosed — ties out)
Net margin improved (1.98%→2.20%, +11%); asset turnover **declined slightly** (3.29x→3.18x, -3.5%, the new capacity has not yet been fully monetised); leverage rose sharply (2.38x→2.96x, +24.4%). Holding FY26 margin and turnover constant but substituting FY25's leverage yields ROE of ~16.66%, i.e. **roughly 78% of the 519bps ROE improvement is attributable to increased leverage, not operating performance** — a materially different read than the headline "RONW up to 20.71%" framing in the MD&A, which attributes the change entirely to "expansion in business operations" without decomposing the leverage contribution. **New, not in B01/B02.**
