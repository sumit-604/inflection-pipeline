**DuPont decomposition (standalone, own calculation from AR p.200-201):**
Net margin: 6.72% FY25 vs 5.68% FY24 (+1.04pp, improving)
Asset turnover: 0.996x FY25 vs 1.114x FY24 (declining — balance sheet swelled with unutilised IPO/QIP cash, dragging turnover)

3. **"Global ambition," present tense, vs zero current international revenue — hedged retreat.** *Opening claim:* "We are planting flags not as exporters of products, but as builders of a global operating system for corporate spend" and "The United States... The Middle East... will serve as a hub" (present/near-present framing, AR p.2, p.8). *Operational section:* Geographical segment note shows 100% revenue "Within India," 0% "Outside India," for both FY25 and FY24 (MD&A, AR p.145) — there is no current international operation, only a stated future intention. *Classification:* **hedged retreat** — the rhetoric implies ongoing global execution ("we are planting flags") while the operational section shows the ambition is 100% prospective with no current-year progress metric disclosed. *Materiality:* **Moderate** — does not change the domestic-thesis numbers, but shows a real gap between front-matter rhetoric and disclosed current-state execution.

**Phase 6 verdict: 🟡 Watch.** The Chairman's letter and front matter are professionally produced and directionally consistent with the underlying growth numbers, but three distinguishable quiet-abandonment patterns exist around the two acquisitions and the "global" framing — this is a distinct finding from Phase 4B's missing-risk-factor analysis (these are things that ARE discussed, but discussed in a way that omits the operational counter-evidence, not things that are simply absent from a risk list).
