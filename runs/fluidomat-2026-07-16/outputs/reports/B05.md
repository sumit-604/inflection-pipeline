### 1C. Trigger evolution / disappearances / timeline conflicts

- **Order-booking disclosure quietly disappeared.** The FY25 AR discloses a specific annual order-booking figure and its YoY change (Rs.5,815.76 lakh, -3.12%, p.26) — a genuine leading indicator. **Neither the Q3 FY26 results (14-Feb-2026) nor the FY26 annual results (30-May-2026) disclose any order-booking figure at all.** This KPI went dark exactly during the period (9M FY26) when revenue fell -15.6% and PAT fell -38.1% YoY — i.e., visibility was withdrawn precisely when it would have mattered most. Flagged as a dropped trigger.
- **Capex completion date is self-conflicting within the single source document.** The Investor Presentation's "Journey" timeline (slide 8) states the modernisation programme's "Expected completion by end 2027." The same presentation's dedicated "Capex & Expansion Plan" slide (slide 22) states "Execution Timeline: 2-3 Yrs / FY26-FY29E" — i.e., completion as late as March 2029, roughly 14+ months later than the Journey-slide claim. No reconciliation is offered anywhere in the document. This is a genuine internal inconsistency, not an interpretation issue.
- **FY25's bullish framing did not carry into FY26.** The FY25 AR frames the year as "another remarkable year," "best-ever performance," 28.33% revenue growth, 40.64% EBITDA margin (p.25-26, 35-36). FY26 delivered flat revenue (+0.4%: Rs.7,246.14 lakh vs Rs.7,218.29 lakh) and a 9.7% PAT decline (Rs.2,006.18 lakh vs Rs.2,222.35 lakh), with EBITDA margin compressing to ~35% (Investor Presentation slide 23). Management's own explanation (slide 21, Jun-2026) attributes this to "a shift in product and customer mix, alongside input cost pressures," calling it a "transitional phase" — directionally plausible but not quantified by product line, customer, or cost category.
- **New trigger (why now?):** the ₹35cr capex programme appears for the first time in Feb-2026 (per the presentation), roughly six months after the FY25 AR's only vague reference to "continuing efforts to introduce higher value products" (p.24 Explanatory Statement) and "continue to invest" (MD&A p.36). Existing capacity utilisation is cited at ~96% (slide 22) as the stated rationale — a plausible, non-manufactured reason for the timing, though it cannot be cross-checked against any disclosed capacity/utilisation time series.
