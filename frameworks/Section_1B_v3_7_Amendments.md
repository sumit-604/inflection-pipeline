## AMENDMENT 17 — COMMODITY CONVERTER CLASSIFICATION AND CYCLE ADJUSTMENTS

`[v3.7: converter classification gate, symmetric through-cycle ROCE smoothing, input-price working capital fork, cycle-aware projections, and a cycle entry gate — operator directive 20-Aug-2026]`

**17.0 Classification gate.** Every Section 1B derivation states upfront: CONVERTER or NON-CONVERTER, with one sentence of evidence. CONVERTER requires all three: (a) primary raw material is a traded/quoted commodity (resin, crude derivatives, rubber, steel, agri produce); (b) pricing is cost-plus or spread-based with limited formulation IP; (c) gross margin historically co-moves with the input price. Genuine formulation IP with demonstrated pricing power = NON-CONVERTER even on commodity inputs; the test is whether the spread is defended by IP or by nothing. Ambiguous cases classify CONVERTER (conservative default) with the ambiguity stated.

**17.1 Pillar 1 symmetric smoothing (converters only).** The ROCE feeding Pillar 1 is through-cycle: the 5-7 year average, or the midpoint of current and cycle-trough ROCE where listed history is shorter. Spot-year ROCE is NEVER the Pillar 1 input for a converter, in either direction. This extends the existing trajectory-smoothing rule (which credits temporarily depressed ROCE) to temporarily INFLATED ROCE; the depressed direction already exists and is unchanged. Show both numbers in the derivation: spot ROCE and through-cycle ROCE used.

**17.2 Pillar 2 third fork: input-price-driven working capital (converters only).** Before assigning STRUCTURAL vs GROWTH-INDUCED, run the volume test: recompute WC days on volumes or constant prices. If WC-per-unit is stable while WC-in-rupees swings, the swing is INPUT-PRICE-DRIVEN: assign 1.00x neutral multiplier, no growth offset, no structural penalty. All cash-quality trend claims for converters — including the FTTCP Cash transition verdict feed — are made on volume-denominated WC only; a rupee-denominated "improvement" caused by a falling input is not a cash transition. Rating-agency language separating price-led from cycle-led WC is admissible evidence for the fork.

**17.3 Projection discipline (converters only).** The bear case must contain at least one input-price mean-reversion year with spreads compressing to the 5-year median spread, even where guidance says otherwise. Guidance delivered at a cycle top is the cycle delivering, not management; the promise-vs-delivery tracker notes cycle position alongside each delivered guidance for converter names.

**17.4 Entry conjunction (Role 2 verdict card, converters only).** A converter BUY verdict requires the input-cycle position stated: the named input, spot price vs its 5-year range. Input in the TOP QUINTILE of its 5-year range → ceiling verdict WATCHLIST regardless of margin of safety on current earnings, with the cycle position as the named resolving condition and a review trigger when the input exits the top quintile. This is an entry gate; destination PE math is unchanged by 17.4.

**17.5 What Amendment 17 does NOT do.** Does not lower any sector cap (caps already price the sector through-cycle). Does not apply any of 17.1-17.4 to NON-CONVERTER names. Does not alter FTTCP verdict machinery beyond the denominating rule in 17.2. Does not interact with the Category-Break Override (a converter qualifying for the override would be extraordinary; if claimed, both sets of rules apply and the override's four conditions are tested on through-cycle numbers).

## INTERACTION WITH THE REST OF THE FRAMEWORK

- **Single-credit stays supreme.** 17.1 changes the ROCE INPUT to the Pillar 1 formula for converters; it does not touch the formula, the 30x elite ceiling (Amendment 11), or the v3.5.1 route selection. A converter on Route A or Route B still normalizes through EXACTLY ONE route, and the through-cycle input of 17.1 is computed after that route is applied, never as a second credit.
- **The sector cap is still absolute.** Nothing in Amendment 17 raises or lowers a cap.
- **17.2 supersedes nothing.** The STRUCTURAL and GROWTH-INDUCED forks and their multipliers are unchanged; INPUT-PRICE-DRIVEN is a third fork available to converter names only, entered via the volume test.
- **17.4 is an entry gate on the Role 2 verdict card**, not a valuation input. Destination PE, fair values, and the Hurdle Ratio are computed exactly as before.
- **FTTCP feed:** the Cash transition verdict for a converter is made on volume-denominated WC per 17.2. No other FTTCP machinery changes.
