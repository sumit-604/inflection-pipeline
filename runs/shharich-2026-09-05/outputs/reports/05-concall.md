### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every negative move in the MD&A ratio table (p.77) is attributed to an
external, unquantified cause: Operating Profit Margin decline, "Due to
increase in Raw Material Price"; Net Profit Margin decline, "Due to market
condition." Neither gives a number (how much raw material rose, which market
condition). At the same time, the one favourable move given a causal
explanation (Interest Coverage Ratio improvement) is credited to an internal
action ("Decrease in Borrowing") that the company's own Notes show did not
happen; borrowings rose. This matches the classic pattern the main
framework's 2B check looks for: blame externals for misses, take credit
(here, wrongly) for gains. There is no instance anywhere in the AR of
management saying "we made a mistake" or proactively flagging a hard topic
(the PAT decline, the margin compression, the unexplained export/FX
step-change) ahead of where a disclosure table forces it. The Chairman's
Message states the favourable revenue comparison explicitly (+31% YoY, both
figures given) but states the profit figure without its unfavourable
year-on-year comparison (-17.6%), even though that comparison appears two
paragraphs later in the Board's Report. Classification: external-blame-heavy,
with one instance of misdirected self-credit.

### 2D. What is never discussed (silences and mis-statements as data)

Genuine silences (confirmed by direct search of the extracted text, per B02/
B03 and independently re-checked in this stage):

  disclosed (270 tonnes, p.5); no production volume, utilisation percentage,

- **Export step-change explanation.** Export revenue moved from Rs 0 (FY25)
  to Rs 8,554.32 lakh (FY26, Note, p.130); no paragraph anywhere explains why,
  to which country or customer, or on what terms.
- **FX hedging policy detail.** Beyond the blanket "no hedging" denial (see
  below), there is no discussion of how a company now 46.4% export-exposed
  manages currency risk in practice.
- **Customer concentration, order book, and named competitors.** No customer
  name, no order-book figure, no specific competitor is named anywhere
  (only the generic "Chinese manufacturers" reference in Threats, p.76).
- **Product-mix disaggregation.** H-Acid vs. reactive/acid/direct dyes
  revenue split is never given despite four product lines being listed
  (p.10).

An active mis-statement, not merely a silence: Note 35.13 (p.132, standalone;
identical language in the consolidated equivalent) states "The Group is of
the view that they dont have any major foreign currency exposure and no
hedging is been done," directly contradicted by the same AR's own export and
FX-gain disclosures (46.4% of revenue export; Rs 169.32 lakh FX gain in
Other Income, Note, p.122). The likely reason for both the silence pattern
and the mis-statement is the same: the export/FX shift appears new and
possibly not yet reflected in the company's own risk-disclosure drafting
process (the standalone risk notes are, per prior-stage findings, copy-pasted
consolidated-template language).

## Credibility grade rationale

NO-CONCALL MODE sets the default at C and permits a rise to B only on
documented AR-guidance-vs-results delivery evidence, and a fall to D on
documented evasion/contradiction. This run found genuine delivery evidence
in one direction: the FY25-vintage CCD capital-deployment table shows funds
substantially reached their stated destinations on or close to schedule, and
the no-dividend cash-conservation rationale matches the observed cash-flow
pattern. Ordinarily that would support holding at C or nudging toward B. But
this stage also independently found, and cross-checked against the company's
own Notes 17/21, a factually incorrect causal explanation for a ratio
improvement (borrowings said to have decreased; they rose 8.4%) repeated in
two places in the same AR; a separate illogical explanation for a falling
Return on Net Worth ("favourable market conditions"); a direct denial of
material foreign currency exposure (Note 35.13) contradicted by the same
AR's 46.4%-of-revenue export book and its own FX gain line; and a Chairman's
Message that benchmarks the favourable year-on-year metric (revenue) while
omitting the unfavourable one (profit, down 17.6%) in the same passage.
These are documented contradictions between management's own words and
management's own numbers, not merely thin disclosure. That combination is
the evasion/contradiction condition the NO-CONCALL rule ties to a fall to D,
and it outweighs the one area of demonstrated delivery. **Grade: D.**

This grade is the sole input to the Role 1 4D probability weights (D = Poor,
45/40/15) per the main framework; it reflects communication credibility, not
company quality, and does not by itself determine the run's verdict.
