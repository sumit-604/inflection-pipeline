## Business Understanding Narrative

Timex Group India designs, assembles and sells watches under a brand portfolio. It owns
the Timex name in India and licenses global brands: Versace, Guess, Philipp Plein,
Ferragamo, Nautica, Furla, GC, Aston Martin, United Colors of Benetton, plus tech lines
TMX, Helix and iconnect (IP p.30, p.49). It assembles at Baddi, Himachal Pradesh, which
made about 4 million units in FY25, capacity raised to 6 million in FY26 (IP p.51). It
sells through 5,025 multi-brand outlets, 371 large-format stores, 713 defence canteens,
183 luxury points, 41 own stores, and e-commerce on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO and
Tata Cliq Luxury (IP p.50). Revenue is volume times price; margin is what remains after
buying movements, paying royalty to brand owners, and funding ads.

What is changing is the P&L shape. Total income grew from Rs 26,554 lakhs in FY22 to
Rs 80,063 lakhs in FY26, a 32% CAGR (IP p.22). EBITDA margin moved from 3.3% to 14.5%,
Q4FY26 alone at 17.4% (IP p.20, p.24). PBT rose from Rs 322 lakhs to Rs 10,194 lakhs (IP
p.25). The balance sheet cleared with it: retained earnings flipped from negative to
positive at FY25 close, and the board recommended its first preference dividend payment
in years against arrears dating to FY2018-19 (AR FY25 p.21, p.112).

Why now: this is not one good quarter. Revenue, EBITDA and PBT each accelerated for four
straight years, and Q4FY26 (73% revenue growth, 172% EBITDA growth) ran ahead of the
full-year rate (IP p.19). Capacity was already doubled to meet it.

What must be true: the margin gain must be structural, not from temporarily lower spend.
Ad spend rose 50% (IP p.19), so part of growth is bought. Royalty rose only 29% against
48% revenue growth (IP p.19), the cleanest sign scale is doing real work on margin.

What breaks it: the promoter has been a large net seller into this exact re-rating.
Holding fell from 74.93% in March 2025 to 51.00% by December 2025 (IP p.26), across Offer
for Sale filings the corpus lists but does not hold. That is the fact a deep run must
explain before trusting the multiple the market now pays.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Archetype: brand/franchise consumer. Revenue mix is one owned legacy brand plus roughly
ten licensed global brands, sold across wholesale, large-format retail, defence canteen
and e-commerce (IP p.49, p.50). The engine is brand pull plus distribution reach: 5,025
multi-brand points plus five e-commerce platforms. Margin comes from advertising-built
resonance and royalty efficiency, not manufacturing cost advantage; Baddi is fulfilment,
not the moat. Because most brands are licensed, pricing power is partly rented, and
royalty is a permanent tax on it, capping the climb versus an owned-brand peer.

## 3. Competitive advantages
Real but concentrated in curation and distribution, not manufacturing. Management
describes "a pyramid architecture where every brand occupies a distinct space" (IP p.6), a
genuine positioning discipline if it holds. The distribution network is a reach advantage
smaller rivals need years to match (IP p.50). Baddi's flexibility, over 150 movement
types with agile changeover, supports fast SKU turnover (IP p.44, p.51).
Against this: core brand equity for most of the portfolio belongs to Versace, Guess and
the rest, not to Timex India. A non-renewal or royalty-term change hits the moat directly.
Moat: forming in distribution and curation, rented in brand.

## 4. The promoters
Timex Group Luxury Watches B.V. is the direct holding company, part of the wider Tanager
Group B.V. (formerly Timex Group B.V.) (AR FY25 p.108; IP p.47). Chairman David Thomas
Payne and Non-Executive Director Marco Zambianchi carry global-group profiles (IP p.48).
MD Deepak Chhabra has 28 years' experience, a footwear-technologist and marketing
background (IP p.48).
The promoter financed the company for years through high-coupon preference shares (13.88%
and 10.75% tranches, all held by the holding company) rather than only equity (AR FY25
p.109-111), carrying it through accumulated losses and unpaid dividends since FY2018-19,
now clearing (AR FY25 p.21, p.112). Against that support: the same promoter cut its
equity stake from 74.93% to 51.00% in nine months through 2025, as operating numbers
turned sharply better (IP p.26). Pledge status NOT FOUND.

## 5. Financial trajectory
Total income (Rs lakhs): FY22 26,554; FY23 38,378; FY24 42,168; FY25 53,982; FY26 80,063,
32% five-year CAGR (IP p.22). EBITDA: FY22 869 (3.3%); FY23 3,859 (10.1%); FY24 3,517
(8.3%); FY25 4,968 (9.2%); FY26 11,614 (14.5%) (IP p.23-24). PBT: FY22 322; FY23 2,967;
FY24 2,776; FY25 4,277; FY26 10,194 (IP p.25). Q4FY26 alone: total income Rs 23,583 lakhs
(+73% YoY), EBITDA Rs 4,100 lakhs (17.4% margin vs 11.1% prior year) (IP p.20).
FY26 carried a one-time Rs 531 lakh exceptional charge (new Labour Code gratuity/leave
rules); PBT before that was Rs 10,725 lakhs (2.5x FY25) versus reported Rs 10,194 lakhs
(2.4x) (IP p.19). Ad spend rose 50%; royalty rose 29%, slower than revenue (IP p.19). Debt
is light: a Rs 2,200 lakh DBS Bank facility, only Rs 378 lakhs drawn (AR FY25 p.114).
Total borrowings, cash and ROCE NOT FOUND.
Inflection: earnings-led and margin-led together, four straight years, accelerating into
FY26, not a one-off or an asset event.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder
Management's claim, quoted once: "The objective is not just faster growth, but
sustainable, high-quality progress" (IP p.6).
FROM R1/R2, a low-margin, strained distributor: 3.3% to 10.1% EBITDA margin for four of
five years, negative retained earnings until FY25. TO a claimed R3, value-added branded
portfolio: 14.5% margin, a cleared balance sheet, doubled capacity. R4 is not yet
supported; no ROCE in the corpus, and licensed brand equity caps the climb. The
R1/R2-to-R3 move over FY22-FY26 fits the one-rung-per-2-3-years base rate.

## 11. Posture: Transition Decision Matrix
- Proof gate: FIRED. Four consecutive years of accelerating revenue, EBITDA and PBT
  growth, with a delivered capacity expansion behind it.
- Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB. The genuinely ugly optic is historical: accumulated losses
  and unpaid preference dividends, now clearing, evidenced by the FY25 flip to positive
  retained earnings. The auditor's opinion carries no going-concern language.
- Recognition gap: INDETERMINATE. The two market-cap figures (see header) disagree by more
  than double; whether the climb is already priced cannot be judged here.
Proof fired, ugliness an artifact, and an unresolved gap point toward RE-RATING LIVE if the
gap is still open, but the market-cap conflict means this cannot be stated with
confidence. The stake sale is a separate governance flag, not one of the three variables.

## 12. Verdict card
**PROCEED to `/step1`, with CMP reconciliation and the promoter sell-down ranked above
everything else.** Timex Group India shows a real, multi-year margin and earnings climb:
EBITDA margin from 3.3% to 14.5% in five years, capacity doubled, royalty efficiency
improving with scale, and a balance sheet moving from unpaid preference dividends to its
first payment recommendation in years. Proven across five years, not one quarter.
Not a clean PROCEED because two facts sit unresolved. The promoter sold down from 74.93%
to 51.00% in nine months while these numbers improved, and the reason is not in this
corpus. The market cap held here does not match the operator's screen figure, so the
entry price this thesis needs is unknown until reconciled live.
Load-bearing facts a deep run must verify first:
1. Live CMP and market cap, reconciled against the Rs 2,473 cr March-2026 figure.
2. Terms and buyers of both Offer for Sale transactions, and any signal of further
   promoter reduction.
3. The full related-party schedule: royalty, fees, preference dividends to the promoter
   group, against total profit.
4. Segment or channel revenue split, to confirm which brands drove growth.
5. The FY2027 Q1 result, once filed, to test whether the Q4FY26 margin holds.
6. The transcript this corpus could not retrieve, for royalty terms and the stake sale.
What changes the view: a clean, verifiable reason for the sell-down removes the main
overhang. A further stake cut below 51%, or a margin reversal toward the FY25 run-rate,
moves this toward WATCH or PASS. No price here.
