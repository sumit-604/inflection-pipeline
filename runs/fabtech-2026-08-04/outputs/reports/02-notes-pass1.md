## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition — turnkey contracts (Note 3(D), AR consolidated p.158-159; RHP Note 2, p.~313-314):** "Contract revenue and related costs, where execution spans multiple accounting periods, are recognized based on actual shipments as of the reporting date." Turnkey contracts are treated as a **single performance obligation satisfied progressively, but the practical recognition trigger is shipment, not an input/output % -completion measure or formal customer acceptance milestone** (the text separately says the obligation is "satisfied upon completion of contractual scope and formal customer acceptance" — the two sentences are in tension). For a business whose revenue mix includes "Installation, commissioning and Consulting services" as a separate line (₹2,874.54 Lakh FY26 standalone, Note 30) this is a judgment-heavy area. 🟡 Watch — revenue could be recognized ahead of completion/acceptance on goods shipped but not yet installed/commissioned/accepted.
- **No separate contract asset / unbilled revenue line found** anywhere in the Balance Sheet or ageing schedules (AR Note 13a, consolidated and standalone — "Unbilled dues" row is present in the ageing template but shows **nil in every bucket**, both years). For a turnkey-equipment/installation business with shipment-based recognition, the natural corollary would be unbilled revenue/contract assets for goods shipped but not yet invoiced. Its total absence is unusual and not explained. 🟡 Watch (NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: any contract asset/unbilled revenue disclosure).
- **Depreciation useful lives:** Furniture & Fixtures — Leasehold Improvement depreciated over 9 years (lease term) vs Schedule II prescribed 10 years — **shorter than Schedule II, i.e. conservative** (AR consolidated Note 3(H), p.160-161). 🟢 Clean.

NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — disclosure gap for a ₹22.97 Cr goodwill balance. 🟡 Watch.
