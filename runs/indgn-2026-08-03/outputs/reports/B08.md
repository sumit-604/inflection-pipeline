## SECTION 2: LEGAL & REGULATORY RECORD

**2A. **2B/2C. Criminal, tax and revenue matters**:
- **Transfer pricing dispute** (✅ VERIFIED, AR contingent-liabilities note, standalone and consolidated financials): a draft transfer-pricing order dated 18 March 2026 for AY 2023-24 proposes an adjustment aggregating **Rs 1,114 million**; final tax demand not yet determined. The Company has filed for resolution via Mutual Agreement Procedure (MAP) within prescribed timelines and, based on management's assessment and external tax advice, has **not provided** for the exposure, carrying it as a contingent liability. This is a live, material, unresolved tax dispute — flagged, not treated as misconduct (MAP resolution is the standard mechanism for cross-border TP disputes and does not itself imply wrongdoing).
- **Income tax demand notice**: a separate Section 156 demand notice for AY 2023-24 of Rs 43,68,84,410 (including interest) was received from the Income Tax Department (✅ VERIFIED, AR Board's Report "Significant and Material Orders" section, line ~5918-5919).
- No SFIO, PMLA/ED, benami, DRI, or criminal economic-offence proceedings found against the Company or its directors in AR disclosures or web search (searched "Indegene NCLT OR SFIO OR PMLA OR ED raid OR income tax raid" — no matches). ✅ CLEAN on this dimension.

**2D. Other regulators / MCA**:
- **ADT-1 filing defect** (✅ VERIFIED, AR "Secretarial Auditor's Report" and Board's Report qualifications): the Company has been unable to file Form ADT-1 for its incumbent statutory auditor (Deloitte Haskins & Sells) because an earlier ADT-1 (SRN T73514663, filed 21 January 2022) for the prior auditor carried an incorrect "period of accounts" end date (22 December 2025 instead of 31 March 2025). The Board passed a circular resolution (21 August 2025) to seek MCA cancellation of the defective filing and filed Form GNL-1 (SRN AC1053605) on 7 January 2026; as of the AR reporting date, the Registrar had not yet acted. This is a **self-identified, self-reported procedural/administrative defect**, not a regulatory finding against the Company, and remediation is in progress.
- **RSU allotment deviation** (✅ VERIFIED, same source): a delay in completion of documentation meant an ESOP/RSU exercise remittance (USD 43.42, Mr. Sudhir N. Bhatt) was credited to the Company's account on 11 March 2026 — *after* the date of share allotment — constituting an "unintended deviation from the spirit" of Section 39 of the Companies Act, 2013, FEMA 1999, and SEBI share-allotment regulations, which require allotment only after receipt of funds. The Company voluntarily approached MCA/SEBI for adjudication, compounding and settlement (Board resolution, 29 April 2026); no orders passed as of the AR date. Self-reported, small-quantum, procedural in nature — a governance-hygiene flag on process controls, not an intent-driven violation.
- No NCLT oppression petitions, CCI, NGT, RBI, IRDAI or TRAI actions found.

**2E. Civil litigation**:
- **TCPA class action** (✅ VERIFIED, AR contingent-liabilities/litigation-expenses note + 📰 MEDIA REPORTED court filings): Indegene Inc. (US subsidiary) faces a civil class action in the US District Court, District of New Jersey (*Progressive Health and Rehab Corp. v. Indegene, Inc.* and related defendants — Indegene Encima Inc., Indegene Wincere Inc., Indegene Healthcare LLC), alleging unsolicited fax advertisements in violation of the Telephone Consumer Protection Act of 1991, tied to a fax-based outreach engagement type from FY19-20 that the Company states is "no longer used" (operator-relayed digest, cross-checked against AR: Rs 203 million one-time exceptional litigation-expense provision recognized in Q4 FY26, AR note "Litigation expenses(1) 203 -"). 📰 MEDIA REPORTED (McGlinchey Stafford legal update): the presiding judge granted class certification on 17 July 2025.
