## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** TruAlt makes ethanol and sells it to the oil marketing companies (OMCs) for petrol blending. The OMCs allot a volume each supply year and pay an administered price. TruAlt controls its cost and uptime, not its price or volume. Of 2,000 KLPD (kilolitres per day) installed, about 1,300 KLPD can run on grain or sugar syrup (guidance record, doc 090cc7a4, page 7). Smaller lines are compressed biogas (CBG), fuel retail and a subsidiary, Leafiniti.

**What is changing?** Dual feed, to run all year. CBG, from 10 to 162 tonnes per day with six plants by Q4 FY27 (guidance record, doc 503863ee, page 6). A sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) plant at about Rs 2,250 crore of capex (doc 090cc7a4, page 13), targeted for FY29 (doc 503863ee, page 29).

**Why now?** FY26 went backwards. Consolidated revenue fell 7% to Rs 1,813 crore. PAT fell 34% to Rs 96.86 crore from Rs 146.63 crore (Q4 FY26 call transcript, doc 8d721d93, pages 9 to 10, full page). Management now guides FY27 volume of at least 40 crore litres, 55 as a stretch (doc 503863ee, page 23), at Rs 67 to 68 per litre (guidance record, doc 87391b64, page 20). FY26 volume was about 24 crore litres (doc 8d721d93, page 10, full page). The guide needs 67% more volume in one year (derived: 40 / 24).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The OMCs must allot and lift at least 40 crore litres in FY27. A Karnataka High Court order of 4 February 2026 backs supply of about 15 crore litres (doc 090cc7a4, page 7). That order must become lifted volume.

**What breaks it?** Allocation. For 2025/26 the OMCs allotted 30 crore litres. TruAlt supplied about 24 crore litres against 37 crore litres targeted on the February call. On the Q4 call an analyst said the mechanism change "was not informed to us" (doc 8d721d93, page 10, full page). A supplier whose volume can miss by 35% without notice sits on the bottom rung.

## 2. Business model and archetype

The engine is litres times administered price, less feedstock cost. Capacity does not bind: management targets 5.5 to 6 crore litres a month (doc 090cc7a4, page 7). The OMC allotment binds.

FY26 consolidated EBITDA margin was 20.98% (doc 8d721d93, pages 9 to 10, full page).

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds. The binding variables are the spread over grain or syrup cost, utilisation and allocation. The reported ROCE of 10.25% (doc 8d721d93, pages 9 to 10, full page) is shown as disclosed. No single year ROCE feeds any view on this card.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent on price. A few state oil companies buy, at an administered price. The one lever is cost: the plan is to run all five plants on syrup for 100 days in the crushing season (doc 503863ee, page 16). That is feedstock flexibility, not pricing power.

CBG may differ: management reports an EBITDA margin above 55% at the first plant (doc 503863ee, page 5). That is one plant, self reported. Verdict: **moat absent in ethanol, unproven in CBG.**

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Contradiction 1, volume.** About 24 crore litres supplied against 37 guided. The allocation mechanism changed and investors were not told (doc 8d721d93, page 10, full page).
- **Contradiction 2, CBG.** The CFO targets FY27 CBG revenue of Rs 400 crore at 60%+ EBITDA (doc 503863ee, page 14). The Q1 FY27 call guides Rs 10 to 12 crore a quarter (doc 87391b64, page 20). That gives Rs 40 to 48 crore a year (derived: times 4), one tenth of Rs 400 crore.
- **Contradiction 3, capex.** The Q4 call says there is no plan for additional capex (doc 8d721d93, page 9, full page). SAF needs about Rs 2,250 crore (doc 090cc7a4, page 13). Two readings: SAF sits in a joint venture, which an earlier filing floated (doc c3c690dd, page 6), or the capex is not yet counted. The funding structure at financial closure separates them.
- **EBITDA basis** likely includes other income (step 5). **Leverage:** borrowings of Rs 1,539.49 crore against PAT of Rs 96.86 crore.
