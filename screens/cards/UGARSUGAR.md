## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Ugar crushes sugarcane in
two Karnataka mills and sells three things from it: sugar, ethanol and power.
Capacity is 24,200 tonnes of cane a day, 59.5 MW of co-generation and an
845 KLPD distillery (CareEdge press release, page 4). In FY26 sugar earned
segment profit of Rs 80.37 crore, co-generation Rs 24.71 crore, industrial
alcohol Rs 20.27 crore and potable alcohol a loss of Rs 3.25 crore (FY26
results, page 8). The money is the spread between the state-set cane price
and the realised sugar and ethanol price.

**What is changing?** The distillery. An 800 KLPD cane-juice ethanol plant
started in December 2022 and can run on grain at 400 KLPD in the off-season
(CareEdge, page 3). Industrial alcohol revenue rose from Rs 663.96 crore in
FY25 to Rs 788.63 crore in FY26 (FY26 results, page 8). The company plans to
run the distillery on maize in the first half of FY27 (CareEdge, page 3).

**Why now?** Profit came back after a loss year. FY25 lost Rs 16.25 crore.
FY26 earned Rs 13.61 crore (CareEdge, page 5). Q1 FY27 earned Rs 1.49 crore
against a Rs 13.73 crore loss a year earlier (Q1 FY27 results, page 4).
CareEdge upgraded the bank facilities on 13-Aug-2026 (CareEdge, page 2).

**What must be true?** Sugar prices must stay high, the grain distillery must
earn a spread, and the company must pay down Rs 668 crore of debt from thin
accruals.

**What breaks it?** Any one of cane price, sugar price, ethanol price or
monsoon. All four are outside management's control. Finance cost of Rs 61.07
crore in FY26 was 3.6 times pre-tax profit (FY26 results, page 7).

## 3. Competitive advantages

Cost position only. The mills sit in a high-recovery, irrigated zone on the
Krishna, with recovery of 10.8% to 11.7% and an 80-village command area of
about 76,000 acres (CareEdge, page 3). Integration spreads risk across three
products. None of this is pricing power. The state sets cane cost and the
centre sets ethanol price and sugar diversion. Moat: absent. A cost
advantage exists at the margin.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1, commodity price-taker. The claim is a move toward R2 through
integration, with ethanol as the main product.

Management's claim, quoted once: "Ethanol production, which till 5-6 years
back was considered a by-product of Sugar Industry has now become an
important product of Industry and time not far when is going to become the
major / main product for the sector" (FY24 annual report, page 42).

The mechanism is capacity, and the capacity exists. The spread does not.
Industrial alcohol lost Rs 16.76 crore of segment profit in Q4 FY26 (Q1 FY27
results, page 5). Record the reading as **R1, integration built, cost
advantage partial, no climb shown.**

## 8. Proof check

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**Found. CareEdge, 13-Aug-2026. CARE BBB-; Stable on Rs 700 crore of bank
facilities, upgraded from CARE BB+; Negative of 21-May-2025** (CareEdge,
pages 2 and 6).

The agency's reason: "significant improvement in the company's profitability
supported by growth in sales volume and sugar realisation, and better
co-generation power segment performance in FY26 and Q1FY27" (page 2). Its
concern: a "leveraged capital structure", a "working capital intensive
business" and a "seasonal, cyclical, and regulated industry" (page 2).
Upgrade triggers: PBDIT margin above 8% and total debt to PBDIT below 5 times
(page 2). FY26 PBILDT was 7.1% of operating income (page 5). Neither trigger
is met.

The agency sees a sugar-price recovery that repays debt. It does not see a
transition.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The business is a leveraged sugar converter in a good price year. The rating
upgrade is real and filed, and it is a credit event, not a quality climb. The
market already pays twice the R1 multiple on trailing earnings. A `/step1`
would spend a full run to confirm a cyclical recovery.

**Load-bearing facts, if the operator overrides**
1. Q2 FY27 loss against Rs 31.69 crore a year ago.
2. Grain-route ethanol spread per litre.
3. FY26 crushing, recovery and sugar realisation from the FY26 annual report.
4. Total debt at 30-Sep-2026 against Rs 667.95 crore.

**What would change the view**
- Industrial alcohol segment profit above Rs 60 crore for a full year, with
  debt falling below Rs 500 crore, reopens the case.
- A sugar-price fall or a cane-price increase makes it worse.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set.
