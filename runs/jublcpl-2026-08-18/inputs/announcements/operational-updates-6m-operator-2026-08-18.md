## 1. Capacity expansion — Polymer (adhesive) plant, Samlaya (Savli), Vadodara, Gujarat
- Q4/FY26 deck (26 May 2026): will commence the adhesive facility at Samlaya in Q1 FY27.
- Reg 30, 3 June 2026: PARTIALLY commenced commercial production of Polymer
  (Adhesive) at Samlaya w.e.f. 3 June 2026; remaining capacity expected in the
  coming 6 to 8 months.
- Q1/FY27 deck (11 Aug 2026):
  - Brownfield capex Rs 50 crore sanctioned at Vadodara (Samlaya).
  - 30,000 MTPA proposed capacity addition: Phase-1 Adhesives (Polymer) at
    Samlaya; Phase-2 SBR Latex targeted at construction chemicals.
  - Rationale: new category entry (SBR latex -> construction chemicals),
    de-risked by a prior-year pilot, brownfield (no greenfield land / approval
    lead-time).
  - Commissioning: Phase-1 Adhesives — commercial production started Q1 FY27
    (commissioned end of Q1 FY27); Phase-2 SBR Latex — to be completed by end
    Q3 FY27.
  - Full run-rate adds 30,000 MTPA combined and opens a construction-chemicals
    revenue line.

## 2. SBR Latex product / portfolio expansion (Q1/FY27 deck)
- Expanded waterproofing portfolio via new SBR latex; successful product
  approval from key customers.
- New SBR latex line at Vadodara planned complete by end Q3 FY27.
- Performance Polymers & Chemicals export volumes hit by geopolitical /
  logistics disruption; domestic demand resilient in Q1 FY27.
- Inflation mitigated via proactive pricing and revenue management.

## 3. Adhesives business — operational trends
- Q4/FY26: sustained high double-digit revenue growth (GST rate cuts +
  discretionary recovery); wider distribution, stronger contractor ecosystem;
  higher A&P and digital brand building. Input cost pressure emerged March 2026,
  managed via pricing + higher RM inventory.
- Q1/FY27: strong double-digit revenue growth on broad-based volume gains;
  margins improved via cost optimization, strategic procurement, supply-chain
  efficiencies; new Samlaya polymer facility commissioned end Q1 FY27.

## 4. Agri Products business — demand & working capital
- Q4/FY26: Agri revenue +45% YoY in Q4, +52% YoY FY26; better SSP placement in
  demand zones, better mix, entry into bulk fertilizer (NPK 20:20:0:13). Agri
  Nutrient (bio-stimulants) hit by FCO regulatory changes. Sharp Sulphur/Acid
  input cost rise; increased working capital to build inventory for off-season
  placement, expected liquidation by Q1 FY27.
- Q1/FY27: demand subdued on weak/uneven monsoon; higher input costs only
  partly offset by price increases; Agri Nutrient business high double-digit
  growth with improved profitability; working capital deployment remained high;
  near-term demand may stay subdued; working capital expected to remain high.

## 5. Demerger of the Agri Division — regulatory / process milestones
- Background (pre-window): Board approved Scheme of Arrangement 4 Nov 2025 to
  demerge the Agri Division of JACPL into Jubilant Agri Solutions Ltd (JASL);
  1:1 share swap (Rs 10 FV); cancellation of JACPL's shares in JASL; JASL to
  list on BSE/NSE; post-scheme JACPL to be renamed "Jubilant Industries
  Limited".
- NSE/BSE Observation Letters / NOCs: 17 April 2026. Scheme filed with NCLT
  Allahabad Bench.
- NCLT First Motion Order dated 8 July 2026 (announced 9 July 2026): allowed
  First Motion; directed JACPL equity shareholders' meeting and unsecured
  creditors' meeting (debt >= Rs 1,00,000) on 5 September 2026; dispensed with
  JACPL secured creditors' meeting (100% consent) and all JASL meetings (WOS,
  consent obtained); confirmed post-effectiveness renaming to "Jubilant
  Industries Limited".
- Q1/FY27 deck (11 Aug 2026) reconfirms the above; Scheme remains subject to
  statutory/regulatory, shareholder and creditor approvals.
- Effect: separation into JASL (pure-play Agri) and residual JACPL (renamed
  Jubilant Industries Ltd) focused on Performance Polymers & Chemicals /
  Consumer products.
