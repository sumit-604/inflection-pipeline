## 3. THE CATALYST STORY, IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue.** Three of India's largest banks signed integrated cash-and-machine contracts inside eighteen months: SBI (live January 2026), HDFC 6,000 ATMs (from FY27), ICICI (ALGO live in FY27), plus IPPB replacements and the FSS asset buy (closed Q1 FY27, adds about 8,000 units). These are filed and signed, not talk. What confirms it: services revenue printing low-teens growth through FY27 as the contracts ramp. What kills it: the contracts ramp but revenue stays flat, meaning the shrinking cash pond is eating the gains faster than the wins add them.

**Margin.** Route automation, pricing discipline, and the mix shift toward technology are lifting margin off the FY26 trough. Q1 FY27 already printed 27.2% on services and guidance was raised to about 27%. What confirms it: a second and third quarter at or above 26.5%. What kills it: margin sliding back toward 24-25% once the Q1 drivers roll off, which would say the print was a one-off.

**Cash.** This is the weak transition. The catalyst that could help is the currency-supply normalisation, from 70% fulfilment at Q1 toward normal by end Q2 FY27, which should improve fresh collections. But the aged receivables, the money already 1-2 years overdue, have no catalyst: the DSO promise was dropped, no factoring facility exists, and the loss allowance was released rather than built. What confirms improvement: the next ageing disclosure shows the 1-2 year bucket moderating toward FY25 levels. What kills it: the bucket widens again while Cash Management revenue falls again, which flips the flag from INDETERMINATE toward structural.

**ROCE.** Capex is guided down sharply, from about Rs 350 Cr in FY26 to Rs 100-125 Cr in FY27, and Rs 8,126m of contracted lease rentals is the harvest arriving. Both are documented. What confirms it: FY28 ROCE crossing 22% on flat capital. What kills it: capex comes in at guidance, the contracts reach full ramp, and ROCE still does not clear 20% by FY28, which would mean CMS bought a zero-spread rental book because it had no choice.

**The one that turns the whole call:** the FY28 post-tax ROCE print. That is the proof gate. Everything else is preface.
