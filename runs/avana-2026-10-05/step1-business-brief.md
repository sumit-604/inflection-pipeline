# Step 1 business brief: Avana Electrosystems Ltd (AVANA)

Identity: Avana Electrosystems Ltd, NSE Emerge (SME) symbol AVANA, ISIN INE1KU201016, Bengaluru (Peenya). Listed 20-Jan-2026 at an issue price of Rs 59. Screener URL: https://www.screener.in/company/AVANA/ (standalone; the /consolidated/ page carries no data, so the company files standalone only). CMP Rs 139, market cap Rs 315 Cr (collector, 2026-10-05).

1. Rs 83.86 Cr FY26 sales, up 36% from Rs 61.49 Cr in FY25; PAT Rs 11.72 Cr vs Rs 8.47 Cr (screener data sheet, inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv). Sales were Rs 28.41 Cr in FY23, so the base roughly tripled in three years.
2. What it sells: customised control and relay panels (11 kV to 400 kV), protection relays, SCADA and substation automation for transmission and distribution substations, generation plants, and solar and wind evacuation (https://www.screener.in/company/AVANA/; RHP in inputs/prospectus/).
3. Mix: control and relay panels 60.26% and relays 39.74% of H1 FY26 revenue. By customer type: private parties 76.24%, tender/government 22.90%, dealers 0.86% (https://www.5paisa.com/sme-ipo/avana-electrosystems-ltd-ipo; verify against RHP).
4. The company reports one segment (reply to NSE, 23-Jun-2026, inputs/announcements/AVANA-Reply-to-NSE-2026-06-23.pdf, item 2).
5. Margin: EBITDA about 19.5% in FY26 and about 19.4% in FY25 on screener figures (PBT + interest + depreciation − other income). The margin source is design-to-spec engineering and in-house relays, not scale. Raw material is 63% of sales in FY26 vs 54% in FY25, so the gross margin fell while the operating margin held.
6. Top 5 customers were 38.79% of revenue in the H1 FY26 stub, vs 22.42% in FY25 (RHP risk factor, prospectus .txt line ~2515). Concentration is rising.
7. Working capital: receivables Rs 22.21 Cr and inventory Rs 22.43 Cr at Mar-2026 against sales of Rs 83.86 Cr. That is about 97 debtor days, down from about 126 days at Mar-2025. Inventory rose 52%. Cash was Rs 27.83 Cr after the IPO.
8. THE ENGINE: an integrated manufacturing unit on KIADB lease-cum-sale land. The RHP funds Rs 1,155.38 lakh of its capex from the fresh issue. The board said on 19-Aug-2026 that commercial production should start by end-October 2026, inside a KIADB timeline that was extended (inputs/announcements/AVANA-Board-outcome-2026-08-19.pdf item 7). CWIP was Rs 4.67 Cr at Mar-2026.
9. Pipeline evidence: vendor registration with MP Power Transmission for 400 kV line C&R panels, and approval as a SCADA vendor up to 220 kV for Chhattisgarh State Power Transmission (Reg 30, 10-Feb-2026, inputs/announcements/AVANA-Reg30-agreements-2026-02-10.pdf). These are approvals, not orders.
10. Latest print: FY26 audited results, approved 21-May-2026. The original filing is scanned. NSE asked for a legible copy, the declaration on unmodified opinion, and the IPO working-capital utilisation certificate, and the company re-filed on 23-Jun-2026. No concalls held. No guidance found on the web.
11. Governance notes: CS resigned 30-Mar-2026 and a new CS joined 10-Apr-2026. The promoter group was revised after listing to add a member with nil holding (NSE SHP revision 23-Jan-2026). Promoters hold 73.64% (SHP 31-Mar-2026). NSE asked about price movement on 10-Apr-2026, and the company replied it had no pending news.
