## Narrative summary (plain language)

Data Patterns opened FY27 with a soft quarter dressed as a growth quarter. Revenue rose 16.8% to Rs 116 Cr, but every profit line beneath it fell: operating EBITDA -2.2%, operating margin -526 bps to 27.0%, PAT -13.5%. Employee and other costs outgrew the incremental revenue. Management called the softness "temporary delays in customer approvals" and guided FY27 to 20-25% revenue growth and 35-40% margins; Q1 landed below both.

Balance sheet held: net cash ~Rs 466 Cr, zero debt, clean Deloitte review. But the quarter disclosed no Q1 cash flow (Reg 33 permits at Q1) and the deck gave only annual cash figures. For a company running ~0.30x cash conversion, the silence is the signal.

Two new events: (1) first-ever acquisition — 100% of STAC (defence composites), stated cost Rs 1.5 Cr but true outlay ~Rs 10 Cr (Rs 8.5 Cr loan to clear the target's liabilities, ~2x its turnover); Rs 1.5 Cr equity paid to promoters, arm's-length basis blank; turns DP into a group reporting consolidated accounts from Q2/Q3. (2) Order book shown two ways — Rs 2,654 Cr incl. "negotiated and pending" vs only Rs 928 Cr confirmed (~65% soft).

Verdict unchanged: WATCHLIST/AVOID on price (~4.7-5.3x above entry zone). Margin and cash softness firm the avoid slightly; no thesis-broken trigger fired. Next real test: Q2/H1FY27 mandatory half-yearly cash flow = first FY27 read on cash conversion.

- **Bull:** revenue still grew double digits; net cash intact; BrahMos seeker tested; Q1 is structurally the smallest quarter (may be seasonal).
- **Bear:** growth with falling profit; margins far below guidance; cash undisclosed; exports thin at Rs 39 Cr; AMCA and EW-suite catalysts unmentioned.

## Sector / AMCA

Sector tailwind intact (indigenisation, rising order books, private-sector primes; BEL reaffirming 28% margin on a Rs 72k Cr book = demand is not the constraint). AMCA remains contested and unawarded. MoD shortlisted three manufacturing consortia: Tata Advanced Systems; L&T + BEL + Dynamatic; Bharat Forge + BEML + Data Patterns. HAL excluded. Bharat Forge consortium split: Bharat Forge 50 / BEML 30 / Data Patterns 20. RFP expected ~April 2026; final decision anticipated by year-end 2026. Firms missing the prime role can still take Tier-II/III work (MoD).

**Thesis linkage:** supports two of the seven DA cracks — "franchise not monopoly" (shared, unawarded upside; DP a 20% junior partner in one of three bids) and "AMCA optionality overweighted vs BEL confidence" (BEL in a rival consortium, still the scale radar/EW supplier regardless of airframe winner).
