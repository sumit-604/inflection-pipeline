## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- Basis of preparation: Indian GAAP (AS framework), NOT Ind AS. The company
  is SME-listed and MCA's 16-Feb-2015 notification exempts SME-Exchange
  companies from mandatory Ind AS adoption (Note 2(a), standalone p.84 and
  consolidated p.121). 🟢 Clean but material: this is a small, less
  standardised framework than Ind AS (no Ind AS 116 ROU/lease liability
  balance sheet recognition - see leases below).
- Depreciation: useful lives per Schedule II EXCEPT Plant & Equipment
  (10 yrs vs Schedule II 15 yrs) and Electrical Fittings (5 yrs vs 10 yrs),
  both shortened based on "technical evaluation carried out by management"
  (Note 2(e), standalone p.85; consolidated Note 2(f) p.122). 🟡 Watch:
  shorter useful life = MORE conservative (higher depreciation charge), the
  opposite direction of an aggressive policy. Residual value 5%.
- Leases: policy text describes only OPERATING lease treatment (expensed
  straight-line), consistent with AS (no Ind AS 116 ROU asset/liability
  recognition) (Note 2(g) standalone p.85-86; consolidated 2(h) p.122).
  Note 34 (both standalone p.101 and consolidated p.139) shows leases are
  "cancellable" (standalone) / "cancellable and non-cancellable, 3-5 years"
  (consolidated) with future minimum lease payments of Nil in FY26 (was
  Rs.3.41 lakh FY25, "not later than one year"). 🟢 Clean, small.
- Borrowing costs: AS-16 capitalisation of borrowing costs directly
  attributable to qualifying assets (Note 2(p) standalone p.88; 2(q)
  consolidated p.125). This is the policy that lets interest on the YEIDA
  plant loan be added to CWIP rather than expensed - see CWIP section below;
  no separate note quantifies the capitalised interest amount in FY26 (NOT
  FOUND IN DOCUMENT - the notes do not break out capitalised borrowing cost
  as a distinct line). 🟡 Watch: screener flagged "possible interest
  capitalisation" (LBF4); the notes confirm the POLICY exists but do not
  disclose the RUPEE AMOUNT capitalised in FY26, which is needed to test the
  flag.
- No first-time standard adoption disclosed in FY26. No policy CHANGE with a
  quantified P&L impact is disclosed (Note 58 standalone / 54 consolidated:
  "Previous year figures have been regrouped or reclassified wherever
  necessary" - a regrouping statement, not a policy change).
- Revenue recognition: risk-and-reward transfer model, net of discounts/
  taxes (Note 2(c) standalone p.84; 2(d) consolidated p.122). No contract
  asset/liability disclosure, no disaggregation by performance obligation
  timing - see Revenue section. 🟡 Watch: no ECL matrix disclosed for trade
  receivables beyond ageing-based provisioning (see Receivables section);
  no formal ECL % matrix by bucket is shown, provisioning appears case-by-
  case against ageing buckets (Note 18/20).
- Impairment: standard value-in-use test language, no growth/discount rate
  assumptions disclosed because no impairment was recognised or tested with
  quantified assumptions in the notes (NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: no impairment
  testing assumptions table for goodwill or PPE).
