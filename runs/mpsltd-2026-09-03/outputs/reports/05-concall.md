### 1C. Trigger evolution across quarters

- **AJE dollar-value pruning never quantified.** All three earnings calls describe AJE revenue being
  deliberately shrunk ("pruning," "resetting by design," "inflated base we had two years ago" — Q4
  FY26 call, Rahul Arora, p.13; "consumer base finds its new level" — Q1 FY27 call, Christine
  Miranda, p.4), but no call in this corpus gives an absolute USD/INR figure for AJE revenue before
  or after the prune. The SPEAR-flagged "~USD 18m → 12m" figure is **NOT FOUND** in any of the four
  documents read. Trend direction is confirmed (ex-AJE Research growth accelerating: 16.2% 9M FY26
  → 17% FY26 full year → 23% Q4 FY26 alone → 26.3% Q1 FY27), but the SPEAR-cited "~18.3%" figure is
  also not found verbatim; closest matching disclosed number is 17% (FY26 full year, ex-AJE organic
  Research growth, Q4 FY26 call, Sukhwant Singh, p.3).
- **Segment mix target quietly shifted.** The Unbound acquisition call (18-Feb-2026) states the
  Vision 2027 target mix as "40:40:20" (Research:Education:Corporate) — with Rahul Arora explicitly
  noting "a similar split forecast was made [years ago] and we were completely wrong" (candid
  admission). By the Q4 FY26 call (May-2026) and Q1 FY27 call (Jul-2026), the disclosed FY27 EBITDA
  split ballpark is instead ~55:35:10. Management never explicitly flags this as a revision of the
  earlier 40:40:20 figure — an investor has to do the arithmetic across two calls to notice the
  change. FLAG.
- **STAR-account count dropped from the narrative.** Q3 FY26 call (Feb 2026) gives a specific,
  quantified trigger: STAR accounts scaled 10 → 30 → "almost tripled" (~90) across three segments,
  with cross-sell index rising from 2.5x to 5.5x over two years (Rahul Arora, p.8-9). This metric is
  not repeated or updated in either the Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27 calls. Not necessarily a bad sign, but a
  quantified metric that disappeared from disclosure — track at next quarter.
- **Unbound margin-ramp timeline slipped.** Promised at acquisition announcement (Feb-2026): Q2 of
  ownership should show "early 20s" EBITDA margin. Delivered at that checkpoint (Q1 FY27, reported
  Jul-2026): monthly run-rate ~USD 800,000, margin 18%-20% (Sukhwant Singh/Prarthana Agarwal via
  Rahul Arora, Q1 FY27 call, p.13) — still at the pre-acquisition historical range (14%-19%), not
  the "early 20s" promised for that specific checkpoint. TIMELINE SLIPPAGE, flagged below.

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

- Labour Code exceptional charge (negative INR 3.5/share) offered as a factual, quantified
  explanation for EPS-guidance confusion, not a deflection (Q3 FY26 call, Rahul Arora, p.13).
- Corporate Learning's multi-year underperformance ("even 3 years back, we were doing significantly
  more top line and margins" — Madhur Rathi, Q3 FY26 call, p.12) was met with a structural
  explanation (three siloed entities, consolidation under one leader) rather than external blame.
- Organic growth pressure-tested directly: when Vikas Mhatre (Q1 FY27 call, p.14) pointed out
  ex-Unbound growth is "early teens," Rahul Arora responded "thank you for pushing us... we are not
  settling for achieved organic growth... Corporate [growth of 6-7%] is the area where a lot of the
  catch-up has to happen" — a rare, specific, self-critical admission under analyst pressure, not
  deflection.
- Walked away from a "great financial deal" in Education because of ethical concerns about
  "shortcuts the management team had taken with some of the customers" (Q1 FY27 call, Rahul Arora,
  p.15) — a proactive, unprompted disclosure that costs the company a deal; a positive signal.
- One clear deflection: management-team turnover (exits/replacements) raised by Krushi Parekh
  (Q1 FY27 call, p.8) was answered only in generic culture/values terms ("EEE," "ownership
  mindset"), with no names, roles, or reasons given.
- One instance of "answered later, not now": Unbound's 2025 revenue was declined ("unable to share...
  accounts not finalized," Unbound acquisition call, Madhur Rathi, p.11) but a range was volunteered
  two calls later (see 2E).
- No instance found of blaming external parties (forex, competitors, macro) for a miss; the one
  macro-risk discussion (Q4 FY26 call, Gunit Singh's risk question) was answered by naming shared,
  sector-wide risk rather than disclaiming company-specific responsibility.

**Overall pattern: BALANCED.** Genuine self-critical admissions and one proactive ethical
walk-away outweigh the single generic non-answer and single delayed disclosure; no
external-blame pattern detected.

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. What management says about competitors

- Unbound vs "legacy competitors like UpToDate or AMBOSS," described as fragmented/single-category
  (clinical reference OR education, not both) versus Unbound's "unified, scalable knowledge model"
  (Unbound acquisition call, Rahul Arora, p.10). Credibility check: directionally plausible and
  falls within normal M&A-pitch framing; not independently verified in this corpus — flagged for
  peer verification.
- On generative-AI/agentic competitors (OpenAI, Anthropic, "Claude Cowork"): MPS/Unbound position
  themselves as AI-model-agnostic integrators sitting on "the last mile" (domain trust, workflow
  embedding), not as competitors to foundation models (Q3 FY26 call, Rahul Arora/Sreenivas T.V.,
  p.9-11; Unbound acquisition call, p.9-10). Credibility check: consistent narrative repeated
  identically across all three earnings calls and the acquisition call — a strength of message
  discipline, but it is also an unfalsifiable, self-serving framing that no analyst seriously
  stress-tested.
- "Most of our competition is not listed" (Q4 FY26 call, Rahul Arora, p.15, used to justify
  withholding a segment margin split) — a reasonable competitive-sensitivity argument, but also
  conveniently limits disclosure.

  — MANAGEMENT VIEW, unverified.
