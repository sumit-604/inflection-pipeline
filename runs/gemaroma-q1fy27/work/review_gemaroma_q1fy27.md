## 1. SUMMARY NARRATIVE

Gem Aromatics is a first-time coverage name and this is an initiation read, not a position change. In the June 2026 quarter the two sets of accounts tell opposite stories. The parent company alone made a small profit of Rs 7.3 crore, up 11% on last year, on revenue of Rs 83.0 crore, up 8.6%. But the whole group, including its two subsidiaries, lost Rs 7.9 crore, against a Rs 8.0 crore profit a year ago. The gap is the subsidiary layer, chiefly the new Dahej plant run through Krystal Ingredients, which swung from adding Rs 1.5 crore to draining Rs 15.1 crore in twelve months. The plant was commissioned in February 2026 and now carries about Rs 9.1 crore of depreciation a quarter, but the new products it was built for, cooling agents, Safranal and phenol derivatives, are still pre-revenue. So the group is paying the fixed cost before the sales arrive. Group operating margin fell from 17.0% to 3.3%. Even the parent's headline profit growth is not real operating strength: strip out higher other income and lower interest cost and the parent's core pre-tax profit actually fell about 9%. Management's deck and press release talk only about revenue growth and "gradual improvement" and never mention the consolidated loss, which is a transparency concern. There is no cash-flow statement this quarter, so cash quality is INDETERMINATE. The verdict is PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. The maiden recommendation is to put the name on the WATCHLIST and monitor, not to buy: this is a genuine transition-alpha candidate, but the turn is entirely unquantified. What would change the call is a specific Dahej utilisation number and a quarter of the subsidiary turning EBITDA-positive. The next hard checkpoint is the Q2 FY27 half-yearly cash flow and the first revenue from Safranal and cooling agents.

## 2. SECTOR INTELLIGENCE

Provenance note: no prior Notion sector work exists (first coverage); all figures below are from THIS quarter's filings, market sizes sourced by the company to a Frost & Sullivan 2025 study (deck pp14-17), which is a company-commissioned source, not independent. Gem operates in specialty ingredients, essential oils and aroma chemicals, selling into oral care, cosmetics, pharma, nutraceuticals and personal care. It is a natural-plus-synthetic play with raw-material cyclicality: this quarter the Clove business was hit by floods in Madagascar (a sourcing origin) that raised raw-material prices and cut sales, now said to be normalised (PR L114-116). Demand is described as seasonally softer in Q1. The structural tailwind the company is pursuing is forward integration from base oils (mint, clove, eucalyptus) into higher-value molecules (menthol, eugenol derivatives, cooling agents, phenol derivatives such as MEHQ, guaiacol, 4-MAP, and citral-chemistry molecules like Safranal). ## 3. BUSINESS-MODEL INTELLIGENCE

Provenance: all from this quarter's filings; no prior model work. Gem makes money by processing botanical and petrochemical feedstocks into essential oils, aroma chemicals and value-added derivatives across three facilities (Silvassa 1997, Budaun 2016, Dahej 2024) with a stated installed capacity of 16,171 MTPA (PR L212 — the only capacity figure disclosed; per-facility tonnage is absent, a silence flag). Revenue mix FY26 (deck): Mint ~65% (Rs 237 Cr), Clove ~23% (Rs 85 Cr), Citral/Other ~10% (Rs 36 Cr), Phenol ~1% (Rs 5 Cr). Geography FY26: India 56%, USA 28%, Brazil 6%, China 3%, RoW 7%. Unit economics historically were sound, group EBITDA margin 15-18% and ROCE ~16% through FY25, but FY26 broke this: revenue fell 27% to Rs 366.5 Cr, EBITDA margin to 11.1%, PAT to Rs 1.4 Cr, and estimated ROCE to ~3%, because the Rs 265 Cr Dahej plant was capitalised (PPE jumped Rs 43 Cr to Rs 243 Cr) with no incremental revenue yet. What this quarter says about model drift: the company has front-loaded a large fixed-cost, capital-intensive bet on advanced chemistry, and the model is now hostage to utilisation. Metrics NOT disclosed that the model turns on: Dahej utilisation %, order book, segment profitability, per-facility capacity, and the debt/accruals funding split.

## 4. COMPETITION INTELLIGENCE

Provenance: this quarter's filings only; no prior peer work loaded, and no peer concall reported in the ±4-week window (stated in Section B). Gem's claimed edge is scale and backward-to-forward integration in niche naturals: "one of the largest processors of Clove Oil and Eugenol in India by volume (FY25)," largest eucalyptus-oil processor at ~58% India share, and entry into "limited-competition, high-margin molecules" via vapor-phase phenol chemistry (deck pp15-16).
