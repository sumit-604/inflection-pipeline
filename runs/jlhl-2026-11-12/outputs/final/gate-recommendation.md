## Monitorables and triggers

1. Read the Dombivli EBITDA footnote in the Q3 FY27 investor presentation (Jan-Feb 2027). The Q2 FY27 figure was a 0.2 Rs Cr loss, partly seasonal. At or above zero confirms the ramp. A loss above 9 Rs Cr for the quarter (3 Rs Cr a month) kills the early ramp reading. This tests the Dombivli transition.
2. Check receivable days and the receivables ageing at the Q4 FY27 print and in the FY27 AR Note 7. Days were 18.4 at Mar-2026 and about 24.7 at Sep-2026 (derived). Above 25, with the unbilled line still growing, moves the cash read toward STRUCTURAL. This tests the cash flag.
3. Track total debt to EBITDA each quarter, both with and without leases. ICRA's downgrade trigger is 1.8x sustained. Sep-2026 was 1.64x without leases and 1.86x with them (derived). This tests whether the build stays funded inside the rating.
4. Track occupancy excluding Dombivli in the quarterly deck. H1 FY27 was 64.8%. At least 65% is healthy; below 62% (the FY26 level) means mature demand is eroding. This tests whether the mature hospitals can keep carrying the build.
5. Compare quarterly depreciation plus finance cost against EBITDA growth, and watch PAT. The Q2 FY27 base was D&A 28.5 Rs Cr and finance cost 13.8 Rs Cr. PAT down again year on year means the squeeze is still deepening. This tests when operating leverage starts to pay.
6. Read the FY27 AR notes for capital commitments, capitalised borrowing cost and the Dombivli depreciation start. ICRA's 890-900 Rs Cr plan excludes BKC (about 354 Rs Cr) and the Pune land (100 Rs Cr new cash). Continued silence leaves the funding need unsized. This tests the funding claim.
7. Watch Pune South cost per bed (1.2 Rs Cr excluding land, CY28) and Mira Road approval and excavation by Mar-2027. A cost above 1.2 Rs Cr per bed, a date slip, or no Mira Road approval by FY27 end kills the trigger. Until Mira Road starts, only 62% embedded growth is under execution. This tests capacity growth.
8. Watch for Dombivli cashless insurer empanelment and the NABH award, expected 6 to 12 months from Oct 2026. On the Q3 FY27 call, management also said it will state the impact of the 30% onco-drug margin cap. Empanelment beyond 12 months or a material cap impact weakens Dombivli occupancy and ARPOB. This tests the Dombivli ramp and pricing.

Falsification line: a Dombivli EBITDA loss above 9 Rs Cr in Q3 FY27 (the 3 Rs Cr a month kill line) in the Q3 FY27 investor presentation footnote. That print would show the Q2 near breakeven was seasonal, and the squeeze would run longer.
