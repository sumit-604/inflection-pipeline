GE Power India Limited (GEPIL) reports Q2 FY 2026-27 Results 
 
Mumbai, 13 November 2026 — GE Power India Limited reported on 13 November 2026 its financial results for the second quarter 
ended 30 September 2026.  
 
For the second quarter (July – September 2026):  
 
 Total income for the quarter from continuing operations was INR 3,425.1 million up by 11.6%, compared to INR 3,069.6 
million in Quarter Ended (QE) 30 September 2025. 
 
 Profit/(Loss) before tax and exceptional items from continuing operations for the quarter is INR 529.8.0 million, compared 
to INR 461.0 million in Quarter Ended (QE) 30 September 2025. 
 
 Profit/(Loss) after tax and exceptional items for the quarter is INR 327.9 million, compared to INR 304.3 million in Quarter 
Ended (QE) 30 September 2025. 
 
 Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) before exceptional items for the Quarter from 
continuing operations Ended (QE) 30 September 2026 is 18.1%, compared to 17.7% for the Quarter Ended (QE) 30 
September 2025.   
 
 The current quarter ended with Order backlog of INR 15,273 million down by (-)16.3% compared to INR 18,250 million in 
quarter ended 30 September 2025. 
 
Puneet Bhatla, Managing Director, GE Power India Limited said, “Our strategic focus on Core Services continues to deliver 
tangible results, driven by a 38% year-on-year order growth this quarter. We have also significantly strengthened our operational 
profitability and balance sheet health—highlighted by a robust net cash position and a substantial reduction in bank guarantees. 
Combined with our expanding international footprint, such as the Shoaiba project win in Saudi Arabia, we remain fully committed 
to building on this positive momentum in the coming quarters.”
