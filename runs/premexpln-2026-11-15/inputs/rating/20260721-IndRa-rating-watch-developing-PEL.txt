== ANALYTICAL APPROACH ==
To arrive at the rating, Ind-Ra continues to fully consolidate the financials of PEL and its subsidiaries: Premier Wires Products Limited (PEL holds an 80% stake) and PELNEXT Defence Systems Private Limited (100% subsidiary).

== DETAILED RATIONALE ==
Ind-Ra has placed the ratings on Rating Watch with Developing Implications following the signing of a definitive share purchase agreement between PEL and Apollo Micro Systems Limited (AMSL) on 9 July 2026 under which AMSL will acquire promoters’ 41.33% stake in PEL for around INR15.5 billion. The transaction is likely to be completed in the next six-to-nine months, subject to the receipt of necessary statutory and regulatory approvals under the applicable laws, including the approval from the jurisdictional National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). 
 AMSL is engaged in defence technology, and specialises in the design, development, and manufacture of advanced electronic, electro-mechanical, and engineering systems. The acquisition highlights a strategic decision to combine the complementary strengths of the companies, drive operational efficiencies, enhance research and development (R&D) capabilities, and expand participation across the defence and aerospace sectors. As per the PEL management, the entire promoter shareholding is being divested, with the sale proceeds accruing directly to the promoters. At present, the post-transaction governance structure, including any changes to the board of directors, has not been disclosed and remains uncertain.

== LIQUIDITY ==
Adequate: As on 31 March 2026, the company’s unencumbered cash and cash equivalents stood at INR512 million (FYE25: INR932 million), reflecting a healthy liquidity position. The company’s average monthly maximum utilisation of the fund-based working capital limits of INR900 million was 4% for the 12 months ended March 2026 and that of the non-fund-based limits of INR1,931 million was 59%. The company has scheduled debt repayments of INR78.51 million for FY27, INR180.14 million for FY28 and INR155.14 million for, FY29, and does not intend to raise any additional debt, indicating a stable capital structure.

== RATING SENSITIVITIES ==
The Rating Watch with Developing Implications indicates that the ratings may be upgraded or affirmed or downgraded upon resolution. The Rating Watch reflects the need to assess the implications of the change in shareholding on the company’s strategic direction, financial policies, capital allocation strategy, and the nature and extent of operational and financial linkages with the incoming promoter group.

== ABOUT THE COMPANY ==
PEL is engaged in the manufacturing of explosives, propellants, and related products primarily for defence, aerospace, and industrial applications. Established in 1980, it operates facilities approved by government agencies and serves domestic and export markets. It operates two key defence and propellant units in Peddakundukur and Katepally (Telangana), along with six bulk explosive manufacturing facilities spread across Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu.
