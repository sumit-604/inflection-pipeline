P/E 28.3 (screener, 2026-10-04). Listed 03-Sep-2026 on BSE SME at Rs 90; issue

1. Revenue Rs 30.18 Cr FY24, Rs 65.03 Cr FY25, Rs 105.71 Cr FY26; PAT Rs 2.83 / 8.56 / 13.51 Cr; EBITDA Rs 19.06 Cr FY26 (~18%) (Prospectus 31-Aug-2026, restated; https://www.indiaipo.in/ipo-blogs/kwick-forensic-solutions-ipo).
2. What it sells: forensic kits and field equipment, cyber and digital forensic tools, DNA lab equipment, mobile crime-scene vans, training and services, to police, forensic labs (CFSL, NFSU), MHA, MoD and state police (Prospectus; https://kwickforensic.com/).
3. FY26 mix: physical evidence 37%, cyber and digital 33%, DNA 11%, mobile CSI vans 10%, services 9%; goods 91% / services 9% (https://www.equityresearchindia.com/post/kwick-forensic-solutions-ipo-27-aug-31-aug-analysis).
4. Margin source: raw material is Rs 79.98 Cr of Rs 105.71 Cr sales FY26 (~24% gross margin), so the model is resale plus integration of OEM tools (Sirchie, Thermo Fisher named as principals) with own kit assembly, not deep manufacturing (screener Data Sheet; equityresearchindia).
5. Customers: top 10 = Rs 82.07 Cr, 77.64% of FY26 revenue. Government share fell 86.98% FY24 -> 78.91% FY25 -> 55.22% FY26; non-government rose to Rs 47.34 Cr (44.78%). Gujarat 29.39%, Bihar 9.02% of FY26 (Prospectus pp. 23-24, risk factors; page lines ~1801-1813).
6. Working capital: receivables Rs 11.94 / 19.79 / 22.88 Cr FY24-26; inventory Rs 3.29 / 7.57 / 10.44 Cr; CFO Rs -2.61 / +4.65 / +7.62 Cr (equityresearchindia; screener Data Sheet).
7. Engine: BNSS 2023 (in force July 2024) mandates forensic examination at scenes of offences punishable by 7+ years. That pushes every state toward mobile forensic vans, field kits, DNA and cyber labs. The prospectus credits BNSS for the DNA ramp (Prospectus, industry and business sections).
8. Latest print: none since listing. SME, half-yearly reporting; H1 FY27 results due after the trading window closed 01-Oct-2026 (BSE filing 28-Sep-2026). No concalls held. No guidance filed. No credit rating (equity issue; prospectus says none required).
9. Post-listing filings: investor conference 30-Sep-2026 with a 22-page presentation; price-movement clarification to BSE 26-Sep-2026; a Reg 30 note 27-Sep-2026 citing a third-party Rs 150 Cr NFSU campus in Varanasi as an "emerging opportunity" (BSE announcements, inputs/announcements/).
10. Shareholding Sep-2026: promoters 64.65% (pre-IPO 88.54%), FII 1.49%, DII 10.85% (screener; filing not collected).
11. Use of proceeds: working capital Rs 31.42 Cr plus general corporate purposes (indiaipo).

## PEERS (no listed pure play; the prospectus says "There is no listed industry peer company engaged in the same line of business")
- Zen Technologies (ZENTEC, https://www.screener.in/company/ZENTEC/consolidated/): sells training simulators and security tech to police and defence on government tenders; same buyer, same budget cycle, same lumpy order-to-cash profile. Higher-margin own IP, so it marks the top of the margin range.
- Allied Digital Services (ADSL, https://www.screener.in/company/ADSL/consolidated/): safe-city and police command-centre integrator for state governments; same state-police buyer, same integration-of-OEM-hardware model, same state receivable risk.
- Dynacons Systems & Solutions (DSSL, https://www.screener.in/company/DSSL/consolidated/): government IT hardware and software resale plus integration on tenders, thin gross margin, WC-funded growth; the closest match to Kwick's resale economics.
- Gap named: no listed forensic, DNA-lab or crime-scene-vehicle maker exists, so peer questions on forensic-specific pricing will be partly unanswerable.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)
1. CUSTOMER SHIFT: government share fell from 86.98% to 55.22% in two years while revenue rose 3.5x. Identify the non-government customers behind Rs 47.34 Cr FY26 (system integrators reselling to government, private labs, or related entities) from the prospectus top-10 table, RPT note and group-entity section.
2. RECEIVABLE DAYS BASIS: the prospectus states debtor days 83 / 89 / 74 (FY24-26). Year-end receivables over sales give about 144 / 111 / 79. Verify which basis the prospectus uses and whether the improvement is real at year end.
3. CASH CONVERSION: CFO Rs -2.61 / +4.65 / +7.62 Cr against PAT Rs 2.83 / 8.56 / 13.51 Cr. Verify against the restated cash flow statement; IPO proceeds of Rs 31.42 Cr go to working capital.
4. DISCLOSURE CONDUCT: the 27-Sep-2026 Reg 30 filing promotes a third-party NFSU campus one day after a BSE price-movement query (26-Sep-2026). Check against the related-party, group-entity and litigation sections of the prospectus.
