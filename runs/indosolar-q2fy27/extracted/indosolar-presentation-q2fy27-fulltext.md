Commenting on the results Mr. Jignesh Rathod, Director and Chief Executive Officer said:

"The solar manufacturing industry is undergoing an important phase of transformation, driven by evolving regulatory
requirements, changing global supply-chain dynamics and India's increasing focus on domestic manufacturing.
While the current operating environment presents near-term challenges, we remain focused on strengthening our
manufacturing capabilities, improving operational efficiencies and positioning the business for long-term growth.

Our priority remains to navigate the current industry transition with operational discipline while building a more efficient
and future-ready manufacturing platform that can contribute to India's clean energy ambitions.

The proposed amalgamation with Waaree Energies marks an important strategic milestone in this journey. We believe
that closer integration with Waaree's manufacturing ecosystem can create meaningful operational synergies, enhance
competitiveness and establish a stronger foundation for sustainable growth."
