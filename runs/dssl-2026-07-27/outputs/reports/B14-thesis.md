## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** ROCE is high at 30.17% on current capital employed (EBIT Rs131.39cr / CE Rs435.47cr, deliberation Pillar 1). ROE is 26.9% (B11). EBITDA margin expanded from 4.2% (FY21) to 10.25% (FY26), and the full year FY26 margin rose from 8.31% to 10.25% YoY, independently validated against Acuité (B01, B05). Net debt is low at Rs68cr, D/E about 0.2x, and much of the debt like growth is Ind AS 116 lease liability matched to specific customer DaaS/CBaaS contracts, not discretionary corporate leverage (B04, B05). The Gate 0 GOOD cap comes from a mechanical cash deal breaker (Block B 4/20, FY18 and FY22 CFO negative years plus FY26 FCF negative on Rs65cr new premises capex), not from moat weakness (B01).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, B05):**
1. RBI relationship deepening (EAP about Rs249cr plus private cloud about Rs750.82cr, NGDC Bhubaneswar, 5 year tenor). Conviction H. Confirms: further large PSU/BFSI awards referencing NGDC class deployments. Kills: RBI order flow stalls or the award is delayed or cancelled.
2. NABARD CBaaS expansion to more cooperative banks (Haryana about 20 banks live, Telangana joining, 38 banks live). Conviction M to H. Confirms: incremental banks going live and rising CBaaS revenue. Kills: onboarding pace stalls or NABARD loses funding priority.
3. Margin normalisation after the Q4 FY26 9.02% dip (from 11.92% in Q3). Conviction M. Confirms: Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin recovers toward or above 11 to 12%. Kills: margin stays near 9% for two more quarters, confirming a structural shift.
4. Order book conversion: Rs2,964cr at 30-May-2026 (2.08x FY26 revenue) at an 18 to 24 month execution cycle, bidding pipeline Rs5,100cr at about 30% win rate. Conviction M. Confirms: book to bill above 1.3x sustained. Kills: book to bill below 1.0x for two consecutive quarters.
5. Data centre and cloud mix resuming its climb past 37%. Conviction M. Confirms: FY27 mix resumes upward. Kills: mix stagnates or reverses for two quarters.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Existing moats are qualification and tender eligibility barriers (CMMI5, ISO 27001), multi year embedded managed services and CBaaS switching costs, and a BFSI/PSU relationship network with repeat orders (B04, durability medium). Emerging moats (B07): qualification lock in (B2, Strong, documented), embedded customer ecosystem (C1, Moderate), execution moat (F2, Moderate, resolves Q1 FY27), working capital trajectory (G2, Moderate), strategic partnerships (H2, Moderate), regulatory tailwind (R1, Moderate, shared with peers). The moat is forming, not yet formed: EM 22.7 sits below the 25 threshold for a Pillar 3b moat premium, so the growth premium is paid only for order book visibility (Pillar 3a +3x), not for moat depth. Direction is expanding but unconfirmed.

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter verdict CAUTION (B08). No deal breaker level finding: no market ban, no criminal conviction, no live SFIO/PMLA, no auditor resignation, no confirmed pledge, no restatement. Credibility grade B (B05): delivered on its two largest concrete promises (RBI order conversion into the Rs750.82cr win, NABARD bank count expansion) and on the full year margin expansion narrative, independently corroborated by filed results. Held back from A by two call non disclosure of As a Service unit economics (IRR/ROCE/gross margin deflected every time), a scripted temporary blip response given verbatim to four analysts on the Q4 margin miss, and an unreconciled data centre mix reversal.

**3E. Market Opportunity.** SAM Rs70,548cr, DSSL current share 2.02%, revenue headroom 49.5x, TAM growth 11.2%, runway STRONG (B09). SOM implied revenue CAGR is 33.9% at year 3 and 30.6% at year 5, well anchored to the Rs2,964cr order book and Rs5,100cr pipeline at year 3, an extrapolation at year 5. The base case 18% revenue CAGR sits comfortably below the year 3 SOM 33.9%, so the growth thesis does not require share gain beyond what the order book already implies.

**3F. Peer Positioning.** Peers ADSL and Aurionpro confirm a real, sector wide India digital infrastructure and BFSI transformation tailwind and elevated gross debtor days (75 to 120 days) that soften but do not eliminate DSSL's 154 day debtor concern (B06). DSSL trades about 10.1x EV/EBITDA versus ADSL 8.9x and Aurionpro 14.9x (B11), mid pack. The valuation is not a demanding premium; on the governing RRM track the destination 24x sits above the current 18.5x, a re-rating setup rather than a paid up multiple.
