Six performance vectors tracked internally:

- "Our opex to AUM has already declined year-on-year from 4.76% in Q4FY25 to 4.13% at
  the end of Q4FY26. We have achieved a further reduction of 7 basis points to 4.06% in

- "As a result, we have achieved ₹308 crores in profit after tax, recording a 20.8%

===== PAGE 5 =====
Product-level update for the quarter:
Prime Personal Loan: monthly disbursements ~₹537 cr (vs ~₹440 cr in Q4); 38% of
disbursements fully straight-through digital (up from 33% in Q4, 28% before that).
Gold Loans: Q1FY27 disbursements ~₹875 cr (vs ₹890 cr prior quarter); 460 branches;
expanding into Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh; plan to add
~400 branches in FY27, mostly Tier 2/3.
Consumer Durable: Q1FY27 disbursements ₹433 cr; 17,300+ retail outlets across 339
locations; 58,000+ cases disbursed in a single month via digital capability.
PFIN EMI Card: presence across touchpoints, driving repeat usage.
Commercial Vehicle: monthly disbursement ~₹104 cr; 1,100+ channel partners (up from
900); 70+ locations across 30 states.
Education Loan: 55% QoQ disbursement growth in Q1, one year after launch; average
monthly disbursement ₹144 cr; 25 to 50 locations; 600+ partner consultant network; ~30%
of sanctions digital.

===== PAGE 6 =====
Arvind Kapil: "I believe it is the single most important aspect to understand about our
company. Two years ago, we set out to build not just a book but a long-term sustainable
franchise with sustainable profits with a simple conviction: Lasting franchises are
built by carefully calibrating risk-first approach."
"Our book has grown to approximately ₹67,000 crores of AUM at a GNPA of 1.37%... We did
not just launch 6 businesses. We built 6 growth engines mounted on a single chassis."
Three differentiators named: (1) bench depth, two levels deep in every new business,
hired from proven operators; (2) digital and AI native by design, not bolted on; (3)
portfolio deliberately diversified across distinct macro drivers (CV, gold, LAP,
business loan, education, CD, Prime PL) so the businesses "don't rise and fall
together."
