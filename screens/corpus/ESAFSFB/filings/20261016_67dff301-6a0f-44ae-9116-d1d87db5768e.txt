Sub: Upgradation of Core Banking System of ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited  
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform that ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited (the “Bank”), as 
part of its ongoing digital transformation programme and continued commitment to leveraging 
technology for enhanced customer experience and operational excellence, is implementing 
‘Oracle FLEXCUBE’ as its new Core Banking System (CBS), along with various business 
applications and other allied applications.  
 
The new CBS system and applications are intended to strengthen the Bank’s technology 
architecture by enhancing operational efficiency, system resilience, scalability, service 
capabilities and further strengthen the Bank’s risk management and regulatory compliance 
framework. They are also expected to support the introduction of innovative products and 
services, reduce time-to-market, improve service delivery across customer touchpoints. 
 
In this connection, we wish to inform that the migration of the Bank’s existing systems and 
applications to the new CBS, business applications and other allied applications, is scheduled 
to be undertaken from October 23 (Friday), 2026 (06:00 PM) to October 26 (Monday), 2026 
(10:00 AM). 
 
During the migration period, banking services, including ATM, Internet Banking, Mobile 
Banking, Debit Cards, Agency Banking and UPI services, will remain temporarily 
unavailable. To minimize inconvenience, the Bank has undertaken extensive customer 
communication through various channels and has advised customers to plan their 
transactions in advance. 
 
The Bank is taking all necessary measures to ensure a smooth migration and will endeavour 
to restore all services at the earliest upon successful completion of the migration activity.
