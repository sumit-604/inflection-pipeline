**FINAL CLASSIFICATION: AVERAGE**

Since classification ≤ AVERAGE, a FLAG-GATE0 is raised (see YAML below)
citing the historical depressors: declining ROCE/ROE trend into FY26,
EBITDA margin compression from 10.56% (FY24) to 5.50% (FY26), the CARE

(2.43x), and a moat profile with only 3 of 12 tests present (all peer-

AARTISURF being the smallest and lowest-margin name in its peer set).
Per CLAUDE.md, low institutional ownership (FII+DII ~0.08%, Jun 2026) is
explicitly NOT treated as a risk factor here — it is a UA-multiplier
qualifier for a later stage, not a Gate 0 depressor.

**Decision line**: Gate 0 mechanical scoring produces AVERAGE on a Core of
55/100 and a MODERATE moat profile (3/12 tests present, all margin-/growth-
side; every peer-relative test — cost, brand, scale — scored zero). This is
not a halt (no STOP verdict exists in this pipeline); it proceeds to
