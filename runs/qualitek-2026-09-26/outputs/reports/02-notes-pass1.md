## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- Both standalone and consolidated FS are prepared under **Indian GAAP** (AS, not Ind AS) — the Company is not yet Ind-AS mandated (small SME-listed entity, net worth threshold). (Note 2.1 consol p.108/[page 114]; Note 2.1 standalone p.161/[page 167]). 🟢 Clean, appropriate for size, but means Ind AS 116 lease-on-balance-sheet accounting and Ind AS 103 business-combination fair-valuing do NOT apply — leases are accounted the old operating/finance-lease way (Note 31 consol, Note 39 standalone) and business combinations are accounted at **cost less net assets acquired = goodwill**, not at fair-value-of-identifiable-assets (AS 21 practice, not Ind AS 103 PPA). This matters for LBF1: no PPA (purchase price allocation) intangible recognition (customer relationships, accreditation value) is required or done; the entire premium over book value sits in goodwill. 🟡 Watch — flag for stage 11/valuation: goodwill quality cannot be assessed via a PPA breakdown because none exists under AS 21.
- **Revenue recognition**: "completed service contract method" — revenue booked only when the test report is issued to the customer (Note 2.4-A consol, Note 2.4-A standalone). No percentage-of-completion, no straight-lining. Conservative and appropriate for a TIC (testing/inspection/certification) business; short cycle stated as reason. 🟢 Clean.
- **Goodwill amortisation policy is bifurcated and unusual**: "Purchased Goodwill" (i.e., goodwill from an asset/slump-sale acquisition, not a share acquisition) is amortised over an estimated 5-year useful life per AS 26 (Note 2.4-C(iii)(a) consol, p.103/[page 109]). "Goodwill on Consolidation" (i.e., goodwill from acquiring an equity stake in a subsidiary) is treated as having an **indefinite life and is NOT amortised**, only tested annually for impairment (Note 2.4-C(iii)(b)). This is the AS 21 convention (goodwill on consolidation is not amortised under Indian GAAP), so it is technically correct, but the two goodwill pools behave very differently for future P&L: the ₹219.86 Lakh purchased goodwill (food-testing lab, Note 11b) WILL amortise ~₹44 Lakh/yr through P&L; the ₹7,811.56 Lakh goodwill on consolidation (ITCPL, QTIPL, LabOps) will NOT amortise unless impaired. 🟡 Watch — the dominant goodwill pool (97% of total) never runs through the P&L unless management calls an impairment; investors should not expect amortisation drag on the big number.
- **No impairment test disclosure with quantified assumptions.** Note 40 (consol) / Note 32 (standalone): "No disclosure is required with regard to Impairment of Assets, since the Group has no indication of a potential impairment loss at present." This is boilerplate — no discount rate, no growth rate, no CGU-level value-in-use walkthrough, despite goodwill on consolidation of ₹7,811.56 Lakh (54% of consolidated net worth of ₹15,242.60 Lakh) sitting on the books after two years of rapid, partly related-party-funded acquisition. (Note 39/40 consol p.137/[page 143]; Note 2.4-D policy text describes methodology in the abstract but no company-specific numbers appear anywhere in the notes). 🔴 Red Flag — thin disclosure relative to the size and recency of the goodwill; a downstream stage should treat consolidated ROCE/ROE ex-goodwill separately from goodwill-inclusive figures.
- **Depreciation useful lives** (Note 2.4-B(vi) consol, same standalone): Plant & machinery 15 yrs, Computers/mobiles 3-5 yrs, Office equipment 5 yrs, Vehicles 8-10 yrs, Office furniture 10 yrs — all match Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013 (no company override to longer lives that would flatter margins). 🟢 Clean.
- **Ind AS 116 / lease discount rate**: N/A — company is on AS (IGAAP), operating leases stay off balance sheet (only future minimum lease payment disclosure, Note 31/39); finance leases are on balance sheet at PV of minimum lease payments discounted at the lease's implicit rate or incremental borrowing rate (rate itself not disclosed numerically). 🟡 Watch (rate not disclosed — cannot audit the PV maths independently).
- **No first-time standard adoption** in FY26; company continues on IGAAP (no Ind AS 116/109/115 transition note found). 🟢 Clean (nothing new to disclose).
- **No revaluation of PPE** during the year, either standalone or consolidated (Note 39(i) consol, Note 35(ii) standalone). 🟢 Clean — no asset-revaluation earnings management.
- **Re-groupings/reclassifications flagged by management itself**: (a) Advance Income Tax re-grouped from Short-term Loans & Advances to Other Non-Current Assets in the current year (Note 13(iii) and Note 17 consol notes; standalone equivalent in Note 14/18); (b) Recoverable from employees re-grouped from Short-term Loans and Advances to Other Current Assets (Note 18 consol). Both are presentation-only reclasses, immaterial in amount (₹226 Lakh and ₹18 Lakh respectively), disclosed transparently. 🟢 Clean but noted for completeness — a downstream stage should not read these as restatements of substance.
