### Method 2, bottom-up (nuclear equipment WIL is qualified for)
- Addressable unit: one 700 MW PHWR plant. WIL claims ₹1,000 Cr of qualified equipment per plant (Inv. Pres. p.26).
- Units in the NPCIL pipeline. The deck table gives 9,800 MW under construction (14 x 700), 5,600 MW at existing sites (2 x 700 per site), 2,800 MW Bhimpur, 7,000 MW second fleet (Inv. Pres. p.29, PDF). The deck's totals run to 2047 and are not a five-year order window.
- Web cross-check: the Mahi Banswara nuclear-island package is an EPC tender above ₹28,000 Cr for four 700 MW units (Swarajya and World Nuclear News, 2026; tender still being prepared in mid-2026). The ten-unit fleet is quoted at ₹1.05 lakh Cr, about ₹10,500 Cr per unit (AngelOne, ahead of Budget 2026).
- Cross-check arithmetic: WIL's ₹1,000 Cr equals 9.5% of ₹10,500 Cr per unit and 14.3% of the ₹7,000 Cr per-unit nuclear-island package (28,000 / 4). Plausible in scale, not proven.
- Conservative: 4 units (Mahi Banswara, the only tendered package) x ₹1,000 Cr = ₹4,000 Cr over 5 years = ₹800 Cr per year.
- Realistic: 10 units (the full fleet programme) x ₹1,000 Cr = ₹10,000 Cr over 5 years = ₹2,000 Cr per year.
- Two readings: 4 units against 10. The separating observation is the NPCIL award calendar for calandria and end shield packages for units 5 to 10, and whether packages for Kaiga 5-6, Gorakhpur 3-4 and Chutka were already placed (NOT FOUND). Central estimate: the 4-unit reading, because it alone has a tender behind it.
- Excluded: 50 BSR plants x ₹700 Cr = ₹35,000 Cr (deck p.28). No BSR order exists, a consultative workshop was held on 22 Jan (web, 2026) and the technology timeline sits beyond five years. Shown as optionality, not TAM.
