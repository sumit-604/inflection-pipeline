## X3. Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 4.9% versus about 8.2%

Source status: the Q1 FY27 results filing is NOT IN CORPUS (no results/
folder; inputs/screening/screener-Quarters.csv carries labels only, no
values). The only corpus source for Q1 FY27 is the Aug-2026 call.

> "Revenue from operations increased to INR304 crores in Q1 '27 from INR263
> crores in corresponding quarter last year ... Combined gross margin
> improved by approximately 3% points to 33% in Q1 '27 ... EBITDA improved to
> INR25 crores in Q1 of '27 compared with EBITDA of INR10 crores in Q1 of
> '26." (Manish Dhanuka, Aug-2026 call, transcript p.3-4)

- The 8.2% [computed] = Rs 25 cr / Rs 304 cr, management's EBITDA on
  revenue from operations. This is the 09b "combined" figure.
- Management gives no definition of EBITDA on the call. [INFERENCE] It
  includes other income. The FY26 reported EBITDA of Rs 94.27 cr rebuilds
  exactly from PBT 3,462.00 + exceptional 733.29 + finance cost 896.15 +
  depreciation 4,335.60 (SA cash flow, FY26 AR PDF p.213), and that PBT
  carries other income of 5,648.69 (SA Note 32, PDF p.247). The same
  convention on Q1 FY27 would put other income inside the Rs 25 cr.
- The 4.9% (dossier) implies about Rs 14.9 cr operating EBITDA, so about
  Rs 10 cr of other income in the quarter [INFERENCE from the two figures].
  The Q1 FY27 other income line is NOT DISCLOSED in the corpus; it sits in
  the Q1 FY27 results filing (statement of profit and loss, "Other income").
- Both figures are "combined" (Orchid plus merged Dhanuka). Whether either
  is standalone or consolidated is NOT DISCLOSED on the call.
- Management's FY26 gross margin on the call is "32% ... compared to
  approximately 36% in financial year '25" (transcript p.3). The dossier's
  31.5% FY26 uses the filed figures; the two differ by rounding or basis.
