### 2A Accounting policy aggressiveness — the central new finding of this stage

**The AR's own disclosed "Net Profit Margin" does not reconcile with the AR's own Statement of Profit & Loss,
and the true, directly-computed figure moves in the opposite direction from what is disclosed.**

Three separate locations in this AR all state Net Profit Margin FY25 = **12.00%**, up from **11.71%** FY24:
- Note 38(vi), Ratios: "Net profit ratio | Net Profit / Sales | % | 12.00 | 11.71 | 2% [change]" (p.185).
- MD&A, Section 10, Key Financial Ratios: identical figures, identical "Reason: -" i.e. no explanation offered for the change (p.85).
- MD&A opening operational table also shows PAT 2,544 / Total Operating Revenue 26,182 in the same section (p.84) — the exact inputs needed to compute the ratio.

Applying the note's own stated formula (Net Profit / Sales) to the AR's own Statement of Profit & Loss
(p.130) and Note 22 Revenue from Operations (p.159):
- FY25: PAT ₹2,544.17 lakh / Revenue from Operations ₹26,181.50 lakh = **9.72%**
- FY24: PAT ₹2,409.78 lakh / Revenue from Operations ₹24,116.53 lakh = **9.99%**

This computed figure **declines** from 9.99% to 9.72% (-0.27pp, -2.7% relative) — the opposite direction and
magnitude from the disclosed 11.71%→12.00% (+0.29pp, +2.5% relative, which is the "change in %" figure the
note itself prints). The computed figures are independently corroborated by a **fourth** location in the same
AR: the front-matter "Operating Performance Highlights" KPI chart (p.4) shows **PAT Margin 10.00% (FY24) →
9.72% (FY25)** — matching this stage's from-scratch computation to two decimal places and confirming the
decline, not the disclosed "improvement." No reconciling note, footnote, or alternate ratio definition
(e.g., ratio computed on Total Income, on a pre-exceptional basis, or on domestic-only sales) was found
anywhere in the AR that bridges the ~2.3 percentage-point gap between what Note 38(vi)/MD&A state and what
the primary financial statements and the front-matter chart both independently show.

**Why this matters more than a rounding error**: B02's Pattern 3 (the pipeline's most consequential Notes
finding) characterized FY25 as "margin improved from 11.71% to 12.00%, but the improvement is substantially a
mechanical inventory effect masking real cost inflation." Read against the AR's own front-matter chart and
this stage's direct P&L computation, **PAT margin did not improve at all — it declined**, from 10.00% to
9.72%. This does not soften B02's underlying concern; it **sharpens** it. The Employee Benefit (+29.7%) and
Other Expense (+38.1%) inflation documented in B02 was severe enough that even with the raw-material
inventory-build cushion fully in place, reported PAT margin still fell. The AR's Note 38(vi)/MD&A disclosure
of an "improving" margin is the figure that does not survive contact with the AR's own numbers; this is a
genuine, unreconciled, internal disclosure-quality flag, not a resolution in the company's favour. (Note: the
Return on Equity figure in the same table, 17.65% vs 20.15%, was independently sanity-checked this stage —
PAT/closing equity computes to 17.82%, a ~0.9pp gap plausibly explained by an averaging convention; this is a
far smaller, non-directional gap and is not elevated as a separate flag.)

Other 2A observations (revenue recognition, capitalisation, depreciation, ECL, Ind AS 116): consistent with
B02's characterisation — no aggressive revenue recognition (ex-works dispatch-point recognition per KAM
response, p.115), no unusual capitalisation, no first-time policy adoption or restatement.
