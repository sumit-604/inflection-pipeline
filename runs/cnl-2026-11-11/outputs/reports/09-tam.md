# Stage 9: TAM / SAM / SOM, Creative Newtech Ltd (CNL), run 2026-11-11

Status: partial (searches skipped, listed in the Search Log). Units: Rs Cr unless stated. FX used: Rs 95 per USD, so USD 1 bn = Rs 9,500 Cr (INR closed 94.93 on 30 Oct 2026, PTI via hdfcsky.com; latest close found, none for November).

Revenue anchor: FY26 consolidated revenue from operations Rs 2,70,461.72 lakh = Rs 2,704.62 Cr (AR FY2025-26, consolidated statement of profit and loss, line "Revenue from Operations" 2,70,461.72 vs 1,78,275.38 lakh in FY25). Geography split (AR Note 23.2B, consolidated): India Rs 1,79,713.32 lakh = Rs 1,797.13 Cr (66.4%); outside India Rs 90,263.93 lakh = Rs 902.64 Cr (33.4%), plus export entitlements Rs 4.84 Cr. FY25 India was Rs 678.41 Cr, so India grew 164.9% while outside India fell 17.7%. Both calculated from the Note.

## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

1A. Boundaries.
- Product scope (sized): laptop accessories, PC-gaming peripherals, surveillance (cameras, recorders), structured cabling and fibre.
- Product scope (in CNL's revenue but NOT SIZED, no market figure found): memory and storage, imaging, networking equipment, cybersecurity software, drones. These are excluded from every TAM below, so TAM and SAM are understated and CNL's share is overstated (the numerator includes them).
- Geography: India only. CNL's Rs 902.64 Cr outside-India revenue (Honeywell about 29 countries via Hong Kong; AR Note 23.2B) has no market sizing and is held flat in the company-level roll-up.
- Customer scope: consumers, SMB and enterprise buyers served through resellers; direct government projects excluded.
- Channel scope: distribution and brand sales through partners (10,000+ partners, 300+ cities; B04). Market reports measure end-user value; CNL books wholesale revenue, so the two are not like-for-like.
- Inclusions/exclusions: BSNL BharatNet middle-mile (about Rs 3,194.83 Cr order, B05) is a network build project, outside distribution. Excluded from SAM; kept as a separate management line.

1B. Management's claim (Q2 FY27 presentation, slides 10, 20-24; undated source links labelled "link", no publisher named).
- India CCTV USD 4.8 bn (2025) to 12.25 bn (2030), 20.60% CAGR = Rs 45,600 Cr (calc: 4.8 x 9,500).
- India data centre USD 10.11 bn (2025) to 21.80 bn (2030), 16.61% CAGR = Rs 96,045 Cr.
- India AI USD 22.85 bn (2025) to 325.34 bn (2033) = Rs 2,17,075 Cr.
- India IoT USD 2.89 bn (2024) to 10.28 bn (2030) = Rs 27,455 Cr.
- Sum of the four = USD 40.65 bn = Rs 3,86,175 Cr (calc; mixed base years, overlapping, so not a valid TAM).
- Q2 FY26/Q2 FY27 calls (B05): surveillance about Rs 30,000 Cr growing 17-18%.
- FY29 revenue target Rs 6,000 Cr, "30% CAGR", 60% market entry / 40% brand (slide 10). Check: 6,000 / 2,704.62 = 2.218; cube root 1.304 = 30.4%.
- Credibility read: BROAD. The AI and IoT markets are software and services pools CNL does not sell into. Slides 23-24 carry copy errors (IoT text says "India Ai Market"; captions read "India Data Centre Market Size"). No CNL revenue is attributed to any of them (B05: no revenue split disclosed).

## SECTION 2: TAM ESTIMATION

Method 1, top-down (ceiling only). Mordor Intelligence, India IT hardware: USD 21.17 bn in 2025, USD 22.61 bn in 2026, 6.78% CAGR to 2031 (via web search result; consumer 54.62% of 2025 revenue). 21.17 x 9,500 = Rs 2,01,115 Cr. The category split (servers, storage, enterprise PCs, printers) was NOT FOUND, so sourced subtractions cannot be made. This is a ceiling, not an estimate. The bottom-up conservative TAM is 20.7% of it (41,675 / 2,01,115).

Method 2, bottom-up by category (third-party category sizes summed; a units x price build was not possible: IDC gives 15.9 mn PC units in 2025, +10.2%, but a peripheral spend per PC was NOT FOUND).
| Category | USD bn | Rs Cr (x9,500) | CAGR | Source (year) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop accessories | 1.27373 | 12,100 | 6.79% (2026-34) | Deep Market Insights (2025) |
| Gaming peripherals | 0.531 | 5,045 | 5.95% (to 2034) | IMARC (2025) |
| Surveillance, conservative | 1.74 | 16,530 | 8.0% (to 2030) | MarketsandMarkets, India video surveillance (2025) |
| Surveillance, realistic | 4.80 | 45,600 | 20.6% (to 2030) | Research and Markets / Mordor via Inc42 / CNL deck (2025) |
| Structured cabling | 0.84176 | 8,000 | 8.6% (2025-26) | IMARC via secondary source (2025) |
Conservative TAM = 12,100 + 5,045 + 16,530 + 8,000 = Rs 41,675 Cr. Realistic TAM = 12,100 + 5,045 + 45,600 + 8,000 = Rs 70,745 Cr.
Overlaps not netted: docking stations (USD 0.2685 bn, Rs 2,551 Cr) and data-centre cables (USD 0.44 bn, Rs 4,180 Cr) are left out as likely subsets of the above.

Surveillance divergence (no shading). Other estimates found: Ken Research electronic security USD 3.05 bn, IMARC video surveillance systems USD 7.5 bn, camera hardware alone USD 2.13 bn (Rs 20,235 Cr). The range is 4.3x (1.74 to 7.5). The 4.8 bn figure appears in three places but traces to one aggregator family, not three independent measures. What separates the estimates is scope (hardware vs systems and services) and no publisher gives a methodology in the excerpts found. Evidence leaning the other way: Aditya Infotech (CP Plus) revenue grew 35.6% in FY26 (Rs 3,112 Cr to Rs 4,220.8 Cr), faster than 8%, though share gain explains part. I carry the conservative figure as central for SAM and SOM because its category (video surveillance, narrower) is closest to what CNL distributes and 20.6% is uncorroborated; the realistic case is run as a sensitivity in 3B.

Method 3, peer aggregation (low confidence).
- Rashi Peripherals FY26 Rs 15,827 Cr (+14.9%; prior Rs 13,773 Cr from INR 137,727 mn); Aditya Infotech FY26 Rs 4,220.8 Cr; CNL Rs 2,704.62 Cr. Sum = Rs 22,752 Cr.
- Redington FY26 Rs 1,19,347 Cr is global (Middle East, Africa); India split NOT FOUND, so excluded.
- Unorganised/other-distributor gross-up, an illustrative range of 30% to 60% (analyst estimate, no source found): 22,752 / 0.70 = Rs 32,503 Cr; 22,752 / 0.40 = Rs 56,881 Cr.
- Scope mismatch: Rashi includes PCs, GPUs and other lines outside the sized categories; peer revenue is wholesale. This checks order of magnitude only.

Method 4, import substitution: skipped (CNL is an importer and brand owner, not a domestic producer; no consumption/production data sought). Method 5, global benchmark: skipped (no per-capita data searched). Two to three methods used: 1 (ceiling), 2, 3.

## SECTION 3: SAM & SOM

3A. SAM (conservative base, 50% channel filter; the 50% is an analyst assumption with no source, so confidence is LOW; sensitivity below).
| Category | Market | Filters applied | SAM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop accessories | 12,100 | Product fit 100% (bag/stand split NOT FOUND, so overstated); channel 50% | 6,050 |
| Gaming peripherals | 5,045 | PC-gaming 70% (IMARC); channel 50% (5,045 x 0.70 x 0.50) | 1,766 |
| Surveillance | 16,530 | Hardware sold through partner channel 50%; direct government and services excluded | 8,265 |

SAM = 6,050 + 1,766 + 8,265 + 4,000 = Rs 20,081 Cr = 48.2% of conservative TAM (28.4% of realistic). Sensitivity: channel 30% gives Rs 12,049 Cr; 70% gives Rs 28,113 Cr. Geography filter already applied (India only); customer filter not applied (no split of CNL sales by customer type found; B04: top-customer share NOT FOUND); capability filter applied through product fit.
Realistic-case SAM (surveillance 45,600 x 50% = 22,800): 6,050 + 1,766 + 22,800 + 4,000 = Rs 34,616 Cr.

3B. SOM.
- Current share: India revenue 1,797.13 / 20,081 = 8.95%. Overstated (numerator includes memory, storage, imaging, networking, software; wholesale vs retail basis). Against the Method 1 ceiling the India share is 0.89% (1,797.13 / 2,01,115). Carry 8.95% as the SAM-definition share.
- SAM growth (conservative): (6,050 x 6.79 + 1,766 x 5.95 + 8,265 x 8.0 + 4,000 x 8.6) / 20,081 = 152,108 / 20,081 = 7.57%. TAM growth on the same weights: 313,217 / 41,675 = 7.52%.
- SAM FY29 (3 yrs) = 20,081 x 1.0757^3 = Rs 24,995 Cr. SAM FY31 (5 yrs) = 20,081 x 1.0757^5 = Rs 28,923 Cr.
- Share trajectory: +4pp in 3 years (aggressive band 3-5pp: India share rose 5.6pp in FY26 per the Note 23.2B shift, partly sales reclassified from export to domestic; the Infinova and Zicom acquisitions are inorganic); +5pp by year 5. The >5pp rule is not met without competitor exit; unorganised share >40% is not evidenced.
- SOM 3-yr = 24,995 x 12.95% = Rs 3,237 Cr. SOM 5-yr = 28,923 x 13.95% = Rs 4,035 Cr.
- Implied India revenue CAGR: yr3 = (3,237 / 1,797.13)^(1/3) - 1 = 1.8012^(0.3333) - 1 = 21.7%. yr5 = (4,035 / 1,797.13)^(0.2) - 1 = 2.2452^(0.2) - 1 = 17.6%.
- Company-level roll-up (outside India held flat at 902.64 Cr; no market data): FY29 = 3,237 + 903 = 4,140; CAGR = (4,140 / 2,704.62)^(1/3) - 1 = 15.3%. FY31 = 4,035 + 903 = 4,938; CAGR = (4,938 / 2,704.62)^(0.2) - 1 = 12.8%.
- Sensitivity, realistic SAM (34,616 at 16.05% weighted growth, 1,120,337/70,745 gives TAM growth 15.8%): SAM FY29 = Rs 54,102 Cr; current share 5.19%; +4pp = 9.19%; SOM = Rs 4,972 Cr; India CAGR 40.4%.
- Management FY29 Rs 6,000 Cr test: if outside India stays 903, India must reach 5,097 Cr = 20.4% of conservative SAM FY29 (+11.4pp, breaks the >5pp rule) or 9.4% of the realistic SAM (+4.2pp, aggressive band). The target works only if the high surveillance growth figure holds.

3C. Capacity cross-check. B07 capex-embedded growth: NOT FOUND (fabless; FY26 capex Rs 3.30 Cr). Physical capacity is not a constraint; funding is. Gap between management's FY29 target and the central SOM at company level: 6,000 - 4,140 = Rs 1,860 Cr; management's plan is the optimistic side. Working capital to fund the gap at 52-day NWC (B05, normal 52-55 days): 3,295 Cr incremental revenue (6,000 - 2,704.62) x 52 / 365 = Rs 469 Cr; at receivables 21.4% of revenue (B05) it is 3,295 x 0.214 = Rs 705 Cr. B05 says no equity raise for 15 months unless a deal near 30% of size. Management FY28 additions of Rs 800-950 Cr (BSNL 500-600 + Infinova 200 + WOZOYO 100-150, B05; B07) include BSNL project billing that sits outside the SAM, and BSNL carries about Rs 1,700 Cr of capex (B04 flag) with funding undisclosed.

## SECTION 4: GROWTH DRIVERS, RISKS & STRUCTURE

4A. Drivers.
- Penetration/new applications: Indian PC shipments a record 15.9 mn in 2025, +10.2% (IDC, Mar 2026); commercial 52.9%. Impact medium.
- Regulatory/import substitution: mandatory STQC testing for CCTV and Make-in-India push (deck slide 21); favours firms with STQC supply (Infinova pending). Impact high on surveillance, uncertain on CNL (no revenue yet).
- Technology enablement/new applications: AI surveillance, data-centre cabling (DC cables USD 0.44 bn, +17.9%, DataM); CNL revenue from either NOT FOUND.
- Premiumisation/price: PC prices up over 10% YoY on component costs (IDC), inflating nominal TAM.
- Formalisation/geographic: unorganised share NOT FOUND; no evidence.

4B. Risks, with monitoring signals.
- Cyclical downturn: IDC expects 2026 shipments about -5% (signal: IDC quarterly tracker).
- Component cost inflation (memory, storage, CPU +30-40% per management, B05): demand destruction (signal: Rashi, Redington margins).
- Grey-market imports in peripherals, -0.4% impact short term (Mordor).
- Regulatory: STQC could cut supply of non-certified cameras (signal: MeitY/STQC notices).
- Principal or licence loss: Honeywell renewal Mar-Aug 2027 (B05).
- Currency: INR near 95 per USD raises import costs.

4C. Structure.
- Named scale: Redington Rs 1,19,347 Cr (global), Rashi Rs 15,827 Cr, Aditya Infotech Rs 4,220.8 Cr (about 86% own brand), CNL Rs 2,704.62 Cr. Top-3 concentration for the sized categories: NOT FOUND.
- PC vendors: HP 29.1%, Lenovo 18.7%, Dell 15.1%, Acer 15.0%, ASUS 7.6% = 85.5% top-5 (IDC 2025). Vendor concentration, not distribution concentration.
- Organised vs unorganised, entries/exits, import share trend: NOT FOUND. Consolidating versus fragmenting: not determinable from sources found. CNL has weak pricing power (B04).

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY & RUNWAY

5A. Funnel (conservative case, India):
Management claim Rs 3,86,175 Cr > ceiling Rs 2,01,115 Cr (Mordor IT hardware) > realistic TAM Rs 70,745 Cr > conservative TAM Rs 41,675 Cr > SAM Rs 20,081 Cr > SOM FY29 Rs 3,237 Cr (FY31 Rs 4,035 Cr) > current India revenue Rs 1,797.13 Cr.

5B. Runway.
- Headroom = SAM / India revenue = 20,081 / 1,797.13 = 11.2x (7.4x against total revenue 2,704.62).
- TAM growth 7.5%; company FY26 total revenue growth 51.7% (270,461.72 / 178,275.38 = 1.517) and India 164.9%, so it is gaining share, partly via credit-funded growth (B05 grade C).
- Years to saturate SAM at FY26 pace: ln(11.17) / ln(1.517 / 1.0757) = 2.414 / 0.344 = 7.0 years; a single-year extrapolation, not a forecast.

5C. Class: GOOD. Headroom is large but the SAM filter is an unsourced assumption, TAM growth is 7.5% on the conservative case, pricing power is weak, and the matrix text was not in my instruction file (classification by judgement).

5D. SAM expansion levers pursued: surveillance (Matrix, Sparsh, Infinova pending, Zicom SaaS), data-centre cabling, WOZOYO own brand. Un-sized, so no revised headroom is stated (NOT FOUND for memory, storage, networking, imaging; surveillance realistic case lifts SAM to Rs 34,616 Cr and headroom to 19.3x: 34,616 / 1,797.13).

5E. Output card. At 15.3% revenue CAGR (company-level, 3-yr) implied by SOM, with PAT margin held at the FY26 level of 2.6% (70.29 Cr PAT per B05, over 2,704.62), the earnings growth embedded here is 15.3% CAGR, which cannot be tested against the current valuation of NOT FOUND x P/E (no price or multiple in the inputs). Management's 30% profit growth on 15.3% revenue growth would need the PAT margin to rise about 12.7% a year (1.30 / 1.153), to roughly 3.7% in year 3; B04 shows no operating leverage so far.
