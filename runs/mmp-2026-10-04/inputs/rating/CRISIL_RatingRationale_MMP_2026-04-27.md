Detailed r ationale
Due to inadequate information and in line with the S ecurities and E xchange B oard of I ndia guidelines, Crisil Ratings had migrated the rating s on the bank facilities of MMP Industries Ltd (MMPIL ; part of the MMPIL group ) to ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2 Issuer N ot C ooperating' . However, the company’s management has subsequently shared the information necessary for a comprehensive review of the rating s . Consequently, Crisil Ratings is migrating the rating s to ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’ .
The ratings reflect the established position of the group and the extensive experience of the promoters in the aluminium powder products industry, an established clientele, efficient working capital management and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offs et by vulnerability to volatility in raw material prices.
Analytical a pproach
Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of MMPIL and its subsidiaries . This is because these entities, collectively referred to as the MMPIL group , are strategically important to , and have significant operational linkages with , MMPIL .

Key r ating d rivers - Strengths
Established market position and healthy clientele: The group has diversified presence in aluminium powder, aluminium foils and conductor cables , and an established clientele consisting of UltraTech Cement Ltd, Shree Cement Ltd , Torrent Pharma ceuticals Ltd and Cadila Pharmaceuticals Ltd . Owing to diversified presence and well-established clientele, operating income is estimated to have grown by around 20% on-year in fiscal 2026 from Rs 691 crore in fiscal 2025.
Extensive experience of the promoters: The pro moters have been in the aluminium powder products industry for over three decades. Arun Bhandari, the key promoter, is a chemical engineer and has significant technical expertise and capabilities. Over the years, the promoters have upgraded technology through continuous research and development and leveraged this to build a strong clientele.
Efficient working capital management: The working capital cycle is managed efficiently, as reflected in gross current assets (GCAs) of around 1 26 days as on March 31 , 2025, as against 1 09 days a year earlier . The GCAs are estimated to have increase d slightly as on March 31, 2026 . While receivables were small at 48 days as on March 31, 2025 , inventory was large at 78 days. The GCAs are likely to improve over the medium term.
Healthy financial risk profile : Capital structure is supported by healthy networth and low total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio of Rs 323 crore and 0.7 0 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2025, which are estimated at similar levels as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection metrics were comfortable, as reflected in interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 6. 78 times and 0.2 8 time, respectively, in fiscal 2025 and are estimated to be slightly lower in fiscal 2026 owing to increase in finance cost . The group has planned solar expansion which will cut energy cost and boost the operating margin, which will be partially funded. Furthermore, it is expected to enhance its cable range to include low tension (LT) power in phases in the subsidiary , MMP Cables Pvt Ltd, which will entail capital expenditure ( capex) of Rs 8 5 -90 crore over the next 2 -3 years . The first investment of Rs 4 0 crore will be made in fiscal s 20 27 and 2028 , which will be partly debt - funded. The financial risk p rofile will likely remain healthy over the medium term despite debt-funded capex over the medium term.
Key r ating d rivers - We a kness es
Vulnerability to volatility in raw material prices: Cost of raw material, such as aluminium ingots and foils, accounts for 75-80% of sales; the prices of raw materials are volatile and governed by demand-supply dynamics . T he group is able to pass on increase in prices with lag of 3-4 months. The operating margin was 6.55-9.29% over the three fiscals through 2025 and is estimated to be slightly lower in fiscal 2026 from fiscal 2025 level owing to higher input costs, direct and indirect costs from the fire i ncident resulting in extraordinary expenses of Rs 17.3 crore , and higher operational costs in the subsidiary MMP Electricals Ltd owing to initial year s of operation. The operating margin is likely to improve over the medium term with higher capacity utilisation and backward integration in wire rod manufacturing , which is expected to lower external dependence and ensure stable supply chain. Improvement in the operating margin over the medium term will remain monitorable.
Liquidity Adequate
Expected cash accrual of Rs 31-50 crore per fiscal will comfortably cover yearly debt obligation of Rs 8-15 c rore over the medium term. Fund-based bank limit was utilised 89% on average during the 12 months through March 2026. Current ratio was healthy at 1 . 38 times as on March 31, 2025. C ash and bank balance was Rs 10.95 crore as on that date .
Outlook Stable
Crisil Ratings believes MMPIL will continue to benefit from its established market position and the extensive experience of the promoters.
Rating s ensitivity factors
Upward factors:
Steady growth in revenue, backed by volume growth, and stable operating margin leading to cash accrual above Rs 40 crore
Sustained working capital cycle and financial risk profile
Downward factors:
Decline in volume sales leading to fall in revenue or operating margin, resulting in net cash accrual of less than Rs 20 crore
Stretched working capital cycle or any large, debt-funded capex weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity
About the g roup
MMPIL was incorporated in 1984 by Arun Bhandari and P M Lodha. Based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, the company manufactures aluminium-based products such as pyro and flake aluminum powder, atomised aluminium powder, aluminium paste and aluminium conductors. It is listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.
MMP Electricals Pvt Ltd , a wholly owned subsidiary , manufactur es composite insulators . It started commercial production in phase 1 in f iscal 2026.
MMP Cables Pvt Ltd is a wholly owne d subsidiary . It is setting up a g reenfield l ow v oltage p ower c able and c overed c onductor facility at Umred MIDC .
MMP Alutech Pvt Ltd is wholly owned subsidiary and has no operations as on date .
