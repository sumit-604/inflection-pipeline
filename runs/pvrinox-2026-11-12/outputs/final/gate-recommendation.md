## Monitorables and triggers (8)

1. Q3 FY27 admissions against price. In the February 2027 results and presentation, check admissions growth against the Q3 FY26 base of 40.5 million and ATP against Rs 293, next to Ormax industry box office. Admissions near +4% to +8% with ATP above +10% while the industry grows above 20% means price led share loss. Admissions near +15% with ATP growth falling supports the content mix reading. This tests whether the recovery is footfall or price.
2. FY27 gross screen openings. H2 needs 66 to 76 openings to reach 90 to 100. Read Reg 30 opening filings and the Q4 FY27 presentation. Below 85 gross, or another cut, breaks the expansion chain. Also check that the signed capital light pipeline is restated against 138 screens (Q4 FY26 call p.9). A drop beyond normal conversions supports the developer problem reading of the kickback matter.
3. Capital light and SMART unit economics. Management promised them by FY27 year end (Q1 FY27 call p.13). Look for what asset light and FOCO screens book in revenue, the fee rate, who funds fit-out, and SMART ATP near Rs 190 with a 3 to 4 year payback across 2 to 3 more cinemas by March 2027. This tests whether screen growth is revenue bearing growth.
4. Lease cash and the lease write back. In the half year and annual cash flow and Notes 18 and 25, lease cash should stay at or below Rs 12,904 million for FY27 and the write back at or below Rs 871 million. Cash after lease payments is the real free cash, and the write back is 31.6% of FY26 profit before tax and exceptional items.
5. The developer kickback matter. Watch for a final outcome of the external assessment, an audit committee statement, any action reaching a director, or a second independent director exit within 12 months. Sources: BSE and NSE filings, the FY27 annual report. This tests Pillar 3 trust and the capital light pipeline.
6. Promoter pledge. In the NSE quarterly shareholding pattern and SAST filings, pledged shares should not rise above 2,944,444 (now 2,122,344 after the 2 November 2026 release), with no invocation and promoter holding at or above 27.0%. This tests promoter financial stress.
7. Advertising. Q3 FY27 ad income growth, with the four films management named (Ramayana, King, Avengers, Love and War; Q2 FY27 call p.9). Low single digit growth means the no blockbuster excuse fails again and part of the weakness is the company's own. Compare UFO's quarterly ad revenue. This tests the 7% of revenue that flows through at high margin.
8. Goodwill test. In the FY27 annual report Note 4A and key audit matter, look for stated headroom, a CGU split, and WACC at or above 10.33%. Goodwill is 77.7% of equity, and any impairment resets book value and the ROE story.
