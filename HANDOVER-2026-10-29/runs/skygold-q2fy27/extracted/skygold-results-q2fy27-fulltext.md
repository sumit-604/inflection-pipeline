6) During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Starmangalsutra Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, was involved in a fraud incident resulting in unauthorized fund transfers aggregating up to Rs 1,070.00 lakhs (symbol shown as rupee sign). The incident involved the unauthorized compromise of a company-issued mobile phone and laptop used by an employee.
