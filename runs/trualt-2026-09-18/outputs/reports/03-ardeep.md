### 6E. Quiet Abandonment Check (mandatory)
Reading the opening letters against the operational sections side by side:

1. **"Disciplined capital allocation" (MD, opening, AR p.28) vs the funding reality of FY26 (operational, CF
   statement and Note 41(D)/52).** The opening claims discipline; the cash flow statement shows a year funded by
   a ₹66,570.56 lakh equity raise and ₹22,057.11 lakh of fresh short-term borrowing, absorbed into a negative
   ₹(61,065.09) lakh free cash flow, with short-term obligations running 15.6x cash on hand at year end.
   **Classification: (a) implicit retraction** — the operational numbers show the opposite of "discipline" in
   the sense of self-funded, cash-generative growth; the opening does not acknowledge this. **Materiality:
   thesis-relevant** — any transition-alpha read of this company needs to know FY26 was externally funded, not
   internally disciplined, cash generation.
2. **"Strengthened balance sheet" (CFO letter, opening, AR p.53) vs net debt actually rising (operational, Note
   53, both AR levels).** **Classification: (a) implicit retraction** — the equity side of the balance sheet did
   strengthen (net worth roughly doubled), but "balance sheet strength" as commonly read includes leverage, and
   net debt rose in absolute terms. **Materiality: thesis-relevant**, directly contradicts a load-bearing company-
   memory fact (Pre-Read item 2).
3. **"Commitment to strong governance" (MD, opening, AR p.28) vs the self-disclosed woman-independent-director
   gap (operational, Corporate Governance Report, AR p.197-198).** **Classification: (c) hedged retreat** — the
   operational section does name the gap directly ("could not be completed... actively pursuing"), so this is
   not a silent drop, but the opening claims a completed state of "strong governance" while the operational
   section admits an open compliance item. **Materiality: modest on its own, but connects to a real post-AR
   penalty (Phase 5A)** — worth flagging as thesis-relevant given the pattern of governance churn found
   elsewhere.
4. **No named quiet abandonment found on the CBG/SAF growth-story side.** The opening letters' CBG and SAF
   claims (JV milestones, grant, construction sites) are matched or exceeded by the operational MD&A detail
   (specific site counts, specific commissioning quarters, Board's Report capex table) — this is a case where
   the front matter and the operations section are consistent, not a quiet abandonment.

Net: **two thesis-relevant quiet abandonments identified** (capital-allocation discipline claim; balance-sheet-
strengthening claim), both on the cash/leverage side of the year, both implicit retractions rather than silent
drops (the underlying numbers are fully disclosed elsewhere in the same document — this is a framing gap between
the letters and the notes, not a non-disclosure).

### PHASE 6 SUMMARY
**Phase verdict: 🔴 RED FLAG.** The front matter's complete silence on CFO, DSCR, and RPT concentration — the
three most load-bearing negative findings of this entire deep dive — while claiming "discipline" and "strength,"
is the single clearest instance in this AR of narrative outrunning the numbers. Every fact needed to correct the
narrative is present elsewhere in the same document (this is not concealment), but a reader who stops at the
opening letters would form a materially more positive view than the audited financials support.
