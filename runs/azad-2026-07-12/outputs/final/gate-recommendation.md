## FLAG-CASH (ACTIVE) — determination: GROWTH-INDUCED

Determination rests on four converging lines of evidence.

Magnitude and direction. Cumulative operating cash flow over FY20 to FY26 is 0.04 times cumulative PAT, and FY26 operating cash flow was negative Rs 123.26 Cr against positive PAT of Rs 132.16 Cr (B01). FY25 was already at the quality threshold: consolidated CFO/PAT 0.62, standalone 0.71 (B03). The working capital cycle stretched to 344 days in FY26 from 287 in FY25, with inventory days rising to 262 from 209 and collection days to 163 from 157 (rating rationale, CARE p.3).

Cause. The rating agency attributes the drain to the capacity build, not to earnings quality. Verbatim (CARE p.1): "The company's working capital cycle remained elongated due to increased receivables and inventory holding. The rise in inventory levels was driven by higher requirements for products under development, particularly those involving customised alloys that must be procured in bulk. In addition, inventory levels remained elevated at the end of FY26 due to the commissioning of three dedicated units for key customers. This is expected to normalise with the commencement of serial production. Receivables also remained elevated, reflecting the nature of operations and extended credit terms in export markets. Meanwhile, the creditor period remained largely stable."

Sector confirmation. Peers run the same pattern during ramps and normalize out of it (B06). MTAR is the direct precedent: working capital days fell from 260 to 172 and operating cash flow swung to positive Rs 196.9 Cr once its ramp stabilized. Capex ahead of utilization is industry wide, not an Azad outlier.

Management posture. Management guides FY27 normalization but missed the H2 FY26 target and quietly re-targeted the working capital band from 140 to 150 days out to 160 to 170 days, then pushed it to FY27, without acknowledging the slip (B05). Operating cash flow questions were deflected every quarter.

Falsifying quarterly metric (GROWTH-INDUCED requires one). Consolidated operating cash flow in FY27 quarterly results. If quarterly consolidated CFO stays negative through FY27 while revenue keeps growing and working capital days fail to decline from the 344 day FY26 level as the three commissioned units enter serial production, the growth induced reading is falsified and the drain reclassifies as structural. First clean read: Q1 FY27 results, expected August 2026.
