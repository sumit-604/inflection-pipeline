## 10. Monitorables and triggers (8)

1. Cash conversion. Read the FY27 audited cash flow and the March 2027 balance sheet when they file (BSE, about May 2027). Divide cash from operations by EBITDA excluding other income, and compute receivable days. Below 30% or above 120 days reads structural; 50% or more and under 90 days reads timing. This tests the cash proof gate.
2. Order execution and revenue run rate. In the Q3 FY27 results (January to February 2027), check quarterly revenue against the H1 pace of about 2,490 INR mn a quarter. A print below 2,000 INR mn without a stated cause, or a second deferral of the 2,371.96 INR mn order, breaks the lumpiness thesis. Execution should reach 80% of that order by March 2027.
3. Margin on the company's own prospectus basis. Compute EBITDA excluding other income from each results filing, not the deck series. Hold is 27% to 29% (FY26 27.15%, H1 FY27 28.6%); below 24%, the FY25 level, says the mix or currency support has gone. This tests the mix margin claim and the peer currency hypothesis.
4. Foreign currency receivables. In the FY27 annual report Note 48, check that foreign currency receivables fall below 1,000 INR mn and forward cover rises above 50%. Ask for the reason the USD balance carries 123 per USD. This tests whether the FY26 build is collectable at the booked value.
5. Inventory. On each half year balance sheet, inventories should fall below the 2,688.41 INR mn of September 2026, and finished goods should stop rising. A further build says margin is held up by stock.
6. Capacity fill. Look for a utilisation figure in a filing or call. Above 75% confirms the operating leverage case; below 55% for four quarters says the doubled plant is not filling.
7. Promoter and rating tripwires. Watch BSE for the Kinnar Mehta cessation reason, a CRISIL withdrawal or the name of the lender behind the Rs 10 crore lines, any promoter sale or pledge after the lock in, and any bank guarantee invocation against the 1,038.77 INR mn outstanding. Any one moves the promoter verdict.
8. Disclosure restoration. The FY27 annual report should restore the segment split (Note 44) and the customer note (Note 45), and the Certoplast tapes JV should file a definitive agreement with capex and funding. Missing segment data keeps the A&D share, and so the sector cap row, untestable.

Falsification line: Q3 FY27 quarterly revenue below 2,000 INR mn without a stated cause, or a second deferral of the 2,371.96 INR mn A&D Solutions order, in the Q3 FY27 results.
