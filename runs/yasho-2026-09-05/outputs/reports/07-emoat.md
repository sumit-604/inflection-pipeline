### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

Historical fixed-asset turnover = Revenue / average net PP&E.
Net PP&E: ₹60,511.63 Lakh (FY26) and ₹58,960.81 Lakh (FY25) (AR Balance Sheet, Note 3, PPE).
Average net PP&E FY25-26 = ₹59,736.22 Lakh. FY26 Revenue from Operations = ₹83,002.83 Lakh
(AR p.18/Financial Statements).
Historical FAT = 83,002.83 / 59,736.22 ≈ 1.39x.

FY27 capex under execution (revised) = ₹250 Cr (Inv. Pres. p.13).
Implied incremental revenue = 250 × 1.39 ≈ ₹347.5 Cr.
Expressed against FY26 revenue base (₹830.03 Cr): 347.5 / 830.03 ≈ **42% above current
revenue.**

Caveat (INF): this is a blended-historical proxy, not a forecast. The FY26 base FAT reflects a
mostly brownfield, already-ramped asset base; the FY27 tranche is largely greenfield (two new
Pakhajan buildings plus the MNC dedicated plant), which typically carries a lower near-term
asset turn during ramp-up. Treat 42% as a rough ceiling, not an expected outcome. This figure
feeds capex_embedded_growth_pct in the YAML.

### 2D. New geography or market entries
