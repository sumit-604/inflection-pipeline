Pursuant to Regulation 30 and 51 read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing

on Thursday, June 04, 2026, have approved the following:  
 
1. 
Appointment of Shri. Ashok Tukaram Dhamankar (DIN: 03066552) as Non-Executive 
Independent Director of the Bank. 
 
Pursuant to the recommendations of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation 
Committee of the Board (NRCCB), the Board approved the appointment of Shri. Ashok 
Tukaram Dhamankar (DIN: 03066552) as Non-Executive Independent Director of the 
Bank, for a period of Three (3) consecutive years, subject to the approval of the 
shareholders of the bank in the ensuing Annual General Meeting or through Postal Ballot. 
 
The disclosures as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Circular No.  HO/ 49/ 14/ 14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/ I/ 3762/ 2026 dated January 
30, 2026, in connection with appointment of Director is given as Annexure- I.    
 
2. 
Appointment of Shri. Hari Velloor (DIN: 11650530) as Non-Executive Director of the Bank. 
 
Pursuant to the recommendations of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation 
Committee of the Board (NRCCB), the Board approved the appointment of Shri. Hari 
Velloor (DIN: 11650530) as Non-Executive Director of the Bank, for a period of Three (3) 
consecutive years, subject to the approval of the shareholders of the bank in the ensuing 
Annual General Meeting or through Postal Ballot. 
 
The disclosures as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read
