## Business, numbers first

1. Revenue Rs 849 Cr FY24, Rs 1,208 Cr FY25, Rs 1,493 Cr FY26. Reported
   operating profit Rs 246 / 407 / 550 Cr (OPM 29% / 34% / 37%). PAT
   -Rs 18 / 68 / 71 Cr. (https://www.screener.in/company/AWFIS/consolidated/)
2. Model: India's largest flexible workspace operator by centres. It leases
   office floors from landlords and sublets desks and managed offices to
   enterprises, GCCs, SMEs and start-ups. Two segments: co-working and allied
   services, and Transform (design-and-build fit-out, 92% third-party).
   Awfis Care (facility management) was sold in FY25.
   (https://www.indiaratings.co.in/pressrelease/76615)
3. Q1 FY27: revenue Rs 425 Cr (+27% YoY). Co-working Rs 352 Cr (+27%),
   Transform Rs 73 Cr (+25%). Reported EBITDA Rs 162 Cr (38.2%). CASH EBITDA
   Rs 44 Cr (10.1%). PBT Rs 24 Cr. PAT Rs 24 Cr (+140%).
   (https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/awfis-space-solutions-ltd-nse-030257962.html)
4. Where the margin really sits: reported EBITDA is Ind AS 116 EBITDA, before
   lease rent. Cash EBITDA (after rent) is about 10% of revenue. The 38%
   headline margin is an accounting artefact. The economic margin is the
   10% line. (same source)
5. Network Q1 FY27: 251 centres, ~170,000 seats capacity, ~159,000
   operational, 18 cities, ~3,600 clients. Occupancy 83% mature (12m+),
   76% overall. GCC clients 24% of rental revenue. Weighted client tenure 38
   months, lock-in 26 months. (same source)
6. Supply model: managed aggregation (MA), where the landlord co-funds fit-out
   for a revenue share, was over 63% of centres in Dec-2024 against 50% in
   Mar-2021. Client leases run ~33 months against 60-120 months to lessors:
   an asset-liability mismatch Ind-Ra calls "moderate ALM risk". Top-5 clients
   under 15% of revenue. (https://www.indiaratings.co.in/pressrelease/76615)
7. Guidance FY27: revenue ~Rs 1,800 Cr, cash EBITDA Rs 195-200 Cr, capex
   Rs 200-210 Cr. H1 FY27 margin hit by commercial resets on leases signed in
   2021 and one 3,000-seat enterprise exit. Net cash (net debt/equity
   -0.08x). (Yahoo call summary above;
   https://www.constructionworld.in/latest-construction-news/real-estate-news/awfis-q1-fy27-pat-jumps-140--as-revenue-rises-27-/96066)
8. Credit: Ind-Ra IND A+/Stable (upgraded May-2025, affirmed 03-Jul-2026).
   (BSE filing 2cce03ab, 03-Jul-2026)
9. Ownership: promoter and promoter group 17.00%, two holders, no pledge,
   June 2026 (BSE shareholding summary). Peak XV, Bisque and Link Investment
   Trust sold 12.2% in a Rs 583 Cr block earlier.
   (https://inc42.com/buzz/peak-xv-others-to-offload-stake-worth-inr-583-cr-in-awfis/)
10. Filings to read closely: CFO change 05-Jan-2026 (Ravi Dugar out, Sumit
   Rochlani in); director resignation 09-Apr-2026; FY26 board meeting

   change in management and issue of securities 13-Aug-2026. The stock rose

## The engine the thesis would rest on

Cash-margin expansion, not revenue growth. Revenue compounds ~25-27% on seat
adds. The transition question is whether cash EBITDA margin climbs from ~10%
as centres mature, MA share rises, premium (Gold/Elite) mix grows and the 2021
lease resets wash through. A business that shows 38% EBITDA but keeps 10% after
rent is a converter until the cash line proves otherwise.
