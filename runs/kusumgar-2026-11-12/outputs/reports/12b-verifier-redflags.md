## 1. INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (graded before scoring)

### CRITICAL (repeated evasion, 2 quarters)

**F1. The order book was refused in two consecutive quarters. The refusal hardened. The same visibility went to the rating agency.**
- Q1 call: "No sir. We do not comment on our order book, sir." Then: "maybe in a couple of quarters we'll be in a better position to analyze and to give you better sort of visibility" (Concall p.12, P. Shrinavas Reddy).
- Q2 Q&A 10: "We do not disclose an order book, a pipeline of bids, win rates or expected share of any tender ... A single number would not be reliable" (Q2 deck p.22). The "couple of quarters" softener is gone.
- CARE, 2 Apr 2026: the reaffirmation is "aided by revenue visibility from healthy orderbook including special projects" (CARE p.2). Management gives visibility to its rating agency and refuses it to shareholders.
- Management itself names a PO stage: "formal POs ... LOIs ... projections" (Concall p.12). So a PO-stage number exists.
- The prospectus gave one contract figure: an FY26 order worth 2,371.96, with 23.61% executed in FY26 and the balance expected in FY27 (Prosp p.340). There has been no update since.
- Why it is material: A&D Solutions is the order-book line (FY25 2,219.02, FY26 1,550.17; Prosp p.199). Without a book, the FY28 base cannot run off forward evidence (A26.1).
- Separating observation: a disclosed PO-stage book at Q3 FY27 or in the FY27 annual report.

**F2. Segment revenue was refused in two consecutive quarters. This contradicts the CEO in the same call and the prospectus KPI set.**
- CEO opening: "Today, we operate across four reported market segments" (Concall p.4). Minutes later: "we are working on single segment, so that's why the segmental report is not applicable to us" (Concall p.7, Aadesh Gosalia).
- Q2 Q&A 5: "For financial reporting we operate as a single segment, and we do not publish a quarterly breakdown by product line or geography" (Q2 deck p.22).
- The prospectus reports the four segment revenues as KPIs 14-17 (Prosp p.124). The company "confirms that it shall continue to disclose all the KPIs ... at least once a year, for a duration of one year after the date of listing" (Prosp p.123, outside the bounded pages).
- The Ind AS 108 single-segment note (Q1 res p.9 note 5; Q2 res p.13 note 4) is a reporting choice. The flag is the refusal in communication, not the accounting note.
- Peer norm: Mayur reads out channel revenue on every call (B06 Q1, MAYURUNIQ Aug p.3, Nov p.4).
- Why it is material: A&D fell from 76.87% of FY25 contract revenue to 54.64% in FY26 (Prosp p.199, derived). The transition thesis is a mix claim, and the mix can no longer be tracked.
- Separating observation: the four-segment KPI table in the first annual KPI disclosure after listing.

### MAJOR

**F3. Management dodged the Q1 mix question and called a spillover-carried quarter representative.**
- The analyst asked how much of Q1 came from the A&D order that spilled over from Q4 (Concall p.7). The answer: "a significant portion of our revenue in Q1 did come from those parachute contracts overflowing, but ... from an annualized basis it wasn't too much off the mark." No number was given.
- In the same call the CFO said "We view Q4 as the anomaly, not Q1" (Concall p.6). Management then said "this Q1 is better reflective of what an average quarter would look like" (Concall p.8).
- The order balance is 1,811.94 (76.39% of 2,371.96; Prosp p.340, p.367; derived). That is up to 36.4% of H1 FY27 revenue of 4,981.90 (Q2 res p.10, derived) if it was executed in H1.
- Q2 Q&A 2: "Growth came mainly from execution of aerospace and defence solutions contracts" (Q2 deck p.22). The share is still not quantified.
- Two readings. (a) The H1 run-rate of about 2,490 a quarter is a new base, and new POs replace the spillover. (b) H1 is inflated by a one-time balance that ends in FY27.
- Separating observation: revenue in Q4 FY27 and Q1 FY28, after the 2,371.96 order completes.

**F4. Management's explanation of the Q4 FY26 spike does not match the prospectus attribution, and the year end was loaded.**
- CFO: "Q4 '26 includes a concentrated push of export segments that was expedited following the resolution of tariff related uncertainties in the US markets" (Concall p.5). Q1 deck: the QoQ decline was "due to higher export shipments in Q4 FY26 post-Trump tariff resolution, a one-off catch-up" (Q1 deck p.3). On the call, the parachute shipment "led to a little bit of a rise in revenue", and the US is "between 10% to 15% of our revenue" (Concall p.7).
- Prospectus: the Net Working Capital rise at 31 Mar 2026 was "primarily due to revenue of ₹639.57 million from bulk sales of combat free fall (CFF) parachute systems being recognised in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2026 and revenue of ₹511.32 million related to a service contract also being recognised in the fourth quarter" (Prosp p.41). Together that is 1,150.89, or 36.8% of Q4 FY26 revenue of 3,127.91 (Q1 res p.8, derived). Neither the call nor either deck mentions the service contract.
- Concentration: Q4 FY26 was 45.2% of FY26 revenue (6,920.03) and 83.5% of FY26 PAT (820.08 of 982.00) (Q1 res p.8, derived). Q3 FY26 PAT was -13.73 (982.00 less H1 FY26 175.65, Q2 res p.10, less Q4 820.08; derived).
- Year-end loading: CARE says the company "registered turnover of ₹620 crore as on March 28, 2026" on a consolidated basis (CARE p.2). FY26 revenue was ₹692.0 crore (Prosp p.124). So about ₹72 crore, 23% of Q4, was booked in the last three days of March (derived). [INFERENCE] This assumes CARE turnover is on the revenue-from-operations basis. CARE's FY25 figure of ₹781.89 crore against filed FY25 revenue of ₹779.0 crore supports that basis (CARE p.4; Prosp p.124).
- Unreconciled: CARE gives 9MFY26 standalone operating income of ₹331.00 crore (CARE p.4). Filed FY26 standalone revenue of 6,757.06 less Q4 of 3,074.41 gives 3,682.65, or ₹368.3 crore (Q1 res p.4, derived). The ₹37.3 crore gap is NOT EXPLAINED in the corpus.
- The tariff lift is not corroborated. CARE wrote on 2 Apr 2026, after Q4 closed, that FY26 scale would moderate "due to some impact of the incremental tariffs on exports to the US and deferment of the execution of high value special projects" (CARE p.2). Arvind said on 30 Jan 2026 that the tariff level "will be similar going forward, till some trade deal happens with the U.S." (ARVIND Jan p.8). No peer reports a lift. The date of any US tariff change in Jan-Mar 2026 is PENDING LIVE VERIFICATION.
- Why it is material: the FY26 base year rests on one quarter of lumpy contract and service revenue, recognised late. Role 1 must not annualise Q4 FY26 or treat FY26 PAT as a recurring base.

**F5. After the first call, management replaced the live call with a written Q&A it authored itself.**
- Preamble: "This Q&A answers the probable questions from analysts and investors" (Q2 deck p.22). Q&A 21 gives two reasons: "the same information at the same time", and "As a supplier to defence customers, we also need to be careful with programme details" (Q2 deck p.25).

Whether it is still?", Concall p.13). A written Q&A removes follow-ups, and the company picks the questions.
- Separating observation: the Q3 FY27 format, live or written.

**F6. A filed Q&A misstates the margin basis. The quarterly margins on the call included other income without a label.**
- Q2 Q&A 11: "EBITDA margin, excluding other income as in our prospectus, was 29.6% in FY24, 25.6% in FY25, 30% in FY26 and 29.7% in H1 FY27" (Q2 deck p.23).
- The prospectus margins excluding other income are 28.18%, 24.18% and 27.15% (Prosp p.124, p.345).
- The Q&A figures are the inclusive margins: (1,318.47 + 66.43)/4,679.08 = 29.6%; (1,883.89 + 112.15)/7,789.97 = 25.6%; (1,878.50 + 197.75)/6,920.03 = 30.0% (Prosp p.345, derived). H1 FY27 excluding other income is 28.6% (1,425.70/4,981.90; Q2 res p.10, derived).
- On the call the CFO gave 41% for Q4 FY26 and 22% for Q1 FY26 (Concall p.6). Both are inclusive. The analyst's 37% and 19% (Concall p.8) are the figures excluding other income: (1,287 - 130)/3,128 = 37.0% and (270 - 39)/1,225 = 18.9% (Q1 deck p.4, derived). The call did not reconcile them.
- FY26 other income was 197.75, of which 147.91 was FX gain (Prosp p.367). The inclusive margin therefore carries FX.

**F7. The filed record contradicts the recurrence claim.**
- CEO: "Once we clear that path with a customer, we become deeply embedded". Also: "once they do, they translate into long-term sticky recurring business" (Concall p.5). Q&A 8 repeats the qualification and switching-cost case (Q2 deck p.23).
- Prospectus: "We generally do not have long-term agreements for the sale of any of our products, including for Combat Free Fall (CFF) parachute systems, so the revenue from this product in Fiscal 2025 may not be indicative of the revenue from this product in future fiscal years" (Prosp p.346).
- A&D Fabrics fell 42.26% in FY26 "primarily due to a large order received in Fiscal 2025 that was not re-ordered in Fiscal 2026". That order gave 2,045.75 in FY25 and 286.16 in FY26 (Prosp p.340, p.367).
- Infrared reflective and extreme cold weather fabric: 2,153.40 in FY24 (47.26% of contract revenue), 1,986.88 in FY25 (25.80%), nil in FY26. CFF: 2,225.88 in FY25 (28.90%), 639.57 in FY26 (9.48%) (Prosp p.342).
- New types of product launched: "Nil" in FY26 and "Nil" in FY24 (Prosp p.342).
- Two readings. (a) Qualification gives incumbency and repeat tenders over years, with lumpy timing. (b) Revenue is a chain of single large orders with no contractual repeat.
- Separating observation: a repeat order on IR/ECW fabric or CFF systems by the end of FY27, visible in the FY27 product or segment data.

**F8. The growth engine runs on a partner's licensed IP. The Q2 Q&A says the company owns the know-how.**

- A&D Solutions was 2,219.02 in FY25 (28.81%) and 1,550.17 in FY26 (22.97%) (Prosp p.199). Licence term, exclusivity, royalty and renewal: NOT FOUND in the pages read.
- Why it is material: the Quality Ladder rung for this line depends on who owns the design.
- Separating observation: licence terms in a material-contract or related disclosure in the FY27 annual report.

**F9. Cash conversion is weak, and management changed the working-capital benchmark.**

The prospectus had said the March build was due to an "increase in finished goods and postponement of sales to first quarter of Fiscal 2027" (Prosp p.373). Finished and semi-finished goods built again in Q1 (218.28) and in Q2 (295.45) (Q2 res p.10).
- H1 OCF of 248.85 is 17.5% of EBITDA excluding other income (1,425.70). H1 FCF is -192.39 (OCF less capex of 441.24). Borrowings rose 320.81 (Q2 res p.11-12, derived).
