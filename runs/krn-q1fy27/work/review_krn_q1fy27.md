### 5A. SUMMARY NARRATIVE (plain English; numbers first)

KRN's Q1 FY27 was a strong quarter on the surface and a more complicated one underneath.
Consolidated revenue nearly doubled to Rs 252.32 Cr from Rs 115.28 Cr a year ago, up 118.9%, and
consolidated profit rose to Rs 32.90 Cr from Rs 12.42 Cr, up 164.9%. The standalone parent grew
more modestly, revenue up 59.1% to Rs 181.97 Cr and profit up 19.0% to Rs 18.67 Cr. The single
biggest fact is that the Neemrana subsidiary has flipped from a loss a year ago to Rs 14.23 Cr of
profit this quarter, which is 43.2% of the whole group's profit and 65.5% of its income. The growth
is real operating growth, not treasury or one-off income, because Other Income actually fell in both
entities. Consolidated operating margin improved by about 4 percentage points to 19.44%.

The caveats are equally important. That 43% of group profit sits in subsidiary accounts the
principal auditor did not review, only management-furnished. The parent is buying roughly Rs 24.5 Cr
of its own subsidiary's output to resell (a trading line that appeared only in FY26 and is still
unexplained), which inflates each entity's headline revenue. Rs 143.18 Cr of the Rs 350 Cr QIP money
is sitting idle in fixed deposits and bonds while the company-level table calls it "Nil" unutilised.
The overseas segment shows more liabilities than assets and never discloses its profit. The filing
also contains three separate arithmetic inconsistencies (a Rs 0.20 Cr PAT typo and two tax-line
sign/footing errors), which point to weak drafting controls. There is no cash-flow statement at Q1,
so cash quality is INDETERMINATE, and there is no concall, so plant utilisation and any data-centre
customer remain unknown.

For the thesis: no pre-committed trigger has fired. The soft-trim cannot fire because revenue growth
is strongly above 30% (one of its two legs) and the cash leg is untestable. Neither thesis-broken
condition can be tested from a results filing. The position stays HELD, still OVERVALUED versus the
sub-Rs 500 entry zone, trailing stop Rs 930, promoter verdict MONITOR unchanged. The protocol
verdict is PROCEED WITH FLAGS: real operating momentum, but capped below a clean pass by
INDETERMINATE cash conversion, heavy reliance on unaudited subsidiary profit, and open governance
items. The next quarter's cleanest test is H1 CFO/PAT plus a real utilisation number.
