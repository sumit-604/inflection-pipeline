### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **ATUM unit economics and current-period revenue.** Requested explicitly at least three times across the three calls (Q1 FY23 Divyanshi, Q2 FY23 Divyanshi Maghdani, implicitly Q1 FY24 Satish Kumar) and never given as an actual reported number, only forward projections. Likely reason: the segment's real revenue base is small enough that disclosing it would undercut the "great momentum" framing.
- **Capital efficiency (ROCE/ROE) is never once mentioned by management in any of the three calls**, despite a rising-debt, rising-capex story (debt rising toward Rs 579cr by FY24 year-end). Given standalone RoCE fell to 3.01% (FY24) and 3.46% (FY25) before recovering to 11.87% (FY26, AR2026 p.27), the silence on returns during the trough years is notable; analysts never forced the topic either in these three calls.
- **The Midnapore capex overrun (~31% above the repeated Rs 100cr figure) is never flagged or explained** in the Q1 FY24 call, the first call held after the overrun would have been substantially incurred.
