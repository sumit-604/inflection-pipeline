## SECTION 3: SAM & SOM

### 3A. SAM — five filters applied to management's own $135B functional breakdown

SAM is derived by applying capability-fit filters to the $135B / $136B
figure from Section 1B (chosen because it is the only source with the
functional-line-item granularity needed for this filtering; the headline
TAM range used for tam_cr above is drawn independently from Method 1, not
from this figure, precisely so SAM does not end up defined in terms of
itself).

| Filter | Bucket affected | Subtraction | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Manufacturing, Supply Chain & Distribution ($21B) | Already excluded at the market-definition stage (Section 1A) | No manufacturing assets, no CDMO capability (AR p.62) |
| Capability fit — field force | Marketing & Sales ($55B) | -$9.67bn (CSO sub-market, Method 2) | Indegene's ECS is digital/content/omnichannel/commercial-technology, not a physical contract-sales-rep deployment business (AR p.18-19); the CSO/field-force slice of "Marketing & Sales" is not a capability Indegene sells |
| Capability fit — full CRO trial execution | Drug Discovery & Clinical Trial ($36B) | -$28.8bn (80% of the bucket; ANALYST ESTIMATE, not sourced to a named report) | Indegene's Enterprise Clinical Solutions is explicitly "data, AI, clinical operations, patient engagement" (AR p.20-21), not full-service CRO trial execution (site management, monitoring, biostatistics), which is the bulk of this $36B bucket and dominated by IQVIA/ICON/Parexel/Syneos per the IC note's own competitive framing; a 20% capability-fit retention (~$7.2bn) is applied as a conservative estimate, flagged clearly as analyst judgment |
| Geography | — | None | Indegene already serves this market globally (NA/Europe/RoW); no evidence of geography-constrained addressability within the defined scope |
| Customer segment | — | None | Indegene's own customer mix (Biopharma 91.6%, Medical Devices 3.1%, Emerging Biotech 3.3%, Q1 FY27) already spans the customer types implied by the $135B figure; no further subtraction |

**SAM = $55B + $24B + $21B - $9.67B (CSO) + $7.2B (20% of clinical) =
~$96.5bn** ($96.53bn precisely: 55 - 9.67 + 24 + 21 + 7.2 = 96.53).

**SAM as % of TAM:** vs the $135B management figure it was filtered from,
96.53/135 = **71.5%**. Vs the independently-sourced realistic TAM (Method
1, $171bn), 96.53/171 = **56.4%** (this is the figure reported in the YAML
block, using the independent realistic TAM as the standard denominator).

In INR: SAM = USD 96.53bn × Rs 88.4 = **Rs 853,325 cr**.

### 3B. SOM at 3 and 5 years

**Current SAM share:** FY26 revenue USD 397mn ÷ SAM USD 96.53bn =
**0.41%**.

**On the standard share-gain heuristic:** the standard rule (1-2pp normal
in 3 years; 3-5pp aggressive; >5pp only on competitor exit/acquisition) is
calibrated for companies with a meaningful starting share (commonly 5-30%
in domestic-market contexts). At Indegene's 0.41% starting share of a
>$95bn global TAM, applying "percentage points" mechanically breaks down —
even reaching 1% share in 3 years (a >2x relative jump) would nominally
read as a huge "+0.6pp" move that looks tiny in absolute pp terms but is
extremely aggressive in relative terms. This is flagged explicitly: SOM
below is instead anchored in Indegene's own disclosed, current growth
trajectory (not a mechanical pp assumption), then cross-checked for
plausibility against the resulting SAM-share movement.

**Growth-rate basis:** FY26 actual revenue growth was 23.6% INR / 18.2%
USD YoY (AR MD&A p.63, per B04); Q1 FY27 growth was 39.7% INR / 26.5% USD
YoY (Inv. Pres., per B04) — both well above the earlier, now-stale MNCL
forecast (16.6% INR for FY27E, per the 2024 note). A moderating growth path
is used: ~20% USD CAGR for years 1-3 (below the FY26 actual print, above
the stale MNCL estimate — a middle path), decelerating to ~17% USD CAGR for
years 4-5 (normal glide-path deceleration as the revenue base compounds).

- SOM_3yr (2029) = USD 397mn × (1.20)^3 = **USD 686mn** = **Rs 6,064 cr**
- SOM_5yr (2031) = USD 686mn × (1.17)^2 = **USD 939mn** = **Rs 8,301 cr**

**Implied SAM at 3/5 years** (SAM grown at the 7.4% Method-1 TAM CAGR,
applied uniformly across TAM/SAM as the best available like-for-like
market-growth rate):
- SAM_3yr (2029) = $96.53bn × (1.074)^3 = **$119.65bn**
- SAM_5yr (2031) = $96.53bn × (1.074)^5 = **$137.9bn**

**Implied SAM share:**
- Yr3: 686/119,650 = **0.573%** (up from 0.41% — a +0.16pp / 1.39x
  relative gain over 3 years)
- Yr5: 939/137,900 = **0.681%** (up from 0.41% — a +0.27pp / 1.66x
  relative gain over 5 years)

Sanity check: these absolute pp moves are small even against the "normal"
1-2pp/3yr heuristic (well inside it), while the underlying growth-rate
assumption (20%/17% USD CAGR) sits comfortably inside Indegene's own
recently disclosed actual growth (18.2%-26.5% USD YoY across the last two
reported periods). The SOM path is not aggressive by either lens.

**SOM-implied revenue CAGR** (this is the FORMAL handoff to Stage 11):
- Yr3: **20.0%** (by construction, the assumed 3-year path)
- Yr5: (939/397)^(1/5) - 1 = **18.8%**

### 3C. Capacity cross-check

B07 (Emerging Moat scan) reports **capex_embedded_growth_pct = 0** and
states explicitly: "capex_embedded_growth_pct is not a meaningful construct
for this company" — Indegene is an asset-light people/technology business
(PPE = 1.3% of total assets per B04); FY26 capex was Rs 443mn against Rs
35,105mn revenue (1.3% capex/revenue ratio, B07 Section 2C). The standard
"capacity gap in ₹Cr, name which side is optimistic" framing from the
prompt does not apply to a capex-constrained read here — redirected, as
B07 itself does, to the real capacity constraint for this business model:
**headcount**.

- Delivery headcount, Q1 FY27: 4,983 (Inv. Pres. fact sheet, per B04/B07).
- Revenue per employee (RPE), Q1 FY27: USD 77.1K annualized (Inv. Pres.,
  per B04/B07), up from USD 51K five years ago (~8.6% CAGR, per B07).
- At CONSTANT RPE ($77.1K), delivering SOM_3yr revenue of USD 686mn implies
  a delivery headcount of 686mn / 0.0771mn = **8,898 employees** — a 79%
  increase over 3 years from the 4,983 base, i.e. ~21.4% headcount CAGR.
- If RPE continues its own recent ~7-9% CAGR trend (management's stated
  GenAI-productivity thesis, per B04/B07) to reach roughly $95K by 2029,
  the headcount required falls to 686mn / 0.095mn = **7,221 employees**, a
  45% increase (~13.2% CAGR) — closely in line with the company's most
  recently observed delivery-headcount growth rate of 13.4% YoY (4,394 →
  4,983, Q1 FY26 → Q1 FY27, per B07 Section 2C).

**Capacity check: SUFFICIENT**, on a people-scaling (not capex-scaling)
basis — the headcount growth required to deliver SOM_3yr (13-21% CAGR
depending on RPE assumption) sits inside or only modestly above the
company's own recently observed hiring pace, and is not gated by a capex
plan (there isn't one; B07 explicitly reports no committed capacity-capex
programme). The genuine constraint flagged here, consistent with B04/B07,
is domain-trained-talent availability and attrition (15.7% TTM voluntary
attrition, per B04) — a talent-market risk, not a capital-constraint risk.

### 4C. Market structure

Per the IC note's own competitive-landscape framing (p.18-19, lines
1200-1241): the market is broadly divided into three categories — (1) large
diversified life-sciences service providers (IQVIA, ICON, Parexel, Syneos
Health) for whom commercialization is one component of a wider business;
(2) specialized commercialization companies (EVERSANA, Real Chemistry,
Avalere Health) — "the market remains fragmented, with many companies
possessing expertise in individual segments"; (3) global technology
services companies with healthcare practices (Accenture, Cognizant, Wipro).

**Structure read:** Fragmented but actively consolidating — named
consolidation events include Fishawack Health + AMO Communications forming
Avalere Health, EVERSANA's acquisition-led expansion, and Indegene's own
three FY26 bolt-ons (BioPharm, Warn & Co, Cake Kommunikations). Competition
is shifting from price toward differentiation (GenAI platform capability,
outcome/output-based pricing — ~60% of Indegene's revenue per B04) rather
than pure per-hour billing competition. No dominant single player: even
IQVIA's broader Technology & Analytics Solutions segment ($6.63bn) is a
small fraction of the $135-171bn total addressable spend. Entrants:
horizontal IT majors (Accenture, Cognizant, Wipro) are adjacent entrants
building healthcare practices, a genuine but not yet dominant competitive
threat per the IC note's own framing. Import share trend: NOT APPLICABLE
(services market).
