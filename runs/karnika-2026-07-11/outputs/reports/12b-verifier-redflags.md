## PART 4 — CREDIBILITY GRADE

B05 assigns **C (Mixed)**, weights 35/45/20. I concur with C but lean to the low
end. B05's one genuinely favourable leg — "absolute delivery genuinely strong,
FY26 PAT +57% / Q4 +48.6%" — is overstated: a management-confirmed one-time
~INR9cr other-income securities gain (May p.4, p.12) inflates exactly those PAT
figures, and if that gain sits inside reported EBITDA the H2 operating-margin
compression is even worse than the ~11.5% B05 already flags. Grade stays C; the
"real, reported growth" cushion under it is thinner than B05 credits.
