===== PAGE 9 =====
Ekta Soni on CBA: "CBA is a product technology, an alternative to cocoa butter. Through
technology from India, from an ESOS plant, we will convert some of the oil liquid
fractions into a hard fraction using enzymes. This converts liquid fractions into solid
fractions, creating a cocoa butter alternative called ECBE (enzymatic cocoa butter
equivalent). Applications... include food segments, chocolate, confectioneries, and the
HoReCa market. This is a forward integration model..."
Roshan Nair (Antique Stockbroking) on Brazil: "We have already started trial production
in the last quarter and are slowly dispatching our production to the Brazilian entity for
processing. It will take some time because a lot of production has to go from an Indian
plant as well... maybe in another 2, 3, or 4 quarters, and we can see a gradual ramp-up."
On employee cost: last quarter carried a performance incentive provision; "currently, the
run rate, which we can take should be around INR 14 crores to INR 15 crores per quarter
going forward."
