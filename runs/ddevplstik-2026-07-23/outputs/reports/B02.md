### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. What is the mechanism and rate basis for the royalty paid to Kkalpana Industries (India) Ltd, and why did it fall 72.5% in FY25 while purchases from the same related party rose 88.9% in the same year — is the royalty rate linked to Kkalpana's sales, DDev's sales, or another formula, and was it renegotiated?
2. The Note 52 lease-liability roll-forward as printed does not foot to the balance sheet (implied closing balance of (₹62.56) Lacs vs. actual ₹429.46 Lacs) — can management confirm the correct "Additions during the year" line (appears it should be ~₹492 Lacs, close to the ₹504.78 Lacs of ROU additions disclosed in the same note) and reissue a reconciling roll-forward?
3. The gratuity plan has received zero employer contributions for two consecutive years despite the plan actuary's own recommended contribution of ₹91.73 Lacs (FY25) / ₹106.91 Lacs (FY24) — what is the funding policy going forward, and is there a plan to close the ~84% funding gap?
4. Finished-goods inventory grew 60.3% against 7.08% revenue growth, crediting ₹13.28 Cr to FY25 COGS (versus ₹0.80 Cr FY24) — is this build tied to specific capacity ramp-up (PVC/HFFR/XLPE per the operator's capacity narrative) awaiting dispatch, or a genuine demand/sell-through slowdown, and what is the expected FY26 normalization path?
5. What is the basis for using a 25-year useful life for Plant & Machinery against the Schedule II default of 15 years — has this been supported by an independent technical/engineering assessment, and if so can that assessment be shared or referenced?
