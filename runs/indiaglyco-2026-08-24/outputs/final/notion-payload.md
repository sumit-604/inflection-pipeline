## BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

India Glycols makes four physical things: bio based glycols and glycol
ethers, potable spirits, fuel grade ethanol, and plant based pharma
ingredients. The glycols and glycol ethers are industrial building blocks;
the company converts molasses and ethylene oxide into MEG, DEG, TEG and heavy
glycols that go into antifreeze, polyester fibre, paints and personal care. It
is the only Indian maker of ethylene based glycol ethers on Sulzer Chemtech
technology, so some buyers cannot swap it out quickly. The potable spirits
line sells IMFL, country liquor and extra neutral alcohol, and it leads in
Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand under state excise licences. The bio fuel line
supplies fuel grade ethanol to oil marketing companies under the national
blending programme, and Ennature makes plant APIs, nutraceuticals and nicotine
with a global position in the Thiocolchicoside API. Customers differ by line:
brand owners and institutions buy the spirits under a 15 year Bacardi tie, an
expanded Amrut distribution arrangement, and CSD and paramilitary canteen
approvals; oil marketing companies take the ethanol; chemical and consumer
formulators and the Clariant JV take the glycols and specialty chemicals.
Spirits buyers qualify a distiller once and then stay, so the Bacardi, Amrut
and CSD approvals lock in volume, while ethanol offtake is set by government
tender rather than by any single end buyer.

Present demand reads through named external signals. OMC ethanol tender and
allocation results by supply year set bio fuel volume and price; UP and
Uttarakhand excise revenue bulletins proxy demand in the core spirits states;
Brent crude oil price sets how competitive the EO and bio MEG lines are; the
China glycol ether and MEG feedstock and export price index drives glycol
ether export margin; Clariant IGL Specialty Chemicals JV disclosures feed the
chemicals outlook; and EU and US brand owner sustainability procurement
commitments pull premium priced bio MEG. Forward growth rests on the E20 to
E25/E100 blending policy notification, the NSU bio amine platform qualifying
at named buyers BASF, Unilever and Mibelle, and CSD and paramilitary brand
approvals widening the spirits channel. Two of the company's own tailwinds
move the wrong way: EBP ethanol allocation fell about 22% year on year even as
national blending held at 20%, and BSPC chemicals revenue fell about 26%
across three years while its India market grew about 6.7% a year.

Competitive advantage sits mostly in spirits. Qualification lock in with
Bacardi, Amrut and CSD scored Strong, and captive ENA backward integration
scored Moderate. The chemicals line has a narrow and eroding edge: the Sulzer
glycol ether position and a six year process innovation margin trend are
Moderate, but the "only maker in India" scarcity claims are self reported and
unverified, and no peer in the set makes glycols so no independent check
exists. The bio fuel line has no moat; it is a government quota taker whose
volume and price are set by tender. Ennature rests on a single product
Thiocolchicoside position with concentration risk, so its edge is real but
thin.
