### 2B. Utilisation trajectory
Capacity utilisation cited inconsistently within the same Nov 2025 call: 80-85% (Krishan Karnani, p.5) and later ~90% (Niranjan Mundhra, in response to Dipika Chadda, p.13) — flagged as an internal inconsistency, not resolved by the May 2026 call, which gives no updated utilisation figure. Management confirms "there will be a need" for capacity expansion beyond the existing ~100,000 sq ft in-house space plus additional rented space (Nov 2025 call, p.13).

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown
- FY24-25 Revenue from Operations = Rs17,254.85L (AR Note 21, p.83)
- Average net PP&E + intangibles = (Rs742.55L + Rs507.83L)/2 = Rs625.19L (AR Note 11, p.81)
- Historical fixed asset turnover = 17,254.85 / 625.19 ≈ **27.6x**
- FY24-25 capex under execution = Rs347.60L (Cash Flow Statement, p.70)
- Implied incremental revenue = 347.60 × 27.6 ≈ Rs9,594L ≈ **Rs95.9cr**
- As % of current (FY24-25) revenue (Rs172.55cr): ≈ **55.6%**

**Caveat (flagged, not suppressed):** this mechanical output is almost certainly distorted upward. Karnika's asset-light, job-work model means most of its output capacity sits on job-workers' balance sheets, not Karnika's own PP&E — the unusually high 27.6x fixed-asset turnover is a symptom of outsourcing, not of efficient owned-capital deployment. This figure should not be read as a credible embedded-growth signal; it is reported per the required arithmetic with this explicit distortion flag.

### 2D. New geography/market entries
- Kidcity Tier 2/3 retail footprint: Bihar, Jharkhand, Eastern UP towns (Ara, Hajipur, Muzaffarpur, Begusarai, Bhagalpur, Forbesganj, Araria, Kishanganj, Jogbani, Gorakhpur, Varanasi, Bhadohi), expanding toward Bengal/Jharkhand/Ranchi/Hazaribagh — 📄 operational, 55+ stores as of May 2026 (May 2026 call, p.5)
- Export: Europe via a Russia channel partner and Middle East retail chains (Landmark Group, Lulu) — 🎙️ targeted, uncontracted (Nov 2025 call, p.8)
- US export order — still "on the pipeline itself" as of May 2026, stalled since Nov 2025 due to US tariff/political developments; management is pivoting domestically toward organised-sector demand from Zara, H&M, DMart instead (May 2026 call, p.8-9)

## SECTION 6: TIMELINE, RISKS & COMBINED ASSESSMENT

### 6A. Moat evolution timeline
- **Next 12m**: Kidcity store count catch-up toward the missed 75+ counter target (55+ actual, May 2026); Q1 FY27 results as the first clean read on post-consolidation execution; US order resolution or further stall
- **12-24m**: Europe/Middle East export relationship conversion (Russia channel, Landmark Group, Lulu); property acquisition for in-house manufacturing if it materialises; fabric-segment revenue visibility; new-logo revenue quantification (Style Bazaar, Bumzy, Hopscotch)
- **24-36m**: Kidcity's guided revenue scale-up (Rs24-25cr FY26 → 3x FY27 guide → Rs200-250cr FY28-29 target) — the key test of whether the H2/H1 evidence converts from "documented acquisition" to "documented value creation"
- **3-5yr**: Karnika standalone Rs300cr+ revenue target; possible NSE Emerge → main board migration (stated intent, Nov 2025 call, p.10)

### 6B. Risks to top-scoring emerging moats
- **H2 (Kidcity strategic partnership, Strong)**: Integration risk is already evidenced, not hypothetical — FY26 Kidcity revenue missed its own Rs30cr guide by ~17-20% (delivered Rs24-25cr) and the 75+ counter target was missed by ~27% (delivered 55+). Management's own words attribute part of the FY26 margin compression to "transitional changes" from consolidation (May 2026 call, p.4). Early warning sign: any further quarter of Kidcity underperformance against its own guidance.
- **H1 (industry consolidation beneficiary, Moderate)**: The organised-vs-unorganised shift is industry-wide; any competitor (including Zara/H&M's own stated interest in Indian organised-sector suppliers, May 2026 call, p.8) could pursue similar bolt-on targets, diluting differentiation.
- **E1 (Kidcity Tier 2/3 first-mover, Moderate)**: Concentrated in just 3 states (Bihar, Jharkhand, Eastern UP); fragile if a larger organised entrant moves into the same underserved geography.
- **A3 (process/automation efficiency, Moderate)**: FY26 full-year EBITDA margin fell to 15% from H1's 19.67%, contradicting the "margin only going up" narrative; raw-material (cotton) cost pressure shows the efficiency gain is not fully insulated from external shocks despite 3-6 month forward yarn hedging.
