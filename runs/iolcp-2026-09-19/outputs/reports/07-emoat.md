### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown
Historical fixed-asset turnover (net basis, FY26):
- Revenue from operations FY26 = Rs 2,319.1 Cr (Investor Presentation p.28 / AR P&L)
- Net PP&E as at 31-Mar-2026 = Rs 1,165.07 Cr (AR Note 3.1, p.147)
- CWIP closing balance as at 31-Mar-2026 = Rs 93.61 Cr (AR Note 3.2, p.147)
- Net fixed assets = 1,165.07 + 93.61 = Rs 1,258.68 Cr
- Historical net FAT = 2,319.1 / 1,258.68 = **1.843x**

Total capex under execution: Rs 495 Cr disclosed (Reg 30, 09-Sep-2026) + Rs 1,200-1,400 Cr
greenfield (guided) = **Rs 1,695-1,895 Cr** (midpoint Rs 1,795 Cr).

Implied incremental revenue = capex × historical FAT:
- Midpoint: 1,795 × 1.843 = **Rs 3,308 Cr**, i.e. **142.6%** above current FY26 revenue
  (2,319.1)
- Range: 1,695 × 1.843 = Rs 3,124 Cr (134.7%) to 1,895 × 1.843 = Rs 3,492 Cr (150.6%)

**Caveat, load-bearing**: roughly 72% of the Rs 1,795 Cr capex base (the Rs 1,200-1,400 Cr
greenfield) has no firm commissioning date across four consecutive call touchpoints (B05
FLAG-CAPEX). A cross-check using ONLY the disclosed, dated Rs 495 Cr tranche: 495 × 1.843 =
Rs 912 Cr, **39.3%** of current revenue — close to, and slightly above, management's own
Sep-2026 statement that the three initiatives together add "25-30% incremental [revenue],
once fully operational (~FY29)" (B05 guidance row). This closeness is a modest corroboration
of the FAT-based method for the dated tranche; the full-total figure (142.6%) should be read
as capacity-ceiling arithmetic, not a near-term forecast, given the greenfield's undated status.
**capex_embedded_growth_pct is set to 142.6% (full total) in the YAML block, per the prompt's
literal formula ("total capex under execution"); the 39.3% dated-tranche cross-check is carried
here for Stage 9 to weight appropriately.**

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

**FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY**

**A1. Rare manufacturing capability** — Evidence: 5 CEP grants in one year (Minoxidil,
Sitagliptin, Clopidogrel, Pantoprazole, Metformin Process-II), 2 new CEP submissions pending,
NMPA China approval + filing, MFDS Korea DMF registrations + filing — a genuinely busy
regulatory-filing year extending an already-large DMF/CEP base (14 USFDA DMFs, 21 EDQM CEPs).
📄 STRONG evidence type, MODERATE strength (extends an existing capability into new
molecules/countries rather than creating a new one). Time to materialise: near-term
(registrations already granted) to medium-term (pending filings).

**A2. Patent and IP pipeline** — Evidence: patents HELD static at 3 in both FY25 and FY26
AR (AR2025 line ~7154, AR2026 line 7492), despite the FY26 AR's own headline language
"Increased patent filings" (AR p.20 highlights section). R&D expense rose Rs 21.02 Cr (FY25)
to Rs 26.01 Cr (FY26), +23.8% YoY, ahead of revenue growth (+11.5%). No licensing revenue,
no lab-partnership evidence found. 📄 for the numbers, but the numbers CONTRADICT the
narrative claim — flagged explicitly as an internal AR inconsistency, not credited as
"increased" IP. WEAK strength. Time to materialise: NOT FOUND (no forward patent-filing
target stated).

**A3. Process innovation** — Evidence: AR's own "Patent Portfolio Highlights" table names
four specific process innovations (nitrosamine-free Valsartan route, recyclable biocatalytic
Sitagliptin process, quality-focused Losartan process minimising azido-impurity formation,
a "sustainable generic intermediate" process), plus a dedicated R&D workstream on
effluent-to-sellable-byproduct conversion ("waste to wealth," AR p.59). No yield or unit-cost
figures are quantified. 📄 documented but qualitative. MODERATE strength. Time to
materialise: ongoing/near-term (these are described as already developed, not projected).

**A4. Product platform / modular architecture** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No SKU-growth-without-
R&D-growth pattern, no shared-architecture language in the corpus.

**FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN**

**B1. Backward integration and RM security** — The company IS extensively backward
integrated (17 MW captive co-generation power plant, IBB self-manufacture for Ibuprofen,
captive acetyls-to-API transfer of Rs 237.72 Cr) but this is an EXISTING structural feature,
already counted in the Gate 0 backward-looking score, not a forming one. No NEW backward-
integration move (5+ year supply agreement, new captive source) was found for the corpus

**B2. Qualification lock-in** — Evidence: management describes both existing API contracts

disclosed. 🎙️ only. WEAK strength (generic claim, plausible but unverifiable at this
evidence tier).

**B3. Supply chain network effect** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND. Not a platform business.

**FAMILY C — CUSTOMER**

**C1. Customer ecosystem / embedded relationships** — Evidence: the CDMO formulations
business is explicitly customer-initiated per management — "they came back to us that why
don't you supply us the bulk tablets instead of sending the APIs... probably this is a

molecules (Pantoprazole, Clopidogrel, Ibuprofen, Metformin) captively consumed into the new

"established primarily to cater to the evolving and long-term contract manufacturing
requirements of the Company's Anchor Customers in European region... a customer-led and
complementary extension of the Company's existing API business" (Reg 30, 09-Sep-2026,
Annexure-2). STRONG strength — a 📄-anchored claim of embedded, customer-pulled vertical
extension, corroborated across two separate concalls (Aug and Sep 2026) by two different
