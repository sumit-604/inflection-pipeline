# TRUALT BIOENERGY — Step 1 business brief

Run: runs/trualt-2026-09-18 | NSE TRUALT, BSE 544545 | CMP Rs 424, market cap
Rs 3,635 cr, trailing PE 23.2x, ROCE 10.4% (screener, 18-Sep-2026).
Listed 03-Oct-2025 at Rs 496 (IPO Rs 839 cr: Rs 751 cr fresh, Rs 88 cr OFS).
(https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/trualt-bioenergy-ipo/2231/)
Promoters: the Nirani family of Bagalkot, Karnataka (Vijaykumar Murugesh
Nirani, Vishal Nirani, Sushmitha Vijaykumar Nirani). Incorporated 2021.

## What the business is

TruAlt makes fuel ethanol and sells it to the oil marketing companies (OMCs)
under the ethanol blending programme. Five distillery units in Karnataka,
2,000 KLPD installed, about 3.6% of India's ethanol capacity. By-products:
extra neutral alcohol, liquid CO2, dry ice. Customer concentration: 99.8% of
FY25 sales from 10 customers, mostly OMCs.
(https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/trualt-bioenergy-ipo-review)
Two subsidiaries make compressed biogas (CBG): Leafiniti Bioenergy (51%; GAIL
put in Rs 13 cr in Mar-2026) and TruAlt Gas (77%). One CBG plant, 10.2 TPD.
(inputs/rating/20260428-*.pdf, Ind-Ra rationale; inputs/announcements/20260319-*.pdf)

## The numbers

Sales Rs 762 cr (FY23), Rs 1,223 cr (FY24), Rs 1,908 cr (FY25), Rs 1,728 cr
(FY26). Net profit Rs 35 cr, Rs 32 cr, Rs 147 cr, Rs 96 cr. FY26 fell because
three units shut for multi-feed conversion: Q2FY26 sales were Rs 115 cr and
the quarter lost Rs 38 cr.
(inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv)
Q1FY27: sales Rs 626.9 cr, up 106% on Rs 303.9 cr; operating profit Rs 132.8
cr; PAT Rs 57.2 cr against Rs 4.7 cr. Volume 8.5 cr litres, split about 50/50
sugar and grain. Utilisation 60%, target 80-85%. 44 cr litres of FY27 OMC
orders in hand. Management says grain ethanol margin compressed from Rs 15-16
to Rs 6-7 per litre with maize at Rs 25.5/kg.
(https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/trualt-bioenergy-ltd-nse-trualt-010330469.html;
https://www.chinimandi.com/trualt-bioenergy-raises-ethanol-capacity-by-43-to-2000-klpd-in-q1-fy27/)

(inputs/screening/screener-Data_Sheet.csv; inputs/rating/20260428-*.pdf)

## The engine the thesis rests on

One engine: the multi-feed conversion. 1,300 of 2,000 KLPD can now run on
grain as well as cane juice and molasses. That turns a 4-5 month crush-season
distillery into a year-round one. The claim is utilisation from 60% to 80-85%
on existing capacity, with no large new capex, and interest cost falling as a
share of sales. Options sit on top: 10 more CBG plants (about Rs 760 cr gross
capex across two JVs) and a sustainable aviation fuel plant (over Rs 2,000 cr,
Rs 150 cr PM JI-VAN support approved Jun-2026). Neither option is funded on
the filed balance sheet yet.
(inputs/announcements/20260618-81dcb00a-*.pdf)

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (verify against the filings first)

1. **Utilisation and grain economics, guidance versus delivery.** Score the
   four concalls (Nov-2025 to Aug-2026) for promised utilisation, grain share
   and per-litre margin against what printed. The Q1FY27 claim is 60% now,
   80-85% target, grain margin down to Rs 6-7/litre. Year-round running is
   the whole thesis; grain margin compression is the whole bear case.
2. **Cash conversion and IPO money.** FY26 CFO Rs -296 cr, inventory Rs 528
   cr, borrowings up despite Rs 751 cr fresh equity. Read the FY26 AR cash
   flow and inventory notes and the three monitoring agency reports in
   inputs/announcements/ (Feb, May, Aug 2026). Establish where the Rs 425 cr
   working-capital tranche went and whether inventory is seasonal cane
   stock or something else.
3. **Promoter pledge and related parties.** The Jun-2026 BSE shareholding
   pattern says promoter shares are pledged: 2,22,95,674 of 6,04,98,650 shares
   (36.85%) (inputs/shareholding/TRUALT-SHP-June-2026.txt). The Nirani family
   also runs sugar mills in the same district. Read the prospectus and FY26
   AR related-party notes for feedstock purchases from promoter-group mills,
   and check who Onkar Agro Sugars & Energy is: it is buying Unit 5 (Badami)
   by slump sale for Rs 171 cr (inputs/announcements/20260804-*.pdf).
4. **Governance churn.** Within four weeks of listing, a 28-Oct-2025
   cessation filing cites a "management decision". Company secretary
   appointment filings follow on 09-Jan-2026 and 03-Feb-2026 (Deepak Kumar
   Gulati signed the prospectus; Monu Kumar signs from Jan-2026). A new
   chairman and a director cessation were filed 28-Jul-2026. Exchanges fined
   the company Rs 4.55 lakh each for board composition (25-Aug-2026). A GST
   demand order arrived 20-Aug-2026. Read these filings in
   inputs/announcements/ for who left and the reasons given.

## PEERS AND WHY

- **GULPOLY (Gulshan Polyols)**: the grain-ethanol comp. A maize and rice
  distillery selling to OMCs. Sales Rs 2,020 cr FY26 but net profit only Rs 25
  cr: the clean read on grain ethanol margin when maize is dear. It tests
  TruAlt's grain-side margin claim.
- **BALRAMCHIN (Balrampur Chini Mills)**: the scale integrated comp. Cane
  plus grain distillery, own feedstock, Rs 6,271 cr sales. It shows what
  distillery margin looks like with captive cane and a strong balance sheet.
- **TRIVENI (Triveni Engineering)**: the dual-feed comp. Cane-juice and
  grain distilleries, multi-feed conversion done earlier, 57,488 KL Q1FY27
  production. Replaced Shree Renuka Sugars, the Karnataka comp first picked:
  Renuka has held no concall since Jun-2022, so it cannot serve the peer
  concall stage.

No listed pure-play standalone ethanol distiller of TruAlt's size exists. All
three comps carry a sugar or polyol book beside ethanol.
