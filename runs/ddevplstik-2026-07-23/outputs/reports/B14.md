## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** Return on capital is elite in absolute terms: ROCE 28.90% FY26, ROE 21.84%, off a net cash balance sheet (net cash Rs 6.42 Cr), Block D 20/20, interest coverage 10.49x, debt to equity 0.052x (B01, B10). The FY26 capex of Rs 73.18 Cr was fully self-funded; the entire Rs 300-400 Cr multi-year programme, including BESS Phase 1, is guided debt-free from internal accrual (B08 3B). CRISIL A+/Stable and A1+ were reaffirmed 17-Jul-2026 after a four-notch upgrade path since 2023 (B08 6D). The one weak block is cash generation: Block B 7/20, cumulative FY24-26 CFO/PAT 0.594, FY26 alone 0.42.

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**
1. Bhiwadi XLPE 48,000 MTPA ramp driving FY27 volume toward 231,000 MT. Conviction M-H. Capacity commissioned late April 2026 on schedule. Confirms it: quarterly volume run-rate tracking the 231k target. Kills it: utilization stays materially below plan into FY28. This is the SHARED CATALYST behind Revenue STARTING and the Pillar 3a premium.
2. Export growth continuation. Conviction M-H, delivered FY26 (+30% revenue, +23% volume; MENA strongest). Confirms it: Q1 FY27 export growth sustains. Kills it: renewed US tariff escalation or fresh Middle East logistics disruption.
3. RM cost normalization post Israel-Iran, margin stabilization to ~11% EBITDA. Conviction M. Confirms it: EBITDA per tonne resumes its steady uptrend. Kills it: renewed geopolitical RM shock.
4. BESS ramp (H2 FY27 commissioning, FY28 revenue Rs 800-900 Cr per GWh). Conviction L-M, guidance revised twice. Treated as free optionality only, valued as a separate SOTP slice worth Rs 0/13/39 per share (B11 3B).

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Backward moat MODERATE (3 of 12: capital efficiency, customer stickiness, switching costs, B01 Block F). Forward emerging moat MODEST at 23/100 with 7 active categories, anchored by qualification lock-in (B2, Strong: VDE, KEMA, CPRI, Powergrid, NTPC, UL approvals with 3-4 year certification cycles), the war chest (G1, Strong), and regulatory tailwinds (R1, Strong: HFFR public-infra mandate, BESS VGF). Direction: constructive but not expanding. Two categories run the wrong way and are flagged as risks not moats: customer concentration rose (top-10 30% to 38% over six years, C2) and working capital deteriorated (G2), the same capex cycle that funds the war chest.

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter TRUSTWORTHY (B08). Clean legal record bar two immaterial items (an Rs 11,800 LODR delay fine, an Rs 2.21 Cr disputed tax demand). Zero pledge, SEBI-filed. RPT within shareholder-approved caps, royalty extraction to related party KIIL fell 72% YoY against a Rs 20 Cr cap. CMD pay fell while profit rose. Delivery grade B: core capacity, margin band and export guidance delivered on schedule across three calls; the FY26 volume target was missed (201,370 MT vs 210k-220k guided, Israel-Iran cited) and BESS guidance was revised down and up twice within two quarters of launch (B05).

**3E. Market Opportunity.** STRONG runway. FY26 revenue Rs 2,948 Cr is 30.8% of a conservative Rs 9,600 Cr serviceable market, leaving 3.25x headroom (B09). SOM-implied 3-year CAGR is 22.1%, well above the 11% base-case revenue assumption used in valuation, so the growth path sits far under the market ceiling (B11 2A). BESS is a separate, nascent equipment runway sized independently.

**3F. Peer Positioning.** Direct segment peers were NOT FOUND. The assigned peers (APCOTEXIND, BEPL) have zero product and end-market overlap; DDev's own named competitors are Dow, Borealis, KLJ Polymers and Shakun Polymer/Orbia (B05, B06). Against the sole usable comparator BEPL, DDev is 2.3x the scale but runs a lower operating EBITDA margin (9.73% vs 15.11%) and lower gross margin (17.30% vs 32.61%) (B10). No peer P/E, P/B, growth or ROCE median is available; the valuation rests on the absolute four-pillar framework, not a relative discount claim.

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

DDev Plastiks is a genuinely good business trading cheap, and the discipline of this framework is refusing to round that up into a clean large buy. The numbers that matter are strong: 28.9% ROCE, net cash, a four-notch credit upgrade, promoters who took less royalty and less salary while profits rose, and a 3.25x runway in a market where the company already holds share. Against a 25x sector-capped destination and a 14x entry, the base case compounds at roughly 34% and even the bear barely loses money because the cheapness itself is the cushion.

The catch sits in one number the company has not yet shown. Cash conversion fell to 42% of profit in FY26 as inventory built through a heavy capex year. If that reverses as new capacity fills, this is a growth-induced blip and the position deserves to grow. If working capital days stay above 90 while volume goes nowhere, the cash drag is structural, the exit multiple falls toward 20x, and a fifth of the fair value evaporates. That resolves on one print in early FY27. Buy Small at the floor, watch that print, and let the evidence decide whether this becomes a core holding.
