## 7. Monitorables and triggers

1. Cash conversion. When the H1 FY27 results file on BSE, divide operating cash flow by profit after tax. A ratio of 0.8 or more supports the growth reading; below the FY26 level of 0.56 with receivables rising faster than revenue says the weakness is structural. This tests the cash flag and the IPO working capital plan.
2. Debtor days and old dues. In the H1 FY27 receivables note, look for year end debtor days at or below 80, dues over six months at or below 12% of gross, and the 2 to 3 year bucket below Rs 1.31 Cr with a stated provision rule. This tests LBF-2 and receivable quality.
3. Who the dealer buyers are. Get the buyer class and ageing of Top Customers 2 and 4 (Rs 12.01 Cr and Rs 8.34 Cr in FY26) through the Halt 1 extraction and the FY26 annual report, and check Maharashtra and Rajasthan police van tenders on GeM in claude.ai. State police end users confirm the billing route reading; private or related buyers reopen LBF-1.
4. Gross margin. Gross margin should hold at or above 27% of revenue in H1 FY27. Below 25% means the opex cushion is spent and the loss falls through to EBITDA, and convergence toward the peer resale band has started.
5. Disclosure test. By the H1 FY27 results, check BSE for any Reg 30 filing that names a Kwick order tied to NFSU, MHA or a state, with a value. None means the 27 September note stays third party positioning. This tests LBF-4.
6. Scheme successor. Watch MHA Parliament replies and the Union Budget documents for a funded successor to SMFC, and state gazettes for BNSS 176(3) notifications. A successor lifts the Rs 416 Cr scheme floor; none means the visible pool shrinks.
7. Supplier power. In H1 FY27, check creditor days (plan 30, FY26 44) and the top supplier share (FY26 40.86%), and get Top Supplier 1's name. A rising share with falling credit confirms supplier power over Kwick and the cyber pass through.
8. Related parties. Check purchases from Gostocks Fintech and Extreme Covet, and whether the Rs 1.37 Cr software under development gets a commissioning date. An MCA lookup showing the MD owns the Gostocks parent, with no arm's length proof, moves the promoter verdict to CONCERN.
