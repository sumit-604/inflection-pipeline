### 2C. Tax and revenue — most material finding of this check
✅ VERIFIED (RHP "Outstanding Litigations" p.322-325, Note 39 Contingent Liabilities p.266)

This is the largest cluster of adverse findings for this promoter/company. On **1 September 2022**, the Income Tax Department conducted a **survey under Section 133A** (not a search u/s 132, but a substantial multi-site survey) across Gaudium IVF group premises. Based on documents, digital data and **WhatsApp chats**, the assessing officer alleged the group **received unaccounted cash income from 74 surrogacy patients, kept unrecorded cash collections, parallel fee ledgers, and cash rent payments**. This single survey has since generated a cascade of assessment orders against **both the Company and Dr. Manika Khanna personally**, covering assessment years 2018-19 through 2023-24:

- **Company, AY2022-23**: Assessment order dated 31 Mar 2024 alleging undisclosed income Rs2,479.22L; current outstanding demand **Rs3,764.85L incl. interest**. Under appeal (CIT(A)); stay granted on 80% of demand after 20% (Rs617.81L) deposited in instalments.
- **Company, Section 271DA penalty** (cash transactions >Rs2L in violation of Section 269ST) — Penalty Order dated 30 Sep 2024, **Rs385.81L**; current outstanding **Rs458.48L incl. interest**. Under appeal; matches the operator-supplied lead exactly.
- **Company, AY2021-22**: demand Rs4.27L (incl. interest); AY2023-24: demand Rs253.09L (incl. interest of Rs18.28L). Both appealed.
- **Dr. Manika Khanna personally**, five separate orders (AY2018-19 through AY2023-24 via her erstwhile proprietorship "Gaudium IVF and Gynae Solutions," acquired by the company in 2021), alleging she personally received unaccounted cash from surrogacy patients: demands of Rs109.52L (AY2018-19), Rs105.44L (AY2019-20), Rs72.69L (AY2020-21), Rs96.65L (AY2021-22), Rs34.90L (AY2023-24) — **total Rs475.45L across 5 claims** (RHP "Claims related to direct and indirect taxes" table, p.322). All under appeal before CIT(A); no adjudication yet.

**Combined Company + Promoter contingent tax exposure ≈ Rs4,974.79L (~Rs49.75cr)** against a Sept-2025 total shareholder's fund (equity) of **Rs5,885.47L** (RHP Capitalisation Statement p.318) — i.e., contingent tax liability equal to roughly **85% of net worth** (independently corroborated by post-RHP market commentary citing "76.4% of equity base," ipocentral.in, 📰 MEDIA REPORTED, undated 2026 IPO-review article, consistent order of magnitude). All amounts are disputed and under appeal; none has been adjudicated against the company or Dr. Khanna as of the RHP date. This is not a single episode — it spans five consecutive assessment years and both the corporate entity and the MD personally, evidencing a **pattern** the operator should weigh heavily, distinct from a one-off dispute. ✅ VERIFIED (documentary; RHP is the primary source, an official offer-document disclosure).
