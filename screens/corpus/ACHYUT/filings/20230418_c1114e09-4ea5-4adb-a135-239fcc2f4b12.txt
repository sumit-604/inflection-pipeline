Summary of the proceedings of the Extra-Ordinary General Meeting 
The Extra Ordinary General Meeting (EGM) of the members of the Achyut Healthcare Limited (“the 
Company”) was held on Monday, 17th April, 2023 at 12:30 PM at the registered office of the Company 
situated at 610 Colonade, Behind Iscon Temple, Opposite Iscon Brts Bus Stand, Iscon – Ambali Road, 
Ahmedabad-380058, Gujarat. 
 
Total numbers of shareholders as on the cut-off date i.e. 10th April, 2023 were 158. Total 9 (Nine) 
members attended the meeting at the venue. The Company has not received any request from 
members to appoint proxies, therefore no proxies have attended the EGM on behalf of members.  
 
Mr. Mahendra C. Raycha, Chairman and Director of the Company chaired the meeting. The requisite 
quorum being present, the chairman called the meeting to order and welcomed the shareholders and 
other invitees present at the EGM. All Directors, KMPs, and the Scrutinizer were present at the EGM.  
 
Chairman of the Audit Committee, Chairperson of Nomination and Remuneration Committee and 
Chairman of Stakeholder Relationship Committee were present at the Meeting. 
 
The Chairman informed the members that the statutory registers required to be kept open for 
inspection during the EGM as per the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 were made available for 
inspection at the registered office. 
 
The Chairman further informed that the remote e-voting commenced on Friday, 14th April, 2023 at 
9:00 A.M. and ended on Sunday, 16th April, 2023 at 5:00 P.M. It was further informed that, Members 
attending the EGM who have not already cast their vote by remote e-voting shall be able to cast their 
vote during the meeting. Thereafter, the Chairman delivered his speech. 
 
The Chairman informed that the Notice convening EGM was sent to the shareholders through e-mail 
and the same was also published in the newspapers. Thereafter, the Notice of the EGM and the 
explanatory statement was taken as read. 
 
The Chairman, thereafter, moved to the item of Business to be transacted at the EGM are as under:  
SPECIAL BUSINESS: 
 
Item No. 
Particulars 
1 
To Consider and Approve Issue of Bonus Shares.
