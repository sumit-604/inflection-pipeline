### PART A: THE FROM STATE

**A1. Archetype, per line**
- Generators for gas engines, gas turbines and steam turbines sold to prime-mover OEMs (the largest line by words; share NOT FOUND, B04): Build-to-spec component maker. Evidence: order-based serial OEM programmes, qualification and design-in (B04.revenue_streams, B04.moats_present). Motors and rail traction parts ride on the same archetype as build-to-print work (B04.revenue_streams), but no motor revenue is disclosed (B05 dropped_triggers).
- Hydro generators and refurbishment: Order-book business. Evidence: project sale, predictability low (B04.revenue_streams).

**A2. The simple analogy**
A turbine or an engine spins a shaft. A generator sits on that shaft and turns the spin into electricity. TD Power builds that generator to the engine maker's specification, much as a firm might build the gearbox for a truck maker (B04, B03). The truck maker cannot ship a truck without a gearbox it has approved. TD Power has made 8,772 such machines to date (presentation slide 24, via B04). Most orders now come from a few engine and turbine makers abroad, and these makers order one purchase order at a time (B04.FLAG-NO-VOLUME-FLOOR; B07.FLAG-EM-NO-VOLUME-FLOOR). The business starts here: a qualified vendor to a small set of makers, with designs it licenses (B04.FLAG-LICENSED-TECH).

### PART B: THE TRANSITION

**B1. FROM to TO (Quality Ladder)**
- Line: generators for gas, steam and engine OEM programmes. FROM R2, cost-advantaged converter-style vendor with project-cycle ordering and a domestic base (domestic sales fell 13.4% in FY26 while exports rose 135.4%, B03.missing_risks; the 10-year ROCE median is 14.6% because FY17 to FY20 returns were low, B01). ## SECTION 3: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Drafted from B01 to B09 per the spec in prompts/13-synthesis-pipeline.md. Stage 13's copy remains the final version.

TD Power Systems builds AC generators, the machines that turn the spin of a gas engine, gas turbine, steam turbine or water turbine into electricity (B04). It also makes motors and parts for rail traction motors, but motors are 105 of the 8,055 units supplied by March 2026 (B03). The filings give no revenue split by product, so the run did not establish the share of any line (B04). Management ranks gas engine and gas turbine generators first by words and puts steam turbine generators at about a quarter of revenue on a call (B04, B05). A power set cannot ship without a generator the engine or turbine maker has qualified, and that is why the customer cannot drop the part easily (B04.moats_present; contract floors NOT FOUND). The customers are makers of prime movers and engine sets, not plant owners: the Siemens Energy ten-year build-to-print framework of 13 Aug 2026, the INNIO long-term supply agreement, and an unnamed major US gas-turbine OEM (B04, B09). Ten customers took 84% of consolidated revenue in FY26, and one unnamed customer rose from 14.10% to 23.17% (B03). These customers order by purchase order, and the Siemens Energy framework states no minimum volume (B04.FLAG-NO-VOLUME-FLOOR). Present demand comes from AI data-centre power and distributed power in the Americas, and exports were 88-93% of quarterly order inflow (B07, B05). The tracker names this demand through "Hyperscaler and data-centre developer capex and behind-the-meter gas procurement" and "Major US gas turbine OEM identified in Q2 FY27 deck" (B09). The peers temper it, because Triveni still calls data-centre steam demand enquiries and only CG booked a data-centre order (B06.FLAG-DATA-CENTRE-NOT-YET-ORDERS). Demand should grow with OEM capacity, which the tracker follows through "Global gas turbine backlog, slot reservations and OEM capacity additions" and "INNIO Jenbacher gas engine bookings, capacity ramp and lead times" (B09). Hydro demand follows "Nepal and South Asia hydropower awards, plus India CEA hydro and pumped storage commissioning", and the Siemens Energy framework is tracked as "Siemens Energy gas and steam turbine orders and its 10-year build-to-print framework" (B09). TD Power's own capacity binds before the market does, at about Rs 3,200 cr for FY28, with Rs 4,000 cr by FY31 only if new capacity is built and no capex plan is filed (B09.capacity_check). Rail demand is shrinking: management takes no fresh rail orders, and "Alstom traction motor stator and rotor supply under the 2017 long-term agreement" tracks the line (B04.FLAG-RAIL-WINDDOWN, B09). The moat sits in gas generators, where OEM qualification scores as a moderate lock-in and the Siemens framework as a strong partnership, though no contract shows a volume floor (B07.active_categories). Core generator designs are licensed from Toyo Denki, Voith and Siemens, expiry is NOT FOUND, and R&D is 0.51% of turnover, so the design edge is rented and not owned (B04, B03). Hydro is project work with no moat, motors are small, and rail is winding down (B04, B07). The talent and competitor-filing tests contribute 0.0, and the forward moat scan reads MODEST at 18.7 against a line of 25 (B07.em_score).

## SECTION 5: PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY

1. TD Power Systems makes AC generators. A generator turns the spin of an engine or turbine into electricity (B04).
2. It built 8,772 generators and motors by September 2026, in 114 countries (B04; presentation slide 24).
3. It reports one business segment. FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 1,856.2 cr, and H1 FY27 revenue was Rs 1,481.4 cr (B04, B05).
4. Its customers are the makers of gas engines, gas turbines and steam turbines. Ten customers gave 84% of FY26 revenue (B03).
5. One unnamed customer gave 23.17% of FY26 revenue, up from 14.10% (B03).
6. These customers pay per order. The Siemens Energy ten-year framework names no minimum volume (B04).
7. Demand comes from data centres and distributed power, mostly abroad. Exports were 88-93% of quarterly order inflow (B07).
8. Demand grows if engine and turbine makers keep adding capacity. The tracker follows those makers by name (B09).
9. The peers do not confirm data-centre orders for generators yet. They say demand is still enquiries (B06).
10. The moat sits in gas generators, where an OEM has qualified TD. It is moderate, and no contract shows a volume floor (B07).
11. The moat does not sit in the designs. The main designs are licensed, and R&D is 0.51% of turnover (B04, B03).
12. The mental model is a climb from a cost-advantaged vendor to a qualified, spec'd supplier. It is a draft, and the proof gate has not fired (Section 2).
13. The fragility read is FRAGILE: six variables must go right, four rest on company narration, and one demand cycle can break the case (Section 4c).
14. The corpus could not establish the product mix, the nameplate capacity, the split of the Rs 240.0 cr rise in other current liabilities, or the Q2 FY27 call content (B04, B13, B00).
15. The biggest open questions: is H1 cash conversion timing or structure, and will Q3 FY27 show revenue of Rs 709 cr or more with order inflow at or above revenue (B05, B13)?
