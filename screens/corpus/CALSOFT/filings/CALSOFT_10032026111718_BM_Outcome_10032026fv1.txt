Subject: Outcome of the Meeting of the Board of Directors held on March 10, 2026 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015, we wish to inform you that the Board of Directors of California Software Company Limited 
(“the Company”), at its meeting held today, i.e., Tuesday, March 10, 2026, at the Registered Office of 
the Company at Workflo, Greeta Towers, Industrial Estate, Perungudi, OMR Phase 1, Chennai – 
600096, and also through video conferencing via Google Meet, has inter alia considered and approved 
the following: 
1. Reconciliation of First and Final Call Money 
The Board took note of the reconciliation summary and bid book furnished by the Registrar and 
Transfer Agent, Integrated Registry Management Services Private Limited, confirming receipt of the 
First and Final Call Money in respect of 81,55,826 partly paid-up equity shares, aggregating to 
₹6,11,68,695. 
2. Constitution of Monitoring Committee 
The Board approved the constitution of a Monitoring Committee for oversight of the Rights Issue 
proceeds and collection of call money, and further designated the Audit Committee of the Company 
to act as the Monitoring Committee for monitoring: 
 
receipt of First and Final Call Money from shareholders 
 
reconciliation of call money received 
 
conversion of partly paid-up equity shares into fully paid-up equity shares 
 
utilisation of funds raised through the Rights Issue 
 
compliance with stock exchange and depository requirements.

3. Conversion of Partly Paid-up Equity Shares into Fully Paid-up Equity Shares 
Based on the reconciliation carried out by the Registrar and Transfer Agent and the Auditor’s Certificate 
confirming receipt of call money, the Board approved conversion of 81,55,826 partly paid-up equity 
shares into fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10 each, subject to receipt of necessary approvals 
from the Stock Exchanges and Depositories. 
4. Authorisation for Regulatory Filings 
The Board authorised the Company Secretary & Compliance Officer to sign, submit and file all 
necessary letters, applications, corporate actions and other documents with the Stock Exchanges, 
Depositories, Registrar and Transfer Agent and other authorities in connection with the conversion of 
partly paid-up equity shares into fully paid-up equity shares.
