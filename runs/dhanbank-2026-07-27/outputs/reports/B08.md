## SECTION 2: LEGAL & REGULATORY RECORD

**2A. SEBI actions.** AR discloses: "During the last three years, there were no penalties or strictures
imposed on the Bank by the Stock Exchange(s) and/or SEBI and/or any other statutory authorities on matters
relating to capital market" and "no significant and material orders passed by the regulators or courts or
tribunals impacting the going concern status ... or future operations" (AR lines 835-841, repeated 3644-3646).
✅ VERIFIED (AR self-disclosure, standard LODR requirement, no contradicting adverse finding surfaced in web
search).

One SEBI-adjacent litigation surfaced: **Dhanlaxmi Bank Ltd vs SEBI**, Kerala High Court judgment 4-Oct-2024
(indiankanoon.org; SEBI website hosts the same judgment: "Judgment of the Hon'ble Kerala High Court in WA
No.1559/2023 – Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited Vrs SEBI and others"). On examination this is **not an action against
the Bank's governance**: Dhanlaxmi Bank is the appellant in its capacity as a **secured lender** asserting
SARFAESI Section 26E priority-of-charge over a mortgaged property against SEBI's recovery/attachment
proceedings (demand notices for Rs 261.35 cr, attachment order 26-Jun-2019, prohibitory order 2-Aug-2019)
against a third-party defaulting borrower/mortgagor. ✅ VERIFIED via Kerala High Court judgment metadata and
SEBI's own enforcement-orders page. This is a routine recovery-priority dispute in which the Bank is a
creditor, not a respondent to a governance charge — recorded here to close the search thread, not as an
adverse finding.

**2B/2C. Criminal cases, tax/revenue actions.** No SFIO, ED/PMLA, IT search-and-seizure, or criminal economic
offence proceeding found against the Bank, its Chairman, MD&CEO, or CFO in any search performed. NOT FOUND
(absence of finding, multiple searches run — see search log).

holder — the Bank's failure was KYC/AML control gap, not an allegation that Bank officers participated in the
underlying offence. ❓ UNVERIFIED detail: exact EOW case name/status not surfaced in search; flagged, not
treated as fact beyond "a current account under EOW investigation" per the RBI order language.

**2D (contd). RBI Prompt Corrective Action (PCA), historical.** Dhanlaxmi Bank was placed under RBI's PCA

regulatory-history fact, material for vintage context but not a live constraint today (current GNPA 2.36%,
31-Dec-2025, ICRA).
