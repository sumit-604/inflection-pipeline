Press Release 
 
Monolithisch India Limited Recognized among Forbes India DGEMS 
2025 ‘Select 200 Companies with Global Business Potential’ 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is proud to announce that it has been selected as one of Forbes 
India DGEMS 2025 ‘Select 200 Companies with Global Business Potential’, a prestigious 
recognition presented jointly by Forbes India and D Globalist. 
 
This exclusive selection places Monolithisch India Limited among a distinguished cohort of high-
growth Indian companies demonstrating strong international scalability, innovation, and 
leadership potential in global markets. 
 
As part of this recognition, the company has been invited to participate in ‘Forbes India presents 
DGEMS 2025’, scheduled to be held on November 21, 2025 at The Taj Palace, New Delhi. 
DGEMS 
is the world’s first forum dedicated to cross-border business expansion and global founder 
collaboration. 
 
Speaking on the recognition, the leadership team of Monolithisch India Limited said: 
 
“Being selected among the DGEMS 2025 ‘Select 200 Companies’ reinforces our 
commitment to excellence, innovation, and global ambitions. This acknowledgement 
strengthens our resolve to continue creating value for our stakeholders while expanding our 
footprint across international markets.” 
 
This milestone marks another step forward in the company’s journey towards global growth and 
long-term strategic expansion. 
 
About Monolithisch India Limited: 
 
Monolithisch is committed to excellence in the manufacturing and supply of high-performance 
industrial refractory materials. With an unwavering focus on quality, innovation, and customer-
centricity, we specialize in the production of premium-grade ramming mass—a critical heat 
insulation and lining consumable extensively used in induction furnaces across the iron, steel, 
and foundry sectors. its advanced refractory products and engineering solutions serving the steel, 
metallurgical, and high-temperature processing industries.
