## 3. VALUE-TRAP TEST (applied to the AVOID's fair value, not to a long)

Is the Rs 330-404 fair value a "melting-number trap" — a figure that looks conservative-and-safe but is actually built on a misclassification?

Partly, yes. The trap in the AVOID is not a melting-ice-cube business; it is a **valuation artifact**: the core multiple is derived from a 12.54% consolidated ROCE and a -25.75 Cr consolidated FCF, both of which are *entirely* the pre-revenue QMPL subsidiary (B01 block_b_trend; analyst_note). The SOTP then ALSO subtracts QMPL as a near-zero Slice 3. The subsidiary is penalised twice. Correcting it lifts fair value ~35%. So the specific number Rs 330-404 is soft. What is NOT soft: the gap to CMP is so large (2.2x-2.7x fair value) that a 35% correction still leaves the stock well above the corrected number. The trap weakens the point estimate; it does not rescue the price.

Counter-trap on the other side: is the standalone "core" genuinely core-clean? The standalone parent carries the *undisclosed same-sector promoter entity* (Taparia Magnetics, B08) and undisclosed relay pre-commercial cost embedded in a single IND AS 108 segment (B11 §3.1, "INPUT UNRESOLVED"). So the standalone 16% ROCE is itself not fully auditable as clean core. The honest read: the true core multiple sits *between* the 13.8x used and the ~15.5x the standalone number implies. That still does not reach CMP.

The pipeline's variant perception: "the market pays 38.5x forward for a 12.54%-ROCE core; we say it is worth 14-17x; we are right and the market is late."

**The market's rebuttal (steelmanned).** The market is not paying 38.5x for the FY27 core. It is paying Rs 758 Cr for (a) a standalone core that actually earns ~16% ROCE and is FCF-positive, (b) a delivered-and-accelerating alloys franchise (1%→10% in two years), (c) a government-approved latching-relay line into a ~5x-larger addressable basket, and (d) an ECMS-approved NdFeB localisation with a sovereign China+1 tailwind and a 5,000t roadmap — in a rare-earth market where India has almost no domestic supply. The market is discounting FY29-31, not FY27. Small-cap strategic-transition names in India routinely trade at 40-60x on exactly this kind of forward optionality, and the framework's insistence on a 3-year window and FY27 forward-core EPS structurally cannot see the option payoff, which is FY29-31 by the pipeline's own admission (deliberation §2: "durable crossover is an FY29-30 event").

**Where the market's rebuttal breaks.** It relies on the *timing* mismatch being the whole story. It is not. Even if I grant the market its 3-5 year horizon AND its optionality AND correct the ROCE double-count AND push the core multiple to the absolute sector-cap ceiling of 25x (Cables/Industrial products, the maximum the framework permits under ANY pillar configuration), I get (§5) a fair value of ~Rs 614 and a Hurdle that still fails. The market is not merely early; it is paying a price that no defensible multiple, on the most generous forward earnings, reaches within the hold. The variant perception SURVIVES — but on a narrower basis than B14 states: not "the core is only worth 14-17x," but "even at the 25x ceiling the price is unreachable."

**Apply the ceiling multiple to the numbers:**

**Maximum defensible fair value ≈ Rs 614.** CMP Rs 882 is still **~30% above** the most generous number the framework can produce.

**Hurdle at the 25x ceiling:** HR(base 12%) = 1.12³ × (25 ÷ 38.5) = 1.405 × 0.649 = **0.91**. HR(bull 17%) = 1.601 × 0.649 = **1.04**. Both far below 1.953. **Still STOP.**

**What EPS CAGR would it take to clear at the 25x ceiling?** (1+g)³ ≥ 1.953 × (38.5/25) = 3.008 → g ≥ **44.3%** sustained for 3 years. The core supports ~12% (bull 17%); the relay (~Rs 40 Cr mature rev) and QMPL (~Rs 60 Cr) options add at most ~Rs 7-9 Cr PAT at *full maturity years out*, nowhere near tripling core EPS inside the 3-year window. A 44% blended EPS CAGR by FY29 is not on the table.

**Conclusion of the decisive test.** Relaxing Pillar 3, crediting the ROCE crossover, upgrading cash quality, and running the core to the absolute sector cap **does not make PERMAGNET investable at Rs 882.** It lifts fair value from Rs 330-404 to ~Rs 450-614, and it lifts the Hurdle from 0.52 to ~0.91 — an improvement, but still a failure. Even the generous fair value implies a 25%-CAGR entry of ~Rs 614/1.953 = **Rs 314** — still less than half of CMP.

**SHARED-CATALYST stress (the framework's explicit ask).** The moment you relax Pillar 3 as above, the SHARED-CATALYST flag stops being MOOT: the alloys furnace would then drive BOTH the Pillar 1 forward-ROCE and the Pillar 3 growth premium (a single point of failure the framework warns against, Section 1B Pillar 3), and the *entire incremental* Rs 210 of upside (Rs 404 → ~Rs 614) rests on two pre-revenue options — relay (slipped once already, guided H2FY27) and NdFeB (zero revenue, inside a negative-net-worth subsidiary with a going-concern EoM). ## 7. PRE-MORTEM — it is August 2028, and the AVOID looks WRONG (stock at Rs 1,300)

*Written in the failure mode where the operator regrets not buying.*

China renewed rare-earth export restrictions in late 2026, and NdFeB scarcity became a front-page story. QMPL's Phase 2 commissioned roughly on time (Q3FY27), first revenue printed Q4FY27, and by FY28 the market stopped valuing PERMAGNET on FY27 core EPS and started paying for the 5,000t-by-FY31 roadmap as a sovereign China+1 asset. The latching-relay line also commercialised in H2FY27 with a named auto customer, adding a second revenue leg. The standalone core — which always earned ~16% ROCE, not the 12.54% consolidated figure the framework anchored on — compounded 15%+, and the consolidated ROCE crossed 13.5% cleanly once QMPL stopped being a pure cost centre. Module B2 turned growth-premium-eligible, the core re-rated toward the 25x cap, and the two option slices, sized at Rs 6-8 Cr base by the pipeline, turned out worth 5-10x that. The framework's 3-year, FY27-forward-core lens simply could not see an FY29-31 payoff that was always the point.

The specific error, in hindsight: the pipeline valued the CORE on a consolidated ROCE and consolidated FCF that were both dragged by the very subsidiary the SOTP quarantined, understating fair value by ~35% at inception, and it applied a 3-year Hurdle to a thesis whose catalysts were explicitly FY29-31. Rs 330-404 was never the right central number; ~Rs 550-650 was, and the market was discounting further out than the model could reach.

**But note what this pre-mortem still concedes:** even in the regret scenario, the *entry discipline* was not wrong — buying at Rs 882 would have required paying 30-40% above even the generous fair value on day one, and the win came from an FY26-line-of-sight-zero catalyst set (two pre-revenue options) firing on schedule. The AVOID mispriced the *number* and the *horizon*; it did not mis-price the *risk* of paying Rs 882 for unprinted revenue.
