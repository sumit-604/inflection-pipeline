## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** JSIPL makes steel pipes and
road safety steel at one plant in Hisar, Haryana. The products are mild steel
black pipes, galvanised pipes and tubes, metal beam crash barriers and
galvanised tubular poles (DRHP, doc cd71cb12, page 172). The margin is the
spread between steel bought and pipe sold. Revenue was Rs 586.40 crore in FY25
(DRHP, doc cd71cb12, pages 65 and 275; rupee figures on this card are derived
from Rs lakh).

**What is changing?** Margin and channel. EBITDA margin rose from 1.7% in FY23
to 6.2% in the nine months to December 2025 (derived, same pages). Dealer sales
rose from 20.25% of revenue to 34.28% over the same span (DRHP, doc cd71cb12,
page 172).

**Why now?** The company went public. The April 2026 DRHP offered up to
1,07,41,149 new shares and 26,86,851 shares for sale by a promoter group
shareholder. The stated objects are debt repayment and general corporate
purposes (DRHP, doc cd71cb12, page 99). Market capitalisation is Rs 612.29
crore (Bull AI company record, 23 September 2026, a service record).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The margin climb must be
operating profit, and it must hold through a full audited year after listing.
FY25 profit before tax was Rs 32.39 crore. Rs 18.34 crore of it was other
income (DRHP, doc cd71cb12, pages 65 and 275).

**What breaks it?** Steel pipe is a commodity. The corpus shows no price
premium, no contract and no spec lock. A steel price swing or a rival's price
cut can take the 6.2% margin back toward the FY24 level of 3.3%.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Revenue is tonnes of pipe times price per tonne. Steel is the main cost, so the
business lives on the spread. The sanctioned power load is 2,336 kW (DRHP, doc
cd71cb12, page 172). Revenue by product: NOT FOUND.

Channel is the one lever the DRHP shows moving. Dealers rose from 30 in FY23 to
53 by 9M FY26, mostly in North India. Dealer sales were Rs 167.86 crore in 9M
FY26 (DRHP, doc cd71cb12, page 172).

**Archetype: commodity converter.** The binding variables are spread,
utilisation, cycle position and cost curve rank. Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17
binds. This card uses no spot year ROCE.
