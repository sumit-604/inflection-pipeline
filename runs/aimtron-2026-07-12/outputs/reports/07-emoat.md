### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

**Documented-only calculation (conservative, audited figures):**
- FY25 standalone capex (Purchase of PP&E, audited cash flow statement): **Rs12.96cr** (AR standalone cash flow, "Purchase of Property, Plant and Equipment" = Rs1,295.93 lakh)
- FY25 standalone net fixed assets (Note 9, net block as of 31-Mar-2025): **Rs30.93cr** (Rs3,092.84 lakh)
- FY25 standalone revenue (per stage brief, audited): **Rs151cr**
- Implied historical fixed-asset turnover = 151 / 30.93 = **4.88x**
- Arithmetic: Rs12.96cr (FY25 capex) × 4.88x (FAT) = **Rs63.2cr** implied incremental revenue = **41.9%** above the Rs151cr base
- This figure is 📄-anchored but almost certainly **understates** the true growth-capex programme, because the Mechatronics greenfield facility had barely begun (CWIP only Rs0.15cr) by FY26 year-end — the bulk of announced growth capex has not yet been spent, let alone capitalised.

**Cross-check using management's own capacity framework (🎙️, not tied to a disclosed rupee capex figure):**
- Existing infrastructure supports ~Rs450-500cr revenue (🎙️) vs. FY25 actual Rs151cr → **~198-231% headroom** from utilisation alone, with no further capex, if demand materialises
- Greenfield facility adds a further ~Rs500cr of capacity (🎙️, "1 SMT line ≈ Rs100cr", 6 new lines) → incremental Rs500cr vs. Rs151cr FY25 base = **+331%**
- Combined target (existing + greenfield) ≈ Rs1,000cr vs. Rs151cr FY25 base = **+562%**, phased over "3 to 5 years" per management's own stated CAGR horizon (40-50% CAGR guidance, AR & Inv. Pres.)

**Reported figure for stage 9 (capex_embedded_growth_pct): 331%**, taken from the greenfield-capacity-addition claim (🎙️) since no audited total capex rupee figure exists for that specific project; the documented-only FAT calculation (41.9%) is provided as the conservative floor. Both figures carry forward with their evidence-type flags — this is a **claim-heavy, not documented, growth story.**
