CRISIL RATING RATIONALE — MENON BEARINGS LIMITED (MBL)
Source: inputs/rating/ratings.pdf (image-only PDF; pypdf/fitz extracted 0 text; transcribed from
rasterized page render by orchestrator, 2026-07-16). Every figure below is from the CRISIL PDF.
Dated: April 23, 2026 | Mumbai. Ratings REAFFIRMED at 'Crisil BBB+ / Stable / Crisil A2'.

===== PAGE 1 =====
Rating Action:
- Total Bank Loan Facilities Rated: Rs.20.96 Crore
- Long Term Rating: Crisil BBB+/Stable (Reaffirmed)
- Short Term Rating: Crisil A2 (Reaffirmed)

Detailed rationale: Crisil reaffirmed BBB+/Stable/A2 on bank facilities of MBL. Ratings continue to
reflect the established market position of MBL in the bearings industry driven by strong clientele and
longstanding presence, and the company's comfortable financial risk profile. Partially offset by revenue
concentration in the automotive (auto) sector, susceptibility to fluctuations in raw material prices and
foreign exchange (forex) rates, and modest scale of operations.

Analytical approach: Crisil COMBINED the business and financial risk profiles of MBL and its
subsidiaries (strategically important, significant operational integration). [i.e. rating is CONSOLIDATED.]

Key rating drivers — STRENGTHS:
- Established market position: >3 decades in bearings. Caters to OEM and replacement demand, domestic
  and overseas. Overseas/exports contributed 30-35% to revenue. Established position + superior product
  quality allow higher margins: OPERATING MARGIN 18.65-20.39% for the two years through fiscal 2025;
  estimated at a similar level in fiscal 2026.
- Strong clientele: key clients include John Deere, Tata Motors Ltd (Crisil AA+/Stable/Crisil A1+),
  International Tractors Ltd and Brakes India Ltd. MBL is either sole supplier or a major supplier of
  bearings for most customers. Steady repeat orders + new customer additions grew revenue to Rs 241 crore
  in fiscal 2025 from Rs 141 crore in fiscal 2021; revenue estimated to have grown over 20% on-year in
  fiscal 2026.
- Comfortable financial risk profile: networth Rs 158 crore, gearing 0.27 time, TOL/adjusted networth
  0.46 time as on March 31, 2025, estimated at similar levels as on March 31, 2026. Debt protection
  metrics strong: interest coverage 12.34 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt 0.54 time for
  fiscal 2025, estimated at similar levels for fiscal 2026. DESPITE DEBT-FUNDED CAPEX towards capacity
  addition in the bi-metal, alkop and brake divisions and solar capex undertaken in fiscal 2026 and likely
  in fiscal 2027, the financial risk profile is expected to remain healthy over the medium term with
  gradual repayment of term loans and healthy accretion to reserve.

Key rating drivers — WEAKNESSES:
- Revenue concentration in the auto industry: 80-90% of total revenue from auto sector; remainder from
  oil & gas, electrical, engineering. Within auto, bulk from commercial vehicle and tractor segments,
  which are inherently cyclical.

===== PAGE 2 =====
- Susceptibility to fluctuations in RM prices and forex: RM costs = 45-50% of operating income/cost
  structure; key RM = steel, aluminium scrap, metal sheets (volatile). Exports 30-35% of revenue -> opmargin
  susceptible to input price & forex moves. Customer agreements include PRICE ESCALATION CLAUSES to
  periodically adjust selling prices for RM price movements; however there is a LAG in implementing price
  changes. Opmargin was 18.65-20.39% in the two years through March 2025; sustenance at healthy level
  remains monitorable.
- Modest, but increasing, scale of operations: revenue modest at Rs 241 crore in fiscal 2025, estimated
  to have grown 20% on-year in fiscal 2026. Modest scale restricts pricing power with customers/suppliers.

LIQUIDITY: ADEQUATE
- Cash accrual expected at more than Rs 31 crore per annum (after dividend payout), comfortably covering
  yearly term debt obligation of Rs 9-9.58 crore over the medium term.
- Bank limit utilisation averaged 35% during the 12 months through March 2026.
- Cash and equivalents stood at Rs 18.08 crore as on September 2025.
- Current ratio healthy at 2.45 times as on March 31, 2025.

OUTLOOK: STABLE. MBL will continue to benefit from strong market position and established clientele and
sustain its healthy financial risk profile.

RATING SENSITIVITY FACTORS:
Upward factors:
- Sustained revenue growth and stable operating margin, leading to net cash accrual of over Rs 40 crore.
- Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle.
Downward factors:
- Decline in revenue or profitability, resulting in net cash accrual below Rs 20 crore.
- SIZEABLE STRETCH IN THE WORKING CAPITAL CYCLE, or larger-than-expected debt-funded capex or
  acquisition or sizeable dividend payout.

About the group: Incorporated 1991, part of Kolhapur (Maharashtra)-based Menon group, promoted by
Ram Menon and family. MBL makes auto components (bearings, bushes, thrust washers, bi-metal strips) at
Kolhapur plant. Listed on BSE and NSE. NITIN MENON is executive chairman.
- Menon Alkop Ltd: wholly owned subsidiary, aluminium die casting + machined components.
- Menon Brakes Pvt Ltd: wholly owned subsidiary, brake lining and brake shoes.
- Menon Bearings New Ventures Ltd: wholly owned subsidiary, NO operations currently.
