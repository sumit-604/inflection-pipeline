**Six diagnostics:**
1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, +10.64% (30.22 vs 27.31, res L124). Total income +12.56% (32.10 vs 28.52, res L127). Both are far below the operator's 30% binary threshold and below the 20% thesis RED line. The prior 10-Jul topline read of Rs 30.48 Cr / +11.61% ties to neither the final revenue-from-ops (30.22) nor total income (32.10); it appears to have been a preliminary estimate now superseded by the reviewed figure. On every definition, the growth leg is weak.
2. **Op EBITDA margin?** Contracted: Q1 FY27 19.76% vs Q1 FY26 23.23% = **−347 bps**. Reported (incl OI) 25.98% vs 27.64% = −166 bps. On the stricter operating definition the margin is below the 22% RED line. **Gross margin — both directions (graft b):** GM 42.20% Q1FY27 vs 40.66% Q1FY26 = **+154 bps YoY**, BUT vs Q4FY26 46.11% = **−389 bps QoQ**, and 42.20% sits **below the FY26 full-year read of 42.93%**. The YoY improvement is off a soft prior-year quarter, not a step-up on the recent run-rate.
3. **Core operating PBT ex-OI?** **Declined −17.34%** (4.91 vs 5.93). Reported PAT declined only −4.96%. **The headline PAT decline materially understates operational deterioration** — the gap is Other Income and a lower tax charge.
4. **What drove the gap (core-PBT −17.34% vs reported-PAT −4.96%)?** Other Income +Rs 0.68 Cr (+56.15%) and a −Rs 0.09 Cr lower total tax (ETR 25.59% vs 25.57% flat, but lower absolute tax on lower PBT). If Other Income reverted to the Q1 FY26 level, post-tax PAT would be ~Rs 0.50 Cr lower, i.e. ~Rs 4.55 Cr / −14.3% YoY — closer to the true operating trajectory.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** Yes, sharply. Depreciation +175.85% and finance cost +125.68% against revenue +10.64% — a classic fixed-cost/lease-absorption gap (Ind AS 116 lease D&A + interest as the EBO/store base builds). This is the ~300 bps margin-compression driver and directly contradicts the deck's "asset-light, debt-free, minimal depreciation ~1.06% of revenue" narrative (FN19); actual Q1 depreciation is 2.49% of revenue (0.75/30.22).
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Yes. OI/PBT jumped to 27.72% (Q1 FY27) from 16.87% (Q1 FY26); FY26 full-year was only 12.61%. Quarterly OI is masking the operating trajectory — strip it and core PBT fell −17.34%.
