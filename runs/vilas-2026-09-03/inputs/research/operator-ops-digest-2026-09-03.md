## 2. Capacity and plants
- CRGO lamination capacity tripled 12,000 -> 36,000 MTPA. Unit 1+2 12,000
  (full). Unit 3 +24,000, commercialised 25-Jul-2025, ramping through FY26.
- FY26 production ~19,856 MT (FY25 12,069 MT), ~64-65% YoY.
- FY27 plan: target 30,000 MT CRGO; ~18,000 MT incremental from Unit 3.
- Unit 3 four product lines:
  1. CRGO laminations, 24,000 MTPA at Unit 3.
  2. Nanocrystalline cores, 240 MTPA. Commissioned FY26; ~30,250 kg sales,
     Rs 3.48 Cr revenue in H2 FY26. FY27 target ~180 MT, Rs 18-20 Cr,
     20-25% PAT margin. One more China machine adding 4-5 MT/month.
  3. Radiator plant, 7,200 MTPA. Commercial sales from Apr-2026. FY27
     utilisation 20-25%, revenue ~Rs 25 Cr, EBITDA ~18-20%.
  4. Copper conductors Phase 1 (PICC, CTC, PI Aluminium). Presentation says
     1,500-1,800 MTPA; concall clarified 3,600 MT/yr install spec, FY27
     target ~1,000 MT sales. Capex Rs 25-30 Cr (term loan + accruals).
     Trial by end-Sep-2026, revenue from H2 FY27. FY27 revenue target
     ~Rs 100-120 Cr at ~4-5% EBITDA.

## 4. Performance and utilisation
- FY26: volumes +64-65% YoY, revenue +30% (Rs 460.7 Cr vs Rs 353.1 Cr).
  Gap driven by CRGO price fall to Rs 180-185/kg.
- Margin drivers: Unit 3 stranded fixed cost; CRGO price drop; some Indian
  mill purchases where prices did not fall with imports.
- FY26 EBITDA 11.17% (FY25 12.68%). PAT margin 8.6% (FY25 9.8%). Net debt
  free; short-term borrowings working-capital linked.
- FY27 guidance: CRGO volume +45-50% (~30,000 MT); revenue +40-50% at CRGO
  ~Rs 200-210/kg. Management math: CRGO ~Rs 600 Cr, copper Rs 100-120 Cr,
  radiators ~Rs 25 Cr, nano Rs 18-20 Cr -> ~Rs 750-780 Cr base; upside to
  ~Rs 1,000 Cr if CRGO Rs 250-280/kg and copper ramps.
- FY27 margins: EBITDA ~11-12%, PAT 7-8% normal. Management says 14% EBITDA
  earlier was cyclical CRGO tightness, not steady state.
- Phasing: H1 weaker (transformer oil price spike, West Asia conflict,
  OEM order delays). ~40% H1 / 60% H2.

## 5. Customers and market
