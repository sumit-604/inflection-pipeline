# Turnaround is real, entry sits far below

Sheela Foam makes branded mattresses and polyurethane foam and sells them through about 13,000 dealers and 1,800 exclusive outlets under Sleepwell, Kurlon, Starlite and SleepX. FY26 revenue was Rs 3,821 Cr, PAT Rs 160 Cr, reported EPS Rs 14.6, and at Rs 807 the shares trade near 55 times reported earnings and about 23 times cash earnings. Five domestic brands and B2B technical foam make up roughly 70% of sales, overseas foam plants in Australia and Spain another 30%, and small stakes in Furlenco furniture rental and Staqo IT sit alongside. Market cap is Rs 8,812 Cr.

The business bottomed in FY25 and is climbing back. PAT troughed at Rs 89 Cr in FY25 and recovered to Rs 160 Cr in FY26, up 78%, while core EBITDA margin rose from about 7 to 8% to 10.8%. The Kurlon merger completed through NCLT during FY26, net debt fell Rs 156 Cr, borrowings came down from the FY24 peak of Rs 1,493 Cr to Rs 908 Cr, and the company paid its first dividend in the decade since listing. The pipeline scored the transition a composite plus 5 of 8, a BUY-candidate, after the operator moved the forward margin read to recovering off the trough rather than stagnant.

Gate 0 reads AVOID at a core score of 32 out of 100, and that number is mechanical, not structural. The FY24 Kurlon acquisition inflated capital employed and collapsed computed ROCE to a FY25 trough of 5.0%, then FY26 recovered to a reported 6.7% as debt was paid down. Reported ROCE is goodwill-depressed; the operating cash ROCE runs near 13%. Cash conversion is FIRING, with CFO to PAT of 2.56 times and operating cash flow of Rs 409 Cr in FY26, though the receivables book grew 27.7% and the cash strength is growth-induced rather than structural.

Management delivers on the headline financials and slips on the operational promises. Grade is B. The 78% profit recovery, the first dividend, Furlenco hitting Rs 370 Cr revenue on target and margin expansion all landed. Against that, the showroom target was cut three quarters running and still missed at about 600 additions, and the Kurlon synergy tail of Rs 40 to 60 Cr has slipped four consecutive quarters on the same overseas machine delay. A useful-life depreciation change lowers FY27 reported depreciation about 20% and flatters the recovery, and Rs 144 Cr of the Rs 161 Cr FY26 profit fell in the second half.

Peers confirm the demand story and contradict the pricing story. Wakefit, Centuryply and Greenlam all corroborate the shift from unorganized to organized and the TDI and polyol cost spike tied to the Middle East conflict. Wakefit directly contradicts SFL's working assumption that competition eases for better-funded peers, stating intensity and price matching rose over the prior six to nine months. SFL's claimed 25 to 35% raw-material pass-through also runs well above the roughly 15% peers report.

Runway is STRONG. The India mattress and branded-foam market sizes to about Rs 21,000 Cr, the organized slice to about Rs 7,000 Cr, SFL holds near 30% of that, and revenue headroom is about 3.3 times. Management's Rs 1 lakh crore market claim reads inflated, about 4.8 times the conservative estimate, and looks like a wider home-and-sleep-economy figure.

Valuation says AVOID at Rs 807. The operator granted a brand-franchise Strategic Premium for the largest mattress brand and floored the governing destination PE at 20 times, above the roughly 16.7 times the framework earned on its own. Destination PE now lands at 20.0 times on the governing RRM track and 21.3 times on the additive track. The Hurdle Ratio is 0.95, a STOP: a 25% compounding return is not reachable at the current price even on bull earnings, because the market already prices the recovery at roughly 18 times FY30 earnings. Year-3 base fair value is Rs 884 on the RRM track, probability-weighted three-year CAGR is about positive 2.4%, and the buyable zone is Rs 362 to 453 with a margin-of-safety price of Rs 362. The current price is about 1.78 times the top of that zone and the stock must fall about 44% to enter. The call is WATCHLIST-for-entry, position Small, since promoter CAUTION bars a larger Tier B sizing.

The devil's advocate case overall SURVIVES and calls the AVOID at Rs 807 indestructible, but argues the entry zone may be too high rather than too low. If the 13% cash ROCE double-counts the Kurlon add-back, the true return on deployed capital is nearer 7 to 11%, which pulls destination PE toward 12 times and an internally consistent entry zone into the mid Rs 200s, a level that would still need a crash. On the governing numbers the zone top of Rs 453 now sits about 2.6% below Rs 465, the lowest annual close the stock has printed since listing, so the operator's 20 times floor lifts the zone to touch the tested price floor and a normal drawdown to prior levels reaches it, a material change from the pre-override top of Rs 378 that sat 18.7% below. Only annual closes are on file, no intraday low. Institutional owners cut their stake from about 31% to 24.7% across the very window the turnaround was building.
