Press Release 
 
Monolithisch India Limited Appoints The Digital Wand as Official Website and 
Digital Partner 
 
Driving Seamless Digital Experience and Brand Engagement 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is pleased to announce the appointment of The Digital Wand as its official 
Website and Digital Partner. 
 
As a strategic digital partner, The Digital Wand will be responsible for the design, development, and 
ongoing management of the Company’s official website, along with supporting digital content, 
security, and compliance updates in alignment with our stakeholder and investor expectations. 
 
"The Digital Wand brings deep expertise in digital transformation, and we are confident that their 
involvement will help us enhance our online presence and deliver a user-friendly and transparent 
experience to all our stakeholders," said Mr. Harsh Tekriwal, Managing Director of Monolithisch India 
Limited. 
 
This engagement reflects Monolithisch India’s commitment to strengthening its digital infrastructure 
in line with its evolving business and regulatory needs.
