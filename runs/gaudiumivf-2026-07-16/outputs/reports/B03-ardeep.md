**Extension 1 — AOC-2 "Professional Fees ₹430.00L" bundles an expensed RPT with a balance-sheet
capitalisation, without disclosure of the split.** AOC-2 (Item 2, Directors' Report Annexure I, p.~48)
discloses Dr. Manika Khanna's FY2024-25 "Professional Fees" as a single line of **₹430.00L**. Cross-referencing
against the Consolidated Notes (Note 43, Related Party Disclosure, p.~154-155, confirmed by direct page read):
Professional Fees (expensed, P&L) to Dr. Manika Khanna = **₹180.00L** in both FY25 and FY24 (flat YoY), and a
separate line "Professional Fees for Development of GAAT" = **₹250.00L** (FY25 only, capitalised as Intangible
Asset Under Development). ₹180.00L + ₹250.00L = **₹430.00L exactly**, confirming that the AOC-2 disclosure line
— the Companies Act mechanism specifically designed to give shareholders visibility into RPT terms — presents
a single blended figure that is 2.4x the true FY25 run-rate of expensed professional fees to the Chairperson,
without separately flagging that more than half the amount (₹250L, 58%) is in fact an intangible-asset addition
under RPT scrutiny, not an operating cost. This sharpens B02's Rank 4 finding (the GAAT capitalisation itself)
with a companion disclosure-quality finding at the statutory-filing level. 🔴 New finding.

**Extension 2 — the ₹900L OCD/loan deployment was a single-meeting, same-day Board decision.** Directors'
