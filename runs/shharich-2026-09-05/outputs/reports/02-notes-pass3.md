## PATTERN-PASS FINDINGS (genuinely new, beyond Pass 1 and Pass 2)

### P3-1 — Note 35.11(E)'s own FY26 column does not sum to its stated total, and the line that would close
the gap (Past Service Cost) is missing from the list entirely 🔴

Section A of Note 35.11 (Change in DBO, standalone p.127/AR p.127, consolidated equivalent) ties exactly:
Opening 102.84 + Interest 6.58 + Current Service Cost 14.83 + **Past Service Cost 27.26** + Benefits Paid
(13.99) + Actuarial loss 1.71 = Closing 139.23. Section E ("(Income)/Expenses recognized in the P&L
Account"), same note, p.131/169, lists: Current service cost 14.83, Interest cost 6.58, (Expected Return on
Plan Assets) 1.01, Net actuarial (gain)/loss 1.61, Effect of Curtailment nil, Plan Amendments nil, Prior
Year Charge nil, "Net Benefit or (expenses)" **24.03**. Summing Section E's own listed components gives
14.83 + 6.58 − 1.01 + 1.61 = **22.01**, not 24.03 — a Rs 2.02 lakh gap that exists *within this one note's
own arithmetic*, for FY26 only (the FY25 column, 9.42 + 6.62 − 1.28 − 4.20 = 10.56, ties exactly to its own
stated total). Section E has no "Past Service Cost" row at all, despite Section A showing Rs 27.26 lakh of
it landing on the obligation this year. Adding the missing Rs 27.26 lakh to Section E's own component sum
(22.01 + 27.26 = **49.27**) lands within Rs 0.23 lakh of Note 32's separately-disclosed P&L "Gratuity"
expense of **Rs 49.04 lakh** (standalone p.123/162) — the cross-note mismatch Pass 2 flagged (Note 32 Rs
49.04 lakh vs Note 35.11(E) Rs 24.03 lakh, a Rs 25.01 lakh gap). This pattern-pass re-read localises the
likely root cause: Note 35.11(E)'s own P&L reconciliation omits the Past Service Cost line it should
include, and even leaving that aside, its four disclosed components do not sum to its own disclosed total.
Same defect appears identically in the consolidated Note 35.11(E) (p.169). This is a genuine intra-note
arithmetic failure, not only a missing narrative (which is all Pass 1 flagged) or a cross-note mismatch
(which is all Pass 2 quantified) — it is wrong within its own four walls.

### P3-2 — Note 35.15 (Fair Values) also misstates the Trade Payables carrying value, and the corruption is
selective, not uniform, across the note 🔴 (refines Pass 2 finding #1)

Direct verification of Note 35.15's carrying-value columns (standalone p.130, consolidated equivalent):
Trade Receivables, Non-current and Current Borrowings, Security Deposits, Bank Deposits >12 months, and
Other Financial Liabilities all tie exactly to their source notes (Notes 9, 17, 21, 4, 20, 24). But **"Trade
Payables 22" is shown as Rs 2,186.41 lakh (FY26) / Rs 2,186.89 lakh (FY25)** in Note 35.15 — against the
correct Note 22 balance of **Rs 2,482.29 lakh (FY26) / Rs 2,449.98 lakh (FY25)** (a Rs 296 lakh / Rs 263
lakh understatement in each year respectively) — alongside the FVTPL investment-line errors Pass 2 already
identified (quoted equity, unquoted equity, mutual funds all showing static, wrong carrying values). This
matters because it rules out an initially plausible, more benign read of Pass 2's finding (that only the
FVTPL/investment rows, which are a comparatively minor Rs 2-3 lakh in absolute size, were corrupted by a
template quirk specific to fair-value-through-P&L instruments). The same broken note also misstates a
working-capital line worth ~19% of total liabilities, while other lines in the identical table are correct
— evidence of a selectively mis-populated disclosure rather than a single template-level defect confined to
one instrument class. Both the standalone and consolidated versions carry the identical error, under the
same unqualified auditor's report.

### P3-3 — Checked and ruled out: no prior-period restatement occurred; the SOCE "Profit for the year" vs
EPS-note PAT gap is Total Comprehensive Income, not an error (two clean results from the pattern re-read)

The Statement of Changes in Equity carries a standard Ind AS column, "Restated balance due to prior period
errors," in both the FY25 and FY26 comparative blocks (standalone p.108, consolidated p.147); the value is
**Nil in every year, both statements** — a genuine check for restatement, with a clean result. Separately,
the SOCE's "Profit / (Loss) for the year" addition to the Profit & Loss reserve is Rs 421.01 lakh
(standalone FY26), which does not equal Note 35.8's disclosed Profit for the Year of Rs 422.22 lakh at
first glance. Tracing this against the P&L statement itself (p.106) resolves it: Profit for the Year 422.22
+ Total Other Comprehensive Income (1.20) [net of tax, driven by the gratuity remeasurement] = Total
Comprehensive Income 421.01, which is what the SOCE actually carries to retained earnings (there is no
separate OCI reserve column in this company's SOCE format). This is standard, correct treatment for a
remeasurement that bypasses P&L recycling under Ind AS 19 — not a numbers-mismatch red flag. Both checks
performed as part of this pass's specific brief (restatements; numbers not matching); both come back clean,
and are recorded here so a downstream reader does not have to re-verify them.

### P3-4 — No new findings from the remaining pattern checks

Events after the balance sheet date: no subsequent-events note exists anywhere in the Notes (standalone or

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Promoter-family remuneration, relatives' salaries and rent together were Rs 596.92 lakh in FY26, 112.7%
   of standalone PBT. What is the Board's policy for aligning promoter-family compensation growth with
   profit growth, and will this ratio be brought back below 100% in FY27?
2. What is the amount, interest rate, tenure and counterparty of the related-party loan(s) referenced in
   Note 35.23(c) ("the Company has granted loans to related parties") and in CARO, and to which subsidiary
   was it advanced?
3. Export revenue rose from Rs 0 in FY25 to Rs 8,554.32 lakh (46.4% of revenue) in FY26. What drove this
   shift, and why does Note 35.13 still state the company has no major foreign currency exposure and does
   no hedging?
4. Of the Rs 3,349.83 lakh of newly-introduced trading revenue (c.1.03% gross margin), how much is expected
   to recur in FY27, and what is management's own internal measure of organic manufactured H-Acid volume
   growth, separate from this trading line?
5. Can management reconcile the FY26 gratuity P&L charge in Note 32 (Rs 49.04 lakh) against the actuarial
   reconciliation in Note 35.11(E) (Rs 24.03 lakh), and explain the nature of the new Rs 27.26 lakh Past
   Service Cost that appears in Note 35.11 Section A but not in Section E?

  Note 35.11(E) does not even sum to its own stated total for FY26; both errors centre on an unexplained

accounting quality: 3/10.
