## The KTM hole, in management's own words
- "still had emotional [notional] growth of one percent, and that growth would have not
  really happened 1%. It would have at least 15% if KTM would have not gone bankrupt and
  that was actually one of the lifetime kind of thing... this is how our business model is
  that we are in so many segments. Everything will never go smooth, something will remain
  resilient." (Q2FY26 call, p.19)
- KTM volumes for the remainder of FY26: "There will be having around 40-50% of their past
  years level, but coming year should be looking very good." (Q2FY26 call, p.17)
- "we had some idle capacity for KTM Yes. So, we are now going to do it and then when KTM
  grows, yes. So now we are flexible." (Q1FY26 call, p.33)
- By Q3FY26 the tone had turned: "KTM since has done extremely robust in the past one
  year, so we are feeling that whatever forecast they are given, that forecast they will
  maintain... but Bajaj is a very aggressive company... they will not allow it to go down".
  (Q3FY26 call, p.21)
- On multi-model depth with one customer: "it's not that if we are working with BMW. So, we
  are working only with one model. We are working with a 300 ton or 390CC model. Also, we
  are working for 800CC also. 1.2 liter also 1.8 liter also." (Q2FY26 call, p.19)
