## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, before reading B05's red flags)

Each item anchored (call, speaker, page).

**IF-1. EBITDA margin frozen at ~4% across all three quarters despite repeated improvement promises.**
Q2: total income ~156.4cr, EBITDA ~6.37cr = 4.07% (Udit Rathi, Nov'25 p.3). Q3: income 160.09cr,
EBITDA 6.41cr = 4.0% (Rajesh Jain, Feb'26 p.3). FY26: income 716cr, EBITDA 28.9cr = 4.04% (Udit
Rathi, Jun'26 p.3). Management promised utilization/backward-integration-driven margin expansion in
Q2 (Nishant Gupta exchange, p.17) and Q3 (Hrudaynath Kolani, Aditi Jain, p.4-5). Not delivered in
the window. THESIS-CRITICAL.

**IF-2. Capacity-utilization figures do not reconcile across quarters.**
Melting shop: 55-60% Q2 (Udit Rathi, Nov'25 p.4-5) → 60-65% Q3 (Rajesh Jain to Raj Shah, Feb'26 p.6)
→ FY26 average ~53% with current run-rate 50-52% Q4 (Udit Rathi p.4; Maya Nambiar exchange p.12).
A full-year average (53%) that sits below two intra-year quarterly figures (55-65%) is not
reconciled and was never surfaced by management. THESIS-CRITICAL.

**IF-3. TMT direct-charging capex slipped its own stated completion date.**
Q3: "that process will be completed by -- in this quarter or early next quarter" (Rajesh Jain to Raj
Shah, Feb'26 p.6). Q4: "we are implementing direct charging system as I said earlier to the TMT
mill" / "we have also initiated implementation" — still not complete (Udit Rathi, Jun'26 p.4).
Cost-savings-critical item, missed vs the management's own window.

**IF-4. Management refuses a specific EBITDA margin band — asked all three quarters, never answered.**
Q2 (Arth Jain, p.7 OPM stagnant-at-4% question), Q3 (Hrudaynath Kolani p.4; Aditi Jain p.5), Q4 (Raj
Shah p.5). Every answer qualitative ("subject to market conditions," "difficult to predict"). This
is the single most financially material recurring question and was deflected each time (repeated
evasion, 3 quarters).

**IF-5. GST/ITC department disputes not volunteered — surfaced only under analyst questioning.**
Never in any opening remarks. Raised only when Vidhi Purohit cited BSE intimations directly (Jun'26
p.9). Management: relates to "alleged issues with input ITC," "various stages of adjudication,"
"don't foresee any demand fructifying" — reassuring but unquantified.

**IF-6. Rooftop-solar / green-power ambition quietly downscaled across the window.**
Q2: team "in touch with potential suppliers," management instruction to "maximize whatever we can"
(Abhishek Sharma exchange, Nov'25 p.14). Q3: "not yet installed... in discussion with consultant"
(Meera Jain, Feb'26 p.8). Q4: "still studying feasibility," now sized at "1 to 2 megawatts... need
not translate to a large consumption" (Sakshi Shinde exchange, Jun'26 p.6-7). Green power via open
access disclosed as "more than one-fourth" (>25%) of consumption (Jun'26 p.3).

**IF-7. FY26 revenue "beat" is a low-base artifact.**
FY26 income +41.7% YoY, well above the ~20% guidance, but the TMT mill was near-idle in FY25 and
only restarted April 2025 (Udit Rathi, Jun'26 p.2-4; Rajesh Jain, Feb'26 p.4). Rolling production
47,440t → ~1,02,000t = ~115% (management says "almost 117%," Jun'26 p.4-5). Growth is base-effect,
not like-for-like proof of a sustainable 20-25% CAGR.

**IF-8. TMT-vs-stainless volume/revenue bifurcation refused, two quarters running.**
Q3: "the bifurcation is not with us right now... we will definitely send you" (Management to
Hrudaynath Kolani, Feb'26 p.3-4). Q4: "I don't have a specific number for the separate volumes for
TMT and stainless steel" (Management to Raj Shah, Jun'26 p.5). Promised offline in Q3, still unfilled
in Q4. Repeated non-disclosure (2 quarters).

**IF-9. Customer-concentration percentage never disclosed.**
Management asserts it is "quite hedged... not dependent on any large customer" (Udit Rathi to Arth
Jain, Nov'25 p.12) but declines to give a top-customer or top-5 share in any call.

**IF-10. Competitors / the "two IBC-acquired plants" oversupply excuse never named or quantified.**
The core external margin excuse (Q2 Udit Rathi p.7; Q4 Vidhi Purohit exchange p.14) is repeated
almost verbatim but with no company names, capacities, or timing — unverifiable from the calls.

**IF-11. Inventory days jumped 29→48; management could not confirm the figure.**
Arth Jain pressed twice; Udit Rathi: "I'm not very sure about the number of days" (Nov'25 p.6).
Working-capital-discipline question management could not answer with precision.

**IF-12. Production-number disclosure REGRESSED between Q1 and Q2 FY26 — analyst flagged it, management deflected.**
Sandeep Goyal (Davis Index): "in the last Q1 result, you mentioned the production number in your
presentation and press release. But this time, I did not see the same production number for Q2... Both
for SMS and rolling product." Udit Rathi deflected: "if that is something specific that you want, our
team can get back to you... Maybe the production numbers we may have missed out to mention" (Nov'25
p.19). A quarter's production figures were dropped from the disclosure pack and management would not
give them on the record. Disclosure-quality/credibility signal directly bearing on the volume-ramp
narrative. NOT SURFACED ANYWHERE IN B05.

**IF-13. FY26 PAT growth (+39.24%) is stated "without considering the exceptional items in the PAT
figures of last year" (Udit Rathi, Jun'26 p.3).** FY25 PAT contained exceptional items; management
adjusts them out to present the growth rate. Disclosed openly (so mild), but a comparability caveat
B05 does not surface. MINOR.
