## NEW FINDING 1 — MD's personal guarantee and directors'/associates' guarantees on secured borrowings
Note 6(a)(viii) (standalone, p.116-117; consolidated equivalent, ~p.166-167): "Unconditional and Irrecoverable Personal Guarantee of Managing Director" secures the term loans. Note 9(a)(v) (p.118-119; consolidated equivalent): short-term cash credit/WCDL facilities are secured by "Personal / Corporate Guarantee of Directors and Associate Concerns." Pass 1 covered the third-party property mortgages but did not note that the Managing Director (Sukrit Bharati) has personally guaranteed the company's secured debt. 🟡 Watch — standard for Indian SME lending, but a real personal-financial-entanglement data point alongside the promoter family's already-large capital and property dealings with the Company.

## NEW FINDING 2 — Corporate-guarantor count is inconsistent between standalone and consolidated notes
Standalone Note 6(a)(ix) (p.117): "Corporate Guarantee of Associate Concerns / Property Owners - M/s Filament & Filaments & M/s Luma Lamps Private Limited" — TWO named corporate guarantors; Reprolite Papers (India) Pvt Ltd is listed only as a property mortgagor in clause (vi), not as a guarantor.
Consolidated Note 6(a)(ix) (equivalent page, ~p.166): "Corporate Guarantee of Associate Concerns / Property Owners - M/s Filament & Filaments, M/s Luma Lamps Private Limited & M/s Reprolite Papers (India) Pvt Ltd" — THREE named corporate guarantors, adding Reprolite Papers.
Same fiscal year, same lender security package, two different counts of who has actually guaranteed the Company's debt. 🔴 Red Flag — a genuine cross-note contradiction on a solvency-relevant disclosure (who stands behind the bank debt if VOEPL defaults).

## NEW FINDING 3 — Name-spelling inconsistency suggests the "Luma Lamp" guarantor may in fact appear in the related-party list, contradicting Pass 1's framing
The Pass 1 report states "None of these three entities appear in the Note 32 related-party list." Re-checking Note 32A(e) (standalone, "Enterprises over which their relatives exercise significant influence or control," p.132) shows a listed entity "Lumalamp Limited." Across the borrowing notes the same apparent entity is rendered three different ways: "M/s Luma Lamp Private Limited" (Note 6(v), Note 9(iv)), "M/s Luma Lamps Private Limited" (Note 6(ix), plural). "Lumalamp Limited" (no space, "Limited" not "Private Limited") in the RP list is a plausible match for the same business under inconsistent legal-name rendering. If confirmed, Luma Lamp is NOT wholly absent from the related-party universe as Pass 1 characterized — it likely IS captured in the Note 32A relationship list, but the guarantee itself is never quantified or referenced as an RPT in Note 32B's transaction table. Separately, "M/s Filaments & Filaments" (Note 6(iii)) is also rendered "M/s Filament & Filaments" (singular) in the same standalone note's clause (ix) — the same typo pattern. Filaments & Filaments and Reprolite Papers (India) Pvt Ltd have no plausible match anywhere in the Note 32A relationship lists under any spelling variant checked. 🔴 Red Flag (refines Pass 1 Finding #3) — this changes the finding from "three entities entirely omitted from the RP universe" to "one entity (Luma Lamp) likely disclosed in the RP relationship list but its guarantee unquantified/unlinked in the RPT table, while two entities (Filaments & Filaments, Reprolite Papers) remain genuinely undisclosed as related parties despite mortgaging/guaranteeing company debt and (for Filaments & Filaments) selling the Company its own plant land for ₹2,418 lakhs." A management question should ask directly whether Filaments & Filaments, Luma Lamp/Lumalamp, and Reprolite Papers are promoter/relative-controlled entities.

## NEW FINDING 4 — Siemens Finance long-term loan (₹1,378.70 lakhs, ~5.5% of total LT borrowings) is disclosed in standalone Note 6 but missing from the equivalent consolidated Note 6 lender table
Standalone Note 6 (p.116-117) lists "Siemens Finance 1,378.70 (FY25) / 581.46 (FY24)" as one of nine secured term lenders, contributing to a standalone Total of ₹8,258.93 lakhs. The consolidated Note 6 lender table (~p.166) lists only eight lenders (Axis, Yes Bank, Mahindra Finance (nil), SIDBI, HDFC GECL, ICICI, Bajaj Finance, Union Bank) with no Siemens Finance line and the table is not shown with a Total row in the extract. The Consolidated Balance Sheet's own Long-Term Borrowings aggregate (₹8,260.44 lakhs) is nonetheless close to the standalone total, implying Siemens Finance economically remains embedded in the consolidated balance-sheet number even though its disclosure line was dropped from the consolidated notes table. 🟡 Watch — an apparent drafting/completeness gap in the consolidated notes rather than an accounting error, but it means a reader of the consolidated notes alone cannot see this lender or its 5.5%-of-book exposure.

## NEW FINDING 5 — New-plant location and count contradict between standalone and consolidated pre-operative-expense footnotes; link to the related-party land purchase is undisclosed
Standalone Note 21(b) (p.126): "Pre-operative expenses includes expenditure incurred on the new plant located at Janori (Plant B2), where commercial operations have not yet started. The new plant will be operational in the next financial year." — ONE plant, "Janori."
Consolidated Note 22(b) (equivalent page): "Pre-operative expenses includes expenditure incurred on the new plants located at Mohadi & Vilholi, where commercial operations have not yet started. Both the new plants will be operational in the next financial year." — TWO plants, "Mohadi & Vilholi," no mention of Janori.
Both footnotes describe the identical ₹43.08 lakh Preliminary/Pre-operative Expenses balance. Separately, standalone Note 14(b) (p.122) discloses an ₹861 lakh related-party land purchase from KMP relative Nikitha Shravan Poddatur at "Gat No. 40 & 41, Mohadi, Nashik" — i.e., "Mohadi" is independently confirmed elsewhere in the standalone notes as a real site tied to a related-party transaction, yet the standalone pre-operative-expense footnote never mentions Mohadi at all, only "Janori." 🔴 Red Flag — investors cannot determine from the standalone notes alone how many new plants are under construction, where, or that one of the sites (Mohadi) sits on land bought from a promoter relative; this compounds Pass 1's Top Finding #3 (related-party land purchase) with a capex-disclosure inconsistency Pass 1 did not connect.

## NEW FINDING 6 — Gratuity discount-rate assumption for the FY24 comparative differs between standalone and consolidated notes
Standalone Note 29 (p.130): FY24 discount rate = 7.25% p.a. Consolidated Note 30 (equivalent page, ~p.172): FY24 discount rate = 7.50%. Both purport to disclose the same actuarial assumption for the same company and the same prior year. 🟡 Watch — a genuine cross-note numeric contradiction on an actuarial input, immaterial in rupee terms given the tiny gratuity balance (₹27.88 lakhs) but indicative of the same drafting-carelessness pattern seen elsewhere in this AR.

## NEW FINDING 7 — Net actuarial LOSS (not gain) recognized on gratuity this year, nuancing Pass 1's characterization
Note 29 (p.130) shows "Net Actuarial Gain/(Loss) recognized in the period: (1.76)" for FY25 versus "0.58" (a gain) for FY24. Pass 1 flagged the doubled withdrawal-rate assumption (5%→10%) as "directionally consistent with...reduce reported expense." The actual net actuarial line moved from a ₹0.58 lakh gain last year to a ₹1.76 lakh loss this year — i.e., the combined effect of all assumption changes (discount rate cut 7.25%→7.00% plus withdrawal-rate increase) was expense-increasing, not decreasing, at the actuarial-gain/loss line specifically, even though the total P&L gratuity expense (₹9.11 lakhs) was roughly flat YoY (₹9.07 lakhs FY24). 🟡 Watch — a refinement, not a reversal, of Pass 1's flag; the amounts are immaterial (sub-₹2 lakh) but the direction claimed in Pass 1 was not fully accurate at the actuarial-gain/loss sub-line.

## NEW FINDING 8 — Balance non-confirmation extends across nearly the entire working-capital position, not just inventory and cash
Note 37, "Balance Confirmations" (standalone, p.136): "The balances in the accounts of Trade Receivables, Trade Payables, Loans and Advances, Other Current Assets and Other Current Liabilities are subject to confirmation/reconciliation, if any; the Management does not expect any significant variance from the reported figures." Pass 1's Top Finding #2 was scoped to inventory (₹21,245.49L) and cash (per Note 38(d)); Note 37 shows the SAME unverified/unconfirmed status also applies to trade receivables (₹3,044.71L), trade payables (₹13,138.60L), loans & advances, and other current assets/liabilities — collectively the bulk of the balance sheet's current-asset and current-liability captions rest on management's own assertion of "no significant variance," not independent confirmation. 🔴 Red Flag — materially elevates the severity of Pass 1's verification-gap finding: essentially the entire net working capital position (not just the two items Pass 1 named) lacks independent balance confirmation.

## NEW FINDING 9 — Unreconciled GST input tax credits, with effects not yet booked; partially visible only in the consolidated notes
Note 38(a) (standalone, p.137): "During the year, the company has availed various input tax credits of GST paid on procurement of goods and services. The company is in the process of reconciliation of such input tax credits with its vendors and the GST returns filed during the year. Effects of such reconciliation, if any, have not been considered in the books of accounts." This is a distinct, currently open and unquantified GST exposure, separate from the FY2017-18 GST dispute Pass 1 already flagged from CARO. It is partly corroborated by the consolidated Note 22 (Other Current Assets, equivalent page ~p.169) line "GST Receivables (Excess ITC): 133.40 (FY25) vs 3.58 (FY24)" — a 37x jump in unreconciled/excess GST credit receivable that is not broken out at all as a separate line in the standalone Other Current Assets note (Note 21, p.126), where it is presumably folded into an unlabelled total. 🟡 Watch — an open-ended accounting exposure that could still move the books once reconciliation completes.

## NEW FINDING 10 — Auditor discloses sample-based ("test check") vouching, an explicit limited-scope-testing statement embedded in the notes
Note 38(b) (p.137): "We have applied test check method of vouching regarding purchase, sales, vouchers, expenses, whenever we found necessary." Combined with Finding 8 (no balance confirmations) and Pass 1's Note 38(d) (no physical verification of cash/inventory), this completes a three-part pattern of explicitly disclosed audit-scope limitation spanning substantive testing method, balance confirmation, and physical verification — unusually candid for a note to state, and worth reading together as a single assurance-quality theme rather than three separate minor items. 🟡 Watch.

## NEW FINDING 11 — ESOP note contains an apparent drafting error contradicting its own movement table (second QC lapse in the AR)
Note 34 (p.134): "There were cancellations or modifications to the awards in year ending 31 March, 2025." As written, this states cancellations/modifications DID occur; the immediately following options-movement table shows only "Forfeited during the year: 12,820" with no "cancelled" or "modified" column populated, and standard drafting convention (and context) strongly suggest the intended sentence was "There were NO cancellations or modifications." 🟡 Watch — a second internal-consistency/proofreading lapse in the same AR (the first being Pass 1's Authorised Share Capital table inconsistency in Note 3), part of a broader QC pattern this pass is surfacing (see Findings 2, 4, 6, 12).

## NEW FINDING 12 — Consolidated MSME note has mislabeled comparative-year headers and a numeric mismatch versus the standalone MSME note

## NEW FINDING 16 — Unusually large "Stipend Expenses – Internship" line, 18.5% of total employee benefit costs

# PASS 2 NEW FINDINGS SUMMARY

This second full re-read surfaced a pattern Pass 1 did not name explicitly: **at least six concrete internal inconsistencies between the standalone and consolidated versions of notes that describe the identical underlying fact** — the count of corporate guarantors on secured debt (Finding 2), the count and location of new plants under construction (Finding 5), the FY24 gratuity discount-rate assumption (Finding 6), the presence/absence of the Siemens Finance lender line (Finding 4), and the MSME year-labelling/amount mismatch (Finding 12) — plus a standalone-only drafting error in the ESOP note (Finding 11). Individually several of these are small in rupee terms, but together they indicate the standalone and consolidated note sets were not cross-checked against each other before filing, which is itself a data point on disclosure control quality that compounds Pass 1's Authorised Capital Table finding.

The second most important new thread is that the auditor's scope-limitation language is broader than Pass 1 captured: Note 37's balance-non-confirmation caveat covers trade receivables, trade payables, loans & advances, and other current assets/liabilities — not just the inventory/cash items Pass 1 flagged — and Note 38(b)'s explicit "test check" (sample) vouching statement adds a third dimension to that same theme (Findings 8, 10). This materially broadens the scope of Pass 1's Top Finding #2.

Third, the name-spelling analysis of the "Luma Lamp"/"Lumalamp" guarantor entity against the Note 32A related-party relationship list (Finding 3) partially refines — without fully reversing — Pass 1's Top Finding #3: the entity is plausibly present in the RP relationship list under an inconsistent name, even though its debt guarantee is never quantified as a related-party transaction; Filaments & Filaments and Reprolite Papers remain genuinely unmatched anywhere in the RP lists.

No new findings materially alter the four items Pass 1 ranked #1, #4-#10 in its Top 10; this pass mainly adds breadth and precision to items #2 and #3, and surfaces a new documentation-quality theme (standalone/consolidated inconsistency) that did not exist as a named finding in Pass 1 at all.
