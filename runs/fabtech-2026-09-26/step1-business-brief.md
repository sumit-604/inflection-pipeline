## The engine the thesis would rest on

An order-book conversion story. The bull reading: IPO money funds the
working capital a turnkey EPC needs, a >Rs 900 Cr book converts at 18-24
months, Saudi localisation and two acquisitions add scale, and contribution
margin steps up. The bear reading: the book has sat at ~Rs 900 Cr for a year,
Q1 grew 10% against a 20-25% guide, operating margin fell from 15% to 9%
while IPO interest propped PAT, and debtors stretched to 182 days with zero
CFO. The observation that separates them: order-book burn (revenue plus
closing book minus opening book = inflow) and debtor days in H2 FY27, the
half management says carries the year.
