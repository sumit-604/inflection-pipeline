## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** A CDMO (55% of FY26 revenue), a hospital-generics business led by inhalation anaesthetics (30%) and an Indian consumer-health brand portfolio (14%), from 17 plants (FY26 deck, p.9; Q2 deck, p.5). FY26 revenue was Rs 8,869.08 crore; the year closed with a loss of Rs 325.94 crore after a Rs 196.14 crore exceptional charge (AR26, p.37). The money is made, or not, in the CDMO (Jul-26 call, p.17).

**What is changing?** Utilisation. Plants in the US, UK and Canada were built ahead of demand, so fixed costs sit on half-empty sites (CARE, p.4). The FY30 vision is a US$2 billion company at a 25% EBITDA margin, early-teen PAT margin and high-teen ROCE, from 12.8% in FY26 (AR25, p.14).

**Why now?** H1 FY27 revenue grew 23% and EBITDA 72%, margin up 391 bps to 13.7% (Q2 deck, p.10). The FY27 revenue growth outlook was raised to 19% to 20% (Q2 deck, p.9).

**What must be true?** The overseas subsidiaries must stop losing money: eleven of them lost Rs 245.59 crore in H1 FY27 (Q2 res, p.14). Tax must fall toward the 24% to 25% the CFO calls normal (Jul-26 call, p.9). Net debt must stop rising (Q2 deck, p.22).

**What breaks it?** Another destocking by a large on-patent customer, which cut CDMO revenue 10% in FY26 (FY26 deck, p.2). A US FDA action overseas; Morpeth holds an open Form 483 (filings/20260914_06e6114c, p.1).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Regulatory record: present.** 427 regulatory and 51 US FDA inspections cleared since FY12 with no Official Action Indicated (Q2 deck, p.15). **Switching costs: present.** 13 of the top 20 customers use two or more sites; 13 have stayed over seven years (FY26 deck, pp.20, 23). **Pricing power: partial.** Ex-US anaesthesia faced "intensified competition" (AR26, p.15). **Cost position: weak today.** Capital intensity 0.60x to 0.66x of revenue, FY22 to FY26 (CARE, p.4).

**Verdict: a qualification and switching-cost moat is present in the CDMO; the returns moat is absent until the overseas sites fill.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R2, converter returns on a spec'd-supplier footprint.** The CDMO has R3 qualities, qualification and switching costs, but earns R2 returns: low double-digit ROCE and PAT near zero for four years (FY23 loss Rs 186 crore, AR25, p.13; FY26 loss Rs 326 crore).

**TO: R3, value-added spec'd supplier with durable 20%-plus returns.** The FY30 vision: 25% EBITDA margin, early-teen PAT margin, high-teen ROCE (AR25, p.14).

Management's claim, once: "the single largest driver for our EBITDA margins to improve will be operating leverage and related to getting more of our facilities at the target scale" (Jul-26 call, p.10).

Ladder check. One rung over three and a half years fits the base rate. A high-teen ROCE reaches the R3 boundary, not R4.

## 8. Proof check

**The triggers claim operating leverage. The last two quarters show it, at the EBITDA line.** Q1 FY27 revenue rose 17% and EBITDA 72%, margin up about 400 bps to 12.5% (Q1 deck, p.2). Q2 FY27 revenue rose 28% and EBITDA 72%, margin up 372 bps to 14.7% (Q2 deck, p.10). About 9 to 10 points of Q1 growth was currency (Jul-26 call, p.14).

**At the PAT line the proof is absent.** H1 FY27 was a loss of Rs 64.35 crore; Q2 a profit of Rs 5.03 crore (Q2 res, p.8). Trailing-four-quarter EBITDA of Rs 1,416 crore on Rs 9,779 crore of revenue is 14.5% (derived from FY26 deck p.36), below FY25's 17.3%.

**Proof point named: a trailing-four-quarter EBITDA margin above 17.3% with the eleven other-audited subsidiaries in aggregate profit.** Neither is met.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**CARE AA; Stable and CARE A1+, reaffirmed 17-Jul-2026** on Rs 3,167 crore of facilities (CARE, p.1). CARE sets a diversified business and promoter backing against "the decline in profitability, capital-intensive nature of the business, and the elevated leverage resulting from debt-funded capex" (CARE, p.1). Net debt to PBILDT was 4.28x, above CARE's 3.5x negative trigger; CARE expects below 3.5x in FY27 and a mid-teens margin (CARE, pp.1, 4). Positive triggers: net debt to PBILDT near 1.0x and ROCE of 15% (CARE, p.2).

The rating sees a safe borrower whose strain is temporary if the plants fill. It does not rate the equity claim.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Two quarters prove recovery at the EBITDA line; PAT, ROCE and overseas-subsidiary profit have not moved (step 8).
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, with the structural reading live.** Artifact: fixed costs on new overseas plants ahead of revenue (CARE, p.4). Structural: four years without an adequate PAT and a tax structure that leaks. The aggregate PBT of the eleven other-audited subsidiaries at the FY27 results separates them.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Trailing PE is not computable on a loss. Enterprise value of about Rs 35,853 crore is 25x trailing EBITDA of Rs 1,416 crore (derived). On the FY30 aspiration, US$2 billion at an early-teen PAT margin, the market cap is already 13x to 14x that FY30 profit (derived at the Rs 92.17 per dollar forward rate, AR26, p.161).

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** The climb is narrated and partly evidenced, the optics are clean at the EBITDA line, and the TO state is priced three and a half years early.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The recovery is real, the balance sheet is rated AA, and the CDMO has qualification and switching costs. Against that, consolidated profit is near zero, tax absorbs the gains, net debt rose Rs 695 crore in a half, and the price pays for FY30 now. A full `/step1` run cannot find a 25% CAGR entry on these numbers.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first, if the operator overrides**
1. The aggregate PBT and tax of the eleven other-audited overseas subsidiaries (Q2 res, pp.8, 14; Jul-26 call, p.9).
2. The original FY27 guidance of 29-Apr-2026 against the 19% to 20% upgrade (Q2 deck, p.9).

**What would change the view**
- Up to WATCH: two quarters with the overseas subsidiaries in aggregate profit, a tax rate below 35%, and net debt flat.
- Up to PROCEED: the above, plus a forward multiple inside the R3 neighbourhood.

## 13. Forward view

**FY26A PAT: loss of Rs 325.94 crore reported; loss of Rs 130 crore before exceptional items** (AR26, p.37; FY26 deck, p.36).

**Basis tier: B, run-rate on a guided revenue line.** Revenue growth of 19% to 20% is guided (Q2 deck, p.9); no profit number is. Margin and tax come from the delivered H1 FY27 and the filed note that H2 is "typically stronger than H1" (Q2 deck, p.12).

- **Arithmetic.** Guided revenue gives H2 FY27 of about Rs 5,700 crore against Rs 4,892 crore in H2 FY26, whose EBITDA margin was 15.2% (derived). At 17% to 19%, H2 EBITDA is Rs 970 crore to Rs 1,090 crore. Less H1 run-rate interest Rs 176 crore and depreciation Rs 470 crore, plus associates and other income Rs 118 crore (Q2 res, p.8), H2 PBT is Rs 420 crore to Rs 560 crore. At the elevated 40% to 50% tax, H2 PAT is Rs 210 crore to Rs 340 crore.
- **FY27E PAT: about Rs 200 crore, band Rs 150 crore to Rs 275 crore,** after the H1 loss of Rs 64.35 crore. **Growth:** not meaningful against a loss.
- **Forward PE: NOT APPLICABLE.** The last reported year and half are losses, so the loss-maker rule applies. The gate to profitability is H2 FY27 EBITDA above about Rs 645 crore at the elevated tax rate (derived); the real gate is the tax line. For the ranking only: Rs 30,994 crore over Rs 200 crore is about 155x.
- **The two readings.** Tax near 50% gives Rs 150 crore; tax near 35% gives Rs 275 crore. The Q3 FY27 tax charge separates them.
