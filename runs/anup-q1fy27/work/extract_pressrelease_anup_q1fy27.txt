47	     Q1 FY27 performance reflects planned execution strategy, very strong order booking
48	
49	  Highlights for Q1 FY27 (Comparison on a YoY basis):
50	     • Revenue of ₹125 Cr achieved in line with planned Q1 execution, while gross margin remains intact.
51	     • Highest pending orderbook visibility (including LOI) of ₹985 Cr.
52	     • Strategic growth initiatives gained momentum with orders secured for new proprietary & licensed
53	         products and the commencement of Air-Cooled Heat Exchanger manufacturing for export markets
54	     • FY27 focus remains on stabilization and consolidation of the business.
55	
56	  Ahmedabad, August 06, 2026: The Anup Engineering Limited (ANUP), one of India’s leading
57	  manufacturers of static process equipment, catering to core industries such as Oil & Gas,
58	  Petrochemicals, emerging new‑age energy segments including LNG/LPG and Hydrogen, as well as
59	  high‑technology sectors such as Nuclear and Aerospace, Energy Storage today announced its financial
60	  results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026.
61	
62	  The result reflects a planned operating performance during the quarter, which also includes
63	  debottlenecking our operations which got disrupted due to geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain
64	  under a lot of strain. This was a step in the right direction to ensure smooth operations during rest of
65	  the year.
66	
67	  Consolidated Financial Highlights:
68	      •   Consolidated revenue for Q1 FY27 stood at ₹125 Cr; EBITDA for the quarter was ₹9.2 Cr.
69	      •   Performance during the quarter reflects a planned lower execution due to low order booking
70	          during last year, while ongoing global uncertainties, supply chain challenges, elevated freight
71	          and energy costs, added additional pressure on availability and prices of raw material.
72	      •   EBITDA margins were impacted entirely on account of lower revenue leading to under-
73	          absorption of fixed costs.
74	      •   Highest ever order booking during the quarter of ~₹315 Cr and ~₹540 Cr YTD.
75	      •   Booked order of more than ₹150 Cr for Thermal Power plants, entering elite group of
76	          manufacturers of Critical Heat-Exchangers for the sector, which is expected to see significant
77	          growth in near future.
78	      •   Bagged order of two proprietary license products in line with strategic intent to move in to
79	          niche segment.
80	      •   Started execution of Two large Air-Cool Heat Exchanger for a marquee customer in Germany.
81	      •   The Company remains committed to protecting margins and maintaining healthy cash flows
82	          despite cost pressures, particularly from elevated steel prices and supply chain disruptions.
83	
84	  Outlook for FY27
85	     • FY27 is expected to be a year of stabilization, strengthening of fundamentals, consolidation
86	         and risk management as the Company navigates an uncertain global business environment
87	         influenced by geopolitical tensions, ongoing conflicts and supply chain challenges, which
88	         seemingly getting in to low impact phase.
89	     • Healthy pending orderbook (including LOI) of ₹985 Cr (of which ~₹240 Cr booked for FY28)
