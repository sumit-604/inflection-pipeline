Dear Sir/ Ma’am, 
 
This is in furtherance to our earlier intimation dated October 22, 2024, wherein Esconet Technologies 
Limited (“the Company”) had informed the Exchange regarding its recognition as a “Preferred Level 
Partner” under the Compute competency in the NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN) Program. 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 
2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”), we hereby inform that the Company has now received an official 
communication from NVIDIA Corporation  dated April 15, 2026 , intimating a further elevation in its 
partnership status. 
 
The Company has been upgraded to the “Elite” level under the Compute competency within the NPN 
Solution Provider Partner Program. 
 
The “Elite” designation represents the highest tier of partnership within the Solution Provider category 
for the specified competency and reflects the Company’s continued advancement, technical 
proficiency, and deeper alignment with NVIDIA’s partner ecosystem and program requirements. This 
progression from “Preferred” to “ Elite” level underscores the Company’s sustained engagement and 
capability development within the NVIDIA ecosystem. 
 
This elevation is expected to further enhance the Company’s positioning and may enable access to 
advanced partner benefits, including higher levels of technical enablement, priority support, and 
expanded go-to-market collaboration opportunities, in accordance with NVIDIA’s program framework. 
 
The Company does not expect any immediate material financial impact solely on account of this 
development. 
 
Exchange is requested to take note of the same.
