### Evasion / granularity-refusal pattern (spans all three calls)
Four separate "we do not disclose that" moments:
- Receivables aging (Q3 p.16) — refused, curt. [B05 caught]
- Product-wise MedTech gross/EBITDA margins (Q3 p.7) — "not disclosed." [B05 caught]
- Pre/post-acquisition growth rates of acquired entities (Q4 p.14) — deferred offline. [B05 missed]
- Quarterly gross/net debt and OCF (Q1 p.14) — deferred to half-yearly. [B05 missed]

Management is precise and generous on P&L and margin metrics, and selectively
opaque on balance-sheet quality. B05 surfaced the theme (graded Transparency
"Weak") and caught two instances, but missed the two later instances and did
not characterise the opacity as a three-quarter pattern. This is thesis-relevant
because the thin OCF (Rs 96.2 Cr FY26), the flagged receivables growth, and the
minority-buyout cash claim all sit on the balance sheet the company will not
detail quarterly. It does NOT reach CRITICAL: no single question was evaded across
2+ quarters, and B05 did name the transparency weakness.
