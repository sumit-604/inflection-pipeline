00089| Management Commentary
00090| 
00091| Commenting on the performance Mr. Sanjay Sharma MD and Co-Founder said :
00092| 
00093|                                                                       "India's micro-enterprises continue to demonstrate strong credit resilience, even as the broader macro environment remains
00094|                                                                       uncertain. Government-led financial inclusion initiatives and rapid digital adoption are meaningfully expanding access to formal
00095|                                                                       credit and reinforcing the long-term structural opportunity in the micro-enterprise segment.
00096| 
00097|                                                                       Moving to the quarter, Q1 is seasonally a weaker quarter, characterized by lower business activity and softer credit demand.
00098|                                                                       Despite the seasonal impact, we witnessed a steady improvement in business momentum during the quarter. Disbursements for
00099|                                                                       the quarter stood at Rs. 1,219 Crore, with a growth of 22% year-on-year. Our Assets Under Management also improved by 28%
00100|                                                                      year-on-year and 4% quarter-on quarter to Rs. 7,324 Crore. This growth was supported by healthy demand from both existing
00101|                                                                       and new customers, along with a continued focus on disciplined credit expansion.
00102| 
00103|                                                                       Asset quality continued its improving trajectory for the sixth consecutive quarter. GNPA stood at 4.49%, improving 28 basis points
00104|                                                                       sequentially, while PAR X stood at 7.01% and PAR 30 remained stable at 6.07%. Credit cost moderated further to 4.01%, a
00105|                                                                       reduction of 29 basis points sequentially, reflecting sustained improvement in portfolio quality and our continued focus on
00106|                                                                       prudent underwriting. Collection efficiency remained healthy through the quarter, with Non-OD collection efficiency at 99.2% and
00107|                                                                       Bucket 1 collections at 54.5%.
00108| 
00109|                                                                       Our Gross Total Income for the quarter improved by 22% year-on-year to Rs. 490 Crore. The reduction in average yield due to
00110|                                                                       change in portfolio mix has been compensated by reduction in finance cost resulting in 20 bps QoQ improvement in NIM.
00111|                                                                       Improved credit environment led to lower provisions whereby profitability improved to by 144% year-on-year to Rs. 75 Crore.
00112| 
00113|                                                                       During the quarter, India Ratings & Research upgraded our long-term rating from IND A to IND A+ with a Stable Outlook, and our
00114|                                                                       commercial paper rating to IND A1+. This upgrade will enable us to further diversify our funding sources, improve access to
00115|                                                                       capital, and optimize our cost of borrowing, thereby strengthening our ability to serve a larger base of micro-enterprise
00116|                                                                       customers with competitive and sustainable credit solutions.
00117| 
00118|                                                                       As we enter Q2 FY27, we are optimistic. We expect business momentum to further improve, supported by improving customer
00119|                                                                       demand, stable collections, and a more favourable operating environment."
