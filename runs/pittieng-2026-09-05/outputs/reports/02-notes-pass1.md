1. **FY26 PAT decline is a tax-rate story, not an operating one.** Standalone PBT -0.13%, consolidated PBT +3.70%, yet PAT fell -8.72%/-3.66% because effective tax rate rose 23.96%→30.49% (standalone) and 24.33%→29.70% (consolidated); no exceptional items exist in either P&L. (Note 24, Note 25.9A; standalone PDF p.80-81, consolidated PDF p.111-112) 🔴

2. **Net debt rose, not fell, in FY26 — the guided decline toward ₹300 Cr has not yet begun in this document.** Consolidated net debt ₹554.56 Cr (up from ₹439.02 Cr, +26.3%); standalone ₹600.01 Cr (up from ₹471.36 Cr); net-debt/equity worsened both bases. Driven by a working-capital reversal: trade payables -25.7%, inventory +20.0% (consolidated). (Note 25.3, Note 5, Note 13C; PDF p.105, 109, 112) 🔴

3. **Single-segment reporting makes the ~40% railway/traction (Wabtec/Alstom) claim unverifiable from the notes.** Only proxy is aggregate top-customer concentration: standalone 3 customers = 40.7% of revenue FY26; consolidated 2 customers = 25.2%. No customer names or end-market labels appear anywhere. (Note 25.6; PDF p.83 standalone, p.114-115 consolidated) 🔴

4. **~₹3,700L of ESOP expense remains unrecognised** against the FY2024 scheme's estimated fair-value pool, to be expensed over the next 6-7 years — a durable, quantifiable future PAT drag invisible in EBITDA-based framing; FY26 expense alone was a 17.5x jump YoY (₹55.54L→₹1,025.52L consolidated). (Note 25.4C; PDF p.82-83 standalone, p.114 consolidated) 🟡

5. **Goodwill of ₹13,609.05L (13.79% of consolidated net worth) carries zero CGU-level impairment disclosure** — no discount rate, growth rate, or headroom given anywhere in the notes. (Consolidated Balance Sheet + Note 25.11 policy-only; PDF p.96, 109) 🔴

6. **A new ₹491.14L GST dispute (FY2018-2023) appears only in the consolidated Note 25.2, absent from standalone** — a subsidiary-level tax exposure invisible to a standalone-only reader. (Note 25.2; PDF p.81 standalone vs p.112 consolidated) 🟡

7. **The FY26 PP&E useful-life/residual-value re-estimate (Note 25.12) adds ₹204.06L to FY26 PBT, and a projected ₹683.18L (FY27) / ₹476.51L (FY28)** — a judgment-based depreciation smoothing that coincides with the capex ramp; worth tracking against actual FY27/28 outturns. (Note 25.12; PDF p.81-82 standalone, p.112 consolidated) 🟡

8. **Receivables quality genuinely improved:** implied DSO fell ~54.5→~39.4 days (consolidated), overdue >6-month balances stayed below 0.5% of gross both years, Trade Receivables Turnover Ratio improved 6.90x→7.87x — a real, disclosed cash-conversion positive that partly offsets Finding #2. (Note 6B, Note 25.21; PDF p.85-86 standalone, p.105-106 consolidated) 🟢

9. **A ₹9,655.16L State Industrial Promotion Subsidy receivable (9.8%+ of current assets, +20.2% YoY) has no disclosed scheme name, tenure, or expected collection date**, and feeds ~77% of Other Income in both statements. (Note 7, Note 18; PDF p.73-74 standalone, p.90 consolidated) 🟡

10. **First-year hedge accounting adoption (from 1-Jan-2026)** creates a new, unbenchmarked OCI Cash Flow Hedge reserve (-₹275L to -₹279L) with no prior-year comparative — adds P&L/OCI volatility with no historical track record to assess going forward. (Note 25.7B; PDF p.84-87 standalone, p.116-118 consolidated) 🟡
