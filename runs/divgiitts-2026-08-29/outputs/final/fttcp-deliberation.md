## 2. OPERATOR OVERRIDES (each with draft determination, operator ruling, operator reasoning, and default-track sensitivity)

The default-track figure is the more conservative of the two drafts (Claude Code draft vs dossier pre-ruling), shown so the cost of each override is visible.

### Override 1 — Pillar 1 ROCE and base PE
- **Draft:** ROCE ~12% (operating, treasury-stripped both sides), RECOVERING to the R2 operating level only, no R3 uplift → base PE 13.5x.
- **Operator ruling:** ROCE 20%, treasury-stripped both sides (interest out of EBIT, surplus cash out of capital employed, one operation). Base PE 19x.
- **Operator reasoning (quoted):** "growth is the premise of the investment. FY29 fair-case operating EBIT ~Rs 117 Cr on ~Rs 575 Cr operating capital reaches 20% from the earnings transition alone. If growth does not arrive we exit."
- **Default-track sensitivity:** the conservative draft carried 12% / 13.5x. Claude Code verification (open item 1): the ~Rs 575 Cr FY29 operating capital is reasonable (FY26 operating CE is Rs 355 Cr; it rises as the Rs 293 Cr cash deploys into Shirwal/Mayflower fixed assets and working capital). BUT the 20% ROCE requires a 24% operating margin holding to FY29, ABOVE management's own 20-22% guide. At the guided 20-22% margin, FY29 operating EBIT is ~Rs 92-108 Cr on Rs 575 Cr = 16-19% ROCE, which would give base PE ~16x, not 19x. The 20% / 19x is the fair-case; the exit rule ("if growth does not arrive we exit") is the operator's stated protection against it.

### Override 2 — Sector cap

It is a candidate for a permanent "Auto components / ancillary" row in the Section 1B cap table (see LESSONS_ARCHIVE), alongside the standing steel and sugar open actions.

- FY29 ~Rs 575 Cr is REASONABLE: as the Rs 293 Cr cash deploys into fixed assets (Shirwal automated line, Mayflower) and working capital scales with revenue toward ~Rs 640 Cr, operating capital employed rises to ~Rs 550-600 Cr.
- LOAD-BEARING CAVEAT: operating EBIT Rs 117 Cr requires ~Rs 640 Cr revenue at a 24% operating margin. At management's guided 20-22% margin, FY29 operating EBIT is ~Rs 92-108 Cr on Rs 575 Cr = 16-19% ROCE. The 20% ROCE (and the 19x base PE) is the fair case, not the guided case.

- NOT ANCHORED: per-unit realization is not disclosed anywhere in the corpus (confirmed NOT FOUND, consistent with B04). So the revenue is a bounded ESTIMATE, not an anchor.
- BOUNDED ESTIMATE: blended FY26 transfer-case ASP is ~Rs 34,000 (Rs 176 Cr / ~52,000 units), but the Indonesia units are a "value-engineered manual-shift" transfer case (management, Q3 FY26), which prices lower, plausibly Rs 15,000-20,000. - IMPLICATION: FY28 must replace ~Rs 75-95 Cr of expiring Indonesia revenue with organic and export growth just to hold the FY27 line. This is the single largest swing in the model and the reason FY28 is the operator's secondary basis year.
