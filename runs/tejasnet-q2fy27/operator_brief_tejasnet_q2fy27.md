## 1. Narrative

1. Tejas designs telecom network equipment in India. It sells optical, router, broadband and 4G/5G radio gear to telecom operators and government projects.
2. Sales in Q2 were Rs 406 Cr. That is 55% more than a year ago, but only 1% more than Q1.
3. The company still loses money. It lost Rs 207 Cr after tax this quarter, about the same as Q1.
4. The yearly improvement is mostly accounting, not operations. Last year carried a Rs 145 Cr one-time factory loss. This quarter booked unusually low warranty and bad-debt costs.
5. Cash still flows out. In the half year, operations used Rs 312 Cr, and Rs 709 Cr left after investment spending.
6. Debt keeps rising. Net debt (borrowings minus cash) is Rs 4,384 Cr. The thesis breaks at Rs 4,500 Cr, so only Rs 116 Cr of room is left.
7. The big order arrived, but only on paper. TCS gave a letter of intent for 18,685 BSNL sites, worth about Rs 1,500 Cr. The filed deck shows no purchase order yet.
8. The order book rose to Rs 2,315 Cr from Rs 1,529 Cr. Of this, 96% is from India. International sales fell to 12% of revenue.
9. Management gave no amounts for collections, debt cuts or inventory release. It also dropped its date for positive operating profit (EBITDA).
10. The good news: shipments for the BSNL order have started. In Q2, money owed by customers (net of advances) fell by Rs 190 Cr.
11. The worry: book value per share fell to about Rs 139. That is below the Rs 140 red line. A share sale below Rs 400 would break the thesis.
12. Watch next quarter: net debt at 31-Dec-2026. Below Rs 4,000 Cr means BSNL cash arrived. Above Rs 4,500 Cr breaks the thesis. At the Q2 pace it lands at Rs 4,492 Cr.
