Sub: Composite Scheme of Arrangement - Record date and Listing of Shares 
We wish to inform you that the Composite Scheme of Arrangement amongst Lily Commercial Private 
Limited (“Transferor Company”), DCM Shriram Industries Limited (“DCMSR” or “the Company” or 
“Transferee Company”), DCM Shriram Fine Chemicals Limited (“DSFCL” or “Resultant Company 1”) 
and DCM Shriram International Limited (“DSIL” or “Resultant Company 2”) and their respective 
shareholders and creditors (“the Scheme”), as sanctioned by the Hon’ble National Company Law 
Tribunal (“NCLT”), New Delhi Bench vide Order dated November 21, 2025, the Company has fixed 
Friday, December 19, 2025, as the “Record Date” to ascertain eligible shareholders of the Company 
who shall be issued and allotted 1 (one) share (face value of INR 2/- each fully paid up) in DSFCL and 
DSIL, for every 1 (one) share (face value of INR 2/- each fully paid up) held in DCMSR by them as on 
the Record Date (as per Share Entitlement Ratio under the Scheme). In this context, we would like to 
bring to your attention, inter alia, the following:  
a) In terms of the Scheme, the Chemical business and Rayon business undertakings of DCMSR has 
been demerged into DSFCL and DSIL respectively. The Appointed Date as per the Scheme is April 1, 
2023.  
b) DCMSR shall continue to be a listed entity. Pursuant to the Scheme, DCMSR shall carry on the sugar 
business undertaking including its power and alcohol business.  
c) On the Record Date, the share price of DCMSR shall be adjusted to take into account the demerger 
of the Sugar business undertaking, through a price discovery mechanism of the stock exchanges.  
d) Upon finalization of the list of eligible Shareholders of DCMSR, the shares in DSFCL and DSIL shall 
be allotted and thereafter, such shares shall be listed on the Stock Exchanges, viz., BSE and NSE.  
e) Guidance on ‘Cost of Acquisition’ of equity shares in DCMSR and equity shares in DSFCL and DSIL 
will be communicated to you in due course.
