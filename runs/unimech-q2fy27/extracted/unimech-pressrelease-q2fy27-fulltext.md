        Unimech Aerospace Continues Powerful Growth Momentum in Q2 FY27:
              Revenue Surges 98% YoY to 1,230 Mn; EBITDA Up 164% YoY;
                                  PAT up 103% YoY to 318 Mn;
                   Consolidated Order Book at 8,546 Mn up 8-times YoY

27th October, 2026, Bengaluru: Unimech Aerospace & Manufacturing Ltd., a global high precision
engineering platform specializing in manufacturing complex products, announced its unaudited
financial results for the quarter and half year ended 30th September 2026.

Commenting on the performance, Mr. Anil Kumar Puttan, Chairman & Managing Director, said,

"The financial year continues to progress on a highly encouraging note for Unimech, with Q2 delivering
strong momentum across our core business lines. In H1 FY27, revenue reached Rs.2,306 Mn, representing
an 85% year-on-year growth driven by robust demand execution and early returns from our strategic
growth initiatives. Backed by a consolidated order book of Rs.8,546 Mn, we are operating from a
foundation of exceptional financial and operational strength.
The most compelling part is the expanding visibility of our business model. Long-term supply agreements
and award programs with premier global aerospace OEMs are successfully transitioning Unimech from
short-cycle tooling into recurring, high-volume 'flying part' manufacturing. In Semiconductors, our
dedicated 20-month development pipeline with global OEM major has generated commercial revenues in
H1, with a sharp step-up anticipated in H2. Furthermore, with parts prove-out happening at full force,
serial production scaling over coming quarters will create a compounding, multi-year revenue runway.
Building scale-ready infrastructure ahead of demand is fundamental to our growth philosophy. Our
automated special-process cell installation is underway, and our Middle East expansion via the Saudi
Arabia JV ensure we possess the floor space, technical capabilities, and global presence required by top-
tier OEMs. As we integrate these assets, we remain deeply focused on disciplined execution, prudent
capital allocation, and driving sustainable, high-margin growth".

About Unimech Aerospace & Manufacturing Ltd: Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Bengaluru,
Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Limited (BSE: 544322 | NSE: UNIMECH) is an advanced integrated
global precision engineering solutions platform serving high growth industries across aerospace, defence,
energy, oil & gas, semiconductors, and other high-performance industrial sectors. The Company
specialises in complex build-to-print and build-to-specification, with capabilities spanning across precision
machining, fabrication, welding, complex assembly, special processes, advanced testing and validation.
Operating from a 5,63,000 sq. ft. manufacturing base spread across units in India and overseas. Unimech
combines deep engineering expertise with advanced 3, 4 and 5-axis CNC machines, and a digitally enabled
production ecosystem to deliver mission-critical components and engineered systems with micron-level
precision. The Company is certified to AS9100, ISO 9001, IATF 16949 and ISO 45001, reflecting its
commitment to quality, safety and compliance for globally regulated industries.
