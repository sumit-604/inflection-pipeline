Management Discussion and Analysis — Q2 FY27 
Kwality Pharmaceuticals Ltd. ("KPL") delivered a strong performance in 02FY27, 
building on the momentum established in the first quarter, with robust revenue growth, 
significant margin expansion and continued progress across its regulatory, 
manufacturing and product development initiatives. The Company continued to 
strengthen its international presence, supported by an accelerating pace of product 
registrations and an expanding portfolio of complex and specialised formulations. 
In Q2FY27, consolidated revenue grew 63% year -on-year to t181 crore from t111 
crore in Q2FY26, registering an 11.7% sequential growth over t162 crore in 
01FY27. EBITDA margins expanded to 27.5% from 23.4% in Q2FY26, reflecting 
improved operating leverage and a favourable product mix. PAT doubled to t 50 
crore from t 25 crore in the corresponding quarter last year, with PAT margins 
expanding to 16% from 13%. On a sequential basis, PAT margins also improved 
from 15.7% in Q1FY27 to 16%. Consequently, consolidated revenue for H1FY27 
stood at Z343 crore, reflecting sustained business momentum and strong execution. 
Building on the improving business outlook and an accelerated pace of product 
registrations across key international markets, management has further revised its 
FY27 revenue guidance upwards to t 720 crore+ from its earlier guidance of t 700 
crore. The increased guidance reflects the growing contribution from newly registered 
products and the Company's ability to convert its expanding regulatory pipeline into 
commercial opportunities. 
During the quarter, the Company acquired 4 acres of land adjacent to its existing 
Amritsar manufacturing campus for the development of Unit 7, a mega general 
manufacturing facility. The proposed facility is envisaged as a fully automated 
manufacturing complex comprising 4+ injectable lines and 6+ oral solid dosage (OSD) 
lines. This strategic expansion is aimed at strengthening the Company's 
manufacturing infrastructure, enhancing its capabilities across multiple dosage forms 
and creating the capacity required to support its long-term growth ambitions. 
On the regulatory front, KPL received EU-GMP certification from Hungary, a Class 
1 European regulatory jurisdiction, for its Cephalosporin and Oncology 
manufacturing facilities. This approval marks a significant advancement in the 
Company's regulatory footprint, further strengthening its credentials in highly regulated 
markets and enhancing its ability to pursue opportunities in complex and high-value 
pharmaceutical segments. 
During the quarter, the Company continued to advance its global product development 
programme. Out of its 40-molecule Bioequivalence (BE) programme for global filings,

generation and enabling the Company to support its growing business requirements more 
efficiently.
