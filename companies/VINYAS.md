## MENTAL MODEL (SIGNED 2026-09-01)

### Part A — the FROM state

Archetype: OUTSOURCING PARTNER (EMS), with an order-book-business overlay.
Builds electronics to customer designs. Materials are 79 paise of every
revenue rupee, which settles the question: this is an assembler, not a
designer. Designers do not spend 79% on parts.

Of the five variables this archetype runs on, two are known and both are
adverse. One customer was 51.13% of revenue and top-10 was 92.28% (FY23,
PROSP p25). Capacity fill is 35-40% against a 50% target that was missed.
Wallet share, contract terms and price per unit are all NOT DISCLOSED.

ANALOGY. A tailor who does not design clothes. Customers bring their own
patterns. He buys most of the cloth, so cloth is 79 paise of every rupee.
The garments go into fighter jets and dialysis machines, so a bad stitch
matters and the tailor must be certified.

UNRESOLVED IN THE ANALOGY (PROSP p111): the pattern-making room may not
belong to shareholders. Build-to-Specification work is designed by "our
Promoter's design team." The prospectus sentence is garbled where the entity
name should sit. Answer is at PROSP p145 "Our Group Entities," not extracted.

### Part A addendum — COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE

Three statements, in order of confidence.

**1. NO FIRM-LEVEL COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE EVIDENCED.**
ROCE decomposes to EBIT margin 11.3% x capital turns 1.40x = 15.8%
(conventional basis: equity 236 + debt 130 = Rs.366 Cr). Crisil puts the
industry band at 11-12% operating margin; peers run 9-13%. The margin leg is
exactly industry-standard and the turns leg is poor, dragged by the 232-day
cash cycle. A business with advantage is strong on one leg or both. Vinyas is
mediocre on both. That is the arithmetic definition of no advantage.

Every certification (AS9100-D, ISO 13485, NADCAP AC7120, IATF 16949, Defence
Industrial Licence) is held by Avalon, Centum, Kaynes and Cyient DLM. ADI
Electronics Bangalore has held AC7120 since ~2018 against Vinyas June-2025 —
seven years late on the accreditation presented as a differentiator.

Gross margin flat across six years (18.0-27.5%, no trend) while materials rose
73% to 79% during a revenue doubling. Crisil notes most contracts carry price
parity and forex variation clauses: input costs pass through to customers.
Pass-through protects margin from inflation and means margin is set by
contract, not won by negotiation. Converter economics.

**2. INDUSTRY-LEVEL BARRIERS ARE REAL, AND VINYAS SHARES THEM.**
Facility and line qualification takes 18-24 months (Jun-2025 and Jun-2026
concalls, filed). This is why India has six or seven serious players rather
than sixty, and why the sector earns 15-26% rather than its cost of capital.
It also means incumbency is genuinely sticky: a customer moving work to a
competitor waits 18-24 months for the first board.

SUPERSEDES an earlier web-layer inference that PO-to-PO working proved weak
switching costs. Short contracts plus slow switching is not the same as short
contracts plus easy switching. The absence of LTAs likely reflects Indian
defence procurement convention. The revenue base is stickier than that
inference allowed — but the stickiness protects any incumbent, not Vinyas
specifically.

**3. TWO CANDIDATE FIRM-LEVEL POSITIONS, BOTH SUPPORTING DURABILITY NOT MARGIN.**

a) Offset. CORROBORATED: PROSP p129 confirms 2011 entry into the Indian
   Defence Offset programme; PROSP p106 describes Vinyas as a "preferred IOP
   for foreign OEMs." Offset contracts were 74.83 / 86.57 / 82.31% of revenue
   FY21-FY23 (PROSP p26). Fifteen years inside offset is a real position.
   ERODING for reasons outside the company's control: IAI is building its own
   radar facility in Tamil Nadu for 2027, and a prime with local capacity can
   discharge offsets through itself; the 75% domestic ring-fence shrinks the
   Buy (Global) pool that creates offset obligation at all.
   NOT DISCLOSED: how IOP status was granted, by whom, whether renewable,
   transferable or time-limited.

b) Sole-source. One management claim (Nov-2025 concall p7, Sumukh Narendra):
   "in a lot of cases, we are the only approved vendor... single tender
   opportunity in a lot of cases." Names no programme, no approving authority.
   Management grade C. Uncorroborated by any document.
   HOW TO READ IT IF TRUE: single-tender procurement in Indian defence
   protects VOLUME, not price. One approved vendor typically means negotiated
   or cost-plus pricing with audit rather than competitive bidding. So
   sole-source status and flat gross margin are compatible. The claim, if
   real, supports revenue durability — a lower discount rate and steadier
   forecasts — NOT margin expansion, because it does not move ROCE.

**CLOSED 2026-09-01 — DRDO REGULATORY LOCK: UNPROVEN, LEANS NEGATIVE.**
BEL (Q3 FY26 call p14) states that on DRDO-developed programmes vendor
selection runs off DRDO's approved vendor list per line item under the LATOT
document, so BEL cannot substitute a supplier DRDO has not approved. That
would be a regulatory lock and the one mechanism that could reverse statement
1. It is not evidenced.
- "DRDO" appears exactly once in the entire corpus, as generic policy
  language (PROSP p123).
- No statement anywhere that Vinyas is DRDO-registered, approved or
  empanelled, or holds line-item approvals.
- The two other "approved vendor" phrases (FY25 AR p15, PROSP p117) describe
  Vinyas managing its OWN supplier list — the opposite direction.
- No SAMAR certificate, no DRDO ToT, no DRDO licensing agreement.
- Neither Crisil rationale (10-Aug, 25-Aug 2026, read web-side) mentions
  DRDO. Customers described only as "counterparties" with "longstanding
  relationships."
- Competitors Aidin Technologies and Park Controls publicly advertise PCB
  assembly and test for DRDO labs. Vinyas makes no such claim. Weak negative
  evidence, but a company holding DRDO approvals usually says so.
- Named customers: BEL and HAL (Jun-2025 concall). Specific programmes NOT
  DISCLOSED, only platform categories. ("Tejas" in the prospectus is a
  shareholder name, not the aircraft.)
- WITHDRAWN: the 2017 ISRO industry-built satellite consortium claim, sourced
  to a blog. No ISRO mention anywhere in the corpus. Refuted.
Only DRDO or BEL line-item documentation could settle this and neither sits in
any Vinyas public filing. Treat as closed unless a BEL tender or PO naming
Vinyas as approved source surfaces.

**CONTRACT-FORM SHIFT — the one thing that moved.**
PROSP p25 at IPO: "we do not enter into any long-term purchase arrangements
with such customers and instead undertake work on a purchase order basis."
Post-listing: a medical-OEM manufacturing agreement (Jun-2025 concall) and the
HAL Korwa 5-year LTA (announced 08-Apr-2026, HUD and IFF electronics). The HAL
LTA is in NO filed document in the corpus, only the exchange announcement;
value, terms and start date undisclosed, so it cannot be sized. If genuine it
is the first defence LTA in company history. WATCH FOR A THIRD: two is a
coincidence, three is a change in contracting model and would be the first
structural improvement anywhere in this file.

### Part B — the transition

FROM: R2, restated as QUALIFIED JOB-SHOP CONVERTER. Margin comes from capacity
fill and certification access, not cost position. Material cost worsened 73%
to 79%, so "cost-advantaged" is not evidenced.
TO (claimed): R3 VALUE-ADDED / SPEC'D SUPPLIER.
Rung-jump: one rung. Within the base rate.

**WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED: IT SCALED SIDEWAYS.**

| | FY23 | FY26 | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Rs.235 Cr | Rs.514 Cr | more than doubled |
| Gross margin | 23.5% | 21.1% | slightly worse |
| Materials % of revenue | 73% | 79% | worse |
| ROCE (Gate 0 basis) | 34.5% | 22.9% | down |
| Receivable days | 33 | 161 | far worse |
| Customer advances | Rs.31.4 Cr | Rs.2.1 Cr | gone |

The company got twice as big. It did not get better. Every quality measure is
flat or down. Six-year ROCE: 23.7 / 25.2 / 34.5 / 24.0 / 24.3 / 22.9%.
Six-year gross margin: 18.04 / 27.53 / 23.47 / 22.95 / 27.02 / 21.11%.

=> GROWTH name, not TRANSITION name. One return engine, not two. Fails the
re-rating requirement. Two independent routes now reach this conclusion: flat
returns through a doubling, and the absence of any firm-level advantage.

**PROOF GATE (three limbs, all must hold):**
- L1. Material cost falls below 76% of revenue and holds two consecutive
      half-years, measured independent of the WIP-inventory charge
      (gross margin sustained above 24%).
- L2. Corroborated by a disclosed mix shift (B2P/B2S, programme mix) or rising
      realisation. Without L2 a single bounce is indistinguishable from FY22
      (27.5%) and FY25 (27.0%), both of which reverted.
- L3. Operating cash flow stays positive through a year in which revenue grows
      above 25%. This has never once happened.

SUPERSEDES the 09b draft gate, which called 21.1% the "FY26 trough." It is not
a trough. FY21 was lower at 18.04%. Range-bound cyclical, no trend.

**UGLINESS TEST: PERMANENT BUT NOT INHERENT.**
Neither "structural" nor "artifact of climb." A one-time, irreversible
financing-structure change made at listing, now embedded in the cost of growth.
This is WORSE than structural, because structural implies the whole industry
carries it and the multiple prices it in. Peers largely do not carry it.

THE CUSTOMER-FUNDING INVERSION (the core finding of this session):

| Rs. Cr | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade receivables | 20.95 | 88.06 | 177.05 | 227.10 |
| Advances from customers | 31.44 | 1.86 | 1.80 | 2.14 |
| Net position with customers | −10.49 | +86.20 | +175.25 | +224.96 |

In FY23 customers were net financiers of Vinyas by Rs.10.5 Cr — advances were
13.4% of revenue, ~49 days of prepayment, and collection ran 33 days. Vinyas
listed October 2023, raised Rs.54.66 Cr fresh equity, and advances collapsed
94% in FY24. Three years later they have not recovered. Revenue grew Rs.280 Cr
FY23-FY26 while the net customer position moved Rs.235 Cr against the company:
**~84 paise of every new revenue rupee went into funding customers.**

NOT INHERENT — four proofs:
- BEL pays its vendors in ~80 days; HAL in ~66. Both primes absorb their own
  long cycles (BEL CCC ~201 days, HAL ~286) rather than passing them down.
- Vinyas's own stated terms: 30-90 days, 60-90 for government (PROSP p83-84).
  Actual collection 161 days.
- Vinyas itself ran 33 receivable days in FY23.
- Seasonality (H2 = 59% of FY26) explains only ~24 of the 161 days.
=> ~50-70 days remain unexplained by terms, seasonality or customer mix.
Unbilled revenue RULED OUT as cause: nil at FY26, never above 2% of revenue.

CFO by year: FY23 +11.6 / FY24 −27.9 / FY25 +8.2 / FY26 −32.3 Cr. Negative in
BOTH acceleration years (FY24 +35%, FY26 +30%).

**PREDICTION ON RECORD (2026-09-01): H1 FY27 operating cash flow will be
negative.** If it prints positive, the model is wrong and needs rebuilding,
not adjusting.

TRANSITION FALSIFIER: gross margin at or below 21% for two more half-years AND
B2P/B2S mix still undisclosed.
BUSINESS FALSIFIER 1: CFO negative across both halves of FY27 with PAT
positive.
BUSINESS FALSIFIER 2: if Nexus/UVT RPT actuals substitute for third-party
demand rather than supplement it, the order book itself needs re-examination.

### Part C — dominant variables (5, ranked)

1. Operating cash flow. Single point of failure.
2. Customer advances / net customer funding position. The discovered driver.
3. ROCE as revenue grows. The cleanest transition test — climbing means
   earning MORE per rupee of capital, not just more rupees.
4. Receivable days against the HAL ramp. HAL pays vendors in 66 days; if HAL
   ramps in FY27 and blended collection does not improve, the slow money sits
   with specific unnamed customers.
5. Funding access. Bank limits ~76% utilised, ~Rs.89 Cr of the Rs.150 Cr raise
   not yet received, growth consumes ~44 paise per rupee.

REJECTS AS NOISE: total market size (capacity exceeds any SOM estimate);
named-competitor share comparisons (qualification game, not share game);
headline revenue growth, PAT growth, P/B and dividend yield standalone; order
book cover (2.7x is highest in the peer group only because execution runs
18-36 months against Avalon's 14 — slower conversion, not better visibility).

### GENUINE POSITIVES (held at equal weight)

- Returns held flat through a doubling. New capital earned what old capital
  earned. Most companies see returns collapse scaling this fast.
- Utilisation 35-40% against Rs.1,200-1,400 Cr of capacity on Rs.65 Cr of
  fixed assets (~8x asset turns). Growth needs no material capex.
- Demand real and rising: FY26 order inflow Rs.960.38 Cr against Rs.600-750 Cr
  guided; order book Rs.1,400 Cr independently verified by Crisil June 2026;
  BEL guides FY27 inflow above Rs.55,000 Cr against Rs.30,045 Cr delivered;
  India defence capital outlay +21.8%.
- Balance sheet improved: gearing 0.87 to 0.55, interest cover 3.27 to 3.92,
  Crisil upgraded to A-/Stable 25-Aug-2026.
- Operating margin ex-other-income rose 10.30% to 11.88% (+158 bps). Real,
  though not from gross margin.
