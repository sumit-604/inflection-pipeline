### 3A. RPT history — the section's central finding

Related-party raw-material purchases were **37.1% of FY26 Cost of Materials Consumed**
(Rs 3,683.61 lakh of Rs 9,916.35 lakh: Phoenix Bio Sciences Rs 2,574.32 lakh +73.6%
YoY, Peptech Biosciences Rs 44.79 lakh, Stalwart Nutritions Rs 986.20 lakh). ✅ VERIFIED
(primary document, Note 41B, AR_FY2026.txt lines 8048-8052, independently
re-transcribed this stage and matching Stage 3's own re-verification exactly). **No
arm's-length pricing benchmark is disclosed anywhere in the AR** for any of these
three lines — the Directors' Report (item 20) and CARO clause xiii both assert
compliance with Sections 177/188 "in the ordinary course of business and at arm's
length" without a comparable-market-price disclosure to test the assertion against.
This is the single largest unresolved RPT-fairness question in the file.

The independence structure around this transaction is now established directly from
the primary document (this stage's own read, CG Report p.43-44, AR_FY2026.txt lines
2270-2295): **Raja Singla and Shivom Singla, both serving Executive Directors of Titan
Biotech, sit on the board of Phoenix Bio Sciences Private Limited** — the entity
supplying 26.0% of FY26 raw material and growing 73.6% YoY. Naresh Kumar Singla and
Suresh Chand Singla (the two Managing Directors) do NOT sit on Phoenix Bio Sciences'
board; the overlap runs through the next generation of executives, not the two MDs
directly. **Independent Chairman Rohit Jain does NOT sit on Phoenix Bio Sciences'
board** — this stage's direct read of the CG Report's full director-by-director table
(AR_FY2026.txt p.42-44) finds his only outside-group directorship among the
RPT-relevant entities is Peptech Biosciences Limited (the associate), not Phoenix Bio
Sciences, Titan Media, Titan Securities, or Connoisseur Management. **This corrects
the run brief's framing**, which named all five entities under the Independent
Chairman: the primary document shows only one (Peptech) carries his name, and
Peptech's own history (a 2012-era demerger, still >85% family-plus-associate economic
interest) is a materially different risk profile from an opaque outside supplier.

The finding that remains fully intact and is, on balance, the correct one to carry
forward at full weight: **an Independent Chairman who chairs the Audit
Committee that reviews RPTs quarterly also sits on the board of one of the company's
equity-method associates and RPT counterparties (Peptech)**, and **two Executive
Directors sit on the board of the single largest RPT raw-material supplier
(Phoenix Bio Sciences)** whose pricing cannot be independently benchmarked from the
disclosed information. Neither breaches SEBI/Companies Act independence rules (both
directors have made the required declarations), but both materially narrow how much
genuinely independent scrutiny the Phoenix Bio Sciences pricing question can expect
from the current board and Audit Committee structure. A third, lower-materiality
overlap (Prem Shankar Gupta, TBL's own CFO, sitting on Titan Media Ltd's board, per
