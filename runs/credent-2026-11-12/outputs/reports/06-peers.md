## Part 4: Triangulation summary
- Claims verified: 0 of 9. Partially verified: 5 (Q2, Q4, Q6, Q7, Q8). Unverifiable: 2 (Q1, Q3).
- Claims contradicted: 2 (Q5 receivable days and cash; Q9 launch losses). Both go to synthesis as priority items.

- Strongest independent confirmation: Q2/Q4 partial. Thyrocare's "10%, 11%" market growth (Q3 FY26, p.16) matches the call's 11.1%; TCIEXP shows 15 to 16% EBITDA is reachable in express logistics at peak (Q3 FY26, p.16). Both support the market framing, not Credent's 45% or its margin step-up.
- Overall: the peer set complicates the narrative. It supports the market growth rate and shows 15-16% EBITDA existed in logistics, but offers no support for the 20% pre-analytics TAM, none for under 45 receivable days, none for launches without loss, and shows manpower margins at 6 to 7.5%. The positive side: pass-through by contract form exists, and low concentration is normal in peers. The weight of peer evidence is moderate because the peers are adjacent, not direct.
