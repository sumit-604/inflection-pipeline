# FTTCP draft — K.C.P. Sugar and Industries Corporation Ltd (KCPSUGIND)

CMP Rs 21.7. Market cap Rs 246 Cr. Run date 2026-07-21. First workup, not a
refresh. Ran in NO-CONCALL MODE: no earnings call transcript exists for this
company, so forward catalysts are read from the annual report, the FY26 and
Q3 FY26 results filings, and the CARE rating, never invented from narrative,
and confidence is materially reduced and flagged throughout.

One sentence before the rulings, because it governs everything below: this is
a company whose investments and cash on the balance sheet (Rs 332 Cr) are
larger than its entire stock market value (Rs 246 Cr), sitting on top of an
operating business that is losing money and shrinking. The forward transition
work says the operating business is going backwards on all four measures. The
investment case, if there is one, is an asset case, not an operating case, and
that belongs to the Phase 3 valuation, not to this forward catalyst gate.

## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

For each transition, what could make it fire, what the evidence is, what would
confirm it, and what would kill it. The honest answer on all four is that no
documented company-specific catalyst is firing inside the window.

**Revenue.** The two things that could turn revenue are urad dal scaling and
the engineering subsidiary winning work. Urad dal is a genuine capex-free lever,
the plant is already installed at 22,000 tonnes a year running at roughly a
quarter of capacity, but the segment revenue actually fell in FY26 and it turned
loss-making as it grew, so it is not firing yet. The engineering subsidiary,
Eimco-KCP, is said to have won a Rs 257 Cr order from Hyundai Engineering on 20
July 2026, which would be about 3.3 times the engineering segment's whole FY26
revenue. That order is operator-relayed and press-reported but it is not in any
exchange filing this analysis can see, so it counts as a pending catalyst, not
evidence. Confirmed by a Reg 30 filing and then revenue recognition. Killed by
the order not appearing in a filing, or the segment continuing to shrink as it
did in FY26.

**Margin.** Sugar prices firming is the one real tailwind, and CARE confirms
sugar realisations firmed. What could make margin fire is that tailwind meeting
stable cane costs. What is happening instead is cane availability and recovery
falling and cane cost rising toward Rs 355 a quintal, so the company is not
capturing the price. Confirmed by the sugar segment result turning positive.
Killed by a fourth straight year of a widening sugar loss.

**Cash conversion.** What would fix cash is a working capital program: a
receivables facility, a debtor-day target actually hit, an inventory reduction.
The search for any of these returned NONE FOUND. There is no factoring facility,
no stated debtor-day target, no management commentary on working capital, and
receivable days went the wrong way. This absence is the single loudest signal in
the whole analysis. Confirmed firing would be a sanctioned and operational
factoring facility in a filing. Nothing kills it further because it is already
declining with no catalyst.

**ROCE.** What would lift ROCE is a capex block commissioning into revenue, an
asset sale releasing capital, or margin recovering on a flat capital base. None
is documented. Capex is falling and below depreciation, and the capital base is
dominated by a Rs 293 Cr investment portfolio that earns market returns, not
operating returns. Confirmed by an asset monetisation or a commissioning event.
Killed by the status quo, which is the base case.

**The intent-and-action check (Step 2E).** For each transition, management has
words but not documented action. Ethanol is called an opportunity while alcohol
output fell 82%. Sugar recovery is called firm while the segment loss widened.
There is vision on growth and none of it is backed by a filed, dated, sanctioned
action inside the window. The one possible action, the Eimco order, is not in the
filing feed, so it cannot be graded as documented action and cannot loosen a
verdict. Step 2E therefore CONFIRMS the conservative read on all four
transitions and does not lift the Kernex cap on cash.

## THE VERDICT

The forward composite is minus 4 out of a range of minus 4 to plus 8, the most
negative score the framework produces. All four operating transitions score
minus 1: revenue, margin, cash and ROCE are each going the wrong way with no
documented catalyst inside the window. The Kernex cap also engages because cash
conversion is declining with catalyst strength NONE, but the raw score already
sits at Hard AVOID, so the cap changes nothing. The position read on the
operating business is AVOID / Hard AVOID. The whole call turns on one print, the
sugar segment result: a fourth consecutive year more negative than FY26's minus
Rs 17.3 Cr confirms the core is structurally impaired, while a clear turn toward
zero would be the first evidence that FY26 was a cyclical trough rather than a
decline. The one thing this verdict deliberately does not weigh is the balance
sheet: the stock trades below the value of its own cash and investments, and
whether that asset floor makes the equity worth owning is a Phase 3
sum-of-the-parts question, flagged below, not a forward-catalyst one.

## THE P/E BASE CARD (for operator approval)

This is a preview of the exit multiple the valuation would use, computed from
Section 1B off what is known now. It is not the final valuation; Role 1 runs the
full dual-track exercise in Phase 3. Every number is anchored.

**A caveat that comes first, because it dominates the card.** A single exit PE
is the wrong primary lens for this company, and B04 already ruled the primary
method sum-of-the-parts. The market values the whole company at Rs 246 Cr while
its investments and cash alone are Rs 332 Cr and its net worth is Rs 460 Cr, so
it trades at about 0.54 times book and below its own liquid assets. A PE on
near-zero operating earnings is close to meaningless. The card below is
therefore a cross-check the operator can approve as the exit multiple on
normalized operating earnings, with the clear note that Phase 3 must lead with
sum-of-the-parts.

| Pillar | Input | Value | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pillar 1 | ROCE used (FTTCP DECLINING → FY+1 lower bound) | about 4% reported | screener CSV FY26; B01 |
| Pillar 1 | Base PE = 0.5 x ROCE + 7.5, floor 9x | 9.5x, at the 9x floor on an operating basis | Section 1B Amendment 5 |
| Pillar 1 | v3.5.1 normalization route | none (not TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED) | Section 1B v3.5.1 |
| Pillar 2 | Cash multiplier | 0.65x STRUCTURAL | Master v3.3 Pillar 2; B13 FLAG-CASH |
| Pillar 3 | Growth + moat + duration premium | +0x | B07 EM NONE; B09 SOM CAGR <20% |
| Strategic | ROCE re-rating / scarcity | +0x | single-credit, nothing to credit |
| UA | min(Raw x 1.25, cap) | NOT APPLIED (fails Gate0/EM qualifier) | Section 1B Amendment 3 |
| Sector cap | Agri processing | 20x | Master v3.3 cap table |

**Destination (exit) PE, both tracks, on normalized operating earnings:**

- Additive track: quality base = Pillar 1 base x cash multiplier = 9.5 x 0.65 =
  about 6.2x, plus 0 plus 0 = about 6x. Far below the 20x cap.
- RRM track: Base PE x RRM. RRM = 1 + (13.5 - r) x 0.12 with r about 15-16% for
  a small, cyclical, weak-governance name = 0.70 to 0.82. 9.5 x 0.75 = about 7x.
  Far below the 20x cap.

So the destination PE preview is roughly 6x to 7x on normalized operating
earnings, against a current PE of about 22x on FY26 reported EPS of Re 0.98
(itself inflated by investment income). On a pure PE lens that is a large
de-rating setup and would point to STOP on the Hurdle in Phase 3. But the pure
PE lens is not the right one here, which is the whole point of the caveat above.

**THE EARNINGS-BASIS QUESTION (operator decides, I do not).** One-year-forward
PE (multiple on forward EPS) versus trailing PE (multiple on trailing EPS). For a
cyclical commodity company at what may be a trough, with reported earnings
distorted by investment income, trailing PE on FY26 earnings is misleading in
both directions, and a normalized mid-cycle operating EPS on a forward basis
would fit the operating cross-check better. But the operator makes this call at
the valuation gate, and it interacts with the sum-of-the-parts decision, because
the investment book should be valued directly, not through any PE. This is
flagged for the operator, not chosen here.

Provisional inputs: the card is provisional on the Phase 3 decision to lead with
sum-of-the-parts, and on a normalized operating EPS that stage 10 must assemble.
