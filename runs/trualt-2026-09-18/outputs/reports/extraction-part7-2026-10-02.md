## 2. The Rs 18 crore gap in interest subvention (consolidated vs standalone)

"Interest subvention income* 7,247.62 5,902.39" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.277)
"Interest subvention income* 5,446.46 5,897.91" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.340)
- Rs lakh, FY26 / FY25. Gap FY26 Rs 1,801.16 lakh; FY25 Rs 4.48 lakh (arithmetic: 7,247.62 - 5,446.46; 5,902.39 - 5,897.91).

Footnote to the consolidated line:
"*With respect to Term Loan 1, the Holding Company is eligible to claim interest subvention from the Department of Food & Public Distribution (DFPD) for the year ended 31 March 2026, as confirmed by the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) ... With respect to Term Loan 2, as per the Department of Food & Public Distribution (DFPD)'s order Holding Company is regular in claiming interest subvention." (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.277)
- The consolidated footnote names only the Holding Company's Term Loans 1 and 2. The standalone footnote is the same text (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.340). Neither names a subsidiary, a subsidiary loan or another scheme.

Accounting policy: "When the assistance is provided by the government in the form of reimbursement of interest paid on term loans, the effect of this interest subvention is regarded as government grant. The benefit received is accounted as other income" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.254)

Receivables do not show the gap:
"Interest subvention receivable [Refer note 28] 3,170.14 2,948.33" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.268); "Interest subvention receivable (refer note 27) 3,163.66 2,943.84" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.332)
- Consolidated minus standalone receivable: FY26 Rs 6.48 lakh, FY25 Rs 4.49 lakh (arithmetic). The FY25 receivable gap (Rs 4.49 lakh) matches the FY25 income gap (Rs 4.48 lakh). The FY26 receivable gap (Rs 6.48 lakh) is far below the FY26 income gap (Rs 1,801.16 lakh). So either the subsidiary cash was received in-year, or the Rs 1,801 lakh is not a subvention receivable. The AR does not say which.

Subsidiary borrowings that could carry subvention:
- LBPL: Canara Bank term loan "Rs 2,940.00 Lakhs ... interest rate of 11.25% p.a." for the 10.2 TPD CBG plant (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.273). Balance Rs 1,674.09 lakh at 31-Mar-2026 (p.271).
- TGPL: NABARD "Rs 18,000.00 lakhs ... under the NABARD Green Lending Facility (NGLF) ... interest rate of 8.90% per annum ... only Rs 4,666.00 lakhs had been disbursed" (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.273). Monthly interest from 31-Dec-2025.
- Neither note mentions a subvention on these loans.

Arithmetic coincidence (not an explanation):
- LBPL turnover Rs 4,043.09 lakh (p.177) less CBG segment revenue Rs 2,247.93 lakh (p.285) = Rs 1,795.16 lakh.
- Consolidated other income less standalone other income = Rs 1,816.90 lakh (arithmetic: 8,645.25 (p.277) - 6,828.35 (p.340)).
- Consolidated revenue less standalone revenue = Rs 2,285.32 lakh (arithmetic: 1,72,750.66 (p.277) - 1,70,465.34 (p.340)), well below LBPL turnover of Rs 4,043.09 lakh.
- Two readings fit these numbers. Reading A: about Rs 18 crore of LBPL's own "turnover" is shown in the consolidated accounts as "interest subvention income". Reading B: a separate subsidiary subvention exists and LBPL turnover falls by elimination of intra-group sales. The observation that separates them: LBPL's own FY26 audited statement of profit and loss (revenue note and other-income note).
- Calls: the only subvention answers concern the parent. "Interest subvention is in the form of actual interest which you are paying under the scheme on your term borrowings of which 50% maximum to the extent of 6% will be given to the credit" (Concall_May_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.14). "There is a government grant of INR 107 crores standing on 30th September, 2025, which is on account of the last two-year PLI and interest subvention to be receivable from the NABARD." (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.9). Management statements, not audited. Neither names a subsidiary.
- Rating view: "around 70% of the interest subvention income came in over FY25-9MFY26" (20260428-d45123bf.pdf, p.3). Group level; no entity split.

Verdict: NOT DISCLOSED. Which entity, loan and scheme account for the Rs 1,801.16 lakh are not stated in any note, footnote or call answer in the corpus.
