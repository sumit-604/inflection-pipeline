## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Garware makes polyester film and coats it into premium products. The two main ones are sun-control film for car and building glass and paint-protection film for cars.
2. It sells mostly abroad. Exports were 75% of sales last year. Overseas subsidiaries resell much of it.
3. Sales this quarter were Rs 696 Cr. That is 22% more than a year ago.
4. A year ago a US tariff hurt sales, so the base was low. Over two years, sales grew only about 6% a year.
5. Operating margin, before other income, was 24.4%. It rose on last year but fell from 27.2% in Q1.
6. Net profit was Rs 129 Cr, up 42% on last year. Profit per share dipped slightly from Q1.
7. Management raised its FY27 margin target to 27%, plus or minus 2%. It kept the Rs 2,500 Cr sales target. That margin includes other income, mostly mutual-fund gains.
8. The good: no debt, about Rs 920 Cr of net cash, and margins well above Indian film peers.
9. The worry: cash from operations covered only 0.75 of profit for the group. For the parent alone it was 0.36. Stock and money owed by the subsidiaries absorbed the cash.
10. The worry: the pollution board seized a Rs 25 lakh bank guarantee for an alleged breach of consent conditions. The results do not mention it.
11. The new TPU line (the base layer for paint-protection film) is "on track" but not yet running. The company still buys this layer from others.
12. Watch next quarter: the Q3 margin before other income. 22.3% or more is good. 19.5% or less means Q1 was a one-off.
