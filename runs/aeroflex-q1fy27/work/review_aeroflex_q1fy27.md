### 2C. Six diagnostic answers

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes. Consol +72.38% (145.38 vs 84.33), standalone +75.55%. Against FY27 guidance of 35% growth, the Q1 run-rate is running well ahead: annualised consol revenue ~581 Cr vs an FY27 guide implied ~596 Cr (35% on FY26 441.94) — Q1 is broadly on the guided full-year trajectory, front-loaded.
2. **Did operating EBITDA margin expand?** Expanded. Consol Q1FY27 23.04% vs Q1FY26 18.35% = +469 bps; standalone 23.76% vs 19.61% = +415 bps. Consol margin sits at the 23% FY27 guide.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow?** Yes, +169.87% consol (25.30 vs 9.37). This is the cleanest test and it is strongly positive: headline growth IS real, not treasury-driven.
4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT growth (+169.9%) and reported PAT growth (+162.2%)?** The gap is small and adverse, driven almost entirely by the tax line. PBT rose +166.8% but PAT rose +162.2% because net tax rose from 2.54 to 7.10 Cr (ETR 26.16% to 27.42%). Other income (+0.27 Cr) is immaterial. No exceptional items either period.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** No absorption deficit. D&A grew +32.3% and finance costs +98.4% against revenue +72.4%, so both scaled BELOW revenue this quarter, which is why margin expanded despite the capex cycle. The absolute finance-cost jump (0.18 to 0.35 Cr) confirms new debt for capex but the base is tiny.
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** No. Other income is 0.60 Cr consol, 2.3% of PBT, down from 3.4% a year ago. There is no treasury masking; strip it and the read is unchanged.

**Step 2 net:** an operationally clean, high-quality growth quarter. Margin expanded YoY, and roughly 98% of the +11.63 Cr PAT increase is core operating (see Step 4 bridge).
