## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Poojaa melts aluminium and
casts it into engine and transmission parts, then machines them. It uses
gravity, low-pressure and high-pressure die casting, and holds over 600 SKUs
(RHP, page 138). Products include intake manifolds, cylinder head covers,
camshaft carriers, clutch housings and EV transmission housings. Two plants at
Chakan, Pune, carry 13,800 MTPA of melting and 6,000 MTPA of casting and
finishing capacity (RHP, page 139). The money is conversion margin on metal,
at a 17.62% EBITDA margin in FY26 (RHP, page 116).

**What is changing?** Capacity, and a slow widening of the customer base.
The IPO funds a third plant that more than doubles casting capacity, from
6,000 to 12,600 MTPA (RHP, page 97). The customer count rose from 39 in FY24 to
58 in FY26 (RHP, page 138). The largest customer fell from 41.94% of revenue to
31.75% (RHP, page 144).

**Why now?** The plants ran full in FY26: 80% of melting and 84% of casting
capacity (RHP, page 143). Revenue grew 32.4% and PAT 29.1% in FY26 (RHP, page
116). Growth needs the new plant.

**What must be true?** Unit 3 must start on time and fill with orders at the
current margin. Its machinery arrives by August 2026 and the capex runs to
30-Jun-2027 (RHP, pages 96 and 100). The commercial-vehicle cycle must hold:
CV parts are 50.88% of revenue (RHP, page 138).

**What breaks it?** Concentration and a CV downturn. The top two customers
take 50.59% of revenue and the top ten 88.64% (RHP, page 144). A cast-parts
maker with this profile has little room to hold price if a big buyer pushes.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming, all issuer-claimed. The RHP cites integrated melting to machining,
IATF 16949 at both plants, reverse-engineering with X-ray inspection, a
17-person design team, and customer quality awards (RHP, pages 139 and 143).
It claims safety-critical parts and a new aerospace vendor approval (RHP, page
138). A magnesium sample line is planned as Unit 4 (RHP, page 139).

Against that: aluminium die casting is a crowded trade. Pricing power is
unproven. The EBITDA margin moved in a narrow 15.80% to 17.97% band over three
years (RHP, page 116). Switching cost exists inside a validated part, not
across the customer. Moat: forming on process breadth, absent on price.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R2, a cost-driven aluminium caster for Indian commercial vehicles. TO R3,
a spec'd supplier of machined, safety-critical parts across auto, EV, energy,
defence and aerospace.

Mechanism: more machining content, more customers, new sectors, and a doubled
plant.

The RHP's claim, quoted once: "We endeavour to provide our customers with
integrated solutions covering design, engineering, melting, casting, cleaning
and value-added processes" (RHP, page 138).

ROCE of 26.38% already sits in the R3 band of 20% to 25% and above. The
concentration and the flat margin do not. Non-auto share rose from 14.00% in
FY24 to 19.21% and then fell to 17.61% (RHP, page 138). **Ladder reading: R2
on pricing, R3 on returns. The claimed diversification has stalled.**

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS**, for now.

The bull case is real on paper. Three years of 30% revenue growth, ROCE above
26%, full plants and an equity-funded doubling of capacity. The bear case is
equal in weight. One document, no post-listing result, no guidance, one
customer at a third of sales, a promoter group with sister companies in the
same trade, and a price at twice the rung neighbourhood. A `/step1` now would
spend a full run to verify an offer document. Re-screen on the first filed
result.

**Load-bearing facts a later `/step1` must verify first**
1. H1 FY27 revenue, PAT and operating cash flow, the first listed filing.
2. Unit 3 commissioning date and first-half output.
3. Identity and contract terms of Customer 1 and Customer 2.
4. Every transaction with Jayshree Pistons, Jayshree Die Castings and the G K
   companies, and whether any compete for the same customers.
5. Issue price and final proceeds.

**What would change the view**
- H1 FY27 revenue growth above 25% with Unit 3 running and positive operating
  cash flow re-opens this as WATCH.
- A top-customer share falling below 25% would weaken the concentration flag.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides
only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
