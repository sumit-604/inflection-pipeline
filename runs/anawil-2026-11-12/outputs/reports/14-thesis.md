## SECTION 10: NARRATIVE (plain English)

Anawil makes steel towers for wind turbines. It began in April 2021 with wire mesh and moved to towers in 2023. It now has two plants with room for 612 towers a year. It made 210 towers in FY26 and 107 in the half to September 2026.

Some things are real. Tower volume grew every year. Profit per tower rose from about Rs 19.5 lakh to Rs 33.7 lakh. Return on capital held near 27% through the IPO. The IPO paid off Rs 115 Cr of debt. The order book covers the next eight months.

Other things are not proven. The plant in Kutch has no customer approval yet. Cash from operations was weak in FY26. Nobody has shown who carries the steel price risk in integrated supply. New orders outside two big ones were near zero for seven months. Turbine makers that build their own towers are gaining share. The promoter group's records with Darpan do not agree with each other.

The price asks for more than the evidence gives. At Rs 381 the stock trades at 22 times next year's base earnings. The framework's exit multiple is 16.1 times. So even if earnings grow as planned, the multiple shrinks. The base case returns about 5% a year from here. The rules as written say AVOID, for two reasons: the backward quality score is AVERAGE, and the downside is about four times the upside.

The thing to watch is order flow. If new tower orders arrive at the old pace, and Kutch gets approved, the volume case holds. If they do not, FY28 can fall to about 196 towers. The H2 FY27 results, due about May 2027, decide most of this. The operator decides whether a lower price alone should reopen the name.
