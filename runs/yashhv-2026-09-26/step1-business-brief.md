## WHAT IT SELLS

Condenser-grade transformer bushings (the insulated lead that carries current through a
transformer tank wall) for transformer OEMs and utilities, plus retrofit and testing services.
Plant at Savli, Vadodara. FY26 mix: RIP (resin impregnated paper) 83%, OIP (oil impregnated
paper) 10%, high current 3%, others 4%; domestic 93%, export 7%; retrofit 6-7% of revenue
(FY26 call summary, https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/yash-fy26-93883, SECONDARY).
Claims over 30% share of India's HV bushing segment up to 245 kV (company profile, SECONDARY).

## THE ONE ENGINE THE THESIS WOULD REST ON

Capacity plus localisation. A Rs 153 cr greenfield plant at Suncity Industrial Park, Vadodara adds
about 6,000 RIP/RIS bushings a year (FY26 sold 7,272 units), scope raised from 220 kV to 550 kV,
and makes RIP/RIS cores that are now imported. Trial production end-Q2 FY27, commercial H2 FY27
(https://www.tndindia.com/trial-production-at-yash-highvoltage-transformer-bushing-factory-expected-in-h1fy27/).
Order book Rs 400 cr at Mar-2026 vs Rs 150 cr at Mar-2025. FY27 guide: invoicing Rs 360-400 cr,
EBITDA margin 24-25%, order booking Rs 500 cr plus; export target 20% plus in 2-3 years through
Yash HV USA Inc. and distributors (Weidmann, Electrolink) (FY26 call summary, SECONDARY).

   customer concentration and receivable days from the AR notes.

   cyber fraud hit H2FY26; the Company Secretary changed in May-2026. Stages 2, 3 and 8 find what was
   restated and why (AR note, auditor's report), and any internal-control remark.
4. **Import-substitution claim.** RIP/RIS cores are imported today; the new plant makes them and
   targets 550 kV. Stages 4, 6 and 9 test the claim against Hitachi Energy and Quality Power
   commentary on bushing supply, pricing and import share.
