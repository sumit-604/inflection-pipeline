- Note 3.1 Business Combination policy (p.169): acquisition method under Ind AS 103; **NCI acquisitions treated as equity transactions** (difference between consideration and NCI carrying value taken to retained earnings, no goodwill on step-acquisitions of existing subsidiaries); **common-control combinations use pooling-of-interest** (net assets at carrying value, difference to capital reserve) — this year an "Impact due to common control business combination" of ₹19.75m was credited directly to retained earnings (Consolidated SOCE, p.166), **bypassing the P&L** — 🟡 correct accounting per Ind AS but a direct-to-equity credit worth naming as a quality item (no P&L benefit, so not earnings management, but changes reported net worth without passing through the income statement).
