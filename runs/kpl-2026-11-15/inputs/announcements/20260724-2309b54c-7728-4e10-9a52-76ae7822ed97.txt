Subject: Kwality Pharmaceuticals Ltd receives CDSCO Nod to Initiate 
Preclinical Pilot Batches of Pembroluzimab 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
Kwality Pharmaceuticals Limited has achieved a significant milestone in its biologics 
development journey after receiving permission from the Central Drugs Standard 
Control Organization (CDSCO) to manufacture pilot batches of Pembroluzimab 
for preclinical studies. 
The approval follows a comprehensive review of the product's development data 
submitted by the Company's R&D team. Based on the data evaluated, CDSCO has 
granted permission to proceed with pilot-scale manufacturing for the generation of 
preclinical batches, marking the product's transition from the research and 
development stage to Pre-Clinical stage. 
KPL aims to be among the first wave of generic manufacturers to launch 
Pembroluzimab following the expiry of the reference product's patent, subject to 
successful development and the necessary regulatory approvals.
