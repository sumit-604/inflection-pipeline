Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited
Silkflex Polymers India Ltd Commences Commercial Production at its 
State-of-Art Automated Manufacturing Facility in Vadodra, Gujarat, India
Kolkata, November 14th, 2025 - Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd, a leading supplier of high-performance 
textile printing inks and auxiliaries with a growing pan-India customer base, is pleased to announce the 
launch of its first manufacturing facility in the country. The Company has commenced commercial 
production of its flagship products “Silkbond 35” and “Silkflex Glue”.
A state-of-the-art, eco-efficient manufacturing hub spread across 10 acres of land, built to power Silkflex India’s 
next phase of growth, featuring a 72,000 sq. ft. fully automated state of art manufacturing facility
Glimpse of Factory Inauguration:
Key Highlights:
•
Scale, Industry & Strategic Significance
✓Marks a major forward integration for Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd, strengthening its presence in the 
specialty textile chemicals industry.
✓Enhances domestic manufacturing capabilities for high-performance, eco-friendly textile binders and 
glues, reducing import dependence.
•
Installed Production Capacity
✓Installed capacity of 500 tonnes a month.
✓Engineered for stable, high-throughput production with scope for modular capacity enhancement.

Commenting on this achievement, Mr. Tushar Lalit Kumar Sanghavi, Chairman & Managing 
Director, Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd, said, 
“We are delighted to announce the launch of our long-awaited manufacturing facility, a milestone that 
marks the beginning of a new and exciting growth phase for Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd. This plant 
represents years of planning, commitment, and belief in India’s potential to lead the next wave of sustainable 
textile chemistry. Built across 10 acres with a highly automated, boiler-less and eco-efficient design, this 
facility strengthens our capability to deliver world-class, environmentally responsible products to the textile 
industry.
With an initial focus on Silkbond 35 and Silkflex Glue, and a production capacity of 500 tonnes a month, we 
are now fully equipped to meet the rising demand from major textile hubs across the western region. This 
launch not only reduces our dependence on imports for these two products but also enhances our ability to 
scale, and serve our customers faster and more efficiently. We are confident that this strategic expansion will 
unlock significant value for all stakeholders and propel Silkflex India into its next era of sustainable and 
profitable growth.”

About Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited
Founded in 2016 by the late Mr. Lalit Kumar Hargovindas Sanghavi and Mr. Tushar Lalit Kumar Sanghavi, 
Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd is the exclusive distributor of Silkflex’s eco-friendly, water-based textile inks and 
wood coating polymers in India. The company has built a strong presence across the textiles, garments, and 
furniture sectors, offering a wide portfolio of 108 textile printing inks and 51 wood coating products sourced 
from Silkflex Polymers SDN BHD, Malaysia.
With a commitment to sustainability and high-performance chemistry, Silkflex Polymers (India) Ltd is expanding 
its footprint by venturing into domestic manufacturing of advanced textile ink products. Its product range is 
backed by globally recognized certifications such as ZDHC Level 3, OEKO-TEX® Eco Passport, GOTS 7.0, and 
GREENGUARD, reinforcing the company’s focus on safe, environmentally responsible, and industry-compliant 
solutions for the Indian market.
