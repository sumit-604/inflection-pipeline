## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Cubex makes copper and copper
alloy tubes, rods and strips at Patancheru, Telangana. Buyers are in power,
switchgear, refrigeration, heat exchangers, automobiles and ships (Annual
Report FY26, doc 3abf8f69, page 20). The margin is the conversion spread
between metal bought and product sold. FY26 revenue was Rs 288.01
crore (Annual Report FY26, doc 3abf8f69, pages 54 and 63; rupee figures on this
card are derived from Rs lakh).

**What is changing?** A stated plan, not yet a build. In May 2025 the company
said it plans vacuum melting and a vacuum arc furnace for nickel alloys,
titanium, special steels and copper alloys. It called this a "major Capex Plan"
and said the funding mode was not yet frozen (Q1 FY26 filing, doc 110e7f8a,
page 3).

**Why now?** Reported profit rose 11.9% in FY26 to Rs 7.45 crore (Annual Report
FY26, doc 3abf8f69, pages 54 and 63). That headline hides the core. Other income
was Rs 8.22 crore, more than the whole of profit after tax.

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The alloy plan must be funded,
built and sold at a premium the copper line does not earn. The plant must also
make money before other income. In FY26 it made Rs 1.42 crore (derived).

**What breaks it?** The core is near zero, and stock is rising while finance
cost climbs. A copper price fall on a large inventory would turn the small core
profit into a loss.
