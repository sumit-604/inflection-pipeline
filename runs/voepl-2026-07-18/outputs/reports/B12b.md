## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, anchored)

Each item: what I found, anchor (call / speaker / Q&A location), and my read.

**IF1 — Mainboard listing slipped in every one of the four consecutive calls (>1 year of "imminent").**
- FY25 (Jun-2025), Siddhanth & Garvit Q&A: "latest by next week, the process will get completed... we are positive."
- Q2 FY26 (Nov-2025), Sidharth Jain Q&A, SS: "make the application before the end of this month [Nov]... anywhere between two and four months."
- Q3 FY26 (Feb-2026), Akhil Shah / Siddhant K. Q&A, SB: "one technicality in terms of the traded volumes... hoping to file soon... hopefully H1."
- Q4 FY26 (Jun-2026), Raghav Maheshwari Q&A, SS: "received the in-principle from BSE... NSE in the next maybe week... another four to six weeks... by end of August."
Read: repeated evasion across 4 quarters — the single strongest recurring credibility signal. Externally blamed (BSE/SEBI/exchange) each time.

**IF2 — FY27 revenue target quietly diluted from ~₹2,000cr to ~₹1,100cr, never framed as a cut.**
- Q2 FY26, Pawan Q&A, SB (re: Arihant conference ₹2,000cr FY27 target): "We hope so, but we'll confirm maybe Q4."
- Q4 FY26, Garvit Goyal Q&A: analyst: "one year back, we were targeting somewhere around INR1,200 crores... now we will be marginally crossing INR1,000 cr." SS: "35%, 40% CAGR on an INR825 crore base." SB: "We are not revising the guidance."
Read: informal target halved across three deferrals; management denies a revision occurred. Communication-consistency red flag.

**IF3 — FY26 revenue guidance walked down twice, then landed below the original floor.**
- FY25 call, SB: "₹1,000 crores... between ₹900 crores and ₹1,000 crores... ₹1,100 crores if season goes well."
- Q2 FY26, Akhil Q&A, SB: "₹800 crores to ₹900 crores would be a more accurate number."
- Q4 FY26, SS opening: "closed the year at INR823 crores... roughly 18% top line growth."
Read: delivered ₹823cr, below the original ₹900cr floor. Blamed on BEE table change / inventory / season.

**IF4 — Voltas-concentration reduction promise reversed within one quarter.**
- Q2 FY26, Nitya Shah Q&A, SB: Voltas "close to 100%. Next year, we anticipate somewhere between 40% and 60%."
- Q3 FY26, Piyush Jain Q&A, SB: "the share of Voltas has either remained the same, volume of Voltas remain the same or is expected to grow."
Read: the diversification headline metric reversed direction while the qualitative story stayed upbeat.

**IF5 — FY26 PAT-margin miss and near-flat PBT despite EBITDA up ~43%.**
- FY25 call, Kunal Tokas Q&A, SB: PAT margin "Between 2.5% and 3%." Q2 FY26, Kunal Q&A, SB: "anywhere between 2% and 3%... for the full year."
- Q4 FY26, SS opening: "PAT... from a 1.7%... moved to 1.8%" (₹15cr); "PBT levels have remained more or less stagnant... first year that we have moved to Ind AS... right of use assets."
Read: PAT missed; EBITDA moved ₹60cr→₹86cr but PBT flat — the EBITDA-to-PBT waterfall is where the value leaks. Ind AS/ROU explanation is plausible but reduces confidence in PBT/PAT-level guidance.

**IF6 — FY26 CapEx overshot original guidance ~30-40%.**
- Q2 FY26, Kunal Q&A, SB: "about ₹100 crores... to maybe ₹110 crores."
- Q3 FY26, Disha Q&A, SB: "almost close to INR120 crores so far... INR130 crores to INR150 number by March end."
Read: overshoot against a standing ₹250cr uncommitted equity approval (Q4 FY26). No specific reason volunteered.

**IF7 — Washing machine vertical silently dropped from the Q4 narrative.**
- Q2 FY26, Siddhant Q&A: capacity 200-250k, ₹60-100cr; Q3 FY26, Shreyans/Garvit Q&A: pilot done, ramp "April/May," peak ₹100cr.
- Q4 FY26 (Jun-2026): zero mention in the presentation or any Q&A (segments now listed as EMS, AC, refrigeration, compressor).
Read: two quarters of active discussion then complete silence — a discretionary line that appears to have missed its field-trial timeline.

**IF8 — ROE double-digit promise never revisited.**
- FY25 call, Bhuvaneswaran Q&A, SB: "next FY [FY26], we should be in double-digit" ROE.
- Not raised by management or any analyst in Q2/Q3/Q4 FY26.
Read: clean silent topic on a capex-heavy, low-ROE-optics business.

**IF9 — Deep-freezer capacity expansion (150k→400k) deferred ~6 months.**
- Q2 FY26, SB presentation: "ramp up the capacity from 150,000 to 400,000 before the end of the next financial year."
- Q3 FY26, SB: "we deferred it for about six months... by Q1 or Q2 in FY27."
- Q4 FY26, Bala Murali Krishna Q&A, SB: candid — "important to conserve cash. Hence, we did not push on projects which were not absolutely essential."
Read: honest, self-attributed slip — a credibility positive, but still an execution deferral.

**IF10 — Chennai AC plant commercial-production date slipped, reframed rather than owned.**
- Q2 FY26, SB: "start operating from first week of January... commercial production by end of Q4."
- Q3 FY26, SB: "by Q1, the plant will become operational. And the real benefit... only for the next financial year."
Read: quiet slip presented as a timing nuance.

**IF11 — Compressor capacity target bounced repeatedly, including inside the Q4 call itself.**
- Q3 FY26, SB: "scale up to 7.5 million if the direction from the government is in line."
- Q4 FY26: SS presentation "2.8 million... to 6 million before the end of this financial year"; SB to Omkar "initial target... about 8 million... 8, 8.5 million"; SB to Hitaindra "7 million to 6.5 million... to optimize the line," then "9 million or 10 million" if demand supports.
Read: the number people should model (6 / 7 / 7.5 / 8.5 / 9.5mn) is unstable within a single call.

**IF12 — Compressor EBITDA-margin guidance drifted down from the original FY25 figure, then partly back up.**
- FY25 call, Manan Madlani Q&A, SB: "7% to 8% EBITDA margin."
- Q2 FY26, Garvit Q&A, SB: "between 5% and 8%." Q3 FY26, Siddhant K. Q&A, SB: "about 5%... overall 9%-10% guide is based on 5%." Q4 FY26, SS/Omkar: "6% and 7% this year."
Read: the compressor margin (the #1 trigger's payoff) has been a moving target; the FY25 "7-8%" starting point silently became "5%" before settling at "6-7%."

**IF13 — Prior EMS/LED metric ("6 crore units") could not be reconciled with the current "4 lakh CPH," management sidestepped.**
- Q3 FY26, Siddhant K. Q&A: "earlier we used to highlight 6 crore units in the LED EMS space. Right now... it's 4 lakh CPH. So, what is the conversion?" SB: "it is difficult to define your product-wise capacity... it becomes more chaotic."
Read: a previously disclosed capacity metric was redefined, breaking like-for-like tracking; the reconciliation was declined. Minor but a genuine transparency gap.

**IF14 — In-year compressor FY26 revenue expectation cut ~50%.**
- Q2 FY26, Siddhant Q&A, SB: "about ₹30 crores odd will come from compressors" for FY26.
- Q3 FY26, Siddhant K. Q&A, SB: 9M compressor "About INR15 crores odd... started mass production only in mid-November or December."
Read: small absolute number, but a halving driven by a delayed mass-production start on the flagship new segment.

**IF15 — Q4 employee-expense jump and trade-receivables increase, both dismissed in one line.**
- Q4 FY26, Abhishek Hindocha Q&A, SS: employee expense — "nothing extraordinary"; receivables — "it is a normal thing... at par with what one would expect" (no DSO figure given).
Read: two balance-sheet/cost questions waved off without a number.

Cross-company (peer-vs-VOEPL) note carried from B06: VOEPL stated the QCO reciprocating-compressor **import allowance is 40%** (Q4 FY26, SS: "only up to 40% of the import that any manufacturer would have done in the base year, which is '24-'25"; SB reinforced). B06 flags two peers (AMBER, EPACK) citing **30%**. See Part 4 caveat — VOEPL was explicit this is the *reciprocating* category, whereas the peers make *rotary/room-AC* compressors, so the two figures may not be the same QCO line item.

## PART 4 — CREDIBILITY GRADE

B05 grade: **B**. I **concur**. Independent read shows a granular, mostly forthcoming management that self-attributes at least one clear miss (freezer capex deferral, tied explicitly to cash conservation) and genuinely delivered on the two things that matter most (FY26 EBITDA margin beat, compressor launch near original timeline with a favourable regulatory resolution). Against that sits a broad, one-directional pattern of downward revisions (FY26 revenue, PAT, FY27 target, Voltas share, freezer, Chennai, listing) handled with reframing on the hardest recurring questions. That mix is a defensible B; a case exists for B-minus given how many separate metrics walked down in the same window, but the EBITDA delivery, compressor execution, and candour on the freezer deferral keep it off C.
