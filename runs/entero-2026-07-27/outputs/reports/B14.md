This is an investable business at an un-investable price. The AVOID is a valuation call, not a quality verdict. The reviewable test each quarter is two sided: has the price fallen into the zone, and has the fundamental ladder held.

## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why the business is real)

The bull case is genuine and is why this is a WATCHLIST name and not a discard. It is stated at full strength before the price is weighed.

**3A. Quality Evidence.** FY26 shows a multi metric inflection. Revenue Rs6,591 Cr, up 29.4% YoY. Core EBITDA margin 4.03%, up 60bps. Statutory ROCE 9.41%, up from 6.68%. Operating cash flow turned positive at +Rs96.2 Cr, the first positive year in a seven year series, against -Rs76.9 Cr in FY25. Net operating working capital days fell to 59 in Q4 FY26 from 70 at FY25 close. The caveat that caps the quality read: seven year cumulative CFO/PAT is still -1.05x, and the FY26 turn is one year old.

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction).**
1. MedTech mix shift (conviction H, 12 to 24 months). Seven FY26 acquisitions took MedTech past a Rs1,000 Cr run rate and above 15% of revenue, aspiration 20%, at roughly twice the base distribution margin. Confirms if FY27 quarterly prints show margin gains sustained without new deals. Kills if margin gain reverses when deal flow stops.
2. FY27 guidance delivery (conviction H, 6 to 12 months). Guided 23% ex M&A revenue, 5.0% EBITDA margin, at least 50% OCF/EBITDA. Confirms if Q1/Q2 FY27 track the guided path as FY26 tracked. Kills if margin lands at the 4.6% bear or OCF conversion stalls.
3. M&A pause holding through FY27 (conviction M to H, 6 to 12 months). Integration focus. Confirms if no large deal (over Rs100 Cr revenue) is announced through Q2 FY27. This is the SHARED CATALYST: the same pause drives Pillar 1 ROCE recovery and part of Pillar 3, a single point of failure.

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Five STRONG moats at Gate 0. The forward emerging moat 25/80 rests on two documented pillars: industry consolidation beneficiary (organized share moving from 8 to 10% toward 20 to 30%, limited buyer competition) and a working capital improvement trajectory. Direction is strengthening, but on one guided fiscal year. No monopoly; MedPlus, Redington and RPTech are active.

**3D. Management Quality.** Founder chief executive is a former Alkem Laboratories chief executive (CA, CFA, ISB MBA), professional management from inception. Credibility grade B: hit the 31.5% like for like growth guide, the 4.0% EBITDA floor and the 59 day NWC target, landed OCF marginally short at Rs96 Cr against a Rs100 Cr plus wording. Against that, three governance dings: a stonewalled debtor ageing request on the December 2025 call, an unreconciled GLP-1 share figure, and a repeated non structural answer on IPM multiplier compression from 1.8x to 1.4x.

**3E. Market Opportunity.** TAM Rs2,50,000 to Rs2,90,000 Cr gross distribution value; SAM Rs1,75,000 Cr. Entero holds 3.8% of SAM, leaving 26.6x revenue headroom, runway class MASSIVE. The binding constraint is not physical but funding: gross borrowings grew about 90% in FY26 against 29% revenue, and Rs1,700 to Rs2,500 Cr of incremental working capital is implied by the three year share gain path.

**3F. Peer Positioning.** No direct pharma/MedTech distribution comparator exists. Twelve peer transcripts partly confirm and partly complicate. Redington and RPTech corroborate insulation from cross border trade shocks. The IPM acceleration to about 12% has zero peer corroboration; MedPlus supports only the 7 to 8% baseline. The MedTech 10% plus EBITDA margin claim has no direct analog and the available thin margin comparators run below Entero's own 4%. A Redington services gross margin disclosure above 20% supports the agency margin logic independently.

**Decision rule trace (Master v3.3 Role 2).** AVOID fires on four independent triggers, any one of which is sufficient: Hurdle Ratio = STOP; Gate 0 = AVERAGE; Promoter = CONCERN; Upside/Downside 0.36x below 2x. This is an over determined AVOID. The Promoter CONCERN cap alone bars any BUY and caps the ceiling verdict at WATCHLIST even if the price were in the zone.

**Position sizing.** No position is taken, so no Small / Medium / Large size applies. For the record, were the price in the zone and fundamentals confirming, the Promoter CONCERN cap and Gate 0 AVERAGE would bind the size to Small (2 to 3%) at most; Large and Medium both require Gate 0 GOOD or better and a promoter verdict of TRUSTWORTHY or above, neither of which holds. No operator override to position size was recorded in the deliberation.

**Entry conjunction (anti value trap).** A future re-engagement executes only when BOTH hold: price inside Rs506 to Rs569 AND the most recent quarterly checklist shows no thesis-broken trigger fired. If the price reaches the zone because the cash turn reversed (NWC above 65, OCF/EBITDA below 50%) or a goodwill impairment landed or NCI stepped past 27%, that is a withdrawn zone, not an opportunity. Price entering the zone on a broken trigger is not a buy.

**Zone reachability (honest assessment).** The entry top of Rs569 sits 55.3% below CMP Rs1,273; the MoS at Rs455 is 64.3% below. Reaching the zone requires roughly a halving of the price with no offsetting fundamental improvement, or a smaller fall paired with disappointing prints (which would itself withdraw the zone). At the operator flat 15.0x forward exit, the base fair value Rs1,112 is already 12.7% below CMP, so even a full de-rate to fair value does not reach the entry zone; the zone is set by the 25% hurdle divisor, not by fair value. Practically, this is a low probability re-entry in the near term absent a broad small cap drawdown or a specific stumble. The honest read: this is a park-and-watch name, not a buy-on-the-next-dip name. A 10 to 20% pullback does not make it actionable; only a move into the Rs506 to Rs569 band with the ladder intact does.

## NARRATIVE

Entero is a business that turned a corner in FY26 and a stock that has already been paid for turning it. Revenue is firing, the MedTech and agency mix shift is lifting margins, working capital days fell to 59, and operating cash flow went positive for the first time in seven years. The FTTCP work reads the four transitions honestly at plus five of eight, a BUY candidate on the business. None of that is in dispute here.

The dispute is price. At Rs1,273 the market pays 48.1x trailing owners' earnings for a distributor that earns a 4% EBITDA margin and a 9.41% statutory ROCE, whose cash turn is one year old and whose seven year cumulative cash to profit is still negative, and whose promoters carry an auditor confirmed fraud finding at three subsidiaries with no narrative in the annual report. Even granting the operator's generous 15.0x forward exit and the full base ladder to Rs62 of FY29 owners' EPS, the fair value lands at Rs1,112, below today's price, for a -4% three year return. The hurdle ratio is 0.74, a STOP, and it stays a STOP on bull earnings because the de-rate from 48x to 15x is the dominant term, not the growth.

So the verdict is AVOID, and it is a valuation verdict, not a quality one. The re-engagement is disciplined and two sided: the price must fall into Rs506 to Rs569, and the FY27 and FY28 prints must confirm the margin, the cash and the working capital, with no thesis-broken trigger fired. That conjunction is unlikely in the near term without a broad drawdown or a specific stumble, and a stumble that drags the price down would withdraw the zone rather than open it. Park it, watch the eight monitorables, and let the price come to the business rather than the other way round.
