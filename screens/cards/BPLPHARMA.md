## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Four companies under one listed parent in Vadodara. The parent (BPL standalone) makes generic injectables and oral solids and sells them through export tenders and registrations in 40+ countries. It did Rs 234.01 cr of revenue in FY26 at a 9.0% EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27 results, page 4). Innoxel Lifesciences, 55.9% owned, is a US FDA and EU GMP cleared complex-generics CDMO. It did Rs 72.4 cr in FY26, mostly licensing and milestone income, and lost Rs 43.3 cr (Investor Presentation, page 20). Varenyam Healthcare sells 45 hospital brands in India, Rs 58.4 cr in FY26 (May 2026 concall, page 5). Varenyam Biolifesciences is a pre-revenue plant under construction.

**What is changing?** Innoxel is moving from development fees to manufacturing revenue. On 20 October 2026 the first product it makes under a CMO contract shipped to the US (Q2 FY27 results, page 14). A second CMO product is expected in FY27. The first own-developed 505(b)(2) product, which carries a profit share, is targeted for Q3 FY28 (Investor Presentation, page 21).

**Why now?** Q2 FY27 is the first clearly profitable group quarter in this corpus. Consolidated revenue was Rs 136.28 cr, up 110.9%. EBITDA was Rs 23.58 cr at 17.3%. Profit to owners was Rs 9.42 cr against a loss of Rs 7.24 cr (Q2 FY27 results, page 14). Innoxel alone earned Rs 14.64 cr of EBITDA at 32.9%.

**What must be true?** Innoxel must replace lumpy milestone income with recurring supply and profit share. Management itself calls milestone income "a one-time revenue, so we don't consider that as our potential revenue" (Nov 2026 concall, page 17).

**What breaks it?** Milestone timing. Innoxel revenue was Rs 37.5 cr in Q4 FY26, then Rs 21.4 cr in Q1 FY27 with an EBITDA loss of Rs 1.8 cr (Investor Presentation, page 7). Innoxel carries about Rs 36 cr a year of depreciation and interest and Rs 161.67 cr of debt (Q2 FY27 results, page 16). A light milestone half with a slow supply ramp puts the group back into loss.

## 2. Business model and archetype

H1 FY27 segments: manufacturing Rs 108.91 cr, trading of pharma products Rs 55.22 cr, CDMO Rs 65.89 cr (Q2 FY27 results, page 10). Injectables are 45% to 50% of standalone revenue, oral solids most of the rest (Nov 2026 concall, page 9).

The standalone business is a tender price-taker. Its Q2 gross margin fell to 34.9% from 39.8% as input costs rose 490 bps of revenue, on an order book priced before the rise (Q2 FY27 results, page 15). Innoxel earns fees per milestone now, a supply price next, and a profit share on own products later (Nov 2026 concall, page 11).

**Archetype: three. Outsourcing partner (CDMO) for Innoxel, the engine of the thesis. Commodity converter for the standalone tender book (Amendment 17 binds it). Brand/franchise consumer for Varenyam Healthcare.** Any valuation must split them and strip Innoxel's 44.1% minority.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming at Innoxel. Absent in standalone. Innoxel holds a US FDA EIR and a Belgian EU GMP clearance with zero critical and zero major observations (May 2026 concall, page 5). The company cites only 226 FDA-catalogued CMO facilities worldwide (Investor Presentation, page 22). A CMO named in a partner's filing is slow and costly to replace, so the asset is regulatory qualification. Varenyam Healthcare has one small scarcity asset: India's first Sugammadex 100 mg/ml, over Rs 10 cr of FY26 revenue (May 2026 concall, page 5). The standalone book shows no pricing power: its margin follows input cost.

## 4. The promoters

The Desai family. Bharat Desai is Chairman and Managing Director (AR FY26, page 38). His son Bhahim Desai runs strategy and investor relations, holds every call, and says he founded Varenyam Healthcare in 2016 (Nov 2026 concall, page 5; AR FY26, page 167).

Promoter group 67.17% at 31 March 2026 (AR FY26, page 62). Desai Shares and Stock Pvt Ltd 39.63%, Hima Desai 13.97%, Bharat Desai 10.76% (AR FY26, page 100). Pledge 0.00%. The preferential issue of 9,12,759 shares at Rs 1,522.85 takes promoters to 59.34% (Investor Presentation, page 36).

Family pay in FY26: Bharat Desai Rs 1.43 cr, Hima Desai Rs 1.72 cr, Bhahim Desai Rs 1.22 cr, Nikita Desai Rs 0.64 cr (AR FY26, pages 167 and 168). That is Rs 5.0 cr in a year when owners lost Rs 8.19 cr (Financials.txt, Data Sheet).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

Group FROM R1, a tender price-taker with a FY26 consolidated EBITDA margin of 4.6%. Innoxel claims R3: a spec'd, regulator-qualified CDMO with switching costs. Innoxel starts below any rung, since it has never earned a full-year operating profit. A climb from loss-making to R3 is a multi-rung leap, which the ladder treats as a red flag until proven.

Management's claim, once: the first US shipment "moves Innoxel from development and validation work into recurring commercial supply" (Nov 2026 concall, page 5).

## 8. Proof check

The trigger claims recurring supply. The last two quarters show a margin turn, not supply. Q2 Innoxel revenue was still "mostly licensing, milestone and development income" (Nov 2026 concall, page 5). Supply revenue is zero in the numbers. Q1 FY27 shows the downside: Innoxel lost Rs 10.36 cr in that quarter (Q2 FY27 results, page 14).

The regulatory proof is present. The revenue proof is absent. Q3 and Q4 FY27 supply revenue is the proof point.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- Related-party acquisition. BPL bought 100% of Varenyam Healthcare in FY25 for 3,51,225 new shares at Rs 1,479.90, Rs 51.98 cr (AR FY26, page 117). Its founder is the CMD's son. Hima, Bhahim and Nikita Desai holdings rose in FY25 (AR FY26, page 100).
- Varenyam Healthcare, a subsidiary, holds 16,261 BPL shares inside the promoter group (AR FY26, page 100). A cross-holding.
- Consolidated EPS includes the minority's profit. Q2 EPS of Rs 17.15 is Rs 11.82 cr over 68.92 lakh shares; on owners' profit it is Rs 13.67. FY26 EPS of Rs (39.62) is Rs (11.88) on owners (Q2 FY27 results, page 9).
- Dilution: 58.20 lakh shares in FY24, 68.92 lakh in FY25, 78.05 lakh post-issue (Financials.txt, Data Sheet; Investor Presentation, page 36). That is 34% in about two and a half years.
- Parent support to Innoxel: loans Rs 39.17 cr and a Rs 39.50 cr corporate guarantee (AR FY26, page 117).
- Guidance record. Management admits earlier guidance was missed and calls this year's "on the softer side" (May 2026 concall, page 8). Varenyam Bio's budget moved from about Rs 160 cr (May 2026 concall, page 6) to Rs 300 cr, and its timeline slipped a quarter.
- Disclosure errors. Slide 34 showed a Rs 180 cr order book against the correct Rs 117 cr (Nov 2026 concall, page 18). The US shipment date reads 20 October and 28 October in the same call (pages 5 and 26).
- Innoxel partners and molecules are never named. Concentration is unknown.
- Auditor: unmodified limited reviews (Q2 FY27 results, pages 2 and 8). No emphasis of matter found in AR FY26 by search.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Clearance and a first shipment are delivered. Recurring supply revenue has not printed. The one profitable Innoxel quarter is milestone income, which management calls one-time.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** The FY25 and FY26 losses come from depreciation on validated Innoxel capacity ahead of revenue: depreciation rose from Rs 9.02 cr to Rs 35.32 cr (Financials.txt, Data Sheet). The standalone input-cost squeeze is structural to that line, but that line is not the climb.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market capitalisation Rs 1,317.1 cr, Bull AI company record, 2026-11-04, a service record and not a filed document. Trailing four-quarter owners' profit is Rs 1.24 cr (Financials.txt, Data Sheet). The guided FY27 multiple is 45x to 123x (step 13), far above the R3 neighbourhood of about 19x. The stock also trades above the Rs 1,522.85 issue price.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** Proof not fired, optics an artifact, the TO state already priced.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.** Hard watch. No `/step1` yet.

The Innoxel asset is real and scarce. The quarter is real. The market prices the recurring-supply and profit-share future, and the corpus holds zero rupees of either.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. Innoxel's Q3 FY27 revenue split between supply and milestone income.
2. The partner's binding forecast for the first CMO product (Nov 2026 concall, page 17).
3. The credit ratings for BPL and Innoxel.
4. The post-allotment share count and the split of the Rs 139 cr across subsidiaries.
5. The Varenyam Healthcare swap valuation and seller list.

**What would change the view**
- Two quarters of Innoxel supply revenue with EBITDA margin above 20% fires the proof gate.
- A price that brings the forward multiple near the R3 neighbourhood re-opens the gap.
- A Q3 milestone drought with Innoxel back in loss confirms the trap.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
