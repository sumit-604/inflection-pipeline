# Stage 6: Peer concall verification. Sudarshan Colorants India Ltd (SUDARCOLOR), run 2026-11-12

Units: Rs Cr unless stated; EUR where the peer states EUR. Main-company numbers come from B05 (Results Q2 FY27, AR FY26, as anchored there).

Anchor convention: (PEER, quarter call, speaker, page). Page is the printed "Page N of M" in the transcript where one exists. ASAHISONG Q2 FY25 and Q4 FY25 carry no printed page numbers; for them the page is the file page marker, shown as "file p.N".

Evidence grading. SUDARSCHEM is the PARENT and promoter of SUDARCOLOR. Its statements about the subsidiary are interested-party evidence. Its statements about the global pigment industry are better evidence, but the parent still talks its own book. MOL (phthalocyanine blue and green pigments, about 75% agro revenue) and ASAHISONG (CPC blue, Azo, API) are adjacent peers, not like for like. SUDARCOLOR's range (Azo and high performance pigments from Roha and Cuddalore) overlaps with ASAHISONG's Azo line only.

## Key finding in five lines

1. No peer contradicts a SUDARCOLOR claim. None confirms the size of the Q2 FY27 jump (269.76 vs 183.14, +47.3%).
2. The parent explains part of the jump: a low Q2 FY26 base (destocking trough), volume recovery from Q4 FY26, and Frankfurt-to-Roha transfers with "intercompany business" gained (SUDARSCHEM Q1 FY27, Rathi, p.12). The group share is NOT FOUND in every call.
3. Independent peers show industry growth of 3-4%, pigment plants at 39-51% utilisation, and price-led growth. A +47% year-on-year quarter does not read as open-market demand.
4. Peers passed input inflation through and held or raised pigment margins. SUDARCOLOR's gross margin fell (39.3% vs 41.7% H1, B05). Peers do not explain that fall.
5. On the minority question, the parent says the Rs 411 transfer is holding-structure rationalisation with "nothing further to that" (SUDARSCHEM Q1 FY27, Mukhopadhyay, p.10). It does not mention merger or delisting. The analyst's "70% stake" does not match B05's 15.89%.

## PART 4: Triangulation summary

- Claims verified: 0 of 8. Partially verified: 8 of 8, with two sub-claims UNVERIFIABLE (payable stretch; named HP Azo, food-contact and recycling grades) and one fact NOT FOUND (group share of Q2 FY27 revenue).
- Claims contradicted: 0. No item goes to synthesis as a contradiction. Three divergences go as priority items: gross margin fell where peers held or rose (Claim 5); inventory build against the parent's group cash release target (Claim 6); parent says 70% stake while B05 shows 15.89% (Claim 8).
- Most consequential gap, in place of a contradiction. The size of the Q2 FY27 jump cannot be placed. Independent peers show 3-4% industry growth, idle plants and price-led growth. Only the parent explains the routing, and only qualitatively. If group sales carry the jump, the Q2 FY27 revenue is a re-allocation of group production, not open-market demand.
- Strongest independent confirmation. The war-driven input shock and pass-through, from three peers across all four quarters, and the tariff reversal from about April 2026 (MOL Q1 FY27 p.4; ASAHISONG Q4 FY26 pp.14-15). Both lift the H2 FY27 read for the tariff and pass-through part of the thesis.
- Overall. The peer set complicates the narrative. It supports "destocking is over", "the industry is flat" (which makes the 3% AR figure credible) and "tariff headwind has eased". It undercuts none of the company's claims. It does not support the idea that SUDARCOLOR's +25.0% H1 is open-market demand, and it shows peers passing costs through while SUDARCOLOR's gross margin fell. The two live readings stay open: (1) base effect plus real demand recovery; (2) Frankfurt-to-Roha production re-allocation. The H1 FY27 Reg 23 split of group versus third-party sales separates them.

Parent's Q2 FY27 call is not in the corpus. Read it when it is filed.

## PART 5: Cross-peer hypothesis

In the 2026 cycle, Indian pigment volume is splitting into two streams. Stream (a) is open-market demand: industry 3-4% (SUDARSCHEM Q4 FY26 p.14), plants at 39-51% utilisation (MOL), margin recovery that comes from price and cost pass-through (MOL pigment 3.3% to 12.1% then 10%; ASAHISONG Blue gross margin 29% to 34%), and Chinese prices up 30-40% (ASAHISONG Q4 FY26 p.22). Stream (b) is production that integrated groups re-allocate from high-cost foreign sites to Indian plants, as the parent moves Frankfurt lines to the Indian Heubach site at Roha. No peer states this split. MOL and ASAHISONG describe only (a). The parent describes only (b), and only in passing. [INFERENCE]

Test: the H1 FY27 Reg 23 filing should show SUDARCOLOR group sales rising faster than third-party sales, with third-party sales near flat. If instead third-party sales also rise by 20% or more, the open-market recovery reading wins and the split does not hold.
