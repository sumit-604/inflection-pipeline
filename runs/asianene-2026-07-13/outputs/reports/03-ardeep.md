## 2A Accounting Policy Aggressiveness — Extension
- **Revenue recognition:** Standard Ind AS 115 input/output-method policy for a percentage-of-completion
  services business (Note 1(b) standalone, p.80-81). Heavy reliance on management estimate (cost-to-
  complete, variable consideration for LD/penalty/warranty) is inherent to the business, but the 234%
  contract-asset growth against 52% revenue growth (finding #4) is the concrete symptom that should
  concern a reader more than the policy language itself, which reads as boilerplate-standard.
- **Depreciation:** Schedule II lives used with internally-assessed exceptions for seismic/oilfield-specific
  equipment (10-15 yr survey/ground electronics, 5-15 yr portable drilling rigs) — industry-reasonable,
  not independently benchmarked against peers in this document.
- **Oil-asset accounting:** "Successful efforts" method, unit-of-production depletion — conservative,
  standard industry practice; exploration costs pre-legal-rights expensed immediately.
- **Ind AS 116 lease treatment:** Company extensively uses the low-value/short-term lease exemption:
  ₹4,001.52L (standalone, Note 39.2(iii)) / ₹4,005.38L (consolidated) of machinery/vehicle hire is expensed
  directly rather than capitalized as ROU assets — this materially understates on-balance-sheet lease
  commitments and reduces visibility into the Company's true fixed-cost/leverage profile. ROU assets on
  balance sheet are just ₹43.52L (down from ₹161.17L) — trivial relative to the ₹4,000L+ of off-balance-
  sheet-style hire charges running through the P&L.
- **No policy changes quantified** during the year beyond the tax-shield exhaustion discontinuity already
  covered under finding #5.

**CFO quality checks:**

**DuPont decomposition (consolidated, closing-balance approximation):**
