### 1C Trigger evolution across the four calls

- **Non-ibu mix shift**: strengthening every call. Q3 FY26: ibu 64% / non-ibu 36% of pharma
  (vs 66%/34% prior year). Q4 FY26: ibu 62% / non-ibu 38%. Q1 FY27: ibu 57% / non-ibu 43%,
  non-ibu revenue +67% YoY, crossing Rs200 Cr/quarter run-rate. This is the one trigger with a
  clean, monotonic, cross-quarter delivery trail.
- **Paracetamol utilisation**: strengthening in narrative, flat in the one number that matters.
  Guided to rise from 55% (Q4 FY26 call) to 70-75% by FY27-end; one quarter later (Q1 FY27
  call) still ~55%, no interim uptick reported. TIMELINE TO WATCH (see Section 4).
  Q3 FY26 call actually cited "around 60%" for the pre-enhancement/enhanced blend, a figure
  not fully reconciled with the "55%" quoted for Q4 FY26 -- management explains the base
  changed (3,600 MTPA -> 10,800 MTPA), not a contradiction but a reporting-basis shift worth
  noting.
- **Greenfield site**: weakening/slipping across every touchpoint. Q3 FY26: EC granted,
  pursuing NOC/highway approvals. Q4 FY26: "4-6 to 6-8 quarters" to come up. Q1 FY27: walked
  back to "probably not in this FY." Sep 2026: "approvals still in process ... initial base work
  started," no capex materially deployed. Four touchpoints, zero firm commissioning date.
  TIMELINE SLIPPAGE (flagged in 4D and the YAML).
  Q3 FY26 call also raised a "CMO for API only" forward-integration idea (explicitly NOT
  formulations); by the Sep 2026 call this had become a formulations CDMO business
  (1,500 mn tablets/yr). Read generously this is strategy sharpening under customer pull
  ("they came back to us that why don't you supply us the bulk tablets"); read skeptically it is
  a pivot from an earlier stated position with no call-out of the change.
- **Ibuprofen capacity**: NEW trigger, appearing only in the Sep 2026 business update call
  (+6,000 MTPA), not foreshadowed as a specific capacity number in any of the three quarterly
  calls (which discussed ibuprofen only as an existing, near-full-utilisation asset). The "why
  now" answer management gives (customer development pipeline, CDMO backward-linkage,
  90-95% utilisation ceiling) is coherent but unverifiable in this corpus; PENDING LIVE
  VERIFICATION against named customer names, which management declines to give.
  Competitor-exit rumours (Solara, SMS Pharma) run in parallel across three calls but
  management explicitly declines to link the new capacity to any competitor's distress
  ("this is a continuation of our strategy ... not depending on any current time situations").
- **Patent pipeline**: DROPPED. Discussed in detail only in Q3 FY26 call (3 process patents --
  sitagliptin, vildagliptin, losartan -- all stalled on nitrosamine-impurity or enzyme-usage
  issues, none commercial). Never mentioned again in Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27, or the Sep 2026 call.
  No analyst asked again either.
- **Buyback**: raised once (Q4 FY26 call), explicitly deprioritised ("till the time we have
  growth projects ... buyback perhaps may be the second option"), never revisited. Resolved,
  not evasive.

### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

Every explicit miss found in the transcripts and its classification:

- **Q2 FY26 power-cost overshoot (referenced retrospectively in Q3 FY26 call)**: blamed on
  Punjab floods. EXTERNAL-BLAME, but plausible and specific (named a weather event, named a
  rupee quantum "Rs7-8 Cr").
- **Q3 FY26 EBITDA margin miss vs internal Q3 expectation**: "we missed by Rs2-3 Cr, not big
  amount" -- CFO names a small quantum and attributes it to non-reduction of husk/fuel prices.
  HONEST-ADMISSION, small stakes, self-quantified without being pressed hard.
  the walked-down FY26 margin sequence (14-15% -> 13% -> 11-12%) is not once flagged by
  management as a "miss" in its own language -- it is narrated as "we are just understanding
  that we will be reaching something 11% to 12%" -- soft language that reframes a guidance cut
  as a clarification. DEFLECTION (mild): the change is disclosed but not named as a miss.
- **Pattern check**: management does credit external factors when costs rise (fuel, floods, war,
  logistics) and credits itself ("operational efficiency," "capacity utilisation," "internal
  efficiencies") when results beat -- a mild version of the externalise-losses/internalise-gains
  pattern, though tempered by the CFO's specific Rs2-3 Cr admission above.
  Management raises hard topics only when an analyst raises them first (guidance cuts,
  competitor distress, ibuprofen margin pressure) in every instance found; no example exists in
  any of the four calls of management proactively volunteering a negative development in its
  opening remarks. Opening remarks across all four calls are uniformly positive ("resilient,"
  "disciplined execution," "encouraging," "strong start").

- US FDA approving five products without a physical site visit, via ANDA co-filing with the
