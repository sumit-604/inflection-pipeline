Dear Sir, 
 
We wish to inform you that the Company has received an Order-In-Original (O-I-O) confirming demand of 
differential Customs duty of Rs. Rs. 27,97,398/-, along with applicable interest, penalty of Rs. 27,97,398/- 
under Section 114A of the Customs Act, 1962, additional penalty of Rs. 100,00,000/- under section 114AA 
of Customs Act, 1962 along with redemption fine of Rs. 50,00,000/-, aggregating to Rs. 2,05,94,796/- 
alleging incorrectly availed ASEAN-India FTA exemption by using Certificates of Origin not conforming to 
the prescribed format under Notification No. 46/2011-Customs read with ASEAN-India FTA Rules of Origin. 
 
The Company will be filing an appeal against this order with the appropriate Authority. 
 
Requisite details as required under Regulation 30 of Listing Regulations read with clause 20 of Para A of 
Part A of Schedule III of Listing Regulations and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-
1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, are provided in Annexure A. 
 
We request you to take this intimation on your record. 
 
Thanking You,
