### 1C. Trigger evolution across the two calls

- **Revenue growth pace: WEAKENING, most material change.** Q_FY23 (Jul 2023): implicit ~60% ("would love to maintain the same rate of growth"). H1FY24 (Nov 2023): explicitly cut to 30% for FY24, management stating first six months were "tough." A halving of the growth commitment inside four months.
- **FY26 Rs 250cr / $25M target: UNCHANGED headline, but credibility weakening.** Reaffirmed in both calls, yet the underlying growth math becomes progressively less plausible: FY23 base ~Rs52cr, guided FY24 growth cut to 30% implies FY24 ~Rs68cr, which would then require roughly 90%+ combined FY25-FY26 growth to reach Rs250cr — a jump the H1FY24 call does not address or reconcile.
- **Blue-chip customer win: DROPPED.** Named as an explicit FY24 goal in Q_FY23 ("get at least one of the blue chip customer this year"). Never mentioned again in the H1FY24 call, in either prepared remarks or Q&A.
- **Client-count target (25-30 → 40): status unclear, no update.** Q_FY23 gave a specific numeric target and a one-year timeframe. H1FY24 call gives no client-count figure at all; management instead cites "diversified" top-five sector mix as a qualitative substitute.

H1FY24: "we have not gotten to a point where we have started evaluating their balance sheet," 6-12 months away. Language moved from "advanced stage" to earlier-stage diligence language while the outer time window also lengthened.
- **Hyperscaler partnerships (Google/AWS/Microsoft), Rust capability, Dubai/Africa expansion, solution-oriented product sales: NEW triggers, all first appearing in the H1FY24 call.** None were mentioned in Q_FY23. ### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

For the one confirmed miss with an in-transcript explanation (FY24 growth guidance cut), management's reason was **external-blame**: "one of the challenges that we have had in the first six months and I don't think this is particularly just for Systango. But I think this is for IT sector in general... customers getting very conservative" (H1FY24, Vinita, p.5). No instance across either call of management saying "we made a mistake" or naming an internal execution failure. Management also raised the funding/demand slowdown proactively in the Jul 2023 call ("I am little conservative about the market," p.4) before analysts asked — a point in favour of transparency — but did not proactively flag, in the Nov 2023 call, that the FY26 Rs250cr target's implied FY25-26 growth math had become extreme after the FY24 cut; that gap was never raised by management or by any analyst on the call.

Pattern check: management credits itself for the FY23 "extraordinary" 60% growth and 60% margin performance (attributed to "market situations were very favourable," "we did well," Q_FY23 p.11) while blaming the H1FY24 slowdown on external, sector-wide conditions — a classic asymmetric attribution pattern (own success = skill/positioning, own miss = macro).

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. What management says about competitors

Management describes the industry as "very saturated and very competitive" (Q_FY23, p.16) and self-positions "very close to companies like Accenture" as a "consultancy as well as integrator" — a scale comparison management itself does not substantiate given Systango's ~300-person headcount vs Accenture's global scale. When directly asked for named peers in H1FY24 ("what sort of peer do we see in India or internationally"), management named only Thoughtworks and repeated Accenture, then stated "I just can't recall any name from top of my head right now" (H1FY24, p.11) — declining to name any India-listed small/micro-cap peer. Credibility check: this is evasive rather than substantive; it gives no basis for investors to benchmark Systango's growth, margin, or valuation against true comparables. This gap is the direct basis for the peer_questions handed to stage 6 below.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence

- Industry-wide IT sector consolidation phase, headcount rationalization across the sector for the "last two or three years" (Q_FY23, Vinita, p.3).
- Funding slowdown among early-stage/growth-stage companies (Systango's core customer base) through H2FY23 and into H1FY24, lengthening customer decision cycles and lead times (Q_FY23 p.4; H1FY24 p.4-5).
- Payment-cycle deterioration cited as an industry signal: customer invoices that used to clear in 2-3 days now taking 30-60 days on contracted terms (Q_FY23, Vinita, p.13) — management view, not an audited receivables disclosure.
- Crypto/blockchain-linked headwinds: FTX collapse and US regulatory action against Binance/Coinbase cited by an analyst as an industry stress point (Q_FY23, Sahil Patni, p.16); management drew a distinction between crypto-trading volatility and underlying blockchain technology demand, calling the trading-side stress "largely a compliance and governance issue" (Q_FY23, p.17).
- BlackRock's prospective spot-ETH ETF filing cited by an analyst as a potential H2FY24 tailwind for Web3 activity; management agreed this "is going to actually help us" (H1FY24, Manan Madlani/Vinita, p.10) — management view, unquantified.
- Demand recovery signal claimed from June/July 2023 trough into November 2023: "we have started seeing demand coming back again... acquiring customers like the way we acquired last year similar time" (H1FY24, Vinita, p.10) — management view, no supporting metric given.

## SPEAR LOAD-BEARING FACT [1] — GRADED

**Claim:** Management guided ~30% revenue growth for FY24 (H1FY24 call, 17-Nov-2023) and Rs 250cr / $25M revenue by FY26 (Q_FY23 call, 7-Jul-2023).
**Delivery:** ~8% actual FY24 revenue growth; Rs 90.4cr actual FY26 revenue (~64% shortfall vs the Rs250cr/$25M target), per injected delivery evidence (May-2026 audited results / Jun-2026 deck).
**Grade:** FAIL on both revenue-side legs of the claim. The only leg of management's guidance framework that held was EBITDA margin (>25% target, delivered 37.6% FY26) — a cost-side promise, not the growth-side promise that anchors the SPEAR fact. This is the primary evidence behind the D credibility grade and the Section 4A #1 trigger's LOW conviction rating.
