**DuPont decomposition (standalone, using year-end balances):**
ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier
- FY26: 2.29% × 1.80x × 2.31x = **9.52%** (ties almost exactly to the disclosed 9.51% Return on Net Worth)
- FY25: 3.63% × 1.63x × 2.71x = **16.03%** (ties closely to the disclosed 16.10%)

**This is a genuinely useful, previously unstated finding: the ROE decline (16.10% → 9.51%) is margin-driven, not
leverage-driven.** Leverage actually *fell* (equity multiplier 2.71x → 2.31x, as CCD-linked equity grew faster than
assets); asset turnover *improved* (1.63x → 1.80x, revenue grew faster than the asset base). The entire ROE
decline is attributable to the net margin compression (3.63% → 2.29%), which in turn traces to the near-zero-
margin trading revenue line and the profit decline discussed in Phase 2/3C. This means the Return on Net Worth
"reason" column's own explanation ("favourable market conditions," see 3C) is not merely vague — it is
*directionally wrong on the mechanism*: the change is not leverage or market-condition driven in the sense
implied, it is a margin-mix story the Company's own disclosures do not name.

more specificity and capital detail in the Board's Report and AGM Notice than in the Chairman's own letter.

### 6C Metrics showcased vs conspicuously absent
Showcased: revenue growth %, absolute net profit, solar capacity (3.35 MW total), export credentials, ISO
certification, decades of experience. **Conspicuously absent from the front matter and Chairman's letter:**
EBITDA margin trend, net margin trend, ROE/Return on Net Worth (both disclosed elsewhere but not by the
Chairman), promoter-family compensation as a share of profit, the export/FX emergence, capacity utilisation, and
any acknowledgement of the profit decline.

### 6D Tone and priority drift vs prior year
Not independently inferable from this single AR (prior-year AR not in this stage's corpus); however, the FY24-26
three-year Board's Report summary table (p.50) shows the Company had a much lower base in FY24 (Turnover
Rs 13,832.65 lakh, PAT Rs 229.46 lakh, EBITDA margin 5.89%) than the FY25 peak (EBITDA margin 8.13%, PAT
Rs 512.40 lakh) — FY26's profit and margin are a *retreat toward the FY24 level* after a stronger FY25, a pattern
the narrative frames entirely as forward progress ("strengthening capabilities for sustainable growth," the AR's
own cover theme) rather than as a partial reversal.

### 6E Quiet Abandonment Check (mandatory)

**Opening claim:** The Chairman's letter states, of Shakambhari Dyechem Private Limited: "the Company is in the
process of setting up its manufacturing facility and **is expected to commence production in the near term**"
(p.7). This is the single forward-looking operational claim in the entire front-matter/opening narrative.

**Where it should have shown up operationally, and did not:** The operational evidence available elsewhere in
this same AR — Note-level CWIP ageing (per B02's Pattern Pass: consolidated CWIP includes Rs 73.55 lakh aged
1-2 years, meaning the subsidiary's capex has been running for over a year with zero assets capitalised to date),
AOC-1's disclosure that Shakambhari Dyechem Private Limited's FY26 turnover is **Nil** and it posted a **loss**
(Rs 9,92,342), and the Board's Report's own admission that the subsidiary crossed the "material subsidiary"
threshold only **after** the FY26 year-end (p.62) — together describe a capex programme that is still, more than
two years after the subsidiary's incorporation (24-04-2024 per AOC-1), pre-revenue and without a stated
completion date anywhere in the AR.

**Classification: (c) hedged retreat.** The opening says "near term" (X); the operational sections do not name a
completion date, a capitalisation date, or a revenue-start date at all (X-lite, with the scale of the delay — over a
year of aged, uncapitalised CWIP — never acknowledged in the letter). This is not an outright contradiction (the
Chairman does not claim production has started, nor does any other section claim it has not); it is a claim made
with confident, time-bound-sounding language ("near term") that the AR's own operational disclosures, read
end to end, do not support with any date, percentage-complete figure, or capitalisation milestone.

**Materiality: significant, and directly load-bearing for this pipeline's own thesis.** The Company memory / Step-1
brief frames the entire investment thesis as "promoter-funded expansion...lifts H-Acid and new-intermediate
volume, so a cyclical converter grows into a larger, steadier chemicals base," with the explicit open question of
whether this is a real volume climb or a spread cycle. The Chairman's "near term" framing of the one concrete
expansion vehicle, when the AR's own Notes-derived evidence shows a stalled, aged, zero-revenue capex
programme, means the front-matter narrative is ahead of the operational reality on the single fact this thesis most
needs to be right about.

No further quiet abandonments were identified in the remaining opening-vs-operational cross-reads (the "strong
and sustainable" strengths section, p.8, and "strengthening strategies" section, p.9, are both generic enough that
no specific, checkable claim is made that operational sections could confirm or withdraw).

### Phase 6 summary
**Phase 6 verdict: 🟡 WATCH.** The front matter is optimistic-but-not-false on the numbers it states; its principal
defect is omission (profit decline, margin compression, the warrant issue, the export/FX story) and one
significant hedged-retreat pattern on the SDPL production-timeline claim (6E).
