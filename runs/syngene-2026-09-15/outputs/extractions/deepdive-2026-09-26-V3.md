## V3 Q5 — Research Services / CDMO split, dedicated-centre revenue, top-10 client share, active client count

### A. Annual-level revenue mix (Research Services vs CDMO / Development & Manufacturing)

**FY25 (Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 180):**
> "Revenue from Research services (Dedicated Centers+ Clinical Development + Discovery Services) – increased by 2% compared with last year... The contribution to total Syngene revenue from Research Services was at approx. 61% for the year similar to previous year."

So FY25: Research Services ≈ 61% of total Syngene revenue (implies CDMO/Development & Manufacturing ≈ 39%), per the AR's own framing — Discovery Services + Dedicated Centers + Clinical Development bundled as "Research Services."

**FY26 (Annual_Report_2026.txt):** No equivalent explicit "% contribution to total revenue" sentence was found in the FY26 MD&A/Board's Report narrative for Research Services (searched exhaustively around the FY26 "(iii) Syngene's Research Services" section, page 188–189, and the Board's Report Research Services block, page 68–69). This is a change from the FY25 report's disclosure style — FY26's Research Services section (page 188) describes capability additions (ADCs, peptides, biotherapeutics, the Phase III win, Dedicated R&D Centre strengthening with BMS through 2035) but gives no revenue percentage.

**External corroboration (CRISIL rating rationale, 2026-09-11_CRISIL_rating-rationale_AA+stable.txt, para starting "Syngene is a leading contract research..."):**
> "the company began operations with the research vertical and slowly emerged to the development business, with a revenue mix of nearly 61% in fiscal 2026 from the research business comprising of discovery services, dedicated centres and translation and clinical research and balance from the CDMO business."

CRISIL states FY26 Research Services mix ≈ 61% (roughly flat vs FY25's 61%), but this is CRISIL's own restatement, not a number sourced directly from the Syngene AR/MD&A text found in this corpus. Flagged as such — anchor is the CRISIL document, not the AR26.

- FY26: Rs 13,926 Mn / Rs 37,387 Mn = 37.2% (computed; anchors: AR2026 p.369 major-customer note and AR2026 p.17 revenue-from-operations figure)

NOT DISCLOSED: which two customers make up the >10% figure, or an actual top-10 aggregate (only "two customers" is disclosed).

### B. Revenue from operations (for the computation) — anchors

- FY25 revenue from operations (consolidated): **Rs 36,424 Mn**, up from Rs 34,886 Mn in FY24 (Annual_Report_2025.txt, page 186, MD&A: "Revenue from operations increased by 4%, from Rs Rs 34,886 Mn in FY24 to Rs 36,424 Mn in FY25"; confirmed in Notes, page 268/ consolidated financial statements page ~271, "Total revenue from operations 36,424 34,886").
- FY26 revenue from operations (consolidated): **Rs 37,387 Mn**, up 3% YoY (Annual_Report_2026.txt, page 17, Chairman's/Value-creation section: "Syngene reported revenue from operations of Rs 37,387 Mn, up 3% year-on-year"). Also stated as "Rs. 3,739 Cr (~US$419 Mn)" in the 2026-04-30 investor deck, page 9.

(Note: the AR25 Board's Report "Total revenue" table, page 91, shows a different, broader figure — Rs 37,142 Mn consolidated FY25 — because it includes other income; the Rs 36,424 Mn figure is specifically "Revenue from operations" per the MD&A and financial-statement line, which is the basis used below for comparability with the FY26 "revenue from operations" figure.)

### C. Revenue from operations per scientist — (computed)

- **FY25: Rs 36,424 Mn ÷ 5,641 scientists = Rs 6.46 Mn per scientist** (computed; anchors: Annual_Report_2025.txt p.186 revenue-from-operations figure of Rs 36,424 Mn, and p.48 scientist headcount of 5,641)
- **FY26: Rs 37,387 Mn ÷ 5,778 scientists = Rs 6.47 Mn per scientist** (computed; anchors: Annual_Report_2026.txt p.17 revenue-from-operations figure of Rs 37,387 Mn, and p.49 scientist headcount of 5,778)

For reference, revenue from operations per total employee (computed, same anchors):
- FY25: Rs 36,424 Mn ÷ 8,235 = Rs 4.42 Mn per employee
- FY26: Rs 37,387 Mn ÷ 8,373 = Rs 4.47 Mn per employee

Revenue-per-scientist was essentially flat year-on-year (Rs 6.46 Mn to Rs 6.47 Mn, computed, +0.2%), against consolidated revenue-from-operations growth of ~3% and scientist headcount growth of ~2.4% (5,641 → 5,778, computed).

## V3 Q7 — Clinical trial revenue; global Phase 3 win detail

### A. Clinical trial / Translational & Clinical Research (T&CR) revenue

No standalone rupee figure or percentage-of-revenue figure for clinical trial revenue, or for T&CR as a discrete line, was found anywhere in the corpus (AR26, AR25, four concalls, three decks, four results filings, press releases). T&CR / clinical trials are folded into the "Research Services" bucket (see Q5). Management explicitly declines to break it out:

- **Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt (Q2FY26), p.6, line ~204-208 (Deepak Jain):** "I would rather refrain from commenting upon how it compares to our business and our margins across each of the businesses because we typically don't break down our businesses, right, in terms of margin structures."
- **Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.9-10, line ~567-571 (Peter Bains):** "the translation and clinical research part of our business, which today is a relatively small part of our business, as an area of strategic growth opportunity... We are not in a position to give any quantitative forward guidance or outlook."

NOT DISCLOSED: T&CR / clinical trial revenue in rupees or as a % of total revenue, for any year or quarter — would be found (if ever disclosed) in a future segment note or investor-deck revenue bridge; none exists in this corpus.

### B. The global Phase III clinical trial win — detail found

**First disclosed:** Q2FY26 concall (06-Nov-2025) and the Q2FY26 press release (05-Nov-2025), then referenced again in AR26, Q3FY26 concall, Q4FY26 concall, and Q1FY27 concall.

- **Sponsor/counterparty:** "a U.S.-based biotechnology company" (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.2, line ~74-75: "Syngene has been awarded our first global Phase III clinical trial by a U.S.-based biotechnology company"; same wording in 2025-11-05_press-release_Q2FY26.txt, line 151 and 178). Name of the sponsor: NOT DISCLOSED anywhere in the corpus ("without our collaboration partner['s]" consent to discuss detail — Concall_Jan_2026_Transcript.txt, p.6, line ~192, on a separate but related confidentiality point about the large biologics customer, not this trial specifically, but no sponsor name is given for the Phase III trial in any document).
- **Trial scope/geography:** "This trial will recruit patients across clinical sites both in India and the United States" (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.2, line ~75-76); "our first global clinical trial recruiting here in India and in the United States" (Concall_Jan_2026_Transcript.txt, line 472).
- **Phase:** Phase III (global), explicitly "first global Phase III clinical trial" — Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt (line 75, 198), 2025-11-05 press release (line 151, 178), Annual_Report_2026.txt page 69 ("Syngene also secured its first global Phase III clinical trial from a U.S. based biotech company") and page 189 ("T&CR initiated the first global Phase 3 clinical trial, demonstrating capability to deliver large, complex multinational studies").
- **Tenure/value timing:** "the economics of this trial will be spread out over the 3 years. And the bulk of that will come in FY '27 and '28" (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.5, line ~212-214, Peter Bains); confirmed again: "we see the bigger economics of this global trial flowing through in fiscal '27 and '28. It's a multiyear trial" (same transcript, p.9, line ~351-352).
- **Contract value / deal size in rupees or dollars:** NOT DISCLOSED. Management was asked directly and declined to quantify (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.5-6, exchange with Kunal Dhamesha, line ~192-209) and again described only qualitatively as "a large market opportunity" (line ~473-475) with "no... quantitative forward guidance or outlook" (line ~569-570).
- **Revenue-recognition nature:** "a nature of a service contract... we would do the trials on patients and generate revenue through the client that asked us to do the trials," built around "patient numbers and the analytics... samples and the follow-up and the reporting" (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.10, line ~443-449, Deepak Jain / Peter Bains). Explicitly NOT linked to the new Biologics/ADC manufacturing facility — "It's a separate business altogether. They're not linked" (same page, line ~450-452).
- **Incremental investment required:** "nothing significant" — patient recruitment split between India (existing hospital-network platform) and the US (via a collaborator); "we'll operationalize it... before the end of this fiscal" (i.e., before end FY26) (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.9, line ~345-352, Peter Bains response to Harith Ahamed).
- **Follow-on capability build (post-award):** Expansion of "clinical trial footprint across Australia and New Zealand, the U.K. and Eastern Europe through strategic partnerships with established CRO players" (Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.txt, p.3, line ~78-81); Annual_Report_2026.txt page 189 adds: "T&CR established strategic partnerships with global CROs in USA, UK, New Zealand, Australia, Sri Lanka and Jordan for conduct of early phase and late phase global clinical trials."
- **Later concall references (status updates), no new sponsor/value detail given:**
  - Concall_Jan_2026_Transcript.txt (Q3FY26), line 472: "first global clinical trial recruiting here in India and in the United States. And that continues to make [progress]" (sentence trails into next topic; no update on value or timeline change).
  - Concall_Apr_2026_Transcript.txt (Q4FY26), line 286: "What we guided in quarter 2 was a win that we had on a global trial. Now the global trial..." (context cut off in extract at line 286; the surrounding text discusses guidance mechanics, not new trial-specific facts — see file for full passage if further detail is required).
  - Concall_Jul_2026_Transcript.txt (Q1FY27), line 335: "Phase III/Phase II are some of the differentiators for us and will definitely be a large contributor" — forward-looking/qualitative, no new figures.
