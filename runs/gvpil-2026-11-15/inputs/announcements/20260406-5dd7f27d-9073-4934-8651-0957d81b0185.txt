Annexure-B 
PROFILE OF MS. RANJITA SINGH 
 
 
Ranjita Singh is an accomplished Human Resources (HR) Professional with over 24 years of multi-
sectoral experience across Energy, Automobiles, Mining, and Media in MNCs, Indian corporates and 
smaller emerging firms. Having held senior roles at global organizations including GE Vernova, Hero 
MotoCorp, Rio Tinto and India Today Group, she brings deep expertise in strategic HR 
transformation, talent management, and organizational development. She believes and sets high 
standards for professional delivery and provides unique skillsets across the HR domain.  She is a 
POSH trainer, a certified both Executive Coach and Emotional Intelligence Coach and puts this 
expertise into driving the Company’s human capital strategy and fostering a high-performance, 
inclusive culture, generating higher value for the firms. 
 
Ranjita is a physics (hons) Graduate complemented by Post Graduation in Human Resource 
Management She is also an ICF certified Executive, Genos Internal Certified Emotional Intelligence 
Coach and certified Corporate Director.
