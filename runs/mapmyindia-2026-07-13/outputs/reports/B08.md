### 3D. Minority treatment — dominant finding of this report

🔴 RED FLAG, extensively corroborated: In late 2024 CE Info Systems announced that CEO
Rohan Verma (the founder's son) would step down as CEO/WTD effective 31 March 2025 to
launch his own consumer-facing (B2C) venture. The company's original plan (announced
~29 Nov–3 Dec 2024) was to transfer its Mappls Mall and Mappls Travel consumer businesses
to the new entity, take only a **10% equity stake**, and additionally commit **Rs 35 crore
via Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs)** to fund it — with Rohan Verma personally
holding the remaining ~90% (Medianama, Business Standard, Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr,
inves4, The Morning Context — all 2026-07-13 search access, original reporting Nov-Dec
2024). 📰 MEDIA REPORTED, corroborated across 7+ independent outlets, treated as
high-confidence.

- Market reaction: the stock fell ~25% over 16 trading days from 8 November 2024,
  including an 8.9-11% single-day decline on the CEO-departure announcement, hitting a
  52-week low of ~Rs 1,534 (Business Standard, Medianama, 2026-07-13).
- Investor reaction: on a subsequent investor call, shareholders explicitly challenged the
  fairness of the structure — a company giving away a consumer business built with public
  shareholder capital for a 10% stake with no immediate royalty and unresolved data-sharing
  terms (Medianama "Shareholders question MapmyIndia's 10% stake," 2026-07-13).
- Proxy-advisory reaction: Shriram Subramanian, founder of InGovern Research Services,
  was quoted publicly: "Reputation and goodwill with investors built over 30 years has now
  been lost… investors that consider good governance will not touch the company with a
  bargepole" (YourStory, "Beyond the Rs 35 crore: Why MapmyIndia's governance crisis won't
  end here," 2026-07-13 search access, dated December 2024). This is the single most
  pointed adverse quote found in this entire background check.
- **Reversal**: the day after the investor call, the board scrapped the Rs 35 Cr CCD
  commitment entirely, retained the MAPPLS consumer brand for the listed company, and cut
  its stake in the new venture to a nominal Rs 10 lakh for 10% — with Rohan Verma now
  funding the venture himself (Business Standard "MapmyIndia jumps on scrapping proposed
  investment," Medianama "MapmyIndia halts investment," 2026-07-13).
- The AR text itself (which covers FY 2024-25 in full, including this episode) is silent
  on the CCD proposal, the reversal, and the investor-call controversy — the only AR
  traces are the bare director-change entries recording Rohan Verma's CEO resignation
  (AR L2127, L3425) and redesignation to non-executive director (AR L3425), with none of
  the surrounding controversy narrated in the Board's Report or MD&A sections reviewed.
  This silence is itself notable: a governance episode serious enough to draw a public
  proxy-advisor rebuke and a 25% stock decline receives no board commentary in the annual
  report.

This is not a hypothetical "creeping RPT" risk — it is a concrete, near-consummated attempt
to transfer a consumer business (built on public shareholder capital) to the founder's son
on terms multiple independent shareholders and at least one proxy advisor considered
unfair, reversed only under acute, immediate investor pressure rather than pre-empted by
the board or independent directors. No AGM resolution, SCORES complaint, or delisting
attempt was found in connection with this episode (it never reached a shareholder vote —
it was reversed at the board/management level before formal approval was sought).

### 5C. Key-person risk and succession visibility

🔴 RED FLAG: Rakesh Kumar Verma, aged 74+, was re-appointed Managing Director for a further
5-year term (25 August 2025 to 25 August 2030) via a special resolution required under the
Companies Act because he exceeds the age-70 threshold (AR L15960-16028, L2323-2325). The
notice explicitly records that Rashmi Verma, Rohan Verma, and Rakhi Prasad — his wife, son,
and daughter — are excluded from voting on this resolution as his relatives (AR L16026-16028),
underscoring how concentrated the family's control of this decision is. **No succession
plan, named deputy, or timeline is disclosed anywhere in the AR.** Compounding this: the
one family member positioned as an operational successor (Rohan Verma, CEO) exited the CEO
role in the same fiscal year to run his own venture, and no external professional CEO was
appointed to fill the gap (5A). Key-man risk is therefore rated as **elevated and
increasing**, not stable: the sole Managing Director is 74+, committed through 2030, with
no visible succession bench and a recently vacated CEO seat.
