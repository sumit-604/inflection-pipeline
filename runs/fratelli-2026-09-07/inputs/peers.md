# Peer selection: Fratelli Vineyards (deep dive)

Date 2026-09-07. Operator: Keerti Kaushik. Corpus source: Bull AI (passages).

## Target
Fratelli Vineyards Ltd. BSE 541741, ISIN INE401Z01019. Premium wine plus wine RTD.
Thesis under test: high gross margin (77-80%) converting to a 20% EBITDA margin
by FY30 (Vision 2030), driven by premiumisation and operating leverage.

## Peer set (final)
| Peer | Ticker | ISIN | Why chosen |

## Rejected (Bull AI auto-peers, not business comparables)
Cropster Agro, KN Agri Resources, Shreeji Global FMCG, Rukmani Devi Garg Agro
Impex. All are agro-commodity traders, surfaced only because Fratelli carries an
"Agro Commodities" classification tag. None competes in branded premium alcobev.

## What each peer tests
- Sula: the ceiling and cyclicality of Indian wine economics. A scaled wine
  leader made 24-29% EBITDA at peak but fell to 17.3% in FY26 as the category
  softened. This bounds Fratelli's 20% FY30 target and shows wine is cyclical.
- Radico: proof that premiumisation works when executed at scale, and how long
  it takes (P&A mix 29% to 46% over six years). A base rate for Fratelli's climb.
- Tilaknagar: a mid-cap doing the same climb now, with pricing per case and a
  luxury launch pipeline. The nearest live analogue to Fratelli's stage.
