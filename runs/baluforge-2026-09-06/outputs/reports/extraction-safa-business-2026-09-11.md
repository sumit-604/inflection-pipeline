## HEADLINE FINDING BEFORE THE QUESTIONS

The corpus contains **no descriptive paragraph about what Safa Otomotiv FZ-LLC does**. Not one
document in the corpus states Safa's business, product, process, customer, supplier or headcount.
Every Safa reference in the two annual reports is a **name in a list or a number in a table**:
the subsidiary roll-call, the AOC-1 financial table, the material-subsidiary table, the BRSR
participation table, the investment note, the loan note, the related-party note and the
consolidation note. None of these carry a narrative field.

The four investor decks **never use the word "Safa" at all** (0 hits across all four). They
describe a UAE facility ("Unit 4", "Ras Al Khaimah") without naming the legal entity that owns
or operates it. The link between "Unit 4" and "Safa Otomotiv FZ-LLC" is **not made anywhere in
this corpus**. Every deck statement below is quoted as a statement about a UAE unit, not as a
statement about Safa.

A second name mismatch runs through the corpus. The Directors' Report and the AOC-1 place Safa
in **Dubai**. The decks place the UAE unit in **Ras Al Khaimah**, and the FY2025 annual report
plant-address block places "Plant Address Unit 4" in the **Al Hulaila Industrial Zone, RAK**.
Dubai and Ras Al Khaimah are different emirates. No document in the corpus reconciles them.
