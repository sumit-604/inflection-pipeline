# Peer selection: Borana Weaves (deep dive)

Date 2026-09-07. Operator: Keerti Kaushik. Corpus source: Bull AI (passages).

## Target
Borana Weaves Ltd. NSE/BSE BORANA, ISIN INE16SF01016. Synthetic greige fabric,
Surat, water jet looms, backward integration into PTY (textured) yarn.
Thesis under test: a 24% EBITDA margin, far above normal weaving. Is it a durable
value-added mix, or a polyester-yarn spread plus a duty windfall that reverts.

## What each peer tests
The three peers bracket Borana's 24% EBITDA and are the core of the
margin-durability test:
- BSL ~7.6%: what commodity fabric/furnishing earns in a weak patch.
- GHCL ~15%: what a well-run integrated textile earns through the cycle.
- Swaraj ~19.3%: the top of the weaver range, with spinning backward integration.
- Borana 24%: above all three. The deep dive must explain why, or treat the
  margin as a spread the peers do not have.

## Fibre caveat
Borana weaves synthetic (polyester) yarn; Swaraj, GHCL and BSL are cotton-led.
The fibre and cost curve differ, so the peers bound the margin range rather than
match Borana input-for-input. This difference is itself part of the answer:
polyester weaving margins move with the PFY/POY spread, which is cyclical.
