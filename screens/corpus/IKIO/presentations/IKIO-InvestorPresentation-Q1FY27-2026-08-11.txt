"IKIO - Transitioning From Lighting to Integrated Technology Solutions"
- Beyond Lighting | Expanding Portfolio: "Transitioning from core Home lighting to a
  diversified portfolio including home & commercial lighting, hearables & wearables,
  energy solutions, electronic components and Automotive lighting driven by strong R&D
  and ODM capabilities."
- Backward Integration & Cost Control: "Extensive backward integration drives superior
  quality control and sustainable margin expansion. In-house manufacturing enables cost
  optimization, operational efficiency, and supply chain reliability."
- Globalizing the Business Model: footprint 20+ countries. "Revenue from outside India
  grew by 53% YoY to Rs 1,101 Mn, with its contribution increasing to 18% in FY26 (vs.
  15% in FY25)."
- Scaling Manufacturing: "Enhancing capacity by ~5 lakh sq. ft. (greenfield facility
  with IPO proceeds)... Block I of ~2 Lac Sq. Ft. commercialized in May 2024. Block II
  of ~2 Lac Sq. Ft partially commercialized in Q2FY27."
- Vision: "To be a preferred manufacturing and solutions provider of Lighting, Energy
  Efficient Products, Electronic Components and Hardware Components in the global
  marketplace."

ANOMALY FLAGGED BY THE READER: the FY26 row contradicts every other statement in the
corpus. The Q4FY26 concall states Other Business was "up 53% to INR426 crores for full
year 2026" and that its contribution rose "to 71% in FY26 from 57% in FY25" (Concall
Q4FY26 p.4). Rs 426 cr = Rs 4,255 Mn. So the FY26 figures are transposed: Other Business
is 4,255 Mn and Home Lighting ODM is 1,698 Mn. The FY23, FY24 and FY25 rows are
internally consistent. The card uses the concall reading.
Deck commentary: "The company has strategically reduced reliance on the traditional
Home Lighting ODM business while steadily expanding the Other Business segments...
Other Business on a Strong Growth Trajectory: Revenue has grown at a 3-Year CAGR of
approximately 54% (FY23-FY26), with momentum sustained in Q1FY27, as revenue grew
approximately 53% YoY to Rs 1,244 Mn."

===== PAGE 15 =====
"IKIO Technologies at a Glance". "a leading ODM (Original Design Manufacturer)
specializing in the design, development and manufacturing of energy-efficient lighting
and electronic solutions for global markets. Backed by strong in-house R&D and
backward-integrated capabilities..." Core strength in lighting across decorative,
indoor and outdoor, product display and RV lighting; expanded into hearables and
wearables, solar panels, ABS pipes, IPS controllers and electronic components.
Key metrics: ~25 years of relevant experience; 3,000+ SKUs; 5 well-integrated facilities
over ~5 lakh sq ft; in-house R&D and product design team of ~50+ employees.

===== PAGE 16 =====
LED Lighting Solutions. Segments: ODM Home (wall, ceiling, spot, bulbs, table lamps,
outdoor); Instore Lighting (downlights, spotlights, high bays, panel, flood); Commercial
Refrigeration (display case, shelving, freezer lights). Categories: Home Lightings;
Product Display and Refrigeration Systems; RV and Mobility Lighting.
India market: "valued at USD 12.5 Bn in 2026 and is expected to reach USD 18.8 Bn in
2031 (~8.4% CAGR)". Tailwinds: smart city expansion, product mix shift, rising domestic
value addition.

===== PAGE 17 =====
Automotive LED Lighting. Products for two-wheelers and four-wheelers. Market "valued at
USD 25.7 Bn in 2026 and is expected to reach USD 33 Bn in 2031". Tailwinds: EV growth,
regulatory push (AIS norms, daytime running lamps), PLI-led localization, premium
vehicle penetration, transition from traditional to LED.

===== PAGE 18 =====
Consumer Electronics. Products: neckband, TWS, speakers. Market "~USD 89.5 Bn in 2025
expected to reach USD 158.4 Bn in 2034". Tailwinds: premiumisation, rising disposable
incomes, tier 2/3 penetration, omnichannel retail.

===== PAGE 19 =====
EMS (OEM/ODM) and Energy Solutions.
OEM/ODM products: automotive, e-mobility, home appliances, consumer electronics, home
automation, telecom, industrial automation, power electronics. Market "USD 33 Bn (2024)
expected to reach USD 155 Bn (2030), a ~30% CAGR". Structural shift: "Historically
dominated by Low-Mix, High-Volume (LMHV) assembly with limited value addition, the
sector is shifting towards High-Mix, Low-Volume (HMLV) manufacturing driven by China+1
and PLI enabling entry into higher-value segments."
Energy Solutions products: electronics and energy systems, switches, hardware
components, energy management. Solar PV market USD 253 Bn in 2023, global solar power
market projected USD 436.36 Bn by 2032.

===== PAGE 20 =====
Backward Integration - Presence Across the Value Chain. Steps: design and development
(in-house R&D, 3,000+ SKUs developed in-house); tool and mould designing ("almost all
mechanical components are manufactured in-house"); fabrication (plastic injection
moulding, plastic extrusion, diffuser cutting, plastic and metal fabrication);
assembly (SMT, auto-insertion, auto-gluing, lens sorting); testing and validation
("very low sales return due to high quality of product and testing"); final product
(quality control, consistent supply, healthy margins).
