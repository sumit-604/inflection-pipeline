A fall back into the 63 to 69 day range of FY24 and FY25 reads the stretch as transient and tied to growth. Inventory at 84 days or above, with the net working capital cycle at 111 days or longer, reads it as STRUCTURAL. The H1 FY27 balance sheet gives the first read.
