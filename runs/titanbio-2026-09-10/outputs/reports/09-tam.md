### Method 2 — Peer-share-inferred bottom-up cross-check (culture media only)

True unit-based bottom-up (units × price) is not buildable: Titan discloses **no
kg/tonne/litre volume** for any product in three years of filings (B04
unit_economics.unit: "NOT FOUND"). As a substitute, this method infers the India
culture media market from its named leader's revenue.

**HiMedia Laboratories Pvt Ltd** (unlisted, not a stage-6 peer because it publishes
no listed accounts) is named across multiple trade sources as India's — and one of
the world's top three — culture media / biosciences suppliers, present in ~140-150
countries (BioSpectrum India; CBInsights; Owler; via WebSearch Sept 2026).
- FY25 (year to Mar-2025) revenue reported at ~Rs 1,060 Cr, +18% YoY.
- An earlier trade citation put FY21-22 "BioSuppliers market" leadership revenue at
  Rs 1,201 Cr (BioSpectrum India) — **STALE**, >4 years old, methodology consistency
  with the FY25 figure not established; carried for direction only.

If HiMedia holds a market share in the 55-70% range typical of a category leader
against a fragmented tail of global majors (Thermo Fisher, Merck, Lonza — all named
by search as global culture-media competitors) and regional players including Titan's
TM Media, the implied total India culture media market sits at Rs 1,514-1,927 Cr —
consistent with, and a genuine cross-check on, Method 1's independently-sourced
Rs 1,866-2,114 Cr for the same line. **Confidence: Medium.** This method cannot be
extended to peptones, collagen, or ox bile — no comparable named-leader revenue
exists for those lines in the sources found.

**SAM growth rate**, blended by component weight within SAM:

### 3C Capacity cross-check

B07's own capex-embedded-growth estimate is **3.4%** — the growth rate the company's
actual, filed capital spending pattern would support. The SOM above implies
18.9-19.7%, more than five times that rate. Testing whether physical capacity could
produce the SOM revenue:

- Gross block ≈ Rs 62 Cr supports Rs 200.35 Cr of like-for-like revenue → asset turn
  ≈ 3.23x (step1 brief, AR FY26 p.104-105).
- To reach SOM Year 5 (Rs 475.5 Cr), incremental revenue needed = Rs 275.15 Cr.
  At the SAME asset turn, incremental PPE required = 275.15 / 3.23 ≈ **Rs 85.1 Cr**
  over 5 years, or ~Rs 17.0 Cr/year.
- FY26 actual PPE+CWIP additions were **Rs 7.40 Cr** — under half the implied need —
  and have FALLEN three years running: Rs 19.48 Cr (FY24) → Rs 9.37 Cr (FY25) →
  Rs 7.40 Cr (FY26) (AR FY24/FY25/FY26 Note 2(a)). 77% of the FY26 investing outflow
  went into a quoted-debt FVTPL portfolio, not plant (AR FY26 Note 5, p.127), while
  the MD&A framed the whole outflow as capacity building — a claim B07 already flags
  as contradicted.
- No capital-commitment note, named capex project, capacity figure, or commissioning
  date exists in any of the three annual reports (B07 input_gap).

**capacity_check: gap of approximately Rs 85 Cr** of cumulative incremental PPE
investment needed through the 5-year SOM horizon, against a FY26 run-rate of Rs 7.4
Cr/year that is falling, not rising, and mostly diverted to financial assets. **The
SOM is the optimistic side of this gap, not the capex plan.** No evidence exists that
capital is being deployed to physically produce the revenue this SOM assumes.

### 5B Runway assessment

- **Revenue headroom** = SAM ÷ current revenue = 4,338.5 / 200.35 = **21.65x**
  (like-for-like basis; 21.04x on reported Rs 206.19 Cr).
- **TAM/SAM growth rate** ≈ 9.0% blended.
- **Company CAGR vs TAM:** FY26 reported growth was 31.8% (28.06% like-for-like),
  well above the 9.0% market growth rate — Titan is nominally "gaining share" in
  FY26. But this is a single post-trough year (FY25 was the margin/revenue trough,
  step1 brief) inside a company scored EM NONE with no capacity evidence behind it;
  one strong year off a depressed base is not the same evidence as a proven,
  durable share-gain engine.
- **Years to saturate SAM:** at the FY26 like-for-like growth rate (28.06%), ~12.4
  years; at the SOM-implied blended rate (~19%), ~17.7 years. Either way, SAM does
  not saturate inside the 3-5 year strategy hold window.

### 5C Runway classification: **GOOD**

Not STRONG or MASSIVE, despite a headroom multiple (21.65x) that would normally
support a higher tier, because a large share of that headroom sits in
globally-scoped, geography-discounted product lines (peptone, ox bile) with weak,
widely-divergent market data and zero disclosed certification or named export
customer to support Titan actually capturing it. Not MODERATE or LIMITED, because the
best-evidenced single line (India culture media, corroborated by two independent
methods) is itself growing double-digit and Titan's current share in aggregate is
still under 5% of SAM. GOOD reflects real, sourced headroom with a material
evidence-quality discount on how much of it is realistically Titan's to take.
