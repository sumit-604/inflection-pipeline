### 6B Classification: CAUTION
Reasoning. CONCERN or AVOID would need a regulator action, a conviction, a pledge problem or proven misappropriation. None is in evidence. What is in evidence is a pattern: a promoter family on both sides of the company through Darpan (guarantor, lender, contractor, landlord, fee recipient, former 51% holder); related-party records that do not agree across the filings; a tax demand the governance filing describes as no dispute; a new promoter loan; and quick turnover of the compliance officer and two independents. These are disclosure and control gaps. Against them: no pledge, no SEBI or criminal matter against the promoters, modest pay, promoter money on the table (the CMD loan, guarantees from five family members), and a monitoring agency that confirms the IPO money paid down debt.
Second reading. A reader who weights the Darpan reconciliation gap and the restatement deal-breaker (6C) more heavily would class this as CONCERN. The observation that separates the two readings: a reconciled Darpan ledger (booking line, Board approval, a market benchmark for the guarantee fee) and the NSE filing on the s.115BAB rectification. Both could arrive with the FY26 annual report and the next half-year notes. Conservatism belongs in position size (Amendment 26.3), not in a shaded input.
