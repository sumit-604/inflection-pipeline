## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, before reading B05/B06)

Anchors use call = quarter, speaker, page of the annexed transcript.

### A. Asset quality / credit

1. **Up Money DA-pool default (central red flag).** A purchased *secured* Direct-Assignment pool bought from a Jalandhar-based NBFC "Up Money" (AUM INR500-600cr) defaulted, driving the GNPA spike. "this was a Jalandhar-based NBFC, Up Money, so it has an AUM of 500 to 600 crores and there we have done a secured DA transaction... that has got defaulted. So, that is why this NPA has shoot up." (Q3 Feb-26, Deepak Baid, p.18). Balance INR19cr; provisioning staggered — ~INR9cr taken in Q3, "still 8 crores more has to be done... In Q1 that will come" (Q3 p.18); by Q4 "balance of INR19 crores and we have made a provision of almost INR11 crores" (Q4 May-26, Deepak Baid, p.14). Said to have affected "almost 30, 35 lenders" (Q3 p.19). Surfaced only under analyst questioning (Rakesh Arora relaying Akash/Harsh), never proactively.

2. **Repeated broken promise that NPAs will fall — GNPA rose every single quarter.** Q1: "we can get our NPAs on a below 1% in future" (Aug-25, Deepak Baid, p.9). Q2: "this quarter and next quarter, we will be recovering it... good downturn towards NPA numbers" (Nov-25, p.4). Q3: "very soon, we should expect a collection." Actual: GNPA 1.28% (Jun-25, p.7) → 1.59% (Sep-25, p.3) → 2.4% (Dec-25, Go India intro slide, p.3) → 2.13% (Mar-26, Q4 Gopal, p.4). NNPA 0.67% → 0.84% → 1.4% → 1.09%. A promise restated as imminent in three consecutive calls and never delivered — a 2+ quarter repeated evasion.

3. **Low PCR against a rising, seasoning NPA book.** PCR held ~47.09% (Q1 p.7) → 47.22% (Q2 p.3) → ~49% (Q4). Net NPA rose to 1.4% (Dec-25). Rachna Mehta pressed on adequacy: "your PCR on Stage 3 assets appears relatively moderate at about 49%... how comfortable are you" (Q4 p.13); answer was an LTV assertion, no coverage-buffer commitment.

4. **Collection efficiency low and DECLINING.** 94.92% (Q1, Deepak, p.13) → 89% (Q3, Deepak, p.16). For a claimed 98% secured book this is weak and worsening; DRHP flagged ~40% cash recovery (Akash Jain, Q1 p.10), and Q3 confirms ~40% of collections still cash (Kuldeep, p.16).

### B. Earnings quality / disclosure integrity

5. **FY26 PAT and Q4 NIM materially inflated by a one-off DA gain-on-sale.** Q4 sold a INR41cr DA pool booking INR8.66cr upfront into NIM/revenue: "In Quarter 4, we have done a DA of around INR41 crores. So on the account of INR8 crores 66 lakhs are included in NIM" (Q4, Management, p.12). Disclosed only after Aryan Ajmera did the arithmetic (PAT up INR29.10→INR49.68cr, revenue INR226→INR320cr, but customers +4.8%). Opening remarks credited "better portfolio mix... stronger operating traction" for the NIM expansion (Gopal, p.3) — i.e., a one-off dressed as core. ~17% of FY26 PAT is this single gain.

6. **NIM restatement, unreconciled.** June-25 NIM shown as 9.66% in the Q1 presentation, then as 10.43% in the Q2 presentation. Anmol Das pressed twice; Deepak first mis-answered, drifted to "10.53 only for June '25," then "10.43 only... some misprint in the last PPT" with no reconciliation (Q2, p.7, p.10). Also an intra-Q1 inconsistency: Deepak cited NIM "9.99%" (p.4) while Gopal cited "9.66%" (p.7) in the same call.

7. **Disclosure regression / repeated deflection on granular asset quality.** Stage 2 was disclosed in Q1 but dropped from the Q2 deck — Jehan Bhadha had to ask for it ("in the last presentation we had disclosed the Stage 2 numbers also. But this time around, that is not there") and also requested 0-30/30-60/60-90 DPD buckets and segment-wise NPA (Q2, p.11); Deepak: "Well noted. We will take care next time." Same SMA-1/2 trend request repeated in Q3 (Akash via Rakesh, p.19). Never confirmed as added.

### C. Guidance credibility

8. **AUM-growth guidance walkback.** Q1 guided "35% to 40% growth... for this financial year" (Aug, Deepak, p.5). Q2, when Vignesh Iyer asked "are we still maintaining our guidance of 35%-40%," Kuldeep dodged: "we cannot quote any number, but definitely this growth is sustainable" (Nov, p.13); Deepak elsewhere cited "almost 32%." Actual FY26 AUM +27% to INR1,626cr (Q4 Gopal, p.3).

9. **PAT-growth miss.** Q1 "profit almost up by 50%" (p.5); FY26 PAT +38% (Q4, p.3) — and that 38% is itself propped by the INR8.66cr DA gain (core growth ~14%).

10. **Branch-plan slippage, never reconciled.** Q1 "35 to 44 branches" (p.9) → Q2 "29 branches" (p.10) → Q3 "20-23 branches" added (p.17) → actual +18 (159→176). Cut three times without acknowledging the original.

11. **Cost-of-borrowing miss.** Q1 "at least 100 to 125 bps" (p.5); actual FY26 -68bps (11.48%→10.80%, Q4 Piyush, p.10).

12. **Rating: two-notch promise, one-notch delivery.** Q1 "at least a two notch up" (p.5); delivered Acuite A-/Positive → A/Stable, one notch and outlook stepped from Positive to Stable (Q4 Gopal, p.4).

13. **ROE walk-down.** Q1 "return on equity is 15% to 15%" (p.13); Q4 walked to ">12%, 12.5% on a minimum side" (Piyush, p.16). FY26 ROE 13.7%.

### D. Business model / governance / optics

14. **Business-model contradiction: "100% own-sourced" vs DA-purchase.** Across Q1-Q3 management repeatedly claimed in-house sourcing / "our 100% MSME customers are onboarded directly by our own team" (Q3, p.9) and a "98% secured own book." Yet the DA-purchase model — buying loan books from other NBFCs where the *originator* retains collection ("we are purchasing from different NBFCs... and we are giving them rights of a collection," Q3, p.18) — means a slice of the book is neither self-originated nor self-collected. Up Money was exactly this. The moat claim and the model are in tension, and DA-purchase was only revealed reactively.

15. **Sequential disbursement drop vs "healthy disbursement" framing.** Anmol Das extracted that Q2 disbursement (INR145cr) fell sequentially from Q1 (~INR164-166cr); Deepak conceded "a little bit drop... due to little bit weather condition" (Q2, p.7-8) — after opening remarks touted "healthy disbursement." FY26 disbursement INR821cr (Q4, p.3).

16. **Format downgrade for the bad-news quarter.** The quarter in which the Up Money default surfaced (Q3, Dec-25) was held not as a standard earnings call but as a soft "Knowledge Session (Virtual Interaction)" moderated by IR advisor Go India Advisors, with no formal financial presentation. Optically convenient for the quarter carrying the DA blowup.

17. **Geographic concentration static despite multi-state narrative.** 82% AUM Rajasthan / 11% MP / 7% Gujarat (Q4, Management, p.11) — essentially unchanged from "80% mother state" in Q1 (p.5) despite adding UP and Maharashtra. Never framed by management as a risk.

18. **Aggressive terminal ambition.** INR5,000-6,000cr AUM in 3-5 years from ~INR1,346cr (Q1, p.13), reiterated in Q3/Q4 — a ~4x aspiration on a book whose asset quality was simultaneously deteriorating.

(Items 17-18 are documented but low-weight; the consolidated count below folds concentration into the caught set and treats the intra-Q1 numeric inconsistency in #6 as the one genuinely missed item.)
