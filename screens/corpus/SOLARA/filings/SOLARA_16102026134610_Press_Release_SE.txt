Base business led the overall growth, revenue up 33% YoY and 9% QoQ, EBITDA more than doubles and PAT remains
positive at INR 285 Mn
Sandeep Rao
Managing Director & CEO
“This is our best quarter in five years, and the improvement is 
coming from every part of the business.”
Our Base business continues to lead our growth. Revenue grew 37% to INR 3,273 Mn, staying above INR 
3,000 Mn for the third quarter in a row, and EBITDA grew 68% to INR 771 Mn, with the margin up about 
440 bps to 23.6% even as raw material costs rose.
Ibuprofen is turning around. Revenue grew 21% over last year and 18% over last quarter, and the 
business is now close to breakeven, from a loss margin of 21% just two quarters ago.
As a result, overall revenue grew 33% to INR 4,178 Mn and EBITDA more than doubled, up 118% to INR 
769 Mn. We are firmly back in profit, with PAT of INR 285 Mn against a loss of INR 101 Mn a year ago. 
Net debt to EBITDA is down to about 1.8x from 3.2x at the end of FY26, giving us room to keep investing 
in growth.
Sandeep Rao, Managing Director & CEO

The company began decoupling from this 
commodity exposure to focus on its higher-
