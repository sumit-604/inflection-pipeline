## HISTORICAL DEPRESSOR ASSESSMENT (per task instruction)

The depressors driving this AVOID score are investment-phase, not legacy
value-destruction. Revenue fell from FY23 (211.76 Cr) through FY25 (145.49
Cr) as the group wound down/repositioned older lines while it built out
Antara's Noida/Dehradun/Gurugram senior-living communities, launched the
Assisted Care (care-homes) network (AR 2024-25 p.29: ~300 beds by FY25,
expanding), and launched AGEasy (senior-care products). FY26 shows the
first inflection — revenue +31.0% (audited, 145.49→190.56 Cr) alongside a
narrowing EBITDA loss (-99.5 Cr FY25 → -83.1 Cr FY26, both independently
cross-checked against operator-relayed context). None of the Block A-C
depressors reflect a mature, previously-profitable business in decline;
they reflect a holding company mid-pivot, funding three new verticals
simultaneously out of rights-issue and warrant proceeds rather than
operating cash flow. This context does not change the mechanical score —
Gate 0 is backward-looking and blind to forward guidance — but it is the
reason this AVOID does not, per CLAUDE.md, halt the pipeline.
