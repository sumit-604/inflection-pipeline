## 3. VALUE TRAP TEST

The signed model classifies the ugly FY26 optic as ARTIFACT OF CLIMB, with a named
treadmill risk (web-dossier Sec 1; carried from B09b Part B5). Per the pipeline
override I carry that verdict INTO this test and contest it in the open: the case
that the ugly optic is a permanent STRUCTURAL-FEATURE, not a temporary artifact.

**The STRUCTURAL-FEATURE argument (contesting ARTIFACT OF CLIMB).**

1. The lower return is arithmetic, not a phase. Capital employed rose a third and is
   permanent (Rs 12,123m to Rs 16,141m combined). Restoring peak profit restores
   20.3%, not 25.2% (11-valuation 2.3). The "recovery" ceiling is a lower plateau by
   construction. That is the definition of a structural feature, not a climb.
2. The spread is thin even in the good case. 16.6% post-tax ROCE against ~13% cost of
   equity is ~3 points (web-dossier Sec 1). A forced move (L1 bid, IBA committee,
   NECESSITY EXECUTED FROM STRENGTH) accepts "whatever the market allows," so
   destination returns need not match origin returns (web-dossier Sec 1). A 3-point
   spread on a book bid at L1 is a permanent low-return feature.
3. The optic is partly an accounting choice, not a build. The ATM useful life was
   extended 7 to 10 years effective 1-Jan-2026, a 43% increase, in the SAME year the
   machine fleet grew 76% (web-dossier Sec 6). It lowered D&A ~Rs 48m and flatters
   EBIT (11-valuation 1A). The "recovery" the bull awaits is partly manufactured by
   the depreciation-life change. If the machines last seven years, the returns on this
   book are materially worse than reported and the contracts were bid too cheaply
   (web-dossier Sec 6).
4. The build is not finished. CWIP Rs 1,134.71m at year end with Rs 2,809.83m added in
   FY26, against FY24 CWIP Rs 147.30m; FY26 capex overshot its own Nov-2025 guide by
   ~17% (web-dossier Sec 1). "Capex drops next year" is exactly what a treadmill says
   every year.

**Verdict on the value-trap test.** ARTIFACT OF CLIMB survives as the more likely
reading, but weakly, and the STRUCTURAL-FEATURE case is live enough to reprice the
Transition Decision Matrix cell. The signed posture is proof NOT fired + ARTIFACT +
gap OPEN -> RESEARCH / WATCH (11-valuation 4.3). Flip ARTIFACT to STRUCTURAL and the
name slides toward proof NOT fired + STRUCTURAL -> VALUE-TRAP RISK. The tension the
model itself underwrites is the tell: management says the segments are "not
separable," yet the whole thesis is that revenue is climbing from one segment to a
higher-return one (web-dossier Sec 1). Both cannot be strongly true. If the business
is one integrated thing, there is no ladder, only a company that got less profitable
in FY26 and may or may not get more profitable again.

**Melting ice cube check.** The units base is NOT melting: national ATMs +1.3% YoY
(web-dossier V1). The VALUE base is melting: withdrawal value -7.2% and accelerating
(web-dossier V1). Fixed fees break the value link on the NEW book only. On the old
transaction-linked exposure, the ice is still melting, which brings us to Section 4.

## 5. CATALYST DEPENDENCY — THE SHARED CATALYST (single point of failure)

The Section 1B SHARED CATALYST flag is YES (fttcp-deliberation Sec 4): the FY28 ROCE
recovery in Pillar 1 AND any future growth premium in Pillar 3 both ride the SAME
capex-fill event. This is one event carrying two pillars.

**What the implicit catalyst is.** Machines already deployed fill up, capex falls to
the Rs 100-125 Cr guide, contracted rentals (Rs 8,126m, +117.6%, web-dossier S7)
arrive, and FY28 consolidated post-tax ROCE crosses 22% on flat capital
(fttcp-deliberation Sec 1). Everything downstream, the re-rating to 17x, the forward
Pillar 1 uplift, the eventual growth premium, waits on that one print.

**Stress-test 1: the catalyst is delayed 2-3 years.** This is not a hypothetical for
a management with a three-year miss streak and three dropped disclosures (web-dossier
Sec 4). Per the pipeline override, on managements with timeline-miss records slippage
is the BASE case for dates. The FY28 ROCE proof gate is the de-facto resolution date
that carries the exit re-rating. Ask the mandatory question: which resolution date
carries the exit, and what does the exit become if it slips one year?

- The exit price is 17.0x x FY30E EPS 31.5 = Rs 535 (11-valuation 3.1). The 17.0x is
  a re-rating from the current 11.35x forward (11-valuation 4.3). That re-rating
  requires the gate to print AND the market to grant the multiple.
- If FY28 slips to FY29 (base case on this record), the market will not award 17x by
  the August 2029 exit; it holds nearer the 11-13x entry multiple. Illustrative slip
  exit (INFERENCE): ~12x x a weighted-down FY30E ~Rs 30 + Rs 19 div ~= Rs 379,
  ~16% CAGR from Rs 243, below the 25% hurdle and below the Rs 284 entry math.
- What happens to the price while I wait: it sits de-rated at 11-13x forward, and
  every missed year moves the name toward the PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP) cell of the
  Transition Decision Matrix, where the downside is double (transition fails AND
  multiple compresses).

**Stress-test 2: the catalyst never comes (Case C, 20%).** ROCE stuck near 15.8%,
the transition falsifier fires (11-valuation 2.4). The re-rating engine is not
delayed; it is dead. The 17.0x destination was always contingent on a proof that had
not fired; if it fails, the fair value collapses to the bear column (Rs 368,
11-valuation 4.1).

Single point of failure, confirmed. One number, the FY28 ROCE print, carries both
pillars, the re-rating, and roughly half the total return. The thesis is right to gate
the position on it. The devil's addition: the base rate for that print landing ON TIME
is worse than the thesis models, because the probability weights (Case A 40%,
11-valuation 2.4) do not price the manager's own timeline-miss record into the DATE,
only into the level.

## 7. PRE-MORTEM

It is August 2028. The stock is Rs 170, down ~40% from a Rs 284 entry. Here is the
most likely path.

FY27 services revenue came in at the low end of the cut guide, and FY28 opened soft.
The signed contracts ramped, but the withdrawal-value pond kept falling ~7% a year
(web-dossier V1), so the share gains landed on a shrinking money base and net services
growth stayed high-single-digit, not the low-teens the thesis needed. The FY28 ROCE
printed ~20%, a MISS against the 22% gate (Case B, 40% weight, 11-valuation 2.4),
because capex did not fall to guide: every new HDFC and ICICI machine needed new steel,
the treadmill the model named (web-dossier Sec 1). Capital kept rising instead of going
flat.

The receivables block that was "industry-event-induced" in 2026 did not clear. The
released loss allowance (cover 0.29x, web-dossier Sec 1 var 4) proved too thin; the
Rs 58 Cr distressed customer loan (fttcp-draft 2.3) was joined by a second name, and
CMS took a provision worth a quarter of profit. The cash flag hardened from
INDETERMINATE to structural, which is a thesis-broken condition (B14 thesis_broken_if).

With the proof gate missed and the cash flag structural, the market did the obvious
thing: it did not re-rate CMS from 11x to 17x. It held it at 11x on lower earnings.
Half the modelled return was re-rating (11-valuation 4.4); that half never arrived,
and the earnings half came in below plan. The transaction-linked BLA book, still
~12-14% of revenue and never explained (role1-handover Sec 10), kept clipping EBIT.
The clean balance sheet limited the loss, which is why the stock is Rs 170 and not
Rs 100. But the thesis, that CMS was a re-rating story gated on one print, was wrong on
the one print.
