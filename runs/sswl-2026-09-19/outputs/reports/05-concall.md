### 1C Trigger evolution

- **EBITDA/wheel** — strengthening every quarter: Rs242 (Q2 FY26) → Rs260 (Q3
  FY26) → Rs282 (Q4 FY26 exit) → Rs314 (Q1 FY27). The single most consistently
  delivered thread across all four calls.
- **Export recovery** — weakening through Q2/Q3 FY26 (US tariff hit, Rs300-400cr
  annualised loss cited Q3 FY26), then strengthening from Q4 FY26 as the
  "level playing field" language appears, but Q1 FY27 actuals (Rs127cr) still
  trail management's own framing of recovery. Timeline kept slipping (see
  timeline_slippages).
- **Steel wheel capacity plan** — DROPPED/DOWNSIZED without proactive
  disclosure. The 205→270 lakh plan referenced from an earlier investor
  presentation was curtailed to ~210 lakh by Q3 FY26 (AMW capacity scrapped,
  equipment redeployed to Jamshedpur/Chennai/Chandigarh); surfaced only
  because an analyst (Anand Kulkarni) compared decks.
- **Steel wheel segment narrative** — REVERSED. Q2 FY26: steel described as "a
  sunset industry" with alloy displacing it (Mohan Joshi). Q1 FY27: "not only
  is aluminium growing, but also steel has started to grow" post-GST cuts,
  with a new Rs150cr brownfield line to capture it (Dheeraj Garg). Worth a
  question of why this reversed and whether it is durable or a GST-cut sugar
  high.
- **Steel-vs-alloy EBITDA/wheel split** — a trigger management NEVER delivers:
  asked Q2 FY26, Q3 FY26 and Q1 FY27, stonewalled every time (see 2E).
- **European wheel-maker M&A option** — appeared once (Q2 FY26, in answer to a
  question about a prior MD comment), described as opportunistic only "at a
  very deep discount," and never mentioned again in Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26 or Q1
  FY27. A quietly DROPPED trigger.
- **Hot stamping** — a NEW trigger, first appearing in Q1 FY27 only, no
  numbers attached. Worth watching whether it reappears with specifics or
  disappears like the M&A option.
- **EV-scooter dominance** — strengthening narrative (Q4 FY26 introduces an
  ~80% share claim) but never quantified in revenue terms in any call,
  including Q1 FY27 where it is not mentioned again at all.

### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

Classified **external-blame-heavy**, with real balancing accountability
evidence:
- US tariffs are invoked as the explanation for every export/margin miss in
  Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 calls — a legitimate, publicly verifiable
  external event, but also the single most repeated excuse in the entire
  corpus.
- The Q1 FY27 manpower shortage (Rs80cr lost revenue) is framed as a national
  labour-market issue ("everyone knows about this") rather than SSWL's own
  hiring/planning failure, even though it resolved within the company's
  control by 20-May-2026.
- Against this: Dheeraj Garg owns the Q2 FY26 quarter candidly and
  repeatedly — "the second quarter destroyed our profitability" is referenced
  unprompted in three subsequent calls (Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26, Q1 FY27), a genuine
  "we had a bad quarter" admission rather than a cover-up.
- When pressed on the FY22 PAT anomaly (Shashank Kanodia, Q3 FY26), Dheeraj
  gives a specific, checkable explanation (one-time Rs60cr steel-appreciation
  gain) rather than deflecting.
- On the MHCV market-share discrepancy (Vikas Sharda, Q3 FY26), the initial
  response disputes the analyst's data ("I do not know why this projection
  has come to you"), then concedes "it could be an error… I will
  double-check" once the analyst cites the source page — honest-admission
  under pressure, but never followed up publicly.
- Hard topics (steel wheel languishing, PAT stagnation, capex ROI) are mostly
  raised by analysts, not volunteered by management; management does not
  proactively flag the cash-conversion decline, the Customs notice, or AMW's
  continuing losses in any call (see 2D).

### 2D What they are NOT saying

- **FY26 cash-conversion collapse** (CFO/EBITDA ~63-65%, per B01/B03) — never
  raised by management or any analyst in any of the 4 transcripts, including
  the Q2 FY26 backward check. Analysts asked about debt levels and factoring
  cost but never framed it as a cash-quality concern.
- **17-Jun-2026 Customs show-cause notice** (Mundra) — absent from the Q1 FY27
  call (16-Jul-2026), a month after the notice, despite the AR's own
  omission of it (B03 FLAG-MISSING-DISCLOSURE). Likely reason for silence:
  materiality judgment call by management/counsel, or simple omission given
  no analyst asked.
- **AMW Autocomponent Ltd's financial state** — discussed only as a capex/land
  asset-reuse story ("saving Rs100cr," "stopping the Chinese from taking
  over this asset"); its three consecutive loss years, growing DTA, and
  adverse CARO land-title finding (B02/B03) are never mentioned in any
  concall.
- **Reverse-factoring / Supplier Finance Arrangement scale** — one analyst
  (Aditya Jhavar, Q1 FY27) surfaced the Rs400-500cr factoring-limit figure
  when asking why reported interest cost exceeded the stated 8-9% average
  cost of debt; management confirmed the range but did not volunteer the
  67.6%-of-payables scale (B02 finding) or its effect on reported CFO.
- **Equity dilution** — Aditya Jhavar (Q1 FY27) explicitly suggested "lighten
  the balance sheet by doing some equity"; management's entire response was
  "Okay." A real, visible silence on capital structure.
- **Product-level segment margin** — withheld across all 4 quarters (2E).

### 3B Industry and market intelligence

- GST 2.0 reform (effective ~Oct 2025) cited in every one of the 4 calls as
  the primary demand driver across PV, CV, tractor and 2W/3W — a broad,
  repeated claim worth checking against peer concalls for whether it is
  industry-wide or company-specific execution.
- Aluminium price trajectory: ~Rs225/kg → ~Rs265-270/kg (H1 FY26, Q2 FY26
  call) → "up ~20% more, another ~10% expected" (Q4 FY26 call) → a Rs150cr
  quarterly cost-pressure event attributed to the "West Asia crisis" (Q1
  FY27 call).
- CV industry growth "16% YoY for nine months" (9M FY26) cited by Dheeraj
  Garg (Q3 FY26 call) without a named source.
- Steel wheel industry narrative reversal (see 1C) — from "sunset industry"
  (Q2 FY26) to renewed growth (Q1 FY27), attributed to GST cuts plus two
  years of OEM price renegotiation finally landing.
- Raw-material pass-through mechanics: aluminium settlement compressed from
  quarterly to monthly, "back-to-back neutral" (Q1 FY27, in response to the
  West Asia commodity spike) — a structural claim worth checking against
  peer disclosure of their own pass-through terms.
