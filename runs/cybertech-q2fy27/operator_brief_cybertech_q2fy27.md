## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Cybertech sells cloud and IT services built on Esri ArcGIS (mapping software) and SAP (business software). Almost all of its revenue (99%) comes from the US.
2. Revenue grew 24.7% from a year ago, to Rs 73.30 Cr in the quarter.
3. Profit after tax did not grow. It stayed at Rs 8.68 Cr, up 0.3%, because interest and investment income fell by Rs 3.21 Cr.
4. The core business did much better. Profit before tax without other income nearly doubled, from Rs 3.46 Cr to Rs 6.81 Cr.
5. The gain sits in the US subsidiaries. Their margin jumped from about 2.5% to 11.2% in one quarter. The Indian parent was flat.
6. The company's own EBITDA counts other income, so its headline margin fell from 18.8% to 16.4%. The operating margin rose from 7.86% to 11.04%.
7. The CEO says demand rose and delivery now mixes own engineers, partners and AI tools. He gives no numbers and no targets for FY27 or FY28.
8. One client gives 55% of revenue and the top three give 68%. The deck does not say over what period. Losing that client would hurt badly.
9. Government work gave about 72% of the quarter's revenue gain. Technology clients, the largest group at 68%, grew only 1.1%.
10. A weaker rupee may have helped. The rupee translation gain was Rs 7.47 Cr over the year to September. Dollar revenue is not disclosed.
11. Two large items have no note. The parent sent Rs 41.13 Cr to an unnamed subsidiary. Equity shows a Rs 12.15 Cr debit, exactly Rs 4.00 a share, likely an unpaid dividend. Neither document mentions the buyback or the subsidiary funding.
12. Watch next quarter: core profit before tax. Rs 6.81 Cr or more means the step holds. Rs 4.43 Cr or less means it was a one-off. Results are due by 14-Feb-2027.
