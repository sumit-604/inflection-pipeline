## PASS 3 — PATTERN RE-READ FINDINGS

Reading the Notes one final time against the DIFFERENT lens of contradiction-hunting and
cross-statement number matching (rather than sequential note-by-note reading), the following
material new findings emerged. NO MANUFACTURING GUARD assessed: this pass is NOT empty —

financial statements, which Pass 1/2 (scoped to Notes 1-49) had not done systematically.

### P3-1. Cash Flow Statement: short-term "Working Capital Loan" draws are classified as an
OPERATING activity working-capital adjustment, not a financing activity — this materially
inflates reported operating cash flow in the higher-growth years and directly explains the
apparent CFO/WC tension flagged at Stage 1.
The Restated Consolidated Cash Flow Statement (p.244/raw 249) lists, under "Working capital
adjustments" (i.e. inside Operating Activities, above "Cash generated from/(used in)
operations"): a line item "(Increase)/Decrease in Working Capital Loan (WCL)" with values FY22
**(₹154.82M)** [use of cash] → FY23 **₹443.13M** → FY24 **₹2,070.62M** → H1FY25 **₹1,498.68M**
[sources of cash]. This is the single largest working-capital-adjustment line in the statement
in FY24 and H1FY25 — larger in magnitude than the inventory build that consumed cash in the same
periods (Inventories: FY24 use of ₹1,792.67M). Financing Activities (same page) separately shows
only "Proceeds from Long term Borrowings" / "Repayment of Long term Borrowings" and lease
payments — i.e. the ₹3,096.33M→₹4,086.20M current/working-capital borrowings book (Note 19) is
being treated as an operating working-capital item, not as debt draw-down, even though
economically it is short-term secured bank/NBFC borrowing (20+ lenders, Note 19.1). Reported net
CFO: FY22 **(₹38.26M)** [negative, confirms Stage 1's cash flag] → FY23 ₹249.59M → FY24 ₹974.57M
→ H1FY25 ₹199.56M. Stripping the WCL line out of the FY24 calculation alone would swing "cash
generated from operations" (₹1,024.75M as reported) sharply negative, since the WCL inflow
(₹2,070.62M) exceeds the reported operating cash figure itself. 🔴 Red Flag — this is a
non-standard classification that makes reported CFO structurally dependent on continued
working-capital debt growth; it should be treated as a going-forward risk (if lenders pull back
on the 20+-facility book, both the balance sheet AND the cash flow statement deteriorate
simultaneously) and is the most important new finding of this pass, feeding directly into
FLAG-CASH.
(Restated Consolidated Cash Flow Statement, p.244/raw 249, cross-read against Note 19/19.1
pp.269-271)

### P3-2. Statement of Changes in Equity: FY24 "Profit for the Period" in the retained-earnings
roll-forward (₹520.96M) does not match the FY24 "Restated Net Profit Attributable to Owners of
the Company" on the face of the P&L (₹521.38M) — a ₹0.42M unexplained gap, exactly equal in
magnitude to that year's remeasurement-gains-on-defined-benefit-plans OCI line shown separately
in the same statement. FY22 shows a smaller (₹0.01M) rounding-level gap (217.14 vs 217.13); FY23
and H1FY25 tie out exactly (323.28 vs 323.28; 351.00 vs 351.00). 🟡 Watch — immaterial in rupee
terms but is exactly the kind of "notes don't match main financial statements" pattern this pass
is designed to surface; adds a third minor drafting/reconciliation inconsistency (after Note 45
and Note 36) in FY24-adjacent statutory tables.
(Statement of Changes in Equity, p.246/raw 251, cross-read against Restated Consolidated
Statement of P&L, p.243/raw 248)

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management / cash-flow presentation**: Working Capital Loan draws classified as operating cash flow rather than financing (P3-1) — the single most significant accounting-quality issue found across all three passes; it does not change reported PAT but materially changes the interpretability of reported operating cash generation.
- **Aggressive/incomplete provisioning**: no warranty provision carried despite a stated warranty value proposition; no ECL allowance quantified despite a stated always-recognise-lifetime-ECL policy; "funded" gratuity plan 0-50% actually funded.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators**: an unidentified customer representing 17.28% of FY24 revenue; a description-only FX hedging policy with zero quantified hedge disclosure against the largest disclosed risk category; an MSME payables disclosure gap for three of four balance sheet dates ("could not be furnished," not a confirmed zero).
- **Related-party entanglement beyond arm's length evidencing**: Kay Kay Overseas Corporation, Amiable Electronics Private Limited, Amit Midha and Electronics Bazar Inc (USA) collectively span trading (both directions), lending, prepayment and lender-security roles with no pricing/comparable evidence disclosed for any of it.
- **Note-drafting/data-integrity failures**: Note 45 equity reconciliation (static figure, nil adjustments across four periods) and Note 36 lease-liability roll-forward (arithmetic error producing a negative closing balance) are two independent, unrelated arithmetic/template errors in statutory notes within the same filing — a pattern worth naming explicitly as a control-quality signal, separate from any single item's own materiality.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal concerning accounting practices. Key concern: working-capital loan draws are
classified as operating cash flow rather than financing, structurally flattering reported CFO
against a backdrop of monotonically deteriorating leverage (Debt-Equity 0.92x to 2.46x) and DSCR
(0.43x to 0.12x, never above 1.0x), compounded by extensive undisclosed and related-party
counterparty entanglement (an unidentified 17.28%-of-FY24-revenue customer; a ₹585.62M on-demand
related-party loan appearing at the last pre-DRHP balance sheet date; multiple related parties
spanning trading, lending and security roles) and two independent note-drafting arithmetic
errors. Key strength: expense capitalisation is transparent and explicable (leasing-asset capex
ties cleanly to a genuine new revenue line), the primary financial statements otherwise tie out
cleanly to the notes on direct cross-check, and no going-concern language or qualification
appears anywhere in the document. Overall accounting quality: 3/10.
