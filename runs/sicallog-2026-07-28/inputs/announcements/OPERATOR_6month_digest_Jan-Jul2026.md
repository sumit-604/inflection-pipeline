## 2. FY26 performance / business update (29-May-2026 press release + FY26 deck)
- Consolidated revenue from operations Rs 3,857 mn FY26 vs Rs 2,218 mn FY25
  (+73.9%). Consolidated EBITDA Rs 783 mn vs Rs 215 mn (+263.7%); EBITDA margin
  9.7% -> 20.3%.
- Segments FY26: Mining Logistics Rs 1,648 mn (up ~310% YoY, 43% of revenue);
  Terminals (CFS + MMLP) Rs 1,399 mn (36%); Warehousing & 3PL Rs 815 mn (21%,
  vs Rs 620 mn FY25).
- Q4 FY26: secured overburden-removal order Rs 34,222 mn (~Rs 3,422 cr) from
  South Eastern Coalfields Ltd (SECL), Porda-Chimtapani OCP, Raigarh,
  Chhattisgarh, ~11-year (~140 month) execution.
- Mar-2026: Chennai CFS highest-ever monthly volumes (subsidiary SMART — Sical
  Multimodal and Rail Transport Ltd).
- Rights issue Rs 930.3 mn (11:5) for MPS compliance; proceeds to debt repayment
  + WC. With non-core asset sales, debt/equity cut 4.1x (FY25) -> 1.6x (FY26).
- Balance sheet/cash (consol): total assets Rs 9,073 mn (Mar-2026); net operating
  cash flow Rs 523 mn FY26 (vs Rs 200 mn FY25); year-end cash Rs 313 mn.
- Strategy: expand overburden removal (coal mining logistics); rail-linked
  terminals across South India; grow CFS/MMLP, warehousing, 3PL.

## FY26 deck operational colour
- Mining Logistics order book (as of 1-Apr-2026): Rs 46,000 mn (~Rs 4,600 cr).
  Projects: Nigahi OCP, Bharatpur, Amlohri, Lajkura, Samleshwari, Jhingurda, and
  new SECL Porda-Chimtapani (Rs 34,222 mn, ~140 months). Capital-efficient
  service model, limited incremental capex, better ROCE.
- CFS: Chennai / Tuticorin / Vizag. FY26 volumes: Chennai 148,192 TEUs (+14%,
  ~55 acres, ~2,08,000 sq ft warehouse, largest in region ~10%+ share, top
  southern CFS per NICDC FY23-FY26); Tuticorin 27,492 TEUs (slight decline,
  ~16.6 acres); Vizag 24,362 TEUs (+31%).
- MMLP: Chennai MMLP commercial ops commenced Dec-2025 (first private rail-linked
  logistics park in Chennai; 82-acre near Ponneri; 50-70 container rakes/month +
  non-container). Bengaluru MMLP upcoming (~65+ acre, Kacharakanahalli).
- Warehousing & 3PL: 20 warehouses, >1.2 mn sq ft (incl. temperature-controlled),
  20,000+ SKUs/month, 1,400+ outbound vehicles/month. Entered pharma "national
  super stockist" model.
