Press Release 
 
Onward Technologies Delivers its Strongest-ever Quarter; Total Revenue up 
22.2% and EBITDA up 33.7% YoY; Raises FY27 Outlook 
EBITDA margin expands to 15.6% and PAT up 41.2% YoY; FY27 outlook revised upward to 15–18% revenue 
growth and 13.8–14.3% EBITDA margin 
Mumbai, India – October 16, 2026: Onward Technologies Limited (BSE: 517536; NSE: ONWARDTEC), a pure-
play digital engineering and ER&D services partner, announced its financial results for the second quarter 
and half year ended September 30, 2026.  
The Company delivered its best performance this quarter in Q2 FY27, with total revenue of ₹171.2 crore, 
reflecting YoY growth of 22.2%.  EBITDA stood at ₹26.6 crore, up 33.7% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 15.6%. 
The improved performance reflects progress in operational efficiency, offshore execution and strategic 
customer engagements, supported by a healthy pipeline of opportunities across its key engineering verticals.  
Building on its Q2 performance, H1 execution and business visibility for the second half of the year, the 
Company now upgrades its FY27 revenue growth of 15%-18% and EBITDA margin at 13.8%-14.3%

Key Business Highlights 
• Strategic Account Expansion: Secured a multi-year contract renewal valued at ₹44.46 crore  
• Data centre Engineering: The Company continues to develop opportunities in engineering solutions for 
AI-driven data centre infrastructure, including power management, cooling and electrical systems. 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Jigar Mehta, Managing Director, Onward Technologies, said: 
“Q2 FY27 represents a significant step forward in both scale and profitability for Onward Technologies. Our 
performance was driven by deeper engagement with strategic customers, improved utilization, higher 
productivity, operating leverage, and disciplined offshore execution. We are also seeing encouraging traction 
across Digital Engineering, AI-led transformation initiatives across our existing customer base in USA and 
Europe. 
Our strong Q2 performance, coupled with a healthy first half and strong business visibility, reinforces our 
confidence in delivering sustainable growth over the next three years. During H1 FY27, we added 432 net new 
employees, taking our global workforce to 2,917 professionals, strengthening our ability to support larger and 
more strategic customer engagements globally. 
As we enter the second half of FY27, our focus remains on delivery-led growth, improving revenue productivity, 
expanding wallet share within existing accounts, and driving operational excellence through greater 
automation, AI adoption, and offshore leverage. We remain committed to creating long-term value for our 
customers, employees, and shareholders through disciplined execution and profitable growth.” 
Financial Strength and Shareholder Returns 
• The Company maintained a healthy debt free balance sheet, with net cash of ₹97.1 crore as of 
September 30, 2026. Onward Technologies has paid dividends for 11 consecutive years, reflecting its 
continued focus on financial discipline and shareholder returns. 
About Onward Technologies Limited 
Onward Technologies Limited (BSE: 517536 | NSE: ONWARDTEC) is a pure-play digital engineering and 
engineering research & development (ER&D) services company serving global clients. Headquartered in 
Mumbai, India, the Company works closely with customers across North America and Europe, supporting 
product development and engineering initiatives across sectors such as Industrial Equipment, Off-Highway, 
Energy, Data Centers, Automotive, Rail Transportation, Healthcare and Life Sciences. With over 2,900 
professionals, Onward Technologies operates through its 12 global offices and India-based delivery centers, 
providing scalable offshore delivery, domain expertise and integrated engineering solutions to its customers. 
More information at www.onwardgroup.com
