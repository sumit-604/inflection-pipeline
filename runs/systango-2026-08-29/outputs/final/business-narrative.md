Debt free software boutique with credibility gap

Systango is an offshore digital engineering boutique listed on NSE Emerge since March 2023 and controlled by the Rathi family. It sells AI and ML work, cloud services, custom app development, and blockchain and Web3 projects. Customers sit mostly in the UK, 66% of FY26 revenue, and the US, 28%. FY26 revenue was Rs 90 Cr, EBITDA margin 37.6%, and the company carries zero bank debt. PAT has been positive every year since FY20.

The company earns mostly by billing person time. Staff augmentation and time and material projects lead, with managed contracts and a thin slice of IP licensing behind them. Revenue per employee nearly doubled, from Rs 16.8 L in FY23 to Rs 29.4 L in FY26, while headcount stayed near flat around 285 to 305. That is the cleanest sign of mix driven quality growth in the file. Client concentration is high: the top 3 clients were 42% of FY26 revenue, the top 10 were 72%.

The transition on offer is a climb into higher value AI and GenAI work backed by hyperscaler tie ups, including a stated Alibaba Cloud alliance and AWS and Google partner claims. The emerging moat scan scored 10 against a floor of 12, classification NONE. No new moat is confirmed. Backward quality is real: Gate 0 core 88, four historical moats, STRONG class. No forward catalyst layers on top of it. This is quality without a proven transition, not a transition setup.

Gate 0 posture is GOOD+. Core score 88 of 100, moat 19 of 60, grand total 107 of 160. The classification sits one tier below the raw score, marked down only on the limited history band, four audited years and a 2023 SME listing. ROCE runs 27% to 33%, there is no down year, and the balance sheet holds zero debt. On the surface the financial picture is clean.

Cash is where the surface cracks. CFO to PAT sat below 0.7x in all four standalone and consolidated cells across FY24 and FY25. Standalone receivables grew 76.3% against revenue growth of 16.4%. Receivable turnover fell from 7.81x to 4.90x, DSO widened from about 47 days to about 75 days, and a new 1 to 2 year ageing bucket appeared from zero. The strain concentrates at the parent: consolidated operating cash flow grew in line with profit. A large intercompany receivable, Systango Inc USA at Rs 353 L, and re routed related party billing sit inside the number.

Management credibility grades D. The FY26 revenue target of Rs 250 Cr or 25 million dollars was delivered at Rs 90.4 Cr, a 64% miss. FY24 growth, guided at about 30% after a cut from about 60%, came in near 8%. Only the EBITDA margin promise above 25% held, at 37.6%. A crypto derivative platform pitched in July 2023 as three months from launch never appeared again. Against that, every hard financial figure in the founders letter reconciles exactly to the audited notes.

Peers both support and contradict Systango's framing. INFOBEAN corroborates the broad FY23 to FY24 IT slowdown and blockchain demand softness. KSOLVES contradicts the claim that receivables stress is industry wide, holding debtors under 60 days as a stated discipline. ONWARDTEC contradicts the implied view that competitor evasiveness is normal, naming L&T Technology Services, KPIT, Cyient, and Tata Technologies directly when asked.

Market runway grades MASSIVE, but only on the market ceiling: the SAM is about 5,454x current revenue. That is headroom, not a forecast. The execution bounded SOM uses a 10% CAGR, half the historical 20%, an explicit haircut for the D grade credibility. The binding constraint is hiring pace, not capital. The 3 year SOM implies about 409 technical staff against 285 today.
