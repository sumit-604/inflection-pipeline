 Detailed Rationale 

 Crisil Ratings has assigned its   ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’   rating s to the bank facilit ies of   Optiemus Infracom Ltd   (Optiemus ; part of the Optiemus group ). 

 The rating s reflect the healthy business risk profile of Optiemus and its subsidiaries - Optiemus Electronics Ltd (OEL) and GDN Entreprises Pvt Ltd (GDN), which are the manufacturing arms, and Bharat Innovative Glass Technologies Pvt Ltd (BIGTECH), a greenfield project company - collectively referred to as the Optiemus group. The grou p has seen continuous improvement in scale and operating profile over the past few years , supported by its transition towards value-added manufacturing across the electronics manufacturing services (EMS) and telecom equipment segments. It has a comfortable financial risk profile backed by improving networth , moderate leverage, and prudent funding structure. 

 The ratings also factor in the group’s entry into cover glass manufacturing through BIGTECH, a greenfield project which Optiemus is undertak ing in 70: 30 joint venture (JV) with Corning Inc ( one of the leading manufacturers of cover glass (Gorilla Glass) for electronics devices, globally ) . The project represents a strategic entry into a high-value, technology-intensive segment, supported by favourable industry tailwinds such as increasing localisation of electronics manufacturing in India and import substitution opportunity, given the current dependence on imports for cover glass. The project benefits from strong technical and operational support from Cor ning Inc, including access to proven technology, raw material sourcing, and established customer linkages, thereby mitigating technology and execution risks. The project entails capital expenditure ( capex ) of ~ Rs 550 crore, with a significant portion already incurred and construction largely completed. Commercial operations are expected to commence from the second half of fiscal 2027. While ramp-up and stabilisation of operations, including achievement of envisaged utilisation levels and firming up of offta ke arrangements, remain monitorable, the project risk is mitigated by its advanced stage of implementation, strong partner support, and favourable demand outlook , along with state and central government support under capital incentive schemes. 

 The group’s operating income rose at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of ~80% between fiscals 2025 from 2021 , driven by significant scale-up in both distribution and electronics manufacturing. The growth was supported by steady shift towards value-added manufact uring under the EMS segment , increasing integration with key customer s, and expansion across product segment s .   Revenue in fiscal 2025 was ~Rs. 1,894 crore. While the fiscal 2025 revenue was elevated due to execution of a large EMS contract and is considered a one-off high base, consolidated revenue of 9MFY26 was Rs. 1,291 crore reflecting a more sustainable run-rate supported by steady order inflow and continued traction across product segments. Over the medium term, revenue is expected to scale up to over Rs 2,000 crore , supported by growth across segments of the group and incremental contribution from the greenfield project in BIGTECH. 

 The operating profitability has shown steady improvement to ~6.1% in fiscal 2025 and is expected over 8% over the medium term. The improvement has been driven by a structural shift in the business mix from fiscal 2024, towards higher-margin manufacturing operations and the transition of the EMS business to a job-work model, which has led to better cost visibility, lower pas s-through revenue, and reduced working capital intensity. Additionally, the upcoming BIGTECH business is expected to provide incremental support to consolidated profitability as utilisation ramps up. 

 The group’s financial risk profile remains comfortable, supported by a strengthening networth , moderate leverage, and a prudent funding structure. Networth has improved significantly, aided by equity infusion (via warrant issue) of ~Rs 200 crore from the promoters, marquee investors and related parties over th e past two fiscals , with an additional ~Rs 90 crore expected over the medium term, providing further balance sheet support. T otal debt is expected to increase to Rs 400–450 crore over fiscals 20 27 and 20 28 (from ~Rs 129 crore as on March 31, 20 25) to fund the BIGTECH and other current project. Nonetheless, gearing is expected to remain low at 0.4–0.5 time and d ebt protection metrics are likely to remain adequate, with interest coverage expected above 4 times and net cash accrual to adjusted debt projected a t a comfortable level, supported by stable operating performance and improving accrual. The debt structure remains favourable, with a moratorium period and back-ended repayment schedule aligned with the expected cash flow ramp-up from the BIGTECH project.

 Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

 E xtensive experience of the promoter s, diversified operations and established and diversified customer profile 

 The Optiemus g roup benefits from the promoters’ experience of over three decades in the electronics distribution segment, with extensive expertise in trading and mobile handset distribution. Over time, the business has transitioned from a distribution-led model to an integrated structure with presence across EMS, including assembly of wearables, hearables, networking and other IT-related products. 

 The promoter s are actively involved in operations, supported by the next generation, which manages execution across entities and drives new business initiatives. The g roup has strengthened its capabilities in recent years through partnerships and technical collaborations, supporting project execution and client engagement in new business verticals. 

 T he g roup has built a diversified customer base comprising established players in the consumer electronics and technology segment. The Optiemus group caters to various original equipment manufacturers ( OEMs ) for manufacturing and assembly of products such as smartwatc hes, laptops, tablets, hearables, and networking equipment. Its clientele includes established brands such as Boat, Xiaomi, , ASUS, Reliance Jio (through vendor ecosystem), CP Plus, and other leading domestic and global players, providing revenue visibility and repeat business opportunities. The addition of customers and expansion into segments such as router manufacturing, along with tie-ups with reputed players, indicate the Optiemus g roup’s ability to scale up operations and broaden its product offerings and support revenue visibility. 

 Sustained scale up in revenue 

 Consolidated operating income clocked CAGR of ~80% between fiscals 2021 and 2025 , backed by execution of large telecom orders in GDN, increasing integration with key customers, expansion across product segments with steady shift towards value-added manufacturing in the EMS segment . The fiscal 2025 revenue w as elevated , at ~Rs 1,894 crore, due to execution of a large contract (in GDN) and is considered a one-off high base . The revenue is expecte d to scale up to over Rs 2,000 crore over the medium term with growth across segments of the group and incremental contribution from the greenfield cover glass manufacturing project in BIGTECH , which will be partly operationalised in fiscal 2027 . 

 Comfortable financial risk profile 

 S trengthening networth , moderate leverage and a prudent capital structure will keep the financial risk profile comfortable . Networth has increased significant ly over the past few years, supported by internal accrual and equity in fusion of ~Rs 200 crore from the promoters, marquee investors and related parties, with an additional ~Rs 90 crore expected over the medium term, providing adequate financial flexibility to support ongoing expansion. 

 While debt is expected to increase to fund the BIGTECH and other projects , peak ing at Rs 400–450 crore over fiscals 20 27 and 20 28, the capital structure is expected to remain comfortable, with gearing projected at 0.4–0.5 time . Debt protection metrics are also expected to remain adequate, supp orted by steady accrual generation and improving operating performance. 

 Key Rating Drivers - We a knesses 

 Execution and stabilisation risks associated with the greenfield cover glass project 

 The group is undertaking large greenfield capex under BIGTECH for cover glass manufacturing, marking its entry into a new and technologically intensive segment. While the project benefits from a strategic partnership with a global technology provider and favourable industry tailwinds, it remains exposed to risks assoc iated with timely execution and stabilisation of operations, and achievement of the envisaged utilisation levels. 

 Given that commercial operations are yet to commence, the ability to ramp up production, achieve operational efficiencies, and establish steady offtake arrangements will be monitorable. Any delay in project implementation or slower-than-expected ramp-up could impact revenue realisation and the overall earnings trajectory. 

 Intense competition in EMS segment and working capital-intensive operati ons 

 The group operates in the highly competitive EMS segment . T he presence of several established domestic and global players can exert pressure on pricing and profitability , particularly in large contracts. While the group has demonstrated strong execution capabilities and scaled up operations in recent years, sustaining profitability amid competitive intensity remains monitorable. 

 Also , the business remains working capital intensive, as reflected in large gross current asset s (GCA s ) of ~197 days as on M arch 31, 2025, driven by inventory holding and receivables from OEM s . Although some improvement is expected with tightening of credit terms and better customer mix, the working capital requirement is likely to remain high . 

 Liquidity Adequate 

 The group’s liquidity profile is adequate, supported by comfortable bank limit utilisation, steady accrual, and limited near-term debt obligation. W orking capital limit utilisation was moderate , averaging 50% over the six months through February 2026, providing sufficient headroom for incremental requirement. Expected annual c ash accrual of Rs 80–150 crore will adequately cover scheduled yearly term debt obligation of Rs 20–40 crore over the medium term. 

 T he group is undertaking sizeabl e capex in the near term, primarily towards the BIGTECH project, which is largely funded through tied-up term debt. The associated debt carries a favourable repayment structure, including a moratorium of ~2 years and back-ended repayment aligned with the expected ramp-up in cash flow from the project. Principal repayment i s expected to commence from fiscal 2028, thereby limiting near-term debt servicing pressure. 

 Outlook Stable 

 Crisil Ratings believes the group will continue to benefit from its improving business profile, supported by steady scale-up in manufacturing operations and stable operating performance. The outlook also factors in the expected ramp-up of the BIGTECH project over the medium term, while maintaining a comfortable financial risk profile. 

 Rating s ensitivity factors 

 Upward factors 

 Sustained improvement in operating performance with operating margin sustaining at 7.5–8 .0 % and healthy cash accrual 

 Successful ramp-up and stabilisation of BIGTECH operations, with healthy utilisation and firm offtake/order visibility 

 Downward factors 

 Slower-than-expected ramp-up or stabilisation of BIGTECH operations, including weaker -than-expected utilisation or inadequate order visibility impacting cash flow 

 Decline in operating performance with operating margin falling below 6% or weakening financial risk profile due to increase in debt or stretched working capital 

 About the Company 

 Optiemus   is the pan-India distributor of Plantronics headsets and sells mobile accessories under its own brand , Mo Life. The company operates four regional warehouses and a mother warehouse in Delhi. 

 About the Group 

 The Optiemus g roup is a diversified electronics manufacturing and technology group with operations spanning distribution, EMS, and emerging participation in high-value component manufacturing. 

 The group operates through key entities Optiemus (distribution and strategic ov ersight), GDN (telecom and electronics manufacturing), and OEL (EMS), collectively catering to a wide range of product segments such as telecom equipment, wearables, mobile accessories and consumer electronics. 

 T he group has entered into cover glass manufacturing through the BIGTECH project (in partnership with Corning Inc), marking its entry into a technology-intensive and high value-added segment. This transition towards manufacturing, along with diversification across products and customers, has streng thened the group’s business profile and positions it favourably within India’s growing electronics manufacturing ecosystem.
