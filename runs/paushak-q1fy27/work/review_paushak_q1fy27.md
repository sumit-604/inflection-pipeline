**Six mandatory diagnostics:**

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, +49.52% (55.88 -> 83.55). This is the MPP-8
commissioning stepping revenue-run-rate above the ~Rs 55 Cr pre-commissioning plateau.
No FY27 revenue guidance is in the passed thesis to benchmark against; carried as a gap.

2. **Op EBITDA margin YoY?** Contracted mildly: Q1FY27 30.70% vs Q1FY26 31.94% = **−124
bps**. But the operating-EBITDA line masks the real story one level up — **gross margin
collapsed 1,374 bps** (82.39% -> 68.65%). Operating EBITDA held near 31% only because
Other Expenses and Employee grew far slower than revenue (operating leverage on fixed
overhead absorbed most of the gross-margin hit). This is the single most important
number in the quarter (A3 F17-a).

3. **Core operating PBT (ex-OI) YoY?** **Grew +15.42%** (13.75 -> 15.87). The headline
PAT growth of +25.52% is therefore **partly real** — core ops did expand — but the
gap between +25.52% PAT and +15.42% core PBT is explained by Other Income and a lower
tax rate, not core operations. Headline overstates operational momentum by ~10 pp.

4. **What drove the PAT-vs-core gap?** Bridge (Step 4): Other Income +Rs 1.27 Cr, tax
rate down 247 bps (23.03% -> 20.56%) worth ~Rs 0.31 Cr, both flattered PAT above core.
Depreciation (+Rs 4.36 Cr) and finance cost (+Rs 1.32 Cr) were the offsetting drags —
capex-absorption costs now fully in the P&L while volume is still ramping.

5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** Yes, dramatically.
Depreciation +107% and finance cost +3,300% against revenue +49.5%. This is the
**textbook capex-absorption gap** (A3 F17-a): fixed capacity cost is fully loaded while
utilization ramps. Mathematically ROCE compresses until volume catches the asset base.
The +49.5% revenue step is the volume beginning to catch up; watch whether it holds.

6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Yes, and favourably for signal quality.
OI/PBT fell to 16.56% (Q1FY27) from 51.25% (Q4FY26). The Q4FY26 spike (Rs 8.21 Cr, the
Nirayu-type FVOCI redemption, paired with the Rs (7.85) Cr reclassifiable-OCI line at
L119) **did not recur** — Q1FY27 OI of Rs 3.15 Cr is close to a clean ~Rs 3 Cr/qtr base
(A3 F1-a). This quarter's PAT is not treasury-inflated to the Q4 degree.
