UGLINESS: not classified. The Part B5 declaration does not exist yet. The phase 1 record supports two readings. ARTIFACT OF CLIMB: long cycle naval work, a year end dispatch bunch and held back stock swell working capital for a while, and the build reverses once orders ship and PSU goods receipts clear. STRUCTURAL FEATURE: fixed price PSU tenders with no escalation clause, 50% advances to foundries, no metal hedge and slow government receipts make working capital a permanent tax on growth. The observation that separates them is the half year to 30 September 2026. If receivables plus inventory fall below the March 2026 level of ₹51.5 Cr and operating cash turns positive, the reading is artifact. If both hold or rise with cash still negative, it is structural.

RECOGNITION GAP: unresolved. Stage 11 sets it in phase 3.
