## 1. THE PAT FIGURE THE FIRST CARD SAID DID NOT EXIST, AND WHY IT IS NOT GOOD NEWS
Source: Q4FY26 investor presentation, document zsoIC7
(1c4444ed-cdad-4983-bb1b-8503e68eb8b6), page 30.
"**PAT 11 [Million INR]**", with the supporting note "**Rs 125 Mn tax expense credit in
Q4FY26**", and the stated reason "Tax credit offset operational losses".
DERIVED, arithmetic shown: a PAT of positive Rs 11 Mn after a tax credit of Rs 125 Mn
implies a **pre-tax loss of about Rs 114 Mn** in Q4FY26. The company's first positive PAT
line is entirely an accounting entry, not trading profit.
This is the single most important thing the sweep found for this name. Any screen that
reads "PAT turned positive in Q4FY26" is reading a deferred tax credit.
