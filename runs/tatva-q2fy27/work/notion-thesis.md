## Story After Reading the Filing + Concall
Tatva spent the last four years building an R&D-heavy specialty chemicals platform while the P&L looked mediocre. That platform is now cashing in. Two structural realities cut against the euphoria. That means the historical ROCE of 20%+ from FY22 is not the go-forward ceiling — the sustainable ROCE in the new mix is more like 12-15%. Second, cash conversion is quietly weakening — receivable days keep drifting up, borrowings jumped, and the borrowing ceiling was raised to Rs 1,000 Cr signalling more leverage coming for capex.
The market is pricing Tatva at Rs 1,194 assuming both the growth AND the historical premium ROCE come back together. The problem is those two assumptions are in tension. If growth delivers, it will be on lower structural ROCE and higher debt, which does not justify Rs 1,194. Real recovery is happening — it just isn't worth the price the market is asking.
