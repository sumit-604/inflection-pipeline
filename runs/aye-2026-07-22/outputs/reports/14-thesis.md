## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why Buy)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** Post-IPO capital is the cleanest strength: CRAR 42.24-42.38% (FY26 / Q1FY27) against a regulatory minimum, LCR 269.61% (Q1FY27), zero Tier II reliance, Rs 710 Cr fresh primary capital earmarked for onward lending (B08 transition_evidence; B04 must_track; B03 strengths_top3). Asset quality is improving off the trough: Gross Stage III fell 4.77% (Mar-26) to 4.49% (Jun-26), Net Stage III 1.79% to 1.67%, PCR 63.66% to 63.80% (B05 promise_delivery row 3). FY26 PAT Rs 193.63 Cr / EPS Rs 9.73, with Q1FY27 PAT Rs 74.5 Cr already running well above the FY26 quarterly average of ~Rs 48 Cr (B11 s1, s4). RoA/RoE are RECOVERING per the FTTCP verdict (fttcp-deliberation.md line 16). Caveat carried honestly: cumulative CFO/PAT is structurally negative (balance-sheet lender under Ind AS 7), and Gate 0 Block B reads INDETERMINATE rather than clean (B01 FLAG-CASH).

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**
1. Credit-cost normalisation toward the 3.25-3.75% comfort range (conviction M; near-medium). Confirms if Gross/Net Stage III keep improving in the next filing; killed if GNPA/NNPA reverse upward. This is the pillar of the whole thesis.
2. Post-IPO capital and funding-cost decline supporting NIM/RoA (conviction M-H; near-medium). Confirms if the PAT/EPS step-up continues (Q3FY26 Rs 42.6 Cr to Q4FY26 Rs 85.91 Cr already delivered, +102% QoQ, B05).
3. Mortgage/LAP mix build toward ~30% of portfolio from ~22% (conviction M; 3-year). Currently flat at ~21.8-23%, so unproven (B05 promise_delivery row 5).
4. AUM growth reacceleration toward 29-30% (conviction L-M). This is the one management MISSED in FY26 (guided 29-30%, delivered ~26.6% on-book, B05 red_flags).

**3C. Moat Assessment.** Real but historically unproven. Strongest category is D1, the 70+ business-cluster proprietary underwriting IP built over 12+ years (Strong, documented; B04 moats_present, B07 D1). Supported by G1 funding access / war chest (post-IPO CRAR, second-agency rating upgrade to IND A+ Jun-26), C1 repeat-customer stickiness (41-50% retention, Moderate), and A3 process/cost-to-income edge. Direction is modestly strengthening, not expanding: EM 19.6 is MODEST and below the 25 premium threshold, which is why Pillar 3 growth visibility was capped at +2x (B07; fttcp-deliberation.md line 52). Honest limits: D1's underwriting edge did not prevent a full asset-quality cycle (GNPA 3.2% to 4.77%), and the "hard to replicate" characterisation rests on a CRISIL report AYE itself commissioned and paid for (B07 top_moat_risks).

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter verdict TRUSTWORTHY (B08). No identifiable promoter; founder-MD Sanjay Sharma and a reconstituted board (ex-NABARD Chairman, ex-RBI CGM, ex-MD PNB Housing) carry clean, regulator-heavy records. Transition evidence is strong: external professional CFO Gaurav Seth (ex-IIFL Home Finance) appointed Apr-2026, auditor upgraded to an MSKA/BDO-network firm, second rating agency upgrade. Only caution items are listing-window CFO churn (two changes in ~4 months) and a 2023 same-day three-ID resignation cluster (one since re-appointed). Delivery record is grade C (Mixed): the single most confidently stated guidance (29-30% FY26 AUM growth) missed, against genuine anchored delivery on asset quality and profit (B05 credibility_basis).

**3E. Market Opportunity.** TAM runway STRONG. Conservative TAM Rs 4,08,000 Cr, realistic Rs 9,12,000 Cr; SAM Rs 1,20,400 Cr; current SAM share 6.1%; revenue headroom 66.3x; TAM growing ~19% (B09). Framework-disciplined SOM-implied 3-year AUM CAGR is 18.3%, which runs below management's ~28-33% aspiration; the operator flagged management guidance as the aggressive case and the SOM figure as the floor (B09 FLAG-SOM-VS-MGMT-ASPIRATION). The market is large enough; the constraint is execution and capital, not demand (5-year SOM Rs 14,540 Cr exceeds the static capital ceiling Rs 13,476 Cr, requiring retained-earnings accretion or a further raise; B09 FLAG-CAPACITY-5YR-GAP).

**3F. Peer Positioning.** Middle of the pack, with the peer read complicating rather than confirming the thesis (B06 net_narrative_effect: "complicates"). Peers verify comparable cost-of-borrowing declines (funding tailwind is sector-wide, not idiosyncratic) but split three ways on credit-cost normalisation, so AYE's clean four-quarter decline is not unambiguously sector-driven. Two AYE claims are directly contradicted by the closest peer analogues: "minimal spillover" from state MFI ordinances (SBFC/Northern Arc Karnataka experience shows deeper, less contained spillover) and the sub-2% mortgage credit-cost aspiration (SBFC reports enhanced credit cost and ~2x SARFAESI recovery timelines on the analogous sub-Rs 7 lakh secured book; B06 contradicted).

## NARRATIVE (plain English)

This is a watchlist name with a clear, measurable trigger set.
