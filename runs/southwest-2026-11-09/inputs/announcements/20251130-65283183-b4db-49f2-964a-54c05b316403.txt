Subject: Announcement about South West Pinnacle becoming accredited prospecting agency to 
explore its own coal block in Jharkhand 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
We are pleased to inform you that in exercise of powers conferred under Mines and Mineral 
(Development and Regulation) Act 1957, Ministry of Coal, Government of India vide its notification 
dated 26th November, 2005 published in Gazette of India on 27th Nov 2025, South West Pinnacle 
Exploration Limited (Company) has been notified as accredited pro specting agency for carrying out 
prospecting operations for the exploration of coal and Lignite. 
The company will now be able to commence exploration activities at full swing and move ahead for 
early development of coal block namely Jogeshwar and Khas Jogeshwar situated in the state of 
Jharkhand, allocated by Ministry of Coal, Government of India.  
This notification shall be valid for a period of three years from the date of its publication in the official 
Gazette or till the expiry or termination of the accreditation granted, whichever is earlier. 
The Government has notified QCI - NABT accredited private prospecting agencies to conduct coal and 
lignite exploration speedly. The impact of it as explained in the press release is summarised below: 
     Gives coal block allottees more flexibility & choice; 
        Boosts efficiency, competition & tech-driven exploration; 
   
  Supports Atmanirbhar Bharat & strengthens energy security; 
   Helps faster operationalization of mines → greater coal & lignite availability; 
   Overall: A future-ready push to accelerate India’s coal sector development. 
This way the notification shall enable the Company to derive many other exploration focused 
advantages as well.
