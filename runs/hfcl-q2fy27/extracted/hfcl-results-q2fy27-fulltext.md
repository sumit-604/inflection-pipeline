New Delhi, 19th October 2026: HFCL Limited (HFCL), a leading technology enterprise specialising in optical fiber,
optical fiber cable and connectivity solutions, telecom products, Defence and Aerospace products, and digital
infrastructure, today announced its best-ever quarterly performance for the second quarter and half year ended
September 30, 2026.

Q2 FY27 marked a strong improvement in both scale and profitability. Consolidated revenue increased to ₹2,541.79
crore from ₹1,043.34 crore in Q2 FY26, growing 143.62% year-on-year and 32.73% sequentially. The improvement
in profitability was even more remarkable, with both profit before tax and profit after tax growing more than
fivefold year-on-year. Consolidated profit before tax stood at ₹538.60 crore, up 406.49% year-on-year and 62.46%
quarter-on-quarter, while consolidated profit after tax reached ₹394.22 crore, registering growth of 448.14% year-
on-year and 60.49% quarter-on-quarter. On half yearly basis the PBT grew from ₹61.64 crore to ₹870.12 crore, up
by 1311.62% while PAT grew from ₹42.62 crore to ₹639.86 crore, up by 1401.31%

The performance reflects increasing scale, operating strength and momentum across HFCL’s businesses. The order
book now standing at ₹28,911 crore, together with advanced opportunity pipeline, provides multi-year revenue
visibility. Demand across AI infrastructure, hyperscale data centres and telecom networks is accelerating, while
international opportunities are steadily converting into firm contracts.
