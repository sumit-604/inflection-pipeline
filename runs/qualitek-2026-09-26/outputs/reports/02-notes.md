## PASS 3 PATTERN FINDINGS

1. **CONTRADICTION, internal to Note 11c(iii): a static ₹151.81 Lakh CWIP balance crosses from the "2-3 years" bucket at FY25 into "more than 3 years" at FY26 (same project, unmoved), while the note's own next line states "there are no projects under capital work-in-progress whose completion is overdue"** (Note 11c(iii)(a) and (b), p.130/[page 136]). A project sitting unmoved past the 3-year ageing threshold reads as overdue by ordinary meaning; management's own overdue-assessment line asserts the opposite. Not resolvable from the notes alone. 🟡 Watch — management question candidate, carried from Pass 2 and elevated here as a genuine pattern-level contradiction, not just a missed item.

2. **DELIBERATE-VAGUENESS CONTRAST: the borrowings note (Note 5) is one of the most granular disclosures in the entire AR — instrument-by-instrument rate, security, EMI schedule for every term loan — while the note governing the year's single largest related-party transaction (the ITCPL share swap, Note 3/26/27) gives no valuation methodology, no valuer name, no fairness-opinion reference at all.** Same filer, same audit, wildly different disclosure depth depending on which stakeholder benefits from opacity (lenders get granular security terms; the promoter-side share swap gets none). 🔴 Red Flag — this is the clearest evidence-tier finding across all three passes: the company demonstrably CAN disclose in detail when it chooses to; the RPT valuation gap is a choice, not a format limitation.

3. **"Prior Period Items" is a new P&L caption this year with zero explanatory note** (consol ₹5.92 Lakh, standalone ₹3.51 Lakh, both FY26, both ₹0 FY25) — first appearance, unexplained composition, small in amount. 🟡 Watch, carried from Pass 1/2 as a pattern candidate; on a third read, no cross-reference anywhere in the notes resolves what this line contains.

4. **No numeric mismatch found between notes and the main financial statements on any figure independently cross-footed in this exercise** (RPT share-swap consideration ties to the investments note; ITCPL/QTIPL/LabOps revenue contributions sum to consolidated revenue within ₹0.45 Lakh rounding; goodwill amortisation ties to the useful-life policy once the partial-year convention is applied; standalone vs consolidated goodwill differ only by the explained ₹113.96 Lakh legacy write-off). 🟢 Clean cross-footing — no evidence of number-level manipulation between the notes and the statements themselves; the concerns in this analysis are disclosure-adequacy and governance-structure concerns, not arithmetic ones.

5. **No restatement of prior-year figures found anywhere in substance.** The two reclassifications flagged in Pass 1 (Advance Income Tax ₹226 Lakh, employee recoverables ₹18 Lakh) are presentation-only, immaterial, and transparently disclosed as reclasses, not restatements of a reported number. No note anywhere uses the word "restated" in connection with FY25 figures. 🟢 Clean.

6. **Going concern language: confirmed NONE, on the third read, across the notes, both auditor's reports (standalone and consolidated), and the secretarial audit report.** Only the boilerplate "on a going concern basis" basis-of-preparation phrase appears; no emphasis-of-matter, no material-uncertainty paragraph. 🟢 Clean.

7. **Events after balance sheet date: confirmed on third read that the notes' "no material events" line is scoped to the 20-May-2026 board approval date, and real subsequent events (Sep-2026 preferential issue/warrants to TIC Services, NCD approval, CS cessation filings) sit outside the notes in other AR sections, correctly per AS convention.** 🟢 Clean framing, no new contradiction found, confirms Pass 1's read.

**PASS 3 SUMMARY**: seven pattern-level findings, two new-in-substance (the CWIP overdue contradiction elevated to pattern status; the borrowings-vs-RPT disclosure-depth contrast, the single sharpest evidence-tier finding of the three passes), five confirmatory (no restatements, no going-concern language, no numeric mismatches, subsequent-events framing clean). No fabricated significance; several checks came back clean and are reported as clean.

### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. What valuation methodology and independent fairness opinion, if any, supported the 6.56:1 ITCPL share-swap exchange ratio, and why was the swap priced 65% above the FY25 arm's-length cash preferential price agreed only twelve months earlier?
2. What discount rate, terminal growth rate, and CGU-level value-in-use assumptions are used to test the ₹78.12 Cr goodwill on consolidation for impairment, given two of the three acquired entities remain loss-making?
3. Why has MSME interest on delayed payments grown in both FY25 and FY26 despite an improving aggregate payable-turnover ratio, and what remediation is planned for the specific vendor cohort affected?
4. The CWIP ageing note shows a ₹151.81 Lakh balance now aged past three years while the same note asserts no project is overdue; which project is this, and what is its actual completion status?
5. When are QTIPL (two years loss-making, 0.3% of consolidated revenue) and LabOps Global (zero revenue, a loss in its first partial year) expected to reach profitability or revenue scale that justifies their acquisition cost?

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management**: none found on a numbers-manipulation basis (no restatements, no revenue-recognition aggressiveness, all cross-footing checks clean); the concerns are governance/disclosure-structure concerns, not P&L manipulation.
- **Aggressive accounting**: none found; depreciation lives, revenue recognition, and goodwill amortisation policy are all conservative-to-neutral and correctly applied under IGAAP/AS.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators**: (a) no independent valuation basis disclosed for a majority related-party share swap; (b) no quantified goodwill impairment assumptions despite a dominant, recently-grown goodwill pool; (c) no impairment testing rigor shown for two loss-making subsidiary investments; (d) a disclosure-depth asymmetry between lender-facing notes (granular) and promoter-facing RPT notes (boilerplate) that is itself a governance signal.

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices, clean on mechanics but concerning on related-party governance. Key concern: an undisclosed valuation basis behind a majority related-party share swap, paired with thin goodwill-impairment and investment-impairment disclosure against a 2.4x larger goodwill pool. Key strength: conservative revenue recognition, no going-concern language, no restatements, and unusually granular borrowings/actuarial/ratio disclosure where the company chooses to disclose in depth. Overall accounting quality: 5/10.
