Annexure-I 
Gist of the Proceedings of the 19th Annual General Meeting of Sagardeep Alloys Limited 
 
1. Date, Time and Venue of the meeting: 
 
The 19th Annual General Meeting of the Company was held on Wednesday, September 
30, 2026 and the meeting commenced at 11:00 A.M. at the Registered Office of the 
Company and the Meeting concluded at 03:00 P.M. 
 
2. Proceedings in brief:  
 
 Mr. Satishkumar Asamal Mehta, Managing Director chaired the proceedings of the 
meeting. 
 
 The requisite quorum being present, the Chairman called the meeting to order. 
 
 The Chairman addressed the members. 
 
 The Chairman informed that remote e-voting commenced at 09:00 A.M. on September 
27, 2026 and concluded at 5:00 P.M. on September 29, 2026. 
 
 The Following items of business set out in the Notice convening the 19th Annual 
General Meeting were commenced for members consideration and approval: 
 
o Adoption of Audited Standalone & Consolidated Financial Statements for the 
year ended 31.03.2026; 
 
o Appointment of Mrs. Sangita Satishkumar Mehta (DIN: 03388025) who is 
liable to retires by rotation. 
 
o To appoint M/s. PNK & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries as Secretarial 
Auditor of the Company. 
 
o To appoint M/s. Priyank Patel & Associates, Cost Accountants as the Cost 
Auditors of the Company. 
 
o To appoint M/s. Ashish Sheth & Associates, Chartered Accountants as the 
Statutory Auditors of the Company 
 
 
 Reply/ Clarifications were provided by the Chairman to the queries raised by the 
members. 
 
 The Chairman also informed the members that M/s. Nimit B Shah & Co, Practising 
Chartered Accountants, were appointed as the Scrutinizer for the purpose of 
scrutinizing the poll at the Meeting and remote e-voting process.
