 Detailed Rationale 

 Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘ Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2 ’ ratings on the bank facilities of Creative Newtech Limited (CNL, part of Creative Group). 

 The ratings reflect the diversified product offering supported by extensive experience of the promoter in the distribution of computer peripherals and consumer electronics, increasing scale of operations and diversified customer base and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition, low operating margin and moderate working capital cycle 

 Analytical Approach 

 Crisil Ratings has now consolidated the business and financials of CNL along with its subsidiaries given the increasing cashflow fungibilities and extension of corporate guarantee by CNL to its subsidiary . Earlier, Crisil Ratings had evaluated standalone business and financial risk profile of CNL. 

 Unsecured loan of Rs 8 lakhs as on March 31, 2026 from the promoter has been treated as debt. 

 Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation 

 Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

 Diversified product offering supported by extensive promoter experience: The promoter of the group have more than two decades of experience in the consumer electronics business which has helped establish a clientele across multiple channels includ ing channel partners, large format stores, sub-distributors and e-commerce players . Additionally, the group benefits from a diversified business profile with presence across multiple technology segments, including consumer electronics, surveillance and security solutions, networking products, cybersecurity solutions, AI and IoT-related products, and emerging technologies such as drones. The product portfolio is supported by long-standing relationships with several reputed domestic and international brands s uch as Honeywell, Cooler Master , Lexar, Fujifilm, Olympus, i-ball, AOC, Sparsh, Matrix, Kaspersky, Dahua, Eizo, Zion, PDRL amongst the others. This has has enabled the group to continuously expand its offerings and address evolving customer requirements and end-user industry demand. Over the years, the group has also diversified its customer base, reducing its dependence on any single customer while strengthening its product portfolio by continuing to add brand/principals over the years 

 Increasing scale of operations and diversified customer base : The group reported a significant improvement in its scale of operations during FY2026, with consolidated revenue increasing by approximately 51% to ~Rs. 2,710 crores from ~Rs. 1,792 crores in FY2025. The growth was driven by a strategic shift from a traditional distribution-led model towards a value-added distribution and market-enablement platform, supported by expansion across multiple technology segments and addition of new principal/brands, products and custome rs. During the year, the group strengthened its presence in high-growth sectors such as surveillance and security, cybersecurity, data centers, artificial intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT) and drone technologies through addition of new brands and partnerships such as Sparsh, Matrix, PDRL amongst the other. With effective addition of newer customers, the concentration in revenues from one of the customers has improved to around 25% as against historical average of 50-60%. The group also continued t o scale up its higher-margin branded business through Honeywell and has also introduced own brand- Wozoyo, thereby improving diversification across revenue streams. Furthermore, the group's appointment as Project Implementation Agency (PIA) for the Odisha circle under the Bharat-Net program is expected to provide healthy revenue visibility over the medium term through both execution and long-term maintenance income. While timely execution of the project and scale-up of newly added business verticals remain c ritical, the group's diversified product portfolio, expanding principal relationships and continuous adding of customers are expected to support continued growth in its scale of operations over the medium term. 

 Comfortable financial risk profile : The group’s financial risk profile remains comfortable, supported by a healthy net worth and moderate leverage levels. Net worth improved to ~Rs. 387 crores as on March 31, 2026, from Rs. 309 crores as on March 31, 2025, supported by steady accretion to reserves d espite moderate dividend payouts. Although debt levels increased during FY2026 to support the significant scale-up in operations and the associated working capital requirements, the capital structure remained comfortable, as reflected in gearing and TOLANW of 0.84 times and 1.40 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2026 (0.23 times and 0.72 times as on March 31, 2025). Going forward, debt levels are expected to remain elevated in line with the growing scale of operations and working capital requirements; ho wever, steady internal accruals are likely to support net worth growth and help maintain a comfortable capital structure in the absence of any large debt-funded capex or acquisitions. Debt protection metrics, though moderated from the previous year, remained adequate, with an interest coverage ratio of over 5 times on a consolidated basis in FY2026 (over 7 times in FY2025). Overall financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable supported by healthy networth, and the expected improvement in the cap ital structure and sustenance of comfortable debt protection metrices. 

 Key Rating Drivers - We a knesses 

 Exposure to intense competition and low operating margins: The group’s profitability remains constrained by the inherently low-margin nature of the consumer electronics distribution business and intense competition from numerous organized and unorganized players. The sector is characterized by low entry barriers, limited value addition, and significant pricing pressure, thereby restricting margin expan sion opportunities. As a result, operating margins have stayed moderate in the range of 3.5–4.5% during FY2024–FY2026. While the increasing scale of the brand business is expected to support profitability over the medium term, the group's ability to sustain growth in these higher-margin segments and improve its consolidated profitability profile remains crucial. Nonetheless, the group continues to be exposed to intense competition and pricing pressures in the distribution business, which may constrain profi tability and remains a key rating monitorable. 

 Moderate working capital cycle: The group’s operations remain moderately working capital intensive, with gross current assets (GCA) increasing to ~120–125 days as on March 31, 2026, from 102 days as on March 31, 2025. The elongation in the working capital cycle was primarily on account of higher debtor days, which increased to 76 days as on March 2026 from 49 days in the previous year, driven by elevated year-end billing and extended credit offered to a few cu stomers. Inventory levels remained moderate at around 24 days. While debtors are generally realized within the stipulated credit period, the ongoing expansion in the customer base, addition of new principals, and expected execution of large projects such as Bharat Net are likely to keep receivable levels relatively higher than the historical average over the medium term. The working capital requirements are partially supported through vendor credit, along with utilization of fund-based working capital limit s and TReDS financing arrangements. With the anticipated growth in scale of operations, the group’s ability to efficiently manage its working capital cycle will remain a key monitorable. 

 Liquidity Adequate 

 Fund based bank limits utilization averaged at around 73% for the last 12 months ended May 2026 . Additionally, CNL has also received enhancement in bank limits which should provide cushion to the liquidity and support the expected increase in the scale of oeprations. In addition to this, group has also started using the TReDS limits to support its working capital operati o ns. Net Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 65-85 crores which are sufficient against moderate term debt obligation over the medium term. Cash and cash equivalents stood at around ~Rs 22 Crores as on March 2026 which is entirely free supporting the liquidity . Current ratio stood at 1.69 times on March 31, 2026. Crisil Ratings believes that comfortable capital structure and healthy networth support financial flexibility and provide cushion in case of any adverse condition or downturn in the business. 

 Outlook Stable 

 Crisil Ratings believes that CNL will continue to benefit over the medium term from the extensive industry experience of its promoters, its diversified product and customer profile along with its healthy relationship with principals and other stakeholders

 About the Group 

 Creative Newtech Limited (CNL) 

 Set up in 1992 by Mr Ketan Patel, CNL is a distributor of consumer electronic goods in the information technology, imaging, lifestyle and security segments for brands such as Honeywell, Cooler Master, Lexar, Fujifilm, Olympus, i-ball, AOC, Sparsh, Matrix, Kaspersky, Dahua, Eizo, Zion, PDRL amongst the others . The company is based in Mumbai and is listed on the National Stock Exchange. 

 Secure Connection Limited (SCL) 

 Secure Connection Limited, incorporate d in Hong Kong, is a majority owned subsidiary of Creative Newtech. It was established to strengthen the group’s international sourcing, trading and distribution capabilities. The subsidiary supports the parent company’s international operations by facilitating imports, exports and distribution activities. The key operation/offerings in this entity is the Honeywell products for which it has a brand licensing arrangements. 

 Creative Peripherals and Distributions Limited 

 Creative Peripherals and Dist. Ltd, incorporated in Hong Kong, is a majority owned subsidiary of Creative Newtech. It was established to strengthen the group’s international sourcing, trading and distribution capabilities. The subsidiary supports the parent company’s international operations by facilitating imports, exports and distribution activities
