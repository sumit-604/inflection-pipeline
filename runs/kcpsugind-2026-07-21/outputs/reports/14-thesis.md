## NARRATIVE (plain English)

KCP Sugar is not really a sugar company you buy for sugar. The sugar mill loses money and has lost more each year for three years; the cash flow just turned negative for the first time. What sits inside the same listed shell is a Rs 332 Cr pile of shares and mutual funds, plus a genuinely good little engineering business (Eimco) that just won a Rs 257 Cr order. Add those, subtract the Rs 128 Cr of debt, and you get about Rs 22.50 a share. The stock trades at Rs 21.71. So the market is already paying you the full asset value — there is no discount to capture today, and the thing underneath that value is a business burning cash that could eat into the asset pile. That is why the answer at today's price is AVOID. The only way this becomes interesting is if the price falls to roughly Rs 10-12, where you would be buying the investment book and the Eimco option at a real discount, and even then only a small starter, only if the cash isn't visibly leaking and the Eimco order has actually been filed. The single most important thing to watch is whether the Reg 30 filing for that Rs 257 Cr order appears, and whether the Rs 332 Cr book stays intact rather than being spent to plug sugar losses.
