eMudhra Limited (the "Company") wishes to provide the following clarification to the Stock Exchanges in
response to certain disclosures made by 3i Infotech Limited on 04 February 2026 (today) under Regulation
30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015.

3i Infotech Limited has alleged corporate financial fraud, criminal breach of trust, cheating, and criminal
conspiracy in connection with the disinvestment of 3i Consumer Services Limited (now eMudhra Limited)
and the purported wrongful redemption of preference shares. The claimed impact is alleged by 3i Infotech
Limited to exceed Rupees 128 crores, plus interest and damages. A complaint is alleged to have been filed
by 3i Infotech Limited with Assistant Commissioner of Police, Economic Offence Wing, Navi Mumbai
Police Commissionerate on 03 February 2026. The Company has not received a copy of the said complaint.

The Company strongly denies all allegations made by 3i Infotech Limited. The matter was first raised via
a letter by 3i Infotech Limited in January 2024, to which the Company responded in detail in February
2024. subsequently at the request of the Company, 3i Infotech Limited held a management meeting in
March 2024 seeking clarifications, which the Company attended physically and provided clarifications.
Further correspondence ensued in September 2024 from 3i Infotech Limited, wherein additional
allegations were raised by 3i Infotech Limited. The Company sought to inspect records held by 3i Infotech
Limited; however, a confirmed and scheduled inspection in January 2025 was cancelled by 3i Infotech
Limited at the last moment. The Company's subsequent offer to make documents available with the
Company for inspection by 3i Infotech Limited, in Bengaluru, received no response for over one year. The
Company wrote further letter to 3i Infotech in January 2025 replying to all their wrongful allegations, for
which the Company has not received any reply so far, though more than one year has elapsed.

The Company notes that these allegations relate to matters approximately fifteen years old, and none
of the Board members/Senior Management at 3i Infotech Limited, during the relevant periods
pertaining to these supposed allegations, remain with 3i Infotech Limited. The current management of
3i Infotech Limited without understanding the transaction with the Company is making false and
baseless allegations after several years. In the opinion of the Company, it is clear that these allegations

[page 2]
are without merit, besides being barred by limitation. The Company intends to contest this matter
through appropriate legal recourse.

This disclosure is made in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
