## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Basis of preparation**: Ind AS per Companies (Ind AS) Rules 2015, RBI NBFC Master Directions, Division III Schedule III format. Going concern explicitly affirmed by management with no material uncertainty. (Note 2.1-2.2, PDF p.144) 🟢
- **New standards adopted FY25**: Ind AS 21 amendment (lack of exchangeability), Ind AS 117 (insurance contracts — not applicable), Ind AS 116 amendment (sale-and-leaseback lease liability). Company states "no significant impact." No quantified P&L impact disclosed for any. (Note 3v, PDF p.161-162) 🟢
- **Revenue recognition — interest income**: EIR method under Ind AS 109 for all amortised-cost instruments; gain on sale/derecognition of assigned loan portfolios recognised **upfront** in P&L, with servicing income recognised over tenure as a % of interest spread. This upfront gain-on-assignment recognition is a structurally aggressive revenue pattern common to NBFCs doing heavy assignment/securitisation volumes — flagged for scrutiny against EIS (Excess Interest Spread) asset quality below. (Note 3a "Recognition of interest income," 3d(b)(i), PDF p.147-148, 150-151) 🟡
- **ECL / impairment policy**: Standard 3-stage Ind AS 109 model (12mECL Stage 1 / LTECL Stage 2 / LTECL Stage 3), Stage 3 = 90+ DPD. LGD is **not** internally modelled — Company uses a flat **65% LGD** "as recommended by the Foundation Internal Ratings Based (FIRB) approach under Basel II" rather than an empirically-derived internal LGD. This is a simplifying (and not obviously conservative) assumption for a diversified NBFC lending book spanning secured and unsecured products. (Note 36(i)A, PDF p.228) 🟡
- **Depreciation useful lives**: PP&E — Plant & machinery 15 yrs, Furniture 10 yrs, Office equipment 5 yrs, Computers 3 yrs, Servers 6 yrs — all per Schedule II Part C (no deviation from statutory norm), WDV method. Intangibles (software): straight-line 5 yrs. No useful-life changes disclosed. (Note 3i-3j, PDF p.155-156) 🟢
- **Impairment testing (goodwill/CGU)**: Discount rate (cost of equity) 30.00%, terminal growth rate 4% — used for Pragati CGU. Recoverable amount ₹55.20 Cr (5,520.30 lakhs) vs goodwill carrying value ₹20.85 Cr (2,085.13 lakhs) as at 31-Mar-25 — comfortable headroom, no impairment. (Note 81, PDF p.253-254) 🟢
- **Ind AS 116 (leases)**: IBR-based discounting, weighted average incremental borrowing rate **9.70%**. ROU asset ₹24.96 Cr (2,495.71 lakhs) at 31-Mar-25 vs ₹12.06 Cr (1,205.55 lakhs) at 31-Mar-24 (107% YoY increase — new branch/office leases ₹19.48 Cr added in FY25). Lease liability ₹29.43 Cr (2,942.93 lakhs) vs ₹13.45 Cr (1,345.35 lakhs) FY24. (Note 34, PDF p.220-221) 🟢
- **First-time items**: None flagged as first-time adoption beyond the routine MCA-notified standards above.

GNPA ratio doubled (0.99% vs 0.47%); NNPA ratio nearly 5x'd (0.43% vs 0.09%); provision coverage on Stage 3 fell from ~82-84% to ~68%. (Notes 27, 36(i), 68-69, PDF p.199-200, 228-230, 255-256)
