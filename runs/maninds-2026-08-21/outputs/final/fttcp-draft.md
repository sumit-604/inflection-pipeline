## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

Revenue. The one big documented catalyst is NPC. The acquisition closed on 21-May-2026, so the FY27 consolidated step-up to about Rs5,000cr is a fact, not a promise. What confirms it firing: NPC quarterly revenue tracking toward Rs300-500cr from Q2FY27. What kills it: NPC utilisation stalling, or the India book falling further from its already-below-kill Rs2,200-2,300cr level. The India organic story is the worry. Its order book shrank three straight quarters while every peer held stable to record, and the Q1FY27 split now shows it below the operator's kill line.

Margin. The mix is the catalyst: exports, value-added product, NPC at 15-18%, and the Dammam coating layer at a guided 25-35%. What confirms it: consolidated EBITDA holding 13% or higher through FY27. What kills it: the steel squeeze printing in H2FY27, when tenders bid on cheap early-2026 plate execute on today's 15-20% higher plate with no pass-through.

Cash conversion. The catalyst is documented and comes from the rating agency, not management: CRISIL says the FY26 stretch was two named resolving events and expects normalisation this fiscal year on the Fujairah-port approval. What confirms it: debtor days retracing toward 100 and gross current assets toward 200 in the Q2FY27 balance sheet. What kills it: debtor days rising again, or the Rs109cr arbitration receivable being written down.

ROCE. No clean catalyst. NPC is accretive but the capex treadmill offsets it near term. This is NONE-adjacent, but not NONE, because NPC is real; it holds STAGNANT, not DECLINING.
