# Forward view: 2026-10-29 shallow screen, eight names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework (operator ruling 2026-09-21), plus
the two additions the operator asked for on 2026-10-29: an FY28 line on the
same tier rules, and a confidence band on each estimate. The additions sit
inside step 13 of every card under "Operator additions, 2026-10-29". They are
not yet in the framework text; promoting them needs an operator ruling and a
framework PR of its own.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result. The three cheapest names below all carry LOW
confidence, and each is marked.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the screener.in company page saved on 29 October 2026
before market open, so prices are the 28-Oct-2026 close, held at

not a filed document. Every earnings figure traces to a filing, cited in the
card.

## Confidence, as defined in the brief

The probability that the actual year's PAT lands within 15% of the estimate.
HIGH is about 70% or better: guided, with a delivered run-rate behind it, fully
taxed, no one-off in the base. MEDIUM is about 50%: run-rate or guide with one
open question. LOW is under 40%: trend only, untaxed base, one-off in the base,
falling revenue, or a structural event such as a merger, a change of control
or a restructuring. Each card names the one observation that would move the
band.

## The screen result, read honestly

The operator asked for names cheap on forward earnings with high probability.
No name in this run is both.

- **Cheap and LOW confidence:** ESAFSFB, SUDARCOLOR, JYOTISTRUC. Each multiple
  rests on one half-year or one quarter annualised, and each base holds a
  structural event: a bank's credit-cost cycle with a Rs 625 crore write-off
  not yet filed, a change of control eleven months old, and a post-insolvency
  balance sheet with Rs 311 crore of debt falling due in year six.
- **HIGH confidence and not cheap:** POOJAAPREC at 38.3x and TIPSMUSIC at
  38.8x. Both are guided, delivered and fully taxed. Both are priced for it.
- **The middle:** SHREEPUSHK at 16.5x on a guided FY27 with a delivered Q1
  behind it, MEDIUM confidence. The guide has moved three times in nine
  months, which is why it is not HIGH.

## Notes per name

- **ESAFSFB.** The forward PAT is the credit-cost call. Q1 FY27 printed 4.4%
  annualised (3.2% without a Rs 64.7 crore extra provision); management guides
  about 2% by Q4 FY27. The Rs 625 crore technical write-off intimated on
  1-Oct-2026 is not yet in a filed result. The Q2 FY27 call is on 29-Oct-2026.
  A Tier 1 raise is planned and would add shares the market cap does not carry.
  The one PROCEED-quality fact is the price: 1.2x book for a bank guiding 2% ROA.
- **SUDARCOLOR.** The only PROCEED. H1 FY27 PAT doubled to Rs 44.49 crore under
  the new owner, with operating cash flow at minus Rs 24.11 crore and
  receivables plus inventories at Rs 500 crore. Related parties took 24.5% of
  FY26 sales and supplied 32% of purchases, and the auditor's key audit matter
  is related-party pricing. The parent holds a filed right to restructure after
  October 2027. Cheap, if the half repeats and the cash follows.
- **JYOTISTRUC.** Rs 2,288 crore of new orders in October 2026 alone. Against
  that: no current tax, Rs 159.39 crore a year of debenture accretion charged to
  reserves instead of the P&L, legacy receivables at 76% of assets with an
  auditor emphasis, operating cash flow of minus Rs 269 crore in FY26, and four
  tribunal rulings since 2024 without non-fund bank limits. PASS on the
  structural reading; the margin line is the thing to watch.
- **SHREEPUSHK.** Guided Rs 1,250 crore of revenue at an 8.5% PAT margin for
  FY27; Q1 delivered 8.2%. Unit 6 (150,000 MTPA) has no production filing yet.
  Q2 FY27 is due by 14-Nov-2026 and will move the band.
- **POOJAAPREC.** BSE SME, listed 4-Aug-2026 at Rs 301, now Rs 1,009. Guided
  50% revenue growth at a 12% PAT margin; H1 delivered Rs 24.93 crore. One
  customer is 31.75% of sales. Promoter-group foundries supply about Rs 35
  crore a year of alloy. The multiple has already paid for FY27 and most of FY28.
- **TIPSMUSIC.** Rewritten from the 18-Oct-2026 card with the Q2 FY27
  transcript now held. Guide unchanged at +20% PAT; H1 banked Rs 109.4 crore,
  so an H2 flat on H2 FY26 still lands inside the band. Growth is volume at a
  flat 5-paise rate; the yield thesis has not fired. Warner is 25% to 30% of
  revenue and renews in FY28.
- **ZENITHHE.** Rs 19.4 crore of market cap, FY26 PAT of Rs 0.51 lakh, Q4
  losses that wiped out nine-month profit two years running. A merger with
  Achyut Healthcare on an unseen valuation report leaves today's holders with
  8.55% of the combined entity. No FY28 basis exists.
- **PPLPHARMA.** Large cap, outside the mandate; screened because the operator
  named it. FY26 was a loss of Rs 325.94 crore. H1 FY27 tax of Rs 138.53 crore
  exceeded profit before tax of Rs 74.18 crore while eleven overseas
  subsidiaries lost Rs 245.59 crore. No forward PE under the loss-maker rule;
  the gate to profitability is the tax line, not the operating line.
