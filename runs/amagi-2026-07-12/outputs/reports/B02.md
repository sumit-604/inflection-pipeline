### P3-1. The "103x cloud commitment jump" is a disclosed, contractual AWS Technology Agreement — resolves Pass 1's biggest open question, but confirms it is real and mostly NOT funded by IPO proceeds
(Note 42(a)(ii), p.360, cross-referenced against Prospectus "Objects of the Issue"/"Our Business — Technology Stack and Cloud Infrastructure," p.156-161, 261) 🟡 Watch — downgraded from Pass 1's "unexplained red flag" to "explained but still material commitment," net new information changes the risk characterisation

Pass 1 flagged the ₹22,531.72 mn (₹2,253.17 Cr) cloud-infrastructure commitment at Sep 30, 2025 as unexplained. The Prospectus (outside the notes, but describing the exact contract underlying Note 42(a)(ii)) discloses: the Company signed a Base Agreement with Amazon Web Services India Pvt Ltd dated January 31, 2024 plus a Pricing Addendum dated April 28, 2025 (together the "Technology Agreement"), effective **May 1, 2025 to April 30, 2031 (six years)**, with a **total spend commitment of ₹24,176.20 mn (USD 272.50 mn)**. This supersedes a prior AWS arrangement (June 1, 2023–May 31, 2028, ₹11,622.32 mn commitment). Only **₹5,500.64 mn** of IPO Net Proceeds is earmarked (through Fiscal 2028) toward this spend — meaning roughly three-quarters of the six-year, ₹24,176.20 mn commitment must be funded from operating cash flow or future capital, not the IPO itself. AWS may terminate with two years' notice; the Company has no such constraint terminating early, but a material breach cure window is 30 days.

**Pattern-pass catch inside the note itself**: Pass 1's table omitted the Sep 30, 2024 comparative figure. The full run is: Sep 30, 2025 ₹22,531.72 mn; **Sep 30, 2024 ₹1,562.87 mn** (not previously extracted); March 31, 2025 ₹218.18 mn; March 31, 2024 ₹392.96 mn; March 31, 2023 ₹361.88 mn. This is a genuinely volatile, non-monotonic commitment balance (spike to ₹1,562.87 mn at Sep-24, fall to ₹218.18 mn at Mar-25, spike again to ₹22,531.72 mn at Sep-25) consistent with the two-tranche AWS contract history (2023-2028 arrangement being drawn down, then superseded by the much larger 2025-2031 Technology Agreement) rather than a single unexplained cliff. Net effect: the underlying commitment is real, contractual, and disclosed elsewhere in the Prospectus with full terms — this materially reduces (but does not eliminate) the governance-quality concern Pass 1 raised, while confirming the magnitude concern (this remains, by a wide margin, the single largest number in the financial disclosures, exceeding FY25 revenue of ₹11,626.37 mn by ~2x on a six-year committed basis).

### P3-2. Geographic segment presentation was retrospectively regrouped for FY25 and restated across all comparative periods — the concrete instance behind Annexure VII Part C's vague "regroupings" language
(Note 41(a), p.360, cross-referenced with Annexure VII Part C, p.383) 🟢 Clean (disclosed, immaterial to P&L/equity) but connects a Pass 2 finding

Note 41(a) states: "The Group has presented its Geographical information for the year ended March 31, 2024 and March 31, 2023 as India, United Kingdom, United States of America and Rest of the world. For the year ended March 31, 2025 management has regrouped its geographical presentation... to America Region, Europe (Including UK), Asia Pacific, Middle East and India... similarly realigned for the period/year ended September 30, 2024, March 31, 2024 and March 31, 2023." Pass 2 flagged Annexure VII Part C ("Material Regroupings") as disclosing only generic boilerplate ("appropriate re-groupings have been made... wherever required") without naming what regrouping actually occurred. Note 41(a) is the concrete substance behind that generic statement — a presentational-only realignment of geography buckets, not a P&L or equity restatement (consistent with Annexure VII Part A's "Total (B) = Nil" reconciliation). Confirms `restatements_found` should record this as a disclosed, non-monetary presentational regrouping rather than leaving it as an open transparency gap.

### P3-3. Third pre-track-record acquisition (Streamwise LLC) whose acquiring entity was later wound up — strengthens the "troubled M&A" pattern to 3-for-3, with a caveat on scope
(Cross-referenced: Prospectus "History and Certain Corporate Matters," p.283, against Note 37/Note 39 subsidiary list, Annexure VI) 🟡 Watch — outside the Notes-to-Accounts strictly defined (Note 39 Business Combinations covers only Croatia/Tellyo/Argoid), flagged for completeness given direct relevance to Pass 1 Finding 6

The Prospectus (Corporate History section, not Note 39) discloses a further, smaller acquisition not captured in Note 39's Business Combinations disclosure: Amagi Media LLC (USA) acquired the assets and business of Streamwise LLC on November 14, 2022 for USD 0.35 mn (₹28.34 mn) — "the business as a going concern and the goodwill of Streamwise LLC." ### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. The AWS Technology Agreement commits ₹24,176.20 mn (USD 272.50 mn) over six years (May 2025-April 2031), of which only ₹5,500.64 mn is IPO-proceeds-funded. What is the annual minimum-spend cadence and take-or-pay penalty structure for the remainder, and how does planned revenue growth cover the balance from operating cash flow?
2. What is the pricing basis and independent fairness assessment (if any beyond the registered-valuer valuation tied to the CCPS bonus-share mechanism) for Vinculum Advisors LLP's acquisition of a 10.19%-10.60% stake at ₹25/share in July 2025, versus institutional cost bases disclosed elsewhere in the Prospectus at ₹21.45-₹172.16/share?
3. Given the India Holding Company has not reported a profit in any period of the five-period track record (including the Group's first-ever profitable half, H1 FY26), what is management's timeline and mechanism for the India entity to become standalone profitable, and how does this square with the position taken in the ongoing transfer-pricing dispute (that current intercompany pricing is arm's length)?
4. What is the specific remediation timeline (not boilerplate "steps are being taken" language) for the audit-trail and India-server-backup deficiencies flagged by the statutory auditor in all three of FY23, FY24 and FY25 — will FY26 be the first year with a clean Rule 11(g) report?
5. The falling unearned-revenue/contract-liability balance (₹1,124.50 mn → ₹767.95 mn, FY23 to Sep-25) alongside +70.8% revenue growth — is this a genuine shift toward usage-based/consumption billing (positive), or does it reflect softening forward bookings? What is the RPO/backlog trend that the company has elected not to disclose under the Ind AS 115 practical expedient?

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management indicator**: the recurring, identically-labelled "Fair value of additional equity shares issuable to shareholder" charge (₹80 mn in FY23, FY24 and FY25 alike) is presented as if one-time via a dedicated note but is in fact a mechanical output of the October 2024 CCPS bonus-ratchet conversion-ratio amendment tied to valuation milestones — not fraud, but a presentation choice that understates the recurring nature of a real P&L charge.
- **Aggressive/unusual accounting treatment**: the Croatia acquisition's "excess of consideration over net assets" (₹32.72 mn) was characterised as employment cost rather than goodwill — non-standard, though conservative in effect (expensed over 2 years, not capitalised and tested for impairment).
- **Undisclosed risk indicator (now resolved by Pass 3)**: the cloud-infrastructure commitment jump, standing alone in Note 42(a)(ii), gave no indication of contract term or structure; cross-referencing the wider Prospectus resolves this to a disclosed, contractual AWS Technology Agreement — the notes themselves, however, remain thin on this point in isolation, which is itself a disclosure-quality observation.
- **Governance/evidentiary reliability**: the three-year unremediated audit-trail deficiency, independently confirmed by the statutory auditor including an explicit inability to rule out tampering in some applications, is the most serious item in this category and is treated as the #1 finding.
- **RPT/pricing fairness**: the Vinculum Advisors LLP ₹25/share related-party stake increase pre-IPO remains unresolved as to fairness basis from the notes alone.

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices with a well-evidenced conservative core offset by real governance gaps. Key concern: a three-year unremediated, auditor-qualified audit-trail deficiency alongside an unresolved related-party share-pricing question pre-IPO. Key strength: revenue recognition, lease, deferred-tax and financial-instrument policies are conservative and disclosed with specific, non-boilerplate numbers, and Annexure VII confirms zero restated P&L/equity adjustments across all five periods. Overall accounting quality: 6/10.
