Management Commentary 
Commenting on the results, Mr. Tarun Sharma, Chairman & Managing Director, Credent Connect N Care 
Limited, said: 
"H1 FY27 has been a milestone half for Credent. We completed our listing on NSE Emerge in August and, in the same 
period, delivered consolidated revenue of ¥138.09 Crore and PAT of ¥14.65 Crore, growing 53.4% and 94.7% respectively 
over last year. Excluding a one-time tax credit, PAT grew 71.0% to ¥12.87 Crore. I thank our investors for the confidence 
they placed in us during the IPO. 
Our model is simple: e sit between India's diagnostic and healthcare institutions and the patient, and make sure every 
sample, every technician and every consignment reaches where it should, on time and in the right condition. As diagnostics 
move deeper into Tier 2 and Tier 3 India, that operating layer becomes more critical, and we are building it at scale. 
In the first half e deepened our presence across existing geographies and scaled the resources deployed at client sites. 
We held EBITDA margin steady at about 13% while scaling our workforce rapidly, and we expect EBITDA margins to 
improve in the second half as new deployments mature and route density increases. 
Looking ahead, we are confident of crossing ¥320 Crore in consolidated revenue in FY27 and reaching a PAT of 328 
Crore. H2 is seasonally stronger for us, and our order book and long-standing client relationships give us good visibility. 
Our focus remains on profitable growth, tighter working capital and steadily improving margins." 
Strategy for the Next Phase of Growth 
Credent will drive growth through five priorities: 
1. Geographic expansion: Extend the sample-logistics and phlebotomy network into new Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, 
adding branches and hub warehouses along existing diagnostic routes. 
2. Deeper wallet share with existing clients: Cross-sell workforce deployment, stationed lab teams and home 
collection to logistics clients, moving more relationships to multi-service contracts. 
3. New client segments: Grow with IVD companies, pharmaceutical firms and hospital chains to broaden the client 
base and reduce concentration. 
4. Technology-led productivity: Scale tracking, route optimisation and workforce-management systems (including 
the Alltrak Technologies platform) to lift output per person and service quality. 
5. Adjacent services and selective acquisitions: Build out corporate wellness and C3 Post courier aggregation, and 
evaluate tuck-in acquisitions that add geography or capability, as done with Credent Healthcare, Credent Team 
and Alltrak Technologies.

About Credent Connect N Care Limited 
Incorporated in 2015 as Credent Cold Chain Logistics Private Limited, Credent Connect N Care Limited is an integrated 
healthcare services and logistics provider. It offers temperature-controlled transport of patient samples to diagnostic 
laboratories, home sample collection through trained phlebotomists, on-site workforce deployment of lab technicians and 
paramedical staff, corporate wellness and technology-enabled operational support. Its clients include diagnostic 
laboratories, IVD companies, pharmaceutical firms, clinics and hospitals across India. Subsidiaries include Credent 
Healthcare Private Limited, Credent Team Private Limited and Alltrak Technologies Private Limited. The Company is 
headquartered in New Delhi and listed on NSE Emerge (symbol: CREDENT) since August 20, 2026.
