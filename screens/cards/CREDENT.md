## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** NOT FOUND. The corpus holds
no annual report, no result filing, no presentation and no transcript. The
business description was looked for in all four and none is held (MANIFEST.md).
The cost lines give the only clue. In FY23 employee cost was Rs 45.61 crore on
sales of Rs 59.32 crore, 76.9% of sales, and no raw material was booked
(Fin, lines 110, 111, 115). In FY26 raw material was Rs 65.36 crore on sales of
Rs 139.39 crore, 46.9%, and employee cost was 28.3% (Fin, lines 110, 111, 115).
The export shows no inventory in any year (Fin, line 161). That shape fits a
payroll-led service business that has added a large bought-in material or
pass-through line. It is an inference from cost lines, not a disclosure.

**What is changing?** The cost mix and the margin. EBITDA margin went from
5.7% in FY23 to 13.6% in FY26 (computed, Fin, lines 110 to 120). Revenue grew
70.4% in FY26 alone, to Rs 139.39 crore from Rs 81.82 crore (Fin, line 110).

**Why now?** FY26 profit after tax doubled to Rs 10.80 crore from Rs 5.53 crore
(Fin, line 123). The company raised equity and listed on NSE SME around this
period. Its first public results cycle closed on 28-Oct-2026 with the H1 FY27
filing, a presentation and an earnings call (MANIFEST.md, known gaps). None of
those are held.

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Profit must turn into cash.
FY26 operating cash flow was minus Rs 7.96 crore against profit of
Rs 10.80 crore (Fin, lines 123, 175). The operator notes that H1 FY27 operating
cash flow was also negative. That note is not verified against a filing.

**What breaks it?** Receivables. They grew to Rs 40.63 crore from
Rs 17.91 crore in FY26, up 127%, while sales grew 70% (Fin, lines 110, 160). If
slow collection is the nature of the customer base, the reported profit stays
on the balance sheet and the company keeps funding it with debt and new shares.
