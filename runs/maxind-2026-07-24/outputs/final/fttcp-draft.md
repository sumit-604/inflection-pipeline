## 3. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

What could make each transition fire, what the evidence is, what confirms it, what kills it.

Revenue is the strongest leg. The Noida Sector 150 partial occupancy certificate actually arrived on 27-May-2026 (documented), which unlocks possession on 340 sold units and about Rs150 Cr of collections plus a residual development-management fee. Estate 361 Phase 2 launched in June 2026 (documented) with a stated gross-sales target near Rs2,100 Cr over its life. AGEasy roughly doubled to about Rs77 Cr in FY26 (documented in results). Estate 360 is fully sold with development-management fee billed to date of about Rs45.6 Cr. What confirms it: two of the three named FY27 residence projects (Noida Sector 105, Bangalore, Dehradun) getting signed, and AGEasy holding its run rate. What kills it: another Chandigarh-style regulatory setback with no replacement, or AGEasy stalling.

Margin depends on two things maturing at once. AGEasy gross margin rose from about 32% to about 47% at the March exit and is guided to EBITDA breakeven by Q4 FY27. Care-home contribution margins are climbing centre by centre (Noida from minus 19% to plus 6%). What confirms it: the FY27 quarterly EBITDA loss continuing to narrow after stripping out lumpy DM fee. What kills it: the care-home maturity curve lengthening again (it already slipped from 4 and 8 quarters to 4 to 6 and 8 to 10 quarters), or AGEasy gross margin giving back its gains under China sourcing cost pressure.

Cash is the pressure point and the swing factor. The one real near-term catalyst is the Noida collection unlock of about Rs150 Cr, which is documented and materially extends runway. Everything structural rests on a Rs200 to Rs250 Cr fundraise that has slipped from a stated 6 to 9 month window to "sometime this year." What confirms it: the fundraise closing, and receivables growth falling back in line with revenue. What kills it: treasury sliding toward Rs40 Cr with no raise, which would turn a monitorable into forced dilution or a going-concern question.

ROCE has no 12-month catalyst. The recovery is a 2 to 3 year, segment-led story (care-home ROCE 25 to 26% at maturity, DM-fee annuity high return), and there is no group-level action that turns capital efficiency positive inside the window. This is a NONE-inside-the-window at the group level, with genuine but slow segment evidence beneath it.

Management intent-and-action check (Step 2E): on revenue and margin, vision is backed by real documented action (OC received, E361 launched, gross margin up, contribution margins turning), so the starting verdicts stand. On cash, the one-time OC unlock is documented action but the structural fix (the fundraise) is vision only, so cash is confirmed at STAGNANT and not loosened. On ROCE, action is real but early, so DECLINING is confirmed. The announcements folder is empty this run, so this check ran on concall, results and AR evidence only, which lowers confidence. Nothing here loosens a verdict; the cross-check confirms the conservative reads.
