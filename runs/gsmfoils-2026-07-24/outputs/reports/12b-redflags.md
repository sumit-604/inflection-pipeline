## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the transcripts alone)

Each item: what I found, anchor (call / speaker / page), and my read.

**IF-1. Ahmedabad utilisation guided ~50% by Mar-26, twice; delivered 25-30%.**
Nov 2025, Sagar to Priyanshu Jain, p4-5: "till March we are expecting at least around
50% of capacity utilization in Ahmedabad... 40% to 50% we are aiming till March."
Reaffirmed Feb 2026, to Priyanshu p4 ("aiming a utilization of around 50%... by end of
this year, that is March 26") and to Charu Manral p8 ("expecting to reach 50% by the
end of this year"). Outcome Apr 2026, to Deepak Poddar p4: "We have reached almost 25%
to 30% over there." A twice-stated target on the single largest capacity story, missed
by roughly half, never proactively explained and not pressed by any analyst.

**IF-2. Ahmedabad monthly revenue guided Rs10-12cr by Mar-26; delivered Rs5-5.5cr.**
Feb 2026, to Priyanshu p4: "around INR10 crores to INR12 crores monthly sale from
there... by end of this year." Outcome Apr 2026, to Deepak Poddar p4: "Around INR5,
INR5.5." A ~50-55% shortfall, unexplained.

**IF-3. Ahmedabad full-capacity monthly-revenue estimate inflated Rs25-27cr -> Rs30-35cr
between two consecutive quarters with no change in the 10,000 MT plant.**
Feb 2026, to Urmish p6: "INR10 crores to INR12 crores is 50%. So obviously 25 to 27
crores"; same call to Nilay p12: "even INR28-INR30 crores monthly run rate." Apr 2026,
to Deepak Poddar p3: "around INR30 crores to INR35 crores per month." The full-capacity
number rose ~25% in one quarter, coinciding with the quarter management first anchored
the Rs400-450cr FY27 headline — the larger denominator helps that headline cohere.

**IF-4. Vasai full-capacity monthly revenue crept up across all three calls.**
Nov 2025, to Manoj p9: "above 95%... from 20 crores... up to 25 to 26 crores." Feb 2026,
to Priyanshu p4: "100% capacity... INR25 crores to INR28 crores"; same call to Devesh
p10: "INR28 crores-INR30 crores will be easily generated." Apr 2026, to Deepak p3:
"already reached around INR25 crores to INR28 crores, still 10% more capacity... more
around INR6 to INR7 crores" (implied ceiling ~Rs31-35cr). A steadily rising ceiling on a
plant whose machines management repeatedly calls fully depreciated and unchanged.

**IF-5. FY27 Rs400-450cr headline appeared only in the third call, after two quarters of
explicit non-commitment.**
Nov 2025, Sandeep Dixit p11: "I won't guide you but... 60%-70% jump on a very lower
side" (vague). Feb 2026, Masood p8: "I'm not fully sure to commit it." Apr 2026, Abhi
Jain p8: "even if we do a very mediocre or average business... around INR400 crores to
INR450 crores," confirmed FY27 to Sandeep Dixit p13. The single largest number on the
register, anchored only once, only after FY26 outperformance gave cover, and framed as
a "mediocre year" floor when on the Q4 exit run-rate (~Rs27cr/month) it in fact requires
25-40% growth.

**IF-6. Lamitubes / Alu-Alu backward integration reversed without acknowledgment.**
Nov 2025, Shivam Kumar p13: "once this Ahmedabad plant is completely set up then we will
set up a plant in Gujarat for Lamitubes... another 6-8 months." Apr 2026, Darshil p9:
"We are looking at forward integration only, sir. We are not looking at backward
integration currently." A stated strategic plan dropped and inverted with no bridge.

**IF-7. Operating cash flow deflected in every call it was raised (2+ quarters).**
Nov 2025, Ayush Chaturvedi p9-10: "the cash flow is negative... not that worried...
Over 18-20 months? — Yes, definitely, 100%." Feb 2026, Urmish Shah p6-7: "we don't
normally look at cash flows... currently we're not worried about cash flow being
negative." A metric never given a number or a firm date, reframed each time as
structural rather than managed.

**IF-8. Receivables spiked to ~Rs94cr in Q4 FY26; management chose to extend credit
leniency in a no-contract business.**
Apr 2026, Deepak Poddar p4-5: war-related LC/dollar payment delays; "we had to let go
for them"; "This INR94 crores around INR30 crores, INR40 crores have already been
received." A discretionary WC decision under commercial pressure, validating the
balance-sheet-fragility question analysts had raised since Nov 2025 (Abhi Chaudhary p6).

**IF-9. Employee cost near-halved YoY (Rs10.8cr -> Rs6.9cr) during a revenue doubling and
new-plant staffing; management could not explain it live.**
Apr 2026, Subhanu Bangal p6: "last year Q4 I see our employee cost almost INR10.8
crores, but this year only INR6.9 crores?" — "Then give me some time, sir... Maybe the
salary part of all the directors this year we haven't taken that much... I will check
and get back to you." The speculative "directors' salary not taken" points at a possible
margin-flattering item, directly relevant to the operating-leverage margin narrative.

**IF-10. New Mumbai/Vasai unit capex promised as imminent, then slipped.**
Feb 2026, promised repeatedly: Masood p8 "finalized within a month or two... by the end
of this quarter"; Priyanshu p9 "by the end of this month... good news"; Bhaskar p10 "by
the end of this March, I will let you know." Apr 2026, Darshil p8: "things are not fully
concentrated here for me to comment... with a quarter or two we may come up with a
announcement." Firm near-term commitment quietly rolled forward.

**IF-11. Vasai machine-speed capex (Rs12-15L) slipped ~3 months, then vanished.**
Nov 2025, Manoj p8-9: framed as this-month/within-the-quarter. Feb 2026, Devesh Rathi
p9: "is that done or is it in process? — No, this is planned for the current month, sir."
Apr 2026: not referenced; reappears only as "few here and there spares" for +20% (p3).

**IF-12. "Top 3 in Tier 2" claim sits in direct tension with "no entry barrier" admission.**
Feb 2026, Anshal Jain p14: "specifically Tier 2, then we are among the top 3 currently...
almost 15 to 20 people." Apr 2026, Darshil p9: "there is no entry barrier in this thing.
To get entry is really easy." The moat claim and the moat denial are one quarter apart.

**IF-13. ROC ~46% -> "50% mark" target stated once, then dropped.**
Nov 2025, Ayush Chaturvedi p9: "our ROC is at around 46%... We could be landing in the
50% mark. — Definitely." Never re-quantified in Feb or Apr.

**IF-14. Export via merchant exporters volunteered as underperforming, then dropped.**
Feb 2026, Vishal Vaya p13: "Export market... We're not getting that much positive
response of lately... Yemen, Vietnam, Europe, US." Not mentioned again in Apr.

**IF-15. Margin-expansion quality: a material part of the 7% -> 11.5% margin build is
cyclical inventory gain on rising aluminium, which management concedes and downplays.**
Nov 2025, Ayush p8: "some is because of the increasing price of aluminium... A little
bit of hedging benefit has been passed on to us." Same call, Sandeep Dixit p9-10
explicitly presses that inventory gains reverse if prices fall — Sagar downplays it as
"cherry on cake, sir." Apr 2026, Harshit Khadka p11: "In this rising trend, the
inventory gain also comes into picture." The reported EBITDA margin the valuation
extrapolates is partly a function of the aluminium price trajectory, not pure operating
leverage, and reverses on a downcycle. This is an earnings-quality flag, distinct from
the Q4 compression event.

**IF-16. Within a single call (Feb 2026) management gave different figures to different
analysts for the same items.**
Ahmedabad Mar-26 target: Rs10-12cr to Priyanshu p4 vs Rs12-15cr to Nilay p12. Vasai
full: Rs25-28cr to Priyanshu p4 vs Rs28-30cr to Devesh p10. Ahmedabad full: Rs25-27cr to
Urmish p6 vs Rs28-30cr to Nilay p12. Each gap is small, but the pattern signals loose,
un-reconciled quantification of the exact numbers the model relies on.

**IF-17. Capacity being doubled with zero order visibility / no contracts.**
Apr 2026, Atharva Kulkarni p12: "do we have any LOIs in place because we are adding a
capacity almost 2x... order visibility? — there is no such contract or orders... no
specific contract or order visibility, but we were quite confident." A demand-risk flag,
not just a structural feature, given the size of the add.

**IF-18. "No other risk... money doesn't sink" over-reassurance, delivered right after a
receivables shock.**
Apr 2026, Abhi Jain p8: "the only risk... is if you are not able to manage your working
capital... in the pharma industry, money doesn't sink. It comes late... There is no
other risk in this thing." Blanket dismissal of aluminium volatility, competition,
concentration and no-contract risk, in the same call as the Rs94cr receivables stretch.

## PART 4 — CONSOLIDATED FINDINGS

- **MAJOR — Earnings-quality flag missed (IF-15).** B05 treats the aluminium cycle only
  as an external excuse for the Q4 margin dip; it does not flag that the *historical*
  margin build (7% -> 11.5%) is partly cyclical inventory gain that reverses on an
  aluminium downcycle, per management's own words ("cherry on cake," Nov p10; "inventory
  gain also comes into picture," Apr p11). This is thesis-relevant because trigger #4 and
  the valuation extrapolate an ~11.5% margin without discounting its cyclical component.
- **MINOR — Employee-cost anomaly under-weighted (IF-9).** Rated Low-Medium; the
  management-volunteered "directors' salary not taken" thread ties a possible
  margin-flattering item to the operating-leverage story and arguably warrants Medium.
- **MINOR — Within-call number inconsistencies not surfaced (IF-16).** Feb 2026 gave
  Rs10-12 vs Rs12-15cr (Ahmedabad), Rs25-28 vs Rs28-30cr (Vasai), Rs25-27 vs Rs28-30cr
  (Ahmedabad full) to different callers; B05 flags cross-call drift but not this.
- **MINOR — 2x-capacity-on-zero-visibility not weighted (IF-17).** Structural no-contract
  feature is noted but the specific demand risk of doubling capacity with no order book
  (Atharva Kulkarni, Apr p12) is not treated as a flag.

No CRITICAL: the one repeated evasion of thesis weight (CFO trajectory, 2+ quarters) was
caught by B05, so it does not escalate. No NOT-SUPPORTED pipeline flags.

### Credibility grade
B05 grades management **C**. I concur. Strong, verifiable topline delivery (FY26 beat)
and genuinely candid moat/entry-barrier admissions hold the floor up; the twice-missed
Ahmedabad ramp, three quietly dropped items (Lamitubes, ROC target, export), serial CFO
deflection, an unexplained employee-cost swing, and margin quality partly cyclical keep
it from B. C is fair — arguably a touch generous, but not below C.
