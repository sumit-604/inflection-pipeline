### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Puretrop buys fruit at one plant near Nashik, processes it, and sells it, mostly for export. It also earns income on a large investment book.
2. Q2 sales were Rs 26.41 Cr, 1.8% below a year ago. Sales have stayed between Rs 26 Cr and Rs 28 Cr for four readable quarters.
3. Profit before tax and before other income fell 57%, to Rs 1.59 Cr. Other expenses nearly doubled to Rs 8.10 Cr. The filing gives no reason.
4. Reported profit fell only 10%. Tax was 7.1% of profit, against about 25% normally. Treasury income also rose.
5. The half year looks strong because Q1 last year was weak. Half-year profit doubled to Rs 6.95 Cr.
6. The fruit margin rose about 10 points in the half. In the same half, the company built 14.6% more stock than last year on flat sales.
7. Part of that margin gain may sit in unsold stock. Stock is Rs 52.91 Cr against Rs 20.23 Cr in March. The March 2027 balance sheet settles it.
8. Operations used Rs 9.92 Cr of cash in the half. Without a deposit release, they used Rs 15.44 Cr.
9. In Q1 the company bought back 13.8% of its shares. The price was about Rs 200, which the filing does not state. That is 22 times last year's continuing earnings per share, at the 52-week high.
10. It paid by selling investments and then borrowed Rs 12.00 Cr short term. After the lost treasury income, earnings per share hardly change.
11. Last year's headline earnings per share of Rs 31.73 is 71% from a business it has exited. The continuing figure is Rs 9.11. Do not use Rs 31.73 as a base.
12. The notes explain none of the rename, the exited business, the buy-back or the cost jump. Watch Q3 profit before other income against the Q3 FY26 column, due by 14-Feb-2027.
