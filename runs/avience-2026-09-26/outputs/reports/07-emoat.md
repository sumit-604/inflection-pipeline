### 2C Growth embedded in capex: arithmetic

- Historical fixed-asset turnover (FY26): Revenue from Operations ₹52.51 Cr / net PPE ₹15.92 Cr (Inv. Pres. slide 27, consolidated) = **3.30x**.
- Capex under execution (RHP total project cost, the incremental YEIDA investment): ₹30.18 Cr.
- Implied incremental revenue at the historical turnover ratio: ₹30.18 Cr × 3.30 = **₹99.6 Cr**.
- As a percentage above current (FY26) revenue: (₹99.6 Cr / ₹52.51 Cr) − 1 ≈ **+90% above current revenue**.
- **capex_embedded_growth_pct = 90** (rounded; arithmetic method, 📄 turnover inputs from the deck's own balance sheet slide).
- 🔍 ANALYST INFERENCE and flag: this fixed-asset-turnover-derived figure (+90%) is materially BELOW management's own "peak capacity" framing of ₹250-265 Cr (+376% to +405% over FY26 revenue, call p.206). The gap is explained by FY27's guided 15-20% utilisation of the new plant: full nameplate capacity is a multi-year, back-half-loaded outcome, not a near-term one. Both figures should be carried to Stage 11/FTTCP with this reconciliation attached, not just the larger management number.
