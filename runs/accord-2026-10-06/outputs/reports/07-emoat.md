Block value uses the productive basis at the latest turnover: 169%. Read it as an arithmetic ceiling, not a forecast, for three reasons. (a) Net PPE of Rs 7.70 Cr supports a plant that sells Rs 70-79 Cr, so the turnover is a job-shop ratio; the expansion adds EHV and heavy lines that need more capital per rupee of sales (🔍). (b) 54% of the productive capex is civil work (7.00 of 13.03) and earns nothing until the machinery lands. (c) The machinery has no purchase order. The two readings: the asset-light turnover holds and Rs 119-146 Cr of capacity arrives by FY28, or EHV heavy lines cut turnover to a fraction and the new plant adds far less. The observation that separates them: machinery purchase orders with values, and the stated MVA of the new plant. Neither exists in the corpus. Revenue from this capex cannot start before about Jul-2027 (🔍).
