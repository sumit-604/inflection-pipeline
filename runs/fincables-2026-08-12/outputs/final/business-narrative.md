Debt free cable maker, unproven fibre transition

Finolex Cables is a Pune based wires and cables maker, listed since the 1990s, now run by professional management after a six year promoter family control fight. FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs 6,321 crore. Electrical wires and cables are 87% of that, communication cables 8%, and FMEG covering fans, lighting and switchgear 4%. The company is effectively debt free, with Rs 0.86 crore of borrowings against Rs 5,099 crore of equity. It also sits on a treasury and investment book above Rs 2,600 crore, plus a 32% stake in associate Finolex Industries whose market value runs many times its carrying cost.

The transition on offer is backward integration into optical fibre. Finolex commissioned its own preform plant in mid March 2026 and is building fibre draw capacity toward 8 million km. It runs solar cable lines at 80 to 85% use and is doubling that capacity. Its extra high voltage cable JV with Sumitomo turned profitable for the first time in FY26, on a thin 4.6% margin, carrying a roughly Rs 380 crore order book. The large treasury and the associate stake mean the operating business and the non operating value have to be read apart.

The quality read is average, not transitional. Gate 0 scores core quality 42 out of 100, classes the moat as NONE with zero of twelve moat tests passed, and the forward looking emerging moat reaches only 22, inside a MODEST band. ROCE fell from 23.7% in FY17 to 14.2% in FY26. EBITDA margin compressed from 16.2% to 9.8% across the same window. This is not the average now, expanding later setup the strategy hunts. The forward moat reaches MODEST and stops there.

FY26 cash conversion broke. Standalone operating cash flow fell to Rs 49 crore, 7.9% of profit, from 38.1% a year earlier. The whole drop is a working capital build: inventory rose Rs 306 crore and receivables Rs 128 crore, while operating profit before working capital changes rose 21.3% to Rs 640 crore. Management attributes the inventory to Middle East raw material pre buying. The Q1 FY27 filing carries no cash flow statement, so the reversal is unconfirmed. Q1 FY27 standalone revenue did rise 44% year on year and PAT 59%.

Management earns a credibility grade of C. It delivered on solar, electron beam cable, exports and the Sumitomo turnaround. It also slipped its preform and fibre draw timelines by five to six months across four straight quarters, narrowed rather than widened its candor on the promoter dispute, and never addressed the receivables build in any of the four calls.

Peers complicate the fibre story rather than confirm it. The supplied peer set of KEI, RR Kabel and Paracables carries no optical fibre exposure, so Finolex's OFC price and market share claims cannot be checked against them. Peers do corroborate the FY26 price hike cadence and the 11 to 12% wires margin band. None contradicts a Finolex claim outright.

Market runway is the STRONG class. India wires, cables, communication and narrow FMEG give a realistic addressable market near Rs 1.35 lakh crore, growing about 11.5% a year. Finolex holds about 7.4% of its serviceable market, leaving roughly 13.5x revenue headroom. Two deep pocketed entrants, the Aditya Birla cement group and Adani, are entering the core wires business that funds the fibre capex, on management's own timeline around CY2027.
