Gurugram  (Haryana ), 8th November 2026 – South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (SWPEL), an 
Integrated Service Provider providing end -to-end Drilling & exploration of Natural resources announced 
its Financial  Results for the Quarter 2 & H1 ended 30th Sep 2026.

Highlights  
 Revenue from operations grew 16% YoY in H1 FY27. Q2 execution was impacted by heavy 
monsoons and scheduled equipment maintenance; these temporary disruptions are 
expected to ease, supporting improved H2 momentum. 
 PAT in H1 FY 27 grew 23% Y on Y to 133 Mn despite near term operational challenges. 
 Q2 PAT was impacted by lower operating revenue, higher diesel prices and other input 
costs. Management views these as  cost-related challenges, rather than concerns with the 
underlying business fundamentals or growth prospects. 
 H1 FY27 comprehensive income, including PAT and OCI, increased to INR 201 Mn from INR 
108 Mn in the corresponding period last year. 
 Funding for planned capex has been secured, with new drilling rigs and equipment expected 
in Q3 FY27. Their deployment is expected to enhance execution capacity, accelerate

 Order book reached an a ll-time high of INR 7,671 Mn, providing strong revenue visibility.

 Exploration has been completed for Jharkhand Coal Block and the Geological  Report

Commenting  on the results,  Mr. Vikas  Jain,  Chairman  & Managing  Director  said,  
 
“Management remains optimistic about the Company's growth prospects for H2 FY 2026–27, supported by its record 
order book of INR 768 crore, planned capacity expansion and encouraging developments across domestic and 
international operations.” 
 
Mr. Vikas Jain explained that “The expected delivery and deployment of new drilling rigs and equipment during Q3 
should significantly enhance execution capabilities, accelerate mobilisation of pending work orders and support 
stronger revenue generation and operating profitability in the second half. With monsoon-related disruptions largely 
behind us, we anticipate improved equipment utilisation and operating efficiencies.” 
 
Mr. Jain, went on to say that “Jharkhand coal block represents an important long-term growth opportunity. 
Following successful completion and approval of the Geological Report, the forthcoming submission of the mining 
plan will mark another key step towards advancing the project.” 
 
Mr. Jain, further added that “Internationally, the improving financial performance of our first Oman JV and 
encouraging copper-gold exploration results from the second JV reinforce our confidence in the region's growth 
potential. The planned surface geophysical surveys and subsequent drilling activities are expected to provide further 
clarity on the mineral prospects. 
With enhanced execution capacity, a strong order pipeline, improving international operations and strategic mineral 
assets, management remains confident of delivering stronger operational performance in H2 while building a 
sustainable platform for long-term.” Mr. Jain stated. 
 
About  South  West Pinnacle  Exploration  Limited  
 
SWPEL is an Integrated Service Provider, providing end-to-end drilling & exploration of natural resources viz. 
Coal, Ferrous, Non-Ferrous & Atomic Minerals and Conventional & non - conventional Oil & Gas and ground 
water investigation. SWPEL’s expertise also extends to carry out explorat ion using technological tools like 
surface geographical investigation, downhole geophysics, 2D/3D Seismic and Passive Seismic Tomography etc.

Company has won a coal block in the state of Jharkhand, spread across 266 Hectares, having Geological 
Reserves of over 84 million tons. It has signed a Coal Mine Development and Production Agreement with the  
ministry of Coal, Government of India. The Company has been recently notified as an accredited prospecting  
agency by Ministry of Coal, Government of India for carrying out prospecting operations for the exploration of 
coal and Lignite. Accordingly, exploration activities at the Coal Block in Jharkhand have since been completed 
and GR has been submitted and approved. The mining plan is presently under preparation and expected to be 
submitted soon. Other mine development activities will be undertaken on fast track mode simultaneously. 
 
Further, Company is having two JVs in Oman. The First one is imparting mining services pursuant to a long term 
mining contract for Copper and Gold in Oman besides carrying out exploration and drilling services through a 
joint venture, namely, Alara Resources LLC., Oman. The second JV, formed recently has been allocated a mining 
block by Sultanate of Oman wherein the exploration activi ties are in progress. After completing air borne 
survey (a latest technique for faster exploration in a large mining block), 12 prospects have been identified for 
further exploration. 
 
The Company has successfully completed more than 166 projects during 20  years of journey since inception 
for most of the leading Government & Private Organizations and presently operating 20 projects on Pan India 
basis, in addition to JV Projects in Oman. SWPEL has completed approximately 3.3 million meters of drilling, 
6.5 Lac meter of Geo physical logging 515 sq.km. of 3 D Seismic surveys, 411 LKM of 2 D seismic Survey for 
exploration of Coal, Mineral, Oil and Gas using 47 state of art drilling rigs having varied capacities to drill upto 
2500 Meters depth and other equipment.
