## Chronological updates (late Feb 2026 to 24 Aug 2026)

- 22 Apr 2026 — Board approved a USD 70m corporate guarantee to lenders of
  Man International Steel Industries Company (MISIC), the wholly-owned Saudi
  subsidiary, to support its Saudi expansion borrowing. Treated as a
  contingent liability; "no impact at this point" on standalone MIIL.

- 21 May 2026 — MISIC completed 100% acquisition of National Pipe Company
  (NPC), Saudi Arabia, for ~USD 102m (~Rs 1,000 cr). NPC: ~430,000 MTPA
  HSAW+LSAW at Dhahran/Dammam; profit-making, debt-free, cash-rich; customers
  incl. Saudi Aramco, SWCC/SWA, KOC, Qatar Petroleum, and EPCs (McDermott,
  L&T, Saipem, Subsea7, Hyundai E&C). Post-deal combined capacity ~1.6 Mn MTPA.

- 25 May 2026 — FY26 results press release. Standalone EBITDA margin 14.0%,
  PAT margin 5.6% (record); consolidated EBITDA 13.0%, PAT 4.7%. Standalone
  order book ~Rs 3,000 cr (6-12m execution). Jammu SS seamless plant 22,000
  MTPA, ~Rs 340 cr FY26 capex, commercial production ~Mar 2027, revenue from
  FY28. Merino Shelters real-estate: full CC for ~20 lakh sq ft, launch guided
  June 2026. FY27 consolidated guidance Rs 5,000-5,500 cr revenue, 13-15%
  EBITDA (excludes Merino Shelters).

- 26 May 2026 — Q4 FY26 concall. NPC funded USD 70m debt (NPC level) + USD 32m
  internal accruals; bought at ~1.5x EV/EBITDA (vs Saudi listed peers 7-10x),
  EPS accretive Day 1; NPC CY25 ~Rs 2,000 cr revenue, ~25% EBITDA (per rating
  report). Dammam coating + double-jointing: ~USD 40m capex, ~4m sq m/yr
  (3LPE/FBE/internal), commissioning ~Mar 2027, expected 25-35% EBITDA. Jammu
  ~Rs 600 cr capex (Rs 389 cr term loan + equity/ICDs), FY28 utilisation
  ~35-40%. Bid book "almost Rs 15,000-16,000 cr" (later Rs 24,000 cr in Q1).

- 1 Jun 2026 — CRISIL rating rationale. NPC expected to lift scale 50-60% and
  group OPBDIT margin to ~14-15% (vs 12.3% FY26), combined capacity ~1.6 Mn
  MTPA. India SAW ~1.2 Mn MTPA + ERW 175,000 MT. Jammu SS 20,000 MT expected
  completion ~Sep 2027 (more conservative than company's Mar 2027). FY26
  working-capital spike from Middle East conflict / UAE shipping blockade,
  mitigated by rerouting to Fujairah. Order book ~Rs 3,000 cr (6-8m execution).

- 18 Jun 2026 — New orders ~Rs 1,000 cr: MIIL India ~Rs 300 cr + NPC ~Rs 700
  cr, execution 6-9m. Consolidated unexecuted order book ~Rs 4,100 cr after.

- 12 Aug 2026 — Q1 FY27 earnings deck. Group capacity >1.6 Mn MTPA across
  LSAW/HSAW/ERW/coating; Anjar (Gujarat), Pithampur (MP), NPC (Saudi); 10
  lines, 20,000+ km supplied, 30+ countries. NPC "only KSA company with both
  LSAW and HSAW". Dammam coating start Mar 2027. Jammu SS: Rs 350 cr spent of
  ~Rs 600 cr, start Mar 2027, targeting chemical/defence/marine/nuclear/power/
  refinery (import substitution). Merino Shelters: launch guided mid-Sep 2026,
  Rs 35-50 cr FY27 cashflow, Rs 70-80 cr/yr from FY28, Rs 700-800 cr over 5-6
  yrs. Q1 FY27 consolidated total income Rs 1,065 cr, EBITDA Rs 155 cr, 14.6%.

- 12 Aug 2026 — Q1 FY27 concall (NOT in corpus). Consolidated order book
  ~Rs 3,600 cr; bid pipeline ~Rs 24,000 cr (~70% MENA, ~35-40% water). NPC
  contributed only 40 days in Q1 (~Rs 43 cr); Q2 run-rate target Rs 300-500
  cr/qtr; NPC EBITDA ~21% now, target 15-18%. India utilisation ~50-60%
  (order-mix constrained, not capacity). ~80-85% of India tonnage is 3LPE
  coated. FY27 consolidated revenue reaffirmed ~Rs 5,000 cr (India ~Rs 3,800
  cr, NPC ~Rs 1,200-1,500 cr), 13-15% EBITDA (called conservative). FY28
  guided 25-30% revenue growth. ~20-25% of volumes exposed to Strait of Hormuz.
