## OPERATOR OVERRIDES

**Override 1: Cash conversion, DECLINING to STAGNANT.**
- Draft determination: cash conversion DECLINING; the draft also computed a lease adjusted conversion of about 0.19x by deducting lease principal repayments from operating cash flow.
- Operator ruling: the buy hardware upfront and lease it out model is a legitimate rent model, not a cash quality defect.
- Operator's words: "They buy hardware upfront and then they put it on lease. Is that not the business model of even E2E or NetWeb also? Most of the AI related companies buy the GPU upfront and then lease it in the form of cloud. This is like a rent model, so yes, initial capex is higher, but certain revenue is also there."
- Analyst response and reasoning: conceded. The framework's own SOTP rule for Ind AS 116 finance lease and build own operate components says do not apply the cash multiplier penalty to that portion, because a structural cash lag that is the business model is not a quality defect and penalizing it is double counting. The 0.19x figure was withdrawn. The narrowed residual concern (core systems integration receivables at 154 days with a frozen provision on a widening overdue tail) was retained, because that pool is milestone billed project work, not the annuity book. Net effect: cash moves from DECLINING (minus 1) to STAGNANT (0), and the Pillar 2 cash multiplier is neutral 1.00x rather than a penalty. Falsifier retained: debtor days above 160 with the overdue tail still widening turns this back toward DECLINING.

**Override 2: Capital efficiency, DECLINING to RECOVERING.**
- Draft determination: ROCE DECLINING, on the mechanical rule that TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED requires a fall above 500 basis points in twelve months and the fall was 411.
- Operator ruling: ROCE is depressed because the new, better businesses are capital front loaded and have not yet earned, not because the business is deteriorating.
- Operator's words: "New businesses are not capital efficient. There are supposed to be better businesses, so why is ROCE not increasing?" and "Is there any idle cash sitting with the company? Is it depressing the ROCE?"
- Analyst response and reasoning: idle cash was tested and rejected as the depressor (Rs12.6cr genuinely liquid; Rs98.6cr is margin money against the guarantee book). The decline is real but its cause is identified and temporary: capital employed grew 43.9% in FY26 against 26.7% EBIT growth, fixed assets went from Rs9cr to Rs158cr for the as a service build, and management stated the revenue on that capital "will come over a period of time." From the FY24 peak of 39.59% the fall is 942 basis points with an identified temporary cause, which is the substance of a recovering verdict even though the strict twelve month trigger was a hair short. Recorded as RECOVERING (+1). ROCE recovery is credited via Pillar 1 only; the Strategic Premium ROCE route stays barred (single credit rule). Open evidence gap flagged for the devil's advocate: management gave no dated go live for the RBI order and no internal rate of return for the annuity book on either call, so the recovery is strong in logic and undated in evidence.

**Override 3: Sector cap row, 25x to 30x.**
- Draft determination: Cybersecurity / VAD 25x, on the current revenue mix (value added reselling, integration, services).
- Operator ruling: the cap is too low for a high growth, modern technology business with strong ROCE.
- Operator's words: "Why are we capping it at 25 if the revenue growth is so high and it is a modern technology based business with strong ROCE?" and "I think 30x is good enough on a trailing basis."
- Analyst response and reasoning: clarified that growth and ROCE are paid in Pillars 3 and 1, not in the cap, and that the legitimate lever is the row. The manifest's 45x SaaS row stays rejected (10% EBITDA, hardware heavy). The Data centres and cloud infrastructure, capital heavy row at 30x is a better fit than 25x, because data centre and cloud is the largest and fastest growing segment at 34% of revenue and the business is now genuinely capital heavy. Moved to 30x. Noted that even with no cap the four pillars only reach the low 30s on current inputs, so the true ceiling is set by fundamentals, not the lid.

**Override 4: Earnings basis, TRAILING.**
- Operator ruling: apply the destination multiple to trailing earnings.
- Operator's words: "30x is good enough on a trailing basis."
- Reasoning recorded: trailing fits a business whose most recent quarter compressed 290 basis points and whose cash conversion is unproven, avoiding capitalizing a margin recovery that has not printed.
