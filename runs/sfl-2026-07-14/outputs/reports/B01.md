**Methodology notes (apply throughout, restated once here):**
- EBIT = PBT + Interest (screener CSV carries no standalone EBIT/ROCE field; computed).
- ROCE = EBIT ÷ Capital Employed, where Capital Employed = Net Worth (Equity Share Capital + Reserves) + Borrowings. The CSV does not break Total Liabilities into current vs non-current, so the strict "Total Assets − Current Liabilities" formula cannot be built from this data; Net Worth + Borrowings is the best available proxy and is used consistently across all 10 years and all peers. **Computed, not screener-native.**
- EBITDA = EBIT + Depreciation.
- Capex proxy = Δ(Net Block + CWIP) + current-year Depreciation (CSV has no itemized "purchase of fixed assets" cash-flow line). FY2017 capex is N/A (no FY2016 base for the delta). **FY2024 capex/FCF is marked N/A**: the Δ(Net Block+CWIP) jump that year (+Rs 2,141.7 Cr) is dominated by the Kurlon/House of Kieraya-Furlenco acquisition consolidating onto the balance sheet, not organic capex, and the CSV does not itemize the cash-flow statement enough to strip the acquisition out. Using the raw delta would conflate acquisition spend with capex, which the formula explicitly excludes — so FY2024 is excluded from FCF-based metrics rather than estimated.
- Working Capital Days requires Trade Payables, which this CSV does not carry as a separate line ("Other Liabilities" is a combined current+non-current bucket including payables, provisions, and possibly deferred tax). Payable Days, and therefore full WC Days, are **N/A (not in provided data)** — B4 and M12 score 0 on this basis, not estimated.
- Current Ratio (D4) is **N/A**: the CSV does not split current vs non-current assets or liabilities (Net Block/CWIP/Investments/Other Assets on the asset side; Borrowings/Other Liabilities on the liability side, with no current/non-current split).
- Block E (Shareholder Alignment) is **entirely N/A**: this CSV carries no shareholding-pattern, promoter-holding, pledge, or contingent-liability data of any kind.

## HISTORY / TRANSITION READ (FLAG-GATE0)

Gate 0 is a mechanical, backward-looking scorecard and the AVOID classification is substantially a function of the FY2024 Kurlon/House of Kieraya-Furlenco acquisition and its integration trough, not a decade-long structural decline:

- Borrowings jumped from Rs 571.10 Cr (FY2023) to Rs 1,493.11 Cr (FY2024) — screener-data — driving Capital Employed from Rs 2,169.28 Cr to Rs 4,413.30 Cr in one year and mechanically collapsing computed ROCE from 13.56% (FY2023) to 7.35% (FY2024) to 5.00% (FY2025, the cycle trough).
- Investing cash flow was −Rs 2,258.49 Cr in FY2024 (screener-data), consistent with a large acquisition outlay; this is also why FY2024 capex/FCF was excluded rather than estimated (see methodology).
- Net profit shows a clear cycle: peak FY2021 Rs 237.72 Cr → trough FY2025 Rs 89.35 Cr → recovery FY2026 Rs 159.61 Cr (+78.6% YoY) (all screener-data).
- Deleveraging is already visible: Borrowings fell from Rs 1,493.11 Cr (FY2024) to Rs 907.98 Cr (FY2026), a 39.2% reduction (screener-data), while FY2026 CFO/PAT conversion strengthened to 2.56x and FCF turned solidly positive (+Rs 212.49 Cr).
- A1 (median ROCE, 19.38%) and A3 (median ROE, 18.51%) — both computed across the full 10-year window including the pre-acquisition years — remain in respectable 3–4 score bands; it is specifically A2 (single-year minimum, FY2025 trough) and A4 (endpoint-to-endpoint trend, FY2017 pre-expansion vs FY2026 post-acquisition) that collapse Block A, and C1/C2 (long-window CAGRs) that collapse Block C.

This does not change the classification (Gate 0 rules do not permit qualitative override at this stage), but it is flagged per FLAG-GATE0 for downstream stages (Section 1B / stage 11 valuation, stage 13 synthesis) to weigh explicitly: the depressors are concentrated in FY2024–FY2025 and show initial reversal in FY2026, consistent with an acquisition-integration trough rather than terminal structural decay.
