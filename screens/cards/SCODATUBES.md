## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Scoda makes stainless steel seamless and welded tubes. Buyers use them in heat exchangers, boilers, oil and gas, and fertiliser plants. It holds vendor approvals from BHEL, EIL, IFFCO, NTPC, HPCL, DAE and Indian Railways units, and serves 349 clients (Q1 FY27 investor presentation, doc 033c51c0, pages 11 to 16, full page). It earns a conversion spread over nickel steel input. FY26 revenue was Rs 518.7 crore, EBITDA Rs 76.2 crore at 14.7%, PAT Rs 38.8 crore (doc 033c51c0, page 10, full page).

**What is changing?** Two things. The company now pierces its own mother hollow, the input tube, at 20,000 MTPA (doc 033c51c0, page 10, full page). Captive use is 70% and management targets 100% by FY28 (Q4 FY26 call, doc 46b739c5, page 3). Second, exports are rising: Rs 179.5 crore in FY26, up 39% (FY26 investor presentation, doc 1f4fc506, page 6).

**Why now?** The IPO raised Rs 220 crore in 2025 (doc 033c51c0, page 11, full page). Net debt to equity fell to 0.3x from 1.1x (doc 1f4fc506, page 5). Q1 FY27 exports rose 81.5% to Rs 57.9 crore (doc 033c51c0, page 8, full page).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Management must deliver its FY27 guide: "we are expecting a 25% revenue growth with 14% to 15% EBITDA margins" (doc 46b739c5, page 2). The welded line must start. The approvals must stay current.

**What breaks it?** Margin. FY26 missed its own 15% to 16% guide (Q4 FY25 call, doc 46cf27b5, page 12). Q1 FY27 margin fell to 12.9% and PAT fell 25.9% (doc 033c51c0, page 8, full page). If the squeeze comes from the input price, the company is a price taker and the climb is narrative.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Capacity: seamless 20,068 MTPA, mother hollow 20,000 MTPA, welded 1,020 MTPA (doc 033c51c0, page 10, full page). Seamless utilisation was 71.17% in FY24 and 79.01% in 9M FY25 (RHP, doc 70f9844b, page 220). Seamless market share was "around between 3% to 4%" (doc 46cf27b5, page 8).

Q1 FY27 mix: domestic Rs 66.5 crore, up 1.5%; exports Rs 57.9 crore (doc 033c51c0, page 8, full page). Export share rose to 47% from 33% (derived: 57.9 / 124.3; 31.9 / 97.4).

The engine: approved tube sold to spec, with the spread widened by captive input and solar power.

**Archetype: Build-to-spec component maker** (library name). The binding variables are customer capex cycle, approval pipeline, content per order, and input pass through. The FROM rung is a converter rung; no spot year ROCE is used on this card.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming. The approvals are the moat. BHEL approved in 2014, 2017 and 2018; EIL in 2018; NTPC in 2022 (doc 033c51c0, pages 11 to 16, full page). A new entrant waits years for the same list. Export certificates (PED, AD2000, DNV) open Europe.

Against that, pass through lags 3 to 4 months (doc 033c51c0, page 6). Pricing power is not shown. Cost advantage is forming through captive hollow and solar. Solar saving: Rs 8.63 crore a year (doc 46b739c5, page 3). Verdict: **moat forming**, resting on approvals.
