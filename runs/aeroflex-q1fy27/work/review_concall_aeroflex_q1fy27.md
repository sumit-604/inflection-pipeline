### 4C. The three most thesis-affecting exchanges

**Exchange 1 — Skid ASP decline (T06, Shobi Gupta, Trinetra; extended at T08).** Q: what is driving the decline in average skid price, is it competition? A: "technically the price has not declined because the value per skid assembly depends on the design... floor to floor... we have supplied skids worth Rs 1 lakh and skids worth Rs 5 lakh... it does not impact our margins because the costing for every skid is done separately." What management said: the falling reported ASP is a mix artifact of tailor-made per-floor designs, costed individually, margin-neutral. What management did NOT say: the exact skid margin (declined at T08 as "blended, same as company average, will not share on public forum") and the volume-by-design mix that would let an outsider verify margin-neutrality. Thesis implication: this **partially resolves** the Role 4 skid-ASP flag (A3-F07) with a coherent and plausible mechanism, but it remains **unverifiable** without the skid margin and design mix. Follow-up we would have asked: at the current ASP of Rs 3.11 lakh, what unit gross margin does a standard skid carry versus the Rs 5 lakh piped design, and is the anchor customer receiving volume-based price concessions?

**Exchange 2 — 15,000 capacity timing and Q4 exit run-rate (T10, Nirvana Laha, Badrinath).** Q: which month does the 15k come, and is the Q4 750-skids/month exit intact? What management said: the capacity milestone is now supplier-dependent and can be Oct or Nov; the Q4 exit run-rate is reaffirmed. What management did NOT say: a firm commissioning date or the supplier's committed delivery schedule. Thesis implication: the 15k milestone that underpins the FY28 80-percent-utilisation and ROCE-recovery path is now caveated (CC-F6-01); a slip from Q3 into H2 compresses the FY28 full-year-utilisation assumption. Follow-up: if the machine supplier slips into December, does the Q4 exit run-rate of 750/month still hold, and what is the fallback?

**Exchange 3 — 25 percent SFN-share year-end target, softened, and the same-customer catalyst concentration (T07 and T08).** Q (T07, Prem, Astute): you guided 25 percent of revenue from SFN by year-end, you are already at 22 percent in Q1 without full capacity, do you reiterate? What management said: deferral of a target it could plausibly beat, plus confirmation that the two new catalysts route through the same largest anchor (or an undisclosed customer).
