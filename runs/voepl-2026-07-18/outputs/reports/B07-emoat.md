### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic

**Capex under execution** (documented capacity tables + management-stated, near-term-committed
figures; excludes the uncommitted compressor phase-2 optionality):
Compressor ~₹150cr + AC ~₹45cr (midpoint of ₹40-50cr) + deep freezer ~₹45cr (midpoint of two
phases) + EMS ~₹25cr = **~₹265cr**.

**Historical fixed asset turnover** (📄, standalone AR FY25, Note 12 Property Plant & Equipment +
Statement of P&L):
- FY25: Revenue ₹697.20cr ÷ Net PP&E closing (excl. CWIP) ₹201.24cr = **3.47x**
- FY24: Revenue ₹531.08cr ÷ Net PP&E closing (excl. CWIP) ₹130.15cr = **4.08x**
- Two-year average (used as the "historical FAT"): **~3.8x**

Cross-check (🎙️, not the anchor): management's own segment-level asset-turn guidance in the Q4
FY26 call — EMS 3-5x, AC 4-5x, refrigeration 4-5x, compressor (pre-backward-integration) 3.5-4x —
blends to roughly ~4.1x, corroborating the AR-derived 3.8x within a reasonable band.

**Arithmetic**: ₹265cr capex under execution × 3.8x historical FAT = **~₹1,007cr implied
incremental revenue**.

**As % of current revenue** (FY26 consolidated, ₹825.99cr, 📄 Inv. Pres. p.5-6/Q4 FY26 call):
₹1,007cr ÷ ₹825.99cr ≈ **122%**.

This is the figure carried in the YAML block (`capex_embedded_growth_pct`) for stage 9 TAM.
It is deliberately more conservative than management's own explicit forward claim — "the current
set of expansion... is good enough to take us beyond INR2,500 crores" (Q4 FY26 call, 🎙️), which
the CFO immediately tempered in the same call: "a jump from INR800 crores to INR2,500 crores in
two years is a big ask... it will take a little more time." The ~122% figure derived here is
anchored to a documented historical ratio rather than management's un-anchored full-suite peak
claim, consistent with the sceptical posture this stage requires.

## SECTION 3: THE 20-CATEGORY SCAN

### FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY

**A1. Rare manufacturing capability** — Moderate-Strong. VOEPL is in a technical partnership with
Huayi (claimed "largest manufacturer of refrigeration compressors... across the world," 🎙️) and
Jiaxipera for reciprocating-compressor manufacture, described as "exclusive manufacturing partners
for India" (🎙️, Q4 FY25 call). Compressor tie-up itself is 📄 (AR FY25 PDF p.8: "a technical
partnership with a leading global manufacturer of reciprocatory compressors"). QCO (Quality Control
Order) caps hermetic/reciprocating-compressor imports at 40% of the FY24-25 base until March 2027
(📄, Inv. Pres. p.13: "Extension of QCO-linked compressor import exemptions till March 2027 with a
limited import exemption of 40%"). Management's claim of being the *sole* current OEM/ODM
reciprocating-compressor manufacturer in India is a 🎙️ assertion, uncorroborated by any independent
source in the document set, though qualification timelines (12-36 months typical for this kind of
BIS-gated component) and the disclosed competitor-entry timeline (2-3 players, "late FY27/FY28,"
Q4 FY26 call, 🎙️) are consistent with a genuine but time-limited rarity window.

**A2. Patent and IP pipeline** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND. R&D spend of ₹8.34cr in FY25 is disclosed (📄,
AR FY25 Annexure II, "Expenditure incurred on R&D"), but no patent filings, licensing revenue, or
lab partnerships appear anywhere in the AR, four concalls, or presentation.

**A3. Process innovation** — Weak. A new in-house tool room is now operational (📄, Q4 FY26 call
and Inv. Pres. "Way Forward" p.9-10) and lean-manufacturing initiatives are mentioned in the AR
(📄, AR FY25 PDF p.11), but no yield disclosures, unit-cost trend, or automation metrics are given.

**A4. Product platform / modular architecture** — Weak-Moderate. SKU growth without proportional
R&D disclosure is visible (AC→freezers→compressors→washing machines→SKD refrigerators, all within
~18 months) and the new tool room is explicitly framed as enabling "faster development, and turn
around time" (📄 claim in filed presentation, Inv. Pres. p.9). No shared-architecture language
beyond this.

### FAMILY B — SUPPLY CHAIN

**B1. Backward integration and RM security** — **Strong**. In-house component value-add ~60% today,
target 75-80% (📄, Inv. Pres. p.10 "Growth Algorithm"). IDU already 75-80% in-house, ODU 60%→70-75%
targeted (🎙️, Q4 FY25 call). ₹100cr PLI sanction specifically tied to "backward integration of
White Goods (Air-Conditioners)" (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.26; Inv. Pres. p.31). New aluminium die-casting
unit, injection-blow-moulding, in-house brass components all added in FY25 (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.11).
This is the single best-evidenced category in the scan.

**B2. Qualification lock-in** — Weak. NABL-accredited testing labs across AC/refrigeration/
compressor/EMS (📄, Inv. Pres. p.25), plus legacy ISO and UL-for-export-to-USA certifications (📄,
AR FY25 milestones). These are largely table-stakes certifications for this industry rather than
evidence of design-in/sole-source contractual lock-in; no multi-year signed agreements or
customer-funded tooling are disclosed.

**B3. Supply chain network effect** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND. VOEPL is a linear contract manufacturer,
not a platform connecting third-party suppliers and buyers.

### FAMILY C — CUSTOMER

**C1. Customer ecosystem / embedded relationships** — Weak-Moderate. The ODM launch is 📄
(Inv. Pres. p.14-15) and management states 40-50% of FY26 AC revenue is now ODM-sourced (🎙️,
Q4 FY26 call, not a filed %). No AMC/installed-base revenue, no disclosed co-development
contracts, and management explicitly says the ODM margin premium has "not [helped] too much" so
far ("this year I don't see too much [uplift]," Q2 FY26 call, 🎙️). Real directional shift, thin
proof of embedded stickiness beyond the anchor relationship itself.

**C2. Customer concentration improving** — Weak-Moderate, and explicitly mixed. AC customer count
did genuinely rise from 1 to 4 (📄, disclosed customer count in Q4 FY26 call/filed presentation
narrative). But Voltas's *share* of AC revenue moved from ~65-70% (FY25, 🎙️) to close to 100% in
H1 FY26 (🎙️, Q2 FY26 call — concentration got *worse*, not better, in the interim) before a
projected decline to 40-60% (🎙️, forward guidance, Q2 FY26 call) that was itself walked back:
by the Q3 FY26 call management said Voltas volumes were "similar or higher than what we did last
year" — this is the exact promise flagged MISSED in B05 ("Voltas-share-reduction reversed"). The
underlying customer-count diversification is real; the concentration-*ratio* improvement is not
yet evidenced and has partially reversed. Scored conservatively.

### FAMILY D — DATA & DIGITAL

**D1. Proprietary data asset** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND.

**D2. Digital platform** — NO EVIDENCE FOUND. AR MD&A names "IoT-enabled appliances, predictive
maintenance and connected solutions" as an R&D focus area (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.36), but no product,

### FAMILY E — GEOGRAPHIC & ACCESS

**E1. Geographic first-mover** — Weak. AR asserts VOEPL is "a preferred vendor in the western
region... close proximity to the port" (📄 claim within AR, but essentially a location assertion,
AR FY25 PDF p.26). The frost-free/multi-door refrigerator "nobody else... doing this" claim (🎙️,
Q3 FY26 call) sits on a trivial revenue base (~₹1.5-2cr/month).

**E2. China+1 beneficiary** — **Strong**. QCO import cap is a documented, already-in-force
government order (📄, Inv. Pres. p.13). AR MD&A explicitly frames the opportunity in "China+1"
terms (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.29-30: "The global 'China+1' strategy... has positioned India as a
favorable alternative"). Addressable gap quantified consistently across calls: ~22mn units India
refrigeration-compressor market, ~13-15mn currently imported (🎙️/📄 mixed, repeated across Q2-Q4
FY26 calls). Commercial production is already live (from Jan-2026), which distinguishes this from
a purely aspirational China+1 narrative — the substitution is underway, not merely anticipated.

### FAMILY F — TALENT & ORGANISATIONAL

**F1. Talent density** — Weak. Senior hires disclosed with real pedigree — from LG Electronics,
Videocon, Haier Appliances, Liebherr India, Amber, Franke Faber (📄, Inv. Pres. "Core Team" p.21) —
a genuine signal of capability-building, but no R&D headcount trend, PhD count, or broad
technical-staff ESOP pool is disclosed. ESOP grants under the 2023 plan went only to 2 named KMP/
senior-management individuals at 20,000 options each (📄, AR FY25 Annexure III) — not evidence of
broad technical-staff equity participation.

**F2. Execution moat** — Moderate, explicitly flagged. Per the injected B05 promise-delivery
record: credibility grade B, 4 delivered / 5 partial / 7 missed. **Delivered**: FY26 EBITDA margin
beat (10.4% actual vs. 8.5-9% originally guided); compressor plant built and commissioned in under
8 months, "3 months ahead of schedule" (📄, Inv. Pres. p.9, Q2 FY26 call). **Missed/slipped**:
mainboard listing guided across 4 consecutive calls without completing within the window originally
implied; Chennai AC plant slipped Q4 FY26→Q1 FY27; FY26 capex overshot guided ₹100-110cr to actual
₹130-150cr; the Voltas-share-reduction promise reversed (see C2); FY27 revenue target diluted from
~₹2,000cr (Arihant conference reference, Q2 FY26 call) to ~₹1,100-1,155cr (35-40% CAGR off the
₹826cr FY26 base, Q4 FY26 call). Net: real execution capability on the single highest-priority
project (compressor), but a majority-missed record on everything else, including the capex
discipline the entire 2A/2C capacity build depends on.

### FAMILY G — FINANCIAL & STRUCTURAL

**G1. War chest** — **NO EVIDENCE FOUND — directly contradicted**. Gate 0 (B01) already flags a
FY26 leverage deal-breaker: ND/EBITDA 4.0x, interest coverage 1.73x. Net debt is *rising* while
investing, not shrinking; the ₹250cr equity approval taken is a blanket enabling resolution, not a
committed raise (🎙️, Q4 FY26 call: "it does not mean that we are going to raise INR250 crores or
the timelines"). This category scores zero and is flagged, not silently dropped.

**G2. Working-capital improvement trajectory** — Weak, reversed. AR FY25 shows genuine FY25
improvement: debtors turnover 19.25x→27.29x, inventory turnover 2.90x→3.21x (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.66,
Key Financial Ratios). But per Gate 0's FY26 data, cash conversion collapsed (CFO/PAT 2.17x→0.22x)
in the same year the capex programme accelerated. Per the operating rules, this INDETERMINATE/
reversed cash-conversion signal is flagged, not credited as an active positive moat.

### FAMILY H — ECOSYSTEM & EXTERNAL

**H1. Industry consolidation beneficiary** — Weak. The QCO functions as a compliance-cost barrier
against under-qualified Chinese imports (📄, same order as E2), but there is no evidence of
competitor exits, anti-dumping action, or bolt-on acquisitions by VOEPL itself.

**H2. Strategic partnerships** — **Strong**. Technical partnership with Huayi/Jiaxipera for
compressor manufacturing (📄, AR FY25 PDF p.8, p.11). MOU signed with the Maharashtra government
for compressor investment (📄, Inv. Pres. milestones p.22, "Signed an MOU with Maharashtra
government for investment in compressor," FY25-26). Two independently disclosed, named partnership
instruments — the strongest documented category alongside B1.

**H3. ESG moat** — Weak. 1.5MW rooftop solar installed (📄, AR FY25 + Inv. Pres. p.36). A stated
FY25 plan to expand to 3.5MW and add rooftop solar at 2 more Nashik units "by March 2025" (🎙️, AR
FY25 PDF p.34) appears NOT delivered — no further mention of expanded solar capacity in either the
Q4 FY26 call or the FY26 Investor Presentation, an unconfirmed/likely-slipped promise. No SBTi,
ZLD, or customer-mandated ESG requirement disclosed.

  2-3 named compressor competitors expected to localise (late FY27/FY28) — the key competitive

### 6B. Risks to each top-scoring emerging moat, with early warning signs

- **B1 (backward integration)** — risk: capex overruns/execution slippage, already evidenced

  further capex-guidance revisions, in-house % disclosure stalling below the stated 75-80% target.

  (precedent already exists — core-metal QCOs were partially reversed per the Q2 FY26 call);

  competition if the QCO eases. Early warning: the March-2027 government decision itself, competitor
  plant-commissioning announcements, VOEPL compressor margin trending below the 6-7% FY27 guide.

  based, not equity-locked ("no sum paid... for the technology," Q4 FY25 call); the Chinese partner
