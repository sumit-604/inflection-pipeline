### 6B Classification: **CONCERN**

The legal, criminal, auditor, pledge and reputation record is genuinely clean, and there are real, verifiable
constructive signals this year (Section 6D). But dimension 10 is not a minor omission: the FY26 AR states
twice, in its two most-read narrative sections (Chairman's letter, MD&A), that GDAL is "wholly-owned," while
the same document's own Form AOC-1 four pages later shows 79.43% — an internally falsifiable misstatement, not
an inference. That sits alongside three straight years of never naming who owns the rest of GDAL across two
separate capital raises, and a second promoter-family private vehicle (Goodluck Green Energy Ltd) that has
never appeared in any annual report and is now being folded into the listco on terms not yet disclosed. Layer
on an unexplained, family-concentrated share sale timed weeks ahead of a capital event that (by construction)
further dilutes the family's own subsidiary stake. No single item is a deal-breaker (6C), but the pattern —
opaque related-vehicle layer plus an affirmative ownership misstatement — is a promoter-conduct finding, not
a disclosure nicety, and it outweighs the clean legal record on its own. This does not read as a static
CONCERN, however: see the transition-evidence scan below.
