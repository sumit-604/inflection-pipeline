Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Sub: Intimation regarding Change in Management 
 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI Listing Regulations"), we hereby 
inform that, the Board of Directors of the Bank, in its meeting held on Thursday, April 30, 2026 
have approved the following: 
1. 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation 
Committee, the Board of Directors of the Bank reclassified and redesignated the following 
officials from ‘Key Managerial Personnel’ to ‘Senior Management Personnel’ of the Bank 
with effect from May 1, 2026: 
 
• 
Shri. Sudev Kumar V – Executive Vice President - Retail Banking 
• 
Shri. Wilson Cyriac – Chief Risk Officer 
• 
Shri. Sivakumar P – Head of Internal Audit 
 
The disclosures as required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations read 
with SEBI Circular no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 
2026 is given as Annexure. 
 
2. 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination, Remuneration and Compensation 
Committee, the Board of Directors of the Bank approved the classification framework   
whereby the following positions of the Bank shall be classified as the ‘Senior Management 
Personnel’ (SMP) of the Bank with effect from May 1, 2026: 
 
• 
All Executive Vice Presidents, and 
• 
Heads of Control Functions, including:  
o 
Chief Risk Officer (CRO), 
o 
Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) and 
o 
Head – Internal Audit, and 
 
Accordingly, the following officials shall be classified as ‘Senior Management Personnel’ 
with effect from May 1, 2026:
