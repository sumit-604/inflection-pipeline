## What this is, and what it is not

This is a pre-spear triage. It reads filed documents through Bull AI. It
sorts six names by fit with the strategy: Indian small and micro cap GARP
transition alpha, 25% CAGR target, 3 to 5 year holds.

It is NOT a valuation. No exit PE, no entry zone, no fair value. Those need
the Section 1B layer set, Stage 11, and a signed Mental Model. It is NOT a
spear pass. The spear runs on live web with Claude web, and it prices the
name against CMP. This screen tells you which names deserve that spear.

All market caps and multiples below carry Bull AI's stored figure. Verify
CMP live before you act. This container cannot.

Verdict language here is triage only: SPEAR, WATCH, or PASS. It is not the
pipeline verdict set. No name here has run the pipeline.

## 1. Borana Weaves (NSE: BORANA) — top spear candidate, one hard question

- Sector: Textiles. Industry: greige fabric (synthetic weaving, Surat).
- Market cap: ~Rs 816 cr (Bull AI). Micro cap. Right size for the strategy.
- Listed 2025 (recent IPO). Short public history.

FY26 delivery (management, AR and concall):
- Revenue Rs 389 cr, up 34% (FY25 Rs 290 cr).
- EBITDA Rs 92 cr, up 45%. Margin 23.5%.
- PAT Rs 65 cr, up 61%. Margin 16.6%.
- Capacity 392 mn metres across 4 units. Utilisation 80.9%.
- Short term debt cut to Rs 13.9 cr from Rs 21.6 cr.

Guidance:
- Double production capacity in 24 months. Add ~5 cr metres a year.
- Solar commissioning June 2026. 70 to 80% of power from renewables.
- New product: RPU coated fabrics for jackets and technical wear.

Implied multiple (verify live): ~12.5x FY26 PAT. Cheap against 34% revenue
growth and 61% profit growth.

Ladder read: weaving sits at R1 to R2, a commodity converter. The claim is a
climb toward value added technical fabric (R3). Amendment 17 binds: this is a
converter, so spot year ROCE and rupee working capital trends do not feed
Section 1B.

The one hard question: 23.5% EBITDA margin and 16.6% PAT margin are far above
commodity weaving economics. Either the mix is genuinely value added
(texturised or coated synthetic), or the margin rides a favourable polyester
spread that mean reverts. This margin is too good for the stated archetype. It
is the load bearing fact a spear must resolve.

Flags:
- Margin durability. Synthetic textile spreads are cyclical. Test spread, not
  spot margin.
- Recent IPO. One to two years of public numbers. Thin history.
- Surat cluster. Check related party sales, customer concentration, promoter
  (Borana family).
- Capacity doubling. Fund source, execution risk, demand absorption.

Strategy fit: strong on size, growth, and price. Weak until the margin
question is answered. **SPEAR, with heavy skepticism on margin.** Load
bearing facts for the spear: (1) is the margin mix or spread, (2) customer and
related party concentration, (3) demand behind the capacity double.

## 2. MSTC Ltd (NSE: MSTCLTD) — quality asset, modest growth, real optionality

- Sector: E-commerce and digital marketplace. PSU (Government of India).
- Industry: digital auction and e-procurement, government disinvestment.
- Market cap: ~Rs 5,168 cr (Bull AI). Small cap.

FY26 delivery:
- Revenue from operations Rs 370 cr, highest in four years.
- EBITDA Rs 307 cr, up 18.2%. (EBITDA above reported revenue reflects the net
  commission model plus other income; verify the accounting in the spear.)
- PAT ex exceptional Rs 222 cr, up 23%. Consolidated PAT Rs 218 cr. EPS 31.03.
- Gross transaction value Rs 797 bn through the platform.
- Sold 10 coal mines, 200 plus mineral blocks in FY26.

Guidance and strategy:
- Exit the legacy trading vertical by Q1 FY27. Move to pure e-commerce.
- Organic growth: double digit, incremental, not high.
- New platforms: EPR certificate trading (live, awaiting approval), travel
  portal, coal and mineral exchanges (feasibility), TReDS (RBI clearance).
- Dividend: minimum 4% of net worth or 30% of PAT, whichever higher (DIPAM).

Implied multiple (verify live): ~24x consolidated PAT.

Ladder read: a near monopoly government mandated e-auction platform. Asset
light, high ROCE, sticky. Economics sit high (R4 neighbourhood on quality),
but the licence and scarcity archetype fits better than franchise.

Bull case: high quality, cash rich, asset light platform at a fair multiple,
with a stack of policy driven call options (EPR trading, ELV scrappage,
mineral exchange, TReDS). Any one firing re-rates a slow grower.

Bear case: core growth is double digit, not 25%. PSU capital allocation and
disinvestment overhang. Optionality is unpriced because it is unproven, and
government timelines slip. Revenue is small and lumpy against GTV.

Flags: PSU governance, DIPAM disinvestment risk, optionality timing, revenue
lumpiness tied to auction calendar.

Strategy fit: quality yes, 25% base growth no. The thesis is optionality on
new platforms. **WATCH.** Spear only if the operator wants to underwrite the
EPR or mineral exchange option as the return driver.

## 3. Syrma SGS Technology (NSE: SYRMA) — real EMS climb, likely priced, off size

- Sector: EMS (electronics manufacturing services).
- Market cap: ~Rs 28,133 cr (Bull AI). Large cap. Off the strategy's size band.

FY26 delivery:
- Revenue Rs 4,857 cr, up 27%.
- Operating EBITDA Rs 545 cr, 11.3% margin, up 68%.
- PAT Rs 346 cr, up 87%, 7.1% margin.
- Q4 revenue Rs 1,477 cr, up 56%. Q4 EBITDA margin 11.9%.
- Net cash: debt Rs 353 cr against cash Rs 820 cr. Working capital 69 to 63 days.
- Order book Rs 6,770 cr, Rs 5,400 cr executable in 12 months.

Guidance:
- FY27 revenue growth 35%. EBITDA Rs 700 cr. Margin 10 to 10.5%.
- 30 to 35% growth sustained into FY28 and FY29.
- PCB backward integration. Commercial production April 2027. Margin accretive.
- MedTech above Rs 500 cr. Exports Rs 1,500 to 1,600 cr. ODM mix to 16 to 19%.

Implied multiple (verify live): ~80x FY26 PAT. Rich.

Ladder read: EMS is R2 to R3. The transition is genuine. ODM mix rising moves
it up the ladder. PCB backward integration lifts margin. Proof is firing.

Transition matrix read: proof fired, ugliness is an artifact of the climb, but
the recognition gap looks closed at ~80x. That maps to EARNINGS ONLY or a
PRICED NARRATIVE risk. Returns from here ride EPS CAGR, not re-rating, and the
multiple leaves no margin for error.

Strategy fit: real business, real climb, wrong price and wrong size for a GARP
entry. **PASS for now.** Re-open only on a large drawdown that re-opens the
recognition gap. Not a spear priority.

## 4. Fratelli Vineyards (BSE: 541741) — premiumisation bet, no profit yet, illiquid

- Sector: Consumer staples. Industry: wine and ready to drink (RTD).
- Market cap: ~Rs 561 cr (Bull AI). Micro cap. BSE only, no NSE listing.

FY26 delivery:
- Net revenue Rs 184 cr, up ~1%. Flat.
- EBITDA Rs 1.06 cr, 0.57% margin. Operating breakeven only.
- Gross margin 77 to 80%. High.
- Luxury segment up 15%. Flagship J'noon up 44%.
- RTD brand Shotgun: 100k cases in year one, 90% plus share of wine in a can.

Guidance:
- FY27 revenue growth ~30% (~Rs 240 cr). PAT breakeven.
- Shotgun to double to 200k cases.
- Vision 2030: Rs 500 plus cr revenue, 20% plus EBITDA margin, 20% CAGR.
- Hospitality resort at Akluj. Capex Rs 70 to 80 cr.

Ladder read: a brand and premiumisation story (R4 to R5 aspiration) with 80%
gross margin, but zero operating profit today. The thesis is a margin climb
from 0.57% to 20% by FY30. That is a multi rung leap. Base rate is one rung per
2 to 3 years, so the claim is aggressive and the proof gate has not fired.

Bull case: 80% gross margin means the model works if brand spend (8 to 9% of
sales) scales into operating leverage. Real category tailwind in Indian
premium wine and RTD. Right size.

Bear case: no profit, flat top line in FY26, Vision 2030 unproven, BSE only
illiquidity, brand spend cycle open ended. A priced narrative if the market
already credits FY30.

Flags: zero current earnings, illiquidity, Vision 2030 credibility, Sekhri
family promoter, alco-bev regulation and state by state distribution.

Strategy fit: right size and a genuine premium story, but pre profit and
speculative. **WATCH.** Spear if the operator wants premium alco-bev exposure;
peer read against Sula Vineyards. The load bearing fact is operating leverage:
does gross margin convert to EBITDA as volume scales.

## 5. Wockhardt (NSE: WOCKPHARMA) — single drug optionality, richly priced, off strategy

- Sector: Healthcare. Industry: novel antibiotics, biosimilars, pharma.
- Market cap: ~Rs 33,908 cr (Bull AI). Large cap.

FY26 delivery:
- Income Rs 3,373 cr. EBITDA Rs 630 cr, 18.6% margin, up 51%.
- Margin lifted from 5.4% three years ago to 18.6%.
- Net debt to equity 0.1 after a QIP.
- Biosimilars up 27%. Emerging markets up 35%. UK (39% of business) up 13%.
- Emrok (anti MRSA) up 50%. Novel antibiotics and biosimilars 23% of revenue.

Guidance:
- Zaynich (WCK 5222) launch in US, India, Europe, emerging markets in two years.
- Enter 7 to 8 key markets in 18 to 24 months.
- Biosimilar business to double in 24 to 36 months. Capacity double in 12 to 15
  months. Capex Rs 200 to 300 cr over three years.
- Bottom line breakeven in 12 to 18 months, then a claimed hockey stick.

Ladder read: a pharma innovator. The valuation is an option on Zaynich, a novel
antibiotic aimed at resistant infections. The upside is binary and priced rich
(~54x EBITDA). Amendment 18 is directly relevant: a within hold option slice
(Zaynich success) must not carry into an exit as an unresolved probability.

Strategy fit: large cap, richly valued, thesis is single asset R&D optionality.
This is not GARP transition alpha. It is a binary biotech bet. **PASS.** Off
strategy by design. Not a spear priority for this book.

## 6. TARC Ltd (NSE: TARC) — deleveraging turnaround, lumpy, off archetype

- Sector: Real estate. Delhi and Gurugram luxury residential.
- Market cap: ~Rs 3,750 cr (Bull AI). Small cap.

FY26 delivery:
- Total income Rs 672 cr (FY25 Rs 39 cr). Driven by Tripundra revenue recognition.
- EBITDA positive Rs 78 cr (FY25 negative Rs 128 cr). Turnaround of ~Rs 205 cr.
- PAT Rs 19 cr (FY25 net loss Rs 231 cr).
- Pre-sales Rs 1,373 cr. Business cashflows Rs 1,132 cr, 2x prior year.
- Tripundra embedded gross margin ~45%. Luxury EBITDA 60 to 65%.

Guidance:
- FY27 Tripundra revenue recognition ~Rs 1,000 cr.
- Kailasa and Ishva ~Rs 8,000 cr recognition in FY29 and FY30.
- ~Rs 10,000 cr cashflows over five years.
- Net debt zero by FY27 to FY28. Debt ~Rs 1,650 cr, refinanced 17 to 18% to 12.75%.

Ladder read: a real estate developer. Earnings are project lumpy and revenue
recognition driven, closer to R0 or R1 accounting driven than an operating
compounder. Valuation is NAV and embedded value, not PE. The thesis is
deleveraging plus land bank monetisation, a special situation.

Strategy fit: this is a balance sheet repair and NAV turnaround, not a quality
climb up the ladder. Debt heavy, cyclical, lumpy. Off archetype for transition
alpha. **PASS for this strategy.** If the operator wants a Delhi NCR real
estate deleveraging play, it is a separate special situations mandate, and the
first checks are promoter record (Amar Sarin, ex Anant Raj) and the debt
repayment schedule against the cashflow guidance.
