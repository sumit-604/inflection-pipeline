## D. FIVE (PLUS TWO) QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. What is driving the standalone-only receivables ageing deterioration (>6-month bucket 6.8%→22.2%, DSO
   85→114 days) when consolidated ageing is stable at ~11%? Is this concentrated in a small number of
   customers, and has the ECL matrix been recalibrated given the >6-month bucket tripled while the allowance
   stayed static at ₹19.57 lakh?
2. What are the commercial terms (interest rate, tenure, repayment schedule) of the ₹457.22 lakh advance to
   Menon Brakes Ltd, which grew 44.9% in FY26 while interest income from all sources fell 50.9%?
3. Has the Board tested the ₹2,823.50 lakh carrying value of the Menon Alkop investment for impairment under
   Ind AS 36 given Menon Alkop's negative reserves (₹(206.98) lakh, implied net worth ≈₹618 lakh), and if so,
   what were the value-in-use assumptions?
4. Why is there no consolidated Ind AS 37 Contingent Liabilities table covering the ₹29 crore Menon Alkop
   corporate guarantee and the ₹12.78 lakh disputed TDS demand, and what is the total quantum of all
   guarantees and disputes across the group?
5. What drove the near-simultaneous Managing Director and Chief Financial Officer change effective 4 March
   2026, and what controls were in place to ensure continuity of financial reporting through the year-end
   close?
6. (Additional) Why do Note 6's own aged-receivables sub-classification and the Note 31 ageing schedule
   differ so materially (2.5x-16x) for the identical balance date — which is the operative definition used

  45% with no disclosed terms.
- **Internal inconsistency**: Note 6 vs Note 31 disagree on aged-receivables quantum for the same date; the
  Board's Report ratio table (undisclosed consolidated basis) shows receivables turnover moving in the
  opposite direction from standalone Note 33 for the same year.
- **Disclosure-completeness regression**: three prior-year standard notes (Other Statutory Information, Code
  on Social Security, prior-year regrouping) dropped since FY22 AR; no Ind AS 19 actuarial assumptions; no
  Ind AS 10 subsequent-events note; no MSME Section 16 interest confirmation; no export/customer
  concentration disclosure.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting quality with one significant, multiply-corroborated concern. Key
concern: a standalone-specific receivables build (>6-month ageing 6.8% to 22.2%, DSO 85 to 114 days) that
converts into a hard cash problem (35.3% operating cash conversion, standalone cash down 69.7%) against a
static ECL allowance and no consolidated Contingent Liabilities table for a ₹29 crore intercompany guarantee.
Key strength: clean PPE/depreciation/borrowings disclosure, stable simple group structure with no goodwill or
minority interest, and a conservative flat dividend funding growth internally, with the deterioration itself
confirmed as standalone-only (not group-wide) and consistent with CRISIL's capex-driven explanation rather
than distress. Overall accounting quality: 5/10.
