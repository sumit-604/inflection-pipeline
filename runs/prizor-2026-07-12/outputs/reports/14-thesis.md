## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Prizor is a fast-growing CCTV assembler riding a real, but shared, policy tailwind, and its reported returns look excellent on the surface (ROCE 31%, margins 21%). The problem is not the story; it is the price and the cash. At Rs 825 the market already pays 87 times audited earnings for a business whose fair destination multiple is 15 times, so every scenario the valuation can build, bear to bull, lands 72% to 91% below today's price. Underneath, the profit has not turned into cash in either year on record, the margins run above the only scaled peer with no disclosed reason, and a Rs 3 crore block of equity traces to no named buyer. The entry price that would make this investable, Rs 63 to Rs 79, is about 90% below the market and has never been seen since the July 2024 listing, so this is DEEP WATCH, not a WATCHLIST correction to wait for. The one number that could change the verdict is the next audited operating cash flow. Until it prints at 40% of profit or better, and until the price is nowhere near today's, the answer is AVOID.
