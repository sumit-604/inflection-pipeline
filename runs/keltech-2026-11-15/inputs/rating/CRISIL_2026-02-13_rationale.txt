Detailed rationale
Crisil Ratings has assigned its ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’ ratings to the bank facilities of Keltech Energies Ltd (KEL).
 
The ratings reflect extensive experience of the promoters, strong market position in the explosives industry marked by
customer and product diversification, efficient working capital cycle and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are
partially offset by susceptibility to regulatory changes, cyclicality in domestic end-user industries and volatility in raw material
prices, and exposure to risks pertaining to the ongoing project.
Analytical approach
Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of KEL.
Key rating drivers - Strengths 
Extensive experience of the promoters: KEL is part of the Goa-based Chowgule group of companies, (which is a
conglomerate engaged in diverse businesses such as mining and export of iron ore, pelletisation, shipbuilding, manufacture
of industrial salt and automotive dealership, and also manages educational institutes in Goa). The promoters’ experience of
over four decades in the industrial explosives industry has given them an understanding of the market dynamics.
 
Strong market position: KEL has been manufacturing industrial explosives since 1977 and has established strong
relationships with suppliers and customers. KEL caters to a large number of clients across various segments such as
cement, quarry, edible oil (for perlites products). The top 10 customers accounted for ~21% of the company’s revenue in
fiscal 2025, indicating minimal customer concentration risk. Furthermore, KEL has diversified its product profile and
expanded into verticals such as perlite and explosive accessories. The diverse product profile and clientele should continue
to support the company’s business risk profile.
 
Efficient working capital cycle: The operations of the company are working capital efficient as reflected in GCA of 114
days as of March 2025 with debtors of 43 days and inventory of 34 days. The company follows prudent policy and extends
low credit of around 30-35 days to its customers and maintains inventory of around 34 days mainly of raw materials. Going
forward, with no major change expected in policy, working capital cycle is expected to remain on similar levels over medium
term.
 
Healthy financial risk profile: KEL’s financial risk profile is supported by strong networth of Rs 124.4 crore as on March 31,
2025, and expected to reach ~Rs 145 crore as on March 31, 2026, driven by steady accretion to reserve. The capital
structure has been healthy due to low reliance on external funds as indicated by gearing of 0.31 time and total outside
liabilities to adjusted networth ratio of 1.31 times as on March 31, 2025. However, the capital structure is expected to
moderate on account of large debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 190 crores over the medium term for setting up
a new plant. KEL’s debt protection metrics have also been healthy due to low interest obligations and comfortable
profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratio were 9.82 times and 0.83 time, respectively, for
fiscal 2025 and are expected to moderate over the medium term on account of interest obligations for additional debt
availed for capex.
Key rating drivers - Weaknesses 
Susceptibility to adverse changes in government regulations and to cyclicality in domestic end-user industries:
The explosives industry players require industrial licenses and various clearances from the government to prevent misuse of
end products, and are regularly monitored by various government departments.. Though KEL takes precautions at all
stages, from manufacturing to delivering, it remains susceptible to regulatory risks and intervention. Any unfavorable change
in policies can strain the company’s profitability. Furthermore, entities in the explosives segment will remain exposed to
cyclicality in end-user industries, such as cement, quarries and road construction, any downturn in these industries will
impact business profile of KEL.
 
Susceptibility to fluctuations in raw material prices: The key raw materials, ammonium nitrate, calcium nitrate and
chemicals, have highly volatile prices being commodity products. Since material costs account for around 70% of production
[page 2]
costs, even slight variation in price can drastically impact the operating margins. Price escalation clause in their supply
contract with counterparts mitigate volatility risk up to some extent. KEL’s margins will remain susceptible to fluctuations in
raw material prices.
 
Exposure to risks pertaining to ongoing project: The company is setting up a TNT (Trinitrotoluene) plant at a cost of ~Rs
190 crore. The project is being funded through term debt of Rs 135 crore and through internal accrual. With advance for
machinery already extended and necessary approvals in place, the plant is expected to commence operations in April 2027.
While the company’s established clientele and presence in a niche industry reduces demand risks, timely completion of the
project without any cost overrun remains monitorable.
Liquidity Adequate
Annual cash accrual is expected at Rs 32-38 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs 5-15 crore over the medium
term. Bank limit utilization averaged 84% for the 12 months through December 2025. The current ratio was moderate at
1.23 times, unencumbered cash and bank balance of around Rs 41 crore and liquid investments of Rs 22 crore as on March
31, 2025, and unencumbered cash and bank balance of around Rs 35 crores and Rs 15 crores liquid investments as of
September 2025.
Outlook Stable
Crisil Ratings believes KEL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and established
relationships with clients.
Rating sensitivity factors
Upward factors
Sustained revenue growth and operating margin above 9%, leading to higher cash accruals
Sustenance of the financial risk profile
Timely completion of the ongoing project with no major cost overrun, and stabilization of operations
 
Downward factors
Decline in revenue and profitability, leading to net cash accrual of less than Rs 20 crore
Substantial increase in working capital requirement or higher capex, weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity
About the company
KEL, part of the Goa-based Chowgule group of companies, was incorporated in August 1977. KEL manufactures industrial
explosives such as cartridge explosives, bulk emulsion explosives and perlite products (cryogenic insulation, industrial filter-
aid, horticulture products, and so on). KEL has 12 manufacturing units across eight states including Maharashtra, Madhya
Pradesh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh.
 
KEL is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange Ltd. The day-to-day operations are managed by Mr. Mahesh Vijay Wataney
(Managing Director).
