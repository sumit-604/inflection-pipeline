### 2C. Growth embedded in capex (arithmetic, then rejection)

Net PPE INR M 246.6 (AR26 p.131 balance sheet). FY26 revenue INR M 12,692.7. Fixed-asset turnover = 12,692.7 / 246.6 = 51.5x (calc). Capex under execution (GEPIL share) INR 18 cr = INR M 180. Implied incremental revenue = 180 x 51.5 = INR M 9,270 = 73.0% of FY26 revenue (calc). This number is not usable. The turnover is inflated by a near-fully depreciated, asset-light base (and PPE that includes Durgapur), the facility moves repair work GEPIL already wins from outsourced to dedicated capacity, and no capacity is stated. The block carries 0 and the flag CAPEX_GROWTH_NOT_MEANINGFUL. The correct reading: there is no capex-led growth in this business. Growth rests on orders, not plant.
