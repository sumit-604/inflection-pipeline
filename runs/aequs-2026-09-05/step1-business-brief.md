# STEP 1 BUSINESS BRIEF — Aequs Ltd (AEQUS)

Run date: 2026-09-05. CMP Rs 242 (04-Sep-2026). Market cap ~Rs 16,242 cr.
Listed 10-Dec-2025. This brief is COMPANY MEMORY, weighed, never anchored
evidence. Every stage number still comes from this run's filings.

## WHAT IT MAKES AND SELLS
- Vertically integrated precision manufacturer. Runs a special economic zone
  (SEZ) ecosystem for certified aerospace parts and engineered components for
  global OEMs. Incorporated 2000. [screener.in/company/AEQUS](https://www.screener.in/company/AEQUS/consolidated/)
- Two reporting segments: Aerospace and Consumer. Aerospace makes engine,
  landing gear, cargo, interior and structural components, assemblies and
  turning. Over 5,000 aerospace products for single-aisle and long-range
  aircraft as of 30-Sep-2025. [finnovate.in](https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/aequs-ipo-review-details-investment-guide)
- Consumer segment makes cookware, small home appliances, outdoor toys,
  figurines, toy vehicles and plastic components for consumer electronics.
  [inc42.com](https://inc42.com/buzz/can-aequs-build-a-consumer-engine-under-its-aerospace-wings/)

## REVENUE MIX AND WHERE THE MARGIN COMES FROM
- Aerospace is ~88% of net external revenue (88.23% H1FY26, 89.19% FY25,
  78.44% FY24). Consumer ~11-12%. [upstox IPO](https://upstox.com/ipo/aequs-limited-ipo/)
- Margin sits in aerospace: ~25% segment EBITDA margin in H1FY26. Consumer is
  loss-making, negative ~24% segment EBITDA margin H1FY26. [tradebrains](https://tradebrains.in/aequs-stock-revenue-surges-55-but-losses-persist-can-it-turn-growth-into-profits/)
- FY26 (year to Mar-2026): consolidated revenue Rs 1,230.4 cr, up 33%. EBITDA
  Rs 154.5 cr, up 43%. EBITDA margin 13% vs 12% FY25. Net loss WIDENED.
  [whalesbook FY26](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/industrial-goodsservices/Aequs-Ltd-FY26-Revenue-Jumps-33percent-to-indian-rupee12304-Mn-Net-Loss-Widens/6a7d71c66817e697e7c7c896)
- FY26 segment revenue: Aerospace Rs 1,046.4 cr (+27%); Consumer Rs 184 cr
  (+84%). [raksha-anirveda](https://raksha-anirveda.com/aequs-limited-reports-strong-fy26-performance-achieves-record-revenue-in-q4/)
- FY25 revenue fell 4%; net loss Rs 102.35 cr. FY24 net loss Rs 14.24 cr. The
  loss deepened into listing. [upstox IPO](https://upstox.com/ipo/aequs-limited-ipo/)

## THE ONE INFLECTION THE THESIS RESTS ON
The USD 1 bn aerospace order book, the 15-year Safran A320 wheel program
(deliveries FY28) and the Safran engine-parts win convert a capex-heavy,
loss-making precision manufacturer into a profitable ~20% ROCE aerospace
franchise. The question the filings must answer: does aerospace operating
leverage reach group profitability BEFORE the Rs 500 cr consumer capex and the
debt/interest load consume it. A climb only if aerospace scale outruns the
consumer drag and the balance sheet.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)
1. GUIDANCE VS DELIVERY. FY27 guidance is aerospace revenue +25-30% at segment
   EBITDA margin above 20% and ~20% manufacturing ROCE. Verify the stated
   aerospace segment EBITDA margin and any ROCE figure against the FY26 AR /
   Q1FY27 results and concall, and hold it as the delivery test for later
   quarters.
2. CONSUMER DRAG AND CAPITAL ALLOCATION. Consumer segment EBITDA margin was
   ~ -24% in H1FY26, yet ~Rs 500 cr of the Rs 660 cr FY27 capex is earmarked
   for it. Verify the actual consumer segment EBITDA and the capex split from
   filings; a large spend into a loss-making vertical is the key
   capital-allocation flag.
3. CASH CONVERSION AND BALANCE SHEET. Net loss FY25 Rs 102 cr, FY26 widened,
   Q1FY27 Rs 53 cr. Verify consolidated debt, interest cost, operating cash
   flow vs capex, and receivables/inventory trend from the AR and results.
   This is the FLAG-CASH determination input.
4. ORDER BOOK AND VISIBILITY. USD 1,004 mn aerospace order book and the
   15-year Safran A320 wheel agreement (deliveries FY28) are the revenue-
   visibility claims. Verify against the Reg 30 announcement and the concall,
   and check conversion cadence (backlog to revenue timing).

components for global OEMs, export-led, capex-heavy machining/forging)

  [screener.in/company/DYNAMATECH](https://www.screener.in/company/DYNAMATECH/)
- UNIMECH (Unimech Aerospace) — aero tooling and precision components for
  Airbus, Boeing, GE, Rolls-Royce; export-led and recently listed like Aequs.
  [screener.in/company/UNIMECH](https://www.screener.in/company/UNIMECH/consolidated/)

No listed pure-play mirrors the consumer-plastics side. The three chosen match
the aerospace economic engine the thesis rests on. MTAR Technologies was
considered but skews nuclear/clean-energy/defence, a weaker commercial-aero
comp than Dynamatic.
