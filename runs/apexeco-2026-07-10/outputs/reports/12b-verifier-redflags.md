   "we are not reliant on a single order or single company" (p.12) — never a top-1/3/5
   number.

   pressure + logistics + metal cost "25 to 40%" from "war and... geopolitical
   things." Original ZLD/bigger-ticket margin-accretion thesis not reconciled.

   partnership, any JV, any... understanding per se... with any of the company in the
