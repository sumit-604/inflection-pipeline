Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("SEBI LODR Regulations") and in accordance with SEBI Circular No. 
SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13, 2023, we wish to inform you that the 
Company has received a Letter of Award ("LOA") from Solarium Green Energy Limited for execution 
of Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) works in relation to a utility-scale ground-
mounted Solar PV Project. 
 
The LOA pertains to execution of Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) works on a 
turnkey basis for development of an approximately 50 MW AC / 65 MW DC Ground Mounted Solar PV 
Project, including associated civil, structural, electrical and allied infrastructure works, together with 
Operation & Maintenance (O&M) Services for a period of three (3) years, in accordance with the terms 
and conditions of the LOA.

do not contain any unusual or extraordinary stipulations.
