## V4 Q8 — Bayview (Syngene USA Inc.) CWIP, pre-operating costs, useful life, impairment

### CWIP totals, standalone vs consolidated
Quote (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF p.216, Standalone Balance Sheet, note 3(a)):
"Capital work-in-progress 3 (a) 5,359 8,754" — standalone CWIP: Rs 5,359mn at 31-Mar-2026, Rs 8,754mn at 31-Mar-2025.

Quote (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF p.302, Consolidated Balance Sheet, note 3(a)):
"Capital work-in-progress 3 (a) 10,404 12,614" — consolidated CWIP: Rs 10,404mn at 31-Mar-2026, Rs 12,614mn at 31-Mar-2025.

Standalone vs consolidated CWIP gap (computed): Mar-2026 = 10,404 − 5,359 = Rs 5,045mn; Mar-2025 = 12,614 − 8,754 = Rs 3,860mn. This gap sits in Syngene USA Inc. and other subsidiaries; it is not broken out by subsidiary anywhere in the AR.

### Bayview direct figure (the only Bayview-specific number filed)
Quote (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF p.293, Standalone Note 42):
"During the quarter ended 31 March 2025, Syngene USA Inc. ... has acquired biologics site in the USA ... from Emergent Manufacturing Operations Baltimore, LLC ... The cost incurred till 31 March 25 eligible for capitalization was accumulated as Capital Work in Progress amounting to Rs. 2,981 million (USD 34.89 million). An amount of Rs. 311 million (USD 3.64 million) was capitalized as Land. These amounts included pre-transaction costs of Rs. 101 million (USD 1.18 million). During the year ended 31 March 2026, additional pre-operating cost of Rs. 787 million (USD 8.4 million) eligible for capitalisation has been accumulated under capital work in progress."

Consolidated equivalent (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF ~p.297, Consolidated Note 43) gives the identical narrative but states the FY26 addition as "Rs. 785 million (USD 8.4 million)" — a Rs 2mn labelling inconsistency between the standalone (787) and consolidated (785) note for the same addition. Flagged.

Bayview cumulative CWIP through FY26 close (computed, standalone basis): 2,981 + 787 = Rs 3,768mn, before the FY27 addition below. This is a company-disclosed running total of "eligible for capitalisation" cost; it does not necessarily equal 100% of Bayview's balance-sheet CWIP if any other cost categories exist that the note does not label "pre-operating cost."

### Jun-2026 (Q1FY27) update
Quote (2026-07-29_Q1FY27_unaudited_results.txt, p.3 of PDF, note to standalone/consolidated results):
"During the year year ended 31 March 2026, additional pre-operating cost of Rs. 786 million eligible for capitalisation is being accumulated under capital work in progress. Further, during the quarter ended 30 June 2026, pre operating cost of Rs. 293 million were capitalised under capital work in progress."

Note: this filing gives the FY26 addition as Rs 786mn — a third figure, differing from both the standalone AR (787mn) and consolidated AR (785mn) for the identical line item. Three filings, three numbers (785/786/787) for the same "FY26 additional pre-operating cost." Flagged as a labelling/rounding inconsistency across filings, not explained in any of the three documents.

Cumulative Bayview pre-operating cost through 30-Jun-2026 (computed, using the Q1FY27 filing's own component figures): 2,981 + 786 + 293 = Rs 4,060mn. No separate Q1FY27 balance-sheet CWIP split by subsidiary is filed, so this computed figure cannot be cross-checked against a reported Bayview CWIP balance; the exact Jun-2026 Bayview CWIP balance is NOT DISCLOSED as a single reported number.

### Useful life / depreciation policy
Quote (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF p.229, standalone accounting policy note 2(b)(ii)):
"Depreciation is calculated on cost of items of property, plant and equipment less their estimated residual values over their estimated useful lives using the straight-line method. Freehold land and land under perpetual lease are not depreciated."
Table given: Building 25–30 years (Schedule II: 30 years); Plant and equipment (incl. electrical installation and lab equipment) 9–14 years (Schedule II: 8–20 years); Computers and servers 3 years; Office equipment 3 years; Furniture and fixtures 6 years; Vehicles 6 years; Leasehold improvements — useful life or lease period, whichever is lower.

This is a company-wide (Group) policy; the AR does not disclose a Bayview-specific or Syngene-USA-specific useful-life table. Any different local (US GAAP) useful-life convention used by Syngene USA Inc. before Ind AS consolidation is NOT DISCLOSED.

### Impairment / CGU test wording
Quote (Annual_Report_2026.txt, PDF p.227-228, standalone accounting policy note, "Impairment of non-financial assets"):
"The Company assess at each reporting date whether there is any indication that the carrying amount may not be recoverable. If any such indication exists, then the asset's recoverable amount is estimated and an impairment loss is recognised if the carrying amount of an asset or cash-generating unit (CGU) exceeds its estimated recoverable amount... The recoverable amount of a CGU (or an individual asset) is higher of its value in use and its fair value less costs to sell... For impairment testing, assets that do not generate independent cash inflows are grouped together into cash-generating units (CGUs). Each CGU represents the smallest group of assets that generates cash inflows that are largely independent of the cash inflows of other assets or CGUs."
Identical wording appears in the consolidated notes.

This is generic Group policy language only. No Bayview-specific CGU test, no impairment indicator discussion for Bayview, and no impairment loss recognised against Bayview assets are disclosed anywhere in the AR or the four results filings. NOT DISCLOSED: any Bayview-specific impairment test or trigger assessment.
