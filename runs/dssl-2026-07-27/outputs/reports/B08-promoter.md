## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. RPT history

Total related-party payment value this year approximately Rs8.50cr against standalone revenue of
Rs1,266.83cr = 0.67% of revenue, immaterial by any standard threshold [VERIFIED, B03 Phase 2B / AR
Note 32.4]. No royalty arrangement, no above-market rent, no management-fee extraction pattern, and
no guarantees for private promoter entities were found. The one item worth real attention: Cybercons
Infosec-specific dealings stepped up sharply this year (a new Rs254.74 lakh purchase transaction, and
related-party advances up from Rs15.00 lakh to Rs165.97 lakh, +1006% YoY) in the same year the
entity's own legal classification is internally contradicted across the AR (1B above, B03 Phase 1F).
CARO clause iii states the company granted no loans/advances "other than loans to staff" during the
year, which only reconciles if the Rs165.97 lakh Cybercons balance is a trade/commercial advance
rather than a loan, a distinction the Notes never make explicit [VERIFIED, B03 Phase 2, New Finding].
This is a disclosure-clarity problem layered on top of a classification problem, both centred on an
entity the promoter-directors themselves also govern; flagged, not proven as extraction (RPT/revenue
ratio argues against material value transfer so far).

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour

Dividend: modest, 5% of face value, under 1% of PAT [VERIFIED, B03 Phase 4D], against strong and
improving cash generation (CFO/PAT 0.885x FY25, FCF ~Rs36.8cr) — a low payout relative to delivered
profitability, though reinvestment into a genuine new capex program (CWIP Rs21.95-24.07cr) provides a
plausible growth-stage rationale rather than cash hoarding for its own sake. Promoter remuneration:
Rs3.90cr combined for the three executive directors = 5.39% of standalone PAT, a defensible ratio
(67.69x/61.80x median-employee multiples), not rising through performance declines — remuneration
rose in a year PAT itself rose 34.2%, i.e. moving in the same direction as performance, not against
it [VERIFIED, AR Annexure II, also B03 Phase 5C]. No preferential allotments or promoter warrants
(lapsed or otherwise) were found; the only dilution is a small ESOP tranche (~0.1-0.2% of paid-up
capital) [VERIFIED, B03 Phase 2H]. The one capital-allocation item genuinely worth flagging: the
Section 180(1)(c) resolution at this AGM seeking to raise the borrowing limit to **Rs1,500 crore**,
described only as an "enabling provision" for unnamed "larger projects," with no financing plan or
project list disclosed, against a current debt base of only Rs51.89cr and net worth of Rs230.92cr
(roughly a 6.5x-net-worth blank-authorisation ask) [VERIFIED, AR Notice Item 5, p.24, also B03 Phase
2F/4B]. This is a governance-process concern (an open-ended shareholder ask without an articulated
use) rather than an evidenced value-destructive act to date, but it sits squarely in this section's
scope and should be watched for what it is eventually used to fund.

### 3C. Promoter share transactions

No open-market promoter selling was found; the only share-count movements this year are the ESOP
allotment (company-wide, not promoter-specific) and the family transmission of the late Mrs. Hasumati
Dalal's shares to Mr. Parag Dalal following her passing, explicitly stated to leave the combined

hypothecated for working-capital facilities, an unrelated matter [VERIFIED, B03 Phase 5D]. The
operator-supplied screener SHP likewise carries no pledge column. A direct attempt in this run to
