Niche specialty chemicals leader recovering from trough

Tatva Chintan makes four families of specialty chemicals: phase transfer catalysts, structure directing agents for zeolites, electrolyte salts, and pharma and agro intermediates. It sells business to business to API, agrochemical, auto catalyst and battery customers across roughly 25 to 35 export countries plus India. Revenue crossed Rs 500 Cr in FY26. The company is worth Rs 3,103 Cr at Rs 1,326 a share. The promoter family owns 72% and has pledged nothing.

FY22 to FY26 was a build phase at the Dahej SEZ, and FY26 was the year the plant started to fill. Reactors ran at 64% and assembly lines at just 31% in FY25, so the whole story is operating leverage off idle capacity. Revenue rose 32% from FY25 to FY26 and turned into EBITDA up 172% and PAT up more than six times. EPS went from Rs 2.44 to Rs 17.98.

Gate 0 posture reads AVERAGE, core 48 of 100, moat THIN. The backward score is held down by two things. Free cash flow has been negative in all eight computable years, cumulative free cash flow to PAT of about negative 1.3, set against the Dahej capex cycle that also dragged ROCE from 19 to 25% before FY21 into single digits since, with a 1.17% low in FY25. The second drag is missing data: the quarterly shareholding filings that would show promoter holding change and pledge were never provided, so part of Block E scored zero.

Cash is the open question. Trade receivables grew 18% in FY25 while revenue fell, the top three customers are 61% of the receivables book, and the company holds zero expected credit loss provision against that concentration. The receivables movement was a Rs 305 Mn adverse cash swing in FY25. The credit rating was cut twice inside a year, from CRISIL A minus Stable to A minus Negative to BBB plus Stable.

Management delivered its near term financial guidance and missed its long cycle promises. EBITDA margin reached the promised 20 to 22% band, tracking 18.0% then 19.4% then 20.9% across three quarters, and the electrolyte salts ramp and the new Dahej block landed with only minor delays. Against that, Jolva groundbreaking slipped three straight quarters, each time reframed as engineering optimization, the first semiconductor dispatch missed its Q4 FY26 target with no acknowledgement, and ROCE questions were deflected twice with no forward number. Credibility grades B.

Peers do not confirm the recovery. Two of the four, Clean Science and Neogen, described persistent or reversing soft demand across the same FY26 window Tatva frames as restocking recovery, with Neogen's own tone flipping from steady recovery to subdued demand between quarters. Neogen also cuts against Tatva's claim to be India's sole hybrid battery electrolyte supplier, since it runs its own India electrolyte salt platform that booked Rs 36 Cr in FY26. Peers do partly corroborate the FY26 raw material cost spike and the industry wide capex slippage.

Runway is GOOD. Conservative served market sizing puts total addressable market near Rs 15,200 Cr, current share of the served market around 4%, and revenue headroom around 15 times. Management's own headline market claim is inflated roughly 80 times against a conservative product specific estimate. The three year revenue target of about Rs 754 Cr sits only Rs 23 Cr above the plant capacity ceiling, so a fourth Jolva slip would make the capex plan, not the market, the binding constraint.
