### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

The Q4 FY26 revenue miss is the only genuine "miss" management addresses directly, and the
pattern is **deflection dressed as external-blame**: a real, named external event (Middle
East geopolitical disruption) is cited for the Q4 leg, but the larger, earlier shortfall
(9M growth already 11-16 points below the guidance floor before Q4) is never mentioned,
named, or explained — a silent gap rather than an honest admission. No instance was found
anywhere in the four decks of management saying "we set the wrong target" or "we should
have revised guidance earlier." The Income Tax search and the Enforcement Directorate
matter (see 4D) are, by contrast, raised **proactively** via the company's own curated FAQ
before any journalist or analyst forced the issue in this corpus — a genuinely transparent
instinct on those two items that sits oddly against the silence on the guidance miss.
**Pattern: mixed — proactive on regulatory/legal disclosure, deflective on the operating
guidance miss.** Excuse pattern: **external-blame-heavy** on the one topic (growth) where
blame was actually offered.
