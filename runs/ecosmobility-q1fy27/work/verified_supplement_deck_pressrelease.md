## E. OPERATIONAL KPIs (press release + deck slides 10/15/19/20)
- **Trips Q1FY27 ~1.48 Mn, +27% YoY, +7% QoQ.** FY26 total 5.23 Mn trips. **REVENUE-PER-TRIP fell ~8% YoY** (rev-from-ops +16.7% vs trips +27%): the mix/realisation is DOWN — the mechanical driver of margin compression.
