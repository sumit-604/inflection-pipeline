## FINDING 1 (MAJOR) — anchor/quarter misattribution in Claim 5 evidence

B06, Claim 5 evidence item (3), reads:

> "(3) SONACOMS, Q3 FY26 call (Jan-2026): BEV revenue and BEV mix hit second-highest
> levels 'despite a very sharp decline in the North America EV market,' attributed
> partly to strong India EV demand; Vivek Vikram Singh notes a 'growing concern in
> the government' to accelerate electrification from '20% to cover... across not just
> automotive,' implying strong policy-driven momentum, not flatness."

This bundles two quotes under one call citation. The first half ("second-highest
levels... despite a very sharp decline") is genuinely in the Jan-2026 (Q3 FY26) transcript
(p.3) — CONFIRMED. The second half — the "growing concern in the government... 20% to
cover... across not just automotive" quote — does **not** appear anywhere in the Jan-2026
transcript. It is verbatim in the SONACOMS Q4 FY26 call (30-Apr-2026, filed as
`SONACOMS-Concall_May_2026_Transcript.pdf`), p.15, in Vivek Vikram Singh's answer to
Gunjan Prithyani's traction-motor question.

This is a genuine quote from the peer set — not a fabrication — but it is misattributed
to the wrong quarter/call. A reader checking the Jan-2026 transcript specifically for this
quote, per B06's citation, would not find it there. Per the Verifier D rubric ("SUBSTANTIVE
without a real, findable citation is MAJOR"), this is graded MAJOR: the claimed anchor
does not contain the material, even though the underlying peer evidence for Claim 5 as a
whole remains sound (SONACOMS's other two quotes, plus both ENDURANCE quotes, independently
confirm the CONTRADICTED verdict without needing this one line). The verdict itself is not
put at risk — only the specific anchor is wrong.

**Severity: MAJOR** (source-fidelity defect on a claim-supporting anchor; does not change
the verdict).
