# Forward view: 2026-10-30 shallow screen, nine names

Step 13 of the Shallow Analysis Framework (operator ruling 2026-09-21), with
the two additions of 2026-10-29 carried on every card: an FY28 line on the same
tier rules, and a confidence band on each estimate. The additions are still not
in the framework text.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result. The two cheapest names below both carry LOW
confidence, and one of them is a PASS.

## Price basis

Market capitalisation is the screener.in company page fetched on 30 October
2026 at about 09:00 IST, held at
`screens/corpus/<TICKER>/market-data-2026-10-30.md`. It is a service record,
not a filed document. The P/E, book value and ROCE on those pages are stale for
DCMSRIND, SINGERIND and PRASOLCHEM; the cards use only price and market cap.
Every earnings figure traces to a filing, cited in the card.

## Confidence, as defined in the brief

The probability that the actual year's PAT lands within 15% of the estimate.
HIGH is about 70% or better, MEDIUM about 50%, LOW under 40%. Each card names
the one observation that would move the band.

## The screen result, read honestly

No name is both cheap on forward earnings and well evidenced.

- **Cheapest, and LOW confidence.** SAMPARK at 12.1x rests on a trend, not a
  run-rate, because its own filings disagree on whether the business is
  seasonal. Four years of operating cash flow total about Rs 3.5 crore against
  Rs 28.9 crore of profit. DCMSRIND at 16.9x is falling earnings: the listed
  company kept only the sugar mill after the scheme, and Q1 FY27 PAT fell 85%.
- **MEDIUM confidence, not cheap.** TCPLPACK, CREDENT and PRASOLCHEM sit at 24x
  to 30x. TCPL's base year carries a Rs 18 crore forex loss that flatters the
  growth. PRASOL's Q1 holds a Rs 25.92 crore inventory gain that annualises to
  a false 19.7x. CREDENT's operating cash flow was negative in FY26 and H1 FY27.
- **Not rankable as a buy screen.** SAKUMA's multiple ignores a pending 41.5%
  share issue at Rs 1.66; most of its profit is other income. OPTIEMUS at about
  84x is a PASS on governance and cash, not on price.

## Basis notes that change the number

- **SAKUMA.** Market cap is pre-issue. After 65 crore new shares the same
  price gives a market cap about 41.5% higher, so the forward PE rises to about
  37x on the base reading [INFERENCE].
- **GRERENEW.** FY27E removes a Rs 1.24 crore liquidated-damages one-off from
  H2 FY26 before annualising.
- **CREDENT.** FY27E doubles H1 PAT after removing a Rs 1.78 crore section
  80JJAA tax credit from one half.
- **PRASOLCHEM.** Tier A rests on guided EBITDA of Rs 240 to 250 crore, which
  excludes the Q1 stock gain.
- **SINGERIND.** CRISIL upgraded the rating to BBB/Stable on 29-Oct-2026. The
  government order runs into Q3 FY27, so the first quarter without it is Q4.
