## EVIDENCE TAXONOMY (applied throughout)
📄 DOCUMENTED — capex committed, contract signed, acquisition/SPA completed, product
launched/moved to production, regulatory filing, specific quantified metric disclosed
in the filed Annual Report. 🎙️ MANAGEMENT CLAIM — stated in concall or presentation
narrative, not backed by a contract, filing, or specific disclosed number. 🔍 ANALYST
INFERENCE — inferred from data patterns, not directly stated.

Caution on AR narrative: the FY26 AR is explicitly repositioned around an "AI-first
knowledge company" theme ("The Engine of Knowledge"). Specific quantified facts
stated in the filed AR (headcounts, manuscript counts, acquisition dates and
considerations, financial ratios) are treated as 📄. Qualitative narrative framing
("we sit at the layer where trusted knowledge makes AI dependable") is not evidence
and is excluded from scoring regardless of how it is worded.
