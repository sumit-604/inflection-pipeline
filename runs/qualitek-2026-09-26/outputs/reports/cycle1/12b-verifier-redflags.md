## 4. CREDIBILITY GRADE

I concur with grade C. The FY25 revenue miss is smaller than B05 states. That
moves one negative from MODERATE toward MINOR. Three missed items add weight
on the other side:
- The AR quietly removes three long-term targets set four months earlier.
- Rs 22 Cr is earmarked for unidentified acquisitions in a new cybersecurity
  vertical.
- A second consecutive year shows guidance changing between the deck and the
  AR.

The positives stand: clean IPO fund use, promoter loan repayment, Mumbai
delivered on time, and volunteered new-lab losses. The grade holds at the
NO-CONCALL default.
