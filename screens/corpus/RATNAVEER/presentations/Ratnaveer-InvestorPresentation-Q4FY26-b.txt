DIVERSIFICATION : COPPER CLAD LAMINATES MANUFACTURING
As part of its strategic diversification initiative, Ratnaveer is establishing a fully integrated Copper Clad Laminates (CCL)
manufacturing facility in Vadodara, Gujarat. Focused on FR-4 grade laminates, the project aims to strengthen India’s
electronics and PCB ecosystem through advanced manufacturing technology, import substitution, and scalable production
capabilities. With a strong focus on quality and innovation,  the facility is designed to cater to growing domestic and future
export demand in high-growth technology sectors.
