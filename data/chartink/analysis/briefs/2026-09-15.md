## What would change this read

The cautious read deepens if the count above the two-hundred-day line closes
under about 900. That would take out the 11 June low of 902 and leave only the
May low behind it. It deepens if fresh yearly lows hold near one stock in
twenty-three through Friday. It deepens if a second washout arrives before any
thrust, as happened in March. It also deepens if healthcare drops under half its
stocks above the forty-week line. That would take away the last broad leader.

The cautious read fails if a thrust day arrives, 300 or more stocks jumping four
and a half percent in one session. It fails if the twenty-day count climbs back
above about 800 within a week, which would mark Tuesday as a shakeout. It fails
if the two-hundred-day count returns above about 1,150, just under Friday's
reading. It also fails if the large-cap list above the day's average price climbs
back above fifty while more than a quarter of bank stocks return above their
four-week line.
