## 6. THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**What could make revenue keep firing.** The order book is Rs2,964cr against Rs1,424cr of annual revenue and executes over 18 to 24 months on management's own estimate. On top of that sits a Rs5,100cr bidding pipeline as at May 2026 that explicitly excludes the RBI award already won, at a stated 30% historical conversion. The named wins are large and public: RBI private cloud at Rs750.82cr, Central Bank of India AI ready cloud with H200 GPUs at Rs125.88cr, Punjab and Sind Bank at Rs108.88cr, plus the NABARD core banking rollout that is adding cooperative banks state by state. Confirmation is book to bill staying above 1.2x and quarterly revenue tracking toward Rs1,700cr for FY27. What kills it is a stalled tender cycle or losing a renewal at one of the top ten customers, who are about 48% of revenue.

**What could make margin start.** The mix keeps moving toward data centre, cloud and managed services, which management says are margin accretive and which the segment disclosure supports. Operating leverage on a larger base helps. Confirmation is Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin back above 11%. What kills it is a second and third quarter near 9%, which would turn a blip into a level, or more deliberate margin sacrifice to win logos.

**What could make cash convert. This is where the catalyst search comes up close to empty.** There is no sanctioned factoring facility, no stated debtor day target, no working capital programme with a date, and no increase in the bad debt allowance despite a receivable tail that grew 239%. The only thing pointing the right way is the rising share of annuity revenue, which collects more evenly than project milestones, and that is slow and partly offset by the fact that the as a service model consumes cash upfront by design. Confirmation would be a filed receivables facility or debtor days below 130 on rising revenue. What kills it is debtor days above 160 with the ageing tail still widening.

**What could make capital efficiency recover.** The Rs158cr of newly deployed assets and the Rs65cr of new premises start earning against contracted five year revenue, and the order book converts on a capital base that does not need to grow as fast again. Confirmation is ROCE stabilising above 28% in the FY27 numbers with capital employed growing slower than operating profit. What kills it is another year of capital employed growing faster than profit, which is what the current device as a service pipeline implies.

**Management intent and action ledger.** On revenue, vision and action agree and the action is documented, so no adjustment is warranted or needed. On margin, there is vision ("we are working towards sustaining these margin profiles") and the documented mix shift, but the one hard recent data point went the other way, so the ledger confirms rather than loosens. On cash, there is vision ("disciplined working capital management") and no documented action at all, which under the protocol confirms the conservative read rather than softening it. On capital efficiency, there is vision ("keep ROCE broadly steady") and documented capex, but the capex is the cause of the decline rather than a cure for it. The ledger loosens nothing here, and on the binding transition it is the AIMTRON pattern exactly: words without a filing behind them.
