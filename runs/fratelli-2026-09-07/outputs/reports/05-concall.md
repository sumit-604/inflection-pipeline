### 1C. Trigger evolution across five quarters

- **Shotgun RTD**: strengthening and broadly ON TRACK. Q4FY25 "soft launch," 4 states; Q1FY26 9 states/5% share; Q2FY26 11 states/6% share, ~50,000 cases H1; Q3FY26 18 states/~7,000 outlets, targeting 100,000 cases; Q4FY26 actual 18 states/9,000 outlets/100,000 cases/Rs18cr revenue (target substantially met, revenue slightly below the Q3 call's implied ~Rs20cr). This is the one trigger with a clean, verifiable delivery record.
- **Hospitality/wine tourism**: WEAKENING, timeline and capex slipping every single quarter it is discussed. Q4FY25: "still in planning stage," target open 2027. Q1FY26: Rs100cr budget stated, then corrected DOWN to Rs65-75cr in the same call. Q2FY26: Rs100cr again, planning an equity fundraise, hoping to break ground "early 2026," open "2028." Q3FY26: "minimal capex... have not broken ground," term sheet signed with an operator, "premature" to give timeline. Q4FY26: project explicitly DEFERRED by about a year, capex re-set to Rs70-80cr, "review in H2 [FY27]... action in calendar year 27-28." Net effect: five quarters of discussion, zero ground broken, target opening year pushed at least one year, and the funding plan (debt vs equity) never settled. FLAG: timeline slippage.
- **EBITDA margin path**: numbers keep resetting without ever being reconciled to the audited P&L. Q4FY25: long-term "10% to 20%" aspiration, stabilizing 2028-2030. Q1FY26: "back to 9-10%" near-term target plus 200-250bps of cost savings. Q2FY26: no explicit near-term number given despite a direct analyst question ("premature to predict"). Q3FY26: explicit "10-12% FY27" target given, plus "15-20% long term, it's an issue of scale." Q4FY26: FY27 net-net breakeven re-affirmed at ~Rs240cr revenue. See Section 2 for the finding that the reported EBITDA figures behind this trajectory (Q3 and full-year FY26) do not reconcile to the audited/filed P&L.
- **FY26 revenue growth guidance**: WEAKENING every single quarter for four consecutive quarters (20-25% → 15-20% → 12-15% → ~7%) before settling near flat on the final call. This is the single cleanest guidance-walkdown pattern in the dataset (Section 2A quantifies it).
- **New/dropped triggers**: CSD channel and exports appear as growing themes only from Q2FY26 onward (not mentioned as a distinct driver in Q4FY25 or Q1FY26); this is a genuinely NEW trigger with a plausible explanation (product approvals took time), not a red flag on its own. No trigger was quietly dropped without acknowledgement, except that "merging the holdco with Fratelli to save cost" (raised and answered "we are evaluating it" on the Q4FY25 call) is never mentioned again in any of the subsequent four calls — a dropped item worth noting.

### DETAILED RECONCILIATION — Finding A: the Q3FY26 "PBT/PAT turned positive" claim

On the Q3FY26 call (16-Feb-2026), Aditya Sekhri stated: *"At the profitability level, we reported a positive PBT of roughly INR0.1 crores compared to a loss in the corresponding period last year. PAT also turned positive, reflecting stronger operating performance."* (Concall_Q3FY26_Feb2026_Transcript.pdf, p.4-5)

The filed consolidated results for the same quarter (Results_Q3FY26_2026-02-12.pdf, p.10), published four days earlier, show: Loss before tax Rs(495.42) lakh, Net loss Rs(872.14) lakh, EPS Rs(2.01). This is a large loss, not a profit.

Reconciliation: earlier in the SAME call, Gaurav Sekhri disclosed a one-time item: *"In the holdco level, Fratelli Vineyards, we have taken a onetime write-off of approximately INR5 crores to settle a long overdue receivable"* (p.4). The FY26 audited standalone cash flow statement confirms a "Balance Write off" of Rs512.82 lakh for the year (Results_Q4FY26_and_FY26_2026-05-30.pdf, p.9). Adding this back to the consolidated PBT: -495.42 + 512.82 = **+17.4 lakh = Rs +0.17cr**, which matches the "roughly INR0.1 crores" PBT claim almost exactly. The same add-back to the recomputed EBITDA (-495.42+319.39+213.88=37.85, +512.82=550.67 lakh = **Rs 5.51cr**) matches the claimed "INR5.5 crores" EBITDA almost exactly too.

So the EBITDA and PBT figures cited on the call are, in substance, EX the one-time write-off — but this exclusion is never labelled "adjusted" in the specific sentence where the numbers are given (the write-off is mentioned three paragraphs earlier, by a different speaker, in a different context). An investor relying on the transcript alone, without independently re-deriving the add-back, would reasonably believe GAAP PBT turned positive in Q3 FY26. It did not.

Critically, the "PAT also turned positive" half of the claim does NOT reconcile even with this generous adjustment: adding back the same Rs512.82 lakh to the net loss of Rs(872.14) lakh still leaves **Rs -3.59cr**, a loss — because the quarter also carried a Rs367.99 lakh (Rs3.68cr) deferred-tax charge that management never mentions when making the "PAT turned positive" claim. Under no reasonable reading of the filed numbers does PAT "turn positive" in Q3 FY26.

### DETAILED RECONCILIATION — Finding B: the Q4FY26 call's "+1% YoY growth" framing

On the Q4FY26 call (02-Jun-2026), Aditya Sekhri stated: *"Net revenue from operations stood at INR184 crores in FY26 compared to INR181 crores in FY25, representing growth of approximately 1% year-on-year."* (Concall_Q4FY26_Jun2026_Transcript.pdf, p.5)

The company's own AUDITED CONSOLIDATED FY25 print — the number the company itself reported to the exchange as "FY25 consolidated revenue" a year earlier, and which every FY26 quarterly filing this stage reviewed cites as the FY25 comparator — is Rs302.10cr (30,209.66 lakh; Results_Q3FY26_2026-02-12.pdf p.10 and Results_Q4FY26_and_FY26_2026-05-30.pdf p.16, both consolidated). Against that true comparator, FY26's Rs181.29cr represents a 40.0% DECLINE, not +1% growth — the identical "40.0% decline" figure B02 (Stage 2) already computed from the audited P&L.

The Rs181cr figure management cites as "FY25" reconciles only to the wine-segment/subsidiary-standalone FY25 number (Rs178.44cr, per Note 37 and the Directors' Report table) — the same figure B02 already found was mislabeled "Consolidated FY 2024-25" in the Annual Report. Per CLAUDE.md's standing instruction to check the basis whenever management repeats a growth number on a call: this basis is wine-only-to-wine-only, not consolidated-to-consolidated, and the call gives no indication that the comparator has changed composition (FY25 still carried a substantial legacy agro/steel trading book; FY26 does not). Framed honestly, the wine-only comparison (~+1-2% growth) is a defensible like-for-like number for the operating business Fratelli is asking investors to underwrite going forward; framed as "revenue... representing growth of approximately 1% year-on-year" without any qualifier, it repeats — on a live call, to analysts — the exact ambiguity B02 flagged as a Red-rated finding in the Annual Report. No analyst on the call caught or queried this; several analysts across the five calls (e.g., Deepesh Sancheti, Q1FY26 call, confused about a "Rs150cr to Rs36cr" comparison) have visibly struggled with which entity/basis is being discussed, and management has never devoted a slide or a standing explanation to permanently resolving this for the audience.

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

Every quarter in which growth disappointed, a DIFFERENT external, regulatory, or macro cause was cited: Q4FY25 (Maharashtra/AP/Delhi excise policy changes, elections/dry-days, internal supply-chain transition); Q1FY26 (Maharashtra spirits excise hike spillover, urban consumption slowdown); Q2FY26 (Telangana/Karnataka retail-license transition, ~20% of revenue base); Q3FY26 (same regulatory factors continuing, plus EU FTA overhang as a forward risk); Q4FY26 (same factors "normalizing," framed as resolved). This is a consistent, rotating EXTERNAL-BLAME pattern: management credits itself for what goes well (premiumization, RTD execution) and attributes nearly every shortfall to a state-level regulatory event outside its control. No instance across five transcripts of management volunteering "we got this wrong" on a growth or margin call; the closest is Aditya's Q3FY26 admission that the premium segment "has been down about 13% to 14% roughly for us" (Concall_Q3FY26_Feb2026_Transcript.pdf, p.15) — a factual concession, but still bundled with the regulatory-cause explanation, not an ownership statement.

**Classification: external-blame-heavy.**
