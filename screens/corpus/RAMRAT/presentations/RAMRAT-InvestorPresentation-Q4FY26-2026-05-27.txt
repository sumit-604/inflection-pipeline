===== Copper Tubes & Pipes slides =====

segment."

contribution."
"Full-range Level Wound Coil (LWC) production successfully commenced; Inner Grooved Tubes (IGT)
production currently in progress."

copper tubes & pipes market, enabling large-scale import substitution while serving global and
domestic HVAC demand."
