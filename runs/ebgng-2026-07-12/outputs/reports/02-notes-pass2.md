### 1. CORRECTION — Note 38 RPT: Electronics Bazar Inc (USA) Sales figures were mis-attributed
by one year in Pass 1; correct chain reveals an undisclosed, unrelated top customer in FY24.
Re-checking the "Current Period/Year" vs "Previous Year" columns across all four RPT tables
(Note 38, pp.282-283) shows the correct Sales chain for Electronics Bazar Inc (USA) is: FY22
₹0.28M → FY23 ₹476.35M → FY24 ₹1,298.72M → H1FY25 ₹1,092.41M (Pass 1 had shifted this back one
year: "FY22 ₹1.02M → FY23 ₹0.28M → FY24 ₹476.35M"). Purchases chain in Pass 1 was correct and
unaffected (FY23 ₹669.12M → FY24 ₹1,661.70M → H1FY25 ₹890.57M).
This changes the FY24 customer-concentration picture in Note 39 (p.287): FY24 discloses two
customers >10% of sales — Customer 1 = ₹1,966.22M (17.28% of FY24 revenue ₹11,381.38M) and
Customer 2 = ₹1,298.72M (11.41%). Customer 2's figure now ties exactly to Electronics Bazar
Inc (USA)'s corrected FY24 sales (₹1,298.72M) — i.e. the related party is the SMALLER of the
two FY24 top customers. **Customer 1 (₹1,966.22M, 17.28% of FY24 revenue) is a separate,
larger customer whose identity is not disclosed anywhere in the notes and does not match any
related party in Note 38.** The H1FY25 conclusion in Pass 1 (top customer = related party,
₹1,092.41M = 17.97%, matches Electronics Bazar Inc exactly) is confirmed correct and unaffected.
🔴 Red Flag — an undisclosed customer representing 17.3% of FY24 revenue is a data gap in its
own right, separate from and larger than the related-party concentration issue already flagged.
(Note 38, pp.282-283; Note 39, p.287)

### 7. Note 36 — the lease-liability roll-forward table contains an internal arithmetic error at
H1FY25 that produces a nonsensical negative closing balance.
The lease-liability movement table (p.279) shows, for the period ended 30-Sep-2024: Opening
balance ₹0.00 (should be FY24's closing balance of ₹94.28M, which is what the table shows in
the prior column), Additions nil, Accretion of interest ₹8.37M, Payments ₹(21.22)M, **Closing
balance ₹(12.85)M** — a negative figure that does not reconcile to the actual lease liability
total of ₹81.43M shown in the same note's separate current/non-current summary table (which
correctly ties to Notes 17 and 20: ₹54.51M + ₹26.92M = ₹81.43M). Correcting the opening balance

error in a statutory note, of the same character as the Note 45(a)(2) equity-reconciliation

correctly rolled forward). 🔴 Red Flag (data-integrity) — should be raised together with the
Note 45 finding as evidence of a broader note-drafting quality-control weakness in this DRHP.
(Note 36, p.279, vs. Note 17 p.269 / Note 20 p.272)
