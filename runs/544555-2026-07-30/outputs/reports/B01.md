**Decision line:** Ameenji Rubber shows genuine top-line and margin momentum
through FY25 (revenue CAGR 18.5%, PAT CAGR 21.9%, three consecutive years of
positive ROE >35%), but every cash-quality metric fails mechanically: cumulative
operating cash flow covers only 38.6% of cumulative profit over FY23–26, free

went public. Combined with only 4 years of usable history (mandating a one-tier
downgrade) and a Block D balance sheet that is levered close to (though not
past) the ND/EBITDA >3x / IC <3x double-trigger, the mechanical Gate 0 score
lands at AVOID. This is a scorecard classification only — per pipeline rules, it
does not halt the run; flags propagate to downstream stages for human weighing.
