Dear Sir/Madam,

We draw your kind attention to the Notice dated August 29, 2026 of the Eighth Annual
General Meeting (“AGM”) of Qualitek Labs Limited (“Company”) scheduled to be held on
Friday, September 25, 2026 at 11:30 A.M., which was filed with the Stock Exchange and
circulated to the Members of the Company.

The said AGM Notice, inter alia, included Item No. 4 as a Special Resolution for approval
for increase in the overall remuneration payable to Directors, including Managing Director
and Whole-time Directors, from the existing limit of 28% to 35% of the net profits of the
Company computed in accordance with Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The Board of Directors of the Company, after due consideration, has today, i.e. September
7, 2026, by way of a Circular Resolution, approved the withdrawal of Item No. 4 from the
Notice of the Eighth Annual General Meeting.

Accordingly, Item No. 4 shall not be considered, discussed or put to vote at the Eighth
Annual General Meeting. Further, no remote e-voting or voting at the AGM shall be
conducted in respect of Item No. 4.

The existing approval of the Members in respect of the overall managerial remuneration
limit of 28% of the net profits of the Company computed in accordance with Section 198
of the Companies Act, 2013, as approved at the Seventh Annual General Meeting, shall
continue to remain in force.
[page 2]
The Company shall issue a Corrigendum to the Notice of the Eighth Annual General
Meeting and communicate the same to the Members. The Corrigendum shall also be made
available on the website of the Company.

The withdrawal of Item No. 4 shall have no effect on the other businesses proposed in the
Notice of the Eighth Annual General Meeting, which shall remain unchanged.

You are requested to kindly take the above information on your records.
