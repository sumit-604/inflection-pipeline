## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

### 2A. Demand environment
All three peers report the same demand rhythm DPABHUSHAN describes: a strong Oct-Nov 2025 festive run
(Navratri/Dhanteras/Diwali), a strong April 2026 Akshaya Tritiya, a May-June 2026 soft patch (Adhik
Maas, heat wave, the PM's appeal, and for SENCO specifically West Bengal elections), and a July-August
2026 recovery. This is broad consensus with DPABHUSHAN's framing on timing. The one divergence: none
of the peers reports anything close to DPABHUSHAN's claimed 20-29% industry-wide volume decline; their
disclosed volume trends are milder (SENCO -10% 9M FY26) or positive (PNGJL +25-27%), and revenue growth
across the peer set through FY26 was consistently strong (SENCO +33% FY26, PNGJL +40% FY26, KALYANKJIL
+43% FY26 consolidated) — the sector as a whole was not contracting.

### 2B. Pricing and input costs
Universal agreement that gold price appreciation (50-70%+ YoY through FY26) is the primary revenue
driver, that consumers are shifting toward lightweight/lower-karat (18k/14k/9k) jewellery, and that
old-gold exchange share has risen sharply everywhere (SENCO 25-30% -> 50%+; PNGJL ~40% -> mid-40s;
KALYANKJIL ~30% -> 46-55%+). This matches DPABHUSHAN's own mix narrative. Where peers diverge sharply
from DPABHUSHAN's implicit framing: all three repeatedly and explicitly separate "making-charge income"
(which they say does not depend on gold price) from "inventory/realisation gain" (which does), and
quantify the latter as a specific, usually modest, percentage-point add-on — never as the dominant
driver of margin improvement DPABHUSHAN's 25-30%-of-improvement framing implies.

### 2C. Capex cycle
This is an industry-wide capacity race, not a lone-expander situation. SENCO plans 18-20+ stores/year
toward 300+ stores by FY30 (from ~200 today); PNGJL plans 25+/year toward 140-177 stores by FY29 (from
78); KALYANKJIL plans 80-90+ Kalyan India stores/year plus 50-55 Candere stores/year plus a new
regional-brand format, alongside continuing conversion of COCO to FOCO in the South. All three are
scaling FOCO/franchise mix as the primary growth engine, matching DPABHUSHAN's own pivot toward FOCO
(Jabalpur pilot). DPABHUSHAN's absolute pace (51 stores by FY30, from 12) is aggressive relative to its
own base but small in absolute store-count terms next to any of the three peers, all of which are
opening more incremental stores per year than DPABHUSHAN's entire planned FY30 network.

### 2D. Competitive mentions
No peer transcript names DPABHUSHAN, Ratlam, or any Madhya Pradesh/Rajasthan regional competitor by
name. The one directly relevant data point: PNGJL entered Indore in FY26 (DPABHUSHAN's flagship claimed
outperformance market) and reports early success there — a store reaching 1.4x annualized stock turn in
its first year (Q4 FY26 call). This is a real, named national/regional entrant into DPABHUSHAN's home
turf performing well, which sits uneasily next to DPABHUSHAN's own unverified claim of dominating a
"leading national jewellery brand" in the same city.

### 2E. Risks peers discuss that DPABHUSHAN does not
- GML/MCX margin-call mechanics: SENCO discusses in detail how margin calls on hedging positions forced
  it to cut its hedge ratio from 80-90% to 40-60% (Nov-2025, Feb-2026 calls); DPABHUSHAN's calls never
  discuss margin-call mechanics despite running GML and MCX positions per its own AR.
- Corporate governance / related-party overhang: KALYANKJIL's Aug-2026 call features a direct analyst
  question referencing a Moneylife magazine report and mutual-fund concerns about RPTs and pledging;
  management answers on the record. No analyst raises DPABHUSHAN's own FY26 AR-flagged RPT disclosures
  or the secretarial note on late XBRL filing of RPTs on any of its four calls.
- Promoter shareholding / SEBI MPS deadline: PNGJL discusses its statutory requirement to bring promoter
  holding down to 75% by September 2027 as an active dilution overhang tied to a hard regulatory

  deadline.
- Labour code cost impact: SENCO quantifies a one-time INR6.2-6.5cr exceptional cost from the new labour
  code; no DPABHUSHAN call mentions labour code costs.
- Employee attrition: KALYANKJIL is asked directly about a 50%+ attrition rate in one division and
  answers with a specific explanation (door-to-door marketing division turnover); no DPABHUSHAN call
  touches employee/HR risk.
- Credit rating mechanics: all three peers discuss rating actions with quantified borrowing-cost

## PART 5: CROSS-PEER HYPOTHESIS

Across the three peers, hedging strategy is emerging as a genuine strategic bifurcation, not a shared
industry constraint, and each peer's position on that spectrum tracks tightly with how much quantified
guidance-reconciliation it is willing to give.

At one pole, KALYANKJIL runs close to fully hedged on gold ("we do not take any margin benefit... we
are fully protected," repeated across all four calls), which converts its margin story into an almost
pure function of mix and cost control — and accordingly it needs to explain very little about gold-price
swings, because there mostly aren't any to explain.

At the other pole, SENCO and PNGJL both run deliberately partial, actively flexed hedge ratios (SENCO:
40-70% across the four quarters reviewed, explicitly adjusted for margin-call and liquidity pressure;
PNGJL: 57% rising to 67% with an explicit glide path to "fully hedged" by FY27) — and both compensate for
that exposure by disclosing the exact ratio and the exact rupee gain or loss it produced, every single
quarter, unprompted.

The testable hypothesis: disclosed hedge ratio and guidance-reconciliation quality are two faces of the
same underlying management-quality variable in this sector, not independent traits. A jeweller that is
willing to tell investors its exact hedge percentage every quarter is, by the same organisational
discipline, willing to bridge a guidance miss the same quarter; a jeweller that hedges partially but
will not quantify how much, or explain what the resulting gain or loss was, should be read as signalling
the same underlying disclosure weakness that produces unreconciled guidance misses. DPABHUSHAN fits
neither pole SENCO/PNGJL/KALYANKJIL occupy — it is significantly unhedged (a new 42kg position per its
own FY26 AR) yet silent about the ratio and the resulting exposure, which on this hypothesis is not an
isolated hedging-disclosure gap but the same trait driving its guidance-discipline problem (Claim 7).
Watch whether any future DPABHUSHAN disclosure (concall or investor deck) begins volunteering a
quantified hedge ratio; on this hypothesis, that single change would be a leading indicator of broader
disclosure-quality improvement, and continued silence is itself diagnostic.
