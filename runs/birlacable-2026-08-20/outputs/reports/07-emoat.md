### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic
- Net fixed assets (Net Block, PPE, Note 2): FY25 ₹12,821.42 lakh; FY26 ₹10,989.12 lakh (AR p.91). Average net block ≈ ₹11,905.27 lakh = ₹119.05cr.
- Standalone Revenue from Operations FY26 = ₹77,111.40 lakh = ₹771.11cr (AR p.20/p.46).
- Implied fixed-asset turnover (FAT) = ₹771.11cr / ₹119.05cr ≈ **6.48x**.
- Capex under execution (outstanding capital commitment, Note 36(b)) = ₹0.0395cr.
- Implied incremental revenue = ₹0.0395cr × 6.48 ≈ **₹0.26cr**.
- As % of FY26 revenue (₹771.11cr): **≈0.03%**.

Conclusion: the capex pipeline embeds economically immaterial forward growth. The real capacity-expansion event already happened and finished in FY24-25 (₹30.11cr added, no overrun per Note 47); FY25-26 was a maintenance-capex year, and FY26-27 opens with almost no committed capital spend on record.
