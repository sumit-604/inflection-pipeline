### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three quarters

- **Saudi Dammam greenfield SAW plant**: Q2 FY26 call — "on track," commissioning "Quarter 4 2026." Q3 FY26 call — pushed to Q1 FY27, explicitly challenged by an analyst as a slip from the original "Q4" date; management says "pretty much on time." Q4 FY26 call — **project scrapped entirely**, replaced by the NPC acquisition (Yash Mehta Q&A, p13: "it's scrapped because of the NPC acquisition"). Trend: **dropped**, reaffirmed as on-track one quarter before being abandoned.
- **Jammu SS plant**: Q2 FY26 call — 1-Apr-2026 firm target. Q3 FY26 call — "slightly delayed," blamed on war and flooding. Q4 FY26 call — FY28 first-year utilisation guided at 35%-40%, implying commissioning has slipped into FY27, well past the original date. Trend: **weakening / timeline slipping**, ~1+ year delay.
- **FY27 consolidated revenue guidance**: Q2 FY26 call — ~Rs.7,000 cr. Q3 FY26 call — internally inconsistent within the same call (25%-30% growth "guideline," "internal goal" 50%-55%, an explicit Rs.5,500-6,000 cr figure, and a reference to a still-earlier Rs.6,500 cr number an analyst quotes back at management). Q4 FY26 call — formal guidance settles at Rs.5,000-5,500 cr, excluding Merino. Trend: **weakening**, cut roughly 21%-29% from the original Rs.7,000 cr figure inside two quarters.
- **EBITDA margin guidance**: Q2 FY26 call — 11%-12%. Q3 FY26 call — upgraded to 13%-14%. Q4 FY26 call — delivered 13% consolidated / 14% standalone, both at or near the top of guided range. Trend: **strengthening**, the one guidance line genuinely delivered and improved on.
- **Order book**: falls every quarter across the primary set (Rs.4,750 cr → Rs.4,000 cr → Rs.3,000 cr) even as each call is framed as "best ever" on the P&L. Trend: **weakening**, not proactively addressed by management until an analyst raised it directly in the Q4 FY26 call.
- **Merino Shelters real estate**: present in every call with a different number and a different launch date each time (see 1B table). Trend: **unchanged in narrative enthusiasm, changing in every specific figure** — a specificity red flag, not a "dropped" trigger.
- **Aramco relationship**: appears as a new, vaguely defined trigger in the Q3 FY26 call ("premature... work in progress"), then substantiated with concrete content in the Q4 FY26 call once NPC (with its own 2-decade Aramco relationship) was acquired. Trend: **new trigger, later strengthened by the acquisition** — the timing suggests the Q3 mention was an early signal of the NPC deal being worked on, not disclosed at the time.
- **US market expansion**: raised once by an analyst in the Q2 FY26 call; management says "no plans currently." Never mentioned again in Q3 or Q4 FY26 calls. Trend: **dropped** (a minor trigger, not central to the thesis).

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

For each miss, the reason given and its classification:
- FY26 revenue growth miss: **silence** — no call explicitly states the target was missed; framed instead as a margin "upgrade."
- H2 FY26 revenue shortfall: **silence** — never reconciled against the Rs.2,200-2,400 cr promise in any later call.
- Saudi plant abandonment: **deflection** — reframed as an opportunistic upgrade ("we got a good deal") rather than an admission that the originally guided project and its Q3 FY26 "on track" status were wrong.
- Jammu delay: **external-blame** — war and flooding named explicitly (Q3 FY26 call, p15).
- FY27 guidance cuts: **external-blame** — commodity/steel-price volatility cited as the reason forecasts move (Q3 FY26 call, p13-14).
- Forex MTM loss in consolidated EBITDA: **external-blame** — currency depreciation described as unpredicted ("nobody predicted war"), with a partial **honest-admission** element (management agreed, under analyst pressure, to add an "extraordinary note" so investors are not misled by the headline EBITDA gap — Q4 FY26 call, p15).
- Order-book decline: **deflection** — answered by pointing to the bid pipeline and NPC rather than addressing why the confirmed order book itself is shrinking.

Pattern check: management blames externals (war, floods, currency, commodity prices) for every miss and never states "we got the [revenue] guidance wrong." The one clear exception — conceding the forex/EBITDA disclosure critique — came only after direct, repeated analyst pressure (Q4 FY26 call), not proactively. Hard topics (order-book decline, the Saudi plant's abandonment, SEBI stay order) were raised by analysts, not volunteered by management.

**excuse_pattern: external-blame-heavy**

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. What management says about competitors (with credibility check)

- **Welspun Corp**: cited as putting up a ~350,000 tpa LSAW mill in Saudi via its East Pipes equity stake, which already has ~400,000-500,000 tpa capacity (Q1 FY26 call, p12-13, Pritesh Chheda Q&A). - **Jindal (SAW)**: cited as putting up ~300,000-400,000 tpa spiral capacity in a Saudi partnership (Q1 FY26 call, p13). Management is imprecise on the exact tonnage ("300,000 or 400,000") — a minor specificity gap but not evasive.
- **US market competitors**: referenced generically by an analyst (Rohan Rawat, Q2 FY26 call, p11-12) noting a competitor's US presence; management does not name the competitor and states no US plans currently. No independent claim made, low credibility risk here since management simply declined to compete rather than overstating its position.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence dropped in the calls

- Saudi LSAW market ~2 mn tons/yr, HSAW ~1.5-2 mn tons/yr demand, versus domestic Saudi annual supply of LSAW ~0.8-0.9 mn tons and spiral ~0.7-0.8 mn tons — a structural demand-supply gap cited as the rationale for Saudi capacity investment (Q1 FY26 call, p12-13, Pritesh Chheda Q&A).
- Saudi/GCC infrastructure demand growth cited at "around 6% CAGR... 6-8% CAGR" across water, oil, gas, hydrogen and carbon capture (Q1 FY26 call, p14).
- Jal Jeevan Mission revival, ~Rs.67,000 cr Union Budget allocation for drinking water/sanitation, expected to lift Central government spending in the "next fiscal year," domestic recovery expected "meaningful... in FY27," with "pace of execution and grants to states" flagged by management itself as a key monitorable (Q3 FY26 call, p2-3).
- DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shift: more than 70% of the current year's business volume moved to DDP terms (Q4 FY26 call, p12), inflating "other expenses" and revenue on a matched, non-margin-diluting basis per management (Q3 FY26 call, p4; Q4 FY26 call, p5-6).
- Hormuz Strait disruption tied to a 2026 regional war: ~20-25% of shipments affected, one shipment rerouted via Fujairah instead of Abu Dhabi, management states no revenue decline and Q1 FY27 internal targets remain on track (Q4 FY26 call, p16-17, p20).
- Aramco's Vision 2030 $80 bn water infrastructure program cited as the medium-term opportunity underpinning NPC's growth (Q4 FY26 call, p3).

## SECTION 4: KEY TAKEAWAYS & TRIGGERS SUMMARY

### 4A. Investment-ready trigger list (ranked by earnings impact)

1. **NPC Saudi acquisition ramp-up** — INORGANIC/VOLUME, near-medium (FY27-29), conviction H. Confirms: NPC quarterly revenue tracking toward the guided Rs.1,500-2,000 cr FY27 band and utilisation improving toward 80-85%. Kills: NPC utilisation stalls, Aramco order book fails to renew/convert, or key-management turnover disrupts execution.
2. **FY27 consolidated revenue guidance (Rs.5,000-5,500 cr)** — VOLUME (aggregate), near (FY27), conviction M given the guidance's repeated downward revision. Confirms: quarterly revenue tracking to ~Rs.1,250-1,375 cr/quarter without further cuts. Kills: a fourth downward revision, or order book falling below ~Rs.2,500 cr.
3. **Order book / bid-pipeline conversion** — VOLUME, near, conviction M. Confirms: order book inflects upward off the Rs.3,000 cr trough with the stated 20-30% bid-win ratio applied to the Rs.15,000-16,000 cr bid pipeline. Kills: order book continues to shrink despite a large bid pipeline.
4. **Jammu SS plant commissioning** — VOLUME/PRICE-MIX (17-22% margin band), medium (FY27-28), conviction M given repeated delay. Confirms: commissioning actually occurs and first shipments follow. Kills: further delay beyond FY27, or GST-subsidy scheme eligibility issues emerge.
5. **EBITDA margin sustain at 13-15%** — PRICE-MIX/COST, near-medium, conviction H (the one guidance line genuinely delivered). Confirms: margin holds ≥13% through FY27 on the enlarged consolidated base. Kills: margin compresses below ~11% on commodity or competitive pressure.
