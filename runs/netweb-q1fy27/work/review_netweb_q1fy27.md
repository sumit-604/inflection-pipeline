**Six mandatory diagnostics:**

2. **Did Op EBITDA margin expand/contract YoY?** Effectively flat: Q1FY27 14.70% vs Q1FY26 14.87% = **−17 bps**. Margin held near the top of the guided 13-14% band despite a large AI/non-govt mix shift.
3. **Did core operating PBT (ex-OI) grow YoY?** Yes, **+160.9%** (Rs 40.50 → 105.67 Cr). Reported PAT (+179.9%) and core operating PBT (+160.9%) both grew strongly, so headline growth is **real and operating-led**, not a treasury artefact. The gap between the two is explained below.
4. **What drove the gap between core operating PBT growth (+160.9%) and reported PAT growth (+179.9%)?** Reported PAT grew faster than core operating PBT because of (a) Other income +Rs 7.36 Cr (from a tiny base), and (b) the deferred-tax **credit** of Rs 3.59 Cr (F8) that held the ETR at 25.25% vs 26.74% a year ago (~150 bps lower). Finance cost (+Rs 10.54 Cr) worked the other way, dragging PAT growth down. Net: the tax shield and Other income lifted reported PAT slightly above the core-operating rate; neither is large enough to change the conclusion that the quarter is operating-driven.
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** D&A flat (+0.3%) against +172% revenue — no capex-absorption deficit; consistent with the "routine capex only, Faridabad SMT fully commissioned" guide. **Finance costs jumped 11.8x** (Rs 0.98 → 11.52 Cr), far faster than revenue — the early-warning marker of a debt-funded working-capital ramp (S2). One quarter's finance cost (11.52) is ~89% of the entire FY26 finance cost (12.95). No borrowings/WCDL note explains it in the filing.
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Other income is Rs 8.47 Cr, only 7.42% of PBT (vs 10.80% in Q4FY26, 2.67% in Q1FY26). It is not masking trajectory — stripping it leaves core operating PBT up +160.9%. No treasury distortion.
