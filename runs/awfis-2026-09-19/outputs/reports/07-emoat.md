### 2B. Utilisation trajectory

Occupancy has been flat, not improving, across the three concalls
scanned: mature (>12m vintage) occupancy sits at 83-84% and blended
occupancy at 75-76% for three straight quarters (B05 red_flags: "blended
occupancy flat at ~75-76% for three straight quarters despite repeated
improvement messaging"). A Q3 FY26 promise of a 100-150bps mature-cohort
improvement in "1-2 quarters" was only PARTIALLY met (blended rose ~100bps,
mature cohort held flat at 84%, B05). Q1 FY27 saw a further ~3,000-seat
single-client exit (~1,800 seats across 3 centres, Investor Pres. p.12)
despite a Q4 FY26 assurance that the FY26 5% churn rate "would not
repeat" (B05: MISSED). This is a stagnation/negative signal, not a
positive utilisation trend, and it directly informs the skepticism applied
to C1 and F2 below.

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic

Total capex under execution (FY27 guidance, midpoint): **Rs 205 Cr**
(Concall_Aug_2026_Transcript p.13; Concall_May_2026_Transcript p.15).

Historical fixed-asset turnover: **1.5x** ("Fixed assets turnover ratio,"
defined as revenue from co-working divided by average closing gross
block, Investor Presentation p.8, footnote).

Implied incremental revenue = Rs 205 Cr × 1.5x = **Rs 307.5 Cr**.

As % of FY26 revenue (Rs 1,493 Cr, Investor Pres. p.20 / AR): **20.6%**.
As % of FY27 revenue guide (~Rs 1,800 Cr, Concall_Aug_2026 / B05
guidance table): **17.1%**.

This is a mechanical multiplier read (capex × historical asset turn), not
a management-disclosed revenue bridge; it should be read alongside 2B's
flat-occupancy finding, since incremental seats only convert to revenue
at the occupancy ramp rate actually observed, not the turnover ratio
alone.
