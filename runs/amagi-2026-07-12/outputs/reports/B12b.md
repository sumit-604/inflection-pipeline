## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from transcripts alone)

Anchors are (call, speaker, PDF page in the _text extract).

1. **Q3 headline growth (22%) propped by accounting timing.** Q3 revenue +22% YoY vs H1/9M +30%; the gap is a top-5 customer's perpetual-license rev-rec front-loaded into H1 FY26 under IND AS output method (Feb call, Vijay, p.5-6 and p.11). Genuine flag: a chunk of the strong H1 optics was timing, not demand.
2. **Internal contradiction on the perpetual-license "overhang."** Same Feb call: Vijay attributes the H1 front-loading distortion specifically to "a perpetual license contract... grandfathered... rev-rec based on channel go-lives" (p.11), yet ~7 pages later Baskar tells Rohan Nagpal "there's no such overhang in the system fundamentally... largely there is no overhang because of any perpetual license or otherwise here" (p.18). A perpetual license both caused a multi-point half-year revenue swing and is claimed to create "no overhang," within one call, from the two co-managers.
3. **"Underlying" growth number drifted down within the Q3 call.** Vijay quotes "25% to 30%" to Kawaljeet (p.8), then "25% to 27%" to Manish (p.11), then "25%-26%" to Rishi (p.15). The single most-cited forward number is soft/negotiable across the same hour.
4. **Labour Code provision run-rate clarity deferred (Q3), then never revisited (Q4).** Feb call, Vijay to Rishi: "We'd be able to provide clarity on the run rate impact... in the subsequent quarters" (p.15). Absent from the May call entirely.
5. **Headcount-by-function KPI requested, "Feedback taken," not delivered.** Feb call, Vivekanand to Management: "Feedback taken Vivek. We will see what we can do" (p.9). No such KPI in the May call; Vivekanand re-probes and again gets only qualitative color (May, p.12-13).
6. **Top-5 renegotiation understated then revealed.** Framed in Q3 as pure strategy, "lock in long-term secure revenue instead of short-term optics on price" with no margin cost named (Feb, Vijay, p.11); in Q4 disclosed as the first of three drivers of sequential gross-margin compression (May, Vijay, p.10).
7. **Direct "we've been largely able to hold price" (Q3) contradicted by Q4 rate haircut.** Feb call, Vijay to Rohan: "We've been largely able to kind of hold price" (p.16). May call, Vijay concedes "customer pricing" drove GM compression and speaks of a "modest hair cut on rate" (p.10-11).
8. **"No competition that we see today," strengthening from Q3.** Q3: competitors are "traditional hardware vendors" + "cloud-first albeit small... Amagi is quite ahead" (Feb, Baskar, p.13-14). Q4: escalates to "there is no competition that we see today as we speak" (May, Baskar, p.12-13) in a fast-moving AI-tooling market, no named comparator.
9. **Customer-add deceleration and Q4 net-negative logos.** Count 396 (FY24) -> 463 (FY25) -> 492 (FY26): net adds fell from +67 to +29, more than halving. Q4 alone: 27 gross adds vs 30 churned = net minus-3 (May, Vijay, p.15-16). Deflected to "full year basis" and "sub-$1,000 housekeeping churn."
10. **NRR ticked down and reported NRR is FX-flattered.** NRR 122% (FY24) -> 127% (FY25) -> 126% (FY26) i.e. a down-tick (May, Vijay, p.7); constant-currency NRR only "north of 120%" (May, Vijay to Bharat, p.16), and FY26 growth was 30% reported vs 23% CC — a ~7pt FX tailwind. The "revenue quality strengthening / anchored 125%+" narrative (May, Baskar, p.3) leans on currency.
11. **Weak, lumpy cash conversion largely narrated via "adjusted" figures.** FY26 adjusted EBITDA ₹156cr but adjusted OCF only ₹60cr and FCF only ₹38cr (~24% of EBITDA) (May, Vijay, p.9). 9M FY26 reported OCF was NEGATIVE ₹76cr and reported FCF NEGATIVE ₹96cr; the positive story rests on "underlying" ₹47cr that adjusts out IPO expenses and ESOP-buyback cash (Feb, Vijay, p.5-6). Management repeatedly foregrounds cash generation; the reported conversion is thin and the adjustments strip real cash outflows (including a buyback to insiders).
12. **LTV/CAC and gross-margin payback declined despite direct request.** Feb, Kawaljeet asks directly; Vijay: "not a metric we officially disclose" (p.8).
13. **TCO "30%+ savings" sales claim self-admittedly unverified.** May, Baskar to Sanjay Ladha: "I'm sure they don't share the numbers with us" (p.17-18).
14. **Managed Services "biggest opportunity" dropped.** Feb, Vijay: with AI "we see some of the biggest sort of opportunity" in Managed Services (p.11); absent from the May call.
15. **Marketplace repeatedly deferred with no metric.** Feb: raised in passing; May, Baskar: "don't have anything meaningful today... we don't kind of separate that growth yet... come back over the next few quarters" (p.14).
16. **M&A guardrails / deal size repeatedly deflected.** Feb, Baskar: "don't have anything in the bag" (p.18); May, Baskar: "have we agreed on the size of the deals? Not really" (p.16-17). Two quarters, ~₹1,664cr undeployed.
17. **3-year revenue/margin guidance repeatedly and near-identically deflected.** Feb and May, Vijay, same "hard to give prescriptive guidance... aspiration to continue the trajectory" (p.11; p.11 May). Same primary analyst (Manish Adukia).
18. **Streaming, the largest segment (>half of revenue), is the slowest and CC-flattered.** Streaming +26% reported (~19% CC after the ~7pt FX tailwind) vs Monetization +36% and Cloud Mod +32% (May, p.3-4/p.7); segment growth is quoted in reported INR, inflating the "all three over 25%" line.
19. **Over-promotion tone.** "once in a lifetime," "no competition," "single most compelling growth opportunity," "grabbing more and more market share" (no share data), across both calls.
