# Addendum: "Acquisition" filing of 23 June 2026

document_id `1ec002b5-6159-4a7f-afb8-2835117be166`, short link
docs.bull-ai.in/d/GoBb8h. Pages 1 to 3 read.

This is NOT an external acquisition and NOT a change in control. It is a
capital infusion into Wendt's own wholly owned Thai subsidiary.

- "the Company has on 23rd June 2026 infused further capital into its wholly
  owned subsidiary in Thailand - M/s. Wendt Grinding Technologies Limited"
- WGTL was incorporated 19 July 2005. Registered capital THB 103,000,000,
  being 10,300,000 shares of THB 10 each, held partly paid at THB 2.50.
- Paid up before infusion THB 25,750,000. After infusion THB 72,100,000.
- Amount paid: THB 4.50 per share, THB 46,350,000, "approximately INR 13.29
  crores". A further THB 3.00 per share is still to come.
- "No change in the shareholding." Consideration in cash, at face value.
- Related party: "the transaction is between the Company and its wholly owned
  subsidiary. No promoter/ promoter group/ group companies have any interest."
- WGTL line of business: "Sales and Distribution of Coated, Bonded and Super
  Abrasives". A distributor, not a plant.
