  110	 Key Business Highlights:
  111	
  112	 •   Revenue from Operations:
  113	     Q1FY27 revenue from operations recorded YoY growth, reflecting an improvement in underlying business
  114	     activity. The quarter is seasonally softer compared to other quarters. During the quarter, the Clove business
  115	     faced challenges following floods in Madagascar, from where the Company sources raw materials, resulting in
  116	     higher raw material prices and impacting sales. The situation has now normalised, supporting a more stable
  117	     operating environment. The Company continues to make progress across its newer product verticals, with
  118	     revenue contribution expected to build progressively as customer approvals and qualifications are completed
  119	     and volumes ramp up.
  120	
  121	 •   Gross Margin & EBITDA Margin:
  122	     Gross margin and EBITDA margin were impacted during the quarter, primarily due to the product mix and higher
  123	     raw material costs. The newly commissioned facility also resulted in higher operating costs. Going forward, as
  124	     the contribution from higher-value products increases and capacity utilization improves, the Company expects
  125	     a stronger product mix and operating leverage to support a gradual improvement in margins.
  126	 •   Profit after Tax:
  127	     Profitability was impacted by higher raw material and operating costs and their consequent impact on the
  128	     bottom line, along with higher depreciation of Rs. 9.1 Cr, following the capitalization of a significant portion of
  129	     the ~Rs. 265 Cr capex incurred for the Dahej facility. As the newer product verticals progressively scale up and
  130	     capacity utilization improves, the Company expects operating leverage to support profitability going forward.
  131	
  132	 •   International Market Expansion - Brazil
  133	     The Company has approved the incorporation of a Wholly Owned Subsidiary in Brazil to distribute essential oils,
  134	     aromatic chemicals and specialty chemicals, strengthening its presence across Brazil and the broader Latin
  135	     American market.
  136	
  137	 •   Update on Krystal Ingredients Pvt. Ltd (Wholly Owned Subsidiary):
  138	     o   Cooling Agents: Customer audits have been successfully completed, and initial orders have been secured for
  139	         GEM Cool 03, GEM Cool 05 and GEM Cool 23, with order flow expected to increase progressively. The
  140	         business is expected to make a revenue contribution from Q3FY27 as customer qualifications are completed
  141	         and commercial supplies scale up.
  142	     o   Safranal: The business is progressing through customer approvals. Revenue contribution is expected to
  143	         commence towards the end of Q2FY27, with a more meaningful contribution from Q3FY27 as commercial
  144	         supplies scale up.
  145	     o   Phenol Derivatives: Trial production is expected to commence towards the end of Q2FY27, followed by
  146	         product approvals and quality-related processes. Commercial production is targeted during Q3FY27, subject
  147	         to completion of the required approvals and quality processes, with meaningful revenue contribution
  148	         expected from Q4FY27. The Company continues to monitor raw material availability and pricing while
  149	         aligning its production plans accordingly.

  199	 About Gem:
  200	 Established in 1997, GEM Aromatics Ltd is one of the leading manufacturers of specialty ingredients, essential oils,
  201	 aroma chemicals, and value-added derivatives. Led by a management team with nearly three decades of industry
  202	 experience, the Company has built a strong foundation rooted in innovation, quality, and customer trust.
  203	
  204	 With a diverse portfolio of over 80 products spanning Mint & Mint Derivatives, Clove & Clove Derivatives, and other
  205	 Synthetic and Natural ingredients, GEM Aromatics continues to expand into new categories such as Citral Derivatives,
  206	 Phenol Derivatives, Cooling Agents, Safranal, and Damascones. Its products cater to industries including oral care,
  207	 cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, wellness, and personal care, serving over 240 domestic and 44 global
  208	 customers across 20 countries. The Company partners with several leading brands, including Colgate-Palmolive,
  209	 Dabur, Patanjali, SH Kelkar, Symrise, and others.
  210	
  211	 GEM Aromatics operates three manufacturing facilities across Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, and Daman & Diu, with an
  212	 installed capacity of 16,171 MTPA. The Company holds multiple international certifications, including ISO 9001:2015,
  213	 ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, FSSC 22000, ISO 22000:2018, and ISO TS 22002-1:2009.
