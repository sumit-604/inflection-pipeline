## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

1A. Product scope: aluminium powders (atomised, pyro/flake, leafing), foil (rolled, converted, printed, lidding), bare aluminium conductors (AAC, AAAC, ACSR, AL59), composite polymer insulators. Excluded: aluminium paste (Toyal JV, 26%, outside revenue), LT and ABC cables (aluminium-core share NOT FOUND, so no TAM), copper cables, HT/EHV cables, circlips (Star Circlips, associate). Geography: India for TAM. Exports sized only as MMP's own FOB inflow, Rs 38.37 Cr in FY26 versus Rs 23.01 Cr in FY25 (AR26 Board report, Rs 3,836.87 L; derived +66.8%). Customers: explosives, AAC, pesticide, pyro and paint makers; pharma and food packagers; state utilities and EPC contractors. Channel: B2B, spot, annual and tender contracts (B04).

1B. Management claim. No product TAM is stated in the AR26 MD&A p.46-50 (qualitative only) or the Q1 FY27 deck. The deck p.24 shows India aluminium industry USD 11 bn (2023) to USD 20 bn (2030), CAGR 7.6%, and global USD 230 bn (2023), 244 bn (2024), 394 bn (2032E), source AR FY25. Credibility: BROAD. It sizes the whole industry, including smelting, not MMP's products. The 7.6% CAGR does not match its endpoints (20/11 over 7 years is 8.1%, derived). The 2023 data is STALE.
Claim in rupees: 11 bn x 96.18 = Rs 105,798 Cr.
Management market statements elsewhere: coal output 1 bn to 1.5 bn t by 2030, electricity demand 243 GW to 366 GW by 2031-32, PMAY-U 2.0 Rs 10 lakh Cr (deck p.25). Government projections, not MMP-specific sizes. Insulator peak revenue Rs 130-140 Cr at 3.5x asset turn (Call p.14-15).

### Powders

 - Conservative 48.66 kt x Rs 240/kg = Rs 1,168 Cr. High 48.66 x Rs 310 = Rs 1,508 Cr.
- Method 1/5 top-down check. Global 680.5 kt and USD 1.98 bn in 2025 (search summaries of several report firms; estimates range USD 1.84-2.22 bn). USD 1.98 bn / 680.5 kt = USD 2.91/kg x 96.18 = Rs 280/kg. This sits inside the listing range. Realistic: 48.66 kt x Rs 280 = Rs 1,362 Cr. India is 7.2% of global volume (48.66/680.5, derived).
- Cross-check from MMP: saleable capacity about 20.1-21.1 kt (pyro 16,800 + leafing 300 + atomised 12,000 less 8,000-9,000 captive; deck p.16) at about 80% utilisation (Call p.16) gives about 16-17 kt. - Method 3 peer aggregation: NOT FOUND (MEPCO, Sri Kaliswari, Deva named by IMARC; revenues not searched). No listed pure play exists (step 1 brief).
- Method 5 per-capita: skipped.
- Divergence: none material (Rs 1,168 to 1,508 Cr, ratio 1.29x). End-use split 2025: pyrotechnics 55%, paints and coatings 18%, other incl. AAC and explosives 27% (IMARC). Odd versus MMP's own framing of explosives, AAC and pesticides as core. Separator: grade-level use data from PESO and AAC producers. Confidence M for volume, L for price.

### Foil
- Method 2. India aluminium foil packaging market USD 1,430.4 mn in 2025 (MarketsandMarkets) x 96.18 = Rs 13,758 Cr. Wider than MMP: it includes converted and flexible packaging value.
- Volume: three readings. 337 kt (400 kt by 2028 at 5.9% CAGR, base year assumed 2025, derived); about 510 kt (China imports 140,234 t in Jan-Nov 2024 at "nearly 30%" share, derived from alcircle/search summary); 915 kt (as printed, not credible against domestic capacity of 250-300 kt). Carried 337 kt. Implied Rs 13,758 Cr / 337 kt = Rs 408/kg (derived). Separator: DGCI&S HS 7607 import data against domestic output. Confidence L.
- Pharma blister and strip demand about 1,500 t per month = 18 kt (search summary, undated, STALE risk). 18 kt x Rs 408/kg = Rs 735 Cr, derived at the market-average price. Pharma foil likely sells above average, so this reads low.
- Method 4 import substitution. Anti-dumping duty USD 479-721 per t (definitive) on Chinese foil up to 80 microns, five years from 17 Mar 2025 (provisional USD 619-873; deck p.25). China held about 30% of the market. Imports annualised about 153 kt (140,234/11 x 12, derived). If duty halves this, 75 kt shifts to domestic mills (derived, scenario, not a forecast). Domestic capacity 250-300 kt. Hindalco 28,000 t (search summary). Organised pharma-foil share about 40%; Ess Dee, Indal, Bilcare lead (search summary, undated, L).
- Foil TAM: conservative Rs 735 Cr (pharma core), realistic Rs 13,758 Cr (all packaging foil). Ratio 18.7x. Reason: scope. MMP makes pharma, food and flexible foil (deck p.19), but the pharma versus other revenue split is NOT FOUND.

Reading B: if all packaging foil is SAM, MMP share is 1.6% of Rs 13,758 Cr. Carried reading A because the filing evidence (AR26 p.51, deck p.19) gives pharma the most weight, and the split is NOT FOUND. Separator: foil revenue by end use.
