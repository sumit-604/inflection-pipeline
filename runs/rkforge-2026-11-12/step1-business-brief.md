## The one engine the thesis would rest on

Utilisation recovery on a finished capex cycle. Capacity grew through FY24 to FY26 while exports fell and the inventory error hit. Revenue is now growing 55% YoY at 72% utilisation, and margin has moved from 13.5% to 18.2%. The thesis is that fixed costs and Rs 2,300 Cr of debt are absorbed as exports and new lines fill the plants, lifting ROCE from 5.6% towards the 20% management states for FY28. The price already reflects part of this: P/E is 74.8 on depressed earnings.

## PEERS (three, picked on the economic engine)

- Bharat Forge (BHARATFORG), https://www.screener.in/company/BHARATFORG/consolidated/ (OK 200). The same engine at larger scale: truck forgings, a large North America and Europe export book, and a move into non-auto lines. Its calls test the US Class 8 and tariff claims.
- M M Forgings (MMFL), https://www.screener.in/company/MMFL/consolidated/ (OK 200). A commercial-vehicle forging exporter that also carried a debt-funded capacity build into the export downturn. The closest read on utilisation, margin and leverage.
- Happy Forgings (HAPPYFORGE), https://www.screener.in/company/HAPPYFORGE/consolidated/ (OK 200). Forged and machined crankshafts and axle parts for trucks and farm equipment. It tests the machining-led margin claim and the domestic truck cycle.

No listed pure play matches the rail wheel or casting lines. CIE Automotive India and Sona BLW were set aside: their mix leans to passenger vehicles and non-forging parts.
