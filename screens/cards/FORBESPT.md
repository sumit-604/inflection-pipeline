## Business Understanding Narrative

Forbes Precision Tools makes the things that cut metal. Taps, dies, drills and related
precision cutting tools, sold under the Totem brand, made at a single plant at MIDC Waluj
in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (search extracts, Financial Results FY2026 Q4 p.9). It is one
business and the company says so in every filing: "The operating segment of the Company is
identified to be, 'Precision cutting tools and related components'" (search extracts, and
repeated verbatim in four filings). There is no second segment, no subsidiary and no joint
venture (search extracts, AGM p.22).

It exists as a listed company because of a demerger. The precision tool business of Forbes
& Company Limited was "transferred to and vested in the company from the appointed date of
April 1, 2023" (search extracts, AGM p.49). Shapoorji Pallonji and Company Private Limited
is the promoter and held 72.56% with no encumbrance at the date of the SAST disclosure held
in the corpus (search extracts, Reg. 29(2) disclosure p.2). The company held its third AGM
in 2025.

Money comes from selling consumable tooling to engineering workshops and manufacturers.
That is a repeat-purchase business with a brand that has been in the Indian market for
decades. In FY2025 it earned Rs 232.7 crore of revenue and Rs 40.9 crore of profit before
tax, a 17.6% margin, with a derived EBITDA of about Rs 51 crore or 22.0% (search extracts,
profit and loss table). Those are good margins for a component maker.

What is changing: on the evidence held, almost nothing. Revenue grew 5.8% in the first half
of FY2026 and derived profit before tax fell 2.2%. There is no capex plan, no capacity
figure, no order position, no export share and no product programme anywhere in the corpus.
The only named changes are a Senior Vice President hired in August 2025 and an HR
replacement after "the recent exit of the HR" (search extracts, Change in Management p.2).

What must be true for a thesis to exist here: something the corpus does not show.

What breaks it: nothing dramatic. The risk on this name is not blow-up, it is drift.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Archetype: build-to-spec component maker, at the consumable end. Revenue is units of
tooling times price. Taps and dies wear out and get replaced, so the demand is repeat and
tracks industrial activity rather than a capex cycle.
The cost structure, from FY2025: materials net of inventory movement were Rs 7,982 lakh or
34.3% of revenue; employee costs Rs 4,403 lakh or 18.9%; other expenses Rs 5,768 lakh or
24.8%; depreciation Rs 1,350 lakh; finance costs Rs 113 lakh (search extracts, profit and
loss table). So gross margin is roughly 66%, and the business is labour and overhead heavy
rather than material heavy. That is the signature of a precision manufacturer with skilled
operators, and it explains why attrition matters here.
The one economic engine is brand plus tool room capability. Totem is an established Indian
cutting tool name, and the barrier is metallurgy, grinding accuracy and a distribution
reach into workshops. None of that is quantified in the corpus.

## 3. Competitive advantages
Probable but undocumented, which is the honest position.
A 22% EBITDA margin and a 66% gross margin on a consumable tool say the company is not
price-taking (derived from the FY2025 profit and loss). A commodity toolmaker competing
with Chinese imports would not hold those. So there is pricing power, and the most likely
source is the Totem brand and a workshop distribution network built over decades.
The Shapoorji Pallonji parentage gives balance sheet credibility and, in principle,
customer access.
Against that, everything a shallow read would want is absent. No market share, no export
share, no customer list, no capacity, no comparison with Addison, Miranda or the imported
competition. And there is one documented operational weakness: attrition at 17.24%, "higher
than normal, some of it at critical positions, due to which, there were some delays in some
the planned operations of the company" (search extracts, AGM p.22). In a business whose
advantage is skilled grinding, losing critical people is the advantage leaking.
Moat: inferred from margin, not evidenced in the corpus.

## 4. The promoters
Shapoorji Pallonji and Company Private Limited, holding 3,74,37,172 shares, 72.56% of
voting capital, with nil encumbrance at the date of the disclosure held (search extracts,
Reg. 29(2) disclosure p.2).
That disclosure is itself the flag. A Regulation 29(2) filing is made when a promoter's
holding changes materially, and Bull AI's availability map lists five such disclosures plus
one Reg. 29(1) filing across FY2025 and FY2026. The rows giving the direction and size of
the transaction were cut off in the retrieved snippet. So the corpus establishes that the
promoter has been transacting in the company's shares repeatedly, and does not establish
whether it was buying or selling. That must be settled before anything else on this name.
Board and governance signals from the corpus are clean: an unmodified audit opinion for
FY2026 from Sharp & Tannan Associates, no qualification in the FY2025 auditors' or
secretarial auditors' reports, cost auditors reappointed, a labour settlement running to
the end of 2028, and no subsidiaries or joint ventures to obscure the picture (search
extracts, Outcome of Board Meeting p.9; AGM p.3, p.22).
Derived, and marked as derived: 3,74,37,172 shares at 72.56% implies about 5.16 crore shares
outstanding. At the Rs 5 interim dividend on a Rs 10 face value, the FY2026 cash outflow is
about Rs 25.8 crore, against FY2025 profit before tax of Rs 40.9 crore. That is a large
distribution for a company that has published no growth plan.

## 5. Financial trajectory
FY2025, audited, year ended 31 March 2025 (Rs lakh): revenue from operations 23,266; other
income 438; total income 23,704; total expenses 19,616; profit before tax 4,088 (search
extracts, profit and loss table). Profit before tax margin 17.6% of revenue. Derived EBITDA
Rs 5,113 lakh, 22.0% of revenue, from revenue less operating expenses before depreciation
and finance costs.
Quarterly: Q1FY25 revenue 5,177, profit before tax 651. Q4FY25 revenue 6,594, profit before
tax 1,438. Q1FY26 revenue 5,241, profit before tax 508.
So Q1FY26 revenue rose 1.2% year on year while profit before tax fell 22.0%.
Half year: H1FY26 revenue 11,568 against H1FY25 10,931, up 5.8%. Derived profit before tax
1,612 against 1,648, down 2.2%. Q2FY26 derived profit before tax 1,104 against 997 a year
earlier, up 10.7%, so the second quarter recovered part of the first quarter's shortfall.
What moved: materials net of inventory movement were 32.2% of revenue in H1FY26 against
32.7% a year earlier, so input costs were not the problem. Employee costs fell. The
compression came from other expenses, 28.8% of revenue against 27.0%, plus depreciation up
Rs 110 lakh and finance costs up Rs 44 lakh (search extracts, profit and loss table).
Rising depreciation with no disclosed capex is worth a question.
FY2026 full-year figures are NOT HELD. The board approved them on 7 May 2026 with an
unmodified opinion, and that is all the corpus records.
Inflection classification: **none.** There is no inflection in the held data. Revenue is
growing at mid single digits and profit is flat to down.

## 8. Proof check
There is little to prove and little proved.
What fired: the business is profitable, audited clean, debt-light on the evidence of Rs 113
lakh of annual finance costs, and pays a dividend. Q2FY26 profit before tax recovered 10.7%
year on year after a weak Q1. The labour settlement to 2028 is a genuine risk removal.
What has not fired: growth. Revenue up 5.8% in the half with profit before tax down 2.2%.
Depreciation and finance costs both rising without a disclosed capex programme to explain
them. And the operational note that attrition "at critical positions" caused "delays in
some the planned operations of the company" is the company itself reporting an execution
shortfall (search extracts, AGM p.22).
Named proof point: the FY2026 full-year numbers, which exist and are audited and which this
corpus does not hold. Until those are read, no view on this name is properly grounded. That
is the first thing to fetch, before any judgement is treated as settled.

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS for this mandate, with the FY2026 numbers named as the one thing that could change
it.** Forbes Precision Tools is a sound, clean, profitable niche manufacturer: Rs 233 crore
of revenue, 22% EBITDA margin, 66% gross margin, an unmodified audit, negligible debt, a
respected brand and a labour settlement running to 2028. Nothing here is wrong.
It is not a transition. The company has published no destination, no capacity plan, no
capex, no order position and no outlook. Its own board report discusses the Indian economy
rather than the business. Revenue grew 5.8% in the last half reported and profit before tax
fell. Under a mandate that hunts a documented rung climb the market has not yet repriced,
there is no climb on the table to assess.
Load-bearing facts to fetch before this is dismissed permanently:
1. The FY2026 audited profit and loss, approved 7 May 2026 and not held here. Two published
   quarters are missing from this card and they could change its shape.
2. The direction and size of the Shapoorji Pallonji SAST transactions. Five Reg. 29(2)
   filings in two years with the direction unknown is the single most important open item.
   A promoter buying and a promoter selling are opposite signals.
3. The FY2025 annual report: balance sheet, cash flow, capacity, exports, customer
   concentration, and related-party dealings with the Shapoorji Pallonji group.
4. Why depreciation and finance costs are rising with no disclosed capex.
5. The credit rating rationale, for capacity and working capital.
6. The price. A BSE-only, uncovered, recently demerged name is where a neglect discount
   actually lives, and this card cannot see it.
What would change the view: a disclosed capacity expansion or export programme, a promoter
buying on the SAST filings, or FY2026 revenue growth in the mid teens with margin held,
would each move this to WATCH and two of them together to PROCEED. Absent those, it belongs
in a neglect or value screen rather than the transition pipeline. No price here.
