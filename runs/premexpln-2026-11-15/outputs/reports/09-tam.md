# Stage 9 TAM / SAM / SOM: Premier Explosives Ltd (PREMEXPLN)
Run date 2026-11-15. Model claude-sonnet-5-5. All figures in Rs Cr unless stated. Source units: AR MD&A prints Rs million; AR statements print INR lakhs; deck prints Rs Mn. Conversion is Rs mn / 10 = Rs Cr. FX used: Rs 93.9 per USD (market close 6-9 Nov 2026, PTI via hdfcsky/newkerala/telanganatoday search results; FBIL reference rate NOT FOUND). A second case at Rs 89.45 per USD (late-2025 close, Financial Express via Magzter) is shown where a USD vendor figure has a 2025 base year.

## Headline
- The market is not the constraint. A qualified-private defence-energetics pool of Rs 2,949 Cr (FY26) sits against Premier defence revenue of Rs 315 Cr (10.7%).
- Premier LOST share inside that pool. Premier defence was Rs 337.4 Cr in FY25 and Rs 315.4 Cr in FY26. Solar defence grew 94% to Rs 2,634 Cr. Derived pool share fell from about 19.9% to 10.7%.
- Central SOM: Rs 773 Cr in FY29 (25.8% 3-yr CAGR from Rs 388.34 Cr), Rs 1,089 Cr in FY31 (22.9% 5-yr CAGR). Alternative reading: 17.0% and 17.3%.
- Committed capacity (capex-embedded) reaches about Rs 518 Cr. The SOM exceeds it by about Rs 254 Cr. The capex plan is the optimistic side, or asset turns must rise well above the FY26 2.17x.
- Management gives no numeric TAM. The TAM figures here are vendor containers, with energetics content NOT FOUND.

## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A Boundaries
- Product scope IN: solid propellants, pyrogen igniters, rocket motors (40 to 2000 mm), pyro devices, chaff and flare countermeasures, HMX/RDX/PETN/CL-20 explosives, warheads/mines/ammunition (planned), solid-propellant plant O&M (ISRO SHAR), and commercial bulk explosives, cast boosters and detonating fuse (Inv. Pres. p.14, p.13; AR p.25 s.5.1-5.3).
- Product scope OUT: electric detonators (production stopped in FY26, AR p.24 s.3.4), missile guidance/seekers/airframes, small-arms ammunition (the vendor ammunition figure includes them; share NOT FOUND), packaged civil fireworks.
- Geography: India defence and space customers plus export. Export was 13.3% of FY26 revenue (B04, AR p.133). Commercial leg is India coal/mining/cement/infra, with plants at Singrauli, Chandrapur, Godavarikhani, Manuguru (Inv. Pres. p.15).
- Customer scope: MoD, DPSUs (BDL, ASL), DRDO, ISRO, IAF for defence; Coal India-type miners, cement, infra for commercial. Defence is 81% of FY26 revenue and 96% of the Oct 2026 order book (AR p.25; Inv. Pres. p.8).
- Channel: government tender and nomination, milestone-based. Commercial is tender and repeat.
- Price segment: spec-in, qualified supply (defence). Commercial bulk is L1 price-led (Premier lost Coal India bulk on price, B05; Solar says CIL and Singareni buy L1, B06).
- Revenue base (confirmed from AR): FY26 revenue from operations Rs 3,883.4 mn = Rs 388.34 Cr (AR p.25 s.7). Split: Defence & Space Rs 3,154 mn = Rs 315.4 Cr (81%); Commercial Rs 729 mn = Rs 72.9 Cr (19%) (AR p.25 s.5.1-5.2). O&M services sit inside Defence & Space (B04).

### 3B SOM
Current share: 315.4 / 2,949.4 = 10.69% of defence SAM. FY25 share (derived): Solar FY25 defence = 2,634 / 1.94 = Rs 1,357.7 Cr; Premier FY25 D&S Rs 3,374 mn = Rs 337.4 Cr (AR p.25). Share = 337.4 / (1,357.7 + 337.4) = 19.9%. Share fell about 9 pp in one year.

Two readings of how fast the SAM grows:
- Reading A (budget-linked): SAM grows at the 15.2% FY27 defence budget growth (Inv. Pres. p.24). FY27 SAM = 3,397.
- Reading B (peer-guided): FY27 SAM = Solar guide 4,500 + Premier FY27 defence. Premier FY27 defence = H1 Rs 203.8 Cr (Inv. Pres. p.7: Rs 2,038 mn) + H2 at the Q2 rate (Rs 124.6 Cr x 2 = 249.2) = Rs 453.0 Cr. This is a run-rate extrapolation, not a forecast. FY27 SAM = 4,953.0. Premier share = 453.0 / 4,953.0 = 9.15%.
- Separating observation: Solar's delivered FY27 defence revenue against Rs 4,500 Cr (Q4 FY27 results, May 2027), and Premier H2 FY27 revenue at or above Rs 170 Cr a quarter (B05 trigger 1).
- Carried: Reading B. Reading A fails a filed check, because Solar's guide alone (4,500) already exceeds A's FY27 SAM of 3,397. Solar delivered +94% in FY26 and beat its guide history (B06); the Q2 FY27 defence growth was +69% YoY (B06). Reading B uses guidance, not delivery, for Solar. That is stated as the weak link.

Share rule applied: normal +1 to 2 pp in 3 years, so +1.5 pp at 3 years; +2.5 pp at 5 years. The aggressive 3-5 pp band is not used: capacity is short (3C), execution grade D (B05), and the share fell 9 pp last year.

Reading B arithmetic (carried):
- Growth after FY27 at 15.2% a year (budget rate; a floor for post-guide years).
- FY29 SAM = 4,953.0 x 1.152^2 = 4,953.0 x 1.3271 = 6,573.
- FY29 share = 9.15% + 1.5 pp = 10.65%. FY29 defence SOM = 6,573 x 0.1065 = 700.0.
- FY29 total SOM = 700.0 + 72.9 (commercial flat) = Rs 772.9 Cr.
- 3-yr CAGR from FY26: (772.9 / 388.34)^(1/3) - 1 = 1.9902^(0.3333) - 1 = 25.8%.
- FY31 SAM = 6,573 x 1.3271 = 8,723. Share = 9.15 + 2.5 = 11.65%. Defence SOM = 1,016.2. Total = 1,089.1.
- 5-yr CAGR: (1,089.1 / 388.34)^(1/5) - 1 = 2.8045^(0.2) - 1 = 22.9%.

Reading A arithmetic (shown for comparison):
- FY29 SAM = 2,949.4 x 1.152^3 = 4,509; share 10.69 + 1.5 = 12.19%; defence SOM 549.7; total 622.6; 3-yr CAGR 17.0%.
- FY31 SAM = 5,984; share 13.19%; defence 789.3; total 862.2; 5-yr CAGR 17.3%.

Cross-check against the order book: defence book Rs 1,642 Cr x 96% = Rs 1,576 Cr (Inv. Pres. p.8, derived from rounded percentage). Remaining after H2 FY27 (249.2) = 1,327. Reading B needs FY28 plus FY29 defence of about 1,275 on a straight line between FY27 453 and FY29 700 (575 + 700). The book covers it, with no allowance for conversion slippage. Premier converted 24.7% of the May 2026 book in FY26 (B04). The Rs 200-300 Cr FY27 order-inflow guide (B05) is the buffer.

### 3C Capacity cross-check
- Capex-embedded growth 33.5%: Rs 60 Cr x 2.17x FY26 net-block turnover = Rs 130.2 Cr, on revenue Rs 388.34 Cr (B07). Installed plus committed capacity revenue = 388.34 + 130.2 = Rs 518.5 Cr. On the 3-yr average turnover of 1.93x: 388.34 + 115.8 = Rs 504.1 Cr (B07: 29.8%).
- SOM FY29 Rs 772.9 Cr less 518.5 = gap of Rs 254.4 Cr (Rs 268.8 Cr on 1.93x).
- Even the FY27 guide of Rs 600 Cr exceeds committed capacity by Rs 81.5 Cr. The run-rate FY27 (453.0 + 72.9 = 525.9) sits near it.
- Which side is optimistic: the capex plan is too small for the SOM, unless turnover rises. Management's own Katepally RDX/HMX target of Rs 150-200 Cr on the Rs 60 Cr programme implies 2.5x to 3.3x on that capex, against 2.17x for the base (B05; B07). Capex beyond Rs 60 Cr is NOT FOUND; the Andhra Pradesh 400-acre site is a long-dated trigger (B05 priority 9). Apollo funding is not in any document. Status of the Rs 28 Cr FY26 plus Rs 32 Cr balance: CWIP Rs 333.5 mn at Sep-26 (Inv. Pres. p.9; B05).
- Statement: SOM beyond about Rs 520 Cr needs more capacity than is committed. The SOM is not optimistic on the market; the capex plan is the optimistic side only if one believes committed capacity delivers the SOM. Stage 11 should hold revenue at or below the capacity line until new capex is evidenced, or size the position for it.

### 5B Runway
- Revenue headroom = SAM / current defence revenue = 2,949.4 / 315.4 = 9.4x (7.6x on total revenue 388.34).
- TAM growth: weighted vendor CAGRs 10.8% (ammunition, 2026-31) and 7.39% (industrial explosives): (24,935 x 10.8% + 12,702 x 7.39%) / 37,637 = 9.7%. Budget growth is 15.2%.
- Company CAGR vs TAM: SOM CAGR 25.8% vs TAM 9.7%, so the case needs share gain against the TAM, yet share of the SAM falls from 10.7% to 9.1% first (FY26 to FY27) because Solar's pool grows faster. Premier is riding the market, not gaining on its leader.
- Years to saturate SAM: never inside the horizon. At 25.8% the SOM stays below 12% of the SAM through FY31.
