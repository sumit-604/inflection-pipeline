### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Dynamic Cables makes power and control cables in Jaipur. Most of its metal is aluminium. Buyers are power utilities, EPC contractors, factories and export clients.
2. Q2 sales grew 29% on a year ago, to Rs 363 Cr. Half-year sales grew 31%, to Rs 712 Cr.
3. Profit grew more slowly. Q2 profit after tax rose 13% on a year ago, to Rs 22 Cr. It fell 11% from Q1.
4. The operating margin fell to 10.0%. That is the lowest of the six periods in the press release.
5. Management gave three different reasons for the lower margin on the call. The three reasons do not agree. So the cause is still open.
6. The company does not file volume. An analyst said Q2 volume grew 6%. Management did not dispute it. If that is right, higher metal prices and product mix gave most of the sales growth.
7. Cash is the main worry. The business made only Rs 7 Cr of operating cash in the half year, against Rs 47 Cr of profit.
8. Stock in the warehouse rose Rs 71 Cr in six months. Last year it rose only Rs 5 Cr in the same half. So the usual seasonal reason does not explain it.
9. To pay for this stock and the new plant, the company sold most of its mutual funds. Net cash of Rs 18 Cr became net debt of Rs 4 Cr. Interest cost rose 49% from Q1.
10. On the call, management called its working capital "prudent". The filed numbers do not support that word this half.
11. The new plant has its machines in place. Management says it adds sales from the second half. It gave no capacity number. The order book is about Rs 829 Cr.
12. BSE fined the company Rs 2,360 for a shareholding filing one day late. The money is trivial. The documents do not show last year's filing record, so a repeat pattern is neither shown nor ruled out.
13. Watch Q3 sales, due by 14-Feb-2027. At Rs 392.5 Cr or more, the stock build was for orders and the plant is adding. At Rs 363 Cr or less, demand is slower than the build.
