## OCR notes and defects
1. **AR26 text layer is corrupt from p.13 to p.260 and is not tagged [OCR].** The `.txt` holds control characters only (a font encoding fault), not garbled OCR. The brief said FY25 was the partly-OCR report; in fact FY25's text is clean except cover pages p.17, p.18 and p.270 ([OCR]). AR26 pages 13-260 were rendered at 200 dpi and re-OCR'd with Tesseract into the session scratchpad; the repo file was not changed. Every AR26 number in the card comes from that OCR, and the tables used (p.45, p.141) were checked visually against the PDF. Recommend re-running `tools/ocr_repair.py` on this file in the collector (it needs `pypdf`, which is not installed on this machine).
2. **credit-ratings/manifest.json lists the six files as `.html`** with near-identical byte counts (172,664 to 172,675). The held files are PDFs with distinct content and MD5s. The log is stale or wrong about format.
