## SECTION 2: SIGNED MENTAL MODEL (operator-signed 15-Nov-2026)

### 2.1 Plain-language model (as signed)

Anawil makes the steel tubes that hold wind turbines up. A tower is about 140 metres tall and weighs 300 to 475 tonnes. It is rolled, welded and painted from thick steel plate in sections. The sections are huge and costly to move, so a plant near the wind farms has an edge.

It sells in three ways. In conversion the customer sends the steel and Anawil charges about Rs 55 to 60 lakh a tower. In conversion plus internals it adds its own ladders, platforms and cable trays for about Rs 1 Cr a tower. In integrated supply it buys the steel and sells a finished tower for Rs 3 to 3.5 Cr, about Rs 5.5 Cr for a 5 MW tower.

The buyers are turbine makers, mainly Envision and Sany, and the developers who order through them, such as JSW and Evren. The main plant is Koppal in Karnataka. The Kutch plant in Gujarat still waits for customer approvals.

Analogy: a tailor near two big clothing brands. Some brands bring their own cloth and pay for stitching. Others ask the tailor to buy the cloth too. The tailor is close, so delivery is cheap. But each brand has in-house tailors and a list of other approved tailors. The tailor gets work when a brand has more suits than its usual tailors can make.

### 2.3 The four dominant variables

1. Envision's orders to Anawil, above all 5 MW (EN-182) repeat orders. Anawil holds one EN-182 order: 10 towers, 474 t each, Rs 58.32 Cr incl. GST, due Q1 FY28 [FILED, NSE intimation 29-Oct-2026]. The large Karnataka 5 MW demand (CleanMax, UPC, Serentica) is open to Anawil only through repeat EN-182 work.
2. JSW's pace of wind commissioning on Sany turbines. This drives the conversion volume (Customer B in the H1 deck, about 112 towers).
3. The mix between integrated and conversion towers, and EBITDA per tower. Integrated earns more rupees per tower on far more revenue and working capital.
4. Kutch approval and fill. A large share of nameplate earns almost nothing today (Gujarat about Rs 3.3 Cr of H1 FY27 revenue [FILED, H1 deck]).

### 2.4 Transition in progress

- Bigger towers: 3.3 MW to 5 MW turbines raise tonnes and value per tower.
- More integrated supply: about two-thirds of H1 FY27 revenue.
- One plant to two: Kutch approved for nothing yet.
- Lighter balance sheet: IPO proceeds repaid Rs 115 Cr of debt [FILED, CARE monitoring report, quarter to 30-Sep-2026].

Against the transition: the turbine makers gaining share make their own towers (Suzlon, Inox, Adani). Two Karnataka round-the-clock projects near Koppal, Bellary (Purvah 300 MW) and Davanagere (Jindal India Power 155 MW), went to Adani 5 MW turbines [SECONDARY, Adani Wind order release reported 27 to 29-Oct-2026].

### 2.5 Competitive position (no moat)

Partial edges: location near Karnataka wind sites; approvals on Envision EN-156, Sany SI-16840, Suzlon S144 and Senvion 2.3M130 [AGENCY, MNRE per-model component annexures]; execution (H1 FY27 EBITDA per tower matched the management bands, Section 3 V5); a clean post-IPO balance sheet.

Thin edges: EN-156 lists 13 approved tower makers and Sany 6 [AGENCY, MNRE annexures]; Cu-Built is Envision's anchor tower vendor [SECONDARY, Cu-Built own claims]; new capacity from DEE Fabricom (32,400 MTPA, Anjar) [COUNTERPARTY, DEE filings], KP Green and Inox's South India tower plant [COUNTERPARTY, peer concalls]; conversion fee flat at about Rs 58.7 lakh.

### 2.6 What the model rejects as noise

National wind targets; the unsigned power-sale-agreement backlog (mostly solar); a weak first half alone (the sector books 60 to 80% of sales in H2); customer letters re-sorted between documents; single-month steel moves.

### 2.7 Model falsifier

The model says Anawil is an overflow supplier, not a preferred one. It is wrong if: a second Envision EN-182 order arrives before 30-Jun-2027; new integrated orders price above the H1 FY27 rate per tonne; Kutch is approved and filling by mid 2027. It is confirmed and weakened if: no repeat EN-182 order; JSW below 300 MW of wind commissioned by Q3 FY27; no new integrated order by 31-Mar-2027.

### 2.8 Proof gate (09b Part B3) carried, with a warning

The 09b gate needs H2 FY27 towers of 206 or more and EBITDA per tower of Rs 33 lakh or more with Model 3 above 50% of revenue. Claude web's H2 scenarios give 140 to 200 towers (Section 3 V5) [INFERENCE]. Expect the tower-count half to fail on the current book while the EBITDA-per-tower half passes. Gate kept as written; the operator may re-rule it at the /fttcp gate.
