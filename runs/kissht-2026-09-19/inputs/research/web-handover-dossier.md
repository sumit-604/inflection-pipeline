## 1. MENTAL MODEL (for signature)

### 1A. Archetype
LENDER, two halves.
- Own book (Si Creva, ~46% of AUM at Jun-26): ordinary unsecured lender. Holds all credit risk. Standalone RoNW 14.18% FY26 [AGENCY: Acuité 25-Sep-2026; FILED: AR AOC-1, Si Creva PAT Rs 148.24 Cr].
- Partner book (~54%): capital-light originator-servicer that KEEPS THE FIRST LOSS. Partners fund at an agreed return; Kissht keeps the residual spread as fee income and covers the first 5% of losses per pool. ~10% retention on co-lent loans. Not a platform: FY26 partner-loan loss charge Rs 154.7 Cr [FILED: AR Note 29] against ~Rs 490 Cr of parent external fee/other revenue [INFERENCE: standalone revenue Rs 698.2 Cr less related-party income from Si Creva]. Roughly a third of that income consumed by defaults.
- Rejected archetype: Platform / SaaS (collector guess, phase 1 rejected; reaffirmed).

### 1B. Analogy (one paragraph)
Kissht lends about Rs 25,000 for one to two years, through an app, to young mass-market Indians earning Rs 25-75k a month, half salaried and half small-business. It charges a lot (borrower APR around 45% on the MAS-co-lent product [COUNTERPARTY: MAS KFS]) and loses a lot (10-12% of the managed book a year, all-in). The price pays for the losses and leaves about 5-6% pre-tax on managed assets. Half the profit comes from loans it holds; half from loans it finds and services for big lenders, on which it pays the first 5% of losses.

### 1C. The transition
FROM (FY23-24): ultra-short checkout/pay-later credit. Average tenor 2.92 months FY24 [FILED: RHP p.38]; revenue 87.82% and credit cost 32.08% of average AUM FY24 [FILED: RHP p.174]; penal/foreclosure fees Rs 283 Cr FY24 [FILED: RHP p.351]. Partner book was synthetic: business-correspondent guarantee Rs 617.8 Cr at FY23 [FILED: RHP p.311] against Partner 4's Rs 715.5 Cr book (~86% cover) [INFERENCE: link not stated in RHP].

Change 1, PROVEN (FY24-25): RBI DLG cap (Jun-2023) cut guarantee to Rs 22.3 Cr at FY24; Partner 4 ran off FY25; tenor to 9.65 months; disbursements halved; PAT dipped 18% then recovered [FILED].

### 1D. Dominant variables (3-4)
1. All-in loss rate on the managed book (definition above). THE swing variable.
2. Partner appetite and concentration (two lenders hold 95% of parent DLG pools).
3. Revenue margin minus all-in loss (the pricing spread as yields are cut).
4. Funding cost (minor; confirmatory).

### 1E. What the model rejects as noise
Market size (TAM ample). Headline GNPA/NNPA/PCR (on-book only by definition, write-off-driven). Headline "credit cost" (on-book impairment ÷ total AUM; denominator-flattered). The 66-74% AUC chart (fraud model). AUM growth per se.

### 1F. Falsifiers
- Transition: all-in loss above 11.6% of avg AUM, or off-book loss rate rising two consecutive half-years, or revenue margin minus all-in loss narrowing two consecutive quarters.
- Business: a top-two partner exits or shrinks its pools by more than a third with no replacement; or RBI cuts the 5% DLG cap / changes DLG recognition again.
- Trust: RBI inspection shown to cover FY25 or later.
