                                  Route Mobile Limited announces Q2FY27 Results

    Q2FY27 Revenue from operations stands at Rs. 1,179.67 crore, PAT stands at Rs. 82.55 crore

Mumbai, October 27, 2026: Route Mobile Limited ("Route Mobile"), a Proximus Global company and a
leading cloud communication platform service provider to enterprises, over-the-top ("OTT") players, and
mobile network operators has announced its inancial results for the second quarter ended on September
30, 2026.

   Highlights for Q2FY27 (Y-o-Y) Consolidated Financials

        · Revenue from operations stood at Rs. 1,179.67 crore as against Rs. 1,119.42 crore in Q2FY26
        · Proit Before Tax and Exceptional items stands at Rs 100.93 crore as against Rs. 137.87 crore in Q2FY26
        · Proit Before Tax stand at Rs 100.93 crore as against Rs 2.00 crore in Q2FY26
        · Proit After Tax, reported was at Rs 82.55 crore as against Rs. (18.83) crore in Q2FY26
        · EPS stands at Rs. 12.63 (basic) and Rs. 12.63 (diluted) for Q2FY2027

Q2FY27 Vs Q1 FY27 (Consolidated)

Revenue from operations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, stood at Rs. 1,179.67 crore as
compared to Rs. 1,151.51 crore in Q1FY27.

Proit Before Tax (PBT) stood at Rs. 100.93 crore for Q2FY27 as compared to Rs. 91.47 crore in Q1FY27.
The company's PBT margin stood at 8.56 %.

Proit After Tax (PAT) reported at Rs. 82.55 crore for Q2FY27 as against Rs. 68.55 crore in Q1FY27.

Mr. Seckin Arikan, Chairman, Route Mobile Limited and CEO, Proximus Global, said, "Our focus at
Route Mobile remains on strengthening our core business while expanding into differentiated, higher-
value solutions that can support long-term value creation. The addition of Heltar's conversational AI
capabilities is an important step in broadening our solutions portfolio. At the same time, we recognize the
importance of translating business momentum into sustainable proitability and delivering value for
shareholders. As we review our irst-half performance, our priorities remain disciplined execution,
prudent cost management and building a strong foundation for the future."

"We delivered revenue performance in Q2 FY27 in line with our guidance, alongside a recovery in gross
proit margin compared with the previous quarter. New product revenues also recorded a signiicant
increase, relecting the contribution of our expanding solutions portfolio. The acquisition of Heltar's
business, completed on July 29, strengthens our capabilities in conversational AI and enables us to address
evolving customer requirements. We are seeing an encouraging pipeline for these offerings and remain
focused on converting these opportunities into revenue while maintaining disciplined execution across the
business.," added Mr. Tushar Agnihotri, CEO, Route Mobile.

About Route Mobile Limited (https://routemobile.com/) (BSE: 543228; NSE: ROUTE)

Established in 2004, Route Mobile Limited ("RML"), a Proximus Global company is a cloud communications
platform service provider catering to enterprises, over-the-top (OTT) players, and mobile network
operators (MNO). RML's portfolio comprises solutions in messaging, voice, email, SMS iltering, analytics,
and monetization. RML has a diverse enterprise client base across various industries, including social media
companies, banks and inancial institutions, e-commerce entities, and travel aggregators. RML is
headquartered in Mumbai, India, with a global presence in Asia Paciic, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and
the Americas. Route Mobile is now a part of Proximus Group, a provider of digital services and
communication solutions operating in Belgium and international markets.
