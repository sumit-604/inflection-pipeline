Detailed rationale

CRISIL Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facility of Menon Bearings Ltd (MBL) to ‘Stable’ from ‘Positive’ while reaffirmed the rating at 'CRISIL BBB+'. The short term rating has been reaffirmed at 'CRISIL A2'.

 

The outlook revision reflects the less-than-expected revenue of the company, primarily because of muted demand from the tractor segment. While revenue grew 11% to Rs 218 crore in fiscal 2023, driven by higher realisations, it fell to Rs 55.53 crore in the first quarter of fiscal 2024 from Rs 62.90 crore in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal. 

 

Operating margin remains healthy above 20%. The working capital cycle is managed efficiently, as indicated by gross current assets of 140-168 days for the three fiscals through 2023. Also, reliance on debt remains limited. The financial risk profile should remain healthy despite debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) planned over the medium term.

 

The company entered new product lines in the automotive (auto) segment, such as brake lining and brake shoes by incorporating a wholly owned subsidiary, Menon Brakes Pvt Ltd, in December 2022. The investment stood at around Rs 8.25 crore as of March 2023, and commercial production began in April 2023. MBL plans capex of Rs 30 crore in fiscal 2024 and Rs 10 crore in fiscal 2025 for new products in the aluminum castings, thrust washers and engine bearing segments. These investments will be funded partly through debt.

 

The ratings continue to reflect the established market position and clientele of MBL and its healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by its modest scale of operations and revenue concentration in the auto sector.

Analytical Approach: Standalone

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description

Strengths:

  • Established market position and clientele: Presence of around three decades in the bearings industry has enabled MBL to establish its position across diverse markets and industries. The company has built healthy relationships with key clients, including John Deere, Tata Motors Ltd (‘CRISIL AA/Stable/CRISIL A1+’) and International Tractors Ltd and Brakes India Ltd (‘CRISIL AA+/Stable/CRISIL A1+’), because of its sound product and service quality.

 

  • Healthy financial risk profile: Capital structure was comfortable, as reflected in networth of over Rs 133 crore, and low gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of 0.11 time and 0.36 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2023. Debt protection metrics were strong, as indicated by interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of 18.36 times and 2.01 time, respectively, in fiscal 2023.

 

Weaknesses:

  • Modest, but increasing, scale of operations: Despite growth in the recent past, revenue remained modest at Rs 218 crore in fiscal 2023. Scale up of operations will be a key monitorable.

 

  • Revenue concentration in the auto industry: Of the total revenue, 80-90% comes from the auto sector and the remaining from the oil and gas, electrical and engineering industries. Within the auto segment, MBL derives bulk of its revenue from the commercial vehicle and tractor segments, which are inherently cyclical.  

Liquidity: Adequate

Cash accrual is expected over Rs 26 crore per annum over the medium term, after factoring dividend payout, and will comfortably cover yearly term debt obligation of Rs 4-6 crore. Bank limit utilisation averaged 23% over the 12 months through September 2023. Cash and equivalent stood at Rs 13.16 crore as on March 31, 2023.

Outlook: Stable

CRISIL Ratings believes MBL will continue to scale up operations due to healthy demand from end-user industries. The company will benefit from its strong market position and established clientele and sustain its healthy financial risk profile. 

Rating Sensitivity Factors

Upward factors:

  • Revenue growth of more than 25% and stable operating margin leading to higher net cash accrual
  • Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle

 

Downward factors:

  • Decline in revenue or profitability resulting in net cash accrual below Rs 15 crore
  • Increase in working capital requirement; larger-than-expected debt-funded capex or acquisition; or sizeable dividend payout; weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity

About the Company

Incorporated in 1991 as a part of the Kolhapur-based Menon group and promoted by Mr Ram Menon and his family members, MBL manufactures auto components such as bearings, bushes, thrust washers and bi-metal strips at its plant in Kolhapur. It also manufactures aluminum die-cast products.
