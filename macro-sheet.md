## HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

Role 1 reads the latest row set below. The equity risk premium and the rupee risk-free rate inform the RRM base r and any bottom-up cost of equity. The market PE rows (Nifty 50, Nifty Smallcap 250) are the denominator for the relative PE expression in Section 1B v3.6 Amendment 15. Nominal GDP growth is the hard ceiling on DCF terminal growth. The GARP regime note is one line on whether earnings growth is scarce or plentiful market-wide, which colours how much a growth premium is worth this month.

Fill every figure from a primary source (RBI, MOSPI, NSE, published index factsheets). Where a figure is not yet available for the month, write NOT FOUND and carry the prior month's value with its own date, never a guess.

## BINDING RULES THAT KEY OFF THIS SHEET

- **DCF terminal growth ceiling:** terminal growth in any DCF may not exceed the nominal GDP growth figure above. State the figure and the month stamp in the DCF. A perpetuity cannot outgrow the economy.
- **Relative PE denominator:** the Section 1B v3.6 relative PE expression uses the market PE rows above (Nifty 50 for larger names, Nifty Smallcap 250 for micro and small caps, or a blend where the name sits between). Cite the month stamp.
- **Staleness:** if the month stamp is more than one calendar month old, flag "macro sheet stale" in the Role 1 output and use the figures with that caveat until the sheet is refreshed.
