PRESS RELEASE 
 
OHL ANNOUNCES Q4 AND FULL YEAR FY 2025-2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS 
 
MUMBAI, MAY 4, 2026: Oriental Hotels Limited (OHL) reported its results for the fourth quarter and 
full year ending March 31, 2026. 
 STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDING MARCH 31, 2026 
 
 STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED MARCH 31, 2026 
 
Mr. Pramod Ranjan, Managing Director & CEO, Oriental Hotels Ltd. said, “In Q4 FY26, OHL reported 
revenue of INR 138 crores and a PAT of INR 29 crores. For the full fiscal year, OHL delivered its highest-
ever annual revenue of INR 501 crores, enabled by 11% growth in RevPAR, EBITDA of INR 140 crores 
and a robust PAT of INR 71 crores.” 
 
He added, “With the completion of major asset upgrades across key hotels in the OHL portfolio, 
coupled with sustained domestic demand, the company is poised to deliver strong performance in the 
upcoming fiscal year.” 
 
 
KEY HIGHLIGHTS  
• 
With a focus on promoting Environmental Stewardship under IHCL’s ESG+ framework of 
Paathya, OHL hotels renewable energy consumption stood at 61%. 
• 
IHCL’s iconic brand Taj is ranked as India’s Strongest Brand 2025 and World’s Strongest Hotel 
Brand 2025 as per Brand Finance-UK. OHL has three Taj branded hotels in its portfolio. 
About Oriental Hotels Limited  
 
Oriental Hotels Limited is an associate company of The Indian Hotels Company Limited. The company 
has seven hotels – Taj Coromandel, Chennai Taj Fisherman’s Cove Resort & Spa, Chennai Taj Malabar 
Resort & Spa, Cochin, Vivanta Coimbatore, Vivanta Mangalore; Gateway Madurai and Gateway 
Coonoor.
