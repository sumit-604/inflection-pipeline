CONFIRMED. FY26 standalone operating profit margin = 18.72% (AR MD&A p.68/69,

Profit 34.19 Cr ÷ Sales 178.86 Cr = 19.12% (screener-data, Quarters section,
last column). Margin held.

**2. FY26 CFO/PAT ~1.5x with net cash ~Rs 250 Cr.**
CONFIRMED. FY26 CFO 155.77 Cr ÷ FY26 PAT 102.79 Cr = 1.52x (screener-data).

FY26), against Nil borrowings (AR: "Outstanding borrowing of company as on
31st March 2026: Nil", SEBI Large Corporate declaration, p.23 of Q4 FY26
filing; AR MD&A: "total cash and investments on the balance sheet now
exceed H250 Crore with zero debt against it", p.68). Matches "~Rs 250 Cr"
reasonably (actual ~267 Cr).

**3. Lyophilised demand ~2x capacity per AGM, no capex announced.**
CONTESTED — the "no capex" half is REFUTED by the numbers. CWIP rose from
Rs 26.39 Cr (FY25) to Rs 51.36 Cr (FY26), nearly doubling (screener-data,
Balance Sheet, Capital Work in Progress row; AR MD&A p.68 confirms same
figures). The AR states outright: "Capital work-in-progress doubled...
confirming that the next phase of capacity expansion, particularly in
lyophilisation and other high-value manufacturing lines, is already
underway" (AR MD&A p.68) and again under Cost Pressure Risk mitigation:
"capital work-in-progress nearly doubled to H51.36 Crore, reflecting ongoing
investments in lyophilisation and other high-value manufacturing
capacities" (AR MD&A p.69). This is capex underway, not absence of capex.
The "~2x demand vs capacity" claim itself is AGM-sourced per the task brief
and is NOT FOUND in any document provided here (NO-CONCALL mode, no AGM
transcript in this corpus) — cannot be verified or refuted from the
provided inputs.

**4. Sunev promoter-entity RPTs (Rs 30 Cr IP purchase FY25) unresolved.**
CONFIRMED at the category level. AR Note 36 (Related Party Disclosures,
standalone, p.111-112) shows "Purchase of Patent IPR & Technology" = (3000.00)
Rs lakhs = Rs 30.00 Cr, booked in the FY25 comparative column, against
"Entities over which KMP or relative of KMP having Significant Influence" —
a category that names Sunev Pharma Solutions Private Limited (the Company's
largest single non-promoter-individual shareholder at 17.21%, AR Note 14.3),
alongside Spine Software Systems Pvt Ltd, Tark AI Private Limited and two
trusts. The same note also shows "Advance for in Licensing for Technology"
(2154.77) Rs lakhs = ~Rs 21.5 Cr to the same category in FY25. The AR does
NOT disaggregate this category by individual entity, so the amount
specifically attributable to Sunev (as opposed to the other three entities
in the group) is NOT FOUND in this document. FY26 shows Rs 0 against both
line items — no repeat transaction in FY26. Status of the FY25 transaction
itself (independent valuation, audit committee note, resolution) is not
further discussed beyond the standard RPT-policy boilerplate ("all Related
Party Transactions... entered into during FY 2025-26 were in the ordinary
course of business and on an arm's length basis", AR p.9558-9576) — this
statement covers FY26 transactions, not the FY25 IP purchase.

## ANALYST NOTE

GOOD rests on Block D (debt-free, net cash ~Rs 267 Cr) and a genuine FY26
breakout (revenue +20%, EBITDA nearly doubled, OPM ~19% held into Q1 FY27).
Block A's headline ROCE trend (13.07% to 18.56%) is real but is a 2-point
series, not a 10-year line — the screener CSV cannot support ROCE for
FY17-FY24, so downstream stages should not read "improving ROCE" as a
long, proven climb; it is one confirmed year-over-year jump. The moat score
(5/60, THIN) is half data-absence (5 tests need peer data not supplied)
and half genuine weakness (customer stickiness and network-effects tests
fail on 3 revenue-decline years and a decelerating 3-year growth window).
Revenue growth is the true binding constraint across Blocks C and F: the
9-year CAGR (7.55%) sits below every growth threshold used in this
scorecard, dragged down by the FY17-FY20 restructuring/loss years, even as
FY26 alone posted +20%. The Sunev-group Rs 30 Cr IP purchase (FY25) is
category-confirmed, not repeated in FY26, but not entity-disaggregated in
this corpus — carries forward as an open governance item, not resolved
here.
