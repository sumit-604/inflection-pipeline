## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from the transcripts alone)

Each item anchored (call, speaker, page).

**A. Guidance walk-back, 60% to 30%, inside four months. [HIGH]**
Jul-2023: "we would love to maintain the same rate of growth" on a stated 60%
FY23 rate (Vinita, p.3). Nov-2023: "we are looking at 30% growth for this year...
close it with a 30% growth rate by the end of March 24" (Vinita, p.5). The growth
commitment was halved between the two calls.

**B. FY26 Rs250cr/$25M target reaffirmed without reconciling the post-cut math. [HIGH]**
Jul-2023: "the next three years... we would definitely want to cross. I would say,
250 plus" (Vinita, p.13). Nov-2023: "still aiming for a 25 million revenue at the
end 25 so three years from now" (p.5), corrected to "become fy26" (p.11). The FY24
cut to 30% makes the residual FY25-FY26 growth needed to reach Rs250cr extreme;
neither management nor any analyst reconciled it.

**C. Internal numeric inconsistency inside the Nov-2023 call. [MEDIUM] — VERIFIED REAL.**
Opening remarks: "we did about 29 crore in Revenue in the first half... we had a
34% Revenue growth on the same period last year" (Vinita, p.2). Q&A: "revenue of
22.39 versus this time on a revenue of 26.92... EBITDA margin, I think we did about
34% this year against 25% last year" (Vinita, p.3). Reconciliation:
- Revenue figure: 29cr (opening) vs 26.92cr (Q&A) — a ~2cr / ~7.7% discrepancy.
- Revenue growth: opening states "34% Revenue growth"; actual from her own Q&A
  numbers is 26.92/22.39 = +20.2%. Analyst Pavan Kumar independently computed
  "some 20% growth" (p.3).
- The "34%" is actually the EBITDA MARGIN (9.77/26.92 = 36.3%, or 9.77/29 = 33.7%),
  which management conflated with "revenue growth" in the prepared remarks.
- EBITDA growth 9.77/5.40 = +81% reconciles with the stated "grew again by 80%."
The inconsistency B05 flags is real. Prepared remarks overstated revenue growth
(34% vs true 20%) and overstated the revenue figure (29 vs 26.92).

**D. Explicit growth-sustainability non-answer. [MEDIUM]**
Jul-2023, Raghava Bar asks if FY21-23 growth is sustainable: "I don't think I'm
very positioned well enough to answer that" (Vinita, p.14). Foreshadowed the cut
delivered four months later.

**E. Blue-chip enterprise customer FY24 goal dropped silently. [MEDIUM]**
Jul-2023: "get at least one of the blue chip customer this year" (Vinita, p.4).
Never mentioned again in the Nov-2023 call, prepared remarks or Q&A.

**F. Client-count target 25-30 to ~40 dropped, no update. [MEDIUM]**
Jul-2023: "probably 40 would be my sweet spot" within "next one year," "double the
revenue" (Vinita, p.10). Nov-2023 gives no client-count figure; management
substitutes a qualitative "diversified top-five" description (p.15-16).

**G. UK JV language regressed AND timeline lengthened. [MEDIUM]**
Jul-2023: "advanced stage of conversations... nothing complete... finalized in the
next six to nine months" (Vinita, p.15). Nov-2023: "we have not gotten to a point
where we have started evaluating their balance sheet" (p.13), "six months to 12
months away" (p.11). The language moved BACKWARD (from advanced-stage to
not-yet-diligencing the balance sheet) while the outer window lengthened.

**H. Repeated "don't have the number handy / made-up" answers on basic metrics. [LOW-MEDIUM]**
Jul-2023: prior-year headcount "I would just make this number up so please don't
help me accountable... 175 to 200" (p.6); top-5 mix "numbers that I don't have
handy" (p.8), "just from top of my head" (p.8); goodwill "I'll have to come back
to you" (p.12) and Bhavya Mantri "I need to check on that" (p.12). Nov-2023:
geographic mix "I don't have these numbers with me" (p.6); attrition "I don't have
an attrition rate for this year" (p.6); equity-shares quantum "I will defer to my
team" (p.8).

**I. Trade receivables up "very significantly"; payment cycle 2-3 days to 30-60 days. [LOW]**
Jul-2023: "the trade receivables have gone of very significantly this year... used
to clear our invoices second day... now they are taking the 30 days or 60 days"
(Vinita, p.13). Management's own volunteered signal of customer-quality / working-
capital deterioration. Not re-confirmed or quantified in Nov-2023.

**J. External-blame framing for the miss; no internal accountability. [MEDIUM]**
Nov-2023: "one of the challenge that we have had in the first six months and I
don't think this is particularly just for Systang. But I think this is for it
sector in general" (Vinita, p.5). No instance across either call of naming an
internal execution cause.

**K. Refuses to name any comparable India-listed peer. [LOW-MEDIUM]**
Nov-2023: "the company's name that I would quote are probably going to be just too
big for us to compare against... other than Accenture... thoughtworks also... I
just can't recall any name from top of my head right now" (Vinita, p.11).

**L. No concall after Nov-2023 despite operations through FY26. [MEDIUM]**
Corpus-level gap; roughly 2.5 years of promise-versus-delivery cannot be heard in
management's own voice.

**M. Crypto-derivative platform "three months away from the launch" never confirmed. [MAJOR — see Part 2]**
Jul-2023: "one such product that I'm really excited about where we are in probably
three months away from the launch is an institutional grade crypto derivative
insights and analytics platform" (Vinita, p.4). This was the July flagship product
claim, dated. In the Nov-2023 call (four months later, i.e., past the promised
launch), the flagship product example silently pivots to an "intelligent document
processing platform" (pp.9-10); the crypto-derivative launch is never confirmed as
having happened and is not tracked.

**N. Unreconciled margin-direction dispute inside the Nov-2023 call. [MINOR]**
Analyst Pavan Kumar: "EBITDA margins have gone down from 33 to 29%" (p.3).
Management rebutted with 25% to 34% improvement and never reconciled why the
analyst saw a decline. A second numeric-confusion strand beyond item C.

**O. "Massive improvement" demand claim while halving guidance. [MINOR]**
Nov-2023: "we have started seeing demand coming back again... a massive Improvement,
especially in the sector where we operate" (Vinita, p.12), and "H2 is gonna be
better than H1 and last year's H2" (p.5), asserted with no metric, in the same call
that cut FY24 growth from 60% to 30%. The optimism and the cut sit in tension.

**P. Equity-for-services and subsidized rates on high-failure-risk early-stage clients. [MINOR]**
Jul-2023: customers "largely early stage companies and growth stage companies"
(p.3); Systango takes minority equity and works at "instead of 35% margin, probably
having 10% margin" (p.7). Nov-2023: equity-for-services "probably 1% Maybe" of
revenue (p.8). A revenue-quality / asset-quality risk: subsidized rates on
startups with high mortality, with a small equity book on the same names.

**Q. Headcount flat/stagnant against the growth narrative. [MINOR]**
Jul-2023: "close to three hundred people," ~305-310 total, 260 billable (pp.3,6),
up from "175 to 200" a year prior. Nov-2023: "301 at the moment globally" (p.6) —
essentially flat over the period. The FY26 "500 people" target (p.5) implies a
ramp that is not visible in the last data point.

## PART 4 — CREDIBILITY GRADE ASSESSMENT

I concur with credibility grade D on transcript evidence ALONE, independent of the
injected delivery outcomes. The Nov-2023 call, four months after the Jul-2023 call,
shows: guidance halved; an unreconciled Rs250cr target; a live internal numeric
inconsistency in prepared remarks (item C); at least two silently dropped dated
commitments (blue-chip customer, crypto-derivative launch); "made-up" and
"don't-have-it" answers on basic metrics; no order book; external-only blame; and
refusal to benchmark against any comparable peer. That set supports a poor grade
without needing the FY26 actuals. D holds.
