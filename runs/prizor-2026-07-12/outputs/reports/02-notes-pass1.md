FRAMEWORK NOTE: The company prepares its financial statements under Indian GAAP
("Accounting Standards" per Section 133, Companies Act 2013), NOT Ind AS. This is
explicit in Note 2(a), Basis of Preparation (p.75/108). Consequences for this
extraction: no Ind AS 116 ROU/lease liability disclosure exists (leases are
classified finance/operating under AS-19 concepts only, Note 2g, p.75); no ECL
matrix (AS has no expected-credit-loss construct); no fair-value hierarchy note;
no Ind AS 108 segment disaggregation beyond a single reportable segment
statement. These are marked NOT APPLICABLE (framework) rather than NOT FOUND,
since Ind AS disclosures are not required of this company.

The annual report contains TWO note sets: Standalone (Notes 1-53, p.75-92) and
Consolidated (Notes 1-54, p.108-125). The subsidiary, Prizor Aitech India
Limited (51% held), was incorporated 9-Oct-2024 and is immaterial for FY25
