## SECTION 3: BUSINESS CONDUCT & ETHICAL TRACK RECORD

### 3A. RPT history

No royalty, above-market rent, management fee, guarantee for a private family entity, or non-arm's-length asset transfer was found in FY26. The main open item is the **undisclosed Taparia Magnetics Pvt Ltd** same-sector entity flagged in 1B — no evidence of transactions with PML was found, but it was also not found in the RPT disclosure to be ruled out.

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour
- Dividend: ₹2/share declared for FY26, total payout ~₹1.89 crore, ~14% payout ratio (✅ AR-FY26 line 7014-7020; corroborated 📰 multiple dividend trackers). Modest but consistent payout, no sign of cash hoarding paired with high promoter salary at an extreme level.
- No preferential allotment, warrant issue, or IPO/FPO activity during FY26 — CARO explicitly confirms nothing to report under clause 3(x) (✅ AR-FY26 line 4644-4656). No warrant-lapse history found or applicable.
- No buyback found in this search pass.
- **MD remuneration**: ratio to median employee remuneration = 34.01x; Sharad Taparia's pay rose 28.01% in FY26 vs. a 13.69% average increase for non-managerial employees and an 18.01% rise in median employee remuneration (✅ AR-FY26 line 2789-2802). This was via a shareholder-approved special resolution revising MD terms w.e.f. 1-Apr-2025 (✅ AR-FY26 line 3682-3683) — process was transparent and put to a shareholder vote, not unilaterally awarded, but the **growth-rate gap between MD pay and broad employee pay is a fair caution item**, particularly against ROE of ~12.4% (📰 screener.in aggregate data cited in search results).

This is flagged as a staleness/completeness caveat, not as an adverse finding — **a clean pledge record is itself the finding here.**
