Sub: Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure 
Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as amended (‘SEBI Listing Regulations’) - 
Receipt of closure directions for Company’s Dahej site 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Regulations, we hereby inform 
that the Company has received a notice on 3rd February, 2026, from the Gujarat Pollution 
Control Board (‘GPCB’) directing closure of operations at the Company’s plant located at Plot 
No. D-2/11/A/1, GIDC Dahej, Taluka - Vagra, District - Bharuch, 392130, Gujarat, India, 
within a maximum timeline of 48 hours from receipt thereof, to ensure safe shut down of 
industrial activity. The notice alleges violations under Section 33-A of the Water (Prevention 
and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974.  
 
GPCB has also directed submission of a bank guarantee of Rs. 15,00,000/- with a validity of 
one year. Apart from this, financial and operational impact is not ascertainable at this point in 
time.  
 
The Company is of the opinion that the alleged violation is unsubstantiated and is in the process 
of filing an urgent Writ Petition with the Gujarat High Court seeking an expedited intervention 
to reverse the directions contained in said notice.  
 
The Company believes in the merits of its technical and legal position and reiterates its 
commitment to maintain the highest standards of environmental compliance. 
 
Kindly take the above on record.
