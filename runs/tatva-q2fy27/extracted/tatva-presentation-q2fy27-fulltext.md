Chairman and Managing Director’s Message

                                         Good evening, ladies and gentlemen.

                                         I am pleased to share that the quarter under review has been a landmark one for
                                         Tatva Chintan, with the Company achieving its highest-ever quarterly turnover. This
                                         is an important milestone in our growth journey, and one that reflects the increasing
                                         commercial traction of our businesses, the widening relevance of our specialised
                                         chemistries and the sustained efforts we have invested in building a stronger
                                         business platform.

                                         What makes this performance particularly encouraging is that it is unfolding across a
                                         portfolio that offers us multiple avenues for growth. Our established business in SDA
                                         continues to provide a strong momentum, while our newer initiatives in energy
                                         storage and agro and pharma intermediates are progressively opening up additional
                                         opportunities and are in a path of consistent growth. We are beginning to see the
                                         benefits of investments made in technology, product development, customer
                                         relationships and manufacturing capabilities translate into tangible business
                                         outcomes.

Chairman and Managing Director’s Message

 With a quarter of historic revenues and productivity, we have achieved significant operating leverage which is
 depicted by decrease in employee cost by 2% accompanied by decrease in other expenses by 2% but
 unfortunately, EBITDA has remained at nearly similar levels primarily due to two temporary phenomena. During
 the quarter we have seen erratic short supplies in some of the solvents which has led to a price increase in these
 solvents by 60%-100%. Some of these solvents are used by us in large volumes. Despite of having healthy
 revenue growth in highest margin SDA, the above impact has increased our overall raw material cost. Secondly,
 we have also experienced a rampant increase in global freights, especially, in certain sectors of US and Europe
 where the increase is more than 100%. Fortunately, we are now seeing a gradual reversal in both the above
 trends. We expect it to normalise withing the third quarter. We foresee the revenue trend growth with similar
 momentum over the next quarters and EBITDA margin to resume its healthy trajectory.

 Our priority, therefore, is two-fold: to sustain the momentum in revenue and to ensure that the growth we
 generate translates into healthy & sustainable profits. We remain confident in the underlying opportunities
 before us and are encouraged by the progress being made across our businesses.

 Across our portfolio, we continue to see encouraging developments.

 In Phase Transfer Catalysts, our focus remains on strengthening an established franchise whose relevance
 extends well beyond its traditional applications. As customers increasingly seek greater process efficiency,
 improved manufacturing economics and more sustainable production methods, PTC chemistry continues to offer
 a compelling value proposition. We believe this provides a sound basis for sustained organic growth, considering
 its contribution in other product categories too.

Chairman and Managing Director’s Message

 In Structured Directing Agents, the implementation of EURO 7 emission standards is already translating into
 a meaningful contribution to our sales, providing tangible evidence of the demand emerging from this regulatory
 transition. This progress reinforces Tatva Chintan’s standing as a reliable supplier of SDA to customers across
 the globe. We believe that the trust we continue to earn through consistent quality, dependable supply and
 technical expertise will strengthen our position further and open up additional opportunities for growth in this
 business.

 In Electrolyte Salts and Solutions, energy storage continues to offer an attractive avenue for expansion. Our
 engagement with existing customers remains encouraging, hybrid-battery application is progressing towards
 commercialisation, and additional products are being scaled up for energy-storage applications. These initiatives
 are intended to broaden our participation in a market where specialised chemistry and technical reliability will
 be increasingly important.

 Our Pharma, Agro and Speciality Chemicals business is gaining encouraging momentum, with our recently
 commercialised agro products performing well and our pharma product beginning to contribute to the business.
 The early traction from these products is an encouraging validation of our development efforts and strengthens
 our confidence in the opportunities ahead. It has also reinforced our resolve to accelerate work on other
 products in our development pipeline, with increased focus on piloting, process development and the steps
 required to bring them to commercialisation. We believe that this expanding pipeline can become an important
 driver of growth for the business in the years ahead.

Chairman and Managing Director’s Message

 In Semiconductor Chemicals, we continue to make encouraging progress across all five products in our
 development portfolio, each advancing well through its respective stages of development, validation and
 customer qualification. We see these products as much more than individual commercial opportunities;
 together, they represent the building blocks of a new phase of growth for Tatva Chintan. The progress achieved
 so far strengthens our conviction in the potential of this business, and we believe the capabilities we are
 developing today will create meaningful opportunities for the Company in the years ahead. This is a segment
 whose contribution we are working patiently to build, and one that we believe shareholders will increasingly see
 shaping the next chapter of our growth.

 Alongside these business developments, our new greenfield project is progressing in line with the planned
 schedule. This facility will play an important role in supporting the next phase of Tatva Chintan’s growth,
 providing the additional manufacturing capacity required to accommodate the organic expansion of our existing
 businesses as well as the commercialisation of new molecules emerging from our development pipeline.

 Ladies and gentlemen, we view this quarter as an affirmation of the direction in which Tatva Chintan is moving.
 The record turnover is a meaningful achievement, and the developments across our business segments give us
 reason to remain confident about the opportunities ahead.

Chairman and Managing Director’s Message

 We are conscious that growth must be accompanied by financial discipline. Cost movements will need to be
 managed, customer pricing adjusted progressively where appropriate, and investments executed with care. But
 these considerations do not diminish our ambition; they are part of the discipline required to pursue it
 successfully.
 Our focus remains firmly on building a larger, more diversified and technologically differentiated business — one
 that can create value not only through higher revenues, but also through stronger customer relationships,
 deeper technical capabilities and sustainable earnings over time.
 We believe the foundations we have laid over the past three decades position us well for the opportunities ahead.
 We will continue to build on those foundations with conviction, disciplined execution and a clear focus on long-
 term value creation.
