## Monitorables and triggers

1. Watch the H2 FY27 consolidated EBITDA margin in the FY27 audited results (about May 2027), and the margin answer on the 24 Nov 2026 call. A full year 25% needs H2 at 27.4 to 27.6%; at or above 27% reads H1 as seasonal timing, and below 24% reads the 25 to 27% guide as set off the H2 FY26 peak. Ask for the standalone versus subsidiary split, since the H1 gap sat in the subsidiaries. This tests the margin climb.
2. Watch FY27 consolidated CFO/PBT in the FY27 cash flow statement. It was 42% in H1 FY27. Above 90% supports a growth driven reading of the cash flag; below 60% reads it as structural. This tests cash conversion.
3. Watch receivables older than six months in FY27 AR Note 15, and ask for the split by private, government, PPP and related party payers. FY26 was 22.4% of gross with Rs 220 Lakh older than three years; below 20% is the healthy line. This tests whether the PPP labs carry a payer problem.
4. Watch borrowings and net debt to EBITDA on the 31 Mar 2027 balance sheet. Borrowings were Rs 11,467 Lakh at 30 Sep 2026, net debt to EBITDA about 3.6 times on annualised H1, and interest cover 3.21 times. Below 3.0 times net debt to EBITDA and above 4 times interest cover would meet the stated plan; above 3.5 times is the red line. This tests the funding of the build.
5. Watch capital work in progress and the capitalisation ratio in the FY27 AR CWIP ageing schedule. CWIP was Rs 6,977 Lakh at 30 Sep 2026 and only 11.5% was capitalised in FY26. CWIP falling as labs commission confirms the build; CWIP still rising with the H2 margin below 24% says the labs are not filling. This tests capex led growth.
6. Watch ITCPL in the FY27 AR AOC-1 and goodwill notes. ITCPL PAT at or above Rs 711 Lakh, with goodwill test inputs and the swap valuation report disclosed, supports the fair price reading of the Rs 7,812 Lakh goodwill. This tests the largest related party deal.
7. Watch BSE filings for the use of the Rs 4,698 Lakh share issue and the TIC warrants. A named, priced target with a valuation report for the Rs 2,200 Lakh acquisition pool, and TIC paying the Rs 20.22 Cr balance within 18 months, support alignment. A lapse of unfunded warrants moves the promoter verdict to CONCERN. This tests capital allocation.
8. Watch BSE Reg 30 filings and the FY27 related party note for governance repair. The company secretary and independent director seats should be filled; the AAL advance of Rs 151.06 Lakh and receivable of Rs 77.82 Lakh should be collected, with no new advances; and the ITC Ltd suit should appear in contingent liabilities. A second independent exit moves the promoter verdict to CONCERN. This tests governance.
