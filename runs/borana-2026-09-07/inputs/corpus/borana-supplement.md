## Business segments (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q4, Page 13)
- Greige Fabric: 92% of FY26 revenue (87% in FY25). "Versatile base for dyeing, printing, technical & decor applications."
- PTY Yarn (partially textured yarn): 8% of FY26 revenue (12% in FY25). Backward-integration product.
- Integrated operations in Surat, 4 units.

## Clientele (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q3, Page 17)
- Named B2B customers: Ganga Tex Trendz (P) Ltd, Oswal International, Nirankari Textiles, Sudarshan Silk Mills, Ambaji Fabrics, Varun Textile, Shri Hari Tex.
- These are textile traders/processors/mills. Concentration and any relation to promoter entities to be checked in the deep dive.

## End-market growth (Investor Presentation, FY2026, Q4, Page 19; Q3, Page 19)
- India synthetic fibre ~4.5-5 million tonnes/annum; ~80% consumed domestically.
- Polyester filament yarn (PFY) ~2.53 mn tonnes (2024) to ~3.06 mn tonnes (2030), CAGR ~3.2%.
- Synthetic fibre overall CAGR ~5.6% to 2030.
- India is the third-largest polyester-yarn exporter (after China, Vietnam); exports to 100+ countries.
- Demand drivers: athleisure, performance/technical textiles, rPET/recycled polyester.

## Demand-absorption note (deep-dive flag)
- The addressable market grows ~3.2% (PFY) to ~5.6% (synthetic fibre) a year.
- Borana grew revenue 34% in FY26 and plans to double capacity in 24 months.
- So growth is share gain plus capacity addition into a modestly growing market.
  Whether demand absorbs the doubled capacity without price concession is a
  load-bearing question. A weaver flooding a slow-growing market usually
  concedes price, which would compress the 24% margin toward the peer range.
