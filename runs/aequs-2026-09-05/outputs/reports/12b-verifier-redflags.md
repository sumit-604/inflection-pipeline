## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built before opening B05/B06)

Thirty items of red-flag grade. Ordered by weight, not by discovery.

### 1.1 Earnings quality and disclosure timing

**RF-09. Reported EBITDA includes other income. Q4 FY26 operational EBITDA was Rs 42mn against a headline Rs 321mn, and the Rs 279mn gap was disclosed only one quarter later.**
- Q4 FY26 call, Jun-2026 PDF p.6, Dinesh Iyer (CFO): *"Q4 delivered INR3,671 million in revenue, our highest quarterly revenue ever... EBITDA was INR321 million at 9% margin. The margin compression from Q3 reflects the timing of consumer electronics capacity coming fully online."* No other-income figure given anywhere in that call's prepared remarks.
- Q1 FY27 call, Aug-2026 PDF p.5, Harish Bang: *"Reported EBITDA, which includes other income, stood at INR215 million... The sequential comparison was affected by the movement in other income, which reduced from INR 279 million in Q4 FY'26 to IN67 million in Q1 FY'27. Excluding other income, operational EBITDA increased from INR 42 million in Q4 FY'26 to INR148 million in Q1 FY'27."*
- Arithmetic: Rs 279mn of the Rs 321mn Q4 FY26 EBITDA (87%) was other income, mainly FX. Operational margin in Q4 FY26 was 1%, not the 9% presented on that call.
- Same contamination at segment level. Jun-2026 PDF p.6: *"For Q4, aerospace segment revenue was INR3,040 million... and EBITDA was INR1,010 million, reflecting a growth of over 100%."* Aug-2026 PDF p.5 then reveals: *"it moderated sequentially from Q4 FY'26 due to significant other income in Q4 FY'26, which mainly included foreign exchange gain."* The FY26 aerospace ROCE of 20% (Jun-2026 PDF p.6) rests on that same base.
- Only partial contemporaneous disclosure, and only under analyst challenge. Jun-2026 PDF p.9, Suraj Malu (Catamaran) asked how a 27% FY26 aerospace margin reconciles with a 20% FY27 guide. Harish Bang: *"27% is the segment EBITDA, which includes other income and it excludes the unallocated corporate costs."* That is the sole other-income mention in the entire Q4 call, it is in Q&A, it covers the segment line only, and it gives no magnitude.
- FY26 full-year other income is **NOT FOUND** in any of the three transcripts.

**Peer contrast, same quarter, both peers do the opposite:**
- AZAD Q1 FY27, Aug-2026 PDF p.5, Ronak Jajoo (CFO): *"Other income came to INR4 crores in this quarter. This line shows a sharp moderation from INR9.1 crores in quarter 1 FY26 and substantial decrease from elevated INR17 crores recorded in quarter 4 FY26... In quarter 4 FY26, other income was heavily bloated by major nonoperating foreign currency gain."*
- AZAD Q1 FY27, Aug-2026 PDF p.9, Ronak Jajoo, asked directly whether rupee depreciation sits in EBITDA: *"This is below the EBITDA margin, this is not build into the EBITDA margin."*
- UNIMECH Q1 FY27, Aug-2026 PDF p.6, Ramakrishna Kamojhala (CFO): *"Q4 FY26 benefited from significant higher other incomes of INR15 crores approximately compared with INR7 crores this quarter. Therefore, on an operating basis, the quality of earnings this quarter is meaningfully stronger, with profitability being driven primarily by the underlying business rather than treasury income."*

Both listed comparators quarantined FX/treasury income from operating performance and volunteered the magnitude in the quarter it mattered. Aequs did the reverse: included it in the headline, and disclosed it only after it reversed.

**RF-10. Q4 FY26 PAT contained an undisclosed Rs 90mn exceptional gain, also revealed only in the following call.**
- Jun-2026 PDF p.6, Dinesh Iyer: *"Reported PAT for Q4 was a loss of INR541 million. This includes higher depreciation from consumer capex and increased tax provisions on the profit from aerospace segment."* No exceptional item named.
- Aug-2026 PDF p.6, Harish Bang: *"The reported Q4 FY'26 PAT loss was INR541 million, included an exceptional gain of INR 90 million, which included a corporate provision reversal. Excluding this gain, the adjusted Q4 PAT loss was INR631 million."*
- Combined with RF-09, the Q4 FY26 results presentation omitted **two** favourable items totalling Rs 369mn, both surfaced later by the successor finance lead after the outgoing CFO had left.

**RF-11. The flagship FY27 profit guidance ("doubling operational EBITDA") has no defined base, and management endorsed an analyst's number that does not reconcile with reported FY26 EBITDA.**
- Jun-2026 PDF p.4, Aravind Melligeri: *"We project doubling our operational EBITDA."* Reaffirmed Aug-2026 PDF p.3.
- FY26 reported EBITDA: Rs 1,545mn = Rs 154.5cr (Jun-2026 PDF p.6). Doubling that is ~Rs 309cr.
- Aug-2026 PDF p.15, Vansh Modi (Swan Investments): *"So, roughly around INR180 crores of EBITDA, is that right?"* Harish Bang: *"Yes. Whatever that number translates to. Yes."*
- The two statements reconcile only if FY26 "operational EBITDA" was ~Rs 90cr, i.e. roughly Rs 600-650mn of FY26's Rs 1,545mn reported EBITDA was other income. That FY26 other-income figure is **NOT FOUND** in any transcript. Either way the guidance carries a ~70% ambiguity band (Rs 180cr vs Rs 309cr) on the single metric management named as the year's proof point.

### 1.3 Operating metrics that moved the wrong way

**RF-12. New aerospace parts added collapsed from 433 to 86 in one quarter, contradicting a moat claim made one call earlier. Nobody asked.**
- Jun-2026 PDF p.4, Rajeev Kaul: *"We added 433 new parts in Q4 alone, taking our total aerospace portfolio to 5,654 SKUs."*
- Aug-2026 PDF p.3, Rajeev Kaul: *"During the quarter, we added 86 new parts, further expanding one of India's most extensive aerospace manufacturing portfolio to 5,740 parts."*
- An 80% quarter-on-quarter fall in the company's own headline operating metric. Directly against two claims from the intervening call:
  - Jun-2026 PDF p.10, Aravind Melligeri: *"It's a complex business to grow, adding hundreds of components every month."*
  - Jun-2026 PDF p.15-16, Aravind Melligeri: *"I don't know how many customers, how many suppliers deliver 100-plus, 150 parts per month... That's a definite MOAT for us as a company."*
- 86 parts in a quarter is ~29 per month against a claimed moat rate of 100-150 per month. Management also nominated this metric as its own performance proxy (Jun-2026 PDF p.16: *"we are adding more parts on a quarterly basis. That reflects our performance"*).

**RF-13. Consumer utilization has fallen for three straight quarters while the 40-50% FY27 exit target is held unchanged, and the metric was substituted when pressed.**
- 31% (Feb-2026 PDF p.10, corrected) → 23% (Jun-2026 PDF p.4, Rajeev Kaul: *"driving capacity utilization from 23% today to a target of 40% to 50% by the year end"*) → 22% (Aug-2026 PDF p.9, Rajeev Kaul: *"current quarter we did around 22% and we still hold by Q4 to touch 40% to 50%"*).
- Pressed twice on the Q1 call by Gaurav Nagori (Avendus Spark), Aug-2026 PDF p.7-8. Rajeev Kaul's answer redirects to a different metric: *"the consumer segment achieved 16% quarter-on-quarter revenue growth, first is that, okay. That is a much better metric to see our utilization of the performance."* Gaurav re-asks: *"My question was that the utilization has dipped Q-o-Q from Q4 to Q1 and yet revenues are up. So, have we taken any capacity expansion...?"* Answer: *"No, we have not done any capacity expansion."* The dip is therefore not a denominator effect and remains unexplained.
- Jyoti Gupta (Ashika), Aug-2026 PDF p.8, put the arithmetic directly: *"there is still the ramp-up of consumer electronics completely the way we're looking at from 23% to coming to 46% doesn't seem or could be achieved by quarter four FY27."*
- The 31% → 23% drop between Q3 and Q4 FY26 is never reconciled at all; the denominator basis may have changed when consumer electronics commissioned, but management never says so.

**RF-14. Yield and rejection rates refused across two consecutive calls, while yield is simultaneously invoked as the explanation for the numbers.**
- Jun-2026 PDF p.15, Deep Shah (New Vernon Capital) asked for *"your rejection rates or yields in this segment."* Rajeev Kaul: *"we cannot comment on a specific yield metrics as these are customer confidential."*
- Aug-2026 PDF p.7, Gaurav Nagori: *"what is the yield that you are really getting in this segment as we speak."* Not answered. Aravind Melligeri: *"it's ultimately a combination of utilization and the yield to get the higher revenue, right? I mean you can derive from that."*
- Management uses yield as the causal explanation for revenue rising on falling utilization, then declines to quantify it, two quarters running. This is a repeated evasion (2+ quarters) on the single operational metric that would evidence the ramp.

### 1.4 Contradictions and dodges

**RF-23. Management states that raising margin would cost win rate. That is an admission of no pricing power, against a "definite MOAT" claim made in the same call.**
Jun-2026 PDF p.18, Aravind Melligeri, answering Bhavika Singhvi on whether vertical integration produces a margin benefit: *"We believe we have a right level of margins for this business. And at the rate at which we are scaling, obviously, if we increase our margin, our win rate might come down. Those are all the competitive environment globally we play. So, it is a commercial call."*
Supporting, same call, Jun-2026 PDF p.13, Rajeev Kaul: *"we are into contract manufacturing. So, we only manufacture for brands. So, we don't own any IPs."*
This is management's own answer to the question analysts asked twice (Feb-2026 PDF p.10, Bhavika Singhvi: *"why your ecosystem advantage is not getting reflected on your margins?"*), and it is the opposite of the moat framing used elsewhere in the same transcript.

**RF-24. The China equipment-dependency question was put directly and neither half of it was answered.**
Jun-2026 PDF p.12, Nikhil Chowdhary (Toro Wealth): *"in DRHP had probably flagged that critical equipment's dependency on China, and lately, we've been hearing some feedback that Chinese have been restricting a lot of equipment's. So, are we facing any issues in getting those equipment's? If there's any non-Chinese vendors, like have we found any non-Chinese alternate vendors for the same?"*
Aravind Melligeri, PDF p.12-13: *"we have had, gone through some of these challenges in the past. It continues to evolve over a period of time. At the same time, we are working with customers always to find alternate different geographic locations, including India. But it takes time to get the suppliers developed and qualified for this kind of equipment. These have happened over decades in China, and it won't happen overnight here."*
Neither question answered: no statement on whether restrictions currently bite, no statement on whether alternate vendors exist. This gates the consumer-electronics ramp that carries the whole FY27 consolidated guidance.

**RF-08. "100% Make in India" flagship claim sits in the same call as "our raw material is like 99% is imported."**
- Aug-2026 PDF p.3-4, Aravind Melligeri, prepared remarks: *"Aequs will deliver completely assembled wheels built from India-sourced aerospace-qualified aluminum with forging, machining, surface treatment, and assembly, all within the Belagavi Aerospace ecosystem. That is 100% Make in India for a flight-critical product."*
- Aug-2026 PDF p.18, Aravind Melligeri, answering Rashmi (Bright Polytech): *"I think our raw material is like 99% is imported. So, I don't think there is any possibility of changing that anytime soon, because we don't have any qualified sources in India for that to change."*
- Aug-2026 PDF p.17 narrows it further: *"only were able to get maybe one or two alloys right now in India to get qualified... There are only two grades we have been able to get it done that also for forging."*
The narrow claim (one product, one or two qualified aluminium grades) is defensible; the headline framing is not, and the two statements sit ~15 pages apart in one call.

**RF-17. CEO and VP Finance gave contradicting five-year mix answers to two different analysts on the same call.**
Aug-2026 PDF p.15, Harish Bang to Vansh Modi: *"over a period of 5 years we expect 40% - 60% consumer."*
Aug-2026 PDF p.18, Aravind Melligeri to Praveen Gupta: *"we still see consumer going to be next 5 years at least lower than the aerospace. Even higher growth of consumer."*
At the top of Harish's band the two answers are irreconcilable. B05 promoted the 40-60% figure into its trigger list without noting the CEO's contradicting answer.

**RF-18. Two different aerospace utilization figures quoted by analysts on the same call, neither corrected; the capacity question was refused outright.**
Aug-2026 PDF p.12, Deepak (Kotak): *"And we are already at 70% utilization."* Not corrected.
Aug-2026 PDF p.18, Praveen Gupta: *"But we have already reached the utilization level of 80%."* Not corrected.
Aug-2026 PDF p.15-16, Nagendra Maurya (Growthx): *"the capacity numbers in terms of million hour from base of FY26 has increased from 3.62 million to currently 4.78 million"* — neither figure matches the company's own prior disclosures (3.96mn at Feb-2026 PDF p.6; 4.70mn at Jun-2026 PDF p.5). Aravind Melligeri declined the question entirely: *"We do not really project on that basis... that's not the tracker to see how the growth is happening."*

**RF-19. Negative operating cash flow presented as improved cash conversion, in the same paragraph.**
Aug-2026 PDF p.6, Harish Bang: *"Cash flow from operations was negative INR414 million, primarily reflecting the additional working capital required to support the higher operating cycle... We saw an improvement in the working capital efficiency with net working capital days reducing from 127 days at the end of FY'26 to 125 days in Q1 FY'27... This reflects better conversion of the higher operating scale into cash."*
A Rs 414mn operating cash outflow is described as better conversion into cash. Unchallenged.

### 1.5 Guidance construction and unsupported targets

**RF-06. The profitability horizon has slipped three times and now runs to FY31.**
Pre-IPO framing, as put to management by Ashok Kumar, Feb-2026 PDF p.23: *"management was guiding for the overall PAT positive will be achieved by the end of FY27... are we still on track?"* Aravind Melligeri declined: *"we cannot comment on it at this stage."*
Jun-2026 PDF p.6, Rajeev Kaul: *"we fully expect to see consolidated PAT hit break-even by H1 FY28."*
Aug-2026 PDF p.10, Harish Bang: *"PAT break-even by H1 of FY28, consumer PAT break-even by FY30, and we'll have a decent PAT in FY31, which will translate to about 20% steady state ROCE in FY31."* Asked for a PAT margin: *"we stick to the milestone what we have given. We are not disclosing anything beyond this."*
The end state is now "a decent PAT" five years out, with no number attached.

**RF-25. Consumer steady-state 18-20% EBITDA and 18-20% ROCE are asserted in all three calls, from a current -49% EBITDA margin, with a stated precondition well outside the guidance horizon.**
Claims: Feb-2026 PDF p.22 (Dinesh Iyer, *"about 18-20% EBITDA margin"*); Jun-2026 PDF p.10 (Aravind, *"overall 20% EBITDA is our long-term goal"*; Harish, *"same ROCEs as we do in our aerospace segment"*); Aug-2026 PDF p.9 (Harish, *"ROCE profile also on a steady state basis would be in the similar range 18% to 20% as aerospace"*).
Current: Q1 FY27 consumer revenue Rs 734mn, segment EBITDA loss Rs 361mn (Aug-2026 PDF p.6) = -49% margin.
Precondition, Jun-2026 PDF p.15, Aravind: *"When you get to somewhere around 75% utilization level, we start seeing the margin levels."* Confirmed to Disha: *"So, this 18% to 20% can be seen at 75% to 80% utilization? — Yeah."*
The FY27 target is 40-50% utilization. The guided margin is therefore unreachable inside the guidance horizon by management's own stated mechanism, and utilization is currently 22% and falling.

**RF-26. A ~10x consumer revenue target for FY29 stated once, never supported, never repeated.**
Jun-2026 PDF p.17-18, Navin Vijay (NS Capital) walks the arithmetic: gross block Rs 830cr + Rs 500cr = Rs 1,330cr, at the stated 1.5x asset turn = *"a turnover of INR2,000 crores in the future. My question is, when do we envisage to hit that number?"* Aravind Melligeri: *"Most probably closer to in '29."*
Against FY26 consumer revenue of Rs 184cr (Jun-2026 PDF p.6: INR1,840 million). Q1 FY27 consumer annualises to ~Rs 294cr. This is the most aggressive number management put on the record in the set and it appears nowhere in B05.

**RF-22. MoU headline commitments do not reconcile to the funded capex plan, and management declined to reconcile them.**
Jun-2026 PDF p.3: Hosur Rs 1,900cr over 10 years (Tamil Nadu MoU) plus Karnataka Rs 2,856cr over 5 years. Total headline Rs 4,756cr.
Aug-2026 PDF p.12, Harish Bang: *"we have laid down a plan from this year to FY31 for the next five years investing about USD350 Million to USD400 Million of overall capex."*
The Karnataka MoU alone covers the same five-year window as the entire funded plan. Asked to split it, Jun-2026 PDF p.19, Dinesh Iyer: *"we can't really split that data."* Asked for the funding breakdown, Aravind Melligeri: *"Let's take that offline."*

**RF-27. The CFO's statement that he had announced his exit "earlier this year" is not supported by the only prior call.**
Jun-2026 PDF p.6, Dinesh Iyer: *"I announced earlier this year that I will be stepping down as CFO at Aequs at the end of June 2026."* The Feb-2026 transcript contains no reference to any CFO transition, and on the same Q4 call Aravind introduces it as news: *"Dinesh Iyer, our Chief Financial Officer, who has informed us of his decision to step down at the end of June 2026 for personal reasons"* (Jun-2026 PDF p.4). Either the announcement was made outside the earnings-call channel or the statement is inaccurate.

**RF-28. Order book has no disclosed basis or roll-forward, and the two largest wins are excluded from it with no value attached.**
$814mn (Feb-2026 PDF p.6) → $889mn (Jun-2026 PDF p.5) → $1,004mn (Aug-2026 PDF p.4). Aug-2026 PDF p.12, Aravind: *"We are not disclosing that contract value. It should show up in order book next quarter because none of the Farnborough contracts... is not reflecting right now in our order book."* No additions/executions/cancellations bridge in any call. Mitigating: AZAD gives only a "rolling order book" number too (AZAD May-2026 PDF p.16) and also declines contract values, so this sits at sector norm.

**RF-31. Q1 FY27 cash: Rs 830mn capex, Rs 414mn operating outflow, cash down Rs 675mn, capex acceleration flagged, funding to come from debt plus a possible USD 150mn raise.**
Aug-2026 PDF p.6 (capex Rs 830mn, CFO -Rs 414mn, cash Rs 3,015mn → Rs 2,340mn); Aug-2026 PDF p.4 (*"we are evaluating acceleration of aerospace capex plan"*); Jun-2026 PDF p.15, Harish Bang (*"for FY27, we will leverage borrowings and also some bit of internal accruals"*); Aug-2026 PDF p.16, Aravind (*"about USD150 million kind of a raise we'll have to do"*).
