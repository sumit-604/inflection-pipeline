  61      TruAlt Bioenergy Delivers Strong Q1 FY27 Performance; Total Income Nearly Doubles
  62            and PAT Rises Over 12x as Dual-Feed Integration Strategy Drives Growth
  63  Bengaluru, July 28, 2026: TruAlt Bioenergy Limited, India's largest ethanol producer by installed capacity and the
  64  country's only dedicated listed biofuels company, today announced a strong performance for the first quarter
  65  ended June 30, 2026, marking its first year-on-year first-quarter results as a listed company. The performance
  66  reflects the successful integration of the Company's dual-feed ethanol platform and the continued execution of its
  67  integrated bioenergy strategy.

  82  Q1 FY27 marked the first full operational quarter following the successful completion of TruAlt's grain integration
  83  programme, representing a significant milestone in the Company's transformation. Installed ethanol capacity
  84  increased by 43% from 1,400 KLPD in Q1 FY26 to 2,000 KLPD in Q1 FY27, with approximately 1,300 KLPD (65%)
  85  now operating on dual-feed technology. This strategic integration has expanded the Company's feedstock basket
  86  from predominantly sugar derivatives to a combination of sugar derivatives and grains (unfit for human
  87  consumption), enabling near year-round ethanol production while enhancing operational flexibility, feedstock
  88  security and long-term production resilience
  89  
  90  Operational Highlights
  91  
  92  The diversified feedstock strategy has enhanced operational flexibility, strengthened feedstock security and
  93  improved profitability, with grain-based operations delivering approximately 6% higher profitability than
  94  sugar-based production. Current capacity utilisation of 60.57% provides significant headroom for future growth
  95  without requiring substantial incremental capital expenditure.
  96  
  97  Strategic Progress Across Growth Businesses
  98  ▪ During the quarter, TruAlt continued advancing its long-term growth initiatives across compressed biogas
  99      (CBG), sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and downstream fuel retailing.
 100  ▪     Construction continues across four CBG plants under the Company's joint venture with Sumitomo Corporation,
 101        while preparatory activities are progressing for six additional CBG plants under its partnership with GAIL
 102        (India) Limited.
 103  ▪     The Company also progressed its proposed 100 million litres per annum Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)
 104        project in Andhra Pradesh, with engineering work, statutory approvals and stakeholder engagement
 105        advancing. The project has received a ₹150 crore grant under the PM JI-VAN Yojana, supporting its
 106        commercial viability.
 107  ▪     TruAlt currently operates seven retail fuel outlets, with further expansion planned under its phased strategy to
 108        establish a network of 100 outlets. Given the heightened geopolitical tensions in West Asia and the resulting
 109        volatility in crude oil markets, the Company adopted a cautious and calibrated approach to expansion,
 110        consciously deferring accelerated rollout while prioritising capital discipline, strategic locations and
 111        sustainable unit economics.

 118  Commenting on the results, Mr. Vijay Nirani, Managing Director, TruAlt Bioenergy Limited, said: “Q1 FY27 marks
 119  an important milestone for TruAlt Bioenergy as we report our first year-on-year first-quarter performance as a listed
 120  company. I am delighted to share that the Company has delivered a strong financial and operational performance
 121  and we are now beginning to see the benefits of the strategic investments we have made over the past year,
 122  particularly our transition from a mono-feed ethanol manufacturing platform to a dual-feed platform. This
 123  transformation has given us the flexibility to produce ethanol from both sugar-based feedstocks and grains (unfit for
 124  human consumption), enabling near year-round operations while creating a more resilient, efficient and profitable
 125  business. Our current capacity utilisation stands at 60.57%, which gives us significant room to grow using our
 126  existing assets. Our immediate focus is on increasing our gross capacity utilisation, improving operational
 127  efficiencies and driving higher production from our existing manufacturing platform. We believe this will support
 128  sustainable growth, improve profitability and generate stronger returns over the coming years.
 129  
 130  We also made good progress across our other business verticals during the quarter. Our CBG projects with
 131  Sumitomo Corporation and GAIL (India) Limited continue to progress well, while our proposed Sustainable Aviation
 132  Fuel (SAF) project is moving forward steadily. In our downstream fuel retail business, we adopted a disciplined
 133  approach to expansion in view of the heightened geopolitical tensions in West Asia and the resulting volatility in
 134  crude oil markets, prioritising long-term value creation over rapid expansion. With a strong manufacturing base, a
 135  diversified portfolio and a supportive policy environment, we are confident about the opportunities ahead and
 136  remain committed to creating sustainable long-term value for our shareholders while contributing to India's energy
 137  security and clean energy transition."

 139  About TruAlt Bioenergy:
 140  TruAlt Bioenergy Limited is one of India’s largest biofuels companies and the country’s only dedicated bioenergy
 141  enterprise, combining deep agricultural linkages with large-scale industrial capabilities to deliver diversified and
 142  sustainable energy solutions. The company has played a pioneering role in India’s bioenergy transition and was
 143  among the early producers of Compressed Biogas (CBG) under the Government of India’s SATAT scheme
 144  launched in 2018. TruAlt Bioenergy is also the first biofuels company in India to attain Oil Marketing Company
 145  (OMC) status, setting a new benchmark for integrated participation across the energy value chain, including its
 146  entry into the public B2C space through fuel retailing. Further strengthening its leadership in next-generation and
 147  future fuels, the company is among the early movers in Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) and is strategically
 148  positioned to establish one of the largest SAF production facilities in India, while actively exploring advanced
 149  biofuels and low-carbon energy solutions.
 150  
 151  Beyond fuels, TruAlt is building a comprehensive circular bioeconomy platform by transforming by-products into
 152  high-value outputs. The company produces Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) and Liquid Fermented Organic
 153  Manure (LFOM) to support sustainable agriculture and soil regeneration, while also capturing and utilising
 154  liquefied CO2 for industrial applications reinforcing its commitment to a zero-waste, future-ready energy
 155  ecosystem. For further information, visit: www.trualtbioenergy.com
