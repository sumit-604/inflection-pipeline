What truly sets us apart is our value chain integration. We do not operate as a job work foundry; 
instead we provide an end-to-end solution ecosystem. We start with co-engineering and design 
where we engage the OEM engineering teams at the concept stage, translating the customer's 
blueprint into optimized lightweight part geometrics.  
 
We have our in-house tooling and simulation software using internal tool design and mold flow 
simulation softwares. We manufacture high-durability dies in-house, cutting development lead 
time and maintaining full IP control. Additionally, we have advanced alloy castings utilized for 
LPDC and HPDC technology.  
We cast dense, defect-free aluminium alloys built to withstand extreme pressure and heat. On 
top of that, we also do micro-tolerance precision machining. Our multi-axis CNC machining 
center executes tight dimensional tolerance, finishing complex surfaces to exact client 
specification.  
 
We focus on quality assurance and testing. Our components undergo CMM inspection, 
spectroanalysis, radiographic testing and pressure leak testing before delivery as ready-to-use 
assembled units. We operate multiple units across a strategic industrial hub in Pune, ensuring 
high operational flexibility and proximity to keep the OEMs' assembly lines. 
Our current installed capacity stands at approximately 13,800 metric tons for melting and 6,000 
metric tons for casting and finishing, which is expected to increase to 28,800 metric tons and 
12,600 metric tons, respectively, by financial year '28, nearly doubling our overall capacity. 
Our strong technical execution has enabled us to build a long-standing relationship with the 
leading Tier 1 OEMs in India and globally while continuously expanding our customer base 
from 39 in FY24 to 58 in first half of FY27, reflecting our growing reach across customers and 
sectors. 
I would like to highlight that order book is our strength. As we speak today, we are backed by a 
robust order booking of almost INR1,180 crores, giving us strong revenue visibility over the 
coming financial year. 
A key cornerstone for our strategy is revenue de-risking through diversification. We have built 
a broad-base portfolio across automotive, non-automotive, and EV applications, catering to 
multiple industries and end market. 
In the automotive segment, we serve two-wheelers, three-wheelers, passenger vehicles, and 
commercial vehicle components, which includes intake manifolds, cylinder head, crankcases, 
and transmission housings. We are also expanding into EV applications, including motor 
housing and battery enclosures. 
Beyond automotive, our presence spans over power and energy distribution, agriculture, 
defence, heavy engineering, and healthcare, with products ranging from conductors, electrodes, 
insulators, switchgear housings, and precision diagnostic equipment components.
