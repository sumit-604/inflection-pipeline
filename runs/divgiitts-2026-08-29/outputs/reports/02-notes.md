## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Please reconcile the "~Rs 275 Cr net cash decline in FY26" figure against Note 10, which shows cash and bank balances rising from ≈Rs 284.8 Cr to ≈Rs 294.5 Cr over the year — what scope, period, or metric does the ~Rs 275 Cr figure refer to?
2. Why is Tejal Transmission Pvt Ltd, in which two non-executive directors (also cousins of the MD/ED) hold board seats, excluded from the Note 34 related-party list, and does the Company plan to restate the related-party disclosure for this omission?
3. Which of the three FY26 remuneration figures for the Managing Director is correct (Note 34: Rs 21.49 Mn; Corporate Governance/Annexure C: Rs 17.75 Mn), and which of the two stated YoY growth rates (73.6% per Note 34 vs 15% per Annexure C) is accurate, and on what base is each calculated?
4. What drove the Rs 7.65 Mn gratuity past service cost recognised in FY26 (nil in FY25), and separately, why did payments to suppliers made beyond the MSME appointed day more than double (Rs 97.28 Mn to Rs 196.09 Mn) in a year management describes as one of disciplined working capital management?
5. Is the BRSR's stated R&D expenditure of "₹117.94 crore" (p.105) a unit error for "₹117.94 million" as stated on the Business Driver page (p.42), and if so, can management confirm whether R&D expenditure as a percentage of revenue rose or fell in FY26 against the FY25 comparative?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management:** No direct evidence found in the core revenue, tax, or provisioning notes; effective tax rate ties to statutory rate almost exactly (Note 32B(b), p.235), and EPS shows no dilution engineering (Note 35, p.238). Not flagged.
- **Aggressive accounting:** Not identified; depreciation policy is conservative-to-neutral vs Schedule II (Note 2.2(e)), no revaluation gains booked (Note 4(a)), no capitalisation of routine opex identified. Not flagged.
- **Undisclosed risk indicators:** Flagged — Tejal Transmission related-party omission (Note 5/34); the AOC-2 vs Note 34 rent-income gap (~19.5x); the unnamed new Leave & License Agreement with the holding company; the rapidly growing, entirely unhedged FX book across three currencies (Note 41); the MSME payment-stretching pattern (Note 20(a)) running counter to the company's own working-capital narrative.
- **Disclosure-integrity gaps (a distinct category from the above, spanning statutory documents rather than Notes alone):** the three-way KMP remuneration mismatch (Note 34/Annexure C/Corp Gov Report); the current-ratio explanation contradicting the Balance Sheet (Note 42); the GST contingent-liability/CARO mismatch (Note 37/CARO); the R&D unit inconsistency (BRSR/Business Driver page). These recur across nearly every cross-document check performed in Pass 2 and Pass 3, forming a pattern worth naming as such rather than treating each as an isolated clerical slip.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices with concerning disclosure-integrity gaps. Key concern: recurring internal contradictions across related-party, remuneration, and cash disclosures that span multiple statutory documents in the same annual report. Key strength: clean revenue recognition, a functionally debt-free balance sheet, and fully provided, well-aged trade receivables. Overall accounting quality: 5/10.
