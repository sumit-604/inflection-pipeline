Company Overview (section header).

===== PAGE 5 =====
Company Information: SSWL is a leader in designing & manufacturing automotive wheels,
both steel and alloy. Aspires to be the Preferred Global Brand of Wheel Solution
provider.
Key Highlights: Founded 1991. Wheel Plants: 5. Operational Capacity ~25 million units
(Steel Wheel 20 million, Alloy Wheel 5.0 million, Aluminium Knuckles 0.5 million).
Employee Strength 7,500+.
Strategic Focus: Strategic Partnerships - Operations Excellence; Diversifying into
Aluminium Product Portfolio.
Financial Performance (6 Year CAGR): Revenue Growth ~20%; PAT ~27%.

===== PAGE 6 =====
Our Journey (timeline): 1991 first steel wheels commercial production at Dappar,
Punjab. 2002 2W & tractor wheels. 2006 LCV wheels. 2007 exports commence, PSA Peugeot
Citroen France & HCV wheels. 2008 Chennai plant. 2009 Renault Romania development,
Jamshedpur plant. 2010 Chennai truck wheels, PV alloy wheels at Mehsana (1.5mn
capacity), hot rolling mills at Saraikela Jharkhand (backward integration). 2017 alloy
capacity expanded to 3mn at Mehsana. 2021 acquisition of AMW Autocomponent Limited at
Bhuj, Gujarat. 2024 commenced commercialization of Aluminium Knuckles. 2025 alloy
wheels expanded to 5mn and aluminium knuckles to 5 lakh.

===== PAGE 7 =====
Strategic Partnerships. Tata Steel Limited: Strategic Alliance Agreement January 2008;
supply chain support, priority for new grade developments. Stake in SSWL: 6.9%.
Nippon Steel Corporation, Japan (formerly Sumitomo Metal Industries): Strategic
Alliance Agreement December 2010; international expertise in steel quality. Stake in
SSWL: 5.4%. Note: % stake as of 31st June 2026.

===== PAGE 8 =====
Our Products (Q1 FY27 revenue split):
Steel Wheels - Rs 953.8 crores (~63%), incl. exports and scraps. Robust, competitively
priced, flexible, quick maintenance.
Alloy Wheels - Rs 532.9 crores (~35%), incl. exports. Aesthetics, strength-to-weight
ratio, corrosion resistance, heat dissipation.
Aluminium Knuckles - Rs 23.0 crores (~2%). Weight reduction, improved fuel efficiency,
corrosion resistance.
