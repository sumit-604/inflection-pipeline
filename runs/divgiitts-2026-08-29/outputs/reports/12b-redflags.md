## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts alone)

Each item: anchor, then my read.

1. **Indonesia-vs-organic transfer-case split asked directly, not answered.** Q1 FY27 call, Sumit Ambekar (Parami): "if I strip away Indonesia, how much domestic transfer case we have done?" Jitendra Divgi: "I don't have the numbers off the top of my head," then pivots to total transfer-case revenue (Rs 75 cr, annualised Rs 300 cr). The single most important question for judging whether the Q1 FY27 beat is durable was not answered with a number.

2. **EV SOP slippage, three consecutive quarters, identical excuse.** April 2026 promised (Q3 FY26 call, Jitendra) → "next month or definitely by July" (Q4 FY26 call, Jitendra) → still not at SOP, PPAP "today," Sigma "progressing towards commercialization" (Q1 FY27 call, Jitendra). Cause each time: OEM (Tata) vehicle testing "took longer than expected."

3. **EV utilization target missed, not acknowledged.** Q3 FY26 (Jitendra, to Saania Jain): "capacity utilization by another 3 months should improve to about 40% to 60%" via Project Sigma (+3,000 units). Q4 FY26 (Jitendra, to Karan Gupta): "still around 25% capacity utilization... 25% to 30%." Prior miss not flagged as a miss.

4. **Automatic-transmission "conclude a contract by July 2026" milestone lapsed.** Stated by management itself, Q3 FY26 call (Jitendra): "we hope to conclude a contract by July 2026... I hasten to add that no contract has been concluded." By Q4 FY26 language reverted to "advanced discussions... completing due diligence"; by Q1 FY27 it is "announcements later this year," SOP H2 CY28. July-2026 milestone never confirmed or explained after it passed.

5. **No cash / net-cash figures in any of three calls** despite repeated "debt-free" and "healthy net cash position" assertions (Q4 FY26 call, Jitendra: "expects to maintain a healthy net cash position"). No analyst asked. Material given 85% YoY Q1 FY27 growth and the US/Shirwal capex commentary.

6. **Export share declining against target, plus an internal inconsistency.** 17% (9M FY26) → 18% (FY26) → 16% (Q1 FY27, "around 16% of the geographic revenue mix," Sudhir), moving away from the reiterated 20-25% medium-term target. Separately, Q4 FY26 call (Sudhir/Jitendra): "crossing 20% in Q2 FY '26" — a point-in-time claim inconsistent with the 17-18% blended and the declining Q1 FY27 print. Q1 FY27 decline not raised by management.

7. **Project Mayflower scope escalation.** Q4 FY26 call (Jitendra, to Mihir Vora): "This INR3 crores is just an initial setting up of offices, infrastructure, maybe a small warehouse." Q1 FY27 call (Jitendra, to Mahesh Bendre): "about $5 million in the first phase" (caveated "please don't hold me to this"). Roughly a 14x nominal step-up in ~2.5 months; commissioning "second half of Calendar '28, the earliest."

8. **Order-book quantification asked, deflected.** Q3 FY26 call, Karan Gupta (ACMIL), asked twice: "the total order book as on quarter 3 if you can tell us... Any quantification?" Jitendra gives qualitative colour, no Rs figure. Never volunteered in later calls.

9. **Persistent over-promotion.** All three calls: "historic milestone," "the mother of all markets," "historic crossroad," "leading the revolution in India to create India's first generation of genuine Tier-1 companies" (Q1 FY27), plus effusive praise of nearly every analyst ("thank you for your compliment"). Does not move a delivery-based grade, but the density is a tone signal.

10. **Revenue-potential escalation then softening.** "~Rs 1,500 cr" (prior-quarter framing) → "Rs 2,000 cr+" (Q4 FY26, to Kashish Shah) → reframed as "addressable potential," with Rs 1,000 cr as the near-term roadmap (Q1 FY27, to Darshil Jhaveri: "2,000 is like a potential in terms of the addressable market... first order of business is... the first 1,000").

11. **Volunteered negative — Mahindra 3-wheeler loss.** Q4 FY26 call (Jitendra): "Mahindra chose to keep the 3-wheeler production in-house... that opportunity did not realize for us." Disclosed proactively, own designs retained. Genuine candor data point.

12. **Indonesia "on hold" rebuttal veers into unverifiable claims.** Q4 FY26 call, Jai Prakash (Korman Capital) on reports the Indonesian government held the orders. Jitendra rebuts (tender timeline, Japanese/Chinese bidders not ready) but adds "Japanese propaganda" framing; Q1 FY27 adds "our intelligence says the Indonesian government seems to have saved upwards of $500 million." Assertions, not evidenced.

13. **FY25 results resubmission — not mentioned anywhere.** No restatement/resubmission referenced across any of the three calls (FY25 comparators used are internally consistent). Corpus gap for Halt 1 if a resubmission occurred outside the transcript record.

14. **Volunteered negative — Q1 FY27 margin peak is a one-off (B05 under-weighted).** Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin printed 29.4% (Sudhir). Management self-cautions, Q1 FY27 call (Jitendra, to Rushit Shukla): "this is like a one-off opportunity that has come, we should not get carried away by this... we still maintained an EBITDA of about 20%." To Darshil Jhaveri: "if I can sustain 20% to 22% and above... that is a very fair offering." Management is guiding margins back down ~700-900 bps from the printed 29.4%. Anyone extrapolating the record quarter's margin is misled. Thesis-relevant to the durability of the Q1 beat.

15. **Narrative reframing of the Indonesia order's durability across quarters (B05 partially caught).** Q3 FY26 call (Jitendra, to Karthi): "it is a specific contract. And obviously, it will conclude before the end of financial year FY '27"; "These are short-term contracts right now." Q1 FY27 call (Jitendra, opening): "we do not view the resulting revenue as entirely one-off. More than 50% of the business has the potential to become recurring in nature." The very order driving the beat shifts from "time-bound one-off" to ">50% recurring" with no reconciliation. B05 flagged the recurring claim as unproven but did not surface the direct contradiction with the earlier one-off characterisation.

16. **Volunteered negative — execution-bandwidth constraint and pushed-out orders (B05 partially caught).** Q1 FY27 call (Jitendra, opening): "the opportunity also therefore presents challenges in terms of execution bandwidth. And that is what continues to preoccupy us"; "Some other opportunities that would otherwise have entered the pipeline were pushed out temporarily because of the immediate execution load, both at our end and on capacities at the OEMs." An admission that Indonesia is crowding out other pipeline work — complicates the "all cylinders firing together" narrative. B05 noted FY28 spillover but not the bandwidth/pushed-out admission.

17. **Management fumbled its own component volume (B05 missed).** Q4 FY26 call, Karan Gupta / Jitendra / Sudhir exchange: "Over 1 million" → "5 lakhs-something?" → "3.3 million" → "7 lakhs? Over 7 lakhs" → "Over 13 lakhs." Management could not cleanly state its own component-parts volume. Minor specificity ding, not thesis-relevant.
