Press Release 
 
 
Jyoti Structures Limited Announces Robust Financial Performance for Q3 & Nine Months Ended 
FY2025-26 
Strong growth momentum continues across key financial metrics 
 
Mumbai, January 23, 2026: Jyoti Structures Limited (“JSL” or “the Company”), a leading 
infrastructure and power transmission engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company, 
today announced its financial results for the quarter and nine months ended on December 31, 2025. 
 
The Company delivered a strong operational and financial performance during the period, driven by 
disciplined execution, effective cost management, and steady progress across its order book. 
Performance during the quarter reflects continued momentum across core businesses.
