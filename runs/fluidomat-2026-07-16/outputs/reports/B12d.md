## 2. Findings

| # | Severity | Location in B06 | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | MAJOR | Claim 2, "Peer evidence" bullet 1 | B06 quotes: *"The EBIT margin declined to 18.2 percentage in Q2 FY '26 compared to 21.5 percentage in the same quarter last year..."* (ELECON, Q2 FY26, Narasimhan R.). The actual Q2 FY26 (Oct-2025) transcript states **19.2 percentage**, not 18.2 percentage: *"The EBIT margin declined to 19.2 percentage in Q2 FY '26 compared to 21.5 percentage in the same quarter last year"* (Narasimhan R., Q2 FY26 call). The figure "18.2 percentage" actually belongs to a **different quarter's different comparison** — Q3 FY26's Gear EBIT margin decline versus Q3 FY25 (27.8%): *"The EBIT margin declined to 18.2 percentage compared to 27.8 percentage in the same quarter last year"* (Narasimhan R., Q3 FY26 call). B06 has blended the "18.2%" figure from one quarter's comparison with the "21.5%" prior-year figure from a different quarter, presented as one verbatim Q2 FY26 quote. This is a fabricated/mismatched quote, not a real citation as presented. It does not flip the claim's overall direction (margin compression driven by mix/employee cost is independently well evidenced elsewhere), but it fails the "real, findable citation" bar for this specific quote. |
| F2 | MAJOR | Claim 1, "Peer evidence" bullet 1, final sentence | B06 quotes: *"steel is also now expanding their executions... Q4 FY26: 'the Gear Division... reported revenue of INR472 crores, reflecting a decline of 21% year-on-year. This was primarily due to delayed order inflows, extended dispatch timelines and customer-led deferment of delivery amid ongoing macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties.'"* attributed to "ELECON, Q4 FY26, Chintan Shah, CFO." The quote is real and accurate, but it was spoken by **Aayush Shah, Director**, in the Q4 FY26 opening overview (not by Chintan Shah, who spoke afterward on financial detail). Misattributing a substantive quote to the wrong management speaker/role is a real citation-accuracy defect. |
| F3 | MINOR | Claim 3, "Peer evidence" bullet 2 | The Q1 FY26 capex quote — *"capital expenditure budget for FY '26 to FY '28 is INR 400 crores"* — is attributed to "Kamlesh Shah" in B06's parenthetical list of quarterly restatements. The actual speaker of that specific line in the Q1 FY26 (Jul-2025) transcript is **Narasimhan Raghunathan (CFO)**, in the financial-highlights section, not Kamlesh Shah (Group CFO, who spoke the macro-overview section). Kamlesh Shah did separately discuss the INR400cr capex allocation elsewhere in the same call, so the substance is not invented, but the specific quoted sentence is misattributed. |
| F4 | MINOR | Claim 5, "Peer evidence" bullet 2 | B06 states international revenue share "had fallen further to 18% of consolidated revenue (from 21-24% in earlier FY26 quarters)." Checked against source: Q1 FY26 overseas share was actually **25%** (domestic 75% / overseas 25%, per Narasimhan Raghunathan, Q1 FY26), which falls outside the stated "21-24%" range. Q2 (21%) and Q3 (24%) are correctly within range. This is a minor numeric imprecision that does not change the overall declining-trend narrative. |
| F5 | MINOR | Part 2E, risk 5 ("First-of-a-kind defense-order margin drag") | Attributed to "Chintan Shah/Dipak Dalwadi, Q4 FY26 and Q3 FY26." In Q3 FY26, the analyst follow-up on the Navy order's margin impact (the "0.5 percentage" figure) was answered by **Narasimhan R.** (CFO at the time), not Dipak Dalwadi. Additionally, ELECON's own figure for this item is internally inconsistent across quarters (0.5% in Q3 FY26 vs. "1% or 2%" recalled in Q4 FY26) — B06 does not flag this self-contradiction, which would have been a genuine additional finding to surface. |
| F6 | MINOR | Part 2E, risk 1 ("Geopolitical/tariff pass-through risk") | The "50% US tariffs under 'Section 2(32)'" quote is attributed to "Aayush Shah, Director" but the transcript tags that specific answer as "Management" (unnamed speaker), in response to Raj Shah's Mexico-tariff question. Minor speaker-attribution imprecision. |
| F7 | MINOR | Claim 3, peer_coverage_map / Part 1 narrative | The Q3 FY26 EP/CP mix figure (48%/52%) is attributed to "Narasimhan R." but the transcript tags this specific line as "Management" (unnamed), not explicitly Narasimhan R., in response to Gaurav Nigam's question. |
| F8 | MINOR | unused_but_relevant | ELECON's Q2 FY26 (Oct-2025) call discloses a large, newly booked power-sector Gear order (~INR80cr, "one of the largest ever") and an active defense pipeline (P-17 Bravo ~INR1,000cr opportunity, NGMV, aircraft carrier follow-on), all directly relevant to Claim 1's "government-driven demand tailwind" question. B06's Claim 1 discussion draws on Q3/Q4 order-conversion evidence but does not cite this Q2 order-booking data point, which would have strengthened the "tailwind is real, but converts slowly/lumpily" argument with an additional concrete anchor. This is an industry-context miss, not claim-decisive (MINOR per rubric, since the broader tailwind point is not weakened by the omission). |

No CRITICAL findings: none of the above flip a claim's verdict, upgrade a claim from silence, or represent an invented/fabricated signal about Fluidomat itself.
