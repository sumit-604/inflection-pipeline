## AUDIT MANDATE & METHODOLOGY

This report audits the numerical accuracy of the stage pipeline outputs (B01-B09) against their claimed source anchors. The audit prioritizes by materiality: verdict-card figures first, then scorecard inputs, then table cells. Every identified discrepancy is marked with its severity, source location, claimed value, source truth, and source-fidelity status.

**Key rules applied:**
- Unit conversions (Rs Cr ↔ Rs Lakh) verified and documented
- Basis differences (consolidated vs standalone, pre-exceptional vs post-exceptional) distinguished from genuine misreads
- A ✓ MATCH is never a finding; a number faithfully transcribed from its source is verified clean
- ✗ MISMATCH, ⊘ ANCHOR NOT FOUND, and material ⊘ UNANCHORED are marked `source_fidelity: true`
