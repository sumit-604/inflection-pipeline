Press Release 
 
“Valiant Advanced Sciences Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary, commences 
commercial operations at its new state-of-the-art greenfield manufacturing facility in 
Saykha, Gujarat.” 
 
Valiant Advanced Sciences Private Limited, a wholly owned material subsidiary of Valiant 
Laboratories Limited, has commenced commercial operations at its new state-of-the-art 
greenfield manufacturing facility located at Saykha GIDC, Bharuch, Gujarat on 
November 07, 2025. The commissioning of this facility represents a major milestone in 
strengthening backward integration, diversifying product portfolio, and establishing a 
presence in the speciality chemicals industry. 
 
The plant specializes in the production of Acetic Anhydride, a key chemical under the 
Ketene and Diketene derivatives category, which caters to both domestic and 
international markets. The facility has been established with a substantial investment, 
funded in part through the proceeds of the Initial Public Offering (IPO) of Valiant 
Laboratories Limited, and is equipped with state-of-the-art manufacturing technology and 
a dedicated R&D setup to support future product development and innovation. 
 
About Valiant Laboratories Limited: 
 
Valiant Laboratories Limited is a specialised pharmaceutical company focused on 
manufacturing high-quality Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), with a strong 
presence in the Paracetamol API segment. Our reputation as a reliable and quality-driven 
supplier is built on our ability to respond to evolving global healthcare needs with 
consistency and precision. From humble beginnings in 1980 to becoming a prominent Indian 
manufacturer of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients, Valiant Laboratories has been defined 
by its ability to adapt, strengthen and progress. Specialising in Paracetamol API, we have 
built a legacy of trust, quality and innovation that continues to guide us through evolving 
market landscapes.
