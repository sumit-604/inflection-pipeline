## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (fresh read, anchored)

**RF-1 — P&K Fertilisers Q1 FY27 EBIT collapse.** Segment result ₹504 lakh (₹50mn) vs
₹1,300 lakh (₹130mn) Q1 FY26 = **-61%/-62%**, on segment revenue roughly flat
(₹13,548 lakh vs ₹13,911 lakh, -3%). Segment margin fell 9.3% → 3.7%. The only narrative
offered ("higher input costs partially offset through price increases," Q1DECK slide 17) does
not account for the magnitude. Anchor: Q1R consolidated segment table (June'26 vs June'25);
Q1DECK slide 21.

**RF-2 — Agri off-season inventory liquidation promise missed; working capital elevated with
no replacement date.** Q1DECK slide 17: "Demand remained subdued… weak and uneven monsoon,"
"Working capital deployment remained high," outlook "Working capital deployment is expected to
remain high," "Focus on liquidation of stock in key operating regions." The liquidation target
is deferred to an indefinite horizon. Anchor: Q1DECK slide 17.

**RF-3 — Consolidated cash-conversion / receivables deterioration (broader than Agri).**
FY26 trade receivables rose ₹2,875mn → ₹4,088mn = **+42%** while revenue grew +21%; cash and
cash equivalents essentially flat (₹59mn → ₹57mn); inventories ₹1,905mn → ₹2,147mn. Debtors
turnover fell 5.66 → 5.28 (AR p.20). Q1 FY27 shows a further inventory build: change in
inventories of finished goods/WIP/stock-in-trade of **(₹6,069 lakh)** in a single quarter,
with cost of materials consumed +40% YoY vs revenue +18%, and segment assets ballooning
(consol. segment assets ₹86,734 lakh June'25 → ₹1,09,655 lakh June'26). Receivables growing
at 2x the revenue rate is an earnings-quality / cash-conversion signal that goes beyond the
narrow Agri-inventory framing. Anchor: Q1DECK slide 24 (historical balance sheet); AR p.20;
Q1R P&L (change-in-inventories line); Q1R consolidated segment-assets table.

**RF-4 — Profitability compression despite strong topline.** Q1 FY27 consolidated: revenue
+18% but EBITDA +7%, PBT +6%, PAT +4%; EBITDA margin -135bps (14.35% → 13.00%), PAT margin
-117bps. FY26 full year: the core Performance Polymers & Chemicals segment grew revenue ~+28%
(₹9,704mn → ₹12,386mn gross) but segment EBIT was **flat** (₹1,650mn → ₹1,663mn, +0.8%),
i.e. blended PPC margin ~17% → ~13% — negative operating leverage in the largest segment,
masked at group level by the strong Adhesives sub-line. Anchor: Q1DECK slides 19-20; AR p.19
segment results.

**RF-5 — No FY27 numeric revenue/EBITDA guidance anywhere.** All AR outlook language is
qualitative ("moderate and steady growth," "positive," "constructive"); the deck carries no
guidance range despite a format that could. Anchor: AR pp.17,25,26,28; Q1DECK (no guidance
slide).

**RF-6 — Nil dividend for FY26 despite +49% PBT-before-exceptional and EPS ~₹84.** Anchor: AR
p.41 Board's Report item 3; PBT growth AR p.19.

**RF-7 — Export / geopolitical volume headwind in PPC.** "Export volumes impacted by
geopolitical disruptions and logistics challenges in key international markets" (Q1DECK slide
9); Red Sea rerouting raising freight/transit (AR p.31). Exports are ~15% of revenue. Anchor:
Q1DECK slide 9; AR p.31.

**RF-8 — Latex demand subdued + excess industry capacity + competitive margin pressure.**
"Demand across global [latex] markets remained subdued during the year, resulting in lower
business volumes… margins remained under pressure due to increased competition and excess
industry capacity" (AR p.26). Structural concern for a segment where JACPL is #2 globally.
Anchor: AR p.26.

**RF-9 (minor disclosure hygiene) — Unexplained exceptional item + an AR/results date
inconsistency.** (a) FY26 carries an exceptional item of ₹34.65mn (₹347 lakh) that is nowhere
narratively explained (FY24 also had a ₹335mn exceptional). (b) AR p.5 ("Demerger Journey")
states the NCLT First Motion order is dated **August 08, 2026**, whereas the primary Q1 results
notes (standalone + consolidated, filed 11-Aug-2026) and Q1DECK slide 26 both state **July 08,
2026** — a self-inconsistent date in the company's own disclosures (the AR value coincides with
its own publication date, so it is most likely a typo for July 08). Anchor: AR p.5 vs Q1R
note 3 vs Q1DECK slide 26; exceptional-item lines AR p.40 / Q1R P&L.
