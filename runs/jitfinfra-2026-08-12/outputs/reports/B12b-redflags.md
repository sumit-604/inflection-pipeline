### The one MISS (MAJOR)

**Trade-receivables surge / cash-conversion risk is absent from B05's "what they are NOT
saying."** Consolidated current trade receivables **₹1,27,107.48 L (₹1,271 cr)** at 31 Mar 2026
vs ₹83,748.37 L (₹837 cr) a year earlier — **+52%**, more than double the 24% revenue growth —
plus non-current trade receivables ₹14,656.79 L and "other financial assets" (current)
₹59,223.36 L (₹592 cr, largely unbilled/retention). The consolidated cash flow confirms the
drain: "(Increase) in Trade Receivable **₹(43,359.14) L**" (~₹434 cr) for the year. This sits
directly against management's own stated Key Priority to "strengthen operating cash
generation, tighten working capital" (B05 Trigger table), and a peer analyst on the EIEL
Aug-2025 call (Sandip Sabharwal) volunteered exactly this risk for state-government/JJM
contractors ("companies which rely largely on state government projects get into a receivable
issue at some stage"). B05 surfaced net worth and borrowings but not the receivables build —
a thesis-relevant omission given cash conversion is load-bearing to the verdict.
(Caveat: this may be captured by the forensic/notes stages B02/B03, which are outside my
inputs; the finding is scoped to B05's self-assigned 2D "financial-health narrative" audit.)
