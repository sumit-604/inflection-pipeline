Reported (Ind AS 116) EBITDA and cash EBITDA are kept apart throughout: reported
EBITDA is pre-rent (lease rental is a below-the-line ROU/interest item under
Ind AS 116); cash EBITDA, introduced as a metric only from the Q1 FY27 call,
deducts actual cash lease rentals paid and ESOP expense. Normalized EBITDA
(the pre-Q1 FY27 metric, adjusted for Ind AS 116/109/102) sits between the two
and is a third, related but distinct, number.

### 1C. Trigger evolution across the three primary quarters (Q3 FY26 → Q4 FY26 → Q1 FY27)

- **GCC demand**: strengthening every quarter. 80+ clients / 21% of space revenue
  (Q3 FY26 call, p.5, p.7) → 100+ clients / 23% of rental revenue, 14 mandates
  closed FY26 (Q4 FY26 call, p.9) → 100+ clients / 24% of rental revenue
  (Q1 FY27 call, p.4). Consistent, quantified, unchanged direction.
- **Premiumization**: strengthening and hardening from "strategy" to
  "non-negotiable." 32 Gold+Elite centers (Q3 FY26 call, p.6) → 35 centers,
  described as "default at Awfis, not a strategy overlay" (Q4 FY26 call, p.4)
  → 37 centers, realization premium quantified at 30-50% for the newest
  cohort (Q1 FY27 call, p.4).
- **Managed Aggregation ratio**: WEAKENING / narrative shift, flagged. Q2 FY26
  call: "no change in strategy," maintain ~65:35 (p.7-8). Q3 FY26 call: MA
  share down to ~62% of portfolio (Vikrant Kashyap Q&A, p.13), attributed to
  Elite/GCC straight-lease additions. Q4 FY26 call: management commits again
  to "maintain the ratio in the 60-40 kind of a range" (p.15). Q1 FY27 call:
  MA share actually 57%, and management explicitly abandons the fixed-ratio
  framing — "we are not fixated on the MA split versus the SL split...this is
  not a shift from MA...it is broadening of the playbook" (Amit Ramani, p.11).
  Three different stated positions in three quarters on the same metric.
- **Union Budget 2026 tax measures (MNC tax holiday, transfer pricing norms)**:
  DROPPED. Cited at length as a fresh tailwind in the Q3 FY26 call (Amit
  Ramani, p.5) and never mentioned again in the Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27 calls.
- **Frame by Awfis (furniture)**: DROPPED / timeline slipping. Q2 FY26 call:
  "next 3 to 6 months you will see us growing that segment" (Amit Ramani,
  p.11). Q3 FY26 call: "we have not made any investment... will in the fourth
  quarter be able to give a more clear guidance" (Ravi Dugar, p.17-18). Q4
  FY26 call: foundations built, contract-manufacturing set up across 5+
  partners, "by H2 we expect to have 2-3 large external mandates closed"
  (Amit Ramani, p.6). Q1 FY27 call: no mention at all — not in opening
  remarks, not in Q&A. A trigger built up in specificity across three
  quarters and then vanished with no revenue figure ever disclosed.
- **Occupancy build-up**: promised improving every quarter, delivered flat.
  Q3 FY26 call: blended 75%, promise of "100 to 150 basis points" mature-
  cohort improvement "in the next one or two quarters" (Sumit Lakhani, p.9).
  Q4 FY26 call: blended 76% (+100bps, partial delivery), mature cohort flat
  at 84% (promise not met, unacknowledged as a miss). Q1 FY27 call: blended
  flat at 76% for a third straight data point, mature cohort DOWN to 83%,
  attributed to a one-off 3,000-seat client exit (Amit Ramani, p.8).
- **Capex guidance**: whipsawed then converged. Original guide (implied
  Rs180-200cr per prior-quarter references) → revised UP to ~Rs220cr (Q2
  FY26 call, p.13) → restated back DOWN to Rs200-210cr (Q3 FY26 call, p.11)
  → delivered Rs208cr (Q4 FY26 call, p.11), inside the Q3-restated band but
  below the Q2 revision.
- **FY27 revenue guidance**: timeline/number slippage within a single
  quarter. Set at coworking 25-27% / Transform 22-25% / total ~25-27% in the
  Q4 FY26 call (p.16, itself a transcript-corrected figure — see 2C). Cut to
  coworking 23-25% / Transform ~20% in the very next call, Q1 FY27 (p.6),
  with no acknowledgment that the range had moved.

Pattern check: management credits itself for strategic discipline when volume
guides are cut, and is largely silent (rather than either blaming externals or
admitting fault) on guidance-band changes that were not volume metrics
(FY27 revenue range, MA:SL ratio). The one clear exception — proactive,
specific disclosure of the Q1 FY27 client exit before any analyst asked — is
real and should be weighed against the pattern, not folded into it.

  asked about on the Q1 FY27 call (13-Aug-2026), despite falling squarely
  inside the review window and one predating the FY26 accounts' board

  proactively surface on an earnings call, and no analyst raised it.
- **The Design & Build business transfer** to wholly owned subsidiary Awfis
  Transform Private Limited (slump sale Rs265.91mn, consideration pending
  revised valuation, per B02-notes) was approved by the board the same day
  as the Q4 FY26 call (25-May-2026) but is not substantively addressed in
  that call; Transform is discussed only as a strategic growth story, never
  as a related-party transfer with an unresolved price. Likely reason: legal
  filing mechanics kept separate from IR narrative.

  "ROCE of 55%," p.4) without ever stating the formula. Per B03-ardeep, the
  FY26 Annual Report's own Schedule III-mandated Note 41 ROCE is 11.87%,

- **Why Ravi Dugar (CFO) left** is never explained beyond thanking him "for

- **Top-10 customer receivable concentration** jumping from 42.74% to

- **Post-lease-service free cash flow**, which per B03-ardeep runs close to

## SECTION 3: COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE FROM CONCALLS

### 3A. Competitor commentary

Management makes almost no named-competitor comparisons across the three
calls. The only self-positioning claims are general: "Awfis is the only
large-scale player in India with a strong presence in small cohort co-working
segments" (Q3 FY26 call, Amit Ramani, p.5-6), and an implicit contrast with
"most of the peers" who "take very large spaces... their model is more around
straight leases" versus Awfis's smaller, diversified centers (Q3 FY26 call,
Sumit Lakhani, p.13, answering the seat-addition-shortfall question). No
credibility check is possible from these transcripts alone; both claims are
unverifiable self-description and are named in 4B for peer cross-check.

### 3B. Industry and market intelligence dropped in the calls

- CY2025 record office leasing: 82 / 82.6 million sq ft (Q3 FY26 call p.4;
  Q4 FY26 call p.3), consistent across calls.
- Flex penetration of total leasing: "roughly one-sixth... approaching
  almost one-fifth near-term" (Q3 FY26 call, p.5) → "21%... expect 25% by
  2027" (Q4 FY26 call, p.3) → "21%... clear path towards roughly 25% by
  2027" (Q1 FY27 call, p.14). Consistent progression.
- GCC share of office absorption: "~40% of total office absorption in

  in H1 2026 (Q1 FY27 call, p.3).
