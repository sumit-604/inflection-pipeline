## 3. THE CATALYST STORY

**Growth (Transition 1).** Two things keep growth going.
- **Partner money.** The parent's partner pools grew about 31% between May and August 2026, by the company's own DLG disclosure. Claude web verified that page live on 03-Oct-2026.
- **Capital.** The IPO put Rs 636.8 Cr into Si Creva's lending by June, with no deviation (monitoring agency report, 29-Jul-2026, p.5). A further Rs 832 Cr preferential issue goes to shareholders on 14-Oct-2026 (board outcome, 17-Sep-2026).
- **What would kill it.** The top two partners hold about 95% of the parent's guaranteed pools. Their agreements are long term, non exclusive and terminable by the partner on notice (RHP p.27). One leaving would cut the partner half sharply.
- **What confirms it.** Pools that keep growing in the monthly DLG tables, and the EGM approving the raise.

**Spread (Transition 2).** Nothing pushes the spread up. Management is cutting price on purpose to win lower-risk borrowers.
- **The cut.** Total income fell from 41.1% to 35.9% of average AUM in a year (Q1 deck p.41).
- **The offset.** Funding cost is falling. The CRISIL rating moved to A- in February 2026 (CRISIL, 13-Feb-2026), and new bond coupons are lower. But debt is only about a quarter of AUM, so the offset is small.
- **What would revive the spread.** A pause in price cuts, or funding cost falling faster than yields. What would confirm the decline: total income below 34% of average AUM in Q2 FY27.

**Asset quality (Transition 3).** Two pieces of evidence point up, and the latest quarter points down.
- **Pointing up.**
  - First-EMI default at 90 days fell from 6.83% to 0.83% between Jun-2023 and Dec-2025 (RHP p.194).
  - Impairment cost fell from 8.8% of average AUM in Q1 FY26 to 6.8% in Q1 FY27, and in each of the last two quarters: 8.5% in Q3 FY26, 7.0% in Q4 FY26, 6.8% in Q1 FY27 (Q4 deck p.42, Q1 deck p.41). The Q2 FY26 figure is NOT FOUND in the corpus.
- **Pointing down.**
  - Stage 2 rose in Q1 FY27 while SBI Cards and Poonawalla improved in the same quarter (Verifier B, P1).
  - Q4 FY26 collection outsourcing ran at nearly twice its run rate (extraction annex 4).
  - The partner half's losses are invisible quarter to quarter. Partners recover from Kissht within 30 days of 90 days overdue (Piramal Q1 FY27 call p.16), so the loss shows up in Kissht's operating expenses, not in anyone's NPA.
- **What would confirm improvement.** Stage 2 back at or below 2.4% in Q2 FY27, with impairment cost at or below 6.8%. What would kill it: Stage 2 above 3.15% again.

**Returns (Transition 4).** Return on assets is high and steady. Return on equity is falling for a mechanical reason: two equity raises in six months. The only thing that lifts RoE back is rebuilding leverage, which takes years. What would confirm a turn: RoAE at or above 20% on the enlarged equity in H2 FY27.
