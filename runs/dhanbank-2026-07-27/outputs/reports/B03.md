### Top 3 Strengths
1. **Capital position materially and durably strengthened** — CRAR 12.71%→16.12%→18.92% (FY24→FY25→FY26), CET1 11.36%→15.24%, leverage ratio 5.14%→6.76%, via a fully-subscribed (1.64x oversubscribed) rights issue that directly addressed the legacy-loss CET1 deduction (Note 1.1/1.2; Pillar III DF-11).

### Top 3 Red Flags
1. **Headline asset-quality improvement is a denominator effect, not a durable trend — and FY26 data shows early signs of reversal.** Fresh slippage flat YoY in FY25, technical write-off pool ~1.9x headline GNPA with halving recovery rate, restructured book +47% YoY concentrated in MSME sub-standard accounts; per the independent March-2026 ICRA rating note, PCR (incl. write-offs) fell from 76.30% to 53.63% and NNPA rose from 0.86% to 1.11% between December 2024 and December 2025 — the exact pattern B02's Pass 3 flagged as a risk is now visible in subsequent-period data.
2. **FY25 operating cash flow turned negative (₹(4.79) Cr) despite rising PAT**, driven by advances outgrowing deposits and funded partly by drawing down the Bank's own interbank liquidity buffer, in the same year the Bank needed a ~56%-dilutive capital raise — multiple funding levers pulled simultaneously in one year (reversed in FY26, but real for the year this AR covers).
3. **Rapid, geopolitically-exposed concentration into gold loans as the primary growth lever** (31% of loan book at FY25-end → 38% by December 2025, per ICRA — not disclosed at all in the Bank's own FY25 AR), against a Kerala-concentrated deposit and borrower base with high Gulf-remittance linkages that ICRA explicitly flags as exposed to Gulf-region geopolitical risk; combined with top-20-depositor concentration of 24.5% of total deposits and top-20-exposure concentration at 147% of Tier I capital.
