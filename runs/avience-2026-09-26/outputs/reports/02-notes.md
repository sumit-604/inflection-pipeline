## PART B: PRIORITY OPEN ITEM RESOLVED - NOTE 40 PROFIT-SPLIT LABELS

**Question carried from Pass 2:** are the Note 40 consolidated "Holding
company" (24%, Rs.205.91 lakh) / "Subsidiary" (76%, Rs.669.38 lakh) FY26
labels swapped, given Rs.669.38 lakh sits within Rs.3.95 lakh of the
parent's own standalone PAT (Rs.673.33 lakh, Note 4) and Rs.205.91 lakh
sits within Rs.1.9 lakh of the subsidiary's own AOC-1 PAT (Rs.203.9966
lakh)?

**Test (i): FY26 standalone PAT in the audited standalone results vs
consolidated PAT.**
- Standalone (parent-only) audited results, year ended 31-Mar-2026: Profit
  for the year Rs.673.33 lakh (AVIENBIO audited standalone results filing,
  PDF p.9, line "Profit for the period/year," column FY26; matches AR Note
  4 standalone exactly).
- Consolidated audited results, same period: Profit for the year after
  minority interest Rs.875.28 lakh (AVIENBIO audited consolidated results
  filing, PDF p.17; identical Rs.875.28 lakh in the 25-Aug-2026 Revised CFS
  filing, PDF p.11 - the revision did not touch this P&L line, consistent
  with B00's note that the elimination error had no profit/equity impact).
- Holding company's share of the CONSOLIDATED total, computed directly:
  673.33 / 875.28 = 76.9%. Residual (= subsidiary's net contribution after
  intra-group elimination) = 875.28 - 673.33 = 201.95 lakh = 23.1%.
- This computed 76.9% / 23.1% split matches the Note 40 table's PRINTED
  PERCENTAGES (76% / 24%) almost exactly, but only if the LABELS are
  swapped: the 76% figure belongs to the Holding company, not the
  Subsidiary as printed, and the 24% figure belongs to the Subsidiary, not
  the Holding company as printed. The rupee amounts confirm this
  independently: Rs.669.38 lakh (labelled "Subsidiary") is 0.6% away from
  the Holding company's own Rs.673.33 lakh standalone PAT; Rs.205.91 lakh
  (labelled "Holding company") is 0.9% away from the Subsidiary's own
  Rs.203.9966 lakh AOC-1 PAT.

**Test (ii): RHP restated standalone vs restated consolidated PAT and
revenue, FY24/FY25/10M-FY26.**
RHP KPI table (RHP PDF p.136 and, identically, p.165; "Basis for Issue
Price" and "Our Business" sections), amounts in Rs lakh:

| Period | Consolidated PAT | Standalone PAT | Standalone share of consol. | Subsidiary net contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY24 | 214.11 | 198.38 | 92.7% | 15.73 (7.3%) |
| FY25 | 723.18 | 513.50 | 71.0% | 209.68 (29.0%) |
| 10M FY26 (Apr'25-Jan'26) | 573.96 | 418.08 | 72.8% | 155.88 (27.2%) |

(RHP standalone FY25 revenue Rs.2,888.95 lakh, RHP PDF pp.136/165, matches
the AR's own standalone FY25 revenue figure exactly, Note 22 standalone -
confirming the RHP and AR datasets are drawn from the same underlying
standalone books and are safe to compare.)

- FY24's low subsidiary contribution (7.3%) is explained by the Aug-2023
  acquisition date: only about 8 months of DR Meditech is in the FY24
  consolidated numbers, so a small subsidiary contribution is expected and
  is NOT evidence for or against the swap.
- FY25 and 10M-FY26 both show the HOLDING COMPANY as the large majority
  contributor to consolidated profit (71.0% and 72.8% respectively), not
  the minority contributor the printed FY26 Note 40 labels would imply
  (24%) and not even close to the printed FY25 labels (Holding 9%). This
  independent, externally-drawn (RHP restated financials, not the AR)
  dataset corroborates the SAME direction as Test (i): across three
  different data sources (AR Note 4, AR results filings, RHP restated
  KPIs), the Holding company is consistently the majority profit
  contributor to the group, not the 24%/9% minority the AR's Note 40 as
  printed would have an investor believe.

**Verdict on the priority item: RESOLVED FOR FY26, HIGH CONFIDENCE. The
Note 40 (consolidated, p.143-144) FY26 "Holding company" / "Subsidiary"
labels ARE SWAPPED.** The correct reading is: Holding company (the listed
parent, whose YEIDA plant ramp is the growth narrative, LBF1) generated
approximately 76-77% of FY26 consolidated profit (~Rs.669-673 lakh); the
subsidiary DR Meditech generated approximately 23-24% (~Rs.204-206 lakh).
This INVERTS Pass 1's Red Flag #1 as originally stated: the profit engine
in FY26 IS where the equity story is framed (the parent), not the
subsidiary. Pass 1's Red Flag #1 is hereby RETIRED for FY26 and replaced
by this corrected, resolved reading in the consolidated findings table
below.

**FY25 status: swap direction supported, exact figures UNRESOLVED.** The
printed FY25 Note 40 labels (Holding 9%/Rs.67.14 lakh; Subsidiary 91%/
Rs.642.76 lakh) sum correctly to the FY25 consolidated PAT (Rs.709.90
lakh, results filing PDF p.17/p.11), so the ERROR (if any) is confined to
which label sits against which number, consistent with the FY26 pattern.
The AR's own standalone FY25 PAT (Rs.500.21 lakh, Note 4/results filing
PDF p.9) is closer to the swapped "Holding" figure (Rs.642.76 lakh, gap
Rs.142.55 lakh / 28.5%) than to the printed "Holding" figure (Rs.67.14
lakh, gap Rs.433.07 lakh / 86.5%), and the RHP-derived FY25 holding share
(71.0%, Test ii) is also far closer to a ~91% swapped reading than to a 9%
printed reading - but neither AR nor RHP FY25 evidence reconciles to the
swapped Rs.642.76 lakh figure as tightly as FY26 resolved. This residual
gap (~Rs.140-240 lakh depending on which comparator is used) is most
plausibly a consolidation adjustment specific to FY25 as the FIRST full
year of DR Meditech's consolidation (e.g., unrealised intra-group profit
elimination, goodwill-related entries, or a one-off subsidiary result not
visible without FY25's own subsidiary standalone financials). **What would
settle it: the subsidiary DR Meditech's own FY25 standalone P&L (would sit
in the FY25 Annual Report's AOC-1, or the subsidiary's own FY25 audited
financials) - not in this corpus.** Recommend Role 5.5 obtain this
specific document; until then, treat FY25's split direction as
"Holding-majority, swap-consistent" but not numerically anchored.

**Consequence for the run:** Pass 1's Red Flag #1 ("subsidiary drives
profit, listed entity is a minority contributor") does NOT hold for FY26
on the corrected reading, and is directionally contradicted for FY25 too
(Holding company likely the majority contributor both years, based on two
independent data sources). The correct structural read is closer to: the
subsidiary's PROFIT CONTRIBUTION has been shrinking as a share of the
group (very roughly ~29% FY25 to ~23-24% FY26 on the corrected reading),
while its NET ASSET share has also shrunk (28% FY25 to 21% FY26, Note 40 -
this side of the table is NOT swapped, per Pass 2's own check that net
assets don't swap cleanly either way, consistent with net assets not being
subject to the same labelling slip as the profit row). This is a
materially different, and materially LESS alarming, structural finding
than Pass 1 originally reported. It should still be flagged (a drafting
error of this kind in a listed company's statutory notes is itself a
disclosure-quality data point, see accounting quality score below), but
the FLAG-STRUCTURE severity moves from "the equity story is not where the
profit is" to "Note 40 has a probable labelling error that a careless
reader would misinterpret as a serious structural problem, and momentarily
did in Pass 1 of this very pipeline."
