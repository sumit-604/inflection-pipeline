## LOAD-BEARING FACTS: CHECKED BEFORE THE STAGE'S OWN WORK

### Fact 1. Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin 15.6% vs FY27 guidance 13.8-14.3%; the Rs 2.9 Cr R&D credit
- FACT. Reported Q2 EBITDA Rs 26.6 Cr, margin 15.6% on revenue from operations Rs 170.2 Cr (PRES p.9; K2 p.3).
- FACT. The credit is Rs 292.60 lakhs, "net of professional fees", for "finalization and true-up" of R&D expenditure credits (R2 note, consolidated p.4 and standalone p.8 of the results PDF; text search hit at the note on the R&D credit). Management: it relates to "the last 12 months of FY26", approval came in September, it is booked "net of employee expenses" (K2 p.6, p.8).
- My arithmetic, margin ex-credit: 26.6 less 2.93 = 23.67 Cr; 23.67 / 170.2 = 13.9% (deck margin basis is revenue from operations: 26.6/170.2 = 15.6%, 19.9/139.0 = 14.3%, PRES p.9). Q2 FY26 was 14.3%. So ex-credit Q2 margin is about 40 bps BELOW the year-ago quarter, and 160 bps above Q1 FY27 (12.3%, PRES p.9). H1 FY27 ex-credit: (44.9 - 2.93) / 319.6 = 13.1% vs H1 FY26 13.6% (PRES p.10), about 50 bps lower.
- My arithmetic, employee cost / revenue from operations: Q2 FY27 reported 118.1/170.2 = 69.4%; ex-credit (118.1 + 2.93)/170.2 = 71.1%; Q2 FY26 97.7/139.0 = 70.3%; Q1 FY27 109.3/149.4 = 73.2% (PRES p.9). The sequential gain is real (73.2% to 71.1% ex-credit). The year-on-year gain exists only because of the credit.
- Management attributes the margin to "higher utilization for our offshore engineers, improved leverage of offshore" (K2 p.6). Filed mix does not show that: offshore share of revenue was 69% in Q2 FY27, 68% in Q1 FY27 and 72% in Q2 FY26 (PRES p.11). Offshore fell year on year. The offshore claim is not supported by the offshore share.
- Open: whether the FY27 guidance 13.8-14.3% includes the credit. NOT FOUND in any call (K2 p.4, p.11). Jigar said only "we feel comfortable giving a revised guidance for the whole year" (K2 p.11). My arithmetic on the guide (basis of the revenue guide also NOT FOUND; revenue from operations used, FY26 Rs 543.85 Cr from R2 54,385.24 lakhs): H2 EBITDA margin needed is 13.5% to 14.6% if the guide counts the credit; 14.5% to 15.5% if the guide excludes it. Q2 ex-credit is 13.9%. The two readings differ by about 100 bps in H2. The observation that separates them: the Q3 FY27 results note and Jigar's answer to "does 13.8-14.3% include the credit".
- Quality of the explanation. Asked how it will be accounted, Jigar said "It happens once a year. It's usually about 10% to 15% of your revenue" (K2 p.8). The credit is Rs 2.93 Cr on revenue of Rs 170.2 Cr (1.7%), so the 10-15% statement cannot be a share of revenue. It may refer to the share of R&D spend. Not clarified. Treat as a garbled answer, not a data point.
- Verdict for fact 1: margin expansion is partly real (sequential), partly credit (year on year), and the offshore-mix explanation fails against the deck.

### Fact 2. Guidance history vs delivery
- FY26 (record from earlier calls). K3 p.4: "double-digit revenue growth and double-digit EBITDA growth" for FY26 and FY27; 11-12% EBITDA goal for FY26, "12% is what we are budgeting for, and that's sustainable" (K3 p.7). K3 p.4: 9M revenue Rs 411.8 Cr, +11.7%, 9M EBITDA margin 13.9%. Delivery: FY26 revenue Rs 550 Cr, +10.5%, EBITDA Rs 71.9 Cr, +60.9%, margin 13.2% (K4 p.3). FY26 revenue growth landed at the 10% floor of "double digit". Q4 FY26 growth was 6.9% year on year and Q4 margin fell to 11.2% (K4 p.4). Asked about the Q4 margin drop Jigar said "Nothing in particular" (K4 p.9).
- FY27 guidance path. Q4 FY26: "sustained double-digit revenue and EBITDA growth" (K4 p.5). Q1 FY27: "robust double-digit revenue growth and double-digit EBITDA growth" (K1 p.6). Q2 FY27: first numeric guide, revenue +15% to +18%, EBITDA margin 13.8% to 14.3% (K2 p.4), described as "a first for Onward Technologies" (K2 p.13). A "10-12%" revenue range was alleged to have been given at an Arihant Capital conference in March 2026; Jigar denied it: "double-digit means 10% plus" (K4 p.15). The conference notes are not in the corpus. NOT FOUND.
- H1 FY27 delivery: revenue from operations Rs 319.6 Cr, +17.5%; EBITDA Rs 44.9 Cr, +21.4% (PRES p.10). H1 already sits inside the new range for growth.
- My arithmetic on the guide. Guidance 15-18% on FY26 Rs 543.85 Cr gives Rs 625.4 to 641.7 Cr. H2 implied = Rs 305.8 to 322.1 Cr vs H1 Rs 319.6 Cr, so H2 equals or falls 4% below H1. At Q2's Rs 170.2 Cr run-rate, H2 would be Rs 340.4 Cr; the guide implies 5% to 10% below that. FY26 H2 was also flat on H1 (H1 FY26 Rs 275.7 Cr per PRES p.10; FY26 Rs 550.9 Cr per PRES p.5 text). So the guide repeats last year's H2-flat shape.
- Management reasons for restraint: Q3 hiring slows for holidays (K2 p.7); the guide is "post the new regulatory environment" for U.S. work permits (K2 p.5); "we obviously prefer to remain prudent" (K2 p.7); "there could be some element of surprise" (K2 p.13). The one observation that separates "conservative guide" from "real H2 slowdown": Q3 FY27 revenue vs Rs 170.2 Cr.
- Verdict for fact 2: headline guidance has been met or beaten in the trailing four quarters. The weakness is basis ambiguity, not outcome.

Unbilled: Jigar said in the same answer that "all that has now been rectified" and that "we will see in Q3 and Q4, things getting normalized again" (K2 p.5): one answer, two tenses.
- Cash conversion. R2 consolidated cash flow (INR lakhs): H1 FY27 operating cash flow 1,844.79 vs H1 FY26 3,502.46 (about -47%); trade and unbilled receivables rose 2,867.01 vs 542.66; (my arithmetic) CFO / EBITDA 1,844.79 lakhs / 4,490 lakhs (deck EBITDA Rs 44.9 Cr) = 41% vs 3,502.46 / 3,700 = 95% a year earlier. Buyback outflow 1,862.86 lakhs, dividend 1,745.39 lakhs, PPE purchase 912.90 lakhs (R2 consolidated cash flow statement). Analysts raised only unbilled and DSO (K2 p.5, p.13).
- Verdict for fact 3: concentration is stable in the deck but the key single-customer figure is silent; receivables are the weakest delivery line across the four quarters.
