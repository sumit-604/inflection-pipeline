Verdict selection: Rule 3 fired. FLAG-CASH is active with FLAG-PROMOTER not active. ## Active flag blocks

### FLAG-PROMOTER: NOT ACTIVE
B08 verdict: TRUSTWORTHY (scorecard 8 clean / 2 caution / 0 red). No identifiable promoter, SEBI ICDR confirmed, private equity and venture backed. Founder MD Sanjay Sharma and the reconstituted board carry clean, regulator heavy records.
Transition evidence: external professional CFO Gaurav Seth appointed 28-Apr-2026 (ex CFO IIFL Home Finance, ex CFO Airtel Payments Bank); 2023 to 2024 board independence rebuild (ex NABARD Chairman as Board Chairperson, ex RBI Chief General Manager, ex MD PNB Housing); auditor upgrade to MSKA & Associates LLP (BDO network); India Ratings upgrade to IND A plus, 24-Jun-2026; all institutional SHA nominee director rights auto terminated on listing (B08, Prospectus p.303).

### FLAG-CASH: ACTIVE. Determination: STRUCTURAL, with a residual INDETERMINATE element
The CFO signal is STRUCTURAL. Cash flow from operations is negative every period (FY23 minus Rs 720.4 Cr through H1 FY26 minus Rs 454.9 Cr) because loan disbursements are classified as an operating outflow under Ind AS 7 for a balance sheet lender funding book growth through borrowings (B01, B03, Prospectus restated cash flow). This is not a manufacturing style cash conversion deterioration; the literal cumulative CFO/PAT of minus 7.25x is a growth scale artefact, not an earnings quality signal.
Rating agency evidence available: ICRA rationale 12-Nov-2025 assigned [ICRA]A (Stable) on Rs 400 Cr NCD, noting CRAR 34.8% (Jun-2025) and 90+dpd at 5.1% (Sep-2025) against its own 5% sensitivity (B00, rating p.1 to 2). A verbatim working capital quote is NOT APPLICABLE: a lender carries no trade working capital concept, so no manufacturing style receivables or working capital commentary exists to reproduce.
Residual INDETERMINATE element, not resolved to PROCEED: reported profit increasingly leans on gain on derecognition (1.94% of total income FY23 to 3.65% FY26, Results FY26 p.6) plus net fair value gains (5.7% of FY26 income), while the asset quality cycle on a roughly 3 year old mortgage book is not yet seasoned through a full stress.
Missing evidence named: (1) the share of FY26 and FY27 PAT that survives stripping gain on derecognition and net fair value gains; (2) securitisation and direct assignment buyer concentration and renewal risk; (3) a full asset quality cycle on the mortgage book. Obtain from the next quarterly other income and segment breakout, the concall Q&A, and a receivables/loan ageing schedule.
Single falsification metric for this residual: net gain on derecognition rising above about 4% of total income (from 3.65% FY26) in the next anchored quarterly filing while reported PAT growth continues to depend on it.

### FLAG-EARNINGS-QUALITY: ACTIVE

### FLAG-DISCLOSURE-GAP: ACTIVE

Separately, the tax restatement pattern carries no dedicated risk factor anywhere in the document (B03).

## Carried caveat: the one MAJOR verifier miss
The verifier layer surfaced one MAJOR red flag the pipeline did not catch upstream: over lending and per borrower leverage risk. FY26 AUM growth came 39% from repeat loans and about 60% from deepening existing branches, in a micro borrower book that itself survived an over lending cycle (Q3 FY26 call, Sanjay Sharma, p.7). This is the item that binds overall confidence to 64.

## Contradicted claims (priority monitoring)

1. Bihar insulation. AYE's implicit framing that a state MFI ordinance or bill causes minimal spillover into its non MFI business loan book is contradicted by the nearest analogue. SBFC, Jul-2025 call, Aseem Dhru: "the ordinance, which had nothing to do with us, brought our collection numbers down sharply, it is not about geography so much, it is more about ticket sizes" (B06 cites p.3 to 4; Verifier D pins the quote to p.7). Northern Arc corroborates the Karnataka spillover framing.
2. Sub 2% mortgage credit cost aspiration. Contradicted by SBFC, Nov-2025 call, Mahesh Dayani: the sub Rs 7 lakh secured MSME segment carries "enhanced credit cost" with SARFAESI recovery timelines "almost twice" the book average (B06 cites p.9; Verifier D pins to p.8). AYE's mortgage book is only about 3 years old and unseasoned.

6. NIM / spread on ATA: watch for sustained compression below 14% against guided 14.25 to 14.75%; investor presentation and results; tests the funding cost tailwind flowing to spread.
7. RoE (annualised): watch for continued decline below the H1 FY26 7.63% or a confirmed rebound; next RoE/RoA reconciliation; tests whether the profitability driven fall reverses after the IPO capital infusion.
8. Mortgage / LAP mix: watch for visible sequential increase beyond the flat 22 to 23% toward the 30% target; investor presentation mix slide; tests the transition to a more stable secured book.
