**🔴 RED FLAG (top-ranked) — the FY26 "Other Income" surge the screener/company-memory flagged
is a mislabelled one-time exceptional gain, not recurring other income.**
Standalone Note 26 "Other income" (AR FY26 p.187): ₹7.10 Cr FY26 vs ₹8.06 Cr FY25 vs ₹10.85 Cr
FY24 (AR FY25 Note 27, p.170) — Other Income has in fact been DECLINING for three straight years.
Consolidated Note 25 (AR FY26 p.255): ₹6.28 Cr FY26 vs ₹8.15 Cr FY25. Exceptional items (Note 59
standalone p.208 / Note 58 consolidated p.279): the Company "sold land in Ahmedabad, Gujarat,
during the June 2025 quarter and also land and building sold in Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu, during
the March 2026 quarter," with profits of ₹36.74 Cr and ₹22.96 Cr respectively = ₹59.70 Cr total,
presented as a SEPARATE exceptional-items line in the P&L (both standalone and consolidated,
identical amount — the land was parent-owned). Other Income (₹7.10 Cr) + Exceptional items
(₹59.70 Cr) = ₹66.80 Cr, matching the company-memory-flagged "₹66.81 Cr" figure almost exactly
(rounding). This confirms the screener/data-vendor figure BUNDLES other income and the one-time
land-sale gain into a single "Other Income" line — the real recurring other income line is small and
shrinking, and the FY26 "surge" is entirely a non-recurring capital gain from disposing of two
non-core land/building parcels. This is THE single most important normalisation adjustment for
any earnings-quality read of FY26: standalone PAT ₹87.83 Cr includes ₹59.70 Cr of pre-tax
exceptional gain (net of the 14.3% LTCG tax = post-tax gain of roughly ₹51.16 Cr, i.e. ₹59.70 Cr –
₹8.54 Cr tax per Note 33/32). Strip the exceptional item and standalone PAT for FY26 would be
roughly ₹87.83 Cr – ₹51.16 Cr ≈ ₹36.7 Cr, still a large improvement over FY25's ₹0.14 Cr, driven by
