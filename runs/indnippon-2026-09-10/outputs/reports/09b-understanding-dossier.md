## SECTION 3: BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

Drafted at Halt 1 per the shared spec in prompts/13-synthesis-pipeline.md. Stage 13's copy stays the final version.

India Nippon Electricals makes the electrical parts that start a two wheeler engine and run its electronics (B04). The core product is the flywheel magneto and ignition system, with about 2 million magnetos made in Q1 FY27 (B05 guidance), and the range now adds integrated starter generator controllers, sensors, DC to DC converters and an EFI engine control unit (B04; B07). Each part is built to one vehicle platform and qualified by the maker, so the maker cannot swap it mid model without new approvals (B04 moats_present). Motorcycles and scooters take 85% of sales, three wheelers 6% and general purpose engines 9%, and replacement spares bring in about 11% (B04.revenue_streams). The customers are a few large vehicle makers: TVS Motor, Hero MotoCorp and Bajaj Auto are named, and two customers took 70.66% of FY26 revenue (B02 rank 4; B09 downstream candidates). They order against platform nominations on 45 to 90 day terms, and the company recovers steel, aluminium and copper cost moves through a rate formula (B02 rank 11; B04 analyst_note). Present demand is two wheeler production, which the TVS Motor, Bajaj Auto and Hero MotoCorp volume signals and the SIAM monthly production and sales data track directly (B09 downstream_candidates). SIAM reports domestic two wheeler sales up 36.1% in FY26, a jump the AR ties to the September 2025 GST 2.0 cut, so GST Council and CBIC GST 2.0 rate slab stability is a live demand signal (B04; B09 FLAG-GST-ONEOFF-BASE-YEAR). Demand should grow if more of each vehicle's value comes from electronics the company makes, but no product revenue split is disclosed, so the run did not establish how far that shift has gone (B04 input_gaps; B07). Electric two wheeler adoption, tracked by Vahan and FADA registration data, raises spend per vehicle, yet peers say that spend goes to product lines built for electric vehicles, not to legacy ignition parts (B06 verified claim 5; B09). The company's own EV content per vehicle is NOT FOUND (B09). The BorgWarner EFI ECU licensing status is the named route into fuel injection electronics and rests on deck claims the AR does not back (B05; B09). China MOFCOM rare earth magnet export licensing sits under both the magneto line and the newer motor lines, so it is a demand risk as well as a supply risk (B09 FLAG-RARE-EARTH-SUPPLY-RISK). The OEM ignition line carries a moderate qualification lock-in moat, and two customer concentration caps how much that lock-in is worth (B07 B2 Moderate; B07 FLAG-CONCENTRATION-VS-MOAT). The electronics line carries a moderate patent moat, 29 patents granted and 43 filed, with no revenue tied to it yet (B04 moats_present; B07 A2). Exports score a moderate China plus one benefit, and the aftermarket and general purpose engine lines show no moat the run could establish (B07 E2; B07 scan). The scan found no talent asymmetry and only a weak barrier around the TVS Motor relationship (B07 I1, I2).
