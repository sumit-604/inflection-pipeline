Rule 3 fires. FLAG-CASH is active and the determination is INDETERMINATE. INDETERMINATE caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS instead of PROCEED WITH FLAGS.

Rule 4 agrees. Overall confidence of 62.5 sits in the 60 to 74 band, which moves a PROCEED verdict down one level. The verdict already sits at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS under the cash cap. ## Monitorables and triggers

1. Read the H1 FY27 cash flow statement when the results file on BSE in November 2026. Operating cash flow should be positive and above half of H1 profit. Closing receivables should be at or below Rs 28.9 Cr, and inventory days at or below 120 on a cost basis. This tests the cash determination.
2. In the same results, check the vendor advance and stock in transit notes. Advances should fall below Rs 3 Cr, and the Rs 6.2 Cr transit stock should turn into inventory or sales. Cash credit should sit below Rs 3 Cr. Advances still above Rs 6 Cr with no named supplier leave the March quarter cluster unexplained.
3. Compute H1 FY27 EBITDA excluding other income, and gross margin after materials and direct cost. EBITDA at or above 12% and gross margin at or above 24.1% support the margin step and the cost pass claim. EBITDA below 10%, or gross margin down 2 points, breaks both.
4. Track H2 FY27 revenue in the FY27 results, due about May 2027. Rs 67.3 Cr or more, with the order book above Rs 150 Cr, confirms the low end of guidance. Less than that, with billing stalled, breaks the order conversion leg.
5. Ask for H1 FY27 MVA made and a capacity certificate above 900.36 MVA, at the next call or presentation. Revenue per MVA near Rs 16 to 17 lakh supports a Rs 150 Cr plant. Near Rs 11 lakh, the plant tops out near Rs 102 Cr.
6. Watch BSE order filings for names and outcomes. The items are the buyers behind the Rs 19.97 Cr and Rs 20.02 Cr orders, and at least 25% of the Rs 87.50 Cr UGVCL order billed by 31 March 2027. Any filed MVVNL, Torrent or NHEV award also counts. These test order quality and the unfiled claims.
7. Read each IPO utilisation statement. At least Rs 3.5 Cr of the Rs 7 Cr building budget should be spent by 31 March 2027, with a machinery order filed and no second change of objects. This tests the Tijara plant path to about July 2027.
8. Check three governance items on MCA and BSE: the ADT-3 for the 2024 auditor change, the bank trail for the Rs 10.67 Cr land payment, and the first ESOP grant price. An adverse finding lifts the promoter read from CAUTION to CONCERN. The one year lock in on free promoter shares ends about 2 March 2027. That date is a reading of the ICDR rules, not verified.
