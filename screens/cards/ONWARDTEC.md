## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Onward sells engineering hours to large Western
equipment makers. About 85% of revenue is time and material, billed by the hour (Q2 FY27 deck,
p.11). The money is the spread between the bill rate and the cost of an engineer in India. FY26
revenue from operations was Rs 543.9 crore and EBITDA Rs 71.9 crore (Q2 FY27 deck, p.29).

**What is changing?** The margin. EBITDA margin went from 9.1% in FY25 to 13.2% in FY26 (Q2 FY27
deck, p.29). The company cut its client list from about 250 to 75 (May 2026 call, p.16). Headcount
fell from 2,581 to 2,485 while revenue grew 11% (AR FY26, p.46). Revenue per engineer is about
Rs 22 lakh, against Rs 8 to 9 lakh four years ago (May 2026 call, p.13).

**Why now?** Q2 FY27 was the best quarter in the company's history. Revenue from operations was
Rs 170.2 crore, up 22.5% on the year (q-2026-09, p.2). Management raised the FY27 guide to 15-18%
revenue growth at a 13.8-14.3% EBITDA margin (Press release 16-Oct-2026, p.2).

**What must be true?** The margin step must come from mix and account depth, not from the rupee or
one-off credits. The Q2 margin holds a one-off R&D credit (step 9). The company does not disclose
constant-currency growth.

**What breaks it?** Five customers give 47% of revenue (Q2 FY27 deck, p.12). And the hourly model
meets AI tools that cut engineering hours. In May 2026 management said it does not yet use AI tools
on billable work (May 2026 call, p.7).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, "ER&D and Digital services" (NSE clarification 29-Dec-2025, p.1). By vertical
in H1 FY27: Industrial Equipment and Heavy Machinery 65%, Transportation and Mobility 32%,
Healthcare and Life Sciences 3% (Q2 FY27 deck, p.5). Industrial includes energy and data-centre power
equipment.

The engine is account depth. Work starts on site, then moves offshore into a dedicated centre that
can grow from 50 to 500 engineers (Oct 2025 call, p.6; Jul 2026 call, p.12). Offshore billing earns
more than onsite (Jan 2026 call, p.16).

By customer location, Q2 FY27 revenue was 47% India, 41% USA and 12% Europe (Q2 FY27 deck, p.11).
The India share is captive centres of Western clients plus a UK branch (Jan 2026 call, p.10).

**Archetype: outsourcing partner.** Client concentration, wallet share, capacity fill, contract
stickiness and price per unit are the variables.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming, at account level only. Becoming a qualified supplier takes "three months to three years"
of pilots and audits (Jan 2026 call, p.9). Dedicated offshore centres create switching cost.
Engagements average 5 to 8 years (Q2 FY27 deck, p.24).

Pricing power is absent. The client gives Onward "a standard rate card", the same as for the
largest three firms (Jan 2026 call, p.10). The MD calls the mechanical side "more and more
commoditized" (Oct 2025 call, p.6). ICRA said larger peers constrain pricing (03-ICRA, p.1). The
advantage is a switching cost, not a price premium.

## 4. The promoters

The Mehta family. Harish Mehta founded the company and co-founded NASSCOM. His son Jigar has been MD
since May 2016. Both were reappointed for five years from 14-May-2026 (q-2026-03, p.2).

Holdings at 31-Mar-2026: JHM Enterprises 34.98%, Jigar 1.78%, Heral 1.11%, Harish 0.98%, Prachi
0.76% (AR FY26, p.81). The group held 40.20% in Sep 2026 (market-data-2026-10-18.md, screener.in
service record, not a filed document). No pledge or encumbrance in FY26 (Reg 31(4) declaration,
team_bbodade filing, p.2).

Skin in the game is mixed. The promoters stayed out of the Rs 18 crore buyback at Rs 328 (Board
outcome 12-May-2026, p.1). That lifted their share from 39.07% to about 40.0% at no cost. Prachi
Mehta bought 30,127 shares in August 2026 and JHM 25,000 in September 2026 (filings 18-Aug-2026 and
17-Sep-2026, p.1).

Pay is high. Harish drew Rs 3.83 crore and Jigar Rs 4.60 crore in FY26 (AR FY26, p.88). The Rs 8.43
crore is 19% of FY26 profit of Rs 44.32 crore, and 40 and 48 times median pay (AR FY26, p.24).

Convergent Finance holds 17.37% through two Infinity entities (AR FY26, p.81). Related-party trade
is small: Rs 0.54 crore rent to a promoter entity (AR FY26, p.88).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R2, a cost-advantaged converter of engineering labour: in FY25 a 9.1% margin, 17% ROCE and about
80 clients (AR FY26, p.46). TO R3, a qualified supplier with dedicated offshore centres: ROCE 25%
and the top 25 clients at 87% of revenue. The mechanism is fewer, deeper accounts and a shift from
mechanical to software work.

Management's claim, quoted once: "we are moving from a mechanical engineering company to a digital
engineering company" (May 2026 call, p.14).

Two readings separate here. The first says the climb is structural: pruning, revenue per engineer
and US growth lifted the margin. The second says part of the lift is the rupee and one-off credits.
The disclosed offshore share did not rise: 69%, 72%, 71% for FY24 to FY26 and 69% in Q2 FY27 (Q2
FY27 deck, p.17 and p.11). **The observation that separates the readings is constant-currency
growth with a margin bridge.** Management has deferred that question to its IR firm twice (Jan
2026 call, p.15; May 2026 call, p.15). One rung in two years fits the base rate.

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim US-led growth, larger accounts and a higher margin. Revenue from operations grew
12.2% in Q1 FY27 and 22.5% in Q2 FY27 (q-2026-09, p.2; the Q1 FY26 base is H1 FY26 less Q2 FY26).
US customer revenue in H1 FY27 was Rs 118.1 crore against Rs 87.4 crore, up 35% (q-2026-09, p.3).

The margin proof is weaker than the headline. Q1 FY27 was 12.3%. Q2 FY27 was 15.6%, but 13.9%
without the R&D credit. H1 FY27 was 13.1% without it, flat on FY26. **The proof point is faster
growth at a held margin, not a second margin step.**

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **One-off credit in Q2 FY27.** Expenses include an R&D credit of Rs 2.93 crore, net of fees, from
  a true-up of earlier claims (q-2026-09, p.3). It adds 1.7 points to Q2 margin. The press release
  and deck do not mention it.
- **Receivable build.** Up Rs 27.9 crore in H1; operating cash flow halved (step 5).
- **Concentration.** Top 5 clients 47%, top 10 63%, top 25 87% (Q2 FY27 deck, p.12). None named.
- **Segment opacity.** NSE asked about segment disclosure in December 2025 (Clarification
  29-Dec-2025, p.1). No vertical margins. The MD said the two smaller verticals earn single-digit
  margins (Oct 2025 call, p.10).
- **Guidance disclosure.** An investor said a broker note carried a 10-12% guide not given on the
  call. The MD denied it (May 2026 call, p.15).
- **Loose India message.** "We don't do any business in India" (Oct 2025 call, p.9). India customer
  location was 52% of FY26 revenue (AR FY26, p.46). Both 2026 contracts came from domestic entities
  (filings 25-Jun-2026 and 21-Sep-2026, p.2).
- **US lawsuit** by a former salesman. Bank balances of USD 12.6 lakh were frozen, then released.
  Contingent liability USD 4.20 lakh (AR FY26, p.87).
- **Audit trail** not enabled in full at the ledger application layer (AR FY26, p.63). Opinion
  unmodified.
- **ESOP trust** bought 3,09,679 shares with a Rs 10.08 crore company loan. The secretarial audit
  flagged a trustee who was a subsidiary director (AR FY26, p.23 and p.25).
- No constant-currency disclosure (step 6); promoter pay at 19% of profit (step 4).

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**The rating is stale. Weigh it low.** The latest ICRA action, 13-Nov-2023, withdrew the rating at
the company's request (01-ICRA, p.1). On 16-Oct-2023 ICRA had moved it to "Issuer Not Cooperating"
for an unpaid surveillance fee (02-ICRA, p.1).

The last full rationale, 27-Mar-2023, gave [ICRA]BBB (Stable)/[ICRA]A3+ on Rs 6.55 crore of working
capital lines (03-ICRA, p.1). ICRA credited debt-free status, liquidity and reputed clients. It cited modest scale, moderate profitability, pricing
constrained by larger peers and currency risk (03-ICRA, p.1-2).

That view predates the margin climb; today's filings win. The pricing constraint still binds.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED.** FY26 margin 13.2% against 9.1% and ROCE 25%, in audited accounts. H1 FY27
  held the margin on 17.5% growth.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** The receivable build, the hiring jump and the Q4 FY26 dip are
  costs of ramping new centres. Concentration is the model, not decay.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED on trailing, OPEN only on the guide.** Market cap Rs 951 crore at
  Rs 427, at the 52-week high of Rs 428 against a low of Rs 208 (market-data-2026-10-18.md,
  screener.in service record). Trailing four-quarter profit of Rs 47.6 crore (q-2025-12 p.2,
  q-2026-03 p.4, q-2026-09 p.2) gives 20.0 times, at the R3 neighbourhood of about 19 times. In May
  2026 an investor put the market cap near Rs 550 crore (May 2026 call, p.6).

**Posture: EARNINGS-ONLY.** The climb is real and the market has paid for much of it since May.
Returns from here ride profit growth. The forward multiple keeps one foot in RE-RATING LIVE.

## 12. Verdict card

**PROCEED to `/step1`.**

This is an audited one-rung climb with a raised Tier A guide and no debt. Growth of 20% or more on
about 17 times forward profit, with net cash near a tenth of market cap, earns the full run. The run
must first split the margin into mix, rupee and credits.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. Constant-currency revenue growth for FY26 and H1 FY27, and the EBITDA bridge.
2. The R&D credit: amount by quarter, whether it recurs, and earlier credits (a Canada tax credit
   hit Q1 FY26, Oct 2025 call, p.11).
3. Receivable ageing at September 2026, by client.
4. Top five clients by revenue and contract tenure.

**What would change the view**
- Q3 FY27 margin at 14% or more without credits, with DSO near 73 days, moves this toward RE-RATING LIVE.
- Constant-currency growth below 8% makes the climb a currency effect and the verdict WATCH.
- Any top-five client cutting hours for AI reasons.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the
name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
