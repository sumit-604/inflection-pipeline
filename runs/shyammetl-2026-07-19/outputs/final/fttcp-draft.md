## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue.** What fires it: capacity that is already commissioned or about to be. The Kharagpur blast furnace commissioned and started commercial production (Q3 FY26 call). The colour coated line at Jamuria commissioned in April 2026 and colour coated volume roughly tripled year on year (Q4 FY26 call). The aluminium foil plant at Sambalpur started commercial production on 16 July 2026 (operator context, to be confirmed against the Reg 30 filing in Phase 3). The FRP plant is due by end September 2026, DRI during FY27, and the wagon plant Phase 1 by September 2026. This is a documented pipeline, not a promise. What kills it: commissioning slips or the new lines run below 60 to 70% utilisation for two quarters after start up.

**Margin.** What could fire it: the mix shifting toward stainless and aluminium, where profit per tonne runs 5 to 10 times the carbon steel base (B04). What confirms it: consolidated margin above 16% held for two quarters. What kills it, or at least delays it: the safeguard duty lapsing again (it already lapsed once in late 2025 before being reimposed, per GPIL and SARDAEN peer calls, B06), or the stainless and specialty lines slipping past FY29. Near term there is no documented margin catalyst inside the window, which is why the verdict is STAGNANT and not STARTING.

**Cash conversion.** What sustains it: net cash, captive power at about Rs 2.5 per unit against grid at Rs 5 to 7, and debtor days already under three weeks at the group. What would break it: the finished goods inventory build, up 53.7% against 15% revenue growth with the write down expense up more than ninefold (B02), converting into a permanent working capital bulge rather than liquidating. That is the single falsification metric.

**ROCE.** What fires it: the commissioned capacity earning as the capital base stops growing, plus captive power lowering the cost base. What works against it: the remaining Rs 10,617 Cr of capex through FY29 keeps adding fresh capital that will not earn for one to two years, the classic treadmill where the denominator grows as fast as the numerator. The subsidiary profit halving is the current warning sign that the treadmill may be winning. This is a genuine two sided call.

**The promoter overhang, ruled here because it governs the disposition.** Two live April 2026 actions sit over the promoter. The Enforcement Directorate provisionally attached about Rs 160 Cr of assets at the material subsidiary Shyam Sel and Power under money laundering law, tied to alleged illegal coal mining, and it is unresolved (B08, media reported, corroborated by the ED press release, and it lines up with management deflecting on an ED coal notice in the Q4 FY26 call). A pollution regulator ordered a plant cluster at Rengali closed, with conditional relief and a three month remediation window (B08). Against that, a SAIL and NMDC calibre independent director joined in 2024, a Rs 1,385 Cr institutional placement explains the promoter stake drop as pure dilution not a sale, pledge is zero, and succession passed cleanly to the second generation. My ruling: this is a real CONCERN, not a disqualifier, and it is the single thing most likely to veto the position at final disposition. It does not lower the transition score, because the transition score measures the business, not the promoter.

**Step 2E, the documented action check.** With the announcements folder empty this run, this ran on concall and annual report evidence only and could not grade recent exchange filings. On that basis it CONFIRMS every verdict above and loosens none. Revenue and cash are already at their ceiling reads. On margin and ROCE there is real commissioning action, but the earnings benefit is not in the numbers yet, so the action supports the current read without lifting it a state. No verdict was too conservative in a way that documented action overturns.

## THE P/E BASE CARD (for operator approval)

This is a preview of the exit multiple the valuation will use, computed from Section 1B off what is known now. It is not the final valuation. Role 1 still runs the full dual track exercise in Phase 3. Several inputs are provisional and marked.

- **Pillar 1 (quality base).** ROCE forward verdict RECOVERING at 40-60%. Continuous formula: base PE = 0.5 x ROCE + 7.5, floor 9x, cap 24x.
  - On current ROCE 13.2%: base PE = 14.1x.
  - On the RECOVERING blend weighted to current and an illustrative FY28 of ~16%: base PE ~ 14.7x.
  - On Route B pre cycle normalized ~20% (blended 60/40 current to anchor per the RECOVERING 40-60% rule): base PE ~ 15.5x.
  - Pillar 1 base: about 14.7x, rising to about 15.5x if Route B is used. The FY28 ROCE and the pre cycle median are illustrative and must be anchored in Phase 3.
- **Pillar 2 (cash multiplier).** GROWTH-INDUCED. Provisional neutral, 1.0x. Consolidated cash conversion is strong; the standalone drain does not warrant a penalty at the group level. Final multiplier is Role 1's call.
- **Pillar 3 (growth and moat premium), combined cap +6x.**
  - 3a growth visibility: capex embedded growth is far above the 15% bar (B07 puts it near 150%) and management delivery grade is B, so two qualifiers hold. Award +2x. The SOM implied revenue CAGR of 12.7% does not clear the 20% bar, and no order book is disclosed, so it is not more.
  - 3b moat formation: EM score 30, STRENGTHENING. Provisional +1x, a forming not confirmed moat.
  - 3c duration: no documented order book of 2.5 years or more (steel is short cycle). +0x.
  - Pillar 3 total: about +3x.
- **Strategic premium.** 0x. The ROCE recovery is credited in Pillar 1, so the strategic ROCE re-rating route is barred, and no other strategic premium is evidenced.
- **Undiscovered alpha.** Not applied (FII plus DII about 16.7%, above the 3% ceiling).
- **Sector cap.** 20x, absolute.

**Resulting destination (exit) PE, both tracks, each under the 20x cap:**
- Additive track: Pillar 1 base ~14.7x, times cash multiplier 1.0x, plus Pillar 3 ~+3x, plus strategic 0 = about **17.7x raw**. Undiscovered alpha not applied. min(17.7x, 20x cap) = **about 17.7x**.
- RRM track: RRM = 1 + (13.5 minus r) x 0.12, in percentage points. For a steel cost of equity around 14.5%, RRM is about 0.88. Applied to the quality base and premiums this pulls the destination toward **about 15 to 16x**. Exact RRM output is Role 1's computation.
- Destination PE preview: **about 15 to 18x on both tracks**, comfortably under the 20x cap. The card is PROVISIONAL on the FY28 ROCE, the pre cycle median, the CWIP figure for the route choice, and the exact Pillar 2 and Pillar 3b multipliers.

**The earnings basis question, for the operator to decide.** The exit multiple can be applied to one year forward earnings or to trailing earnings.
- One year forward P/E fits a cyclical business mid ramp, because trailing earnings are depressed by capacity that is commissioned but not yet fully earning, so trailing understates.
- Trailing P/E is the more conservative choice here, because the forward ramp is unproven, the promoter matter is live, and the subsidiary profit is falling.
- I do not pick this. It is the operator's call at the gate.

**The tension this card surfaces.** The stock trades at about 26.7x trailing FY26 earnings today. The destination preview is about 15 to 18x. That is a de-rating path, not a re-rating one, so clearing the 25% return hurdle from the current price would need a very high earnings growth rate. On these preview numbers the hurdle is hard to clear at Rs 1,022. That is a Phase 3 finding to confirm, not a decision made here, but it is the reason the disposition stays cautious.
