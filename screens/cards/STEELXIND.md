## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Steel Exchange India makes billets and rolls them into TMT rebar at its plant in Vizianagaram, Andhra Pradesh. It sells under the brand SIMHADRI TMT (guidance record, doc 0baf3c20, page 2). It belongs to the Vizag Profiles Group (press release, doc 05f79691, page 3, search snippet). It earns a margin per tonne: FY26 EBITDA per tonne averaged about Rs 6,800 to 7,000 (doc 3554d050, page 12).

**What is changing?** Two things. The balance sheet is being repaired, and management claims volume will double. Debt cost fell from 18.75% to 13% (guidance record, doc b0c048a8, page 4). Debt to equity fell to 0.27 at Q1 FY27 from 0.42 at Q4 FY26 (Q1 FY27 press release, revised, doc 05f79691, pages 1 to 2, full page).

**Why now?** Q4 FY26 looked like a turn. EBITDA was Rs 50.10 crore against Rs 22.97 crore in Q3, a margin of 17.41% (doc 3554d050, page 4). EBITDA per tonne reached about Rs 9,000 to 9,500 against about Rs 6,000 earlier (doc 3554d050, page 12). Q4 rebar volume was 49,760 tonnes against about 41,000 a year earlier (doc b0c048a8, page 10).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Volume must double as claimed, margin must hold near Rs 7,000 per tonne, and new equity must arrive. IMR Resources subscribed Rs 300 crore of warrants, expected within six months of May 2026 (doc b0c048a8, page 5).

**What breaks it?** The next quarter already did. Management said volume would be "almost double" from the next quarter (doc b0c048a8, page 11). Q1 FY27 total income then fell 11.2% year on year to Rs 270.71 crore from Rs 304.95 crore (doc 05f79691, pages 1 to 2, full page). PAT rose because interest fell.

## 2. Business model and archetype

One engine: tonnes sold times EBITDA per tonne, less a heavy interest bill. FY26 total income was Rs 1,066.42 crore, EBITDA Rs 138.03 crore and PAT Rs 26.99 crore (doc 3554d050, page 4). EBITDA margin was 12.9% (derived: 138.03 / 1,066.42). PAT margin was 2.5% (derived).

Per tonne margin has sat in one band for five years. The nine months to December 2020 earned Rs 6,094 per tonne (guidance record, doc 68d5aed8, page 19). FY26 averaged Rs 6,800 to 7,000. Q4 FY26 was the outlier.

**Archetype: commodity converter.** Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds. The binding variables are the spread over raw material cost, utilisation and cycle position. No single year ROCE is used on this card.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent. Rebar is a commodity. The brand has local standing: SIMHADRI TMT was approved for two Andhra Pradesh port projects, Machilipatnam and Mulapeta (doc 0baf3c20, page 2). That is an approval list, not pricing power.

Land allows expansion to 1.5 million tonnes (doc 68d5aed8, page 12). A diversification into specialty steels under the PLI scheme is stated (doc 0baf3c20, page 3), with no capacity, date or rupee figure. Verdict: **moat absent.**

## 8. Proof check

The proof point was volume, dated to the next quarter. Q1 FY27 total income fell 11.2% year on year and 5.9% on Q4 (derived: 270.71 / 287.70). Q1 tonnage is NOT FOUND.

Two readings: volume doubled and realisation fell enough to hide it, or volume did not double. A hidden doubling needs realisation to fall by more than half (derived). **The proof gate did not fire.** The Q1 FY27 tonnage disclosure separates the two readings.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** The volume doubling did not appear in the quarter it was promised for.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** A settlement with lenders in 2020, equity funded by warrants, and a promotional release are features of how this company raises money. They are not a build phase.
- **Recognition gap: not open.** The trailing PE is 52.8x (derived: 1,426.03 / 26.99). The forward PE on run rate is 23.7x. The R1 neighbourhood is near 12x.

**Posture: VALUE-TRAP RISK.**

The fair counterweight: debt and its cost did fall, and Q4 FY26 showed the plant can earn Rs 9,000 per tonne. The repair is real. It is not a climb.
