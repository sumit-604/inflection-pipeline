Anawil Wire & Engineering Ltd 
Strong Revenue Growth Drives Robust EBITDA and PAT Performance in H1FY27 
Vapi, Gujarat, November 06, 2026: Anawil Wire & Engineering Limited, a specialised player in tubular wind 
tower manufacturing for the onshore wind energy sector, has  announced its financial results for the first 
half of the year ended 30th September, 2026. 
Key Financial & Operational Highlights 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Key Performance Drivers 
• Strong Revenue Growth & Evolving Business Mix : Revenue from operations grew 272.2% YoY to Rs 
153.97 Cr, supported by 72.6% growth in tower production to 107 towers  and higher realisations from 
integrated supply contracts. Model 3 contributed 64.3% of revenue, followed by Model 1 (21.2%), Model 
2 (10.8%) and Others (3.7%). Notably, H1FY27 revenue surpassed full-year FY26 revenue of Rs 143.27 Cr. 
• Robust Earnings Growth & Changing Margin Profile: EBITDA grew 255.8% YoY to Rs 36.08 Cr, while PAT 
increased 539.3% YoY to Rs 14.39 Cr. EBITDA margin stood at 23.4%, reflecting a higher contribution from 
material-inclusive Model 3 contracts, which carry relatively lower percentage margins. Initial operating 
expenses at the Kutch, Gujarat plant during its early ramp-up phase also impacted profitability.

• Absolute EBITDA per Tower – Key Profitability Metric: Given the varying material requirements across 
business models, absolute EBITDA per tower remains a key indicator of operating profitability . It 
increased 104.7% YoY and 14.8% over FY26 to Rs 32.82 Lakh . Initial expenses at the Kutch plant limited 
further improvement, with higher utilisation expected to support better cost absorption and profitability. 
• Healthy Order Book & Strong Execution Visibility : The confirmed order book stands at Rs 395.58 Cr, 
comprising Model 3 (51 .3%), Model 1 (2 4.5%) and Model 2 (24 .2%), excluding additional LOIs worth  
Rs 44.05 Cr. The existing order book is targeted for execution by the end of Q1FY28, providing strong 
near-term revenue visibility. 
• Capacity Expansion & Strong FY27 Growth Outlook: With H1 typically being seasonally softer than H2 in 
tower production, stronger execution is expected in H2FY27, backed by a healthy order book. Capacity 
expansion at Kutch, Gujarat, is nearing completion, with trial runs underway, while in -house kits 
manufacturing at Koppal, Karnataka, strengthens backward integration and operational self -reliance. 
Higher execution and improving utilisation are expected to support the Company's FY27 revenue 
guidance of Rs 450–500 Cr and PAT guidance of Rs 55–60 Cr. 
 
Commenting on result, Mr. Nimish Kumar Rameshchandra Vashi, Chairman & Managing Director:  
"H1FY27 marks a significant step forward in our growth journey, supported by higher tower production, 
stronger execution and an increasing contribution from integrated supply contracts. The evolving 
business mix has enabled us to undertake a larger scope of work, driving higher revenue realisations and 
absolute EBITDA per tower. 
Given the different material requirements across our business models, we believe absolute EBITDA per 
tower is a more meaningful measure of operating performance than percentage margins alone. While 
integrated supply contracts carry relatively lower percent age margins, they generate higher absolute 
EBITDA per tower. Initial operating expenses at our Kutch, Gujarat plant, ahead of its full ramp -up, have 
limited further improvement in profitability. We expect better cost absorption as utilisation increases.  
With H1 typically being seasonally softer than H2, we anticipate a significant pick -up in execution during 
H2FY27, backed by our strong order book. Our capacity expansion at Kutch is nearing completion, while 
the addition of in -house kits manufacturing at Koppal, Karnataka, strengthens backward integration. 
These initiatives position us to scale production, undertake larger orders and work towards achieving our 
FY27 financial guidance”.

About Anawil: 
Anawil Wire & Engineering Limited is a precision -led manufacturer of tubular wind turbine towers for the 
onshore wind energy sector, serving leading WTG OEMs and renewable energy companies. With 
manufacturing facilities at Koppal, Karnataka and Kutch, Guja rat, the Company has a combined annual 
capacity of 612 towers and capabilities to manufacture customised towers of up to 140 metres. Its 
integrated manufacturing and engineering capabilities, expanding capacity and focus on in-house tower 
internals position Anawil to capture the growing opportunities in India’s renewable energy infrastructure 
market.
