### 2A Accounting policy aggressiveness — EXTENSION

line on the face of the P&L INCLUDES other income.** Line V of the Consolidated
P&L reads "Earnings before interest expense, taxes, depreciation and

figure the market has been tracking. This is a real, if common, presentation
choice that inflates the headline profitability metric by roughly 160bps of
margin; it is not disclosed as a non-GAAP reconciliation anywhere in the AR.
Rated 🟡 — carried forward as part of FLAG-CASH below.

revenue metric the pricing claim implies exists. Materiality: MODERATE — it
does not change the thesis on its own, but it means the "AI-native
re-pricing" narrative cannot yet be tested against a hard number in this AR,
and should be a named monitorable (see Phase 8).

**2. CONFIRMED — implicit retraction: Group-level "operational strength"

CONSOLIDATED profit, requiring a (45.6)% elimination adjustment. The letter
and MD&A never state that the entity whose shareholders are reading this
report is, on a standalone basis, structurally a near-breakeven service
centre for a foreign subsidiary. Classification: (a) implicit retraction —
the opening frames Group strength as the Company's own without naming the
entity-level split; operations (standalone P&L, RPT note) show a materially
different picture with no acknowledgement of the gap. Materiality: MODERATE —
standard practice for many Indian listcos with an overseas-heavy group
structure, but material enough to name given (i) this is the FIRST AR as a
listed company, where the standalone/consolidated relationship is least
familiar to new retail shareholders, and (ii) the RPT/transfer-pricing study
for FY26 was still "in process" at sign-off (Phase 2B), meaning the
standalone economics themselves are not yet independently certified as
arm's-length.

No further quiet abandonments were identified beyond these two; other
