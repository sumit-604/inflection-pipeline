## SIGNED MENTAL MODEL DECLARATION
Lines and archetypes:
1. Core cephalosporin API, oral and sterile, including merged Dhanuka Labs:
   CONVERTER (spread business, price taker). Fixed cost about Rs 350 cr a
   year; breakeven about Rs 260 cr revenue a quarter; each extra Rs 100 cr
   revenue adds about Rs 33 cr EBITDA [INFERENCE from FILED].
2. Jammu 7-ACA plant (Orchid Bio-Pharma, 1,000 t/yr): CONVERTER slice
   (Amendment 17), valued as unfinished until it earns. Cost path Rs 489 to
   Rs 750 cr [MGMT]. COD guided Feb to Mar 2027, 12-month ramp [MGMT].
3. Enmetazobactam (Exblifep): IP option. US approved Feb-2024, exclusivity
   to about 2034 [AGENCY]. Europe Advanz, Russia Pharmasyntez 7-Jul-2026
   [FILED]. No US partner.
4. Cefiderocol for GARDP: cost plus 25% at PBT [MGMT, two calls].
   Commissioning Dec-2026.
5. Synmedic (98% held, not consolidated): not a line. Leak to watch.

Dominant variables:
1. Core price spread. Gross margin 31.5% FY26, about 33% Q4 FY26 and
   Q1 FY27 [FILED]. One point is about Rs 13 cr EBITDA a year.
2. Jammu on time, on cost, and cheaper than Chinese 7-ACA. Technology is
   shared with Chinese makers [MGMT, May-2023], so the edge is location and
   PLI (max about Rs 65 cr in total, ends FY29) [INFERENCE from DoP rules].
3. Cash wall FY27 and FY28. About Rs 40 cr principal in FY27 and about
   Rs 130 cr a year from FY28 [FILED maturities plus INFERENCE].
4. Exblifep US partnering.

Analogy: a flour mill in a town of flour mills, building its own wheat farm
with the same seeds foreign farms use, holding one patented recipe not yet
sold in the biggest market, with loan instalments due before the harvest.

Model rejects as noise: quarterly revenue swings from inventory timing;
other income; the hospital division; the Russia headline dollar value; the
"Rs 600 cr" PLI headline; price moves around the merger share listing.

Model falsifier: material cost at or above 63.5% of sales four quarters
after Jammu COD (about the Mar-2028 quarter).

Correction 37 (applied at signing): the draft said Orchid "may be the only
Indian 7-ACA maker". Struck. The run's own dossier records a reported
Aurobindo 7-ACA project of about 2,000 t/yr (Correction 7.1). Status of that
project not verified live. Orchid is one of two PLI applicants, not
necessarily the only domestic maker.

Competitive advantages:
- Real: scale in cephalosporins (domestic share about 34% merged [MGMT]);
  clean FDA record, all VAI 2010 to 2025 [AGENCY]; sterile pricing held
  (+17% FY26) while oral fell 13% [INFERENCE from FILED]; long Otsuka GCLE
  relationship (also a single-source dependency).
- Emerging: own 7-ACA; Exblifep IP; GARDP credential.
- Not advantages: oral pricing (Covalent is larger, about 10% margin, group
  Pen-G coming, debt to about 3.5x by Mar-2027 [AGENCY, ICRA 31-Mar-2026]);
  regulated-market share falling 40% to about 30% [FILED]; rising debt;
  disclosure quality.

Fragility: FRAGILE (cash wall precedes the plant's proof).
