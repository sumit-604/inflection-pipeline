## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Sudarshan Colorants makes organic and inorganic pigments, pigment preparations and dyes at Roha, Cuddalore and Nagda (AR26, p.17). The colour goes into paints, plastics, printing inks and special uses (AR26, p.18). One segment, "Pigments" (Q2 res, p.6). FY26 revenue was Rs 780.63 crore and profit Rs 44.53 crore (AR26, p.21). India took Rs 574.45 crore of customer revenue and exports Rs 200.49 crore (AR26, p.94). No borrowings (AR26, p.19); 440 employees (AR26, p.20).

**What is changing?** The owner. Heubach of Germany filed for insolvency in April 2024 (AR26, p.21). On 3-Mar-2025 Sudarshan Chemical Industries Ltd (SCIL) bought the Heubach pigment business worldwide for EUR 127.5 million, about Rs 1,160 crore (Acq, p.4), and with it 54.36% of this company (AR26, p.21). The open offer at Rs 602.03 took the promoter group to 70.25% on 3-Oct-2025 (AR26, p.21). The claimed business change is integration into "ONE Sudarshan": select AZO products moving from Frankfurt to Roha, Cuddalore building commodity and specialty lines, group procurement (AR26, p.9).

**Why now?** H1 FY27 revenue rose 25% to Rs 492.15 crore and profit 33% to Rs 44.49 crore (Q2 res, p.3). Q2 FY27 is the strongest quarter in the corpus: revenue Rs 269.76 crore, profit Rs 24.13 crore (Q2 res, p.3). The MD wrote that destocking "has largely run its course" (AR26, p.10). Trailing PE is 16.7 (MD).

**What must be true?** The H1 FY27 sales must be third-party demand, not group channel stocking. The Rs 69.77 crore of receivables and Rs 99.07 crore of inventory added in six months must turn to cash (Q2 res, p.5). Transfer prices must leave the margin here. The parent must keep the listed entity as its Indian node rather than fold it in at a low ratio.

**What breaks it?** H2 FY27 repeats H2 FY26, when pre-exceptional profit before tax fell to Rs 18.03 crore (Rs 10.15 crore plus Rs 7.88 crore) from Rs 44.56 crore in H1 (Q3-26, p.5; FY26 res, p.3; Q2-26, p.3). Related-party pricing moves against the Indian entity. Or SCIL, which bought the 15.89% block from its Dutch subsidiary at Rs 411 a share on 1-Oct-2026 (Q2 res, p.6), uses the merger right it reserved (LoF, p.25).

## 2. Business model and archetype

Sale of goods Rs 750.11 crore, services Rs 24.83 crore in FY26 (AR26, p.87). Exports were 26% of customer revenue against 32% in FY25 (AR26, p.19; AR25, p.16). Domestic revenue grew 3.6% in FY26; exports fell 24% (derived: AR26, p.94).

Margin by product is not disclosed. The group plumbing is. In FY26 the company bought Rs 99.62 crore of goods from Sudarshan Switzerland HLD1 AG and Rs 40.95 crore from Sudarshan Gujarat MFG. It sold Rs 137.25 crore to Sudarshan Singapore SLO, Rs 20.99 crore to Sudarshan USA SLO and Rs 10.15 crore to SCIL (AR26, p.93). Related parties took 24.5% of sales and supplied 32% of purchases, the purchase share up from 11% (AR26, p.39). Stock-in-trade purchases were Rs 135.93 crore, 17% of revenue (FY26 res, p.3).

**Archetype: Commodity converter for the core pigment lines** (oversupply and small-player competition head the risk table, AR26, p.19; Amendment 17 binds any deep run). A **build-to-spec layer** sits on top for high-performance grades in automotive coatings and food-contact plastics (AR26, pp.10, 17). Because a quarter of sales and a third of purchases run through the group, outsourcing-partner metrics also apply: wallet share within ONE Sudarshan, capacity fill, transfer price per unit.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Specification know-how: present, inherited.** Clariant-era grades, an R&D centre in Navi Mumbai, 500 products to 75 countries through group entities (AR26, pp.17, 19). **Group access: forming.** Customers "who previously sourced certain specialty grades elsewhere have returned" (AR26, p.10). Not quantified. **Pricing power: weak on the core.** Operating margin 7% in FY26, 10% in FY25; ROCE 11% (AR26, p.19). **Cost position: partial.** Renewables are 58% of electricity; Roha solar saved Rs 2.52 crore (AR26, pp.9, 26).

**Verdict: a qualification moat on specialty grades; a converter with no pricing power on the core. The group moat is claimed, not shown.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R2, cost-advantaged converter, low end.** Margin from cost position and inherited grades, ROCE 11% to 13%, no pricing power on the core.

**TO: R3, value-added spec'd supplier.** Localised premium HP Azo for exports from FY27, recycling-compatible and food-contact grades, products transferred from Frankfurt to Roha (AR26, pp.9, 10).

Management's claim, once: "specialty and high-performance grades showing better resilience than commodity pigments, validating our direction of moving the portfolio up the value chain" (AR26, p.10).

Ladder check. One rung, so the base rate holds. The second reading is R2 to R2: a captive node whose margin is set by group transfer prices. The observation that separates them is the gross margin on third-party sales against group sales, which no filing splits.

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point is two quarters of profit on rising revenue, and it is present.** Q1 FY27 PAT Rs 20.36 crore against Rs 17.09 crore; Q2 Rs 24.13 crore against Rs 16.28 crore (Q1 res, p.2; Q2 res, p.3; Q2-26, p.3). H1 EBITDA margin 12.2% against 10.7% (derived: Q2 res, p.3). Q2 revenue is 47% above the Rs 183.14 crore of Q2 FY26 (Q2-26, p.3). On the full six-quarter series, profit troughed at minus Rs 2.00 crore in Q3 FY26 (Q3-26, p.5) and has risen three quarters in a row; the last two are the first since the change of control to beat the year-ago quarter on both revenue and profit.

**What the quarters do not prove.** Where the revenue came from. No product, geography or related-party split is filed for the half. The cash went the other way: minus Rs 24.11 crore from operations, inventories up 63%, receivables up 37% (Q2 res, pp.4-5). The half could be third-party demand returning, or the group filling the Indian plants and channel. The observation that separates them is the Reg 23(9) disclosure for H1 FY27, which the corpus does not hold.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED on earnings, not on cash.** Two quarters of revenue and margin growth after the change of control (step 8). The cash and the related-party split are unproven.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, with one structural strand.** The FY26 trough came from the Heubach insolvency, destocking and integration (AR26, p.10) and one-off charges (step 5). The group dependence is structural; it does not leave with the trough.
- **Recognition gap: OPEN on the run-rate reading.** Trailing PE 16.7 (MD); 17.7x on filed TTM profit of Rs 55.67 crore (derived). Forward PE 11.1x on the H1 run-rate (step 13) against an R3 neighbourhood of about 19x. On the reversion reading the forward PE is 17.1x and the gap is closed.

**Posture: RE-RATING LIVE, conditional.** The cell holds only if H2 FY27 holds the H1 margin and the receivables turn to cash. If the half was group-fed, the posture drops to PRICED NARRATIVE.

## 12. Verdict card

**PROCEED to /step1.**

Two quarters doubled the profit run-rate, Rs 206 crore of liquid funds sit against a Rs 988 crore market cap, and the forward multiple on the delivered half is about 11x. Against that: the cash did not come, the group sits on both sides of the trade, and the parent holds a filed option to restructure or merge after October 2027.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. The related-party share of H1 FY27 sales and purchases, from the Reg 23(9) disclosure, against 24.5% and 32% in FY26 (AR26, p.39).
2. Who owes the Rs 244.95 crore of receivables, what the Rs 255.22 crore of inventory is, and whether Q3 FY27 operating cash flow turns positive (Q2 res, pp.4-5).
3. The AZO transfer from Frankfurt to Roha: volumes, revenue, margin; the HP Azo export start (AR26, pp.9-10).
4. SCIL's plan for the listed entity after the no-delisting window from 3-Oct-2025 (LoF, p.24), and why the 15.89% block moved onshore at Rs 411 (Q2 res, p.6).

**What would change the view**
- Up, to a run with conviction: Q3 FY27 revenue above Rs 240 crore with receivables flat, positive operating cash flow, related-party sales at or below the FY26 share.
- Down to PASS: related-party sales above 35% of H1 revenue; H2 FY27 margin under 8%; a GST demand from the August search; a merger or delisting proposal priced off a trough year.
