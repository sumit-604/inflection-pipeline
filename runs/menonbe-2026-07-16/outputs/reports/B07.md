### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

- Total planned incremental capex under execution (next ~2 years, per 2A): **₹34.50cr** (Inv. Pres. slide 15).
- Historical fixed-asset turnover, FY26 (screener-data, per B01 M3): Revenue ₹293.81cr ÷ Net Block ₹119.40cr = **2.46x**.
- Implied incremental revenue = ₹34.50cr × 2.46x = **₹84.87cr**.
- As % of FY26 revenue (₹293.81cr): 84.87 ÷ 293.81 = **28.9%** incremental revenue potential embedded in the disclosed forward capex programme, IF the historical FAT ratio holds for the new capacity (a working assumption, not a company disclosure).
- Cross-check against management's own bottom-up guidance: combined forward order-pipeline figures quoted across the FY26 calls — Bi-Metal "more than ₹50cr," Alkop "~₹30cr," Brakes "~₹10cr" (all "next year and this year"/"next two years," Q4/FY26 call opening remarks) — sum to **~₹90cr**, i.e. ~30.6% of FY26 revenue, broadly consistent with the capex-implied 28.9% figure. This rough agreement is the strongest quantitative support in this scan for a real (if 🎙️-weighted) growth pipeline; it is NOT the same as it being converted into signed orders.
- capex_embedded_growth_pct carried to YAML: **28.9%** (from the FAT arithmetic; the ~30.6% pipeline figure is a secondary cross-check, not used as the headline number to avoid double-sourcing).
