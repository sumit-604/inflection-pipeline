## Business Understanding Narrative

Wendt India makes the things that grind other things. Super abrasives, meaning
diamond and cubic boron nitride wheels and tools, are 63% of consolidated
revenue (Result Q4FY26 p7). It also builds grinding machines and accessories,
and makes precision components. It works out of Sipcot, Hosur, with a
registered office in Bangalore. It owns two wholly owned subsidiaries: Wendt
Grinding Technologies Ltd in Thailand and Wendt GmbH in Germany (Result Q4FY26
p10). Abrasives are consumables. A customer who specifies a Wendt wheel on a
process buys it again every few weeks. So revenue is an installed base of
qualified processes, and margin is the spec premium over an ordinary wheel.

What is changing is not a transition. It is a deterioration. Consolidated
revenue was flat in FY26, up 1.1% to Rs 236.3 crore. Consolidated profit fell
63%, from Rs 39.5 crore to Rs 14.6 crore (Result Q4FY26 p7). Earnings per share
went from Rs 197.43 to Rs 72.75. One segment caused all of it.

Why now: the Machines and Accessories segment collapsed. Its revenue fell 27%,
from Rs 46.5 crore to Rs 33.9 crore, and its result swung from a Rs 8.5 crore
profit to a Rs 13.6 crore loss (Result Q4FY26 p7). A Rs 22 crore swing on a
company earning Rs 14.6 crore. Super Abrasives held up, earning Rs 28.4 crore
on Rs 148.9 crore of revenue, a 19.1% segment margin.

What must be true for a thesis here: the machines loss must be a fixable event
rather than a structural exit, and the abrasives core must keep its 19% margin.
Neither can be tested. The company has published no transcript, and no
presentation since May 2025.

What breaks it: nothing needs to break. It has already happened, and no one
from the company has explained it. That is the finding.

The economic engine is Super Abrasives. It is 63% of revenue and 154% of the

subsidiary is a distributor, not a plant: "Sales and Distribution of Coated,
Bonded and Super Abrasives" (Acquisition p2).
Archetype from the library: **build-to-spec component maker**, read as a
consumable. Design-win pipeline and content per unit are the right levers,
where the design win is process qualification at a customer's grinding station.

## 3. Competitive advantages
Real, and narrowing. A 19.1% segment margin on super abrasives, held while
group profit fell 63%, says the abrasives franchise has pricing power its
sibling segments do not. Switching costs are genuine: an abrasive qualified
into a precision grinding process is not swapped casually.
The balance sheet is the other advantage. Borrowings were nil at 31 March 2026,
against Rs 253.9 crore of consolidated equity, Rs 28.0 crore of cash and
Rs 47.4 crore of current investments (Result Q4FY26 p8). The company paid
Rs 30 per share of dividend for FY26 (Result Q4FY26 p6). It does not need
anyone's money.
Against that, scale is small and shrinking in two of four segments, and the
Thai arm has not grown: THB 91.3 million, 88.7 million and 94.7 million across
FY24, FY25 and FY26 (Acquisition p3). A moat is **present in one segment,
absent in the rest**.

Derived, not quoted: FY26 consolidated EBITDA was Rs 32.0 crore on a 13.6%
margin, against Rs 52.6 crore and 22.5% in FY25. Return on equity fell from
16.2% to 5.7%.
Balance sheet: zero borrowings, equity Rs 253.9 crore, total assets Rs 305.6
crore (Result Q4FY26 p8). Inventories rose from Rs 37.5 crore to Rs 53.1
crore while revenue was flat. Trade receivables fell from Rs 68.4 crore to
Rs 54.2 crore.
Cash: operating cash flow fell from Rs 33.6 crore to Rs 21.9 crore (Result
Q4FY26 p9). Still positive, still covering the Rs 8.0 crore dividend.
Depreciation rose 46%, from Rs 10.1 crore to Rs 14.7 crore, against flat
revenue. FY25 carried a Rs 35.9 crore intangible purchase (Result Q4FY26 p9),
and the amortisation of it is now running through the P&L.
Classification: **margin-led decline, with an asset event behind it**. Revenue
did not fall. Margin did, and part of the fall is the depreciation and
amortisation of a large FY25 intangible now hitting a flat revenue base.
Cash conversion: POSITIVE but weakening. Rs 21.9 crore of operating cash on
Rs 14.6 crore of profit.

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS.**
Not on company quality. Wendt has zero debt, a Big Four auditor, a 19% margin
in its core franchise, and a 45-year record. It pays dividends out of real
cash. Nothing here suggests a bad business.
It fails the strategy. This funnel hunts a transition the market has not
repriced. Wendt has no transition. It has a 63% profit decline, one segment
bleeding, no earnings call in its history, no presentation for sixteen months,
and no filed result for five months. A transition thesis cannot be built, let
alone tested, on a company that says nothing.
