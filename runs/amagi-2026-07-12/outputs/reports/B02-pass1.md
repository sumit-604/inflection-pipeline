## AUDIT TRAIL / INTERNAL CONTROLS — DETAILED (Note 52, p.376-377; Annexure VII, p.378)

For **every one of the three audited annual periods** (FY23, FY24, FY25), the statutory auditor (S.R. Batliboi & Associates LLP) issued **qualified/modified remarks under the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014 Rule 11(g) reporting** on:
1. **No India-located backup servers** for daily backup of books of account maintained in electronic mode — true in FY23 (whole company), FY24 (3 applications), and FY25 (4 applications) — i.e. this deficiency has NOT been remediated across three consecutive audit cycles, and if anything the number of non-compliant applications INCREASED from FY24 to FY25.
2. **Audit trail (edit log) feature not enabled** for certain accounting software applications in FY25 (2 applications fully; 3 applications for privileged/admin-access changes specifically) and FY24 (3 applications via third-party SOC-report gaps) — auditor states they are "unable to comment upon whether... any instances of audit trail feature were being tampered with" for the non-enabled applications (i.e., a genuine audit-scope limitation, not merely a disclosure technicality).
3. **Prior-year audit trail not preserved** as required by record-retention rules, in at least one software application in FY25.

Management response each year: "is in the process of taking steps to ensure that the books of account are maintained as required under applicable statute" (same boilerplate language repeated FY23/FY24/FY25) — i.e., **no evidence of actual remediation timeline or completion across three years of the same finding.**

🔴🔴 **RED FLAG — HIGH PRIORITY.** This is a company-wide books-of-account and IT-general-controls deficiency, independently confirmed by the statutory auditor's modified opinion (not merely a company self-disclosure), persisting unremediated across the ENTIRE three-year audited track record presented in the Prospectus. This does not indicate fraud, but it materially weakens the evidentiary reliability of every other number in these financial statements — an unenabled or non-preserved audit trail means transactions could theoretically have been altered without a recoverable log, and the auditor could not rule this out for the affected applications. For a pipeline that requires every number to carry a source anchor and treats INDETERMINATE evidence as capping the verdict, this finding should propagate as a flag against overall evidentiary confidence in the notes-derived numbers, not just as a one-line governance comment.
