Message from the Board
FY2026 was another year of strong execution and profitable growth for Dynacons. Revenue 
from Operations grew 12.4% YoY to ₹1,424 crore, while EBITDA increased 41.4% YoY to 
₹146 crore, resulting in EBITDA margin expansion to 10.2%. The year reflects the strength 
of our integrated solutions platform, disciplined execution, and increasing contribution 
from higher-value infrastructure and managed services engagements.
As of 31 March 2026, the Company’s order book stood at ₹2,230 crore, providing strong 
revenue visibility. Supported by continued order inflows, the order book further increased 
to ₹2,964 crore as of 30 May 2026, reinforcing confidence in our growth outlook and 
reflecting the strength of our market positioning across key technology segments. During 
the year, we secured several marquee wins across the BFSI and public sector segments, 
including cloud infrastructure, SD-WAN, Digital Workplace Solutions, Core Banking as a 
Service, and Device-as-a-Service projects. These wins reinforce our strong positioning in 
mission-critical enterprise IT transformation programs.
Demand momentum remains robust across data centre modernization, cloud 
infrastructure, cybersecurity, managed services, and AI-ready technology environments. 
The increasing adoption of AI workloads, automation, and data-driven operations is 
accelerating investments in modern digital infrastructure, creating significant opportunities 
for Dynacons to support customers through their transformation journey.
With strong execution capabilities, deep relationships with leading technology partners, a 
nationwide delivery footprint, and a growing portfolio of recurring services, Dynacons 
remains well positioned to capitalize on emerging opportunities while continuing to drive 
sustainable growth and profitability.
