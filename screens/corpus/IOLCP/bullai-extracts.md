## Business model and capacity
- Two segments, pharmaceuticals (APIs) and chemicals. Pharma share of segment revenue
  FY23 57%, FY24 59%, FY25 58%, FY26 60%. (Q1FY27 deck, p.12)
- "Largest producer of Ibuprofen... backward integrated with ~35% Global share", capacity
  "12000+ MT". (Q2FY23 deck, p.4)
- Non-ibuprofen capacities and filings: Metformin Hydrochloride 7,200 MTPA (USDMF, CEP,
  ANVISA GMP approved, China DMF active); Fenofibrate 276 MTPA (same set); Paracetamol
  10,800 MTPA (USDMF under review, CEP and ANVISA approved); Levetiracetam multiproduct
  plant; Clopidogrel Bisulphate 240 MTPA. (Q1FY27 deck, p.12)
- Chemicals: ethyl acetate raised to 1,20,000 MTPA, acetic anhydride to 32,000 MTPA,
  Triacetin 6,000 MTPA installed, Pantoprazole enhanced to 276 MTPA. (FY26 deck, p.10)
- "100,000 TPA capacity for Ethyl Acetate and a REACH certification enabling exports to
  more than 40 countries... key supplier to... pharmaceuticals, packaging, textiles,
  paints, food processing, and inks." (AR FY2025, p.53)
- R&D: 114-member team including over 50 scientists, DSIR recognised, 16 USFDA DMFs,
  19 CEPs with EDQM, 3 patents, 20 products commercialised, 8 in pipeline. (AR FY2025,
  p.53). "in R&D, we are having around 2% to 3% expenses annually." (Q4FY26 call, p.12)

## The transition, quoted
- "The non-Ibuprofen share in the pharmaceuticals segment has increased from 18% to 37%
  over the past six years, with a strong trajectory to further drive revenue and margin
  expansion over the next 2-3 years." (Q1FY27 deck, p.31; repeated AR FY2026 summary in
  FY26 deck, p.29)
- "For FY '29 only after three to four years, we are expecting that non-Ibu segment will
  contribute around 50% to 55% of our API segment with good margin equivalent to the other
  Ibuprofen margins." (Q1FY27 call, p.9)
- "we are targeting to further have 50-50 ratio for the '27 from ibuprofen and
  non-ibuprofen... around INR1,800 crores comes from the API business and INR900 crores
  will come from the chemical business." (Q3FY26 call, p.10)
- "Target non-Ibuprofen business to contribute 50% to total pharma business" and
  "accelerate our DMF filing to cover 5-6 products per year". (Q2FY23 deck, p.30)
- Increase in non-ibuprofen revenue "from Rs 35 crore in FY18 to Rs 431 crore in FY24".
  (FY24 deck, p.29)

## Proof points and margin mechanics
- "with Q1 EBITDA margin at 14.6%, like ahead of the FY27 guidance range of 14% to 15%...
  the key factor for this rise in EBITDA margin are higher capacity utilization, better
  product mix, operational efficiencies, and also stronger non-ibu segment API demand.
  Also some improvements in the finished product prices." (Q1FY27 call, p.6)
- Pharma grew "around 42%... Y-o-Y" in Q1FY27; on the volume-versus-realisation split
  management would not give exact numbers. (Q1FY27 call, p.6)
- "In the non-ibu portfolio, the paracetamol turnover contributed mainly for this
  increase." (Q1FY27 call, p.11)
- Cost pass-through: "prices of power cost, logistic cost... increased in, during the
  quarter, but we are able to pass on the major part of the increased cost to the
  customers. So we have no major material impact on our business." (Q1FY27 call, p.11)
- Q4FY26: revenue Rs 619 cr (+17.4% YoY), "highest-ever quarterly revenue"; EBITDA Rs 94
  cr, +40%, margin +251 bps to 15.2%; FY26 capex Rs 160 cr "funded entirely through
  internal accruals". (Q4FY26 call, p.4)
- Ethyl acetate "increased our capacity from 1,10,000 to 1,20,000 tons or so", running at
  "98% to 100% capacity utilization". (Q4FY26 call, p.8)

## The cyclical constraint on the core
- "the prices of ibuprofen has corrected to some extent from the last quarter also,
  primarily domestic prices. This may be the reason that SMS Pharma has launched Ibuprofen
  from the last one and a half years. The prices, we think are at the bottom for the
  domestic market and as regards to the total capacity of Ibuprofen, it is somewhere near
  35,000 ton globally and capacity is running at, as far as IOL is concerned, it is 75 to
  80 percent." Export prices "more than 10 dollar", domestic "around 9 to 9.25 dollar".
  (Q3FY25 call, p.10)
- "Ibu in the sole market, we are maintaining the numbers. In domestic, it is already
  bottomed out". No new ibuprofen plant ordered by IOL in Europe, US or elsewhere.
  (Q3FY25 call, p.14)
- "the annual growth rate of 3% to 4% every year for ibuprofen demand also."
  (Q4FY26 call, p.16)
- "on a sustainable basis having around 85% to 90% capacity utilization. But we don't know
  how the others are doing." (Q4FY25 call, p.7)
- FY25 outlook as filed: "Expectations of sluggish global growth, oversupplied industry
  situation and ongoing conflict in the Red Sea likely to impact margin recovery in H1
  FY25"; recovery would need "a rebound in demand from China and permanent capacity
  closures to help ease oversupply conditions". (FY24 deck, p.27)
- Risk as the company states it: "Dependence on key products like Ibuprofen makes it
  vulnerable to market fluctuations and regulatory changes." Mitigation is portfolio
  diversification beyond ibuprofen. (AR FY2025, p.54)
- SCOT: strengths include "Strong backward integration capabilities", "A strong balance
  sheet with no debt"; challenges include "Volatility in the pricing of raw materials";
  FY24 version lists "Dependence on bulk drugs makes the Company vulnerable to price
  fluctuations". (AR FY2023, p.22; AR FY2024, p.51)
