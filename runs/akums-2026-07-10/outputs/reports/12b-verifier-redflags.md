## PART 1: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built from raw transcripts alone)

Each item anchored (call, speaker, printed page).

**IF1 — Schedule M: raised as a tailwind, then two consecutive quarters of
explicit refusal.** Q2, Sahil raises it unprompted as a positive: "very hopeful
that this will result in a shift in business to quality-conscious manufacturers"
(Q2, Sahil, p10). Q3, asked for ground truth: "No comment. As this is government
method, it is not appropriate to comment on it" (Q3, Sahil to Axay Shah, p11).
Q4, asked again: "I will like to skip this question because honestly, we have no
visibility on ground of how the government plans to roll this out" (Q4, Sahil to
Praveen Jayaraman, p9). Meanwhile the Q4 scripted opening still promotes it:
CDMO growth "reflects a structural strength supported by growing customer
preference for compliant manufacturers" (Q4, Sandeep Jain, p2). Promote-when-
convenient / decline-when-tested asymmetry across 2+ quarters.

**IF2 — Cash deployment: unresolved across all three quarters.** Q2 "nothing
very close which we would have it, but there are things in the pipeline which we
are actively working with bankers" (Q2, Sahil to Jay Modi, p14). Q3 "actively
evaluating multiple things... nothing as of now is binding in nature" (Q3, Sahil
to Puranwala, p6). Q4 "we expect that we'll soon utilize some parts of the cash
for growth" (Q4, Sahil to Ankur Kumar, p11). Note: on Sajal Kapoor's Q4 follow-up
management DID name the categories it evaluated and passed on — metered-dose
inhalers, oncology injectables, large molecules, and Akumentis brand/portfolio
acquisitions where "current valuations... are significantly higher than what we
currently trade at" (Q4, Sahil, pp17-18). Still no named target, size or date;
18% payout on ~INR1,682cr net cash.

**IF3 — Domestic branded (Akumentis) guidance miss.** Analyst-recalled guide
"previously you gave the guidance of mid to teen digits" (Q3, Ikshit Naredi, p14),
undisputed by management; FY26 actual +2.9% (INR446cr vs INR434cr; Q4, Sumeet, p4).
Reason surfaced only under questioning ("reducing a few HQs," then in Q4 "we
hardly took a price hike," Q4 Sahil p12).

**IF4 — CDMO volume-surge driver unexplained by management.** Q3 push: "what has
suddenly changed... we have consistently seeing issues in the volume" (Vivek
Agrawal, p6); Q4: "what it is driving is still to be thought through and looked
out" (Q4, Sahil to Aditya Sarin, p7). Growth is wallet-share deepening of
existing customers only (Q4, Sahil to Avnish Burman, p14) — durability +
concentration risk on the single largest FY26 earnings driver.

**IF5 — API loss trajectory non-monotonic despite three quarters of "improving"
language.** EBITDA -INR14cr (Q2, p5) → -INR7cr (Q3, p4) → -INR12cr (Q4, p5); FY26
-INR40cr vs FY25 -INR44cr. Management's own verdict: "this was a year of miss,
honestly" (Q4, Sahil, p8). Breakeven timeline also softened: Q3 "management
actions... to bring it a breakeven" (p11) → Q4 "we might still see some losses"
full year (p10).

**IF6 — European CDMO growth pillar is single-customer / single-plant.** "one of
the largest pharma companies globally... we will be the sole supplier... six-year
commercial supply contract" from Plant 2 in Haridwar (Q2, Sahil, p11). EUR35m/yr
MAT to Dec 2032.

**IF7 — European flagship contract is FIXED-PRICE for ~6-7 years, into an
acknowledged input-cost-inflation environment.** "for Europe, it's a fixed
pricing" (Q3, Sahil to Sangeeta, p10); "this is a fixed price contract till 2032"
(Q4, Sahil to Richa, p11); "EUR35 million for the next six years till 2032" (Q4,
p11). This removes the cost-plus pass-through protection the rest of the business
relies on — in the same call management describes a "negative loop of what's
happening in the Middle East... crude-linked inflation in solvents" it can only
"backward integrate to a certain extent" (Q4, Sajal Kapoor exchange, pp6-7).
Management claims it "already taken into our costing the inflationary patterns"
(Q4, p16), but a six-year fixed price on the #1 growth pillar is a structural
margin risk distinct from concentration.

**IF8 — FY26 operating cash flow is largely a non-recurring customer advance,
not recurring operations.** FY26 CFO INR1,181cr vs INR465cr prior, "majorly
attributable to the European contract that was assigned to the company" (Q4,
Sumeet, p4). Management itself disclosed the cleaner figure in Q2: "Excluding the
part consideration, the free cash flow was INR90 crores" (Q2, Sumeet, p4). The
advance also generates a notional interest charge (INR19cr/qtr, INR58cr in 9M;
Q2 p11, Q3 p13) that reverses against future supply. Cash-quality caveat for
downstream cash-conversion scoring.

**IF9 — Working-capital deterioration volunteered in Q4.** "slight increase in
the working capital days from 91 days to 105 days... largely due to the buildup
of inventory, which was secured by advanced payments to creditors and we wanted
to ensure the supply during the wartime" (Q4, Sumeet, p4). A volunteered negative
(14-day WC increase + advance payments to suppliers).

**IF10 — Governance: dual "Managing Director" listing in Q3 only.** Q3 roster and
the spoken introduction both name "Mr. Sanjeev Jain, Managing Director; Mr.
Sandeep Jain, Managing Director" (Q3, cover p1 and Ankit Jain, p2); Sanjeev Jain
never speaks and does not appear in the Q2 or Q4 rosters (Sandeep only). For a
promoter-family (Jain) company this is a related-party/governance item, not a
mere roster typo, since it appears in the live introduction too.

**IF11 — Dropped trigger: Jammu plant.** Raised Q2 ("we were looking at setting
up a plant in Jammu... given the legislation change that is on hold," Sumeet,
p10); never mentioned again in Q3/Q4.

**IF12 — New trigger appearing only in Q4: GLP-1.** "GLP is a large and a massive
opportunity... still evaluating when to enter... pricing still remains very
volatile and going down south... we'll inform in our subsequent calls" (Q4, Sahil
to Puranwala, p17). No prior mention Q2/Q3.

## PART 3: PROMISE-DELIVERY SPOT CHECKS (5 checked)

   Outcome: CDMO EBITDA margin 84/804=10.4% (Q2) → 126/916=13.75% (Q3) →

   Promise: Q2 "INR107 crores [H1]... INR100 crores, INR125-odd crores more for
   the next six months" (Sumeet, p13). Outcome: "FY26, we did a capex of INR222
   crores" (Q4, p13). 222 within range. CONFIRMED.
3. **"domestic branded mid-teens" → missed (2.9%).** Promise analyst-referenced
   and undisputed (Q3 Naredi, p14); outcome INR446 vs INR434 = 2.9% (Q4 p4).
   CONFIRMED (B05 correctly labels the promise "referenced/implied").
4. **"CDMO volume sustained double digits" 7%→16%+→25%+ → delivered (flagged).**
   Q2 "over 7%" volume (p3); Q3 "16.3%" CDMO growth "driven by double-digit
   volume" (pp2,4); Q4 "volume variance north of 25%" two quarters (p7).
   CONFIRMED direction. Minor imprecision: B05's 7/16/25 series mixes a volume %,
   a revenue-growth %, and a volume-variance % — accelerating but not like-for-like.
5. **"Plant 2 EU GMP approval by Q4 CY2025" → "partial / ~1-month slip."**
   Promise: Q2 "expected to get the approval in Q4 of this year" (Sandeep, p3).
   Outcome: "in January, received the EU GMP audit of Plant 2" (Q3, p8).
   CONFIRMED the approval landed in Jan 2026. CAVEAT: "Q4 of this year" plausibly
   meant fiscal Q4 (Jan-Mar 2026), in which case Jan 2026 is ON TIME, not a slip;
   B05's "partial/1-month slip" is a defensible but slightly harsh reading. Not
   material either way.
