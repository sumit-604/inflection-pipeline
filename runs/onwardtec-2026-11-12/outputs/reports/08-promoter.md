What is WEB-DERIVED (📰, aggregator Trendlyne via search summary, ❓ on the arithmetic):
- Mar 2021 promoter holding 61.71%, 1,00,82,281 shares. Trendlyne series then reads 45.79% at Mar 2022 and 45.07% at Jun 2023, 38.12% at Mar 2024. The same snapshot lists a 57.64% value for Jul 2021 that does not fit the series, so the quarter-by-quarter path is unreliable.
- Convergent Finance release of 27 May 2021 (syndicated ANI release, ThePrint): up to INR 702 million (USD 9.6 million), Convergent as advisor to Infinity Investment Management, about 24.78% on a fully diluted basis; Business Standard headline "raises Rs 70 crore from funds advised by Convergent Finance" 📰. Arithmetic ties to the EGM terms: 11 lakh x Rs 130 = Rs 14.3 Cr and 43 lakh x Rs 130 = Rs 55.9 Cr, total Rs 70.2 Cr.

DERIVED (mine; ❓ until the Mar 2022 to Dec 2023 shareholding patterns are read):
- Mar 2021 share count = 1,00,82,281 / 61.71% = about 1.634 Cr. Mar 2024 share count = 85.84 lakh / 38.12% = about 2.252 Cr. Count rose by about 61.8 lakh.
- If promoters had held 1,00,82,281 shares unchanged, they would hold 44.8% of 2.252 Cr. So about 16.9 points of the 23.6 point fall (61.71 to 38.12) is dilution, and about 6.7 points is a reduction in the promoters' own share count of about 15 lakh shares (100.8 lakh to 85.8 lakh, down 14.9%).
- Dilution reconciles only if the full 54 lakh instruments (11 lakh shares plus 43 lakh warrants) were issued: 1.634 + 0.54 = 2.174 Cr, plus ESOP and other issues. The Mar 2022 value of 45.79% on an unchanged promoter count implies about 2.20 Cr shares, which fits. Infinity Direct then held 39 lakh at Mar 2024, which is 15 lakh fewer than 54 lakh. So either part of the warrant shares went to another holder or Infinity sold about 15 lakh shares. NOT FOUND which.
- The trend data place the promoter reduction between Jun 2023 (45.07%) and Mar 2024 (38.12%) (aggregator, ❓). Buyer, price and route (open market, block, off-market, inter-se to JHM) are NOT FOUND. Search found no Reg 29 or bulk-deal record for 2023. The Oct 2024 Reg 29(2) filing by "Infinity Direct Holdings & PACs" (BSE listing seen 3 Oct 2024) matches the Sep 2024 split, not a sale.

Verdict on the mechanism: the 2021 fall to about 45% is a clean dilution by a primary issue to a PE investor, filed and priced at Rs 130, with no promoter participation and an investor board seat. The further fall from about 45% to 38.12% is a secondary sell-down of about 15 lakh promoter shares in or around FY24 whose mechanism is not in the corpus. Since Mar 2024 the promoters have bought every period. The single observation that separates "orderly partial exit to fund diversification" from "unexplained insider sale" is the Reg 29(2) / Reg 31(4) filings and bulk-deal data for Jun 2023 to Mar 2024. Without them, the sell-down stays a caution, not an adverse finding. No pledge was ever found.

Rights of the Infinity block (asked by B03): board seat for Convergent's founder, no fees, "as per Investment Agreement"; Articles amended to carry Investment Agreement terms (EGM21 p.4). Further rights (vetoes, ROFR, tag-along, exit rights, nominee threshold) are NOT FOUND. Infinity Direct is the only entity the AR names as "significant influence" besides JHM (AR26 Note 27 p.87). Dividends paid to Infinity: Rs 99.00 + 96.00 = 195.00 in FY26, same total as FY25 195.00 (AR26 Note 27 p.88 ✅).
The 17.37% block is an overhang: no volume data is held, so the overhang size in days is NOT FOUND. Position-sizing implication only.
