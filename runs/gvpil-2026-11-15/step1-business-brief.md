14. THE ENGINE: a shift from a loss-making FGD and new-build EPC legacy to an asset-light, services-led core. The core is aftermarket spares, R&M and life extension on the coal fleet, plus third-party (non-OEM) services. It runs on a net-cash balance sheet once Durgapur leaves. The evidence: core services backlog rose (Rs 1,124 cr to Rs 1,218 cr) while the total fell, and the normalised margin is 11%. India's thermal capex revival (the "60+ GW pipeline", FY25 AR p.17) is the second reading. The observation that separates them: core services inflow and normalised margin in H2 FY27, after one-offs. Q2 FY27 operating margin of 9.8% sits below the 11% base.
