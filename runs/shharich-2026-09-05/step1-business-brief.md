# STEP 1 BUSINESS BRIEF — Shree Hari Chemicals Export Ltd (SHHARICH)

Run date: 2026-09-05. CMP Rs 248 (04-Sep-2026). Market cap ~Rs 156 cr. P/E
~13.3. Listed since 1987 (BSE 524336). This brief is COMPANY MEMORY, weighed,
never anchored evidence. Every stage number still comes from this run's filings.

## WHAT IT MAKES AND SELLS
- B2B maker and exporter of dye intermediates. Core product is H-Acid, a key
  intermediate for reactive and acid dyes used in textile dyeing. One of the
  largest H-Acid producers in India. Factory at Mahad, Raigad, Maharashtra.
  Incorporated 1987. [screener.in/company/524336](https://www.screener.in/company/524336/consolidated/)
- Wholly owned subsidiary Shakambhari Dyechem Pvt Ltd (SDPL) is the expansion
  vehicle. The company subscribed Rs 4.95 cr to SDPL's rights issue on
  23-Mar-2026 to fund SDPL capex and working capital. [equitybulls](https://www.equitybulls.com/category.php?id=368222)

## REVENUE MIX AND WHERE THE MARGIN COMES FROM
- H-Acid dominates the mix. This is a spread business: margin is output H-Acid
  price minus input (naphthalene chain) cost, not pricing power. A commodity
  converter, cyclical, not a specialty franchise.
- Consolidated FY26 revenue Rs 184 cr, up ~30%; net profit Rs 4.1 cr, DOWN ~19%
  on cost pressure and a labour-code provision. FY25 revenue Rs 141 cr, profit
  Rs 5.1 cr. [whalesbook FY26](https://www.whalesbook.com/corporate-news/English/chemicals/Shree-Hari-Chemicals-FY26-Revenue-Soars-30percent-Profit-Hit-by-Labour-Code-Costs/6a06fcb7e125a601ebdb12f4)
- Quarters swing hard with the H-Acid spread. Jun-2025 was a loss (-Rs 1.67 cr);
  Sep-2025 was strong (sales Rs 74.5 cr). Revenue and profit track the cycle,
  not a steady ramp. [screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/524336/consolidated/)
- ROCE 11.4%, ROE 11.0%, near cost of capital. Book value Rs 80.2, so the stock
  trades ~3.1x book. Debt Rs 29 cr (Mar-2026). No dividend despite profits.
  [screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/524336/)

## CUSTOMER AND EXPORT PROFILE
- Export-led "Export" in the name; B2B supply to dye and textile-chemical
  makers. H-Acid is a globally traded intermediate; Indian prices move with
  Chinese supply and global H-Acid price hikes. [msn / cbinsights sector](https://www.msn.com/en-in/money/other/chemical-stocks-surge-kiri-industries-bodal-bhageria-hit-upper-circuits-amid-global-h-acid-price-hike/ar-AA2aOmJh)

## LATEST PRINT AND THE INFLECTION
- Q1 FY27 (quarter to Jun-2026): consolidated sales Rs 54.91 cr, up ~126% YoY;
  net profit Rs 5.92 cr, versus a loss a year earlier. A sharp swing up.
  [business-standard Q1FY27](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/shree-hari-chemicals-export-reports-consolidated-net-profit-of-rs-5-92-crore-in-the-june-2026-quarter-126081401620_1.html)
- 25-Aug-2026: board approved a Rs 40.24 cr preferential issue of 22,85,000
  convertible warrants at Rs 176.10 to promoter-group members (Priyamvada,
  Avanticka, Smradhi Agarwal), plus expansion in existing and new units for dye
  intermediates and other organic/inorganic chemicals. Promoter stakes rise
  materially. [equitybulls warrants](https://www.equitybulls.com/category.php?id=374487)

THE ONE INFLECTION: promoter-funded expansion (Rs 40.24 cr warrants + SDPL
capex) that lifts H-Acid and new-intermediate volume, so a cyclical Rs 180 cr
converter grows into a larger, steadier chemicals base. It is a climb only if
the Q1 FY27 turnaround is added VOLUME from expansion, not a one-off H-Acid
price spike. A converter riding the spread cycle is not a transition.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority)
1. VOLUME VS SPREAD. The Q1 FY27 jump (sales +126%, profit swing to Rs 5.92 cr)
   must be split into H-Acid price/spread versus real volume growth. If it is
   the spread cycle, spot-year ROCE and margin must NOT feed Section 1B or FTTCP
   (v3.7 Amendment 17 converter rule). Verify volume, realisation, and spread
   from the FY26 AR, the Q1 FY27 results, and any concall.
2. EXPANSION AND CAPITAL ALLOCATION. Verify the Rs 40.24 cr warrant terms, the
   allottees, the pricing (Rs 176.10 vs CMP Rs 248), and the actual capacity
   and product mix (H-Acid vs new intermediates) funded through SDPL. Confirm
   from the Reg 30 board-outcome filing and the postal-ballot/EGM notice.
3. CASH CONVERSION AND CAPITALISED INTEREST. Screener flags possible
   capitalisation of interest and no dividend despite profits. Verify operating
   cash flow versus reported PAT, receivables and inventory trend, and whether
   interest is being capitalised. This is the FLAG-CASH input.
4. SUBSIDIARY AND RELATED PARTY. SDPL drives the consolidated-vs-standalone gap
   and is the expansion vehicle. Verify SDPL's financials, the related-party
   transactions with the parent, and the promoter warrant flow into the group.

## PEERS SELECTED (economic-engine match: H-Acid / dye-intermediate converters,
spread-driven and cyclical, small to mid-cap)
- BODALCHEM (Bodal Chemicals) — the largest Indian dye-intermediate maker
  (H-Acid, vinyl sulphone, K-acid). The direct benchmark for the H-Acid spread
  cycle and cost curve. ROCE 5.6% now shows the cycle trough.
  [screener.in/company/BODALCHEM](https://www.screener.in/company/BODALCHEM/consolidated/)
- AKSHARCHEM (AksharChem India) — H-Acid and vinyl sulphone plus pigments and
  precipitated silica; the closest small-cap structural comp on product and
  size. ROCE 26.9%. [screener.in/company/AKSHARCHEM](https://www.screener.in/company/AKSHARCHEM/consolidated/)
- SHREEPUSHK (Shree Pushkar Chemicals) — integrated dyes, dye intermediates and
  fertilisers; a small/mid-cap dye-chem converter with the same spread economics.
  ROCE 13.0%. [screener.in/company/SHREEPUSHK](https://www.screener.in/company/SHREEPUSHK/consolidated/)

Kiri Industries was considered but is distorted by its Longsheng litigation.
Bhageria Industries was considered but has pivoted into solar EPC, so its
margin and capex dynamics no longer mirror a dye-intermediate converter. The
three chosen mirror the H-Acid spread engine the thesis rests on.
