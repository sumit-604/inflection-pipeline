3. Brief profile  Arun Chandavarkar retired as CEO & Joint Managing Director 
of Biocon Limited on 30 November 2019 after a 29 -year 
career. During his tenure, Chandavarkar was instrumental in 
transforming Biocon from an enzymes business into a 
globally recognized biopharma company. He contributed to 
its 2004 IPO and led key initiatives including biologics 
partnerships, major R&D and infrastructure investments, 
divestment of the enzymes business, and organizational 
restructuring to support growth. 
He holds a B.Tech. in Chemical Engineering from Indian 
Institute of Technology Bombay and a PhD in Chemical 
Engineering from Massachusetts Institute of Technology. He 
is a former Chairperson of the biotechnology committee at 
Confederation of Indian Industry and serves as a non -
executive director at Biocon Biologics and an independent 
director at Mynvax Private Limited.
