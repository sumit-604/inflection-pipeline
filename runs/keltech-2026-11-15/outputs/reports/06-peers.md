## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

**2A Demand.** Split by segment. Commercial explosives at the leader: weak FY26 (flat coal and overburden, p.10 Q3FY26), strong H1 FY27 on a rain-light monsoon (SOLARINDS Q2FY27, p.5), with Q2 FY27 domestic value up about 57% (derived, Q1). Analyst states Coal India "record offtake" (Amit Dixit, SOLARINDS Q2FY27, p.5), not management. Management expects "growth momentum will be better in the second half" (Nuwal, p.5). Defence energetics: consensus on strong demand, constrained by export licences (Premier Q1FY27, p.11) and trials (Solar). Premier revenue fell 28% in Q1 FY27 on dispatch and licence delays, not demand (PREMEXPLN Q1FY27, Chowdary, pp.3, 9). Seasonality: Q2 is an off-season lean quarter for commercial explosives (APOLLO Q2FY27, Sai Kumar, p.4). Keltech's Q2 FY27 revenue growth of +36.9% in the monsoon quarter matches the leader's direction.

**2B Pricing and input costs.** Peers describe a price-led year: AN, gas and crude lifted selling prices 18% to 20% at Solar (SOLARINDS Q4FY26, p.7). Escalation clauses and inventory protected Solar's margin (28.5% in Q4 FY26, 29.02% in Q2 FY27; SOLARINDS Q4FY26, p.3; Q2FY27, p.3). Premier's fixed contracts did not: EBITDA fell from 15% to 6% (PREMEXPLN Q1FY27, Deepak/Kumar, pp.11-12). Keltech EBITDA ex other income 8.21% in Q2 FY27 (B05 trigger 3) is far below Solar's consolidated mix margin, but Solar's margin is driven by defence and international mix, so it is not a like-for-like benchmark. The IDL and Premier evidence is the better comparator for Keltech's bulk-led margin.

**2C Capex cycle.** Not a lone expander. It is a leader-led capacity race. Solar spends Rs 2,050 Cr a year including regional explosives plants; IDL builds silos and plans 15 plants; Premier is stuck on land (Andhra Pradesh 400 acres on price, Odisha land rejected; PREMEXPLN Q4FY26, Chowdary, pp.12-14). Keltech's Rs 190 Cr TNT plant is therefore a small player's bet in a segment where an Indian competitor (Apollo/IDL, 500 t TNT licence) is also building, not a lone expansion in a cautious set.

**2D Competitive mentions.** No peer names Keltech, perlite or Chowgule in any of the 12 transcripts. Silence is uninformative: Keltech is small and the peers are not its main competitors on disclosed lines. The peer-to-peer link that matters is Apollo's purchase of 41.33% of Premier for about Rs 1,550 Cr (APOLLO Q1FY27, p.3; CCI approval APOLLO Q2FY27, p.2), which signals that listed defence energetics assets command a strategic price, and which merges IDL and Premier energetics under one owner.

**2E Risks peers discuss that B05 does not list** (B05 red_flags: TNT pace, segment basis, cash, finance cost, no calls, family settlement):
1. Plant accidents and licence suspension at explosives sites: Premier had two accidents in FY26, one at the flares plant and one at Katepally; licences were suspended (PREMEXPLN Q4FY26, Chowdary, pp.6, 11, 17).
2. Export and import licence delay or rejection: an order was cancelled for an export licence (PREMEXPLN Q4FY26, Varun Jain, p.7); licence clock 3 to 5 months (Q3FY26, p.9).
3. Shipping and maritime disruption delaying imported plant components (PREMEXPLN Q1FY27, Chowdary, p.8).
4. Qualification and inspection time after erection: plant and product qualification 1 to 2 months (PREMEXPLN Q4FY26, p.7); pre-dispatch inspection timing (p.9).
5. Demand destruction from high AN prices for a few months (SOLARINDS Q4FY26, Nuwal, p.12).
6. New entrant risk, for example Kalyani (SOLARINDS Q4FY26, Umesh Raut, p.7).
