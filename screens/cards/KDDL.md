## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** KDDL has two businesses. The standalone company makes watch components, bracelets, precision engineering parts and luxury packaging. FY26 standalone revenue was Rs 506 crore, up 31.9%, with EBITDA of Rs 116.9 crore (Q4 FY26 call, doc 44af3e82, page 5). The larger business is Ethos, a listed subsidiary that retails luxury watches through more than 100 boutiques (page 9). Consolidated FY26 revenue was Rs 2,153.4 crore (Q4 FY26 investor presentation, doc 0797cf68, page 7).

**What is changing?** Scale. Revenue grew 30.7% in FY26 against a guide of 15% to 20% (doc 0797cf68, page 7; Q4 FY25 call, doc 582181e0, page 5). Ethos plans to double its boutique network in three years (doc 44af3e82, page 9). KDDL bought the Swiss brand Favre-Leuba and expects its sales to "more than double" in FY27 (page 4).

**Why now?** Q1 FY27 consolidated PAT rose 50.7% to Rs 44.7 crore on revenue up 36.3% (Q1 FY27 investor presentation, doc 96ba2a93, page 6, full page; page 18). That repairs a weak FY26 bottom line.

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** Store growth must convert to profit. FY26 revenue grew 30.7% while PAT fell 5%. Depreciation rose 42% to Rs 122.4 crore from Rs 86.1 crore as stores opened (doc 96ba2a93, page 18). New boutiques must mature fast enough to carry that charge.

**What breaks it?** The holding structure and the price. Minorities own about 40% to 49% of Ethos (derived from the 61.07% and 51.34% stakes), so a large share of group profit is not KDDL's. On attributable profit the forward multiple is 43x to 45x.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Two engines. Ethos retails luxury watches through boutiques. Its engine is same store sales, store count and inventory turn. Inventory fell to 221 days at cost from 247 (doc 44af3e82, page 14).

The standalone engine is precision manufacturing for export. Precision engineering revenue was Rs 200 crore against Rs 147 crore (doc 44af3e82, page 5). Q4 FY26 standalone EBITDA margin was 25.1% (doc 0797cf68, page 5).

**Archetype: Brand/franchise consumer**, luxury watch retail through Ethos, with a precision components arm. The binding variables are volume growth, pricing power, distribution reach, gross margin and store productivity. For the listed parent it behaves as a holding company on Ethos.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Present. Ethos holds brand partnerships and more than 100 boutiques (doc 44af3e82, page 9). A new entrant needs years to build both. That is scarcity at the distribution layer.

The components arm sells to specification for export. Its Q4 FY26 margin of 25.1% suggests some value from specification work. Pricing power at Ethos is not shown in what was read. Verdict: **moat present in distribution, forming in components**.
