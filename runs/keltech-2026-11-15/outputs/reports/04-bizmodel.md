### 1E The chai-stall-uncle version
Keltech is a big kitchen that cooks a dangerous powder for people who break rocks. It buys one main ingredient, ammonium nitrate, at a price it cannot control. About 67 paise of every rupee it earns goes back out for that ingredient (AR p.99). It then keeps about 30 paise before wages, transport and other costs, and about 5 paise as final profit (S3). Think of a tiffin caterer who must buy rice at market price and sell lunches at a price set by big office clients. The caterer lives on volume and on not wasting rice. Right now the kitchen is building a second, bigger kitchen for a new dish (the TNT plant), paid mostly with a bank loan (AR p.106; CRISIL p.2). Nobody outside the company knows yet who will eat the new dish.
