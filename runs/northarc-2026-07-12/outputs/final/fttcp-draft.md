## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

What sustains growth. The balance sheet can fund it: CRAR is 22.6% against a roughly 15% floor, so the guided AUM doubling by FY29 needs funding access, not fresh capital (B09, Investor Presentation p.37). The D2C channel, the higher-yield half, grew from 51.8% to 59% of the book and management targets 65-70% (B03, B05). What confirms it firing: FY27 AUM growth at or above 20% with the D2C mix past 62%. What kills it: a funding-market squeeze that forces the book to slow, or MFI and consumer sector stress cutting disbursements.

What holds the spread. Cost of funds fell to an incremental 8.5-8.6% and the D2C mix lifts blended yield (B05, Investor Presentation p.37). What confirms it: NIM holding at or above 9.4% through FY27 while cost of funds stays under 8.6%. What kills it: the rate cycle compressing asset yields faster than funding cost falls, or the funding-cost advantage eroding as peers keep improving (two peers already guide further declines, B06).

What would turn asset quality, the swing factor. The documented catalyst is the Stage II early-bucket improvement, from 2.6% to 1.5% of book, and a credit cost that already eased to 2.8% (Investor Presentation p.28, p.37). What confirms it firing: two consecutive quarters of credit cost at or below 2.8% with GNPA at or below 1.2% and provision coverage rebuilding above 50%. What kills it: credit cost back above 3.0%, or coverage falling further while GNPA rises, which would show the FY26 improvement was buffer drawdown. Absence signal to watch: the FY27 credit-cost guide has already been revised three times without reconciling, and the Pragati Finserv and Aviom HFC exposures went quiet on the Q4 call. Continued silence on either is a negative signal in its own right.

What lifts returns. RoA and RoE recover on three levers: credit-cost normalisation, operating leverage as the cost-to-income ratio holds near 35.6%, and deployment of the IPO equity that currently dilutes RoE (Investor Presentation p.37). What confirms it: FY27 exit RoE holding above 14%. What kills it: credit cost re-accelerating, which is the shared catalyst that would sink both returns and asset quality at once.

Catalyst honesty note: no catalyst was invented to fill a table. The asset-quality recovery rests on genuine but contested evidence and is scored STAGNANT for that reason, not promoted.
