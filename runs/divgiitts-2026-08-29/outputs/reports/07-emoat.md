### 2B. Utilisation trajectory per facility

- **Transfer Case**: ~50% utilisation at Q3FY26 volumes (13,500-15,000 units/quarter against an 8,000-9,000 units/month achievable capacity, 🎙️ Q3FY26 call, per B05); management itself flags that pushing utilisation past ~70% "may not be at the current profit margins... there may be price pressures" (🎙️, a candid disclosure).

- **Fixed Asset Turnover Ratio** (company-wide, 📄 AR Financial Ratios, p.144): FY21 1.16x → FY22 1.30x → FY23 peak 1.39x → FY24 0.97x → FY25 low 0.63x → FY26 recovering to 0.92x. This shows capacity was added well ahead of utilisation for several years (FY24-FY25), with FY26 the first year of documented recovery.

### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Total capex under execution (contracted, not yet spent) = Rs 50.52 cr (📄 AR Note 38, capital commitments as at 31-Mar-2026).
Historical fixed asset turnover (most recent, FY26) = 0.92x (📄 AR Financial Ratios, p.144).

Implied incremental revenue = Rs 50.52 cr × 0.92 = **Rs 46.5 cr**.
As % of FY26 revenue from operations (Rs 352.9 cr, 📄 standalone P&L) = **~13%** uplift, if the contracted capex converts to revenue at the company's own most-recent historical turnover rate.

🔍 This is an analyst inference built on two 📄 documented inputs; it is a mechanical extrapolation, not a management guidance figure, and should be read as an order-of-magnitude check rather than a forecast — the company's own FAT ratio has ranged from 0.63x to 1.39x over the last six years, so the true multiplier is uncertain.
