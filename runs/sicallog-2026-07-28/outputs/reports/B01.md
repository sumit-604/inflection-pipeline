## DECISION LINE

Mechanical Gate 0 classification is AVOID, driven by a decade dominated by
NCLT-insolvency-era losses and near-zero/negative equity, with cumulative CFO
still short of cumulative PAT and no confirmed moat evidence in the data
provided. Per pipeline operating rules, this classification does not halt the
run — company-quality signals never stop the pipeline, and there is no STOP
verdict in this framework. The classification and every deal-breaker are
flagged (FLAG-GATE0) for downstream stages to weigh against the
post-resolution turnaround narrative: FY26 marked the first reported profit
(Rs45.40cr) since the NCLT takeover, cash flow from operations has grown
roughly 7.9x over three years (Rs6.61cr FY23 to Rs52.29cr FY26), a Rs93cr
rights issue (promoter foregoing entitlement, now confirmed completed —
promoter holding fell from 89.87% (Sep-2025) to 73.50% (Mar-2026) per the
screener shareholding pattern) has resolved the MPS-compliance overhang, and a
fresh institutional (FII) entry of 3.09% appeared in Mar-2026 after being
0.00% every prior quarter. However, the FY26 profit and the FY26 ROCE
improvement are **substantially driven by a Rs55.59cr one-time land-sale gain**
(pre-exceptional consolidated PBT FY26 was only Rs1.50cr) — this materially
qualifies the turnaround narrative on an operating basis and should be
weighted heavily by any downstream stage that treats FY26 as evidence of
sustained operating recovery. Promoter pledge remains genuinely NOT FOUND
(absent from both the screener SHP file and the FY25 AR) and should be
confirmed before this is relied upon as clean.
