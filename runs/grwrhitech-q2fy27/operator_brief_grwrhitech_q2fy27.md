## 1. NARRATIVE

1. Garware makes polyester film and coats it into premium products. The two main ones are sun-control film for car and building glass and paint-protection film for cars.
2. It sells mostly abroad. Exports were 75% of sales last year. Overseas subsidiaries resell much of it.
3. Sales this quarter were Rs 696 Cr, 22% more than a year ago. A US tariff hurt last year, so the base was low. Over two years, sales grew only about 6% a year.
4. Operating margin, before other income, was 24.4%. It rose on last year but fell from 27.2% in Q1.
5. The call added a one-off. About Rs 11 Cr of US tariff refunds went into first-half profit. The filing does not show it.
6. Without that refund, the Q2 margin could be near 22.8%. Another Rs 16 Cr of refund comes in Q3, the last one.
7. Management raised its FY27 margin target to 27%, plus or minus 2%, including other income. On the call it spoke of FY28 sales above Rs 3,000 Cr. No filed document carries that number.
8. The good: no debt, about Rs 920 Cr of net cash, and margins well above Indian film peers.
9. The worry: cash from operations covered only 0.75 of profit for the group, and 0.36 for the parent.
10. The worry: the new TPU line (the base layer for paint-protection film) had not started commissioning on 23-Oct. The anti-dumping duty on Chinese film was not imposed. Both triggers moved the wrong way.
11. The worry: only the sales director spoke on the call. Twelve of seventeen open questions got no answer. On the pollution-board penalty, he said an old boiler ran during trials against the board's order.
12. Watch next quarter: the Q3 margin before the refund, against last year's 18.95%. 22.7% or more is good. 18.95% or less puts the guidance at risk.
