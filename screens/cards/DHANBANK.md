## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Dhanlaxmi takes deposits and lends them. It has 264 branches, 148 of them in Kerala (ICRA-Aug26, p.2). It earns the spread between loan yield and deposit cost, plus fees. FY26 net interest income was Rs 622.33 crore and net profit Rs 102.75 crore (AR26, p.12). It has no promoter. CARE calls the holding "well diversified" with no identifiable promoter (`credit-ratings/04-CARE.pdf`, p.1).

**What is changing?** The loan book is turning into a gold-loan book. Gold loans rose from Rs 3,799 crore to Rs 6,512 crore in FY26, from 31.12% to 43.04% of loans (AR26, p.123). ICRA puts the share at 46% of net loans at 30 June 2026 (ICRA-Aug26, p.1). Corporate loans fell from Rs 3,234 crore to Rs 3,050 crore (AR26, p.8). Gold carried almost the whole of the Rs 2,923 crore rise in gross advances.

**Why now?** Three things moved together. A Rs 297.54 crore rights issue closed in February 2025 at Rs 21 a share (AR25, p.13). The RBI reinstated the bank's general permission to open and shift branches on 19 November 2025 (AR26, p.12). And profit before tax rose from Rs 71.62 crore to Rs 138.22 crore in FY26 (AR26, p.80).

**What must be true?** Scale must pull the cost-to-income ratio down for good. It was 88.66% in FY24, 85.42% in FY25 and 73.45% in FY26 (AR25, p.12; AR26, p.12). Return on assets must cross about 0.8% and hold there.

**What breaks it?** A fall in the gold price. ICRA says the gold rally drove both the loan growth and the capital headroom, through the low risk weight on gold loans (ICRA-Aug26, p.1-2). A sharp fall slows the book, lifts risk-weighted assets as the mix shifts, and tests loan-to-value on a book where the average LTV is 65.47% (AR26, p.123).

## 2. Business model and archetype

Four reported segments. In FY26 retail banking earned Rs 1,100.93 crore of revenue, corporate Rs 378.17 crore and treasury Rs 294.32 crore (Q4FY26, p.9). Segment profit after provisions was Rs 64.31 crore in retail, Rs 62.21 crore in treasury and a loss of Rs 8.75 crore in corporate. Corporate also lost Rs 26.26 crore in FY25. The corporate book destroys value after provisions, and the bank is shrinking it.

The deposit base is old, Kerala-heavy and concentrated. CASA was Rs 5,380 crore, 28.86% of deposits (AR26, p.12). The 20 largest depositors held 25.97% of deposits (AR26, p.124). The bank has served as principal banker to the Travancore Devaswom Board since the 1970s (AR26, p.30). ICRA notes that a sizeable part of the top depositor's money is non-callable (ICRA-Aug26, p.2).

**Archetype: lender.** Read on NII, PPOP, credit cost, PAT, RoA, RoE, NIM, CASA and capital adequacy. EBITDA does not apply.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Thin. The one durable asset is a century-old Kerala deposit franchise with temple and community banking. It does not show in the cost of deposits: 5.61% in FY26 against 5.58% in FY25 (AR26, p.131). Gold lending is secured and short, with gross NPA of 0.05% (AR26, p.123). But every bank and gold NBFC in Kerala sells the same product. Scale is a disadvantage: ICRA puts the loan market share at 0.2% (ICRA-Aug26, p.2). **Moat: absent.** A cost advantage could form if the fixed cost base is spread over a much larger book.

## 4. The promoters

There is no promoter. The 31 March 2026 holding was resident individuals 41.77%, foreign portfolio investors 15.42%, bodies corporate 15.27%, NRIs 17.71% and directors 8.20% (AR26, p.64).

Two holders matter. Dr. Jineesh Nath C.K., a non-executive director since 31 July 2024, holds 2,95,93,200 shares, 7.49%. He sits on the audit, credit and nomination committees and chairs three others (AR26, p.40, p.55). The chairman, K.N. Madhusoodanan, is classed as independent and holds 0.94%, including 0.23% through Mavanal Granites Pvt Ltd, which he promotes (AR26, p.39-40).

The RBI has placed its own officers on the board under Section 36AB since at least September 2022. Two sit today, with terms to November 2027 (AR26, p.15; AR25, p.14). ICRA cites them in every rationale held. The MD and CEO, Ajith Kumar K.K., took charge on 20 June 2024 for three years (AR25, p.14). His term ends June 2027.

Related-party transactions are nil beyond KMP pay (AR26, p.18). The board approved an ESOP of 2,76,28,919 options on 19 August 2026, about 7.0% of the 39.47 crore shares (`filings/..19082026154827_BM.pdf`, p.2). Shareholders approved it at the 23 September 2026 AGM (`filings/..23092026192755_Outcome.pdf`, p.1).

Q4 FY26 earned 53% of the year's PPOP. Two lines explain it. Other income was Rs 69.29 crore, and employee cost fell to Rs 71.04 crore from Rs 87.22 crore in Q3. Employee cost was back at Rs 92.98 crore in Q1 FY27. ICRA's nine-month cost-to-income was 83.41% (`credit-ratings/02-ICRA.pdf`, p.2); the full year closed at 73.45%. One quarter moved the annual ratio by 10 points.

Two readings. One: the bank has stepped up, since Q1 FY27 NII rose 27.7% and PPOP 55% year on year without Q4 help. Two: FY26 was one strong quarter, and Q1 FY27's RoA of 0.45% is the true level. **The observation that separates them is Q2 FY27 PPOP.** Above Rs 55 crore supports the first. Near Rs 30 to 40 crore supports the second.

Asset quality improved partly by write-off. Gross NPA fell from Rs 364.11 crore to Rs 286.38 crore, but Rs 95.38 crore went out as technical write-off and Rs 113.14 crore as upgrades, against Rs 178.49 crore of new NPAs (AR26, p.115). Fresh slippage was 1.51% of advances in FY26 and 0.48% annualised in Q1 FY27 (ICRA-Aug26, p.2).

**Classification: earnings-led, gold-mix and capital driven, with one outlier quarter.** PBT growth is real. PAT growth of 54% understates it, because FY26 carried a 25.7% tax charge against 7.0% in FY25.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

Lender rungs, specialised per CLAUDE.md: rungs run on RoA, RoE, asset quality, NIM, CASA and capital.

**FROM R1, a sub-scale lender earning below its cost of equity.** RoE 4.78% to 6.97%, RoA 0.40% to 0.53%, cost to income above 85% until FY26. The bank spent 2011 to 2019 under the RBI's Monitorable Action Plan and Prompt Corrective Action, and its net loans shrank from Rs 9,065 crore to Rs 6,289 crore (ICRA-Aug26, p.1). Losses from FY2013 to FY2018 still block CSR spending (AR26, p.20).

**TO R2, a cost-advantaged secured lender**, with RoA near 1%, cost to income near 60% and low credit cost. The mechanism is a secured gold book on a fixed branch cost base, funded by a capital raise.

Management's claim, quoted once: "we aspire to sustain the business growth we achieved in FY 2025-26" (AR26, p.10). That is a growth claim. No RoA, cost or margin target appears in the corpus.

The climb is **underway, not proven**. CRAR and asset quality now sit at R2 levels. RoA, RoE and cost to income do not.

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim that capital plus gold lending would grow the book and lift earnings. Advances rose 27.4% year on year to Rs 15,571.66 crore at June 2026 (Q1FY27, p.6). Q1 FY27 PBT was Rs 35.54 crore against Rs 12.18 crore.

The proof point the ladder needs is RoA above 0.8% for a run of quarters. **That proof is absent.** The best quarter, Q4 FY26, printed 0.84% and leaned on other income and a low staff-cost quarter. Q1 FY27 fell back to 0.45% (Q1FY27, p.4).

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Gold-price dependence.** Gold is 46% of net loans, and it includes loans to gems and jewellery traders and gold-loan NBFCs (ICRA-Aug26, p.2). Growth and capital headroom both rest on the gold price. Load-bearing.
- **RBI directors on the board since at least 2022.** Two Section 36AB nominees sit on the audit committee (AR26, p.15). The corpus does not say why. Flag, not investigated.
- **Q4 FY26 outlier.** 53% of FY26 PPOP came in one quarter. The corpus does not split the Rs 69.29 crore of other income between recoveries from written-off accounts, treasury and the PSLC fee of Rs 3.58 crore (Q4FY26, p.14).
- **Deposit concentration.** Top 20 depositors were 25.97% of deposits, up from 24.50% (AR26, p.124).
- **CASA ratio slipping.** 31.66% at September 2024 (`credit-ratings/06-CARE.pdf`, p.2), 29.02% at March 2025, 28.86% at March 2026 (AR25, p.12; AR26, p.12). Growth comes from term deposits.
- **Future capital need.** ICRA expects modest internal accruals and says market position "will depend on the raising of fresh capital" (ICRA-Aug26, p.2). Dilution risk on top of the 7% ESOP.
- **Brickwork kept the bank in "Issuer Not Cooperating"** from 2023 until withdrawal in July 2025 (`credit-ratings/03-Brickwork.pdf`, p.1, p.3).
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, two standard key audit matters, no emphasis of matter (AR26, p.72-73). Branch auditors covered 73.49% of advances. No RBI divergence disclosure was required (AR26, p.119). RBI penalties were small and operational (AR26, p.127).
- **AR26 dashboard defect.** It shows FY26 net NPA as Rs 118 crore and FY24 as 1.05% (AR26, p.8). The schedule shows Rs 75.40 crore for FY26 (AR26, p.115), and AR25 shows 1.25% for FY24 (AR25, p.12).
- **Governance fights:** the corpus holds none for FY25 to FY27. One independent director resigned on 3 March 2025 citing other commitments (AR25, p.15). The one postal ballot passed with 99.98% (AR26, p.58). Earlier history is NOT FOUND in the corpus.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

ICRA, 19 August 2026: [ICRA]BBB- (Stable) on Tier II bonds and a Rs 20,000 crore fixed deposit programme, [ICRA]A3 on certificates of deposit (ICRA-Aug26, p.1). The agency credits the track record, the Kerala franchise and capital of 16.74% Tier I after the rights issue. It names four weaknesses: weak profitability from high operating cost, small scale, asset quality that "remains monitorable", and concentration of exposures and deposits. Top 20 exposures were 147% of Tier I capital at March 2025 (ICRA-Aug26, p.2).

On the transition, ICRA takes the bear reading: gold prices drove the growth, and its ability to sustain growth "remains monitorable" (ICRA-Aug26, p.2). An upgrade needs scale and profitability together. CARE rated BB+ (Stable) through December 2024 and withdrew in July 2025 after the bonds were repaid (`credit-ratings/04-CARE.pdf`, p.2).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Capital, asset quality and growth have moved. RoA has not: 0.45% in Q1 FY27, with one quarter at 0.84%.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL, pending proof.** The cost base has kept RoA below 0.6% for a decade, and three ICRA rationales name it. Scale could make it an artifact. Four quarters below 75% cost to income without a Q4-type quarter would change the call.
- **Recognition gap: NARROW.** Price basis, a screener.in service record dated 2026-10-18, not a filed document: market cap Rs 1,413 crore, price Rs 35.8, book value Rs 38.3, trailing PE 12.2. Price to book is 0.93x on June 2026 balance-sheet equity of Rs 1,513.21 crore (Q1FY27, p.6). At a 7% RoE the market already pays near book. It pays for part of the climb before the climb shows.

**Posture: VALUE-TRAP RISK, held as DEEP WATCH.** Proof not fired and structural ugliness. If the cost base proves to be scale-fixable, the cell becomes RESEARCH / WATCH.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The balance sheet is repaired: CRAR 19.19%, net NPA 0.47%, SMA at an all-time low of 0.57% of advances (Q1FY27, p.4; AR26, p.30). The earnings climb is not yet visible. The strategy needs RoE to move from 7% toward the low teens. No document guides that, and the one quarter that looked like it did not repeat.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first, if the watch trigger fires**
1. Q2 FY27 PPOP, cost to income and annualised RoA.
2. The split of the gold book between retail borrowers, jewellers and gold-loan NBFCs, and its LTV at today's gold price.
3. Why the RBI keeps two Section 36AB directors on the board, and any supervisory action behind it.
4. The make-up of Q4 FY26 other income of Rs 69.29 crore and the fall in Q4 staff cost.
5. Identity and tenor of the top 20 depositors.

**What would change the view**
- Two more quarters at RoA of 0.8% or more, with cost to income below 70%, moves this to PROCEED.
- A fall in the gold price that cuts loan growth below 15%, or slippage back above 1.5%, moves it to PASS.
- A fresh equity raise below book value moves it toward PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
