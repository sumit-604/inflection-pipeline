## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **No changes in accounting policies during the year** — explicitly stated
  (Note 2(i), p.174: "There were no changes in accounting policies during the
  year of these consolidated financial statements"). 🟢

- **New Ind AS amendments adopted 1 Apr 2025** (Ind AS 21 FX effects, Ind AS 1
  current/non-current liability classification, Ind AS 7/107 supplier finance
  disclosure, Ind AS 12 Pillar Two top-up tax relief) — company assessed **no
  significant impact** from any of them (Note 3.21, p.183). No new standards
  pending adoption. 🟢 Routine, well disclosed.

- **PP&E useful lives — company estimates SHORTER than Schedule II for two
  categories** (Note 3.2, p.177/22622):
  | Asset | Schedule II life | Company estimate |
  |---|---|---|
  | Computers & accessories | 3 yrs | 3 yrs |
  | Furniture & fittings | 10 yrs | 3–5 yrs (accelerated) |
  | Office equipment | 5 yrs | 3–5 yrs |
  | Vehicles | 8 yrs | 5–6 yrs (accelerated) |
  Company depreciates furniture/fittings and vehicles FASTER than Schedule II
  default — this is CONSERVATIVE (front-loads expense), not aggressive. 🟢

- **Intangible asset useful lives** (Note 3.3, p.178/22505): Trademarks
  3–10 yrs; Technologies/customer relationship/non-compete 2–10 yrs; Brand
  3 yrs; Internally developed software 3 yrs. These are the lives applied to
  the large intangible base created by the FY26 acquisitions (₹6,753mn / ₹675
  cr gross other intangibles, Note 6). A 2–10 year range is wide; the actual
  lives assigned to BioPharm intangibles are disclosed per-asset in Note 7b
  (below) — reasonable, not stretched to the long end uniformly. 🟡 Watch —
  amortisation profile depends heavily on where in the 2–10yr band each
  acquired intangible sits; not separately itemised by useful life in the PPA
  tables.

- **Goodwill: not amortised, tested annually for impairment** (Note 3.3,
  p.178). Ind AS 101 transition exemption applied — goodwill/other intangibles
  carried at FY-transition deemed cost (Note 6, note 2, p.184/24093). Standard.

- **Cash-flow hedge accounting adopted effective 1 January 2026** (Note 3.5,
  p.178/22746): "The Company, effective 01 January 2026, as part of its risk
  management strategy implemented cash flow hedge for its derivative
  instruments." This is a NEW accounting policy election mid-year, confirmed
  by caveat brief. 🟡 Watch — new hedge program, only 3 months of experience;
  ineffectiveness/OCI volatility not yet tested through a full cycle (see
  Note 27a below — hedge loss already recognised in OCI in the first quarter
  of operation).

- **Revenue recognition — principal vs agent judgment flagged as a critical
  estimate** (Note 2(iv)(i), p.175/22211): "The Group enters into contracts
  with customers that include integrated service arrangements involving
  third-party vendor services. Revenue from such arrangements is recognized
  on a net basis when the Group acts as an agent and on a gross basis when it
  acts as the principal." This judgment call is disclosed as an area of
  significant management judgment — standard disclosure but worth flagging
  since gross vs net presentation is a lever on reported revenue growth. 🟡

- **Impairment test discount/growth rate assumptions** (Note 6, "Goodwill
  Impairment testing," p.185/24046):
  | CGU | FY26 terminal growth | FY26 pre-tax discount | FY25 terminal growth | FY25 pre-tax discount |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Enterprise Commercial Solutions | 5.00% | 12.50% | — | — |
  | Enterprise Medical Solutions | 5.00% | 12.0% | 2.40%(EMS)/4.90%(Omnichannel FY25 naming) | 12.50% / 17.2% |
  Terminal growth rate assumption RAISED from 2.4%/4.9% (FY25) to a uniform
  5.0% (FY26) across both CGUs — a less conservative assumption that increases
  headroom in the impairment test, right in the same year ₹7.4bn (₹740cr) of
  new goodwill was booked from three acquisitions. No impairment recognised;
  management states any reasonably possible change in key assumptions would
  not cause carrying value to exceed recoverable amount (Note 6, note 3,
  p.185/24096). 🟡 Watch — flag for stage 11 sensitivity check; a goodwill
  base that nearly tripled in one year (₹3,565mn→₹11,343mn) tested with a
  RAISED growth assumption warrants independent scrutiny of the DCF cash
  flow projections behind it, not visible in the notes themselves.

- **ECL model**: 12-month ECL for most financial assets; lifetime ECL for
  trade receivables (Note 3.5, p.179/23054). Standard Ind AS 109 approach.

- **Ind AS 116 leases**: no explicit discount rate % disclosed in the notes
  (uses "incremental borrowing rate in the country of domicile" — NOT FOUND
  as a single number). ROU / lease liability: consolidated ROU net carrying
  value ₹1,354mn (₹135.4cr) at 31-Mar-26 vs ₹947mn (₹94.7cr) 31-Mar-25 (+43%)
  (Note 5, p.184/24031); lease liability ₹1,444mn (₹144.4cr) vs ₹1,016mn
  (₹101.6cr) (+42%) (Note 4a movement table, p.183/23965). Growth driven by
  new office additions (₹699mn/₹69.9cr) plus acquisition-related lease
  liabilities (₹66mn/₹6.6cr). 🟢 Consistent with headcount/office expansion,
  no red flag.

- **First-time standard adoptions**: none material this year (see above).
