   directed the Company to exclude credit enhancements under First Loss Default
   Guarantee (FLDG) arrangements from the computation of Expected Credit Loss

   actively second-guessing the Company's ECL methodology on FLDG books — worth
   weighing alongside the KYC penalty as a pattern of RBI supervisory friction

3. **BSE administrative/timeliness penalties (NCD compliance)** — Corporate
