## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

The one thing genuinely working is volume. The idle TMT bar mill restarted, rolling tonnage doubled to 102,972 MT, and Q4 FY26 was a record quarter at Rs 244.44 Cr. That is documented and it is why revenue scores STARTING. It confirms if Q1 and Q2 FY27 hold above the Rs 155 Cr per quarter FY26 first half run rate. It dies if the base effect fades and quarterly sales slip back toward Rs 155 Cr.

Everything meant to turn that volume into profit is a claim, not an action. Direct charging is supposed to save Rs 3,000 to 4,000 per tonne, but the capex is uncommitted (AR Note 1 NIL), it slipped its stated window, and a peer already runs the same process. Utilization is supposed to climb to 80%, but the disclosed figures do not reconcile between quarters, and ROCE fell anyway. Cost of debt did come down from 18% to 16%, which is real and delivered, and GreenPro certification arrived on time, which is also real, but neither moves margin or cash. The green premium management keeps invoking has been asked for three times and never quantified.

The absence that matters most is on cash. Across three concalls there is no named working capital program, no debtor day target hit in reported numbers, and no factoring facility. Under the protocol, silence on the missing transition across multiple calls is itself the signal.
