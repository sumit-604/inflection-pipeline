**CFO/PAT of 8.70x is an Ind AS 116 artefact, not a cash-conversion quality
signal in the traditional sense.** The lessee accounting model adds back the
full non-cash D&A (Rs3,838.90mn) and the full accrued lease interest
(Rs1,546.43mn) inside CFO, while the actual cash cost of leases (principal
Rs2,505.11mn + interest paid Rs1,616.53mn = Rs4,121.64mn) is booked entirely
in **financing activities**, not operating. This structurally inflates CFO
relative to PAT for any Ind AS 116 lessee at scale; it is not evidence of
aggressive working-capital management. The economically comparable "cash
flow after lease service" is CFO Rs6,162.38mn less lease cash service
Rs4,121.64mn = **Rs2,040.74mn, a more meaningful post-lease operating cash
figure**, still well ahead of the Rs1,375.99mn implied cash EBITDA computed
under LBF1 above (the gap is working-capital release and other-income timing
differences).

FCF (CFO - capex): Rs6,162.38mn - Rs2,084.20mn (PPE/CWIP, p.132) -
Rs20.50mn (intangibles) = **Rs4,057.68mn FCF before lease service**; after
lease service (a fairer economic FCF for a lessee), Rs4,057.68mn -
Rs4,121.64mn = **Rs(63.96)mn, essentially break-even** — the single most
important number this AR yields for LBF1 and not one the company's own
"Net Cash Flow from Operations Rs616 Cr" headline metric (p.11) surfaces.
