===== PAGE 7 =====
Chairman cum Managing Director's Message (Santosh Kataria). "The Indian jewellery
industry in FY2025 experienced significant shifts. Gold prices surged by approximately
33%, reaching historic highs of Rs 1,01,078 per 10 grams. This price escalation led to a
7% decline in jewellery consumption volumes; however, the value of gold jewellery
consumption increased by 28%... investment demand for gold bars and coins rising by
25%." Company results: "D.P. Abhushan Limited achieved a remarkable 42% year-on-year
growth in revenue, reaching Rs 3,312 Crores in FY25. Our EBITDA surged by 74% YoY to
Rs 175 Crores, and our Profit After Tax (PAT) grew by an impressive 82% to Rs 113
Crores." Footprint expanded to 11 stores across MP and Rajasthan during FY25, starting
with the Ajmer showroom (~6,000 sq ft) and the Neemuch showroom (~7,700 sq ft, G+3,
150 km from Ratlam).

===== PAGE 8 =====
Chairman's message continues: second Ratlam showroom opened April 2025 (~15,000 sq ft
super built-up, Basement+G+4). "we are well on course to double our store outlets by
FY 2028." Notes silver's investment appeal (21% price rise over the prior three months
versus gold's 5%), plans to diversify precious-metal offerings across gold and silver.
Launched the Amoura collection (minimal, lightweight diamond jewellery for daily wear)
as "a critical strategic goal: significantly boosting our revenue from diamond-studded
jewellery."
