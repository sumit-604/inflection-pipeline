**Fast, and you pay for it.** ZELIO at 42x on +75% to +80%; MENONBE, MINDACORP

## Three that screen well and mislead

**SCAGRO, 8.1x on +1,084%.** PASS. The cheapest number on the page rests on one
half-year with a gross margin that jumped from 5.7% to 30-34% and no cash
behind it. The FY26 audit is qualified because documents were withheld, there is
no promoter, 19 allottees hold 92.1%, a Rs 42.40 cr fire loss has no exceptional
item, the filed deck is stamped "MANAGEMENT REVIEW DRAFT - DO NOT DISTRIBUTE",
and H1 operating cash flow was minus Rs 0.09 cr against Rs 19.77 cr of PAT.

**RAJPALAYAM, 8.1x.** The multiple is consolidated and driven by associate
earnings (Ramco group), which gave Rs 137.10 cr in FY26 against a standalone
loss of Rs 15.12 cr. The standalone mill points to Rs 35 – 42 cr.

**SHREEPUSHK, 16.9x on +56%.** The cheapest real candidate, and the card calls
it a priced narrative: Q2 chemical realisation rose to about Rs 1.71 lakh/t on
16.8% lower volume. Whether that is a price spike or a capacity climb is the
check.
