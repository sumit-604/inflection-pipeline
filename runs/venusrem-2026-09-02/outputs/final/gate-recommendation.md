## FLAG-CASH — GROWTH-INDUCED

**Determination:** GROWTH-INDUCED, with a named repeatability caveat.

**Basis:** FY26 CFO/PAT was 1.52x, the second straight year above 1.0x (B12a; B00). Net working-capital days improved 12.4 days, 68.96 to 56.52 (B01). Receivables are clean and improving: days about 53 to 51, over-one-year ageing 0.19% (B02 receivables_trend). The working-capital absorption that offsets the headline is capacity-linked: inventory rose 28.7% versus 13.0% core sales, led by WIP (+55.1%) and stores/spares (+100.6%), consistent with the lyophilisation capacity build (B02 finding 8; B03). The business generates cash structurally; the FY26 movements track the growth and capex phase, not a cash drain.

**Repeatability caveat named:** part of FY26 CFO rode a supplier-credit stretch. Trade payables rose 61.4% and payable days moved from about 61 to about 79 (B02 finding 7; B03 FLAG-CASH). A Rs 112.65 Cr treasury shift into equity and hybrid FVTOCI instruments now carries mark-to-market risk (B02 finding 10; B03 missing_risks). The rating-agency working-capital commentary that would normally corroborate is ABSENT: Venus carries no credit rating in the corpus (B00 input_gaps; B01 rating NOT FOUND). No rating rationale quote is available.

**Single falsifying quarterly metric:** trade payable days holding above ~79 in an FY27 print, or reverting toward ~61 with CFO/PAT dropping below ~1.0x, would confirm the FY26 cash strength rode a one-time supplier-credit pull rather than durable conversion (B03 monitorables). Watch the FY27 quarterly balance sheet.
