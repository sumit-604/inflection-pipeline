## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Patil Automation designs and
builds robotic welding and assembly lines. It builds each line on its own shop
floor in Chakan, runs the trial there, then ships and commissions it (Deck,
page 12; Call, page 15). Each line is a project of four to five months (Call,
page 17). The money is project margin on turnkey lines sold to vehicle makers
and, now, to other industries.

**What is changing?** The customer. Automotive was 98.30% of revenue in FY24.
It fell to 67.00% in FY26, and non-automotive rose to 33.00% (Deck, page 23).
The new work is defence, heavy fabrication, and battery-pack and battery
energy storage lines (Call, pages 9 and 10). Two acquisitions add defence and
powertrain skills: 60% of Mii Robotics and 60% of Pentaco Automation (Deck,
page 25).

**Why now?** The new plant is running and management has guided a big year.
The MD guided FY27 revenue of Rs 260 to 270 crore and a profit margin of "10
plus up to 11" (Call, page 6). That is 50% or more above FY26 total income of
Rs 172.79 crore (Deck, page 5).

**What must be true?** The company must convert a Rs 118 crore order book and
a Rs 800 crore proposal pipeline into Rs 260 crore of dispatches inside one
year (Call, pages 5 and 8). It must also turn profit into cash. FY26 did not.

**What breaks it?** Cash. Standalone operating cash flow was negative Rs 4.38
crore in FY26 (Deck, page 32). Fifteen months after its IPO, the company is
raising Rs 96.38 crore more and moving unspent IPO capex money into working
capital (Board 9-Sep, pages 1 and 2). A project business that needs money this
fast may be buying its growth.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Turnkey project execution. Five product verticals: welding automation,
assembly automation, special-purpose machines, material handling and EV
automation (Deck, page 16). The plant is about 2 lakh sq ft at Chakan with
about 3,500 units of annual capacity, plus 15,000 sq ft at Faridabad from April
2026 (Deck, page 18). The MD puts the capacity of the current setup at Rs 260
to 300 crore of revenue (Call, page 7).

Revenue split in FY26: standalone Rs 157 crore, Pentaco Rs 18 crore, Mii
Robotics Rs 2.33 crore (Call, page 4). Within automotive, 33% was EV work (Call,
page 9). More than 60% of automotive business is repeat business (Call, page
19).

**Archetype: order-book business** (capital goods). Order inflow,
book-to-bill, execution pace, working capital and margin on backlog are the
variables. It also carries the build-to-spec trait: its revenue follows its
customers' capex cycle.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Forming, and narrow. The MD names three: a large in-house trial floor, faster
delivery (four to 4.5 months against six for others), and breadth across
sectors under one roof (Call, pages 15 and 20). He claims prices "20% to 22%,
25% less than China" (Call, page 12). That is an issuer claim with no
supporting filing.

The honest read: this is a crowded field of small integrators. The MD names
Wipro PARI, Comau, Jendamark and Faith Automation, and "more than 10, 15
companies" of similar size (Call, page 15). Switching cost exists inside a
customer's programme, not across programmes. Moat: forming on execution speed,
absent on price.

## 4. The promoters

Promoter and promoter group held 69.29% at 31-Mar-2026 (Deck, page 37). Manoj

Three things to read. First, standalone PAT rose 35.41% but EPS rose only
1.18%, from Rs 7.62 to Rs 7.71, because the IPO added shares (Deck, page 9).
Second, standalone other income of Rs 6.77 crore is 32.8% of standalone PBT.
Third, cash went the wrong way. Inventory rose from Rs 17.94 crore to Rs 47.79
crore, up 166%, and operating cash flow turned negative (Deck, pages 32 and

Returns fell on the new equity. ROCE went from 21.82% to 14.70% and ROE from
27.41% to 11.60% (Deck, page 34). Debt to equity fell to 0.14.

**Classification: earnings-led, with cash conversion failing.** EBITDA margin
climbed from 11.24% in FY23 to 17.02% in FY26 (Deck, page 31). The margin
climb is real. The cash climb is not yet.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM R1, a commodity job shop that builds welding lines for automotive capex
cycles, with 98% of revenue from one industry. TO R3, a multi-sector turnkey
automation supplier where design and trial capability give some pricing
power.

Mechanism: sector diversification (non-automotive from 1.64% to 33.00% in two
years), in-house design (a 160-seat design hub), and acquired defence and
powertrain skills.

Management's claim, quoted once: "we are the only who is doing everything"
(Call, page 12).

The margin supports a move off R1: EBITDA margin rose five points in three
years. The returns do not yet support R3: ROCE of 14.70% sits below the R3
band of 20 to 25%. The IPO cash drags that number, so read it as unproven, not
as failed. **Ladder reading: R1 to R2 delivered on margin. R3 claimed, not yet
shown on returns or cash.**

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim fast growth and diversification. The half-year numbers
back both. H2 FY26 standalone revenue rose 32.3% and PAT 49.06% against H2
FY25 (Deck, page 7). The sector mix moved exactly as claimed (Deck, page 23).

The triggers do not claim cash. The proof there is absent. Operating cash flow
was negative, inventory nearly tripled, and the company now needs Rs 9.16
crore of IPO money for working capital by 31-Dec-2026 (Board 9-Sep, page 2).

**Name the gap:** the latest financial period ends 31-Mar-2026. The company
reports half-yearly. Six months are unobserved. The H1 FY27 result, due by
November 2026, is the first test of the Rs 260 crore guide.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Dilution soon after listing.** Up to 39,18,000 new shares at Rs 246, which
  is 17.96% of the 2,18,20,800 shares outstanding (Board 9-Sep, pages 1 and 7;
  Deck, page 37). The implied share price from the Bull AI market cap is about
  Rs 352. Rs 246 sits about 30% below it. Motilal Oswal Financial Services
  takes 14,10,000 shares on conversion (5.48%).
- **IPO-proceeds variation.** Rs 9.16 crore moves from plant capex to working
  capital (Board 9-Sep, page 2). A completed plant with unspent capex money,
  redirected to fund inventory, is a working-capital signal.
- **Negative operating cash flow** of Rs 4.38 crore on Rs 15.85 crore of PAT
  (Deck, page 32).
- **Other income is 32.8% of standalone PBT.** Rs 6.77 crore of Rs 20.65 crore
  (Deck, page 9).
- **Order-book coverage.** Rs 118 crore covers 44% to 45% of the Rs 260 to 270
  crore guide. Short cycles make that possible, not certain.
- **Capacity ceiling.** The guide uses 87% to 100% of the stated Rs 260 to 300
  crore capacity (Call, page 7). Little room for slippage.
- Family-run board; independent-director quality untested. Corporate
  governance filings are not required on SME platforms (filing 0a9ca470, page 1).
- No results filing in the index. Credit rating, pledge and consolidated cash
  flow NOT FOUND.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: FIRED on growth and margin, NOT FIRED on cash.** Revenue,
  margin and sector mix all moved as claimed. Operating cash flow did not.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, provisionally.** Inventory is shop-floor WIP
  for a larger order load, and IPO cash depresses ROCE. The H1 FY27 cash flow
  separates the two readings. If inventory releases into receivables and cash,
  it was an artefact. If it rebuilds, it is structural.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Price basis, not a corpus document: Bull AI
  company record, market capitalisation Rs 768.20 crore, read 2026-09-28. That
  is 43.2x FY26 consolidated PAT and 25.9x to 29.5x the guided FY27 PAT. Both
  sit above the R3 neighbourhood of about 19x.

**Posture: EARNINGS-ONLY on the guide, PRICED NARRATIVE on cash.** Returns
must come from delivered PAT growth. The multiple already pays for R3.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The growth is real and guided at Tier A. The sector shift is real and dated.
The cash is not. A company that raises equity twice in 15 months and
redirects capex money to inventory has not yet shown it can fund its own
growth. The H1 FY27 result settles this within weeks. Waiting costs little.

**Load-bearing facts a `/step1` must verify first**
1. H1 FY27 revenue and operating cash flow. On FY26's 43/57 half split, the
   Rs 260 crore guide implies about Rs 111 crore in H1.
2. Inventory and receivables at 30-Sep-2026, against the Rs 47.79 crore
   inventory at 31-Mar-2026.
3. Consolidated other income and its source (IPO cash or operations).
4. Order book at 30-Sep-2026, and the value of the 19-Jun-2026 order award.
5. Margin by sector: defence and BESS lines against automotive.
6. Whether the EGM of 3-Oct-2026 passed both resolutions, and the use of the
   Rs 96.38 crore.

**What would change the view**
- H1 FY27 revenue on track and positive operating cash flow moves this to
  PROCEED.
- H1 revenue below about Rs 100 crore, or another inventory build, moves it
  to PASS.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides
only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
