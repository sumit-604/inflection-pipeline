## THE CATALYST STORY IN PLAIN WORDS

**Revenue.** Four channels are pushing volume. The unorganized-to-organized
dealer programme grew 65 percent in volume and 111 percent in value in FY26,
with 8,000 plus dealers across 5,000 plus towns, and it reaches lower-priced
semi-urban demand the branded network never touched (Q4 FY26 call, operator
digest). E-commerce grew 39 percent on platforms and 136 percent on brand dot
com in FY26. Furlenco furniture rental hit Rs 370 Cr revenue, up more than 60
percent, and is being placed store-in-store inside Sleepwell and Kurlon
outlets, 40 plus integrated stores live with a target of 100 in FY27. Kurlon
distribution is expanding in the North where it was weak. What confirms it: two
FY27 quarters of India standalone growth above 12 percent with U2O value growth
holding. What kills it: the showroom slippage repeating (the FY26 target was
cut from 1,000 to 800 to 700 and landed near 600) and U2O value growth fading.

**Margin.** The levers are Kurlon synergy capture, operating leverage on
volume, and the international turnaround (Joyce Australia at about 12.5 percent
and Interplasp Spain at about 11.9 percent EBITDA in Q3 FY26, both documented
delivered). The second Jabalpur VPF line is operational and lifted output 2.5x,
a real cost and yield gain. Against these, the raw material spike is the live
threat: TDI and polyol swung 25 to 35 percent on Middle East conflict and GNFC
shutdowns, and management calls its own pricing outlook "crystal ball gazing."
Wakefit's May 2026 call says competition and price matching rose over the last
six to nine months, which cuts against SFL's premiumisation assumption. What
confirms it: core EBITDA above 12 percent in Q1 and Q2 FY27 despite the spike.
What kills it: gross margin compression above 150 bps in either quarter.

**Cash.** The deleveraging is documented (net debt down Rs 156 Cr in FY26,
about Rs 300 Cr of India debt repayable in one to one and a half years), the
first ever dividend was declared, and non-core real estate of Rs 100 to 125 Cr
was sold to repay debt. These sustain a transition that is already firing. What
confirms it: net debt continuing down and the first FY27 quarter holding
positive CFO. What kills it: receivables growth staying above revenue growth,
ex-Kurlon, for two quarters with CFO turning negative.

**ROCE.** Three documented catalysts. Kurlon has moved from loss-making to
about group EBITDA with 80 percent of the Rs 250 Cr synergy target captured,
which lifts the EBIT numerator. Deleveraging cuts interest and shrinks the
capital base. And management guides FY27 capex of only Rs 125 to 150 Cr against
a plant base that can carry 2 to 2.5x current volume, so revenue can grow
without the capital base growing, which is the cleanest ROCE lever there is.
The depreciation policy change also lifts reported EBIT, but that is an
accounting flatter, not economic improvement, and it is flagged as such. What
confirms it: the synergy machine finally installed in Q1 FY27 and reported ROCE
rising above 6.7 percent. What kills it: another synergy-machine slip with no
hard date, or a fresh large capex cycle announced.

The composite is +4 of 8: Revenue STARTING plus one, Margin STAGNANT zero, Cash
FIRING plus two, ROCE RECOVERING plus one. That places SFL at DEEP WATCH
leaning BUY-ON-DIPS, a small starter defensible only inside a strict entry

EBITDA margin holds above 8 percent through the TDI and polyol spike, because
that is what tells you the FY26 recovery to 10.8 percent was a real
premiumisation and operating-leverage step and not raw material cost timing
plus an accounting change.
