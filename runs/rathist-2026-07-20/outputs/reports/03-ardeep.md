### 2A. Accounting policy aggressiveness
- **Revenue recognition** (Note 2(e), p.87): point-in-time on delivery/shipment, standard and not aggressive
  on its face. But cross-referencing Board's Report p.17 ("Company manufactured 47,440.040 MT rolled products
  ... as against 59,488.595 MT... of previous year") against Sale of Products revenue (Note 20, p.83:
  ₹501.17 Cr FY25 vs ₹487.80 Cr FY24, +2.7%) shows a **20.3% volume decline against 2.7% revenue growth** —
  meaning average realisation/unit price rose sharply, or product mix shifted materially, or job-work
  proportion changed (job work MT fell from 11,181.49 to 193.44 — a near-total exit from job work, which
  would mechanically raise average realised revenue per tonne of owned production even with flat pricing).
  No disaggregation is disclosed to distinguish these explanations. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT: product/customer
  revenue disaggregation.
- **Depreciation** (Note 2(j), p.88-89): straight-line, Schedule II useful lives, standard. **Governance note
  carried forward and confirmed**: Notes on Accounts item 20 (p.94): "Company has purchased second hand
  vehicles during the year, registration of three vehicles are yet to get transferred in name of the Company
  however applicable depreciation is charged on the same" — depreciating assets not yet legally titled to the
  Company; minor in amount (Vehicles net block ₹1.49 Cr) but a control-quality signal.
- **Inventory method**: weighted average, lower of cost and NRV (Note 2(h), p.89) — standard. Verification
  method is the issue (see 1D/2E).
- **Capitalisation incl. borrowing costs** (Note 2(l), p.89): qualifying-asset borrowing costs capitalised,
  standard; PPE/CWIP additions (₹22.60 Cr + ₹6.27 Cr WIP) tie cleanly to the Cash Flow Statement's investing
  outflow (₹22.60 Cr + ₹6.27 Cr).
- **Impairment/ECL** (Note 2(g), p.88): "simplified approach," lifetime ECL "from initial recognition" —
  policy stated but **no ECL matrix or rate disclosed anywhere**, and the ₹24.77 Cr "Others" receivables
  bucket carries essentially zero provision (Note 7 provision for doubtful debts: only ₹4.49 Lakh against a
  ₹24.82 Cr gross book).
- **Ind AS 116 (leases)**: NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT — no lease liability/ROU asset note visible; Note 13/16
  show only "Finance/Lease Obligations" of ₹5.37 Lakh (immaterial), suggesting minimal lease exposure, but no
  explicit Ind AS 116 policy note was located.
- **Quantified policy change**: none disclosed; Notes on Accounts item 22 (p.94) gives only the generic
  "figures regrouped or recast wherever necessary" boilerplate, unquantified — confirmed, nothing new found.
- **New: non-standard waiver accounting.** Note 12(d) General Reserve shows "Credited on account of Waiver of
  Term Loan by Lenders" ₹12.76 Cr (FY24), credited **directly to reserves, bypassing the Statement of Profit
  and Loss entirely** — distinct from the ₹19.84 Cr FY24 waiver that WAS run through P&L as Exceptional

  in the same year with no note explaining the distinction. This is an accounting-policy aggressiveness point

   ambition. **Operational section**: Board's Report's own production table (p.17) shows rolled-product
   output fell from 59,488.6 MT to 47,440.0 MT, a **20.3% volume decline**, with job-work volume falling
   from 11,181.5 MT to just 193.4 MT — a near-total exit from job-work business. This volume contraction is
   never referenced anywhere in the Outlook, MD&A growth narrative, or Risk sections. **Classification**:
   **(b) silent drop** — the opening growth-and-expansion framing simply does not engage with the operational
   section's volume data at all. **Materiality**: moderate-to-high — it changes the read of "growth" from an
   operating reality to a revenue-realisation/mix story that the AR does not explain, directly relevant to
   whether FY25's modest revenue growth (+2.0%) is sustainable or a one-off pricing/mix effect.

2. **Claim**: "aggressive receivables management system to ensure timely collections" (MD&A Credit Risk,
   p.43). **Operational section**: Note 7/Notes item 17 (already covered in 6A above) show the opposite.
   **Classification**: **(a) implicit retraction** — the operational data doesn't just fail to support the
   claim, it affirmatively contradicts it, with no acknowledgement anywhere in the document. **Materiality**:
   high — this is a working-capital-quality claim directly relevant to the cash-conversion thesis risk.

3. **Claim**: "The Company has significantly improved its net worth, managed debt and resumed banking
   relationships... to fund future growth initiatives" (MD&A Outlook, p.47), framed as an organic-sounding
   turnaround. **Operational section**: Note 12 (Other Equity) shows the improvement is substantially
   sourced from lender waivers and promoter-directed preferential capital raises, not retained operating
   profit (cumulative pre-exceptional PBT across FY24+FY25 is only ₹13.01 Cr against a ₹151.83 Cr swing in
   Other Equity from FY23 to FY25). **Classification**: **(c) hedged retreat** — the claim is not false
   (net worth did improve, debt was resumed) but frames a substantially capital-markets-driven repair as if
   it were operationally earned, without naming the scale of the exceptional-item and capital-raise
   contribution. **Materiality**: high — directly affects whether the "financial turnaround" framing extends
   to core earnings power, which Phase 3 shows it largely does not yet.

No further quiet abandonments beyond these three were identified with clear evidentiary support from the
sections read.

### Phase 6 summary

**Phase 6 Verdict: 🔴 Red Flag.** The front-matter narrative is not fabricated — every individual factual
claim traces to something real in the operational sections — but the *framing* consistently selects the more
favourable half of each story (net worth improved, without naming the source; margins improved on one basis,
without flagging the ex-exceptional volatility; growth ambition stated, without engaging with the volume
decline that actually occurred). This is the "hedged retreat"/"silent drop" pattern the 6E check exists to
catch, and it recurs three times across independent sections of this AR.
