MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Against the same quarter last year (Q2 FY26) — Consolidated revenue of ₹136.3 Cr is 110.9% higher than the ₹64.6 Cr of Q2 FY26, a quarter held back by
deferred export shipments and a near one-month break on the general injectables line. EBITDA ₹26.1 Cr at 19.2% of revenue against ₹4.4 Cr at 6.7%; profit after
tax ₹11.8 Cr (₹9.4 Cr attributable to owners) against a loss of ₹(8.6) Cr. Innoxel's swing to a ₹5.4 Cr profit from a ₹(11.0) Cr loss drove the step-up, while BPL
Standalone gross margin eased to 34.8% from 44.1% as COGS rose to 65.2% of revenue.
Against the preceding quarter (Q1 FY27) — Revenue 45.4% higher at ₹136.3 Cr; EBITDA margin +888 bps to 19.2%; profit after tax ₹11.8 Cr against a loss of ₹(3.6)
Cr in Q1 FY27. Innoxel revenue doubled to ₹44.5 Cr with other expenses broadly unchanged; BPL Standalone revenue +41.5% to ₹78.6 Cr; Varenyam Healthcare
revenue down 21.5% to ₹19.7 Cr.
