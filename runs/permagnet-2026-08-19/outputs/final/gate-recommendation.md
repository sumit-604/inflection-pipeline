## FLAG-CASH

Determination: GROWTH-INDUCED.

The dominant swing is a pre revenue growth investment, not a collection or conversion failure in the core business. Consolidated CFO fell 55.1%, from Rs 39.15 Cr to Rs 17.59 Cr, against only a 6.2% consolidated PAT decline (B03 FLAG-CASH, AR-FY26 p.78, p.122). Consolidated debt to equity rose 238%, from 0.17 times to 0.56 times, entirely via a Rs 47.81 Cr unsecured external commercial borrowing drawn into the Quantum Magnetics subsidiary (B02 rank 3, Note 16 consolidated p.131-132). That subsidiary booked Rs 0.78 Cr revenue and a Rs 5.62 Cr loss in FY26 (B05; B02 rank 1). The standalone parent stayed free cash flow positive in both years, at plus Rs 8.70 Cr in FY25 and plus Rs 3.63 Cr in FY26 (B01 block_b_trend), which is what separates this from a structural cash problem.

The second signal is standalone receivables, and it is the reason this flag stays live rather than clearing. Standalone trade receivables rose 39.4% against 13.0% revenue growth, with receivable days moving from 72.19 to 76.03, a second consecutive adverse comparison point (B02 receivables_trend, Note 9 standalone p.82-83, Ratios Note 22 p.112). Ageing quality is high, 99.3% of the book under six months, which argues against structural bad debt, but no receivables ageing composition detail exists beyond that single figure.

Rating agency verbatim, the standing rating constraint: Acuite names the operations "working-capital-intensive operations" alongside raw material price volatility (rating PDF, Acuite press release 20-Feb-2026, scanned image render read; B00 rating and analyst_note). The rating was UPGRADED to ACUITE BBB / Stable and A3+ from BBB- / A3 on Rs 34.48 Cr facilities, so the working capital intensity is a standing feature the agency underwrote, not a fresh deterioration alarm.

Falsification metric (single, quarterly): the next filing shows standalone receivables growing more than 1.5 times revenue growth for a third consecutive adverse point with receivable days above 76.03. That print would reclassify this flag from GROWTH-INDUCED to STRUCTURAL. Watch standalone Ratios Note 22 and the trade receivables note in the Q2 FY27 or next annual filing.

Missing evidence named: the full standalone and consolidated receivables ageing composition schedule beyond the 99.3% under six months headline, and the subsidiary capex commissioning date for the Rs 8.08 Cr consolidated CWIP, are both absent from the provided documents.
