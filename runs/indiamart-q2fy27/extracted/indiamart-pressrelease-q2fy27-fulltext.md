                           IndiaMART InterMESH Limited
    Second Quarter Ending September 30, 2026 - Results Press Release

Noida, India, October 27, 2026: IndiaMART InterMESH Limited (referred to as "IndiaMART" or the
"Company"), today announced its financial results for the second quarter ending September 30, 2026.

                                            Q2 FY2027 vs. Q2 FY2026
 66
    Consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs. 429 Crore, YoY growth of 10%
    Standalone EBITDA of Rs. 156 Crore representing EBITDA margin of 40%
    Consolidated Cash generated from Operations of Rs. 135 Crore

 Consolidated Financial Highlights (Q2 FY2027):

IndiaMART reported consolidated Revenue from Operations of Rs. 429 Crore as compared to Rs. 391
Crore in the corresponding quarter of last year, representing a growth of 10%. This includes IndiaMART
Standalone Revenue of Rs. 389 Crore and Busy Infotech Revenue of Rs 37 Crore.

Collections from Customer grew to Rs. 443 Crore for the quarter, representing YoY growth of 9%,
primarily comprising IndiaMART Standalone Collections of Rs. 394 Crore and Busy Infotech
Collections of Rs 46 Crore.

Deferred Revenue as on September 30, 2026 increased to Rs. 2,029 Crore representing a YoY growth
of 16%. This primarily includes IndiaMART Standalone Deferred Revenue of Rs. 1,863 Crore and
Busy Infotech Deferred Revenue of Rs. 155 Crore.

Net Profit for the quarter was Rs. 91 Crore. Cash Flow from Operations for the quarter was Rs. 135
Crore. Cash and Investments balance stood at Rs. 3,234 Crore as on September 30, 2026.

Press Release  October 27, 2026

Standalone Financial Highlights (Q2 FY2027):

Standalone Revenue from Operations increased to Rs. 389 Crore as compared to Rs. 360 Crore last
year representing a growth of 8%. The growth was primarily driven by improvement in realization
from paying suppliers.

Collections from Customer grew to Rs. 394 Crore for the quarter representing a YoY growth of 8%
and Deferred Revenue as on September 30, 2026 increased to Rs. 1,863 Crore representing a YoY
growth of 14%.

EBITDA for the quarter was Rs. 156 Crore representing margin of 40%. Net Profit for the quarter was
Rs. 106 Crore.

Operational Highlights (Q2 FY2027):

IndiaMART registered Unique business enquiries of 27 million in Q2FY27. Supplier Storefronts grew
to 8.9 million, an increase of 4% YoY and paying suppliers at the end of the quarter were 217K.

Commenting on the performance, Mr. Dinesh Agarwal, Chief Executive Officer, said:

We remain focused on driving sustainable growth by building a trusted and reliable marketplace for
both buyers and sellers. Our efforts to strengthen platform trustworthiness are fostering greater
confidence and deeper, more meaningful engagement across the marketplace. AI remains central to
enhancing user experience, from discovery and matchmaking to conversational tools, while we also
explore new monetisation avenues. With a robust business model and strong cash generation, we remain
well positioned to create long-term value for all our stakeholders.
