🔴 **RED FLAG — the single most significant notes finding.** The "1–2 years overdue" undisputed receivables bucket grew from ₹177.94m to ₹1,516.32m, an 8.5x increase, while the "not due" bucket shrank 29%. This is the direct, quantified evidence behind the company-memory G-1 concern (DSO 116→126 days): a large cohort of receivables that were current or freshly overdue a year ago has aged into the 1–2 year bucket without being collected, even as headline CFO/PAT conversion (LBF-1) looks strong. This directly conflicts with the "cash conversion above 1.2x" thesis pillar and must be reconciled at Halt 1: cash flow strength this year is being achieved despite, not because of, receivables quality.
