## SECTION 2: TAM ESTIMATION, MULTIPLE METHODS

Five segments are sized separately (Section 1A rationale). Precision Pipes
& Auto Tubes could not be reliably sized: the only web-sourced proxy
(Tube Products of India, "India's undisputed market leader," >Rs800cr
turnover) sits below Goodluck's own Rs1,107cr FY26 segment revenue,
which cannot be true of a market Goodluck does not lead — the proxy is
too narrow (likely one product line, not total addressable market).
**TAM for this segment: NOT FOUND at a reliably-scoped level in this
corpus or the searches run.** It is carried in company revenue and SOM
projections at its own historical growth rate, but excluded from the
quantified TAM/SAM/SOM ceiling math below. This understates the true
company TAM and headroom.

### Method 2: Bottom-up (defence, the LBF2 priority segment)

Addressable unit: one 155mm artillery shell body (ready-to-fill, GDAL's

  (AR2026 p.34-35), the more representative blended realisation since
  ERFB long-range appears to be a premium sub-variant, not the mix norm

Global bottom-up TAM, using the **supply-constrained** unit count (the
only figure both management and an independent web search corroborate,
unlike the demand-side figure — see below):
- Management: "world is having a capacity of almost 3 million to 3.5
  million... in next 3 years" (Concall_Feb_2026, line 521-523);
  "quantity available or quantity to be started in the next 1 or 2
  years, it may be 2 million to 3 million" (Concall_Jun_2026, line
  608-609)
- Independent web search: US 2026 production >1M shells; European
  combined production ~1.2-1.3M shells/yr by end-2025 (~4x increase);
  combined US+Europe ≈ 2.3-2.4M units in 2026
- Taking the lower-bound, corroborated range 2.3-3.5M units/yr ×
  Rs 21,500-26,700/shell blended realisation = **Rs 49,500-93,500cr**,
  midpoint **≈ Rs 65,000cr** global shell-body TAM (2026)

**The demand-side figure is deliberately excluded from the headline.**
7.5-9 million units/yr × the same price range = Rs 1,61,000-2,40,000cr,
3-4x higher than the supply-constrained figure. Management itself will
not vouch for the demand-side number ("we cannot vouch for the data,"
Jun-2026 line 611). Per the stage's conservative-bias rule, the
supply-constrained, partially-corroborated figure is used; the demand
figure is named as upside optionality only, not counted.

### 3C Capacity cross-check

Using B07's capex_under_execution_rs_cr (Rs 500cr, GDAL Phase-2,
150,000 → 400,000 shells/yr, Reg 30 filing 06-Aug-2026,
inputs/announcements/20260806-Project_Update_Of_Subsidiary_Company.txt)
and capex_embedded_growth_pct (12%, CRISIL FY27 guide Rs4,500-4,700cr

- **Defence**: 400,000-shell capacity at 75-80% utilisation and the same
  blended realisation (~Rs 21,500-26,700/shell) implies a revenue
  ceiling of ~Rs 690-855cr. This stage's 5yr defence SOM (Rs 610cr) sits
  **comfortably inside** that ceiling. Capacity is not the binding
  constraint on the defence SOM; **execution timeline is**. GDAL's
  Phase-2 has already slipped once (Oct-2025 "within one year" promise →
  now targeted complete Sept-2027, commercial Q4 FY28, a confirmed
  ~11-month-to-2-year slip per B07's FLAG-B07-F2-NEGATIVE and B05's
  grade-D promise-delivery record: 1 delivered, 2 partial, 4 missed of 7
  tracked promises). This stage's SOM already applies a conservative

  the 3yr figure instead.
- **CR/Pipes**: already at ~98% annualised utilisation (Q1 FY27 press

  capacity (part of the Rs 300-350cr/yr consolidated capex guide, split

  plausible but neither is capacity-verified in this corpus.

**Capacity check: sufficient for the quantified SOM at both 3yr and 5yr
horizons.** The gap, where one exists, is on the execution-timeline side
(GDAL Phase-2), not on physical capacity size. Stage 11 should treat the
Sept-2027/Q4-FY28 GDAL Phase-2 date as at-risk, not a base-case anchor,
given the confirmed prior slip and the D-grade delivery record.

### 4C Market structure

- **CR/Pipes**: fragmented, organised + unorganised, price-led competition; large peer set (HITECH, APL Apollo, Surya Roshni, Jindal Pipes and others not in this run's three-peer set). Goodluck is a mid-size player.
- **Defence shells**: highly concentrated, a "select group" of Arms Act licence holders (exact count NOT FOUND, AR calls it a "select group" without a number, per B07). Structure is actively forming, not yet consolidating or fragmenting — new entrants (Sunita Tools, Reliance Infra, BALUFORGE) are arriving alongside legacy state-owned Ordnance Factory Board capacity, in a category that barely existed for private players three years ago.
- **Solar structures**: fragmented, many EPC-linked fabricators; Goodluck's claimed 30%+ share does not reconcile with the revenue math above (Section 2), so the true concentration level is NOT FOUND independently.
- **Forgings**: fragmented globally; India dominated in the auto/powertrain sub-segment by Bharat Forge, with Goodluck a smaller niche player in flanges/rings/oil-and-gas.
