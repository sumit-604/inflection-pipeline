(formerly Jayesh Electricals Ltd) is an "Enterprise over which Key Managerial Person have significant influence."

Atlas's capital (Notice, p.14). Atlas manufactures/refurbishes transformers — VILAS's own end-customer category.

**Both-direction transaction table (Note 41b, p.74, cross-checked against AOC-2 Annexure-II p.30 and Notice p.13):**
