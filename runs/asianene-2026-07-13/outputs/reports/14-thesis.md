## SECTION 3: THE BULL CASE (Why the business is a live turnaround)

**3A. Quality Evidence.** Revenue re-based hard off the FY23 trough: Sales Rs305cr (FY24) -> Rs465cr (FY25) -> Rs791cr (FY26), PAT positive and rising, +70% YoY FY26 (B01, B10). FY26 CFO turned positive at +Rs52.99cr after two negative years (-Rs44.10cr FY24, -Rs33.08cr FY25) (B10). FTTCP scored revenue FIRING/FIRING (+2) and cash STARTING (+1). Kuiper was acquired cheap at US$9.25M (~3x EBITDA) (B10). BUT: ROCE is STAGNANT at 13.71% (structurally low, not recovering) (B10, deliberation); cumulative 2yr CFO/PAT is 0.21x on a single positive print; EBITDA margin fell 15.6% -> 12.5% despite 70% growth (B04); borrowings rose ~6.7x FY25->FY26 while management still calls itself "net zero-debt" (B04). The quality signal is one good print inside a still-unproven cash model.

**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked, from B05):**
1. Oilmax merger completion/consolidation — INORGANIC, target Sep/Oct-2026, conviction M. Confirms: NCLT/shareholder approvals proceed, Oilmax consolidates. Kills: NCLT delay or renewed valuation-fairness challenge. SHARED CATALYST (drives revenue, margin and any ROCE lift at once).
2. Kuiper scale-up to $60-65M FY27 / $100M FY29, margin to 11-12% — conviction M. Confirms: run-rate >~Rs40-45cr/mo, margin expands. Kills: run-rate stalls, West Asia disruption spreads.
3. Vedanta/ONGC integrated-field-development model replication — VOLUME, conviction M. Confirms: ONGC/Oil India award a similar integrated contract. Kills: model stays single-client (peers could not corroborate, B06).
4. Oilmax organic field ramp (Amguri/Tiphuk/Duarmara/Indrora) — conviction L. Has slipped every one of three calls; Duarmara ended with no committable date (B05).
5. FY27 standalone 30-40% growth guide, order-backed (Vedanta Rs865cr, Mahanadi Rs459cr; 90-95% covered) — conviction M. Caveat: order book fell Rs2,000cr -> Rs1,750cr while the guide was raised (B05, deliberation).

**3C. Moat Assessment.** EM 28.0 STRENGTHENING, held below EXPANSION by a grade-C, external-blame-heavy execution record (B07). Active moats are documented (17 doc items): Oilmax backward integration, Vedanta/MCL qualification lock-in, Kuiper geographic first-mover, war chest (B07). Existing moats are medium-durability at best; pricing power is WEAK and the business is CYCLICAL (B04). Direction: strengthening on paper, unproven in delivery.

**3D. Management Quality.** Promoter verdict CONCERN (B08): ~50.8% of standalone revenue is related-party concentrated in newly-formed AESL-majority JVs; MD remuneration disclosed inconsistently (Rs277L vs Rs157L in the same AR); professional CEO replaced by promoter as Executive MD in 2023 (promoter-centricity increasing); a fully-impaired subsidiary loan where the auditor states recovery steps not taken. Counterweights: pledge 0%, credentialed board, 99.99% majority-of-minority merger assent (B08). Credibility grade C: delivered on corporate actions (merger process, Kuiper close, Vedanta mobilisation) but missed FY26 guidance twice, both externally blamed (B05).

**3E. Market Opportunity.** TAM realistic Rs29,580cr, SAM Rs7,290cr, current SAM share 6.8%, headroom 14.8x, TAM growth 12%, runway GOOD (B09). BUT SOM-implied 28.2% 3yr CAGR fails the capacity cross-check: B07 capex-embedded growth is only 2.5%/yr, a Rs180-220cr 3yr capacity gap (B09). Management's ~$100bn-by-2030 claim is capex commitment, not services TAM (41.8x inflated, B09).

**3F. Peer Positioning.** No comparable Indian peer combines E&P asset ownership with a large third-party services business (verified differentiation, B06). BUT peers (DEEPINDS, JINDRILL) run 44-48% EBITDA margins vs AESL's 12.5%, and directly CONTRADICT AESL's monsoon excuse for the Q2 FY26 miss (DEEPINDS +69% revenue, 46.6% margin, no weather mention) (B06). ## PLAIN-ENGLISH NARRATIVE

The business is a real turnaround. Revenue tripled off the FY23 trough, cash flow turned positive last year, Kuiper was bought cheaply, and the order book is order-backed for FY27. FTTCP scored it +4, a genuine transition. The problem is entirely the price. At Rs340 the stock trades at 30x earnings against a disciplined destination of about 13x, so even if earnings grow 20-25% a year for three years the stock still delivers a negative return, because the multiple has to fall faster than earnings rise. On top of that, the promoter is merging his own private company into the listed entity at a valuation the market called rich, and on the disclosed terms that merger shrinks per-share value by a third to a half rather than adding to it. Half of standalone revenue is already related-party. None of this is disqualifying for a turnaround bought cheaply, but it is disqualifying at 30x. The verdict is AVOID at Rs340. The business goes on the watch list; the re-entry zone is Rs75-93, and even there the promoter and Gate-0 caps hold the ceiling at a small speculative position, executed only if the merger accounting and cash quality clear the checklist first.
