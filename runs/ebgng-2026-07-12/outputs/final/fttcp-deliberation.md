## OPERATOR OVERRIDES AND DIRECTED REFINEMENTS

**Override 1 — Pillar 1 anchored on operational ROCE.**
- Draft determination: ROCE base built on reported ROCE (FY26 24.06 percent, FY25 38.19 percent), the standard Gate 0 basis with cash in the denominator.
- Operator ruling: strip surplus cash and use operational ROCE. In the operator's words: "have you calculated operational ROCE that is after removing the surplus cash?"
- Result: operational ROCE about 28.3 percent (FY26), Pillar 1 base about 21.5x rather than about 19.5x. Ruling 6 downgraded to genuinely uncertain because surplus cash explains only about 4 of the 14 point ROCE fall; the rest is working capital redeployment.

**Override 2 — Destination PE set at 20x on a growth duration argument.**
- Draft determination: mechanical additive destination PE about 16x (Pillar 1 base 21.5x times cash multiplier 0.65x, plus a growth premium of 2x, strategic 0x), because the documented duration premium (Pillar 3c) pays nothing without an order book.
- Operator ruling, in the operator's words: "take the destination price-to-earnings as 20 because the growth duration is large. The consumption of laptops and electronics in India, because of the high population, is huge. Penetration is not a scope, so more duration for the growth can be given. The other way is to simply use the forward price-to-earnings. That can be done. I think a forward price-to-earnings of 20 can be given to the company for valuation purposes."
- Result: Phase 3 uses a destination or forward PE of 20x. It is below the 25x sector cap. The mechanical stack does not pay this on documented evidence; the operator credits the long growth runway as duration above the mechanical framework.

**Override 3 — Sector row confirmed.**
- Draft determination: sector row genuinely uncertain between Recycling / Manufacturing (25x) and a lower working capital heavy distribution read (near 20x).
- Operator ruling, in the operator's words: "sector will be Recycling and Manufacturing only."
- Result: Recycling / Manufacturing, 25x, confirmed. Not binding under the operator set 20x destination, but locked for the record.
