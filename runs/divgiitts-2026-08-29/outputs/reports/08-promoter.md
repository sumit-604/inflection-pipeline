### 3D Minority treatment — the central finding of this check

**Chairman's and Managing Director's letters, both dated as part of the FY2025-26 Annual Report and signed by the Board on 11-Aug-2026, describe a US subsidiary as an already-established, operating fact of the year under review — when the entity did not exist until after the year had closed and the report was substantially written.**

The sequence, fully corroborated by independent web sources (MarketScreener, ScanX, InvestmentGuruIndia — corporate-announcement based, VERIFIED tier):
- The Board approved incorporation of a wholly-owned US subsidiary on **25 May 2026**.
- "Divgi Transmission Technologies And Systems Inc." was actually incorporated in Delaware on **4 June 2026** — i.e., **more than two months after FY26 closed (31-Mar-2026)** and only weeks before the AR itself was signed (11-Aug-2026).

Yet the Chairman's letter states: *"What came, last year, was the establishment of our subsidiary in the United States... Our United States subsidiary has already begun drawing the attention of local OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers and state-level government administrations"* (AR pp.25-26). The MD's letter similarly frames it as "a functioning subsidiary" with "genuine customer interest" from the year under review (AR pp.32-33, 27). Both letters use narrative-past and present-perfect tense to describe a two-month-old, single-purpose Delaware entity — approved by the Board only in the *following* financial year — as a proven, trust-earning, customer-validated fact of FY26.

The Board's Report itself (Item 13, p.75) is accurate and consistent with the true timeline: *"The Company does not have any subsidiary, joint venture and associate company during the year under review."* This is signed by the same Chairman and MD.

**This is not the compilation/review control gap that explains the AR's other internal contradictions** (GST/CARO, current-ratio note, KMP-pay figures, R&D units) — those are plausibly explained by independent teams drafting different sections without cross-checking. The US-subsidiary narrative is a deliberate framing choice, repeated across multiple paragraphs in both the Chairman's and the MD's first-person letters, that presents a fact known by the signing date (11-Aug-2026) to be only ~10 weeks old as an accomplished milestone of "last year." It is presented to shareholders in the immediate run-up to the AGM (18-Sep-2026) at which those same two executives are asking for special pay incentives that their own resolutions concede may exceed statutory limits (Items 6-7). Minority shareholders reading only the glossy front matter — the parts of the AR most investors actually read — would reasonably believe the US expansion is a year further advanced, and a year more proven, than it is.

- **Rating: 🔴 RED.** This is the most consequential single finding in this check: a promoter-level (not staff-level) narrative-integrity issue, timed adjacent to a shareholder vote on promoter compensation.
