## What this file is

- Verbatim extracted text of the filed PDFs. Nothing is summarised, reordered
  or paraphrased. `[page N]` markers are the PDF page numbers; cite them.
- Each document keeps its exchange cover letter, where the filing carried one.

  folder and on the branch.

## What this file is not

- It is not OCR. The text layer of these PDFs is machine-readable, so the
  transcripts come through clean. In the decks, words inside charts and
  graphics may arrive broken, out of order, or not at all. Where a deck number
  matters, read the PDF page, not this text.
- The scanned cover page of the July 2026 deck is the one exception: its text
  is garbled because that page is an image.
- It carries no analysis. The run's own reading of these documents is in
  `outputs/reports/05-concall.md`, `07-emoat.md` and `12b-verifier-redflags.md`.
