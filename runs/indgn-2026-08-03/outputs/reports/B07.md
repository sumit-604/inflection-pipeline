## SECTION 2: CAPACITY & CAPEX PIPELINE

Indegene is an asset-light people/technology services business, not a
capacity-constrained manufacturer. Sections 2A-2C are answered against that
reality rather than force-fitted to a manufacturing capex table.

### 2A. Capex programme

No committed capacity-expansion capex programme is disclosed. Total capex
(purchase of property, plant & equipment) was ₹443M in FY26 vs. ₹300M in
FY25 (📄 AR p.173, consolidated cash flow statement) — office fit-out/IT
infrastructure scale, not capacity build. No project-level capex table,
funding source, or commissioning schedule is disclosed (NOT FOUND).

### 2B. Utilisation trajectory

Not applicable in the manufacturing-utilisation sense. The closest analogue
— delivery-workforce utilisation — is not disclosed as a % metric; the
company instead discloses revenue-per-employee (RPE), which is the
functional productivity metric for this business: $56K (3 yrs ago) → $67.5K
(Q1 FY26) → ~$75K (FY26) → $77.1K (Q1 FY27) (📄 Inv. Pres. p.3, p.7; Q4 FY26
call).

### 2C. Growth embedded in "capex"

Arithmetic: FY26 capex ₹443M vs. FY26 revenue ₹35,105M implies a capex/revenue
ratio of 1.3%. Applying even a generous fixed-asset turnover multiple to this
capex base would generate an economically trivial revenue read-through
relative to the ₹35,105M base — this business does not scale off physical
capex. **capex_embedded_growth_pct is not a meaningful construct for this
company and is reported as 0 (not applicable) rather than estimated.** The
real capacity driver is headcount (5,087→5,826 total employees, Q1 FY26→Q1
FY27, 📄 Inv. Pres. p.21) and technology-driven productivity (RPE trend
above), neither of which maps to a capex-turnover formula.

### 2D. New geography/market entries

- BioPharm (US, Oct-2025), Warn & Co (UK, Oct-2025), Cake Kommunikations
  (Austria/DACH, Jan-2026) — all 📄 closed acquisitions (AR p.61-62).
- New delivery centers: Hyderabad (2024), London/Pune/Mumbai (FY25-26),
  Noida (FY26) — 📄 (AR p.24-25 "Our Journey").
- New Spain legal entity (Indegene Spain S.L.U.) — non-anchored digest lead
  only, not independently located in the AR text search performed; treat as
  directional (🔍/lead), not confirmed 📄 in this scan.

## SECTION 3: THE 20-CATEGORY SCAN

### FAMILY A — PRODUCT & TECHNOLOGY

**A1 — Rare manufacturing capability**: NOT APPLICABLE (business-model
mismatch; Indegene has no manufacturing plant/qualification-timeline
dynamic).

**A2 — Patent and IP pipeline**: NO EVIDENCE FOUND. The AR explicitly states
"R&D expenditure is not separately disclosed in the Company's financial
statements" (AR BRSR section, p.~100 per "Capex 0%... no specific capital
expenditure... towards technologies" disclosure) and no patent filings are
disclosed anywhere in the AR search performed. The company holds trademarks
(Cortex™, Invisage™, Tandem™, Content Super App) but trademarks are brand
protection, not a technology moat, and the AR itself frames its IP posture
around trademarks and CSR-funded external "intellectual property creation"
(AR p.~46, p.~185 CSR sections) rather than a filed-patent pipeline for its
own platforms.

**A3 — Process innovation**: MODERATE. Revenue per employee — a genuine,
quarter-by-quarter disclosed unit-economics metric — has risen from $51,000
five years ago to $77,100 in Q1 FY27 (📄 Q4 FY26 call; Inv. Pres. p.3, p.7).
Management also cites a pilot compressing agency-of-record creative
turnaround from "3 months to 3 days" for a top-20 client (🎙️ Q4 FY26 call) —
this is a single named pilot, not yet a disclosed run-rate metric, so it is
kept separate from the RPE evidence.

**A4 — Product platform/modular architecture**: WEAK. Management explicitly
frames its GenAI build as "a platform approach versus a point solution
approach" (🎙️ Q2 FY26 call) and the AR shows a build/embed/extend/expand
roadmap graphic for NCCI/NCCA/NAEM/MLR modules (AR p.29 "Dual-Pronged GenAI
Strategy"). No SKU count, launch-frequency cadence, or R&D-cost-vs-output
ratio is disclosed to substantiate platform leverage numerically.

### FAMILY D — DATA & DIGITAL

**D1 — Proprietary data asset**: STRONG. Invisage (Indegene's own HCP

reasoning layer ("learns and compounds with every use," AR p.26) — the
learning-loop claim itself is 🎙️, but the underlying data-asset scale and
provenance is 📄.

**D2 — Digital platform**: MODERATE. Cortex, Content Super App, NEXT family,
NAEM and Tectonic are all described as "already in production" (📄 AR p.26,
p.29) with at least one revenue-quantified proof point (Tectonic: $1M in
Q1 FY26 → $2M cumulative H1 FY26 → 5 paying customers by FY26 year-end, 2
converted from pilot to long-term — 📄 Q2/Q4 FY26 calls). However, this
revenue is still small in absolute terms relative to the ~₹35,105M FY26
base, and the broader "digital revenue %" / participant-growth / lock-in
metrics the standard category asks for are not disclosed at platform level
(NOT FOUND beyond the Tectonic figures).

specific sub-metrics).

**F2 — Execution moat**: WEAK/MIXED. RPE trend (above) is a genuine,
multi-year execution proof point (📄). However, the company does not give
formal revenue guidance, so guidance-delivery cannot be assessed in the
standard sense. On the one forward commitment management did make — EBITDA
margin recovery to ~20% within "6 to 8 quarters" from the investment phase
flagged in Q2 FY26 — the promise is not yet delivered as of this scan:
FY26 PAT fell 1.4% YoY (Gate 0 flag) and Q4 FY26/Q1 FY27 EBITDA margins
(16.4%/16.9%) remain below the ~20% historical range (📄 AR p.51; Inv. Pres.
p.4-5). Management cites a FY22 precedent of a similar margin dip and
recovery (🎙️ Q4 FY26 call) as the basis for confidence, but that is a claim,
not yet a repeated, current-cycle proof point. Cross-reference to B05
concall promise-delivery record: **NOT FOUND in this run** — no B05 report
was available in this run folder at the time of this scan, so this row
relies on this stage's own reading of the three main concalls only.

### FAMILY G — FINANCIAL & STRUCTURAL

FY26 call), and free cash flow rose from ₹4,119M to ₹6,065M (📄 Q4 FY26

pricing over time-and-material billing (🎙️/📄 mixed, Q4 FY26 call).

## SECTION 4: REGULATORY & POLICY TAILWINDS (R1)

**4A. Regulatory approvals in pipeline**: NOT FOUND. Indegene is a services
provider, not a regulated-product company; it has no drug/device approval
pipeline of its own. Management does reference client-facing regulatory
dynamics — an FDA warning letter issued to an unnamed company over
apparently AI-generated content (🎙️ Q4 FY26 call) — as a data point on
industry AI-governance risk, not a tailwind for Indegene specifically.

**4B. Government policy tailwinds**: NOT FOUND. No PLI, tax incentive,
import-substitution, or procurement-preference scheme applicable to
Indegene is disclosed. The company does discuss macro policy dynamics
extensively (Most Favored Nation drug pricing, Section 232 pharma tariffs,
DTC marketing enforcement, H-1B visa fee changes) as industry-level context
in the Oct-2025/Nov-2025 calls, concluding these have "practically no
impact on Indegene" (🎙️ Q2 FY26 call — company employs ~550 people in the
US, ~45 on H1B, deputing only 5-8 per year).

**4C. Regulatory moat assessment**: The genuine structural tailwind here is
demand-side, not a direct regulatory unlock for Indegene: an industry-wide
patent-cliff/loss-of-exclusivity wave through 2030 combined with sustained
drug-pricing pressure (IRA, MFN) is pushing biopharma clients toward
efficiency-driven centralization of commercial/medical operations — the
exact demand Indegene's model is built to capture (📄/🎙️ AR p.28; Q2/Q3
FY26 calls). This is a real and durable industry dynamic but is better
classified as a market-structure tailwind than a "regulatory moat" in the
category's strict sense (no license, approval, or scheme is being granted
to Indegene). Time to kick in: already underway; sustainability: multi-year
(patent cliff dynamics run through 2030 per management, AR p.28).
