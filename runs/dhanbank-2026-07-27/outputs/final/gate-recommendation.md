## FLAG-CASH (asset quality analogue) ACTIVE

Determination: INDETERMINATE.

For a bank the manufacturing cash conversion metric (CFO to PAT) does not apply the usual way, because operating cash flow is balance sheet driven and swings with deposit and loan growth (CFO to PAT ran -0.07x in FY25 and 6.15x in FY26 on that mechanism, cumulative 2.45x FY17 to FY26, B01). The determination is therefore made on the asset quality and provisioning analogue, which is the earnings quality question that matters for a lender.

Evidence:
- Headline gross NPA improved 2.98% to 1.89% in FY26, but the improvement is denominator driven by gold loan advances growth and flattered by write offs; fresh slippage was flat year on year (Rs 152.26 Cr vs Rs 152.24 Cr) and gross reductions were lower (B02, Note 4a-i, p.113-114).
- The cumulative technical write off pool is Rs 692.47 Cr, roughly 1.9x the headline gross NPA, with the annual recovery rate halved 6.4% to 3.2% year on year (B02, Note 4a-i, p.113).
- ICRA's gross NPA including write offs is 7.45% at December 2025 against the 1.89% headline; ICRA attributes the PCR dip to write off of highly provided NPAs (B05; ICRA p.3).
- PCR excluding write offs fell to 53.63% at December 2025, while net NPA rose 0.86% to 1.11% in the same quarter that headline gross NPA fell (B03, B04; ICRA p.3).
- Liquidity is adequate and not the concern: LCR 126.41%, NSFR 145.48% (B03; ICRA Q3 FY26).

Why INDETERMINATE and not GROWTH-INDUCED or STRUCTURAL: the reported improvement is genuinely part growth optics and part write off flattering, but the underlying credit trend cannot be resolved from the current record. FY25 shows real operating repair alongside early reversal signals in the FY26 interim data. The evidence to settle it does not yet exist in provided filings.

Missing evidence (names the cap): FY27 quarterly prints of PCR excluding write offs, gross NPA including write offs, and fresh slippage by segment, plus the full FY2026 annual report Schedule 18 detail (BSE / exchange filings and the next ICRA surveillance note).

Single falsifying metric to watch in FY27: PCR excluding write offs falling further below 53.63% (December 2025) while net NPA rises above 1.11%. If both move that way in the first two FY27 quarterly results, the headline gross NPA improvement is confirmed optical.

## Monitorables and triggers (deduplicated)

1. Watch PCR excluding write offs against 53.63% and net NPA against 1.11% in the first two FY27 quarterly results (BSE filings). Falling PCR alongside rising net NPA confirms the headline gross NPA improvement was optical. This is the thesis's central test.
2. Watch the gap between headline gross NPA (1.89%) and gross NPA including write offs (7.45% at December 2025) in the next ICRA surveillance note. A widening gap means write off pace is out of line with peers.
3. Watch the technical write off pool (Rs 692 Cr) and its recovery rate (3.2%) in Schedule 18 of the full FY26 annual report. Pool growth outpacing gross NPA reduction widens the shadow bad debt exposure.
4. Watch gold loan mix (38%) and any first time LTV or concentration disclosure in quarterly filings. A rise past 40% to 45% without disclosure tests the gold price dependence of recent growth.
5. Watch cost to income against 75% in FY27 filings. Reverting above 80% tells you the improvement was a denominator effect of fast growth, not structural cost control.
6. Watch CRAR and CET1 (18.92% and 15.24%) drawn down by advances growth. A forced fresh capital raise is the signal that internal accruals cannot fund the runway ICRA already flags as capital gated.
7. Watch top 20 exposure (147% of Tier I) and top 20 depositor (24.5%) concentration in rating updates. A single large exit on either side stresses both funding and capital.
8. Watch the Corporate/Wholesale segment result narrowing from the FY26 Rs 8.75 Cr loss level in the quarterly segment note. Failure to narrow tests whether the stated Retail and MSME pivot is resolving the structural drag.

## Falsification line

The single most damaging FY27 print: PCR excluding write offs falling below 53.63% while net NPA rises above 1.11% in the first two quarters, which would confirm the entire headline gross NPA improvement was write off flattered and denominator driven.
