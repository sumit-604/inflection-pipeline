## PART A: INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (built before reading B05/B06 verdicts)

Anchors use the page markers in each transcript.

### F1 — Bikaner curtailment: severity doubled and resolution date kept slipping
- Q3 FY26 (Mar, p.18): grid estimate for end of backdown "October to December
  2026"; curtailment not yet quantified.
- Q4 FY26 (May, p.16): curtailment "about 30% today"; grid forecast end
  "by September of this year," management urges "be more conservative."
- Q1 FY27 (Aug, p.13, p.22): curtailment "about 70%," "expect that curtailment
  to actually continue throughout the duration of the current financial year";
  "this number keeps changing... difficult to hazard a guess."
- Impact: Rs170 Cr full-year at 70% (Aug p.13): Rs1,870 Cr run-rate x ~13% =
  ~Rs240 Cr, x70% = ~Rs170 Cr, i.e. ~9% of run-rate EBITDA (management: "higher
  by maybe 8% or 9%," Aug p.12).
- Grade: MEDIUM. Real, quantified, proactively disclosed, but three-quarter
  forecast failure.

### F2 — Bikaner sits on TGNA, so the curtailment is NOT revenue-protected
- Q3 FY26 (Mar, p.18): Puneet Gulati — "And you have a TGNA there at this time."
  Kuldeep — "Yes, we would get a... that standard process would apply."
- Q4 FY26 (May, p.11): asked if PPAs protect against curtailment — "no, not in
  the way that if the grid is curtailed, I'll still pay you... corporate
  customers do not have pay-for-performance contracts and if... you are unable
  to generate the power, they are not liable to pay."
- Why it matters: this is the MECHANISM behind the Rs170 Cr hit. Peers (ACME,
  JSW per B06) describe GNA-connected capacity as revenue-protected and
  TGNA-connected as unprotected. CleanMax's own Q3 call establishes Bikaner is
  on TGNA and its customers do not pay for uncurtailed power. That directly
  answers B06's self-declared "single most consequential finding" (Bikaner
  GNA-vs-TGNA status), which B06 said it "cannot establish."
- Grade: MAJOR (thesis-relevant fact, disclosed by management, absent from B05).

### F3 — EBITDA presented under conflicting labels/figures in one call
- Q1 FY27 (Aug, p.5): "our EBITDA has increased about 74% to about INR494 crores."
- Aug p.8 (Nikunj): "grown 74% on a cash EBITDA basis, and on a reported basis,
  it's a 68% increase to INR462 crores."
- Aug p.9: "INR494 crores is the adjusted reported EBITDA," broken into
  Rs334 Cr (stabilised) + ~Rs35 Cr (7% RE services) + Rs125 Cr (new assets).
- So "cash EBITDA"/"adjusted reported EBITDA" = Rs494 Cr while "reported EBITDA"
  = Rs462 Cr, no bridge, unchallenged. The FY28 guidance is stated as "minimum
  reported EBITDA... INR3,000 crore" (Aug p.6) — the lower-basis label — so the
  ambiguity bears directly on the headline guidance.
- Grade: MEDIUM.

### F4 — Quarterly generation / PLF data requested across two quarters, deferred both times; a Q3 commitment to provide it went undelivered
- Q3 FY26 (Mar, p.11): Shaunak Godbole asks for quarterly generation; Kuldeep —
  "I don't have it readily available... we'll provide that on a continued basis
  every quarter." Nishant Chandra also probes generation (p.13, p.18).
- Q3 FY26 (Mar, p.17): "going forward we will provide" quarterly split.
- Q1 FY27 (Aug, p.14): Abhishek Puri — "PLF you mentioned TTM, which does not
  give the understanding about how your assets performed in a given quarter...
  solar-wind breakup." Management again defers to GHG-report disclosure and
  keeps only TTM PLF ("that's the right way... rather than within one quarter").
- Why it matters: quarterly generation/PLF would let analysts independently see
  the Bikaner curtailment drag rather than trust curtailment-masking TTM PLF and
  a blended 99% "grid uptime." The Q3 promise to publish quarterly was not kept.
- Grade: MAJOR (repeated 2-quarter deferral plus an unkept disclosure promise;
  see severity note below on why not CRITICAL).

### F5 — RE Services revenue Rs300 Cr (7.32x, called "six fold") against a shrinking order book
- Q1 FY27 (Aug, p.8): RE services revenue Rs300 Cr vs Rs41 Cr — "a six fold
  increase" (300/41 = 7.32x, an understatement).
- Order book: 215 MW contracted-yet-to-execute at start FY26 (May p.4) ->
  147 MW at end Q1 FY27 (Aug p.16). Management confirms it "is an order book
  driven business," order-to-execution "within 12 months" (Aug p.16-17).
- Recurrence/sustainability of the Rs300 Cr print against a 147 MW residual book
  was not addressed. Grade: LOW-MEDIUM.

### F6 — No customer-concentration metric disclosed in any call
- 42% of contracted capacity is Data & AI (~2.4 GW) among named hyperscalers
  (Aug p.3), yet no top-5/top-10 revenue or EBITDA share is given in any call;
  the 13 MW average PPA is used as an implicit diversification proxy (Mar p.15,
  May p.6, Aug p.15). Grade: LOW-MEDIUM.

### F7 — Two strategic JV announcements dropped without follow-up
- Osaka Gas JV "CORE" 51/49, 400+ MW/3 yrs, Rs176 Cr equity in — Q3 FY26 only
  (Mar p.7); no mention Q4 or Q1 FY27.
- Apple JV #2, 51/49, 150 MW, Rs104 Cr equity — Q4 FY26 only (May p.8);
  no mention Q1 FY27. Grade: LOW.

### F8 — FY28 Rs3,000 Cr guidance embeds a full-year-mature assumption on late-adds
- FY28 rests on 4.6 GW opex by 1-Apr-2027 (Aug p.6). Management's own
  stabilisation lag is "three to six months" post-COD (May p.4). Capacity
  commissioned late in FY27 will not run a full stabilised year in FY28, an
  optimism embedded in the math and not addressed. Grade: LOW.

### F9 — Market-share source softened as the number rose
- Q3 FY26 (Mar p.17): "12% all-India market share," sourced to the DRHP
  industry report. Q1 FY27 (Aug p.16, p.21): "about 14%... as per some recently
  released statistics," no named source. Number up, sourcing weaker. Grade: LOW.

### F10 — Forward EBITDA / run-rate / debt-by-FY disclosure repeatedly declined
- Q3 FY26 (Mar p.11): run-rate EBITDA on 2.7 GW under execution declined —
  "not providing the corresponding EBITDA run rate and debt guidance."
- Q3 FY26 (Mar p.10): debt-by-FY declined — "not giving debt forecast by
  financial year."
- Q1 FY27 (Aug p.18): current run-rate EBITDA as of 30-Jun declined, with a
  hardened stated policy — "we don't really publish a number every quarter of
  the run rate EBITDA because that gives a wrong indication to the markets."
- Peak-debt-by-FY28 was finally answered (Rs16,000 Cr, Aug p.16), issued
  alongside the new FY28 EBITDA guidance. Grade: LOW-MEDIUM (consistent policy,
  not a hidden problem; capex per quarter/per project was likewise declined,
  Mar p.8 and Aug p.13).

### F11 — Absolute net debt jumped ~Rs2.1k Cr in one quarter; 38% is non-earning
- Q1 FY27 (Aug p.8): net debt Rs11,809 Cr vs Rs9,684 Cr at 31-Mar-2026;
  38% (Rs4,483 Cr) is against under-construction assets generating no cash flow;
  44% (Rs5,154 Cr) against >12-month operational assets. Gross run-rate leverage
  ~6.3x (11,809/1,870) vs the stabilised 4.3x management quotes (May p.10).
  Disclosed transparently, but the absolute jump and CWIP-debt share are a
  capital-intensity risk. Grade: LOW.

### F12 — Blended "99%+ grid uptime" headline sits beside 70% CTU curtailment
- Q4 FY26 (May p.7): "grid uptime... well in excess of 99%... almost a no
  curtailment scenario... last year we had no curtailment." Q1 FY27 (Aug p.6):
  "grid uptime has remained very high at above 99%... 87% of our EBITDA is STU
  plus rooftop," while the CTU asset runs 70% curtailed. The blended headline
  de-emphasises the CTU problem; the reconciliation is available but only if the
  reader separates STU from CTU. Grade: LOW.

Severity note on F4: Verifier B's rubric makes a MISSED repeated evasion
(2+ quarters) CRITICAL. F4 is a repeated 2-quarter deferral, but management
provides TTM PLF plus GHG-based generation, offers a methodological rationale,
and does not use it to hide a material adverse fact (Bikaner curtailment is
disclosed separately and prominently). It is a broken disclosure promise, not
concealment of a problem, so I hold it at MAJOR rather than CRITICAL and flag
the borderline explicitly.
