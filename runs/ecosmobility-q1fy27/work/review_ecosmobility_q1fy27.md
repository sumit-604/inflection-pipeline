4. **What drove the gap between core-PBT decline and reported-PAT growth?** Two non-operating levers (consolidated bridge): Other Income +₹0.873 Cr and a lower effective tax rate (28.82% → 24.08%, worth +₹0.767 Cr of PAT). The FY26 tax rate was inflated by a Q1FY26 deferred-tax charge (₹0.319 Cr); its absence this year flatters the YoY (A3 F8-01).
5. **Are D&A and finance costs scaling faster than revenue?** No absorption-gap risk yet. Consolidated D&A +5.6% and finance costs +19.4% (off a ₹0.23 Cr base) both trail revenue +16.7%; standalone D&A actually fell 8.3%. Capex intensity is not visibly rising in the P&L — but see the Ecos Fleet Management WOS flag (F15-01), which the P&L cannot yet reveal.
6. **Is Other Income concentration changing?** Yes, rising. Other Income / PBT climbed to ~19.6% consolidated (from 15.4%). Treasury/other income is doing more of the lifting each quarter. Strip it and core operations are shrinking, not growing.
