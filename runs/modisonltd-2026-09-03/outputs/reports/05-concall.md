### 1C Trigger evolution, SOURCE 1 → SOURCE 2 (no intervening transcript exists)

- **Revenue target: STRENGTHENING.** Rs 500 cr/FY25 target essentially met (actual
  FY25 revenue implied ≈ Rs 490 cr, from FY26 Rs 710.33 cr ÷ 1.449 growth — about
  98% of target). Rs 1,000 cr/2030 target now tracking ~2-3 years ahead of
  schedule and has been raised to Rs 1,360 cr/2030 (SOURCE 2). This is the
  cleanest delivered promise in the record.
- **EBITDA margin band: STRENGTHENING then partially WALKED BACK.** 2022's "safe
  bracket 12-14%" was beaten in FY26 (16.2% stated, ≈16.6% independently computed
  from the Q1 FY27 filing's FY26 column — see Section 2A). But FY27 guidance of
  "at least 10-12%" is below even the original 12-14% band, an unexplained step
  down in ambition on top of being a step down from the FY26 peak.
- **Value-chain migration to sub-assemblies (L&T, GM Modular): DROPPED/no update.**
  Actively discussed with specifics in SOURCE 1; entirely absent from SOURCE 2's
  AGM presentation and Q&A.
- **DRDO/DMRL/DRDL defense partnership: DROPPED/no update.** Named specifically
  in SOURCE 1 ("very soon you should have good news"); SOURCE 2 mentions only
  generic "ISRO/BHEL/government connects," with no reference to the named
  agencies or partnership status.
- **Undisclosed new-metal-segment expansion: DROPPED.** Promised "sooner rather
  than later" in SOURCE 1 (Sep-2022); no launch, announcement, or mention
  anywhere in SOURCE 2 nearly four years later.
- **Precious-material-compound exports (Clariant silver salts): DROPPED/no
  update.** First export order was a highlighted win in SOURCE 1; not mentioned
  in SOURCE 2.
- **LV/HV factory commissioning (Nov-2022 / FY23 targets): UNCONFIRMED.** No
  completion statement in SOURCE 2 or in the results filings reviewed. Given
  the subsequent revenue growth this capacity presumably came online, but
  management never closed the loop publicly on the specific dates it gave.
- **NEW in SOURCE 2, no SOURCE 1 precedent:** ISRO alloy win, GE "Best Supplier"
  award, HV "crown" business, AI/data-centre demand narrative, ERP
  implementation. These are all plausible organic developments over a
  four-year gap, not suspicious in themselves, but none is quantified with a
  revenue contribution.
- **Self-reported LV market share: MATERIAL, UNEXPLAINED SHIFT.** 16-17%
  (SOURCE 1, 2022) to 30-35% (SOURCE 2, 2026) — close to a doubling — with no
  bridge, methodology, or third-party corroboration offered either time. HV
  share is roughly stable (80% in 2022 vs 72-82% in 2026).
- **LV market growth rate: MATERIAL, UNEXPLAINED SHIFT.** ">15% CAGR" (SOURCE 1,

### 2B Excuse pattern analysis

- Margin degrowth (SOURCE 1, addressing Deepak Poddar's 8-quarter EBITDA
  question): explained via a specific, quantified mechanism (silver price fell
  from ~Rs 70,000/kg to Rs 54,000-60,000/kg; stock-valuation lag) — this is
  **external-blame but evidenced and specific**, not vague deflection.
- Fire incident (SOURCE 2): framed as a smooth 7-10 day recovery with "no
  orders lost," omitting the Rs 10.63 cr charge already in the accounts —
  **minimization/deflection**, not silence, but not a full accounting either.
- Right-to-win / wallet-share question (SOURCE 2, Virat Kacharia): not directly
  answered; management offered one-on-one meetings instead — **deflection**.
- MCPL related-party dependency answer (SOURCE 2): gave a specific number
  (~25%) that appears to conflict with the AR's ~48.76% figure — this is either
  an error, a stale/differently-scoped reference point, or a materially
  misleading answer. Classified as a **credibility miss** regardless of intent,
  since the effect on a shareholder listening live is reassurance that
  understates the AR's own disclosed dependency.
- Positive pattern: management does proactively volunteer hard commodity-price
  numbers (silver kg-by-kg price moves in SOURCE 1; hedging candor "sometimes
  we do, sometimes we don't" in SOURCE 2) rather than staying vague — a mark in
  favour of transparency on the margin-mechanics topic specifically, even while
  other topics (MCPL, fire, market share) show real gaps.

Overall pattern classification: **external-blame-heavy**, with margin
volatility consistently and specifically externalized to commodity prices
across both sources (2022 and 2026), and a serious unreconciled RPT figure and
a downplayed fire loss layered on top.
