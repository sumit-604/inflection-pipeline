## PHASE 1: AUDITOR'S REPORT & CARO

### 1A. Core opinion — HEADLINE STRUCTURAL FINDING

The FY26 Annual Report does **not** contain a Section 143 Companies Act
statutory audit report. What is printed under "Independent Auditor's
Report" (AR pp.66-67) is titled **"Independent Auditor's Report on
Standalone Financial Results of Vinyas Innovative Technologies Limited for
the Half year and Year Ended March 31, 2026"** — a SEBI Regulation 33 /
LODR format report on the "Statement" of financial results, not a
Companies Act opinion on the financial statements. It contains: an
Opinion paragraph, Basis for Opinion, Management's/Auditor's
Responsibilities, and an "Other Matter" paragraph on the half-year
balancing figure (Auditor's Report FY26 pp.66-67). It contains **no**
Key Audit Matters section, **no** CARO 2020 annexure, **no** Section
143(3) statutory reporting matters (a)-(f), and **no** internal-financial-
controls annexure. Same auditor: P Chandrasekar LLP, Rajesh D (Partner,
Membership 234709), dated 28-May-2026 (Auditor's Report FY26 p.67).

**Backward comparison (FY25 AR).** The FY25 Annual Report contained the
full statutory form: "Report on the Standalone Financial Statements,"
Key Audit Matters (two: uncertain tax positions; trade receivables
valuation), CARO 2020 Annexure A (clauses i-xxi), and Annexure B
reference to internal financial controls (FY25 Auditor's Report pp.73-79,
Arun R, Partner, Membership 208425, dated 29-May-2025).

**Verdict: this is a real backward-read finding, not a data gap.** The
FY26 AR shows a year-on-year *regression* in statutory disclosure
architecture: the company's shareholder-facing document dropped CARO and
KAM entirely versus the prior year. Whether a full CARO/143(3) report was
filed separately with the ROC (Form AOC-4) cannot be determined from this
corpus — but it was not placed before shareholders in the FY26 Annual
Report they are asked to adopt at the AGM. This is flagged as a Phase 1
red flag in its own right, independent of what the missing CARO might
have said.
