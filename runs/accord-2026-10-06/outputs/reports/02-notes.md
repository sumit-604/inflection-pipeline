### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT
1. Who are the vendors behind the Rs 624.41 lakh advance, and what purchase and delivery date does each cover? Is it the same purchase as the Rs 619.50 lakh stock in transit?
2. Which account holds the offset to stock in transit, and was any of it booked in purchases at 31-Mar-26?
3. Why did the cash credit rise by Rs 453.95 lakh in Q4 while Rs 2,040.21 lakh of IPO money was unspent? What funds the Rs 1,067 lakh land?
4. Is any bill discounted with recourse at year end, and what was the amount? Why is there no warranty provision against Rs 532.46 lakh of warranty-period guarantees?
5. What changed in Q4 to lift materials cost to 75.8% of revenue, and how do the FY25 comparatives bridge (overdraft reclassification, ABL loan columns, DTA, 8.24 finance cost shift)?
