## The picture in one paragraph

Wednesday was the fourth repair day since the 8 October break, and the
strongest underneath. The Nifty slipped about half a percent, yet most stocks
rose. 145 stocks jumped four and a half percent or more, and only 10 fell that
much, the fewest hard fallers in a month. New yearly lows collapsed from 98 to
28. The count of stocks above the two-hundred-day average climbed to 919, so
about two thirds of the 8 October damage is now repaired. The count above the
twenty-day average already stands higher than before the break. Turnover came
back to its year-ago level after two thin days. Two things did not fit. The big
names paused, with pharma, finance and auto pulling the Nifty down. And the
VIX, the price of protection, rose while the broad market rose. The market
under the index is healing faster than the index itself.

## Where the market stands

Start with the three trend lines under each of about 2,260 stocks. The short
line is the twenty-day average. The middle line is the fifty-day average. The
long line is the two-hundred-day average. On Wednesday 919 stocks stood above
the long line, about four in ten. 733 stood above the middle line, about one in
three. 799 stood above the short line, about one in three. All three counts
rose for the fourth day in a row. The short count gained the most again, about
130 stocks in one day.

Each line now tells a different part of the repair. The short count stood at
about 720 the day before the break. Tonight it stands at about 800. So the
short trend has made back everything the break took, and a little more. The
middle count stood at about 760 before the break. Tonight it stands at about
730, so it is nearly home. The long count stood at about 965 before the break
and fell to about 835 on Thursday. Tonight it stands at 919. That is about two
thirds of the way back. The short line healed first, the middle line is close,
and the long line still has work to do. That is the normal order of a repair.

Put that in a longer story. On 7 August about 1,375 stocks held the long line,
the high of this history. On 23 March only about 300 did. The 15 September shock pushed about 350
stocks down four and a half percent in a day. The 8 October shock pushed about
310 down. Each time the long count dropped and then crept back. Tonight it sits
a little past halfway from the March low to the August high.

The day itself was the best of the repair by the count of big moves. 145 stocks
rose four and a half percent or more, against 10 that fell that much. That is
the most big risers since 6 October, when about 200 jumped. It is also the
fewest big fallers in the last month. About 56 stocks in a hundred closed
higher, a little fewer than Tuesday. A thrust day, the kind that marks a real
turn, still looks different in this history. On 1 April more than 1,100 stocks
jumped. On 8 April almost 900 did. Wednesday had 145. It was a strong day, not
a thrust.

Inside the day the buyers kept control. About one stock in five traded four
percent above its prior close at some point. About four in ten of those held
the gain into the close. Only about one stock in eighteen traded four percent
below its prior close. A quarter of those closed near the low. That is the shape of a market where dips are scarce, not only
bought.

The volume improved. Turnover came to about 465 crore against about 460 crore
a year ago. After two sessions below the year-ago figure, it is back level. The
share of busy stocks rose off the bottom of its range, though it still sits in
the lower part. This is no volume surge.

New lows tell the clearest story of the night. 98 stocks made a new yearly low
on Tuesday. On Wednesday only 28 did. That is the fewest since 23 September.
New highs held at about 40. For the first time in the repair, new highs beat
new lows. On 8 October new lows outran new highs by about six to one.

Fear moved the wrong way. The India VIX, a gauge of what traders pay for
protection, rose about four percent to about 14.6. It sits about seven percent
above its thirty-week average and about a quarter above its level three months
ago. On Tuesday the VIX fell while the Nifty rose. On Wednesday it rose while
the Nifty fell. Gold funds are down about six percent over the month,
against about five for the Nifty. The gap between them narrowed, and gold still
gave no shelter this month.

## What would change this read

The read tonight is a repair that has turned strong underneath while the index
lags. It turns worse if the long count falls back below 900 and then below about
835, Thursday's low. It turns worse if new yearly lows climb back above about
100, or if the deep down tail grows past about 15 names. A third shock day with
about 300 hard fallers would match the September and October pattern and open
the way to a lower low. A VIX above about 15 while breadth also weakens would
say the index worry has spread under the surface. The middle count slipping back
under about 620 would undo the first repair test.

The cautious read fails if the repair gets a thrust and the big names join. The
first test is a day of about 300 or more stocks jumping four and a half percent,
on turnover well above its year-ago level. The second is the long count clearing
about 965, the level the day before the 8 October break. The third is the VIX
closing under 14 while new lows stay under 100. A Nifty that rises with the
broad market, not against it, would support all three. Until the thrust and the
big names arrive, the data favours patience, with the edge now tilted toward
repair.
