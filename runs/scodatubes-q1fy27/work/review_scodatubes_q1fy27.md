**Six mandatory diagnostics:**

1. **Did revenue grow YoY?** Yes, **+27.64%** (124.345 vs 97.417). This is well above FY26's full-year +7% and above the walked-back FY27 guidance implied trajectory. Top-line is the one genuinely good number this quarter.
