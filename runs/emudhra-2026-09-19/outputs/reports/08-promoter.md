### 2B. Criminal cases — the 3i Infotech dispute (load-bearing fact)

Full chronology, cross-checked against three primary documents
(clarification letter 04-Feb-2026, investor-call transcript 06-Feb-2026,
EOW-closure letter 18-Aug-2026) plus web search:

- **Jan-2024**: 3i Infotech's new management (post-2023/24 change of
  control, per Srinivasan's account now "predominantly owned by SREI
  Infrastructure at 10-12%, rest retail") first raises the matter by
  letter.
- **Feb-2024**: eMudhra replies in detail; **Mar-2024** management meeting
  held in person; **Sep-2024** further allegations raised by 3i Infotech;
  **Jan-2025** a scheduled document-inspection meeting is cancelled by 3i
  Infotech the evening before; eMudhra's follow-up offer to make records
  available in Bengaluru goes unanswered for over a year.
- **3-Feb-2026**: 3i Infotech discloses (Reg 30) that it has filed a
  complaint with the Economic Offence Wing (EOW), Navi Mumbai Police,
  alleging corporate financial fraud, criminal breach of trust, cheating
  and criminal conspiracy, claim value exceeding Rs 128 crore plus
  interest/damages, relating to (i) the Dec-2010 disinvestment of 3i
  Consumer Services (now eMudhra) to Indus Innovest at what 3i Infotech
  now calls an undervalue, and (ii) the 2015-2017 redemption of Rs 25
  crore preference shares issued to 3i Infotech at the time of that
  disinvestment.
- **4-Feb-2026**: eMudhra denies all allegations (Reg 30), states the
  matter is time-barred (15-16 years old) and that no board member or
  senior manager from the relevant 2010 period remains at 3i Infotech.
  VERIFIED: inputs/announcements/20260204-d507034d.
- **6-Feb-2026**: Investor conference call. Srinivasan's detailed defence,
  on the record: (a) the 2010 hive-off was valued by Deloitte at Rs 30-35
  crore but was actually transacted at Rs 55 crore (i.e., above the
  independent valuation, the opposite of the undervalue allegation); the
  3i Infotech board at the time included ICICI-nominee/eminent directors
  (H.N. Sinor, Vishakha Mulye, a Birla Group CEO); Srinivasan states he
  held no 3i Infotech shares and was purely a salaried CEO, with no
  ownership stake in Indus Innovest or relationship to Ravi Jagannathan;
  (b) the preference-share redemption was staged under a term sheet
  (2015-2023 window, event-triggered acceleration on share sale, capital
  raise, or IPO), the Rs 25 crore was redeemed in tranches through 2017,
  which 3i Infotech itself acknowledged at the time by returning the
  preference certificates and filing the redemption with the Registrar of
  Companies. VERIFIED: inputs/announcements/20260209-3i-Infotech-
  Investor-Call-Transcript (Reg 30(6)-filed transcript, on the record,
  not merely a company blog post).
- **13-Aug-2026**: Navi Mumbai EOW informs 3i Infotech's lawyers that
  after preliminary inquiry the matter "is not criminal in nature and
  could be a civil dispute," and closes the complaint. VERIFIED:
  inputs/announcements/20260818-7509d90d (Reg 30 filing) and corroborated
  independently by media (Whalesbook, ScanX, Multibagg — 📰 MEDIA
  REPORTED, consistent with the primary filing).
- **Post-closure**: media (Whalesbook, 📰) reports 3i Infotech "is in the
  process of filing a complaint with SEBI" and that "the possibility of
  3i Infotech pursuing a civil lawsuit remains." This is UNVERIFIED
  against any primary source available to this run — no SEBI complaint,
  order, or civil suit filing was found in web search as of 19-Sep-2026.
  Flagged as an open, named risk (Monitorable, not a confirmed fact).

**Read**: this is the single most load-bearing legal item on the
