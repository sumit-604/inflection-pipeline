## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts alone)

R1. HFC / Aviom NCLT exposure — promise made, resolution not confirmed.
   Q2 (PDF p.11-12, Chintan Shah): HFC exposure "subjudice," direct
   assignment, holding title docs. Q3 (PDF p.16, Aditya Singhania): Ashish
   says the company will file the final resolution bid with NCLT "in the
   next two weeks... planning to file by 15th February," NCLT+RBI approval
   "between four and six weeks," "hoping it should come this quarter." Q4
   (PDF p.9, Digant Haria): "is there any one-off... is that Aviom write-
   back or something?" Atul: "Aviom we continue to provide, so there is no
   one-off." So the HFC is Aviom; in Q4 it WAS named and addressed, but no
   NCLT-resolution/timeline update was given against the Q3 promise. Real
   red flag on the unfulfilled resolution timeline; NOT the total silence
   B05 asserts.

R2. Forward RoA guidance diluted. Q2 (PDF p.18, Bharat Sharma): from
   "2.7% to 2.8%... 100 basis point improvement over the next 4 to 6
   quarters" (implies ~3.7-3.8%). Q3 (PDF p.14, Atul to Darshil): "For
   FY'27 we are targeting a ROA of 3.2%." Q4 (PDF p.8, Ashish to Digant):
   "3 plus return on assets." Level walked down, never reconciled.

R3. RoE target timeline slippage. Q2 (PDF p.18): "late teens RoE over
   the next 2 to 3 years." Q3 (PDF p.14): "15 to 16 ROE by 6 to 7
   quarters." Q4 (PDF p.8, then p.11 to Raghav): "mid-teens and late-teens
   over next 8 to 10 quarters," clarified "15% to 17%." Band roughly held;
   the quarter-count moved OUT at each restatement.

R4. FY27 credit-cost guidance worsened vs original. Q2 (PDF p.13,
   Hitaindra): "the following year credit cost should be between 2.3% to
   2.5%. It should not be more than that." Q3 (PDF p.14, Ashish to
   Darshil): "we should be around 2.7% to 3.0%." Q4 (PDF p.12,
   Pardhasaradhi): "2.7% to 2.8%." Net worse than the Q2 original.

R5. ECL coverage collapse concurrent with a fresh overlay. Q4 (PDF
   p.10-11, Raghav pressed three times): Stage-2 coverage 24% (Dec) -> 12%
   (Mar), "lower than even FY24 levels," and Stage-3 coverage also falling.
   Explained by RBI FLDG guideline (Feb-2026) + secured-mix shift. Same
   quarter, company created a ₹66cr management overlay "for potential
   unforeseen events" (PDF p.5; p.12 Pardhasaradhi). Thinning buffers +
   simultaneous prudential overlay is an internal-tension flag.

R6. Segment disclosure narrowed despite repeated requests. Q3 (PDF p.16,
   Aditya Singhania): branch-led vs digital MSME split requested; Atul:
   "not as part of this disclosure." Q4 (PDF p.13, Pavan Kumar): "earlier
   on a quarterly basis you used to give credit cost by segment... I can't
   see the quarterly basis." Ashish: "It will be in the full-year basis."
   Granularity reduced from quarterly to annual.

R7. Headline FY26 credit cost (2.8%) is flattered. Q4 (PDF p.12-13,
   Pardhasaradhi to Pavan Kumar): the 2.8% FY26 credit cost is "after
   adjusting for the FLDG benefit and the overlay of INR66 crore"; without
   them "would have been closer to around 120 crores... 2.9% to 3%." So the
   "delivered in line with guidance" credit cost is net of a regulatory
   reclassification benefit plus an overlay. Earnings-quality nuance.

R8. Cost-of-funds narrative reversal. Q2/Q3 framed declining COF as a
   tailwind (Q2 p.5, Q3 p.4-5). Q4 (PDF p.12-13, Atul): "interest rate
   bottoming out," shifting to fixed rate (30%->40%), "G-Sec rates going up
   by close to 50 basis points," hedge costs up, "with inflation going up,
   in next couple of quarters we should see RBI also increasing rates." A
   tailwind reframed as a headwind on a core margin driver.

R9. Rural/MFI branch volatility. 285 (Q2 p.3) -> 278 (Q3 p.3,
   "optimized") -> 342 (Q4 p.4, +64 in one quarter) into the segment that
   was the prior two years' primary stress driver. Management did give a
   calibrate-then-rebuild rationale (Q4 p.8), so "unbridged" is slightly
   strong.

R10. New credit-fund launches dropped. Q2 (PDF p.17): raising for
   "three-four new funds" (climate, MSME x2, women-entrepreneurs). Q3/Q4:
   specific funds never re-named; credit-fund AUM ₹3,207cr (Q3 p.4) ->
   ₹3,092cr (Q4 p.5), i.e. shrank. Q4 opening does contain a generic "new
   sets of funds being launched" (p.8).

R11. MSME branch-count internal inconsistency. Q3 (PDF p.3): "addition
   of 17 new branches during the quarter." Q4 (PDF p.4): "addition of 17
   new branches during FY26." 17-in-a-quarter vs 17-in-a-year cannot both
   be true given Q2 already reported +15 in H1.

R12. Q3 yield jump / risk-adjusted-yield opacity. Q3 (PDF p.11, Shalin
   Kapadia): yields "jumped so much... almost by 80 basis points despite
   very minimal change in the AUM mix." Answer: digital cohort selection,
   "came with a little bit of higher credit cost." Combined with the ₹23cr
   one-off and the "look at net income minus provisions" framing (Digant,
   p.6), headline yield/provisioning is hard to read without management's
   self-defined risk-adjusted-yield lens.
