### D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. What discount rate and long-term growth rate assumptions were used in the FY22 impairment testing of Goodwill (₹251.99cr) and of the investment in Divya Software Solutions (carried at ₹76.02cr against negative consolidated net assets of ₹11.31cr), and why was no impairment recognised?
2. What specifically was reclassified between "Other Financial Liabilities" and "Trade Payables" for the FY21 comparatives (~₹73-100cr across both standalone and consolidated balance sheets), and why does the note carry no narrative beyond "Reclassification items"?
3. What drove the approximately 68% collapse in aggregate subsidiary-level profit contribution in FY22 (International Foam Technologies Spain -39.5%, Joyce Foam Australia -24.4%, Staqo World swinging to a loss), and is this pattern one-off or has it continued in subsequent years?
4. What incremental borrowing rate was used to discount Ind AS 116 lease liabilities (not numerically disclosed in either note set), and what specifically drove the near-doubling of the warranty charge (STA +97.2%, CON +94.7%) and the 35-41% YoY growth in advertisement expense?
5. Given that guarantees for subsidiary borrowings (₹293.01cr, 24.2% of standalone net worth) sit entirely outside the Contingent Liabilities note, what is the Board's own assessment of aggregate off-balance-sheet exposure and its trajectory as Group borrowings continue to rise (+64.3% YoY in FY22)?

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management signal (moderate):** PPE useful lives extended well beyond Schedule II combined with WDV method, corroborated by a D&A charge growing 0.4%-10.9% YoY against 22-25% revenue/capex growth — a quantified, notes-anchored depression of the depreciation charge with no counterfactual disclosed.
- **Aggressive accounting signal (moderate-to-high):** zero impairment on a subsidiary investment (Divya Software Solutions) carried at cost against negative and worsening consolidated net assets, while a deferred tax asset is simultaneously recognised on that same entity's tax losses — an internally inconsistent set of judgements about the entity's recoverability.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator:** off-balance-sheet corporate guarantees to subsidiaries (₹293.01cr) growing 63.5% YoY sit entirely outside the Contingent Liabilities note; while Ind AS-compliant, the practical effect is that a reader of Note 51/52 alone would materially underestimate Group off-balance-sheet exposure.
- **Disclosure-quality flag:** an unexplained prior-year balance-sheet reclassification (~₹73-100cr) and an unexplained goodwill reduction (₹11.08cr) both carry zero narrative in an AR that is otherwise detailed; a statutory ratio note (Note 60) narrates variance reasons selectively, only for ratios that improved.

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices with named concerns. Key
concern: a non-impaired, negative-net-worth subsidiary investment sitting
alongside an unexplained prior-year reclassification and a Group-level
profit decline the standalone headline does not show. Key strength: clean
receivables/payables/MSME position, transparent revenue recognition, and an
unmodified audit opinion with no going concern language in either year.
Overall accounting quality: 6/10.

## FORWARD CONTEXT (weighed, not anchored — dated separately from the FY22 notes above)

The FY22 notes above flagged, on a standalone basis, that PPE useful lives sit
well beyond Schedule II norms and that the WDV depreciation method combined
with those lives was demonstrably suppressing the D&A charge relative to
revenue and capex growth (Findings #8, #14 above; the single largest
"expense capitalisation honesty" concern in this report). Per FY26 concall
disclosures reviewed elsewhere in this run (outside the scope of this note
set and not independently anchored here), SFL changed its depreciation policy
effective 1-Jan-2026 from WDV to Straight Line Method and extended the useful
life of key plant & machinery from 20 to 40 years, adding approximately
₹14cr to FY26 PBT. This is the direct continuation of the same earnings-
quality thread this stage flagged from FY22 evidence: the useful-life/method
choice remains an active lever four years later, and the FY26 change moved in
the direction of *more*, not less, life extension. Separately, FY25 was
disclosed as a PAT trough (₹89cr) with FY26 a recovery (₹160cr) — consistent
in direction (though not verifiable in magnitude from this FY22-only note set)
with the Group-level margin-compression pattern (Findings #1, #4) first
surfacing in these FY22 notes. Operators should treat the FY22-anchored
findings in this report as evidence that this earnings-quality thread is
long-running and pre-dates the FY26 disclosure, not as a new or isolated
FY26 event.
