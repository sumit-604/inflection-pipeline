## Where the market stands

Each stock in the record has three trend lines: 20 days, 50 days and 200 days. A stock above a line is in an uptrend on that clock. On Monday 1,461 stocks stood above the short line, more than six in ten. 1,080 stood above the middle line, a little under half. 1,048 stood above the long line, about 45 in every hundred. The short trend belongs to the buyers. The middle trend is close to even. The long trend still leans to the sellers, but less each week.

The year gives these counts their scale. On 23 March the long count hit a low of 285, on a day when 952 stocks fell hard. On 1 April came a great thrust day, when 1,121 stocks rose hard. The long count then climbed for four months to 1,361 on 7 August. It slid through September and fell to 877 on 8 October. A mid October repair lifted it to 985, and then the floor broke. On 23 October only 605 stocks held the long line. Monday's 1,048 sits more than 440 stocks above that low. Apart from Sunday, the count last stood higher on 25 September.

The bounce began twelve sessions ago. On 23 October only 189 stocks held the 20 day line. By Thursday 5 November the count had reached 1,486. Then Friday brought the first real test. 151 stocks fell hard and only 79 rose hard. The short count dropped to 1,271 and the long count to 981. New yearly lows rose to 33. That was a normal pullback after a sharp run. The worst autumn day, 21 October, had 411 hard fallers. Friday had less than half of that.

The record holds a row dated Sunday 8 November. It has fewer fields than a normal day and no daily folder behind it. It shows 108 hard risers against 14 hard fallers and a short count of 1,535. Monday itself was calm. The hard risers led the hard fallers by 75 to 53. About one stock in eight traded four percent above its prior close at some point. ## What would change this read

The read is an early uptrend, resting on thin volume, led by one industry and the largest names. It turns worse if a day of 300 or more hard fallers returns and goes unanswered. A short count back under about 1,270, Friday's level, would say the bounce is losing its grip. A long count back under about 980 would undo the last week's progress. A long count under about 605, the 23 October low, would erase the bounce. New yearly lows above about 100 would say sellers have returned. A VIX close above about 18 would say fear has taken charge. A sharp fall in healthcare on heavy volume would remove the one group carrying the conviction.

The cautious read fails if the money arrives and the fear gauge falls. The first test is a wide up day, with 200 or more hard risers, on turnover above about 610 crore. Monday reached about 390. The second test is the long count above about 1,165, half the market. Monday reached 1,048. The third test is a VIX close under 15. Monday closed near 17.4. The fourth test is gains held into the close. About half of the stocks that trade four percent up should keep it. Monday held fewer than four in ten. Two of these in the same week, with the short count above 1,270, would confirm the uptrend.
