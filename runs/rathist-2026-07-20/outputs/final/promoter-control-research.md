# Promoter and Control Deep Dive — Rathi Steel & Power (RATHIST)

Operator-requested during the FTTCP review (2026-07-20). The business is ordinary, so the operator asked whether the real bet is a new promoter or control group. This memo answers that.

STATUS OF EVIDENCE. Two tiers. Tier 1, ANCHORED: the shareholding structure taken from the FY2024-25 annual report Note 11 (a filing). Tier 2, NON-ANCHORED: everything from web research, because this session's egress proxy blocked every primary source (BSE filings, MCA master data, the company website, Zauba, Tofler, screener, Trendlyne all returned CONNECT 403). Tier 2 is REPORTED or INFERRED from search-engine snippets only. Treat Tier 2 as leads, not evidence, until the named filings are pulled from a normal browser.

## Bottom line (decided)

The "capable new promoter took over a cleaned-up asset" thesis is NOT supported and leans contradicted. Control still sits with the founding Rathi family, moved out of the two convicted individuals and into a family holding vehicle, PCR Holdings Private Limited (formerly Archit Securities Private Limited, 26.41%). "PCR" maps to Punam Chand Rathi, the P.C. Rathi group. The family stepped off the board for optics while keeping control, and the executive slate is Rathi-Group-linked insiders, not outside turnaround operators. The revival was an ordinary promoter-led debt settlement with ACRE, not an external acquisition. The one genuinely new and unresolved item is a large, opaque non-promoter block from the FY24 recap whose owners are unnamed. Net for the investment case: the promoter axis does not rescue an ordinary business; it adds governance risk.
