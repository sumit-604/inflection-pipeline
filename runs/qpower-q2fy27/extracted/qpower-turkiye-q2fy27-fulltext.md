Subject: Intimation under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Commencement of
Manufacturing Operations at PCS Facility in Nide, Türkiye

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are pleased to inform you that the new Power Conversion Systems (PCS) manufacturing facility
of our subsidiary, Endoks Energy Systems, at Nide, Türkiye, commenced manufacturing
operations on 16 October 2026.

The facility is dedicated to the manufacturing of power electronic converters and inverters for utility-
scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and other grid-connected applications.

This development represents an important step in the Group's expansion into power electronics and
energy storage technologies, complementing its established portfolio of high-voltage equipment
and power quality solutions.

The new facility strengthens the Group's manufacturing and system integration capabilities,
supports the execution of its international BESS order book and enhances its ability to address the
growing requirements for grid flexibility, renewable energy integration and energy storage
infrastructure.

The facility will progressively ramp up production in accordance with its operational plans and
customer delivery schedules.

The above information is being submitted for your records pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI
(Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
