₹1,400.00 lakh MoU-stated total, consistent with a modest final

OPEN ITEM 3 — MSME AGEING TENSION: RECONCILED, BUT RECHARACTERISED AS A
WORSE FINDING THAN "INTERNAL TENSION," PLUS A NOTE-ATTRIBUTION CORRECTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Correction to Pass 1: the ₹80.90 lakh MSME 1-2-year ageing bucket Pass 1
attributed to "standalone Note 37" is actually in CONSOLIDATED Note 35
(p.143), not the standalone note. On direct re-read, STANDALONE Note 37
("Disclosure Related to Trade Payables," p.111) shows the ENTIRE FY26
ageing table — MSME-Due, Others-Due, Disputed dues, MSME-Undue,
Others-Undue, and Total — populated with "-" (nil) across every single
bucket, while the FY25 table in the SAME note (immediately below it)
carries real figures (MSME-Due ₹93.65 lakh, Others-Due ₹889.70 lakh,
Total ₹1,657.40 lakh). This is despite the standalone balance sheet
showing FY26 trade payables of ₹1,800.39 lakh (MSME ₹208.64 lakh + Others
₹1,591.75 lakh, per Note 7, p.101) — a real, non-zero year-end balance
that the standalone ageing note fails to break down by age bucket at all
for FY26. 🔴 Red Flag, NEW — a genuine standalone-level disclosure gap
distinct from anything in Pass 1 (the consolidated Note 35 IS correctly
populated for FY26; only the standalone Note 37 table is blank for the
current year).

On the substantive tension itself (Note 34(b) "no principal paid beyond
the appointed date" = nil, both years, vs the ageing note showing MSME
dues aged 1-2 years): these two disclosures are not actually
contradictory once the definitions are separated — Note 34(b) measures
principal PAID during the year that was late; the ageing note measures
the AGE of amounts still unpaid and outstanding at the balance sheet
date. If the ₹80.90 lakh (consolidated) of 1-2-year-old MSME dues remain
wholly UNPAID at year end, "principal paid beyond the appointed date" is
correctly reported as nil — nothing was paid, on time or late, so there
is nothing to disclose under 34(b). [INFERENCE]: this reconciliation
makes the underlying fact WORSE than Pass 1's "apparent tension" framing
implied — it is not a reporting inconsistency but evidence that roughly
₹80.90 lakh of the consolidated group's ₹211.12 lakh MSME-due balance
(38.3% of MSME dues) has sat entirely UNPAID for more than a year,
un-remedied, with no interest-on-delay accrual disclosed against that
specific aged tranche beyond the blanket ₹2.92 lakh (FY26)/₹4.10 lakh
(FY25) interest-on-delay figures in Note 34/36. 🔴 Red Flag, upgraded
from Pass 1's 🟡 Watch — reclassify from "reconcile the tension" to
"confirmed: a material share of aged MSME dues remains unpaid beyond a
year," a question for management, not just a disclosure nuance.
