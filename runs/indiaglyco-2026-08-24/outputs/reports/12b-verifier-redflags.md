## PART 1 — Independent red-flag list (from raw transcripts)

Anchors are (call, speaker, page).

R1. FX / currency hedging never mentioned in any call, despite explicit
currency exposure. Q1 FY27, Rupark: "The rupee hit a record low" and
"freights for westbound cargos ... up anywhere between 5 to 20 times"
(Aug 2026, p3-4). Large export book (New Park/Middle East, US, Europe) and
import book (glycols, propylene oxide). Zero hedge disclosure across four
calls. Severity: MAJOR.

R2. OMC ethanol-allocation pressure under-disclosed against a uniformly
upbeat Bio-Fuels narrative. Nov 2025, Rupark: "the OMCs have given not
complete allocations. They have given some allocations" and "some bit of
excess ethanol capacity" (Nov 2025, p17-18). The pipeline's flagged ~-22%
YoY allocation decline is never named. Management frames excess capacity as
an opportunity (in-house ethanol for chemicals), not a demand risk.
Severity: MAJOR.

R3. Bio-glycols/MEG realisation decline not addressed by name. Glycols
commentary is uniformly positive: Q1 FY27 "glycols business value up 83%"
(Aug 2026, p7); Q4 "our glycols did well" (Jun 2026, p7). The prior-stage
-29.9% realisation drop is never reconciled. Severity: MAJOR
(structurally unverifiable from spirits/sugar peers per B06).

R4. Performance-chemicals / NSU "doubling" promise missed, rolled forward
without a clean acknowledgment. Nov 2025: "nearly a doubling of revenue and
contribution for the year ... For H2, it is much more than doubling"
(p11), plus "potentially ... 10x ... over the next few years" (p11). Q4:
"hoping for in excess of doubling this business this year" (Jun 2026, p13).
Q1 FY27: "performance chemicals business grew by 40%, which was lower than
what we had targeted" (Aug 2026, p7). Severity: MAJOR.

R5. NCLT demerger timeline slipped ~6 weeks, not proactively flagged.
Q4: "21st May we are hopeful ... first 10 days of the June we will get the
order" (Jun 2026, p18). Q1 FY27: "NCLT has now sanctioned the scheme ...
on 17th of July 2026" (Aug 2026, p4). No proactive mention of the slip.
Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

R6. Segment-wise debt breakup deflected. Q4, Balasubramanian asks for "that
debt break-up in all three segments"; Anand: "if you want the debt profile,
I will give you ... which is in process and I will share with you"
(Jun 2026, p18) — not given on call. (Note: in Nov 2025 a two-way split was
given, 600 IGL / 800 IGL Spirits, p9.) Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

R7. Two materially different historical EBITDA-margin series across calls,
never reconciled. Nov 2025 + Feb 2026 cite FY22 ~11%, then 13% / 14% /
14.9% / 15.0% (Nov p4; Feb p5). Jun 2026 + Aug 2026 cite FY22 9.6%, FY23
11.9%, FY24 12.9%, FY25 13.9%, FY26 15.5% (Jun p3; Aug p3). The base year
is restated from 11% down to 9.6%, which enlarges the improvement story.
Severity: MAJOR (I weight this above B05's "low").

R8. LanzaTech dropped from the narrative after a soft Q3 update. Feb 2026,
Rupark on LanzaTech: "The volumes have picked up lower from what we have
thought ... I don't have a ready data to tell you volume" (p11). Name absent
from Q4 and Q1 FY27 (replaced by generic "carbon smart ethoxylates").
Severity: MINOR-MAJOR.

R9. Gross prepayment far exceeds net-debt reduction — not flagged by the
pipeline. Q4, Varun Mehta: "we have repaid around 800 crores, but net debt
from last year is only down by 220 crores." Anand: "you are not able to see
the reduction, because we have reduced our cash credit limits ... not
visible" (Jun 2026, p18). A 804 crore "prepayment" that nets to only ~220
crore of deleveraging materially softens the debt-reduction thesis.
Severity: MAJOR.

R10. Ennature Q1 FY27 "best-ever" numbers are internally inconsistent
within one call. Opening (Rupark): "revenue rising 65% year-on-year and
EBITDA increasing 188%" (Aug 2026, p3). CFO (Anand): "net revenue is 90
crores, up by 53% ... EBITDA is 10 crores, which is almost about 100%
year-on-year growth" (p4). Later (Rupark): "best-ever quarterly performance
at 83 crores, growth of over 65% in the prior quarter" (p8). Three revenue
frames (90 vs 83; +65% vs +53% YoY) and two EBITDA frames (+188% vs ~+100%),
unreconciled. Severity: MINOR (smallest segment) but a real contradiction.

R11. Spirits Q1 FY27 volume grew faster than revenue, the reverse of the
premiumization thesis. Pragyam: "number of cases grew ... but revenue did
not grew proportionately"; Manoj: state mix (Delhi) and mass-premium mix,
"You will find our revenue growth exceeding the volume growth in due course"
(Aug 2026, p14-15). Premiumization is the central spirits thesis; this
quarter contradicted it. Severity: MINOR.

R12. Two-part collaboration question half-dodged. Feb 2026, Rohit asks
about "LanzaTech, and one was with Lululemon"; management answers LanzaTech
only and never addresses Lululemon (Feb 2026, p11). Severity: MINOR.

R13. Sequential-EBITDA-growth question deflected to email under insistence.
Q1 FY27, Aman presses twice on EBITDA growth "10.26% ... negative 5.16% ...
1.69%"; after one partial (Clariant dividend) answer, "I request you; you
send us a mail" (Aug 2026, p15). Severity: MINOR.

Observation (not counted as a distinct red flag): 98 crore silver sale on
catalyst change (purchase cost 51 crore) kept out of the P&L. Q4, Anand:
"silver sale during the shutdown was 98 crores ... purchase price ... 51
crores ... no profit on the silver ... has not been shown in the books"
(Jun 2026, p15). Disclosed and explained; neutral, but a 47 crore gross gap
routed around the P&L is worth naming. Severity: MINOR.

### Pipeline flags I did NOT independently raise — support test
Every B05/B06 red flag is SUPPORTED by transcript evidence; none is
invented. Two are OVERSTATED in framing:

- B05 `repeated_evasions` lists `quarters_asked: [Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26]` for the
  segment-debt-breakup deflection. The clear on-call deflection is Q4 only
  (Balasubramanian). In Q3 the debt questions were year-end gross debt +
  capex (answered) and repayment roadmap (answered); I find no Q3
  segment-breakup deflection. The "deflected every time" across two quarters
  is imprecise. B05 lacked the Nov 2025 call, where the request was actually
  answered (600/800 split). Severity: MINOR (overstated, not fabricated).

- B06 marks the UP-market volume claim `contradicted` by Globus (~1 crore
  cases/month vs IGL's 23-25 lakh). This is reconcilable, not a true
  contradiction: IGL's 23-25 lakh figure is explicitly IMFL only (Feb 2026,
  Vaziraney, p7), and IGL's own country-liquor figure is ~90 lakh cases/month
  in UP (Nov 2025, Shukla, p14). 90 lakh IMIL + ~23-25 lakh IMFL ≈ 1.1 crore,
  which matches Globus's total-market ~1 crore. B06 did flag it for operator
  resolution as a likely segment-scope mismatch, which is the right call, but
  the `contradicted` label overstates it. Severity: MINOR (overstated).
