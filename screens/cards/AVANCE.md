## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Avance trades IT products. The accounts call it "one segment of activity of dealing in IT products" (AR26 p100). In FY26 standalone revenue was Rs 101.08 crore and cost of products Rs 101.09 crore (AR26 p79). The trading spread is nil. The profit comes from the treasury. Standalone other income was Rs 13.81 crore: Rs 3.81 crore interest and Rs 10.00 crore long-term capital gain (AR26 p79). That is 104% of standalone profit before tax of Rs 13.22 crore. A subsidiary, Avance Ventures Pvt Ltd, runs a second trading book: Rs 58.17 crore turnover and Rs 0.07 crore profit before tax in FY26 (AR26 p35).

**What is changing?** Two things. First, the subsidiary is scaling. Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue was Rs 60.65 crore against Rs 17.89 crore standalone (Q1R p8, p4). The subsidiary sold about Rs 42.8 crore in one quarter. Second, management has turned over. The MD of 23 years died on 1 July 2025. Two MDs followed within 12 months (AR26 p19).

**Why now?** Consolidated profit after tax rose to Rs 13.24 crore in FY26 from Rs 5.30 crore (AR26 p16). The Rs 10.00 crore capital gain explains the rise. Strip out other income and the consolidated business lost Rs 0.60 crore in FY26, after earning Rs 5.15 crore in FY25 (computed from AR26 p16).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The subsidiary must earn a real spread at scale. The Rs 229.37 crore of trade advances and the Level 3 investments must be worth their book value (AR26 p74-75). Book value is the only case for a company priced at 0.40 times equity.

**What breaks it?** The book. Trade advances equal 58% of consolidated equity of Rs 394.16 crore (AR26 p75, p109). They carry no provision. Unnamed lenders fund Rs 164.06 crore of unsecured loans at almost no interest. If the advances do not come back, equity is mostly share capital over negative retained earnings.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Standalone is back-to-back trading. Employee cost was Rs 0.08 crore for the whole year (AR26 p79). The company has no product, plant or disclosed IP.

Consolidated adds Avance Ventures. It has Rs 0.10 crore of capital and Rs 157.07 crore of total assets (AR26 p35). Consolidated inventory rose to Rs 90.80 crore from Rs 35.63 crore (AR26 p109). The other two subsidiaries have no turnover (AR26 p35).

The one economic engine is the balance sheet: interest on Rs 25.64 crore of loans and advances, and gains on investments (AR26 p75, p79).

**Archetype: no library fit.** The nearest is commodity converter, as a zero-spread pass-through trader, and Amendment 17 would bind. The earnings engine sits at R0. This needs an operator ruling, not a forced label.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The screen hit on a capital gain. No operating business sits under it yet. The balance sheet carries the only value claim, and nothing in this corpus verifies it.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first, if it ever returns**
1. Who holds the Rs 229.37 crore of trade advances, and whether any of it has come back as goods or cash.
2. Who lent the Rs 164.06 crore of unsecured loans, on what terms, and whether any lender is connected to the company.
3. The Q2 FY27 consolidated results, once OCR works.
4. What was sold to book the Rs 10.00 crore gain, and to whom.
5. The real date and reason for the auditor resignation.

**What would change the view**
- Two quarters of consolidated operating profit above Rs 2 crore with tax provided.
- Recovery of trade advances in cash, disclosed in a filing.
- A named, arm's-length lender for the unsecured debt.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`. It is not a position.
