### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

No documented case of a missed promise accompanied by an excuse was found in the AR or the two results filings — the only genuine (partial) shortfall identified (WtE commissioning) was framed by management from the outset as a multi-year buildout rather than presented, missed, and then explained. `excuse_pattern` is therefore set to **"insufficient-evidence"** rather than forced into one of the three standard buckets — there is no excuse to classify because no clear miss-with-explanation pair exists in the documents provided.

One point of genuine, voluntary transparency worth noting: the ₹1,029.28 lakh ESOP exceptional charge was disclosed proactively in the Q3 FY26 filing (Note 5) *before* year-end, rather than surfacing for the first time in the FY26 results — a mild positive signal on proactive disclosure of a dilutive/costly item (ESOP conversion drops JUISL's holding in JWIL from 54.60% to 50.01% on full exercise).

### 2C. Tone ratings (1-5, with evidence)

No concall exists, so tone in the analyst-facing sense (defensiveness under questioning, spontaneity) cannot be rated. What can be assessed is the *written disclosure tone* of the AR/Board's Report against the audited numbers:

- **Transparency (legal/going-concern matters): 4/5.** The auditor's going-concern and emphasis-of-matter paragraphs on JITFWIL/NRDA and JUWMBL (Bathinda), and the detailed Jalandhar/Ferozepur arbitration history (Board's Report / FY26 Results, Note 2), are disclosed in full procedural detail, including adverse developments (Supreme Court restoring Section 34 petitions on 20.02.2026).
- **Transparency (financial-health narrative): 2/5.** The MD&A prose never mentions the consolidated net worth position (see 2D) despite prominent tables on revenue/EBITDA/PBT growth — a material omission from the *narrative*, even though the number itself is fully disclosed in the audited financial statements.
- **Specificity: 4/5.** Guidance is generally quantified (order book breakdowns, MW figures, grant amounts, named projects) rather than vague.
- **Consistency: 4/5.** Cross-checked figures tie out: JWIL revenue "+21% YoY" in Board's Report (p.3) matches the consolidated Water Infrastructure segment revenue growth of ~21.2% (₹2,232.37 cr FY26 vs ₹1,842.77 cr FY25, FY26 Results segment note); EPC + O&M order book components (₹8,767 cr + ₹2,585 cr = ₹11,352 cr) sum correctly (AR MD&A p.84).
- **Accountability: not assessable** — no admitted miss exists in the documents to test against.
- **Over-promotion: 3/5.** The MD&A leads with extensive macro/TAM slides (global GDP, India water-treatment CAGR of 10.6%, WtE market CAGR of 2.6%) before company-specific detail — standard AR practice, but the volume of market sizing relative to balance-sheet discussion is notable.

### 2D. What they are NOT saying

- **Deeply negative consolidated net worth is never narrated in the MD&A.** FY26 Results (consolidated balance sheet): Other Equity ₹(51,841.88) lakh, Net Worth ₹(51,327.81) lakh — i.e. consolidated net worth of approximately **₹(513) crore**, marginally worse than FY25's ₹(510.78) crore. This sits alongside standalone (parent-only) net worth of a positive ₹320.78 crore (FY26 Results, standalone balance sheet) — a large divergence explained by subsidiary-level accumulated losses/leverage, but the MD&A prose (which discusses revenue growth, EBITDA margins, and market TAM at length) makes no mention of this figure at all.
- **Group leverage scale is not discussed narratively.** Consolidated non-current + current borrowings total approximately ₹3,94,548 lakh (~₹3,945 crore) as at 31 Mar 2026 (FY26 Results, consolidated balance sheet) against the JWIL-specific leverage ratios that Board's Report does disclose (Debt/Equity 0.30, Gearing 23.15%). The group-wide borrowing base is far larger than the JWIL-only figures presented, and is not reconciled or discussed in the MD&A risk section beyond generic "financing and interest-rate risk" language (AR p.86).
- **Going-concern qualifications are legally disclosed but not flagged as risk items in the MD&A's own "Risk Management" section** (AR p.85-86), which lists concession/tariff risk, receivables risk, execution risk, financing risk, commodity risk, feedstock risk and regulatory/environmental risk — but does not name the two subsidiaries (JITFWIL/NRDA, JUWMBL) actually carrying auditor-flagged going-concern doubt.
