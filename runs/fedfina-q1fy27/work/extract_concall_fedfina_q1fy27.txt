151	This quarter there has been a regulatory transition for the gold lending industry, which I expect will
152	be a common theme across the sector this season. Effective April 1, the RBI revised the LTV
153	framework, requiring onboarding LTVs on bullet loans to be calculated on the total amount due at
154	maturity — that is, principal and interest together — rather than on principal alone. In response, and
155	to support our customers while maintaining prudent risk standards, we have adopted a periodic
156	interest-due structure, and we continue to operate at LTVs below the regulatory limits. As customer
157	repayment behaviour is unlikely to change immediately, reported overdue levels are expected to
158	remain elevated in the near term. We view it as customer behaviour in transition rather than an asset
159	quality concern.
