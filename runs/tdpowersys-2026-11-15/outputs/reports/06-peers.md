Anchor convention: "p.N" is the "[page N]" marker in the page-marked .txt (the PDF page). In most files the transcript's own "Page N of M" label is one lower. 4. Governance. Peer transcripts cannot settle this. No evidence in this stage.

## PART 2: Unprompted cross-read

### 2A Demand environment
Consensus at the OEM and equipment level, split downstream. CG backlog is Rs 17,333 cr, up 45% (CG Q1 FY27 p.2-3); Azad's book is about Rs 6,500 cr, 11x-12x FY26 revenue (AZAD Q4 FY26 p.6); gas turbine makers are sold out 4-5 years (TRITURBINE Q3 FY26 p.12-13; Q1 FY27 p.11). Against that, Triveni's domestic orders fell 35.4% in Q1 FY27 and domestic enquiries dipped, while its export orders rose 53.4% (TRITURBINE Q1 FY27 p.3). CG's revenue growth slowed to 16% from 22% and management said "there can be a lull" (CG Q1 FY27 p.6-7). Data-centre demand is large in enquiries (Triveni: "well in excess of half of the enquiry book", Q4 FY26 p.25) but Triveni has not yet booked it (Q1 FY27 p.11). Only CG disclosed a booked data-centre order (CG Q3 FY26 p.3-4). Silence that informs: no peer mentions generator demand, and no peer mentions a generator shortage. TD's framing of a data-centre generator boom therefore rests on the gas turbine OEM tier (Azad, Triveni confirm sold-out OEMs) and on TD's own disclosure, not on peers who sell generators.

### 2B Pricing and input costs
Peers split by contract structure. CG: price variation clauses with no cap in Power, margin up 280-380 bps (CG Q3 FY26 p.3, p.12; Q4 FY26 p.4); motors repriced 17.5% plus about 5% and still short of commodity inflation (CG Q4 FY26 p.12; Q1 FY27 p.4-5). Azad: raw material cost cut from about 12% to 5% of sales through Indian suppliers and a 5% cap on input swings, EBITDA 37.6% (AZAD Q1 FY27 p.4, p.17-18; Q2 FY26 p.10-11). Triveni: fixed-price, bought-out content 30-70% of cost, EBITDA 18.0% vs 25.8% a year earlier; management says orders taken a year ago at lower margin, with order-booking margins "substantially higher" now (TRITURBINE Q1 FY27 p.3, p.9, p.14). TD's gross margin trail (Q4 FY26 30.7%, Q1 34.7%, Q2 33.5%, target moved from 35% to 34%) fits a partial-pass-through profile closer to Triveni than CG. This is an inference; TD's price-variation coverage is a document question for stage 3 or the AR, not a peer fact. Rupee: Azad says a 5-6% revenue lift but a "natural hedge" (AZAD Q1 FY27 p.15).

### 2C Capex cycle
Industry-wide capacity race in the demand chain, with one asset-light exception. CG: transformer capacity from about 17,000 to 75,000 MVA in a year, plus 45,000 MVA greenfield, switchgear greenfield Rs 748 cr, EHV breakers +80% (CG Q4 FY26 p.10; Q1 FY27 p.3, p.5). Azad: eight dedicated plants, Rs 392 cr capitalised in FY26, QIP Rs 700 cr (AZAD Q4 FY26 p.7; Q2 FY26 p.17). Triveni: "no plans right now of setting up new factories", Sompura expansion only (TRITURBINE Q2 FY26 p.22). CG's own words on the race: "everybody is trying to expand the capacities... Now is that capacity enough for the whole world? The answer is no" (CG Q1 FY27 p.4). So TD's Rs 3,200 cr FY28 and Rs 4,000 cr plus FY29-30 plan, funded by Rs 675 cr raised, is one entry in a race, not a lone bet in a cautious set. Azad also raised Rs 700 cr by QIP and tied capex to firm contracts (AZAD Q3 FY26 p.4); TD's QIP object is NOT FOUND in B05.
