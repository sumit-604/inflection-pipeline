## Active flags

### FLAG-CASH, determination GROWTH-INDUCED
Evidence for growth induced over structural: consolidated OCF/PAT ran 1.62x and improving from 1.09x, standalone 1.63x from 0.71x (B02 Cash Flow Statement p.173; B03 Phase 3A recompute). Consolidated trade receivables rose 30.7% and unbilled 77.5% against 23.6% revenue growth (B02 Note 12 p.189-190), but the build concentrates in the BioPharm consolidation and in large deals whose revenue is recognised into FY27 (B05 flags; B12b missed-flag anchor). Standalone receivables fell 31% while standalone revenue grew 11.6% (B02 receivables_trend, Standalone Note 8/22). Billed DSO improved 72 to 62 days (B04, Inv. Pres. slide 9) and the ECL allowance was reversed on a genuine ageing improvement, corroborated twice (B02 Note 12 and Note 21). Net signal is mixed but not clean deterioration.
Residual evidence gap: the credit rating rationale that would independently confirm the working capital read was NOT collected (B00 input gap, rating MEDIUM; no rating rationale quote available). Obtain the latest rating rationale from the agency or exchange filing to close it.
Falsification metric (single, GROWTH-INDUCED requires it): in Q2 FY27 results, consolidated OCF/PAT prints below 1.0x while consolidated trade receivables excluding the BioPharm base grow faster than revenue excluding acquisitions. If that combination appears, reclassify the flag toward STRUCTURAL and re-open the cash quality thesis.

### FLAG-CONTINGENT-LIABILITY
Combined disclosed contingent liabilities of Rs 1,531 mn (transfer pricing Rs 1,114 mn + TCPA maximum Rs 417 mn) equal 38.2% of FY26 consolidated PAT, above the 25% threshold (B03; B02 Note 33 p.204, Note 37 p.206). Unresolved contradiction on the largest item: Board's Report Item 10 discloses a FINAL Section 144B tax demand of Rs 436.88 mn for AY2023-24, while Note 33 describes the same AY as a pending draft order with no final order received (B03; B08 adverse finding). Both statuses cannot be true. Resolve before sizing the exposure.

### FLAG-GOODWILL
Goodwill and intangibles reached 51.5% of consolidated net worth (Rs 16,176 mn of Rs 31,387 mn) in the same year the enterprise commercial CGU impairment-test discount rate fell 17.2% to 12.5% and terminal growth rose to 5.0%, with Rs 6,429 mn of new BioPharm goodwill landing in that CGU (B03; B02 Note 6 p.185, Note 7 p.185-188). Less conservative assumptions were adopted in the exact year the goodwill was booked.

## Contradicted claims, priority monitoring

1. Management's six to eight quarter margin recovery framing, "we have done this before", is contradicted by the peer set (B06 flag Q4). The one comparable multi quarter recovery, IKS Health's AQuity transformation, beat its own timeline by three to four quarters; Sagility absorbed its BroadPath margin drag inside one fiscal year (B06 contradicted, IKS Nov 2025, Sagility May 2026 calls). The precedent argues Indegene is slow, not proven.
2. BioPharm is consolidated at GROSS revenue (gross ~$38.1m vs net ~$29.2m, media pass-through), which inflates the INR headline growth rate and dilutes blended margin, and was never framed upstream as a revenue-quality issue (B12b MAJOR fully missed; Oct meet p.6, Q2 p.12, Q3 p.7, Q4 p.10).
3. The Board's Report versus Note 33 tax-status contradiction: a FINAL Rs 436.88 mn Section 144B demand versus a pending draft order for the identical AY2023-24 (B03; B08).

## Monitorables and triggers, deduplicated

1. Cash quality. Watch consolidated OCF/PAT and receivables ex-BioPharm versus revenue ex-acquisition in Q2 FY27 results; the falsification threshold above tests whether the working capital build is growth induced or structural.
2. Organic growth. Watch constant currency ex-M&A growth in quarterly results; reacceleration above the Q3 FY26 18.3% pace confirms the demand thesis, staying near the Q4 FY26 12% print for two straight quarters kills it.
3. EBITDA margin recovery. Watch reported margin toward the near 20% historical range by H2 FY27 in quarterly results; sustained non-recovery undercuts the whole investment-phase payoff story.
4. Transfer pricing and Section 144B. Watch next AR Note 33 and Board's Report for any provision, MAP outcome, or resolution of the final-versus-draft contradiction; this settles the largest contingent liability.
5. TCPA settlement. Watch next AR Note 37 for court approval and actual payout against the Rs 203 mn provisioned and Rs 417 mn maximum; tests provision adequacy.
6. Goodwill impairment. Watch next AR Note 6 for any charge or disclosed headroom; tests whether the FY26 assumption easing was justified given 51.5% goodwill-to-net-worth.
7. Contingent consideration outflows. Watch next AR Note 16 and 27a for actual earnout payments against the Rs 2,914 mn carrying value as earnouts lapse over FY27 and FY28; tests the real cash cost of the deals.
8. Governance follow through. Watch the next Corporate Governance Report for director attendance returning above 75% and resolution of Tenneti's long tenure; tests whether FY26 gaps were transition related or a pattern.

## Falsification line
Q2 FY27 organic constant currency ex-M&A growth prints at or below the Q4 FY26 12% YoY level for a second consecutive quarter. That single print would confirm the growth deceleration is structural rather than an acquisition-consolidation optic, and would do the most damage to the transition thesis.
