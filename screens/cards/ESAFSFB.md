## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** ESAF is a small finance bank with 821 outlets in 24 states and 2 union territories (Q1 deck, p.28). It takes retail deposits, 91% retail and 71% from Kerala (Q1 deck, pp.6-7), and lends to low-income households. Gross advances were Rs 23,216 crore at 30-Jun-2026: MARG (MSME, agri, retail, gold) Rs 12,909 crore, Emerging Household Rs 7,351 crore, legacy group microfinance Rs 2,362 crore, corporate Rs 594 crore (Q1 deck, p.9). Gold alone is Rs 9,728 crore, 42% of the book (Q1 deck, p.11; CARE, p.1). Money comes from the spread: yield on advances 17.2%, cost of deposits 7.0%, NIM 7.9% in Q1 FY27 (Q1 deck, p.4). Microfinance is served through 31 business correspondents, which cost Rs 399 crore in FY26 (Q1 deck, p.28; AR26, p.129).

**What is changing?** The book is moving from unsecured group loans to secured and individual loans. Secured advances rose from 31% at 31-Mar-2024 to 62% at 30-Jun-2026 (CARE, p.2). Legacy group loans fell 58% in a year (Q1 deck, p.9). The 2022 and 2023 microfinance stress was sold: Rs 1,019 crore of NPAs went to asset reconstruction companies in FY26 (CARE, p.1), including Rs 1,694 crore of outstanding for Rs 183 crore in December 2025 (ARC, p.1). Slippages fell from Rs 468 crore in Q1 FY26 to Rs 75 crore in Q1 FY27 (Q1 deck, p.18).

**Why now?** Profit turned in Q3 FY26 and has risen each quarter: Rs 7 crore, Rs 24 crore, Rs 80 crore (Q4 res, p.8; Q1 res, p.5). CARE moved its outlook to Stable from Negative on 01-Sep-2026 (CARE, p.1); Brickwork upgraded to A- on 17-Aug-2026 (BWR, p.2). Management guides credit cost of about 2% and ROA of 2% by the end of FY27 (Aug-26 call, pp.7-8). The stock trades at 1.2 times book (MD: price Rs 40.9, book Rs 34.6).

**What must be true?** Credit cost must fall from 4.4% to about 2% without new stress in the Emerging Household book, 32% of advances at a 22.1% yield (Q1 deck, p.13). The gold book must hold at a 72% loan-to-value (Aug-26 call, p.11). NIM must stay above 7.5% as the mix turns secured (Aug-26 call, p.7). The Tier 1 raise hoped for by March 2027 must price above book (Aug-26 call, p.9).

**What breaks it?** A second microfinance cycle inside the Emerging Household book, whose GNPA rose to Rs 362 crore from Rs 326 crore in two quarters (Q1 deck, p.14). A gold price fall against a 42% gold book. Gearing of 18.18 times on tangible net worth of Rs 1,597 crore, still below pre-stress levels (CARE, p.2). Or the Rs 625 crore Q2 write-off proving to be new stress rather than old (Q2 KBU, p.2).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Distribution: present.** 821 outlets, 69% rural and semi-urban, 1.04 crore customers, a correspondent network built over nine years (Q1 deck, p.28; Aug-26 call, p.5). Management calls it the asset it could not use during the stress (May-26 call, p.7).

**Funding cost: absent.** CASA 23.4%, cost of deposits 7.0% (Q1 deck, p.4). CARE lists CASA as a weakness (CARE, p.3). Brickwork names "CASA drift" as a credit risk (BWR, p.4).

**Pricing power: partial, mix-driven.** The 17.2% yield comes from the 22% to 24% unsecured segments; gold yields 14.7% and MSME 10.3% (Q1 deck, p.11). Brickwork says larger banks "offer more attractive loan terms" (BWR, p.4).

**Underwriting: unproven on the new book.** The bank did not insure its microfinance book under CGFMU and "will be taking a call going forward" (Aug-26 call, p.11).

**Verdict: a distribution moat is present; funding and underwriting moats are absent or forming.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

For a lender the rungs run on asset quality and RoA. Specialised rungs used here: R1 credit-cycle taker (RoA swings with the cycle, credit cost sets earnings); R2 low-cost funder (margin from CASA and opex); R3 secured diversified lender (RoA 1.5% to 2% held through a cycle, GNPA under 4%); R4 franchise bank (RoA above 2%, RoE above 20%, strong CASA).

**FROM: R1.** ROA went 1.86% in FY24 to (1.94)% in FY25 to (0.6)% in FY26 (AR26, p.35). The microfinance cycle set earnings.

**TO: R3, claimed.** Management's claim, once: "ROA, we expect that it will be 2% by year-end" (Aug-26 call, p.8), with a steady state of 2% to 2.5% (Aug-26 call, p.10).

Ladder check. The claim skips R2: CASA 23.4% and cost of deposits 7.0% (Q1 deck, p.4), so the climb rests on asset mix alone. R1 to R3 in two years, FY26 loss to FY28 steady state, beats the one-rung-per-2-to-3-years base rate. That speed is itself a flag.

## 8. Proof check

The last two filed quarters are Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27; Q2 FY27 is not filed. The test runs on those two.

**Direction confirmed on four lines.** PAT Rs 24 crore to Rs 80 crore; NII Rs 518 crore to Rs 584 crore; slippages Rs 106 crore to Rs 75 crore; NNPA 1.8% to 0.8% (Q1 deck, pp.18, 23). Neither quarter had an ARC sale or write-off (Q1 deck, p.18), so the moves are organic.

**The proof point is credit cost, and it has not moved.** 4.1% in Q4 FY26, 4.4% in Q1 FY27 (Q1 deck, p.16); provisions Rs 214 crore and Rs 242 crore (Q1 deck, p.23). Management rests the 2% exit on net NPA stock of Rs 184 crore and gross slippage of 1.4% annualised (Aug-26 call, p.7). CARE calls sustained profitability "contingent on further moderation in credit costs" (CARE, p.1).

**What the next filing must explain.** The Q2 FY27 update shows a Rs 625 crore technical write-off, half the Rs 1,253 crore GNPA stock at June (Q2 KBU, p.2; Q1 deck, p.18). FY26 and Q1 FY27 had none. Micro loans fell 4.6% in the quarter (Q2 KBU, p.1). Two readings: a clean-up of fully provided legacy accounts at no P&L cost, or new stress needing fresh provisions. The observation that separates them is the Q2 FY27 provision line beside the GNPA movement table.

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

**CARE A-/Stable on Rs 800 crore of Tier II and A1 on Rs 500 crore of CDs, 01-Sep-2026**, outlook revised from Negative (CARE, p.1). A year earlier CARE cut the rating from A to A- after four quarterly losses and FY25 slippage of 10.29% (CARE-25, p.1). CARE now credits NNPA of 0.83% and net stressed assets at 11.9% of net worth from 45.9% (CARE, p.1). Its downgrade triggers: CET1 below 15% (now 15.48%), RoTA below 1% (now 1.04%), net stressed assets above 12.5% of net worth (now 11.94%) (CARE, pp.2-3). All three sit within one point of the trigger.

**Brickwork A-/Stable on Rs 20 crore, 17-Aug-2026**, upgraded from BBB+ (BWR, p.2). It would cut on GNPA above 7% or CRAR below 17% (BWR, p.5).

Both agencies rate the recovery as real but conditional. Neither rates the 2% ROA destination; CARE says lower secured yields "could keep margins lower than the historical highs" (CARE, p.1).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Profit is back, but the gate is credit cost near 2%, and it printed 4.4% (step 8). The Q2 write-off reopens the organic-decline question.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB.** The losses, the 34% GNPA ratio on legacy group loans and the ARC sales belong to the 2022 and 2023 vintages (Nov-25 call, p.14), now 10% of the book. The structural items, Kerala, CASA, gold, gearing, do not explain the loss; they cap the destination rung.
- **Recognition gap: OPEN.** Price Rs 40.9 against book Rs 34.6 (MD), about 1.2 times; step 13 run-rate PE about 6.6 times. An R3 lender trades well above book. The stock has already run from Rs 19.7 to Rs 46.0 in a year (MD).

**Posture: RESEARCH / WATCH.** Narrative until the credit-cost gate fires. Not a trade; watch the gate.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

The recovery is filed and consistent: slippages down 84%, NNPA 0.8%, PPOP up 179%, ROA 1% (Q1 deck, pp.4, 18, 23). The destination is a guide, not a print. The quarter that tests it is filed after this screen.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. The Q2 FY27 provision line and the P&L effect of the Rs 625 crore write-off; whether GNPA fell without it (Q2 KBU, p.2; Q1 deck, p.18).
2. Credit cost on the glide to about 2% by Q4 FY27, and the Emerging Household GNPA trend (Aug-26 call, p.7; Q1 deck, p.14).
3. The Tier 1 raise: size, price and dilution of the 63.25% promoter block (Aug-26 call, p.9; Q1 deck, p.27).
4. Gold book LTV of 72% and its behaviour if gold falls (Aug-26 call, p.11).

**What would change the view**
- Up to PROCEED: the Q2 FY27 filing shows credit cost under 3% with the write-off at no P&L cost, PAT above Rs 90 crore, Emerging Household GNPA flat or lower. Re-screen on that filing.
- Down to PASS: the write-off carried fresh provisions, or credit cost stays above 4% for a second quarter, or the equity raise prices below book.

## 13. Forward view

- **Growth: NOT MEANINGFUL.** The FY26 base is a loss of Rs 166.40 crore.
- **Forward PE: about 6.6 times.** Market cap Rs 2,111 crore (MD, 28-Oct-2026 close) divided by Rs 320 crore.
- **The two readings.** Credit cost near 4% keeps FY27 near Rs 320 crore. A glide to 2% by Q4, with ROA 2% on about Rs 33,000 crore of assets giving a Q4 quarter near Rs 165 crore, sums on a straight line to about Rs 490 crore and a forward PE of about 4.3 times (derived). The observation that separates them is the Q2 FY27 provision line (step 8).
- **Read with steps 5, 8 and 9.** The planned Tier 1 raise adds shares the market cap above does not carry (Aug-26 call, p.9).
