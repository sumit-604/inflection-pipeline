**2B. Utilisation trajectory (capital adequacy, the lender analogue of asset utilisation).**
CRAR (Tier 1+2) rose from 25.3% (Mar-26) to 40.2% (Jun-26) post-IPO (IP1 p.36),
i.e. the freshly raised capital sits largely UNutilised for growth as of Jun-26 —
a large embedded-growth runway, not yet drawn down. A second capital infusion
(preferential) arriving on top of an already-underutilised base is the specific
fact the Rs 832 Cr rationale gap makes hard to interpret (📄 CRAR fact; 🔍 the "why
raise more now" inference is unresolved).

**2C. Growth embedded in the capital raised — arithmetic.**
Total growth capital raised or approved: ₹850 Cr (IPO) + ₹832.2 Cr (preferential,
pending) = ₹1,682.2 Cr.
Management's stated steady-state on-book leverage target: 2.5x-3.0x debt-to-equity
(Krishnan Vishwanathan, Concall_Jun_2026 p.22).
Implied incremental on-book lending capacity at the leverage band's midpoint (2.75x):
₹1,682.2 Cr x 2.75 = ₹4,626 Cr.
As % of current total AUM (₹8,001 Cr, Jun-26, IP1 p.7): 4,626 / 8,001 = **57.8%**.
Caveat (🔍, stated plainly): this is capacity, not committed disbursement; ~75% of
the IPO tranche is already spoken for at Si Creva, and the preferential tranche is
not yet shareholder-approved. The number describes headroom, not a locked pipeline.

**2D. New geography or market entries.** None. LAP branch additions (98 -> 101,
Q4 FY26 -> Q1 FY27) deepen existing states (Karnataka, AP, Telangana, TN, UP,
Maharashtra, Gujarat — IP1 p.11); no new state or international entry is claimed
anywhere in the corpus.

## SECTION 3: THE 22-CATEGORY SCAN

*A1 — rare manufacturing capability.* NO EVIDENCE FOUND (archetype mismatch; lender, not a manufacturer).

*A2 — patent and IP pipeline.* NO EVIDENCE FOUND. No patent filing, application, or

*A3 — process innovation.* Evidence: underwriting model AUC (area-under-curve)
performance disclosed with a full version history across three independent filed
documents — AUC 66% (V21, 2023) -> 68% (V22, 2024) -> 70% (V34, 2025) -> 74% (V40,

model version is not yet dated. Countervailing note: this improvement already sits
inside current financials (credit cost fell 8.85% -> 6.80% over the same window),
so the genuinely "emerging" slice is the FORWARD trajectory of further AUC gains,
not the stack's existence.

*A4 — product platform / modular architecture.* The "unified cloud-native platform"
diagrams in both investor decks (IP1 p.15) show LAP, insurance and (nascent) mutual
fund products sitting on one origination/LMS backbone. But the specific test —
SKU growth without proportional R&D spend, accelerating launch frequency — cannot
be checked: no R&D-spend line is disclosed anywhere in the corpus (B04 gap), and
only two product launches (LAP 2024, MF/insurance 2025-26) have occurred in ten
years. NO EVIDENCE FOUND against the strict test; the architecture diagram alone is
a marketing asset, not proof of a platform economics moat.
