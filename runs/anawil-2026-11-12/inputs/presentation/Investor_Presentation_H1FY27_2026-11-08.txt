Management Commentary 
5
H1FY27 marks a significant step forward in our growth journey, supported by higher tower production, stronger 
execution and an increasing contribution from integrated supply contracts. The evolving business mix has 
enabled us to undertake a larger scope of work, driving higher revenue realisations and absolute EBITDA per tower.
Given the different material requirements across our business models, we believe absolute EBITDA per tower is a 
more meaningful measure of operating performance than percentage margins alone. While integrated supply 
contracts carry relatively lower percentage margins, they generate higher absolute EBITDA per tower. Initial 
operating expenses at our Kutch, Gujarat plant, ahead of its full ramp-up, have limited further improvement in 
profitability. We expect better cost absorption as utilisation increases.
With H1 typically being seasonally softer than H2, we anticipate a significant pick-up in execution during H2FY27, 
backed by our strong order book. Our capacity expansion at Kutch is nearing completion, while the addition of               
in-house kits manufacturing at Koppal, Karnataka, strengthens backward integration. These initiatives position us 
to scale production, undertake larger orders and work towards achieving our FY27 financial guidance.
Mr. Nimish Kumar Rameshchandra Vashi 
Chairman & Managing Director
