**This is CFO/PAT-ratio-meaningless territory, and the framework's generic "<0.7 flag" threshold does not apply mechanically here.** For a growing NBFC lender under Ind AS 7, loan disbursements net of collections are classified within operating cash flow (not investing), so a lending book growing AUM at 30%+ per year will structurally show large negative CFO even when core lending economics are healthy — the negative CFO here is a *funding-model artifact of growth*, not evidence of earnings manipulation on its own. The Company's own Risk Factor 7 explicitly discloses this: "We have incurred negative cash flows in the past and may continue to have negative cash flows in the future" ([PROSPECTUS] p.37-ish, cross-referenced in the KPI/statistical section), financed by financing activities (new borrowings). The correct cash-quality lens for this business is: (i) collection efficiency (98.92%/96.69%/96.76% per [PROSPECTUS] MD&A, not independently re-verified at page level this pass, cited per instruction as MD&A content), (ii) whether the loan book funding itself is matched by tenor (it is — see the positive ALM gap at every bucket, Phase 2F above), and (iii) whether reported PAT itself is inflated by non-cash items — which is exactly where the DA/ARC gain-on-sale finding (Phase 2, Rank 1) becomes the *real* cash-quality question for this Company, not the headline CFO/PAT ratio. **This reframing does not soften B02's FLAG-CASH; it relocates the correct locus of the cash-conversion concern from "CFO negative" (structural, expected, disclosed) to "how much of PAT is a non-cash gain-on-sale, and is the >36x FY24→FY25 stressed-asset-to-ARC transfer volume itself a form of accelerating recognition of gains that would otherwise show up as cash collections over time" (judgment-dependent, undisclosed in aggregate, and the actual substance of B02's INDETERMINATE cash-conversion flag).**

### 6E. Quiet Abandonment Check (mandatory)

Reading the "Our Strengths" / Basis-for-Offer-Price qualitative narrative (the opening-equivalent section) against the operational sections (restated notes, risk factors, CARO history):

- **Claim:** "Comprehensive credit assessment, underwriting and risk management framework" is asserted as a qualitative pricing factor and strength.

- **Classification:** **hedged retreat** — the strength is asserted at face value in the qualitative-factors section; the operational sections do not directly contradict it but describe a risk-management outcome (rising stress, unusual pre-NPA ARC disposals) that sits uneasily beside the claim, without the qualitative section acknowledging the tension or scaling back the claim's framing.
- **Materiality:** moderate.
