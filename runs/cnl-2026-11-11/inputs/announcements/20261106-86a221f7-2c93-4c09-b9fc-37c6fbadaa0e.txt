Overview and Strategic Rationale for the Acquisition of  
Zicom SaaS Private Limited 
Zicom SaaS Private Limited (“Zicom SaaS”) is an established company operating in India's 
electronic security, surveillance and technology -based security services industry. 
Incorporated in 2011 as part of the Zicom Group, which has been associated with the security 
industry since the 1990s, Zicom SaaS provides security monitoring and management 
solutions to banks, financial institutions, corporate offices, retail outlets, factories, 
warehouses, commercial establishments and residential societies. 
Unlike t raditional security companies that primarily focus on selling CCTV cameras and 
security equipment, Zicom SaaS operates on a Security as a Service (SaaS)  model. Under 
this model, customers can obtain surveillance equipment, remote monitoring, maintenance 
and security management services against regular monthly or annual subscription or service 
charges. This enables customers to access professional security services without 
independently establishing and managing complex security operations. 
One of Zicom SaaS's key strengths is its 24×7 centralised Command and Control Centre , 
through which it can remotely monitor security systems installed at multiple locations, 
identify suspicious activities, generate alerts and coordinate necessary action. For instance, 
banks operating hundreds of branches and ATMs across different cities can monitor their 
security systems through a centralised facility. Its services also include AI -based 
surveillance, virtual security guards, remote intervention, monitoring of CCTV equipment  
performance and maintenance services. In addition, Zicom SaaS provides technology -based 
solutions for attendance monitoring, customer footfall analysis, energy management and 
other operational requirements, allowing customers to utilise their surveillance  
infrastructure for multiple purposes. 
Creative Newtech Limited (“the Company” or “CNL”) has an established presence in India's 
technology distribution industry, supported by its extensive network of channel partners, 
brand relationships and experience in developing technology businesses. As part of its long-
term growth strategy, the Company is exploring opportunities to expand its presence in 
surveillance cameras, recording equipment, networking products and related security 
technologies. The proposed acqu isition of Zicom SaaS is intended to strengthen this 
business by combining CNL's distribution capabilities with Zicom SaaS's monitoring 
infrastructure, technical expertise, existing customer relationships and managed 
security services.

The primary objecti ve of the acquisition is to expand the Company's business beyond the 
supply of surveillance equipment into providing complete security solutions covering 
cameras, installation, monitoring, analytics, maintenance and ongoing support. While 
equipment sales g enerally generate revenue at the time of sale, customers continue to 
require monitoring, maintenance, technical support and equipment upgrades throughout 
the life of the equipment. By offering these services, the Company could develop recurring 
monthly or annual revenue streams and maintain longer-term relationships with customers 
rather than limiting its involvement to the initial sale of products. 
The acquisition is also expected to create opportunities for business expansion through the 
combined strength s of both companies. Zicom SaaS's existing relationships with banks, 
financial institutions, enterprises, retail customers and residential societies may generate 
additional demand for surveillance cameras, equipment replacements, upgrades and related 
technology products. Similarly, Creative Newtech's established distribution network, 
dealers, system integrators and channel partners could help expand Zicom SaaS's 
monitoring and managed security services across India, creating opportunities for cross -
selling and reaching new customers. 
Further, the same surveillance infrastructure can support additional applications beyond 
security. For example, cameras installed at retail outlets or commercial establishments may 
also be used for customer footfall analysis, attendance monitoring, operational management 
and energy monitoring. This creates opportunities to offer additional technology -based 
services to existing customers and generate additional business without necessarily 
requiring separate infrastructure. 
The Bo ard of Directors believes that the proposed acquisition represents an 
important step towards diversifying the Company's business from primarily 
technology distribution into integrated surveillance and managed security services. It 
is expected to strengthen  the Company's surveillance business, expand its technical and 
service capabilities, provide access to new customer segments, create opportunities for 
recurring revenue and improve its ability to participate in larger institutional projects. 
Subject to successful completion and integration of the acquisition, customer requirements 
and commercial viability, the Company intends to develop surveillance and managed 
security services as an important long-term business vertical. The Board believes that the 
proposed acquisition is aligned with the Company's overall growth strategy and has 
the potential to support sustainable business growth, diversify revenue sources, 
strengthen customer relationships and create long -term value for the Company and 
its shareholders.
