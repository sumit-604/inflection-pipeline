## What the numbers say

- Revenue is extremely lumpy. Consolidated Q4 FY26 was 6,482.56, Q1 FY27 was
  18,306.99, a 2.8x swing between adjacent quarters. This is a film release
  calendar, not a run rate.
- Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue grew 34.3% over Q1 FY26. Consolidated PAT grew
  263%, from 350.31 to 1,271.47.
- Operational expenses ran 86.1% of standalone revenue in Q1 FY27 and 82.7%
  in FY26.
- Non-controlling interests lost 568.52 across FY26. Minority-held subsidiaries
  are loss-making while the parent's share is profitable.
- EQUITY EXPANSION. Paid-up capital went from 1,418.78 at 30 June 2025 to
  5,210.71 at 31 March 2026, a 3.67x increase. Note 5 discloses only a bonus
  issue in the ratio 2:5 on 2 December 2025, which alone would take 1,418.78
  to about 1,986. The remaining increase is not explained in this filing.
  Load-bearing and unresolved.
- No dividend in any period shown.
