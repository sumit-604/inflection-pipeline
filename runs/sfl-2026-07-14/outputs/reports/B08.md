**3B. Capital allocation behaviour**:
- Dividend: AR FY22's five-year proposed-dividend table shows **NIL**
  dividend in every year shown (✅, line 4919). Web search confirms SFL
  paid **no dividend from its 2016 listing through FY25** and declared its
  **first-ever dividend (₹1/share, record date 9-Jul-2026, for FY2025-26)**

  (📰 multiple corporate-announcement sources, 2026). A decade of zero
  payout despite sustained profitability, alongside family remuneration
  that scales with profit (below), is the textbook "cash retained,
  family paid via profit-linked incentive" pattern the framework flags —
  though the more charitable reading is that retained cash funded organic
  capacity expansion and, more recently, ~₹2,150 crore (Kurlon) and
  ~₹960 crore (Furlenco, cumulative) of M&A. Both readings are defensible;
  flagged ⚠️, not 🔴, given the M&A context.
- Promoter/WTD remuneration structure (✅ AR FY22 AGM notice, Items 5-8):
  Rahul Gautam — basic salary up to ₹6 lakh/month + up to 1.5% of PBT

  Rakesh Chahar — up to ₹4 lakh/month + up to 0.75% of PBT; Tushaar
  Gautam — up to ₹2 lakh/month + up to 0.75% of PBT. Combined family
  incentive cap (excluding Chahar) is up to 3.0% of PBT on top of base
  salaries, with a further 0.75% for Chahar (non-family) — i.e., up to
  3.75% of company PBT can flow to the four WTDs in incentive alone. This
  is disclosed and shareholder-approved (not concealed), but it is a
  meaningful profit claim concentrated in one family for a company that
  paid no dividend for a decade; flagged ⚠️.
- No preferential-allotment-to-promoters or promoter-warrant-lapse
  pattern was found. The 2023 dilutive event (below) was a QIP to
