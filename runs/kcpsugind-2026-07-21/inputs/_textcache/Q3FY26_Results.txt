5. It has been explained to us by the Company's Management that , the Sugar Industry and the incidental co­
generation activity being seasonal in nature and since the sugar season does not match with the 
Company's financial year, recognition of expenses strictly in the period in which they have been incurred

therefore, a consistent practice followed by the Company, to identify such expenses incurred during the 
off-season that are relatable to the coming season, and to defer them and recognize them only in the 
season period.

incidental co-generation activity being seasonal in nature and since the sugar season does not match
