   "our visionary promoter, Mr. Ameen ji, had to travel suddenly... I am hoping
   that in the next earnings call, he is there to lay out the vision" (p15). MD

   26.63%, PAT +103.53%, net margin 10.19% (p3). No mechanical driver for a
   near-doubling of margin on 8% revenue growth, first post-IPO print;
   sustainability not addressed. MAJOR.

4. **Internally inconsistent guidance.** EBITDA margin "20% to 25%" (p8),
   restated "23%-24%" (p8), then "20% to 25%" again (p14); revenue "20% to 25%
   CAGR" (p8) far above the 8.47% actually delivered. MAJOR.

   will conclude that. So, we will be compliant on that front as well" (p5) — i.e.
   not yet compliant. Subsidiary otherwise dormant (p8). MAJOR (governance).

   later, "There are about 11 players in vestibules." (p11). Uncorrected; undercuts
   the "few players, better margins" niche-moat narrative. MAJOR.

7. **Railway-pad capacity contradiction.** "about INR1.35 crore pieces a year"
   then, same answer, "when we show a capacity of 1.35 lakhs" — 100x
   inconsistency, uncorrected (p7). MAJOR.
