## Active flags

### FLAG-PROMOTER (verdict CAUTION)

No deal-breaker triggered. A cluster of regulatory and legal friction sits alongside a real, rising, personally leveraged share pledge.

- Top finding 1: promoter-group pledge of 11,597,866 shares, 20.02% of promoter holding, to 360 One Prime as at 31-Mar-2026, securing a Rs 670 Cr KEMPINC LLP loan used to buy more CleanMax stock from Brookfield (AR Note 19(g), p.420; RHP Capital Structure pledge schedule). This is a leveraged own-stock purchase and a margin-call risk on the controlling shareholder, not a cash-extraction risk to the company. Pledge trend is rising from a near-zero FY25 base.
- Top finding 2: Green Earth criminal complaint naming Kuldeep Jain under IPC 406/420/467/468/471/120B/506, pending since Aug-2020, now in mediation (RHP p.871-872, p.879). Unresolved six years; the one item that does not decompose into a self-corrected or regulation-forced event.
- Also on record: SEBI AIF concentration-limit settlement, Rs 19.3 lakh paid, Oct-2024 (RHP p.878); RoC Section 454 personal penalty on Kuldeep Jain, Dec-2025 (RHP p.64-65); two pending GST summons, no allegation established (RHP p.877-878).
- Transition evidence: Temasek (via Jongsong) ~Rs 760 Cr and Bain ~Rs 350 Cr pre-IPO entry; Dinesh Khara, ex-SBI Chairman, on the board (AR p.331); CFO stable 11 years; Big-4 auditor retained and engaged to certify the RPT arm's-length framework (AR pp.14, 7327-7333).

### FLAG-CASH (determination: INDETERMINATE)

The cash-flow deficit itself is growth-induced: operating cash flow scaled 20x from Rs 86 Cr to Rs 1,731 Cr while capex scaled 3.1x from Rs 1,866 Cr to Rs 5,754 Cr, so capex on new contracted capacity outran a rising operating cash base, and revenue never declined (B01 block_b_trend). But the QUALITY of the operating-profit crossover cannot be confirmed from the filed record, which makes the overall cash read INDETERMINATE, not clean growth-induced.

The indeterminacy sits in a cluster that all lands in the IPO / crossover year:
- CFO/PAT 20.2x FY26 (72.3x FY25), distorted by a near-breakeven PAT base and Rs 862.92 Cr trade-payables growth (B03).
- Consolidated current liabilities exceeded current assets by Rs 1,724.10 Cr, 2.8x the standalone figure; going-concern language present, Board sees no material uncertainty (Note 58, AR p.688). Consolidated verbatim: "the Group's current liabilities exceeded its current assets by Rs 17,240.95 Million...the Board...does not anticipate any material uncertainty related to going concern."
- True all-in group interest ~Rs 1,098-1,115 Cr FY26 vs Rs 785.92 Cr in the P&L; ~Rs 312-329 Cr capitalised, 28-30% of total interest vs 7-10% in FY25 (Notes 3, 36, AR p.401, 431).
- Useful life of solar/wind assets extended 25 to 30 years mid-year, coinciding with a favourable impairment discount-rate change, both cutting the depreciation and impairment charge in the crossover year (Note 2, AR p.400-401).
- Free cash flow minus Rs 4,023 Cr FY26, widening from minus Rs 1,780 Cr FY24; borrowings 5,570 to 12,684 Cr (B01).

Missing evidence that would resolve the determination:
1. FY26 PBT rebuilt with capitalised interest reverted to the FY25 ratio and depreciation at the pre-change 25-year life. Where: FY27 Notes 3/36 equivalents, or the Stage 11 rebuild.
2. The EBITDA definition reconciled to CARE PBILDT (operating-only), since no call states whether reported EBITDA includes other income, and the FY28 Rs 3,000 Cr guidance rides on the undefined basis (B05).
3. Q2 FY27 operating PBT before other income at or above zero (due late Oct-2026). This is the single next-quarter print that decides whether the crossover is structural.

## Publish check

PUBLISH CANDIDATE.

Observation: GNA versus TGNA connectivity decides whether grid curtailment costs a renewable IPP real money. On GNA (permanent) connectivity, peers like ACME and JSW are paid on schedule even when curtailed; on TGNA (temporary) connectivity, curtailed power is unpaid. CleanMax's Bikaner 525 MW sits on TGNA, so its 70% curtailment is a real ~Rs 170 Cr full-year hit, not a protected one. Worth publishing because the distinction is invisible in headline curtailment numbers and useful to any RE investor.
