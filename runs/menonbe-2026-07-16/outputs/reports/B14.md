**3B. Growth Triggers (ranked by conviction, from B05).**

## NARRATIVE

Menon Bearings is a genuinely good manufacturer priced for its own good returns. It earns 29.8% ROCE and the fattest margin in its peer group, and the export programs that carry the growth case (Allison, Federal-Mogul DRiV) are named and HIGH conviction. The problem is not the business, it is the price and the plumbing. At Rs 190 the destination multiple of 25x already equals the trailing 24.3x, so there is no re-rating cushion and the entire 25% case has to come from earnings that the base case grows at 19%, not 25%. Probability-weighted the return is 13.4%, well short of the Tier A hurdle.

The one thing that would break the thesis is the same thing that could make it: exports. The receivables that are building are building because of the export ramp, so if exports fire, cash stays growth-induced and the multiplier holds at 0.85x; if exports stall, working capital releases but growth dies and the hurdle reverts to STOP. That single root, flagged SHARED CATALYST, is why the whole position is Small and why entry is disciplined to Rs 132 to 148 with a hard falsification metric on standalone DSO.

Around that sit the softer watch items: a mixed-credibility management that has slipped the dynamometer four times and given Alkop three different targets, a promoter picture held at CAUTION by an insider CFO hire and an unresolved pledge, and a Gate 0 AVOID that is mostly a filing-gap artifact rather than distress. None forces AVOID on quality, all argue for patience. Action today is simple: watch, do not buy, and let the price come to the zone while the receivables line tells you whether the cash is real.

**Publication candidate:** NO. Governance CAUTION, credibility C, and an unresolved pledge disclosure make this unsuitable for a public post at this time.
