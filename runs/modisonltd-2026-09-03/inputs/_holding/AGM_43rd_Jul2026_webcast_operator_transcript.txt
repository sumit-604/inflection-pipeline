===== CEO PRESENTATION (M.N. Nikam) =====
PRODUCT PORTFOLIO. Two divisions:
- Low Voltage (LV): silver and silver-based alloys, copper and copper-brass
  alloys, brazing assemblies, variety of products.
- High Voltage (HV): copper-tungsten products, and "crowns" for GE and other
  customers.
Also medium-voltage (MV) presence (see Q&A): brazing washers for vacuum
interrupters, finger contacts.

EHS / people: national safety day, world safety week, environmental day (150+
trees planted), employee health checks, cricket matches, women's day, continuous
training. Kaizen up ~50% this year, 150 kaizens on shop floor; office kaizen
planned next year.

ISRO development: Modison developed a new alloy for ISRO previously imported from
Russia. Team took ~8-10 months; ISRO had struggled ~5-6 years and tried 5-6
suppliers. Modison is successful vendor; technology-transfer document and
material-transfer document given to ISRO.

Recognition: General Electric (GE) "Best Supplier" award this year (received from
GE CEO). CEO also received highest-quality award. Close working with GE, Siemens,
ABB on joint improvement projects. Supplier partnerships emphasised.
CSR: Modison wing inaugurated at a Hindi vidyalaya (WPI) school in Gujarat.
