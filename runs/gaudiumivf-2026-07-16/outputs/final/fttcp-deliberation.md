## 2. Operator overrides

### Override 1: ROCE calculated after removing surplus cash

- Draft first determination: backward ROCE TEMPORARILY DEPRESSED, reported about 20% in FY26 (down from about 54% in FY25), with a recovering forward path fed to Pillar 1 through the Amendment 4.5 normalized blend.
- Operator ruling: measure ROCE on operating capital with the spare cash taken out.
- Operator's words: "I have told multiple times ROCE should be calculated after taking out the spare cash."
- Applied: FY26 operating ROCE with the roughly Rs 81 Cr idle IPO deposits removed is about 37% (EBIT Rs 35.15 Cr over operating capital about Rs 95.7 Cr), in line with the standalone core near 39%. Backward verdict moves to SUSTAINED premium; backward composite from 1 positive to 2 positive. Pillar 1 now uses the current 37% operating ROCE, which lands the ROCE base multiple at the 24x cap, with the surplus cash valued separately in the SOTP. The FTTCP composite stays +2.

### Override 2: growth and strategic premiums must be scored, not zeroed

- Draft first determination: Pillar 3 growth premium +0x (on the emerging-moat-gated table, EM 13); Strategic premium +0x. Raw destination PE 19.2x.
- Operator ruling: a high-growth business in a large, high-visibility market must earn a growth score; the framework has to value high growth correctly.
- Operator's words: "You are giving zero marks for growth premium. You are giving zero marks for strategic premium. There is a high growth market, there is a lot of visibility, and there is a large population. You need to give a score to it. It is a high growth business. We need to get a framework correct for valuing a high growth business."
- Applied: the v3.4 decoupled Pillar 3a pays documented growth machinery independent of the moat score. Gaudium qualifies on two tests, the documented IPO earmark to take hubs from 7 to 26 and the SOM-implied revenue CAGR above 20% with the 3-year capacity check passing, which is +2x, held at +2x by the C credibility grade cap. Strategic premium set at +1x for the founder-clinician brand, the ART-Act licensing barrier and premium clinic pricing, below the +2 to +4x franchise band because competition is not limited and the company is a small player. Raw destination PE moves to 22.2x, round 22x, range 20.5 to 24x. The moat-formation sub-premium stays +0x on EM 13, and the growth premium is expected to rise as the credibility grade reaches B and the cash multiplier turns.

### Override 3: value on one-year-forward earnings multiples

- Draft first determination: valuation shown on trailing FY26 earnings, entry about Rs 57.
- Operator ruling: carry out the valuation on one-year-forward earnings multiples.
- Operator's words: "if you are using this valuation that you have given, then carry out the valuation exercise using the one-year forward earnings multiples."
- Applied: current multiple on FY27 earnings, exit priced on the year-ahead EPS at the FY29 exit. Forward PE at CMP is about 32x rather than 41x trailing. On the 22x destination and a 25% base EPS CAGR, exit value is about Rs 181 base, entry for a 25% return is about Rs 93, MoS about Rs 74, and the three-year return from CMP is about 10% base. The operator did not raise the base EPS CAGR when offered, so it stands at 25%, with bull 30% held to the C-grade cap and bear 15%.

Verdict as it stands:

- Valuation on one-year-forward multiples: WATCHLIST at CMP Rs 137, not a buy. The Hurdle is STOP at CMP on both base and bull, since a 25% return is not available at about 32x forward against a 35x sector ceiling. The three-year base return from CMP is about 10%.
