GIGAWATTS: "4.5 gigawatt of aluminum frame manufacturing expansion". Aluminium frame
capacity is stated in mass units in every other disclosure in the corpus. The figure
4.5 GW is identical to the cell capacity named in the same sentence. The corpus contains
no correction. Record as stated; the unit is inconsistent with all other disclosures.

B-N3. Frame commissioning dates in the corpus, oldest to newest: Q1 FY27 (call
27-May-2026, PAGE 4), Sep 26 (presentation PAGE 13), Q2 FY27 (presentation PAGE 25),
Q3 FY-27 (AR PAGE 21, and presentation PAGE 26). Sep-2026 sits in Q2 FY27, so PAGES 13
