### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Paramount makes power, telecom and railway cables from copper and aluminium. It earns a conversion margin on the metal. About 28% of sales go abroad, mostly to the US.
2. Sales grew 31% on a year ago, to Rs 560.40 Cr (R001, R003). Profit after tax rose to Rs 24.24 Cr from Rs 13.26 Cr (R115, R117).
3. This time the profit came from the factories. Last year, interest and other income carried it. Other income fell to Rs 3.80 Cr from Rs 19.95 Cr (R007, R009).
4. The operating margin rose to 7.30% from 1.34% (REV 1.3). One line did all the work. Other expenses fell to 15.9% of sales from 23.2%. The company gave no reason.
5. Gross margin fell 1.5 points (REV 1.3). High metal prices raise sales but thin the margin percentage. So the recovery rests on cost control, not on better prices.
6. Cash did not follow profit. Operating cash flow was minus Rs 31.89 Cr for the half year (R317). That is the second negative half year in a row.
7. Customers pay faster now, in about 66 days against 79 at March (REV Step 5). But the company paid suppliers faster and built Rs 42.76 Cr of finished stock (R028).
8. The share issue raised about Rs 100 Cr. The filing says Rs 73.39 Cr is used "with no deviation" but names no purpose (REV 0D). On the call, management said idle issue money paid suppliers [ASR].
9. The new Narmadapuram plant needs Rs 300 Cr. Only about Rs 33 Cr shows on the balance sheet (REV 6A). Long-term loans are not yet arranged [ASR]. Management still claims a part start in Q1 FY28.
10. Management keeps FY27 sales growth at 15 to 18%, later 15 to 20% [ASR, call l.195, l.425]. The first half grew 24%. So the guidance implies a slower second half. The plants run near full.
11. A one-off US tariff refund will lift Q3 profit [ASR]. Strip it out before you judge Q3.
12. Watch the Q3 operating margin without the refund, due about February 2027. At 7.5% or more, the recovery holds. Below 6.9%, the Q2 margin was a peak.
