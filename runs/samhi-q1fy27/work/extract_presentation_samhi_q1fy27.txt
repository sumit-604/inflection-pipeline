Highlights for the quarter
▪ Strong revenue growth despite geo-political headwinds: Comparable1 Revenue growth of +10.8% YoY
▪ Occupancy level ~79.3%2 (up from ~74.2% Q1-FY26) ; RevPAR growth of +9.6% YoY
      • Domestic travelers now constitute 81% of total room nights we sold in first quarter up from 78% same period last year. More resilient
           cohort given geo-political instability

▪ Operating margins of ~36% (ex GST impact) – scope for improvement in subsequent periods; growth in upscale inventory (not
  impacted by GST changes) to improve operating margins to ~40%

▪ The on-going growth pipeline within our core portfolio and the progress in RARE India gives us a formidable path to growth
