**Exchange 1 — Slippage deferral (Sonal Minhas / Sovan; also Sanjay on NIM).**
- Q: "the credit cost is reported but slippage is not… do you report slippage numbers?"
- A: "we haven't reported slippage number. I do not have that number readily available and we can share that eventually." Separately, Sanjay cites *"the reducing slippages causing an upside on the NIM."*
- **Said:** product-level PAR granularity; **Did NOT say:** the slippage number itself.
- **Thesis implication:** the credit-cost/NIM improvement narrative leans partly on an **undisclosed** metric. This is the cleanest Q2 resolver — does the improvement come from lower *incurred* slippage (bull) or from overlay/denominator management (bear)?
- **Follow-up we'd ask:** "Will you disclose gross quarterly slippage (₹ Cr and % of AUM) as a standing metric from Q2, and is it consistent with the ~3.68% ex-overlay credit cost?"

**Exchange 2 — Overlay mechanics (Shalin Kapadia / Gaurav-Sovan).**
- A: reported credit cost 4.01% **includes a ~₹6 Cr management-overlay build** (~33 bps → ex-overlay ~3.68%); total overlay on BS ₹11 Cr (stage 2 & 3); target **0.5% of book**; *"in good years we should create that overlay… absorb some of the profits into these overlays."*
- **Thesis implication:** the headline credit-cost print is **management-smoothed**. Bull: prudent cross-cycle buffering. Bear: the true incurred number is being obscured, and in a bad year the buffer is released to defend the 3.5-4% band. Directly informs whether tripwire-5 is a clean read.
- **Follow-up:** "Ex-overlay incurred credit cost for each of the last 4 quarters, and the overlay build-vs-release rule in a normal year."

**Exchange 3 — Credit-cost floor & the mortgage lever (Ananga Rana / Sanjay + Sovan).**
- A: at 7% PAR X → 3.5-4% credit cost and **4.5-5% post-tax RoA**; and *"not in this financial year but eventually"* mortgage to 30% → **~50 bps lower** credit cost (3-3.25/3.5%).

- **Thesis implication:** the structural improvement is a **medium-term (mortgage-mix) story, not FY27** — respects the single-credit discipline; do not price the mortgage lever into FY27.
