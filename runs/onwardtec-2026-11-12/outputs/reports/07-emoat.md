### 2C. Growth embedded in capex

Arithmetic, as the prompt requires:
- Capex under execution: Rs 25 Cr (labs, Q4 FY26 call p.18). The Rs 3.31 Cr of open commitments (AR26 p.87) sits inside or beside it; the filings do not say which. I use the Rs 25 Cr only.
- Historical fixed asset turnover: FY26 revenue from operations 54,385.24 lakhs / net PPE 4,782.72 lakhs = 11.37x (AR26 p.46 and p.65, consolidated).
- Implied incremental revenue: Rs 25 Cr x 11.37 = Rs 284.3 Cr.
- Versus current revenue Rs 543.85 Cr: 284.3 / 543.85 = 52.3%.

Analyst reading (INF). The 52.3% is a mechanical output and I do not rely on it. Onward is asset light. Revenue comes from billed headcount, not from plant. Net PPE is 8.8% of revenue (4,782.72 / 54,385.24). Capex here buys seats and labs for hiring that is already under way: 432 net hires in H1 FY27 (Inv. Pres. p.7). The better ceiling on embedded growth is the company's own FY27 guide of 15% to 18% (Q2 FY27 call p.4), which implies Rs 625 to Rs 642 Cr on the FY26 base (INF arithmetic: 543.85 x 1.15 and x 1.18). On a PPE plus right-of-use base (8,515.25 lakhs, AR26 p.65) turnover is 6.39x and the same arithmetic gives 29.4%. The block carries 52.3 as the prompt-method output, and this paragraph flags it as non-informative.
