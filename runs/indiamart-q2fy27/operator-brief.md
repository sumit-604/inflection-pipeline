### 1. NARRATIVE

1. IndiaMART runs an online B2B listing site in India. Suppliers pay yearly subscriptions in advance to be found by business buyers. It also owns Busy, an accounting software unit.
2. Revenue grew 9.6% on a year ago, to Rs 428.6 Cr. Core profit before tax, without treasury income, grew 26.4%, to Rs 137.0 Cr.
3. The core profit gain is real, but costs drove it. Staff costs fell slightly while revenue grew. Management says the margin is high because it spends too little on sales. It guides the margin back to about 33%, with no date.
4. Treasury income turned negative, at minus Rs 7.6 Cr. Part of the Rs 3,234 Cr treasury now sits in equity factor funds. The company again gave no split between booked gains and paper marks.
5. Reported profit therefore grew only 9.8%, to Rs 90.8 Cr. Cash stays strong: over twelve months, operating cash is 1.43 times profit.
6. Demand fell again. Unique enquiries fell 14% on a year ago, to 27 million. Active buyers fell to 40 million. Paying suppliers fell for the fourth quarter in a row, to 217,000.
7. Your page holds an exit rule: enquiries down more than 5% for two quarters. Q1 was down 11% (or 16% or 7%, by base). Q2 is down 14%. The rule has fired as written on every reading.
8. Management says part of the fall is deliberate. It now puts some data behind a login to keep it from AI tools. Management did not quantify that part. The review rates it unproven.
9. Collections grew 9%, slower than revenue. The consolidated prepaid book grew only about Rs 15 Cr in the quarter. Management implies revenue growth of 8% to 10% from here.
10. Revenue per paying supplier rose 10% while the base shrank 2%. Part of that gain may be small suppliers leaving, not higher prices.
11. Busy grew billing 20%, but its margin fell to 12% from 17%. New licences fell to 11,000 from 12,000. The Q1 licence promise is missed.
12. The group put Rs 82.2 Cr into loss-making startups in Q2. The Fleetx follow-on implies about Rs 1,344 Cr of value for a firm losing about Rs 72 to 84 Cr a year. Associate losses took Rs 15.3 Cr from Q2 profit.
13. Your rulings are due on four items: whether the enquiry rule is live, add against exit precedence, the unexecuted trim to 1.5%, and the operating EPS base. Watch Q3 paying-supplier net additions; they are the cleanest single test.
