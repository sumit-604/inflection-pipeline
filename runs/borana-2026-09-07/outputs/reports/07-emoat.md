### 2C Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown

Capex under execution (closing CWIP, FY26): **Rs 80.94 Cr** (AR FY2026
Note 3, p.106, per B02/B03).
Historical fixed asset turnover: the only independently disclosed
full-year ratio in the corpus is **2.68x (FY24)**, RHP p.176 (auditor-
certified KPI table). FY25 and FY26 fixed asset turnover are NOT FOUND as
a named ratio in either annual report's extracted text (Note 40's ratio
disclosures cover inventory/receivables/payables/net-capital turnover, not
fixed-asset turnover specifically); this run could not independently
recompute a reliable FY26 figure because CWIP conversion was itself
near-zero in FY26 (only Rs 8.24 lakh of Rs 80.94 Cr capitalised, B03),
which would distort any FY26-based ratio.

Arithmetic: Rs 80.94 Cr × 2.68x = **Rs 216.9 Cr** implied incremental
revenue potential, once fully commissioned and ramped.
Against FY26 revenue from operations of Rs 388.59 Cr (AR FY2026 p.6):
216.9 / 388.59 = **~55.8%** above current revenue.

This is a rough proxy, not a forecast, for three stated reasons: (1) the
fixed-asset-turnover multiplier is two years stale and predates the
capital-intensity mix of the current CWIP; (2) the Rs 80.94 Cr CWIP figure
bundles the still-unreconciled Unit 4B/hybrid-power spend (B02 finding 10)
with any early Unit 4 Expansion spending, with no rupee split disclosed
anywhere in the corpus (B03 input gap); (3) FY26's near-zero
capitalisation rate means the timing of when (or whether) this embedded
growth shows up in reported revenue is itself unproven.

**capex_embedded_growth_pct carried to YAML: 56** (rounded; treat as an
order-of-magnitude proxy, not a point forecast).
