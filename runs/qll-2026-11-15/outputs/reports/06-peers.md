## PART 1: CLAIM-BY-CLAIM VERIFICATION

### Claim 1. Peer-consistent EBITDA margin at 20-30% growth, and does margin hold when growth is acquired or new-footprint
Claim (B05): "QLL guides 25-27% against 21.6% in H1 FY27; tests whether the guide is peer-consistent or stretched."
Verdict: PARTIALLY VERIFIED (level corroborated, path not).

Peer evidence:
- Level. Vimta EBITDA margin 36.4% Q1 FY27, 35.5% Q2 FY27, 36.0% H1 FY27, on income growth of 13.7%, 9.9% and 11.8% (VIMTALABS, Q1 FY27 call, Siva Rama Krishna, p.5; Q2 FY27 call, Siva Rama Krishna, p.5). Krsnaa EBITDA margin 25% Q1 FY27 and 27% Q2 FY27, 26% H1 FY27 against 28% a year earlier, on revenue growth of 22%, 32% and 27% (KRSNAA, Q1 FY27 call, Chandra Singh, p.8; Q2 FY27 call, Chandra Singh, pp.7-8). Lal EBITDA margin 31.0% Q1 FY27 and 32.1% Q2 FY27 on revenue growth of 19.1% and 19.6% (LALPATHLAB, Q1 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.5; Q2 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.6). QLL H1 FY27 consolidated 21.6% (B05) sits below all three peers. The 25-27% guide sits at Krsnaa's level and below Lal and Vimta, so the level is not an outlier.
- Path under fast growth. The only peer growing 22-32% from new footprint is Krsnaa. Its margin fell as growth rose, and its management called the drag timing: "I want to be very clear, this is a timing issue and not a structural one" (KRSNAA, Q3 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.4). It guided to hold the 28% FY26 level in FY27 (KRSNAA, Q4 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.8) and printed 25% and 27% in Q1 and Q2 FY27 (sources above). The next quarter did not restore the level; Q2 recovered 2 points.
- Fixed cost from day one. "[projects] carry their full fixed cost base from the very first day... while still operating below mature utilization levels, and therefore, you see compression in the margins" (KRSNAA, Q1 FY27 call, Yash Mutha, pp.4-5).
- Acquired growth. No peer has a large acquired share. Lal's acquisitions are small: Shahbazkers about Rs 6 Cr revenue (LALPATHLAB, Q4 FY26 call, Ved Goel, p.6); S N GeneLab FY26 revenue about Rs 60 Cr and EBITDA about Rs 15 Cr, a 25% margin (LALPATHLAB, Q2 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.7). No peer reports margin through a large acquisition.
- Margin expansion with growth. Lal is the only peer expanding margin with 19% growth, and it says the source is price and mix: CGHS/ECHS tariff gain of 2-3% on revenue, operating leverage on existing labs, and test mix (LALPATHLAB, Q1 FY27 call, Shankha Banerjee, pp.6-7; Q2 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.14). Its growth is organic on existing capacity, not new footprint.

Peers silent: none; all three spoke. Vimta and Lal are not acquisition-led.
Net read: a 25-27% margin is a plausible level for this peer set. No peer shows margin rising while new-footprint growth runs above 25% inside four quarters. The QLL guide needs H2 FY27 to add about 4 points to 21.6% (B05); the one fast-growth peer lost 2 points instead.

### Claim 2. Organic growth base rates; 35-40% FY27 growth after 47% standalone organic growth in FY26
Claim (B05): "QLL claims 35-40% FY27 growth; standalone organic was 47% in FY26; need a peer base rate."
Verdict: PARTIALLY VERIFIED (direction yes, magnitude no).

Peer evidence:
- Vimta total income growth: Q3 FY26 10.2% (Q3 FY26 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.3), FY26 19.5% (Q4 FY26 call, Siva Rama Krishna, p.5), Q1 FY27 13.7%, Q2 FY27 9.9% (sources in Claim 1). Management calls a 20-25% CAGR "a stretch number, but definitely doable" (VIMTALABS, Q4 FY26 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.8), and says its markets grow 8-11% and its units "almost double that rate" (VIMTALABS, Q1 FY27 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.14). Vimta missed its Rs 500 Cr FY26 revenue goal (Q4 FY26 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.13; reported FY26 Rs 416 Cr, p.5).
- Krsnaa like-to-like growth 13% FY26 (Q4 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.10) and 12% Q1 FY27 (Q1 FY27 call, Yash Mutha, p.3); headline growth of 22-27% in FY27 came from one large state PPP project, Rajasthan, and was 37% only "had some of these contracts continued" (Q2 FY27 call, Yash Mutha, p.3). A year earlier its guide of 15-16% FY26 growth printed 1% (Q4 FY26 call, Mayur, question, pp.12-13, management did not dispute the figures).
- Lal organic revenue growth 10.6% Q3 FY26 and 12.2% FY26 (Q3 FY26 call, Ved Goel, p.6; Q4 FY26 call, Shankha Banerjee, p.3), then 19.1% and 19.6% in FY27. Management splits the FY27 rate: patient volume 6-7% plus price and mix 6-7% plus a temporary 2-3% from CGHS, and calls "mid-teens organic" sustainable (LALPATHLAB, Q2 FY27 call, Shankha Banerjee, p.12). The Q3 FY26 call placed industry growth near 7-8% (Prakash Kapadia, question, p.7).
- No peer discloses an organic rate near 47%.

Peers silent: none.
Net read: peers show growth well above the industry's 7-8% is possible, and all three printed 19-27% in FY27. The top organic rate is Lal's 19-20% on a Rs 800+ Cr base, the top like-to-like rate is Krsnaa's 12-13%. A 35-40% FY27 rate needs acquired or newly built revenue, which is where Krsnaa's 22-32% came from. Peer guides slipped: Vimta missed Rs 500 Cr, Krsnaa missed 15-16% by 14 points. That is a base rate for guide slippage, not proof QLL will slip.

Peers silent: Vimta (all four calls).
Net read: the one government-linked peer shows 4-5 month DSO, rising receivables and repeated promise slippage on collection dates. That corroborates the direction of QLL's PPP receivable risk. It does not set a CFO/PBT norm. The contrast with Lal (17 days) shows the cause is the payer, not the lab model.

Net read: both lab-heavy peers show new capacity first dilutes margin and ROCE, then recovers over multiple years (Vimta says 2-3 years for biologics, three years for the building). QLL ties its margin recovery to H2 FY27. That timing is faster than any peer's stated ramp.

## PART 2: UNPROMPTED CROSS-READ

### 2A Demand environment
Split. Diagnostics demand is strong: Lal revenue +19.6% Q2 FY27 on patient volume +9% (LALPATHLAB, Q2 FY27 call, Shankha Banerjee, p.4) and "market buoyancy is definitely there somewhere" (p.8); Krsnaa +27% H1 FY27 (Q2 FY27 call, Yash Mutha, p.3). Contract research and testing is soft: Vimta income growth slowed to 9.9% in Q2 FY27 because of "a certain lull in the R&D pipeline on the pharma side" and longer customer decision cycles (VIMTALABS, Q2 FY27 call, Harita Vasireddi, pp.3 and 6). Food testing is hit by export and import disruption; electronics testing is "stable" and the industry is "the lowest growth rate in India... about 4.5%" in electronics manufacturing (Q1 FY27 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.14). Vimta's defence-OEM share of E&E capacity is 70-80% (Q2 FY27 call, p.10). QLL's 33% H1 FY27 revenue growth (B05) is not matched by the one peer that tests industrial, pharma and E&E samples; it reads as capacity and acquisition led, not a sector wave.

### 2B Pricing and input costs
- Vimta margins 35-36%; war raised consumable cost and lead times (Q4 FY26 call, p.7); rupee appreciation, facility cost and the new labour code lowered Q1 FY27 margin (Q1 FY27 call, p.4); management expects 100-200 bps medium-term correction from mix (Q2 FY27 call, Harita Vasireddi, p.8). Attrition "quite high across the industry" (Q3 FY26 call, p.11).
- Lal has not raised price for 4 years, passed through a GST cut, and gained 2-3% from CGHS/ECHS rates (Q2 FY27 call, Shankha Banerjee, pp.10 and 15). Gross margin about 81%; management expects the improving gross-margin trend not to continue given dollar and geopolitical inflation (Q2 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.14). Most reagents are imported (Q3 FY26 call, Ved Goel, p.13).
- Krsnaa PPP prices are 50-70% below market, with 2-5% escalators written into contracts (Q3 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.3; Q4 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.10). Pathology carries lower margin than radiology, so a pathology-heavy mix (65-70%) pressures margin (Q3 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.17).
QLL's margin narrative needs a rise in margin on mix and utilisation. Peers report input and labour inflation pressing on margin, with mix as the offset only at Lal.

### 2C Capex cycle
Broad expansion, not a lone expander. Vimta capex plan Rs 80 Cr FY27 after about Rs 100 Cr of building and Rs 20-25 Cr of biologics (Q1 FY27 call, Siva Rama Krishna, p.5; Q2 FY27 call, p.9). Lal plans Rs 100-150 Cr, most recently Rs 140-150 Cr (Q1 FY27 call, Ved Goel, p.21), with 12-15 new labs. Krsnaa capitalises about Rs 500 Cr in FY27 (Q4 FY26 call, Yash Mutha, p.13). This is a capacity race. The funding differs: Lal has net cash Rs 1,746 Cr and Vimta is net debt-free; Krsnaa funds growth with ADB NCDs of Rs 430 Cr, vendor finance, and promoter warrants (Q4 FY26 call, Chandra Singh, p.7; Q2 FY27 call, p.5). QLL's borrowing-led build (B05) resembles Krsnaa, not Lal or Vimta.

### 2D Competitive mentions of QLL
None in 12 calls. Peers name other competitors only generically (unorganised labs, Metropolis, Eurofins as a global model; LALPATHLAB Q2 FY27 p.13; VIMTALABS Q1 FY27 p.12).

### 2E Risks peers discuss that QLL does not (feeds missing-risks)
1. Government payer delay and project suspension to force payment (Krsnaa, Q3 FY26 p.15; Q4 FY26 pp.16-17; Q1 FY27 p.17; Q2 FY27 p.5).

5. Rupee and dollar effect on imported consumables (Lal Q3 FY26 p.13; Vimta Q1 FY27 p.4).
6. New labour code charges: Vimta Rs 1.6 Cr (Q3 FY26 call, p.5), Lal Rs 30 Cr one-off (Q3 FY26 call, Ved Goel, p.6). Whether QLL booked one is not in B05.
7. Talent attrition (Vimta, Q3 FY26 p.11).
8. Management turnover at Krsnaa: CFO left, interim CFO (Q3 FY26 p.7; Q4 FY26 p.17). QLL had company secretary and independent director exits in the corpus (B00 corpus_manifest announcements dated 9 Feb 2026, 30 Jul 2026, 29 Oct 2026); a pattern worth testing, not a peer finding.
9. Cost reclassification between lines (Lal moved courier cost from employee to other expense, Q4 FY26 p.17; Krsnaa explained a "fees to hospital" line, Q1 FY27 pp.8-9). Read QLL line items for similar regrouping.
10. Pharma R&D pipeline lull and regulator wait (Vimta, Q2 FY27 p.6 and p.12).

## PART 4: TRIANGULATION SUMMARY

- Claims verified: 1 of 6 (Claim 4, mechanism).
- Claims partially verified: 5 of 6 (Claims 1, 2, 3, 5, 6). Claims unverifiable: 0.
- Claims contradicted: 0. No peer states the opposite of a claim. Two items run close to it and go to synthesis as priority items: the margin path under fast growth (Claim 1) and the 47% organic base (Claim 2).
- Most consequential gap: no peer shows margin rising while new-footprint growth runs above 25% inside four quarters. Krsnaa, the nearest case, guided to hold 28% and printed 25% then 27%. QLL's guide needs about +4 points from the 21.6% H1 print (B05). The separating observation: H2 FY27 margin at or above 27% reads H1 as timing; below 24% reads the guide as set off the H2 FY26 peak (B05 analyst_note).
- Strongest independent confirmation: Vimta and Krsnaa both report that new lab capacity dilutes margin and ROCE first, and recovers over years (Claim 4). It confirms the mechanism QLL itself cites for its H1 FY27 margin gap.
- Overall: the peer set complicates the QLL narrative. It supports a 25-27% margin level, the dilution mechanism and the plausibility of a large overseas share (Vimta). It does not support margin recovery inside two quarters, a 35-40% growth rate from organic sources, or cash conversion without a payer problem when government customers are involved. It also supplies the benign reading: the fastest-growing peer called its margin dip timing and it partly recovered within a quarter.

## PART 5: CROSS-PEER HYPOTHESIS

cross_peer_hypothesis: "In this Indian lab-services set, the source of growth, not its pace, decides whether margin and cash conversion move up or down. Growth from new footprint (Krsnaa: Rajasthan, new MRIs, retail; Vimta: Rs 100 Cr building plus biologics) cuts EBITDA margin and ROCE together for about four to eight quarters, and when the payer is government it also stretches receivables to 139-155 days. Growth from price and mix on existing capacity (Lal: CGHS tariff, test mix, operating leverage) lifts margin by about 4 points and holds DSO at 17 days.
