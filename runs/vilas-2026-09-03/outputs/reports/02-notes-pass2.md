NEW FINDING 2 — ₹546.88 Lakh (₹5.47 Cr) unreconciled gap between IPO fund-utilization "IPO expenses"
remark and the P&L IPO Expenses line 🔴
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Note 45 (p.75), IPO fund utilization table: the "General Corporate Purposes" object shows Actual Utilized
Amount = ₹1,274.47 Lakhs, with the Remarks column stating the entire utilized amount is "IPO expenses."
The identical table appears in Board's Report Sec.10 (p.18).

But Note 29(b) Other Expenses (p.69) records the P&L "IPO Expenses" line for FY25 (the year of listing) at
only ₹727.59 Lakhs — and per Note 2.XV (p.55), IPO expenditure is Company policy to expense ENTIRELY in
year of incurrence, never amortized. Note 16 (p.63) shows a further ₹20.30 Lakhs of "Miscellaneous
Expenses... Expenses in connection with Initial Public Offer" carried at FY24 year-end and written off to
Nil by FY25 — even adding this in full, cumulative P&L-recognized IPO expense is ₹747.89 Lakhs, still
₹526.58 Lakhs short of the ₹1,274.47 Lakhs the Fund Utilization table attributes to "IPO expenses."

Note 4 (Reserves & Surplus, p.57) shows no offsetting explanation either: the Securities Premium
roll-forward records only "Add: Premium Received during the year ₹8,877.60L," with no "Less: share issue
expenses adjusted against Securities Premium" line (which would be the normal alternative treatment under
Companies Act if issue costs were not expensed through the P&L) — so this ~₹5.27 Cr cannot be traced to
Securities Premium either.

This is a genuine, quantifiable, unreconciled gap (roughly 5.5% of the ₹95.26 Cr gross IPO proceeds)
between two disclosures in the same Annual Report purporting to describe the same category of spend.
It does not by itself prove misstatement — the Fund Utilization table's "Remarks" column may be a loose
catch-all mischaracterizing other General Corporate Purpose spend as "IPO expenses" — but as filed, the
two numbers do not reconcile and no bridge is disclosed. Recommended as a direct operator/management
question. (Note 45 p.75; Board's Report Sec.10 p.18; Note 29(b) p.69; Note 2.XV p.55; Note 16 p.63;
Note 4 p.57.)

NEW FINDING 6 — Note 9/Note 20 cross-reference: a debit-balance Cash Credit facility is netted into cash,
not shown as gross short-term borrowing 🟡
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Note 9 (p.58) states: "Since the year end balance in the Cash Credit Account was a debit amount, the same
has been presented under the head of 'Balances lying with Bank in Current Accounts.'" This ties to Note 20
(p.65), "Balances with Banks - In Current Accounts/Dr. Balance of Cash Credit Accounts," ₹729.94L FY25
(down from ₹926.74L FY24). The practical effect: the ICICI Cash Credit facility's exposure at any given
period-end can swing between appearing as a Short-Term Borrowing (Note 9, when in credit/drawn) and as
Cash & Cash Equivalents (Note 20, when in debit/undrawn-and-in-surplus), depending on the sign of the
balance on that date. This means Note 9's ₹1,140.97L short-term borrowings figure is not necessarily
representative of the facility's peak or average utilization through the year — a nuance for anyone
building a gross-debt or peak-drawn-facility view from the Notes alone. (Note 9 p.58; Note 20 p.65.)
