### 1C. Trigger evolution across the five quarters

- **Saudi capacity strategy (greenfield → NPC acquisition)**: Q2 FY26 call — "on track," commissioning "Quarter 4 2026." Q3 FY26 call — pushed to Q1 FY27, challenged by an analyst as a slip; management says "pretty much on time." Q4 FY26 call — **greenfield project scrapped entirely**, replaced by the NPC acquisition. Q1 FY27 call — the NPC platform is now delivering: Aramco-approved vendor status confirmed, ~Rs.43 cr revenue booked from a 20-day partial-quarter consolidation, and concrete integration actions disclosed (wastage cut from double-digit to single-digit %, spiral mill being upgraded to 100-inch to unlock water-order business the prior owners could not access, consumables re-sourced away from Japan-only suppliers). Trend: **dropped-then-replaced, now showing early tangible delivery** on the replacement trigger, though revenue/margin guidance for NPC itself still moved within the Q1 FY27 call (Rs.1,500 cr vs ~Rs.1,200 cr for FY27).
- **Jammu SS plant**: Q2 FY26 call — 1-Apr-2026 firm target. Q3 FY26 call — "slightly delayed," blamed on war and flooding. Q4 FY26 call — FY28 first-year utilisation guided at 35%-40%, implying a slip past FY26. Q1 FY27 call — "construction remains on track, production expected by March '27" — the first quarter with a single, unqualified, unrevised statement of this trigger since the original date was missed. Trend: **weakening through Q4 FY26, stabilising in Q1 FY27** but still roughly a year later than the original 1-Apr-2026 date, and not yet delivered.
- **FY27 consolidated revenue guidance**: Q2 FY26 call — ~Rs.7,000 cr. Q3 FY26 call — internally inconsistent (25-30% growth, "internal goal" 50-55%, Rs.5,500-6,000 cr, and an even earlier Rs.6,500 cr figure quoted back by an analyst). Q4 FY26 call — settles at Rs.5,000-5,500 cr, excluding Merino. Q1 FY27 call — reaffirmed at ~Rs.5,000 cr headline, but the India component softened to ~Rs.3,800 cr (from ~Rs.4,000 cr) and the Saudi/NPC component is stated as both ~Rs.1,500 cr (CFO, opening remarks) and ~Rs.1,200 cr "some-odd crores" (MD, Q&A) **within the same call**. Trend: **weakening/still unstable**, cut ~21-29% from the original Rs.7,000 cr figure across two quarters, and still moving inside a single call five quarters in.
- **EBITDA margin guidance**: Q2 FY26 call — 11-12%. Q3 FY26 call — upgraded to 13-14%. Q4 FY26 call — delivered 13% consol / 14% standalone. Q1 FY27 call — delivered 15.3% standalone / 14.6% consolidated (partial-quarter NPC drag), and management states a new long-run target of 14-16% for the next 3-5 years while explicitly declining to commit to a higher (17%) number an analyst proposed. Trend: **strengthening, the one guidance line genuinely and repeatedly delivered across four consecutive quarters**.
- **Order book**: falls every quarter in the primary set through Q4 FY26 (Rs.4,750 cr → Rs.4,000 cr → Rs.3,000 cr). Q1 FY27 call reports a consolidated order book of Rs.3,600 cr — an apparent recovery — but the breakdown shows India standalone at only Rs.2,200-2,300 cr, a **continued decline** from Rs.3,000 cr, now below the Rs.2,500 cr level; the consolidated headline recovers only because NPC's own inherited book (~Rs.1,300-1,400 cr implied) is added for the first time. Trend: **weakening on a like-for-like basis, masked by a consolidation-scope change**; a disclosure-threshold policy (orders announced only once cumulative wins cross Rs.500 cr) was revealed for the first time in Q1 FY27, offering a partial (not full) explanation for some of the apparent softness.
- **Merino Shelters real estate**: present in every call with a different figure and (until Q1 FY27) a slipping launch date. Q1 FY27 call: RERA registration and commencement certificate now both in hand, launch confirmed for "mid of September" 2026 — the first quarter this specific promise did not slip further — but the cash-quantum figure diverges again within the same call (Rs.35-50 cr vs Rs.80-100 cr). Trend: **launch timing stabilising, specificity on quantum still a five-quarter-running red flag**.
- **Aramco relationship**: new, vaguely defined trigger in Q3 FY26 ("premature... work in progress"), substantiated by the NPC acquisition in Q4 FY26 (2-decade relationship claimed), and now explicitly upgraded in Q1 FY27 to "Aramco-approved vendor status" with management stating a deliberate strategy to skew NPC's order mix toward Aramco (higher margin) versus water orders (lower margin) to push blended margin "up to almost 20-plus percent." Trend: **new trigger, steadily strengthening and increasingly specific**.
- **Far East / Asia-Pacific supply diversification**: a wholly new trigger introduced in the Q1 FY27 call, tying the Hormuz Strait disruption to a "genuinely new structural tailwind" for Japan, Korea, Vietnam, Bangladesh, and Philippines diversifying LNG/oil sourcing away from Gulf transit risk (p3). Trend: **new, unsubstantiated by order numbers yet** — a narrative claim to be tested against peer concalls (see 4B).
- **Competitive intensity in Saudi**: Q1 FY26 call named Welspun (via East Pipes) and Jindal SAW as capacity-adders. Q1 FY27 call adds East Pipes and Arabian Pipes by name directly, and for the first time management concedes "there might be some pressure on the price" from new entrants, while maintaining its "no massive demand-supply gap issue" stance for "the next 3 to 4 years." Trend: **strengthening disclosure of competitive risk**, a incremental concession worth tracking.
- **US market expansion**: raised once by an analyst in the Q2 FY26 call; management says "no plans currently." Never mentioned again in Q3, Q4 FY26, or Q1 FY27 calls. Trend: **dropped** (a minor trigger, not central to the thesis).

### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

For each miss, the reason given and its classification:
- FY26 revenue growth miss: **silence** across all five calls — no call explicitly states the 20% target was missed; framed instead as a margin "upgrade."
- H2 FY26 revenue shortfall: **silence** — never reconciled in any later call, including Q1 FY27.
- Saudi greenfield plant abandonment: **deflection** — reframed as an opportunistic upgrade ("we got a good deal") rather than an admission the originally guided project was wrong; Q1 FY27 call does not revisit this history at all.
- Jammu delay: **external-blame** — war and flooding named explicitly (Q3 FY26 call, p15); Q1 FY27 call drops the external-blame framing entirely and simply restates "on track," without reconciling the ~11-month slip against the original date.
- FY27 guidance cuts/inconsistency: **external-blame** for the Q3→Q4 FY26 cut (commodity/steel-price volatility cited); **silence** for the Q1 FY27 intra-call Rs.1,500cr-vs-Rs.1,200cr NPC figure — nobody on the call flags or reconciles the two numbers.
- Order-book decline: **deflection** through Q4 FY26 (pointing to bid pipeline/NPC rather than explaining the standalone decline); Q1 FY27 call similarly does not address why India's own order book kept falling even as the consolidated figure was presented as an improvement — this is now a **five-quarter pattern of not naming the standalone decline as a decline**.
- Gross margin QoQ collapse (53%→35%, Q1 FY27): **honest-admission-in-progress** — CFO could not explain the move live and said "I will come back to you on this point" rather than deflecting with a vague answer; a genuine acknowledgment of not having the number ready, but leaves the point unresolved as of this call.
- Forex MTM loss (Q4 FY26 call): **external-blame** with a partial **honest-admission** — management agreed under analyst pressure to add an "extraordinary note" disclosure.

Pattern check: management blames externals (war, floods, currency, commodity prices) for misses, or simply stays silent and reframes the topic, and across five quarters has never once stated plainly "we got the [FY26 revenue] guidance wrong." The Q1 FY27 gross-margin non-answer is the closest thing to a spontaneous "I don't have that yet" admission in the full set, which is a small but real positive data point for the accountability dimension. Hard topics (order-book decline on a like-for-like basis, the original Saudi plant's abandonment, the SEBI stay order) continue to be raised only by analysts, never volunteered by management, across all five calls.

**excuse_pattern: external-blame-heavy**
