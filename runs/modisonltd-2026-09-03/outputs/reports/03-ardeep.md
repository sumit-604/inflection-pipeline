Materiality: MODERATE — R&D is only ₹1.59cr (0.22% of revenue), so the absolute rupee impact on the thesis is small, but the pattern (stated priority, contradicted allocation, no acknowledgement) is itself a signal about how much weight to put on OTHER unquantified Strategic Outlook claims in this same section.
2. **"maintained a healthy liquidity profile... supported by robust operational cash flows"** (MD&A p.75) vs **Operating Cash Flow of -₹64.18cr disclosed two lines below in the same table, and the full negative-CFO/rising-leverage picture across Notes 24/41/50 and the Cash Flow Statement** (Phase 3A). Materiality: **HIGH — this changes the thesis.** It is the single clearest instance in the document of management narrative not merely omitting a fact (which would be 4B's territory) but actively mischaracterising a fact it discloses in the same breath.
3. No evidence of a silent drop or hedged retreat was found on customer concentration, export strategy, or the Make-in-India/PLI framing — these are stated in general terms and not meaningfully tested against operational detail because the single-segment presentation prevents the comparison from being made either way (this is a Phase 4B disclosure gap, not a 6E abandonment, since there is no operational section to compare against).

**Phase 6 Verdict: 🟡 Watch, with the liquidity-narrative item weighted 🔴.** The "About Us"/Strategic Outlook substitute for a Chairman's Letter is professionally written and well-sourced externally, but contains one high-materiality implicit retraction (liquidity/cash flow) and one moderate-materiality implicit retraction (R&D as a "key strategic priority" while spend fell).
