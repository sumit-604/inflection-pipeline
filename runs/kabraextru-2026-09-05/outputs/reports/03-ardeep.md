### 6E Quiet Abandonment Check (mandatory)
**Finding 1 — the strongest and most material of this check.** MD&A Business Outlook (Section D, AR FY26 p.38,

customer base."** The operational section that should corroborate this — Note 38, the segment result table, four
pages later in the audited Notes — shows the OPPOSITE: Battery Division segment loss **widened** from Rs25.53
Cr to Rs43.35 Cr (+69.8%) even as segment revenue grew +7.2%. The MD&A makes no acknowledgement anywhere
that this is the SECOND consecutive year (per the AR FY25 parallel claim, Phase 4C) the "moving toward
profitability" framing has been offered while the segment result moved further AWAY from profitability. Classified
as **(a) implicit retraction** — the opening says X (moving toward profitability), operations say not-X (loss
widening faster than revenue grows), with no acknowledgement of the contradiction anywhere in the document.
**Materiality: HIGH — this changes the thesis directly.** It is precisely the growth-optionality-vs-loss-carrier tension
named in COMPANY MEMORY's verification priority #2, and the AR's own front-matter narrative does not engage
with it at all.

**Finding 2 — the ~40% market-share claim.** AR FY25's opening Key Strengths section named a specific,
quantified competitive claim ("~40% market share... as on FY25"). AR FY26's equivalent opening section (Key
Strengths, MD&A p.33-34) makes the SAME qualitative claim of "strong leadership position" and "market leader...
over four decades" but drops the quantified 40% figure entirely, with no updated figure, no explanation, and no
acknowledgement that a number once given is no longer being given. Classified as **(c) hedged retreat** — the
opening still says "leadership," but the specific, checkable version of that claim (a number) has been quietly
replaced with an unquantified version. **Materiality: MODERATE** — this is a competitive-positioning claim, not a
financial one, but it was material enough to be the operator's own carried verification priority #1, and its
disappearance without comment is exactly the kind of walk-back this check exists to catch.

**Finding 3 — "strong balance sheet."** The Chairman's Letter's closing paragraphs assert a "strong balance sheet"
supporting "long term sustainable value." The operational/financial sections (Balance Sheet, Note 17, Note 35 —
these sit deep in the audited statements, the clearest "operational section" contrast available) show thin cash,
rising net debt, a credit downgrade, and negative FCF. Classified as **(a) implicit retraction** — opening says X
(strong balance sheet), the audited numbers say not-X, with zero acknowledgement. **Materiality: HIGH** — this is
a direct, quantifiable contradiction on the single most load-bearing claim in the letter's closing argument.

No further quiet abandonments beyond these three were identified with the same directness; the CRISIL downgrade,

then contradicted, and are not double-counted here.

**Phase 6 summary:** The front matter tells a clean, forward-looking, growth-narrative story that is directly

by the operational sections four to sixty pages later in the SAME document, with zero acknowledgement of either
contradiction anywhere in the text.

**Phase 6 verdict: 🔴 RED FLAG.**
