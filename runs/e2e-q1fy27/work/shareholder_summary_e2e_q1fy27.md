# E2E Q1 FY27 — Plain-language summary for a minority shareholder (2026-07-23)

Companion to the v3 review. Weigh, not anchor. Decision Status unchanged (WATCHLIST / BUY ON DIPS).

1. E2E had a genuinely great quarter: revenue up 334% and, for the first time, real operating profit, not accounting tricks. The business is working.
2. The growth is high quality: it came from filling up their GPUs and switching on new chips, not from raising prices. That is the good kind of growth.
3. They finally showed the recurring-revenue number (Rs71.8 crore/month) and, importantly, customers are now signing 1-3 year contracts and paying advances. That makes the income stickier than before.
4. But the big new worry: they have borrowed heavily. Debt jumped to about Rs450 crore, so the company has swung from net cash to net debt, and management refused to say how high the borrowing will peak.
5. This matters to you because more debt means more risk if growth slows, and interest costs eat into your profits.
6. Management is open about what already happened but dodgy about the future: they refused to give guidance on almost every number analysts asked for, and named zero customers across three documents.
7. That evasiveness is a yellow flag on trust, not a red one yet, but worth watching.
8. Watch also the new offshore/subsidiary entities (SovCloud, a Delaware company) - they could be used to raise money or park debt outside the main company's books, which reduces your visibility.
9. On price: after adjusting for the 1:10 stock split, the current ~Rs446 is roughly fair only if the strong growth continues AND the debt stays manageable - it is not obviously cheap.
10. Nothing has broken in the thesis, so the stance stays: hold and watch, not add.
11. The one thing to watch next quarter (September results): does the Rs71.8 crore/month hold, do they name a real anchor customer, and how big is the debt.
12. Bottom line: excellent business, improving durability, but rising leverage and thin disclosure mean you are being asked to trust management on the riskiest parts - so size your position for that uncertainty and let Q2 confirm the story before doing anything.
