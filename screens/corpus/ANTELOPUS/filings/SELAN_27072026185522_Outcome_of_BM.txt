RESOLVED FURTHER THAT pursuant to Regulation 31A(3)(a)(ii) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations

Regulations”), including any statutory modification(s) or re-enactment thereof, for the time being in force

from “Promoter Group Shareholder” to “Public Shareholder” . 
RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Board of Directors hereby take note that as required under the
