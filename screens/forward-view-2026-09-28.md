line, growth against FY26, and the forward price-to-earnings ratio. The
operator asked for the topline too, so this run adds FY27E revenue.

**Read this with the cards, not instead of them.** A cheap forward multiple on
fast growth is a screen result, not a conclusion.

## Where each forward number can mislead

- **NAMOEWASTE** screens cheapest on growth. The guide needs battery revenue to
  rise from Rs 12 to 18 crore to about Rs 200 crore in one year, from a plant
  at 10% utilisation. Last year's guide missed by about Rs 25 crore. At FY26
  growth repeated it is 40.4x. H1 FY27 is the proof gate, due within weeks.
- **LCL** is the only PROCEED. The low end is Q1 FY27 times four; the high end
  is the June order book of Rs 1,778.2 crore. FY26 revenue is still below
  FY23's Rs 1,847 crore, so the open question is cycle peak or structure.
- **ROLEXRINGS** management growth claims contradict its filed numbers. India
  Ratings forecasts FY27 growth of 7% to 9%; on that view the forward PE is
  24.3x to 24.8x. Revenue is flat since FY22.
- **PATILAUTOM** dilutes 17.96% on the Rs 96.38 crore preferential (EGM
  3-Oct-2026). Diluted forward PE 30.5x to 34.9x. Standalone operating cash
  flow was negative.
- **UGARSUGAR** books deferred tax only. At an assumed 25.17% tax the low
  forward PE is 30.2x. Interest paid exceeded operating cash flow.
- **SAHASRA** other income was 72% of FY26 PBT. The PAT range rests on an
  operating margin the company has not yet delivered.
- **WHIRLPOOL** carries a 20% price jump on 23-Sep-2026 on a promoter-exit
  report. The multiple prices a deal, not the earnings.
- **HOLMARC** FY26 results and annual report are indexed, but the reader
  returns empty for both. Forward PE left blank per the tier D rule.
