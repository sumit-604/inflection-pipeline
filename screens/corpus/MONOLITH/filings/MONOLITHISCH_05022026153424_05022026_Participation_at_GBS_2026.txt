Press Release 
 
Monolithisch India Limited at GBS 2026 — “A Decade of Disruption, A Century of Change” 
Promoters to Engage in High-Impact Global Leadership Dialogue at Asia’s Premier 
Business Summit 
 
Monolithisch India Limited is proud to announce that its promoters will be attending and actively 
participating in the GBS 2026 – “A Decade of Disruption, A Century of Change” summit scheduled 
for 13-14 February, 2026 in New Delhi. The summit, organised by The Times Group’, ET NOW, 
GBS (Global Business Summit), stands as Asia’s definitive thought leadership platform addressing 
the unprecedented scale and speed of global transformation. 
 
Now in its 10th edition, GBS 2026 brings together more than 2,000 global delegates, 400+ CEOs, 
policymakers, industry leaders, and visionary thinkers from over 55 countries to explore the 
tectonic shifts reshaping economies, governance, technology ecosystems, and human capital. 
Anchored in the theme “A Decade of Disruption, A Century of Change”, the agenda emphasises 
how disruption has evolved from episodic shocks to a constant force altering business 
fundamentals and societal frameworks. 
 
Commenting on the participation, Mr. Harsh Tekriwal, Managing Director - Promoter of 
Monolithisch India Limited, said: 
 
“Monolithisch India Limited is honoured to engage in the Global Business Summit 2026. In a 
world defined by rapid change—from technological breakthroughs and economic realignments 
to evolving governance and sustainability imperatives—our presence underlines our 
commitment to strategic foresight, resilient growth, and collaborative leadership.” 
 
As a forward-looking enterprise, Monolithisch India Limited recognises that the challenges and 
opportunities defining the 21st century call for cross-sector dialogue, strategic alignment, and 
visionary leadership. Participation in GBS 2026 will enable the company’s promoters to amplify 
Monolithisch’s commitment to innovation, governance excellence, and sustainable value creation 
on the global stage.
