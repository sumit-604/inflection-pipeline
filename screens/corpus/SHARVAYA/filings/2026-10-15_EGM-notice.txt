The following are the impact and objects of the proposed acquisition: 
REASONS FOR ACQUISITION 
The proposed acquisition of 100% equity shareholding of Success Electronics Private 
Limited (“SEPL”) by Sharvaya Metals Limited (“SML” or “the Company”) is a strategic 
initiative aimed at strengthening, diversifying and expanding the Company's 
manufacturing capabilities in the aluminium high pressure die castings and further into 
EV component sectors. 
Sharvaya Metals Limited has established its presence in aluminium alloy manufacturing, 
aluminium extrusion, precision machining, die manufacturing and the production of 
aluminium components for defence, aerospace, electric vehicles and engineering 
components. The Company has also recently commissioned a state-of-the-art aluminium 
extrusion facility, further enhancing 
its capability to manufacture value-added aluminium 
products. 
Success Electronics Private Limited is an established manufacturer of aluminium high- 
pressure die-cast (HPDC) components, with existing manufacturing facilities comprising 
218-tonne and 418-tonne HPDC machines. Importantly, Success Electronics Private 
Limited is currently engaged in manufacturing and supplying aluminium casting 
components required for electric vehicles, and other automotive components. It also 
possesses experience in supplying components to the broader automotive industry

===== PAGE 19 =====
moreover, the company is free from bank debts and as on date there is no bank debt on 
the company. 
The proposed acquisition is strategically significant as both companies are already 
participating in the automotive manufacturing ecosystem through complementary 
manufacturing processes. While Sharvaya manufactures precision-machined aluminium 
extruded components, Success Electronics Private Limited manufactures aluminium die- 
cast components. Combining these operations is expected to strengthen the group's 
participation in the automotive and EV supply chain catering wide range of Aluminium 
component requirement to the entire Automobile industry. 
High-pressure die casting is an important manufacturing process for producing complex, 
dimensionally accurate aluminium components, including motor vehicle housings, 
mounting brackets, covers and other engineered components. The acquisition will provide 
Sharvaya with access to this manufacturing capability without having to establish an 
entirely new HPDC business from the initial stages. 
The acquisition is also intended to create a more integrated aluminium manufacturing 
ecosystem encompassing aluminium alloy manufacturing, billet casting, extrusion, high- 
pressure die casting, tooling, precision machining and value-added finishing. Such 
integration is expected to facilitate better utilisation of manufacturing infrastructure, 
technical resources and engineering expertise across the group. 
Further, the acquisition will enable Sharvaya to diversify its product portfolio, expand its 
presence in higher-value engineered aluminium components and pursue additional 
business opportunities with existing and prospective automotive customers. 
The proposed transaction is therefore aligned with the Company's long-term business 
strategy of pursuing inorganic growth, enhancing value addition, strengthening its 
manufacturing capabilities and establishing itself as a diversified aluminium component 
manufacturing enterprise. 
OBJECTS OF ACQUISITION 
The principal object of the proposed acquisition is to expand Sharvaya Metals Limited's 
manufacturing operations by acquiring an established aluminium high-pressure die 
casting business and integrating its complementary manufacturing capabilities with those 
of the Company. 
The acquisition is intended to achieve the following strategic and operational objectives: 
a) Strengthening the Electric Vehicle Business: To consolidate the Company's position 
in the electric vehicle component manufacturing industry by combining Sharvaya's 
existing EV battery housing and precision machining operations with Success 
Electronics established manufacturing and supply of aluminium die-cast components. 
b) Creation of an Integrated Manufacturing Platform: To establish a comprehensive 
aluminium manufacturing ecosystem comprising alloy manufacturing, extrusion, die

within the group.

Pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/242 dated 09.12.2020, under Regulation 
44 of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015, listed entities are required to provide remote e-voting facility to its shareholders, in 
respect of all shareholders’ resolutions. However, it has been observed that the participation by the 
public non-institutional shareholders/retail shareholders is at 
a negligible level. 
Currently, there are multiple e-voting service providers (ESPs) providing e-voting facility to listed entities 
in India. This necessitates registration on various ESPs and maintenance of multiple user IDs and 
passwords by the shareholders. 
In order to increase the efficiency of the voting process, pursuant to a public consultation, it has been 
decided to enable e-voting to all the demat account holders, by way of a single login credential, 
through their demat accounts/ websites of Depositories/ Depository Participants. Demat account 
holders would be able to cast their vote without having 
to register again with the ESPs, thereby, not only 
facilitating seamless authentication but also enhancing ease and convenience of participating in e-voting 
process. 
In terms of SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/242 dated December 9, 2020 on e-Voting 
facility provided by Listed Companies, Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode are 
allowed to vote through their demat account maintained with Depositories and Depository Participants. 
Shareholders are advised to update their mobile number and email Id in their demat accounts in order 
to access e-Voting facility.
