## NARRATIVE

Divgi makes the gearbox parts that let a pickup switch to four-wheel drive. The business is changing. Export orders and an Indonesia batch are lifting revenue fast. Operating profit per share should climb from Rs 24 this year toward Rs 34 in three years. That part is real and running.

Two things are not real yet. The move up in quality has not started. The EV gearbox has missed its launch date three quarters in a row. The automatic-transmission contract keeps slipping. So the higher-quality business the story promises is still a promise.

The price is the problem. At Rs 1,175 the market pays 45 times ex-cash forward earnings for a business the framework values near 20 times. Fair value is Rs 815. The stock sits 44% above it. Even on the operator's generous 30x multiple, and even on bull earnings, the 25% target cannot be reached from here. The return you would earn is negative.

There is a price where the maths works: Rs 570 to Rs 650. Buy there and the return comes mostly from the discount closing to Rs 815, plus fair value growing about 11% a year. Not from the business compounding at 25%. That is the honest label.

Two more gates guard that price. The Q2 FY27 result must show the transfer-case line above Rs 70 Cr at a 24% margin. And the governance cloud must clear: a US-subsidiary story told in the wrong tense, a related party left off the list, pay figures that do not match, and an AGM asking to approve incentives that may breach the pay ceiling. The house rule holds the position at zero until the September AGM and the FY27 report clear it.

So the answer is a watch, with a number. Not a buy today. Watch the price toward Rs 650, watch the Q2 print, watch the AGM. Act only when all three line up.
