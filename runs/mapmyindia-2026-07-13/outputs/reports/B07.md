### 2C Growth embedded in capex — arithmetic shown
Total capex under execution (FY26, PP&E + intangibles, cash basis) = **Rs42.40 Cr**
(B01/results FY26 p.9). Fixed asset turnover, FY26 = Revenue Rs474.10 Cr ÷ Net
Block Rs105.31 Cr = **4.50x** (B01, M3 capital-efficiency test, screener-data,
computed).

Implied incremental revenue = 42.40 × 4.50 = **Rs190.8 Cr**, or **40.3% above
FY26 revenue of Rs474.1 Cr**.

**Caveat carried forward, not smoothed over**: this is a mechanical proxy. A large
share of "capex" here is IoT hardware capitalised against specific signed
customer contracts (IOCL, government fleets) rather than general-purpose capacity
addition, so the FAT multiplier likely overstates the speed at which this capex
converts to *broad-based* incremental revenue versus contract-specific revenue
that is already substantially locked in via the order book. `capex_embedded_growth_pct: 40.3` carried to the YAML with this caveat attached.

## SECTION 4: REGULATORY & POLICY TAILWINDS (R1)

### 4A Regulatory approvals in pipeline
No approval-pending items in the classic sense (drug/plant-license style) apply
to this business. The relevant "approval" event is the **Survey of India G2G
contract** (signed Oct 2025, 📄) which functionally unlocks MMI's role as

### 4B Government policy tailwinds
| Policy | Amount/duration | Enrolment status | Shared with competitors? |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Geospatial Policy (NGP) 2022 | Liberalizes geospatial data access sector-wide; market projected to reach Rs1 lakh Cr by 2030 (AR p.35) | Active since 2022 | Yes — sector-wide liberalization benefits all domestic geospatial players, not MMI alone |

| Data localization provisions | Restricts foreign players from high-resolution data access "not permitted for foreign players" (AR p.27) | Active | This element specifically favours domestic incumbents like MMI over Google/HERE-class foreign entrants |
| DIGIPIN (geo-coded addressing), Mission Mausam (Rs2,000 Cr AI weather), Operation Dronagiri | Cited as FY25-26 budget initiatives (AR p.35) | Active/early | Shared sector tailwinds; no MMI-specific contract disclosed against these specific line items in the provided documents |

### 4C Regulatory moat assessment
**Emerging, not yet fully active as an MMI-exclusive moat.** The liberalization
plus data-localization combination is real and documented, and MMI has already
converted part of it into a signed, revenue-bearing contract (Survey of India).
But the policy tailwind itself is industry-wide — it lifts the whole domestic
geospatial sector, not MMI uniquely — so R1 is scored Moderate (HM, raw 3) rather
than Strong (HH, raw 4): MMI's edge rests on being best-positioned to capture the
tailwind (scale, 30-year data asset, existing government relationships), not on
possessing an MMI-exclusive regulatory right. Time to kick in further: 12-24
months as the National Geospatial Mission's Rs100 Cr FY26 allocation and the
Survey of India platform scale from contract-signed to full deployment.
Sustainability: high, so long as the data-localization provision holds — a
policy-reversal risk exists but no reversal signal is present in the documents.
