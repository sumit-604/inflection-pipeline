"Management Commentary

" We are pleased to report that Q2 FY27 has been another record quarter for Netweb Technologies, with our highest-ever quarterly Revenue from
       Operations and Profit After Tax. H1 FY27 PAT reached 2,220 Mn, surpassing our full-year FY26 PAT of 2,058 Mn in just six months, underscoring the
       scale of our business and strong growth momentum.
       In Q2 FY27, Revenue from Operations grew 335.7% YoY to 13,233 Mn. Operating EBITDA increased 334.0% YoY to 1,973 Mn, with a margin of 14.9%,
       while PAT rose 334.8% YoY to 1,367 Mn, translating into a margin of 10.2%. Our ability to sustain margins while scaling, reflects strong execution and
       operational discipline amid rising demand for high-performance computing and AI infrastructure.
       AI Systems remained the primary growth driver, contributing 7,731 Mn (58.4% of Revenue from Operations) and growing 1,068.1% YoY. HPC and Private
       Cloud also delivered robust performance, contributing 2,482 Mn and 2,448 Mn, respectively, highlighting the strength of our diversified growth
       portfolio. As of 30 September 2026, our order book stood at 26,290 Mn, with an additional L1 position of 6,525 Mn, providing strong revenue visibility
       for the coming quarters.
       The quarter also marked a significant milestone in our growth journey, as we successfully raised 12,000 Mn through our first capital raise since listing
       in July 2023. We are grateful to our investors for their overwhelming response and continued confidence in our business, growth strategy, and long-
       term potential.
       The world is witnessing an unprecedented surge in demand for AI compute, with India emerging as a significant growth market. Government bodies,
       public institutions and large enterprises increasingly view compute as strategic infrastructure, prioritising secure, high-performance systems that
       enable critical data and workloads to remain within the country while optimising AI token economics. Achieving cost-efficient token generation
       requires not just access to advanced compute, but also strong engineering, system design and solutioning capabilities. Netweb has invested in building
       these capabilities over the years, positioning us to address the evolving requirements of this market.
       Simultaneously, global cloud service providers (CSPs), data centre operators, enterprises, and Neocloud providers are expanding GPU-intensive
       infrastructure to support the growing computational demands of AI training, inference and other AI-driven applications, with India emerging as an
       important destination for their expansion plans. These developments, coupled with the growing need for indigenous computing capabilities and end-
       to-end technology stacks, are reinforcing the importance of the Sovereign infrastructure. With our integrated design and engineering capabilities, in-
       house manufacturing and end-to-end solution deployment expertise, Netweb is well positioned to meet these requirements.
       Our strategies for supercomputing & private cloud have also delivered excellent results. Furthermore, as the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM)
       2.0 places greater emphasis on designing and building in India, and HPC adoption expands across enterprises, indigenous design & manufacturing
       and bespoke solutioning are becoming increasingly important differentiators in the high-end computing landscape.
       We believe these structural drivers will support a sustained growth cycle in high-end computing space. Our active pipeline of 1,11,518 Mn provides
       significant visibility into potential business opportunities and reinforces our confidence in the growth outlook.
       As a leading Indian-origin OEM in High-end Computing Solutions, we continue to advance our technological capabilities through sustained investment
       in innovation and a chip-agnostic design philosophy. Combined with our alignment with the Make in India vision, a strengthened balance sheet
       following the recent capital raise and a robust order book, these strengths position us to capitalise on the expanding demand for high-end computing,
       deliver sustainable long-term growth and create enduring value for our stakeholders.

    Sanjay Lodha, CMD

Notes

1. Operating EBITDA is calculated as Profit before Tax (PBT) plus `Depreciation and amortization expenses' and `Finance cost' less `Other income'; Operating EBITDA Margin is calculated as Operating EBITDA divided
     by `Revenue from operations'

2. Profit before Tax (PBT) margin is a percentage of `Profit before tax' divided by `Total Income'

3. Profit after Tax (PAT) margin is a percentage of `Profit for the period/year' divided by `Total Income'

4. Free Cash is calculated as `Cash and cash equivalents' and non-lien cash from `Bank balances other than cash and cash equivalents'

5. Net Debt is calculated as Non-current & current `Borrowings' plus Non-current & current `Lease Liabilities' less Free Cash

6. Net Fixed Assets is calculated as sum of `Property, plant and equipment', `Capital work-in-progress', `Right-of-use assets', `Other intangible assets' & `Intangible assets under development'

7. Net Current Assets is calculated as `Current assets' less `Current liabilities' plus current `Borrowings' plus current `Lease liabilities' less Free Cash

8. Gross Fixed Asset Turnover Ratio for the full years are calculated as `Revenue from operations' divided by average of the current and previous years' Gross Fixed Assets and for September'26 it is calculated as
     annualized `Revenue from operations' for the quarter divided by average of September'26 & March'26 Gross Fixed Assets; Gross Fixed Assets is sum of Gross Carrying Amount of `Property, plant and equipment',
     and `Other intangible assets'

9. Return on Equity for full years are calculated as PAT for the respective years divided by average of the current and previous years' `Total Equity' and for September'26 it is calculated as annualized PAT for
     September'26 divided by average of September'26 & March'26 `Total Equity'

10. Return on Capital Employed for full years are calculated as EBIT for the respective years divided by average of the current and previous years' Capital Employed and for September'26 it is calculated as annualized
     EBIT for September'26 divided by average of September'26 & March'26 Capital Employed; EBIT is calculated as PBT plus `Finance cost' ; Capital Employed is calculated as sum of `Total equity', Non-current &
     current `Borrowings', and Non-current & current `Lease Liabilities'

11. Receivable Days for the full years are calculated as average of the current and previous years' `Trade receivables' divided by `Revenue from operations' for the respective years as multiplied by 365 days and for
     September'26 it is calculated as average of September'26 & March'26 `Trade receivables' divided by `Revenue from operations' for September'26 quarter as multiplied by 183 days

12. Inventory Days for the full years are calculated as average of the current and previous years' `Inventory' divided by `Revenue from operations' for the respective years as multiplied by 365 days and for September'26
     it is calculated as average of September'26 & March'26 `Inventory' divided by `Revenue from operations' for September'26 quarter as multiplied by 183 days

13. Payable Days for the full years are calculated as average of the current and previous years' `Trade Payables' divided by `Revenue from operations' for the respective year as multiplied by 365 days and for
     September'26 it is calculated as average of September'26 & March'26 `Trade Payables' divided by `Revenue from operations' for September'26 quarter as multiplied by 183 days

14. Annualized Q2 FY27 ratios in the presentation has been calculated by multiplying the relevant H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA, EBIT, PAT or Revenue from Operations by 2 for the computation of Net Debt/Operating
     EBITDA, ROCE, ROE and Gross Fixed Asset Turnover respectively
