## Active flag blocks

### FLAG-CASH

Determination: **INDETERMINATE**.

Cited evidence:
- Consolidated CFO to PAT was 0.478x in FY25 and 0.675x in FY26. Free cash flow was minus Rs 590.42 lakh in FY25 and minus Rs 754.24 lakh in FY26 (B03 flags; AR cash flow statement p.120). Cumulative FY24 to FY26 CFO to PAT is 0.573 and FCF was negative all three years (B01 block_b_trend).
- Receivables older than six months rose from 10.2% to 38.9% of the standalone book (18.4% to 38.2% consolidated). The six month to one year bucket rose from Rs 35.88 lakh to Rs 320.54 lakh. Provision coverage on the aged book fell from 15.8% to 8.3%. Management's own ratio note blames "extended credit cycles for certain customers" (B02 flags and top_findings rank 1 and 6; AR Note 18/42 standalone p.97, p.106; Note 20 consolidated p.135).
- Receivables composition: Rs 51.77 lakh of related party trade receivables (Ayush Diagnostics, Imperative Healthcare, Biocorn Healthcare) sit only in the consolidated note; the standalone note denies any. No split of the aged book by government, private or related party customer is disclosed (B02 top_findings rank 9; AR Note 20.1/36 consolidated p.135, p.141 to 142).
- Finished goods inventory rose 80.9% against revenue growth of 16.1% consolidated and 22.2% standalone (B02 flags; AR Note 17 standalone p.96, Note 19 consolidated p.134). Part of FY26 PBT growth is an unsold inventory build, a P&L inventory change credit of Rs 167.87 lakh standalone (B03 flags; AR p.82).
- Capex commissioning timeline: YEIDA CWIP rose from Rs 461.91 lakh to Rs 1,285.28 lakh with nothing capitalised to PPE. The IPO schedule set production for Oct 2026; the building milestone slipped about three months to Sep 2026. SIDBI loan repayment has already begun and no interest line appears in finance cost, which points to full capitalisation of borrowing cost, amount not disclosed (B05 promise_delivery; B02 top_findings rank 7 and 12; AR Note 13 p.95, Note 28 p.100; RHP pdf p.130 to 131; transcript p.3).
- Working capital plan versus guidance: the RHP plan assumed FY27 receivable days of 80, down from 128. On the call management cited 90 to 120 days on large government projects and a Rs 35 to 40 Cr working capital need at Rs 100 Cr revenue that is "not available today" (B12b findings; RHP pdf p.125 to 126; transcript p.6).
- The Q4 FY26 bank stock statements did not agree with the books; the auditor lists CARO clause (ii)(b) against the holding company with no rupee figure (B02 top_findings rank 13; AR Note 47 p.108; Annexure 1 p.115 to 116).

Rating agency verbatim quote: NOT AVAILABLE. Avience is an unrated issuer. The RHP states no rating was required for the issue (RHP pdf p.76), and no rating on bank facilities was found (B00 input_gaps).

Why INDETERMINATE: two readings fit the record. The GROWTH-INDUCED reading says the free cash outflow is the YEIDA plant build, the reagent rental model ties up stock and placed instruments ahead of revenue, and CFO to PAT improved from 0.478x to 0.675x. The STRUCTURAL reading says the aged receivables quadrupled as a share of the book, provisions fell, and finished goods jumped 81%, all during a half year in which revenue grew only 5%. Receivables building against slowing sales are not growth funding. The record cannot separate the two because the aged book has no customer split and no rating agency working capital view exists.

The one observation that separates them: the H1 FY27 half yearly result (due by Nov 2026). GROWTH-INDUCED holds if consolidated CFO is positive for the half and the aged receivables share falls back below 30%. STRUCTURAL is confirmed if the aged share rises past 40% or CFO turns negative while revenue grows.

Missing evidence to resolve the flag:
- Receivables ageing split by customer class (government, private, related party) at 30 Sep 2026. Source: management, via the next analyst meet or H1 FY27 notes.
- H1 FY27 cash flow statement. Source: NSE half yearly results filing.
- Rupee amount of borrowing cost capitalised into YEIDA CWIP, and the CARO (ii)(b) stock statement difference. Source: management; FY27 AR notes.
- Any credit rating rationale if the company takes one for the new bank lines. Source: rating agency site, NSE filing.

Cap effect: INDETERMINATE cash caps the verdict at PROCEED WITH CAVEATS. The confidence band moves it one level to PROCEED WITH FLAGS.

## Monitorables and triggers

1. Uttarakhand order. Watch NSE for a Reg 30 order filing, and the H1 FY27 result for a government or Uttarakhand revenue line. H1 FY27 growth under 30% YoY with no such line kills the FY27 guidance. Tests the single largest input behind the 60% growth claim.
2. H1 FY27 revenue against the slowing base. H2 FY26 revenue was Rs 2,568.65 lakh, up only 5% YoY. Watch whether H1 FY27 grows off H1 FY26's Rs 2,681.93 lakh at the guided pace. Source: NSE half yearly results. Tests whether FY26's second half slowdown was a pause or a trend.
3. Cash conversion. Watch consolidated CFO to PAT in the half yearly cash flow statement; 0.70x or better is the bar, and a third year below it confirms FLAG-CASH as structural. Tests whether profit growth is turning into cash.
4. Receivable quality. Watch the share of receivables older than six months (38.9% standalone in FY26) and the provision against them (8.3%). Reversal toward 10 to 18% clears the concern; a rise past 40% confirms it. Source: results and FY27 AR Note 18. Tests the collection risk behind government orders.
5. YEIDA plant. Watch for CWIP (Rs 1,285.28 lakh) moving to PPE and a production start by Oct 2026. Still uncapitalised at the Dec 2026 half is the kill signal. Source: results notes, Reg 30. Tests the only capacity behind the maker transition.
6. Manufactured share of turnover. Watch any mix disclosure from 27% toward 50%. Below about 35% by FY27 end means the transition has stalled and the business stays a reseller. Source: H1 FY27 deck and results.
7. Mindray. Watch the purchase share (63.0% in 10M FY26) and any signed exclusivity or OEM agreement via Reg 30. A rise back toward 72.7%, or any territory cut, hits 72% of revenue. Tests supplier dependence.
8. Reporting controls. Watch the first opinion of the new auditor, a rupee figure for the CARO (ii)(b) difference, and correction of the Note 40 labels in the FY27 AR. Any new revision or qualification tests whether the FY26 slips were one year events.

## Falsification line

The single most damaging next print: H1 FY27 results (due by Nov 2026) showing revenue growth under 30% YoY with no Uttarakhand or government linked revenue, printed alongside receivables older than six months above 40% of the book.
