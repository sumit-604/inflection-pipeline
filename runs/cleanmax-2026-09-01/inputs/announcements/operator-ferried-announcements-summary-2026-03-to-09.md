## 1. Capacity Additions and Commissioning
- **Q3 FY26 commissioning & portfolio (as of 31 Dec 2025 / 1 Mar 2026) — disclosed 17 Mar 2026.**
  - RE Power Sales contracted capacity 5.7 GW: 3.0 GW commissioned as of 1 Mar 2026 (up ~76% vs start of FY26); 2.7 GW under execution, expected commissioned within 24 months.
  - Additional 523 MW RE Services (customer-owned assets) operational, 226 MW contracted under execution.
  - Trailing-12-month commissioning (Power Sales) ~1.3 GW.
- **Kalavad Hybrid Project, Gujarat — 185 MW (Press Release, 7 Apr 2026).**
  - Commissioned 185 MW wind–solar hybrid at Kalavad, Gujarat.
  - Serves 17 corporate customers under group captive (incl. Apar Industries, Borosil Renewables).
  - Raises CleanMax operational energy-sale capacity in Gujarat to ~844 MW as of 31 Mar 2026.
  - Post-stabilisation, expected to add ~₹165 Cr annual revenue in FY27.
- **Rajasthan CTU Solar Project — Bikaner Solar Park (Disclosure, 26 May 2026).**
  - 351.4 MWp commissioned by Clean Max Celestial Pvt. Ltd. in Bikaner Solar Park on 26 May 2026.
  - Part of the first CTU-connected portfolio serving large tech / Data & AI customers via EAPA/VPPA contracts.
- **Record Q1 FY27 commissioning — ~530 MW (Press Release, 7 Jul 2026).**
  - ~530 MW commissioned in Q1 FY27 (highest ever quarterly): 403 MW RE Power Sales (~350 MWp solar, 53 MW wind) + 126 MWp RE Services (EPC + O&M; assets owned by C&I customers).
  - Operational portfolio ~3.6 GW → ~4.2 GW by 30 Jun 2026: ~3.5 GW RE Power Sales + ~0.7 GW RE Services.
  - Major Q1 additions by state: Gujarat ~170 MW, Karnataka ~160 MW, Maharashtra ~110 MW, plus Haryana & Chhattisgarh.
  - As of 30 Jun 2026, trailing-12-month commissioning (Power Sales) ~1.7–2.0 GW.
