### 3B Industry and market intelligence
- Transformer industry runs on dispatch flow; delivery is clearance-gated: "we have to get the clearance from the customers before dispatching" (Tr. p10, L396-398).
- LME-linked pricing; delivery within 3 months is fixed price; 6-12 months carry 100% IEEMA price variation (Tr. p8, L326-332). Utility orders run 8-9 months to one year (L319-320). Private orders above Rs 1 Cr get a price variation clause (L313-314).
- Raw material handling: 15% advance to hedge fixed prices for 3 months; oil stocked 1-1.5 months; copper and CRGO booked about 1 month (Tr. p11, L451-454).
- Transformer oil availability was a procurement constraint in FY26 (Tr. p10, L400-402).
- Welders are scarce; robotic welding under evaluation after a China visit three months earlier (Tr. p13, L511-514).
- 20-30% automation possible in the industry (Tr. p13, L522).
- Customers require 2-3 quotations before award (Tr. p9, L357-358).
- No industry growth rate, no capacity-addition figure, no import or export figure was cited. Absence noted.
