## FORENSIC NARRATIVE (supporting the findings)

**F8 — the headline is engineered around a PAT decline.** The release headlines PBT
("Pre-tax profits marginally higher at ₹18 Crs", line 67) and never states Net Profit YoY.
Net Profit fell 46.6% (₹20.6cr -> ₹11.0cr, line 103) while PBT rose 6.5% (line 102). The
mechanism: in Q1FY26 PAT (20.6) EXCEEDED PBT (17.0), and in FY26 PAT (100.8) EXCEEDED PBT
(80.6) — i.e. a large positive item BELOW the PBT line (tax credit and/or JV/associate
share) flattered prior-period PAT. In Q1FY27 that reverses: implied ETR is (18.1−11.0)/18.1
= 39.2%, well above statutory 25.17%. Whether the drag is tax normalisation or JV losses
turning negative cannot be separated from this document — lean bear, generate an A4 question.
This directly confirms the AVOID thesis note "tax-flattered PAT; JV income turned negative."

**F16 — selective framing.** "Sales up 10% YoY to ₹484 Crs" (pre-sales/bookings, 0.85 msf)
is the headline growth number; the actual recognised "Total revenues" grew only 4% to ₹271cr
(line 115). Margins are given only in absolute Rs (gross ₹56cr, EBITDA ₹42cr) — computed as
% they are 20.7% and 15.5%, both under the operator's re-entry thresholds (28% / 16%). The
"record-high first-quarter sales" superlative (line 74) is a first-quarter-only, sales-value-
only frame that sidesteps margin and PAT.

**F16-2 — run-rate vs guidance.** Against FY27 guidance, Q1 is ~14-18% across every metric
(sales ~14%, collections ~17%, handovers ~18%, volume ~16%), yet the release asserts "Full
year outlook remains intact... on-track" (line 68) and leans entirely on "strong H2 launch
line-up." The gap between a sub-quarter-pace start and an unchanged full-year target is the
single largest forward risk in the document.

**F7 — no legal cover.** Eight forward statements (ledger §5), including explicit guidance
language ("expects", "targeted during H2FY27", "remains confident of sustaining growth
momentum"), appear with no safe-harbour disclaimer and no hedge phrase anywhere. Asymmetric
optimism with no downside qualifier.
