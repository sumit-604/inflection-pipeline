Management Commentary (Q2 & H1FY27)

Commenting on the performance, Mr. Sudhir Rai – Chairman & Managing Director said:
“Kross Limited has continued its strong momentum in Q2 FY27, with revenue from operations at ₹235.1 crore, year-on-year growth of 79.6%. EBITDA was ₹30.6 crore, while
profit after tax stood at ₹19.7 crore. For H1 FY27, revenue stood at ₹419.4 crore, EBITDA at ₹53.3 crore and PAT at ₹33.2 crore. Capacity expansion investments are beginning to
support growth, with utilisation expected to improve in the coming quarters.

Segmental Highlights
M&HCV Segment: Momentum continued with robust OEM demand. Q3 and Q4 volumes expected to be stronger than Q2 and historically also H2 is always 10-15% up than
H1. Price revisions with most OEMs and Tier-1 customers concluded, supporting profitability despite input-cost pressures.
Trailer Segment: Q2 volumes exceeded Q4 FY26 levels. Commercial production of our extruded axle beams has commenced and capacity utilization is currently expected to
deliver cost benefits going forward.
Tractor & Agri Segment: Demand remained consistent, supported by relatively low inventory levels of tractors in the market. Momentum expected to continue into Q3;
contribution of this segment set to rise over the coming years.
Exports: Exports now contribute approximately 8% of our total component business. We remain committed to building a broader customer base and increasing the
contribution of exports to our overall business.

Capacity Expansion & Strategic Initiatives:
• Tipping Jacks: Supplies progressing in line with plan; further volume scale-up expected in H2 FY27.
• Axle beam extrusion plant: Commercial production has commenced; current utilisation approximately 60–65%.
• Seamless tube facility: mills installed, production trials targeted for February–March 2027.
• Forging capacity Expansion initiatives on track to support higher volumes and improved cost competitiveness.
• High-pressure moulding line (Foundry) Progressing as planned to double existing casting capacity.
• Axle Shaft production by Robotic Forging facility: Strategic increase in shaft manufacturing capacity & enhanced investment in Robotic Automation for forging operations

   and specialized machining facilities
• Cold Drawn Tubes: Forward Integration into manufacturing of Cold Drawn Tubes

Outlook
With healthy order books, sustained demand in the trailer and tractor segments, and multiple capacity expansion initiatives nearing completion, Kross is well positioned to
deliver healthy growth in FY27. Our focus remains on profitable growth, operational efficiency, customer relationships and disciplined execution.

Axle Beam Extrusion Plant Update

 Pioneering shift from fabricated to extruded technology

 Key Highlights

• Commercial production of extruded axle beams has commenced
• Current axle beam capacity stands at 7,500 units per month
• This initiative is expected to deliver cost efficiencies and further strengthen manufacturing capabilities.
• The Company is actively engaging distribution partners to showcase the technological advantages of its

   extruded axle beams.
• After-sales service remains a priority, supported by a network of ~120 people.
• Clear Competitive Edge: Lower material cost (no welding), Lighter weight and Superior technical

   performance resulting in improved tyre life
• Export Potential: Attractive opportunity in Europe and the US markets

This Extruded Technology adoption is expected to drive market share gains, improve
profitability, and open new export revenue streams
