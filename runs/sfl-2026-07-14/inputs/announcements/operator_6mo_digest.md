## Jan 2026 — Kurlon Composite Scheme effective
NCLT-approved Composite Scheme (Kurlon group entities -> Kurlon Enterprise
Ltd -> Sheela Foam) effective; certified NCLT order filed with RoC Mumbai
(Form INC-28) on 15 Jan 2026. Record date 30 Jan 2026, 52 SFL shares per 100
Kurlon shares. Legally completes the restructuring; enables full operational
consolidation of Kurlon.

## Q3 FY26 (to Dec 2025; Feb 2026 call)
- Kurlon merger "complete in all respects," all RoC filings done.
- Combined India (SFL+Kurlon) ~10% core EBITDA for 9M FY26, "in line with
  SFL pre-acquisition margins"; ~80% of targeted Rs 250 Cr Kurlon synergies
  realized, remainder tied to a new comfort-layer material/machinery (install
  by mid-2026).
- Retail: ~600 net new showrooms 9M (on track ~700 FY26); combined EBO ~3,800
  (~2,900 Sleepwell, ~900 Kurlon) + ~2,500 category-exclusive dealers. COCO
  stores 24 operating (from 40-50 at Kurlon acquisition), all EBITDA positive.
- E-commerce 9M: ~53% YoY, ~Rs 180 Cr; brand.com >50% YoY.
- U2O (unorganized-to-organized, ex "Small Town India"): 8,000+ dealers,
  5,000+ towns, 24 states; 9M turnover Rs 75 Cr ~100% YoY, run-rate ~Rs 120 Cr.
- GCC: exclusive stores across Emirates; commenced outsourced local UAE
  manufacturing for Sheela+Kurlon; e-com via Amazon, noon, own site.
- Joyce (Australia) Q3 EBITDA ~12.5% (9M ~10%); Interplasp (Spain) Q3 ~11.9%
  (9M ~9.7%) on waste reduction, sourcing, cost control.
- Furlenco raised Rs 125 Cr equity (SFL Rs 30 Cr, rest new investors) at Rs
  1,050 Cr valuation; 9M annualized run-rate ~Rs 400 Cr, 9M PAT Rs 18 Cr,
  cash profit Rs 68 Cr; store-in-store roll-out in Sleepwell/Kurlon outlets.
- Solar 500 kWp Jabalpur operational (+1,000 kWp planned Erode/Talwada/
  Nandigram); 30 KLD STP Nandigram.
- Non-core real estate (Jhagadia land, some Bengaluru) sold ~Rs 100-125 Cr,
  used to repay debt.

## ACCOUNTING CHANGE (flag for notes / accounting quality / normalized EPS)
- Effective 1 Jan 2026 India shifted WDV -> Straight Line Method; key plant &
  machinery useful life revised 20 -> 40 years (technical assessment; a 22yr
  foaming line still at full capacity). Boosts FY26 PBT by ~Rs 14 Cr (~8% of
  FY26 PBT Rs 182 Cr). Also implies capex is long-lived: current capacity
  can support ~2-2.5x present volume on debottlenecking/efficiency capex only.

## Capex / capacity
- FY26 consolidated capex ~Rs 235 Cr (incl CWIP): debottlenecking, efficiency
  (compression machines, logistics), maintenance (India/Australia/Spain).
- FY27 planned capex Rs 125-150 Cr: maintenance + debottlenecking at
  high-utilization plants + marketing/showroom capex.
- Logistics: 32-ft trucks ~33% (target ~35%), EV SCVs, CNG ~7% North (target
  ~40% group), milk runs.

## Raw material / pricing
- TDI & polyol extreme volatility (Rs 40-50/kg swings multiple times/month:
  Middle East conflict, GNFC shutdowns). Response: multi-source imports
  (China/Korea/Japan) + new strategic supplier. Price hikes taken (B2C
  "decent," industrial B2B fastest pass-through). Management expects EBITDA
  margin sustained/improving on volume + mix, not RM tailwinds.

## Capital allocation / balance sheet
- First-ever dividend (20% of face value) recommended FY26.
- Net debt down Rs 156 Cr FY26; Indian debt (~Rs 300 Cr) repayable in
  ~1-1.5 years if not deployed to growth.
