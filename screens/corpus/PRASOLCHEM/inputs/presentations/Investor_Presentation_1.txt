Chairman’s Message
Mr. Nishith Shah,
Chairman & Whole Time Director
“We delivered a strong performance for the second consecutive quarter, clocking our highest ever Q2 
revenue of Rs. 413 crores, taking our H1 FY27 revenue to Rs. 846 crores. This represents a YoY growth of 
39% for the quarter and 37% for the first half. The growth was led by better realisations and an improving 
product mix. Raw material prices continued to rise in Q2 and in response, we have been progressively 
increasing product prices and engaging with customers on a regular basis.
Demand across our Acetone-based and Phosphorus-based products remained healthy despite external 
challenges. Higher freight costs and limited vessel availability amid the Middle East crisis led to delays in 
some export shipments. We saw healthy contributions from a wide range of products, reflecting the 
strength of the diversified business we have built over the decades. During the quarter, we also further 
diversified our raw material sourcing base to strengthen supply chain resilience.
Our business is built on deep chemistry, supported by extensive forward and backward integration and a 
diversified presence across multiple applications. Innovation and strong R&D capabilities remain at the 
core of our business. Our capex programme is progressing as planned, and our new product pipeline 
remains robust. We remain on track to deliver our FY27 guidance of revenue of Rs.1,550–1,650 crores and 
EBITDA of Rs. 240–250 crores, excluding the impact of price fluctuations arising from geopolitical-led 
supply chain disruptions and foreign exchange gains or losses, if any.”
