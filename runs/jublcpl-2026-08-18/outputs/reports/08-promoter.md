## SECTION 2: LEGAL & REGULATORY RECORD

### 2A. SEBI actions
✅ **VERIFIED — SEBI adjudication order dated 31-Jan-2018** against (1) Jubilant Life Sciences Ltd,
(2) Jubilant Stock Holding Pvt Ltd, (3) Mr Shyam Sunder Bhartia, (4) Mr Hari Shankar Bhartia, and
(5) Mr Amit Arora (SVP, FP&A), for violation of the PIT (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations
— trading in Jubilant Life Sciences shares while in possession of unpublished price-sensitive
information, relating to conduct in 2013-2014; Jubilant Life Sciences itself was also fined for
delayed disclosure to exchanges under the erstwhile Listing Agreement (Clause 36) / Section 21 SCRA.
Penalty: Rs 10 lakh each on the five noticees (total commonly reported as Rs 50 lakh). All parties
appealed to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) on 24-Apr-2018. **SAT order dated 07-Nov-2019**:
penalty on Jubilant Life Sciences Ltd (as a company, for the listing-agreement disclosure delay)
reduced from Rs 10 lakh to Rs 5 lakh; Jubilant Stock Holding Pvt Ltd's appeal was dismissed (penalty
upheld). Sources: SEBI enforcement order page (title/existence confirmed via search, direct PDF
fetch blocked by network egress proxy — see Search Log), corroborated by Business Standard
(31-Jan-2018) and Medical Dialogues coverage of the reduced penalty.
This is the single most material adverse finding in this check: it directly names the two founding
promoters — Shyam Sunder Bhartia and Hari Shankar Bhartia — the same individuals who remain
Chairman and Co-Chairman/MD of the wider Jubilant Bhartia Group of which JACPL is part, though the
order itself concerns a different listed entity (the pre-split Jubilant Life Sciences, predecessor
of Jubilant Pharmova/Jubilant Ingrevia), not JACPL. No SEBI order specifically against JACPL, its
current board (Priyavrat Bhartia, Shamit Bhartia, Mohandeep Singh, or the three independent
directors), or its RTA/auditor was found in searches covering 2018-2026. No market ban, no SAT
finding of fraud/manipulation (the finding was a PIT-regulation/disclosure-timing breach, settled via
adjudication and reduced on appeal, not admission-free consent order).

Loans or advances... were provided to firms/companies in which Directors are interested during FY
2025-26" (p.74). All RPTs route through Audit Committee review/approval per policy. **Governance
design flag**: the **Finance Committee — which holds delegated authority to borrow up to Rs 1,250

Bhartia (Chairman), Shamit Bhartia (Member), and Mohandeep Singh (Member); zero independent
directors** (AR Corporate Governance Report p.67, ✅ VERIFIED). This is a structural conflict-of-
interest exposure (promoter-controlled borrowing authority with no independent check at the
committee level) even though no misuse was evidenced in FY26 RPT figures.

### 3B. Capital allocation behaviour
No dividend recommended for FY26 despite consolidated PAT of Rs 1,278.66mn, retained earnings
carried forward of Rs 2,879.28mn, and a strong balance sheet (Debt/Equity 0.06, net debt Rs 230.51mn,
interest coverage 23.91x — AR Financial Highlights p.6-7, Note 37 p.148). Cash retention ahead of the
pending Agri-division demerger and ongoing capex (Rs 405mn FY26 capex) is a plausible explanation,
but zero-dividend-despite-profit is recorded as a CAUTION per the standard checklist, not excused.
No preferential allotments/warrant lapses found; ESOP schemes (2013, 2018) are functioning normally
(40,792 + 44,600 shares allotted on option exercise in FY26).
**KMP remuneration**: total KMP remuneration (short-term + long-term benefits) rose from Rs 59.20mn
(FY25) to **Rs 96.86mn (FY26), +63.6% YoY** (AR Note 40, p.152-154, ✅ VERIFIED — this matches and
confirms the figure flagged upstream at B03), against an average employee (ex-managerial) increase of
only 9.80% and a median employee salary of Rs 6.23,995. The CEO/WTD's remuneration-to-median ratio is
99.91x (Annexure 4, Section 197 disclosure, p.55); footnoted as partly explained by Mr Mohandeep
Singh's and Mr Hariom Pandey's appointments being effective for only part of the prior year
(comparability distortion, not pure like-for-like growth) — a real mitigant, but the absolute ratio
remains a CAUTION-level signal for a company of this size. Separately, AR's table of special
resolutions from the last three AGMs records, at the **15th AGM**, a **"Waiver of excess
remuneration paid to Mr Manu Ahuja, CEO & Whole-time Director"** (p.70) — i.e., a prior CEO's pay
exceeded Schedule V limits and required retrospective shareholder ratification. This is an adverse
governance data point on managerial-remuneration discipline predating the current CEO.
