## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** The filings do not say. The company reports one
segment, "IT and Software development business" (q-2026-06.pdf, p.5, note 3). The auditor lists
software development, BPO and IT infrastructure services (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.100). The
annual report and AGM deck sell four "growth engines": Flowzo CRM, SIRU pharmacy network, AI and
data, and fintech plus semiconductor IP (AGM deck, p.47). No document names a customer, a
product's revenue, a contract or a country. The directors' report says the company earned Rs 19.5
crore in foreign exchange (AR FY26, p.58). The related-party note in the same report shows export
earnings of 0 (AR FY26, p.121).

**What is changing?** The reported numbers. Revenue went from Rs 5.45 crore in FY25 to Rs 19.66 crore
in FY26. Profit before tax went from Rs 1.00 crore to Rs 14.32 crore, a 73% pre-tax margin
(q-2026-03.pdf, p.5). Q4 FY26 alone booked Rs 14.03 crore of revenue at an 88% pre-tax margin.

**Why now?** A new controlling shareholder recapitalised the company. A rights issue under a Letter
of Offer dated 7-Jan-2025 took the promoter, Dr Vasudevan Mahalingam, from 35.73% to 62.26%
(market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record; AR FY25, p.155). The board then approved a Rs 200
crore QIP, a USD 100 million FCCB route and a Rs 500 crore investment limit (postal ballot notice,
pp.3, 6, 21).

**What must be true?** The receivables must turn into cash. They have not. Trade receivables
of Rs 18.40 crore at March 2025 were 3.4 times FY25 revenue (q-2026-03.pdf, pp.5-6). Operating cash
flow has been negative in each year FY22 to FY26 (screener/Financials.txt, service record). The filed figures are negative Rs 2.08 crore in FY25 and negative Rs 2.60
crore in FY26 (q-2026-03.pdf, p.7), in a year of Rs 14.32 crore profit before tax.

**What breaks it?** The same receivables. The auditor says profit is "overstated" by the Rs 10.88
crore it holds as long overdue (q-2026-03.pdf, p.2). A further Rs 20 crore of receivables became
an "advance for investment in foreign company" (q-2026-03.pdf, p.5, note f).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reported segment, sale of services, Rs 19.66 crore (AR FY26, p.120). Cost base for FY26: employee
cost Rs 3.85 crore, other expenses Rs 0.92 crore, depreciation Rs 0.56 crore (q-2026-03.pdf, p.5).
Permanent employees: 45 (AR FY26, p.56). The highlights page claims "100+ Employees" and "5000+
Customers" (AR FY26, p.7). The FY25 report priced its CRM at "$10 per month for up to 5 users"
(AR FY25, p.24). At that price Rs 19.66 crore is not plausible from 5,000 small customers.

Revenue per permanent employee is Rs 44 lakh against a median pay of Rs 8.31 lakh (AR FY26, p.56).
An IT services firm does not earn a 73% pre-tax margin. A licence or IP sale can. No document says
which this is.

**Archetype: indeterminate.** The filed segment is IT services (outsourcing partner). The narrative
claims platform/network. Neither has a KPI in the corpus.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent on the evidence. No product revenue, user count, retention figure or price is disclosed for
FY26. The FY25 report claimed "over 1000 merchants" and 95% retention for dSpeedUp (AR FY25, p.25).
The FY26 report drops dSpeedUp, dRyZe and dBotMinds and leads with Flowzo and SIRU (AGM deck,
pp.47-49). Ryuklabs presented SIRU at the AGM as its technology partner (AGM deck, p.65).
Who owns SIRU: NOT FOUND. The deck disclaims manufacturing: "We are not presenting ourselves as a
semiconductor manufacturer" (AGM deck, p.41). A moat cannot be assessed when the product line
changes names in a year and no product reports a number.

## 4. The promoters

Dr Vasudevan Mahalingam, MD and CEO, on the board since 12-Aug-2016, M.Pharm and Ph.D.
(AR FY25, p.18). His spouse, Dr Manimala Vasudevan, is Whole-time Director and CFO (AR FY26,
pp.12, 77). He holds 3,84,96,620 shares, 62.26% (AR FY26, p.118). He draws no pay. The FY25
report says remuneration is "voluntarily deferred and may be claimed in the future" (AR FY25, p.18).

**The promoter has not paid most of his call money.** At the AGM cut-off of 22-Sep-2026 he held
1,53,22,972 fully paid and 2,31,73,648 partly paid shares (AGM voting result, pp.2-3). At Rs 7.50 a
share the unpaid call is Rs 17.38 crore. He holds 86% of all partly paid shares outstanding. The
board extended the call three times (filings of 2-Mar, 28-May and 23-Sep-2026). Two of the three
cite requests from shareholders.

Related party: a loan from the MD of Rs 1.35 crore "to fund day-to-day operations" (AR FY26, pp.51,
119). At March 2024 that loan was Rs 8.31 crore (AR FY25, p.157). Rs 8.25 crore of rights money
went to "Repayment of Promoter Loan" (AR FY25, p.154). [INFERENCE] The promoter's application money
was largely his own loan, set off. Pledge: none, per the Reg 31(4) declaration (filing 28-Mar-2026,
p.2). Related-party revenue: none disclosed. Note 3 to the accounts reads "Includes dues from
companies where directors" and stops there (AR FY26, p.117). Whether any receivable is from a
director-linked company: NOT FOUND.

**Rights terms (asked for).** 4,63,71,318 partly paid shares at Rs 10, face value Rs 10: Rs 2.50 on
application, Rs 7.50 first and final call (Monitoring Committee report, 10-Mar-2026, pp.1, 3). Size

## 8. Proof check

The trigger is "transformation". The proof point would be cash from customers. It is absent. Q4 FY26
and Q1 FY27 printed profit. Receivables absorbed both quarters. The proof gate has not fired.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Qualified audit opinion, recurring.** Rs 10.88 crore receivables unprovided, "profit is
  overstated"; Rs 3.80 crore tax asset unreconciled; Rs 3.11 crore subsidiary unimpaired; no lease
  accounting (AR FY26, p.99). Frequency: "Reoccurs" (q-2026-03.pdf, p.8).
- **Emphasis of matter.** Rs 20 crore advance for investment without "sufficient appropriate audit
  evidence" of recoverability (AR FY26, p.99). Counterparty: iAgent Corporation (AR FY26, p.118).
  CARO clause iii says no investments or loans were made in the year (AR FY26, p.108).
- **Subsidiary is a shell.** Aspire Communications: total assets Rs 1,11,785, turnover nil (AR FY26,
  p.60). Management calls it a semiconductor and IP investment (AR FY26, p.55).
- **Six material weaknesses in internal control**, including no internal finance team (AR FY26,
  p.106). No internal audit system (p.110). Audit trail not enabled (p.104).
- **Earlier reviews:** no fixed asset register; opening balances that differ from audited figures
  (q-2025-09.pdf, p.4); income not reconciled with GST returns (q-2025-12.pdf, p.3).
- **Disputed income tax Rs 16.06 crore** across 16 demands (AR FY26, p.109). No contingent
  liability note exists in FY26. FY25 said "no contingent liabilities" (AR FY25, p.155).
- **TDS unpaid**, to be paid with interest (q-2026-06.pdf, p.5, note 8).
- **Drafting residue in a filed report.** The FY26 directors' report contains an editor's note:
  "Please confirm the median base (monthly vs annual) before publishing" (AR FY26, p.56).
- **Promoted numbers do not tie.** Deck and report show PAT Rs 10.09 crore; audited PAT is Rs 10.63
  crore (AGM deck, p.34; AR FY26, p.7; q-2026-03.pdf, p.5). The Q1 FY27 filing restates Q4 FY26 EPS
  as 1.00; the audited figure is 1.49 (q-2026-06.pdf, p.5; q-2026-03.pdf, p.5).
- **Exchange price queries** on 9-Oct-2025 and 19-Aug-2026. Range Rs 10.2 to Rs 44.8 (market data,
  service record).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** No cash, no customer, no product metric.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** Uncollected receivables since FY22, recurring qualifications, control
  weaknesses, and a promoter in arrears on his own call. These are not costs of a climb.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Rs 271 crore market cap (screener.in service record, 2026-10-18) is
  18.5 times trailing profit and 6.1 times net worth of Rs 44.38 crore (q-2026-03.pdf, p.6). The
  market already pays for the reported profit as if it were cash.

**Posture: AVOID.** Gap closed plus structural ugliness is the dominating overlay.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.** Not a quality judgement on intent. The filed evidence cannot separate a real export
business from booked revenue, and the auditor says so.

**Load-bearing facts if anyone reopens it**
1. Trade receivables and operating cash flow at 30-Sep-2026 (Q2 FY27 results).
2. Who iAgent Corporation is, and whether shares were allotted for the Rs 20 crore.
3. Whether the promoter paid Rs 17.38 crore of call money by 9-Oct-2026, or his shares were forfeited.
4. Named customers and the source of Rs 19.5 crore of claimed foreign exchange earnings.

**What would change the view**
- A half-year with collections at or above revenue and the receivable balance falling.
- An unqualified FY27 audit opinion.
- The promoter paying his call in full.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set.
