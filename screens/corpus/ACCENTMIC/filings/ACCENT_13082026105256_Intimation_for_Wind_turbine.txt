Dear Sir/Madam, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read with Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI (Listing Obligations 
and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we wish to inform the Exchange(s) that the 
Board/Management of the Company has approved proposed capital expenditure towards 
installation of a 4.45 MW Wind Turbine Generator (WTG) on a turnkey basis to be met out 
of internal accruals/borrowings. 
In this regard, a Purchase Order has been placed on 12th August, 2026 with Inox Wind 
Limited for the said turnkey installation. 
Rationale for the proposed capital expenditure: 
The proposed investment has been undertaken by the management after careful evaluation of 
the following: 
1. The present electricity consumption and associated cost being incurred at the 
Company's Plant-I and Plant-II. 
2. A precise assessment of the anticipated energy requirement of Phase-I of Plant-III, 
upon its commercialisation. 
3. The objective of offsetting and reducing the Company's overall power/energy bill 
through captive consumption of electricity units generated from the proposed wind 
energy asset. 
Expected Benefit: 
Pursuant to installation and commissioning of the said Wind Turbine, the Company expects 
substantial reduction in its month-on-month factory electricity bill which is expected to have a
