## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES (Note 1, standalone pp.110-113; consolidated pp.149-152)

- Basis of preparation: historical cost except certain financial instruments and defined-benefit plan assets
  at fair value; operating cycle assumed 12 months. Standard, no deviation. 🟢
- Depreciation: WDV method per Schedule II useful lives, **except Effluent Treatment Plant depreciated over
  8 years** (Note 1(d), p.111 standalone / Note 1(e) p.150 consolidated) — a disclosed deviation, and a
  faster (more conservative) write-off than a typical Schedule II plant & machinery life, not a red flag.
  🟢
- Revenue recognition (Note 1(e), p.111 standalone / Note 1(f) p.150 consolidated): policy is written in
  pre-Ind AS 115 "risks and rewards transferred" language ("Company retains no effective control... no
  significant uncertainty exists regarding consideration") rather than the Ind AS 115 five-step
  control/performance-obligation framework that has applied since FY2018-19. Stale policy wording; low
  materiality for a single point-in-time-sale manufacturer but a disclosure-quality miss. 🟡
- Ind AS 116 (leases): a Right-of-Use asset and lease liability are recognised for the **first time this
  year** (Note 2(iii), p.115/154: opening Rs Nil, addition Rs 32.64 lakh, depreciation Rs 2.11 lakh, closing
  Rs 30.53 lakh; Notes 18/25 pp.120-121 standalone show non-current lease liability Rs 17.93 lakh and
  current Rs 11.50 lakh). **No discount rate for the lease is disclosed anywhere in the Notes** (Ind AS 116
  para 53(i) expects this). NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT. 🟡
- ECL / impairment of financial assets: policy note describes the Ind AS 109 ECL model (Note 1(h)(viii),
  p.112) but no ECL matrix, no expected-loss rate, and no ageing-linked provisioning % is disclosed anywhere
  in Notes 9/35.17. Trade receivables Provision for Doubtful Debts is shown as "-" (nil) in both FY26 and
  FY25 (Note 9, p.117/156) despite Note 34 (Other Expenses, p.124 standalone) showing a P&L charge of
  **"Provision for Doubtful Debt" Rs 46.22 lakh in FY25** that cannot be traced to any balance sheet
  provision movement disclosed in the Notes (see also section 12 below — Note 14's insurance-claim
  provision of Rs 66.22 lakh is unchanged in both years, so it is not the source). Unreconciled. 🔴
- No impairment testing / goodwill note — no goodwill or intangible assets under development on the books
  (Note 35.23(b)); impairment policy (Note 1(j)) is boilerplate with no CGU testing disclosed this year.
  NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT (not applicable). 🟢
- Capitalisation threshold: not explicitly quantified anywhere in Notes. NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT.
- First-time adoption: none flagged other than Ind AS 116 lease recognition noted above.
- Borrowing cost policy (Note 1(l), p.113 standalone / Note 1(m) p.152 consolidated): standard — directly
  attributable borrowing cost on qualifying assets is capitalised. Given CWIP roughly doubled this year
