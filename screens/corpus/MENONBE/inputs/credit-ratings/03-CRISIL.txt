 Detailed r ationale 

 CRISIL Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank   facility   of   Menon Bearings Ltd (MBL) to   ‘ Stable ’   from ‘ Positive ’  while   reaffirm ed the  rating at  ' CRISIL BBB+' . The short term rating has been reaffirmed at ' CRISIL A2 ' . 

 The outlook revision reflects   the less-than- expected revenue of the company , primarily because of muted demand from the tractor segment . While r evenue   grew 11 %   to Rs 218 crore   in   fiscal 202 3 ,   driven by higher realisations , it fell to Rs 55. 53 crore in the first quarter of fiscal 2024 from Rs 62.9 0 crore in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal .   

 Operating margin   remain s   healthy   above   2 0 % . The working capital cycle   is managed efficiently, as indicated b y   gross current assets of 14 0 -168 days   for the three fiscals through 202 3 . Also,   reliance on debt remains limited. The financial risk profile should remain healthy despite debt-funded capital expenditure ( capex ) planned over the medium term. 

 The company entered new product line s in the auto motive (auto) segment , such as brake lining and brake shoes by incorporating a wholly owned subsidiary , Menon Brakes Pvt Ltd , in Dec ember 2022. The investment stood at around Rs 8.25 cr ore as of March 2023 , and commerci al production began in April 2023. MBL plans capex of Rs 30 crore in fiscal 2024 and Rs 10 crore in fiscal 2025 for new products in the a luminum castings, thrust washers and engine bearing segments. These investments will be funded partly through debt . 

 The ratings   continue to reflect   the   established market position and clientele   of MBL and   its   healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by   its modest   scale of operations and   revenue concentration   in the   auto sector. 

 Analytical A ppr oach : Standalone 

 Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description 

 Strengths : 

 Established market position and clientele:   Presence of around three decades in the bearings industry has   enabled   MBL to establish i ts position across diverse markets and   industries. The company has built healthy relationships   with key clients, including John Deere, Tata Motors Ltd (‘CRISIL AA/Stable/CRISIL A1+’ ) and   International Tractors Ltd and Brakes India Ltd (‘CRISIL AA+/Stable/CRISIL A1+’),   because of its   sound product and service quality. 

 Healthy financial risk profile:   Capital structure   was comfortable, as reflected in networth of over Rs 133 crore,   and low   gearing and   total outside liabilities   to tangible networth ratio of   0. 1 1 time and   0. 36 time,   respectively,   as on   March 31, 202 3 . Debt protection metrics   were   strong,   as indicated by   interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios   of 1 8.36   times   and   2.01   time, respectively,   in   fiscal 202 3 . 

 Weakness es : 

 Modest, but i ncreas ing , scale of operations: Despite growth in the recent past, revenue remained mode s t at Rs 218 crore in fiscal 202 3 . Scale up of operations will be a key monitorable. 

 Revenue concentration in the   auto   industry:   Of the total   revenue, 80-90%   comes from   the   auto   sector and the   remaining from the oil and gas, electrical and engineering industries.   Within the   auto   segment, MBL derives   bulk of its   revenue from the commercial vehicle and tractor segments,   which are inherently cyclical.   

 Liquidity: Adequate 

 Cash   accrual is expected over Rs 2 6 crore   per annum   over the medium term, after factoring dividend payout , and will comfortably cover   yearly term debt obligation of Rs 4-6 crore. Bank   limit utilisation averaged 23 % over the 12 months   through   Sep tember 202 3 . Cash and equivalent   stood at   Rs 13.16 crore as on March 31, 202 3 . 

 Outlook : Stable 

 CRISIL Ratings believes MBL will continue   to scale up operations due to healthy demand from end - user industr ies. The company will   benefit from its strong market position and established clientele and sustain its healthy financial risk profile .  

 Rating Sensitivity Factors 

 Upward factors: 

 R evenue growth of more than   25%   and   stable   operating margin leading to higher net cash accrual 

 Sustenance of   healthy   financial risk profile   and   improvement in   the   working capital cycle 

 Downward factors: 

 Decline   in   revenue or profitability resulting in net cash accrual below Rs 1 5 crore 

 Increase in working capital requirement;   large r-than-expected debt-funded capex or acquisition;   or   sizeable   dividend payout;   weakening the financial risk profile   and   liquidity 

 About the C ompany 

 Incorporated in 1991   as a part of the Kolhapur - based Menon group   and promoted by Mr Ram Menon and   his   family   members, MBL manufactures auto   components such a s bearings, bushes, thrust washers and bi-metal strips at its plant in Kolhapur. It also manufactures aluminum die-cast products.
