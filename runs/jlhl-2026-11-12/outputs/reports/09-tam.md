## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

### 1A Boundaries
- Product: in-patient (IPD) and out-patient (OPD) services of multi-specialty tertiary and quaternary hospitals (IPD 78.3%, OPD 17.5% of income, B04). Includes cardiac, neuro, onco, transplant, robotic programmes (RHP p.179 text; B04 moats).
- Excluded: pharmacy retail (2.6%), cafeteria (0.9%), hotel (0.7%), other (0.1%) = 4.2% of income (B04); diagnostics-only and primary or secondary care; medical tourism as a separate pool.
- Geography: three regions. Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR: Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli, Mira-Bhayandar, BKC), Pune Metropolitan Region (Baner now, Pune South CY28), Indore. Pipeline: Pune South 500 beds CY28, Mira Road 300 beds CY29, BKC 400 beds CY31 (Pres Q2 FY27 p.11).
- Customer: self-pay about 55%, insurance about 44%, government schemes about 1% (Pres Q2 FY27 p.7 values 54.8 / 44.2 / 1.0; legend order prints scrambled, labels matched to AR26 p.20 by B04). Government-scheme patients are effectively outside JLHL's model.
- Channel: owned hospitals, doctor-referral and walk-in. No franchise or managed-bed model.
- Price segment: ARPOB Rs 71,800 per occupied census bed per day, H1 FY27 (Pres Q2 p.7). This is a premium chain price point, far above the regional market average (see Method 2).
- Currency and time basis: FY27 is the anchor year because CRISIL's projection ends there.
