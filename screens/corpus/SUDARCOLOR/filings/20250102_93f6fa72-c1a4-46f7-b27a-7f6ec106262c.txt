Subject: Intimation regarding Fire Incident occurred at the Company Plant located in Roha, 
Maharashtra under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) 
Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI Listing Regulations”). 
Dear Sir, 
Pursuant to Regulation 30 read along with Para-B, Part-A of Schedule III of the SEBI Listing 
Regulations, it is hereby informed that on Wednesday, January 1, 2025, at approximately 19:55 HRS, 
fire broke out at the Company’s Plant, located at Plot No. 113/114, MIDC Dhatav, Near Police Station,  
Kolad–Roha Road , A.V. Roha A.V. P. O. - Dhatav, Taluka – Roha ,Dist.-Raigad 402 116, India. The 
warehouse is used for storage of pigment in process goods used for final manufacturing. There have 
been no injuries or casualties. 
The team at our Factory with support from the neighbouring industries, got the situation under control 
around 20:45 hrs. 
The Company is currently investigating the root cause of the incident and assessing the extent of 
damage. We will provide an update on our findings in due course. 
 
Additionally, we would like to inform you that the company has adequate insurance coverage. The claim 
process with the insurance company has been initiated, and we have requested a surveyor to assess the 
situation. The Company is committed to ensuring the highest safety standards. 
 
Additional details as per SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/123 dated July 13,

is mentioned in Annexure-A. 
This is for your information and record.
