## Monitorables and triggers

1. Watch the non ibuprofen share of pharma revenue each quarter in the investor presentation and call. It was 43% in Q1 FY27. A fall back below 37%, the FY26 level, breaks the mix shift engine that carries the whole transition.
2. Watch paracetamol utilisation in the Q2 and Q3 FY27 releases. It must print above 60%, and management must say which of 65% or 55% was true for Q1 FY27. A print still near 55% by Q3 FY27 says the new unit fills slowly and IOL may be selling on price to fill it.
3. Watch debtor days and receivables growth on the 30-Sep-2026 half year balance sheet. Above 95 days with receivables outgrowing revenue marks the cash squeeze as structural and tests the internally funded capex claim.
4. Watch for any term loan sanction in BSE filings and for capital commitments in the next AR (₹69.35 Cr in FY26). A loan appearing, or FCF turning negative while commitments rise, breaks the internally funded claim.
5. Watch for first commercial revenue from the tablet plant and the tolling unit in Q3 FY27, and for the customers to be named in a Reg 30 filing. A slip past Q3 FY27, or contracts that stay "being finalised", removes the two new legs of the thesis.
6. Watch purchases from promoter group entities in the next AR Note 40 and quarterly RPT disclosures. Growth beyond 8.8% of material consumed, or another unexplained shareholder table change, tests whether the promoter consolidation serves minority holders.
7. Watch segment margins in the Q2 FY27 results: pharma above 12% and chemicals above 2.6%, with no inventory gain help. Read them against the paracetamol spot index and the acetyl spread index. Margin that tracks the spread and not the mix says the gain is price, not transition.
8. Obtain the 30-Jun-2026 CARE rationale and read its working capital and liquidity paragraphs. It is the missing evidence that settles FLAG-CASH and lifts the freshness cap.
