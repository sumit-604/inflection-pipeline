## PLAIN-LANGUAGE BRIEF (standing deliverable)

### 1. Summary narrative

Fujiyama reported its first quarter of FY27 on 13 August 2026. Revenue was Rs 1,346 crore,
up 125 percent from Rs 597 crore a year ago and up 49 percent from Rs 901 crore last
quarter. That beat the company's own Q1 guide of about Rs 900 crore by half. Operating
profit tracked revenue. Operating EBITDA was Rs 255 crore, a margin of 18.9 percent,
slightly better than the 17.7 percent of a year ago.

Reported profit still fell. Net profit was Rs 57.8 crore against Rs 67.6 crore a year ago,
and reported earnings per share dropped to Rs 1.88 from Rs 2.41. The whole drop is one
event. A fire hit the Bawal plant on 6 May 2026 and destroyed assets worth Rs 143.6 crore
of carrying value. The company booked that as a one-off, below the operating line. Strip it
out and profit before the fire was about Rs 165 crore, a margin of 12.3 percent, inside the
guided 11 to 13 percent band, and up about 144 percent from last year. So operations were
strong and the headline was weak, and the gap between the two is the fire.

The auditor signed both the standalone and the consolidated results clean. It added a note
drawing attention to the fire, which is a flag, not a qualification. This is also the
company's first consolidated result, after it bought 31 percent each of two associates,
Zayo Cables and Zayo Energy, on 25 April 2026. Those two added almost nothing this quarter,
a loss share of Rs 10,000. The price paid and whether the sellers are connected to the
promoter are not disclosed.

What we could not test matters as much as what we could. A first-quarter filing carries no
cash flow statement and no balance sheet, so cash conversion, net debt and inventory days
are all blank. That blank is loud here because the profit and loss shows inventory rising
Rs 101 crore in one quarter, more than half of the whole prior year's build. The thesis'
main worry is a working-capital drain, and the one number that would settle it is not in
this filing. It comes at the half-year in November.

For the position, nothing changed by rule. This is a watch-list name, effectively avoid at
Rs 265, with an entry zone of Rs 186 to Rs 215. No thesis-broken trigger fired and no red
signal fired. The stock at Rs 265 still fails the hurdle test; at the entry zone it passes.
So the entry zone holds and the verdict is proceed with caveats. The operating momentum is
real, the fire is a one-off, and the two open questions that decide the next re-rating are
the insurance claim and the half-year cash numbers.
