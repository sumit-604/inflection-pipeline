100	Mr. Kishore Kathri, Company Secretary, welcomed the members of the Company and briefed them
101	about the process to participate in the meeting. He informed that the statutory registers and other
102	documents as required under applicable laws were made available for inspection on the website of
103	the Company. It was further informed that as the AGM was being held through VC/OAVM, the facility
104	for appointment of proxies by the members was not applicable and hence, the proxy register was not
105	available for inspection. The members were also briefed about the process for questions & answers
106	and e-voting.
107	
108	Mr. Vikram Vuppala, Chairman & Managing Director, chaired the meeting and welcomed the
109	members to the 17th AGM of the Company and requested the Directors who had joined the 17th AGM
110	through VC to introduce themselves. Accordingly, the Directors introduced themselves and confirmed
111	their presence at the AGM. The Chairman also acknowledged the attendance of representatives of
112	M/s. B S R and Co., Chartered Accountants, the Statutory Auditors of the Company, the representative
113	of Makarand M. Joshi & Co., the Secretarial Auditor and Ms. Rashida Hatim Adenwala, Practising
114	Company Secretary as the Scrutinizer for the meeting. The requisite quorum being present as per
115	statutory requirements, he called the meeting to order.
116	
117	Thereafter, the Chairman delivered his speech.
118	
119	With the permission of the Members present, the Notice convening the 17th AGM and the Statutory
120	Auditor’s Report were taken as read. The Chairman informed the Members that the Statutory
121	Auditor’s Report did not contain any qualifications, reservations, adverse remarks or disclaimers.
122	
123	Thereafter, the Chairman proceeded to take up the resolutions set out in the Notice of the AGM. In
124	respect of Item Nos. 5 and 6, in which Mr. Vikram Vuppala, Chairman & Managing Director, was
125	interested, Mr. Om Prakash Manchanda, Independent Director, chaired the proceedings and
126	conducted the business relating to the said items. Upon conclusion of Item Nos. 5 and 6, Mr. Vikram
127	Vuppala resumed the Chair and proceeded with the remaining business of the Meeting.
