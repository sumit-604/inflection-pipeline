### 2C. Growth embedded in capex — the arithmetic

Capex under execution (i.e., appearing as CWIP or a committed, board-approved
capital project in the audited FY26 accounts) = **Rs 0**. The entire
disclosed CWIP balance (Rs 112.52 lakh) is a stale, more-than-3-years-old
residential flat advance, unrelated to any production asset (AR p.93). PP&E
additions for the year were Rs 29.11 lakh total across all asset classes
(AR p.93), against depreciation of Rs 315.69 lakh (per 03-ardeep.md,
capex/depreciation = 9.2%).

Arithmetic: Capex under execution (Rs 0 cr) × any historical fixed-asset
turnover ratio = **Rs 0 cr implied incremental revenue = 0% above current
revenue.**

**capex_embedded_growth_pct = 0%** on a strict, evidence-based basis. This
is separate from management's own illustrative, unfunded thumb-rule for
Boron Oxide *if* it were ever commissioned at full capacity: 4,000 MTPA ×
current Boric Acid realisation (~Rs 127–128/kg, i.e. ~Rs 1.27–1.28 lakh/
tonne, Bharuka transcript p.17) × a 2.5x Boron Oxide price multiplier
(Kalra transcript p.8–9) ≈ Rs 127–130 cr of theoretical peak annual revenue
at full utilisation — against FY26 revenue of Rs 215.45 cr, i.e. roughly
+59% if fully realised. This figure is **entirely a management verbal
thumb-rule, unbacked by any capex, timeline commitment, or contract**, and
is presented here only to show the scale of the claim against the scale of
the (zero) capex evidence behind it. It is not used as capex_embedded_growth_pct.
