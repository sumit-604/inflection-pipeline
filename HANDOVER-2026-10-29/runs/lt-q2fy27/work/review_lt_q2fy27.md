- [INFERENCE] The cycle is strong in Gulf hydrocarbons and Indian private capex. It is weak in Gulf solar timing and Indian water projects.

## 3. BUSINESS-MODEL INTELLIGENCE
- L&T earns most of its core profit from fixed-price EPC contracts. It books margin only after a job passes a recognition threshold. [ASR-MGMT]
- Large new orders therefore dilute margin first. This quarter shows that pattern in CarbonLite and hydrocarbon. [MGMT, ASR-MGMT]
- Clients pay advances. Advances fund working capital and build a cash pile. The cash pile earns treasury income. [ASR-MGMT, FILED]
- That loop lifted both cash flow and profit this quarter. It reverses as jobs use up the advances. [INFERENCE]
- The listed IT and finance subsidiaries supplied 39% of H1 owners profit. [MGMT]
- The core projects portfolio earns 57% on segment funds employed because advances shrink those funds. Corporate funds of Rs 43,158 crore earn 5.4%. [MGMT]
- The model is drifting. L&T exits old concession assets but builds an AI data center with about USD 750 million of GPUs and new debt. [ASR-MGMT, FILED]
- [INFERENCE] The company moves part of its capital back into owned assets. Returns on those assets are not yet visible.

## 4. COMPETITION INTELLIGENCE
- Where L&T wins: scale on mega orders, its own fabrication yards in Oman and India, and repeat Gulf clients. [ASR-MGMT]
- The Oman framework names three rivals by origin only: one Egyptian, one Chinese and one Korean contractor. [ASR-MGMT]
- [INFERENCE] Chinese and Korean contractors usually compete on price in Gulf awards.
- An analyst on the call said L&T will not bid without a "differentiated margin". Management did not reply to that line. [ASR, analyst statement; A1 R412]
- Where L&T is weaker: Indian water projects carry overruns. Excluding them would lift infra margin by 40-50 bps. [ASR-MGMT]
- Solar EPC is weaker too. Module supply and cost depend on China. [ASR-MGMT]
- IT services face pricing pressure and forex hits in the TPS segment. [MGMT]
- [INFERENCE] Domestic thermal BTG competition likely includes BHEL. This is general industry knowledge, not verified this run.
