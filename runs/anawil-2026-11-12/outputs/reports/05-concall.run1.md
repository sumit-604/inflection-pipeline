**Reproduction check on EBITDA per tower (new finding).** PR p.2 states Rs 32.82 lakh, up 104.7% YoY and 14.8% over FY26. H1 FY27 EBITDA is Rs 3,608.24 lakh (H1 p.4, derived: 1,815.41 + 1,286.53 + 528.16 - 21.86). Divided by 107 towers it is Rs 33.72 lakh. Divided by 108 it is Rs 33.41 lakh. Rs 32.82 lakh implies 109.9 towers (B03). The growth rates imply bases of Rs 16.03 lakh (H1 FY26) and Rs 28.59 lakh (FY26). The base on filed numbers is Rs 16.36 lakh (1,014.13 / 62) and Rs 29.16 lakh (6,122.99 / 210, AFS via B03). On filed bases the growth is +100.6% (32.82 / 16.36) and +12.6% (32.82 / 29.16). The gap is small, but the company calls this metric the "key" one and its own count does not tie. Every figure here is derived from anchored numbers.

**Guidance arithmetic (shows what the target needs; not a verdict).** All figures derived from PR p.2, H1 p.4, Pres p.18, p.21, p.22, List p.7.
- H1 FY27 revenue is 30.8% to 34.2% of guidance. H1 shares in earlier years: 28.9% FY26 (41.37 / 143.27), 19.8% FY25, 29.5% FY24 (List p.7). Guidance needs H2 at Rs 296 to 346 Cr, which is 66% to 69% of the year. That is below every past H2 share (70.5%, 80.2%, 71.1%). Seasonality alone does not make the guidance a stretch. [INFERENCE] The guidance may have been set with the H1 print known, which makes it a conservative-looking number against the stated seasonality.
- The stretch is towers. H2 revenue of Rs 296 to 346 Cr needs 240 to 280 towers at the order-book realisation of Rs 1.236 Cr per tower (395.58 / 320), or 206 to 240 towers at the H1 realisation of Rs 1.44 Cr (B03). The record half so far is 148 towers (H2 FY26, B03). The need is 1.4x to 1.9x the record half.
- Koppal alone has a half-year nameplate of 210 towers (35 a month, Pres p.18). The H2 need of 206 to 280 towers equals 98% to 133% of that. If Kutch stays in trial runs, guidance depends on Koppal running at or above nameplate. If Kutch contributes, it must supply at least 30 to 70 towers in H2 (derived, assuming Koppal at 210). H2 FY26 total (148) was 70% of Koppal's half-year nameplate. [INFERENCE] The Kutch approval is the load-bearing event for the revenue guidance. Management does not tie the two together in any document read.
- Tower weight lowers the effective ceiling. The 29 Oct order is for 474 t towers (Ann 2026-10-29 p.2). Installed tonnage of 207,000 t supports about 437 such towers, not 612 (B03, from Pros p.25).
- PAT: H2 needs Rs 40.6 to 45.6 Cr (guidance less H1 Rs 14.39 Cr), which is 1.20x to 1.35x H2 FY26 PAT of Rs 33.70 Cr (3,370.22 lakh, H1 p.4) on 2.9x to 3.4x the revenue. H2 net margin needed is 13.2% to 13.7%, against 9.3% in H1 FY27 (Pres p.9) and 33.1% in H2 FY26. Finance cost should fall in H2 once Rs 115 Cr of debt is repaid. At most about Rs 1.2 to 1.6 Cr per half on the Sep-26 debt of Rs 33.6 Cr (3,360.79 lakh at 9.25% on bank debt and 6% on the CMD loan, derived) against Rs 5.28 Cr in H1. This is my derivation, not management guidance.
