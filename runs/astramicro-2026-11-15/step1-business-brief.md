## BRIEF

1. Rs 1,163 Cr consolidated FY26 revenue, Rs 193 Cr PAT; revenue CAGR FY22-FY26 about 12% (750 to 1,163). Market cap Rs 16,174 Cr, PE 74.8 at Rs 1,704 (13 Nov 2026 close). (S1)
2. What it sells: RF and microwave subsystems, super components and systems for radar, EW, telemetry, space and weather radar. It makes GaN and GaAs MMICs up to 40 GHz. It works both build to print and build to spec. (P1, Company Overview slide)
3. Q2 FY27 consolidated: revenue Rs 263 Cr (+22.6% YoY), EBITDA Rs 89 Cr (33.8% margin vs 22.3%), PAT Rs 52 Cr (+117.5%). H1 FY27: revenue Rs 440 Cr (+6.1%), EBITDA 27.8%, PAT Rs 64 Cr. Q1 FY27 was weak: revenue Rs 177 Cr, OPM 19%. (P1 consolidated P&L; S1)
4. Margin source: gross margin rose to 57.4% in Q2 FY27 from 50.8%. Management credits a mix of domestic defence production orders and lower opex. H1 FY27 revenue is 93.0% production orders, 0.2% development. (P1; P2)
5. Revenue mix Q2 FY27: defence 82.7%, exports incl. deemed 6.2%, meteorology 5.8%, space 4.8%. The mix swings hard by quarter: Q4 FY26 had space 19.5% and exports 16.9%. (P1, Revenue Break-up)
6. Customers, FY26 standalone Rs 1,155 Cr: DRDO 28.2%, BEL and other DPSUs 22.7%, IMD 18.0%, exports via ARC and Aelius 11.9%, ISRO/SAC 6.4%, private 3.0%. Government and DPSU buyers dominate. (P1, Industry Leading Customers)
7. Order book: standalone Rs 3,937 Cr at 30 Sep 2026, up from Rs 2,156 Cr at 30 Jun 2026. Q2 inflow Rs 2,036 Cr, executed Rs 256 Cr. Consolidated order book Rs 4,708 Cr. Standalone book is 82.0% production, 7.3% development, 4.3% export, 6.4% AMC. (P1; P2)
8. The single largest order: HAL radar production, Rs 1,878.8 Cr, received Q2 FY27, for Uttam radar subsystems. One secondary source quotes Rs 2,205 Cr, likely including GST. (P1, Major Orders Q2 FY27; https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/astra-microwave-q1fy27-orderbook-guidance-cmswwk6gu022m0zo43fo9ruiw)
9. Guidance: FY27 revenue about Rs 1,350 Cr (15-20% growth), FY28 about Rs 1,600 Cr, as given on the Q1 FY27 call. Q2 FY27 call guidance: NOT FOUND (transcript not yet filed). The deck targets 15-20% growth with more revenue from complex system fabrication in three to five years. (multibagg URL above; P1)
10. Working capital is the weak point. FY26 debtor days 216, inventory days 193, CCC 374 days (FY25: 459). CRISIL gross current assets about 429 days in FY26. CFO was negative in FY23, FY24, FY25, then +Rs 387 Cr in FY26. H1 FY27 consolidated CFO is minus Rs 148 Cr; working capital absorbed Rs 200 Cr; short term borrowings rose to Rs 412 Cr from Rs 224 Cr. (P1 Working Capital slide and Consolidated Cashflow; C1; S1)
11. JV and subsidiaries: Astra Rafael Comsys (ARC), JV with Rafael, Israel, formed Aug 2019, for SDR, EW and SIGINT; Astra and associates hold 51%; equity accounted (share of JV profit Rs 7 Cr in H1 FY27). ARC order book about Rs 836 Cr with an FY27 revenue target of about Rs 360 Cr per the Q1 FY27 call (ICICI Securities note https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_astramicrowave_q1fy27.pdf); other secondary sources give FY27 ARC revenue of Rs 400-600 Cr, so this is unsettled. Subsidiaries: Astra Defence Technologies (ex Bhavyabhanu, EMS), Aelius Semiconductors Singapore (fabless MMIC), Astra Space Technologies (satellite equipment). JV Navictronics 50:50 for NavIC chips. (P1, Subsidiaries & JV; https://newprojectstracker.com/blog/astra-rafael-comsys-to-enter-into-electro-optic-systems-manufacturing/ ; multibagg URL above)
12. Corporate actions: demerger of space, meteorology and hydrology into Astra Space Technologies; exchange NOC dated 29 Oct 2026; ratio NOT FOUND. That business had FY26 revenue Rs 110.6 Cr (space) and order book Rs 187.1 Cr at Mar 2026. MD change: Dr M V Reddy MD from Oct 2026, succeeding S Gurunatha Reddy. (https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ASTRAMICRO_29102026164303_NOCLetter.pdf ; P1 Space slide; multibagg URL above)
13. Ownership: promoters hold 6.54%, FII 10.48%, DII 16.52% (Sep 2026). Preferential warrants: 20,13,885 at Rs 864, 25% paid; Rs 130.5 Cr balance unpaid, conversion deadline 29 Dec 2026. Promoter group got only 46,296 of them. (S1; https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/astra-microwave-to-raise-rs174-cr-via-convertible-warrants-issue ; https://www.investywise.com/astra-microwave-products-limited-no-deviation-in-use-of-preferential-issue-funds/)
14. Rating: CRISIL A+/Stable, upgraded from A/Positive on 28 Aug 2026; A1 short term; bank lines Rs 1,786.5 Cr. CRISIL flags the large working capital need and tender-based procurement. (C1)
15. THE ENGINE: a move up from component and subsystem supplier to radar production at scale. The HAL Uttam order alone is about 1.6x FY26 standalone revenue. The thesis rests on whether that book converts to revenue at Q2-type 33% EBITDA margins without a fresh working capital blowout. (P1)

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS

1. Guidance vs delivery: FY27 guidance about Rs 1,350 Cr against H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 440 Cr. H2 must deliver about Rs 910 Cr, about 67% of the year. Verify against the Q2 FY27 call and the Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 transcripts; also check the FY26 guidance against FY26 delivery (Rs 1,163 Cr).
2. Cash conversion: FY26 CFO +Rs 387 Cr after three negative years, then H1 FY27 CFO minus Rs 148 Cr with short term debt up to Rs 412 Cr. Verify receivable and inventory notes and contract liabilities; test whether FY26 was a one-year collection catch-up.
3. Concentration and the HAL order: Rs 1,878.8 Cr from HAL (Uttam radar) is about 48% of the standalone book. Verify execution schedule, scope (subsystem vs full radar), margin, and advance terms in the filing and call.
4. Governance and structure: demerger of space, meteorology and hydrology (ratio, appointed date, cost allocation), the Oct 2026 MD change, the 6.54% promoter holding, and the Rs 130.5 Cr unpaid warrant balance due by 29 Dec 2026. Verify ARC's 51% stake and why it is equity accounted, plus related party sales to ARC (FY26 export order Rs 138 Cr from ARC).
