    Trident Lifeline Limited wins recognition among Gujarat’s Top 50
    SMEs under Fastest Growing SMEs

      Surat, Gujarat - August 20, 2026 – Trident Lifeline Limited has been honoured in the “Fastest
      Growing SMEs” category at the inaugural Top 50 SME Awards – Gujarat, recognising the
     company among Gujarat’s leading SMEs. The recognition reflects the company’s continued
      progress, entrepreneurial  spirit and  contribution  to Gujarat’s MSME ecosystem, while
       reinforcing its commitment to sustainable growth, business excellence and long-term value
       creation. The company extends its gratitude to Axis Bank, India SME Forum, the Jury and its
      stakeholders for this recognition and looks forward to building on this milestone.





      The award was received in the presence of the Minister of State for Finance, Government
        of Gujarat, Shri Kamlesh Bhai Patel; IAS K. C. Sampat; IAS Kshipra Agre; Executive
        Director of Axis Bank, Munish Sharda; and Chairman, India SME Forum, Prahlad Kakkar.

     Recognition Highlights :
         Selected from 1,524 nominations received from enterprises across Gujarat, reflecting the
         scale and competitiveness of the recognition.

         Successfully underwent a stringent, multi-stage evaluation process involving detailed
         scrutiny and assessment against prescribed parameters

         The  final  selection was made by an eminent and independent Jury comprising
         distinguished business leaders and industry experts following detailed deliberations.

     A milestone that reflects Trident Lifeline Limited’s continued journey of growth and
      excellence

    About Trident Lifeline Limited:

     Trident Lifeline Limited (“Trident Lifeline” or the “Company”) is a pharmaceutical and healthcare
    company with expertise in manufacturing finished dose pharmaceutical products, with a
     growing presence across global markets. The Company offers a diversified portfolio spanning
     pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, herbal products, injectables, personal care and medical
     devices, catering to a broad range of healthcare needs. Its operations are supported by a
     hybrid manufacturing model combining strategic manufacturing partnerships with integrated
      capabilities, enabling scalability, product quality and supply reliability. Trident Lifeline focuses
    on delivering quality-led healthcare solutions across domestic and international markets,
     supported by  regulatory-compliant  manufacturing  capabilities, an  expanding  product
      portfolio and an established global distribution network.
