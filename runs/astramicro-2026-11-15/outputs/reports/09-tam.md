## Section 3: SAM and SOM

3A. SAM, five filters applied to the realistic TAM (Rs 66,052 Cr):
1. Product fit. Keep radar and EW only: Credence radar 27% = Rs 17,834 Cr; EW Rs 6,995 Cr (INFERENCE as above). Comms/C4ISR, EO and fire control, avionics are not summed because their shares are NOT FOUND. Result Rs 24,829 Cr (37.6% of TAM).
2. Geography and customer. Domestic industry share 75% (IDSA): 24,829 x 0.75 = Rs 18,622 Cr. Export demand for these categories is NOT FOUND and is left out.
3. Channel. A subsystem supplier captures part of a system's value. Content share NOT FOUND.
4. Capability. Only qualified programmes count. Qualified share NOT FOUND.
5. Not-served programmes. NOT FOUND.

So the sourced filters give a ceiling of Rs 18,622 Cr a year (28.2% of TAM). Filters 3 to 5 cannot be sourced. The only content-level data is management's programme pool.

SAM carried: management's pool, spread over FY27-FY31 (the Jun call moved the pool to FY30-31) with 10.5% yearly growth. Weights 1 + 1.105 + 1.2210 + 1.3492 + 1.4909 = 6.1662. FY27 SAM = 27,500 / 6.1662 = Rs 4,460 Cr. FY26 SAM = 4,460 / 1.105 = Rs 4,036 Cr. SAM is 6.8% of the realistic TAM (4,460 / 66,052), 25.7% of the conservative TAM, and 23.9% of the Rs 18,622 Cr ceiling (calc). A 24% pass-through of the content and qualification filters is plausible for a subsystem maker, but it is not evidenced. Tier-3 SAM, order of magnitude only.

Two readings of the pool timing, both shown. 5-year spread (carried): SAM FY27 Rs 4,460 Cr. 4-year spread (Feb call text): 27,500 / 4.6753 = Rs 5,882 Cr. The observation that separates them is the award date of the BEL QRSAM contract and the Su-30 production orders. QRSAM slipped on three calls (B05). I carry the later reading.

3B. SOM.
- Current share of SAM: FY26 defence plus exports Rs 1,000.08 Cr / SAM FY26 Rs 4,036 Cr = 24.8%.
- Share-gain rule: 1-2pp in 3 years is normal; 3-5pp is aggressive and needs capacity and execution; more than 5pp needs a competitor exit. Evidence for the aggressive tier: backlog Rs 3,937 Cr is 3.4x FY26 revenue (deck PDF p.6, calc); Uttam is a nominated DcPP award, not a share taken from a rival. Evidence against the top of the tier: capacity gap (3C), FY26 growth 10.67% against 15-20% guided, B05 grade B. I carry 4pp, the middle of the tier. Variants: 3pp and 5pp. The cap at 5pp holds by year 5, because competitor exit is not evidenced (management's "single qualified source" claim is unverified, and DP says it builds a parallel Su-30 radar, B06).
- SAM path (10.5% growth): FY27 4,460; FY28 4,928; FY29 5,446; FY30 6,018; FY31 6,650.
