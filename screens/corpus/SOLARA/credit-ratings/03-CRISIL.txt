 Detailed Rationale 

 Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Ltd (Solara) to ‘ Stable’ from ‘ Negative’ while reaffirming the rating at 'Crisil BB B ' . The rating on the short-term bank facility has been reaffirmed at 'Crisil A 3 +'. 

 The outlook revision factors in the steady revival in business performance in the over the four quarters ending December 2024 . For the nine months ended December 2024 the company has achieved revenue of around Rs. 1010 crores. The operating margins improved to 15.95% for the nine months through December 2024; the margins stood at (7.2) % in fiscal 2024. The improvement in performance is attributable to favourable demand and product mix and increase share from regulated markets, 

 An improvement in operatin g performance resulting in healthy accruals and equity infusion have resulted in an improvement in financial profile. During the current fiscal year, Rs.120 crores have infused in the form of equity through rights issue. This, coupled with a debt reduction to the tune of Rs. 193 during the year, has resulted in an improvement in gearing to around 1 time as on December 31, 2024; the ratio was 1.54 times as on March 31, 2024. This has also enhanced the debt protection metrics. 

 The ratings continue to reflec t Solara's established market position in its key active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) a long with strong relationships with customers and suppliers and moderate financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to regulatory change and volatility in operating profitability.

 Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description 

 Strengths : 

 Established market position in key APIs, along with strong customer and supplier relationships: Solara has a strong portfolio of APIs in key therapeutic segments, with expertise in anthelmintic, anti-malaria, anti-infective and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory. Furthermore, it has been increasing its focus on the   non-steroidal anti-inflammatory segment by adding capacity and working on other therapeutic segments. Solara has a diversified customer base, with more exposure to regulated markets. Its longstandin g presence in the industry has helped Solara build healthy relationships with customers and suppliers. 

 Moderate financial risk profile: Solara’s financial risk profile is moderate with estimated net worth of around Rs. 714 crores as of 31 st March 2025 with a gearing and TOL/TNW of around 1.05 times and 1.54 times estimated as on the same date . The improvement comes from the debt reduction and equity infusion during the year. As a result, the debt protection metrics have also seen stark improvement with an estimated interest coverage ratio and NCATD (net cash accruals to total debt ) of 2.12 times and 0.17 ti mes estimated for fiscal 2025. The ratios were (0.89) times and (0.46) times in fiscal 2024. 

 Weakness es : 

 Exposure to risks relating to strict regulations: Most of the products manufactured   by Solara face increased inspections and regulatory actions by authorities, such as the US Food and Drug Administration (US FDA). Additionally, production of a few products involves waste discharge, which needs to be treated in effluent treatment plants (ETPs). Thus, Solara needs to invest continuously to upgrade ETPs and bring efficiency in the process to reduce waste discharge. 

 Volatility in operating profitability: Operating profitability fluctuated between (7.3%) and 9.3% in the last 3 fiscal years ending March 2024. In the current fiscal year, the operating margins are estimated to be around 17 percent for the full year with improvement in gross margins and the company’s cost optimization strategies. Going forward, the margins are expected to be in the range of around 17 to 20 percent and the ability of the company to demonstrate sustained operating margin levels will be a key sensitivity factor. 

 Liquidity : Adequate 

 Bank limit utilization remained high at around 94.9 percent for the past twelve months ended June 2024. Annual c ash accrual s are expected to be over Rs. 120 crore which is sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs. 100 crore per annum over the medium term. The promoters are likely to extend support in the form of equity and unsecured loans to meet incremental working capital requirements and repayment obligations on a need basis . 

 Outlook : Stable 

 Crisil Ratings believes Solara will continue to benefit over the medium term from the management’s extensive experience. 

 Rating s ensitivity factors 

 Upward Factors: 

 Significant growth in turnover and maintaining operating margins above 1 5 % 

 Improvement in the financial risk profile 

 Sustenance of working capital parameters and liquidity risk profile 

 Downward Factors: 

 Sharp fall in revenues or d ecline in operating profitability to less than 1 2 % 

 Large debt-funded capital expenditure or significant stretch in   the   working capital cycle impacting liquidity. 

 About the Company 

 Established in October 2017, Solara was formed through the demerger of the API business of Strides Shasun Ltd (currently named Strides Pharma Sciences Ltd). Solara acquired the human API business from Sequent Scientific Ltd during the same time, and hence, is a pure play API company. The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange.
