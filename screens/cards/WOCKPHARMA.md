## Business Understanding Narrative

Wockhardt is a research-driven pharma company with three engines: novel
antibiotics, biosimilars (diabetes), and a base pharma business across the UK,
India, and emerging markets (IP FY26 Q1 p.4; AR FY25 p.66). The base business
pays the bills: UK is 39% of revenue, India and rest-of-world 46%, EU 12% (AR FY25
p.66). The value, and the valuation, sit in the R&D pipeline, above all Zaynich
(WCK 5222), a novel antibiotic for drug-resistant gram-negative infections with a
claimed total addressable market of ~US$9 bn across the US, Europe, and India (IP
FY26 Q1 p.32). Zaynich has US FDA approval, with breakpoints covering ~95% of
carbapenem-resistant US cases (Concall FY27 Q1 p.7).

What is changing is a margin recovery plus the Zaynich launch. EBITDA margin went
from 9% (FY24) to 14% (FY25) to ~18.6% (FY26), and EBITDA grew 51% to Rs 630 cr in
FY26 (IP FY26 Q1 p.9; IP FY26 Q2 p.8). A Rs 1,000 cr QIP cut net debt to near zero
(IP FY25 Q4 p.8). Management plans to launch Zaynich in the US, India, Europe, and
emerging markets over two years and expects a bottom-line "hockey stick" after
12-18 months (guidance).

Why now: the base business has been fixed and deleveraged, and the biggest drug is
at the launch line.

What must be true: Zaynich must sell, at price, in hard-to-crack hospital markets,
and biosimilars must double as guided.

What breaks it: Zaynich commercialisation disappoints, the launch costs a lot
before it earns, and the market has already paid for success.

## 2. Business model and archetype
Innovation-led pharma. The base (UK specialty, India branded, EM) funds R&D; the
upside is novel antibiotics and diabetes biosimilars (IP FY26 Q1 p.4, p.11).
Archetype: research-driven innovator, closest to a licence/scarcity business if a
novel drug wins, otherwise an outsourcing/branded-generic base. The economic
engine today is the recovered base plus biosimilars; the economic engine tomorrow
is meant to be Zaynich. R&D is expensed, which suppresses reported EBITDA (AR FY25
p.66).

## 3. Competitive advantages
Genuine in one narrow place: novel-antibiotic R&D. Wockhardt has a multi-molecule
gram-negative and gram-positive pipeline (Zaynich, Foviscu, Odrate, Emrok, Miqnaf)
and a US-approved breakthrough drug (IP FY26 Q1 p.14; Concall FY27 Q1 p.7). That is
a scarcity asset if it commercialises. The base business has ordinary
branded-generic economics. Moat: a potential scarcity moat in resistant-infection
therapy, unproven at commercial scale.

## 4. The promoters
Khorakiwala family; Dr Murtaza Khorakiwala is MD, Zahabiya Khorakiwala in
leadership (Concall FY27 Q1 p.27). One disclosure point to note: the company
reports net debt "excluding promoter debt" (IP FY25 Q4 p.6), so promoter loans sit
outside the headline gearing. Promoter shareholding and pledge were not in the held
corpus. Verify.

## 5. Financial trajectory
Income FY24 Rs 2,879 cr, FY25 Rs 3,033 cr (+5%), FY26 Rs 3,373 cr (IP FY26 Q1 p.9;
Q2 p.8). EBITDA Rs 251 cr (FY24), Rs 418 cr (FY25), Rs 630 cr (FY26); margin 9% to
14% to 18.6%. FY26 PBT Rs 238 cr (IP FY26 Q2 p.8); PAT not in the held corpus but
modest relative to EBITDA given R&D and interest. QIP of Rs 1,000 cr took net debt
to Rs 64 cr (FY25) with equity Rs 4,657 cr (IP FY25 Q4 p.8). Segment growth FY26:
biosimilars +27%, Emrok +50%, novel antibiotics plus biosimilars 23% of revenue
(IP FY26 Q2 p.9). The inflection is margin-led on the base, with the real
step-change still ahead and binary (Zaynich).

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder
FROM a low-margin branded-generic and specialty base TO a novel-drug innovator with
scarcity pricing (Zaynich). This is not a steady rung climb; it is a binary R&D
event. The relevant framework caution is v3.8 Amendment 18: a within-hold option
slice (Zaynich success) must not be carried into an exit as an unresolved
probability. Management's claim, quoted once: Zaynich is "a once-in-a-generation
... patient impact, humanitarian, and commercial opportunity" (Concall FY27 Q1
p.11). Treat that as an option, not a base case.

## 7. Growth-trigger register
- Zaynich launch in US, India, Europe, EM over two years; 7-8 key markets in 18-24 months. Source guidance. Status: underway (US approved).
- Biosimilars to double in 24-36 months; capacity double in 12-15 months. Source guidance. Status: stated/underway.
- Miqnaf launched in India; Foviscu and Odrate in trials. Source IP FY26 Q1 p.14. Status: mixed.
- Bottom-line breakeven in 12-18 months, then "hockey stick". Source guidance. Status: stated.
- Capex Rs 200-300 cr over 3 years, mainly biologicals. Source guidance. Status: stated.

## 8. Proof check
The base recovery is proven: margin tripled off the bottom and debt is near zero
(IP FY26 Q1 p.9; IP FY25 Q4 p.8). The Zaynich thesis is not yet proven in the one
way that matters, commercial sales at price. US approval and breakpoints are real
milestones (Concall FY27 Q1 p.7), but revenue conversion in hospital formularies
is the unproven step. The ESOP exercise prices show the market already re-rated the
stock hard, from a Rs 168-465 range to Rs 917-1,649 (AR FY25 p.201). Proof of base
turnaround: yes. Proof of the drug economics: not yet.

## 9. Flags from the documents
- Valuation is an option on one molecule; downside is double if Zaynich
  underdelivers and the multiple compresses (v3.8 Amendment 18 lens).
- Net debt is quoted "excluding promoter debt" (IP FY25 Q4 p.6). Understand the
  full obligation before trusting the 0.10 gearing.
- New own-salesforce build in the US and EU adds cost before it earns (Concall
  FY26 Q1 p.13).
- R&D expensed, so reported earnings understate spend intensity but also mean any
  R&D cut would flatter margins artificially (AR FY25 p.66).
Not seen: promoter holding/pledge, FY26 PAT, contingent liabilities.

## 11. Posture: Transition Decision Matrix
- Proof gate: fired on the base turnaround, NOT fired on Zaynich commercial economics.
- Ugliness: none; optics are clean and improving.
- Recognition gap: CLOSED and then some; the stock has re-rated hard (AR FY25 p.201)
  and trades ~54x EBITDA on the screen.
Clean optics, an unproven binary upside, and a closed-to-negative gap put this near
PRICED NARRATIVE for anyone underwriting Zaynich at face value. It is a rich option,
not a GARP compounder.

## 12. Verdict card
**PASS; off strategy.** Wockhardt is a large-cap, richly valued bet on a single
novel antibiotic. That is binary R&D optionality, not the small/micro-cap
GARP-transition the strategy hunts. Load-bearing facts to verify first if ever
pursued as a special case: (1) live CMP and multiple, (2) Zaynich actual sales
ramp and pricing post-launch, (3) full debt including promoter loans, (4) launch
cash burn versus the 12-18 month breakeven claim. What would change the view: two
to three quarters of real Zaynich revenue at price would reframe it, but the
entry multiple would still have to re-open a gap. No price here.
