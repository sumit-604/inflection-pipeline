### 1C. Trigger evolution across the record

- **ITCPL / Lifesciences pipeline**: STRENGTHENING. From "in advanced stage"
  (FY25 AR, Sep-2025) to fully consolidated and generating a named PSU
  contract via IOCL (Reg 30 filing, 25-Jun-2026, Rs 3.77 Cr, 24 months).
- **Mumbai food lab**: DELIVERED. Promised H2 FY26 (FY25 AR, Sep-2025),
  confirmed complete "by way of slump sale" (H1 FY26 MD&A, Nov-2025).
- **Hyderabad acquisition**: TIMELINE SLIPPAGE, then apparent DROP. Promised
  complete H2 FY26 (FY25 AR, Sep-2025), reiterated "expected to complete by
  June 2026" (FY26 deck, 21-May-2026). No completion is evidenced anywhere
  in the FY26 AR (3-Sep-2026, filed three months after the promised date)
  or in any Reg 30 announcement through this run's corpus close
  (26-Sep-2026). Management does not name the slip in either document.
- **USFDA Bengaluru pharma lab**: internally inconsistent framing. The same
  FY26 AR that carries the FY27 "USFDA lab underway, commercial by June
  2026" language (deck, 21-May-2026) elsewhere describes the Bengaluru lab
  in the past tense as already acquired and consolidated within FY26
  ("The addition of our USFDA-approved pharmaceutical laboratory in
  Bengaluru," FY26 AR chairman's letter, p.5-6 area; also MD&A p.14 area).
  Read together, the lab appears acquired ahead of its "commercial" ramp
  date, but the corpus never resolves whether these are the same asset or
  two separate USFDA-track acquisitions.
- **EBITDA margin ambition**: WEAKENING, undisclosed. Stated at "30%"
  twice (H1 FY25 deck, Nov-2024; FY25 deck, May-2025), then replaced by a
  "25-27%" FY27 guide (FY26 deck/AR, May/Sep-2026) with no acknowledgment
  that the target moved down.
- **PAT margin guidance**: internally inconsistent, unexplained (see 1B).
- **Revenue-doubling cadence ("2X every 2 years")**: technically exceeded
  (FY26 delivered 4.27x FY24, against a 2x ask), but the outperformance is
  acquisition-driven (ITCPL, QTIPL, LabOps, Mumbai, Bengaluru), a fact the
  MD&A commentary never disaggregates (see also B03 missing_risks: "organic-
  vs-acquired growth disaggregation not offered in MD&A").
- **NEW trigger, never disclosed at IPO**: the entire M&A-led growth
  strategy. The DRHP business-strategy section (Sep-2023) describes a
  purely organic plan — develop supplier/customer relationships, expand
  geographically via own new labs in Noida/Pune, strengthen the in-house
  LIMS platform — and contains no mention of acquisitions anywhere in "Our
  Business Strategy" (DRHP, p.135-136). By the FY25 AR (Sep-2025), barely
  20 months post-listing, the strategy had become four acquisitions in one
  year plus two more named as pending. This is a NEW trigger that appeared
  with no disclosed reason for the pivot; it is the single most consequential
  finding of this stage and feeds directly into LBF1 (organic vs acquired
  growth).

target changes elsewhere.

## MANAGEMENT COMMUNICATION HANDOFF SUMMARY

Qualitek's written record over three years shows a company that delivers
on hard, checkable commitments (IPO fund use, promoter loan repayment,
named acquisitions it chooses to complete on time) but goes silent, rather
than explaining, when a promise slips or a target is quietly revised down.
The single largest undisclosed shift is the pivot from an organic,
single-region growth story at IPO to a four-acquisition-in-a-year platform
build, a change in business model that investors were never told to
expect. Grade C reflects that balance: genuine delivery evidence exists,
but it does not outweigh the pattern of silent target dilution and the
one still-open, twice-promised acquisition.
