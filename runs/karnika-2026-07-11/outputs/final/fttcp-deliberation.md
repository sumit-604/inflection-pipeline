**FTTCP verdict: DEEP WATCH leaning AVOID.** Band 1 to 2. Extreme margin of safety

Read plainly, this is a one engine car. Revenue is firing on real demand and spare
capacity. The other three engines, margin, cash, and capital efficiency, are all
STAGNANT, each gated on evidence that is either missing (rating, debtor days, FY26 ROCE)

promoter CONCERN (B08) and an INDETERMINATE cash flag (Phase 1). Growth alone, without a
forward catalyst to convert it into margins, cash, and returns, is an accounting
transition, not a wealth transition.

Confidence in the overall verdict: MODERATE. The revenue leg is solid. The three
STAGNANT legs are all low confidence and evidence gated, which is itself the finding:
the forward case cannot be made cleaner without evidence the company has not disclosed.
The single fact that would most change this verdict: a clean Q1 FY27 print that shows
consolidated EBITDA margin back above 17% with a disclosed debtor days figure inside 90
days. That one print would lift two engines at once and move the composite toward BUY
