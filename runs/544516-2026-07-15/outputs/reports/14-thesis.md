## NARRATIVE (plain English)

Airfloa is a real business doing real things. It makes railway coach interiors and components for Indian Railways and is pushing into aerospace and defence, revenue jumped 66% to Rs 319.6 Cr, and it earns a genuine 25% return on capital. That is the watchable core, and it is why this is a watch name and not a discard.

The problem is two-fold and both parts are about honesty with yourself. First, the price. At Rs 315 the Hurdle Ratio STOPs on both valuation tracks, meaning even on grade-C-capped bull earnings there is no path to 25% a year. The corrected valuation is harder than the first pass: the growth-visibility premium is zero, because the market runway the company points to (the SOM) exceeds the factory it can actually build in the near term by about Rs 118 Cr, so there is no earned premium on top of the quality base. That drops the fair values, cuts the probability-weighted return to under 3%, and collapses the risk-reward to about even money. The entry zone is Rs 155 to Rs 193, which is 63 to 103% below today.

Second, the cash. Profit rose but operating cash flow went the other way, to minus Rs 57.45 Cr, because receivables ballooned. The operator ruled this growth-induced rather than structural, which is defensible, but it is provisional with a hard falsifier. The devil's advocate showed the original three-part AND-gate was spoofable: management could flip a single half-year OCF positive with a mobilisation advance or a year-end collection push while the real problem, the provision-free over-6-month receivables bucket sitting at 23.38%, keeps climbing. Debtor days already fell to 195 even as absolute receivables rose Rs 86.4 Cr, so the weakest leg was moving opposite the most diagnostic one. The falsifier is therefore re-cast as an OR-gate with the ageing share as the primary signal: if EITHER the aged bucket stays above 23.4% and rising with no provisioning, OR OCF stays negative on a full-half basis, cash is structural, the multiple drops to about 13x, and the entry zone falls toward Rs 146-182. Add the governance items (personal penalties under appeal, Rs 38 Cr of personal guarantees on partly unregistered family land) and a defence JV that has already slipped twice, and the verdict is clear. Avoid at Rs 315. Read the ageing note first. Re-engage only in the zone, only if the cash confirms, and only Small.
