## BUSINESS UNDERSTANDING NARRATIVE

TAAL Tech sells engineering hours. Its Bangalore engineers do plant engineering,
product design, aerospace design, transport engineering and building information
modelling for industrial clients outside India. Time and material contracts,
billed by engineer time, are 95.5% of revenue. Fixed price milestone projects are
4.5%. A sale of goods line is 0.01% and does not matter. So the whole company is
one revenue stream sold two ways. The customer buys design and engineering
capacity it does not want to hire and carry in house, and switching means
requalifying a delivery team in the middle of a live project. That is why the
annual report can say more than 70% of business comes from clients of ten years
or more (AR p.37).

The filings never name a customer. Note 42 (AR p.102) discloses one customer at
24.05% of FY26 revenue, up from roughly 21% the year before, and a second close
to 10%. Revenue is 99.82% billed outside India: Rs34.78 lakh inside, Rs19,708.14
lakh outside (consolidated Note 42, p.156). The run did not establish who these
customers are, what they buy, or how long the contracts run.

Present demand reaches the company through six signals the run named. The
unnamed top customer at 24.05% of revenue is the largest single one. The other
five are the quarterly results of the listed India ER&D peer set (LTTS, Cyient,
Tata Elxsi, Onward Technologies, Sasken), the USD INR and EUR INR rates, the
North America and Europe industrial and aerospace capex cycle read through ISM
and Markit PMI and the Boeing and Airbus order backlogs, the adoption pace of
GenAI tooling in engineering services, and the Bangalore ER&D engineer wage and
attrition trend.

Forward demand did not verify. No peer transcript in the January to June 2026
window describes a sector step up: Tata Elxsi grew 0.9% to 1.3% quarter on
quarter in constant currency, Cyient's digital engineering unit shrank 2.4% then
0.5%. One exception maps to a TAAL Tech vertical: Cyient's aerospace linked
Transportation and Mobility cluster grew 13.2% to 14.8% year on year in constant
currency. Total market was measured at Rs23,678cr to Rs23,875cr and the served
market at Rs1,100cr, both explicit floors built from peer aggregation after web
search failed on all 20 calls. Runway class is GOOD and revenue headroom is 5.6x
against those floors.

Competitive advantage is thin on filed evidence. The emerging moat scan scored 4
and classified NONE. One category cleared Moderate: customer ecosystem and
embedded relationships (C1), resting on the ten year tenure statement and the
rising top customer share. Categories 21 and 22 both scored zero. The fixed price
line has no moat. The time and material line has one partial moat, client tenure,
and the same rising customer share that evidences it is also the top risk to it.
R&D was Rs37.55 lakh, 0.20% of revenue, and technology absorption is reported
"NA" (Annexure A, p.49). No patent, design registration or IP filing appears
anywhere in the 164 page annual report.

## Growth accelerating, cash falling, disclosure nearly absent

TAAL Tech is a Bangalore engineering services firm with 521 employees and no
debt. It bills global industrial clients for engineer time. Revenue per employee
is about Rs36.5 lakh a year. FY26 revenue was Rs197cr and profit after tax was
Rs57cr. The company was an aircraft charter operator until an accident stopped
the flying, and the engineering arm was merged into the listed parent by NCLT
order in May 2025.

Revenue accelerated for three straight quarters. Rs45.79cr in December 2025,
Rs57.04cr in March 2026, Rs64.81cr in June 2026. That last quarter was 41.6%
above the same quarter a year earlier, after three years at roughly 7% growth.
The acceleration is filed, arithmetically correct, and verified by two separate
audits in this run. Nothing management has written explains it. The only
candidate sentence in the annual report says the company "won multiple strategic
accounts" and names no account, no size and no date.

Gate 0 scored 81 out of 160 and classified GOOD, with a MODERATE moat and no deal
breakers. The balance sheet block scored a full 20. That GOOD leans on a long run
profit growth rate of 35% measured from FY2017, a year when profit was Rs3.81cr
on a thin post demerger capital base. Measure the same thing over FY2022 to
FY2026 and profit growth is 15% to 16%. Both readings are true. The second one is
the decision relevant one.

Cash stopped following profit. Operating cash flow was Rs18.74cr against profit
of Rs56.72cr, a conversion of 0.33x, down from 0.84x a year earlier. Unbilled
revenue nearly tripled, from Rs4.64cr to Rs13.75cr, and rose from 10.4% to 24.1%
of the exit quarter's revenue. Trade receivables rose 34.8% to Rs52.04cr. Full
year revenue grew 6.6%. So working capital grew about eight times faster than
sales. The receivables ageing itself is clean: the tail older than six months is
2.0% of gross, against 1.5% a year earlier.

Reported margin and real margin move in opposite directions. The annual report's
own ratio table shows operating profit margin rising from 36.00% to 37.66%. That
ratio is profit before tax divided by revenue, and it includes Rs1,902.72 lakh of
other income. Revenue minus total expenses over revenue, the actual operating
margin, fell from 28.34% to 27.84%. A mark to market gain of Rs1,204.05 lakh on
the investment book is 21.2% of consolidated profit, and its year on year
increase is 84% of the entire increase in pre tax profit.

The margin question got most of its answer late in the run, from a verifier, not
from a stage. The US subsidiary, TAAL Technologies Inc, had turnover of
Rs5,350.15 lakh and profit of Rs303.90 lakh, a net margin of 5.68% (AOC-1,
p.162). It billed the Indian parent Rs4,734.48 lakh, which is 88.5% of its own
turnover and matches the parent's cost of technical services line exactly (Note
37(B), p.98). The Indian parent earns 28.3% on Rs19,010.10 lakh. So the onshore
arm is a thin billing conduit, not a delivery organisation, and delivery sits
offshore at Indian cost. Consolidation removes Rs4,878.76 lakh of that intra
group billing. This explains where the profit is booked. It does not remove the
US tax risk: a 5.68% onshore margin is what a low risk service provider earns,
and no transfer pricing study or advance pricing agreement is disclosed anywhere.

Management says almost nothing. No earnings calls. No investor presentation. No
numeric guidance of any kind across 164 annual report pages and three results
filings. The credibility grade is C, the floor for a company with no calls, and
it reflects an absence of evidence to grade rather than proven bad faith. The
same filing that stayed silent on the cash fall and the revenue jump also carried
two numerical errors inside audited financial statements. One, an investments
classification swap, drew a corrigendum the next day. The other, a "Summarised
Consolidated" table in Note 41 that is the standalone accounts with one expense
line deleted, overstates profit before tax by 57% and profit after tax by 77.6%
and was never corrected.

The governance trail has holes. The company lent Rs10cr, unsecured, to Vishkul
Enterprises Private Limited, the holding company that owns 50.74% of it. Form
AOC-2 returns "NA" for every field. The auditor's CARO annexure discloses the loan
in clause 3(iii)(a) and states four lines later in clause 3(iii)(d) that no loan
was given during the year. No board approval date, rate, tenure or security
appears anywhere in the report. Narayan Karbhase joined TAAL Tech's audit
committee on 4 August 2025 and is also a director of Vishkul, the borrower. No
document records the conflict or a recusal. The promoter verdict is CONCERN, with
no deal breakers.

The same record carries real positives, on the same evidence bar. Zero debt in
both years and a current ratio of 9.49x. Zero promoter pledge, corroborated by
three independent aggregators. Dividend payout rose from about 17% to about 38%
of profit. Two credentialed independent directors were added. The auditor change
was a documented firm merger casual vacancy, not a resignation, and both the old
and new opinions were unmodified. No whistleblower complaints, no SEBI strictures
against the company, no ESOP dilution.

Peers checked two claims and contradicted both. No peer transcript in the January
to June 2026 window describes the demand step up TAAL Tech's numbers would need,
and every peer working capital disclosure points the other way: Onward
Technologies improved days sales outstanding from 73 to 70, Cyient converted free
cash flow to profit at 163% and then 80.5% and narrated collections discipline
each time. Peers also confirmed one thing in TAAL Tech's favour. AI has not yet
compressed core ER&D services pricing, scope or headcount in this window.

The market is large enough not to be the constraint. Total market floor
Rs23,678cr to Rs23,875cr, served market floor Rs1,100cr, revenue headroom 5.6x,
runway class GOOD. Both figures are floors built from peer aggregation because
web search failed on all 20 calls, so the true numbers are almost certainly
larger. The path to Rs313cr in three years and Rs379cr in five implies 16.6% and
13.9% revenue growth and a move from 521 engineers to about 1,000, inside the
hiring band Onward Technologies actually achieved over five years.
