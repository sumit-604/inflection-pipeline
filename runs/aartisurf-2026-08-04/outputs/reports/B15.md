Once that quarter rolled off and was not replaced, the trailing E collapsed and the P followed it down toward the earned 12.2x, exactly the de-rating the Hurdle Ratio STOP had priced (HR 0.94; B11 §4B). Growth of the topline could not outrun a halving of the multiple on a shrinking earnings base. The buyer lost 43% doing precisely what the AVOID told them not to do.
