## WHAT IT MAKES AND SELLS

Titan Biotech ferments and processes animal and plant proteins into biological
raw materials. Core products are peptones, yeast and biological extracts, agar,
prepared culture media, collagen peptides, ox bile extract, animal-nutrition
ingredients and plant growth promoters (AR FY26 p.71, Directors' Report item 7).
Customers are pharma, biotech fermentation, nutraceutical, food and beverage,
diagnostics, cosmetics, veterinary and agriculture. The culture-media line trades
under the TM Media brand (AR FY26 p.100).

Three plants, all Bhiwadi, Rajasthan: Unit I RIICO Phase III, Unit II and Unit
III Chopanki (AR FY26 letterhead). Head office Netaji Subhash Place, Delhi.
Permanent employees 527 at 31-Mar-2026, headcount up 15.1% in the year (AR FY26
p.104). The company reports ONE segment (AR FY26 p.100); there is no product-wise
revenue split in the filings.

## WHERE THE MARGIN COMES FROM

Raw material is the swing factor. Cost of materials consumed was Rs 99.16 cr on
Rs 206.19 cr of revenue in FY26 (48%). FY25 was the margin trough: OPM fell to
16% from 21%, and management names raw-material price increases it could not
always pass on as the primary risk (AR FY26 p.101). Power and fuel rose to Rs
12.52 cr from Rs 9.53 cr. Employee cost is 15.7% of revenue.

The FY21 spike (revenue Rs 142 cr, OPM 32%) was the COVID culture-media and
diagnostics surge. Revenue then fell to Rs 124 cr in FY22 and took four years to
clear the FY21 level. Any forward case must separate the FY26 recovery from that
one-off base.

## THE ONE INFLECTION THE THESIS WOULD REST ON

Capex. Investing outflow was Rs 34.41 cr in FY26 against Rs 9.87 cr in FY25, and
the annual report describes it as "net investment in property, plant, equipment
and intangibles to build capacity and capabilities for future business growth"
(AR FY26 p.104). Gross block moved to Rs 62 cr from Rs 57 cr; investments moved
to Rs 60 cr from Rs 33 cr, so a large part of the outflow went into financial
assets rather than plant. Revenue then grew 31.8% in FY26 and 27.2% in Q1FY27,
after two flat years.

The second leg is mix. The company says it is developing health-supplement
products to diversify the portfolio (AR FY26 p.71), and the collagen-peptide
brands (Titagen, Unstergen, OXIBIL) sit at the higher-value end of the same
fermentation and extraction base.

The thesis question is whether the FY26 and FY27 acceleration is a durable
value-added mix climb, or the delayed recovery of a customer restock after the
FY25 trough. The filings do not settle it. There are no earnings calls.

## LOAD-BEARING FACTS (the run's first verification priority)

1. **Freight gross-up inside reported revenue.** Every FY26 and Q1FY27 filing
   carries the note: "Freight amount has been added in revenue from operations
   for the purpose of calculation of sales including GST in current year.
   Freight also added in total in other expenses to neutralise the impact"
   (Q1FY27 filing p.4, note 5; FY26 audited results). Cartage and freight
   outward was Rs 584.41 lakh in FY26 against Rs 426.95 lakh in FY25 (AR FY26
   p.138). Stage 1 and stage 2 must establish whether the FY25 comparative was
   restated on the same basis, and restate the growth rate if not. On the
   arithmetic available, reported FY26 growth of 31.8% is roughly 28% ex the
   gross-up. Every forward revenue basis depends on which number is real.

2. **Peptech Biosciences: an associate carrying six times its cost.** 36.87%
   held at a cost of Rs 12.30 cr, attributable net worth Rs 74.33 cr, FY26
   profit considered in consolidation Rs 6.51 cr (AR FY26 p.80, Form AOC-1).
   But consolidated FY26 profit exceeds standalone by only Rs 2.44 cr
   (Rs 2,988.52 lakh vs Rs 2,744.72 lakh, FY26 audited results). Stage 2 must
   reconcile the two figures and identify what Peptech does, whether it is
   listed, and whether related-party sales run through it. Section 1B treats a
   non-operating associate through the equity bridge only, never at the exit
   multiple, so the answer changes the per-share build.

3. **TM Media brand versus Titan Media Ltd associate.** The MD&A presents the
   culture-media business as "Titan Biotech (TM Media)" (AR FY26 p.100) while a
   48.44%-held associate is named Titan Media Ltd (AR FY26 p.75). Stage 2 and
   stage 4 must establish whether any part of the culture-media engine sits
   outside the consolidated P&L, and what the related-party flows between them
   are.

4. **Capex of Rs 34.41 cr with no capacity disclosure.** FY26 investing outflow
   was Rs 34.41 cr against Rs 9.87 cr in FY25 (AR FY26 p.104), yet gross block
   rose only Rs 5 cr while investments rose Rs 27 cr. Stage 3 must split the
   outflow between plant and financial assets, and find any stated capacity,
   line or commissioning date. Without it there is no engine behind the growth
   claim, and the Emerging Moat and TAM stages have nothing to size.
