## SECTION 1: MARKET DEFINITION

1A Boundaries.
- Product: spares, repairs and outage services, upgrades and retrofits for boilers, steam turbines, generators of coal-fired plants. Excludes new-build EPC, nuclear (Q3 FY26 call p.8 of 21: "doesn't include nuclear"), hydro, gas (sold).
- Geography: India. Export boiler spares for GE machines (Rs 450-600 Cr per management, Q3 FY26 call p.14 of 21) shown apart, not in SAM.
- Customers: central and state gencos, IPPs, captive plants.
- Channel: direct OEM and non-OEM bids; the unorganised repair shops are in TAM, not in SAM.
- Inclusions: GE-make fleet (about Rs 500 Cr a year, per management) and non-GE fleet of geometrically similar design, including Chinese-make and a few Indian makes (Q3 FY26 call p.12 of 21).
- Exclusions: FGD EP (run-off), Durgapur boiler factory (discontinued, demerger to JSW Energy not yet effective).

| 12 May 2026 | Rs 3,500-4,000 Cr "target market"; "around INR 4,000 crores of targeted fleet", about 18% share | None given | Q4 FY26 call p.8 of 16 | Broad |

## SECTION 3: SAM AND SOM

3A SAM. Filters: product fit (spares, repairs, upgrades; ex new-build), geography (India), channel (OEM-comparable bids), customer (fleets of similar geometry), capability (Chinese and some Indian makes under NPI work). Management applied these in the 2,500 figure; I cannot rebuild each subtraction because fleet MW is NOT FOUND. SAM central 2,500 (calc: not rebuilt; management defined). SAM as % of TAM floor 4,234 = 59% (calc), Confidence L.
Second reading: 4,000 (May). Separating observation: a fleet MW list behind the denominator, or a Q1/Q2 FY27 call that reconciles the two.

3B SOM. Base: share 29.4% on 2,500. Market growth 4% a year (Section 4A proxy).
- SAM FY29 (3 yr): 2,500 x 1.04^3 = 2,812. SAM FY31 (5 yr): 2,500 x 1.04^5 = 3,042 (calc).
- Share path: +2pp in 3 years (top of the normal 1-2pp band) = 31.4%; +3pp in 5 years = 32.4%. Share is already high, and >5pp needs a competitor exit; none is evidenced.
- SOM 3 yr = 2,812 x 31.4% = 883. SOM 5 yr = 3,042 x 32.4% = 986 (calc).
- CAGR from Rs 734 Cr: 3 yr (883/734)^(1/3) - 1 = 6.4%. 5 yr (986/734)^(1/5) - 1 = 6.1% (calc).
- Second reading on 4,000: base 18.4%; 3-yr SAM 4,499 x 20.4% = 918; CAGR 7.8% (calc). Aggressive: +5pp to 23.4% gives 1,053, CAGR 12.8% (calc), shown, not carried.
- Cross-check: management guided 5-8% revenue growth on the Q3 FY26 call (p.12 of 21, "5% to 8% figure", net of new-build decline); SOM CAGR 6.1-6.4% sits inside it. Past core intake growth was 32% in FY26 and 25% over four years (Q4 call p.8 of 16), well above SOM; the share-gain rule caps it once share is near 30%.
- The handoff is a core-order-intake rate. Core revenue is NOT FOUND (about 55% core in Q2 FY26, one quarter only, B04). Stage 11 should not apply it to total revenue without the line split.

3C Capacity. B07 capex-embedded growth 0%: the only programme is a repair facility, GVPIL share about Rs 18 Cr, replacing outsourced work, with no stated capacity. The model is asset-light. Installed capacity NOT FOUND, so no Rs Cr gap can be named. Nothing in the filings says 6% growth needs new capacity. The SOM is not the optimistic side. Whether the capex plan is silent on capacity because none binds, or because it is undisclosed, is open.

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY AND RUNWAY

5A Funnel (Rs Cr a year).
TAM floor 4,234 (L) -> SAM 2,500 (M; second reading 4,000) -> GVPIL FY26 core intake 734 (29.4% of SAM) -> SOM FY29 883 -> SOM FY31 986.
Own fleet inside SAM about Rs 500 Cr; non-GEPIL about Rs 2,000 Cr (Q3 call p.14).

5B Runway. Headroom = 2,500 / 734 = 3.4x (calc, intake basis; company revenue basis 2,500 / 1,269 = 2.0x is not like for like). Market 4% a year, GVPIL intake growth recent 32% (FY26): GVPIL is gaining share, not riding the market. Years to saturate SAM at 6.3%: ln(3.4)/ln(1.063) = 20 years, theoretical only: share above 50% in a contested market is not supported by any evidence.

5C Class: MODERATE. Share is already near 30% on the defined base, market growth is a low single-digit proxy, and growth rests on share gain in non-GE fleets. On the 4,000 reading headroom is 5.4x (calc) and the class would rise to GOOD. Not carried.

5D Levers pursued: non-GEPIL fleets, Chinese-make units (NPI work); export boiler spares for GE machines (Rs 450-600 Cr claimed, Shoaiba notice of award 12 Aug 2026, Saudi Arabia). Addition is not sized in filings; if the export slice were fully captured the SAM would add up to Rs 450-600 Cr (management's own range, a ceiling not a forecast). FGD is run-off, not a lever.

5E Output card. At 6.1-6.4% core order growth (SOM-implied), with a margin of 12.4% EBITDA ex other income (FY26, B04 calc) held flat, earnings growth embedded is about 6% a year before operating leverage and before other income. The current P/E is not in this stage's inputs (NOT FOUND), so I cannot say whether it supports the valuation. A 6% earnings rate would not support a growth multiple by itself. Stage 11 owns that test.
