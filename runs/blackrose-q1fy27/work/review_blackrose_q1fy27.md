4. **Gap between core-PBT growth and reported PAT growth?** Core PBT +175.25% vs PAT +145.34%. The

   PBT collapsed from 17.95% to 7.47%. Stripping OI does not change the read — core operations carried
   the quarter. Clean.

**Step 2 read:** On the numbers disclosed, Q1 FY27 is a genuinely strong, operationally-driven
quarter YoY. The decisive limitation is that the single most important thesis question — is the

unanswerable from this single-segment filing.
