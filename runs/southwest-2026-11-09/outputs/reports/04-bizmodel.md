## SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL IN PLAIN ENGLISH

South West Pinnacle rents out drilling rigs and geoscience crews, with the operators, to coal, mineral, gas and water-survey clients, who pay a contract fee per metre or per project (AR p.58; Deck p.5). It also owns a coal block that has not yet produced and holds minority stakes in two Oman joint ventures (AR p.60).

### 1E. Chai-stall-uncle version
Think of a company that owns a fleet of earth-moving machines and rents them out with drivers to big firms. The firm pays by the job or by the metre dug, and the big firms pay slowly. Our company drills holes so miners and gas firms can see what is under the ground. Three customers give it almost three-quarters of its sales (AR p.146). It also bought a plot of coal land and wants to dig it itself from FY2028-29 (AR p.60), which is a different and much bigger bet than renting out machines. Right now the machines keep it busy but the cash is stuck with the customers who have not paid yet (AR p.115).
