## PATTERN PASS — NEW FINDINGS

### 1. Ratio Analysis annexure (Note 37(xvii)) carries a stale prior-year signature date and UDIN — NEW
Every signed page of the FY2024-25 financial statements (Balance Sheet p.68, Statement of Profit & Loss p.69, Cash Flow Statement p.70, main Notes 1-37 signature block p.95, Independent Auditor's Report p.59, CARO Annexure A p.65, CARO Annexure B p.67) carries the identical sign-off: **"Date: 27.05.2025, UDIN: 25061132BMIYTN1259."** The Note 37(xvii) Ratio Analysis annexure (p.96) — which is physically the very next page after the main Notes signature block and presents the FY25-vs-FY24 ratio table already covered in Pass 1 — carries a **different signature block: "Date: 27th Day of May 2024, UDIN: 24061132BKAUAZ5291."** Indian UDINs encode the generation year as the leading two digits (issued by ICAI); "24..." denotes a UDIN generated in calendar 2024, i.e. consistent with signing off the *prior* year's (FY2023-24) annual report, not this one. 🟡 Watch — this is a genuine pattern-only finding (invisible on a note-by-note read): it strongly suggests the Ratio Analysis page template was copy-pasted from last year's annual report without updating the signature block, even though the ratio *figures themselves* on that page are correctly FY25-vs-FY24 (cross-checked against Pass 1's Note 37(xvii) table extraction — the numbers there tie to the FY25 Balance Sheet/P&L). The substance of the disclosure is not in question, but the sign-off provenance of this specific annexure is: it is unclear whether the auditor's FY25 engagement formally re-covered this page, or whether it was administratively rolled forward. Note anchor: Note 37(xvii), p.96, vs main signature block p.95 and all other FY25 statement pages.

## D. FIVE QUESTIONS FOR MANAGEMENT

1. Who is/are the counterparty(ies) behind the ₹885.36L outstanding (₹18.88Cr gross disbursed) unsecured inter-corporate loans disclosed only as "Body Corporates" in Note 19, and what is the commercial rationale, interest rate, and repayment schedule, given the funds originate from post-IPO surplus?
2. What is the arm's-length pricing basis for the ₹222.72L of rent paid to the three promoter-directors personally (86.3% of total rent expense, Note 33) and for the ₹2,288.34L of related-party sales (13.3% of revenue), particularly the ₹2,026.48L to Shree Garments, whose proprietor is a named member of the Note 3(F) promoter shareholder group?
3. Given the stated accounting policy of carrying receivables "after making adequate provisions for doubtful balances" (Note 2(q)), why has the doubtful-debt provision been NIL in both FY24 and FY25 despite ₹7,456.84L of receivables, ₹445.10L of which is over six months old?
4. What is the Board's risk-management framework and stated rationale for deploying treasury cash into listed/unlisted equities, mutual funds, and derivatives/speculation positions (Note 12/22: ₹866.32L year-end, ₹2,671.73L gross purchases in-year), and is this activity expected to continue or wind down?
5. Who was the lender behind the unsecured loan that generated ₹94.79L of FY24 interest expense and was fully repaid by FY25 (Note 27), given that the KMP loan/advance amounts disclosed in Note 33 (₹5.06L, ₹7.65L) are too small to be the source — and separately, why does the Note 37(xvii) Ratio Analysis annexure carry a signature date (27 May 2024) and UDIN (24061132BKAUAZ5291) that predate the FY25 audit engagement, when every other statement page is signed 27.05.2025?

## E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management / quality-of-earnings**: ~₹175L of Other Income (capital gains + derivatives + speculation) from a non-core securities-trading book folds into reported PBT growth (Note 12, Note 22).
- **Aggressive/inconsistent accounting**: stated doubtful-debt provisioning policy not matched by NIL provisions in either year (Note 2(q) vs Note 17); cash-equivalents classification includes a >12-month FD against a stated ≤12-month policy (Note 2(t) vs Note 18).
- **Undisclosed risk indicators**: two separate unnamed counterparties tied to material cash flows — the Note 19 inter-corporate loan recipient and the Note 27 unsecured-loan lender — neither named anywhere in the notes; MSMED Section 22 interest-on-delayed-payment disclosure absent despite a 266% rise in MSME dues (Note 8).
- **Governance/RPT concentration**: 13.3% of revenue and 86.3% of rent expense flow to promoter-family-controlled or promoter-personal counterparties with no arm's-length benchmarking disclosed (Note 33).
- **Documentation integrity**: Note 37(xvii) Ratio Analysis annexure carries a stale FY24 signature date/UDIN inconsistent with every other FY25 statement page; Note 33's RPT table is headed "2023-24" while its primary column is FY2024-25 data.

## F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate to concerning accounting practices. Key concern: two unnamed loan counterparties (₹885.36L inter-corporate loan, Note 19; a now-repaid unsecured loan that cost ₹94.79L in FY24 interest, Note 27) sit alongside 13.3%-of-revenue related-party concentration and 86.3% of rent paid to promoter-directors personally, none of it arm's-length benchmarked. Key strength: an unqualified audit opinion, no going-concern issue, no restatements, and mechanically clean core operating notes (inventory, PPE, deferred tax, borrowings, EPS) with receivables ageing quality actually improving even as the balance grows faster than revenue. Overall accounting quality: 4/10.
