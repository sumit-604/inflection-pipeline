## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from raw transcripts, anchored)

**IF1 — Related-party Oilmax merger at ~50x trailing earnings; fairness challenge deflected, not engaged.**
Sep25 call, Vinod Darjee (individual investor, p.8): "the ratio is 50x trailing earnings...
what is our rationale to pay such a high multiple, especially for the related promoter group
entity that you are merging with?" Dalpat Mehta (Suncity Advisors, p.11-12): "it seems that
it is biased for the shareholders of Oilmax and not in favour of Asian." Sumit Maheshwari
responded three times with the same circular assertion — the valuer used a "very conservative
approach," "necessary discounts of lack of marketability," "made in the Favor of retail
shareholders of Asian" — never engaging the 50x math or the ~₹2,500cr implied valuation
(Ashwani Agarwal, p.11, "the valuation is INR2,500 crores"). Two independent challengers,
promoter-controlled counterparty, unresolved. **Governance red flag, HIGH.**

**IF2 — FY26 guidance missed after a confident reaffirmation made when the miss was already visible.**
Sep25 (Vaibhav Mishra, p.6): standalone target ₹650-700cr rev / ₹110-120cr EBITDA, "guidance...
remains the same." Nov25 (Charvin, p.4), with H1 revenue only ₹217.4cr and H1 PAT ₹1.7cr
already on the table: Kapil Garg "we remain very confident of achieving our guidance we have
provided earlier." May26 (Sumit, p.5): "the company was unable to fully meet the previously
communicated guidance for the year," blamed on West Asia conflict + "some delays from the
client side," framed as "timing-related... rather than a loss." Two consecutive external-blame
shortfalls in one fiscal year. **MEDIUM.**

**IF3 — Oilmax field-production timelines (Amguri, Tiphuk, Duarmara) slipped in all three calls — repeated evasion (2+ quarters).**
Duarmara: Sep25 "come into production later in the year" (p.7) → Nov25 "expecting to put this
field into production later this year" (Mohit Agrawal, p.11) → May26 "difficult for me to
commit to a date to you today" (Vaibhav Badjatya, p.6-7). Tiphuk: Sep25 "workover rig heading
there next week... near future" (p.7) → May26 "commencing production in the coming months"
(p.3). Amguri grid connection: Sep25 "later this year" (p.7) → May26 "expected over the next
few months" (p.3). Ever-shifting goalposts, never a firm date. **MEDIUM (repeated evasion).**

**IF4 — Order book declined ₹2,000cr → ₹1,750cr while FY27 growth guidance was raised.**
Nov25 (Kapil, p.2): "more than INR2,000 crores as on date, excluding taxes and the Kuiper
portfolio." May26 (Sumit, p.5): "order book of approximately INR 1,750 crores... excluding
taxes and excluding the Kuiper portfolio," against a raised FY27 standalone guide of 30-40%.
**LOW-MEDIUM.**

**IF5 — Oilmax FY23/FY24 standalone financials promised "separately," never delivered.**
Sep25 (Vaibhav Mishra, p.6): "provide the revenue and EBITDA numbers for Oilmax... FY'23,
FY'24" → "we'll provide you separately. Currently, we do not have those numbers handy." Never
supplied or referenced in Nov25 or May26. **LOW.**

**IF6 — Kuiper purchase consideration/valuation never disclosed in any call**, despite Kuiper
being the single largest driver of reported O&G revenue growth. **LOW.**

**IF7 — Anirit Ventures rights-issue plan raised once, deflected, never revisited.**
Nov25 (Lakshay Chhabra, p.3): asks about Anirit Ventures "and the rights issue." Sumit: "It
has, as of now, nothing to do with Asian Energy... the current con call is for Asian Energy
business." Not revisited in May26. **LOW.**

**IF8 — Consolidated "70% growth / ₹791cr" headline masks a material STANDALONE guidance miss (under-quantified).**
May26 (Sumit, p.4): "revenue from operations of INR791 crores... growth of 70% year-on-year."
That ₹791cr is consolidated and includes ~7 months of Kuiper. Kuiper ran at ₹40-45cr/month
(Nov25, p.10) and management itself estimated "roughly INR250 crores" for Oct25-Mar25 plus one
month in Q2 — i.e. ≈₹290cr of the FY26 total is Kuiper. Stripping Kuiper, standalone FY26
revenue is on the order of ~₹500cr, versus the ₹650-700cr standalone guidance — a roughly
20-28% standalone shortfall, larger than the "timing-related" framing implies and obscured by
the acquisitive headline. (Inference; management never disclosed clean standalone ex-Kuiper
FY26 revenue.) **MAJOR — thesis-relevant: the organic engine underdelivered materially.**

**IF9 — Kuiper FY27 guide is near-flat versus current run-rate, contradicting Nov25 "grow significantly."**
Nov25 (Sumit, p.10): running ₹40-45cr/month and "for FY27, we are expecting Kuiper revenue to
grow significantly from the current run rate." May26: FY27 guide "$60 million to $65 million"
(Charchit, p.6), against a "current annualized run rate of between INR530 crores to INR540
crores" (Sunny Gosar, p.9-10, undisputed) ≈ $62-64mn. So the FY27 guide is essentially flat on
the current run-rate; the real step-up is deferred to the FY29 $100mn target. "Grow
significantly" collapsed to ~flat in year one. **MINOR (soft walk-back; long-term target intact).**

**IF10 — Unexplained "write-off" bundled into the ₹9cr Kuiper exceptional, not probed.**
May26 (Sumit, p.5): FY26 PAT "is after adjusting for an one-time exceptional item of INR 9
crores related to Kuiper acquisition costs **and a write-off**." The nature/quantum of the
write-off is never explained or asked; it rides inside the "acquisition cost" adjustment.
**MINOR.**

**IF11 — ESOP-deflection on Oilmax PAT margin comparability, adjacent to the valuation-fairness question.**
May26 (Charchit Maloo, p.5-6): posits Oilmax FY26 rev ~₹115-130cr "with I think 35% PAT
margin, right?" Sumit confirms revenue is "on similar line on FY25" but on margin: "we have
granted some ESOPs... so the PAT margins and those numbers may not be comparable." The one
metric that would let an analyst independently sanity-check the 50x-earnings valuation (PAT
margin) is deflected via an ESOP adjustment. **MINOR.**

**IF12 — Monsoon excuse for the Q2 miss is contradicted by a same-quarter peer (cross-read).**
AESL Nov25 (Sumit, p.3, p.8): Q2 EBITDA-margin decline "primarily due to... prolonged and
unseasonal monsoon," "the entire quarter was washout, work only for 20, 25 days." Directly
contradicted by DEEPINDS Q2 FY26 (Nov 10 2025 call, verified by me): "revenue for Q2 FY26
stood at Rs. 221 crores up by 69.2% year-on-year... EBITDA margin of 46.6%," with no monsoon
or weather mention anywhere. Adjacent-segment peer, same national monsoon, no disruption. This
weakens the "industry-wide, unavoidable" framing. **MEDIUM (peer-contradicted excuse).**
