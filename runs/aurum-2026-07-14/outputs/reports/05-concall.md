### 2B. Excuse pattern analysis

- Miss #9 (NestAway "completed" claim): DEFLECTION — the shrinking-portfolio fact is reframed as intentional strategy ("Win a Pin Code," "classic BCG strategy of churning") rather than acknowledging the earlier "completed... do not foresee reduction" statement was wrong.
- Miss #10 (ecosystem revenue cadence): HONEST-ADMISSION — genuinely rare, volunteered without being re-pressed on the exact prior promise; Ashish Deora directly says the company "has not been able to do justice" to its own stated metric.
- Miss #11 (SM-REIT timeline): mixed EXTERNAL-BLAME / DEFLECTION — blames the newness of the regulatory product and "slow investor uptake" (external), while never once, across four calls, giving a concrete reason internal to Aurum for the delay (e.g., specific asset that fell through, specific due-diligence blocker).
- Unconfirmed PropTiger profitability (#6): SILENCE — the specific promised confirmation is simply never revisited on the Q4 call; management substitutes an adjacent positive metric (record gross commission) instead of answering the profitability question directly.

Pattern check: management does proactively raise hard topics unprompted at
least once — the Q2 FY26 "strategic missteps" answer (Onkar Shetye, in
response to a direct but open-ended question from Rahul Kumar Paliwal)
volunteered three specific failures (student living/Kota, Rental SaaS
stagnation, fractional-ownership timing vs SM-REIT regulation) without
hedging. That is a genuine positive data point. Set against that: on two
of the three tracked misses (NestAway, SM-REIT) the pattern is
reframing/soft-blame rather than direct ownership. Overall pattern:
**balanced**, tilted toward deflection on strategic/soft commitments but
genuinely honest on at least two occasions (Q2 mistakes list; Q4 ecosystem
revenue admission).
