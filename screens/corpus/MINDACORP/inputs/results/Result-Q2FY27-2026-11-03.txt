Delhi/NCR, November 03, 2026: Minda Corporation Limited ('Minda Corp' or the 'Company'; NSE: MINDACORP, BSE: 
538962), the flagship company of Spark Minda, announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
Minda Corp achieved quarterly revenue of Rs. 2,189 crores, registering growth of 42.6% YoY, driven by demand 
across key vehicle segments, product premiumisation and an expanding customer base. During the quarter, the 
Company reported EBITDA of Rs. 257 crores, with a margin of 11.8%, marking a 17-bps YoY improvement. Profit 
Before Tax (PBT) Rs. 157 crores, with a margin of 7.2%, while Profit After Tax (PAT) reached Rs. 143 crores, with a 
margin of 6.0%. 
The Company continued to secure sizeable orders across multiple product domains, reflecting growing customer 
engagement and increasing opportunities across its diversified product portfolio. EV -related products contributed 
approximately 35% of new order wi ns during Q2FY2027, while larger orders across multiple business verticals 
further strengthened the Company's order pipeline. 
On the technology front, we continue to make progress in strengthening our capabilities through strategic 
partnerships and new product programmes. During the year, Minda Corporation invested Rs. 80 Crore in its group 
companies, Spark Minda Green Mobility Systems Private Limited, Minda HCMF Technologies Private Limited  and 
Spark Minda – Toyodenso India Private Limited to support its business expansion.  
Commenting on the results, Mr. Ashok Minda, Chairman and Group CEO, said: 
“Q2 and H1FY27 reflected continued progress in our long-term growth strategy, driven by demand across key vehicle 
segments and execution across our businesses. During the period, we expanded our order pipeline with new business 
wins and advanced our technology capabilities through product development and strategic partnerships. EV-related 
products accounted for approximately 35% of new order wins in Q2FY27, highlighting our progress in addressing the 
industry’s transition towards smarter, connected and electrified mobility. 
Looking ahead, Minda Corporation remains focused on expanding its product portfolio, strengthening customer 
relationships and increasing its presence in electronics and EV systems. The Company will continue to invest in R&D, 
localization and manufacturing capabilities to increase its content per vehicle and address evolving customer 
requirements. Alongside these investments, the focus remains on improving operational efficiencies, expanding 
opportunities across domestic and export markets, and delivering sustainable long-term growth.”

About Minda Corporation (BSE: 538962; NSE: MINDACORP) 
 
Minda Corporation is one of the leading automotive component manufacturing companies in India with a pan -India 
presence and significant international footprint. The Company was incorporated in 1985. Minda Corporation is the flagship 
Company of Spark Minda, which was part of the erstwhile Minda Group. The Company has a diversified product portfolio 
that encompasses Mechatronics, Information and Connected Systems and Plastic and Interior for auto OEMs. These 
products cater to 2/3 wheelers, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, off-roaders and after-market. The Company has 
a diversified customer base including Indian and global original equipment manufacturers and Tier -1 customers. For 
assimilating the latest technologies, Minda Corporation has a dedicated R&D facility and collaborations with the pioneers 
and leaders of the automobile industry. This has provided Minda Corporation with the cutting-edge in product design and 
technology to meet strict international quality standards.
