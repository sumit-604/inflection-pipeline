This brief follows the 5 October brief. The history file now carries
slightly revised counts, 901 for 1 October and 926 for Monday, and this brief
uses them. The 5 October brief named four things that would make the
cautious view fail. Two came true on Tuesday. The fifty-day count climbed back

## The picture in one paragraph

Tuesday was the broadest up day in two months, and it repaired the two lines
that broke last week. About seven stocks in ten rose. 202 stocks jumped four
and a half percent or more, the most since early August, against 23 that fell
that hard. The count of stocks above the fifty-day line jumped from 645 to 749,
back above its washout level. The count above the two-hundred-day line rose
to 976, back above the shelf that broke last week. New yearly highs drew level
with new lows for the first time in eight sessions. The down tail shrank by
more than half in one day. Fear fell back to its normal level. Two things hold
the read short of a turn. Volume ran about a fifth below its year-ago level, so
this was wide buying on light turnover. And 202 is still well short of the 300
that marks a true thrust in this record. The small end leads the large end, as
it has all autumn. The market moved from damaged to repairing. It has not yet
proved it can hold.

their two-hundred-day line. On 15 September came the washout, when 343 stocks

## What would change this read

The read tonight moves from cautious to repairing. The repair fails if the
fifty-day count falls back under about 700 within a few sessions. That is what
happened after 23 September, when a strong day lifted the long count to 1,186
and the next session sent 85 stocks down hard. It fails if the two-hundred-day
count slips back under about 970. It fails if new lows pull clear of new highs
again. Tonight they sit level, 57 lows against 56 highs. It also fails if the next wide day
arrives on volume still a fifth below a year ago and then fades.

The cautious read fails outright if the repair extends. The tests are a thrust
day of 300 or more stocks jumping four and a half percent, on volume above its
year-ago level. Or new yearly highs beating new lows for several sessions. Or
the long count climbing past about 1,180, the 23 September high, while the VIX
stays near 14 or below. In June the long count went from about 906 to above
1,220 within a week once the thrust came. Tuesday has the breadth of that
pattern. It still needs the force.
