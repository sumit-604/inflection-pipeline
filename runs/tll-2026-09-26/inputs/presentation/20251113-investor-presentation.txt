Management Commentary

•   Trident Lifeline has continued its growth momentum, building on
   the performance of Q1 in Q2 and overall H1FY26. Revenue from
   Operations in H1FY26 stood at ₹4,790.17 lakh, registering a robust
   growth of 53% YoY.
•  EBITDA margins remained healthy at 27%, although they witnessed
   some moderation from the higher base of 30% in H1FY25. EBITDA
    for H1FY26 stood at ₹1,295.37 lakh, registering an increase of 36%
    YoY.
•   Profit after Tax for the half-year stood at ₹841.71 lakh, registering a
   growth of 31% YoY. The increase in PAT is commendable given the
    elevated Finance Costs and Depreciation on a YoY basis due to
   ongoing business investments.
•  The outlook for the remaining year remains strong, with business
    operations projected to grow at both standalone and consolidated
    levels, driven by Trident Lifeline’s strategic approach to organic and
    inorganic expansion.
