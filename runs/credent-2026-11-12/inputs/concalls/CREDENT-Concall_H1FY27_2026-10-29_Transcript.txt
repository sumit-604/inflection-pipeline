diagnostic laboratories, hospitals, and other healthcare enterprises.

Starting with our journey, it began in 2015, when we are incorporated Credent Cold Chain 
Logistics Private Limited, with the focus on specialized temperature-controlled transportation 
for diagnostic companies. We recognize that healthcare logistics requires specialized handling, 
timely transportation, temperature integrity, and coordination across collections and testing 
locations. 
Working closely with the customers, we identify opportunities to support them across the 
broader range of services. We gradually expanded our geographical network, introduced home 
collection phlebotomy capabilities, and strengthened our healthcare logistic infrastructure. 
Now, our turnover crossed INR25 crores in 2022, INRS0 crores in 2023, INR75 crores in 2024. 
And in 2024, we rebranded ourselves as the Credent Connect N Care Limited Private Limited, 
reflecting our transition beyond cold chain transportation towards an integrated healthcare 
logistics and service platform. We further strengthened our capabilities through the acquisitions 
of Credent Healthcare Private Limited and Credent Team Private Limited in 2025, followed by 
Alltrak Technologies in 2026. 
These developments have expanded our capabilities across healthcare services, clinical 
workforce management, and technology-enabled operations. Our successful public listing in 
August 2026 represents another important milestone as we prepare for the next phase of growth. 
Today, our business is built around five interconnected services categories. First is healthcare 
services, which includes home collection, station phlebotomy, on-demand phlebotomy, and 
preventive health checkup services. These services support diagnostic laboratories, hospitals, 
and healthcare institutions. 
Secondly, we offer a logistic service where we provide intra-city and inter-city logistics through 
road, rail, air transportation. This includes temperature-controlled and cold chain movement for 
time-sensitive diagnostic samples and healthcare consignment. 
Third, we offer a technology and IT services where we provide technology platform and 
application support for home collections and sample movement, workforce management, and 
laboratory operations and other healthcare workflows. 
Fourth category comprises professionals and other services which includes advisory services, 
corporate and wellness programs, technology-cnabled operations, support for healthcare 
organizations. 
Fifth, we offer a C3 wellness through which we provide an integrated corporate wellness 
solutions include preventive healthcare for the corporate, for their employee health checkup, on- 
site health scan, and diagnostic and consultation. These services are complementary and allow 
us to support customers across multiple stages of their healthcare operations.
