## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Shree Pushkar runs two businesses off one acid complex at Lote, Ratnagiri. Chemicals: sulphuric acid, then dye intermediates (H-acid, vinyl sulphone, K-acid) and Dyecol reactive dyes (Aug-26 deck, p.3). Spent acid and gypsum feed fertilisers: single super phosphate, sulphate of potash and NPK, sold through 800 distributors (Aug-26 deck, pp.3, 10). FY26 revenue was Rs 976.6 crore, 54% chemicals; PAT was Rs 70.1 crore (Aug-26 deck, pp.3, 9). Both lines sell at the market's price. Money comes from the spread over sulphur, rock phosphate and benzene, and from plant utilisation.

**What is changing?** Capacity. Rs 512 crore of capex adds 450,000 MTPA of complex fertilisers (Unit 6 at Ratnagiri, Unit 8 at Meghnagar), 72,000 MTPA of chemicals and 10 MW of solar; Rs 209 crore was spent by June 2026 (Aug-26 deck, p.13). Management guides Rs 1,250 crore of revenue in FY27 and Rs 1,700 to 1,750 crore in FY28 (Aug-26 call, pp.6, 11). Price is the second change. Sulphur went from USD 250 to 300 to about USD 1,100 after the West Asia conflict; H-acid rose from Rs 525 to Rs 750 per kg (Aug-26 call, p.8; May-26 call, p.10).

**Why now?** Unit 5's dye line began commercial production on 10-Sep-2026 (U5, p.1). Unit 6 trials were due by September 2026 (Aug-26 call, p.13). The Q2 FY27 result is due by 14-Nov-2026 and is not in the corpus. The stock closed at Rs 543 against a 52-week range of Rs 272 to 551 (MD).

**What must be true?** Unit 6 must run in the Rabi season; the Rs 1,250 crore guide assumes it (Aug-26 call, p.11). Volumes must return: Q1 FY27 chemicals volume fell 38.6% and fertiliser volume 12.8% (Aug-26 PR, p.2). The 8.5% to 9% PAT margin must hold with sulphur at three times its old price (Aug-26 call, p.9; May-26 call, p.6).

**What breaks it?** Unit 6 slips past Rabi, as Units 5 and 6 already slipped from Q4 FY26 to September 2026 (Feb-26 PR, p.3; Aug-26 call, p.13). Finished prices fail to carry tripled inputs; management halted dispatches in March 2026 for that reason (May-26 call, p.7). Or the guide moves a fourth time (step 9).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Cost position: present, from integration.** One site turns sulphur into acid, acid into intermediates and dyes, and waste into fertiliser (Aug-26 deck, p.10); solar supplies 20.6 MW DC (filings/20260817_d805addf, p.1). **Pricing power: absent.** The company sells less when it cannot get its price (Feb-26 call, p.16) and stopped dispatches in March 2026 to wait for higher prices (May-26 call, p.7). That is timing, not pricing power. **Scarcity: claimed.** Management says China cancelled its VAT refund on dye-intermediate exports and India now exports H-acid to China (Feb-26 call, pp.7, 17). No third-party evidence in the corpus.

**Verdict: a cost moat is present; no pricing moat exists; the specialty layer is unproven in numbers.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R1/R2 boundary.** A price-taker with a cost position from integration. ROCE 13% to 15%, PAT margin 7%, utilisation 55% to 70%.

**TO: R2 at twice the scale.** Complex fertilisers backed by in-house phosphoric and sulphuric acid at Unit 8 (Feb-26 call, p.14). Reactive dyes at Unit 5. The claim is scale and integration, not pricing power.

Management's claim, once: "somewhere in 2029 there should be minimum Rs. 2500 thousand revenue... somewhere 9% to 10% PAT lines we should get" (Feb-26 call, p.15).

Ladder check. A scale step inside R2, not a rung climb. The strategy hunts rung climbs the market has not priced.

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point is Unit 6 running at a margin. It is absent.** The plant was "90%, 95%" complete in May 2026 and held back on raw-material cost (May-26 call, p.8). The August target was trials by September (Aug-26 call, p.13). No filing followed.

**What the last two quarters show.** Q4 FY26 PAT fell 22% to Rs 12.9 crore after a self-imposed dispatch halt (May-26 PR, p.2; May-26 call, p.7). Q1 FY27 PAT rose 9.4% to Rs 22.9 crore on volumes down 17% (Aug-26 PR, p.2). Chemicals realisation about doubled to Rs 1.51 lakh per MT; fertilisers rose 19% to Rs 21,400 per MT (derived from Aug-26 PR, p.2). Two readings: a value-over-volume strategy that holds, or a one-quarter price windfall on held inventory. The Q2 FY27 volume table beside its gross margin separates them.

**The test is mixed.** Profit moved up in one of two quarters. Volumes moved down in both. The new capacity contributed nothing yet.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Unit 6 has not started (step 8). Q1 FY27 growth came from price on falling volume.
- **Ugliness: ARTIFACT-OF-CLIMB, with a structural overlay.** Rs 149 crore of CWIP earns nothing (May-26 deck, p.12), and the volume dip came from a deliberate hold; both reverse when the plant runs. The 7% margin and 13% ROCE are structural to a converter and do not.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Consolidated trailing PE is about 24.4x (step 9). The R2 neighbourhood is about 15x to 17x. The FY27 guided profit gives 16.5x (step 13). The price already pays an R2 multiple for a plant that is not running.

**Posture: PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP).** Clean optics: net cash, A+ rating, 20% profit growth. The transition is a scale step narrated at Rs 2,500 crore. The TO state is priced. Hard WATCH, no position, until Unit 6 runs and the price re-opens the gap.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**

A cash-funded doubling of capacity in a business that earns 7% on sales at the market's price. The balance sheet can carry it. The price leaves no room for a slip.

**Load-bearing facts to verify first**
1. Unit 6 commercial production, with a dated Reg 30 filing and its first-quarter volumes (Aug-26 call, p.13; Aug-26 deck, p.13).
2. The Q2 FY27 volume table and gross margin against Q1 FY27's 9,113 MT and 31.9% (Aug-26 PR, p.2).
3. The chemicals revenue split between acids, intermediates and Dyecol dyes (AR26, p.28 gives one line).
4. The FY27 tax rate after Unit 5, Unit 6 and solar capitalisation (May-26 call, p.12).

**What would change the view**
- Up to PROCEED: a Unit 6 commercial-production filing, plus Q2 FY27 PAT above Rs 25 crore with chemicals volume back above 13,000 MT.
- Down to PASS: Unit 6 still not filed at the Q3 FY27 result, or Q2 FY27 PAT below Rs 20 crore, or a fourth revision of the FY27 guide.
