cash surplus of INR 7,815 million compared to a net cash surplus of INR 5,906 million as of

flows for H1 FY27 amounting to INR 5,678 million. 
 Operationally, we continued to witness robust momentum across the network. Inpatient volumes

demand across our specialties and the continued scale-up of our newer facilities. Occupied bed 
days increased by 22% with network occupancy improving to 67% on expanded bed c apacity 
compared to 64% in Q2 FY26. 
 Overall, our census bed capacity increased to 3,115 in Q2 FY27 from 2,658 beds in Q2 FY26, a

 Average length of stay, or ALOS, for the quarter was 2.89 days compared to 3.06 days in the 
same quarter las t year. International patient revenue grew by 30% year -on-year to INR 993

continues to witness strong growth trajectory, with revenue increasing 53% year -on-year to   
INR 699 million, supported by both hospital pharmacies and our expanding retail pharmacy
