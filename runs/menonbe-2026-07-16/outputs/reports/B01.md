## DECISION LINE

Mechanical Gate-0 classification is **AVOID**, driven by (a) a mandatory one-tier downgrade
for limited continuous history (only 4 years, FY23-FY26, usable — the FY06-08 legacy window
is disconnected by a 15-year filing gap) landing on top of an already-AVERAGE core score,
and (b) a deal-breaker-triggering Block B (5/20) reflecting negative free cash flow in both
measurable years and rising working-capital days. Return metrics (Block A, 19/20) and
balance-sheet strength (Block D, 18/20) are genuinely strong, and the moat picture is
MODERATE (cost advantage, capital efficiency, and customer stickiness all present) — this
is not a distressed or over-levered company. The AVOID classification is substantially a
function of (i) a data-continuity artifact (15-year filing gap pre-FY23) and (ii) a capex-
heavy expansion phase (Net Block +56% FY23→FY26, borrowings +218%) that mechanically
depresses near-term FCF — both legitimate mechanical triggers, but not necessarily signs of
deteriorating underlying business quality. Per pipeline rules this does NOT halt the run;
flags propagate to downstream stages for human weighing, and position-sizing overrides for
"documented post-IPO rebase / legacy cleanup" style cases are noted as permissible per the
deal-breaker override language, though the historical gap here is a filing-continuity issue
rather than a rebase event.
