===== PAGE 3-4 (CFO opening) =====
"For the quarter ended March '26, overall performance of the company substantially
improved. Poultry and poultry products segment delivered a robust performance due to the
improved realization from the sale of day-old chicks and grown-up boilers, particularly in
the last quarter."
"For the financial year '25-'26, the company registered a better performance as compared to
previous year on account of better realization in the fourth quarter."

===== PAGE 4 (volumes and realisations, FY26 vs FY25) =====
Commercial broiler birds: 8.69 crore kg in FY26 against 9.17 crore, "a reduction of almost
49 lakh kgs during this financial year."
Layer chicks: 4.57 crore (FY26) vs 4.38 crore (FY25).
Broiler chicks: 11.46 crore (FY26) vs 10.77 crore (FY25).
Realisations FY26 vs FY25: commercial broiler bird Rs 89.59 vs Rs 90.37 per kg (down Rs
0.78); broiler chicks Rs 34.94 vs Rs 37.90 (down Rs 2.96); layer chicks Rs 45.15 vs Rs
41.50 (up Rs 3.65).
Cost of production FY26: commercial broiler bird "almost touching average INR89 during the
year"; layer chicks Rs 35 to Rs 37 per bird; broiler chicks "somewhere around INR32".
Internal transfer: day-old chicks supplied to the commercial broiler division at cost,
"INR28 per number throughout the year ... fixed for the year."

===== PAGE 5 (structure) =====
"almost 80% under integration and 20% for open market" of broiler volume.
"if you see the profile of Venky's ... Animal Health Product division and Oil Seed division.
Our main focus is there. So majority of the expansion and majority of the production and
majority of the EBITDA is coming from those divisions, though principally, we call
ourselves as a poultry company. But if you see the profile, our profile is too balanced so
that almost 49% to 50% is poultry and balance is coming from Oil Seed and AHP division."

===== PAGE 6 (poultry growth) =====
"With regard to poultry growth, our growth is coming to 7% to 8% as an industry. And we do
expect that, that growth is likely to happen in Venky's also."
On volatility: "those first 2 quarters were not as expected, but later on, it was
compensated by quarter 3 and quarter 4."

===== PAGE 7 (feed and Q4 margin) =====
"yearly 2025, '26, the average price is INR31,658 per ton for the feed prices ... as far as
quarter 3 is concerned, the broiler feed rate is ranging somewhere around INR39,000 per ton."
Q (Siddhart, ithoughtPMS): "this quarter, the margin in the poultry segment has almost 14%
operating margin ... what is the reason for this? And do you think that this performance
can be maintained?"
J. K. Handa: "With regard to quarter 4, the main attributing factor was broiler realization
and, of course, input costs. So that is the reason why the percentage and the overall
absolute profit was higher. We do expect such kind of performance in future also, but there
are certain things which, of course, we have to see with regard to ingredient prices."

===== PAGE 8 (prices turning down after the quarter) =====
J. K. Handa: "In regard to the broiler prices, it was very good prices in the last quarter,
ranging up to INR105 to INR110 where the Venky's is operating ... which has continued in
the month of April also, similar to that and May also in the first 10 days, but now
gradually it is falling maybe due to the multiple factors of the demand supply situation."
"And in regard to the broiler chicks, same is the case. The prices were quite up in the
last quarter. It went up to even INR55 per kg per bird, number bird, day-old broiler chicks,
which has come down to the level of INR35. And even some part of the Northern India, it has
come down to the level of INR22, INR21."

===== PAGE 9 (Animal Health and Oilseed) =====
Deepak Khosla on AHP: "this business is a very consistent kind of business. Last year, we
have built up a new facility, which is adding value to our product line, which is giving us
a bottom line improvement ... for whole year, I think it will sustain 22%, 23% and above,
and we are likely to see the improvement in the top line also."
N. K. Toshniwal on oilseed: "last 6 months, there has been a gradual increase in the
capacity utilization ... we have been able to increase our outside sales other than the
group to the 20% of our production now ... our dependence on the group will become lower and
our 60% to 65% revenue will be coming out from the outside the group only."

===== PAGE 10 (CCI notice) =====
Q (Harsh Shah, Merisis Advisors): "There has been recently an article about CCI antitrust
investigation. Can you throw some light?"
Rohan Bhagwat: "this CCI investigation is regarding certain practices in the parent

products. However, being a group as a part of legality, we have received the notice. So as

===== PAGE 10-11 (Q1 seasonality and Q4/April realisations) =====
Dr. Pedgaonkar: "we have more than 95% of the farms which are open. So because of these
conventional farms in India, usually, the productivity is comparatively 10% to 12% less if
we compare it to quarter 2 and quarter 3. So definitely, the productivity will be less in
quarter 1."
Q4 FY26 average realisations (J. K. Handa): commercial broiler Rs 102.67 per kg; day-old
chicks Rs 51.32; layer Rs 46.15. April 2026: broiler around Rs 97 to Rs 100; layer chicks
Rs 46; broiler chicks Rs 38 to Rs 39.

===== PAGE 12 (the volume plateau) =====
Q (Madhur Rathi, Counter Cyclical Investments): "I was seeing our commercial boiler
production in FY2020, it was 10.4 crores kgs. And I think in FY26 also it is the same. So I
don't think it has grown over the past 6 years ... from FY16 till FY20, every year,
commercial grown up boiler in kgs was increasing. But after that, it has basically plateaued
out. And whereas, so basically, the overall poultry division itself, it doesn't seem it has
been really growing at 8%, 9% the industry average."
J. K. Handa: "The prices were INR120 or INR115 or INR114 in the last quarter, whereas in the
April, it is ranging somewhere around INR102 and or INR100 or so. And presently, it is
falling down to the level of INR90 or INR93. So top line is also hampering with the
realization. Bottom line is also affecting the numbers."

===== PAGE 13 (shareholder request to demerge AHP) =====
Q (Akhil Parekh, 360 ONE Capital, page 10): "if you look at some of the listed peers, they
get far better valuation ... are there any internal discussions in terms of separating
Animal Health business and getting it separately listed? ... because it's a part of entire
Venky's, which includes a volatile business as well of poultry, Animal Health is not getting
the right multiple."
Deepak Khosla: "Venky's portfolio is very interesting. It has a Poultry, it has Food
Processing, it has Oil Seed business and Animal Health. They actually complement each other.
I think this is a perfect set of models ... presently, this model is fantastic."
Madhur Rathi (page 13) repeats the request: "the Animal Healthcare division, since it's an
independent division, it might be housed in Venky's India. But since there are no
interdivision transactions, it's an independent unit, so it can be split off, and it will
generate huge value for the shareholders. And sir, also, we should do a share buyback."

===== PAGE 13-14 (oilseed history and economics) =====
Q (Sahil Jain, Sannidhi Partners): "since FY21, where you hit a peak of INR2,500 crores and
until last year, which was FY25, you came down to almost INR1,100 crores."
N. K. Toshniwal: "After COVID, we had certain setbacks and the constraints, which it took
about 2.5 years to recover ... In the commodity business, the main thing is capacity
utilization, which makes all the difference ... we are debt free. We are not using any money
for the capex ... There are absolutely no loan for the division."
Capacity utilisation "around 65% to 70%. And this year, we expect to be around 80%."
Page 18: "a certain amount of profit come from the inventory, which is a speculative profit.
And there, we have a fairly expertise. We are able to buy the goods in advance."
"40% is oil, 60% is the meal."

===== PAGE 17 (run-rate guidance) =====
Animal Health "EBITDA Run Rate: Expected to maintain INR 110 crores". Soya "EBITDA

===== PAGE 18-19 (receivables and debt) =====

year, it has come down this year."
"On the working capital front, we are having the sufficient working capital in place. It is
almost INR160 crores ... no plans to increase the liability as such."

===== PAGE 19-20 (industry maturity and FY27 volume plan) =====
Dr. Pedgaonkar: "Slowly, slowly, industry is getting mature with a consistent placement by
all the integrators ... earlier, poultry was having 35% to 40% in non-veg segment. Now it is
crossing more than 55% ... we feel that there will be less volatility in future."
FY27 plan: layer chicks from 4.57 crore to "5 crores plus"; broiler chicks and contract
broiler farming "at least 2%, 3% more, 4%, 5% more".
"more than 75% of expansion comes from existing players".
