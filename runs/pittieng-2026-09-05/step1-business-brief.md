# STEP 1 BUSINESS BRIEF — Pitti Engineering Ltd (PITTIENG)

Identity: Pitti Engineering Ltd. NSE PITTIENG / BSE 513519. Screener consolidated
page loads (HTTP 200). CMP Rs 1,085, market cap Rs 4,085 Cr (2026-09-05).

- What it makes. Electrical steel laminations, motor cores, stator and rotor
  sub-assemblies, shafts, iron castings, machined components, die-cast rotors, and
  press tools. Formerly Pitti Laminations. It is the largest CRNGO electrical-steel
  buyer in India, near 10-12% of domestic supply. Stamping diameter runs 30 mm to
  1,300 mm. Source: https://scientificinvesting.substack.com/p/scaling-through-integration-and-value
- The economic engine. A single-product stamper turned vertically integrated
  component platform. It added precision machining, iron castings, and full
  sub-assembly across FY23 to FY25 through two acquisitions and two group-entity
  consolidations. Source: https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/pitti-engineering-growth-strategy-88310
- End markets (Q1 FY27 mix). Traction motors and railway systems 40%, power
  generation 15%, plus renewables and data-centre DG sets. Source:
  https://tradebrains.in/indian-markets/why-is-this-engineering-company-emerging-as-a-key-player-across-data-centres-railways-power-12446600
- Customers. Wabtec and Alstom for railway traction motor cores (the two primary
  loco OEMs in Indian Railways tenders). Cummins for data-centre DG-set stator and
  rotor laminations. Source: search result, scientificinvesting.substack.com.
- Exports. 27% of revenue FY26, 31% in Q1 FY26. Source:
  https://ca.investing.com/news/company-news/pitti-engineering-q1-fy27-slides-strong-growth-rising-capacity-utilization-93CH-4791801
- FY26 (consolidated). Revenue Rs 1,952.9 Cr, up 12.2%. Adjusted EBITDA Rs 325.8 Cr,
  up 19.9%. PAT Rs 117.8 Cr, DOWN year on year. EPS Rs 31.77. Dividend Rs 2.50.
  Source: https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/pitti-engineering-reports-fy26-revenue-growth-of-12-2-adjusted-ebitda-rises-19-9-to-325-8-crores/40341755
- Why PAT fell while EBITDA rose. Finance cost and depreciation climbed on the
  capex and on a strategic BIS-certified electrical-steel inventory build; the Q4
  FY26 tax rate jumped to 29% from 14.34%. Source:
  https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/result-analysis/pitti-engineering-q4-fy26-margin-pressures-dent-profitability-despite-revenue-growth-3993259
- Q1 FY27 print. Revenue Rs 529 Cr, up 16% YoY. Adjusted EBITDA Rs 89 Cr, up 14%,
  margin 16.8%. PBT flat at Rs 36.2 Cr. Adjusted PAT Rs 32 Cr vs Rs 26 Cr.
  Depreciation Rs 28.4 Cr, finance cost Rs 22.6 Cr. Source:
  https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/pitti-q1fy27-97738
- Net debt. Rose to Rs 525+ Cr on the strategic inventory build; management guided a
  drawdown toward Rs 300 Cr by April 2026 as BIS-certified supply deals with Korean
  and Japanese mills came in. Source: whalesbook.com FCF note (search result).
- The one inflection. Mix shift up the value chain plus a volume ramp. Greenfield
  machined-components capex of Rs 290 Cr (Rs 60 Cr spent), casting capacity to
  36,000 MT, both commissioning by Q1 FY29. Management guides FY28 turnover above
  Rs 2,500 Cr at a 90,000-ton operating level with EBITDA margin near 17-17.2%, and
  FY27 EBITDA near Rs 370 Cr. Source:
  https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-pitti-engineering-posts-solid-q1-2026-growth-shares-rise-93CH-4851334

## PEERS SELECTED (auto, no operator pause)

Engine matched, not the generic Capital Goods basket. The engine is a build-to-spec
engineered-component maker climbing from a converter to a value-added, spec'd
supplier on a customer capex cycle (railways, power, renewables), with an
electrical-steel input pass-through and rising content per unit.

- VILAS (Vilas Transcore Ltd). The direct product analog: CRGO electrical-steel
  laminations and cores for transformers. Tests lamination conversion economics and
  electrical-steel input pass-through. URL loads (200), consolidated available.
- SANSERA (Sansera Engineering Ltd). Precision forged and machined engineered
  components to auto, aero and industrial OEMs, diversifying into non-auto with an
  export tilt. Tests the climb to value-added machining plus the export mix shift.
  URL loads (200), consolidated available.
- RKFORGE (Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd). Engineered forged components with a heavy
  railway (wheels, wagons) content ramp, backward integration, and a capex cycle.
  Tests the railway-demand and capex-cycle engine and the integration playbook.
  URL loads (200), consolidated available.

No pure listed electrical-lamination-plus-machining peer at Pitti's scale exists;
VILAS is the closest product match (SME scale), SANSERA and RKFORGE are the closest
structural comps on the value-add and capex-cycle engine.
