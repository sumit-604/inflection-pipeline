## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG READ (from ASL sources, fresh)

Numbers re-derived from source; every one carries an anchor. "Rs" figures in lakhs unless a
crore unit is stated (crore figures come from the CARE note, which reports in Rs crore).

### 1.1 Transparency / one-time-item red flags (AR vs results)

- **Chairman's headline FY25 EBITDA overstated ~9.2% by an unflagged one-time gain.** Chairman's
  Letter cites EBITDA of Rs 4,981.53 lakhs (AR, Chairman's Letter, line ~705 / p.9). Independently
  re-derived from the FY26 results (FY25 column, results 9ac5e08e p.11): PBT before exceptional
  1,694.49 + Finance 1,155.15 + Depreciation 1,711.65 = **4,561.29** — which matches the Board's
  Report EBITDA (AR line 2775) to the rupee; adding the Rs 420.25 lakh exceptional insurance receipt
  (results note 3, p.13; AR line 2784) yields 4,981.54 ≈ the Chairman's figure. Overstatement
  (4,981.53−4,561.29)/4,561.29 = **9.2%**, unflagged in the letter. VERIFIED and material.
- **Two further FY25 one-times embedded, disclosed only in results footnotes:** Rs 634 lakh state
  government grant inside Other Operating Revenue and Rs 194 lakh insurance inside Other Income
  (results notes 4-5, p.13). Confirmed.
- **At least four different FY25 EBITDA/operating-margin readings across the AR's own sections,
  none reconciled:** EBITDA 4,539.71 / margin 6.85% (MD&A financial-performance table, AR line
  2406-2407); EBITDA 4,561.29 → 6.92% on revenue 65,908.54 (Board's Report, AR line 2775);
  Operating Profit Margin 7.53% (MD&A ratio table, AR line 2430); Chairman's raw figure implies
  ~7.56%. Prior-year figure is likewise given as both 10.70% and 10.68% and 7.83% across sections
  (AR lines 860, 2430, 2407). VERIFIED.

### 1.2 Profitability / credit red flags

- **FY26 margin miss vs the AR's own stated priority.** Company Operating Margin fell to 5.49%
  (FY26) from 7.53% (FY25), and Q4 FY26 printed 5.22% (results 9ac5e08e p.14). The AR's stated
  priority was to "protect and enhance margins" (Chairman's Letter p.8; CEO Conversation p.6).
  CARE downgraded 19-Dec-2025, A-/BBB+ → BBB+/BBB, citing "continued subdued profitability margin
  in H1FY26" (rating p.1). VERIFIED.
- **CARE's 7% PBILDT negative-sensitivity floor already breached on a sustained basis.** "Reported
  PBILDT margin has continued to remain below the negative sensitivity threshold of 7% on a
  sustained basis in FY25 and H1FY26" (rating p.1); PBILDT margin 10.51% FY24 → 5.32% FY25 →
  5.33% H1FY26 (rating p.1). ASL therefore already sits inside CARE's own negative-rating-action
  zone on margin, not merely below the 8% positive trigger.
- **Debt/PBILDT above expectation; debt-funded, lone-expander capex.** 1.60x FY24 → 3.18x FY25 →
  2.80x H1FY26, "against the expectations of below 2.50x" (rating p.1-2); ~Rs 85 cr capex, ~Rs 60
  cr debt-funded, Pithampur completion targeted Dec-2026, only ~Rs 23 cr spent to H1FY26 (rating
  p.3). Independently corroborated in the results: standalone Capital WIP rose 1,129.50 → 3,961.73
  and FY26 PPE/CWIP additions were 4,510.08 vs FY25 1,997.85 (results 9ac5e08e p.15-16).
- **Imminent preference-share redemption, funding not yet evidenced.** NCRPS Rs 2,323.41 lakhs
  outstanding at 30-Jun-2026, "due for redemption during FY 2026-2027" (results 81847a21 p.8);
  CARE dates it 19-Aug-2026 and notes the company "plans to raise funds in near term" (rating
  p.2, Annexure-1). Cash accruals "tightly matched to meet the debt obligations" with "weak debt
  service coverage for FY26 and FY27" (rating p.2); FY26 DSCR 1.42 (results 9ac5e08e p.13).
- **~92% of FY25 revenue from top-5 customers, undisclosed in AR narrative** (rating p.3). Confirmed
  absent from Chairman's Letter/CEO Conversation/MD&A.
- **No quantified FY26 forward guidance anywhere in the AR** (no margin band, revenue %, capex
  quantum, debt target); Business Outlook is qualitative (MD&A p.41). Consistent with what I see.

### 1.3 Items I looked for and did NOT find as red flags (clearing checks)

- **Going concern / audit opinion:** unmodified audit opinion for FY26 (results 9ac5e08e p.6, 10,
  17) and clean limited review for Q1 FY27 (results 81847a21 p.3, 5). No modification, no

- **Q1 FY27 margin recovery is real and, on the numbers, company-specific quality:** Operating
  Margin 7.49% (results 81847a21 p.8), and materials cost/revenue improved to 84.0% (22,919.52 /
  27,287.01) from 87.0% a year earlier (18,775.75 / 21,589.92) — cost ratio fell even as B06 shows
  peers absorbing fresh West-Asia-war input inflation in the same quarter. Supports B06's reading.

### Detail on the three under-weightings (all MINOR)

- **#9 (MINOR).** B05 leaned on CARE's 8%-positive and 2.5x-debt thresholds but never states that
  CARE's 7% PBILDT *negative*-sensitivity threshold was already tripped on a sustained basis
  (FY25 5.32%, H1FY26 5.33%; rating p.1). That is the difference between "below where CARE wants it"
  and "already in downgrade-trigger territory," i.e. live further-downgrade risk. Decision survives
  (grade already C, narrative already "complicates"), so MINOR.
- **#10 (MINOR).** Cost of Materials Consumed rose 54,184.43 → 72,093.50 lakhs (+33%), outpacing
  revenue +30% (results p.11). A material share of the +30% "renewed growth momentum" B05 marked as
  an unqualified beat is raw-material-price passthrough at a compressing margin, not evidenced as
  volume-led. B06's 2A cross-read ("no peer describes a demand boom explaining ASL's +30%") is the
  partial catch; B05's own framing overstates the quality of the delivery.
- **#11 (MINOR).** B05 promise-delivery row 2 marks revenue growth "delivered" and writes "no
  explanation needed as this beat expectations," which glosses over that headline net profit fell
  to 1,267.83 from 1,499.00 lakhs and diluted EPS fell to 14.96 from 17.71 (results p.11). The
  optical decline is, however, driven by the FY25 one-times B05 itself flagged (420.25 + 634 + 194
  lakhs); on a clean basis FY26 earnings rose. So this is a presentational gap — B05 should have
  connected the one-times it flagged to the optical EPS drop — not a genuine hidden red flag. MINOR.
