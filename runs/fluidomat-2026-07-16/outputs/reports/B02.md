## PASS 3: PATTERN / CROSS-REFERENCE RE-READ

Read the Notes a third time hunting specifically for contradictions between sections, numbers that don't
reconcile across statements, deliberately thin disclosure, restated prior-year figures, post-balance-sheet
events, and going-concern language. Two genuine new findings surfaced that Pass 1 and Pass 2 did not catch
(both are cross-statement numerical checks, the kind only a dedicated pattern pass finds):

### NEW FINDING 1 — Cash Flow Statement disinvestment proceeds do not match Note 45c / Annexure 3 (unreconciled, three-way)

The Cash Flow Statement (p.84), Investing Activities section, shows **"Disinvestment in Subsidiary Company:
Rs 0.00 (FY25) / Rs 16.54 lakh (FY24)"**. But the same one-time event (wind-up of the wholly owned foreign
subsidiary, Fluidomat UK Private Limited) is disclosed **twice elsewhere in this Annual Report at a different
figure**:
- Note 45c (Related Party Transactions, p.103): "Disinvestment in Subsidiary Company ... Rs 0.00 (FY25) /
  Rs 13.70 lakh (FY24)."
- Annexure 3 to Board's Report (p.45, Foreign Exchange Earnings and Outgo): "Proceeds from closure of
  Foreign Wholly Owned Subsidiary ... Rs 0.00 (FY25) / Rs 13.70 lakh (FY24)."

Two of the three disclosures agree at Rs 13.70 lakh; the Cash Flow Statement stands alone at Rs 16.54 lakh —
an unreconciled **Rs 2.84 lakh gap** for the identical FY24 transaction. A benign explanation is possible
(e.g. the Rs 13.70 lakh figure is specifically labelled "foreign exchange earned ... actual inflows," so it may
capture only the FX-denominated portion of a larger, partly INR-settled disinvestment proceeds of Rs 16.54
lakh) but the Annual Report does not offer this or any reconciliation. 🟡 Watch — cross-statement numerical
inconsistency; immaterial in quantum (Rs 2.84 lakh) but exactly the kind of internal-consistency gap a
pattern re-read exists to catch, and it sits inside the same related-party/subsidiary-exit transaction that
Pass 1 already flagged for an unquantified P&L gain/loss on disposal.

### NEW FINDING 2 — Headline Basic/Diluted EPS in the P&L Statement is computed on Total Comprehensive Income, not on net profit (Ind AS 33 deviation)

Note 35 (EPS, p.101) discloses two separate EPS figures:
- "A. Net Profit ... Basic/Diluted EPS: Rs 45.10 (FY25) / Rs 26.50 (FY24)" — computed on PAT of Rs 2222.35
  lakh / Rs 1305.24 lakh over 49.27 lakh shares.
- "C. EPS on Total Comprehensive Income (A+B): Rs 45.15 (FY25) / Rs 28.23 (FY24)" — computed on Total
  Comprehensive Income (PAT + OCI) of Rs 2224.63 lakh / Rs 1390.70 lakh.

The **Statement of Profit & Loss itself (p.83)** and the **Board's Report financial-results table (p.25)**
both report the **"C" figure (45.15 / 28.23) as if it were the standalone "Basic EPS" and "Diluted EPS"** —
i.e., the headline EPS the market sees is inflated by one-time OCI (fair-value gains on the mutual fund
book, Rs 2.28 lakh FY25 / Rs 85.46 lakh FY24), which Ind AS 33 requires to be excluded from the EPS
numerator (EPS is defined on profit attributable to ordinary equity holders, not total comprehensive
income). The distortion is minor in FY25 (45.15 vs the "clean" 45.10, +0.1%) but **material in FY24: 28.23
vs the clean 26.50, a 6.5% overstatement**, entirely attributable to that year's large one-off mark-to-market
gain on the mutual fund portfolio (itself already flagged in Pass 1 as the driver of the 38.78%→3.72% ROI
collapse). 🟡 Watch — a genuine, two-year-consistent (so it is a policy choice, not a one-off slip) headline
EPS presentation that does not follow Ind AS 33's numerator definition, and which happens to flatter the
year with the largest treasury windfall. Feeds directly into the accounting-quality score below (disclosure
transparency / revenue-recognition-adjacent presentation conservatism).

No other pattern-level contradictions, restatements, or going-concern language were found beyond what Pass 1
and Pass 2 already surfaced (BGR Energy IBC omission from Note 36; stale independent directors retained on
the Note 45a related-party roster; the Rs 0.73 lakh FY24 FX-outgo mismatch between Note 38 and Annexure 3;
zero ECL on aged receivables; no Ind AS 19 actuarial gratuity disclosure). Note 47's "Previous year figures
have been regrouped and/or rearranged wherever considered necessary" remains a generic reclassification
statement, not a substantive restatement — no restated P&L or balance sheet line exists anywhere in the
document. Going-concern language: **NONE** anywhere (Directors' Responsibility Statement, Board's Report
p.28, and CARO Annexure A clause (xix), p.79, both give clean going-concern assessments; no material
uncertainty language).

1. Why is there zero ECL/doubtful-debt provision against the Rs 143.33 lakh of trade receivables aged beyond
   2 years (FY25), and what is the actual collection status of the 2-3yr and >3yr buckets specifically?
2. Does the Rs 47.77 lakh FY25 "Bad debts/liquidated damages/Tender/Vendor Registration fee" line in Note 33
   include any amount written off or discounted as part of the BGR Energy Systems settlement executed
   08.10.2024, and why was this multi-year IBC recovery action never disclosed in Note 36 (Contingent
   Liabilities)?
3. Why does the Cash Flow Statement show Rs 16.54 lakh of FY24 proceeds from the Fluidomat UK subsidiary
   disinvestment while Note 45c (Related Party Transactions) and Annexure 3 (Foreign Exchange Earnings and
   Outgo) both show Rs 13.70 lakh for the same transaction?
4. Why is the headline Basic/Diluted EPS reported in the Profit & Loss Statement and Board's Report computed
   on Total Comprehensive Income (including one-time OCI mark-to-market gains) rather than on net profit for
   the period, as Ind AS 33 requires?
5. What is the current status of the two unsatisfied MCA charges (id 90205616 and 90204976) relating to
   long-repaid loans, and what is management's timeline to locate the charge-holders and file Form CHG-4?

### E. NOTES-BASED RED FLAGS

- **Earnings management / presentation**: Headline Basic/Diluted EPS computed on Total Comprehensive Income
  rather than net profit, inflating the FY24 reported figure by 6.5% via a one-off treasury mark-to-market
  gain (Note 35 vs P&L Statement vs Board's Report).
- **Provisioning gap**: Zero ECL against aged trade receivables (2-3yr and >3yr buckets) in either year,
  despite Rs 143.33 lakh combined FY25 exposure — an aggressive-by-omission provisioning stance.
- **Undisclosed risk indicator**: A material, multi-year (2022-2024) insolvency-recovery action against a
  named customer (BGR Energy Systems) is completely absent from the Notes' contingent-liabilities section,
  surfacing only in the Board's Report.
- **Cross-statement inconsistency**: Rs 2.84 lakh unreconciled gap between the Cash Flow Statement and two
  other AR sections (Note 45c, Annexure 3) describing the identical subsidiary-disinvestment transaction; a
  separate Rs 0.73 lakh FY24 gap between Note 38 and Annexure 3 on FX outgo.
- **No aggressive revenue recognition or capitalisation practices identified.**

### F. ONE-LINE NOTES VERDICT

The notes reveal moderate accounting practices with two genuine cross-statement inconsistencies. Key
concern: zero ECL provisioning against aged receivables compounded by the undisclosed BGR Energy IBC
litigation and an EPS computation that runs through total comprehensive income rather than net profit. Key
strength: a genuinely debt-free, well-capitalised balance sheet with clean MSME payables and immaterial
contingent liabilities. Overall accounting quality: 6/10.
