Shallow Analysis Framework v1.0. Screened 2026-10-18 by Claude Code.
NSE RATNAVEER | BSE 543978 | ISIN INE05CZ01011 | Savli, Vadodara, Gujarat. Listed September 2023.
Corpus: `screens/corpus/RATNAVEER/` (ledger in `MANIFEST.md`). Every number cites a file and a page. Page numbers are PDF pages.
Market data is a screener.in service record dated 2026-10-18, not a filed document.

**Verdict: PASS.** A 10.5% margin stainless converter at 42.6x trailing earnings, with Rs 179 crore of self generated costs capitalised in two years, negative FY26 operating cash, and a promoter who took none of his own Rs 330 crore rights issue.

---

## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Ratnaveer buys stainless steel coil and scrap and sells finished stainless products from five plants in Gujarat. Finishing line sheets (polished, brushed and coated sheet) were 52.38% of FY26 revenue. Washers were 14.73%, sheet metal parts such as solar roof hooks 11.03%, scrap sales 8.98%, pipes and tubes 6.49%, and nuts and bolts 5.50% (filings/..._14052026144651_Investor_Presentation_Q4FY2026, p.21). FY26 revenue was Rs 1,068.74 crore and profit after tax Rs 64.31 crore (results/Ratnaveer-result-20260512.pdf, p.7). The money is a processing spread on stainless steel. About 75% of raw material is bought back to back against confirmed orders (credit-ratings/01-Infomerics.pdf, p.4).

**What is changing?** Two things are claimed. First, a shift inside stainless toward value added parts: fasteners, circlips, electropolished tubes. Second, an entry into an unrelated product, copper clad laminate (CCL), the base board of printed circuits. The Ministry of Electronics approved a Rs 338 crore CCL project on 30 March 2026 (screener/Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.16). Line 1 is due in November 2026 (filings/..._27072026092410_Press_release, p.1).

**Why now?** Capital has arrived. A Rs 185.50 crore QIP closed in December 2025 (filings/..._04122025234225_Intimation_on_Allotment, p.1). A Rs 329.99 crore rights issue closed in September 2026 at Rs 264 a share (filings/..._10092026220128_OBM_10092026, p.1-2). The share price is Rs 343 against a 52 week low of Rs 129 (market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record).

**What must be true for the thesis to work?** The CCL line must make saleable product on time and win PCB customers it does not yet have under contract. Management says it holds only "soft confirmations" (screener/Concall_May_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.12). The stainless business must also turn reported profit into cash. In FY26 it did not.

**What breaks it?** Earnings quality. In FY25 the company capitalised Rs 122.65 crore of self generated R&D, dies and tools. In FY26 it capitalised Rs 56.82 crore more, of which Rs 56.36 crore was raw material (screener/Annual_Report_2025.pdf, p.138; screener/Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.142). Those two years together equal 1.6 times the Rs 111.13 crore of profit reported. If those costs belong in the P&L, the margin story does not exist.

## 2. Business model and archetype

Six product lines, one reported segment: "manufacture and exporting of Stainless Steel Washers, Sheet Metal Products, Stainless Tube & Pipe and Stainless Steel Finished Steel" (screener/Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.139). The plants run scrap to ingot (melting, Waghodia), ingot to sheet (rolling, Vatva), then finishing, washers, fasteners and tubes at Savli (p.6).

Exports are small and shrinking. Sales outside India were Rs 55.46 crore in FY26, 5.2% of revenue, against Rs 49.91 crore in FY25 (results/Ratnaveer-result-20260512.pdf, p.8). Q1 FY27 exports fell to Rs 9.96 crore from Rs 15.44 crore a year earlier (results/Ratnaveer-result-20260724.pdf, p.5, read visually). Exports were about 20% of sales in FY23 (credit-ratings/04-Infomerics.pdf, p.3).

Price pass-through is real but partial. The MD says 80% to 85% of orders are covered back to back (screener/Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.20). The agency says about 75% (credit-ratings/01-Infomerics.pdf, p.4).

**Archetype: two, plus a third in construction.** Commodity converter for finishing sheet and scrap, 61% of revenue. Build-to-spec component maker for washers, fasteners and solar hooks, about 31%. Section 1B v3.7 Amendment 17 binds the converter slice. CCL, when it exists, is a second converter: copper foil, glass fabric and resin pressed into board.

## 3. Competitive advantages

Absent in finishing sheet and scrap. Narrow and real in washers. The company holds 3,300 washer SKUs and 3,500 fastener SKUs (screener/Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.39). Plant approvals and 15 to 20 year customer relationships give some stickiness, but sales are "order-to-order" with no long term contracts (screener/Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.6). Washer EBITDA per tonne rose only from Rs 24,709 in FY23 to Rs 26,011 in FY26 (Investor_Presentation_Q4FY2026, p.21). That is not pricing power. In CCL the claimed edge is first mover status and a 50% capital subsidy (screener/Concall_May_2026_Transcript.pdf, p.21), with machines and process bought from an unnamed Chinese vendor (p.7). Moat: narrow in washers, absent elsewhere, unproven in CCL.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

FROM the R1 to R2 boundary. Sixty one percent of revenue is converter sheet and scrap, ROCE is 12.3% (market-data, service record), and margin is 10.5%. TO a claimed R3 through value added parts, plus a new CCL engine.

Management's claim: "transitioning from a focused export manufacturer into a disciplined, diversified industrial conglomerate" (Investor_Presentation_Q4FY2026, p.3).

The climb has not shown in the mix. Value added products were 18% of revenue in H1 FY24 (credit-ratings/03-Infomerics.pdf, p.2). Management put them at 8% in H1 FY26 (screener/Concall_Nov_2025_Transcript.pdf, p.3). The definitions may differ; neither source defines the term. CCL is not a rung climb. It is a second converter business in a new industry, priced off copper and resin, with a subsidy. Ladder reading: **horizontal diversification, not a climb.**

## 8. Proof check

The triggers claim margin expansion from value added mix and solar power. Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin was 10.2%, the same as Q1 FY26. Q4 FY26 printed 11.4%. No upward step shows. Revenue grew 18.9% year on year in Q1 FY27 (results/Ratnaveer-result-20260724.pdf, p.4). Finance cost doubled to Rs 6.72 crore from Rs 3.41 crore. **Proof point absent.** CCL has no revenue to test.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **Capitalised self generated costs.** Rs 122.65 crore in FY25 and Rs 56.82 crore in FY26, certified by one individual, carried by the auditor as an Other Matter, not a qualification (Annual_Report_2026.pdf, p.90). Rs 56.36 crore of FY26's amount is raw material (p.142).
- **Negative operating cash flow** of Rs 48.41 crore in FY26; receivable days doubled.
- **Promoter took no rights shares** (inference, step 4) while holding cheaper warrants.
- **Equity dilution.** Shares rose from 5.30 crore in March 2025 to 8.39 crore in September 2026, 58% (AR 2026, p.46; filings/..._10092026220128, p.1). A 1.02 crore share preferential issue to non-promoters was withdrawn at the December 2024 EGM (AR 2026, p.76).
- **QIP allottees.** Six offshore VCC and PCC funds took 96% of the QIP (filings/..._04122025234225, p.3). Surfaced, not investigated.
- **Contingent tax disputes** of Rs 24.46 crore: customs Rs 7.23 crore, GST Rs 11.24 crore, income tax Rs 4.50 crore, sales tax Rs 1.48 crore (AR 2026, p.94, summed).
- **Recurring Emphasis of Matter** in limited reviews: tax determined "as estimated by the management" (results/Ratnaveer-result-20260724.pdf, p.3; filings/..._03112025161628, Q2 FY26).
- **Inconsistent filed numbers.** Employees: 211 permanent (AR 2026, p.54), 380 (p.12), 800+ (p.43), 2,000 (Concall_May_2026, p.3). Foreign exchange earned Rs 2.68 crore (AR 2026, p.58) against Rs 55.46 crore export sales. Q1 FY27 EPS printed Rs 10.36 against Rs 18.24 crore PAT on 7.16 crore shares (results/..._20260724, p.4). A warrant allottee is listed as the company itself (AR 2026, p.83).
- **Q4 FY25 finance cost Rs 0.04 crore** against Rs 12.68 crore for the year; the MD could not explain it on the call (Concall_Nov_2025, p.11).
- **Governance.** MD is also CFO. Audit trail not enabled at database level (AR 2026, p.141). Four secretarial lapses (p.51-52).
- **CCL cost drift.** Rs 338 crore (ECMS), Rs 351 crore plus Rs 46 crore solar plus Rs 250 crore working capital (Concall_May_2026, p.10), and Rs 472.34 crore (Gujarat approval).

## 10. Independent check: the credit rating

Infomerics upgraded the bank lines to IVR A-/Stable and IVR A2+ on 28 April 2026 (credit-ratings/01-Infomerics.pdf, p.1). It credits revenue growth, an operating cycle down to 107 days, and Total Debt/EBITDA of 1.67x in FY25. It expects EBITDA margin of 10% to 11% in FY26 and "similar" in FY27, with FY27 revenue above Rs 1,400 crore (p.2). The agency does not underwrite the 13% margin management guides. The rationale is standalone, built on company data, and does not assess the CCL project risk. The four rationales show a steady climb from IVR BBB in June 2023 (04-Infomerics.pdf, p.1). The rating predates the FY26 cash flow statement. Today's filings win.

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** No margin step. Value added share not shown to rise. CCL not yet producing.
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL.** Capitalisation of self generated costs is a three year practice, not a one time launch cost. Cash conversion is 42% of PAT over two years.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** Market capitalisation Rs 2,878 crore at 42.6x trailing earnings and 4.1x book (market-data-2026-10-18.md, service record). The company's own deck put the stock at Rs 179 in May 2026 (Investor_Presentation_Q4FY2026, p.24). The price already carries CCL.

**Posture: AVOID** (gap CLOSED plus STRUCTURAL overlay). The other reading: if the receivables are launch credit and the tooling is genuine, ugliness is ARTIFACT and the cell is PRICED NARRATIVE (TRAP). Neither cell is constructive.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

The bull case is fair to state. Revenue grew 79% in two years, the agency upgraded twice, guidance for FY26 was met, and a subsidised import substitution project is 60% built. The bear case carries the same evidence bar and more of it. Profit depends on a capitalisation policy whose size exceeds reported profit. Operating cash turned negative. The promoter sold the market Rs 330 crore of equity at Rs 264 and did not buy any, while holding warrants at Rs 159.25. The multiple already prices a business that does not yet sell a sheet.

**What would change the view**
1. CCL Line 1 invoices to named PCB customers by March 2027.
2. FY27 note 61A capitalisation falling below 10% of PAT, with operating cash flow above PAT.
3. Receivable days back under 40 in the H1 FY27 balance sheet.
4. A price that re-opens the gap with these three in hand.

No Role 1 valuation was run and no target price is set. This verdict decides only whether the name earns a full `/step1`, and it is not a position.
