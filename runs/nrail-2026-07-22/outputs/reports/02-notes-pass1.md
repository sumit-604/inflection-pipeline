## 1. ACCOUNTING POLICIES & CHANGES

- **Revenue recognition judgment**: Revenue recognised at point in time on dispatch/delivery, "based on lead time assessment for transfer of goods from one location to other location" (Note 2.3.1, p.104). This is a non-standard phrasing of the control-transfer judgment; no further elaboration of what "lead time assessment" entails. 🟡 Watch.
- **Depreciation useful lives vs Schedule II**: Management uses useful lives that diverge from Schedule II "based on technical evaluation and assessment" — e.g., Building 10–60 years, Plant & Machinery 10–40 years, Computers & Server 3–6 years (Note 2.6, p.106). Wide bands with no quantified P&L impact of the divergence disclosed. 🟡 Watch.
- **Capitalisation threshold**: NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT.
- **Impairment testing assumptions (growth/discount rates)**: General policy described (Note 2.11, p.111); no numeric assumptions disclosed because no goodwill and no explicit PPE impairment test detailed for FY25 (FY24's Unit III impairment of ₹792.46L is described without assumption detail — see Other Critical Notes).
- **ECL matrix conservatism**: Simplified Ind AS 109 lifetime-ECL approach (Note 2.10.1, p.108); loss allowance is flat at ₹1.89L in both FY25 and FY24 despite gross trade receivables growing from ₹8,697.75L to ₹18,644.55L (Note 10, pp.127–128) — allowance rate fell from ~0.022% to ~0.010% of gross receivables. 🟡 Watch (see Trade Receivables section for full detail).
- **Ind AS 116 discount rate, ROU, lease liability**: Discount rate is the Company's incremental borrowing rate, not numerically disclosed. ROU assets ₹1,915.57L (FY25) vs ₹2,261.42L (FY24); lease liabilities (current + non-current) ₹2,244.18L (FY25) vs ₹2,635.24L (FY24) (Note 2C p.119, Note 48 p.160). 🟢 Clean — reconciles.
- **First-time standard adoptions**: Ind AS 117 (Insurance Contracts) and Ind AS 116 amendment (Lease Liability in Sale and Leaseback) adopted April 1, 2024 with "no material impact" (Note 3, p.117). Ind AS 21 amendment (foreign-currency exchangeability) notified May 9, 2025, effective April 1, 2025 (next FY), impact "currently assessing" (Note 3B, p.117). 🟢 Clean, informational only.
