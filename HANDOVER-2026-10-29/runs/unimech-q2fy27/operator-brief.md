### 1. NARRATIVE

1. Unimech makes aerospace tooling and precision parts in Bengaluru. It sells about 95% abroad. It now also makes metal bellows through Hobel, bought in April 2026.
2. Q2 sales were Rs 123.00 Cr, up 98.4% on a year ago. The operating margin was 39.84%, against 29.92% a year ago.
3. Hobel drives much of that jump. Q2 last year had no Hobel. So the year-on-year growth is part bought, part organic.
4. The organic part is unclear. Management said Hobel made 30% of H1 sales. If that is right, sales without Hobel fell about 12% from Q1. The company gives no Hobel figures in its filings.
5. The firm order book fell for a second quarter, from Rs 280.3 Cr to Rs 266 Cr. The Rs 855 Cr headline adds Rs 589 Cr of agreements and award letters. Those do not oblige customers to buy.
6. The "8 times" order book growth in the press release compares the total book with last year's firm book. On the same firm basis, growth is 153%.
7. The new Honeywell agreement runs from April 2026 to November 2036. It sits in the Innomech subsidiary. It states no value and no volume. Honeywell "may issue" orders.
8. Cash turned weaker. H1 operating cash was 0.896 times profit, just under the 0.90 bar for the Pillar 2 upgrade. Net cash fell from Rs 479.71 Cr to Rs 65.45 Cr after the Hobel payment. Borrowings rose 43.6% to Rs 160.98 Cr.
9. All Rs 250 Cr of IPO money is now spent. CARE reports "no deviation", but the company's own statement says "yes", approved by shareholders in December 2025. CARE's total row also does not add up (Rs 153.30 Cr against Rs 247.80 Cr of object rows).
10. The Saudi joint venture needs more money. The deck shows about USD 30 Mn of joint investment. Unimech's remaining 51% share is about Rs 108 Cr. Net cash after the first tranche is about Rs 26 Cr. An equity raise is planned for early 2027.
11. Disclosure stays thin. CRISIL went unmentioned for a sixth call. Management now refuses segment margins as a policy. So the Hobel margin trigger cannot be tested before the FY27 annual report.
12. Watch the Q3 firm order book, due about February 2027. At Rs 300 Cr or more, the conversion story holds. Below Rs 266 Cr, the book shrinks for a third quarter.
