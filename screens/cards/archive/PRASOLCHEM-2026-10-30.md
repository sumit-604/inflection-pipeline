## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Prasol makes acetone-based and phosphorus-based specialty chemicals at Khopoli and Mahad in Maharashtra (Prosp, p.302). FY26 revenue was Rs 1,232.59 crore: acetone-based 42.75%, phosphorus-based 38.30%, other specialty 18.33% (Prosp, p.303). It sells over 150 products to 1,618 customers in five end markets: lubricants and mining, paints and inks, pharma, agro, and personal care (Prosp, pp.40, 302). Exports were 27.29% (Prosp, p.303). The margin is the spread between imported acetone or yellow phosphorus and the derivative price. Raw materials were 61.15% of FY26 expenses (Prosp, p.63).

**What is changing?** Three things. Mahad, the 2020 greenfield plant, moved from 12.72% use in FY24 to 44.09% in FY26 (Prosp, p.31). The mix moves from first derivatives toward secondary and tertiary ones (Call, p.5). A Rs 250 to 300 crore Phase 1 capex adds 60,000 tonnes to 98,644 tonnes by FY29 (Deck, p.22).

**Why now?** Q1 FY27 PAT was Rs 61.02 crore against Rs 24.34 crore a year earlier (Q1 res, p.6). The company listed at Rs 676 on 16-Sep-2026 (Q1 res, p.7). Management guides FY27 revenue of Rs 1,550 to 1,650 crore and EBITDA of Rs 240 to 250 crore (Call, p.8; Deck, p.6).

**What must be true?** The FY26 margin gain must come from mix and Mahad, not only from cheaper acetone. Acetone fell 26.89% to Rs 59.81 a kg in FY26 (Prosp, p.63). The Phase 1 lines must start on the stated dates. Receivables must stop growing faster than sales.

**What breaks it?** An acetone squeeze that the price list cannot pass on. Another fatal incident and a closure order, as at Mahad from Oct-2023 to May-2024 (Prosp, p.28). Or MIDC takes back the Mahad plot FS 26 under its notice to vacate (Prosp, p.30).

## 2. Business model and archetype

Volumes are not disclosed; capacity is. Khopoli ran 63,256 tonnes on 78,784 installed in FY26, 80.29% use (Prosp, p.31). Mahad ran 8,756 on 19,860 (Prosp, p.31). The issuer claims it was India's largest acetone importer in CY22 to CY25 and the only isophorone maker in India (Prosp, p.302, citing a CARE report it paid for).

Margin source, by the numbers. Gross margin rose 26.1%, 28.5%, 30.7% over FY24 to FY26 (Deck, p.26). Over the same years Mahad's loss before tax fell from Rs 22.77 crore to Rs 12.17 crore (Prosp, p.32). Acetone fell 26.89% in FY26 while yellow phosphorus rose 8.79% (Prosp, p.63). Part of the gain is cost, not price [INFERENCE].

Distribution adds low-margin volume. Purchases of stock-in-trade were Rs 175.09 crore in FY26 (Prosp, p.384). The chairman puts traded products at a 5% to 6% EBITDA (Call, p.19).

**Archetype: Commodity converter (Amendment 17 binds).** Spread, utilisation and input cycle drive the result; Q1 FY27 proved it. A **build-to-spec layer** sits on top: 1 to 4 year customer approvals and grade-specific streams (Deck, p.18; Call, p.8).

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Qualification and stickiness: present.** Eight disclosed accounts of 10 to 16 years grew from one product to four to nine (Prosp, p.58). Top 10 customers are only 23.68% of revenue (Prosp, p.57). **Scale in acetone sourcing: claimed.** The largest-importer claim is the issuer's (Prosp, p.302). **Pricing power: weak.** EBITDA margin was 6.9% to 11.3% over three years (Prosp, p.208). Q1 FY27 margin rose on an input spike, not on a price list (Call, p.9). **R&D: thin.** Spend was Rs 3.34 crore in FY26, 0.27% of income (Prosp, p.54). The VP R&D retired on 30-Sep-2026 (Q1 res, p.3).

**Verdict: a moat forming in qualification and breadth; none in price. The cost edge is claimed, not shown.**

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R2, cost-advantaged converter, low end.** Margin from sourcing scale and integration; EBITDA margin 7% to 11%; ROCE 13% to 22% in a falling-acetone year.

**TO: R3, value-added spec'd supplier.** Secondary and tertiary derivatives, grade streams built per customer, 13 products launched in three years (Call, pp.8, 10).

Management's claim, once: "we see sustainable gross margins in the range of about 30%, 32% on a normalized basis" (Call, p.9).

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point is two quarters of underlying margin above 13% on rising revenue. One quarter exists.** Q1 FY27 underlying EBITDA margin was 14.8% on revenue up 35.7% (derived; Q1 res, p.6). Underlying PAT was about Rs 42 crore, double the FY26 quarterly average of Rs 20.78 crore [INFERENCE: one-off taxed at the Q1 rate of 26.97%].

**What the quarters do not prove.** Q4 FY26, the quarter before, went the other way: 9.6% margin and PAT down 18% on Q1 FY26 (Q1 res, p.6). The separating observation is Q2 FY27: underlying EBITDA near Rs 60 crore with no stock gain.

## 12. Verdict card

**WATCH.**
