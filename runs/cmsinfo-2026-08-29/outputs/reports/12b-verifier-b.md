## PART 1 — INDEPENDENT RED-FLAG LIST (from transcripts alone, with anchors)

### R-1. Guidance cut on revenue and raised on margin in the same breath (Aug-2026)
FY27 services revenue goal moved from Rs2,700-2,800 Cr (May) to Rs2,650-2,750 Cr (Aug);
EBITDA margin raised from 25-26% (May) to ~27% (Aug). Rajiv Kaul, opening remarks:
"we are setting our full year services revenue goal at INR2,650 crores to INR2,750
crores against the INR2,700 crores to INR2,800 crores earlier... we are raising our
EBITDA margin guidance to about 27% from the 25% to 26% we had guided in May." (Aug-2026,
p.2/17). ACKNOWLEDGED and quantified by management, so this is disciplined disclosure,
not a covert walkback. Load-bearing.

### R-2. ATM-count target silently dropped
74,000-75,000 ATMs by March/April 2026 set twice (Anush, Nov-2025 p.3/20: "74,000 to
75,000 ATMs... by March"; Rajiv, Feb-2026 p.13/18: "74,000, 75,000 ATMs by the end of
March or April"). By Aug-2026 the number is "about 70,000 ATMs" cited in passing (Anush,
p.9/17). Never delivered, never acknowledged as a miss.

### R-3. DSO-normalisation promise silently dropped
Anush, Feb-2026 (p.3/18): "DSOs are getting streamlined and should be back to normal
levels by end of March." Never revisited in May or Aug. No analyst re-asked. Directly
relevant to cash conversion, the thesis spine.

### R-4. Segment reporting retired exactly as the cash-management line weakened
Cash-management revenue fell from ~Rs417 Cr peak to ~Rs384 Cr (Akshat Hariya / Anush,
Feb-2026 p.13/19). In the same call management moved to 3 "platforms" where, per Rajiv,
"splitting a margin is not possible" (Feb-2026) and later "I do not think we even have
the live numbers" (May-2026 p.12/20). The lens that revealed the decline was removed as
the decline was surfacing.

### R-5. SBI scope halved, disclosed only under a direct analyst question
Nov-2025 framed the SBI RFP as "concluded... incremental INR500 crores" (Rajiv, p.5/20).
Feb-2026 opening reframed it as "almost 10,000 ATMs as the sole eligible bidder" that
"would have been INR100 crores to INR125 crores of incremental revenue" (Rajiv, p.2/18).
Only when Krushi Parekh asked directly did Anush confirm the RFP was scrapped and rebid
and "we got about 5,000 of those volumes with rest going to other industry participants"
(Feb-2026, p.11/19). Reactive, not proactive, disclosure of a scope halving.

### R-6. Employee-cost jump answered evasively (Feb-2026)
Abhishek Chauhan: "dramatic increase in the employee benefit expense this quarter of
about INR18 crores... what was the specific reason?" (Feb-2026, p.8/19). Pankaj answered
by repeatedly redirecting to "add both the service and security and employee cost"
(p.8-9/19), not the specific line asked. The analyst pushed back: "service and security
is different from employee benefit." The granular cost question was never cleanly
answered.

### R-7. Buyback executed at Rs340 then defended non-committally as the stock fell
Feb-2026 Rajiv on buyback: "This is not a decision which the management team makes... I
don't think I could forecast this at all" (p.17-18/19). Buyback then approved Rs168 Cr at
Rs340 (May-2026, p.6/20; executed 49.39 lakh shares, Aug-2026 p.4/17). By Aug an
individual investor (Dhruv) noted the stock at "10x to 12x" and a weak reaction; Rajiv:
"I don't think you should think in terms of the buyback as a reward" (Aug-2026, p.6/17).
Capital-return posture stays vague; the executed buyback is underwater.

### R-8. Currency-supply shock is entirely self-reported
The Rs25 Cr Q1 FY27 hit (Rs18 Cr BLA + Rs7 Cr cash-logistics) is stated by three CMS
voices (Rajiv/Anush/Pankaj, Aug-2026 p.2-4/17) and by no independent source in the
corpus. When Praveen Kumar raised reverse causality ("banks chose to supply lesser to
ATMs... where they saw lesser potential"), Anush rebutted it as no "conspiracy theory"
(p.7-8/17) but the rebuttal is itself management-sourced.

### R-9. Two-year forecasting-miss streak, self-admitted, no named fix
Rajiv, Aug-2026 (p.7/17): "FY22, '23, '24 were 3 years where we met or exceeded all
forecast... '25, '26, I think we have come under." Candid, but when asked what changed in
the forecasting process he offered only "we will put our best foot forward" (p.7/17). A
repeated miss with no visible remedy against guidance-based value.

### R-10. MS&Tech segment revenue base does not reconcile Nov to Feb
Nov-2025 (Pankaj, p.4/20): "Managed Services & Tech revenue increased by 5% sequentially
from INR258 crores to INR271 crores" — i.e. Q2 FY26 MS&Tech = Rs271 Cr. Feb-2026 (Pankaj,
p.3/19): "Managed Services & Technology is up 18% quarter-on-quarter from INR216 crores
to INR254 crores" — i.e. the Q2 base is stated as Rs216 Cr. Rs271 Cr vs Rs216 Cr for the
same quarter, a Rs55 Cr gap, with no reconciliation offered. Most likely a re-segment
re-basing (the 3-platform switch happened between these calls), but management never said
so, and the Tech growth line is the re-rating engine of the thesis. Numbers-do-not-tie
item. (Numerical existence is Verifier A's gate; I flag the unreconciled disclosure.)

### R-11. Three different Q3 FY26 EBITDA-margin figures inside one call (Feb-2026)
Pankaj in the same financial update: "EBITDA margins expanded by 160 basis points, moving
from 23.9% in Q2 to 25.5% in Q3" (p.4/19); then "In FY2025, our EBITDA margin was 26.1%
which has dropped to 22.8% in this quarter. However, in Q3, we should see that the overall
EBITDA margin expanded by 100 basis points to 24.5%" (p.5/19). Q3 EBITDA margin is stated
as 25.5%, 24.5%, and 22.8% within one commentary, without a stated basis for each.
Disclosure-clarity red flag; precise arithmetic belongs to Verifier A.

### R-12. Headline EBITDA raise masks a sharp EBIT-margin compression (Aug-2026)
Management led Q1 FY27 with EBITDA margin "expanding 170 basis points... to 27.2%"
(Pankaj, p.4/17) and Rajiv reinforced "amongst the highest margins of any business
services company in India" (p.5/17). The EBIT line tells the opposite story: "EBIT for Q1
was INR32 crores at 10.3% margin versus 14.1% a year ago" (Pankaj, p.4/17), a ~380 bps
YoY EBIT compression from depreciation on the FY26 capex. The metric chosen for the
guidance raise (EBITDA) is precisely the one that excludes the cost that is falling to the
bottom line. Selective-metric framing on the single most load-bearing claim.

### R-13. FY26 capex crept up ~17% while revenue guidance was being cut
Nov-2025: "Full year estimate of around INR300 crores" (Pankaj, p.4/20). Feb-2026:
"INR300 crores to INR325 crores" (Pankaj, p.14/19). May/Aug-2026: actual Rs350 Cr ("FY26
saw peak capex of INR350 crores," Aug-2026 p.4/17). The initial Rs300 Cr guide became
Rs350 Cr with no acknowledgement of the overrun, in the same period services-revenue
guidance was trimmed.

### R-14. Provisioning guidance walked up within one quarter
Nov-2025 (Pankaj, p.8/20): normal + ECL provisioning "in the range of 4% revenue in line
with our historical trend." Feb-2026 (Pankaj, p.9/19): "this year, we are expecting the
same to be range of around 4.25% to 4.5% of the revenue," against a disclosed FY25 of
3.7%. A quiet upward re-guide on a receivables-stress proxy, not flagged as a change.

### Lower-weight observations (logged, not scored):
- Same-store retail framing softened from "volumes rebound meaningfully" (Feb-2026 p.3/19)
  to "same-store volumes have held constant" (Aug-2026 p.3/17).
- "Transaction fee model is dead" rhetoric (Rajiv, May-2026 p.3/20) sits against a residual
  12% transaction-linked BLA estate that then drove the Q1 miss (Anush, Aug-2026 p.9/17).
- Rs650 Cr Q1 entry run-rate given "almost 95% certainty" (Rajiv, Feb-2026 p.7/19) came in
  at Rs625 Cr, "INR25 crores short" (Rajiv, Aug-2026 p.2/17).

## PART 4 — ASSESSMENT

No CRITICAL. No MISSED item rises to thesis-changing weight, and no missed item is a
repeated (2+ quarter) evasion that B05 failed to see. The heavy, decision-relevant red
flags (DSO silence, ATM-count drop, segment-reporting retirement, reactive SBI-scope
disclosure, self-reported currency shock, forecasting-miss streak) are all caught and
correctly weighted by B05, and B06 independently hardens the DSO flag.

The one genuinely under-weighted item is R-12: B05 grades the cut-revenue/raise-margin
narrative (LBF-3) as "strong" evidence for a quality mix-shift, but the raised metric is
EBITDA while EBIT compressed ~380 bps YoY on depreciation. A full-metric read makes the
"quality" claim less clean than B05 presents. This is a MAJOR calibration note for Stage
11, not an invented or omitted signal.

Two clean MISSES are both low weight: an unreconciled MS&Tech revenue base (Rs271 vs
Rs216, most plausibly a resegmentation) and three conflicting Q3 EBITDA figures inside the
Feb call. Both are numerical-disclosure items where Verifier A holds the source-fidelity
gate.

Credibility grade: B05 assigns overall B. I CONCUR, at the low end of B. The DSO
contradiction surfaced by B06 (CMS-specific deterioration against improving peers, with
AGSTRA's receivables-then-CIRP precedent) plus the EBITDA-over-EBIT framing argue against
anything higher than B, but management's repeated, named self-admissions and quantified
bridges keep it out of C.
