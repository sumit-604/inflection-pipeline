# Step 1 business brief: Supreme Power Equipment Ltd (NSE SME: SUPREMEPWR)

Run date 2026-10-06. Screener: https://www.screener.in/company/SUPREMEPWR/consolidated/ (HTTP 200). CMP Rs 239, mcap Rs 598 Cr, trailing P/E 28.6 (screener page, 2026-10-06). This brief is COMPANY MEMORY, never anchored evidence.

1. What it sells: power and distribution transformers from Thirumazhisai (Chennai), Tamil Nadu; founded 1994, listed NSE Emerge Dec-2023; 21,367 units supplied to date. (Q1 FY27 press release, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/SPEL_14082026201753_SPELQ1FY27PressRelease.pdf p3-4)
2. Scale: FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 181.64 Cr, EBITDA Rs 33.29 Cr, PAT Rs 20.44 Cr (same press release p4). Q1 FY27 total income Rs 48.31 Cr (+37.33%), EBITDA Rs 8.89 Cr (+32.02%), PAT Rs 4.90 Cr (+10.04%) (same, p2).
3. Order book Rs 590.06 Cr on 13-Aug-2026, about 3.2x FY26 revenue; Q1 FY27 inflow 10 orders, Rs 195.64 Cr, 7-17 month tenors (same, p2-3).
4. The engine: Kannur (Chennai) second plant, commercial Feb-2026, takes capacity from 2,500 to 9,000 MVA and rating from 25 MVA/132 kV to 200 MVA/220 kV; Unit 1 at 70-80% utilisation; "revenue potential at full capacity 500-550 Cr" (Q1 FY27 presentation, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/SPEL_17082026131115_InvestorPresentationQ1FY27.pdf, extracted text lines 672-698).
5. Mix shift: Q1 FY27 orders are mostly 20 MVA power transformers plus first 160 MVA/220 kV (Rs 23.30 Cr) and 112.5 MVA/330 kV (Rs 13.50 Cr) units; EPC and private customers, Hyderabad and Karnataka heavy (same press release p3).
6. Margin source: raw material about 70% of cost (copper, CRGO, oil); escalation clauses on orders over six months, about 50% of the book per CRISIL (CRISIL rationale 20-Mar-2026, https://www.crisil.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/SupremePowerEquipmentLimited_March%2020_%202026_RR_384597.html).
7. Credit: CRISIL upgraded to BBB/Stable and A3+ (from BBB-/A3) on 20-Mar-2026; Rs 80 Cr facilities incl. Rs 40 Cr ICICI term loan; GCA 210-289 days, receivables 111-211 days over three fiscals (same CRISIL rationale; NSE filing SPEL_20032026151559_CreditRatingupdate.pdf p3).
8. Group structure: Danya Electric Company, 90% held, consolidated by CRISIL; the 25-Sep-2026 AGM approved "material related party transactions between the Company and Danya Electric Company (Partnership firm)" (AGM proceedings, https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/SPEL_25092026154034_ProceedingsofAnnualGeneralMeeting2026.pdf p3, item 6).
9. Ownership: promoter group 57.16%, no pledge; FPI 0.58%, domestic institutions 0.04% (NSE SHP 31-Mar-2026 XBRL).
10. Governance events: non-executive director D. Krishna Iyer resigned 13-Aug-2026 ("other commitments"); MD and WTD re-appointed for 36 months; AGM also raised borrowing and Section 185/186 loan/guarantee limits (NSE XBRL 13-Aug-2026; AGM proceedings p3-4).

Peers chosen (economic engine: small Indian transformer makers scaling into higher MVA/kV classes on a capacity add, private/EPC order mix):
- INDOTECH (https://www.screener.in/company/INDOTECH/): Indo Tech Transformers, Chennai power transformers up to 220 kV, mid-size capacity ramp; closest product and geography match. No transcripts on screener.
- SHILCHAR (https://www.screener.in/company/531201/, BSE-only): small high-margin power/distribution transformer maker with private and renewable customers; the margin and working-capital benchmark for what this business could become.
- DANISH (https://www.screener.in/company/DANISH/): Danish Power, recently listed small transformer maker, renewable and private mix, similar scale and listing age.
Alternates not taken: Voltamp (mature, large), Transformers & Rectifiers (much larger, 400 kV+).

Sector cap row: "Cables / Industrial products" 25x (section-1b chunk 05, line 38); same row the KECL run used for transformers and switchgear.

LOAD-BEARING FACTS (first verification priority for every stage):
1. Guidance versus delivery: FY27 revenue guide Rs 275-300 Cr in the press release and presentation versus "250-300" reported from the 17-Aug call; FY26 guided 225 then 200 then 190 against actual 181.64; Q1 FY27 guided 50-60 against 48.23. Verify each against the transcripts and results.
2. Danya Electric: 90% "stake" (CRISIL) in an entity the AGM notice calls a partnership firm, with material RPT approval; standalone revenue reported above consolidated. Verify the legal form, the inter-company sales, the minority share and who holds the other 10%.
3. Cash conversion: FY26 CFO 25.90 against capex about 57 and borrowings 18.74 to 49.98; inventory 62.10 and payables 62.20. Verify from the FY26 AR cash flow and balance sheet; test whether the working-capital gain is payables-funded.
4. Operating leverage: Q1 FY27 revenue +37% but PAT +10%; employee cost 1.92 versus 0.81; Kannur at 20-25% utilisation. Verify the cost lines and whether margin dilution is ramp (temporary) or price (structural).
