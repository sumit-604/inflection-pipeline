## Monitorables and triggers

1. Q4 FY26 credit cost against the 1.1% guardrail. Watch the credit cost to average assets print in the quarterly results; a figure at or below 1.1% confirms the reset is behind, a breach reopens it. Tests the core of the turnaround thesis.
2. FY27 credit cost and cost to income guidance at the Q4 FY26 call. Watch whether an actual FY27 number is issued or deferred again; these are the two hardest promises, already pushed once. Tests management follow through and the emerging moat's F2 grade.
3. Gold AUM growth versus gold tonnage growth. Watch tonnage in the investor presentation against headline gold AUM; divergence means growth is gold price, not volume. Tests the quality of the core engine and the peer contradiction above.
4. Gold mix ceiling. Watch whether gold stays inside 45 to 49% of book or drifts toward unconstrained. Tests concentration into a price cyclical product.
5. Mortgage and ST LAP Stage 3 plus ST LAP disbursals. Watch mortgage segment Stage 3 (currently 3.4 to 3.8% versus gold 0.1 to 0.3%) and whether ST LAP disbursals rise above Rs 208 Cr. Tests whether the small ticket LAP repair is real, not just held flat.
6. CRAR and Tier 2 capital. Watch for a resumed CRAR figure and a Tier 2 raise in Q4 FY26; both disclosures dropped in Q3 as leverage rose (D and E ratio 3.78 to 3.99). Tests capital adequacy against 34.7% AUM growth.
7. Stage 3 cross note reconciliation. Watch whether the FY26 annual report carries an explicit bridge; three different Stage 3 gross loan totals in the FY25 filing did not tie out. Tests disclosure transparency on the single most important credit metric.
8. C suite stability. Watch for any further senior exit within 12 months of the reset. Tests whether the rebuilt leadership can execute the stated three year plan.
