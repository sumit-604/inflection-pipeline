**Verdict: PASS.** A Rs 10.5 crore formulations maker whose plant lost money in FY26. The listed shell is the vehicle for a group merger that leaves its holders 8.55% of the merged company. The market pays 485 times trailing profit.

## Business Understanding Narrative

**What does it do, and how does it make money?** Zenith makes pharmaceutical formulations at a WHO-cGMP plant in Changodar, Ahmedabad: tablets, capsules, oral liquids, dry powder and compressible granules (Scheme, p.5). FY26 revenue was 1,052.22; exports 569.13, domestic 480.28, to 11 countries (AR26, p.77; Scheme, p.5). One customer, ZORIAK C.A., took 28% (AR26, p.77). Profit before tax was 1.60 and after tax 0.51 (AR26, p.53). Other income of 41.44 was 26 times profit before tax (AR26, p.71). The plant loses money; fixed deposits and loans earn it.

**What is changing?** On 14-Jul-2026 the board approved a merger of Achyut Healthcare Ltd, a group company, into Zenith. Achyut holders get 119 Zenith shares for every 50 Achyut shares; Zenith's share count goes from 5,37,39,000 to 62,81,68,660 (Scheme, p.7). Achyut brings net worth of 3,506.12 against Zenith's 742.42 (Scheme, p.3), and a plant under construction at Bhat aimed at EU GMP (Scheme, pp.4-5).

**Why now?** Nothing in the operating numbers says now. Revenue fell three years running: 1,447.24, 1,132.97, 1,052.22 (AR25, p.18; AR26, p.21). Operating margin was negative 3.79% in FY26 (AR26, p.79). The timing is the group's: Achyut reached the main board in January 2026 and the scheme followed in July (ACHYUT card, memory).

**What must be true?** The Bhat plant must start and win an EU GMP certificate nobody holds yet. The exchange ratio must be fair to Zenith's public holders, who will own 8.55% of the merged company (derived from Scheme, p.7). And the existing plant must stop losing money.

**What breaks it?** The plant slips again. The ratio moves value from Zenith's 38,000 public holders to the group. Or the business keeps paying 12% of sales as commission and writing off 2.6% as bad debts (AR26, p.72).

## 2. Business model and archetype

One reportable segment, "Pharmaceuticals Business" (Q2 res, p.2); the Q2 and Q3 FY26 results called it "Pharmaceuticals Trading" (H1-26 res, p.2; Q3 res, p.2). Manufacturing dominates: materials consumed 428.71 against traded goods of 68.52 (AR26, p.53).

Where the money goes, FY26 (AR26, pp.53, 72): materials, traded goods and inventory change 484.15, 46% of revenue; employees 180.23, 17%, of which directors 39.00; commission 121.54, 11.6%; transport, testing and promotion 80.12; bad debts 27.18; depreciation 25.84. Revenue less all expenses is negative 39.84 before other income (derived). Other income of 41.44 is interest on deposits 7.54, interest on loans 16.75, duty drawback 4.19 and a currency gain 6.67 (AR26, p.71). The MD&A says plant registrations exist in Kenya, Nigeria and Uzbekistan and "export orders are started"; it also says sales and margin "decreased substantially" under competition (AR26, p.44).

**Archetype: Outsourcing partner, weak form.** A generic formulator with no brand and one 28% customer. The lever set is client concentration, wallet share, capacity fill and price per unit. None is disclosed beyond the one customer.

## 3. Competitive advantages

**Absent.** The company says so: "Very high competition from large and small and new comers in the Pharma field it is very difficult to stand in stable position" (AR26, p.44). Many products sit under DPCO price control (AR26, p.44). Commission at 11.6% of sales and bad debts at 2.6% are the price of selling. ROCE was 3.8% in FY26 and 7.1% in FY25 (AR26, p.79). A WHO-cGMP plant and three overseas registrations are a licence to compete, not a moat.

## 6. The transition and the quality ladder

**FROM: R1, commodity price-taker, with an R0 optic.** No pricing power, ROCE below the cost of capital, and profit from interest, not the plant. An operating engine exists, so R1 not R0, but it earns nothing.

**TO: R3, value-added spec'd supplier.** The mechanism is Achyut's EU GMP plant, which "will unlock new market opportunities in the regulatory markets with rigorous compliance standards" (Scheme, p.5). Management's claim for Zenith, once: the merger leverages the "brand value, reputation and long-standing manufacturing expertise of the Transferee Company" (Scheme, p.5).

Ladder check. Two rungs, through a plant another company has not finished and a certificate nobody holds. Base rate is one rung per two to three years; a multi-rung claim is itself a flag. Zenith's letterhead already lists "Unit 2: Survey No. 1078, Bhat" (Scheme, p.1), Achyut's plant site (Scheme, p.4). The two share a site before they share a balance sheet.

## 8. Proof check

**The proof point would be a rising export line or a manufacturing margin above zero. Both are absent.** Export sales fell to 569.13 from 686.32 and the top customer to 297.28 from 332.95 (AR26, p.77). Operating margin went from negative 1.34% to negative 3.79% (AR26, p.79).

The last two quarters cut both ways. Q1 FY27 revenue of 343.27 was the best quarter since Q4 FY25 (Q2 res, p.2; FY26 res, p.7). Q2 FY27 then fell to 201.14, 11% below Q2 FY26 and the lowest quarter in the corpus, with PBT of 1.23 (Q2 res, p.2). H1 FY27 PAT beat H1 FY26, but FY26 ended at 0.51 after a 34.20 Q4 loss (AR26, p.53; FY26 res, p.7). The half-year is not proof of the year.

## 9. Flags from the documents

- **The ratio dilutes Zenith's holders to 8.55%.** 5,37,39,000 shares become 62,81,68,660 (Scheme, p.7). Book value per share is Rs 1.38 at Zenith and Rs 1.45 at Achyut (derived from Scheme, pp.3, 7), near parity; the ratio gives 2.38 Zenith shares per Achyut share. The valuation report is not held (Scheme, pp.3-4). Achyut's net worth is mostly a plant under construction (ACHYUT card, memory).
- **Rs 1.63 crore lent to an unnamed borrower**, 22% of net worth, at about 10% (AR26, pp.67, 71). The board's report points to "Note 4 and 5" for section 186 particulars; those are intangibles and PPE (AR26, pp.22, 66-67). Counterparty NOT FOUND.
- **One customer at 28%** (AR26, p.77).
- **Selling costs eat the margin.** Commission 121.54, promotion 23.84, bad debts 27.18 in FY26; bad debts 26.90 in FY25 and 54.99 in FY24 (AR26, p.72; AR25, p.66). Three years of write-offs, 109.07, exceed three years of PAT, 27.56 (derived).
- **Director pay 24 times PBT**, raised in 2026 with a 100% increment clause, and **a Rs 100 crore related-party window** passed by 5.8% of public shares (step 4).
- **A contradiction on controlled substances.** The scheme says Zenith makes "Psychotropic and Narcotics Products" (Scheme, p.5). The secretarial auditor says it "is not engaged in manufacture of any ... Psychotropics substances" (AR26, p.41).
- **Secretarial audit observations.** Board decisions by oral vote with no record; no documented board evaluation; POSH annual return not filed; promoters' annual SAST disclosure not filed (AR26, pp.40-41). Audit trail not enabled at database level (AR26, p.78). Note 24 shows exports of 560.13 that do not sum; Note 42 shows 569.13 that does (AR26, pp.71, 77).
- **Auditor.** Doshi Doshi & Co, unmodified, no contingent liabilities, no litigation (FY26 res, p.4; AR26, pp.46, 73). The same firm audits Achyut (ACHYUT card, memory). **Valuation:** PE 485, 2.56 times book (MD).

## 11. Posture: the Transition Decision Matrix

- **Proof gate: NOT FIRED.** Falling exports, negative operating margin, no plant output anywhere in the group (step 8).
- **Ugliness: STRUCTURAL-FEATURE.** Three years of falling revenue, a 12% commission load and recurring bad debts in a DPCO-exposed generic business. Nothing marks these as temporary costs of a climb.
- **Recognition gap: CLOSED.** PE 485 and 2.56 times book for 0.24% on equity (MD; AR26, p.79).

**Posture: VALUE-TRAP RISK, with the gap-CLOSED plus STRUCTURAL overlay, which maps to AVOID.** The price assumes a merged company that does not yet exist, built around a plant that does not yet run.

## 12. Verdict card

**PASS.**

No operating transition is visible. The listed entity is about to become 8.55% of something else, on a ratio the corpus cannot test. A `/step1` here would study a shell. The only question worth time is the post-merger entity, after sanction and a first Bhat manufacturing quarter.
