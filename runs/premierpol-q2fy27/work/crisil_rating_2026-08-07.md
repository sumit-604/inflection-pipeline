## Financials and liquidity stated by Crisil (p1, p2)
- FY26 operating income 297.0 Cr (FY25 264.5 Cr); reported PAT 31.9 Cr (26.0 Cr); PAT margin 10.7% (9.83%); adjusted debt to adjusted net worth 0.1x (0.2x); interest coverage 57.4x (32.6x) (p2 table).
- FY26 operating margin 15.6%, from 14.4% in FY25, on better realisations and a higher share of value-added products (p1).
- Net worth about 146 Cr at 31-03-2026; NCAAD 1.9x; TOL/ANW about 0.4x (p1).
- Net cash accruals about 35 Cr in FY26 against repayments under 3 Cr; projected 35-40 Cr a year. Repayments: about 3 Cr in FY27, nil in FY28, about 2 Cr in FY29 (p2).
- Average bank limit use about 22% over the 13 months to June 2026 (p2).
- Free cash and bank balance of more than 25 Cr at 31-03-2026; current ratio about 2.4x (p2).

## Plans and outlook (p1, p2)
- "Debt-funded expansion at the Chennai facility" is under way; TOL/ANW is expected to stay at a similar level (p1). Liquidity is expected to stay comfortable despite the "proposed debt-funded capex for the Chennai expansion" (p2).
- FY27 revenue projected to grow 10-11%, on expanded capacities, new products and demand (p1).
- Operating margin expected at about 14-16%, with the ability to largely pass on raw material price moves (p1).
- Upgrade triggers: sustained volume-led growth with operating margin near 15%; efficient working capital (p2).
- Downgrade triggers: revenue decline or operating margin below 12-13%; a stretched working-capital cycle or large debt-funded capex that weakens liquidity (p2).

## Company facts (p2)
- Incorporated 1992, operations from 1993. Plants at Sahibabad AND Sikandarabad (Uttar Pradesh). Products: PVC floor coverings, artificial leather, geomembranes, PVC films and sheeting.
- Promoters: Mr A N Goenka and Mr Amitabh Goenka.
