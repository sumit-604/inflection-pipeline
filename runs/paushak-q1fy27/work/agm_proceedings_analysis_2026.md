**1. Auditor-transition flag (Q1 F4-a) -> RESOLVED to benign.** The Q1 review flagged
the CNK transition as AMBIGUOUS (reason/timing undisclosed in the Reg 33 Other-Matter
para) and raised it as management Question 5. The AGM confirms CNK & Associates LLP is

the FY26 financials they audited were adopted as ordinary business item 1 (L98). Combined
with the FY26 AR memory (Sec 139 mandatory rotation, unmodified opinion), this is an
orderly, board- and shareholder-sanctioned rotation. The governance-yellow around the
auditor change **retires.** Question 5 substantially answered.

**2. "Inter alia" board-business concern (Q1 F13-a) -> SOFTENED.** The Q1 board outcome
used "inter alia approved" while itemizing only results, against a live management change
(COO exit / Jain Parkash WTD). The AGM agenda is **fully routine** — 3 ordinary + 2
special resolutions, no capital raise, no ESOP, no M&A, no related-party mega-item, no
surprise governance resolution (L96-108). Nothing material was being hidden behind
procedural language; the management-change items were handled at board level (normal) and
did not require a shareholder resolution. The concern **de-escalates** (does not fully
vanish, since the Q1 board minutes themselves are not public).

**3. Dividend maintained (L99-100).** A FY26 dividend was declared and adopted even while
the company is absorbing MPP-8 capex and carrying new debt (Q1 finance cost > all of
FY26). Modest positive on capital discipline and promoter alignment. Amount NOT in this
document (in the AGM notice / results).

family) re-appointed by rotation (L101) and his FY26 commission put to shareholders via
Special Resolution (L105-106) — per FY26 memory this is the ~Rs 135 Lacs commission, down
~10% YoY. Correct process. He also fielded all member Q&A (L90-92); no independent
executive (MD/CEO) is named as answering — consistent with the thin senior-executive bench
observed after the COO exit, but unremarkable for an AGM.

**5. Low engagement texture.** 92 members, no Chat Box questions, 43-minute meeting.
Consistent with the low-institutional-interest / informed-avoidance observation from
memory. Minor, not decision-relevant.

**6. SILENCE — no operational content.** The proceedings carry NO commentary on the RM
