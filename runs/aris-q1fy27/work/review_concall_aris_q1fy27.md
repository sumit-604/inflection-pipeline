Cash-conversion cap **stays INDETERMINATE**. **Our follow-up:** "Give absolute trade receivables and the >6-month bucket in ₹ cr at 30-Jun; and reconcile 66 vs 97."

**Exchange 2 — Credit losses / zero ECL (Augustia D, CFO).** Q: nature of past credit + derivative losses; will credit losses recur? Insurance is a genuine new mitigant but unverifiable. **Follow-up:** "What changed between Q4 (4.46 cr) and Q1 (nil) — is a catch-up charge coming in Q2, and what share of the book is actually insured?"

**Exchange 3 — Net cash → net debt bridge (Ishid Desai, CFO).** Q: at 31-Mar we were ~65 cr **net cash**, now guiding to 75-80 cr **net debt** — what gives confidence? A: "inflows very strong, collected >1,100 cr last year… availing WC limits gives leeway for 35-40% growth… **Q3/Q4 meaningfully better on cash and revenue**." **Said:** the debt build is deliberate WC funding for growth; H2-weighted collections. **Did NOT say:** a hard number for Q1 cash consumption. **Thesis implication:** an **~80 cr swing from net cash to net debt in one quarter** sits in tension with the "NWC days improved" narrative and cannot be checked (no CFS, no balance sheet). **NEW flag.** **Follow-up:** "Show the Q1 cash-flow statement — operating cash flow, WC movement, and SCF drawdown."
