## Step B. Business

1. Explosives earn 86% of revenue. FY26 segment revenue: explosives Rs 457.67 Cr, perlite Rs 55.84 Cr, other Rs 16.55 Cr, of Rs 532.06 Cr total. [Q2]
2. Products are cartridge and bulk emulsion explosives, MMAN, accessories, perlite filter aid and cryogenic insulation. The company runs 12 plants in eight states. [CR] [SCR]
3. Explosives volume rose 8.4% to 63,154 MT in FY26 from 58,280 MT. Buyers are PSUs, new MDO (mine developer operator) accounts and dealers. [AR26 p25]
4. No customer reaches 10% of revenue. CRISIL puts the top ten at about 21% of FY25 revenue. [AR26 p49] [CR]
5. Exports were Rs 82.84 Cr in FY26, 15.6% of revenue (derived). The AR names no markets. [AR26 p27]
6. Margin is a conversion spread. Materials are about 70% of production cost. OPM was 8.0% in FY26. Explosives segment EBIT margin was 5.7% in FY26 and 6.7% in Q2 FY27 (derived). [CR] [SCR] [Q2]
7. Q2 FY27 revenue rose 36.9% to Rs 160.53 Cr. PAT rose 33.1% to Rs 8.25 Cr. H1 FY27 PAT rose 13.4% on 31.9% revenue growth. [Q2]
8. H1 FY27 operating cash flow was Rs 6.38 Cr against Rs 16.33 Cr PAT (39%). Trade payables rose Rs 19.45 Cr and funded the gap. [Q2]
9. The engine is a TNT (defence explosive) plant at Garamsur. CRISIL states a cost of about Rs 190 Cr, Rs 135 Cr term debt, operations from April 2027. [CR] [AR26 p37]
10. The AR says only "to be commissioned fully in 2027" and gives no TNT capacity or offtake. No defence revenue appears in any segment today. [AR26 p27] [Q2]
11. The market prices the TNT plant ahead of delivery. The stock trades at 58.7x TTM earnings of Rs 30.6 Cr, against a 52-week low of Rs 2,900 and CMP Rs 16,564. TTM profit growth is 4%. [SCR]
12. Long-term borrowings rose to Rs 68.27 Cr at Sep 2026 from Rs 42.13 Cr at Mar 2026. H1 capex was Rs 25.30 Cr. [Q2]
13. No guidance exists. The company held no analyst or investor presentations in FY26. [AR26 p41]
