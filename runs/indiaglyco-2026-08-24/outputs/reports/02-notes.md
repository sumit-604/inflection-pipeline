   Pass 1 quoted two different "revenue" figures in different sections:

   One residual watch item surfaced by this check: "Miscellaneous Income"

2. **DRI/Customs note narrates events out of chronological order in a way

   **Thereafter**, a Show Cause Notice... dated 03.08.2022 was issued...

   the SCN and Order-in-Original followed the Gujarat HC stay, but both
   are dated before the stay order (03.08.2022 and 23.03.2024, both
   earlier than 17.10.2024). The note's narrative sequencing is
   internally inconsistent with its own dates. This does not change any
   number, but it means the stay's actual scope and effect (an order
   dated after the Order-in-Original it apparently was meant to
   prevent) cannot be resolved from the note as written. Yellow, watch —
   disclosure clarity gap on an active, previously-material litigation

RECONCILING PASS 1 AND PASS 2
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pass 2 tempered two Pass 1 readings and sharpened others:

- **Tempered — promoter control.** Pass 1's Finding #2 read Kashipur
  Holdings Ltd's (KHL) fall below 50% (50.35% to 49.77%, Note 18(c)) and
  its reclassification from Holding Company to related-party-by-
  significant-influence as a standalone dilution concern. Pass 2
  aggregated the full promoter list (Note 18(e)) and found the promoter
  GROUP (KHL + Estate of Sajani Devi Bhartia + five other named promoter
  entities) held 59.62% (FY26) vs 61.02% (FY25) — a modest 1.4pp
  reduction, not a loss of control. KHL's individual sub-50% status is
  therefore an Ind AS 24 single-largest-shareholder technicality for RPT
  disclosure purposes, not evidence of a control change. This
  consolidated report treats the finding accordingly: watch-rated for
  the unresolved allottee-identity question, not for control risk.
- **Sharpened — JV profit base.** Pass 1 (Investments section) took the
  Ind AS 112 JV summary's "Profit for the year: 4,641.68" figure
  (Note 58(a) standalone / 51(a) consol) at face value alongside the
  100%-basis Assets/Liabilities/Revenue in the same table, describing
  CISCPL as "profitable, well-disclosed." Pass 2 cross-checked against
  Form AOC-1 Part B and found 4,641.68 is actually IGL's 49% equity-
  accounted share; the JV's true 100%-basis FY26 profit is
  approximately Rs 9,472.81 Lakh (4,641.68 / 0.49), with Clariant's 51%
  share of Rs 4,831.13/4,831.14 Lakh reconciling almost exactly to the
  AOC-1 "not considered in consolidation" line. Net margin on the naive
  reading is 7.25%; on the corrected reading, 14.80%. This is a
  disclosure-clarity defect in the note (mixed consolidation bases
  presented in one table) and does not restate IGL's own consolidated
  numbers, but it materially changes how an investor should read CISCPL
  as a standalone earnings engine. Carried forward as a top finding.
- **Sharpened — working capital narrative.** Pass 1 read the combined
  contraction of inventory, receivable, and payable turnover as an
  unambiguous positive (all cycles compressing, DSO improving,
  inventory turnover improving). Pass 2 added the Financial Ratios
  note's current ratio (0.85 FY26 vs 0.90 FY25, both below 1.0) and
  negative Net Capital Turnover (-18.94x vs -20.22x), showing the
  turnover-ratio improvements sit alongside worsening point-in-time
  liquidity, not loosening. This consolidated report treats the overall
  working-capital picture as mixed: the flow-metric improvements are
  real, but the stock-metric (current ratio) deterioration is a genuine,
  separate signal that Pass 1 did not surface.
- **Sharpened — risk posture on FX.** Pass 1 logged the FX sensitivity
  number (1% USD move now swings PBT by Rs 295.69 Lakh vs Rs 155.46 Lakh
  prior year, Note 45(i)(a)) without explaining the driver. Pass 2 found
  the driver: outstanding hedge notional collapsed 92.2% (USD 52.86mn to
  USD 3.73mn) while the unhedged export/import exposure roughly doubled,
  with zero management narrative anywhere in the notes. This is the
  single highest-severity finding across all three passes and is rated
  Red Flag, not Watch, in this consolidated report.
